# freight_terminal_safety_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The freight terminal safety inspection form is used to identify and document safety risks and hazards at freight terminals. It helps terminals evaluate their safety status, pinpoint areas for improvement, and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your terminal's information in the `terminal_info` field.
2. Fill out the `terminal_name` field with the name of your terminal.
3. Date and time of the inspection should be entered in the `inspection_date` field.
4. Provide your name and title/position in the `inspector_info` field.
5. Select all areas that need inspection from the `sections_to_inspect` field.
6. For each section selected, choose the correct status from the `section_inspection_status` field.
7. Add any observations or comments related to the inspection in the `observation_and_comments` field.
8. Enter your name in the `terminal_inspector` field.
9. Finally, sign and date the form using the `terminal_signature` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Terminal Info** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your terminal.
* **Terminal Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your terminal.
* **Inspection Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date and time of the inspection.
* **Inspector Info** (Text, Optional): Enter your name and title/position.
* **Sections to Inspect** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all areas that need inspection:
	+ Yard Area
	+ Terminal Operations
	+ Terminal Equipment
	+ Terminal Access
	+ Security
* **Section Inspection Status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the status for each selected area:
	+ Compliant
	+ Non-Compliant
* **Observation and Comments** (Note, Optional): Provide any observations or comments related to the inspection.
* **Terminal Inspector** (Text, Optional): Enter your name.
* **Terminal Signature** (Date, Optional): Date and sign the form.
