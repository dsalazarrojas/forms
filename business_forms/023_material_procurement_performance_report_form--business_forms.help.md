# Material Procurement Performance Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Material Procurement Performance Report Form is used to collect information about the performance of material procurement activities. This form helps in tracking and evaluating the efficiency and effectiveness of material procurement processes. It is essential for identifying areas of improvement and making data-driven decisions to optimize procurement operations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Supplier Name**: Enter the name of the supplier you are interacting with.
	* **Procurement Category**: Select the category of the material or service being procured (Materials, Services, or Equipment).
	* **Submission Date**: (Optional) Date of submission, usually the date when the report is sent to the procurement team.
2. Provide optional information:
	* **Procurement Date**: (Optional) Date when the procurement took place.
	* **Procurement Value**: (Optional) Monetary value of the procurement.
	* **Supplier Rating**: (Optional) Rate the supplier's performance on a scale of 1-5.
	* **Procurement Frequency**: (Optional) Frequency of the procurement (Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-Annually, or Annually).
	* **Procurement Method**: (Optional) Method of procurement (Online, Off-site, or On-site).
3. Enter contact information:
	* **Contact Name**: Full name of the person submitting the report.
	* **Contact Email**: Email address of the person submitting the report.
	* **Contact Phone**: (Optional) Phone number of the person submitting the report.
	* **Contact Note**: Additional notes about the contact person.
4. Add any additional notes:
	* **Supplier Note**: Additional notes about the supplier.
	* **Procurement Note**: Additional notes about the procurement activity.
	* **Procurement Method**: Additional notes about the procurement method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the supplier you are interacting with.
* **Procurement Category (Optional)**: Select the category of the material or service being procured (Materials, Services, or Equipment).
* **Procurement Date (Optional)**: Date when the procurement took place (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Procurement Value (Optional)**: Monetary value of the procurement (USD).
* **Supplier Rating (Optional)**: Rate the supplier's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Procurement Frequency (Required)**: Frequency of the procurement (Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-Annually, or Annually).
* **Procurement Method (Optional)**: Method of procurement (Online, Off-site, or On-site).
* **Contact Name (Required)**: Full name of the person submitting the report.
* **Contact Email (Required)**: Email address of the person submitting the report.
* **Contact Phone (Optional)**: Phone number of the person submitting the report.
* **Contact Note (Optional)**: Additional notes about the contact person.
* **Supplier Note (Optional)**: Additional notes about the supplier.
* **Procurement Note (Optional)**: Additional notes about the procurement activity.
* **Submission Date (Optional)**: Date of submission, usually the date when the report is sent to the procurement team.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
* Use the correct format for dates (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Keep notes sections for any additional information that might be relevant to the report.
* Review and submit the report carefully to avoid errors.
