# Remit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Remit Form is a data collection form used to record remittance information for various transactions. It captures details from both the sender and receiver sides, including contact information, transaction details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Remit Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Sender Details section with the sender's information:
	* **Sender Details**: Enter a brief description of the sender.
	* **Sender Name**: Enter the name of the sender.
	* **Sender Email**: Enter the email address of the sender.
	* **Sender Phone**: Enter the phone number of the sender.
2. Fill in the Receiver Details section with the receiver's information:
	* **Receiver Details**: Enter a brief description of the receiver.
	* **Receiver Name**: Enter the name of the receiver.
	* **Receiver Phone**: Enter the phone number of the receiver.
3. Enter the transaction details:
	* **Amount Details**: Enter a brief description of the transaction amount.
	* **Amount Currency**: Enter the currency of the transaction.
	* **Transaction Date**: Select the date of the transaction.
	* **Transaction Time**: Select the time of the transaction.
4. Enter the payment information:
	* **Amount Description**: Enter a brief description of the transaction amount.
	* **Payment Mode**: Enter the mode of payment (e.g., bank transfer, cash, etc.).
	* **Payment Details**: Enter any additional payment details.
5. Add sender and receiver address information:
	* **Sender Address**: Enter the sender's address.
	* **Sender City**: Enter the sender's city.
	* **Sender Country**: Enter the sender's country.
	* **Sender Postal Code**: Enter the sender's postal code.
	* **Sender Zip**: Enter the sender's zip code.
	* **Receiver Address**: Enter the receiver's address.
	* **Receiver City**: Enter the receiver's city.
	* **Receiver Country**: Enter the receiver's country.
