# informed_client_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients, including their consent, medical history, contact details, and other relevant medical information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review your client's medical condition and history to ensure the information provided is accurate and relevant.
2. Complete each field with the requested information, paying attention to the type of information required for each field.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed, as indicated by the "required" label.
