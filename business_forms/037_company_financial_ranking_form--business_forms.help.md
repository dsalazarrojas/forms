# Company Financial Ranking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect financial data on companies. It is meant to gather crucial information about a company's financial standing. The form will help in ranking and evaluating companies based on their provided information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by ensuring the company's name and fiscal year are accurately entered in the first two fields.
2. Enter the annual revenue generated by the company.
3. If applicable, provide the net income for the reported period.
4. Enter the total number of employees, or leave empty if not available.
5. Specify the industry sector the company primarily operates in.
6. If needed, add any comments supporting the ranking decisions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company name (company_name)**: Enter the legal name of the company you are evaluating. This is an essential field as it identifies the company being ranked.
* **Fiscal year (fiscal_year)**: This is the year for which the company's financial figures are being reported. Ensure it's correctly entered for accurate data comparison.
* **Annual Revenue (annual_revenue)**: Report the company's annual revenue for the specified fiscal year. This is a required field for the purpose of this form.
* **Net income (net_income)**: If available, enter the net income for the reported fiscal year. This field is optional but can provide additional insight into the company's financial status.
* **Number of employees (employees_count)**: If you have access to this information, report the full-time equivalent number of employees. This field is optional and helps in understanding the company's scale of operation.
* **Industry sector (industry_sector)**: Identify the primary industry the company operates in. This information is crucial for comparative analysis across the sector.
* **Comments for ranking (rating_criteria_comments)**: If you have any supporting comments for the ranking decision, add them here. This is an optional field but can provide valuable context to the evaluation process.

## Tips

- Ensure all required fields are accurately filled out before submission.
- Enter figures accurately, considering the fiscal year specified.
- If a field is optional, only fill it out if the information is available and relevant to your evaluation.
- Double-check the industry sector for correct classification.
