# Eco Friendly Supplier Recognition Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from suppliers about their sustainable practices and certifications. The information collected will help us recognize and reward eco-friendly suppliers who have implemented sustainable practices and have relevant certifications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out the "Supplier Information" page (page 1) with your supplier's details.
3. Proceed to the "Contact Information" page (page 2) and fill out your contact details.
4. Move on to the "Company Information" page (page 3) and provide information about your company.
5. On the "Sustainability" page (page 4), list your company's sustainable practices and certifications.
6. On the "Certifications" page (page 5), select any relevant certifications your company has achieved.
7. Review your information on the "Submission" page (page 6) and make sure everything is correct before submitting.
8. Finally, click on "Back" (page 7) to go back and review your information or "Submit" (page 8) to submit your application.
