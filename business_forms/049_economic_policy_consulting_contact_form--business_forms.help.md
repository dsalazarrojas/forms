# Economic Policy Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or organizations seeking economic policy consulting services. It allows you to provide your name, contact information, and specific details about your requirements, such as preferred date and time for a consultation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, including both your first and last name.
2. Provide a valid email address for communication.
3. Enter your phone number, including the country code if applicable.
4. Describe your organization, including the name of your company or institution.
5. Write a clear and concise message about your economic policy consulting needs.
6. Select the type of service you are interested in, choosing from "consulting", "research", or "development".
7. Choose one or more relevant options that best describe your needs.
8. Select a date and time for the consultation that suits you.
9. Choose a time slot for the consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address that we can use for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, including the country code if applicable.
* **Organization**: Describe your organization, including the name of your company or institution.
* **Message**: Write a clear and concise message about your economic policy consulting needs. Be specific about what services you are looking for.
* **Type of Service**: Choose the type of service you are interested in:
	+ **Consulting**: We provide expert consulting services to help you achieve your economic policy goals.
	+ **Research**: We offer research services to gather and analyze relevant data and information.
	+ **Development**: Our development services help you create and implement effective economic policy strategies.
* **Options**: Choose one or more relevant options that best describe your needs.
* **Date**: Select a date for the consultation that suits you.
* **Time**: Choose a time slot for the consultation that works for you.
