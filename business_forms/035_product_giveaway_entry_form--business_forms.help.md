# Product Giveaway Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for entering the "Product Giveaway" contest. Fill out the form completely and accurately to be eligible for the giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Enter your Street address.
6. Enter your City.
7. Select your State.
8. Enter your Country.
9. Enter your ZIP code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you use for communication. We'll use this to contact you if you win.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up questions or to confirm the shipping address.
* **Street** (`street`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the street address where you live or work.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city where you live or work.
* **State** (`state`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the state you live in from the provided list.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the country you live in.
* **ZIP** (`zip`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the ZIP code of the area you live or work in.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us.
* We'll use the information provided to contact you if you win the giveaway.
