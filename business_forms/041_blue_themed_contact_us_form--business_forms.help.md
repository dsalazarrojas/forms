# Blue Themed Contact Us Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Blue Themed Contact Us Form is designed to collect information from visitors who want to get in touch with the company's team. This form is used to handle various types of requests, such as sales inquiries, technical support, or partnership opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to initiate the form submission process.
2. Fill out the required fields with your information, including your full name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
3. Select the category of your inquiry from the dropdown menu.
4. Provide a brief subject and description of your inquiry in the text boxes.
5. Optionally, select your preferred contact method and best time to contact.
6. If you have a specific deadline or need a response within a certain timeframe, select the relevant option from the dropdown menu.
7. Finally, click on the "Submit" button to send your inquiry to the company's team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Contact Our Team --** (`contact_intro`, note, not required): This is a note field that serves as an introduction to the form. It is not a required field, but it provides a brief message encouraging visitors to get in touch with the team.
* **Full Name** (`visitor_name`, text, required): This field is for your name, which will be displayed on your inquiry.
* **Email Address** (`visitor_email`, email, required): This field is for your email address, where you can be contacted by the company's team.
* **Phone Number** (`visitor_phone`, text, not required): This field is for your phone number, if you would like to be contacted over the phone.
* **Company Name** (`visitor_company`, text, not required): This field is for your company name, if you are representing a company.
* **Job Title** (`visitor_position`, text, not required): This field is for your job title or position, if you have one.
* **Inquiry Category** (`inquiry_category`, select_one, required): This dropdown menu offers different categories for your inquiry, such as General inquiry, Sales, Support, etc.
* **Subject** (`inquiry_subject`, text, required): This field is for a brief description of your inquiry or request.
* **Message** (`inquiry_message`, text, required): This field is for a more detailed description of your inquiry or request.
* **When do you need a response?** (`timeline`, select_one, not required): This dropdown menu offers different timeframes for when you need a response, such as ASAP, Within 24 hours, etc.
* **Approximate Budget (if applicable)** (`budget_availability`, text, not required): This field is for your approximate budget, if you have one.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, required): This dropdown menu offers different methods of contact, such as Email, Phone, Video call, etc.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time`, select_one, not required): This dropdown menu offers different time slots for contact, such as Morning, Afternoon, Evening, etc.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): This field is for the date of your submission.
* **Submission Date Hint:** This field is not displayed on the form and should be filled out by the system administrator when the submission is made. It is not required for the user to fill out.
