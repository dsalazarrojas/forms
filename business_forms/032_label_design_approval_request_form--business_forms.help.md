# Label Design Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Label Design Approval Request Form is designed to collect information from users who wish to request approval for a label design. It is intended to be used for internal purposes, such as label design approval requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This should be a valid email address.
4. Select your department from the "Department" dropdown menu. You can choose from Sales, Marketing, Finance, or Other.
5. Select your role from the "Role" dropdown menu. You can choose from CEO, Manager, Employee, or Other.
6. Fill in your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
7. Enter the Date of Last Review in the "Date of Last Review" field in the format of HH:MM:SS.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Please enter a valid email address.
* **Department (Select Multiple)**: Please select your department from the available options. If you don't see your department listed, you can select "Other" and provide more information in the "Other" field.
* **Role (Select One)**: Please select your role from the available options.
* **Date of Birth (Date)**: Please enter your date of birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Date of Last Review (Time)**: Please enter the date and time of your last review in the format of HH:MM:SS.
* **Other (Text)**: If you select "Other" in the "Department" field, please provide more information about your department in this field.
