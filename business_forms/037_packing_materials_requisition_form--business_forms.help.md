# Packing Materials Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Packing Materials Requisition Form is used to record the requisition of packing materials for shipping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Enter the assigned tool for the packing material
2. Enter the packing material being requisitioned
3. Enter the shipping location
4. Enter any shipping notes
5. Enter the quantity of materials ordered
6. Enter the quantity received
7. Enter the person responsible for ordering the materials
8. Enter the person the materials were ordered for
9. Select the shipping method
10. Enter the shipping method details
11. Enter the form ID
12. Enter the date and time the form was submitted
13. Enter the user who submitted the form
14. Enter any additional notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID** (1): A unique identifier for the form.
* **Assigned Tool** (2): The tool assigned for the packing materials requisitioned.
* **Packing Materials Requisitioning** (3): A description of the packing materials being requisitioned.
* **Shipping Location** (4): The location where the materials are being shipped to.
* **Shipping Notes** (5): Any additional notes for the shipping process.
* **Packing Materials Ordered** (6): The quantity of materials ordered.
* **Quantity Ordered** (7): The quantity of materials ordered.
* **Packing Materials Ordered By** (8): The person responsible for ordering the materials.
* **Packing Materials Ordered To** (9): The person the materials were ordered for.
* **Shipping Method** (10): Select the shipping method to be used.
* **Shipping Method Details** (11): Additional details for the shipping method.
* **Form ID** (12): A unique identifier for the form.
* **Form Date** (13): The date the form was submitted.
* **Form Time** (14): The time the form was submitted.
* **Form User** (15): The user who submitted the form.
* **Notes** (16): Any additional notes.
* **Email** (17): Contact email address.
* **Phone** (18): Contact phone number.
* **Assigned To** (19): The person assigned to receive the materials.
* **Assigned To Date** (20): The date the materials were assigned to.
* **Assigned To Time** (21): The time the materials were assigned to.
* **Assigned To User** (22): The user who was assigned to receive the materials.
* **Packing Materials Ordered Date** (23): The date the materials were ordered.
* **Packing Materials Ordered Time** (24): The time the materials were ordered.

Note: This form is not intended to be completed multiple times for the same materials and date. If you need to record multiple requisitions, please create a new form or contact the administrator for assistance.
