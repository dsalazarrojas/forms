# Inside Sales Daily Emails Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inside Sales Daily Emails Report Form is designed to collect sales-related data from inside sales representatives. It serves as a daily report for inside sales teams to report their sales activities, providing insights into their performance and progress. This form helps inside sales teams track their sales activities, categorize their efforts, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the **Category** field to choose the relevant category for your sales activities.
2.  Type in the **Inside Sales Daily Emails Report Form** field to provide a brief description of your activities.
3.  Enter the **Form ID** field with the ID of the form used for reporting.
4.  Optionally, provide an **Output File** field with the name or file path of the output file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Category**: This field is required and allows you to categorize your sales activities for better tracking and analysis. Select the category that best represents your sales activities.
*  **Inside Sales Daily Emails Report Form**: Provide a brief description of your sales activities, including any relevant details or accomplishments. This field is required.
*  **Form ID**: Enter the ID of the form used for reporting. This field is required and helps in tracking and identifying the specific form used.
*  **Output File**: If you have created an output file for your sales activities, enter the name or file path here. This field is optional and helps in keeping a record of your output.
*  **Assigned Tool**: If you have used a specific tool for your sales activities, enter the name of the tool here. This field is optional and helps in tracking the tools used.
