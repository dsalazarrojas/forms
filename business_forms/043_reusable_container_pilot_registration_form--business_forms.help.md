# Reusable Container Pilot Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help pilot teams gather information for registration purposes. It includes various fields to capture details such as name, email, phone number, address, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reviewing the form's pages. This form has multiple pages, with each page containing various fields to fill out.
2. Fill out each page according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Text): Enter your name in this field.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address in this format: `example@example.com`.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number in this format: `123-456-7890`.
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Select One** (Select One): Choose one of the provided options (option 1, option 2, or option 3).
* **Select Multiple** (Select Multiple): Choose one or more of the provided options (option 1, option 2, or option 3).
* **Note** (Note): Enter any additional comments or notes about your response.
* **Business Form Section** (Text): Enter any additional information about the business form section.
* **Address** (Text): Enter your address.
* **City** (Text): Enter your city.
* **Country** (Text): Enter your country.
* **Region** (Text): Enter your region.
* **Zip** (Text): Enter your zip code.
* **Select Date** (Select One): Choose one of the provided options (option 1, option 2, or option 3).
* **Select Time** (Select One): Choose one of the provided options (option 1, option 2, or option 3).
* **Select Integer** (Select One): Choose one of the provided options (option 1, option 2, or option 3).
* **Select Decimal** (Select One): Choose one of the provided options (option 1, option 2, or option 3).
* **Select Multiple Integer** (Select Multiple): Choose one or more of the provided options (option 1, option 2, or option 3).
* **Select Multiple Decimal** (Select Multiple): Choose one or more of the provided options (option 1, option 2, or option 3).
* **Date Time** (Text): Enter the date and time in the format `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Integer** (Number): Enter a numerical value for the integer field.
* **Decimal** (Number): Enter a decimal value for the decimal field.
* **Note 2** (Note): Enter any additional comments or notes about your response.

Note: Fields marked as "Select One" or "Select Multiple" require you to choose one or more options from the provided list.
