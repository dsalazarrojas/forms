# customs_inspection_breach_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to document and track customs inspection breaches and incidents, ensuring all relevant information is captured and facilitating prompt response.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Date Of Incident" field.
2.  Specify the location of the incident in the "Incident Location" field.
3.  Provide details of what happened in the "Details Of Incident" field.
4.  Describe the actions taken to address the incident in the "Actions Taken" field.
5.  Add any relevant notes in the "Notes" field.
6.  Select the person assigned to follow up on the incident in the "Assigned To" field.
7.  Specify the status of the incident in the "Incident Status" field.
8.  Enter the date and time of resolution in the "Resolution Date" field, if applicable.
9.  Add any notes about the resolution in the "Resolution Notes" field, if applicable.
10.  Enter follow-up notes and any actions taken in the "Follow Up Notes" field.
11.  Select the incident report type in the "Incident Report Type" field.
12.  Enter the follow-up status in the "Follow Up Status" field.
13.  Enter the follow-up date and time in the "Follow Up Date" field, if applicable.
14.  Specify the follow-up location in the "Follow Up Location" field, if applicable.
15.  Enter the follow-up notes in the "Follow Up Notes" field, if applicable.
16.  Describe the follow-up action taken in the "Follow Up Action Taken" field, if applicable.
17.  Specify the follow-up action taken by in the "Follow Up Action Taken By" field, if applicable.
18.  Add any follow-up status notes in the "Follow Up Status Notes" field, if applicable.
19.  Enter the incident ID in the "Incident ID" field.
20.  Enter the incident number in the "Incident Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Breach Incident**: Enter a brief description of the incident.
*   **Date Of Incident**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
*   **Time Of Incident**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Details Of Incident**: Provide a detailed account of what happened.
*   **Actions Taken**: Describe the actions taken to address the incident.
*   **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments.
*   **Assigned To**: Select the person assigned to follow up on the incident.
*   **Incident Status**: Specify the current status of the incident.
*   **Resolution Date**: Enter the date and time of resolution (if applicable).
*   **Resolution Time**: Enter the time of resolution (if applicable).
*   **Resolution Notes**: Add any notes about the resolution (if applicable).
*   **Follow Up Notes**: Enter follow-up notes and any actions taken.
*   **Incident Report Type**: Select the type of incident report.
*   **Follow Up Status**: Select the follow-up status.
*   **Follow Up Date**: Enter the date of follow up (if applicable).
*   **Follow Up Time**: Enter the time of follow up (if applicable).
*   **Follow Up Location**: Enter the location of follow up (if applicable).
*   **Follow Up Notes**: Add follow-up notes and any actions taken (if applicable).
*   **Follow Up Action Taken**: Describe the follow-up action taken (if applicable).
*   **Follow Up Action Taken By**: Specify the person who took the follow-up action (if applicable).
*   **Follow Up Status Notes**: Add any follow-up status notes (if applicable).
*   **Incident ID**: Enter the unique ID for the incident.
*   **Incident Number**: Enter the unique number for the incident.
*   **Incident Type**: Enter the type of incident (if applicable).

## Tips

*   Make sure to capture all relevant information about the incident.
*   Be as detailed as possible in the "Details Of Incident" field.
*   Ensure that the follow-up actions and status are accurately recorded.
*   Update the status of the incident as necessary.
*   Review and update the form regularly to ensure that the information is up-to-date.
