# performance_metrics_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The performance metrics monitoring form is a tool for collecting and tracking business or operational performance metrics from users. This form helps in monitoring performance indicators and providing insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the performance metrics in the **performance metrics** field (id: 1).
2. Specify the **metric name** in the **metric name** field (id: 2).
3. Enter the **metric value** in the **metric value** field (id: 3).
4. Choose the **metric type** from the options provided in the **metric type** field (id: 4).
5. Enter the **metric target** value in the **metric target** field (id: 6).
6. Choose how often the metric is reported in the **metric frequency** field (id: 7).
7. Choose the unit of measurement for the metric in the **metric unit** field (id: 8).
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the **metric note** field (id: 9).
9. Enter the **metric email** contact information for further follow-up in the **metric email** field (id: 10).
10. Enter the **metric phone** number for direct contact in the **metric phone** field (id: 11).
11. Select the **performance indicators** that are relevant to the metric in the **performance indicators** field (id: 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performance Metrics** (id: 1, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the performance metric you're tracking.
* **Metric Name** (id: 2, text, required: false): Enter a specific name for the metric, e.g., "Sales Revenue".
* **Metric Value** (id: 3, number, required: false): Enter the current value of the metric.
* **Metric Type** (id: 4, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of metric from the options provided, e.g., "Sales", "Production".
* **Metric Target** (id: 5, number, required: false): Enter the target value for the metric.
* **Metric Target Value** (id: 6, number, required: false): Enter the target value for the metric.
* **Metric Frequency** (id: 7, select_multiple, required: false): Choose how often the metric is reported, e.g., "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly".
* **Metric Unit** (id: 8, select_one, required: false): Choose the unit of measurement for the metric, e.g., "USD", "Percentage".
* **Metric Note** (id: 9, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the metric.
* **Metric Email** (id: 10, email, required: false): Enter the email contact information for the metric.
* **Metric Phone** (id: 11, text, required: false): Enter the phone number for direct contact.
* **Performance Indicators** (id: 12, select_multiple, required: false): Select the performance indicators that are relevant to the metric, e.g., "Indicator1", "Indicator2", "Indicator3".
