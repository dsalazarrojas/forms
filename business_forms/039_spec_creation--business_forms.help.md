# spec_creation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to create and manage specifications for business products. It captures key information about products, including names, types, statuses, priorities, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Product Name** in the text field under "Product Name".
2. Select the **Product Type** from the dropdown menu. Options include "Service" and "Product".
3. Enter the **Spec Name** in the text field under "Spec Name".
4. Provide a detailed description of the **Spec Description** in the text field.
5. Select the **Spec Status** from the dropdown menu. Options include "In Progress", "Complete", and "Cancelled".
6. Enter the **Spec Priority** as a numerical value.
7. Enter the **Created By** in the text field.
8. Enter the date and time the **Created At** in the date field.
9. Enter the **Updated By** in the text field.
10. Enter the date and time the **Updated At** in the date field.
11. Select the team member or members to be assigned to the product from the dropdown menu under "Assigned To". Options include "John Doe" and "Jane Doe".
12. Enter any additional **Notes** in the text field.
13. Enter the **Spec Version** as a numerical value.
14. Enter the date and time the product was **Created At (UTC)** in the date field.
15. Enter the date and time the product was **Updated At (UTC)** in the date field.
16. Enter the **Team Lead** in the text field.
17. Enter the date the product was **Spec Created** in the date field.
18. Enter the date the product was **Spec Updated** in the date field.
19. Enter the person or team member who **Assigned By** in the text field.
20. Enter the person or team member who **Updated By** in the text field.
21. Select the status of the product from the dropdown menu under "Spec Status 2". Options include "In Progress", "Complete", and "Cancelled".
22. Enter the detailed description of the product under "Spec Description 2".
23. Enter the name of the product under "Spec Name 2".
24. Enter the person or team member who assigned this product under "Assigned By 2".
25. Enter the date and time the product was created in the date field "Created At 2".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the product being specified.
* **Product Type** (select one, required): Select the type of product, either "Service" or "Product".
* **Spec Name** (text, required): Enter a name for the specification.
* **Spec Description** (text, required): Provide a detailed description of the specification.
* **Spec Status** (select one, required): Select the status of the specification, either "In Progress", "Complete", or "Cancelled".
* **Spec Priority** (number, required): Enter the priority of the specification as a numerical value.
* **Created By** (text, required): Enter the name of the person or team that created the specification.
* **Created At** (date, required): Enter the date and time the specification was created.
* **Updated By** (text, required): Enter the name of the person or team that updated the specification.
* **Updated At** (date, required): Enter the date and time the specification was last updated.
* **Assigned To** (select multiple, required): Select the team member or members assigned to the specification, either "John Doe" or "Jane Doe".
* **Notes** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the specification.
* **Spec Version** (number, required): Enter the version number of the specification.
* **Created At (UTC)** (date, required): Enter the date and time the specification was created in UTC time zone.
* **Updated At (UTC)** (date, required): Enter the date and time the specification was last updated in UTC time zone.
* **Team Lead** (text, required): Enter the name of the team lead responsible for the specification.
* **Spec Created** (date, optional): Enter the date the specification was created.
* **Spec Updated** (date, optional): Enter the date the specification was last updated.
* **Assigned By** (text, required): Enter the name of the person or team that assigned the specification.
* **Updated By 2** (text, required): Enter the name of the person or team that updated the specification.
* **Spec Status 2** (select one, required): Select the status of the specification, either "In Progress", "Complete", or "Cancelled".
* **Spec Description 2** (text, required): Enter a detailed description of the specification.
* **Spec Name 2** (text, required): Enter the name of the specification.
* **Assigned By 2** (text, required): Enter the name of the person or team that assigned the specification.
* **Created At 2** (date, required): Enter the date and time the specification was created.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
- Review and verify the information provided before submission.
- Use the correct format for date and time entries.
- Use the correct options for product type and status.
- If unsure, refer to the relevant business process guidelines.
