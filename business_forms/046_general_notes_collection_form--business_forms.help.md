# General Notes Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect general notes from users in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category of the note from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter a title for your note in the "Title" field.
3. Provide a detailed description of your note in the "Notes" field.
4. Select the categories that apply to your note from the "Categories" field.
5. Choose the priority of your note from the dropdown menu.
6. If necessary, attach a file or image to your note using the "Attachments" field.
7. Enter the date of your submission using the "Submission Date" field.
8. Enter the time of your submission using the "Submission Time" field.
9. Select who submitted the note from the "Submitter's Info" field.
10. Enter the date and time of your submission using the "Submission Date and Time" field.
11. Select the time zone of your submission from the "Submission Date and Time Time Zone" field.
12. Choose the format of your submission date and time from the "Submission Date and Time Format" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Notes Collection Form** (`title`, required/optional): The main title of the form.
* **Category** (`category`, optional): Select a category for your note from the dropdown menu.
* **Title** (`title`, optional): Enter a title for your note.
* **Notes** (`notes`, optional): Provide a detailed description of your note.
* **Categories** (`categories`, required): Select the categories that apply to your note from the "Categories" field.
* **Priority** (`priority`, required): Choose the priority of your note from the dropdown menu.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, optional): If necessary, attach a file or image to your note.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, optional): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, optional): Enter the time of your submission.
* **Submitter's Info** (`submitted_by`, required): Select who submitted the note from the "Submitter's Info" field.
* **Submission Date and Time** (`submission_date_time`, optional): Enter the date and time of your submission.
* **Submission Date and Time Time Zone** (`submission_date_time_tz`, required): Select the time zone of your submission from the "Submission Date and Time Time Zone" field.
* **Submission Date and Time Format** (`submission_date_time_format`, required): Choose the format of your submission date and time from the "Submission Date and Time Format" field.
