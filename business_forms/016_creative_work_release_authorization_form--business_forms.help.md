# Creative Work Release Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary information and authorization for the release of a creative work, such as a visual art piece, music composition, or other original content created by an artist or artist team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the release date of the work, if applicable.
2.  Enter the name of the work you are releasing.
3.  Enter the name of the artist or team involved in creating the work.
4.  Enter the name of the client or entity commissioning the work.
5.  If applicable, enter the name of the client's company.
6.  Briefly describe the work, including any notable features or details.
7.  Provide the URL of the work if it is publicly available.
8.  Add any additional notes about the release, such as exhibition or event details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Release Date**: Enter the date of the work's release, if applicable. This is the date the work will be made available to the public. If no release date is known, this field may be left blank.
*   **Work Name**: Enter the title or name of your creative work. This is the primary identifier for the work and will be used in various mediums, such as digital platforms, exhibitions, and publications.
*   **Artist Name**: Enter the name of the artist or team involved in creating the work. This is crucial for crediting the creator and providing proper documentation.
*   **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client or entity commissioning the work. This is essential for understanding the context and ownership of the work.
*   **Client Company (optional)**: If the client has a company or organization associated with the commission, enter its name here.
*   **Work Description**: Briefly describe your creative work, including any notable features or details that are relevant to understanding and contextualizing the piece.
*   **Work URL (optional)**: If the work is publicly available, provide the URL where it can be accessed. This can be useful for further reference and promotion.
*   **Release Notes (optional)**: Add any additional notes about the release, such as exhibition or event details, that are relevant to the work's dissemination.

Note: The required fields are marked as such in the form, and it's essential to complete them accurately to ensure proper authorization and documentation.
