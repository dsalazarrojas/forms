# 360 Degree Action Planner - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 360 Degree Action Planner is a tool designed to help you plan and track your goals and objectives. This form is used to gather information about your personal and professional details, as well as your experience with the tool.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in your personal details in the "Personal and Plan Details" section, including your full name, email address, department, job title, and years employed.
*   Enter your goal description, priority level, and start date for your action plan.
*   Select the status of your action plan, choose an owner for your plan, and enter the due date.
*   Rate your satisfaction with the 360 Degree Action Planner tool, and provide feedback on communication from management and professional development support.
*   Finally, rate your work-life balance and provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal and Plan Details
* **Full name** (Full Name, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email address** (Email address, email, required): Enter your work email.
* **Department** (Department, text, required): Enter your department.
* **Job title** (Job title, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Date of hire** (Date of hire, date, optional): Enter the date you were hired.
* **Years employed** (Years employed, integer, optional): Enter the approximate number of years you've been employed.
* **Action Plan ID** (Action Plan ID, text, required): Enter a unique identifier for your action plan.
* **Goal description** (Goal description, text, required): Enter a description of your goal.
* **Priority level** (Priority level, select_one, required): Select the priority level of your action plan (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
* **Owner name** (Owner name, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for your action plan.
* **Start date** (Start date, date, required): Enter the start date of your action plan.
* **Due date** (Due date, date, required): Enter the due date of your action plan.
* **Status** (Status, select_one, required): Select the status of your action plan (Not started, In progress, On hold, Completed, Cancelled).
* **Progress percentage** (Progress percentage, integer, optional): Enter a value between 0 and 100 to indicate your progress.
* **Risk assessment** (Risk assessment, select_one, optional): Select the risk assessment of your action plan (None, Low, Moderate, High, Critical).
* **Impact rating** (Impact rating, select_one, optional): Select the impact rating of your action plan (Low, Medium, High, Strategic).
* **Action plan comments** (Action plan comments, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your action plan.
### Feedback on Planner Tool
* **How satisfied are you with the 360 Degree Action Planner?** (How satisfied are you with the 360 Degree Action Planner?, select_one, required): Select how satisfied you are with the tool (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
* **How would you rate communication from management?** (How would you rate communication from management?, select_one, required): Select how you rate communication from management (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Do you feel supported in professional development?** (Do you feel supported in professional development?, select_one, required): Select how you feel about professional development support (Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly disagree).
* **Rate your work-life balance** (Rate your work-life balance, integer, optional): Rate your work-life balance on a scale of 1-10.
* **What could improve your experience at work?** (What could improve your experience at work?, text, optional): Enter any suggestions or comments to improve your experience.
* **Additional comments or notes** (Additional comments or notes, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
