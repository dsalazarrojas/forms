# Receiving Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the receiving management practices within your department or section. It will help us understand your current processes and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For each question, ensure you are referring to the specific department or section within the organization.
3. Mark the required fields with a checkmark or other indicator to show you have completed them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Management Practices** (`General Management Practices`, text, required): This section asks about the current management practices in place within your department or section. Please describe the procedures and policies that are used to manage receiving.
- **What are the current management procedures used?** (`What are the current management procedures used?`, text, required): This question aims to understand the existing management procedures, including any protocols or checklists used during receiving.
- **Management Goals and Objectives for the next quarter** (`What are the management goals and objectives for the next quarter?`, text, optional): This is an opportunity to provide insight into the department's or section's goals and objectives for the next quarter. Please describe the management goals and objectives.
- **How often is receiving done at the department/section?** (`How often is receiving done at the department/section?`, select_one, required): Choose the most appropriate answer from the options provided to indicate how often receiving is performed at your department or section.
  • **Often**: Receiving is frequently performed multiple times a day or week.
  • **Occasionally**: Receiving is performed regularly but not very frequently.
  • **Rarely**: Receiving is rarely done.
- **Are there any specific actions taken during receiving?** (`Are there any specific actions taken during receiving?`, text, optional): This section is for providing information on any specific actions taken during receiving. Please elaborate on the procedures followed.
- **Are there any issues that have arisen during receiving?** (`Are there any issues that have arisen during receiving?`, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the options provided to indicate any issues that have occurred during receiving.
  • **True**: An issue has arisen during receiving.
  • **False**: No issues have arisen during receiving.
  • **Other (please describe)**: Provide a detailed description of any issues that have not been captured in the options.
- **Can you provide more information about receiving management?** (`Can you provide more information about receiving management?`, text, optional): This is an opportunity to share any additional insights or thoughts about receiving management in your department or section.
- **What are your suggestions for improvement?** (`What are your suggestions for improvement?`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the current receiving management practices.
- **What date is the last time receiving was done?** (`What date is the last time receiving was done?`, date, required): Enter the date when receiving was last performed.
- **What time is receiving usually done?** (`What time is receiving usually done?`, time, required): Select the most common time of day when receiving is performed.
  • **Morning**: Receiving is usually done in the morning.
  • **Afternoon**: Receiving is usually done in the afternoon.
  • **Evening**: Receiving is usually done in the evening.
- **What is the average number of items received?** (`What is the average number of items received?`, number, required): Enter the average number of items received during the specified period.
- **What is the average weight of items received?** (`What is the average weight of items received?`, number, required): Enter the average weight of items received during the specified period.
- **Are there any specific receiving locations?** (`Are there any specific receiving locations?`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply from the options provided to indicate any specific locations where receiving is performed.
  • **True**: Receiving is performed at a specific location.
  • **False**: Receiving is not performed at a specific location.
  • **Other (please describe)**: Provide a detailed description of any specific locations where receiving is performed.
- **What is the average time spent on receiving?** (`What is the average time spent on receiving?`, number, required): Enter the average time spent on receiving during the specified period.
- **What is the average number of items received per week?** (`What is the average number of items received per week?`, number, required): Enter the average number of items received per week.
- **What time of day is receiving usually done?** (`What time of day is receiving usually done?`, select_one, required): Select the most common time of day when receiving is performed.
  • **Morning**: Receiving is usually done in the morning.
  • **Afternoon**: Receiving is usually done in the afternoon.
  • **Evening**: Receiving is usually done in the evening.
- **What type of receiving is usually done?** (`What type of receiving is usually done?`, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the options provided to indicate the type of receiving performed.
  • **Physical**: Receiving is performed physically, i.e., in-person or on-site.
  • **Virtual**: Receiving is performed virtually, i.e., online or remotely.
  • **Other (please describe)**: Provide a detailed description of any other type of receiving performed.
- **Additional comments** (`Additional comments`, text, optional): This is an opportunity to provide any additional insights or comments on receiving management practices within your department or section.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have reviewed each question carefully before proceeding.

- Your input will help in streamlining the receiving process and improving overall efficiency within your department or section.
