# painting_commission_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The painting commission form is designed for artists to provide detailed information about their commissioned works. It helps to efficiently collect relevant data for the production process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your artist name in the `artist_name` field.
2. Upload or enter a reference image of your artwork in the `artist_image` field.
3. Provide a title for your artwork in the `artwork_title` field.
4. Describe your artwork in the `artwork_description` field.
5. Specify the medium (material or type) of your artwork in the `artwork_medium` field.
6. Select the type of commission (e.g., "commission type") in the `commission_type` field.
7. Enter your desired price per piece in the `price_per_piece` field.
8. Specify the quantity of art pieces needed in the `quantity` field.
9. Select your preferred payment method (e.g., "bank transfer") in the `payment_method` field.
10. Enter your preferred payment amount in the `payment_amount` field.
11. Optionally, include any additional payment notes in the `payment_notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_header**: This is a note field for any additional information about the form.
* **artist_name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter your name as an artist.
* **artist_image** (`artist_image`, text, required): Upload or enter a reference image of your artwork.
* **artwork_title** (`artwork_title`, text, required): Provide a title for your artwork.
* **artwork_description** (`artwork_description`, text, required): Describe your artwork.
* **artwork_medium** (`artwork_medium`, text, required): Specify the medium (material or type) of your artwork.
* **commission_type** (`commission_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of commission (e.g., "commission type").
* **price_per_piece** (`price_per_piece`, number, required): Enter your desired price per piece.
* **quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Specify the quantity of art pieces needed.
* **payment_method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method (e.g., "bank transfer").
* **payment_amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter your preferred payment amount.
* **payment_notes** (`payment_notes`, text, required): Optionally, include any additional payment notes.

Note: This form template is a general guide and can be adapted according to specific needs. Review the form carefully to ensure all necessary fields are filled in correctly.
