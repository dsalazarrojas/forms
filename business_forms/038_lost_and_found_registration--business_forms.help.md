# Lost And Found Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lost And Found Registration form is a tool used to record and track lost items within the business or organization. It helps in identifying the item, verifying its ownership, and facilitating its recovery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `Lost And Found Registration` field, describing the lost item in detail.
2. In the `Lost And Found Form Fields` field, provide any additional information about the lost item, such as its category, description, and any other relevant details.
3. Select the `Submit` option to send the form to the business or organization for processing.
4. If you wish to cancel the registration, select the `Cancel` option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lost And Found Registration** (Lost And Found Registration, text, required): Describe the lost item in detail, including its category, description, and any other relevant details.
* **Lost And Found Form Fields** (Lost And Found Form Fields, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the lost item.
* **Submit** (Submit, select_one, required): Select this option to send the form to the business or organization for processing.
* **Cancel** (Cancel, select_one, required): Select this option to cancel the registration.

## Tips
- Double-check the information provided to ensure it is accurate and complete.
- If you are not sure about any information, contact the business or organization for assistance.
- Be sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
