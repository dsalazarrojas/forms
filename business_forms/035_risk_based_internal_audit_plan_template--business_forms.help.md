# Risk Based Internal Audit Plan Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to assist in creating a comprehensive risk-based internal audit plan that focuses on the most critical risks to the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, start by selecting the relevant options for each field. 

- Ensure that the audit cycle is correctly identified.
- Indicate whether the audit cycle is active or inactive.
- Specify the coverage scope of the audit.
- Identify the reportable risks that need to be assessed.
- Determine the audit objectives.
- Indicate whether the risk-based audit plan is in place.
- Specify the audit process used.
- Determine the risk level (High, Low).
- Identify the risk factors.
- Assess the risk level (Yes, No).
- Summarize the risks.
- Provide a description of the risk level.
- Specify the risk level frequency.
- Determine the risk level priority.
- Specify the risk level impact.
- Specify the risk level likelihood.
- Indicate the audit plan status (Active, Inactive).
- Indicate whether the audit plan has been revised.
- Specify the audit cycle status (Active, Inactive).
- Indicate the completion status (Active, Inactive).
- Identify the person assigned to.
- Specify the due date.
- Specify the completion date.
- Indicate the completion status (Active, Inactive).
- Indicate the approval status (Active, Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audit Cycle** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the current audit cycle number.
* **Audit Cycle** (`2`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the audit cycle.
* **Coverage Scope** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required): Check Yes or No to confirm the coverage scope.
* **Risks Reportable Risks** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the reportable risks to be assessed.
* **Audit Objectives** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required): Determine the audit objectives.
* **Risk-Based Audit Plan** (`6`, `text`, required): Specify the risk-based audit plan in place.
* **Audit Process** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Select the audit process used.
* **Risk Level** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Determine the risk level (High, Low).
* **Risk Factors** (`9`, `text`, required): Identify the risk factors.
* **Risk Assessment** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Assess the risk level (Yes, No).
* **Risk Summary** (`11`, `text`, required): Summarize the risks.
* **Risk Level Description** (`12`, `text`, required): Provide a description of the risk level.
* **Risk Level Frequency** (`13`, `text`, required): Specify the risk level frequency.
* **Risk Level Priority** (`14`, `number`, required): Determine the risk level priority.
* **Risk Level Impact** (`15`, `text`, required): Specify the risk level impact.
* **Risk Level Likelihood** (`16`, `text`, required): Specify the risk level likelihood.
* **Audit Plan Status** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the audit plan status (Active, Inactive).
* **Audit Plan Revision** (`18`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether the audit plan has been revised.
* **Audit Cycle Status** (`19`, `text`, required): Specify the audit cycle status.
* **Audit Cycle Completion Status** (`20`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the completion status (Active, Inactive).
* **Assigned To** (`21`, `text`, required): Identify the person assigned to.
* **Due Date** (`22`, `date`, required): Specify the due date.
* **Completion Date** (`23`, `date`, required): Specify the completion date.
* **Completion Status** (`24`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the completion status (Active, Inactive).
* **Approval Status** (`25`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the approval status (Active, Inactive).
