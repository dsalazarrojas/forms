# E Commerce Blockchain Pilot Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The E Commerce Blockchain Pilot Application Form is designed to collect necessary information from businesses and organizations interested in utilizing blockchain technology for e-commerce purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and relevance.
2. Enter your business name, website, and contact information in the respective fields.
3. Select the correct category (e-commerce, finance, technology) that best describes your business.
4. Provide a detailed description of your project or proposed use of blockchain technology.
5. Fill out the submission date and time fields with the dates and times of your submission.
6. Identify the innovation team and program manager associated with your application.
7. Enter your business contact and innovation contact information.
8. Select the type of blockchain technology you are interested in (public, private, consortium).
9. Choose the method of blockchain deployment (cloud-based, on-premises, hybrid).
10. Select the submission status (approved, rejected, pending).
11. Add any additional notes or comments relevant to your submission.
12. Enter the submitter and submission date information.
13. If applicable, fill out the reviewer and review date fields.
14. Choose the review status (passed, failed, not reviewed).
15. Enter any review comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Business Name**: Enter your business name as it appears on your website or marketing materials.
- **Category**: Select the category that best describes your business (e-commerce, finance, technology).
- **Website**: Enter your business website URL.
- **Address**: Enter your business address.
- **Email**: Enter your business email address.
- **Phone**: Enter your business phone number.
- **Description**: Provide a detailed description of your project or proposed use of blockchain technology.
- **Submission Date**: Enter the date of your submission.
- **Submission Time**: Enter the time of your submission.
- **Innovation Team**: Enter the name and title of the team member who submitted the application.
- **Program Manager**: Enter the name and title of the program manager associated with your application.
- **Business Contact**: Enter the name and title of your business contact.
- **Innovation Contact**: Enter the name and title of your innovation contact.
- **Blockchain Technology**: Select the type of blockchain technology you are interested in (public, private, consortium).
- **Blockchain Deployment**: Choose the method of blockchain deployment (cloud-based, on-premises, hybrid).
- **Submission Status**: Select the current status of your submission (approved, rejected, pending).
- **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes relevant to your submission.
- **Submitter**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the application.
- **Submission Date**: Enter the date of your submission.
- **Reviewer**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed your submission.
- **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
- **Review Status**: Select the status of the review (passed, failed, not reviewed).
- **Review Comments**: Enter any review comments or notes.
- **Submitter**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the application.
- **Submission Date**: Enter the date of your submission.
