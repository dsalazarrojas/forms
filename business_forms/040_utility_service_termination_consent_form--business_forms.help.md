# Utility Service Termination Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Utility Service Termination Consent Form is used to capture the customer's consent for terminating a utility service such as Electricity, Gas, Water, or Other. This form is essential for the utility company to record the customer's agreement to end the service and the terms of the termination.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Utility Service Termination Consent Form** (`utility_service_termination_consent_form`): Enter a brief statement indicating your consent for terminating the utility service.
2. **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further details.
4. **Account Number** (`account_number`): Provide your account number associated with the utility service you are terminating.
5. **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`): If you are terminating the service, please provide a brief reason. This will help us understand your situation.
6. **Utility Service Type** (`utility_service_type`): Select the type of utility service being terminated (e.g., Electricity, Gas, Water, or Other).
7. **Termination Method** (`termination_method`): Choose how you would like to receive your termination notice (Mail, Email, In-person, or Phone).
8. **Termination Effective Date** (`termination_effective_date`): Enter the date when you want the termination to take effect.
9. **Termination Effective Time** (`termination_effective_time`): Enter the time when you want the termination to take effect.
10. **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`): If you have a specific reason for terminating the service, please provide a brief explanation.
11. **Utility Company Name** (`utility_company_name`): If you are terminating service from another company, please provide the name of that company.
12. **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`): Sign this form to confirm your consent for terminating the utility service.
13. **Customer Date** (`customer_date`): Enter the date of your signature.
14. **Customer Time** (`customer_time`): Enter the time of your signature.

**Tips**

* Please fill out the form accurately to ensure smooth termination of your utility service.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Your termination will only take effect once we receive this completed form.
