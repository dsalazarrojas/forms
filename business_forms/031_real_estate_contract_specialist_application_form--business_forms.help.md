# Real Estate Contract Specialist Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for individuals to apply for the position of a real estate contract specialist. To provide an effective and efficient application process, please carefully complete each section as described below.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill out your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Email, Contact Number) in the corresponding fields.
- Select your desired Contract Type and Property Type.
- Enter your desired contract start and available end dates.
- In the Job Preferences section, write any additional information you would like to share about your ideal work situation or what you're looking for in a position.
- Enter the location for the contract in the Contract Location field.
- Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to be known professionally.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to be known professionally.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your professional email address where you can be contacted by our team.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of your current or most recent position.
* **Years Experience** (`Years Experience`, number, required: false): Enter the number of years you have been working in the industry as a real estate contract specialist.
* **Contract Type** (`Contract Type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of contract you are looking to work on (Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
* **Property Type** (`Property Type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the types of properties you are most interested in or experienced with (Single Family, Multi Family, Townhome, Condo).
* **Desired Contract Start Date** (`Desired Contract Start Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you would like your contract to begin.
* **Available Contract End Date** (`Available Contract End Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you would like your contract to end.
* **Job Preferences** (`Job Preferences`, note, required: false): Write any additional information you would like to share about your ideal work situation or what you're looking for in a position.
* **Contract Location** (`Contract Location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where you would like to work on the contract.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Ensure you enter your desired dates in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
- Be as specific as possible with your job preferences to help us better understand your needs.
