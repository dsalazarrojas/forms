# General Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is a general contact form for customers or business partners to send us their questions, comments, or requests. Fill out this form to reach out to us, and we'll do our best to respond promptly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the required fields (none are currently marked as required, but be sure to provide a first and last name, and a valid email address).
3. Enter your contact information in the respective fields:
	* `first_name`: Your first name
	* `last_name`: Your last name
	* `email`: Your email address
	* `phone`: Your phone number (optional)
	* `company`: The name of your company or organization (optional)
	* `job_title`: Your job title or position (optional)
	* `department`: The department you're associated with (optional)
	* `job_function`: A description of your job function (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter a valid email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact options.
* **Company** (`company`, text, Optional): If you're contacting us on behalf of a company, enter the name here.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, Optional): Enter your job title or position for context.
* **Department** (`department`, text, Optional): Enter the department you're associated with.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, Optional): Briefly describe your job function or role.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, but you're free to fill out as many or as few fields as you'd like.
* We'll do our best to get back to you promptly with your inquiry or request.
