# Financial Statement Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to financial documents, allowing users to submit their requests with relevant information such as access level, contact details, and reason for request. It is intended for internal use, and the information collected will be reviewed and evaluated to grant access accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Request Access" page.
2. Fill in the necessary information:
	* Select the financial documents you would like to access.
	* Choose the access level (High, Low, or Medium).
	* Provide a reason for your request (optional).
	* Enter your email address and phone number.
	* Add any additional information relevant to your request.
3. Review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Access** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your request for access to financial documents.
* **Financial Documents** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the financial documents you would like to access.
* **Access Level** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the level of access you require (High, Low, or Medium).
* **Request Reason** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Provide a reason for your request (optional).
* **Contact Information** (`5`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`6`, `text`, required: true): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Additional Information** (`7`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional information relevant to your request.
* **Acceptance** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you accept the access granted or not (Accept, Decline, or Pending).
* **Request Date** (`9`, `date`, required: true): Enter the date you are making the request.
* **Access Granted Date** (`10`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when access was granted (not applicable if declined).
* **Access Level Hint** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Additional hint for access level.
* **Request Reason Hint** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Additional hint for request reason.
* **Contact Information Hint** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Additional hint for contact information.
* **Phone Number Hint** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Additional hint for phone number.
* **Additional Information Hint** (`15`, `text`, required: false): Additional hint for additional information.
* **Acceptance Hint** (`16`, `text`, required: false): Additional hint for acceptance.
* **Request Date Hint** (`17`, `text`, required: false): Additional hint for request date.
* **Access Granted Date Hint** (`18`, `text`, required: false): Additional hint for access granted date.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your request is complete.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities.
