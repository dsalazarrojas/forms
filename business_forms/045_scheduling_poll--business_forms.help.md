> # Scheduling Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scheduling Poll form is used to collect information for event planning and coordination. It helps in creating a poll that suits your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **What is the purpose of this poll?** (`poll_question`, text, optional)
   Enter a brief description of the purpose of this poll.
2. **Start time** (`start_time`, time, optional)
   Enter the start time of the poll in 24-hour format (e.g., 10:00 PM).
3. **End time** (`end_time`, time, optional)
   Enter the end time of the poll in 24-hour format (e.g., 11:00 PM).
4. **Select options** (`poll_options`, select_multiple, optional)
   Choose multiple options for the poll. These will be the questions or topics to be voted on.
5. **Attendee emails** (`attendee_emails`, text, optional)
   Enter the email addresses of people to be included in the poll (separated by commas).
6. **Poll issuer** (`poll_issuer`, text, optional)
   Enter a name or title to represent the poll issuer.
7. **Poll issuer email** (`poll_issuer_email`, email, optional)
   Enter the email address of the poll issuer.
8. **Poll issuer phone** (`poll_issuer_phone`, text, optional)
   Enter the phone number of the poll issuer.
9. **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional)
   Add any additional notes or comments about the poll.
10. **Poll schedule** (`poll_schedule`, select_one, optional)
   Choose a pre-defined schedule for the poll.
11. **Poll schedule time** (`poll_schedule_time`, time, optional)
   Enter the time of the poll schedule.
12. **Poll schedule date** (`poll_schedule_date`, date, optional)
   Enter the date of the poll schedule.
13. **Poll issuer time zone** (`poll_issuer_timezone`, text, optional)
   Enter the time zone of the poll issuer.
14. **Poll issuer location** (`poll_issuer_location`, text, optional)
   Enter the location of the poll issuer.
15. **Poll issuer address** (`poll_issuer_address`, text, optional)
   Enter the address of the poll issuer.

- **What is the purpose of this poll?**: A brief description of the poll's purpose.

- **Poll issuer**: Enter a name or title to represent the poll issuer.
- **Poll issuer email**: Enter the email address of the poll issuer.
- **Poll issuer phone**: Enter the phone number of the poll issuer.
- **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the poll.
- **Poll schedule**: Choose a pre-defined schedule for the poll.
- **Poll schedule time**: The time of the poll schedule.
- **Poll schedule date**: The date of the poll schedule.
- **Poll issuer time zone**: The time zone of the poll issuer.
- **Poll issuer location**: Enter the location of the poll issuer.
