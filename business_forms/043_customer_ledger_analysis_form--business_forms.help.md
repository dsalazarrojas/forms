# Customer Ledger Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Ledger Analysis Form is used to collect information about a customer's outstanding balance and transaction categories. This form helps to track and analyze customer transactions and identify any discrepancies. By providing accurate and detailed information, you can use this form to perform a thorough analysis of a customer's ledger.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please ensure you have all necessary information ready** before starting the form. This will save time and reduce errors.
2. **Fill out each field carefully** as it is required/optional (see below).
3. **Select all applicable categories** from the Transaction Categories field.
4. **Enter any discrepancies** you have found in the Discrepancies field.
5. **Double-check your answers** before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**: Enter the customer's name and any other relevant details you need to identify them.
* **Ledger Period**: Choose the date range for which you want to analyze the customer's transactions.
* **Outstanding Balance**: Enter the customer's current outstanding balance. This will be a numerical value.
* **Transaction Categories**: Select all applicable categories for the customer's transactions from the list (Category A, Category B, Category C).
* **Discrepancies**: Enter any discrepancies you have found during the analysis, such as errors or irregularities in the customer's ledger.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and update the form regularly to ensure accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
* This form is meant to be used for internal purposes only, so please do not share it with anyone outside the organization.
