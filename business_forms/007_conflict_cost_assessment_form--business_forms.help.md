# Conflict Cost Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the cost of conflicts in the workplace. It helps in identifying the financial impact of conflicts on a business and provides a recommended investment in resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Select the correct type of conflict from the options provided.
3. Enter dates in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
4. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this cost assessment.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person conducting this assessment.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department where the conflict occurred.
* **Type of Conflict** (`conflict_type`, select_one, required): Choose the primary category of the conflict from the options provided.
* **Conflict Description** (`conflict_description`, text, required): Briefly describe the conflict situation.
* **Duration of Conflict** (`conflict_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the duration of the conflict from the options provided.
* **Number of Employees Involved** (`employees_involved`, number, required): Enter the total number of people directly involved.
* **Management Time Spent** (`management_time_spent`, number, required): Enter the hours spent by managers addressing this issue.
* **Average Management Hourly Rate** (`management_hourly_rate`, number, optional): Enter the average hourly cost of management time.
* **HR Time Spent** (`hr_time_spent`, number, required): Enter the hours spent by HR personnel.
* **Average HR Hourly Rate** (`hr_hourly_rate`, number, optional): Enter the average hourly cost of HR time.
* **Estimated Productivity Loss** (`productivity_loss_percentage`, select_one, required): Choose the percentage reduction in team productivity.
* **Increase in Absenteeism** (`absenteeism_increase`, select_one, required): Choose the likelihood of increased absenteeism.
* **Risk of Employee Turnover** (`turnover_risk`, select_one, required): Choose the likelihood of employee turnover.
* **Number of Employees Considering Leaving** (`employees_considering_leaving`, number, optional): Enter the number of employees expressing intent to leave.
* **Estimated Recruitment Replacement Cost** (`recruitment_cost_estimate`, number, optional): Enter the estimated cost to replace employees if they leave.
* **Legal or External Advisor Costs** (`legal_costs`, number, optional): Enter any costs for legal counsel or mediators.
* **Additional Training Costs Incurred** (`training_costs`, number, optional): Enter any costs for conflict resolution training.
* **Impact on Customer Relations** (`customer_impact`, select_one, required): Choose the level of impact on customer relations.
* **Number of Customer Complaints** (`customer_complaints`, number, optional): Enter the number of customer complaints related to this conflict.
* **Project Delays Attributed to Conflict** (`project_delays`, number, optional): Enter the number of projects delayed due to this conflict.
* **Estimated Cost of Delays** (`delay_cost_estimate`, number, optional): Enter the financial impact of project delays.
* **Impact on Team Morale** (`morale_impact`, select_one, required): Choose the level of negative impact on team morale.
* **Total Estimated Cost of Conflict** (`total_estimated_cost`, number, optional): Enter the sum of all costs associated with this conflict.
* **Recommended Investment in Resolution** (`roi_resolution_investment`, number, optional): Enter the suggested budget for resolving this conflict.
* **Overall Cost Assessment Summary** (`assessment_summary`, text, required): Enter a brief summary of the findings.
