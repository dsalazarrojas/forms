# lash_procedure_consent_intake - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Procedure Consent Intake form is used to collect personal and medical information from patients to ensure their safety and comfort during the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
2. If you have difficulty filling out any section, please ask for assistance.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please let the medical team know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your first name, such as John.
* **Last Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your last name, such as Smith.
* **Email (Text, Required)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Text, Required)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Birth Date (Date, Optional)**: Enter the date of your birth in YYYY-MM-DD format, or leave blank if not applicable.
* **Age (Number, Optional)**: Enter your age.
* **Address (Text, Optional)**: Enter your current address.
* **Gender (Text, Optional)**: Enter your gender.
* **Marital Status (Select One, Optional)**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Married
	+ Single
	+ Divorced
* **Medical Conditions (Text, Optional)**: Enter any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes.
* **Emergency Contact (Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Text, Optional)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Insurance (Text, Optional)**: Enter your insurance information.
* **Insurance ID (Text, Optional)**: Enter your insurance ID number.
* **Medical History (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select any of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (Text, Optional)**: Enter the relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Insurance Provider (Text, Optional)**: Enter your insurance provider's name.
* **Medical History Date (Date, Optional)**: Enter the date of your medical history.
* **Medical History Description (Text, Optional)**: Enter a description of your medical history.
* **Medical History Provider (Text, Optional)**: Enter your medical history provider's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2 (Text, Optional)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact 2.
* **Insurance Start Date (Date, Optional)**: Enter the start date of your insurance.
* **Insurance End Date (Date, Optional)**: Enter the end date of your insurance.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2 (Text, Optional)**: Enter the relationship with your emergency contact 2.
* **Medical History Provider 2 (Text, Optional)**: Enter your medical history provider 2's name.
