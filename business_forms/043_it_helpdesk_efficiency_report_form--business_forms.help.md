# it-helpdesk-efficiency-report-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "it-helpdesk-efficiency-report-form" is used to collect and manage data related to IT helpdesk support issues. This form helps in gathering information on incident handling and resolution, including categorization, priority, due dates, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you understand its purpose.
2. Fill in the relevant fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Save your progress as you go along, as you can't go back and change answers once you submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Response Times** (`1`, `time`, required: false): This field is used to measure the time taken to respond to the issue. Enter the duration in the format "HH:MM:SS".
- **Resolution Rates** (`2`, `number`, required: false): This field indicates the percentage of issues resolved within a certain timeframe.
- **User Satisfaction** (`3`, `number`, required: false): Enter a number indicating the level of user satisfaction (e.g., 1-5 or 0-10).
- **Response Channel** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how the issue was responded to (e.g., email, phone, chat, etc.).
- **Issue Type** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all applicable issue types (e.g., hardware, software, network, etc.).
- **Resolution Date** (`6`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the issue was resolved.
- **Resolved By** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose who resolved the issue.
- **Notes** (`8`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments about the issue.
- **Email** (`9`, `email`, required: false): Enter the contact email for the user.
- **Phone** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact phone number for the user.
- **Category** (`11`, `select_one`, required: false): Categorize the issue under a specific category (e.g., hardware, software, etc.).
- **Subcategory** (`12`, `select_one`, required: false): Further categorize the issue under a subcategory.
- **Priority** (`13`, `select_one`, required: false): Set the priority level of the issue.
- **Assigned Group** (`14`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the group or team assigned to handle the issue.
- **Due Date** (`15`, `date`, required: false): Set a due date for the issue to be resolved.
- **Assigned User** (`16`, `select_one`, required: false): Assign the issue to a specific user.
- **Comments** (`17`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments about the issue.
- **Attachments** (`18`, `text`, required: false): Attach any relevant files or documents.
- **Custom Field 1** (`19`, `text`, required: false): Enter any custom data for this field (e.g., tracking number, etc.).
