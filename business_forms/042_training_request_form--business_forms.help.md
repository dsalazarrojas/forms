# Training Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Training Request Form is designed to gather information from employees about their training needs and preferences. This form helps us understand what training opportunities our employees are interested in, allowing us to tailor our training programs to better meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Request Form** (`training_request_form`, text, optional): This field is a free-text area where you can describe your training request. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your needs.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter your official company email address associated with your personnel record.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the "Training Request Form" field to help us understand your requirements.
- Use your official company email address in the "Employee Email" field.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
