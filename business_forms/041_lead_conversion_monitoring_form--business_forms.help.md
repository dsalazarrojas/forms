# Lead Conversion Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to track and monitor lead conversions in the sales process, ensuring that leads are properly converted and escalated to the next stage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Lead Source**: Select the source of the lead, e.g., Website, Social Media, Referral, or Other.
2. **Lead Status**: Choose the current status of the lead, e.g., Qualified, Disqualified, or Pending.
3. **Lead Stage**: Select the current stage of the lead, e.g., Lead, Opportunity, or Customer.
4. **Contact Date**: Enter the date of the last contact with the lead (if applicable).
5. **Next Step**: Provide a brief description of the next step or action to be taken with the lead.
6. **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date for the next follow-up (if applicable).
7. **Lead Owner**: Select the person responsible for the lead, e.g., John Doe, Jane Doe, or Other.
8. **Sales Person**: Select the sales representative assigned to the lead, e.g., John, Jane, or Other.
9. **Lead Source Type**: Choose the type of lead source, e.g., New, Referral, or Other.
10. **Lead Type**: Select the type of lead, e.g., Lead, Opp, or Other.
11. **Lead Category**: Select the category of the lead, e.g., A, B, or C.
12. **Lead Subcategory**: Select the subcategory of the lead, e.g., X, Y, or Z.
13. **Lead Priority**: Choose the priority level of the lead, e.g., High, Medium, or Low.
14. **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the lead.
15. **Created By**: Select the person who created the lead, e.g., Jim, Jane, or Other.
16. **Updated By**: Select the person who last updated the lead, e.g., Jim, Jane, or Other.
17. **Created Date**: Enter the date the lead was created.
18. **Updated Date**: Enter the date the lead was last updated.
