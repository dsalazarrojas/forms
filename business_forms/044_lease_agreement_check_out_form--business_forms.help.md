# Lease Agreement Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lease Agreement Check Out Form is designed to help property owners and managers document the condition of a property at the start and end of a tenancy. This form is not a substitute for professional property inspections or appraisals, but rather a supplementary tool to support communication between parties.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Gather necessary information about the tenant and property.
2.  Complete each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3.  Use the Condition Check List to document any damages or issues with the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Tenant Details (Page 1)

*   **Tenant Details (`tenant_details`, text, required: false**): Enter the tenant's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

### Property Details (Page 1)

*   **Property Details (`property_details`, text, required: false**): Enter the address, unit number, or other identifying information about the property.

### Move Out Date (Page 1)

*   **Move Out Date (`move_out_date`, date, required: false**): Enter the date the tenant plans to move out of the property.

### Condition Check List (Page 1)

*   **Condition Check List (`condition_check_list`, select multiple, required: false**): Use this list to document any damages or issues with the property. Select all applicable options from the list.

### Description (Page 1)

*   **Description (`condition_description`, note, required: false**): Add any additional comments or descriptions about the property's condition.

## Tips

*   Be thorough and accurate when documenting the property's condition.
*   Use photos or videos to support your descriptions if possible.
*   Keep the form for your records and refer to it when communicating with the tenant.
*   Consider having the tenant sign the form to acknowledge their agreement with the documented condition.
*   Review and update the form regularly to ensure it reflects changes in the property's condition over time.
