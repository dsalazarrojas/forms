# Gardening Supply Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for gardening supply subscriptions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Choose the type of subscription you want to create (e.g., Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
2. Select the subscription plan (e.g., Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter).
3. Choose the frequency of the subscription (e.g., Once, Every 2 weeks, Every 4 weeks).
4. Enter the start and end dates for the subscription.
5. Select the payment method (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal).
6. Choose the payment cycle (e.g., Every 2 weeks, Every 4 weeks).
7. Enter any additional payment details, if required.
8. Enter the customer's name, email, phone number, and address.
9. Enter the customer's city, state, zip, and country.
10. Enter any notes or comments for the subscription.
11. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **delivery_details**: Enter the delivery address and any specific delivery instructions.
* **subscription_type**: Select the type of subscription you want to create (e.g., Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
* **subscription_plan**: Choose the subscription plan (e.g., Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter).
* **frequency**: Select the frequency of the subscription (e.g., Once, Every 2 weeks, Every 4 weeks).
* **start_date**: Enter the start date of the subscription in date format (e.g., 2024-02-15).
* **start_time**: Enter the start time of the subscription in 24-hour time format (e.g., 14:00).
* **end_date**: Enter the end date of the subscription in date format (e.g., 2024-03-15).
* **end_time**: Enter the end time of the subscription in 24-hour time format (e.g., 16:00).
* **subscription_status**: Select the status of the subscription (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **subscription_note**: Enter any additional notes for the subscription.
* **email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **address**: Enter the customer's address.
* **city**: Enter the customer's city.
* **state**: Enter the customer's state.
* **zip**: Enter the customer's zip code.
* **country**: Enter the customer's country.
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments for the subscription.
* **payment_method**: Select the payment method (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal).
* **payment_cycle**: Choose the payment cycle (e.g., Every 2 weeks, Every 4 weeks).
* **payment_method_details**: Enter any additional payment details.
* **customer_name**: Enter the customer's name.
* **customer_email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **customer_phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **customer_note**: Enter any additional notes or comments for the customer.
