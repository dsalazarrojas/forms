# Auction Trading Post Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from consignors for an auction trading post. It is used to gather details about the consignor and their agreement terms with the trading post.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the Consignor Full Name field with your full name.
2.  If you have a business name, enter it in the Business Name field. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
3.  Enter your email address in the Email Address field.
4.  Provide your phone number in the Phone Number field.
5.  Select the facility where items are located from the Trading Post Location dropdown menu.
6.  Enter a high-level summary of the items for sale in the Summary of Items for Sale field.
7.  Enter the agreed sales commission rate as a percentage in the Agreed Sales Commission (%) field.
8.  Choose your payout terms from the Payout Terms dropdown menu.
9.  Select "I Agree" to the liability waiver terms in the Liability Waiver field.
10.  Enter the contract expiration date in the Contract Expiration Date field.
11.  Fill in the Date of Signature field with the current date.
12.  Enter your digital signature in the Digital Signature (Type Name) field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <u>Consignor Information</u>

*   **Consignor Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name.
*   **Business Name (if applicable)** (text, optional): If you have a business name, enter it here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
*   **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone Number** (text, required): Provide your phone number.

### <u>Contract Details</u>

*   **Trading Post Location** (select_one, required): Select the facility where items are located.
*   **Summary of Items for Sale** (text, required): Provide a high-level list of the items you are selling.
*   **Agreed Sales Commission (%)** (number, required): Enter the commission rate as a percentage.
*   **Payout Terms** (select_one, required): Choose your payout terms.
*   **I agree to the liability waiver terms** (select_one, required): Select "I Agree" to the terms.
*   **Contract Expiration Date** (date, required): Enter the date when items must be retrieved if unsold.
*   **Date of Signature** (date, required): Fill in the current date.
*   **Digital Signature (Type Name)** (text, required): Enter your digital signature name.

## Tips
*   Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to the trading post.
