# Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering events. It helps us gather important information about the event and its registrants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Event Registration Form Data" page.
2. Enter the name of the event in the "Event Name" field.
3. Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
4. Enter the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
5. Enter the name of the event organizer in the "Event Organizer" field.
6. Enter the location of the event in the "Event Location" field.
7. Check the registration status of the registrant(s) in the "Registrants" field.
8. Enter the name of the registrant(s) in the "Registrant Name" field(s).
9. Enter the email of the registrant(s) in the "Registrant Email" field(s).
10. Enter the phone number of the registrant(s) in the "Registrant Phone" field(s).
11. Add any comments about the registrant(s) in the "Comments" field.
12. Select the status of the form submission in the "Event Registration Form Submitted", "Event Registration Form Declined", or "Event Registration Form Pending" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Registration Form Data** (`event_registration_form_data`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any additional comments or notes about the event.
- **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
- **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event organizer.
- **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
- **Registrants** (`registrants`, select multiple, optional): Check the registration status of the registrant(s).
- **Registrant Name** (`registrant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the registrant(s).
- **Registrant Email** (`registrant_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the registrant(s).
- **Registrant Phone** (`registrant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the registrant(s).
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments about the registrant(s).
- **Event Registration Form Submitted** (`event_registration_form_submitted`, select one, optional): Select the status of the form submission.
- **Event Registration Form Declined** (`event_registration_form_declined`, select multiple, optional): Select the status of the form submission.
- **Event Registration Form Pending** (`event_registration_form_pending`, select one, optional): Select the status of the form submission.
