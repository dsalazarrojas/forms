# Adhesion Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adhesion Agreement Form is a standardized template designed to formalize the terms and conditions of our services. Please review and complete this form to ensure you understand the agreements and expectations before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Read and understand the Standard Terms and Conditions (Section 7).
2. Choose your selected service plan (Section 5).
3. Provide your client/customer name (Section 2).
4. Enter your business entity information (if applicable, Section 3).
5. Select a proposed commencement date (Section 6).
6. Confirm your acceptance of the Standard Terms and Conditions (Section 8).
7. Agree to the Data Processing and Privacy Addendum (Section 9).
8. Confirm your understanding of the 7-day cooling-off period and cancellation policy (Section 10).
9. Enter your official contact email (Section 11).
10. Provide your full billing address (Section 12).
11. Enter any applicable tax identification number (Section 13).
12. Confirm your authority to enter into this agreement (Section 14).
13. Select a date for the agreement (Section 15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client / Customer Name (2)**: Enter the name of the client or customer. This is a required field.
* **Company / Organization (if applicable)**: If you are representing a business, enter its name here. This field is not required but can help us understand your entity.
* **Selected Service Plan (5)**: Choose the service plan you wish to use for your project. This field is required and will help us provide accurate pricing and terms.
* **Proposed Commencement Date (6)**: Enter the date you propose for the project's start. This field is required.
* **Summary of Key Terms (7)**: Read the summary of our standard terms and conditions, including payment terms, service quality, liability, and dispute resolution. This field is not required but is essential for understanding the agreement.
* **I have read and I accept the Standard Terms and Conditions (8)**: Confirm you understand and accept the terms and conditions. This field is required.
* **I agree to the Data Processing and Privacy Addendum (9)**: Confirm you agree to our data processing and privacy policies. This field is required.
* **I understand the 7-day initial cooling-off period and cancellation policy (10)**: Ensure you understand our cancellation policy. This field is required.
* **Official Contact Email for Notifications (11)**: Enter your email address for important notifications and updates. This field is required.
* **Full Billing Address (12)**: Provide your full billing address for accurate invoicing and payment processing. This field is required.
* **Tax ID / VAT Number (13)**: If your business has a tax ID or VAT number, enter it here. This field is not required but can help us with tax-related issues.
* **I certify that I have the legal authority to enter into this agreement (14)**: Confirm you have the authority to sign this agreement on behalf of your business or entity. This field is required.
* **Date of Agreement (15)**: Select the date you sign this agreement. This field is required.
