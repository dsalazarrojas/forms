# Induction Period Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Induction Period Review Form is used to review the induction period of new employees. This form helps evaluate their employment and performance, ensuring accurate records and preventing duplication of effort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields with the required information about the new employee.
2. Ensure that the date of joining and probation period start date are accurate.
3. Select the evaluation type from the options provided.
4. Add any feedback on the new employee.
5. Select any action items that are relevant to the new employee.
6. Add any comments about the new employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **new_employees_name** (Text): Enter the name of the new employee.
* **reporting_manager_name** (Text): Enter the name of the reporting manager.
* **reporting_manager_email** (Email): Enter the email address of the reporting manager.
* **date_of_joining** (Date): Enter the date the new employee joined.
* **probation_period_start_date** (Date): Enter the start date of the probation period.
* **evaluation_type** (Select One): Choose the type of evaluation from the options provided (Option 1 or Option 2).
* **feedback_on_new_employee** (Text): Add any feedback about the new employee.
* **action_items** (Select Multiple): Select any action items that are relevant to the new employee (item1, item2, item3).
* **comments** (Text): Add any comments about the new employee.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form correctly and accurately.
- Double-check the dates and other fields to ensure they are correct.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant person or refer to the employee handbook.
