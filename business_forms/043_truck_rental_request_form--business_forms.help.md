# Truck Rental Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information required for a truck rental request. Please fill it out accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information in the "Contact" field.
2. Select the relevant request information in the "Request Information" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the desired rental location in the "Rental Location" multi-select menu.
4. Specify the rental dates in the "Rental Dates" field.
5. Enter the desired rental duration in the "Rental Duration" field.
6. Select the type of vehicle you need in the "Vehicle Type" dropdown menu.
7. Provide additional information about the vehicle in the "Vehicle Details" field.
8. Enter any additional details in the "Additional Info" field.
9. Add any notes in the "Notes" field for the reviewer.
10. Enter your email address in the "E-mail" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact** (1): Enter your name and contact details.
* **Request Information** (2): Select the type of information or services you require for the rental.
* **Rental Location** (3): Choose the location where you need the rental vehicle.
* **Rental Dates** (4): Enter the dates for which you require the rental.
* **Rental Duration** (5): Enter the duration of the rental period.
* **Vehicle Type** (6): Select the type of vehicle you need for the rental.
* **Vehicle Details** (7): Provide additional details about the vehicle you need.
* **Additional Info** (8): Enter any additional information about your rental request.
* **Notes** (9): Add any notes for the reviewer.
* **Submit** (10): Click this button to submit your form.
* **Review** (11): Review your submitted form before submission.
* **E-mail** (12): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (13): Enter your phone number.

Tips:

* Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submission.
* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review your form before clicking "Submit" to avoid any errors.
