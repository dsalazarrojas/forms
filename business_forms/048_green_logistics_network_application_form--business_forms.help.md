# Green Logistics Network Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Green Logistics Network Application Form is used to collect information from businesses that wish to join the Green Logistics Network. This information will be used to create a company profile within the network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
*   Review each field carefully before proceeding.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards, licenses, or other official documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your company's email address.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your company's address.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person to contact for further information.
*   **Company Description** (`company_description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your company's purpose, products, or services.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your company's phone number.
*   **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of business you are applying to be:
	+ Option 1: Manufacturing
	+ Option 2: Service
	+ Option 3: Other
*   **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of your business.
*   **Year Established** (`year_established`, number, required: false): Enter the year your company was established.
*   **Sustainability Policies** (`sustainability_policies`, text, required: false): Describe your company's sustainability policies.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   If you need help or have questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.
*   If you need to save your progress, please use the form's built-in save feature.
