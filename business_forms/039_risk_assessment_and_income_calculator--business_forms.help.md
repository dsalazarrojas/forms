# Risk Assessment And Income Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess an individual's or business's income and expenses to identify potential risks. It provides a framework to calculate and evaluate financial data to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Income and expenses" section with accurate and up-to-date information about your income and expenses.
2. Select the relevant risk factors that apply to your situation.
3. Enter your annual income and expenses for the relevant fields.
4. Evaluate your risk level based on your income and expenses.
5. Add any additional review notes if necessary.
6. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Income and expenses** (`income_section_1`, text, optional): This section is where you should enter your current income and expenses information. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to get an accurate assessment.
* **Risk factors** (`risk_factors`, select multiple, optional): Select all the risk factors that apply to your situation. These factors will be used to evaluate your risk level.
* **Annual income** (`income_1`, number, optional): Enter your annual income for the relevant field. This information will be used to calculate your risk level.
* **Annual income** (`income_2`, number, optional): Enter your annual income for the relevant field. This information will be used to calculate your risk level.
* **Monthly expenses** (`expenses_1`, number, optional): Enter your monthly expenses for the relevant field. This information will be used to calculate your risk level.
* **Monthly expenses** (`expenses_2`, number, optional): Enter your monthly expenses for the relevant field. This information will be used to calculate your risk level.
* **Risk level** (`risk_factors_2`, select one, optional): Evaluate your risk level based on your income and expenses. This will help you identify potential risks.
* **Review date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your review. This is an optional field for tracking review dates.
* **Review time** (`review_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your review. This is an optional field for tracking review times.
* **Review note** (`review_note`, note, optional): Add any additional review notes if necessary. This is an optional field for recording notes.
* **Submitter name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the submitter of this form. This information is for internal use.
* **Submitter email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the submitter. This information is for internal use.
* **Submitter phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the submitter. This information is for internal use.
* **Submitter address** (`submitter_address`, text, optional): Enter your address as the submitter. This information is for internal use.

Note: All fields with "optional" status will only be used for internal evaluation and are not mandatory for the risk assessment calculation.
