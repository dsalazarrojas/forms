# it_system_outage_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for reporting and managing IT system outages. It helps track and resolve issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and answer each field based on the incident.
2. Ensure you have permission to fill out the form, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **incident_type** (Select One): Indicate whether the incident is 'Yes' or 'No'. This field helps categorize the incident type.
* **date** (Date): Enter the date of the incident in YYYY-MM-DD format. This field is optional.
* **time** (Time): Enter the time of the incident. This field is optional.
* **impact** (Select One): Select 'Yes' if the incident has impact on business processes, otherwise 'No'.
* **severity** (Select One): Select 'Yes' if the severity of the incident is high, otherwise 'No'.
* **description** (Text): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **incident_status** (Select One): Choose the current status of the incident as Active or Inactive.
* **incident_status_history** (Text): Enter any status history of the incident.
* **root_cause** (Text): Provide the root cause of the incident.
* **resolution** (Select One): Indicate whether the incident has been resolved, otherwise 'No'.
* **resolution_time** (Number): Enter the time taken to resolve the incident.
* **incident_manager** (Select One): Indicate whether an incident manager is involved, otherwise 'No'.
* **incident_manager_team** (Select One): Select whether the incident manager team is involved.
* **reporting_user** (Select One): Select whether the reporting user is involved.
* **reporting_user_department** (Select One): Select whether the reporting user's department is involved.
* **reporting_user_title** (Select One): Select whether the reporting user's title is involved.
* **outage_start_time** (Time): Enter the start time of the outage.
* **outage_end_time** (Time): Enter the end time of the outage.
* **total_duration** (Number): Enter the total duration of the outage.
* **business_affected** (Select One): Indicate whether business processes are affected, otherwise 'No'.
* **it_contacted** (Select One): Indicate whether IT was contacted, otherwise 'No'.
* **incident_comments** (Note): Enter any comments or notes about the incident.
* **business_impact** (Note): Enter any comments or notes about business impact.
* **root_cause_analysis** (Note): Enter any comments or notes about root cause analysis.
