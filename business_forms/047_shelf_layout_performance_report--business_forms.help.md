# Shelf Layout Performance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shelf Layout Performance Report is a tool used by store owners and managers to assess the overall performance of their store's shelves. This report helps store owners identify strengths and weaknesses in their shelf layout and make data-driven decisions to improve their store's organization and customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of your store in the **store_name** field. This is a required field, as it helps us identify your store's performance with its name.
2.  Choose a name for the section you are evaluating in the **section_name** field. This field is optional, but it can help you keep track of different sections of your store's shelves.
3.  Choose the **shelf_execution** rating for the evaluated section. This is a select-one field with five options: Excellent, Very Good, Fair, Poor, and Needs Attention. Choose the rating that best describes the condition of the section's shelf.
4.  Enter your numerical rating for this section in the **rating** field. This is a number field that allows you to provide a more detailed numerical rating for this section (e.g., 1-5).
5.  Add any additional comments about this section's shelves in the **comments** field.
6.  Upload photos of the section's shelves in the **photos** field. This is optional, but it can help us provide more detailed feedback on the layout.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **store_name**: The name of your store. Enter the name of your store, for example, 'ABC Supermarket' or 'Downtown Convenience'.
*   **section_name**: The name of the section you are evaluating. Enter a name for the section, like 'Canned Goods', 'Bakery', etc. This field is optional but helps us track different sections of your store.
*   **shelf_execution**: This field has five options: Excellent, Very Good, Fair, Poor, and Needs Attention. Choose the option that best describes the condition of this section's shelf.
*   **rating**: Enter a numerical rating for this section, on a scale of 1-5.
*   **comments**: Add any additional comments or observations about the section's shelves. This field is optional, but it can help us provide more detailed feedback.
*   **photos**: Upload photos of the section's shelves. This field is optional but can help us provide more detailed feedback on the layout.
