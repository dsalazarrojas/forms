# Miscalculation Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a refund for a miscalculation on an invoice or payment. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary information to initiate the refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are submitting a legitimate refund request. Please note that duplicate or incorrect submissions will be rejected.
2. Fill in the required information in the sections below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Account Number
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, optional): Please enter your account number if you have one. This will help us process your refund request more efficiently.
### Date of Incident
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the miscalculation occurred.
### Total Amount of Refund
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of the refund you are requesting.
### Reason for Refund
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Briefly describe the reason for the miscalculation.
### Additional Details
* **Details** (`details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that will help us understand the miscalculation and process your refund request.

## Tips
* Please make sure to attach any relevant supporting documents or proof to your submission, if required.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer support team for assistance.
* We will review and process your request as soon as possible.
