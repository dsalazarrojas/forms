# customer_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "customer_contact_form" form is used to collect contact information and details from customers or potential customers who have inquiries or requests regarding products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields (note that not all fields are required).
*   Fill in your company name if you are representing a company.
*   Enter your customer name.
*   Enter your email address.
*   Enter your phone number (if applicable).
*   Enter your physical address (if applicable).
*   Enter the name of the city where you are located.
*   Enter the name of the state where you are located.
*   Enter your zip code.
*   Choose the country you are located in.
*   Optionally, provide a message about your inquiry.
*   If you have been assigned a tool from the business, select it from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company** (text, Optional): Enter your company name, if you are representing a business or organization.
* **Customer Name** (text, Optional): Enter your name if you are the customer or contact person.
* **Email** (email, Optional): Enter your email address so that the company can reach you.
* **Phone Number** (text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Address** (text, Optional): Enter your physical address where you can be reached.
* **City** (text, Optional): Enter the city where you are located.
* **State** (text, Optional): Enter the state where you are located.
* **Zip Code** (number, Optional): Enter your postal zip code.
* **Country** (text, Optional): Select the country where you are located.
* **Message** (text, Optional): Optionally, provide a message about your inquiry or concern.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_one, Optional): If you have been assigned a tool from the business, select it from the provided options.
