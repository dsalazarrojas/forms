# Energy Recovery Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to assist participants in completing the Energy Recovery Program registration form. The form is designed to collect essential details for program enrollment and ensure that participants understand the program's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your personal details as requested, including Participant ID (if applicable).
2. Select the preferred dates you are available for the program.
3. Provide a clear consent indicating your understanding of the program's terms and conditions.
4. Enter your organization's information (if applicable).
5. Enter the clinic or wellness provider information (if applicable).
6. Select the energy type you are looking for (if applicable).
7. Specify the recovery type (if applicable).
8. Enter the session length and sessions per week (if applicable).
9. Choose the frequency of the program (if applicable).
10. Enter the duration of the program (if applicable).
11. Provide any additional notes or comments you may have.
12. Select the submission option to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (<code>participant_details</code>, text, required/optional): This section is for entering your personal details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Preferred Dates** (<code>preferred_dates</code>, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the dates you are available for the program.
* **Consent** (<code>consent</code>, text, required/optional): This is where you provide a clear consent indicating your understanding of the program's terms and conditions.
* **Organization** (<code>organization</code>, text, required/optional): Enter your organization's information if you are registering on behalf of an organization.
* **Clinic** (<code>clinic</code>, text, required/optional): Enter the clinic or wellness provider information (if applicable).
* **Wellness Providers** (<code>wellness_providers</code>, text, required/optional): Select the wellness providers you are interested in.
* **Participant Consent** (<code>participant_consent</code>, text, required/optional): This is where you provide a clear consent indicating your understanding of the program's terms and conditions.
* **Clinic Type** (<code>clinic_type</code>, text, required/optional): Select the type of clinic or wellness provider.
* **Notes** (<code>notes</code>, text, required/optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **E-mail** (<code>e-mail</code>, text, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Date** (<code>date</code>, date, required/optional): Enter the date you are registering for the program.
* **Time** (<code>time</code>, time, required/optional): Select the time you are registering for the program.
* **Energy Type** (<code>energy_type</code>, text, required/optional): Select the energy type you are looking for.
* **Recovery Type** (<code>recovery_type</code>, text, required/optional): Select the recovery type you are interested in.
* **Session Length** (<code>session_length</code>, number, required/optional): Enter the session length and sessions per week.
* **Frequency** (<code>frequency</code>, text, required/optional): Select the frequency of the program.
* **Duration** (<code>duration</code>, text, required/optional): Enter the duration of the program.
* **Notes 1** (<code>notes_1</code>, text, required/optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Notes 2** (<code>notes_2</code>, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Energy Level** (<code>energy_level</code>, text, required/optional): Select the energy level you are interested in.
* **Participant ID** (<code>participant_id</code>, text, required/optional): Enter your participant ID (if applicable).
* **Participant Status** (<code>participant_status</code>, text, required/optional): Select the participant status.
* **Participant Status Date** (<code>participant_status_date</code>, text, required/optional): Enter the date of the participant status.
* **Submit** (<code>submit</code>, select_one, required/optional): Select the submit option to complete the form.

## Tips

- Please make sure to provide accurate and clear information to ensure smooth registration and program planning.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the program administrators.
- Review and understand the program's terms and conditions before submitting the form.
- Complete all required fields before submitting the form.
- You may need to enter your participant ID for program tracking purposes.
