# Pet Insurance Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Insurance Billing Form is used for pet owners to input payment details for their pet's insurance policy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct payment date.
2. Enter the payment amount.
3. Choose the payment method (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other).
4. Enter your credit card number or other payment information.
5. Enter your name as the owner of the pet.
6. Enter your pet's address.
7. Enter the name of the pet.
8. Enter your pet's policy number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **payment_date**: Select the date you are making the payment on.
* **payment_amount**: Enter the amount you are paying for your pet's insurance.
* **payment_method**: Choose how you want to make the payment (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other).
* **card_number**: If you chose Credit Card as your payment method, enter your credit card number.
* **owner_name**: Enter your name as the owner of the pet.
* **owner_address**: Enter the address where the pet is kept.
* **pet_name**: Enter the name of your pet.
* **policy_number**: Enter your pet's policy number.

## Tips

- Make sure to double-check your payment date and amount to ensure accuracy.
- If you choose Other as your payment method, please specify the method in the field below.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your pet insurance provider for assistance.
