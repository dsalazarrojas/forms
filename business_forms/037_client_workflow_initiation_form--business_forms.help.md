# Client Workflow Initiation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Workflow Initiation Form is a crucial document that provides essential information about your project or request. It helps our team understand your needs, expectations, and requirements, ensuring a successful project kickoff.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Read each section carefully** and fill out the form accordingly.
2. **Answer all questions thoroughly**, providing as much detail as possible.
3. **Double-check your answers** before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name or Company**: Enter your company name or the name of the client.
	+ Please provide the full name of your company or the client's name.
* **Primary Contact Person**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for your project or request.
	+ Provide the full name of the contact person.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
	+ Please use a valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
	+ Provide a working phone number.
* **Client ID (if applicable)**: If you are a returning client, provide your client ID.
	+ This helps us track your projects and requests efficiently.
* **Type of Request**: Select the type of request or project you have (e.g., New Project, Change Request, Enhancement, Support Request, Maintenance, or Other).
	+ Choose the most accurate option.
* **Project Name**: Enter a name for your project or request.
	+ This will be used to reference your project throughout the process.
* **Project Description**: Provide a detailed description of what you need.
	+ Be as thorough as possible to ensure we understand your requirements.
* **Project Scope**: Describe what is included in this project.
	+ List all the necessary components, features, or tasks.
* **Priority Level**: Choose the priority level of your project (Low, Medium, High, Urgent, or Critical).
	+ Select the most appropriate priority level.
* **Reason for Priority Level**: Briefly explain why this project requires the chosen priority level.
	+ If necessary.
* **Impact of Not Completing This Project**: Describe the potential impact of not completing this project.
	+ This will help us understand the importance of your project.
* **Desired Start Date**: Enter the desired start date for your project.
	+ This helps us plan the project schedule accordingly.
* **Project Deadline**: Enter the project completion deadline.
	+ Be realistic and achievable.
* **Key Milestone Dates**: List any important checkpoint or deliverable dates.
	+ These are essential for project tracking.
* **Estimated Budget**: If applicable, enter the estimated budget for your project.
	+ This helps us plan resources and costs.
* **Has Budget Been Approved?**: If your budget has been approved, select 'Yes'.
* **Team Resources Needed**: List the required team members or resources for your project.
	+ This helps us assign the right people to your project.
* **Specialized Skills or Expertise Required**: List any specialized skills or expertise needed for your project.
	+ This ensures we have the right people for the job.
* **Internal Stakeholders or Approvers**: List internal stakeholders or approvers for your project.
	+ This helps us involve the right people in the project process.
* **External Dependencies or Constraints**: List any external integrations, approvals, or constraints that affect your project.
	+ This helps us consider these factors in our planning.
* **Approvals Needed Before Starting**: List any required approvals before project initiation.
	+ This ensures a smooth project kickoff.
* **Supporting Documents or Files**: Attach any relevant supporting documents or files.
	+ This helps us understand your requirements better.
* **Existing Assets or Documentation Available**: If applicable, list any existing assets or documentation that can be used for your project.
	+ This saves time and effort.
* **Success Criteria**: Define how we will know the project is complete.
	+ This ensures successful project delivery.
* **Additional Notes or Specifications**: Enter any additional notes or specifications for your project.
	+ This helps us understand your requirements fully.

By following these guidelines, you will be able to complete the Client Workflow Initiation Form accurately and effectively, ensuring a successful project kickoff and a positive outcome.
