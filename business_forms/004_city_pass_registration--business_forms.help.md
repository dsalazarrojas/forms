# City Pass Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The City Pass Registration form is a mandatory online form used to register for a city pass, a form of identification or access granted by the city authorities. This form collects personal, identification, and residency information to verify your eligibility for a city pass.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, date of birth, and select your gender.
2. Move on to the identification and residency section, where you will select the type of identification you possess, enter your ID number, and specify the expiration date of your ID.
3. Next, provide your address information, including your street address, city, and postal code.
4. In the contact section, enter your email address and phone number.
5. Select the type of city pass you are requesting (annual, student, senior citizen, temporary visitor, or family pass).
6. Confirm whether you have provided proof of residency (a valid utility bill or lease agreement).
7. Finally, accept the city pass terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the options provided (Male, Female, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say).
* **Type of ID**: Select the type of identification you possess (Passport, National ID card, Driver's license, Resident card, or Other).
* **ID Number**: Enter the number on your identification document.
* **ID Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of your identification document.
* **Current Residency Status**: Select your residency status (Current resident - 1 year or more, Current resident - less than 1 year, Former resident, or Not a resident).
* **Street Address**: Enter the street address where you reside.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your residence.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **City Pass Type Requested**: Select the type of city pass you are requesting (Annual resident pass, Student pass, Senior citizen pass, Temporary visitor pass, or Family pass).
* **Proof of Residency Provided**: Confirm whether you have provided proof of residency (True, False, or Will provide later).
* **I accept the City Pass terms and conditions**: Confirm your acceptance of the city pass terms and conditions (Yes, I accept or No, I do not accept).
