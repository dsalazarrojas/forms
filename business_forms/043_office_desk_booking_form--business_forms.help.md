# office_desk_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The office desk booking form is used to book a desk for a specific user in the office environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the desired date for booking.
2. Choose a time slot that suits you best.
3. Select your preferred desk(s) from the available options.
4. Enter your contact information (email and phone number).
5. Choose the assigned tool for the booking (e.g. chatjimmy).
6. Select the file format for the output (e.g. 043 office desk booking form--business forms).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user** (`1`, `select_one`, required): Select a number between 1 and 10 to identify yourself.
* **date** (`date`, required): Enter a date for the desk booking.
* **time** (`time`, required): Enter a time slot that suits you best.
* **desk** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more desks from the available options.
* **comment** (`6`, `note`, optional): If you have any specific requirements or preferences for the booking, you can add them here.
* **email** (`7`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **phone** (`8`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **assigned_tool** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Choose the tool you need for the booking (e.g. chatjimmy).
* **output_file** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Select a file format for the output.

## Tips
- Make sure to select a correct date and time slot.
- Choose a desk that suits your needs and preferences.
- Enter accurate contact information.
- Select the correct tool for the booking.
- Double-check your selections before submitting the form.
