# Subscribe To My List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Subscribe To My List" form is a simple subscription form designed to collect user information for the purpose of subscribing to a list of categories and tools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your **First Name** (id: 1) and **Last Name** (id: 2) fields with your full names.
2. Enter your **Email** (id: 3) address as per the required format (e.g., [username]@email.com).
3. Optionally, provide your **Phone** (id: 4) number for further communication.
4. Provide your **Date of Birth** (id: 5) in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
5. Choose your **Category** (id: 8) from the available options.
6. Choose your **Assigned Tool** (id: 9) from the available options.
7. Optionally, fill out your **Description** (id: 7) field to provide additional details.
8. Leave the **Time of Registration** (id: 6) field blank or fill it out if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (id: 1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (id: 2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (id: 3, email, required): Enter your email address in the required format.
* **Phone** (id: 4, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 5, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Time of Registration** (id: 6, time, optional): Leave this field blank or fill it out if required.
* **Description** (id: 7, note, optional): Provide a description of yourself or your subscription.
* **Category** (id: 8, select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple categories from the available options.
* **Assigned Tool** (id: 9, select_one, required): Choose one tool from the available options.
