# Hair Stylist Contract Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hair Stylist Contract Form is designed to be a comprehensive tool for hair stylists to collect and document important information from clients, including their contact details, service requirements, and payment terms. This form can be used to streamline the contracting process, ensuring both the stylist and client are on the same page. By filling out this form, clients can provide clear and concise information, and stylists can use this data to deliver exceptional services and manage their business effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Client Information
Fill out the first page, Client Information, with your personal details as a client. This includes:

* **Client Name** (`client_information`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Information** (`client_information`): Provide your contact number and email address so the stylist can get in touch with you.

### Step 2: Service Details
Move to the second page, Service Details, and fill out the required information. This includes:

* **Service Requirements** (`service_details`): Describe the specific services you require, such as a haircut, color correction, or chemical treatment.
* **Service Duration** (`service_details`): Indicate the length of the service, including the start and end time, date, or duration.

### Step 3: Payment Terms
Proceed to the third page, Payment Terms, and enter the relevant details. This includes:

* **Payment Amount** (`payment_terms`): Specify the amount you are willing to pay for the services rendered.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_terms`): Select your preferred payment method, such as cash, card, or bank transfer.

### Step 4: Stylist Agreement
Move to the fourth page, Stylist Agreement, and read through the terms of service. 

### Step 5: Client Review
Fill out the fifth page, Client Review, to provide feedback on the services received.

### Step 6: Payment Details
Go to the sixth page, Payment Details, and enter any additional payment information.

### Step 7: Stylist Contract Form Footer
The seventh page, Stylist Contract Form Footer, is for any additional notes or comments.

### Step 8: Stylist Contract Form Signature
The final step is to sign the eighth page, Stylist Contract Form Signature, to acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the terms.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each page accurately and completely, as this will help ensure a smooth and efficient experience for both the stylist and yourself.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, do not hesitate to ask your stylist for assistance.
* Once you have filled out the form, please present it to your stylist for review and signature.
* By signing the form, you are agreeing to the terms and conditions outlined in the Stylist Agreement page.
