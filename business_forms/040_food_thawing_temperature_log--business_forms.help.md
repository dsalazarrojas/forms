# food_thawing_temperature_log - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to document the temperature and time of thawing for food items in the kitchen. This helps ensure that food is thawed to a safe temperature and for the correct amount of time, reducing the risk of spoilage and maintaining food quality.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct temperature unit (Fahrenheit or Celsius) for the food being thawed.
2. Enter the actual temperature of the food after thawing.
3. Record the time the food was thawed, including the start and end times.
4. Specify the type(s) of food being thawed (e.g., Chicken, Fish, or Meat).
5. Add any additional notes about the thawing process, if necessary.
6. Enter your name and email (optional, but recommended for record-keeping).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Temperature**: Enter the actual temperature of the food after it has been thawed. This should be a numerical value.
* **Time**: Record the time the food was thawed, including the start and end times. This can be entered in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
* **Date**: Record the date the food was thawed. This is not required, but recommended for tracking purposes.
* **Unit**: Choose the correct temperature unit for the food being thawed (Fahrenheit or Celsius).
* **Food Type**: Select the type(s) of food being thawed (e.g., Chicken, Fish, or Meat).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the thawing process, such as any challenges or issues encountered.
* **User Name**: Enter your name, which will be recorded for tracking and record-keeping purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will also be recorded for tracking and record-keeping purposes.
