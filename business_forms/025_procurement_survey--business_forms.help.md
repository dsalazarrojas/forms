# procurement survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from customers about their experience with the seller, allowing us to evaluate and improve our seller's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page and answer the questions according to your experience with the seller.
2. Enter your responses in the provided fields.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure your feedback is complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: false): A brief description of your experience with the seller. Please provide a concise summary of your interaction with the seller.
* **Seller Services** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Describe the services provided by the seller, including any notable features or issues.
* **Seller Products** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Describe the products or items you purchased from the seller.
* **Seller Feedback** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional comments or feedback about the seller's performance.
* **Overall Experience** (`5`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience with the seller, using a numerical value (e.g., 1-5).
* **Seller Communication** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Describe the communication style and quality of the seller, including any notable strengths or weaknesses.
* **Time to Fulfill** (`7`, `number`, required: false): Estimate the time it took for the seller to complete the order or fulfill your request.
* **Seller Punctuality** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether the seller met their promised delivery time or if you experienced delays.
* **Product Packaging** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Describe the packaging quality and condition of the products you received.
* **Seller Response Time** (`10`, `time`, required: false): Estimate the time it took for the seller to respond to your inquiries or messages.
* **Communication Styles** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Describe the communication styles and tone used by the seller, including any notable strengths or weaknesses.
* **Seller Communication Styles** (`12`, `text`, required: false): (Duplicate of `11`, but this field is likely an error in the original YAML schema).
* **Shipping Costs** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Report any notable costs associated with shipping or delivery.
* **Shipping Lead Time** (`14`, `number`, required: false): Estimate the lead time for shipping or delivery.

Note: The fields marked as `required: false` are not compulsory, but providing a complete and honest answer will help us better understand your experience and provide valuable insights for improvement.
