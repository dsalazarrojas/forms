# Bid Opportunity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form guides you through assessing the potential of a bid opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the **RFP/Tender Opportunity Name**, **RFP ID or Tender Number** and **Client or Agency Name** in their respective fields. This will help you keep track of the opportunity.
2. Select the **Currency** in which the bid is offered (USD, CAD, EUR, or Other).
3. Enter the **Estimated Contract Value** of the bid.
4. Assess the **Strategic Fit** of the opportunity with your company by selecting how well it aligns with your strategy (Excellent fit, Good fit, Moderate fit, Poor fit, or Not aligned).
5. Evaluate the **Position in Target Market** by selecting how it will impact your market position (Strengthens position significantly, Strengthens position, Neutral, Weakens position, or Not relevant).
6. Determine the **Our Competitive Advantage** by selecting how it will impact your competitive edge (Significant advantage, Moderate advantage, Equal to competitors, Slight disadvantage, Major disadvantage).
7. Enter the **Estimated Cost to Win** of the bid, which includes bid preparation and sales costs.
8. Estimate the **Estimated Win Probability**, which is the percentage chance of winning.
9. Calculate the **Estimated Gross Margin** of the bid.
10. Determine the **Return on Investment (ROI)**, which is the expected return on investment.
11. Evaluate the **Resource Availability** by selecting how available your resources are (Fully available, Mostly available, Some constraints, Significant constraints, or Unable to commit).
12. Assess the **Internal Team Capability** by selecting how capable your team is (Fully capable, Mostly capable, Some gaps, Significant gaps, or Not capable).
13. Determine the **Need for External Resources or Partnerships** by selecting how much external help is required (No additional resources needed, Minor external help required, Significant partnerships needed, Major partnerships critical, or Dependent on external parties).
14. Enter the **Bid Submission Deadline** and **Internal Decision Deadline** in their respective fields.
15. Evaluate the **Time Available to Prepare Bid** in days.
16. Assess the **Bid Timeline Feasibility** by selecting how tight the schedule is (Plenty of time, Adequate time, Tight schedule, Very tight schedule, or Insufficient time).
17. Select the **Key Risk Factors** that could go wrong (Strong competitor presence, Unclear requirements, Price competition, Resource constraints, Technology risks, Regulatory complexity, Scope creep, Client instability).
18. Determine the **Overall Risk Level** by selecting the risk exposure (Low risk, Moderate risk, High risk, Very high risk, or Unacceptable risk).
19. Enter your **Recommendation** on whether to pursue or decline the opportunity (Strong go - pursue immediately, Go - recommend bidding, Conditional - depends on factors, No go - do not recommend, Strong no go - recommend declining, or Strong no go - recommend declining).
20. Enter your **Decision Rationale**, explaining why you made the recommendation.
21. Enter the name of the person who completed the **Assessment**.
22. Enter the date when the **Assessment** was completed.
23. Enter any **Additional Comments or Considerations**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Opportunity Information

* **RFP/Tender Opportunity Name**: Enter the title of the project or contract.
* **RFP ID or Tender Number**: Enter the reference number of the RFP.
* **Client or Agency Name**: Enter the name of the client or agency issuing the RFP.
* **Estimated Contract Value**: Enter the estimated value of the contract.

### Strategic Assessment

* **Strategic Fit**: Select how well the opportunity aligns with your strategy.
* **Position in Target Market**: Select how the opportunity will impact your market position.
* **Our Competitive Advantage**: Select how the opportunity will impact your competitive edge.

### Financial Analysis

* **Estimated Cost to Win**: Enter the estimated cost of winning the bid.
* **Estimated Win Probability**: Enter the percentage chance of winning.
* **Estimated Gross Margin**: Enter the expected profit margin.
* **Return on Investment (ROI)**: Select the expected return on investment.

### Resource Requirements

* **Resource Availability**: Select how available your resources are.
* **Internal Team Capability**: Select how capable your team is.
* **Need External Resources or Partnerships**: Select how much external help is required.

### Timeline and Deadlines

* **Bid Submission Deadline**: Enter the date when the bid is due.
* **Internal Decision Deadline**: Enter the date when you must decide to bid.
* **Time Available to Prepare Bid**: Enter the number of days available.
* **Bid Timeline Feasibility**: Select how tight the schedule is.

### Risk Assessment

* **Key Risk Factors**: Select the risk factors that could go wrong.
* **Overall Risk Level**: Select the risk exposure.

### Bid Decision

* **Recommendation**: Select whether to pursue or decline the opportunity.
* **Decision Rationale**: Enter the reason for the recommendation.
* **Assessment Completed By**: Enter the name of the person who completed the assessment.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date when the assessment was completed.
* **Additional Comments or Considerations**: Enter any other relevant factors.

### Tips

* Please ensure that you have all relevant information available before starting the assessment.
* Some fields are required to be filled out to complete the form.
* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form to ensure a reliable assessment.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
