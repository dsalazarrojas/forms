# Commission Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commission Agreement Form is a document that outlines the terms of the commission agreement between the principal company and the salesperson or agent. It defines the commission structure, payment frequency, duties, and responsibilities of the salesperson or agent, as well as the products or services to be sold.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the Principal Company Name and Registered Office Address to identify the company paying the commission.
2. Enter the Salesperson's Name, ID or Social Security Number, and Email Address to identify the salesperson or agent.
3. Describe the salesperson's duties and responsibilities in the Brief Description of Salesperson's Duties field.
4. List the products or services to be sold in the Products or Services to be Sold field.
5. Enter the Commission Rate as a percentage and the Fixed Commission Amount per Sale (if applicable).
6. Choose the Commission Payment Frequency (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
7. Enter the Minimum Sales Quota (if applicable).
8. Select the Governing State Law that applies to this agreement.
9. Enter the Effective Date of the Agreement and the Termination Date (if applicable).
10. Type your full name to sign the agreement in the I Agree to the Terms of this Commission Agreement field.
11. Enter the Date of Signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Principal Company Name** (`company_name`, required, text): Enter the name of the company paying the commission.
* **Company Registered Office Address** (`company_address`, required, text): Enter the registered office address of the principal company.
* **Salesperson's Name** (`salesperson_name`, required, text): Enter the full name of the salesperson or agent.
* **Salesperson's ID or Social Security Number** (`salesperson_id`, required, text): Enter the ID or Social Security Number of the salesperson or agent.
* **Salesperson's Email Address** (`salesperson_email`, required, email): Enter the email address of the salesperson or agent.
* **Brief Description of Salesperson's Duties** (`salesperson_duties`, required, text): Describe the duties and responsibilities of the salesperson or agent.
* **Products or Services to be Sold** (`product_list`, required, text): List the specific products or services to be sold.
* **Commission Rate (%)** (`commission_rate`, required, number): Enter the commission rate as a percentage.
* **Fixed Commission Amount per Sale** (`fixed_amount_per_sale`, required, number): Enter the fixed commission amount per sale (if applicable).
* **Commission Payment Frequency** (`payment_schedule`, required, select_one): Choose the commission payment frequency (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* **Minimum Sales Quota** (`minimum_sales_quota`, optional, number): Enter the minimum sales quota (if applicable).
* **Governing State Law** (`governing_state`, required, text): Select the state whose laws apply to this agreement.
* **Effective Date of Agreement** (`agreement_start_date`, required, date): Enter the effective date of the agreement.
* **Termination Date** (`agreement_end_date`, optional, date): Enter the termination date (if applicable).
* **I Agree to the Terms of this Commission Agreement** (`signatures_agreement`, required, text): Type your full name to sign the agreement.
* **Date of Signature** (`date_of_signature`, required, date): Enter the date of signature.
