# this_form_has_been_approved_by_the_florida_realtors_and_the_florida_bar - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for real estate transactions in Florida, specifically for the sale of a property. It has been approved by the Florida Realtors and the Florida Bar, indicating that it meets their standards for legal and regulatory compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Seller**
  * Name: Enter the seller's name.
  * This field is optional, but it's recommended to include it for identification purposes.
2. **Property Address**
  * Enter the property address where the sale is taking place.
  * This field is required.
3. **Purchase Price**
  * Enter the purchase price of the property.
  * This field is required.
4. **Down Payment**
  * Enter the down payment made by the buyer.
  * This field is required.
5. **Interest Rate**
  * Enter the interest rate of the loan.
  * This field is optional.
6. **Loan Term**
  * Enter the term of the loan, in years.
  * This field is required.
7. **Loan Amount**
  * Enter the total amount of the loan.
  * This field is required.
8. **Seller Fees**
  * Enter any fees charged by the seller.
  * This field is optional.
9. **Buyer Fees**
  * Enter any fees charged by the buyer.
  * This field is optional.
10. **Closing Date**
  * Enter the date of the closing.
  * This field is required.
11. **Closing Time**
  * Enter the time of the closing.
  * This field is required.
12. **Closing Location**
  * Enter the location of the closing.
  * This field is optional.
13. **As Is Purchase Contract**
  * Check this box to indicate that the sale is as-is.
  * This field is required.
14. **Buyer Signature**
  * Enter the buyer's signature.
  * This field is required.
15. **Seller Signature**
  * Enter the seller's signature.
  * This field is required.
16. **Buyer Contact Name**
  * Enter the buyer's name.
  * This field is required.
17. **Buyer Telephone**
  * Enter the buyer's telephone number.
  * This field is required.
18. **Buyer Email**
  * Enter the buyer's email address.
  * This field is required.
19. **Buyer Address**
  * Enter the buyer's address.
  * This field is required.
20. **Buyer City**
  * Enter the city where the buyer resides.
  * This field is required.
21. **Buyer State**
  * Enter the state where the buyer resides.
  * This field is required.
22. **Buyer Zip**
  * Enter the buyer's zip code.
  * This field is required.
23. **Loan Number**
  * Enter the loan number.
  * This field is required.
24. **Loan Term Years**
  * Enter the term of the loan, in years.
  * This field is required.
25. **Lender**
  * Enter the lender's name.
  * This field is required.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Review the form carefully to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
- If you are unsure about any field or section, please consult with a real estate professional or attorney.
- Save a copy of the completed form for your records.
