# Best Agent Voting Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Best Agent Voting Poll form is designed to collect opinions and ratings from employees on their colleagues who are being nominated for an award. This form helps the awards committee to evaluate and select the best agent for the award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and employee ID in the "Voter Full Name" and "Employee ID" fields, respectively.
2. Select the department of the nominee from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the name of the agent you are voting for in the "Nominated Agent Name" field.
4. Select the category where the nominee excels most in the "Category for Nomination" field.
5. Rate the nominee's performance in the following areas:
	* Quality of Work (1-5)
	* Reliability and Consistency (1-5)
	* Communication Skills (1-5)
	* Problem Solving Ability (1-5)
6. Provide a detailed reason for the nomination in the "Detailed Reason for Nomination" field.
7. Describe a specific achievement of the nominee in the "Example of a Specific Achievement" field.
8. Enter the date of the vote in the "Date of Vote" field.
9. Provide any additional feedback for the awards committee in the "Additional Feedback for Awards Committee" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Voter Full Name**: Enter your full name as a voter.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department**: Select the department of the nominee from the dropdown list.
* **Nominated Agent Name**: Enter the name of the agent you are voting for.
* **Category for Nomination**: Select the area where the nominee excels most.
* **Quality of Work**: Rate the nominee's quality of work from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Reliability and Consistency**: Rate the nominee's reliability and consistency from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Communication Skills**: Rate the nominee's communication skills from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Problem Solving Ability**: Rate the nominee's problem-solving ability from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Detailed Reason for Nomination**: Provide a detailed reason why you are nominating this agent.
* **Example of a Specific Achievement**: Describe a specific situation where the nominee demonstrated exceptional performance.
* **Additional Feedback for Awards Committee**: Provide any additional feedback or comments for the awards committee.
* **Date of Vote**: Enter the date of the vote.
* **Committee Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments for the awards committee.
