# Complaint Resolution Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Complaint Resolution Intake Form is designed to collect information about customer complaints or issues they have experienced with the business. It helps the support team to quickly identify and address each problem, ensuring efficient resolution and improved customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your **Customer Full Name**, ensuring you enter your full legal name.
2. Provide your **Account or Order Number**, which is a unique reference for your purchase or account.
3. Enter your **Contact Phone Number**, which should be a number that the support team can reach you at during business hours.
4. Specify the **Date of Incident**, when the issue occurred.
5. Describe the **Product or Service** you are complaining about.
6. Provide a brief **Issue Summary**, such as Billing Error or Delayed Shipping.
7. Give a detailed **Detailed Description of the Issue**, including full details of what happened.
8. Select your **Preferred Resolution**, which can be Full Refund, Partial Refund/Credit, Product Replacement, Correction of Error, Formal Apology, or Other.
9. Finally, enter the name of the **Intake Officer** recording the complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name** (`customer_full_name_intake`): Enter your full legal name.
* **Account or Order Number** (`account_order_number_intake`): A unique reference for your purchase or account.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_intake`): A number that the support team can reach you at during business hours.
* **Date of Incident** (`complaint_incident_date_intake`): When did the issue occur?
* **Product or Service** (`product_service_type_intake`): Specify the item or service in question.
* **Issue Summary** (`complaint_issue_summary_intake`): A brief title for the complaint, such as Billing Error or Delayed Shipping.
* **Detailed Description of the Issue** (`detailed_issue_description_intake`): Please provide full details of what happened.
* **Preferred Resolution** (`desired_resolution_intake`): Choose the resolution method you prefer.
	+ Full Refund: A full refund for the issue.
	+ Partial Refund / Credit: A partial refund or credit for the issue.
	+ Product Replacement: Replace the defective or non-functional product.
	+ Correction of Error: Correct the error or defect.
	+ Formal Apology: A formal apology for the issue.
	+ Other: Select one of the above options or specify a different resolution.
* **Intake Officer Name** (`intake_officer_name_intake`): The name of the staff member recording the complaint.
* **Date of Intake** (`intake_date_final`): Today's date, automatically populated.

## Tips

* Ensure you provide accurate and detailed information about your issue to help the support team resolve it efficiently.
* Avoid duplicate submissions by ensuring you've completed this form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you need help or clarification, contact our support team for assistance.
* Please note that all fields marked as required are essential for efficient processing of your complaint.
