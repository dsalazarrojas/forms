# Service Repair Warranty Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to request service repair and warranty services from our service center.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields, denoted by a "required" label in the YAML definition.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field, such as service center, technician, and warranty type.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details**: This field is for gathering customer information, including name and contact details.
* **Repair Details**: This field is for describing the issue with your device/product and any repair instructions.
* **Appointment Time**: Select a time for your appointment.
* **Appointment Date**: Select a date for your appointment.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your repair.
* **Customer Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your repair.
* **Service Center**: Select the service center where you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Technician**: Select one or more technicians for your repair.
* **Repair Type**: Select the type of repair you require.
* **Appointment Status**: Select the status of your appointment.
* **Customer Signature**: Enter your signature for agreement purposes.
* **Service Date**: Enter the date of your service appointment.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your service appointment.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your service appointment.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of your service appointment.
* **Service Repair Status**: Select the status of your repair.
* **Customer Agreement**: Select the agreement you agree to for your repair.
* **Warranty Status**: Select the status of your warranty.
* **Warranty Date**: Enter the date of your warranty.
* **Warranty Start Time**: Enter the start time of your warranty.
* **Warranty End Time**: Enter the end time of your warranty.
* **Warranty Duration**: Enter the duration of your warranty.
* **Warranty Type**: Select the type of warranty you require.

Please note that this form requires selecting multiple options for some fields, such as technician and repair type. Select all applicable options for each field.
