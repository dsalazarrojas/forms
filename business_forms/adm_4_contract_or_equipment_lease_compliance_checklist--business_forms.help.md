# ADM 4 Contract Or Equipment Lease Compliance Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The ADM 4 Contract or Equipment Lease Compliance Checklist is a tool used to assess the compliance of contracts or equipment leases with relevant regulatory, policy, and company directives. This form is designed to help auditors or inspectors evaluate the condition and status of equipment leases, ensuring that all required documents are present, financial obligations are met, and safety and regulatory requirements are fulfilled.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, select the "Contract / Lease number" field and enter the unique identifier assigned to the contract or lease.
2. Enter the "Inspector/Auditor name" and "Inspector role or department" to identify the person performing the inspection.
3. Select the "Location or site inspected" where the equipment is located.
4. Enter the "Inspection date" and "Inspection time" of the audit.
5. Select the "Contract or agreement type" from the provided options (e.g., Equipment lease, Service contract, Purchase order, etc.).
6. Enter the "Vendor / lessor name" who is providing the equipment or service.
7. Indicate whether all "Required contract documents" are present (True, False, or Partial).
8. Determine if "Financial obligations" are met (Compliant, Non-compliant, or Not applicable).
9. Evaluate the "Equipment physical condition" (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Unusable).
10. Assign a "Safety score" (1-10) based on visible hazards and controls.
11. Identify and "Issues found" during the inspection (select all that apply).
12. Check if "Relevant regulatory or policy requirements" are met (True, False, or Not applicable).
13. Describe any "Recommended corrective actions" and assign a responsible party and priority.
14. Enter the "Target date for corrective action completion" if applicable.
15. Schedule a "Follow-up inspection date" if necessary.
16. Attach any supporting evidence (e.g., photos, contract pages, invoices) in the "Supporting Evidence" section.
17. Provide any additional "Comments or notes" about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector/Auditor name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your badge.
* **Inspector role or department**: Identify your role or department performing the inspection.
* **Inspection date**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Inspection time**: Enter the time of the inspection in 24-hour format (HH:mm).
* **Location or site inspected**: Enter the building name, floor, room number, or lease address.
* **Contract / Lease number**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the contract or lease.
* **Contract or agreement type**: Select the primary agreement governing the asset.
* **Vendor / lessor name**: Enter the company or individual providing the equipment or service.
* **All required contract documents present**: Indicate if all required documents are present.
* **Financial obligations met**: Determine if financial obligations are met (Compliant, Non-compliant, or Not applicable).
* **Equipment physical condition**: Evaluate the condition of the equipment (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Unusable).
* **Safety score (1-10)**: Assign a score based on visible hazards and controls.
* **Issues identified (select all)**: Select all issues found during the inspection.
* **Relevant regulatory or policy requirements met**: Check if requirements are met (True, False, or Not applicable).
* **Recommended corrective actions**: Describe any necessary actions and assign a responsible party and priority.
* **Target date for corrective action completion**: Enter the target date for corrective action completion if applicable.
* **Follow-up inspection date**: Schedule a follow-up inspection date if necessary.
* **Supporting Evidence**: Attach any supporting evidence (e.g., photos, contract pages, invoices).
* **Additional comments or notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
