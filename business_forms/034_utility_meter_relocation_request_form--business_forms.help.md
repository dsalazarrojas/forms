## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for the relocation of utility meters. It's meant to be completed by customers who need to move or have moved their utility meters.

2.  In the "Meter Relocation Details" field, provide details about the meter relocation, including the type of meter, its current location, and the desired new location.
3.  Schedule a specific time for the work in the "Schedule Work" field.
4.  If applicable, add your utility provider's information in the "Utility Provider Info" field.
