# Business Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your business. It will help us understand your company's characteristics, goals, and challenges. Please provide accurate and concise answers to give us a comprehensive view of your business.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. If a question does not apply to your business, select "N/A" or "No response".
4. You can save your progress and return to the form later if needed.
5. Once you have completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to send your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name (Business Name)** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Your company or organization name.
* **Respondent Name (Respondent Name)** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Your name.
* **Respondent Title or Position (Respondent Title or Position)** (`respondent_title`, `text`, required): Your role in the organization.
* **Email Address (Email Address)** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required): Your contact email.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`respondent_phone`, `text`, optional): Your contact phone number.
* **Type of Business (Type of Business)** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your business model from the following options: Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, Non-profit, Cooperative.
* **Industry or Sector (Industry or Sector)** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): Select your primary business sector from the following options: Manufacturing, Retail, Services, Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Education, Real Estate, Hospitality, Other.
* **How long has your business been operating? (How long has your business been operating?)** (`years_in_business`, `number`, required): In years.
* **Number of Employees (Number of Employees)** (`number_of_employees`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of staff from the following options: 1-5, 6-10, 11-25, 26-50, 51-100, 101-500, 500+.
* **Annual Revenue (Annual Revenue)** (`annual_revenue`, `select_one`, required): Select your approximate annual revenue from the following options: Less than $100000, $100000 - $500000, $500000 - $1 million, $1 - $5 million, $5 - $10 million, $10 - $50 million, Over $50 million.
* **Primary Business Location (Primary Business Location)** (`business_location`, `text`, optional): City and state or country.
* **Do you operate internationally? (Do you operate internationally?)** (`international_operations`, `select_one`, required): Select from the following options: Yes, extensively, Yes, limited, No, domestic only.
* **Main Products or Services (Main Products or Services)** (`main_products_services`, `text`, required): Brief description of your products or services.
* **Primary Market or Customer Base (Primary Market or Customer Base)** (`primary_market`, `text`, required): Who are your main customers?
* **Competitive Advantages (Competitive Advantages)** (`competitive_advantages`, `text`, optional): Select all that apply from the following options: Market competition, Finding qualified talent, Cash flow management, Technology infrastructure, Customer acquisition, Regulatory compliance, Supply chain issues, Economic uncertainty.
* **Major Business Challenges (Major Business Challenges)** (`major_challenges`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the following options: Market competition, Finding qualified talent, Cash flow management, Technology infrastructure, Customer acquisition, Regulatory compliance, Supply chain issues, Economic uncertainty.
* **Growth Plans (Growth Plans)** (`growth_plans`, `select_one`, required): Select from the following options: Yes, aggressive growth, Yes, moderate growth, Maintain current size, Uncertain, Planning to reduce size.
* **Level of Technology Adoption (Level of Technology Adoption)** (`technology_adoption`, `select_one`, required): Select from the following options: Highly advanced, Modern, Basic systems, Minimal technology, No technology.
* **Business Services (Business Services)** (`business_needs`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the following options: Consulting, Financing, Marketing support, Technology solutions, Training and development, Legal services, Accounting and bookkeeping, Networking opportunities.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other information you would like to share.
