# Process Optimization Research Collaboration Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register your interest in process optimization research collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal information and interests.
2. Select your preferred collaborations for research projects.
3. Provide a brief submission with any additional comments or details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address. Please use a valid email address.
* **Interest 1** (`interest_1`, text, optional): Describe your primary interest area in process optimization research.
* **Interest 2** (`interest_2`, text, optional): Describe your secondary interest area in process optimization research.
* **Collaboration 1** (`collaboration_1`, select_one, required): Select your preferred collaboration option for research projects.
* **Collaboration 2** (`collaboration_2`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple preferred collaboration options for research projects.
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, optional): Provide a brief submission with any additional comments or details.

## Tips
* Please register only once with your correct and complete information to avoid duplication.
* Use a valid email address to ensure we can contact you with updates and opportunities.
* Be specific when describing your interests and preferred collaborations to help us connect you with suitable research projects.
