# Automation Network Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a partnership contact form designed to collect information from potential partners interested in collaborating with us. It will help us assess your company's fit with our business needs and determine the best way to collaborate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to our partnership form. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose of this form.
2. **Contact Information**: Fill out your contact information, including your first name, last name, job title, email, phone number, and company details.
3. **Company Information**: Provide your company's name, website, address, city, state, ZIP code, and country.
4. **Partnership Information**: Describe your interest in partnering with us, and what type of partnership you're interested in.
5. **Company Details**: Share your company's size, experience, and capabilities.
6. **Partnership Timeline**: Choose when you'd like to start the partnership.
7. **Resources Needed**: Select the resources you require for the partnership.
8. **References**: Provide references for your company.
9. **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your company.
10. **How Did You Hear About Us**: Choose how you heard about us.
11. **Referral Name**: If you were referred, provide the name of the referrer.
12. **Contact Preference**: Select how you'd like to be contacted.
13. **Best Time to Contact**: Choose when you're available to be contacted.
14. **NDA Required**: Check if you require a non-disclosure agreement (NDA).
15. **Terms and Conditions**: Check if you agree to our terms and conditions.
16. **Submitter Signature**: Add your digital signature.
17. **Submission Date**: Select the date of submission.
