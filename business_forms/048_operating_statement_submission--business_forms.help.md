# operating_statement_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The operating statement submission form is designed to guide you through the process of uploading operating statements and submitting them for processing. This includes providing reporting information, uploading required documents, and selecting relevant categories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Upload your operating statement in the designated text field.
2. Select the reporting period for which the statement is intended.
3. Choose the start date of the reporting period.
4. Choose the end date of the reporting period.
5. Upload any additional documents required by the form.
6. Provide a contact email and phone number for further communication.
7. Select the tool assigned to process your statement.
8. Provide a brief description of the statement.
9. Categorize the statement under the relevant category (Business Forms).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Upload Statement** (`upload_statement`, text, required): This is the main field for uploading your operating statement. Please ensure that you upload the most recent and accurate statement possible.
* **Reporting Period** (`reporting_period`, select_one, required): This field allows you to select the reporting period for which the statement is intended. Choose the correct period from the options provided.
* **Reporting Start Date** (`reporting_start_date`, date, required): This field is used to select the start date of the reporting period.
* **Reporting End Date** (`reporting_end_date`, date, required): This field is used to select the end date of the reporting period.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, required): This field allows you to upload any additional documents required for processing the statement.
* **Contact Email** (`email`, email, required): This is your contact email address for further communication regarding the statement.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): This is your contact phone number for further communication regarding the statement.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): This field allows you to select the tool assigned to process your statement.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of the statement.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category under which the statement falls (Business Forms).
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, not required): This is a note field for submitting the form.
