# web_development_signup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The web development signup form is used to collect information from developers who are interested in participating in web development projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. If you have a website, enter it in the "Website" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the date you are available to work on the project.
6. Select the time you are available to work on the project.
7. Optionally, add any notes about your availability in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name 1** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your full name or the name of your company/organization.
* **Name 2** (`name_2`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on official documents or in professional settings.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address associated with your web development profile.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, optional): Enter the URL of your personal or company website.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select the date you are available to work on the project.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Select the time you are available to work on the project.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about your availability or special requirements.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information, as it will be used to facilitate collaboration and project planning.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our team.
