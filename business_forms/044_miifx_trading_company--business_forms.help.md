# Miifx Trading Company - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Miifx Trading Company form is designed to collect user information and preferences for investment purposes. The form will help us provide you with tailored investment recommendations based on your interests, goals, and risk tolerance.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete the form, please fill in the required information in each field.
2.  Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information to ensure you receive the best investment recommendations.
3.  Please do not duplicate information or submit incomplete forms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communication about your investment account.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, number, required): Enter your phone number for direct communication about your investment account.
* **Investment Interest** (`Investment Interest`, select_one, required): Please select one of the investment options (Stocks, Bonds, ETFs, Mutual Funds, Other) that interest you the most.
* **Investment Goals** (`Investment Goals`, select_multiple, required): Select the investment goals that best describe your objectives (Conservative, Aggressive, Growth, Income, Long-term Growth, Short-term Growth).
* **Risk Tolerance** (`Risk Tolerance`, select_multiple, required): Select the risk tolerance level that best describes your risk-taking ability (Low, Medium, High).
* **Age** (`Age`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Location** (`Location`, text, required): Please enter your current location.
* **Occupation** (`Occupation`, text, optional): Please enter your occupation for further reference.
* **Have a LinkedIn Profile?** (`Have a LinkedIn Profile?`, select_one, required): If you have a LinkedIn profile, please select "Yes", otherwise select "No".
* **Have a Twitter Profile?** (`Have a Twitter Profile?`, select_one, optional): If you have a Twitter profile, please select "Yes", otherwise select "No".
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information that will help us better serve you.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, checkbox, required): Please read and agree to our terms and conditions before proceeding.
* **Consent to Receive Marketing Materials** (`Consent to Receive Marketing Materials`, checkbox, required): Please indicate if you are willing to receive marketing materials from us.

Note: The form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to complete. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
