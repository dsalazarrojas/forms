# Global Signatory Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Global Signatory Form is a document that ensures all parties involved in a business transaction or project are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions. It is a crucial step in the process of signing a contract or approving a business proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name and any other relevant contact information (such as email and phone number) on **page 1**.
2. On **page 2**, select your consent to act as a signatory by choosing "Yes" from the dropdown menu.
3. On **page 2**, select your approval status by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
4. On **page 3**, select whether you have given your digital signature by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
5. On **page 4**, select whether you have given your confirmation by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Global Signatory Form Business Forms** (1): This is the title of the form, which is used to identify the purpose of the document.
* **Name** (2): Please fill out your name accurately and thoroughly.
* **Consent** (3): This field is used to indicate whether you have given your consent to act as a signatory. Select "Yes" if you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Digital Signature** (4): This field is used to indicate whether you have given your digital signature. Select "Yes" if you have given your digital signature, or "No" if you have not.
* **Approval** (5): This field is used to indicate whether you have approved the terms and conditions. Select "Yes" if you approve, or "No" if you do not.
* **Confirmation** (6): This field is used to indicate whether you have confirmed the terms and conditions. Select "Yes" if you confirm, or "No" if you do not.
* **Email** (7): Please fill out your email address accurately and thoroughly.
* **Phone** (8): This field is used to enter your phone number.
