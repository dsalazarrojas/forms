# self_employed_business_expenses - Help Guide
## Purpose
The self-employed business expenses form is designed to collect information about business-related expenses for self-employed individuals. It helps track and categorize expenses, providing a clear understanding of business expenses and facilitating accurate financial reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the **Business Form Section** to choose the type of business form you want to fill out.
2. Select the relevant **Business Expenses** related to your business.
3. Enter the **Expense Date** when the expense was incurred.
4. Enter the **Expense Amount** for each expense.
5. Choose the **Expense Frequency** for regular business expenses.
6. Select the **Business Use**, **Personal Use**, **Expense Nature**, **Expense Type**, **Expense Category**, **Expense Sub Category**, **Expense Approver**, **Approval Status**, and **Expense Report** fields according to your business needs.
7. Enter any additional **Notes** and **Notes2** if necessary.
8. Enter the **Employee Name**, **Employee Position**, and **Email**, and **Phone** fields with your relevant information.
9. Choose the **Report Frequency** and **Report Date** for your expense reports.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Form Section**: Select the type of business form you want to fill out.
* **Business Expenses**: Choose the relevant expenses related to your business.
* **Expense Date**: Enter the date when the expense was incurred.
* **Expense Amount**: Enter the expense amount.
* **Expense Frequency**: Choose the frequency of regular business expenses.
* **Business Use**: Select the business use of the expense.
* **Personal Use**: Select the personal use of the expense.
* **Expense Nature**: Select the nature of the expense.
* **Expense Type**: Select the type of expense.
* **Expense Category**: Select the category of expense.
* **Expense Sub Category**: Select the sub category of expense.
* **Expense Approver**: Choose the approver of the expense.
* **Approval Status**: Choose the status of the expense.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name.
* **Employee Position**: Enter your position.
* **Expense Report**: Choose the type of expense report.
* **Report Frequency**: Choose the frequency of expense reports.
* **Report Date**: Enter the date of the expense report.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the expense.
* **Notes2**: Enter any additional notes about the expense report.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Expense Description**: Enter a brief description of the expense.
* **Total**: Select the total amount of the expense.
* **Expense Submitter**: Choose the person submitting the expense.
* **Expense Approver**: Choose the person approving the expense.
