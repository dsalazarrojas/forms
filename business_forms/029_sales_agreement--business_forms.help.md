# sales_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sales agreement form is designed to capture necessary information for sales-related activities. It covers contact details, purchase details, payment terms, and signatures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in the form's first section, "sales_agreement_page_1," where you can provide any additional information about the sale or agreement.
2. Enter contact information for both the customer and the business partner, including their contact info, email, phone number, and address.
3. Specify the purchase details, such as the purchase amount.
4. Add any sales terms that apply to the agreement.
5. In the second section, "sales_agreement_page_2," enter any further details as necessary.
6. Complete the form by signing with both the sales representative and the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **sales_agreement_page_1** (text, optional): This is the first page of the sales agreement form where you can add any additional information about the sale or agreement.
* **contact_info** (text, optional): Enter the contact information for the customer, including their name, email, phone number, and address.
* **contact_info_business_partner** (text, optional): Enter the contact information for the business partner.
* **purchase_details** (text, optional): Specify the details of the purchase, including the amount.
* **purchase_amount** (number, optional): Enter the purchase amount.
* **sales_terms** (text, optional): Add any sales terms that apply to the agreement.
* **payment_terms** (text, optional): Specify any payment terms that should be followed.
* **sales_agreement_page_2** (text, optional): Enter any further details as necessary on this page of the form.
* **sales_rep** (text, optional): Enter the name of the sales representative.
* **customer_name** (text, optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **customer_email** (email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **customer_phone** (text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **customer_address** (text, optional): Enter the customer's address.
* **business_partner** (text, optional): Enter the name of the business partner.
* **business_email** (email, optional): Enter the business partner's email address.
* **business_phone** (text, optional): Enter the business partner's phone number.
* **business_address** (text, optional): Enter the business partner's address.
* **signature_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the sales representative's signature.
* **sales_rep_signature** (text, optional): Sign with your name as the sales representative.
* **customer_signature** (text, optional): Sign with the customer's name.
* **sales_rep_signature_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the sales representative's signature.
* **customer_signature_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the customer's signature.
* **payment_terms_1** (text, optional): Enter the first payment term.
* **payment_terms_2** (text, optional): Enter the second payment term.
* **signature_block** (text, optional): This field is likely intended for a signature block, although its description is not provided in the form data.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
