# Photography Session Invoice - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record details of a photography session, including customer information, session details, payment terms, and payment status. It is intended for use by photographers to generate invoices for their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Select the start and end dates of the session using the "Session Start Date" and "Session End Date" fields.
3. Enter your name as the photographer in the "Photographer" field.
4. Enter the session duration in the "Session Duration" field.
5. Enter the total hours worked in the "Total Hours" field.
6. Enter the hourly rate in the "Hourly Rate" field.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if session services were rendered in the "Session Services" field.
8. Enter the billing address in the "Billing Address" field.
9. Enter the city in the "Billing City" field.
10. Enter the state in the "Billing State" field.
11. Enter the postal code in the "Billing Postal Code" field.
12. Enter the payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.
13. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if payment method is accepted in the "Payment Method" field.
14. Enter the payment date in the "Payment Date" field.
15. Select the payment time in the "Payment Time" field.
16. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
17. Select the payment status as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Payment Status" field.
18. Enter your signature as the photographer in the "Customer Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (1)**: Enter the name of the customer who participated in the photography session.
* **Session Start Date (2)**: Select the date when the photography session started.
* **Session End Date (3)**: Select the date when the photography session ended.
* **Photographer (4)**: Enter your name as the photographer who conducted the session.
* **Session Duration (5)**: Enter the total duration of the session in minutes or hours.
* **Total Hours (6)**: Enter the total number of hours worked during the session.
* **Hourly Rate (7)**: Enter your hourly rate for the photography services provided.
* **Session Services (8)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if session services were rendered.
* **Billing Address (9)**: Enter the address where payment should be sent to.
* **Billing City (10)**: Enter the city where payment should be sent to.
* **Billing State (11)**: Enter the state where payment should be sent to.
* **Billing Postal Code (12)**: Enter the postal code where payment should be sent to.
* **Payment Terms (13)**: Enter any special payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Method (14)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if payment method is accepted.
* **Payment Date (15)**: Select the date when payment was made.
* **Payment Time (16)**: Select the time when payment was made.
* **Payment Amount (17)**: Enter the total payment amount.
* **Payment Status (18)**: Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the payment status.
* **Customer Signature (19)**: Enter your signature as the photographer, confirming that the information provided is accurate.
