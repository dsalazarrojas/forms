# Ride Sharing Vehicle Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ride Sharing Vehicle Inspection Form is designed to provide a structured way to evaluate and report on the condition of vehicles used for ride-sharing services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the vehicle type that best describes the vehicle you're inspecting.
2. Evaluate each vehicle component and choose the corresponding condition from the options provided.
3. Fill out each page according to the instructions and options provided.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
5. Review your answers to ensure they accurately represent the vehicle's condition.
