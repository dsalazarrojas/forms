# Production Line Communication Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for production teams to report and track issues related to production lines, such as delays, errors, or other production-related problems.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the issue description field to provide a brief summary of the issue.
2. Select the category of the issue from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the priority of the issue from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter any relevant comments or notes about the issue.
5. Select the production status (in progress, on hold, or completed).
6. Choose the production line affected by the issue.
7. Select any team members involved in resolving the issue.
8. Enter the issue date and time.
9. Add any additional comments or updates for team leads and managers.
