# Authorized Representative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register an authorized representative for a business or organization. It is a crucial part of the authorization process, which ensures that the representative has the necessary permissions and authorities to act on behalf of the company.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Date of this registration.
* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Legal company name.
* **Company Registration Number** (`company_registration_number`, text, optional): Business or tax ID number.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Principal business address.
* **Representative Full Name** (`representative_name`, text, required): Legal name of representative.
* **Representative Title** (`representative_title`, text, required): Official title or role.
* **Representative Email** (`representative_email`, email, required): Business email address.
* **Representative Phone** (`representative_phone`, text, required): Business phone number.
* **Type of Authorization** (`authorization_type`, select_one, required): What is representative authorized for.
	+ General Authorization
	+ Financial Authority
	+ Legal Representation
	+ Regulatory Compliance
	+ Insurance Claims
	+ Contract Negotiation
	+ Multi-Purpose
* **Scope of Authorization** (`authorization_scope`, text, required): Describe authority boundaries.
* **Financial Authority Limit** (`financial_limit`, number, optional): Maximum amount if applicable.
* **Is This an Update to Prior Authorization?** (`prior_authorization`, select_one, required): Replacing previous authorization or not.
	+ No - New Authorization
	+ Yes - Updating Previous
	+ Yes - Adding New Authority
	+ Yes - Revoking Previous
* **Previous Representative Name** (`previous_representative_name`, text, optional): If updating authorization.
* **Authority to Sign Documents** (`signature_authority`, select_one, required): Can representative sign on behalf.
	+ No Signing Authority
	+ Limited Signing - Specific Documents
	+ Broad Signing Authority
	+ Unlimited Signing Authority
* **Contract Negotiation Authority** (`contract_negotiation_authority`, select_one, optional): Can negotiate and approve contracts.
	+ No Authority
	+ Limited - Under $50k
	+ Limited - Under $250k
	+ Limited - Under $1M
	+ Full Authority
* **Banking and Account Authority** (`banking_authority`, select_one, optional): Bank account authority granted.
	+ No Banking Authority
	+ View Only
	+ Transaction Authority - Limited
	+ Full Account Authority
* **Regulatory Compliance Authority** (`regulatory_authority`, select_one, optional): Government/regulatory authority.
	+ No Regulatory Authority
	+ Limited Reporting
	+ Full Compliance Authority
	+ Authorized Signatory
* **Validity Start Date** (`validity_start_date`, date, required): When does authorization begin.
* **Validity End Date** (`validity_end_date`, date, optional): When does it expire.
* **Authorization Duration Type** (`duration_type`, select_one, required): How long authorization is valid.
	+ Fixed Term
	+ Indefinite - Until Revoked
	+ One-Time/Single Transaction
	+ Annual - Renewal Required
* **Background Check Completed** (`background_check_completed`, select_one, required): Verification completed.
	+ Yes - Completed
	+ In Progress
	+ Not Required
	+ Pending
* **Board or Management Approval** (`board_approval`, select_one, required): Has leadership approved.
	+ Yes - Approved
	+ Pending Approval
	+ No - Not Approved
	+ N/A
* **Notarization Required** (`notarization_required`, select_one, required): Does this need notarization.
	+ Yes - Notarized
	+ Yes - Will Be Notarized
	+ No - Not Required
* **Revocation Clause** (`revocation_clause`, text, optional): Who can revoke this authorization.
* **Authority to Sign Documents** (`signature_authority`, select_one, required): Can representative sign on behalf.
	+ No Signing Authority
	+ Limited Signing - Specific Documents
	+ Broad Signing Authority
	+ Unlimited Signing Authority

Note: Make sure to review the form carefully and fill out each field accurately and truthfully. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
