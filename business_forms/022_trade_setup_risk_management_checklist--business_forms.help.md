# Trade Setup Risk Management Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about trade setup risk management, helping traders and risk management teams make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" with the relevant information.
2. For non-required fields, provide information if available or relevant to the trade setup.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trade Entry** (`trade_entry`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the trade entry, including any relevant details about the trade setup.
* **Stop Loss** (`stop_loss`, number, required): Enter the stop loss value for the trade setup, which is the price at which the trade will automatically close if the market moves against the trader.
* **Position Size** (`position_size`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the position size for the trade setup, which is the amount of a currency or asset being traded.
* **Risk Checks** (`risk_checks`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether risk checks were performed for the trade setup, such as reviewing market conditions, news, and other relevant factors.
* **Entry Type** (`entry_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of trade entry, which can be "Yes" or "No", likely indicating whether this is a market order or a limit order.
* **Target Price** (`target_price`, number, required): Enter the target price for the trade setup, which is the price at which the trader aims to close the trade.
* **Stop Price** (`stop_price`, number, required): Enter the stop price for the trade setup, which is the price at which the trade will automatically close if the market moves against the trader.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one, required): Select the risk level for the trade setup, which can be "High", "Low", or other relevant categories.
* **Trade Date** (`trade_date`, date, required): Enter the trade date, which is the date the trade setup was initiated.
* **Trade Time** (`trade_time`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the trade time, which is the time the trade setup was initiated.
* **Risk Rating** (`risk_rating`, number, required): Enter the risk rating for the trade setup, which is a subjective measure of the trade's risk level.
* **Position Size Units** (`position_size_units`, select_one, required): Select whether the position size is in units of the asset being traded or not.
