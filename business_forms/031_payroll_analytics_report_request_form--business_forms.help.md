# Payroll Analytics Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll Analytics Report Request Form is designed to collect information from employees to request a payroll analytics report. This report provides insights into the company's payroll data, helping management to make informed decisions about employee compensation and benefits. To avoid duplicate requests, please note that if you have already submitted a similar report in the last 6 months, this form will not be processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start on the first page.
2. Enter your information in the fields below.
3. Review and submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Main Page
* **Main Page**: This is the introduction page with general information about the report. Please read and review the information provided.

### Employee Info
* **Employee Info** (`text`, optional): Enter your name and employee ID number to help us identify you in the report.
*   **Hint:** If you are not sure who to contact, please provide your department name or location.

### Department Info
* **Department ID** (`number`, optional): Enter your department ID number, if known.
*   **Hint:** If you are not sure what your department ID is, you can ask your manager or HR representative for assistance.

### Employee and Manager Info
* **Manager Name** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of your manager or supervisor.
*   **Hint:** If you are not sure who your manager is, please ask someone from HR for assistance.

## Tips
* Before submitting the form, ensure that you have all required information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your manager or HR representative for assistance.
* The report will be sent to the requested email address in 1-2 business days.
