# Transportation Route Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Route Validation Form is used to gather information about transportation-related activities, such as shipments and deliveries.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the title of the transportation route in the "Transport Route Validation Form" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of the route, including any relevant details, in the "Route Details" field.
3. Enter information about the vehicle, including its number and type, in the "Vehicle Information" field.
4. Enter information about the driver, including their name and phone number, in the "Driver Information" field.
5. Enter the start and end locations of the route in the "Start Location" and "End Location" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the start and end times of the route in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
7. Indicate whether the vehicle is active or inactive in the "Vehicle Type" field.
8. Check if the driver is assigned to the route in the "Driver Assigned" field.
9. Select the status of the driver and route in the "Driver Status" and "Route Status" fields, respectively.
10. If applicable, enter the distance and weight of the route in the "Route Distance" and "Route Weight" fields.
11. If applicable, enter the speed of the route in the "Route Speed" field.
12. Enter any additional remarks or comments about the route in the "Remarks" field.
13. Enter any additional route comments in the "Route Comments" field.
14. Finally, enter the date of the form in the "Form Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transport Route Validation Form** (1, text, required): Enter the title of the transportation route.
* **Route Details** (2, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the route, including any relevant details.
* **Vehicle Information** (3, text, required): Enter information about the vehicle, including its number and type.
* **Driver Information** (4, text, required): Enter information about the driver, including their name and phone number.
* **Start Location** (5, text, required): Enter the start location of the route.
* **End Location** (6, text, required): Enter the end location of the route.
* **Start Time** (7, time, required): Enter the start time of the route.
* **End Time** (8, time, required): Enter the end time of the route.
* **Vehicle Number** (9, text, required): Enter the vehicle number.
* **Vehicle Type** (10, select_one, required): Indicate whether the vehicle is active or inactive.
* **Driver Assigned** (11, select_multiple, required): Check if the driver is assigned to the route.
* **Driver Status** (16, select_one, required): Select the status of the driver.
* **Route Status** (18, select_one, required): Select the status of the route.
* **Route Distance** (13, number, optional): Enter the distance of the route (if applicable).
* **Route Weight** (14, number, optional): Enter the weight of the route (if applicable).
* **Route Speed** (15, number, optional): Enter the speed of the route (if applicable).
* **Remarks** (12, note, optional): Enter any additional remarks or comments about the route.
* **Route Comments** (23, text, optional): Enter any additional route comments.
* **Route Comments Date** (25, date, optional): Enter the date of the route comments.

Please note that some fields are optional and should only be filled out if they are relevant to the specific route. Always ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
