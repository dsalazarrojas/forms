# Pet Product Supply Chain Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Product Supply Chain Update Request Form is used to update information about pet products in the supply chain. This form helps to track and manage the status of pet products, including their availability, shipment dates and times, and contact information for shipment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant information about the pet product, including the product code, description, and product status.
2. Select the shipment date and time from the provided date and time inputs.
3. Provide a brief description of the shipment in the shipment notes field.
4. Enter the address and contact information for the shipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Product Update Request (1)** (`pet_product_update_request`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the update request.
* **Product Code (2)** (`product_code`, text, required/optional): Enter the unique code for the pet product.
* **Product Description (3)** (`product_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the pet product.
* **Shipment Date (4)** (`shipment_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the shipment.
* **Shipment Time (5)** (`shipment_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time of the shipment.
* **Product Status (6)** (`product_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Product Available
	+ Product Unavailable
	+ Product Out of Stock
* **Product Status Hint (7)** (`product_status_hint`, text, required/optional): Enter a hint about the product status, such as a note or description.
* **Shipment Notes (8)** (`shipment_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the shipment.
* **Shipment Address (9)** (`shipment_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address for the shipment.
* **Contact Person (10)** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for the shipment.
* **Contact Phone (11)** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Shipment Email (12)** (`shipment_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Shipment Date (13)** (`shipment_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the shipment.
* **Shipment Time (14)** (`shipment_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time of the shipment.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to avoid any discrepancies in the supply chain tracking.
* Use the product status hints to provide additional context about the product status.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the supply chain team for assistance.
