# meeting_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide feedback on meetings and events. It helps to capture comments, suggestions, and ratings from attendees, ensuring that the meeting organizers can improve future events and provide better experiences for participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field, which is a required field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field, which is also a required field.
3. Share your comments and observations about the meeting in the "comment" field, which is a required field.
4. Submit the form to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (Text, Required): Enter your name to help the meeting organizers know who you are and can address any feedback you provide.
* **email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address so that the meeting organizers can contact you if they need more information or clarification.
* **comment** (Text, Required): Share your thoughts and observations about the meeting. Be specific about what worked well and what could be improved.

## Tips
- Please be honest and constructive in your feedback, as it will help the meeting organizers improve future events.
- Keep your comments concise and focused on the meeting's content and organization.
- If you have any suggestions or recommendations, be specific about how they can be implemented.
- If you have any concerns or complaints, address them clearly and specifically.
