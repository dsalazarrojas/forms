# Client Brief Case Study Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps business clients document their project case studies, providing valuable information about their projects and outcomes. By filling out this form, clients can share their success stories, highlighting the challenges faced, solutions implemented, and results achieved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by describing the project's background and goals. 
2. Provide details about the primary problem or challenge faced.
3. Describe the solution or service implemented.
4. List the main deliverables or products/services provided.
5. Report tangible results (metrics, ROI, savings) and intangible benefits (efficiency, satisfaction, brand growth).
6. Share a short quote or testimonial about your satisfaction.
7. Confirm if your company gives permission to publish the case study publicly (with or without your company name).
8. Provide contact information for approval and publication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Company Name (id: 2)** 
  * This is the name of your company.
  * Please type the name of your company accurately.

* **Project Title/Name (id: 3)** 
  * This is the title of your project.
  * Briefly describe the project's main objective or goal.

* **Completion Date (id: 4)** 
  * This is the date your project was completed.
  * Format the date in a standard (DD/MM/YY) or (MM/DD/YY) format.

* **What was the primary problem or challenge you faced? (id: 6)** 
  * Describe the main issue your project addressed.
  * Be as specific as possible.

* **What were the specific goals for this project? (id: 7)** 
  * List the objectives of your project.
  * Be as specific as possible.

* **Briefly describe the solution or service provided (id: 9)** 
  * Summarize the solution or service delivered.
  * Be concise.

* **Key Deliverables (id: 10)** 
  * List the main products or services delivered.
  * Be as detailed as possible.

* **Tangible Results (Metrics, ROI, Savings) (id: 12)** 
  * Report any measurable outcomes.
  * Use numbers and metrics to describe the results.

* **Intangible Benefits (Efficiency, Satisfaction, Brand Growth) (id: 13)** 
  * Describe any intangible benefits achieved.
  * Be as specific as possible.

* **Short Quote or Testimonial (id: 14)** 
  * Summarize your satisfaction with the project.
  * Be concise and brief.

* **Do we have permission to publish this case study publicly? (id: 15)** 
  * Choose how you want your project to be published.
  * Select the option that best represents your company's preference.

* **Contact Person for approval (id: 16)** 
  * Provide contact information for publication approval.
  * Ensure your company representative can be reached.

* **Email Address (id: 17)** 
  * Enter your contact email address.
  * Format it accurately.

* **Submission Date (id: 19)** 
  * The date you are submitting this form.
  * Be accurate and in a standard format.

* **Link to project photos or assets (id: 20)** 
  * Share any relevant project links.
  * Help others understand your project better.

* **Industry Category (id: 21)** 
  * Select the category your project belongs to.
  * Choose the best fit for your industry.
