# Anonymous Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect complaints and issues submitted by employees or individuals regarding workplace concerns or issues within an organization. It aims to provide a safe and anonymous reporting mechanism for employees to report incidents, problems, or concerns without fear of retribution or consequences. This form will help the organization to understand and address these issues effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the date when the issue occurred in the "Date of Complaint" field.
2. Choose the department or area affected in the "Department or Area Affected" field.
3. Select the category of your complaint in the "Complaint Category" field. Common categories include "Workplace conduct", "Safety concerns", "Harassment or discrimination", "Ethics or fraud", "Quality or service issue", "Policy violation", "Environmental concern", or "Other".
4. Choose the priority level of your complaint in the "Priority Level" field. Select "Critical" for urgent matters, "High" for issues requiring immediate attention, "Medium" for important but non-urgent matters, or "Low" for informational purposes.
5. If applicable, list the people involved in the "Person or People Involved" field.
6. Provide a detailed description of the issue in the "Detailed Description of Complaint" field. Be as specific as possible to help the organization understand the problem.
7. Choose how long this issue has been occurring in the "How long has this been occurring" field. This could be a single incident, less than a week, between 1-4 weeks, 1-3 months, more than 3 months, or ongoing.
8. Describe the impact of the issue on the work environment or business in the "Impact of the Issue" field.
9. Select who is affected by this issue in the "Who is affected by this issue" field. Options include yourself, your team or department, other employees, customers or clients, public or community, or company reputation.
10. Indicate if you have any evidence or documentation to support your complaint in the "Evidence or Documentation Available" field.
11. Choose whether there were any witnesses to the issue in the "Were there witnesses" field.
12. If you have reported this issue before, select the correct option in the "Have you reported this before" field.
13. If you have reported this issue before, describe what happened with the previous report in the "What happened with previous report" field.
14. If you attempted to resolve the issue yourself, select the correct option in the "Have you attempted to resolve this yourself" field.
15. Describe the desired resolution or outcome of the issue in the "Desired Resolution or Outcome" field.
16. If you are concerned about retaliation, select the level of concern in the "Concern about Retaliation" field.
17. If you wish to remain completely anonymous, select the "Do you want to remain completely anonymous" field.
18. If you wish to be contacted, select the preferred contact method in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
19. If you are willing to provide contact information, enter it in the "Contact Information" field.
20. Finally, add any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Complaint**: Enter the date when the issue occurred.
* **Department or Area Affected**: Select the department or area that was affected by the issue.
* **Complaint Category**: Select the category of your complaint from the available options.
* **Priority Level**: Choose the priority level of your complaint based on its urgency and importance.
* **Person or People Involved**: List the people involved in the issue, if applicable.
* **Detailed Description of Complaint**: Provide a detailed description of the issue.
* **How long has this been occurring**: Select how long the issue has been occurring.
* **Impact of the Issue**: Describe the impact of the issue on the work environment or business.
* **Who is affected by this issue**: Select who is affected by this issue.
* **Evidence or Documentation Available**: Indicate if you have any evidence or documentation to support your complaint.
* **Were there witnesses**: Choose if there were any witnesses to the issue.
* **Have you reported this before**: Select if you have reported this issue before.
* **What happened with previous report**: If you reported this issue before, describe what happened with the previous report.
* **Have you attempted to resolve this yourself**: Select if you have attempted to resolve the issue yourself.
* **Desired Resolution or Outcome**: Describe the desired resolution or outcome of the issue.
* **Concern about Retaliation**: Select the level of concern you have about retaliation.
* **Do you want to remain completely anonymous**: Select if you wish to remain completely anonymous.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select the preferred method of contact, if you wish to be contacted.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, if you are willing to provide it.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information regarding the issue.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing the issue in the "Detailed Description of Complaint" field.
* If you have any evidence or documentation, attach it with your submission.
* If you are concerned about retaliation, please select the correct level of concern.
* If you wish to remain completely anonymous, please be aware that the organization will still make efforts to address the issue without disclosing your identity.
* If you are willing to provide contact information, please be prepared for follow-up communication from the organization.
