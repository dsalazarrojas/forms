# recruitment_agency_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client information and service request details from recruitment agencies. It is intended to be used by agencies to provide quotes for various services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Client Information page with the client's details.
2. Provide details about the service requested on the Service Request page.
3. Add any necessary quote details on the Quote Details page.
4. Review and agree to the Quote Terms and Conditions.
5. Sign and confirm the quote on the Client Signature page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (Page 1, Text): Enter the client's name, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **Service Request** (Page 2, Text): Describe the service required, including any specific job titles, locations, or other requirements.
* **Quote Details** (Page 3, Text): Provide any additional information needed to create an accurate quote, such as specific dates or times.
* **Quote Terms and Conditions** (Page 4, Text): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the quote.
* **Client Signature** (Page 5, Text): Sign and confirm the acceptance of the quote.
