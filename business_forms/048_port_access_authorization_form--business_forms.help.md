# port_access_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The port_access_authorization_form is used to collect and manage information from users for port access authorization. This form is designed to gather essential details to verify user identity and ensure accurate management of port access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email, and date of birth to provide basic contact information.
2. Fill in your contact phone number to allow us to contact you regarding port access.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached regarding port access.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY` (e.g., 02/12/1990).
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached regarding port access.

## Tips
* Ensure the information entered is accurate and up-to-date to prevent any delays in the port access authorization process.
* Double-check that all required fields are filled out to ensure smooth processing.
