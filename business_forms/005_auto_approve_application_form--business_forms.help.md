# Auto Approve Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from applicants for auto-approve applications, including personal details, employment information, and application specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the applicant details section, providing your first name, last name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and current employment details.
2. Select your employment status and employment length with your current employer.
3. Choose the type of application you are submitting (e.g. Personal Loan, Credit Card, Membership, etc.)
4. Provide the amount (if applicable) you are requesting.
5. Describe the purpose of your application in a brief statement.
6. Estimate your credit score range.
7. Finally, agree to the application terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter the email address you want to receive application status notifications on.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your mobile or home phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g. 01/01/1990).
* **Current Employer** (text, required): Enter the name of the company you are currently employed by.
* **Monthly Income** (number, required): Enter your gross income before taxes, including any bonuses or commissions.
* **Years at Current Job** (number, required): Enter the total number of years you have been employed with your current employer.
* **Employment Status** (select one, required): Select your primary employment status from the options provided.
* **Application Type** (select one, required): Choose the type of application you are submitting (e.g. Personal Loan, Credit Card, Membership, etc.).
* **Requested Amount** (number, optional): Enter the amount you are requesting if applicable.
* **Purpose of Application** (text, required): Briefly describe the reason for your application.
* **Credit Score Estimate** (select one, required): Select your estimated credit score range from the options provided.
* **Agree to Terms** (select one, required): Confirm that you agree to the application terms and conditions.

## Tips
Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
