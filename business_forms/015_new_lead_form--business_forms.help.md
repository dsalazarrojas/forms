# new_lead_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "new_lead_form" is used for capturing lead details and other relevant information. It is a comprehensive form with multiple fields that helps users provide a complete picture of a lead.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields: 
   - **Reference**: Enter a brief description of the lead.
   - **Potential Behavior**: Select whether the lead has potential.
   - **Lead Status**: Select the status of the lead.
   - **Assigned Tool**: Select whether a tool is assigned to the lead.
   - **Lead Status Id**: Enter the ID of the lead status.
   - **Form Id**: Enter the ID of the form.
   - **Lead Source**: Enter the source of the lead.
   - **Lead Type**: Select the type of lead.
   - **Customer**: Enter the customer name.
   - **Customer Id**: Enter the customer ID.
   - **Contact**: Enter the contact name.
   - **Contact Id**: Enter the contact ID.
   - **Company**: Enter the company name.
   - **Company Id**: Enter the company ID.
   - **Lead Owner**: Enter the lead owner name.
   - **Lead Owner Id**: Enter the lead owner ID.
   - **Created By**: Enter the name of the person who created the lead.
   - **Created By Id**: Enter the ID of the person who created the lead.
2. Fill out the optional fields:
   - **Lead Details**: Enter a brief description of the lead.
   - **Location**: Enter the location of the lead.
   - **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the lead.
   - **Form Ids**: Select multiple form IDs.
   - **Lead Source**: Enter the source of the lead.
   - **Lead Type**: Select the type of lead.
   - **Customer**: Enter the customer name.
   - **Contact**: Enter the contact name.
   - **Company**: Enter the company name.
   - **Lead Owner**: Enter the lead owner name.
   - **Created By**: Enter the name of the person who created the lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Details** (`Lead Details`, `text`, required/optional): A brief description of the lead.
* **Reference** (`Reference`, `text`, required): A brief description of the lead.
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, optional): The location of the lead.
* **Potential Behavior** (`Potential Behavior`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the lead has potential.
* **Prophets** (`Prophets`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple prophets associated with the lead.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Additional notes about the lead.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, `select_one`, required): Select whether a tool is assigned to the lead.
* **Assigned Tool Id** (`Assigned Tool Id`, `number`, required): Enter the ID of the assigned tool.
* **Lead Status** (`Lead Status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the lead.
* **Lead Status Id** (`Lead Status Id`, `number`, optional): Enter the ID of the lead status.
* **Form Ids** (`Form Ids`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple form IDs.
* **Form Id** (`Form Id`, `number`, required): Enter the ID of the form.
* **Lead Source** (`Lead Source`, `text`, optional): Enter the source of the lead.
* **Lead Type** (`Lead Type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of lead.
* **Customer** (`Customer`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer name.
* **Customer Id** (`Customer Id`, `number`, optional): Enter the customer ID.
* **Contact** (`Contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact name.
* **Contact Id** (`Contact Id`, `number`, optional): Enter the contact ID.
* **Company** (`Company`, `text`, optional): Enter the company name.
* **Company Id** (`Company Id`, `number`, optional): Enter the company ID.
* **Lead Owner** (`Lead Owner`, `text`, optional): Enter the lead owner name.
* **Lead Owner Id** (`Lead Owner Id`, `number`, optional): Enter the lead owner ID.
