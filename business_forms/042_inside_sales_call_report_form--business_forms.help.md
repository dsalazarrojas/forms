# Inside Sales Call Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record and document the details of inside sales calls, ensuring that the team has an accurate and comprehensive record of customer interactions, follow-ups, and outcomes. This information can be useful for tracking customer relationships, identifying sales opportunities, and guiding future sales strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the relevant fields based on your experience with the customer.
2.  Choose the correct option for each field to accurately represent your interaction with the customer.
3.  Be as detailed as possible in your answers, especially in fields like `Customer Response` and `Follow-up Action`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Contact** (`First Contact`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if this is the first contact you've had with the customer. You may want to mark this as true if you're submitting this form for the initial call, or false otherwise.
*   **Customer** (`Customer`, `text`, required: false): Enter the customer's name as it appears on your records or any other relevant details you've gathered during the call.
*   **Product or Service** (`Product or Service`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the product or service that was discussed during the call.
*   **Next Contact Date** (`Next Contact Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the expected or actual date of the next contact with the customer. Be specific and include a full date if possible.
*   **Follow-up Date** (`Follow-up Date`, `date`, required: false): Similar to `Next Contact Date`, enter the date you're expecting or plan to follow up with the customer. If the date is uncertain or not applicable, you may leave this field blank.
*   **Follow-up Method** (`Follow-up Method`, `text`, required: false): Describe the method you plan to use for the follow-up, such as email, phone, or visit. This could be a brief note like "follow-up via email" or "phone call scheduled."
*   **Customer Response** (`Customer Response`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant responses or comments from the customer during or after the call. Be as detailed as possible to ensure that you capture the customer's thoughts and needs accurately.
*   **Follow-up Action** (`Follow-up Action`, `checkbox`, required: false): Choose one or both of the options for the follow-up action, such as "Schedule Next Call" and "Send Follow-up Email."
*   **Outcome** (`Outcome`, `text`, required: false): Describe the outcome of the call, such as "Next step agreed upon," "Customer needs more information," or "Product demo scheduled."
*   **Follow-up Outcome** (`Follow-up Outcome`, `text`, required: false): Record the outcome of the follow-up action, similar to the outcome of the initial call. This could be a brief summary of what transpired or the customer's response.
