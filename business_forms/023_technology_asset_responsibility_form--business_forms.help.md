# technology_asset_responsibility_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Technology Asset Responsibility Form is used to document and track information about technology assets, including their type, status, ownership, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of asset from the options provided.
2. Choose the current status of the asset.
3. Indicate if the asset has an owner.
4. Specify if the asset is located at a particular site or location.
5. Enter a brief description of the asset.
6. Enter the serial number of the asset, if applicable.
7. Enter the date the asset was purchased.
8. Enter the cost of the asset.
9. Enter the cost of the asset.
10. Select a category for the asset.
11. Enter any additional notes about the asset.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Asset Type** (`id: 1`, select_one, optional): Choose the category of the asset (e.g. Computer, Software, Hardware, etc.)
- **Asset Status** (`id: 2`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the asset (e.g. Available, Inactive, Unknown).
- **Asset Owner** (`id: 3`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if the asset has an owner (Yes, No).
- **Asset Location** (`id: 4`, select_multiple, optional): Specify if the asset is located at a particular site or location (Yes, No).
- **Asset Description** (`id: 5`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the asset.
- **Asset Serial Number** (`id: 6`, text, optional): Enter the serial number of the asset, if applicable.
- **Asset Purchase Date** (`id: 7`, date, optional): Enter the date the asset was purchased.
- **Asset Purchase Price** (`id: 8`, number, optional): Enter the cost of the asset.
- **Asset Cost** (`id: 9`, number, optional): Enter the cost of the asset.
- **Asset Category** (`id: 10`, text, optional): Select a category for the asset.
- **Asset Notes** (`id: 11`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the asset.
