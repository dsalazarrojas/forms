# Loyalty Rewards Program Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Loyalty Rewards Program Subscription Form is used by customers to subscribe to the loyalty rewards program. Customers can fill out this form to opt-in to one of our three available loyalty programs: Loyalty Program A, Loyalty Program B, or Loyalty Program C. This form also includes fields for their contact preferences and language requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's first name in the "customer-first-name" field.
2. Enter the customer's last name in the "customer-last-name" field.
3. Enter the customer's email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter the customer's phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Choose one of our three loyalty programs from the "loyalty-program" dropdown menu.
6. Select your preferred contact method from the "preferred-contact-method" dropdown menu.
7. Select your preferred language from the list of available languages.
8. If you have any special requests, type them in the "special-requests" field.
9. Confirm that you have read and agree to our terms and conditions by selecting "True" from the "terms-and-conditions" dropdown menu.
10. If you have any additional comments, type them in the "additional-comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer-first-name**: Enter the customer's first name here.
* **customer-last-name**: Enter the customer's last name here.
* **email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **loyalty-program**: Select one of our three loyalty programs: Loyalty Program A, Loyalty Program B, or Loyalty Program C.
* **preferred-contact-method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted: Email, Phone, or Text Message.
* **preferred-language**: Select your preferred language: English, Spanish, or French.
* **special-requests**: If you have any special requests, type them here.
* **terms-and-conditions**: Confirm that you have read and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **additional-comments**: If you have any additional comments, type them here.
