# Predictive Reordering Pilot Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Predictive Reordering Pilot Registration form is designed to help us understand your company's goals, needs, and suitability for our Predictive Reordering Pilot program. Please complete this form to express your interest in participating in the pilot program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that you have read and understand the Terms of Service and have given your consent to participate.
3. Provide detailed answers to the Pilot Goals and SaaS and Operations fields to help us better understand your company's needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**:
	+ Label: Company Name
	+ Description: Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards or letterhead.
* **Address**:
	+ Label: Address
	+ Description: Enter your company's address, including the street number and name, city, and state.
* **City**:
	+ Label: City
	+ Description: Enter the city where your company is located.
* **Pilot Goals**:
	+ Label: Pilot Goals
	+ Description: Describe the specific goals you hope to achieve through our Predictive Reordering Pilot program.
* **Terms of Service**:
	+ Label: Terms of Service
	+ Description: Please select 'Yes' if you have read and agree to our terms of service.
* **Consent**:
	+ Label: Consent
	+ Description: Please select 'Yes' to indicate that you have given your consent to participate in the pilot program.
* **Company Details Terms**:
	+ Label: Company Details Terms
	+ Description: Enter any additional terms or conditions related to your company's participation in the pilot program.
* **SaaS and Operations**:
	+ Label: SaaS and Operations
	+ Description: Provide detailed information about your company's SaaS and operations, including any specific challenges or concerns you may have.
