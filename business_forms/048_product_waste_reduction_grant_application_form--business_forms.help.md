# Product Waste Reduction Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist applicants in providing detailed information about their project that seeks to reduce waste. To be eligible for the grant, the project must fall under one of the categories listed in the form and be deemed active or inactive status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. Use the select options carefully, as they might affect the grant eligibility.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
5. Submit the form once you have filled out all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **project_details** (text, required/optional): This field is intended to provide a brief description of the project.
	+ A concise and clear description of the project will help the reviewer understand its scope and goals.
* **project_proposal** (text, required/optional): This field should contain a detailed description of the project proposal, including the project objectives and the expected outcomes.
	+ This will provide the reviewer with a comprehensive understanding of the project's goals and expectations.
* **project_budget** (text, required/optional): Enter the project budget, including estimated costs and funding sources.
	+ This will help the reviewer understand the project's financial requirements and feasibility.
* **documents** (text, required/optional): Attach relevant documents supporting the project, such as project plans or reports.
	+ This will provide additional context and evidence for the project.
* **grant_amount** (number, required/optional): Enter the grant amount being requested.
	+ This will help the reviewer understand the grant amount required for the project.
* **project_duration** (time, required/optional): Enter the project duration in months.
	+ This will help the reviewer understand the project timeline.
* **project_start_date** (date, required/optional): Enter the project start date.
	+ This will help the reviewer understand the project start date.
* **submitter_name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person or organization submitting the form.
	+ This will allow the reviewer to identify the applicant.
* **submitter_email** (email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person or organization submitting the form.
	+ This will provide the reviewer with a contact email for follow-up questions.
* **submitter_phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person or organization submitting the form.
	+ This will provide the reviewer with a contact phone number for follow-up questions.
* **grant_agency** (select_one, required/optional): Select one of the available grant agencies (Waste Management, Environmental Protection, Public Works, or Community Development).
	+ This will help the reviewer understand which grant agency is applicable for the project.
* **project_status** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select the project status (Active or Inactive).
	+ This will help the reviewer understand the project's current status.
* **project_category** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select the project category (Energy, Waste, Water, or Landfill).
	+ This will help the reviewer understand the project's category.
