# Franchise Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Franchise Application Form is designed for individuals or businesses that are interested in applying for a franchise. The form collects relevant information about the applicant, their business, and their financial status to help assess their eligibility for a franchise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form data section with any additional information you would like to provide about yourself and your business.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Enter your contact email.
6. Enter your contact phone number.
7. Enter your business name.
8. Enter a brief description of your business history.
9. Enter your business model or structure.
10. Select your financial status as Active or Inactive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data**: Enter any additional information you would like to provide about yourself and your business. This can include any relevant details that are not covered by the other fields.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business.
* **Business History**: Briefly describe the history of your business.
* **Business Model**: Enter your business model or structure, such as sole proprietorship, partnership, or corporation.
* **Financial Status**: Select your current financial status as Active or Inactive. 

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible to increase the chances of a successful application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your application thoroughly and respond as soon as possible.
