# custom_printing_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information and order details for custom printing orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the customer details section with the customer's name, address, and other relevant information.
2. In the printing order section, provide a description of the printing order you want to place.
3. Summarize the order in the order summary section.
4. If necessary, add any additional information in the form footer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (id: 1, text, required: false):
  This field is used to collect the customer's name, address, and other relevant information. 
* **Printing Order** (id: 2, text, required: false):
  In this field, describe the printing order you want to place. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Order Summary** (id: 3, text, required: false):
  Provide a brief summary of the order in this field. 
* **Form Footer** (id: 4, text, required: false):
  Use this field to add any additional information that might be relevant to the order, such as special requests or comments.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill out all required fields (if any) and double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
