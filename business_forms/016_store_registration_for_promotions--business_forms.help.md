# Store Registration For Promotions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting and storing information about store promotions. It is intended to help you manage and track promotions for your store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following sections:

1. **Store Information**: Enter the basic details about your store, such as its name.
2. **Store Administrators**: Check if you have store administrators, or leave blank if not.
3. **Contact Details**: Enter any relevant contact information about your store.
4. **Store Channels**: Check if your store has any channels (e.g., social media).
5. **Store Pictures**: Upload any images or videos related to your store.
6. **Store Videos**: Upload any videos related to your store.
7. **Promotional Channels**: Check if your store promotes through various channels (e.g., email, phone).
8. **Store Location**: Enter the location of your store.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Information (store_information)**: Enter a brief description of your store.
* **Store Administrators (store_administrators)**: Check if your store has administrators, which can be useful for support and maintenance tasks.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: This field is for any additional contact information you may want to provide about your store.
* **Store Channels (store_channels)**: Check if your store uses various channels to promote its products or services.
* **Store Pictures (store_pictures)**: Upload images or videos that represent your store.
* **Store Videos (store_videos)**: Upload videos that showcase your store.
* **Promotional Channels (promotional_channels)**: Check if your store promotes through various channels, such as social media or email.
* **Store Location (store_location)**: Enter the address of your store.
