# Container Drop Off Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record the details of a container drop off, ensuring accurate and efficient tracking and inventory management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, making sure to enter accurate information for each field.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Review and proofread your entries for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Container Drop Off Log** (`log_title`, optional): Enter a brief title or description of the container drop off.
* **Drop Off Date** (`drop_off_date`, required): Record the date of the container delivery.
* **Drop Off Time** (`drop_off_time`, required): Record the time of the container arrival.
* **Container ID Number** (`container_id`, required): Enter the unique identifier for the container.
* **Container Type** (`container_type`, required): Select the type of container, such as 20ft Standard, 40ft Standard, etc.
* **Container Size** (`container_size`, required): Choose the dimensions of the container.
* **Seal Number** (`seal_number`, required): Enter the security seal number.
* **Carrier Name** (`carrier_name`, required): Record the name of the shipping company.
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, required): Enter the full name of the driver.
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license`, required): Record the driver's license number.
* **Vehicle Number** (`vehicle_number`, required): Enter the truck number.
* **License Plate** (`license_plate`, required): Record the license plate number.
* **Origin Location** (`origin_location`, required): Record the location where the container came from.
* **Destination Location** (`destination_location`, required): Record the location where the container was delivered.
* **Cargo Description** (`cargo_description`, required): Describe the contents of the container.
* **Cargo Weight** (`cargo_weight`, optional): Enter the weight of the cargo in kilograms.
* **Contains Hazardous Materials** (`hazardous_materials`, required): Indicate if the container contains hazardous materials.
* **Hazmat Class** (`hazmat_class`, optional): If applicable, select the hazmat class.
* **Container Condition** (`container_condition`, required): Rate the condition of the container on arrival.
* **Damage Description** (`damage_notes`, optional): Enter any damage or issues with the container.
* **Receiving Dock Number** (`receiving_dock`, required): Record the dock number assigned to the container.
* **Received By** (`received_by`, required): Enter the staff name who received the container.
* **Inspection Completed** (`inspection_completed`, required): Indicate if the inspection was completed.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, optional): Enter any other relevant information.
