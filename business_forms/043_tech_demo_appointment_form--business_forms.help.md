# Tech Demo Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Tech Demo Appointment Form" is used to collect information from clients about their technical demo appointments. This form helps to ensure that all necessary information is gathered accurately and efficiently for the demo.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in all the fields as accurately as possible with the requested information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for "Contact Preferences", "Demo Preferences", "Client Preferences", and "Demo Length" as they are crucial for us to provide the best possible service.
3. Enter the "Company Name", "Contact First Name", "Contact Last Name", "Company Email", and "Company Website" correctly as they are essential contact details.
4. Select the correct "Time Zone" and "Preferred Language" for the demo.
5. Enter the "Company Industry" and "Company Address" accurately as they help us understand the client's background.
6. Enter the "Phone Number" correctly as it is used for communication.
7. Select the "Demo Date" and "Demo Time" for the scheduled demo.
8. Confirm the email address entered in the "Confirm Email" field to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (1)** (`Text`): Please enter the client's name and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Preferences (2)** (`Select Multiple`): How would you like to be contacted? (e.g., Yes/No)
* **Demo Details (3)** (`Text`): Enter a brief description of the demo you are interested in.
* **Demo Preferences (4)** (`Select One`): Are you interested in a live demo or a recorded one? (e.g., Yes/No)
* **Schedule Appointment (5)** (`Text`): Enter the appointment schedule (e.g., date and time).
* **Confirm Appointment (6)** (`Text`): Confirm the appointment details (e.g., date, time, and duration).
* **Client Preferences (7)** (`Select Multiple`): What are the client's preferences? (e.g., Yes/No)
* **Additional Comments (8)** (`Note`): Any further comments or instructions.
* **Company Name (9)** (`Text`): Enter the company name.
* **Contact First Name (10)** (`Text`): Enter the contact first name.
* **Contact Last Name (11)** (`Text`): Enter the contact last name.
* **Company Email (12)** (`Email`): Enter the company email address.
* **Company Website (13)** (`Text`): Enter the company website.
* **Company Industry (14)** (`Text`): Enter the company industry.
* **Company Address (15)** (`Text`): Enter the company address.
* **Phone Number (16)** (`Text`): Enter the phone number.
* **Time Zone (17)** (`Time`): Select the time zone.
* **Preferred Language (18)** (`Select One`): In which language would you like the demo to be in? (e.g., Yes/No)
* **Demo Length (19)** (`Select Multiple`): How long would you like the demo to be? (e.g., Yes/No)
* **Demo Time (20)** (`Time`): Enter the demo time.
* **Demo Date (21)** (`Date`): Enter the demo date.
* **Company Size (22)** (`Select Multiple`): What is the company size? (e.g., Yes/No)
* **Contact Job Title (23)** (`Select One`): What is the contact's job title? (e.g., Yes/No)
* **Contact Department (24)** (`Select One`): Which department is the contact from? (e.g., Yes/No)
* **Confirm Email (25)** (`Email`): Confirm the email address entered.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have filled in all the required fields.
- Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
