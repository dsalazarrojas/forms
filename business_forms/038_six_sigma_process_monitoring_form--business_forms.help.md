# Six Sigma Process Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Six Sigma Process Monitoring Form is designed to help you monitor and improve business processes. This form helps you identify areas of concern and track performance metrics to make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the business process that you are monitoring (Logistics, Manufacturing, Operations, or Finance).
2. Choose the quality assurance metrics that are relevant to your process (On-time Delivery, On-budget, On-quality, or Other).
3. Identify any process vulnerabilities that affect your performance (Equipment Failure, Human Error, Process Design, or Resource Constraints).
4. Conduct a root cause analysis to understand the underlying causes of issues.
5. Create a corrective action plan to address the root causes.
6. Select the performance metrics you want to monitor (Production Time, Cost, Quality, or Other).
7. Set performance targets for each metric (numeric value).
8. Enter the value of the performance target.
9. Add any additional notes or comments.
10. Enter your email address and phone number for follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Process** (`form_2_page`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the business process that you are monitoring (e.g., Manufacturing, Logistics, Operations, or Finance).
* **Quality Assurance Metrics** (`form_3_page`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the quality assurance metrics that are relevant to your process (e.g., On-time Delivery, On-budget, On-quality, or Other).
* **Process Vulnerabilities** (`form_4_page`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Identify any process vulnerabilities that affect your performance (e.g., Equipment Failure, Human Error, Process Design, or Resource Constraints).
* **Root Cause Analysis** (`form_5_page`, `text`, required: false): Conduct a root cause analysis to understand the underlying causes of issues.
* **Corrective Action Plan** (`form_6_page`, `text`, required: false): Create a corrective action plan to address the root causes.
* **Performance Monitoring** (`form_7_page`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the performance metrics you want to monitor (e.g., Production Time, Cost, Quality, or Other).
* **Performance Target Metrics** (`form_8_page`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Set performance targets for each metric (e.g., Throughput, Lead Time, Inventory, or Other).
* **Performance Target** (`form_9_page`, `number`, required: false): Enter the value of the performance target.
* **Performance Target Value** (`form_10_page`, `text`, required: false): Enter the value of the performance target.
* **Notes** (`form_11_page`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email Address** (`form_12_page`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number** (`form_13_page`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
