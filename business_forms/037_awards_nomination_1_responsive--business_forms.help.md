The "Awards Nomination 1 Responsive" form is designed to facilitate nominations for employee awards within an organization. This form allows users to provide detailed information about the nomination, including the nominator's and nominee's details, the award category, and the reason for nomination. The form is used to recognize and celebrate employees who have demonstrated exceptional performance, leadership, or innovation within the organization.

The form consists of several sections, including Nominator Details, Nominee Details, Award and Honor Selection, and Nomination Details. The fields in this form are designed to guide the user in providing comprehensive information about the nomination, including the nominee's name, department or team, job title, award category, relationship to the nominee, reason for nomination, specific examples of excellence, impact on the organization, nomination scope, date of nomination, and certification of the nomination's basis in professional merit.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Nominator Details**: Enter your name and email address to confirm your identity and facilitate communication.
2.  **Nominee Details**: Provide the full name, department or team, and job title of the person being nominated.
3.  **Award and Honor Selection**: Choose the most appropriate award category that aligns with the nominee's accomplishments.
4.  **Nomination Details**: Describe your professional connection to the nominee, the reason for nomination, specific examples of the nominee's excellence, the impact on the organization, and the scope of the nomination (individual or group).
5.  **Nomination and Certification**: Select the date of nomination and confirm that the nomination is based on professional merit.
6.  **Digital Signature**: Type your full name to sign and confirm the nomination.

## Awards Nomination 1 Responsive - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate the nomination process for employee awards within the organization, recognizing exceptional performance, leadership, or innovation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nominator Full Name** (`nominator_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the nominator.
* **Nominator Email Address** (`nominator_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for confirmation and follow-up.
* **Nominator Department or Team** (`nominator_department`, text, required): Indicate the department or team you are associated with as the nominator.
* **Nominee Full Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Department or Team** (`nominee_department`, text, required): Indicate the department or team the nominee is associated with.
* **Nominee Job Title** (`nominee_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the nominee.
* **Award Category** (`award_category_select`, select_one, required): Choose the most appropriate award category that aligns with the nominee's accomplishments.
* **Relationship to Nominee** (`nominator_relationship`, select_one, required): Describe your professional connection to the nominee.
* **Detailed Reason for Nomination** (`nomination_reason`, text, required): Provide a detailed reason for nominating the person.
* **Specific Examples of Excellence** (`specific_examples`, text, required): Provide 1-2 specific examples of the nominee's excellence.
* **Impact on Organization** (`organizational_impact`, text, required): Describe the impact of the nominee's work on the organization.
* **Nomination Scope** (`nomination_scope`, select_one, required): Indicate whether this nomination is for an individual or a group.
* **Date of Nomination** (`nomination_date`, date, required): Enter the date of nomination.
* **Nomination Certification** (`nomination_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that this nomination is based on professional merit.
* **Digital Signature** (`nomination_signature`, text, required): Type your full name to sign and confirm the nomination.
