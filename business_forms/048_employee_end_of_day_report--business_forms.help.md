# Employee End Of Day Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees provide a detailed report of their work progress, task completion status, and any notes or comments related to their work for the end of each day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Employee Name** in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your **Employee ID** in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Select the **End Date** of your workday from the calendar dropdown.
4. Write any **Notes** about your work progress in the "Notes" field.
5. Indicate the **Comments** for the day's work in the "Comments" field.
6. Select your current **Employee Status** (Active or Inactive) in the "Employee Status" field.
7. Enter the number of **Completed Tasks** for the day in the "Completed Tasks" field.
8. Select the **Task Status** (Active or Inactive) of the tasks assigned to you in the "Task Status" field.
9. Indicate whether tasks are **Assigned To** you (Yes or No) in the "Assigned To" field.
10. Enter the **End Time** of your workday from the clock.
11. Write any **Notes for Manager** in the "Notes for Manager" field.
12. Enter the number of **Employee Hours** worked on tasks in the "Employee Hours" field.
13. Enter the **Time Spent on Task** in the "Time Spent on Task" field.
14. Enter the total **Time Spent** on tasks in the "Total Time Spent" field.
15. Select the **Task Completion Status** (Active or Inactive) of the tasks completed in the "Task Completion Status" field.
16. Write any **Task Completion Comments** in the "Task Completion Comments" field.
17. Write any **Task Completion Notes** in the "Task Completion Notes" field.
18. Write any **Employee Completion Notes** in the "Employee Completion Notes" field.
19. Write any **Employee Completion Comments** in the "Employee Completion Comments" field.
20. Select the **Task Completion Status** (Active or Inactive) of the task completion in the "Task Completion Status" field.
21. Write any **Notes for Employee** in the "Notes for Employee" field.
22. Select the **Manager Status** (Active or Inactive) in the "Manager Status" field.
23. Enter the number of **Completed Tasks Count** in the "Completed Tasks Count" field.
