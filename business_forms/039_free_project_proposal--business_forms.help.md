# free_project_proposal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The free_project_proposal form is used to collect and present information for a business project proposal. It helps project managers create a structured format for gathering project details and stakeholder information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a title for the project.
2. Choose the current status of the project (Active or Inactive).
3. Enter the name of the stakeholder(s) involved in the project.
4. Specify the position(s) of the stakeholder(s).
5. Enter the department(s) of the stakeholder(s).
6. Enter the email address of the stakeholder(s).
7. Enter the phone number of the stakeholder(s).
8. Add any additional notes about the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description or title of the project.
- **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the project (Active or Inactive).
- **Stakeholder Name** (`stakeholder_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the stakeholder(s) involved in the project.
- **Stakeholder Position** (`stakeholder_position`, text, optional): Specify the position(s) of the stakeholder(s).
- **Stakeholder Department** (`stakeholder_department`, text, optional): Enter the department(s) of the stakeholder(s).
- **Stakeholder Email** (`stakeholder_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the stakeholder(s).
- **Stakeholder Phone** (`stakeholder_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the stakeholder(s).
- **Stakeholder Notes** (`stakeholder_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the project.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
- Use the correct format for email addresses and phone numbers.
- If multiple stakeholders are involved, separate their information with commas.
- Review and update the form regularly to ensure that project information is up-to-date.
