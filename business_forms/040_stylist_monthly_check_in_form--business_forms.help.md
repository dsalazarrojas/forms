# Stylist Monthly Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from stylists about their monthly activities, services rendered, revenue earned, and next check-in details. It helps management track progress, identify trends, and provide valuable insights for future planning and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the month for which you want to report your activities and earnings.
2. Enter your total services rendered, including all appointments, sessions, and other services provided.
3. Check the box if you rendered any services for that month (Step 3 is not applicable if not rendered services).
4. Enter the total revenue earned from services rendered.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your activities and progress.
6. Indicate whether you will have a follow-up with the client (yes/no).
7. Choose the next check-in date and time.
8. Enter any comments or notes for the follow-up (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Month (1)**: Enter the correct month (e.g., January, February, etc.).
- **Total Services (2)**: Enter the total number of services rendered, including all appointments, sessions, and other services provided.
- **Services Rendered (3)**: Check this box if you rendered any services for that month.
- **Revenue (4)**: Enter the total revenue earned from services rendered.
- **Notes (5)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your activities and progress.
- **Follow-up (6)**: Indicate whether you will have a follow-up with the client (yes/no).
- **Next Check-in (7)**: Choose the date for the next check-in with the client.
- **Next Check-in Time (8)**: Enter the time for the next check-in with the client.
- **Follow-up Comments (9)**: Enter any comments or notes for the follow-up (optional).
