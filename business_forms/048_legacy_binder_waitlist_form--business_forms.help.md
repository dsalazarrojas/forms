# Legacy Binder Waitlist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legacy Binder Waitlist Form is used to submit information for a waitlist for a specific legacy binder. This form is used to collect contact information, waitlist dates and times, and waitlist lengths for each section of the binder.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page of the form with the necessary information.
2. Make sure to fill out the correct page for each section of the binder.
3. If you have multiple sections of the binder, you will need to fill out multiple pages.
4. Be sure to fill out all required fields for each page.
5. Review your form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_info** (text, optional):

* **phone_number** (text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number for this section of the binder.
* **email** (email, optional):

* **waitlist_time** (time, optional):

* **waitlist_length** (text, optional):

* **waitlist_length_type** (select_one, optional):
	+ Select the type of waitlist length.
