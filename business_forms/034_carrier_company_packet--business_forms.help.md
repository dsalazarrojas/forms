# Carrier Company Packet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Carrier Company Packet form is a crucial document for carriers to provide accurate and up-to-date information. It ensures that all necessary details are collected for smooth communication, payment processing, and verification of insurance and safety ratings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information for your company.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Review and agree to the Broker-Carrier Agreement.
4. Confirm non-solicitation and non-compete clauses.
5. Choose alternative payment terms if applicable.
6. Verify insurance requirements, including cargo and auto liability.
7. Select preferred payment method and provide necessary bank details (if ACH selected).
8. Confirm agreement to quick pay and 2% processing fee (if quick pay selected).
9. Declare a satisfactory safety rating.
10. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`Company Name`, required, text): Enter your company's name as it appears on official documents.
* **DOT and MC Number** (`DOT and MC Number`, required, text): Enter your company's DOT and MC numbers as registered with the FMCSA.
* **Authorized Representative** (`Authorized Representative`, required, text): Enter the name of the authorized representative for your company.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, required, email): Enter your company's email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Contact Information** (`Authorized Representative`, required, text): Enter your contact information, including phone number and physical address.
* **Broker-Carrier Agreement** (`Broker-Carrier Agreement`, optional, note): Review the attached contract and agree to its terms.
* **I have read and agree to the Broker-Carrier Agreement** (`I have read and agree to the Broker-Carrier Agreement`, required, select_one): Confirm that you have read and agree to the broker-carrier agreement.
* **I acknowledge the non-solicitation and non-compete clauses** (`I acknowledge the non-solicitation and non-compete clauses`, required, select_one): Confirm that you understand and acknowledge the non-solicitation and non-compete clauses.
* **I agree to the standard Net 30 payment terms** (`I agree to the standard Net 30 payment terms`, required, select_one): Choose whether you agree to the standard net 30 payment terms or select alternative terms.
* **Documentation Checklist** (`Documentation Checklist`, optional, note): List the necessary documentation to be submitted with this packet.
* **W-9 form provided?** (`W-9 form provided?`, required, select_one): Confirm if you have provided the W-9 form.
* **COI (Certificate of Insurance) provided?** (`COI (Certificate of Insurance) provided?`, required, select_one): Confirm if you have provided the COI.
* **Operating Authority letter provided?** (`Operating Authority letter provided?`, required, select_one): Confirm if you have provided the operating authority letter.
* **Cargo Insurance (Min 100,000 dollars) verified?** (`Cargo Insurance (Min 100,000 dollars) verified?`, required, select_one): Confirm if your cargo insurance meets the minimum requirement.
* **Auto Liability (Min 1,000,000 dollars) verified?** (`Auto Liability (Min 1,000,000 dollars) verified?`, required, select_one): Confirm if your auto liability insurance meets the minimum requirement.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`Preferred Payment Method`, required, select_one): Choose your preferred payment method (ACH, Check, or QuickPay).
* **Bank Name and Account or Routing (if ACH selected)** (`Bank Name and Account or Routing (if ACH selected)`, optional, text): Enter your bank details for ACH payments.
* **If QuickPay, I agree to the 2 percent processing fee** (`If QuickPay, I agree to the 2 percent processing fee`, required, select_one): Confirm if you agree to the 2% processing fee for QuickPay.
* **Satisfactory Safety Declaration** (`Satisfactory Safety Declaration`, required, select_one): Declare your satisfactory safety rating.
* **Authorized Digital Signature** (`Authorized Digital Signature`, required, text): Type your name as your digital signature.
* **Date of Signature** (`Date of Signature`, required, date): Enter the date you sign the form.

**Tips**

* Make sure to review and agree to all required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure accuracy and completeness of all information provided.
* Double-check insurance and safety ratings to avoid errors.
* Confirm preferred payment method and provide necessary details.
* Review the Broker-Carrier Agreement and acknowledge its terms.
* Ensure all required documentation is submitted with the packet.
* Confirm non-solicitation and non-compete clauses understanding.
* Review and agree to payment terms and processing fees.
* Declare a satisfactory safety rating.
* Sign and date the form accurately.
