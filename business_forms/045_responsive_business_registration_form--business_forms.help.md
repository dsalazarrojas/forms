# Responsive Business Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive information about businesses for easy access and reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following fields with accurate and up-to-date information:

- **Business Type**: Choose one of the two options to select the type of your business.
- **Contact Information**: Enter your business's contact information, including address, phone number, fax number, email, and website.
- **Social Media Presence**: Provide your business's social media handles.
- **Owner Information**: Enter your name and any additional notes or descriptions.
- **Form Submitter Information**: Enter your name, email, phone number, website, and any additional notes or descriptions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Type
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of your business from the provided options to help categorize and differentiate your business.

### Business Address
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter your business's physical address for easy reference.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your business's contact information, including any relevant details such as phone numbers, fax numbers, and email addresses.

### Logo
* **Logo** (`logo`, text, required): Upload a logo representing your business for easy recognition.

### Banner Image
* **Banner Image** (`banner_image`, text, required): Upload a banner image representing your business for easy reference.

### Business Name
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business's name for easy identification.

### Owner Information
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the owner of the business.

### Form Submitter Information
* **Form Submitter** (`form_submitter`, text, required): Enter your name and role in submitting this form.
* **Form Submitter Email** (`form_submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for easy contact.
* **Form Submitter Phone** (`form_submitter_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Form Submitter Website** (`form_submitter_website`, text, required): Enter your website for easy reference.
* **Form Submitter Notes** (`form_submitter_notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes or descriptions about your submission.
* **Form Submitter Social Media** (`form_submitter_social_media`, text, required): Enter your social media handles for easy reference.
* **Form Submitter Description** (`form_submitter_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your submission.
* **Form Submitter Logo** (`form_submitter_logo`, text, required): Upload a logo representing you or your business.
* **Form Submitter Banner Image** (`form_submitter_banner_image`, text, required): Upload a banner image representing you or your business.
