# senate_finance_committee_proposal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to propose new finance-related projects to the Senate Finance Committee. It gathers information about the proposed projects in a structured and standardized way, ensuring consistency and comparability across proposals.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out the form, please make sure to have all the necessary information ready, including:

- A clear and concise title for your proposal
- A detailed description of the project
- The requested budget and start and end dates
- A clear explanation of the amount requested
- A justification for your request
- Contact information for the person responsible
- Department information
- Project details
- A final recommendation from the committee

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Please enter a clear and concise title for your proposal.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a detailed description of your proposal, including the project goals and objectives.
* **Budget Request** (`budget_request`, `number`, required: false): Please enter the requested budget for your project.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Please enter the start date of your project.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Please enter the end date of your project.
* **Amount Requested** (`amount_request`, `number`, required: false): Please enter the amount requested for your project.
* **Justification** (`justification`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a clear explanation of your request.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for your project.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the department responsible for your project.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, required: false): Please enter any additional details about your project.
* **Committee Recommendation** (`committee_recommendation`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a final recommendation from the committee.

Please note that all fields are non-mandatory, meaning that you can skip any of them if you do not have the necessary information. However, please try to fill out as much of the form as possible to ensure a thorough review and evaluation of your proposal.
