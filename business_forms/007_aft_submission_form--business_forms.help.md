# AFT Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for submitting requests for Automated Funds Transfer (AFT) to our department. Please complete the form with accurate and detailed information about your request, including your contact details, the nature of the AFT, and any supporting documentation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Submitter Details**: Enter your full name, email address, and organization name to identify yourself and your request.
2. **Request Information**: Select the type of AFT request you are submitting, such as a new setup, modification, cancellation, or status inquiry.
3. **Request Details**: Enter the requested effective date for the AFT, the account/customer reference number, and the currency type for the transfer.
4. **Supporting Documentation**: Confirm that you have the required supporting documentation ready to upload.
5. **Upload Documents**: Upload the supporting documents, including the specified types of documents, such as authorization forms, voided checks, contracts/agreements, invoices, and tax documentation.
6. **Urgency Level**: Indicate the urgency level of your request, which may require additional justification if selected as "High".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Organization Name**: Enter your organization's name to identify your affiliation.
* **Request Type**: Select the nature of your AFT request from the options provided.
* **Requested Effective Date**: Enter the date when you want the AFT to take effect.
* **Account/Customer Reference Number**: Enter the relevant account/customer reference number for the AFT.
* **Currency**: Select the currency type for the transfer, such as USD, EUR, or GBP.
* **Transfer Frequency**: Choose the frequency of the transfer, including one-time, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually.
* **Total Transfer Amount**: Enter the total amount for the transfer, if applicable.
* **Description and Justification**: Provide a detailed explanation and justification for the AFT request.
* **Supporting Documentation**: Confirm that you have the required supporting documentation ready for upload.
* **Upload Supporting Documents**: Upload all relevant files, such as PDF, JPG, or PNG, with a maximum size of 10MB.
* **Urgency Level**: Indicate the urgency level of your request, which may require additional justification if selected as "High".
* **Internal Tracking Code**: Enter an internal tracking code for administrative use, if applicable.

Please make sure to complete all required fields accurately and upload all necessary supporting documentation to ensure a smooth submission process.
