# Market Access Innovation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about market access innovation proposals, including review processes, proposal details, support needs, data collection, and contact details. Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to facilitate the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*).
3. Select the relevant options for each field where applicable.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Innovation Proposals** (`innovation_proposals`, text, optional): This field is for describing your innovation proposal. Please provide a detailed description of your proposal, including any relevant information, such as its purpose, goals, and expected outcomes.
* **Review Process** (`review_process`, text, optional): This field is for explaining the review process you have chosen or would like to implement. Please be specific about how your proposal will be reviewed, including any relevant details about the review team, timelines, and criteria.
* **Proposal Details** (`proposal_details`, select_one, optional): This field is for selecting the details of your proposal. Please choose one of the available options to describe your proposal accurately.
* **Support Needs** (`support_needs`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for selecting the support needs for your proposal. Please choose multiple options that accurately describe the support you require.
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, time, optional): This field is for specifying the data collection method. Please select a valid time range for your data collection, if applicable.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): This field is for providing your organization's name. Please enter your organization's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_one, optional): This field is for selecting the contact person for your proposal. Please choose one of the available options to ensure that we have the correct contact person.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): This field is for providing your email address. Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This field is for providing your phone number. Please enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This field is for leaving any additional notes or comments about your proposal.
* **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, note, optional): This field is for leaving any additional review notes or comments about your proposal.
