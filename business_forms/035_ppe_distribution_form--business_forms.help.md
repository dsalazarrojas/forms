# PPE Distribution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track the distribution of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the top section of the form.
2. Select the relevant details about the PPE, such as the role it was distributed to, the team ID, and the item ID.
3. Enter the date the PPE was issued and distributed.
4. Choose whether the PPE was distributed to or not.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the distribution process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Fill in your name as the person responsible for completing the form.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the role that the PPE was distributed to (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Team ID** (`team_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the ID of the team that the PPE is being distributed to.
* **Item ID** (`item_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter the ID of the item being distributed.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the serial number of the PPE being distributed.
* **Date Issued** (`date_issued`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the PPE was issued.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where the PPE was distributed.
* **Date Distributed** (`date_distributed`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the PPE was distributed.
* **Distributed To** (`distributed_to`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose whether the PPE was distributed to or not (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the distribution process.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all relevant information is complete and accurate before submitting the form.
* This form is intended to be used for new distributions only; if the PPE is being returned or transferred, please use the appropriate equipment management process.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please consult with the relevant authorities or seek additional guidance.
