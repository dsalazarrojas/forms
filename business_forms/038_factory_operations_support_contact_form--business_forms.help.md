# Factory Operations Support Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Operations Support Contact Form is a tool for factory operations staff to report issues or request support from the support team. This form helps the support team to quickly identify and address these issues, ensuring a smooth operation of the factory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the issue you are experiencing (Operations, Production, Maintenance, Quality Control, or Other).
2. Describe the issue in the space provided.
3. Enter your contact information (email address and phone number).
4. Select the person to whom the issue should be assigned (John, Jane, or Bob).
5. Provide the date and time the issue occurred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: Select the category of issue you are experiencing.
  - Operations: Issues related to factory operations, such as equipment malfunction or production delays.
  - Production: Issues related to production, such as defects or quality control.
  - Maintenance: Issues related to maintenance, such as equipment repair or replacement.
  - Quality Control: Issues related to quality control, such as product defects or non-compliance.
  - Other: Issues not related to the above categories.
* **Issue Description**: Describe the issue you are experiencing in a few sentences.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your email address and phone number so that we can contact you for further information.
* **Assigned To**: Select the person to whom the issue should be assigned to ensure timely resolution.
* **Issue Date**: Provide the date the issue occurred.
* **Issue Time**: Provide the time the issue occurred.

## Tips
* Use the correct category to ensure that your issue is reviewed by the right person.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the issue.
* Ensure that you enter a valid email address and phone number.
* If the issue is urgent, please mark it as such and we will respond as soon as possible.
