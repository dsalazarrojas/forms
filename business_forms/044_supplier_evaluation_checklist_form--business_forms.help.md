# Supplier Evaluation Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate and document the quality and performance of a supplier. It helps to identify areas of improvement and maintain a record of evaluations for future references.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the supplier's name in the "Supplier Name" field.
2. Select the evaluation date from the calendar.
3. Provide the name and contact information of the contact person for the supplier.
4. Enter the supplier's email address.
5. Enter the supplier's phone number.
6. Enter the supplier's address.
7. Select the evaluation criteria that apply (e.g., "Criteria 1", "Criteria 2", etc.).
8. Enter your evaluation comment for the supplier.
9. Enter the score for the supplier's performance.
10. Provide a brief reason for the score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (id 1, text, optional): Enter the name of the supplier being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date** (id 2, date, optional): Select the date when the evaluation was performed.
* **Contact Person** (id 3, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who will be contacted for further information.
* **Contact Email** (id 4, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (id 5, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Supplier Address** (id 6, text, optional): Enter the supplier's address.
* **Evaluation Criteria** (id 7, multi-select, optional): Select the criteria that apply for the evaluation (e.g., "Criteria 1", "Criteria 2", etc.).
* **Evaluation Comment** (id 8, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the supplier.
* **Score** (id 9, number, optional): Enter a score between 1 and 5 to evaluate the supplier's performance.
* **Reason for Score** (id 10, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for the score given.
