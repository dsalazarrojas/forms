# Field Service Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for business field service operations to document and report on service events. It captures essential information about the service provided, customer interaction, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the address of the location where the service was provided.
2. Select the date and time of the service.
3. Enter the customer's name and any relevant contact information.
4. Choose the service provided from the options listed.
5. Add any additional notes about the service.
6. Enter the customer's phone number.
7. Enter the customer's email address.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Street Address**: Enter the physical address where the service was provided.
* **Date**: Enter the date the service was provided.
* **Time**: Enter the time the service was provided.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the person or business that received the service.
* **Customer Phone Number**: Enter the customer's phone number for future reference.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address for future reference.
* **Service Provided**: Select the specific service provided to the customer from the options listed.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the service provided.
* **Service Provided Options**: Select one of the options to indicate the type of service provided.
