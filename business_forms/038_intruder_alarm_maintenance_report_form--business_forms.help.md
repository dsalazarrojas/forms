# intruder_alarm_maintenance_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The intruder alarm maintenance report form is designed to collect and track information about the maintenance and testing of intruder alarm systems in a business setting. This form helps users to report on the current status, test results, and maintenance history of the alarm systems, ensuring that they are functioning correctly and meeting the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have reviewed and understand the form's purpose and fields.
2. For each field, provide accurate and detailed information about the intruder alarm system being reported on.
3. Fill out all fields with relevant information, and make sure to answer all required fields.
4. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check the intruder alarm system** (`intruder_alarm_system_check`, `text`, required): Check if the intruder alarm system is functioning correctly and working as expected.
* **What signals does the intruder alarm system send when it detects an intruder?** (`alarm_signals`, `text`, required): Describe the different signals or alarms that the system sends when an intruder is detected.
* **What is the volume level of the intruder alarm system?** (`alarm_volume_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of volume of the alarm system, e.g., Low, Medium, High, or Silent.
* **Is the intruder alarm system currently active?** (`system_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether the system is currently active or not.
* **What is the status of the intruder alarm test?** (`alarm_test_status`, `select_one`, not required): Choose whether the test was passed, failed, or unknown.
* **Is the intruder alarm system due for maintenance?** (`maintenance_schedule`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the system is due for maintenance.
* **What type of intruder alarm system is being used?** (`system_type`, `text`, required): Describe the type of alarm system being used, e.g., Intruder Alarm 1, Intruder Alarm 2, etc.
* **What is the frequency of the intruder alarm signals?** (`alarm_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose the frequency of the alarm signals, e.g., Low, Medium, High.
* **What is the sensitivity level of the intruder alarm system?** (`alarm_sensitivity`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of sensitivity of the alarm system, e.g., Low, Medium, High.
* **When was the last maintenance performed on the intruder alarm system?** (`maintenance_last_performed`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the last maintenance performed.
* **Describe the intruder alarm system and its location** (`alarm_system_description`, `text`, required): Describe the system and its location in the building.
* **What is the description of the intruder alarm test result?** (`alarm_test_result_description`, `text`, required): Describe the result of the test, e.g., passed, failed, etc.
* **Was the intruder alarm test passed?** (`alarm_test_result_passed`, `select_one`, not required): Choose whether the test was passed or not.
* **What is the date of last maintenance?** (`last_maintenance_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the last maintenance.
* **When was the intruder alarm system last tested?** (`system_last_tested`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the last test.
* **Who performed the last maintenance on the intruder alarm system?** (`last_maintenance_by`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the person who performed the last maintenance.
* **What type of intruder alarm is being used?** (`alarm_type`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the type of alarm being used, e.g., Intruder Alarm 1, Intruder Alarm 2, etc.
* **What is the frequency of the intruder alarm test?** (`alarm_test_frequency`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the frequency of the test, e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly.
* **Describe the intruder alarm system and its location** (`alarm_system_description`, `text`, required): Describe the system and its location in the building.
* **Describe the status of the intruder alarm system** (`alarm_system_status_description`, `text`, required): Describe the current status of the system.
* **What is the date of the next test?** (`test_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the next test.
* **What is the status of the next test?** (`test_status`, `text`, required): Describe the status of the next test, e.g., pending, completed, etc.
* **What is the status of the next alarm test frequency?** (`alarm_test_frequency_status`, `text`, required): Describe the status of the next test frequency.
* **What is the status of the last alarm test result?** (`alarm_test_result_status`, `text`, required): Describe the status of the last test result.
* **Who performed the last test?** (`last_tested_by`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who performed the last test.

## Tips
