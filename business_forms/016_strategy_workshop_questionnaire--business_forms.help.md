# Strategy Workshop Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information about a strategy workshop session, enabling effective planning and organization of such events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields according to your needs.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information in each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all necessary information is included.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Name** (Session Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the strategy workshop session you are planning.
* **Stakeholders** (Stakeholders, select_multiple, required/optional): List all stakeholders attending the session, such as team members, clients, or external partners.
* **Objective** (Objective, text, required/optional): Describe the main objective of the session, including the desired outcome and any specific goals.
* **Session Date** (Session Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the session, in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
* **Session Time** (Session Time, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the session, in the format "HH:mmA".
* **Expected Outcome** (Expected Outcome, text, required/optional): Describe the expected outcome of the session, including any specific results or achievements.
* **Actual Outcome** (Actual Outcome, text, required/optional): Describe the actual outcome of the session, including any notable events or results.
* **Recommendations** (Recommendations, text, required/optional): List any recommendations or suggestions made during or after the session.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, required/optional): Provide any additional information or comments about the session.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and detailed information in each field to ensure the success and effectiveness of the session.
