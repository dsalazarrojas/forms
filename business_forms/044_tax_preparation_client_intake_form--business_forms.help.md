# Tax Preparation Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Preparation Client Intake Form is used to collect information from clients about their tax preparation needs. This form is used to provide details about a client's business or personal situation, which is then used to prepare their taxes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes your relationship with the tax preparation services (Client Information, Business Information, Tax Filing, Financial Information, or Tax Preparation).
2. Enter your contact information (contact number and/or email).
3. Provide your client name.
4. Specify the type of business you have (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, or Corporation).
5. Select the tax year for which you need tax preparation services.
6. Provide your business address and other related business contact information (city, state, postal code, country, phone, and fax).
7. Provide your personal address and other related personal contact information (city, state, postal code, country, phone, and fax).
8. Specify the financial institution where your business or personal account is held (financial institution).
9. Enter the routing slip number and account number of your business or personal account (routing slip number and bank account number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **category**: Select the category that best describes your relationship with the tax preparation services.
	+ Client Information: For individual clients.
	+ Business Information: For business clients.
	+ Tax Filing: For tax filing services.
	+ Financial Information: For financial information related to tax preparation.
	+ Tax Preparation: For tax preparation services.
* **contact_number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **client_name**: Enter your name.
* **business_type**: Specify the type of business you have (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, or Corporation).
* **tax_year**: Select the tax year for which you need tax preparation services.
* **business_address**: Enter your business address.
* **business_city**: Enter your business city.
* **business_state**: Enter your business state.
* **business_postal_code**: Enter your business postal code.
* **business_country**: Enter your business country.
* **business_phone**: Enter your business phone number.
* **business_fax**: Enter your business fax number.
* **personal_address**: Enter your personal address.
* **personal_city**: Enter your personal city.
* **personal_state**: Enter your personal state.
* **personal_postal_code**: Enter your personal postal code.
* **personal_country**: Enter your personal country.
* **personal_phone**: Enter your personal phone number.
* **personal_fax**: Enter your personal fax number.
* **financial_institution**: Specify the financial institution where your business or personal account is held.
* **routing_slip_number**: Enter the routing slip number of your business or personal account.
* **bank_acount_number**: Enter the bank account number of your business or personal account.
* **account_holder_name**: Enter the name of the account holder.
