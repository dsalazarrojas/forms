# 21 Day Wellness Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 21 Day Wellness Release Form is designed to collect personal information and medical history from individuals participating in the 21-day wellness program. This form will help us understand your needs and provide a safe and successful experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully and answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
- Ensure you answer all required fields (marked as required).
- If you have any doubts or concerns, refer to the field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`Full name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal first and last name.
* **Email address** (`Email address`, `email`, required): Type the email address we'll use to send you event updates.
* **Phone number** (`Phone number`, `phone`, optional): Include the country code if outside the U.S.
* **Date of birth** (`Date of birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth for age verification.
* **Age** (`Age`, `integer`, optional): This field will be auto-calculated if you enter your date of birth.
* **Mailing address** (`Mailing address`, `text`, optional): Enter your street, city, state, and ZIP.
* **Emergency contact name** (`Emergency contact name`, `text`, optional): Enter the full name of your primary emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`Emergency contact phone`, `phone`, optional): Include the country code if outside the U.S.
* **Experience level** (`Experience level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level that best describes your fitness background:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Professional
* **How did you hear about us?** (`How did you hear about us?`, `select_one`, optional): Select the primary source:
	+ Social media
	+ Friend/Family
	+ Website
	+ Flyer/Poster
	+ Other
* **Program start date** (`Program start date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the 21-day program begins.
* **Program end date** (`Program end date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the 21-day program ends.
* **Known medical conditions** (`Known medical conditions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all conditions that apply:
	+ Diabetes
	+ Hypertension
	+ Asthma
	+ Heart disease
	+ None
* **Current medications** (`Current medications`, `text`, optional): List any prescription or over-the-counter medications.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `text`, optional): Specify any food or medication allergies.
* **Past injuries or surgeries** (`Past injuries or surgeries`, `text`, optional): Provide details if applicable.
* **Consent to participate** (`Consent to participate`, `select_one`, required): By selecting Yes, you agree to take part in the program.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Release of liability** (`Release of liability`, `select_one`, required): Agree to release the organizers from liability.
	+ Agree
	+ Disagree
* **Electronic signature** (`Electronic signature`, `text`, required): Type your full name as your signature.
* **Any special requirements or accommodations?** (`Any special requirements or accommodations?`, `text`, optional): For example, dietary restrictions or mobility needs.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`Additional comments or notes`, `text`, optional): Share anything else that might help us support you.

Please note that some fields have specific instructions or explanations to ensure accurate and safe participation in the program. Refer to these explanations if you have any doubts or concerns.
