# Data Strategy Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Data Strategy Review Request Form to help you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the form title that clearly represents the request for data strategy review in the "Form Title" field.
2. In the "Form Category" field, enter the category of the business or industry that the form is related to.
3. Provide a brief and accurate description of the request in the "Form Description" field.
4. Enter the name of the business or company that the form is related to.
5. Enter the location of the business or company.
6. Enter the name of the contact person for the data strategy review request.
7. List all the data sources related to the request.
8. List all the data types related to the request.
9. Describe the quality of the data requested.
10. Describe the goals of the data strategy review in the "Data Strategy" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a concise and accurate representation of the request for data strategy review.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, text, required/optional): Enter the category of the business or industry that the form is related to.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief and accurate description of the request.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the business or company.
* **Data Manager** (`data_manager`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact information of the person who will handle the data strategy review request.
* **Business Location** (`business_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the business or company.
* **Data Sources** (`data_sources`, text, required/optional): List all the data sources related to the request.
* **Data Types** (`data_types`, text, required/optional): List all the data types related to the request.
* **Data Quality** (`data_quality`, text, required/optional): Describe the quality of the data requested.
* **Data Strategy** (`data_strategy`, text, required/optional): Describe the goals of the data strategy review.
