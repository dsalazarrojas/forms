# policy_compliance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "policy_compliance_form" is a form used to ensure that employees and stakeholders understand and agree to company policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the policy compliance form carefully and ensure you have understood the policies and procedures outlined.
2. Fill out the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review the provided options and select the correct choices for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title** (1, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the policy being reviewed or agreed upon.
* **policy_compliance_agreement** (2, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the policy agreements that apply to you. Please note that this field allows for multiple selections.
* **review_and_sign** (3, note, required/optional): This field is intended for you to add any additional comments or notes about the policy compliance review. You can type in any relevant information or concerns you have.
* **review_page** (4, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the reviewed policy.

## Tips
* Please review the provided policy documents carefully before completing this form.
* Ensure that you understand the policies and procedures outlined before agreeing to them.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the policies, please reach out to the relevant department or contact person for clarification.
* Please note that incomplete or inaccurate submissions may lead to delays or issues with policy compliance.
