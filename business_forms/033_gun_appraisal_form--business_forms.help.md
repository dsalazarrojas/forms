# gun-appraisal-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gun-appraisal-form is designed to collect comprehensive data about a gun, including its general information, description, and other relevant details. This form is crucial for maintaining accurate records and inventory of guns in various collections or stores.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the gun, such as its description and serial numbers.
2. Specify the gun's condition, materials, and caliber.
3. Add any notable features and details about the gun.
4. Enter the gun's manufacturer and model.
5. Specify the year of manufacture and approximate age of the gun.
6. Determine if the gun is in working condition and if it's complete.
7. Classify the gun as a firearm or replica.
8. Specify if the gun is in working or non-working condition.
9. Indicate if the gun is safe or not.
10. Specify if the gun is in a collection and its value.
11. Determine if the gun is in inventory and its value.
12. Add any additional notes about the gun.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Provide a brief description of the gun, including its type, caliber, and any other relevant details.
* **Gun Description**: Enter a detailed description of the gun, including its materials, features, and notable characteristics.
* **Gun Serial Numbers**: Enter the serial number of the gun, if available.
* **Gun Condition**: Specify the gun's condition, including any repairs or maintenance needed.
* **Gun Materials**: Specify the materials used to make the gun.
* **Gun Caliber**: Enter the gun's caliber.
* **Gun Features**: Enter any notable features of the gun, such as its weight, length, or other distinguishing characteristics.
* **Gun Manufacturer**: Enter the gun's manufacturer.
* **Gun Model**: Enter the gun's model.
* **Gun Year of Manufacture**: Enter the year the gun was manufactured.
* **Gun Approximate Age**: Estimate the gun's approximate age.
* **Gun Approximate Value**: Enter the gun's approximate value.
* **Gun Is In Working Condition**: Determine if the gun is in working condition.
* **Gun Is Complete**: Determine if the gun is complete.
* **Gun Is Firearm**: Classify the gun as a firearm or replica.
* **Gun Is Antique**: Classify the gun as antique or not.
* **Gun Is Firearm or Replica**: Classify the gun as a firearm or replica.
* **Gun Is Working or Non-Working**: Determine if the gun is working or non-working.
* **Gun Is Safe**: Determine if the gun is safe or not.
* **Gun Is In Collection**: Determine if the gun is in a collection.
* **Gun Collection Name**: Enter the name of the collection the gun is in.
* **Gun Collection Value**: Enter the value of the gun in the collection.
* **Gun Is In Inventory**: Determine if the gun is in inventory.
* **Gun Inventory Value**: Enter the value of the gun in inventory.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the gun.

Note: This form is designed to be self-explanatory, so it's essential to read each field carefully and fill it out accordingly. If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the field's label for guidance.
