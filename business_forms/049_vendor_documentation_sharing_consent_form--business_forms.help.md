# Vendor Documentation Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect vendor-specific information to ensure that the company has the necessary permissions to share documents with them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter the vendor's company name**: Type the vendor's company name in the field provided.
2. **Select the vendor type**: Choose the type of vendor you are (Yes or No).
3. **Provide your email address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
4. **Enter your phone number**: Type your phone number so we can reach you.
5. **Enter your address**: Provide your business address for contact purposes.
6. **Select document type to share**: Choose the types of documents you are willing to share (Yes or No).
7. **Select who to share documents with**: Choose who you want to share documents with (Yes or No).
8. **Add any additional notes**: Enter any relevant comments or notes about your business operations.
9. **Select if you want to be assigned**: Choose if you want to be assigned a specific person to contact (Yes or No).
10. **Select the assigned person**: Choose who should be assigned to handle your documentation (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Type the vendor's name.
* **Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of vendor you are (Yes or No).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Type your phone number so we can reach you.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide your business address for contact purposes.
* **Document Type** (`document_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the types of documents you are willing to share (Yes or No).
* **Share With** (`share_with`, select_multiple, optional): Choose who you want to share documents with (Yes or No).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any relevant comments or notes about your business operations.
* **Assignee** (`assignee`, select_one, optional): Choose if you want to be assigned a specific person to contact (Yes or No).
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Choose who should be assigned to handle your documentation (Yes or No).

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct vendor type to ensure accurate data collection.
* Provide your email address to ensure we can contact you.
* Enter your phone number and business address to enable easy contact.
* Select the correct document types and sharing options to ensure accurate data collection.
