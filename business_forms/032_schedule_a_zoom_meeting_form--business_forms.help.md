# Schedule A Zoom Meeting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a meeting with a registrant using Zoom. It collects meeting details, registrant information, and meeting duration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the meeting details in the "Meeting details" field. This field is a text area where you can provide a brief description of the meeting.
2. Select the meeting date from the calendar or enter it manually in the "Meeting date" field.
3. Choose the meeting time from the time picker or enter it manually in the "Meeting time" field.
4. Enter the registrant's information in the "Registrant information" field. This field is a text area where you can provide any additional details about the registrant.
5. Enter the registrant's name in the "Name" field.
6. Enter the registrant's email address in the "Email" field. This field will help the meeting organizer to follow up with the registrant after the meeting.
7. Enter the registrant's phone number in the "Phone" field. This field is optional but can be useful for further communication.
8. Choose the meeting duration in the "Meeting duration" field. This field is a numeric field where you can select the duration of the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting details**: A text field where you can enter a brief description of the meeting.
* **Meeting date**: The date of the meeting, select from the calendar or enter manually.
* **Meeting time**: The time of the meeting, select from the time picker or enter manually.
* **Registrant information**: A text field where you can enter any additional details about the registrant.
* **Name**: The name of the registrant.
* **Email**: The email address of the registrant. This will help the meeting organizer to follow up with the registrant after the meeting.
* **Phone**: The phone number of the registrant. This field is optional but can be useful for further communication.
* **Meeting duration**: The duration of the meeting. This field is a numeric field where you can select the duration of the meeting.
