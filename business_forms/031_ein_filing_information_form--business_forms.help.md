# EIN Filing Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EIN Filing Information Form is a critical document for businesses to provide accurate and compliant tax information to the Internal Revenue Service (IRS). This form is used to update or modify an existing EIN, ensuring your business information is up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review and fill in the required fields, including business name, address, and contact information.
2. Select the correct EIN application type (Individual, Business, Exempt, or Non-Exempt).
3. Enter the date you want to file for.
4. Provide your business's contact information, including fax and phone numbers.
5. Enter your business's email address.
6. Identify the authorized representative for your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name**: Enter your business's name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Business Legal Name (if different)**: If your business has a different name than your official name, enter that name here.
* **Address**: Enter your business's street address.
* **EIN Application Type**: Select the correct type of EIN application:
	+ Individual: For businesses owned by one or more individuals.
	+ Business: For businesses with more than one owner.
	+ Exempt: For tax-exempt organizations, such as non-profit organizations.
	+ Non-Exempt: For non-profit organizations that are required to file for an EIN.
* **Authorized Representative**: Enter the name of the authorized representative for your business.

## Tips
* Double-check your business information to ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.
* Use this form for EIN updates only; do not use it for initial EIN applications.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult the IRS guidelines or contact your tax advisor.
