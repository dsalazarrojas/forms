# Schedule Drawing Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Schedule Drawing Submission Form is a tool for users to submit schedule drawings to a relevant department or team. It is designed to collect project details and schedule drawings in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in the **Schedule Drawing Submission** field, which is a text area where you can provide a brief description of your schedule drawing.
2.  In the **Project Details** field, provide any additional information about your project that is relevant to the schedule drawing.
3.  If you have multiple schedule drawings, you can upload them in the **Schedule Drawing File**, **Schedule Drawing File 2**, and **Schedule Drawing File 3** fields.
4.  In the **Project Details 2** and **Project Details 3** fields, provide any additional information about your project that is relevant to the schedule drawing.
5.  Finally, in the **Date** and **Time** fields, enter the date and time you are submitting the schedule drawing.
