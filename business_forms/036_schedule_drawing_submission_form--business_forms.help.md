# Schedule Drawing Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Schedule Drawing Submission Form is a tool for users to submit schedule drawings to a relevant department or team. It is designed to collect project details and schedule drawings in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in the **Schedule Drawing Submission** field, which is a text area where you can provide a brief description of your schedule drawing.
2.  In the **Project Details** field, provide any additional information about your project that is relevant to the schedule drawing.
3.  If you have multiple schedule drawings, you can upload them in the **Schedule Drawing File**, **Schedule Drawing File 2**, and **Schedule Drawing File 3** fields.
4.  In the **Project Details 2** and **Project Details 3** fields, provide any additional information about your project that is relevant to the schedule drawing.
5.  Finally, in the **Date** and **Time** fields, enter the date and time you are submitting the schedule drawing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Schedule Drawing Submission**: This field is where you can provide a brief description of your schedule drawing.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: No
*   **Project Details**: This field is for providing any additional information about your project that is relevant to the schedule drawing.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: No
*   **Schedule Drawing File**: This field is for uploading a schedule drawing file.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: No
*   **Schedule Drawing File 2**: This field is for uploading another schedule drawing file.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: No
*   **Project Details 2**: This field is for providing any additional information about your project that is relevant to the schedule drawing.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: No
*   **Schedule Drawing File 3**: This field is for uploading another schedule drawing file.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: No
*   **Project Details 3**: This field is for providing any additional information about your project that is relevant to the schedule drawing.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: No
*   **Date**: This field is for entering the date you are submitting the schedule drawing.
    *   Type: `date`
    *   Required: No
*   **Time**: This field is for entering the time you are submitting the schedule drawing.
    *   Type: `time`
    *   Required: No
