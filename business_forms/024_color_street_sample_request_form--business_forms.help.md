# Color Street Sample Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Color Street Sample Request Form is designed for business partners who want to try out our products and potentially purchase from us in the future. This form will help you request a free sample of our products and provide some basic contact information to ensure the sample is sent to you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Enter your full name in the **Full Name** field.
- Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
- Enter your complete mailing address in the **Complete Mailing Address** field.
- Select the type of sample you're interested in from the **Which sample type would you like to try?** dropdown menu.
- If you're interested, select your **Occupation** to help us understand how our products hold up.
- In the **Who referred you or how did you find us?** field, enter how you heard about Color Street.
- In the **Can I invite you to my Facebook VIP group?** field, select whether you're interested in joining our group.
- Optionally, enter your Facebook name in the **Your Facebook Name (to find you)** field.
- If you're interested, select **True** in the **Are you interested in purchasing full sets in the future?** field.
- Finally, if you have any additional notes, you can enter them in the **Additional Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name**: This is the name that we'll use to address the sample to you.
- **Email**: This is the email address we'll use to contact you with more information about our products and samples.
- **Complete Mailing Address**: This is the address where we'll send the sample to you.
- **Which sample type would you like to try?**: This is where you can select the type of sample you'd like to receive (Solid, Glitter, or Designer Choice).
- **What is your occupation?**: This will help us understand how our products hold up in different types of work environments.
- **Who referred you or how did you find us?**: Please let us know how you heard about Color Street.
- **Can I invite you to my Facebook VIP group?**: This will help us connect with you further and keep you updated on promotions and new products.
- **Your Facebook Name (to find you)**: If you're interested, please enter your Facebook name so we can add you to our group.
- **Are you interested in purchasing full sets in the future?**: This will help us understand your potential future purchasing behavior.
- **Additional Notes**: If you have any additional information you'd like to share with us, please enter it here.
