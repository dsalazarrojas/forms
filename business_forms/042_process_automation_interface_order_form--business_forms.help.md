# Process Automation Interface Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Automation Interface Order Form is a tool used to manage and track orders for automation and integration solutions within your organization. This form collects information about the business, solution, and customer details to ensure efficient order processing and assignment of tasks to the correct team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Business Name** field with the name of the business associated with the order.
2. Enter the **Solution ID** for the automation or integration solution being ordered.
3. Select the **Solution Type** from the options provided (automation or integration).
4. Enter the **Order Date** and **Order Time** for the order.
5. Enter the **Delivery Address** and other relevant customer information, including their **Contact First Name**, **Contact Last Name**, **Contact Email**, and **Contact Phone**.
6. Select the **Payment Method** from the options provided (credit, cash, or other).
7. Enter the **Payment Terms** for the order.
8. Add any additional **Notes** about the order.
9. Assign the task to a specific **Tool** and **Team**.
10. Enter the **Assigned Date** and **Assigned Time** for the task.
11. Select the **Assigned Status** from the options provided (pending, in progress, or completed).
12. Select the **Customer Status** from the options provided (active, inactive, or pending).
13. Enter the **Customer Comments** for any further communication with the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business associated with the order.
* **Solution ID**: Enter the ID for the automation or integration solution being ordered.
* **Solution Type**: Select the type of solution being ordered (automation or integration).
* **Order Date**: Enter the date the order was placed.
* **Order Time**: Enter the time the order was placed.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where the order will be delivered.
* **Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Company**: Enter the company name of the customer.
* **Customer Job Title**: Enter the job title of the customer contact.
* **Customer Department**: Enter the department name of the customer contact.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the address of the customer.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used for the order (credit, cash, or other).
* **Payment Terms**: Enter the terms of payment for the order.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to complete the task.
* **Assigned Team**: Select the team assigned to complete the task.
* **Assigned Date**: Enter the date the task was assigned to.
* **Assigned Time**: Enter the time the task was assigned.
* **Assigned Status**: Select the status of the task (pending, in progress, or completed).
* **Customer Status**: Select the status of the customer (active, inactive, or pending).
* **Customer Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the customer.
