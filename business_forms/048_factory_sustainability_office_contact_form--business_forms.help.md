# Factory Sustainability Office Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for providing contact and company information for the Factory Sustainability Office. It's intended to help us understand more about your company and provide you with a way to contact us. Please provide as much detail as you can.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the Company Name field with your company's name.
2. Enter your Contact Email in the Contact Email field.
3. Enter your Contact Phone number in the Contact Phone field.
4. Provide a message in the Contact Message field explaining your inquiry or question.
5. If applicable, select the Department that you'd like to contact (e.g. Sales, Marketing, etc.).
6. Fill in the Company Address field with your company's address.
7. Enter the Contact Name of the person filling out this form.
8. Select the type of company you represent (Yes/No).
9. Select the location of your company (Yes/No).
10. Enter your Company Website (if applicable).
11. Enter your Contact Number (if applicable).
12. Select whether you have company representatives (Yes/No).
13. Enter the number of employees at your company.
14. Enter your company rating.
15. Enter your company license number (if applicable).
16. Select the industry your company belongs to (Yes/No).
17. Select the contact date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your company's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, note, required): Provide a brief message explaining your inquiry or question.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department you'd like to contact (e.g. Sales, Marketing, etc.).
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter your company's address.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person filling out this form.
* **Company Representative** (`company_representative`, text, optional): Select whether you have a representative (Yes/No).
* **Company Location** (`company_location`, text, optional): Select whether your company is located in the area (Yes/No).
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter your company's website (if applicable).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number (if applicable).
* **Company Representatives** (`company_representatives`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you have company representatives (Yes/No).
* **Number of Employees** (`company_employees`, number, optional): Enter the number of employees at your company.
* **Company Rating** (`company_rating`, number, optional): Enter your company's rating.
* **Company License** (`company_license`, text, optional): Enter your company's license number (if applicable).
* **Company License Number** (`company_license_number`, text, optional): Enter your company's license number (if applicable).
* **Company Industry** (`company_industry`, select_one, optional): Select the industry your company belongs to (Yes/No).
* **Contact Date** (`contact_date`, date, optional): Select the date and time you'd like to contact us.
* **Contact Time** (`contact_time`, time, optional): Select the time you'd like to contact us.
