# B2B Drop Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The B2B Drop Off Form is a standardized form for companies to drop off goods. It helps ensure that all necessary information is collected accurately and efficiently, making the process smooth and error-free.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information for both the sender and receiver companies.
2. Double-check that you have filled out all the required fields accurately.
3. Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sender company (1)** (`Sender company`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the company that is dropping off goods.
* **Receiver company (2)** (`Receiver company`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the company receiving the goods.
* **Contact person at pickup (3)** (`Contact person at pickup`, text, **optional**): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for receiving the goods, if applicable.
* **Contact phone (4)** (`Contact phone`, text, **optional**): Enter the phone number of the contact person, if applicable.
* **Item description (5)** (`Item description`, text, **required**): Provide a detailed description of the items being dropped off, including any relevant information such as serial numbers, models, or quantities.
* **Quantity (6)** (`Quantity`, number, **required**): Enter the number of units being dropped off.
* **Drop off date (7)** (`Drop off date`, date, **required**): Enter the planned date for the drop off, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Condition notes (8)** (`Condition notes`, text, **optional**): Note any pre-existing damage or condition of the items being dropped off.
* **Delivery instructions (9)** (`Delivery instructions`, text, **optional**): Provide instructions for where to leave the items onsite, if applicable.
* **Signature required (10)** (`Signature required`, select_one, **required**): Select "Yes" if the receiver is required to sign upon receipt of goods.

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Ensure that the drop off date is realistic and achievable.
- If unsure about any field, ask for clarification or support from the relevant personnel.
