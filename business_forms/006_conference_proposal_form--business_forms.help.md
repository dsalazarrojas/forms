# Conference Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Proposer Full Name
* **Proposer Full Name** (`proposer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete name as it should be displayed on the conference program and any marketing materials.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email for conference communication.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number in case we need to call you.

### Organization
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company or institution.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your professional title.

### Proposal Title
* **Proposal Title** (`proposal_title`, `text`, required): Enter a title for your proposed session that is clear and descriptive.

### Proposal Type
* **Proposal Type** (`proposal_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of session you are proposing (e.g., Keynote Presentation, Breakout Session, etc.).

### Proposal Abstract
* **Proposal Abstract** (`proposal_abstract`, `text`, required): Write a 300-word abstract describing your proposed session, including what attendees will gain from it.

### Learning Objectives
* **Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`, `text`, required): Describe what attendees will take away from your session.

### Target Audience
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `select_one`, required): Choose who your session is intended for (e.g., Beginners, Intermediate Level, etc.).

### Expected Attendance
* **Expected Attendance** (`expected_attendance`, `select_one`, required): Estimate the number of attendees you expect.

### Session Duration
* **Preferred Session Duration** (`session_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose the length of your proposed session.

### Co-Presenters
* **Co-Presenters** (`co_presenters`, `text`, optional): List the names and affiliations of any co-presenters.

### Audio Visual Requirements
* **Audio Visual Requirements** (`av_requirements`, `select_multiple`, optional): Specify any audio visual equipment you will need.

### Room Setup
* **Preferred Room Setup** (`room_setup`, `select_one`, required): Choose the preferred room arrangement for your session.

### Previous Presentations
* **Have you presented at conferences before** (`previous_presentations`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your speaking experience.

### Travel Support
* **Do you need travel support** (`travel_support_needed`, `select_one`, required): Check if you require funding for travel.

### Special Requirements
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): List any accessibility or other needs you may have.

### Additional Comments
* **Any other information** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information for the committee.

### Submission Agreement
* **I certify this proposal is original and agree to terms** (`submission_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that your proposal is original and agree to the terms and conditions.
