# Brand Development Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Brand Development Appointment Form" is designed to gather essential information from clients interested in scheduling brand development appointments. This form captures contact details, appointment preferences, and project brief information to ensure a smooth and efficient consultation process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2.  Review each field carefully to ensure you provide the required information.
3.  If you have any questions or concerns, refer to this guide for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name**: This is a required field where you'll enter your full name.
* **Company / Brand Name**: This field is also required, where you'll enter the name of your company or brand.
* **Email Address**: This is a required field where you'll enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Preferred Appointment Date**: Choose a date for your appointment from the calendar.
* **Preferred Time Slot**: Select the time slot that suits you best (Morning, Midday, or Afternoon).
* **Meeting Type**: Choose the type of meeting you'd like to have (In-Person, Zoom / Virtual, or Phone Call).
* **Primary Service Interest**: Select the primary service you're interested in (New Brand Identity, Rebranding, Marketing Strategy, Logo Design, or Brand Audit).
* **What are your primary goals for this consultation?**: Describe your primary goals for this consultation in this field.
* **Current Website or Social Media Links**: If applicable, provide links to your website or social media platforms.
* **How did you hear about us?**: Share how you heard about our services.
* **Date Submitted**: This field will be populated automatically, showing the date you submitted the form.

Note: Please ensure that you have provided all the required fields before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
