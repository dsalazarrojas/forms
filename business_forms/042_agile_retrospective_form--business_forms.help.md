<thinking>Agile Retrospective Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Agile Retrospective Form is designed to help teams reflect on their experiences and progress during a sprint or iteration. It's a tool to identify successes, challenges, and areas for improvement, and to document lessons learned for future reference. This form helps teams to:

* Identify what went well and what didn't
* Document lessons learned and key takeaways
* Identify areas for improvement and action items for next sprint

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the sprint or iteration being reviewed
2. Select the date of the retrospective meeting
3. Fill in the team name and facilitator's name
4. Enter the duration of the sprint or iteration
5. For each of the following sections:
	* What percentage of sprint goals were met? (Select the corresponding option)
	* What goals were successfully achieved during the sprint? (Enter text)
	* What goals were not met and why? (Enter text)
	* What practices worked well during the sprint? (Enter text)
	* What individual contributions stood out positively? (Enter text)
	* What challenges did the team face during the sprint? (Enter text)
	* What impediments hindered the team's progress? (Enter text)
	* What key lessons did the team learn during this sprint? (Enter text)
	* What opportunities for improvement were identified? (Enter text)
	* What specific action items will be implemented in the next sprint? (Enter text)
	* Who will be responsible for each action item? (Enter text)
	* When should each action item be completed? (Enter text)
	* Enter any additional comments or observations about the sprint (Enter text)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sprint/Iteration Name** (`sprint_iteration_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the sprint or iteration being reviewed.
* **Retrospective Date** (`retrospective_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the retrospective meeting.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team conducting the retrospective.
* **Facilitator Name** (`facilitator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person facilitating the retrospective.
* **Sprint Duration** (`sprint_duration`, text, required): Enter the duration of the sprint or iteration.
* **-- Sprint Goals Review --** (`sprint_goals_review_header`, note, required): This section is for reviewing the sprint goals.
	+ **Sprint Goals Met** (`sprint_goals_met`, select_one, required): Select the percentage of sprint goals met (0-25%, 26-50%, 51-75%, 76-100%).
	+ **Sprint Goal Achievements** (`sprint_goal_achievements`, text, optional): Enter the goals that were successfully achieved during the sprint.
	+ **Sprint Goal Shortfalls** (`sprint_goal_shortfalls`, text, optional): Enter the goals that were not met and why.
* **-- What Went Well --** (`what_went_well_header`, note, required): This section is for reviewing what went well during the sprint.
	+ **Successful Practices** (`successful_practices`, text, optional): Enter what practices worked well during the sprint.
	+ **Team Collaboration Highlights** (`team_collaboration_highlights`, text, optional): Enter what aspects of team collaboration were particularly effective.
	+ **Individual Contributions** (`individual_contributions`, text, optional): Enter what individual contributions stood out positively.
* **-- What Did Not Go Well --** (`what_did_not_go_well_header`, note, required): This section is for reviewing what didn't go well during the sprint.
	+ **Challenges Encountered** (`challenges_encountered`, text, optional): Enter what challenges the team faced during the sprint.
	+ **Impediments Identified** (`impediments_identified`, text, optional): Enter what impediments hindered the team's progress.
* **-- Lessons Learned --** (`lessons_learned_header`, note, required): This section is for documenting lessons learned from the sprint.
	+ **Key Lessons Learned** (`key_lessons_learned`, text, optional): Enter what key lessons the team learned during this sprint.
* **Improvement Opportunities** (`improvement_opportunities`, text, optional): Enter what opportunities for improvement were identified.
* **-- Action Items --** (`action_items_header`, note, required): This section is for documenting action items for the next sprint.
	+ **Action Items for Next Sprint** (`action_items_for_next_sprint`, text, optional): Enter what specific action items will be implemented in the next sprint.
	+ **Responsible Team Members** (`responsible_team_members`, text, optional): Enter who will be responsible for each action item.
	+ **Target Completion Dates** (`target_completion_dates`, text, optional): Enter when each action item should be completed.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the sprint.

**Tips**

* Please be honest and objective when filling out this form.
* Be specific when describing what went well and what didn't.
* Use bullet points or short paragraphs to keep your answers clear and concise.
* Review and refine your answers before submitting the form.
* Use this form as a tool for continuous improvement and learning.
