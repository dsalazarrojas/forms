# Retail Mobile Payment Pilot Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for the Retail Mobile Payment Pilot Application from retail businesses. Ensure that you are a new participant in the pilot program and that your business has not already submitted an application. This form will be used to gather information about your business and its payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your business's official name in the "Business Name" field.
2.  Provide your contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field.
3.  Enter the contact email and phone number of the contact person.
4.  Enter the business's location in the "Business Location" field.
5.  Select all relevant payment methods accepted by your business in the "Payment Method" field.
6.  Provide your bank's ID in the "Bank ID" field, if applicable.
7.  Choose the type of application your business is submitting in the "Application Type" field.
8.  Enter the date and time your business joined in the "Date Joined" and "Time Joined" fields, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Business Name** (1, text, required): Enter your business's official name as it appears on your registration documents.
*   **Contact Person** (2, text, required): Provide the name of the person responsible for your business.
*   **Contact Email** (3, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone** (4, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Business Location** (5, text, required): Enter your business's location (e.g. street address).
*   **Payment Method** (6, select_multiple, required): Select all the payment methods your business accepts (e.g. Cash, Credit, Mobile).
*   **Bank ID** (7, number, required): If applicable, enter your bank's ID number.
*   **Application Type** (8, select_one, required): Choose the type of application your business is submitting (e.g. Merchant, Bank, Fintech).
*   **Date Joined** (9, date, required): Enter the date your business joined, if applicable.
*   **Time Joined** (10, time, required): Enter the time your business joined, if applicable.
