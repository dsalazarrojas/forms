# heavy_equipment_purchase_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about the heavy equipment purchase process. It is used to guide users in providing accurate and complete details for processing orders and managing the purchase experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary details about the equipment to be purchased.
2. Answer each field accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### <img class="icon" src="info"> Equipment Details (`<id: 1>`, `label: Equipment Details`, `type: select_multiple`, **Required: false**)

*This field is for selecting whether the equipment is available for purchase or not.*

### <img class="icon" src="info"> Customer Info (`<id: 2>`, `label: Customer Info`, `type: select_one`, **Required: false**)

*Select the relevant customer information for the purchase.*

### <img class="icon" src="info"> Equipment Specifications (`<id: 3>`, `label: Equipment Specifications`, `type: text`, **Required: false**)

*Enter the technical specifications of the equipment being purchased, including any relevant models or features.*

### <img class="icon" src="info"> Delivery Address (`<id: 4>`, `label: Delivery Address`, `type: text`, **Required: false**)

*Enter the address where the equipment will be delivered to, ensuring accurate location details are provided.*

### <img class="icon" src="info"> Contact Details (`<id: 5>`, `label: Contact Details`, `type: select_multiple`, **Required: false**)

### <img class="icon" src="info"> Payment Method (`<id: 6>`, `label: Payment Method`, `type: select_one`, **Required: false**)

*Choose the payment method to be used for this purchase, if applicable.*

### <img class="icon" src="info"> Payment Amount (`<id: 7>`, `label: Payment Amount`, `type: number`, **Required: false**)

*Enter the total payment amount for this purchase, if applicable.*

### <img class="icon" src="info"> Order Date (`<id: 8>`, `label: Order Date`, `type: date`, **Required: false**)

*Enter the date this order was placed, for reference purposes.*

### <img class="icon" src="info"> Order Status (`<id: 9>`, `label: Order Status`, `type: select_one`, **Required: false**)

*Indicate the current status of this order, which is active or inactive.*
