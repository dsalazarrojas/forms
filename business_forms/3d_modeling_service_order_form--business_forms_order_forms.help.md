# 3D Modeling Service Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 3D Modeling Service Order Form is designed to be completed by clients who are interested in commissioning a 3D modeling service for their project. This form gathers information about the client's project requirements, contact details, and other relevant specifications to ensure the service provider has all the necessary information to fulfill their project needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Review the form sections and fields carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
2.  Fill in your Client Information: Full name, Email address, and Phone number.
3.  Provide the Project Details: Project name, Type of 3D model required, Complexity Level, Desired Completion Timeframe, Budget Range, and Maximum Polygon Count (if applicable).
4.  Specify the File Format Preference and Texture Requirements.
5.  Answer the Additional Services questions: Is animation required? and Do you need professional rendering service?.
6.  Enter any Additional Comments or Special Instructions.
7.  Finally, review the form carefully before submitting it to us for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required/required): Enter your legal name or company representative.
* **Company name (if applicable)** (`company_name`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, enter your company name.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required/required): We'll use this for project updates and delivery.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, required/optional): For urgent project communications.
* **Order date** (`order_date`, `date`, required/required): Date for the project order.
* **Project name** (`project_name`, `text`, required/required): Brief identifier for your 3D model.
* **Type of 3D model required** (`model_type`, `select_one`, required/required): Select from: Product visualization, Architectural model, Character/model figure, Industrial design, Game asset, Animation-ready model, 3D printing-ready.
* **Complexity level** (`complexity_level`, `select_one`, required/required): Select from: Low (basic geometry), Medium (moderate detail), High (intricate details), Very high (photorealistic).
* **Number of reference images provided** (`reference_images_count`, `integer`, optional/optional): Number of reference images provided.
* **Desired completion timeframe (days)** (`estimated_completion_days`, `integer`, required/required): From 3 to 90 days.
* **Budget range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select from: Under $500, $500-$1,000, $1,000-$2,500, $2,500-$5,000, Over $5,000.
* **Preferred output format(s)** (`file_format_preference`, `select_multiple`, required/required): Select all that apply: OBJ, FBX, STL, GLTF, BLEND, MAX, C4D.
* **Texture requirements** (`texture_requirements`, `select_one`, required/required): Select from: No textures needed, Basic color only, Detailed textures, PBR materials, Custom textures provided.
* **Maximum polygon count (if applicable)** (`polygon_limit`, `integer`, optional/optional): Enter 0 for no limit.
* **Target application or platform** (`target_application`, `text`, optional/optional): Where will the model be used.
* **Revisions included** (`revision_includes`, `select_one`, required/required): Select from: 1 revision, 2 revisions, 3 revisions, Unlimited revisions.
* **Is animation required?** (`animation_needed`, `select_one`, required/required): Select from: True, False, Not sure.
* **Professional rendering service** (`rendering_service`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select from: Standard rendering, Photorealistic rendering, Multiple angle renders, Not needed.
* **Special instructions or customization notes** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional/optional): Any specific requirements or preferences.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional/optional): Anything else we should know about your project.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all the required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us directly.
* Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.
* You may need to provide additional information or clarification for some of the fields. If this is the case, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
