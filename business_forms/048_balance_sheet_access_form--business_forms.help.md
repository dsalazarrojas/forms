# Balance Sheet Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request access to a balance sheet for various purposes such as financial analysis, audit, or loan application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in the requester's information, including full name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Specify the type of access requested (view-only, download, print, analysis, or full access).
3.  Enter the company or department name whose balance sheet is being requested.
4.  Choose the financial period (month, quarter, year) for which access is needed.
5.  Select a reason for requesting access (financial analysis, audit, loan application, etc.).
6.  Provide a detailed explanation of the access need.
7.  Enter the manager or supervisor's name and email address.
8.  Indicate the manager's approval status.
9.  Enter the requester's job title and department.
10.  Confirm agreement to confidentiality requirements and data handling policies.
11.  Specify the duration of access needed (one-time, 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, or ongoing).
12.  Mention any special conditions or restrictions.
13.  Finally, submit the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Employee ID (if applicable)**: If you're an employee, enter your employee ID number for easier tracking.
* **Email Address**: Enter your work email address for communication and access verification.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your work phone number for easy contact.
* **Type of Access Requested**: Select the type of access you need, such as view-only, download, print, analysis, or full access.
* **Company or Department Name**: Enter the name of the company or department whose balance sheet you need access to.
* **Financial Period Requested**: Choose the financial period (month, quarter, year) you need access to.
* **Reason for Access**: Select a reason for requesting access, such as financial analysis, audit, loan application, etc.
* **Detailed Explanation of Access Need**: Provide a clear explanation of why you need access to the balance sheet.
* **Manager or Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the manager or supervisor who authorized this request.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the manager or supervisor who authorized this request.
* **Manager Approval Status**: Indicate the manager's approval status (approved, pending approval, denied).
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title and department.
* **Department**: Enter your department.
* **Manager Approval Status**: Indicate the manager's approval status (approved, pending approval, denied).
* **Confidentiality Agreement**: Confirm agreement to confidentiality requirements and data handling policies.
* **Data Handling Agreement**: Confirm agreement to data handling policies.
* **Access Duration**: Specify the duration of access needed (one-time, 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, or ongoing).
* **Special Conditions or Restrictions**: Mention any special conditions or restrictions.
* **Submit Request**: Confirm that you're submitting this access request.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Clearly explain why you need access to the balance sheet.
* Double-check the manager's approval status to ensure it's accurate.
* Confirm agreement to confidentiality requirements and data handling policies.
* Be specific about the duration of access needed.
* Mention any special conditions or restrictions.
