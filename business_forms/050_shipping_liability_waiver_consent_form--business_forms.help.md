# Shipping Liability Waiver Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shipping Liability Waiver Consent Form is a document that aims to safeguard businesses from potential liabilities associated with shipping goods or services. This form is a crucial step in ensuring that both the shipper and the customer are aware of and consent to the risks and liabilities involved in shipping.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Shipping Liability Waiver Consent Form, follow these steps:

1.  Please provide your full name and any other relevant details as prompted.
2.  Acknowledge and consent to the risks and liabilities associated with shipping by typing in the "shipping_risks_declaration" field.
3.  Declare your understanding of the risks and liabilities in the "shipping_liability_waiver_consent" field.
4.  Sign the form by typing your signature in the "signature" field.
5.  Provide your email address in the "email" field (if applicable).
6.  If you have a preferred phone number, please input it in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Liability Waiver Consent Form** (shipping_liability_waiver_consent_form): This field is a unique identifier for the form. Please leave it as it is, as it is not meant to be edited.
* **Risks and Liabilities** (risks_and_liabilities): This field is where you should type a declaration of the risks and liabilities associated with shipping. Please be as specific as possible about any hazards or perils you are aware of.
* **Shipping Risks Declaration** (shipping_risks_declaration): Similar to the above field, please type a detailed declaration of the risks and liabilities associated with shipping.
* **Shipping Liability Waiver Consent** (shipping_liability_waiver_consent): This field should contain a written consent to the shipping risks and liabilities, acknowledging that you have understood and agreed to them.
* **Customer Declaration** (customer_declaration): Please type a declaration that you are the customer, and that you are aware of and understand the shipping risks and liabilities involved.
* **Signature** (signature): This field requires your handwritten or typed signature, confirming that you are the person filling out the form and that you understand the content.
* **Email** (email): If you have an email address, please input it in this field so we can contact you for any updates.
* **Phone** (phone): If you prefer to be contacted via phone, please input your phone number here.

## Tips
Please fill out this form carefully, and do not hesitate to ask if you have any questions or concerns about the shipping process or the form itself. This form is a legal document, and your understanding and consent are crucial to ensuring a smooth and safe shipping experience.
