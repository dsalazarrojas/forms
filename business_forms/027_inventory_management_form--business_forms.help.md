# inventory_management_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for managing inventory. It allows users to input and store information about stores, employees, products, and suppliers. This includes store ID, employee ID, product ID, quantity, product name, product description, price, reorder point, reorder quantity, vendor status, supplier ID, and supplier name.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the Store ID (field 1).
*   Enter the Employee ID (field 2).
*   Enter the Product ID (field 3).
*   Enter the Quantity (field 4).
*   Enter the Product Name (field 5).
*   Enter the Product Description (field 6).
*   Enter the Price (field 7).
*   Enter the Reorder Point (field 8).
*   Enter the Reorder Quantity (field 9).
*   Select the Vendor status (field 10).
*   Enter the Supplier ID (field 11).
*   Enter the Supplier Name (field 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Store ID (1)** (`number`, required: false): Enter the unique identifier for the store.
*   **Employee ID (2)** (`number`, required: false): Enter the unique identifier for the employee.
*   **Product ID (3)** (`number`, required: false): Enter the unique identifier for the product.
*   **Quantity (4)** (`number`, required: false): Enter the current quantity of the product.
*   **Product Name (5)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the product.
*   **Product Description (6)** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the product.
*   **Price (7)** (`number`, required: false): Enter the price of the product.
*   **Reorder Point (8)** (`number`, required: false): Enter the quantity at which the product should be reordered.
*   **Reorder Quantity (9)** (`number`, required: false): Enter the quantity to order when the reorder point is reached.
*   **Vendor (10)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select whether the product is from a vendor or not.
*   **Supplier ID (11)** (`number`, required: false): Enter the unique identifier for the supplier.
*   **Supplier Name (12)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the supplier.
