# CAPA Action Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This document serves as a guide to completing the CAPA Action Form, a tool for quality and compliance documentation.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
2. Answer every field according to your knowledge and available information.
3. Ensure all fields are completed accurately and consistently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. CAPA Header** (`CAPA Action Form`, **Note**, Not Required): This section explains the purpose and importance of the form.
* **2. CAPA Reference Number** (`CAPA Reference Number`, **Text, Required**): Enter a unique identifier for the action. This will help track and reference the action.
* **3. Source of Non-Conformance** (`Source of Non-Conformance`, **Select One, Required**): Choose the source of the issue (Internal Audit, External Audit, Customer Complaint, etc.).
* **4. Detailed Description of the Issue** (`Detailed Description of the Issue`, **Text, Required**): Provide a detailed account of what happened, where, and when.
* **5. Immediate Correction Taken** (`Immediate Correction Taken`, **Text, Required**): Describe the immediate actions taken to contain the issue.
* **6. Root Cause Analysis** (`Root Cause Analysis`, **Text, Required**): Perform the 5 Whys method to identify the root cause of the issue.
* **7. Corrective Action Plan** (`Corrective Action Plan`, **Text, Required**): Develop a plan to eliminate the cause of the issue.
* **8. Preventive Action Plan** (`Preventive Action Plan`, **Text, Required**): Create a plan to prevent similar issues in the future.
* **9. Assigned Action Owner** (`Assigned Action Owner`, **Text, Required**): Identify the person responsible for implementing the action.
* **10. Target Completion Date** (`Target Completion Date`, **Date, Required**): Set a target date for completion.
* **11. Resources or Budget Required** (`Resources or Budget Required`, **Text, Not Required**): Provide any resources or budget needed for implementation.
* **12. Method for Verification of Effectiveness** (`Method for Verification of Effectiveness`, **Select One, Required**): Choose a method to verify the action's success (Re-audit, Performance Monitoring, etc.).
* **13. Post-Action Risk Level** (`Post-Action Risk Level`, **Select One, Required**): Evaluate the post-action risk level (Negligible, Low, Medium, High).
* **14. Quality Manager Approval Signature** (`Quality Manager Approval Signature`, **Text, Required**): Enter the name for digital approval.
* **15. CAPA Status** (`CAPA Status`, **Select One, Required**): Track the action's current status (Open, In Progress, Pending Verification, Closed).

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and consistently.
* Review each field carefully to understand its purpose and relevance.
* Use the form as a guide, but also use your professional judgment.
* If a field is not applicable, leave it blank or note the reason why.
* This form is designed to support quality and compliance documentation processes.
* Consult your supervisor or quality team if you need clarification on any field.
