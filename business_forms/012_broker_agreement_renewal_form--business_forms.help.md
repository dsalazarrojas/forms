# Broker Agreement Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the renewal process of broker agreements with clients. It ensures that all necessary information is gathered and confirmed before renewal, maintaining accurate records and facilitating smooth communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your broker information: Enter your full name, license number, and contact email and phone number.
2. Specify agreement details: Enter the current agreement number, original agreement date, and current agreement end date.
3. Select agreement type and renewal period: Choose the type of agreement and the duration of the renewal from the provided options.
4. Enter property information: Provide the address of the property involved and its type.
5. Confirm compliance and authorization: Confirm your license is current and in good standing, and that errors and omissions insurance is active.
6. Acknowledge agreement renewal: Check to agree to the renewal terms.
7. Sign the form: Enter your full legal name to sign the form and provide the date of signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Broker Full Name** (`broker_name`, text, required): Enter your full, legal name as registered with the real estate licensing agency.
- **Broker License Number** (`broker_license_number`, text, required): Enter your current real estate broker license number.
- **Broker Email** (`broker_email`, email, required): Enter your business email address where you can be contacted.
- **Broker Phone** (`broker_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for business purposes.
- **Firm Name** (`firm_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the real estate brokerage firm you represent.
- **Current Agreement Number** (`current_agreement_number`, text, required): Enter the number of the agreement being renewed.
- **Original Agreement Date** (`original_agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the date the original agreement was signed.
- **Current Agreement End Date** (`current_agreement_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date the current agreement expires.
- **Agreement Type** (`agreement_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of agreement from the options provided.
- **Requested Renewal Period** (`renewal_period`, select_one, required): Choose the duration of the renewal from the options provided.
- **Renewal Start Date** (`renewal_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date the renewal should begin.
- **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property involved in the agreement.
- **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of property from the options provided.
- **Listing Price** (`listing_price`, number, optional): Enter the asking price for the property.
- **Commission Rate** (`commission_rate`, number, required): Enter the agreed commission percentage.
- **Have Terms Changed** (`terms_changed`, select_one, required): Check if there are any changes to the agreement terms.
- **Description of Changes** (`term_changes_description`, text, optional): Describe any changes if applicable.
- **Performance Summary** (`performance_summary`, text, optional): Provide a brief summary of your activity during the current agreement.
- **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback from the client.
- **License Status** (`license_status`, select_one, required): Confirm that your license is current and in good standing.
- **E and O Insurance Current** (`errors_omissions_insurance`, select_one, required): Confirm that errors and omissions insurance is active.
- **I Agree to Renewal** (`agreement_acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Check to acknowledge agreement renewal.
- **Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature`, text, required): Type your full legal name to sign the form.
- **Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of signature.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields carefully and accurately.
- Review each field for any discrepancies or errors before submission.
- Ensure that all relevant details are provided to maintain accurate records and facilitate communication.
- Review and understand the agreement terms before agreeing to renewal.
