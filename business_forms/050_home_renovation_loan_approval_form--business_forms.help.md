# Home Renovation Loan Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for loan applicants to apply for a home renovation loan. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to help us process your loan application efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Provide your name and contact information.
2. Enter the desired loan amount and loan term in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Applicant** (`applicant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the total amount of money you are seeking as a loan for your home renovation project.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, number, required: false): Enter the length of time you would like the loan to be for (e.g., 1 year, 5 years).

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and honest information to ensure a smooth loan application process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
* Ensure the loan amount and term match your project's estimated costs and timeline.
