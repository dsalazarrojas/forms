# Administrative Terms Glossary Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and manage administrative terms and their definitions within the organization. This form will help to streamline the submission process for new terms and ensure consistency in their usage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the Submitter Name and Email fields with your contact information.
2. Select the department you belong to from the list of options.
3. Enter the submission date and time.
4. Define the term you wish to submit.
5. Identify the category of the term.
6. Provide a clear and concise definition of the term.
7. If the term is an acronym, provide the full expansion.
8. List any related terms or synonyms.
9. Describe the usage context of the term.
10. Provide an example of how the term is used.
11. Optionally, provide information about the origin and etymology of the term.
12. Describe how the term is used specifically in your department.
13. Choose the frequency of use.
14. Assess the importance level of the term.
15. Suggest alternative terms.
16. Provide external references or sources.
17. Leave comments for the reviewer.
18. Leave an electronic signature to confirm submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Name** (ID 2): Your name as the submitter of the term.
* **Submitter Email** (ID 3): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Submitter Department** (ID 4): Select which department you belong to from the list of options.
* **Submission Date** (ID 5): Date and time of submission.
* **Term to Define** (ID 6): Enter the term you want to define.
* **Term Category** (ID 7): Identify the category of the term.
* **Definition** (ID 8): Provide a clear and concise definition of the term.
* **Acronym Expansion** (ID 9): If the term is an acronym, enter the full expansion.
* **Related Terms** (ID 10): List any related terms or synonyms.
* **Usage Context** (ID 11): Describe the usage context of the term.
* **Example Usage** (ID 12): Provide an example of how the term is used.
* **Origin/Etymology** (ID 13): Information about the origin of the term.
* **Department-Specific Use** (ID 14): Describe how the term is used specifically in your department.
* **Frequency of Use** (ID 15): Choose the frequency of use.
* **Importance Level** (ID 16): Assess the importance level of the term.
* **Suggested Alternatives** (ID 17): Suggest alternative terms.
* **External References** (ID 18): Provide external references or sources.
* **Approval Status** (ID 19): Status of the submission to be filled by the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Comments** (ID 20): Comments from the reviewer.
* **Submitter Signature** (ID 21): Electronic signature to confirm submission.
