# Change Impact Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and details from potential clients interested in our consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the required fields with the information requested.
2. Be as specific as possible with your answers to help us provide the best guidance and support.
3. If you have any additional information you would like to share, please use the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquiry Date**: <span class="label">inquiry_date</span> - The date of your contact.
	+ This is the date when you reached out to us.
* **Contact Name**: <span class="label">contact_name</span> - Your full name.
	+ This is your name that we will use to address you in our communication.
* **Email Address**: <span class="label">contact_email</span> - Your email address.
	+ This is the email address we will use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: <span class="label">contact_phone</span> - Your contact phone number.
	+ This is the number we will use to call you if you prefer phone calls.
* **Company Name**: <span class="label">company_name</span> - The name of your organization.
	+ This is the name of the company or organization you are representing.
* **Company Size**: <span class="label">company_size</span> - The number of employees in your organization.
	+ This will help us understand the scale of your change initiative.
* **Industry**: <span class="label">industry</span> - The primary industry sector of your organization.
	+ This will help us understand your specific needs and provide relevant services.
* **Job Title**: <span class="label">job_title</span> - Your position in the organization.
	+ This will help us understand your role and perspective in the change initiative.
* **Service Interest**: <span class="label">service_interest</span> - The services you are interested in from our consulting firm.
	+ Select all that apply, and we will provide guidance accordingly.
* **Project Timeline**: <span class="label">project_timeline</span> - When you need our services.
	+ This will help us plan and execute our support.
* **Project Scope**: <span class="label">project_scope</span> - The estimated scope of the change initiative.
	+ This will help us understand the scale and complexity of your change initiative.
* **Budget Range**: <span class="label">budget_range</span> - The estimated budget for your consulting services.
	+ This will help us tailor our services to your financial needs.
* **Current Challenges**: <span class="label">current_challenges</span> - The change challenges you are currently facing.
	+ This will help us understand your specific pain points and provide relevant guidance.
* **Change Drivers**: <span class="label">change_drivers</span> - The factors driving the need for change.
	+ Select all that apply, and we will provide guidance accordingly.
* **Previous Change Experience**: <span class="label">previous_change_experience</span> - Your experience with major change initiatives.
	+ This will help us understand your strengths and weaknesses in change management.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: <span class="label">preferred_contact_method</span> - How you would like us to contact you.
	+ This will help us tailor our communication approach to your preferences.
* **Best Time to Contact**: <span class="label">best_time_to_contact</span> - The best time to reach you.
	+ This is optional, but it will help us schedule follow-up calls and meetings.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: <span class="label">how_did_you_hear</span> - The source of your referral to our consulting firm.
	+ This will help us evaluate the effectiveness of our marketing efforts.
* **Referred By**: <span class="label">referred_by</span> - The name of the referring person, if applicable.
	+ This will help us thank and reward your referrers.
* **Additional Information**: <span class="label">additional_information</span> - Any other details you would like to share.
	+ This is where you can provide any extra information not covered by other fields.
* **Contact Signature**: <span class="label">contact_signature</span> - Your full name as a signature.
	+ This is the last field to complete, but it's required to confirm your identity.
