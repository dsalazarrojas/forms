# packing_quantity_estimator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The packing quantity estimator form is used to estimate the packing quantity and cost for items being shipped or stored in the warehouse.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the move details in the "Move Details" field. This field is required.
2. List the inventory items in the "Inventory List" field. This field is optional.
3. Select your packing preferences in the "Packing Preferences" field. This field is optional.
4. Enter the estimated packing quantity in the "Packing Quantity" field. This field is optional.
5. Enter the estimated weight of the item in the "Estimated Weight" field. This field is optional.
6. Enter the packing cost in the "Packing Cost" field. This field is optional.
7. Select the type of packing cost from the "Packing Cost Type" field. This field is optional.
8. Enter the packing date in the "Packing Date" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Move Details** (`Move Details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the move, including the item being moved, the reason for the move, and any relevant details.
- **Inventory List** (`Inventory List`, text, optional): List the items being moved, including the quantity and any relevant details.
- **Packing Preferences** (`Packing Preferences`, text, optional): Select the packing preferences for the items being moved, such as fragile or hazardous materials.
- **Packing Quantity** (`Packing Quantity`, number, optional): Enter the estimated quantity of packing materials needed for the move.
- **Estimated Weight** (`Estimated Weight`, number, optional): Enter the estimated weight of the item being moved.
- **Packing Cost** (`Packing Cost`, number, optional): Enter the estimated cost of packing the item.
- **Packing Cost Type** (`Packing Cost Type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of packing cost, such as Flat Fee or Per Unit.
- **Packing Date** (`Packing Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the item was packed.

Tips:
- Make sure to accurately estimate the packing quantity and cost to avoid errors.
- Use the "Inventory List" field to keep track of multiple items being moved.
- Use the "Packing Preferences" field to indicate fragile or hazardous materials.
- Select the correct "Packing Cost Type" to ensure accurate pricing.
