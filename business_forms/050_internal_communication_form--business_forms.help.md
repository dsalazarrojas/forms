# internal_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for internal communication within your organization. It allows you to share feedback, updates, and internal communications with your team, colleagues, or superiors. The form is divided into sections to help you categorize and manage your internal messages effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of internal communication you want to share:
	* Update: Share updates, news, or information within your organization.
	* Feedback: Provide feedback or suggestions to your colleagues or superiors.
2. Type your message in the designated text field.
3. Review and submit your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **internal_update_form** (Text, optional): Share updates, news, or information within your organization.
- **share_feedback** (Text, optional): Provide feedback or suggestions to your colleagues or superiors.
- **share_updates** (Text, optional): Share updates or news with your team or colleagues.
- **internal_feedback** (Text, optional): Share internal feedback or suggestions.
- **share_update** (Text, optional): Share updates or news with your team or colleagues.
- **internal_update** (Text, optional): Share internal updates.
- **internal_feedback** (Text, optional): Share internal feedback.
- **update_internal** (Text, optional): Share internal updates or news.
- **internal_communication** (Text, optional): Share internal communication, such as messages or comments.
- **share_updates_and** (Text, optional): Share updates and feedback with your team or colleagues.
- **internal_feedback_and** (Text, optional): Share internal feedback and updates.
- **updates_and_feedback** (Text, optional): Share updates and internal feedback.
- **internal_update_form_13** (Text, optional): This field is currently not clearly labeled for a specific purpose. It seems to be a placeholder or a redundant field, and you can ignore it for now. 

# Tips
* Be concise and clear in your internal communication to ensure efficient information exchange.
* Use this form for official internal communication within your organization.
* You can also use this form to share internal news or updates.
* If you're unsure about the purpose of any field, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
