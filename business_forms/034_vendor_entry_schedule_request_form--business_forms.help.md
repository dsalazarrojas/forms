# Vendor Entry Schedule Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request vendor entry schedules for a specific date and time. It captures the vendor's details, the schedule entry date and time, and any site access requests. This form is designed to be completed by vendors or their representatives to provide accurate and timely entry information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "Vendor Details" section with your company's name and any other relevant information.
2.  Select a date and time for your scheduled entry using the "Schedule Entry Date" and "Schedule Entry Time" fields.
3.  If necessary, provide any site access requests in the "Site Access Request" field.
4.  Click the "Submit" button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vendor Details** (`vendor_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your company's name and any other relevant information.
*   **Schedule Entry Date** (`schedule_entry_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date for your scheduled entry.
*   **Schedule Entry Time** (`schedule_entry_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time for your scheduled entry.
*   **Site Access Request** (`site_access_request`, text, required/optional): If you need access to the site during your scheduled entry, provide details here.
*   **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required/optional): Click this button to submit your request.
*   **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your company's name.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number.

## Tips
When filling out this form, please ensure that you are entering accurate information. This includes your company's details, schedule entry date and time, and site access requests. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
