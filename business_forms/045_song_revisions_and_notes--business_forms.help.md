# Song Revisions And Notes - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for tracking and managing song revisions and notes for music producers. It allows you to store information about a song, including its title, artist, version, status, and attachments. The form can be filled out by a music producer or a member of their team to keep track of progress and revisions made to a song.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the title of the song in the `Song Title` field.
*   Enter the artist who created the song in the `Song Artist` field.
*   Enter the version of the song in the `Song Version` field.
*   Select the date the song was last modified from the calendar in the `Date Modified` field.
*   Add any notes or comments about the song in the `Notes` field.
*   Select the status of the song from the options provided in the `Song Status` field.
*   Enter the name of the producer who is working on the song in the `Producer Name` field.
*   Enter the email address of the producer in the `Producer Email` field.
*   Enter the phone number of the producer in the `Producer Phone` field.
*   Enter a unique identifier for the song in the `Song ID` field.
*   Select any revisions made to the song from the options provided in the `Revisions` field.
*   Add any comments about the version of the song in the `Version Comments` field.
*   Add any comments about the song in the `Comments` field.
*   Select whether attachments are associated with the song from the options provided in the `Attachments` field.
*   Enter the name of the attachment in the `Attachment Name` field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Song Title** (text, required: false)
    Enter the title of the song.
*   **Song Artist** (text, required: false)
    Enter the name of the artist who created the song.
*   **Song Version** (number, required: false)
    Enter the version of the song.
*   **Date Modified** (date, required: false)
    Select the date the song was last modified from the calendar.
*   **Notes** (text, required: false)
    Add any notes or comments about the song.
*   **Song Status** (select_one, required: false)
    Select the status of the song from the options: Approved, In Progress, or Rejected.
*   **Producer Name** (text, required: false)
    Enter the name of the producer who is working on the song.
*   **Producer Email** (email, required: false)
    Enter the email address of the producer.
*   **Producer Phone** (text, required: false)
    Enter the phone number of the producer.
*   **Song ID** (number, required: false)
    Enter a unique identifier for the song.
*   **Revisions** (select_multiple, required: false)
    Select any revisions made to the song from the options: Revision 1, Revision 2, Revision 3, Revision 4, Revision 5.
*   **Version Comments** (text, required: false)
    Add any comments about the version of the song.
*   **Comments** (text, required: false)
    Add any comments about the song.
*   **Attachments** (select_multiple, required: false)
    Select whether attachments are associated with the song from the options: Yes or No.
*   **Attachment Name** (text, required: false)
    Enter the name of the attachment, if applicable.
