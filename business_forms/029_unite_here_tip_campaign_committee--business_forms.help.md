# Unite Here Tip Campaign Committee - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Unite Here Tip Campaign Committee" form is for creating new committee members and ensuring that all necessary information is collected for their profile. This form is used to prevent duplicate records and ensure that each committee member has a unique profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields. Please note that these fields are required and will be used to identify you as a committee member.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. If you have already submitted a record with this email, you will not be able to submit this form.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if applicable.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field, if applicable.
5. Select your department from the "Department" field. If you don't see your department listed, you may not have the necessary permissions to access this form.
6. Select one or more roles that you would like to be associated with as a committee member from the "Role" field.
7. Add any additional note or comments in the "Note" field, if applicable.
8. Enter the name of the tool assigned to you, if applicable.
9. Finally, enter the name of the "Tip Campagne Committee" you are a member of in the "Tip Campagne Committee" field. This is a required field, as it identifies which committee you are a part of.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name. Please ensure that you enter your correct first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name. Please ensure that you enter your correct last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address. If you have already submitted a record with this email, you will not be able to submit this form.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable. This field is not required but can be helpful for committee communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth, if applicable. This field is not required but can be used for committee record keeping.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select your department from the list. If you don't see your department listed, you may not have the necessary permissions to access this form.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more roles that you would like to be associated with as a committee member.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional note or comments, if applicable. This field is not required but can be used for committee record keeping.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you, if applicable. This field is not required but can be used for committee record keeping.
* **Tip Campagne Committee** (`tip_campagne_committee`, text, required): Enter the name of the "Tip Campagne Committee" you are a member of. This is a required field as it identifies which committee you are a part of.
