# Product And Service Listing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your company's products and services. It's used to create listings that will be displayed for customers to browse through your offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering the name of your service or product in the "service_name" field.
2. In the "description" field, provide a brief description of your service or product.
3. Enter the price or price range for your service or product in the "pricing" field.
4. Optionally, upload an image of your service or product in the "service_image" field.
5. If you have a video demonstration or presentation of your service or product, upload it in the "service_video" field.
6. Enter a valid email address in the "inquiry_email" field to receive customer inquiries.
7. Enter a phone number in the "inquiry_phone" field for customers to contact you.
8. Provide a brief message in the "inquiry_message" field if you want to include any additional information.
9. Finally, click the "submit" button to complete the form and create a new listing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **service_name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your service or product. This is the title that will be displayed in the listing.
* **description** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of your service or product to help customers understand what they're getting.
* **pricing** (Number, Required): Enter the price or price range for your service or product. This will be displayed in the listing.
* **service_image** (Text, Optional): Upload an image of your service or product. This will help customers get a visual representation.
* **service_video** (Text, Optional): Upload a video demonstration or presentation of your service or product. This can help customers better understand how your service or product works.
* **inquiry_email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address to receive customer inquiries and messages. This is how customers will contact you about your service or product.
* **inquiry_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter a phone number for customers to contact you. This can be useful if they have questions or need to discuss your service or product.
* **inquiry_message** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief message to include any additional information you'd like to share with customers.
* **submit** (Text, Required): Click this button to create a new listing with the information you've provided.
