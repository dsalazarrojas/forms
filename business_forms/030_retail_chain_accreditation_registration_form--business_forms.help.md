# Retail Chain Accreditation Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from store owners or managers to register their retail chain for accreditation. The data collected will be used to verify the store's details and ensure compliance with relevant regulations or industry standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Page**: This is the initial page of the form. You can skip this page if it's not relevant to your store's information.
2. **Store Information**: This section is crucial, as it requires accurate and detailed information about your store. Please enter the following:
	* **Store Information**: A brief description of your store, including its name, address, and other relevant details.
3. **Store Contact**: This section is optional, but it's recommended that you fill it out if your answer is 'Yes'.
	* **Store Contact**: A yes or no answer to whether you want to be contacted for further information.
4. **Location**: Select one of the options to indicate your store's location.
5. **Store Hours**: Enter your store's operating hours in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00).
6. **Manager**: Enter your manager's name.
7. **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
8. **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
9. **Store Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your store.
10. **Store Notes 2**: Enter any additional notes about your store (if needed).
11-20. **Store Information 2-10**: These fields are not clearly labeled or explained, so we recommend skipping them for now.
21. **Store Hours 2**: Enter your store's operating hours in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00).
22-25. **Manager Notes 2-5**: Enter any additional notes about your manager or store.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and update your store's information regularly to ensure accuracy and compliance.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed by store owners or managers, but if you're filling it out on behalf of someone else, please ensure you have their permission to do so.
