# Fulfillment Service Quotation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fulfillment Service Quotation Request Form is designed to collect information from customers to prepare a detailed quotation for fulfillment services. This form helps the fulfillment team to understand the customer's service requirements, pricing information, and contact details, enabling them to provide an accurate and efficient quotation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please provide a brief description of the service you are requesting.
2. Specify the details of the service you require (e.g., product, quantity, etc.).
3. If applicable, provide any relevant pricing information (e.g., budget range).
4. Indicate if you require a specific fulfillment provider (e.g., internal or external).
5. Enter your contact information (e.g., name, email, phone number, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Service Requirements
* **Service Requirements** (`service_requirements`, text, required: false): Describe the specific service you are requesting. This field should include details about the product, quantity, and any other relevant information that will help us understand your needs.
### Pricing Info
* **Pricing Info** (`pricing_info`, text, required: false): If you have any specific pricing information (e.g., budget range), please enter it here.
### Fulfillment Provider
* **Fulfillment Provider** (`fulfillment_provider`, select multiple, required: false): Choose whether you require a specific fulfillment provider. You can select one or more of the following options:
	+ 'Yes' (Internal) - Select if you require an internal fulfillment provider.
	+ 'No' (External) - Select if you do not have a specific provider in mind.
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information (e.g., name, email, phone number) so we can get in touch with you for further information or questions.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and clear as possible when filling out this form to ensure accurate quotation preparation.
* If you have any additional requirements or questions, do not hesitate to contact us directly.
* We will respond to your request within 24-48 hours with a detailed quotation.
