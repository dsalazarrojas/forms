# Rental Agreement Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Agreement Declaration Form is a document used to collect information from both the tenant and the landlord regarding the rental agreement, ensuring a clear record of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the tenant information (page 1).
2. Fill in the landlord information (page 1).
3. Confirm the rental terms (page 2).
4. Sign and date the form as the tenant (page 3).
5. Sign and date the form as the landlord (page 3).
6. Complete the time and date of signature (page 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rental Agreement Declaration Form** (`text`, required): This field is a summary of the rental agreement.
* **tenant_info** (`text`, required): Enter the tenant's name and contact information.
* **landlord_info** (`text`, required): Enter the landlord's name and contact information.
* **rental_terms** (`text`, required): Confirm the rental terms and conditions.
* **tenant_signature** (`text`, required): Sign and date the form as the tenant.
* **landlord_signature** (`text`, required): Sign and date the form as the landlord.
* **date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of the rental agreement.
* **time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of the rental agreement.
* **date_of_signature** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of signing.
* **tenant_email** (`email`, optional): Enter the tenant's email address.
