## Waste Disposal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Waste Disposal Form is designed to collect information from customers about their waste disposal requirements. This form helps us understand the type of waste, description, and pickup details for effective waste management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "customer_name" field to identify yourself.
2. Provide your email address in the "customer_email" field to contact you back.
3. Enter your phone number in the "customer_phone" field for further communication.
4. In the "waste_type" field, describe the type of waste you need to dispose of (e.g., household, commercial, hazardous).
5. Provide a detailed description of the waste in the "waste_description" field.
6. Select the pickup date for your waste disposal in the "pickup_date" field.
7. Choose the pickup time for your waste disposal in the "pickup_time" field.
8. Enter the location where the waste will be picked up in the "pickup_location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, optional): Enter your name to help us identify you and communicate with you efficiently.
* **Customer Email** (customer_email, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you back with any updates or questions.
* **Customer Phone** (customer_phone, text, optional): Provide your phone number for further communication.
* **Waste Type** (waste_type, text, optional): Describe the type of waste you need to dispose of (e.g., household, commercial, hazardous).
* **Waste Description** (waste_description, text, optional): Give a detailed description of the waste, including its characteristics and any specific concerns you may have.
* **Pickup Date** (pickup_date, date, optional): Select the date for your waste pickup.
* **Pickup Time** (pickup_time, time, optional): Choose the time for your waste pickup.
* **Pickup Location** (pickup_location, text, optional): Enter the exact location where the waste will be picked up.
