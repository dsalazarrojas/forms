# Business Development End Of Day Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather business development information from employees at the end of each day, providing the management team with a clear understanding of their daily progress and performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have access to this form and follow the guidelines provided.
2.  Start by filling in the report date at the top of the form.
3.  Enter your name and employee ID in the respective fields.
4.  Select the correct reporting period for your activities.
5.  Provide a summary of your daily activities in the "Activities Completed Today" field.
6.  Enter the number of calls, meetings, and emails you have made/sent.
7.  Report the number of proposals submitted, deals closed, and leads generated.
8.  Describe any obstacles or challenges encountered.
9.  Mention any customer feedback or support needed.
10.  List the planned activities for tomorrow.
11.  Mention any follow-ups needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Report Date** (`report_date`, required): Enter the date of this report.
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, required): Enter your full name.
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, required): Enter your employee identification number.
*   **Department** (`department`, required): Choose your department from the provided options.
*   **Reporting Period** (`reporting_period`, required): Select the period you are reporting for (e.g., Today, This Week, This Month).
*   **Activities Completed Today** (`activities_completed`, required): Provide a detailed description of your daily activities.
*   **Number of Calls Made** (`calls_made`, required): Enter the total number of business development calls you've made.
*   **Meetings Conducted** (`meetings_conducted`, required): Enter the total number of meetings you've conducted.
*   **Emails Sent** (`emails_sent`, required): Enter the total number of business emails sent.
*   **Proposals Submitted** (`proposals_submitted`, required): Enter the total number of proposals submitted.
*   **Deals Closed** (`deals_closed`, required): Enter the number of deals closed.
*   **Total Value of Closed Deals** (`deals_value`, optional): Enter the financial value of closed deals.
*   **New Leads Generated** (`leads_generated`, required): Enter the number of new leads generated.
*   **Major Accomplishments Today** (`major_accomplishments`, required): Describe any key accomplishments or breakthroughs.
*   **Obstacles Encountered** (`obstacles_encountered`, optional): Describe any obstacles or challenges you've faced.
*   **Support Needed** (`support_needed`, optional): List any resources or help needed.
*   **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, optional): Share any feedback from customers.
*   **Planned Activities Tomorrow** (`planned_activities`, required): List the activities planned for tomorrow.
*   **Follow-ups Needed** (`follow_ups_needed`, optional): Mention any follow-ups needed.
