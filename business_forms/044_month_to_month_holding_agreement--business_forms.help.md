# Month To Month Holding Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create a month-to-month holding agreement between a landlord and a tenant for a rental property. This agreement outlines the terms and conditions of the rental, including the rent, payment frequency, and notice period.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the address field with the address of the rental property.
2. Select the landlord's information from the options provided.
3. Enter the monthly rent amount and select the payment frequency (e.g., monthly, weekly, biweekly).
4. Choose a start and end date for the rental period.
5. Enter any additional tenant information if required.
6. Enter the security deposit amount.
7. Choose the notice period for termination of the agreement.
8. Agree or disagree with the month-to-month holding agreement terms and sign the agreement.
9. The landlord and tenant should both sign the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the rental property where you will be living.
* **Landlord Information** (`Landlord Information`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the title of the landlord (Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr.).
* **Rent** (`Rent`, `number`, required): Enter the monthly rent amount.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the rental period starts.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the rental period ends.
* **Rent Frequency** (`Rent Frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you will pay rent (e.g., monthly, weekly, biweekly).
* **Tenant Information** (`Tenant Information`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information about yourself as the tenant.
* **Security Deposit** (`Security Deposit`, `number`, required): Enter the security deposit amount.
* **Notice Period** (`Notice Period`, `number`, required): Enter the number of days notice you or the landlord needs to give for termination of the agreement.
* **Month-to-Month Holding Agreement Terms** (`Month-to-Month Holding Agreement Terms`, `note`, required): Read through and agree to the terms of the agreement.
* **Agreement Termination** (`Agreement Termination`, `text`, required): This field should be left blank as it is a section for the landlord and tenant to add any additional termination terms.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, `text`, required): Both the landlord and tenant should sign the agreement to confirm their acceptance of the terms.
* **Landlord Signature** (`Landlord Signature`, `text`, required): The landlord should sign the agreement to confirm their acceptance of the terms.
* **Tenant Signature** (`Tenant Signature`, `text`, required): The tenant should sign the agreement to confirm their acceptance of the terms.
