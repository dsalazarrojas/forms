# General Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "General Incident Report Form" is designed to collect details and information about incidents that have occurred within the organization. This form will help gather relevant data to document and review incidents, ensuring accurate records and facilitating incident resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all required information before starting to fill it out.
2. Provide detailed and accurate answers to each question.
3. Ensure that you answer all required fields.
4. Select the correct "Report Type" (Emergency or Non-emergency) based on the nature of the incident.
5. If witnesses were involved, list their names in the "Witness Names" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`):
	+ Enter a brief description of what happened.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`):
	+ Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Date** (`incident_date`):
	+ Enter the date the incident happened (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Time** (`incident_time`):
	+ Enter the time the incident happened (HH:MM:SS format).
* **Witness Names** (`witness_names`):
	+ If witnesses were involved, enter their names.
* **Description** (`incident_description`):
	+ Enter a brief and detailed description of what happened.
* **Notes** (`incident_notes`):
	+ Enter any additional information or context about the incident.
* **Report Type** (`report_type`):
	+ Choose whether the incident was an "Emergency" or "Non-emergency" based on its nature.
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`):
	+ Enter your email address or the name of the person submitting the report.
* **Reported On** (`reported_on`):
	+ The date the report is submitted (will be auto-filled).
