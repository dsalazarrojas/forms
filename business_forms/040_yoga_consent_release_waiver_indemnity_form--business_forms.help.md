# Yoga Consent Release Waiver Indemnity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from yoga participants for consent and waiver purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, and Signature.
2. Provide your Date of Birth and Date of Class information.
3. Select your Class Type (e.g., YOGA, Hatha, Vinyasa, Kundalini, Restorative, or Other).
4. Answer the questions about Injuries or Health Conditions, Medical Conditions, and Medical Provider.
5. Select your Emergency Contact and Emergency Phone Number.
6. Provide any Additional Comments.
7. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`form_last_name`, text): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text): Please enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`form_date_of_birth`, date): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Date of Class** (`form_date_of_class`, date): Please enter the date of the yoga class you will be attending in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Class Type** (`form_class_type`, select_one): Select the type of yoga class you will be attending (e.g., YOGA, Hatha, Vinyasa, Kundalini, Restorative, or Other).
* **Participant Consent** (`form_participant_consent`, select_multiple): Select all that apply:
	+ I understand that I am participating in a yoga class.
* **Injuries or Health Conditions** (`form_injuries`, select_multiple): Select all that apply:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ None of the above
* **Medical Conditions** (`form_medical_conditions`, select_multiple): Select all that apply:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ None of the above
* **Emergency Contact** (`form_emergency_contact`, text): Please enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`form_emergency_phone`, text): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Provider** (`form_medical_provider`, text): Please enter the name of your medical provider (e.g., doctor or healthcare professional).
* **Medical Insurance** (`form_medical_insurance`, text): Please enter your medical insurance information (e.g., provider, policy number).
* **Waiver** (`form_waiver`, select_one): Select:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Additional Comments** (`form_additional_comments`, text): Please enter any additional comments or information you would like to share with the instructor.
* **Signature** (`form_signature`, note): Sign your name, indicating your consent and agreement to the terms and conditions of the class.
* **Date** (`form_date`, date): The date you are completing this form.
* **Time** (`form_time`, time): The time you are completing this form.
* **Class Attendance** (`form_class_attendance`, select_one): Select:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Class Attendance Date** (`form_class_attendance_date`, date): The date you attended the class in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Class Attendance Time** (`form_class_attendance_time`, time): The time you attended the class.
* **Certificate** (`form_certificate`, select_one): Select:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Certificate Date** (`form_certificate_date`, date): The date the certificate was issued in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Certificate Time** (`form_certificate_time`, time): The time the certificate was issued.
