# Case Staffing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Staffing Form is a tool used to document and coordinate case staffing discussions, ensuring that all relevant information is recorded for effective case management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields such as Case Number, Case Name, and Current Placement or Living Situation.
2. Select the Type of Staffing, which indicates the purpose of the meeting (e.g., Initial Staffing, Periodic Review, Crisis Staffing, etc.).
3. Enter the meeting date and time in the Staffing Date field.
4. Identify the Team Leader or Facilitator and the Primary Case Manager.
5. Record the attendee names and titles for both the client and their family members.
6. Update the client's status by selecting the Client Status Update option.
7. Select the Current Risk Level, which indicates the level of risk associated with the client.
8. Identify current risk factors, which may include self-harm, suicide risk, violence risk, substance use, domestic violence, neglect, abuse, exploitation, medical concerns, mental health crisis, housing instability, or other.
9. Update the safety plan by selecting the Safety Plan Status option.
10. Record the treatment plan updates, including any modifications or revisions.
11. Record any new goals established.
12. Assign tasks and responsibilities to team members.
13. Document the follow-up plan, including the next staffing date and next client contact date.
14. Record any additional notes or discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staffing Date** (ID: 2) (`date`, required): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Staffing Time** (ID: 3) (`time`, required: false): Enter the meeting time.
* **Case Information** (ID: 4) (`note`, required: false): Record any general information about the case.
* **Case Number** (ID: 5) (`text`, required): Enter the unique identifier for the case.
* **Case Name** (ID: 6) (`text`, required): Enter the name or title of the case.
* **Case Type** (ID: 7) (`select_one`, required): Select the category of the case (e.g., Child welfare, Adult protective services, etc.).
* **Case Open Date** (ID: 8) (`date`, required): Enter the date the case was started.
* **Type of Staffing** (ID: 9) (`select_one`, required): Select the type of staffing discussion (e.g., Initial staffing, Periodic review, etc.).
* **Meeting Attendees** (ID: 10) (`note`, required: false): List the names and titles of attendees.
* **Team Leader or Facilitator** (ID: 11) (`text`, required): Enter the name of the team leader or facilitator.
* **Primary Case Manager** (ID: 12) (`text`, required): Enter the name of the primary case manager.
* **Attendee Names and Titles** (ID: 13) (`text`, required): List the names and titles of attendees.
* **Was Client Present** (ID: 14) (`select_one`, required): Select the client's attendance status (e.g., Yes - attended full meeting, Yes - attended partial meeting, etc.).
* **Were Family Members Present** (ID: 15) (`select_one`, required): Select the family members' attendance status.
* **Absent Members** (ID: 16) (`text`, required: false): List the names of absent members.
* **Client Status Update** (ID: 17) (`note`, required: false): Record the client's current status.
* **Current Placement or Living Situation** (ID: 18) (`text`, required): Enter the current living situation of the client.
* **Placement Stability** (ID: 19) (`select_one`, required): Select the level of stability in the placement.
* **Progress Since Last Staffing** (ID: 20) (`text`, required): Record any progress made since the last staffing.
* **Goals Review** (ID: 21) (`note`, required: false): Record a review of the goals.
* **Goals Achieved** (ID: 22) (`text`, required: false): List any achieved goals.
* **Goals In Progress** (ID: 23) (`text`, required): List the goals currently being worked on.
* **Goals Not Met** (ID: 24) (`text`, required: false): List the goals that have not been met.
* **Barriers Identified** (ID: 25) (`text`, required: false): Record any identified barriers.
* **Service Review** (ID: 26) (`note`, required: false): Record a review of the services provided.
* **Current Services Being Provided** (ID: 27) (`select_multiple`, required): Select the services currently being provided (e.g., Case management, Individual therapy, etc.).
* **Service Effectiveness** (ID: 28) (`select_one`, required): Select the effectiveness of the services provided.
* **Services to Discontinue** (ID: 29) (`text`, required: false): Record any services to discontinue.
* **Services to Add** (ID: 30) (`text`, required: false): Record any services to add.
* **Risk Assessment** (ID: 31) (`note`, required: false): Record a risk assessment.
* **Current Risk Level** (ID: 32) (`select_one`, required): Select the current risk level (e.g., Low - minimal concerns, Moderate - some concerns, etc.).
* **Current Risk Factors** (ID: 33) (`select_multiple`, required): Select the current risk factors.
* **Safety Plan Status** (ID: 34) (`select_one`, required): Select the safety plan status (e.g., Yes - current and adequate, Yes - needs updating, etc.).
* **Treatment Plan Updates** (ID: 35) (`note`, required: false): Record any treatment plan updates.
* **Was Treatment Plan Revised** (ID: 36) (`select_one`, required): Select whether the treatment plan was revised.
* **New Goals Established** (ID: 37) (`text`, required: false): Record any new goals established.
* **Action Items** (ID: 38) (`note`, required: false): Record any action items assigned.
* **Tasks and Responsibilities** (ID: 39) (`text`, required): List any tasks and responsibilities assigned.
* **Deadlines and Timeframes** (ID: 40) (`text`, required: false): Record any deadlines or timeframes.
* **Follow-Up Plan** (ID: 41) (`note`, required: false): Record a follow-up plan.
* **Next Staffing Date** (ID: 42) (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of the next staffing.
* **Next Client Contact Date** (ID: 43) (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of the next client contact.
* **Additional Notes** (ID: 44) (`text`, required: false): Record any additional notes or discussion.
* **Facilitator Signature Date** (ID: 45) (`date`, required): Enter the date the form was signed by the facilitator.

## Tips

* Always ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before signing it off with the facilitator's signature.
* Update the form regularly to reflect any changes or updates in the case management process.
* Consult with the team leader or facilitator if you are unsure about any field or section of the form.
