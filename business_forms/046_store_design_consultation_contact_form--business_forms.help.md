# store_design_consultation_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather contact information from customers to facilitate store design consultations.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `store_design_consultation_contact_form` field with the title of the consultation (e.g. "Design Consultation").
2. Select a date and time for the consultation that works best for you.
3. Choose your preferred method of contact (e.g. call, email, text).
4. Indicate the location within the store where you would like to be contacted (e.g. store front, store back, store entrance).
5. Select your preferred language for the consultation.
6. If you have any additional contact details, you can provide them in the `contact_details` field.
7. Finally, indicate the size of your store.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **store_design_consultation_contact_form**: The title of the consultation (e.g. "Design Consultation").
* **preferred_date_of_contact**: The date and time you would like to have the consultation.
* **preferred_time_of_contact**: The specific time you would like to have the consultation (e.g. 10:00 AM).
* **contact_method_preferred**: Your preferred method of contact (e.g. call, email, text).
* **contact_method_preferred**: Your preferred method of contact (e.g. call, email, text) - note: same as above, but with a different label.
* **store_location_preferred**: The location within the store where you would like to be contacted (e.g. store front, store back, store entrance).
* **preferred_contact_language**: The language you would like to use for the consultation (e.g. english, spanish, french).
* **contact_details**: Any additional information you would like to provide to help with the consultation (e.g. phone number, email address).
* **store_size**: The size of your store.
