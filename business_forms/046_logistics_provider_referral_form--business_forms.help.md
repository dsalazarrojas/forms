# logistics_provider_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The logistics provider referral form is used to gather information about logistics providers for business use. This includes details such as contact person, email, phone number, services offered, experience, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by selecting the logistcs providers that you would like to refer to.
*   Enter the contact person's name and email address.
*   Provide the phone number and any additional services that the provider offers.
*   Indicate the provider's experience level.
*   Add any relevant notes about the provider.
*   Finally, confirm whether the submission is made by you or someone else.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Logistcs Providers**: This field is a select multiple field that allows you to choose one or more logistcs providers. Choose the providers you would like to refer to from the available options.
*   **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person associated with the logistcs provider. This field is a text field.
*   **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person. This field is an email field that requires a valid email address format.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person. This field is a text field.
*   **Services**: List any additional services offered by the logistcs provider. This field is a text field.
*   **Experience**: Choose the experience level of the logistcs provider. This field is a select one field with options "Yes" and "No".
*   **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the logistcs provider. This field is a note field.
*   **Submitted By**: Confirm whether you or someone else submitted the referral form. This field is a select one field with options "Yes" and "No".
