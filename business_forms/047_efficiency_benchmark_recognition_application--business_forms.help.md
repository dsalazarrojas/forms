# Efficiency Benchmark Recognition Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Efficiency Benchmark Recognition Application form is designed to collect information about individual or team achievements in efficiency benchmarks within your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the applicant's details in the "Applicant Details" section (Page 1).
	* Provide the applicant's name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
2. Add a title to the nomination in the "Nomination Title" section (Page 2).
	* This title should briefly describe the reason for the nomination.
3. Enter the date the nomination was submitted in the "Nomination Date" section (Page 3).
	* Use the calendar icon to select the correct date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Details
**Applicant Details** (Page 1): `name`, `type: text`, `required: false`)
Enter the details of the applicant, including their name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.

### Nomination Title
**Nomination Title** (Page 2): `type: text`, `required: false`)
Provide a brief title for the nomination, describing the reason for recognizing the applicant's efficiency benchmark.

### Nomination Date
**Nomination Date** (Page 3): `type: date`, `required: false`)
Enter the date the nomination was submitted using the calendar icon to select the correct date.
