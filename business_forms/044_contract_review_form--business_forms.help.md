# Contract Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contract Review Form is designed to gather feedback and insights from reviewers on various aspects of contracts and agreements within the organization. It ensures a standardized process for reviewing contracts and making informed decisions about them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title in fields 1-2.
2. Select your department from the list in field 3.
3. Enter the review date in field 4.
4. Enter the contract number and title in fields 5-6.
5. Choose the type of contract from field 7.
6. Select the contract value (in dollars) in field 8.
7. Select the duration of the contract in field 9.
8. Evaluate the business terms, legal terms, and financial terms in fields 10-12.
9. Assess the overall risk level in field 13.
10. List any identified risks in field 14 (if necessary).
11. Check the compliance status in field 16.
12. Provide recommendations for approval or non-approval in field 17.
13. Enter any additional comments or conditions for approval in fields 18-22.
14. Sign your name as the reviewer in field 24.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Reviewer Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as the reviewer.
* **2. Reviewer Title** (text, required): Enter your job title as the reviewer.
* **3. Department** (select_one, required): Select your department from the list:
	+ Legal
	+ Finance
	+ Operations
	+ Sales
	+ Procurement
	+ Human Resources
	+ IT
	+ Executive
	+ Other
* **4. Review Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the review.
* **5. Contract Number** (text, required): Enter the contract reference number.
* **6. Contract Title** (text, required): Enter the name of the contract.
* **7. Contract Type** (select_one, required): Select the category of the contract:
	+ Service agreement
	+ Vendor contract
	+ Employment contract
	+ Partnership agreement
	+ Licensing agreement
	+ Lease agreement
	+ Purchase agreement
	+ Sales agreement
	+ Other
* **8. Contract Value** (number, required): Enter the contract value in dollars.
* **9. Contract Duration** (select_one, required): Select the duration of the contract:
	+ One-time
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 2-5 years
	+ 5+ years
	+ Ongoing
* **10. Business Terms Review** (select_one, required): Evaluate the business terms:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **11. Legal Terms Review** (select_one, required): Evaluate the legal terms:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **12. Financial Terms Review** (select_one, required): Evaluate the financial terms:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **13. Risk Level Assessment** (select_one, required): Assess the overall risk level:
	+ Low risk
	+ Medium risk
	+ High risk
	+ Critical risk
* **14. Identified Risks** (text, optional): List any identified risks.
* **15. Compliance Status** (select_one, required): Check the compliance status:
	+ Fully compliant
	+ Mostly compliant
	+ Partially compliant
	+ Not compliant
* **16. Required Changes** (text, optional): Enter any mandatory revisions.
* **17. Recommended Changes** (text, optional): Enter suggested revisions.
* **18. Negotiation Points** (text, optional): Enter items to negotiate.
* **19. Deal Breakers** (text, optional): Enter non-negotiable items.
* **20. Approval Recommendation** (select_one, required): Select your recommendation:
	+ Approve as is
	+ Approve with minor changes
	+ Approve with major changes
	+ Do not approve
	+ Requires further review
* **21. Conditions for Approval** (text, optional): Enter any conditions for approval.
* **22. Additional Comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **23. Reviewer Signature** (text, required): Enter your name as the reviewer's signature.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and access to the contract documents before starting the review.
* Reviewers should thoroughly read and understand the contract terms and conditions before completing the form.
* Reviewers should enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
* This form is designed for reviewing contracts, and it is not intended for other purposes.
* Reviewers can add any additional comments or conditions for approval in fields 18-22.
* The overall risk level and compliance status will influence your recommendation for approval or non-approval.
* Reviewers should sign their name as the reviewer's signature in field 24.
* Reviewers can enter any non-negotiable items or deal breakers in fields 18 and 20.
