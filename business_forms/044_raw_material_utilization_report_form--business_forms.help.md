# Raw Material Utilization Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Raw Material Utilization Report Form is used to document the utilization of raw materials during production processes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by selecting the **Category** and **Material Type** of the raw material used.
2. Enter the **Production Date** and **Production Time** of the material usage.
3. Enter the quantity of **Material Used** and **Wastage Amount**.
4. Add any **Remarks** regarding the production process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`material_category_id`): Select the category of the raw material from the available options to identify its type.
* **Material Type** (`material_type_id`): Choose the specific type of material being used.
* **Production Details** (`production_details`): Enter a brief description of the production process.
* **Production Date** (`production_date`): Enter the date of production.
* **Production Time** (`production_time`): Enter the time of production.
* **Material Used** (`material_used`): Enter the quantity of material used during production.
* **Wastage Amount** (`wastage_amount`): Enter the amount of material wasted or lost during production.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`): Add any additional comments or observations about the production process.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate reporting.
* Use the **Production Date** and **Production Time** fields to reference the specific production batch.
* Be detailed in your **Production Details** and **Remarks** fields to provide useful insights.
* Review and verify the entered data for accuracy before submitting the form.
