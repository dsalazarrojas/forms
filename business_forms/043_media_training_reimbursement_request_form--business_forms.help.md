# media-training-reimbursement-request-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "media-training-reimbursement-request-form" is a form used to request reimbursement for media training expenses. This form is intended for employees to submit their expenses related to media training programs, including travel, meal, and other expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you are submitting your request for the correct purpose.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Submit Form" to indicate whether you want to submit your form.
3. Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field, which is the name of the employee submitting the request.
4. Provide the date of the media training in the "Training Date" field.
5. Enter the duration of the media training in the "Training Duration" field.
6. Enter the location of the media training in the "Training Location" field.
7. Enter any travel expenses associated with the media training in the "Travel Expense" field.
8. Enter any meal expenses associated with the media training in the "Meal Expense" field.
9. Enter any other expenses associated with the media training in the "Other Expense" field.
10. Enter the date of submission in the "Submission Date" field.
11. Select the name of the approver from the dropdown list.
12. Enter the title of the approver in the "Approver Title" field.
13. Select "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Submission Status" field, depending on the status of your submission.
14. Enter any comments from the approver in the "Approver Comment" field.
15. Enter your name in the "Submitter Name" field, which is your name as the submitter.
16. Enter your email address in the "Submitter Email" field.
17. Enter your phone number in the "Submitter Phone" field.
18. Enter your department in the "Submitter Department" field.
19. Enter your job title in the "Submitter Job Title" field.
20. Enter the topic of the media training in the "Training Topic" field.
21. Enter the type of media used in the "Media Type" field.
22. Enter the date of the media in the "Media Date" field.
23. Enter the duration of the media training in the "Media Duration" field.
24. Enter the location of the media training in the "Media Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Form** (Yes, No): 
    Select "Yes" to submit your form for reimbursement, "No" otherwise.
* **Employee Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the employee submitting the request.
* **Training Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the media training you attended.
* **Training Duration** (number, required): Enter the duration of the media training you attended.
* **Training Location** (text, required): Enter the location where the media training was conducted.
* **Travel Expense** (number, required): Enter any travel expenses associated with the media training.
* **Meal Expense** (number, required): Enter any meal expenses associated with the media training.
* **Other Expense** (number, required): Enter any other expenses associated with the media training.
* **Submission Date** (date, required): Enter the date you are submitting your request.
* **Approver Name** (select one, required): Select the name of the approver.
* **Approver Title** (text, required): Enter the title of the approver.
* **Approver Approval Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the approver's approval.
* **Approver Comment** (text, required): Enter any comments from the approver.
* **Submission Status** (select one, required): Select the status of your submission as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Submitter Comment** (text, required): Enter any comments from you as the submitter.
* **Submitter Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Department** (text, required): Enter your department.
* **Submitter Job Title** (text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Training Topic** (text, required): Enter the topic of the media training.
* **Media Type** (text, required): Enter the type of media used in the training.
* **Media Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the media training.
* **Media Duration** (number, required): Enter the duration of the media training.
* **Media Location** (text, required): Enter the location of the media training.

Please review the form carefully and ensure that you are submitting your request for the correct purpose and filling out all required fields.
