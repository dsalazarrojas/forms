# Supply Chain Query Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Chain Query Contact Form is designed to collect contact information and query details from users to facilitate communication and issue resolution within the supply chain. This form is intended to gather relevant information to help resolve the user's query in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
*   Ensure that the "contact_method" and "query_type" fields are filled out as they are mandatory.
*   You can attach files or documents as attachments if necessary.
*   Provide a detailed description of your query in the "query_description" field.
*   If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask your supervisor or manager for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information**: This field is used to provide your contact details, such as your name, email, and other relevant contact information. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   **Attachment**: If you need to attach files or documents related to your query, please do so here. This can help us better understand the context of your query.
*   **Query Details**: This field is used to provide a brief description of your query. Please keep it concise but ensure that it's descriptive enough for us to understand your issue.
*   **Contact Method**: In this field, you can select one or more ways through which you'd like to be contacted. Please choose the method(s) that best suit your needs.
*   **Query Type**: This field is used to categorize the type of query you have. Please select the option that best describes your query from the provided options.
*   **Query Description**: This field is used to provide a detailed description of your query. Please ensure that it's clear and concise.
*   **Contact Person**: If you have a preferred contact person or department for your query, please provide their name and title.
*   **Contact Email**: If you have a preferred email address for your query, please provide it here.
*   **Query Type (Select One)**: This field is a single-select field where you can choose one of the provided options for your query type.
*   **Query Type (Select Multiple)**: This field is a multi-select field where you can choose one or more options for your query type.
*   **Contact Method (Select One)**: This field is a single-select field where you can choose one of the provided options for your contact method.
*   **Contact Method (Select Multiple)**: This field is a multi-select field where you can choose one or more options for your contact method.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date to facilitate efficient issue resolution.
*   If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask your supervisor or manager for guidance.
*   Please do not submit duplicate queries, especially for the same issue. If you've already submitted a query, please follow up on the previous submission.
*   If you have any further questions or clarifications, please don't hesitate to reach out.
