# Deal Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Deal Evaluation Form is a tool designed to assess the potential of a business deal. It helps to gather essential information about the deal, including its potential revenue, partner type, deal term, size, duration, and value. This form ensures that all relevant details are considered in the evaluation process, providing a comprehensive overview of the opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Deal Evaluation Form, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the first page of the form.
2. Enter the deal's Potential Revenue in the "Potential Revenue" field.
3. Select the type of partner involved in the deal by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Partner Type" field.
4. Enter the deal's term in the "Deal Term" field.
5. Choose the size of the deal by selecting "Small", "Medium", "Large", or "Extra Large" in the "Deal Size" field.
6. Enter the deal's duration in the "Deal Duration" field.
7. Enter a brief description of the deal's value in the "Deal Value" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Potential Revenue** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, `required: true`):
	+ Enter the potential revenue of the deal as a number.
* **Partner Type** (`id: 3`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`):
	+ Choose "Yes" if the partner is involved, "No" otherwise.
* **Deal Term** (`id: 4`, `type: number`, `required: true`):
	+ Enter the term of the deal as a number of months or years.
* **Deal Size** (`id: 5`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: true`):
	+ Select all applicable deal sizes, such as "Small", "Medium", "Large", or "Extra Large".
* **Deal Duration** (`id: 10`, `type: date`, `required: true`):
	+ Enter the duration of the deal in the date format (e.g., "YYYY-MM-DD").
* **Deal Value** (`id: 11`, `type: text`, `required: true`):
	+ Enter a brief description of the deal's value.
