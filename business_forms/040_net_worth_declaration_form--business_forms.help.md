# net_worth_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The net worth declaration form is used to collect information about an individual's or business's financial situation. It is intended for internal use, such as financial reporting or assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and understand each field before proceeding.
2. Enter general information (page 1).
3. Enter asset values without commas in the assets field (page 2).
4. Enter liability values without commas in the liabilities field (page 3).
5. Provide notes about your financial situation (page 4).
6. Enter your email address (page 5).
7. Enter your phone number (page 6).
8. Select a category (page 7) or type a specific category (page 8) to indicate what type of entity you are declaring net worth for.
9. Review and submit the form (page 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (page 1): Enter any additional information about your financial situation, if required.
* **Assets** (page 2): Enter the values of your assets, such as cash, stocks, bonds, or real estate. Do not include commas in your entry.
* **Liabilities** (page 3): Enter the values of your liabilities, such as loans, credit cards, or mortgages. Do not include commas in your entry.
* **Notes** (page 4): Provide any additional comments or context about your financial situation.
* **Email** (page 5): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (page 6): Enter your phone number.
* **Category** (page 7): Select one of the following categories:
	+ Individual
	+ Business
	+ Other
* **Category (Other)** (page 8): If you selected "Other", please type a specific category to describe your entity.
* **Submit** (page 9): Review and submit your form once you have completed all necessary fields.
