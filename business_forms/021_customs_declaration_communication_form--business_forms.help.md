# Customs Declaration Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customs Declaration Communication Form is a document used to communicate essential information between parties involved in the custom declaration process. It helps ensure accuracy and completeness in the declaration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Customer Information (Page 1)**: Enter the name and contact details of the customer. This section is used to identify the customer and gather their communication preferences.
	* Please enter the customer's full name, address, phone number, and email (if available).
2. **Goods Information (Page 1)**: Enter a brief description of the goods being declared. This section is used to identify the goods being imported or exported.
	* Please enter a short description of the goods, including any relevant details such as weight, value, and quantity.
3. **Contact Person (Page 1)**: Enter the name and contact details of the contact person responsible for communication. This section is used to ensure that the correct person is notified for any updates or issues.
	* Please enter the contact person's name, email, and phone number.
4. **Email and Phone (Page 2)**: Enter the email and phone number of the customer. This section is used for communication and updates.
	* Please enter the customer's email and phone number.
5. **Notes (Page 2)**: Enter any additional notes or comments related to the declaration. This section is used to provide any relevant information.
	* Please enter any additional comments or notes about the declaration.
6. **Customs Information (Page 3)**: Enter the customs declaration information, including the declaration type and goods type. This section is used to identify the customs declaration type and goods category.
	* Please select the declaration type (Import/Export) and goods type (Textiles, Food, or Other).
7. **Delivery Information (Page 4)**: Enter the delivery information, including the date shipped, time shipped, date arrived, and time arrived. This section is used to track the shipment and delivery.
	* Please enter the date and time shipped and the date and time arrived.
8. **Payment Information (Page 5)**: Enter the payment method and amount paid. This section is used to record payment details.
	* Please select the payment method (Wire Transfer, Cash, or Other) and enter the amount paid.
9. **Customer Details (Page 6)**: Enter any additional customer details. This section is used to provide further information about the customer.
	* Please enter any additional customer details or comments.
10. **Declaration Type and Goods Type (Page 7)**: Select the declaration type and goods type. This section is used to identify the customs declaration type and goods category.
	* Please select the declaration type (Import/Export) and goods type (Textiles, Food, or Other).
11. **Customs Declaration Number (Page 8)**: Enter the customs declaration number. This section is used to identify the customs declaration number.
	* Please enter the customs declaration number.
12. **Declaration Date and Time (Page 9)**: Enter the declaration date and time. This section is used to record the declaration date and time.
	* Please enter the declaration date and time.
13. **Shipment Date and Time (Page 10)**: Enter the shipment date and time. This section is used to track the shipment.
	* Please enter the shipment date and time.
14. **Arrival Date and Time (Page 11)**: Enter the arrival date and time. This section is used to track the arrival.
	* Please enter the arrival date and time.
15. **Payment Method and Amount (Page 12)**: Select the payment method and enter the amount paid. This section is used to record payment details.
	* Please select the payment method (Wire Transfer, Cash, or Other) and enter the amount paid.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask the relevant party or seek guidance from the customs declaration process.
* This form is used to gather essential information for the customs declaration process. Please fill it out carefully to avoid delays or complications.
