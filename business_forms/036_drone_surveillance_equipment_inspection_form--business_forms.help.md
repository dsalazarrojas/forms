# drone_surveillance_equipment_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document information about drone surveillance equipment inspections. The data collected will be used to maintain accurate records, identify potential issues, and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant section for each field and enter the required information accurately.
2. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all fields are completed, and any relevant remarks are entered in the corresponding fields.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: General Information
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant general information about the equipment and inspection.
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`, text, required: false): Enter details about the equipment being inspected, including make, model, and any other relevant characteristics.
* **Inspection Schedule** (`inspection_schedule`, text, required: false): Enter the schedule for the current and next inspections.

### Page 2: Equipment Details
* **Drone Type** (`drone_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of drone (Fixed Wing, Rotorcraft, Hybrid).
* **Drone Condition** (`condition`, number, required: false): Enter the current condition of the equipment on a scale from 1-5.

### Page 3: Inspection Schedule
* **Date Last Inspection** (`date_last_inspection`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the previous inspection.
* **Date Next Inspection** (`date_next_inspection`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection.

### Page 4: Inspection Results
* **Inspection Results** (`inspection_results`, text, required: false): Enter the results of the current inspection.
* **Inspection Frequency** (`inspection_frequency`, number, required: false): Enter the frequency of inspections for the equipment.
* **Inspection Status** (`inspection_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the inspection (Passed, Failed, Pending).
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, required: false): Enter any remarks related to the inspection.

### Page 5: Equipment Condition and Certification
* **Certification Status** (`certification_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the certification status (Valid, Invalid).
* **Serial No** (`serial_no`, text, required: false): Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **Certification Validity** (`certification_validity`, number, required: false): Enter the validity of the certification on a scale from 1-5.
* **Removals** (`remarks_certification`, text, required: false): Enter any remarks related to the certification.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, select_one, required: false): Select the condition of the equipment (Good, Fair, Poor, Not Applicable).
* **Removals** (`remarks_equipment_condition`, text, required: false): Enter any remarks related to the equipment condition.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are completed accurately to maintain the effectiveness of the form.
- Enter relevant remarks for each field to provide additional context.
- Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.
