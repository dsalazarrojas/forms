# Personal Finance Journal - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you track your income and expenses, providing a clear and organized view of your financial transactions. It includes fields for income sources, categories, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the source(s) of your income from the options provided.
2. Choose the category that best describes your expense.
3. Enter the amount of your income and expense.
4. Choose the frequency at which you receive income or pay expenses.
5. Add any additional notes you would like to associate with each entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **income_source** (`income_source`, select_multiple): Select from the options provided to indicate the source of your income, such as Income from Salary, Income from Investments, or Income from Dividends.
* **expense_category** (`expense_category`, select_one): Choose the category that best describes your expense, such as Food, Housing, Transportation, etc.
* **income_amount** (`income_amount`, number): Enter the total amount of your income for the selected source.
* **expense_amount** (`expense_amount`, number): Enter the total amount of your expense for the selected category.
* **date** (`date`, date): Record the date of your income or expense entry.
* **time** (`time`, time): Note the time of your income or expense entry.
* **income_frequency** (`income_frequency`, select_one): Choose the frequency at which you receive income, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **expense_frequency** (`expense_frequency`, select_one): Choose the frequency at which you pay expenses, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
