## shoplifting_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shoplifting Incident Form is designed to gather information about a shoplifting incident, including witness details, suspect information, and incident report. This form is used to document and report incidents of shoplifting that have occurred within a store or shopping area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide witness details, including their name and contact information.
2. Enter suspect information, including age, height, weight, eye color, hair color, gender, race, and any distinguishing features.
3. Report the incident, including the shop's name, the date and time it occurred, and any relevant notes.
4. Fill out any other required fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Witness Details**: Provide a detailed account of the witness's identity, including their name, address, and phone number. (Required)
* **Suspect Info**: Enter the suspect's age, height, weight, eye color, hair color, gender, and race. (Required)
* **Incident Report**: Report the incident, including the shop's name, the date and time it occurred, and any other relevant details. (Required)
* **Suspect Address**: Enter the suspect's address. (Required)
* **Witness Statement**: Provide a detailed account of what happened. (Optional)
* **Witness Phone**: Enter the witness's phone number. (Optional)
* **Suspect Age Group**: Select the suspect's age group (e.g., Teenager, Adult, Elder). (Required)
* **Suspect Distinguishing Features**: Enter any distinguishing features of the suspect. (Optional)
* **Suspect Distinguishing Features 2**: Enter any additional distinguishing features of the suspect. (Optional)
* **Witness Hair Description**: Describe the suspect's hair. (Optional)
* **Witness Eye Description**: Describe the suspect's eye color. (Optional)
* **Witness Note**: Enter any additional notes about the witness. (Optional)

Note: All fields marked as "Required" must be filled out. Optional fields can be left blank if not applicable.
