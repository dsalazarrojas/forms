# Vendor Banking Information Update - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update the banking information of a vendor.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Double-check your entries to ensure they are correct before submitting the form.
3. If you are unsure about any information, refer to your banking documentation or contact the vendor directly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the vendor's bank account holder.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the vendor's bank account holder.
* **Account number** (`account_number`, text, optional): Enter the vendor's account number.
* **Bank name** (`bank_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the vendor's bank.
* **Bank routing number** (`bank_routing_number`, text, optional): Enter the vendor's bank routing number.
* **Account type** (`account_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of account (Checking, Savings, or Other).
* **Bank branch** (`bank_branch`, text, optional): Enter the bank branch where the vendor's account is held.
* **Bank phone** (`bank_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the vendor's bank.
* **Bank email** (`bank_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the vendor's bank.
* **Bank address** (`bank_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the vendor's bank.
* **Vendor bank ID** (`vendor_bank_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the vendor's bank account. 

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any information, refer to your banking documentation or contact the vendor directly.
- This form is not intended for sensitive or high-value information.
