# Project Proposal Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for communication between project team members and stakeholders for submitting and tracking project proposals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **project_proposal** field with a brief description of your project.
2. In the **communication** field, describe how you plan to communicate with the project team and stakeholders.
3. Provide details about the project in the **project_details** field.
4. Specify the submission status in the **submission_status** field (Active or Inactive).
5. If applicable, select the relevant submission due date in the **submission_due_date** field.
6. Indicate if you want to be assigned as a reviewer in the **submission_reviewers** field (Yes or No).
7. Enter any comments or notes about the submission in the **submission_comments** field.
8. Sign and date the form as the submission reviewer in the **submission_reviewer_signature** field.
9. Sign and date the form as the project manager in the **project_manager_signature** field.
10. Enter your name as the project manager in the **project_manager** field.
11. Specify the project status in the **submission_status_2** field (Active or Inactive).
12. Specify the project status in the **submission_status_3** field (Active or Inactive).
13. Enter any comments or notes about the project in the **project_manager_comments** field.
