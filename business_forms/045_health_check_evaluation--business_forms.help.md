# health_check_evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information, medical history, and other relevant details for health evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Information" field.
2. Provide the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.
3. Specify the patient's symptoms in the "Symptoms" field.
4. Enter the patient's medical record number in the "Medical Record Number" field.
5. Enter the contact information of the doctor in charge in the "Doctor Contact Info" field.
6. Enter the doctor's name in the "Doctor Name" field.
7. Enter the doctor's phone number in the "Doctor Phone Number" field.
8. Enter the doctor's email address in the "Doctor Email" field.
9. Enter the doctor's address in the "Doctor Address" field.
10. Enter the doctor's specialty in the "Doctor Specialty" field.
11. Enter the medical group's name in the "Medical Group" field.
12. Enter the doctor's license number in the "Doctor License Number" field.
13. Enter the medical group's address in the "Medical Group Address" field.
14. Enter the patient's medical record number in the "Patient Medical Record Number" field.
15. Enter the patient's medical record status in the "Patient Medical Record Status" field.
16. Enter the date of the visit in the "Visit Date" field.
17. Enter the time of the visit in the "Visit Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's basic information such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Medical History**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or allergies.
* **Symptoms**: Specify the patient's current symptoms or health concerns.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's unique medical record number.
* **Doctor Contact Info**: Enter the contact information of the doctor in charge, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the doctor who will be handling the patient's case.
* **Doctor Phone Number**: Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email**: Enter the doctor's email address.
* **Doctor Address**: Enter the doctor's office address.
* **Doctor Specialty**: Enter the doctor's specialty or area of expertise.
* **Medical Group**: Enter the name of the medical group or organization the doctor belongs to.
* **Doctor License Number**: Enter the doctor's license number.
* **Medical Group Address**: Enter the address of the medical group.
* **Patient Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's unique medical record number.
* **Patient Medical Record Status**: Enter the status of the patient's medical record, such as "active" or "inactive".
* **Visit Date**: Enter the date of the visit.
* **Visit Time**: Enter the time of the visit.
