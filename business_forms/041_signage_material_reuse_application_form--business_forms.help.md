# Signage Material Reuse Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a signage material reuse application. It is intended for internal use within the organization and is not intended for external sharing or public display. The form helps to track and manage applications for the reuse of signage materials in different projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information about your project before filling out the form.
2.  Scroll through the pages and fill out each page as instructed.
3.  Enter your information in the required fields, and select any additional options as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (project_details, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your project, including the project name, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Display Locations** (display_locations, select_multiple, required): Choose the display locations where you would like to reuse the signage material.
* **Supporting Documents** (supporting_documents, text, required): Attach any relevant documents or files that support your application, such as images or PDFs.
* **Date** (date, date, required): Enter the date you would like to start reusing the signage material.
* **Time** (time, time, required): Enter the time you would like to start reusing the signage material.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Note** (note, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the form or the application process.
