# Digital Shelf Space Licensing Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help brands apply for licensing space on digital shelf space platforms. It collects relevant information about the brand and product to facilitate the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Brand Details page with your brand's name, description, and any other relevant information.
2. Proceed to the Product Details page and provide a clear and concise description of your product.
3. Move on to the Space Requirements page and specify the space requirements for your product.
4. On the Start Dates page, provide the desired start date for your product's availability on the platform.
5. In the Ecommerce Team Details page, provide information about your ecommerce team, including their contact details.
6. The Marketplace Team Details page requires information about your team that will be interacting with the platform.
7. Evaluation Criteria page is where you specify the criteria you expect the platform to follow in evaluating your product.
8. Finally, submit your application by clicking the "Submit" button.

* **Brand Details (Brand Details)**
  Brand details (Brand Details): A field to provide your brand's name, description, and any other relevant information.
* **Product Details (Product Details)**
  Product details (Product Details): A field to provide a clear and concise description of your product.
* **Space Requirements (Space Requirements)**
  Space requirements (Space Requirements): A field to specify the space requirements for your product, such as storage, display, and shipping needs.
* **Start Dates (Start Dates)**
  Start dates (Start Dates): A field to provide the desired start date for your product's availability on the platform.

  Marketplace team details (Marketplace Team Details): A field to provide information about your team that will be interacting with the platform.
* **Evaluation Criteria (Evaluation Criteria)**
  Evaluation criteria (Evaluation Criteria): A field to specify the criteria you expect the platform to follow in evaluating your product.
