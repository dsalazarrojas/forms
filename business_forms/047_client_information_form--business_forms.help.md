# Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Information Form is a vital tool for our team to understand your needs, provide tailored services, and build strong relationships. Please answer the form questions accurately to ensure we can serve you better.
## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with your correct contact information and personal details.
- Choose the right answers for your industry and preferred contact method.
- Select the urgency level that best describes your request.
- Optionally, provide additional information to help us serve you better.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (`client_first_name`, text, required):** Your legal first name.
* **Last Name (`client_last_name`, text, required):** Your legal last name.
* **Email Address (`client_email`, email, required):** Your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number (`client_phone`, text, required):** Your best contact number.
* **Street Address (`client_address`, text, required):** Your complete mailing address.
* **City (`client_city`, text, required):** Your city of residence or office location.
* **State (`client_state`, text, required):** Your state or province of residence or office location.
* **ZIP Code (`client_zip`, text, required):** Your ZIP code or postal code.
* **Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, optional):** Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Company Name (`company_name`, text, optional):** Your business name.
* **Job Title (`job_title`, text, optional):** Your job title or position.
* **Industry (`industry`, select_one, optional):** Your business sector.
* **Purpose of Visit or Inquiry (`purpose_of_visit`, select_one, required):** Choose why you are contacting us.
* **Service Needed (`service_needed`, text, required):** Describe the service you require.
* **How Did You Hear About Us? (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional):** How you found out about us.
* **Referred By (`referred_by`, text, optional):** If someone referred you, please mention their name.
* **Preferred Contact Method (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required):** Choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Best Time to Contact (`best_time_to_contact`, select_one, optional):** Your preferred contact time.
* **Urgency Level (`urgency_level`, select_one, required):** Select the level of urgency for your request.
* **Additional Information (`additional_information`, text, optional):** Share any extra details that might help us serve you better.
* **Would You Like to Receive Our Newsletter? (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, optional):** Choose if you want to receive our monthly updates.
* **I Accept the Terms and Conditions (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required):** Select to agree with our terms and conditions.
* **Form Date (`form_date`, date, required):** The date you are submitting this form.
