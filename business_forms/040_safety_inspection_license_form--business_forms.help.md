# Safety Inspection License Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for safety inspection licenses. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your business is in compliance with relevant safety standards and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully review each field and its corresponding label to understand what information is required.
2. Enter your first name, last name, and email address in the designated fields.
3. Provide your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD).
4. Select your license type from the available options.
5. Enter your license number and expiration date.
6. Enter the date when your license was issued.
7. Describe the relevant safety standards that apply to your business.
8. Identify the safety inspector who will be conducting the inspection.
9. Enter your contact number and address.
10. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (3): Enter your email address here. Please use a valid email address.
* **Date of Birth** (4): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD).
* **License Type** (5): Select the type of license you hold from the available options.
* **License Number** (6): Enter your license number here.
* **Expiration Date** (7): Enter the date when your license expires.
* **Issue Date** (8): Enter the date when your license was issued.
* **Safety Standards** (9): Describe the safety standards that apply to your business.
* **Safety Inspector** (10): Identify the safety inspector who will be conducting the inspection.
* **Contact Number** (11): Enter your contact number here.
* **Address** (12): Enter your address here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for guidance.
* Double-check your entries for errors and inconsistencies.
* If you need assistance, please reach out to your supervisor or manager.
