# Hiring Manager Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for hiring managers to provide feedback on their overall satisfaction and staffing levels within their teams. The purpose of this form is to collect valuable insights from hiring managers to help us improve our services and processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the level of satisfaction you feel you have with your current staffing levels:
	* Satisfied
	* Dissatisfied
2. Select the level of staffing you feel is ideal for your team:
	* High
	* Low
3. Enter your name and title:
	* Enter your first and last name in the provided text field.
	* Enter your job title or position.
4. Provide feedback on your current experience:
	* This is an optional field, but it's encouraged that you provide any comments or concerns you have about your current staffing levels.
5. Enter your ideal staffing level:
	* Select the level you feel is ideal for your team.
6. Enter your current satisfaction level (1-10):
	* This is a numeric field where you can enter your current satisfaction level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hiring Manager Satisfaction** (Required, Select multiple): Select all the reasons why you are satisfied or dissatisfied with your current staffing levels. This field allows you to provide multiple reasons, so feel free to select all that apply.
* **Manager Name** (Required, Text): Enter your first and last name.
* **Title** (Required, Text): Enter your job title or position.
* **Satisfaction Level** (Required, Select one): Select the level of satisfaction you feel you have with your current staffing levels.
* **Feedback Comments** (Optional, Note): Provide any comments or concerns you have about your current staffing levels.
* **Current Staffing Level** (Required, Select one): Select the level of staffing you feel is current for your team.
* **Ideal Staffing Level** (Required, Select one): Select the level of staffing you feel is ideal for your team.
* **Manager Satisfaction** (Required, Number): Enter your current satisfaction level on a scale of 1-10.
