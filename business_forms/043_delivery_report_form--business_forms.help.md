# delivery_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The delivery report form is a tool to collect and record information about customer deliveries.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide delivery details**: In this section, give a brief description of the delivery.
2. **Add customer information**: Enter any relevant details about the customer, such as their name, contact number, or email.
3. **Enter delivery address**: Provide the delivery address, including any relevant details such as city, state, or zip code.
4. **Contact the customer**: Enter the customer's contact information, such as their phone number or email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Details**: A text field where you can provide a brief description of the delivery.
* **Customer Info**: A text field for any relevant customer details, such as name, contact number, or email.
* **Delivery Address**: A text field for the delivery address, including city, state, and zip code.
* **Delivery Contact**: A text field for the customer's contact information, such as phone number or email.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate information is captured.
* Double-check the information you enter for accuracy, especially the delivery address.
