# Innovation Process Evaluation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from companies interested in evaluating their innovation processes. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary details for a thorough evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
3. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Innovation Process Evaluation Request Form
This is the title of the form, which you are currently viewing. Please fill out the subsequent fields to provide detailed information.

### 2. Company
* **Company** (`Company`, `text`, required/optional: false)
	+ This field is for the name of the company submitting the evaluation request.
	+ Please enter the name of your company exactly as it is registered.

### 3. Request Date
* **Request Date** (`Request Date`, `date`, required/optional: true)
	+ This field is for the date when the evaluation request was made.
	+ Use the calendar icon to select the correct date.

### 4. Evaluation Score
* **Evaluation Score** (`Evaluation Score`, `number`, required/optional: true)
	+ This field is for rating the company's innovation process.
	+ Please rate your innovation process out of 10.

### 5. Comments
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required/optional: false)
	+ This field is for any additional comments regarding the company's innovation process.
	+ Please provide any relevant comments for a more detailed evaluation.

### 6. Contact Person
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, `text`, required/optional: true)
	+ This field is for the person responsible for the evaluation request.
	+ Please enter the name of the contact person.

### 7. Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required/optional: false)
	+ This field is for the contact email of the company.
	+ Please enter a valid email address.

### 8. Company Phone
* **Company Phone** (`Company Phone`, `text`, required/optional: false)
	+ This field is for the phone number of the company.
	+ Please enter the correct phone number.

### 9. Company Website
* **Company Website** (`Company Website`, `text`, required/optional: false)
	+ This field is for the website of the company.
	+ Please enter the correct website URL.

### 10. Company Address
* **Company Address** (`Company Address`, `text`, required/optional: true)
	+ This field is for the physical address of the company's office.
	+ Please enter the accurate address of your company.

### 11. Product Name
* **Product Name** (`Product Name`, `text`, required/optional: true)
	+ This field is for the name of the product or service being evaluated.
	+ Please enter the accurate product name.

### 12. Evaluation Type
* **Evaluation Type** (`Evaluation Type`, `select_one`, required/optional: true)
	+ This field is for the type of evaluation needed.
	+ Please select 'Yes' if the company is looking for an evaluation, and 'No' if not.
