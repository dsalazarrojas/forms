# schimmas_cleaning_incident_and_improvement_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about incidents and areas of improvement within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as prompted. Each field has a specific purpose and is described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (1, date, optional): This field is used to record the date of the incident.
* **Incident Description** (2, text, optional): Describe the incident in as much detail as possible, including what happened and any relevant context.
* **Non-Conformance** (3, select_multiple, optional): Choose all non-conformances that apply to the incident.
* **Cause of Non-Conformance** (4, text, optional): Describe the underlying cause of the non-conformance.
* **Action Taken** (5, text, optional): Describe any actions taken to address the incident.
* **Lesson Learned** (6, text, optional): Record any lessons learned from the incident that can be applied to future improvement.
* **Recommended Preventative Measure** (7, text, optional): Suggest any preventative measures that can be taken to prevent similar incidents in the future.
* **Involvement Level** (8, select_one, optional): Indicate the level of involvement of the personnel or departments affected by the incident.
Note: Fields marked 'optional' are not required to be filled out. However, providing this information will help to ensure a complete and accurate report.
