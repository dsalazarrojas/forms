# Inventory Forecast Input Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to input and record forecast data for inventory management. It is used by stakeholders such as inventory managers, purchasing agents, and other team members to predict and track inventory levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Product Name** field with the name of the product being forecasted.
2. Enter the **Location ID** (a unique identifier for the location where the product is stored).
3. Select a valid **Inventory Date** (the date for which the forecast is being made).
4. Choose the **Forecast Type** (e.g. "Regular Forecast", "Holiday Forecast", etc.).
5. Enter the **Quantity** of the product being forecasted.
6. Enter the **Forecast Quantity** (the predicted quantity of the product at the specified date).
7. Enter the **Actual Quantity** (the current quantity of the product).
8. Select the **Forecasted At** date (the date for which the forecast is valid).
9. Select the **Created At** date (the date the forecast was created).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product being forecasted.
* **Location ID** (`location_id`, number, required): Enter a unique identifier for the location where the product is stored.
* **Inventory Date** (`inventory_date`, date, required): Select a valid date for which the forecast is being made.
* **Forecast Type** (`forecast_type`, text, required): Choose the type of forecast being made (e.g. "Regular Forecast", "Holiday Forecast", etc.).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of the product being forecasted.
* **Forecast Quantity** (`forecast_quantity`, number, required): Enter the predicted quantity of the product at the specified date.
* **Actual Quantity** (`actual_quantity`, number, required): Enter the current quantity of the product.
* **Forecasted At** (`forecasted_at`, date, required): Select the date for which the forecast is valid.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): Select the date the forecast was created.
