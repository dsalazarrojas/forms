# Hand Receipt Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hand Receipt Form is used to record the details of goods sold or transferred between parties in a business setting. It serves as a formal record for both the buyer and seller to acknowledge the transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with the required information for your transaction. The form can be completed in multiple pages, each page representing a separate section.
2. Enter the Shop Owner's name and Store Manager's name on pages 1 and 2, respectively.
3. On page 3, enter the number of goods sold.
4. Enter the date and time of sale on pages 4 and 5.
5. On page 6, enter the total cost of the goods sold.
6. Enter the Receipt Number on page 7.
7. Add any remarks or comments about the sale on page 8.
8. On pages 9-12, enter the Business Owner's name, Sale Price, Store Name, Store Address, Store Phone, and Store Email.
9. Review and verify the accuracy of the form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shop Owner** (Page 1, Text): Enter the name of the shop owner who sold or transferred the goods.
* **Store Manager** (Page 2, Text): Enter the name of the store manager who managed the sale or transfer of goods.
* **Goods Sold** (Page 3, Number): Enter the quantity of goods sold or transferred.
* **Date** (Page 4, Date): Enter the date of the sale or transfer of goods.
* **Time of Sale** (Page 5, Time): Enter the time of the sale or transfer of goods.
* **Total** (Page 6, Number): Enter the total cost of the goods sold.
* **Receipt Number** (Page 7, Text): Enter a unique number for the receipt.
* **Remarks** (Page 8, Note): Enter any additional comments or notes about the sale.
* **Business Owner** (Page 9, Text): Enter the name of the business owner of the shop.
* **Sale Price** (Page 10, Number): Enter the sale price of the goods.
* **Store Name** (Page 11, Text): Enter the name of the store where the sale was made.
* **Store Address** (Page 12, Text): Enter the address of the store where the sale was made.
* **Store Phone** (Page 13, Text): Enter the phone number of the store where the sale was made.
* **Store Email** (Page 14, Email): Enter the email address of the store where the sale was made.

# Tips
- Make sure to review and verify the accuracy of the form before submitting it.
- Use a unique receipt number for each transaction.
- Keep a record of all transactions for future reference.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If there are any issues or discrepancies, contact the store or shop owner for clarification.
