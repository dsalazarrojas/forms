# eco_logistics_certification_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for certification of eco-friendly practices. It helps to verify and validate the details of the applicant, including their contact information, practice description, and supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure you are submitting the correct type of supporting documents as required.
3. Double-check your selections for the "Select One" and "Select Multiple" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name and other basic details.
* **E-mail** (`applicant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Eco Practice Description** (`eco_practice_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your eco-friendly practice.
* **Supporting Files** (`supporting_files`, note, required: false): Upload any supporting documents or files related to your practice.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required: false): Choose one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple options from the list.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date in the correct format.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time in the correct format.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click this button to submit your application.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled in correctly before submitting the form.
* Double-check your selections for the "Select One" and "Select Multiple" fields.
* Supporting files should be relevant to your application and not exceeding the recommended size limit.
