# membership-complaint-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a membership complaint, allowing members to report issues and provide details for resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **membership-complaint-form** field with a brief title or summary of the complaint.
2. In the **member_complaint** field, describe the issue being reported.
3. In the **description** field, provide more detailed information about the complaint.
4. In the **complaint-details** field, provide additional notes or context about the complaint.
5. Select a **complaint-category** from the list of options to categorize the complaint.
6. Select relevant **complaint-subcategory** options to further specify the issue.
7. Select specific **complaint-subcategories** from the list of options.
8. In the **complaint-subcategory-detail** field, provide more detailed information about the selected subcategories.
9. Select a **complaint-cause** from the list of options to describe the cause of the complaint.
10. In the **complaint-effect** field, describe the effect or impact of the complaint.
11. In the **complaint-resolution** field, suggest a resolution or desired outcome.
12. Select a **complaint-cause-of-complaint** from the list of options to specify the cause of the complaint.
13. Select a **complaint-effect-of-complaint** from the list of options to describe the effect of the complaint.
14. In the **contact-information** field, provide any relevant contact information related to the complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **membership-complaint-form** (`membership-complaint-form`, text, required): A brief title or summary of the complaint.
* **member_complaint** (`member_complaint`, text, optional): Describe the issue being reported.
* **description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide more detailed information about the complaint.
* **complaint-details** (`complaint-details`, text, optional): Additional notes or context about the complaint.
* **complaint-category** (`complaint-category`, select_one, optional): Select a category for the complaint.
* **complaint-subcategory** (`complaint-subcategory`, select_multiple, optional): Select relevant subcategories for the complaint.
* **complaint-subcategories** (`complaint-subcategories`, select_multiple, optional): Select specific subcategories for the complaint.
* **complaint-subcategory-detail** (`complaint-subcategory-detail`, text, optional): More detailed information about the selected subcategories.
* **complaint-cause** (`complaint-cause`, text, optional): Describe the cause of the complaint.
* **complaint-effect** (`complaint-effect`, text, optional): Describe the effect or impact of the complaint.
* **complaint-resolution** (`complaint-resolution`, text, optional): Suggest a resolution or desired outcome.
* **complaint-cause-of-complaint** (`complaint-cause-of-complaint`, select_one, optional): Specify the cause of the complaint.
* **complaint-effect-of-complaint** (`complaint-effect-of-complaint`, select_one, optional): Describe the effect of the complaint.
* **contact-information** (`contact-information`, text, optional): Provide any relevant contact information related to the complaint.

Note: Please ensure that all fields that are marked as "optional" are filled out when submitting the form, as they are an essential part of the complaint information.
