# Volunteer Kit Issue Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report issues with volunteer kits, which are used by volunteers in the field. It is used to track and record the details of any problems that may arise with the kits, including lost, damaged, or stolen kits. This information helps to identify trends and prevent future issues.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) as you go.
* Enter the Volunteer Kit ID number.
* Enter the name of the volunteer who was using the kit.
* Enter the name of the resource associated with the kit.
* Enter the date the issue occurred.
* Enter the location where the issue occurred.
* Enter the contact information for the volunteer.
* Enter any additional notes or comments about the issue.
* Select the channel through which the kit was distributed (e.g., Volunteer Pickup, Mailing, Email, Phone).
* Enter the name of the person or organization receiving the kit.
* Select the status of the volunteer (e.g., Active, Inactive, Retired).
* Select the reason for the issue (e.g., Lost, Damaged, Stolen).
* Select the condition of the kit (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
* Enter the number of kits that were affected by the issue.
* Enter the number of volunteer hours spent on the issue.
* Enter the volunteer's rate per hour.
* Enter the latitude and longitude of the kit location.
* Enter the latitude and longitude of the volunteer location.
* Enter an alternative location for the volunteer (used for tracking purposes).
* Enter an alternative location for the kit (used for tracking purposes).
* Enter the elevation of the volunteer location.
* Enter the elevation of the kit location.
* If applicable, enter the name of the person or organization assigned to handle the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Kit ID** (\*): Enter the unique identifier for the volunteer kit.
* **Volunteer Name** (text): Enter the name of the volunteer who was using the kit.
* **Resource Name** (text): Enter the name of the resource associated with the kit.
* **Issue Date** (date): Enter the date the issue occurred.
* **Kit Location** (\*): Enter the location where the issue occurred.
* **Volunteer Contact** (text): Enter the contact information for the volunteer.
* **Additional Notes** (\*): Enter any additional comments or notes about the issue.
* **Kit Distribution Channel** (\*): Select the channel through which the kit was distributed (e.g., Volunteer Pickup, Mailing, Email, Phone).
* **Kit Reciever** (\*): Enter the name of the person or organization receiving the kit.
* **Volunteer Status** (\*): Select the status of the volunteer (e.g., Active, Inactive, Retired).
* **Issue Reason** (select multiple): Select the reason for the issue (e.g., Lost, Damaged, Stolen).
* **Kit Condition** (\*): Select the condition of the kit (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Kit Quantity** (\*): Enter the number of kits that were affected by the issue.
* **Volunteer Hours** (\*): Enter the number of volunteer hours spent on the issue.
* **Volunteer Rate** (\*): Enter the volunteer's rate per hour.
* **Kit Location Lat** (\*): Enter the latitude of the kit location.
* **Kit Location Long** (\*): Enter the longitude of the kit location.
* **Volunteer Location Lat** (\*): Enter the latitude of the volunteer location.
* **Volunteer Location Long** (\*): Enter the longitude of the volunteer location.
* **Volunteer Location Alt** (\*): Enter an alternative location for the volunteer (used for tracking purposes).
* **Kit Location Alt** (\*): Enter an alternative location for the kit (used for tracking purposes).
* **Volunteer Location Elevation** (\*): Enter the elevation of the volunteer location.
* **Kit Location Elevation** (\*): Enter the elevation of the kit location.
* **Assigned** (text): If applicable, enter the name of the person or organization assigned to handle the issue.
