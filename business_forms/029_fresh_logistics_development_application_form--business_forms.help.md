# Fresh Logistics Development Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fresh Logistics Development Application Form is designed to collect information from businesses about their logistics development needs and requirements. This form helps us understand your business's operational needs and requirements, enabling us to provide tailored support and solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in.
3. Double-check your email address and phone number for correctness.
4. Submit the form when you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your business's name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your business's phone number.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter your business's name.
* **Operational Needs** (`operational_needs`, `text`, required): Describe your business's operational needs and requirements.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting this application.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your information before submission.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact us.
