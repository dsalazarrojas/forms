# Accounting Firms Client Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accounting Firms Client Profile Form is designed to collect essential information about your business, allowing us to provide personalized service tailored to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in one section at a time, following the instructions below each section.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields (marked as "required") before proceeding to the next section.
3. Review your input carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Legal Name** (`company_legal_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name as registered with tax authorities.
* **DBA Name** (`dba_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your "Doing Business As" (DBA) name, if applicable.
* **Primary Contact Person** (`primary_contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for our services.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your preferred email address for billing and correspondence.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Office Address** (`office_address`, `text`, required): Enter your physical business address.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, `select_one`, required): Select your main business activity from the provided options.
* **Federal Tax Classification** (`federal_tax_classification`, `text`, required): Enter your company's federal tax classification (e.g., C-Corp, S-Corp, Partnership).
* **Accounting Method Used** (`accounting_method_used`, `select_one`, required): Select the accounting method your company uses (Cash, Accrual, Hybrid).
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, `number`, optional): Enter the approximate number of employees in your company.
* **Company Founded Date** (`company_founded_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date your company was founded.
* **Preferred Communication Frequency** (`preferred_communication_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you prefer us to check in with you (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure our services are tailored to your needs.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to us for clarification.
* We'll review your submission and follow up with you to confirm the details before starting our services.
