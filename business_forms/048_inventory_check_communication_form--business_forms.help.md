# Inventory Check Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Check Communication Form is designed to facilitate communication between stock management teams and other relevant parties about the inventory level and stock management status. This form helps ensure that all parties are informed and on the same page regarding inventory levels, stock management, and any communication needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully and provide clear and concise answers to the questions asked.
2. Fill in the necessary fields for the current inventory level and stock management status.
3. Specify any communication needs or requirements for the team or relevant parties.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inventory Level** (`inventory_level`): This field is used to report the current inventory level. Please enter the current quantity of items in stock.
* **Stock Management** (`stock_management`): This field is used to describe the stock management status. Please provide a brief description of the current stock management procedures or any issues that need to be addressed.
* **Communication Needs** (`communication_needs`): This field is used to identify any communication needs or requirements for the team or relevant parties. Please specify any urgent messages, updates, or information that need to be shared.

## Tips
* Be as concise as possible when filling in the fields to ensure clear and efficient communication.
* Use clear and simple language to describe the stock management status and communication needs.
* Double-check the form for any mistakes or missing information before submitting it.
