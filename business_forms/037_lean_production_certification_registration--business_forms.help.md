# Lean Production Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lean Production Certification Registration form is a crucial tool for registering participants in a Lean Production Certification program. It helps administrators collect and track participant data, including their certification status, training provider, and preferred sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your name and any other relevant details in the **Participant Details** field.
2.  Enter the name of your training provider in the **Training Provider** field.
3.  Describe your experience in the **Experience** field.
4.  Select your preferred sessions from the list provided in the **Preferred Sessions** field.
5.  Choose your current certification status from the options in the **Certification Status** field.
6.  Enter any additional submission details in the **Submission Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant details.
*   **Training Provider**: Enter the name of the training provider you have completed your training with.
*   **Experience**: Describe your experience in the relevant field.
*   **Preferred Sessions**: Select the sessions you prefer to attend.
*   **Certification Status**: Choose your current certification status from the options provided.
    -   **Active**: You are currently certified.
    -   **Inactive**: You are not currently certified.
*   **Submission Details**: Enter any additional details about your submission.

## Tips
*   Ensure you fill out all required fields to ensure accurate submission.
*   Double-check your details before submitting to prevent errors.
