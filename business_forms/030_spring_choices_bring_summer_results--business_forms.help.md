# spring_choices_bring_summer_results - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about an individual's personal preferences and habits related to exercise, diet, and health.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the required fields. You will be asked to select the options that best fit your situation. Make sure to answer all questions honestly to get accurate results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your first name.
* **Email** (`2`, `email`, required/optional): This field is for your email address.
* **Phone** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your phone number.
* **Date of birth** (`4`, `date`, required/optional): This field is for your date of birth.
* **Weight** (`5`, `number`, required/optional): This field is for your current weight in kilograms.
* **Height** (`6`, `number`, required/optional): This field is for your height in centimeters.
* **Waist size** (`7`, `number`, required/optional): This field is for your waist size in centimeters.
* **Blood pressure** (`8`, `number`, required/optional): This field is for your blood pressure reading.
* **Blood type** (`9`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is for your blood type (A, B, AB, or O).
* **Medical conditions** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for any medical conditions you have (Heart disease, Diabetes, Hypertension, Cancer, or Other).
* **Allergies** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for any allergies you have (Peanuts, Gluten, Soy, Dairy, Shell fish, or Other).
* **Fitness level** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for your current fitness level (Low, Medium, High).
* **How often you exercise** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for how often you exercise (Daily, Weekly, Rarely).
* **How long you exercise** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for how long you exercise (Under 30 minutes, 30-59 minutes, 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4+ hours).
* **Sleep duration** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for how long you sleep (Under 6 hours, 6-8 hours, 8-10 hours, 10+ hours).
* **What do you eat** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for your diet preferences (Vegan, Vegetarian, Omnivore, Carnivore).
* **What motivates you** (`17`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for what motivates you to exercise (To improve appearance, For fun, For health, To get better health).
* **What is your goal** (`18`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for your exercise goal (Weight loss, Weight gain, Health improvement, Fitness performance).
* **Which program** (`19`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for which type of program you are interested in (Strength, Cardio, Endurance, Other).
* **How often do you exercise in a program** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for how often you exercise in a program (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **How intense do you exercise** (`21`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for how intense you exercise (Low, Medium, High).
* **How often do you exercise outside** (`22`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for how often you exercise outside (Daily, Weekly, Rarely).
* **How often do you exercise indoors** (`23`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for how often you exercise indoors (Daily, Weekly, Rarely).
* **How often do you exercise on weekdays** (`24`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for how often you exercise on weekdays (Daily, Weekly, Rarely).
* **How often do you exercise on weekends** (`25`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for how often you exercise on weekends (Daily, Weekly, Rarely).
