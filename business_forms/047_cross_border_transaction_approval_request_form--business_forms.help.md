# cross_border_transaction_approval_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cross-border transaction approval request form is used to document cross-border transactions, ensuring that all relevant information is captured and recorded properly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transaction Date** (`transaction_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the transaction.
* **Transaction Amount** (`transaction_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the transaction.
* **Transaction Amount Currency** (`transaction_amount_currency`, text, required): Enter the currency of the transaction (e.g., USD, EUR, etc.).
* **Transaction Description** (`transaction_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the transaction.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, required): Enter the date of approval for the transaction.
* **Approval Amount** (`approval_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of approval for the transaction.
* **Approval Amount Currency** (`approval_amount_currency`, text, required): Enter the currency of approval amount (e.g., USD, EUR, etc.).
* **Approval Description** (`approval_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the approval reason.
* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or entity requesting the approval.
* **Requester Email** (`requester_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person or entity requesting the approval.
* **Requester Phone** (`requester_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or entity requesting the approval.

# Tips
* Double-check all fields before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* If you need assistance or clarification on any field, contact your supervisor or relevant department.
* Save a copy of the form for future reference.
