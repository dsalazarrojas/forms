# Transport Delay Root Cause Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and analyze transport delays, identify the root cause, and capture solutions and actions taken or planned to prevent future delays.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the transport delay report.
2. Select the category related to the delay (e.g., Logistics, Transport, Warehouse).
3. Provide a brief description of the delay.
4. Enter the date and time of the delay.
5. Indicate whether or not transport was involved.
6. Enter the location of the delay.
7. Describe the event that led to the delay.
8. Identify the cause(s) of the delay.
9. Specify the type of delay (e.g., On Time, Delay, Late).
10. Identify the root cause of the delay.
11. Describe the solutions and actions taken or planned to prevent future delays.
12. Add any additional information about the delay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transport Delay Root Cause Report Form Title**: Enter a brief title for the report, e.g., "Transport Delay on [Date] at [Location]".
* **Category**: Select the category related to the delay (e.g., Logistics, Transport, Warehouse).
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the delay, e.g., "Transport delay due to traffic congestion".
* **Date**: Enter the date of the delay.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the delay.
* **Transport Type**: Indicate whether or not transport was involved in the delay (e.g., "Yes", "No").
* **Location**: Enter the location of the delay.
* **Description of Event**: Describe the event that led to the delay, e.g., "Vehicle accident on the highway".
* **Cause of Delay**: Identify the cause(s) of the delay, e.g., "Weather conditions", "Mechanical issue with vehicle".
* **Delay Type**: Specify the type of delay (e.g., "On Time", "Delay", "Late").
* **Root Cause of Delay**: Identify the root cause of the delay.
* **Solutions and Actions**: Describe the solutions and actions taken or planned to prevent future delays.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information about the delay.
