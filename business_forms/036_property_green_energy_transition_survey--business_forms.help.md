# Property Green Energy Transition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for property owners, managers, or administrators to provide data on their properties' energy usage, upgrades, and contact details. It's a key component in assessing properties' progress in transitioning to green energy sources.

## How To Complete This Form
This form can be completed in a few steps:

* Ensure you have a valid property to survey and haven't filled out this form previously.
* Select the correct answers to each question, based on your property's data.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (1, introduction): A brief description of the property, explaining why you're taking part in the green energy transition survey.
* **Property Details** (2, property_details): Describe your property, including its energy usage and potential areas for improvement.
* **Energy Use** (3, property_energy_use): Specify the property's primary use of energy (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Upgrades** (4, property_upgrades): List any existing or planned energy upgrades for the property, such as solar panels or energy-efficient appliances.
* **Contact Details** (5, property_contact_details): Provide your contact information, including your name and email address.
* **Building Details** (6, property_building_details): Describe the property's building type, age, and location.
* **Survey Completion** (7, survey_completion): Confirm that you've completed the survey accurately.
* **Energy Upgrades** (8, property_energy_upgrades): Specify any energy upgrades planned for the property.
* **Property Owner** (9, property_owner): Identify the property owner's contact information.
* **Building Type** (10, property_building_type): Classify the property's building type (e.g., single-family home, commercial building, etc.).
* **Property Size** (11, property_size): Enter the property's size in square feet.
* **Year Built** (12, property_year_built): Enter the property's age in years.
* **Building Address** (13, property_address): Provide the property's address.
* **City** (14, property_city): Specify the city where the property is located.
* **State** (15, property_state): Specify the state where the property is located.
* **Postal Code** (16, property_postal_code): Enter the property's postal code.
* **Country** (17, property_country): Specify the country where the property is located.
* **Contact Email** (18, contact_email): Enter the contact email of the property owner or administrator.
* **Phone** (19, contact_phone): Enter the property owner's or administrator's phone number.
* **Property Notes** (20, property_notes): Add any additional notes about the property.

Note: The form requires the most essential information for the energy transition survey, and users must fill out the required fields (marked with *) first.
