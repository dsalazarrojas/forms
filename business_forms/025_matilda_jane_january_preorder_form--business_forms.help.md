# matilda_jane_january_preorder_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers or users to provide information about themselves before preordering something. Please fill out the form accurately and completely, but note that it is not mandatory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone Number (if applicable).
5. Select your Date Of Birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name here. If you don't have a first name, you can leave this blank.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name here. If you don't have a last name, you can leave this blank.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your Email address here.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number here (if applicable). If you don't have a phone number, you can leave this blank.
* **Date Of Birth** (`Date Of Birth`, `date`, required: false): Select your Date Of Birth from the calendar picker.
