# itw inventory form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The itw inventory form is designed to collect and manage information about inventory items. This form helps users keep track of item names, quantities, prices, categories, and other relevant details. It is an essential tool for maintaining accurate and up-to-date inventory records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the item name in the **item_name** field.
2. Enter the quantity of the item in the **item_quantity** field.
3. Enter the price of the item in the **item_price** field.
4. Select the **item_category** from the available options.
5. Select the **item_subcategory** options that apply to the item.
6. Provide a brief description of the item in the **item_description** field.
7. Enter the date the item was added in the **date_added** field.
8. Enter the date the item was last modified in the **date_modified** field.
9. Select the **item_status** of the item from the available options.
10. Upload an image of the item in the **item_image** field.
11. Enter any relevant tags for the item in the **item_tags** field.
12. Enter the assigned user for the item in the **assigned_user** field.
13. Enter the assigned tool for the item in the **assigned_tool** field.
14. Provide any comments about the item in the **comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **item_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the item.
* **item_quantity** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of the item.
* **item_price** (number, optional): Enter the price of the item.
* **item_category** (select_one, optional): Select the category of the item from the available options.
* **item_subcategory** (select_multiple, optional): Select the subcategories that apply to the item.
* **item_description** (note, optional): Provide a brief description of the item.
* **date_added** (date, optional): Enter the date the item was added.
* **date_modified** (date, optional): Enter the date the item was last modified.
* **item_status** (select_one, optional): Select the status of the item from the available options.
* **item_image** (text, optional): Upload an image of the item.
* **item_tags** (text, optional): Enter any relevant tags for the item.
* **assigned_user** (text, optional): Enter the assigned user for the item.
* **assigned_tool** (text, optional): Enter the assigned tool for the item.
* **comments** (text, optional): Provide any comments about the item.
