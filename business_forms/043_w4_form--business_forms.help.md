# w4_form.report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The w4_form.report form is a tool used to collect information from employees and their employers. This form helps in gathering necessary details for tax purposes or other HR-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name on page 1.
2. Provide the employee's address and the employer's name on page 1.
3. Enter the W-4 code (if applicable) on page 1.
4. Sign the form with the employee's signature on page 1.
5. Sign the form with the employer's signature on page 1.
6. Select the date on page 1.
7. Confirm the employee's agreement on page 2.
8. Fill in the employer's address on page 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the employee filling out the form.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address associated with the employee's information.
* **W-4** (`w4`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant W-4 code or information.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign the form to confirm its completion.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the employer associated with the employee's information.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address associated with the employer's information.
* **Employee/Employer Agreement** (`employee_employer_agreement`, text, required/optional): Confirm the agreement between the employee and employer.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the form completion.
