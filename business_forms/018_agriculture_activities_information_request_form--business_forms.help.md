# Agriculture Activities Information Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request data on agriculture activities, ensuring that the requestor's requirements are captured and communicated effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your information as a requestor:
	* Enter your full name in the "Requestor Name" field.
	* Enter your organization or agency name in the "Organization" field.
2. Specify your contact information:
	* Enter your email address for data delivery in the "Email Address" field.
3. State the purpose of your request:
	* Select from the options provided: Academic Research, Government Reporting, Commercial Analysis, Educational, or Media / Press.
4. Define your data requirements:
	* Select the primary subject of interest, choosing from Crop Production, Livestock Statistics, Land Use / Zoning, Market Prices, or Climate / Weather Impact.
	* Enter the geographic scope of your request in the "Region of Interest" field.
	* Select all relevant crops and/or livestock in the "Specific Crops/Livestock" field.
5. Provide additional details:
	* Specify the time period for which you need the data in the "Time Period" field.
	* Set a deadline for receiving the data in the "Deadline" field.
6. Choose your preferred delivery format:
	* Select from Excel / CSV, PDF Report, JSON / API Access, or Hard Copy.
7. Agree to confidentiality terms:
	* Select "I Agree" to confirm you will not share sensitive personal data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name (`Requestor Name`, `text`, required):**: Enter your full name as the person requesting the data.
* **Organization (`Organization`, `text`, required):**: Enter your company or agency name.
* **Email Address (`Email Address`, `email`, required):**: Enter your email address for data delivery.
* **Purpose of Request (`Purpose of Request`, `select_one`, required):**: Select from the options provided to state the purpose of your request.
* **Data Category Needed (`Data Category Needed`, `select_one`, required):**: Choose the primary subject of interest for your request.
* **Region of Interest (`Region of Interest`, `text`, required):**: Enter the geographic scope of your request.
* **Specific Crops/Livestock (`Specific Crops/Livestock`, `select_multiple`, optional):**: Select all relevant crops and/or livestock.
* **Time Period (`Time Period`, `text`, required):**: Enter the time period for which you need the data.
* **Deadline (`Deadline`, `date`, required):**: Set a deadline for receiving the data.
* **Preferred Data Format (`Preferred Data Format`, `select_one`, required):**: Select your preferred format for data delivery.
* **Confidentiality Agreement (`Confidentiality Agreement`, `select_one`, required):**: Select "I Agree" to confirm you will not share sensitive personal data.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you need to request data on multiple subjects, select all relevant options in the "Specific Crops/Livestock" field.
* Be specific when defining the time period and deadline to ensure timely data delivery.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
