# production_batch_integrity_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The production_batch_integrity_report form is used to document and report on the integrity of a production batch, ensuring accurate and comprehensive documentation of production-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the batch details, including batch number and batch size.
2. Record the inspection results, if any.
3. If issues occurred during production, select the relevant problems (Production Issues, Packaging Issues, or Quality Issues).
4. Document the corrective actions taken.
5. Enter the production date, time, location, and production shift.
6. Provide information about the production machine and operator involved.
7. Enter the production quality check result.
8. Record the batch weight and weight tolerance.
9. Enter the production shift start and end times.
10. Sign the shift start and end signatures.
11. Enter the production manager and supervisor's names.
12. Add any additional notes, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **batch_details** (Text, Required): Enter a brief description of the batch being reported.
* **inspection_results** (Text, Optional): Record any inspection results or findings related to the batch.
* **issues** (Select Multiple, Optional): List any Production Issues, Packaging Issues, or Quality Issues that occurred during production.
* **corrective_actions** (Text, Optional): Document any corrective actions taken to address issues.
* **batch_number** (Number, Required): Enter the batch number.
* **production_date** (Date, Required): Enter the production date.
* **production_time** (Time, Required): Enter the production time.
* **production_location** (Text, Optional): Enter the production location.
* **production_shift** (Text, Optional): Enter the production shift.
* **production_line** (Text, Optional): Enter the production line.
* **production_machine** (Text, Optional): Enter the production machine involved.
* **production_operator** (Text, Optional): Enter the production operator's name.
* **production_quality_check** (Text, Optional): Record the production quality check result.
* **batch_size** (Number, Optional): Enter the batch size.
* **batch_weight** (Number, Optional): Enter the batch weight.
* **batch_weight_units** (Text, Optional): Enter the unit of measurement for the batch weight.
* **batch_weight_tolerance** (Text, Optional): Enter the batch weight tolerance.
* **batch_weight_tolerance_range** (Text, Optional): Enter the batch weight tolerance range.
* **production_shift_start** (Time, Required): Enter the production shift start time.
* **production_shift_end** (Time, Required): Enter the production shift end time.
* **shift_start_signature** (Text, Optional): Sign the shift start signature.
* **shift_end_signature** (Text, Optional): Sign the shift end signature.
* **production_manager** (Text, Optional): Enter the production manager's name.
* **production_supervisor** (Text, Optional): Enter the production supervisor's name.
* **notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes about the production batch.

## Tips
Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form to ensure that production batches are properly documented and issues are properly identified and addressed.
