# Bond Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bond Release Form is used to document the details of a bond release, ensuring that the individual understands their obligations and responsibilities after release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Date of Release (Field 1).
2. Enter the Time of Release (Field 2).
3. Enter the Name of the Released Individual (Field 3).
4. Enter the Date of Birth of the Released Individual (Field 4).
5. Enter the Case Number (Field 5).
6. Enter the Booking Number (Field 6).
7. Enter the Original Charges (Field 7).
8. Enter the Date of Arrest (Field 8).
9. Enter the Duration in Custody (Field 9) if applicable.
10. Enter the Facility Name (Field 10).
11. Select the Reason for Release (Field 11).
12. Enter the Bond Amount (Field 12) if applicable.
13. Enter any Bond Conditions (Field 13) if applicable.
14. List any Personal Property Returned (Field 14) if applicable.
15. Enter the Date Property Inventory was Signed (Field 15) if applicable.
16. Enter the Name of the Releasing Officer (Field 16).
17. Enter the Officer's Badge Number (Field 17).
18. If applicable, enter the Next Court Date (Field 18).
19. Select whether Supervision is required (Field 19).
20. If applicable, enter the name of the Assigned Supervision Officer (Field 20).
21. Select whether a Mental Health Referral was made (Field 21).
22. Select whether a Substance Abuse Referral was made (Field 22).
23. Select whether the Released Individual acknowledges receipt of this form (Field 23).
24. Enter the date the Released Individual signed this form (Field 24).
25. Select whether release instructions were provided (Field 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Release (1)** (`release_date`, date, required): Enter the date the individual was released from custody.
* **Time of Release (2)** (`release_time`, time, required): Enter the time the individual was released from custody.
* **Name of Released Individual (3)** (`released_individual_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the individual being released.
* **Date of Birth (4)** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the individual being released.
* **Case Number (5)** (`case_number`, text, required): Enter the case number for the individual being released.
* **Booking Number (6)** (`booking_number`, text, required): Enter the booking number for the individual being released.
* **Original Charges (7)** (`charges`, text, required): Enter the original charges against the individual.
* **Date of Arrest (8)** (`arrest_date`, date, required): Enter the date the individual was arrested.
* **Duration in Custody (9)** (`custody_duration`, text, required): Enter the duration the individual was held in custody.
* **Facility Name (10)** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility where the individual was held.
* **Reason for Release (11)** (`release_reason`, select_one, required): Select the reason for the individual's release.
* **Bond Amount (12)** (`bond_amount`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the bond amount.
* **Bond Conditions (13)** (`bond_conditions`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any conditions of release.
* **Personal Property Returned (14)** (`personal_property_returned`, text, optional): If applicable, list any personal property returned.
* **Date Property Inventory Signed (15)** (`property_signature_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date the property inventory was signed.
* **Releasing Officer Name (16)** (`releasing_officer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the officer releasing the individual.
* **Officer Badge Number (17)** (`releasing_officer_badge`, text, required): Enter the badge number of the releasing officer.
* **Next Court Date (18)** (`next_court_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the next court date.
* **Supervision Required (19)** (`supervision_required`, select_one, required): Select whether supervision is required.
* **Assigned Supervision Officer (20)** (`supervision_officer_assignment`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the assigned supervision officer.
* **Mental Health Referral (21)** (`mental_health_referral`, select_one, required): Select whether a mental health referral was made.
* **Substance Abuse Referral (22)** (`substance_abuse_referral`, select_one, required): Select whether a substance abuse referral was made.
* **Released Individual Acknowledges Receipt (23)** (`released_individual_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select whether the individual acknowledges receipt of this form.
* **Released Individual Signature Date (24)** (`released_individual_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date the individual signed this form.
* **Release Instructions Provided (25)** (`instructions_provided`, select_one, required): Select whether release instructions were provided.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Enter dates in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Ensure that the individual signs this form to acknowledge receipt.
