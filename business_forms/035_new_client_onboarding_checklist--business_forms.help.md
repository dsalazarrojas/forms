# new_client_onboarding_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information about a new client during the onboarding process. It's a crucial step to ensure that the client's details are accurately recorded and that the onboarding process goes smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form as thoroughly as possible, especially if the "required" fields are indicated in the form.
2.  Make sure to answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
3.  Use the available options for select multiple fields to provide multiple choices.
4.  If a field is not required, it's not essential to fill it out unless it's necessary for your specific situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info** (`Client Info`, `text`, optional): This field is for collecting the client's name, address, and other relevant details. Enter the client's information as accurately as possible.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field allows you to select the contact method you'll be using to communicate with the client. Choose all the relevant options that apply to your situation.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the client's email address here. This field is used for communication and notifications.
* **Business Hours** (`Business Hours`, `time`, optional): Enter the client's business hours, e.g., 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. This field helps in scheduling meetings or appointments.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional information that you think might be helpful to the client's profile. This field is for miscellaneous notes or reminders.
