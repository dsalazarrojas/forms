# Retail Space Lease Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to extend an existing retail space lease agreement between a landlord and a tenant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked with an asterisk \*) with the requested information.
2. Review the optional fields carefully and fill them in if necessary.
3. Make sure to sign and date the form where required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord Name** (1): Enter the name of the landlord who is extending the lease.
* **Landlord Email** (2): Enter the email address of the landlord who is extending the lease. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to include it for communication purposes.)
* **Tenant Name** (3): Enter the name of the tenant who is extending the lease.
* **Lease Start Date** (4): Enter the date when the original lease started.
* **Lease End Date** (5): Enter the date when the original lease was scheduled to end.
* **Lease Duration** (6): Enter the new duration of the lease extension. (Note: If you are unsure, you can consult the original lease agreement for guidance.)
* **Renewal Terms** (7): Choose the renewal term that applies to the lease extension. (Note: This field is typically used for internal reference only and not intended for public display.)
* **Landlord Phone** (8): Enter the phone number of the landlord who is extending the lease. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to include it for communication purposes.)
* **Tenant Phone** (9): Enter the phone number of the tenant who is extending the lease. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to include it for communication purposes.)
* **Rent** (10): Enter the new rent amount for the lease extension. (Note: If you are unsure, you can consult the original lease agreement for guidance.)
* **Security Deposit** (11): Enter the new security deposit amount for the lease extension. (Note: If you are unsure, you can consult the original lease agreement for guidance.)
* **Renewal Fee** (12): Enter the new renewal fee amount for the lease extension. (Note: If you are unsure, you can consult the original lease agreement for guidance.)
* **Lease Extension Terms** (13): Choose the lease extension term that applies to the agreement.
*   \* Lease Type\* (14): Select all applicable lease types.
* **Lease Status** (15): Choose the current status of the lease.
* **Lease Notes** (16): Enter any additional lease terms or notes that may be relevant to the lease extension.
* **Landlord Agreement** (17): Choose the landlord's agreement regarding the lease extension.
* **Tenant Agreement** (18): Choose the tenant's agreement regarding the lease extension.
* **Lease Signed Date** (19): Enter the date when the lease was originally signed.
* **Lease Signed By** (20): Enter the name of the person who originally signed the lease.
* **Lease Terms and Conditions** (21): Enter any additional terms or conditions that may be relevant to the lease extension.
* **Tenant Signature** (22): Sign the form as proof of tenant's agreement to the lease extension.
* **Tenant Date** (23): Enter the date of the tenant's signature.
* **Landlord Signature** (24): Sign the form as proof of landlord's agreement to the lease extension.
* **Landlord Sign Date** (25): Enter the date of the landlord's signature.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully and fill it out accurately to avoid any confusion or disputes.
* Consult the original lease agreement if you are unsure about any field.
* Keep a copy of the form for reference purposes.
* Make sure to have both the tenant and landlord sign and date the form where required.
* If there are any changes to the lease terms, make sure to have both parties sign and date the updated form.
