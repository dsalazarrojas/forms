# local_vendor_accelerator_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The local_vendor_accelerator_application_form is designed to collect information from local vendors interested in becoming a local accelerator.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, select the correct application type (Service or Product) on page 1 of the form.
2. Provide your business name and any other relevant details.
3. Enter your street address, postal code, and website (if applicable).
4. Include a logo representing your business (if available).
5. Describe your business in the "Business Description" field.
6. Enter your contact number and email address.
7. Specify your business hours in 24-hour format (e.g., 10:00-20:00).
8. Enter a unique vendor ID (if applicable).
9. Select the status of your application (Completed, Draft, or other).
10. Add any additional notes about your application.
11. Enter your business address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Type** (`application_type`, select_multiple): Select the type of application (Service or Product).
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text): Enter your business name.
* **Street** (`street`, text): Enter your street address.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text): Enter your postal code.
* **Website** (`website`, text): Enter your business website (if applicable).
* **Logo** (`logo`, text): Upload a logo representing your business (if available).
* **Business Description** (`business_desc`, text): Describe your business in a few sentences.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Business Hours** (`business_hours`, time): Specify your business hours in 24-hour format (e.g., 10:00-20:00).
* **Vendor ID** (`vendor_id`, text): Enter a unique vendor ID (if applicable).
* **Application Submission** (`application_submission`, select_one): Select the status of your application (Completed, Draft, or other).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, text): Enter your business address.
