# Trademark Ownership Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and verify information about trademark ownership. It's used to declare ownership of a trademark, providing details such as registration date, country, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the following information:
	* Provide your legal name as the trademark owner or representative.
	* Enter the address where you're currently living or doing business.
	* Enter the name of the business where you're the owner or representative.
	* Enter the address of the business.
	* Choose the correct status of the trademark (active, inactive, pending).
	* Select the type of the trademark (common, certification, service, trademark class).
	* Provide a brief description of the trademark.
	* Enter the date and country of trademark registration, if applicable.
	* Select the country where the trademark is registered, if applicable.
2. Check the options carefully and make sure you're providing accurate information to avoid any potential issues or discrepancies.
