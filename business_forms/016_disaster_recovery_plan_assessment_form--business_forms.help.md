# disaster_recovery_plan_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Recovery Plan Assessment Form is designed to help organizations assess their disaster recovery plans and identify areas for improvement. It is intended for use by business continuity professionals, IT specialists, and other stakeholders involved in disaster recovery planning and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled out.
2. For each section, provide as much detail as possible based on your organization's disaster recovery plan.
3. Be concise and clear in your answers, avoiding unnecessary information or jargon.
4. Use the space provided for each field to provide detailed answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (page 1): A brief overview of your organization's disaster recovery plan and its purpose.
* **Business Awareness** (page 1): Describe the level of awareness and understanding of the disaster recovery plan within your organization.
* **Risk Assessment** (page 2): Select one of the following:
	+ High: The organization has a high level of risk and understanding of the disaster recovery plan.
	+ Medium: The organization has a medium level of risk and understanding of the disaster recovery plan.
	+ Low: The organization has a low level of risk and understanding of the disaster recovery plan.
* **Business Continuity** (page 2): Describe the measures in place to ensure business continuity in the event of a disaster.
* **Incident Response** (page 3): Describe your organization's incident response procedures and protocols.
* **Recovery Plan** (page 3): Describe your organization's recovery plan and procedures.
* **Communication Plan** (page 4): Describe how your organization will communicate with stakeholders during and after a disaster.
* **Training and Exercises** (page 4): Describe any training and exercises conducted for disaster recovery team members.
* **Testing and Evaluation** (page 5): Describe any testing and evaluation of your disaster recovery plan.
* **Emergency Contact List** (page 5): List the emergency contacts for your organization.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (page 5): Identify the person responsible for emergency contact.
* **Plan Review and Update** (page 6): Describe how often your disaster recovery plan is reviewed and updated.
* **Plan Testing and Exercises** (page 6): Describe any testing and exercises conducted on your disaster recovery plan.
* **Incident Reporting** (page 7): Describe how incidents are reported and documented.
* **Employee Involvement** (page 7): Describe how employees are involved in disaster recovery planning and management.
* **Plan Awareness and Training** (page 8): Describe how employees are trained on the disaster recovery plan.
* **Review and Update** (page 8): Describe how often your disaster recovery plan is reviewed and updated.
* **Incident Notification** (page 9): Describe how incidents are notified to stakeholders.
* **Business Continuity Plan** (page 9): Describe your organization's business continuity plan.
* **Incident Response Procedure** (page 10): Describe your organization's incident response procedures.
* **Emergency Contact Procedure** (page 10): Describe the emergency contact procedure for your organization.
* **Testing and Evaluation Plan** (page 11): Describe any testing and evaluation of your disaster recovery plan.
* **Recovery Process** (page 11): Describe the recovery process for your organization.
* **Final Submission** (page 12): Provide any additional comments or questions.

Note: This guide is intended to be a general assistance, but you should review your organization's specific disaster recovery plan and procedures for any additional requirements.
