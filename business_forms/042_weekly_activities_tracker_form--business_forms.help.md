# weekly_activities_tracker_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track weekly activities for various business forms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form for each business form you are tracking.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
4. Review and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Completed Projects** (`completed_projects`, number, required: false): This field is used to report the number of completed projects for the week.
* **Weekly Goal** (`weekly_goal`, number, required: false): This field is used to report the weekly goal or target for the week.
* **Activity Hours** (`activity_hours`, number, required: false): This field is used to report the total hours spent on activities for the week.
* **Activity Minutes** (`activity_minutes`, number, required: false): This field is used to report the total minutes spent on activities for the week.
* **Weekly Activities** (`weekly_activities`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all activities that were performed during the week. You can choose one or more options from the list.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of projects for the week. You can only choose one option from the list.
* **Completed Activities** (`completed_activities`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all activities that were completed during the week. You can choose one or more options from the list.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): This field is used to add any additional comments or notes for the week.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Business Forms** (`weekly_activities_tracker_form_business_forms`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of business form you are tracking.
* **Business Forms 1** (`weekly_activities_tracker_form_business_forms_1`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of business form 1 you are tracking.

Note: Some fields might have multiple options, and you should choose only one option per field, if it's a single-select option field. 

Please fill out the form according to your business form type and needs.
