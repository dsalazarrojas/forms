# webinar_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The webinar_feedback_form is used to collect feedback from attendees of a webinar. This form asks for opinions on the quality of the webinar, including the content, presentation, facilitation, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one or more of the options for "Webinar Feedback" to indicate your level of satisfaction with the webinar.
2. Choose a rating for "Content Rating", "Presentation Rating", and "Facilitator Rating" to express your opinion on each of these aspects of the webinar.
3. Select a rating for "Overall Experience" to rate your experience of the webinar as a whole.
4. Provide any additional comments you may have about the webinar in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Webinar Feedback**: 
	+ (Select one or more)
		- **Yes**: You were satisfied with the webinar.
		- **No**: You were not satisfied with the webinar.
* **Content Rating**:
	+ (Select one)
		- **Poor**: The content was not engaging or informative.
		- **Fair**: The content was somewhat engaging or informative, but could be improved.
		- **Good**: The content was mostly engaging and informative.
		- **Excellent**: The content was very engaging and informative.
* **Presentation Rating**:
	+ (Select one)
		- **Poor**: The presentation was not well-organized or clear.
		- **Fair**: The presentation was somewhat well-organized or clear.
		- **Good**: The presentation was mostly well-organized and clear.
		- **Excellent**: The presentation was very well-organized and clear.
* **Facilitator Rating**:
	+ (Select one)
		- **Poor**: The facilitator was not effective or engaging.
		- **Fair**: The facilitator was somewhat effective or engaging.
		- **Good**: The facilitator was mostly effective or engaging.
		- **Excellent**: The facilitator was very effective or engaging.
* **Overall Experience**:
	+ (Select one)
		- **Poor**: Your overall experience was not satisfactory.
		- **Fair**: Your overall experience was somewhat satisfactory.
		- **Good**: Your overall experience was mostly satisfactory.
		- **Excellent**: Your overall experience was very satisfactory.
* **Comments**: 
	+ A text field for any additional comments you may have about the webinar.
