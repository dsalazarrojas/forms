# store_opening_permit_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The store opening permit request form is designed to collect information for a store opening permit request. This form is used to gather details about the store's operational hours, address, and owner's information, which will be reviewed and verified by the relevant authorities to ensure compliance with local regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information required for the store opening permit request.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure the information is complete and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Name** (`store_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the store as it will be displayed on the sign and promotional materials.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the physical address of the store, including the street number and postal code.
* **Store City** (`store_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city where the store is located.
* **Store State** (`store_state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state where the store is located.
* **Store ZIP** (`store_zip`, `text`, required: false): Enter the ZIP code of the store's location.
* **Store Owner Name** (`store_owner_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the store owner or manager.
* **Store Owner Email** (`store_owner_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the store owner or manager.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the store.
* **Store Hours of Operation** (`store_hours_of_operation`, `text`, required: false): Enter the hours of operation for the store, including days and time ranges.
* **Store License Number** (`store_license_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the license number assigned to the store by the licensing agency.
* **Store Occupancy Date** (`store_occupancy_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the store began operating.
* **Store Occupation** (`store_occupation`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the store's business type or occupation.
* **Store License Expiration Date** (`store_license_expiration_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the store's license is set to expire.
* **Store Business Name** (`store_business_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the business as it appears on official documents.
* **Store Tax ID** (`store_tax_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter the store's tax identification number or ID.
* **Store License Type** (`store_license_type`, `text`, required: false): Enter the type of license or permit held by the store.
* **Store License Status** (`store_license_status`, `text`, required: false): Enter the current status of the store's license.
* **Store Licensing Agency** (`store_licensing_agency`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the agency that issued the store's license.
* **Store Additional Info** (`store_additional_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the store that is relevant to the permit request.
* **Store Notes** (`store_notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the store that is relevant to the permit request.
