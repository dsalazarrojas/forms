# Expense Reimbursement Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Reimbursement Checklist Form is a tool used to submit expense reimbursement requests for official business purposes. This form is designed to gather essential information about the expense, allowing your organization to process and approve reimbursement requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields as accurately and completely as possible.
2. If a field is marked as "required," ensure you complete it before submitting the form.
3. If you're unsure about any field or have questions, feel free to reach out to your supervisor or department representative for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Expense Reimbursement Checklist Form**: This is the title of the form, providing a clear label for your submitted requests.
* **expense_reimbursement_checklist_form_page_1**: This field is for the first page of the expense reimbursement checklist form. You will need to fill it out as instructed by your organization's guidelines.
* **Expense Amount**: Enter the total amount of the expense you are requesting reimbursement for. Make sure to enter the correct amount in numeric format (e.g., 100.50).
* **Submitted By**: Select the person submitting this expense reimbursement request from the list. Typically, this will be you or the person responsible for managing expense accounts in your organization.
* **Expense Date**: Enter the date the expense was incurred. This date should be in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
