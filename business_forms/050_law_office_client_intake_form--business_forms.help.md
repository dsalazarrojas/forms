# law-office-client-intake-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather essential information from clients visiting a law office, allowing the office to prepare and provide personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field with the requested information, making sure to enter accurate details.
2. Review and edit your information before submitting.
3. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address, following the standard email format (e.g., `name@domain.com`).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number, without any special characters (e.g., parentheses, dashes).
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): If you represent a company, enter the name of your business.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your street address or office location.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select the date of your meeting or consultation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select the time of your meeting or consultation.
* **Assigned** (`assigned`, select_one, required: true): Choose who will be meeting with you:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or details about your meeting, use this text area.
* **Email Verified** (`email_verified`, text, required: false): If your email address has been verified, enter the verification code or confirmation message. 

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the law office staff.
- This form is meant to streamline the intake process; if you need assistance, don't hesitate to ask.
