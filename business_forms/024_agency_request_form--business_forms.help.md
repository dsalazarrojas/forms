# Agency Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agency Request Form is designed to help our agency understand your business needs and provide the best possible services to meet your expectations. By filling out this form, you will be able to submit a request for agency services, and our team will be able to get in touch with you to discuss further.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide your primary contact person's name and information in the "Primary Contact Person" field.
3. Enter your business email address in the "Business Email" field.
4. Select the type of service you are interested in from the "Type of service requested" dropdown menu.
5. Briefly describe your current business goals and objectives in the "Current business goals and objectives" field.
6. Identify your target audience from the "Target Audience" dropdown menu.
7. Choose an estimated monthly budget for your project from the "Estimated Monthly Budget" dropdown menu.
8. If applicable, list your key competitors in the "Key competitors in your industry" field.
9. If known, specify the proposed project start date in the "Proposed Project Start Date" field.
10. Finally, let us know how you heard about our agency in the "How did you hear about our agency?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (text, required):** Enter the name of your company.
* **Primary Contact Person (text, required):** Enter the name of the person responsible for this request.
* **Business Email (email, required):** Enter your business email address.
* **Type of service requested (select_one, required):** Choose the type of service you are interested in (PR, SEO, Content Marketing, Social Media, Paid Advertising).
* **Current business goals and objectives (text, required):** Describe your current business goals and objectives.
* **Target Audience (select_one, required):** Choose your target audience (B2B, B2C, Non-Profit, Government/Public Sector).
* **Estimated Monthly Budget (select_one, required):** Choose an estimated monthly budget for your project (Under $2,000, $2,000 - $5,000, $5,000 - $10,000, $10,000+).
* **Key competitors (text, optional):** List any key competitors in your industry.
* **Proposed Project Start Date (date, optional):** If known, specify the proposed project start date.
* **How did you hear about our agency? (text, optional):** Let us know how you heard about our agency.
