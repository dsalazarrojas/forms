# supplier_compliance_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to verify the compliance of a supplier, gathering essential information for reporting and monitoring purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the supplier's name in the "reporter" field.
2. Provide the supplier's address in the "contact_details" field.
3. Select the supplier's type from the "supplier_type" dropdown.
4. Choose the supplier's legal status from the "legal_status" multi-select dropdown.
5. Enter the supplier's business license number in the "business_license" field.
6. Enter the supplier's certification number in the "certification_number" field.
7. Enter the supplier's email address in the "contact_email" field.
8. Enter the supplier's phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
9. (Optional) Select the second contact person from the "reporter2" dropdown (if applicable).
10. Enter the contact person's address in the "contact_address" field (if applicable).
11. Enter the contact person's city in the "contact_city" field (if applicable).
12. Enter the contact person's zip code in the "contact_zip" field (if applicable).
13. Enter the contact person's state in the "contact_state" field (if applicable).
14. Enter the contact person's country in the "contact_country" field (if applicable).
15. Enter the date of the report in the "report_date" field.
16. Enter the time of the report in the "report_time" field.
17. Add any additional notes about the supplier in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reporter** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the person reporting the supplier.
* **Contact Details** (2, text, required): Enter the supplier's address.
* **Supplier Type** (3, select_one, required): Select the type of supplier (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Legal Status** (4, select_multiple, required): Choose the supplier's legal status (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Business License Number** (5, text, required): Enter the supplier's business license number.
* **Certification Number** (6, number, required): Enter the supplier's certification number.
* **Contact Email** (7, email, required): Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (8, text, required): Enter the supplier's phone number.
* **Reporter 2** (9, select_one, optional): (Optional) Select the second contact person (if applicable).
* **Contact Address** (11, text, optional): (Optional) Enter the contact person's address.
* **Contact City** (12, text, optional): (Optional) Enter the contact person's city.
* **Contact Zip** (13, text, optional): (Optional) Enter the contact person's zip code.
* **Contact State** (14, text, optional): (Optional) Enter the contact person's state.
* **Contact Country** (15, text, optional): (Optional) Enter the contact person's country.
* **Report Date** (16, date, required): Enter the date of the report.
* **Report Time** (17, time, required): Enter the time of the report.
* **Notes** (18, text, required): Add any additional notes about the supplier.
