# Incident Improvement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Improvement Report Form is a tool used to collect and analyze data on incidents that occur within the organization, with the aim of identifying areas for improvement and suggesting measures to prevent their recurrence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Category**: Select the category of the incident from the dropdown menu. This could be Safety, Quality, or Environment.
2.  **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident, including the events that led up to it and any subsequent actions taken.
3.  **Incident Date**: Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
4.  **Incident Time**: Enter the time at which the incident occurred.
5.  **Incident Date Range**: If applicable, enter the date range for the incident, in the format mm/dd/yyyy – mm/dd/yyyy.
6.  **Incident Time Range**: If applicable, enter the time range for the incident.
7.  **Non-Conformance ID**: Enter the ID number of the nonconformance related to the incident.
8.  **Non-Conformances**: Select any nonconformances that were involved in the incident from the dropdown menu.
9.  **Root Cause**: Describe the root cause of the incident.
10.  **Corrective Actions**: Describe any corrective actions taken as a result of the incident.
11.  **Preventive Measure**: Suggest a measure or action that could be taken to prevent similar incidents in the future.
12.  **Preventive Measure Effectiveness**: Assess the effectiveness of the preventive measure suggested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: Choose the category of the incident from the options provided (e.g., Safety, Quality, or Environment). This helps in directing the report to relevant teams or departments.
* **Description**: A comprehensive description of the incident, including any relevant details, such as events leading up to it and actions taken afterwards.
* **Incident Date**: The date on which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: The time at which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date Range**: If the incident spanned multiple days or periods, enter the start and end dates of the incident.
* **Incident Time Range**: If the incident occurred at specific times or over a period, enter the start and end times.
* **Non-Conformance ID**: Enter the ID of the nonconformance related to the incident.
* **Non-Conformances**: Select any nonconformances that were involved in the incident, to help identify areas where the incident occurred.
* **Root Cause**: A clear description of the root cause of the incident, which could be a process, equipment, or personnel issue.
* **Corrective Actions**: Describe any actions taken as a result of the incident, including any repairs, maintenance, or changes made to equipment or procedures.
* **Preventive Measure**: Suggest a measure or action that could be taken to prevent similar incidents in the future, based on the root cause and any lessons learned.
* **Preventive Measure Effectiveness**: Assess the potential effectiveness of the suggested measure or action, using the provided options (Very Effective, Somewhat Effective, Not Effective at All).
* **Preventive Measure Comments**: Add any additional comments or details regarding the suggested preventive measure.
