# Client Interest Survey For Insurance Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential clients about their interest in different types of insurance. The information collected will help insurance agents provide relevant information to clients based on their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in your personal details, including Full Name, Age, Email Address, and Phone Number.
- Choose the types of insurance that interest you from the list provided (e.g., Life, Health, Auto, etc.).
- Select why you are considering new insurance or your reasons for unsatisfaction with your current provider.
- If applicable, provide details about your household (Number of Dependents, Marital Status, Home Ownership Status).
- Indicate your preferred time for a consultation (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
- Select your preferred days for contact (Monday to Sunday).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Age** (2, `number`, required): Enter your age to help us understand your life stage.
* **Email Address** (3, `email`, required): Provide your active email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (4, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **-- Insurance Interests --** (5, `note`, optional): This section is optional, but it helps us understand what types of insurance you are interested in.
* **Which types of insurance are you interested in?** (6, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Life
	+ Health
	+ Auto
	+ Home
	+ Renters
	+ Business
	+ Disability
* **Current Insurance Provider** (7, `text`, optional): If you already have an insurance provider, please enter their name.
* **Why are you considering new insurance?** (8, `select_one`, required):
	+ Better Rates
	+ More Coverage
	+ New Purchase
	+ Unsatisfied with Current Provider
* **-- Household Information --** (9, `note`, optional): This section is optional, but it helps us understand your household situation.
* **Number of Dependents** (10, `number`, optional): If you have dependents, please enter their count.
* **Marital Status** (11, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Single
	+ Married
	+ Divorced
	+ Widowed
* **Home Ownership Status** (12, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Own
	+ Rent
* **-- Consultation --** (13, `note`, optional): This section is optional, but it helps us understand your availability for a consultation.
* **Preferred time for a consultation** (14, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
* **Best day to contact you** (15, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Mon
	+ Tue
	+ Wed
	+ Thu
	+ Fri
	+ Sat

Note: Please fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
