# Wood Paneling Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a wood paneling order. It includes fields for customer details, wood type and finish selection, quantity, price, delivery date and time, comments, email, phone number, and a note section for additional customer feedback. This form is typically used by sales teams or customer service representatives to quickly and efficiently capture essential details for processing a wood paneling order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the **Customer Info** section.
	* Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. Select the type of wood you're interested in from the **Wood Type** options provided.
	* Choose one or more options that fit your needs.
3. Select the desired **Wood Finish** from the options provided.
	* Choose the finish that suits your preferences.
4. Enter the **Quantity** of wood paneling you require.
	* Ensure this is a numerical value.
5. Enter the **Price** of the order.
	* Ensure this is a numerical value.
6. Select a **Delivery Date** for your order.
	* Choose a date that suits your schedule.
7. Choose a **Delivery Time** for your order.
	* Select a time that fits your needs.
8. Add any **Comments** you'd like to include with your order.
9. Enter your **Customer Email** address.
10. Enter your **Customer Phone** number.
11. Add any additional **Customer Note** you'd like to include with your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info**: Enter your name and contact information to be associated with this order.
* **Wood Type**: Choose the type of wood you're interested in from the provided options.
* **Wood Finish**: Select the desired finish for your wood paneling.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of wood paneling pieces you require.
* **Price**: Enter the price for your order.
* **Delivery Date**: Select a date for delivery of your order.
* **Delivery Time**: Select a time for delivery of your order.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or instructions for the sales team or delivery team.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with updates.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Customer Note**: Add any additional notes or instructions for the sales team or delivery team.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your selections and entries for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
