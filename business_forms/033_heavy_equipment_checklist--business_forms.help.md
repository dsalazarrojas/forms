# Heavy Equipment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the condition and maintenance of heavy equipment. It's essential to provide accurate and detailed answers to ensure the equipment is properly maintained and safe to operate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Heavy Equipment Checklist Machine Model** field with the exact model of the heavy equipment you are checking.
2. Enter the **Heavy Equipment Checklist Year of Manufacture** with the year the equipment was manufactured.
3. If applicable, provide the **Heavy Equipment Checklist Last Serviced** date and time when the equipment was last serviced.
4. Enter the **Engine Hours** of operation for the equipment.
5. Answer the **Heavy Equipment Checklist Oil Leaks** question with either "Yes" or "No".
6. List any issues with the **Heavy Equipment Checklist Brakes** by selecting all applicable options (e.g., "Yes" for each problem).
7. Enter any details about the **Heavy Equipment Checklist Tires** in the text field.
8. Describe any issues with **Heavy Equipment Checklist Moving Parts** in the text field.
9. Provide information about the **Heavy Equipment Checklist Safety Equipment** in the text field.
10. Answer the **Heavy Equipment Checklist Operable** question with either "Yes" or "No".
11. Add any additional **Heavy Equipment Checklist Notes** about the equipment's condition.
12. Enter the **Heavy Equipment Checklist Date** of the inspection or maintenance.
13. Enter the **Heavy Equipment Checklist Time** of the inspection or maintenance (if applicable).
14. Enter the **Heavy Equipment Checklist Email** and **Heavy Equipment Checklist Phone** fields if relevant.
15. Add any further **Heavy Equipment Checklist Additional Comments** about the equipment's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Machine Model** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_machine_model`, text, required: false): Enter the exact model of the heavy equipment being inspected.
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Year of Manufacture** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_year_of_manufacture`, text, required: true): Enter the year the equipment was manufactured.
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Last Serviced** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_last_serviced`, text, required: false): Enter the date and time when the equipment was last serviced.
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Engine Hours** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_engine_hours`, text, required: false): Enter the total hours of operation for the equipment.
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Oil Leaks** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_oil_leaks`, select_one, required: false): Answer "Yes" if the equipment has any oil leaks or "No" if not.
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Brakes** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_brakes`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" for each applicable issue with the brakes.
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Tires** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_tires`, text, required: false): Enter any details about the tires, such as tread wear or condition.
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Moving Parts** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_moving_parts`, text, required: false): Describe any issues with moving parts, such as loose or worn-out parts.
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Safety Equipment** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_safety_equipment`, text, required: false): Provide information about any safety equipment installed on the equipment.
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Operable** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_operable`, select_one, required: true): Answer "Yes" if the equipment is operable or "No" if not.
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Notes** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the equipment's condition.
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Date** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_date`, date, required: true): Enter the date of the inspection or maintenance.
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Time** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the inspection or maintenance (if applicable).
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Email** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_email`, email, required: false): Enter the relevant contact email for the equipment.
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Phone** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the relevant phone number for the equipment.
* **Heavy Equipment Checklist Additional Comments** (`heavy_equipment_checklist_additional_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the equipment's condition.
