# Contract Parameter Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about your client's preferences for their contract parameters, including client contact information, survey date, and other contract specifics. Your input will help us better understand their needs and create a suitable agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your client's contact information (name, email, phone number) in sections 1-3.
2. Select the survey date for the contract parameters.
3. Choose the type of agreement (section 5).
4. Indicate the preferred contract duration (section 6).
5. Select the payment terms (section 7).
6. Specify the expected budget range (section 8).
7. Determine the scope flexibility (section 9).
8. Identify the priority of deliverables (section 10).
9. Choose the preferred communication methods (section 11).
10. Select the reporting frequency (section 12).
11. Specify the termination clause (section 13).
12. Set liability limits (section 14).
13. Check if any additional insurance requirements (section 15).
14. Specify the intellectual property terms (section 16).
15. Determine the confidentiality requirements (section 17).
16. Choose the dispute resolution method (section 18).
17. Identify the governing law (section 19).
18. If applicable, specify any special requirements (section 20).
19. Add any additional comments or preferences in section 21.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your client's full name or company name.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Enter your client's contact email address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your client's contact phone number.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Select the date for the survey.
* **Contract Type Preference** (`contract_type_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of agreement (e.g., Service agreement, Retainer agreement).
* **Contract Duration Preference** (`contract_duration_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select the preferred length of the contract (e.g., One-time project, 6 months).
* **Payment Terms Preference** (`payment_terms_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose the payment terms (e.g., Upfront payment, Net 30).
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Specify the expected budget range (e.g., Under $5,000, $50,000-$100,000).
* **Scope Flexibility** (`scope_flexibility`, `select_one`, required): Determine the scope flexibility (e.g., Completely fixed, Very flexible).
* **Deliverable Priority** (`deliverable_priority`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the priority of deliverables (e.g., Speed, Quality).
* **Communication Preference** (`communication_preference`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the communication methods (e.g., Email, Phone calls).
* **Reporting Frequency** (`reporting_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select the reporting frequency (e.g., Daily, Monthly).
* **Termination Clause Preference** (`termination_clause_preference`, `select_one`, required): Specify the termination terms (e.g., Either party with 30 days notice).
* **Liability Cap Preference** (`liability_cap_preference`, `select_one`, required): Set liability limits (e.g., No cap, Contract value).
* **Insurance Requirements** (`insurance_requirements`, `select_multiple`, required): Check if any additional insurance requirements (e.g., General liability, Workers compensation).
* **Intellectual Property Terms** (`intellectual_property_terms`, `select_one`, required): Specify the intellectual property terms (e.g., Client owns all work product).
* **Confidentiality Requirements** (`confidentiality_requirements`, `select_one`, required): Determine the confidentiality requirements (e.g., Yes, mutual NDA).
* **Dispute Resolution Preference** (`dispute_resolution_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose the dispute resolution method (e.g., Negotiation first).
* **Governing Law Preference** (`governing_law_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select the governing law (e.g., Client's state).
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, required): Specify any unique needs or requirements.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required): Add any additional comments or preferences.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML fields and explains each field's purpose in a concrete and practical manner. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please let us know.
