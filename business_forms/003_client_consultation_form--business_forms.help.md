# Client Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Consultation Form is designed to gather information from clients to facilitate effective consultations. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your needs are met and the consultation process is efficient.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, paying attention to required fields.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you represent a company, please enter the name of your company.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
* **Project Information** (`section_project`, note, optional): This section is for providing additional information about your project. You can describe your project requirements, goals, and any other relevant details.
* **What is the primary goal of this consultation?** (`consultation_goal`, text, required): Please describe the main objective of the consultation.
* **Estimated Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Select the estimated budget range for your project.
* **Preferred Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Choose a preferred start date for your project.
* **Expected Project Duration** (`project_duration`, select_one, optional): Select the expected duration of your project.
* **How did you hear about our services?** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Select how you heard about our services.
* **Additional notes or requirements** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or requirements that are relevant to your project.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure that your needs are met.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
* Please fill out the form completely before submitting it to avoid duplication of efforts.
