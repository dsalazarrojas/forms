# Project Management Maturity Assessment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Management Maturity Assessment Application Form is designed to evaluate your project's management processes, identifying areas of improvement and providing insights for better project outcomes. 

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Organization Details
Complete the first page by providing your organization's basic information:
- **Organization Name**: Enter your organization's name.
- **Organization Type**: This field is for the type of your organization (e.g., private company, government agency, etc.).
- **Country**: Choose your country from the drop-down list.
- **Industry**: Choose your organization's industry from the drop-down list.

### Step 2: Project Goals
In the second page, describe your project's goals:
- **Project Goals**: Describe your project's objectives, what you aim to achieve, and why it matters.

### Step 3: Participant Details
Provide participant information:
- **Participant Details**: Enter your participant's name and any other necessary details.

### Step 4: Project Scope
Define your project's scope:
- **Project Scope**: Describe your project's scope, goals, and deliverables.

### Step 5: Project Phases
Describe your project's phases:
- **Project Phases**: Explain each phase of your project, its duration, and key milestones.

### Step 6: Assessment Goals
Define the goals for assessment:
- **Assessment Goals**: Explain what you aim to achieve with this assessment and how it will help your project.

### Step 7: Participant Info
Provide additional participant information:
- **Participant Info**: Enter any further details about participants, such as roles or job titles.

### Step 8: Participant Role
Select the participant's role:
- **Participant Role**: Choose the role(s) that best describe the participant's involvement in your project.

### Step 9: Participant Name
Enter the participant's name:
- **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant(s) filling out this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization Details**: This page is for basic information about your organization, such as name, type, country, and industry.
  *   **Organization Name**: Enter your organization's name.
  *   **Organization Type**: This field is for the type of your organization (e.g., private company, government agency, etc.).
  *   **Country**: Choose your country from the drop-down list.
  *   **Industry**: Choose your organization's industry from the drop-down list.
- **Project Goals**: Describe your project's objectives, what you aim to achieve, and why it matters.
  *   This field helps us understand your project's purpose and significance.
- **Participant Details**: Enter your participant's name and any other necessary details.
  *   This field is for providing further participant information.
- **Project Scope**: Describe your project's scope, goals, and deliverables.
  *   This field explains what your project entails and what it aims to accomplish.
- **Project Phases**: Explain each phase of your project, its duration, and key milestones.
  *   This field details your project's timeline and important events.
- **Assessment Goals**: Explain what you aim to achieve with this assessment and how it will help your project.
  *   This field explains the purpose of the assessment and how it benefits your project.
- **Participant Info**: Enter any further details about participants, such as roles or job titles.
  *   This field provides additional participant information for reference.
- **Participant Role**: Choose the role(s) that best describe the participant's involvement in your project.
  *   This field indicates the participant's level of involvement in your project.
- **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant(s) filling out this form.
  *   This field is for identifying the participant(s) and their role in your project.

## Tips

- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form to get an accurate assessment of your project's maturity level.
- If you're unsure about any field, you can refer to your project's documentation or ask your team for help.
- This form is designed to help you identify areas for improvement and provide insights for better project outcomes.
