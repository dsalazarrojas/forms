# Business Hours And Contact Preferences Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect business information and contact preferences from business owners to help them manage their interactions with customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the business name and type.
2. Enter your contact person's name and email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, text message, social media, in-person, or online chat).
5. Choose the expected response time (1 hour, 4 hours, 24 hours, 2 business days, or 1 week).
6. (Optional) Enter any additional availability notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, text, required): Enter the type of your business (e.g., retail, restaurant, service-based).
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, text message, social media, in-person, or online chat).
* **Expected Response Time** (`response_time`, select_one, required): Choose the expected response time (1 hour, 4 hours, 24 hours, 2 business days, or 1 week).
* **Monday - Opening Time** (`monday_open`, time, optional): Enter the opening time on Monday.
* **Monday - Closing Time** (`monday_close`, time, optional): Enter the closing time on Monday.
* **Tuesday - Opening Time** (`tuesday_open`, time, optional): Enter the opening time on Tuesday.
* **Tuesday - Closing Time** (`tuesday_close`, time, optional): Enter the closing time on Tuesday.
* **Wednesday - Opening Time** (`wednesday_open`, time, optional): Enter the opening time on Wednesday.
* **Wednesday - Closing Time** (`wednesday_close`, time, optional): Enter the closing time on Wednesday.
* **Thursday - Opening Time** (`thursday_open`, time, optional): Enter the opening time on Thursday.
* **Thursday - Closing Time** (`thursday_close`, time, optional): Enter the closing time on Thursday.
* **Friday - Opening Time** (`friday_open`, time, optional): Enter the opening time on Friday.
* **Friday - Closing Time** (`friday_close`, time, optional): Enter the closing time on Friday.
* **Saturday - Opening Time** (`saturday_open`, time, optional): Enter the opening time on Saturday.
* **Saturday - Closing Time** (`saturday_close`, time, optional): Enter the closing time on Saturday.
* **Sunday - Opening Time** (`sunday_open`, time, optional): Enter the opening time on Sunday.
* **Sunday - Closing Time** (`sunday_close`, time, optional): Enter the closing time on Sunday.
* **Additional Availability Notes** (`availability_notes`, text, optional): Enter any special availability notes (e.g., holidays, closures).
