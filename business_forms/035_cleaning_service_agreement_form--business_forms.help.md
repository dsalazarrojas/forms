# Cleaning Service Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document the details of a cleaning service agreement between a service provider and a client. It includes sections for service provider information, client information, property details, service schedule, payment terms, and termination procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide service provider information (Section 1)
	* Enter the cleaning service company name (Field 2: `provider_company_name`).
	* Enter the company address (Field 3: `provider_address`).
	* Enter the company phone number (Field 4: `provider_phone`).
	* Enter the company email address (Field 5: `provider_email`).
	* (Optional) Enter the business license number (Field 6: `provider_license_number`).
2. Provide client information (Section 2)
	* Enter the client's full name (Field 9: `client_name`).
	* Enter the client's company or organization name (Field 10: `client_organization`).
	* Enter the client's address (Field 11: `client_address`).
	* Enter the client's phone number (Field 12: `client_phone`).
	* Enter the client's email address (Field 13: `client_email`).
3. Provide property details (Section 3)
	* Select the type of property (Field 15: `property_type`).
	* Enter the approximate square footage of the property (Field 16: `square_footage`).
	* Provide instructions for accessing the property (Field 17: `access_instructions`).
	* Provide parking instructions (Field 18: `parking_instructions`).
4. Schedule services (Section 4)
	* Select the primary type of service (Field 20: `service_type`).
	* Select the frequency of service (Field 21: `service_frequency`).
	* Select the services included (Field 22: `specific_services`).
	* Select services not included (Field 23: `services_excluded`).
	* Select the start date of service (Field 25: `service_start_date`).
	* Select the expected service duration (Field 26: `service_duration`).
	* Select preferred service days (Field 27: `preferred_days`).
	* Select preferred service time (Field 28: `preferred_time`).
5. Set payment terms (Section 5)
	* Enter the service fee (Field 30: `service_fee`).
	* Select the billing frequency (Field 31: `billing_frequency`).
	* Select the due date for payment (Field 32: `payment_due`).
	* Select accepted payment methods (Field 33: `payment_methods`).
	* Describe the late fee policy (Field 34: `late_fee_policy`).
6. Terminate the agreement (Section 6)
	* Describe the cancellation policy (Field 36: `cancellation_policy`).
	* Select the termination notice required (Field 37: `termination_notice`).
7. Additional terms and conditions (Section 7)
	* Describe any guarantees or insurance coverage (Field 39: `satisfaction_guarantee` and Field 40: `damage_policy`).
	* Describe insurance coverage (Field 41: `insurance_coverage`).
	* Select bonding status (Field 42: `bonding_status`).
8. Signatures and agreement dates (Section 8)
	* Enter the name of the service provider representative (Field 51: `provider_representative`).
	* Enter the service provider's electronic signature (Field 52: `provider_signature`).
	* Enter the date of the service provider's signature (Field 53: `provider_signature_date`).
	* Enter the client's full name (Field 54: `client_signature`).
	* Enter the client's electronic signature (Field 54: `client_signature`).
	* Enter the date of the client's signature (Field 55: `client_signature_date`).
