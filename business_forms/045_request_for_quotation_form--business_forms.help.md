# Request For Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Request For Quotation Form is designed to gather information for a quotation request from a business user. This form is intended for business-to-business (B2B) purposes and should only be used for requesting quotations for specific products or services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Make sure you have the necessary information for the form before starting.
2. Select the correct business id from the options provided.
3. Choose the correct supplier from the options provided.
4. Enter the desired quantity for the product or service requested.
5. Enter the unit price for the product or service requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Id** (`business_id`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to identify the business requesting the quotation. Enter the correct business id to ensure accurate tracking and assignment of the quotation.
* **Supplier** (`supplier`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the correct supplier from the provided options to ensure the quotation is sent to the correct provider.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the desired quantity for the product or service requested. This will be used to calculate the total cost of the quotation.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, `number`, required: false): Enter the unit price for the product or service requested. This will be used to calculate the total cost of the quotation.
