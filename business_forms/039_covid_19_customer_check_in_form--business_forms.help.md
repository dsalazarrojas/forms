# Covid 19 Customer Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Customer Check In Form is designed to collect information about patients who have received the Covid 19 vaccine. This form helps track the vaccine administration process, including patient data, vaccination details, and follow-up appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields for patient information (date vaccinated, vaccine type, medical office, etc.).
2. Enter the medical office's contact information (phone number and email).
3. Provide the follow-up appointment date and any additional notes.
4. Enter the patient's contact information (phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's name and any other relevant information.
* **Date Vaccinated** (`date_vaccinated`, `date`, required): Enter the date the patient was vaccinated.
* **Vaccine Type** (`vaccine_type`, `text`, required): Select the type of vaccine administered.
* **Vaccination Site** (`vaccination_site`, `text`): Enter the location where the vaccine was administered (e.g., medical office, pharmacy, etc.).
* **Medical Office** (`medical_office`, `text`): Enter the name of the medical office where the vaccine was administered.
* **Medical Office Contact** (`medical_office_contact`, `text`): Enter the contact information for the medical office (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Medical Office Phone** (`medical_office_phone`, `text`): Enter the medical office's phone number.
* **Follow-up Appointment** (`follow_up_appointment`, `date`): Enter the date and time of the follow-up appointment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`): Add any additional notes about the patient's vaccination.
* **Vaccine Dose** (`vaccine_dose`, `text`): Enter the dose of the vaccine administered.
* **Vaccination Time** (`vaccination_time`, `time`): Enter the time the vaccine was administered.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, `number`): Enter the patient's age.
* **Medical Office Address** (`medical_office_address`, `text`): Enter the address of the medical office.
* **Medical Office License** (`medical_office_license`, `text`): Enter the medical office's license number.
* **Medical Office Zip** (`medical_office_zip`, `text`): Enter the medical office's zip code.
* **Medical Office Email** (`medical_office_email`, `email`): Enter the medical office's email address.
* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, `date`): Enter the date the vaccine was administered.
* **Vaccination Time** (`vaccination_time`, `time`): Enter the time the vaccine was administered.
* **Vaccine Administered** (`vaccine_administered`, `text`): Enter the type of vaccine administered.
* **Medical Office Staff** (`medical_office_staff`, `text`): Enter the name of the medical office staff member.
* **Medical Office Staff Phone** (`medical_office_staff_phone`, `text`): Enter the medical office staff member's phone number.
* **Medical Office Staff Email** (`medical_office_staff_email`, `email`): Enter the medical office staff member's email address.
* **Medical Office Staff Address** (`medical_office_staff_address`, `text`): Enter the address of the medical office staff member.
* **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, `text`): Enter the contact information for the follow-up appointment.
