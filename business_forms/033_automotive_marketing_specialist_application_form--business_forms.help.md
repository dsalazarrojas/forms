# Automotive Marketing Specialist Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from potential applicants who are interested in an Automotive Marketing Specialist position. This form will help us assess your qualifications, experience, and skills to determine if you're a suitable fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through each section and question before answering.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Make sure to provide specific examples and details when possible.
4. Use the options provided to select the most accurate response.
5. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Information (Page 1)
- **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name.
- **Email Address** (Email): Enter your valid email address.
- **Phone Number** (Text): Enter your phone number.
- **Current Address** (Text): Enter your current residential address.

### Professional Experience (Page 2)
- **Years of Marketing Experience** (Number): Enter the number of years you've been working in marketing.
- **Years of Automotive Industry Experience** (Number): Enter the number of years you've been working in the automotive industry.
- **Current Employment Status** (Select One): Select your current employment status (Employed, Unemployed, Self-Employed, Student, or Contract Work).

### Marketing Skills (Page 3)
- **Areas of Marketing Expertise** (Select Multiple): Select all the areas of marketing expertise you have, such as Digital Marketing, Social Media Marketing, Content Marketing, Email Marketing, SEO/SEM, Paid Advertising, Brand Management, Market Research, Event Marketing.
- **Automotive Industry Knowledge** (Select One): Rate your knowledge level in the automotive industry (Excellent, Good, Fair, Limited, None).

### Technical Skills (Page 4)
- **Marketing Software and Platforms** (Select Multiple): Select all the marketing software and platforms you are proficient in, such as CRM, Analytics, Email Marketing, Social Media Management, Marketing Automation, Adobe Creative Suite, Video Editing.

### Key Responsibilities (Page 5)
- **Most Relevant Professional Accomplishments** (Text): Describe your biggest marketing wins or accomplishments.
- **Experience with Automotive Marketing Campaigns** (Text): Share your experience with automotive marketing campaigns.

### Education and Certifications (Page 6)
- **Highest Degree Attained** (Select One): Select your highest degree attained (High School, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, Master Degree, PhD).
- **Field of Study** (Text): Enter your field of study.
- **Professional Certifications** (Text): List any professional certifications you've received (e.g., Google Ads, HubSpot).

### Position Interest (Page 7)
- **Why Are You Interested in This Position** (Text): Explain why you're interested in the Automotive Marketing Specialist position.
