# Remote Work Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize remote work for employees. It ensures that the manager is aware of and concurs with the remote work arrangement. The form should only be completed by the employee and the manager.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Select the correct options for each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to your manager.
