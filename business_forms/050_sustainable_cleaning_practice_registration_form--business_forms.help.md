# Sustainable Cleaning Practice Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about sustainable cleaning practices from users. It is intended for businesses and organizations that want to showcase their commitment to eco-friendly cleaning methods. The form is used to gather relevant details about the registered sustainable cleaning practices, which will be stored for reference and potential use in further analysis or reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for your sustainable cleaning practice from the list of options provided.
2. Describe your practice in the space provided under the "Description" field.
3. If applicable, upload the output file related to your practice (e.g., a document or report).
4. Review your submission for completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, optional)
A brief description of your sustainable cleaning practice, providing an overview of what it's all about.

### **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional)
Select the category that best fits your sustainable cleaning practice from the provided options. This helps categorize and filter practices for easier reference and search.

### **Description** (`description`, text, optional)
Elaborate on your sustainable cleaning practice, including the methods, materials, and outcomes. This field should provide a detailed understanding of what makes your practice sustainable.

### **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional)
If applicable, upload a relevant output file related to your practice, such as a document or report. This can include supporting data, results, or visuals that help illustrate your sustainable cleaning practice.

## Tips
- Ensure that your description provides a clear, concise overview of your practice.
- Make sure to choose the most relevant category that accurately reflects your practice.
- If uploading an output file, ensure it is in a format that is easily accessible and understandable.
- Double-check your submission for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
