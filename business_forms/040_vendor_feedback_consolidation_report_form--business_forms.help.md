# Vendor Feedback Consolidation Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Feedback Consolidation Report Form is designed to collect feedback from end-users about their experience with a particular vendor. This form is used to gather information about the vendor's performance, communication, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide the vendor's name under the "Vendor" field.
2. Rate your overall experience with the vendor by selecting one of the five options (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, Poor) and provide a comment if necessary.
3. Answer the follow-up questions, which include:
    - Follow-up Date: Select a date for the last follow-up with the vendor.
    - Follow-up Frequency: Choose how often you follow up with the vendor (Daily, Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
    - Follow-up Type: Select the type of follow-up (Phone call, Meeting, Email, Mail).
    - Follow-up Result: Describe the outcome of the last follow-up.
    - Follow-up Outcome: Choose the outcome of the last follow-up (Positive, Negative, Neutral).
    - Follow-up Notes: Provide any notes or comments about the last follow-up.
    - Follow-up Next Steps: Describe the next steps you plan to take.
    - Follow-up Last Comment: Provide any final comment about the last follow-up.
4. Enter your email address under the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number under the "Phone" field.
6. Select the follow-up status (In progress, Complete, Closed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor (text, required)**: Enter the name of the vendor.
* **Overall Experience (select_one, required)**: Rate your overall experience with the vendor (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, Poor).
* **Communication (select_one, optional)**: Rate your experience with communication from the vendor (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, Poor).
* **Responsiveness (select_multiple, optional)**: Rate the vendor's responsiveness (On time, Late, Never met expectations).
* **Quality (select_multiple, optional)**: Rate the quality of the vendor's services or products (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, Poor).
* **Value (select_multiple, optional)**: Rate whether the vendor provides value to you (True, False).
* **Comments (note, required)**: Provide a comment about your experience with the vendor.
* **Email (email, required)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (text, required)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Rating Comment (text, optional)**: Provide any comment about your rating.
* **Rating Date (date, required)**: Select the date when you provided the rating.
* **Follow-up Date (date, required)**: Select the date for the last follow-up with the vendor.
* **Follow-up Frequency (select_multiple, optional)**: Choose how often you follow up with the vendor (Daily, Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
* **Follow-up Type (select_multiple, required)**: Select the type of follow-up (Phone call, Meeting, Email, Mail).
* **Follow-up Result (text, optional)**: Describe the outcome of the last follow-up.
* **Follow-up Outcome (select_one, required)**: Choose the outcome of the last follow-up (Positive, Negative, Neutral).
* **Follow-up Notes (text, optional)**: Provide any notes or comments about the last follow-up.
* **Follow-up Next Steps (text, optional)**: Describe the next steps you plan to take.
* **Follow-up Last Comment (text, optional)**: Provide any final comment about the last follow-up.
* **Follow-up Status (select_one, required)**: Choose the status of the follow-up (In progress, Complete, Closed).
* **Follow-up Type (select_multiple, required)**: Select the type of follow-up (Phone call, Meeting, Email, Mail).
* **Follow-up Comment (text, optional)**: Provide any final comment about the last follow-up.

Note that some fields are optional and do not have to be filled out. However, providing as much information as possible will help us to better understand your experience with the vendor and provide more accurate insights for future vendor selection and relationship management.

### Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible in the comments and notes fields.
* If you have any other comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the comments field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
