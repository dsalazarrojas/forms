# SIPOC Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The SIPOC Form is designed to collect essential information from suppliers to facilitate accurate analysis, decision-making, and communication within the business. The form is a key tool for gathering detailed data about suppliers, their products or services, and relevant contacts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the supplier's name or company name in the "suppliers" field.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of the inputs or services offered by the supplier.
3. Describe the process or manufacturing process used by the supplier.
4. Detail the outputs or products provided by the supplier.
5. List the customers or clients that the supplier works with.
6. Add any additional notes or comments related to the supplier.
7. If available, provide the supplier's email address.
8. If available, enter the supplier's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **suppliers** (`text`, required): Enter the supplier's name or company name.
* **inputs** (`text`, required): Describe the inputs or services offered by the supplier.
* **process** (`text`, required): Describe the process or manufacturing process used by the supplier.
* **outputs** (`text`, required): Detail the outputs or products provided by the supplier.
* **customers** (`text`, required): List the customers or clients that the supplier works with.
* **notes** (`note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments related to the supplier.
* **email** (`email`, optional): If available, provide the supplier's email address.
* **phone** (`text`, optional): If available, enter the supplier's phone number.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields (suppliers, inputs, process, outputs, and customers) are filled out for every supplier.
* If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the supplier or contact them directly for clarification.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out this form to ensure accurate information is captured.
* The information collected in this form will be used to inform business decisions and communication with the supplier.
