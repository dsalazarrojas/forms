# Process Safety Expert Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a contact form for users to reach out to a process safety expert for assistance or guidance on process safety-related matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This should be a valid email address that the process safety expert can use to contact you.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your **Company Name** in the "Company" field.
6. Enter your **Job Title** in the "Job Title" field.
7. Select your areas of **Expertise** from the list of options provided in the "Expertise" field. This will help the process safety expert direct your question to the most relevant expert.
8. Enter your **Location** in the "Location" field.
9. In the "Message" field, provide a detailed description of your question or issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name to identify yourself to the process safety expert.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name to identify yourself to the process safety expert.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address so the process safety expert can contact you directly.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so the process safety expert can contact you by phone if necessary.
* **Company**: Enter your company name for the process safety expert's reference.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title to help the process safety expert understand your role and responsibilities.
* **Expertise**: Select one or more areas of expertise from the list provided:
	+ **Chemical**: Expertise in chemical-related processes and safety.
	+ **Mechanical**: Expertise in mechanical-related processes and safety.
	+ **Electrical**: Expertise in electrical-related processes and safety.
	+ **Civil**: Expertise in civil-related processes and safety.
	+ **Structural**: Expertise in structural-related processes and safety.
	+ **Environmental**: Expertise in environmental-related processes and safety.
* **Location**: Enter your location to help the process safety expert understand your context.
* **Message**: Provide a detailed description of your question or issue, including any relevant details or context.
