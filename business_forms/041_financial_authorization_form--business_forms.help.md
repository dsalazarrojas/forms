# financial_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The financial authorization form is a document used to request approval for financial transactions. It is used to gather information about a transaction, including the type of transaction, amount, date, and other relevant details. This form is used to ensure that financial transactions are properly authorized and tracked within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of transaction being authorized.
2.  Enter the amount of the transaction.
3.  Enter the date of the transaction.
4.  Enter the date of authorization.
5.  Enter your name.
6.  Enter the name of the organization involved.
7.  Enter your phone number.
8.  Enter your email address.
9.  Provide a brief description of the transaction.
10. Provide a reason for the transaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Transaction Type** (`transaction_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of transaction being authorized. Choose from the options: transaction type, Other.
*   **Amount** (`amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of the transaction.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the transaction.
*   **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of authorization.
*   **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
*   **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the organization involved.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
*   **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the transaction.
*   **Reason** (`reason`, text, required: false): Provide a reason for the transaction.
