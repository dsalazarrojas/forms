# Travel Expense Receipt Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Expense Receipt Validation Form is a tool used by employees to submit and validate their travel-related expenses. This form is essential for ensuring that all travel expenses are properly documented and validated for reimbursement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the start date of your trip in the "Start Date" field.
2. Enter the end date of your trip in the "End Date" field.
3. Select the type of travel you undertook (Business or Personal) in the "Travel Type" field.
4. Choose the type of expense you are claiming (e.g., Food, Lodging, Transportation, etc.) in the "Expense Type" field.
5. Enter a unique receipt ID for the trip in the "Receipt ID" field.
6. Enter the total expense amount in the "Total Expense" field.
7. Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Employee Email" field.
9. Enter the date you are submitting the form in the "Submit Date" field.
10. Enter the name of the person submitting the form in the "Submitter Name" field.
11. Enter the email address of the person submitting the form in the "Submitter Email" field.
12. Enter the contact information of the person submitting the form (phone number, title, organization) in the fields provided.
