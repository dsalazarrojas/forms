# Industrial Compliance Renewal Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for companies to register their industrial compliance certifications and updates. It is designed to be a general-purpose form for various industries and categories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure it matches your needs.
2. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) in the field labels.
3. Provide accurate and clear information in each field.
4. Double-check your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (page 1)
* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about your company's compliance renewal registration.
### Registration Information (page 2)
* **Registration Information** (`registration_information`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide details about your company's registration for industrial compliance.
### Certification Information (page 3)
* **Certification Information** (`certification_information`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide details about your company's certification for industrial compliance.
## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately.
- Double-check your submission before submitting.
- If you need further assistance, contact us at form@gic.mx.
