# production_intelligence_certification_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect application details, certification level, motivation, contact details, submission details, submission status, assigned user, and submission comments from users for certification registration. This form is used by production intelligence team to verify the submitted applications and provide certification status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in the application details in the first field. This includes any relevant information about your application.
2.  Select the correct certification level (Basic, Advanced, or Expert) for your application.
3.  Enter your motivation for submitting this application.
4.  Provide your contact details, including any relevant information about how to get in touch with you.
5.  Enter any submission details, including any additional information about your submission.
6.  Select the current submission status (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
7.  Select one or more users to be assigned to review your application.
8.  Add any submission comments, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Application Details**: A text field to enter your application details.
  - This is a free-text field for you to enter any relevant information about your application.

- **Certification Level**: A dropdown menu to select your certification level.
  - Select the level of certification that you feel you have achieved.

- **Motivation**: A text field to enter your motivation for submission.
  - Enter a brief statement explaining why you are submitting this application.

- **Contact Details**: A text field to enter your contact details.
  - Enter any relevant contact information, such as email or phone number.

- **Submission Details**: A text field to enter any additional submission details.
  - Enter any additional information about your submission that may be relevant.

- **Submission Status**: A dropdown menu to select the current status of your submission.
  - Select the current status of your submission from the list: Pending, Approved, or Rejected.

- **Assigned User**: A multiselect field to select one or more users to assign your submission to.
  - Select one or more users from the list provided to be assigned to review your application.

- **Submission Comments**: A text field to add any comments on your submission.
  - Enter any additional comments that may be relevant for the review process.
