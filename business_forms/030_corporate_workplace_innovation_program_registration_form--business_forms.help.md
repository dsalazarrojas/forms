# corporate_workplace_innovation_program_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The corporate workplace innovation program registration form is designed to collect information from employees or users who wish to register for the corporate innovation program. This form is used to gather necessary details about the applicant, such as their selected category, a brief description of their idea, and a unique form ID.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category for your registration (Category field).
2. Provide a brief description of your idea (Description field).
3. Select a unique form ID (Form Id field).
4. Enter a title for your registration (Title field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, Select One, Required): Select the category that best fits your registration, such as "Business Forms" or "Registration Forms".
* **Description** (`Description`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your idea or registration.
* **Form Id** (`Form Id`, Select Multiple, Required): Select a unique ID for your registration from the provided options, such as "9092" or "20460".
* **Title** (`Title`, Text, Optional): Enter a title for your registration.
