# ESG Policy Alignment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The ESG Policy Alignment Request Form is used to gather information from users who have requested alignment with the organization's ESG policies. This form helps the sustainability team and compliance team to review and evaluate the request, ensuring that the organization is meeting its ESG standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. Each field is required to ensure a proper review.
2. Choose the correct options for the Sustainability Review field, as this will determine who reviews your request.
3. Upload any relevant supporting documents, including sustainability plans and other documents.
4. Provide a clear and concise comments section, explaining your request and any supporting information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`): Enter a brief description of your request for ESG alignment.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`): Upload any relevant documents that support your request, such as existing sustainability plans or policies.
* **Sustainability Review** (`sustainability_review`): Choose who will review your request:
	+ Compliance Team: Review by the Compliance Team
	+ Sustainability Team: Review by the Sustainability Team
* **Request Review** (`request_review`): Choose which teams will review your request:
	+ Sustainability Team
	+ Compliance Team
* **Upload Sustainability Plan** (`upload_sustainability_plan`): Upload a new sustainability plan to support your request.
* **Upload Supporting Documents** (`upload_supporting_documents`): Upload additional documents that support your request.
* **Sustainability Rating** (`sustainability_rating`): Enter a numerical rating of your request's sustainability impact.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`): Enter the date you would like your request to be reviewed.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`): Enter the time you would like your request to be reviewed.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide a clear and concise description of your request and any supporting information.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your organization's address.
* **Organization** (`organization`): Enter your organization's name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`): Enter your job title or position.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter your department or team.
* **Upload Other Documents** (`upload_other_documents`): Upload any other relevant documents that support your request.
* **Upload Sustainability Plan Update** (`upload_sustainability_plan_update`): Upload an update to your sustainability plan.
* **Upload Other Sustainability Documents Update** (`upload_other_sustainability_documents_update`): Upload updates to other sustainability documents.
* **Uploaded Sustainability Documents** (`uploaded_sustainability_documents`): List any documents you have uploaded.
* **Uploaded Other Documents** (`uploaded_other_documents`): List any other documents you have uploaded.
* **Uploaded Sustainability Plan Update** (`uploaded_sustainability_plan_update`): List any updates to your sustainability plan.
* **Uploaded Other Sustainability Documents Update** (`uploaded_other_sustainability_documents_update`): List any updates to other sustainability documents.

## Tips
- Be concise and clear in your comments section.
- Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
- Review your form before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
