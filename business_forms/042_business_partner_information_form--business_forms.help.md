# Business Partner Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information about your business partner to facilitate partnerships and collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information for each field. All fields marked as required must be completed.
2. Be sure to choose the correct options for select fields such as industry, company size, years in business, annual revenue, and business type.
3. Enter the accurate information in the text fields for partner name, email, phone number, company name, address, city, state, zip, website, primary contact name, title, email, phone, and capabilities.
4. For select multiple fields like partnership interests and target customers, you can select multiple options.
5. For fields like references and additional information, you can provide as much detail as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Name**: Enter the full legal name of your business partner.
* **Email Address**: Enter the primary email address of your business partner.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number of your business partner.
* **Company Name**: Enter the legal business name of your company.
* **Company Address**: Enter the full address of your company.
* **City**: Enter the city location of your company.
* **State**: Enter the state or province of your company.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the ZIP or postal code of your company.
* **Company Website**: Enter the URL of your company website (optional).
* **Industry**: Choose the primary industry of your business partner.
* **Company Size**: Choose the number of employees in your company.
* **Years in Business**: Enter how long your company has been in business.
* **Annual Revenue**: Choose the approximate annual revenue of your company.
* **Business Type**: Choose the legal structure of your business.
* **Tax ID Number**: Enter the EIN or tax identification number of your business.
* **Primary Contact Name**: Enter the main contact person's name.
* **Primary Contact Title**: Enter the job title of the primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Partnership Interest**: Select the type of partnership interests you have.
* **Company Capabilities**: Enter the core capabilities of your company.
* **Target Customers**: Describe the customers you serve.
* **Professional References**: List 2-3 professional references (optional).
* **Additional Information**: Provide any other relevant details about your business partner.
