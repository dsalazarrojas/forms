## Financial Optimization Program Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Optimization Program Contact Form is a tool used to collect contact information from users interested in participating in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Enter your contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
5. In the "Department" field, select the department that best aligns with your role or area of interest within the organization.
6. In the "Expected Response Time" field, select the time frame in which you would like to receive a response to your inquiry.
7. Finally, enter a brief message describing your interest in the Financial Optimization Program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of the person to be contacted.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, Optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by phone.
* **Department** (`Department`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose the department that best aligns with your role or area of interest within the organization. Options include: Financial Planning, Accounting, Operations, HR, and IT.
* **Expected Response Time** (`Expected Response Time`, `time`, Optional): Select the time frame in which you would like to receive a response to your inquiry. This field is used to help tailor the program experience to your needs.
* **Message** (`Message`, `text`, Optional): Enter a brief message describing your interest in the Financial Optimization Program.
