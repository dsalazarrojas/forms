# Local Host Partnership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register new partnerships with the company, collecting information about the individual or company seeking to partner with us, and verifying their contact and partner details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure that all information entered is up-to-date and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Company Name** (text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter your address.
* **Contact Number** (text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (text, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Person** (text, required): Enter the name of the person you are contacting.
* **Contact Role** (text, required): Enter the role of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
* **Partner Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your partner.
* **Partner Email** (text, required): Enter the email address of your partner.
* **Partner Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of your partner.
* **Partner Address** (text, required): Enter the address of your partner.
* **Partner Company Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your partner's company.
