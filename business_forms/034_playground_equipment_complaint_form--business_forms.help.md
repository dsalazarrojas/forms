# playground_equipment_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The playground equipment complaint form is used to report issues or concerns about the playground equipment in the park or playground area. This form helps to inform the maintenance team about the problems and ensure that they are addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information about yourself, including your name and contact details in the "Contact Information" field.
2. Provide details about the equipment that's causing the issue in the "Equipment Details" field.
3. Describe the problem or issue with the equipment in the "Issue Description" field.
4. Specify where the report location is in the "Report Location" field.
5. If you have any additional information or comments, enter them in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information**:
	+ (playground_equipment_complaint_form_2, text, required): Please enter your name and contact information, such as phone number and email address, so that we can get in touch with you to follow up on your complaint.
* **Equipment Details**:
	+ (playground_equipment_complaint_form_3, text, required): Describe the equipment that's causing the issue, including its location and type (e.g., swing, slide, etc.).
* **Issue Description**:
	+ (playground_equipment_complaint_form_4, text, required): Clearly describe the problem or issue with the equipment, including any symptoms or behaviors that you've observed.
* **Report Location**:
	+ (playground_equipment_complaint_form_5, text, required): Identify the location of the equipment where the issue is happening, such as the playground name or park area.
* **Additional Information**:
	+ (playground_equipment_complaint_form_6, text, optional): If you have any additional comments, photos, or information that might help us address the issue, please enter them here.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that we can efficiently address the issue.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We'll get in touch with you to follow up on your complaint and keep you updated on the progress.
