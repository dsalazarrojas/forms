# Product Defect Report Form 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document product defects, damages, or losses reported by buyers. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the issue is properly addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Start by selecting the type of product that is being reported (Product, Service, or Other).
2. Enter the product name, description, and category.
3. Choose the condition of the product (New, Used, or Refurbished).
4. Select the type of report being made (Defect, Damage, or Loss).
5. Enter the buyer's name, email, phone number, and address.
6. Attach any relevant images or documents to support the report.
7. Indicate if the product will be reordered.
8. Enter any additional comments or explanations.
9. Finally, select a date for the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product being reported. This is a text field for you to enter a brief description of the product.
* **Product Description**: Provide a more detailed description of the product, including any relevant details about its condition.
* **Product Category**: Select the category of the product from the options provided (Product, Service, or Other).
* **Product Condition**: Choose the condition of the product from the options provided (New, Used, or Refurbished).
* **Report Type**: Select the type of report being made from the options provided (Defect, Damage, or Loss).
* **Buyer Name**: Enter the name of the buyer who is making the report.
* **Buyer Email**: Enter the buyer's email address for contact purposes.
* **Buyer Phone**: Enter the buyer's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Buyer Address**: Enter the buyer's physical address for contact purposes.
* **Defect Image**: Attach any relevant images or documents that support the report.
* **Product Image**: Attach any relevant images or documents that support the product description.
* **Reported By**: Enter the person responsible for submitting the report.
* **Reported Date**: Select a date for the report, which will help in tracking the report's timeline.
* **Reorder**: Indicate if the product will be reordered due to the defect or damage.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or explanations about the report.
* **Reorder By**: Enter the person responsible for reordering the product.
* **Report Date**: Select a date for the report, which will help in tracking the report's timeline.
