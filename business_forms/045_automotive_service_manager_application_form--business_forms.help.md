# Automotive Service Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential candidates for an Automotive Service Manager position.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Share your current address, including your city, state, and zip code.
3. Provide your years of management experience, including both management and automotive service experience.
4. List your previous management positions and the size of teams you have managed.
5. Assess your leadership skills, customer service focus, technical knowledge, financial acumen, and key skills.
6. Select all relevant management areas of experience.
7. Share your key achievements in previous roles.
8. Explain why you are interested in the Service Manager role.
9. Outline your career goals and objectives.
10. Add any professional certifications or education you have completed.
11. Indicate your availability to start work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Current Address**: Enter your current address, including city, state, and zip code.
* **Years of Management Experience**: Enter the number of years you have experience in management roles.
* **Years of Automotive Service Experience**: Enter the number of years you have experience in the automotive service industry.
* **Previous Management Positions**: List all previous management positions you have held, including job titles and dates of employment.
* **Size of Teams Managed**: Enter the approximate number of employees you have managed in previous roles.
* **Leadership Skills**: Assess your leadership skills as:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Needs Development
* **Customer Service Focus**: Assess your customer service focus as:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Needs Development
* **Automotive Technical Knowledge**: Assess your automotive technical knowledge as:
	+ Expert
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Basic
* **Financial and Budget Management**: Assess your financial and budget management skills as:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Limited
* **Management Areas of Experience**: Select all areas of management experience that apply to you:
	+ Staff Scheduling
	+ Budget Management
	+ Service Quality Control
	+ Customer Relations
	+ Inventory Management
	+ Equipment Maintenance
	+ Team Training
	+ Performance Management
	+ Facility Operations
* **Key Achievements**: Describe your notable accomplishments in previous roles.
* **Why Are You Interested in Service Manager Role**: Explain why you are interested in the Service Manager role.
* **Career Goals and Objectives**: Outline your career goals and objectives.
* **Professional Certifications**: List any professional certifications you have completed (e.g., ASE, etc.).
* **Highest Education Level**: Select your highest level of education completed.
* **Available Start Date**: Indicate your availability to start work.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to the best of your ability.
