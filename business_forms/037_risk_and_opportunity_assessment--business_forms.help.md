# Risk And Opportunity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk And Opportunity Assessment Form is used to evaluate potential threats and opportunities within the organization. This form helps identify and categorize risks and opportunities, determining their likelihood and impact on the business.

## How To Complete This Form
- Select one of the options for "Risk And Opportunity Assessment Form" from the provided choices.
- In the "Title" field, enter a brief description of the risk or opportunity.
- Choose one of the options for "Risk Category" that best describes the risk or opportunity.
- Select one of the options for "Risk Likelihood" that best describes the likelihood of occurrence.
- Select one of the options for "Risk Action" to determine the best course of action to take for the risk or opportunity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Risk And Opportunity Assessment Form**: This is the main question that asks whether the risk or opportunity is assessed as 'Yes' or 'No'. This helps to identify the type of risk or opportunity.
* **Title**: Enter a brief description of the risk or opportunity. This field is used for reference purposes only and does not affect the risk category or likelihood.
* **Risk Category**: This field categorizes the risk or opportunity as 'Yes' or 'No'. The options are used to identify the type of risk or opportunity.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the risk or opportunity.
* **Impact**: Select one of the options that best describes the impact of the risk or opportunity on the business.
* **Likelihood**: Select one of the options that best describes the likelihood of the risk or opportunity occurrence.
* **Action**: This field is used to determine the best course of action to take for the risk or opportunity.
* **Owner**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the risk or opportunity.
* **Owner**: The name of the person responsible for the risk or opportunity.
