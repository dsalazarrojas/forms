# Manufacturing Defect Root Cause Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Manufacturing Defect Root Cause Request Form is used to report a manufacturing defect that requires root cause analysis. This form helps identify and address the root cause of the defect by capturing relevant information about the defect.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. Make sure to include all necessary information, such as date and time of the defect, location where it occurred, and any attachments that were included.
2. Choose the correct root cause option from the drop-down menu.
3. Add any additional comments or details about the defect.
4. Attach any relevant files or images related to the defect.
5. Enter the reviewer's information, including their name, email, and phone number.
6. Enter the submission date and time.
7. Click the submit button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User** (<name>): Please enter your name, which will help us identify who is reporting this defect.
* **Root Cause** (`root_cause`): This is the main option for the cause of the defect. Please choose one of the available options.
* **Description** (`description_1`): Describe the defect in as much detail as possible.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date when the defect occurred.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time when the defect occurred.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the defect occurred.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Enter the name of the person or team to whom this defect has been assigned.
* **Telephone** (`telephone`): Enter the phone number of the person or team to whom this defect has been assigned.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or details about the defect.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`): Attach relevant files or images related to the defect.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Reviewer ID** (`reviewer_id`): Enter the ID of the reviewer who will review this defect.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`): Enter the email of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`reviewer_phone`): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`): Enter the date the form was reviewed.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`): Enter the time the form was reviewed.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`): Add any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
* **Attachment Name** (`attachment_name`): Enter the name of the attached file.
* **Attachment Type** (`attachment_type`): Enter the type of attachment (e.g. image, document, etc.).
* **Attachment URL** (`attachment_url`): Enter the URL of the attached file.
* **Attachment Size** (`attachment_size`): Enter the size of the attached file.
* **Attachment Status** (`attachment_status`): Choose the status of the attachment (e.g. pending, completed, error).
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click this button to complete and submit the form.
