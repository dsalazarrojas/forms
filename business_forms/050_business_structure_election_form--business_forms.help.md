# Business Structure Election Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Structure Election Form is a document used to elect a specific business structure for your company. This form is used to declare your business structure and provide supporting documentation for your election.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Provide detailed answers to the questions and follow the guidelines for each field.
3. Attach supporting documents as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entity Name**: Enter the legal name of your business.
* **Entity ID Number**: Enter the EIN or business registration number.
* **Current Entity Type**: Select the current structure of your business.
* **Entity Formation Date**: Enter the date when your business was formed.
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the principal or representative.
* **Title or Role**: Enter the position of the contact person.
* **Email Address**: Enter the business email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the contact number.
* **Elected Business Structure**: Select the structure you are electing for your business.
* **Election Effective Date**: Enter the date when the election should take effect.
* **Reason for Structure Election**: Explain why you are making this election.
* **Tax Year Effective**: Enter the tax year this election applies to.
* **Any Prior Tax Elections**: If applicable, mention any previous elections made.
* **Accountant or Tax Advisor Name**: Enter the name of the professional advising on the election.
* **Accountant Contact Information**: Enter the contact information of the accountant or tax advisor.
* **Owner or Member Names**: List all the owners or members of your business.
* **Ownership Percentages**: Enter how ownership is divided among the owners or members.
* **Documents Attached**: List any attached supporting documents.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of election submission.
* **Authorized Signature**: Enter the printed name of the authorized person.

## Tips

* Make sure to attach supporting documents as required.
* Double-check for accuracy and completeness in all fields.
* Consult a professional if you are unsure about any field.
