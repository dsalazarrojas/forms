# Factory Modernization Grant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Factory Modernization Grant Registration Form" is a tool used to gather information from applicants seeking funding for their factory modernization projects. This form is designed to help the application process by collecting essential details about the project and the applicant's background.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out each section according to the guidelines provided.
3. Be thorough and accurate when answering each question.
4. If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **form_page_1**
* **form-page-1**: This page is the first step in the form. It is an optional page that allows you to provide a brief introduction to your project.

### 2. **form_page_2**
* **form-page-2**: Select one of the provided options to indicate the type of project you are applying for. This will help the review process.

### 3. **form_page_3**
* **form-page-3**: Select multiple options from the list to indicate the specific areas of factory modernization you are looking to modernize. This will help the review process.

### 4. **form_page_5**
* **form-page-5**: Enter the total project cost in the provided field. This is a required field to ensure accurate budget calculation.

### 5. **form_page_8**
* **form-page-8**: Enter the number of employees that will benefit from this project. This is a required field for impact assessment.

### 6. **form_page_9**
* **form-page-9**: Provide any additional comments or details about your project.

### 7. **form_page_11**
* **form-page-11**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### 8. **form_page_24**
* **form-page-24**: Add any other comments or details that are not covered in the previous sections.

### 9. **form_page_23**
* **form-page-23**: This field is currently missing a label and type, please provide the correct label and type to complete the field-by-field explanation.

Note: Please review each field carefully and ensure you understand its purpose before filling out the form. If you are still unsure, refer to this guide for clarification.
