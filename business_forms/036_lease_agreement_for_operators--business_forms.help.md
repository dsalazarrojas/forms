# lease_agreement_for_operators - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about lease agreements for operators. It is essential to provide accurate and relevant details to ensure that the lease terms are clearly understood by all parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the lease period, including the start and end dates if applicable.
2. Enter the lease amount.
3. Indicate whether the lease frequency is 'Yes' or 'No'.
4. Specify the rent frequency by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' for the applicable fields.
5. Enter the lease start date.
6. Enter the lease end date.
7. Provide the property address.
8. Enter the operator's name.
9. Enter the lease terms and conditions.
10. Enter the payment terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lease Period** (`lease_period`, `number`, `required: false`): Enter the length of the lease agreement.
* **Lease Amount** (`lease_amount`, `number`, `required: false`): Enter the amount of the lease payment.
* **Lease Frequency** (`lease_frequency`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Indicate whether the lease is recurring ('Yes') or not ('No').
* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`, `date`, `required: false`): Enter the start date of the lease agreement.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, `date`, `required: false`): Enter the end date of the lease agreement.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter the address of the property being leased.
* **Operator Name** (`operator_name`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the operator.
* **Lease Terms** (`lease_terms`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter any additional lease terms and conditions.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter any payment terms and conditions.
