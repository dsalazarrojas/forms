# Strategic Portfolio Review Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Strategic Portfolio Review Program Registration form, which is designed for participants to provide information about their participation in the Strategic Portfolio Review Program. The form is intended to gather details about the participant's objectives, consulting firm information, strategy team details, and other relevant information to facilitate the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Participant Details** in the first page.
2.  Describe your **Objectives** for participating in the program on the second page.
3.  Provide a brief note on **Consent**, acknowledging your participation.
4.  Enter **Consulting Firm Information**, including details about your organization, on the third page.
5.  Enter **Strategy Team Information**, including details about your team, on the fourth page.
6.  Enter **Organizers Information**, including contact details about those leading the program, on the fifth page.
7.  Provide **Participant Objectives**, a clear explanation of what you hope to achieve from the program on the sixth page.
8.  Finally, enter **Program Details**, describing the specific program you're interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Details**: 
    *  **Name** (`participant_details`, `text`, required): Enter your name to identify yourself as a participant.
*   **Objectives**: 
    *  **Objective 1** (`objectives`, `text`, required): Describe your main objective for participating in the Strategic Portfolio Review Program.
*   **Consent**: 
    *  **Consent** (`consent`, `text`, required): Provide a brief note to acknowledge your understanding of the program's terms.
*   **Consulting Firm Information**: 
    *  **Firm Name** (`consulting_firm_info`, `text`, required): Enter your consulting firm's name to be included in the program records.
*   **Strategy Team Information**: 
    *  **Team Name** (`strategy_team_info`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your strategy team for better identification.
*   **Organizers Information**: 
    *  **Organizer's Contact** (`organizers_info`, `text`, required): Enter the contact information of the person leading the program.
*   **Participant Objectives**: 
    *  **Objective 1** (`participant_objectives`, `text`, required): Clearly state your goals for participating in the program.
*   **Program Details**: 
    *  **Program Title** (`program_details`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the program you are interested in, and the details that align with your objectives.

Note: The required fields are marked with `required` as `true`.
