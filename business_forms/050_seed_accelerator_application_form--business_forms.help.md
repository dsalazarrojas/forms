# Seed Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from businesses or entrepreneurs seeking to participate in a seed accelerator program. The program provides funding, mentorship, and resources to help early-stage startups grow and scale.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the text boxes with relevant information about your business.
2. Make sure to provide clear and concise answers to each field.
3. If you have any trouble with the form, contact [contact information].

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Leads 
* **Business Leads** (text box): Describe your business or startup. This field is meant to introduce your company and provide a brief overview.
* **Business Ideas** (text box): Share your business ideas, including any relevant concepts or strategies you'd like to implement.
* **Mentorship Guidance** (text box): If you're looking for specific guidance or mentorship, describe what kind of support you need.
* **Startup Foundation** (text box): Outline your startup's foundation, including your company history, mission, and values.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear in your answers. This will help the review process and increase your chances of getting accepted into the seed accelerator program.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or contact the seed accelerator team for guidance.
* This form is meant to be completed by business owners or representatives, so make sure to provide accurate and authorized information about your company.
