# Inventory Status Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to communicate the status of the company's inventory to employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the product that you are communicating about from the options provided on the "Product" field.
2. Enter the current quantity of the product in stock on the "Quantity" field.
3. (Optional) Enter the date and time of the update on the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
4. (Optional) Add any additional notes or comments about the product on the "Note" field.
5. (Optional) Enter the contact information of the person communicating this update on the "Email" and "Phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product**: Select one of the options provided. This field is required and indicates the product that you are communicating about.
* **Quantity**: Enter the current quantity of the selected product in stock. This field is required and must be a numeric value.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the update. If not provided, the current date will be used. This field is optional.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the update. If not provided, the current time will be used. This field is optional.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the product. This field is optional.
* **Email**: Enter the contact information of the person communicating this update. This field is optional.
* **Phone**: Enter the contact number of the person communicating this update. This field is optional.
* **First Page**: This page is not applicable as it is a blank page and does not require any input.
