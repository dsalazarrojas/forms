# Process Optimization Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Optimization Research Application Form is designed to collect project-related information to help identify areas for process improvement. Please fill out this form to provide your project team with valuable data for process optimization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting "Submit" to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter your project details, including project title, project leads, contact person, and email.
3. Provide your contact information, project manager's name, and team members' names.
4. Select the project status, business process, and phase description.
5. Enter project goals, outcomes, and phase outcomes.
6. Specify the expected start and end dates, actual start and end dates, expected and actual durations, and project budget.
7. Fill out the project phases and phase outcomes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required: no): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Project Leads** (`project_leads`, select_multiple, required: no): Select all team members involved in the project.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: no): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for this project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: no): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: no): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required: no): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: no): Enter any additional comments or notes about your project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required: no): Select the current status of your project (Active, Pending, Completed).
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required: no): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Project Goal** (`project_goal`, text, required: no): Enter your project's main objective.
* **Project Outcomes** (`project_outcomes`, text, required: no): Enter the expected outcomes of your project.
* **Business Process** (`business_processes`, select_one, required: no): Select the business process related to your project (Process 1, Process 2, Process 3).
* **Expected Start Date** (`expected_start_date`, date, required: no): Enter the date you expect your project to start.
* **Expected End Date** (`expected_end_date`, date, required: no): Enter the date you expect your project to end.
* **Actual Start Date** (`actual_start_date`, date, required: no): Enter the actual start date of your project.
* **Actual End Date** (`actual_end_date`, date, required: no): Enter the actual end date of your project.
* **Expected Duration** (`expected_duration`, number, required: no): Enter the expected duration of your project.
* **Actual Duration** (`actual_duration`, number, required: no): Enter the actual duration of your project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, required: no): Enter your project budget.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: no): Enter your contact information.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, text, required: no): Enter the name of your project manager.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, text, required: no): Enter the names of team members involved in your project.
* **Project Phases** (`project_phases`, text, required: no): Enter the phases of your project.
* **Phase Description** (`phase_description`, text, required: no): Enter a brief description of each project phase.
* **Phase Outcomes** (`phase_outcomes`, text, required: no): Enter the expected outcomes of each project phase.
