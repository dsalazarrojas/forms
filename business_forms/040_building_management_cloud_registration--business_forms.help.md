# Building Management Cloud Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your property management business and its needs for cloud-based management. The data you provide will help us understand your business requirements and provide you with a tailored solution to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form and ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Enter your property manager's or owner's name and job title in the relevant fields.
3. Provide the property's name, address, and city, state, and ZIP code.
4. Choose the type of property and its size in square footage.
5. List the current management systems in use and any pain points or challenges you are experiencing.
6. Select your primary goals for cloud management, such as centralizing property data or improving operational efficiency.
7. Choose the services and features of interest to you, such as maintenance management or financial tracking.
8. Indicate whether you need to integrate with existing systems and which systems are involved.
9. Specify the types of user roles and the number of users you expect to have access to the system.
10. Select the preferred implementation timeline and any additional training or support needs.
11. If necessary, indicate a need for help with data migration or a budget range for cloud management.
12. Finally, provide any additional information or comments about your business or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property manager or owner name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for managing the property.
* **Job title**: Enter the job title of the property manager or owner.
* **Company or organization name**: Enter the name of the company or organization that owns the property.
* **Email address**: Enter the email address of the property manager or owner.
* **Phone number**: Enter the contact phone number of the property manager or owner.
* **Property name**: Enter the name of the property being managed.
* **Property address**: Enter the address of the property being managed.
* **City, state, and ZIP code**: Enter the city, state, and ZIP code of the property being managed.
* **Property type**: Select the type of property (e.g., single-family home, office building, commercial retail).
* **Total square footage**: Enter the total square footage of the property.
* **Number of units or spaces**: Enter the number of units or spaces in the property (if applicable).
* **Year built**: Enter the year the property was built.
* **Current management systems in use**: List the current management systems being used for the property.
* **Pain points or challenges**: List any pain points or challenges you are experiencing with the current management systems.
* **Primary goals for cloud management**: Select the primary goals for cloud management (e.g., centralize property data, improve operational efficiency).
* **Services and features of interest**: Select the services and features of interest to you (e.g., maintenance management, financial tracking).
* **Need to integrate with existing systems**: Indicate whether you need to integrate with existing systems and which systems are involved.
* **Systems needing integration**: If necessary, list the systems that need integration.
* **Management staff count**: Enter the number of management staff members.
* **Current management systems and pain points**: Repeat the information from the "Current management systems in use" and "Pain points or challenges" fields.
