# Invention Disclosure Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invention Disclosure Declaration Form is a standardized document used to collect detailed information about an invention. This form is essential for accurately capturing and documenting inventions, ensuring that intellectual property rights are protected, and facilitating collaboration among researchers and developers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the inventor's details, including their name, contact information, and other relevant personal details.
2.  Provide the invention's name and description, including any figures, tables, or equations that are relevant to the invention.
3.  Specify the date the invention was disclosed and the date it was discovered or developed.
4.  Indicate the invention's disclosure status and priority filing status.
5.  Enter any patent application numbers or related art information.
6.  Describe the invention in detail, including figures, tables, and equations (if applicable).
7.  Provide any other relevant information related to the invention.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Inventor Details**: This field captures the inventor's name and contact information, which is essential for communication and follow-up actions.
    *   **Invention Name**: Enter a descriptive name for the invention that identifies it uniquely.
    *   **Invention Description**: Describe the invention in detail, including its functionality, features, and any relevant context.
    *   **Date Disclosed**: Record the date the invention was disclosed or shared with others.
    *   **Date Discovered/Developed**: Enter the date the invention was discovered or developed.
*   **Date Disclosed**: This field is related to when the invention was officially disclosed or shared with others.
*   **Disclosed To**: Specify who or which entity the invention was disclosed to.
*   **Inventors Names**: Provide a list or name of inventors involved in the development of the invention.
*   **Patent Applications**: If applicable, enter any patent application numbers or related art information.
*   **Related Art**: Provide a description of similar or related inventions or technologies that have influenced or inspired this invention.
*   **Invention Description Including Figures**: Enter a detailed description of the invention, including any figures, tables, or illustrations that are relevant.
*   **Invention Description Excluding Figures**: Describe the invention in detail, excluding any figures, tables, or illustrations.
*   **Invention Description Including Equations**: Provide a detailed description of the invention, including any equations or mathematical formulas that are relevant.
*   **Priority Date**: Record the date the invention was given priority status.
*   **Inventor Contact Info**: Enter the inventor's contact information for follow-up actions.
*   **Invention Disclosure Status**: Select the current status of the invention's disclosure (Pending, Published, Confirmed, Cancelled, Withdrawn).
*   **Priority Filing**: Check if the invention has a priority filing status (True, False, Not Applicable).
*   **Priority Filing Date**: Record the date the invention's priority filing status was applied.
*   **Patent Number**: Enter any patent application numbers or related art information.
*   **Invention Status**: Provide a general status description of the invention.
*   **Invention Priority**: Enter a priority number or status for the invention.
*   **Invention Description Including Tables**: Describe the invention in detail, including any tables or illustrations.
*   **Invention Description Excluding Tables**: Describe the invention in detail, excluding any tables or illustrations.
*   **Invention Description Including Equations**: Describe the invention in detail, including any equations or mathematical formulas.
