# StylistPartnershipAgreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The StylistPartnershipAgreement form is designed to document and formalize a partnership between a salon and a stylist. This form captures the necessary details to establish a comprehensive partnership agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and review the fields below
- Fill in the details for the salon and stylist as required
- Review and agree to the Terms and Conditions
- Confirm your consent to the terms
- Select the contact details you wish to provide
- Sign and date the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Salon Details** (1, `salon_details`, not required): Enter the details of the salon, such as its name, address, and any other relevant information.
- **Stylist Details** (2, `stylist_details`, not required): Enter the details of the stylist, such as their name, contact information, and any other relevant information.
- **Terms and Conditions** (3, `terms_conditions`, not required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the partnership. This is a critical field that outlines the expectations and responsibilities of both parties.
- **Consent** (4, `consent`, not required): Confirm your consent to the terms and conditions by selecting 'yes'.
- **Contact Details** (5, `contact_details`, not required): Select the contact details you wish to provide, such as name, phone number, email, or address.
- **Signature** (6, `signature`, not required): Sign and date the form to complete it.
- **Salon Signature** (7, `salon_signature`, not required): The salon owner or representative should sign and date the form to validate the partnership.

## Tips
- Be sure to review and understand the terms and conditions carefully before agreeing to them.
- Make sure to provide accurate contact information for your salon.
- Sign and date the form to confirm your commitment to the partnership.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
