# Commercial Leasing Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commercial Leasing Certification Registration Form is designed to collect information about the individual seeking commercial leasing certification. This form helps us assess your qualifications and background to ensure you are prepared for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field explanation carefully before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name (1)** (`text`, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address (2)** (`email`, required): Enter your primary email address for certification updates.
* **Phone Number (3)** (`text`, required): Enter your contact phone number for communication purposes.
* **Professional Information (4)** (`note`, not required): This is an optional section for any additional professional information you'd like to provide.
* **Company Name (5)** (`text`, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Job Title (6)** (`text`, required): Enter your job title within your organization.
* **Profession (7)** (`select_one`, required): Select your profession from the provided options (Real Estate Agent, Broker, Property Manager, Attorney, Leasing Consultant, Asset Manager, Business Owner, or Other).
* **Years of Experience (8)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the number of years of experience you have in commercial real estate from the provided options (Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, 10+ years).
* **Professional License Number (9)** (`text`, not required): If you hold a professional license, enter the number here.
* **License State (10)** (`text`, not required): Enter the state in which you hold a professional license, if applicable.
* **Certification Program (11)** (`note`, not required): This is an optional section for additional information about the certification program.
* **Certification Level (12)** (`select_one`, required): Select your level of certification from the provided options (Basic, Advanced, Professional, Master).
* **Area of Specialization (13)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the area of specialization relevant to your certification from the provided options (Office Leasing, Retail Leasing, Industrial Leasing, Medical Leasing, Mixed Use, General Commercial).
* **Education (14)** (`note`, not required): This is an optional section for additional education information.
* **Highest Education Level (15)** (`select_one`, required): Select your highest level of education from the provided options (High School, Associate Degree, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, Doctorate, Professional Degree).
* **Relevant Certifications (16)** (`text`, not required): List any relevant certifications you have earned.
* **Coursework (17)** (`note`, not required): This is an optional section for additional coursework information.
* **Preferred Start Date (18)** (`date`, required): Choose your preferred start date for the program.
* **Study Format (19)** (`select_one`, required): Select your preferred study format from the provided options (Online Self-Paced, Online Live Classes, In-Person Classes, Hybrid).
* **Study Schedule (20)** (`select_one`, required): Choose your preferred study schedule from the provided options (Weekday Mornings, Weekday Afternoons, Weekday Evenings, Weekends, Flexible).
* **Payment (21)** (`note`, not required): This is an optional section for additional payment information.
* **Tuition Payment (22)** (`number`, required): Enter your payment amount for the program tuition.
* **Payment Method (23)** (`select_one`, required): Select your payment method from the provided options (Credit Card, Debit Card, Wire Transfer, Check, Company Sponsorship, Financing).
* **Payment Plan (24)** (`select_one`, required): Choose your payment plan from the provided options (Full Payment, 2 Installments, 3 Installments, 6 Installments).
* **Employer Sponsorship (25)** (`select_one`, required): Select whether your employer is paying for your education from the provided options (Yes Full, Yes Partial, No Self-Pay).
* **Agreements (26)** (`note`, not required): This is an optional section for additional agreements information.
* **Code of Ethics Agreement (27)** (`select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to abide by the code of ethics.
* **Exam Requirements (28)** (`select_one`, required): Select whether you understand the exam requirements.
* **Continuing Education (29)** (`select_one`, required): Select whether you understand the continuing education requirements.
* **Additional Information (30)** (`note`, not required): This is an optional section for additional comments.
* **Career Goals (31)** (`text`, not required): Enter your career goals and aspirations.
* **How Did You Hear About Us (32)** (`select_one`, required): Choose how you learned about the program from the provided options (Website, Social Media, Colleague Referral, Professional Association, Advertisement, Other).
* **Additional Comments (33)** (`text`, not required): Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.
