# Work From Phone Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work From Phone Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for work-from-home arrangements. This form gathers necessary details to evaluate and process these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your application form with the required information.
2. Select your job preferences and job role from the provided options.
3. Choose your work location.
4. Enter your contact details and email address.
5. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, optional): Fill in your application form with a brief description of your interest in working from home.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Job Preferences** (`job_preferences`, select_one, optional): Select one option that best describes your work preferences:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Working Hours** (`working_hours`, number, optional): Enter your available work hours, if applicable.
* **Work From Home** (`work_from_home`, select_one, optional): Select one option that best describes your work-from-home preferences:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Job Role** (`job_role`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options that best describe your job role(s):
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select one option that best describes your job type:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): Describe the location where you plan to work from.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have reviewed and understand the form's purpose and scope before filling it out.
* Double-check your application form and contact details for accuracy before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact our support team.
