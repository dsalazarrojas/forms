# Investment Readiness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Investment Readiness Assessment Form is a tool designed to help investors assess a company's readiness for investment. It is a comprehensive checklist that includes various fields to gather essential information about the business. This form ensures that both investors and entrepreneurs have a clear understanding of a company's potential and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the form's title and category fields. 
2. Provide a brief description of your business, including its short and long descriptions.
3. Determine the type of investment and investor type that best suits your business needs.
4. Select the correct number of employees, financials, and equity ownership.
5. Specify your business's legal entity and industry.
6. Choose your location and select the relevant social media platforms.
7. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
8. Add any other relevant information about your business.
9. Provide a score for the investor's score.
10. Confirm your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investment Readiness Assessment Form** (`form_title`, text, **not required**): Enter the title of the business, such as a company name or a project name.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose one or multiple categories that best describe your business, such as "Business Forms" or "Other".
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, **not required**): Enter a brief description of your business.
* **Short Description** (`form_description_short`, text, **not required**): Enter a shorter version of your business description.
* **Long Description** (`form_description_long`, text, **not required**): Enter a detailed description of your business.
* **Type of Investment** (`form_investment_type`, select_multiple, **required**): Determine the type of investment that best suits your business needs, such as "Seed Funding" or "Series A".
* **Investor Type** (`form_investor_type`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose the type of investor that best fits your business needs, such as "Venture Capitalist" or "Angel Investor".
* **Employee Count** (`form_employees_count`, select_one, **required**): Select the number of employees in your business.
* **Financials** (`form_financials`, select_one, **required**): Determine your business's financials, such as "Less than $500,000" or "More than $10M".
* **Equity Ownership** (`form_equity`, select_multiple, **required**): Specify your business's equity ownership, such as "Private Corporation" or "Partnership".
* **Legal Entity** (`form_legal_entity`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose your business's legal entity, such as "S-Corp" or "Sole Proprietorship".
* **Industry** (`form_industry`, select_one, **required**): Select the industry your business operates in.
* **Location** (`form_location`, select_one, **required**): Choose your business location.
* **Website** (`form_website`, text, **not required**): Enter your business's website URL.
* **Facebook** (`form_facebook`, text, **not required**): Enter your Facebook URL.
* **LinkedIn** (`form_linkedin`, text, **not required**): Enter your LinkedIn URL.
* **Twitter** (`form_twitter`, text, **not required**): Enter your Twitter URL.
* **Instagram** (`form_instagram`, text, **not required**): Enter your Instagram URL.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, **required**): Enter your business contact email.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, **not required**): Enter your business contact phone number.
* **Other Info** (`form_other_info`, text, **not required**): Enter any other relevant information about your business.
* **Investor Comments** (`form_investor_comments`, text, **not required**): Enter any comments from the investor.
* **Investor Recommendation** (`form_investor_recommendation`, text, **not required**): Enter any recommendations from the investor.
* **Investor Score** (`form_investor_score`, number, **required**): Enter a score for the investor's assessment.
* **Signature** (`form_signature`, select_one, **required**): Confirm your signature, indicating that you have reviewed and completed the form accurately.
