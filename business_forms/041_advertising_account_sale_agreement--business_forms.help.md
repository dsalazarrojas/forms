# Advertising Account Sale Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the sale of an advertising account, including the seller's and buyer's information, account details, and transaction terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the seller's information, including their company name, contact person's name, address, and email.
2. Enter the buyer's information, including their company name, contact person's name, address, and email.
3. Enter the account details, including the account name, platform, creation date, and performance metrics.
4. Select the payment terms for the sale (Full payment upfront, Installment plan, Performance-based, or Other).
5. Enter the sale price and the transfer date for the account.
6. Confirm that both parties accept the terms of the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seller Information** (`seller_company_name`): The legal name of the selling company.
* **Seller Contact Person** (`seller_contact_person`): The name of the primary contact person for the seller.
* **Seller Address** (`seller_address`): The complete address of the selling company.
* **Seller Email** (`seller_email`): The business email for communication with the seller.
* **Buyer Information** (`buyer_company_name`): The legal name of the buying company.
* **Buyer Contact Person** (`buyer_contact_person`): The name of the primary contact person for the buyer.
* **Buyer Address** (`buyer_address`): The complete address of the buying company.
* **Buyer Email** (`buyer_email`): The business email for communication with the buyer.
* **Account Name** (`account_name`): The name of the advertising account being sold.
* **Account Platform** (`account_platform`): The platform on which the account is located (e.g., Google Ads, Facebook Ads, etc.).
* **Account Creation Date** (`account_creation_date`): The date the advertising account was created.
* **Account Performance Metrics** (`account_performance_metrics`): Key performance metrics of the account (e.g., impressions, clicks, etc.).
* **Sale Price** (`sale_price`): The total price for the advertising account sale.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): The agreed-upon payment terms for the sale (e.g., full payment upfront, installments, etc.).
* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`): The date on which the account will be transferred.
* **Agreement Acceptance** (`agreement_acceptance`): Both parties must accept the terms of the agreement.
