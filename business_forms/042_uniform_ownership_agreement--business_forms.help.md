**Uniform Ownership Agreement - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The Uniform Ownership Agreement form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a partnership or joint venture between two parties. It ensures both parties have a clear understanding of their ownership percentage, agreement type, and terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Company Name field with the name of the company or entity that is entering into the partnership.
2. Enter the Partner Name in the field provided.
3. Specify the Percentage of Ownership as a numerical value (e.g., 50% for a 50-50 partnership).
4. Choose the Ownership Date on which the ownership agreement was executed.
5. Select the Type of Agreement (e.g., "Yes" for a binding agreement or "No" for an informal agreement).
6. Enter the Agreement Term in years.
7. Optionally, add any additional Notes about the agreement.
8. Provide the Contact Email and Contact Phone number of the partner or contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the company or entity that is entering into the partnership.
* **Partner Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the partner or entity that is entering into the partnership.
* **Percentage of Ownership** (Number, Optional): Specify the percentage of ownership that the partner will hold (e.g., 50%).
* **Ownership Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date on which the ownership agreement was executed.
* **Type of Agreement** (Select One, Optional): Select "Yes" for a binding agreement or "No" for an informal agreement.
* **Agreement Term** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of years the partnership agreement is valid for.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the agreement.
* **Contact Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the contact person for the partner.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the partner.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully review and agree upon all terms and conditions before completing this form.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records and share it with all parties involved in the partnership.
* Update this form as needed to reflect changes in the partnership agreement.
* Use this form as a starting point for further negotiation and discussion with your partner.
