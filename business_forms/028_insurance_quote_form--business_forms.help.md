# Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an insurance quote. It is divided into sections that correspond to different aspects of an insurance policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the client's information in the 'Client Info' section. This includes their name and address.
2. Select the coverage type in the 'Coverage Type' section. This will determine what type of policy you are interested in.
3. Enter the policy start date, end date, term years, and any other coverage details in the 'Coverage Details' section.
4. Choose your payment method and amount in the 'Payment Method' section.
5. Enter any necessary notes and comments in the 'Notes' section.
6. Review the form to ensure all required fields are filled and that all information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (`client_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Policy Holder** (`policy_holder`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's or policyholder's name.
* **Coverage Type** (`coverage_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of coverage you are interested in. For example, health, life, or property.
* **Coverage Area** (`coverage_area`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the area of coverage, such as national, state, or international.
* **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the policy.
* **Policy End Date** (`policy_end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the policy.
* **Policy Term Years** (`policy_term_years`, `number`, required: false): Enter the term length of the policy.
* **Policy Limit** (`policy_limit`, `number`, required: false): Enter the policy limit or cap on coverage.
* **Policy Deductible** (`policy_deductible`, `number`, required: false): Enter the deductible amount.
* **Policy Deductible Notes** (`policy_deductible_notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any notes or comments about the deductible.
* **Policy Address** (`policy_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the policy address.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you will pay for the policy. For example, monthly, quarterly, or annually.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how often you will pay for the policy. For example, monthly, quarterly, or annually.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the payment status. For example, paid, pending, or overdue.
* **Payment Term** (`payment_term`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the payment term. For example, short term, long term, or flexible term.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required: false): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Notes** (`payment_notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any payment notes or comments.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the policy.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any contact information.
* **Payment Address** (`payment_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address for payment.
* **Payment Method Notes** (`payment_method_notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any payment method notes or comments.
* **Policy Deductible Address** (`policy_deductible_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address for policy deductible.
* **Policy Deductible Notes** (`policy_deductible_notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any policy deductible notes or comments.
