# Customer Loss Survey Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Loss Survey Evaluation Form is designed to help us understand the reasons behind customer loss. This form provides valuable information about our customers' experiences, allowing us to identify areas for improvement and increase customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the customer's file and recall your interaction with them.
2. Select the most relevant reason for the customer's loss from the drop-down menus.
3. Provide a detailed description of the customer's experience, including any notable interactions or issues.
4. Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was the reason for the customer canceling?** (1)
	+ This field helps us understand the primary reason for the customer's loss.
* **What was the status of your customer service?**
	+ Select the most relevant reason for the customer's poor experience (e.g., Poor quality of products/services, Long wait time, Difficulty communicating with customer service).
* **Was this a single or repeat customer?** (3)
	+ Select whether the customer was a one-time or repeat customer.
* **Which of the following was the primary reason?** (4)
	+ Select the primary reason for the customer's loss (e.g., Price, Quality, Service).
* **Was this customer a new or repeat customer?** (5)
	+ Select whether the customer was new or had previously done business with us.
* **How would you rate the quality of our service?** (6)
	+ Rate the quality of service on a scale of 1-5 (very poor to excellent).
* **What was the average order value?** (7)
	+ Enter the average order value.
* **What was the average order frequency?** (8)
	+ Select how often the customer ordered from us (e.g., Less than once a month, Once a month, Every two weeks, Once a week, More than once a week).
* **What was the average order total?** (9)
	+ Enter the average order total.
* **Was the customer satisfied with their order?** (10)
	+ Select whether the customer was satisfied or not satisfied with their order.
* **Was this a sale or return?** (11)
	+ Select whether the customer made a sale or return.
* **What was the reason for this sale or return?** (12)
	+ Provide a detailed description of the reason for the sale or return.
* **Was the customer looking for a discount?** (13)
	+ Select whether the customer was looking for a discount.
* **What was the average order total with discount applied?** (14)
	+ Enter the average order total with discount applied.
* **Was this a gift order?** (15)
	+ Select whether the customer ordered as a gift.
* **Was the customer satisfied with their experience?** (16)
	+ Select whether the customer was satisfied or not satisfied with their experience.
* **What was the customer service rep attitude/behavior?** (17)
	+ Select whether the customer service rep's attitude/behavior was positive or negative.
* **Was the customer knowledgeable about the product/service?** (18)
	+ Select whether the customer had knowledge about the product or service.
* **How did the customer contact our business?** (19)
	+ Enter a detailed description of how the customer contacted our business.
* **Was the customer contacted via phone or email?** (20)
	+ Select how the customer was contacted (e.g., Phone, Email).
* **Was the customer contacted via chat or online form?** (21)
	+ Select how the customer was contacted (e.g., Chat, Online form).
* **Was the customer contacted via social media or text?** (22)
	+ Select how the customer was contacted (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, Text message).
* **What was the reason for the contact?** (23)
	+ Provide a detailed description of the reason for the contact.
* **Was this a follow-up contact?** (24)
	+ Select whether this was a follow-up contact.
* **Was the customer contacted by a supervisor?** (25)
	+ Select whether the customer was contacted by a supervisor.
