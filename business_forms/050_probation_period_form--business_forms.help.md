# Probation Period Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Probation Period Form is designed to collect information about an employee's probation period, a temporary period of observation or trial period given to new employees to assess their performance and potential fit within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please select the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields to specify the duration of the probation period.
2. In the "Remarks" field, provide any comments or observations about the employee's performance during the probation period.
3. If required, fill out the "Reason" field to explain the reason for the probation period.
4. Optionally, you can add any comments from the employer and/or employee in the "Employer Comments" and "Employee Comments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Start Date (Required: False)
• **Start Date** (`start_date`, date): The date the employee's probation period started. This is an optional field, but it helps track the start date of the employee's probation period.

### End Date (Required: False)
• **End Date** (`end_date`, date): The date the employee's probation period ended. Like the "Start Date" field, this is an optional field that helps track the end date of the employee's probation period.

### Remarks (Required: False)
• **Remarks** (`remarks`, text): Add any comments or observations about the employee's performance during the probation period. This can include positive or negative feedback, areas for improvement, and other relevant notes.

### Reason (Required: False)
• **Reason** (`reason`, text): If the employee is on a probation period, you may need to specify the reason for it. This can be due to performance issues, training, or other company-specific reasons. This field is optional, but it helps provide context for the probation period.

### Employer Comments (Required: False)
• **Employer Comments** (`employer_comments`, text): Add any comments or feedback from the employer about the employee's performance during the probation period. This can be a good opportunity to provide constructive criticism or positive feedback.

### Employee Comments (Required: False)
• **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, text): Add any comments or feedback from the employee about their own performance during the probation period. This can be a good way to encourage self-reflection and improvement.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure accurate and comprehensive records.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form to avoid confusion.
- Review and revise the form as needed to ensure it meets your specific organizational needs.
