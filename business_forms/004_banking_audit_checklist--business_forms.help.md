# Banking Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Banking Audit Checklist is a form designed to guide auditors through the process of evaluating the compliance of a bank or branch. This document helps auditors ensure that all relevant checks are performed and documented to identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Auditor Name: This is the name of the person conducting the audit.
2. Enter the Audit Date: This is the date when the audit was performed.
3. Select the Branch or Unit Audited: Enter the location or department being audited.
4. Select the Compliance Checks Performed: Choose all applicable checks completed during the audit (e.g., Cash handling procedures, Loan file documentation, etc.).
5. Enter a Summary of Findings: Provide a concise summary of key observations made during the audit.
6. Determine Overall Compliance: Select the overall compliance rating (Compliant, Partial compliance, or Non-compliant).
7. (Optional) Enter Recommended Actions: Suggest corrective measures and owners for further action.
8. Enter the Follow-up Date: (Optional) Set a date for reassessment or remediation review.
9. Sign with your Name: Enter your typed name as the auditor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Auditor Name** (`auditor_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the lead auditor conducting the review.
* **Audit Date** (`audit_date`, date, required): Enter the date the audit was performed.
* **Branch or Unit Audited** (`branch_or_unit`, text, required): Specify the location or department being audited.
* **Compliance Checks Performed** (`compliance_checks`, select_multiple, required): Choose all applicable checks completed during the audit, such as Cash handling procedures, Loan file documentation, etc.
* **Summary of Findings** (`findings_summary`, text, required): Provide a concise summary of key observations made during the audit.
* **Overall Compliance Determination** (`overall_compliance`, select_one, required): Select the overall compliance rating (Compliant, Partial compliance, or Non-compliant).
* **Recommended Actions** (`recommended_actions`, text, optional): Suggest corrective measures and owners for further action. (Optional)
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Set a date for reassessment or remediation review. (Optional)
* **Auditor Signature Name** (`auditor_signature`, text, required): Enter your typed name as the auditor.
