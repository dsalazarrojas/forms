# Safety Stock Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information for a safety stock request. A safety stock is a strategic inventory level that ensures that items are available to meet customer demand. This form is designed to gather details about the request, item, and requester's information to facilitate the processing and fulfillment of the safety stock request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. On the first page, provide a brief description of the safety stock request.
2. On the second page, specify the item details, such as item code, quantity, and unit price.
3. On the third page, enter the requester's details, including name, email, and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **request_details** (`request_details`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the safety stock request, explaining why the request is being made and what it's intended for.
* **item_details** (`item_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the item code, quantity, and unit price of the item being requested.
* **requestor_details** (`requestor_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the name, email, and contact information of the person making the request.

## Tips
* Ensure that the description provided in the request_details field is clear and concise to avoid any confusion in processing the request.
* Double-check the item code, quantity, and unit price entered in the item_details field to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to enter the correct contact information in the requestor_details field to ensure that the requester can be reached for further clarification or follow-up questions.
