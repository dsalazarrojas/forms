# Equipment Lifecycle Optimization Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Equipment Lifecycle Optimization Application Form is used to gather data about equipment being used in a business, ensuring accurate and up-to-date information for informed decision-making and strategic planning.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information about the equipment.
2.  Ensure that you have all the necessary data required for the form.
3.  Review and validate your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Name** (`user_name`): Enter your name to identify you as the person filling out this form.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **User Email** (`user_email`): Enter a valid email address to contact you about any updates or queries about this form.
    *   This field is optional, but it's recommended for easier communication.
*   **Business Name** (`business_name`): Enter the name of the business using the equipment.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter a contact number for the business or person responsible for the equipment.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Select the type of equipment being used (Type A, Type B, Type C).
    *   This field is optional, but it helps in categorizing and tracking equipment.
*   **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`): Select the current status of the equipment (Active, Inactive, Decommissioned).
    *   This field is optional, but it helps in tracking equipment usage and maintenance.
*   **Acquisition Date** (`acquisition_date`): Enter the date when the equipment was acquired.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Maintenance Start Date** (`maintenance_start`): Enter the date when equipment maintenance started.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Maintenance End Date** (`maintenance_end`): Enter the date when equipment maintenance ended.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Decommissioning Date** (`decommissioning_date`): Enter the date when the equipment was decommissioned.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`): Enter a brief description of the equipment.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`): Select the current condition of the equipment (Good, Fair, Poor).
    *   This field is optional, but it helps in tracking equipment condition.
*   **Business Description** (`business_description`): Enter a brief description of the business using the equipment.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the person responsible for the equipment.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Business Phone** (`business_phone`): Enter a contact phone number for the business.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Business Email** (`business_email`): Enter a valid email address for the business.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the equipment.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments about the equipment.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Equipment Location** (`equipment_location`): Select the location where the equipment is being used (Location A, Location B, Location C).
    *   This field is optional, but it helps in tracking equipment location.
*   **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`): Select the user assigned to the equipment (User 1, User 2, User 3).
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Review Status** (`review_status`): Select the current review status of the equipment (Approved, In Review, Pending Review).
    *   This field is optional, but it helps in tracking review status.
*   **Approval Date** (`approval_date`): Enter the date when the equipment was approved.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Rejection Date** (`rejection_date`): Enter the date when the equipment was rejected.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Reason for Rejection** (`reason_for_rejection`): Enter the reason why the equipment was rejected.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Business Category** (`business_category`): Select the category of the business (Category A, Category B, Category C).
    *   This field is optional, but it helps in categorizing business operations.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you have all the necessary data before filling out the form.
*   Review and validate your input before submitting the form.
*   If you have any doubts or questions, please consult with the business operations team or the equipment owner before submitting the form.
