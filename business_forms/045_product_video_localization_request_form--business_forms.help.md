# Product Video Localization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit requests for video localization services. Please fill out the form with accurate and relevant information to ensure a smooth localization process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant team members.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name**: <product_name> (`Product Name`, text, required/optional) 
   Please enter the name of the product for which you're requesting localization.
* **Product URL** (`product_url`, text, required/optional): 
   Enter the URL of the product website where the video can be found.
* **Target Language** (`target_language`, select_one, required/optional) 
   Choose the target language for the localization.
* **Localization Requirement** (`localization_requirement`, select_one, required/optional) 
   Select whether the localization is a 'Yes' or 'No' requirement.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, date, required/optional) 
   Enter the deadline for the localization completion.
* **File** (`file`, text, required/optional) 
   Please provide a file name for the localization job.
* **Target Language Options** (`target_language_options`, select_multiple, required/optional) 
   Select one or more target languages for the localization.
* **Submission File** (`submission_file`, text, required/optional) 
   Enter the file name for submission.
* **Submission Deadline** (`submission_deadline`, time, required/optional) 
   Enter the time for submission deadline.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, note, required/optional) 
   Enter any additional submission notes.
* **Submission Email** (`submission_email`, email, required/optional) 
   Enter the email address for submission.

## Tips
- Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that the form is filled out completely and accurately to avoid any issues during the localization process.
