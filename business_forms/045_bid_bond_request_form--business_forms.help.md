# Bid Bond Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from companies that are requesting a bid bond. It covers various aspects of a company's business, contact information, project details, and bond requirements. This form will help ensure that all necessary information is provided to facilitate a smooth bid bond process.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Company Information
* **Company Legal Name (Company Name)** (`required`): Enter your company's official registered name.
* **Company Type** (`required`): Select the type of business entity (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Limited Liability Company, Corporation, or Other).
* **Company Registration or License Number** (`required`): Enter your business license ID.
* **Tax ID or EIN** (`required`): Enter your Federal Tax Identification Number.
* **Years in Business** (`required`): Indicate how long your company has been operating.
* **Bank Reference Name and Contact** (`required`): Enter the name of your primary bank and their contact information.

### Step 2: Contact Information
* **Contact Person Name** (`required`): Enter the name of the primary contact person for this request.
* **Contact Person Title** (`required`): Enter the title or position of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (`required`): Enter your company's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`required`): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Office Address** (`required`): Enter your company's full business address.

### Step 3: Project Information
* **Project or Contract Name** (`required`): Enter the name of the project or contract.
* **Project Owner or Agency** (`required`): Enter the name of the party awarding the contract.
* **Estimated Bid Amount** (`required`): Enter the amount of your bid.
* **Currency** (`required`): Select the currency of your bid (e.g., USD, CAD, EUR, or Other).
* **Bid Submission Deadline** (`required`): Enter the deadline for bid submission.
* **Project Start Date** (`not required`): If applicable, enter the date when work is expected to begin.
* **Project Duration** (`not required`): If applicable, enter the expected length of the project.

### Step 4: Bond Information
* **Type of Bond Requested** (`required`): Select the type of bond you are requesting (e.g., Bid Bond, Performance Bond, Payment Bond, Combined Bid and Performance, or Other).
* **Bond Amount as Percentage of Bid** (`required`): Enter the bond amount as a percentage of your bid (typically 5-10%).
* **Total Bond Amount Required** (`required`): Enter the dollar amount of the bond.
* **Type of Bond Requested** (`required`): Select the type of bond you are requesting (e.g., Bid Bond, Performance Bond, Payment Bond, Combined Bid and Performance, or Other).

### Step 5: Financial Information
* **Annual Company Revenue** (`required`): Enter your company's revenue for the last fiscal year.
* **Current Bonding Capacity** (`not required`): If applicable, enter your existing bonding limit.
* **Company Credit Rating** (`not required`): If applicable, select your company's credit rating (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Unknown).
* **Additional Information or Context** (`not required`): If necessary, provide any other relevant information or context.

### Step 6: Claims and Compliance
* **Any Prior Bond Claims Against Your Company** (`required`): Select whether you have any prior bond claims against your company.
* **Describe Prior Claims (if Applicable)** (`not required`): If applicable, provide details about any previous claims.
* **Compliance with Contract Terms** (`required`): Select whether you commit to comply with all contract terms.

Note: This help guide is intended to cover the essential fields to fill out the form correctly. It's recommended that the user reviews the form's fields carefully and ensures they have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
