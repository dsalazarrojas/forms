# Policy Modernization Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request a review of an existing policy, which may involve updating or refining its content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name and last name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number (optional).
4. Input the policy number of the policy you'd like to review.
5. Select the type of review you require (Policy Review or Policy Update).
6. Choose a review date and time that suits you.
7. Enter any review comments regarding the policy.
8. Provide any review recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, **Required**): Enter your first name as it appears in your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, **Required**): Enter your last name as it appears in your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, **Required**): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, **Optional**): Enter your phone number for follow-up inquiries.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, **Required**): Enter the number of the policy you'd like to review.
* **Review Type** (`review_type`, **Required**): Choose between Policy Review or Policy Update to specify the type of review you need.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, **Required**): Select a date for the review to be conducted.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, **Required**): Choose a time for the review to be conducted.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, **Optional**): Enter any comments about the policy you'd like to review.
* **Review Recommendations** (`review_recommendations`, **Optional**): Enter any recommendations you have for the policy review.
## Tips
* Ensure that you have already been involved with the policy and that you have not already submitted a review request for the same policy number.
* Double-check that you've entered your details correctly before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the policy review team.
