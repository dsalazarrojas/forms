# Utility Consumption Dashboard Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect user consent for accessing the Utility Consumption Dashboard, a platform for tracking and analyzing utility consumption data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal details section with your name, address, and contact information.
2. Select the types of utilities you wish to view on the dashboard.
3. Choose the desired view and access levels for your dashboard.
4. Specify how often you wish to access the dashboard.
5. Provide a consent date for dashboard access.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Street Number** (`Street Number`, text, required): Please enter the number of your street or building.
* **Street Name** (`Street Name`, text, required): Please enter the name of your street or building.
* **City** (`City`, text, required): Please enter the name of your city.
* **State** (`State`, text, required): Please enter the abbreviation for your state (e.g., CA for California).
* **Zip Code** (`Zip Code`, text, required): Please enter your zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address for contact.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Consent Date** (`Consent Date`, date, required): Date of the consent for accessing the dashboard.
* **Utility Types** (`Utility Types`, select_multiple, required): Select the utilities you want to view on the dashboard.
	+ Electricity
	+ Gas
	+ Water
	+ Internet
	+ Other
* **Dashboard View** (`Dashboard View`, select_one, required): Choose the view mode for the dashboard.
	+ Public
	+ Private
* **Dashboard Access** (`Dashboard Access`, select_multiple, required): Choose the access level for the dashboard.
	+ View Only
	+ Edit
	+ View and Edit
* **Dashboard Frequency** (`Dashboard Frequency`, number, required): Enter how many days/weeks/months/years you want to access the dashboard.
	+ Note: This field requires a numerical value.

## Tips

- Ensure you complete this form accurately and honestly.
- The required fields have asterisks (\*) indicating that they are mandatory.
- The select fields have options that will help you choose the correct settings for your dashboard.
