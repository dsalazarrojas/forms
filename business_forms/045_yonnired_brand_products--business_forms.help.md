# yonnired_brand_products - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about brand products from a customer, including shop on form, total price, customer contact, shop total, customer phone, shop complete, shop price, customer email, shop send, and send time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your response to "Shop On Form" and choose the appropriate option.
2. Enter the "Total Price" in the designated field.
3. If using cashapp to collect payments, enter the customer's contact information in the "Customer Contact" field.
4. Choose the "Shop Total" option that corresponds to your shop's total.
5. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
6. Indicate whether the shop is complete in the "Shop Complete" field.
7. Enter the shop price in the numerical "Shop Price" field.
8. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
9. If using cashapp to collect payments, enter a note in the "Shop Send" field.
10. Enter the send time in the "Send Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shop On Form** (`select_multiple`, Required/Optional): Choose whether to shop on form.
* **Total Price** (`number`, Required/Optional): Enter the total price of the shop.
* **Customer Contact** (`note`, Required/Optional): If using cashapp to collect payments, enter the customer's contact information for the send total.
* **Shop Total** (`select_one`, Required/Optional): Choose the shop total that corresponds to your shop's total.
* **Customer Phone** (`text`, Required/Optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Shop Complete** (`select_one`, Required/Optional): Indicate whether the shop is complete.
* **Shop Price** (`number`, Required/Optional): Enter the shop price.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, Required/Optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Shop Send** (`note`, Required/Optional): If using cashapp to collect payments, enter a note in this field.
* **Send Time** (`time`, Required/Optional): Enter the send time for the shop.
