# swms_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "swms_form_template" form is an internal business form used to assess and evaluate controls, ensuring compliance with relevant regulations and standards. It provides a structured approach to identifying potential risks and hazards and documenting controls to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have a clear understanding of its purpose and your responsibilities as the user.
2. Complete each page carefully, taking into account the specific guidance provided.
3. Answer each question honestly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
4. Submit the completed form to your designated reviewer or approver for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **introduction (1)** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief introduction to the risk assessment and control evaluation, explaining the context and scope of the assessment.
* **risk_assessment (2)** (`text`, required: false): Describe the identified risks and hazards associated with the controls or activities being assessed.
* **controls (3)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select one of the options to indicate whether controls are in place.
	+ 'Yes': Controls are in place to mitigate the identified risks and hazards.
	+ 'No': No controls are in place, or they are insufficient.
* **controls_type (4)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable options to describe the type of controls in place.
	+ 'Yes': Specific control measures are in place.
* **controls_description (5)** (`text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the controls in place.
* **controls_precautions (6)** (`text`, required: false): Describe any additional precautions taken to mitigate the identified risks and hazards.
* **controls_precautions_2 (7)** (`text`, required: false): If necessary, provide further details on additional precautions taken.
* **controls_precautions_3 (8)** (`text`, required: false): Additional precautions taken.
* **controls_precautions_4 (9)** (`text`, required: false): More on additional precautions.
* **controls_hazard (10)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable options to indicate the presence or absence of hazards associated with the controls.
	+ 'Yes': Hazards are present.
* **controls_hazard (11)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable options to indicate the presence or absence of hazards associated with the controls.
	+ 'Yes': Hazards are present.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate in your responses.

- Ensure all fields are completed honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you're unsure about any aspect of the form, consult with your supervisor or relevant stakeholders.
