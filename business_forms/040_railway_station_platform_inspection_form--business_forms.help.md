Railway Station Platform Inspection Form - Help Guide
=============================================

## Purpose
The Railway Station Platform Inspection Form is used to assess the condition of a railway station platform. This form helps track and record the inspection results, ensuring that the platform is safe and ready for use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form data section to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. In Section 1, enter any additional comments or observations about the platform's condition.
3. In the Platform Condition field, select the current condition of the platform (e.g., 1 for Poor, 2 for Fair, 3 for Good, 4 for Very Good, 5 for Excellent).
4. In the Safety Inspection field, select the outcome of the safety inspection (e.g., Pass, Fail, Pass with comments).
5. In the Remarks field, enter any additional comments or observations about the platform's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): A space to enter any additional comments or observations about the platform.
* **Platform Condition** (`platform_condition`, number, optional): A scale to evaluate the platform's condition, with higher numbers indicating better condition.
* **Safety Inspection** (`safety_inspection`, select_one, required): A field to record the outcome of the safety inspection.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, optional): A field to enter any additional comments or observations about the platform's condition.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about the platform's condition, please select the corresponding rating and enter a note in the Remarks field.
* If you need to add more sections, please let the administrator know.

Note: The Platform Condition and Safety Inspection fields require selection from a scale or options, while the Form Data and Remarks fields are for free-text entry.
