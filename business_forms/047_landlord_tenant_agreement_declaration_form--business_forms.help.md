# landlord_tenant_agreement_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landlord-Tenant Agreement Declaration Form is used to create a record of the landlord-tenant agreement, which is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of the rental property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the correct information.
2. Provide details about the landlord's and tenant's agreement terms, if applicable.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Landlord Name
* **Landlord Name** (1, text, required): Fill in the name of the landlord or property owner.
### Tenant Name
* **Tenant Name** (2, text, required): Fill in the name of the tenant who is renting the property.
### Property Address
* **Property Address** (3, text, required): Enter the address of the rental property.
### Start Date
* **Start Date** (4, date, optional): If applicable, enter the start date of the lease term.
### End Date
* **End Date** (5, date, optional): If applicable, enter the end date of the lease term.
### Rent
* **Rent** (6, number, optional): If applicable, enter the rent amount for the lease.
### Utilities
* **Utilities** (7, number, optional): If applicable, enter any additional utilities included in the rent, such as electricity or water bills.
### Deposit
* **Deposit** (8, number, optional): If applicable, enter the deposit amount paid by the tenant.
### Lease Terms
* **Lease Terms** (9, text, optional): If applicable, enter any other terms of the lease agreement.
### Tenant Signature
* **Tenant Signature** (10, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm the agreement as the tenant.
### Landlord Signature
* **Landlord Signature** (11, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm the agreement as the landlord.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Sign the form with your own handwriting for authentication purposes.
* Keep a record of the filled form for future reference and updates.
