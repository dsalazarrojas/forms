# Honey Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Honey Order Form is used to provide information about a honey order. Please fill out the form with the necessary details to ensure accurate processing and delivery of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Customer Information** section with your details (customer_name, customer_email, customer_phone).
2. Provide the **Order Details** by filling in the order_item_id, order_item_price, and order_quantity fields.
3. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu.
4. Add any additional **Order Notes** in the notes field.
5. Confirm your **Order Date** and **Order Time**.
6. Complete the **Order Status** field.
7. Review and submit the form for processing.
