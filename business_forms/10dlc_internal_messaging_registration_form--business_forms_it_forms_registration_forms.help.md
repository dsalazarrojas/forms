# 10DLC Internal Messaging Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to help the 10DLC team evaluate and approve internal messaging programs within your company. The form gathers details about the program's purpose, scope, and compliance with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the sections as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your program's specifics.
3. If you have any additional comments or requests, use the "Additional Comments or Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name** (id: 2, text): Enter your official company name as it appears in company records.
* **Your Company Email Address** (id: 3, email): Enter your company's email address where messages will be sent.
* **Your Department/Team** (id: 4, text): Specify the department or team associated with this messaging program.
* **Your Internal Phone Number** (id: 5, phone): Enter your internal phone number, including extension or direct line.
* **Name of Messaging Program/Campaign** (id: 7, text): Provide the name of your internal messaging program, e.g., "Company-Wide HR Updates", "IT System Alerts".
* **Brief Description of Messaging Program** (id: 8, text): Describe the purpose and content of your messaging program.
* **Primary Use Case Category** (id: 9, select_one): Select the main purpose of your messaging program:
	+ Employee Communications
	+ System Alerts & Notifications
	+ HR Information & Benefits
	+ IT Support & Updates
	+ Team Collaboration
	+ Operational Logistics
* **Target Audience Type** (id: 10, select_one): Specify who will receive these messages:
	+ All Employees
	+ Specific Departments/Teams
	+ Leadership/Management
	+ Project Teams
* **Estimated Monthly Volume** (id: 11, integer): Provide an approximate number of messages sent per month.
* **Desired Sender ID / Brand Name** (id: 12, text): Choose a name that will appear to recipients, e.g., "Company HR", "IT Support".
* **Expected Message Content Types** (id: 13, select_multiple): Select all types of information that will be shared:
	+ Announcements
	+ Alerts & Warnings
	+ Policy Updates
	+ Meeting Invites/Reminders
	+ HR Communications (e.g., benefits)
	+ IT Notifications (e.g., outages)
	+ Internal Marketing/Promotions
* **Planned Messaging Start Date** (id: 14, date): Enter the date when you plan to start sending messages.
* **Message Category** (id: 16, select_one): Specify the main category of messages:
	+ Transactional (e.g., alerts, confirmations)
	+ Promotional/Informational (e.g., internal marketing, newsletters)
* **Will messages contain sensitive company or personal data?** (id: 17, select_one): Choose:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Potentially (ensure encryption/masking)
* **How can recipients opt-out or manage preferences?** (id: 18, select_one): Select the mechanism for recipients to opt-out:
	+ STOP keyword (for SMS)
	+ Unsubscribe link (for app/email)
	+ Contact Administrator/HR
	+ Not applicable (mandatory communications)
* **Fallback communication method if messages fail to deliver** (id: 19, text): Provide an alternative communication method if messages don't reach recipients.
* **Approving Manager's Full Name** (id: 21, text): Enter the full name of the approving manager.
* **Approving Manager's Email Address** (id: 22, email): Enter the email address of the approving manager.
* **Date of Application** (id: 23, date): Enter the date of application.
* **Applicant Declaration** (id: 24, select_one): Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
	+ I confirm that all information provided above is accurate and complete to the best of my knowledge.
	+ Other
* **Additional Comments or Notes** (id: 25, text): Provide any further context or requests.

Note: Please complete this form carefully, as incomplete submissions might lead to delays in the approval process. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the 10DLC team for assistance.
