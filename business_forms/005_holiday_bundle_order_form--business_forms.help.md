# Holiday Bundle Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a new holiday bundle order. Fill in the required and optional fields to provide detailed information for order processing and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with all the required details.
2. Review the form before submitting it to ensure all the information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Holiday Bundle Order** (`holiday_bundle_order_form`, text, optional): Fill in the name of the holiday bundle order.
* **Bundle ID** (`bundle_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the holiday bundle you want to order.
* **Bundle Name** (`bundle_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the holiday bundle.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the holiday bundle you want to order.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the holiday bundle.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Fill in the shipping address for the holiday bundle order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes for the holiday bundle order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately.
* Review the form before submitting it to avoid any mistakes or errors.
* Use the bundle id to check if the same bundle is ordered before submitting the form. 
* Use the form for both internal and external purposes like customer order status follow-up.
