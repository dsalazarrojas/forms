# sprinkler_system_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the delivery of sprinkler systems to customers. Its purpose is to collect information about the customer, the details of the delivery, the order fulfillment process, and other relevant specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please provide accurate and detailed information about the customer, the order, and the delivery process.
- Enter the customer's name, contact details, and any other required information under the "Customer Info" section.
- Under "Delivery Details", provide the date and time of the delivery, as well as any relevant details about the order.
- In "Order Fulfillment", describe the process and any issues or challenges encountered during the delivery.
- In "Sprinkler System Info", provide any specific details about the sprinkler system being delivered.
- Select all relevant system components that are part of the order from the "System Components" dropdown list.
- For "Customer Contact", provide any additional contact information or notes from the customer.
- Add any additional notes or comments about the order under "Order Notes".
- Finally, select the "Delivery Status" from the dropdown list to update the order status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Info
* **Customer Info** (`Customer Info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
### Delivery Details
* **Delivery Details** (`Delivery Details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the date and time of the delivery, as well as any relevant details about the order.
### Order Fulfillment
* **Order Fulfillment** (`Order Fulfillment`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the process and any issues or challenges encountered during the delivery.
### Sprinkler System Info
* **Sprinkler System Info** (`Sprinkler System Info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any specific details about the sprinkler system being delivered.
### System Components
* **System Components** (`System Components`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all relevant system components that are part of the order from the dropdown list.
### Customer Contact
* **Customer Contact** (`Customer Contact`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional contact information or notes from the customer.
### Order Notes
* **Order Notes** (`Order Notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
### Delivery Status
* **Delivery Status** (`Delivery Status`, `select_one`, required): Select the delivery status from the dropdown list to update the order status.
### Delivery Location
* **Delivery Location** (`Delivery Location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the delivery location.
### Delivery Date/Time
* **Delivery Date/Time** (`Delivery Date/Time`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the delivery date and time.
### Notes
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
### Delivery Date
* **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the delivery date.
### Delivery Time
* **Delivery Time** (`Delivery Time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the delivery time.
### Customer Signature
* **Customer Signature** (`Customer Signature`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's signature.
### Installer Signature
* **Installer Signature** (`Installer Signature`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the installer's signature.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled with accurate and detailed information.
- If there are any issues or challenges during the delivery, please describe them under "Order Fulfillment".
- The "Delivery Status" field should be updated regularly to reflect the current status of the order.
- Any additional notes or comments can be added under "Order Notes".
