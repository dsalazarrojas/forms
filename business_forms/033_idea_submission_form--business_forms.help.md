# Idea Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Idea Submission Form is designed for employees, customers, or partners to submit their innovative ideas for review and potential implementation within our organization. This form aims to collect essential information from the submitters, ensuring that their ideas are thoroughly understood and can be properly evaluated.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**, a required field that will help us understand who you are and what you're submitting.
2. Enter your **Last Name**, a required field that will help us understand who you are and what you're submitting.
3. Enter your **Email**, a field that will allow us to contact you and share updates about your submitted idea.
4. Enter your **Phone**, an optional field where you can provide a contact number in case we need to reach out to you for further discussion.
5. Enter your **Department**, an optional field that indicates which team or area your idea is related to. Please choose from the options provided in the drop-down menu, or type a new value if your department is not listed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: A required field for your name.
* **Last Name**: A required field for your name.
* **Email**: A field for your contact email.
* **Phone**: An optional field for your contact phone number.
* **Department**: An optional field for the department your idea is related to.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields (First Name, Last Name, and Email) before submitting the form. If you're unsure about any information, feel free to ask, and our team will be happy to guide you through the submission process.
