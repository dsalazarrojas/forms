# Minor Injuries Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Minor Injuries Form is designed to collect information about minor injuries sustained by employees in the workplace. This form helps HR or risk management teams document and report injuries, which is an essential process for workplace safety and compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Injury Date field with the date the injury occurred.
2. Enter the Business Name in the corresponding field.
3. Enter the Employee Name who was involved in the injury.
4. Provide a detailed description of the injury in the Injury Description field.
5. If applicable, enter the name and contact information of the treatment provider.
6. Attach a copy of the medical report from the treatment provider.
7. If a witness was present, enter their name and a brief statement describing what they saw.
8. If a witness has provided a statement, enter their name and contact information.
9. If additional witness statements are provided, enter them in the Witness Statement 2 field.
10. If a witness has signed a statement, enter their signature in the Witness Signature field.
11. Repeat steps 8-10 for each additional witness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Injury Date** (`injury_date`, date, required): Enter the date the injury occurred.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the business where the injury occurred.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee who was involved in the injury.
* **Injury Description** (`injury_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the injury.
* **Treatment Provider** (`treatment_provider`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name and contact information of the treatment provider.
* **Medical Report** (`medical_report`, text, required): Attach a copy of the medical report from the treatment provider.
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, text, optional): If a witness was present, enter their brief statement describing what they saw.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the witness.
* **Witness Contact Info** (`witness_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the witness.
* **Witness Statement 2** (`witness_statement_2`, text, optional): If additional witness statements are provided, enter them here.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the witness.
* **Witness Name 2** (`witness_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of the additional witness.
* **Witness Title** (`witness_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the additional witness.
* **Witness Signature 2** (`witness_signature_2`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the additional witness.
* **Witness Title 2** (`witness_title_2`, text, optional): Enter the title of the second witness.
* **Witness Signature 3** (`witness_signature_3`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the third witness.
* **Witness Title 3** (`witness_title_3`, text, optional): Enter the title of the third witness.
* **Witness Signature 4** (`witness_signature_4`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the fourth witness.
* **Witness Name 3** (`witness_name_3`, text, optional): Enter the name of the third witness.
* **Witness Title 4** (`witness_title_4`, text, optional): Enter the title of the fourth witness.
* **Witness Signature 5** (`witness_signature_5`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the fifth witness.
* **Witness Name 4** (`witness_name_4`, text, optional): Enter the name of the fourth witness.
* **Witness Title 5** (`witness_title_5`, text, optional): Enter the title of the fifth witness.
* **Witness Signature 6** (`witness_signature_6`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the sixth witness.
* **Witness Name 5** (`witness_name_5`, text, optional): Enter the name of the fifth witness.
* **Witness Title 6** (`witness_title_6`, text, optional): Enter the title of the sixth witness.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Attach a copy of the medical report from the treatment provider.
* If a witness has signed a statement, ensure that their signature is attached.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult your HR or risk management team for guidance.
