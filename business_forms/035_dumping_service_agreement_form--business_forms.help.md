# dumping_service_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect agreement information from clients or customers for a dumping service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Provide job details in the first field.
2. Select multiple service locations from the provided options.
3. Enter schedules in the second field.
4. Enter signed terms in the third field.
5. Fill in client details in the fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job details** (`job_details`, text, required: false): Enter brief details about the job or service required.
* **Service locations** (`service_locations`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple locations where the service will be provided. Options are: Haulers, Contractors, Property managers.
* **Schedules** (`schedules`, text, required: false): Enter any specific schedules or timing requirements for the service.
* **Signed terms** (`signed_terms`, text, required: false): Enter any signed terms or agreements related to the service.
* **Client details** (`client_details`, text, required: false): Enter client information, such as name, contact, or address.

## Tips
* Ensure that the fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form before submission to ensure all required (if any) and optional fields are filled.
* Double-check the service locations selection to ensure it matches the client's needs.
* Be specific when entering schedules and signed terms.
* Enter client details as required for the service.
