# Construction Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the rental and delivery of construction equipment for a specific project. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your order is processed correctly and delivered promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your company name and contact information, including the contact person, email address, and primary phone number.
2.  Select the primary equipment type that you need, such as earthmoving, material handling, or power generation.
3.  Enter the specific model or size of the equipment if known.
4.  Enter the quantity of equipment needed.
5.  List any attachments or accessories required.
6.  Select the start and estimated return dates for the rental period.
7.  Choose the delivery method: whether you want the equipment delivered to your site or if you will be picking it up.
8.  If delivered, provide the site address for delivery.
9.  Let us know if you require a certified operator for the equipment.
10. Enter the on-site contact phone number.
11.  Provide any special delivery or site access instructions.
12. Enter any additional equipment or service requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Billing Email Address** (Billing Email Address, email, required): Enter the email address associated with your account.
* **Primary Phone Number** (Primary Phone Number, text, required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Project ID or Name** (Project ID or Name, text, required): Enter the ID or name of your project.
* **Primary Equipment Type** (Primary Equipment Type, select_one, required): Select the main category of equipment needed.
*   *Earthmoving (Excavator, Backhoe, Dozer)*
  *Material Handling (Forklift, Telehandler)*
  *Aerial Work (Scissor Lift, Boom Lift)*
  *Compaction (Roller, Plate Compactor)*
  *Power Generation (Generator, Light Tower)*
  *Tools (Breaker, Saw, Drill)*
* **Specific Model or Size (If known)** (Specific Model or Size (If known), text, optional): Enter the specific model or size of the equipment if known.
* **Quantity Needed** (Quantity Needed, number, required): Enter the quantity of equipment needed.
* **Attachments or Accessories Needed** (Attachments or Accessories Needed, text, optional): List any attachments or accessories required.
* **Rental Start Date** (Rental Start Date, date, required): Select the start date for the rental period.
* **Estimated Return Date** (Estimated Return Date, date, required): Select the estimated return date for the rental period.
* **Delivery or Pickup** (Delivery or Pickup, select_one, required): Choose the delivery method.
  *Deliver to Site*
  *Customer Pickup*
* **Site Delivery Address** (Site Delivery Address, text, optional): Enter the site address for delivery if chosen.
* **Certified Operator** (Do you require a certified operator?, select_one, required): Let us know if you require a certified operator.
  *No (Self-operated)*
  *Yes (With operator)*
* **On-site Contact Phone Number** (On-site Contact Phone Number, text, required): Enter the on-site contact phone number.
* **Special Delivery or Site Access Instructions** (Special Delivery or Site Access Instructions, text, optional): Enter any special delivery or site access instructions.
* **Additional Equipment or Service Requirements** (Additional Equipment or Service Requirements, text, optional): Enter any additional equipment or service requirements.
