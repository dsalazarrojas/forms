# Private Swim Lesson Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who are interested in taking private swim lessons. The form is used to register participants for private lessons and to track their lesson details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Participant Name
	* Lesson Date
	* Lesson Time
	* Swim Level
	* Lesson Type
	* Swim Frequency
	* Payment Method
	* Payment Status
2. Enter any additional information that is relevant to the participant's lesson.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Swim School ID**: Enter the ID number of the swim school that the participant is registered with. This helps the swim school keep track of their students.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant taking the private swim lesson.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the email address of the participant taking the private swim lesson. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant taking the private swim lesson.
* **Lesson Date**: Select the date that the private swim lesson will take place.
* **Lesson Time**: Select the time that the private swim lesson will take place.
* **Lesson Duration**: Select the length of time that the private swim lesson will last. This can be entered as a number in minutes.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the swim instructor that will be leading the private swim lesson.
* **Lesson Schedule**: Enter any notes or details about the lesson schedule, such as "Repeats weekly" or "Repeats bi-weekly".
* **Participant Notes**: Enter any additional information that is relevant to the participant's lesson, such as "Has a physical disability" or "Is a beginner swimmer".
* **Swim Level**: Select the swim level of the participant taking the private swim lesson:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Lesson Type**: Select the type of private swim lesson that the participant is taking:
	+ Private Group Lesson (for multiple participants)
	+ Private Lesson (one-on-one instruction)
* **Swim Frequency**: Select how often the participant would like to take private swim lessons:
	+ Weekly
	+ Semi-Monthly
	+ Monthly
* **Lesson Fees**: Enter the fee for the private swim lesson. This can be a number.
* **Payment Method**: Select how the participant would like to pay for the private swim lesson:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Check
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of the payment for the private swim lesson:
	+ Paid
	+ Unpaid

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the swim school can communicate effectively with the participant.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the swim school directly.
