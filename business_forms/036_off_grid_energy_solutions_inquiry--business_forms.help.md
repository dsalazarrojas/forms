# Off Grid Energy Solutions Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Off Grid Energy Solutions Inquiry form is designed to collect information about off-grid energy solutions for a specific location. This form is meant to help users understand their energy needs and potential solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **System Type** (goals_system_type): Choose the type of off-grid energy solution you are inquiring about (e.g., solar, wind, hydro, biomass, or other).
2. **Location**: Enter the location where the off-grid energy solution will be installed (e.g., address, city, state).
3. **Energy Source**: Select the energy source you are interested in (e.g., Solar, Wind, Hydro, Biomass, or Other).
4. **System Size**: Enter the size of the off-grid energy system you are inquiring about (e.g., in kW or MWh).
5. **Capacity**: Enter the capacity of the off-grid energy system you are inquiring about (e.g., in kW or MWh).
6. **Budget**: Enter your budget for the off-grid energy solution (e.g., in $).
7. **System Location**: Enter the location where the off-grid energy system will be installed (e.g., address, city, state).
8. **Installation Type**: Choose the type of installation you are inquiring about (e.g., Grid-Tied, Off-Grid, Net-Metered, or Other).
9. **Site Visit**: Schedule a site visit for a professional assessment of your off-grid energy needs (optional).
10. **Site Access**: Enter any access restrictions or requirements for the site visit (e.g., parking, elevator access).
11. **Owner Type**: Choose the type of ownership for the off-grid energy solution (e.g., Owner, Renter, Other, Company).
12. **Site Access Prophets**: Check whether you have any site access restrictions or requirements (e.g., Yes, No).
13. **Project Details**: Enter any additional project details or notes (e.g., specific system requirements).
14. **System Size Unit**: Enter the unit of measurement for the system size (e.g., kW or MWh).
15. **Energy Tariff**: Select the energy tariff you are interested in (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
16. **Contact Name**: Enter your name and contact information (e.g., name, email, phone number).
17. **Contact Email**: Enter your email address (e.g., [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com)).
18. **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
19. **Contact Note**: Enter any additional contact notes or information (e.g., availability for site visit).
20. **Company**: Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
21. **Job Title**: Enter your job title (if applicable).
22. **Project Status**: Choose the current status of your off-grid energy project (e.g., Active, Inactive).
23. **Project Status Reason**: Enter the reason for the project status (if applicable).
24. **Project Status**: Choose the current status of your off-grid energy project (e.g., Active, Inactive).
25. **Site Visit Date**: Schedule a site visit for a professional assessment of your off-grid energy needs (if applicable).
