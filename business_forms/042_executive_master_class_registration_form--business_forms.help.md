# executive_master_class_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the executive master class registration form. This form is designed to collect information from participants registering for the executive master class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the participant information section with your details.
2. In the registration details section, provide the necessary information about your registration.
3. If you have specific requirements for your participation, please select the relevant options in the participant requirements section.
4. Provide billing details and payment information in the billing details section.
5. Confirm your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information (1)
* **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`, text, required/optional): In this section, please provide your basic information including any relevant details about yourself.

### Registration Details (2)
* **Registration Details** (`Registration Details`, text, required/optional): Here, you can enter any relevant details about your registration, such as your preferred dates or times.

### Participant Requirements (3)
* **Participant Requirements** (`Participant Requirements`, text, required/optional): If you have any specific requirements for your participation, please select the relevant options from the dropdown list.

### Participant Requirements Select (8)
* **Participant Requirements Select** (`Participant Requirements Select`, select_multiple, required/optional): This is a dropdown list where you can select multiple options that apply to your requirements.

### Participant Requirements Select One (9)
* **Participant Requirements Select One** (`Participant Requirements Select One`, select_one, required/optional): Here, you can select one option from the list that best applies to your requirements.

### Participant Requirements Select Multiple (10)
* **Participant Requirements Select Multiple** (`Participant Requirements Select Multiple`, select_multiple, required/optional): This is another dropdown list where you can select multiple options that apply to your requirements.

### Participant Requirements Select Multiple 2 (11)
* **Participant Requirements Select Multiple 2** (`Participant Requirements Select Multiple 2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Similar to the previous section, select the relevant options from the dropdown list.

### Participant Information Date (12)
* **Participant Information Date** (`Participant Information Date`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth or any other relevant date in this field.

### Participant Information Time (13)
* **Participant Information Time** (`Participant Information Time`, time, required/optional): If you have a preferred time for your participation, enter it in this field.

### Billing Details (4)
* **Billing Details** (`Billing Details`, text, required/optional): Here, you can enter any billing information, such as invoice numbers or payment methods.

### Payment Information (5)
* **Payment Information** (`Payment Information`, text, required/optional): In this section, provide any payment details such as payment status or methods.

### Confirm and Submit (6,7)
