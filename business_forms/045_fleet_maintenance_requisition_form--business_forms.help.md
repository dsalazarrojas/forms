# fleet_maintenance_requisition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and track routine maintenance tasks for company vehicles. It helps to ensure that all necessary repairs and replacements are performed on time, reducing the risk of vehicle breakdowns and related costs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "New Form" to start filling out the form.
2. Enter the vehicle number in the "vehicle_number" field.
3. Select the maintenance type from the "maintenance_type" dropdown list (e.g., oil change, tire replacement, etc.).
4. Provide a brief description of the maintenance task in the "description" field.
5. Enter the schedule date for the maintenance task in the "schedule_date" field.
6. Enter the schedule time for the maintenance task in the "schedule_time" field.
7. Choose the service provider (mechanic, dealer, or external) for the maintenance task in the "service_provider" field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments regarding the maintenance task in the "notes" field.
9. Click "Submit" to save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vehicle_number** (Number, required): Enter the vehicle's registration number or Fleet ID.
* **maintenance_type** (Select, required): Select the type of maintenance required (e.g., oil change, tire replacement, etc.).
* **description** (Text, optional): Provide a brief description of the maintenance task.
* **schedule_date** (Date, optional): Enter the date when the maintenance task is scheduled.
* **schedule_time** (Time, optional): Enter the time when the maintenance task is scheduled.
* **service_provider** (Select, optional): Choose the service provider (mechanic, dealer, or external) for the maintenance task.
* **notes** (Note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the maintenance task.

## Tips
* Use this form to schedule and track routine maintenance tasks for company vehicles.
* Ensure that you enter accurate and detailed information in each field to facilitate efficient processing.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the vehicle's maintenance records for guidance.
