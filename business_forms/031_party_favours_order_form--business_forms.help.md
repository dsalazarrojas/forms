# party_favours_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about party favors, including order details and customer contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the party favors in the **party_favours_order_form_name** field.
2. Select the date of the order from the **order_date** field.
3. Enter the time of the order in the **order_time** field.
4. Indicate whether party favors were ordered in the **party_favours** field using the options 'Yes' or 'No'.
5. Enter the customer's name in the **customer_name** field.
6. Enter the customer's email address in the **customer_email** field.
7. Enter the customer's phone number in the **customer_phone** field.
8. Add any additional notes about the order in the **party_favours_order_form_notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **party_favours_order_form_name**: Enter the name of the party favors being ordered.
* **order_date**: Select the date the order was placed. Use the format MM/dd/yyyy (e.g., 02/22/2023).
* **order_time**: Select the time the order was placed. Use the format hh:mm (e.g., 14:30).
* **party_favours**: Indicate whether party favors were ordered by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **customer_name**: Enter the customer's name as it appears on their account or contact information.
* **customer_email**: Enter the customer's email address as it appears on their account or contact information.
* **customer_phone**: Enter the customer's phone number as it appears on their account or contact information.
* **party_favours_order_form_notes**: Add any additional notes about the order, such as special requests or instructions.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
- If the party favors were not ordered, leave the **party_favours** field blank.
- Be concise in the **party_favours_order_form_notes** field; focus on essential details only.
