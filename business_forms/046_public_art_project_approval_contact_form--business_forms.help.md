# public_art_project_approval_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for contacting public art project approval. It's meant for artists and others who want to submit their public art projects for evaluation and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter the Artist Name of the project in the Artist Name field.
6. Enter the name of the Organization associated with the project in the Organization field.
7. Enter the Proposal Date for the project in the Proposal Date field.
8. Enter the Proposal Deadline for the project in the Proposal Deadline field.
9. Select the Decision Making Body that will be making the decision on the project from the Decision Making Body field. You can also provide an alternative Decision Making Body in the Other field.
10. Provide any additional Comments about the project in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your personal last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Artist Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the artist who created or is proposing the project.
* **Organization** (text, required): Enter the name of the organization associated with the project.
* **Proposal Date** (date, required): Enter the date the proposal was received.
* **Proposal Deadline** (date, required): Enter the deadline for when the proposal was due.
* **Decision Making Body** (select_one, required): Select the body that will be making the decision on the project. Options include:
	+ Artist
	+ City
	+ County
	+ Public Art Board
* **Other** (text, optional): Enter an alternative Decision Making Body not listed above.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the project.
