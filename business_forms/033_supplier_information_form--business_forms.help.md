# Supplier Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Information Form is designed to gather essential information about a supplier for business operations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
- Ensure that you have all the required information before submitting the form.
- Review your entries carefully before finalizing the form.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, refer to the field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name** (Required): Enter the full name of the supplier.
* **Supplier Address** (Required): Enter the full address of the supplier.
* **Supplier City** (Required): Enter the city where the supplier is located.
* **Supplier State** (Required): Enter the state where the supplier is located.
* **Supplier Phone** (Optional): Enter the phone number of the supplier (if available).
* **Supplier Email** (Required): Enter the email address of the supplier.
* **Supplier Tax ID** (Optional): Select one of the provided tax ID options (if available).
* **Supplier Note** (Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the supplier.
* **Contact Name** (Required): Enter the full name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (Required): Enter the email address of the contact person.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with your supervisor or relevant personnel for guidance.
- Keep your entries up-to-date and review them regularly to reflect any changes in supplier information.
