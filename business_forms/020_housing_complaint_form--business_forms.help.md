# Housing Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Housing Complaint Form is a tool used to report issues or complaints related to housing. This form allows users to provide a clear and concise description of the problem, categorize the issue, and attach any relevant supporting documents. This information will be used to investigate and resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Housing Complaint**: Enter a clear and concise title of the issue you are reporting.
2. **Category**: Select the category that best describes the type of complaint you are reporting (e.g. Business Forms, Complaint Forms, Administrative Forms, or Other).
3. **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the problem you are experiencing.
4. **Date**: Enter the date on which the issue occurred or is currently occurring.
5. **Time**: Enter the time on which the issue occurred or is currently occurring.
6. **Issue**: Describe the specific issue you are experiencing with your housing.
7. **Concern**: If there is a specific concern or worry related to the issue, enter it here.
8. **Attachment**: If there is any relevant supporting documentation or evidence, such as photos or videos, you can attach it here.
9. **Submit**: Click the submit button to send your complaint.
