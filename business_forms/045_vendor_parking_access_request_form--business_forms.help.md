# Vendor Parking Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Parking Access Request Form is a form used to request access for a vendor to park their vehicle on company premises for a specific period. This form helps in managing parking requests from vendors efficiently and ensures that they provide accurate information about their parking needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a request, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name accurately.
2. Enter your valid Email address for contact purposes.
3. Provide your Phone number for communication.
4. Specify the Vehicle Model of the vehicle you will be bringing to the premises.
5. Choose the Parking Location on campus where you will be parking.
6. Select the dates you will be parking on campus.
7. Choose the time you will be arriving on campus for parking.
8. Provide a brief description of your request in Request Details.
9. Select the assigned tool that you will be using.
10. Confirm that you have submitted this request correctly by selecting the correct option in Submit Request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email** (email, required): Provide your valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number for efficient communication.
* **Vehicle Model** (text, optional): Specify the model of the vehicle you will be bringing to the premises.
* **Parking Location** (text, optional): Choose the parking location on campus where you will be parking.
* **Parking Dates** (date, optional): Select the dates you will be parking on campus. Format should be DD/MM/YY or MM/YY.
* **Parking Time** (time, optional): Choose the time you will be arriving on campus for parking. Format should be 24-hour clock (HH:MM).
* **Request Details** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of your request. This will help us understand your parking needs better.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_one, required): Select the assigned tool that you will be using.
* **Submit Request** (select_multiple, required): Confirm that you have reviewed and submitted your request correctly.
