# sports_trading_card_royalty_tracking_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "sports_trading_card_royalty_tracking_checklist" form is a tool for tracking royalty payments related to sports trading cards. It helps users to monitor and report progress, category, description, royalty payment status, and other related information. This form is essential for ensuring accurate and timely royalty payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Title" field with a concise and informative title for the royalty tracking checklist.
2. Select the "Category" for the royalty tracking checklist (e.g., "sports", "business", or "other").
3. Provide a brief "Description" of the royalty tracking checklist, if necessary.
4. Choose the "Output File" type for the royalty tracking data (e.g., "pdf", "csv", or "email").
5. Enter the "Form ID" (optional).
6. Select the category (multiple) for the royalty tracking checklist (id: 6).
7. Report the "Reporting Progress" (id: 8).
8. Enter the "Licensed Period" for the royalty payment tracking.
9. Report the "Reporting Status" (id: 10).
10. Enter the "Last Progress Update" and "Last Progress Update By" (ids: 11 and 12, optional).
11. Select the "Royalty Payment Status" (id: 13).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (id: 1, text): Enter a concise and informative title for the royalty tracking checklist. This will be displayed as the heading for the royalty tracking data.
* **Category** (id: 2, select_one): Select the category for the royalty tracking checklist (e.g., "sports", "business", or "other").
* **Description** (id: 3, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the royalty tracking checklist, if necessary.
* **Output File** (id: 4, select_multiple): Choose the type of output file for the royalty tracking data (e.g., "pdf", "csv", or "email").
* **Form ID** (id: 5, number, optional): Enter the form ID for the royalty tracking checklist (optional).
* **Category** (id: 6, select_multiple): Select multiple categories for the royalty tracking checklist (e.g., "sports", "business", "other").
* **Reporting Progress** (id: 8, select_one): Report the progress of the royalty payment tracking (e.g., "pending", "complete", or "overdue").
* **Licensed Period** (id: 9, date): Enter the period for which the royalty payment is licensed.
* **Reporting Status** (id: 10, select_one): Report the status of the royalty payment tracking (e.g., "pending", "complete", or "overdue").
* **Last Progress Update** (id: 11, date, optional): Enter the last progress update date (optional).
* **Last Progress Update By** (id: 12, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who last updated the progress (optional).
* **Royalty Payment Status** (id: 13, select_one): Select the status of the royalty payment (e.g., "pending", "complete", or "overdue").
