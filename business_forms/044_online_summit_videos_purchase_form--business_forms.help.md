# Online Summit Videos Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers who wish to purchase online summit videos. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to facilitate the purchase process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) below. These fields are:
	* Name
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Terms
	* Agree
	* Submit
2. For fields marked as optional, you can choose to fill them out or leave them blank.
3. Read and agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" in the Terms field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for video-specific fields such as format, resolution, quality, and tags.
5. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form and submit your purchase request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Online Summit Videos Purchase Form**: This is a header that indicates the form purpose.
* **Name**: Please enter your name to associate it with your purchase request.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about your purchase.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to contact you in case of any questions or issues.
* **Terms**: Check "Yes" to agree to the terms and conditions of the purchase.
* **Agree**: Check "Yes" to agree to the terms and conditions of the purchase.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete and submit your purchase request.
* **Terms and Conditions**: This field is a detailed explanation of the terms and conditions, but it's not a required field.
* **Video**: Please leave this field blank unless you are uploading a video.
* **Video Terms**: Please leave this field blank unless you are uploading a video.
* **Video Description**: This field is optional and can be used to provide a description of the video.
* **Video Tags**: Please enter tags related to the video.
* **Video Category**: Select "Yes" if the video belongs to a specific category.
* **Video Submitter**: Select "Yes" if you are the video submitter.
* **Video Price**: Enter the price of the video, if you are uploading a video.
* **Video Duration**: Enter the duration of the video, if you are uploading a video.
* **Video Format**: Select "Yes" if the video format is in a specific format.
* **Video Resolution**: Select "Yes" if the video resolution is in a specific format.
* **Video Quality**: Select the quality of the video, which can be "Good" or "Poor".
* **Video File Size**: Enter the file size of the video, if you are uploading a video.
* **Upload Video**: If you are uploading a video, enter the file name or URL.
* **Video Description**: This field is optional and can be used to provide a description of the video.
