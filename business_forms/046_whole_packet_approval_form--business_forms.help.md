# Whole Packet Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Whole Packet Approval Form is a business form designed to help internal teams manage multi-document requests and keep approvals organized in Jotform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the request in the "Whole Packet Approval Form" field.
2. Select the relevant category for the request from the "Business Forms" dropdown.
3. Enter a brief description of the request in the "Collect consistent packet reviews and approval decisions" field.
4. Write a short paragraph explaining the importance of using this form for internal teams (see "Collect consistent packet reviews and approval decisions with the Whole Packet Approval Form").
5. If applicable, assign the request to a team member by selecting from the "Assigned to" dropdown.
6. Identify the approver(s) who will review and approve the request by selecting from the "Approver" dropdown (multiple selections allowed).
7. Enter the date and time of approval in the "Date Approved" and "Time Approved" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Whole Packet Approval Form (1)**: Enter a brief title for the request.
* **Business Forms (2)**: Select the category that best describes the type of request.
* **Collect consistent packet reviews and approval decisions (3)**: Enter a brief description of the request.
* **Collect consistent packet reviews and approval decisions with the Whole Packet Approval Form (4)**: Write a short paragraph explaining the importance of using this form for internal teams.
* **Assigned to (6)**: If applicable, select a team member to whom the request should be assigned.
* **Approver (7)**: Select the person or people responsible for reviewing and approving the request.
* **Date Approved (8)**: Enter the date on which the request was approved.
* **Time Approved (9)**: Enter the time of day on which the request was approved.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct category for the request to ensure it reaches the right team.
* Use the "Assigned to" field to assign the request to a team member if necessary.
* Select all approvers if multiple team members need to review and approve the request.
* Fill out the "Date Approved" and "Time Approved" fields accurately to track the approval timeline.
