# Credit My Rent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help clients credit their rent payments. It is intended to be a user-friendly and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details and the details of your rental property.
2. Ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the rental property where you are crediting your rent payment.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or support team.
* This form is for crediting rent payments only, so ensure you are using it for its intended purpose.
