# Profile Information Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Profile Information Sharing Consent Form is used to collect your consent for sharing your personal information with others. This form is necessary for our business operations and is used to determine which information you are willing to share.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the options that indicate which information you are willing to share with us.
2. Enter any additional notes or comments you have regarding the sharing information.
3. If you want to share your email address with us, enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. If you want to share your phone number with us, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. If you want to specify a date and time for sharing your information, you can select the date and time from the provided date and time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info**: This is a text field where you can enter any additional information about yourself, if needed.
* **Sharing Information**: This field allows you to select which types of information you are willing to share with us. Select "True" for each type of information you want to share:
	+ **Email Sharing**: 
	+ **Phone Sharing**: 
	+ **Note Sharing**:
* **Email**: If you want to share your email address with us, enter it here.
* **Phone**: If you want to share your phone number with us, enter it here.
* **Sharing Date**: If you have a specific date for sharing your information, enter it here.
* **Sharing Time**: If you have a specific time for sharing your information, enter it here.
* **Note**: This is a text field where you can enter any additional notes or comments regarding the sharing information.
