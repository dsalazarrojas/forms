# Team Collaboration Award Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Collaboration Award Recommendation Form is designed to collect information about a team member's outstanding collaboration contributions. This form helps to identify and recognize team members who excel in collaboration, teamwork, and performance. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the team member you are nominating.
2. Enter the name of the nominator (the person filling out the form) and their department.
3. Enter the email address of the nominator for contact information.
4. Enter the phone number of the nominator for additional contact information.
5. Enter the job title of the nominator.
6. Enter the name of the team member being nominated.
7. Enter the title of the team member being nominated.
8. Select the award type that best describes the team member's contribution.
9. Select the award year and month of the team member's contribution.
10. Enter the date of the team member's award.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name**: Please enter the name of your team, e.g., "Marketing Department."
* **Nominator Name**: Enter your name as the person filling out this form.
* **Nominator Department**: Enter your department.
* **Nominator Email**: Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Nominator Phone**: Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Nominator Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Team Member Name**: Enter the name of the team member you are nominating.
* **Team Member Title**: Enter the title of the team member you are nominating.
* **Award Type**: Please select the type of award that best describes the team member's contribution:
	+ Team Collaboration Award
	+ Team Leadership Award
	+ Team Excellence Award
* **Award Year**: Enter the year of the team member's award.
* **Award Month**: Enter the month of the team member's award.
* **Award Date**: Enter the date of the team member's award.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the team member being nominated is aware that they have been selected.
- Only one form should be submitted per team member being nominated.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
