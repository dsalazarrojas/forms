# Housekeeping Team Coordination Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Housekeeping Team Coordination Contact Form is a tool for team members, management, and stakeholders to communicate and coordinate on housekeeping-related issues. It helps to prevent duplication of work and ensures that all necessary information is captured for reference and follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out your contact name and email in the "Contact Name" and "Contact Email" fields, respectively.
2. Select your department from the options provided in the "Department" field.
3. Indicate if you are currently on shift or off shift in the "Shift" field.
4. Choose the type of issue you are experiencing from the options provided in the "Issue Type" field.
5. Provide a brief description of the issue in the "Issue Description" field.
6. If necessary, specify the date and time the issue occurred in the "Issue Date" and "Issue Time" fields.
7. If the issue is related to a report or request, indicate the "Report Date" if known.
8. Finally, choose your request type from the options provided in the "Request Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Please enter your name so we can contact you about the issue.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can respond to you regarding the issue.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select your department from the options provided to help us route the issue to the correct team.
* **Shift** (`shift`, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" if you are currently on shift, and "No" if you are off shift.
* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of issue you are experiencing (e.g., maintenance, equipment, etc.).
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of the issue.
* **Issue Date** (`issue_date`, date, optional): If known, enter the date the issue occurred.
* **Issue Time** (`issue_time`, time, optional): If known, enter the time the issue occurred.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, optional): If the issue is related to a report or request, enter the date of the report.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of request or report (e.g., maintenance, repair, etc.).
