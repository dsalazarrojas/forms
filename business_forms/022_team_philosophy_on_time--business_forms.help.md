This form is used to capture team members' thoughts and insights on the philosophy and values that guide their time management, including their approach to meetings, deadlines, accountability, and work duration. This form aims to help teams establish a shared understanding of their work habits and identify areas for improvement. It is recommended that only the team captain or designated team lead fills out this form to maintain consistency and accuracy.

## Team Philosophy On Time - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form helps you to establish a shared understanding of your team's approach to time management and work habits.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer the questions honestly and thoughtfully, considering your team's dynamics and work style.
2.  Fill out each page (or section) of the form, working from top to bottom.
3.  Provide specific examples or anecdotes to illustrate your points when possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Time Management** (`time_management`, text, optional): Describe your team's approach to managing time, including how you prioritize tasks, set schedules, and adjust to changing circumstances.
* **Meetings** (`meetings`, select multiple, optional): How often do you have meetings in your team? Do you schedule them regularly, as needed, or rarely? Be specific about the types of meetings and their purpose.
* **Deadlines** (`deadlines`, text, optional): What is your approach to setting and meeting deadlines? Describe your team's expectations and how you handle missed deadlines.
* **Accountability** (`accountability`, select one, optional): Do you hold yourself and team members accountable for their actions and progress towards goals? Be honest and specific about your expectations and how you enforce accountability.
* **Meetings Frequency** (`meetings_frequency`, text, optional): How often do you schedule meetings, and what triggers these meetings (e.g., weekly, daily, as needed)?
* **Work Duration** (`work_duration`, text, optional): How long do you typically work on a task or project before taking a break or switching to another task?
* **Breaks** (`breaks`, number, optional): How many breaks do you take during a workday, and how long do they last?
* **Work Frequency** (`work_frequency`, text, optional): How often do you work on a specific task or project, and what factors influence your work frequency (e.g., availability, priority, deadlines)?

## Tips

* Be specific and honest in your answers to provide valuable insights for your team.
* Review and discuss your answers with your team to ensure everyone is on the same page.
* Use this form as a starting point for ongoing discussions and improvements to your team's work habits.
