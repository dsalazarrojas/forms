# Invoicing Tool Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to provide information about the requisioning needs of business forms in the invoicing tool. It is used to submit requests for new business forms that will be used to create invoices. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the business forms you require (page 1) by choosing "Yes" or "No" for each form type.
2. Enter a title for the new form (page 3) to help identify it in the system.
3. Enter a description of the new form (page 4) to explain its purpose and usage.
4. If applicable, enter the ID of the tool assigned to this new form (page 5).
5. Optionally, provide a list of form IDs (page 6) for integration purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Forms** (`business_forms`, select_multiple): Select the business forms you need for the new form. Choose "Yes" for forms you need, and "No" for forms you don't need.
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text): Enter a title for the new form to help identify it in the system.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text): Enter a description of the new form to explain its purpose and usage.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text): If applicable, enter the ID of the tool assigned to this new form for integration purposes.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, number): Optionally, provide a list of form IDs for integration purposes.
