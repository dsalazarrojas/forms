# Single Product W Single Choice Orders - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for single product orders with a single choice selection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the product you are ordering in the "Product Name" field.
3. Specify the quantity of the product you would like to order.
4. Select your single choice from the available options in the "Single Choice" field.
5. Confirm your selection by selecting all applicable options in the "Confirm" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (customer_name, text, optional):** Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Product Name (product_name, text, optional):** Enter the name of the product you are ordering.
* **Quantity (quantity, number, optional):** Enter the number of items you would like to order.
* **Single Choice (single_choice, select_one, optional):** Select one of the available options for your order.
* **Confirm (confirm, select_multiple, optional):** Select all applicable options to confirm your order.
  - **Option 1, Option 2, Option 3 (select_multiple, optional):** Select all applicable options to confirm your order.
  
## Tips
- Please ensure you enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
- If you have any issues or questions while completing this form, contact your administrator or support team.
