# white_label_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect business information and terms from partners, enabling businesses to establish a mutually beneficial agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form data (optional).
* Provide your business name (partner name).
* Select a date for the agreement (Agreement Date).
* Read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (Form Data, text, optional): Enter any additional business information you'd like to share.
* **Partner Name** (Partner Name, text, optional): Provide the name of your business or organization.
* **Agreement Date** (Agreement Date, date, optional): Choose a date for the agreement, if applicable.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Terms and Conditions, note, optional): Read through and agree to the terms and conditions of the agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Ensure that your partner's name is spelled correctly.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before agreeing to them.
* Use this form for establishing mutually beneficial partnerships or agreements.
