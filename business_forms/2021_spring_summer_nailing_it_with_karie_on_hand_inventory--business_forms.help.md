# 2021 Spring/Summer Nailing It With Karie On Hand Inventory - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from Karie On Hand distributors on their experience with the 2021 Spring/Summer collection. It will collect information on their current stock levels, preferred catalog formats, shipping addresses, and overall satisfaction with the product. This data will help us better understand their needs and improve future product offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly, as it will help us better understand your needs.
2. Make sure to select all relevant options for each field.
3. Enter only whole numbers for the "Quantity to reorder" field.
4. Use the provided options for the "Which aspects applied to your experience with 2021 Spring/Summer Nailing It With Karie On Hand Inventory?" field, if none apply, leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (Distributor Name): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email address** (Email address): Enter your business email for order confirmations.
* **Phone number** (Phone number): Enter your contact number for order inquiries.
* **Date** (Date): Select the date for order confirmations.
* **Current stock level** (Current stock level for Spring/Summer collection): Select the current stock level for the Spring/Summer collection.
* **Which Spring/Summer shades are selling fastest?** (Select all): Select all selling shades from the list.
* **Quantity to reorder** (Quantity to reorder (sets)): Enter the quantity to reorder.
* **Budget allocated** (Budget allocated for this order ($)): Enter the total amount available for purchase.
* **Preferred catalog format** (Preferred catalog format): Select the preferred catalog format.
* **Marketing materials needed** (Marketing materials needed (select all)): Select all marketing materials needed.
* **Shipping address** (Shipping address): Enter the address where the inventory should be delivered.
* **Expected delivery date** (Expected delivery date): Select the expected delivery date.
* **How satisfied** (How satisfied are you with 2021 Spring/Summer Nailing It With Karie On Hand Inventory?)): Select your level of satisfaction with the product.
* **Overall rating** (Overall rating (1-10)): Enter a rating from 1 to 10.
* **Which aspects applied to your experience with 2021 Spring/Summer Nailing It With Karie On Hand Inventory?** (Select all): Select all applicable aspects of your experience.
* **Open feedback** (What would you improve or change about 2021 Spring/Summer Nailing It With Karie On Hand Inventory?): Enter any open feedback about the product.
* **Additional comments** (Additional comments or notes): Enter any other information about your inventory needs.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional comments" field.
* If you have any marketing materials requirements, please select all applicable options in the "Marketing materials needed" field.
