# LVCC Boarding Contract - Help Guide

## Purpose
The LVCC Boarding Contract form is used to provide essential information about a pet's boarding stay, allowing us to provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information for each field.
2. Make sure to review each field before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Boarding Form**: This is the title of the form, which should be left blank.
* **Pet Name**: Enter the name of the pet you are boarding.
* **Pet Type**: Select the type of pet you are boarding (Dog, Cat, or Other).
* **Pet Breed**: Select the breed of the pet (Poodle, Siamese, or Other).
* **Owner Name**: Enter the owner's name for the pet.
* **Contact Info**: Provide any relevant contact information for the pet's owner.
* **Phone**: Enter the pet's owner's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the pet's owner's email address.
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the date the pet will arrive at the boarding facility.
* **Departure Date**: Enter the date the pet will leave the boarding facility.
* **Special Request**: If there are any special requests or instructions for the pet's care, enter them here.
* **Payment**: Select how payment for the pet's stay will be made (Pay on Arrival, Pay on Departure, or Other).
* **Signature**: Sign the contract to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the pet's stay.
* **Boarding Contract Terms**: Review and acknowledge the boarding contract terms.
* **Acknowledgement**: Sign to acknowledge that you have reviewed and understand the terms.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date you signed the contract.
* **Contract Date**: Enter the date the contract was created.
* **Boarding Contract Number**: Enter a unique identification number for the boarding contract.
* **Pet Medical Info**: Add any relevant medical information about the pet, if applicable.

## Tips
- Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure accurate submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- The form should take a few minutes to complete; take your time and be thorough.
