# Society Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Society Declaration Form is used to provide essential information about a society or organization to the relevant authorities. This form helps in verifying and processing the declaration of a society for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about your society handy.
2. Fill out the required and optional fields as per your knowledge and availability.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Society Details** (`society_details`): This field is used to enter a brief description or purpose of the society.
* **Authorized Confirmation** (`authorized_confirmation`): This field is used for authorized personnel to confirm the declaration of the society.
* **Society Type** (`society_type`): This field is used to select the type of society (Business, Non-Profit, or Non-Profit/Charitable).
* **Society Status** (`society_status`): This field is used to select the status of the society (Active, Inactive, or Dissolved).
* **Society Reg. No** (`society_reg_no`): This field is used to enter the registration number of the society.
* **Society Name** (`society_name`): This field is used to enter the name of the society.
* **Authorized Name** (`authorized_name`): This field is used to enter the name of the authorized personnel.
* **Email** (`email`): This field is used to enter the email address of the authorized personnel.
* **Phone** (`phone`): This field is used to enter the phone number of the authorized personnel.
* **Address** (`address`): This field is used to enter the address of the authorized personnel.

## Tips
Please note that this form is for internal purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for actual legal documents or procedures required for registering a business or organization. Ensure that you have the necessary information and approvals before filling out this form.
