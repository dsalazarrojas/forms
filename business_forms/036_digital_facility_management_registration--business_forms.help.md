# Digital Facility Management Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about digital facilities for management and tracking purposes. It helps create a central database of registered facilities, which can be referenced and updated later.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter the site address in the "Site Address" field.
3. Enter the site name in the "Site Name" field.
4. Specify the service needs for the facility in the "Service Needs" field. You can choose one or multiple options from the dropdown list.
5. Enter any additional details about the site in the "Site Details" field.
6. Select the site location from the dropdown list in the "Site Location" field.
7. Enter a brief description of the site in the "Site Description" field.
8. Enter the name of the facility manager in the "Facility Manager Name" field.
9. Enter the phone number of the facility manager in the "Facility Manager Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Address** (`site_address`, text, optional): Enter the physical address of the facility.
* **Site Name** (`site_name`, text, optional): Enter a name for the facility that will be used for reference.
* **Service Needs** (`service_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Specify the services offered by the facility. You can choose one or multiple options from the dropdown list.
* **Site Details** (`site_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the site that is not covered in other fields.
* **Site Location** (`site_location`, select_one, required): Select the location of the site from the dropdown list.
* **Site Description** (`site_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the facility.
* **Facility Manager Name** (`facility_manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person managing the facility.
* **Facility Manager Phone** (`facility_manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the facility manager.
