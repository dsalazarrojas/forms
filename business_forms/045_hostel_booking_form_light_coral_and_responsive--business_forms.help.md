# Hostel Booking Form Light Coral And Responsive - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help you book a hostel room quickly and efficiently. It includes fields for your personal details, preferred room type, and other essential information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the required fields to the best of your abilities.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Submit your completed form for review and confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_1** (text): Please enter your name.
* **form_2** (select_one): Select your preferred room type.
	+ Option 1: Double bed
	+ Option 2: Single bed
	+ Option 3: Shared room
* **form_3** (select_multiple): Select your preferred amenities. You can choose multiple options.
	+ Option 4: Wi-Fi
	+ Option 5: TV
* **form_4** (date): Please enter your check-in date (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **form_5** (time): Please enter your check-in time (24-hour format, e.g., 14:00).
* **form_6** (number): Please enter the number of guests in your group.
* **form_7** (number): Please enter the number of rooms you would like to book.
* **form_8** (note): Please add any special notes or requests (e.g., for a specific room location).
* **form_9** (email): Please enter your valid email address.
* **form_10** (text): Please enter your contact number (including country code).
* **form_11** (text): Please enter your preferred arrival method (e.g., car, walk, etc.).
* **form_12** (text): Please enter your preferred departure method (e.g., car, walk, etc.).
* **form_13** (text): Please enter any special requirements (e.g., extra bed, etc.).
* **form_14** (text): Please enter any other comments or requests.
* **form_15** (text): Please confirm your email address again for verification purposes.
