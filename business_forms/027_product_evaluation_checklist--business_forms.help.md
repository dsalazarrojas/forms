# Product Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Evaluation Checklist is a form used to gather information about a product's evaluation, essential for making informed decisions in a business setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the product and determine whether it meets the required standards (Product Labeling).
2. Provide specific details about the product's performance, features, or issues encountered (Evaluation Details).
3. Input the weights or scores for the product's evaluation (Weights).
4. Record the date and time of the evaluation (Evaluation Date, Evaluation Time).
5. Add any additional comments or observations about the product's evaluation (Evaluation Comment).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Labeling**: Determine if the product meets the required standards by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Evaluation Details**: Provide a brief description of your specific thoughts on the product's performance.
* **Weights**: Input the weight or score for the product's evaluation, if applicable.
* **Evaluation Date**: Select the date when the evaluation took place.
* **Evaluation Time**: Select the time spent on the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Comment**: Add any additional comments or observations about the product's evaluation.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field thoroughly and accurately to ensure the evaluation is informative and helpful.
- Use the weights field for priority or scoring purposes only if applicable.
- Ensure that all fields are completed before submitting the form, as this will help maintain the evaluation's credibility.
