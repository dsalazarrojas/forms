# Laptop Computer Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laptop Computer Agreement form is used to record and agree upon the terms of a laptop computer loaned or issued to an employee.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Details in the "Employee Details" field.
2. Provide the laptop's Hardware Specifications in the "Hardware Specifications" field.
3. Review and agree to the terms in the "Agreement Terms" field (note: this field is not editable but is for the IT representative to acknowledge their review of the terms).
4. Sign the form to confirm your acceptance of the agreement terms as an Employee and as the designated signatory.
5. The IT representative will review and sign the form as "IT" to acknowledge their acceptance of the agreement terms.
6. Record the Date Received and Date Returned fields, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details (Employee Details)** (`employee_details`, text, optional): Enter your employee information, including your name, ID number, and contact details.
* **Hardware Specifications (Hardware Specifications)** (`hardware_specifications`, text, optional): Describe the laptop's specifications, including make, model, serial number, and any other relevant details.
* **Agreement Terms (Agreement Terms)** (`agreement_terms`, note, optional): Review and agree to the terms of the laptop computer loan, which includes usage, maintenance, and return procedures.
* **Employee Signature (Employee Signature)** (`employee_signature`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are an Employee, Supervisor, or IT representative, and sign to confirm your acceptance of the agreement terms.
* **IT Signature (IT Signature)** (`it_signature`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are Manager, IT, or Supervisor, and sign to confirm your acceptance of the agreement terms.
* **Date Received (Date Received)** (`date_received`, date, optional): Record the date you received the laptop computer, if applicable.
* **Date Returned (Date Returned)** (`date_returned`, date, optional): Record the date you returned the laptop computer, if applicable.
* **Employee Phone (Employee Phone)** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter your employee phone number.
* **Manager Name (Manager Name)** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager.
* **IT Name (IT Name)** (`it_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the IT representative.
* **Supervisor Name (Supervisor Name)** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Department (Department)** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Asset Tag (Asset Tag)** (`asset_tag`, text, optional): Enter the asset tag number of the laptop computer.
* **Laptop Make (Laptop Make)** (`laptop_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of the laptop computer.
* **Laptop Model (Laptop Model)** (`laptop_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the laptop computer.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have read and understood the terms of the laptop computer loan before filling out this form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that you have entered all required information.
* Sign the form as the designated signatory to confirm your acceptance of the agreement terms.
