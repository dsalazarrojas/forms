# Meeting Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of meetings within the organization. The goal is to assess how well meetings achieve their intended outcomes and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the `meeting_id` to identify the specific meeting being surveyed.
2.  Enter your `attendee_id` if you were an attendee of the meeting.
3.  Select the `Effectiveness Scale` that best describes how effective you found the meeting to be.
4.  Choose how frequently you usually meet with the same group of people (e.g., `Daily`, `Weekly`, `Monthly`, etc.).
5.  If you want to add more details about your experience, use the `Effectiveness Scale Comment` field.
6.  Answer whether you plan to meet with the same group again (e.g., `True`, `False`, or `Maybe`).
7.  In the `Meeting Notes` field, you can include any additional comments or observations about the meeting.
8.  Finally, use the `Comments` field for any general feedback or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Meeting Effectiveness Survey**: This is the main title of the form.
*   **meeting_id**: Enter the unique ID assigned to the meeting being surveyed.
*   **attendee_id**: Enter your ID if you were an attendee of the meeting.
*   **Effectiveness Scale**: Select one of the following options to rate the meeting's effectiveness:
    *   Somewhat
    *   Very
    *   Not At All
*   **Meeting Frequency**: Choose how frequently you usually meet with the same group:
    *   Daily
    *   Weekly
    *   Monthly
    *   Quarterly
    *   Annually
*   **Effectiveness Scale Comment**: Provide more details about your experience with the meeting, if needed.
*   **Will Meet Again**: Answer whether you plan to meet with the same group again:
    *   True
    *   False
    *   Maybe
*   **Meeting Notes**: Enter any additional comments about the meeting.
*   **Comments**: Provide any general feedback or suggestions about the form or the meeting process.
