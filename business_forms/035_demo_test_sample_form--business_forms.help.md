# demo_test_sample_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect various types of data from users for business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Provide your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
4. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
5. Enter your company name in the `company` field.
6. Provide your address in the `address` field.
7. Enter your city in the `city` field.
8. Select your state from the drop-down menu in the `state` field.
9. Enter your zip code in the `zip` field.
10. Enter any additional comments in the `comments` field.
11. Select the date of your event in the `date` field.
12. Select the time of your event in the `time` field.
13. Choose the product you are interested in from the drop-down menu in the `product` field.
14. Enter the version of the product you are using in the `version` field.
15. Select the status of your request in the `status` field.
16. Enter your priority level in the `priority` field.
17. Choose the language of your request in the `language` field.
18. Select your time zone in the `timezone` field.
19. Enter any additional notes in the `note` field.
20. Upload any relevant attachments in the `attachment` field.
21. Enter your geographical location in the `geo_location` field.
22. Enter your latitude in the `geo_latitude` field.
23. Enter your longitude in the `geo_longitude` field.
24. Enter your altitude in the `geo_altitude` field.
25. Select your time zone (optional) in the `geo_timezone` field.
