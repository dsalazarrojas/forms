# Molecule Design Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Molecule Design Submission Form is a tool used to collect essential details from users about their molecule designs. It helps facilitate the submission and processing of these designs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Submission Info section with your details.
2.  Add the molecular structures related to the submission.
3.  Enter the context of the submission.
4.  Add any relevant files for the submission.
5.  Enter your email address.
6.  Provide your phone number.
7.  Enter the date of the submission.
8.  Enter the time of the submission.
9.  Add any additional note about the submission.
10.  Select one option from the Select One field.
11.  Select multiple options from the Select Multiple field.
12.  Enter a decimal value.
13.  Enter an integer value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Info** (`submitter_info`, text, optional): This field is used to provide additional information about the submission.
* **Molecular Structures** (`molecular_structures`, text, optional): Enter the molecular structures related to the submission.
* **Context** (`context`, text, optional): Enter the context of the submission.
* **Submitter Files** (`submitter_files`, text, optional): Add any relevant files for the submission.
* **Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. Format should be in the standard email format (username@domain.com).
* **Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`submitter_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the submission.
* **Time** (`submitter_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the submission.
* **Note** (`submitter_note`, note, optional): Add any additional note about the submission.
* **Select One** (`submitter_select_one`, select_one, optional): Select one option from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Multiple** (`submitter_select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the dropdown menu.
* **Decimal** (`submitter_decimal`, number, optional): Enter a decimal value.
* **Integer** (`submitter_integer`, number, optional): Enter an integer value.
