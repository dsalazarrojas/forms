# Field Notes Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Notes Form is used to record notes and observations related to a specific field or location. This form is a quick and easy way to gather information and keep track of notes for reference and review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: "Field Date" and "Field Location".
2. Enter the "Field Time" if necessary.
3. Choose the relevant "Tags" from the dropdown list (if applicable).
4. Enter your notes and additional notes in the "Notes" and "Additional Notes" fields, respectively.
5. Click submit to save your entries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Field Date** (`field_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the relevant field or location. This is a required field to help keep track of when the notes were taken.
* **Field Time** (`field_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of day the notes were taken. This field is optional but helps with time stamping the notes.
* **Field Location** (`field_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the name or description of the field or location. This field is useful for identification and searching.
* **Tags** (`field_tags`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one or more tags to categorize the notes (if applicable). The available options are "Yes" and "No".
* **Notes** (`field_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter your notes and observations about the field or location. This is a free-text field for you to type in your thoughts.
* **Additional Notes** (`field_notes_2`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or information that may be relevant to the field or location. This field is meant to provide extra space for more detailed notes.
* **Tags** (`field_tags`, select_one, required/optional): Enter any additional tags that may be relevant to the field or location. This field is meant to be used sparingly and only if additional tags are necessary.
