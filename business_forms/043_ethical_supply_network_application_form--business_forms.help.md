# Ethical supply network application form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your organization and its supply chain practices. The goal is to assess the level of transparency and integrity in your business operations. Please provide detailed and accurate information about your company, including its description, address, and contact details. Additionally, please specify your supply chain practices, such as certifications and sourcing methods. This form is an essential part of the ethical supply network application process and will help us understand your business needs and operations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Be as detailed as possible when answering the questions about your business practices and policies.
3. Select relevant certifications and industry categories to best represent your business operations.
4. Ensure accurate and up-to-date contact and business details.
5. Review the terms and conditions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Title** (`application_title`, Text, Required): Enter a brief title that describes the purpose of your application.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text, Required): Provide your organization's name and surname.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, Text, Required): Enter your organization's full name.
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, Text, Required): Enter your organization's physical address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Required): Enter a phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, Text, Required): Describe your business operations and practices.
* **Supply Chain Transparency** (`supply_chain_transparency`, Text, Required): Explain how you maintain transparency in your supply chain.
* **Sourcing Practices** (`sourcing_practices`, Text, Required): Describe your sourcing methods and processes.
* **Supply Chain Certifications** (`supply_chain_certifications`, Select Multiple, Required): Select any relevant certifications your organization holds.
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, Select One, Required): Choose the status of your application (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional information about your application.
* **Uploaded Documents** (`uploaded_documents`, Note, Optional): Attach any relevant documents to support your application.
* **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`, Select Multiple, Required): Review and agree to our terms of service.
* **Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy`, Select Multiple, Required): Review and agree to our privacy policy.
* **Terms of Service Agreement** (`terms_of_service_agreement`, Select One, Required): Select the term you agree to.
* **Privacy Policy Agreement** (`privacy_policy_agreement`, Select One, Required): Select the policy you agree to.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, Text, Required): Enter a brief description of your business type.
* **Business Size** (`business_size`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the size of your business (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Location** (`location`, Text, Required): Enter your organization's location.
* **Business Industry** (`business_industry`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the industry your business operates in.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of your application.
* **Application Time** (`application_time`, Time, Required): Enter the time of your application.
* **Uploaded File** (`uploaded_file`, Note, Optional): Attach any relevant files to support your application.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, Select One, Required): Choose who submitted the application (User 1, User 2, User 3).

Please review and submit this form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed accurately.
