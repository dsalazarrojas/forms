# Sustainable Logistics Council Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Logistics Council Registration Form is designed to gather information about our members to provide them with relevant resources and support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Company Name in the "Company Name" field.
6. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
7. Select your Role from the options provided in the "Role" field.
8. Enter your Department in the "Department" field (if applicable).
9. Enter your Division in the "Division" field (if applicable).
10. Select your Country from the options provided in the "Country" field.
11. Enter your Industry in the "Industry" field (if applicable).
12. Enter your Company Type in the "Company Type" field (if applicable).
13. Enter your Company Size in the "Company Size" field (if applicable).
14. Enter your Website in the "Website" field (if applicable).
15. Enter your Twitter handle in the "Twitter" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name, which we'll use to address you in our communications.
* **Last Name**: Your last name, which we'll use to personalize our content.
* **Email**: A valid email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone**: Your phone number, which we'll use to contact you in case of urgent matters.
* **Company Name**: The name of your company, which helps us understand your affiliation.
* **Address**: Your work address or the address where you'd like to receive mail.
* **Role**: Select "Member" to indicate you're an active participant in our community, or "Non-Member" if you're not currently involved.
* **Department**: The department you work in (if applicable).
* **Division**: The division you work in (if applicable).
* **Country**: The country where you're based.
* **Industry**: The industry you work in (if applicable).
* **Company Type**: The type of company you work for (if applicable).
* **Company Size**: The size of your company (if applicable).
* **Website**: Your company's website (if applicable).
* **Twitter**: Your Twitter handle (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure we can provide you with the right resources.
* If you're a member of a team or organization, please register yourself as an individual.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can contact our support team for guidance.
