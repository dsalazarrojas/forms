# Investment Opportunity Evaluation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you provide a complete and accurate evaluation of an investment opportunity. It captures essential information about the company, its financial needs, and its risk level. This form will help our team assess the potential of the investment and identify areas that require further investigation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General Information section with the basic details about the company, such as its name, type, and contact information.
2. Provide a brief description of the company, its products or services, and its business model.
3. Specify the company's funding needs, its risk level, and provide supporting documents that validate its claims.
4. For each section, ensure you provide accurate and concise information that reflects the company's current situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`General Information`): This section provides a basic introduction to the company, including its name, type, and contact information. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter the name of the company as it appears on its official documents or website.
* **Company Type** (`company_type_1`): Select the type of company, such as Private Limited or Publicly Traded.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person_1`): Enter the name of the person responsible for the investment opportunity.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address_1`): Enter the address of the company's headquarters or main office.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`): Enter the URL of the company's official website.
* **Company Description** (`company_description_1`): Provide a brief description of the company, its products or services, and its business model.
* **Funding Needs** (`funding_needs_1`): Specify the amount of funding the company requires.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level_1`): Select the risk level of the investment opportunity, such as low, medium, or high.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents_1`): Upload any supporting documents that validate the company's claims.
* **Company Revenue** (`company_revenue_1`): Enter the current revenue of the company.
* **Company Location** (`company_location_1`): Enter the location of the company's headquarters or main office.
* **Industry** (`industry_1`): Enter the industry the company operates in.
* **Company Description 2** (`company_description_2`): Provide a more detailed description of the company, its products or services, and its business model.
* **Funding Needs 2** (`funding_needs_2`): Specify the amount of funding the company requires.
* **Risk Level 2** (`risk_level_2`): Select the risk level of the investment opportunity, such as low, medium, or high.
* **Supporting Documents 2** (`supporting_documents_2`): Upload any additional supporting documents that validate the company's claims.
* **Financial Projections** (`financial_projections_1`): Enter any financial projections or forecasts for the company.
* **Team Structure** (`team_structure_1`): Describe the company's team structure and key personnel.
* **Company Ownership** (`company_ownership_1`): Specify the ownership structure of the company.
