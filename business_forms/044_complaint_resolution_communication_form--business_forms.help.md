# Complaint Resolution Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the resolution of customer complaints through effective communication. It ensures that the necessary information is collected and documented for accurate and prompt issue resolution, improving the customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the unique Complaint Case ID to reference the issue at hand.
2. Provide the customer's name who will receive the communication.
3. Select the type of communication: Status Update (No action required), Request for Additional Information, Final Resolution Offer, or Apology and Acknowledgment.
4. Write a detailed message for the customer.
5. If applicable, outline the next steps for the customer to take.
6. Enter the expected timeframe for the final resolution.
7. Rate the clarity of the communication to help improve service.
8. Choose the preferred method for future updates on this case.
9. Enter the name of the representative communicating with the customer.
10. Date and time of the communication.
11. Optionally, log this communication in the CRM for internal tracking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Case ID** (`case_reference_id_comm`, text): This field is used to uniquely identify the customer's case. Enter the relevant ID to ensure accurate tracking and reference.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name_comm`, text): Provide the name of the person receiving this communication for personalized interaction.
* **Type of Communication** (`communication_type_comm`, select_one): Select the type of communication: Status Update (No action required), Request for Additional Information, Final Resolution Offer, or Apology and Acknowledgment.
* **Message Content** (`communication_message_body`, text): Describe the details of the communication in a clear and concise manner to ensure the customer understands the update.
* **Next Steps for the Customer** (`next_steps_for_customer_text`, text): If applicable, list the next actions required of the customer to advance the case.
* **Expected Timeframe for Final Resolution** (`expected_timeframe_resolution`, text): Enter the time frame for the final resolution, such as 48 hours or 5 business days.
* **Rate the Clarity of this Communication** (`communication_quality_feedback_ask`, select_one): Help us improve by rating the clarity of the communication, from Very Clear to Confusing.
* **Preferred Future Contact Method** (`preferred_future_contact_method`, select_one): Choose how you would like to receive future updates, such as via Email, Phone Call, or no further updates needed.
* **Representative Name** (`rep_name_communicating`, text): Enter the name of the representative communicating with the customer.
* **Date of Communication** (`communication_timestamp_date`, date): Enter the current date and time of the communication.
* **Internal Copy - This communication has been logged in the CRM** (`internal_copy_notification`, note): This field is for internal tracking purposes; it's optional and not visible to the customer.

## Tips
* Ensure accurate and clear communication to prevent misinterpretation and confusion.
* Double-check the customer's details before sending the communication.
* Rate the clarity of the communication to help us improve our service.
* Choose the best contact method for future updates to avoid unnecessary contact attempts.
* This form is designed to be completed with the customer in mind, so please be respectful and professional in your communication.
