# Client Stories Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and testimonials from clients who have used our services. We want to hear about their experience, what we did well or poorly, and how we helped them achieve their goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Share Your Success Story**: This is the introduction section where you can share a brief overview of your experience with our services.
2. **Your Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or business cards.
3. **Company or Organization**: If you represent a company or organization, please enter it here.
4. **Your Title or Role**: If applicable, please enter your title or role within your organization.
5. **Email Address**: Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
6. **Phone Number**: This is an optional field for you to enter your phone number.
7. **How Long Have You Been a Client?**: Please select the time frame that best represents how long you've been working with us.
8. **Initial Challenge or Problem**: Describe the challenge or problem you faced when you first came to us.
9. **Why Did You Choose Us?**: Tell us what attracted you to our services or what made you decide to work with us.
10. **Solution and Results Achieved**: Describe how we helped you and what results you achieved through our services.
11. **Measurable Business Outcomes**: If possible, please provide specific numbers, percentages, or metrics that demonstrate the impact of our services on your business.
12. **Most Positive Impact**: Describe the most significant positive impact we've had on your business.
13. **Best Aspects of Working With Us**: Tell us about the best parts of our services or how we did well.
14. **Would You Recommend Us?**: Let us know if you would recommend us to others.
15. **Testimonial or Quote**: If you'd like to share a brief quote about your experience with us.
16. **May We Use Your Photo for Marketing?**: If you're willing, please grant us permission to use your photo for marketing purposes.
17. **May We Use Your Story as a Case Study?**: If you're willing, please grant us permission to use your story as a case study.
18. **LinkedIn Profile URL**: If you have a LinkedIn profile, please share it with us for verification.
19. **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them with us here.

* **client_intro**: A brief overview of your experience with our services.
* **client_name**: Your name as it appears on your ID or business cards.
* **client_company**: The name of the company or organization you represent.
* **client_title**: Your title or role within your organization.
* **client_email**: A valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **client_phone**: Your phone number, optional.
* **engagement_length**: The time frame of your engagement with us.
* **initial_challenge**: Describe the challenge or problem you faced when you first came to us.

* **solution_results**: Describe how we helped you and what results you achieved through our services.
* **measurable_outcomes**: Specific numbers, percentages, or metrics that demonstrate the impact of our services on your business.
* **positive_impact**: Describe the most significant positive impact we've had on your business.
* **best_aspects**: Tell us about the best parts of our services or how we did well.
* **would_recommend**: Let us know if you would recommend us to others.
* **testimonial_quote**: A brief quote about your experience with us.
* **photo_permission**: Grant us permission to use your photo for marketing purposes.
* **case_study_permission**: Grant us permission to use your story as a case study.
* **linkedin_profile**: Share your LinkedIn profile URL with us for verification.
* **additional_comments**: Additional comments or feedback you'd like to share with us.
