# Worker Productivity Analysis Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for the Worker Productivity Analysis Program. It helps us understand participant goals, consent, and session details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (denoted by *Required*).
2. Enter participant details, goals, and consent information.
3. Provide session details, including participant ID, session date, start time, and duration.
4. Choose participant status (Active or Inactive).
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant participant information.
* **Goals**: Describe your goals and objectives for participating in the program.
* **Consent**: Confirm your understanding and agreement with the program terms and conditions.
* **Participant ID**: Enter a unique participant ID (number) to identify you in the program.
* **Session Details**: Provide the date, start time, and duration of your session.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Session Date**: Select the date of your session.
* **Session Start Time**: Select the start time of your session.
* **Session Duration**: Enter the duration of your session.
* **Participant Status**: Choose whether you are Active or Inactive in the program.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form carefully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your manager or program administrator.
* Review your information before submitting the form to ensure its accuracy.
* If you have already participated in the program, please do not submit this form again.
