# fee_retention_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fee_retention_record_form is used to record and document fees collected and retained by the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the reason for the retention in the "reason" field.
2. Enter the amount of the fee collected in the "amount" field.
3. Select the date the fee was received in the "date_received" field.
4. Enter the name of the person authorized by the company to collect and retain the fee in the "authorized_by" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### reason
* **Reason** (`reason`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the reason for retaining the fee.

### amount
* **Amount** (`amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount of the fee collected.

### date_received
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the fee was received.

### authorized_by
* **Authorized By** (`authorized_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person authorized by the company to collect and retain the fee.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in each field to ensure the form is filled out correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your company's policies and procedures for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required information is included.
