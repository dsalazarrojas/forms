# new_employee_email_creation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help employees create or modify their email addresses within the organization's system. It is intended for administrative tasks such as setting up or editing email accounts for new hires or existing employees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling in the employee details in the "employee_details" page.
2. Move to the "email_details" page and select the required options for your email configuration.
3. On the "email_domain" page, choose the correct email domain from the available options.
4. Enter the email address in the "email_address" field.
5. Confirm your email address by selecting "Yes" in the "confirm_email" field.
6. If necessary, review any errors or messages displayed in the "confirm_email_error", "save_all_error", or "cancel_all_error" fields.
7. Finally, submit your changes by selecting "Yes" in the "submit" field or cancel the changes by selecting "Yes" in the "cancel" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_details** (`title: employee_details`, `label: employee_details`): Enter the employee's details, such as name, ID, or other relevant information.
* **email_details** (`title: email_details`, `label: email_details`): Select the email configuration options, such as "Yes" or "No" for each option.
* **job_title** (`title: job_title`, `label: job_title`): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the job title of the employee.
* **email_domain** (`title: email_domain`, `label: email_domain`): Choose the correct email domain from the available options.
* **email_address** (`title: email`, `label: email`): Enter the email address to be created or modified.
* **confirm_email** (`title: confirm_email`, `label: confirm_email`): Confirm that the email address is correct by selecting "Yes".
* **error_email** (`title: error_email`, `label: error_email`): Review any errors or messages displayed for email configuration issues.
* **submit** (`title: submit`, `label: submit`): Select "Yes" to submit the changes.
* **cancel** (`title: cancel`, `label: cancel`): Select "Yes" to cancel the changes.
* **back** (`title: back`, `label: back`): Review previous pages or steps.
* **save** (`title: save`, `label: save`): Select "Yes" to save the changes.
* **cancel_all** (`title: cancel_all`, `label: cancel_all`): Select "Yes" to cancel all changes.
* **save_all** (`title: save_all`, `label: save_all`): Select "Yes" to save all changes.
* **cancel_all_error** (`title: cancel_all_error`, `label: cancel_all_error`): Review any error messages for cancellation.
* **save_all_error** (`title: save_all_error`, `label: save_all_error`): Review any error messages for saving changes.
* **save_all_success** (`title: save_all_success`, `label: save_all_success`): Displayed when all changes are saved successfully.
* **save_all_hint** (`title: save_all_hint`, `label: save_all_hint`): Additional hint for saving changes.
* **save_all_message** (`title: save_all_message`, `label: save_all_message`): Displayed when saving all changes successfully.

## Tips

* Be sure to review the "error_email" field for any issues with the email configuration.
* Double-check your email address in the "email_address" field before submitting changes.
* If you encounter any errors, review the "save_all_error" and "cancel_all_error" fields for more information.
