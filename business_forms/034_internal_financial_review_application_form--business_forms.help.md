# Internal Financial Review Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Internal Financial Review Application Form is designed for internal use within the organization, enabling employees and management to review financial information and make informed decisions. It is essential to complete this form accurately and consistently, ensuring that all relevant details are captured and considered for the review process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the Employee**: Choose the relevant employee from the list of options provided in the "Employee Details" field.
2. **Specify the Review Type**: Select the type of review required (e.g., Financial Review or Operational Review) using the options in the "Review Request Type" field.
3. **Provide Date and Time Information**: Enter or select the date and time of the review using the "Date of Review" and "Time of Review" fields.
4. **Submit Comments and Approval**: Enter any relevant comments in the "Review Comments" field and select the "Employee Approval" option (Approve or Deny) to indicate the approval status.
5. **Submit the Form**: Complete all required fields and click on the "Submit" button to submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Internal Financial Review Form** (`internal_financial_review_form` , text, optional): This is the main title of the form, which should remain unchanged.
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details` , select_one, optional): Select the relevant employee from the list of options provided. This field helps ensure that the review is focused on the correct person or entity.
* **Review Request Type** (`review_request_type` , select_one, optional): Choose the type of review required, such as Financial Review or Operational Review. This field helps categorize and prioritize reviews.
* **Date of Review** (`date_of_review` , date, optional): Enter the date of the review in the format "YYYY-MM-DD." This field is essential for tracking review timelines.
* **Time of Review** (`time_of_review` , time, optional): Enter the time of the review in the format "HH:MM:SS." This field helps ensure that reviews are conducted efficiently and effectively.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments` , text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information relevant to the review. This field is useful for providing context and insights.
* **Employee Approval** (`employee_approval` , select_one, optional): Select "Approve" or "Deny" to indicate the approval status of the review. This field finalizes the review process.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted` , date, optional): The system will automatically generate this field with the date the form was submitted. This field is for reference and monitoring purposes only.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by` , text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form. This field is for record-keeping and accountability.
* **Department Reviewed** (`department_reviewed` , select_one, optional): Select the department responsible for the review, such as Finance or HR. This field helps track departmental responsibilities.
