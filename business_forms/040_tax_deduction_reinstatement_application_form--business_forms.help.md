This is a form used to help employees or individuals request to reinstate their tax deductions that were previously disallowed. This form is intended for internal use within an organization and is not designed for external use. 

The form collects personal and financial information, as well as details about tax returns and supporting documents, to facilitate the reinstatement process. It also includes fields for declarations, submissions, and reviews to ensure accuracy and approval.

# Tax Deduction Reinstatement Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the reinstatement of tax deductions that were previously disallowed. It is intended for internal use within an organization and requires completion by authorized personnel.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details in the **Personal Details** field.
2. Provide information about your previous tax return in the **Tax Return** field.
3. Attach or describe your financial statements in the **Financial Statements** field.
4. Specify the tax deduction details in the **Tax Deduction Details** field.
5. Attach or describe any supporting documents in the **Supporting Documents** field.
6. Complete the **Declaration Details** field with any relevant information about your declaration.
7. Submit your form in the **Submission** field.
8. Review and confirm your form in the **Review and Confirmation** field.
9. Ensure your form is reviewed and validated in the **Review and Validation** field.
10. Sign your form in the **Signature** field, indicating your approval.
11. Confirm and review your form again in the **Review and Confirmation 2** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (ID 1, type: text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to collect your personal details, such as name, address, and contact information.
* **Tax Return** (ID 2, type: text, required: false)
	+ Enter the relevant information about your previous tax return, including any necessary details.
* **Financial Statements** (ID 3, type: text, required: false)
	+ Attach or describe your financial statements, such as bank statements or proof of income.
* **Tax Deduction Details** (ID 4, type: text, required: false)
	+ Specify the details about the tax deduction you are requesting, including the amount and reason.
* **Supporting Documents** (ID 5, type: text, required: false)
	+ Attach or describe any supporting documents, such as receipts or invoices, that back up your claim.
* **Declaration Details** (ID 6, type: text, required: false)
	+ Complete this field with any relevant information about your declaration, including any supporting statements.
* **Submission** (ID 7, type: text, required: false)
	+ Submit your form, making sure to include all necessary information.
* **Review and Confirmation** (ID 8, type: text, required: false)
	+ Review your form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* **Review and Validation** (ID 9, type: text, required: false)
	+ Ensure that your form has been reviewed and validated by the relevant authorities.
* **Signature** (ID 10, type: text, required: false)
	+ Sign your form, indicating your approval and understanding of the information provided.
* **Review and Approval** (ID 11, type: text, required: false)
	+ Review and confirm your form again before submitting it for approval.
* **Form Completion** (ID 12, type: text, required: false)
	+ Ensure that your form is complete and accurate before submitting it.
* **Review and Confirmation 2** (ID 13, type: text, required: false)
	+ Review your form one last time before submitting it for final approval.

Note: Some fields are currently optional, but it's best to fill in all fields for a smooth and accurate form submission process.
