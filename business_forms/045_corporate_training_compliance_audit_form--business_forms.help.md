# corporate-training-compliance-audit-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for corporate training compliance auditing. It's used to assess the compliance status of training programs implemented in an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Introduction: Enter a brief text overview of the training programs implemented.
2. Training Programs: Select the type of training program implemented (Compliance Program, Non-Compliance Program, or Other).
3. Training Methods: Choose all applicable methods used (Online Training, Classroom Training, On-the-job Training, or Other).
4. Training Materials: Enter the date of the training materials used.
5. Feedback: Select the feedback status of the training program (Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, or Other).
6. Date: Enter the date of the audit.
7. Time: Enter the time of the audit.
8. Auditor Signature: Select the auditor's signature status (Signed as above or Not signed).
9. Auditor Name: Enter the name of the auditor who conducted the audit.
10. Auditor Organization: Enter the name of the organization that conducted the audit.
11. Title: Enter the title of the auditor who conducted the audit.
12. Department: Enter the department of the auditor who conducted the audit.
13. Phone: Enter the phone number of the auditor who conducted the audit.
14. Email: Enter the email of the auditor who conducted the audit.
15. Approver Signature: Select the approver's signature status (Signed as above or Not signed).
16. Approver Name: Enter the name of the approver who approved the audit.
17. Approver Organization: Enter the name of the organization that approved the audit.
18. Approver Title: Enter the title of the approver who approved the audit.
19. Approver Department: Enter the department of the approver who approved the audit.
