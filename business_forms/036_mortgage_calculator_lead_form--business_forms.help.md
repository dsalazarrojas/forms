# mortgage_calculator_lead_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mortgage_calculator_lead_form is designed to collect information from potential homebuyers who are interested in obtaining a mortgage. The form helps us understand the buyer's needs and preferences, allowing us to provide personalized guidance and support throughout the lead process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your potential buyer information by selecting "Potential Buyer Information" and entering your First name, Last name, Email address, Phone number, and Company (if applicable).
2. Enter the loan amount you are interested in, down payment percentage, and interest rate.
3. Select the loan term you are considering.
4. Choose whether you would like to calculate your mortgage or not.
5. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Lead Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Potential Buyer Information (Select)** (`potential_buyer_info`, Select Multiple, optional): This field allows you to select which type of potential buyer information you would like to provide. You can select one or more of the following options: First name, Last name, Email address, Phone number, and Company.
* **Loan Amount (Text)** (`loan_amount`, Number, optional): Enter the loan amount you are interested in.
* **Down Payment Percentage (Text)** (`down_payment_percentage`, Number, optional): Enter the down payment percentage.
* **Interest Rate (Text)** (`interest_rate`, Number, optional): Enter the interest rate.
* **Loan Term (Text)** (`loan_term`, Number, optional): Enter the loan term.
* **Calculate Mortgage (Select)** (`calculate_mortgage`, Select One, optional): Select "Yes" if you would like to calculate your mortgage, and "No" if you do not want to calculate.
* **First Name (Text)** (`first_name`, Text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Text)** (`last_name`, Text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address (Email)** (`email`, Email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (Text)** (`phone`, Text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Company (Text)** (`company`, Text, optional): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Lead Status (Select)** (`lead_status`, Select One, optional): Select "New lead" if you are a new lead, or "Existing lead" if you are an existing lead.
* **Lead Source (Select)** (`lead_source`, Select One, optional): Select the source of your lead, such as "Social media", "Referral", or "Print ad".
* **Lead Comment (Text)** (`lead_comment`, Text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
