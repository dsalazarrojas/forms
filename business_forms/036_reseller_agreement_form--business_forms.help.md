# Reseller Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from resellers and owners regarding their agreement, terms, and other relevant details for business or sales purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out all required fields. Required fields are marked with an *.
- For select fields with multiple options, select one or more relevant options based on your agreement terms.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to your business or sales team for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reseller Information** (*reseller_information*, *text*, required: false): Enter the reseller's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Agreement Details** (*agreement_details*, *text*, required: false): Enter any relevant details about the agreement, such as its purpose and scope.
* **Reseller Terms** (*reseller_terms*, *text*, required: false): Enter any terms or conditions that apply to the reseller.
* **Agreement Terms** (*agreement_terms*, *text*, required: false): Enter any terms or conditions that apply to the agreement.
* **Reseller Name** (*reseller_name*, *select_multiple*, required: false): Select whether the reseller is a *Yes* or *No*.
* **Reseller Address** (*reseller_address*, *text*, required: false): Enter the reseller's address.
* **Owner Name** (*owner_name*, *select_one*, required: false): Select *Yes* or *No*.
* **Owner Address** (*owner_address*, *text*, required: false): Enter the owner's address.
* **Owner Phone** (*owner_phone*, *text*, required: false): Enter the owner's phone number.
* **Agreement Duration** (*agreement_duration*, *date*, required: false): Enter the agreement duration in date format.
* **Agreement Date** (*agreement_date*, *date*, required: false): Enter the agreement date in date format.
* **Reseller Email** (*reseller_email*, *email*, required: false): Enter the reseller's email address.
* **Owner Email** (*owner_email*, *email*, required: false): Enter the owner's email address.
* **Owner Company** (*owner_company*, *text*, required: false): Enter the owner's company name.
* **Reseller Company** (*reseller_company*, *text*, required: false): Enter the reseller's company name.
* **Agreement Price** (*agreement_price*, *number*, required: false): Enter the agreement price.
* **Reseller Tax ID** (*reseller_tax_id*, *select_multiple*, required: false): Select *Yes* or *No* for tax ID.
* **Owner Tax ID** (*owner_tax_id*, *select_multiple*, required: false): Select *Yes* or *No* for tax ID.
* **Owner License** (*owner_license*, *select_one*, required: false): Select *Yes* or *No*.
* **Reseller License** (*reseller_license*, *select_one*, required: false): Select *Yes* or *No*.
* **Agreement Notes** (*agreement_notes*, *note*, required: false): Enter any notes about the agreement.
* **Reseller Notes** (*reseller_notes*, *note*, required: false): Enter any notes about the reseller.
* **Agreement Status** (*agreement_status*, *select_one*, required: false): Select *Yes* or *No*.
* **Reseller Status** (*reseller_status*, *select_one*, required: false): Select *Active* or *Inactive*.
* **Submission Date** (*submission_date*, *date*, required: false): Enter the submission date in date format.
