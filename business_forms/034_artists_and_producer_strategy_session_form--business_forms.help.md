# Artists And Producer Strategy Session Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artists And Producer Strategy Session Form is a collection of information about the artist or band seeking a strategy session with a producer. This form helps the producer understand the artist's project, goals, and preferences for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your artist or band name.
2. Provide your contact email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Enter the name of the producer you are working with.
5. Enter their email address.
6. Select the music genre of your project.
7. Choose a preferred date for the session.
8. Select a preferred time for the session.
9. Choose the format for the session (In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid).
10. Enter the location or virtual platform where the session will take place.
11. Specify the duration of the session in hours.
12. Outline the topics to discuss during the session.
13. Provide an overview of your current project or album.
14. Share your goals for the session.
15. Specify if you have any producer input or feedback requested.
16. If applicable, enter the number of tracks under development.
17. Enter the project deadline or target release date.
18. Provide an expected production timeline.
19. If applicable, specify the budget available for the session.
20. Share any demos or materials to be reviewed.
21. Specify if you plan to schedule follow-up sessions.
22. Select the number of follow-up sessions needed.
23. Add any additional notes or special requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Band Name**: Enter the name of your artist or band.
* **Artist Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Artist Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Producer Name**: Enter the name of the producer you are working with.
* **Producer Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the producer you are working with.
* **Music Genre**: Select the genre of music your project falls under.
* **Preferred Session Date**: Choose a date for the strategy session.
* **Preferred Session Time**: Choose a time for the session to take place.
* **Session Format**: Choose between In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid formats.
* **Location or Virtual Platform**: Enter the location or link for the session to take place.
* **Session Duration - Hours**: Enter the length of the session in hours.
* **Strategy Session Agenda**: Outline the topics to be discussed during the session.
* **Current Project or Album Overview**: Provide a brief overview of your current project or album.
* **Artist Goals for This Session**: Share your goals for the session.
* **Producer Input or Feedback Requested**: Specify if you have any feedback or input required from the producer.
* **Number of Tracks Under Development**: If applicable, enter the number of tracks in development.
* **Project Deadline or Target Release Date**: Enter the project deadline or target release date.
* **Expected Production Timeline**: Provide a timeline for the production process.
* **Budget Available for Session**: If applicable, enter the budget available for the session.
* **Demos or Materials to Be Reviewed**: Share any demos or materials that need to be reviewed.
* **Planning Follow-Up Sessions**: Specify if follow-up sessions are planned.
* **Additional Notes or Special Requirements**: Add any additional notes or special requirements for the session.
