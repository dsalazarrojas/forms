# Bookkeeping Service Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bookkeeping Service Quotation Form is designed to collect information about your business to create an accurate quote for bookkeeping services. This form is intended for businesses and should not be used by individual users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the requested information.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (marked as `required` in the form).
3. For select fields (e.g., `business_structure`, `monthly_volume`, `current_system`, `services_needed`, `accounting_software`, `budget_range`, `timeline_needed`), select the relevant option from the list.
4. For `services_needed`, select multiple services that apply to your business.
5. In the `additional_notes` field, provide any other relevant information for the service provider to consider when creating the quotation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Name (`business_name`)
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of your business. Please enter the name as it appears on your business card or license.
### Contact Person (`contact_person`)
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): This is the full name of the person who will be our contact for this quotation.
### Business Email (`business_email`)
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, `email`, required): This is the email address associated with your business. We may use this to communicate with you about your quotation.
### Phone Number (`business_phone`)
* **Phone Number** (`business_phone`, `text`, required): This is the contact phone number for your business.
### Business Address (`business_address`)
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required): This is the full address of your business.
### Business Structure (`business_structure`)
* **Business Structure** (`business_structure`, `select_one`, required): Please select the type of business entity you are operating under (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, etc.).
### Years in Operation (`years_operating`)
* **Years in Operation** (`years_operating`, `number`, required): Please enter the number of years your business has been operating.
### Monthly Transaction Volume (`monthly_volume`)
* **Monthly Transaction Volume** (`monthly_volume`, `select_one`, required): Please select the approximate number of transactions your business handles per month (e.g., 0-50, 50-100, 100-250, 250-500, 500+).
### Current Bookkeeping System (`current_system`)
* **Current Bookkeeping System** (`current_system`, `select_one`, required): Please select how you currently manage your finances (e.g., No system, Manual spreadsheets, Basic software, Advanced software, Accountant managed).
### Services Needed (`services_needed`)
* **Services Needed** (`services_needed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all services you require from us (e.g., General bookkeeping, Payroll processing, Tax preparation, Financial statements, Bank reconciliation, Invoice management, Expense tracking, Audit support).
### Number of Employees (`employees_count`)
* **Number of Employees** (`employees_count`, `number`, optional): If you have employees, please enter the number of people on your payroll.
### Preferred Accounting Software (`accounting_software`)
* **Preferred Accounting Software** (`accounting_software`, `select_one`, optional): If you have a preferred accounting software, please select it from the list (e.g., QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks, Wave, No preference, Other).
### Budget Range (`budget_range`)
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Please select the estimated monthly budget for the services you require.
### Timeline Needed (`timeline_needed`)
* **Timeline Needed** (`timeline_needed`, `select_one`, required): Please select when you would like to start the bookkeeping services (e.g., Immediately, Within 2 weeks, Within 1 month, Flexible).
### Additional Notes (`additional_notes`)
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any other relevant information about your business or requirements.
