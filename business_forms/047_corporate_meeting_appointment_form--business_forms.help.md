# corporate_meeting_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate_meeting_appointment_form is a tool used to schedule and organize corporate meetings by gathering essential information from attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a meeting topic in the "Meeting Topic" field.
2. Indicate whether attendees will be present or not in the "Attendees" field.
3. Enter the date of the meeting in the "Date" field.
4. Enter the start and end times of the meeting in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields.
5. Specify the duration of the meeting in the "Duration" field.
6. Enter the location of the meeting in the "Location" field.
7. Add any notes or additional comments about the meeting in the "Notes" field.
8. Specify any preferences for the meeting in the "Preferences" field.
9. Select the assigned tool or resource for the meeting in the "Assigned Tool" field.
10. Indicate the availability of attendees in the "Availability" field.
11. Select the type of meeting in the "Meeting Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Meeting Topic (1)
• **Meeting Topic**: <input type="text" name="meeting_topic"> (Text, Optional) - Enter a brief description of the meeting topic.

### Attendees (2)
• **Attendees**: <input type="select_multiple" name="attendees"> (Select Multiple, Optional) - Check the boxes for each attendee who will be present at the meeting.

### Date (3)
• **Date**: <input type="date" name="date"> (Date, Optional) - Choose the date of the meeting.

### Start Time (4)
• **Start Time**: <input type="time" name="start_time"> (Time, Optional) - Select the start time of the meeting.

### End Time (5)
• **End Time**: <input type="time" name="end_time"> (Time, Optional) - Select the end time of the meeting.

### Duration (6)
• **Duration**: <input type="number" name="duration"> (Number, Optional) - Enter the duration of the meeting in minutes.

### Location (7)
• **Location**: <input type="text" name="location"> (Text, Optional) - Enter the location of the meeting.

### Notes (8)
• **Notes**: <textarea name="notes"> (Note, Optional) - Add any additional comments or notes about the meeting.

### Preferences (9)
• **Preferences**: <input type="select_multiple" name="preferences"> (Select Multiple, Optional) - Check the boxes for any meeting preferences.

### Assigned Tool (10)
• **Assigned Tool**: <input type="select_one" name="assigned_tool"> (Select One, Optional) - Select the assigned tool or resource for the meeting.

### Availability (11)
• **Availability**: <input type="select_multiple" name="availability"> (Select Multiple, Optional) - Check the boxes for the availability of attendees.

### Meeting Type (12)
• **Meeting Type**: <input type="select_one" name="meeting_type"> (Select One, Optional) - Select the type of meeting.

## Tips
- Double-check that all fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Review your answers for accuracy before saving the form.
- Use the correct format for dates and times (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY and HH:MM AM/PM).
