# Ergonomic Office Furniture Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ergonomic office furniture order form is a tool used by employees to order office furniture. It is essential for ensuring accurate delivery of supplies and furniture to the correct addresses and quantities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name as it appears on your ID or business card.
2. Enter your work email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Provide your delivery address, including the address line 1, city, and state.
5. Select the type of order you wish to make (Chair, Desk, or Table).
6. Specify the quantity of each item you are ordering.
7. If necessary, add any additional comments about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name** (user_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or business card.
- **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your work email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, optional): Enter your work phone number in case we need to contact you.
- **Delivery Address** (delivery_address, text, required): Enter your delivery address line 1 and line 2.
- **Delivery City** (delivery_city, text, optional): Enter the city of the delivery address.
- **Delivery State** (delivery_state, select_one, required): Select your state of residence.
- **Delivery Zip** (delivery_zip, text, required): Enter the postal zip code of the delivery address.
- **Order Type** (order_type, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the items you would like to order (Chair, Desk, Table).
- **Furniture Quantity** (furniture_quantity, number, required): Enter the quantity of each item you would like to order.
- **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, note, optional): If you have any additional information or requests, please enter them here. 

Please note that all fields marked as 'required' must be completed before submitting the form.
