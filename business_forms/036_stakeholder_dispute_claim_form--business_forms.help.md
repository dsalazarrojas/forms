# Stakeholder Dispute Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stakeholder Dispute Claim Form is used to document and track disputes between stakeholders. This form is designed to collect information about the claimant, the issue, and the resolution process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Name.
2. Enter your Company Name.
3. Select whether a dispute exists (Yes or No).
4. Describe the issue.
5. Enter the Claim Amount.
6. Select the Claim Date.
7. Indicate if you have Supporting Documents.
8. Select the Dispute Status (if applicable).
9. Enter the Resolution Outcome.
10. Enter any additional Resolution Comments.
11. Enter the Dispute Date (if applicable).
12. Enter the Assigned Tool.
13. Enter your User Email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **User Name** (`user_name`, text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, Required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
- **Is There a Dispute?** (`issue_type`, select_one, Optional): Check this box if you are experiencing a dispute.
    * If yes, select one of the following options: `Yes`, `No`.
    * This indicates whether a dispute exists or not.
- **Dispute Description** (`issue_description`, text, Required): Describe the issue you are experiencing.
- **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, number, Required): Enter the amount claimed.
- **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, Required): Select the date the claim was made.
- **Do You Have Supporting Documents?** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, Optional): Check if you have any supporting documents for your claim.
    * If yes, select one or more of the following: `Yes`, `No`.
- **Dispute Status** (`dispute_status`, select_one, Optional): Select the current status of the dispute.
    * If applicable, select one of the following options: `In Progress`, `Resolved`, `Pending`.
- **Resolution Outcome** (`resolution_outcome`, text, Optional): Enter the outcome of the resolution.
- **Resolution Comments** (`resolution_comments`, text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the resolution.
- **Dispute Date** (`dispute_date`, date, Optional): Select the date the dispute started.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, Required): Enter the name of the tool assigned to resolve the dispute.
- **User Email** (`user_email`, email, Required): Enter your email address.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask your support team for assistance.
