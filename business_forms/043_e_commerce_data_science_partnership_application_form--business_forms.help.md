# E Commerce Data Science Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for businesses seeking to partner with our organization on data science projects. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the sections below, following the instructions for each field. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Application Form (page 1)
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your project idea or proposal in this field. 

### Contact Details (page 2)
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number to help us get in touch with you. We will use this information to reach out to you with more questions and updates about your application. 

### Company Details (page 3)
* **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, optional): Provide information about your company, including name, address, and any other relevant details. 

### Project Details (page 4)
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Describe your data science project in detail, including objectives, timelines, and budgets. 

### Payment Terms (page 5)
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select, optional): Do you agree to pay for our data science services? 
  * Choose "Yes" if you are prepared to pay for the services.
  * Choose "No" if you would like us to cover all costs or discuss alternative arrangements.

### Terms and Conditions (page 6)
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select, optional): Have you read and agreed to our terms and conditions? 
  * Choose "Yes" if you have read and agreed.
  * Choose "No" if you have not read or are unsure.

### Submit (page 7)
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Once you have filled out all the fields above, click "Submit" to send your application. We will review your submission and get back to you with more information or questions.
