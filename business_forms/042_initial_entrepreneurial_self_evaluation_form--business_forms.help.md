# Initial Entrepreneurial Self Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help entrepreneurs evaluate their business ideas and progress towards establishing a business. It provides a structured approach to categorize and track business progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes your business.
2. Enter a brief description of your business.
3. Enter a unique ID for your form.
4. Select the user responsible for this form.
5. Choose the tool associated with this form.
6. Select the status of your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: This is the title of your business form. It's a short and descriptive label that summarizes your form's purpose. (required)
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your business form. Choose from "Business Forms", "Other Forms" or a custom category. (optional)
* **Description**: This is a brief description of your business form. Provide a short paragraph describing your business. (optional)
* **Form Category**: Select a category for your form, such as "Business Forms", "Personal Finance", "Education" or "Other Forms". (optional)
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for your form, which will be used to identify your form. (optional)
* **Form ID**: Enter your form's unique ID, which should be a number. (required)
* **Created**: This field is not applicable for this form, as it's a readonly field. Please ignore it.
* **Last Updated**: This field is not applicable for this form, as it's a readonly field. Please ignore it.
* **User**: Select the user responsible for this form. Choose from "Yes", "No" or a custom option. (optional)
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool associated with this form. Choose from "chatjimmy" or "Other Tool" or a custom option. (optional)
* **Status**: Select the status of your form. Choose from "Draft", "Published" or "Unpublished". (optional)
* **Status Date**: This field is not applicable for this form, as it's a readonly field. Please ignore it.

## Tips
* Make sure to choose a unique and descriptive title for your form.
* Select a category that best describes your business.
* Enter a brief and descriptive description of your business.
* Enter a unique ID for your form to avoid confusion.
* Select the user responsible for this form.
* Choose the tool associated with this form.
* Select the status of your form accurately.
