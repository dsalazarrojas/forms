# inventory_management_service_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The inventory management service intake form is designed to collect essential information about a new inventory management service request. This form is used to process requests efficiently and ensure that they are assigned to the right team member.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Business Name** in the text field provided.
2. Choose the correct **Business Type** from the dropdown list (Retail or Manufacturing).
3. Enter the **Location** in the text field.
4. Enter your **Email** address.
5. Enter your **Phone Number**.
6. Describe the **Inventory Description** in the text field.
7. Select the **Inventory Level** from the dropdown list (Low, Medium, or High).
8. Enter the desired **Delivery Window**.
9. Select the **Date** for the request.
10. Select the **Time** for the request.
11. Add any additional **Note** regarding the request.
12. Choose the team member to **Assign To** from the dropdown list (chatjimmy or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (text): Enter the name of the business requesting the service.
* **Business Type** (select_one): Choose the type of business (Retail or Manufacturing).
* **Location** (text): Enter the physical location of the business.
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter a valid phone number.
* **Inventory Description** (text): Describe the inventory management request.
* **Inventory Level** (select_multiple): Select the urgency level of the request (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Delivery Window** (number): Enter the time frame for delivery (in hours).
* **Date** (date): Select the date for the request.
* **Time** (time): Select the time for the request.
* **Note** (note): Add any additional information regarding the request.
* **Assign To** (select_one): Select the team member to assign the request to (chatjimmy or Other).
* **Created By** (text): This field is not used as part of the form, so it can be ignored.

Note: This form has been designed with the goal of streamlining the inventory management service intake process, ensuring that requests are processed efficiently and assigned to the right team member. By providing clear and concise information, you can help us better serve your business needs.
