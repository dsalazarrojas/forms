# new_customer_set_up_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information from new customers to create their records in our system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out all the pages of the form, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Review each page carefully before proceeding to the next.
3. Double-check that all fields are filled out correctly, especially the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: First Name (`id: 1`)
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's first name as it appears on their identification.

### Page 1: Last Name (`id: 2`)
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's last name as it appears on their identification.

### Page 1: Company (`id: 3`)
* **Company Name** (`company`, `text`, required/optional): If the customer is associated with a company, enter the company name.

### Page 1: Street Address (`id: 4`)
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's street address.

### Page 2: Email (`id: 5`)
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the customer's valid email address.

### Page 2: Phone (`id: 6`)
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.

### Page 3: Customer Type (`id: 7`)
* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of customer, either **Individual** or **Business**.

### Page 3: Date of Birth (`id: 8`)
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the customer's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Double-check for any errors before proceeding to the next page.
* If you need any help or have questions, feel free to contact our support team.
