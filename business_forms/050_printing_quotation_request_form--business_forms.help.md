# printing_quotation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for creating printing quotation requests, which are used to gather information from end users for the purpose of providing a quote for printing services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are completing it for the correct purpose (creating a genuine quotation request).
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section for more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Paper size** (paper_size): This field allows you to select the size of the paper required for your printing needs. Select 'Yes' for standard sizes or 'No' if you need non-standard sizes.
* **Paper orientation** (paper_orientation): This field is used to select the orientation of the paper required for your printing needs. Choose 'Yes' for standard orientations or 'No' if you need non-standard orientations.
* **Quantity** (quantity): This field is required and asks for the number of prints you need. Please enter a numerical value.
* **Color** (color): Select 'Yes' if you require color printing or 'No' if you need black and white printing.
* **Customer name** (customer_name): This field is required and asks for the name of the customer.
* **Customer email** (customer_email): This field is required and should be filled out with the customer's email address.
* **Customer phone** (customer_phone): This field is required and should be filled out with the customer's phone number.
* **Additional notes** (additional_notes): This field is optional and can be used to provide any additional information about your printing needs.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our printing team for assistance.
