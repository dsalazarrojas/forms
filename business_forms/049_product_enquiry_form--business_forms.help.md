# Product Enquiry form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Product Enquiry form is designed for customers to express interest in purchasing a product or service from your company. It helps us understand their needs and preferences, allowing us to provide better support and guidance.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enlist your details in the "Enquiry for the product" field. Please provide a brief description of the product you're interested in.
2. Indicate which services you're interested in by selecting "Yes" or "No" next to each service.
3. Fill in your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field, if you'd like to be contacted directly.
5. Finally, submit the form to send your enquiry to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enquiry for the product** (`enquiry_product`, text, required): Briefly describe the product you're interested in. For example, "Enquiry for the latest smartphone model".
* **Interested services** (`product_services`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which services you're interested in by selecting "Yes" or "No" next to each service. For example, "Yes" for installation service and "No" for maintenance service".
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as you'd like to be referred to. Please use your real name as it will help us provide better support.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you. Please ensure it's the email address you use to receive notifications.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted directly, please enter your phone number. This is not required but will help us get in touch with you faster.
* **Service 1**, **Service 2**, **Service 3** (`service_1`, `service_2`, `service_3`, select_one, optional): Indicate which services you're interested in by selecting "Yes" or "No" next to each service. For example, "Yes" for service 1 and "No" for services 2 and 3.
