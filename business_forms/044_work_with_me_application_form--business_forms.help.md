# Work With Me Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Work With Me Application Form" is designed to collect essential information from users who wish to work with us. This information helps us to better understand their needs and preferences, ensuring a smooth and efficient onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact details to identify yourself.
2. Enter your email address to ensure we can reach out to you.
3. Indicate your job title to help us understand your role and responsibilities.
4. Enter your phone number to facilitate communication and follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: no): Enter your full name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: no): Enter your personal or professional email address, where we can reach you.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: no): Describe your job title and role within your organization, to help us understand your responsibilities.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: no): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any issues with your onboarding process. If you are submitting on behalf of someone else, please ensure you have their permission to do so. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
