# Creator Payment Setup Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about creators in our system. The purpose of this form is to help us understand who the creator is, how to contact them, and how often they should be paid. This information is essential for us to properly configure their payment settings and ensure they receive their rightful payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the appropriate creator type from the "Creator Type" dropdown list.
2.  Enter the creator's name in the "Creator Name" field.
3.  Enter the creator's email address in the "Creator Email" field.
4.  (Optional) Enter the creator's phone number in the "Creator Phone" field.
5.  Choose the onboarding status of the creator from the "Onboarding Status" dropdown list.
6.  Select how often the creator should be paid from the "Payout Frequency" dropdown list.
7.  Choose the payment terms from the "Payment Terms" dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Creator Type**: Select the type of creator you are setting up payment settings for. This field is used to determine how to process payments for this creator.
* **Creator Name**: Enter the name of the creator. This field is required for us to know who the creator is.
* **Creator Email**: Enter the email address of the creator. This field is used for communication and payment notifications.
* **Creator Phone**: (Optional) Enter a valid phone number for the creator. This field is not required but can be helpful for additional contact information.
* **Onboarding Status**: Choose the onboarding status of the creator. This field is used to determine if the creator is active, inactive, or unknown.
* **Payout Frequency**: Select how often the creator should be paid. This field is used to determine the payment schedule for the creator.
* **Payment Terms**: Choose the payment terms for the creator. This field is used to determine the terms under which the creator is paid.
