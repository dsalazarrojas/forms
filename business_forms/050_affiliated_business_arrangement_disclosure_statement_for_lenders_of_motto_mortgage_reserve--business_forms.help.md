# Affiliated Business Arrangement Disclosure Statement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to disclose affiliated business arrangements between a lender and a service provider. An affiliated business arrangement is a relationship that may influence the lender's decision to make a mortgage loan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the borrower's name.
2. Enter the property address.
3. Enter the date of the disclosure.
4. Select the lender's name as it appears on the public record.
5. Describe the relationship between the lender and the service provider.
6. Enter the name of the service provider.
7. Describe the service provided by the service provider.
8. Enter the estimated charge for the service.
9. Acknowledge that you understand the disclosure form.
10. Sign the disclosure form to confirm that you understand it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Disclosure Statement --** (`1`): This section provides a brief description of the form's purpose.
* **Borrower Name** (`2`): Enter the borrower's name as it appears on the public record.
* **Property Address** (`3`): Enter the address of the property being financed.
* **Date** (`4`): Enter the date of the disclosure.
* **-- Lender Information --** (`5`): This section provides a brief description of the lender's information.
* **Lender Name** (`6`): Select the lender's name as it appears on the public record.
* **Relationship Disclosure** (`7`): Describe the relationship between the lender and the service provider.
* **-- Service Provider Selection --** (`8`): This section provides a brief description of the service provider.
* **Service Provider Name** (`9`): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Service Description** (`10`): Describe the service provided by the service provider.
* **Estimated Charge ($)** (`11`): Enter the estimated charge for the service.
* **-- Acknowledgment --** (`12`): This section provides a brief description of the acknowledgment.
* **Borrower Acknowledgment** (`13`): Select the acknowledgment option that best describes your understanding of the disclosure form.
* **Borrower Signature** (`14`): Select the signature option that indicates you have read and understand the disclosure form.
* **Date Signed** (`15`): Enter the date that you signed the disclosure form.
