# zero_waste_strategy_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from businesses and organizations interested in participating in the Zero Waste Strategy program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your organization in the "organization_name" field.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the person filling out the form, including their email address in the "email_address" field.
3. Select the role of the person filling out the form at their organization in the "role_at_organization" field.
4. Enter the department of the person filling out the form in the "department" field.
5. Enter the contact phone number of the person filling out the form in the "contact_phone" field.
6. Select the size of your organization (Small, Medium, Large) in the "company_size" field.
7. Enter the location of your organization in the "location" field.
8. Enter the start and end dates of the period you would like to participate in the Zero Waste Strategy program in the "start_date" and "end_date" fields.
9. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "note" field.
10. Select the type of your organization in the "organization_type" field.
11. Enter the industry of your organization in the "industry" field.
12. Enter the address of your organization in the "address" field.
13. Enter the city and state of your organization in the "city" and "state" fields.
14. Enter the zip code of your organization in the "zip" field.
15. Enter any additional phone numbers for your organization in the "phone1" and "phone2" fields (optional).
16. Enter the URL of your organization's website in the "company_website" field.
17. Enter the number of employees in your organization in the "employee_count" field.
18. Enter the annual turnover (in currency unit, e.g., dollars) in the "annual_turnover" field.
19. Select the type of your organization (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3) in the "company_type" field.
20. Select the certification level of your organization (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3) in the "certification_level" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **organization_name** (text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **contact_person** (text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the person filling out the form.
* **email_address** (email, required): Enter the email address of the person filling out the form.
* **role_at_organization** (select_one, required): Select the role of the person filling out the form at their organization.
* **department** (text, required): Enter the department of the person filling out the form.
* **contact_phone** (text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the person filling out the form.
* **company_size** (select_one, required): Select the size of your organization (Small, Medium, Large).
* **location** (text, required): Enter the location of your organization.
* **time** (time, required): Enter the time period you would like to participate in the Zero Waste Strategy program.
* **start_date** (date, required): Enter the start date of the period you would like to participate in the Zero Waste Strategy program.
* **end_date** (date, required): Enter the end date of the period you would like to participate in the Zero Waste Strategy program.
* **note** (note, required): Provide any additional notes or comments.
* **organization_type** (select_one, required): Select the type of your organization.
* **industry** (text, required): Enter the industry of your organization.
* **address** (text, required): Enter the address of your organization.
* **city** (text, required): Enter the city of your organization.
* **state** (text, required): Enter the state of your organization.
* **zip** (text, required): Enter the zip code of your organization.
* **phone1** (text, optional): Enter any additional phone number for your organization.
* **phone2** (text, optional): Enter any additional phone number for your organization.
* **company_website** (text, required): Enter the URL of your organization's website.
* **employee_count** (number, required): Enter the number of employees in your organization.
* **annual_turnover** (number, required): Enter the annual turnover (in currency unit, e.g., dollars) of your organization.
* **company_type** (select_one, required): Select the type of your organization.
* **certification_level** (select_one, optional): Select the certification level of your organization (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3).

## Tips

Please be accurate and honest when filling out this form, as the data collected will be used to create a personalized plan to help your organization reduce waste and improve its environmental performance. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Zero Waste Strategy team.
