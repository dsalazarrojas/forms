# Contract Review Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to review and assess contracts, agreements, and other business-related documents to ensure that all parties involved have a clear understanding of the terms, conditions, and obligations outlined in the document.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the contract or agreement carefully and ensure you understand all the terms and conditions involved.
2. Answer each question honestly, based on your assessment of the contract or agreement.
3. If a question does not apply or is not relevant, choose "Not applicable" or "Not included" as the response.
4. For questions where the answer is yes, ensure that all parties have a clear understanding of the term or condition.
5. For questions where the answer is no, suggest revisions or improvements where necessary.
