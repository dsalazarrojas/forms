# Investor Statement Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investor Statement Verification Form is used to gather information about an investor's financial situation and status. This form helps the investor or their representative to provide accurate and up-to-date information about their accounts and verification status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for questions regarding your financial institution, investor type, category, account type, account status, account holder, verification method, verification status, account verification, financial institution verification status, and signature verification. 
2. Fill in your investor's name and date of birth.
3. Provide a valid email address and phone number.
4. Complete the address fields (city, state, and zip code) accurately.
5. Enter the verification date of when you last verified your account.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are using this financial institution.
* **Investor Name** (`investor_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your records.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, optional): Enter the account number you'd like to be verified.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the physical address where your account is held.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where your account is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where your account is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code where your account is located.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, optional): Enter your social security number for verification purposes.
* **Investor Statement** (`investor_statement`, note, optional): Provide a written statement explaining your investor status and account details.
* **Verification Date** (`verification_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you last verified your account.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign and date this form to confirm the accuracy of the information.
* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are using this financial institution.
* **Investor Type** (`investor_type`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if this applies to you.
* **Investor Category** (`investor_category`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if this applies to you.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if this applies to you.
* **Account Status** (`account_status`, select_one, optional): Select 'Active' if your account is active.
* **Account Holder** (`account_holder`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if this applies to you.
* **Verification Method** (`verification_method`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are using this method.
* **Verification Status** (`verification_status`, select_one, optional): Select 'Active' if your account is verified.
* **Account Verification** (`account_verification`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if your account is verified.
* **Financial Institution Verification Status** (`financial_institution_verification_status`, select_one, optional): Select 'Active' if your financial institution is verified.
* **Signature Verification** (`signature_verification`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are verified.
