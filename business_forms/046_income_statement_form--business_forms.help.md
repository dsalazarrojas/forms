# Income Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Income Statement Form is a financial report that shows the revenue, costs, and expenses of a business over a specified period of time. This form helps business owners and accountants track the financial performance of a company by breaking down the income and expenses into categories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine the period of time you want to report on (e.g., monthly, quarterly, yearly).
2.  Enter the start and end dates of the reporting period in the `period_start_date` and `period_end_date` fields, respectively.
3.  Calculate and enter the total revenue and cost of goods sold for the reporting period.
4.  Enter the operating expenses and non-operating expenses for the reporting period.
5.  Select the category of expenses (Operating, Nonoperating, or Total) from the `categories` field.
6.  Enter the specific categories of expenses (Operating, Nonoperating, or Total) for the reporting period in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **period_start_date**: Enter the start date of the reporting period. This field is required.
*   **period_end_date**: Enter the end date of the reporting period. This field is required.
*   **revenue**: Enter the total revenue for the reporting period. This field is required.
*   **cost_of_goods_sold**: Enter the cost of goods sold for the reporting period. This field is required.
*   **gross_profit**: Enter the gross profit for the reporting period. This field is not required.
*   **operating_expenses**: Enter the operating expenses for the reporting period. This field is not required.
*   **nonoperating_expenses**: Enter the nonoperating expenses for the reporting period. This field is not required.
*   **net_income**: Enter the net income for the reporting period. This field is not required.
*   **categories**: Select one of the following categories (Operating, Nonoperating, or Total) from the dropdown menu. This field is required.
*   **operating_expenses**: Enter the specific operating expenses for the reporting period (e.g., salaries, rent). This field is not required.
*   **nonoperating_expenses**: Enter the specific nonoperating expenses for the reporting period (e.g., rent, taxes). This field is not required.
*   **total_expenses**: Enter the total expenses for the reporting period. This field is not required.
*   **categories**: Select one of the following categories (Operating, Nonoperating, or Total) from the dropdown menu. This field is required.
