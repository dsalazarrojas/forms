# Cruise Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cruise Job Application Form is a tool used to collect job application information from potential employees. It helps the company gather essential details about the job seeker's qualifications, work history, and desired job characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section with accurate and relevant information.
2. Make sure to check if a field is optional before proceeding.
3. Be as detailed as possible, especially when providing work history and qualifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Application** (`job_application`, text, optional): A short description of why you're applying for a job on the cruise.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, number, optional): The number of years of work experience you have.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, optional): Choose the job title that best matches your qualifications. 
*   Options: Cruise Director, Cruise Manager, Cruise Captain
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): The number of hours you're willing to work per week.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the job type that best matches your qualifications. 
*   Options: Sales, Marketing, Operations
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, optional): Choose the job level that best matches your qualifications. 
*   Options: Entry-Level, Mid-Level, Senior-Level
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Choose the department you're interested in applying for. 
*   Options: Human Resources, Marketing, Operations
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the job status that best matches your qualifications. 
*   Options: Full-time, Part-time, Freelance
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, select_one, optional): Choose the job location you're interested in applying for. 
*   Options: Onboard, Ashore
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, number, optional): The number of hours you're willing to work per week.
* **Salary** (`salary`, number, optional): The expected salary for the position.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when providing work history and qualifications.
- Make sure to check for spelling and grammar errors before submitting.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask a recruiter for clarification.
