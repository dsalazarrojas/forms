# shift close - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shift Close form is used to record and review shift-related data for employees. This includes recording sales, inventory, and other key metrics related to employee shifts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the store name in the "Store Name" field.
2. Enter the total sales amount in the "Total Sales" field.
3. Enter the current inventory level in the "Inventory In" field.
4. Enter the current inventory level after the shift in the "Inventory Out" field.
5. Enter any total refunds made during the shift in the "Total Refunds" field.
6. Enter your name as the employee who closed the shift in the "Employee Name" field.
7. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
8. Select the start and end dates of the shift in the "Shift Start" and "Shift End" fields, respectively.
9. Enter any additional notes about the shift in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the store where the shift was worked.
* **Total Sales** (`total_sales`, number, optional): Enter the total sales amount made during the shift.
* **Inventory In** (`inventory_in`, number, optional): Enter the current inventory level at the start of the shift.
* **Inventory Out** (`inventory_out`, number, optional): Enter the current inventory level after the shift.
* **Total Refunds** (`total_refunds`, number, optional): Enter any total refunds made during the shift.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the employee who closed the shift.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Shift Start** (`shift_start`, date, optional): Select the start date and time of the shift.
* **Shift End** (`shift_end`, date, optional): Select the end date and time of the shift.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the shift.
