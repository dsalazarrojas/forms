# Training Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to verify employee training information to ensure accurate and up-to-date records. It captures essential details such as employee ID, name, role, and training date/time. Please fill out this form carefully to provide accurate information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
* Enter your name in the "Name" field.
* Select your role from the "Role" dropdown list.
* Enter the date of your training in the "Date" field.
* Enter the time of your training in the "Time" field.
* Add any additional notes about your training in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Form (Page 1) (Optional)**: This is the title of the form.
* **Employee Id (2)**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **Name (3)**: Enter your full name.
* **Role (4)**: Select your role from the dropdown list (e.g., "Role A", "Role B", or "Role C").
* **Date (5)**: Enter the date of your training in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" (e.g., "2022-03-15").
* **Time (6)**: Enter the time of your training in the format "HH:MM" (e.g., "14:30").
* **Notes (7)**: Add any additional comments or details about your training in this text field.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate verification.
* Double-check the date and time formats to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact your HR representative.
* This form is designed for internal use only; please do not share your information with unauthorized parties.
