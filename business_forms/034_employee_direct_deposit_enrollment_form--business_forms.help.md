# Employee Direct Deposit Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for employees to enroll their direct deposit accounts. It gathers information about the employee's account details and bank account information to facilitate direct deposit payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name and ID.
2. Select your direct deposit account type.
3. Enter your bank routing and account numbers.
4. Provide your bank's address and contact information.
5. Choose your direct deposit frequency.
6. If applicable, enter your payroll ID and account manager details.
7. Confirm your bank information for the second bank account (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Direct Deposit Account** (`direct_deposit_account`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of direct deposit account you wish to enroll.
* **Bank Name** (`bank_name`, text, optional): Enter your bank's name.
* **Routing Number** (`routing_number`, number, required): Enter your bank's routing number.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, number, required): Enter your bank's account number.
* **Bank Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your bank's physical or mailing address.
* **Bank Street Address** (`bank_street_address`, text, optional): Enter your bank's street address.
* **Bank City** (`bank_city`, text, optional): Enter your bank's city.
* **Bank State** (`bank_state`, text, optional): Enter your bank's state.
* **Bank ZIP Code** (`bank_zip`, text, optional): Enter your bank's ZIP code.
* **Bank Phone** (`bank_phone`, select_one, optional): Select your bank's contact method.
* **Bank Contact** (`bank_contact`, text, optional): Enter your bank's contact person or department.
* **Direct Deposit Frequency** (`direct_deposit_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you'd like to receive direct deposits.
* **Enrollment Date** (`enrollment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you'd like to start direct deposits.
* **Payroll ID** (`payroll_id`, text, optional): Enter your payroll ID number.
* **Account Manager** (`account_manager`, text, optional): Enter your account manager's name.
* **Bank Name 2** (`bank_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of your second bank account (if applicable).
* **Bank Address 2** (`bank_address_2`, text, optional): Enter the address of your second bank account (if applicable).
* **Bank City 2** (`bank_city_2`, text, optional): Enter the city of your second bank account (if applicable).
* **Bank State 2** (`bank_state_2`, text, optional): Enter the state of your second bank account (if applicable).
* **Bank ZIP Code 2** (`bank_zip_2`, text, optional): Enter the ZIP code of your second bank account (if applicable).
* **Bank Phone 2** (`bank_phone_2`, select_one, optional): Select your bank's contact method for the second bank account.
* **Bank Contact 2** (`bank_contact_2`, text, optional): Enter your contact person or department for the second bank account.

Note: Required fields are marked as "required" in the help guide to ensure you have the necessary information to complete the form correctly. If you're unsure about any field, please contact your HR representative for further assistance.
