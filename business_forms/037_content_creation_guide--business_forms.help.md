# Content Creation Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form guides content creators and project managers to provide necessary information for content creation projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Enter the project title and project identification number in the **Content Project Title** and **Content Project Identification Number** fields, respectively.
2. Select the **Primary Objective for the Content** from the dropdown menu (Awareness, Education, Conversion, Retention, Engagement, or Other).
3. Describe the target audience for the content in the **Description of the Target Audience** field.
4. Choose the **Preferred Brand Voice** from the dropdown menu (Professional, Casual, Innovative, Traditional, Authoritative, or Other).
5. Select the **Preferred Content Format** from the dropdown menu (Blog Post, Video, Infographic, White Paper, Ebook, Podcast, or Other).
6. Set the **Requested Content Creation Deadline**.
7. Optionally, provide an estimated budget for the project.
8. Confirm that the information provided is accurate by selecting **I Agree** in the **Guide Completed?** field.
9. Add any additional comments or details in the **Any additional details or comments?** field.
10. Confirm the accuracy of the information provided by selecting **I Agree** in the **I confirm that the information provided is accurate** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Content Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the content creation project.
* **Content Project Identification Number** (`project_id`, text, required): Enter the identification number for the content project.
* **Primary Objective for the Content** (`primary_content_objective`, select_one, required): Select the primary objective of the content (Awareness, Education, Conversion, Retention, Engagement, or Other).
* **Description of the Target Audience** (`target_audience_description`, text, required): Describe the target audience for the content.
* **Preferred Brand Voice** (`brand_voice_preference`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred brand voice (Professional, Casual, Innovative, Traditional, Authoritative, or Other).
* **Preferred Content Format** (`preferred_content_format`, select_one, required): Select the preferred content format (Blog Post, Video, Infographic, White Paper, Ebook, Podcast, or Other).
* **Requested Content Creation Deadline** (`content_creation_deadline`, date, required): Set the requested content creation deadline.
* **Estimated Budget** (`estimated_budget`, number, optional): Optionally, enter the estimated budget for the project.
* **Guide Completed?** (`guide_completed`, select_one, required): Confirm that the guide has been completed (True or False).
* **Any additional details or comments?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or details.
* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate** (`signature_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm the accuracy of the provided information (I Agree or I Disagree).
