# Commercial Energy Audit Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on a commercial energy audit inspection. It will help identify areas for improvement and make recommendations for energy-saving measures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the information requested for each field.
2. Answer all fields, including those marked as "required".
3. Be thorough and accurate in your answers, as this data will be used to inform recommendations for energy improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Auditor Name (`auditor_name`)

* **Auditor Name**: The name of the energy auditor completing the form.
* **Type:** Text field.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Audit Date (`audit_date`)

* **Audit Date**: The date of the energy audit inspection.
* **Type:** Date field.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Company Name (`company_name`)

* **Company Name**: The name of the business being audited.
* **Type:** Text field.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Facility Address (`facility_address`)

* **Facility Address**: The complete address of the facility being audited.
* **Type:** Text field.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Facility Type (`facility_type`)

* **Facility Type**: The type of business or facility being audited (e.g., Office, Retail, Warehouse, etc.).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Total Square Footage (`total_square_footage`)

* **Total Square Footage**: The total square footage of the facility.
* **Type:** Number field.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Electrical Systems (`electrical_panel_condition`)

* **Electrical Panel Condition**: Rate the condition of the electrical panel (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Hazardous).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Lighting Assessment (`lighting_assessment`)

* **Lighting Assessment**: Rate the overall lighting efficiency of the facility (e.g., Very Efficient, Efficient, Average, Inefficient, Very Inefficient).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### LED Lighting Percentage (`led_percentage`)

* **LED Lighting Percentage**: The percentage of LED lighting in the facility.
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### HVAC Inspection (`hvac_system_age`)

* **HVAC System Age**: Rate the age of the HVAC system (e.g., 0-5 years, 6-10 years, 11-15 years, 16-20 years, 20+ years).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### HVAC Efficiency Rating (`hvac_efficiency`)

* **HVAC Efficiency Rating**: Rate the current efficiency of the HVAC system (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Air Filter Condition (`filter_condition`)

* **Air Filter Condition**: Rate the current state of the air filter (e.g., Clean, Slightly Dirty, Dirty, Very Dirty, Needs Replacement).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Thermostat Settings (`thermostat_settings`)

* **Thermostat Settings**: Rate the settings of the thermostat (e.g., Optimal, Acceptable, Needs Adjustment, Poor).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Building Envelope (`insulation_condition`)

* **Insulation Condition**: Rate the condition of the insulation (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Inadequate).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Window Efficiency (`window_efficiency`)

* **Window Efficiency**: Rate the performance of the windows (e.g., High Efficiency, Moderate Efficiency, Low Efficiency, Very Low Efficiency).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Air Leaks Detected (`air_leaks_detected`)

* **Air Leaks Detected**: Rate the presence of air leaks (e.g., None, Minor, Moderate, Significant, Severe).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Door Seals Condition (`door_seals`)

* **Door Seals Condition**: Rate the condition of the door seals (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Missing).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Equipment Assessment (`equipment_energy_star`)

* **Equipment Energy Star**: Rate the percentage of energy-efficient equipment in the facility (e.g., 0-25%, 26-50%, 51-75%, 76-100%).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Equipment Maintenance (`equipment_maintenance`)

* **Equipment Maintenance**: Rate the regularity of equipment maintenance (e.g., Regular, Occasional, Rare, Never).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Phantom Load Observed (`phantom_load`)

* **Phantom Load Observed**: Rate the presence of standby power waste (e.g., None, Minimal, Moderate, Significant).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Recommendations (`energy_efficiency_rating`)

* **Energy Efficiency Rating**: Rate the overall energy efficiency of the facility (e.g., A - Excellent, B - Good, C - Average, D - Below Average, F - Poor).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Priority Improvements (`priority_improvements`)

* **Priority Improvements**: List the top priority improvements for the facility.
* **Type:** Text field.
* **Required:** Yes.

### Estimated Payback Period (`estimated_payback_period`)

* **Estimated Payback Period**: Rate the estimated payback period for energy improvements (e.g., Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, 10+ years).
* **Type:** Select one option.
* **Required:** No.

### Estimated Annual Savings (`estimated_annual_savings`)

* **Estimated Annual Savings**: The potential dollar savings from energy improvements.
* **Type:** Number field.
* **Required:** No.

### Additional Recommendations (`additional_recommendations`)

* **Additional Recommendations**: Other suggestions for energy improvements.
* **Type:** Text field.
* **Required:** No.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate in your answers.
* If unsure, use your best judgment.
* This form is part of a broader effort to improve the energy efficiency of commercial facilities.
