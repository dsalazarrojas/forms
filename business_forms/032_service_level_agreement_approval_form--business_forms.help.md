# Service Level Agreement Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Level Agreement Approval Form is used to obtain approval for service delivery. It captures critical information about the service, stakeholders, and approval status, ensuring a smooth and satisfactory service delivery process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Service Title** in the "Service Level Agreement Approval Form" field.
2. Enter a brief description of the service in the **Service Description** field.
3. Select the **Service Level** from the provided options (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
4. Choose the **Service Name** for the service being approved.
5. Select the **Stakeholders** and **Stakeholder Roles** involved in the approval process.
6. Choose the **Approver** responsible for signing off on the service.
7. Enter the **Approver Email** and **Approver Phone** for contact information.
8. Optionally, provide any **Approver Comments** regarding the service.
9. Select the **Service Level Agreement Approval** status (Approved, Not Approved, or Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Service Level Agreement Approval Form**: This is the heading for the form.
- **Service Description**: Briefly describe the service being approved. 
- **Service Level**: Choose the service level required for this service (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
- **Service Name**: Enter the name of the service being approved.
- **Stakeholders**: Select the stakeholders involved in the approval process.
- **Stakeholder Roles**: Choose the roles of the selected stakeholders.
- **Approver**: Select the approver responsible for signing off on this service.
- **Approver Email**: Enter the approver's email address.
- **Approver Phone**: Enter the approver's phone number.
- **Stakeholder Signature**: This field is not required but may be used for signature capture.
- **Approver Signature**: This field is not required but may be used for signature capture.
- **Date Approved**: This field is automatically populated when the approval is finalized.
- **Signature Date**: This field is automatically populated when the form is completed.
- **Signature**: This field is not required but may be used for additional comments.
- **Approver Comments**: Provide any additional comments from the approver.
- **Stakeholder Comments**: Provide any additional comments from the stakeholders.
- **Service Level Agreement Approval**: Select the approval status for the service (Approved, Not Approved, or Pending). 

Note: Some fields are not required but are provided for optional use.
