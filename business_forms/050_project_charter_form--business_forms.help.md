# Project Charter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Charter Form is a document that outlines the essential information about a project, serving as a foundational guide for project management and team collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in the project name and details in the designated fields.
- Select the targeted key performance indicators (KPIs) that are relevant to the project.
- Enter the financial details and budget for the project.
- Choose the milestones that are expected to be achieved during the project.
- Identify any risks associated with the project.
- Provide a detailed description of the project budget and its impact.
- Select the start date for the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the project in a clear and concise manner.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief overview of the project, including its objectives, goals, and scope.
* **Targeted KPI** (`targeted_kpi`, `text`, required: false): Select the KPIs that are directly related to the project's success.
* **Financial Details** (`financial_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the financial information related to the project, including costs, revenue, and any other relevant financial metrics.
* **Budget** (`budget`, `number`, required: false): Enter the budget for the project, including any allocated funds and expected expenditures.
* **Milestones** (`milestones`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the significant milestones that the project should achieve, such as completion of certain tasks or reaching specific goals.
* **Risks** (`risks`, `text`, required: false): Identify any potential risks that may impact the project's success.
* **Project Budget Details** (`project_budget_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the project budget, including how it will be used and managed.
* **Impact Details** (`impact_details`, `text`, required: false): Explain the potential impact of the project on the organization, stakeholders, or customers.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the project is expected to start.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in the project's scope, timeline, or budget.
* Use clear and concise language when filling in the form to ensure that all team members understand the project's objectives and expectations.
* Keep the form concise and focused on essential information to avoid overwhelming the project team.
