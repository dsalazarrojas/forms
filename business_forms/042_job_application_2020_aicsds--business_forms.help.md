# Job Application 2020 AICSDS - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Application 2020 AICSDS form is designed to collect essential information about job applicants, including their personal details, job preferences, and application status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Ensure you are filling out the form for a legitimate job application and not for any other purpose.
2.  Start by filling out the "Applicant Info" page, providing your name, contact information, and any other required details.
3.  Proceed to the "Job Details" page and enter the job title, job description, and any other relevant details about the position you are applying for.
4.  Move on to the "Department" page and select the department you are applying for.
5.  Go to the "Job Status" page and enter the current status of your job application.
6.  Fill out the "Date Of Birth" field with your date of birth in the required format.
7.  Finally, update the "Application Status" field with the current status of your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Info**: Enter your name, contact email and phone number in this field. This is where we get to know more about you.
*   **Job Details**: Enter the job title and description that you are applying for. This will help us match your application with the job posting.
*   **Department**: Select the department that you are applying to.
*   **Job Status**: Enter the status of your job application, e.g., "Applied", "Interview Scheduled", etc.
*   **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
*   **Application Status**: Update the status of your application, e.g., "New", "In Progress", etc. 

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any delays in the application process.
- If you are unsure about any field, it is recommended to contact the relevant personnel or HR department for clarification.
- Please do not provide duplicate applications for the same job posting. 

This guide is intended to help you fill out the form correctly and efficiently. If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out.
