# Content Background Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Background Assessment form is designed to collect essential information about a content project, including project details, content needs and preferences, and budget and timeline requirements. This form helps ensure that content teams have a clear understanding of the project's purpose, scope, and constraints, facilitating effective content creation and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure you have all necessary information before starting to fill out the form.
2. Provide accurate and detailed answers to each question, especially for required fields.
3. Select the correct options for select fields, such as content goals, preferred brand voice, and content format.
4. If you are unsure about any field or section, use additional comments to clarify your requirements.
5. Review the form before submitting to ensure you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Title** (`project_title`, **Text**, Required): Enter a descriptive title for the content project.
- **Content Project Identification Number** (`project_id`, **Text**, Required): Enter a unique identification number for the content project.
- **Description of the Target Audience** (`target_audience_description`, **Text, Required): Describe the target audience for the content project.
- **Primary Goals for the Content** (`content_goals`, **Select Multiple, Required): Select all applicable goals for the content, such as Awareness, Education, Conversion, Retention, and Engagement.
- **Preferred Brand Voice** (`brand_voice_preference`, **Select One, Required): Choose the preferred brand voice for the content project, such as Professional, Casual, Innovative, Traditional, or Authoritative.
- **Preferred Content Format** (`preferred_content_format`, **Select One, Required): Select the preferred content format for the project, such as Blog Post, Video, Infographic, White Paper, or Ebook.
- **Key Messaging Points to Include** (`key_messaging_points`, **Text, Required): Provide key messaging points that need to be included in the content.
- **Estimated Budget for the Project** (`estimated_budget`, **Number, Optional): Enter an estimated budget for the project.
- **Requested Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, **Date, Required): Enter the requested project deadline.
- **Assessment Completed?** (`assessment_completed`, **Select One, Required): Confirm whether the assessment is completed.
- **Any additional details or comments?** (`additional_comments`, **Text, Optional): Provide any additional details or comments about the project.
- **I confirm that the information provided is accurate** (`signature_confirmation`, **Select One, Required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.

**Tips**:

* Double-check the required fields to ensure they are completed accurately.
