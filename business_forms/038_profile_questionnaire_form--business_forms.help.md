# Profile Questionnaire Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about a company or project for internal and external purposes. It helps to create a comprehensive profile, including personal and contact details, project status, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct page (e.g., User Profile, Personal Details, etc.) from the navigation menu at the top of the form.
2. Fill out each field as required or optional, depending on the field label.
3. Enter your information accurately and completely.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **UserProfile** (`user_profile`, text, optional): This field is for entering your personal details, such as your name, title, or designation.
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are interested in our services.
* **ContactInfo** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, such as your name, phone number, or email address.
* **ProjectDetails** (`project_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your project, including its purpose and goals.
* **BusinessInfo** (`business_info`, text, optional): Enter information about your company, such as its name, address, or industry.
* **TechnologyDetails** (`technology_details`, text, optional): Describe the technologies used in your project.
* **OtherInfo** (`other_info`, text, optional): Add any other relevant information about your project.
* **ProjectStatus** (`project_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the current status of your project (e.g., In Progress, Completed, Cancelled).
* **BusinessName** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **CompanyName** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **ContactPerson** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for your project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your company's website.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your project.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* You can add or remove pages according to your needs, but please note that some pages may be required for certain purposes.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
