# window_deal_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for submitting information about window deals. It helps to collect property details, deal type, window specifications, and supporting documents related to a window deal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Property Details section, providing any relevant information about the property.
2. Select the correct deal type from the options provided.
3. Enter any additional window specifications in the Window Specs section.
4. Upload any supporting documents related to the window deal.
5. Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the property.
* **Deal Type** (`deal_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of deal you are submitting (Type A, Type B, or Type C).
* **Window Specs** (`window_specs`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional specifications related to the window.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, required/optional): Upload any relevant documents to support your submission.
