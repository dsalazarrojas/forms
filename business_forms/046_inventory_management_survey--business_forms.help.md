# Inventory Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Management Survey is designed to collect information about an organization's inventory management practices and processes. This form aims to gather data on various aspects of inventory management, including inventory types, stock management, stock purchasing processes, and more. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Carefully read each question to understand the context and what information is being requested.
2. Choose the most appropriate answer options for each question based on your organization's inventory management practices.
3. Fill out each section thoroughly and accurately to ensure the best possible use of the survey results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the type of inventory that you manage?** (`inventory_description`, select_one, optional): This question asks you to indicate the type of inventory you manage, such as physical or digital goods.
* **How many items are in your inventory?** (`inventory_size`, number, optional): This question seeks the approximate number of items currently in your inventory.
* **What is the total value of your inventory?** (`inventory_value`, number, optional): This question asks for the total value of all items in your inventory.
* **What stock management process do you use?** (`stock_management_process`, select_multiple, optional): This question offers options for the stock management process used, such as FIFO (First In First Out) or FEFO (First Expiry First Out).
* **How often do you manage your stock?** (`stock_management_frequency`, select_one, optional): This question asks for the frequency of stock management, like Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **How many people manage your stock?** (`stock_management_team`, number, optional): This question seeks to know the number of individuals involved in stock management.
* **Where do you store your stock?** (`stock_location`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks about the storage locations for your stock, including warehouses, stores, or other.
* **What method do you use to track your stock?** (`stock_tracking_method`, select_one, optional): This question inquires about the stock tracking method, either manual or automated.
* **What is your stock loss rate?** (`stock_loss_rate`, number, optional): This question asks for the stock loss rate, an indication of stock discrepancies.
* **What is your stock shrink rate?** (`stock_shrink_rate`, number, optional): This question seeks the stock shrink rate, another measure of stock discrepancies.
* **What is your stock obsoletion rate?** (`stock_obsoletion_rate`, number, optional): This question asks for the stock obsoletion rate, an indicator of stock disposal or replacement.
* **What is the reason for stock obsoletion?** (`stock_obsoletion_reason`, select_multiple, optional): This question provides options for reasons behind stock obsoletion, such as obsolescence or obstruction.
* **What method do you use for stock obsoletion?** (`stock_obsoletion_method`, select_one, optional): This question asks for the method used for stock obsoletion, whether automated or manual.
* **What is your stock purchasing process?** (`stock_purchasing_process`, select_multiple, optional): This question allows selection from stock purchasing processes, like bulk or Just-In-Time (JIT).
* **What is your stock return policy?** (`stock_return_policy`, select_one, optional): This question asks for the stock return policy, whether always, never, or sometimes applicable.
* **What selling strategy do you use?** (`stock_selling_strategy`, select_one, optional): This question seeks the selling strategy, either just-in-time or just-in-time and stockout.
* **What stock sourcing method do you use?** (`stock_sourcing_method`, select_multiple, optional): This question allows selection from stock sourcing methods, including local or international suppliers.
* **How often do you purchase stock?** (`stock_purchasing_frequency`, select_one, optional): This question inquires about the frequency of stock purchases, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **What method do you use to purchase stock?** (`stock_purchasing_method`, select_one, optional): This question asks for the stock purchasing method, whether online or offline.
* **What is your stock return rate?** (`stock_return_rate`, number, optional): This question seeks the stock return rate, an indicator of returns to stock.
* **What is your stock selling rate?** (`stock_selling_rate`, number, optional): This question asks for the stock selling rate, another measure of stock sales.
* **What is the purchasing cost for stock?** (`stock_purchasing_cost`, number, optional): This question seeks the cost associated with purchasing stock.
* **How has the value of your inventory changed over time?** (`stock_value_change`, number, optional): This question inquires about changes in the inventory's total value over time.
