# Conflict Resolution Agreement Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and record the terms of a conflict resolution agreement between two parties. It helps to prevent future conflicts and promotes transparency by creating a clear record of the agreed-upon terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in two parts:
	* Part 1: Gather information about the involved parties and the conflict details.
	* Part 2: Declare your understanding and agreement to the terms of the resolution.
2. Make sure both parties have a clear understanding of the terms before filling out the form.
3. Complete the form as soon as possible after the conflict has been resolved.
4. Review the form carefully before signing it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Involved Parties --**:
	+ This section gathers information about the parties involved in the conflict.
* **First Party Full Name** (`party_one_name`):
	+ Please enter the full name of the first party.
* **Second Party Full Name** (`party_two_name`):
	+ Please enter the full name of the second party.
* **Date of Agreement** (`date_of_agreement`):
	+ Enter the date on which the conflict was resolved.
* **-- Conflict Summary --**:
	+ This section is a brief summary of the conflict.
* **Nature of the Dispute** (`nature_of_dispute`):
	+ Select the type of conflict that occurred (Interpersonal, Professional/Workplace, Contractual, Financial, or Other).
* **Brief Conflict Description** (`conflict_description`):
	+ Describe the conflict in brief.
* **-- Resolution and Agreements --**:
	+ This section gathers information about the resolution and agreements made.
* **Key Points of Agreement** (`key_agreement_points`):
	+ List the key points of agreement made to resolve the conflict.
* **Required Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`):
	+ Describe any tasks or deadlines agreed upon to resolve the conflict.
* **Was a third-party Mediator involved?** (`mediator_involved`):
	+ Select whether a third-party mediator was involved in the conflict resolution process.
* **Mediator Name (if applicable)** (`mediator_name`):
	+ If a third-party mediator was involved, enter their name.
* **-- Declaration --**:
	+ This section is the declaration of understanding and agreement to the terms of the resolution.
* **We hereby declare that we understand and agree to the terms above** (`agreement_declaration`):
	+ Select "We agree" if you agree to the terms.
* **I agree to keep the details of this resolution confidential** (`confidentiality_clause`):
	+ Select "I agree" if you agree to keep the details of this resolution confidential.
* **First Party Signature (Type Name)** (`party_one_signature`):
	+ Enter the name of the first party, typically in the format "Last Name, First Name".
* **Second Party Signature (Type Name)** (`party_two_signature`):
	+ Enter the name of the second party, typically in the format "Last Name, First Name".
