# Team Dysfunction Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess the overall performance and dysfunction of a team within an organization. It aims to evaluate various aspects of team functioning, including collaboration, communication, decision-making, adaptability, leadership, problem-solving, conflict resolution, feedback, continuous improvement, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant team members from the options provided.
2. Choose the level of collaboration, productivity, decision-making, adaptability, leadership, problem-solving, conflict resolution, feedback, continuous improvement, and overall performance within your team.
3. Review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your team's dynamics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Members**: Select all the team members you want to assess. This field is not required, so you can choose as many or as few team members as you like.
* **Collaboration**: Indicate the level of collaboration within your team. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Productivity**: Select the level of productivity within your team. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Communication**: Rate the quality of communication within your team. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Decision Making**: Assess the level of effective decision-making within your team. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Adaptability**: Indicate the level of adaptability within your team. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Leadership**: Rate the leadership quality within your team. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Problem Solving**: Evaluate the ability of your team to solve problems. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Conflict Resolution**: Assess the team's ability to handle conflicts. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Feedback**: Rate the level of feedback within your team. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Continuous Improvement**: Indicate the level of continuous improvement within your team. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Team Dysfunction**: Select the level of team dysfunction, if any. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Overall Performance**: Assess the overall performance of your team. You can choose from High, Medium, or Low.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective when assessing your team's dynamics.
* Consider multiple perspectives when filling out this form.
* Use this form as a starting point for discussions and improvements within your team.
