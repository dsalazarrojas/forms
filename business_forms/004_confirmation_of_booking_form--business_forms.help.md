## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out all required fields (marked 'true' under 'required').
2. Double-check that the correct booking details are entered, especially booking date and time.
3. Select 'Confirmed' as the 'Confirmation Status' once you've reviewed and verified all information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Reference Number (1, text, required)**: Unique identifier for this booking.
* **Booking Date (2, date, required)**: Date when the booking was made.
* **Customer Name (3, text, required)**: Full name of the person making the booking.
* **Email Address (4, email, required)**: Contact email for confirmation and communication.
* **Phone Number (5, text, required)**: Contact phone number for any last-minute updates or concerns.
* **Company Name (6, text, optional)**: Organization or business name if applicable.
* **Type of Service Booked (7, select_one, required)**: What service or product is being booked (e.g., Event Venue Rental, Equipment Rental, etc.).
* **Service Details (8, text, required)**: Brief description of the specific service or product booked.
* **Event or Service Date (9, date, required)**: Date when the service will be provided.
* **Event or Service Time (10, time, required)**: Start time for the service.
* **Expected Duration (11, select_one, required)**: Length of time the service will last.
* **Number of Guests or Participants (12, number, optional)**: Expected attendance or participants.
* **Special Requirements (13, text, optional)**: Any special needs or accommodations for the service.
* **Additional Services Requested (14, select_multiple, optional)**: Select any add-on services needed.
* **Payment Status (15, select_one, required)**: Current payment status (e.g., Paid in Full, Deposit Paid, etc.).
* **Payment Method (16, select_one, optional)**: How payment will be made (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, etc.).
* **Total Booking Amount (17, number, optional)**: Total cost of the booking.
* **Deposit Amount Paid (18, number, optional)**: Amount of deposit if applicable.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledged (19, select_one, required)**: Confirmation that customer has read and understood cancellation terms.
* **Terms and Conditions Accepted (20, select_one, required)**: Agreement to booking terms.
* **Preferred Confirmation Method (21, select_one, required)**: How confirmations should be sent (e.g., Email, SMS, etc.).
* **Additional Notes (22, text, optional)**: Any other information or requests.
* **Booking Staff Name (23, text, required)**: Name of staff member who processed the booking.
* **Confirmation Status (24, select_one, required)**: Final status of this booking (e.g., Confirmed, Pending Approval, etc.).
