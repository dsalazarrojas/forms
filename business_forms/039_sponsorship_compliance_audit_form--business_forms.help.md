# Sponsorship Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Sponsorship Compliance Audit Form is used to document and verify sponsorships and partnerships within your business. It ensures that all sponsorships are properly vetted and monitored to maintain compliance with relevant regulations and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. All fields are optional, but providing detailed information helps ensure thorough reviews.
2. Select the correct category for your sponsorship (Business Forms or Other).
3. Enter a brief description of the sponsorship or partnership.
4. Specify the tool assigned to manage the sponsorship (e.g., a specific software or system).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Id**: A unique ID for your form, which helps identify and track the sponsorship.
* **Form Title**: A descriptive title for your sponsorship, which should include the name or name of the sponsor.
* **Category**: This field determines the category of the form. Select 'Business Forms' for standard business-related sponsorships or 'Other' for non-standard or external partnerships.
* **Description**: A short description of the sponsorship or partnership, including details on its nature and terms.
* **Assigned Tool**: The software, system, or method used to manage the sponsorship. This helps in tracking and monitoring its progress.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when completing the form to ensure proper documentation and compliance.
* Review and update the form as necessary to reflect changes in the sponsorship or partnership.
