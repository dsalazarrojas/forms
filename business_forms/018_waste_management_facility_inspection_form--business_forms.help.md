# Waste Management Facility Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waste Management Facility Inspection Form is used to inspect and monitor waste management facilities to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the facility name and address.
2. Enter the contact name and phone number of the facility representative.
3. Enter the contact email address of the facility representative.
4. Select the inspection date and time (if applicable).
5. Determine if the facility is active or inactive.
6. Check if the facility handles hazardous waste streams and types.
7. Check if the facility is in regulatory compliance.
8. Provide any corrective action taken.
9. Determine if corrective action was taken.
10. Enter any facility comments or notes.
11. Enter any inspector comments.
12. Upload any facility pictures or videos.
13. Upload any facility documents.
14. Enter any other information relevant to the facility.
15. Enter facility notes or any other information.
16. Enter the facility's signature.
17. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name** (1): Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Contact Name** (2): Enter the name of the facility representative who can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone** (4): Enter the phone number of the facility representative.
* **Contact Email** (5): Enter the email address of the facility representative.
* **Facility Address** (3): Enter the physical address of the facility.
* **Inspection Date** (6): Select the date of the inspection (if applicable).
* **Inspection Time** (7): Select the time of the inspection (if applicable).
* **Facility Type** (8): Determine if the facility is active or inactive.
* **Facility Status** (10): Select whether the facility is Active or Inactive.
* **Waste Stream** (11): Check if the facility handles hazardous waste streams.
* **Waste Type** (12): Check if the facility handles specific types of waste.
* **Facility Location** (13): Enter the physical location of the facility.
* **Regulatory Compliance** (14): Check if the facility is in regulatory compliance.
* **Corrective Action** (15): Enter any corrective action taken by the facility.
* **Corrective Action Taken** (16): Determine if corrective action was taken.
* **Facility Comments** (17): Enter any comments or notes from the facility.
* **Inspector Comments** (18): Enter any comments or notes from the inspector.
* **Facility Pictures** (19): Upload pictures of the facility (if applicable).
* **Facility Videos** (20): Upload videos of the facility (if applicable).
* **Facility Documents** (21): Upload documents related to the facility (if applicable).
* **Facility Other** (22): Enter any other information relevant to the facility.
* **Facility Notes** (23): Enter any notes or comments about the facility.
* **Facility Signature** (24): Enter the facility's signature (if applicable).
* **Submit** (25): Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
