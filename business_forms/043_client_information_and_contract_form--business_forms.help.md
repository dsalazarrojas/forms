# Client Information And Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients for a photography contract. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the client and photographer have a clear understanding of the terms and conditions of the shoot, including the type of shoot, date, location, and services included.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please ensure you have all necessary information before starting to fill out the form.
* Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This will be used as your primary contact email.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This will be used as your best contact number.
* Enter your street address in the "Street Address" field. This will be used for mailing purposes.
* Enter your city in the "City" field.
* Enter your state in the "State" field.
* Enter your ZIP code in the "ZIP Code" field.
* Select the type of shoot you are booking (e.g., Portrait, Wedding, etc.) in the "Type of Shoot" field.
* Enter the scheduled date of the shoot in the "Shoot Date" field.
* Enter the location of the shoot in the "Shoot Location" field.
* Enter the start time of the shoot in the "Shoot Start Time" field.
* Select the expected duration of the shoot (e.g., 1 Hour, 2 Hours, etc.) in the "Expected Duration" field.
* Select the photography package you have chosen in the "Photography Package" field.
* Enter the total price of the package in the "Package Price" field.
* Enter the deposit amount required to secure the booking in the "Deposit Amount" field.
* Indicate whether you have paid the deposit in the "Has Deposit Been Paid?" field.
* Enter the date by which the remaining balance is due in the "Balance Due Date" field.
* Select any additional services you would like to request in the "Additional Services" field.
* Enter any special requests or requirements in the "Special Requests" field.
* Select how you would like your photos to be used (e.g., Personal Use Only, etc.) in the "Photo Usage Rights" field.
* Indicate whether you give permission for photos to be used in the photographer's portfolio in the "May Photos Be Used in Portfolio?" field.
* Confirm that you understand the 48-hour cancellation policy and any rescheduling terms in the "I Understand the Cancellation Policy" field.
* Confirm that you understand the photo delivery timeline in the "I Understand the Photo Delivery Timeline" field.
* Confirm that you understand the photographer's liability limitations in the "I Understand the Liability Limitations" field.
* Confirm that you agree to all terms and conditions in the "I Agree to All Terms and Conditions" field.
* Sign the contract by entering your full name in the "Client Electronic Signature" field.
* If applicable, enter your name in the "Photographer Name" field.
* If applicable, sign the contract by entering your name in the "Photographer Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email. This will be used as the main contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your best contact number.
* **Street Address** (`client_address`, `text`, required): Enter your complete mailing address.
* **City** (`client_city`, `text`, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`client_state`, `text`, required): Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code** (`client_zip`, `text`, required): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Type of Shoot** (`shoot_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of shoot you are booking (e.g., Portrait, Wedding, etc.).
* **Shoot Date** (`shoot_date`, `date`, required): Enter the scheduled date of the shoot.
* **Shoot Location** (`shoot_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the shoot.
* **Shoot Start Time** (`shoot_start_time`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of the shoot.
* **Expected Duration** (`shoot_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select the expected duration of the shoot (e.g., 1 Hour, 2 Hours, etc.).
* **Photography Package** (`package_selected`, `select_one`, required): Select the photography package you have chosen.
* **Package Price** (`package_price`, `number`, required): Enter the total price of the package.
* **Deposit Amount** (`deposit_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the deposit amount required to secure the booking.
* **Has Deposit Been Paid?** (`deposit_paid`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have paid the deposit.
* **Balance Due Date** (`balance_due_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date by which the remaining balance is due.
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional services you would like to request.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): Enter any special requests or requirements.
* **Photo Usage Rights** (`usage_rights`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like your photos to be used (e.g., Personal Use Only, etc.).
* **May Photos Be Used in Portfolio?** (`photographer_portfolio`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you give permission for photos to be used in the photographer's portfolio.
* **I Understand the Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand the 48-hour cancellation policy.
* **I Understand the Photo Delivery Timeline** (`delivery_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand the photo delivery timeline.
* **I Understand the Liability Limitations** (`liability_clause`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand the photographer's liability limitations.
* **I Agree to All Terms and Conditions** (`contract_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to all terms and conditions.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of signing the contract.
* **Client Electronic Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the contract by entering your full name.
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name if you are a business or organization.
* **Photographer Signature** (`photographer_signature`, `text`, optional): Sign the contract by entering your name if you are a business or organization.

## Tips

* Double-check all fields to ensure accuracy before submitting.
* Use the "Help" button if you have any questions or concerns.
* Contact the photographer if you need clarification on any field or term.
* Review and understand all terms and conditions before signing the contract.
