# policy-renewal-communication-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for policy renewal communication purposes, intended for use by policy owners, administrators, or agents to update and communicate policy information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have the required information and access to necessary fields.
2. Complete each field with the appropriate information:
	* **Customer Info**: Enter the customer's contact information.
	* **Policy Number**: Enter the policy number, which is a required field.
	* **Policy Date**: Enter the policy start date.
	* **Contact Info**: Select the desired contact method for the customer (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
	* **Renewal Date**: Enter the policy renewal date.
	* **Renewal Time**: Enter the policy renewal time. (Optional)
	* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the policy.
	* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address, which is a required field.
	* **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number, which is a required field.
	* **Assigned Agent**: Select the agent assigned to the policy (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3). (Required)
	* **Policy Type**: Select the policy type (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3). (Required)
	* **Follow Up**: Select the desired follow-up method for the policy (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3). (Required)
	* **Next Step**: Select the next step for the policy (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3). (Required)
	* **Policy Owner**: Select the policy owner (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3). (Required)
	* **Policy Status**: Select the policy status (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3). (Required)
	* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the policy. (Optional)
3. Review your completed form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: Customer contact information.
* **Policy Number**: The unique identifier for the policy, required for policy renewal communication.
* **Policy Date**: The start date of the policy.
* **Contact Info**: Select the contact method for the customer (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* **Renewal Date**: The date to renew the policy.
* **Renewal Time**: The time to renew the policy, optional. 
* **Notes**: Additional comments or notes for the policy.
* **Email**: The customer's email address, a required field for communication.
* **Phone**: The customer's phone number, a required field.
* **Assigned Agent**: The agent assigned to handle the policy, required.
* **Policy Type**: The type of policy (e.g., insurance, loan, or contract).
* **Follow Up**: The method to follow up on the policy (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* **Next Step**: The next action or step to take on the policy.
* **Policy Owner**: The owner of the policy.
* **Policy Status**: The status of the policy (e.g., active, inactive, or pending).
* **Notes**: Additional comments or notes about the policy. 
* **Next Step**: This field is not visible in the form as it seems to be an extra field.
