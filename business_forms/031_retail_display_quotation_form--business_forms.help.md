# retail_display_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information and quotation details for retail display purposes. It includes fields for customer name, email, phone number, quotation request, quotation details, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Select the correct options for "Quotation Request" and "Payment Method" fields according to your needs.
3. Fill in the customer's name, email, and phone number accurately.
4. Enter the required quotation details in the "Display Quotation Details" field.
5. Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their identification or as they prefer to be addressed.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address where you can contact them.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number where they can be reached.
* **Quotation Request** (`quotation_request`, select_one, required): Select the type of quotation you are preparing for the customer (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Display Quotation Details** (`display_quotation_details`, text, required): Enter the essential details about the quotation, such as the product(s) being quoted, price, and any other relevant information.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Select the payment method(s) you will use for the quotation (e.g., Option A, Option B, Option C, or Option D).
