# Content Creation Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Content Creation Payment Form is a tool used to record and track payments received for content creation services. It helps to ensure that all financial transactions are accurately documented and accounted for. This form is used to collect information from clients, including their details, payment details, and confirmation of services rendered.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the type of content creation service rendered (e.g., Photography, Videography, etc.).
2. Enter the client's name and identification number.
3. Enter the client's email address.
4. Enter the total payment amount and date of payment.
5. Choose the preferred payment method (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, etc.).
6. Confirm that the payment has been made.
7. Provide any additional comments or questions.
8. Sign and confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the client as it appears on their identification document or ID card.
* **Client Identification Number** (`client_id`, text, required): Enter the client's identification number as it appears on their identification document or ID card.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Content Creation Service Rendered** (`service_rendered`, select_one, required): Select the type of content creation service rendered (e.g., Photography, Videography, etc.).
* **Total Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the total payment amount received.
* **Date of Payment** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of payment.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred payment method (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, etc.).
* **Payment Confirmed?** (`payment_confirmed`, select_one, required): Confirm that the payment has been made.
* **Any questions or comments?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or questions.
* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate** (`signature_confirmation`, select_one, required): Sign and confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Date of Payment Receipt** (`date_of_receipt`, date, required): Enter the date of payment receipt.

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled out in order to submit the form. Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submission.
