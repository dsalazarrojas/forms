# Financial Process Innovation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to register financial process innovation programs, initiatives, or challenges within the organization. It captures information from the project lead and their contact details to track and follow up on these innovations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of financial process innovation: Business Process Improvement, Challenge, or Internal Improvement Initiative.
2. Enter the project title.
3. Enter your name and title as the project lead.
4. Enter your first name and last name (if providing contact information).
5. Enter your email address.
6. (Optional) Enter your phone number and department (if applicable).
7. Review your entry to ensure all required fields are filled in before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Finance Innovation Program**:
  * Select the type of financial process innovation your project represents.
  * This field is required to ensure accurate tracking and categorization of your project.
* **Project Title**: 
  * Enter a concise and descriptive title for your project.
  * This field is required to help identify and differentiate your project.
* **Project Lead**:
  * Enter your name and title as the lead for your project.
  * This field is required to ensure we can contact and follow up with you.
* **First Name**:
  * Enter your first name (if you are providing contact information).
  * This field is optional and only required if you are providing your contact information.
* **Last Name**:
  * Enter your last name (if you are providing contact information).
  * This field is optional and only required if you are providing your contact information.
* **Email**:
  * Enter your email address for contact purposes.
  * This field is required to ensure we can reach out to you about your project.
* **Phone**:
  * Enter your phone number (if you are providing contact information).
  * This field is optional and only required if you are providing your contact information.
* **Department**:
  * Enter your department name (if you are providing contact information).
  * This field is optional and only required if you are providing your contact information.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submission.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the instructions above or contact your administrator for clarification.
- This form is intended to track and follow up on financial process innovations within the organization. Please ensure your entry is accurate and up-to-date.
