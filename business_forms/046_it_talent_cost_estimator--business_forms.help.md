# IT Talent Cost Estimator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Talent Cost Estimator form is a tool to help you quickly estimate the costs associated with talent acquisition or talent-related expenses. It helps you categorize and identify potential costs based on the type of role, location, and duration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options for "role" to indicate the type of role you are estimating costs for.
2. Choose the "location" where the role will be located.
3. Select the "duration" for which you want to estimate the costs.
4. Decide if "cost estimate" is required or not.
5. Review and submit your selections to get your estimated costs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **role** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
Select the type of role you are estimating costs for, such as software developer, project manager, or data scientist.
* **location** (`select_one`, required: false)
Choose the location where the role will be located, such as New York, Los Angeles, or Singapore.
* **duration** (`select_one`, required: false)
Select the duration for which you want to estimate the costs, such as week, month, or quarter.
* **cost estimate** (`number`, required: false)
Decide if a cost estimate is required, and if so, enter a numerical value for the estimated cost.
