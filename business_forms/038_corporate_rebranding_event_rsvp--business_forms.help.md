# corporate_rebranding_event_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to RSVP to the corporate rebranding event. Please fill out this form to indicate your attendance and any necessary details related to the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the event in the **Event Name** field.
2. Provide your **Guest Count**, if applicable.
3. Enter your **Company Name**, if you are attending on behalf of your company.
4. Indicate your role in the **Role** field, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
5. Select the type of event in the **Event Type** field, such as Conference, Meeting, or Seminar.
6. Enter the date of the event in the **Date** field.
7. Enter the time of the event in the **Time** field.
8. Provide the location of the event in the **Location** field.
9. If you have any special requests, enter them in the **Special Request** field.
10. Enter your **Email** address and **Phone** number for contact purposes.
11. Optionally, add any additional **Comments** about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Event Name**
* Label: Event Name
* Type: text
* Purpose: Enter the name of the event you are RSVPing to. This will help the event organizers know what to expect.

### **Guest Count**
* Label: Guest Count
* Type: number
* Purpose: If you are attending with a group, enter the number of guests you will be bringing. This is optional, but helps the event organizers plan accordingly.

### **Company Name**
* Label: Company Name
* Type: text
* Purpose: If you are attending on behalf of your company, enter the name of your company. This helps the event organizers keep track of company attendance.

### **Role**
* Label: Role
* Type: select_one
* Purpose: Indicate your role in the event, such as 'Yes' if you are attending or 'No' if not. This helps the event organizers keep track of attendance and invitations.

### **Event Type**
* Label: Event Type
* Type: select_multiple
* Purpose: Select the type of event, such as Conference, Meeting, or Seminar. This helps the event organizers tailor the event to your needs.

### **Date**
* Label: Date
* Type: date
* Purpose: Enter the date of the event. Make sure to enter the date in the correct format.

### **Time**
* Label: Time
* Type: time
* Purpose: Enter the time of the event. This helps the event organizers plan for the event duration.

### **Location**
* Label: Location
* Type: text
* Purpose: Enter the location of the event. This helps the event organizers plan for venue and logistics.

### **Special Request**
* Label: Special Request
* Type: note
* Purpose: If you have any special requests or needs, enter them in this field. This helps the event organizers accommodate your requests.

### **Email**
* Label: Email
* Type: email
* Purpose: Enter your email address for contact purposes. This helps the event organizers contact you about the event.

### **Phone**
* Label: Phone
* Type: text
* Purpose: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This helps the event organizers contact you about the event.

### **Comments**
* Label: Comments
* Type: text
* Purpose: Add any additional comments or notes about the event. This helps the event organizers tailor the event to your needs.
