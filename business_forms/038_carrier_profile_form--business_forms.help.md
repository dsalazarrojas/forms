# Carrier Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Carrier Profile Form is a critical document used by carriers to provide information about their company. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we have the correct information about your company.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please make sure you have the necessary permissions and authority to complete this form on behalf of your company.
2.  The form is divided into different sections that cover various aspects of your company profile, including company information, contact details, fleet and equipment, operational preferences, and safety and compliance.
3.  Answer each question carefully and accurately, and make sure to provide all required information.
4.  Review your submission before submitting it to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name**: Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards or marketing materials.
    *   This field is required.
*   **DOT and MC Number**: Enter your company's DOT (Department of Transportation) and MC (Motor Carrier) Number, if applicable.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Tax ID or EIN**: Enter your company's Tax ID or EIN (Employer Identification Number) as issued by the IRS.
    *   This field is required.
*   **SCAC Code**: If your company has a SCAC (Standard Carrier Alpha Code) code, enter it here. Otherwise, leave it blank.
*   **Main Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person in charge of your company's operations.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the main contact person.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the main contact person.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Dispatch Contact**: Enter the name of the person in charge of truck scheduling.
*   **Dispatch Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person in charge of dispatch.
*   **Number of Tractors**: Enter the total number of power units in your fleet.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Number of Trailers**: Enter the total number of trailers in your fleet.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Equipment Specialties**: Select all the types of equipment your company specializes in.
    *   Examples: Flatbed, Dry Van, Reefer, Step Deck, RGN, Intermodal, Auto Hauler.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Trailer Features**: Select all the trailer features your company has in their fleet.
    *   Examples: Air Ride, E-Track, Vented, Lift Gate, Logs, Straps.
*   **Preferred Lanes and Corridors**: List major routes or states your company prefers.
*   **Home Terminal Location**: Enter the city and state of your home terminal.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Do you offer LTL (Less Than Truckload) services?**: Select 'True' if your company offers LTL services, 'False' otherwise.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Latest Safety Rating**: Select the latest safety rating of your company from FMCSA (Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration).
    *   Options: Satisfactory, Conditional, Unsatisfactory, Unrated.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Are you a SmartWay partner?**: Select 'True' if your company is a SmartWay partner, 'False' otherwise.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Digital Signature**: Type your name as the digital signature.
    *   This field is required.

Please note that all fields with a star (*) symbol are required and must be completed accurately and thoroughly.
