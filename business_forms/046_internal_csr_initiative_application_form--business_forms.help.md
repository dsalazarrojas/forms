# Internal CSR Initiative Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Internal CSR Initiative Application Form is designed to gather essential information about a project, its goals, resources, budget, start and end dates, location, and management structure.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter Project Title**: Please enter a brief title for your project.
2. **What are your project goals?**: Describe the objectives and outcomes of your project.
3. **What impact will your project have on the environment?**: Explain how your project will positively impact the company's social responsibility.
4. **What resources do you need to complete the project?**: List all necessary resources (people, equipment, funds, etc.).
5. **What is the estimated budget for the project?**: Provide a detailed estimate of the project's budget.
6. **When do you hope to start the project?**: Enter the planned start date of your project.
7. **When do you hope to finish the project?**: Enter the planned end date of your project.
8. **Where will you be conducting this project?**: Specify the location where the project will take place.
9. **Who is the project manager?**: Enter the name and title of the project manager.
10. **What is the project manager's phone number?**: Provide the phone number of the project manager.
11. **What is the project manager's email?**: Enter the email address of the project manager.
12. **What is the project manager's title?**: Specify the title of the project manager.
13. **What department does the project manager belong to?**: Enter the department where the project manager works.
14. **Who is the project manager's manager?**: Enter the name and title of the project manager's manager.
15. **What is the project manager's manager's phone number?**: Provide the phone number of the project manager's manager.
16. **What is the project manager's manager's email?**: Enter the email address of the project manager's manager.
17. **What is the project manager's manager's title?**: Specify the title of the project manager's manager.
18. **What department does the project manager's manager belong to?**: Enter the department where the project manager's manager works.
19. **Who is the project manager's manager's manager?**: Enter the name and title of the project manager's manager's manager.
20. **What is the project manager's manager's manager's phone number?**: Provide the phone number of the project manager's manager's manager.
21. **What is the project manager's manager's manager's email?**: Enter the email address of the project manager's manager's manager.
22. **What is the project manager's manager's manager's title?**: Specify the title of the project manager's manager's manager.
23. **What department does the project manager's manager's manager belong to?**: Enter the department where the project manager's manager's manager works.
24. **Who will be reviewing this project?**: Enter the name(s) and title(s) of the people reviewing this project.
25. **Is this project approved?**: Select the status of your project (Approved or Not Approved).
