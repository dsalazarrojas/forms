# Fencing Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fencing Material Order Form is designed for businesses to order fencing materials. This form gathers information about the business and their order details to facilitate the order process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the business name and contact information (name, first name, last name, email, phone number).
2. Select the type of material needed (Aluminum, Wood, PVC, Vinyl, Steel).
3. Enter the quantity of material needed.
4. Enter the order date and delivery date.
5. Add any additional notes about the order.
6. Select the quantity of material for each type of material needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Name** (`business_name`): Enter the name of your business.
- **First Name** (`contact_first_name`): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`): Enter your business email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your business phone number.
- **Material Type** (`material_type`): Select the type of material needed for the order.
- **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`): Enter the quantity of material needed for the order.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`): Enter the date when the order was placed.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`): Enter the date when the material is to be delivered.
- **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional information about the order.
- **Material 1** (`material_ordered_1`): Select the quantity of material 1 needed for the order.
- **Material 2** (`material_ordered_2`): Select the quantity of material 2 needed for the order.
- **Material 3** (`material_ordered_3`): Select the quantity of material 3 needed for the order.
- **Material 4** (`material_ordered_4`): Select the quantity of material 4 needed for the order.
- **Material 5** (`material_ordered_5`): Select the quantity of material 5 needed for the order.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Review your order details carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at [contact information].
