# Estate Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Management Form is designed to gather and store information about properties under management. This form will help you to quickly and efficiently enter details about each property, its manager, status, lease information, financials, and insurance-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the page where you wish to enter information for a property (e.g., "Estate Details").
2. Enter the property details, including the property type (e.g., Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
3. Fill in the address of the property.
4. Add any notes or comments about the property.
5. If applicable, enter the property manager's contact information.
6. If applicable, enter the property insurer's contact information.
7. Review and complete all other relevant fields as needed, including status, lease date, lease end date, rental amount, payment method, and payment terms.
8. Upload any relevant property photos or documents.
9. Enter the property manager and property insurer's email address and phone number if applicable.
10. Verify that all required fields are completed and that any necessary contact information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Estate Details** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the property.
* **Property Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of property (e.g., Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the property.
* **Property Manager** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the property manager.
* **Status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the current status of the property (e.g., Available, Sold, Rented).
* **Lease Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the lease.
* **Rental Amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the rental amount for the property.
* **Payment Method** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the payment methods for the property (e.g., Bank Transfer, Check, Cash).
* **Payment Terms** (Text, Optional): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Notes** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the property.
* **Property Photos** (Text, Optional): Upload any photos of the property.
* **Documents** (Text, Optional): Upload any relevant documents for the property.
* **Email Address** (Email, Optional): Enter the property manager or insurer's email address.
* **Phone Numbers** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the phone numbers for the property manager or insurer (e.g., Home, Work, Mobile).
* **Phone Number 1, 2, 3** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone numbers for the property manager or insurer.
* **Property Manager Contact Info** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional contact information for the property manager.
* **Property Insurer Contact Info** (Text, Optional): Enter any contact information for the property insurer.
* **Property Insurer Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number for the property insurer.
* **Property Insurer Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address for the property insurer.
* **Property Insurer Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address for the property insurer.
* **Property Insurer Notes** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the property insurer.
