# Complaint Resolution Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track complaints that have been resolved in a business setting. It captures essential information about the complaint, including details of the incident, resolution, and customer satisfaction with the outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a unique ID for the report.
2. Provide the full name of the person who filed the initial complaint.
3. Enter the original intake date of the complaint.
4. Enter the date when the final resolution was accepted.
5. Calculate the total number of calendar days from intake to closure.
6. Briefly describe the final outcome for the customer.
7. Select the customer's satisfaction level from the provided options.
8. Determine and describe the underlying cause of the complaint.
9. Describe what steps were taken to prevent recurrence.
10. Enter the name of the person who finalized this report.
11. Enter the date this report was reviewed and closed by leadership.
12. Review and confirm that the case is closed.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to maintain a clear and accurate record of complaints and their resolutions.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Review the form before submitting it to leadership for review and approval.
- This form is intended for internal use and should not be shared with customers or external parties.
