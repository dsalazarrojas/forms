# Material Reallocation Approval Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Material Reallocation Approval Request" form is used to request approval for the reallocation of materials from one location to another. This form helps to formalize the process, ensuring that all necessary details are documented and reviewed by the relevant stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether the reallocation involves "Yes" or "No" material(s).
2. Enter the quantity of the material(s) being reallocated.
3. Indicate whether the reallocation involves different locations or not ("Yes" or "No").
4. Provide a reason for the material reallocation.
5. Specify whether the approval is required for the reallocation ("Yes" or "No").
6. Enter the name of the material(s) being reallocated.
7. Choose a start date for the reallocation.
8. Choose an end date for the reallocation.
9. Add any additional notes or comments regarding the reallocation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **material_type** (Select Multiple, required: no): This field allows you to specify whether the reallocation involves material types such as 'Yes' or 'No'. Selecting "Yes" indicates that the reallocation affects one or more material types. 
* **quantity** (Number, required: no): Enter the quantity of material(s) being reallocated.
* **locations** (Select Multiple, required: no): Check if the reallocation involves different locations by selecting "Yes" or "No". 
* **reason_for_reallocation** (Text, required: no): Provide a clear reason for the reallocation. This could be due to stock update, inventory management, or production planning. 
* **approval** (Select Multiple, required: no): Specify whether approval is required for the reallocation ("Yes" or "No"). 
* **material_name** (Text, required: no): Enter the name of the material(s) being reallocated. 
* **start_date** (Date, required: no): Choose a start date for the reallocation. 
* **end_date** (Date, required: no): Choose an end date for the reallocation. 
* **notes** (Note, required: no): Add any additional comments or notes about the reallocation. 

## Tips

* Make sure to provide a clear reason for the reallocation to ensure that the approvers can make an informed decision.
* If the reallocation involves multiple locations, ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access those locations.
* If approval is required, ensure that you have the necessary approvals before proceeding with the reallocation.
* Review and revise your request thoroughly before submitting it for approval.
