# document_editing_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The document editing submission form is used to collect information from end-users on their document editing submissions. This form gathers data on user input, submission type, document type, date and time submitted, note, email, and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the required fields and provide the necessary information. The form does not require all fields to be filled out, so you can choose which ones to complete. Please note that providing incomplete or missing data may affect the processing and analysis of your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input (text)**: Enter your input regarding the document you are submitting.
- **Submission Type (select_one)**: Indicate whether your submission is for a "Yes" or "No".
- **Document Type (select_multiple)**: Check all the types of documents you are submitting.
- **Date Submitted (date)**: Enter the date you submitted the document.
- **Time Submitted (time)**: Enter the time you submitted the document.
- **Note (note)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your submission.
- **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
- **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- If you have any additional questions or concerns about this form, please contact us.
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- Providing incomplete or missing data may affect the processing and analysis of your submission.
