# waiver_brow_lamination_tint - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information related to a waiver of liability, specifically for brow lamination tinting. It is used to gather details about the form title, category, description, liabilities, risks, signature, and contact information of the issuer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Form Title** in the text field.
2. Select the **Category** from the provided options.
3. Enter the **Description** of the waiver.
4. Fill in **Liability 1**, **Liability 2**, and **Liability 3** fields with relevant information.
5. Input **Risk 1**, **Risk 2**, and **Risk 3** values as numbers.
6. Enter your **Signature**.
7. Provide the **Date** and **Time** of the waiver.
8. Enter your **Issuer**, **Company**, **Position**, and **Address**.
9. Enter the **City**, **State**, and **ZIP**.
10. Select your **Country** from the provided options.
11. Enter your **Phone** and **Fax** numbers.
12. Provide your **Email** address.
13. Add any additional **Note** if necessary.
14. Finally, select the **Submit** option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title (1)** (text, optional): Enter the title of the form.
* **Category (2)** (select_one, optional): Select a category for the form.
* **Description (3)** (text, optional): Enter a description of the form.
* **Liability 1 (4)** (text, optional): Input liability information.
* **Liability 2 (5)** (text, optional): Input liability information.
* **Liability 3 (6)** (text, optional): Input liability information.
* **Risk 1 (7)** (number, optional): Input risk value as a number.
* **Risk 2 (8)** (number, optional): Input risk value as a number.
* **Risk 3 (9)** (number, optional): Input risk value as a number.
* **Signature (10)** (text, optional): Enter your signature.
* **Date (11)** (date, optional): Provide the date of the waiver.
* **Time (12)** (time, optional): Provide the time of the waiver.
* **Issuer (13)** (text, optional): Enter your issuer information.
* **Company (14)** (text, optional): Enter your company information.
* **Position (15)** (text, optional): Enter your position information.
* **Address (16)** (text, optional): Enter your address.
* **City (17)** (text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State (18)** (text, optional): Enter your state.
* **ZIP (19)** (text, optional): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Country (20)** (text, optional): Select your country.
* **Phone (21)** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Fax (22)** (text, optional): Enter your fax number.
* **Email (23)** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Note (24)** (note, optional): Add any additional note.
* **Submit (25)** (select_one, optional): Select the submit option.
