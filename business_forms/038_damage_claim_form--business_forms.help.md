# Damage Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for damage claims. Please fill it out with accurate and complete details about the damage incident, asset involved, and claimant.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please ensure all required fields (indicated by *) are completed.
* Enter the date and time of the damage incident accurately.
* Provide a clear description of the damage incident.
* List all relevant witness information, including witness names, contact details, and availability.
* Enter the claimant's details, including name, address, contact method, and contact number.
* Answer the claimant's inquiries accurately, if applicable.
* Attach supporting documents, if relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Details**: Enter a brief description of the damage incident.
* **Date Of Damage**: Enter the date and time of the damage incident.
* **Time Of Damage**: Enter the time of the damage incident.
* **Damage Description**: Provide a clear description of the damage incident.
* **Asset Name**: Enter the name of the asset involved in the damage incident.
* **Asset Value**: Enter the value of the asset involved in the damage incident.
* **Insurance Info**: Enter any relevant insurance information related to the damage incident.
* **Witness Details**: Select all witnesses who witnessed the damage incident from the provided options.
* **Witness Names**: Enter the names of all witnesses who witnessed the damage incident.
* **Witness Contacts**: Enter the contact details of all witnesses who witnessed the damage incident.
* **Witness Relationships**: Enter the relationships of all witnesses who witnessed the damage incident.
* **Witness Availability**: Enter the availability of all witnesses who witnessed the damage incident.
* **Claimant Details**: Enter the details of the claimant.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the claimant.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the claimant.
* **Contact Details**: Select the contact method and details of the claimant.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the claimant.
* **Claimant Relationships**: Enter the relationships of the claimant.
* **Claimant Availability**: Enter the availability of the claimant.
* **Claimant Inquiries**: Select the claimant's inquiries from the provided options.
* **Claimant Comments**: Enter any additional comments from the claimant.
* **Supporting Documents**: Attach any supporting documents related to the damage incident.
* **Witness Statement**: Enter a statement from any witness who witnessed the damage incident.
* **Claimant Statement**: Enter a statement from the claimant.
* **Claimant Statement**: Enter any additional comments from the claimant.

Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, and do not hesitate to reach out to your authorized contact if you have any questions or concerns.
