# Property Viewing Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Viewing Screening Form collects relevant information from a property viewer or tenant about their interests and availability for a specific property. This information helps property managers or agents to better understand the viewer's or tenant's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, indicated by the asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your name in the "Tenant Name" field.
3. Select your contact preferences for the property viewing opportunity.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose the date and time you are available to view the property.
6. If applicable, enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the property viewing opportunity in the "Note" field.
8. Select your interests related to the property using the "Tenant Interests" field.
9. Enter the property ID and User ID if required.
10. Choose your user type.
11. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
12. Select the status of the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as the tenant or property viewer.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how you would like to be contacted (Yes/No).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date Available** (`date_available`, date, optional): Choose a date you are available to view the property.
* **Time Available** (`time_available`, time, optional): Choose a time you are available to view the property.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the property viewing opportunity.
* **Tenant Interests** (`tenant_interests`, select_one, optional): Select your interests related to the property (Yes/No).
* **Property ID** (`property_id`, number, optional): Enter the property ID if required.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, number, optional): Enter the User ID if required.
* **User Type** (`user_type`, select_one, optional): Choose your user type.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the property (Active/Inactive).
