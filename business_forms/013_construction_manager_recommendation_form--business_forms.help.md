# Construction Manager Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Manager Recommendation Form is a tool used to provide feedback on a construction manager's performance and abilities. This form helps assess their leadership, technical skills, and other key qualities that make them suitable for future projects. It is intended for individuals who have worked with or have a professional relationship with the construction manager being recommended.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the recommender's details in the first section, including their name, job title, and relationship to the manager.
2. Select the manager's level of proficiency in each skill area (leadership, technical skills, budgeting, communication, and scheduling) using the rating scale (1-5).
3. Provide additional comments or feedback about the manager's strengths and any specific situations worth noting.
4. Indicate whether you would recommend the manager for future projects and provide a date for the recommendation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name** (Text, Required): This field is for you to enter your full name.
* **Your Job Title and Organization** (Text, Required): Enter your job title and the name of your organization.
* **Your Professional Relationship to the Manager** (Select One, Required): Choose how you are related to the manager, such as Supervisor, Colleague/Peer, Subcontractor, Client/Project Owner, or Vendor/Supplier.
* **How long have you worked with this manager?** (Text, Required): Estimate the length of time you have worked with the manager.
* **Full Name of Construction Manager** (Text, Required): Enter the full name of the manager being recommended.
* **Project(s) where you worked together** (Text, Required): Describe the projects you have worked on with the manager.
* **Leadership and Team Management Skills** (Number, Required): Rate the manager's leadership and team management skills using the 1-5 scale.
* **Technical Construction Knowledge** (Number, Required): Rate the manager's technical construction knowledge using the 1-5 scale.
* **Budgeting and Financial Oversight** (Number, Required): Rate the manager's budgeting and financial oversight skills using the 1-5 scale.
* **Communication and Stakeholder Relations** (Number, Required): Rate the manager's communication and stakeholder relations skills using the 1-5 scale.
* **Project Scheduling and Time Management** (Number, Required): Rate the manager's project scheduling and time management skills using the 1-5 scale.
* **Prioritizes Health and Safety Standards** (Number, Required): Rate the manager's prioritization of health and safety standards using the 1-5 scale.
* **What is this manager's single greatest professional strength?** (Text, Required): Describe the manager's greatest strength.
* **Would you recommend this manager for future projects?** (Select One, Required): Choose whether you would recommend the manager for future projects, with options for Yes - Highly Recommended, Yes - With Reservations, or No.
* **Additional Comments or Testimonial** (Text, Optional): Provide any specific achievements or situations worth noting.
* **Date of Recommendation** (Date, Required): Enter the date you are making this recommendation.
