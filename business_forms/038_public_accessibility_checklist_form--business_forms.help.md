# public_accessibility_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public_accessibility_checklist_form is designed to help businesses and organizations assess their facilities' accessibility features for individuals with disabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and answer the questions regarding the accessibility features of your facility. For each question, please indicate whether the feature is available or not. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Accessibility** (id: 1): This section checks for basic accessibility features such as wheelchair ramps, automatic doors, and accessible entrances.
* **Entrance and Pathway Accessibility** (id: 2): This section assesses the accessibility of entrance and pathway areas, including clear floor space, accessible doors, and smooth paths for wheelchairs and mobility scooters.
* **Seating and Dining Areas Accessibility** (id: 3): This section evaluates the accessibility of seating and dining areas, including accessible tables, seating, and accessible restrooms.
* **Accessible Toilets and Showers** (id: 4): This section checks for accessible toilets and showers, including grab bars, emergency alarms, and non-slip flooring.
* **Accessible Doors and Halls** (id: 5): This section assesses the accessibility of doors and halls, including automatic door openers, clear door widths, and door thresholds.
* **Accessible Restrooms** (id: 6): This section evaluates the accessibility of restrooms, including grab bars, emergency alarms, and non-slip flooring.
* **Elevator Accessibility** (id: 7): This section checks for accessible elevators, including wheelchair-accessible elevators, button height and width, and door opening and closing speeds.
* **Accessible Parking** (id: 8): This section assesses the accessibility of parking areas, including accessible parking spaces, accessible parking signs, and accessible pathways to entrance.
* **Public Accessibility Statement** (id: 9): This section requires a statement regarding your facility's accessibility policy and procedures.
* **Emergency Evacuation** (id: 10): This section checks for accessible emergency evacuation procedures and accessible evacuation routes.
* **Accessible Services** (id: 11): This section evaluates the accessibility of services such as accessible customer service counters, ATMs, and ticket machines.
* **Accessible Building Features** (id: 12): This section assesses the accessibility of building features such as accessible building entrance, accessible corridors, and accessible building amenities.
* **Accessible Electrical Outlets** (id: 13): This section checks for accessible electrical outlets, including outlet height and width, and cord protection.
* **Fire Safety Features** (id: 14): This section evaluates the fire safety features, including fire alarms, fire extinguishers, and fire evacuation procedures.
* **Emergency Services** (id: 15): This section assesses the emergency services available, including accessible fire alarm call boxes and emergency phone numbers.
* **Accessible Lifts and Elevators** (id: 16): This section checks for accessible lifts and elevators, including wheelchair-accessible elevators and button height and width.
* **Accessible Telephones** (id: 17): This section evaluates the accessibility of telephones, including accessible telephone height and volume control.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (id: 18): This section assesses the availability of fire extinguishers, including their location and accessibility.
* **Wheelchair Accessible Paths** (id: 19): This section checks for wheelchair-accessible paths, including clear floor space, smooth paths, and avoidances of obstacles.
* **Accessible Stairways** (id: 20): This section evaluates the accessibility of stairways, including accessible stair railings and clear floor space.
* **Accessible Doors** (id: 21): This section assesses the accessibility of doors, including automatic door openers, clear door widths, and door thresholds.
* **Emergency Call Boxes** (id: 22): This section checks for emergency call boxes, including their location and accessibility.
* **Accessible Lights and Signs** (id: 23): This section evaluates the accessibility of lights and signs, including high-contrast colors and accessible sign height and spacing.

Note: This is a basic checklist. If you have any doubt or uncertainty, please consult your local accessibility regulations or contact your local accessibility expert.
