# Machine Health Tracking Partner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide your business information for potential partnership opportunities. This information will be reviewed internally to assess compatibility and potential for collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your business name and address to help us understand your location and contact information.
2. Select your business category from the options provided.
3. Enter your contact name and phone number to ensure we can get in touch with you.
4. Provide a brief description of your business to give us a better understanding of your services or products.
5. Finally, choose your business type from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Name**: A required field to identify your business and help us understand your identity. Please enter your full business name.
* **Business Category**: Select one of the options provided to categorize your business. This helps us understand your industry or sector.
* **Business Address**: Enter your business address to help us locate you and understand your location.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name, and we'll use it to get in touch with you.
* **Phone**: Enter your business phone number to ensure we can reach you for further communication.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your business to facilitate communication.
* **Business Description**: Briefly describe your business to give us an understanding of your services or products.
* **Business Type**: Select one of the options provided to categorize your business type.
