# Gdpr Opt In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals about their consent to be contacted regarding GDPR-related matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred response to "Opt In" question.
2. If you choose to opt in, please provide your contact information (if applicable) and confirm your consent.
3. Review and agree to our agreement terms, which outline how we will handle your information under GDPR.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Opt In** (`optin`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you want to opt in or not by selecting one or more options from the list.
* **Contact Info** (`contact`, text, required: false): If you opt in, you can provide your contact information (name and address) to allow us to contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you prefer to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required: false): Confirm your consent to be contacted by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or requests, please provide them here.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, note, required: false): Review and acknowledge that we will keep your information private under GDPR regulations.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, text, required: false): Review our agreement terms, which outline how we will handle your information under GDPR.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are completing this form only if you have a legitimate interest in our services or products.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact us.
