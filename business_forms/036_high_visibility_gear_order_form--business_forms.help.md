# High Visibility Gear Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The High Visibility Gear Order Form is a tool for employees to request high visibility gear for their work. It helps to gather essential information about the gear ordered, ensuring that the correct gear is delivered to the right person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category of high visibility gear you need (e.g., clothing, accessories).
2. Specify the quantity of gear you require.
3. Choose the color and size of the gear you need.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
5. Select the date and time when you need the gear delivered.
6. You can add any additional comments or notes about your order in the "High Visibility Gear Order Form Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category** (High Visibility Clothing, High Visibility Accessories): Select the type of high visibility gear you are requesting.
- **Quantity** (number): Enter the number of gear you need.
- **Color** (Red, Blue, Black): Choose the color of the high visibility gear you require.
- **Size** (Large, Medium, Small): Select the size of the high visibility gear you need.
- **Email** (email address): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (phone number): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Date** (date): Select the date when you need the gear delivered.
- **Time** (time): Select the time when you need the gear delivered.
- **High Visibility Gear Order Form Comments** (optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
- **Assigned Tool** (chatjimmy, chatjimmy2): Select the person assigned to fulfill your order.
