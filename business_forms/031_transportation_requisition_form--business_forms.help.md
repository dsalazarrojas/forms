# Transportation Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and communicate transportation-related information for official business purposes. It gathers essential details about a trip, including travel dates, times, and destinations, as well as the type of vehicle used and the contact person responsible for the trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Trip Details**: Start by filling in the "Trip Details" field with a brief description of the trip.
2. **Specify Travel From and To**: Enter the departure and arrival locations of the trip in the "Travel From" and "Travel To" fields.
3. **Select Travel Date and Time**: Use the calendar or time picker to select the travel date and time in the "Travel Date" and "Travel Time" fields.
4. **Choose Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle used for the trip from the options: Car, Bus, or Train.
5. **Enter Vehicle Capacity**: If necessary, enter the capacity of the vehicle used.
6. **Provide Travel Reason**: Briefly explain the purpose of the trip.
7. **Add Contact Person Information**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the trip, including relevant details about the purpose and duration of the trip.
* **Travel From** (`travel_from`, text, optional): Enter the location from which you will be traveling.
* **Travel To** (`travel_to`, text, optional): Enter the destination of your trip.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, optional): Select the date of your trip using the calendar or date picker.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, optional): Select the time of your trip.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of vehicle used for the trip from the options: Car, Bus, or Train.
* **Vehicle Capacity** (`vehicle_capacity`, number, optional): Enter the capacity of the vehicle used, if necessary.
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, text, optional): Briefly explain the reason for the trip.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the trip.
