# Confidentiality Agreement Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Confidentiality Agreement Release Form is used to release information that was previously considered confidential. It helps ensure that the information is released properly and that all parties involved understand their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field carefully and accurately, paying attention to any specific instructions or requirements for each field.
2. Make sure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. If a field is not required, it can be left blank, but be aware that omitting required fields may affect the validity of the release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name (1)** (`requestor_name`, text, required): Enter your complete name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address (2)** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number (3)** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address (4)** (`address`, text, required): Enter your full address including street number and street name.
* **City (5)** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city of residence.
* **State (6)** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state of residence.
* **ZIP Code (7)** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Organization (8)** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization you represent.
* **Job Title (9)** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Release Type (10)** (`release_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of release you're requesting, such as Full Release from NDA, Partial Release, Time-Limited Release, Purpose-Specific Release, or Third-Party Disclosure.
* **Original Agreement Date (11)** (`original_agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the confidentiality agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Reference Number (12)** (`agreement_reference`, text, optional): If a contract ID is available, enter it here.
* **Information to be Released (13)** (`information_to_release`, text, required): Specify the information you're requesting to be released.
* **Reason for Release Request (14)** (`release_reason`, text, required): Explain why you're requesting a release.
* **Scope of Release (15)** (`release_scope`, select_one, required): Indicate the scope of the release, such as Specific Documents Only, Specific Topic Area, All Related Information, or Unlimited Release.
* **Third Party Recipient (16)** (`third_party_recipient`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the third-party recipient.
* **Third Party Relationship (17)** (`third_party_relationship`, text, optional): If applicable, explain why the third party is involved.
* **Release Duration (18)** (`release_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of the release, such as One-Time Disclosure, 30 Days, 90 Days, 1 Year, or Indefinite.
* **Intended Use of Information (19)** (`intended_use`, text, required): Describe what you plan to do with the released information.
* **Further Disclosure Planned (20)** (`further_disclosure`, select_one, required): Indicate if you plan to share the information further, with specified parties, or publicly.
* **Specified Parties (21)** (`specified_parties`, text, optional): If you choose to share with specified parties, enter their names here.
* **Security Measures (22)** (`security_measures`, text, required): Describe the security measures you'll take to protect the released information.
* **Return or Destruction Plan (23)** (`return_destruction`, select_one, required): Choose the plan for returning or destroying the released information, such as Return After Use, Destroy After Use, Retain Indefinitely, or As Directed.
* **Compensation (24)** (`compensation`, text, optional): If any payment is involved, describe it here.
* **Liability Acceptance (25)** (`liability_acceptance`, select_one, required): Accept or reject liability for any damages.
* **Legal Review (26)** (`legal_review`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you've received legal advice on the release.
* **Attorney Name (27)** (`attorney_name`, text, optional): If you've had legal advice, enter your attorney's name here.
* **Understanding Statement (28)** (`understanding_statement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the implications of the release.
* **Voluntary Request (29)** (`voluntary_request`, select_one, required): Confirm that this is a voluntary request or required by circumstances.
* **Release Authorization (30)** (`release_authorization`, select_one, required): Authorize the release of information.
* **Date of Authorization (31)** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the current date.
* **Authorizing Party Name (32)** (`authorizing_party`, text, required): Enter the name of the party authorizing the release.
* **Authorizing Party Title (33)** (`authorizing_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the party authorizing the release.
