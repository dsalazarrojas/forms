## Chloe's Care - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from customers for the purpose of providing personalized skincare recommendations and services. The form helps us understand your skin type, concerns, goals, and preferences, allowing us to offer tailored advice and products to improve your skin health.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Information** with your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide your **First Name** and **Last Name** to help us get to know you better.
3. Enter your **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, and **Shipping Address** for contact and delivery purposes.
4. Choose your **Date of Birth** (optional) for personalized recommendations.
5. Describe your **Skin Profile** (optional) to help us understand your skin concerns and preferences.
6. Select your **Skin Type** (Normal, Dry, Oily, Combination, Sensitive, or Not sure).
7. Check all the **Main Skin Concerns** that apply to you (e.g., Acne or breakouts, Fine lines or wrinkles, Dark spots or hyperpigmentation, etc.).
8. Choose your **Skincare Goals** that you'd like to achieve (e.g., Clearer skin, Anti-aging, Brightening, etc.).
9. List any **Current Skincare Routine** you have, including products and frequency.
10. Choose how often you follow a skincare routine (Twice daily, Once daily, Few times a week, Rarely, Never).
11. Select your **Product Preferences** (e.g., Cleanser, Toner, Serum, Moisturizer, etc.).
12. Choose all the **Ingredients Preferences** that apply to you (e.g., Natural ingredients, Organic, Vegan, etc.).
13. Check if you have any **Allergies or Sensitivities** to certain ingredients.
14. Select your **Budget Range** (Under $50, $50-$100, $101-$200, etc.).
15. Choose your **Order Type** (One-time purchase, Subscription, Gift set, etc.).
16. List any **Products Ordered**.
17. Enter the **Quantity** of items ordered.
18. If this is a gift, write a **Gift Message** for the recipient.
19. Choose your **Shipping Preferences** (Standard shipping, Express shipping, etc.).
20. Select your **Shipping Method** (Pick up in person, Standard shipping, etc.).
21. Enter any **Special Delivery Instructions**.
22. Choose how you will **Pay** (Credit card, Debit card, PayPal, etc.).
23. Enter any **Promotional Code** you have.
24. If you'd like, request a free **Skincare Consultation**.
25. Choose your **Consultation Preference** (Phone call, Video call, Email, etc.).
26. Check if you'd like to **Join our Newsletter** for exclusive offers and tips.
27. Select if you'd like to receive **SMS Updates**.
28. Choose how you heard about **Chloe's Care** (Social media, Friend or family, etc.).
29. Enter any **Additional Comments** or questions.
30. Accept the **Terms and Conditions**.
31. Sign with a **Digital Signature**.
32. Set the **Order Date**.

* **Skin Type**: Select your skin type (Normal, Dry, Oily, Combination, Sensitive, or Not sure).
* **Main Skin Concerns**: Check all the concerns that apply to you (e.g., Acne or breakouts, Fine lines or wrinkles, etc.).

* **Ingredients Preferences**: Choose all the ingredients that apply to you (e.g., Natural ingredients, Organic, Vegan, etc.).
* **Allergies or Sensitivities**: Check if you have any allergies or sensitivities to certain ingredients.

* **Special Delivery Instructions**: Enter any special delivery instructions.

* **SMS Updates**: Check if you'd like to receive SMS updates.

* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or questions.
