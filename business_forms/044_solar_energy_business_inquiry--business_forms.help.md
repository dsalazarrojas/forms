# Solar Energy Business Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect business inquiry information from customers or potential customers interested in solar energy services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the First Name field with your name.
2. Enter your Last Name in the corresponding field.
3. Provide your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your Business Name, which is the name of your company or organization.
6. Choose the size of your company from the Company Size options (Small, Medium, Large).
7. Select the type of your business from the Company Type options (Solar Panel Installation, Solar Energy Consulting, Solar Energy Services).
8. Describe the services you offer in the Services Offered field.
9. Enter the Location of your business.
10. Provide the URL of your website in the Website field.
11. Share your social media handle in the Social Media field.
12. Write any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.
13. Choose how you submitted the form from the Submission Method options (Email, Phone, Form).
14. Select the version of the form you're using in the Form Version field.
15. Mark whether this form has been submitted before in the Form Submitted field.
16. Choose the status of the form from the Form Status options (New, Existing, Archived).
17. Enter the name of the customer you're representing in the Customer Name field.
18. Provide the customer's Email address in the Customer Email field.
19. Enter the customer's Phone number in the Customer Phone field.
20. Write any additional notes or comments about the customer in the Customer Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your Email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your Phone number.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, required): Enter the type of industry your business is in.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Choose the size of your company (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of your business (Solar Panel Installation, Solar Energy Consulting, Solar Energy Services).
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, text, required): Describe the services you offer.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter your business location.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required): Provide the URL of your website.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, text, required): Share your social media handle.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Write any additional notes or comments.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Choose how you submitted the form (Chat Jimmy, Chat John, Chat Jane).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Submission Method** (`submission_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you submitted the form (Email, Phone, Form).
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, required): Select the version of the form you're using.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, text, required): Mark whether this form has been submitted before.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the form (New, Existing, Archived).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the customer you're representing.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's Email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter the customer's Phone number.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, required): Write any additional notes or comments about the customer.
