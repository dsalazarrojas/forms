# daily-restaurant-operations-report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report on the daily operations of a restaurant, including key metrics on sales, expenses, and performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the restaurant's name in the "restaurant-name" field.
2. Enter the manager's name in the "manager-name" field.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for "staff-presented" to indicate whether staff are present.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for "staff-presents" to indicate whether staff are present.
5. Enter the number of staff present in the "staff-quantity" field.
6. Enter the total cash sales for the day in the "total-cash-sales" field.
7. Enter the total card sales for the day in the "total-card-sales" field.
8. Enter the total cash discounts for the day in the "total-cash-discounts" field.
9. Enter the total card discounts for the day in the "total-card-discounts" field.
10. Enter the total tax refunds for the day in the "total-tax-refunds" field.
11. Enter the total gift card discounts for the day in the "total-gift-card-discounts" field.
12. Enter the sales per hour for the day in the "sales-per-hour" field.
13. Enter the average sale amount for the day in the "average-sale" field.
14. Enter the average transaction amount for the day in the "average-transaction" field.
15. Enter the total sales for the day in the "total-sales" field.
16. Enter the total expenses for the day in the "total-expenses" field.
17. Enter the cost of goods sold for the day in the "cost-of-goods-sold" field.
18. Enter the cost of goods sold per average transaction in the "cost-of-goods-sold-per-avg-transaction" field.
19. Enter the cost of goods sold per average sale in the "cost-of-goods-sold-per-avg-sale" field.
20. Enter the cost of goods sold per sale in the "cost-of-goods-sold-per-sale" field.
21. Enter the profit per sale for the day in the "profit-per-sale" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **restaurant-name**: <i>Enter the name of the restaurant, e.g., "The Daily Grind".</i>
* **manager-name**: <i>Enter the name of the manager on duty, e.g., "John Doe".</i>
* **date**: <i>Enter the date of the report, e.g., "2022-01-01".</i>
* **total-seats**: <i>Enter the total number of seats in the restaurant, e.g., "100".</i>
* **tables**: <i>Enter the number of tables occupied, e.g., "50".</i>
* **occupied-seats**: <i>Enter the number of occupied seats, e.g., "75".</i>
* **staff-presented**: <i>Select "Yes" if staff are present, "No" otherwise.</i>
* **staff-presents**: <i>Select "Yes" if staff are present, "No" otherwise.</i>
* **staff-quantity**: <i>Enter the number of staff present, e.g., "10".</i>
* **total-cash-sales**: <i>Enter the total cash sales for the day, e.g., "1000.00".</i>
* **total-card-sales**: <i>Enter the total card sales for the day, e.g., "500.00".</i>
* **total-cash-discounts**: <i>Enter the total cash discounts for the day, e.g., "50.00".</i>
* **total-card-discounts**: <i>Enter the total card discounts for the day, e.g., "20.00".</i>
* **total-tax-refunds**: <i>Enter the total tax refunds for the day, e.g., "10.00".</i>
* **total-gift-card-discounts**: <i>Enter the total gift card discounts for the day, e.g., "15.00".</i>
* **sales-per-hour**: <i>Enter the sales per hour for the day, e.g., "500.00".</i>
* **average-sale**: <i>Enter the average sale amount for the day, e.g., "50.00".</i>
* **average-transaction**: <i>Enter the average transaction amount for the day, e.g., "20.00".</i>
* **total-sales**: <i>Enter the total sales for the day, e.g., "2000.00".</i>
* **total-expenses**: <i>Enter the total expenses for the day, e.g., "1000.00".</i>
* **cost-of-goods-sold**: <i>Enter the cost of goods sold for the day, e.g., "500.00".</i>
* **cost-of-goods-sold-per-avg-transaction**: <i>Enter the cost of goods sold per average transaction, e.g., "25.00".</i>
* **cost-of-goods-sold-per-avg-sale**: <i>Enter the cost of goods sold per average sale, e.g., "30.00".</i>
* **cost-of-goods-sold-per-sale**: <i>Enter the cost of goods sold per sale, e.g., "40.00".</i>
* **profit-per-sale**: <i>Enter the profit per sale for the day, e.g., "20.00".</i>

## Tips
* Please double-check the accuracy of your entries to ensure that they reflect the actual performance of the restaurant.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your manager or support team for assistance.
