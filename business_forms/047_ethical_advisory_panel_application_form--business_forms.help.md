# Ethical Advisory Panel Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethical Advisory Panel Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in serving on an Ethical Advisory Panel. The panel provides recommendations to organizations on how to improve their business practices and promote ethical decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" page.
2. Answer the questions in the "Application Form" page.
3. Submit your application in the "Submission" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information**: This page is where you provide your contact details. 
  Please enter your name, email address, and phone number.
  This information will be used to contact you for further instructions
  and to keep you updated on the application progress.
### Application Form
* **Application Form**:
  Please answer the questions on this page, providing as much detail as possible.
  This will help the selection committee assess your qualifications and experience.
  Be as specific as you can about your skills and areas of expertise.
### Submission
* **Submission**: 
  Here, you can upload your submission. Please ensure it is in the required format.
  If you are unsure, please refer to the submission guidelines provided on our website.
  Make sure to proofread and edit your submission before finalizing it.
  It should be well-written and free of errors to show your attention to detail.
