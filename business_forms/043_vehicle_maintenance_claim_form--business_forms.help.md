# Vehicle Maintenance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for vehicle maintenance claims. This form is designed to gather essential details from the customer and claimant to process their claim efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section with the relevant details.
2. Provide information about the vehicle, including its make, model, and any relevant details.
3. Describe the damage or issue with the vehicle.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your identity as the claimant.
5. If present, sign and date the witness signature section as a witness to the claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: Enter your name and contact information as the customer who is submitting the claim.
* **Vehicle Info**: Describe the vehicle that requires maintenance or repair, including its make, model, and any other relevant details.
* **Damage Details**: Provide a detailed description of the damage or issue with the vehicle.
* **Claimant Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm your identity as the claimant. This section is required.
* **Claimant Date**: Date and time you are submitting the claim.
* **Claimant Time**: Provide the time you are submitting the claim.
* **Witness Signature**: If a witness is present, sign and date this section to confirm their identity as a witness. This section is not required but recommended for additional verification.
