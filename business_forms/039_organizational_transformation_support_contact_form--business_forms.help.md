# Organizational Transformation Support Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for submitting requests for organizational transformation support. It is intended to collect information from end users who need assistance with various aspects of organizational transformations, such as business model, digital transformation, organizational design, operational, or other types of transformations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Type your message in the "Message" field.
6. Select the type(s) of transformation you are seeking help with from the "What kind of transformation are you looking for?" dropdown menu. You can select one or multiple options.
7. Select the category that best describes the type of form you are submitting from the "Category" dropdown menu.
8. Choose the person you would like to be assigned to from the "Assigned To" dropdown menu.
9. Select the status of your transformation from the "Transformation Status" dropdown menu.
10. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First Name)**: A field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)**: A field to enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)**: A field to enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: A field to enter your phone number.
* **Message (Message)**: A field to type a message describing your transformation needs.
* **What kind of transformation are you looking for? (transformation_type)**: A dropdown menu to select the type of transformation you need help with (e.g., Business Model, Digital Transformation, Organizational Design, Operational, or Other).
* **Category (category)**: A dropdown menu to select the category that best describes the type of form you are submitting (e.g., Business Forms, IT Forms, Operational Forms).
* **Assigned To (assigned_to)**: A dropdown menu to select the person you would like to be assigned to (e.g., chatjimmy, jenny, other).
* **Transformation Status (transformation_status)**: A dropdown menu to select the status of your transformation (e.g., In Progress, Complete, Cancelled, On Hold).
* **Notes (notes)**: A field to add any additional notes or comments about your transformation needs.

## Tips
* Ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information in the form.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, please ask for help from your support team.
* If you need to edit or update your transformation request, please contact your support team for assistance.
