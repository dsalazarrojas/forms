# Real Estate Finance Partnership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Finance Partnership Application form is used to collect information from potential partners about their capacity, goals, investment terms, and expected return on investment. This form helps us understand their needs and requirements, enabling us to tailor our services to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each page and field to ensure you understand what information we are requesting.
2.  Answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3.  Ensure you have permission to share any confidential or sensitive information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Partnership Inquiry** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This field is for any additional information you would like to share about your partnership inquiry.
* **Funding Capacity** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you have the capacity to fund a partnership (`Yes` or `No`).
* **Investor Inquiries** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any investor inquiries you have, or choose `No` if none.
* **Investment Goals** (`4`, `date`, required: false): Enter your investment goals, such as the date you plan to invest.
* **Expected Return** (`5`, `number`, required: false): Enter your expected return percentage on investment.
* **Funding Sources** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether you have any funding sources (`Yes` or `No`).
* **Collaboration Goals** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any collaboration goals, such as communication and coordination.
* **Real Estate Assets** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter a description of your real estate assets.
* **Business Experience** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business experience and background.
* **Funding Capacity Assessment** (`10`, `time`, required: false): Enter a time estimate for your funding capacity.
* **Collaboration Assessment** (`11`, `time`, required: false): Enter a time estimate for your collaboration capacity.
* **Investment Terms** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Enter any investment terms and conditions.
* **Communication Channels** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Enter any communication channels you prefer.
* **Financial Information** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Enter any financial information related to your investment.
* **Risk Factors** (`15`, `text`, required: false): Enter any risk factors you are aware of.
* **Legal Terms** (`16`, `text`, required: false): Enter any legal terms and conditions.
* **Collaboration Outcomes** (`17`, `text`, required: false): Enter any expected collaboration outcomes.
* **Partner Requirements** (`18`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate any partner requirements, such as expertise or experience.
* **Partner Commitment** (`19`, `number`, required: false): Enter a commitment level, such as a number or percentage.
* **Investment Requirements** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate any investment requirements, such as minimum investment amount.
* **Collaboration Terms** (`21`, `text`, required: false): Enter any collaboration terms and conditions.
* **Partner Contribution** (`22`, `text`, required: false): Enter any partner contribution.
* **Collaboration Outcomes** (`23`, `text`, required: false): Enter any expected collaboration outcomes.
* **Partner Expectations** (`24`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate any partner expectations, such as communication frequency.
* **Review Process** (`25`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you will participate in our review process (`Yes` or `No`).
