# Materiality Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Materiality Assessment Questionnaire is a tool designed to help organizations assess the materiality of their business operations and decisions. It helps to identify areas where the organization's activities have a significant impact on the environment and society, and to prioritize these areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them honestly.
2. For questions 3-25, select one or more of the options provided, based on the relevance and importance of each issue.
3. Enter a numerical value (0-10) to indicate the impact of each issue on the organization.
4. Submit the form to review and submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a note field to introduce the questionnaire and provide context.
* **Materiality Principles**: This is a text field where you can describe the materiality principles that guide your organization's decision-making.
* **Sustainability Issues**: Select one or more of the issues that are most relevant to your organization's operations, such as Climate Change, Biodiversity, etc. (Required)
* **Company Sustainability**: Enter a numerical value to indicate the importance of sustainability to your organization (Optional)
* **Sustainability Issues Importance**: Select one or more of the issues you previously selected, and enter a numerical value to indicate their importance to your organization (Required)
* **Sustainability Impact** (fields 6-25): Enter a numerical value to indicate the impact of each issue on your organization (Optional)
* **Sustainability Issues 2**: Select one or more of the issues that are most relevant to your organization's operations, such as Economic, Biodiversity, etc. (Required)
* **Submit**: Enter your name and any additional comments or questions you may have (Optional)

Note: The required fields are marked as "Required" in the field descriptions.
