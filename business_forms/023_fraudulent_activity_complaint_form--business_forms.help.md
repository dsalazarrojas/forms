# Fraudulent Activity Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report instances of suspected fraudulent activity within our organization. It is essential to provide accurate and detailed information to help investigate and address these incidents effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible, using the provided options for select fields.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident in the `report_date` and `report_time` fields.
3. Provide the suspect's name, address, phone number, and email address in the respective fields.
4. Select the specific fraudulent activity that occurred in the `fraudulent_activity` field.
5. Enter any additional relevant witness statements or information about the incident in the `witness_statement` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **complaint_form** (Text, Not Required): Enter a brief description of the fraudulent activity that occurred.
* **report_date** (Date, Required): The date on which the fraudulent activity was reported or noticed.
* **report_time** (Time, Not Required): The time of the reported incident. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **suspect_name** (Text, Not Required): The name of the individual or entity suspected of committing the fraudulent activity.
* **suspect_address** (Text, Not Required): The address associated with the suspect.
* **suspect_phone** (Text, Not Required): The phone number of the suspect.
* **suspect_email** (Email, Not Required): The email address of the suspect.
* **suspect_dob** (Date, Not Required): The date of birth of the suspect.
* **fraudulent_activity** (Select One, Not Required): Choose the specific type of fraudulent activity that occurred from the provided options:
	+ Item 1
	+ Item 2
	+ Item 3
	+ Item 4
	+ Item 5
* **witness_statement** (Text, Not Required): Enter any relevant witness statements or information about the incident.
