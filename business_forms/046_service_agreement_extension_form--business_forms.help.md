# Service Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect details for service agreement extensions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please ensure you are using the correct form and have read and understood its purpose.
2. Fill out the form with the necessary information.
   - Enter the **business_name** field with the name of the business requesting the service agreement extension.
   - Enter the **service_agreement_id** field with the ID of the service agreement you wish to extend.
   - Enter the **extension_date** field with the desired extension date.
   - Enter the **extension_time** field with the desired extension time.
   - Enter the **reason_for_extension** field with a brief explanation of the reason for the extension.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): This field is for entering the name of the business requesting the service agreement extension.
* **Service Agreement ID** (`service_agreement_id`, text, optional): This field is for entering the ID of the service agreement you wish to extend.
* **Extension Date** (`extension_date`, date, optional): This field is for entering the desired extension date for the service agreement.
* **Extension Time** (`extension_time`, time, optional): This field is for entering the desired extension time for the service agreement.
* **Reason for Extension** (`reason_for_extension`, text, optional): This field is for entering a brief explanation of the reason for the extension.

## Tips
* Ensure accuracy in filling out the form to avoid any potential misunderstandings.
* Review the form before submission to ensure completeness.
* If you have any questions or issues while filling out the form, please contact form@gic.mx.
