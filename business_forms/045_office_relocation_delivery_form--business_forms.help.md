## office_relocation_delivery_form - Help Guide

### Purpose

The `office_relocation_delivery_form` is a form used to track and manage office relocation delivery information. This form is used to provide details about the delivery method, schedule, and status of office relocations. It helps administrators to keep track of multiple deliveries and manage their status.

### How To Complete This Form

1. **Delivery Information**: Enter the delivery information, including the office ID and item ID, if applicable.
2. **Delivery Method**: Select the method of delivery, such as Delivery Method, Pick up, Courier, Drop off, or Delivery Personnel.
3. **Delivery Status**: Select the current status of the delivery, such as Delivered, Pending, Completed, or Cancelled.
4. **Delivery Schedule**: Enter the date of the delivery.
5. **Delivery Time**: Enter the time of the delivery.
6. **Delivery Date**: Enter the date of the delivery.
7. **Remarks**: Enter any additional remarks or comments about the delivery.
8. **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback.
9. **Contact Details**: Enter the contact details of the person responsible for the delivery.
10. **Delivery Personnel Details**: Enter the details of the delivery personnel.
11. **Delivery Personnels**: Enter the list of delivery personnel.
12. **Delivery Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the delivery.
13. **Delivery Status 2**: Select the current status of the delivery, if it has changed.
14. **Remarks 2**: Enter any additional remarks or comments about the delivery.
15. **Delivery Method 2**: Select the method of delivery, if it has changed.
16. **Delivery Notes 2**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the delivery.
17. **Item Details**: Enter any additional details about the item being delivered.
18. **Contact Details 2**: Enter the contact details of the person responsible for the delivery.
19. **Delivery Status 3**: Select the current status of the delivery, if it has changed.
20. **Delivery Personnels Details**: Enter the details of the delivery personnel.
21. **Remarks 3**: Enter any additional remarks or comments about the delivery.
