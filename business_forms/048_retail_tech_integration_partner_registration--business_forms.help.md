# Retail Tech Integration Partner Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Tech Integration Partner Registration form is used to collect information from potential integration partners about their interests and consent to be a retail technology integration partner. This form helps us understand your company's goals, interests, and expectations, ensuring a smooth integration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your partner details on the first page.
2. Select your integration interests on the second page.
3. Choose your consent on the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Details (Page 1)** (`partner_details`, text, required: false): This is the first page where you can enter your company's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Integration Interests (Page 2)** (`integration_interests`, select_multiple, required: false): On this page, select all the options that best describe your company's integration interests. This will help us understand your company's goals and expectations.
	+ Option 1: This option is not specified, but it is likely related to retail technology integration.
	+ Option 2: This option is not specified, but it is likely related to retail technology integration.
	+ Option 3: This option is not specified, but it is likely related to retail technology integration.
* **Consent (Page 3)** (`consent`, select_one, required: false): On this page, choose one of the options that best describe your company's consent status. This will help us understand your company's agreement to be a retail technology integration partner.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Review all the fields carefully before submission to avoid any mistakes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
