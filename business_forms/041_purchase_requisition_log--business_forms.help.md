# Purchase Requisition Log - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for tracking and logging purchase requests, including the category of the purchase, details of the purchase, and information about the requestor. It is intended to be completed by the person making the purchase request, and can be used by purchasing teams to review and manage the request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the purchase from the options provided.
2. Enter a brief description of the purchase.
3. Choose the type of purchase (e.g., "type 1", "type 2", etc.).
4. Enter the name of the person making the purchase request.
5. Enter the email address of the person making the purchase request.
6. Enter the phone number of the person making the purchase request.
7. Enter the department responsible for the purchase.
8. Enter the cost center responsible for the purchase.
9. Enter the date of the purchase.
10. Enter the time of the purchase.
11. Enter the amount of the purchase.
12. Enter the approved amount of the purchase.
13. Add any additional notes about the purchase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category of the purchase from the list of options provided.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the purchase.
* **Purchase Type** (`Purchase Type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the type of purchase (e.g., "type 1", "type 2", etc.).
* **Requestor Name** (`Requestor Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person making the purchase request.
* **Requestor Email** (`Requestor Email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person making the purchase request.
* **Requestor Phone** (`Requestor Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person making the purchase request.
* **Department** (`Department`, text, required/optional): Enter the department responsible for the purchase.
* **Cost Center** (`Cost Center`, text, required/optional): Enter the cost center responsible for the purchase.
* **Purchase Date** (`Purchase Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the purchase.
* **Purchase Time** (`Purchase Time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the purchase.
* **Amount** (`Amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of the purchase.
* **Approved Amount** (`Approved Amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the approved amount of the purchase.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the purchase.
