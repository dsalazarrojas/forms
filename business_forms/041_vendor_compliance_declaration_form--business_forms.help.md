# Vendor Compliance Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Compliance Declaration Form is used to collect information from vendors, ensuring they meet company policies and regulations. Vendors must fill out the form with accurate details about their business operations, compliance status, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your vendor name and address on the first page.
2. Provide your contact details, including your phone and email.
3. Enter your website (if applicable).
4. Confirm your compliance status by selecting the correct option (Yes or No).
5. Check the compliance status that applies to your business operations.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about your agreement with the company.
7. Date and time-stamp your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Vendor Name** (text, required: false)
Enter your business name as it appears on your company documents and marketing materials.

### 2. **Vendor Address** (text, required: false)
Provide your business address where we can contact you.

### 3. **Vendor Phone** (text, required: false)
Enter your phone number so we can contact you.

### 4. **Vendor Email** (email, required: false)
Enter your email address for easy communication.

### 5. **Vendor Website** (text, required: false)
If you have a website, enter the URL.

### 6. **Vendor License** (select_multiple, required: false)
Check the compliance status that applies to your business operations (Yes or No).

### 7. **Vendor Compliance** (select_one, required: false)
Select the compliance status from the list (Compliance 1, Compliance 2, or Compliance 3).

### 8. **Vendor Agreement** (note, required: false)
Add any additional comments about your agreement with the company.

### 9. **Vendor Comments** (text, required: false)
Provide any additional comments or notes about your vendor profile.

### 10. **Vendor Signature Date** (date, required: false)
Date-stamp your signature.

### 11. **Vendor Signature Time** (time, required: false)
Time-stamp your signature.

Note: Some fields are not required, but please fill out as many fields as possible to ensure we have accurate and up-to-date information about your business.
