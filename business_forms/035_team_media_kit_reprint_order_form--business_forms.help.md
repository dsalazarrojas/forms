# teamMediaKitReprintOrderForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request a reprint of a team media kit for internal use. It is essential to ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to facilitate the reprinting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2.  Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **OrderForm** (`orderform`, text, required): Enter your order request in this text field.
* **Contact Information** (`companyinformation`, text, required): Enter your company information, including your name, department, title, job title, phone number, and email address.
* **Media Kit Options** (`mediakitoptions`, select_multiple, not required): If applicable, select "Yes" if you require a media kit to be printed with your order.
* **Delivery Date** (`deliverydate`, date, not required): Enter the date you would like to receive the printed media kit.
* **Delivery Time** (`deliverytime`, time, not required): Enter the time you would like to receive the printed media kit.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or instructions for the reprinting process.
* **Order Details** (`orderdetails`, text, required): Enter detailed information about the order you are submitting.
* **Name** (`orderdetailstitle`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Department** (`orderdetailshelp`, text, not required): Enter your department.
* **Job Title** (`orderdetailstitle`, text, not required): Enter your job title.
* **Phone** (`orderdetailshelp`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`orderdetailsemail`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`orderdetailshelp`, text, not required): Enter your address.
* **Postal Code** (`orderdetailspostalcode`, text, not required): Enter your postal code.
* **Province** (`orderdetailshelp`, text, not required): Enter your province.
* **Country** (`orderdetailshelp`, text, not required): Enter your country.
* **Notes 2** (`orderdetailssubmitting`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or instructions for the reprinting process.
* **Order Summary** (`ordersummary`, text, not required): Enter a summary of your order.
* **Order Summary Text** (`ordersummarytext`, text, not required): Enter a detailed text about your order.
* **Order Summary Notes** (`ordersummarynotes`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or instructions for the order summary.
* **Attachments** (`ordersummaryattachments`, select_multiple, not required): If you have attachments related to your order, select "Yes". 
* **File** (`ordersummaryattachmentsfile`, text, not required): Enter the file name and location if you have selected "Yes" for attachments.
* **File 2** (`ordersummaryattachmentsfile2`, text, not required): Enter the file name and location if you have selected "Yes" for attachments.
