# Compensation Benchmark Data Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from you to provide accurate and relevant compensation data for your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Provide your name, email address, and job title.
2. Specify the date of your request.
3. Select the type of benchmark data you need (salary survey data, market pricing analysis, etc.).
4. Choose the job roles and levels you want to benchmark.
5. Select the industries and locations you want to compare (e.g., technology, healthcare, etc.).
6. Specify the data sources you prefer (e.g., Radford, Mercer, etc.).
7. Choose the data elements you need (e.g., base salary percentiles, bonus targets, etc.).
8. Select the percentiles you are interested in (e.g., 25th, 50th, 75th, etc.).
9. Specify the purpose of your request (e.g., annual compensation review, new role creation, etc.).
10. Provide the urgency and deadline for your request.
11. Choose how you want the data delivered (e.g., Excel spreadsheet, PDF report, etc.).
12. Indicate if you need a presentation or brief overview of the findings.
13. Specify the confidentiality level of your data (e.g., public, internal, etc.).
14. Add any additional requirements or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, required): The date you are submitting this request.
* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): Your full name.
* **Requester Email** (`requester_email`, `email`, required): Your email address.
* **Requester Title** (`requester_title`, `text`, required): Your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Your department.
* **Type of Benchmark Data** (`request_type`, `select_one`, required): The type of data you need (e.g., salary survey data, market pricing analysis, etc.).
* **Job Roles to Benchmark** (`job_roles`, `text`, required): The job roles you want to benchmark.
* **Job Levels** (`job_levels`, `select_multiple`, required): The job levels you want to benchmark (e.g., entry level, mid-level, senior, etc.).
* **Industry Focus** (`industry_focus`, `select_multiple`, required): The industries you want to compare (e.g., technology, healthcare, finance, etc.).
* **Geographic Scope** (`geographic_scope`, `select_one`, required): The locations you want to include (e.g., national, global, etc.).
* **Specific Locations** (`specific_locations`, `text`, optional): If you have specific locations in mind, list them here.
* **Company Size for Comparison** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required): The size of companies you want to compare (e.g., startups, small, medium, etc.).
* **Data Sources** (`data_sources`, `select_multiple`, required): The sources of data you prefer (e.g., Radford, Mercer, etc.).
* **Data Elements Needed** (`data_elements`, `select_multiple`, required): The specific data points you need (e.g., base salary percentiles, bonus targets, etc.).
* **Percentile Focus** (`percentile_focus`, `select_multiple`, required): The percentiles you are interested in (e.g., 25th, 50th, 75th, etc.).
* **Purpose of Request** (`purpose`, `select_one`, required): The purpose of your request (e.g., annual compensation review, new role creation, etc.).
* **Request Urgency** (`urgency`, `select_one`, required): The urgency of your request (e.g., within 1 week, within 2 weeks, etc.).
* **Required By Date** (`deadline_date`, `date`, required): The date by which you need the data.
* **Budget Code** (`budget_code`, `text`, optional): If applicable for paid data.
* **Other Stakeholders** (`stakeholders`, `text`, optional): Who else needs this data.
* **Have you Requested Before?** (`previous_requests`, `select_one`, required): If you have requested compensation data before.
* **Preferred Format** (`format_preference`, `select_one`, required): How you want the data delivered (e.g., Excel, PDF, etc.).
* **Do you Need a Presentation?** (`presentation_needed`, `select_one`, required): If you want a presentation or brief overview of the findings.
* **Confidentiality Level** (`confidentiality_level`, `select_one`, required): The sensitivity of your data (e.g., public, internal, etc.).
* **Additional Requirements** (`additional_requirements`, `text`, optional): Any special needs or comments.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, optional): For internal use by compensation team.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, optional): Compensation analyst assigned to your request.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other information.

## Tips
* Please provide as much accurate and specific information as possible.
* If you have already requested compensation data, you can skip some fields as they will be pre-populated.
* If you need help or have any questions, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
