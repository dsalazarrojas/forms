# Trade Secret Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended to collect information about trade secrets, such as their classification, description, and ownership. It is designed to help identify and manage trade secrets within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out each field as instructed. You can find more information on each field's purpose and guidelines within the Field-by-Field Explanation section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trade Secret Information** (`trade_secret_information`, text, optional): A brief description of the trade secret.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, note, optional): If applicable, describe the agreement associated with the trade secret.
* **Trade Secret Classification** (`trade_secret_classification`, select_one, optional): Select the classification of the trade secret from the available options (Class A, Class B, Class C).
* **Trade Secret Description** (`trade_secret_description`, text, optional): A detailed description of the trade secret.
* **Potential Owners** (`potential_owners`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if the trade secret has potential owners or not.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_one, optional): Select if a contact person is associated with the trade secret.
* **Date Obtained** (`date_obtained`, date, optional): The date the trade secret was obtained.
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, date, optional): The last date the trade secret information was updated.
* **Trade Secret Name** (`trade_secret_name`, text, optional): A name associated with the trade secret.
* **Trade Secret Owners** (`trade_secret_owners`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if the trade secret has trade secret owners or not.
* **Trade Secret Classifications** (`trade_secret_classifications`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple trade secret classifications.
* **Trade Secret Description 2** (`trade_secret_description_2`, text, optional): A second detailed description of the trade secret.
* **Date Obtained 2** (`date_obtained_2`, date, optional): The date the second piece of trade secret information was obtained.
* **Last Updated 2** (`last_updated_2`, date, optional): The last date the second piece of trade secret information was updated.

## Tips

- Please provide clear and accurate information to help manage and protect the trade secret.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the relevant guidelines and regulations within your organization.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your supervisor or relevant personnel.
