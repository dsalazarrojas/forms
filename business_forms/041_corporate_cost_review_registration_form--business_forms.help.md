# corporate_cost_review_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from companies for corporate cost reviews. It aims to collect data on company details, employee information, and status updates related to cost reviews. This form is a crucial tool for finance and management teams to track and evaluate cost-related matters within a company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form efficiently, follow these steps:

* Enter the company's name in the "Company" field.
* Provide the name of the employee who submitted the request in the "Employee" field (optional).
* Enter the name of the manager in charge of the cost review in the "Manager" field.
* Select the date of the request for the cost review in the "Request Date" field.
* Update the status of the cost review in the "Status" field (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* Attach any relevant supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents" field.
* Select the finance reviewer's name from the drop-down list in the "Finance Reviewer" field.
* Choose one or multiple management reviewers from the drop-down list in the "Management Reviewer" field (optional).
* Mark the finance team's approval status in the "Finance Approved" field (Approved or Rejected).
* Mark the management team's approval status in the "Management Approved" field (Approved or Rejected).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company (Company)** (`text`, Required): Enter the name of the company requesting the cost review.
* **Employee (Employee)** (`text`, Optional): Enter the name of the employee who submitted the request.
* **Manager (Manager)** (`text`, Required): Enter the name of the manager in charge of the cost review.
* **Request Date (Request Date)** (`date`, Required): Select the date the cost review request was submitted.
* **Status (Status)** (`select_multiple`, Required): Update the status of the cost review (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* **Supporting Documents (Supporting Documents)** (`note`, Required): Attach any supporting documents relevant to the cost review.
* **Finance Reviewer (Finance Reviewer)** (`select_one`, Required): Select the finance reviewer's name from the drop-down list.
* **Management Reviewer (Management Reviewer)** (`select_multiple`, Optional): Choose one or multiple management reviewers from the drop-down list.
* **Finance Approved (Finance Approved)** (`select_one`, Required): Mark the finance team's approval status (Approved or Rejected).
* **Management Approved (Management Approved)** (`select_one`, Required): Mark the management team's approval status (Approved or Rejected).

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields marked as "Required" are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Use the "Supporting Documents" field to attach any supporting files, such as excel spreadsheets or PDFs, that will aid in the cost review process.
* If the "Finance Reviewer" field is not filled, the form will not be completed properly.
* If the "Management Reviewer" field is not filled, the form will not be marked as completed.
