We will create a user-facing help guide for the "Employee Self Evaluation Form" to ensure end-users understand its purpose, how to complete it, and what each field means.

To complete this form, users should have a basic understanding of their job titles, departments, and performance areas. They should also be prepared to provide honest and constructive feedback about their performance and their colleagues'.

Before starting, please ensure that you have reviewed the performance areas and departments listed in the form and are prepared to answer questions accordingly.

## Employee Self Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help employees and managers conduct a self-evaluation of an employee's performance. This form will help identify areas of strength and weakness in performance and provide a platform for constructive feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please select your job title from the list of provided options.
2.  Choose one or multiple departments that you are responsible for, based on your job function.
3.  Evaluate your performance in each department using the score provided.
4.  Provide comments on your performance in each department.
5.  Enter your name and your manager's name.
6.  Enter the date the evaluation was completed.
7.  Finally, provide a rating for your overall performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **job_title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title as it is listed in the provided options.
*   **department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Choose one department that you are responsible for.
*   **evaluation_score** (`evaluation_score`, number, optional): Evaluate your performance in each department using a score out of 10.
*   **performance_area** (`performance_area`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple departments that you are responsible for.
*   **evaluation_comments** (`evaluation_comments`, note, optional): Provide comments on your performance in each department.
*   **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
*   **manager_name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager.
*   **date_completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date the evaluation was completed.
*   **employee_rating** (`employee_rating`, number, optional): Provide a rating for your overall performance.
*   **evaluation_comments_2** (`evaluation_comments_2`, note, optional): Provide additional comments for your manager's review.

## Tips

*   Be honest and objective when evaluating your performance.
*   Use the provided scores and options to guide your feedback.
*   Keep your comments constructive and focused on your performance and areas for improvement.
