# risk_exposure_adjustment_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The risk exposure adjustment application form is used to adjust exposure limits and request additional supporting documents for risk exposure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the exposure limits and supporting documents to be included in your request.
2. Enter the relevant information into the exposure limits fields (fields 1-4).
3. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the supporting documents fields (fields 5-19).
4. Enter the date of the risk exposure (field 20).
5. If required, enter the date of the request (field 21).
6. Upload any additional supporting documents as text in the corresponding fields (fields 22-25).
