# Startup Landing Page - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Startup Landing Page form is designed to collect and showcase essential information about your startup company. This form helps you present your startup in a professional and concise manner, making it easier for others to understand your business and connect with you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about your startup.
2. For optional fields, provide relevant information if you have it available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start-up name**: Enter the name of your startup (e.g., "GreenTech Solutions").
* **Founders name(s)**: Select 'Yes' if you have one or more founders.
* **Enter short description**: A brief description of your startup (e.g., "GreenTech Solutions is a sustainable energy solutions provider").
* **Tag line**: A short phrase describing your startup (e.g., "Sustainable energy for a better tomorrow").
* **Enter bio**: A short paragraph about your startup's founder(s) (e.g., "Our founder, John, has over 10 years of experience in the industry and has a passion for sustainability").
* **Founder profile link**: A link to your founder's LinkedIn profile or a personal website.
* **Start-up image URL**: The URL of your startup's image (e.g., a logo or banner image).
* **Start-up logo**: Select 'Logo image URL' if you have a custom logo, or 'Custom logo' if you want to upload a custom logo.
* **Founder image URL**: A URL to an image of your founder.
* **Start-up video URL**: A URL to a video about your startup.
* **Start-up social media links**: A comma-separated list of your startup's social media links (e.g., Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook).
* **Funding Stage**: Select 'Yes' if your startup is in the funding stage.
* **Funding type**: Select 'Yes' if your startup is seeking funding.
* **Investment Stage**: A description of your startup's investment stage (e.g., seed, series A, etc.).
* **Start-up locations**: Select 'Yes' if your startup has multiple locations.
* **Start-up employee count**: The number of employees you have.
* **Start-up location**: Select 'Yes' if your startup has a specific location.
* **Start-up URL**: The URL of your startup's website.
* **Start-up category**: Select 'Yes' if your startup falls under a specific category (e.g., tech, finance, etc.).
* **Start-up industry**: Select 'Yes' if your startup operates in a specific industry.
* **Start-up sector(s)**: Select multiple sectors that apply to your startup (e.g., 'Yes' for tech, 'Yes' for environmental).
* **Start-up website URL**: The URL of your startup's website.
* **Start-up email**: Your startup's email address.
* **Start-up phone number**: Your startup's phone number.
* **Start-up postal code**: The postal code of your startup's location.
