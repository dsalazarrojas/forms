# Discovery Call Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discovery Call Application Form is designed to provide a structured way for users to input their information and preferences for a discovery call. This form helps us understand the user's requirements and tailor the discovery call to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your user information, including your name, contact number, and email.
2. Select the desired discovery time that suits you best.
3. Enter the discovery date if applicable.
4. Provide your company name and website (if applicable).
5. Enter the name of the sales representative you would like to work with.
6. Indicate the lead source that referred you to us.
7. Add any additional notes about your discovery call.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information (1)
* **User Information (1)** (`user_input`, text, required/optional): Please provide your name, contact number, and email to enable us to contact you and tailor the discovery call to your needs.

### Discovery Time (2)
* **Discovery Time** (`discovery_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time that suits you best for the discovery call.

### Discovery Date (3)
* **Discovery Date** (`discovery_date`, date, required/optional): If the discovery call is scheduled for a specific date, please provide it here.

### Contact Number (4)
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number to ensure we can reach you.

### Email (5)
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you and tailor the discovery call to your needs.

### Company Name (6)
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): If you're representing a company, please provide your company name.

### Company Website (7)
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, required/optional): If you have a company website, please provide the URL.

### Sales Rep (8)
* **Sales Rep** (`sales_rep`, text, required/optional): If you have a preferred sales representative, please provide their name.

### Lead Source (9)
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, text, required/optional): If you were referred to us through a specific lead source, please indicate it here.

### Notes (10)
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional information or preferences you have about the discovery call.
