# Customer Shipping Location Details - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer shipping information for efficient and accurate delivery processes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in all the required fields to ensure that your shipping location information is up-to-date and accurate.
- You can type the shipping location name, address, city, and country in the corresponding fields.
- Select the assigned tool from the provided options (e.g., chatjimmy, other).
- If applicable, enter the shipping location phone number and email address.
- You can add any additional notes about the shipping location in the "Shipping Location Note" field.
- If multiple users are assigned to the shipping location, select all applicable users.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipping Location Name**: Enter the name of the shipping location (e.g., Store A, Warehouse X).
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the physical address where the goods or services will be shipped to (e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, USA).
* **City**: Enter the city where the shipping location is situated (e.g., Anytown).
* **Region**: Enter the region where the shipping location is situated (e.g., State/Province).
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code associated with the shipping location (e.g., 12345).
* **Country**: Enter the country where the shipping location is situated (e.g., USA).
* **Shipping Location Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the shipping location (e.g., special instructions, delivery restrictions).
* **Shipping Location Phone**: Enter the phone number associated with the shipping location (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Shipping Location Email**: Enter the email address associated with the shipping location (e.g., [customer@example.com](mailto:customer@example.com)).
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool associated with the shipping location (e.g., chatjimmy, other).
* **Created By**: Select all users who are assigned to the shipping location (e.g., user1, user2, user3).

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date to avoid delays or issues with delivery.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can contact your point of contact for clarification.
- This form is for business use only, and the information collected will be used to facilitate efficient and accurate delivery processes.
