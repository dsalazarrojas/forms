# Trade Show Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from trade show attendees for registration and event management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the trade show in the **Trade Show Name** field.
2. Provide your name in the **Attendee Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Attendee Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Attendee Phone** field.
5. If applicable, enter your title and company information in the corresponding fields.
6. Enter the date of the trade show in the **Show Date** field.
7. Enter the time of the trade show in the **Show Time** field.
8. Enter the location of the trade show in the **Show Location** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trade Show Name** (`trade_show_name`, required): Enter the name of the trade show you are registering for.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business cards or identification.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for trade show communications.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for trade show inquiries.
* **Attendee Title** (`attendee_title`, optional): Enter your title or position at your company (e.g., CEO, Marketing Manager).
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, optional): Enter the physical address of your company.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of your company.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, optional): Enter the website of your company.
* **Industry** (`industry`, optional): Enter the industry your company operates in.
* **Company Representative** (`company_representative`, optional): Enter the name of the company representative attending the trade show.
* **Show Date** (`show_date`, required): Enter the date of the trade show.
* **Show Time** (`show_time`, required): Enter the time of the trade show.
* **Show Location** (`show_location`, required): Enter the location of the trade show.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to the event organizers.
