# Raw Material Sourcing Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from suppliers regarding raw material sourcing. It helps the company's procurement team to gather necessary data for efficient quotation and purchasing processes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the supplier's name and contact person.
2.  Provide the email and phone number of the supplier.
3.  Describe the material you are requesting in the Material Description field.
4.  Specify the quantity of the material needed.
5.  Enter the unit price for the material.
6.  Calculate the total price by multiplying the unit price by the material quantity (Note: This field will be automatically calculated if filled in correctly).
7.  Fill in the supplier's address, zip, city, and country information.
8.  Specify the material's weight.
9.  Choose the unit of measurement for the material.
10.  Enter any additional supplier notes.
11.  Select the person submitting this form from the options provided.
12.  Set the date and time of submission.
13.  Confirm the supplier's status regarding the quote request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the full name of the supplier.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person you are contacting at the supplier.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the supplier.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the supplier.
*   **Material Description** (`material_description`, text, required/optional): Describe the material you are sourcing.
*   **Material Quantity** (`material_quantity`, text, required/optional): Specify the quantity of the material needed.
*   **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, required/optional): Enter the unit price for the material.
*   **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, required/optional): This field will be automatically calculated if filled in correctly.
*   **Supplier Address** (`supplier_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the supplier's address.
*   **Supplier Zip** (`supplier_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the supplier's zip code.
*   **Supplier City** (`supplier_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the supplier's city.
*   **Supplier State** (`supplier_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the supplier's state.
*   **Supplier Country** (`supplier_country`, text, required/optional): Enter the supplier's country.
*   **Material Weight** (`material_weight`, text, required/optional): Specify the weight of the material.
*   **Material Unit** (`material_unit`, text, required/optional): Choose the unit of measurement for the material.
*   **Supplier Notes** (`supplier_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes regarding the supplier.
*   **Select one of the following** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Select the person submitting this form from the options provided.
*   **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date this form was submitted.
*   **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time this form was submitted.
*   **Select one of the following** (`supplier_confirmation`, select_multiple, required): Select the supplier's confirmation status from the options provided.
