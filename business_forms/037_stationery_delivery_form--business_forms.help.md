# Stationery Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The stationery delivery form is used to collect information from customers for efficient delivery of stationery services. This includes details about the business, delivery, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information under the "Business Information" section with your company's details.
2. Provide delivery details in the "Delivery Info" section.
3. List the stationery items you require in the "Stationery Items" section.
4. If there are any special requests, please specify them in the "Special Requests" section.
5. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" section.
6. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Information** (`business_information`, text, required): Enter your business name, address, and contact information in this field.
* **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, text, required): Enter the delivery address and any other relevant delivery details here.
* **Stationery Items** (`stationery_items`, text, required): List the stationery items you require for delivery.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Specify any special requests for delivery here.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required): Enter your payment method and amount.
* **Review** (`review`, text, optional): Review your information before submitting the form.
* **Stationery Item ID** (`stationery_item_id`, text, optional): Enter the stationery item ID if applicable.
* **Stationery Item Type** (`stationery_item_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of stationery item if applicable.
* **Select One Business** (`select_one_business`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you want to deliver to one business.
* **Select Multiple Businesses** (`select_multiple_business`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you want to deliver to multiple businesses.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of delivery if applicable.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of delivery if applicable.
* **Text** (`text`, text, optional): Enter any additional text information.
* **Integer** (`integer`, number, optional): Enter an integer value if applicable.
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, number, optional): Enter a decimal value if applicable.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any notes or comments about the delivery.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Stationery Item ID** (`stationery_item_id`, text, optional): Enter the stationery item ID.
* **Stationery Item Type** (`stationery_item_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of stationery item.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, optional): Enter any special requests.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text, optional): Enter your payment method.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount.
