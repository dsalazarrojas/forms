# registration_counter_supply_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request supplies from the central store for the counter. Fill out the form with your details to submit a request for supplies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information for your supply request. All fields marked as "required" must be completed before submitting the form.
2. Select the correct "Supply Type" from the provided options.
3. Enter your "Contact Person" information, as this is a required field.
4. Optionally, enter your "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" if you have them.
5. Review your form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supply Request**: This is the main description of the supply you are requesting. Please provide a brief description of the item.
* **Counter ID**: This field is used to identify which counter this supply request is for. Enter the relevant counter ID to ensure your request is processed correctly.
* **Item Description**: This field is used to provide additional details about the item being requested. This can be a brief description or any other relevant information.
* **Quantity Requested**: Enter the quantity of the item you are requesting. This will help the supply team process your request more efficiently.
* **Supply Type**: Select the type of supply you are requesting. This will help the supply team process your request correctly.
* **Contact Person**: This is a required field where you must enter the name of the person responsible for this supply request.
* **Contact Email**: If you have a contact email, please enter it here so the supply team can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
* **Contact Phone**: This is an optional field where you can enter your contact phone number. This can be useful for the supply team to reach out to you for any further questions or updates.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your supply request before submitting to ensure all details are accurate.
* The supply team will process your request as soon as possible after receiving your form submission.
