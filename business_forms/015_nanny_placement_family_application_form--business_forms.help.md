# Nanny Placement Family Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for families seeking nanny placement services. It helps our agency understand your family's needs and preferences to find the best match for your nanny.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form completely and accurately. Please ensure that you only fill it out once for each new placement request.
2. Be thorough in your responses, as they will help our agency get a better understanding of your family's requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Details** (Page 1): Provide general information about your family, including your name, address, and contact information.
* **Childcare Needs** (Page 2): Describe the type of care your children require, including any special needs or requirements.
* **Preferences** (Page 3): Share any specific preferences you have for your nanny, such as language or cultural requirements.
* **Contact Details** (Page 4): Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
* **Submission** (Page 5): If you have any specific submission requirements or deadlines, please list them here.
* **Nanny Placement** (Page 6): Describe the type of nanny placement you are seeking, such as part-time or full-time.
* **Emergency Contact** (Page 7): List an emergency contact person and their contact information in case we need to reach out to them.
* **Medical Conditions** (Page 8): If you have any medical conditions that may impact the nanny's duties or requirements, please list them here.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 9): Share any additional information about your family or the nanny placement that will help us better understand your needs.
* **Family Income** (Page 10): If you'd like to share your family's income information, this is the space to do so.
* **Nanny Placement Agency** (Page 11): Please list the name and contact information of the agency or agency you are currently working with.
* **Nanny Placement Agency Address** (Page 12): If you have any additional information about the nanny placement agency, please list their address here.

**Tips**

* Please be honest and accurate in your responses, as they will help us find the best match for your family's needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
