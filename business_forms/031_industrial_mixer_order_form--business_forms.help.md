# industrial_mixer_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you place an order for industrial mixers from our company. Please fill out the form with your contact information, order details, and any special requirements you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your contact information in the "Customer Details" field.
2. Select the type of mixer you're interested in from the "Mixer Type" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the size of the mixer you need from the list in the "Mixer Size" field.
4. Enter the quantity of mixers you would like to order.
5. Provide your shipping details in the "Shipping Details" field.
6. If you have any special requirements or requests, type them in the "Customer Requirements" field.
7. Select your preferred payment terms from the list in the "Payment Terms" field.
8. Enter your billing details in the "Billing Details" field.
9. Add any additional notes about your order in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details (Customer Details)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can contact you to confirm your order.
* **Mixer Type (Mixer Type)**: Select the type of mixer you're interested in ordering. This will help us ensure we have the right product for you.
* **Mixer Size (Mixer Size)**: Choose the size of the mixer you need. Be sure to select the correct size to avoid any confusion.
* **Order Quantity (Order Quantity)**: Enter the number of mixers you would like to order.
* **Shipping Details (Shipping Details)**: Provide your shipping address and any specific delivery instructions.
* **Customer Requirements (Customer Requirements)**: If you have any special requirements or requests, type them in this field. This is your chance to let us know about any specific needs or concerns you may have.
* **Delivery Window (Delivery Window)**: Enter your desired delivery date or date range. This will help us ensure we can deliver your order on time.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms)**: Select your preferred payment terms. Our standard payment terms are 30 days, but we also offer 60 days and net 30 options.
* **Billing Details (Billing Details)**: Enter your billing information, including your company name and address.
* **Notes (Notes)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order. This is your chance to provide more information about your order or request special attention.
