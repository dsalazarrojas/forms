# Social Media Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Manager Application Form is designed to collect information about candidates for the Social Media Manager position. This form is used to evaluate candidates' qualifications, work experience, and job preferences to determine the best fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number.
2. Provide your Job Title, Work Experience, and Skills relevant to the Social Media Manager position.
3. Attach your Resume and Portfolio (if applicable).
4. Indicate your Job Interest and Job Type (Full Time, Part Time, or Freelance).
5. Select your Availability dates (Available Start Date and Available End Date).
6. Enter your Salary Range and Location.
7. Write a brief Job Description.
8. Provide a Job Reference (if applicable).
9. Choose your preferred Application Status (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe your relevant work experience.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your relevant skills (Social Media, Content Creation, Marketing, or Communication).
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Attach your resume.
* **Resume Attachment** (`resume_attachment`, text, optional): Attach your resume (if not attaching through Resume).
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, optional): Attach your portfolio.
* **Portfolio Link** (`portfolio_link`, text, optional): Enter your online portfolio link (if not attaching portfolio).
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): This field is a duplicate of the previous "Skills" field. Please fill in the first "Skills" field.
* **Job Interest** (`job_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your job interests (Social Media Management, Content Strategy, or Branding).
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Enter your educational background.
* **Previous Work** (`previous_work`, text, optional): Describe your previous work experience.
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, date, optional): Enter your available start date.
* **Available End Date** (`available_end_date`, date, optional): Enter your available end date.
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, number, optional): Enter your expected salary range.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter your location.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Write a brief job description.
* **Job Reference** (`job_reference`, text, optional): Provide a job reference (if applicable).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Choose your job type (Full Time, Part Time, or Freelance).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your job status (Open, Closed, or Archived).
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, optional): Enter the application date.
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your application status (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
