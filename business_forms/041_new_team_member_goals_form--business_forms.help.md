# new_team_member_goals_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "new_team_member_goals_form" is designed to help new team members set and track their goals, which will aid them in achieving their objectives and improving performance within the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the first page with your details.
2. On the second page, specify your goals for the next [X] weeks/months/quarter.
3. On the third page, review and confirm your goals.
4. Ensure to submit the form once you've completed all the pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **new_team_member_details** (Text):
	+ Label: new_team_member_details
	+ Type: Text
	+ This field is used to input your name and any relevant details about yourself as a new team member.
* **new_team_member_goals** (Text):
	+ Label: new_team_member_goals
	+ Type: Text
	+ This field is used to specify your goals for the next [X] weeks/months/quarter. Please be as concrete and specific as possible.
* **new_team_member_review** (Text):
	+ Label: new_team_member_review
	+ Type: Text
	+ This field is used for you to review and confirm your goals before submitting the form. Please ensure that your goals are accurate and achievable.
