# Content Creation Contract Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to extend an existing content creation contract. Fill out the form to add new terms and deliverables to an existing contract. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide the original contract's details and identification number.
2. Enter the client's full name and the content creator's full name.
3. Share new terms and deliverables for the contract extension.
4. Set the start and end dates for the contract extension.
5. Describe the new deliverables.
6. Specify the compensation for the contract extension.
7. Confirm that you have read and agree to the privacy policy regarding your data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Original Contract Identification Number** (`original_contract_id`, required): Enter the contract's original identification number.
* **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, required): Enter the client's full name.
* **Content Creator Full Name** (`creator_name`, required): Enter the content creator's full name.
* **Extension Start Date** (`extension_start_date`, required): Specify the start date of the contract extension.
*   *Note: This date should be after the end date of the original contract.*
* **Extension End Date** (`extension_end_date`, required): Specify the end date of the contract extension.
*   *Note: This date should be after the extension start date.*
* **Description of New Deliverables** (`new_deliverables_description`, required): Provide a description of the new deliverables for the contract extension.
* **Compensation for the Extension** (`extension_compensation_amount`, required): Specify the compensation for the contract extension.
* **Extension Agreement Signed?** (`extension_agreement_signed`, required): Confirm if you have signed the extension agreement.
*   *Note: This confirms that you have read and agreed to the extension terms.*
* **I Confirm that the Information Provided is Accurate** (`signature_confirmation`, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
*   *Note: This confirms that the information is accurate before proceeding with the extension.*
* **I Have Read and Agree to the Privacy Policy** (`privacy_agreement`, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the privacy policy.
*   *Note: This confirms that you have read and agreed to the privacy policy regarding your data.*
* **Date of Extension Agreement** (`date_of_extension`, required): Specify the date on which you have signed the extension agreement. 
*   *Note: This date should be after the original contract end date.*
