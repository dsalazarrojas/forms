# Marketing Data Partnership Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for potential partners to share their marketing data and contact information with us. This form is used to gather data to evaluate potential partnerships. Please fill out the form completely and honestly to ensure we can assess your interest and suitability for partnership.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out. Required fields are marked with a *.
3. Double-check your email address to ensure it's correct.
4. Submit the form once you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): A field to input your personal first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): A field to input your personal last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): A field to input your work email address.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, optional): A field to input your work phone number (if applicable).
* **Company** (`5`, `text`, optional): A field to input your company name (if you're representing a company).
* **Message** (`6`, `text`, required): A field to input your message or a brief description of why you're interested in this partnership opportunity.
* **Industry** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Select your industry type from the options provided. This will help us narrow down potential partners.
* **Job Title** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your job title from the options provided. This will help us understand your role within your company.
* **Country** (`9`, `select_one`, optional): Select your country of residence from the options provided. This will help us tailor our partnership approach to your region.

## Tips

* Ensure that you only select one industry and country option.
* If you have any attachments or additional information to share, please email them to us separately.
* We'll review your submission and contact you if we're interested in exploring further partnership opportunities.
