# pricing_estimation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect estimates of a customer's product pricing and other related details from customers during sales calls.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the customer's name in the "customer_name" field.
2.  Provide the customer's contact information in the "customer_contact" field.
3.  Enter the customer's email address in the "customer_email" field.
4.  Add the customer's phone number in the "customer_phone" field.
5.  Enter the name of the product being discussed in the "product_name" field.
6.  Estimate the price of the product in the "product_price" field.
7.  If there's a competitor in the market, enter their name in the "competitor" field.
8.  If you'd like to add any additional comments, use the "customer_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **customer_name**: Enter the name of the customer who has been contacted for the sale.
*   **customer_contact**: Provide any relevant contact information for the customer, such as their address or any other relevant details.
*   **customer_email**: Enter the customer's email address.
*   **customer_phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
*   **product_name**: Enter the name of the product being discussed.
*   **product_price**: Enter an estimated price for the product.
*   **competitor**: If there's a competitor in the market, enter their name.
*   **customer_comments**: If you'd like to add any additional comments, enter them here.

## Tips
This form is intended to be filled out for each customer during a sales call. Make sure to review the form for each customer before submitting it for review. If you've already filled out this form for a customer in the past, do not fill it out again. If you're unsure about any information, contact the sales team for guidance.
