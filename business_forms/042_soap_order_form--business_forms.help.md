# Soap Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soap Order Form is a tool for customers to place orders for soap products. It requires customers to provide their name, contact information, order details, and delivery preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Customer Name**. Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
2. Enter a valid **Customer Email**. This is used for communication and order tracking purposes.
3. Choose a **Date** for your order delivery. Select the date you'd like to receive your soap order.
4. Indicate the **Quantity** of soap you'd like to order. Please enter a number to specify how many bars of soap you'd like.
5. Select the type of **Soap** you'd like to order from the options provided:
	* Bar
	* Liquid
6. Select the **Soap Color** you'd like to have from the options provided:
	* Red
	* Blue
	* Green
	* Yellow
7. Enter your **Customer Phone**. This is for delivery confirmation and any follow-up communication.
8. If you have any additional **Notes**, feel free to add them in the designated field. This is optional and helps us understand any special requests or preferences you may have.
9. Choose where you'd like to have your order **Delivered**:
	* Store
	* Online
10. Select the **Delivery Date** for your order. This is when you'd like to receive your soap products.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (Text, Required): This is the name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Customer Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address for communication and order tracking.
* **Date** (Date, Required): Choose the date you'd like to receive your order.
* **Quantity** (Number, Required): Enter the number of soap bars you'd like to order.
* **Soap Type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of soap you'd like:
	+ Bar
	+ Liquid
* **Soap Color** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the color of soap you'd like:
	+ Red
	+ Blue
	+ Green
	+ Yellow
* **Customer Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for delivery confirmation and follow-up communication.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or requests you'd like to share.
* **Delivery Location** (Select One, Required): Choose where you'd like to have your order delivered:
	+ Store
	+ Online
* **Delivery Date** (Date, Required): Select the date you'd like to receive your soap order.
