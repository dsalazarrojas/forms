# Field Level Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Level Risk Assessment Form is designed to help identify and categorize risks in a structured way, facilitating effective risk management across the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review each field carefully and understand its purpose.
* Fill in the required fields (those marked as "required") according to the options provided.
* For fields with multiple options, select the most appropriate one that best describes the risk's situation.
* Provide clear and concise answers in text fields.
* If a field does not apply, leave it blank or select "None" or "Not Applicable" if applicable.
* Ensure you have all necessary information before submitting the form to maintain data quality and consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Risk Level (Page 1)
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, `select_one`, High/Low): Indicate the overall risk level, selecting between High, Low, or the risk level that best describes the situation.

### Risk Description (Page 1)
* **Risk Description** (`risk_description`, `text`): Briefly describe the risk in one or a few sentences. Be clear and concise in your explanation.

### Risk Frequency (Page 2)
* **Risk Frequency** (`risk_frequency`, `select_one`, Rare/Occasional/Regular): Indicate how frequently this risk occurs.

### Risk Impact (Page 3)
* **Risk Impact** (`risk_impact`, `select_multiple`, High/Medium/Low): Select all that apply to describe the impact of the risk. For example, if a risk affects both high and low level, select both.

### Risk Probability (Page 4)
* **Risk Probability** (`risk_probability`, `number`, ): Enter a numerical value between 0 and 100 to describe the risk's likelihood.

### Has risk been previously assessed? (Page 5)
* **Has risk been previously assessed?** (`risk_previously_assessed`, `select_one`, True/False): Indicate if the risk has been previously assessed in the organization. This will help track changes over time.

### Risk Status (Page 6)
* **Risk Status** (`risk_status`, `select_one`, Open/Closed): Describe the risk's status in the organization.

### Risk Action (Page 7)
* **Risk Action** (`risk_action`, `select_multiple`, Yes/No): Indicate the current action taken on this risk. Select all that apply.

### Comments (Page 8)
* **Comments** (`risk_comments`, `note`): Provide any additional information about the risk. This is an open field, so use it as needed.

### Recommendations (Page 9)
* **Recommendations** (`risk_recommendations`, `text`): Suggest actions or next steps for addressing the risk. Be concise and practical.

### Next Steps (Page 10)
* **Next Steps** (`risk_next_steps`, `text`): Describe the future steps or plans related to this risk.

### Responsible Party (Page 11)
* **Responsible Party** (`risk_responsible_party`, `select_one`, Yes/No): Indicate if a specific party (person, team, or department) is directly responsible for this risk.

### Review Status (Page 12)
* **Review Status** (`risk_review_status`, `select_one`, Not Reviewed/Reviewed): Indicate if this risk has been reviewed by a relevant authority or team.

This guide should be followed closely to ensure accurate and consistent completion of the Field Level Risk Assessment Form, which will help facilitate effective risk management across the organization.
