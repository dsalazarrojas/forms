# Fashion Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Order Form is designed to collect customer details and order information for fashion orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your fashion order information in the text field.
2. Provide your customer contact details in the second text field.
3. Enter your shipping address in the third text field.
4. Provide your customer order information in the fourth text field.
5. Enter your payment details in the final text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fashion Order Form** (`fashion_order_form`, text, optional): Enter your fashion order information in this field.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Provide your customer contact details, such as your name, email, and phone number.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Enter your customer details, including any relevant information for the order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your delivery address to ensure accurate shipping.
* **Customer Order** (`customer_order`, text, optional): Enter your order information, including any specific items or products you wish to order.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter your payment details, including payment method and amount.
