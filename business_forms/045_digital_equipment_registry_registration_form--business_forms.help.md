# Digital Equipment Registry Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register digital equipment for internal reference and tracking purposes. Before proceeding, ensure you only submit one form per digital device. If you're registering multiple devices, use the "Equipment List" field to select all relevant devices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the "User Input" section**:
	* Enter the name and contact information of the user associated with the digital equipment.
2. **Select all applicable equipment from the "Equipment List"**:
	* Choose the type of digital equipment you are registering (e.g., smartphone, tablet, laptop).
3. **Provide equipment details (optional)**:
	* If you want to include additional information about the equipment, such as its serial number or manufacturer, use this field.
4. **Enter purchase date and price (optional)**:
	* If you have records of when and how much you purchased the equipment, enter the date and price here.
5. **Submit the form**:
	* Once you've filled out all required and optional fields, click the submit button to register the digital equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required):
	+ Enter the name of the person or department associated with the digital equipment.
* **Equipment List** (`equipment_list`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the type of digital equipment you are registering (e.g., smartphone, tablet, laptop).
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter additional information about the equipment, such as its serial number or manufacturer.
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any other relevant information about the user associated with the digital equipment.
* **Date Purchased** (`date_purchased`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date you purchased the digital equipment.
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the price you paid for the digital equipment.
