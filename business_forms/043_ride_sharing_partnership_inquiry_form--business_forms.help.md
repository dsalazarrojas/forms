# ride_sharing_partnership_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ride sharing partnership inquiry form is used to gather information from potential partners or collaborators interested in exploring partnership opportunities with our company. This form helps us understand their interests, needs, and preferences, allowing us to tailor our approach to their specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:
- Fill out the form with your name and contact details (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone).
- Choose your company or location from the list (if applicable).
- Select the industry or services you offer (if applicable).
- Specify the type of partnership you are interested in (if applicable).
- Write a brief message or reason for your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Company**: Select your company from the list (if you are representing a company).
* **Location**: Choose your location from the list (e.g., New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston).
* **Industry**: Select one or more industries that apply to your business or services (e.g., Technology, Finance, Marketing).
* **Services Offered**: Describe the services you offer or would like to partner on.
* **Partner Type**: Select one or more types of partnership you are interested in (e.g., Company, Individual, Organization).
* **Partner Name**: Enter the name of your partner or company (if applicable).
* **Inquiry Type**: Choose the type of partnership you are interested in (e.g., Business Development, Partnerships, Other).
* **Inquiry Reason**: Briefly explain why you are interested in partnering with us.
* **Follow Up**: Schedule a follow-up call or meeting (optional).
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
* **Message**: Write a brief message to express your interest in partnering with us.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- If you have any supporting documents or materials, please attach them to your submission.
- We will review and respond to your inquiry within a reasonable time frame.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
