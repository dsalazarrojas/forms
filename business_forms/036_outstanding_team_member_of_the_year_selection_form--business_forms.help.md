# Outstanding Team Member Of The Year Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to select and recognize an outstanding team member of the year for their contributions and dedication to their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the outstanding team member of the year from the available options.
2. Provide a brief reason for their nomination.
3. Enter the year of nomination.
4. Fill in the team member's email and phone number.
5. Choose the assigned tool that the team member uses.
6. Write a descriptive paragraph about the team member's outstanding work.
7. Enter the person or department to whom the form should be addressed.
8. Enter a brief description of the category or team the team member belongs to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Member of the Year** (`title`, select_one, required): This field is where you select the outstanding team member of the year. Please choose from the available options.
* **Nomination Reason** (`Nomination Reason`, text, required): Enter a brief reason for why this team member is being nominated. This can be a sentence or two explaining their outstanding work.
* **Year of Nomination** (`Year of Nomination`, number, required): Enter the year when the team member's outstanding work was done.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the team member's email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter the team member's phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, select_one, required): Choose the tool or software used by the team member.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, required): Write a descriptive paragraph about the team member's outstanding work.
* **Assigned To/By** (`Assigned To/By`, text, required): Enter the person or department to whom this form should be addressed.
* **Category** (`Category`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the category or team the team member belongs to.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or details about the team member.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields correctly to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer back to the form's purpose and explanation above.
* If you need more information about the form, contact your supervisor or team lead for assistance.
