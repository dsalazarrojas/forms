# Farm Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about farms, including farm name, category, crop type, location, and contact information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Farm Name** (`farm_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your farm.
- **Farm Category** (`farm_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of your farm from the dropdown menu.
- **Crop Type** (`crop_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the relevant crop types from the dropdown menu.
- **Farm Location** (`farm_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your farm.
- **Soil Quality** (`soil_quality`, select_one, optional): Select the soil quality of your farm from the dropdown menu.
- **Farm Health** (`farm_health`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the farm health.
- **Harvest Date** (`harvest_date`, date, optional): Enter the harvest date of your crops in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Farm Size** (`farm_size`, number, optional): Enter the farm size.
- **Farm Equipment** (`farm_equipment`, text, optional): Enter any equipment used for farming.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the contact person.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the farm.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the farm.
- **Farm Category Other** (`farm_category_other`, text, optional): Enter any other farm category.
- **Farm Location Other** (`farm_location_other`, text, optional): Enter any other farm location.
- **Crop Type Other** (`crop_type_other`, text, optional): Enter any other crop type.
- **Farm Equipment Other** (`farm_equipment_other`, text, optional): Enter any other farm equipment.
- **Contact Person Other** (`contact_person_other`, text, optional): Enter any other contact person.
