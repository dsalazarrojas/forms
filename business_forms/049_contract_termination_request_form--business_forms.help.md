# Contract Termination Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request contract termination from the company. It is a formal process that requires careful consideration and adherence to the company's contract termination policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary approvals before submitting the form.
3. Review the contract terms and conditions to ensure that you are not requesting termination for an unnecessary or unjustified reason.
4. Attach any supporting documents or evidence as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Name** (1, Text, Required): Enter your full name as the person requesting contract termination.
* **Requestor Title** (2, Text, Required): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (3, Select One, Required): Select your department from the list.
* **Request Date** (4, Date, Required): Enter the date of the request.
* **Contract Number** (5, Text, Required): Enter the contract reference number.
* **Contract Title** (6, Text, Required): Enter the name of the contract.
* **Contract Date** (7, Date, Required): Enter the original contract date.
* **Other Party Name** (8, Text, Required): Enter the counterparty name.
* **Termination Type** (9, Select One, Required): Select the type of termination.
* **Termination Reason** (10, Text, Required): Explain why you are requesting termination.
* **Proposed Termination Date** (11, Date, Required): Enter the proposed termination date.
* **Notice Period Required** (12, Number, Optional): Enter the number of days required for notice period.
* **Notice Period Given** (13, Number, Optional): Enter the number of days given.
* **Contract Value** (14, Number, Required): Enter the total contract amount.
* **Amount Paid to Date** (15, Number, Required): Enter the amount paid so far.
* **Outstanding Payments** (16, Number, Optional): Enter the amount still owed.
* **Deliverables Status** (17, Select One, Required): Select the status of deliverables.
* **Outstanding Obligations** (18, Text, Required): Describe what remains undone.
* **Transition Plan** (19, Text, Required): Explain how to handle transition.
* **Property to Return** (20, Text, Optional): Enter any property involved.
* **Confidential Information** (21, Text, Optional): Explain how to handle confidential data.
* **Legal Review Required** (22, Select One, Required): Select whether legal review is required.
* **Financial Impact** (23, Select One, Required): Select the financial impact of termination.
* **Termination Penalty** (24, Number, Optional): Enter the penalty amount if any.
* **Approvals Needed** (25, Select Multiple, Required): Select the approvers required for termination.
* **Additional Comments** (26, Text, Optional): Enter any other relevant information. 

**Tips:**
* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form.
* Ensure that you have all necessary approvals before submitting the form.
* Attach supporting documents or evidence as needed.
* Review contract terms and conditions carefully.
* Double-check your selections for accuracy.
