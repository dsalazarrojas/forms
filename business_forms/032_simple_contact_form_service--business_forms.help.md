# Simple Contact Form Service - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Contact Form Service is a tool for users to provide contact information, select a category, and optionally upload a file, allowing your business to receive and manage customer feedback and inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information on the first page.
2. Select the relevant category for your contact or inquiry on the second page.
3. (Optional) If you want to upload a file, such as a document or attachment, fill in the "Output File" field on the third page.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send the form data to the relevant department or contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (1)**:
	+ A text field to enter your name, phone number, and/or email address.
* **Category (2)**:
	+ A dropdown menu to select a category for your contact or inquiry, such as "Business Forms", "Contact Forms", etc.
* **Description (3)**:
	+ A text field to provide a brief description of your contact or inquiry, if desired.
* **Output File (4)**:
	+ An optional text field to upload a file, such as a document or attachment, to accompany your contact or inquiry.
* **Assigned Tool (5)**:
	+ A text field to enter or select a tool assigned to your contact or inquiry, if applicable.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk or a red asterisk icon).
- If you are uploading a file, please ensure it is in a supported format.
- You can upload multiple files if necessary, but please follow the format and size guidelines.
