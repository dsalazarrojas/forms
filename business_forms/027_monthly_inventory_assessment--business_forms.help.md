# Monthly Inventory Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Monthly Inventory Assessment form is intended to help businesses track and analyze their inventory levels, identify potential issues, and inform decision-making processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a different aspect of inventory management. Please complete each section as instructed.
2. Review each field carefully and select the most accurate options or enter the required information.
3. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields, as indicated by the "required" label in the field descriptions.
4. Once you have completed all sections, review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Count** (`Stock Counts`, Select Multiple, not required): Select the count of a product or item in stock.
* **Locations** (`Stock Locations`, Select Multiple, not required): Choose the locations where the product is currently stored.
* **Issue** (`Issues`, Text, not required): Describe any issues or problems associated with the product.
* **Date** (`Date`, Date, not required): Enter the date of the assessment (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Time** (`Time`, Time, not required): Enter the time of the assessment (HH:MM format).
* **Issue Description** (`Issue Description`, Text, not required): Provide a detailed description of the issue.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully and select the most accurate options or enter the correct information.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the form's purpose and sections to ensure you're providing relevant information.
* If you need to add or modify any information, please use the provided fields to do so.
