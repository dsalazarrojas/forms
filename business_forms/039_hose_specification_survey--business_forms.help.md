# hose_specification_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the specifications of a hose for internal business operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Select the correct options for each field from the dropdown menus.
3. Make sure to enter your contact information in the relevant fields.
4. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Hose ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the hose.
- **Size**: Select the size of the hose from the options (small, medium, large).
- **Color**: Select multiple colors that apply to the hose from the options (blue, red, green).
- **Length**: Enter the length of the hose.
- **Material**: Select the material of the hose from the options (steel, plastic, wood).
- **Diameter**: Enter the diameter of the hose.
- **Wall Thickness**: Enter the wall thickness of the hose.
- **Material Thickness**: Enter the material thickness of the hose.
- **Weight**: Enter the weight of the hose.
- **Height**: Enter the height of the hose.
- **Manufacturer**: Select the manufacturer of the hose from the options (abc, def, ghi).
- **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the hose.
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
- **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Department**: Select the department involved in the hose specification (sales, engineering, quality assurance).
- **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Fax**: Enter the fax number of the contact person.
- **Mobile**: Enter the mobile number of the contact person.
- **Address**: Enter the address of the contact person.
- **City**: Enter the city of the contact person.
- **State**: Enter the state of the contact person.
- **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the contact person.
- **Country**: Enter the country of the contact person.
- **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete the form.

Please note that this form is for internal business use only, and all fields are optional unless specified as required.
