# life_design_detailed_information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to aid life designers in assessing their personal and health information for providing personalized life solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Assessment** (`Personal Assessment`, text, required/optional): This field is used to gather your thoughts and feelings about your life. Describe your personal situation and any challenges you are facing.
* **Requirements for Participating** (`Requirements for Participating`, text, required/optional): List any specific needs or requirements you have for participating in a life design program or service.
* **Health Information** (`Health Information`, text, required/optional): Please provide any relevant health information, such as medical conditions or medication, that may impact your life design process.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
