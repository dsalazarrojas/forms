# Job Tracking - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Job Tracking form is a tool for tracking job-related activities, providing a structured way to record and manage job-related data. Its primary purpose is to gather information about individual jobs and allow users to monitor and analyze job progression. Please note that this form is not a comprehensive solution for complex job management but rather a simple tool for tracking basic job data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Job Tracking form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the job date in the format specified for this field.
2. Select the job status from the options provided.
3. Input the job start time in the format specified for this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Date** (`Job Date`, `date`, required/optional: False): Enter the date the job started.
* **Job Status** (`Job Status`, `select_one`, required/optional: False): Select the job status using the provided options: Active or Inactive.
* **Job Start Time** (`Job Start Time`, `time`, required/optional: False): Enter the time the job started.

## Tips
* Ensure to enter accurate job dates and times to maintain precise record-keeping.
* Review the job status frequently to reflect any changes in job activity.
* If a job's status is Inactive, use the Job Start Time to indicate when the job stopped being active.
