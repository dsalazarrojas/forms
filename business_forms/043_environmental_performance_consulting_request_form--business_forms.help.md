# Environmental Performance Consulting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Performance Consulting Request Form is designed for clients to request services related to environmental performance consulting from our team. This form helps us understand your company's needs and preferences, ensuring we can provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in your company's information, including the **Company Name**, **Contact Name**, and **Phone** fields.
*   Choose your company's size (small, medium, large) in the **Company Size** field.
*   Select the industries you operate in from the **Industry** field (Finance, Manufacturing, Technology).
*   Choose the services you need from the **Service Requested** field (Sustainability Consulting, Environmental Audits, Energy Efficiency).
*   Select your preferred contact method from the **Preferred Contact Method** field (Phone, Email, Chat).
*   Provide a description of your request in the **Description** field.
*   If applicable, select a follow-up date from the **Follow-up Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name**: Enter your company's name.
*   **Contact Name**: Enter your name and title.
*   **Email**: Enter your company's official email address.
*   **Phone**: Enter your company's phone number.
*   **Company Size**:
    +   Small: Less than 10 employees.
    +   Medium: 10-50 employees.
    +   Large: 51 or more employees.
*   **Industry**: Select all the industries your company operates in.
*   **Service Requested**:
    +   Sustainability Consulting: Services to help you manage and maintain your company's environmental sustainability.
    +   Environmental Audits: Assessments and evaluations of your company's environmental performance.
    +   Energy Efficiency: Consulting services to help you reduce energy consumption.
*   **Preferred Contact Method**:
    +   Phone: You would like to receive calls from our team.
    +   Email: You would like to receive emails from our team.
    +   Chat: You would like to communicate with our team via chat.
*   **Description**: A brief description of your request.
*   **Submission Date**: The date you're submitting your request.
*   **Submission Time**: The time you're submitting your request.
*   **Assigned To**: The person assigned to handle your request.
*   **Assigned Date**: The date your request was assigned.
*   **Comments**: Any additional comments or notes about your request.
*   **Follow-up Needed**:
    +   True: You would like to be contacted for follow-up questions or services.
    +   False: You do not need further contact or services.
*   **Follow-up Date**: The date for any follow-up contact or services.

Remember, this form is not a duplicate, so please submit it only once for the same services or department. If you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out to the assigned contact.
