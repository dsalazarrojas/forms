# Virtual Office Hours Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Office Hours Form is a tool for gathering information about virtual office hours sessions. This form is used to capture details about the sessions, such as title, description, category, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the title and description of the virtual office hours session.
2. Choose the category of the session (Business Forms or Other Forms).
3. Select the tool assigned to the session (chatjimmy or other).
4. If applicable, enter the form ID and output file information.
5. Choose who the session was submitted to (Professor or Other).
6. Fill out the submitted date and time.
7. Enter the form version.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Virtual Office Hours Form** (form_title, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for the virtual office hours session.
* **Description** (form_description, text, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of the virtual office hours session.
* **Category** (form_category, select_one, required/optional): Select the category of the virtual office hours session. The options are Business Forms or Other Forms.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool, select_one, required): Select the tool assigned to the virtual office hours session. The options are chatjimmy or other.
* **Form ID** (form_ids, number, required/optional): If applicable, enter the form ID for the virtual office hours session.
* **Output File** (output_file, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter information about the output file for the virtual office hours session.
* **Submitted** (submitted, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the virtual office hours session was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (submitted_by, time, required/optional): Enter the time when the virtual office hours session was submitted.
* **Submitted To** (submitted_to, select_one, required): Select who the virtual office hours session was submitted to. The options are Professor or Other.
* **Form Version** (form_version, text, required/optional): Enter the version of the virtual office hours form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields for accurate data collection.
- Double-check the submitted date and time for accuracy.
- Ensure that the submitted to field is accurate for correct routing.
