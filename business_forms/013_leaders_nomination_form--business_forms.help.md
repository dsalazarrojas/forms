#ประก Leaders Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect nominations for leaders within an organization. It helps recognize and appreciate individuals who have made significant contributions to the organization's success.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the following information:
	* Nominee Name: Enter the name of the individual you are nominating.
	* Nominator Title: Enter your job title or position within the organization.
	* Nominator Email: Enter your organizational email address.
	* Nominee Position: Enter the position or role of the individual you are nominating.
	* Nominator Position: Enter your position or role within the organization.
	* Nomination Date: Select the date of the nomination (optional).
	* Nomination Time: Select the time of the nomination (optional).
	* Nominee Notes: Add any additional notes about the nominee (optional).
	* Nominator Notes: Add any additional notes about yourself (optional).
	* Reason for Nomination: Provide a brief explanation of why you are nominating this individual.
	* Nominee Contact Info: Enter any additional contact information about the nominee (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`): Enter the name of the individual you are nominating.
* **Nominator Title** (`nominator_title`): Enter your job title or position within the organization.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`): Enter your organizational email address.
* **Nominee Position** (`nominee_position`): Enter the position or role of the individual you are nominating.
* **Nominator Position** (`nominator_position`): Enter your position or role within the organization.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`): Select the date of the nomination (optional). If no date is selected, the current date will be used.
* **Nomination Time** (`nomination_time`): Select the time of the nomination (optional). If no time is selected, the current time will be used.
* **Nominee Notes** (`nominee_notes`): Add any additional notes about the nominee (optional).
* **Nominator Notes** (`nominator_notes`): Add any additional notes about yourself (optional).
* **Reason for Nomination** (`reason_for_nomination`): Provide a brief explanation of why you are nominating this individual.
* **Nominee Contact Info** (`nominee_contact_info`): Enter any additional contact information about the nominee (optional).
