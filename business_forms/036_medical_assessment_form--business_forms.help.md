# medical_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document the patient's medical history, including medical conditions, allergies, medications, and medical tests. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to inform treatment decisions and ensure patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Medical History**: Enter the patient's medical history, including any previous surgeries, illnesses, or conditions. This field is required.
2. **Medical Conditions**: Select all the medical conditions that apply to the patient from the provided options. This field is required.
3. **Allergies**: If the patient has any known allergies, enter them in this field. (Optional)
4. **Other Medical Conditions**: If the patient has any other medical conditions not listed in the previous field, enter them here. (Optional)
5. **Current Medications**: Enter the patient's current medications, including dosages and frequency. (Optional)
6. **Medication History**: Enter the patient's past medical history, including dates and details of past illnesses or conditions. (Optional)
7. **Medical Tests**: Enter any recent or upcoming medical tests or procedures. (Optional)
8. **Medical Procedure**: Enter any upcoming or recent medical procedures the patient is undergoing. (Optional)
9. **Medical History Date**: Enter the date of the patient's most recent medical history. (Optional)
10. **Medical History Time**: Enter the time of the patient's most recent medical history. (Optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Medical History**: The patient's medical history should include any previous surgeries, illnesses, or conditions the patient has experienced.
*   **Medical Conditions**: Select all the medical conditions that apply to the patient from the provided options. This field is required to ensure the patient's condition is accurately recorded.
*   **Allergies**: If the patient has any known allergies, enter them in this field. If unsure, leave blank.
*   **Other Medical Conditions**: If the patient has any other medical conditions not listed in the previous field, enter them here.
*   **Current Medications**: Enter the patient's current medications, including dosages and frequency. If unsure, leave blank.
*   **Medication History**: Enter the patient's past medical history, including dates and details of past illnesses or conditions. If unsure, leave blank.
*   **Medical Tests**: Enter any recent or upcoming medical tests or procedures. If unsure, leave blank.
*   **Medical Procedure**: Enter any upcoming or recent medical procedures the patient is undergoing. If unsure, leave blank.
*   **Medical History Date**: Enter the date of the patient's most recent medical history. If unsure, leave blank.
*   **Medical History Time**: Enter the time of the patient's most recent medical history. If unsure, leave blank.

## Tips
*   Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   Enter accurate and complete information to ensure patient care is tailored to their specific needs.
*   If unsure about any field, refer to this guide for clarification.
*   If you need further assistance, contact your healthcare provider.
