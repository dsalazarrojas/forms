# Business Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Insurance Quote Form is designed to collect information from business prospects who are interested in obtaining insurance quotes for their business. This form helps us understand the prospect's business details, insurance needs, and preferences, allowing us to provide accurate and relevant quotes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Enter your business's legal name and phone number.
3. Select your business's industry sector and estimated annual gross revenue.
4. Indicate your desired coverage types, liability limits, and preferred deductible level.
5. Let us know if you have current or previous insurance coverage.
6. Choose your preferred method of receiving the quote.
7. Select a target policy start date.
8. Finally, provide the date you would like to submit your quote request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (`prospect_full_name_quote`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it should be displayed on the quote.
* **Email Address** (`prospect_contact_email_quote`, `email`, required): Enter your business email address where you would like to receive the quote.
* **Phone Number** (`prospect_phone_number_quote`, `text`, required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Legal Name** (`business_legal_name_quote_form`, `text`, required): Enter your business's legal name as registered with the relevant authorities.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector_quote_selection`, `select_one`, required): Select the most relevant category that best describes your business's industry.
* **Estimated Annual Gross Revenue** (`annual_revenue_estimate_bracket_quote`, `select_one`, required): Choose your estimated annual gross revenue range.
* **Desired Coverage Types** (`desired_coverage_types_selection_quote`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the insurance coverage types you are interested in.
* **Desired Liability Limits** (`desired_liability_limits_quote_choice`, `select_one`, required): Select the desired liability limit per occurrence.
* **Preferred Deductible Level** (`preferred_deductible_level_quote_choice`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred out-of-pocket deductible level.
* **Do you have current or previous insurance coverage** (`previous_insurance_coverage_history`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have current or previous insurance coverage.
* **Preferred Quote Delivery Method** (`quote_delivery_method_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to receive the quote.
* **Target Policy Start Date** (`expected_policy_start_date_quote`, `date`, required): Select the target policy start date.
* **Quote Request Submission Date** (`quote_request_submission_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you would like to submit your quote request.
