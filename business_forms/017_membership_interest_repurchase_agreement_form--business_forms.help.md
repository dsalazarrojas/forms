# Membership Interest Repurchase Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for companies that want to repurchase their shares or securities from their existing shareholders. It's a way to collect information about the company's intentions and to document the agreement between the company and the shareholder.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, including the company name and address, representative name, email, contact number, and other relevant details.
2. Select the appropriate agreement type from the dropdown menu.
3. Add any additional agreement details in the text field.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your intention to repurchase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name and Address** (`company_name_address`, text, not required): Enter the name and address of the company interested in repurchasing their shares or securities.
* **Company Representative Name** (`company_representative_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the company representative who will be handling the repurchase.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the company representative.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Enter the contact number for the company representative.
* **Company Representative Contact Number** (`company_representative_contact_number`, text, not required): Enter the contact number for the company representative.
* **Agreement Type** (`agreement_type`, select one, not required): Select the type of agreement you want to enter into.
* **Agreement Details** (`agreement_details`, text, not required): Add any additional information you want to include in the agreement.
* **Signature** (`signature`, time, not required): Date and sign the form to confirm your intention to repurchase.
