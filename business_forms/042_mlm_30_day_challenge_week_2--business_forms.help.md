# mlm 30 day challenge week 2 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The ML 30 Day Challenge is a self-assessment exercise designed to help you evaluate your progress and set realistic expectations for reaching specific goals. This form guides you through a brief weekly self-assessment for week 2 of the challenge. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly, taking about 2-3 minutes to complete.
2. Enter your answers in the text fields provided.
3. Select the correct answers for the progress question.
4. Submit your form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro** (`title`, `text`, required): Briefly introduce yourself and describe your current situation, including any relevant details or experiences that have occurred since the last challenge week (week 1). 
* **Progress** (`progress`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the progress indicators that apply to you from the list:
	+ Yes, I've completed my task for the week
	+ No, I didn't complete my task for the week
* **Goals** (`goals`, `text`, optional): If you feel that your goals for the week have changed or if you'd like to set new ones, provide a brief explanation here. You can set realistic expectations and describe the changes or new goals you want to achieve in the upcoming week.
