# Eco Friendly Materials Supplier Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Friendly Materials Supplier Contact Form is designed to collect contact information and material requests from suppliers of eco-friendly materials. It's a simple and straightforward form that helps us gather essential details about your company and material needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
2.  Provide a brief description of your company in the "Company Info" section.
3.  Share your specific material request in the "Material Request" section.
4.  Choose your preferred contact method (phone, email, or other) in the "Contact Method" section.
5.  Finally, provide additional details about your contact method in the "Contact Method Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Contact Info (<name>: contact_info, <type>: text):**
    Your name, email address, and other relevant contact information.
*   **Company Info (<name>: company_info, <type>: text):**
    A brief description of your company, including its name and any relevant details.
*   **Material Request (<name>: material_request, <type>: text):**
    A specific description of the material you're looking for, including its type and quantity needed.
*   **Contact Method (<name>: contact_method, <type>: select_one):**
    Your preferred contact method: Phone, Email, or Other. Please select one option.
*   **Contact Method Details (<name>: contact_method_details, <type>: text):**
    If you've chosen Phone or Email as your contact method, please provide your contact number or email address. If you've selected Other, please describe your preferred contact method.

## Tips

-   Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
-   If you've already submitted a similar form or have a pre-existing relationship with the supplier, please don't submit this form again.
-   Our team will review and follow up with your request as soon as possible.
