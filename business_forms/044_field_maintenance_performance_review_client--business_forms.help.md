# field_maintenance_performance_review_client - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to review and maintain performance metrics for clients. It's likely used for assessing their progress, evaluating their performance, or evaluating their maintenance needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a title for the review in the `form_title` field. This will help you and the client identify the review.
2. Choose the period of performance you want to review in the `performance_period` field. This could be a specific date range or a general period of time.
3. Select the start and end dates of the review period using the `start_date` and `end_date` fields. This will help you track the duration of the review.
4. Choose the type of review you want to conduct in the `review_type` field. Options are:
	* Yes
	* No
5. Select the reviewer(s) who will be conducting the review in the `reviewer` field. Options are:
	* Yes
	* No
6. Enter the client's name in the `client_name` field.
7. Enter the client's phone number in the `client_phone` field.
8. Add any additional review notes in the `review_notes` field.
9. Select the review's status in the `review_status` field. Options are:
	* Active
	* Inactive

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title** (`form_title`, text, optional): A title for the review. This will help you and the client identify the review.
* **performance_period** (`performance_period`, number, optional): The period of performance you want to review. This could be a specific date range or a general period of time.
* **start_date** (`start_date`, text, optional): The start date of the review period.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, text, optional): The end date of the review period.
* **review_type** (`review_type`, select_one, optional): The type of review you want to conduct. Options are:
	* Yes
	* No
* **reviewer** (`reviewer`, select_multiple, optional): The reviewer(s) conducting the review. Options are:
	* Yes
	* No
* **client_name** (`client_name`, text, optional): The client's name.
* **client_phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): The client's phone number.
* **review_notes** (`review_notes`, text, optional): Additional notes for the review.
* **review_status** (`review_status`, select_one, optional): The review's status. Options are:
	* Active
	* Inactive

## Tips
* Make sure you have the necessary information ready before starting the form.
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and consistency.
* This form is designed to be completed by one or more reviewers. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
