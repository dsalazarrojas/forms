# Editor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect editor's personal details for internal purposes. It is not intended for external publication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the First Name field with the editor's first name.
2. Please fill in the Last Name field with the editor's last name.
3. Enter the editor's Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter the editor's Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter the editor's Date of Birth in the Date Of Birth field, in the format MM/DD/YY.
6. Enter the editor's Time of Birth in the Time Of Birth field, in the format HH:MM:SS.
7. You can add any additional notes about the editor in the Note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (editor_first_name)**: Enter the editor's first name.
* **Last Name (editor_last_name)**: Enter the editor's last name.
* **Email (editor_email)**: Enter a valid email address for the editor.
* **Phone (editor_phone)**: Enter the editor's phone number.
* **Date Of Birth (editor_date_of_birth)**: Enter the editor's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Time Of Birth (editor_time_of_birth)**: Enter the editor's time of birth in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Note (editor_note)**: Add any additional notes about the editor.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the email address is valid and formatted correctly.
* The date and time fields are not required, but if entered, should be in the correct format.
* You can add or remove pages as needed.
* Please review the form's purpose and required fields before submitting.
