# Main Contract Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Main Contract Agreement form is designed to gather essential information about contracts between parties in a business setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the contract terms and conditions carefully.
2. Fill out the form with accurate information about the contract.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
4. Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contract Terms**: This field is for entering the terms and conditions of the contract. It is a text field where you can enter the main points of the agreement.
* **Date Contract Signed**: This field is for entering the date when the contract was signed.
* **Time Contract Signed**: This field is for entering the time when the contract was signed.
* **Party A**: This field is for entering the name or description of Party A in the contract.
* **Party B**: This field is for entering the name or description of Party B in the contract.
* **Contract Duration**: This field is for entering the duration or length of the contract in number of days, weeks, or years.
* **Start Date**: This field is for entering the start date of the contract.
* **End Date**: This field is for entering the end date of the contract.
* **Terms and Conditions**: This field is for indicating whether the contract has terms and conditions.
* **Amendment Clause**: This field is for indicating whether the contract has an amendment clause.
* **Amendment Date**: This field is for entering the date of the amendment, if applicable.
* **Time of Amendment**: This field is for entering the time of the amendment, if applicable.
* **Party C**: This field is for entering the name or description of Party C in the contract, if applicable.
* **Additional Terms**: This field is for entering any additional terms or conditions not included in the main contract.
* **Additional Terms 1**: This field is for entering any further additional terms or conditions not included in the main contract.

Note: The fields with a label "select_multiple" allow you to select multiple options from the provided list.
