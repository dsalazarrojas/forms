# Regional Product Listing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Regional Product Listing Request Form is a tool for B2B customers to submit a request for a new product listing in our catalog. This form is designed to gather essential information about the product, allowing our team to efficiently review and process the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information about the product.
* Ensure that the product is not already listed in our catalog to avoid duplicate requests.
* Submit the form to initiate the review and approval process for the new product listing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product Name (Text)
 * **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you wish to list.

### Description (Note)
 * **Description**: Provide a brief description of the product, including its features and benefits.

### Tags (Select Multiple)
 * **Tags**: Select up to three relevant tags that describe the product. These tags will help our team categorize and filter the product in our catalog. (Example: tag1, tag2, tag3)

### Price (Number)
 * **Price**: Enter the price of the product. Note that prices must be numeric values.

### Region (Select One)
 * **Region**: Select the region where the product is to be listed. This ensures that the product is visible to customers in the correct region.

### Product Image (Date)
 * **Product Image**: Unfortunately, this field appears to be a misconfiguration. Please disregard this field as it is not currently functional. Instead, provide the URL of the product image or a brief description of the image.

### Date (Time)
 * **Date**: Enter the date when the product is available for listing. Note that this field is likely intended to capture the release date of the product but may not be currently functional.

### Contact Email (Email)
 * **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for the product listing request.

### Contact Phone (Text)
 * **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the product listing request.

### Additional Info (Note)
 * **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about the product, such as its product code or vendor information.

## Tips
* Be sure to proofread your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our team for assistance.
* We will review and process the request within a reasonable time frame, and we will be in touch with you to confirm the outcome of your request.
