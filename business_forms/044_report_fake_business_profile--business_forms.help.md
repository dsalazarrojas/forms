# Report Fake Business Profile - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report fake business profiles on the platform. It helps us identify and address suspicious or fake business profiles, which can improve the overall user experience on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the required fields: Business Type, Form Status, Report Reason, Language, and Reported By.
- Provide the business name and address if possible.
- Add any additional comments or notes to explain the reason for the report.
- Finally, select the correct Form Submit option to indicate that the form has been submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Type** (`report_fakes_business_profile_business_type`): Select the type of business you are reporting (Service or Product).
* **Form Status** (`report_fakes_business_profile_form_status`): Choose the status of the form, whether it is Active or Inactive.
* **Report Reason** (`report_fakes_business_profile_report_reason`): Select the reason for reporting the business profile (Fake Listing, Selling Fake Listings, or Spam).
* **Language** (`report_fakes_business_profile_form_language`): Select the language of the business profile (English, Spanish, or French).
* **Reported By** (`report_fakes_business_profile_reported_by`): Select who reported the business profile (User or Admin).
* **Form Submit** (`report_fakes_business_profile_submit`): Select one of the two options to indicate that the form has been submitted.
* **Business Name** (`report_fakes_business_profile_business_name`): Enter the name of the business being reported.
* **Address** (`report_fakes_business_profile_address`): Enter the address of the business being reported if possible.
* **City** (`report_fakes_business_profile_city`): Enter the city of the business being reported if possible.
* **State** (`report_fakes_business_profile_state`): Enter the state of the business being reported if possible.
* **Zip** (`report_fakes_business_profile_zip`): Enter the zip code of the business being reported if possible.
* **Phone** (`report_fakes_business_profile_phone`): Enter the phone number of the business being reported if possible.
* **Email** (`report_fakes_business_profile_email`): Enter the email address of the business being reported if possible.
* **Notes** (`report_fakes_business_profile_notes`): Add any additional comments or notes to explain the reason for the report.
* **Comments** (`report_fakes_business_profile_comments`): Add any additional comments or notes to provide more information about the report.
* **Form Submit Reason** (`report_fakes_business_profile_submit_reason`): Add any additional comments or notes to explain the reason for submitting the form.
* **Form Notes** (`report_fakes_business_profile_form_notes`): Add any additional comments or notes to provide more information about the form.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to help us better understand the issue.
- Use the available fields to provide as much information as you can about the business profile being reported.
- If you are reporting a business profile that you have previously reported, please make sure to provide an update on the reason for re-reporting.
