# Industrial Policy Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from clients or potential clients interested in industrial policy consulting services. The form helps us to categorize and filter the type of industry the client belongs to and assign the most suitable tool for their consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact name in the "Contact Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field (if you'd like us to contact you via email).
3. Select the type of industry your company belongs to in the "Organization" field (this will help us assign the correct tool for your consultation).
4. Choose the tool you'd like to use for your consultation in the "Assigned Tool" field (or provide an alternative in the "Other (please specify)" field if your preferred tool isn't listed).
5. Optionally, you can provide information about your phone number, job title, department, and email address in the fields provided, but this is not required.
6. Click submit to send your contact request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, `text`, required): Enter your contact name in this field.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): This field is not required, but if you'd like us to contact you via email, please provide your email address.
* **Message** (`Message`, `note`, optional): You can enter any additional information or questions you have in this field, if needed.
* **Organization** (`Organization`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of industry your company belongs to from the dropdown list.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide your phone number, you can do so here.
* **Industry** (`Industry`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of industry your company belongs to from the dropdown list.
* **Category** (`Category`, `text`, optional): You can provide any additional category information here, if needed.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, `select_one`, required): Select the tool you'd like to use for your consultation (or specify an alternative in the "Other (please specify)" field).
* **Other (please specify)** (`Other (please specify)`, `text`, optional): If you don't see your preferred tool listed, please provide its name in this field.
* **Assigned Tool Website** (`Assigned Tool Website`, `text`, optional): You can provide the website of the tool you've chosen.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, if you'd like to be identified as the person submitting the form.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title, if you'd like to be identified as the person submitting the form.
* **Department** (`Department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department, if you'd like to be identified as the person submitting the form.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): This field is not required, but if you'd like to be contacted via email, please provide your email address.
