# PowerCore We Did Business Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PowerCore We Did Business Template is a form designed to collect information about business activities. It helps the organization track and analyze the events, locations, and details of completed business transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and clear information.
3. Review the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description or note about the business activity.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, text, required/optional): Select or type the type of business that was done (e.g., meeting, call, email).
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the business activity took place.
* **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the business activity occurred.
* **Time of Event** (`time_of_event`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the business activity took place.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the business activity.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address associated with the business activity.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number associated with the business activity.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the business that was involved in the activity.
