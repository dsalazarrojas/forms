# Cir Check Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cir Check Request Form is designed to gather information from organizations seeking financial support from our foundation. This form helps us understand the organization's identity, project details, and financial requirements, ensuring a thorough review and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, please ensure you have all the necessary information and documents ready. Here's a step-by-step guide to help you complete the form:

1. Enter your name and contact information (fields 1-4).
2. Select your organization's type (field 6).
3. Provide your email address and phone number (fields 5-6).
4. Enter your organization's address (field 7).
5. Enter your tax ID or EIN number (field 8).
6. Specify the purpose and date of your request (fields 9-10).
7. Enter the amount of funds requested (field 11).
8. Describe the purpose of your donation in detail (field 12).
9. Outline the project or program that will benefit from the funds (field 13).
10. Provide information about the beneficiaries of your project (field 14).
11. Specify your project timeline (field 15).
12. Enter your total project budget (field 16).
13. Confirm if you have other funding sources (field 17).
14. List any other funding sources if applicable (field 18).
15. Confirm if you have received donations from us before (field 19).
16. Provide professional references (field 20).
17. Specify if supporting documents are available (field 21).
18. Confirm if you are willing to be contacted for more information (field 22).
19. Enter any additional comments (field 23).
20. Sign the form with your full name (field 24).
20. Date the signed form (field 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name as the person making the request.
* **Organization Name (2)**: Enter your organization's name, which should be a church, charity, or nonprofit.
* **Organization Type (3)**: Select the type of organization you represent (Church, Charity, Nonprofit, Foundation, School, or Other).
* **Email Address (4)**: Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Phone Number (5)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Organization Address (6)**: Enter your organization's street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Tax ID or EIN Number (7)**: Enter your tax ID or EIN number for verification.
* **Date of Request (8)**: Enter the date of your request.
* **Requested Check Amount (9)**: Enter the amount of funds requested.
* **Purpose of Donation Request (10)**: Select the purpose of your donation request (General operations, Building fund, Community outreach, Education programs, Relief efforts, Youth programs, or Other).
* **Please Describe Purpose in Detail (11)**: Enter a detailed description of your purpose.
* **Program or Project Description (12)**: Describe the project or program that will benefit from the funds.
* **Who Will Benefit (13)**: Describe the beneficiaries of your project or program.
* **Project Timeline (14)**: Enter your project's start and end dates.
* **Total Project Budget (15)**: Enter your total project budget.
* **Other Funding Sources (16)**: Confirm if you have other funding sources.
* **List Other Funding Sources (17)**: If you have other funding sources, list them here.
* **Have You Received Donations Before (18)**: Confirm if you have received donations from us before.
* **Professional References (19)**: Enter professional references' names and contact information.
* **Supporting Documents Available (20)**: Select all supporting documents you have available (501(c)(3) letter, Annual report, Financial statements, Program budget, Letters of support, or None).
* **May We Contact You for More Information (21)**: Confirm if you are willing to be contacted for more information.
* **Any Additional Comments (22)**: Enter any additional comments.
* **Authorized Signature (23)**: Sign the form with your full name.
* **Date (24)**: Date the signed form.

Note: Please ensure to fill out all required fields and follow the guidelines for each field to ensure a smooth and accurate review process.
