# Bhuntas Collection/E Store Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bhuntas Collection/E Store Onboarding Form is designed to collect information from vendors or sellers who want to onboard onto the Bhuntas platform. This form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of the vendor's business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your vendor information, including name, type, and contact details.
2. Enter your business details, including business registration number, tax ID, and address.
3. Provide information about your products, including categories, description, and pricing range.
4. Share your experience and background as a seller, including years of selling experience and online selling experience.
5. Select your preferred currency and payment method.
6. Choose your shipping capabilities and regions you can ship to.
7. Agree to the platform's terms and conditions.
8. Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor or Collection Name** (`vendor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your store or collection.
* **Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of vendor you are (Individual seller, Small business, Established brand, Collection/boutique, Designer, Manufacturer, Distributor, or Other).
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your main contact person.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Business Registration Number** (`business_registration`, `text`, optional): Enter your business registration number (if applicable).
* **Tax ID or EIN** (`tax_id`, `text`, required): Enter your tax ID or EIN.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required): Enter your business address.
* **City** (`business_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of your business address.
* **State or Province** (`business_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province of your business address.
* **Postal Code** (`business_postal`, `text`, required): Enter the postal code of your business address.
* **Country** (`business_country`, `text`, required): Enter the country of your business address.
* **Primary Product Categories** (`product_categories`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the categories that best describe your products (Clothing, Accessories, Jewelry, Home goods, Electronics, Handcrafted items, Vintage, Art and crafts, Health and wellness, Beauty products, or Other).
* **Brief Description of Your Products** (`product_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your products.
* **Average Product Price Range** (`average_price_range`, `select_one`, required): Select the typical price range of your products (Under $25, $25 - $50, $50 - $100, $100 - $250, $250 - $500, Over $500, Mixed range).
* **Estimated Number of Products** (`inventory_estimate`, `number`, optional): Enter an estimate of the number of SKUs you have.
* **Years of Selling Experience** (`selling_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of years you have been selling (Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, Over 10 years, No experience).
* **Online Selling Experience** (`online_selling_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of online selling experience (Extensive experience, Some experience, Limited experience, New to online selling).
* **Current Sales Channels** (`current_sales_channels`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the places where you currently sell (Own website, Social media, Other marketplaces, Physical store, Pop-up events, Wholesale, Not currently selling).
* **Bank Account Holder Name** (`bank_account_holder`, `text`, required): Enter the name on your bank account.
* **Bank Account Type** (`bank_account_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of bank account you have (Checking account, Savings account, Business account, PayPal or digital wallet).
* **Preferred Currency** (`currency_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select the preferred currency for payments (USD - US Dollar, EUR - Euro, GBP - British Pound, CAD - Canadian Dollar, AUD - Australian Dollar, Other).
* **Can You Handle Your Own Shipping** (`shipping_capability`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you can handle your own shipping (Yes, fully equipped, Yes, with some limitations, No, need marketplace support, No, need fulfillment service).
* **Shipping Regions** (`shipping_regions`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the regions you can ship to (Domestic only, North America, Europe, Worldwide, Need to discuss).
* **I Agree to Platform Terms and Conditions** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to the platform's terms and conditions (I agree, I do not agree).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your business.

Note: The required fields are marked with **.
