# Factory Process Evaluation Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help us evaluate and understand your company's needs and schedule for the Factory Process Evaluation Program. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start on the "Company Details" page, where you will need to enter some general information about your company.
2. On the "Objectives" page, specify the evaluation type and scope you are interested in.
3. On the "Contact Details" page, provide your preferred dates and contact information.
4. On the following pages, select the evaluation method, company type and evaluation frequency you prefer.
5. Finally, you can add any additional comments, if needed, and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Details**: Enter a brief description of your company.
* **Objectives**: Specify the type and scope of evaluation you are interested in (Evaluation, Review, or Assessment, and Factory, Process, or Program).
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name as it appears on your business documents.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact name, email, and phone number.
* **Preferred Dates**: Select the dates you prefer for the evaluation (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, or As Needed).
* **Evaluation Type**: Select the type of evaluation you are interested in (Evaluation, Review, or Assessment).
* **Evaluation Scope**: Select the scope of evaluation you are interested in (Factory, Process, or Program).
* **Evaluation Frequency**: Select the frequency of evaluation you prefer (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, or As Needed).
* **Email**: Enter your business email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your business phone number.
* **Company Type**: Select the type of company you are (Limited, Partnership, Corporation, or Sole Owner).
* **Evaluation Method**: Select the method of evaluation you prefer (Phone, Email, In-person, or Online).
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about your company.
* **Submission Date**: Select the date you wish to submit your application.

Tips:
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Use the options in the dropdown menus to guide you.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact us for further clarification.
