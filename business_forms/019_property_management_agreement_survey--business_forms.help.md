# Property Management Agreement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Management Agreement Survey is a feedback form designed to measure your satisfaction with our property management service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the relevant option for each field.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
4. Submit the form to share your feedback with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's the quality of our property management service?**: Rate your experience with our property management service. You may select one or more of the options: Very Satisfactory, Satisfactory, Average, or Unsatisfactory.
* **How reliable are the property management services?**: Rate the reliability of our property management services from 1 to 10.
* **How well are you communicated with regarding the property?**: Describe in a few words how well you were communicated with during the management process.
* **What's your overall satisfaction?**: Rate your overall satisfaction with our property management service. You may select one of the options: Very Satisfactory, Satisfactory, Average, or Unsatisfactory.
* **What value do you believe we provided?**: Rate the value you believe we provided from 1 to 10.
* **How would you rate the cost of our property management service?**: Rate the cost of our service from 1 to 10.
* **Any suggestions?**: Share any suggestions or comments you have about our property management service.
* **Contact information**: Please provide your email address so we can get back to you with any updates or follow-ups.

Note: Please be honest and thorough in your responses, as your feedback is invaluable to us. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
