# Eviction Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eviction Discharge Form is a document used to record the details of a tenant's discharge from a rental property. This form serves as a formal acknowledgment of the termination of a tenancy agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the person or entity (landlord/property manager) who is authorizing the discharge.
2.  Enter the name of the person or entity (tenant) being discharged.
3.  Provide a brief explanation for the reason(s) for the discharge.
4.  Enter the date the discharge was effective.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Landlord** (`landlord_name`, `text`, required: false):
    The name of the person or entity (landlord/property manager) who is authorizing the discharge.
*   **Tenants** (`tenant_name`, `text`, required: false):
    The name of the person or entity (tenant) being discharged.
*   **Reason for Discharge** (`reason_for_discharge`, `text`, required: false):
    A brief explanation for the reason(s) for the discharge. This field is meant to provide context for the discharge.
*   **Date Discharged** (`date_discharged`, `date`, required: false):
    The date when the discharge was effective.

## Tips
*   Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authority to complete this form.
*   This form is not a substitute for any legal document and may need to be complemented with other forms or documentation for official purposes.
*   Use this form as a starting point and expand upon it as needed to fit your specific situation.
