# Retail Tailor Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Tailor Referral Form is designed to collect information about customers who have referred new clients to the business. This form helps the business to track referrals, identify their sources, and determine the most effective marketing methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Customer Name, Customer Phone, and Customer Email of the person who referred the new client.
2. Enter the name of the Tailor or Alterations who referred the new client.
3. Enter your Employee ID or name if you are an employee of the business.
4. Enter the Business Name of the referral source.
5. Select the Date of the referral.
6. Choose the Referral Source from the list of options (e.g., Online Search, Print or TV Ad, Social Media, Word of Mouth, or Other).
7. Select the Referral Method(s) used (e.g., Online, Offline).
8. Add any additional Tailor Notes about the referral.
8. (Optional) Enter a Follow-up Date for a scheduled appointment or action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person who referred the new client.
* **Customer Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the person who referred the new client.
* **Customer Email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the person who referred the new client.
* **Tailor/Alterations Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the Tailor or Alterations who referred the new client.
* **Employee ID** (Text, Optional): If you are an employee of the business, enter your Employee ID number.
* **Employee Name** (Text, Optional): If you are an employee of the business, enter your name.
* **Business Name** (Text, Required): Enter the Business Name of the referral source.
* **Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the referral.
* **Referral Source** (Select One, Required): Choose the Referral Source from the list of options (e.g., Online Search, Print or TV Ad, Social Media, Word of Mouth, or Other).
* **Referral Method** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the Referral Method(s) used (e.g., Online, Offline).
* **Tailor Notes** (Text, Optional): Add any additional Tailor Notes about the referral.
* **Follow-up Date** (Date, Optional): (Optional) Enter a Follow-up Date for a scheduled appointment or action.
