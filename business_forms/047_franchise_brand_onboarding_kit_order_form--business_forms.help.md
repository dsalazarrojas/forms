# Franchise Brand Onboarding Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Franchise Brand Onboarding Kit Order Form is a comprehensive tool for franchisees to order and manage onboarding kits for their teams. This form helps us understand your onboarding needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your franchise team from the options provided.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your name and contact information (first name, last name, phone number, and address).
4. Select the type of kit you need.
5. Specify the number of kits and any additional requests.
6. Add any notes about your onboarding process.
7. Enter the requested date and time for delivery.
8. Provide information about your franchise location.
9. Enter your franchise name and number.
10. Specify the onboarding manager's contact information.
11. Select the review status of your onboarding process.
12. Add any comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Franchise Team** (`franchise_team`): Choose the team you are representing.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address where you would like to receive updates about your onboarding process.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_1`): Enter the address line 1 of your franchise location.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_2`): Enter the address line 2 of your franchise location.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city of your franchise location.
* **State** (`state`): Enter the state of your franchise location.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter the zip code of your franchise location.
* **Country** (`country`): Enter the country of your franchise location.
* **Kit Type** (`kit_type`): Choose the type of kit you need.
* **Number of Kits** (`number_of_kits`): Specify the number of kits you need.
* **Additional Kit Requests** (`additional_kit_requests`): Describe any additional kit requests you have.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any notes about your onboarding process.
* **Date Requested** (`date_requested`): Enter the date you would like to receive your kits.
* **Time Requested** (`time_requested`): Enter the time you would like to receive your kits.
* **Franchise Location** (`franchise_location`): Enter the name of your franchise location.
* **Franchise Name** (`franchise_name`): Enter the name of your franchise.
* **Franchise Number** (`franchise_number`): Enter the number of your franchise.
* **Onboarding Manager** (`onboarding_manager`): Enter the onboarding manager's contact information.
* **Onboarding Manager Email** (`onboarding_manager_email`): Enter the onboarding manager's email address.
* **Onboarding Manager Phone** (`onboarding_manager_phone`): Enter the onboarding manager's phone number.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`): Select the review status of your onboarding process.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any comments about your experience.
