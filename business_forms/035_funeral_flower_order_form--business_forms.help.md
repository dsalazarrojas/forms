# Funeral Flower Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for businesses to collect order information from customers for funeral flowers. It's meant for both online and offline order collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name, email, and any other relevant contact information in the "Customer Information" section.
2. Provide details about the order, such as the type of flowers, delivery date, and any special requests, in the "Order Details" section.
3. Enter the delivery information, including the address and any special delivery instructions, in the "Delivery Information" section.
4. Enter any payment details, including the method of payment and any additional information, in the "Payment Details" section.
5. Confirm the order in the "Confirm Order" section.
6. Add any additional comments or information about the customer in the "Customer Comment" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (customer_info)**: Enter the customer's name, email, and any other relevant contact information. This will help the business contact the customer with order updates and confirmations.
* **Order Details (order_details)**: Provide a detailed description of the order, including the type of flowers, delivery date, and any special requests. This will help the business understand the customer's preferences and ensure accurate order fulfillment.
* **Delivery Information (delivery_info)**: Enter the delivery address and any special delivery instructions, such as "please leave at the door" or "deliver to the reception desk". This will help the business ensure timely and accurate delivery of the order.
* **Payment Details (payment_details)**: Enter the method of payment and any additional information, such as a purchase order number or an invoice number. This will help the business track payments and fulfill orders efficiently.
* **Confirm Order (order_confirmation)**: Confirm the order details to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* **Customer Comment (customer_comment)**: Add any additional comments or information about the customer, such as any special requests or allergies. This will help the business provide personalized service and ensure customer satisfaction.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language in the "Customer Comment" section to provide additional context about the customer.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
