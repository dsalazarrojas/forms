# retail_media_network_partner_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register retail media network partners and gather information about their businesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your company name, address, and contact information.
2. Select your business type (Brand, Agency, or Tech Provider).
3. Write a brief description of your company.
4. Enter your company website and social media links.
5. Enter your contact email and phone number.
6. Select your company size, industry sector, and sales channels.
7. Choose your communication channels and assigned partner.
8. Enter your employee range and company size (prophets).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's name.
  - This field is required and should match the company's actual name.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter your contact's name.
  - This field is required and should be a personal name.
- **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, required): Enter your company's address.
  - This field is required and should be a physical address.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your company's email address.
  - This field is required and should be a valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your company's phone number.
  - This field is required and should be a valid phone number.
- **Partnership Proposal** (`partner_proposal`, `text`, optional): Write a brief proposal for why you would like to partner with us.
  - This field is optional and should be a brief message.
- **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your business type (Brand, Agency, or Tech Provider).
  - This field is required and should be one of the three options provided.
- **Company Description** (`company_description`, `text`, required): Write a brief description of your company.
  - This field is required and should be a brief text.
- **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, required): Enter your company's website URL.
  - This field is required and should be a valid website URL.
- **Social Media Links** (`social_media_links`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the social media links you would like to share with us.
  - This field is required and should be one of the options provided.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact's email address.
  - This field is required and should be a valid email address.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact's phone number.
  - This field is required and should be a valid phone number.
- **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter your business's name.
  - This field is required and should match the company name.
- **Partner Since** (`partner_since`, `select_one`, optional): Select when you first partnered with us.
  - This field is optional and should be one of the options provided.
- **Sales Contact** (`sales_contact`, `email`, required): Enter your sales contact's email address.
  - This field is required and should be a valid email address.
- **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your company's size (Less than 10, 10 to 50, 50 to 200, or More than 200).
  - This field is required and should be one of the options provided.
- **Employee Range** (`employee_range`, `number`, required): Enter your company's employee range.
  - This field is required and should be a number.
- **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, `select_one`, required): Select your company's industry sector (E-commerce, Entertainment, Finance, Health, Media & Entertainment, Retail, or Technology).
  - This field is required and should be one of the options provided.
- **Sales Channel** (`sales_channel`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the sales channels you use (Social Media, Email, Phone, Online Advertising, or Offline Advertising).
  - This field is required and should be one of the options provided.
- **Marketing Channel** (`marketing_channel`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the marketing channels you use (Social Media, Email, Phone, Online Advertising, or Offline Advertising).
  - This field is required and should be one of the options provided.
- **Communication Channel** (`communication_channel`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the communication channels you use (Email, Phone, Chat, or Phone call).
  - This field is required and should be one of the options provided.
- **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required): Select the person you would like to be assigned to (chatjimmy or another person).
  - This field is required and should be one of the options provided.
- **Company Size (prophets)** (`company_size_profits`, `number`, required): Enter your company's size in prophets.
  - This field is required and should be a number.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure a smooth registration process.
- If you are having trouble with a field, please contact us for support.
- This form is only for retail media network partners, so if you are not a retail media network partner, please do not fill out this form.
- If your company email is already registered in the system, you will be notified and not allowed to proceed with the registration.
