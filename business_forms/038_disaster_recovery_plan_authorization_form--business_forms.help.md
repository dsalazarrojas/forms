# Disaster Recovery Plan Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Recovery Plan Authorization Form is a document that authorizes an individual to access and manage disaster recovery plans within an organization. This form collects personal details and contact information from the authorized person.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form with the following steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Input your **Email** address, which will be the primary contact method for notifications and updates.
3. Provide your **Phone** number for emergency contact purposes.
4. Enter your **Job Title** and **Department** to ensure accurate authorization.
5. Specify your **Team** to associate you with the correct group for plan management.
6. In the **Emergency Contact Person** field, enter the name and contact information of the person you want to be notified in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name for proper identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for proper identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address for notifications and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title for accurate authorization and record-keeping.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department for accurate authorization and record-keeping.
* **Team** (`team`, text, required): Enter your team for association with the correct group for plan management.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the person you want to be notified in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are accurate and complete for proper processing.
* If unsure about any field, refer to your organization's policy or contact your supervisor for guidance.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
