# Round Report Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Round Report Submission Form is designed to capture essential details from employees about their work hours, including shift start and end times, breaks, billed hours, payable hours, deductions, and gross pay. This form is used to track and verify employee work hours and ensure accurate payroll calculations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information and documentation ready before starting the form.
2. Enter your first name and last name in the designated fields.
3. Select your shift start and end times in the time format (HH:MM).
4. Calculate your total breaks taken, billed hours, payable hours, deductions, and gross pay accurately.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
6. Verify that your email address is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your shift in the HH:MM format (e.g., 08:00).
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your shift in the HH:MM format (e.g., 17:00).
* **Shift Duration** (`shift_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your shift in minutes.
* **Total Breaks** (`total_breaks`, number, optional): Enter the total number of breaks taken during your shift.
* **Total Billed Hours** (`total_billed_hours`, number, optional): Enter the total hours worked on your shift as billed.
* **Total Payable Hours** (`total_payable_hours`, number, optional): Enter the total hours you are to be paid for, including breaks and time off.
* **Total Deductions** (`total_deductions`, number, optional): Enter the total deductions made from your pay.
* **Total Gross Pay** (`total_gross_pay`, number, optional): Enter the total gross pay earned.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes related to your shift.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Ensure that you double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If unsure about any field, please refer to your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
- Please enter all fields accurately to ensure correct payroll processing and reporting.
