# Risk Reward Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Reward Analysis Form is designed to help you assess and document the risks and rewards associated with business decisions or actions. This form guides you in identifying, evaluating, and recording potential risks and their corresponding rewards, which can aid in making informed decisions that balance risk and reward.

## How To Complete This Form
### Preparation is Key
Before filling out this form, make sure you have a clear understanding of the specific decision or action you are analyzing. Identify the potential risks and rewards associated with it.
### Fill Out the Form
1. **Risk Level (`risk_level`, number, required):** Enter a number indicating the level of risk involved in the decision or action, with higher numbers typically indicating higher risk levels.
2. **Reward Level (`reward_level`, number, required):** Enter a number indicating the potential reward or benefit from the decision or action, with higher numbers generally signifying higher potential rewards.
3. **Risk Probability (`risk_probability`, number, required):** Provide a number to express the likelihood or probability of the risk occurring, with higher numbers indicating higher risk, though the exact interpretation might vary by context and application.
4. **Risk Impact (`risk_impact`, number, required):** Enter a number indicating the potential impact or consequence of the risk, with higher numbers usually implying greater consequences.
5. **Risk Mitigation (text, `mitigation`, optional):** Describe any specific actions or measures taken to reduce or mitigate the risk.
6. **Control Actions (multiple select, `control_actions`, optional):** Choose all applicable actions to control or reduce the risk, such as specific procedures, policies, or steps taken.
7. **Mitigation Actions (multiple select, `mitigation_actions`, optional):** Indicate any additional measures taken to address or mitigate the risk, such as specific actions or steps taken.
8. **Submit:** After completing all fields, review your inputs and submit the form.
