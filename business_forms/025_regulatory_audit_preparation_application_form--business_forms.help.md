# regulatory_audit_preparation_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information to prepare for regulatory audits. It is used to identify the scope, readiness, and other relevant details of the audit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form header (page 1) with a brief title or header.
2. On page 2, select the **Regulatory Audit Scope** field and enter a brief description of the audit scope.
3. On page 3, select the **Regulatory Audit Readiness** field and choose whether the audit scope is **Scope Identified** or **Scope Not Identified**.
4. On page 4, select the **Expected Audit Date** field and enter the expected date of the audit.
5. On page 5, select the **Regulatory Audit Type** field and choose whether the audit is **Internal**, **External**, or **Third Party**.
6. On page 6, select the **Audit Sponsor** field and choose the organization or department responsible for the audit.
7. On page 7, select the **Regulatory Audit Frequency** field and choose the frequency of the audit (Annually, Bi-Annually, or Quarterly).
8. On page 8, select the **Regulatory Audit Status** field and choose the status of the audit (Scheduled, Completed, or Delayed).
9. On page 9, enter any **Additional Comments** about the audit in the text field.
10. On page 10, select the **Assigned Compliance Officer** field and choose the officer responsible for the audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (Page 1): Enter a brief title or header for the form.
* **Regulatory Audit Scope** (Page 2): Briefly describe the scope of the audit.
* **Regulatory Audit Readiness** (Page 3): Choose whether the audit scope is **Scope Identified** or **Scope Not Identified**.
* **Expected Audit Date** (Page 4): Enter the expected date of the audit.
* **Regulatory Audit Type** (Page 5): Choose the type of audit (Internal, External, or Third Party).
* **Audit Sponsor** (Page 6): Choose the organization or department responsible for the audit.
* **Regulatory Audit Frequency** (Page 7): Choose the frequency of the audit (Annually, Bi-Annually, or Quarterly).
* **Regulatory Audit Status** (Page 8): Choose the status of the audit (Scheduled, Completed, or Delayed).
* **Additional Comments** (Page 9): Enter any additional comments about the audit.
* **Assigned Compliance Officer** (Page 10): Choose the officer responsible for the audit.
