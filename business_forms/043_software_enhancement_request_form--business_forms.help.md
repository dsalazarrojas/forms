# Software Enhancement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit software enhancement requests to the development team. It helps the team understand your needs and requirements for improvements to the software.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a clear and concise description of the enhancement you are requesting in the "Description" field.
2. Fill out the "Contact Name" field with your name and contact information so we can get back to you.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to ensure we can respond to your request.
4. If you have a preferred method of communication, select it from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu.
5. Add any additional notes or comments you think would be helpful in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Description** (required): Describe the enhancement you are requesting in a clear and concise manner.
* **Contact Name** (required): Enter your name and contact information so we can respond to your request.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address so we can respond to your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (optional): Select how you would like to be contacted about your request (e.g., Chat, Email, Phone).
* **Additional Notes** (optional): Add any additional information that would be helpful for us to know about your request.

Note: The fields labeled as "Software Enhancement Request Form X" are for internal use and can be ignored for the purposes of this guide.
