# Retrospective Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retrospective survey is designed to collect information from the business to help identify areas of improvement and understand the business's performance. It is a tool to gather data on the business's goals, targets, and key challenges and opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On the "What is the primary purpose of this survey?" page, enter a brief description of the survey's purpose.
2. On the "What are the goals of the business?" page, select up to three goals that the business is currently working towards.
3. On the "What metrics are being tracked?" page, select up to three metrics that are being measured to track progress.
4. On the "What are the targets?" page, select up to three targets that the business is aiming to achieve.
5. On the "What is the score?" page, enter a numerical value representing the business's current score.
6. On the "When is this score valid?" page, select a date when the score is relevant.
7. On the "Additional Comments" page, enter any additional comments or notes about the business.
8. On the "E-Mail Address" page, enter the business's email address (if applicable).
9. On the "What are the key challenges the business is facing?" page, select up to three challenges that the business is currently facing.
10. On the "What are the key opportunities the business is facing?" page, select up to three opportunities that the business is currently facing.
11. On the "What are the key strengths the business is facing?" page, select up to three strengths that the business possesses.
12. On the "What are the key weaknesses the business facing?" page, select up to three weaknesses that the business faces.
13. On the "Additional Comments (2)" page, enter any additional comments or notes about the business.
14. On the "Submit" page, select "Yes" if the survey is complete, "No" if not, or "Other" if there are additional pages that need to be filled out.
