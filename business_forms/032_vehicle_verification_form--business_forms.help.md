# Vehicle Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify vehicle details for any purpose related to vehicle-related services, such as insurance claims, vehicle registration, or other vehicle-related administrative tasks. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure that the information is up-to-date and correct.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant options for each field as needed.
2. Enter the correct vehicle details in the provided fields.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out (if any are marked as required).
4. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all fields are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Verification Form** (Vehicle Verification Form, number, required: false)
This is the title of the form.
* **Vehicle Identification Number** (vehicle_identification_number, text, required: false)
Enter the unique identification number of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Description** (vehicle_description, text, required: false)
Describe the vehicle in detail, including its make and model.
* **Vehicle Year** (vehicle_year, number, required: false)
Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (vehicle_make, text, required: false)
Enter the make of the vehicle, such as Toyota or Ford.
* **Vehicle Model** (vehicle_model, text, required: false)
Enter the model of the vehicle, such as Camry or Mustang.
* **Vehicle Color** (vehicle_color, text, required: false)
Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Registration** (vehicle_registration, text, required: false)
Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Vehicle Plates Number** (vehicle_plates_number, text, required: false)
Enter the number of plates on the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Owner Info** (vehicle_owner_info, text, required: false)
Enter any relevant information about the vehicle owner, such as their name or contact details.
* **Vehicle Condition** (vehicle_condition, text, required: false)
Describe the condition of the vehicle, such as "running", "not running", or "damaged".
* **Vehicle Photos** (vehicle_photos, text, required: false)
Upload or enter any relevant photos of the vehicle.
