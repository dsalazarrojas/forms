# Manufacturing Logistics Company Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Logistics Company Referral Form is used to collect information about a manufacturing company for potential partnerships or collaborations. This form helps gather relevant details such as company name, address, industry, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name in the "Company Name" field. This is the name of the company you are referring to.
2. Upload or link the company logo in the "Company Logo" field.
3. Enter the job title of the person being referred in the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter the company's website in the "Company Website" field.
5. Enter the company's email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter the company's phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Choose the referral source from the dropdown menu in the "Referral Source" field. This can be social media, email, phone, or other.
8. Enter the referral date in the "Referral Date" field.
9. Enter any additional follow-up notes in the "Follow-up Note" field.
10. Enter the company address in the "Company Address" field.
11. Enter the city and region of the company's location in the "City" and "Region" fields, respectively.
12. Enter the country of the company's location in the "Country" field.
13. Enter the postal code of the company's location in the "Postal Code" field.
14. Enter the name of the referral in the "Referral Name" field.
15. Enter a brief description of the company in the "Company Description" field.
16. Choose the industry of the company from the "Industry" field.
17. Choose the category of the company's industry from the "Industry Category" field.
18. Check if the company is open to business partnerships in the "Business Partners" field.
19. Choose the status of the follow-up in the "Follow-up Status" field. This can be active or inactive.
20. Enter any additional follow-up notes in the "Follow-up Next Action" field.
21. Enter the reason for the referral in the "Referral Reason" field.
22. Choose the date and time of the next follow-up in the "Follow-up Date" and "Follow-up Time" fields, respectively.
23. Check if the referral has been assigned to someone in the "Assigned To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`Company Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company you are referring to.
* **Company Logo** (`Company Logo`, text, required): Upload or link the company logo.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the person being referred.
* **Company Website** (`Company Website`, text, required): Enter the company's website.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter the company's email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter the company's phone number.
* **Referral Date** (`Referral Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the referral.
* **Referral Source** (`Referral Source`, select_one, required): Choose how the referral was made (social media, email, phone, etc.).
* **Follow-up Note** (`Follow-up Note`, text, required): Enter any additional follow-up notes.
* **Company Address** (`Company Address`, text, required): Enter the company address.
* **City** (`City`, text, required): Enter the city of the company's location.
* **Region** (`Region`, text, required): Enter the region of the company's location.
* **Country** (`Country`, text, required): Enter the country of the company's location.
* **Postal Code** (`Postal Code`, text, required): Enter the postal code of the company's location.
* **Referral Name** (`Referral Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the referral.
* **Company Description** (`Company Description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the company.
* **Industry** (`Industry`, text, required): Choose the industry of the company.
* **Industry Category** (`Industry Category`, text, required): Choose the category of the company's industry.
* **Business Partners** (`Business Partners`, select_multiple, required): Check if the company is open to business partnerships.
* **Follow-up Status** (`Follow-up Status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the follow-up (active or inactive).
* **Follow-up Next Action** (`Follow-up Next Action`, text, required): Enter any additional follow-up notes.
* **Referral Reason** (`Referral Reason`, text, required): Enter the reason for the referral.
* **Follow-up Date** (`Follow-up Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the next follow-up.
* **Follow-up Time** (`Follow-up Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the next follow-up.
* **Assigned To** (`Assigned To`, select_one, required): Choose if the referral has been assigned to someone.

## Tips
- Double-check the company name, address, and other details to ensure accuracy.
- Use the correct format for dates and times (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD for dates, HH:MM:SS for times).
- Be clear and concise when filling out the follow-up notes and additional notes. 

This help guide will guide users through the process of completing the Manufacturing Logistics Company Referral Form accurately and efficiently.
