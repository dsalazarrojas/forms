# Firmographics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect firmographic information about a company, which is used for internal business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer all the questions truthfully and accurately. Make sure to select the correct options from the dropdown lists and enter any additional information in the text fields where necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
- **Is your company large?** (`is_large_organization`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if your company has over 100 employees, 'No' otherwise.
- **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, required): Enter the number of employees in your company.
- **Company Industry** (`company_industry`, select_multiple, required): Select all the industries that apply to your company.
- **Company Location** (`company_location`, select_multiple, required): Select all the locations where your company operates.
- **Company Founder** (`company_founder`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if your company was founded by the person filling out the form, 'No' otherwise.
- **Number of Offices** (`number_of_offices`, number, optional): Enter the number of offices your company has, if applicable. (Note: This field is optional and not always applicable to all companies)
- **Established Date** (`established_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your company was established, if applicable. (Note: This field is optional and not always applicable to all companies)
- **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your company's website, if applicable. (Note: This field is optional and not always applicable to all companies)
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person in charge of the company.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the person in charge of the company.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person in charge of the company.
- **Other Industry** (`company_industry_other`, text, optional): If your company is not listed in the previous options, enter the name of your industry.
- **Other Location** (`company_location_other`, text, optional): If your company operates in a different location than listed, enter the name of that location.
- **Other Number of Offices** (`number_of_offices_other`, text, optional): If your company has more or fewer offices than listed, enter the correct number.
- **Other Established Date** (`established_date_other`, text, optional): If your company was established on a different date, enter the correct date.
- **Other Website** (`website_other`, text, optional): If your company has a different website, enter the URL.
- **Other Founder** (`company_founder_other`, text, optional): If your company was founded by someone other than the person filling out the form, enter the name of the founder.
- **Other Phone** (`contact_phone_other`, text, optional): If your company has a different contact phone number, enter the number.
