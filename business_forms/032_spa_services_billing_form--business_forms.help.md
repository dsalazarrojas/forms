# Spa Services Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Spa Services Billing Form is used to collect information about spa services provided to clients, including payment details. This form helps spa owners and staff to track services, payments, and manage client interactions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **service type** (e.g., Massage, Facial) that was provided to the client.
2. Enter the **date** the service was provided.
3. Enter the **time** the service started.
4. Provide the client's **name**, a brief **service description** (if applicable), and **charges** incurred (including any additional **tip**).
5. Select the **payment method** used (e.g., Cash, Card).
6. Choose the **payment status** (e.g., Pending, Paid).
7. Add any additional **notes** about the service or payment.
8. Enter the **client's email** and **phone number** (if applicable).
9. Provide the **spa owner's name** and **spa name** (if applicable).
10. Enter any **service charge** (if applicable).
11. Enter the **tip** (if applicable).
12. Enter the **total** amount due.
13. Enter the **payment ID**, **payment method ID**, **spa ID**, **client ID**, **service ID**, and **note ID** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **service_type**: Select the type of service provided to the client (e.g., Massage, Facial).
* **date**: Enter the date the service was provided.
* **time**: Enter the time the service started.
* **client_name**: Enter the client's name.
* **service_description**: Provide a brief description of the service provided.
* **charges**: Enter the charges incurred for the service.
* **tip**: Enter any additional tip given by the client.
* **payment_method**: Select the payment method used (e.g., Cash, Card).
* **payment_status**: Choose the payment status (e.g., Pending, Paid).
* **note**: Add any additional notes about the service or payment.
* **email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **address**: Enter the spa's address (if applicable).
* **spa_owner**: Enter the spa owner's name (if applicable).
* **spa_name**: Enter the spa name (if applicable).
* **service_charge**: Enter any additional service charge (if applicable).
* **total**: Enter the total amount due.
* **payment_id**: Enter the payment ID (if applicable).
* **payment_method_id**: Enter the payment method ID (if applicable).
* **spa_id**: Enter the spa ID (if applicable).
* **client_id**: Enter the client ID (if applicable).
* **service_id**: Enter the service ID (if applicable).
* **note_id**: Enter the note ID (if applicable).
* **status**: Choose the status of the service (e.g., Pending, Completed).
