# 360 Feedback Orchard Hill Church - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the 360 Feedback Orchard Hill Church form, which is designed to collect your feedback and experience with the 360 Feedback program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter Your Name**: In this field, please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Email Address**: Enter your email address that is associated with your 360 Feedback account. This must be a valid email address.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted for follow-up purposes. This is optional.
4. **Role at Church**: Select the option that best describes your role within the church or organization.
5. **Years with Church**: Enter the number of years you have been associated with the church.
6. **Date of Last Visit**: If you have visited the church recently, enter the date of your most recent visit. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
7. **Satisfaction Level**: Select one of the given options to indicate how satisfied you are with 360 Feedback Orchard Hill Church.
8. **Overall Rating**: Rate your overall experience with 360 Feedback Orchard Hill Church on a scale of 1-10.
9. **User Experience Rating**: Rate your experience with the user interface on a scale of 1-10.
10. **Support Responsiveness Rating**: Rate the responsiveness of support you received on a scale of 1-10.
11. **Best Aspects**: Select all the features that you found most useful and enjoyable about 360 Feedback Orchard Hill Church.
12. **Most Frequently Used Features**: Select all the features you use most often with 360 Feedback Orchard Hill Church. (Leave blank if none)
13. **Feedback & Recommendations**: Enter any specific areas you would like to see improved or any recommendations for enhancement.
14. **Recommendation**: Select one of the given options to indicate if you would recommend 360 Feedback Orchard Hill Church to others.
15. **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional thoughts or comments about your experience.
