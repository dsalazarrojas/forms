# Transportation Reimbursement Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request transportation reimbursement for business or personal trips. Fill in your travel details, mode of transportation, and other relevant information to make a valid reimbursement request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete all required fields (indicated by *) before submitting the form.
2. Fill in the correct date of travel, start date, and end date of your trip.
3. Select the mode of transportation used during your trip.
4. Enter the start and end locations of your trip.
5. Attach a copy of your receipt or proof of payment for your reimbursement.
6. Enter the reimbursement amount and the reason for your reimbursement.
7. Select the type of trip (business or personal).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Details** (Travel Details, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of your travel details.
* **Date Of Travel** (Date Of Travel, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your travel.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your trip.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your trip.
* **Mode Of Transportation** (Mode Of Transportation, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the mode of transportation used during your trip. Select all that apply.
	+ Air
	+ Train
	+ Bus
	+ Rental Car
* **Start Location** (Start Location, text, required/optional): Enter the start location of your trip.
* **End Location** (End Location, text, required/optional): Enter the end location of your trip.
* **Receipt** (Receipt, text, required/optional): Attach a copy of your receipt or proof of payment for your reimbursement.
* **Reimbursement Amount** (Reimbursement Amount, number, required/optional): Enter the total reimbursement amount.
* **Reimbursement Reason** (Reimbursement Reason, text, required/optional): Explain the reason for your reimbursement request.
* **Expense Type** (Expense Type, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of trip (business or personal).

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
