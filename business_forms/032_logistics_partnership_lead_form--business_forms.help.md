# logistics_partnership_lead_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential partners in the logistics industry. It helps in gathering contact details, company description, services offered, and lead source, which aids in streamlining the logistics partnership process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the form in its entirety, as this will help in ensuring that all relevant details are collected.
2. Begin with the "Business Name" field, where you can enter your company's name.
3. Next, provide your "Contact Person" details, including their name and any other relevant information.
4. Enter your "Email" and "Phone" in the respective fields, which are required for communication purposes.
5. Fill in your company's "Company Name", "Job Title", "Company Address", "City", "Country", and "Zip Code" for contact and geographic details.
6. Briefly describe your company and "Services" offered in the provided text areas.
7. Select your "Industry" from the given options (Logistics, Transportation, Supply Chain, or Other).
8. Identify the "Services" your company offers (Warehousing, Packaging, Freight, Customs, or Other).
9. Enter the "Lead Date" and "Lead Time" if applicable.
10. Lastly, select the "Assigned to" method of communication (Chat, Email, Phone, or Other).
11. Add any additional "Notes" in the text area provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Contact Person** (text, required): Provide the name of the contact person.
* **Email** (text, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your company phone number.
* **Company Name** (text, optional): Enter your company's name.
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Company Address** (text, optional): Enter your company's address.
* **City** (text, optional): Enter the city where your company is located.
* **Country** (text, optional): Enter your company's country of operation.
* **Zip Code** (text, optional): Enter your company's zip code.
* **Description** (text, optional): Briefly describe your company.
* **Company Type** (text, optional): Enter your company type.
* **Lead Source** (text, optional): Indicate the source of the lead.
* **Other** (text, optional): Provide any other details if "Lead Source" is "Other".
* **Industry** (select_one, optional): Select your industry type (Logistics, Transportation, Supply Chain, or Other).
* **Services** (select_multiple, optional): Select the services offered by your company (Warehousing, Packaging, Freight, Customs, or Other).
* **Lead Date** (date, optional): Enter the lead date if applicable.
* **Lead Time** (time, optional): Enter the lead time if applicable.
* **Assigned to** (select_one, required): Select the method of communication (Chat, Email, Phone, or Other).
* **Notes** (text, optional): Provide any additional notes.
