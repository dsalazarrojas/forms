# Cold Logistics Equipment Credit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cold Logistics Equipment Credit Application Form is a tool used to gather information from businesses applying for cold logistics equipment credit. This form helps us understand the company's needs and requirements, ensuring we can provide accurate quotes and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information in the "Business Information" section, including the registered business name, tax ID or registration number, business address, and years in operation.
2. Select all the equipment types that apply to your business needs from the "Equipment and Credit Request" section.
3. Specify the total estimated credit amount and desired payment terms.
4. Enter your primary contact's name and email address.
5. Select your bank name and provide a business reference contact's name and email address.
6. Confirm that all provided information is accurate by selecting "I agree" in the "Declaration of Accuracy" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Registered Business Name
* **Registered Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): This is the full legal name of your business entity. Please enter the exact name as it appears on your official documents.

### 2. Tax ID or Registration Number
* **Tax ID or Registration Number** (`tax_id`, `text`, required): This is your business identification number. Enter the correct number to verify your business identity.

### 3. Business Address
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required): This is your business's physical office location. Enter the address where your business can be contacted.

### 4. Years in Operation
* **Years in Operation** (`years_operation`, `number`, required): This is the number of years your business has been in operation. Please enter the accurate number to demonstrate your business's stability.

### 5. Primary Business Contact
* **Primary Business Contact** (`primary_contact`, `text`, required): This is the person responsible for this application. Please enter their name and title.

### 6. Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): This is the email address of your primary business contact. Enter a valid email address to ensure we can reach you.

### 7. -- Equipment and Credit Request --
*   **-- Equipment and Credit Request --** (this is a section header, `type: note`): This section provides information on the equipment and credit request details.

### 8. Type of Equipment Needed
* **Type of Equipment Needed** (`equipment_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the equipment types that apply to your business needs from the provided options.

### 9. Total Estimated Credit Amount
* **Total Estimated Credit Amount** (`credit_amount`, `number`, required): This is the requested credit limit in USD. Enter the accurate amount to ensure we can assess your credit requirements.

### 10. Desired Payment Terms
* **Desired Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred payment terms from the provided options.

### 11. Bank Name
* **Bank Name** (`bank_name`, `text`, required): This is your primary business banking institution. Enter the correct name to verify your banking relationships.

### 12. Business Reference Contact
* **Business Reference Contact** (`business_reference`, `text`, required): Provide the name and email of a professional reference who can vouch for your business.

### 13. Declaration of Accuracy
* **Declaration of Accuracy** (`declaration`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that all provided information is accurate by selecting "I agree."
