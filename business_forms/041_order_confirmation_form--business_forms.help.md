# Order Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Confirmation Form is used to gather all necessary information for confirming a customer's order. This form ensures that both the customer and shipping parties have the correct details, making it easier to track and fulfill orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these general steps:

1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that it matches the customer's original request.
3. Double-check that shipping information is correct to avoid any delays or issues during delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter the customer's name exactly as it appears on their identification or contact information.
* **Customer E-mail** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address to ensure they can be reached with order updates and notifications.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Provide the customer's phone number for any urgent matters or updates.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required): Enter the customer's full address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Please describe the ordered item or items, including any special instructions or requirements.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required): Detail the shipping method and any relevant shipping instructions.
* **Shipping E-mail** (`shipping_email`, email, optional): Enter the shipping party's email address for updates and notifications.
* **Shipping Phone** (`shipping_phone`, text, optional): Provide the shipping party's phone number for urgent matters.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, text, optional): Update the order status as needed (e.g., "in progress," "complete," etc.).
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date the order was placed.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the order was placed.
* **Dispatch Date** (`order_dispatch_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the order was dispatched or shipped.
* **Dispatch Time** (`order_dispatch_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the order was dispatched or shipped.

Please review each field carefully and make sure to double-check the information for accuracy to avoid any issues during the order process.
