# Smart Building Access Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Building Access Registration form is used for users to register their access to a smart building. This form provides a way for users to provide essential information about their access, which is vital for maintaining the security and efficiency of the building.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Smart Building Access Registration form, please follow these steps:
- Fill out the User Name field with your full name as it appears on your official documents.
- Enter the Building Name where you will be accessing the smart building.
- Select your Access Level, which determines your level of access in the building:
	+ Admin: Administrator with full access to the building.
	+ Manager: Building manager with access to all areas.
	+ Staff: Building staff member with limited access to certain areas.
	+ Tenant: A user with basic access to the building.
- Select the Access Type to indicate how you will be using the smart building:
	+ Door: Access for door entry.
	+ Elevator: Access for elevator use.
	+ Security Desk: Access for security-related purposes.
- Enter your Start Date, which indicates when your access begins.
- Enter your End Date, which indicates when your access ends.
- Provide a Reason for your access, which helps us understand your purpose.
- Enter the Contact Name and Contact Phone number of the person you would like to be contacted if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Building Name (2)**: Enter the name of the building you will be accessing.
* **Access Level (3)**: Select your level of access in the building.
* **Access Type (4)**: Select how you will be using the smart building.
* **Start Date (5)**: Enter the date when your access begins.
* **End Date (6)**: Enter the date when your access ends.
* **Reason (7)**: Provide a brief explanation of your purpose for accessing the building.
* **Contact Name (8)**: Enter the name of the contact person to be reached if needed.
* **Contact Phone (9)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person to be reached if needed.
