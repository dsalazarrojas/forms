# Supplier Delivery Reliability Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Delivery Reliability Evaluation Form is used to assess the reliability and efficiency of suppliers in delivering goods and services.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure you have the necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields first, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol next to the field label.
3. Provide accurate and honest responses to each question, based on your experience with the supplier.
4. Use the provided hints and options to guide your responses.
5. Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, `text`, `true`): Enter the name of the supplier you are evaluating.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, `true`): Enter the email address of the supplier's contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, `true`): Enter the phone number of the supplier's contact person.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, `false`): Enter the supplier's contact address, if available.
* **Delivery Frequency** (`delivery_frequency`, `select_multiple`, `true`): Check the options that apply to the supplier's delivery frequency. This may include options such as 'Yes', 'No', 'Daily', 'Weekly', etc.
* **Average Order Value** (`average_order_value`, `number`, `true`): Enter the average value of orders delivered by the supplier.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Check the options that apply to the payment method used by the supplier, such as 'Yes', 'No', 'Invoice', 'Credit Card', etc.
