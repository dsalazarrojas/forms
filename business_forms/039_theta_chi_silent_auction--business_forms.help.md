# Theta Chi Silent Auction - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Theta Chi Silent Auction form is designed to collect bids and bidder information for silent auctions organized by Theta Chi. This form will help you record and track bids on auction items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the auction item on the "Auction Item" page.
2. Provide your bidder information on the "Bidder Information" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Auction Item** (`auction_item_form`, text, optional): Enter the name of the auction item you are bidding on.
* **Bidder Information** (`bidder_info_form`, text, optional): Please provide your information so we can contact you if you win the auction. This may include your name, email, and contact number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any issues with bid tracking.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, contact the form creators at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
