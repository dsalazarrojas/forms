# Freight Inspection Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document and track the inspection process for freight shipments, capturing key information about the shipment, any damages, and inspection results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section, providing any relevant details about the shipment.
2. Describe any damages found during the inspection in the Damage Information section.
3. Record the inspection results, including whether the shipment passed or failed inspection.
4. Add any comments or notes about the shipment in the Comments and Comments 2 sections.
5. Attach damage pictures and videos if applicable.
6. Indicate whether damage videos and pictures were taken in the Damage Pictures List and Damage Videos List fields.
7. Select the inspection result and damage level.
8. Assign the relevant tools and personnel to the inspection.
9. Provide any additional notes or comments about the shipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, required: false): This section is for capturing any general information about the shipment, such as its description, weight, or dimensions.
* **Freight Details** (`freight_details`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant details about the shipment, like its contents or special handling requirements.
* **Damage Information** (`damage_info`, note, required: false): Describe any damages found during the inspection, including their nature and extent.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Sign and date your inspection report.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the assigned personnel responsible for inspecting the shipment.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, required: false): Record the date and time the shipment was inspected.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any comments or notes about the shipment.
* **Damage Pictures** (`damage_pictures`, text, required: false): Attach images of any damages found during the inspection.
* **Damage Videos** (`damage_videos`, text, required: false): Attach videos of any damages found during the inspection.
* **Damage Pictures List** (`damage_pictures_list`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether damage pictures were taken (`Yes` or `No`).
