**Theft Detection Incident Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Theft Detection Incident Form is used to report and track incidents of theft within the organization. This form collects information about the incident, including date, time, location, description, and status updates.

**How To Complete This Form**

1.  Fill in the incident date and time fields if they are known. If not, you can leave them blank.
2.  Enter a brief description of the incident in the Incident Description field.
3.  Identify the suspect's name, contact info, and current status (identified, captured, or arrested).
4.  Select the status of the investigation (Open, In Progress, or Closed).
5.  Provide a witness statement if applicable.
6.  If the suspect has been identified, provide additional information about their charge (Misdemeanor or Felony).
7.  If the incident was reported by someone, enter their name, phone number, department, and email address.
8.  Enter the date and time the incident was reported.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the incident. If the date is not known, leave this field blank.
*   **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the incident. If the time is not known, leave this field blank.
*   **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
*   **Suspect Name** (`suspect_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the suspect.
*   **Suspect Contact Info** (`suspect_contact_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional contact information for the suspect.
*   **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, `text`, optional): If you are not the victim or involved in the incident, provide any relevant witness statements or information.
*   **Investigation Status** (`investigation_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current status of the investigation (Open, In Progress, or Closed).
*   **Suspect Identified** (`suspect_identified`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if the suspect has been identified.
*   **Suspect Captured** (`suspect_captured`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if the suspect has been captured or apprehended.
*   **Suspect Arrested** (`suspect_arrested`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if the suspect has been arrested.
*   **Suspect Charge** (`suspect_charge`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the charge against the suspect (None, Misdemeanor, or Felony).
*   **Incident Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if the incident was reported by someone.
*   **Reporter Name** (`incident_reporter_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
*   **Reporter Phone** (`incident_reporter_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who reported the incident.
*   **Reporter Email** (`incident_reporter_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person who reported the incident.
*   **Reporter Department** (`incident_reporter_department`, `text`, optional): Enter the department of the person who reported the incident.
*   **Incident Date Reported** (`incident_date_reported`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the incident was reported.
*   **Incident Time Reported** (`incident_time_reported`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the incident was reported.

## Tips

*   Please fill in all relevant fields.
*   If you are unsure about any field, leave it blank or contact your supervisor for guidance.
*   If you are reporting an incident, be as detailed as possible.
*   If you are not involved in the incident, provide any relevant witness statements.
*   Update the investigation status regularly.
*   If the suspect has been captured or arrested, update their status accordingly.
*   If the suspect has been identified, provide additional information about their charge.
