# Lunch And Learn Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lunch And Learn Planning Form is a tool used to gather essential information for organizing in-person learning and development events within the workplace. It helps event planners collect details about the company, speakers, event details, learning objectives, agenda, materials, evaluation, and comments to create a well-structured and engaging event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the company details, including the name, to identify the host organization.
2.  Provide the speaker's name and any other relevant details.
3.  Describe the event details, such as the date, time, and location.
4.  Select the learning objectives for the event, choosing one or more from the options provided.
5.  Choose the time slot for the event from the available options.
6.  Provide any learning materials that will be used during the event.
7.  Evaluate the event by selecting the satisfaction level from the provided options.
8.  Enter any additional comments about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **company_details (Text):** Enter the name of the company that is hosting or organizing the event.
*   **speaker_details (Text):** Provide the name and any other relevant details about the speaker or presenter.
*   **event_details (Text):** Describe the event date, time, and location in the format specified (e.g., "Monday, March 15, 2025, at 10:00 am in Conference Room A").
*   **learning_objective (Select Multiple):** Select one or more from the options:
    *   **Learning and development:** Focus on acquiring new skills or knowledge.
    *   **Team building:** Encourage team collaboration and bonding.
    *   **Communication skills:** Improve verbal and written communication within the team.
    *   **Problem solving:** Solve real-world problems or scenarios.
    *   **Leadership development:** Enhance leadership skills, such as delegation or decision-making.
    *   **Productivity:** Boost individual or team productivity.
    *   **Time management:** Optimize time allocation and scheduling.
*   **agenda_item (Select Multiple):** Choose the time slot for the event from:
    *   9:00 am - 9:45 am
    *   10:00 am - 10:45 am
    *   11:00 am - 11:45 am
    *   12:00 pm - 1:00 pm
    *   1:00 pm - 1:45 pm
    *   2:00 pm - 2:45 pm
    *   3:00 pm - 3:45 pm
*   **learning_materials (Text):** List any materials that will be used during the event, such as handouts, videos, or presentations.
*   **evaluation (Select One):** Evaluate the event's effectiveness by selecting:
    *   **Excellent**
    *   **Good**
    *   **Fair**
    *   **Poor**
*   **comments (Text):** Enter any additional comments or suggestions about the event.
*   **assigned_to (Email):** Enter the email of the person responsible for the event.
