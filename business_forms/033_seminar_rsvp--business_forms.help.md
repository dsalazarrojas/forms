# seminar_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The seminar RSVP form is used to collect information from attendees about their seminar attendance status, contact details, and any comments or additional information they may want to provide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the seminar details from the first field. If you cannot find your seminar, please contact the event organizer.
* In the "Attendance Status" field, select one of the provided options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* In the "Number of Attendance" field, enter the number of people attending the seminar.
* In the "Contact Info" field, enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* In the "Comments" field, enter any comments or questions you may have.
* In the "Email" field, enter your email address.
* In the "Phone" field, enter your phone number.
* In the "Comments (Email)" field, enter any additional comments or questions you may have that you'd like to receive an email response to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seminar Details**:
	+ Enter the name of the seminar you are attending.
* **Attendance Status**:
	+ Select one of the provided options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3) to indicate your attendance status.
* **Number of Attendance**:
	+ Enter the number of people attending the seminar with you.
* **Contact Info**:
	+ Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Comments**:
	+ Enter any comments or questions you may have.
* **Email**:
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone**:
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Comments (Email)**:
	+ Enter any additional comments or questions you may have that you'd like to receive an email response to.
 
## Tips

* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to receive any follow-up communication from the event organizer.
* If you have any issues with completing the form, please contact the event organizer for assistance.
