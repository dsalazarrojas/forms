# Sales Appointment Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to confirm sales appointments and capture relevant information about the customer and the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Lead ID**, **Customer Name**, **Customer Phone**, and **Customer Email** fields with the relevant information.
2. Enter the **Start Time** and **End Time** of the appointment, as per the scheduled time agreed upon with the customer.
3. Select the **Appointment Type** from the options provided (Sales, Demo, or Consult).
4. In the **Appointment Notes** field, provide a brief description of the appointment, including any relevant details or discussions.
5. If applicable, select the **Sync with Zoom**, **Sync with Google Calendar**, or **Sync with More** options to sync the appointment with the respective calendar systems.
6. In the **Notes** field, include any additional comments or reminders about the appointment.
7. Finally, review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead ID (id: 2)**: Enter the unique ID number assigned to the lead.
* **Customer Name (id: 3)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Phone (id: 4)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email (id: 5)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Start Time (id: 6)**: Enter the start time of the appointment.
* **End Time (id: 7)**: Enter the end time of the appointment.
* **Sales Agent Name (id: 8)**: Enter the name of the sales agent handling the appointment.
* **Sales Agent Phone (id: 9)**: Enter the phone number of the sales agent.
* **Appointment Type (id: 10)**: Select the type of appointment (Sales, Demo, or Consult).
* **Appointment Notes (id: 11)**: Enter a brief description of the appointment.
* **Sync with Zoom (id: 12)**: Select to sync the appointment with Zoom.
* **Sync with Google Calendar (id: 13)**: Select to sync the appointment with Google Calendar.
* **Sync with More (id: 14)**: Select to sync the appointment with other calendar systems.
* **Notes (id: 15)**: Enter additional comments or reminders about the appointment.
* **Form Data (id: 16)**: Enter any additional data related to the appointment.
* **Custom Form Data (id: 17)**: Enter custom data specific to the company or department.
* **Custom Form Data 2 (id: 18)**: Enter custom data specific to the company or department.
* **Sales Agent Name 2 (id: 19)**: Enter the name of the second sales agent involved in the appointment.
* **Customer Email 2 (id: 20)**: Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone 2 (id: 21)**: Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Sync with Custom Calendar (id: 22)**: Select to sync the appointment with a custom calendar system.
* **Sync with Custom Calendar 2 (id: 23)**: Select to sync the appointment with another custom calendar system.
* **Sync with Outlook (id: 24)**: Select to sync the appointment with Outlook.
* **Sync with Gmail (id: 25)**: Select to sync the appointment with Gmail.
