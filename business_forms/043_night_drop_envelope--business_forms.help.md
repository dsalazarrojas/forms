# Night Drop Envelope - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information, contact details, and vehicle-related data for processing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" or "Complete" button to begin.
2. Fill out the form fields according to the guidelines below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (1)** (`Customer Information`, text, optional): This field is used to collect general customer information. Please enter a brief description of the customer, if necessary.
* **Contact Number (2)** (`Contact Number`, text, optional): Enter the customer's contact phone number for further communication.
* **Vehicle Information (3)** (`Vehicle Information`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the customer's vehicle, including make, model, and any other relevant details.
* **Vehicle Year (4)** (`Vehicle Year`, number, optional): Enter the year of the customer's vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make (5)** (`Vehicle Make`, text, optional): Enter the make of the customer's vehicle.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the customer's provided information or ask for clarification.
* Double-check your submission before clicking "Submit" to ensure everything is complete and correct.
