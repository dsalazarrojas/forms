# Dumpster Rental Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dumpster Rental Form is used to collect information from customers interested in renting dumpsters for waste disposal. This includes delivery date, time, customer contact information, and specific requirements such as dumpster size and delivery schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `Customer Details` page with your name, address, and any other information as prompted.
2. Select a `Delivery Date` from the available dates.
3. Choose a `Delivery Time` that suits your needs.
4. Enter your `Phone` number for us to contact you.
5. Provide your `Email` address for correspondence.
6. Enter the `Delivery Address` where the dumpster will be delivered.
7. Select a `Delivery Schedule` that fits your needs (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
8. Choose the desired `Dumpster Size` from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details (Customer Details, text, required: false)**: This field is used to collect your name and address. Please enter your name and address as prompted.
* **Delivery Date (date, required: false)**: Select the date you would like to receive your dumpster.
* **Delivery Time (time, required: false)**: Choose a time that suits your needs for dumpster delivery.
* **Phone (Phone, text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Email (Email, email, required: false)**: Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Delivery Address (Delivery Address, text, required: false)**: Enter the address where you would like to receive your dumpster.
* **Delivery Schedule (Delivery Schedule, select_one, required: false)**: Select a delivery schedule that fits your needs. Options include Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday.
* **Dumpster Size (Dumpster Size, select_multiple, required: false)**: Choose the desired dumpster size from 1 cubic yard, 2 cubic yards, 3 cubic yards, 4 cubic yards, or 5 cubic yards.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields to submit the form successfully.
* You can select multiple dumpster sizes if needed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your selected contact information.
