# Press Release Distribution Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to subscribe to our press release distribution list. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure you receive the press releases you are interested in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your information and press the "Submit" button when you have finished. You can skip any fields that are not relevant to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Please enter your first name as it appears on your business card or identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text): Please enter your last name as it appears on your business card or identification documents.
* **Organization** (text): Please enter the name of the company or organization you represent.
* **Role** (text): Please enter your job role or position within your organization.
* **Email** (email): Please enter your company email address (or a personal email address if you don't have a company email).
* **Company** (text): Please enter the name of the company you represent (if different from your personal organization).
* **Phone** (text): Please enter your company phone number (or a personal phone number if you don't have a company phone).
* **Job Title** (text): Please enter your job title within your company.
* **_Note: This form is not intended for submitting press releases. Please do not use this form to submit your own press releases._
