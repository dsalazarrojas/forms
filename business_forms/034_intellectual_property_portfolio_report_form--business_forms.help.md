# Intellectual Property Portfolio Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document information about intellectual property portfolios. Use it to track and manage your portfolios, including patents, trademarks, copyrights, and trade secrets.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of intellectual property portfolio you are reporting on (Patent Portfolio, Trademark Portfolio, Copyright Portfolio, Trade Secret Portfolio, or Other).
2. Enter the name of your portfolio.
3. Enter the date of application or submission.
4. Provide a brief description of your portfolio.
5. Enter the name and contact information of the owner or agent.
6. Specify the ownership relationship (Owner, Co-Owner, or Agent).
7. Select the type of intellectual property (Patent, Trademark, Copyright, or Trade Secret).
8. Enter the status of your portfolio (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
9. Add any comments you have regarding your portfolio.
10. Select the specific intellectual property items included in your portfolio.
11. Indicate who submitted the report (Employee, Manager, or Other).
12. Enter the date the report was submitted.
13. Indicate who reviewed the report (Employee, Manager, or Other).
14. Enter the date the report was reviewed.
15. Add any comments regarding the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Type** (`Form Type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of intellectual property portfolio you are reporting on. This field helps categorize and track different types of portfolios.
* **Portfolio Name** (`Portfolio Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your portfolio. This field helps identify and differentiate between various portfolios.
* **Application Date** (`Application Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of application or submission. This field helps track the timeline of your portfolio's progress.
* **Description** (`Description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your portfolio. This field helps others understand the context and contents of your portfolio.
* **Owner Name** (`Owner Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the owner or agent. This field helps identify the responsible party for the portfolio.
* **Owner Contact** (`Owner Contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact information of the owner or agent. This field helps facilitate communication and verification.
* **Ownership Relationship** (`Ownership Relationship`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify the ownership relationship (Owner, Co-Owner, or Agent). This field helps clarify the ownership structure.
* **Portfolio Type** (`Portfolio Type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of intellectual property (Patent, Trademark, Copyright, or Trade Secret). This field helps categorize and track different types of intellectual properties.
* **Status** (`Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Enter the status of your portfolio (Pending, Approved, or Rejected). This field helps track the progress and outcome of your portfolio.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any comments regarding your portfolio. This field helps provide additional information and context.
* **Portfolio Items** (`Portfolio Items`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the specific intellectual property items included in your portfolio. This field helps provide a detailed list of the contents of your portfolio.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate who submitted the report (Employee, Manager, or Other). This field helps track the responsible party for submission.
* **Submitted Date** (`Submitted Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the report was submitted. This field helps track the timeline of submission.
* **Reviewed By** (`Reviewed By`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate who reviewed the report (Employee, Manager, or Other). This field helps track the responsible party for review.
* **Review Date** (`Review Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the report was reviewed. This field helps track the timeline of review.
* **Comments Review** (`Comments Review`, `text`, required: false): Add any comments regarding the review. This field helps provide additional information and context.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you select the correct form type and fill out all required fields for accurate tracking and reporting.
- Use the "Description" field to provide a clear and concise summary of your portfolio.
- Be accurate when entering ownership relationships and portfolio types to avoid confusion.
- Use the "Comments" and "Comments Review" fields for additional context and clarification.
- Review and update your report regularly to reflect changes in your portfolio's status and progress.
