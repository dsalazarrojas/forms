# Mortgage Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Payment Form is designed to collect and process mortgage payment information for a lender or financial institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your home address in the "Home Address" field. This should be the address of your property.
2. Enter the loan amount in the "Loan Amount" field. This is the total amount borrowed from the lender.
3. Enter your interest rate in the "Interest Rate" field. This is the rate charged on your loan.
4. Enter the loan term in the "Loan Term" field. This is the number of years the loan is taken over.
5. Enter your monthly payment in the "Monthly Payment" field. This is the date of your monthly payment.
6. Select your payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu. This can be Credit Card, Cash, or Online Banking.
7. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field. This is your phone number.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is your email.
9. Enter the name of your loan provider in the "Loan Provider" field. This is the name of your lender.
10. Select your mortgage type from the "Mortgage Type" dropdown menu. This can be Fixed Rate, Adjustable Rate, or Hybrid.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Address**: Your home address. Enter your house number and street address.
* **Loan Amount**: The total amount borrowed from the lender.
* **Interest Rate**: The rate charged on your loan.
* **Loan Term**: The number of years the loan is taken over.
* **Monthly Payment**: Enter the date of your monthly payment.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you will make your payment (Credit Card, Cash, or Online Banking).
* **Contact Number**: Your phone number.
* **Email Address**: Your email.
* **Loan Provider**: The name of your lender.
* **Mortgage Type**: Select the type of mortgage you have (Fixed Rate, Adjustable Rate, or Hybrid).
* **Payment Method**: Select how you will make your payment (Credit Card, Cash, or Online Banking).
