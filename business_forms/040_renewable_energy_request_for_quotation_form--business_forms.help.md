# Renewable Energy Request For Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a quote from a renewable energy provider. It includes details such as user information, provider information, and project specifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your name, email, phone number, company name, address, city, state, and zip code.
2.  Select the city, provider, and zip code of the provider who will be providing the energy services.
3.  Select the type of energy you need and the energy capacity in kW.
4.  Choose the installation date and the start and end times of the project.
5.  Enter any additional notes about the project.
6.  Enter any additional information about the provider, such as their email, phone number, address, city, state, and country.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name**: Please enter your name as the customer requesting the quote.
- **Email**: Enter your email address that the provider can use to contact you for further questions or concerns.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number that the provider can use to contact you directly.
- **Company**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
- **Address**: Enter your company's address, including street number and name.
- **City**: Enter the city where your company is located.
- **State**: Select the state where your company is located.
- **Zip**: Enter the zip code of your company's location.
- **Energy Type**: Select the type of energy you are requesting (e.g. Solar, Wind, etc.).
- **Energy Capacity**: Enter the total energy capacity you are looking for in kW.
- **Installation Date**: Select the date you want the provider to start installation.
- **Start Date**: Select the date you want the project to start.
- **End Date**: Select the end date of the project.
- **Note**: Any additional notes or comments you would like to add to the project.
- **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the provider you are choosing to work with.
- **Provider Email**: Enter the email address of the provider.
- **Provider Phone**: Enter the phone number of the provider.
- **Provider Address**: Enter the address of the provider.
- **Provider City**: Enter the city where the provider is located.
- **Provider State**: Select the state where the provider is located.
- **Provider Zip**: Enter the zip code of the provider's location.
- **Provider Country**: Enter the country where the provider is located.
- **Customer Name**: Enter your name as the customer who is requesting the quote.
- **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
- **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
