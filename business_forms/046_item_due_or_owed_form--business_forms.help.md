# Item Due Or Owed Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Item Due Or Owed Form" is used to collect information about items that are due or owed to the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, ensure you have all the necessary information at hand. The form consists of five fields:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Amount Owed** (`amount_owed`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount owed to the organization. This is a numerical value representing the monetary value of the item or service.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date by which the item is due or the deadline for repayment.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact information for the person or organization associated with the item, such as their name, phone number, or email.
* **Status Updates** (`status_updates`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional status updates related to the item, such as payment confirmation or resolution.
* **Item Due Or Owed Form** (`item_due_or_owed_form`, text, required/optional): This field is likely intended to collect a description of the item or service being tracked. However, the label is weak and unclear. A better label would be "Item Description" or "Item Name".

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out, especially if they are marked as required.
- Use the "Status Updates" field to provide any relevant updates or comments on the item's status.
