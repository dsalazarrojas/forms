# Employee Undertaking And Indemnity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a formal agreement between the employee and the employer, outlining the terms and conditions of employment. It's a crucial document that helps maintain a healthy and transparent working relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Employee Name field with your official name as it appears in your identification documents.
2. Enter your Role or position within the company in the Role field.
3. Provide the date of your undertaking in the Undertaking Date field. This date should match the start of your employment contract.
4. Enter the address of your employer in the Employer Address field.
5. Enter your address in the Employee Address field.
6. Fill out the Employer Phone, Employee Phone, Employer Email, and Employee Email fields with the correct contact information for both you and your employer.
7. Write any relevant text in the Undertaking Text field if needed.
8. Sign and date the form in the Employee Signature, Employer Signature, Date, and Time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Your official name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Role** (Role, text, optional): Your position or job title within the company.
* **Undertaking Date** (Undertaking Date, date, optional): The date when this undertaking starts.
* **Employer Address** (Employer Address, text, optional): The physical address of your employer.
* **Employee Address** (Employee Address, text, optional): Your physical address.
* **Employer Phone** (Employer Phone, text, optional): The phone number of your employer.
* **Employee Phone** (Employee Phone, text, optional): Your phone number.
* **Employer Email** (Employer Email, email, optional): The email address of your employer.
* **Employee Email** (Employee Email, email, optional): Your email address.
* **Undertaking Text** (Undertaking Text, text, optional): Any additional text or comments related to your undertaking.
* **Employee Signature** (Employee Signature, text, optional): Your handwritten signature as proof of your agreement.
* **Employer Signature** (Employer Signature, text, optional): Your employer's signature as proof of their agreement.
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): The date when this form is completed.
* **Time** (Time, time, optional): The time when this form is completed.
* **Employee Undertaking** (Employee Undertaking, text, optional): Any additional comments or terms related to your undertaking.
