# Banking Settlement Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Banking Settlement Overview form is designed to collect essential information for banking settlements. It is intended to gather details about the company, contact person, account number, and other relevant settlement-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the company involved in the banking settlement.
2. Provide the business address of the company.
3. Enter the name of the primary contact person.
4. Enter the email address and phone number of the contact person.
5. Specify the type of settlement (e.g., Loan Settlement, Credit Card Settlement, etc.).
6. Enter the original amount of the settlement.
7. Enter the agreed settlement amount.
8. Select the date of the agreement.
9. Choose the method of payment (e.g., Lump Sum Payment, Installment Plan, etc.).
10. (Optional) If using an installments plan, select the payment schedule.
11. (Optional) Enter the number of payments.
12. (Optional) Enter the date of the first payment and final payment.
13. Check the current status of the settlement.
14. Provide a reference number or case/file number (if applicable).
15. Enter the name of the legal representative (if applicable).
16. Confirm if the terms and conditions have been accepted.
17. Indicate whether documentation has been submitted.
18. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter the name of the company involved in the banking settlement.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`): Provide the business address of the company.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`): Enter the settlement account number.
* **Settlement Type** (`settlement_type`): Select the type of settlement (e.g., Loan Settlement, Credit Card Settlement, etc.).
* **Original Amount** (`original_amount`): Enter the original balance amount.
* **Settlement Amount** (`settlement_amount`): Enter the agreed settlement amount.
* **Settlement Date** (`settlement_date`): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Choose the method of payment (e.g., Lump Sum Payment, Installment Plan, etc.).
* **Payment Schedule** (`payment_schedule`): (Optional) Select the payment schedule if using an installments plan. This field is not required if using a lump sum payment.
* **Number of Payments** (`number_of_payments`): (Optional) Enter the total number of payments if using an installments plan.
* **First Payment Date** (`first_payment_date`): (Optional) Enter the date of the first payment. This field is not required if using a lump sum payment.
* **Final Payment Date** (`final_payment_date`): (Optional) Enter the date of the last payment. This field is not required if using a lump sum payment.
* **Settlement Status** (`settlement_status`): Select the current status of the settlement.
* **Reference Number** (`reference_number`): Enter a reference number or case/file number (if applicable).
* **Attorney Name** (`attorney_name`): Enter the name of the legal representative (if applicable).
* **Terms Accepted** (`terms_accepted`): Confirm if the terms and conditions have been accepted.
* **Documentation Provided** (`documentation_provided`): Indicate whether documentation has been submitted.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date the form was submitted.

Please note that the fields marked as "Optional" are not required for the form submission, but entering the information can provide additional context or detail for the settlement process.
