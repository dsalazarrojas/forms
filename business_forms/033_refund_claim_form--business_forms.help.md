# refund_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The refund claim form is used by customers to request a refund for their order. Fill out this form if you are a customer who has an issue with your order and would like to receive a refund.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the refund claim form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name to identify yourself as the customer.
2. Enter your order ID to specify which order you are requesting a refund for.
3. Provide a brief reason for the reason you are requesting a refund.
4. Enter the amount you are requesting as a refund.
5. Choose the date and time you requested the refund.
6. Enter an alternative contact method, either your phone number or email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your order receipt.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the order you are requesting a refund for. If you don't know your order ID, please contact our support team.
* **Reason for Refund** (`reason_for_refund`, text, optional): Briefly explain the reason you are requesting a refund. This will help us to understand the nature of the issue.
* **Amount Claimed** (`amount_claimed`, number, optional): Enter the amount you are requesting as a refund.
* **Date Refund Requested** (`date_refund_requested`, date, optional): Choose the date and time you requested the refund.
* **Time Refund Requested** (`time_refund_requested`, time, optional): Choose the time you requested the refund.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter an alternative contact method, your phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter an alternative contact method, your email address.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all relevant fields accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any supporting documents or evidence, please attach them to your refund request.
- Our support team will review your refund request and contact you within 3-5 business days.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
