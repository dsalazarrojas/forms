# Content Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture requests for content creation from team members. It is used to gather information about the type of content needed, the desired outcome, and other relevant details to ensure that the content team can create high-quality content that meets the expectations of the requester.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information about your request.
- Be specific about the type of content needed and its intended use.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field's hint for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, **text**, required): Enter your full name as it should appear on the content.
* **Requester Email** (`requester_email`, **email**, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Department** (`department`, **select_one**, required): Select the department you belong to from the list.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, **date**, required): Enter the date when you need the content.
* **Content Type Needed** (`content_type_needed`, **select_one**, required): Choose the type of content you need, such as blog post, website copy, or social media post.
* **Content Topic** (`content_topic`, **text**, required): Describe the topic of the content in a few words.
* **Content Objective** (`content_objective`, **select_one**, required): Select the goal of the content, such as to inform or educate, generate leads, or build brand awareness.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, **text**, required): Specify who the content is intended for.
* **Key Messages** (`key_messages`, **text**, required): Enter the main points you want to convey in the content.
* **Tone of Voice** (`tone_of_voice`, **select_one**, required): Choose the tone of voice for the content, such as professional, casual, or inspirational.
* **Word Count** (`word_count`, **select_one**, required): Select the desired length of the content, such as under 500 words or 2000+ words.
* **SEO Keywords** (`seo_keywords`, **text**, optional): Enter any relevant keywords to be included in the content.
* **Call to Action** (`call_to_action`, **text**, required): Specify the action you want the reader to take after reading the content.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, **date**, required): Enter the date by which you need the content.
* **Distribution Channels** (`distribution_channels`, **select_multiple**, required): Choose where the content will be published, such as the company website, social media, or email newsletter.
* **Visual Requirements** (`visual_requirements`, **select_multiple**, required): Select the visuals needed for the content, such as a featured image or custom graphics.
* **Reference Materials** (`reference_materials`, **text**, optional): Attach any relevant links or documents for reference.
* **Competitor Examples** (`competitor_examples`, **text**, optional): Attach any links to similar content for reference.
* **Budget** (`budget`, **select_one**, optional): Choose the budget for the content, such as internal creation or a specific price range.
* **Approval Required** (`approval_required`, **text**, required): Specify who needs to approve the content.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, **text**, optional): Add any other relevant information about the request.

## Tips

- Be clear and specific about your needs and expectations.
- Use the hints provided in each field to ensure you are entering the right information.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the content team for assistance.
