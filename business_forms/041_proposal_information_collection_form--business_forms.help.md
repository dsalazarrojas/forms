# Proposal Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Proposal Information Collection Form is a standardized template designed to gather essential details about a proposal for a business opportunity. This guide will walk you through each field of the form to help you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the proposal title and category.
2. Enter a brief summary of the proposal.
3. Select the proposal category from the available options.
4. Provide your contact information and submission date.
5. Optional: upload attachments related to the proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **proposal_title** (text, required/optional): Enter a descriptive title for the proposal.
* **proposal_category** (select_one, required): Choose the category that best describes the proposal.
* **proposal_summary** (text, optional): Provide a brief summary of the proposal (approx. 100-200 words).
* **deadline_date** (date, optional): Enter the proposal deadline date (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **proposal_submission_date** (date, optional): Enter the date the proposal was submitted.
* **submitter** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the submitter.
* **first_name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **contact_phone_number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **attachment_1** (text, optional): Enter the file name and path of the first attachment.
* **attachment_2** (text, optional): Enter the file name and path of the second attachment.

## Tips

* Be specific and clear in your title and category selection.
* Keep your summary concise and focused on the proposal's main points.
* Ensure that you have provided all required fields (proposal category and submitter).
* Attach relevant supporting documents to the proposal to facilitate review.
