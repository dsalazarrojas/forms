# Service Agreement Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain acknowledgment of a service agreement from users, ensuring they understand and accept the terms and conditions associated with the service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Review the form carefully**: Before starting, take a moment to read through the form to understand what is expected of you.
2. **Complete the form in sections**: The form is divided into two pages. Complete each page in order to ensure you understand and agree to all the terms and conditions.
3. **Check your answers**: Before submitting, review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Agreement Acknowledgment Form Page 1** (`service_agreement_acknowledgment_form_page_1`, text, optional): This field is a general acknowledgment of the service agreement. You can type a brief statement to acknowledge your understanding and acceptance of the service agreement.
* **Acknowledge** (`acknowledgment`, select_one, not required): Please select "True" to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the service agreement. You can select "False" if you are unable to confirm.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms and conditions`, note, not required): This field provides a detailed explanation of the terms and conditions associated with the service agreement. Read through this carefully to ensure you understand your obligations.
* **Service Agreement Acknowledgment Form Page 2** (`service_agreement_acknowledgment_form_page_2`, text, optional): This field is a secondary acknowledgment of the service agreement. You can type another brief statement to further confirm your understanding and acceptance of the service agreement.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the service agreement, contact the service provider before submitting this form.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting.
