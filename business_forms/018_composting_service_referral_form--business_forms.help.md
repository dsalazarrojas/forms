# Composting Service Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to capture referral information about individuals who have been referred to our composting service by existing customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
2. Enter your referral information in the corresponding fields.
3. Be as thorough as possible when providing additional information about the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral ID** (`referral_id`, text, required): Unique identifier for the referral.
* **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, required): Date of referral.
* **Referrer Name** (`referrer_name`, text, required): Your full name as the person making the referral.
* **Referrer Email** (`referrer_email`, email, required): Your email address.
* **Referrer Phone** (`referrer_phone`, text, optional): Your phone number (only if applicable).
* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are a current customer, a past customer, or if this is a first-time referral.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, optional): Your account number (if applicable).
* **Referred Person Name** (`referred_person_name`, text, required): Full name of the person being referred.
* **Referred Person Email** (`referred_person_email`, email, required): Email of the person being referred.
* **Referred Person Phone** (`referred_person_phone`, text, required): Phone number of the person being referred.
* **Referred Address** (`referred_address`, text, required): Address of the person being referred.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): City of the person being referred.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Postal code of the person being referred.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Type of property the person being referred is associated with.
* **Estimated Compost Volume** (`estimated_volume`, select_one, optional): Estimated amount of compostable waste per week.
* **Service Interest** (`service_interest`, select_multiple, required): List of services the person being referred is interested in.
* **How Did They Hear About Us** (`how_did_they_hear`, select_one, optional): How the person being referred learned about our service.
* **Start Date Preference** (`start_date_preference`, select_one, optional): When the person being referred would like to start service.
* **How Did You Hear About Referral Program** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): How you learned about our referral program.
* **Consent to Contact** (`consent_to_contact`, select_one, required): Indicate if the person being referred consents to be contacted.
* **Marketing Communications Consent** (`marketing_consent`, select_one, required): Indicate if you consent to receive marketing communications from us.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Any other details about the referral.
* **Referral Status** (`referral_status`, select_one, required): Current status of the referral (e.g., submitted, contacted, service scheduled, etc.).

Note: The form fields with an asterisk (\*) are required fields. Please ensure they are completed accurately and thoroughly.
