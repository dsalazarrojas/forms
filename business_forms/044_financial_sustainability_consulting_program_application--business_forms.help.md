# Financial Sustainability Consulting Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Sustainability Consulting Program is designed to support businesses in achieving their financial sustainability goals. This program aims to provide businesses with expert advice and resources to help them navigate financial challenges and achieve long-term success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your business name and contact details in the "User Information" section.
2. Describe the challenges your business is currently facing in the "Challenges" section.
3. State your goals for the program in the "Goals" section.
4. Specify if you need support with business or financial goals in the "Business Goals" and "Financial Goals" sections.
5. Indicate if you require support with non-financial aspects or online platforms in the "Non-Financial Support" and "Online Platform" sections.
6. Finally, confirm whether you need support with financial or non-financial aspects in the "Financial Support" and "Non-Financial Support" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (1)**: Enter your business name and contact details to help us get in touch with you.
* **Business Name (2)**: Type in your business name as it appears on your business cards or website.
* **Contact Details (3)**: Provide your email address, phone number, or other contact information so we can reach out to you.
* **Challenges (4)**: Describe the financial challenges your business is currently facing, such as cash flow issues or financial instability.
* **Goals (5)**: State your goals for the program, e.g., improving cash flow, increasing revenue, or reducing debt.
* **Business Goals (6)**: Check if you need support with business goals, such as marketing, operations, or strategy.
* **Financial Goals (7)**: Check if you need support with financial goals, such as budgeting, forecasting, or financial planning.
* **Support Needed (8)**: Check if you need any support, and select "Yes" if you do.
* **Financial Support (9)**: Check if you need support with financial aspects, such as budgeting, forecasting, or financial planning.
* **Non-Financial Support (10)**: Check if you need support with non-financial aspects, such as marketing, operations, or human resources.
* **Online Platform (11)**: Check if you need access to online platforms, such as training tools or resources.
