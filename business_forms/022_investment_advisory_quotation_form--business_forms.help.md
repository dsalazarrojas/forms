# Investment Advisory Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Advisory Quotation Form is a tool used to collect information about a client's investment needs and preferences. It helps the investment advisor create a personalized investment plan based on the client's goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the client.
2. Provide your company's name and contact information.
3. Enter your phone number and email address.
4. Enter your address.
5. Specify the amount you plan to invest.
6. Choose the type of investment you are interested in.
7. Select the investment timeframe.
8. Choose the investment period.
9. Add any additional notes or comments.
10. Select the category and subcategory for your investment.
11. Assign a user to work on your investment plan.
12. Assign a tool to be used for your investment.
13. Enter the date and time you submit your form.
14. Choose the current status of your investment.
15. Leave any additional user input fields blank, as they are not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter your name as the client.
* **Company**: Enter your company's name.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Investment Amount**: Enter the amount you plan to invest.
* **Investment Type**: Choose the type of investment you are interested in.
* **Investment Timeframe**: Select the timeframe for your investment.
* **Investment Period**: Choose the period for your investment.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your investment.
* **Category**: Select the category for your investment.
* **Sub Category**: Select the subcategory for your investment.
* **Assigned User**: Assign a user to work on your investment plan.
* **Assigned Tool**: Assign a tool to be used for your investment.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submit your form.
* **Submission Time**: Select the time you submit your form.
* **Status**: Choose the current status of your investment.
* **Created at**: This field is auto-generated and cannot be edited.
* **Updated at**: This field is auto-generated and cannot be edited.
* **User Input 1-5**: These fields are for additional input from the user, but are not required.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that you have provided accurate and complete information.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to ask your investment advisor for clarification.
* This form is designed to help your investment advisor create a personalized investment plan for you.
* If you have any concerns or issues with the form, please let us know.
