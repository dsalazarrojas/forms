# Estate Planning Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Planning Appointment Form is a document used to gather essential information for estate planning purposes. It is meant to assist professionals in their work with clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Choose the relevant **Date** for your appointment.
4. Select the **Time** for your appointment.
5. Indicate whether the appointment is for **Appointment Type** by selecting "Yes" or "No".
6. If necessary, choose which **Professionals** will be involved in the appointment (e.g., Lawyer, Financial Advisor, etc.).
7. Enter your **Contact Phone** number for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (first_name, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Select the date for the appointment.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Select the time for the appointment.
* **Appointment Type** (appointment_type, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the appointment is related to estate planning ("Yes") or not ("No").
* **Professionals** (professionals, select_multiple, optional): Choose which professionals will be involved in the appointment.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up purposes.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are accurate and relevant to your appointment. If you're unsure about any field, please ask the relevant professional for guidance.
