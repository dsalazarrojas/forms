# physical_security_supervisor_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to record the findings and actions taken during a physical security inspection of a facility. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all areas of the facility are inspected, identify potential security risks, and take action to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2.  Answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3.  Use the select\_multiple field for multiple selections.
4.  If a field is not applicable, leave it blank or mark it as N/A (not applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Main Content** (`page_1`): This is the main content of the inspection, where you can describe the overall condition of the inspected area.
*   **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`): Enter the date of the inspection in the format YYYY-mm-dd.
*   **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`): Sign and date the form to confirm that you conducted the inspection.
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter the name of the employee being inspected.
*   **Employee Position** (`employee_position`): Enter the position of the employee being inspected.
*   **Location** (`location`): Enter the location of the inspection.
*   **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`): Enter the date of the inspection in the format YYYY-mm-dd.
*   **Time of Inspection** (`time_of_inspection`): Enter the time of the inspection in the format 24-hour time format (HH:MM).
*   **Type of Inspection** (`type_of_inspection`): Select one of the following:
    *   General
    *   Specific
    *   Other (please specify)
*   **Inspection Area** (`inspection_area`): Select one or more of the following:
    *   General
    *   Specific
    *   Other (please specify)
*   **Inspection Items** (`inspection_items`): Select one or more of the following:
    *   Door
    *   Window
    *   Other (please specify)
*   **Findings** (`findings`): Describe any safety or security issues found during the inspection.
*   **Actions Taken** (`actions_taken`): Describe any actions taken to address the safety or security issues.
*   **Incidents** (`incidents`): Describe any incidents or near-miss incidents.
*   **Recommendations** (`recommendations`): Describe any recommendations or suggestions for improvement.
*   **Comments** (`comments`): Additional comments or feedback about the inspection.
*   **Supervisor Signature 2** (`supervisor_signature_2`): Sign and date the form to confirm that you conducted the inspection.
*   **Date of Review** (`date_of_review`): Enter the date of the review in the format YYYY-mm-dd.
*   **Time of Review** (`time_of_review`): Enter the time of the review in the 24-hour time format (HH:MM).
*   **Reviewer** (`reviewer`): Enter the name of the reviewer.
*   **Reviewer Position** (`reviewer_position`): Enter the position of the reviewer.
*   **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`): Sign and date the form to confirm that you reviewed the inspection results.

## Tips

*   Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
*   Use clear and concise language when filling in the form.
*   Be honest and transparent about any findings or issues.
*   Take all necessary actions to address any safety or security issues identified during the inspection.
*   Keep a copy of the completed form for future reference.
