# sleep_baby_sleep_intake_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from parents about their baby's sleep patterns and concerns. It is intended to help healthcare professionals provide guidance and support to parents in addressing their child's sleep needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name as the parent or caregiver.
2.  Provide information about your baby's sleep patterns, including the time they go to sleep and wake up.
3.  Describe any concerns you may have about your baby's sleep.
4.  If you have any additional information you would like to share, please do so in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (text, required): Please enter your name as the parent or caregiver.
* **Sleep Time** (time, required): Enter the time your baby goes to sleep each night.
* **Parent Bedtime** (time, required): Enter the time your baby goes to bed each night.
* **Baby Age** (number, required): Enter your baby's age in months.
* **Parent Concerns** (text, required): Describe any concerns you have about your baby's sleep.
* **Baby Sleep Patterns** (text, required): Describe your baby's typical sleep patterns, including any difficulties you have noticed.
* **Parent Worrying About** (text, required): Describe any worries or concerns you have about your baby's sleep.
* **Notes** (text, required): Share any additional information you think would be helpful for healthcare professionals to know.
