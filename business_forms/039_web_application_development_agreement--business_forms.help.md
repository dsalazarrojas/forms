# Web Application Development Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for capturing agreement details between the client and the developer for a web application development project.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
3. If a field has options, select the relevant value that best suits your project requirements.
4. Once you have filled out all the fields, review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_information`, `text`, required: false): This section is for capturing the client's basic information, including name and company name.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, required: false): This section is for capturing the project's details, such as project start date and deadline.
* **Project Delivery** (`project_delivery`, `text`, required: false): This section is for capturing the project delivery information, such as project status and payment terms.
* **Client Requirements** (`client_requirements`, `text`, required: false): This section is for capturing the client's requirements and expectations for the project.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required: false): This section is for capturing the payment terms and conditions, such as payment amount and frequency.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, required: false): This field is for capturing the project start date.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, `date`, required: false): This field is for capturing the project deadline.
* **Payment Schedule** (`payment_schedule`, `text`, required: false): This section is for capturing the payment schedule and terms.
* **Payment Terms Frequency** (`payment_terms_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is for selecting the payment frequency, such as weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required: false): This field is for capturing the payment amount.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the payment method, such as bank transfer, PayPal, or cheque.
* **Payment Deadline** (`payment_deadline`, `date`, required: false): This field is for capturing the payment deadline.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, `text`, required: false): This section is for capturing any additional notes or comments from the client.
* **Developer Notes** (`developer_notes`, `text`, required: false): This section is for capturing any additional notes or comments from the developer.
* **Agreement Term** (`agreement_term`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the agreement term, such as 30 days, 60 days, or 90 days.
* **Agreement Duration** (`agreement_duration`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the agreement duration, such as 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, or 1 year.
* **Payment Term** (`payment_term`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the payment term, such as 14 days, 30 days, 60 days, or 90 days.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is for selecting the payment frequency, such as weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the payment status, such as active or inactive.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required: false): This field is for capturing the client's signature.
* **Developer Signature** (`developer_signature`, `text`, required: false): This field is for capturing the developer's signature.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, `date`, required: false): This field is for capturing the agreement date.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting the project status, such as ongoing, done, or cancelled.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is for capturing the client's name.
* **Client Company** (`client_company`, `text`, required: false): This field is for capturing the client's company name.
