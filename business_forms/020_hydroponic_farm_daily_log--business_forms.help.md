# hydroponic_farm_daily_log - Help Guide
## Purpose

The hydroponic farm daily log is designed to monitor and document the day-to-day activities and progress of plants within a hydroponic farm. This form helps farm operators track nutrient levels, crop health, temperature, and other essential metrics to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions for optimal plant growth and farm management.


## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select a specific farm or crop to ensure that the form is filled out consistently and accurately.
2.  Review any existing entries for consistency before making new ones.
3.  Complete each page with the required and optional fields as follows:
   1.  **Daily Log** (Page 1):
      - Provide a brief description of the day’s activities and any significant events.
   2.  **Nutrient Measurements** (Page 2):
      - Record any measurements or observations regarding nutrient levels within the farm.
   3.  **Crop Health** (Page 3):
      - Note down observations or comments about the health of the crops.
   4.  **Temperature and Humidity** (Page 4):
      - Record the current temperature and humidity levels within the farm.
   5.  **Crop Status** (Page 5):
      - Select the current status of the crops, such as Healthy, Diseased, Pests, Waterlogged, or Overwatered.
   6.  **Temperature** (Page 6):
      - Record the current temperature reading.
   7.  **Water Level** (Page 7):
      - Choose the current water level status from the options Full, Low, Empty, or Critical.
   8.  **Notes** (Page 8) and **Observations** (Page 9) (Optional):
      - Record any additional comments, observations, or notes about the farm or crops.
   9.  **Email** (Page 10) and **Phone Number** (Page 11) (Optional):
      - Enter your contact information for any follow-up or communication.
   10. **Research Notes** (Page 12) (Optional):
      - Record any additional notes or information regarding research or other relevant data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Log (Page 1)**: A place to document your daily activities or any significant events on the farm.
* **Nutrient Measurements (Page 2)**: Record the measurements or observations of nutrient levels, but this field is optional.
* **Crop Health (Page 3)**: Note down any comments or observations regarding the health of the crops.
* **Temperature and Humidity (Page 4)**: Record the current temperature and humidity levels.
* **Crop Status (Page 5)**: Select the current status of the crops from the options provided.
* **Temperature (Page 6)**: Record the current temperature reading.
* **Water Level (Page 7)**: Choose the current water level status from the options provided.
* **Notes (Page 8)** and **Observations (Page 9)**: Record any additional comments, observations, or notes.
* **Email (Page 10)** and **Phone Number (Page 11)**: Enter your contact information for any follow-up or communication.
* **Research Notes (Page 12)**: Record any additional notes or information regarding research or other relevant data.
