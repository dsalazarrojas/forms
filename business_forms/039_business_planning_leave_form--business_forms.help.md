# Business Planning Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Planning Leave Form is used to capture details about employee leave requests, including dates, duration, and purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the employee information fields: Employee Name, Employee ID, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Provide your department and direct manager's name, email, and approval status.
3. Enter the dates for when you would like to take leave: Leave Request Date, Leave Start Date, and Leave End Date.
4. Specify the total number of working days requested.
5. Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g. Personal, Business, etc.).
6. Describe the business planning objectives, expected deliverables, and business impact of your leave.
7. Indicate how your duties will be covered during your leave period.
8. If applicable, provide any critical deadlines you have during your leave period.
9. Specify any emergency contact information.
10. Provide any additional context or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Your full name, e.g. John Doe.
* **Employee ID**: Your company-provided ID number, e.g. JD-1234.
* **Email Address**: Your work email, e.g. johndoe@example.com.
* **Phone Number**: Your work phone number, e.g. 123-456-7890.
* **Department**: The department you belong to, e.g. Marketing Department.
* **Direct Manager Name**: Your supervisor's name, e.g. Jane Smith.
* **Manager Email**: Your supervisor's email, e.g. janesmith@example.com.
* **Manager Approval**: The status of your manager's approval, e.g. Approved, Not Yet Approved, or Pending.
* **Leave Request Date**: The date this leave request is made.
* **Leave Start Date**: The date you would like to start your leave.
* **Leave End Date**: The date you would like your leave to end.
* **Total Days Requested**: The number of working days you are requesting for leave.
* **Type of Leave**: The type of leave you are requesting (e.g. Personal, Business, etc.).
* **Business Planning Objectives**: Describe the objectives of your business planning, e.g. "Develop a new marketing strategy."
* **Expected Deliverables**: Describe the deliverables you expect to produce during your leave, e.g. "A marketing plan report."
* **Expected Business Impact**: Describe the expected impact on the company, e.g. "Increased brand awareness."
* **Coverage Arrangements**: Describe how your duties will be covered during your leave period.
* **Critical Deadlines**: List any critical deadlines you have during your leave period.
* **Emergency Contact**: Specify an emergency contact person and their contact information.
* **Previous Leave Balance**: If applicable, enter the number of days remaining in your leave balance.
* **Additional Notes**: Any additional information about your leave request.
* **Manager Approval**: The status of your manager's approval.
