# Wage Deduction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wage Deduction Form is used to record wage deductions for employees' paychecks. This form should be used when an employee's pay needs to be reduced due to various reasons such as loans, taxes, or other financial deductions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's information in the `employee_info` field.
2. Enter the amount to be deducted from the employee's pay in the `deduction_amount` field.
3. Choose the type of deduction by selecting an option in the `deduction_type` field.
4. Select the pay period affected by the deduction using the `pay_period` field.
5. If applicable, choose the payment frequency using the `pay_frequency` field.
6. Choose the date the payment will be made using the `date_of_payment` field.
7. Choose the method of payment using the `payment_method` field.
8. Optionally, add any additional notes or comments in the `notes` field.
9. Enter the employee's email address in the `email` field.
10. If necessary, select the option for who created the entry using the `created_by` field.
11. Click "Submit" to save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_info** (Text): Enter the employee's name and/or ID for easy identification.
* **Deduction Amount** (Number): Enter the amount to be deducted from the employee's pay. This should be a numerical value with two decimal places.
* **Deduction Type** (Select One): Choose the type of deduction, such as loan, tax, or other.
* **Pay Period** (Select Multiple): Select the pay periods affected by the deduction.
* **Pay Frequency** (Select One): Choose the payment frequency, such as weekly or bi-weekly.
* **Date Of Payment** (Date): Enter the date the payment will be made.
* **Time Of Payment** (Time): Choose the time of payment, such as AM or PM.
* **Payment Method** (Select One): Choose the method of payment, such as bank transfer or cash.
* **Notes** (Note): Add any additional comments or notes about the deduction, if needed.
* **Email** (Email): Enter the employee's email address, if available.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter the employee's phone number, if available.
* **Created By** (Select One): Select who created the entry, for audit or tracking purposes.
* **Phone** (Text): This field is not actually necessary, as it was not required in the original form schema, but we have to account for it as per your instructions.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review and confirm the accuracy of the information before submitting.
* Double-check that the employee's name and ID is correct in the `employee_info` field.
* Use the correct format for date and time entries (YYYY-MM-DD and 12:00 AM/12:00 PM).
