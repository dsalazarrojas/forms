# Pop Up Feedback Form By Julie Potts - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users about a specific event, allowing Julie Potts to gather valuable insights and follow up on any necessary actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Feedback Form Data" section with your comments and thoughts about the event.
2. If relevant, select an option from the "Event Details" dropdown to provide more context about the event.
3. In the "Follow up" field, provide any additional information that Julie Potts should know to follow up on your feedback.
4. If you need to be contacted, select your preferred follow-up method (Phone, Email, In Person) in the "Follow up Type" field.
5. If you have an email address, provide it in the "Email" field to ensure that Julie Potts can reach out to you directly.
6. If you have a phone number, provide it in the "Phone" field to enable Julie Potts to contact you.
7. If you have a specific follow-up date and time in mind, enter it in the "Follow up Date" and "Follow up Time" fields.
8. Use the "Additional Comments" field to provide any additional context or information that is not covered in the previous fields.
9. Finally, the "Created By" field is not intended for user input but is populated automatically by the system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Form Data** (Feedback Form Data, text, optional): This is the main section of the form where you can provide any feedback you have about the event.
* **Event Details** (Event Details, select_one, optional): Select an option to provide more context about the event.
* **Follow up** (Follow up, text, optional): This field is used to provide any additional information that Julie Potts should know to follow up on your feedback.
* **Follow up Type** (Follow up Type, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more methods by which you would like Julie Potts to follow up with you (Phone, Email, In Person).
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Provide your email address if you would like Julie Potts to contact you directly.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Provide your phone number if you would like Julie Potts to contact you by phone.
* **Follow up Date** (Follow up Date, date, optional): Enter a specific date if you have one in mind for a follow-up.
* **Follow up Time** (Follow up Time, time, optional): Enter a specific time if you have one in mind for a follow-up.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, note, optional): Use this field to provide any additional context or information that is not covered in the previous fields.
* **Created By** (Created By, text, not applicable): This field is populated automatically by the system and should not be changed by the user.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback and follow-up information.
* If you have any additional comments or context, use the "Additional Comments" field to provide them.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact Julie Potts directly.
