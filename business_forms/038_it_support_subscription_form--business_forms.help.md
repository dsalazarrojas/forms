# IT Support Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Support Subscription Form is designed to collect essential information from users for efficient IT support. This form will aid the IT support team in resolving your technical issues by gathering details about your identity and support request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Make sure all required fields are filled out.
3. Use a valid email address for the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the correct format (e.g., XXX-XXX-XXXX or XXX.XXXX.XXXX).
5. If you have any attachments or further details, please use the Details field.
6. Review your form before submitting to ensure it is correct and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name, such as John.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name, such as Doe.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address, such as johndoe@example.com.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the correct format, such as 123-456-7890 or 123.456.7890.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Support Request** (`support_request`, text, required): Describe the specific issue you are experiencing.
* **Details** (`details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or attachments that may aid the support team in resolving your issue.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your completed form.
