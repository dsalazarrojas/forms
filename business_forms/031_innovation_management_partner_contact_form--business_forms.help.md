# Innovation Management Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innovation Management Partner Contact Form is designed to gather information from potential partners for the purpose of collaboration and partnership opportunities. It's used to capture details about the partner and their areas of interest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure it's a valid email address.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your business location in the "Location" field.
5. Describe your inquiry or interest in the "Inquire" field.
6. List your areas of interest or expertise in the "Interest" field.
7. Select your agreement to share information for partnership purposes in the "Agree" field.
8. Provide any additional information you'd like to share in the "Additional information" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`partner_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name for our reference.
* **Email** (`partner_email`, Text, Optional): Enter a valid email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone** (`partner_number`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for easier communication.
* **Location** (`partner_location`, Text, Optional): Enter your business location for context.
* **Inquire** (`partner_inquiry`, Text, Optional): Briefly describe your inquiry or interest in innovation management.
* **Interest** (`partner_interest`, Text, Optional): List your areas of interest or expertise.
* **Agree** (`partner_agree`, Select One, Optional): Select "I agree to share information for partnership purposes" to indicate your consent.
* **Additional information** (`partner_notes`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional context or details about your interest in partnership.
