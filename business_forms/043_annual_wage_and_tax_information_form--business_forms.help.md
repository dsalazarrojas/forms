# Annual Wage And Tax Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and report annual wage and tax information for employees. It is intended to ensure accurate tax reporting and compliance with relevant laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary documents and information before starting the form.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, following the instructions for each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the employee's full name, including first and last names.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the employer-assigned ID number for the employee, if applicable.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security`, `text`, required): Enter the employee's Social Security Number for tax purposes.
* **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, `number`, required): Enter the calendar year for which you are reporting.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the employer.
* **Employer ID or EIN** (`employer_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the employer's identification number or Employer Identification Number (EIN).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the employee's employment status, such as Full-time, Part-time, Seasonal, Contractor, or Temporary.
* **Gross Wages or Salary** (`gross_wages`, `number`, required): Enter the total earnings of the employee before any deductions.
* **Federal Income Tax Withheld** (`federal_tax_withheld`, `number`, required): Enter the total federal income tax deducted.
* **Social Security Tax** (`social_security_tax`, `number`, required): Enter the FICA Social Security tax deducted.
* **Medicare Tax** (`medicare_tax`, `number`, required): Enter the FICA Medicare tax deducted.
* **State Income Tax Withheld** (`state_tax_withheld`, `number`, optional): Enter the total state income tax deducted.
* **State of Employment** (`state_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the state in which the employee was employed.
* **Local Tax Withheld** (`local_tax_withheld`, `number`, optional): Enter the total local tax deducted.
* **Local Tax Jurisdiction** (`local_jurisdiction`, `text`, optional): Enter the city or county name.
* **Dependent Care Benefits** (`dependent_care_benefits`, `number`, optional): Enter pre-tax childcare benefits.
* **Health Insurance Premiums** (`health_insurance_premiums`, `number`, optional): Enter pre-tax health insurance premiums.
* **Retirement Plan Contributions** (`retirement_contributions`, `number`, optional): Enter contributions to 401(k), 403(b), or IRAs.
* **Other Deductions** (`other_deductions`, `number`, optional): Enter union dues, parking, etc.
* **Tips Reported to Employer** (`tips_reported`, `number`, optional): Enter total tips received and reported.
* **Nontaxable Benefits** (`nontaxable_benefits`, `text`, optional): Enter commutier benefits, FSA, etc.
* **Net Pay or Take-Home Pay** (`net_pay`, `number`, required): Enter the total pay after all deductions.
* **I Verify this Information for W2 Reporting** (`w2_verification`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the information is correct for W2 reporting.
* **Withholding Adjustments** (`withholding_adjustments`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether any withholding adjustments are needed.

Note: This form is intended to help ensure accurate tax reporting and compliance with relevant laws and regulations. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR or payroll department.
