# Invoice Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for creating invoices for customer orders. It's intended for internal use within the organization to manage customer orders and payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide the customer's details, including their name, email address, and phone number.
3. Enter the payment details, such as the payment method (e.g., text, email, phone) and any additional payment terms or conditions.
4. Fill out the address sections for the billing and shipping addresses.
5. Enter the order details, including the order total and any additional notes.
6. Set the payment status and date of the invoice.
7. Optionally, select the payment date, due date, and assigned agent.
8. Review and fill out any other required fields as needed.
9. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Customer Details**: Enter the customer's name, email address, and phone number.
* **Payment Details**: Select the payment method (e.g., text, email, phone) and any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Billing Address**: Enter the billing address for the customer.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address for the customer.
* **Order Details**: Enter the order total and any additional notes.
* **Payment Status**: Set the payment status (e.g., text, email, phone).
* **Date of Invoice**: Enter the date of the invoice.
* **Due Date**: Set the due date for the payment.
* **Payment Date**: Optionally, select the payment date.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Fax**: Enter the customer's fax number.
* **Customer Service**: Select the customer service channels you'd like to use (e.g., text, email, phone).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Assigned Agent**: Select the agent assigned to handle the order.
* **Order ID**: Enter the order ID.
* **Invoice Total**: Enter the total amount of the invoice.
* **Payment Terms and Conditions**: Select any payment terms and conditions.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete and submit the form.
