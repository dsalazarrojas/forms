# Personal Finance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your personal finance situation to help with financial planning and analysis. Please answer the questions accurately to provide a clear picture of your financial situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question accurately and honestly.
2. Select the corresponding options for each field as requested.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Family Income
* **Family Income** (`family_income`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your family's total income.

### Monthly Savings
* **Monthly Savings** (`monthly_savings`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your monthly savings amount.

### Financial Goals
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the financial goals that apply to you, such as saving for a down payment on a house, retirement, or education expenses.

### Investment Return
* **Investment Return** (`investment_return`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your expected investment returns.

### Debt
* **Debt** (`debt`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the level of debt you have, with options 0 (no debt), 1 (moderate debt), or 2 (high debt).

### Credit Card Balance
* **Credit Card Balance** (`credit_card_balance`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your credit card balance.

### Income
* **Income** (`income`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your total income.

### Savings Goal
* **Savings Goal** (`savings_goal`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your savings goal amount.

### Financial Institutions
* **Financial Institutions** (`financial_institutions`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the financial institutions you use, such as banks, credit unions, or brokerages.

### Emergency Fund
* **Emergency Fund** (`emergency_fund`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your emergency fund amount.

### Financial Independence
* **Financial Independence** (`financial_independence`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the level of financial independence you have, with options 0 (not financially independent), 1 (moderately financially independent), or 2 (highly financially independent).

# Tips
* Be accurate and honest when answering the questions.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
