# Win Loss Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Win Loss Tracking Form is designed to track sales outcomes, providing valuable insights into the success or failure of sales efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the outcome (Won, Lost, or No Decision) that describes the sales interaction.
* Choose the current stage of the sales process (Lead, Demo, or Closed).
* Enter the name of the sales person involved with the customer.
* Enter the name of the customer.
* Choose the status of the lead (New Lead, Qualified, Disqualified, Qualified for Demo, or Closed).
* Choose the status of the account manager.
* Select the status of the owner.
* (Optional) Enter any comments about the outcome.
* (Optional) Enter a rating (1-5) for the outcome.
* (Optional) Enter a probability percentage for the outcome.
* (Optional) Enter the time spent on the sales interaction.
* (Optional) Enter the date and time of the sales interaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Outcome** (Won, Lost, No Decision): This field describes the result of the sales interaction.
* **Current Stage** (Lead, Demo, Closed): This field tracks the current status of the sales process.
* **Sales Person** (text): Enter the name of the sales person involved with the customer.
* **Customer Name** (text): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Account Manager** (text): Enter the name of the account manager.
* **Account Manager Status** (Active, Inactive, Not Applicable): This field tracks the status of the account manager.
* **Owner** (text): Enter the name of the owner.
* **Owner Status** (Active, Inactive, Not Applicable): This field tracks the status of the owner.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date and time of the sales interaction.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time spent on the sales interaction.
* **Category** (text): Enter a category for the outcome (optional).
* **Type** (text): Enter a type for the outcome (optional).
* **Comments** (note): Enter any comments about the outcome (optional).
* **Rating** (number): Enter a rating (1-5) for the outcome (optional).
* **Probability** (number): Enter a probability percentage for the outcome (optional).
* **Lead Status** (New Lead, Qualified, Disqualified, Qualified for Demo, Closed): This field tracks the status of the lead.
* **Last Updated** (date): This field tracks the last updated date of the sales outcome.
* **Last Updated By** (text): Enter the name of the person who last updated the sales outcome.
* **Last Updated By Status** (Active, Inactive, Not Applicable): This field tracks the status of the person who last updated the sales outcome.
* **Next Action** (text): Enter the next action to be taken (optional).
