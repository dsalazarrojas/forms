# Private Label Packaging Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about private label packaging orders for custom products. It is intended for customers who are placing special orders for custom packaging for their products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about the product being ordered.
2. Ensure that you have the correct delivery address and date for the product.
3. Enter your contact information (customer name, email, and phone number) so we can keep you updated on the status of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (Product Name, text, required): Enter the name of the product being ordered. This will help us identify the product and ensure that we provide the correct custom packaging.
* **Product Description** (Product Description, text, optional): If desired, enter a brief description of the product to help us understand the custom requirements for the packaging.
* **Product Image** (Product Image, text, required): Enter the image or description of the product to help us understand the visual requirements for the custom packaging.
* **Delivery Address** (Delivery Address, text, required): Enter the address where the custom packaging will be delivered. This is important for ensuring that the correct address is selected for the shipment.
* **Delivery Date** (Delivery Date, date, required): Select the date for the delivery of the custom packaging. This will help us ensure that the packaging is delivered on time.
* **Delivery Time** (Delivery Time, time, required): Select the time for the delivery of the custom packaging. This is important for scheduling the delivery process.
* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required): Enter your name as the person placing the order.
* **Customer Email** (Customer Email, email, required): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on the status of your order.
* **Customer Phone** (Customer Phone, text, optional): If you have any special instructions or questions about the order, you can enter your phone number here. This is not required but can be helpful for communication.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
- Double-check the delivery address, date, and time to avoid any issues with the delivery process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
