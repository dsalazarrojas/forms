# financialForecastReportForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The financialForecastReportForm is designed to collect financial information from users, including financial forecasts, revenue, profit, and loss data. This form is used to gather insights into the financial performance of a business or organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the "Financial Forecast" text field with a brief description of the financial forecast. 
2. Select the relevant "Year" in the dropdown menu.
3. Select the relevant "Month" in the dropdown menu.
4. In the "Revenue" field, select one or more options that apply to your financial forecast. 
   - Options may include "Option 1", "Option 2", and "Option 3".
5. Enter the "Profit" amount for the financial forecast.
6. Enter the "Loss" amount for the financial forecast.
7. Select a date for the financial forecast in the "Date" field.
8. Select a time for the financial forecast in the "Time" field.
9. In the "Currency" field, select the relevant currency for the financial forecast. 
   - Options may include "Option 1", "Option 2", and "Option 3".
10. In the "Financial Institution" field, select one or more relevant financial institutions. 
    - Options may include "Option 1", "Option 2", and "Option 3".
11. Enter the "Contact Person" who is responsible for the financial forecast. 
12. Enter the email address of the "Contact Person". 
13. Enter the phone number of the "Contact Person". 
14. Enter the address of the "Contact Person" or the financial institution.
15. Leave any additional comments about the financial forecast in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Forecast** (Text, **financial_forecast**, Required): A brief description of the financial forecast.
* **Year** (Number, **year**, Required): Select the year for which the financial forecast is relevant.
* **Month** (Text, **month**, Required): Select the month for which the financial forecast is relevant.
* **Revenue** (Select Multiple, **revenue**, Optional): Select one or more options that apply to your financial forecast.
* **Profit** (Number, **profit**, Optional): Enter the profit amount for the financial forecast.
* **Loss** (Number, **loss**, Optional): Enter the loss amount for the financial forecast.
* **Date** (Date, **date**, Required): Select a date for the financial forecast.
* **Time** (Time, **time**, Optional): Select a time for the financial forecast.
* **Currency** (Select One, **currency**, Required): Select the relevant currency for the financial forecast.
* **Financial Institution** (Select Multiple, **financial_institution**, Optional): Select one or more relevant financial institutions.
* **Contact Person** (Text, **contact_person**, Optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the financial forecast.
* **Email** (Email, **email**, Optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (Text, **phone**, Optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Address** (Text, **address**, Optional): Enter the address of the contact person or the financial institution.
* **Comments** (Note, **comments**, Optional): Leave any additional comments about the financial forecast.

**Tips:**

* Ensure you fill out the form accurately and according to the guidelines above.
