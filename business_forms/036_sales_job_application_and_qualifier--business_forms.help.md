# Sales Job Application And Qualifier - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Job Application And Qualifier form is a crucial step in the hiring process for the sales team. It gathers information about a job applicant's personal details, sales experience, and availability, helping the team to assess their fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your first name in the `First Name` field.
* Enter your last name in the `Last Name` field.
* Provide your work-related email address in the `Email` field, formatted as a standard email address (e.g., `[name]@[domain].com`).
* Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
* Select your job title from the available options (`Sales Representative`, `Account Manager`, or `Sales Manager`).
* Choose your sales territory from the available options (`North`, `South`, `East`, `West`, or `All`).
* Select your date and time of availability using the date and time fields.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, use the `Note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`sales_job_application_and_qualifier_1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`sales_job_application_and_qualifier_2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`sales_job_application_and_qualifier_3`, `email`, required): Enter your work-related email address in this field, formatted as a standard email address.
* **Phone** (`sales_job_application_and_qualifier_4`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Job Title** (`sales_job_application_and_qualifier_5`, `select_one`, required): Select your job title from the available options.
* **Sales Experience** (`sales_job_application_and_qualifier_6`, `number`, required): Enter your sales experience in years.
* **Sales Territory** (`sales_job_application_and_qualifier_7`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your sales territory from the available options.
* **Date** (`sales_job_application_and_qualifier_8`, `date`, required): Select your date of availability using the date field.
* **Time** (`sales_job_application_and_qualifier_9`, `time`, required): Select your time of availability using the time field.
* **Note** (`sales_job_application_and_qualifier_10`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes, use this field.
