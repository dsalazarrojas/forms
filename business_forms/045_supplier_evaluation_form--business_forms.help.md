# Supplier Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Evaluation Form is designed to gather information about a supplier's performance and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the supplier's name in the "Supplier Name" field.
2. Enter the supplier's address in the "Supplier Address" field.
3. Fill out the supplier's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. Specify the contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field.
5. Check whether the supplier is active or inactive in the "Supplier Type" field.
6. Select the supplier's status as either "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Supplier Status" field.
7. If necessary, enter the date and time of evaluation in the "Evaluation Date" and "Evaluation Time" fields respectively.
8. Fill out any additional comments or notes about the supplier in the "Evaluation Note" field.
9. Enter any additional evaluation comment in the "Evaluation Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, required: false): Fill out the supplier's name in this field.
* **Supplier Address** (`supplier_address`, text, required: false): Enter the supplier's address in this field.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Fill out the supplier's contact number in this field.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Specify the contact person's name in this field.
* **Supplier Type** (`supplier_type`, select_one, required: false): Check whether the supplier is active or inactive in this field.
* **Supplier Status** (`supplier_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the supplier's status as either "Active" or "Inactive" in this field.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of evaluation in this field.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of evaluation in this field.
* **Evaluation Note** (`evaluation_note`, note, required: false): Fill out any additional comments or notes about the supplier in this field.
* **Evaluation Comment** (`evaluation_comment`, text, required: false): Enter any additional evaluation comments in this field.
