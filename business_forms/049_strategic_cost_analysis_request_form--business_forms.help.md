# Strategic Cost Analysis Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who are requesting a strategic cost analysis for their projects.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required and optional fields are filled in. Please provide accurate information to the best of your abilities.
- Review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure you're not missing any critical information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request Form** (`1`, `text`, optional):
  * This field is where you can enter a brief description of your project.
  * Please provide a clear and concise summary of your request.
- **Cost Category** (`2`, `select_one`, optional):
  * This field asks you to select a category for your project.
  * Choose the most relevant category from the provided options.
- **Cost Type** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional):
  * In this field, select all relevant types of costs associated with your project.
  * This will help in understanding the scope and complexity of your request.
- **Cost Location** (`4`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter the location where the project will be executed.
  * Ensure you provide a specific location to allow for accurate calculations.
- **Cost Date** (`5`, `date`, optional):
  * Enter the date you are requesting this cost analysis for.
  * If this is a recurring project, please provide the start and end dates.
- **Cost Amount** (`6`, `number`, optional):
  * Estimate the total cost of your project.
  * Use the currency specified in the **Cost Currency** field.
- **Project Description** (`7`, `note`, optional):
  * Provide a detailed description of your project.
  * This will help us understand the project's objectives and requirements.
- **Submit Request** (`8`, `select_one`, optional):
  * Choose how you would like to submit this request.
  * Options will vary depending on the company's submission process.
- **Review Status** (`9`, `select_multiple`, optional):
  * Select all relevant review statuses.
  * This will help in understanding the current status of your request.
- **Comments** (`10`, `text`, optional):
  * Add any additional comments or explanations for your request.
  * This is an opportunity to provide further context.
- **Attachments** (`11`, `note`, optional):
  * Attach any supporting documents or files related to your project.
  * This will help in understanding the project's scope and requirements.
- **Submitted By** (`12`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter the person submitting this request.
  * This will help in tracking and verifying the request.
- **Submitted Date** (`13`, `date`, optional):
  * Enter the date this request was submitted.
  * This will help in tracking the request's timeline.
- **Submitted Time** (`14`, `time`, optional):
  * Enter the time this request was submitted.
  * This will help in understanding the timing of the request.
- **Submitted By Email** (`15`, `email`, optional):
  * Enter the email of the person submitting this request.
  * This will help in contacting the submitter.
- **Submitted By Phone** (`16`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter the phone number of the person submitting this request.
  * This will help in reaching out to the submitter.
- **Cost Breakdown** (`17`, `note`, optional):
  * Provide a detailed breakdown of the project's costs.
  * This will help in understanding the project's financial implications.
- **Cost Frequency** (`18`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter the frequency of the project's costs.
  * This will help in understanding the project's financial obligations.
- **Cost Currency** (`19`, `select_multiple`, optional):
  * Select all relevant currencies associated with your project.
  * This will help in understanding the project's financial implications.
- **Cost Currency Symbol** (`20`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter the symbol for your selected currency.
  * This will help in presenting the project's financial information.
- **Cost Rounding Precision** (`21`, `select_one`, optional):
  * Select the precision for rounding costs in your project.
  * This will help in presenting the project's financial information.
- **Cost Rounding** (`22`, `select_multiple`, optional):
  * Select all relevant rounding options for your project.
  * This will help in presenting the project's financial information.
- **Cost Rounding Symbol** (`23`, `text`, optional):
  * Enter the symbol for rounding costs in your project.
  * This will help in presenting the project's financial information.
- **Submitted By** (`24`, `select_multiple`, optional):
  * Select all relevant submitter information.
  * This will help in understanding the submitter's status.
  * Options will vary depending on the company's submission process.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask for guidance.
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure you've covered all necessary information.
