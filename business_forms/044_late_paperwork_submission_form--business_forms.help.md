**Late Paperwork Submission Form - Help Guide**

**Purpose:**
This form is for submitting late paperwork and reporting overdue documents. It's used by employees to provide information about missed deadlines and submit documentation for review.

Before filling out this form, please note that all fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out. You can check the form's purpose and the fields' labels to ensure you have all the necessary information.

**How To Complete This Form:**
1. Fill out all required fields carefully and accurately.
2. Enter your employee name, employee ID, and contact information in their respective fields.
3. Indicate if the document is late by selecting "Yes" in the "overdue_document_type" field.
4. Provide a clear and concise description of the late document in the "document_description" field.
5. Enter the date the document was due in the "due_date" field.
6. Confirm the submission by selecting "Yes" in the "submitter" field.
7. Review and fill out the "late_paperwork_submission_form_notes" field for any additional information or comments.

**Field-by-Field Explanation:**

* **overdue_documents**: Enter a brief description of the late document, such as the document name or type.
* **employee_name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or badge.
* **employee_id**: Enter your employee ID number for reference.
* **overdue_document_type**: Select "Yes" to indicate that the document is late.
* **document_description**: Provide a clear and detailed description of the late document.
* **due_date**: Enter the date the document was originally due.
* **submitter**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you are submitting this late document.
* **contact_number**: Enter your contact phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **email**: Enter your work email for communication.
* **late_paperwork_submission_form_notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the submission.
* **submission_form_id**: Enter any reference ID for the submission form.
* **document_id**: Enter any relevant document ID.
* **submission_date**: Enter the date you are submitting this document.
* **submitter_id**: Enter any additional submitter ID information.
* **late_paperwork_submission_form_comments**: Add any comments or feedback about the submission.
* **late_paperwork_submission_form_attachments**: Attach any relevant documents or files to this submission.
* **submission_status**: Select the status of the submission for tracking purposes.
* **submission_status_description**: Add any description of the submission status.
* **late_paperwork_submission_form_submitter**: Enter the submitter's name or title.
* **late_paperwork_submission_form_date**: Enter the date of the submission.
* **late_paperwork_submission_form_time**: Enter the time of the submission.
* **late_paperwork_submission_form_location**: Enter the location of the submission.
* **late_paperwork_submission_form_comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the submission.
* **late_paperwork_submission_form_submission_form**: Enter any reference to the submission form.
* **late_paperwork_submission_form_form_version**: Enter any version number of the submission form.

Note: All fields not marked as "required" are optional and can be filled out if necessary. Please ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting this form.
