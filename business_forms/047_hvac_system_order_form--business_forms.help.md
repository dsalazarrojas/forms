# hvac_system_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HVAC System Order Form is used to collect information from customers or clients for the installation, maintenance, or repair of HVAC systems. This form helps to document the details of the order, ensuring that all necessary information is captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the corresponding field.
2. Enter your **Customer Email** in the field provided.
3. Provide your **Customer Phone Number** for easy contact.
4. Enter the **Order Date** for the service or installation.
5. Select whether the order requires a **System Type** (e.g., HVAC system, air conditioner, heater).
6. Check whether the order includes a **System Model** (e.g., specific type of HVAC system).
7. Enter your **Address** where the HVAC system will be installed or serviced.
8. Enter your **Zip Code** for shipping or delivery purposes.
9. Provide a **System Description** of the order, including any special instructions or details.
10. Add any **Customer Message** to communicate with the assigned contractor or technician.
11. Indicate whether the order will be assigned to a specific **Contractor** (e.g., for service or maintenance).
12. Finally, enter the **Tool Assigned** for the job (e.g., specific type of tool required for the task).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
### Customer Email
* **Customer Email**: Enter your business or personal email address for correspondence.
### Customer Phone Number
* **Customer Phone Number**: Provide your phone number for easy contact and communication.
### Order Date
* **Order Date**: Enter the date for the service or installation, if applicable.
### System Type
* **System Type**: Indicate whether the order is for a specific type of HVAC system (e.g., air conditioner, heater).
### System Model
* **System Model**: Check whether the order includes a specific type of HVAC system model.
### Address
* **Address**: Enter the address where the HVAC system will be installed or serviced.
### Zip Code
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code for shipping or delivery purposes.
### System Description
* **System Description**: Provide a detailed description of the order, including any special instructions or details.
### Customer Message
* **Customer Message**: Enter any additional comments or messages for the assigned contractor or technician.
### Assigned Contractor
* **Assigned Contractor**: Indicate whether the order will be assigned to a specific contractor (e.g., for service or maintenance).
### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool required for the job, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information for errors before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the assigned contractor or support team.
