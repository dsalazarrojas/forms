# Construction Site Covid 19 Protocol Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Site Covid 19 Protocol Form is a health screening questionnaire designed to assess the risk of COVID-19 transmission on the construction site. The form is intended to be completed by construction workers to provide information about their health status, recent activities, and interactions with others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

*   Review each field carefully and answer honestly.
*   Enter your date of birth or screening date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   Select "True" or "False" for questions that require a yes or no response.
*   Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
*   Ensure that you provide accurate information about your health status, recent activities, and interactions with others.
*   Review and sign the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Screening Date** (`screening_date`): Enter the date of your most recent health screening.
*   **Screening Time** (`screening_time`): Enter the time of your most recent health screening.
*   **Full Name** (`worker_name`): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID number (if applicable).
*   **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter the name of your employer.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your job title or position.
*   **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter your best contact number.
*   **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address.
*   **Site Location** (`site_location`): Enter the name of the construction site you are working at.
*   **Temperature Reading** (`temperature`): Enter your body temperature in Fahrenheit.
*   **Do You Have a Fever** (`fever_present`): Select "True" if you have a temperature over 100.4 F, and "False" otherwise.
*   **Do You Have a New or Worsening Cough** (`cough_present`): Select "True" if you have a new or worsening cough.
*   **Do You Have Shortness of Breath** (`shortness_of_breath`): Select "True" if you have shortness of breath or difficulty breathing.
*   **Have You Had Chills or Repeated Shaking** (`chills_present`): Select "True" if you have had chills or repeated shaking.
*   **Do You Have Muscle Pain** (`muscle_pain`): Select "True" if you have muscle pain or body aches.
*   **Do You Have a New or Worsening Headache** (`headache_present`): Select "True" if you have a new or worsening headache.
*   **Do You Have a Sore Throat** (`sore_throat`): Select "True" if you have a sore throat.
*   **Loss of Taste or Smell** (`loss_of_taste_smell`): Select "True" if you have lost taste or smell.
*   **Close Contact with a Confirmed Case** (`close_contact_exposure`): Select "True" if you have had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case in the past 14 days.
*   **Tested Positive for COVID-19** (`positive_test`): Select "True" if you have tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
*   **Waiting for Test Results** (`waiting_for_results`): Select "True" if you are waiting for test results.
*   **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`): Select your COVID-19 vaccination status.
*   **Booster Dose Received** (`booster_received`): Select "True" if you have received a booster dose.
*   **Screening Result** (`screening_result`): Select your screening result.
*   **Site Induction Completed** (`site_induction_completed`): Select "True" if you have completed site induction.
*   **Planned Destination After Site** (`planned_destination`): Enter the location you plan to go after leaving the site.
*   **Worker Signature** (`worker_signature`): Sign your full name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Screener Name** (`screener_name`): Enter the name of the person conducting the screening.
*   **Screener Signature** (`screener_signature`): Sign your full name as it appears on your official identification.

## Tips
*   Please answer honestly and accurately to ensure accurate and effective health monitoring.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the site management or health authorities.
*   Review and understand the form before submitting it.
*   Ensure that you have not completed this form in the past 14 days if you have previously tested positive for COVID-19.
*   If you are unsure about any information, please consult the person conducting the screening.
*   Please sign and date the form before submitting it.
