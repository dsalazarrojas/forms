# Insurance Agent Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Agent Application Form is used to collect information from individuals applying to become insurance agents. It helps us understand your qualifications, experience, and preferences to determine whether you would be a good fit for our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill out your first name and last name in the corresponding text fields.
* Enter your email address in the email field.
* If applicable, provide your date of birth.
* If you have experience working as an insurance agent, enter the number of years you have been in the field.
* If you are currently working with an insurance company, enter the name of the company and your current address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): If applicable, provide your date of birth.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, text, required: false): If you have an insurance license, enter the number here.
* **Experience Years** (`experience_years`, number, required: false): If you have experience as an insurance agent, enter the number of years.
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, text, required: false): If you are currently working with an insurance company, enter the name of the company here.
* **Insurance Agency Address** (`insurance_agency_address`, text, required: false): If you are currently working with an insurance company, enter their address here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, but feel free to leave fields marked as optional.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy to ensure it can be reached for further communication.
* If you have any relevant attachments or supporting documents, you may attach them separately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
