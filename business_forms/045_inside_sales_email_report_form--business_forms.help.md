# Inside Sales Email Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Inside Sales Email Report Form is used to track and report on the status of leads. It helps to ensure that all necessary information is collected and recorded for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as prompted.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Use the provided options for select fields.
4. Enter your email in the Sales Person Email field.
5. Write a brief description of the lead in the Email Body field.
6. Enter the lead's phone number in the Lead Phone Number field.
7. Select the lead's status from the Lead Status options.
8. Choose the action taken from the Action Taken options.
9. Enter the follow up date and time in the Follow up Date and Follow up Time fields if applicable.
10. Select the email sent status from the Email Sent Status options.
11. Enter any follow up notes or outcomes for the lead in the Follow up Notes and Follow up Outcome Notes fields.
12. Identify the lead's owner by entering their name in the Lead Owner field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Lead Email Form
* **Lead Email Form**: Enter the email address of the lead.
* **Sales Person Email**: Enter your sales email address.
* **Email Body**: Enter a brief description of the lead.
* **Lead Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the lead.
* **Lead Status**: Select the status of the lead from the options provided.
* **Action Taken**: Choose the action taken from the options provided.
* **Follow up Date**: Enter the date of the follow up.
* **Follow up Time**: Enter the time of the follow up.
* **Next Follow up Date**: Enter the next follow up date.
* **Next Follow up Time**: Enter the next follow up time.
* **Email Sent Status**: Select the status of the email from the options provided.
* **Follow up Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the follow up.
* **Follow up Outcome**: Choose the outcome of the follow up from the options provided.
* **Follow up Outcome Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the follow up outcome.
* **Lead Owner**: Identify the lead owner by entering their name.

Please note that some fields may not be required, depending on the specific lead situation. Ensure to fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
