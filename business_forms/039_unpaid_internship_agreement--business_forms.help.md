# Unpaid Internship Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of an unpaid internship agreement between a company and an intern. It captures essential information about the internship, including start and end dates, duration, work hours, pay frequency, compensation type, internship type, job position, job description, work location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Agreement Details section with the relevant information about the internship.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the internship in the respective fields.
3. Specify the internship duration, work hours, and pay frequency in the respective fields.
4. Choose the compensation type and internship type from the available options.
5. Enter the job position, job description, and work location details.
6. Enter the supervisor's name and contact information.
7. Sign and date the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Details (id: 1)**:
  * This field is a note-type field, where you can enter any relevant details about the internship.
* **Start Date (id: 2)**:
  * Enter the date when the internship is expected to start.
* **End Date (id: 3)**:
  * Enter the date when the internship is expected to end.
* **Internship Duration (id: 4)**:
  * Enter the number of weeks or months the internship is expected to last.
* **Internship Start (id: 5)**:
  * Enter the time of the day when the internship is expected to start.
* **Internship End (id: 6)**:
  * Enter the time of the day when the internship is expected to end.
* **Work Hours (id: 7)**:
  * Enter the number of hours the intern is expected to work per week.
* **Work Days (id: 8)**:
  * Enter the number of days the intern is expected to work per week.
* **Pay Frequency (id: 9)**:
  * Choose how frequently the intern will be paid (Biweekly or Monthly).
* **Compensation Type (id: 10)**:
  * Choose if the intern will receive a stipend or no compensation.
* **Internship Type (id: 11)**:
  * Choose if the internship is full-time or part-time.
* **Job Position (id: 12)**:
  * Enter the job title or position of the intern.
* **Job Description (id: 13)**:
  * Enter a brief description of the intern's job responsibilities.
* **Work Location (id: 14)**:
  * Enter the office address or location where the intern will work.
* **Work Location City (id: 15)**:
  * Enter the city where the intern will work.
* **Work Location State (id: 16)**:
  * Enter the state where the intern will work.
* **Work Location Country (id: 17)**:
  * Enter the country where the intern will work.
* **Work Location Zip (id: 18)**:
  * Enter the zip code of the work location.
* **Work Location Phone (id: 19)**:
  * Enter the phone number of the work location.
* **Work Location Email (id: 20)**:
  * Enter the email address of the work location.
* **Supervisor Name (id: 21)**:
  * Enter the name of the supervisor or mentor.
* **Supervisor Contact (id: 22)**:
  * Enter the contact information of the supervisor.
* **Agreement Date (id: 23)**:
  * Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Expiration (id: 24)**:
  * Enter the date the agreement expires.
* **Agreement Signature (id: 25)**:
  * Sign and date the agreement.
