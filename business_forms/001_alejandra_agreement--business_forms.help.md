# Alejandra Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alejandra Agreement form is used to collect personal and agreement-related information for business purposes. This form helps create or manage service agreements, non-disclosure agreements, consultancy agreements, or other types of agreements between businesses or individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full legal name as it appears on your ID.
2. Provide a valid email address for contract communications.
3. Choose the correct agreement type from the options provided.
4. Enter the effective start date of the agreement.
5. If applicable, select the duration of the agreement in months (enter 0 for indefinite).
6. Briefly summarize the key terms and obligations of the agreement.
7. Type your full name as your electronic signature for the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (Required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email address** (Required): Enter a valid email address for contract communications.
* **Company name** (Optional): Enter the name of your organization or employer if applicable.
* **Phone number** (Optional): Enter a secondary contact phone number.
* **Agreement type** (Required): Select the best match for your agreement type.
* **Agreement start date** (Required): Enter the effective date of the agreement.
* **Agreement duration months** (Optional): Enter the length of the agreement in months (enter 0 for indefinite).
* **Key terms and obligations** (Optional): Briefly summarize the key terms and obligations of the agreement.
* **Electronic signature name** (Required): Type your full name as your electronic signature for the agreement.
