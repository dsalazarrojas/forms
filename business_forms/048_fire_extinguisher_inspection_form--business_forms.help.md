# fire_extinguisher_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Extinguisher Inspection Form is designed to help facility staff inspect and maintain fire extinguishers in a facility. This form is used to record the type of fire extinguisher, its status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of fire extinguisher being inspected from the dropdown menu.
2. Check the current status of the fire extinguisher (Working, Not Working, or Maintenance Required).
3. Enter the date and time of the inspection.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about the fire extinguisher.
5. Select the inspection frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Semi-Annually).
6. Enter the last maintenance date and date of the next inspection.
7. Check the maintenance status of the fire extinguisher (Working, Not Working, or Maintenance Required).
8. Select the location where the fire extinguisher is installed.
9. Enter any additional details about the fire extinguisher, such as its capacity, pressure, weight, color, label, or tag.
10. Enter the last inspector's name and any other notes or comments.
11. Select the inspection frequency for the fire extinguisher (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Semi-Annually).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fire Extinguisher Inspection Form**: This is the main page of the form.
* **Extinguisher Type**: Select the type of fire extinguisher being inspected from the dropdown menu.
* **Inspected By**: Enter the name of the person or personnel who performed the inspection.
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date and time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Fire Extinguisher Status**: Check the current status of the fire extinguisher (Working, Not Working, or Maintenance Required).
* **Inspection Frequency**: Select the frequency of the inspection (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Semi-Annually).
* **Last Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the last inspection.
* **Last Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the last person who performed the inspection.
* **Fire Safety Equipment List**: Enter any additional details about the fire equipment list.
* **Fire Extinguisher Location**: Enter the location where the fire extinguisher is installed.
* **Fire Extinguisher Type**: Enter any additional details about the fire extinguisher, such as its type.
* **Fire Extinguisher Capacity**: Enter the capacity of the fire extinguisher.
* **Fire Extinguisher Pressure**: Enter the pressure of the fire extinguisher.
* **Fire Extinguisher Weight**: Enter the weight of the fire extinguisher.
* **Fire Extinguisher Color**: Enter the color of the fire extinguisher.
* **Fire Extinguisher Label**: Enter the label of the fire extinguisher.
* **Fire Extinguisher Tag**: Enter the tag of the fire extinguisher.
* **Fire Extinguisher Model**: Enter the model of the fire extinguisher.
* **Fire Extinguisher Maintenance Status**: Check the current maintenance status of the fire extinguisher (Working, Not Working, or Maintenance Required).
* **Fire Extinguisher Last Maintenance Date**: Enter the date of the last maintenance.
* **Fire Extinguisher Inspection Due Date**: Enter the date when the next inspection is due.
* **Fire Extinguisher Inspection Frequency**: Select the frequency of the inspection (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Semi-Annually).
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
