# non_solicitation_agreement_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a non-solicitation agreement declaration form used to document an individual's non-solicitation agreement with a business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in Business Name**: Enter the name of the business you are affiliated with.
2. **Enter Business Address**: Provide the address of the business.
3. **Select Non-Solicitation Agreement**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are entering into a non-solicitation agreement with the business.
4. **List Employee Names**: Select the name of the employees involved in the agreement (e.g. John and Jane).
5. **Enter Employee Titles**: Type in the job title of the employees.
6. **Enter Dates of Service Start and End**: Fill in the start and end dates of the employee's service.
7. **Enter Signature**: Sign the form as an acknowledgment of your agreement.
8. **Enter Date and Time**: Fill in the current date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the business you are affiliated with.
- **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Provide the address of the business.
- **Non-Solicitation Agreement** (`solicitation_agreement`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are entering into a non-solicitation agreement with the business.
  - 'Yes': I am entering into a non-solicitation agreement with the business.
  - 'No': I am not entering into a non-solicitation agreement with the business.
- **Employee Names** (`employee_names`, select_multiple, required): Select the name(s) of the employee(s) involved in the agreement (e.g. John and Jane).
- **Employee Titles** (`employee_titles`, text, required): Type in the job title of the employee(s) (e.g. Sales Representative).
- **Dates of Service Start** (`employee_dates_of_service_start`, date, required): Enter the start date of the employee's service.
- **Dates of Service End** (`employee_dates_of_service_end`, date, required): Enter the end date of the employee's service.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign the form as an acknowledgment of your agreement.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Fill in the current date.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Fill in the current time (optional).

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Ensure the information is accurate and up-to-date.
