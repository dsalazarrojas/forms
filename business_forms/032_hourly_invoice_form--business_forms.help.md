# Hourly Invoice Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Hourly Invoice Form is a tool used for capturing hourly invoices and related information for employees. The form collects details that enable accurate tracking and processing of employee hours worked, payment terms, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. Make sure to provide the required information for each field.
2. **Start by filling out the invoice details** on page 1:
   - **Invoice Date**: Enter the date of the invoice.
3. **Invoice Number**: Enter the unique identifier for this invoice.
4. **Work Hours**: Enter the total number of hours worked for the invoice.
5. **Hourly Rate**: Enter the rate per hour for which you were paid.
6. **Total Invoice**: Automatically calculated based on work hours and hourly rate.
7. **Payment Terms**: Select the preferred payment terms for this invoice (Cash, Credit, Other).
8. **Payment Method**: Select one or more payment methods (Bank Transfer, Cash, Check, Online Payment, Other).
9. **Enter additional details** on page 2:
   - **Employee Name**: Enter your name as the person who worked on this project.
   - **Client Name**: Enter the client's name for whom the project was completed.
   - **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project for which you were working.
   - **Work Hours Note**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the hours worked.
