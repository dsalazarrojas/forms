# Complaint Resolution Protocol Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Complaint Resolution Protocol Approval Form is used to obtain approval for a single complaint resolution protocol from internal management. This form ensures that all necessary steps and costs are clearly documented and authorized before proceeding with the resolution process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Complaint Case Reference** (required) to identify the case for which the protocol is being designed.
2. Describe the **Proposed Resolution Strategy** (required) to outline the steps to be taken to resolve the complaint.
3. Estimate the **Total Cost of Resolution** (required) and include any relevant refunds, replacement costs, and labor.
4. Specify the **Internal Resources Required** (optional) for the protocol, e.g., IT support, legal review, logistics team.
5. Identify any **Risks or Potential Complications** (optional) that may arise during the resolution process.
6. Select the **Legal and Compliance Review Status** (required) to indicate the level of review completed (Fully Approved by Legal, Approved with Conditions, Not Required for this Case Level, Pending Review).
7. Enter the **Approving Manager Name** (required) of the person authorized to sign off on this protocol.
8. Enter the **Approver Role/Title** (required) for the approving manager.
9. Date the **Protocol Approval Date** (required) to record the date the protocol was officially authorized.
10. Confirm the **Protocol Approval Acknowledgement** (required) by selecting "I so authorize".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Case Reference** (id: 2, required): Enter the unique case ID or reference number for which the protocol is being designed.
* **Proposed Resolution Strategy** (id: 3, required): Provide a detailed description of the steps to be taken to resolve the complaint. Be specific and clear about any actions, costs, or resources involved.
* **Estimated Total Cost of Resolution** (id: 4, required): Enter an estimate of the total cost of the resolution process, including any refunds, replacement costs, or labor involved.
* **Internal Resources Required** (id: 5, optional): List the internal resources required for the protocol, such as IT support, legal review, or logistics team.
* **Identified Risks or Potential Complications** (id: 6, optional): Identify any potential risks or complications that may arise during the resolution process.
* **Legal and Compliance Review Status** (id: 7, required): Select the status of the legal review completed (Fully Approved by Legal, Approved with Conditions, Not Required for this Case Level, Pending Review).
* **Approving Manager Name** (id: 8, required): Enter the name of the person authorized to sign off on this protocol.
* **Approver Role/Title** (id: 9, required): Enter the role or title of the approving manager.
* **Protocol Approval Date** (id: 10, required): Date the protocol was officially authorized.
* **Protocol Approval Acknowledgement** (id: 11, required): Confirm the authorization by selecting "I so authorize".
* **Digital Sign Acknowledgement** (id: 12, required): Final notification that the authorization has been recorded in the system.
