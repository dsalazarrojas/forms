# Guard Line Input Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Guard Line Input Form is a critical tool used for collecting and recording data related to guard lines. It helps in tracking and monitoring guard line activity, ensuring the safety and integrity of various systems or processes.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Determine the Patrol Date
The patrol date is the date when the guard line was patrolled or inspected. Please enter the correct date in the format MM/DD/YYYY, where MM is the month, DD is the day, and YYYY is the year.

### Step 2: Enter Checkpoint ID
The checkpoint ID is a unique identifier for each checkpoint on the guard line. Enter the correct checkpoint ID to associate with the patrol date.

### Step 3: Enter Incident ID (if applicable)
If an incident occurred during the patrol, enter the corresponding incident ID to record it in the system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patrol Date** (`patrol_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the guard line was patrolled or inspected.
* **Checkpoint ID** (`checkpoint_id`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for each checkpoint on the guard line.
* **Incident ID** (`incident_id`, number, optional): Enter the corresponding incident ID if an incident occurred during the patrol.
 
## Tips
- Double-check the entered data for accuracy and consistency.
- If you are unsure about any field, please review the form instructions or contact the relevant authorities.
- Ensure that you have permission to access and complete the form.
- Regularly review and update your completed form to reflect changes in guard line activity.
