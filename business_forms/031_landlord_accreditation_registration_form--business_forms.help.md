# Landlord Accreditation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for Landlords to register their details with the organization. The form collects information about the landlord's property and license type for administrative purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and property details (address, type, and status) on **Landlord Details** and **Landlord Property Details** fields.
2. Select your **Code of Conduct Agreement** by choosing one of the options (**Yes** or **No**).
3. Fill in your **Contact Person** name and **Email** and **Phone** fields.
4. Select your **Landlord License Type** from the options (**License 1**, **License 2**, or **Other**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord Details**: Enter your name as the landlord.
* **Landlord Property Details**:
	+ **Property Address**: Enter your property's street address.
	+ **Property Type**: Select the type of property from the options (**Apartment**, **House**, **Duplex**, or **Other**).
	+ **Property Status**: Select the status of your property (**Rent**, **Own**, **Lease**, or **Other**).
	+ **Property Size**:
		- **Property Size Unit**: Leave blank for units not applicable.
* **Code of Conduct Agreement**: Select **Yes** if you agree to the code of conduct.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name for your property.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Landlord Name**: Enter your name as the landlord.
* **Property Address**: Enter your property's street address.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property from the options (**Apartment**, **House**, **Duplex**, or **Other**).
* **Property Status**: Select the status of your property (**Rent**, **Own**, **Lease**, or **Other**).
* **Property Size**: Enter the size of your property.
* **Property Size Unit**: Enter the unit of measurement (e.g., sqft, sqm, etc.) for the property size.
* **Landlord License Type**: Select your license type from the options (**License 1**, **License 2**, or **Other**).

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the organization's support team for assistance.
