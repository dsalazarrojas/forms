# Vendor Fee Review Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Fee Review Consent Form is used to collect contact information from vendors and obtain their consent to review their fees. This form helps to ensure that vendors are aware of and agree to the review of their fees, which is essential for internal auditing and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your vendor contact details in the **Vendor Contact Details** field.
2. Choose whether you consent to the review of your fees by selecting one or more of the options in the **Consent to Fee Reviews** field.
3. In the **Signed Response** field, provide your signed acknowledgment of the form. This is where you physically sign this document.
4. In the **Vendor Signed Response** field, provide your vendor's signed acknowledgment of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Contact Details (Page 1)**: Provide your name, company name, contact number, and email address to be contacted in regards to this form.
* **Vendor Fee Reviews (Page 1)**: Check whether you consent to the review of your fees by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the multiple-choice dropdown.
* **Signed Response (Page 1)**: This is where you sign your name, date, and acknowledge that you have read and understood the purpose of this form.
* **Vendor Signed Response (Page 2)**: Similar to the above field, but for your vendor's signed acknowledgment of the form.

Note: This form is only to be completed when you have been asked to do so, and not otherwise. It is an internal auditing process and not a formal contract or agreement. By signing this form, you are not committing to any agreement, you are simply acknowledging that you are aware and consent to the review of your fees.
