## Channel Partner Lead Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about potential business leads from channel partners. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary details about the leads, including their company name, contact information, and sales opportunity description. This data will help channel partners identify potential sales opportunities and prioritize follow-up activities. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the submission date for when the lead was submitted.
2. Enter your channel partner's company name.
3. Provide your own name as the contact person.
4. Enter your company's email address.
5. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
6. Fill in the lead company's name.
7. Enter the name of the contact person at the lead company.
8. Enter the lead company contact title.
9. Enter the prospect's email address.
10. Enter the prospect's phone number.
11. Select the lead company's size (number of employees).
12. Select the lead company's industry or sector.
13. Describe the sales opportunity in the opportunity description field.
14. Enter an estimated deal size (if applicable).
15. Select the product or service the prospect is interested in.
16. Choose the expected sales timeline.
17. Select the lead priority level (hot, warm, or cold).
18. Enter the requested margin or commission percentage (if applicable).
19. (Optional) Add any additional lead information that may be relevant.
20. Mark if the lead has been previously disclosed to the partner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Submission Date**: Enter the date when you submitted this lead. (Date, required)
* **Channel Partner Name**: Enter your company name. (Text, required)
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter your name as the contact person. (Text, required)
* **Partner Email**: Enter your company email. (Email, required)
* **Partner Phone**: Enter your company phone number. (Text, required)
* **Lead Company Name**: Enter the name of the lead company. (Text, required)
* **Lead Contact Person Name**: Enter the contact person's name at the lead company. (Text, required)
* **Lead Contact Title**: Enter the contact person's title at the lead company. (Text, not required)
* **Lead Email**: Enter the prospect's email address. (Email, required)
* **Lead Phone**: Enter the prospect's phone number. (Text, required)
* **Lead Company Size**: Select the number of employees the prospect company has. (Select one, not required)
* **Lead Industry**: Select the industry or sector the lead company belongs to. (Text, required)
* **Opportunity Description**: Describe the sales opportunity in this field. (Text, required)
* **Estimated Deal Size**: Enter an estimated deal size. (Number, not required)
* **Product or Service Interested In**: Select the product or service the prospect is interested in. (Text, required)
* **Sales Timeline**: Choose the expected sales timeline. (Select one, not required)
* **Lead Priority Level**: Select the lead priority level. (Select one, required)
* **Request Partner Margin**: Enter the requested margin or commission percentage. (Number, not required)
* **Additional Lead Information**: Add any other relevant information about the lead. (Text, not required)
* **Lead Has Been Disclosed to Partner**: Mark if the lead has been previously disclosed to the partner. (Select one, required)
