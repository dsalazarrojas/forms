1. Fill in the company details section (fields 1-6) with your company's information, including name, representative, email, phone number, address, and notes.
2. In the partner details section (fields 7-10), enter your partner's information, such as their representative, email, phone number, and address.

4. In the signatures section (fields 13-19), sign the agreement, indicating your acceptance and commitment to the partnership terms.
