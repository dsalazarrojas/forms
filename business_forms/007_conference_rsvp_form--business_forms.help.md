# Conference RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for attendees to RSVP (Respond to the invitation to the conference) and provide necessary information for the organizers to prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

- To complete this form, please select the appropriate option for each field, and fill in any required text fields.
- Make sure to read the hints for each field for a clear understanding of what we need from you.
- Once you have filled out all required fields, click the submit button to send your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name, as you would like to be addressed at the conference.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Provide your email address where we will send you confirmation and important details about the event.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, please provide a contact number for us to reach out to you.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required): Enter your company or institution name to help us tailor our communication and event experience to your needs.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to share your job title for networking purposes, please enter it here.
* **Will you attend the conference** (`rsvp_status`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you can attend the conference, and we'll take care of the rest.
* **Which days will you attend** (`attendance_days`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the days you plan to attend, and we'll make sure to include you in all relevant activities.
* **Which tracks interest you** (`session_tracks`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select areas that interest you, and we'll tailor our content to your preferences.
* **Meal Preference** (`meal_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose your meal preference for catering purposes, so we can accommodate your dietary needs.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please specify them here.
* **Will you attend networking events** (`networking_events`, `select_one`, required): Let us know if you plan to attend networking events, so we can include you in our social activities.
* **Do you need accommodation assistance** (`accommodation_needed`, `select_one`, required): If you need help with booking or recommendations, please let us know.
* **Do you need transportation information** (`transportation_needed`, `select_one`, optional): If you need assistance with airport transfers or local transportation, please let us know.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_requirements`, `text`, optional): If you have any accessibility needs, please specify them here.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`tshirt_size`, `select_one`, optional): If you'd like to receive a conference swag bag, please choose your t-shirt size.
* **May we contact you about future events** (`marketing_consent`, `select_one`, required): Let us know if you'd like to be contacted about future events, and we'll keep you informed about new opportunities.
* **How did you hear about this conference** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, optional): Help us improve our outreach by telling us how you heard about this event.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any questions or special requests, please use this field to express them.
* **I understand the RSVP deadline** (`rsvp_deadline_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Confirm you are aware of the RSVP deadline, so we can finalize our arrangements.

## Tips

- Double-check all required fields before submitting your form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- We'll keep your information private and secure, as per our privacy policy.
