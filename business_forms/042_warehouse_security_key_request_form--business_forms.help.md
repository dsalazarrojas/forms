# Warehouse Security Key Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Security Key Request Form is used to request access to a warehouse key, which grants users access to a specific area within the warehouse. This form helps to ensure that access is only granted to authorized personnel and that requests are properly reviewed and approved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required information, marked with an asterisk (*) in the label.
2. Select the correct access type from the options provided in the "Access Type" field.
3. Choose the approver from the options provided in the "Approver" field.
4. Select the relevant policy acknowledgment from the options provided in the "Policy Acknowledgment" field.
5. Sign the "Requester Signature" field, which is a note field that allows users to provide their signature.
6. Ensure that all fields are filled in and that the "Requester Signature" field is properly signed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester** (1, optional): Enter your employee number.
* **User Name** (2, text): Enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or in our HR system.
* **Key Description** (3, text): Describe the purpose and use of the requested key.
* **Access Type** (4, select_one): Select the type of access you need for the key. This will determine who can approve your request.
* **Approval Date** (5, date): Enter the date you would like your request to be approved by.
* **Approver** (6, select_one): Select the person who will approve your request.
* **Policy Acknowledgment** (7, select_multiple): Select the policies that apply to your request.
* **Requester Signature** (8, note): Sign your name, indicating that you are submitting this request. 

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, contact your supervisor or our help desk. 

Note: The form fields will only be displayed when the corresponding page is navigated to. The "pages" field in the YAML defines the structure of the form, and each field will be shown on its designated page.
