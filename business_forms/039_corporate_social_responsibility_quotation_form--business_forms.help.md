# Corporate Social Responsibility Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiative, which is a critical component of a company's commitment to social and environmental responsibility. The CSR initiative is a program aimed at creating a positive social impact, and this form helps in gathering details about the quotation for CSR-related services or products.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the company that is submitting the CSR quotation request.
2. Fill in the address of the billing contact person or entity.
3. Provide the name of the project manager responsible for the CSR initiative.
4. Clearly describe the CSR initiative or project.
5. Select a date for the quotation, which represents when the CSR initiative is expected to take place.
6. Enter the quotation value, which is the cost of the CSR initiative or service.
7. Specify the currency of the quotation value (e.g., USD, EUR, etc.).
8. Enter the name of the vendor who will be providing the CSR services or products.
9. Fill in the address of the vendor's contact person or entity.
10. Provide the contact information of the vendor's representative (phone, email, or fax).
11. If necessary, include any additional notes about the CSR initiative or quotation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, **text**, optional): Enter the name of the company submitting the CSR quotation request.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, **text**, optional): Enter the address where the CSR initiative will be billed or invoiced.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, **text**, optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the CSR initiative.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, **text**, optional): Enter the name of the project manager responsible for the CSR initiative.
* **CSR Initiative** (`csr_initiative`, **text**, optional): Clearly describe the CSR initiative or project.
* **Quotation Date** (`quotation_date`, **date**, optional): Select a date for the quotation, which represents when the CSR initiative is expected to take place.
* **Quote Value** (`quote_value`, **number**, optional): Enter the quotation value, which is the cost of the CSR initiative or service.
* **Quote Currency** (`quote_currency`, **text**, optional): Specify the currency of the quotation value (e.g., USD, EUR, etc.).
* **Vendor** (`vendor`, **text**, optional): Enter the name of the vendor who will be providing the CSR services or products.
* **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, **text**, optional): Enter the address of the vendor's contact person or entity.
* **Vendor Contact** (`vendor_contact`, **text**, optional): Provide the contact information of the vendor's representative (phone, email, or fax).
* **Notes** (`notes`, **note**, optional): If necessary, include any additional notes about the CSR initiative or quotation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, **text**, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person responsible for the CSR initiative.
* **Email** (`email`, **email**, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person or representative.
* **Fax** (`fax`, **text**, optional): Enter the fax number of the contact person responsible for the CSR initiative.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you need to add any additional information not covered in this form, include it in the "Notes" field.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all necessary details are included.
