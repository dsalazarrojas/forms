# Tattoo Consent Form Dtp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tattoo Consent Form Dtp is designed to collect client consent and relevant information for tattooing services. This form helps ensure that both the client and the tattoo artist are on the same page regarding the tattoo process, aftercare, and any allergies or medical conditions that may affect the tattoo.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2.  Enter your name in the "Tattoo Consent Form Dtp" field.
3.  In the "Client Tattoo Consent" field, provide a written statement of your consent for the tattooing process.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
5.  Click on the "Submit" button to finish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Tattoo Consent Form Dtp (1)**: This field is a text box where you can enter your name.
    *   Please type your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
*   **Client Tattoo Consent (2)**: This field is for you to provide a written statement of your consent for the tattooing process.
    *   Please include any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns you may have that could affect the tattoo process.
    *   Be specific and clear about your consent for the chosen tattoo design, placement, and aftercare instructions.
*   **Client Phone (3)**: This field is for you to provide your phone number.
    *   Please enter a valid phone number where you can be reached for any updates or concerns after the tattooing process.

## Tips
