# Distribution Cost Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distribution Cost Adjustment Request Form is designed to facilitate adjustments to the cost of distribution of goods to customers. This form gathers essential information about the request, including general information, customer details, order details, adjustment type, unit cost, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the General Information page**: Provide any relevant background information regarding the request.
2. **Enter Customer information**: Enter the relevant details about the customer.
3. **Provide Order details**: Enter the details about the order in question.
4. **Select the adjustment type**: Choose the type of adjustment being requested.
5. **Enter unit cost and total cost**: If applicable, enter the unit cost and total cost of the adjustment.
6. **Provide justification**: Explain why the adjustment is being requested.
7. **Attach a document**: Attach any relevant document to support the request.
8. **Select the status**: Choose the status of the request.
9. **Add comments**: Enter any additional comments or information.
10. **Enter the date submitted and date adjusted (if applicable)**: Enter the date the request was submitted and the date it was adjusted (if applicable).
11. **Enter the submitted and adjusted by information**: Enter the names of the users who submitted and adjusted the request.
12. **Add comments and notes from the customer and distribution team**: Enter any comments or notes from the customer and distribution team.
13. **Enter the approved and completed information (if applicable)**: Enter the information regarding the approval and completion of the request.
