# Value-Based Licensing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Value-Based Licensing Application Form is used to collect information from end users to process their value-based licensing requests. This form helps us to understand the specifics of the request and provide accurate licensing solutions. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields. The required fields are indicated by a red asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Provide your address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure that your email address is valid.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
7. Provide your time of birth in the "Time of Birth" field.
8. Select the type of license you are applying for in the "License Type" field.
9. Enter any additional value-based information in the "Value" field.
10. If you have any additional comments or notes, please enter them in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of your value-based licensing application.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This is where you can provide your contact information, including your name and any additional details that would help us process your request.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address for contact and delivery purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please ensure that this is a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth in the HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **License Type** (`license_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of license you are applying for from the provided options.
* **Value** (`value`, text, optional): Enter any additional value-based information that would help us process your request.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes that would be helpful in processing your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will review your application carefully and respond to you soon after receiving it.
