# fleet_vehicle_requisition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a fleet vehicle requisition, which is a request for a vehicle to be added to a company's fleet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide the contact person's first name in the "Contact First Name" field.
3. Enter the contact person's last name in the "Contact Last Name" field.
4. Enter the contact person's email address in the "Contact Email" field.
5. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. Select the type of vehicle required in the "Vehicle Type" field.
7. Choose the year of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Year" field.
8. Enter the make of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Make" field.
9. Enter the model of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Model" field.
10. Enter the color of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Color" field.
11. Enter the purpose of the request in the "Purpose of Request" field.
12. Enter the department responsible for the vehicle in the "Department" field.
13. Enter the team leader's name in the "Team Leader" field.
14. Enter the manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
15. Enter the manager's email address in the "Manager Email" field.
16. Enter the manager's phone number in the "Manager Phone" field.
17. Enter the department's budget in the "Department Budget" field.
18. Choose whether the vehicle is approved or not in the "Approved By" field.
19. Enter any additional remarks in the "Remarks" field.
20. Enter the date and time of submission in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
21. Enter any additional information in the "Date Range" field.
22. Provide any comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
23. Choose whether the form has been submitted or not in the "Submitted By" field.
24. Choose whether the form has been submitted or not in the "Submitted On" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company.
* **Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Vehicle Type**: Choose the type of vehicle required (Sedan, SUV, Truck).
* **Vehicle Year**: Choose the year of the vehicle (2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022).
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color**: Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Purpose of Request**: Enter the purpose of the request.
* **Department**: Enter the department responsible for the vehicle.
* **Team Leader**: Enter the team leader's name.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the manager's name.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Department Budget**: Enter the department's budget.
* **Approved By**: Choose whether the vehicle is approved or not.
* **Remarks**: Enter any additional remarks.
* **Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Time**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Date Range**: Enter any additional information about the date range.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or notes.
* **Submitted By**: Choose whether the form has been submitted or not.
* **Submitted On**: Choose whether the form has been submitted or not.
