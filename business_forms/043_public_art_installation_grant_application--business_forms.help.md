# Public Art Installation Grant Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Art Installation Grant Application is a form designed to gather information from artists and applicants for the evaluation of their public art installation grant applications. It is a crucial part of the application process, and its completion will help ensure that your application is reviewed fairly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Artist Details" section.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of your art proposal in the "Art Proposal" section.
3. Include detailed information about your project, such as location, duration, and expected outcomes, in the "Project Details" section.
4. Specify the budget breakdown and estimated costs for your project in the "Budget Details" section.
5. Upload supporting documents or materials related to your project in the "File Uploader" section.
6. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist Details** (`Artist Details`, text, required): This is where you'll enter your name and contact information. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Art Proposal** (`Art Proposal`, text, required): In this section, please provide a clear and concise description of your art proposal, including your vision, goals, and expected outcomes.
* **Project Details** (`Project Details`, text, required): Provide detailed information about your project, such as location, duration, and expected outcomes. Be specific about what you plan to achieve and how you plan to accomplish it.
* **Budget Details** (`Budget Details`, text, required): Specify the budget breakdown and estimated costs for your project. This will help us understand how you plan to manage and finance your project.
* **File Uploader** (`File Uploader`, text, required): Upload any supporting documents or materials related to your project, such as images, videos, or PDFs.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number. This will help us get in touch with you if we need more information.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your application for review.
