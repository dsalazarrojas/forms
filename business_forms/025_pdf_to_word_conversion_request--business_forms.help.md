# pdf_to_word_conversion_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the conversion of PDF files to Word documents. The converted Word document can be used for editing, modification, or sharing of the PDF content.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, which include:
	* Client Details: Enter the details of the client or the party involved in the PDF.
	* Upload PDF: Upload the PDF file for conversion.
	* Conversion Preferences: Choose whether the PDF should be converted immediately or within a certain timeframe.
	* Turnaround Time: Select the desired turnaround time for the conversion.
	* File Details: Enter any additional information about the file for reference.
	* Form Submission: This field is not required, but it can be used to provide any additional comments or notes about the submission.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`Client Details`, `text`, required): Enter the details of the client or party involved in the PDF, such as their name, contact information, or other relevant details.
* **Upload PDF** (`Upload PDF`, `text`, required): Upload the PDF file that needs to be converted to Word.
* **Conversion Preferences** (`Conversion Preferences`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether the PDF should be converted immediately (`Yes`) or within a specified timeframe (`No`).
* **Turnaround Time** (`Turnaround Time`, `select_one`, required): Select the desired timeframe for the conversion. This can be immediate, 1 day, 2 days, or 3 days.
* **File Details** (`File Details`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information about the file, such as its purpose or intended use.
* **Form Submission** (`Form Submission`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the submission, if needed.
* **Form Submission Notes** (`Form Submission Notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the submission, if needed. 

## Tips
* Make sure to upload the correct PDF file.
* Double-check the conversion preferences and turnaround time to ensure they meet your needs.
* Enter any additional information in the File Details field for reference.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the support team.
