# Support Resources Finder - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Support Resources Finder form is designed to guide customers through a series of steps to provide the necessary information for support. This form is meant to be completed by customers experiencing problems and is divided into sections to help you find the support resources you need.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of support you need: Choose from the options (Technical, Personal, or Financial) that best describes your issue.
2. Provide your contact information: Enter your email address or other relevant contact details.
3. Describe your issue in a few words: Enter a brief description of your problem.
4. Choose how you'd like to be contacted: Select the method you prefer to communicate with you (Email, Phone, or In-person).
5. Confirm your language preference: Select your language preference.
6. Confirm your role: Select your role or position.
7. Provide your organization name (if applicable): Enter the name of your organization.
8. Provide a detailed description of your issue: Enter a thorough description of your problem.
9. Confirm if you've tried any previous solutions: Select if you've tried any previous solutions.
10. Confirm if you'd like to proceed with support: Select whether you'd like to move forward with support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Page** (`select_page`, `select_one`, Technical, Personal, Financial): This field asks you to select the type of support you need. Choose the option that best describes your issue.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info_page`, `email`): This field is where you enter your email address or other relevant contact details.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description_page`, `text`): This field asks you to describe your issue in a few words. Try to be as brief as possible.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method_page`, `select_one`, Email, Phone, In-person): This field asks you to select the method you prefer to communicate with you.
* **Language** (`language_page`, `text`): This field is where you confirm your language preference.
* **Role** (`role_page`, `text`): This field asks you to confirm your role or position.
* **Organization Name** (`org_name_page`, `text`): This field is where you provide the name of your organization (if applicable).
* **Issue Description Long** (`issue_description_long_page`, `text`): This field asks you to provide a detailed description of your issue.
* **Tried Before** (`tried_before_page`, `checkbox`, Option A, Option B): This field asks you to confirm if you've tried any previous solutions.
* **Proceed with Support** (`proceed_page`, `select_one`, Yes, No): This field asks you to confirm if you'd like to proceed with support.
