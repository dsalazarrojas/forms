# welcome_to_our_open_house - Help Guide
## Purpose
The welcome_to_our_open_house form is designed to gather contact information from attendees of an open house event hosted by a business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the form fields with your relevant information.
3. Review your submission to ensure it's accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and/or company name.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your business email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, number, optional): Enter your business phone number.
* **address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your business address.
* **message** (`message`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or questions you have.
* **contact_time** (`contact_time`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time of your desired contact.
* **contact_date** (`contact_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of your desired contact.
* **business_type** (`business_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of business you are (Retail, Wholesale, Other).
* **message** (`message`, note, optional): Repeated field for any additional comments or questions.
* **contact_method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select the method(s) of contact you prefer (Phone Call, Email, Meeting).

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields, if any, are completed.
* Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers directly.
