# Department Purchase Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Department Purchase Request Form is a tool for departments to communicate their purchasing needs to the finance team. This form captures essential information about the purchases required, ensuring that the finance team is informed and can process the requests accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the relevant department from the form.
2. Enter the reason for the purchase, including any relevant details.
3. Enter the name of the store where the purchase will be made.
4. Enter the date of the purchase.
5. Enter the time of the purchase.
6. Enter the amount of the purchase.
7. Enter a list of items bought.
8. Enter the total cost of the purchase.
9. Enter the name of the contact person.
10. Enter the contact person's email.
11. Enter the contact person's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the name of the department making the purchase request.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for the purchase, including any relevant details.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the store where the purchase will be made.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the purchase.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the purchase.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of the purchase.
* **Items Bought** (`items_bought`, text, optional): Enter a list of items bought.
* **Cost** (`cost`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the purchase.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understood the company's purchasing policies and procedures before filling out this form.
* This form is for official business purposes only; personal purchases are not allowed.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
