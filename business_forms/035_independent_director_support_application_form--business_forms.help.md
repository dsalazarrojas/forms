# Independent Director Support Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential Independent Directors for support application purposes. It's intended for individuals looking to join the industry or seeking support from an internal team. The form helps us understand your background, experience, and motivations for joining the industry.

**Please ensure you're filling out this form for the first time. Repeated submissions will be considered duplicate applications.**

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Job Title.
3. Provide your Email Address.
4. Add your Phone Number.
5. Share your Independent Director Experience.
6. Enter your Company Name.
7. Specify your Position in the company.
8. Explain Why You'd Like to Join the Industry.
9. Share How You Heard About Us.
10. Attach your Resume (if applicable).
11. Add any Additional Notes.
12. Finally, click the Submit button to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name to help us better understand who you are.
* **Job Title** (Text): Briefly describe your current job title or role.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your valid email address for future communication.
* **Phone** (Text): Provide your phone number for easy contact.
* **Independent Director Experience** (Number): Share your experience as an Independent Director, if applicable.
* **Company Name** (Text): Enter your current or past company name.
* **Position in Company** (Text): Specify your position in the company, e.g., CEO, Director, etc.
* **Why Join Industry** (Text): Explain why you're interested in joining the industry or seeking support.
* **How did you hear about us?** (Text): Share the source of your interest in our organization.
* **Resume** (Text): Attach your resume if you'd like to showcase your experience and qualifications.
* **Portfolio** (Text): Attach any relevant portfolio links or files, if applicable.
* **Additional Notes** (Note): Leave any additional comments or details about your application.
* **Submit** (Date): Finalize your application by clicking this button.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out correctly to avoid incomplete applications.
* Attach relevant documents like resumes and portfolios to showcase your qualifications.
* Review your application carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
