# Warehouse Carbon Neutrality Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for businesses to apply to the Warehouse Carbon Neutrality Program, aimed at evaluating and reducing their carbon footprint. To participate in the program, you must fill out this form carefully and honestly to ensure an accurate assessment of your business's carbon neutrality status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form's sections to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in your company information (Section 2: Company Info).
3. Identify your contact person responsible for this application (Section 3: Contact Person).
4. Provide details about your warehouse, including its address (Section 4: Address).
5. Indicate whether your business is part of any existing carbon neutrality programs (Section 6: Carbon Neutrality Programs).
6. If applicable, provide your business's current carbon footprint and reduction targets (Sections 7: Carbon Footprint and 8: Carbon Reduction Target).
7. Describe your methods for reducing carbon emissions (Section 9: Reduction Methods).
8. If you have already achieved any reductions, specify the amount and unit of reduction (Section 10: Reduction Amount and 11: Reduction Unit).
9. Calculate and report your baseline, projected, and actual carbon emissions (Sections 12: Baseline Emissions, 13: Projected Emissions, and 14: Actual Emissions).
10. If applicable, provide information on carbon credits (Section 15: Carbon Credit).
11. Specify how often you plan to report your carbon emissions (Section 16: Reporting Frequency).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Info (Section 2)**: This is your company's basic information, including its name and any relevant details.
* **Contact Person (Section 3)**: Please provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for this application.
* **Address (Section 4)**: Enter your warehouse's address to help us locate and assess your operations.
* **Carbon Neutrality Programs (Section 6)**: Indicate if your business already participates in any existing carbon neutrality programs. This is important for us to understand your current status and avoid duplication of efforts.
* **Carbon Footprint (Section 7)**: If applicable, report your current or projected carbon emissions. This helps us understand your business's current and expected impact on the environment.
* **Reduction Methods (Section 9)**: Describe any methods your business is using or plans to use for reducing carbon emissions.
* **Reduction Amount (Section 10)**: If you have achieved any reductions, provide the amount in the format specified in the Reduction Unit field.
* **Reduction Unit (Section 11)**: Specify the unit of the reduction amount (e.g., tonnes CO2).
* **Baseline Emissions (Section 12)**: Report your baseline carbon emissions to serve as a reference for future comparisons.
* **Projected Emissions (Section 13)**: Provide projections of your future carbon emissions.
* **Actual Emissions (Section 14)**: Report any actual emissions that have been recorded.
* **Carbon Credit (Section 15)**: If applicable, provide details on any carbon credits you may have earned or purchased.
* **Carbon Credit Method (Section 16)**: Describe the method used to acquire your carbon credits.
* **Reporting Frequency (Section 17)**: Indicate how often you plan to report your carbon emissions, which could be annually or quarterly.
