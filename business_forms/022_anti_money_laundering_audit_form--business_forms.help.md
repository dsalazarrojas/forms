# Anti Money Laundering Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by auditors to record and report Anti-Money Laundering (AML) audit results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name as the lead auditor for this review.
2. Select the start and end dates of the audit period.
3. Assess the overall AML risk level.
4. Summarize the major issues found during the audit.
5. Provide recommended actions to address those issues.
6. (Optional) Check if the compliance officer has acknowledged the recommendations.
7. (Optional) Enter a follow-up deadline for completion of actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Auditor name (`auditor_name`, text, required)**: Enter your name as the lead auditor for this review.
* **Audit period start date (`audit_period_start`, date, required)**: Select the start date of the audit period.
* **Audit period end date (`audit_period_end`, date, required)**: Select the end date of the audit period.
* **Risk level assessed (`risk_level_assessed`, select one, required)**: Assess the overall Anti-Money Laundering risk level as Low, Moderate, High, or Critical.
* **Summary of key findings (`findings_summary`, text, required)**: Briefly describe the major issues found during the audit.
* **Recommended corrective actions (`recommended_actions`, text, required)**: Provide action items to remediate the identified gaps.
* **Compliance officer acknowledgement (`compliance_officer_acknowledgement`, select one, optional)**: If the compliance officer has acknowledged the recommendations, select "Acknowledged". Otherwise, select "Not acknowledged".
* **Follow up deadline (`follow_up_deadline`, date, optional)**: Enter a date by which the recommended actions should be completed, if applicable.
