# Talent Payment Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to submit payments for talents. The form gathers information on whether a payment is made, the talent's details, the amount of the payment, and any additional notes. This is a critical form for internal use, so ensure that only valid payment methods and accurate talent information are submitted.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information to complete the form.
2. Select the payment method for this submission (yes/no).
3. Enter the name of the talent being paid.
4. Enter the amount of the payment.
5. If desired, enter the email of the talent (if not required).
6. If desired, enter the phone number of the talent (if not required).
7. If desired, add any additional notes about this submission.
8. Click "Submit" to submit the form and complete the process.
9. If you need to go back or make changes, click "Back" to return to the previous page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Method (payment_method)** (`select_one`, required): Select how the payment was made (yes/no).
* **Talent (talent)** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the talent being paid.
* **Amount (amount)** (`number`, required): Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, optional): If you have the email of the talent, enter it here.
* **Phone (phone)** (`text`, optional): If you have the phone number of the talent, enter it here.
* **Note (note)** (`note`, optional): If you have any additional information to add, enter it here.
* **Submit (submit)** (`text`, optional): Click to submit the form and complete the process.
* **Back (back)** (`text`, optional): Click to return to the previous page, or if you need to make changes.
