# Office Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Office Check In Form is designed for employees to check in when they arrive at the office, providing essential information to ensure a smooth and organized work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Office Check In Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Check In" field, which is the first page of the form. This field is used to confirm your arrival at the office.
2. Next, enter your date of arrival in the "Date" field. This will help the team keep track of your check-in records.
3. Select your expected time of arrival from the "Time" field. This will allow colleagues to schedule meetings and tasks accordingly.
4. Provide your name in the "Name" field. This is an important identifying detail for communication and record-keeping purposes.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone" field. This will enable easy communication with the team and colleagues.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field. This is an optional field for providing extra context or details about your work.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will allow colleagues to reach out to you for work-related matters.
8. Specify the office location where you will be working in the "Office Location" field. This helps in organizing work activities and resources.
9. Finally, select your expected departure time from the "Expected Departure Time" field. This will help in planning meetings and tasks accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Check In** (`office_check_in_form_1`, text, required): Confirm your arrival at the office by filling out this field.
- **Date** (`office_check_in_form_2`, date, required): Enter the date of your arrival.
- **Time** (`office_check_in_form_3`, time, required): Select your expected time of arrival.
- **Name** (`office_check_in_form_4`, text, required): Enter your name for identification and record-keeping purposes.
- **Phone** (`office_check_in_form_5`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number for easy communication.
- **Note** (`office_check_in_form_6`, note, optional): Provide additional notes or comments about your work (if needed).
- **Email** (`office_check_in_form_7`, email, optional): Enter your email address for work-related communication.
- **Office Location** (`office_check_in_form_8`, text, optional): Specify the office location where you will be working.
- **Expected Departure Time** (`office_check_in_form_9`, time, optional): Select your expected departure time to plan meetings and tasks.
