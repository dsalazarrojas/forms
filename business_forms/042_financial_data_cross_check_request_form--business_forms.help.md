# Financial Data Cross Check Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request financial data cross checks, which involves verifying financial data for accuracy and completeness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Business Form ID and Business Form Name fields with the relevant information.
2. Provide any additional comments or explanations about the financial data cross check request in the Comments field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field so that we can contact you with the results of the cross check.
4. Optionally, add any additional comments or explanations about the business form in the Business Form Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Form ID** (`business_form_id`): Enter the ID number of the business form for which you are requesting a financial data cross check.
* **Business Form Name** (`business_form_name`): Enter the name of the business form, for example, "Financial Reports" or "Budget Review".
* **Comments** (`financial_data_cross_check_request_form_comments`): Add any additional comments or explanations about the financial data cross check request.
* **Business Form Comments** (`business_form_comments`): Add any additional comments or explanations about the business form, for example, background information or context.
* **Email** (`financial_data_cross_check_request_form_email`): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with the results of the cross check.
