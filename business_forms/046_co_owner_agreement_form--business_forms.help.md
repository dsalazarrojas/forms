# Co-Owner Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Co-Owner Agreement Form is a critical document for any business partnership, as it defines the relationship between co-owners and outlines their rights, responsibilities, and obligations. This form helps to prevent disputes, ensure clarity, and promote a smooth partnership experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Co-Owner Agreement Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the names of the Co-Owners 1 and 2.
2. Enter the name of the business entity.
3. Define the ownership structure and the percentages of ownership for each co-owner.
4. Describe the roles and responsibilities of each co-owner.
5. Decide on the dispute resolution method (Mediation, Arbitration, or Litigation).
6. Sign and date the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Co-Owner 1 Full Name**: Enter the full name of Co-Owner 1.
* **Co-Owner 2 Full Name**: Enter the full name of Co-Owner 2.
* **Business Entity Name**: Enter the name of the business entity being established.
* **Ownership Structure**: Describe the ownership structure of the partnership, such as sole ownership or joint ownership.
* **Ownership Percentage - Co-Owner 1**: Enter the percentage of ownership for Co-Owner 1.
* **Ownership Percentage - Co-Owner 2**: Enter the percentage of ownership for Co-Owner 2.
* **Initial Capital Contribution - Co-Owner 1**: Enter the initial capital contribution of Co-Owner 1 in USD.
* **Initial Capital Contribution - Co-Owner 2**: Enter the initial capital contribution of Co-Owner 2 in USD.
* **Primary Role and Responsibilities of Co-Owner 1**: Describe the primary role and responsibilities of Co-Owner 1.
* **Primary Role and Responsibilities of Co-Owner 2**: Describe the primary role and responsibilities of Co-Owner 2.
* **Description of Voting Rights and Decision-Making Authority**: Describe how voting rights and decision-making authority will be handled in the partnership.
* **Do you agree to the standard Buy-Sell Provision?**: Choose "True" to agree to the standard buy-sell provision or "False" to decline.
* **Preferred Dispute Resolution Method**: Choose the preferred dispute resolution method (Mediation, Arbitration, or Litigation).
* **Signature of Co-Owner 1**: Sign and date the agreement as Co-Owner 1.
* **Signature of Co-Owner 2**: Sign and date the agreement as Co-Owner 2.
* **Date of Agreement**: Date the agreement when both co-owners have signed and agreed on the terms.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML fields and assumes that the business logic is straightforward. If you are unsure about any aspect of this form, please consult a legal expert for guidance.
