# Club Treasurer Award Nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Treasurer Award Nomination form is designed to collect information for the Club Treasurer Award. The award recognizes and acknowledges the treasurer's dedication, skills, and accomplishments in managing the club's finances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please ensure you provide accurate and valid information in all required fields. This includes:

* Nomination date
* Nominator full name
* Nominator email
* Nominator position
* Treasurer name being nominated
* Treasurer email address
* Club name
* Length of treasurer tenure
* Budget management
* Financial record accuracy
* Transparency and communication
* Primary reason for nomination
* Specific examples or achievements

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, required): The date when the nomination is made.
* **Nominator Full Name** (`nominator_name`, text, required): The person making the nomination.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, required): Contact information for the nominator.
* **Nominator Position** (`nominator_position`, text, required): The role or position the nominator holds in the club.
* **Treasurer Name Being Nominated** (`nominee_name`, text, required): The name of the person being nominated for the award.
* **Treasurer Email Address** (`nominee_email`, email, required): The email address of the person being nominated.
* **Club Name** (`club_name`, text, required): The name of the club.
* **Length of Treasurer Tenure** (`tenure_length`, text, required): The duration the person has served as treasurer.
* **Budget Management Skills** (`budget_management`, select_one, required): A rating of the person's budget management skills, with options ranging from "Poor" to "Excellent".
* **Financial Record Accuracy** (`financial_accuracy`, select_one, required): A rating of the person's financial record accuracy, with options ranging from "Poor" to "Excellent".
* **Transparency and Communication** (`transparency_communication`, select_one, required): A rating of the person's financial communication skills, with options ranging from "Poor" to "Excellent".
* **Problem-Solving Ability** (`problem_solving`, select_one, optional): An optional rating of the person's problem-solving skills.
* **Innovation and Process Improvement** (`innovation_improvement`, select_one, optional): An optional rating of the person's innovation and process improvement skills.
* **Primary Reason for Nomination** (`nomination_reason`, text, required): A text field to state the primary reason for the nomination.
* **Specific Examples or Achievements** (`specific_examples`, text, required): A text field to provide specific examples or achievements of the nominee.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_one, optional): An optional select-one field to indicate if any supporting documents are attached.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields have valid and accurate information.
* Provide specific examples or achievements to support the nomination.
* You may provide supporting documents if applicable.
* If optional fields are not filled out, they will not impact the evaluation of the nomination.
