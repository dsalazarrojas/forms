# Audit Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for creating an audit report, which is a document that summarizes the findings and results of an audit conducted on a company or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the basic information:
	* **Report Title**: Enter a brief title of the audit report.
	* **Report Number**: Enter the unique reference number of the audit report.
	* **Report Date**: Enter the date of the report issuance.
2. Provide details about the report:
	* **Preparer's Name**: Enter the name of the person who prepared the report.
	* **Preparer's Title**: Enter the title of the person who prepared the report.
	* **Audited Entity**: Enter the name of the entity or department audited.
3. Specify the audit details:
	* **Audit Period Start**: Enter the start date of the audit period.
	* **Audit Period End**: Enter the end date of the audit period.
	* **Audit Type**: Select the type of audit conducted (e.g., Financial, Internal, Compliance, IT, Operational, Performance, Investigative).
4. Summarize the findings and recommendations:
	* **Main Objectives**: Briefly describe the main objectives of the audit.
	* **Audit Scope**: Describe the scope of the audit.
	* **Audit Methodology**: Describe the methodology used in the audit.
	* **Executive Summary**: Provide a brief executive summary of the audit report.
5. Evaluate the results:
	* **Overall Opinion**: Select the overall opinion about the audit (e.g., Unqualified, Qualified, Adverse, Disclaimer of Opinion).
	* **Key Findings**: Summarize the key findings of the audit.
	* **Findings Count**: Enter the total number of findings.
	* **High Risk Findings**: Enter the number of high-risk findings.
	* **Recommendations Summary**: Summarize the recommendations.
6. Provide additional information:
	* **Management Response**: Enter a summary of the management response to the audit findings.
	* **Action Plan Deadline**: Enter the deadline for management action plan.
7. Determine the distribution and classification:
	* **Distribution List**: Select who will receive this report.
	* **Report Classification**: Select the classification of this report (e.g., Public, Internal Use Only, Confidential, Restricted).
8. Finalize the report:
	* **Approved By**: Enter the name of the report approver.
	* **Approval Signature**: Enter your full name as electronic signature.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled before submitting the form. If a field is marked as "optional", it can be left blank or filled in at your discretion.
