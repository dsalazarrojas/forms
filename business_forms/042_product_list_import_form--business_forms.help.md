# Product List Import Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product List Import Form is a data entry tool used to collect and store product information into the business's product management system. This form helps ensure accurate and up-to-date product data is stored correctly, enabling efficient product management and sales operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Category ID" field and enter the corresponding category code for the product.
2. Enter the "Product Code" and "Product Name" fields with the product's unique code and name.
3. Enter the "Quantity" field with the available quantity of the product.
4. Enter the "Unit Price" field with the product's price in the selected currency.
5. Select the "Currency" field with the applicable currency for the product (e.g., USD, EUR, JPY, CNY, INR).
6. In the "Product Description" field, enter a brief description of the product.
7. Enter the "Product Image URL" field with the URL of the product's image.
8. Select the "Product Status" field with the current status of the product (active, inactive, archived).
9. In the "Product Tags" field, select any additional tags that apply to the product.
10. In the "Product Category" field, select the primary category for the product.
11. In the "Product Subcategory" field, select any subcategory that applies to the product.
12. In the "Description 2" field, enter any additional product description.
13. In the "Image 2" field, enter the URL of the product's secondary image.
14. In the "Image 3" and "Image 4" fields, enter the URLs of the product's additional images.
