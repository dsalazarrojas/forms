# Inventory Management Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about an item in your inventory, including its description, status, and associated data such as location, date, and price.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below for each field:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is inventory?** (`title`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the item in your inventory.
* **Item 1** (`inventory_management_quiz_item_1`, `select_one`, required): Select the item you are describing from the available options.
* **Description** (`inventory_management_quiz_item_1_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a detailed description of the item.
* **Quantity** (`inventory_management_quiz_item_1_quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity of the item in stock.
* **Location** (`inventory_management_quiz_item_1_location`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant locations where the item is stored or located.
* **Status** (`inventory_management_quiz_item_1_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the item, e.g., active, inactive, or pending.
* **Notes** (`inventory_management_quiz_item_1_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the item.
* **Date** (`inventory_management_quiz_item_1_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the item was received or updated.
* **Time** (`inventory_management_quiz_item_1_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the item was received or updated.
* **Price** (`inventory_management_quiz_item_1_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the price of the item.
* **Email** (`inventory_management_quiz_item_1_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the contact email of the person related to the item.
* **Phone** (`inventory_management_quiz_item_1_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the person related to the item.
