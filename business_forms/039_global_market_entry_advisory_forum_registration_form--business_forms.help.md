# Global Market Entry Advisory Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants in the Global Market Entry Advisory Forum. The form collects information from participants about their interests, preferences, and submission acknowledgments.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your participant information, including your name and email address.
2.  Select your preferences for participation in the forum.
3.  Indicate your interests in the specific topics provided.
4.  Add any additional comments or information you have.
5.  Confirm that you have acknowledged your submission.
6.  Preview your submission before finalizing your registration.
7.  Submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Information** (Participant Information, required): This field is used to collect your basic information, such as your name and email.
    Fill in your name and email address to ensure accurate registration.
*   **First Name** (First Name, required): This field is used to collect your first name.
    Enter your first name to ensure accurate registration.
*   **Email** (Email, required): This field is used to collect your email address.
    Enter your email address to ensure you can receive updates and notifications.
*   **Phone** (Phone, required): This field is used to collect your phone number.
    Enter your phone number for any necessary contact.
*   **Participant Preferences** (Participant Preferences, required): This field is used to determine your preferences, such as whether you will participate in the forum.
    Select your preferences to ensure your participation status is accurate.
*   **Topic Interest 1** (Topic Interest 1, required): This field is used to determine your interest in a specific topic.
    Select your interest in this topic to ensure accurate representation.
*   **Topic Interest 2** (Topic Interest 2, required): This field is used to determine your interest in another specific topic.
    Select your interest in this topic to ensure accurate representation.
*   **Topics of Interest** (Topics of Interest, required): This field is used to collect any additional topics of interest you may have.
    Enter any additional topics you are interested in to ensure accurate representation.
*   **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, optional): This field is used for any additional comments or information you may have.
    Use this field to provide any additional context or information.
*   **Submission Acknowledgement** (Submission Acknowledgement, required): This field is used to determine whether you have acknowledged your submission.
    Confirm that you have reviewed and acknowledged your submission.
*   **Submit and Participate** (Submit and Participate, required): This field is used to submit your registration.
    Click this button to finalize your registration and participate in the forum.
*   **Submit and Preview** (Submit and Preview, required): This field is used to preview your submission before finalizing your registration.
    Review your submission before finalizing your registration.
*   **Cancel** (Cancel, optional): This field is used to cancel your registration.
    Click this button to cancel your registration if necessary.
*   **Other** (Other, required): This field is used for any additional information you may want to provide.
    Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your registration.
