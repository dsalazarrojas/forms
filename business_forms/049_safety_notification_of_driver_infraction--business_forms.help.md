# Safety Notification Of Driver Infraction - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report safety concerns regarding driver infractions, including traffic accidents, reckless driving, and other driver-related offenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the driver infraction in the **Driver Infraction** field.
2. Provide the **Location** where the infraction occurred.
3. Enter the **Vehicle Registration Number** of the vehicle involved.
4. Enter the **Date of Event** when the infraction occurred.
5. Enter the **Time of Event** when the infraction occurred.
6. Provide a detailed description of the infraction in the **Details** field.
7. If applicable, enter the **Driver Name** of the driver involved.
8. Indicate whether the **Reporting Officer** is present by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
9. If necessary, enter the **Contact Number** of a contact person.
10. Enter the **Email** of a contact person.
11. If applicable, enter the **Vehicle Owner**.
12. Select the type of **Incident Type** that occurred.
13. Enter the date the infraction was **Date Reported**.
14. Enter the time the infraction was **Time Reported**.
15. Select the type of **Vehicle Type** involved.
16. Enter the **License Number** of the driver's license.
17. If applicable, enter the **Witness Name** and **Witness Contact**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Driver Infraction** (driver_infraction): Enter a brief description of the driver infraction.
* **Location** (location): Provide the location where the infraction occurred.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (vehicle_registration_number): Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Date of Event** (date_of_event): Enter the date when the infraction occurred.
* **Time of Event** (time_of_event): Enter the time when the infraction occurred.
* **Details** (details): Provide a detailed description of the infraction.
* **Driver Name** (driver_name): If applicable, enter the name of the driver involved.
* **Reporting Officer** (reporting_officer): Indicate whether the reporting officer is present by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number): If necessary, enter the contact number of a contact person.
* **Email** (email): Enter the email of a contact person.
* **Vehicle Owner** (vehicle_owner): If applicable, enter the vehicle owner's name or contact information.
* **Incident Type** (incident_type): Select the type of incident that occurred.
* **Date Reported** (date_reported): Enter the date the infraction was reported.
* **Time Reported** (time_reported): Enter the time the infraction was reported.
* **Vehicle Type** (vehicle_type): Select the type of vehicle involved.
* **License Number** (license_number): Enter the driver's license number.
* **Witness Name** (witness_name): If applicable, enter the name of a witness.
* **Witness Contact** (witness_contact): If applicable, enter the contact information of a witness.
