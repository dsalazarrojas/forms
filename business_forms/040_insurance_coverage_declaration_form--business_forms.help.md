# Insurance Coverage Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Coverage Declaration Form is a document that captures essential information about your insurance coverage. It helps ensure that all relevant details are recorded correctly, and it is crucial for insurance claims and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Coverage Details**: This section captures the basic details of your insurance coverage.
2. **Coverage Limits**: Enter the limits of your coverage, if applicable. If not applicable, please leave this field blank or fill it with "N/A" if required is true.
3. **Policy Details**: Enter the policy details for your coverage. This includes any relevant information about the policy.
4. **Coverage Exclusions**: Select all that apply from the list of provided options.
5. **Policy Type**: Select the type of policy that best describes your coverage.
6. **Coverage Start Date**: Enter the start date of your coverage.
7. **Coverage End Date**: Enter the end date of your coverage.
8. **Policy Effective Date**: Enter the effective date of your policy.
9. **Policy Status**: Select the status of your policy.
10. **Policy Number**: Enter the policy number for your coverage.
11. **Insured Name**: Enter the name of the insured person or entity.
12. **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of the insurance provider.
