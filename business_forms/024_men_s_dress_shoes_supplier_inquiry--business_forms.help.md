# Men's Dress Shoes Supplier Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from suppliers of men's dress shoes for business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the supplier's name in the "Supplier Name" field.
2. Enter the contact person's name in the "Contact Name" field.
3. Enter the supplier's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the supplier's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the style ID of the men's dress shoes you are inquiring about in the "Style ID" field.
6. Enter the product ID of the men's dress shoes you are inquiring about in the "Product ID" field.
7. Optionally, enter a brief description of the product in the "Description" field.
8. Enter the price of the men's dress shoes in the "Price" field.
9. Optionally, upload an image of the product in the "Upload File" field.
10. Enter the expected delivery date and time in the "Delivery Date" and "Delivery Time" fields.
11. Indicate whether or not the supplier offers delivery in the "Delivery Method" field.
12. Select the payment method from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field.
13. Optionally, enter any additional payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.
14. Enter the due date for payment in the "Due Date" field.
15. If applicable, enter any other relevant details about the product or supplier in the "Other Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the supplier you are inquiring about.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for the supplier.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the supplier's phone number.
* **Style ID** (`style_id`, number, required): Enter the style ID of the men's dress shoes you are inquiring about.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, text, required): Enter the product ID of the men's dress shoes you are inquiring about.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Optionally, enter a brief description of the product.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the men's dress shoes.
* **Image** (`image`, text, optional): Optionally, upload an image of the product.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the expected delivery date of the men's dress shoes.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Enter the expected delivery time of the men's dress shoes.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether or not the supplier offers delivery.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method from the options provided.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Optionally, enter any additional payment terms.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, optional): Enter the due date for payment.
* **Other Details** (`other_details`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any other relevant details about the product or supplier.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant business stakeholders.
