# crisis_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The crisis communication form is used for internal reporting and documentation of emergency situations within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required and optional fields.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.
3. Save the form before submitting it to your supervisor or emergency response team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Status** (`emergency_status`, text, required/optional): Describe the current emergency status, e.g., "On-site" or "Remote".
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and title of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Team Involved** (`team_involved`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all teams or departments that are involved in the emergency response.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the emergency response team.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select the method of contact, e.g., "Phone" or "Email".
* **Select One Option** (`select_one_option`, text, required/optional): Enter the specific contact method option, e.g., "Phone Number" or "Email Address".
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.

## Tips
* Double-check the form before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or emergency response team for guidance.
* Keep the form up-to-date and accurate to ensure timely and effective emergency response.
