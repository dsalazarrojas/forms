# Bulk Discount Eligibility Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us understand your business requirements to assess your eligibility for bulk discounts. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information to provide the best service.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please select "When submitting this inquiry" to choose the date you are inquiring about bulk discounts.
- Fill out the "Company Name", "Contact Person Name", "Contact Title", and "City" fields with accurate and up-to-date information about your business.
- Enter your "Email Address", "Phone Number", and "Company Address" in the respective fields.
- Choose the correct "Type of Business", "Years in Business", and "Approximate Annual Revenue" that best represents your business.
- Select the "Products Interested In" and "Specific Product Description" fields to provide detailed information about what you need.
- Fill out the "Estimated Annual Purchase Volume" and "Minimum Order Requirements" fields with accurate information about your purchasing habits.
- If applicable, select the "Frequency of Purchases", "Currently Working With Supplier", "Budget Range", "Preferred Payment Terms", and "Additional Information" fields to provide more details about your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Inquiry Date** (inquiry_date): This field is required to understand when you are inquiring about bulk discounts.
*   **Company Name** (company_name): Enter your business name for identification purposes.
*   **Contact Person Name** (contact_name): Provide your full name for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Title** (contact_title): Enter your job title for reference (optional).
*   **Email Address** (contact_email): Enter your primary contact email address.
*   **Phone Number** (contact_phone): Enter your business contact number.
*   **Company Address** (company_address): Enter your business address for delivery purposes.
*   **City** (company_city): Enter your city name for geographical reference.
*   **State or Province** (company_state): Enter your state or province for geographical reference.
*   **Zip or Postal Code** (company_zip): Enter your postal code for delivery purposes.
*   **Type of Business** (business_type): Choose the industry or sector of your business.
*   **Years in Business** (years_in_business): Choose how long your business has been established.
*   **Approximate Annual Revenue** (annual_revenue): Choose the approximate annual revenue of your business (optional).
*   **Products Interested In** (product_interest): Select all the products you are interested in purchasing from our catalog.
*   **Specific Product Description** (product_description): Provide details about the products you need.
*   **Estimated Annual Purchase Volume** (estimated_volume): Enter the approximate annual purchase volume of your business.
*   **Minimum Order Requirements** (minimum_order): Enter your minimum order size (optional).
*   **Frequency of Purchases** (purchasing_frequency): Choose how often you buy from our catalog (optional).
*   **Currently Working With Supplier** (current_supplier): Check if you are currently working with a supplier (optional).
*   **Budget Range** (budget_range): Choose your budget range for purchasing (optional).
*   **Preferred Payment Terms** (payment_terms): Choose your preferred payment terms (optional).
*   **Additional Information** (additional_info): Provide any other information that may help us better understand your business requirements.
