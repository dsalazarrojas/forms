# loan_pre_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a business for the purpose of a loan or financial assistance application. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information in this form to ensure a smooth and efficient review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your business name and type in the respective fields.
2.  Choose the correct loan purpose from the provided options.
3.  Briefly describe the purpose of your loan in the "Business Plan Summary" field.
4.  Enter your contact address in the "Contact Address" field.
5.  Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6.  Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field (if applicable).
7.  Enter your business registration number in the "Business Registration Number" field.
8.  Enter the loan amount and term in the respective fields.
9.  Choose the correct loan term from the provided options.
10.  Optionally, enter any additional contact notes in the "Contact Notes" field.
11.  If your business type does not appear in the list, choose "Other (please specify)" and provide further explanation in the "Business Type Other" field.
12.  Finally, review and submit the form to proceed with your loan application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (text, required): Enter your business name as it appears on your business registration documents.
* **Business Type** (select_one, required): Choose the correct type of your business from the provided options.
* **Loan Purpose** (text, required): Briefly describe the purpose of your loan.
* **Business Plan Summary** (text, required): Provide a brief summary of your business plan.
* **Contact Address** (text, required): Enter your business address.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter your business email address (if applicable).
* **Business Registration Number** (text, required): Enter your business registration number.
* **Loan Amount** (number, required): Enter the amount of money you are seeking.
* **Loan Term** (select_one, required): Choose the correct loan term from the provided options.
* **Contact Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional contact notes.
* **Business Type Other** (select_multiple, optional): Choose "Other (please specify)" and provide further explanation.
* **Assigned User** (select_one, optional): Choose the user to be assigned to this loan application.
* **Created By** (select_one, required): The system-generated field indicating who created this form.
* **Updated By** (select_one, optional): The user who last updated this form.
* **Deleted By** (select_one, optional): The user who last deleted this form.
* **Created Date** (date, required): The date this form was created.
* **Updated Date** (date, optional): The date this form was last updated.
* **Deleted Date** (date, optional): The date this form was last deleted.
* **Version** (text, required): Enter the version of this form.
* **Status** (select_one, required): Choose the status of your loan application (submitted, pending, approved, or rejected).
* **Status Note** (text, optional): Add any notes regarding your loan application status.
* **Submitted Date** (date, required): The date your loan application was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (select_one, required): The user who submitted this loan application.
* **Submitted Time** (time, required): The time your loan application was submitted.
