## Wastewater Treatment Plant Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wastewater Treatment Plant Inspection Form is a comprehensive checklist of essential information that is used to inspect and analyze the performance of a wastewater treatment plant. It helps to identify any issues or deficiencies in the plant's treatment process, chemical parameters, and physical characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the plant's site details, including its name, location, and type of wastewater treatment process.
* Provide information about the inspector, including their name, title, and contact information.
* Describe the treatment process used by the plant, including the type of treatment method and its purpose.
* Record any chemical parameters that are measured during the inspection, such as Ammonia, BOD, COD, Ph, TDS, and TSS.
* Report any physical characteristics of the water, such as Water Level, pH, ORP, and TDS.
* Record the sampling and testing procedures used during the inspection, including Sample Type, Sample Number, Sample Date, Sample Location, and Sample Collector.
* Indicate which of the chemical parameters and physical characteristics were measured and provide the results.
* Enter any comments or observations about the plant's performance and any issues or deficiencies found during the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Details** (`site_details`): Enter the name and location of the wastewater treatment plant.
* **Inspector Information** (`inspector_info`): Enter your name, title, and contact information as the inspector.
* **Treatment Process** (`treatment_process`): Describe the type of treatment method used by the plant and its purpose.
* **Chemical Parameters** (`chemical_parameters`): Choose the chemical parameters that were measured during the inspection, such as Ammonia, BOD, COD, Ph, TDS, and TSS.
* **Physical Characteristics** (`physical_characteristics`): Choose the physical characteristics that were measured during the inspection, such as Water Level, pH, ORP, and TDS.
* **Sampling and Testing** (`sampling_and_testing`): Record the sampling and testing procedures used during the inspection, including Sample Type, Sample Number, Sample Date, Sample Location, and Sample Collector.
* **Regulatory Compliance** (`regulatory_compliance`): Enter any comments or observations about the plant's performance and any issues or deficiencies found during the inspection.
* **Sampling and Testing 2** (`sampling_and_testing_2`): Select the chemical parameters and physical characteristics that were measured and provide the results.
* **Chemical Parameters 2** (`chemical_parameters_2`): Select the chemical parameters that were measured during the inspection and provide the results.
* **Physical Characteristics 2** (`physical_characteristics_2`): Select the physical characteristics that were measured during the inspection and provide the results.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or observations about the plant's performance.
