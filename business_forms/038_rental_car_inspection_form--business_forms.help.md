# Rental Car Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Car Inspection Form is used by rental companies to document the condition and status of their vehicles after a rental period. This form helps track the vehicle's condition, mileage, and other details to ensure accurate reporting and maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Enter the vehicle's condition before and after the rental period** in the `vehicle_condition_before_rental` and `vehicle_condition_after_rental` fields.
2.  **Record the odometer reading** at the start and end of the rental period in the `odometer_reading` and `mileage_reading` fields.
3.  **Enter the rental period dates** in the `rental_date` and `return_date` fields.
4.  **Indicate the vehicle's status** as `Active` or `Inactive` in the `vehicle_status` field.
5.  **Check the vehicle type** as `Yes` or `No` in the `vehicle_type` field.
6.  **Enter the rental duration** in the `rental_duration` field.
7.  **Check if there is any vehicle damage** using the `vehicle_damage` field.
8.  **Add any comments** about the vehicle's condition in the `comments` field.
9.  **Enter your name and contact information** as the inspector in the `inspector_name`, `inspector_company`, `inspector_phone`, and `inspector_email` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vehicle_condition_before_rental**: Describe the vehicle's condition before the rental period. This field is used to document the initial state of the vehicle.
* **odometer_reading**: Enter the vehicle's odometer reading at the start of the rental period.
* **vehicle_condition_after_rental**: Describe the vehicle's condition after the rental period. This field is used to document any changes or damages to the vehicle.
* **mileage_reading**: Enter the vehicle's odometer reading at the end of the rental period.
* **rental_date**: Enter the start date of the rental period.
* **return_date**: Enter the end date of the rental period.
* **vehicle_status**: Indicate the vehicle's status as `Active` or `Inactive`.
* **vehicle_type**: Check `Yes` or `No` if the vehicle is available for rental.
* **rental_duration**: Enter the duration of the rental period.
* **vehicle_damage**: Check if there is any vehicle damage.
* **comments**: Add any additional comments about the vehicle's condition.
* **inspector_name**: Enter your name as the inspector.
* **inspector_company**: Enter your company as the inspector.
* **inspector_phone**: Enter your phone number as the inspector.
* **inspector_email**: Enter your email as the inspector.
