# AI Development Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive guide to provide us with all the necessary information for a successful AI project quotation. It's crucial that you complete this form accurately and thoroughly to receive a precise quote for your project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through the sections and fields, and fill out the requested details. Make sure to review each section carefully, as they are all essential for our quotation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`client_full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name or company representative name.
* **Email address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the email address we will use to send your quote.
* **Phone number** (`client_phone`, phone, optional): Enter your phone number for urgent communications (optional).
* **Company name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your company name.
* **Project title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter a brief name for your AI project.
* **Type of AI solution needed** (`project_category`, select_one, required): Select the primary focus area of your AI project. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Machine Learning Model
	+ Natural Language Processing
	+ Computer Vision
	+ Predictive Analytics
	+ Chatbot/Virtual Assistant
	+ Recommendation System
	+ Other
* **Detailed project description** (`project_description`, text, required): Explain what you want the AI to accomplish.
* **Expected project timeline** (`timeline_expectation`, integer, required): Enter how soon you need the project completed (in weeks).
* **Budget range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Approximate the budget you're considering for the project. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Under $5,000
	+ $5,000 - $15,000
	+ $15,000 - $30,000
	+ $30,000 - $50,000
	+ Over $50,000
* **Data availability** (`data_availability`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have data available for training, if you need help collecting data, or if you're unsure.
* **Integration requirements** (`integration_requirements`, select_multiple, optional): Specify where the AI solution will be deployed. You can choose multiple options:
	+ Web application
	+ Mobile app
	+ Desktop software
	+ Cloud platform
	+ On-premise system
	+ API service
	+ Other
* **Technical expertise level** (`technical_expertise_level`, select_one, required): Assess your team's level of technical expertise. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Expert
* **Maintenance preference** (`maintenance_preference`, select_one, optional): Indicate who will maintain the AI solution after deployment. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Our team will maintain
	+ Need ongoing support from developer
	+ Hybrid approach
* **Special requirements or constraints** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any unique technical or business requirements.
* **Reference examples** (`reference_examples`, text, optional): Provide links to similar solutions that you admire.
* **Previous AI experience** (`previous_ai_experience`, text, optional): Describe any prior AI implementations in your organization.
* **Request date** (`quotation_date`, date, required): Enter the date you'd like to receive the quote.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other information that might help us prepare your quote.
