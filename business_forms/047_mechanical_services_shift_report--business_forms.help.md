# mechanical_services_shift_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mechanical_services_shift_report form is designed to document and track details about mechanical services provided during shifts. It is a flexible reporting tool that allows users to submit information with varying degrees of detail.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Begin by providing Shift Details (field 2). This field should contain information about the start and end times of the shift, as well as any notable events or circumstances that occurred during the shift.
2.  Enter the Scheduled Shift (field 3) details. This field is for scheduling information, including the scheduled start and end times of the shift.
3.  Provide Team Member Details (field 5) if applicable. This field is for identifying the team members involved in the mechanical services provided during the shift.
4.  Specify the Service Level (field 6) if known. This field is for indicating the level of service provided, such as routine, maintenance, or emergency services.
5.  Enter any notable Shift Report (field 8) details. This field is for summarizing the shift's activities and outcomes.
6.  Complete any additional fields as needed, but the above-mentioned fields are the primary focus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Shift Details (field 2)**: Enter the shift's start and end times, and provide any notable events or circumstances that occurred during the shift.
*   **Scheduled Shift (field 3)**: Enter the scheduled start and end times of the shift.
*   **Team Member Details (field 5)**: If applicable, provide information about the team members involved in the mechanical services provided during the shift.
*   **Service Level (field 6)**: Indicate the level of service provided, such as routine, maintenance, or emergency services.
*   **Shift Report (field 8)**: Summarize the shift's activities and outcomes.
*   **Notes (field 11)**: Provide any additional comments or context about the shift.

## Tips

*   Use the form to document and track mechanical services shifts consistently and thoroughly.
*   Focus on providing essential information about each shift to ensure accurate reporting.
*   Use the form as a flexible reporting tool, and submit only the details you have available.

By following this guide, end-users should be able to complete the form effectively and accurately document mechanical services shifts.
