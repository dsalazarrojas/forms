# Treasury Digitalization Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Digitalization Program Registration form is designed to collect information from organizations and individuals about their participation in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct page for each section.
2. Fill in the required fields for each page.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Name**: Enter your name as the representative of the organization.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Role**: Enter your role in the organization.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Address**: Enter your manager's address.
* **Department**: Enter the name of your department.
* **Team Members**: Confirm whether your team members are participating in the program.
* **Digitalization Programs**: Confirm whether your organization is participating in digitalization programs.
* **Submit**: Confirm your submission status.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your submission.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your submission.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your submission.

Note: Please review your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
