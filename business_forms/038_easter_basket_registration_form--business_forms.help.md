# Easter Basket Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easter Basket Registration Form is a form used to register participants for the Easter Basket program. Please fill out the form to express your interest and register for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name and Last Name to identify yourself as a participant.
2. Enter your School Name, if you are a student or affiliated with a school.
3. Provide your Field 4 information, which is a required field.
4. If you have an Email address, enter it for contact purposes.
5. If you have a Phone number, enter it for communication.
6. Enter the Date you are available to participate.
7. Enter the Time you are available to participate.
8. Add a Note about your availability or any relevant details.
9. If you have a School ID, enter it if applicable.
10. Select if you've been assigned a tool for the program.
11. Finally, select the Category of your choice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **School Name** (text): If you're a student or affiliated with a school, enter your school name.
* **Field 4** (text): This is a required field. Please enter the requested information.
* **Email** (email): If you have an email address, enter it here. This will be used for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (text): If you have a phone number, enter it here. This will be used for communication.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date you are available to participate in the program.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time you are available to participate in the program.
* **Note** (note): Enter any additional information or details about your availability.
* **School ID** (number): If you have a School ID, enter it here.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_one): Select 'Yes' if you have been assigned a tool for the program, or 'No' if you have not.
* **Category** (text): Select the Category of your choice, indicating your interest in the program.
