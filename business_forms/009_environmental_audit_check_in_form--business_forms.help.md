# environmental_audit_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track the results of the environmental audit check-in process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the Check In Date when the audit was conducted.
2. Add any relevant notes or comments in the Check In Notes field.
3. Choose the correct environmental impacts from the Environmental Impacts field. If the impact is not listed, select "Other" and add the impact in the Environmental Impacts Other field.
4. Select the sustainability actions to be taken from the Sustainability Actions field. Multiple actions can be selected.
5. Enter the person responsible for taking the assigned actions in the Assigned To field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, required/optional): The date when the environmental audit was conducted.
* **Check In Notes** (`notes`, optional): Any additional comments or remarks about the audit.
* **Environmental Impacts** (`environmental_impacts`, optional): The potential positive, negative, or neutral effects of the audit on the environment. Choose from the following options:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Neutral
* **Environmental Impacts Other** (`environmental_impacts_other`, optional): Any other environmental impacts not listed in the previous field.
* **Sustainability Actions** (`sustainability_actions`, optional): A list of actions to be taken to improve sustainability based on the audit results. Choose from the following options:
	+ Reduce energy consumption
	+ Reduce water consumption
	+ Reduce waste
	+ Increase recycling
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, optional): The person responsible for taking the assigned actions.
