# Partner Commission Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be filled out by potential partners interested in collaborating with us. It helps us gather necessary details to evaluate their application and determine if they are a good fit for our partnership program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the text field at the top of the form.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Tell us how you heard about our program in the "How did you hear about us?" field.
5. Select your partner type (Business or Individual) from the dropdown menu in the "Partner Type" field.
6. Choose your preferred submission methods (Online, Phone, or Email) in the "Submission" field.
7. Select your available workdays from the list in the "Workdays" field.
8. Choose the terms of your agreement (AM or PM) in the "Agreement" field.
9. (Optional) Attach any supporting documents in the "Attachments" field.
10. If applicable, provide the terms of your agreement in the "Agreement Terms" field.
11. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "Terms and Conditions" field.
12. If not applicable, select "No" in the "None" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (form_1)**: Please enter your name.
* **Email (form_2)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (form_3)**: Enter your phone number.
* **How did you hear about us? (form_4)**: Provide how you heard about our program.
* **Partner Type (form_5)**: Select your partner type.
* **Submission (form_6)**: Choose your preferred submission methods.
* **Workdays (form_7)**: Select your available workdays.
* **Agreement (form_8)**: Choose the terms of your agreement.
* **Attachments (form_9)**: (Optional) Attach any supporting documents.
* **Agreement Terms (form_10)**: If applicable, provide the terms of your agreement.
* **Terms and Conditions (form_11)**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **None (form_12)**: If not applicable, select "No".
