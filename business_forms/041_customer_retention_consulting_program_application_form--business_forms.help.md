# customer_retention_consulting_program_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "customer_retention_consulting_program_application_form" is used for customers to apply to a customer retention consulting program. This program is designed to help customers achieve their business goals by identifying and addressing retention challenges and setting clear goals and start dates. By completing this form, customers will be able to provide necessary information for the program and demonstrate their eligibility for participation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to understand what information is required and what each field is used for.
2.  Fill out the form with your Prospect Information, Retention Challenges, Goals, Ideal Start Date, Contact Information, and Consultant Information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prospect Information** (`prospect_info`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide us with your business information so we can understand your background and context.
* **Retention Challenges** (`retention_challenges`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Please select the specific challenges that you are currently experiencing with customer retention. This could include things like high employee turnover, difficulty meeting sales targets, or lack of customer engagement.
* **Goals** (`goals`, `text`, required: `false`): Please describe the specific goals you have for participating in our program, such as improving customer satisfaction or increasing employee engagement.
* **Ideal Start Date** (`ideal_start_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Please select a date when you would like to start the program. We will do our best to accommodate your requested start date.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide us with your contact information so we can get in touch with you about your application.
* **Consultant Information** (`consultant_info`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide us with any additional information about the consultants you are working with or would like to work with.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields, but feel free to skip optional ones.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
