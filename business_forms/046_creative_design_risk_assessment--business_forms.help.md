# creative_design_risk_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to identify, assess, and document potential risks associated with creative design projects. By filling out this form, you can ensure that every project team member has a clear understanding of the possible risks and can work together to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the project details and identify potential risks.
2. Select all applicable risks from the Risk Factors field.
3. Choose the likelihood of each selected risk (Low, Medium, High).
4. Choose the impact of each selected risk (Minor, Moderate, Major).
5. Select the probability of each selected risk (Unlikely, Possible, Probable).
6. If necessary, describe any mitigation actions to be taken.
7. Summarize your risk assessment and comments in the Risk Assessment Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Assessment** (`1`, `number`, required): Enter a numerical value to indicate the level of risk associated with the project.
* **Risk Factors** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable risks that may impact the project. These are pre-defined risk factors that may include:
	+ Risk of reduced revenue
	+ Risk of project delay
	+ Risk of design failure
* **Risk Likelihood** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Choose the likelihood of each selected risk (Low, Medium, High).
* **Risk Impact** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the impact of each selected risk (Minor, Moderate, Major).
* **Risk Probability** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Choose the probability of each selected risk (Unlikely, Possible, Probable).
* **Mitigation Actions** (`6`, `note`, optional): If you have identified any mitigation actions to be taken, describe them in detail.
* **Risk Management Strategy** (`7`, `text`, optional): If you have a risk management strategy, describe it in this field.
* **Risk Assessment Comments** (`8`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the risk assessment.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing the risks and mitigation actions.
* Use the provided risk factors to select only applicable risks.
* Ensure that all selected risks have a risk likelihood, impact, and probability assigned to them.
* Use clear and concise language when summarizing your risk assessment and comments.
