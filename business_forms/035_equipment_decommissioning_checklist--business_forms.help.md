# Equipment Decommissioning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Decommissioning Checklist is used to evaluate and document the readiness of a piece of equipment for decommissioning.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and accurately, based on your evaluation of the equipment.
2. For select-one and select-multiple fields, choose the correct options from the list provided.
3. Enter dates and times as necessary, using the format specified for each field.
4. Use the note fields to capture any relevant comments or observations that may be useful in the evaluation process.
5. Review your answers to ensure that you've covered all relevant aspects of the equipment's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checklist Title** (`checklist-title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for the equipment being evaluated.
	+ This field is intended to capture a concise description of the equipment or item being assessed.
* **Item 1** (`item-1-label`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the item is still functional or not.
	+ This question assesses the functional status of the equipment, and the answer should reflect its ability to perform its intended purpose.
* **Item 1** (`item-1-label`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each applicable item.
	+ This field allows the evaluator to identify specific issues or defects with the equipment.
* **Date 1** (`date-1`, date, optional): Enter the date the equipment was last serviced or inspected.
	+ This date is used to track maintenance history and identify patterns or trends.
* **Time 1** (`time-1`, time, optional): Enter the time the equipment was last serviced or inspected.
	+ This time helps in identifying specific time-related issues or schedules.
* **Note 1** (`note-1`, note, optional): Capture any comments or observations about the equipment.
	+ Use this field to note any additional information that may be relevant to the evaluation.
* **Item 2** (`item-4-label`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the item is still functional or not.
	+ Similar to item 1, this question assesses the functional status of the equipment.
* **Item 2** (`item-4-label`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each applicable item.
	+ This field helps identify specific issues or defects with the equipment.
* **Note 2** (`note-2`, note, optional): Capture any comments or observations about the equipment.
	+ Use this field for any additional information that may impact the evaluation.
* **Date 2** (`date-2`, date, optional): Enter the date the equipment was last serviced or inspected.
	+ Track the maintenance history and identify patterns.
* **Time 2** (`time-2`, time, optional): Enter the time the equipment was last serviced or inspected.
	+ Note the time to identify specific schedules or patterns.
* **Note 3** (`note-3`, note, optional): Capture any final comments or observations about the equipment.
	+ Use this field to summarize the evaluation or note any follow-up actions.

## Tips

- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- Take your time to evaluate each question carefully to ensure accurate answers.
