# Facebook Likes And Twitter Followers - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a business's Facebook likes and Twitter followers. It's a simple and straightforward form designed to gather relevant data about a company's online presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the form in the "Form Title" field.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Twitter Followers" field to indicate the presence or absence of Twitter followers.
3. Enter a description of the business in the "Description" field (there are two identical fields, please fill in the first one).
4. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Business Type" field to indicate the type of business (this is likely to be used to categorize businesses).
5. Enter the business name in the "Business Name" field.
6. Enter the contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
7. Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Payment Method" field to indicate if the business uses a payment method.
9. Enter any additional notes about the business in the "Notes" field.
