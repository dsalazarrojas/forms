## Purpose
The daily schedule overview form helps managers and team leads to track and organize team members' schedules, meetings, and other activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Schedule Priority" field with the priority of your tasks (if applicable).
2. Select whether you have any meetings or appointments scheduled using the "Meetings" field.
3. Enter any notes or comments you want to share with your team using the "Notes" field.
