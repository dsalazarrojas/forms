# Factory Energy Recovery Project Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and track information about factory energy recovery projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the project title and name in the `project_name` field.
2.  Provide the location of the project in the `project_location` field.
3.  Select the type of project from the `project_type` options.
4.  Choose the status of the project from the `project_status` multi-select options.
5.  Enter the start and end dates of the project in the `project_start_date` and `project_end_date` fields respectively.
6.  Enter the project budget in the `project_budget` field.
7.  Enter the project manager's email address in the `project_manager_email` field.
8.  Enter the project manager's phone number in the `project_manager_phone` field.
9.  Enter the contact person's name in the `contact_person` field.
10. Enter the contact person's phone number in the `contact_phone` field.
11. Enter the contact person's email address in the `contact_email` field.
12. Enter the contact company's name in the `contact_company` field.
13. Enter any additional project note in the `project_note` field.
14. Enter the project's physical address in the `project_address` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Factory Energy Recovery Project Registration** (`factory_energy_recovery_project_registration_form`): Enter the title of the project.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`): Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`): Enter the physical location of the project.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`): Select the type of project from the options: Energy Efficiency, Energy Generation, Other.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`): Select all applicable statuses from the options: In progress, Completed, Cancelled, Not Started.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`): Enter the start date of the project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`): Enter the end date of the project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`): Enter the total budget for the project.
* **Project Manager Email** (`project_manager_email`): Enter the email address of the project manager.
* **Project Manager Phone** (`project_manager_phone`): Enter the phone number of the project manager.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Company** (`contact_company`): Enter the name of the contact company.
* **Project Note** (`project_note`): Enter any additional information about the project.
* **Project Address** (`project_address`): Enter the physical address of the project.
