# Team Appreciation Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Appreciation Communication Form is used to show appreciation to team members. Use it to send a message of gratitude to your colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the team's name in the **Team Name** field.
2. Select the type of appreciation you want to show in the **Appreciation Type** field.
3. Enter a message in the **Message** field to explain why you are showing appreciation.
4. If necessary, select who the appreciation is for in the **Employee Names** field.
5. Choose a date and time for the appreciation in the **Date** and **Time** fields.
6. If applicable, choose who is assigned to perform the appreciation in the **Assigned To** field.
7. If applicable, enter the name of the person assigned to perform the appreciation in the **Other Assigned To** field.
8. Indicate if you or someone else is submitting the appreciation form in the **Submitted By** field.
9. If applicable, enter the name of the person submitting the form in the **Other Submitted By** field.
10. If you have a specific **Form ID**, please select it in the **Form ID** field, otherwise select **No**.
11. If applicable, enter a custom **Other Form ID** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team being appreciated.
- **Appreciation Type**: Choose how you want to show appreciation, such as Team Lunch, Team Dinner, or Publicly Acknowledgment.
- **Message**: Enter a message explaining why you are showing appreciation.
- **Employee Names**: Select who you are showing appreciation to, if not everyone.
- **Date**: Choose a date for the appreciation event.
- **Time**: Choose a time for the appreciation event.
- **Assigned To**: Choose who is assigned to perform the appreciation, if necessary.
- **Other Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person assigned to perform the appreciation, if not chosen in **Assigned To**.
- **Submitted By**: Indicate if you or someone else is submitting the form.
- **Other Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form, if not chosen in **Submitted By**.
- **Form ID**: If you have a specific form ID, select it, otherwise select **No**.
- **Other Form ID**: Enter a custom Form ID, if not selected in **Form ID**.
