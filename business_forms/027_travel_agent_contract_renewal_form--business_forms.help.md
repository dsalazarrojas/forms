# Travel Agent Contract Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to determine if a travel agent wants to renew their contract and gather information required for the renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you want to renew your contract, and if so, provide the required information in the subsequent fields. If you select "No", the form will be automatically submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contract Renewal Form** (`contract_renewal_form`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you want to renew your contract.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Your full name as it appears on your contract.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Your email address as it appears on your contract.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Your phone number as it appears on your contract.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required: false): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the contract renewal.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, required: false): The start date of your existing contract.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, required: false): The end date of your existing contract.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, required: false): The date you are interested in renewing your contract.

## Tips
* Make sure to select "Yes" if you are interested in renewing your contract.
* If you select "Yes", make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information in the subsequent fields.
* If you select "No", the form will be automatically submitted.
* Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the contract renewal.
