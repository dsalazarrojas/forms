# Construction Vendor Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about your construction business for registration and certification purposes. By completing this form, you will provide the necessary details for your company to be included in the company's vendor directory and to receive work assignments. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. Make sure to review the required fields carefully and ensure you have all necessary documentation to support your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`): Enter the date you registered as a vendor with the company.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter your business' legal name as registered with the relevant authorities.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`): Select the type of business structure your company operates under (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, etc.).
* **Company Address** (`company_address`): Enter the full address of your business location.
* **City** (`company_city`): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State** (`company_state`): Enter the state or province where your business is located.
* **ZIP Code** (`company_zip`): Enter the postal code of your business location.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your business.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`): Enter the title or position of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Vendor Category** (`vendor_category`): Select the category of services or products your business offers (e.g., Building Materials, Equipment Rental, etc.).
* **Products or Services Offered** (`products_services`): Describe the detailed products or services you offer.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`): Select how many years your business has been operating.
* **Tax ID Number** (`tax_id`): Enter your business' Tax ID Number (EIN or SSN).
* **Business License Number** (`business_license`): Enter your business' license number.
* **License State** (`license_state`): Enter the state where your business license was issued.
* **License Expiration** (`license_expiry`): Enter the date your business license expires.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`): Enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Policy Number** (`insurance_policy_number`): Enter your policy ID number.
* **Liability Coverage Amount** (`liability_coverage`): Enter the coverage limit of your liability insurance.
* **Insurance Expiration** (`insurance_expiry`): Enter the date your insurance policy expires.
* **Workers Compensation** (`workers_compensation`): Select whether your business has workers' compensation coverage.
* **Bonding Capacity** (`bonding_capacity`): Enter the maximum bond amount you have available.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`): List any relevant certifications your business holds.
* **References** (`references`): List any relevant client references.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Select your standard payment terms (e.g., Net 15 Days, Net 30 Days, etc.).
* **Delivery Areas** (`delivery_areas`): Enter the locations where you deliver your products or services.
* **Delivery Lead Time** (`delivery_lead_time`): Enter your typical delivery time.
* **Minority-Owned Business** (`minority_owned`): Select whether your business is certified as a minority-owned business.
* **Woman-Owned Business** (`woman_owned`): Select whether your business is certified as a woman-owned business.
* **Veteran-Owned Business** (`veteran_owned`): Select whether your business is certified as a veteran-owned business.
* **Authorized Signature** (`authorized_signature`): Enter your full name as the authorized signer of this form.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all required fields truthfully and accurately.
- Attach any necessary documentation to support your answers.
- Review your submission carefully before submitting.
- If you need help or have questions, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
