# Participation Waiver And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in an event where a waiver and release is required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's name and any other required information.
2. Ensure that you have the participant's consent and understanding of the waiver and release terms.
3. Sign the form as the parent or guardian of the participant if they are a minor.
4. Sign the form as the participant if they are 18 or older.
5. Ensure that the participant's contact information is accurate and up to date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant name** (text, required/optional): This is the name of the participant who will be attending the event.
* **parent name** (text, required/optional): This is the name of the parent or guardian who is signing this waiver and release.
* **date of participation** (date, required/optional): This is the date on which the participant will participate in the event.
* **event date** (date, required/optional): This is the date on which the event will take place.
* **event start time** (time, required/optional): This is the time at which the event will start.
* **event end time** (time, required/optional): This is the time at which the event will end.
* **location** (text, required/optional): This is the location where the event will take place.
* **waived by** (text, required/optional): This is the name of the person who is signing this waiver and release.
* **waived on** (date, required/optional): This is the date on which this waiver and release was signed.
* **signature** (text, required/optional): This is the participant's signature.
* **participant name signature** (text, required/optional): This is the participant's name as they would like to have it signed.
* **participant address** (text, required/optional): This is the participant's address.
* **participant phone** (text, required/optional): This is the participant's phone number.
* **participant email** (email, required/optional): This is the participant's email address.
* **parent phone** (text, required): This is the phone number of the parent or guardian who is signing this waiver and release.
* **parent email** (email, required/optional): This is the email address of the parent or guardian who is signing this waiver and release.
* **event description** (text, required/optional): This is a description of the event.
* **participant notes** (note, required/optional): This is any additional information about the participant.
* **waiver terms** (text, required/optional): This is the text of the waiver and release.
* **waiver conditions** (text, required/optional): This is any additional conditions of the waiver and release.
* **participant aware** (text, required/optional): This is a statement indicating that the participant is aware of the waiver and release terms.
* **participant consent** (text, required/optional): This is a statement indicating that the participant consents to the waiver and release terms.
* **participant release** (text, required/optional): This is a statement indicating that the participant releases all liability.

Note: Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
