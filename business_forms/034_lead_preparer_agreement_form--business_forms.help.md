# Lead Preparer Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document agreements between a business and a client regarding the scope of work, schedule, and expectations for a project or service. It is essential to accurately complete this form to ensure a clear understanding of the project's requirements and timelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the client's name from the options provided in the "client" field.
3. Enter the start date for the project or service.
4. Enter the expected completion date for the project or service.
5. Describe the responsibilities of all parties involved in the project or service.
6. Outline the project or service timelines.
7. Select the relevant acceptance options, if applicable.
8. Add any additional comments or remarks if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Preparer Agreement Form** (`title`, `text`, required): This is the title of the form, which should not be changed.
* **Client** (`client`, `select_one`, required): Select the relevant client name from the options provided.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the project or service in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Expected Completion Date** (`expected_completion_date`, `date`, required): Enter the expected completion date of the project or service in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Responsibilities** (`responsibilities`, `text`, required): Describe the tasks, duties, and obligations of all parties involved in the project or service.
* **Timelines** (`timelines`, `text`, required): Outline the key milestones, deadlines, and timelines for the project or service.
* **Acceptance** (`acceptance`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the options that apply to you or your organization.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks, or sign the document with your name.

## Tips
* Ensure you complete the form accurately and truthfully to avoid misunderstandings or disputes.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled in.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
