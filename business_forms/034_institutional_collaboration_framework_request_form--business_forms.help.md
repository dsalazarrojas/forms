# Institutional Collaboration Framework Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Institutional Collaboration Framework Request Form is designed to collect information about potential collaborations between institutions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select "Partnership Objectives" and describe the objectives of the collaboration.
2. In the "Scope of Work" field, provide a brief description of the collaboration project.
3. In the "Timeline" field, specify the expected start and end dates of the collaboration.
4. Enter the name of the partner institution.
5. Enter the name of the contact person from the partner institution.
6. Enter the email address of the contact person.
7. Enter the phone number of the contact person.
8. In the "Partnership Proposal" field, describe the proposal for the collaboration.
9. In the "Agreement" field, provide any existing agreement or document related to the collaboration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partnership Objectives** (`partnery_objectives`, text, optional): Briefly describe the objectives of the collaboration.
* **Scope of Work** (`scope_of_work`, text, optional): Describe the collaboration project.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, time, optional): Specify the expected start and end dates of the collaboration.
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the partner institution.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person from the partner institution.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Partnership Proposal** (`partnership_proposal`, text, optional): Describe the proposal for the collaboration.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, optional): Provide any existing agreement or document related to the collaboration.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in each field.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your participation in this collaboration framework and look forward to reviewing your request.
