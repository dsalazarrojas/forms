# Firewood Update Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Firewood Update Submission form is used to update information about firewood, including its type, quantity, status, photos, and facility manager.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and ensure you have all the necessary information to complete them accurately.
2. Scroll through each page to fill out the required fields.
3. Select the correct options for each field as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Firewood Type** (`firewood_type`, text, required: false): Enter the type of firewood, such as "Oak", "Pine", or "Maple".
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the number of firewood pieces available.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the current status of the firewood from the options "Available", "Sold Out", "In Transit", or "Reserved".
* **Photos** (`photos`, select_multiple, required: true): Select up to three photos to include with the firewood update. You can choose from the options "photo1", "photo2", and "photo3".
* **Facility Manager** (`facility_manager`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the facility manager responsible for the firewood.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have permission to submit updates and double-check that the submission is not a duplicate.
