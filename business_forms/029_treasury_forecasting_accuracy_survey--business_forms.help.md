# Treasury Forecasting Accuracy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a short and simple survey designed to gauge the accuracy of your company's current forecasting. We would like to know more about the methods and data sources you use for forecasting, so we can better understand your needs and provide more accurate forecasts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your current date to help us track the progress of your survey.
2. Rate the accuracy of your current forecasting on a scale of 1-5 (Low, Medium, High).
3. Indicate which methods you use for forecasting (e.g., Cash forecasting, Forecasting software, Manual forecasting, Other).
4. Choose the level of automation for your forecasting (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
5. Select the data sources you use for forecasting (e.g., Financial reports, Historical data, External data, Other).
6. Describe any common challenges you face when forecasting.
7. Provide your current date to indicate that you have completed the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Start (<type> date, <required> false)**: Enter the current date to mark the start of your survey.
* **current forecast accuracy (<type> number, <required> false)**: Rate the accuracy of your current forecasting on a scale of 1-5. A higher value indicates more accurate forecasting.
* **forecasting methods used (<type> select_multiple, <required> false)**: Choose the methods you use for forecasting (e.g., Cash forecasting, Forecasting software, Manual forecasting, Other).
* **forecast automation level (<type> select_one, <required> false)**: Select the level of automation for your forecasting (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **data sources used (<type> select_multiple, <required> false)**: Select the data sources you use for forecasting (e.g., Financial reports, Historical data, External data, Other).
* **accuracy rating (<type> select_one, <required> false)**: Rate the accuracy of your current forecasting on a scale of 1-5. A higher value indicates more accurate forecasting.
* **common challenges (<type> text, <required> false)**: Describe any common challenges you face when forecasting.
* **Survey End (<type> time, <required> false)**: Enter your current date to mark the completion of your survey.

Tips:
* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them.
* You can skip any fields that do not apply to your current forecasting practices.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
