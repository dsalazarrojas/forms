# Termination For Cause Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Termination For Cause Contract Form is used to document the termination of a contract due to a specific reason. This form is essential for ensuring a smooth and transparent termination process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the contract number at the top of the form.
2. Enter the contract date.
3. Provide a clear and concise reason for termination in the "Reason for Termination" field.
4. Specify the notice period and termination date.
5. If applicable, upload or attach supporting documents to provide additional context.
6. Choose the termination type (Contractor, Employee, Partner, or Other).
7. Identify the reason for termination type (Contractor, Employee, Partner, or Other).
8. Select the signature type (Contractor, Employee, Partner, or Other) for both the authorizing party and the party being terminated.
9. Date and time the contract was signed.
10. Sign and date the contract in the location specified.
11. If required, provide the signature and date of two witnesses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, required): Enter the contract number assigned to the terminated contract.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, required): Enter the date the contract was signed.
* **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, required): Provide a clear and concise reason for terminating the contract.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, required): Specify the notice period before the contract's termination.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, required): Enter the date the contract will be terminated.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, optional): Upload or attach supporting documents to provide additional context.
* **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, required): Choose the reason for termination (Misconduct, Default, Termination at will, etc.).
* **Termination Amount** (`termination_amount`, required): Enter the termination amount.
* **Termination Type** (`termination_type`, required): Choose the termination type (Contractor, Employee, Partner, Other).
* **Reason for Termination Type** (`reason_for_termination_type`, required): Identify the reason for termination type (Contractor, Employee, Partner, Other).
* **Signature of Authorizing Party** (`signature_of_authorizing_party`, required): Select the signature type (Contractor, Employee, Partner, Other).
* **Signature of Party Being Terminated** (`signature_of_party_being_terminated`, required): Select the signature type (Contractor, Employee, Partner, Other).
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, required): Date and time the contract was signed.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, required): Date and time the contract was signed.
* **Location Signed** (`location_signed`, required): Specify the location where the contract was signed.
* **Signature of Witness 1** (`signature_of_witness_1`, optional): Optional witness's signature.
* **Signature of Witness 2** (`signature_of_witness_2`, optional): Optional witness's signature.
* **Witness 1 Name** (`witness_1_name`, optional): Optional witness's name.
* **Witness 2 Name** (`witness_2_name`, optional): Optional witness's name.
* **Witness 1 Title** (`witness_1_title`, optional): Optional witness's title.
* **Witness 2 Title** (`witness_2_title`, optional): Optional witness's title.
* **Witness 1 Signature** (`witness_1_signature`, optional): Optional witness's signature.
* **Witness 2 Signature** (`witness_2_signature`, optional): Optional witness's signature.
* **Witness 1 Date** (`witness_1_date`, optional): Optional witness's date.
* **Witness 2 Date** (`witness_2_date`, optional): Optional witness's date.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the relevant parties or seek additional guidance.
* Upload or attach supporting documents to provide context and clarity.
* Ensure that all signatures are valid and authentic.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it for final review and approval.
