# Process Optimization Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for businesses interested in utilizing the Process Optimization Accelerator to streamline and improve their processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields carefully and accurately.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field based on your business needs.
3. Upload any necessary attachments or files as required.
4. Once completed, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Details** (`form_1`, `text`, Required: false): Enter your company's name, department, or any other relevant business details.
* **Improvement Opportunities** (`form_2`, `select_multiple`, Required: false): Choose all improvement opportunities your business is interested in, such as:
	+ Improve Process
	+ Reduce Process
	+ Eliminate Process
* **Business Challenges** (`form_3`, `text`, Required: true): Describe the specific business challenges you are facing.
* **Process Improvement Strategies** (`form_4`, `text`, Required: false): Outline the strategies you plan to use to improve your business process.
* **Process Improvement Methods** (`form_5`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select the method you will use to improve your process, including:
	+ Analyze
	+ Implement
	+ Test
* **Timeline** (`form_6`, `date`, Required: true): Provide a specific start and end date for your project.
* **Time Zone** (`form_7`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select the time zone in which your business operates, including:
	+ EST/EDT
	+ CDT
	+ MST/MDT
	+ PST/PDT
* **Contact Information** (`form_8`, `text`, Required: false): Enter your contact information, including name and title.
* **Name** (`form_9`, `text`, Required: true): Enter your name as it appears on your business's letterhead.
* **Job Title** (`form_10`, `text`, Required: true): Enter your job title.
* **Business Phone** (`form_11`, `text`, Required: true): Enter your business phone number.
* **Email** (`form_12`, `email`, Required: true): Enter your business email address.
* **Address** (`form_13`, `text`, Required: true): Enter your business address.
* **Business Type** (`form_14`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select the type of business you represent, including:
	+ Company
	+ Organization
	+ Team
* **Business Size** (`form_15`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select the size of your business, including:
	+ 1-10
	+ 11-50
	+ 51-100
	+ 101-200
	+ 201-500
	+ 501-1000
	+ 1000+
* **Business Location** (`form_16`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select where your business is located, including:
	+ Online
	+ Physical
	+ Hybrid
* **Contact Preference** (`form_17`, `select_one`, Required: true): Select your preferred contact method, including:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Chat
* **Additional Comments** (`form_18`, `text`, Required: false): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Signature** (`form_19`, `text`, Required: false): Sign your name to confirm you are submitting this application.
* **Attachments** (`form_20`, `text`, Required: false): Attach any relevant documents or files as required.
* **Upload Attachments** (`form_21`, `text`, Required: false): Upload any additional attachments as necessary.
* **Upload File** (`form_22`, `text`, Required: false): Upload a file as required.
* **Upload File 2** (`form_23`, `text`, Required: false): Upload another file as necessary.
* **Upload File 3** (`form_24`, `text`, Required: false): Upload yet another file as required.
* **form_25** (`form_25`, `select_multiple`, Required: false): Choose all the options that apply to your business, including:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5
