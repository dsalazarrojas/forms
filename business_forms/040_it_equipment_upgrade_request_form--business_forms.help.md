# it_equipment_upgrade_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit upgrade requests for IT equipment. It captures necessary information to track and process the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your email address to whom the request will be assigned.
2. Provide the reason for the upgrade in the text area.
3. Select the equipment ID from the dropdown list.
4. Choose the desired upgrade frequency, i.e., one-time or recurring.
5. Add any additional comments if needed.
6. Enter your phone number so that the request can be contacted if required.
7. Choose the date of your request.
8. Select the status of your request.
9. Add any additional notes if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **requestor_email (Email)**: This is the email address that will be used to contact you regarding your request.
* **reason_upgrade (Text Area)**: This field is for providing a brief reason for the upgrade.
* **equipment_id (Dropdown List)**: This is a list of equipment IDs that you can choose from to select the correct equipment that needs an upgrade.
* **upgrade_frequency (Dropdown List)**: This field is for selecting the frequency of the desired upgrade. Select 'daily', 'weekly', or 'monthly' based on your requirement.
* **comments (Text Area)**: This field is for providing any additional comments regarding your request.
* **assigned_tool (Text Area)**: This field is for providing any tool that is assigned to you for the requested upgrade.
* **requestor_phone (Text Area)**: This is your phone number that will be used in case we need to contact you.
* **request_date (Date Field)**: This field is for selecting the date of your request.
* **status (Dropdown List)**: This field is for choosing the status of your request, i.e., 'pending', 'approved', or 'declined'.
* **notes (Text Area)**: This field is for providing any additional notes that are required.
