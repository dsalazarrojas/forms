# Small Business Loan Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Small Business Loan Intake Form is designed to collect necessary information from small business owners to assess their creditworthiness and make informed loan decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide a brief description of your business in the Business Description field.
3. Specify your business type and years of operation.
4. Enter the loan amount, term, and interest rate you are seeking.
5. Provide the location and address of your business.
6. Enter your business address and any additional address information (if applicable).
7. Enter your phone number and email address to contact you.
8. Specify the name of your bank and other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`<first_name>`, text, required: false): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`<last_name>`, text, required: false): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Business Name** (`<business_name>`, text, required: false): Enter your business name for identification purposes.
* **Business Description** (`<business_description>`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your business in a few sentences.
* **Business Type** (`<business_type>`, text, required: false): Specify your business type (e.g., retail, service, etc.).
* **Loan Amount** (`<loan_amount>`, number, required: false): Enter the amount you are seeking as a loan.
* **Loan Term** (`<loan_term>`, number, required: false): Enter the term of the loan (number of months or years).
* **Interest Rate** (`<loan_interest_rate>`, number, required: false): Enter the interest rate you are seeking.
* **Business Years in Operation** (`<business_years_in_operation>`, number, required: false): Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Business Location** (`<business_location>`, text, required: false): Enter the location of your business (city, state, zip, etc.).
* **Business Address** (`<business_address>`, text, required: false): Enter the address of your business (street address, etc.).
* **Business Address 2** (`<business_address2>`, text, required: false): Enter any additional address information (if applicable).
* **Phone Number** (`<phone_number>`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`<email>`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Bank Name** (`<bank_name>`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your bank.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly. This will help us make a more informed loan decision. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our loan officers for assistance.
