# Carpenter Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Carpenter Referral Form is a tool used to collect information about potential clients and their projects, so that carpenters can follow up and provide services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for the client and project information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name**: Type your full name to identify yourself as the referrer.
* **Your Email Address**: Enter your email address to enable the carpenter to contact you.
* **Your relationship to the carpenter**: Choose how you know the carpenter, such as Former Client, Architect, or Friend/Colleague.
* **Potential Client Name**: Enter the potential client's name.
* **Client Email Address**: Enter the potential client's email address.
* **Client Phone Number**: Enter the potential client's phone number.
* **Client Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how the client would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or SMS).
* **Referral Project Category**: Choose the category of the project (Residential, Commercial, Renovation, New Build, Custom Furniture, or Repairs).
* **Project Location (City and State)**: Enter the city and state where the project is located.
* **Brief Description of the Project**: Describe the type of carpentry work needed for the project.
* **Estimated Project Value**: Choose the estimated value of the project ($1,000 or less, $1,000-$5,000, $5,000-$20,000, or over $20,000).
* **Urgency of the Project**: Choose how urgent the project is (High, Medium, Low, or Planning Only).
* **Has the client already been informed of this referral?**: Check if the client has already been informed.
* **Suggested time for the carpenter to call the client**: Enter the best time for the carpenter to contact the client.
* **Is this referral eligible for a referral fee?**: Check if the referral is eligible for a fee.
* **Digital Signature**: Type your full name as acknowledgment of the referral.
* **Brief description of the project**: Enter any additional details the carpenter should know about the project.
* **Date of Referral**: Enter the date of the referral.
* **Digital Signature**: Type your full name as acknowledgment of the referral.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Provide as much detail as possible for the client and project information.
* Only refer clients with a clear need for carpentry services.
* Do not use this form for self-promotion or to solicit work from the same client multiple times.
