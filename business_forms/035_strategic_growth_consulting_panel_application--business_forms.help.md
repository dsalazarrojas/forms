# Strategic Growth Consulting Panel Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about experts who wish to be considered for the Strategic Growth Consulting Panel. The panel is a group of subject matter experts that will provide consultation and guidance to organizations on strategic growth and development matters. By completing this form, you will be helping us identify potential panel members based on their areas of expertise and availability. Please make sure you have a genuine interest in being part of the panel before submitting your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and understand the form purpose.
2. Fill out each section as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed.
4. Double-check that you have selected the correct options in the "Expertise" and "Contact Info" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expert Profile**:
	+ This section is a text field where you can provide a brief description of your experience, background, and qualifications.
	+ Please provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your expertise.
* **Experience**:
	+ This section is a free-form text area where you can describe your relevant experience and skills.
	+ Keep your answer concise and focused on your relevant experience.
* **Availability**:
	+ This section is a date field where you can indicate your availability for consultation and engagement.
	+ Enter the date you are available to start consulting.
* **Expertise**:
	+ This section is a multiple-choice field where you can select areas of expertise.
	+ Choose "Yes" for areas where you have expertise and "No" for areas where you do not.
* **Contact Info**:
	+ This section is a multiple-choice field where you can select your preferred contact method (email, phone, or note).
	+ Choose how you would like to be contacted for further information.
