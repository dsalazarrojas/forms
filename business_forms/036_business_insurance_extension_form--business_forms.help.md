# Business Insurance Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an extension to an existing business insurance policy. It is intended for use by business owners or their representatives to provide necessary information for the extension process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are filled out.
2. Provide the current policy number and the name of the insured business.
3. Select the type of extension being requested (e.g., Time Extension, Coverage Limit Extension, etc.).
4. Enter a detailed explanation for the reason of the extension request.
5. If applicable, provide information about any modifications or changes to be made to the policy.
6. Enter the proposed end date for the extension.
7. If necessary, provide details about any new risks or operations.
8. Provide the contact information for the primary representative for the extension request.
9. Confirm if any claims have been filed during the current policy term.
10. Sign the form to certify the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Policy Number** (`current_policy_number_extension`, `text`, required): The current policy number of the business. This will help identify the policy to be extended.
* **Insured Business Name** (`insured_business_name_extension`, `text`, required): The name of the insured business.
* **Extension Type** (`extension_request_type_choice`, `select_one`, required): The type of extension being requested (e.g., Time Extension, Coverage Limit Extension, etc.).
* **Reason for Extension** (`reason_for_extension_request_text`, `text`, required): A detailed explanation for the reason of the extension request.
* **Primary Contact for Extension** (`extension_primary_contact_name`, `text`, required): The name of the primary representative for the extension request.
* **Contact Email Address** (`extension_contact_email_address`, `email`, required): The email address of the primary representative for the extension request.
* **Have any claims been filed during the current policy term** (`claims_during_policy_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): A confirmation if any claims have been filed during the current policy term.
* **Date of Request** (`extension_request_date_signed`, `date`, required): The date the extension request is being made.
* **Authorized Representative Signature** (`extension_auth_signature`, `text`, required): A signature of the authorized representative to certify the request.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out to avoid delays or rejections.
* Provide clear and concise information in the reason for extension and additional coverage amount fields.
* If new risks or operations are involved, provide detailed descriptions.
