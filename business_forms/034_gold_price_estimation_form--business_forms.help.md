# Gold Price Estimation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help estimate the value of gold items by gathering relevant information from the user. It is intended for internal use within a business or organization that deals with gold valuation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the gold item in the "Jewelry Description" field.
2. Select the type of material the gold item is made of from the "Material" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the weight of the gold item in the "Weight" field.
4. Select the price range of the gold item from the "Price Range" dropdown menu.
5. If you have any customer inquiries related to the gold item, enter them in the "Customer Inquiries" field.
6. If you have any customer contact information (email, phone, or chat), enter it in the corresponding field.
7. Enter the estimated value of the gold item in the "Estimated Value" field.
8. If available, enter the actual value of the gold item in the "Actual Value" field.
9. If there is a difference between the estimated and actual values, enter the difference in the "Difference" field.
10. Add any additional comments or notes about the gold item in the "Notes" field.
11. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Jewelry Description (User Input)**: Enter a brief description of the gold item.
* **Material (Material)**: Select the type of material the gold item is made of. Options: gold, silver, platinum.
* **Weight (Weight)**: Enter the weight of the gold item.
* **Price Range (Price Range)**: Select the price range of the gold item from the dropdown menu. Options: high, low, medium.
* **Customer Inquiries (Customer Inquiries)**: Enter any customer inquiries related to the gold item.
* **Customer Inquiries Count (Customer Inquiries Count)**: Enter the number of customer inquiries.
* **Customer Inquiry Type (Customer Inquiry Type)**: Select the type of customer inquiry (email, phone, chat).
* **Customer Email (Customer Email)**: Enter the customer email contact information.
* **Customer Phone (Customer Phone)**: Enter the customer phone contact information.
* **Customer Chat (Customer Chat)**: Enter any additional customer chat information.
* **Estimated Value (Estimated Value)**: Enter the estimated value of the gold item.
* **Actual Value (Actual Value)**: Enter the actual value of the gold item (if available).
* **Difference (Difference)**: Enter the difference between the estimated and actual values (if applicable).
* **Notes (Notes)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the gold item.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete and submit the form.
