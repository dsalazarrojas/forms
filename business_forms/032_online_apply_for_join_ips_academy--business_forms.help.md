# Online Apply For Join Ips Academy - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help individuals apply to join Ips Academy, a platform for knowledge sharing and skill development. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure that you are applying for a role that is relevant to your current position.
2. Fill in the details requested on each page.
3. Submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Apply Now** (`apply_now`, text, optional): Enter a brief introduction, stating why you are interested in joining Ips Academy. This field is optional.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number and email address.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options provided, indicating your preference for a role in Ips Academy.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options that best fit your skills and interests.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available to start in the selected role.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are available to start in the selected role.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information that will help us to understand your application better.
* **Submit Application** (`submit_application`, text, optional): Once you have completed all the fields, click this button to submit your application.
 

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to avoid any issues with submitting your application.
- If you are unsure of any of the options provided, reach out to our team for help.
