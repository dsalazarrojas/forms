# PropTech Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential partners or collaborators in the PropTech industry. We aim to understand your company's details, contact person, and your collaboration goals with us.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as you can.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): A text field where you should enter your company's name.
* **Company Description** (`company_description`): A text field where you can describe your company's background and services offered.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): A text field where you should enter your name as the contact person for your company.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): A text field where you should enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): A text field where you can enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Collaboration Goals** (`collaboration_goals`, `collaboration_goals_2` to `collaboration_goals_23`): A series of text fields where you can describe your collaboration goals with us. Please provide as much detail as possible for each goal.

## Tips
* Ensure that your company's name and contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Be concise and clear in your company description and collaboration goals.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to reach out to us.

Note: Since the form seems to be designed for multiple collaborations goals, it's recommended to review and submit each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
