# Perishable Goods Alliance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Perishable Goods Alliance Registration Form is designed to help businesses establish a partnership with suppliers for the sale and delivery of perishable goods. This form serves as a basic contact information sharing tool to initiate communication between the registered business and the alliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please complete the following steps:

1. Enter your first name in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `Last Name` field.
3. Provide your email address in the `Email` field.
4. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
5. Enter your address in the `Address` field.
6. Enter the name of the city where you reside in the `City` field.
7. Enter your postal code in the `Postal Code` field.
8. Enter the name of the country where you reside in the `Country` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as you would like it to be known in official communications.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as you would like it to be known in official communications.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached for official communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your business or personal address for official deliveries or meetings.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the city where your business or residence is located.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required: false): Enter the postal code or zip code of your business or residence.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the country where your business or residence is located.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are accurate and up-to-date to facilitate efficient communication.
* This form does not require any sensitive or business information, so you do not need to provide that data here.
* If you experience any issues or have questions, please reach out to us for further assistance.
