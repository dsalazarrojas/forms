# Ministry Partnership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ministry Partnership Form is a document used to establish partnerships between ministries and external organizations. It helps to formalize the relationship, outline expectations, and define the scope of work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields, such as expected outcome.
3. Provide accurate and clear information in all fields.
4. Double-check your answers for errors and inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Ministry Organisation** (`ministry_organisation`, text, optional): Enter the name of the ministry organization you're representing.
- **Partner** (`partner`, select_one, optional): Select the type of partnership you're establishing (Partner, Supporter, Collaborator, Supplier, Investor, or Donor).
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the ministry organization.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Enter the date you've signed the partnership.
- **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, date, optional): Enter the date the partnership was reviewed.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Enter your signature.
- **Partner Type** (`partner_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple types of partnership you're establishing (Partner, Supporter, Collaborator, Supplier, Investor, or Donor).
- **Goals** (`goals`, text, optional): Enter the goals of the partnership.
- **Goals Achievement** (`goals_achievement`, text, optional): Enter the expected achievement of the partnership.
- **Expected Outcome** (`expected_outcome`, text, required): Enter the expected outcome of the partnership.
- **Actual Outcome** (`actual_outcome`, text, optional): Enter the actual outcome of the partnership.

## Tips
- Be accurate and clear in your responses to ensure the partnership is well-defined.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor for guidance.
