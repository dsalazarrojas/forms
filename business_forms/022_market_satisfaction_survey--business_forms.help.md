# Market Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience at our store, allowing us to identify areas of improvement and provide better customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all the questions honestly, providing as much detail as possible to help us get a clear picture of your experience.
2. Select one of the options provided for each question, unless otherwise specified.
3. If a question is not applicable to your experience, you may leave the field blank.
4. If you want to provide more information about your experience, use the "Customer Satisfaction Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Support (customer_support, select_multiple, required)**: Please rate the quality of customer support you received during your visit. You can select one or more of the options: very good, good, neutral, bad, very bad.
* **Customer Satisfaction Level (customer_satisfaction_level, number, required)**: Please rate your overall satisfaction with our market on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very bad and 5 is very good.
* **Product Quality Rating (product_quality_rating, number, required)**: Please rate the quality of the products you purchased or experienced during your visit, on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very bad and 5 is very good.
* **Time Spent in Store (time_spent, number, required)**: Please report the amount of time you spent in our store, including the time spent browsing, purchasing, and waiting in line.
* **Overall Satisfaction (overall_satisfaction, select_one, required)**: Please rate your overall satisfaction with our market on a scale of: very good, good, neutral, bad, very bad.
* **Customer Satisfaction Note (customer_satisfaction_note, note, required)**: Please provide any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Store Visit Frequency (store_visit_frequency, select_one, required)**: How often do you visit our store? Please select one of the options: often, occasionally, rarely.
* **Employee Friendliness (employee_friendliness, select_one, required)**: How friendly were our employees during your visit? Please select one of the options: very friendly, friendly, neutral, unfriendly, very unfriendly.
* **Other Notes (other_notes, note, optional)**: Please provide any additional comments or notes about your experience that are not covered by the other questions.
* **Product Price Range Note (product_price_range_note, note, optional)**: If you'd like to provide feedback on the product price range, please add it here.
