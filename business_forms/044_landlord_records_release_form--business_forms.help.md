# landlord_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "landlord_records_release_form" is used to document the release of records from a landlord's records, typically after a tenant has vacated a rental property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields for tenant and landlord information.
2. Select the release authorization and records type from the drop-down menus.
3. Enter the date and signature information as required.
4. Sign and date the form as the landlord and tenant, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tenant_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the tenant who is vacating the property.
* **landlord_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the landlord or property manager responsible for the property.
* **release_authorization** (select multiple, optional): Choose the release authorization option that applies to the records release. Options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **records_type** (select one, optional): Choose the type of records being released. Options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **third_party_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of any third-party individual or organization involved in the records release.
* **date_released** (date, optional): Enter the date the records were released.
* **signature_date** (date, optional): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **tenant_signature** (note, optional): Sign the form as the tenant, if applicable.
* **landlord_signature** (note, optional): Sign the form as the landlord or property manager.
* **landlord_signature_date** (date, optional): Date the landlord or property manager signed the form.

Please note that all required fields must be filled in, and that the signatures should be authentic and legible.
