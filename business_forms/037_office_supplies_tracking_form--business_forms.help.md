# Office Supplies Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Supplies Tracking Form is designed to provide a structured way to track office supplies, allowing for accurate inventory levels, usage, and other relevant metrics. This form will help you monitor your office supplies and optimize your operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the office supplies tracking form with the necessary information for the selected office supplies.
2. Choose the correct inventory levels (e.g., Low, Medium, High) for each office supply from the provided options.
3. Record the usage dates and quantities for each office supply.
4. Evaluate the productivity impact of the office supplies (High, Medium, Low).
5. Provide a brief tracking note for each office supply (optional).
6. If needed, estimate the time spent on manual paperwork reduction.
7. Set the date and time of the form's completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### office_supplies (text)
- **Office Supplies Tracking Form**: Enter the name of the office supply you are tracking.

### inventory_levels (select_multiple)
- **Inventory Levels**: Select from the options provided (Low, Medium, High) to represent the current state of the office supply.

### usage_track (date)
- **Usage Track**: Record the date of the last usage of the office supply.

### wastage_reduction (number)
- **Wastage Reduction**: Estimate the reduction of the office supply due to wastage (e.g., broken, expired, etc.).

### productivity_impact (select_one)
- **Productivity Impact**: Evaluate the impact of the office supply on productivity (High, Medium, Low).

### office_supplies_usage_tracking_note (note)
- **Office Supplies Usage Tracking Note**: Provide a brief note on the usage of the office supply (optional).

### manual_paperwork_reduction (time)
- **Manual Paperwork Reduction**: Estimate the time spent reducing manual paperwork for the office supply.

### office_supplies_tracking_form_completion_date (date)
- **Office Supplies Tracking Form Completion Date**: Set the date and time of the form's completion.

## Tips
- Be accurate when recording usage dates and quantities to ensure accurate tracking.
- Regularly review and update the form to reflect changes in inventory levels and office supplies usage.
- Use the office supplies usage tracking note to provide context and insights into the office supplies usage.
