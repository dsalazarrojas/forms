# Contract Negotiation Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect comprehensive information about your contract negotiation requests, allowing us to assess the needs and requirements of your request.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have a legitimate request that has not been previously submitted.
2.  Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3.  Select the correct options from the dropdown menus carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requestor Name** (Text, Required): Your full name as the requestor.
*   **Requestor Title** (Text, Required): Your job title.
*   **Department** (Select One, Required): Choose the department you are from.
*   **Request Date** (Date, Required): The date of the request.
*   **Contract Type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of contract you are requesting.
*   **Contract Title** (Text, Required): Name of the contract.
*   **Other Party Name** (Text, Required): Name of the other party involved.
*   **Other Party Type** (Select One, Required): Type of entity the other party is.
*   **Estimated Value** (Number, Required): The dollar amount of the contract.
*   **Contract Duration** (Select One, Required): Length of the agreement.
*   **Proposed Start Date** (Date, Required): The planned start date of the contract.
*   **Business Objectives** (Text, Required): The goals of the agreement.
*   **Key Terms** (Text, Required): Important terms to be included in the contract.
*   **Deal Breakers** (Text, Required): Non-negotiable items.
*   **Risk Assessment** (Select One, Required): Risk level of the agreement.
*   **Budget Approved** (Select One, Required): Whether budget is allocated.
*   **Stakeholders** (Text, Required): People who need to be involved in the process.
*   **Previous Relationship** (Select One, Required): Whether you have worked with the other party before.
*   **Legal Review Needed** (Select One, Required): Whether legal involvement is required.
*   **Urgency Level** (Select One, Required): The level of urgency for the agreement.
*   **Additional Information** (Text, Optional): Any other details you wish to include.

Tips:

*   Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid mistakes.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
