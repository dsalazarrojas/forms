# Agent Performance Evaluation Form Ashton Agency - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Performance Evaluation Form is designed to help managers at Ashton Agency assess the skills, knowledge, and achievements of their agents. It's essential to ensure agents receive constructive feedback and guidance for growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Agent Name** field with the agent's name.
2. Enter the **Team Lead Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Select the **Date of Review** when the evaluation was conducted.
4. For each of the competency fields (Local Market Expertise, Negotiation and Closing Skills, Responsiveness and Communication Speed, and Integrity and Professional Ethics), select the level of proficiency you've observed in the agent.
5. Enter the **Total Sales Volume for the Quarter** and **Number of New Listings Secured** in their respective fields.
6. Provide a **Recommendation for Promotion or Bonus** based on your evaluation.
7. Optionally, add any **Manager's Summative Comments** and **Agent's Self-Reflection** in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agent Name**: Enter the name of the agent being evaluated.
* **Team Lead Name**: Enter the name of the team lead who conducted the evaluation.
* **Date of Review**: Select the date when the evaluation was conducted.
* **Local Market Expertise**: Assess the agent's knowledge of local market trends and property values. Choose from:
	+ Expert: Demonstrated exceptional knowledge and understanding of the market.
	+ Proficient: Shows a good understanding, but may need further education.
	+ Developing: Has some knowledge, but needs improvement.
	+ Needs Support: Lacks understanding of the market.
* **Negotiation and Closing Skills**: Evaluate the agent's ability to finalize deals effectively. Choose from:
	+ Advanced: Demonstrated exceptional closing skills.
	+ Capable: Shows good closing skills, but may need further development.
	+ Basic: Has some closing skills, but needs improvement.
	+ Ineffective: Lacks closing skills.
* **Responsiveness and Communication Speed**: Assess the agent's turnaround time for clients. Choose from:
	+ Always Immediate: Responds promptly to client requests.
	+ Usually Prompt: Responds quickly to client requests, but may take some time.
	+ Sometimes Delayed: Responds with some delay.
	+ Consistently Slow: Responds slowly or rarely.
* **Integrity and Professional Ethics**: Evaluate the agent's adherence to agency values. Choose from:
	+ Beyond Reproach: Exhibited exceptional integrity and adherence to agency values.
	+ Ethical: Exhibited good ethics and values.
	+ Questionable: May have some ethics concerns.
	+ Major Concerns: Lacks ethics and values.
* **Total Sales Volume for the Quarter**: Enter the agent's gross sales value for the quarter.
* **Number of New Listings Secured**: Enter the total number of new listings secured by the agent.
* **Recommendation for Promotion or Bonus**: Provide a recommendation based on your evaluation. Choose from:
	+ Highly Recommend: Strongly recommend the agent for promotion or bonus.
	+ Recommend: Recommend the agent for promotion or bonus.
	+ Neutral: Neither recommend nor not recommend.
	+ Do Not Recommend: Do not recommend for promotion or bonus.
* **Manager's Summative Comments**: Add any final thoughts on the agent's performance.
* **Agent's Self-Reflection**: Encourage the agent to provide feedback on their own performance.
