# Controller Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Controller Application Form is designed to gather information from applicants for a controller position. This form will help us evaluate your qualifications, experience, and interests to determine your fit for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question truthfully and completely.
2. Be sure to attach any required documents, such as your resume and cover letter.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, Text, **Required**): Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, **Required**): Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, Text, **Required**): Enter your best contact number for communication.
* **Address** (`address`, Text, **Required**): Enter your full mailing address.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_title`, Select One, **Required**): Select the job title you're applying for. Options include Financial Controller, Assistant Controller, Division Controller, Plant Controller, Corporate Controller, and Other.
* **Department** (`department`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the department you're applying to.
* **Preferred Location** (`location_preference`, Text, **Optional**): Enter your preferred work location.
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, Date, **Required**): Enter the date you can start work.
* **Expected Salary** (`salary_expectations`, Text, **Optional**): Enter your expected annual salary.
* **Highest Degree Earned** (`highest_degree`, Select One, **Required**): Select the highest degree you've earned. Options include High School Diploma, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, Master Degree, MBA, PhD, and Other.
* **Major or Field of Study** (`major`, Text, **Required**): Enter your major or field of study.
* **University or Institution** (`university`, Text, **Required**): Enter the name of your university or institution.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, Number, **Optional**): Enter the year you graduated.
* **Are you a CPA** (`cpa_certified`, Select One, **Required**): Select whether you're a certified public accountant.
* **Are you a CMA** (`cma_certified`, Select One, **Required**): Select whether you're a certified management accountant.
* **Are you a CIA** (`cia_certified`, Select One, **Required**): Select whether you're a certified internal auditor.
* **Other Certifications** (`other_certifications`, Text, **Optional**): Enter any additional certifications you hold.
* **Years of Relevant Experience** (`years_experience`, Number, **Required**): Enter the years of experience in accounting or finance.
* **Current Employer** (`current_employer`, Text, **Optional**): Enter your current employer's name.
* **Current Job Title** (`current_title`, Text, **Optional**): Enter your current job title.
* **Have you been a Controller Before** (`previous_controller_experience`, Select One, **Required**): Select whether you have previous experience as a controller.
* **Industry Experience** (`industry_experience`, Select Multiple, **Required**): Select all industries you have experience in. Options include Manufacturing, Technology, Healthcare, Financial Services, Retail, Government, Non-Profit, and Other.
* **Accounting Software Proficiency** (`accounting_software`, Select Multiple, **Required**): Select all accounting software you're proficient in. Options include QuickBooks, SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Xero, Sage, Microsoft Dynamics, and Other.
* **ERP Systems Experience** (`erp_systems`, Text, **Optional**): Enter your experience with ERP systems.
* **Microsoft Excel Proficiency** (`excel_proficiency`, Select One, **Required**): Select your level of proficiency in Microsoft Excel. Options include Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced.
* **Years of Management Experience** (`management_experience`, Number, **Optional**): Enter the years of management experience leading teams.
* **Largest Team Size Managed** (`team_size`, Number, **Optional**): Enter the number of direct and indirect reports you've managed.
* **Experience with Financial Reporting** (`financial_reporting`, Select One, **Required**): Select your level of experience with financial reporting. Options include Extensive, Moderate, Limited, and None.
* **Experience with Budget Management** (`budget_management`, Select One, **Required**): Select your level of experience with budget management. Options include Extensive, Moderate, Limited, and None.
* **Experience Coordinating Audits** (`audit_coordination`, Select One, **Required**): Select your level of experience coordinating audits. Options include Extensive, Moderate, Limited, and None.
* **Experience with Tax Compliance** (`tax_compliance`, Select One, **Required**): Select your level of experience with tax compliance. Options include Extensive, Moderate, Limited, and None.
* **Reference 1 Name** (`reference_1_name`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the name of your professional reference.
* **Reference 1 Contact** (`reference_1_contact`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the contact information for your professional reference.
* **Reference 2 Name** (`reference_2_name`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the name of your second professional reference.
* **Reference 2 Contact** (`reference_2_contact`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the contact information for your second professional reference.
* **Why are you interested in this position** (`why_interested`, Text, **Optional**): Enter why you're interested in the position.
* **Key Strengths** (`key_strengths`, Text, **Optional**): Enter your key strengths.
* **Resume Attached** (`resume_attached`, Select One, **Required**): Select whether you've attached your resume.
* **Cover Letter Attached** (`cover_letter_attached`, Select One, **Optional**): Select whether you've attached your cover letter.
* **How did you hear about this position** (`how_did_you_hear`, Select One, **Optional**): Select how you found out about this position.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, **Optional**): Enter any additional comments you'd like to share.

Note: This form is subject to change, and additional fields may be added or removed. Please contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
