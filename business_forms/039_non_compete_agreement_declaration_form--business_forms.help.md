# Non Compete Agreement Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees regarding their non-compete agreements. Non-compete agreements are legally binding contracts that prevent employees from working for competitors or starting their own businesses in the same industry. This form helps ensure that employees understand their obligations and responsibilities under these agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name and employee ID**: On page 1, please enter your name and employee ID.
2. Complete the **Agreement Details** field with a clear and concise description of your non-compete agreement. This should include the date and details of the agreement.
3. Sign the **Signature** field by printing your name and date.
4. Select from the options in the **Non Compete Agreement** field to indicate which non-compete agreement you are currently under.
5. Finally, sign the form by printing your name in the **Employee Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details** (Page 1): Enter your name and employee ID in this field.
* **Agreement Details** (Page 2): Provide a clear and concise description of your non-compete agreement, including the date and details of the agreement.
* **Signature** (Page 2): Print your name and date in this field to sign the agreement.
* **Non Compete Agreement** (Page 3): Select from the options to indicate which non-compete agreement you are currently under.
* **Date** (Page 4): This field is automatically populated with the current date. However, please ensure that the date of your non-compete agreement is entered correctly.
* **Non Compete Agreement** (Page 5): This field allows you to select from the options to indicate which non-compete agreement you are currently under. Please choose the option that best describes your situation.
* **Employee Signature** (Page 5): Print your name in this field to sign the agreement.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have reviewed and understood your non-compete agreement before completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please consult with your HR representative or the relevant authorities.
* Please complete the form accurately and honestly, as it is a legally binding document.
