# Corporate Minutes Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document the details of business meetings, including meeting date, time, location, attendees, and outcomes. It will help management and team members track and record meeting information in a systematic and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the meeting date and time in the respective fields.
2. Select the meeting location from the options provided.
3. Choose whether each attendee attended the meeting by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Meeting Attendees" field.
4. Type in the meeting topic and outcome.
5. Record any relevant actions taken as a result of the meeting in the "Meeting Actions" field.
6. Provide a brief summary of the meeting in the "Meeting Notes" field.
7. Attach any relevant documents or materials related to the meeting in the "Meeting Attachments" field.
8. Indicate whether the meeting decisions were taken by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Meeting Decisions" field.
9. Select the current status of the meeting from the options provided in the "Meeting Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required: no): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required: no): Enter the time of the meeting in 24-hour format (e.g., 13:00).
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, required: no): Type in the physical or virtual location where the meeting took place.
* **Meeting Attendees** (`meeting_attendees`, select_multiple, required: no): Check "Yes" for each attendee who was present.
* **Meeting Topic** (`meeting_topic`, text, required: no): Briefly describe the main topic discussed during the meeting.
* **Meeting Outcome** (`meeting_outcome`, text, required: no): Summarize the outcome or results of the meeting.
* **Meeting Actions** (`meeting_actions`, text, required: no): Describe any actions taken as a result of the meeting.
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`, note, required: no): Provide a brief summary of the meeting.
* **Meeting Attachments** (`meeting_attachments`, text, required: no): Attach any relevant documents or materials related to the meeting.
* **Meeting Decisions** (`meeting_decisions`, select_multiple, required: no): Check "Yes" for each decision taken during the meeting.
* **Meeting Status** (`meeting_status`, select_one, required: no): Select the current status of the meeting from the options provided.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use the correct format for time (24-hour format).
* Be concise and clear in your entries to ensure accurate and easy reference.
* Attach only relevant and necessary attachments.
* Review and update the form regularly to maintain accurate and up-to-date records.
