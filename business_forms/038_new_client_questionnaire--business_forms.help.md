# New Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Client Questionnaire is designed to collect relevant information about new clients, including personal and business details. This form helps us better understand their preferences and needs, enabling us to provide tailored services and improve our communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Enter your Address for our records.
4. Choose your preferred contact method (Contact Form, Email, Phone, or Mail).
5. If you prefer to contact us through an alternative method, specify it in the "Other (please specify)" field.
6. Enter your Date of Birth (if applicable).
7. Indicate the Time of Contact (if applicable).
8. If you are representing a business, provide the Business Name and Address.
9. Enter the Business Phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address for secure communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for easy contact.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for our records.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Contact Form, Email, Phone, or Mail).
* **Other (please specify)** (`preferred_contact_method_other`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted through an alternative method, specify it here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your Date of Birth (if applicable).
* **Time of Contact** (`time_of_contact`, time, optional): Indicate the Time of Contact (if applicable).
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): If you are representing a business, enter its name.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the business you represent.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the business you represent.
