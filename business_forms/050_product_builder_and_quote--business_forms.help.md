# product_builder_and_quote - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users quickly generate a product quote for their customers, streamlining the sales process and improving customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product you wish to quote from the "Choose Product" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the quantity of the product you're interested in purchasing.
3. Enter the name of the product you're quoting.
4. Enter the price of the product.
5. Enter the customer's name.
6. Enter the customer's email address.
7. Enter the customer's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Choose Product** (`product`, select_one, optional): Select the product you wish to quote from the available options.
  - This field allows you to choose the product for which you're generating a quote.
- **Choose Quantity** (`quantity`, select_one, optional): Choose the quantity of the product you're interested in purchasing.
  - This field helps you select the number of units you want to quote.
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product you're quoting.
  - Provide the name of the product for the quote.
- **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the product.
  - Enter the price of the product to be quoted.
- **Quote** (`quote`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or notes about the quote.
  - This field is for any additional information or comments about the quote.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name.
  - Enter the customer's name for the quote.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
  - Provide the customer's email address for communication purposes.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
  - Enter the customer's phone number for further communication.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately to ensure a complete quote is generated.
- Double-check the product and customer details before submitting the form.
- Review the quote before sharing it with the customer to ensure accuracy and professionalism.
