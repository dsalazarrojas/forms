# Business Promotion Flyer Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by businesses to submit an inquiry for a custom promotion flyer. It gathers information about the type of flyer needed, distribution location, target audience, and other requirements to create a tailored promotion that meets their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the inquiry date.
2. Provide your name and contact information.
3. Choose the type of flyer you need.
4. Enter your company name and website (if applicable).
5. Select the distribution location.
6. Identify your target audience.
7. Estimate the number of copies needed.
8. Enter the main message of your promotion.
9. (Optional) Enter any special offers or deals.
10. Specify the required design elements.
11. Provide the campaign timeline.
12. (Optional) Enter the campaign duration.
13. Choose a budget range.
14. (Optional) Provide information about previous marketing campaigns.
15. Set an expected response rate goal.
16. Add any additional details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inquiry Date (id: 1)**: Date of this inquiry.
* **Your Name (id: 2)**: Full name.
* **Email Address (id: 3)**: Contact email.
* **Phone Number (id: 4)**: Contact phone.
* **Company Name (id: 5)**: Business name.
* **Company Website (id: 6)**: URL (optional).
* **Type of Flyer Needed (id: 7)**: Choose the type of flyer you need (e.g., Product Promotion, Service Promotion, etc.).
* **Distribution Location (id: 8)**: Where will the flyer be distributed.
* **Target Audience (id: 9)**: Who is this for?.
* **Estimated Quantity (id: 10)**: How many copies are needed.
* **Main Message (id: 11)**: Main message of the promotion.
* **Special Offers or Deals (id: 12)**: (Optional) Enter any special offers or deals.
* **Required Design Elements (id: 13)**: (Optional) Logo, images, colors, etc.
* **Campaign Timeline (id: 14)**: When should the flyer be ready.
* **Campaign Duration (id: 15)**: (Optional) How long will the campaign run.
* **Budget Range (id: 16)**: Choose a budget range.
* **Previous Marketing Campaigns (id: 17)**: (Optional) What campaigns have you run.
* **Expected Response Rate Goal (id: 18)**: Target response rate.
* **Additional Details (id: 19)**: Any other information.

Please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly to ensure that your promotion flyer needs are met. If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out.
