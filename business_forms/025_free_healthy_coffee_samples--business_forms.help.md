# Free Healthy Coffee Samples - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about free healthy coffee samples.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Form Title` field with a brief title that summarizes your experience with free healthy coffee samples.
2. Provide the `Category` in which your experience falls. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
3. In the `Description` field, briefly describe your experience with free healthy coffee samples.
4. If you had to change your habits or behaviors due to your experience, please type 'yes' in the `No Need to Change Habits` field.
5. If you have a safe alternative, please type 'yes' in the `Safe Alternative` field.
6. If the coffee industry showed resilience during your experience, type 'yes' in the `Coffee Industry Resilient` field.
7. Type 'WOW' in the `WOW` field if your experience was exceptional or surprising.
8. In the `Output File` field, you can type anything you'd like, but please be aware that this field is not actually used in this form.
9. In the `Assigned Tool` field, you can type anything you'd like, but please be aware that this field is not actually used in this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: This is the title of your experience with free healthy coffee samples. Please keep it brief.
* **Category**: This is the category in which your experience falls. Please select the category that best describes it.
* **Description**: Briefly describe your experience with free healthy coffee samples.
* **No Need to Change Habits**: If you didn't need to change your habits or behaviors due to your experience, type 'yes'.
* **Safe Alternative**: If you found a safe alternative to what you were looking for, type 'yes'.
* **Coffee Industry Resilient**: If the coffee industry showed resilience during your experience, type 'yes'.
* **WOW**: Type 'WOW' if your experience was exceptional or surprising.
* **Output File**: This field is not actually used in this form, but you can type anything you'd like if you need to.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is not actually used in this form, but you can type anything you'd like if you need to.

## Tips
* Please be as concise as possible when filling in the fields.
* Please do not duplicate your response if you've already submitted this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
