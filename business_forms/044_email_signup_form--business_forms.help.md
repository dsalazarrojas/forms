# Email-Signup-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and preferences from potential customers or leads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
*   Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
*   Select your lead type from the "Lead Type" dropdown menu.
*   Indicate your consent to receive email marketing materials in the "Consent to Email Marketing" dropdown menu.
*   If desired, enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
*   Fill in any other custom fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact name and phone number.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and news from the company.
*   **Lead Type** (`company_lead_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you wish to be contacted (e.g., Newsletter, Product promotion, Other).
*   **Consent to Email Marketing** (`consent_to_email_marketing`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you consent to receive email marketing materials from the company.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your preferences.
*   **Custom Field 1** (`custom_field_1`, text, optional): Fill in any additional information for this custom field.
*   **Custom Field 2** (`custom_field_2`, text, optional): Fill in any additional information for this custom field.
*   **Custom Field 3** (`custom_field_3`, text, optional): Fill in any additional information for this custom field.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your information is accurately captured.
*   Be as detailed as possible in the "Comments" field to provide context for your preferences.
*   If you have any issues or questions, contact the form administrator for assistance.
