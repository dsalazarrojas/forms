# LVCC Dog Training Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for assessing a dog's training needs, including training level, breed, age, and training schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, and provide accurate information about your dog's training needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Training Level (id: 2)**: Select your dog's training level from the options: Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced.
- **Dog Breed (id: 3)**: Choose all applicable breeds that your dog belongs to from the options: Poodle, Labrador, Golden Retriever, Bulldog, Boxer, or Dachshund.
- **Dog Age (id: 4)**: Enter your dog's age in numbers.
- **Trainer Name (id: 5)**: Enter your name as the trainer or the name of the person training the dog.
- **Training Days (id: 6)**: Enter the number of days you will be training your dog.
- **Training Frequency (id: 7)**: Select the frequency of your training schedule from the options: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
- **Training Start Date (id: 8)**: Enter the date you plan to start training your dog.
- **Training End Date (id: 9)**: Enter the date you plan to stop training your dog.
- **Training Time (id: 10)**: Enter the time of day you plan to train your dog.
- **Training Location (id: 11)**: Enter the location where you will be training your dog.
- **Additional Comments (id: 12)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your training requirements.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check the dates and times entered for each field. 
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact your supervisor or support team.
