# ImportExportPermitReleaseForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ImportExportPermitReleaseForm" is designed to gather information necessary for import/export permit releases, which involves coordinating and documenting the release of goods from one country to another. This form helps ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and facilitates smooth international trade operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Business Name**, which should be the name of your company or organization.
2. Enter your **Contact Person**'s name and contact information.
3. Provide your **Email** address for correspondence and communication.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** for further communication and verification purposes.
5. Specify your **Address** where the goods will be imported or exported from.
6. Provide detailed **Permit Details**, which should include all necessary information related to the permit release.
7. Describe the **Goods Description**, which should include a brief description of the goods being released.
8. Enter the **Goods Value**, including the monetary value of the goods.
9. Choose the **Export Date** for the goods release.
10. Identify the **Release Person**, who will be responsible for the permit release.
11. Choose the **Certification** status for the goods, which may vary depending on the regulatory requirements.
12. Add any **Notes** if required or necessary.
13. Select the **Business Type** of your organization.
14. Enter the **Permit Number**, which is a unique identifier for the permit release.
15. Specify the **Goods Type**, which should match the description provided.
16. Enter the **Export Location**, where the goods will be exported from.
17. Enter the **Import Location**, where the goods will be imported to.
18. Enter the **Export Country**, where the goods will be exported from.
19. Enter the **Import Country**, where the goods will be imported to.
20. Enter the **Goods Weight**, which is the total weight of the goods being released.
21. Add **Remarks** or additional comments if necessary.
22. Choose the **Permit Status**, which indicates the current status of the permit release.
23. Select the **Permit Type**, which depends on the type of permit required.
24. Enter the second **Release Person**, who will be co-signing the permit release.
25. Enter the third **Release Person**, who will be co-signing the permit release.
