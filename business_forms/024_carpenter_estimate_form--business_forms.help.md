# Carpenter Estimate Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Carpenter Estimate Form is a tool used to collect information about a carpentry project. This form will help the carpenter understand the project's requirements and provide an accurate estimate for the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form correctly, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the customer's contact information (Customer Name, Email Address, Phone Number).
2. Provide the job location (Job Location Address) where the work will be performed.
3. Choose the type of carpentry work required (Type of Carpentry Work).
4. Enter a detailed description of the job or project (Description of the job or project).
5. Provide an approximate measurement of the project's dimensions (Approximate Dimensions and Measurements).
6. Choose the preferred material type (Preferred Type of Wood or Materials).
7. Select the desired finish for the project (Desired Finish).
8. Specify the site conditions (Where will the work be done?).
9. If necessary, provide information about any access or structural issues (Any access or structural issues?).
10. Indicate if waste removal is required (Will waste removal be required?).
11. Estimate the project's budget (Estimated Budget for the project).
12. Choose the currency for the estimate (Currency).
13. Specify how you found out about the carpenter's services (How did you hear about my carpentry services?).
14. Confirm if you have provided photos of the work area (Have you provided photos of the work area?).
15. Acknowledge that this is an estimate and not a final quote (I understand this is only an estimate and not a final quote).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Customer Name, text, Required): Enter the customer's full name.
* **Email Address** (Customer Email, email, Required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone Number** (Customer Phone, text, Required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Job Location Address** (Job Address, text, Required): Enter the address where the work will be performed.
* **Type of Carpentry Work** (Carpentry Type, select_one, Required): Select the primary category of carpentry work required.
* **Description of the job or project** (Job Description, text, Required): Enter a detailed description of the job or project.
* **Approximate Dimensions and Measurements** (Dimensions, text, Required): Enter the approximate dimensions and measurements of the project.
* **Preferred Type of Wood or Materials** (Preferred Materials, text, Optional): Enter the preferred type of wood or materials to be used.
* **Desired Finish** (Desired Finish, select_one, Required): Select the desired finish for the project.
* **Where will the work be done?** (Work Environment, select_one, Required): Choose where the work will be performed (Indoor, Outdoor, Workshop, Multi-site).
* **Any access or structural issues?** (Structural Access, text, Optional): Provide information about any access or structural issues.
* **Will waste removal be required?** (Waste Removal, select_one, Required): Indicate if waste removal is required.
* **Estimated Budget for the project** (Estimated Budget, number, Optional): Enter the estimated budget for the project.
* **Currency** (Currency, select_one, Required): Choose the currency for the estimate (USD, GBP, CAD, AUD, EUR).
* **How did you hear about my carpentry services?** (Source Referral, text, Optional): Specify how you found out about the carpenter's services.
* **Have you provided photos of the work area?** (Photo Upload, select_one, Required): Confirm if photos of the work area have been provided.
* **I understand this is only an estimate and not a final quote** (Estimate Acknowledgment, select_one, Required): Acknowledge that this is only an estimate and not a final quote.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to ensure a reliable estimate.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the carpenter for clarification.
* Ensure you have provided photos of the work area to help with the estimation.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to the carpenter.
