# Quotation Request Form (Construction Supplier) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request quotes from construction suppliers for materials and services related to a construction project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "Required".
2. Select all applicable materials and services from the provided options.
3. Enter the requested date and time for the quote.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Construction Supplier** (1): This field is used to enter the name of the construction supplier you are requesting a quote from.
* **Materials** (2): Select all the materials you are interested in obtaining for your project.
* **Construction Services** (3): Choose one or more construction services you need from the provided options.
* **Requested Date** (4): Enter the date you need the materials and services by.
* **Requested Time** (5): Enter the specific time you need the materials and services by.
* **Email** (6): Enter your email address for the supplier to contact you.
* **Phone** (7): Enter your phone number for the supplier to contact you.

## Tips
* Double-check that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Make sure to select all relevant materials and services to ensure the supplier provides a comprehensive quote.
* Be specific with your requested date and time to avoid misunderstandings.
* Proofread your email and phone number for accuracy.
