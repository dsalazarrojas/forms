# equipment_calibration_partner_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register a partner that provides equipment calibration services. This form is used for internal purposes only and is not intended for public access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your Email and Phone Number in the "Email" and "Phone Number" fields.
3. Provide your Company Name in the "Company Name" field.
4. Select your Business Category from the dropdown menu in the "Business Category" field. This should match the category of services your company provides.
5. Enter your Company Website in the "Website" field.
6. Provide your Location in the "Location" field.
7. Add any additional information about yourself/your company in the "Description" field. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the surname.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Business Category** (`business_category`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the category of services you provide from the dropdown menu. Options are:
	+ Equipment Calibration Services
	+ Calibration Services
	+ Testing and Inspection
	+ Other
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company website.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company location.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional information about yourself/your company.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your partner information is complete and up-to-date.
* You can leave optional fields blank if not applicable.
