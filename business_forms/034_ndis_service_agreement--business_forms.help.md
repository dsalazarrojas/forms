# ndis-service-agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ndis-service-agreement" form is used to document the service agreement between the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) participants and their service providers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand the fields and sections.
2. Enter the **Service Agreement Title** to describe the purpose and scope of the agreement.
3. Enter the **Participant Name**, **Service Provider Name**, and **Service Provider Organisation** to identify the parties involved.
4. Provide a brief **Service Description** to outline the services offered.
5. Enter the **Funding Start Date** and **Funding End Date** to record the funding period.
6. Enter any **Participant Concerns** and **Participant Goals** to understand the participant's needs and expectations.
7. Select the **Participant Communication Preference** (Email, Phone, SMS, or In-person) and **Participant Communication Frequency** (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Fortnightly, or Rarely) for the participant.
8. Similarly, select the **Service Provider Communication Preference** and **Service Provider Communication Frequency** for the service provider.
9. Enter the **Participant Support Plan** to outline the support arrangements.
10. Provide the **Service Provider Address**, **Participant Phone**, **Service Provider Phone**, **Participant Email**, and **Service Provider Email** to facilitate communication.
11. Add any **Participant Notes** and **Service Provider Notes** to record important points.
12. Collect **Participant Signature** and **Service Provider Signature** (optional).
13. Enter the **NDIS Service Provider Registration Number** and **NDIS Funding Amount** as required.
14. Review and submit the form once all fields are complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Agreement Title**: A brief title to describe the purpose and scope of the agreement.
* **Participant Name**: The name of the participant in the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS).
* **Service Provider Name**: The name of the service provider.
* **Service Provider Organisation**: The name of the service provider organisation.
* **Service Description**: A brief description of the services offered.
* **Funding Start Date**: The date the funding for the services begins.
* **Funding End Date**: The date the funding for the services ends.
* **Participant Concerns**: Any concerns or issues the participant wants to raise.
* **Participant Goals**: The participant's goals and expectations for the services.
* **Participant Communication Preference**: The preferred method of communication (Email, Phone, SMS, or In-person) for the participant.
* **Participant Communication Frequency**: The frequency of communication (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Fortnightly, or Rarely) for the participant.
* **Service Provider Communication Preference**: The preferred method of communication (Email, Phone, SMS, or In-person) for the service provider.
* **Service Provider Communication Frequency**: The frequency of communication (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Fortnightly, or Rarely) for the service provider.
* **Participant Support Plan**: A plan outlining the support arrangements for the participant.
* **Service Provider Address**: The address of the service provider.
* **Participant Phone**: The phone number of the participant.
* **Service Provider Phone**: The phone number of the service provider.
* **Participant Email**: The email address of the participant.
* **Service Provider Email**: The email address of the service provider.
* **Participant Notes**: Any additional notes for the participant.
* **Service Provider Notes**: Any additional notes for the service provider.
* **Participant Signature**: The participant's signature (optional).
* **Service Provider Signature**: The service provider's signature (optional).
* **NDIS Service Provider Registration Number**: The registration number of the service provider with the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS).
* **NDIS Funding Amount**: The amount of funding allocated for the services.
