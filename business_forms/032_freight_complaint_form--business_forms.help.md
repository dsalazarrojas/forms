# Freight Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freight Complaint Form is used to collect information from customers about issues they've experienced with their shipping. This form helps us to gather details about the problem, the shipping company involved, and any other relevant information that will help us to resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please enter a brief description of the issue you've experienced with your shipping in the "shipping_issue" field.
2.  Enter the name of the shipping company that was involved in the issue in the "shipping_company" field.
3.  Provide your contact information in the "contact_info" field.
4.  Enter the date of the shipping that was involved in the issue in the "shipping_date" field. Please use the date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
5.  Enter the time of the shipping that was involved in the issue in the "shipping_time" field. Please use the format "HH:MM:SS".
6.  Enter the shipping address that was involved in the issue in the "shipping_address" field.
7.  Describe the issue you experienced in more detail in the "issue_description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **shipping_issue** (text, required): A brief description of the issue you've experienced with your shipping.
* **shipping_company** (text, required): The name of the shipping company that was involved in the issue.
* **contact_info** (text, optional): Your contact information, including your name and any relevant contact details.
* **shipping_date** (date, required): The date of the shipping that was involved in the issue. Please use the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **shipping_time** (time, required): The time of the shipping that was involved in the issue. Please use the format "HH:MM:SS".
* **shipping_address** (text, optional): The shipping address that was involved in the issue.
* **issue_description** (text, required): A detailed description of the issue you experienced.
