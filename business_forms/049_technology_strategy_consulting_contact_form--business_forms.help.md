# Technology Strategy Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect contact information from potential clients or partners interested in technology strategy consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name (if you wish to be contacted by name).
2. Enter your Email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
3. If you have an organization, please provide it. This will help us to better understand your context.
4. Select one or more reasons why you are interested in technology strategy consulting (e.g., IT strategy development, Digital transformation, Technology roadmaps).
5. If you have specific technologies of interest (e.g., cloud services, data analytics, cybersecurity), select them from the list.
6. Provide a brief description of your Technology Challenge.
7. Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone or email).
8. If you have a company, please provide it. This will help us to better understand your context.
9. If you have a Job Title, please provide it. This will help us to better understand your context.
10. If you have a Contact Number, please provide it. This will help us to better understand your contact method.
11. If you have a Company Address, please provide it. This will help us to better understand your location.
12. Finally, select your Job Function. If you don't know, you can leave this blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name, which will be used to personalize the communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: true): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: false): If you have an organization, please provide it to help us better understand your context.
* **Contact Reason** (`contact_reason`, select_multiple, required: true): Select one or more reasons why you are interested in technology strategy consulting. This will help us to better understand your needs.
* **Technologies of Interest** (`technology_interests`, select_multiple, required: false): If you have specific technologies that interest you (e.g., cloud services, data analytics, cybersecurity), select them from the list.
* **Technology Challenge** (`technology_challenge`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your technology challenge or problem.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required: true): Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone or email).
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): If you have a company, please provide it to help us better understand your context.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): If you have a job title, please provide it to help us better understand your position.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): If you have a contact number, please provide it to help us better understand your contact method.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required: false): If you have a company address, please provide it to help us better understand your location.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, required: false): If you have a job function, please provide it to help us better understand your role.

## Tips
