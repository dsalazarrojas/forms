# Service Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers about their service consultation experiences. It's essential to provide accurate and complete answers to each question to ensure that the business can serve you better.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you fill out all the required fields (marked as *required*).
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that they are correct.
4. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information that you'd like to share about your experience with our service.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This field is for your contact phone number, which will help us get in touch with you for further consultation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you regarding your consultation.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your experience with our service in more detail.
* **Select Option 1** (`selected_option_1`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options provided to help us understand your preference.
* **Select Option 2** (`selected_option_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the options provided to help us understand your preference.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your consultation, if applicable.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your consultation, if applicable.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Use this field to add any additional notes about your consultation.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Please select one of the categories to help us understand the type of service you're inquiring about.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options to submit your form.
* **Form Submit** is a required field but it's not a field to fill in, you should select one of the options to submit your form.
 
This form is designed to be completed once, so please make sure to provide accurate and detailed information in each field. If you have any doubts or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
