# Complaint Resolution Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Complaint Resolution Acknowledgment Form is used to confirm that a complaint has been fully resolved and to gather feedback on the resolution process. This form is a way to ensure that the issue has been fully addressed and that all parties are satisfied with the outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you are filling it out for the correct purpose.
2. Enter the original complaint ID/reference number in the "Original Complaint ID / Reference Number" field.
3. Provide your name as the person who filed the initial complaint in the "Name of Complainant" field.
4. Enter the date when the final resolution was communicated to you in the "Date of Resolution" field.
5. Provide a brief summary of what was done to resolve the issue in the "Summary of Resolution Provided" field.
6. Select one of the options for "Do you accept this resolution as a final settlement?" to indicate your acceptance or request for further action.
7. Rate your satisfaction with the resolution process using the scale in the "How satisfied were you with the resolution process?" field.
8. If a representative handled your case, enter their name in the "Name of Representative who handled your case" field (if applicable).
9. If you have any remaining concerns or additional feedback, type them in the "Any remaining concerns or additional feedback?" field.
10. Select today's date in the "Date Signed" field.
11. Finally, review the form again and ensure that you are comfortable with the accuracy of the information provided. If you are satisfied, click the "Submit" button to finalize the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Resolution Acknowledgment and Feedback --**: This is a header for the form, indicating that it's about acknowledging the resolution and providing feedback.
* **Original Complaint ID / Reference Number**: Enter the unique ID or reference number of the initial complaint.
* **Name of Complainant**: Enter your name as the person who filed the initial complaint.
* **Date of Resolution**: Enter the date when the final resolution was communicated to you.
* **Summary of Resolution Provided**: Briefly describe what was done to resolve the issue.
* **Do you accept this resolution as a final settlement?**: Select one of the three options to indicate your acceptance or request for further action.
* **How satisfied were you with the resolution process?**: Rate your satisfaction with the resolution process using the provided scale.
* **Name of Representative who handled your case**: If a representative helped you, enter their name here (if applicable).
* **Any remaining concerns or additional feedback?**: Type any remaining concerns or additional feedback you have.
* **Date Signed**: Select today's date.
* **Digital Signature**: This is a note indicating that by submitting the form, you acknowledge the terms.

Tips:
* Ensure that you are filling out this form accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any concerns or questions, reach out to the representative or contact the organization for assistance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
