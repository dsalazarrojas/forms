# Corporate Culture Hackathon Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Culture Hackathon is a platform where employees can submit their ideas to enhance the company culture. The form is designed to gather relevant information about the applicants and their ideas. Please ensure you have a legitimate reason for submitting an idea to the hackathon. If you're unsure, refer to your company's internal guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Application Form with your personal and contact information.
2. Provide information about yourself in the Participant Information section, including any relevant details about your role in the company.
3. Submit your idea in the Idea Submission section, providing a clear and concise description of your concept.
4. Evaluate your idea in the Evaluation Form section, rating it based on the provided criteria.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Please fill out this section with your full name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide information about yourself, including your job title, department, and any other relevant details about your role in the company.
* **Idea Submission** (`idea_submission`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your idea in a clear and concise manner, including the problem you're trying to solve and the proposed solution.
* **Evaluation Form** (`evaluation_form`, `text`, required/optional): Rate your idea based on the provided criteria, such as creativity, feasibility, and impact.

## Tips
* Ensure you have a clear understanding of your idea before submitting it.
* Be innovative and think outside the box.
* Review the submitted ideas and provide constructive feedback to help refine them.
