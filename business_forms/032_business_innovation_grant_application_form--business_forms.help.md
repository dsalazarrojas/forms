# Business Innovation Grant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to help you apply for a business grant to support innovative projects. The form is divided into several sections to guide you through the necessary information required for consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by providing the required information in each section.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed, as indicated by the "Required" label.
3. Be as thorough as possible when providing information, as it will help the reviewers better understand your project.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all necessary fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Applicant Name** (`lead_applicant_full_name_grant`, text, Required): The primary point of contact for this application.
* **Business Legal Name** (`business_legal_name_grant_app`, text, Required): The registered name of the business or entity.
* **Grant Category** (`grant_category_selection_type`, select_one, Required): Select the category most relevant to your project (Sustainable Technology, Digital Transformation, Community Impact / Social Innovation, Product R&D, Market Expansion / Internationalization).
* **Project Title** (`project_title_grant_app`, text, Required): A concise name for your innovation project.
* **Problem Statement** (`problem_statement_innovation_grant`, text, Required): Describe the specific challenge that this project addresses.
* **Proposed Innovation Description** (`proposed_innovation_desc_grant`, text, Required): Explain your solution and why it is innovative.
* **Expected Impact Areas** (`expected_impact_metrics_selection`, select_multiple, Required): Select all areas that apply (Increased Revenue, Job Creation, Carbon Footprint Reduction, Improved Operational Efficiency, New Market Access).
* **Project Timeline (Months)** (`project_timeline_months_estimate`, number, Required): The total duration from start to completion.
* **Total Budget Request (USD)** (`total_budget_request_grant_amount`, number, Required): The grant amount you are applying for.
* **Matching Funds Availability Status** (`matching_funds_availability_status`, select_one, Required): Indicate the percentage of matching funds available (Yes, 50% or more, Yes, 25% - 49%, Yes, less than 25%, No matching funds available).
* **Core Team Qualifications** (`core_team_qualifications_summary`, text, Required): Briefly describe the expertise of key personnel.
* **Sustainability Plan** (`project_sustainability_plan_desc`, text, Required): Explain how the project will continue after grant funding ends.
* **Date of Submission** (`grant_app_submission_date_final`, date, Required): The date you are submitting this application.
* **Business Innovation Grant Application Form** (`section_applicant_identity_grant`, note, Optional): A note about the applicant and business identity.
* **Innovation Proposal** (`section_innovation_proposal_details`, note, Optional): A note about the innovation proposal details.
* **Budget and Sustainability** (`section_budget_sustainability`, note, Optional): A note about the budget and sustainability.
