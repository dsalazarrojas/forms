# digital_business_card_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect business card registration information from business entities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Company, Job Title, and Business Card fields.
2. If applicable, you can provide your Company Address and Job Address.
3. Select the Business Relationship if you have a pre-existing relationship with us.
4. Upload your Business Card.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This is your first name. 
* **Last Name**: This is your last name. 
* **Email**: This is your email address. Please use a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Phone**: This is your phone number. Please use a valid phone number that you have access to.
* **Company**: This is your company name.
* **Job Title**: This is your job title within the company.
* **Company Address**: This is the address of your company. (Optional)
* **Job Address**: This is the address where you work. (Optional)
* **Business Card**: This is a text description of your business card. 
* **File**: Please upload your business card image file.
* **Business Relationship**: This is a dropdown to select the type of relationship you have with us. The options are None and Other. Please select None if you do not have a pre-existing relationship with us, and Other if you have a different type of relationship not listed.
