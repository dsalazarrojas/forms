# Registration Anomaly Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Registration Anomaly Review Form is designed to record and review any registration anomalies that occur within the system. This form helps identify and document the issue, ensuring it is resolved efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, taking into consideration the specific information requested.
2. Use the "Notes" field to add any additional details or clarification required.
3. Review your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
4. If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please do not hesitate to contact the relevant authorities for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form** (`registration_anomaly_review_form_page`): This is the main section of the form where you will enter the details of the anomaly.
* **Date of Anomaly** (`date_of_anomaly`): Please enter the date when the anomaly occurred. 
* **Review By** (`review_by`): Enter the name of the person or team reviewing the anomaly.
* **Resolved By** (`resolved_by`): Enter the name of the person or team who resolved the anomaly.
* **Date of Resolution** (`date_of_resolution`): Enter the date when the anomaly was resolved.
* **Reason of Resolution** (`reason_of_resolution`): Enter the reason why the anomaly was resolved.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Use this field to add any additional details or clarification required.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number.

Please note that providing accurate and complete information in each field will aid in the resolution process. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the relevant authorities. 

Tips:
* If you need to add more details or clarification, please use the "Notes" field.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please do not hesitate to contact the relevant authorities.
* Please fill out the form only once for each anomaly.
