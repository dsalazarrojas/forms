# Small Business Development Center Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Small Business Development Center Intake Form is a critical document that helps us understand your business needs and provide you with the right support. Please answer all the questions accurately to help us tailor our assistance to your specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Use the correct format for any date fields (e.g., MM/DD/YY).
3. Select the correct options for the select_one and select_multiple fields.
4. Be as detailed as possible when filling out the free-text fields like Notes and Description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (ID: 1, `First Name`, required, `text`): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (ID: 2, `Last Name`, required, `text`): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (ID: 3, `Email`, required, `email`): Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (ID: 4, `Phone`, required, `text`): Enter your business phone number without any special characters or spaces.
* **Business Name** (ID: 5, `Business Name`, required, `text`): Enter your business name as it appears on your business license or registration documents.
* **Business Type** (ID: 6, `Business Type`, required, `select_one`): Select the type of your business from the following options:
	+ Sole Proprietorship
	+ Partnership
	+ Corporation
	+ Limited Liability Partnership
	+ Limited Liability Corporation
* **Industry** (ID: 7, `Industry`, required, `text`): Enter the type of industry your business operates in.
* **Address** (ID: 8, `Address`, required, `text`): Enter your business address as it appears on your business registration documents.
* **City** (ID: 9, `City`, required, `text`): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State** (ID: 10, `State`, required, `text`): Enter the state where your business is located.
* **Zip** (ID: 11, `Zip`, required, `text`): Enter your business zip code.
* **Country** (ID: 12, `Country`, required, `select_multiple`): Select all the countries where your business operates from the following options:
	+ USA
	+ Canada
* **Time Zone** (ID: 13, `Time Zone`, required, `time`): Enter your business time zone (e.g., EST, PST, GMT).
* **Date of Birth** (ID: 14, `Date of Birth`, required, `date`): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Date Completed** (ID: 15, `Date Completed`, required, `date`): Enter the date you completed this form in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Notes** (ID: 16, `Notes`, optional, `note`): Add any additional information about your business that you think is important for us to know.
* **Assigned Tool** (ID: 17, `Assigned Tool`, required, `select_one`): Select the type of tool that will be assigned to your business from the following options:
	+ Chatbot
	+ ChatJIM
	+ Email Support
* **Output File** (ID: 18, `Output File`, optional, `text`): If you have an output file related to your business, please attach it here.
* **Form ID** (ID: 19, `Form ID`, optional, `text`): Enter any additional form ID if you have it.
* **Category** (ID: 20, `Category`, optional, `text`): Enter any category that your business falls under.
* **Description** (ID: 21, `Description`, optional, `text`): Enter a brief description of your business.
* **Form IDs** (ID: 22, `Form IDs`, optional, `text`): Enter any additional form IDs if you have them.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (ID: 23, `Assigned Tool 2`, optional, `select_one`): Select the type of tool that will be assigned to your business from the following options:
	+ Chatbot
	+ ChatJIM
	+ Email Support
* **Category 2** (ID: 24, `Category 2`, optional, `text`): Enter any category that your business falls under.
* **Description 2** (ID: 25, `Description 2`, optional, `text`): Enter a brief description of your business.

## Tips
* Always use the correct format for date fields (e.g., MM/DD/YY).
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out free-text fields.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure you can submit your application successfully.
