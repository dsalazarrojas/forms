# Feedbackform - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Feedbackform is a tool designed to collect feedback from users about a product or service. It is an essential instrument for gathering valuable information to help us improve our offering and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select a category for your feedback by choosing one of the options provided in the feedback category field.
2. Type your feedback message in the feedback message field.
3. If you have any contact information or would like to be contacted, please fill in your email and/or phone number in the contact fields.
4. Once you have filled in all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.
5. If you change your mind or need to cancel, click the "Cancel" button instead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Category** (Feedback category): 
  This field lets you choose the type of feedback you are providing, such as "Feedback on Product" or "Feedback on Service". 
  Choose the one that best describes your feedback.
* **Feedback Message** (Feedback message): 
  This is where you write your comment, suggestion, or concern about the product or service. Be as specific as possible to help us understand your feedback.
* **Contact Information** (Contact info): 
  If you would like to be contacted, please fill in your email address or phone number. We'll be happy to get back to you if needed.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact phone): 
  If you would rather contact us by phone instead of email, please fill in your phone number. 
  Please note that we might not be able to contact you by phone for every feedback, but we'll try our best. 
* **Submit** (Submit): 
  Once you've filled in all the required fields, click this button to send your feedback to us. 
  Please make sure to review your form before submitting it.
* **Cancel** (Cancel): 
  If you change your mind or need to cancel, click this button to reset the form and start over.
