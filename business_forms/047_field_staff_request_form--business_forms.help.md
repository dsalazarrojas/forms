# Field Staff Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Staff Request Form is used to request field staff to work on a task. This form helps in gathering information about the task and the staff member's availability and assignment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Select the request date for the task.
2. Enter the name of the staff member who is requesting assistance.
3. Choose whether the task is 'Yes' or 'No' in the request type field.
4. Provide a brief description of the task in the request details field.
5. Select the staff members who are available to work on the task.
6. Choose whether the assigned tool is available or not.
7. Choose the status of the task.
8. Add any comments about the task.
9. Enter the date the task is assigned to the staff member.
10. Select the due date for the task.
11. Choose the priority of the task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date): Select the date when the request is made.
* **Requested By** (`requested_by`, text): Enter the name of the staff member making the request.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, select_one): Choose whether the task is 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, note): Provide a brief description of the task.
* **Assigned Staff** (`assigned_staff`, select_multiple): Select the staff members who are available to work on the task.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one): Choose whether the assigned tool is available or not.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one): Choose the status of the task (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note): Add any comments about the task.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date): Enter the date the task is assigned to the staff member.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date): Select the due date for the task.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one): Choose the priority of the task (Yes or No). 

## Tips
* Make sure to select only one request date.
* Only enter one staff member's name in the Requested By field.
* Only select one staff member in the Assigned Staff field.
* Only select one status and one priority in the task.
