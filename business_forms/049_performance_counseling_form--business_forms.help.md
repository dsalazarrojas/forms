# Performance Counseling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a tool for performance counseling, allowing employees and managers to collaborate on addressing performance issues, creating resolution plans, and tracking progress. It's essential to use the form for its intended purpose and not for other uses.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields for both the employee and manager sections.
2. Ensure that the employee and manager signatures are obtained, as they are required fields.
3. Select the correct performance issues and resolution status options that apply to the situation.
4. Enter accurate and honest information in the issue description, resolution plan, employee email, manager email, and any other relevant fields.
5. Review and verify the entered information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date** (`3`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are filling out this form.
* **Time** (`4`, `time`, not required): Enter the time you are submitting this form. (This field is not required.)
* **Issue Description** (`5`, `text`, required): Describe the performance issue or concern you are addressing.
* **Resolution Plan** (`6`, `text`, required): Create a plan to resolve the performance issue or concern.
* **Employee Email** (`7`, `email`, required): Enter your official work email address.
* **Manager Email** (`8`, `email`, required): Enter your manager's official work email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`9`, `text`, not required): Enter your official work phone number. (This field is not required.)
* **Manager Phone** (`10`, `text`, not required): Enter your manager's official work phone number. (This field is not required.)
* **Employee Note** (`11`, `note`, not required): Add any additional information or comments you'd like to share with your manager.
* **Manager Note** (`12`, `note`, not required): Add any additional information or comments you'd like to share with the employee.
* **Performance Issues** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Select the performance issue or concern that you are addressing (e.g., Poor attendance, Lack of motivation, Poor communication skills, or Other).
* **Resolution Status** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the current status of the resolution (e.g., Open, Closed, In Progress, Not Applicable).
* **Performance Rating** (`15`, `number`, required): Enter a numerical value (e.g., 1-5) representing your performance rating.
* **Employee Signature** (`16`, `text`, not required): This field is not required, but it's recommended for employee confirmation.
* **Manager Signature** (`17`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's signature as a confirmation of their approval.
* **Approved By** (`18`, `select_one`, required): Select the person who approved this performance counseling (e.g., Manager, Director, Not Applicable).
* **Comments** (`19`, `text`, not required): Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share with the employee.
* **Attachments** (`20`, `text`, not required): Attach any relevant documents or files to support the performance counseling process. (This field is not required.)
