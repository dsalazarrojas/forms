# Artist Call Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Call Submission Form is used by artists and art organizations to submit artworks, statements, and other relevant information for review and selection processes related to specific art calls or events.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Provide your personal contact information, including name, email address, and phone number.
* Choose your primary and secondary art medium(s) from the provided list.
* Write a brief artist statement (200-300 words) describing your artistic practice and vision.
* Write a longer artist biography (200-300 words) providing background information about yourself.
* Provide a detailed description of your artwork, including its title, size, and any other relevant information.
* Confirm that you agree to the call terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Name or Pseudonym** (Artist Name or Pseudonym, text, required): Enter your name or pseudonym as you want to be credited.
* **Legal Name** (Legal Name, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (Artist Email, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (Artist Phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Website or Portfolio URL** (Artist Website, text, optional): Enter a link to your website or online portfolio.
* **Social Media Handles or Pages** (Social Media, text, optional): Enter any relevant social media handles or pages.
* **Primary Art Medium** (Primary Medium, select_one, required): Choose your primary art medium from the list provided.
* **Secondary Mediums (if applicable)** (Secondary Mediums, text, optional): If applicable, enter any secondary art mediums you work with.
* **Artist Statement** (Artist Statement, text, required): Write a brief artist statement describing your artistic practice and vision (200-300 words).
* **Artist Biography** (Artist Bio, text, required): Write a longer artist biography providing background information about yourself (200-300 words).
* **Years Actively Creating Art** (Years Practicing, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of years you have been actively creating art.
* **Title of Submission** (Submission Title, text, required): Enter the title of your artwork or project.
* **Description of Submission** (Submission Description, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your artwork.
* **Dimensions or Scale** (Submission Dimensions, text, optional): If applicable, enter the size of your artwork.
* **Previous Exhibition History** (Exhibition History, text, optional): List any major exhibitions or shows you have been part of.
* **Awards or Recognition** (Awards, text, optional): If applicable, enter any grants, fellowships, or awards you have received.
* **Artist Call Name or Event** (Call Name, text, required): Enter the name of the artist call or event this submission is for.
* **I Agree to the Call Terms and Conditions** (Agree Terms, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the submission guidelines.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (Additional Comments, text, optional): If applicable, provide any additional information or questions for the call organizers.
