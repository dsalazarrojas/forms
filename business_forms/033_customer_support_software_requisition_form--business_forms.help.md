# Customer Support Software Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Support Software Requisition Form is designed to help you request software support or assistance from our technical support team. Please use this form to provide a clear and detailed description of your issue or request, and our team will respond with the necessary help and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `form_title` field with a brief description of your support request.
2. Provide a detailed description of your issue or request in the `form_description` field.
3. Select the `form_category` category that best describes your request.
4. Upload any relevant files (e.g., screenshots, error reports, etc.) in the `form_file_1`, `form_file_2`, and `form_file_3` fields.
5. Enter your phone numbers for contact in the `form_phone_1`, `form_phone_2`, and `form_phone_3` fields.
6. Enter your email address in the `form_email_1` field.
7. Select a date and time for your issue or request in the `form_date`, `form_time`, and `form_date_time` fields.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments in the `form_note` field.
9. Select the time period for your issue or request in the `form_date_range`, `form_time_range`, and `form_date_period` fields.
10. Enter specific numbers or counts in the `form_integer_1`, `form_integer_2`, and `form_decimal_1`, `form_decimal_2` fields.
11. Select one option from the `form_select_one_1` field.
12. Select multiple options from the `form_select_multiple_1`, `form_select_multiple_2`, and `form_select_multiple_3` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title**: A brief description of your support request. (Required)
* **form_description**: A detailed description of your issue or request. (Required)
* **form_category**: The category that best describes your request (e.g., "Business Forms"). (Optional)
* **form_file_1**, **form_file_2**, and **form_file_3**: Upload any relevant files to support your request.
* **form_phone_1**, **form_phone_2**, and **form_phone_3**: Enter your phone number(s) for contact. (Optional)
* **form_email_1**: Enter your email address. (Required)
* **form_date**: Select a date for your issue or request.
* **form_time**: Select a time for your issue or request.
* **form_date_time**: Enter a date and time for your issue or request. (Optional)
* **form_note**: Enter any additional notes or comments. (Optional)
* **form_date_range**: Enter a date range for your issue or request. (Optional)
* **form_time_range**: Enter a time range for your issue or request. (Optional)
* **form_date_period**: Enter a date period for your issue or request. (Optional)
* **form_integer_1** and **form_integer_2**: Enter specific numbers or counts. (Optional)
* **form_decimal_1** and **form_decimal_2**: Enter specific decimal numbers or counts. (Optional)
* **form_select_one_1**: Select one option from the available options.
* **form_select_multiple_1**, **form_select_multiple_2**, and **form_select_multiple_3**: Select multiple options from the available options.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have a clear understanding of your request and the information you need to provide.
* Use the available options in the select fields to help the support team better understand your request.
* Attach any relevant files to support your request.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure the support team can assist you efficiently.
