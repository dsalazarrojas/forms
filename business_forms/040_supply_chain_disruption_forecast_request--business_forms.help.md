# Supply Chain Disruption Forecast Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Chain Disruption Forecast Request form is used to identify potential supply chain disruptions that may impact business operations. This form helps stakeholders assess and prioritize disruptions for resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if a supply chain disruption is anticipated. If so, click "Yes" on the "Supply Chain Disruption Forecast Request" page.
2. Select the segment and region affected by the potential disruption.
3. Choose the priority level for the disruption (not currently available in the form but will be in the future).
4. Enter the expected resolution date for the disruption.
5. Provide a brief description of the disruption and its impact on the business.
6. Select the product affected by the disruption (if applicable).
7. Indicate if the disruption is expected to affect any specific segment or region.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Supply Chain Disruption Forecast Request** (`supply_chain_disruption_forecast_request`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the potential supply chain disruption you are reporting.
- **Segment** (`segment`, select_one, optional): Select the segment of the business that will be affected by the disruption (e.g., manufacturing, logistics, etc.).
- **Region** (`region`, select_multiple, optional): Select all regions that will be affected by the disruption (e.g., North America, Europe, Asia, etc.).
- **Product** (`product`, select_one, optional): If the disruption is related to a specific product, select it from the options provided.
- **Priority** (`priority`, number, optional): Although not currently available in this form, in the future, this field will allow you to select a priority level for the disruption.
- **Expected Resolution Date** (`expected_resolution_date`, date, optional): Enter the date by which the disruption is expected to be resolved.
- **Impact** (`impact`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the disruption's impact on the business.
- **Cause** (`cause`, text, optional): Describe the root cause of the potential supply chain disruption.
- **Affected Segment** (`affected_segment`, select_one, optional): Indicate if the disruption will affect any specific segment of the business.
- **Affected Region** (`affected_region`, select_multiple, optional): Select all regions that will be affected by the disruption.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have a clear understanding of the potential disruption before submitting this form.
- Review the existing disruption requests to avoid duplicating submissions.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to this help guide or contact your internal stakeholders for assistance.
