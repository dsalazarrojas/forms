# Business Made Simple - BUSINESS MRI - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Made Simple - BUSINESS MRI form is a diagnostic tool designed to provide an in-depth analysis of your business. By completing this form, you will get a comprehensive overview of your business health, including its strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability, and select the most accurate option for each field.
2. If you are unsure or have any additional information, please provide further details in the "Additional Business Health Comments" section.
3. Review each field carefully and answer truthfully, as your input will help us provide an accurate assessment of your business health.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (id: 2)**: Enter your full legal business name.
* **How Clear is Your Business Mission? (id: 3)**: Rate your business mission clarity on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Not clear at all" and 5 is "Crystal clear".
* **Rate Your Current Marketing Effectiveness (id: 4)**: Select the option that best describes your marketing effectiveness, with options "Poor", "Fair", "Good", "Excellent", or "Unsure".
* **Does Your Customer Understand What You Offer Within 5 Seconds? (id: 5)**: Select "True" or "False" to indicate whether your customer understands your offerings within 5 seconds.
* **Do You Have a Defined Sales Funnel? (id: 7)**: Select the option that best describes your sales process, with options "Yes, fully automated", "Yes, manual process", "No, but we are building one", or "No funnel exists".
* **What is Your Average Lead-to-Customer Conversion Rate? (id: 8)**: Enter a percentage estimate of your conversion rate.
* **Which Product Line is Most Profitable? (id: 9)**: Enter the name of your most profitable product line.
* **Do You Regularly Audit Product Inventory and Performance? (id: 10)**: Select the option that best describes your product inventory auditing frequency, with options "Weekly", "Monthly", "Quarterly", "Annually", or "Never".
* **How Would You Rate Your Internal Workflows? (id: 12)**: Select the option that best describes your internal workflows, with options "Highly inefficient", "Needs improvement", "Efficient", or "Optimized".
* **Estimated Monthly Operating Expenses (id: 13)**: Enter a whole number estimate of your monthly operating expenses.
* **Do You Have at Least 3 Months of Operating Cash in Reserve? (id: 14)**: Select "True" or "False" to indicate whether you have sufficient cash reserves.
* **Is Your Team Aligned with the Company Goals? (id: 15)**: Select the option that best describes your team alignment, with options "Fully aligned", "Mostly aligned", "Partially aligned", or "Disconnected".
* **What is Your Primary Growth Bottleneck? (id: 16)**: Describe the main challenge affecting your growth.
* **Additional Business Health Comments (id: 17)**: Use this field to provide any additional information that may be relevant to your business health assessment.

Note: Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
