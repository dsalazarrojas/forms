# Blockchain Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a survey about your perspectives on blockchain and cryptocurrency technologies. The goal is to understand your views on various aspects of blockchain, including its adoption, potential use cases, and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions in the survey, making sure to select all the options that apply to you.
2. If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the topic, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
3. Please take your time and answer honestly, as your feedback is valuable to us.
