# Freight Cargo Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freight Cargo Check In Form is used to record details of freight cargo being checked into your facility. This includes cargo type, origin location, delivery date, weight, description, cargo status, cargo details, contact person, and contact number. The form is designed to ensure accurate and efficient processing of cargo transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Freight Cargo Check In Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of cargo being checked in (Truckload, Intermodal, Air, or Sea).
2. Enter the origin location where the cargo started from.
3. Enter the delivery date of the cargo.
4. Enter the weight of the cargo.
5. Provide a brief description of the cargo.
6. Choose the current status of the cargo (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
7. Enter any additional cargo details in the note field (Cargo Details).
8. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the cargo.
9. Enter the contact number of the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cargo Type**: Select the type of cargo being checked in (Truckload, Intermodal, Air, or Sea).
* **Origin Location**: Enter the location where the cargo started from. This is where the cargo originated.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date when the cargo is expected to be delivered.
* **Weight**: Enter the weight of the cargo.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the cargo.
* **Cargo Status**: Choose the current status of the cargo (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **Cargo Details**: Enter any additional details about the cargo.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the cargo.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the contact person.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Review all fields carefully to ensure accuracy.
- Contact your supervisor if you have any questions or concerns.
