# Document Formatting Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify that documents are formatted correctly to meet company standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the document layout to ensure it meets company standards.
2. Check if the header section is present and correctly configured.
3. Check if the footer section is present and correctly configured.
4. Review the body content to ensure it is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Document Layout (1)**: This field is a text field where you can describe the document's layout. Please enter a brief summary of the document's layout, including the font size, font family, and any other relevant details.
* **Header Section (2)**: This field allows you to select whether the document has a header section. Choose 'Yes' if the document has a header section, 'No' otherwise.
* **Footer Section (3)**: This field is similar to the Header Section field, where you select whether the document has a footer section. Choose 'Yes' if the document has a footer section, 'No' otherwise.
* **Body Content (4)**: This field is a text field where you can describe the body content of the document, including any images, tables, or other relevant details.

## Tips
* Make sure to be as concise as possible when filling out the form to ensure your answers are accurate and easy to read.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, ask your supervisor for guidance.
* Review the document thoroughly before submitting the form to ensure it meets company standards.
