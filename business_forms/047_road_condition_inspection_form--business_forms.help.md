# Road Condition Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Road Condition Inspection Form is used to assess and document the overall condition of a road, including its defects, rating, and comments. This form helps to ensure that the road's condition is thoroughly inspected and recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each field to indicate whether the road condition meets the specified criteria. If a field is marked as "Yes," please provide additional notes or comments for further context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Section 1**: This is the first section of the form, which serves as an introductory section.
* **Section 2: Section One**: This is the second section of the form where you will enter information about the road's condition.
* **Section 3: Section Two**: This is the third section of the form where you will enter information about the road's condition.
* **Section 4: Section Three**: This is the fourth section of the form where you will enter information about the road's condition.
* **Road Condition Rating**: Select one of the following options:
	+ **Good**: The road is in good condition.
	+ **Fair**: The road is in fair condition.
* **Road Condition Rating Note**: If you select "Fair" for the rating, provide additional information about why the road's condition is fair.
* **Road Defects**: Select "Yes" if there are any noticeable defects on the road.
* **Road Defects Note**: If you select "Yes," provide additional information about the defects.
* **Road Condition Comments**: Select "Yes" if there are any comments about the road's condition.
* **Road Condition Comments Note**: If you select "Yes," provide additional information about the comments.
* **Photos**: Select "Yes" if photos are provided to support the inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each field honestly and accurately.
* Provide as much detail as possible when providing notes or comments.
* Use "Yes" or "No" consistently for each field to ensure consistency in the data collected.
