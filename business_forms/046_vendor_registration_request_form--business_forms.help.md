# Vendor Registration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Registration Request Form is a tool for vendors to provide their information to the company. This form helps the company to track and manage vendor relationships, which includes verifying their identity, storing their contact information, and determining their role and assigned status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last name** field.
3. Enter your company's name in the **Company name** field.
4. Enter your company's address in the **Company address** field.
5. Enter your phone number in the **Phone number** field.
6. Enter the name of the contact person for your company in the **Contact person** field.
7. Select your role in the **Role** field.
8. Provide a brief description of your company in the **Description** field.
9. If you are a new vendor, select **Yes** in the **Assigned to** field.
10. If you've previously been assigned to the company, select **No** in the **Assigned to** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your personal first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your personal last name.
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address.
* **Company name**: Enter your company's name.
* **Company address**: Enter your company's physical address.
* **Phone number**: Enter your company's phone number.
* **Contact person**: Enter the name of the contact person for your company.
* **Role**: Select your role or position within your company.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your company.
* **Assigned to**: Select **Yes** if you are a new vendor, **No** otherwise.
* **Assigned to**: This field is currently labeled as **None**, but it is actually indicating whether you are assigned to the company or not, based on the options **Yes** and **None**.
