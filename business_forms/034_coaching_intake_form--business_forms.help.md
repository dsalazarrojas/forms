# Coaching Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather relevant information from individuals seeking coaching services. The purpose of this form is to ensure that you, as the client, understand what to expect from the coaching process and to allow the coach to tailor their approach to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your responses to help the coach understand your needs.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
4. Complete all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
5. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: \*\*Your legal full name.
* **Email Address**: \*\*Primary contact email.
* **Phone Number**: \*\*Contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address**: \*\*Your full address (optional).
* **Date of Birth**: \*\*For identification purposes (optional).
* **Current Occupation**: \*\*Your job or profession.
* **Employer Name**: \*\*Name of your organization (optional).
* **Marital Status**: \*\*Your relationship status.
* **Number of Dependents**: \*\*How many people depend on you (optional).
* **Type of Coaching Requested**: \*\*What coaching you need (e.g., Life Coaching, Career Coaching, etc.).
* **Primary Concern or Challenge**: \*\*What brings you to coaching.
* **Coaching Goals**: \*\*What you want to achieve.
* **Have You Had Coaching Before**: \*\*Previous coaching experience.
* **Previous Coaching Details**: \*\*Describe past experience (optional).
* **Current Stress Level**: \*\*Rate your stress level from 1-10.
* **Support System**: \*\*Do you have people who support you (optional).
* **Relevant Health Conditions**: \*\*Any conditions the coach should know about (optional).
* **Current Medications**: \*\*List any medications you are taking (optional).
* **Mental Health Support**: \*\*Are you currently in therapy or counseling (optional).
* **Preferred Session Format**: \*\*How you want to meet (e.g., In Person, Video Call, etc.).
* **Preferred Session Time**: \*\*When works best for you (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, etc.).
* **Availability**: \*\*When are you available (select multiple days).
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: \*\*How did you find us (e.g., Online Search, Social Media, etc.).
* **Who Referred You**: \*\*Name of referrer (optional).
* **What Are Your Expectations**: \*\*What you expect from coaching.
* **How Committed Are You**: \*\*Rate your commitment level from 1-5.
* **What Might Get in Your Way**: \*\*Potential challenges to success.
* **Additional Information**: \*\*Anything else you want to share (optional).

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask the coach for clarification.
* This form is a critical step in the coaching process, so please complete it carefully.
