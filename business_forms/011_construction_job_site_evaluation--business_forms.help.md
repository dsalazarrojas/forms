# Construction Job Site Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the safety and operational status of a construction job site. It helps to identify potential hazards and provide a rating of the site's overall safety rating.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Answer the select_one questions (e.g., "Proper PPE is being worn by all workers") based on your observation.
3. If you have identified any hazards, provide a detailed description in the "Description of Any Hazards Identified" field.
4. If necessary corrective action is required, describe the action taken or scheduled in the "Action Taken or Required" field.
5. Enter the overall site safety rating (1-10) in the "Overall Site Safety Rating" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name/Location**: Enter the name of the project and its location.
* **Date of Evaluation**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluator's Name and Title**: Enter your name and title.
* **Time of Day**: Enter the time of day when the evaluation was conducted.
* **Proper PPE is being worn by all workers**: Answer "Yes - 100% Compliance" if all workers are wearing required personal protective equipment (PPE).
* **Site Housekeeping and Cleanliness**: Rate the site's housekeeping and cleanliness, choosing from "Excellent", "Acceptable", "Needs Improvement", or "Poor".
* **First Aid Kit and Emergency Info Accessible**: Answer "True" if the first aid kit and emergency information are readily available.
* **Fire Extinguishers Present and Inspected**: Answer "True" if fire extinguishers are present and inspected within the last month.
* **Necessary Safety/Hazard Signage Posted**: Answer "True" if necessary safety and hazard signs are posted.
* **General Condition of Tools and Equipment**: Rate the condition of tools and equipment, choosing from "Excellent", "Good/Fair", or "Immediate Repair Required".
* **Scaffolds and Ladders Secured and Safe**: Answer "Safe" if scaffolds and ladders are securely and safely set up.
* **Description of Any Hazards Identified**: If you've identified any hazards, describe them in this field.
* **Action Taken or Required**: If necessary corrective action is required, describe the action taken or scheduled.
* **Immediate Corrective Action Required?**: Answer "True" if immediate corrective action is required.
* **Overall Site Safety Rating (1-10)**: Enter a rating of the site's safety, with 1 being extreme risk and 10 being highly safe.
* **Required Follow-up Date**: Enter the date for follow-up evaluation.
