# Ingredient List Revision Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ingredient List Revision Request Form is used to request revisions to the ingredient list. This form is used to provide additional information to the team about the changes that are needed, such as the reason for the revision and the priority level of the change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the form title.
* Provide your customer's name and any relevant information about them.
* Briefly explain the reason for the revision.
* Provide any justification for the revision, if necessary.
* Attach any supporting documents, such as images or videos.
* Select the priority level for the revision.
* Choose the ingredients that need revision.
* Select the date the revision was requested and completed.
* Add any additional comments about the revision.
* Enter your name and contact information for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: The title of the form.
* **Customer Name**: The name of the customer who is requesting the revision.
* **Reason for Revision**: Briefly describe the change request.
* **Justification**: Provide any additional information about why the revision is needed.
* **Supporting Documents**: Attach any images or videos that support the revision request.
* **Priority**: Select the priority level of the revision (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Ingredient List**: Choose the ingredients that need revision.
* **Date Requested**: The date the revision was requested.
* **Date Completed**: The date the revision was completed.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the revision.
* **Submitter Name**: The name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email**: The email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone**: The phone number of the person submitting the form.

Note: All fields are optional, but the more information you provide, the better we can understand and address your revision request.
