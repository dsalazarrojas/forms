# Small Business Fair Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Small Business Fair Application Form is designed to gather essential information from small business owners who wish to participate in the fair or event. The form helps to assess their suitability and qualification for participating in the fair or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, marked as "Required".
2. Choose relevant options for fields with dropdown options.
3. Ensure all fields are filled out carefully and accurately.
4. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**1. Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is used to input the name of the event you are applying for.
	* Please provide the exact name of the event.

**2. Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for the name of the person filling out the form on behalf of the business.
	* Please provide your name.

**3. Email** (`email`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for your email address, where you can be reached for further communication.
	* Please provide a valid email address.

**4. Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for your contact phone number, where you can be reached for further communication.
	* Please provide your phone number.

**5. Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, `select_one`, required: false):
	* Choose the type of vendor you are (Retailer, Food Vendor, Service Provider).

**6. Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for a brief description of your business and products or services offered.
	* Please be concise and provide essential information.

**7. E-Signature** (`e_signature`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for your electronic signature, confirming you have read and understood the form guidelines.
	* Please sign above.

**8. Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for your business address.
	* Please provide your physical address.

**9. Vendor Website** (`vendor_website`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for your website.
	* Please provide your website URL.

**10. Vendor License Number** (`vendor_license_number`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for your business license number.
	* Please provide the correct license number.

**11. Vendor License Exp. Date** (`vendor_license_exp_date`, `date`, required: false):
	* This field is for the expiration date of your business license.
	* Please provide the correct date.

**12. Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`, `date`, required: false):
	* This field is for the start date of the event you are applying for.
	* Please provide the exact start date.

**13. Event End Date** (`event_end_date`, `date`, required: false):
	* This field is for the end date of the event you are applying for.
	* Please provide the exact end date.

**14. Vendor Setup Fee** (`vendor_setup_fee`, `number`, required: false):
	* This field is for any setup fee associated with your participation in the event.
	* Please provide the fee amount.

**15. Services Offered** (`vendor_services`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	* Choose the services you offer (Food, Beverage, Retail, Other).

**16. Requirements** (`vendor_requirements`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for any specific requirements you need for participating in the event.
	* Please provide any requirements.

**17. Payment Terms** (`vendor_payment_terms`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for your payment terms.
	* Please provide your payment terms.

**18. Insurance** (`vendor_insurance`, `select_one`, required: false):
	* Choose if you have insurance ('True' or 'False').

**19. Insurance Provider** (`vendor_insurance_provider`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for your insurance provider's name or information.
	* Please provide the name of the insurance provider.

**20. Insurance Provider Address** (`vendor_insurance_provider_address`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for the insurance provider's address.
	* Please provide the address of the insurance provider.

**21. Insurance Provider Phone** (`vendor_insurance_provider_phone`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for the insurance provider's phone number.
	* Please provide the phone number of the insurance provider.

**22. Insurance Provider Email** (`vendor_insurance_provider_email`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for the insurance provider's email address.
	* Please provide the email address of the insurance provider.

**23. Event Notes** (`event_notes`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for any additional event-specific notes.
	* Please provide any notes.

**24. Vendor Notes** (`vendor_notes`, `text`, required: false):
	* This field is for any notes or information about your business.
	* Please provide any notes.

**25. Vendor Signature Date** (`vendor_signature_date`, `date`, required: false):
	* This field is for the date of your electronic signature.
	* Please sign this form on the date specified above.

Please note that all fields are optional and only required if specifically marked as 'required' within the form. It is recommended that you fill out all fields accurately and completely to ensure a smooth application process. If you have any further questions or need assistance, please contact us.
