# image_to_pdf_converter_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for converting an image into a PDF file. It guides you through the process of selecting and configuring various options for the conversion process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Upload Image**: Select and upload the image you want to convert into a PDF.
2. Select the **PDF Type** to choose the type of PDF you want to create.
3. Choose the **Orientation** of the PDF.
4. Select the **Image Resolution** (if applicable).
5. Select the **PDF Quality** and **PDF Orientation**.
6. Optionally, enter an **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, and **Image Size** if required.
7. Select the **Output File** name and save it to your local drive.
8. Click on **Save to Drive** to save the file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Upload Image**: Upload the image you want to convert into a PDF. This field is required.
* **Select PDF Type**: Select the type of PDF you want to create. This field is required and has options 1, 2, and 3.
* **Orientation**: Choose the orientation of the PDF. This field is required and has options 1 and 2.
* **Image Size**: Enter the size of the image. This field is optional.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address. This field is not required but might be for administrative or support purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number. This field is not required but might be for administrative or support purposes.
* **Image Resolution**: Choose the resolution of the image. This field is optional.
* **Output File**: Enter the name of the output file. This field is required.
* **Description**: Enter a description for the PDF. This field is optional.
* **PDF Quality**: Select the quality of the PDF. This field is required and has options 1 and 2.
* **PDF Orientation**: Choose the orientation of the PDF. This field is optional and has options 1 and 2.
* **Upload to**: Enter the upload location for the PDF. This field is optional.
* **Overwrite**: Select whether to overwrite an existing PDF file. This field is required.
* **Overwrite Confirmation**: Confirm whether to overwrite the file. This field is optional.
* **PDF Name**: Enter the name of the PDF. This field is optional.
* **PDF Title**: Enter the title of the PDF. This field is optional.
* **PDF Author**: Enter the author of the PDF. This field is optional.
* **PDF Subject**: Enter the subject of the PDF. This field is optional.
* **PDF Description**: Enter the description of the PDF. This field is optional.
