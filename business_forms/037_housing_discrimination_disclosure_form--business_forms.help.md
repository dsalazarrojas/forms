# Housing Discrimination Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Housing Discrimination Disclosure Form" is used to report instances where a tenant is found to be ineligible to rent, own, or occupy a housing unit.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions provided.
3. If you need assistance or clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Details** (`tenant_details`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for providing a brief description of the tenant's details.
* **Discrimination** (`discrimination`, text, required: false)
	+ Please describe any known or suspected discrimination incidents or behaviors related to this case.
* **Housing Type** (`housing_type`, number, required: true)
	+ Select the type of housing unit that the tenant was found to be ineligible to rent, own, or occupy.
* **Housing Address** (`housing_address`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide the address of the housing unit that is the subject of the ineligibility determination.
* **Reason for Ineligibility** (`reason_for_ineligibility`, text, required: true)
	+ Describe the reason why the tenant is found to be ineligible to rent, own, or occupy a housing unit.
* **Date of Ineligibility** (`date_of_ineligibility`, date, required: false)
	+ If known, provide the date when the tenant's ineligibility was determined.
* **Time of Ineligibility** (`time_of_ineligibility`, time, required: false)
	+ If known, provide the time when the tenant's ineligibility was determined.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out for assistance.
* This form is an important tool to help maintain records and ensure equal access to housing opportunities.
