# Freelance Service Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freelance Service Quote Form is a structured way for clients to request services from freelancers and for freelancers to respond with quotes that meet the client's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the service requested in the "Service Requested" field.
3. Choose a start date for the project in the "Start Date" field.
4. Choose an end date for the project in the "End Date" field.
5. Enter a valid email address for the client in the "Client Email" field.
6. Enter the client's phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
7. Enter the quote value for the service in the "Quote Value" field.
8. Enter any comments or additional information from the client in the "Client Comments" field.
9. Enter any comments or additional information from the freelancer in the "Freelancer Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`Client Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the client.
* **Service Requested** (`service_requested`, text, required: false): A brief description of the service requested.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Choose the start date for the project.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Choose the end date for the project.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): A description of the project or service.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address for the client.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Quote Value** (`quote_value`, number, required: false): Enter the quote value for the service.
* **Client Comments** (`client_comments`, text, required: false): Any comments or additional information from the client.
* **Freelancer Comments** (`freelancer_comments`, text, required: false): Any comments or additional information from the freelancer.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your freelancer for clarification.
