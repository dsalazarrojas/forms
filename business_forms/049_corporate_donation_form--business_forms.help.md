# corporate_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from donors for corporate donations. This form will help the organization understand the donor's details, including their contact information, donation amount, and purpose of the donation. This form is not intended for personal donations, please use a different form for personal donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Donor Information in the first field.
2. Enter the Donation Amount, which is the amount of the donation.
3. Select the Donation Date for the donation.
4. Choose the Purpose of the Donation from the options provided.
5. Enter the Company Address, if applicable.
6. Enter the Donor Email address.
7. Enter the Donor Phone number.
8. Add any comments about the donation.
9. Select your agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information** (`donator_info`, text, optional): Enter the name and other details of the donor.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the donation.
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, date, optional): Select the date for the donation.
* **Purpose of Donation** (`purpose_of_donation`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the reason for the donation from the provided options.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the company making the donation.
* **Donor Email** (`donator_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the donor.
* **Donor Phone** (`donator_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the donor.
* **Comments** (`donator_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments about the donation.
* **Agreement** (`donator_agreement`, select_one, optional): Select your agreement to the terms and conditions.
