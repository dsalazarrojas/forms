# retail_sustainability_score_program_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for the Retail Sustainability Score Program. The program assesses the sustainability performance of retail companies and provides recognition for their efforts. To be eligible, companies must demonstrate their commitment to various sustainability practices and provide supporting documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Complete the application details page.
2.  Select and confirm your sustainability practices on the Practices page.
3.  Choose your focus areas on the Focus Areas page.
4.  Attach any required supporting documentation on the Supporting Documentation page.
5.  Enter your contact information on the Contact Info page.
6.  Confirm your submission on the Submission Confirmation page.
7.  Agree to the accuracy of the information provided on the Submission Confirmation Agreement page.
8.  Confirm your understanding of the submission process on the Submission Confirmation Agreement 2 page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Application Details**: Enter your company's name and other relevant details about your application.
    (Required: False)
*   **Practices**: Select the sustainability practices your company has implemented or is implementing. Choose as many as are relevant to your business. Examples include:
    \- Reduce waste and pollution
    \- Source materials sustainably
    \- Improve supply chain management
    \- Support biodiversity conservation
    \- Engage customers and educate
    \- Improve supply chain transparency and accountability
    \- Manage chemical use
    \- Improve water management
    \- Engage with local communities
    \- Improve employee engagement
    (Required: False)
*   **Focus Areas**: Choose the areas of focus for your sustainability initiatives. Select all that apply:
    \- Energy and Climate
    \- Water and Waste
    \- Supply Chain
    \- Product and Packaging
    \- Human Rights and Labor
    \- Biodiversity and Ecosystems
    \- Community and Social
    \- Resource Efficiency
    (Required: False)
*   **Supporting Documentation**: Attach any relevant documentation or data to support your application. This could include sustainability reports, certification documents, or other relevant records.
    (Required: False)
*   **Contact Info**: Enter your company's contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
    (Required: False)
*   **Submission Confirmation**: Confirm that you have reviewed and submitted the form correctly.
    (Required: False)
*   **Submission Confirmation Agreement**: Agree that the information provided is accurate and true.
    (Required: False)
*   **Submission Confirmation Agreement 2**: Confirm that you understand the submission process and the implications of providing false or misleading information.
    (Required: False)

## Tips

*   Make sure to carefully review each page and ensure that all required information is provided.
*   Ensure that all selected practices and focus areas are accurate and applicable to your business.
*   Attach supporting documentation as required.
*   Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
*   Carefully review the submission confirmation pages to ensure that you understand the implications of providing false or misleading information.
