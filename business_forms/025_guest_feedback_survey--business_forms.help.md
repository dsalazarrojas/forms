# Guest Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from guests who have stayed at a hotel or accommodation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your answer for "Rate Your Stay" and provide a brief explanation in the "Comments" field.
2. Answer the questions about your overall satisfaction and the length of your stay.
3. Provide your contact information (name, email, and phone number) so we can get back to you if needed.
4. Answer the multiple-choice questions regarding your experience with various aspects of your visit, such as services, room environment, staff, location, and amenities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rate Your Stay** (`rating`, select_one, required): Please rate your overall experience at our hotel or accommodation from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, required): Please rate your overall satisfaction with a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Length of Stay** (`stay_length`, number, required): Please indicate the number of days you stayed with us.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you arrived at our hotel or accommodation.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you departed from our hotel or accommodation.
* **Assisted By** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Please select whether you were assisted by our chatbot or our staff member, chatjimmy.
* **Services Used** (`comments_on_services`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the services you used during your stay.
* **Room Environment** (`comments_on_environment`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the aspects of our room environment that you liked or disliked.
* **Staff** (`comments_on_staff`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the aspects of our staff that you liked or disliked.
* **Location** (`comments_on_location`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the aspects of our location that you liked or disliked.
* **Pricing** (`comments_on_pricing`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the aspects of our pricing that you liked or disliked.
* **Food and Beverages** (`comments_on_food_and_beverages`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the aspects of our food and beverages that you liked or disliked.
* **Amenities** (`comments_on_amenities`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the amenities you used during your stay.
* **Recreation** (`comments_on_recreation`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the recreational activities you used or enjoyed during your stay.
* **Other** (`comments_on_other`, select_multiple, required): Please select all other aspects of your stay that you would like to comment on.
* **Security** (`comments_on_security`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the aspects of our security that you liked or disliked.
* **Parking** (`comments_on_parking`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the aspects of our parking facilities that you liked or disliked.
* **Internet** (`comments_on_internet`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the aspects of our internet facilities that you liked or disliked.
* **Cleanliness** (`comments_on_cleanliness`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the aspects of our cleanliness that you liked or disliked.
* **Checkin** (`comments_on_checkin`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the aspects of our checkin process that you liked or disliked.
