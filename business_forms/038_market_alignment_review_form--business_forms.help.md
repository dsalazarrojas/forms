# Market Alignment Review Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Market Alignment Review Form is a tool used to evaluate and review the alignment of market-related processes, procedures, and decisions within an organization. It helps identify areas that are well-aligned and those that require improvement to ensure consistency and efficiency in market-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Market Alignment Review Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the pages that you would like to review.
2. Fill in the relevant information for each page, using the fields provided.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure that you are providing accurate and relevant information.
4. Once you have completed all the pages, click "Submit" to save your review.
