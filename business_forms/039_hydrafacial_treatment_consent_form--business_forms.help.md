# Hydrafacial Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydrafacial Treatment Consent Form is a document that allows patients to provide information about themselves, details about the treatment they are receiving, and to give their consent for the treatment. This form is used to collect relevant data for administrative purposes and to ensure that the patient has agreed to receive the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter Patient Information**: Fill in your name, address, contact number, and email address in the designated fields. If you don't have an email address or a phone number, please leave those fields blank.
2. **Provide Treatment Details**: Describe the treatment you are receiving, including any relevant medical history or information that the healthcare provider needs to know.
3. **Give Consent**: Select "Yes" if you agree to receive the treatment, or "No" if you do not want to receive it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information. This will help the healthcare provider to contact you with any questions or updates about your treatment.
* **Treatment Details**: Describe the treatment you are receiving, including any relevant medical history or information that the healthcare provider needs to know.
* **Consent**: Select "Yes" if you agree to receive the treatment, or "No" if you do not want to receive it.
* **Notes**: This field is for any additional comments or information that you want to provide to the healthcare provider.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you want to receive updates or notifications from the healthcare provider.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are scheduled to receive the treatment.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are scheduled to receive the treatment.
* **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider assigned to your treatment.
