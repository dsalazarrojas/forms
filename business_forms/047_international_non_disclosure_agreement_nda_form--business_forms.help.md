# International Non Disclosure Agreement (NDA) Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to create a confidential and non-disclosure agreement for any business-related activities or projects. It gathers client, company, and agreement information to establish a clear understanding between parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **form-title**, a brief description of the agreement.
2. Enter **Client Information**, providing essential details about the client's contact and company information.
3. If applicable, enter **Company Information**, providing more details about the company.
4. Specify the **Confidentiality Terms**, outlining what information is considered confidential.
5. Enter the **Duration of Disclosure**, indicating the length of time the agreement will last.
6. List the **Confidential Materials**, any information that will be shared.
7. Choose how **Return of Materials** will be handled.
8. Fill in the **Contact Information**, providing details about who to contact in case of queries.
9. Select the **Agreement Signature** type, indicating the level of agreement (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
10. Fill in the **Agreement Date**, the date the agreement is signed.
11. Choose the **Agreement Time**, when the agreement was signed.
12. Add any **Agreement Signer Information** if required.
13. Include a **Signature Block** for the agreement signers.
14. If necessary, provide a **Agreement Signature Image**.
15. Enter the **Agreement Signature Date**, when the agreement was signed.
16. Fill in the **Agreement Signature Time**, when the agreement was signed.
17. Enter the **Agreement Signature Signer**, the person signing the agreement.
18. Add the **Agreement Signature Position**, if necessary.
19. Enter the **Agreement Signature Title**, for reference.
20. Provide the **Agreement Signature Email**, for contact purposes.
21. Add the **Agreement Signature Phone**, for contact.
22. Enter the **Agreement Signature Fax**, if necessary.
23. Provide the **Agreement Signature Address**, for contact.
24. Add any **Agreement Signature Signer Information**, if required.
25. Fill in the **Agreement Signature Terms**, specifying any additional terms or conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form-title** (Text, Required): This field is for a brief description of the agreement.
* **Client Information** (Text, Required): Enter the client's name, contact information, and company details.
* **Company Information** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional company information if required.
* **Confidentiality Terms** (Text, Required): Clearly define what information is considered confidential.
* **Duration of Disclosure** (Text, Optional): Specify how long the agreement will last.
* **Confidential Materials** (Text, Required): List any confidential materials that will be shared.
* **Return of Materials** (Text, Optional): Choose how materials will be returned after the agreement.
* **Contact Information** (Text, Optional): Provide details about who to contact in case of queries.
* **Agreement Signature** (Select One, Required): Choose the level of agreement (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Agreement Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Signer Information** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional signer information.
* **Signature Block** (Note, Required): This is a placeholder for the signature of the agreement.
* **Agreement Signature Image** (Text, Optional): Provide a signature image.
* **Agreement Signature Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Signature Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Signature Signer** (Text, Optional): Enter the signer's details.
* **Agreement Signature Position** (Text, Optional): Enter the signer's position.
* **Agreement Signature Title** (Text, Optional): Enter the signer's title.
* **Agreement Signature Email** (Text, Optional): Enter the signer's email.
* **Agreement Signature Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the signer's phone number.
* **Agreement Signature Fax** (Text, Optional): Enter the signer's fax number.
* **Agreement Signature Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the signer's address.
* **Agreement Signature Signer Information** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional signer information.
* **Agreement Signature Terms** (Text, Required): Specify any additional terms or conditions.
* **Agreement Signature Image** (Text, Optional): This field is not used in this example, but it could be used to upload a signature image.
* **Agreement Signature Date** and **Agreement Signature Time** (Date and Time, Required): These fields are used to record the date and time of signing.
* **Agreement Signature Signer** (Text, Optional): Enter the signer's name and contact information.
* **Agreement Signature Position** (Text, Optional): Enter the signer's position.
* **Agreement Signature Title** (Text, Optional): Enter the signer's title.
* **Agreement Signature Email** (Text, Optional): Enter the signer's email.
* **Agreement Signature Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the signer's phone number.
* **Agreement Signature Fax** (Text, Optional): Enter the signer's fax number.
* **Agreement Signature Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the signer's address.
* **Agreement Signature Signer Information** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional signer information.
* **Agreement Signature Terms** (Text, Required): Specify any additional terms or conditions.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields before signing.
- Review the agreement carefully before signing.
- Save a copy of the signed agreement for future reference.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions to share confidential information.
- Clarify any doubts or questions with the other party before signing.
