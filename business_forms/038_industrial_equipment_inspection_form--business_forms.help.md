# industrial_equipment_inspection_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The industrial equipment inspection form is used to collect data on the condition and status of equipment in the workplace. This form helps to identify and document any issues or defects with the equipment, as well as track who is assigned to use or maintain it. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. **General Information**: This section is where you enter the basic details about the equipment being inspected. Enter the equipment ID, which is a unique identifier for the equipment.
2. **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment being inspected from the available options (Equipment A, Equipment B, Equipment C).
3. **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
4. **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection.
5. **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the person performing the inspection.
6. **Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the inspection.
7. **Email**: Enter the email address of the person performing the inspection or assigned to use the equipment.
8. **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person performing the inspection or assigned to use the equipment.
9. **Category**: Select the category to which the equipment belongs (Business Forms, Other Forms).
10. **Assigned To**: Select the person or team assigned to use or maintain the equipment.
11. **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool or equipment assigned to the selected person or team.
12. **Assigned User**: Enter the username of the person or team assigned to use or maintain the equipment.
13. **Created By**: Enter the name of the person who created the form.
14. **Updated By**: Enter the name of the person who last updated the form.
15. **Created At**: Enter the date and time the form was created.
16. **Updated At**: Enter the date and time the form was last updated.
17. **Deleted By**: Enter the name of the person who deleted the form.
18. **Deleted At**: Enter the date and time the form was deleted.
