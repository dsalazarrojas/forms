# pros_and_cons_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **pros_and_cons_evaluation_form** is used to evaluate the pros and cons of a decision or proposal. It helps users weigh the advantages and disadvantages of a particular option or course of action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the **Introduction** page for context and information about the decision or proposal being evaluated.
2. Select all relevant **Pros** and **Cons** from the provided options. These are the advantages and disadvantages of the decision or proposal.
3. Describe the **Decision Making Process** that led to the evaluation. This is an optional field, but it helps to provide context and transparency.
4. Enter the **Next Steps** for the decision or proposal, including any necessary actions or deadlines.
5. Review the **Conclusion** for a summary of the evaluation.
6. Select a **Submit** option to complete the form and provide the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a read-only page that provides context and information about the decision or proposal being evaluated.
* **Pros**: Select all relevant advantages of the decision or proposal. This field is required.
* **Cons**: Select all relevant disadvantages of the decision or proposal. This field is required.
* **Decision Making Process**: This is an optional field where you can describe the process that led to the evaluation. It helps to provide context and transparency.
* **Next Steps**: Enter the next steps or actions for the decision or proposal, including any necessary deadlines. This field is optional.
* **Conclusion**: This is a read-only page that summarizes the evaluation.
* **Submit**: Select a submit option to complete the form and provide the evaluation. This field is required.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide all relevant pros and cons of the decision or proposal.
* Be specific and concrete in your evaluation.
* Use the **Decision Making Process** field to provide context and transparency.
* Make sure to review the **Conclusion** page to ensure the evaluation is accurate.
* Take necessary next steps and actions after submitting the form.
