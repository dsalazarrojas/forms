# Maintenance Outsourcing Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and scheduling information from users who are interested in outsourcing their maintenance tasks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by reviewing each section carefully.
2. Fill out the form in sections, starting with the **Contact Information** section.
3. If you are not sure which schedule preference best suits you, select "No" for all schedule preferences, and we will discuss further.
4. If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them in the **Message** field.
5. Once you've completed all sections, review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name and other relevant contact information. (Required: false, Type: number)
* **Service Inquiries**: Describe your maintenance outsourcing needs. (Required: false, Type: text)
* **Preferred Schedule**: Select a day of the week and time slot for our team to contact you. (Required: false, Type: time)
* **Schedule Preferences**: Select the times of the day you are available for our team to contact you. (Required: false, Type: select_multiple)
* **Contact Preferences**: Indicate whether you would like to be contacted via phone or email. (Required: false, Type: select_one)
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed. (Required: false, Type: email)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed. (Required: false, Type: text)
* **Message**: Provide any additional comments or questions you may have. (Required: false, Type: note)

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to contact us.
* We will review your form submission and contact you to discuss your maintenance outsourcing needs.
* Please note that this form is not a commitment, and we will be in touch to discuss further details after receiving this form.
