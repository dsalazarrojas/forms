# Innkeeper Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innkeeper Agreement Form is used to document and standardize agreements between innkeepers and property owners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct **Agreement Type** for your business from the provided options (Hotel Agreement or B&B Agreement).
2. Enter your **Property Owner's** name and contact information (this will be the owner's name that you are entering into, not your own name).
3. Provide the complete **Property Address**, including the address where the agreement is taking place.
4. Record the **Signature Date** of the agreement.
5. Sign your **Owner Signature** on the agreement.
6. Enter your **Innkeeper Name** and **Innkeeper Title**.
7. Provide your **Contact Number** and **Contact Email** for the innkeeper.
8. Specify the **Agreement Term** and the terms of the agreement.
9. Enter the **Innkeeper Agreement**, a brief description of the agreement.
10. Sign the **Signature** section with your signature.
11. Record the **Date of Signature** for the innkeeper.
12. Print your name as the **Printed Name** for the signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agreement Type**: Select the type of agreement you are entering (Hotel Agreement or B&B Agreement).
* **Property Owner**: Enter the name of the property owner you are entering into the agreement with.
* **Property Address**: Enter the full address of the property where the agreement is taking place.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the signature for the agreement.
* **Owner Signature**: Sign your agreement with your signature.
* **Innkeeper Name**: Enter your name as the innkeeper.
* **Innkeeper Title**: Enter your title as the innkeeper.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number as the innkeeper.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email as the innkeeper.
* **Agreement Term**: Enter the terms of the agreement.
* **Innkeeper Agreement**: Enter a brief description of the agreement.
* **Signature**: Sign your name as the innkeeper.
* **Date of Signature**: Record the date of your signature.
* **Printed Name**: Print your name as the innkeeper.
