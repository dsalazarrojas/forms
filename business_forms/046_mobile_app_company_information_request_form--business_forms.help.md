Mobile App Company Information Request Form - Help Guide
=============================================

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about mobile apps and their companies. It helps us to understand the details of your company, app, and its features. Please fill in the form with accurate and complete information to help us provide you the best possible support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the form fields with accurate and complete information about your company and mobile app.
2. Make sure to choose the correct options from the dropdown menus, such as app platform, app type, and company size.
3. If any field is not applicable to your company or app, you can leave it blank or select "Other (please specify)".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): This is the name of your company. Please fill in the name of your company, e.g., "ABC Company".
* **App Name** (`app_name`, text, optional): This is the name of your mobile app. Please fill in the name of your app, e.g., "XYZ App".
* **App Platform** (`app_platform`, select_one, optional): Choose the platform on which your app is developed, e.g., Android, iOS, Windows, etc.
* **App Type** (`app_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the types that apply to your app, e.g., Games, Social Media, Productivity.
* **App Description** (`app_description`, text, optional): This is a brief description of your app. Please describe it in a few sentences.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`, text, optional): This is the URL of your company website. If you don't have a website, you can leave it blank.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): This is the email address of the person to contact for more information. Please fill in a valid email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): This is the phone number of the person to contact for more information. Please fill in a valid phone number.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): This is the address of your company. Please fill in the address if you want us to contact you directly.
* **Company Description** (`company_description`, text, optional): This is a brief description of your company. Please describe it in a few sentences.
* **Partner Request** (`partner_request`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes if you are interested in partnering with us.
* **Follow-up Request** (`follow_up_request`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes if you want us to follow up with you.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): This is where you can describe the next steps you want us to take.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): If there is any additional information you want to share with us, please fill it in.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the number of employees in your company.
* **Company Stage** (`company_stage`, select_one, optional): Choose the stage of your company, e.g., Development, Launch, Growth, etc.
* **Company Status** (`company_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your company, e.g., Public, Private, Bootstrapped, etc.
* **App Status** (`app_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your app, e.g., Development, Live, Discontinued, etc.
* **Company Location** (`company_location`, text, optional): This is the location of your company.
* **App Location** (`app_location`, text, optional): This is the location of your mobile app.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, optional): This is the industry of your company or app.
* **Company Industry** (`company_industry`, text, optional): This is the industry of your company.
* **Company Founder** (`company_founder`, text, optional): This is the founder of your company.
* **Company Year** (`company_year`, date, optional): This is the year your company was founded.
* **Company Since** (`company_since`, select_one, optional): Choose how long your company has been in existence, e.g., Founders, Investors, Partners, etc.
* **Company Stage** (`company_stage`, text, optional): This is the stage of your company. Please describe it in a few sentences.

Tips
----

* Please fill in all the fields that apply to your company and app.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask us for clarification.
* Make sure to proofread your submission before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any additional questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
