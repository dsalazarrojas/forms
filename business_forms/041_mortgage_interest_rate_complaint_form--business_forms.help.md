# Mortgage Interest Rate Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to submit complaints or issues related to their mortgage interest rates. It allows users to specify the type of complaint, provide details about their loan, and include any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of complaint you are experiencing from the "Complaint Type" dropdown menu.
2. If you need to report an issue with your loan, enter the amount of your loan in the "Loan Amount" field.
3. If you have any specific concerns or issues with your interest rate, please describe them in the "Rate Issue" text field.
4. Enter your lender's name in the "Lender" field.
5. Provide your contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
6. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
7. Finally, enter any additional comments you have about your complaint in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Complaint Type**: Select the type of complaint you are experiencing. Options include "High Interest Rate", "Low Interest Rate", and "Other". Please choose one that best describes your issue.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: false
- **Loan Amount**: If you need to report an issue with your loan, enter the total amount borrowed.
  * Type: number
  * Required: false
- **Rate Issue**: If you have any specific concerns or issues with your interest rate, please describe them here.
  * Type: note
  * Required: false
- **Lender**: Enter the name of your lender.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email to ensure we can reach you to discuss your complaint.
  * Type: email
  * Required: true
- **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number to ensure we can reach you to discuss your complaint.
  * Type: text
  * Required: true
- **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or details about your complaint.
  * Type: note
  * Required: false

Note: Please fill out as much information as you can, and don't hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or need assistance.
