# Legal Service Retainer Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the terms of a retainer service agreement between the Service Provider and the Client. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and address in the "name" and "address" fields.
2. Choose whether the Service Provider will offer services to you in the "services_offered" field.
3. Select a fee structure from the "fee_structure" field.
4. Choose the start and end dates for the retainer service in the "start_date" and "end_date" fields.
5. Enter the fee amount in the "fee_amount" field.
6. Decide on payment terms in the "payment_terms" field and payment frequency in the "payment_frequency" field.
7. Provide your consent in the "consent" field (optional).
8. Sign and date the agreement in the "signature", "date", and "time" fields.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "note" field (optional).
10. Provide your email address and phone number in the "email" and "phone" fields (optional).
