# Ingredient Origin Confirmation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for confirming the origin of an ingredient, allowing you to verify the supplier and their documentation related to the origin of the ingredient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Supplier Information**: Enter the supplier's name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. **Specify Origin Details**: Select the country and state where the ingredient originated from the provided options. If the origin country is not among the listed options, use the "Origin City" field to specify the location.
3. **Attach Documentation**: Upload or attach any relevant documentation such as certificates, COA, or certificates of analysis that support the origin of the ingredient.
4. **Specify Origin Date and Time**: Enter the date and time when the ingredient originated, if known.
5. **Add Supplier and Origin Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the supplier and/or originator to provide more context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name** (`Supplier Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the supplier or company that provided the ingredient.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the supplier.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the supplier.
* **Origin Country** (`Origin Country`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the country where the ingredient originated from the options provided.
* **Origin State** (`Origin State`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or multiple states where the ingredient originated from the options provided.
* **Origin City** (`Origin City`, `text`, required: false): If the origin location is not among the listed states, enter the city name.
* **Origin Document** (`Origin Document`, `text`, required: false): Attach or upload the original documentation that confirms the ingredient origin.
* **Document Type** (`Document Type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of document (e.g., Certificate of Origin, COA, Certificate of Analysis) that confirms the ingredient's origin.
* **Supplier Document** (`Supplier Document`, `text`, required: false): Attach or upload any documentation from the supplier that may be relevant to the origin.
* **Origin Date** (`Origin Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the ingredient originated, if known.
* **Origin Time** (`Origin Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time when the ingredient originated, if known.
* **Supplier Comments** (`Supplier Comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments from the supplier that may be relevant to the origin.
* **Origin Comments** (`Origin Comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the origin of the ingredient.
