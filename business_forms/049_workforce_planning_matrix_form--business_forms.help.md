# Workforce Planning Matrix Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Planning Matrix Form is designed to gather essential information about team structure, priorities, and other key details. This form is intended for team leaders to provide accurate and complete data for workforce planning and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Department Name**: Enter the name of the department you are representing. This is a required field.
2. Select the **Staff Priorities** for the team members, using the options Senior Team Member, Junior Team Member, Team Leader, or Other.
3. Provide the **Next Steps** for the team, which can include any relevant updates or instructions for team members.
4. Enter the **Email** and **Phone** contact details for the team.
5. Enter any additional **Department Notes** related to the team.
6. **Team Leader Name**, **Team Leader Title**, and **Team Leader Department**: Enter the name, title, and department of the team leader.
7. **Staff Title**, **Staff Location**, and **Staff Count**: Enter the title, location, and count of team members.
8. **Priority Level**: Select the priority level of the team, using the options High, Medium, or Low.
9. **Next Steps 2**: Provide any further next steps or updates for the team.
10. **Team Lead**: Indicate whether the team has a team lead by selecting True or False.
11. **Team Lead 2**, **Team Lead 3**, and **Team Lead 4**: These fields are optional and seem to be duplicates of Team Lead 5, likely for consistency and data validation purposes.
