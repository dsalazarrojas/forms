## Annual Report Submission Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used for companies or organizations to provide necessary information about their annual report, ensuring all required fields are accurately filled out within the given regulatory deadline.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Company or Organization Name**: Enter the legal registered name of your company or organization.
2. **Company Registration Number**: Provide your official identification number.
3. **Date of Business Registration**: If applicable, enter the date your company was registered.
4. **Fiscal Year Covered by Report**: Specify the year covered by the report being submitted.
5. **Reporting Period End Date**: Indicate the last day of the fiscal year this report covers.
6. **Authorized Signatory Name**: Enter the name of the person authorized to sign on behalf of the company or organization.
7. **Signatory Title**: Specify the position or title of the authorized signatory.
8. **Signatory Email**: Enter the contact email of the signatory.
9. **Signatory Phone Number**: If applicable, enter the contact phone number of the signatory.
10. **Type of Annual Report**: Select the type of report submitted (Standard Annual Report, Audited Financial Statements, etc.).
11. **Has the report been audited?**: Indicate if the report has been audited or reviewed by the relevant authorities.
12. **Auditor or Audit Firm Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the auditor or audit firm.
13. **Financial Statements Included**: Confirm if financial statements are included in the report.
14. **I declare financial information is accurate and complete**: Confirm that financial information provided is accurate and complete.
15. **To the best of my knowledge, no fraud exists**: Declare if no fraud concerns are known or suspected.
16. **Report complies with applicable regulations?**: Confirm that the report complies with applicable regulations.
17. **Comparison with prior year included?**: Indicate if comparisons with prior years are included in the report.
18. **Material notes and disclosures included?**: Confirm that all material notes and disclosures are included in the report.
19. **Report Submission Format**: Choose the format in which the report was submitted (PDF, Word, Excel, etc.).
20. **Actual Submission Date**: Indicate the date the report was actually submitted.
21. **Required Filing Deadline**: Enter the regulatory deadline for submission of this report.
22. **I certify this report is being submitted in compliance**: Confirm that the report has been submitted in compliance with all applicable regulations.
22. **Supporting documents retained for inspection?**: Confirm if supporting documents are available for inspection.

Each field is designed to capture critical information to ensure transparency and compliance. Ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of each field before filling out the form to avoid any potential issues during submission or review.
