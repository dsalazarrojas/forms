# Equal Pay Calculator For Collaborations - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to calculate equal pay for collaborators on a project. It allows teams to allocate budget according to collaboration roles and budget allocations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the collaboration roles section with the roles involved in the collaboration.
2. Enter the total project budget and select the collaboration currency.
3. Choose whether each role has a budget allocation.
4. Select the preferred payment method for each role.
5. Allocate the budget according to the role percentage.
6. Enter any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Collaboration roles**: Select all roles involved in the collaboration. (Select multiple)
	+ Choose from the available options 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Total amount**: Enter the total project budget. (Number)
* **Pay method**: Select whether each role has a budget allocation. (Select one)
	+ Choose from the available options 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Project budget**: Enter the total project budget. (Text, free-form input)
* **Role budget**: Allocate the budget according to the role percentage. (Text, free-form input)
* **Collaboration budget**: Enter any additional collaboration budget. (Text, free-form input)
* **Collaboration currency**: Select the currency of the collaboration. (Select one)
	+ Choose from the available options 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Collaborators**: Select all collaborators involved in the collaboration. (Select multiple)
	+ Choose from the available options 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Preferred pay method**: Select the preferred payment method. (Select one)
	+ Choose from the available options 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Role percentage**: Allocate the budget according to the role percentage. (Select multiple)
	+ Choose from the available options 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Role percentage total**: Enter the total role percentage. (Number)
* **Budget allocation**: Enter any additional budget allocation. (Text, free-form input)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes. (Note)

## Tips

* Ensure that you're filling out this form for collaborations with multiple roles and budget allocations.
* If you're external teams, you might need to adapt this form to your specific needs.
* Use this form to get a quick estimate of the project budget allocation.
