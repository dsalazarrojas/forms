# public_relations_package_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public_relations_package_registration form is used to register a public relations package for a campaign or follow-up activity. It captures essential information from prospects or customers to track their engagement with a product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the prospect's first name in the "Prospect First Name" field.
2. Enter the prospect's last name in the "Prospect Last Name" field.
3. Enter the company name in the "Company" field.
4. Enter the prospect's email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the prospect's phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select the package type from the list in the "Package Type" field.
7. Enter a brief description of the package in the "Package Details" field.
8. Enter the campaign date in the "Campaign Date" field.
9. Enter the campaign time in the "Campaign Time" field.
10. Enter the follow-up date in the "Follow-up Date" field.
11. Enter the follow-up time in the "Follow-up Time" field.
12. Specify the duration of the campaign in the "Campaign Duration" field.
13. Select the package status from the list in the "Package Status" field.
14. Select the campaign status from the list in the "Campaign Status" field.
15. Select the follow-up method from the list in the "Follow-up Method" field.
16. Enter any additional follow-up details in the "Follow-up Details" field.
17. Enter any campaign notes in the "Campaign Notes" field.
18. Enter the next follow-up date in the "Next Follow-up Date" field (if applicable).
19. Enter the next follow-up time in the "Next Follow-up Time" field (if applicable).
20. Select the next follow-up method from the list in the "Next Follow-up Method" field (if applicable).
21. Enter the follow-up contact name in the "Follow-up Contact Name" field (if applicable).
22. Enter the follow-up contact phone number in the "Follow-up Contact Phone" field (if applicable).
23. Enter the follow-up contact email in the "Follow-up Contact Email" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prospect First Name**: Enter the first name of the prospect or customer.
* **Prospect Last Name**: Enter the last name of the prospect or customer.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company associated with the prospect or customer.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the prospect or customer.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the prospect or customer.
* **Package Type**: Select the type of public relations package from the list.
* **Package Details**: Enter a brief description of the package.
* **Campaign Date**: Enter the date of the campaign.
* **Campaign Time**: Enter the time of the campaign.
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up activity.
* **Follow-up Time**: Enter the time of the follow-up activity.
* **Campaign Duration**: Enter the duration of the campaign.
* **Package Status**: Select the status of the package from the list.
* **Campaign Status**: Select the status of the campaign from the list.
* **Follow-up Method**: Select the follow-up method from the list.
* **Follow-up Details**: Enter any additional follow-up details.
* **Campaign Notes**: Enter any notes related to the campaign.
* **Next Follow-up Date**: Enter the date of the next follow-up activity (if applicable).
* **Next Follow-up Time**: Enter the time of the next follow-up activity (if applicable).
* **Next Follow-up Method**: Select the method of the next follow-up activity from the list (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Contact Name**: Enter the name of the follow-up contact (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the follow-up contact (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the follow-up contact (if applicable).
