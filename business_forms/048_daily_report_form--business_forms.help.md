# Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Report Form is designed to facilitate users in reporting their daily activities or tasks. The form gathers essential information for administrative or project management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Daily Report Form**: This is the title of the form, indicating its purpose.
2. **Date**: Enter the date of the reported activity or task.
3. **Time**: Enter the time of the reported activity or task.
4. **Notes**: Provide a brief description of the activity or task.
5. **Email**: Enter your email address for contact or reference purposes.
6. **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact or reference purposes.
7. **Category**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the category of the reported activity or task.
8. **Sub Category**: Choose one or multiple options to further categorize the reported activity or task.
9. **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the activity or task.
10. **Assigned Tool**: Enter any tools or equipment used during the reported activity or task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Daily Report Form** (`title`, required): This is the title of the form, indicating its purpose.
* **Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date of the reported activity or task.
* **Time** (`time`, required): Enter the time of the reported activity or task.
* **Notes** (`text`, required): Provide a brief description of the activity or task.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address for contact or reference purposes.
* **Phone** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact or reference purposes.
* **Category** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the category of the reported activity or task.
* **Sub Category** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose one or multiple options to further categorize the reported activity or task.
* **Description** (`text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the activity or task.
* **Assigned Tool** (`text`, required): Enter any tools or equipment used during the reported activity or task.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure accurate reporting.
- Use the "Category" and "Sub Category" fields to provide a clear classification of the reported activity or task.
- In the "Description" field, provide a detailed and concise explanation of the activity or task.
- If you're unsure about any field or its purpose, refer to the form's title or contact your supervisor for guidance.
