> # Trainee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the performance, skills, and behavior of a trainee in various aspects of their work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the trainee's name in the "Trainee Name" field.
2. Evaluate the trainee's performance in each of the given areas (Overall Performance, Communication, Technical Skills, etc.) by selecting a numerical value (e.g., 1-5) and providing a description in the corresponding "Description" field.
3. Provide feedback and recommendations for the trainee in the "Feedback", "Recommendation", and "Additional Comments" fields.
4. Enter an overall numerical evaluation in the "Overall" field and provide a description in the "Overall Description" field.
