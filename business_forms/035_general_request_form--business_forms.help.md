# General Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Request Form is a versatile form used to collect general information from clients or customers. It's designed to gather essential details about the type of request, contact preference, and other related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (if any) with the requested information.
2. Select the desired option for "Type of Request" and "Contact Preference" fields as per your needs.
3. Add any comments or details in the "Comments" field if necessary.
4. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Request Form (1)**: Type your general request in this text field. This is the main area where you can describe your inquiry or ask for help.
* **Name (2)**: Enter your name or the name of the person requesting. This is a general identifier to help with communication.
* **Email (3)**: Provide your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
* **Type of Request (5)**: Select the type of request you're making. Options include:
	+ General Request
	+ Other
	+ Lead Request
* **Contact Preference (6)**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Both
* **Comments (7)**: Add any additional information or context about your request in this field. This is optional but helpful for us to better understand your needs.

## Tips
- Be as clear and concise as possible when filling in the form.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.

This help guide is designed to make it easy for you to complete the General Request Form efficiently and accurately. If you have any further questions or need clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
