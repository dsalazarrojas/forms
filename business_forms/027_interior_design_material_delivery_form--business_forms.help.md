# interior_design_material_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The interior design material delivery form is used to collect and track information related to interior design material deliveries from suppliers. This includes supplier details, delivery specifics, order information, items being delivered, shipping details, and delivery status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the supplier information section with the details of the supplier who is delivering the materials.
2. Enter the delivery details, including the delivery specifics.
3. Add the order information, which includes the order reference number and other relevant details.
4. List the order items being delivered.
5. Provide shipping information, including the shipping address and any relevant details.
6. Update the delivery status as necessary.
7. Add any order notes for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Information** (`Supplier Information`, text, optional): Enter the details of the supplier who is delivering the materials, such as their name and contact information.
* **Delivery Details** (`Delivery Details`, text, optional): Enter the specifics of the delivery, including the date, time, and any other relevant details.
* **Order Information** (`Order Information`, text, optional): Enter the order reference number and any other relevant details related to the order.
* **Order Items** (`Order Items`, text, optional): List the items being delivered.
* **Shipping Information** (`Shipping Information`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address and any other relevant details.
* **Delivery Status** (`Delivery Status`, text, optional): Update the delivery status as necessary.
* **Order Notes** (`Order Notes`, text, optional): Add any notes or comments related to the order.
* **Supplier Email** (`Supplier Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the supplier.
* **Supplier Phone** (`Supplier Phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the supplier.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in each field.
* Use this form for its intended purpose to avoid confusion and duplication of efforts.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure it accurately reflects the current delivery status.
* Use the form as a tool to track and manage deliveries efficiently.
* Consider setting up notifications or reminders for upcoming deliveries or updates to the delivery status.
