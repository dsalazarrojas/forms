# Pest Control Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pest Control Appointment Form is used to collect information from customers to schedule a pest control appointment. This form helps the pest control service provider to understand the customer's needs and prepare for the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name in the "customer_name" field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "customer_email" field. This is a required field to ensure that the service provider can contact the customer after the appointment.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "customer_phone" field. This is also a required field to ensure that the customer can be contacted for any last-minute changes or updates.
4. Select the date of the appointment in the "appointment_date" field. This is a required field to schedule the appointment at the correct date and time.
5. Choose the time of the appointment in the "appointment_time" field. This is a required field to schedule the appointment at the correct time.
6. Select the service required from the options provided in the "service_required" field. This is a required field to ensure that the service provider has the necessary equipment and personnel to perform the service.
7. (Optional) Enter any additional information or comments in the "additional_info" field.
8. (Optional) Enter any specific details about the service required in the "service_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_name** (Text): This is the customer's name.
* **customer_email** (Email): This is the customer's email address. Please enter a valid email address to ensure that the service provider can contact the customer after the appointment.
* **customer_phone** (Text): This is the customer's phone number. Please enter a valid phone number to ensure that the customer can be contacted for any last-minute changes or updates.
* **appointment_date** (Date): This is the date of the appointment. Please select a date that works for you.
* **appointment_time** (Time): This is the time of the appointment. Please select a time that works for you.
* **service_required** (Select One): This is a list of available services. Please select the service required to ensure that the service provider has the necessary equipment and personnel to perform the service.
* **additional_info** (Note): This is an optional field for you to enter any additional information or comments about the appointment.
* **service_details** (Note): This is an optional field for you to enter any specific details about the service required.
