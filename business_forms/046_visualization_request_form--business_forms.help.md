# visualization_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request data visualization and/or reporting from the data analysis team. Use this form to clearly communicate your data needs and ensure timely support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Visualization Request` field with a clear and concise description of the data visualization you require.
2. Enter the `Business Name` associated with the request.
3. Provide your contact information by entering your `Contact Email` and `Contact Phone`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visualization Request** (`<title>`): Enter a brief description of the requested data visualization (e.g., "Sales performance by region").
* **Business Name** (`business_name`): Enter the name of the business unit or department associated with the request.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further questions or discussions.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your work phone number to ensure timely communication.

## Tips
- Be as clear and concise as possible when describing your data visualization request.
- Avoid submitting duplicate requests by checking the form's submission history.
- If you have any further questions, you can contact the data analysis team at your email or phone number provided in the form.
