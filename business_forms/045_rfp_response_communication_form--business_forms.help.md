# Rfp Response Communication Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The RFP Response Communication Form is a tool used by companies to respond to Requests for Proposal (RFPs) received from potential clients. This form helps to ensure a structured and organized response to the RFP, making it easier for the client to review and evaluate the proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your response, which should include the RFP number and the name of your company.
2. Provide your company's name and contact information, including contact name and email.
3. Enter your contact phone number.
4. Specify who or what this response is to (e.g., "Client Name," "RFP Committee," etc.).
5. Enter the RFP number.
6. Answer the questions posed by the RFP, providing clear and concise responses to:
	* What is your company’s name?
	* Can you provide a brief description of your company?
	* What makes your company unique?
	* How will you approach this project?
	* Why do you believe your company is the right choice for this project?
	* Can you provide a detailed proposal in response to this RFP?
7. Enter the total cost for this project.
8. Enter the total number of days to complete this project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **RFP Response Communication Form** (`title`, text, required: false)
	* Enter a brief title of your response to the RFP.
* **company** (`company`, text, required: false)
	* Enter your company's name.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false)
	* Enter your name as the contact person for your company.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false)
	* Enter your email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false)
	* Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Response To** (`response_to`, text, required: false)
	* Specify who or what this response is addressed to (e.g., "Client Name," "RFP Committee," etc.).
* **RFP Number** (`rfp_number`, number, required: false)
	* Enter the number of the RFP you are responding to.
* **What is your company’s name?** (`question1`, text, required: false)
	* Enter your company's name.
* **Can you provide a brief description of your company?** (`question2`, text, required: false)
	* Enter a brief description of your company.
* **What makes your company unique?** (`question3`, text, required: false)
	* Explain what makes your company unique.
* **How will you approach this project?** (`question4`, text, required: false)
	* Explain how you will approach this project.
* **Why do you believe your company is the right choice for this project?** (`question5`, text, required: false)
	* Explain why you believe your company is the right choice for this project.
* **Can you provide a detailed proposal in response to this RFP?** (`question6`, note, required: false)
	* Attach a detailed proposal in response to this RFP.
* **What is the total cost for this project?** (`question7`, number, required: false)
	* Enter the total cost for this project.
* **What is the total number of days to complete this project?** (`question8`, number, required: false)
	* Enter the total number of days to complete this project.
