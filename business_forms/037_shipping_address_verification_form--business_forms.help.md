# shipping_address_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shipping address verification form is used for verifying shipping addresses for orders or order groups, preventing shipping errors and ensuring correct delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with the correct shipping address information for the order or order group.
2. Select the correct shipping method from the available options.
3. Enter the delivery date and time if required.
4. Enter any relevant contact information, including email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Street Address** (street_address, text, optional): Enter the physical address of the shipping location.
* **Apartment Number** (apartment_number, text, optional): Enter the apartment number if applicable.
* **City** (city, text, optional): Enter the city of the shipping address.
* **State** (state, text, optional): Enter the state of the shipping address.
* **Zip** (postal_code, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the shipping address.
* **Country** (country, text, optional): Enter the country of the shipping address.
* **Shipping Method** (shipping_method_1, select_one, optional): Select one of the available shipping methods.
* **Additional Shipping Methods** (shipping_method_2, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional shipping methods applicable.
* **Delivery Date** (delivery_date, date, optional): Enter the delivery date if required.
* **Delivery Time** (delivery_time, time, optional): Enter the delivery time if required.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter an email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (phone, text, optional): Enter a phone number for contact purposes.
