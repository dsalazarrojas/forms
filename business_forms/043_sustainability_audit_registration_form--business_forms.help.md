# sustainability_audit_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sustainability audit registration form is designed to collect information about organizations and their sustainability audit requests. This form is meant to be a straightforward way to gather necessary details from the user for the purpose of processing their sustainability audit request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your organization's details on the "Organization Details" page. This includes filling out the required and optional fields to give us a comprehensive understanding of your organization.
2. Submit your audit request on the "Audit Request" page.
3. Choose a preferred date for the audit from the "Preferred Date" page.
4. Enter your contact details on the "Contact Details" page. This includes selecting the assigned person or organization from a list of options, providing their email address and contact phone number.
5. Add any additional contact notes on the "Contact Notes" page.
6. Finally, add any additional notes on the "Notes" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Details** (`Organization Details`, text, required/optional): Provide your organization's name, address, and other relevant details. This information is crucial for us to understand your organization's context.
* **Audit Request** (`Audit Request`, text, required/optional): Describe the purpose and nature of your audit request. This will help us to tailor our support to your specific needs.
* **Preferred Date** (`Preferred Date`, date, required/optional): Select a date that suits you best for the audit. Please note that we will do our best to accommodate your preferred date.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, required/optional): Fill in your contact person's name, email address, and phone number. This ensures we can contact them efficiently.
* **Assigned To** (`Assigned To`, select_one, required/optional): Choose from a list of options who will be handling your request. This is crucial for us to direct the necessary resources.
* **Contact E-mail** (`Contact E-mail`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact person's email address so we can keep in touch with you.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact person's phone number for further communication.
* **Contact Notes** (`Contact Notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional contact notes or instructions. This will help us to better understand your needs.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required/optional): Add any general notes or observations. This is an opportunity for you to provide us with any additional context.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure a smooth processing of your sustainability audit request. If you have any doubts or need further clarification, please feel free to contact us.
