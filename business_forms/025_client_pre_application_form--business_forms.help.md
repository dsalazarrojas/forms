# Client Pre Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Pre Application Form is a mandatory form for clients who wish to apply to work with our corporation. It helps us understand their business and application details, allowing us to provide a smooth and guided experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the requested fields accurately and thoroughly.
2. For each field, make sure to provide the most up-to-date information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Current Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Please enter your current job title or role within your business.
* **Business Information** (`section_business_info`, note, optional): This section is for any additional business information that you would like to provide.
* **Legal Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Please enter your business's legal name as it is registered with the relevant authorities.
* **Business Structure** (`business_structure`, select_one, required): Please select the structure of your business from the options provided: Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, or Corporation.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, required): Please enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Estimated Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, optional): Please select your estimated annual revenue from the provided options.
* **Number of Employees** (`employee_count`, number, optional): Please enter the number of employees you have.
* **Application Details** (`section_application`, note, optional): This section is for any additional application details that you would like to provide.
* **Primary Purpose of this Application** (`application_purpose`, text, required): Please enter the primary reason for your application.
* **Desired Service or Product** (`desired_service`, select_one, required): Please select the desired service or product from the provided options.
* **Have you applied with our corporation before?** (`previous_application`, select_one, optional): If you have applied with us before, please select 'True'.
* **Contact Information** (`section_verification`, note, optional): This section is for any contact information that you would like to provide.
* **Business Website URL** (`business_website`, text, optional): Please enter your business's website URL if applicable.
* **Primary Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Please enter the primary contact email for your business.
* **I certify that all information provided is accurate** (`info_accuracy_cert`, select_one, required): Please select 'True' to confirm that all information provided is accurate.

Note: The form is divided into three main sections: Business Information, Application Details, and Contact and Verification. Each section is designed to guide you through the application process. Make sure to review each field carefully and provide accurate information to ensure a smooth experience.
