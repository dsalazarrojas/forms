This form is designed to help organizations and businesses submit applications to join the Digitalization Advisory Network (DAN). The purpose of the form is to gather information about the applicant's organization, including its name, contact person, email address, phone number, address, and category of operation. This form is meant to be completed by someone authorized by the organization, such as a representative or manager.

The information collected will be used by DAN to evaluate the application and provide support to the approved members. The form is divided into several sections to ensure that all necessary information is collected in a clear and concise manner.

The following is a guide to help applicants complete the form correctly.

## Purpose
The Digitalization Advisory Network Application Form is a tool used to collect information from organizations that wish to join the DAN. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to increase the chances of approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the organization's name in the "Organization Name" field.
2.  Enter the contact person's name and title in the "Contact Person" field.
3.  Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Phone" field.
5.  Fill in the organization's address in the "Address Line 1" and "Address Line 2" fields.
6.  Select the category of operation for your organization from the list in the "Category" field.
7.  Enter a brief description of your organization in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required/optional): This is the name of the organization applying for membership in the Digitalization Advisory Network.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): This is the name and title of the person authorized to represent the organization in the application process.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required/optional): This is the email address of the contact person where the organization's representative will receive communication from the Digitalization Advisory Network.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): This is the phone number of the contact person where the organization's representative will receive calls or messages from the Digitalization Advisory Network.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, required/optional): This is the first line of the organization's address.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, text, required/optional): This is the second line of the organization's address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): This is the city where the organization is located.
* **State** (`state`, select, optional): This is the state or province where the organization is located. Select one of the provided options.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, required/optional): This is the zip code or postal code of the organization's address.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): This is a brief description of the organization's goals and objectives.
* **Category** (`category`, select multiple, optional): This is the category of operation for the organization. Select multiple options that best describe the organization's activities.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): This is an optional field for the organization to specify a tool they would like to be assigned to them after approval.

Note: The organization must be a real entity with a physical presence. Address and phone number are not required but highly recommended.
