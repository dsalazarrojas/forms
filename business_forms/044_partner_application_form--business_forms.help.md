# Partner Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Partner Application Form is designed to collect information from potential partners who are interested in partnering with our company. The form will be reviewed and evaluated to determine the potential partner's alignment with our company's goals and standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer all optional fields truthfully.
3. Review your answers for completeness and consistency.
4. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Data** (Text): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Contact Information** (Select Multiple): Select from the options to indicate your preferred contact methods (e.g., Email, Phone, etc.).
* **Company Information** (Text): Enter your company's name, address, and other identifying details.
* **Partner Experience** (Text): Describe your experience with our company or in the industry.
* **Partner Qualifications** (Text): List any relevant qualifications or certifications you hold.
* **Partner Services** (Text): Describe the services you offer or specialize in.
* **Business Details** (Text): Provide details about your business model, financials, and ownership structure.
* **Partner Agreement** (Text): Outline the terms and conditions of your agreement with us.
* **Communication Channels** (Text): List your preferred communication channels (e.g., Email, Phone, Meetings, etc.).
* **Business Model** (Text): Describe your business model and how you operate.
* **Ownership Structure** (Text): Explain your ownership structure and decision-making process.
* **Financials** (Text): Provide an overview of your financial situation and performance.
* **Tax ID Number** (Text): Enter your tax ID number or social security number.
* **Partner Decision** (Select One): Choose the option that best describes your partner decision.
* **Partner Status** (Select One): Choose the option that best describes your current status as a partner.
* **Partner Relationship** (Text): Describe your relationship with our company (e.g., current partner, prospective partner, etc.).
