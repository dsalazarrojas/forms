# product_authenticity_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and verify the authenticity of products, likely for use in B2B or B2C sales contexts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your contact information in the "User Information" field.
2. Select your type of business (B2B or consumer) from the "User Type" dropdown.
3. Describe the product you are verifying in the "Product Information" field.
4. Choose the type of product (electronic or physical) in the "Product Type" field.
5. Enter the date and time of purchase in the "Purchase Date" and "Purchase Time" fields, respectively.
6. Select the authenticity status of the product in the "Product Authenticity Verification" field.
7. Record the date and time of verification in the "Verification Date" and "Verification Time" fields, respectively.
8. If necessary, upload a product image or note any relevant details in the "Product Image" field.
9. Provide business information related to the product in the "Business Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (User Information)**: A field for entering contact details such as name, email, and phone number.
* **User Type (User Type)**: A field to categorize yourself as a business or consumer for sales context purposes.
* **Product Information (Product Information)**: A field for providing a detailed description of the product.
* **Product Type (Product Type)**: A field to select between electronic or physical products.
* **Purchase Date (Purchase Date)**: A field for recording the date of purchase.
* **Purchase Time (Purchase Time)**: A field for recording the time of purchase.
* **Product Authenticity Verification (Product Authenticity Verification)**: A field to verify the authenticity of the product.
* **Verification Date (Verification Date)**: A field for recording the date of verification.
* **Verification Time (Verification Time)**: A field for recording the verification time.
* **Product Image (Product Image)**: A field for noting any relevant images or details about the product.
* **Business Information (Business Information)**: A field for providing business details related to the product.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you need help with any field, contact the support team at form@gic.mx.
