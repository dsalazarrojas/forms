# Consulting Services Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request approval for consulting services, providing essential details about the requestor, project, and consultant. It's a crucial document for official decision-making within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed before submitting.
2. Be accurate and clear in providing personal and project details.
3. Double-check the approval workflow status after submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Full Name** (`requestor_full_name`, **text**, required): Enter your full name.
* **Requestor Department** (`requestor_department`, **text**, required): Indicate the department requesting this service.
* **Request Submission Date** (`request_submission_date`, **date**, required): Enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, **text**, required): Provide a clear and concise title for the project.
* **Business Justification** (`business_justification`, **text**, required): Explain why this project is necessary and beneficial for the organization.
* **Estimated Project Cost** (`estimated_project_cost`, **number**, required): Enter the total budget for the project.
* **Budget Source** (`budget_source`, **text**, optional): Specify where the funding comes from (e.g., internal, external, or other).
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, **text**, required): Enter the full name of the consultant.
* **Consultant Company** (`consultant_company`, **text**, required): Enter the name of the consulting company.
* **Manager Approval Status** (`manager_approval_status`, **select_one**, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Approved
	+ Pending
	+ Rejected
* **HR Approval Status** (`hr_approval_status`, **select_one**, required): Select one of the following options:

	+ Rejected
