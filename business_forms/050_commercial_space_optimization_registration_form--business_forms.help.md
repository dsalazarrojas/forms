# Commercial Space Optimization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The commercial space optimization registration form is designed to help us understand your organization's needs and provide a starting point for our commercial space optimization consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your organization's name and details.
2. Select your primary business industry.
3. Describe your primary challenges with your current space.
4. Define your primary goals for this project.
5. Estimate the total square footage for optimization.
6. Provide the number of regular occupants/employees.
7. Choose a desired project start date and target budget range.
8. Enter the project lead contact's name, title, email address, and phone number.
9. (Optional) Provide any additional context or specific requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization Name** (client_organization_name, text, required): Please enter your organization's name as it is known to the public or in official communications.
- **Primary Business Industry** (primary_business_industry, select_one, required): Select the industry category that best describes your organization's primary business.
- **Primary Challenges with Current Space** (current_space_challenges, select_multiple, required): Describe your primary challenges with your current space using the provided options or add any other challenges not listed.
- **What are your primary goals for this project?** (optimization_goals, text, required): Define your primary goals for this project, such as improving employee satisfaction or reducing operational costs.
- **Estimated Total Square Footage for Optimization** (total_square_footage_optimization, number, required): Estimate the total square footage of the space you would like to optimize.
- **Number of Regular Occupants/Employees** (number_of_occupants, number, required): Enter the number of regular occupants or employees.
- **Desired Project Start Date** (desired_project_start, date, required): Choose the desired start date for your project.
- **Target Budget Range for Optimization** (optimization_budget_range, select_one, required): Select the target budget range for your project.
- **Lead Contact Name** (project_lead_name, text, required): Enter the name of the project lead.
- **Job Title** (project_lead_title, text, required): Enter the job title of the project lead.
- **Professional Email** (project_lead_email, email, required): Enter the email address of the project lead.
- **Phone Number** (project_lead_phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of the project lead.
- **Additional Context or Specific Requirements** (additional_project_info, text, optional): Provide any additional context or specific requirements for your project.

## Tips
- Please register a single organization per form submission.
- If you have multiple organizations, submit a separate form for each.
- Ensure that you are providing accurate and clear information about your organization's needs and goals.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
