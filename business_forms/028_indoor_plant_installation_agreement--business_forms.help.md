# indoor_plant_installation_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect details from clients about their indoor plant installation project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Agreement Form (page 1).
2. Enter your Client Details (page 2).
3. Specify the Project Scope (page 3).
4. Select a schedule for the project (page 4).
5. Confirm your consent to proceed with the project (page 5).
6. Provide your contact information (page 6).
7. Select the services and plants you want to include in the project (page 7-8).
8. Enter the quantity and cost of the plants (page 9-10).
9. Specify the total cost and payment terms (page 11-12).
10. Choose your preferred payment method and payment due date (page 13-14).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Form** (`1`, `text`, required): Please sign this form to indicate your agreement to the project terms.
* **Client Details** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Project Scope** (`3`, `text`, required): Describe the specific details of the project.
* **Schedule** (`4`, `date`, required): Select a date for the project to start and finish.
* **Consent** (`5`, `number`, required): Confirm that you understand and agree to the project terms (enter `1` for yes, `0` for no).
* **Client Phone** (`6`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Client Email** (`7`, `email`, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Service Details** (`8`, `text`, required): Describe the services you want to include in the project.
* **Plant Types** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the types of plants you want to install (e.g., Fiddle Leaf, Snake Plant, Spider Plant, Bamboo Palm, Peace Lily).
* **Plant Quantity** (`10`, `number`, required): Enter the number of plants you want to install.
* **Plant Cost** (`11`, `number`, required): Enter the cost of each plant.
* **Service Cost** (`12`, `number`, required): Enter the cost of the services included in the project.
* **Total Cost** (`13`, `number`, required): The total cost of the project is calculated based on the plant and service costs.
* **Payment Terms** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Method** (`15`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you want to pay for the project (e.g., Cash, Credit, Debit).
* **Payment Due Date** (`16`, `date`, optional): Select the date by which you want to pay for the project.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the project is executed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us directly.
* Review the calculated total cost carefully before proceeding with the project.
