# Stock Options Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Options Declaration Form is designed to gather information about stock options from employees. It is a crucial document that helps businesses to accurately track and manage their stock options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled.
2. Enter your employee details in the "employee" field.
3. Select the relevant stock options you have from the "options" field.
4. Enter the date you received the stock options in the "date" field.
5. Enter the time of receiving the stock options in the "time" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
8. Enter any additional notes about your stock options in the "note" field.
9. Enter the assigned tool in the "assigned_tool" field.
10. Enter the category of the stock options in the "category" field.
11. Enter a title for your stock options in the "title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee (1)** (`text`, optional): Please enter your employee details here.
* **options (2)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all the relevant stock options you have from the list below.
  - Option 1
  - Option 2
  - Option 3
* **date (3)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date you received the stock options.
* **time (4)** (`time`, optional): Enter the time you received the stock options.
* **email (5)** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address here.
* **phone (6)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **note (7)** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your stock options.
* **assigned_tool (8)** (`text`, optional): Enter the assigned tool here.
* **category (9)** (`text`, optional): Enter the category of the stock options.
* **title (10)** (`text`, optional): Enter a title for your stock options here.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* Use the "options" field to select all relevant stock options you have.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
