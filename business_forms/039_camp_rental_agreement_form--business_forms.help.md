# Camp Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Rental Agreement Form is a crucial tool for camp rentals. It allows renters to provide essential information about themselves and their event, while also ensuring that they understand their responsibilities and obligations during the rental period.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. Ensure that all fields are filled out with the correct information to facilitate the rental process.
2. **Review each section carefully**. The form is divided into three main sections: Renter Information, Event and Facility Details, and Terms and Conditions.
3. **Sign and date the form**. The Renter Electronic Signature field is a required field and must be completed by the renter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Renter Name (Individual or Rep)**: Please enter the name of the individual or representative responsible for the rental.
* **Organization Name (If applicable)**: If the renter is an organization, please enter the name of the organization.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter the email address of the renter or their representative.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the renter or their representative.
* **Facilities to be Rented**: Select all facilities that the renter wishes to rent. Please be specific and only select the facilities that apply.
* **Nature of the Event**: Provide a brief description of the event, such as "Family Reunion" or "Corporate Retreat".
* **Expected Number of Attendees**: Enter the expected number of people attending the event.
* **Rental Start Date and Time**: Enter the start date and time of the rental period.
* **Rental End Date and Time**: Enter the end date and time of the rental period.
* **Agreement to Rental Fees and Deposits**: Confirm that the renter agrees to pay all rental fees and deposits as outlined in the quote.
* **Facility Usage Rules and Clean-up Policy**: Confirm that the renter agrees to follow all camp rules and leave facilities in good condition.
* **Insurance and Liability Requirement**: Confirm that the renter acknowledges the requirement to provide a certificate of insurance.
* **Renter Electronic Signature**: Please type your full name in this field to confirm your agreement.
* **Date of Agreement**: This field will be automatically populated with the current date.
* **Internal Approval Status**: This field is for internal use only and indicates the approval status of the rental.

By following these steps and explanations, the Camp Rental Agreement Form will ensure that all parties involved are clear on their responsibilities and obligations during the rental period.
