# Waste Management Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waste Management Application Form is designed to collect information from users about waste management services they require. This form helps facilitate communication between the service provider and the customer, ensuring the collection and proper disposal of waste in an organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the fields provided, which are labeled as required/optional.
2. Enter your User Information, including any relevant details that may affect your service request.
3. Provide Service Details about the type of service you require.
4. Enter your Contact Information to allow us to contact you regarding your service request.
5. Enter your Address, which is necessary for logistics and service delivery.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send your service request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter any relevant details that may affect your service request.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide a description of the service you require, including any specific needs or preferences.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number, email, and/or any other contact details we may need to contact you.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your physical address to ensure proper logistics and service delivery.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional):
	+ Click this button to send your completed form and request the service.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required and optional fields to ensure your service request is complete.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to prevent errors.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us at [insert contact email/phone number].
