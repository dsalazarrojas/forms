# Team Collaboration Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help team members provide updates on their check-in activities for projects, including the project name, check-in time, location, method, and notes. It's a crucial tool for team leads and managers to track team progress and stay informed about ongoing projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the project you are checking in for by choosing the project name from the options provided.
2. Enter your check-in time using the date field.
3. Choose the location where you checked in (e.g., office, home, or remote).
4. Select the method you used to check in (e.g., in-person, phone, or video conference).
5. Provide any additional notes or comments about your check-in activity.
6. For projects that require regular check-ins, select the frequency of check-ins (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
7. If applicable, select the teams and projects you are involved in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project** (Select One): This field allows you to select the project you are checking in for. Choose the correct project name from the list of options.
  - **Team Lead** (Text): Enter the name of the team lead who assigned you the project.
  - **Check In Time** (Date): Enter the date and time you checked in for the project.
  - **Early/Late** (Time): Enter the time you arrived early or late for the check-in (if applicable).
  - **Check In Location** (Text): Select the location where you checked in (e.g., office, home, or remote).
  - **Check In Method** (Text): Choose the method you used to check in (e.g., in-person, phone, or video conference).
  - **Check In Notes** (Note): Provide any additional notes or comments about your check-in activity.
  - **Project Name** (Text): Enter the name of the project you are involved in (if not already selected in the "Project" field).
  - **Check In Frequency** (Number): Select the frequency of check-ins required for this project (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
  - **Days/Weeks/Months** (Select One/Select One): Select the frequency of check-ins for this project (e.g., daily, weekly, or monthly).
  - **Check In Method 2** (Text): Choose the method you used to check in (if different from the first method).
  - **Check In Comments** (Text): Provide any additional comments or notes about your check-in activity.
  - **Team** (Select One/Select Multiple): Select the teams you are involved in (if applicable).
  - **Team 2** (Select Multiple): Select the teams you are involved in (if different from the first team).
  - **Project 2** (Select Multiple): Select the projects you are involved in (if different from the first project).
  - **Check In Frequency 2/3/4/5** (Number): Select the frequency of check-ins required for this project (if different from the first frequency).
  - **Check In Report** (Text): Enter any additional information about your check-in activity (if applicable).
  - **Email** (Email): Enter your email address (if you want to receive updates or notifications).
  - **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number (if you want to receive calls or notifications).
