# Stakeholder Analysis Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Stakeholder Analysis Form is designed to identify and analyze stakeholders involved in a project. It helps to gather detailed information about each stakeholder, their role, contact information, and relationship with the project. This form is crucial for understanding stakeholder engagement, influence, and potential impact on the project's success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Project Name** field with the name of the project being analyzed.
2. Provide a brief description of the project in the **Project Description** field.
3. Select the **Stakeholder List** field and choose one or more stakeholders from the list that are relevant to your project.
4. For each selected stakeholder, fill in their **Stakeholder Name**, **Stakeholder Role**, **Stakeholder Organisation**, and **Stakeholder Influence** fields.
5. Provide a brief description of the stakeholder's relationship with the project in the **Stakeholder Relationship** field, if applicable.
6. Enter the stakeholder's contact information, including **Stakeholder Contact** and **Spike** (optional).
7. If applicable, add any additional comments about the stakeholder in the **Stakeholder Comments** field.
8. Finally, select the **Stakeholder Influence** field and choose one of the three options: **Positive**, **Negative**, or **Neutral**, depending on the stakeholder's influence on the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project being analyzed.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the project.
* **Stakeholder List** (`stakeholder_list`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more stakeholders from the list that are relevant to your project. 
* **Stakeholder Name** (`stakeholder_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the stakeholder.
* **Stakeholder Role** (`stakeholder_role`, text, required): Enter the role of the stakeholder in the project.
* **Stakeholder Organisation** (`stakeholder_organisation`, text, required): Enter the stakeholder's organisation.
* **Stakeholder Contact** (`stakeholder_contact`, text, required): Enter the stakeholder's contact information.
* **Email** (`stakeholder_email`, email, optional): Enter the stakeholder's email address (if applicable).
* **Address** (`stakeholder_address`, text, optional): Enter the stakeholder's address (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`stakeholder_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the stakeholder.
* **Relationship** (`stakeholder_relationship`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the stakeholder's relationship with the project.
* **Influence** (`stakeholder_influence`, select_one, required): Select one of the three options: **Positive**, **Negative**, or **Neutral**, depending on the stakeholder's influence on the project.
* **Interest** (`stakeholder_interest`, number, optional): Enter the stakeholder's level of interest in the project (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`stakeholder_comments`, note, required): Add any additional comments about the stakeholder.
* **Notes**: Note fields are for additional information that may be relevant to the stakeholder, but are not part of the required fields.
