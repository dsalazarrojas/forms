# Air Conditioning Daily Requisition Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Air Conditioning Daily Requisition Report Form is used for technicians to submit their daily work reports, including details on the work order, location, time spent, and materials used. This helps management keep track of daily activities for invoicing, accounting, and planning.

## How To Complete This Form

- **Fill out each field carefully**, making sure to provide exact values and descriptions where applicable. 
- Ensure you have a valid work order (Job Number) to reference against. 
- Select the correct tools needed for each job from the list provided. 
- Record accurate arrival and departure times, including the time spent on the job. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Technician Name**: Enter the name of the technician who performed the work. 
*   **Date**: Enter today's date in the format YYYY-MM-DD. 
*   **Job Number**: Enter the work order number from which you received the job. 
*   **Site Address**: Enter the address of the site where the work was done. 
*   **Copper Pipe Used (ft)**: If you used any copper pipe during the job, record the total length. 
*   **Insulation Used (tubes)**: If insulation was used during the job, enter the count. 
*   **Refrigerant Added (lbs)**: If refrigerant was added during the job, enter the exact weight. 
*   **Electrical Wire (ft)**: If electrical wire was used during the job, record the total length. 
*   **Fittings / Consumables**: If any fittings or consumables were used during the job, enter the count. 
*   **Tools Requested**: Select all tools used during the job from the list provided. 
*   **Arrival Time**: Record the time you arrived at the site. 
*   **Departure Time**: Record the time you departed the site. 
*   **Total Hours Worked**: Enter the total hours worked, excluding lunch breaks. 
*   **Daily Notes**: Provide any notes about the job, including any issues or delays encountered. 

## Tips

- Always provide accurate and detailed information to ensure that the data is used correctly for billing, planning, and future reference. 
- If unsure about any field, check with your supervisor or refer to previous job reports for guidance. 
- This form is a daily report and should be submitted at the end of each working day.
