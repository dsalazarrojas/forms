# Customer Credit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Credit Request Form is a tool for customers to provide information required for credit checks, financial planning, and order fulfillment. This form is intended for customers who are requesting credit and should not be used for other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the customer's name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the desired payment terms from the options provided.
3. Fill in the shipping and billing addresses, if applicable.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments as requested.
5. Review the form for accuracy before submission.
