# Client Suitability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Suitability Form is a questionnaire designed to gather information about your financial situation, investment goals, and risk tolerance. This information will help your investment advisor provide personalized recommendations tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please select "Do not know" or "Unable to assess" if it applies.
3. Please note that some fields are marked as "required," which means you must provide an answer to these questions to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Full Name (<name>, text, required)
Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. Date of Birth (<date_of_birth>, date, required)
Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YY".

### 3. Email Address (<client_email>, email, required)
Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.

### 4. Phone Number (<client_phone>, text, required)
Enter your contact phone number.

### 5. Employment Status (<employment_status>, select_one, required)
Indicate your employment status: Employed full-time, Employed part-time, Self-employed, Retired, Unemployed, or Other.

### 6. Annual Income Range (<annual_income>, select_one, required)
Select your annual income range: Under $50,000, $50,000 - $100,000, $100,000 - $250,000, $250,000 - $500,000, $500,000+, or No income.

### 7. Current Financial Situation (<financial_situation>, select_one, required)
Describe your current financial situation: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Unable to assess.

### 8. Investment Experience (<investment_experience>, select_one, required)
Rate your level of investment experience: Extensive experience, Moderate experience, Some experience, Minimal experience, No experience.

### 9. Investment Goals (<investment_goals>, select_one, required)
Identify your investment goals: Capital preservation, Income generation, Growth, Balanced approach, Mixed goals.

### 10. Investment Time Horizon (<investment_horizon>, select_one, required)
Specify your investment time horizon: Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, 10+ years.

### 11. Risk Tolerance (<risk_tolerance>, select_one, required)
Determine your risk tolerance: Very conservative, Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive, Very aggressive.

### 12. Liquid Assets for Investment (<liquid_assets>, select_one, required)
Select the range of liquid assets available for investment: Under $25,000, $25,000 - $100,000, $100,000 - $250,000, $250,000 - $500,000, $500,000+.

### 13. Do You Currently Have Investments? (<existing_investments>, select_one, required)
Do you have existing investments? Yes, substantial, Yes, moderate, Yes, minimal, False.

### 14. Knowledge of Investment Products (<investment_knowledge>, select_one, required)
Rate your knowledge of investment products: Very knowledgeable, Knowledgeable, Somewhat knowledgeable, Not knowledgeable.

### 15. Experience with Investment Losses (<prior_losses>, select_one, required)
Have you experienced significant investment losses? Yes, substantial, Yes, moderate, Yes, minimal, False.

### 16. I understand the risks and confirm this investment is suitable for me (<suitability_confirmation>, select_one, required)
Confirm that you understand the risks and believe this investment is suitable for you: I confirm, I do not confirm.

### 17. Date of Agreement (<agreement_date>, date, required)
Enter the date you are willing to agree to the terms of the investment.
