# Account Activation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request account activation for a business or organization. It collects information about the requestor and their account requirements to facilitate the activation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* **Requestor Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
	* **Requestor Email**: Enter your business email address.
	* **Requestor Phone**: Enter your business phone number.
2. Provide your company information:
	* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
	* **Company Address**: Enter the complete address of your company.
3. Select your account type:
	* Choose the type of account you are requesting from the list:
		+ Standard Business Account
		+ Premium Business Account
		+ Enterprise Account
		+ Trial Account
		+ Partner Account
		+ Other
4. Specify your account details:
	* **Requested Service**: Briefly describe the service or product related to your account request.
	* **Number of Users**: Enter the number of users who will need access to this account.
	* **Billing Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for billing.
	* **Billing Contact Email**: Enter the email address for billing communications.
	* **Billing Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number for billing inquiries.
5. Provide additional information:
	* **Activation Reason**: Explain why you need this account activated.
	* **Expected Usage Volume**: Select the expected volume of transactions or usage:
		+ Low (under 100/month)
		+ Medium (100-500/month)
		+ High (500-1000/month)
		+ Very High (over 1000/month)
		+ Variable
	* **Special Requirements**: If applicable, describe any special requirements for the account.
6. Confirm your account details:
	* **Compliance Requirements**: Select any compliance requirements for this account:
		+ SOC 2
		+ GDPR
		+ HIPAA
		+ PCI DSS
		+ SOX
		+ ISO 27001
		+ None
		+ Other
	* **Requested Activation Date**: Enter the date when you need the account activated.
	* **Technical Contact Name**: Enter the name of the technical contact person.
	* **Technical Contact Email**: Enter the email address for technical communications.
7. Confirm your agreement:
	* **Terms Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of service.
