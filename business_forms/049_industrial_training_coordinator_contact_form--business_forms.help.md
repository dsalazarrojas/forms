# industrial_training_coordinator_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and training needs from users to help in coordinating industrial training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
2. Describe your training needs in the "Training Needs" section.
3. Specify your preferred contact method in the "Preferred Contact" section.
4. If you have any other inquiries or requests, you can add them in the "Other Inquiries" section.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can add them in the "Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required): Please provide your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you regarding your training needs.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, `text`, required): Describe the type of training you are looking for, including the topic, duration, and any specific requirements or constraints.
* **Preferred Contact** (`preferred_contact`, `text`, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or in-person meeting).
* **Other Inquiries** (`other_inquiries`, `text`, optional): If you have any other requests or questions, please add them here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): If you have a preferred tool or software for the training, please specify it here.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, optional): This is a unique identifier for your submission. Please ignore it if you are not interested in tracking your submissions.
