# equipment_failure_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about equipment failure incidents in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the incident details in the "Incident Details" field.
*   Choose the type of equipment that failed in the "Equipment Type" field.
*   Describe the failure in the "Failure Description" field (optional).
*   Select the maintenance status of the equipment in the "Maintenance Status" field.
*   Enter the date of the incident in the "Incident Date" field (optional).
*   Enter the time of the incident in the "Incident Time" field (optional).
*   Specify the location of the equipment in the "Equipment Location" field.
*   Enter the name of the person who reported the incident in the "Reported By" field.
*   Select the status of the incident in the "Incident Status" field.
*   Choose the priority of the incident in the "Incident Priority" field.
*   Select the category of the incident in the "Incident Category" field.
*   Choose the subcategory of the incident in the "Incident Subcategory" field.
*   Enter a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
*   Enter the date the incident was reported in the "Incident Reported Date" field.
*   Enter the serial number of the equipment in the "Equipment Serial Number" field.
*   Enter a unique identifier for the incident in the "Incident ID" field.
*   Enter the part number of the equipment in the "Equipment Part Number" field.
*   Enter the model of the equipment in the "Equipment Model" field.
*   Enter the date and time range of the incident in the "Incident Date Range" and "Incident Time Range" fields.
*   Enter a status update for the incident in the "Incident Status Update" field.
*   Enter a resolution for the incident in the "Incident Resolution" field.
*   Enter the cause of the equipment failure in the "Equipment Failure Cause" field.
*   Enter a description of the equipment failure in the "Equipment Failure Description" field.
*   Choose the resolution status of the incident in the "Incident Resolution Status" field.
