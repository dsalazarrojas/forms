# Employee Morale Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Employee Morale Survey is a tool used by management to gauge employee satisfaction and engagement. This survey aims to collect information about employee experiences, perceptions, and opinions about their workplace and work environment. It is crucial that this data is collected to identify areas of improvement, make informed decisions, and foster a more positive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the Employee Morale Survey, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and answer every question truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Start with the first page, "Employee Experience".
3. Read each question carefully and enter your response in the provided fields.
4. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
5. If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Lead** (`page_3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all team members or supervisors who directly report to you.
* **Work Hours** (`page_4`, `time`, optional): Enter your normal work hours or any adjustments you have made to suit your needs.
* **Work Life Balance** (`page_5`, `number`, optional): Rate how balanced you feel your work and personal life are.
* **Overall Experience** (`page_6`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your job and work environment.

## Tips

* Answer honestly and accurately, as your input will help shape your workplace's future development.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas, feel free to share them in the comment section.
* Remember, this survey is confidential and anonymous.
