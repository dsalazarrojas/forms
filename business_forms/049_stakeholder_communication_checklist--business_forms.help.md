# Stakeholder Communication Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stakeholder Communication Checklist is a form designed to help project managers track and document their communication with stakeholders at regular intervals. This form provides a template for scheduling and recording communications, ensuring consistency and clarity in stakeholder engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Project Manager** name or title.
2.  Enter the **Stakeholder Name** and their relevant contact information (if provided).
3.  Select the **Communication Method** used for the stakeholder communication (e.g., email, phone, in-person, video call, or text message).
4.  Choose the **Communication Frequency** (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
5.  Enter the **Communication Date** and **Communication Time** of the last or next scheduled communication with the stakeholder.
6.  Add any **Notes** related to the communication or stakeholder engagement.
7.  Enter the **Email** and **Phone** contact information of the stakeholder (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Project Manager**: Enter the name or title of the project manager responsible for the communication.
*   **Stakeholder Name**: Enter the name of the stakeholder being communicated with.
*   **Communication Method**: Select how you will communicate with the stakeholder (e.g., via email, phone, in-person, video call, or text message).
*   **Communication Frequency**: Choose how often you will communicate with the stakeholder (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
*   **Communication Date**: Enter the date of the last or next scheduled communication with the stakeholder.
*   **Communication Time**: Enter the time of the last or next scheduled communication with the stakeholder.
*   **Notes**: Add any additional information or comments about the communication or stakeholder engagement.
*   **Email**: Enter the email address of the stakeholder (if available).
*   **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the stakeholder (if available).

## Tips

*   Make sure to update the form regularly to reflect current stakeholder communication status.
*   Use the **Notes** field to capture any important details or concerns related to stakeholder engagement.
*   Use the **Communication Method** and **Communication Frequency** fields to establish a consistent communication schedule.
*   Ensure that all relevant contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
