# link_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a link for a business listing. This form is used to provide a link to your business website or other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. Note: This field is required to ensure we can get in contact with you.
4. Fill in your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Website URL in the "Website" field.
6. Type in a message in the "Message" field, if you have any additional comments or information to provide.
7. Choose a valid Submission Date for your link in the "Submission Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your First Name as it should be displayed on your business listing.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your Last Name as it should be displayed on your business listing.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your business phone number.
* **Website** (`website`, Text, Optional): Enter your business website URL.
* **Message** (`message`, Note, Optional): If you have any additional comments or information to provide, type them here.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, Date, Optional): Choose a valid date for your link submission.
