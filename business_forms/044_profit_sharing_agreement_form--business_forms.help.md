# Profit Sharing Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Profit Sharing Agreement Form is used to record and document the terms of a profit-sharing arrangement between partners in a business venture. This form ensures clarity and transparency in defining the roles, percentages, and payment terms of all partners involved in the partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the General Information section with any relevant details about the partnership, such as the names and contact information of all partners.
2. In the Partners section, select the names of all partners involved in the partnership from the provided options. Multiple partners can be selected.
3. In the Share Percentage section, enter the percentage of profits each partner will receive.
4. For the Role section, select the role of each partner (Managing Partner, Partner, or Co-owner).
5. In the Consent section, add any additional details or comments about the partnership.
6. In the Signature section, capture the date and time of signing.
7. Set the Agreement Term using the date selector.
8. In the Term Duration section, enter the length of time the agreement will be in effect.
9. For the Distribution Term, select the frequency of profit distribution (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
10. Finally, choose the Payment Method for profit distribution (Check, Bank Transfer, or Electronic Payment).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_information`, `text`, required: false): This section is for providing any general information about the partnership.
* **Partners** (`partners`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all partners involved in the partnership from the provided options.
* **Share Percentage** (`share_percentage`, `number`, required: false): Enter the percentage of profits each partner will receive.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the role of each partner (Managing Partner, Partner, or Co-owner).
* **Consent** (`consent`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional details or comments about the partnership.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `time`, required: false): Capture the date and time of signing.
* **Agreement Term** (`agreement_term`, `date`, required: false): Set the date when the agreement will come into effect.
* **Term Duration** (`term_duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of time the agreement will be in effect.
* **Distribution Term** (`distribution_term`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the frequency of profit distribution (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the method of profit distribution (Check, Bank Transfer, or Electronic Payment).

## Tips
* Ensure all partner names are correctly selected in the Partners section.
* Double-check the Share Percentage to ensure accurate profit distribution.
* Confirm the Agreement Term and Term Duration to ensure clarity on the agreement's duration.
* Carefully select the Distribution Term and Payment Method to ensure smooth and timely profit distribution.
