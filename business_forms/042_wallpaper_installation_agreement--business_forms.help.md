# Wallpaper Installation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for obtaining an agreement from a client for the installation of wallpaper. It is used to gather information about the client, the scope of the job, and other relevant details for the installation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's details in the "Client Details" field.
2. Describe the scope of the job in the "Job Scope" field.
3. Provide details about the installation in the "Installation Details" field.
4. Indicate the installation scope in the "Installation Scope" field. Select "Yes" if the wallpaper will be installed on multiple walls, otherwise select "No".
5. In the "Consent" field, confirm that you understand the terms of the agreement.
6. In the "Client Confirmation" field, ask the client to confirm they have read and understand the agreement.
7. In the "Installers Confirmation" field, confirm that you, as the installer, understand the agreement and will perform the work according to its terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Details** (1, text, required/optional): Enter the client's name, address, and contact information.
- **Job Scope** (2, text, required/optional): Describe the area to be covered, type of wallpaper, and any other relevant details about the job.
- **Installation Details** (3, text, required/optional): Provide the details about the installation, such as date, time, and location.
- **Installation Scope** (4, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether the wallpaper will be installed on multiple walls or not.
- **Consent** (5, text, required/optional): Confirm that you understand the terms of the agreement.
- **Client Confirmation** (6, text, required/optional): Ask the client to confirm they have read and understand the agreement.
- **Installers Confirmation** (7, text, required/optional): Confirm that you, as the installer, understand the agreement and will perform the work according to its terms.
