# Associates Sales Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from associates regarding their sales contract, including details about their employment status, sales performance, and contract terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
    - Please ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
    - Fill in each field accurately and completely.
    - Review your work before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Date** (contract_date, date, required): Enter the date of this agreement.
* **Contract ID Number** (contract_id, text, required): Enter the reference number for this contract.
* **Principal/Owner First Name** (principal_first_name, text, required): Enter the first name of the business owner.
* **Principal/Owner Last Name** (principal_last_name, text, required): Enter the last name of the business owner.
* **Business Name** (principal_business_name, text, required): Enter the legal business name.
* **Principal Business Address** (principal_address, text, required): Enter the full address of the business.
* **Principal Phone Number** (principal_phone, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the business.
* **Principal Email Address** (principal_email, email, required): Enter the contact email address of the business.
* **Associate First Name** (associate_first_name, text, required): Enter the first name of the sales associate.
* **Associate Last Name** (associate_last_name, text, required): Enter the last name of the sales associate.
* **Associate Address** (associate_address, text, required): Enter the full address of the sales associate.
* **Associate Phone Number** (associate_phone, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the sales associate.
* **Associate Email Address** (associate_email, email, required): Enter the contact email address of the sales associate.
* **Employment Type** (employment_type, select_one, required): Choose the correct employment type:
	+ Full-Time Employee
	+ Part-Time Employee
	+ Independent Contractor
	+ Commission-Only
	+ Other
* **Employment Start Date** (start_date, date, required): Enter the date when employment begins.
* **Sales Territory or Region** (sales_territory, text, required): Enter the geographic area assigned for sales.
* **Products or Services to Sell** (products_services, text, required): Enter what the associate will sell.
* **Sales Targets or Quotas** (sales_targets, text, required): Enter the expected sales goals.
* **Compensation Structure** (compensation_structure, text, required): Enter how the associate will be paid.
* **Base Salary (if applicable)** (base_salary, number, optional): Enter the monthly or annual salary (if applicable).
* **Commission Percentage** (commission_percentage, number, optional): Enter the percentage of sales (if applicable).
* **Benefits Provided** (benefits_provided, text, optional): Enter health insurance, bonuses, etc. (if applicable).
* **Confidentiality and Non-Compete Terms** (confidentiality_terms, text, required): Enter the restrictions on associates (e.g., confidentiality, non-compete terms).
* **Termination Clause** (termination_clause, text, required): Enter how this contract can end.
* **Contract Term Length** (contract_term, text, required): Enter the duration of the agreement.

Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and efficient process.
