# Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from businesses or individuals for internal business purposes. It should not be used for general public inquiries or to gather information about others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Full name" field.
2. Provide your business email address in the "Business email address" field.
3. If applicable, enter your company name and job title in the "Company name" and "Position or title" fields.
4. Enter your direct contact phone number in the "Phone number" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Choose your preferred method of contact (Email, Phone, or No preference) in the "Preferred contact method" field.
7. Optionally, specify a time window for contact in the "Best time to contact" field.
8. If you have any additional information, type it in the "Additional information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full name (Full name)**: Enter your name as it should be displayed in our records.
- **Company (Company name)**: If you work for a company, enter its name here.
- **Position (Position or title)**: Enter your current job title or position.
- **Business email address (Email)**: Enter your work email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone number (Phone number)**: Enter your direct contact phone number.
- **Address (Address)**: Enter your company or personal address.
- **Preferred contact method (Preferred contact method)**: Choose how you prefer to be contacted (Email, Phone, or No preference).
- **Best time to contact (Best time to contact)**: If you have a preferred time for contact, specify it here.
- **Additional information (Additional information)**: Type in any other notes about your business or yourself.
