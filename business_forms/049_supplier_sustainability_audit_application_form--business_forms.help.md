# Supplier Sustainability Audit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Sustainability Audit Application Form is used to collect information from suppliers regarding their sustainability practices and certifications. This form helps us to assess the supplier's commitment to sustainability and ensure their alignment with our company's sustainability standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide the supplier's details in the "supplyier_details" field.
2. In the "certificates" field, please list any relevant sustainability certificates or certifications held by the supplier.
3. In the "sustainability_practices" field, describe the supplier's current sustainability practices or initiatives.
4. In the "contact_details" field, provide the supplier's contact information for follow-up.
5. In the "certification_details" field, provide any additional information about the supplier's certifications.
6. In the "sustainability_practices_2" field, describe any additional sustainability practices or initiatives implemented by the supplier.
7. In the "additional_comments" field, provide any additional comments or information that might be helpful for the audit process.
8. If the supplier has multiple certifications, select the correct option in the "certification_status_2" field to indicate the status of each certification.
9. In the "supplier_sustainability_status" field, select the correct option to indicate the supplier's sustainability status.
10. In the "supplier_sustainability_practices_3" field, describe any additional sustainability practices or initiatives implemented by the supplier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplyier Details**: This field is used to capture the supplier's details.
* **Certificates**: This field is used to list any relevant sustainability certificates or certifications held by the supplier.
* **Sustainability Practices**: This field is used to describe the supplier's current sustainability practices or initiatives.
* **Contact Details**: This field is used to capture the supplier's contact information for follow-up.
* **Certification Details**: This field is used to provide any additional information about the supplier's certifications.
* **Sustainability Practices 2**: This field is used to describe any additional sustainability practices or initiatives implemented by the supplier.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or information that might be helpful for the audit process.
* **Certification Status 2**: This field is used to indicate the status of each certification held by the supplier.
* **Supplier Sustainability Status**: This field is used to select the correct option to indicate the supplier's sustainability status.
* **Supplier Sustainability Practices 3**: This field is used to describe any additional sustainability practices or initiatives implemented by the supplier.
