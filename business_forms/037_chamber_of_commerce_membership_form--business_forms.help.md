# Chamber Of Commerce Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chamber of Commerce Membership Form is a tool used to collect information from businesses interested in becoming members of the Chamber.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a successful application.
2. Review each field for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
3. Submit the form to the Chamber of Commerce office.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (Application Date, date, Required): Date of membership application, in the format of month/day/year.
* **Business Legal Name** (Business Legal Name, text, Required): Your business's legal name as registered with the state and local government.
* **DBA Name** (DBA Name, text, Optional): If your business operates under a different name, enter that name here.
* **Business Structure** (Business Structure, select_one, Required): The legal structure of your business, such as sole proprietorship, partnership, LLC, corporation, etc.
* **Business Category** (Business Category, select_one, Required): The primary industry or category your business falls under, such as agriculture, finance, education, etc.
* **Business Description** (Business Description, text, Required): Brief description of your products or services.
* **Street Address** (Street Address, text, Required): Your physical business address.
* **City** (City, text, Required): City location of your business.
* **State** (State, text, Required): State where your business is located.
* **ZIP Code** (ZIP Code, text, Required): Your business's zip code.
* **Business Phone** (Business Phone, text, Required): Primary phone number of your business.
* **Business Fax** (Business Fax, text, Optional): Fax number of your business, if available.
* **Business Email** (Business Email, email, Required): Primary email address of your business.
* **Business Website** (Business Website, text, Optional): Your business's website URL, if available.
* **Social Media Links** (Social Media Links, text, Optional): Links to your business's social media profiles.
* **Year Established** (Year Established, number, Required): The year your business was founded.
* **Number of Employees** (Number of Employees, select_one, Required): Number of employees in your business.
* **Primary Contact Name** (Primary Contact Name, text, Required): Full name of your primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Title** (Primary Contact Title, text, Required): Job title of your primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Email** (Primary Contact Email, email, Required): Email address of your primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Phone** (Primary Contact Phone, text, Required): Phone number of your primary contact person.
* **Membership Type** (Membership Type, select_one, Required): Select the type of membership you are interested in, such as regular business membership, non-profit membership, etc.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (How Did You Hear About Us, select_one, Required): How you became aware of the Chamber, such as through a member referral, advertisement, etc.
* **Referred By Member** (Referred By Member, text, Optional): If your membership was referred by a member, enter their name here.
* **Previous Chamber Membership** (Previous Chamber Membership, select_one, Required): If you have been a member before, select the correct answer.
* **Agreement Signature** (Agreement Signature, text, Required): Type your name as a signature, as authorized by your business.
* **Signature Date** (Signature Date, date, Required): Date you sign the application.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
- If you have any questions, do not hesitate to contact the Chamber of Commerce office.
- Review each field before submitting the form.
- Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting.
- If you are unsure of any field, contact the Chamber of Commerce office for clarification.
