# data_privacy_compliance_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to ensure that data handling processes within the organization are compliant with data privacy regulations and the company's data protection policies.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: User Information

* **User Name** (`User Name`, required): Enter your official user name or ID.

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.

### Step 2: Data Handling Information

* **Data Types** (`data_types`, required): Select all applicable data types from the list:
	+ Data types: Financial, Personal, or Sensitive data.
* **Data Subjects** (`data_subjects`, required): Select all applicable data subjects from the list:
	+ Data subjects: Employees, Customers, or Other.
* **Data Handling** (`data_handling`, required): Enter the frequency of data handling (e.g., Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).

### Step 3: Data Protection

* **Data Protection** (`data_protection`, required): Describe the measures taken to protect data.
* **Output** (`output`, required): Check all applicable options:
	+ Yes, for internal use.
	+ No, for external use.

### Step 4: Additional Information

* **Comments** (`comments`, optional): Enter any comments or notes about data handling.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, optional): Enter the contact person's name and email for more information.
* **Department** (`department`, required): Enter your department name.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your department head or supervisor for guidance.
* This form is a mandatory compliance requirement for all employees and users.
