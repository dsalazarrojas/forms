# Product Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from customers about our company's products or services. By filling out this form, you will be helping us understand how our products or services meet your needs, what we do well, and where we can improve. Your input will be kept confidential and will help us to make informed decisions about our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question thoroughly and honestly. All questions are required to help us gather accurate feedback.
2. For the "product_survey_form_page_1" field, please enter the length of time you have been using our products or services.
3. For the "competitor_impression" field, select one of the options that best describes your impression of our company's products or services compared to other competitors.
4. For the "product_impression" field, select all the options that best describe your impression of our products or services.
5. For the "satisfaction" field, enter a number between 1 and 10 to rate your overall satisfaction with our products or services.
6. For the "overall_experience" field, tell us about your experience with our products or services in your own words.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **product_survey_form_page_1** (Required, text):
	+ This field asks about the length of time you have been using our products or services.
* **competitor_impression** (Required, select_one):
	+ This field asks about your impression of our company's products or services compared to other competitors. Choose one of the options that best describes your feelings:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Neutral
		- Bad
		- Very Bad
* **product_impression** (Required, select_multiple):
	+ This field allows you to select all options that best describe your impression of our products or services. Choose as many as apply:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Neutral
		- Bad
		- Very Bad
* **satisfaction** (Required, number):
	+ This field asks about your overall satisfaction with our products or services on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **overall_experience** (Required, text):
	+ This field is a free text field where you can share your thoughts and experiences about our products or services. Please be as detailed as possible.
