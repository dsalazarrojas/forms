# Nomination Form For Deserving Individual - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual who deserves recognition for their outstanding contributions, achievements, or qualities. The purpose of this form is to identify and nominate individuals who have made significant positive impacts in their community or field, and to provide supporting materials for their recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the individual being nominated in the "Nomination Title" field.
2. Describe the individual's achievements and qualities that make them deserving of recognition in the "Nomination Description" field.
3. Explain why you think this individual deserves recognition in the "Nomination Reason" field.
4. Select the category under which the individual's contribution falls (e.g., Community Service, Arts and Culture, Education, or Other).
5. Enter the date when the individual's contribution was made, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Enter the time when the individual's contribution was made, in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
7. Enter the location where the individual's contribution was made.
8. If applicable, select the types of supporting materials available (e.g., Supporting Documents, Testimonials, or Other).
9. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Title** (`individual_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter the name of the individual being nominated.
* **Nomination Description** (`individual_description`, Text, **Required**): Describe the individual's achievements and qualities that make them deserving of recognition.
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, Text, **Required**): Explain why you think this individual deserves recognition.
* **Category** (`category`, Select One, **Required**): Select the category under which the individual's contribution falls (e.g., Community Service, Arts and Culture, Education, or Other).
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, Date, **Required**): Enter the date when the individual's contribution was made, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Nomination Time** (`nomination_time`, Time, **Required**): Enter the time when the individual's contribution was made, in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Nomination Location** (`location`, Text, **Required**): Enter the location where the individual's contribution was made.
* **Supporting Materials** (`supporting_materials`, Checkbox, **Optional**): Select the types of supporting materials available (e.g., Supporting Documents, Testimonials, or Other).
* **Submit** (`submit`, Text, **Optional**): Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure the form is submitted correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can ask for help or clarification.
* Supporting materials should be attached or uploaded separately, if available.
