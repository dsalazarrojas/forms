# Business E-Wallet Payment Setup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the setup of business payment information for e-wallet services. It collects essential details from the merchant, including contact information, e-wallet provider, accepted payment methods, and bank account details, to enable efficient and secure payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Business Name**, **Business Registration Number**, and **Business Type** fields to provide your business details.
2. Select your primary **Industry Category** that best describes your business type.
3. Provide contact information for your **Contact Person**, including **Contact Title**, **Contact Email**, and **Contact Phone**.
4. Fill out the **Bank Name**, **Account Holder Name**, **Account Number**, and **Routing Number** fields with your bank account details.
5. Select your desired **E-Wallet Provider** and **Settlement Frequency**.
6. Choose the **Payment Methods** you accept, including Debit Card, Credit Card, Mobile Wallet, Bank Transfer, Cryptocurrency, QR Code Payment, and USSD.
7. Specify your **Expected Monthly Transaction Volume**, **Average Transaction Value**, and **Expected Monthly Turnover**.
8. Decide if you want to enable **Two-Factor Authentication**.

* **Business Name**: Enter your business's legal name.
* **Business Registration Number**: Enter your business registration or tax ID number.
* **Business Type**: Choose the type of business entity (Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, Non-Profit, Other).
* **Industry Category**: Select your primary industry category.
* **Contact Person**: Enter your primary contact person's name.
* **Contact Title**: Enter the title of your contact person (Mr./Mrs./Ms./Dr./Other).
* **Contact Email**: Enter your business email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your business phone number.
* **Bank Name**: Enter the name of your bank.
* **Account Holder Name**: Enter the name on your bank account.

* **Average Transaction Value**: Enter the average amount per transaction.
* **Expected Monthly Turnover**: Enter your total expected monthly revenue.

* **Two-Factor Authentication**: Decide if you want to enable two-factor authentication for added security.
