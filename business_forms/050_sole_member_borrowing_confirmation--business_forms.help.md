# Sole Member Borrowing Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sole Member Borrowing Confirmation form is used to document and confirm the borrowing of a sole member by a company. This form helps in maintaining accurate records and ensures that the sole member is aware of their borrowing status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the entity type from the options provided.
2. Enter the entity name.
3. Enter the date and time of borrowing using the date and time format.
4. Enter the reason for borrowing.
5. Select the approval, consent, and signature status.
6. Enter the date and time of approval, consent, and signature.
7. Select the rejection status (if applicable).
8. Enter the reason for rejection (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entity Type**: Select the type of entity (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Entity Type Name**: Enter the name of the entity.
* **Borrowing Date**: Enter the date of borrowing in the format (e.g., 2023-02-15).
* **Borrowing Time**: Enter the time of borrowing in the format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Reason for Borrowing**: Enter the reason why the sole member is borrowing.
* **Approval Date**: Enter the date of approval in the format (e.g., 2023-02-15).
* **Approval Time**: Enter the time of approval in the format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Approval Status**: Select the status of approval (Active, Inactive).
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date of consent in the format (e.g., 2023-02-15).
* **Consent Time**: Enter the time of consent in the format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Consent Status**: Select the status of consent (Yes, No).
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of signature in the format (e.g., 2023-02-15).
* **Signature Time**: Enter the time of signature in the format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Signature Status**: Select the status of signature (Active, Inactive).
* **Reason for Rejection**: Enter the reason for rejection.
* **Rejection Date**: Enter the date of rejection in the format (e.g., 2023-02-15).
* **Rejection Time**: Enter the time of rejection in the format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Rejection Status**: Select the status of rejection (Active, Inactive).

Note: Fields with a 'null' hint are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
