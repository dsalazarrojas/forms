# E Commerce Risk Management Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The E Commerce Risk Management Application Form is used to submit an application for risk management. It's designed for merchants to provide essential information about their business and risk profile. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by *) and review them carefully.
2. Select the correct risk level and risk categories for your business.
3. Indicate your risk management status and submission status.
4. Add any additional notes about your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Information**: Please provide a brief description of your business (e.g. "E-commerce store for selling clothing").
*   **Merchant Information**:* *Required*: Enter the name of your company and other relevant details (e.g. "ACME Inc.").
*   **Contact Person**: Provide the name and contact information of the person managing your business (e.g. "John Doe: johndoe@example.com").
*   **Risk Level**: Choose a risk level for your business from the options: High, Medium, Low.
*   **Risk Category**: Select one risk category for your business from the options: High, Medium, Low.
*   **Merchant Type**: Select which type of merchant you are, from the options: Physical, Digital, Hybrid.
*   **Business Type**: Select the type of business you operate, from the options: B2B, B2C, C2C.
*   **Business Duration**: Enter how long your business has been operational in years (e.g. "5 years").
*   **Risk Factors**: This is a text field where you can add any additional risk factors that you think are relevant to your business (e.g. "We have a high risk of fraud due to online payments").
*   **Risk Management**: Indicate if you have risk management in place, from the options: Yes, No.
*   **Merchant Status**: Select the status of your merchant from the options: Active, Inactive.
*   **Submission Status**: Select the status of your submission from the options: Submitted, Pending, Approved.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your business that will help us assess your risk profile.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
- Select only one risk category for your business.
- Review your submission status and risk management status to avoid delays.
