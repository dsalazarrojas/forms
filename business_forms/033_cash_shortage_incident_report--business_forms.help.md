# Cash Shortage Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a cash shortage incident in a business setting, ensuring that the necessary information is collected and recorded accurately and promptly.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Report Information

* Complete the `Report Date` field with the date you are filling out the form, in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* Complete the `Incident Date` field with the date of the cash shortage, in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* Complete the `Incident Time` field with the time of the cash shortage, in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* Click on the next page to continue.

### Step 2: Enter Location Information

* Enter the `Business Name` of the store or business where the cash shortage occurred.
* Enter the `Location Address` where the shortage occurred.
* Select the `Department or Area` where the shortage happened.
* Enter the `Cash Register or Location Number` where the shortage occurred.
* Click on the next page to continue.

### Step 3: Enter Reporting Person Information

* Enter the `Name of Person Reporting Shortage`, which is the employee who discovered or reported the shortage.
* Enter the `Position or Title` of the person reporting the shortage.
* Enter the `Phone Number` of the person reporting the shortage.

### Step 4: Enter Cashier Information

* Enter the `Cashier Responsible for Register` where the shortage occurred.
* Enter the `Shift Date` when the cash shortage happened, in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* Enter the `Shift Start Time` when the cash shortage started, in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* Enter the `Shift End Time` when the cash shortage ended, in HH:MM AM/PM format.

### Step 5: Enter Shortage Details

* Enter the `Expected Cash Balance` that was expected at the time of the shortage.
* Enter the `Actual Cash Balance` that was found at the time of the shortage.
* Calculate the `Shortage Amount` by entering the expected balance minus the actual balance.
* Enter the `Suspected Cause of Shortage`, which is the reason why the cash shortage occurred.
* Enter the `Immediate Actions and Follow-up Steps` taken to address the shortage.

## Tips

* Ensure that the report is completed accurately and thoroughly, as it will help in investigating and resolving the issue.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or manager for guidance.
* Make sure to save a copy of the report for your reference and record-keeping purposes.
