# Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Accident Incident Form is a tool used to collect and analyze data on vehicle accidents. This form helps gather relevant information on the type of accident, involved parties, location, and severity of injuries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of accident that occurred.
2. Choose the type of vehicle involved in the accident.
3. Select the location where the accident took place.
4. Identify the parties involved in the accident.
5. Assess the severity of injuries sustained.
6. Provide the vehicle registration number (if available).
7. Describe the accident in detail.
8. Offer any witness descriptions or additional notes.
9. Include any relevant investigator notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vehicle_accident_form**:
	+ A text field to enter a brief description of the accident.
* **accident_type**:
	+ Select the type of accident (Vehicle Collision or Other).
* **vehicle_type**:
	+ Choose the type of vehicle involved in the accident (Car, Truck, Motorcycle, Truck Trailer, or Other).
* **accident_location**:
	+ Select the location where the accident occurred (City, Country Road, Freeway, Highway, Parking Lot, or Private Road).
* **involved_parties**:
	+ Identify all parties involved in the accident (Driver, Passenger, Pedestrian, or Vehicle).
* **injury_severity**:
	+ Assess the severity of injuries sustained (Minor or Major).
* **vehicle_registration_number**:
	+ Enter the vehicle registration number (if available).
* **accident_description**:
	+ Describe the accident in detail.
* **witness_description**:
	+ Provide any witness descriptions or additional notes.
* **investigator_notes**:
	+ Include any relevant investigator notes or observations.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
* Use clear and concise language when describing the accident.
* If you're unsure about any information, ask your supervisor or relevant authorities for guidance.
