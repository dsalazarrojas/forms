# Parking Lot Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parking Lot Incident Form is designed to record and document incidents occurring in a parking lot. It gathers information about the incident, including its date, time, location, description, details, cause, status, and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Report the incident and provide a brief description.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident.
3. Specify the location where the incident occurred.
4. Provide detailed information about the incident.
5. Identify the cause of the incident.
6. Select the current status of the incident (Open, Closed, or Resolved).
7. Enter a final resolution or outcome.
8. Add any additional notes about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Reported**: Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Date**: Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Time**: Enter the time on which the incident occurred.
* **Location**: Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Details**: Enter any additional information about the incident.
* **Incident Cause**: Identify the underlying cause of the incident.
* **Incident Status**: Select the current status of the incident (Open, Closed, or Resolved).
* **Incident Resolution**: Enter a final resolution or outcome of the incident.
* **Incident Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate recording and management of the incident.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Use the form to document all incidents, regardless of their severity or duration.
* Update the form as needed to reflect changes in the incident's status or resolution.
