# Activity And Grade Ledger Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Activity And Grade Ledger Form" is a tool designed to track and evaluate business activities performed by employees. This form captures key information about each activity, including the date it was performed, the business name and owner, the type of activity, and the employee's assessment of its quality and impact.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the date when the activity was performed using the "Date" field.
2. Input the name of the FES business being evaluated in the "Business Name" field.
3. Enter the name of the person responsible for the business activities in the "Business Owner" field.
4. Choose the type of activity from the "Type of Activity" dropdown menu.
5. Provide a brief description of the specific activity performed in the "Activity Description" field.
6. Input the number of hours spent on the activity in the "Time Spent (Hours)" field.
7. Rate the quality of the activity performed using the "Quality Score" field.
8. Rate the potential impact of the activity using the "Impact Score" field.
9. Indicate the level of completion of the activity using the "Completion Status" dropdown menu.
10. Input any challenges faced while performing the activity in the "Challenges Encountered" field.
11. Input any lessons or takeaways from the activity in the "Lessons Learned" field.
12. Input any next steps or actions planned for future activities in the "Next Action Plan" field.
13. Assign an overall grade for the day's activities using the "Overall Grade" dropdown menu.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Be as specific as possible when inputting data in the "Activity Description" and "Challenges Encountered" fields.
* When rating the quality and impact scores, use a scale of 1-10.
* Choose the most relevant options from the "Type of Activity" and "Completion Status" dropdown menus.
* Assign an overall grade that accurately reflects the performance of the activity.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure accurate tracking and evaluation of business activities.
