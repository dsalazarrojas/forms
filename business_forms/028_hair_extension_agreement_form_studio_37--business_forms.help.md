# Hair Extension Agreement Form Studio 37 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients about their preferences for hair extension services provided by Studio 37.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Carefully read each section and provide accurate information about your service and payment preferences.
2. Fill in the details about the service you wish to receive.
3. Select the type of hair you have or prefer for the service.
4. Enter the length of the service in the format specified (e.g., number).
5. If applicable, enter the weight of the hair.
6. Choose the length of service you prefer (if available).
7. Select your preferred method of payment.
8. Choose your preferred payment terms (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **service_details**: This is the primary section for describing the service you wish to receive.
* **hair_type**: Please select the type of hair you have or prefer from the provided options (e.g., Bleach Blonde, Chestnut, etc.).
* **length**: Enter the length of the service you wish to receive, using the provided format.
* **weight**: If applicable, enter the weight of the hair you wish to receive (e.g., number).
* **service_length**: Choose the length of the service you prefer. Please note that this field is not required, so select only if applicable.
* **payment_method**: Select your preferred method of payment from the options provided (e.g., Cash, Credit, Debit, etc.).
* **payment_terms**: Select the payment terms you prefer from the options provided (e.g., 30 days, 45 days, etc.). This field is only required if applicable.
