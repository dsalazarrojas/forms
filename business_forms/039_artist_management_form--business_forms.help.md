# Artist Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about an artist or performer, including their professional name, contact details, artistic profile, management team, and agreement terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the **Artist Information** section, which includes the **Artist or Performer Name**, **Legal Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** fields. This information is crucial for contacting and communicating with the artist.
2. In the **Management Contact** section, provide the **Manager Name**, **Manager Email**, and **Manager Phone Number** if applicable. If the artist has a manager, this information should be filled out accurately.
3. The **Artist Profile** section includes fields to describe the artist's **Primary Genre or Discipline** and **Artist Biography**. This information helps management teams understand the artist's style and background.
4. In the **Management Responsibilities** section, select all applicable management duties from the provided list. This section outlines the scope of work for the management team.
5. For the **Commission and Terms** section, fill out the **Management Commission Rate Percent** and select the **Contract Term Length**. This information defines the agreement terms between the artist and their management team.
6. Finally, in the **Goals and Support** section, provide any **Artist Career Goals and Objectives** and **Additional Support or Resources Needed**. This section helps management teams understand the artist's aspirations and requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Performer Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, required): This field is for the artist's professional name. Please use the name they are known by in the industry.
* **Legal Name** (`legal_name`, `text`, required): This field is for the artist's full legal name. Use their official name as per their identity documents.
* **Email Address** (`artist_email`, `email`, required): This field is for the artist's primary contact email. Ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Phone Number** (`artist_phone`, `text`, required): This field is for the artist's contact number. Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, optional): If the artist has a manager, enter their name. This information helps management teams communicate with the manager directly.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, optional): This field is for the manager's contact email. If applicable, enter their email address.
* **Manager Phone Number** (`manager_phone`, `text`, optional): This field is for the manager's contact number. If applicable, enter their phone number.
* **Management Company or Agency** (`management_company`, `text`, optional): If the artist is managed by a company or agency, enter their name.
* **Primary Genre or Discipline** (`primary_genre`, `text`, required): Select the artist's main genre or discipline. This helps management teams understand their style and background.
* **Artist Biography** (`artist_biography`, `text`, required): This field is for a brief professional bio and background description. Keep it concise and accurate.
* **Years Active in Industry** (`years_active`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the number of years the artist has been active in the industry.
* **Management Duties** (`management_duties`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable management duties from the provided list.
* **Scope of Booking Authority** (`booking_scope`, `text`, optional): If applicable, describe the scope of booking authority for the management team.
* **Financial Authority and Responsibilities** (`financial_authority`, `text`, optional): If applicable, describe the financial authority and responsibilities for the management team.
* **Management Commission Rate Percent** (`commission_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the management commission rate as a percentage. This defines the agreement terms between the artist and their management team.
* **Contract Term Length** (`contract_term`, `select_one`, required): Select the duration of the management agreement. This defines the contract term length.
* **Artist Career Goals and Objectives** (`career_goals`, `text`, required): Describe the artist's career goals and objectives.
* **Additional Support or Resources Needed** (`support_needed`, `text`, optional): If applicable, describe any additional support or resources required by the artist.
* **I Agree to These Management Terms** (`management_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes, I Agree" to confirm acceptance of the agreement terms.
