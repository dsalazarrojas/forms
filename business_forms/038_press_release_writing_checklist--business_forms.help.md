## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the contact information, including your name, contact number, and email address.
- Choose the correct press contact from the list of options provided.
- Select the category for the press release (Business, News, or Sports).
- Choose the type of press release (Company Press Release, Product Release, Event Release, or Financial Release).
- Enter the deadline and published date and time for the press release.
- Upload any necessary files or images for the press release.
- Provide additional information as needed in the text field.
