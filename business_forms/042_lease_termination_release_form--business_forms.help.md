# Lease Termination Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to formalize the process of terminating a rental agreement between a landlord and a tenant. Both parties are required to complete the form to provide a clear understanding of the reasons for termination and the terms of the release.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Sign the "Landlord Signature" and "Tenant Signature" fields to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of the release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the landlord.
* **Tenant Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the tenant.
* **Lease Start Date** (`<date>`, required/optional): Enter the date when the lease started.
* **Lease End Date** (`<date>`, required/optional): Enter the date when the lease ended.
* **Reason for Termination** (`<text>`, required/optional): Enter the reason for which the lease is being terminated.
* **Lease Term** (`<text>`, required/optional): Enter the lease term or agreement.
* **Notice Period** (`<text>`, required/optional): Enter the notice period given to the tenant before the lease termination.
* **Landlord Signature** (`<text>`, required/optional): Enter your signature as the landlord, confirming that you have read and understood the terms of the release.
* **Tenant Signature** (`<text>`, required/optional): Enter your signature as the tenant, confirming that you have read and understood the terms of the release.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required information.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure that you understand the terms of the release.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
