# Public Transport Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Public Transport Incident Form is used to report incidents that occurred on public transportation services. This form is intended for reporting incidents such as accidents, injuries, or near-misses involving public transport vehicles or passengers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the form title to open it.
2. Fill out each field as instructed, making sure to complete all required fields (indicated by the 'Required' label).
3. Review your completed form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, Required): Enter the date when the incident occurred in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, Not Required): Enter the time of the incident in HH:MM format.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, Not Required): Describe the location where the incident happened, such as the stop name or nearby landmark.
* **Description of incident** (`incident_description`, `text`, Required): Provide a detailed account of what happened during the incident.
* **Affected Route** (`incident_affected_route`, `select_one`, Not Required): Select the public transportation route that was affected by the incident from the provided options (Route 1, Route 2, ...).
* **Affected Vehicle(s)** (`incident_affected_vehicle`, `select_multiple`, Not Required): Select all vehicles affected by the incident from the provided options (Vehicle 1, Vehicle 2, ...).
* **Cause of incident** (`incident_cause`, `text`, Not Required): Explain the reason why the incident occurred.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident to help with investigation and resolution.
* If you are unsure about any field, leave it blank and submit the form anyway.
* Please review this guide before submitting the form to ensure you have all the required information.
