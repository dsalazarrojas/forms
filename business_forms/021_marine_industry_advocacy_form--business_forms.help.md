# Marine Industry Advocacy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help you report and provide feedback on marine industry-related issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your contact information on Page 1
2. Describe the issue you want to report on Page 2
3. Choose your stakeholder perspective and follow-up preferences on Page 3
4. Provide any supporting documentation on Page 4
5. Specify the date and time of the issue on Page 5
6. Choose the issue follow-up options on Page 6
7. Indicate the location of the issue on Page 7
8. Select the type of issue on Page 8

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: Your name, email, and phone number will help us contact you to discuss your report.
* **Issue Description**: Please provide a brief description of the issue you want to report.
* **Stakeholder Perspective**: Choose the perspective from which you are making the report (e.g. Industry Association, Local Government, Community Group, etc.).
* **Supporting Documentation**: If you have any documents or files that support your claim, please upload them here.
* **Issue Date**: Please specify the date when the issue occurred.
* **Issue Time**: If applicable, please specify the time when the issue occurred.
* **Follow Up**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g. email, phone, email and phone).
* **Issue Location**: Provide the location where the issue occurred.
* **Issue Type**: Choose the type of issue you are reporting (e.g. pollution, environmental impact, regulatory, etc.).

## Tips

* Please ensure that the issue you report is not a duplicate.
* Please fill out as much information as possible.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documentation, please upload them with your report.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
