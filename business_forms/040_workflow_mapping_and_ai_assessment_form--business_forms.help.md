This form is intended to help assess and document the current state of AI integration within a business process or workflow. The form collects information on the workflow title, description, and current AI integration status. This information can be used to identify areas where AI integration could be improved or expanded upon. The form's purpose is to gather insights on the current workflow and AI integration, allowing for data-driven decision-making and strategic planning.

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Enter the title of the workflow in the **Workflow Title** field. This field is a text input where you can enter a brief name that describes the workflow.
2.  In the **Workflow Description** field, provide a detailed explanation of the workflow, including its purpose and scope.
3.  For **AI Integration Title**, enter the title of the AI system or tool currently integrated into the workflow.
4.  In the **AI Integration Description** field, describe the AI system or tool's role and functionality within the workflow.
5.  In the **AI Integration Status** field, select one of the following options:
    *   **High**: The AI system or tool is fully integrated and actively used within the workflow.
    *   **Medium**: The AI system or tool is partially integrated and has some functionality, but not fully utilized.
    *   **Low**: The AI system or tool is not integrated at all or has minimal functionality within the workflow.

**Workflow Mapping And AI Assessment Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on the current state of AI integration within a business process or workflow. It helps identify areas where AI integration could be improved or expanded upon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Workflow Title (Workflow Title)**: Enter a brief name that describes the workflow.
*   **Workflow Description (Workflow Description)**: Provide a detailed explanation of the workflow, including its purpose and scope.
*   **AI Integration Title (AI Integration Title)**: Enter the title of the AI system or tool currently integrated into the workflow.
*   **AI Integration Description (AI Integration Description)**: Describe the AI system or tool's role and functionality within the workflow.
*   **AI Integration Status (AI Integration Status)**: Select one of the following options:
    *   **High**: The AI system or tool is fully integrated and actively used within the workflow.
    *   **Medium**: The AI system or tool is partially integrated and has some functionality, but not fully utilized.
    *   **Low**: The AI system or tool is not integrated at all or has minimal functionality within the workflow.

## Tips
*   Be specific and detailed when filling out the **Workflow Description** field to ensure accurate and helpful information is collected.
*   If multiple AI systems or tools are integrated into the workflow, select the **High** option for the one that is most actively used.
*   If no AI system or tool is integrated into the workflow, select the **Low** option for the **AI Integration Status** field.
