# Expenditure Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expenditure Request Form is a tool used to submit requests for business-related expenditures that require approval from management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "First Page" tab to start the form.
2. On this page, you will see a "Submit" button to save your progress and move on to the next page.
3. Click on the "Purpose of Expenditure" tab to select the purpose of your request from the dropdown list.
4. Enter the "Budgeted Expenditure" and "Expenditure Amount" fields with the relevant information.
5. Finally, enter your "Requestor's Name" in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page (expenditure_request_form_1)**: This page serves as the starting point for your expenditure request.
* **Purpose of Expenditure (expenditure_request_form_2)**: Select the reason for your expenditure from the options provided.
* **Budgeted Expenditure (expenditure_request_form_3)**: Enter the budgeted expenditure amount, if known.
* **Expenditure Amount (expenditure_request_form_4)**: Enter the actual expenditure amount, if known.
* **Requestor's Name (expenditure_request_form_5)**: Enter your name to associate your request with your identity.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review your request carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with a manager or colleague for guidance.
* You can save your progress and return to the form later to complete it.
