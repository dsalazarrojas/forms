# Store Promotion Collaboration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Store Promotion Collaboration Request Form is a tool used to collect information on store promotion collaborations from store representatives, retailers, or agents. This form helps to assess and evaluate each promotion collaboration request, ensuring that all necessary information is gathered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure that the `brand_name`, `campaign_title`, `campaign_description`, `promotion_details`, `retailer_name`, `agency_name`, and `contact_email` fields are filled out.
3. Choose the correct `proposal_type` for your promotion collaboration request.
4. If you are representing an agency, fill out the `agency_name` field.
5. If you have any additional information to share about the collaboration, you can provide it in the `collaboration_details` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **brand_name** (Required): The name of the brand promoting the store promotion.
* **campaign_title** (Required): A short title for the promotion collaboration.
* **campaign_description** (Required): A brief description of the promotion collaboration.
* **promotion_details** (Required): Details about the promotion collaboration.
* **proposal_type** (Required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are proposing this collaboration or not.
* **retailer_name** (Required): The name of the retailer or store involved.
* **agency_name** (Required): The name of the agency involved.
* **contact_email** (Required): The email of the contact person.
* **brand_logo** (Optional): A text field for entering the brand logo.
* **collaboration_details** (Optional): Additional information about the collaboration.
* **contact_person** (Optional): A text field for entering the contact person's name.
* **contact_phone** (Optional): A text field for entering the contact person's phone number.
* **proposal_submission** (Optional): A text field for entering proposal submission information.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help with this form, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
