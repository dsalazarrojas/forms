# Channel Membership Revenue Split Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Channel Membership Revenue Split Tracker is a form designed to track revenue splits for channels, providing a clear and organized way to report and manage channel membership revenue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Tracking Period** where you want to track revenue splits.
2. Enter the **Reporting Date**, the date of the report.
3. Enter the **Channel Name** to identify the channel.
4. Enter the **Channel Manager Name** who manages the channel.
5. If necessary, add **Manager Contact Information** for easier communication.
6. Enter the **Total Gross Revenue** before splits.
7. Set the **Platform Commission Percentage** taken by the platform.
8. Calculate the **Platform Commission Amount** based on the percentage and gross revenue.
9. Set the **Creator Revenue Percentage** for the channel creator.
10. Calculate the **Creator Revenue Amount** based on the percentage and gross revenue.
11. Set the **Network Revenue Percentage** for the network (if applicable).
12. Calculate the **Network Revenue Amount** based on the percentage and gross revenue (if applicable).
13. Set the **Agency Revenue Percentage** for the agency (if applicable).
14. Calculate the **Agency Revenue Amount** based on the percentage and gross revenue (if applicable).
15. Set the **Other Party Percentage** for other parties (if applicable).
16. Calculate the **Other Party Revenue Amount** based on the percentage and gross revenue (if applicable).
17. Enter the number of **Active Members** in the channel this period.
18. Enter the number of **New Members** joined this period.
19. Set the **Payment Status** to indicate if the payment has been processed.
20. Add any **Notes or Discrepancies**, if any issues or notes are present.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tracking Period** (`1`, text, required): Enter the month and year for tracking revenue splits.
* **Reporting Date** (`2`, date, required): Enter the date of the report.
* **Channel Name** (`3`, text, required): Enter the name of the channel.
* **Channel Manager Name** (`4`, text, required): Enter the person managing the channel.
* **Manager Contact Information** (`5`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the channel manager.
* **Total Gross Revenue** (`6`, number, required): Enter the revenue before splits.
* **Platform Commission Percentage** (`7`, number, required): Set the percentage taken by the platform.
* **Platform Commission Amount** (`8`, number, required): Calculate the commission based on the percentage and gross revenue.
* **Creator Revenue Percentage** (`9`, number, required): Set the percentage for the channel creator.
* **Creator Revenue Amount** (`10`, number, required): Calculate the creator revenue amount based on the percentage and gross revenue.
* **Network Revenue Percentage** (`11`, number, optional): Set the percentage for the network.
* **Network Revenue Amount** (`12`, number, optional): Calculate the network revenue amount based on the percentage and gross revenue.
* **Agency Revenue Percentage** (`13`, number, optional): Set the percentage for the agency.
* **Agency Revenue Amount** (`14`, number, optional): Calculate the agency revenue amount based on the percentage and gross revenue.
* **Other Party Percentage** (`15`, number, optional): Set the percentage for other parties.
* **Other Party Revenue Amount** (`16`, number, optional): Calculate the other party revenue amount based on the percentage and gross revenue.
* **Active Members** (`17`, number, optional): Enter the number of active members this period.
* **New Members** (`18`, number, optional): Enter the number of new members joined this period.
* **Payment Status** (`19`, select_one, required): Set the payment status.
* **Notes or Discrepancies** (`20`, text, optional): Add any issues or notes present.
