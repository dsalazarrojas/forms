# Software Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request software and related resources from the IT department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of software you are requesting (e.g. Apple, Microsoft, Google).
2. Choose the department that will be utilizing the software (e.g. Finance, HR, IT).
3. Enter the name of the software you are requesting.
4. Provide a reason for the request.
5. Enter the contact person's name.
6. Enter the contact person's email.
7. Enter the contact person's phone number.
8. Enter a brief description of the software.
9. Enter the software version.
10. Enter the category of the software.
11. Enter the subcategory of the software.
12. Choose the status of the software request (e.g. Pending, Approved, Rejected).
13. Enter any additional comments.
14. Choose the person assigned to fulfill the software request.
15. Enter the date and time of the software request.
16. Enter the person who submitted the software request.
17. Enter the date and time the software request was submitted.
18. Enter any comments from the person who submitted the software request.
19. Choose the person who requested the software.
20. Choose the person who will approve the software request.
21. Enter any comments from the person who requested the software.
