# Marketplace Listing Validation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to validate and verify the accuracy of marketplace listings. It helps ensure that all required fields are filled out completely and correctly, which can help avoid potential issues with listings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the marketplace where your product is listed (e.g., Amazon, eBay, Etsy).
2. Choose the type of product you are listing (e.g., product1, product2, product3, product4).
3. Provide a brief description of the product.
4. Enter the price of the product.
5. Upload a clear and high-quality image of the product.
6. (Optional) Enter shipping information (e.g., shipping costs, delivery times).
7. (Optional) Enter payment information (e.g., payment methods, terms).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Marketplace (<name>, <type>, required/optional): <label>
Select the marketplace where your product is listed. Choose from the options: Amazon, eBay, Etsy.

### Product Type (<name>, <type>, required/optional): <label>
Choose the type of product you are listing. Select from the options: product1, product2, product3, product4.

### Product Description (<name>, <type>, required/true>): <label>
Provide a brief description of the product. This will help the platform understand the details of your product.

### Price (<name>, <type>, required/true>): <label>
Enter the price of the product. Please make sure to include any applicable taxes or fees.

### Image (<name>, <type>, required/true>): <label>
Upload a clear and high-quality image of the product. This should be a clear and well-lit image of the product from different angles.

### Shipping Information (<name>, <type>, optional/false>): <label>
(If applicable) Enter shipping information, including shipping costs and delivery times.

### Payment Information (<name>, <type>, optional/false>): <label>
(If applicable) Enter payment information, including payment methods and terms.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate information to avoid any issues with listings.
- Review your form submission for any errors before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the marketplace support for assistance.
- Duplicate listings can lead to issues with the platform; check for duplicates before submitting this form.
