# Business Communication Phrase Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you request a specific business communication phrase that meets your needs. It will guide you through the process of providing detailed information about your request, ensuring that the phrase is tailored to your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your full name and email address. This will help us understand who is requesting the phrase.
2. Specify the department and company name related to your request. This will help us tailor the phrase to your organizational context.
3. Provide a clear description of the phrase you need, including what it should communicate. This will help us create an accurate and effective phrase.
4. Choose the target language and communication context for the phrase. This will ensure that the phrase is suitable for your intended audience.
5. Select the tone and style of the phrase, considering who will be reading it.
6. Indicate the length and frequency of the phrase's use. This will help us create a phrase that is both concise and practical.
7. Provide any additional context or information that may be helpful in creating the phrase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Information** (`requestor_info`): This section is for you to provide your personal details, including your full name and email address.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents or business cards.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your official email address associated with your department or organization.
* **Department** (`department`): Choose the department related to your request, such as Marketing, Sales, or Human Resources.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter the full name of your organization.
* **Phrase Request** (`phrase_request`): Describe the phrase you are requesting, including its purpose and content.
* **Phrase Description** (`phrase_description`): Provide more detailed information about the phrase, including its context and intended audience.
* **Target Language** (`target_language`): Select the language the phrase should be in, such as English, Spanish, or French.
* **Communication Context** (`communication_context`): Choose where the phrase will be used, such as email, website, or social media.
* **Tone** (`tone`): Select the tone of the phrase, such as formal, professional, or friendly.
* **Target Audience** (`audience`): Identify who will read the phrase, such as customers, employees, or executives.
* **Industry-Specific Terminology** (`industry_specific`): If you have specific industry terminology or requirements, please provide them here.
* **Existing Content or Draft** (`existing_content`): If you have a draft or existing content related to the phrase, please share it here.
* **Key Message** (`key_message`): Clearly state the main point or key message of the phrase.
* **Call to Action** (`call_to_action`): If you want to prompt the reader to take a specific action, provide it here.
* **Length Preference** (`length_preference`): Choose the length of the phrase, from very brief to long and flexible.
* **Brand Guidelines** (`brand_guidelines`): If your organization has specific brand guidelines, please indicate here.
* **Would You Like Examples** (`examples_preferred`): Do you want us to provide 2, 3, or 5 options for the phrase?
* **Deadline** (`deadline`): Choose when you need the phrase, from immediately to within a specific timeframe.
* **How Often Will This Be Used** (`usage_frequency`): Indicate how often you plan to use the phrase.
* **Additional Context** (`additional_context`): Provide any other information that may be helpful in creating the phrase.

## Tips
Remember to provide as much detail as possible to ensure that we create a phrase that meets your requirements. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
