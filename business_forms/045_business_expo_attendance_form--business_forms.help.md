# Business Expo Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Expo Attendance Form is designed to collect information from individuals or companies attending a business expo. This form helps us understand your presence, preferences, and needs, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Attendee Information" section with your full name, email address, phone number, company name, job title, and industry.
2. Select your attendance type (Exhibitor, Attendee, Sponsor, Speaker, Media, or Other) and indicate your expo name, date, and booth number (if applicable).
3. Specify the number of attendees from your company and any dietary requirements.
4. Indicate your parking needs and preferred badge title.
5. Select your interest in attending networking events.
6. If you have any special requests or accommodations, please specify them in the "Special Requests or Accommodations" field.
7. Finally, indicate your preferred follow-up contact method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your complete name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Email Address**: Provide your professional email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title within your company.
* **Industry**: Select your industry sector.
* **Expo Name**: Enter the name of the expo you are attending.
* **Expo Date**: Enter the date of the expo.
* **Attendance Type**: Choose your role at the expo (Exhibitor, Attendee, Sponsor, Speaker, Media, or Other).
* **Booth Number**: If you are an exhibitor, enter your booth location.
* **Areas of Interest**: Select the areas you are interested in (Technology Solutions, Marketing Services, Financial Services, HR Solutions, Equipment, Consulting, Networking, or Training).
* **Number of Attendees from Company**: Indicate how many people from your company are attending the expo.
* **Dietary Requirements**: Select any dietary requirements you have (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Halal, Kosher, or None).
* **Parking Needed**: Indicate if you need parking assistance.
* **Badge Title Preference**: Choose how you want your badge to be displayed (Job Title, Company Name, Company and Title, or Custom).
* **Networking Event Interest**: Select your interest level in attending networking events (Yes, Very Interested, Interested, Maybe, or Not Interested).
* **Special Requests or Accommodations**: Specify any special requests or accommodations you may need.
* **Preferred Follow-Up Contact**: Choose how you want to be contacted (Email, Phone, or No Follow-Up).

By filling out this form accurately and completely, you will help us better understand your needs and preferences, ensuring a successful and enjoyable experience at the business expo.
