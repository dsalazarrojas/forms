# Client Workshop Performance Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Workshop Performance Review Form is designed to gather feedback from clients about their experience with a workshop. This feedback is essential for us to understand what worked well and what can be improved in our future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Workshop Performance Review Form**: Please start by answering the questions on the first page.
2. Read each question carefully and provide honest answers to the best of your ability.
3. Be sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Feedback or Comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the workshop you attended.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the workshop took place.
* **Facilitator or Trainer Name** (`facilitator_name`, text, optional): If you wish to provide feedback about the facilitator, please enter their name here.
* **Your Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name, as this will help us identify your feedback.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address, which we will use to contact you if needed.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Workshop** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your overall satisfaction with the workshop:
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **Rate the Workshop** (`overall_rating`, number, required): Rate the workshop from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **The Workshop Met Your Expectations** (`met_expectations`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate whether the workshop met your expectations:
	+ Strongly Disagree
	+ Disagree
	+ Neutral
	+ Agree
	+ Strongly Agree
* **Quality of Workshop Content** (`content_quality`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to rate the quality of the workshop content:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Very Good
	+ Excellent
* **Content Was Relevant to Your Role or Goals** (`content_relevance`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate whether the content was relevant to your role or goals:
	+ Strongly Disagree
	+ Disagree
	+ Neutral
	+ Agree
	+ Strongly Agree
* **Content Was Practical and Actionable** (`content_practical`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate whether the content was practical and actionable:
	+ Strongly Disagree
	+ Disagree
	+ Neutral
	+ Agree
	+ Strongly Agree
* **You Learned New Information or Skills** (`new_knowledge`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate whether you learned new information or skills:
	+ Strongly Disagree
	+ Disagree
	+ Neutral
	+ Agree
	+ Strongly Agree
* **Facilitator's Knowledge and Expertise** (`facilitator_knowledge`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to rate the facilitator's knowledge and expertise:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Very Good
	+ Excellent
* **Facilitator Explained Concepts Clearly** (`facilitator_clarity`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate whether the facilitator explained concepts clearly:
	+ Strongly Disagree
	+ Disagree
	+ Neutral
	+ Agree
	+ Strongly Agree
* **Workshop Engagement Level** (`engagement_level`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your engagement level with the workshop:
	+ Very Low
	+ Low
	+ Moderate
	+ High
	+ Very High
* **Workshop Pacing and Flow** (`pacing_rating`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate whether the pace of the workshop was appropriate:
	+ Too Slow
	+ Slow
	+ Just Right
	+ Fast
	+ Too Fast
* **Quality of Materials and Resources Provided** (`materials_quality`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to rate the quality of materials and resources provided:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Very Good
	+ Excellent
* **Materials Will Be Useful After Workshop** (`materials_usefulness`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate whether the materials will be useful after the workshop:
	+ Strongly Disagree
	+ Disagree
	+ Neutral
	+ Agree
	+ Strongly Agree
* **How Likely Are You to Apply What You Learned?** (`apply_learnings`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to indicate how likely you are to apply what you learned:
	+ Very Unlikely
	+ Unlikely
	+ Neutral
	+ Likely
	+ Very Likely
* **Expected Benefits from Applying Learning** (`expected_benefits`, text, optional): If you have any expected benefits from applying what you learned, please describe them here.
* **Barriers to Implementing Learning** (`implementation_barriers`, text, optional): If you have any barriers to implementing what you learned, please describe them here.
* **What Was the Best Part of the Workshop?** (`what_went_well`, text, optional): Please describe what you found most valuable or enjoyable about the workshop.
* **What Could Be Improved?** (`improvements`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please describe them here.
* **Additional Feedback or Comments** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): If you have any additional feedback or comments, please provide them here.
