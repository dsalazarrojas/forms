# Photography Client Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential information and agreements between a photography business or studio and their clients. It covers various aspects of a photography session, including session details, client information, services offered, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Session Details**: Enter a brief description of the photography session, including the type of photography services to be provided (e.g., portrait, event, etc.).
2. **Client Information**: Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
3. **Photography Services**: Select the services that will be provided during the session (e.g., Service A, Service B, Service C).
4. **Service Terms**: Enter any specific terms or conditions related to the services offered.
5. **Payment Terms**: Enter the payment details, including the amount and method of payment.
6. **Client Signatures**: Enter the client's signature to confirm their agreement.
7. **Business Terms**: Enter any additional business terms or conditions.
8. **Contact Details**: Enter the client's contact information and any other relevant details.
9. **Session Date**: Enter the date of the photography session.
10. **Session Time**: Enter the time of the photography session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Details**: This field is for entering a brief description of the photography session. 
- **Client Information**: This field is for entering the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
- **Photography Services**: This field allows you to select the services that will be provided during the session. Select all applicable services.
- **Service Terms**: This field is for entering any specific terms or conditions related to the services offered.
- **Payment Terms**: This field is for entering the payment details, including the amount and method of payment.
- **Client Signatures**: This field is for the client to sign and date their agreement.
- **Business Terms**: This field is for entering any additional business terms or conditions.
- **Contact Details**: This field is for entering the client's contact information and any other relevant details.
- **Session Date**: This field is for entering the date of the photography session.
- **Session Time**: This field is for entering the time of the photography session.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and understand all terms and conditions before proceeding with the session.
* Ensure that the client's contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Keep the client informed about the terms and conditions of the session.
* Store the completed form securely and confidentially.
