# Construction Project Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Project Records Release Form is used to request access to specific project records for various purposes such as auditing, legal review, or marketing. This form captures information about the requestor, the project, and the specific records being requested, as well as the approval and consent of the records release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Requestor Identification" section with your full name and name of your organization.
2. Choose your relationship to the project from the dropdown menu.
3. Provide your primary email address and phone number.
4. Specify the project name and location/ address.
5. Choose the records period or date range you are requesting.
6. Select the type of records you are requesting access to.
7. Enter the purpose of the records release request.
8. Acknowledge your authority to request these records and agree to confidentiality and data privacy terms.
9. Choose your preferred delivery method.
10. Enter the date of release authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Requestor Identification** (`section_requestor_ident`, note, not required): This is a brief introduction to the requestor's identification.
* **2. Full Name of Requesting Individual** (`requestor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **3. Requesting Organization** (`requestor_company`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **4. Relationship to the Project** (`requestor_type_ident`, select_one, required): Choose your relationship to the project from the dropdown menu.
	+ Project Owner/Client
	+ General Contractor
	+ Subcontractor
	+ Architect/Engineering Firm
	+ Legal Counsel/Representative
	+ Insurance Professional
	+ Financial Auditor/Institution
* **5. Primary Email Address** (`requestor_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **6. Primary Phone Number** (`requestor_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number in the format (555) 555-5555.
* **7. Project Name** (`project_name_ident`, text, required): Enter the name of the project.
* **8. Project Location/Address** (`project_location_address`, text, required): Enter the location/address of the project.
* **9. Specify Records Period or Date Range** (`records_period_requested`, text, required): Specify the records period or date range you are requesting.
* **10. Type of Records to be Released** (`records_checklist_req`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply.
	+ Daily Logs and Site Reports
	+ Approved Submittals and Shop Drawings
	+ RFIs and Coordination Records
	+ Change Orders and Directives
	+ Quality Control/Inspection Reports
	+ Safety Incident/Audit Records
	+ Progress Photos and Videos
	+ Final As-built Documentation
	+ Billing and Payment Applications
* **11. Purpose of the Records Release Request** (`purpose_of_release_desc`, text, required): Enter the purpose of the records release request.
* **12. Acknowledgment of Authority to Request** (`authority_to_request_stmt`, select_one, required): Choose whether you acknowledge or disagree with your authority to request these records.
* **13. Confidentiality and Proper Use Agreement** (`confidentiality_agree_stmt`, select_one, required): Choose whether you agree or disagree with the confidentiality and proper use terms.
* **14. Data Privacy and GDPR Compliance Acknowledgment** (`data_privacy_compliance_ack`, select_one, required): Choose whether you accept or reject the data privacy and GDPR compliance terms.
* **15. Preferred Delivery Method** (`delivery_method_pref`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred delivery method.
* **16. Additional Release Instructions or Notes** (`additional_release_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any additional release instructions or notes.
* **17. Date of Release Authorization** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of release authorization.
* **18. Approving Officer (Name and Title)** (`authorized_officer_name_label`, text, optional): Enter the name and title of the approving officer (for internal use).
