# Bookkeeping Discovery Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bookkeeping Discovery Survey is a form designed to help us understand your business's current bookkeeping needs and challenges. This information will help us recommend the best bookkeeping solutions for your business, based on your specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your accurate and honest answers.
2. For each field, please choose the most suitable option that describes your business.
3. If you have any specific comments or additional information, please include them in the space provided.
4. Once you have completed the form, submit it to schedule a discovery call with our expert.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* Label: Your Name
* Type: text
* Required: Yes
