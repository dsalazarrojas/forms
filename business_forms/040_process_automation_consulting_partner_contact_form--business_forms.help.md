# Process Automation Consulting Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and project requirements from potential business consulting partners who are interested in partnering with us for process automation services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your area(s) of interest from the options listed under "Areas of Interest".
2. Describe your project requirements and goals in the text field "Project Requirements".
3. Enter your contact information, including name, title, company, and email address.
4. Enter your phone number and any additional contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Type a brief message in the "Message" field to let us know how we can assist you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information**: This field is where you enter your name, title, and company name to help us identify you and your organization.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Areas of Interest**: Select one or more areas of interest from the list to help us understand your business needs.
	+ Type: select multiple
	+ Required: false
* **Project Requirements**: Describe your project requirements and goals to help us tailor our response.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Email**: Enter your email address for us to send you follow-up messages and partnership opportunities.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you directly.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Message**: Type a brief message to let us know how we can assist you.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing your project requirements and areas of interest.
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date contact information.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
