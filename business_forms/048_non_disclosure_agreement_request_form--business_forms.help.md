# Non Disclosure Agreement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a request for a Non-Disclosure Agreement, a legal contract that protects sensitive business information from unauthorized disclosure. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary details about the request, including the business name, contact information, and agreement specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the business that is requesting the Non-Disclosure Agreement.
2. Provide the contact person's name and email address for the business.
3. Enter the business's phone number.
4. Enter the date on which the agreement will take effect.
5. Provide the date on which the agreement will expire.
6. Briefly describe the reason for the request.
7. If applicable, describe any sensitive information that will be covered under the agreement.
8. Confirm that you have read and understand the terms of the Non-Disclosure Agreement.
9. Acknowledge that you have received a copy of the agreement and understand its terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Name
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the business that is requesting the Non-Disclosure Agreement.

### Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the business.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person for the business.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the business.

### Agreement Date
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the Non-Disclosure Agreement will take effect.

### Expiration Date
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the Non-Disclosure Agreement will expire.

### Reason for Request
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Briefly describe the reason for the request for a Non-Disclosure Agreement.

### Sensitive Information
* **Sensitive Information** (`sensitive_information`, note, optional): If applicable, describe any sensitive information that will be covered under the agreement.

### Confidentiality Agreement
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, note, optional): Describe the terms of the Non-Disclosure Agreement and ensure they are understood.

### Acknowledgement
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm that you have received a copy of the agreement and understand its terms.

### Date Acknowledged
* **Date Acknowledged** (`date_acknowledged`, date, optional): Enter the date on which you acknowledged the agreement.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields accurately reflect the details of your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the contact person.
* Review the completed form carefully before submitting it.
