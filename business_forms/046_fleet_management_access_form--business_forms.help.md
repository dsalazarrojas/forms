# fleet_management_access_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fleet management access form is used to request access to a fleet of vehicles. This form is used to capture essential information about the access request, including vehicle access status, driver information, access date, and start time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. On the "Vehicle Access Request" page, select whether you are requesting access to a vehicle or not.
3. On the "Driver Info" page, select whether you are a driver or not.
4. On the "Access Date" page, enter the date you need access to the vehicle.
5. On the "Start Time" page, enter the time you need access to the vehicle.
6. Review your information and click on the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Access Request** (`vehicle_access_request`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you are requesting access to one or more vehicles.
* **Driver Info** (`driver_info`, select_one, required): Select whether you are a driver or not.
* **Access Date** (`access_date`, date, required): Enter the date you need access to the vehicle. Format: yyyy-mm-dd.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the time you need access to the vehicle. Format: hh:mm.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have selected the correct vehicle(s) you are requesting access to.
* If you are a driver, please select "Yes" for "Driver Info".
* If you need to request access to multiple vehicles, select "Yes" for "Vehicle Access Request" for each vehicle.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
