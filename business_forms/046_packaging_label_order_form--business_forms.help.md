# Packaging Label Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for packaging label orders.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the guide above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **label_size (2x3, 2x4, 3x3, 3x4, 3x6, 4x6)**:
	+ Select the size of the label.
* **label_material (Paper Label, Plastic Label, Sticker Label)**:
	+ Choose the material of the label.
* **quantity**:
	+ Enter the quantity of labels needed.
* **label_material_2 (1.5, 2.2, 2.5, 3.5, 4.5, 5.5)**:
	+ Choose the material of the second type of label.
* **size_2 (2x3, 2x4, 3x3, 3x4, 3x6, 4x6)**:
	+ Select the size of the second type of label.
* **material_1 (100-500, 501-1000, 1001-2000, 2001-5000)**:
	+ Select the range of quantities for the first type of label.
* **quantity_2**:
	+ Enter the quantity of the second type of label.
* **order_date**:
	+ Enter the date of the order.
* **order_time**:
	+ Enter the time of the order.
* **order_note**:
	+ Enter any additional notes about the order.
* **order_email**:
	+ Enter the customer's email address.
* **order_phone**:
	+ Enter the customer's phone number.
* **order_address**:
	+ Enter the customer's address.
* **contact_email**:
	+ Enter the contact person's email address.
* **contact_phone**:
	+ Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **contact_address**:
	+ Enter the contact person's address.
* **contact_zip**:
	+ Enter the contact person's ZIP code.
* **order_id**:
	+ Enter a unique ID for the order.
* **order_type**:
	+ Enter the type of order.
* **order_status (New, In Progress, Completed)**:
	+ Select the status of the order.
* **order_status_2 (Open, Closed, On Hold)**:
	+ Select the status of the order.
* **order_notes**:
	+ Enter any additional notes about the order.
* **order_notes_2**:
	+ Enter any additional notes about the second type of label.
* **submit**:
	+ Submit the form to confirm the order.
* **cancel**:
	+ Cancel the order.
## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid delays or errors in the order fulfillment process.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, consult your supervisor or manager before proceeding.
