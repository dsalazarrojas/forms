# doula_services_contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "doulas_services_contract" form is designed to capture information from clients or customers who are seeking doula services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with the required information, which includes First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone.
2.  Select the services you are interested in, such as Pregnancy Support, Prenatal Massage, Postpartum Care, and Other Services (if applicable).
3.  If you select "Other Services," you can specify the services in the "Other Services" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Birth Date** (`Birth Date`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
- **Services** (`Services`, select_multiple, required): Select the services you are interested in. Options include Pregnancy Support, Prenatal Massage, Postpartum Care, and Other Services.
    -   If you select "Other Services," fill out the "Other Services" field.
- **Other Services** (`Other Services`, text, required if "Other Services" is selected): Specify other services you are interested in. If "Other Services" is not selected, this field will be ignored.
    -   Be as specific and detailed as possible about the services you are looking for.
## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- If you have any special requests or preferences, please specify them in the "Other Services" field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
