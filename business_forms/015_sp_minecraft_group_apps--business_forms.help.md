# SP Minecraft Group APPS - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an SP Minecraft Group APPS, including its name, description, category, visibility, and tags.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the name of the Minecraft group in the "sp-minecraft-group-name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the group in the "sp-minecraft-group-description" field.
3. Select the category of the group from the "sp-minecraft-group-category" field. Options are "Business" and "Personal".
4. If the group category is not listed, enter your custom category in the "sp-minecraft-group-category-other" field.
5. Determine the visibility of the group in the "sp-minecraft-group-visibility" field. Options are "Public" and "Private".
6. If the group visibility is not listed, enter your custom visibility in the "sp-minecraft-group-visibility-other" field.
7. Add relevant tags to the group in the "sp-minecraft-group-tags" field.
8. Enter the name of the person who created the group in the "sp-minecraft-group-created-by" field.
9. Enter the date the group was created in the "sp-minecraft-group-created-at" field.
10. Select the category of the form from the "sp-minecraft-group-apps-category" field. Options are "Business Forms" and "Personal Forms".
11. Provide a brief description of the form in the "sp-minecraft-group-apps-description" field.
12. Add any additional notes about the form in the "sp-minecraft-group-apps-notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **sp-minecraft-group-name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the Minecraft group.
* **sp-minecraft-group-description** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the group.
* **sp-minecraft-group-category** (select_one, optional): Select the category of the group (Business or Personal).
* **sp-minecraft-group-category-other** (text, optional): Enter a custom category for the group if not listed.
* **sp-minecraft-group-visibility** (select_one, optional): Determine the visibility of the group (Public or Private).
* **sp-minecraft-group-visibility-other** (text, optional): Enter a custom visibility for the group if not listed.
* **sp-minecraft-group-tags** (text, optional): Add relevant tags to the group.
* **sp-minecraft-group-created-by** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person who created the group.
* **sp-minecraft-group-created-at** (date, optional): Enter the date the group was created.
* **sp-minecraft-group-apps-category** (select_one, optional): Select the category of the form (Business Forms or Personal Forms).
* **sp-minecraft-group-apps-description** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the form.
* **sp-minecraft-group-apps-notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about the form.
