# Global Project Needs Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a global project, including its objectives, challenges, resource needs, and other relevant details. It helps to ensure that the project is adequately equipped and resourced to meet the needs and expectations of all parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the project's objectives under "Project Objectives" and describe the main goals and outcomes of the project.
2.  List any challenges you expect to face under "Project Challenges".
3.  Specify the resources you need to complete the project under "Resource Needs".
4.  Select the relevant project initiatives for the project under "Project Initiatives" (you can select multiple options).
5.  Choose the start and end dates for the project under "Start Date" and "End Date".
6.  Enter the start and end times for the project under "Start Time" and "End Time".
7.  Provide the budget for the project under "Budget".
8.  Enter the location of the project under "Location".
9.  Specify the size of the team involved in the project under "Team Size".
10.  Describe the priority level of the project under "Priority".
11.  Choose the status of the project under "Status" (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
12.  Add any additional notes or comments under "Notes".
13.  Enter the contact name under "Contact Name".
14.  Enter the contact email under "Contact Email".
15.  Enter the contact phone number under "Contact Phone".
16.  Enter the contact phone extension under "Contact Phone Extension".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Project Objectives** (Text): Enter a brief description of the project's main objectives and outcomes.
*   **Project Challenges** (Text): List any challenges you expect to face during the project.
*   **Resource Needs** (Text): Specify the resources required to complete the project.
*   **Project Initiatives** (Select Multiple): Select the relevant project initiatives for the project.
*   **Project Initiative 1** (Select One): Select the most relevant project initiative for the project.
*   **Project Initiative 2** (Select Multiple): Select additional project initiatives for the project.
*   **Start Date** (Date): Enter the date on which the project will start.
*   **End Date** (Date): Enter the date on which the project will end.
*   **Start Time** (Time): Enter the start time of the project.
*   **End Time** (Time): Enter the end time of the project.
*   **Budget** (Number): Enter the budget for the project.
*   **Location** (Text): Enter the location of the project.
*   **Team Size** (Number): Specify the size of the team involved in the project.
*   **Priority** (Text): Describe the priority level of the project.
*   **Status** (Select One): Choose the status of the project (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
*   **Notes** (Note): Add any additional notes or comments about the project.
*   **Contact Name** (Text): Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone Extension** (Text): Enter the contact phone extension of the contact person.

Note to users: This form is designed to gather information about global projects. Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to ensure that the project is properly assessed and planned.
