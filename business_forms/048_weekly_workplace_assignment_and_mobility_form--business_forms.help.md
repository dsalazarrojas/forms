# Weekly Workplace Assignment And Mobility Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to weekly workplace assignments and mobility. It is intended for use in a work environment where employees need to report their availability and schedule changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Week Start Date field with the date you start your workweek.
2. Fill in the Week End Date field with the date you end your workweek.
3. Fill in the Target Date field with the date you expect to be available for the next work assignment or mobility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Week Start Date** (`week_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you start your workweek.
- **Week End Date** (`week_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you end your workweek.
- **Target Date** (`target_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you expect to be available for the next work assignment or mobility.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
* Double-check the dates entered to ensure accuracy and consistency.
