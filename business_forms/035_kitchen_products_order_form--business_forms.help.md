# kitchen_products_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for ordering kitchen products from our company. To complete the form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and submit it. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Company Information** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your company name.
* **Product Selected** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more kitchen products you want to order.
* **Quantity Ordered** (`4`, `number`, required): Enter the number of the selected product(s) you want to order.
* **Order Date** (`5`, `date`, required): Enter the date for the order.
* **Order Time** (`6`, `time`, required): Enter the time for the delivery.
* **Client Name** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company or individual who will be receiving the order.
* **Contact Number** (`8`, `text`, required): Enter a contact number for the client.
* **Email** (`9`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the client.
* **Message to Client** (`10`, `note`, optional): Enter any message for the client.
* **Delivery Time** (`11`, `time`, required): Enter the time for delivery.
* **Delivery Date** (`12`, `date`, required): Enter the date for delivery.
* **Address** (`13`, `text`, required): Enter the address where the order will be delivered.
* **City** (`14`, `text`, required): Enter the city where the order will be delivered.
* **State** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter the state where the order will be delivered.
* **Zip** (`16`, `text`, required): Enter the zip code where the order will be delivered.
* **Country** (`17`, `text`, required): Enter the country where the order will be delivered.
* **Product Description** (`18`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the product ordered.
* **Product Image** (`19`, `text`, required): Enter a URL or path to the product image.
* **Price** (`20`, `number`, required): Enter the price of the product.
* **Tax** (`21`, `number`, required): Enter the tax amount.
* **Total** (`22`, `number`, required): The total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method** (`23`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method (Credit, Debit, or Cash).
* **Payment Date** (`24`, `date`, required): Enter the date of payment.
* **Submit** (`25`, `text`, required): Click to submit the form.

Please note that some fields have specific requirements for formatting or content, so make sure to check the hints and labels for any specific guidelines.
