**summit_speaker_template - Help Guide**

# Purpose
This template is designed to gather speaker information and agreements for summits, conferences, or other events. It ensures that speakers provide necessary details about themselves and confirm their agreements with the event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.
2. Answer all required fields, which are marked as "required".
3. For fields with options, select the most appropriate choice from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **speaker_agrees**: A checkbox to indicate your agreement to be a speaker. (Required)
* **speaker_info**: A text field to provide your contact information. (Required)
* **speaker_agreement_signed**: A dropdown menu to confirm your agreement. (Required)
* **speaker_info_1-10**: Text fields to provide additional speaker information.
* **speaker_agreement_signed_1-10**: Date fields to confirm your agreement on specific dates. (Required)
* **speaker_agreement_signed_date**: A time field to confirm your agreement on a specific date. (Required)

## Tips

* Ensure you review and agree to all terms and conditions before submitting this form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers for assistance.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by speakers, not by event organizers or administrators. If you are an event organizer, you will need to create a separate form for speakers to fill out.
