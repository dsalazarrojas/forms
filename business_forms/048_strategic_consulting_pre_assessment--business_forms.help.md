# Strategic Consulting Pre Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients or customers about their challenges, goals, and project requirements for our strategic consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the required information about yourself and your project.
2. Select the correct options for each field, taking into account your specific situation.
3. Don't hesitate to reach out to us if you need any clarification or have any questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Company Size** (select one):
	+ 1-10: Small business or solo operation.
	+ 11-50: Medium-sized business or team.
	+ 51-100: Large business or team.
	+ 100+: Enterprise or very large organization.
* **Industry** (select multiple):
	+ Finance: Financial services, banking, or investing.
	+ Healthcare: Healthcare services, hospitals, or medical research.
	+ Technology: Software, hardware, or IT services.
	+ Marketing: Advertising, branding, or public relations.
	+ Other: Other industries, specify in the challenge field below.
* **Challenge** (note): Briefly describe the challenge you're facing.
* **Goal** (select one):
	+ Grow: Expand your business or reach new markets.
	+ Improve: Enhance efficiency, productivity, or quality.
	+ Optimize: Reduce costs, waste, or resources.
	+ Enhance: Improve customer satisfaction, experience, or engagement.
	+ Other: Specify in the challenge field above.
* **Readiness** (select multiple):
	+ High: Very prepared and ready to start.
	+ Medium: Somewhat prepared, but still learning.
	+ Low: Not very prepared, need guidance.
* **Engagement Date** (date): Enter the date you're looking to start the engagement.
* **Start Time** (time): Enter the time you'd like to start the engagement.
* **End Time** (time): Enter the time you'd like the engagement to end.
* **Duration** (number): Enter the number of hours or days you estimate for the engagement.
* **Budget** (select one):
	+ $0-10,000: Small project or low budget.
	+ $10,001-50,000: Medium-sized project or budget.
	+ $50,001-100,000: Large project or budget.
	+ $100,001-500,000: Very large project or budget.
	+ $500,001+: Enterprise project or budget.
* **Priority** (select one):
	+ High: High-priority project or challenge.
	+ Medium: Medium-priority project or challenge.
	+ Low: Low-priority project or challenge.
* **Team Size** (number): Enter the number of people on your team.
* **Team Lead** (select one):
	+ Existing: You have an existing team lead.
	+ New: You'll need help finding or establishing a team lead.
* **Meeting Link** (text): Enter the link to any relevant meeting invites or scheduling links.
* **Notes** (note): Add any additional comments or information about your project.

Note: This form is for informational purposes only. The answers you provide will help us better understand your project needs and provide a tailored proposal for our services.
