This is a form for capturing the terms of a joint venture between two parties. The form is meant to gather information about the joint venture terms, including the partner involved, the terms of the partnership, the date of the joint venture start, and the acknowledgment of the joint venture terms. This form is designed to be completed by the parties involved in the joint venture, and it will help ensure that all parties are on the same page and understand their responsibilities and obligations.

# Joint Venture Terms Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Joint Venture Terms Acknowledgement Form is a document that captures the terms of a joint venture between two parties. It is used to provide a clear understanding of the partnership's terms, conditions, and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the name of the venture partner**: In this field, please enter the name of the other party involved in the joint venture.
2. **Enter a brief description of the joint venture terms**: In this field, please provide a brief description of the terms of the joint venture, including any important details or conditions.
3. **Select the joint venture terms**: In this field, please choose one of the options provided to acknowledge the joint venture terms.
4. **Enter the date of the joint venture start**: In this field, please enter the date when the joint venture is expected to start.
5. **Review and submit the form**: Once you have completed all the fields, please review the form carefully and submit it to ensure that all the necessary information is captured correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **joint_venture_terms**: This field is where you can enter a brief description of the joint venture. 
* **venture_partner**: This field is meant for the name of the other party involved in the joint venture.
* **our_partners**: This field is currently not used in the form and seems to be a duplicate of the previous field.
* **joint_venture_terms_agreed**: This field is a selection of options that you must choose from to acknowledge the joint venture terms.
* **joint_venture_terms_description**: This field is a description of the joint venture terms.
* **date_of_joint_venture_start**: This field is where you can enter the date when the joint venture is expected to start.
