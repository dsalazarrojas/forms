# Splicing Maintenance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for power plant operators to report and record splicing maintenance activities, ensuring accurate and consistent capture of necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the title of the splicing maintenance activity.
2. Provide the name and title of the power plant operator performing the maintenance.
3. Specify the location of the splicing equipment being maintained.
4. Describe the splicing equipment being maintained.
5. Enter the date the maintenance was performed.
6. Enter the time the maintenance was performed.
7. (Optional) Add any comments or notes about the maintenance activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Splicing Maintenance Form Title** (`form_title`, required):
	+ A brief title of the splicing maintenance activity.
* **Power Plant Operator** (`power_plant_operator`, required):
	+ The name and title of the power plant operator performing the maintenance.
* **Splicing Equipment Location** (`splicing_equipment_location`, required):
	+ The location of the splicing equipment being maintained.
* **Splicing Equipment Description** (`splicing_equipment_description`, required):
	+ A detailed description of the splicing equipment being maintained.
* **Maintenance Date** (`maintenance_date`, required):
	+ The date the maintenance was performed.
* **Maintenance Time** (`maintenance_time`, required):
	+ The time the maintenance was performed.
* **Comments** (`comments`, optional):
	+ Additional notes or comments about the maintenance activity.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure reliable maintenance data.
- Use specific details about the splicing equipment and maintenance activity to assist operators in accurately reporting maintenance activities.
- Double-check dates and times to avoid errors.
