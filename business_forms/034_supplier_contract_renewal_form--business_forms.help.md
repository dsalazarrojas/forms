# Supplier Contract Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Contract Renewal Form is used to renew or update an existing supplier contract. This form helps to modify or extend the contract terms, conditions, or details with the supplier. Ensure that you're using this form for the correct purpose and not duplicating any other process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the existing contract and identify the fields that need to be updated or modified.
2. Fill in the supplier's information, such as their ID, contact person, and contract details (contract number, contract date, contract status, etc.).
3. Choose the correct contract type and category from the available options.
4. Update the contract value, term, and renewal term if necessary.
5. Enter the supplier's address, email, and phone number.
6. Confirm the current contract status and report the date of the update.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier ID** (`id`, `text`, required: false): Enter the supplier's unique identifier.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person to contact regarding this contract.
* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter the contract's unique identifier.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the contract was signed.
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Option 1: Active
	+ Option 2: Inactive
	+ Option 3: Pending
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the supplier.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Renewal Term** (`renewal_term`, `number`, required: false): Enter the renewal term in months or years.
* **Renewal Start Date** (`renewal_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the renewal term.
* **Renewal End Date** (`renewal_end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the renewal term.
* **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Option 1: Fixed
	+ Option 2: Periodic
	+ Option 3: On-demand
* **Contract Category** (`contract_category`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Option 1: Category 1
	+ Option 2: Category 2
	+ Option 3: Category 3
* **Contract Value** (`contract_value`, `number`, required: false): Enter the contract value.
* **Contract Term** (`contract_term`, `number`, required: false): Enter the contract term.
* **Supplier Address** (`supplier_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the supplier's address.
* **Supplier Email** (`supplier_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Supplier Phone** (`supplier_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the supplier's phone number.
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Option 1: Active
	+ Option 2: Inactive
	+ Option 3: Pending
* **Reported Date** (`reported_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the update was reported.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the supplier's name.
