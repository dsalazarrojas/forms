# digital_risk_advisory_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The digital_risk_advisory_contact_form is a contact form for prospects to reach out to your organization regarding digital risk and advisory matters. This form helps to gather essential information from prospects, allowing your team to provide tailored support and guidance. Please use this form only for legitimate purposes, and ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete. Do not use this form to request non-digital-related services or support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Propect Name** field. This field is optional but helps us understand who is reaching out to us.
2. In the **Contact Details** field, provide any relevant information that will allow our team to contact you efficiently.
3. Enter your contact number in the **Contact Number** field in the format **XXX-XXX-XXXX**.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. This will be used as the primary contact method.
5. Select the **Risk Type** that best describes your inquiry. Choose from the options **Risk Management**, **Compliance**, or **Security**.
6. In the **Description** field, provide a brief but detailed description of the risk you're encountering or concern you have.
7. Enter your organization's name in the **Business Name** field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Propect Name (prospect_name)
* **Propect Name**: This is an optional field where you can enter your name. We'll use this to understand who is contacting us.

### Contact Details (contact_details)
* **Contact Details**: Here, you can provide any additional information that will help us contact you efficiently. You might include your office number, department, or other contact information.

### Contact Number (contact_number)
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number in the format **XXX-XXX-XXXX** to help us reach out to you.

### Email (email)
* **Email**: Provide your email address to serve as the primary contact method.

### Risk Type (risk_type)
* **Risk Type**: Choose the type of risk you're encountering or concerned about. Select from **Risk Management**, **Compliance**, or **Security**.

### Description (description)
* **Description**: In this field, please provide a brief but detailed description of the risk you're facing or the concern you have. Be as specific as possible.

### Business Name (business_name)
* **Business Name**: If you're contacting us on behalf of your organization, enter the business name here. This helps us understand the context of your inquiry.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled correctly and accurately.
* Use the **Risk Type** options to help us quickly categorize and prioritize your request.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask and we'll be happy to help.
* Please note that this form is for digital-related matters only. If you have non-digital-related questions or concerns, please contact us through other means.
