# Paycheck Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paycheck Form is designed to collect and track employee's paycheck information for accurate and timely payment records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name in the "company" field.
2. Enter the employee's name in the "employee name" field.
3. Select the date of the last payment in the "date last payment" field.
4. Choose the pay period from the "pay period" dropdown list.
5. Enter the amount paid to the employee in the "amount paid" field.
6. Select the payment method (e.g., Check, Cash, Wire Transfer, or Online Bank Transfer) from the "payment method" dropdown list.
7. Enter the paycheck number in the "paycheck number" field.
8. Optionally, add any notes related to the payment in the "notes" field.
9. Select the payment status (e.g., Pending, Approved, Declined, Refunded, or Canceled) from the "payment status" dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of the company.
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee.
- **Date Last Payment** (`date_last_payment`, date, optional): Select the date of the last payment made to the employee.
- **Pay Period** (`pay_period`, select_one, required): Choose the pay period (1st Quarter, 2nd Quarter, 3rd Quarter, 4th Quarter).
- **Amount Paid** (`amount_paid`, number, required): Enter the amount paid to the employee.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method (Check, Cash, Wire Transfer, Online Bank Transfer).
- **Paycheck Number** (`paycheck_number`, text, required): Enter the paycheck number.
- **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any notes related to the payment (e.g., special circumstances).
- **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select the payment status (Pending, Approved, Declined, Refunded, Canceled).
