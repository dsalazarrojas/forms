# Tailgate Safety Meeting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tailgate Safety Meeting Form is a tool used to document the details of a tailgate safety meeting, ensuring that all necessary safety protocols are met during the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee attending the tailgate meeting.
2. **Company Name**: Optional, enter the name of the company if applicable.
3. **Location**: Enter the location where the tailgate meeting is being held.
4. **Date**: Enter the date of the tailgate meeting.
5. **Time In**: Enter the time the meeting begins.
6. **Time Out**: Enter the time the meeting ends.
7. **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the supervisor conducting the meeting.
8. **Supervisor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the supervisor.
9. **Emergency Contact**: Optional, enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
10. **Company Number**: Enter the company number (if applicable).
11. **Location Map**: Check if a location map is provided for the meeting location.
12. **Location Type**: Check if the location is a yes or no location (e.g., indoors or outdoors).
13. **Location Address**: Enter the address of the meeting location.
14. **Location State**: Enter the state where the meeting location is situated.
15. **Location City**: Enter the city of the meeting location.
16. **Location Zip**: Enter the zip code of the meeting location.
17. **Location Country**: Enter the country of the meeting location.
18. **Location Notes**: Optional, add any additional notes about the location.
19. **Tailgate Objectives**: Check if the meeting objectives were discussed.
20. **Safety Meeting Topics**: Check if the safety meeting topics were discussed.
21. **Safety Meeting Notes**: Optional, add any additional notes about the meeting.
22. **Safety Standards**: Check if the safety standards were reviewed.
23. **Safety Equipment**: Check if the safety equipment was discussed.
24. **Safety Procedure**: Optional, add any additional notes about the safety procedure.
