## Purpose
This form is designed to help users request a financial report duplication, providing a structured way to specify the report type, period, department, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the type of report you need (Financial or Other).
* Choose the period of the report (Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4).
* Select the department responsible for the report (Accounting, Finance, or Marketing).
* Choose the delivery method (Email, Fax, or Print).
* Enter the date the report should start, if applicable.
* Enter the date the report should end, if applicable.
* Specify the report's frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* Select the report's status (Active or Inactive).
* Enter the due date for the report, if required.
* Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
