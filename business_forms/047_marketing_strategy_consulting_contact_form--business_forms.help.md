# marketing_strategy_consulting_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to be used by visitors to the business who are interested in inquiring about marketing strategy consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:
- Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
- Fill out the required fields (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Message).
- If you wish to provide a phone number, please do so.
- If you wish to select a category for your inquiry, please choose from the options provided.
- If you wish to specify a date and time for the consultation, please use the provided date and time picker.
- If you wish to specify who you would like to be assigned to your inquiry, please select from the options provided.
- If you wish to provide a description of your needs, you can use the text area for this purpose.
- Finally, click on the "Submit" button to send your inquiry to the business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): This is the first name of the person inquiring about our services.
* **Last Name** (text, required): This is the last name of the person inquiring about our services.
* **Email** (email, required): This is the email address of the person inquiring about our services.
* **Phone** (text, optional): This is the phone number of the person inquiring about our services.
* **Message** (text, optional): This is the message or description of the service you are inquiring about.
* **Date** (date, optional): This is the date on which you would like to schedule the consultation.
* **Time** (time, optional): This is the time on which you would like to schedule the consultation.
* **Assigned To** (select_one, optional): This is the person who you would like to be assigned to your inquiry.
* **Category** (select_multiple, optional): This is the category under which you would like to categorize your inquiry.
* **Description** (note, optional): This is a more detailed description of your needs and requirements.
