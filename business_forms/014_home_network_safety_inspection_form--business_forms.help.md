# home_network_safety_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The home network safety inspection form is designed to help you assess and identify potential safety risks and vulnerabilities associated with your home network. This form helps you evaluate the safety of your network and its impact on your home and family. By completing this form, you will be able to identify areas of concern and take necessary steps to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the questions on each page of the form.
2. Respond to each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. Answer all questions, even if you're unsure of the answers.
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Network Inspection** (`network_inspection`, text, not required)
    This is where you can describe any recent changes or updates you have made to your network.
* **Network Vulnerabilities** (`network_vulnerabilities`, text, not required)
    Here, you can list any known vulnerabilities or weaknesses in your network that may impact its security.
* **Network Risks** (`network_risks`, text, not required)
    In this section, you can identify any potential risks or concerns related to your network, such as hacking, spamming, or unauthorized access.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
- If you're unsure of an answer, take your best guess and move on.
- Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure you've covered all the necessary information.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
