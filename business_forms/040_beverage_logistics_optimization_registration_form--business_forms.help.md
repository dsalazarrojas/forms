# Beverage Logistics Optimization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beverage Logistics Optimization Registration Form is designed to collect information about your company's beverage logistics operations. The information collected will help us understand your current state and identify areas for improvement to provide effective logistics optimization solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure we understand your logistics needs.
2. Be honest and detailed in your responses to help us provide relevant solutions.
3. Review and submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name (1)** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Person Name (2)** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Title (3)** (`contact_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address (4)** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person where we will send updates.
* **Phone Number (5)** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person for communication.
* **Company Address (6)** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of your company.
* **Company Size (7)** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select the number of employees in your company.
* **Annual Revenue (8)** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, required): Select your approximate annual revenue range.
* **Types of Beverages Distributed (9)** (`beverage_types`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of beverages your company handles.
* **Distribution Area (10)** (`distribution_area`, select_one, required): Select the geographic area you currently serve.
* **Current Logistics Setup (11)** (`current_logistics`, select_one, required): Select your current logistics setup.
* **Fleet Size (12)** (`fleet_size`, select_one, required): Select the number of vehicles you operate.
* **Current Logistics Challenges (13)** (`logistics_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Select the challenges you face with your current logistics setup.
* **Optimization Goals (14)** (`optimization_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select the goals you aim to achieve through logistics optimization.
* **Current Technology Usage (15)** (`technology_usage`, select_multiple, required): Select the technology you currently use for logistics.
* **Optimization Budget (16)** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Select your optimization budget range.
* **Implementation Timeline (17)** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Select when you want to start the optimization process.
* **Current Issues (18)** (`current_issues`, text, required): Describe specific problems you face with your current logistics setup.
* **Success Metrics (19)** (`success_metrics`, text, required): Specify the metrics that matter most to you.
* **Additional Comments (20)** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant information about your logistics needs.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) and are necessary for us to fully understand your logistics needs and provide effective solutions.
