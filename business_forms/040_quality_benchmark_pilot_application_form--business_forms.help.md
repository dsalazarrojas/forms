# Quality Benchmark Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Benchmark Pilot Application Form is used to collect participant information and assess their suitability for joining the quality improvement pilot program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
- Enter your name and address in the participant name and address fields.
- Select whether you are applying as a New or Existing participant.
- Provide your contact information for the participant and contact person (if applicable).
- Enter the application date.
- Provide any additional comments or details about your readiness for participating in the program.
