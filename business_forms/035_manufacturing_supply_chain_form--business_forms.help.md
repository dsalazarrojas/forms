# Manufacturing Supply Chain Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Supply Chain Form is designed to collect general information about a manufacturer's or supplier's supply chain, including their contact details, production capacity, and supply chain type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Manufacturer Name** in the "manufacturer-name" field.
2. Enter your **Company Name** in the "company-name" field.
3. Provide your **Company Address** in the "company-address" field.
4. Enter the name of your **Company Contact Person** in the "company-contact-person" field.
5. Enter your **Contact Email** in the "contact-email" field.
6. Enter your **Company Phone** in the "company-phone" field.
7. Enter your **Company Website** in the "company-website" field.
8. Choose the type of **Manufacturing Type** from the options provided in the "manufacturing-type" field.
9. Enter the **Subtype of Manufacturing** in the "manufacturing-sub-type" field.
10. Describe your **Product Description** in the "product-description" field.
11. Enter the details of your **Manufacturing Process** in the "manufacturing-process" field.
12. Enter the **Production Capacity** in the "production-capacity" field.
13. Enter the **Production Frequency** in the "production-frequency" field.
14. Select your **Supply Chain Type** from the options provided in the "supply-chain" field.
15. Enter any additional **Notes** in the "notes" field.
16. If necessary, enter the **Assigned Tool** in the "assigned-tool" field.
17. Enter the **Form ID** in the "form-id" field.
18. Enter the **Category** in the "category" field.
19. Enter a brief **Description** of the form in the "description" field.
20. If applicable, enter the **Output File** in the "output-file" field.
21. Select the **Form Category** from the options provided in the "form-category" field.
22. Enter the **Assigned User** in the "assigned-user" field.
23. Enter the **Form Version** in the "form-version" field.
24. Enter the **Created By** in the "created-by" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Manufacturer Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the manufacturer.
- **Company Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of your company.
- **Company Address** (text, required: false): Enter your company's address.
- **Company Contact Person** (text, required: false): Enter the name of your company's contact person.
- **Contact Email** (email, required: false): Enter your contact email.
- **Company Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your company phone number.
- **Company Website** (text, required: false): Enter your company website URL.
- **Manufacturing Type** (text, required: false): Choose the type of manufacturing from the options provided.
- **Subtype of Manufacturing** (text, required: false): Enter the subtype of manufacturing.
- **Product Description** (text, required: false): Describe your product.
- **Manufacturing Process** (text, required: false): Enter the details of your manufacturing process.
- **Production Capacity** (number, required: false): Enter the production capacity.
- **Production Frequency** (text, required: false): Enter the production frequency.
- **Supply Chain Type** (select multiple, required: false): Select the type of supply chain.
- **Notes** (note, required: false): Enter any additional notes.
- **Assigned Tool** (text, required: false): If necessary, enter the assigned tool.
- **Form ID** (text, required: false): Enter the form ID.
- **Category** (text, required: false): Enter the category.
- **Description** (text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the form.
- **Output File** (text, required: false): If applicable, enter the output file.
- **Form Category** (text, required: false): Select the form category.
- **Assigned User** (text, required: false): Enter the assigned user.
- **Form Version** (text, required: false): Enter the form version.
- **Created By** (text, required: false): Enter the created by field.
