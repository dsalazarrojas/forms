# Employee Grievance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Grievance Form is designed for employees to report issues or concerns they have with their manager, team, or work environment. This form helps to collect information about the grievance, which will be used to address and resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the contact person who is involved in the grievance (e.g., manager, team member, or HR representative).
2. Choose the type of grievance (e.g., issue with manager, team, or work environment).
3. Provide a detailed description of the grievance.
4. Specify the date when the grievance occurred.
5. If necessary, add any attachments related to the grievance.
6. Add any additional comments about the grievance.
7. Sign the form, selecting the person who is signing it (e.g., manager, team leader, or support staff).
8. Finally, enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.

Tips:

* Please provide as much detail as possible when describing the grievance.
* Make sure to attach any relevant documents or files related to the grievance.
* Sign the form to confirm that it is being submitted by the correct person.
* If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact HR or your supervisor.
