# Supply Chain Efficiency Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help companies evaluate their supply chain efficiency and sustainability. It collects information about the company's structure, operations, and performance in the supply chain management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and answer the questions that apply to your company.
2. For each field, provide accurate and relevant information about your company.
3. If a field is not applicable, mark it as "Not Applicable" or provide a brief explanation.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name as it appears on official documents and records.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for supply chain operations in your company.
* **Company Location**: Enter your company's physical location (city, state, country).
* **Annual Revenue**: Enter your company's annual revenue in numerical format (e.g., 100,000).
* **Customer Base**: Enter a brief description of your company's customer base (e.g., B2B, B2C, retail, etc.).
* **Employee Count**: Enter the total number of employees in your company.
* **Production Capacity**: Enter your company's production capacity (number of units produced per unit time).
* **Inventory Level**: Enter your company's current inventory level.
* **Supply Chain Length**: Enter the length of your company's supply chain (number of steps from raw materials to end-customer delivery).
* **Lead Time**: Enter the average lead time for your company's supply chain.
* **Supply Chain Efficiency**: Select one of the following options:
	+ High: The supply chain operates efficiently with minimal waste and delays.
	+ Medium: The supply chain operates with some level of inefficiency, but it's manageable.
	+ Low: The supply chain operates with significant waste and delays.
* **Supply Chain Sustainability**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Sustainable: The supply chain has minimal impact on the environment.
	+ Not Sustainable: The supply chain has a significant impact on the environment.
* **Reason for Non-Sustainability**: If you selected "Not Sustainable," choose the reason for it:
	+ Not Sustainable
	+ Environmental concerns
	+ Financial constraints
	+ Lack of resources
	+ Other
* **Action Plan for Sustainability**: Describe your company's plan to improve sustainability.
* **Action Plan Details**: Provide detailed information about your company's sustainability action plan.
* **Sustainability Goal**: Describe your company's sustainability goal.
* **Sustainability Target**: Set a target for your company's sustainability progress.
* **Sustainability Progress**: Enter your company's current sustainability progress.
* **Sustainability Benefit**: Describe the benefits of your company's sustainability efforts.
* **Sustainability Improvement Plan**: Describe your company's plan to improve sustainability.
* **Sustainability Improvement Progress**: Enter your company's current improvement progress.
* **Sustainability Improvement Benefit**: Describe the benefits of your company's sustainability improvement efforts.
* **Sustainability Improvement Plan Details**: Provide detailed information about your company's sustainability improvement plan.

Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your company's supply chain efficiency and sustainability are well evaluated and improved.
