# fire_modification_certs - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "fire_modification_certs" form is used to record information related to fire modification certifications, including fire modification status, certification dates, and inspection results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the fire modification certificate in the "fire_modification_cert" field.
2. Select the fire modification date from the calendar in the "fire_modification_date" field.
3. Enter the fire modification time in the "fire_modification_time" field.
4. Select the fire modification cause from the options provided in the "fire_cause" field.
5. Enter the fire modification description in the "fire_modification_description" field.
6. Select the fire inspection date from the calendar in the "fire_inspection_date" field.
7. Enter the fire inspection time in the "fire_inspection_time" field.
8. Select the fire inspection status from the options provided in the "fire_inspection_status" field.
9. Select the fire inspection result from the options provided in the "fire_inspection_result" field.
10. Enter the fire inspection notes in the "fire_inspection_notes" field.
11. Enter the certification date in the "cert_date" field.
12. Enter the certification time in the "cert_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **fire_modification_cert** (`fire_modification_cert`, text, required/optional): Enter the fire modification certificate.
* **fire_modification_date** (`fire_modification_date`, date, required/optional): Select the fire modification date from the calendar.
* **fire_modification_time** (`fire_modification_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the fire modification time.
* **fire_cause** (`fire_cause`, select_one, required/optional): Select the fire modification cause from the options provided.
* **fire_modification_description** (`fire_modification_description`, note, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the fire modification.
* **fire_inspection_date** (`fire_inspection_date`, date, required/optional): Select the fire inspection date from the calendar.
* **fire_inspection_time** (`fire_inspection_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the fire inspection time.
* **fire_inspection_status** (`fire_inspection_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the fire inspection status from the options provided.
* **fire_inspection_result** (`fire_inspection_result`, select_one, required/optional): Select the fire inspection result from the options provided.
* **fire_inspection_notes** (`fire_inspection_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter the fire inspection notes.
* **cert_date** (`cert_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the certification date.
* **cert_time** (`cert_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the certification time.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field according to your knowledge and experience.
* Enter accurate and clear information in the fields to ensure accurate certification records.
* Review each field's explanation before proceeding to ensure understanding.
