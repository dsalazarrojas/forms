# Intellectual Property Rules Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intellectual Property Rules Acknowledgement Form is used to collect acknowledgments from users related to intellectual property rules, including their name, contact information, and dates related to IP rules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Go to the form and click on the "Submit" button.
* Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Enter your name, email, and other required contact information in the provided fields.
* Enter the correct date and time of the acknowledgement.
* Enter your acknowledgement statement in the provided text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, *required*): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, *required*): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, *required*): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, *optional*): Enter your phone number (not required).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, *required*): Enter the date of the acknowledgement.
* **Acknowledgement Date** (`acknowledgement_date`, `date`, *required*): Enter the date of the acknowledgement.
* **Acknowledgement Time** (`acknowledgement_time`, `time`, *required*): Enter the time of the acknowledgement.
* **I-PR Acknowledgement** (`i_pr_acknowledgement`, `text`, *required*): Enter the acknowledgement statement.
* **I-PR Sentences** (`i_pr_sentences`, `text`, *required*): Enter the sentences related to the acknowledgement.
* **I-PR Agreement Terms** (`i_pr_agreement_terms`, `text`, *required*): Enter the agreement terms related to the acknowledgement.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields correctly.
* Double-check the date and time before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the form administrator.
