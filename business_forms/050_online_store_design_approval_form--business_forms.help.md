# Online Store Design Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Store Design Approval Form is used to streamline the online store design approval process between the store owner and designer. It ensures that both parties are on the same page and that the design meets the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Store Design section with your online store design details.
2. Provide Business Details, including your business name, contact information, and any relevant business information.
3. Fill out the Design Approvals section with any comments or approval requests.
4. Add any Form Completion comments or notes.
5. Enter Client Details, if applicable.
6. Review and Approve or Reject the submission.
7. Add any Submission Notes or Comments.
8. Select the Submission Status.
9. Enter the Submission Date and Time.
10. Enter the Store Design Status.
11. Add any Submission Status Notes or Comments.
12. Enter the Business Status.
13. Add any Submission Status Comments.
14. View the Submission History.
