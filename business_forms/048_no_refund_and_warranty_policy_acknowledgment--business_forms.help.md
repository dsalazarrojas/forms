# no_refund_and_warranty_policy_acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the customer's acknowledgment and acceptance of the no-refund and warranty policy. It is essential for ensuring that both the customer and the business are on the same page regarding their responsibilities and commitments under the policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information in each field, and select the "Acknowledge and Accept" option from the dropdown menu at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Acknowledge** (`acknowledgment`, `text`, required): Provide a clear and concise statement acknowledging the no-refund and warranty policy.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their identification or government-issued ID.
* **Provider Business Name** (`provider_business_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the business providing the product or service.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of the business or representative that the customer can reach out to for further information or assistance.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the contact email of the business or representative that the customer can reach out to for further information or assistance.
* **Acceptance Date** (`acceptance_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which the customer accepted the policy.
* **Acceptance Time** (`acceptance_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time on which the customer accepted the policy.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, required): Provide a clear and concise statement summarizing the terms and conditions of the no-refund and warranty policy.
* **Acknowledgment of Terms** (`acknowledgment_terms`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the two options to indicate whether you have read and agree to the terms and conditions or not.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required): A signature field where the customer can sign their name to confirm their acknowledgment and acceptance of the policy.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understood all the terms and conditions before selecting the "I have read and agree to the terms and conditions" option.
* If you have any questions or concerns regarding the no-refund and warranty policy, please reach out to the contact email or contact number provided.
