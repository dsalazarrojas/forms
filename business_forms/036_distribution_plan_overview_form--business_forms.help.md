# Distribution Plan Overview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information for distribution planning, enabling teams to effectively manage and prioritize resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the target areas where the distribution plan will be applied.
2. Choose the resource(s) best suited for the task.
3. Specify the timeline for the distribution plan.
4. Provide a contact email and phone number (if applicable) for further questions or updates.
5. Identify the team member(s) assigned to the distribution plan.
6. Add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Distribution Plan Overview** (`distribution_plan_overview`, text, required): Briefly describe the distribution plan, specifying the scope and objectives.
- **Target Areas** (`target_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable areas that the distribution plan will cover (e.g., North, South, East, West).
- **Resources** (`resources`, select_one, required): Choose the most suitable resource(s) for the distribution plan.
- **Timelines** (`timelines`, date, optional): Specify the start and end dates for the distribution plan, if applicable.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the distribution plan.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address where you can be reached for further questions or updates.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further questions or updates.
- **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Choose the team member(s) responsible for the distribution plan.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the distribution plan.
- **Distribution Plan Overview Form Comments** (`distribution_plan_overview_form_comments`, text, optional): Add any general comments or feedback about the form itself.
