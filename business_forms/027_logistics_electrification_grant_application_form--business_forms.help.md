# logistics_electrification_grant_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from applicants for a grant related to logistics electrification. This grant aims to support initiatives that promote the use of electricity to improve logistics efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your User Name, Email, Phone number, and Company name.
2. Provide your current Address.
3. Describe your proposed project or initiative in the Description field.
4. Indicate whether your project involves Electrification Initiatives or not.
5. Enter your expected Budget for the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (Required, Text): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or business card.
* **Email** (Required, Email): Enter your professional email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (Required, Text): Enter your business phone number where you can be reached.
* **Company** (Required, Text): Enter the name of your company as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Address** (Required, Text): Enter your business address where we can reach you or send any correspondence.
* **Description** (Required, Text): Briefly describe your proposed project or initiative and how it relates to logistics electrification.
* **Electrification Initiatives** (Required, Select Multiple): Check all the Electrification Initiatives that your project involves, such as 'Yes, for automated processes' or 'No, for energy efficiency'. 
* **Budget** (Required, Number): Enter your expected budget for the project.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please contact us at your preferred contact method.
* Ensure that your project meets the grant's eligibility criteria before applying.
