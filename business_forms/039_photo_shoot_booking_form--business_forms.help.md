# Photo Shoot Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Shoot Booking Form is designed for customers to book multiple photo shoots with different preferences and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer details, including your name, email, phone, and any additional messages.
2. Select your preferred photo session (Session 1 or Session 2).
3. Choose the type of photo shoot (Indoor or Outdoor).
4. Enter the date and start time for your photo shoot.
5. Select the duration of your photo shoot.
6. Specify the number of attendees for your photo shoot.
7. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone.
8. Repeat steps 2-7 for Session 2, if applicable.
