# idea_details_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an idea, providing a structured and organized way to document details about a concept or proposal. The goal is to gather essential information to develop and improve the idea.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering a title for your idea. This title should be a concise and descriptive name for your concept.
2. Provide a detailed description of your idea, outlining its key points and features.
3. Select the platforms where your idea can be implemented or applied (Web, Mobile, Desktop, Cloud, or Other).
4. Describe the solution or approach to addressing the problem you're trying to solve.
5. Identify the specific problem you're trying to solve.
6. Indicate the types of devices that can be used to implement your idea (Smartphone, Tablet, Laptop, Desktop, or Other).
7. Confirm whether your idea is connected to other platforms or systems ('True' or 'False').
8. Choose a level of difficulty for coding implementation (Easy, Harder, or Very Hard).
9. Select the number of platforms that are connected to your idea (from 100 to 50,000).
10. Upload any supporting output files or materials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Title**: Enter a brief title for your idea. This will serve as a summary or label for your concept.
* **Prophets Description**: Provide a detailed description of your idea, including its features and key points.
* **Platform**: Select one or more platforms where your idea can be applied (e.g., Web, Mobile, Desktop, Cloud, Other).
* **Solution**: Describe the approach or solution to addressing the problem you're trying to solve.
* **Prophets Problem**: Identify the specific problem you're trying to solve.
* **Devices**: Indicate the types of devices that can be used to implement your idea (e.g., Smartphone, Tablet, Laptop, Desktop, Other).
* **Connected**: Confirm whether your idea is connected to other platforms or systems ('True' or 'False').
* **No Coding**: Choose the level of difficulty for coding implementation (Easy, Harder, Very Hard).
* **Platforms Connected**: Select the number of platforms that are connected to your idea (e.g., 100, 500, 1000, 5000, 10,000, 50,000).
* **Output File**: Upload any supporting output files or materials related to your idea.

Tips:

* Be as detailed as possible when describing your idea and its features.
* Be clear and concise when selecting the platforms and devices for implementation.
* Ensure that you're honest about the difficulty level of coding implementation.
* Attach relevant output files to support your idea and make it more credible.
