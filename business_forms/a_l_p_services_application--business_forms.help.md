# A.L.P Services Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The A.L.P Services Application form is designed for organizations to submit their requests for various services, such as account setup, managed services, one-time consulting, data migration, and training & workshops. This form is used to gather essential information from applicants, including their contact details, organization details, and project requirements. The collected data helps us to better understand their needs and provide more accurate proposals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information before starting the application.
2. Enter the applicant's full name, contact email address, phone number, and organization name.
3. Provide the organization's website (if applicable).
4. Select all services you are interested in (account setup & onboarding, ongoing managed services, one-time consulting, data migration, and training & workshops).
5. Enter the preferred project start date (if you have one).
6. Estimate your approximate annual budget (in USD).
7. Specify the number of users or team members for the service (if applicable).
8. Describe the primary goal for engaging A.L.P services.
9. (Optional) Provide references or case studies that demonstrate your fit.
10. Confirm that you agree to be contacted regarding this application.
11. Add any additional notes or supporting information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant full name** (`applicant_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter the primary contact's legal name.
* **Contact email address** (`applicant_email`, `email`, required): Provide a business email for follow-up.
* **Contact phone number** (`applicant_phone`, `phone`, required): Include country code if outside local area.
* **Organization / Company name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter the registered business name or DBA.
* **Organization website** (`organization_website`, `text`, optional): Provide the full URL or leave blank.
* **Services requested** (`services_requested`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose any A.L.P services you are applying for (account setup & onboarding, ongoing managed services, one-time consulting, data migration, and training & workshops).
* **Preferred project start date** (`preferred_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the preferred project start date (if you have one).
* **Approximate annual budget** (`approximate_annual_budget`, `decimal`, optional): Enter the best estimate to help scope the proposal (in USD).
* **Number of users / team members** (`team_size`, `integer`, optional): Enter the whole number of users that will access services.
* **Primary goal** (`primary_goal`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the expected outcome (one sentence).
* **References or case studies** (`references_or_case_studies`, `text`, optional): Provide links or short notes that demonstrate fit.
* **Acceptance terms** (`acceptance_terms`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to be contacted regarding this application.
* **Additional notes or supporting information** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information or notes to support your application.
