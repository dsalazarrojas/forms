# Non Scheduled Work Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document non-scheduled work agreements between employees and management. It captures relevant details about the work, the client, and the agreement terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the agreement in the `dateOfAgreement` field.
2. Enter the scope of the work in the `workScope` field.
3. Enter the client's details in the `clientDetails` field.
4. Enter the agreement terms in the `agreementTerms` field.
5. Select the status of the work in the `workStatus` field (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
6. Enter any comments about the work in the `comments` field.
7. Enter the name of the manager who signed the agreement in the `managerName` field.
8. Enter the email of the manager who signed the agreement in the `managerEmail` field.
9. Enter the phone number of the manager who signed the agreement in the `managerPhone` field.
10. Enter the title of the manager who signed the agreement in the `managerTitle` field.
11. Enter the name of the employee who performed the work in the `employeeName` field.
12. Enter the role of the employee who performed the work in the `employeeRole` field.
