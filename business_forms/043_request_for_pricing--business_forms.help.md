# Request For Pricing - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request For Pricing form is designed to gather information from customers or clients who are interested in requesting a price quote for a specific business opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of product or service you are interested in by choosing from the "Pricing Type" dropdown menu.
2. If you are interested in both goods and services, select all relevant options for "Goods/Services".
3. Enter the item number, price, and quantity you are interested in.
4. Select the currency for your price range and currency symbol.
5. Choose the price range you are interested in by selecting from the "Price Range" dropdown menu.
6. Select the currency symbol and currency for the price range by choosing from the "Currency" dropdown menus.
7. Enter your contact information, including name, email, phone number, company, address, zip code, city, state, and country.
8. Add any additional comments or questions you may have.
9. Finally, select a date and time for the submission, and click the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pricing Type** (`pricing_type`): Choose the type of product or service you are interested in, either "Goods" or "Service".
* **Goods/Services** (`goods_service`): If you are interested in both goods and services, select all relevant options.
* **Item Number** (`item_number`): Enter the number of the item you are interested in.
* **Item Price** (`item_price`): Enter the price of the item you are interested in.
* **Price Range** (`price_range`): Choose the price range you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`): Enter the quantity of the item you are interested in.
* **Currency** (`currency`): Select the currency for your price quote.
* **Currency Symbol** (`currency_symbol`): Select the currency symbol you would like to use for your price quote.
* **Price Range Currency** (`price_range_currency`): Select the currency for your price range.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`): Enter your company name.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your address.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`): Enter your zip code.
* **City** (`city`): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`): Enter your state.
* **Country** (`country`): Enter your country.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Date** (`date`): Select a date for the submission.
* **Time** (`time`): Select a time for the submission.
* **Price Range** (`price_range`): Choose the price range you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click the "Submit" button to send your request for pricing.

Tips:
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a prompt and accurate response.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* This form should not be used for duplicate or unnecessary requests.
