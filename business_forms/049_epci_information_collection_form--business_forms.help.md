# epci_information_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and update information about ongoing or completed projects, ensuring accurate and up-to-date records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Company Name** of the project.
2. Select the **Project Status** from the available options (Active, Inactive, On Hold).
3. Enter a brief **Project Description**.
4. Select the **Project Location**.
5. Enter the **Project Start Date** and **Project Completion Date** using the correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
6. Enter the **Project Manager**'s name and title (if applicable).
7. Enter the **Customer Name**.
8. Enter the **Customer Phone** number.
9. Enter the **Customer Email** address.
10. Enter the **Customer Address**.
11. Enter the **Company Address**.
12. Enter the **Contact Person**'s name and title (if applicable).
13. Enter the **Customer Contact Person**'s name and title (if applicable).
14. Enter the **Customer Contact Email** address.
15. Enter the **Customer Contact Phone** number.
16. Enter the **Project ID** (if applicable).
17. Select the **Project Status 2** from the available options (Active, Inactive, On Hold).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Name (text, required: false)
- This field is used to enter the name of the company involved in the project.

### Project Status (select_one, required: false)
- Select from the options: Active, Inactive, On Hold, to indicate the current status of the project.

### Project Description (text, required: false)
- Enter a brief description of the project.

### Project Location (text, required: false)
- Enter the location where the project is taking place.

### Project Start Date (date, required: false)
- Enter the date when the project started using the YYYY-MM-DD format.

### Project Completion Date (date, required: false)
- Enter the date when the project was completed using the YYYY-MM-DD format.

### Project Manager (text, required: false)
- Enter the name and title of the person in charge of the project.

### Customer Name (text, required: false)
- Enter the name of the customer.

### Customer Phone (text, required: false)
- Enter the phone number of the customer.

### Customer Email (email, required: false)
- Enter the email address of the customer.

### Customer Address (text, required: false)
- Enter the address of the customer.

### Company Address (text, required: false)
- Enter the address of the company.

### Contact Person (text, required: false)
- Enter the name and title of the contact person.

### Customer Contact Person (text, required: false)
- Enter the name and title of the customer contact person.

### Customer Contact Email (email, required: false)
- Enter the email address of the customer contact person.

### Customer Contact Phone (text, required: false)
- Enter the phone number of the customer contact person.

### Project ID (text, required: false)
- Enter the project ID (if applicable).

### Project Status 2 (select_one, required: false)
- Select from the options: Active, Inactive, On Hold, to indicate the current status of the project 2.

Tips:
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- If a field is not applicable, leave it blank.
- If you're unsure about any information, consult with the project manager or customer representative.
