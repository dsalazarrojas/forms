## Women's Boutique Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers when placing an order at a women's boutique. It gathers data on customer details, order items, shipping information, payment details, and order summaries. This form is intended to streamline the ordering process and ensure accurate and complete customer information is captured for efficient order fulfillment and potential follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Info** (Page 1) to provide your name, contact details, and any other relevant information about yourself.
2. List the items you'd like to order in the **Order Items** section (Page 1).
3. Provide your **Shipping Info** (Page 1) to ensure your order is delivered correctly.
4. Enter your **Payment Info** (Page 1) for secure and efficient payment processing.
5. Review your **Order Summary** (Page 1) to ensure all items are included and details are accurate.
6. Add any **Customer Notes** (Page 1) if you have special requests or specific instructions for your order.
7. Enter your **Contact Info** (Page 1) so we can contact you about your order or any related matters.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Customer Info (Page 1)**
* **Name** (`text`, optional): Please enter your full name as you'd like it to be displayed for your order.
* **Contact Info** (`text`, optional): Provide your email address, phone number, or any other contact method for communication.

### **Order Items (Page 1)**
* **Order Items** (`text`, optional): List the items you'd like to order, including descriptions and quantities if applicable.

### **Shipping Info (Page 1)**
* **Shipping Info** (`text`, optional): Enter your shipping address for accurate and prompt delivery.

### **Payment Info (Page 1)**
* **Payment Info** (`text`, optional): Enter your payment method and details to complete the transaction.

### **Order Summary (Page 1)**
* **Order Summary** (`text`, optional): Review and confirm your order details before submission.

### **Customer Notes (Page 1)**
* **Customer Notes** (`text`, optional): Add any special requests or instructions for your order.

### **Contact Info (Page 1)**
* **Contact Info** (`text`, optional): Enter your preferred contact method for follow-up on your order.

Tips: If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, please reach out to [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
