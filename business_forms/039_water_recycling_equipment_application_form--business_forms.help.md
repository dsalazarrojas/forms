# Water Recycling Equipment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from businesses applying for water recycling equipment. It is used to gather essential details to process your application efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If you need help with any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business name as it appears on your company documents.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person for this application.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email** (`applicant_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the project where the equipment will be used.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the project and how the water recycling equipment will be used.
* **Equipment Requested** (`equipment_requested`, `text`, required: false): Describe the specific equipment you are applying for.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the project.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total cost of the project.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one option from the list to provide additional information.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select multiple options from the list to provide additional information.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of day for the project.
