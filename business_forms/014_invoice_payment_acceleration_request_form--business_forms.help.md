# Invoice Payment Acceleration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request payment acceleration from the finance team. Please use it to submit requests for invoices that need urgent payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review your request carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the vendor or supplier for whom you are requesting payment acceleration.
* **Invoice Date** (`invoice_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the invoice for which you are requesting payment acceleration.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the desired payment date for the accelerated payment.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the total amount to be paid.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or context about the request.
* **Finance Email** (`finance_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for the finance team to contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Vendor Contact** (`vendor_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the vendor or supplier.
* **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the vendor or supplier.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness before sending it to the finance team.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or the process, contact the finance team for clarification.
