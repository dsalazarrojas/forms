# Developer Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Developer Enrollment Form" is a form used for internal purposes to collect information about developers who are enrolling in a company program or initiative. This form helps the company track and manage developer participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Developer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email address that you check regularly.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. If you are affiliated with a company, enter your company name in the "Company" field.
5. Select your role in the "Role" field. Choose "Developer" if you are a developer, "Partner" if you are a partner of the company, or "Vendor" if you are a vendor.
6. Select one or more programs you are interested in or already participating in from the "Program" field.
7. Read and sign the "Agreement" field. This is an important step, as it indicates your understanding and acceptance of the program terms and conditions.
8. Enter the date you are enrolling in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Developer Name (text)**: Enter your full name.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly. This will be used for communication with you about the program.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number, which will help us contact you for any questions or follow-ups.
* **Company (text)**: Enter your company name, if applicable.
* **Role (select_one)**: Choose your role from the dropdown list: Developer, Partner, or Vendor.
* **Program (select_multiple)**: Select one or more programs you are interested in or already participating in.
* **Agreement (note)**: Read and sign this field to indicate your understanding and acceptance of the program terms and conditions.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date you are enrolling in the program.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with your enrollment.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us for assistance.
* If you are already registered with the company, please do not fill out this form.
