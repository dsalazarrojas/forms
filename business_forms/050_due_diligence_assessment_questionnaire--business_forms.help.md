# Due Diligence Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Due Diligence Assessment Questionnaire is a comprehensive form designed to gather information about a company's entity, contact person, financial, legal, business structure, ownership, management, and risk management aspects. This form helps assess and provide data-driven insights for various business stakeholders, such as investors, partners, or advisors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Due Diligence Assessment Questionnaire, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name and location in the "Company Name and Location" field.
2. Provide contact person information in the "Contact Person Information" field.
3. Provide financial information in the "Financial Information" field.
4. Enter legal information in the "Legal Information" field.
5. Answer questions related to the company's business structure in the "Business Structure" field.
6. Answer questions related to the company's ownership structure in the "Ownership" field.
7. Provide information about the company's management team in the "Management" field.
8. Describe the company's risk management practices and policies in the "Risk Management" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name and Location** (`entity_info`, text, not required): This field is used to enter the company name and its location.
* **Contact Person Information** (`contact_info`, text, not required): This field is used to provide contact person's name, email, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
* **Financial Information** (`financial_info`, text, not required): This field is used to provide financial information about the company, such as revenue, expenses, and funding details.
* **Legal Information** (`legal_info`, text, not required): This field is used to provide legal information about the company, such as licenses, permits, and certifications.
* **Business Structure** (`business_structure`, text, not required): This field is used to describe the company's business structure, such as company type (e.g., LLC, Corporation), ownership percentage, and other relevant details.
* **Ownership** (`ownership`, text, not required): This field is used to provide information about the company's ownership structure, such as ownership percentage and ownership type (e.g., individual, entity).
* **Management** (`management`, text, not required): This field is used to provide information about the company's management team, including names, titles, and contact information.
* **Risk Management** (`risk_management`, text, not required): This field is used to describe the company's risk management practices and policies, such as risk assessment, mitigation, and monitoring.

## Tips
When completing this form, please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date. Also, be concise and clear in your answers to ensure the quality of the assessment. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
