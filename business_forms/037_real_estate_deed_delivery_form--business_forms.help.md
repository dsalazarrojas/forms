# Real Estate Deed Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Deed Delivery Form is used to collect information for the delivery of real estate deeds. It helps capture relevant details about the real estate transaction, including the category of the deed and a brief description of the property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the deed from the available options.
2. Provide a brief description of the property being delivered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, select_one): Select the category of the deed from the options provided. This will help determine the type of deed being delivered.
* **Description** (`description`, text): Provide a brief description of the property being delivered. This can include the property's address, size, or other relevant details.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct category for the deed.
- Provide an accurate and brief description of the property.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed (though in this case, the form is not strictly required due to the `required: false` property in the YAML).
