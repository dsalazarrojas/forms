# Load Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Load Agreement Form" is used to document the terms of a load agreement between shippers, carriers, and brokers. It provides a standardized way to collect relevant information and details about the load, ensuring clarity and accuracy in communicating with all parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the correct response.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure all required and optional fields are completed accurately.
