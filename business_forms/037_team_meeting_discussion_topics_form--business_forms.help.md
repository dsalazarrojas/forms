# Team Meeting Discussion Topics Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Meeting Discussion Topics Form is a standardized template used to collect necessary information for team meetings. This form ensures that all relevant details are discussed during the meeting and helps in organizing follow-up activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Describe the main topic of the meeting in the "Meeting Topic" field.
2.  Outline the key questions to be discussed in the "Meeting Questions" field.
3.  Specify the meeting agenda in the "Meeting Agenda" field.
4.  Provide the meeting date in the "Meeting Date" field.
5.  Enter the meeting time in the "Meeting Time" field.
6.  Select the meeting location in the "Meeting Location" field.
7.  Identify the attendees in the "Attendees" field.
8.  Add any relevant meeting notes or minutes in the "Meeting Notes" field.
9.  Check if there is a follow-up action item in the "Follow-up Action Item" field.
10.  If applicable, specify the next meeting date and time in the "Next Meeting Date" and "Next Meeting Time" fields.
11.  Check if the meeting location will change in the "Meeting Location Change" field.
12.  Describe the meeting topic in the "Meeting Topic (2)" field.
13.  Choose one of the options for the "Meeting Question" field.
14.  Optionally, add additional questions or notes in the "Meeting Question 1", "Meeting Question 2", and "Meeting Agenda 2" fields.
15.  Finally, confirm the meeting topic in the "Meeting Topic (2) (1)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Topic**: (<name>: Meeting Topic, <type>: text, required: true) - Describe the main topic of the meeting.
* **Meeting Questions**: (<name>: Meeting Questions, <type>: text, required: true) - List the key questions to be discussed.
* **Meeting Agenda**: (<name>: Meeting Agenda, <type>: text, required: true) - Outline the meeting agenda.
* **Meeting Date**: (<name>: Meeting Date, <type>: date, required: true) - Enter the meeting date.
* **Meeting Time**: (<name>: Meeting Time, <type>: time, required: true) - Enter the meeting time.
* **Meeting Location**: (<name>: Meeting Location, <type>: text, required: true) - Select the meeting location.
* **Attendees**: (<name>: Attendees, <type>: text, required: true) - Identify the attendees.
* **Meeting Notes**: (<name>: Meeting Notes, <type>: note, required: true) - Add any meeting notes or minutes.
* **Follow-up Action Item**: (<name>: Follow-up Action Item, <type>: checkbox, required: true) - Check if there is a follow-up action item.
* **Next Meeting Date**: (<name>: Next Meeting Date, <type>: date, required: false) - Specify the next meeting date.
* **Next Meeting Time**: (<name>: Next Meeting Time, <type>: time, required: false) - Enter the next meeting time.
* **Meeting Location Change**: (<name>: Meeting Location Change, <type>: checkbox, required: false) - Check if the meeting location will change.
* **Meeting Topic (2)**: (<name>: Meeting Topic (2), <type>: text, required: true) - Describe the meeting topic.
* **Meeting Question**: (<name>: Meeting Question, <type>: select_one, required: true) - Choose one of the options.
* **Meeting Question 1**: (<name>: Meeting Question 1, <type>: text, required: false) - Optionally, add additional questions or notes.
* **Meeting Question 2**: (<name>: Meeting Question 2, <type>: text, required: false) - Optionally, add additional questions or notes.
* **Meeting Agenda 2**: (<name>: Meeting Agenda 2, <type>: text, required: true) - Outline the meeting agenda.
* **Meeting Topic (2) (1)**: (<name>: Meeting Topic (2) (1), <type>: text, required: false) - Confirm the meeting topic.

Tips:
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Use the "Meeting Notes" field for any additional information or notes.
* Be as specific as possible when entering dates and times.
* If a field is not applicable, mark it as "N/A" or leave it blank.
* Review and submit the form carefully before proceeding with the meeting.
