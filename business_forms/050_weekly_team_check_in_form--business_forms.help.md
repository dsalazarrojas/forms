# Weekly Team Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for team members to provide a weekly update on their progress, challenges, and next steps.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with your progress, challenges, and next steps for the past week.
2.  Select one or more options that apply to your challenges and achievements.
3.  Provide a brief update on your progress and next steps.
4.  Enter any relevant meeting notes from the team meeting.
5.  Enter your email address and/or phone number if you want to be contacted.
6.  If applicable, specify who the task is assigned to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Check In**: Enter a brief update on your progress for the past week. This should include any successes, challenges, or changes that have occurred. 
* **Challenges**: Select one or more options that describe your challenges for the past week. Choose from: "Challenges" or "Other". 
* **Achievements**: Select one or more options that describe your achievements for the past week. Choose from: "Yes" or "Other". 
* **updates**: Enter a brief update on your progress and any next steps you plan to take. 
* **Next Steps**: Enter a date when you plan to take action on the next steps mentioned in the update. 
* **Meeting Notes**: Provide a brief summary of key points discussed during the team meeting. 
* **Email**: Enter your email address to be contacted regarding your progress. 
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to be contacted regarding your progress. 
* **Assigned To**: If this task is assigned to someone else, select their name from the options provided. 

## Tips
* Please keep your responses brief and focused on key points.
* Use the "Other" option when selecting challenges or achievements that are not listed.
* Ensure you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you need help with the form, please reach out to your team lead or manager.
