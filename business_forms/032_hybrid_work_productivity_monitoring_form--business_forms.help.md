# Hybrid Work Productivity Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and monitor the productivity of employees in a hybrid work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee ID.
2. Enter the start and end times of your workday.
3. Report the number of work hours spent on tasks.
4. Indicate the number of focus blocks completed.
5. Record your break time.
6. Report whether you have attended any meetings.
7. Report the number of tasks completed and remaining.
8. Indicate the areas of focus for your work.
9. Add any comments or notes about your work.
10. Optionally, provide a form ID for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Your employee ID is a unique identifier for your account. This is a required field.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of your workday.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of your workday.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): Report the number of hours worked during your day.
* **Focus Blocks** (`focus_blocks`, number, optional): Report the number of focus blocks completed.
* **Break Time** (`break_time`, number, optional): Report the number of break times taken.
* **Meetings** (`meetings`, select_multiple, optional): Report whether you have attended any meetings. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Tasks Completed** (`tasks_completed`, number, optional): Report the number of tasks completed.
* **Tasks Remaining** (`tasks_remaining`, number, optional): Report the number of tasks remaining.
* **Focus Areas** (`focus_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the areas of focus for your work. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes about your work.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Report the tool assigned to you for this task. Options are 'ChatJIMMY' or 'Other'. If 'Other', enter the tool name in the 'Other' field.
* **Other Tool** (`assigned_tool_other`, text, optional): If 'Other' is selected in the 'Assigned Tool' field, enter the tool name here.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time this form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Report whether you submitted this form. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'. If 'Yes', optionally enter the name of the person who submitted it.
* **Other Submitter** (`submitted_by_other`, text, optional): If 'Other' is selected in the 'Submitted By' field, enter the name of the person who submitted it.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Report whether you are assigned to someone. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'. If 'Yes', optionally enter the name of the person assigned to you.
* **Other Assignee** (`assigned_to_other`, text, optional): If 'Other' is selected in the 'Assigned To' field, enter the name of the person assigned to you.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments about your work.
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, note, optional): Add any submission notes.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): Enter a unique ID for this form. This is optional but recommended for reference.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Report the category of this form. Options are 'Business Forms', 'Other', 'Forms', or 'General Forms'.
