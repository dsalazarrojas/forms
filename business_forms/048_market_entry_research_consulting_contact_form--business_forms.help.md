# Market Entry Research Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Entry Research Consulting Contact Form is designed to collect contact and project information from customers interested in researching market entry opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your contact information in the fields provided.
2. Describe the project you are interested in.
3. Provide details about the project status, project type, and email type.
4. Select the submit option that best suits your needs.
5. Optionally, include any additional notes about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info**:
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you.
	+ **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be used in our communication.
* **Project Info**: Describe the project you are interested in, including any relevant details.
* **Project Status**: Select the status of your project (Planning, In Progress, Completed).
* **Project Type**: Select the type of project (Software, Hardware, Service).
* **Email Type**: Select the type of email address you have (Work, Personal).
* **Submit**:
	+ **Submit**: Send your form submission directly to our team.
	+ **Save for Later**: Save your form submission for later review.
* **Date**:
	+ This field is optional and will automatically be populated with the current date.
* **Time**:
	+ This field is optional and will automatically be populated with the current time.
* **Note**:
	+ Enter any additional notes about your project.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask.
* Our team will review your submission and contact you with any further questions or concerns.
