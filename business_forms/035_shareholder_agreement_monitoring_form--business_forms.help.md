# Shareholder Agreement Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to monitor shareholder agreements, which are crucial documents that outline the terms and conditions of a business partnership or investment. Shareholder agreements specify the rights, obligations, and responsibilities of shareholders, ensuring a clear understanding among all parties involved. This form will help you collect and organize relevant information about shareholder agreements, allowing you to efficiently track and manage these agreements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the sharehold agreement in the "Sharehold Agreement Monitoring" section.
2. Provide a brief description of the sharehold agreement in the "Sharehold Agreement Details" section.
3. Select the current status of the sharehold agreement from the options provided in the "Sharehold Agreement Status" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Sharehold Agreement Monitoring** (Page 1, Field 1): Enter the title of the sharehold agreement, e.g., "Sharehold Agreement - XYZ Corporation."
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: No

- **Sharehold Agreement Details** (Page 1, Field 2): Provide a brief description of the sharehold agreement, including its key terms and conditions.
  * Type: `text`
  * Required: No

- **Sharehold Agreement Status** (Page 1, Field 3): Select the current status of the sharehold agreement from the available options:
  * Options: `Status 1`, `Status 2`, `Status 3`
  * Type: `select_multiple`
  * Required: No
