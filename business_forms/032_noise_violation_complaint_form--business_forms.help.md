# Noise Violation Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Noise Violation Complaint Form is a tool for reporting noise disturbances in your area. The form requires detailed information about the incident, including the address where the noise is occurring, a brief description of the issue, and other relevant details. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Address** where the noise disturbance is occurring.
2. Provide a brief **description** of the noise issue.
3. Select the **type of noise** you are experiencing (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, or Other).
4. Choose the **date** and **time** of the disturbance.
5. Enter your **Location** (the area where you are reporting the issue).
6. Enter your **Complainant Name** (your name or the name of the person reporting the issue).
7. Provide your **Contact Number** and **Email** so that we can contact you for further information.
8. Enter any additional information about the issue in the **Submitted By** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Address**: The address where the noise disturbance is occurring.
* **description**: A brief description of the noise issue.
* **type of noise**: The type of noise you are experiencing (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, or Other).
* **date**: The date of the disturbance.
* **time**: The time of the disturbance.
* **Location**: The area where you are reporting the issue.
* **Complainant Name**: Your name or the name of the person reporting the issue.
* **Contact Number**: Your contact information so that we can reach out to you for further information.
* **Email**: Your email address for communication purposes.
* **Submitted By**: Any additional information about the issue that you would like to provide.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible about the issue to help us investigate efficiently.
* If you are reporting on behalf of someone else, please ensure that you have their permission to do so.
* If you have already reported this issue, please do not submit duplicate reports. We will investigate and follow up on the original report.
