# iamdfs_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a user intake form used to collect necessary information from clients or users. It is intended to gather specific details that are critical for services, support, or account creation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the first page.
2. On the second page, enter the required information in the free-text field.
3. You can skip the first and second pages as they are not required, but you must complete the second page for the form to be submitted successfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **second_page**: (second_page, text, required): Enter the necessary information required for the current process.
