# Fleet Management Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fleet Management Check In Form is used to track and record key details about vehicle check out events. This form should be completed by drivers or fleet managers to provide an accurate and comprehensive record of the vehicle's status at the time of check out.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct vehicle from the options provided in the "Check Out Vehicle" field.
2. Record the current odometer reading in the "odometer reading" field.
3. Enter the current fuel level (in gallons or percentage) in the "fuel level" field.
4. Choose the correct fuel type from the options provided in the "fuel type" field.
5. Enter the amount of fuel used during the trip in the "fuel quantity" field.
6. Record the location where the vehicle was checked out in the "location" field.
7. Select the date and time of check out using the date and time fields.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the check out event in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Check Out Vehicle
* **Check Out Vehicle**: Select the vehicle that is being checked out.
### Vehicle Number
* **Vehicle Number**: Enter the unique identification number assigned to the vehicle.
### Odometer Reading
* **Odometer Reading**: Record the current odometer reading of the vehicle.
### Fuel Level
* **Fuel Level**: Enter the current fuel level of the vehicle (in gallons or percentage).
### Fuel Type
* **Fuel Type**: Choose the type of fuel used in the vehicle (e.g., gasoline, diesel, or other).
### Fuel Quantity
* **Fuel Quantity**: Record the amount of fuel used during the trip.
### Location
* **Location**: Enter the location where the vehicle was checked out.
### Check Out Date
* **Check Out Date**: Select the date of check out using the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
### Check Out Time
* **Check Out Time**: Select the time of check out in the standard time format.
### Notes
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the check out event.

## Tips
* Be sure to complete all required fields and provide as much detail as possible to ensure accurate and thorough records.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please reach out to your fleet manager for assistance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all relevant information is included.
