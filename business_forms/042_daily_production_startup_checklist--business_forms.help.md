# daily_production_startup_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the daily production startup checklist form, which is designed to facilitate a systematic and secure approach to starting up production on a daily basis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form sections and ensure you have all required and optional information ready.
2. Fill out each field, following the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **startup_time** (`startup time`, optional): Enter the time of production startup.
* **startup_date** (`startup_date`, optional): Enter the date of production startup.
* **equipment_verification** (`equipment_verification`, optional): Check the relevant equipment, such as machinery or tools, to ensure they are in good working order.
* **safety_verification** (`safety_verification`, optional): Confirm that all safety measures are in place, such as personal protective equipment (PPE) and lockout/tagout procedures, to prevent accidents.
* **startup_protocol** (`startup_protocol`, optional): Select the standard or protocol to follow for starting up production.
* **production_team** (`production_team`, optional): Enter the names and numbers of team members involved in production.
* **supervisor_name** (`supervisor_name`, optional): Enter the name and title of the supervisor responsible for the production area.
* **comments** (`comments`, optional): Leave any comments or notes about the production startup process.
* **Machinery** (equipment_verification): Check if the relevant machinery is in good working order.
* **Tools** (equipment_verification): Check if the relevant tools are in good working order.
* **Other** (equipment_verification): Check any other equipment or materials related to the production process.
* **Wear Personal Protective Equipment** (safety_verification): Confirm if PPE is being worn by production team members.
* **Use Lockout/Tagout Procedures** (safety_verification): Confirm that lockout/tagout procedures are being followed.
* **Regularly Inspect Equipment** (safety_verification): Confirm that equipment is regularly inspected.
* **Standard 1** (startup_protocol): Select Standard 1 as the startup protocol.
* **Standard 2** (startup_protocol): Select Standard 2 as the startup protocol.
* **Standard 3** (startup_protocol): Select Standard 3 as the startup protocol.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Double-check equipment and safety measures before starting up production.
* Review the startup protocol selected to confirm it aligns with company standards.
* Enter team members' names and numbers correctly.
* Provide any additional comments or notes about the production startup process.
