# Financial Forecasting Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather financial data for forecasting and auditing purposes. It is intended for finance teams to review and analyze historical and predicted financial performance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access and submit this form.
2. Select the financial forecasting period for which you are reporting.
3. Enter your forecasted revenue and actual revenue for the selected period.
4. Enter the start date of the forecasting year.
5. Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Financial Forecasting Period
*   **Financial Forecasting Period** (`financial_forecast_period`, select_one, required: false): Select the financial forecasting period from the dropdown menu. Choose from the following options: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December.

### 2. Forecasted Revenue
*   **Forecasted Revenue** (`forecasted_revenue`, number, required: false): Enter the forecasted revenue for the selected period. This should be a numerical value.

### 3. Actual Revenue
*   **Actual Revenue** (`actual_revenue`, number, required: false): Enter the actual revenue for the selected period. This should be a numerical value.

### 4. Year Forecast Start Date
*   **Year Forecast Start Date** (`year_forecast_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the forecasting year. This should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips

*   Ensure you have entered accurate and up-to-date information to ensure reliable forecasts and audits.
*   If you are unsure about any field, consult with your finance team or the form's administrator.
*   Review and submit the form carefully before finalizing it.
