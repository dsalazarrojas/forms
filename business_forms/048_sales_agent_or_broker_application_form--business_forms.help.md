To avoid duplication, we'll be using a form for Sales Agents or Brokers to apply or renew their licenses, which is a critical part of maintaining accurate records in our database. This form will be completed by the sales agents or brokers themselves, and it ensures that all required information is provided, including contact details, broker information, and license specifics, as well as agency specifics, and termination or renewal information. This form will be used for both application and renewal purposes, and it's essential that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays in the process.

# sales_agent_or_broker_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sales_agent_or_broker_application_form is a crucial document for sales agents or brokers to submit their applications or renewals. It ensures that all necessary information is provided for accurate record-keeping and compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting.
2.  Complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
3.  Review the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `select_one`, required): Choose your title (Mr, Ms, Other) to indicate your contact title.
* **Contact Address** (`Contact Address`, `text`, required): Enter your contact address for correspondence and communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number for communication.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address for communication and verification.
* **Broker Information** (`Broker Information`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of yourself and your business.
* **License Number** (`License Number`, `text`, required): Enter your sales agent or broker license number.
* **Broker Experience** (`Broker Experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have been working as a sales agent or broker.
* **Broker Specialty** (`Broker Specialty`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your area of specialty (Commercial, Residential, Both).
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Date of Application** (`Date of Application`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting this application.
* **Broker Licenses** (`Broker Licenses`, `text`, required): Enter the type of licenses you hold.
* **Broker Status** (`Broker Status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current status (Active, Inactive, Suspended).
* **Additional Licenses** (`Additional Licenses`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose any additional licenses you hold (Series 3, Series 4, Series 6).
* **Broker Agency** (`Broker Agency`, `text`, required): Enter your broker agency name.
* **Broker Agency Status** (`Broker Agency Status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your agency status (Active, Inactive, New).
* **Broker Agency Type** (`Broker Agency Type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your agency type (Individual, Partnership, Corporation).
* **Broker Termination Date** (`Broker Termination Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date your agency was terminated.
* **Broker Renewal Date** (`Broker Renewal Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date your agency was renewed.
* **Broker Termination Reason** (`Broker Termination Reason`, `text`, required): Enter the reason for termination.
* **Additional Documentation** (`Additional Documentation`, `note`, required): Provide any additional documentation as required.
* **Broker Agency Address** (`Broker Agency Address`, `text`, required): Enter your agency address.
* **Broker Agency Phone** (`Broker Agency Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your agency phone number.
* **Broker Agency Email** (`Broker Agency Email`, `email`, required): Enter your agency email address.
* **Broker Agency License Number** (`Broker Agency License Number`, `text`, required): Enter your agency license number.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form for errors before submitting.
* Contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
