# Skincare_Business_Collaboration_Application_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Business Collaboration Application Form is designed to facilitate collaboration and partnerships between businesses operating in the skincare industry. This form collects information about businesses, their operations, and contact details to establish connections and facilitate networking.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the form title and category, which should accurately reflect your business's name and what type of business it is.
2. Provide a brief description of your business, including its purpose and services offered.
3. Enter your business name, which should match the name you've used for your business title.
4. Add your website URL, if your business has one.
5. Enter your social media handles for Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter.
6. Include your phone number, so potential collaborators can easily get in touch with you.
7. Enter your email address, which will be used for communication with your business.
8. Provide your business address, which can help with mail or delivery coordination.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about your business.
10. Select the type of business you operate, such as Skincare Professional or Skincare Business.
11. Choose how you prefer to be contacted (Local, Online, or Both).
12. Enter the location of your business, which could be a physical address or a virtual one.
13. Enter the venue where your business operates, if applicable.
14. Select the start and end dates of your business operations, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skincare Business Collaboration Application Form** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which should match your business's name.
* **Category** (`form_category`, text, required/optional): Select the category that best describes your business's operation, for example, Skincare Professional or Skincare Business.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe your business, including its purpose and services offered.
* **Business Name** (`form_business_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your business's name, which should match the name you've used for your business title.
* **Website** (`form_website`, text, required/optional): Add your business's website URL, if you have one.
* **Facebook** (`form_facebook`, text, required/optional): Enter your Facebook social media handle, if you have one.
* **Instagram** (`form_instagram`, text, required/optional): Enter your Instagram social media handle, if you have one.
* **Twitter** (`form_twitter`, text, required/optional): Enter your Twitter social media handle, if you have one.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, required/optional): Include your phone number, so potential collaborators can get in touch with you.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication with your business.
* **Address** (`form_address`, text, required/optional): Provide your business address, which can help with mail or delivery coordination.
* **Notes** (`form_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your business.
* **Business Type** (`form_business_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of business you operate, such as Skincare Professional or Skincare Business.
* **Partnership Type** (`form_partnership_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose how you prefer to be contacted, such as Local, Online, or Both.
* **Location** (`form_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of your business, which could be a physical address or a virtual one.
* **Venue** (`form_venue`, text, required/optional): Enter the venue where your business operates, if applicable.
* **Start Date** (`form_start_date`, date, required/optional): Select the start date of your business operations, if applicable.
* **End Date** (`form_end_date`, date, required/optional): Select the end date of your business operations, if applicable.
