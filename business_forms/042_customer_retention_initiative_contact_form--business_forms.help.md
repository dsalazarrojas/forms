# Customer Retention Initiative Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Retention Initiative Contact Form is designed to collect feedback and information from customers on their experience with the company's products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide specific details about your experience with the company, including any issues you faced and how you would like to see them addressed.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
• **First name** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.

### Last Name
• **Last name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.

### Email
• **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.

### Phone
• **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.

### Feedback
• **Feedback** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the reason for your contact.

### Reason Why
• **Reason why** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the reason why you are contacting us. Options: Not Satisfied with Service Quality, Not Satisfied with Product Quality, Not Satisfied with Communication, Not Satisfied with Support, Other (please specify).

### Agree to Follow Up
• **Agree to follow up** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you agree to follow up on your contact.

### Consent
• **Consent** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you consent to follow up on your contact.

### Notes
• **Notes** (`9`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments about your experience.

### Category
• **Category** (`10`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the department that handled your issue. Options: Sales, Marketing, Support, Finance.

### Department
• **Department** (`11`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you were assigned to a department. Options: Yes, No.

### Team
• **Team** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the team members involved in your issue. Options: John, Mary, David, Jane, Joe.

### Date
• **Date** (`13`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your contact.

### Time
• **Time** (`14`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your contact.

### Customer Type
• **Customer type** (`15`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you are a customer. Options: Yes, No.

### Customer ID
• **Customer ID** (`16`, `number`, required: false): Enter your customer ID.

### Customer Name
• **Customer name** (`17`, `text`, required: false): Enter your customer name.

### Customer Email
• **Customer email** (`18`, `email`, required: false): Enter your customer email.

### Customer Phone
• **Customer phone** (`19`, `text`, required: false): Enter your customer phone number.

### Customer Notes
• **Customer notes** (`20`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments about your customer.

### Sales Notes
• **Sales notes** (`21`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional sales notes or comments about your customer.

### Category 2
• **Category 2** (`22`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any additional categories. Options: Category 2, Category 3, Category 4.

### Team 2
• **Team 2** (`23`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any additional team members. Options: Team 2, Team 3, Team 4.

### Customer Notes 2
• **Customer notes 2** (`24`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional customer notes.

### Sales Notes 2
• **Sales notes 2** (`25`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional sales notes.

Please note that some fields are optional and some options may not apply to your situation. Answer all questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible to ensure accurate feedback and information collection.
