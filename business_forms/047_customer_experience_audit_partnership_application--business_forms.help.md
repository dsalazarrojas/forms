This form is used to collect information from potential partners who wish to form a partnership with the company. The purpose of the form is to gather details about the partner's experience, expertise, and qualifications, which will be used to evaluate their suitability for the partnership.

The form has several sections, each with its own set of fields, which are explained below.

# customer_experience_audit_partnership_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Customer Experience Audit Partnership Application** form is designed to help us assess a potential partner's experience and suitability for a partnership. Please complete this form to provide us with the necessary information to evaluate your partnership potential.
## How To Complete This Form
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
- Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly, to the best of your ability.
- Once you've completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it for review.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, *): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, *): Enter your company or organization email address.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, optional): Enter your company or organization phone number.
* **Experience Years** (`experience_years`, number, *): Enter the number of years you have experience in your field.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, *): Select one of the following experience levels:
	+ Level 1
	+ Level 2
	+ Level 3
* **Experience Description** (`experience_description`, text, *): Provide a detailed description of your experience and qualifications.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Attach any relevant supporting documents, such as certificates or licenses.
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, text, *): Click this button to submit your completed form for review.
## Tips
- Make sure to provide clear and concise answers to the questions.
- Attach any relevant supporting documents, if applicable.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us for further guidance.
