# Credit Check Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Credit Check Permission Form is used to collect necessary information from individuals for a credit check. It will gather contact details, employment information, and permission to conduct a credit check.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields: [**Contact Person**, **Company**, **Address**]
2. Provide your **Contact Number**, **Email**, and **Date of Birth**.
3. Select your **Job Title**.
4. Choose whether you grant **Permission** to conduct a credit check.
5. Add any **Notes** you'd like to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person**: The name of the person granting the permission.
* **Contact Number**: A valid contact number for the person granting the permission.
* **Company**: The company the person is associated with or works for.
* **Job Title**: The person's job title within the company.
* **Email**: A valid email address for the person granting the permission.
* **Date of Birth**: The person's date of birth.
* **Address**: The person's business or residential address.
* **Permission**: Check "True" if you grant permission for a credit check, or "False" otherwise.
* **Notes**: Any additional information or comments about the credit check.
