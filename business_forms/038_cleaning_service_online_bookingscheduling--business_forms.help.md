# Cleaning Service Online BookingScheduling - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to guide users through the process of booking cleaning services for their properties. The form is designed to collect essential information to provide a personalized and efficient cleaning service. It covers various aspects, including contact details, property information, service selection, scheduling, and payment options.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately and efficiently, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out your **Contact Information** section, which includes your name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the **Property Information** section, choose the type of property you have and its size. If applicable, select the number of bedrooms and bathrooms you have.
3. Next, select the **Type of Cleaning** you need and the **Service Frequency** (how often you want the cleaning service).
4. Choose any **Additional Services** you'd like to include, such as carpet cleaning or window cleaning.
5. Schedule your **Preferred Date** and **Preferred Time** for the cleaning service.
6. Select your preferred **Day of Week** for the service.
7. If applicable, indicate if you have **Pets in the Home** and provide any **Special Instructions** for the cleaning team.
8. Specify how you'd like to **Access the Property** and where to **Park** the service team.
9. Choose a **Preferred Payment Method** from the available options.
10. Finally, confirm the **Terms and Conditions** and **Marketing Communications** consent, and sign your name electronically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Your contact email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number where we can call or text you.
* **Property Type**: The type of property you have (residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Property Size**: The approximate square footage of your property.
* **Number of Bedrooms**: The number of bedrooms you have (if applicable).
* **Number of Bathrooms**: The number of bathrooms you have (if applicable).
* **Service Address**: The full address of the property where the service will be performed.
* **City**: The city or town where the service will be performed.
* **ZIP Code**: The postal code of the service location.
* **Preferred Date**: The date when you would like the cleaning service to be performed.
* **Preferred Time**: The preferred time of day for the cleaning service.
* **Preferred Day of Week**: The day of the week when you'd like the cleaning service.
* **Pets in Home**: Indicate if you have any pets in the property.
* **Special Instructions**: Any special instructions or requests for the cleaning team.
* **Allergies or Sensitivities**: Any allergies or sensitivities we should be aware of.
* **Access Instructions**: How the service team should access your property.
* **Parking Information**: Where the service team should park.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: How you'd like to pay for the service (credit card, debit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: How you found our services.
* **Terms and Conditions Acceptance**: Confirm you agree to our terms of service.
* **Marketing Communications Consent**: Consent to receive marketing communications from us.
* **Customer Signature**: Your electronic signature confirming your booking.

Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure we provide the best possible service for your property. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
