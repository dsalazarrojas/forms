# port_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The port_incident_form is a structured form for reporting and documenting incidents that occur within a business or organization. Its purpose is to collect and track critical information about the incident, including date, time, location, summary, cause, effects, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief description of the incident in the "Incident Summary" field.
2. Enter the date the incident occurred in the "Incident Date" field.
3. Enter the time the incident occurred in the "Incident Time" field.
4. If applicable, enter the location where the incident occurred in the "Incident Location" field.
5. Describe what happened in the "What Happened" field.
6. If relevant, provide a brief description of the incident's cause in the "Incident Cause" field.
7. Describe the effects of the incident in the "Effects" field.
8. Indicate the current status of the incident in the "Status" field.
9. Select the type of service that responded to the incident in the "Responded" field (if applicable).
10. Add any additional comments about the incident in the "Comments" field (if applicable).
11. Select the person who reported the incident in the "Reported By" field.
12. Select the role of the person who reported the incident in the "Reported By Role" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Summary**: Enter a brief description of the incident (required).
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred (required).
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred (required).
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred (optional).
* **What Happened**: Provide a detailed description of the incident (required).
* **Incident Cause**: Enter a brief description of the incident's cause (optional).
* **Effects**: Describe the effects of the incident (optional).
* **Status**: Indicate the current status of the incident (e.g., Open, Closed) (required).
* **Responded**: Select the type of service that responded to the incident (e.g., Emergency Services, Logistics Services, Security Services) (optional).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the incident (optional).
* **Reported By**: Select the person who reported the incident.
* **Reported By Role**: Select the role of the person who reported the incident.
