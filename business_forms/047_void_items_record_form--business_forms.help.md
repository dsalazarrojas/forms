# void_items_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the reasons for voiding items in a business setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Complete the "customer" field by selecting the appropriate option for whether the item was voided at the customer's request.
2.  Choose a reason for voiding the item from the "reason_for_return" field.
3.  If necessary, provide additional details in the "actions_taken" field.
4.  On the second page, you can add any additional notes or comments about the item being voided in the "item_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **customer** (`customer`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select whether the item was voided at the customer's request.
*   **reason_for_return** (`reason_for_return`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose a reason for voiding the item from the provided list.
*   **actions_taken** (`actions_taken`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional details on actions taken when voiding the item.
*   **item_details** (`item_details`, `note`, required/optional): A note field for any additional details or comments about the item being voided.
