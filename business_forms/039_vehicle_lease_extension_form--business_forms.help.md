# vehicle_lease_extension_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The vehicle lease extension form is designed to collect information necessary for extending the lease of a vehicle. It is meant to be used by lessees and leasing companies to document and confirm agreement on lease terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **vehicle_lease_info** field with your vehicle's details (make, model, and year).
2. Provide your **vehicle_owner_info** (your name and contact information).
3. Select the **vehicle_lease_terms** field to indicate whether you agree to the lease extension (Yes/No).
4. Enter the **lease_extension_details** field with any additional information about the lease extension.
5. Select your desired **lease_extension_terms** (Yes/No).
6. Add any **user_comments** you have regarding the lease extension.
7. Enter the **leasing_company_details** field with any information about the leasing company.
8. Sign the **signature** field to confirm your agreement.
9. Finally, select the **lease_end_date_extension** field to indicate the new lease end date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vehicle_lease_info** (Text): Enter your vehicle's details such as make, model, and year.
* **vehicle_owner_info** (Text): Provide your name and contact information as the vehicle's owner or lessee.
* **vehicle_lease_terms** (Select Multiple): Check "Yes" to agree to the lease extension, or "No" to decline.
* **lease_extension_details** (Text): Enter any additional information about the lease extension, such as the reason for extension or any specific conditions.
* **lease_extension_terms** (Select Multiple): Check "Yes" to agree to the lease extension terms, or "No" to decline.
* **user_comments** (Text): Add any comments or notes about the lease extension.
* **leasing_company_details** (Text): Enter information about the leasing company, such as their contact details.
* **signature** (Text): Sign the form to confirm your agreement to the lease extension.
* **lease_end_date_extension** (Date): Select the new lease end date.
