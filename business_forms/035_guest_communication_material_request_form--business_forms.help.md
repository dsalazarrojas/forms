# Guest Communication Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Communication Material Request Form is designed to allow guests to request communication materials such as brochures, newsletters, or other materials related to their visit. This form helps our staff prepare and provide the necessary materials for an enhanced guest experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information, including your name, contact information, and any other details requested.
2. Choose the materials you are interested in requesting (e.g., 'Yes' for Yes, 'No' for No).
3. Upload any supporting files or documents required for your request.
4. Enter a brief message with any additional comments or questions you may have.
5. Submit the form to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Information** (Text, Required: False)
	+ Enter your name and any other relevant personal information.
* **Material Information** (Text, Required: False)
	+ Describe the materials you are interested in, or any specific materials you would like to request.
* **Request Reason** (Text, Required: False)
	+ Provide a brief reason for your request, if applicable.
* **Requested Date** (Date, Required: False)
	+ Specify a date for when you would like to receive the requested materials.
* **Material Requested** (Select One, Required: False)
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you are requesting materials, 'No' otherwise.
* **Contact Information** (Text, Required: False)
	+ Enter your contact information, such as phone number or email address.
* **Email** (Email, Required: False)
	+ Enter your email address where we can reach you.
* **Message** (Note, Required: False)
	+ Type in any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **File Upload** (Text, Required: False)
	+ Upload any supporting files or documents required for your request.
