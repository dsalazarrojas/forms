# Service Proposal Value Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the value of a proposed service. It collects information on the benefits, costs, and overall value of the service, as well as feedback from stakeholders and decision-makers. The assessment helps to determine the feasibility and potential impact of the service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the proposed service in the "Proposed Service" field.
2. List the key benefits of the service in the "Capture Benefits" field.
3. List the key costs associated with the service in the "Capture Costs" field.
4. Enter the overall value of the service (e.g., monetary value or intangible benefits) in the "Overall Value" field.
5. Rate the quality of the proposed service using the scale "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" in the "Rate" field.
6. Identify the stakeholders involved with the service by selecting one or more options in the "Review Service" field.
7. Identify the stakeholders expected to be impacted by the service by selecting one or more options in the "Expected Stakeholders" field.
8. Record the date when the review decision was made in the "Review Decision" field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about the service in the "Note" field (optional).
10. Provide your email address so that we can contact you regarding this assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Proposed Service (Text)**: A brief description of the proposed service.
* **Capture Benefits (Text)**: List the key benefits of the service, including any intangible or monetary benefits.
* **Capture Costs (Text)**: List the key costs associated with the service, including any financial or resource costs.
* **Overall Value (Number)**: The overall value of the service, expressed in monetary units or as a numerical value (e.g., intangible benefits).
* **Rate (Select One)**: Rate the quality of the proposed service using the scale: "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Review Service (Select Multiple)**: Identify the stakeholders involved with the service by selecting one or more options.
* **Capture Stakeholders (Text)**: List the stakeholders captured with the service.
* **Expected Stakeholders (Select Multiple)**: Identify the stakeholders expected to be impacted by the service by selecting one or more options.
* **Review Decision (Date)**: Record the date when the review decision was made.
* **Note (Note)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the service (optional).
* **Email (Email)**: Provide your email address so that we can contact you regarding this assessment.
* **Phone (Text)**: Record your phone number for communication purposes.
