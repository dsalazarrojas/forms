# Quality Control Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Control Checklist Form is a tool used for tracking quality control processes and measures in production and manufacturing environments. It helps ensure that quality control measures are in place and that corrective actions are taken when issues arise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general questions section with any relevant information about the production process.
2. Describe the production process in the production process field.
3. Enter the date of the production process in the production process date field.
4. Specify any corrective actions taken in the corrective action field.
5. Enter the date of the corrective action in the corrective action date field.
6. Select from the quality control measures options which measures are in place.
7. Enter any notes about quality control measures in the quality control measures notes field.
8. Enter the number goals for each goal field (e.g. 10, 20, 30, etc.).
