# Corporate Merger Announcement RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees of a corporate merger announcement, allowing us to plan and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your information is included in our records.
2. Select "Yes" if you will be attending the event, and "No" if you will not.
3. If you are bringing guests, enter the number of guests you plan to bring.
4. Enter the names and email addresses of your guests (if applicable).
5. If you have any questions about the merger, you can enter them in the "Event Questions" field.
6. Enter any comments or messages you'd like to share with us in the "Message" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Company (Required)** (`text`, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Name (Required)** (`text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Role (Required)** (`text`, required): Enter your role within the company.
* **Will you be attending? (Required)** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you can attend, and "No" if you cannot.
* **Number of guests (Optional)** (`number`, required: false): Enter the number of guests you plan to bring, if any.
* **Guest Name (Optional)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the names of your guests, separated by commas (e.g., "John, Mary, Jane").
* **Guest Email (Optional)** (`email`, required: false): Enter the email addresses of your guests, separated by commas (e.g., "john@example.com, mary@example.com, jane@example.com").
* **Event Questions (Optional)** (`text`, required: false): Ask us any questions you may have about the merger.
* **Message (Optional)** (`note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or messages you'd like to share with us.
