# Waste Transfer Docket Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waste Transfer Docket Form is used to record the details of a waste transfer, including the generator information, transfer details, and receiver information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the generator information, including the generator name and any other relevant details.
* Enter the transfer date and time.
* Specify the transfer amount.
* Select the transfer type (Liquid, Dry, or Other).
* Add any notes or comments.
* Enter the receiver information, including their name and any other relevant details.
* Provide a signature to confirm the transfer.
* Enter the date the form was submitted.
* Enter the transporter information, including their name and any other relevant details.
* Select the receiver type (Generator, Transferer, or Receiver).
* Specify the waste type (Liquid or Dry).
* Enter a description of the waste being transferred.
* Enter the weight of the waste being transferred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Generator Information** (<generator>):
	+ This field is used to capture the name and other relevant details of the generator who is sending the waste.
* **Transfer Date** (<transfer_date>):
	+ This field is used to enter the date of the waste transfer.
* **Transfer Time** (<transfer_time>):
	+ This field is used to enter the time of the waste transfer.
* **Transfer Amount** (<transfer_amount>):
	+ This field is used to enter the amount of waste transferred.
* **Transfer Type** (<transfer_type>):
	+ This field is used to select the type of waste being transferred (Liquid, Dry, or Other).
* **Notes** (<notes>):
	+ This field is used to add any additional comments or notes about the transfer.
* **Receiver Information** (<receiver_info>):
	+ This field is used to capture the name and other relevant details of the receiver.
* **Signature** (<signature>):
	+ This field is used to obtain a signature from the transferer to confirm the transfer.
* **Date Submitter** (<date_submitted>):
	+ This field is used to enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Transporter** (<transporter>):
	+ This field is used to enter the name and other relevant details of the transporter.
* **Receiver** (<receiver>):
	+ This field is used to select the type of receiver (Generator, Transferer, or Receiver).
* **Waste Type** (<waste_type>):
	+ This field is used to select the type of waste being transferred (Liquid or Dry).
* **Waste Description** (<waste_description>):
	+ This field is used to enter a description of the waste being transferred.
* **Transfer Weight** (<transfer_weight>):
	+ This field is used to enter the weight of the waste being transferred.
