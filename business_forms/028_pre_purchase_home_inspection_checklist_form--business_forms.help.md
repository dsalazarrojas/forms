# Pre Purchase Home Inspection Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Purchase Home Inspection Checklist Form is a comprehensive form used to collect crucial information about a property before a purchase. This form helps buyers make informed decisions by providing a detailed overview of the property's condition, features, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the property's address in the "Property Address" field.
2. Select the type of property from the "Type of Property" dropdown.
3. Choose the condition of the property from the "Condition of Property" dropdown.
4. Enter the buyer's first name, last name, phone number, and email in the "Home Buyer Info" field.
5. Select the type of roof from the "Roof Type" dropdown.
6. Choose the materials used for the exterior walls in the "Exterior Wall Materials" field.
7. Select the type of foundation from the "Foundation Type" field.
8. Enter the property's lot size in the "Lot Size" field (if applicable).
9. Enter the age of the property in the "Age of Home" field (if applicable).
10. Select the status of the property from the "Status of Property" dropdown.
11. Choose the buyer's agreement type from the "Home Buyer Agreement" dropdown.
12. Enter the inspection fee in the "Inspection Fee" field.
13. Select whether the inspection fee is included in the purchase price or not in the "Inspection Fee Included" field.
14. Enter the buyer's contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
15. Enter the property's neighborhood or area in the "Property Neighborhood" field.
16. Enter the property's zip code in the "Property Zipcode" field.
17. Select the type of home from the "Type of Home" dropdown.
18. Choose the style of home from the "Style of Home" dropdown.
19. Enter the number of bedrooms and bathrooms in the "Number of Bedrooms" and "Number of Bathrooms" fields respectively.
20. Enter the property's square footage in the "Square Footage" field.
21. Enter the year the home was built in the "Year Built" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address (1)** (`text`, `Property Address`, Required): Enter the property's address in the required format.
* **Type of Property (4)** (`select_one`, `Type of Property`, Required): Select the type of property, such as Single Family, Townhouse, or Condo.
* **Condition of Property (6)** (`select_one`, `Condition of Property`, Required): Choose the condition of the property, such as Excellent, Fair, or Poor.
* **Roof Type (7)** (`select_one`, `Roof Type`, Optional): Select the type of roof, such as Sloping, Flat, or Gable.
* **Exterior Wall Materials (8)** (`select_multiple`, `Exterior Wall Materials`, Optional): Choose the materials used for the exterior walls, such as Brick, Vinyl Siding, or Wood.
* **Foundation Type (9)** (`select_multiple`, `Foundation Type`, Optional): Select the type of foundation, such as Slab, Basement, or Crawl Space.
* **Lot Size (10)** (`number`, `Lot Size`, Optional): Enter the property's lot size, if applicable.
* **Age of Home (11)** (`number`, `Age of Home`, Optional): Enter the age of the property, if applicable.
* **Status of Property (12)** (`select_one`, `Status of Property`, Required): Select the status of the property, such as For Sale, Leased, Rented, or Owned.
* **Home Buyer Info (3)** (`select_multiple`, `Home Buyer Info`, Required): Enter the buyer's first name, last name, phone number, and email in the required format.
* **Home Buyer Agreement (13)** (`select_one`, `Home Buyer Agreement`, Required): Choose the buyer's agreement type, such as Buy or Rent.
* **Inspection Fee (14)** (`number`, `Inspection Fee`, Required): Enter the inspection fee.
* **Inspection Fee Included (15)** (`select_one`, `Inspection Fee Included`, Required): Select whether the inspection fee is included in the purchase price or not.
* **Contact Info (16)** (`email`, `Contact Info`, Required): Enter the buyer's contact information.
* **Property Neighborhood (18)** (`text`, `Property Neighborhood`, Required): Enter the property's neighborhood or area.
* **Property Zipcode (19)** (`text`, `Property Zipcode`, Required): Enter the property's zip code.
* **Type of Home (20)** (`select_one`, `Type of Home`, Required): Select the type of home, such as Single Family, Townhouse, or Condo.
* **Style of Home (21)** (`select_multiple`, `Style of Home`, Required): Choose the style of home, such as Single Level or Multi Level.
* **Number of Bedrooms (22)** (`number`, `Number of Bedrooms`, Required): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **Number of Bathrooms (23)** (`number`, `Number of Bathrooms`, Required): Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
* **Square Footage (24)** (`number`, `Square Footage`, Required): Enter the property's square footage.
* **Year Built (25)** (`number`, `Year Built`, Required): Enter the year the home was built.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* The property address should match the property's actual address.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
* This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for a thorough home inspection.
