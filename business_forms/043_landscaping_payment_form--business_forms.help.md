# Landscaping Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about a payment made for landscaping services. Please fill out this form only if a payment has been made.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, click the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
- Fill out the required field "Client Name", which is the name of the client making the payment.
- Optionally, select "Yes" or "No" from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu, indicating the method of payment (e.g. check, credit card, etc.).
- Enter the "Payment Amount", which is the amount paid for the services rendered. This field is required.
- Select the "Payment Date" and "Payment Time" from the calendar and time picker, respectively, if relevant.
- Optionally, add a "Payment Note", providing any additional information about the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Payment Method**: Indicates the method of payment (e.g. check, credit card, etc.). Select "Yes" if you are making a payment.
  - **Client Name**: The name of the client making the payment. This field is required.
  - **Payment Amount**: The amount paid for the services rendered. This field is required.
  - **Payment Date**: The date the payment was made. If left blank, the current date will be used.
  - **Payment Time**: The time the payment was made. If left blank, the current time will be used.
  - **Payment Note**: Optional notes about the payment, e.g. payment method, payment reference, etc.
