# Non Disclosure Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect non-disclosure declaration information from individuals or entities that need to access sensitive or confidential information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with accurate and truthful information. You will need to provide your:

* First Name (optional)
* Last Name (optional)
* Email (optional)
* Phone (optional)
* Non-Disclosure Agreement (optional, select one of the provided options)
* Date Signed (optional)
* Date Received (optional)
* Time Signed (optional)
* Time Received (optional)
* Non-Disclosure Declaration (optional, provide a brief explanation)
* I Agree (optional, select one or more options)
* Comments (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Non-Disclosure Agreement**: Choose one of the provided options to declare your level of non-disclosure.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed this declaration (if required).
* **Date Received**: Enter the date you received this declaration (if required).
* **Time Signed**: Enter the time you signed this declaration (if required).
* **Time Received**: Enter the time you received this declaration (if required).
* **Non-Disclosure Declaration**: Briefly explain your understanding of the non-disclosure declaration (if required).
* **I Agree**: Select "I Agree" to confirm you understand the non-disclosure declaration, "I Disagree" to indicate you do not understand, and "I Do Not Understand" to request further clarification.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information you think is relevant to this declaration. 

## Tips
* Be truthful and accurate when filling out this form.
* Ensure that you have not submitted a similar declaration or agreement previously.
* Review and understand the non-disclosure declaration before signing.
* Keep a copy of this declaration for your records.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the contact person responsible for this form.
