# self_employment_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The self-employment verification form is designed to collect information about your self-employment status for official purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Click on the "self_employment_verification_form_page" link, which will take you to the first page of the form.
2. On the first page, please enter your self-employment status in the "self_employment_verification" field.
3. Proceed to the second page by clicking "Next" or navigating through the form's pages.
4. On the second page, select your employment status from the options provided in the "self_employment_status" field.
5. On the third page, enter the verification date in the "verification_date" field.
6. On the fourth and final page, you can attach or upload any relevant verification documents in the "verification_document" field.
7. Once you have completed all the pages, click "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **self_employment_verification** (`self_employment_verification_form_page`, text, optional): Enter your self-employment status in this text field. Be as specific as possible, as it will be used for official purposes.
* **self_employment_status** (`self_employment_verification_form_page_2`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided that best describes your current employment status.
* **verification_date** (`self_employment_verification_form_page_3`, date, optional): Enter the date related to your self-employment verification process.
* **verification_document** (`self_employment_verification_form_page_4`, note, optional): If necessary, attach or upload any relevant verification documents related to your self-employment status.

## Tips

- Be accurate and honest when filling out this form to ensure a smooth and efficient verification process.
- Ensure that you have all necessary information and documents before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field or section, feel free to seek guidance from the relevant authorities or the form creators.
