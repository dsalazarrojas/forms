# corporate_innovation_leadership_program_application - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect data from applicants for the Corporate Innovation Leadership Program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form. This includes:
	* Your first name
	* Last name
	* A valid email address (if you don't have an email address, you can leave this field blank)
	* Your phone number (if you don't have a phone number, you can leave this field blank)
	* How many years of experience you have in your field (if you don't have experience, you can enter 0)
	* Your motivation for applying to the program
	* Your job category (Innovation Consultant, Project Manager, or Operations Manager)
	* The skills you bring to the program (check all that apply)
	* Date (if applicable)
	* Time (if applicable)
	* Any additional comments you'd like to make about your application
	* Your preferred tool to be assigned (if applicable)
	* Output files (if applicable)
	* Description (if applicable)
	* Category (if applicable)
	* Form ID (unique identifier for your application)
2. Fill out each field carefully, as it may impact how your application is processed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): Enter a valid email address, if you have one. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you have one. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Experience in Years** (5, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have in your field. If you don't have experience, enter 0.
* **Motivation** (6, text, optional): Enter your motivation for applying to the program.
* **Job Category** (7, select_one, required): Select one of the following: Innovation Consultant, Project Manager, or Operations Manager.
* **Skills** (8, select_multiple, required): Select all the skills that apply to your experience and qualifications.
* **Date** (9, date, optional): Enter the date (if applicable).
* **Time** (10, time, optional): Enter the time (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (11, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about your application.
* **Assigned Tool** (12, text, optional): Enter your preferred tool to be assigned.
* **Output File** (13, text, optional): Enter the name of a file to be outputted.
* **Description** (14, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your background and qualifications.
* **Category** (15, text, optional): Enter a category for your application.
* **Form ID** (16, text, required): Enter a unique identifier for your application.
* **Output File 2** (17, text, optional): Enter the name of another file to be outputted.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (18, text, optional): Enter another preferred tool to be assigned.
* **Created By** (19, text, optional): Enter who created this application (if applicable).
* **Created At** (20, date, optional): Enter the date this application was created.
* **Updated At** (21, date, optional): Enter the date this application was updated.
* **Form IDs** (22, text, optional): Enter unique identifiers for this application.
* **Output File 3** (23, text, optional): Enter the name of another file to be outputted.
* **Description 2** (24, text, optional): Enter another brief description of your background and qualifications.
* **Category 2** (25, text, optional): Enter another category for your application.
