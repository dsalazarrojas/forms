# Beauty Product Innovation Grant Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beauty Product Innovation Grant Application is designed for businesses or individuals seeking funding for their beauty product innovation projects. This form collects information about the applicant, project details, product innovation, financial information, and team and expertise.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the correct type of applicant (Individual, Startup, Small Business, Research Institution, or Other).
2. Fill out the required fields for Applicant Information, including Full Name or Business Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Company or Project Name.
3. In the Company or Project Details section, provide accurate information about your company or project location and year founded or project started.
4. In the Product Innovation section, describe your product category, product description, innovative aspects, and what problem it solves.
5. In the Development Stage section, select the current development stage of your project.
6. In the Financial Information section, provide the grant amount requested and total project budget.
7. In the Team and Expertise section, describe your team size and key qualifications.
8. In the Expected Impact section, describe the expected market impact and sustainability or social impact.
9. Finally, agree to the grant terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name or Business Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the applicant or business applying for the grant.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): This is the contact email address of the applicant or business.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): This is the contact phone number of the applicant or business.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of applicant (Individual, Startup, Small Business, Research Institution, or Other).
* **Company or Project Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the company or project applying for the grant.
* **Company Location** (`company_location`, `text`, required): This is the location of the company or project.
* **Year Founded or Project Started** (`founding_year`, `number`, required): This is the year the company or project was founded or started.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, `text`, required): This is the category of the product being innovated (Skincare, makeup, haircare, fragrance, wellness, etc.).
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required): This is a description of the product innovation.
* **Innovative Aspects** (`innovation_aspects`, `text`, required): Describe the innovative aspects of the product (formula, ingredients, technology, delivery system, packaging, etc.).
* **What Problem Does This Solve** (`problem_solved`, `text`, required): Describe the problem the product innovation solves.
* **Target Market** (`target_market`, `text`, required): Describe the target market and demographics for the product.
* **Current Development Stage** (`development_stage`, `select_one`, required): Select the current development stage of the project (Concept, Research, Prototype, Pre-Launch, Launch-Ready).
* **Timeline to Market** (`timeline_to_market`, `text`, required): Describe the timeline to market for the product.
* **Grant Amount Requested** (`grant_amount_requested`, `number`, required): This is the grant amount requested by the applicant or business.
* **Total Project Budget** (`total_project_budget`, `number`, required): This is the total project budget.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, `number`, required): This is the size of the team.
* **Key Team Qualifications** (`key_qualifications`, `text`, required): Describe the key qualifications of the team.
* **Expected Market Impact** (`market_impact`, `text`, required): Describe the expected market impact of the product.
* **Sustainability or Social Impact** (`sustainability_impact`, `text`, required): Describe the sustainability or social impact of the product.
* **I Agree to Grant Terms and Conditions** (`agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to agree to the grant terms and conditions.
