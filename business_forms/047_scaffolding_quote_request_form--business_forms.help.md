# scaffolding_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **Scaffolding Quote Request Form** is designed for customers to request scaffolding services from a construction company. This form helps gather necessary information about the customer, their contact details, project specifics, and other relevant details to provide an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information, including your name and address.
2. Share your contact details, such as phone number and email address.
3. Describe the project you are interested in, including any specific details or requirements.
4. Specify if scaffolding is required and what type of scaffolding is needed.
5. Add any additional details about the quote request.
6. If applicable, sign the form as the client to confirm your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional):
    This section is for you to enter your name and address as the customer.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
    Enter your phone number and email address so we can contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional):
    Describe the project you are requesting scaffolding services for, including any specific details or requirements.
* **Scaffolding Required** (`scaffolding_required`, text, optional):
    Specify if scaffolding is needed and what type of scaffolding is required (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Scaffolding Quote Details** (`scaffolding_quote_details`, text, optional):
    Add any additional information you want to include about the quote request.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, note, optional):
    If applicable, sign this form as the client to confirm your request.
