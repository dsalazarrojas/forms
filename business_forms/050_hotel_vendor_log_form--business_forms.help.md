# Hotel Vendor Log Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hotel Vendor Log Form is designed to collect information about hotel vendors. This includes details about the vendor, the services they provide, payment information, contact details, and any other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate through the form pages using the tabs provided.
2. Fill out each page with the corresponding information:
	* **Main**: Enter any general information about the vendor.
	* **Vendor Details**: Provide specific details about the vendor, such as their name, address, phone number, and other relevant information.
	* **Services**: List the services provided by the vendor.
	* **Payment**: Enter payment information for the vendor.
	* **Contact**: Enter contact information for the vendor, such as their email and phone number.
	* **Other**: Provide any additional information about the vendor that does not fit in the other categories.
	* **Terms**: Agree to any terms and conditions specified by the hotel.
3. Review and submit the form to log the vendor's information.
