# Directors And Officers Insurance Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a company's directors and officers insurance policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for the "Company Info" field.
2. Provide personal details of the directors and officers in the "Director Details" and "Officer Details" fields.
3. Select the correct option for the "Officer Position" field.
4. Select the type of coverage the company has in the "Coverage Type" field.
5. Provide the start and end dates of the coverage in the "Coverage Start" and "Coverage End" fields.
6. Provide information about the underwriter in the "Underwriter Details" and "Underwriter Info" fields.
7. Provide the client's company and address information in the "Client Name", "Client Company", "Client Address", "Client City", "Client State", and "Client Country" fields.
8. Provide the director and officer's name, address, phone number, and email in the "Director Name", "Director Address", "Director Phone", "Director Email", "Officer Name", "Officer Address", "Officer Phone", "Officer Email" fields.
9. Provide the director and officer's date of birth in the "Director Date of Birth" and "Officer Date of Birth" fields.
