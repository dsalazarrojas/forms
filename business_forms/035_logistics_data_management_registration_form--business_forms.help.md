# Logistics Data Management Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect logistics data management information from users for business operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Phone Number** (`phone-number-label`, `phone_number`, required): Enter your phone number to help us contact you.
2. **Email** (`email-label`, `email`, required): Enter your work email to help us communicate with you effectively.
3. **Address** (`address-label`, `address`, required): Provide your business address to help us locate you.
4. **Select One Option** (`select-one-label`, `select_one`, required): Select one option from the dropdown menu.
5. **Select Multiple Options** (`select-multiple-label`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options from the dropdown menu.
6. **Note Field** (`note-field-label`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
7. **Date** (`date-label`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your appointment or meeting.
8. **Time** (`time-label`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your appointment or meeting.
9. **Decimal** (`decimal-label`, `decimal`, optional): Enter a decimal number for any specific reason.
10. **Integer** (`integer-label`, `integer`, optional): Enter an integer number for any reason.
11. **Text Area** (`text-area-label`, `text_area`, required): Enter any additional information or comments.
12. **File** (`file-label`, `file`, optional): Upload any file you think is necessary to attach with your submission.
