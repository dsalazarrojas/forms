# Property Insurance Renewal Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Insurance Renewal Registration Form is designed to gather essential information for the renewal of property insurance policies. This form helps ensure accurate and timely renewal processing by providing a standardized platform for collecting relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through this guide and the form carefully to ensure you understand the required information.
- Fill in your policy number, policy type (new or renewal), and property type (home, auto, or commercial).
- If applicable, provide your start and end dates for the policy term.
- Enter your contact information, including first name, last name, email, and phone number.
- Add any additional notes or comments relevant to your policy renewal.
- Review your information carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, `text`, required: false): This is the unique identifier for your property insurance policy. Enter the policy number as it appears on your policy documents.
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, select_one, required: false): Select either "New Policy" or "Renewal" to indicate the type of policy you are renewing.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of property insured, which could be "Home", "Auto", or "Commercial".
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): If your policy has a start date, enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): If your policy has an end date, enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the contact person responsible for the policy.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the contact person responsible for the policy.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person. This will be used for communication related to the policy.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes relevant to your policy renewal. This field is optional but may be useful for providing extra context.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have all necessary documents and information readily available.
- If you are unsure about any field or have questions, refer to the help guide or contact your insurance provider for assistance.
