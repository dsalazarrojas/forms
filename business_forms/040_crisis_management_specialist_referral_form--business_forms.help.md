# crisis_management_specialist_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The crisis management specialist referral form is designed to facilitate the efficient referral of clients to a specialist team for crisis management support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the specialist that you would like to refer the client to.
2. Provide the client's details, including their name, organisation, and any other relevant contact information.
3. Add any additional specialist details if required, such as specialist skills and experience.
4. Submit the form for review and assignment to a specialist team member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can provide any additional context or information about the client referral.
- **Contact Details**: This field is used to select multiple specialist contacts who are available to take on the referral. Please select all applicable specialists.
- **Specialist Referral**: This field is used to select a single specialist who will handle the referral.
- **Specialist Availability**: This field is used to select the availability of the specialist who will take on the referral. Please note that this field only applies if specialist availability is a factor in your decision-making process.
- **Specialist Note**: This field is used to provide any additional notes or comments about the specialist referral.
- **Specialist Contact Details**: This field is used to enter any additional contact information for the specialist.
- **Specialist Availability Details**: This field is used to select the specific time slot for the specialist's availability.
- **Client Details**: This field is used to enter the client's name and any other relevant information.
- **Specialist Skills**: This field is used to enter any relevant specialist skills that the client requires.
- **Specialist Experience**: This field is used to enter any relevant specialist experience that the client requires.
- **Form Referral**: This field is used to enter the form referral number for the specialist team's records.
- **Client Referral**: This field is used to enter any client referral information if it is available.
- **Specialist Organisation**: This field is used to enter the specialist's organisation or team.
- **Client Organisation**: This field is used to enter the client's organisation or team.
- **Submit**: This field is used to submit the form for review and assignment to a specialist team member.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have the necessary approvals and permissions before making a referral.
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are completed.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your specialist team or supervisor for guidance.
