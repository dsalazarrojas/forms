# Lawn Care Service Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the lawn care services provided to customers, including customer contact details, service type, dates, times, payment details, and notes. The information collected will be used for internal record-keeping and billing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the type of service provided (e.g., mowing, pruning, fertilization).
3. Enter the date and time the service was performed.
4. Describe the work done, including any additional notes or comments.
5. Enter the service status and customer satisfaction rating.
6. If payment has been made, enter the payment total and date.
7. Select the payment method used (e.g., cash, credit card, check).
8. Add any additional information or notes as required.
9. Sign the form to confirm that the service was provided and that the customer has been informed of the payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name, email address, and phone number.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of service provided, such as mowing, pruning, or fertilization.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the service was performed.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the service was performed.
* **Work Description** (`work_description`, text, optional): Describe the work done, including any additional notes or comments.
* **Service Status** (`service_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the service, such as completed or incomplete.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, number, optional): Enter a satisfaction rating from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information or notes about the service.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter any payment details, such as the payment method used.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name or ID of the service provider.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the service was performed.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the job, such as completed or pending.
* **Work Order** (`work_order`, text, optional): Enter any work order number or ID.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, optional): Select the payment terms, such as paid or pending.
* **Payment Total** (`payment_total`, number, optional): Enter the total payment amount.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of payment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method used.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or notes.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter the billing address.
* **Notes for Service Provider** (`notes_for_service_provider`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments for the service provider.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, note, optional): Sign the form to confirm that the customer has been informed of the payment details.
* **Service Provider Signature** (`service_provider_signature`, note, optional): Sign the form to confirm that the service was provided and that the customer has been informed of the payment details.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the customer information and payment details for accuracy.
- Enter any additional information or notes as required.
- Sign the form to confirm that the service was provided and that the customer has been informed of the payment details.
