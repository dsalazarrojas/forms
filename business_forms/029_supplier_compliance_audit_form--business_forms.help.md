# Supplier Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Compliance Audit Form is designed to help you evaluate the compliance of your suppliers with your company's policies and procedures. This form is meant to be filled out by suppliers to provide information about their compliance status and any potential issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information.
2. Complete each section of the form, answering all the questions and providing any requested documentation or supporting evidence.
3. Be as thorough and accurate as possible in your responses.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and commitment to comply with our policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **main-page**: This is the main page of the form where you will provide an overview of your company's compliance status and any outstanding issues.
* **audit-questions**: On this page, you will be asked a series of questions about your company's compliance with our policies. Please answer each question to the best of your ability and provide any supporting evidence or documentation.
* **audit-questions-2**: This page is a continuation of the audit questions, please answer each question and provide supporting evidence or documentation as needed.

## Tips
* Please be thorough and accurate in your responses, as this information will be used to evaluate your compliance status.
* If you are unsure about any question or require clarification, contact us for guidance.
* Ensure that you have the necessary approvals and authorizations before completing this form.
* Please sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and commitment to comply with our policies.
