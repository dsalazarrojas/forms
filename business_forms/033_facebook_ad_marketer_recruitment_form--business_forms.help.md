# Facebook Ad Marketer Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential job applicants for a Facebook Ad Marketer position. It helps recruiters or HR staff gather essential details about the business, its type, budget, location, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the name of your business in the "Business Name" field.
2. Select the type of business from the options provided in the "Business Type" field.
3. Choose the expected budget for the ad campaign from the options in the "Budget" field.
4. Select the geographic location where your business is based or operates from the options in the "Location" field.
5. Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or Skype) in the "Contact Information" field.
6. Provide a brief description of the criteria you would like to emphasize for the job application.
7. Describe your budget and the reasons behind it.
8. Add a description of your location and reasons why it's a good fit.
9. Add any additional contact information (e.g., phone number, email address) and a description of why you chose it as a means of contact.
10. Finally, provide any additional information or criteria that you would like to highlight for the job application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of your business to help us better understand who you are.
* **Business Type** (`select_one`, optional): Select the type of business you represent (e.g., e-commerce, non-e-commerce, or other).
* **Budget** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose your expected budget for the ad campaign (e.g., $10, $50, $100, $500, $1000, $5000).
* **Location** (`select_one`, optional): Select the geographic location where your business is based or operates (e.g., New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, etc.).
* **Contact Information** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, Skype).
* **Criteria** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional information about the project or business you would like to highlight.
* **Criteria Description** (`text`, optional): Describe the criteria you mentioned in the "Criteria" field.
* **Budget Description** (`text`, optional): Explain your budget choices and why you made those selections.
* **Location Description** (`text`, optional): Describe your chosen location and why it's a good fit for your business.
* **Contact Information Description** (`text`, optional): Describe your chosen contact information and why you selected those options.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`text`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Skype** (`text`, optional): Enter your Skype ID.
* **Other** (not explicitly defined in the form but implied as an option for other contact information): You can choose other means of contact not listed here (e.g., WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.).
