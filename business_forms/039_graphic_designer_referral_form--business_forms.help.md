# Graphic Designer Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for graphic designers to submit referrals for clients they have worked with. The form collects information about the client's project, including their name, contact details, and a brief description of the work done. It also helps to track if a specific tool has been assigned to the client during the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the project in the "Graphic Designer Referral Form" field.
2. Provide the name and email address of the client in the "client_name" and "email" fields respectively.
3. Enter the client's phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Enter the name of the company the client belongs to in the "company" field.
5. Select the date the referral was made in the "referral_date" field.
6. Describe the referral in the "referral_message" field.
7. If a tool was assigned to the client during the project, select "Yes" or "No" in the "assigned_tool" field.
8. Optionally, fill in the unique identifier of the form in the "form_id" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Graphic Designer Referral Form** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title of the project you are referring.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client you are referring.
* **Client Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company the client belongs to.
* **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, optional): Select the date you are making the referral.
* **Referral Message** (`referral_message`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the work you did for the client.
* **Tool Assigned** (`assigned_tool`, select, optional): If a specific tool was assigned to the client during the project, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No".
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the form (if required by your organization).
