# corporate-social-responsibility-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate social responsibility (CSR) survey is designed to collect information on various aspects of CSR, including stakeholder perceptions, feedback, performance measurement, and communication. This data will be used to help organizations evaluate and improve their CSR strategies and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each field carefully before answering.
2. Answer each question in as much detail as possible.
3. If a field is not applicable, select "No" or leave it blank.
4. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the CSR survey. Please read through this section before proceeding.
* **Stakeholder Perceptions** (`2`, `number`, required: false): This question asks about the level of awareness and engagement of different stakeholders in your organization's CSR activities.
* **Feedback** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your feedback or comments about your organization's CSR practices.
* **Performance Measurement** (`4`, `number`, required: false): This question asks about the measurement of CSR performance.
* **Transparency Enhancement** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ 'Yes' if your organization provides transparency about its CSR activities and outcomes.
	+ 'No' otherwise.
* **Continuous Improvement** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all 'Yes' if your organization continuously improves its CSR practices.
	+ 'No' otherwise.
* **CSR Goals** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all 'Yes' if your organization has set CSR goals.
	+ 'No' otherwise.
* **Communication Channel** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ 'channel-prophets' if your organization uses prophets as a communication channel.
	+ 'email' if your organization uses email as a communication channel.
	+ 'phone' if your organization uses phone as a communication channel.
* **Frequency of Communication** (`9`, `number`, required: false): This question asks about the frequency of CSR communication.
* **Level of Communication** (`10`, `number`, required: false): This question asks about the level of CSR communication.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`11`, `number`, required: false): This question asks about the satisfaction level of CSR efforts.
* **CSR Website Availability** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ 'Yes' if your organization's CSR website is available.
	+ 'No' otherwise.
* **CSR Reporting Frequency** (`13`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ 'Yes' if your organization reports CSR activities frequently.
	+ 'No' otherwise.
* **CSR Reporting Format** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all 'Yes' if your organization reports CSR activities in different formats.
	+ 'No' otherwise.
* **CSR Reporting Urgency** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all 'Yes' if your organization reports CSR activities urgently.
	+ 'No' otherwise.
* **CSR Reporting Timeliness** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all 'Yes' if your organization reports CSR activities on time.
	+ 'No' otherwise.
* **CSR Disclosure** (`17`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all 'Yes' if your organization discloses CSR information.
	+ 'No' otherwise.
* **CSR Stakeholder Engagement** (`18`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all 'Yes' if your organization engages with stakeholders on CSR.
	+ 'No' otherwise.
* **CSR Training** (`19`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all 'Yes' if your organization provides CSR training.

* **CSR Budgeting** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all 'Yes' if your organization has CSR budgeting.

* **CSR Metrics** (`21`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about CSR metrics.
* **CSR Goals and Objectives** (`22`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about CSR goals and objectives.

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* This form is designed to help your organization improve its CSR practices. Your input is valuable to us.
