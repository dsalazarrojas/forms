# Referral Program Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Referral Program Form is a tool used to document and track referrals to your business. This form helps to capture relevant details about the referral, including the client's name, email, and phone number, as well as comments about the referral.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of the referral program.
2. Enter the client's name.
3. Enter the client's email address.
4. Enter the client's phone number (or other contact information if applicable).
5. Enter a unique number associated with the referral.
6. Provide any comments about the referral.
7. Select the type of referral number (phone number, website, or other).
8. Add any additional comments about the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral Program Form** (referral_program_form_title): This is the title of the referral program.
* **Client Name** (client_name): Enter the name of the client who referred the business.
* **Client Email** (client_email): Enter the email address of the client who referred the business.
* **Client Number** (client_number): Enter a unique number associated with the client.
* **Referral Name** (referral_name): Enter the name of the referral.
* **Referral Email** (referral_email): Enter the email address of the referral.
* **Referral Number** (referral_number): Enter a unique number associated with the referral.
* **Comments about Referral** (referral_comments): Add any additional comments about the referral.
* **Referral Number Type** (referral_number_type): Select the type of referral number (phone number, website, or other).
* **Comments about Referral** (referral_comments): Add any additional comments about the referral.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about the referral.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to your internal documentation or contact your supervisor for clarification.
- This form is intended to be used for new referrals only. If you've already documented a referral, do not submit it again.
