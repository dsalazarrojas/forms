# professional_recommendation_letter_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document an employee's professional experience, including job titles, dates employed, job functions, and contact information for each job.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and title.
2. Enter your current and previous work email.
3. List your current and previous job titles, one at a time.
4. For each job, enter the company you worked for, the date you started and ended employment, and how long you were employed.
5. Select or enter the job function(s) you performed in each role.
6. Enter your contact information for each job, including work phone numbers.
7. Repeat steps 3-6 for each additional job title.
