# engineering_shift_log_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document engineering shifts, capturing relevant information for reporting and auditing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the shift start and end times in the "shift_start_time" and "shift_end_time" fields, respectively.
2. Add any relevant notes about the shift in the "shift_notes" field.
3. Enter the name, email, and phone number of the team lead for the shift in the "team_leads_name", "team_leads_email", and "team_leads_phone" fields, respectively.
4. Enter the name, email, and phone number of the shift supervisor in the "shift_supervisors_name", "shift_supervisors_email", and "shift_supervisors_phone" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, date, required/optional): Enter the start time of the shift.
  - This field is used to record the start time of the shift.
- **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the end time of the shift.
  - This field is used to record the end time of the shift.
- **Shift Notes** (`shift_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any notes about the shift.
  - This field is used to capture any relevant information about the shift, such as notable events or issues that occurred.
- **Team Lead's Name** (`team_leads_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the team lead for the shift.
  - This field is used to record the name of the team lead who supervised the shift.
- **Team Lead's Email** (`team_leads_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the team lead.
  - This field is used to record the email address of the team lead.
- **Team Lead's Phone** (`team_leads_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the team lead.
  - This field is used to record the phone number of the team lead.
- **Shift Supervisor's Name** (`shift_supervisors_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the shift supervisor.
  - This field is used to record the name of the shift supervisor.
- **Shift Supervisor's Email** (`shift_supervisors_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the shift supervisor.
  - This field is used to record the email address of the shift supervisor.
- **Shift Supervisor's Phone** (`shift_supervisors_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the shift supervisor.
  - This field is used to record the phone number of the shift supervisor.
