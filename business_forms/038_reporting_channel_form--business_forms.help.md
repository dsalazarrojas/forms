# reporting_channel_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report issues and feedback on various channels, such as email, phone, or other custom channels. It captures essential details about the issue, including the issue description, category, and priority. This form is used to track and manage issues reported through different channels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reporting channel from the available options (phone, email, or other).
2. Enter a clear and concise description of the issue.
3. Choose the issue category (bug, feature, or other).
4. Optionally, upload an attachment related to the issue, if necessary.
5. Enter any additional comments regarding the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporting Channel** (`title`, text): Enter the reporting channel through which the issue was reported (e.g., phone, email, or custom).
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text): Provide a clear and concise description of the issue.
* **Issue Category** (`issue_category`, select_one): Choose the category of the issue (bug, feature, or other).
* **Reporting Channel ID** (`reporting_channel_id`, number): Enter the ID of the reporting channel (e.g., a phone number or email address).
* **Issue Priority** (`issue_priority`, select_one): Prioritize the issue as low, medium, or high.
* **Issue Resolution** (`issue_resolution`, text): Describe how the issue was resolved.
* **Issue Comments** (`issue_comments`, text): Enter any additional comments regarding the issue.
* **Issue Attachments** (`issue_attachments`, note): Upload any files or documents related to the issue.
* **Reporting Channel Type** (`reporting_channel_type`, select_one): Choose the type of the reporting channel (phone, email, or other).
* **Issue Attachment URL** (`issue_attachment_url`, text): Enter the URL of the attachment.
* **Issue Attachment Filename** (`issue_attachment_filename`, text): Enter the filename of the attachment.
* **Issue Attachment Type** (`issue_attachment_type`, select_one): Choose the type of attachment (image, document, or audio).
* **Issue Attachment Data** (`issue_attachment_data`, text): Enter any additional data about the attachment.
* **Reporting Channel Other** (`reporting_channel_other`, text): Enter any other details about the reporting channel.
* **Issue Comments Other** (`issue_comments_other`, text): Enter any additional comments regarding the issue.
* **Issue Comments Other Other** (`issue_comments_other_other`, text): Enter any additional comments regarding the issue.
