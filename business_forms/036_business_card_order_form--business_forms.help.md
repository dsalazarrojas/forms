# Business Card Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Card Order Form is used to collect information for ordering business cards.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. For each section, fill out the most important fields first.
3. If a field is optional, fill it out only if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the card.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Position** (`position`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Website URL** (`website`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your company website, if applicable.
* **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, note, optional): This section is for delivery details, if applicable.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter the full street address for delivery.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter your country.
* **Card Details** (`card_details`, note, optional): This section is for card specifications, if applicable.
* **Quantity** (`card_quantity`, select_one, required): Select the number of cards you need.
* **Card Size** (`card_size`, select_one, required): Select the card size you need.
* **Card Stock** (`card_stock`, select_one, required): Select the paper thickness of the card.
* **Card Finish** (`card_finish`, select_one, required): Select the surface finish of the card.
* **Print Sides** (`print_sides`, select_one, required): Select the number of sides to print on the card.
* **Card Design** (`card_design`, select_one, required): Select how you will provide the design for the card.
* **Design Notes** (`design_notes`, text, optional): Enter any specific design requirements, if applicable.
* **Rush Order** (`rush_order`, select_one, required): Select if you need expedited processing.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, note, optional): This section is for payment details, if applicable.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method you will use.
* **Promo Code** (`promo_code`, text, optional): Enter any promotional code, if applicable.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any other requests, if applicable.
