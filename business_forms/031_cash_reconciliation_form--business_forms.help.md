# Cash Reconciliation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to reconcile cash accounting records with the bank statement, identifying any discrepancies and documenting the process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Reconciliation Date**, which should match the date of the latest bank statement.
2. Enter the **Business Name**, which should match the name of the entity being reconciled.
3. Enter the **Accounting Period**, which should match the period covered by the bank statement.
4. Enter the name and title of the person performing the reconciliation in the **Reconciliation Performed By** field.
5. In the **Bank Account Information** section, enter the **Bank Name** and **Account Number** (last four digits acceptable).
6. Enter the date of the bank statement in the **Bank Statement Date** field.
7. In the **Books and Records** section, enter the cash balance as per the accounting records in the **Cash Balance Per Books** field.
8. Enter the cash balance as per the latest bank statement in the **Cash Balance Per Bank Statement** field.
9. In the **Outstanding Items** section, enter the total outstanding checks, outstanding deposits not yet credited, and any other outstanding items.
10. In the **Bank Charges and Adjustments** section, enter any bank service charges or fees.
11. In the **Errors and Corrections** section, describe any errors found in the books or bank statement.
12. In the **Reconciliation Results** section, select the **Reconciliation Status** from the dropdown menu, and enter a detailed explanation of any discrepancies in the **Notes and Variance Explanation** field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reconciliation Date**: Date of the latest bank statement.
* **Business Name**: Name of the entity being reconciled.
* **Accounting Period**: Period covered by the bank statement.
* **Reconciliation Performed By**: Name and title of person performing the reconciliation.
* **Bank Name**: Name of the bank.
* **Account Number**: Last four digits of the account number (acceptable).
* **Bank Statement Date**: Date of the latest bank statement.
* **Cash Balance Per Books**: Cash balance as per accounting records.
* **Cash Balance Per Bank Statement**: Cash balance as per the latest bank statement.
* **Outstanding Checks Total**: Total outstanding checks.
* **Outstanding Deposits Not Yet Credited**: Outstanding deposits not yet credited.
* **Bank Service Charges or Fees**: Bank service charges or fees.
* **Cash Balance Per Books**: Cash balance as per accounting records.
* **Cash Balance Per Bank Statement**: Cash balance as per the latest bank statement.
* **NSF or Returned Check Charges**: NSF or returned check charges.
* **Errors Found in Books**: Describe any errors found in the books.
* **Errors Found in Bank Statement**: Describe any errors found in the bank statement.
* **Reconciliation Status**: Select from: Reconciled - No Variance, Reconciled - Variance Explained, Not Reconciled - Variance Unexplained, Pending Review.
* **Notes and Variance Explanation**: Detailed explanation of any discrepancies.

## Tips
* Double-check the bank statement and accounting records for accuracy before completing this form.
* Ensure all discrepancies are properly documented and explained.
* If the reconciliation status is not reconciled, ensure that the discrepancies are explained and a plan for resolution is documented.
