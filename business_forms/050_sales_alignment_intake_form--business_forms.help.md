# Sales Alignment Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to capture relevant information about a customer's sales process and alignment with our company's goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, but do not submit it until you have reviewed each section carefully.
2. Enter the company name and contact information in the first section.
3. Provide the sales rep name (if applicable) in the second section.
4. Describe the decision process and sales outcomes in the third section.
5. Select the sales pipeline and next steps in the fourth section.
6. Enter any sales outcome notes and account manager notes in the fifth section.
7. Specify the decision made by in the sixth section.
8. Enter alignment outcomes and alignment rationale in the seventh section.
9. Add any additional comments in the last section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`): The company that the sales process is related to.
  Please enter the name of the company as it appears on their website or marketing materials.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): The contact person at the company.
  Please enter the name of the contact person who will be working with your team.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): The contact email of the contact person.
  Please enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`): The contact phone number of the contact person.
  Please enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Sales Rep Name** (`sales_rep_name`): The sales representative who is working with the company.
  Please enter the name of the sales representative who is working with the company (if applicable).
* **Account Manager Name** (`account_manager_name`): The account manager who is responsible for the sale.
  Please enter the name of the account manager who is responsible for the sale (if applicable).
* **Decision Process** (`decision_process`): Describe the decision-making process used by the company.
  Please describe the decision-making process used by the company, including any specific steps or procedures.
* **Alignment Strategy** (`alignment_strategy`): Describe how the company would like to be aligned with our goals.
  Please describe how the company would like to be aligned with our goals and objectives.
* **Key Challenges** (`key_challenges`): List the key challenges that the company is facing.
  Please list the key challenges that the company is facing and how they are affecting the sales process.
* **Sales Outcomes** (`sales_outcomes`): Select the sales outcomes that have occurred.
  Please select the sales outcomes that have occurred, including closed-won, closed-lost, open, or other.
* **Sales Pipeline** (`sales_pipeline`): Select the sales pipeline that the sale is related to.
  Please select the sales pipeline that the sale is related to.
* **Decision Date** (`decision_date`): The date the decision was made.
  Please enter the date the decision was made.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`): Describe the next steps in the sales process.
  Please describe the next steps in the sales process, including any specific actions or tasks.
* **Sales Outcome Notes** (`sales_outcome_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the sales outcome.
  Please enter any additional notes about the sales outcome.
* **Account Manager Notes** (`account_manager_notes`): Enter any additional notes from the account manager.
  Please enter any additional notes from the account manager.
* **Decision Made By** (`decision_made_by`): Specify who made the decision.
  Please specify who made the decision.
* **Alignment Outcomes** (`alignment_outcomes`): Select whether the company has alignment outcomes.
  Please select whether the company has alignment outcomes.
* **Sales Alignment Outcomes** (`sales_alignment_outcomes`): Select whether the company has sales alignment outcomes.
  Please select whether the company has sales alignment outcomes.
* **Alignment Rationale** (`alignment_rationale`): Describe the alignment rationale.
  Please describe the alignment rationale.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments.
  Please enter any additional comments.
* **Decision Factors** (`decision_factors`): Select the decision factors that were considered.
  Please select the decision factors that were considered, including price, product fit, timing, or other.
* **Alignment Factors** (`alignment_factors`): Select the alignment factors that were considered.
  Please select the alignment factors that were considered, including price, product fit, timing, or other.
* **Other Factors** (`other_factors`): Enter any other factors that were considered.
  Please enter any other factors that were considered.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes.
  Please enter any additional notes.
