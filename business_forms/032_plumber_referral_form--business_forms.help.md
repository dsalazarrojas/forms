# Plumber Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for plumbers who refer customers to your business. The form will help you keep track of customer referrals and their associated details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields (marked as "Required" in the field-by-field explanation section).
* Select the appropriate options for the "Job Type" and "Referral Source" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referral Information (1)**: Enter the description of the referral.
* **Name (2)**: Enter the name of the customer.
* **Contact Information (3)**: Enter the contact information of the customer.
* **Email (4)**: Enter the customer's email address. Please enter a valid email address in the correct format (e.g., example@example.com).
* **Phone Number (5)**: Enter the customer's phone number. Please enter the correct format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Date Visited (6)**: Select the date of when the customer visited the plumber.
* **Time Visited (7)**: Select the time of when the customer visited the plumber.
* **Job Type (8)**: Select the type of job the customer referred you to (e.g., emergency call, routine service).
* **Referral Source (9)**: Select how the customer heard about your business (e.g., social media, email, word of mouth).
* **Customer Name (10)**: Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Email (11)**: Enter the customer's email address. Please enter a valid email address in the correct format (e.g., example@example.com).
* **Customer Phone (12)**: Enter the customer's phone number. Please enter the correct format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Customer Address (13)**: Enter the customer's address.
* **Customer Zip (14)**: Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Customer City (15)**: Enter the customer's city.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and correctly.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the customer or contact us for help.
