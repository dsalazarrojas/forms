# Advance Loan VIP Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting information from individuals who are applying for an advance loan as a VIP. It will be used to assess the loan request and determine the feasibility of the application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill in your full name in the "Applicant Full Name" field.
2.  Enter your company affiliation or employer name in the "Company Affiliation" field.
3.  Provide your mobile number in the "Mobile Number" field.
4.  Enter your professional email address in the "Professional Email" field.
5.  Select the loan amount you are requesting in the "Requested Loan Amount" field.
6.  Choose the loan purpose from the options provided in the "Loan Purpose" field.
7.  Select the preferred term length for the loan in the "Preferred Term Length" field.
8.  Enter your annual gross income before taxes in the "Annual Gross Income" field.
9.  Select your employment status from the options provided in the "Employment Status" field.
10.  Estimate your credit score in the "Credit Score Estimated" field. 
11.  Select whether an account manager was requested for VIPs in the "Account Manager Requested" field.
12.  Enter any special financial conditions or notes in the "Special Financial Conditions or Notes" field.
13.  Choose a preferred funding date in the "Preferred Funding Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Company Affiliation** (`company_affiliation`, `text`, required): Enter your current employer or business name.
*   **Mobile Number** (`mobile_number`, `text`, required): Enter your mobile number for contact purposes.
*   **Professional Email** (`professional_email`, `email`, required): Enter your professional email address.
*   **Requested Loan Amount** (`requested_loan_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of the loan you are requesting in USD.
*   **Loan Purpose** (`loan_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Choose the purpose of the loan from the options provided.
*   **Preferred Term Length** (`preferred_term_length`, `select_one`, required): Select the duration of the loan from the options provided.
*   **Annual Gross Income** (`annual_gross_income`, `number`, required): Enter your annual gross income before taxes.
*   **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your employment status from the options provided.
*   **Credit Score Estimated** (`credit_score_estimated`, `number`, optional): Estimate your credit score.
*   **Account Manager Requested** (`account_manager_requested`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether an account manager was requested for VIPs.
*   **Special Financial Conditions or Notes** (`special_financial_conditions_or_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any special financial conditions or notes.
*   **Preferred Funding Date** (`preferred_funding_date`, `date`, optional): Choose a target date for receiving funds.
