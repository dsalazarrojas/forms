# Industrial Process Improvement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who have visited or interacted with a site or location where an industrial process is being performed. The data collected will help management identify areas of improvement and assess the effectiveness of current processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Ensure that you only complete this form once per site or location.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the "Comments" field.
* If you are a supervisor or manager, please include any relevant comments or feedback in the "Supervisor Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: This field is used to capture information about the individual completing the form. Please enter your name, title, or any other relevant identifier.
* **Date Visited**: This field is used to track the date of your visit or interaction with the site or location.
* **Location Visited**: Enter the name or description of the site or location you visited or interacted with.
* **Comments**: Provide any feedback or observations about the industrial process, including suggestions for improvement. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Contact Information**: Please enter your first and last name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Supervisor Comments**: If you are a supervisor or manager, please enter any relevant comments or feedback about the industrial process.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or manager for guidance.
* If you have already completed this form, please do not submit another.
