# Business Demographic Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your business's demographics, including its characteristics and the characteristics of your customers. This data will help us better understand your business and its needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page carefully and fill in the required information.
2. For select_one fields, choose one option from the list.
3. For select_multiple fields, choose all applicable options.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's name as it appears on official documents and marketing materials.
* **Years in Business** (`years_established`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years your business has been established.
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Sole Proprietor: A business owned and operated by one person.
	+ Partnership: A business owned and operated by two or more people.
	+ Corporation: A business with a board of directors and shareholders.
	+ LLC: A limited liability company with owners who are not personally liable for business debts.
	+ Non-Profit: A business that operates for a social cause, not for profit.
	+ Franchise: A business that operates under a franchisor's brand.
	+ Other: Select this option if your business doesn't fit into any of the above categories.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry`, `text`, required): Enter your primary industry or sector.
* **Geographic Location** (`geographic_location`, `text`, required): Enter your primary location or service area.
* **Number of Employees** (`employee_count`, `select_one`, required):
	+ 1 person: Fewer than 2 employees.
	+ 2 - 5: 2-5 employees.
	+ 6 - 10: 6-10 employees.
	+ 11 - 25: 11-25 employees.
	+ 26 - 50: 26-50 employees.
	+ 51 - 100: 51-100 employees.
	+ 100+: More than 100 employees.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Under 50k: Less than 50,000.
	+ 50k - 250k: Between 50,000 and 250,000.
	+ 250k - 1M: Between 250,000 and 1,000,000.
	+ 1M - 5M: Between 1,000,000 and 5,000,000.
	+ 5M - 10M: Between 5,000,000 and 10,000,000.
	+ 10M+: More than 10,000,000.
* **Market Coverage** (`market_coverage`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Local: Local area.
	+ Regional: Regional area.
	+ National: National level.
	+ International: Global level.
* **Target Customer Age Range** (`target_customer_age`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Under 18: Less than 18 years old.
	+ 18 - 25: 18-25 years old.
	+ 25 - 35: 25-35 years old.
	+ 35 - 50: 35-50 years old.
	+ 50 - 65: 50-65 years old.
	+ 65+: More than 65 years old.
	+ All Ages: All age groups.
* **Target Customer Income Level** (`customer_income`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Under 30k: Less than 30,000.
	+ 30k - 60k: Between 30,000 and 60,000.
	+ 60k - 100k: Between 60,000 and 100,000.
	+ 100k - 150k: Between 100,000 and 150,000.
	+ 150k+: More than 150,000.
	+ Varies: Income varies.
* **Target Customer Education Level** (`customer_education`, `select_one`, required):
	+ High School: High school diploma or equivalent.
	+ Some College: Some college or university education.
	+ Bachelor Degree: Bachelor's degree.
	+ Graduate Degree: Master's or doctoral degree.
	+ Varies: Education varies.
* **Customer Gender Distribution** (`customer_gender`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Primarily Male: Primarily male customers.
	+ Primarily Female: Primarily female customers.
	+ Mixed: Equal or mixed gender customers.
	+ No Gender Focus: No specific gender focus.
* **Primary Customer Types** (`primary_customer_types`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Consumers: End-user customers.
	+ Small Businesses: Small businesses as customers.
	+ Large Businesses: Large businesses as customers.
	+ Government: Government agencies as customers.
	+ Non-Profits: Non-profit organizations as customers.
	+ Institutions: Academic or research institutions as customers.
* **Primary Owner Age** (`owner_age`, `number`, required): Enter the age of the primary business owner.
* **Primary Owner Gender** (`owner_gender`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Male: Male owner.
	+ Female: Female owner.
	+ Non-Binary: Non-binary owner.
	+ Prefer Not to Say: Do not disclose owner's gender.
* **Minority or Women-Owned Business** (`minority_owned`, `select_one`, required):
	+ True: Minority or women-owned business.
	+ False: Not minority or women-owned.
	+ In Progress: In progress to being certified minority or women-owned.
