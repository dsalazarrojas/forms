# Candidate Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information and feedback from interviewers after a candidate has gone through the hiring process. It helps to document the candidate's performance, skills, and fit with the company culture, providing a comprehensive view for informed decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each candidate you've interviewed.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, based on your interaction with the candidate.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your answers, especially in the 'strengths summary' and 'concerns or gaps identified' fields.
4. Review and submit the form once you've completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name**: Enter the full name of the candidate you've interviewed.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title that the candidate is being interviewed for.
* **Interview Type**: Select the stage of the hiring process this interview represents (e.g., phone screen, initial interview, etc.).
* **Date of Interview**: Enter the date the interview took place.
* **Interviewers**: List all staff participating in the evaluation.
* **Technical Competence**: Rate the candidate's ability to handle core job tasks (1=Lowest, 2, 3, 4, 5=Highest).
* **Industry Experience**: Rate the candidate's knowledge of the field (1=Lowest, 2, 3, 4, 5=Highest).
* **Cultural Alignment**: Rate the candidate's fit with the company's values and work style (1=Lowest, 2, 3, 4, 5=Highest).
* **Communication and Interpersonal Skills**: Rate the candidate's ability to convey ideas clearly (1=Lowest, 2, 3, 4, 5=Highest).
* **Problem-Solving Ability**: Rate the candidate's capacity to think critically under pressure (1=Lowest, 2, 3, 4, 5=Highest).
* **Summary of Candidate's Strengths**: Provide a detailed description of the candidate's strengths.
* **Concerns or Gaps Identified**: Note any areas that may require more training or investigation.
* **Motivation for the Role**: Explain why the candidate is interested in your company.
* **Next Step Recommendation**: Choose how you think the candidate should proceed (e.g., advance to next round, put on hold, etc.).
* **Salary Expectations Discussed**: Enter the amount in local currency mentioned by the candidate.
* **Available Start Date**: Enter the start date the candidate is available to begin work.
* **Evaluator Signature or Name**: Enter your name for the official record.
* **Evaluation Submission Date**: Enter the date you submit this form.
