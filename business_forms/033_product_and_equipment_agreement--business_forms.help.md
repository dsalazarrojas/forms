# product_and_equipment_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `product_and_equipment_agreement` form is a tool for capturing critical information about a piece of equipment, including its administrative and logistical details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the necessary details about the equipment, such as its type, description, and purchase date.
2. Specify the equipment's location, condition, and warranty status.
3. Provide the warranty expiration date, manufacturer, model number, and product code (if applicable).
4. Enter the purchase and sale prices.
5. Confirm the purchase date.
6. Update the status of the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Equipment Information
* **Equipment Information** (`product_and_equipment_agreement_page_1`, text, Required/Optional): Enter a brief description of the equipment being reported.
* **Type of Equipment** (`equipment_type`, select_one, Required/Optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the equipment is of a specific type.
* **Description of Equipment** (`equipment_description`, text, Required/Optional): Provide a detailed description of the equipment.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the serial number of the equipment (if applicable).
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, Required/Optional): Enter the date the equipment was purchased.
* **Location** (`location`, text, Required/Optional): Specify the current location of the equipment.
* **Condition** (`condition`, text, Required/Optional): Indicate the current condition of the equipment.
* **Warranty Status** (`warranty_status`, select_one, Required/Optional): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the warranty status.
* **Warranty Expiration Date** (`warranty_expiration_date`, date, Required/Optional): Enter the date when the warranty expires.
* **Manufacturer** (`manufacturer`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the manufacturer of the equipment.
* **Model Number** (`model_number`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the model number of the equipment.
* **Product Code** (`product_code`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the product code of the equipment (if applicable).
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, Required/Optional): Enter the original purchase price of the equipment.
* **Sale Price** (`sale_price`, number, Required/Optional): Enter the current sale price of the equipment.
* **Confirm Purchase Date** (`purchase_date_confirmation`, select_multiple, Required/Optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm the purchase date.
* **Status** (`equipment_status`, select_one, Required/Optional): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to update the status of the equipment.
* **Maintenance Status** (`maintenance_status`, select_one, Required/Optional): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to update the maintenance status of the equipment.
