# Solar Roof Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solar Roof Survey Template is a helpful tool for gathering information about a property's solar roof potential. It helps owners or managers assess their roof's characteristics, energy goals, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the building information section with the property's address and details.
2. Provide details about the roof, including its size and characteristics.
3. Share information about the owner or contact person, including their name and contact details.
4. Specify the energy goals for the property, such as reducing energy consumption or generating renewable energy.
5. Describe the energy assessment, including any existing solar installations or energy-efficient measures.
6. Choose the size of the solar system desired for the property.
7. Indicate the installation site for the solar system.
8. Provide electrical information, such as the electrical load and voltage.
9. List any financial incentives or benefits available for installing solar panels.
10. Plan the next steps for the solar roof installation, including any necessary permits or approvals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Solar Roof Form** (`solar_roof_survey_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Building Info** (`building_info`): Enter the address and details of the building, including its age, size, and usage.
* **Roof Info** (`roof_info`): Provide details about the roof, including its size, pitch, and any existing solar installations.
* **Owner Info** (`owner_info`): Share information about the owner or contact person, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Enter the contact person's name, email, and phone number.
* **Energy Goals** (`energy_goals`): Describe the energy goals for the property, such as reducing energy consumption or generating renewable energy.
* **Energy Assessment** (`energy_assessment`): Describe any existing energy-efficient measures or solar installations on the property.
* **System Size** (`system_size`): Choose the size of the solar system desired for the property.
* **Installation Site** (`installation_site`): Indicate the location where the solar system will be installed.
* **Electrical Info** (`electrical_info`): Provide electrical information, such as the electrical load and voltage.
* **Financial Incentives** (`financial_incentives`): List any financial incentives or benefits available for installing solar panels.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`): Plan the next steps for the solar roof installation, including any necessary permits or approvals.
