# Significant Contracts Declaration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Significant Contracts Declaration" form is designed to collect information about contracts that are significant, substantial, or impactful. This form is intended for individuals or organizations to provide details about contracts they have entered into, including their terms, signatories, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "General Information" page to provide an overview of the contract.
2. Move on to the "Contract Details" page to specify the terms and conditions of the contract.
3. Continue to the "Contract Signatories" page to list the individuals or entities involved in the contract.
4. You may be required to fill in multiple "Contract Terms" pages, depending on the number of terms specified in the contract. Each "Contract Terms" page will have a unique label (e.g., "Contract Terms 1", "Contract Terms 2", etc.).
