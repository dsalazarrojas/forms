# employee_injury_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about employee injuries, including the details of the incident, the employee involved, and the report's submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages and ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Start by filling out the first page, "Employee Injuries", where you can provide any relevant details about the incident.
3. Proceed to the second page, "Report Date", and input the date you are reporting the injury.
4. On the third page, "Report Details", type in a brief description of what happened during the incident.
5. Move on to the fourth page, "Report Time", and input the time you are reporting the incident.
6. On the fifth page, "Report Date" (again, as the form is split across two pages), enter the date of the incident again.
7. The next pages, "Employee", "Incident Date", "Incident Time", and "Incident Location", are all about the employee and the details of the incident. Fill out these fields accordingly.
8. On the "Witness" page, provide any names or contact information of witnesses.
9. On the "Description" page, write a detailed description of what happened.
10. Finally, on the "Injury Type" page, select the type of injury that occurred from the provided options.
11. End with the "Medical Report" page, where you can input any additional medical information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Injuries**: A brief description of the incident and injuries sustained.
* **Report Date**: The date when you are submitting this report.
* **Report Time**: The time when you are submitting this report.
* **Report Details**: A brief description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Date**: The date of the incident.
* **Incident Time**: The time of the incident.
* **Incident Location**: The location where the incident occurred.
* **Witness**: Any names or contact information of witnesses.
* **Description**: A detailed description of what happened.
* **Injury Type**: Select the type of injury that occurred from the provided options.
* **Medical Report**: Any additional medical information related to the incident.
