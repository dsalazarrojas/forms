# Equipment Transfer Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record the transfer of equipment within the organization, including details about the equipment, the transfer, and the current status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Enter the Transfer Details (page 1) with the basic information about the transfer.
3. Enter the Equipment Details (page 2) with the details about the equipment being transferred.
4. Choose the correct options for the Equipment Status, Maintenance Category, Maintenance Status, and Maintenance Priority.
5. Enter the Equipment Cost and any additional Comments.
6. Verify the accuracy of the information entered in the form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transfer Details** (page 1, id: 1): Enter a brief description of the equipment transfer, including the reason for the transfer.
* **Equipment Details** (page 2, id: 2): Enter the details of the equipment being transferred, including the equipment's model, description, serial number, and tag.
* **Transfer Date** (page 2, id: 3): Enter the date of the equipment transfer. This is usually the current date.
* **Transferred By** (page 3, id: 4): Enter the person who transferred the equipment.
* **Comments** (page 4, id: 5): Add any additional comments about the transfer.
* **Date of Last Maintenance** (page 4, id: 6): Enter the date of the last maintenance performed on the equipment, if applicable.
* **Next Maintenance Due Date** (page 4, id: 7): Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance, if applicable.
* **Transfer Location** (page 5, id: 8): Enter the location where the equipment was transferred from and to.
* **Transferred To** (page 5, id: 9): Enter the department or unit that received the equipment.
* **Transferred From** (page 5, id: 10): Enter the department or unit that transferred the equipment from.
* **Transferred By Title** (page 6, id: 11): Enter the title of the person who transferred the equipment.
* **Equipment Serial Number** (page 6, id: 12): Enter the equipment's serial number, if applicable.
* **Equipment Model** (page 6, id: 13): Enter the equipment's model.
* **Equipment Description** (page 6, id: 14): Enter a brief description of the equipment.
* **Equipment Tag** (page 6, id: 15): Enter any equipment tag numbers, if applicable.
* **Transferred To Department** (page 7, id: 16): Enter the department that received the equipment.
* **Transferred From Department** (page 7, id: 17): Enter the department that transferred the equipment from.
* **Transferred By User** (page 7, id: 18): Enter the user who transferred the equipment.
* **Equipment Status** (page 8, id: 19): Choose the current status of the equipment.
* **Maintenance Category** (page 8, id: 20): Choose the category of the maintenance performed on the equipment.
* **Maintenance Status** (page 8, id: 21): Choose the current status of the maintenance performed on the equipment.
* **Maintenance Priority** (page 8, id: 22): Choose the priority of the next scheduled maintenance.
* **Equipment Cost** (page 9, id: 23): Enter the cost of the equipment, if applicable.
* **Comments 2** (page 9, id: 24): Add any additional comments.
* **Transfer Date2** (page 10, id: 25): Enter the date of the equipment transfer, if different from the original transfer date.

## Tips

* Ensure that all necessary information is entered accurately to avoid discrepancies.
* Use the correct date format when entering dates.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the equipment's documentation or consult with the equipment's owner.
