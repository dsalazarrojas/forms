# Relocation Consultant Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Relocation Consultant Referral Form is used to gather information from clients and consultants about potential relocation opportunities. This form helps us understand the client's and consultant's needs, preferences, and interests related to relocation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the client's first name.
2. Enter the client's last name.
3. Enter the client's email address.
4. Enter the client's phone number.
5. Enter the referral date.
6. Select the referral type (Yes or No).
7. Enter the company the client is relocating from.
8. Enter the name of the consultant who referred the client.
9. Select whether the consultant was referred through an external source (Yes or No).
10. Select whether the client has been referred to the consultant (Yes or No).
11. Enter the consultant's phone number (if applicable).
12. Enter the consultant's email address (if applicable).
13. Enter the consultant's company (if applicable).
14. Enter the location of the consultant's office (if applicable).
15. Enter any notes from the consultant regarding the referral.
16. Enter the name of the client being referred.
17. Enter the client's phone number (if applicable).
18. Enter the client's email address (if applicable).
19. Enter the company the client is relocating to.
20. Enter the location of the client's new office (if applicable).
21. Enter any notes from the client regarding the referral.
22. Enter any comments from the referral process.
23. Enter any comments from the consultant regarding the referral.
24. Select whether the consultant referred the client (Yes or No).
25. Enter any notes from the consultant regarding the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: The client's first name.
* **Last Name**: The client's last name.
* **Email**: The client's email address.
* **Phone**: The client's phone number.
* **Referral Date**: The date the referral was made.
* **Company**: The company the client is relocating from.
* **Consultant Name**: The name of the consultant who referred the client.
* **Referral Type**: Select Yes if the referral was made and No if not.
* **Referral Source**: Select Yes if the consultant was referred to through an external source and No if not.
* **Consultant Phone**: The consultant's phone number (if applicable).
* **Consultant Email**: The consultant's email address (if applicable).
* **Consultant Company**: The consultant's company (if applicable).
* **Consultant Location**: The location of the consultant's office (if applicable).
* **Consultant Notes**: Any notes from the consultant regarding the referral.
* **Client Name**: The name of the client being referred.
* **Client Phone**: The client's phone number (if applicable).
* **Client Email**: The client's email address (if applicable).
* **Client Company**: The company the client is relocating to.
* **Client Location**: The location of the client's new office (if applicable).
* **Client Referral Notes**: Any notes from the client regarding the referral.
* **Referral Comments**: Any comments from the referral process.
* **Consultant Referral Comments**: Any comments from the consultant regarding the referral.
* **Consultant Referral Source**: Select Yes if the consultant referred the client through an external source and No if not.
* **Consultant Referral Notes**: Any notes from the consultant regarding the referral.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If a field is not applicable, please leave it blank.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
