# work_call_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The work_call_request_form is used to collect information from employees about their work calls.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the work call before filling out the form.
2. Start by filling out the form as follows:
	* **Employer**: Enter the name of the employer you will be meeting with.
	* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the meeting.
	* **Work Date**: Enter the date of the meeting in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
	* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the meeting in the format hh:mm.
	* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the meeting in the format hh:mm.
	* **Meeting Location**: Enter the location of the meeting.
	* **Purpose**: Enter a brief description of the purpose of the meeting.
	* **Employee**: Enter your name.
	* **Employee Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Employer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the employer.
	* **Employee Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Meeting Length**: Enter the length of the meeting in minutes.
	* **Teleconference**: Check if the meeting will be held via teleconference.
	* **Meeting Type**: Select all types of meetings that apply (e.g. "Yes" for in-person, "No" for virtual).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employer**: This is the name of the person or organization you will be meeting with.
* **Job Title**: This is the title of the job you will be meeting about.
* **Work Date**: This is the date of the meeting, in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Start Time**: This is the start time of the meeting, in the format hh:mm.
* **End Time**: This is the end time of the meeting, in the format hh:mm.
* **Meeting Location**: This is the location of the meeting.
* **Purpose**: This is a brief description of the purpose of the meeting.
* **Employee**: This is your name.
* **Employee Email**: This is your email address.
* **Employer Phone**: This is the phone number of the person or organization you will be meeting with.
* **Employee Phone**: This is your phone number.
* **Meeting Length**: This is the length of the meeting in minutes.
* **Teleconference**: Check this if the meeting will be held via teleconference.
* **Meeting Type**: Select all types of meetings that apply (e.g. "Yes" for in-person, "No" for virtual).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* This form is intended to be filled out only once per meeting. If you need to schedule another meeting with the same employer, please fill out a new form.
