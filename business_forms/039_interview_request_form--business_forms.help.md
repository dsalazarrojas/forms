# interview_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from candidates for a job interview. It is essential that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the interview process goes smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the candidate's personal information, including their name, email, and contact number.
2. Provide the job title for which the candidate is applying.
3. If applicable, select a date and time for the interview.
4. Specify the location for the interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Info** (`candidate_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the candidate, such as their qualifications or relevant experience.
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of the candidate, including their first and last name.
* **Candidate Email** (`candidate_email`, email, optional): Enter the candidate's email address, ensuring it is in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`candidate_phone`, text, optional): Enter the candidate's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`candidate_address`, text, optional): Enter the candidate's address for mailing purposes.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the specific job title for which the candidate is applying.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, optional): Select a date and time for the interview, using the standard date format (e.g., `MM/DD/YYYY`).
* **Interview Time** (`interview_time`, time, optional): Select a time for the interview, using the 24-hour clock format (e.g., `HH:MM`).
* **Interview Location** (`interview_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the interview will take place.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the email format for the candidate's email address.
* Use the correct date and time format for the interview date and time.
