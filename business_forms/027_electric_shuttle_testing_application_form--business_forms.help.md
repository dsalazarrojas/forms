# Electric Shuttle Testing Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals who want to test an electric shuttle. It is meant to collect relevant details from applicants to facilitate the testing process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form once you have provided all necessary details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info** (text, optional): This field is meant for the user to provide any relevant information about themselves, such as their name, contact details, or any other relevant context.
* **applicant_info** (text, optional): This field is for the applicant to provide information about themselves, including their name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **test_details** (text, optional): This field is where the applicant can provide any specific details about the test they wish to undertake, such as the test date, time, and any specific requirements.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient testing process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us through the contact information provided.
* We reserve the right to request additional information or clarification as needed.
* This form is for electric shuttle testing purposes only, and any other use of this form may result in it being declined.
