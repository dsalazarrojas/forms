# Financial Planner Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather contact details and other necessary information from individuals or organizations that may require the services of a financial planner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact details section (fields 1-3) with the relevant contact information of the individual or organization being referred.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the referral party (fields 4-6).
3. Specify the reason for the referral (field 7).
4. Choose the source of the referral (field 8).
5. Add any additional comments about the referral (field 9).
6. Select the type of referral (field 13: Self, Client, or Other).
7. Choose how to follow up with the referral party (field 14: None, Email, or Phone).
8. Enter the phone number to follow up with the referral party (field 15: follow_up_phone).
9. Enter the email to follow up with the referral party (field 16: follow_up_email).
10. Enter the date and time for the follow-up (fields 17 and 18).
11. Add any follow-up notes (field 19).
12. Set the next follow-up time (field 20).
13. Add any additional notes (field 21).
14. Enter the name of the financial planner responsible for the referral (field 11: financial_planner).
15. Enter the submitted date and time (fields 22 and 23).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details (Field 1)**: Enter the name and contact information of the individual or organization being referred.
* **Client Name (Field 2)**: Enter the name of the client or individual being referred.
* **Referral Name (Field 3)**: Enter the name of the referral party.
* **Referral Phone (Field 4)**: Enter the phone number of the referral party.
* **Referral Email (Field 5)**: Enter the email of the referral party.
* **Contact Information (Field 6)**: Enter any additional contact information for the referral party.
* **Referral Reason (Field 7)**: Specify the reason for the referral.
* **Referral Source (Field 8)**: Specify the source of the referral.
* **Referral Comments (Field 9)**: Add any additional comments about the referral.
* **Referral Type (Field 13)**: Select the type of referral: Self, Client, or Other.
* **Follow-up (Field 14)**: Choose how to follow up with the referral party: None, Email, or Phone.
* **Follow-up Phone (Field 15)**: Enter the phone number to follow up with the referral party.
* **Follow-up Email (Field 16)**: Enter the email to follow up with the referral party.
* **Follow-up Date (Field 17)**: Enter the date for the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Time (Field 18)**: Enter the time for the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Notes (Field 19)**: Add any follow-up notes.
* **Next Follow-up (Field 20)**: Set the next follow-up time.
* **Notes (Field 21)**: Add any additional notes.
* **Financial Planner (Field 11)**: Enter the name of the financial planner responsible for the referral.
* **Planner Notes (Field 12)**: Add any additional notes from the financial planner.
* **Submitted By (Field 22)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date (Field 23)**: Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
