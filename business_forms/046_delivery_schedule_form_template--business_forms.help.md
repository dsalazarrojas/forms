# delivery_schedule_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is used for creating and managing delivery schedules. It provides a basic structure for capturing essential information related to deliveries, such as delivery date, time, status, customer details, and more. The goal is to ensure consistent and accurate data entry for easy tracking and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start Form" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Provide the delivery date in the "Delivery Date" field. This should be the date when the delivery is expected or has taken place.
3. Enter the delivery time in the "Delivery Time" field. This should be the time of day when the delivery is expected or has occurred.
4. Select the current delivery status from the drop-down menu in the "Delivery Status" field. This indicates whether the delivery is active or inactive.
5. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
6. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer EMail" field.
7. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Date**: A required date field where you should enter the delivery date.
* **Delivery Time**: A required field to input the delivery time.
* **Delivery Status**: A select-one field to indicate whether the delivery is active or inactive.
* **Customer Name**: A field to enter the customer's name.
* **Customer EMail**: A field to enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone**: A field to enter the customer's phone number.
