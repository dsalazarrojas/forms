# real_estate_market_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **real_estate_market_assessment_form** is designed to collect data on properties in the real estate market. This form will help you assess the market trends and analyze the properties in the area.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, navigate through the pages using the navigation buttons at the top.
- Each page will ask you to fill in a specific piece of information about the property.
- Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Property Type** (`property_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of property, such as "house", "condo", "townhouse", etc.
- **Listing Type** (`listing_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the current listing type of the property, such as "Active", "Pending", "Sold", etc.
- **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the property, such as a city, state, or region.
- **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the property, such as "Act of sale", "Active", "Sold", etc.
- **Property Use** (`property_use`, select_multiple, optional): Select the use of the property, such as "Single", "Multi", "Mixed", etc.
- **Property Size** (`property_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of the property, such as 1000, 2000, etc.
- **Property Bedrooms** (`property_bedrooms`, number, optional): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
- **Property Bathrooms** (`property_bathrooms`, number, optional): Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
- **Property Year Built** (`property_year_built`, number, optional): Enter the year the property was built.
- **Property Year Renovated** (`property_year_renovated`, number, optional): Enter the year the property was renovated.
- **Property Lot Size** (`property_lot_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of the property's lot.
- **Property Lot Type** (`property_lot_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of property lot, such as "land", "waterfront", etc.
- **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the property.
- **Property City** (`property_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the property.
- **Property State** (`property_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the property.
- **Property Country** (`property_country`, text, optional): Enter the country of the property.
- **Property Postal Code** (`property_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter the postal code of the property.
- **Market Trend** (`market_trend`, select_one, optional): Choose the current market trend, such as "Increasing", "Decreasing", "Stable", etc.
- **Listing Price** (`listing_price`, number, optional): Enter the listing price of the property.
- **Sold Price** (`sold_price`, number, optional): Enter the sold price of the property.
- **Market Condition** (`market_condition`, select_one, optional): Choose the current market condition, such as "Hot", "Cold", "New", etc.
- **Property Type of Use** (`property_type_of_use`, text, optional): Enter the type of property use, such as "residential", "commercial", etc.
- **Listing Agent** (`listing_agent`, text, optional): Enter the listing agent's name.
- **Property Description** (`property_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the property.
- **Property Additional Info** (`property_additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the property.

**Tips:**
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Use the correct format for each field, such as numbers for numerical fields and text for text fields.
- If a field is optional, you can leave it blank if it's not applicable to your property.
- If you're unsure about a field, try to use your best judgment or ask for help.
