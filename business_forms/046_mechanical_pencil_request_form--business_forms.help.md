# Mechanical Pencil Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit a mechanical pencil request for a specific purpose. The form can be completed by selecting the options provided in the form submission field, entering your name, department, and other relevant details. This form will help the user to create a formal request for a mechanical pencil.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Click on the "Form Submission" field and choose whether the request is a "Yes" or "No".
- Enter your full name in the "Name" field.
- In the "Department" field, select whether you are in the "Yes" or "No" department.
- Enter the quantity of mechanical pencils you are requesting in the "Quantity" field.
- Provide the reason for requesting the mechanical pencils in the "Reason" field.
- Select the date you would like to receive the mechanical pencils in the "Date" field.
- Choose any relevant categories for the request in the "Category" field.
- Provide any additional comments in the "Comment" field (optional).
- Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
- Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
- Select the person assigned to process the request in the "Assigned To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission** (`form_submit`, select_multiple, Required: false)
	+ Select "Yes" if you are submitting this form to request a mechanical pencil, and "No" otherwise.
* **Name** (`name`, text, Required: false)
	+ Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on the requested mechanical pencil.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, Required: false)
	+ Select "Yes" if you are affiliated with the department, and "No" otherwise.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, Required: false)
	+ Enter the number of mechanical pencils you are requesting.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, Required: false)
	+ Provide a brief explanation for why you are requesting the mechanical pencils.
* **Date** (`date`, date, Required: false)
	+ Select the date you would like to receive the mechanical pencils.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, Required: false)
	+ Choose any relevant categories for your request (e.g., "Office Supply", "School Project", etc.).
* **Comment** (`comment`, note, Required: false)
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes for the request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required: false)
	+ Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, Required: false)
	+ Select the person assigned to process the request.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.
* Make sure to select "Yes" or "No" options correctly for the "Form Submission", "Department", and "Assigned To" fields.
* Enter your email address and phone number correctly to ensure that you can be contacted for updates on your request.
