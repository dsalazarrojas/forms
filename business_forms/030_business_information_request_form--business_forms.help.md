# Business Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request information from businesses in a structured and formalized way, ensuring that your requests are properly documented and met with the required confidentiality agreements. Please fill out this form to initiate a request for business information, providing all necessary details and agreements to facilitate a successful and secure exchange of data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Provide your name and the name of your company as the requesting party.
2. Specify the name of the business you are requesting information about.
3. Select the specific information you require (financial statements, credit history, etc.).
4. Indicate the purpose of your request (vendor onboarding, market research, etc.).
5. Choose the urgency level of your request (standard, priority, or urgent).
6. Select the desired format for the information (digital PDF, online portal access, etc.).
7. Set a deadline for receiving the information.
8. Agree to the confidentiality agreement and acknowledge your authority to make this request on behalf of your company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requesting Party --** (Section, note): This section is for the company requesting information.
* **Requesting Company Name** (Text, required): Enter the full name of your company.
* **Requesting Person Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of the person making this request, who is an authorized representative.
* **Professional Title** (Text, required): Enter the title of the person making this request.
* **Target Business and Information --** (Section, note): This section provides details about the business you are requesting information about.
* **Target Company Name** (Text, required): Enter the full name of the business you are requesting information about.
* **Specific Information Required** (Select Multiple, required): Select all relevant information types you require (financial statements, credit history, etc.).
* **Purpose of Request** (Select One, required): Choose the reason for your request (vendor onboarding, partnership evaluation, etc.).
* **Urgency Level** (Select One, required): Choose the urgency level of your request (standard, priority, urgent).
* **Desired Format for Information** (Select One, required): Select the format you prefer for the information (digital PDF, online portal access, etc.).
* **Deadline for Information Receipt** (Date, required): Set a deadline for receiving the information.
* **Confidentiality Agreement --** (Section, note): This section is a reminder of the importance of maintaining confidentiality regarding the received information.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (Select One, required): Acknowledge your agreement to treat all received information as strictly confidential.
* **Authority to Make Request** (Select One, required): Acknowledge your authority to make this request on behalf of your company.
* **Date of Request** (Date, required): This field automatically populates with the current date.

## Tips
* Double-check all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate request.
* Be specific when selecting the information you require to avoid delays or confusion.
* Review the confidentiality agreement carefully before submitting the form.
* Contact us if you have any questions or concerns about this form.
