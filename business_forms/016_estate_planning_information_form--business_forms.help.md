# estate_planning_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The estate_planning_information_form is a tool used to collect data on estate planning details. This form is designed to assist individuals and organizations in gathering and managing essential information on their estate plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form sections and identify the information that applies to your situation.
2. Fill in the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Estate Plan Details (1)
* **Estate Plan Details**: This field is used to provide a brief description of your estate plan. Enter a clear and concise summary of your estate plan details.

### Beneficiaries (2)
* **Beneficiaries**: Select all beneficiaries who will receive assets from your estate. Multiple selections are allowed.
  - Beneficiary 1
  - Beneficiary 2
  - Beneficiary 3

### Assets (3)
* **Assets**: Enter the value of your assets. This can include real estate, investments, and other valuable possessions.

### Estate Documents (4)
* **Estate Documents**: List any relevant estate planning documents, such as wills and trusts. This can be a helpful reference for your estate plan.

### Asset Distribution Plan (5)
* **Asset Distribution Plan**: Select the plan that best suits your needs. This can include options like "Plan 1", "Plan 2", or "Plan 3".

### Asset Values (6)
* **Asset Values**: Enter the value of your assets. This can include real estate, investments, and other valuable possessions.

### Asset Types (7)
* **Asset Types**: Select all types of assets you own, such as real estate, investments, or other valuable possessions. Multiple selections are allowed.
  - Type 1
  - Type 2
  - Type 3

### Asset Locations (8)
* **Asset Locations**: Enter a brief description of the locations of your assets.

### Asset Value Range (9)
* **Asset Value Range**: Enter the range of values for your assets.

### Estate Plan Status (10)
* **Estate Plan Status**: Select the current status of your estate plan. This can include options like "Status 1", "Status 2", or "Status 3".
