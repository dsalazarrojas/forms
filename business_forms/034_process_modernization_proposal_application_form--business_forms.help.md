# Process Modernization Proposal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Modernization Proposal Application Form is designed to collect information from employees on process improvement proposals. The form guides employees through a series of questions to submit a proposal for a process improvement, which can lead to increased efficiency and productivity in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select only one process improvement proposal type that you would like to pursue.
2. Provide a descriptive explanation of the proposed process improvement in the "Description of the Proposed Process Improvement" field.
3. Choose all that apply from the list of options related to the process improvement proposal you selected.
4. Provide a descriptive explanation of the selected options in the "Description of Selected Choices" field.
5. Enter the date and time you created and submitted your proposal.
6. Enter the name of the process you are proposing to improve.
7. Enter the potential impact and costs associated with the process.
8. Provide a descriptive explanation of the benefits and risks associated with the proposal.
9. Select the status of the proposed process.
10. Enter the owner and assignee of the proposed process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page** (`main_page`, text, required): This is the initial page of the form where you will select the type of process improvement proposal you would like to pursue.
* **What Process Improvement Proposal to Pursue?** (`process_choices`, select_one, required): Choose only one process improvement proposal type that you would like to pursue. This will be the focus of the rest of the form.
* **Description of the Proposed Process Improvement** (`process_description`, text, required): Provide a descriptive explanation of the proposed process improvement.
* **Select All That Apply** (`process_choices_1`, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the list of options related to the process improvement proposal you selected.
* **Description of Selected Choices** (`process_description_2`, text, required): Provide a descriptive explanation of the selected options.
* **Date Created** (`date_created`, date, required): Enter the date you created this proposal.
* **Time Created** (`time_created`, time, required): Enter the time you created this proposal.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted this proposal.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, required): Enter the time you submitted this proposal.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Choose who submitted this proposal.
* **Name of Proposed Process** (`process_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the process you are proposing to improve.
* **Potential Impact of the Process** (`process_impact`, number, required): Enter the potential impact of the process improvement.
* **Costs Associated with the Proposed Process** (`process_costs`, number, required): Enter the costs associated with the process improvement.
* **Description of Benefits of the Process** (`process_benefits`, text, required): Provide a descriptive explanation of the benefits associated with the proposed process.
* **Description of Risks Associated** (`process_risks`, text, required): Provide a descriptive explanation of the risks associated with the proposed process.
* **Status of Proposed Process** (`process_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the proposed process.
* **Owner of Proposed Process** (`process_owner`, text, required): Enter the owner of the proposed process.
* **Assignee of Proposed Process** (`process_assignee`, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the list of assignees related to the proposed process.

## Tips

* Please provide a clear and descriptive explanation for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor for clarification.
* Submit only one proposal per form.
* If you have multiple proposals, submit multiple forms.
* Please note that each proposal will be reviewed and evaluated separately.
