# Waiver Of Notice Of Meeting Of The Board Of Directors - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to inform the Board of Directors of a meeting's details when a member will be absent. It allows the absenter to provide a reason for their absence and ensures that the Board of Directors is aware of the meeting's date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Meeting Date" field and select the date of the meeting you will be absent from.
2. Enter your "Full Name" in the text field provided.
3. In the "Reason For Absence" field, provide a brief explanation for your absence from the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date** (Meeting Date, Date, Required/Optional: Not Required): This field is used to select the date of the meeting you will be absent from. Click on the calendar icon to choose the date from the calendar picker.
* **Full Name** (Full Name, Text, Required/Optional: Required): This field is used to enter your full name. Please ensure that it is spelled correctly.
* **Reason For Absence** (Reason For Absence, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): This field is used to provide a brief explanation for your absence from the meeting. Please be as concise as possible.

## Tips
* Be sure to select the correct meeting date to ensure that the Board of Directors is aware of the correct meeting details.
* If you are unable to attend a meeting, it is essential to provide a valid reason for your absence.
* Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
