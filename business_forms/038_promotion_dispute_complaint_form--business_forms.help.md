# Promotion Dispute Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about promotions that have been disputed by a customer. It's intended for customers to provide details about the issue they're experiencing with a promotion, which will be reviewed and addressed by our support team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the type of complaint by choosing an option from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand the nature of the issue.
2. Enter a detailed description of the problem you're experiencing. Please be as concise as possible while still providing enough information for us to understand the issue.
3. If you'd like to be contacted regarding this complaint, provide your email address. This will allow us to reach out to you directly to discuss further.
4. If you'd like to be contacted regarding this complaint, provide your phone number. This will allow us to contact you directly to discuss further.
5. If you have a Promotion ID, please enter it here so we can look up the promotion you're disputing. This will help us to efficiently locate the relevant promotion.
6. Enter the name of the business offering the promotion, so we can identify the company related to the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of complaint is this?** (`complaint_type`, `select_one`): Select the option that best describes the type of issue you're experiencing with the promotion.
* **Description of the complaint** (`description`, `note`): Please provide a detailed account of the problem you're experiencing. This should be as concise as possible, but still clear and understandable.
* **Email address** (`contact_email`, `email`): If you'd like to be contacted about this complaint, enter your email address here. This will allow us to reach out to you directly to discuss further.
* **Phone number** (`contact_phone`, `text`): If you'd like to be contacted about this complaint, enter your phone number here. This will allow us to contact you directly to discuss further.
* **Promotion ID** (`complaint_id`, `number`): If you have a Promotion ID, enter it here so we can look up the promotion you're disputing. This will help us to efficiently locate the relevant promotion.
* **Business name** (`business_name`, `text`): Enter the name of the business offering the promotion, so we can identify the company related to the issue.
