# Business Snap Shot - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect a comprehensive snapshot of your business's current state, including its operational and financial details. It will guide you in providing a clear picture of your company, which can be used for assessments, goal setting, or identifying areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your business name and type.
2. Enter the date you are completing this snapshot.
3. Enter the number of active clients and employees you have.
4. Select your current operational status.
5. Enter your average monthly revenue and revenue growth rate.
6. Select your top three business challenges.
7. Choose your primary business goal.
8. Choose a timeline for achieving your goal.
9. Confirm if your processes are documented.
10. List the technology and tools used in your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Enter your company's name accurately and clearly.
* **Type of Service Business**: Describe the type of services your business provides (e.g., cleaning, landscaping, consulting, etc.).
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date you are completing this snapshot, as this will help track changes and progress over time.
* **Owner Name**: Enter your name accurately and clearly.
* **Years of Industry Experience**: Enter the number of years you have worked in your field.
* **Relevant Education or Training**: If applicable, list any relevant education or training you have received.
* **Current Number of Active Clients**: Enter the number of active clients your business has.
* **Current Number of Employees**: Enter the number of employees, excluding the owner.
* **Current Operational Status**: Choose the current operational status of your business from the options provided.
* **Average Monthly Revenue**: Enter your current earning level.
* **Revenue Growth Rate**: Enter your revenue growth rate over the past year (if applicable).
* **Estimated Profit Margin**: Enter your estimated profit margin (if applicable).
* **Top 3 Business Challenges**: Identify the top three challenges affecting your business.
* **Time Management Challenge**: Choose how much time you spend on non-revenue activities (if applicable).
* **Primary Business Goal**: Choose the primary goal for your business from the options provided.
* **Goal Achievement Timeline**: Choose a target date to achieve your primary goal.
* **Documented Processes**: Confirm if your business processes are documented.
* **Technology and Tools Used**: List any software, tools, or platforms your business relies on for operations.
