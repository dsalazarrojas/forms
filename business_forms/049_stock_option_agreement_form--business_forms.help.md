# Stock Option Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Option Agreement Form is used to collect participant information and agreement terms for a Stock Option plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name in the "participant_name" field.
2. Select your participant role in the "participant_role" field. You can select multiple roles if applicable.
3. Enter your participant email address in the "participant_email" field.
4. Review and agree to the "agreement_terms" field. This is a text area where you can provide additional comments or agreements.
5. If you have already accepted the terms, mark "Yes" in the "participant_acceptance" field.
6. If you wish to sign the agreement, you can use the "participant_sign" field for a note about your signature, or upload a scan of your physical signature in the "participant_signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_name** (text, required): Enter your name, which will be used as a reference for this Stock Option agreement.
* **participant_role** (select_multiple, optional): Select your role in the Stock Option plan. This could be "Employee", "Executive", "Director", etc.
* **participant_email** (email, required): Enter your email address that we will use to communicate with you about the Stock Option plan.
* **agreement_terms** (text, optional): Review and provide any additional comments or agreements related to the Stock Option plan.
* **participant_acceptance** (select_one, optional): If you have already accepted the terms, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No".
* **participant_sign** (note, optional): Use this field to add a note about your signature, or upload a scan of your physical signature.
* **participant_signature** (number, optional): Upload a scan of your physical signature. Please note that this is not required, but it's a good practice for official documentation.
