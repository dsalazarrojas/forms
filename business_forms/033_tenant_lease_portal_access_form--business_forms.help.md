# Tenant Lease Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Lease Portal Access Form is used to request access to the portal by tenants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your lease agreement.
* **Lease ID** (`lease_id`, number, required): Enter the ID number assigned to your lease.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address associated with your lease.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Request Lease Documents** (`lease_documents`, select multiple, required): Check the boxes for which documents you need access to:
	+ 'Yes' to access lease documents.
	+ 'No' to not access lease documents.
* **Request Payments** (`payments`, select multiple, required): Check the boxes for which payments you need access to:
	+ 'Yes' to make payments.
	+ 'No' to not make payments.
* **Request Communications** (`communication`, select multiple, required): Check the boxes for which communications you need access to:
	+ 'Yes' to receive communications.
	+ 'No' to not receive communications.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the landlord or property manager you are working with.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about which documents, payments, or communications you need access to, you can consult with your landlord or property manager.
* Once you submit the form, a member of our team will review your request and follow up with you directly.
