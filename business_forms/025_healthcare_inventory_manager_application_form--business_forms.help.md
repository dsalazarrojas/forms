# healthcare_inventory_manager_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare inventory manager application form is used to track and manage healthcare-related items. This form helps to maintain accurate records of items, suppliers, and stock levels, ensuring smooth inventory operations and efficient ordering processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information about the item.
2. Select the correct options for "Item Status" and "Last Ordered By" as relevant to the item's current state.
3. Enter the correct dates for "Date Ordered," "Last Ordered Date," "Date Received," and "Last Received Date" as applicable.
4. Enter the supplier's contact information, including their email, phone number, and address.
5. Provide details about the item, including item location, category, and subcategory.
6. Add any additional notes or remarks about the supplier and item.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have accurate and up-to-date information about the item, supplier, and stock levels.
* Double-check the options for "Item Status" and "Last Ordered By" to ensure correct selection.
* Enter dates accurately to reflect the correct timeline of the item's ordering and receipt.
* Use the correct email format for the supplier's email address.
* Enter the supplier's phone number in the correct format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* Enter the supplier's address accurately to ensure correct delivery and communication.
* Use clear and concise text for item and supplier notes.
* Review and verify all information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
