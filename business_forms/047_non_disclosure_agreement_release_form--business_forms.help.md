# Non Disclosure Agreement Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the release of confidential information and outline the terms of the release.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Enter the **Party Name**, which should be the name of the party releasing the information.
3. Enter the **Date Agreed**, which should be the date the release is agreed upon.
4. Enter the **Date Released**, which should be the date the release is being made.
5. Enter the **Signature**, which should be the signature of the party releasing the information.
6. Enter the **Signed By**, which should be the title of the party signing the document.
7. Enter the **Date Signed**, which should be the date the signature was made.
8. Enter the **Release Date**, which should be the date the non-disclosure agreement is released.
9. Enter any additional **Notes** if needed.
10. Finally, click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Details**: This field is used to provide additional details about the non-disclosure agreement, but it's not required.
* **Party Name**: Enter the name of the party releasing the information.
* **Date Agreed**: Enter the date the release is agreed upon.
* **Date Released**: Enter the date the release is being made.
* **Confidential Info**: This field is used to provide additional information about the confidential material being released, but it's not required.
* **Release Type**: Enter the type of release (e.g., "full release", "limited release", etc.).
* **Party Details**: Enter any additional details about the party releasing the information, but it's not required.
* **Signature**: Enter the signature of the party releasing the information.
* **Signed By**: Enter the title of the party signing the document.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date the signature was made.
* **Reason Signed**: Enter a reason for signing, but it's not required.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the release, but it's not required.
* **Non-Disclosure Agreement**: This field is used to provide a summary of the non-disclosure agreement, but it's not required.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date the non-disclosure agreement is released.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of the release (if applicable).
* **Last Updated**: The last time this form was updated (not editable).
* **Last User**: The last user who updated this form (not editable).
* **Created By**: The person who created this form (not editable).
* **Created Date**: The date this form was created (not editable).
* **Updated By**: The person who last updated this form (not editable).
* **Updated Date**: The date this form was last updated (not editable).
* **Non-Disclosure Agreement Release**: This field is used to provide a summary of the release, but it's not required.
* **Release Reason**: Enter a reason for release, but it's not required.
* **Signed By Title**: Enter the title of the party signing the document.
