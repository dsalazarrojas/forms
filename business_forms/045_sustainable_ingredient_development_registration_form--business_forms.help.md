# Sustainable Ingredient Development Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Ingredient Development Registration Form is used to register and document sustainable ingredients that meet certain sustainability criteria. This form helps businesses and organizations gather information about sustainable ingredients, including their category, description, price, weight, and origin.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the **Form Information** section, which is located at the top of the form.
2. Select the **Sustainable Category** of the ingredient from the drop-down menu. This is a critical field as it determines the type of sustainable ingredient you are registering.
3. Enter a brief **Description** of the ingredient, including its characteristics and features.
4. If applicable, upload an **Image** of the ingredient for reference.
5. Enter the **Price** of the ingredient, including the currency.
6. Enter the **Weight** of the ingredient, including the unit of measurement (e.g., grams, kilograms, pounds).
7. Enter the **Origin** of the ingredient, providing details about where it comes from.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Information (required: false)**: This field is for administrative purposes only and is not required for the registration process. It can be left blank.
* **Sustainable Category (sustainable_category, required: true)**: This field determines the type of sustainable ingredient you are registering. Choose from the following options:
	+ Cotton
	+ Dye
	+ Fabric
	+ Fiber
	+ Ink
	+ Materials
	+ Other
	+ Semi-metals
	+ Synthetic
* **Description (sustainable_ingredient_description, required: false)**: Enter a brief description of the sustainable ingredient, including its characteristics and features.
* **Image (sustainable_ingredient_image, required: false)**: If you have an image of the ingredient, upload it here for reference.
* **Price (sustainable_ingredient_price, required: false)**: Enter the price of the sustainable ingredient, including the currency.
* **Currency (sustainable_ingredient_price_currency, required: true)**: Select the currency of the ingredient's price from the drop-down menu.
* **Weight (sustainable_ingredient_weight, required: false)**: Enter the weight of the sustainable ingredient.
* **Weight Unit (sustainable_ingredient_weight_unit, required: true)**: Select the unit of measurement for the weight (e.g., grams, kilograms, pounds).
* **Origin (sustainable_ingredient_origin, required: false)**: Enter the origin of the sustainable ingredient, providing details about where it comes from.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* Ensure that you select the correct category and currency for the ingredient.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant team members or refer to the form's documentation.

Please note that this form is used for registration and documentation purposes only. If you have any further questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or support team.
