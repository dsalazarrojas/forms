# Restroom Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restroom Rental Agreement Form is used to document the agreement made between the renter and the lessor for the use of a restroom facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:
1. Select the appropriate option from the "Approval" field. This field is required to ensure that both parties are in agreement about the terms of the rental.
2. Enter the date range of the rental period in the "Rental Dates" field. This field is where you will enter the start and end dates of the rental period.
3. Enter the location where the restroom is located in the "Location" field.
4. Add any relevant notes about the rental in the "Notes" field.
5. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Select the current status of the agreement from the "Agreement Status" field.
8. Enter any terms and conditions that apply to the rental in the "Terms and Conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rental Information (Rental Dates)**: This field is where you will enter the start and end dates of the rental period. Enter the dates in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Location**: This field is where you will enter the location of the restroom that is being rented.
* **Approval**: This field is where you will select the level of approval that has been given for the rental. This ensures that both parties are in agreement about the terms of the rental.
* **Notes**: This field is where you can add any additional information about the rental, such as any specific conditions or requirements.
* **Contact Information**: This field is where you will provide your contact information, including any relevant details that need to be known by the lessor.
* **Email**: This field is where you will enter your email address, which will be used for communication between the renter and the lessor.
* **Agreement Status**: This field is where you will select the current status of the agreement, including the options for "Option 1", "Option 2", and "Option 3".
* **Terms and Conditions**: This field is where you will enter any specific terms and conditions that apply to the rental.
* **Terms and Conditions**: This field is not actually required, but it's a good place to include any important details about the rental.
