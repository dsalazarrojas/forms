# Business Information Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Information Intake Form is a tool used to collect essential details from prospective clients, enabling us to understand their business needs and challenges. This form is not intended for general information gathering or marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your prospective client's name in the "Prospective Client Name" field.
2. Provide your business's name in the "Business Legal Name" field.
3. Select your primary industry sector from the "Industry Sector" dropdown menu.
4. Choose your current employee count from the "Current Employee Count" dropdown menu.
5. Describe the primary business challenge you'd like us to help with in the "Primary Business Challenge" field.
6. Select all relevant goals for the consulting engagement in the "Goals for Consulting Engagement" field.
7. Choose your current annual revenue bracket from the "Current Annual Revenue Bracket" dropdown menu.
8. If applicable, provide a link to your current marketing materials in the "Link to Current Marketing Materials" field.
9. Select your preferred contact method in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
10. If applicable, mention the source of your referral in the "Referral Source" field.
11. Finally, ensure you provide a submission date in the "Submission Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prospective Client Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the prospective client.
* **Business Legal Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your business's name.
* **Industry Sector** (`name`, `select_one`, required): Select your primary industry sector.
* **Current Employee Count** (`name`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current employee count.
* **Primary Business Challenge** (`name`, `text`, required): Describe the main problem you'd like us to help with.
* **Goals for Consulting Engagement** (`name`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant goals for the consulting engagement.
* **Current Annual Revenue Bracket** (`name`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current annual revenue bracket.
* **Target Completion Date** (`name`, `date`, optional): If applicable, provide your target completion date for the project.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`name`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred contact method.
* **Referral Source** (`name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, mention the source of your referral.
* **Link to Current Marketing Materials** (`name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide a link to your current marketing materials.
* **Submission Date** (`name`, `date`, required): Ensure you provide a submission date for the form.
