# Toll Fee Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by businesses to claim toll fees for various operations. Please submit your claim with accurate and relevant information to ensure timely processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your business name and address in the designated fields.
2. Enter your name and address as the claimant.
3. Select the date you incurred the toll fee.
4. Enter the amount of the toll fee you are claiming.
5. Provide a brief description of the toll fee.
6. Select the tool used for the operation (e.g. Tool1, Tool2, Tool3).
7. Select the users who submitted the claim (e.g. User1, User2, User3).
8. Enter the date and time the claim was submitted.
9. Enter the date the claim was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the claimant.
* **Claimant Address** (`claimant_address`, text, required: false): Enter your business address.
* **Date** (`toll_fee_date`, date, required: false): Select the date you incurred the toll fee.
* **Amount** (`toll_fee_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of the toll fee you are claiming.
* **Description** (`toll_fee_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the toll fee.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): Enter your business name.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required: false): Enter your business address.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Select the tool used for the operation (e.g. Tool1, Tool2, Tool3).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the users who submitted the claim (e.g. User1, User2, User3).
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, time, required: false): Select the time the claim was submitted.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the claim was submitted.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your internal business operations documentation.
* Please submit your claim in a timely manner to avoid delays in processing.
