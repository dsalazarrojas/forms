# inventory_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about products in inventory, including products and their quantities at various locations. The form will help to manage and track inventory levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **product list**: Enter a list of products. You can choose from a variety of options, including Apple, Banana, Cherry, Date, Fig, Orange, and Pear.
2.  **quantity**: Enter the quantity of the selected product.
3.  **location**: Enter the location where the product is currently stored.
4.  **product**: Select a product from the list to add to the inventory.
5.  **amount**: Enter the quantity of the selected product.
6.  **product list 2**: Enter another list of products, which is not required.
7.  **product**: Select multiple products to add to the inventory.
8.  **quantity 2**: Enter the quantity of the selected products.
9.  **location 2**: Enter the location where the selected products are stored.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **product list**: This field is used to enter a list of products. Select up to one product from the options provided.
* **quantity**: This field is used to enter the quantity of the selected product. Enter a numerical value.
* **location**: This field is used to enter the location where the product is currently stored. Enter the location name.
* **product**: This field is used to select a product to add to the inventory. Select up to one product from the options provided.
* **amount**: This field is used to enter the quantity of the selected product. Enter a numerical value.
* **product list 2**: This field is used to enter another list of products. It is not required, but can be useful for additional product information.
* **product**: This field is used to select multiple products to add to the inventory. Select up to one product from the options provided.
* **quantity 2**: This field is used to enter the quantity of the selected products. Enter a numerical value.
* **location 2**: This field is used to enter the location where the selected products are stored. Enter the location name.
* **quantity 2** is actually a redundant field, as it seems to be a duplicate of the quantity field.
*   It is highly recommended to review the provided fields carefully and only enter the relevant information.
*   If a field is not relevant to your task, please do not enter any information in it.
*   If you are unsure about a field, please ask for assistance.
