## Business Internet Quote Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Internet Quote Form is designed to collect information about a business's internet connectivity needs. This information is used to generate a quote for the business's internet services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Business Identity and Location section with your business name and installation address.
2. Provide information about your business's internet service requirements, including desired speed tier and required features.
3. Enter your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
4. Set a date by which you would like the quote to be generated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business. (required)
* **Installation Address**: Enter your business's address, including street, city, state, and zip code. (required)
* **Current Internet Provider**: If you have an existing internet service, enter the name of your current provider. (optional)
* **Number of Users**: Enter the total number of users requiring internet access at your business. (required)
* **Desired Speed Tier**: Select the desired speed tier for your business based on your bandwidth needs. (required)
* **Required Features**: Select all features that you require for your business. (required)
* **Contract Length Preference**: Select the contract length preference for your business. (required)
* **Current Monthly Spend (USD)**: Enter your current monthly spend on internet services. (optional)
* **Primary Contact Name**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for your business. (required)
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person. (required)
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the primary contact person. (required)
* **Date by which Quote is Needed**: Enter the date by which you would like the quote to be generated. (required)

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to ensure a proper quote is generated.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact us.
