# Annual Report Submission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Report Submission Request Form is a template for requesting submission of reports from department heads and units. The form is used to collect information about the report details, submission requirements, and preferences for department heads. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the report is submitted on time and meets the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Department or Division Name field with the name of the submitting unit.
2. Enter the name of the Department Head.
3. Provide the Department Head's contact email address.
4. Enter the Department Head's phone number (optional).
5. Fill out the Report Submission Contact field with the name of the person submitting the report.
6. Enter the Contact Email address for the report submission.
7. Select the Reporting Fiscal Year being reported.
8. Specify the Reporting Period covered in the report.
9. Choose the Type of Report (e.g., Financial, Operational, Performance, etc.).
10. Choose the Report Format (e.g., PDF, Word document, Excel spreadsheet, etc.).
11. Enter the Estimated Report Length (if applicable).
12. Specify whether Key metrics and KPIs are included in the report.
13. Choose whether a comparison with the prior year is included.
14. Select whether a Budget vs Actual analysis is included.
15. Choose whether an Executive Summary or Highlights is included.
16. Specify whether Challenges and obstacles are discussed.
17. Choose whether a Future Outlook and plans are included.
18. Enter the Preferred Submission Deadline.
19. Enter the Earliest Available Submission Date.
20. Specify whether an extension of the submission deadline is needed.
21. Enter a reason for the anticipated extension (if needed).
22. Choose the Preferred Submission Method.
23. Add any Additional Notes or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department or Division Name** (1): Name of the submitting unit.
* **Department Head Name** (2): Person responsible for the report.
* **Department Head Email** (3): Contact email of the Department Head.
* **Department Head Phone** (4): Contact phone number of the Department Head.
* **Report Submission Contact** (5): Person submitting the report.
* **Contact Email** (6): Email for correspondence.
* **Reporting Fiscal Year** (7): Year being reported.
* **Reporting Period** (8): Specific dates covered in the report.
* **Type of Report** (9): Choose the type of report (e.g., Financial, Operational, Performance, etc.).
* **Report Format** (10): Choose the format of the report (e.g., PDF, Word document, Excel spreadsheet, etc.).
* **Estimated Report Length** (11): Number of pages in the report.
* **Key metrics and KPIs included** (12): Specify whether key metrics and KPIs are included.
* **Comparison with prior year** (13): Choose whether a comparison with the prior year is included.
* **Budget vs Actual analysis included** (14): Specify whether a budget vs actual analysis is included.
* **Executive summary or highlights** (15): Choose whether an executive summary or highlights are included.
* **Challenges and obstacles discussed** (16): Specify whether challenges and obstacles are discussed.
* **Future outlook and plans** (17): Choose whether future outlook and plans are included.
* **Preferred Submission Deadline** (18): Enter the preferred submission deadline.
* **Earliest Available Submission Date** (19): Enter the earliest available submission date.
* **May need extension** (20): Specify whether an extension is needed.
* **Reason for extension** (21): Enter a reason for the anticipated extension.
* **Preferred Submission Method** (22): Choose the preferred submission method (e.g., Email, Shared drive or portal, In-person delivery, etc.).
* **Additional Notes or requests** (23): Add any additional notes or requests.
