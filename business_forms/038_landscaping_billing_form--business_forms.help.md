# Landscaping Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landscaping Billing Form is used to record the details of landscaping services provided to clients, including client information, services rendered, and payment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Client Name in the first field.
2. Select the Invoice Date from the calendar.
3. Enter the Amount Due for the services rendered.
4. Select the services rendered by choosing one or more options from the Services Rendered dropdown list.
5. Choose the Billing Method from the dropdown list.
6. Enter the Contact Number of the client, if available.
7. Enter the Email of the client, if available.
8. Enter the Client Address, if available.
9. Choose the Payment Status from the dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name**: A text field to enter the name of the client.
- **Invoice Date**: A date field to select the date of the invoice.
- **Amount Due**: A numeric field to enter the amount due for the services rendered.
- **Services Rendered**: A dropdown list of services rendered, select one or more options.
  - Select "Service A" for lawn care.
  - Select "Service B" for tree trimming.
  - Select "Service C" for landscape installation.
- **Billing Method**: A dropdown list of billing methods, select one option:
  - Option 1 for online payment.
  - Option 2 for bank transfer.
  - Option 3 for check.
- **Contact Number**: A text field to enter the contact number of the client.
- **Email**: A text field to enter the email of the client.
- **Client Address**: A text field to enter the client address.
- **Payment Status**: A dropdown list to select the payment status:
  - Pending for payments not yet made.
  - Paid for payments made.
  - Unpaid for payments yet to be made.
