# vaginal_steam_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of the vaginal_steam_intake_form is to collect information about a patient's vaginal steam habits. This form gathers data about the duration, frequency, and any potential medical conditions related to the patient's steam activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill in your **Patient Name**.
* Answer the questions about your **steam duration**, **steam frequency**, and **medical conditions**.
* If you have any **steam-related notes**, please feel free to include them.
* Enter your **contact information** (email and phone number) and your **doctor's contact information** (name, email, and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: This is where you enter your name as a patient. Please use your full name as it appears on your medical records.
* **Steam Duration**: This field is asking about the length of time you spend steamming each time. Please enter the duration in the format of a number.
* **Steam Frequency**: This field asks about how often you steam. Please select one of the options provided (Daily, Weekly, Monthly) that best describes your steam frequency.
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions related to your steam activities, please select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Steam Frequency**: This field is asking about how many times you steam each day. Please enter a number.
* **Time of Day**: This field is asking about the time of day when you usually steam. Please enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Date**: This field is asking for the date you completed this form. Please enter the date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Doctor Name**: This field is for you to enter the name of your doctor. Please enter the doctor's name as it appears on your medical records.
* **Notes**: This field is for any additional steam-related notes or information you'd like to share with your doctor.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number.
* **Doctor Name**: This field is for you to enter the name of your doctor. Please enter the doctor's name as it appears on your medical records.
* **Doctor Email**: Please enter your doctor's email address.
* **Doctor Phone**: Please enter your doctor's phone number.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your doctor or medical staff.
* This form is for your medical records, so please make sure to keep it confidential.
