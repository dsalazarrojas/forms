# software_development_process_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the effectiveness of a software development process in place within an organization. It assesses various aspects such as team organization, communication, and adaptability to changes in project scope, timeline, resources, and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question based on your experience and knowledge of the software development process in your organization. Please select the most accurate option for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **What is the overall project goal** (`software_development_process_audit_form_1`): <i>Required</i>
This question asks about the overall objective of the project. Please select an option that best describes the project's main goal.

*   **High**: The project goal is clearly defined and well-understood by all team members.
*   **Medium**: The project goal is partially defined and understood by most team members.
*   **Low**: The project goal is not well-defined or understood by team members.

### 2. **Is the software development process well-defined** (`software_development_process_audit_form_2`): <i>Required</i>
This question assesses the level of definition and organization of the software development process.

*   **True**: The process is clearly defined and organized.
*   **False**: The process is not well-defined or disorganized.
