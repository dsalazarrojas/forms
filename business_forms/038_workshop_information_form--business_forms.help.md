# Workshop Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Information Form is a simple form designed to collect workshop details and contact information for the event organizer. This form is meant to be completed by workshop organizers or administrators to provide a centralized location to store and share information about upcoming workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the workshop in the "Event Name" field.
2. Select the type of workshop from the "Type of Workshop" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the start and end times of the workshop in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
4. Enter the workshop location in the "Workshop Location" field.
5. Enter the date of the workshop in YYYY-MM-DD format.
6. Enter the name and contact information of the event organizer, including their phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Information** (`workshop_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is a general description of the workshop. You can enter a brief summary of the workshop.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the workshop.
* **Type of Workshop** (`workshop_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the type of workshop (Presentation, Training, or Workshop).
* **Workshop Start Time** (`workshop_start_time`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the start time of the workshop in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Workshop End Time** (`workshop_end_time`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the end time of the workshop in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the workshop in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Phone** (`event_organizer_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Email** (`event_organizer_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email of the event organizer.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter all required information to ensure accurate and complete workshop information.
- Use the "Type of Workshop" dropdown menu to select the correct type of workshop.
- Use the 24-hour format (HH:MM) for time fields.
- Use the YYYY-MM-DD format for date fields.
- Enter accurate and up-to-date information for the event organizer's contact details.
