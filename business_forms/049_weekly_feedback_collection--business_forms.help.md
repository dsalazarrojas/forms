# weekly_feedback_collection - Help Guide
## Purpose

The weekly_feedback_collection form is used to gather user feedback on their experiences with our services. It provides a structured format for users to provide their thoughts and suggestions on topics, satisfaction, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the "What are you currently working on" field with a brief summary of your current project or task.
2. Provide any additional comments or thoughts in the "Any additional comments" field if you have any.
3. Rate your satisfaction with our services by selecting one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
4. If you have any other feedback on your satisfaction, you can specify it in the "If other please specify" field.
5. Provide any suggestions on how we can improve in the "How can we improve" field.
6. Enter your email address for internal use.
7. Enter your phone number for internal use.
8. Add any weekly suggestions you have in the "Any weekly suggestions" field.
9. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.
10. Review and edit if necessary the "Weekly Feedback Collection Form" text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are you currently working on**: A text box where you can input the name and description of the current project or task you are working on.
* **Any additional comments**: A text box where you can provide any additional comments or thoughts you have.
* **What did you enjoy about our services**: A dropdown menu with four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **If other please specify**: A text box where you can provide any other feedback on your satisfaction.
* **How can we improve**: A text box where you can provide any suggestions on how we can improve.
* **Email**: A text box where you can enter your email address for internal use.
* **Phone**: A text box where you can enter your phone number for internal use.
* **Any weekly suggestions**: A text box where you can add any weekly suggestions you have.
* **Weekly Feedback Collection Form**: A text box for you to review and edit the form's text content.
* **Category**: A dropdown menu where you can select one or more categories: Business Forms, User Forms, or Product Forms.
* **Submit**: A button to complete and submit the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in each field.
* Use the "What are you currently working on" field to keep track of your current projects and tasks.
* Provide clear and concise feedback in the "Any additional comments" field.
* Be specific with your satisfaction rating and provide explanations in the "If other please specify" field if necessary.
* Use the "Any weekly suggestions" field to share any ideas or proposals you have.
* Review and edit the "Weekly Feedback Collection Form" text box for accuracy.
* Select the correct category for your feedback in the "Category" field.
* Submit the form completely before closing it to ensure that all required fields are filled.
