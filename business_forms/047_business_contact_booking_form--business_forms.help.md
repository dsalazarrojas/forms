# Business Contact Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from visitors and prospects who want to book appointments with the company. Please fill it out to schedule meetings, consultations, or other business-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, carefully read each field explanation below to understand what's required and why.
2.  Fill out the form fields one by one, following the instructions for each section.
3.  Make sure to submit the form once you've completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for contact and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Provide your contact phone number to ensure smooth communication.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): If you're representing an organization, enter its name.
* **Position** (`position`, `text`, optional): Specify your job title or role within the company.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of service you need, such as General Consultation or Product Demo.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required): Select a date for the meeting that suits you best.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, required): Choose a time slot for the meeting that works for you.
* **Meeting Format** (`meeting_format`, `select_one`, required): Decide how you'd like to meet, e.g., In-Person, Video Conference, or Phone Call.
* **Expected Duration** (`duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose the meeting duration from 15 minutes to 2 hours.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Suggest a location for the meeting, if needed.
* **Purpose of Meeting** (`purpose`, `text`, required): Clearly state the purpose of the meeting.
* **Number of Attendees** (`attendees`, `number`, required): Specify how many people will be attending the meeting.
* **Attendee Names** (`attendee_names`, `text`, optional): If there are other attendees, list their names.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific needs, such as equipment or materials, describe them.
* **Urgency** (`urgency`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how soon you need the meeting, e.g., Within 1 Week, Within 3 Days, or ASAP.
* **Alternative Dates** (`alternative_dates`, `text`, optional): Suggest alternative dates if the preferred date and time aren't possible.
* **Reminder Preference** (`reminder_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you'd like to be reminded, e.g., Email, SMS, or Phone Call.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard_about_us`, `select_one`, optional): Specify where you learned about the company or service.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional information that might be useful for the meeting.

By following this guide, you'll be able to complete the form accurately and provide the necessary information to schedule a successful meeting with the company.
