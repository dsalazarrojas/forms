# Safety Suggestion Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Safety Suggestion Form is a simple and easy-to-use tool for employees to report safety suggestions, concerns, and proposed solutions to promote a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Provide a clear and concise suggestion, concern, or proposed solution in the corresponding field.
3. Make sure to fill in all fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Suggestion** (`employee_suggestion`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your safety suggestion, concern, or proposed solution.
* **Safety Concern** (`safety_concern`, text, optional): If you've identified a safety concern, describe it in this field.
* **Proposed Solutions** (`proposed_solutions`, text, optional): If you have ideas for solving the safety concern, share them here.
* **Risk Area** (`risk_area`, text, optional): If you've identified a specific risk area, provide more details about it.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Your name is required to provide context and accountability.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Your employee ID number (if available).
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): The department you're reporting from (if available).
* **Date Suggestion Made** (`date_suggestion_made`, date, optional): The date you made this suggestion.
* **Time Suggestion Made** (`time_suggestion_made`, time, optional): The time you made this suggestion.

## Tips
* Please be specific and clear in your submissions to facilitate efficient review and analysis.
* Do not repeat submissions; each form is a new submission.
* If you're unsure about a field, ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* This form is designed to be user-friendly, but if you have any questions or need help, feel free to reach out!
