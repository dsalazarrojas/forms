# Project Expense Reimbursement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Project Expense Reimbursement Form is a tool used to track and record expenses associated with projects. It helps to keep track of individual expenses, their date, amount, type, and status of reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the project expense type (Project-related expenses, Miscellaneous expenses, or Other expenses).
2. Enter the date of the expense (optional).
3. Enter the amount of the expense.
4. Enter a brief description of the expense (optional).
5. If applicable, attach a receipt or specify that no receipt is attached.
6. Specify whether a receipt is attached or not.
7. Enter the date when the expense was assigned to someone (optional).
8. Specify the status of the reimbursement (Reimbursed, Not Reimbursed, or Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **project_expense_reimbursement_form** (`project_expense_reimbursement_form`, text, optional): This field is the main heading of the form and is not required.
* **date_of_expense** (`date_of_expense`, date, optional): Enter the date of the expense if it occurred. This field is optional and allows you to track when the expense happened.
* **amount_expensed** (`amount_expensed`, number, required): Enter the total amount of the expense. This is a required field as it helps to calculate the total amount reimbursed.
* **expense_type** (`expense_type`, select multiple, optional): Choose the category of the expense (Project-related expenses, Miscellaneous expenses, or Other expenses). This helps to differentiate the type of expenses.
* **expense_description** (`expense_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the expense. This field is optional and allows for a more detailed explanation of the expense.
* **expense_date** (`expense_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the expense was incurred. This field is optional and is used to record the date of the expense.
* **expense_time** (`expense_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the expense. This field is optional and helps to track the time of the expense.
* **receipt_attachment** (`receipt_attachment`, select multiple, optional): Specify whether a receipt is attached or not. This field helps to determine if a receipt is available for verification.
* **receipt_url** (`receipt_url`, select multiple, optional): Specify whether a receipt URL is attached or not. This field is used to provide a link to the receipt.
* **submitted_by** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **assigned_to** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or department assigned to handle the expense.
* **assigned_date** (`assigned_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the expense was assigned to someone. This field is optional and helps to track the assignment date.
* **status** (`status`, select one, optional): Choose the status of the reimbursement (Reimbursed, Not Reimbursed, or Pending). This field helps to track the status of the expense reimbursement.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Make sure to attach receipts or provide a URL to receipts for expenses over $100.
* Enter accurate information to ensure efficient tracking and processing of expenses.

Each section explains how to complete each field, and the field-by-field explanations provide a detailed breakdown of each field. By following these steps, users should be able to fill out the form efficiently and accurately.
