# Smart Process Automation Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Process Automation Contact Form is designed to collect contact information from users interested in smart process automation solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the first field labeled "contact_form_1" and enter your name.
2. Next, click on the second field labeled "contact_form_2" and enter your email address.
3. Then, click on the third field labeled "contact_form_3" and provide a brief description of your inquiry.
4. Finally, click on the fourth field labeled "contact_form_4" and enter any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contact_form_1** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **contact_form_2** (`Email`, text, required): Enter your business email address.
* **contact_form_3** (`Description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your inquiry, including any relevant details about your project or requirements.
* **contact_form_4** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have that are not already included in the description field.
