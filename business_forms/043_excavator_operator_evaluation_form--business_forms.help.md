# Excavator Operator Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Excavator Operator Evaluation Form is designed to assess the performance and skills of an excavator operator, ensuring they can operate and maintain equipment safely and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of excavator you operate from the options: CAT, CRAWLER, or BACKHOE.
2. Enter the number of experience hours you have as an excavator operator.
3. Select the type of excavator equipment you use from the options.
4. Indicate the current condition of your equipment, choosing from Good, Fair, or Poor.
5. Describe the work site environment you operate in, selecting from Urban, Rural, or Coastal.
6. Rate your excavator operator rating as 1-3, 4-6, or 7-10.
7. Indicate your training status, choosing from Completed, In Progress, or None.
8. Describe the safety equipment you use, selecting from Available, Missing, or Damaged.
9. Select the safety procedures you adhere to, follow, or comply with, including Adhere to, Follow, or Comply.
10. Indicate if you report incidents, choosing from True or False.
11. Select the reporting procedures you adhere to, follow, or comply with, including Adhere to, Follow, or Comply.
12. Indicate your training program status, choosing from Completed, In Progress, or None.
13. Describe the condition of your safety equipment, selecting from Good, Fair, or Poor.
14. Select the work site supervisor's availability, absence, or damage, including Available, Missing, or Damaged.
15. Rate your excavator operator attitude as Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
16. Select the reporting frequency, choosing from Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
17. Indicate the certification type, choosing from Completed, In Progress, or None.
18. Select the excavator technical issues you have, including Resolved, Not Resolved, or None.
19. Enter the number of hours you operate the excavator.
20. Rate your average excavator operator rating on a scale of 1-10.
21. Select the rating scale for your excavator operator rating, choosing from 1-3, 4-6, or 7-10.
22. Enter any comments or feedback regarding your evaluation.
23. Enter the name of the user assigned to you for evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Excavator Type**: Select the type of excavator you operate from the options provided. This will help assessors understand the type of equipment you are familiar with.
* **Experience Hours**: Enter the number of hours you have spent operating an excavator. This will help evaluate your level of experience.
* **Excavator Equipment**: Select the type of excavator equipment you use from the options. This will help assessors understand your familiarity with the equipment.
* **Equipment Condition**: Describe the condition of your equipment, selecting from Good, Fair, or Poor. This will help assessors understand the state of your equipment.
* **Work Site Environment**: Describe the environment where you operate the excavator, selecting from Urban, Rural, or Coastal. This will help assessors understand the external factors affecting your work.
* **Excavator Operator Rating**: Rate your own performance as an excavator operator on a scale of 1-10. This will help assessors evaluate your overall competence.
* **Training Status**: Indicate your current training status, selecting from Completed, In Progress, or None. This will help assessors understand your training level.
* **Safety Equipment**: Describe the safety equipment you use, selecting from Available, Missing, or Damaged. This will help assessors understand your adherence to safety protocols.
* **Safety Procedures**: Select the safety procedures you adhere to, follow, or comply with, including Adhere to, Follow, or Comply. This will help assessors evaluate your adherence to safety protocols.
* **Reporting Incidents**: Indicate if you report incidents, choosing from True or False. This will help assessors understand your incident reporting habits.
* **Reporting Procedures**: Select the procedures you follow for reporting, including Adhere to, Follow, or Comply. This will help assessors evaluate your reporting habits.
* **Training Programs**: Indicate your training program status, selecting from Completed, In Progress, or None. This will help assessors understand your training level.
* **Safety Equipment Condition**: Describe the condition of your safety equipment, selecting from Good, Fair, or Poor. This will help assessors understand your adherence to safety protocols.
* **Work Site Supervisor**: Select the availability of your work site supervisor, selecting from Available, Missing, or Damaged. This will help assessors understand the support you receive.
* **Excavator Operator Attitude**: Rate your attitude as an excavator operator, selecting from Positive, Negative, or Neutral. This will help assessors understand your work attitude.
* **Reporting Frequency**: Select the reporting frequency, choosing from Daily, Weekly, or Monthly. This will help assessors understand your reporting habits.
* **Certification Type**: Indicate your certification type, selecting from Completed, In Progress, or None. This will help assessors understand your certification level.
* **Excavator Technical Issues**: Select the technical issues you have, including Resolved, Not Resolved, or None. This will help assessors understand any technical challenges you face.
* **Excavator Operation Hours**: Enter the number of hours you operate the excavator. This will help assessors understand your operating hours.
* **Excavator Operator Rating Average**: Rate your average operator rating on a scale of 1-10. This will help assessors evaluate your overall performance.
* **Excavator Operator Rating Scale**: Select the rating scale for your operator rating, choosing from 1-3, 4-6, or 7-10. This will help assessors understand your rating.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback regarding your evaluation. This will help assessors provide constructive feedback.
* **Assigned User**: Enter the name of the user assigned to you for evaluation. This will help assessors contact you.
