# funding-application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a funding application, which is likely related to loan or investment proposals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone.
2. Provide information about the loan, including Loan Type, Loan Amount, Loan Term, and Loan Rate.
3. Share your financial information, including Credit Score, Income, Assets, and Liabilities.
4. Specify your employment status and details, including Employment Type, Years Employed, and Experience.
5. Add any additional comments or notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or institution (if applicable).
- **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
- **Loan Type** (`loan_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of loan you are applying for (e.g., mortgage, investment).
- **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required): Enter the loan amount you are requesting.
- **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, select_one, required): Choose the loan term (e.g., short, medium, long).
- **Loan Rate** (`loan_rate`, number, required): Enter the loan rate (interest rate) for your loan.
- **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, number, required): Enter your credit score.
- **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current employment status (e.g., employed, unemployed).
- **Income** (`income`, number, required): Enter your income.
- **Assets** (`assets`, text, required): List your assets.
- **Liabilities** (`liabilities`, text, required): List your liabilities.
- **Income Source** (`income_source`, select_multiple, required): Choose your income source (e.g., salary, investment).
- **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, required): Choose your employment type (e.g., full-time, part-time).
- **Years Employed** (`years_employed`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been employed.
- **Experience** (`experience`, number, required): Enter your relevant experience.
- **Education** (`education`, select_multiple, required): Choose your education level (e.g., high-school, college).
- **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of property you are applying for a loan for (e.g., residential, commercial).
- **Property Value** (`property_value`, number, required): Enter the value of the property you are applying for a loan for.
- **Loan Amount Recommended** (`loan_amount_recommended`, number, optional): Enter the loan amount recommended by a financial advisor.
- **Loan Rate Recommended** (`loan_rate_recommended`, number, optional): Enter the loan rate recommended by a financial advisor.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Enter accurate and truthful information to ensure a smooth evaluation process.
- Review and understand the implications of providing incomplete or false information.
