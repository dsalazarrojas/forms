# Strategic Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strategic Planning Form is designed to help business stakeholders create a comprehensive strategic plan for our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read this help guide to understand each field's purpose.
2. Answer each question carefully, ensuring that your responses align with our organization's existing strategic plans and goals.
3. Fill in the form with your input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Long Term Goals
* **Long Term Goals** (`long_term_goals`, text, optional): 
	+ Briefly describe our organization's long-term goals and objectives. This will help us understand the direction and vision for our future endeavors.

### Strategies
* **Strategies** (`strategies`, text, optional): 
	+ Describe the high-level strategies that will be used to achieve our long-term goals.

### Goals and Objectives
* **Goals and Objectives** (`goals_objectives`, select_multiple, optional): 
	+ Select all the goals and objectives that align with our long-term goals and strategies. Choose 'Yes' if the goal is relevant and 'No' if it is not.

### Target Audience
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, optional): 
	+ Specify the target audience for this strategic plan. This will help us tailor our approach and communication to effectively engage them.

### Timeline
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, date, optional): 
	+ Provide a rough estimate of when we expect to achieve our strategic objectives. This could be a specific date or a range of dates (e.g., Q1 2024).
