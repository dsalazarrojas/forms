# Bakery Facebook Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bakery Facebook Order Form is a tool designed for customers to place orders for various baked goods from your bakery. This form allows customers to specify their preferred products, quantities, and delivery details, ensuring seamless ordering and payment processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required customer information: **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**. These details are mandatory.
2.  Select the **Product Type** from the available options: **Cake**, **Cupcakes**, **Cookies**, **Pastries**, **Bread**, or **Dessert Bundle**.
3.  If choosing **Cake** or **Cupcakes**, you will also have to choose a **Cake Type** and **Cake Size**.
4.  Enter the **Number of Servings Needed** for your selected product.
5.  Specify the **Quantity** of the product you want to order.
6.  If you have any **Flavor Preferences**, **Frosting Type**, or **Decoration or Design Request**, provide them in the respective fields.
7.  Select the **Delivery Method** from the available options: **Home Delivery**, **Store Pickup**, or **Customer Arrangement**.
8.  If selecting **Home Delivery**, enter your **Delivery Address**.
9.  Choose a **Payment Method** from the options: **PayPal**, **Credit Card**, **Debit Card**, or **Cash on Delivery**.
10. Confirm your order by selecting **I Confirm**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Customer Information:**
    *   **Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification.
    *   **Email Address**: Your email address where we can contact you for order updates.
    *   **Phone Number**: Your contact number for any last-minute inquiries.
*   **Product Selection:**
    *   **Product Type**: Choose the type of baked goods you want to order (e.g., **Cake**, **Cupcakes**, **Cookies**, etc.).
*   **Cake Specifics (if applicable):**
    *   **Cake Type**: Select the type of cake you want (e.g., **Chocolate**, **Vanilla**, **Red Velvet**, etc.).
    *   **Cake Size**: Choose the size of the cake you want to order.
*   **Order Details:**
    *   **Number of Servings Needed**: Specify the number of servings you require.
    *   **Quantity**: Enter the total number of items you want to order.
*   **Customization:**
    *   **Flavor Preferences**: Mention any special flavors or combinations you prefer.
    *   **Frosting Type**: Choose the type of frosting you want (e.g., **Buttercream**, **Cream Cheese**, etc.).
    *   **Decoration or Design Request**: Specify any special decoration or design you have in mind.
*   **Dietary Requirements**: Mention any dietary restrictions or preferences (e.g., **Vegan**, **Gluten-free**).
*   **Delivery Details:**
    *   **Delivery Method**: Select how you want to receive your order (e.g., **Home Delivery**, **Store Pickup**, etc.).
    *   **Delivery Address**: Enter your address if choosing **Home Delivery**.
*   **Payment Information:**
    *   **Estimated Price Range**: Get an estimated price range for your order.
    *   **Payment Method**: Choose how you want to pay for your order (e.g., **PayPal**, **Credit Card**, etc.).
*   **Order Confirmation:**
    *   **I Confirm This Order and Accept Our Terms**: Confirm your order and accept our terms and conditions.

Note: This form is designed for customers to place orders for baked goods from your bakery. Please fill it out carefully to ensure accurate order processing.
