# identity_badge_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Identity Badge Request Form is used to request a new badge for employees in your organization. This form captures essential information to process the request and ensure the badge is issued correctly. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Job Title, Date Of Birth, Date Requested, Employee ID, Badge Type, Supervisor Approval, Date Badge Issued, Badge Number, and Reason Denied.
2. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for Department and Badge Type.
3. If you are a supervisor, select 'Yes' or 'No' for Supervisor Approval and Department Head Approval.
4. Enter your name in Supervisor Name and Employee Signature fields.
5. Enter your supervisor's name in Supervisor Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Job Title** (Text, Optional): Enter your job title or position in the company.
* **Department** (Select One, Optional): Select 'Yes' if you are requesting the badge for a new employee in your department, otherwise select 'No'.
* **Date Of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 02/15/1990).
* **Date Requested** (Date, Required): Enter the date you are requesting the badge (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Employee ID** (Number, Required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Badge Type** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the type of badge you are requesting (e.g., ID, Access, etc.).
* **Supervisor Approval** (Select One, Optional): Select 'Yes' if your supervisor has approved the badge request, otherwise select 'No'.
* **Supervisor Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Department Head Approval** (Select One, Optional): Select 'Yes' if your department head has approved the badge request, otherwise select 'No'.
* **Employee Signature** (Note, Required): Sign your name to confirm you have read and understood the form.
* **Supervisor Signature** (Note, Required): Your supervisor must sign their name to confirm they have reviewed and approved the badge request.
* **Date Badge Issued** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the badge was issued (optional).
* **Badge Number** (Number, Required): Enter the badge number issued to you.
* **Badge Status** (Select One, Optional): Select the status of your badge (Active or Inactive).
* **Reason Denied** (Text, Required): Enter the reason why the badge request was denied.
* **Reason Denied Other** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the reason denied.
* **Date Denied** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the badge request was denied.
* **Denied By** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person who denied the badge request.
* **Badge Issued By** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of who issued the badge.
* **Badge Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the badge.
* **Supervisor Comments** (Note, Optional): Enter any comments from your supervisor.
* **Employee Comments** (Note, Optional): Enter any comments from you.
* **Form ID** (Text, Optional): Enter the ID of the form you are submitting.
