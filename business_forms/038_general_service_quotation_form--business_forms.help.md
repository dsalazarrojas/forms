# General Service Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Service Quotation Form is a tool used to gather information from customers for service requests. It helps to generate a formal quotation that includes the customer's details and the details of the service required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and email address.
2. Provide a description of the service you need.
3. Choose the type of service you require.
4. Select the price range you are willing to pay for the service.
5. If necessary, include any specific delivery terms or instructions.
6. Specify the date and time you would like to receive the service.
7. Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion.
8. Optionally, add any additional notes about your service request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name (1)
• **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed in the quotation.
### Service Description (2)
• **Service Description**: Briefly describe the service you are requesting.
### Service Type (3)
• **Service Type**: Choose from the options provided:
	+ Option A
	+ Option B
	+ Option C
### Price Range (4)
• **Price Range**: Select the price range you are willing to pay:
	+ Option 1-100
	+ Option 2-200
	+ Option 3-300
### Delivery Terms (5)
• **Delivery Terms**: If you have any specific delivery instructions, enter them here.
### Date (6)
• **Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive the service.
### Time (7)
• **Time**: Enter the time you would like to receive the service.
### Email (8)
• **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
### Phone (9)
• **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion.
### Note (10)
• **Note**: Add any additional comments or instructions about your service request.
### Service Type Description (11)
• **Service Type Description**: Provide a brief description of the service you are requesting, if applicable.
### Price (12)
• **Price**: Enter the price you are willing to pay for the service.

This form helps to gather all the necessary information to create a comprehensive service quotation that can be used to finalize the service agreement. By filling out this form, you will receive a formal quotation that includes all the details you provided, which can be used for your reference and to ensure that both you and the service provider are on the same page.
