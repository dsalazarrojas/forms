# Grant Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Grant Application form, used to collect information for grants from organizations. The form is designed to gather essential details about the grant, contact person, and grant terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Grant Application form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact person's information, including whether they are the primary contact person ('Yes' or 'No').
2. Enter the contact person's email address.
3. Provide the contact person's phone number.
4. If applicable, enter the organization's name, grant amount, and grant end date.
5. Select the grant category, grant types, and grant period.
6. Specify the grant start and end dates.
7. Choose the grant status (Active or Inactive).
8. Select the grant amount currency.
9. Enter the grant amount value and grant currency symbol (e.g., '$').
10. Provide a brief fund request.
11. Attach any relevant documents or files.
12. Confirm the grant agreement terms.
13. Add any additional information or comments.
14. Optionally, attach more documents or files.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grant Application Form** (`grant_application_form`, 1): Please enter a brief description of the grant application.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, 2): Select 'Yes' if the person filling this form is the primary contact person. If not, please select 'No'.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, 3): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, 4): Provide the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, 5): Please enter the organization's address, if applicable.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, 6): Enter the name of the organization applying for the grant.
* **Grant Amount** (`grant_amount`, 7): Enter the grant amount.
* **Grant Amount Currency** (`grant_amount_currency`, 8): Select the currency for the grant amount (e.g., USD).
* **Grant Amount Value** (`grant_amount_value`, 9): Enter the grant amount value.
* **Grant Currency Symbol** (`grant_currency_symbol`, 10): Enter the symbol for the grant amount (e.g., '$').
* **Fund Request** (`fund_request`, 11): Please provide a brief description of the fund request.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, 12): Attach any relevant documents or files to support the grant application.
* **Grant Agreement** (`grant_agreement`, 13): Confirm the grant agreement terms.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, 14): Provide any additional comments or information about the grant.
* **Grant Notes** (`grant_notes`, 15): Enter any notes or comments about the grant.
* **Grant Comments** (`grant_comments`, 16): Add any additional comments about the grant.
* **Attachments 2** (`attachments_2`, 17): Attach more documents or files to support the grant application.
* **Grant Application** (`grant_application`, 18): Please confirm that this is a grant application.

Tips:

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Attach relevant documents or files to support your grant application.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the grant administrator for assistance.
