# Fire Safety Officer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Officer Request Form is a document used to report and document fire risks and hazards in the workplace, identify fire safety measures and training needs, and provide emergency contact information. This form will help to ensure that the workplace is a safe and compliant environment for all employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the options that apply to your situation. Answer each question carefully and completely, as this information will be used for fire safety compliance and risk assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Fire Officer**: Please select "Yes" if you are requesting a fire officer to review and assess fire risks and hazards in your work area. This will trigger a request for fire safety support and resources.
* **Fire Risks and Hazards**: List all potential fire risks and hazards that you have identified in your work area. Be as specific as possible and include any relevant details.
* **Fire Safety Measures**: Describe the measures you plan to take to mitigate or address the fire risks and hazards identified. This could include installing fire alarms, fire drills, or other safety protocols.
* **Fire Safety Equipment**: Please select "Yes" if you have fire safety equipment available for use in your work area. This could include fire extinguishers, fire alarms, or other safety devices.
* **Fire Safety Training**: Select "Yes" if you or your team will receive fire safety training to address identified risks and hazards. Provide details about the training, such as date, time, and location.
* **Emergency Contact**: Select "Yes" if you have an emergency contact person in case of a fire incident. Provide their name and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Select "Yes" if you have an emergency contact number in case of a fire incident. Provide the phone number or other contact information.
* **Contact Person**: Select "Yes" if you have a contact person who can assist with fire safety protocols and emergency response. Provide their name and contact information.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when completing this form, as fire safety is a top priority in the workplace.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your supervisor or fire safety officer for clarification.
* This form will be reviewed and assessed to identify fire safety risks and hazards, and to provide recommendations for improvement.
