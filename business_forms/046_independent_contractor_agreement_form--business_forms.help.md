# Independent Contractor Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Independent Contractor Agreement Form is used to document the terms and conditions of an independent contractor agreement between an employer and a contractor. This form helps ensure that both parties understand their obligations and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the contract terms to ensure they are accurate and complete.
2. Enter the name of the contractor in the "Contractor Name" field.
3. Specify the contract length in the "Contract Length" field.
4. Outline any payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contractor Name** (`contractor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the independent contractor.
* **Contract Length** (`contract_length`, number, required): Enter the length of the contract in months or years.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required): Describe the payment terms, including the payment schedule, payment method, and any other conditions that apply.

## Tips

* Be sure to review and understand the contract terms before completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with the relevant parties or seek additional guidance.
* Keep the contract length and payment terms clear and concise, as they will be used to communicate with the contractor.
* Save the completed form for future reference and to ensure that the contract terms are consistently applied.
