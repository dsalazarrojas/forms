# Retirement Planning Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your financial readiness for retirement. It's a tool to help you better understand your current situation and make informed decisions about your retirement planning.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Use numeric values for questions that require numbers.
3. If you're unsure about a question, please provide your best estimate or answer.
4. Review and double-check your answers for any errors or inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your age**: Please enter your current age.
* **What is your expected retirement date**: Enter the year you plan to retire.
* **What is your income**: Enter your current annual income.
* **What is your expenses**: Enter your current annual expenses.
* **Do you have any debt**: Please select 'Low', 'Moderate', or 'High' to indicate the amount of debt you have.
* **Do you have any dependents**: Please select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you have dependents that will be relying on you for financial support during retirement.
* **What is your investment portfolio**: Select the type of investments you currently hold, such as 'Cash', 'Bonds', 'Stocks', 'Real estate', or 'Retirement accounts'.
* **How much do you know about retirement savings plans**: Please rate your knowledge level from 1 to 10.
* **How much do you know about retirement income sources**: Please rate your knowledge level from 1 to 10.
* **Do you have an emergency fund**: Please select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you have an emergency fund set aside.
* **Have you consulted a financial advisor**: Please select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you have consulted a financial advisor.
* **retirement_planning_questionnaire_13** (Time): Please enter the amount of time you have until retirement.
* **How many years until retirement**: Please enter the number of years you have until retirement.
* **What is your risk tolerance**: Please select 'Low', 'Moderate', or 'High' to indicate your risk tolerance.
* **Do you have any retirement accounts**: Please select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you have any retirement accounts.
* **retirement_planning_questionnaire_17** (Email): Please enter your email address (this field is not used for any calculations, it's just for your reference).
* **How much can you save**: Please enter the amount you plan to save each month.
* **What is your investment goal**: Please select 'Low', 'Moderate', or 'High' to indicate your investment goal.
* **Do you have a financial advisor**: Please select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you have a financial advisor.
* **What is your expected retirement income**: Please enter your expected annual retirement income.
* **What is your expected retirement expenses**: Please enter your expected annual retirement expenses.
* **What is your emergency fund**: Please select 'True' or 'False' to indicate if you have an emergency fund set aside.
* **retirement_planning_questionnaire_24**: This is a free text field for any additional information or comments you'd like to provide.
* **retirement_planning_questionnaire_25** (Note): This is a free text field for any additional information or comments you'd like to provide.
