The Fulfillment Partner Accreditation Application form is designed to collect information from business partners who wish to become accredited and partner with the company for fulfillment services. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all partners meet the minimum requirements and standards for a successful partnership. The form is a critical step in the accreditation process, and completion of this form will help to verify the partner's company details, services, experience, and certifications.

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure that you have read and understood the instructions carefully before proceeding.
2.  Fill in all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3.  Be as detailed and accurate as possible in your responses.
4.  Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Details (Company Details)** (<id: 1, <type: text, required: true>): This field is used to collect the name of your company, including its legal name and any trading names or DBAs (Doing Business As) that may be used.
*   **Services (Services)** (<id: 2, <type: select_multiple, required: true>): This field allows you to select the services that your company offers, such as Warehouse Management, Logistics, Shipping, and Returns.
*   **Experience (Years of Experience)** (<id: 3, <type: number, required: true>): This field is used to determine the years of experience that your company has in offering fulfillment services.
*   **Certifications (Certifications)** (<id: 4, <type: text, required: false>): This field is optional and allows you to list any relevant certifications or accreditations that your company holds.
*   **Company Services (Company Services)** (<id: 5, <type: text, required: false>): This field is optional and allows you to describe the services that your company offers, beyond those selected in the Services field.
*   **Warehouse Details (Warehouse Details)** (<id: 6, <type: text, required: true>): This field is used to provide information about your company's warehouse, including its name, address, and any other relevant details.
*   **Warehouse Services (Warehouse Services)** (<id: 7, <type: select_multiple, required: true>): This field allows you to select the warehouse services that your company offers, such as Fulfillment, Shipping, and Returns.
*   **Contact Details (Contact Details)** (<id: 8, <type: text, required: true>): This field is used to collect contact information for your company, including the contact person's name, title, email, and phone number.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are completed in English.
*   If you need help or have questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
*   Once you have completed the form, please review it carefully before submitting it for review.

Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form.
