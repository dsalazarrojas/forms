# credit_repair_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking credit repair services.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps listed above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your family name.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
## Tips
* Be truthful with your information to receive accurate help.
* This form is designed for initial inquiries only.
* If you need follow-up information, contact the relevant party directly.
