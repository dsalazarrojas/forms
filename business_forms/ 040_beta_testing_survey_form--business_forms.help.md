# Beta Testing Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beta Testing Survey Form is a tool designed to help us understand the experiences and needs of our beta testers, gathering vital information about their interactions with our beta testing product.

## How To Complete This Form
To get the most out of this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full legal name in the 'Full name' field.
2. Enter a valid email address in the 'Email address' field for contact purposes.
3. Enter your phone number in the 'Phone number' field, including your country code if you're outside your local region.
4. Select a relevant date from the 'Date' field.
5. Choose the option that best applies to you in the 'Primary selection' field.
6. If required, provide a whole number for the 'Quantity or rating' field.
7. Select any applicable options in the 'Agreement' field.
8. If necessary, enter an estimated value in the 'Estimated value' field in decimal format.
9. Enter a preferred time for scheduling in the 'Preferred time' field, if applicable.
10. Finally, provide any additional comments or notes in the 'Final comments' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Full name
* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal full name for identification purposes.

### 2. Email address
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes, as it will be used for further communication.

### 3. Phone number
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Include your country code if you're outside your local region.

### 4. Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the relevant date associated with your testing experience.

### 5. Primary selection
* **Primary selection** (`primary_selection`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best applies to your situation.

### 6. Quantity or rating
* **Quantity or rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, number, optional): If required, enter a whole number for quantity or rating.

### 7. Agreement
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options that apply to you.

### 8. Estimated value
* **Estimated value** (`estimated_value`, number, optional): Enter an estimated value in decimal format if required.

### 9. Preferred time
* **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Enter a preferred time if scheduling is required.

### 10. Final comments
* **Final comments or notes** (`final_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience.

### 11. Additional details
* **Additional details** (`additional_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional relevant information about your experience.
