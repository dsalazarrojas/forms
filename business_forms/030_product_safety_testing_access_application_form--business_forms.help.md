# Product Safety Testing Access Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Safety Testing Access Application Form is used to apply for access to conduct product safety testing. This form is typically used by companies or organizations that need to test their products for safety purposes. The form requires basic contact and company information, as well as the testing requirements.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name in the Company Name field.
2. Provide your contact information, including your phone number in the Contact Number field and your email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your name and department in the Employee Name and Department fields, respectively.
4. Select the product you want to test in the Product field.
5. Choose the type of testing you need to conduct in the Testing Type field.
6. Select the date you plan to start testing in the Testing Date field.
7. Fill in any additional testing details in the Testing Type field.
8. Review and submit your application form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (Form Data, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the testing purpose.
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required: false): Enter the official name of your company.
* **Address** (Address, text, required: false): Enter the physical address of your company.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the employee requesting access.
* **Department** (Department, select_multiple, required: false): Select the department or team member requesting access.
* **Product** (Product, select_one, required: false): Choose the product you want to test.
* **Testing Type** (Testing Type, text, required: false): Describe the type of testing you need to conduct.
* **Testing Date** (Testing Date, date, required: false): Select the date you plan to start testing.
* **Department** (Department, select_multiple, required: false): Select the department or team member who will be performing the testing.
* **Product** (Product, select_one, required: false): Select the product that will be tested.

Note: The "Department" field seems to be duplicated with id 7 and id 8, I will use id 7 "Department" in the explanation as per the provided fields.
## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your testing request is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact the product safety team.
* Make sure to provide a valid date for the testing date.
