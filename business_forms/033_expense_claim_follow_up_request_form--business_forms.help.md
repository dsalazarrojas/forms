# Expense Claim Follow Up Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by employees to request a follow-up on the status of an expense claim. It's intended for situations where an employee needs to follow up with their manager or a team member about the status of an expense claim.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Business Inquiries**: Select the reason for the follow-up request. This field is required.
	* 'Yes' if you need to follow up on the status of your expense claim
	* 'No' if you need to request additional information or clarify something specific about your expense claim
2. **Claim Details**: Enter a brief description of the expense claim that requires follow-up.
3. **Follow Up Request**: Describe the specific issue or question you need to follow up on.
4. **Date Of Request**: Enter the date when you initially requested a follow-up.
5. **Follow Up Status**: Select the status of the follow-up request (Active or Inactive).
6. **Expense Amount**: Enter the total amount of the expense claim (optional).
7. **Employee Name**: Enter your name as the person making the expense claim.
8. **Employee Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
9. **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager handling your expense claim.
10. **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the manager handling your expense claim (optional).
11. **Department Name**: Enter the department associated with the expense claim (optional).
12. **Department**: Enter the department where the expense was incurred (optional).
13. **Follow Up Date**: Select the date when you need the follow-up to occur.
14. **Follow Up Time**: Select the time when you need the follow-up to occur.
15. **Follow Up Frequency**: Select whether you need a follow-up at regular intervals (Yes or No).
16. **Next Follow Up Date**: Select the date when the next follow-up is needed (optional).
17. **Next Follow Up Time**: Select the time when the next follow-up is needed (optional).
18. **Next Follow Up Frequency**: Select whether the next follow-up needs to be at regular intervals (Yes or No).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have a valid reason for requesting a follow-up and that you're not duplicating efforts.
* If you're unsure about whether you need to fill out this form, please reach out to your manager or the relevant team member for guidance.
* This form is intended for expense claims that require follow-up. If you're submitting a new expense claim, please use the expense claim form instead.
