# morning_cleaning_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "morning_cleaning_checklist" form is designed to track and record the status of morning cleaning tasks in a facility or office setting. It helps monitor and report on the completion of these tasks, promoting efficiency and accountability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "morning_task_list" field with a list of tasks completed during the morning cleaning shift.
2. Enter the "what_time" field with the time when the cleaning shift started.
3. Enter the "start_time" field with the start time of the specific cleaning task(s).
4. Enter the "end_time" field with the end time of the specific cleaning task(s).
5. Indicate which areas were cleaned by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "cleaning_area" field, as applicable.
6. Confirm if the cleaning tasks were completed by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "cleaning_task" field.
7. Select the priority level of the cleaning task(s) by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "priority" field.
8. Choose the current status of the cleaning task(s) by selecting "Active" or "Inactive" in the "status" field.
9. Add any additional notes about the task(s) in the "notes" field.

* **start_time** (time, optional): Enter the start time of the specific cleaning task(s).
* **end_time** (time, optional): Enter the end time of the specific cleaning task(s).
