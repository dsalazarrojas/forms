# Roofing Materials Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request roofing materials from a supplier. It ensures that all the necessary information is provided to the supplier for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely with the required information.
2. Select the material type from the list provided.
3. Enter the quantity and unit price of the material requested.
4. Enter the supplier's contact information, including phone number, email address, and fax number.
5. Enter the delivery date and order due date.
6. Select the status of the requisition (approved, rejected, or pending).
7. Add any additional comments or remarks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Materials Requested** (`materials_requested`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the materials requested.
* **Contractor Info** (`contractor_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the contractor or building owner.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person in charge of the requisition.
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of material requested from the list provided.
* **Material Description** (`material_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the material requested.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the supplier.
* **Supplier Phone** (`supplier_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the supplier.
* **Quantity Requested** (`quantity_requested`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of material requested.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, required/optional): Enter the unit price of the material.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, required/optional): Enter the total cost of the material.
* **Order Due Date** (`order_due_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the order is due.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the material is expected to be delivered.
* **Status** (`status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the status of the requisition (approved, rejected, or pending).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the supplier.
* **Fax Number** (`fax_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the fax number of the supplier.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the street address of the supplier.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of the supplier.
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state of the supplier.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the supplier.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required/optional): Enter the country of the supplier.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, required/optional): Enter any remarks or comments about the requisition.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments about the requisition.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the requisition.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): Enter your signature.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the requisition.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required/optional): Enter the country of the supplier.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the supplier.
