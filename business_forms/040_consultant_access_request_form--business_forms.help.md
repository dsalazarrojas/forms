> # Consultant Access Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to request access for consultants or external partners to access company resources, such as project portals, email accounts, and internal systems. It helps the requesting consultant to provide essential information about themselves, their projects, and the type of access they require. The form also ensures that the consultant has read and agrees to the Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) and has the necessary security clearance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you provide all required fields.
3. If you're unsure about any information, please contact the internal sponsor or manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consultant Full Name** (`consultant_full_name_ident`): Your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Consultant's Company/Firm** (`consultant_company_legal_name`): The full name of your company or firm.
* **Consultant Email Address** (`consultant_email_ident`): Your professional email address.
* **Primary Project Assignment** (`primary_project_assignment`): A brief description of your primary project assignment.
* **Internal Sponsor/Manager Name** (`internal_sponsor_name_ident`): The name of the internal person responsible for your work.
* **Requested Access Start Date** (`access_start_date_ident`): The date when you want to start accessing company resources.
* **Estimated Access End Date** (`access_end_date_ident`): The estimated date when you no longer need access to company resources.
* **Types of Access Required** (`access_type_checklist`): Check all types of access you require, such as physical site access, VPN, email account, project portal, or administrative system access.
* **Brief Justification for the Requested Access** (`justification_for_access_desc`): A brief explanation of why you need access to these resources.
* **Security Background Check Passed** (`security_background_check_passed`): If applicable, indicate if you have already passed a security background check.
* **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) Signed** (`nda_signed_checklist`): If you have an NDA, indicate if you've already signed one or if you'll attach it with this request.
* **Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) Agreement** (`acceptable_use_policy_ack`): Check that you have read and agree to the AUP.
* **Hardware/Equipment Provisioning Required?** (`equipment_provisioning_req`): If you need company equipment, such as a laptop or mobile phone, check the type of equipment you need.
* **Delivery Address for Equipment** (`equipment_delivery_address`): If you require equipment delivery, enter the address.
* **Additional Access Instructions or Notes** (`additional_access_instructions`): Any additional information about your access request.
* **Date of Access Request** (`submission_date`): The date you are submitting this access request.
* **Authorized Approving Manager (Name and Title)** (`authorized_manager_name_label`): The name and title of the internal manager who will approve your access request.
