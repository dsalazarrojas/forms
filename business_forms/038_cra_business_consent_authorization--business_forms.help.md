# CRA Business Consent Authorization - Help Guide

## Purpose

The CRA Business Consent Authorization form is used by businesses to provide consent for various authorizations, such as data access, information disclosure, tax representation, government inquiries, or service provision. This form is required for businesses to acknowledge and consent to these authorizations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about your business.
2. Select the correct type of business from the provided options.
3. Fill in the primary contact person's details.
4. Select the purpose and scope of authorization.
5. Fill in the authorized parties and dates.
6. Check the box to acknowledge and consent to the authorization.
7. Sign and date the form as the authorized representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`Business Name`, text, required): The name of your business.
* **Business Registration or CRA Number** (`Business Registration or CRA Number`, text, required): A government identifier for your business.
* **Business Street Address** (`Business Street Address`, text, required): The street address of your business.
* **City, State, ZIP Code** (`City, State, ZIP Code`, text, required): The city, state, and ZIP code of your business's location.
* **Business Phone Number** (`Business Phone Number`, text, required): The phone number of your business.
* **Business Email Address** (`Business Email Address`, email, required): The email address of your business.
* **Type of Business** (`Type of Business`, select_one, required): The type of business (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, Non-profit, or Other).
* **Primary Contact Person Name** (`Primary Contact Person Name`, text, required): The name of your primary contact person.
* **Contact Person Title** (`Contact Person Title`, text, required): The title of your primary contact person (Owner, Manager, Authorized Representative).
* **Contact Person Phone** (`Contact Person Phone`, text, required): The phone number of your primary contact person.
* **Contact Person Email** (`Contact Person Email`, email, required): The email address of your primary contact person.
* **Purpose of Authorization** (`Purpose of Authorization`, select_one, required): The purpose of the authorization (Data Access, Information Disclosure, Tax Representation, Government Inquiry, Service Provision, or Other).
* **Scope of Authorization** (`Scope of Authorization`, text, required): The extent of authorized activities.
* **Authorized Parties** (`Authorized Parties`, text, required): The parties being authorized.
* **Effective Date of Authorization** (`Effective Date of Authorization`, date, required): The date when the authorization is effective.
* **Authorization Expiration Date** (`Authorization Expiration Date`, date, optional): The date when the authorization expires (if applicable).
* **Business Acknowledges and Consents** (`Business Acknowledges and Consents`, select_one, required): Whether the business acknowledges and consents to the authorization (Yes, I Consent or No, I Do Not Consent).
* **Authorized Representative Signature** (`Authorized Representative Signature`, text, required): The full name of the authorized representative.
* **Date of Authorization** (`Date of Authorization`, date, required): The date of authorization.
* **Additional Terms or Conditions** (`Additional Terms or Conditions`, text, optional): Any special restrictions or requirements.
