# intern_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intern Survey is a tool used by intern supervisors to gather feedback from interns about their performance, experiences, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the questions and sections of the survey carefully to ensure you provide comprehensive feedback.
2. Answer all required questions, marked as such in the form.
3. Be honest and thorough in your answers, providing concrete examples and suggestions where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Intern Survey** (`1`, `note`, required/optional): A brief introduction to the survey.
	+ This section is a note section for the introduction of the survey.
* **Company Name** (`2`, `text`, required/true): The name of the company where the intern worked.
	+ Please type the company name as it appears on your business cards or company website.
* **Supervisor** (`3`, `text`, required/true): The name of the supervisor who is completing this survey.
	+ Please type your name as it appears on your business cards or company email signature.
* **Start Date** (`4`, `date`, required/true): The start date of the internship.
	+ Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.
* **End Date** (`5`, `date`, required/true): The end date of the internship.
	+ Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.
* **Experience Points** (`6`, `number`, required/true): Rating of the intern's overall experience.
	+ Use a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Overall Experience** (`7`, `select_one`, required/true): Select one option that best describes the intern's overall experience.
	+ Internship Experience, Not Very Good, Average, Good, Very Good, Excellent
* **Areas for Improvement** (`8`, `select_multiple`, false): Choose one or more areas for improvement.
	+ Communication, Teamwork, Problem Solving, Leadership, Time Management, Other
* **Feedback** (`9`, `text`, required/true): A general feedback for the intern.
	+ Please be specific and concrete with your feedback.
* **Recommendations** (`10`, `text`, required/true): Suggestions for the intern's improvement.
	+ Please provide concrete and action-oriented suggestions.
* **Questions** (`11`, `text`, false): Additional comments or questions for the supervisor.
	+ If you have any questions or comments for the supervisor, please type them here.
* **Comments** (`12`, `text`, false): Additional comments about the intern's experience.
	+ Please use this section to provide any general comments about the intern.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`13`, `text`, required/true): Suggestions for the intern's improvement.
	+ Please be specific and concrete with your suggestions.
* **Next Steps** (`14`, `text`, required/true): Steps for follow-up actions.
	+ Please type the next steps for the intern's improvement.
* **Final Comments** (`15`, `text`, required/true): Additional comments from the supervisor.
	+ Please type any final thoughts or comments from the supervisor.
* **Overall Rating** (`16`, `number`, false): Rating of the intern's overall performance.
	+ Please use a number between 1 and 5.
* **Rating Comment** (`17`, `text`, false): Comments on the rating.
	+ Please type a comment on the overall rating.
* **Rating Score** (`18`, `number`, required/true): The rating score for the intern's performance.
	+ Please type the score as a number between 1 and 5.
* **Feedback Comments** (`19`, `text`, false): Additional comments on the feedback.
	+ Please type any additional comments on the feedback.
* **Follow-up Plan** (`20`, `text`, false): Plan for follow-up actions.
	+ If you have any plan for the next steps, type it here.
* **Follow-up Date** (`21`, `date`, false): The date for follow-up actions.
	+ If you have any follow-up dates, type them here.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`22`, `text`, false): Frequency of follow-up actions.
	+ If you have any follow-up frequency, type it here.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`23`, `text`, false): Comments on follow-up actions.
	+ If you have any comments on follow-up actions, type them here.
* **Follow-up Next Steps** (`24`, `text`, false): Next steps for follow-up actions.
	+ If you have any next steps for follow-up actions, type them here.
* **Follow-up Next Steps Comments** (`25`, `text`, false): Comments on follow-up next steps.
	+ If you have any comments on follow-up next steps, type them here.

## Tips
* Please be thorough and honest in your answers.
* Use concrete examples and suggestions where possible.
* Please review the entire survey before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the supervisor or HR department.
