# Watch Selling Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Watch Selling Application form is designed to collect information about a watch being sold or traded. This form is intended for sellers to provide details about the watch, including its characteristics, condition, and sales information. This form helps the buyer and seller communicate effectively and efficiently, ensuring a smooth transaction process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about the watch.
2. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
3. Provide your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
4. Enter the watch's identification number ("Watch ID") and details about its condition ("Condition").
5. Select the brand and model of the watch ("Brand" and "Model") and upload photos if available ("Photos").
6. Enter the price of the watch.
7. Check the watch's status ("Status") and whether it comes with a warranty ("Warranty").
8. Enter the shipping information, including the shipping address, zip code, city, and country.
9. Enter your name and email address as the seller's contact information.
10. Upload any additional seller notes or comments in the "Seller Notes" field.
11. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as the buyer.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as the buyer.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address as the buyer.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number as the buyer.
* **Watch ID** (5, number, required): Enter the watch's identification number.
* **Brand** (6, text, required): Enter the brand of the watch.
* **Model** (7, text, required): Enter the model of the watch.
* **Photos** (8, text, required): Upload photos of the watch if available.
* **Price** (9, number, required): Enter the price of the watch.
* **Condition** (10, select_one, required): Check the watch's condition (Yes or No).
* **Status** (11, select_multiple, required): Select the watch's status (Active or Inactive).
* **Warranty** (13, select_one, required): Check whether the watch comes with a warranty (Yes or No).
* **Shipping Cost** (14, number, required): Enter the shipping cost.
* **Shipping Address** (15, text, required): Enter the shipping address.
* **Shipping Zip** (16, text, required): Enter the shipping zip code.
* **Shipping City** (17, text, required): Enter the shipping city.
* **Shipping State** (18, text, required): Enter the shipping state.
* **Shipping Country** (19, text, required): Enter the shipping country.
* **Seller Name** (20, text, required): Enter your name as the seller.
* **Seller Email** (21, email, required): Enter your email address as the seller.
* **Seller Phone** (22, text, required): Enter your phone number as the seller.
* **Seller Notes** (23, text, required): Enter any additional notes or comments as the seller.
* **Seller Location** (24, text, optional): Enter your location as the seller.
* **Submit** (25, text, required): Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in accurate and complete information about the watch.
* Use proper grammar and spelling in your entries.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled before clicking the "Submit" button.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us for assistance.
