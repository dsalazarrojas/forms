# Gun Exchange Lifecycle Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document information about a gun exchange between two parties, including the firearm's details and participant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide participant information in the first page. Please enter your name, which will be recorded as the participant of this exchange.
2. Enter the Firearm ID Number of the firearm being exchanged.
3. Select the make of the firearm from the available options: Smith & Wesson, Glock, Ruger, or Other (please specify).
4. Enter the model of the firearm being exchanged.
5. Select the caliber of the firearm from the available options: 9mm, 40mm, 45mm, or Other (please specify).
6. Enter the serial number of the firearm being exchanged.
7. Explain the reason for the exchange in the provided text field.
8. Select the condition of the firearm from the available options: New, Used, Refurbished, or Other (please specify).
9. Please sign the form as a participant to confirm your involvement in the exchange.
10. Date and sign the form to complete the participant's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (Participant Information)**: This field is for you to provide your details as a participant in the exchange.
* **Firearm ID Number (Firearm ID Number)**: Enter the unique identifier of the firearm being exchanged.
* **Firearm Make (Firearm Make)**: Select the make of the firearm from the available options.
* **Firearm Model (Firearm Model)**: Enter the model of the firearm being exchanged.
* **Firearm Caliber (Firearm Caliber)**: Select the caliber of the firearm from the available options.
* **Firearm Serial Number (Firearm Serial Number)**: Enter the serial number of the firearm being exchanged.
* **Reason for Exchange (Reason for Exchange)**: Explain why you are exchanging the firearm in this text field.
* **Firearm Condition (Firearm Condition)**: Select the condition of the firearm from the available options.
* **Participant Signature (Participant Signature)**: Please sign the form as a participant to confirm your involvement.
* **Participant Date (Participant Date)**: Date and sign the form to complete the participant's signature.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have a valid reason for exchanging the firearm before submitting this form.
* Double-check the firearm details before submitting the form.
* Make sure to sign the form as a participant to confirm your involvement.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records.
* If you have any doubts or questions about the form, please contact the form owner at form@gic.mx.
