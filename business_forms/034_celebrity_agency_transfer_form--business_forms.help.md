# Celebrity Agency Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the transfer of a celebrity's representation from one agency to another. It gathers essential information from both the celebrity and the current agency, as well as from the new agency, to ensure a smooth transition of representation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the celebrity's information, including their legal name, stage name, date of birth, contact email, and phone number.
2. Enter the current agency's information, including their name, agent's name, contact information, and representation start date.
3. Enter the new agency's information, including their name, agent's name, contact information, and the date when new representation should start.
4. Specify the reason for the transfer, select the existing agency contract status, and indicate if there are any pending commitments or projects.
5. Confirm that the information is accurate and authorize the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Legal Name** (1, text, required): The legal name of the celebrity.
  Enter the celebrity's full name as it appears on official documents.
- **Stage Name or Professional Name** (2, text, optional): The stage name or professional name used in the entertainment industry.
  This is the name used in public appearances, performances, or credits.
- **Date of Birth** (3, date, required): The date of birth of the celebrity.
  This is a required field for identification and age verification purposes.
- **Email Address** (4, email, required): The contact email address of the celebrity.
  This is the email address used for communication with the celebrity or their team.
- **Phone Number** (5, text, required): The contact phone number of the celebrity.
  This is the phone number used for direct communication with the celebrity or their team.
- **Current Agency Name** (8, text, required): The name of the current agency.
  This is the name of the agency representing the celebrity before the transfer.
- **Current Agent Name** (9, text, required): The name of the current agent.
  This is the name of the agent responsible for the celebrity's current representation.
- **Current Agent Contact Information** (10, text, required): Phone and email contact information of the current agent.
  This is the contact information used to communicate with the current agent.
- **Representation Start Date** (11, date, optional): The date when the current representation began.
  This is the date when the celebrity started working with the current agency.
- **New Agency Name** (13, text, required): The name of the new agency.
  This is the name of the agency that will represent the celebrity after the transfer.
- **New Agent Name** (14, text, required): The name of the new agent.
  This is the name of the agent that will represent the celebrity after the transfer.
- **New Agent Contact Information** (15, text, required): Phone and email contact information of the new agent.
  This is the contact information used to communicate with the new agent.
- **Transfer Effective Date** (17, date, required): The date when the new representation should start.
  This is the date when the transfer should take effect.
- **Reason for Transfer** (18, select_one, required): The reason for transferring to the new agency.
  Select one of the provided options, such as Career Development, Better Representation, Contract Termination, Agency Closure, Personal Preference, Financial Reasons, or Other.
- **Detailed Reason for Transfer** (19, text, optional): Provide a detailed explanation for the reason for transfer.
  This is an optional field for providing additional context or justification.
- **Existing Agency Contract Status** (21, select_one, required): The status of the existing agency contract.
  Select one of the provided options, such as Active with Notice Period, Terminated, Expired, Under Review, Mutual Termination.
- **Notice Period Required (days)** (22, number, optional): The number of days for the notice period.
  This is the number of days the current agency has to fulfill the notice period before the transfer takes effect.
- **Pending Commitments or Projects** (23, text, optional): List any ongoing commitments or projects that may be affected by the transfer.
  This is an optional field for listing any current commitments or projects that the celebrity has.
- **I Confirm the Information and Authorize this Transfer** (25, select_one, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate and authorize the transfer.
  Select 'Yes' to confirm and authorize the transfer, or 'No' to indicate that the information is not accurate or the transfer should not proceed.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the "Reason for Transfer" and "Detailed Reason for Transfer" fields.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the provided hints or consult with the current or new agency representative.
