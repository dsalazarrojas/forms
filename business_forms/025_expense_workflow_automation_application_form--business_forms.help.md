# Expense Workflow Automation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Workflow Automation Application Form is a tool to streamline the expense request process for employees. It gathers necessary details about their expense requests, including submission status and contact information. This form is meant for internal use within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields marked as "Required" under "type: text", "select_one", "select_multiple", "date", and "email". 
2. Select the appropriate options for "Select Multiple" and "Submit".
3. Enter the date and time in the respective fields. 
4. Type in any additional information in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Expense Form** (workflow_form, text, Required): Enter a brief description of your expense request.
* **Request Details** (form_id_2, text, Required): Provide detailed information about your expense request.
* **Submit** (form_id_3, select_one, Required): Indicate the status of your expense submission by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Select Multiple** (form_id_4, select_multiple, Optional): Choose multiple options for your expense submission.
* **Date** (form_id_5, date, Optional): Enter the date of your expense submission. 
* **Time** (form_id_6, time, Optional): Enter the time of your expense submission.
* **Note** (form_id_7, note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your expense request.
* **Email** (form_id_8, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (form_id_9, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
