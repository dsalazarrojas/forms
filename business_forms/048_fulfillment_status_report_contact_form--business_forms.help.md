# Fulfillment Status Report Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by authorized personnel to report the status of fulfillments. It provides a way to report the status of a fulfillment and gather additional details and order information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and provide any additional details as needed. You can report the status of a fulfillment as either Active or Inactive. If you need to provide more information about the order, you can do so in the Details field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please provide your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please provide your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Fulfillment Status** (`fulfillment_status`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the following options for the status of the fulfillment:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Details** (`details`, note, optional): Please provide any additional details about the order, such as notes from the customer or internal comments.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, text, optional): If you have the order ID, please provide it for reference purposes.
