# small_business_grant_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for businesses to apply for the small business grant fund.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields to the best of your ability.
2. Please ensure that your contact information is correct so we can get in touch with you if we need more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method that you will use to complete the grant, either Card, Check, or PayPal. 
* **Amount**: This field is not required, but please note that it will be used as a reference for the grant amount.
* **Business Address**: Please fill out the business address, this will be used for grant purposes.
* **Contact Details**: This field is used to get in touch with you regarding your grant application.
* **Contact Name**: Fill out the contact name for the person in charge of the business.
* **Email**: Please enter the email address of the business.
* **Phone**: Enter the business phone number so we can contact you.
* **Business Details**: This field is used to gather more information about the business. 
* **Card**: This field will only be visible to you after selecting Card as a payment method and is not editable after selecting another payment method.
* **Check**: This field will only be visible to you after selecting Check as a payment method and is not editable after selecting another payment method.
* **PayPal**: This field will only be visible to you after selecting PayPal as a payment method and is not editable after selecting another payment method.
