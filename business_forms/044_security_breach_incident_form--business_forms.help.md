# security_breach_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and document security breaches or incidents within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., unauthorized access, data breach, etc.)
2. Provide a description of the incident, including the date and time it occurred
3. Enter the incident date and time
4. Enter the impact of the incident (e.g., number of users affected, financial loss, etc.)
5. Enter the severity of the incident (e.g., low, medium, high)
6. Select the status of the incident (e.g., reported, investigated, resolved)
7. Enter your name and email address as the reporter
8. Enter any relevant information about the responsible party (if applicable)
9. Enter any relevant information about the affected party (if applicable)
10. Provide any additional comments or information about the incident

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Security Breach Description** (`security_breach_description`): A brief description of the incident, including any relevant details.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): The date on which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): The time at which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (`security_breach_incident_type`): Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., unauthorized access, data breach, etc.).
* **Description** (`security_breach_description`): Another entry point for providing a detailed description of the incident.
* **Impact** (`security_breach_impact`): Enter the impact of the incident, such as the number of users affected or financial loss.
* **Severity** (`security_breach_severity`): Select the severity of the incident (e.g., low, medium, high).
* **Status** (`security_breach_incident_status`): Select the status of the incident (e.g., reported, investigated, resolved).
* **Reporter's Name** (`security_breach_reporter`): Enter your name and title/position as the reporter.
* **Reporter's Email** (`security_breach_reporter_email`): Enter your email address as the reporter.
* **Reporter's Phone Number** (`security_breach_incident_reporter_phone`): Enter your phone number as the reporter.
* **Responsible Party's Name** (`security_breach_responsible_party`): Enter the name and title/position of the responsible party (if applicable).
* **Affected Party's Name** (`security_breach_affected_party`): Enter the name and title/position of the affected party (if applicable).
* **Incident Location** (`security_breach_incident_location`): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Details** (`security_breach_incident_details`): Provide any additional comments or information about the incident.
* **Incident Cause** (`security_breach_incident_cause`): Enter any information about the cause of the incident.
* **Preventative Controls** (`security_breach_preventative_controls`): Enter any information about the preventative controls in place.
* **Response** (`security_breach_incident_response`): Enter any information about the response taken.
* **Follow-up** (`security_breach_incident_follow_up`): Enter any follow-up actions or next steps.
* **Incident Notes** (`security_breach_incident_notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Incident Reference** (`security_breach_incident_reference`): Enter any reference numbers or IDs related to the incident.
* **Incident Attachment** (`security_breach_incident_attachment`): Attach any relevant files or documents.
* **Comments** (`security_breach_incident_comments`): Enter any additional comments or information about the incident.
