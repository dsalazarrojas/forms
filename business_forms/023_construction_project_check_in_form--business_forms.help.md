# Construction Project Check-In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to gather information about visitors checking in to a construction site. It helps site managers or supervisors to track and monitor visitors, ensure they have necessary safety equipment, and confirm their agreement to follow site rules.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Person Checking In (visitor_name)**: Provide your full name as a visitor.
* **Email Address (visitor_email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (visitor_phone)**: Enter your phone number in the format (555) 555-5555.
* **Reason for Visit (check_in_type)**: Select the reason for your visit from the provided options.
* **Employer/Company (company_name)**: Enter the name of your employer or company.
* **Entry Time (entry_time)**: Enter the time of arrival at the site.
* **Have you completed a site safety orientation? (site_orientation_verified)**: Confirm if you have completed a site safety orientation.
* **Do you have all necessary PPE for this site? (required_ppe_check)**: Confirm if you have all necessary PPE.
* **Are you bringing major equipment on site? (equipment_brought_on_site)**: Confirm if you are bringing major equipment on site.
* **Equipment Description and ID/Serial Number (equipment_desc)**: Describe the equipment and provide its ID/Serial Number if applicable.
* **Specialized Tools or Materials (tools_checked_in)**: List any specialized tools or hazardous materials you are bringing onto the site.
* **Emergency Contact Name (emergency_contact_name)**: Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (emergency_contact_phone)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **I agree to follow all posted site safety rules and instructions? (site_rules_agreement)**: Carefully read and agree to follow all posted site safety rules and instructions.
* **Date of Check-In (check_in_date)**: Enter the date of arrival at the site.
