# Fire Safety Equipment Test Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a fire safety equipment test request. It will help assess the current state of fire safety equipment, including sprinklers and extinguishers, and gather contact details for the person making the request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options for the fire safety equipment tests requested.
2. Enter the details of the equipment being tested, including the type of sprinkler or extinguisher.
3. Enter the contact details for the person making the request, including their first and last name, email, and phone number.
4. Enter the date and time of the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fire Safety Equipment Test Request**: Select all the types of fire safety equipment that need to be tested. This is a multiple-select option, so you can select multiple options.
* **Alarms**: Enter any specific details about the alarms related to the fire safety equipment.
* **Sprinkler Type**: Select whether the sprinkler is 'Yes' or 'No' in case it's needed.
* **Sprinklers**: Enter any specific details about the sprinklers being tested.
* **Extinguisher Type**: Select whether the extinguisher is 'Yes' or 'No' in case it's needed.
* **Extinguishers**: Enter any specific details about the extinguishers being tested.
* **Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the person making the request.
* **Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the person making the request.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the person making the request.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person making the request.
* **Date of Request**: Enter the date of the request in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time of Request**: Enter the time of the request in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Assigned Tool**: Select whether a specific tool is assigned for the task or not.
* **Fire Safety Equipment Test Request Form**: This is a note field for any additional information about the fire safety equipment test request.
