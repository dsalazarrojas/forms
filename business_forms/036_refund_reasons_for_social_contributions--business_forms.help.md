# Refund Reasons For Social Contributions - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refund Reasons For Social Contributions form is used to record the reasons for refunding social contributions made by employees. This form must be completed accurately to ensure that the refund process is handled efficiently and correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the base form to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
2. Select the reason for refund from the contribution details section.
3. Enter any additional details about the refund in the additional details section.
4. Enter the reason for the refund in the refund reason section.
5. Enter the date and time of submission in the submission date and submission time sections.
6. Enter any notes about the refund in the notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Base Form** (base_page, text, optional): The base form is the starting point for the refund process. Enter any relevant information about the refund.
* **Contribution Details** (contribution_details, select_one, optional): Select the reason for refund from this section.
* **Additional Details** (additional_details, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the refund, if required.
* **Refund Reason** (refund_reason, select_one, optional): Select the reason for refund from the options provided.
* **Notes** (notes, note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the refund.
* **Submission Date** (submission_date, date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (submission_time, time, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Employee ID** (employee_id, number, optional): Enter the employee ID number.

## Tips
- Ensure that the refund reason and contribution details are accurately recorded.
- Enter any additional details or notes as required.
- Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

Note: The required fields are highlighted in bold to emphasize their importance.
