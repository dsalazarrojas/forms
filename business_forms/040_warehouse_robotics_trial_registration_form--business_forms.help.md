# warehouse_robotics_trial_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The warehouse robotics trial registration form is designed to collect information about trials involving warehouse robotics, including site evaluation, trial duration, and other relevant details. This form helps ensure that trials are executed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure you understand what each field is asking for.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your knowledge about the trial.
3. Fill in any required fields.
4. Double-check your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Trial Request** 
(Trial Request, note, required: false)

This field is a note section where you can provide a brief description or explanation of the trial. You can enter any relevant details about the trial, such as its purpose or objectives.

### **Site Evaluation** 
(Site Evaluation, select_one, required: false)

This field allows you to select the evaluation status of the site. Choose the option that best describes the status of the site where the trial will take place.

* Option 1
* Option 2
* Option 3

### **Site Address** 
(Site Address, text, required: false)

Enter the physical address of the site where the trial will occur. This is an important field, as it will help ensure that the trial can be located and executed smoothly.

### **Trial Date** 
(Trial Date, date, required: false)

Enter the date when the trial is scheduled to take place. Make sure to use the correct format for dates (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).

### **Trial Duration** 
(Trial Duration, number, required: false)

Enter the expected duration of the trial. This can be expressed in hours, days, or any other relevant time unit.

### **Trial Site Status** 
(Trial Site Status, select_one, required: false)

Choose the status of the trial site from the available options. This will help in understanding the site's readiness for the trial.

* Site Status 1
* Site Status 2
* Site Status 3

### **Trial Site Qualification** 
(Trial Site Qualification, select_multiple, required: false)

Select the qualification(s) of the trial site from the available options. This is crucial in understanding the site's readiness and capabilities.

* qualification1
* qualification2
* qualification3

### **Trial Site Follow-up** 
(Trial Site Follow-up, date, required: false)

Enter the date for a follow-up activity related to the trial site. This can be a reminder for a scheduled follow-up or a future trial.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field or option, review the form carefully or seek clarification from the relevant team members.
* This form is designed to be user-friendly, so don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need assistance.
