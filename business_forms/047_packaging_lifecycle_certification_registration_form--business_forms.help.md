# Packaging Lifecycle Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps organizations register their products or services for packaging lifecycle certification. It is designed to collect essential information about the organization, lifecycle stages, documentation, and contact person responsible for the certification process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Read the form carefully**: Take a few minutes to understand each field and its purpose before starting to fill in the form.
2. **Fill in the Organization Details**: Provide a brief description of your organization, including its name and address.
3. **Select Lifecycle Stages**: Choose the lifecycle stages that apply to your product or service. These stages are:
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
4. **Provide Documentation**: Briefly describe the documentation related to your product or service.
5. **Enter Contact Person Details**: Fill in the contact person's name, title, and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Organization Details
* **Organization Details** (`organization_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your organization, including its name and address.

### Lifecycle Stages
* **Lifecycle Stages** (`lifecycle_stages`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the lifecycle stages that apply to your product or service. These stages are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Documentation
* **Documentation** (`documentation`, text, optional): Briefly describe the documentation related to your product or service.

### Contact Person
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Fill in the contact person's name, title, and contact information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any) accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure it is correct and consistent.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for clarification.
* You can modify the form as needed to suit your specific requirements.
