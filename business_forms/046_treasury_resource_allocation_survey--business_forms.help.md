# Treasury Resource Allocation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information from team members to understand their roles, objectives, challenges, and other relevant details to aid in making informed decisions for resource allocation within the Treasury team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please navigate through the pages below, answering each question to the best of your ability. You can type your responses directly into the text fields or select from the provided options. Do not hesitate to ask for clarification or assistance if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the survey and its purpose.
* **Whats the role of the Treasury team**: Describe the main functions and responsibilities of the Treasury team.
* **What are the treasury objectives**: Specify the primary goals and objectives of the Treasury team.
* **What are the funding needs**: Outline the resources required to achieve the team's objectives.
* **What are the budget priorities**: Identify the key budget areas that need attention.
* **What are the challenges**: List any obstacles or challenges the team is currently facing.
* **Additional comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the team's resource allocation needs.
* **Email address**: Enter your official email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company.
* **Team members**: List the team members involved in the survey.
* **Location**: Specify the location of the team.
* **Date completed**: Enter the date you completed the survey.
* **Time completed**: Enter the time you completed the survey.
* **Additional comments 2**: Enter any additional comments or observations.
* **Team lead**: Enter the name of the team lead.
* **Team member**: Enter your team member name.
* **Department**: Enter the department you belong to.
* **Job title**: Enter your job title.
* **Manager**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Reporting manager**: Enter the name of your reporting manager.
* **Direct report**: Enter the name of your direct report.
* **Supervisor**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your survey to complete the form.
* **Submit 2**: This is a duplicate of the "Submit" field.

Note that some fields are required, so make sure to provide the necessary information for each field marked as "required". If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out for assistance.
