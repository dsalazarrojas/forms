# late_winter_2020_vitality_society_course_content_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "late_winter_2020_vitality_society_course_content_submission" form is used to submit course content for the Late Winter 2020 Vitality Society course. Fill out this form to provide essential details about your course and ensure it is listed in the course catalog.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the course you are submitting.
2. Provide a brief description of the course.
3. Enter the name of the instructor who will be teaching the course.
4. Enter the email address of the instructor.
5. Enter the phone number of the instructor (optional).
6. Select the start date of the course.
7. Select the end date of the course.
8. Describe the course content in detail.
9. Outline the course outcome, including expected learning objectives.
10. Provide a detailed description of the course outcome.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, Optional): Enter the name of the course you are submitting.
* **Course Description** (`course_description`, Optional): Provide a brief description of the course.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, Optional): Enter the name of the instructor who will be teaching the course.
* **Instructor Email** (`instructor_email`, Optional): Enter the email address of the instructor.
* **Instructor Phone** (`instructor_phone`, Optional): Enter the phone number of the instructor (if available).
* **Course Start Date** (`course_start_date`, Optional): Select the start date of the course.
* **Course End Date** (`course_end_date`, Optional): Select the end date of the course.
* **Course Content** (`course_content`, Optional): Describe the course content in detail.
* **Course Outcome** (`course_outcome`, Optional): Outline the course outcome, including expected learning objectives.
* **Course Outcome Description** (`course_outcome_description`, Optional): Provide a detailed description of the course outcome.

## Tips
* Ensure the course you are submitting is not already listed in the catalog to avoid duplication.
* Double-check the instructor's email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Enter the course start and end dates carefully to ensure they are correct.
* Provide detailed descriptions for the course content and outcome to help attendees understand the course objectives.
