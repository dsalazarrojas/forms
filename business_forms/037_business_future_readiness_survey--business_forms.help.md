# Business Future Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Future Readiness Survey is designed to evaluate an organization's preparedness for future challenges and opportunities. This form assesses key areas such as strategic direction, market changes, innovation, talent, financial resilience, risk, and compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Respond to each question in the sections labeled as "Respondent Information" and "Respondent Section" with your details.
2. In the "Strategic Readiness" section, indicate your level of understanding of the future strategy.
3. In the "Innovation and Technology" section, evaluate the organization's capability to innovate and the adequacy of technology investment.
4. In the "Talent and People" section, assess the ability to attract talent and the investment in skill development.
5. In the "Financial Readiness" section, evaluate financial resilience and flexibility.
6. In the "Risk and Compliance" section, assess awareness of future risks and regulatory compliance readiness.
7. In the "Top Concerns for Future" section, write down your top concerns about the future.
8. In the "Suggested Areas of Focus" section, provide suggestions on areas the business should prioritize.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Information (ID: 2)**: Enter your name to confirm your identity.
* **Respondent Role (ID: 3)**: Describe your position in the organization.
* **Respondent Department (ID: 4)**: Enter your department or team affiliation.
* **Respondent Email (ID: 5)**: Enter your work email address for contact purposes.
* **Clear understanding of future strategy (ID: 7)**: Select the level of understanding you have about the organization's future strategy.
* **Strongly Disagree (ID: 9)**: Select the level of preparedness for market changes.
* **Strongly Disagree (ID: 11)**: Evaluate the organization's capability to innovate.
* **Strongly Disagree (ID: 15)**: Assess the ability to attract talent.
* **Strongly Disagree (ID: 17)**: Evaluate the strength of the leadership pipeline.
* **Strongly Disagree (ID: 19)**: Assess the organization's financial resilience and flexibility.
* **Strongly Disagree (ID: 21)**: Select the level of awareness about future risks.
* **Strongly Disagree (ID: 23)**: Evaluate the organization's regulatory compliance readiness.

## Tips

* Be honest and sincere in your assessments. This survey aims to provide a realistic picture of your organization's future readiness.
* Consider current challenges and successes when evaluating each section.
* This survey is designed to help identify areas that need improvement and areas of strength, so be open-minded and objective.
