# Opinion Piece Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback about a particular piece of communication. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Select the options that best match your opinion for each question.
3. Provide clear and concise answers for the text fields.
4. Be honest and objective in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Opinion Piece Communication Form** (`opinion_piece_communication_form_page`, select_multiple): Select the options that best describe your opinion about the communication piece.
* **Opinion Piece Comment** (`opinion_piece_comment`, text): Share your thoughts about the communication piece. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Opinion Piece Rating** (`opinion_piece_rating`, number): Rate your opinion about the communication piece on a scale of 1-5.
* **Opinion Piece Score** (`opinion_piece_score`, number): Provide a score for the communication piece based on your opinion.
* **Opinion Piece Recommendation** (`opinion_piece_recommendation`, select_one): Suggest improvements or changes for the communication piece.
* **Opinion Piece Liking** (`opinion_piece_liking`, select_one): Select whether you like or dislike the communication piece.
* **Opinion Piece Liking Scale** (`opinion_piece_liking_scale`, select_one): Choose a scale that best describes your liking for the communication piece.
* **Opinion Piece Liking Scale Range** (`opinion_piece_liking_scale_range`, select_multiple): Select a range that describes your liking for the communication piece.
* **Opinion Piece Suggestion** (`opinion_piece_suggestion`, text): Provide suggestions for improvement.
* **Opinion Piece Impression** (`opinion_piece_impression`, note): Share your overall impression of the communication piece.
* **Opinion Piece Liking Scale Range Value** (`opinion_piece_liking_scale_range_value`, number): Provide a value for the liking scale range.
* **Opinion Piece Liking Scale Range Low** (`opinion_piece_liking_scale_range_low`, number): Enter a low value for the liking scale range.
* **Opinion Piece Liking Scale Range High** (`opinion_piece_liking_scale_range_high`, number): Enter a high value for the liking scale range.
* **Opinion Piece Liking Scale Range Difference** (`opinion_piece_liking_scale_range_diff`, number): Calculate the difference between the low and high values of the liking scale range.
* **Opinion Piece Comment** (`opinion_piece_comment`, text): Provide a comment about the liking scale range.
* **Opinion Piece Comment Response** (`opinion_piece_comment_response`, text): Provide a response to the comment.
* **Opinion Piece Recommendation Suggestion** (`opinion_piece_recommendation_suggestion`, text): Provide a suggestion for recommendation.
* **Opinion Piece Recommendation Comment** (`opinion_piece_recommendation_comment`, text): Provide a comment for recommendation.
* **Opinion Piece Liking Scale Range Range Value** (`opinion_piece_liking_scale_range_range_value`, number): Provide a value for the liking scale range range.
* **Opinion Piece Liking Scale Range Range Low Value** (`opinion_piece_liking_scale_range_range_low_value`, number): Enter a low value for the liking scale range range.
* **Opinion Piece Liking Scale Range Range High Value** (`opinion_piece_liking_scale_range_range_high_value`, number): Enter a high value for the liking scale range range.
* **Opinion Piece Liking Scale Range Range Difference Value** (`opinion_piece_liking_scale_range_range_diff_value`, number): Calculate the difference between the low and high values of the liking scale range range.

Please note that this is a comprehensive guide, but it may not cover every possible scenario. If you're still unsure about any question or field, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
