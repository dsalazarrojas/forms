# Factory Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Safety Inspection Form is a tool used to report on the findings of a factory safety inspection. The form helps identify safety concerns and provides a record of the inspection's results.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the sections of the form that apply to the inspection and fill in the relevant information.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
3. Make sure to include comments and observations in the "Comments" field.
4. Review the form for completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Inspection** (`1`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the overall condition of the factory, including any areas of concern.
* **Fire Protection Systems** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): Check "Yes" if fire protection systems are present and functioning properly, or "No" if they are not.
* **Electrical Hazard** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all "Yes" if there are electrical hazards present, or select "No" if there are none.
* **Safety Equipment** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): Check "Yes" if safety equipment is present and in good condition, or "No" if it is not.
* **Hazardous Materials** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Check "Yes" if hazardous materials are present, or "No" if they are not.
* **Personal Protective Equipment** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all "Yes" if personal protective equipment is present and used correctly, or "No" if it is not.
* **First Aid Kits** (`7`, `select_one`, optional): Check "Yes" if first aid kits are present and easily accessible, or "No" if they are not.
* **Emergency Exit Routes** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all "Yes" if emergency exit routes are clearly marked and accessible, or "No" if they are not.
* **Electrical Lockout/Tagout** (`9`, `select_one`, optional): Check "Yes" if electrical lockout/tagout procedures are in place, or "No" if they are not.
* **Safety Procedures** (`10`, `text`, optional): Describe any safety procedures that are in place, or suggest new procedures that would improve safety.
* **Inspection Report** (`11`, `text`, optional): Provide a detailed report of the inspection findings and recommendations.
* **Date Completed** (`12`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the inspection was completed.
* **Time Completed** (`13`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the inspection was completed.
* **Employee Name** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as the person conducting the inspection.
* **Employee Position** (`15`, `text`, optional): Enter your position or title.
* **Employee Department** (`16`, `text`, optional): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Employee Phone** (`17`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Employee Email** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Supervisor Name** (`19`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Position** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter your supervisor's position or title.
* **Supervisor Department** (`21`, `text`, optional): Enter your supervisor's department.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Email** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Comments** (`24`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
* **Submission Date** (`25`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the form is submitted.
