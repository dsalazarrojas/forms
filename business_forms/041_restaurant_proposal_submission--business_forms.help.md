# restaurant-proposal-submission-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect restaurant proposal submissions for business development purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by providing the requested information in the corresponding fields.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Concept** (`restaurant_concept`, text, **false**): A brief description of the restaurant concept, including its unique selling points and offerings.
* **Business Plan** (`business_plan`, text, **false**): A detailed outline of the business strategy, including target market, marketing and sales, operational plan, and financial projections.
* **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`, text, **false**): The proposed name of the restaurant, ensuring it's unique and not already in use.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, **false**): The name of the person or entity submitting the proposal.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, **false**): A valid email address that can be used for communication and updates.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, **false**): A contact phone number that can be reached for further discussion or questions.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, **false**): Select one of the provided options to indicate the desired location for the restaurant. 
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager`, select_one, **false**): Select one of the provided options to indicate the property manager responsible for the location. 
* **Tips**
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask a representative for clarification.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submission.
* Please note that this form is for restaurant proposal submissions only.
* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
