# Agile Transformation Consulting Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from companies interested in Agile transformation consulting services. The purpose of this form is to help us understand your company's specific needs and goals for Agile transformation, allowing us to provide tailored consulting services that meet your unique requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as required).
2. Select your options for the select fields (e.g., company industry, company size, current Agile adoption level, etc.).
3. Provide detailed explanations for the text fields (e.g., primary contact name, specific business challenges, expected outcomes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Company Name** (`applicant_company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Email** (`primary_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Phone** (`primary_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Company Industry** (`company_industry`, select_one, required): Select your company's industry from the provided options.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select the size of your organization from the provided options.
* **Current Agile Maturity** (`current_agile_maturity_header`, note, not applicable): This field is for information only and does not require input.
* **Current Agile Adoption Level** (`current_agile_adoption_level`, select_one, required): Select your current level of Agile adoption from the provided options.
* **Agile Frameworks Currently Used** (`agile_frameworks_currently_used`, select_multiple, required): Select the Agile frameworks currently in use at your company.
* **Primary Transformation Goal** (`primary_transformation_goal`, select_one, required): Select your primary goal for Agile transformation from the provided options.
* **Specific Business Challenges** (`specific_business_challenges`, text, required): Describe the specific business challenges your company faces that Agile transformation should address.
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, required): Describe the specific outcomes you expect from Agile transformation.
* **Consulting Services Needed** (`consulting_services_needed_header`, note, not applicable): This field is for information only and does not require input.
* **Consulting Services Required** (`consulting_services_required`, select_multiple, required): Select the consulting services you need from the provided options.
* **Preferred Engagement Model** (`preferred_engagement_model`, select_one, required): Select the preferred engagement model for your company.
* **Transformation Timeline** (`transformation_timeline`, select_one, required): Select the expected timeline for the Agile transformation from the provided options.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, not applicable): This field is not required but provides insight into your budget range.
* **Decision Makers** (`decision_makers`, text, required): Describe the key decision makers for this consulting engagement.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, not applicable): Provide any additional information about your Agile transformation needs.

Note: Some fields may not have options or may be marked as "not applicable." These fields are for information only and do not require input.
