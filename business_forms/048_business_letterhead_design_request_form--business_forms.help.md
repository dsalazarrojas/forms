# Business Letterhead Design Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the creation of a business letterhead design. It is intended for business owners or managers to provide details about their company's branding and preferences for the design of their letterhead. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the design meets the client's needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name and company name as they should appear on the letterhead.
2. Select the required contact information to be included on the letterhead.
3. Choose a preferred color palette and design style that matches your brand's aesthetic.
4. Select the paper size and file format for the final output.
5. Set a target completion date for the design.
6. Provide any additional design notes or font preferences.
7. Finally, submit the form to initiate the design process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
* **Client Name** (`letterhead_client_name_full`): Enter the client's name as it should appear on the letterhead.
* **Company Name to appear on Letterhead** (`letterhead_company_display_name`): Enter the company name as it should appear on the letterhead.
* **Company Slogan or Tagline** (`company_slogan_tagline_text`): Enter the company's slogan or tagline if desired (optional).

### Design Specifications
* **Required Contact Info on Letterhead** (`contact_info_to_include_selection`): Select all the contact information that should be included on the letterhead, such as Physical Address, Phone Number, Email Address, Website URL, Social Media Handles, and Logo.
* **Preferred Color Palette** (`preferred_color_palette_description`): Enter a description of your preferred color palette, such as "Navy Blue and White" or specific HEX codes.
* **Design Style** (`letterhead_design_style_choice`): Choose the design style that best matches your brand's aesthetic, such as Minimalist and Clean, Professional and Corporate, Modern and Creative, or Traditional and Formal.
* **Paper Size** (`preferred_paper_size_letterhead`): Select the standard paper size for the final file, such as A4 (Standard International), Letter (8.5 x 11 inches), or Legal (8.5 x 14 inches).

### Final Output
* **File Format Needed** (`required_file_format_selection`): Select the file formats required for your workflow, such as High-Res PDF (Print ready), Microsoft Word Template (.dotx), Source File (AI / PSD / Figma), or PNG (With transparent background).
* **Target Completion Date** (`design_delivery_deadline_date`): Set the target completion date for the design.
* **Additional Design Notes** (`additional_design_notes_text`): Enter any specific font preferences or layout requirements.
* **Request Date** (`letterhead_request_submission_date`): Enter the date you are submitting this request.
* **Additional Design Notes** (`additional_design_notes_text`): Enter any additional design notes or comments.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed to ensure accurate and timely completion of the design.
* Double-check your selections and entries for accuracy and consistency with your brand's branding and preferences.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
