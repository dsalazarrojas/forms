# New Supplier Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about potential new suppliers for review and consideration. The purpose of this form is to gather general information, contact details, and other relevant data from suppliers who wish to do business with your company. The form is a standard template and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any form platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please provide your general information in the field "general_info".
2.  Enter your contact details in the "contact_details" field.
3.  Provide an overview of your products or services in the "offerings" field.
4.  Attach any relevant documents for our review in the "documents" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information
*   **General Information** (text, required): This field is where you can provide a brief overview of your company or individual. Please include your company name, business name, or your personal name, and any other relevant details you think would be helpful for our review.

### Contact Details
*   **Contact Details** (text, optional): In this field, you can provide your contact information, such as your email address, phone number, or any other means of communication. We will use this information to get in touch with you for further questions or follow-up inquiries.

### Offerings
*   **Offerings** (text, optional): Here, you can describe your products or services, including their features, benefits, and any other relevant information that would help us understand what you are offering. Be as detailed as possible, but feel free to focus on the most important aspects of your offerings.

### Documents
*   **Documents** (text, optional): If you have any supporting documents, such as business cards, product brochures, or contracts, you can attach them here. This will help us get a better understanding of your business.
