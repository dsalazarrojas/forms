# Renovation Material Delivery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Renovation Material Delivery Form is used to gather information about a renovation project's materials and schedule. It helps to streamline the material delivery process, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and communicated effectively between departments. This form should be used for every renovation project.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Project Details**: Provide a brief description of the project, including its name and location.
2. Fill in the **Customer Info** section with the customer's name and contact information.
3. Enter the date and time for **Delivery Date** and **Signature Time**, respectively.
4. Select the desired material from the **Material Order** field, if applicable.
5. Enter the **Delivery Address** where the materials will be delivered.
6. Provide the customer's **Contact Number** and **Email** for communication purposes.
7. Add any **Order Notes** to help the logistics team understand the delivery requirements.
8. Select the **Material Ordered** and **Quantity Ordered** to calculate the total cost.
9. Choose the **Order Status** and **Delivery Status** to update the project's status.
10. Select the **Material Return** and **Delivery Return** options based on the project's needs.
11. Select the **Customer Signature** and choose the date and time for **Signature Date** and **Signature Time**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details**: A brief description of the project and its location.
	+ Enter the project name and location to provide context.
* **Customer Info**: Customer's name and contact information.
	+ Enter the customer's name and contact number for communication purposes.
* **Material Order**: Select the desired material for the project.
* **Delivery Date**: The date when the material is to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time**: The time when the material is to be delivered.
* **Delivery Address**: The location where the material will be delivered.
* **Contact Number**: The customer's contact number for communication purposes.
* **Email**: The customer's email for communication purposes.
* **Order Notes**: Additional information about the delivery requirements.
* **Material Ordered**: Select the material that is ordered.
* **Quantity Ordered**: The quantity of the material ordered.
* **Unit Price**: The price per unit of the material ordered.
* **Total Cost**: The total cost of the material ordered.
* **Order Status**: The status of the order (Active or Inactive).
* **Delivery Status**: The status of the delivery (Active or Inactive).
* **Material Return**: Select if the material can be returned.
* **Delivery Return**: Select if the delivery can be returned.
* **Customer Signature**: A space for the customer to sign.
* **Signature Date**: The date the customer signed.
* **Signature Time**: The time the customer signed.
* **Material Delivery Form**: Select if the form is related to material delivery.
* **Customer Approval**: Select if the customer has approved the order.
