# Expense Statement Generation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Expense Statement Generation Request Form is designed to request an expense statement from the system. The form is used to provide relevant information about an employee's expenses, including the period for which the statement is being requested, employee details, and expense type and category.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the period for which you would like to receive the expense statement by entering the "Period From" and "Period To" dates.
2. Enter your Employee ID and Name.
3. Select the type of expense you are requesting (Company or Personal).
4. Choose the category of expense (Travel, Food, Accommodation, Entertainment, or Other).
5. Enter the expense amount and attach a receipt for reimbursement.
6. Confirm whether you would like to submit the request by selecting "Yes" or "No".
7. Sign your employee signature and the form will be sent to the manager for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Period From**: Enter the start date of the period for which you would like to receive the expense statement.
* **Period To**: Enter the end date of the period for which you would like to receive the expense statement.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your Employee ID.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it is recorded in the employee database.
* **Expense Type**: Select the type of expense you are requesting (Company or Personal).
* **Expense Category**: Choose the category of expense (Travel, Food, Accommodation, Entertainment, or Other).
* **Expense Amount**: Enter the total amount of expense incurred during the selected period.
* **Upload Reimbursement Receipt**: Attach a receipt for reimbursement of the expense.
* **Submit**: Confirm whether you would like to submit the request by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Employee Signature**: Sign your employee signature to confirm the request.
* **Manager Signature**: This is for the manager's review and approval.
* **Date Signed**: Date and time of when the form was signed.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have all necessary supporting documents, such as receipts, before submitting the request.
* Make sure to select the correct period and expense type to ensure accurate expense statements are generated.
* Attach a clear and legible receipt for upload to facilitate easy reimbursement processing.
* The manager's signature serves as an approval and acknowledgment of the request.
* The date and time of submission is recorded for audit and record-keeping purposes.
