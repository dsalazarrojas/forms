# Team Management Software Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for team management software requisitions. Please fill it out accurately and completely to ensure that your software requests are processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields (if any) with the requested information.
3. Answer each field according to your needs and understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Team Management Software** (`team_management_software_requisition_form_1`): Enter the type of team management software being requested (e.g., project management, collaboration, etc.).
- **Project Name** (`project_name_2`): Enter the name of the project that requires team management software.
- **Software Requisitions** (`software_requisitions_3`): Choose whether team management software requisitions are necessary for this project.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you need team management software.
	+ Select 'No' otherwise.
- **Project Description** (`project_description_4`): Enter a brief description of the project that requires team management software.
- **Software Requester** (`software_requester_5`): Choose who requested the team management software (you, team lead, or project lead).
	+ Select 'Yes' if you requested the software.
	+ Select 'No' otherwise.
- **Team Lead** (`team_lead_6`): Choose whether the team lead is requesting team management software.
	+ Select 'Yes' if team lead is requesting the software.
	+ Select 'No' otherwise.
- **Project Lead** (`project_lead_7`): Choose whether the project lead is requesting team management software.
	+ Select 'Yes' if project lead is requesting the software.
	+ Select 'No' otherwise.
- **Software Request Date** (`software_request_date_8`): Enter the date when the team management software is required.

## Tips
- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor for clarification.
- Please review the form carefully before submitting it. 

This form is designed to ensure that team management software requests are processed accurately and completely, and that all necessary information is collected for processing software requests. Please fill it out carefully and accurately.
