# Order Fulfillment Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by business stakeholders to track and update the status of orders in the fulfillment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the available sections of the form, which include:
	* Order Details (Order ID, Customer Name, Customer Address)
	* Order Status (Active/Inactive)
	* Shipping Details (Shipping Details, Shipping Tracking Number)
	* Fulfillment Status and Reason (Fulfillment Status, Fulfillment Status Reason)
	* Tracking Information (Tracking Status, Shipping Carrier)
	* Estimated and Actual Arrival Dates (Estimated Arrival Date, Actual Arrival Date)
	* Fulfillment Cost
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the unique ID of the order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the customer.
* **Order Status** (`order_fulfillment_monitoring_form_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the order (Active or Inactive).
* **Order Date** (`order_fulfillment_monitoring_form_3`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the order.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the shipping details of the order.
* **Shipping Tracking Number** (`shipping_tracking_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the shipping tracking number of the order.
* **Fulfillment Status** (`fulfillment_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the fulfillment (Active or Inactive).
* **Fulfillment Notes** (`fulfillment_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter the notes about the fulfillment of the order.
* **Fulfillment Status Reason** (`fulfillment_status_reason`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reason for the fulfillment status.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping`, `text`, required: false): Enter the shipping details of the order.
* **Prophets** (`order_fulfillment_prophets`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the prophets associated with the fulfillment (Yes or No).
* **Inventory Level** (`inventory_level`, `number`, required: false): Enter the current inventory level.
* **Fulfillment Cost** (`fulfillment_cost`, `number`, required: false): Enter the estimated fulfillment cost.
* **Fulfillment Time** (`fulfillment_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the fulfillment time.
* **Tracking Status** (`tracking_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the tracking (Active or Inactive).
* **Shipping Carrier** (`shipping_carrier`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the shipping carrier (Yes or No).
* **Estimated Arrival Date** (`estimated_arrival_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the estimated arrival date.
* **Actual Arrival Date** (`actual_arrival_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the actual arrival date.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact phone number of the customer.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the contact e-mail of the customer.
