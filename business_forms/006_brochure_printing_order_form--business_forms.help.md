# Brochure Printing Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for brochures. It collects information on client details, printing specifications, artwork, and shipping information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the client information section with your details.
2.  Select the brochure format and finished size for your brochure.
3.  Choose the paper stock and printing color for your brochure.
4.  Select the coating option if desired.
5.  Enter the quantity of brochures to print.
6.  Provide artwork information, including status and file format.
7.  Enter the number of pages for your brochure.
8.  Add any design requirements if applicable.
9.  Enter your shipping address and select the desired delivery method.
10.  Specify the requested delivery date if applicable.
11.  Add any additional notes or instructions.
12.  Confirm your order by agreeing to the printing terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or business placing the order.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the company name if applicable.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for order communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Brochure Format** (`brochure_format`, select_one, required): Select the format you need for your brochure: Bi-Fold, Tri-Fold, Z-Fold, Gate Fold, Double Parallel, or French Fold.
* **Finished Size** (`finished_size`, select_one, required): Select the finished size for your brochure: Letter (8.5x11 folded to 8.5x5.5), Legal (8.5x14 folded to 8.5x7), A4 (210x297mm), or Custom Size.
* **Paper Stock** (`paper_stock`, select_one, required): Choose the paper stock type: 80lb Gloss Text, 100lb Gloss Text, 80lb Matte Text, 100lb Matte Text, 14pt Cardstock, 16pt Cardstock, or Recycled Stock.
* **Print Color** (`print_color`, select_one, required): Select the print color specifications: Full Color Both Sides, Full Color One Side, Black One Side, Black Both Sides, or Spot Color.
* **Coating Options** (`coating`, select_multiple, optional): Select any coating options: No Coating, UV Coating, Aqueous Coating, Soft Touch Lamination, or Matte Lamination.
* **Quantity Ordered** (`quantity_ordered`, number, required): Enter the number of brochures to print.
* **Artwork Status** (`artwork_status`, select_one, required): Choose the artwork status: Ready to Print, Need Design Services, or Need Modifications.
* **File Format** (`file_format`, select_multiple, optional): Select the format of your artwork: PDF, AI, PSD, EPS, INDD, JPG, or PNG.
* **Number of Pages** (`number_of_pages`, number, required): Enter the number of pages in the brochure.
* **Design Requirements** (`design_requirements`, text, optional): Describe any design requirements if applicable.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter your full shipping address.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Select the delivery method: Standard Ground Shipping, Express Shipping, Rush Production, or Customer Pickup.
* **Requested Delivery Date** (`requested_delivery_date`, date, optional): Specify the desired delivery date if applicable.
* **Additional Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional): Add any special instructions or notes.
* **I Agree to the Printing Terms and Conditions** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm your order by agreeing to the printing terms and conditions.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by customers who want to order brochures. Please ensure you fill out all required fields to complete your order correctly.
