# Consulting Talent Partnership Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect partnership contact information from companies interested in collaborating with the consulting firm. It will help the consulting firm understand the company's needs and provide relevant services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's full name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Select the industry your company operates in from the "Industry" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your company's website URL in the "Company Website" field (optional).
4. In the "Type of Partnership Interested In" section, select one or more partnership types that interest you.
5. Describe what you are looking to achieve through partnership in the "Primary Partnership Goals" field.
6. If relevant, estimate the number of talent roles you are looking to fill in the "Estimated Talent Needs" field.
7. In the "Contact Person Details" section, enter the name of the contact person.
8. Enter your job title and email address.
9. Choose your preferred contact method from the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown menu.
10. Provide more information about your inquiry in the "Inquiry Details" field (optional).
11. Enter today's date in the "Submission Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter your company's full name.
* **Industry**: Select one or more industries your company operates in.
* **Company Website**: Enter your company's website URL (optional).
* **Type of Partnership Interested In**: Select one or more partnership types you are interested in.
* **Primary Partnership Goals**: Describe what you are looking to achieve through partnership.
* **Estimated Talent Needs**: If relevant, enter the number of talent roles you are looking to fill.
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the full name of the contact person.
* **Job Title**: Enter the contact person's job title.
* **Email Address**: Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose your preferred contact method (Email, Phone Call, or LinkedIn).
* **Inquiry Details**: Provide more information about your inquiry (optional).
* **Submission Date**: Enter today's date.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your inquiry is processed correctly.
* Be as specific as possible when answering the "Primary Partnership Goals" and "Estimated Talent Needs" fields.
* If you have a preferred contact method, please select it to ensure the consulting firm knows how to reach you.
* If you have any additional information or questions, provide it in the "Inquiry Details" field (optional).
