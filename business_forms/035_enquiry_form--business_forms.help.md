# enquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The enquiry_form is a tool for users to submit inquiries for various services or issues they need help with.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of inquiry you'd like to make ('Yes' or 'No').
2. Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
3. Enter your email address for the relevant team to contact you.
4. Enter your phone number, so the team can call you back.
5. Provide a brief message about your inquiry.
6. Select a date for your inquiry.
7. Choose a time for your inquiry.
8. (Optional) You can also choose to inquire about additional details or tools needed for the task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How would you like to enquire** (`enquiry_type`, required): Select 'Yes' if you want to enquire about a service, and 'No' if you don't.
- **Name** (`name`, required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
- **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address, so the team can contact you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, required): Enter your phone number, so the team can call you back.
- **Message** (`message`, required): Provide a brief description of your inquiry.
- **Date of Enquiry** (`enquiry_date`, required): Choose a date for your inquiry.
- **Time of Enquiry** (`enquiry_time`, required): Select a time for your inquiry.
- **Tool** (`assigned_tool`, optional): You can choose an option if you have any tools in mind for the task.
- **Tool ID** (`assigned_tool_id`, optional): Enter the tool's ID if you've already selected a tool.
- **Enquiry Details** (`enquiry_details`, optional): You can choose to enquire about additional details.
- **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, required): Provide any additional information that the team may need to know.
 
## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields marked as required are filled in accurately.
- You can submit multiple 'Yes' or 'No' for 'Enquiry Details' field, depending on the services or issues you're inquiring about.
- Be as detailed as possible when providing 'Additional Details', so the team can better understand your concern.
