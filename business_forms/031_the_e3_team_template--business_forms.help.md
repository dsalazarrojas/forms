# the_e3_team_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "the_e3_team_template" form is designed to collect comprehensive information about the E3 team, including their contact details, categorization, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team member's **first name** in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter the team member's **last name** in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter the team member's **email** in the "email" field.
4. Enter the team member's **phone number** in the "phone" field.
5. Enter a brief **note** for any additional comments or information in the "note" field (optional).
6. Select the **category** of the team member from the drop-down list.
7. For **select_multiple** fields, select all applicable options that apply to the team member.
8. Select the **select_one** field to indicate a single option that applies to the team member.
9. Enter the **date** of the event or occurrence in the "date" field (if required).
10. Enter the **time** of the event or occurrence in the "time" field (if required).
11. Select the **date range** for the event or occurrence in the "date_range" field (if required).
12. Select the **time range** for the event or occurrence in the "time_range" field (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (Required, text): Enter the team member's first name.
* **last_name** (Required, text): Enter the team member's last name.
* **email** (Required, email): Enter the team member's email address.
* **phone** (Required, text): Enter the team member's phone number.
* **note** (Optional, note): Enter any additional comments or information about the team member.
* **date** (Optional, date): Enter the date of the event or occurrence (if required).
* **time** (Optional, time): Enter the time of the event or occurrence (if required).
* **category** (Required, select_one): Select the category of the team member.
* **select_multiple** (Optional, select_multiple): Select all applicable options that apply to the team member.
* **select_one** (Optional, select_one): Select a single option that applies to the team member.
* **date_range** (Optional, date): Enter the date range for the event or occurrence (if required).
* **time_range** (Optional, time): Enter the time range for the event or occurrence (if required).

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* If a field is optional, fill it in if it's relevant to the team member's data.
* Double-check your selections in the select fields to ensure accuracy.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure all fields are completed correctly.
