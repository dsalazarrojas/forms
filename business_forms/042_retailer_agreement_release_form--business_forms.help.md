# Retailer Agreement Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from retailers, including agreement details, store information, and contact details. It helps the company manage and track agreements with retailers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each select and select multiple fields.
3. Provide detailed notes and descriptions where necessary, such as in the Agreement Term Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retailer Agreement ID** (`retailer_agreement_id`): A unique identifier assigned to the agreement.
* **Agreement Date** (`retailer_agreement_date`): The date when the agreement was signed or made.
* **Partner Details** (`partner_details`): Select the level of partnership or collaboration with the retailer.
	+ Select one of the options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Business Name** (`business_name`): The name of the business or retailer.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`): A field for notes and descriptions of the agreement terms.
* **Retail Location** (`retailer_location`): Whether the store is located within a retail location or not.
	+ Select one of the options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`): Select which store hours the retailer operates.
	+ Select one or more of the options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`): The current status of the agreement.
	+ Select one of the options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Retailer Notes** (`retailer_notes`): Any additional notes or comments about the retailer.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`): The address of the store or retail location.
* **Store Contact** (`store_contact`): The contact information for the store.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`): The email address of the partner or retailer.
* **Agreement Type** (`agreement_type`): The type of agreement made with the retailer.
	+ Select one of the options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Agreement Term** (`agreement_term`): Select which agreement terms apply to the partnership.
	+ Select one or more of the options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`): The phone number of the partner or retailer.
* **Partner Fax** (`partner_fax`): The fax number of the partner or retailer.
* **Partner Website** (`partner_website`): The website of the partner or retailer.
* **Partner Contact** (`partner_contact`): The contact information for the partner or retailer.
* **Store Manager** (`store_manager`): The name of the store manager.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`): The phone number of the store manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): The email address of the store manager.
* **Agreement Term Notes** (`agreement_term_notes`): Any additional notes or comments about the agreement terms.
* **Agreement Term Frequency** (`agreement_term_frequency`): Notes on the frequency of the agreement term.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`): The physical location of the store.
* **Manager Address** (`manager_address`): The address of the store manager.
