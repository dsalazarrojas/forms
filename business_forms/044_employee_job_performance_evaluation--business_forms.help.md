# Employee Job Performance Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of an employee, assessing their skills, job areas, and overall job performance. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to provide valuable insights for employee growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate options for the following fields:
	* Job Performance Evaluation: Choose whether the employee's performance is satisfactory or not.
	* Job Area: Select the department or team the employee works in (e.g., Sales, Marketing, HR).
2. Describe the employee's skills in the text field.
3. Provide a performance rating (number) and performance score (number) for the employee.
4. Identify areas where the employee needs improvement and indicate whether they have been addressed.
5. Provide an action plan to help the employee improve in those areas.
6. Enter the date of the review.
7. Enter the name of the reviewer.
8. Enter any additional comments or feedback for the reviewer.
9. Enter the employee's ID number.
10. Enter any comments from the manager.
11. Enter any comments from the reviewer.
12. Mark the evaluation as completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Performance Evaluation**: Select whether the employee's performance is satisfactory or not.
* **Job Area**: Select the department or team the employee works in (e.g., Sales, Marketing, HR).
* **Skills**: Describe the employee's skills and expertise in a text field.
* **Performance Rating**: Provide a numerical rating (1-5) for the employee's performance.
* **Performance Score**: Provide a numerical score (1-5) for the employee's performance.
* **Functional Areas**: Identify areas where the employee needs improvement.
* **Areas of Improvement**: Indicate whether those areas have been addressed.
* **Action Plan**: Provide a plan to help the employee improve in those areas.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Reviewer**: Enter the name of the person conducting the review.
* **Reviewed by**: Enter the name of the person being reviewed.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback for the reviewer.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any comments from the manager.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Evaluation Completed**: Mark the evaluation as completed.

Note: Some fields are not required, but all fields are recommended for a thorough evaluation.
