# Quality Control Release Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Quality Control Release Survey" form is used to collect data related to the release of products or batches from a company's quality control process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and gather all necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the form fields as follows:
	* **Release Survey**: Enter a brief description or notes about the release survey, if necessary.
	* **Inspection Dates**: Enter the dates of the quality inspection, in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
	* **Product IDs**: Enter the product IDs associated with the inspected products or batches.
	* **Batch IDs**: Enter the batch IDs associated with the inspected products or batches.
	* **Results**: Select the correct results of the quality inspection (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
	* **Release Decisions**: Select the release decision for the inspected products or batches (e.g., "Released" or "Not Released").
	* **Drag and Drop Interface**: Enter the number of products or batches that require manual inspection or review.
	* **Efficient Data Collection**: Enter any additional information or comments about the data collection process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Survey**: A text field for entering release survey notes or descriptions.
* **Inspection Dates**: A date field for entering the dates of the quality inspection.
* **Product IDs**: A text field for entering product IDs associated with the inspected products or batches.
* **Batch IDs**: A text field for entering batch IDs associated with the inspected products or batches.
* **Results**: A multiple-choice field for selecting the results of the quality inspection (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Release Decisions**: A single-choice field for selecting the release decision for the inspected products or batches (e.g., "Released" or "Not Released").
* **Drag and Drop Interface**: A numerical field for entering the number of products or batches that require manual inspection or review.
* **Efficient Data Collection**: A text field for entering any additional information or comments about the data collection process.
