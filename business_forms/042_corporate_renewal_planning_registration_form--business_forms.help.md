# Corporate Renewal Planning Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Renewal Planning Registration Form is used to collect information from corporate employees for planning and registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Enter your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
3. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
4. Enter your Email address in the field labeled "Email".
5. Enter your Phone number in the field labeled "Phone".
6. Enter your company name in the field labeled "Company".
7. Enter your role in the field labeled "Role".
8. Enter the event name in the field labeled "Event".
9. Select a date for your event participation in the field labeled "Date".
10. If applicable, enter the time for your event participation in the field labeled "Time".
11. If necessary, enter any additional notes in the field labeled "Note".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field. This is a required field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field. This is a required field.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address. This will be used for correspondence and event reminders.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company**: Enter your company name or department where you work.
* **Role**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Event**: Enter the name of the event you will be participating in.
* **Date**: Select a date for your event participation. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time**: If applicable, select the time for your event participation. Use 24-hour format.
* **Note**: If necessary, enter any additional notes for your event participation.
