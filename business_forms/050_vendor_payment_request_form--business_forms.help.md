# Vendor Payment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a payment request to the system for a vendor's account.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your company name in the **Company Name** field.
3. Enter the date of your request in the **Request Date** field.
4. Enter the payment amount you are requesting in the **Payment Amount** field.
5. Enter the vendor's ID in the **Vendor ID** field.
6. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your payment request.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the payment amount you are requesting.
* **Vendor ID** (`vendor_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the vendor's ID number.
