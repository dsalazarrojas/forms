# AI Impact On Small Businesses - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the impact of Artificial Intelligence on small businesses. It aims to understand how AI is currently being used, the benefits and challenges faced by businesses, and their future plans regarding AI implementation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly. Each section is designed to gather specific information about your business's AI experience and plans.
2. Select all relevant options for each multiple-choice question.
3. If you have any additional comments or insights, please use the "Additional Comments or Insights" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business / Company Name**: Enter your business's official name.
* **Industry Sector**: Select the category that best describes your business's primary industry (e.g., Retail / E-commerce, Professional Services, etc.).
* **Number of Employees**: Choose the number of employees in your organization (e.g., Solopreneur, Micro, Small, etc.).
* **Years in Operation**: Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **What is your current level of AI adoption?**: Select the level of AI adoption you currently have in place (e.g., Not currently using or considering, Actively researching / Planning, etc.).
* **Which AI-related tools are you currently using?**: Select all the AI-related tools you are currently using (e.g., Generative AI, AI Chatbots, etc.).
* **Primary Benefits Experienced or Expected**: Select all the primary benefits you have experienced or expect from AI (e.g., Increased Efficiency / Productivity, Cost Savings / Reduced Overheads, etc.).
* **What are the main barriers to adopting AI in your business?**: Select all the main barriers you face when adopting AI (e.g., High Implementation Costs, Lack of In-house Technical Expertise, etc.).
* **Impact on Hiring and Staffing**: Choose the impact AI has had on your workforce (e.g., Reduced need for staff, Increased need for staff, etc.).
* **Future Investment Plans (Next 12 Months)**: Select how you plan to change your AI spending in the next 12 months (e.g., Significantly Increase, Slightly Increase, etc.).
* **How concerned are you about competitors using AI more effectively than you?**: Rate your level of concern on a scale of 1 to 5 (Not concerned, Somewhat concerned, etc.).
* **Additional Comments or Insights**: Share any other thoughts or insights about how AI is impacting your specific niche or business.
