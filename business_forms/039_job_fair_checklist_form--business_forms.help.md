# Job Fair Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you collect and organize information for job fairs and job postings. It guides users through a comprehensive checklist of details about the event, job, and company, ensuring a standardized and thorough collection of data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each field as needed.
2. Enter relevant details in the provided fields.
3. Review your input to ensure accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (`general-information`): A brief description of the event.

### Company Information
* **Company Name** (`company-name`): Enter the full name of the company.
* **Job Title** (`job-title`): Enter the title of the job posting.
* **Job Role** (`job-role`): Enter the role or position being offered (optional).
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location of the company or event.

### Job Details
* **Job Type** (`job-type`): Select the type of job (e.g., full-time, part-time, internship).
* **Start Date** (`start-date`): Enter the start date of the job.
* **End Date** (`end-date`): Enter the end date of the job.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact-phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact-email`): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Event Description** (`event-description`): A brief description of the event.
* **Job Description** (`job-description`): A detailed description of the job responsibilities and requirements.
* **Job Responsibilities** (`job-responsibilities`): List the job responsibilities.
* **Company Link** (`company-link`): A link to the company's website or social media page.
* **Job Link** (`job-link`): A link to the job posting or application page.
* **Event Notes** (`event-notes`): Additional notes about the event.
* **Job Notes** (`job-notes`): Additional notes about the job.
* **Job Status** (`job-status`): Select the status of the job (e.g., Active, Pending, Rejected).
* **Job Requirements** (`job-requirements`): List the job requirements.
* **Job Requirements List** (`job-requirements-list`): Select the job requirements.
* **Event Status** (`event-status`): Select the status of the event (e.g., Status 1, Status 2, Status 3).
* **Event Notes** (`event-notes`): Additional notes about the event.
* **Company Notes** (`company-notes`): Additional notes about the company.
* **Event Date** (`event-date`): Enter the date of the event.

### Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* Review your input carefully before submitting.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
