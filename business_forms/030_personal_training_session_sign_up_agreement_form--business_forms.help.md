# Personal Training Session Sign Up Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for personal training sessions sign up, where clients provide their details and agree to the terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the fields provided.
2. Enter your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
3. Choose a date and time for your personal training session from the drop-down options.
4. Click on **E-Signature** to digitally sign this form.
5. Agree or disagree with the terms and conditions by selecting the corresponding option.
6. If you have a second person accompanying you, enter their **First Name** and **Last Name**, and also agree or disagree with the terms and conditions for them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your valid email address.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select a date for your personal training session.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Select a time for your personal training session.
* **E-Signature** (`esig_name`, `note`, required: false): Digitally sign this form by clicking on this field.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **I agree with the terms and conditions** (`agreement`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you agree or disagree with the terms and conditions.
* **First Name 2** (`first_name_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the first name of the second person accompanying you.
* **Last Name 2** (`last_name_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the last name of the second person accompanying you.
* **I agree with the terms and conditions 2** (`agreement_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you agree or disagree with the terms and conditions for the second person.
* **E-Signature 2** (`esig_2`, `note`, required: false): Digitally sign this form for the second person accompanying you.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- The form will only be processed if all required fields are filled in correctly.
- Make sure to carefully read and agree with the terms and conditions before completing this form.
