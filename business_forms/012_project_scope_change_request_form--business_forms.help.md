# Project Scope Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Scope Change Request Form is used to collect and track changes to a project's scope.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that all necessary information is captured.
2. Ensure that you select the correct "Requested By" option, which includes the user who is making the request.
3. Enter the "Request Description" field with a clear and concise description of the change.
4. Fill out the "Request Date" field with the current date.
5. Enter the "Request Reason" field with a brief explanation of why the change is needed.
6. For the "Requesting Manager's" fields, fill out the fields with the name, email, job title, department, phone number, and location of the person making the request.
7. For the "Affected Project Team Members" fields, list the team members who will be affected by the change, along with their name, email, department, job title, phone number, and location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Scope Change Request Form Data** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data`, text, required): A description of the change, e.g., "Add new feature" or "Delete feature".
* **Request Description** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_1`, text, optional): A clear and concise description of the change.
* **Request Date** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_2`, date, optional): The date the request was made.
* **Request Reason** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_3`, text, optional): A brief explanation of why the change is needed.
* **Requested By** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_4`, select_one, optional): The user who is making the request.
* **Requested By Email** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_5`, email, optional): The email of the person making the request.
* **Requested By Phone** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_6`, text, optional): The phone number of the person making the request.
* **Requested By User Name** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_7`, text, optional): The name of the person making the request.
* **Requested By User Department** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_8`, text, optional): The department of the person making the request.
* **Requested By User Title** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_9`, text, optional): The job title of the person making the request.
* **Requesting Manager's Name** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_10`, text, optional): The name of the manager making the request.
* **Requesting Manager's Email** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_11`, email, optional): The email of the manager making the request.
* **Requesting Manager's Job Title** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_12`, text, optional): The job title of the manager making the request.
* **Requesting Manager's Department** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_13`, text, optional): The department of the manager making the request.
* **Requesting Manager's Phone** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_14`, text, optional): The phone number of the manager making the request.
* **Requesting Manager's Location** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_15`, text, optional): The location of the manager making the request.
* **Affected Project Team Members** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_16`, text, optional): A list of team members who will be affected by the change.
* **Affected Project Team Members Location** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_17`, text, optional): The location of the team members affected by the change.
* **Affected Project Team Members Department** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_18`, text, optional): The department of the team members affected by the change.
* **Affected Project Team Members Job Title** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_19`, text, optional): The job title of the team members affected by the change.
* **Affected Project Team Members Phone** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_20`, text, optional): The phone number of the team members affected by the change.
* **Affected Project Team Members Email** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_21`, email, optional): The email of the team members affected by the change.
* **Project Scope Change Request Form Data 23** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_22`, text, optional): Not used, but included in the form.
* **Project Scope Change Request Form Data 24** (`project_scope_change_request_form_data_23`, text, optional): Not used, but included in the form.

## Tips

* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form.
* If unsure about any field, ask the project manager or team lead for guidance.
* Ensure that all necessary fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check that the form is correctly filled out before submitting it.
