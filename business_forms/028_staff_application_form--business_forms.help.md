# Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Application Form is designed to gather essential information from staff members about their personal details, work experiences, and availability. The form is divided into several sections, each covering a specific area of the staff member's profile.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide all required information for each section.
3. Make sure to review and edit your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, required/optional): This section is for providing basic information about yourself, including your name, address, and contact details.
* **On-Call Positions** (`on_call_positions`, text, required/optional): This field is for listing any on-call positions you may hold or have held in the past.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required/optional): Describe your previous work experiences, highlighting relevant skills and achievements.
* **Education** (`education`, text, required/optional): List any educational qualifications or training you have completed.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, required/optional): Specify your work availability, including days off, vacation periods, or any other relevant information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Provide information about your emergency contact person, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, required/optional): This section is for providing any other relevant information about yourself that is not covered in the other sections.

Note: The staff member's supervisor or HR representative will review and verify the information provided before making any decisions or taking any actions. The form is designed to be used in conjunction with other evaluation methods, such as performance reviews and feedback sessions.
