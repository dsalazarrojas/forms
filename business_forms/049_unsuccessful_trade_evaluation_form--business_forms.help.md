# Unsuccessful Trade Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate an unsuccessful trade and provide a reason for its failure. It is a necessary step in the process to identify areas for improvement and prevent similar issues in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the ticket number of the trade that was submitted.
2. Provide a reason for the trade to be unsuccessful. This is a required field.
3. Enter any additional details regarding the reason for the trade to be unsuccessful.
4. Review and update the status of the trade as needed.
5. Enter any comments or actions required for the trade.
6. Select the appropriate status for the trade (e.g., completed, in progress, pending, rejected).
7. Enter any further review details or comments.
8. Enter the username of the person who submitted the trade.
9. Enter the username of the person who reviewed the trade.
10. Enter the date on which the trade was submitted.
11. Enter the date on which the trade was reviewed.
12. Enter the status of the review.
13. Enter any additional comments or further review details.
14. Enter the reason for rejection.
15. Enter the details for the reason for rejection.
16. Enter the username of the person who performed the follow-up action.
17. Enter the date of the follow-up action.
18. Select the status of the follow-up action (e.g., completed, in progress, pending, rejected).
19. Enter any additional comments or further review details.
20. Select the status of the trade as needed.
