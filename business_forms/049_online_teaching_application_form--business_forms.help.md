# Online Teaching Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for online teaching positions. It is designed to gather essential data such as contact details, teaching experience, and availability. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the First Name field. This is the name you want to be known by in our records.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the Last Name field. This is your surname.
3. Enter your valid **Email** address in the Email field. This is how we will contact you for further information or to schedule an interview.
4. Upload your CV in the **CV Upload** field. This should include your resume, which will be reviewed for your application.
5. Select the type of **Course(s) you are applying for** in the What type of course? field. Choose one or more options to indicate your area of expertise.
6. Enter any **Certifications** you have in the Certifications field. This could include any relevant qualifications or licenses.
7. Choose the **Course Type** for which you are applying. Select one option to indicate the type of course you are interested in teaching.
8. Enter the **Start Date** you are available to begin teaching. This is the date when you are ready to start your new role.
9. Enter the **End Date** you are available to end teaching. This is the date when you will finish your teaching commitment.
10. Specify the number of **Hours** you are able to commit to teaching in the Hours field. This will help us understand your availability.
11. Add any **Comments** in the Comments field. This is an opportunity to provide any additional information about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is to be known in our records. (Required)
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname. (Required)
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address to be contacted for further information. (Required)
* **CV Upload**: Upload a copy of your resume to be reviewed as part of your application. (Optional)
* **What type of course?**: Select one or more options from the list to indicate your area of expertise. (Required)
* **Certifications**: Enter any relevant qualifications or licenses you hold. (Optional)
* **Course Type**: Choose the type of course you are interested in teaching. (Required)
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when you are available to begin teaching. (Optional)
* **End Date**: Enter the date when you will finish your teaching commitment. (Optional)
* **Hours**: Specify the number of hours you can commit to teaching. (Required)
* **Comments**: Add any additional information about your application. (Optional)
