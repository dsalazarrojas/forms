# financial_operations_review_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The financial operations review request form is used to submit a request for financial assistance from the finance team. It's a simple form that gathers the necessary information to review and process the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit Request" section and enter a brief description of your request.
2. In the "Request Context" field, provide any additional context that's helpful for the finance team to understand your request.
3. If you're attaching any documents to support your request, click on the "Upload Documents" field and upload them.
4. Select the finance team(s) that you'd like to review your request from the "Finance Team" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Submit Request
* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your request for financial assistance.

### Request Context
* **Request Context** (`request_context`, text, optional): Provide any additional context that's helpful for the finance team to understand your request.

### Upload Documents
* **Upload Documents** (`documents_to_attach`, text, optional): If you're attaching any documents to support your request, click on this field and upload them.

### Finance Team
* **Finance Team** (`finance_team`, select multiple, optional): Select the finance team(s) that you'd like to review your request. The available options are: 
  * Finance Team A
  * Finance Team B
  * Finance Team C

## Tips
* Make sure to provide enough detail in the "Request Context" field to help the finance team understand your request.
* If you're attaching documents, make sure they're relevant to your request and follow any applicable file size or format guidelines.
* Check your email for any updates from the finance team after submitting your request.
