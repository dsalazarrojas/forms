# Consent Form Background Check - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Consent Form Background Check" is used to collect information required for background checks, ensuring the authenticity of the applicant's identity and the validity of their authorization for background investigation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. List any other names you have used in the past, including maiden names or aliases.
3. Enter your Social Security Number for accurate identification.
4. Provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Enter your driver's license number.
6. Enter the state that issued your driver's license.
7. List your current street address.
8. Provide a list of previous addresses for the past seven years, including city and state.
9. Read and agree to the background check authorization.
10. Acknowledge receipt of the disclosure regarding background investigation.
11. Verify that you have received a copy of the Summary of Your Rights under the FCRA.
12. Sign your name as it appears on your identification documents.
13. Date the authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Legal Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Other Names Used** (`other_names_used`, text, optional): List any other names you have used in the past, including maiden names or aliases.
* **Social Security Number** (`ssn`, text, required): Enter your Social Security Number for accurate identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Driver's License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, required): Enter your driver's license number.
* **Driver's License Issuing State** (`dl_state`, text, required): Enter the state that issued your driver's license.
* **Current Street Address** (`current_address`, text, required): Enter your current street address.
* **Previous Addresses** (`previous_addresses`, text, optional): List your previous addresses for the past seven years, including city and state.
* **Background Check Authorization** (`background_check_consent`, select_one, required): Select "I authorize" to consent to the background check.
* **Disclosure Acknowledgment** (`disclosure_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select "I acknowledge" to confirm receipt of the disclosure regarding background investigation.
* **FCRA Summary Receipt** (`fcra_summary_receipt`, select_one, required): Verify that you have received a copy of the Summary of Your Rights under the FCRA.
* **Applicant Electronic Signature** (`digital_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Authorization** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date the authorization.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check the format of date fields (MM/DD/YYYY).
* List all previous addresses for the past seven years.
* Verify that you have received the Summary of Your Rights under the FCRA.
* Sign your name as it appears on your identification documents.
