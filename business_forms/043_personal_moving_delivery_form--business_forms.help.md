# Personal Moving Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track information about a person's moving plans, including delivery details, moving type, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your moving information, including delivery details and preferences.
2. Answer the questions and select options accordingly.
3. Enter any additional notes or instructions that are relevant to your move.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Details** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, optional): Enter your current address, including the city, state, and zip code.
* **Moving Type** (`id: 2`, `type: select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are moving, 'No' otherwise.
* **How Often Do You Move** (`id: 3`, `type: select_multiple`, optional): Select all options that apply to your moving frequency.
* **Start Date** (`id: 4`, `type: date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to start your move.
* **End Date** (`id: 5`, `type: date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to complete your move.
* **Current Address** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, optional): Enter your current address information, including city, state, and zip.
* **New Address** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, optional): Enter the new address information, including city, state, and zip.
* **City** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, optional): Enter the city of your current address.
* **Destination City** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, optional): Enter the destination city for your move.
* **State** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, optional): Enter the state of your current address.
* **Destination State** (`id: 11`, `type: text`, optional): Enter the destination state for your move.
* **Current Zip** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, optional): Enter the zip code of your current address.
* **New Zip** (`id: 13`, `type: text`, optional): Enter the zip code of your new address.
* **Country** (`id: 14`, `type: text`, optional): Enter the country of your current address.
* **Destination Country** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, optional): Enter the destination country for your move.
* **Phone** (`id: 16`, `type: text`, optional): Enter your current phone number.
* **Email** (`id: 17`, `type: email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or organization you'd like to be contacted.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 19`, `type: email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person or organization you'd like to be contacted.
* **Move Details** (`id: 20`, `type: text`, optional): Enter any additional information about your move.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`id: 21`, `type: text`, optional): Enter any specific delivery instructions or instructions for your move.
* **Insurance** (`id: 22`, `type: select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have insurance for your move, 'No' otherwise.
* **Packing** (`id: 23`, `type: select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you require packing services, 'No' otherwise.
* **Payment** (`id: 24`, `type: select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are paying for your move, 'No' otherwise.
* **Notes** (`id: 25`, `type: note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or information about your move.

Note that this form is meant to be completed once, or updated if necessary. Please be sure to submit the completed form to the relevant parties for processing.
