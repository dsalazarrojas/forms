# Warehouse Lease Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the lease authorization of a warehouse. It is intended to be used by warehouse employees or management to record and track lease agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the start date of the lease in the **start_date** field.
2. Provide the name of the tenant (company or individual) in the **tenant_name** field.
3. Select the date of authorization in the **authorization_date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### start_date
* **start_date** (date): Enter the start date of the lease. This is the date when the lease begins.
* **type**: date
* **required**: no

### tenant_name
* **tenant_name** (text): Enter the name of the tenant (company or individual) who is leasing the warehouse.
* **type**: text
* **required**: no

### authorization_date
* **authorization_date** (date): Enter the date when the lease is authorized or approved.
* **type**: date
* **required**: no

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and correct information in each field to ensure proper tracking and record-keeping of the lease agreement.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the relevant authorities or the person responsible for lease management.
