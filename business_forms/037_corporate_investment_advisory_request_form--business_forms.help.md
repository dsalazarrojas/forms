# Corporate Investment Advisory Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide our team with information about your investment goals, preferences, and needs, so we can offer you personalized guidance and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure that we accurately understand your investment goals and needs, please complete all required fields. 

1. **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, required): Enter your company's name or name as it appears on official documents.
2. **Investment Goals** (`investment_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more goals from the list:
	* Short-term growth
	* Medium-term growth
	* Long-term growth
3. **Advisory Needs** (`advisory_needs`, select_one, optional): Select one or more needs from the list:
	* General advice
	* Investment recommendations
4. **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact for further assistance.
5. **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
6. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
7. **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date you are interested in receiving our advisory services.
8. **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time you are available to receive our advisory services.
9. **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Details**: This field is required and should include your company's name or name as it appears on official documents, such as your business registration or tax returns.
- **Investment Goals**: Select one or more goals that describe your investment objectives. You can choose from short-term, medium-term, or long-term growth.
- **Advisory Needs**: If you need general advice or specific investment recommendations, select the corresponding option.
- **Contact Person**: If you have a specific person to contact for further assistance, enter their name here.
- **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with our advisory services and updates.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
- **Date**: Select the date you are interested in receiving our advisory services. This will help us to provide you with a better response.
- **Time**: Select the time you are available to receive our advisory services. If you select a specific time, we will do our best to schedule our response accordingly.
- **Note**: If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them here. This will help us to provide you with a more personalized response.

## Tips
- Please note that all fields are optional, but providing as much information as possible will help us to provide you with more accurate and personalized guidance.
- If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- We will review and respond to your submission within a reasonable timeframe.
