# small_business_credit_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for small business credit applications, gathering information about the business and owner for credit assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Enter the correct information for each field.
3. Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Enter the street address of your business.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where your business is located.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the zip code of your business location.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of business you have (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your business.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the owner of the business.
* **Owner Address** (`owner_address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Owner Phone Number** (`owner_phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Owner's Date of Birth** (`owner_dob`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Social Security Number** (`owner_ss_number`, text, required): Enter your Social Security Number.
* **Bank Name** (`bank_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your bank.
* **Bank Routing Number** (`bank_routing_number`, text, required): Enter your bank routing number.
* **Number of Employees** (`business_employees`, number, required): Enter the number of employees in your business.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, required): Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Business Years in Industry** (`business_years_in_industry`, number, optional): Enter the number of years your business has been in the industry.
* **Owner's Employer ID** (`owner_employer_id`, text, optional): Enter your Employer ID.
* **Owner Credit Score** (`owner_credit_score`, text, optional): Enter your credit score.
* **Bank Account Holder** (`bank_account_holder`, select_one, optional): Select who the bank account holder is (First Name, Last Name, Business Name).
* **Bank Account Type** (`bank_account_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of bank account (Checking, Savings, Credit).
* **Owner Credit Authorized** (`owner_credit_authorized`, select_multiple, required): Select whether the owner is authorized for credit.
* **Owner Employment Status** (`owner_employment_status`, select_one, required): Select your employment status (Employed, Self Employed, Retired).
* **Owner Income** (`owner_income`, number, required): Enter your income.
* **Owner Credit Authorized** (`owner_authorized`, select_multiple, required): This is not necessary as this field is already listed. Please remove it.
 
Please note that some fields may have specific formatting requirements based on their type (text, number, date, etc.).
