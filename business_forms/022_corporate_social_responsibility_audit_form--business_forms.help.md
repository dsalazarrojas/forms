# Corporate Social Responsibility Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your organization's commitment to corporate social responsibility (CSR). This form helps to evaluate your company's CSR practices and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form on your computer or mobile device.
2. Review each field carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Corporate Social Responsibility Audit Form** (`note`, required: false): This is a note field that serves as a title for the form. Please ignore it.
* **Company Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of your company as it is officially registered.
* **Company Address** (`text`, required: false): Enter the address of your company's primary location.
* **CSR Initiatives** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Check all the CSR initiatives that your company is involved in. Options include:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Stakeholder Feedback** (`select_one`, required: false): Check if your stakeholders provide feedback on your CSR practices. Options include:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Company Website** (`text`, required: false): Enter the URL of your company's website.
* **CSR Initiatives Description** (`text`, required: false): Describe your company's CSR initiatives in detail.
* **CSR Objectives** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Check all the CSR objectives that your company aims to achieve. Options include:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips
* Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about a particular field, consult with your company's CSR team or leadership.
* Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.
