# workers_comp_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The workers' comp waiver form is a document that captures essential information from employees regarding a work-related injury. This form helps employers to understand the employee's situation and provides a basis for potential support or next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your **Employee Name**, which is the name of the person completing the form.
2.  Enter your **Job Title**, which is the position you hold in the company.
3.  Provide the **Date Injured**, which is the date the injury occurred.
4.  Enter your **Employer Name**, which is the name of your employer.
5.  Choose your **Employee/Employer Relationship**, which indicates whether you are an employee or not (e.g., independent contractor).
6.  Describe the **Injury Description**, including details about the incident.
7.  Enter the **Date Signed**, which is the date you signed this form.
8.  Sign your name as the **Employee Signature**, which is a confirmation of your authenticity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Job Title**: Enter the job title you hold in the company.
  \* Example: "Sales Manager"
- **Date Injured**: Enter the date the injury occurred in `MM/DD/YYYY` format.
- **Employer Name**: Enter the name of your employer or organization.
- **Employee/Employer Relationship**: Select whether you are an employee or not.
  \* Employee: You are a full-time or contract employee.
  \* Not Employee: You are an independent contractor or have a different relationship.
- **Injury Description**: Provide a detailed description of the injury, including the incident and its effects.
- **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed this form, in `MM/DD/YYYY` format.
- **Employee Signature**: Sign your name as a confirmation of your authenticity.
  \* This is your signature, not a digital stamp or seal.
- **Injury Date Signed**: Not applicable; this is an automatically generated field.
