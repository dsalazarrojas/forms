# Dog Training Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for potential clients to provide necessary information about their dog and their requirements for dog training services. The goal is to get an accurate quotation for the services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your potential client information (page 1).
2. Provide information about your dog (page 2).
3. Select the services you are interested in (page 3).
4. Enter your training quote (page 4).
5. Add any additional trainer information (page 5).
6. Enter your contact details (page 6).
7. Select any additional services you need (page 7).
8. Specify your payment terms (page 8).
9. Choose your payment method (page 9).
10. Sign and date the form (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Potential Client** (page 1, required): Enter your name or name of your organization.
* **Dog Information** (page 2, required): Please provide information about your dog, including breed, age, and any medical conditions.
* **Service Options** (page 3, required): Select all the services you are interested in for your dog.
* **Training Quote** (page 4, required): Enter the quote amount for the services selected.
* **Trainer Information** (page 5, required): Please provide any additional information about your trainer or training team.
* **Contact Details** (page 6, required): Enter your email address or phone number so we can contact you.
* **Additional Services** (page 7, required): Select if you need any additional services beyond the standard training services.
* **Payment Terms** (page 8, optional): Specify any special payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Method** (page 9, required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Signature** (page 10, required): Date and sign this form to confirm your submission.
