# Change Of Address Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to notify our organization of a change of address for your account or business. Please fill out this form completely to ensure that your address is updated accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure that all fields marked as required are filled out completely.
2. Use the format specified for fields, such as date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, required): Date of request. Please enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): Full legal name of the person making the address change request.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, `email`, required): Email address used for confirmation and communication.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, `text`, required): Contact phone number for follow-up or urgent matters.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, `text`, optional): Your account or reference number. This can be left blank if not applicable.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, optional): Name of business if applicable.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, `text`, required): Full current address. Include all necessary details such as apartment number, floor, etc.
* **New Address** (`new_address`, `text`, required): Full new address. Include all necessary details such as apartment number, floor, etc.
* **Move Type** (`move_type`, `select_one`, required): Type of move (Residential move, Business relocation, Temporary assignment, Permanent change, Seasonal residence).
* **Move Date** (`move_date`, `date`, required): Date of move. Please enter the date you will move to the new address.
* **Services Affected** (`services_affected`, `select_multiple`, required): Which services need address update (Billing statements, Product delivery, Service notifications, Marketing communications, Account correspondence, All services).
* **Mail Forwarding** (`forwarding_needed`, `select_one`, required): Whether mail forwarding is needed. Select "Yes" if you need mail forwarding, "No" otherwise.
* **Forwarding Duration** (`forwarding_duration`, `select_one`, optional): How long mail forwarding will last (30 days, 60 days, 90 days, 6 months, 1 year, Indefinitely). This is only applicable if "Mail Forwarding" is selected as "Yes".
* **Update Priority** (`update_priority`, `select_one`, required): The urgency of the address update (Immediate, Within 1 week, Within 2 weeks, Within 30 days).
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional): Any special delivery instructions or information.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, `select_one`, required): Preferred contact method (Email, Phone, Mail, Text message).
* **Verification Required** (`verification_required`, `select_one`, required): Verification of the address change (Yes - I verify, No - I do not verify).
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, `select_one`, required): Authorization of the address change (Yes - I authorize, No - I do not authorize).
* **Requester Signature** (`requester_signature`, `text`, required): Type your full legal name as signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Date of signing. Please enter the date you sign this form.
