# Production Worksheet Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Production Worksheet Form is used to collect and record production data, ensuring accurate and up-to-date information about production activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately with all required fields.
2.  Review each field carefully to ensure correctness.
3.  If a field does not apply to your situation, leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Production Date** (`production_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of production.
*   **Order ID** (`order_id`, number, required: false): Enter the ID of the order.
*   **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the order.
*   **Production Quota** (`production_quota`, number, required: false): Enter the production quota.
*   **Product ID** (`product_id`, select_one, required: false): Choose the relevant product from the available options (Product A, Product B, Product C).
*   **Production Date Time** (`production_date_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of production.
*   **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity produced.
*   **Production Location** (`production_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of production.
*   **Production Status** (`production_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more options from Available, Out of Stock, On Hold.
*   **Remarks** (`remarks`, note, required: false): Enter any additional remarks or comments.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter contact email.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter contact phone number.

## Tips

*   Always review and correct the form before submitting.
*   Ensure accurate and precise information is entered in each field.
*   If you are unsure about a field or its relevance, consult with your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
