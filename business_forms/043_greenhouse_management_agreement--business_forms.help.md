# Greenhouse Management Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting and managing greenhouse rental or lease agreements. Please complete the form to provide necessary details for a successful greenhouse management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in the Greenhouse Name field with the specific name of the rented or leased greenhouse.
- Enter the address where the greenhouse is located in the Address field.
- Provide the Owner's Name and contact information in the corresponding fields.
- Enter the start date and start time of the rental/lease in the Start Date and Start Time fields respectively.
- Enter the end date and end time of the rental/lease in the End Date and End Time fields respectively.
- Provide payment details in the Payment Details field.
- Select the payment terms (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly) in the Payment Terms field.
- Select the payment frequency (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly) in the Payment Terms Frequency field.
- Fill in any additional comments or information in the Notes field (optional).
- Enter your Email and Phone number in the corresponding fields for easy contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Greenhouse Name** (1, text, required): The name of the rented or leased greenhouse.
* **Address** (2, text, required): The physical address where the greenhouse is located.
* **Owner Name** (3, text, required): The name of the owner or manager of the greenhouse.
* **Start Date** (4, date, required): The date when the rental/lease starts.
* **Start Time** (5, time, required): The time when the rental/lease starts.
* **End Date** (6, date, required): The date when the rental/lease ends.
* **End Time** (7, time, required): The time when the rental/lease ends.
* **Payment Details** (8, text, required): Any additional payment details or instructions.
* **Payment Terms** (9, select_multiple, required): The frequency of payments (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Payment Terms Frequency** (10, select_one, required): The specific payment term (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Notes** (11, note, optional): Any additional comments or information regarding the rental/lease.
* **Email** (12, email, required): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (13, text, required): Your phone number for contact purposes.
