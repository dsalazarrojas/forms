# Facility Energy Partner Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Facility Energy Partner Contact Form is a tool for collecting information from organizations and facilities that partner with the energy sector.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by providing the name of the organization and their address.
2. Select the contact person from the list of options.
3. Enter the email address and phone number of the contact person.
4. Write a brief description of the organization.
5. Choose the facilities that this organization is partnered with.
6. Select the person assigned to this organization.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about this organization.
8. Finally, choose the category that best describes the organization's affiliation with the energy sector.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization.
* **Organization Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the physical address of the organization.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_one, required): Select the person to contact within the organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Write a brief description of the organization.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date when the organization was contacted.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the contact.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, select_multiple, required): Select the facilities that this organization is partnered with.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select the person assigned to this organization.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments about this organization.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): Choose the category that best describes the organization's affiliation with the energy sector.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* Use the correct format for dates and times.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact a representative for clarification.
