# Project Task Delegation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request the delegation of tasks to team members or other stakeholders in a project. It ensures that tasks are accurately and efficiently assigned to the right team members.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and understand what information is required and what options are available.
2. Fill out the form with the necessary information, such as the project manager's ID, task ID, assignee ID, assigned date, assigned time, and assigned status.
3. Select the relevant team members or stakeholders who are to be assigned to the task.
4. Provide any additional comments or explanations for the task assignment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Manager ID**: This field is used to identify the project manager responsible for the task. Enter the ID of the project manager who will be handling the task.
* **Task ID**: This field is used to identify the specific task to be assigned. Enter the ID of the task that needs to be delegated.
* **Assignee ID**: This field is used to identify the team member who will be assigned to the task. Enter the ID of the team member to whom the task will be assigned.
* **Assigned Date**: This field is used to specify the date when the task was assigned. Enter the date when the task was assigned to the team member.
* **Assigned Time**: This field is used to specify the time when the task was assigned. Enter the time when the task was assigned to the team member.
* **Assigned By**: This field is used to specify the person who assigned the task. Enter the name or ID of the person who assigned the task.
* **Comments**: This field is used to provide additional comments or explanations for the task assignment. Enter any relevant comments or details about the task assignment.
* **Assigned Status**: This field is used to specify the current status of the task assignment. Select from the options: "Not Assigned", "Assigned", "Re-Proposed".
* **Reason for Re-Assignment**: This field is used to specify the reason for re-assigning the task. Enter the reason for re-assigning the task.
* **Assigned To**: This field is used to select the team member to whom the task will be assigned. Select from the available team members.
* **Assigned To Status**: This field is used to specify the status of the task assignment for the team member. Select from the available options.
* **Assigned To Status Reason**: This field is used to specify the reason for the status of the task assignment. Enter the reason for the status of the task assignment.
* **Comments 2**: This field is used to provide additional comments or explanations for the task assignment. Enter any relevant comments or details about the task assignment.
* **Task Details**: This field is used to specify any additional details about the task. Enter any relevant details about the task.
* **Assigned To 2**: This field is used to select the team member to whom the task will be assigned. Select from the available team members.
* **Assigned To Status 2**: This field is used to specify the status of the task assignment for the team member. Select from the available options.
* **Assigned By Status**: This field is used to specify the status of the person who assigned the task. Select from the available options.
* **Assigned By Status Reason**: This field is used to specify the reason for the status of the person who assigned the task. Enter the reason for the status of the person who assigned the task.

Tips:

* Make sure to select the correct team member to whom the task will be assigned.
* Ensure that the assigned status is accurate and reflects the current status of the task.
* Enter any additional comments or explanations for the task assignment.
* Double-check the task details to ensure that all relevant information is provided.
