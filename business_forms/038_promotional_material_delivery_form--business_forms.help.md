# promotional_material_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The promotional_material_delivery_form is designed to collect and document the details of promotional materials delivered to customers or partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information ready, including contact details, delivery date, time of day, promotional material type, and any other relevant details.
2. Select the correct promotional material type from the drop-down list.
3. Enter the promotional material details in the notes field.
4. Enter the promotional material URL if applicable.
5. Enter the promotional material size.
6. Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact information of the person responsible for delivering the promotional material.
* **Promotional Material** (`promotional_material`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the promotional material being delivered.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the promotional material was delivered.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of day when the promotional material was delivered.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for delivering the promotional material.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for delivering the promotional material.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for delivering the promotional material.
* **Promotional Material Details** (`promotional_material_details`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the promotional material being delivered.
* **Promotional Material Type** (`promotional_material_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of promotional material being delivered (e.g., Printed Materials, Digital Materials, Other).
* **Promotional Material URL** (`promotional_material_url`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL of the promotional material if applicable.
* **Promotional Material Size** (`promotional_material_size`, number, required/optional): Enter the size of the promotional material.
