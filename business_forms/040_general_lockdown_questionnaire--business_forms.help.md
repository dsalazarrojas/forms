# General Lockdown Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Lockdown Questionnaire is a form designed to gather information for a specific purpose. It is meant to be filled out by users who are aware of the purpose and implications of answering the questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Fill out the form in order, with each question being answered in sequence.
3. For multiple choice questions, select only one answer per question.
4. For open-ended questions, provide detailed answers.
5. For date questions, select the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. For select multiple questions, select all applicable answers.
