# Herbalife - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect business-related data from authorized representatives of the Herbalife organization. It is essential to ensure that the contact person and email address are valid to guarantee that the submitted data is legitimate and can be properly used by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Enter your contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter your job title (if applicable) in the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
6. Select a date from the "Date" field (optional).
7. Select a time from the "Time" field (optional).
8. Enter any additional comments or remarks in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, required): Enter your company's official name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, required): Enter the name of the person submitting this form.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, optional): Enter your job title if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your company.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter your company's phone number (if available).
* **Date** (`date`, optional): Select a date for the submission (e.g., the date the form was completed).
* **Time** (`time`, optional): Select a time for the submission (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Enter any additional remarks or comments regarding the submission.
