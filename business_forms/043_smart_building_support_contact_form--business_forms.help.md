# Smart Building Support Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Building Support Contact Form is a tool for users to report issues or seek assistance with a smart building. This form will help our support team understand the problem and provide the best possible support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your input in the text box provided.
2. Provide a brief description of the issue you're experiencing with the smart building.
3. Enter the date you would like to have the issue resolved, if applicable.
4. Enter your email address so our support team can contact you for further assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (`user_input`, text, **Optional**): Please enter any relevant information about the smart building you're experiencing issues with.
- **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, **Optional**): Enter a brief description of the problem you're encountering with the smart building. This will help us understand the issue better.
- **Requested Date** (`requested_date`, date, **Optional**): If you have a specific date you would like to have the issue resolved by, please enter it here. This will help us schedule our support accordingly.
- **Email** (`email`, email, **Optional**): Enter your email address so our support team can contact you for further assistance or to provide updates on your issue. This is required to ensure we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information in the user input field to help us understand your issue better.
- If you're not sure about the specific date you would like to have the issue resolved by, you can leave this field blank.
- Please ensure your email address is accurate and up-to-date to receive our support team's communications.
