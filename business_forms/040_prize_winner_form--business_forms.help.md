# Prize Winner Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about prize winners from customers. It is intended for use in prize-awarding or contest contexts where users need to verify and document prizes won. The form is used to capture details such as name, email, and phone number of the winner, as well as information about the prize itself, including its category, value, and description.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Prize Winner Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **First Name** and **Last Name** of the winner in the respective fields.
2. Enter the **Email** and **Phone Number** of the winner in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the **Prize Category** that the winner received.
4. If desired, enter the **Entry Date** and **Entry Time** of the prize entry.
5. Select the **Winner Status** from the dropdown menu to indicate whether the winner has been verified or not.
6. Enter the **Prize Value** and provide a brief **Prize Description**.
7. Optionally, upload a **Prize Image** and enter a **Prize Image URL**.
8. Enter the **Image URL** for the winner's image.
9. Optionally, enter additional **Winner Image URLs** for extra images.
10. Finally, enter the **Total Entries** and **Winner Count** to provide further details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prize Winner Form**: This is the header of the form, indicating that it is for capturing prize winner information.
* **First Name**: A text field to enter the first name of the winner.
* **Last Name**: A text field to enter the last name of the winner.
* **Email**: A field to enter the email of the winner, which should be in a standard email format (e.g., `example@domain.com`).
* **Phone Number**: A text field to enter the phone number of the winner.
* **Address**: A text field to enter the address of the winner.
* **Prize Name**: A text field to enter the name of the prize.
* **Prize Category**: A text field to select the category of the prize.
* **Entry Date**: A date field to enter the date the prize was entered.
* **Entry Time**: A time field to enter the time the prize was entered.
* **Winner Status**: A dropdown menu to select whether the winner has been verified or not.
* **Prize Value**: A number field to enter the monetary value of the prize.
* **Prize Description**: A text field to enter a brief description of the prize.
* **Prize Image**: A file upload field to upload an image of the prize.
* **Prize Image URL**: A text field to enter a URL of the prize image.
* **Image URL**: A text field to enter the URL of an image related to the winner.
* **Winner Image**: A file upload field to upload an image of the winner.
* **Winner Image URL**: A text field to enter a URL of the winner's image.
* **Total Entries**: A number field to enter the total number of entries for the prize.
* **Winner Count**: A number field to enter the count of winners for the prize.
* **Prize Image URL** ( duplicate field, but for consistency): A text field to enter the URL of a prize image again.
* **Winner Image Url 2**: A text field to enter the URL of a second winner image.
* **Image Url 2**: A text field to enter the URL of a second image.
* **Winner URL**: A text field to enter the URL of the winner's page or profile.
* **Prize URL**: A text field to enter the URL of the prize's page or profile.

Note: Some fields are optional, so feel free to fill in as much information as you deem necessary to document the prize winner.
