# Subcontractor Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subcontractor Details Form is used to collect information about individuals or companies that a business has a working relationship with for subcontracted work or services. This form is typically completed by the business to maintain accurate and up-to-date records of their subcontractors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the First Name of the subcontractor in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the Last Name of the subcontractor in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the subcontractor's Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the start date of the contract in the "Contract Start Date" field. This should be the date when the contract with the subcontractor began.
5. Enter the end date of the contract in the "Contract End Date" field. This should be the date when the contract with the subcontractor is expected to end.
6. Enter the subcontractor's Phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter the subcontractor's physical address in the "Address" field.
8. Select the type of subcontractor from the options provided in the "Subcontractor Type" field. This will help categorize the subcontractor for organizational purposes.
9. Enter the name of the subcontractor in the "Subcontractor Name" field. This is a unique identifier for the subcontractor.
10. Enter the total contract amount in the "Contract Amount" field. This is the total value of the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the given name of the subcontractor.
* **Last Name**: Enter the surname of the subcontractor.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the subcontractor for communication and payment purposes.
* **Contract Start Date**: Enter the date when the contract began, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Contract End Date**: Enter the date when the contract is expected to end, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Phone**: Enter the subcontractor's phone number for communication and contact purposes.
* **Address**: Enter the subcontractor's physical address for logistical and administrative purposes.
* **Subcontractor Type**: Select the category of subcontractor from the provided options.
* **Subcontractor Name**: Enter a unique identifier for the subcontractor, typically their full name or business name.
* **Contract Amount**: Enter the total value of the contract.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed to ensure accurate records.
- Enter dates in the format YYYY-MM-DD for the contract start and end dates.
- If the subcontractor type is not provided as an option, you may need to contact the relevant department for assistance.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department for clarification.
