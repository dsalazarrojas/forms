# e_mail_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer or user information through email submission. It is intended to gather essential details such as name and contact information to improve communication and service quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your First Name**: This is the first part of your name.
2. **Enter your Last Name**: This is the second part of your name.
3. **Enter your Email**: This is the email address we will use to contact you.
4. **Enter your Phone (optional)**: If you have a phone number, please enter it to allow us to contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This field is a text input field where you should enter your first name. Please provide your first name to help us identify you.
* **Last Name**: This field is another text input field where you should enter your last name. This is required for proper identification.
* **Email**: This is a email input field where you should enter a valid email address. We will use this to contact you regarding your submission.
* **Phone**: This is an optional text input field for your phone number. If you provide your phone number, it will allow us to contact you more effectively. 

## Tips
Remember to fill out your details accurately to ensure we can contact you effectively.
If you have any issues with the submission process or need assistance, please contact us.
