# Strategic Capabilities Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users evaluate their organization's strategic strengths and challenges. It's used to inform strategic decisions and identify areas to focus on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

* Start by going to the page labeled "introduction" and provide a brief overview of your organization's current strategic state.
* On the page "Strategic Strengths", select the level of your organization's strategic strengths (High, Medium, or Low) to indicate how well your organization is positioned in each strategic area.
* On the page "Challenges", select "Yes" if your organization is currently experiencing the challenge, or "No" if it's not.
* On the page "Priorities", select all the priorities that apply to your organization.
* On the page "Upload Documents", you can upload relevant documents to support your assessment.
* On the page "Upload Strategic Documents", you can upload any additional strategic documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **<label>Introduction</label> (<name> introduction</name>, <type> Note</type>, required/optional> <false>): A brief overview of your organization's current strategic state.
* **<label>Strategic Strengths</label> (<name> strategic_strengths</name>, <type> Select Multiple</type>, required/optional> <false>): Select the level of your organization's strategic strengths:
	+ High: Your organization is well-positioned in this area.
	+ Medium: Your organization is moderately positioned in this area.
	+ Low: Your organization is not well-positioned in this area.
* **<label>Challenges</label> (<name> challenges</name>, <type> Select One</type>, required/optional> <false>): Are you currently experiencing this challenge?
	+ Yes: Your organization is experiencing the challenge.
	+ No: Your organization is not experiencing the challenge.
* **<label>Priorities</label> (<name> priorities</name>, <type> Select Multiple</type>, required/optional> <false>): Select all the priorities that apply to your organization.
* **<label>Upload Documents</label> (<name> uploaded_documents</name>, <type> Text</type>, required/optional> <false>): You can upload relevant documents to support your assessment.
* **<label>Upload Strategic Documents</label> (<name> upload_strategic_documents</name>, <type> Text</type>, required/optional> <false>): You can upload any additional strategic documents.

## Tips
* Be specific and honest when completing this form.
* Take your time to think about each question carefully.
* If you're unsure about any question, consult with your colleagues or superiors.
* Remember that this form is for internal use only.
