# Safety Data Sheet Binder Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Data Sheet Binder Order Form is designed to collect contact information for shipping and billing purposes, allowing you to place an order for a Safety Data Sheet Binder.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "company" field with your company's name.
2. Enter the name and contact information for the person handling the order, in the "contact person" field.
3. Provide their email address in the "contact email" field.
4. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "contact phone" field.
5. Fill in the shipping address in the "shipping address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **company** (text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **contact person** (text, required): Enter the name of the person handling the order.
* **contact email** (email, required): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **contact phone** (text, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **shipping address** (text, required): Enter the shipping address for the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you need to place an order for multiple binders, please submit one form per binder.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
