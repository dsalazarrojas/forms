# credit_card_renewal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The credit card renewal form is designed to collect and update customer's credit card information. This form is used to replace or modify existing credit card details, ensuring accurate and up-to-date records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Fill in the form accurately and completely.
3. Double-check your entries before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required): Enter the customer's full name as it appears on their credit card.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Provide the customer's email address for communication purposes.
* **Card Type** (Card Type, select_one, optional): Select whether the credit card is 'Yes' or 'No' for renewal purposes (if applicable).
* **Expiration Month** (Expiration Month, select_one, optional): Select the expiration month of the credit card (if applicable).
* **Expiration Year** (Expiration Year, number, optional): Enter the expiration year of the credit card (if applicable).
* **CVV (Card Verification Value)** (CVV (Card Verification Value), number, optional): Enter the three-digit code found on the back of the credit card (if applicable).
* **CVV (Card Verification Code)** (CVV (Card Verification Code), number, optional): Enter the three-digit code on the front of the credit card (if applicable).
* **Card Number** (Card Number, text, optional): Enter the credit card number without any spaces or hyphens.
* **Billing Zip** (Billing Zip, text, optional): Enter the customer's billing zip code.
* **Billing City** (Billing City, text, optional): Enter the customer's billing city.
* **Billing State** (Billing State, text, optional): Enter the customer's billing state.
* **Billing Country** (Billing Country, text, optional): Enter the customer's billing country.
* **Billing Address 1** (Billing Address 1, text, optional): Enter the customer's billing address line 1.
* **Billing Address 2** (Billing Address 2, text, optional): Enter the customer's billing address line 2.

Note: If a field is not required, it is optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
