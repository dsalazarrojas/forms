# Facility Maintenance Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Facility Maintenance Check In Form is used for maintenance check in, issue reporting, and other activities related to facility maintenance. This form is designed to gather relevant information for maintenance tasks and updates.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Check in**: Enter a brief description of the maintenance check-in, if applicable.
2. Select the relevant options for **issue-reporting**, such as option-1, option-2, and option-3.
3. For **task-scheduling**, select the relevant options, such as option-1, option-2, and option-3.
4. Enter the **date** of the maintenance activity.
5. Enter the **time** of the maintenance activity.
6. Enter any additional **notes** related to the maintenance activity.
7. Enter the **email** address of the person submitting the form.
8. Enter the **phone** number of the person submitting the form.
9. Provide any additional **notes** related to the maintenance activity.
10. Select the relevant options for **file-upload**, such as option-1, option-2, and option-3.
11. Select the status of the **file-upload**, such as option-1, option-2, and option-3.
12. Enter any additional **comments** related to the maintenance activity.
13. **Submit** the form by selecting the relevant options in the **form-submitted** field, such as option-1, option-2, and option-3.
14. Select the status of the form in the **form-status** field, such as option-1, option-2, and option-3.
15. Enter the name of the person who **submitted** the form.
16. Enter the email address of the person who **submitted** the form.
17. Enter the name of the person who **updated** the form.
18. Enter the date and time when the form was **updated**.
19. Select the user who **updated** the form, such as option-1, option-2, and option-3.
20. Select the status of the form **updated** status, such as option-1, option-2, and option-3.
