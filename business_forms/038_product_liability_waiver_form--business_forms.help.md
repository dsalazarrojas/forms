# Product Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Product Liability Waiver Form is used to collect information about a company's product, including details about its usage and warranties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company's name and address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide the contact number, email, and other relevant contact information for the company.
3. Enter the product name and description, as well as its category and subcategory.
4. Enter the date and time the product was used.
5. Confirm the product warranty and user agreement.
6. Sign the form electronically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): The name of the company that is providing the product.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): The address of the company that is providing the product.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): The phone number of the company contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): The email address of the company contact person.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): The name of the product being used.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): A brief description of the product.
* **Date Used** (`date_used`, date, optional): The date the product was used.
* **Time Used** (`time_used`, time, optional): The time the product was used.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, text, optional): The type of product being used.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, text, optional): The category of the product.
* **Product Subcategory** (`product_subcategory`, text, optional): The subcategory of the product.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional): A more detailed description of the product.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): The name of the user who will be using the product.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required): The email address of the user who will be using the product.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required): The phone number of the user.
* **User Address** (`user_address`, text, required): The address of the user.
* **User State** (`user_state`, text, optional): The state of the user's location.
* **User City** (`user_city`, text, optional): The city of the user's location.
* **User Country** (`user_country`, text, optional): The country of the user's location.
* **User Zip** (`user_zip`, text, optional): The zip code of the user's location.
* **Product Warranty** (`product_warranty`, note, optional): A statement confirming the product warranty.
* **User Agreement** (`user_agreement`, note, optional): A statement confirming the user agreement.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): The date the form was signed.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, required): The time the form was signed.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, required): The name of the person who signed the form.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be left blank if not applicable. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
