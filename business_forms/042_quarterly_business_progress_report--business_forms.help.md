# Quarterly Business Progress Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quarterly Business Progress Report is a form used to report on the progress and status of various business units and teams. It helps to track and analyze performance, identify areas of improvement, and inform decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Quarterly** field (ID 1) with the current quarter number (e.g., Q1, Q2, etc.).
2. Enter the **Business Progress** (ID 2) as a numeric value, indicating the current progress (e.g., 20, 50, etc.).
3. In the **Document Outcomes** field (ID 3), provide a brief description of the outcomes achieved during the quarter.
4. For the **Next Quarter Priorities** field (ID 4), select the priorities for the next quarter (yes/no).
5. In the **Review Notes** field (ID 5), add any relevant notes or comments about the review process.
6. Enter the **Next Steps** field (ID 6) with any next steps or actions planned for the next quarter.
7. Select the **Performance Metrics** (ID 7) as applicable (yes/no).
8. Enter the **Review Date** (ID 8) and **Review Time** (ID 9) to schedule the review.
9. In the **Team Members** (ID 10) field, select members involved in the review process (yes/no).
10. Select the **Team Leaders** (ID 11) involved in the review process (yes/no).
11. Provide **Team Lead Comments** (ID 12) if necessary.
12. Enter the **Business Units** field (ID 13) with any relevant information.
13. Select **Review By** (ID 14) as yes/no, indicating whether the review is conducted by the team lead or not.
14. Choose the **Status** (ID 15) as Active/Inactive.
15. Enter **Comments** (ID 16) if needed.
16. Select **Submitted By** (ID 17) as yes/no, indicating whether the report is submitted or not.
17. Enter the **Submitted Date** (ID 18) and **Submitted Time** (ID 19) if the report is submitted.
18. Enter the **Submitted By Email** (ID 20) field, if necessary.
19. Enter the **Submitted Email** field (ID 21) field, if necessary.
20. Enter the **Next Review Date** (ID 22) and **Next Review Time** (ID 23).
21. Select **Next Review By** (ID 24) as yes/no.
22. Select **Review Frequency** (ID 25) as yes/no.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quarterly** (ID 1): Enter the current quarter number (e.g., Q1, Q2, etc.).
* **Business Progress** (ID 2): Enter the current progress as a numeric value (e.g., 20, 50, etc.).
* **Document Outcomes** (ID 3): Provide a brief description of the outcomes achieved during the quarter.
* **Next Quarter Priorities** (ID 4): Select priorities for the next quarter (yes/no).
* **Review Notes** (ID 5): Add any relevant notes or comments about the review process.
* **Next Steps** (ID 6): Enter any next steps or actions planned for the next quarter.
* **Performance Metrics** (ID 7): Select as applicable (yes/no).
* **Review Date** (ID 8): Enter the date for the review.
* **Review Time** (ID 9): Enter the time for the review.
* **Team Members** (ID 10): Select members involved in the review process (yes/no).
* **Team Leaders** (ID 11): Select team leaders involved in the review process (yes/no).
* **Team Lead Comments** (ID 12): Provide any comments from team leaders.
* **Business Units** (ID 13): Enter any relevant information about the business units.
* **Review By** (ID 14): Select whether the review is conducted by the team lead or not (yes/no).
* **Status** (ID 15): Choose the status as Active/Inactive.
* **Comments** (ID 16): Enter any comments.
* **Submitted By** (ID 17): Select whether the report is submitted or not (yes/no).
* **Submitted Date** (ID 18): Enter the date the report is submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (ID 19): Enter the time the report is submitted.
* **Submitted By Email** (ID 20): Enter the email of the person submitting the report.
* **Submitted Email** (ID 21): Enter the email of the person submitting the report.
* **Next Review Date** (ID 22): Enter the date for the next review.
* **Next Review Time** (ID 23): Enter the time for the next review.
* **Next Review By** (ID 24): Select whether the review is conducted by someone (yes/no).
* **Review Frequency** (ID 25): Select whether the review is conducted regularly (yes/no).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as required).
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your team lead or the review process guide for guidance.
