# sponsorship_collaboration_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from potential sponsors, partners, or collaborators about their interest in working with our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your relevant information. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partnership Leads** (`partnership_leads`): This section is not currently used in the provided form schema.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact details in this field.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as it would be used for communication purposes.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Company** (`company`): Enter your company name or the name of the organization you represent.
* **Message** (`message`): Enter any additional information or comments you'd like to include with your inquiry.
* **Collaboration Inquiry** (`collaboration_inquiry`): Select the type of collaboration you're interested in (Partnership or Sponsorship).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`): Select your preferred method of contact (Email, Phone, or Mail).
* **Expected Response Time** (`expected_response_time`): Select the time frame in which you'd like to receive a response from us.

Tips:

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your inquiry is processed correctly.
* If you're not sure what type of collaboration you're interested in, don't worry! Just select "Partnership Inquiry" and we'll follow up to clarify.
* If you'd like to be contacted through multiple methods (e.g., both email and phone), just select all applicable options for "Preferred Contact Method".
