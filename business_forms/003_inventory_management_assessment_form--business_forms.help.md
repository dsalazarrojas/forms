# Inventory Management Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Management Assessment Form is designed to help organizations evaluate and improve their inventory management processes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and understand what information is being requested.
2. Answer each field accurately and thoroughly, providing relevant data about your current inventory management practices.
3. For fields that ask for multiple options, select the choices that best represent your current state.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stock Levels** (`stock_levels`, number, required: false): This field asks you to evaluate your current stock levels. Please enter a numerical value to represent your stock levels, for example, "10" for low stock, "50" for moderate stock, or "100" for high stock.
* **Management Strategies** (`management_strategies`, select_one, required: false): Choose one strategy that best describes your current management approach:
	+ A: Just-in-time ordering
	+ B: Periodic ordering
	+ C: Other (please specify)
* **Stock Tracking** (`stock_tracking`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all methods you use for tracking your stock:
	+ D: Manual tracking
	+ E: Automated tracking
	+ F: Other (please specify)
* **Stock Levels Frequency** (`stock_levels_frequency`, number, required: false): Enter a numerical value to represent how often you check your stock levels, for example, "daily" for daily checks, "weekly" for weekly checks, or "monthly" for monthly checks.
* **Stock Values** (`stock_values`, number, required: false): Enter a numerical value to represent your current stock value, for example, 10000 for a moderate stock value, or 50000 for a high stock value.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form. Your input will help identify areas for improvement and optimization in your inventory management processes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your team or supervisor for assistance.
* Remember to revisit and review your form responses periodically to track progress and identify areas that require continued improvement.
