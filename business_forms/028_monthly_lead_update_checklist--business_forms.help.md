# Monthly Lead Update Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Lead Update Checklist is a sales performance tracking tool used to document interactions with leads throughout the month. This form helps sales teams maintain accurate records of lead status, contact information, and update activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the lead source from the options provided.
2. Choose the current status of the lead (Active or Inactive).
3. Enter the lead's name.
4. Provide the lead's email address.
5. Enter the lead's phone number.
6. Enter the lead's company name.
7. Select the date of the last update (or choose the current date if it's the first update).
8. Select the time of the last update (or choose the current time if it's the first update).
9. Add any notes about the recent interactions or updates with the lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Source**: Select the source where the lead was acquired, such as a trade show, referral, or online search. This helps in tracking the origin of leads.
* **Monthly Lead Status**: Choose the current status of the lead (Active or Inactive) to reflect its current state. This field ensures that the sales team is aware of the lead's status and can prioritize efforts accordingly.
* **Lead Name**: Enter the name of the lead, which helps in personalizing interactions and building relationships.
* **Lead Email**: Enter the email address of the lead, which is essential for communication and follow-up activities.
* **Lead Phone**: Enter the phone number of the lead, which enables efficient communication and outreach.
* **Lead Company**: Enter the name of the lead's company, which provides context for sales efforts.
* **Monthly Lead Update Date**: Select the date of the last update, which helps in tracking the progression of interactions with the lead.
* **Monthly Lead Update Time**: Select the time of the last update, which complements the date and provides a full timestamp.
* **Monthly Lead Update Notes**: Add any relevant notes or comments about recent interactions or updates with the lead, which facilitates better understanding and decision-making.
