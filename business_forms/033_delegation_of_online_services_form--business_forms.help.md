# Delegation Of Online Services Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Delegation Of Online Services Form is designed to help users delegate tasks or services to others within an organizational setting. This form is used to provide or request access to online services and gather relevant information about the services being delegated.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you understand what information is being requested.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the delegated service owner for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delegate Title (Delegate Title)** (`delegate_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a title that describes the service being delegated.
* **Start Date (Start Date)** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the service delegation.
* **Delegate Organisation (Delegate Organisation)** (`delegate_organisation`, select_one, required/optional): Select the organisation that will be providing the service.
* **End Date (End Date)** (`end_date`, time, required/optional): Enter the end date of the service delegation.
* **Delegate Contact Email (Delegate Contact Email)** (`delegate_contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person or team providing the service.
* **Delegate Contact Phone (Delegate Contact Phone)** (`delegate_contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person or team providing the service.
* **Delegate Contact Address (Delegate Contact Address)** (`delegate_contact_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the person or team providing the service.
* **Service Title (Service Title)** (`service_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a title that describes the service being provided.
* **Service Description (Service Description)** (`service_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a description of the service being provided.
* **Service URL (Service URL)** (`service_url`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL of the service being provided.
