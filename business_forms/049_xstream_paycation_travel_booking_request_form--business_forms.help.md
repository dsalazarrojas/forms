# Xstream Paycation Travel Booking Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request travel for work-related activities. You can use it to book travel for conferences, seminars, meetings, or any other business-related travel.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last name as they appear on your company ID or passport.
2. Enter your email address associated with your Xstream Paycation account.
3. Provide your phone number for easy contact.
4. Choose the date and time you would like to travel.
5. Select the location of your travel destination.
6. Choose the modes of transportation you would like to use (e.g., air, car, bus, train).
7. Add any additional notes or comments about your trip.
8. Indicate how frequently you plan to travel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your company ID or passport.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your company ID or passport.

### Email
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Please enter the email address associated with your Xstream Paycation account.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number for easy contact.

### Travel Date
* **Travel Date** (`Travel Date`, date, optional): Choose the date you would like to travel.

### Travel Time
* **Travel Time** (`Travel Time`, time, optional): Choose the time you would like to travel.

### Travel Location
* **Travel Location** (`Travel Location`, select one, optional): Choose the location of your travel destination. Options include:
	+ New York
	+ Los Angeles
	+ Chicago
	+ Houston
	+ Phoenix
	+ Philadelphia
	+ San Antonio
	+ San Diego
	+ San Jose
	+ Austin

### Travel Mode
* **Travel Mode** (`Travel Mode`, select multiple, required): Choose the modes of transportation you would like to use. Options include:
	+ air
	+ car
	+ bus
	+ train

### Notes
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your trip.

### Travel Frequency
* **Travel Frequency** (`Travel Frequency`, number, optional): Indicate how frequently you plan to travel.
