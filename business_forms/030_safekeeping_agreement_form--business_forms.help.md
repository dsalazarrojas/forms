# Safekeeping Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safekeeping Agreement Form is used to document the details of items being stored on our premises, including the depositor's and custodian's information, storage terms, and dates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all required fields (if applicable) with the requested information.
2. Ensure that you enter the correct date in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Depositor Name** (Depositor Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization storing the item.
* **Depositor Phone** (Depositor Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or organization storing the item.
* **Depositor Email** (Depositor Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person or organization storing the item.
* **Item Id** (Item Id, text, optional): Enter the unique identifier of the item being stored.
* **Item Description** (Item Description, text, optional): Briefly describe the item being stored.
* **Storage Terms** (Storage Terms, text, optional): Specify any special storage requirements or conditions.
* **Custodian Name** (Custodian Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization responsible for the item's storage.
* **Custodian Phone** (Custodian Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or organization responsible for the item's storage.
* **Custodian Email** (Custodian Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person or organization responsible for the item's storage.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, optional): Enter the date the item was stored.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, optional): Enter the date the item is expected to be returned or retrieved.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- Double-check dates for correctness.
- If the item is being stored for an extended period, please be sure to update the end date as needed.
- For multiple items, create separate forms for each item.
