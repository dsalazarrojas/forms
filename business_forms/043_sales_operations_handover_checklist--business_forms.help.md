# sales_operations_handover_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sales operations handover checklist is a crucial tool for sales teams to ensure a seamless handover of sales tasks and responsibilities. It helps identify and track the progress of various sales tasks, enabling teams to stay on top of deadlines and maintain customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Go to the "Client Details" page (Page 1) and fill out the required information about the client, such as their name and contact details.
2.  On the "Prophets" page (Page 2), select whether the sales operation involves prophets or not. This helps identify potential conflicts of interest.
3.  Move on to the "Deal Details" page (Page 3) and enter any relevant information about the deal, such as its status and any associated notes.
4.  In the "Sales Notes" section (Page 4), add any additional comments or notes about the sales operation, including the sales manager's name.
5.  Select the next steps to be taken in the "Next Steps" page (Page 5).
6.  Choose the follow-up frequency and time on the "Follow-up" page (Page 6).
7.  Choose the sales manager responsible for the sales operation in the "Sales Manager" page (Page 7).
8.  Select members of the sales team involved in the sales operation in the "Sales Team" page (Page 8).
9.  Select the current status of the client on the "Client Status" page (Page 9).
10.  Enter the handover date and time on the "Handover Date" and "Handover Time" page (Page 10 and 11).
11.  Add any additional client comments or notes in the "Client Comments" section (Page 12).
12.  Enter any final sales comments or notes in the "Sales Comments" section (Page 13).
13.  Select the follow-up time on the "Follow-up Time" page (Page 14).
