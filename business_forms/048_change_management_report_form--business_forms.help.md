# Change Management Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about changes being managed across the organization. It is essential to provide an overview of the status of ongoing changes, including key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics to track progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the report.
2. Enter your name and job title.
3. Provide the name of the department responsible for the change initiative.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the reporting period.
5. For each change initiative, select its current status (On track, At risk, Behind schedule, On hold, Completed, Cancelled).
6. Enter the percentage of completion.
7. List the milestones achieved this period and upcoming milestones.
8. Select the current budget status (Under budget, On budget, Over budget, Not applicable).
9. If applicable, enter the amount over or under budget.
10. Describe how resources are being used.
11. Select the level of stakeholder engagement (Highly engaged, Moderately engaged, Minimally engaged, Resistant).
12. Select the rate of change adoption (Exceeding targets, Meeting targets, Below targets, Significant resistance).
13. Enter any current issues and risks.
14. Describe any actions being taken to mitigate issues and risks.
15. If applicable, list key decisions needed from leadership.
16. If applicable, describe the support required.
17. Enter any key learnings from this period.
18. Plan the activities for the next period.
19. Enter any recommendations for leadership.
20. Sign and date the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date**: Enter the date of the report. The date should be in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Report Author**: Enter your name as the person preparing the report.
* **Author Title**: Enter your job title. This field is not required but helps with reporting hierarchy.
* **Department**: Enter the name of the department responsible for the change initiative.
* **Change Initiative Name**: Enter the name of the change project.
* **Reporting Period Start**: Enter the start date of the reporting period.
* **Reporting Period End**: Enter the end date of the reporting period.
* **Project Status**: Select the current status of the change initiative (On track, At risk, Behind schedule, On hold, Completed, Cancelled).
* **Completion Percentage**: Enter the percentage of completion. This should reflect the progress made towards the project goals.
* **Milestones Achieved**: List the milestones completed this period.
* **Upcoming Milestones**: List the milestones planned for the next period.
* **Budget Status**: Select the current budget status (Under budget, On budget, Over budget, Not applicable).
* **Budget Variance**: If applicable, enter the amount over or under budget.
* **Resource Utilization**: Describe how resources are being used for the project.
* **Stakeholder Engagement**: Select the level of stakeholder engagement (Highly engaged, Moderately engaged, Minimally engaged, Resistant).
* **Change Adoption Rate**: Select the rate of change adoption (Exceeding targets, Meeting targets, Below targets, Significant resistance).
* **Issues and Risks**: Enter any current issues and risks.
* **Mitigation Actions**: Describe any actions being taken to mitigate issues and risks.
* **Key Decisions Needed**: If applicable, list key decisions needed from leadership.
* **Support Required**: If applicable, describe the support required.
* **Lessons Learned**: Enter any key learnings from this period.
* **Next Steps**: Plan the activities for the next period.
* **Recommendations**: Enter any recommendations for leadership.
* **Report Signature**: Sign and date the report with your full name. This serves as an indication that the report is completed and reviewed.
