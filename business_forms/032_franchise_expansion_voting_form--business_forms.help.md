# Franchise Expansion Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Franchise Expansion Voting Form is designed for stakeholders to vote on proposed markets for franchise expansion. This form allows you to weigh in on the potential expansion opportunities and provide feedback for the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the proposed markets listed below. Select the markets you support for franchise expansion by choosing "Yes" from the "Proposed Markets" field.
2. Provide feedback on your thoughts and suggestions by typing in the "Feedback" field. This is optional, but your input will help the team make informed decisions.
3. Cast your vote as a stakeholder by inputting a number in the "Stakeholder Votes" field.
4. If you're a team member, enter your team's name in the "Team Name" field.
5. Indicate the location(s) where you'd like to see the franchise expanded to by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Proposed Markets
* **Proposed Markets** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the markets you support for franchise expansion.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`text`, required: false): Provide your thoughts and suggestions for the team.

### Stakeholder Votes
* **Stakeholder Votes** (`number`, required: false): Cast your vote as a stakeholder by inputting a number.

### Team Name
* **Team Name** (`text`, required: false): If you're a team member, enter your team's name here.

### Location
* **Location** (`select_one`, required: false): Indicate the location(s) where you'd like to see the franchise expanded to by selecting "Yes" or "No".
