# event_concept_innovation_proposal_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an event concept innovation proposal. It's intended for event organisers or proposers to submit their proposals for events that aim to innovate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information about your event concept.
2. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Concept Innovation Proposal Application** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): The main title of this form is the application for an event concept innovation proposal.
  - This is the title of your proposal.
- **Event Organiser Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): The name of the event organiser or proposer.
  - Please fill in your name as it appears on your business cards or contact information.
- **Event Concept** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): A brief description of your event concept.
  - This is a place to describe your event, including any relevant details that might help review your proposal.
- **Event Description** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): A more detailed description of your event.
  - This is an optional field for providing additional information about your event. This can include details such as event schedule, speaker information, or any other relevant details.
- **Event Date** (`5`, `date`, required/optional): The date of the event.
  - Please use the format mm/dd/yyyy for this field.
- **Event Time** (`6`, `time`, required/optional): The time of the event.
  - Enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
- **Event Location** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): The location of the event.
  - Enter the physical address of the event.
- **Event Organiser Email** (`8`, `email`, required/optional): The email of the event organiser or proposer.
  - This is your contact information for follow-up questions.
- **Event Organiser Phone** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): The phone number of the event organiser or proposer.
  - This is your contact number for follow-up questions or emergencies.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
- Review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure that all information is correct.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organiser or proposer listed on this form.
