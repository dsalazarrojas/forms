# Weekly Employee Timesheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Employee Timesheet Form is a tool used to collect employee work hours, tasks, and other relevant data for each week. This form helps management track and analyze company-wide metrics, enabling informed decisions to be made.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the company name where indicated.
2. Fill out your employee name and ID in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the start and end dates of the week you are reporting on.
4. Record your total work hours for the week.
5. Describe the tasks you completed during the week.
6. Enter the start and end times for each task.
7. Check the Shift Id option to indicate if you worked shifts during the week, and select 'Yes' or 'No'.
8. If you worked shifts, enter the shift name and ID.
9. Enter your weekly salary.
10. Choose your current employee status (Happy or Sad).
11. Decide if you want to share your timesheet with your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company**: Enter the name of your company where indicated.
- **Employee**: Fill out your employee name and ID in the corresponding fields.
  *   This field is used to identify you as an employee and associate your timesheet data with your profile.
- **Week Start Date**: Select the date when the week began.
  *   Use the date format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Week End Date**: Select the date when the week ended.
  *   Use the date format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Work Hours**: Record your total work hours for the week.
  *   Enter the total hours you worked during the week.
- **Task**: Describe the tasks you completed during the week.
  *   Briefly outline the tasks you worked on and their respective hours.
- **Task Start Time**: Enter the start time for each task.
  *   Use the 24-hour clock format hh:mm.
- **Task End Time**: Enter the end time for each task.
  *   Use the 24-hour clock format hh:mm.
- **Shift Id**: Select 'Yes' if you worked shifts during the week, 'No' otherwise.
  *   Check this field to indicate if you worked shifts.
- **Shift Name**: If you worked shifts, enter the name of the shift.
  *   Enter the name of the shift you worked.
- **Shift ID**: Enter the ID of the shift you worked.
  *   Enter the ID of the shift you worked.
- **Weekly Salary**: Enter your weekly salary.
  *   Enter the amount you earned for the week.
- **Employee Status**: Select your current status as an employee (Happy or Sad).
  *   Indicate your current state of mind.
- **Share With Manager**: Select 'True' or 'False' to share your timesheet with your manager.
  *   Decide if you want to share your timesheet with your manager.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Review the form before submitting.
* Ask your manager or HR representative if you have any questions or concerns.
* Submit the form by the end of each week to ensure timely processing.
