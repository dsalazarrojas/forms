# Damaged Goods Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to report damaged or defective products. Please fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about your experience with the product.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and address.
2. Provide the order number and date of the order.
3. Describe the product name and details about the damage.
4. Attach images of the damaged product if necessary.
5. Select the current status of the product.
6. Provide your contact information.
7. Enter the name of the business that sold you the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address**: Enter your full address, including street address and postal code.
* **Order Number**: Enter the order number associated with your purchase.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you received the order.
* **Product Name**: Describe the product that is damaged.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the damage to the product.
* **Attach Images**: Select "Yes" if you are attaching images to support your complaint.
* **Product Status**: Select the current status of the product, either "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Customer Contact**: Enter your contact information, including phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business that sold you the product.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person who sold you the product.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when describing the damage to the product.
* Attach images to support your complaint, if possible.
* If you are not sure about any field, please contact our customer support team for assistance.

This form is designed to help us understand your issue and potentially resolve it quickly and efficiently. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form.
