**Affiliate Revenue Report Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Affiliate Revenue Report Form is used to collect and track affiliate performance data, including click-throughs, conversions, sales, commission earned, and payouts. This form helps to keep track of a affiliate's revenue generated from referrals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Affiliate Name** and **Affiliate ID**, which should match the information provided by the affiliate partner.
2. Select the **Reporting Period**, which is the time frame for which you want to report revenue data.
3. Enter the **Total Clicks**, **Total Conversions**, and **Total Sales Amount**, which are the number of clicks, conversions, and gross sales value for the selected reporting period.
4. Enter the **Commission Rate** (as a percentage), which is the agreed rate for this affiliate.
5. Calculate the **Total Commission Earned** by multiplying the **Total Sales Amount** by the **Commission Rate**.
6. Enter the **Net Payout Amount**, which is the final amount to be paid to the affiliate after any adjustments or refunds.
7. Select the **Payment Status**, which indicates the current status of this payout (Pending, Approved, Paid, or Rejected).
8. Optionally, add any **Notes / Comments** regarding the report or payment status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Affiliate Name**: Enter the name of the affiliate to whom this report belongs.
* **Affiliate ID**: Enter the unique identifier for this affiliate.
* **Reporting Period**: Select the time frame for which you want to report revenue data (e.g., Jan 2024).
* **Total Clicks**: Enter the number of referral link clicks for the selected reporting period.
* **Total Conversions**: Enter the number of sales/leads generated for the selected reporting period.
* **Conversion Rate (%)**: Calculate the conversion rate as a percentage (optional).
* **Total Sales Amount**: Enter the gross value of sales for the selected reporting period.
* **Commission Rate (%)**: Enter the agreed commission rate as a percentage.
* **Total Commission Earned**: Calculate the total commission earned by multiplying the **Total Sales Amount** by the **Commission Rate**.
* **Net Payout Amount**: Enter the final amount to be paid to the affiliate after any adjustments or refunds.
* **Payment Status**: Select the current status of this payout (Pending, Approved, Paid, or Rejected).
* **Notes / Comments**: Add any notes or comments regarding the report or payment status.
