# Support For Ethical Financing Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you apply for ethical financing support. Please fill it out carefully to provide all necessary details about your organization and its needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your organization name and contact information in the "Organization" field.
2. Provide your name and contact details in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Fill out your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. If applicable, specify the values-based financing options in the "Values Based Financing" field.
7. Mention any social enterprises you are involved with in the "Social Enterprises" field.
8. Select the current status of your application from the "Application Status" dropdown list.
9. Briefly describe the review process your application underwent in the "Review Process" field.
10. Any additional comments or notes about your application can be placed in the "Application Comments" field.
11. If your application has been decided, note the date of the decision in the "Application Decision Date" field.
12. If you have any further comments or feedback, please enter them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (Organization, text, required): Please enter your organization's name as it should be known by us.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, required): Enter your name and any relevant contact information.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Address, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Values Based Financing** (Values Based Financing, text, optional): If you are applying for values-based financing, provide a description of the specific initiative and how it aligns with your organization's goals and values.
* **Social Enterprises** (Social Enterprises, text, optional): Mention any social enterprises you are involved with that aligns with the application.
* **Application Status** (Application Status, select_one, optional): Select the current status of your application.
* **Review Process** (Review Process, text, optional): Describe the review process your application underwent.
* **Application Comments** (Application Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Application Decision Date** (Application Decision Date, date, optional): Note the date your application was decided, if applicable.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Add any further comments or feedback.
