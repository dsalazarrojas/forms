# Rental Agreement Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Rental Agreement Acknowledgment Form is a document used to acknowledge and record the terms of a rental agreement between a landlord and a tenant. This form is essential for maintaining a clear understanding of the agreement's conditions, ensuring both parties are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the rental agreement carefully to understand its terms and conditions.
2. Fill in the tenant information section with the relevant details (if required).
3. Acknowledge the agreement terms in the provided text area.
4. Add your signature to confirm that you have read and understood the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Information (Page 1)**: This section is for recording the details of the tenant, such as name, address, and contact information. If you are a tenant, fill in your personal details. If you are a landlord, you may skip this section.
* **Agreement Terms (Page 1)**: This is the main section where you acknowledge the terms of the rental agreement. Please read the agreement carefully and type in the relevant text that confirms your understanding of the terms.
* **Signature (Page 1)**: This is where you add your official signature to confirm that you have read and understood the rental agreement. Make sure to use your actual signature (printed or digital) to avoid any confusion.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the rental agreement carefully before completing this form.
- If you are a tenant, ensure you understand all the terms before signing the form.
- If you are a landlord, double-check the tenant's information and agreement terms before accepting their signature.
- Keep a record of the completed form for future reference.
