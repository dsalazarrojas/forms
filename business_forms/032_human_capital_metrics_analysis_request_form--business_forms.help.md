# Human Capital Metrics Analysis Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for the analysis of human capital metrics for a specific employee or group of employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's ID in the `Employee ID` field.
2. Select the department(s) the employee belongs to in the `Department` field.
3. Enter the job title of the employee in the `Job Title` field.
4. Enter the start and end dates for the period you are requesting analysis for in the `Start Date` and `End Date` fields respectively.
5. Select the current job status of the employee in the `Job Status` field.
6. Choose the team members (if any) for which you are analyzing metrics in the `Team Members` field.
7. Enter the total hours worked by the employee or team member in the `Total Hours Worked` field.
8. Enter the employee's or team member's hourly wage in the `Hourly Wage` field.
9. Enter the total compensation for the employee or team member in the `Total Compensation` field.
10. Select the type of benefits the employee or team member is covered for in the `Benefits` field.
11. Finally, select any relevant training and development programs or opportunities the employee or team member has participated in in the `Training and Development` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID (1)**: Enter the unique identifier for the employee or team member.
* **Department (2)**: Select the department the employee or team member belongs to from the dropdown list.
* **Job Title (3)**: Enter the job title of the employee or team member.
* **Start Date (4)**: Enter the start date for the period you are requesting analysis for (e.g., a fiscal quarter or calendar year).
* **End Date (5)**: Enter the end date for the period you are requesting analysis for (e.g., a fiscal quarter or calendar year).
* **Job Status (6)**: Select the current job status of the employee or team member (active, inactive, or pending).
* **Team Members (7)**: Choose other team members for which you are analyzing metrics (if applicable).
* **Total Hours Worked (8)**: Enter the total hours worked by the employee or team member during the specified period.
* **Hourly Wage (9)**: Enter the employee's or team member's hourly wage.
* **Total Compensation (10)**: Enter the total compensation for the employee or team member during the specified period.
* **Benefits (11)**: Select the type of benefits the employee or team member is covered for (e.g., health insurance, retirement plan).
* **Training and Development (12)**: Select any relevant training and development programs or opportunities the employee or team member has participated in.

## Tips
* Ensure you have the necessary approval and information to complete this form accurately.
* Verify the employee or team member's job status and benefits before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with an HR representative or supervisor for guidance.
