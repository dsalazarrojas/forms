# Request Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information related to customer evaluations, such as contact persons, evaluation times, and methods. Fill out this form to ensure that all relevant details are captured for each evaluation request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in the "Contact Person" field, where you enter the name of the person who made the evaluation request.
2. Enter the "Email" address of the contact person in the "Email" field.
3. Provide the "Phone Number" of the contact person in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Fill in the "Date" of the evaluation in the "Date" field.
5. Choose the "Time" of the evaluation in the "Time" field.
6. In the "Request Details" field, provide a brief description of the evaluation request.
7. For the "Contact Method", select the method of contact you used (Contact Person, Email, Phone Number, Meeting, Call, or Video Call).
8. If you will be following up with the contact person, select "True" or "Maybe" for the "Follow up" field. If not, select "False".
9. If you had a meeting with the contact person, select "True" or "False" for the "Meeting" field.
10. If you had a video call with the contact person, select "True" or "False" for the "Video Call" field.
11. If you made a call to the contact person, select "True" or "False" for the "Call" field.
12. In the "Follow up Method" field, describe the method of follow-up you used.
13. If you've scheduled a meeting with the contact person, fill in the "Meeting Time" field with the date and time of the meeting.
14. If you held a meeting with the contact person, fill in the "Meeting Place" field with a description of the meeting location.
15. In the "Meeting Agenda" field, describe the topics discussed during the meeting.
16. If you followed up with the contact person, fill in the "Follow up Time" field with the date and time of the follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Person** (Text): Enter the name of the person who made the evaluation request.
* **Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Date** (Date): Fill in the date of the evaluation.
* **Time** (Time): Fill in the time of the evaluation.
* **Request Details** (Text): Provide a brief description of the evaluation request.
* **Contact Method** (Select One): Select the method of contact used (Contact Person, Email, Phone Number, Meeting, Call, or Video Call).
* **Follow up** (Select Multiple): Select whether follow-up will be done ("True", "False", Maybe, or Later).
* **Meeting** (Select One): Select whether a meeting was held ("True" or "False").
* **Video Call** (Select One): Select whether a video call was made ("True" or "False").
* **Call** (Select One): Select whether a call was made ("True" or "False").
* **Follow up Method** (Text): Describe the method of follow-up used.
* **Meeting Time** (Date): Fill in the date and time of the meeting, if applicable.
* **Meeting Place** (Text): Describe the location of the meeting, if applicable.
* **Meeting Agenda** (Text): Describe the topics discussed during the meeting, if applicable.
* **Follow up Time** (Date): Fill in the date and time of the follow-up, if applicable.

Note: This form is designed to guide the user through the process of capturing evaluation details for customer requests. Each field has a specific purpose, and following these steps will help ensure that all relevant information is collected accurately.
