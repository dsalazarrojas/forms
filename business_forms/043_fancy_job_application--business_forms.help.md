# ประกy Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **ประกy Job Application** form is used to submit job applications for current or former employees of the company. This form allows applicants to provide their name, job title, work experience, job description, contact information, and resume uploads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Name** in the designated field.
2. Enter your **Job Title** in the designated field.
3. Provide your **Work Experience 1** and **Work Experience 2** in the designated fields.
4. Enter a brief **Job Description** in the designated field.
5. Enter your **Contact Number** in the designated field.
6. Enter your **Email** in the designated field.
7. Upload your **Resume Upload 1**, **Resume Upload 2**, and **Resume Upload 3** (if applicable) in the designated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your current or previous job title.
- **Work Experience 1** (`work_experience_1`, `text`, required): Describe your work experience 1, e.g., "Software Engineer".
- **Work Experience 2** (`work_experience_2`, `text`, required): Describe your work experience 2, e.g., "Project Manager".
- **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the job you are applying for.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number.
- **Email** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address.
- **Resume Upload 1** (`resume_upload_1`, `text`, required): Upload your resume file 1.
- **Resume Upload 2** (`resume_upload_2`, `text`, required): Upload your resume file 2.
- **Resume Upload 3** (`resume_upload_3`, `text`, optional): Upload your resume file 3 (if necessary).
- **Referees** (`referees`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if you have provided referees' information.
- **Referees 2** (`referees_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you have provided referees' information 2.
- **Job Application Status** (`job_application_status`, `select_one`, optional): Set to "Active" once the application is submitted.
- **Work Experience 3** (`work_experience_3`, `text`, optional): Describe your work experience 3 (if applicable).
- **Referees 3** (`referees_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you have provided referees' information 3.
- **Referees 4** (`referees_4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you have provided referees' information 4.
- **Referees 5** (`referees_5`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you have provided referees' information 5.
- **Referees 6** (`referees_6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you have provided referees' information 6.
- **Referees 7** (`referees_7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you have provided referees' information 7.
- **Resume 1** (`upload_resume_1`, `text`, optional): Upload your resume file 1.
- **Resume 2** (`upload_resume_2`, `text`, optional): Upload your resume file 2.
- **Resume** (`upload_resume`, `text`, optional): Upload your resume file (if necessary).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Ensure that you only upload relevant resume files.
- If you have referees' information, select "Yes" for the relevant fields.
- Once you have submitted your application, set the **Job Application Status** to "Active".
