# Multi Store Account Merge Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Multi Store Account Merge Request Form is a user-facing form used to request the merge of multiple store accounts into one. This form collects necessary information to facilitate the merge process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Submit the form to our team for review.
3. Our team will review your request and process the merge accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Provide your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Provide your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, optional): Enter the store's address.
- **City** (`store_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the store is located.
- **Zip** (`store_zip`, number, optional): Enter the store's zip code.
- **State** (`store_state`, text, optional): Enter the state where the store is located.
- **Country** (`store_country`, text, optional): Enter the country where the store is located.
- **Phone** (`store_phone`, text, optional): Enter the store's phone number.
- **Reason for Merging** (`merge_request_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for merging the store accounts.
- **Store ID** (`store_id`, number, optional): Enter the store's ID.
- **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, optional): Enter the store's name.
- **Store Address**, **Store City**, **Store Zip**, **Store State**, **Store Country**, and **Store Phone** fields are required for the store account information.
- **Reason for Merging** field is optional but will help us understand the motivation behind the request.

**Tips**:
- Ensure that you have permission from all store owners to merge their accounts.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
