# Team Equipment Restock Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Equipment Restock Request Form is a tool used to notify the team that a restock is required for a specific equipment type. This form is for planned restocks and is not intended for urgent situations where equipment is already out of stock and needed immediately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field. Note that some fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
2. Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send your request to the team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Team Equipment Restock Request
• **Team Equipment Restock Request** (`team_equipment_restock_request_form`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the restock request. This could include any additional information that would be helpful for the team to know, such as the location where the equipment will be used or any special handling requirements.

### Equipment Type
• **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select one, not required): Select the type of equipment that needs to be restocked. This will help the team ensure that the correct equipment is ordered.

### Quantity
• **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, not required): Enter the quantity of equipment that needs to be restocked. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank and the team will determine the correct quantity.

### Date Requested
• **Date Requested** (`date_requested`, date, not required): Enter the date when you would like the equipment to be restocked. This will help the team plan ahead and ensure that the equipment is ordered and delivered on time.

### Time Requested
• **Time Requested** (`time_requested`, time, not required): Enter the time when you would like the equipment to be restocked. This will help the team coordinate the delivery with other teams and ensure that the equipment is ready when needed.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about the equipment type or quantity, it's better to err on the side of caution and leave the information blank. The team will follow up with you if they need more details.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the team directly. They will be happy to assist you.
* Please make sure to submit the form only once for each equipment type. Duplicate submissions can lead to delays and confusion.
