# Music Royalty Audit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Royalty Audit Request Form is used to request a royalty audit, which is an examination of a company's music publishing and royalty payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Artist Name: Enter the name of the artist or group being audited.
	* Label Name: Enter the name of the record label or publisher being audited.
	* Submitter Name: Enter your name and title.
	* Submitter Email: Enter your email address.
2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* Audit Period: Select the period of time you want to audit.
	* Start Date: Enter the start date of the audit period.
	* End Date: Enter the end date of the audit period.
	* Supporting Documents: You may upload supporting documents related to the audit.
	* Comments: You may add any additional comments or information related to the audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Id** (`Form Id`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to identify the form and is not required.
* **Artist Name** (`Artist Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the artist or group being audited.
* **Label Name** (`Label Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the record label or publisher being audited.
* **Audit Period** (`Audit Period`, `date`, optional): Select the period of time you want to audit.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of the audit period.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of the audit period.
* **Submitter Name** (`Submitter Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name and title.
* **Submitter Email** (`Submitter Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Supporting Documents** (`Supporting Documents`, `note`, optional): You may upload supporting documents related to the audit.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): You may add any additional comments or information related to the audit.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* You may upload supporting documents such as contracts, invoices, or other relevant documents related to the audit.
