# Microsoft Discovery Questions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather general information about a company, including company details, contact information, and business hours and contact preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Enter your first name and last name in the "Contact First Name" and "Contact Last Name" fields respectively.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Fill in your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the type of company you are representing in the "Company Type" field.
6. Enter a brief description of your company in the "Company Description" field.
7. Enter your job title in the "Contact Title" field.
8. Enter the department you are part of in the "Department" field.
9. Enter the number of employees in your company in the "Employee Count" field.
10. Select the size of your company in the "Company Size" field.
11. Select the industries your company operates in, separated by commas, in the "Industry" field.
12. Enter your company website in the "Company Website" field.
13. Enter your business hours in the "Business Hours" field.
14. Select your time zone in the "Time Zone" field.
15. Enter a brief description of your business hours in the "Business Hours Description" field.
16. Select your native language in the "Contact Language" field.
17. If your language is not listed, enter it in the "Other Language" field.
18. Select the types of phones you have, separated by commas, in the "Contact Phone Type" field.
19. Select the types of emails you have, separated by commas, in the "Contact Email Type" field.
20. Enter any additional contact notes in the "Contact Notes" field.
21. Enter the frequency of follow up in the "Follow Up Frequency" field.
22. Enter the date you would like to be followed up by in the "Lead Time" field.
23. Finally, enter the time you would like to be followed up by in the "Follow Up Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): The company name you represent.
* **Contact First Name** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Your first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): Your last name.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, required: `false`): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): Your phone number.
* **Company Type** (`6`, `select_one`, required: `false`): The type of company you are representing (e.g., SMB, Large Business, Non-Profit, Government, Other).
* **Company Description** (`7`, `text`, required: `false`): A brief description of your company.
* **Contact Title** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`): Your job title.
* **Department** (`9`, `text`, required: `false`): The department you are part of.
* **Employee Count** (`10`, `number`, required: `false`): The number of employees in your company.
* **Company Size** (`11`, `number`, required: `false`): The size of your company.
* **Industry** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): The industries your company operates in (e.g., IT, Finance, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Retail, Other).
* **Company Website** (`13`, `text`, required: `false`): Your company website.
* **Business Hours** (`14`, `time`, required: `false`): Your business hours.
* **Time Zone** (`15`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Your time zone.
* **Business Hours Description** (`16`, `text`, required: `false`): A brief description of your business hours.
* **Contact Language** (`17`, `text`, required: `false`): The language you are most comfortable with.
* **Other Language** (`18`, `text`, required: `false`): If your language is not listed, enter your native language here.
* **Contact Phone Type** (`19`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): The types of phones you have (e.g., Mobile, Work, Home, Other).
* **Contact Email Type** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): The types of emails you have (e.g., Work, Personal, Other).
* **Contact Notes** (`21`, `note`, required: `false`): Any additional contact notes.
* **Follow Up** (`22`, `text`, required: `false`): Frequency of follow up.
* **Follow Up Frequency** (`23`, `number`, required: `false`): The frequency of follow up.
* **Lead Time** (`24`, `date`, required: `false`): The date you would like to be followed up by.
* **Follow Up Time** (`25`, `time`, required: `false`): The time you would like to be followed up by.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out.
* This form is for internal business purposes only.
