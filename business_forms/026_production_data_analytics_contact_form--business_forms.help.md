# Production Data Analytics Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Production Data Analytics Contact Form. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Your Name** (*), please provide your full name.
2. Enter a valid **Email** (*), this will be used to contact you back.
3. Enter your **Company Name** (*), this will help us understand who you're representing.
4. Provide a brief **Message** (*), describe your request and we'll get back to you.
5. Select the **What type of analytics are you interested in?** option (Data, Reporting, Insights) that suits your needs.
6. Choose a date for your inquiry on **Date** (*).
7. Select a time for your inquiry on **Time** (*).
8. Choose the **What type of reporting are you looking for?** option (Reporting, Data, Insights) that best suits your needs.
9. In the **Additional Information** field, you can add any extra details that will help us serve you better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name** (`name`, `text`, *): Please provide your full name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, *): Enter a valid email address for us to get back to you.
- **Company Name** (`company`, `text`, *): This will help us understand who you're representing.
- **Message** (`message`, `text`, *): Describe your request or inquiry.
- **What type of analytics are you interested in?** (`analytics_type`, `select_multiple`): This field will help us understand what you need.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, *): This is where you select a date for your inquiry.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, *): This is where you select a time for your inquiry.
- **What type of reporting are you looking for?** (`reporting_type`, `select_multiple`, *): This field will help us serve you better.
- **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `note`, _): This is a field where you can add any extra details that will help us get a better understanding of your request.
