# Tax Filing Service Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for a tax filing service quotation. It is used to gather details about the client, the services required, and other relevant data to provide a quote for the tax filing service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's information, including their name, organization, and relationship.
2. Select the services required, such as Individual Tax Filing, Business Tax Filing, or Estate Tax Filing.
3. Choose the tax year and service type (Federal, State, or Local).
4. Provide the client's address, phone number, and email address.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.
6. Select the assigned user and the user who will create and update the quotation.
7. Set the assigned date and due date for the quotation.
8. Set the completed date and the user who completed the quotation.
9. Set the status of the quotation (Open, In Progress, or Closed).
10. Set the client status (Active or Non Active).
11. Select the user to whom the quotation is assigned.
12. Set the completed date and time for the quotation.
