# Product Recall Success Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Recall Success Monitoring Form is used to collect information about products that have been recalled. This data helps to track the progress and effectiveness of recall actions taken by the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company name that is responsible for the recall.
2. Enter the name of the product that is being recalled.
3. Provide the email of the contact person for the recall.
4. Enter the phone number of the contact person.
5. Select the date of birth of the contact person (this field is for internal use only and will not be shared with the public).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company** (`company`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the company responsible for the recall.
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the product being recalled.
- **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person for the recall.
- **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the recall.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): This field is for internal use only and will not be shared with the public; enter the date of birth of the contact person if available. 

## Tips
- Be accurate when filling out the form, as this data will help to track the progress and effectiveness of recall actions.
