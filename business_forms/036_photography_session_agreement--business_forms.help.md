# Photography Session Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a contract or agreement between the photographer and the client for a photography session. It captures essential details and terms to ensure a smooth and professional experience for both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for the photography session.
2. Review the terms and conditions carefully and agree to them by signing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details**
  This field is for capturing the session details, such as date, time, and location.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Your Photographer**
  This field is for the photographer's contact information, including name and signature.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Session Details**
  Similar to the "Session Details" field, this captures more session information.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Date and Time**
  Fill out the date and time for the photography session.
  * Type: date
  * Required: false

* **Location**
  Enter the location of the photography session.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Costs**
  Enter the costs associated with the photography session.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Terms and Conditions**
  Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the photography session.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Customer Signature**
  Sign the form to confirm agreement to the terms and conditions.
  * Type: note (signature)
  * Required: false

* **Photographer Signature**
  Sign the form as the photographer to confirm agreement to the terms and conditions.
  * Type: select_one (options: John Doe, Jane Smith, Mary Johnson)
  * Required: false

* **E-signature**
  A text field for electronic signature.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Contact Number**
  Enter the client's contact number.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Contact Email**
  Enter the client's contact email.
  * Type: email
  * Required: false

* **Contact Address**
  Enter the client's contact address.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Payment Terms**
  Enter payment terms or conditions.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Photography Terms**
  Enter photography terms or conditions.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Session Terms**
  Enter session terms or conditions.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Client Name**
  Enter the client's name.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Contact Phone**
  Enter the client's contact phone number.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Session Date**
  Fill out the date of the photography session.
  * Type: date
  * Required: false

* **Session Time**
  Fill out the time of the photography session.
  * Type: time
  * Required: false

* **Session Length**
  Enter the length of the photography session.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Session Location**
  Enter the location of the photography session.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Session Notes**
  Enter any additional session notes or comments.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Payment Terms Details**
  Enter payment terms or conditions details.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

* **Photography Terms Details**
  Enter photography terms or conditions details.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

# Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
- Review the terms and conditions carefully before signing.
- Ensure all signatures are legible and clear.
- Store the form in a secure location after completion.
