# Sales Tax Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users calculate sales tax liabilities based on given sales tax rate, sales tax amount, and purchase amount.

To use this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the **Sales Tax Rate** into the field labeled "Tax Rate" (Field 1).
* Enter the **Sales Tax Amount** into the field labeled "Sales Tax Amount" (Field 2).
* Enter the **Purchase Amount** into the field labeled "Purchase Amount" (Field 3).
