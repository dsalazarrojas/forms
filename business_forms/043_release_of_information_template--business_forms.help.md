# Release Of Information Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information necessary for releasing confidential or sensitive information to third parties. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form fields as instructed, providing accurate and detailed information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out, as marked.
3. Review the form for completeness before submission.
