# Vendor Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Recommendation Form is used to gather information about vendors, service providers, or other types of suppliers for the team's consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Form Start" page to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked as `required` in the form.
3. Click on the "Form End" page to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name**: This field is for the name of the vendor you are recommending. Please enter the full name of the vendor as it appears on their official website or business card.
* **Email**: If the vendor has a public email address, please enter it here. This helps us ensure we have accurate contact information.
* **Phone**: If the vendor has a public phone number, please enter it here. This helps us ensure we have accurate contact information.
* **Vendor Type**: Choose from the dropdown menu to select the type of vendor you are recommending (Vendor, Service Provider, or Other).
* **Vendor Location**: Please enter the location of the vendor's office or main address.
* **Rating**: If you have had a previous interaction or experience with the vendor, please rate them from 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Recommendation**: If you have any notes or comments about the vendor, please enter them here. This helps us understand why you are recommending this vendor.
* **Form Comments**: Please enter any additional comments or notes about the vendor here.
* **Form Start** and **Form End**: These two pages are used to mark the beginning and end of the form. Please click on them as instructed in the "How To Complete This Form" section.
