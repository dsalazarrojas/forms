# Nutritional Label Review Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to review and report on the nutritional label of a product. It captures information about the product name, serving size, ingredient list, and other relevant details that are reviewed by a team of experts. The form is used to ensure compliance with nutrition labeling regulations and to keep track of reviews and their status.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out each section carefully, making sure to enter accurate information about the product being reviewed.
2. Enter the product name in the "Product Name" field.
3. Enter the serving size and unit in the "Serving Size" and "Serving Size Unit" fields.
4. Enter a list of ingredients in the "Ingredient List" field.
5. Enter the nutrition facts, such as nutrition labels and ingredient lists, in the "Nutrition Facts" field.
6. Decide whether the product complies with nutrition labeling regulations and select "In Compliance" or "Not In Compliance" in the "Compliance Decision" field.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes about the review in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter the date and time of the review in the "Review Date" field.
9. Enter the name of the reviewer in the "Review By" field.
10. Select the product label review ID from the options in the "Product Label Review ID" field.
11. Select the review status from the options in the "Review Status" field.
12. Enter the name of the labeler in the "Labeler" field.
13. Enter the reviewer's ID in the "Reviewer" field.
14. Select the review type from the options in the "Review Type" field.
15. Enter the review version number in the "Review Version" field.
16. Enter the date of the last review in the "Last Reviewed" field.
17. Enter the date of the first review in the "First Reviewed" field.
18. Enter the date of the next review in the "Next Reviewed" field.
19. Enter the date the record was last updated in the "Last Updated" field.
20. Enter the name of the creator of the record in the "Created By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product being reviewed.
* **Serving Size** (`product_weight_oz`, number, required): Enter the serving size of the product.
* **Serving Size Unit** (`serving_size_unit`, text, required): Enter the unit of the serving size (e.g., "oz", "g", "ml", etc.).
* **Label Statement** (`label_statement`, text, required): Enter a statement about the label, such as "Label is clear and accurate" or "Label is misleading".
* **Ingredient List** (`ingredient_list`, text, required): Enter a list of ingredients in the product.
* **Nutrition Facts** (`nutrition_facts`, text, required): Enter any additional nutrition facts or labels related to the product.
* **Compliance Decision** (`compliance_decision`, select_one, required): Select "In Compliance" if the label is compliant with regulations, or "Not In Compliance" if it is not.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the review.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Enter the date and time of the review.
* **Review By** (`review_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Product Label Review ID** (`product_label_review_id`, select_one, required): Select the product label review ID from the options.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the review from the options.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the review.
* **Labeler** (`labeler`, text, required): Enter the name of the labeler.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, text, required): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Label Reviewer ID** (`label_reviewer_id`, text, required): Enter the reviewer's ID.
* **Review Type** (`review_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of review from the options.
* **Review Version** (`review_version`, text, optional): Enter the version number of the review.
* **Last Reviewed** (`last_reviewed`, date, required): Enter the date of the last review.
* **First Reviewed** (`first_reviewed`, date, required): Enter the date of the first review.
* **Next Reviewed** (`next_reviewed`, date, optional): Enter the date of the next review.
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, date, optional): Enter the date the record was last updated.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the creator of the record.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the updater of the record.

Note: This help guide is not meant to be a comprehensive guide to the form, but rather a starting point for users to understand the purpose and usage of the form.
