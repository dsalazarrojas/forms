# utility_bill_verification_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The utility_bill_verification_form is designed to verify information related to utility bills, specifically for customers to report their bill details to the management. This form helps to ensure accuracy and completeness of billing information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out.
2. **Use the format specified for date fields** (MM/DD/YY).
3. **Select the correct service provider from the options** (Electricity, Gas, Water, Cable, Internet, or Phone).
4. **Enter your email and phone number if you have one**.
5. **Provide the address where the bill is being billed and the address where the service is being provided**.
6. **Enter any additional details about the bill in the "Bill details" field**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your billing information.
* **Bill Date** (`bill_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the bill (e.g., 01/01/2022).
* **Bill Amount** (`bill_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of the bill.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, select_one, required): Choose the type of utility service provider (Electricity, Gas, Water, Cable, Internet, or Phone).
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you have one.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, note, optional): Enter the physical address where the bill is being billed.
* **Service Address** (`service_address`, note, optional): Enter the address where the service is being provided.
* **Bill Details** (`bill_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional details or remarks about the bill.

## Tips

* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you need help with any field, contact the management team.
