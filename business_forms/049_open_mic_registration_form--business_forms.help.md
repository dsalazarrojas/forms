# open_mic_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from performers about their availability and participation in events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Click on the "Submissions" button at the end of each page. This will allow you to review and confirm your submitted information.
2. Fill out each section as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performer Info** (`performer_info`, `text`, required): This is the first page of the form where you will enter your name and any other relevant performer information.
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, `text`, required): Enter the name and details of the event you are participating in.
* **Performer Availability** (`performer_availability`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide more information about your availability for the event, you can do so here.
* **Performer Contact** (`performer_contact`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information so that event organizers can get in touch with you.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required): Provide any additional details about the event you are participating in.
* **Confirm Info** (`confirm_info`, `text`, required): Confirm that the information you provided is accurate.
