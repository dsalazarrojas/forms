# Sweepstakes Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect entry details for a sweepstakes competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Entry Details section with your information.
2. Provide your email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your name in the Name field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
5. Enter a sweepstakes slogan in the Sweepstakes Slogan field (optional).
6. Choose the Entry Date and Entry Time fields according to the sweepstakes entry schedule.
7. Select the Entry Method by choosing from the options: Phone, Online, or In Person.
8. Enter the Entry Reason in the Entry Reason field (optional).
9. Enter any additional Entry Message in the Entry Message field (optional).
10. Enter any reference information in the Entry Reference field (optional).
11. Select the Entry Type as Sweepstakes or Drawing.
12. Choose the Entry Status as Active or Inactive.
13. Select the Assigned To field as John or Jane.
14. Enter the Assigned Date and Assigned Time fields according to the sweepstakes schedule.
15. Enter the Entry Id field (optional).
16. Enter the Form Id field (optional).
17. Enter the Created At, Updated At, Deleted At, Created By, Updated By, Deleted By fields (optional).
18. Enter any Entry Comments in the Entry Comments field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entry Details**: Provide a brief description of your entry, including any necessary details for the competition.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to allow for communication and prize notifications.
* **Name**: Enter your full name to identify yourself as the entrant.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in case the competition requires phone verification.
* **Sweepstakes Slogan**: Enter a brief catchphrase or slogan to stand out in the competition.
* **Entry Date**: Enter the date you made the entry according to the sweepstakes schedule.
* **Entry Time**: Enter the time you made the entry according to the sweepstakes schedule.
* **Entry Method**: Select how you entered the sweepstakes competition: Phone, Online, or In Person.
* **Entry Reason**: Enter a brief reason why you entered the sweepstakes competition.
* **Entry Message**: Enter any additional comments or thoughts about your entry.
* **Entry Reference**: Enter any reference information related to your entry, such as a URL or a code.
* **Entry Type**: Select whether this is a Sweepstakes or Drawing entry.
* **Entry Status**: Choose Active if you are eligible to continue participating in the competition, Inactive if you are not.
* **Assigned To**: Select who the entry was assigned to, John or Jane.
* **Assigned Date**: Enter the date you were assigned to.
* **Assigned Time**: Enter the time you were assigned to.
* **Entry Id**: Enter the ID of your entry (if provided by the competition organizers).
* **Form Id**: Enter the ID of the form used for the entry (if provided by the competition organizers).
* **Created At**: Enter the date and time the entry was created.
* **Updated At**: Enter the date and time the entry was last updated.
* **Deleted At**: Enter the date and time the entry was deleted.
* **Created By**: Enter the name or handle of the person who created the entry.
* **Updated By**: Enter the name or handle of the person who last updated the entry.
* **Deleted By**: Enter the name or handle of the person who deleted the entry.
* **Entry Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your entry or the competition.
