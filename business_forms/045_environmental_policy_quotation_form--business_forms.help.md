# Environmental Policy Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for environmental policy quotations. It is meant to be completed by contacts who provide quotes or estimates for policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the policy type**: Choose the type of policy you are providing a quote or estimate for.
2. Fill in the contact information**: Enter the name and phone number of the contact.
3. Enter the quote amount**: Enter the quote amount in the relevant currency.
4. Enter quote terms**: Enter any additional terms or conditions for the quote.
5. Enter any additional information**: If necessary, enter any additional information or comments about the policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policy Type**: Select the type of policy this quote or estimate is for.
* **Policy Date**: (Optional) The date the policy was created or is effective.
* **Contact Name**: The name of the contact or individual providing the quote or estimate.
* **Contact Phone**: (Optional) The phone number of the contact.
* **Policy Number**: (Optional) A unique identifier for the policy.
* **Quote Amount**: The amount of the quote or estimate.
* **Quote Currency**: Select the currency of the quote or estimate.
* **Policy Description**: (Optional) A brief description of the policy.
* **Quote Terms**: Enter any additional terms or conditions for the quote or estimate.
* **Contact Reported at**: (Optional) The date the contact information was reported.
* **Contact Reported 2**: (Optional) The date any updates to the contact information were reported.
* **Quote Expiration**: (Optional) The date the quote or estimate expires.
* **Quote Expiration 2**: (Optional) The date the quote or estimate 2 expires.
* **Quote Expiration 3**: (Optional) The date the quote or estimate 3 expires.
* **Policy Version**: (Optional) A version number for the policy.
* **Quote Currency 2**: Select the currency of the quote or estimate 2.
* **Quote Currency 3**: Select the currency of the quote or estimate 3.
* **Quote Currency 4**: Select the currency of the quote or estimate 4.
* **Quote Terms 2**: Enter any additional terms or conditions for the quote or estimate 2.
* **Quote Terms 3**: Enter any additional terms or conditions for the quote or estimate 3.
* **Quote Amount 3**: The amount of the quote or estimate 3.
* **Policy Goals**: (Optional) A brief description of the policy goals.
