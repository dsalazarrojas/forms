# Corporate Political Donation Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for companies to disclose their political contributions, such as campaign contributions, to the relevant authorities. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and honestly to maintain transparency and compliance with relevant laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the company name, address, and state where the company is located.
2. Provide the contribution date, amount, and type (goods, services, or other).
3. Enter the purpose of the contribution, if applicable.
4. Add the contact information for the person responsible for the contribution (contact first name, last name, email, and phone).
5. Review and confirm the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: The name of the company making the political contribution. This is a required field.
* **Address**: The address of the company making the contribution. This is a required field.
* **City**: The city where the company's address is located. This is a required field.
* **State**: The state where the company's address is located. This is a required field.
* **Zip Code**: The postal code of the company's address. This is a required field.
* **Contribution Date**: The date when the contribution was made. If you don't know the exact date, enter the closest estimate.
* **Contribution Amount**: The amount contributed to the political campaign or cause. This field is required.
* **Contribution Type**: The type of contribution made, such as goods, services, or other. This field is required.
* **Contribution Purpose**: A brief description of the purpose of the contribution, if applicable.
* **Contribution Frequency**: A description of how often the contributions are made, if applicable.
* **Contact First Name**: The first name of the person responsible for the contribution.
* **Contact Last Name**: The last name of the person responsible for the contribution.
* **Contact Email**: The email of the person responsible for the contribution. This should be a valid email address.
* **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the person responsible for the contribution.
* **Reviewer First Name**: The first name of the person reviewing or validating the contribution.
* **Reviewer Last Name**: The last name of the person reviewing or validating the contribution.
* **Reviewer Email**: The email of the person reviewing or validating the contribution. This should be a valid email address.
* **Reviewer Title**: The title of the person reviewing or validating the contribution.
* **Reviewer Department**: The department of the person reviewing or validating the contribution.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Any additional comments or notes from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Date**: The date when the review was completed.
* **Reviewer Timestamp**: The timestamp when the review was completed.
* **Reviewer Signature**: A digital signature of the person reviewing or validating the contribution.
