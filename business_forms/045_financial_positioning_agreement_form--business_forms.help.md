# Financial Positioning Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Financial Positioning Agreement Form is a document used for setting up or maintaining financial agreements between parties. This form helps in capturing essential details such as names, dates, and terms of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Complete the form in a sequential manner, starting from the first page.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled in before proceeding to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Positioning Agreement** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_title`): Enter a brief description of the agreement.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter the name of the client or participant involved in the agreement.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`): Record the date the agreement was made or is scheduled to be made.
* **Description** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_description`): Provide a detailed description of the agreement.
* **Terms Of Agreement** (`terms_of_agreement`): List the terms and conditions of the agreement.
* **Participant Acknowledgement** (`participant_acknowledgement`): Confirm the participant's understanding of the agreement.
* **Agreement Date 2** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_agreement_date`): Record another date related to the agreement.
* **Participant** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_participant`): Enter the name of the participant involved in the agreement.
* **Terms Of Agreement Date** (`terms_of_agreement_date`): Record the date for the terms of agreement.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Place a signature from the participant.
* **Participant Signature** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_participant_signature`): Record the participant's signature.
* **Advisor Signature** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_advisor_signature`): Record the advisor's signature.
* **Client Name 2** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_client_name`): Enter the client's name again, possibly for verification.
* **Terms** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_terms`): List the terms of the agreement again.
* **Agreement Date 3** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_agreement_date_3`): Record another date related to the agreement.
* **Participant Acknowledgement 2** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_participant_acknowledgement_2`): Confirm the participant's understanding again.
* **Terms 2** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_terms_2`): List the terms again.
* **Agreement Date 4** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_agreement_date_4`): Record another date.
* **Participant 2** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_participant_2`): Enter the participant's name again.
* **Terms 3** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_terms_3`): List the terms again.
* **Agreement Date 5** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_agreement_date_5`): Record another date.
* **Participant 3** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_participant_3`): Enter the participant's name again.
* **Terms 4** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_terms_4`): List the terms again.
* **Agreement Date 6** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_agreement_date_6`): Record another date.
* **Participant 4** (`financial_positioning_agreement_form_template_participant_4`): Enter the participant's name again.

Please note that some field names and labels seem redundant. Ensure to fill in the required information accurately to maintain the integrity of the form.
