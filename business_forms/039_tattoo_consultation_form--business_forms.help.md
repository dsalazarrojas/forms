# Tattoo Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tattoo Consultation Form is a tool for businesses to guide their clients through the process of integrating their form with external services such as Zapier, Trello, and Google Calendar. The form aims to help users set up automatic workflows to create new cards in Trello and send notifications to Google Calendar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to understand the setup process for integrating your form with external services.
2. Customize the form with your business information to link it to your website and Facebook book now button.
3. Create a free Zapier account to integrate with Trello.
4. Create a Trello board for clients.
5. Add a card to the board and enable custom fields integration.
6. Set up a new Zap in Zapier to create new cards in Trello when a form submission is received.
7. Test the Zap with a fake submission.
8. Once set up, each form submission will create a new card in Trello automatically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customize the form for your business** (`custom_form_1`): This field allows you to add a text input for your business name or a brief description to make the form more personalized.
- **Link form to your website and Facebook book now button** (`link_1`): Enter the URL of your website and Facebook book now button link to promote your business on the form.
- **Create a Zapier free forever account** (`step_1`): This field is for creating a free account on Zapier, which is used to integrate your form with Trello and Google Calendar.
- **Create a Trello free forever account** (`step_2`): Similarly, this field is for creating a free account on Trello, which is used to create boards, lists, and cards.
- **Create a board in Trello called Clients** (`step_3`): In this field, you will create a board for your clients in Trello.
- **Add a card to the board and click custom fields integration** (`step_4`): This step is to add a new card to the board and enable custom fields integration to connect with Zapier.
- **Create a new Zap in Zapier** (`step_5`): Set up a new Zap in Zapier to create new cards in Trello when your form is submitted.
- **Select Create new card in Trello Client board as the second step** (`step_6`): In this step, you will select the action for the Zap to create a new card in the specified Trello board.
- **Test the Zap with a fake submission** (`step_7`): Test the Zap with a fake submission to ensure it is working correctly.
- **Done! Each time your client fills out your form it will create a new card in Trello** (`step_8`): Congratulations! You have completed the setup. Every time a client submits your form, a new card will be created in Trello automatically.
- **Take it a step further by zapping your Trello cards to Google Calendar** (`step_9`): Optionally, you can set up the Zap to send notifications to Google Calendar when a card is moved to the Appointment list in Trello.
- **None** (`step_10`): This field is not used in the form, please ignore it.
