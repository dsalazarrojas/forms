# office_inventory_information_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **office_inventory_information_record_form** is a form used to collect and record information about office inventory, including items such as furniture, equipment, supplies, and other assets. This form helps office administrators track and manage the inventory, providing a centralized repository of information about each item.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and ensure that you are collecting information for a new item in the inventory.
2. Complete each field with the relevant information for the item:
	* **Office** (Field 1): Enter the name of the office where the item is located.
	* **item_type** (Field 2): Select the type of item from the list of options (e.g., Office Furniture, Office Equipment, etc.).
	* **item_name** (Field 3): Enter the name or description of the item.
	* **item_desc** (Field 4): Provide a brief description of the item.
	* **item_price** (Field 5): Enter the price of the item.
	* **item_price_unit** (Field 6): Select the price unit from the available options (e.g., USD, EUR, etc.).
	* **item_quantity** (Field 7): Enter the quantity of the item.
	* **item_condition** (Field 8): Select the condition of the item from the available options (e.g., New, Used, etc.).
	* **item_color** (Field 9): Enter the color of the item (if applicable).
	* **item_status** (Field 10): Select the status of the item from the available options (e.g., Active, Inactive, etc.).
	* **item_location** (Field 11): Enter the location of the item.
	* **item_note** (Field 12): Add any additional notes or comments about the item.
	* **assigned_to** (Field 13): Enter the person or department assigned to the item.
	* **asset_tag** (Field 14): Enter the asset tag number (if applicable).
	* **purchase_date** (Field 15): Enter the date of purchase.
	* **purchase_time** (Field 16): Enter the time of purchase.
	* **warranty_start_date** (Field 17): Enter the start date of the warranty.
	* **warranty_end_date** (Field 18): Enter the end date of the warranty.
	* **asset_status** (Field 19): Select the status of the asset from the available options (e.g., Active, Inactive, etc.).
	* **asset_location** (Field 20): Enter the location of the asset.
	* **asset_condition** (Field 21): Select the condition of the asset from the available options (e.g., Good, Fair, etc.).
	* **asset_tag_status** (Field 22): Select the status of the asset tag from the available options (e.g., Active, Inactive, etc.).
	* **notes** (Field 23): Add any additional notes or comments about the asset.
	* **asset_tags** (Field 24): Select the categories for the asset from the available options (e.g., Office Furniture, Office Equipment, etc.).
	* **submit** (Field 25): Confirm that the information is accurate and complete before submitting.
