# Sustainable Crop Procurement Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to capture information about suppliers and their sustainable crops. It helps our team verify the authenticity and reliability of the suppliers' claims, ensuring a more sustainable and responsible procurement process.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please complete the form by following the instructions for each field below.
*   Enter your first name in the text field marked **First Name**.
*   Enter your email address in the email field marked **Email**.
*   Enter your phone number in the text field marked **Phone**.
*   Enter your physical address in the text field marked **Address**.
*   Select the product type(s) you are registering for in the select multiple field marked **Product Type**. Please choose all applicable options.
*   Enter the name of your company/organization in the text field marked **Report Name**.
*   Enter your company/organization's address in the text field marked **Supplier Address**.
*   Enter your contact phone number in the text field marked **Supplier Phone**.
*   Enter your email address in the email field marked **Supplier E-mail**.
*   Indicate if your product has been certified as sustainable in the select one field marked **Certification**. Please select **Yes** if your product is certified.
*   If your product has been certified, enter the date of certification in the date field marked **Certification Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Enter your personal name as it is to be used in our records.
*   **Email**: Enter the email address you use to receive notifications and communication.
*   **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Address**: Enter your physical address.
*   **Product Type**: Select all product types that apply to your registration.
*   **Report Name**: Enter your company/organization's name as it will be displayed in our records.
*   **Supplier Address**: Enter your company/organization's address.
*   **Supplier Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for your company/organization.
*   **Supplier E-mail**: Enter your company/organization's email address.
*   **Certification**: If your product has been certified as sustainable, select **Yes**.
*   **Certification Date**: Enter the date of certification if your product is certified. If not certified, leave this field blank.
*   **Address**: Enter your company/organization's address.
