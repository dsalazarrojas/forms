# Energy Audit Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Audit Check Out Form is a critical document that captures all the necessary information related to an energy audit, ensuring that the process is complete and accurate. This form is essential for identifying areas of improvement and providing a comprehensive report on the energy usage and efficiency of a building or home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions in the "Energy Usage Questions" section, indicating whether you have implemented or plan to implement energy efficiency improvements such as air sealing, LED light bulbs, radiant barriers, solar attic fans, or weatherstripping.
2.  In the "Energy Efficiency Improvements" section, select one or more energy efficiency improvement options that you plan to implement or have already implemented.
3.  In the "Date Of Visit" field, enter the date of the energy audit visit.
4.  In the "Time Of Visit" field, enter the time of the energy audit visit (optional).
5.  In the "Contact Information" field, provide your name and contact details for further communication.
6.  In the "Phone Number" field, enter your phone number for contact purposes.
7.  In the "Email" field, enter your email address for further communication (optional).
8.  In the "Additional Comments" field, provide any additional comments or notes about the energy audit process or results.
9.  Finally, sign the "Signature" field to confirm that the form has been filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Energy Audit Check Out Form** (`energy_audit_check_out_form`, text, required): This is the main title of the form that summarizes the energy audit process.
* **Energy Usage Questions** (`energy_usage_questions`, select_multiple, required): This section asks you to indicate whether you have implemented or plan to implement energy efficiency improvements such as air sealing, LED light bulbs, radiant barriers, solar attic fans, or weatherstripping.
* **Energy Efficiency Improvements** (`energy_efficiency_improvements`, select_one, required): This section asks you to select one or more energy efficiency improvement options that you plan to implement or have already implemented.
* **Energy Audit Results** (`energy_audit_results`, note, not required): This field is for you to note any additional information about the energy audit results, such as comments or observations.
* **Date Of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Enter the date of the energy audit visit.
* **Time Of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, not required): Enter the time of the energy audit visit (if applicable).
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Provide your name and contact details for further communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for further communication (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, not required): Provide any additional comments or notes about the energy audit process or results.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign the form to confirm that it has been filled out accurately and completely.
