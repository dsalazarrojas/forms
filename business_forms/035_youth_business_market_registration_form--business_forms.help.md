# youth_business_market_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering a youth business in a market. It is designed to gather necessary information from both the organizers and participants of a youth business market.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide participant information: Enter your name and contact details in the "Participant Information" section.
2. Enter business details: Provide the name of your business, market date and time, registration fee, and event organizer information.
3. Select event specifics: Choose "Yes" or "No" for event organizer, business license, and participant count.
4. Provide additional details: Fill in the "Event Description", "Event Location", and "Additional Participant Information" sections with specific details about your event.
5. Select business category and additional info: Select the "Business Category" and provide additional information in the "Additional Info" and "Additional Info 2" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (Participant Information, text, optional)**: Enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Business Name (text, optional)**: Enter the name of your youth business.
* **Market Date (date, optional)**: Enter the date of the market.
* **Market Time (time, optional)**: Enter the time of the market.
* **Registration Fee (number, optional)**: Enter the registration fee for the market.
* **Event Organizer (select_one, optional)**: Choose "Yes" if you are an event organizer.
* **Contact Person (text, optional)**: Enter the contact person's name and contact details.
* **Email (email, optional)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (text, optional)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Event Details (note, optional)**: Enter a description of your event in the text box.
* **Business License (select_multiple, optional)**: Choose "Yes" if you have a business license.
* **Participant Count (number, optional)**: Enter the number of participants.
* **Business Type (select_multiple, optional)**: Choose "Yes" if your business type is relevant.
* **Market Location (select_multiple, optional)**: Choose "Yes" if you are holding your market at a specific location.
* **Event Description (text, optional)**: Enter a description of your event.
* **Additional Participant Information (text, optional)**: Enter any additional participant information.
* **Business Category (select_one, optional)**: Choose "Yes" if your business category is relevant.
* **Additional Info (text, optional)**: Enter any additional information.
* **Additional Info 2 (text, optional)**: Enter any more additional information.
* **Participant Info 3 (text, optional)**: Enter any participant information.
* **Participant Info 4 (text, optional)**: Enter any more participant information.

Note: This guide is for reference only and does not include every field in this form.
