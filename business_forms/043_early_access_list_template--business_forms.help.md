# Early Access List Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Early Access List Template is a form used for collecting and managing information related to early access lists and their associated settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Email** in the designated field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **User Name** in the designated field.
3. Select your **Role** from the provided options (Admin, Developer, User).
4. Select the **Early Access List** you want to access from the provided options (Early Access List 1, Early Access List 2, Early Access List 3).
5. If applicable, enter the **Product Name**, **Product Description**, **Company Name**, **Contact Phone**, **Company Website**, and **Product Price**.
6. Select the **List Type** (Public, Private, Early Access) and **List Status** (Active, Inactive, Archived) that apply to your early access list.
7. Select the **List Owner** (List Owner 1, List Owner 2, List Owner 3) if applicable.
8. Enter any additional **User Notes** and **Company Notes** if needed.
9. Enter the **List Start Date** and **List End Date** if applicable.
10. Select the **Time Zone** and **Time Zone Offset** if necessary.
11. Select the **Assigned Tool** (Tool 1, Tool 2, Tool 3) if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address that will be associated with your user account.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it should be displayed on the early access list.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role within the organization (Admin, Developer, User).
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company associated with your early access list.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product or item on the early access list.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, note, optional): Enter a description of the product or item.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter a contact phone number for the company or product.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the company or product.
* **Early Access List** (`early_access_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more early access lists to be associated with your account.
* **List Start Date** (`list_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the early access list.
* **List End Date** (`list_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the early access list.
* **Time Zone** (`timezone`, time, optional): Select the time zone for the early access list.
* **Time Zone Offset** (`timezone_offset`, number, optional): Enter the offset from the time zone selected.
* **List Type** (`list_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of early access list (Public, Private, Early Access).
* **List Status** (`list_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the early access list (Active, Inactive, Archived).
* **List Owner** (`list_owner`, select_multiple, optional): Select the owner of the early access list.
* **User Notes** (`user_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes related to your account.
* **Company Notes** (`company_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes related to the company or product.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the product or item.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, number, optional): Enter your unique user ID.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, optional): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
* **User Agent** (`user_agent`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your user account.
* **Company Agent** (`company_agent`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the company or product.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to your account.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review the options carefully before selecting them to ensure they are accurate and relevant to your early access list.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, refer to the form's help guide or consult with the administrator for clarification.
* This form is designed for internal use within the organization and should be filled out by authorized personnel only.
