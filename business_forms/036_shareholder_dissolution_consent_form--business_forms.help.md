Shareholder Dissolution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shareholder Dissolution Consent Form is a document used to formally consent to the dissolution of a shareholder's interest in a company. This form is used to obtain formal consent from the shareholder prior to the dissolution process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the shareholder information in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the options for the shareholder's approvals and consents.
3. Fill in the company information in the relevant fields.
4. Enter the dates filed and dissolved.
5. Provide a reason for dissolution.
6. Enter the shareholder's contact information if applicable.
7. Enter the authorized signer's contact information if applicable.
8. Sign and date the form where indicated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Information** (`Shareholder Dissolution Consent Form`, text, required): This section provides a brief description of the form.
* **Shareholder Information** (`Shareholder Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the shareholder whose interest is being dissolved.
* **Company Information** (`Company Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company being dissolved.
* **Docket Number** (`Docket Number`, text, required): Enter the docket number associated with the dissolution process.
* **Date Filed** (`Date Filed`, date, required): Enter the date the dissolution was filed.
* **Date Dissolved** (`Date Dissolved`, date, required): Enter the date the shareholder's interest was dissolved.
* **Reason for Dissolution** (`Reason for Dissolution`, text, required): Enter the reason for the dissolution of the shareholder's interest.
* **Shareholder Approvals** (`Shareholder Approvals`, select_multiple, required): Select the approvals and consents from the shareholder for the dissolution.
* **Consent** (`Consent`, select_one, required): Select the shareholder's consent for the dissolution.
* **Authorized Signer** (`Authorized Signer`, text, required): Enter the name of the authorized signer.
* **Authorized Signer Title** (`Authorized Signer Title`, text, required): Select the title of the authorized signer.
* **Authorized Signer Phone** (`Authorized Signer Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the authorized signer.
* **Authorized Signer Email** (`Authorized Signer Email`, text, optional): Enter the email of the authorized signer.
* **Authorized Signer Date** (`Authorized Signer Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the authorized signer's signature was obtained.
* **Authorized Signer Sign** (`Authorized Signer Sign`, text, required): Enter the name of the authorized signer's signature.
* **Authorized Signer Signature** (`Authorized Signer Signature`, text, optional): Enter the authorized signer's signature.

Note: The fields marked as `required` are mandatory and must be filled in to complete the form. The fields marked as `optional` can be left blank.
