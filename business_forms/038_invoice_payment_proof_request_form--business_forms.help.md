# Invoice Payment Proof Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Payment Proof Request Form is a tool used by employees to request a payment proof from a customer. It helps to gather the necessary information for processing the proof request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Enter the **Invoice Payment Proof Request Form** title in the corresponding field.
2. Select the **payment_request** field and enter a description of the payment request.
3. Fill out the **date_of_payment** field with the date of the payment.
4. Enter the **amount_paid** field with the amount paid.
5. For the **method_of_payment**, select the payment method used.
6. Enter the **client_name** field with the customer's name.
7. Select the proof type from the options provided in the **proof_type** field. Options include:
	* Payment Confirmation
	* Voided Payment
	* Partial Payment
8. Upload any proof related to the payment in the **uploaded_proof** field.
9. Enter the customer's email address in the **email** field.
10. Enter the customer's phone number in the **phone** field.
11. Add any notes related to the proof in the **proof** field.
12. Add any additional notes related to the proof in the **notes** field.
13. Enter the employee's name in the **submitted_by** field.
14. Select a date for when the proof was submitted in the **submitted_at** field.
15. Enter the employee's name in the **submitted_by_name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **payment_request**: This field is where you enter a description of the payment request. 
* **date_of_payment**: This field is used to enter the date of the payment. Please use the date format as required.
* **amount_paid**: This field is used to enter the amount paid.
* **method_of_payment**: This field is used to select the payment method used.
* **client_name**: This field is used to enter the customer's name.
* **proof_type**: This field is used to select the type of proof required. Please select one of the options provided.
* **uploaded_proof**: This field is where you can upload any proof related to the payment.
* **email**: This field is used to enter the customer's email address.
* **phone**: This field is used to enter the customer's phone number.
* **proof**: This field is used for any additional notes related to the proof.
* **notes**: This field is used for any additional notes about the proof.
* **submitted_by**: This field is used to enter your name.
* **submitted_at**: This field is used to select the date when the proof was submitted.
* **submitted_by_name**: This field is used to enter your name.
