# Bookstore Partnership Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for bookstores and their partners to outline the terms of their partnership. It is a necessary document for establishing a partnership agreement, ensuring both parties understand their roles, responsibilities, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Be accurate and honest in your input, as this document will serve as a binding contract.
3. Save the form before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bookstore Name** (`bookstore_name`, text, required):
  Your bookstore's name should be filled in to identify the bookstore partner.
* **Contact Person** (`bookstore_contact`, text, required):
  Enter the name of the contact person handling the partnership.
* **Bookstore Email** (`bookstore_email`, email, required):
  Type in the email address of the bookstore contact person.
* **Bookstore Phone** (`bookstore_phone`, text, required):
  Enter the phone number of the bookstore's contact person.
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required):
  Fill in the name of the individual or organization partnering with the bookstore.
* **Partner Type** (`partner_type`, select_one, required):
  Select the type of partnership (Author, Publisher, Distribution partner, etc.).
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, required):
  Enter the partner's email address.
* **Partnership Focus** (`partnership_focus`, text, required):
  Describe the main purpose of the partnership.
* **Partnership Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required):
  Specify when the partnership should begin.
* **Partnership Duration** (`duration`, select_one, required):
  Choose the length of the partnership (6 months, 1 year, 2 years, etc.).
* **Commission or Revenue Share** (`commission_rate`, text, optional):
  If applicable, enter the commission or revenue share terms (percentage or other terms).
* **Bookstore Responsibilities** (`responsibilities_bookstore`, text, required):
  Describe what the bookstore will do under the partnership.
* **Partner Responsibilities** (`responsibilities_partner`, text, required):
  Describe what the partner will do under the partnership.
* **Agreement Acknowledgement** (`agreement_acknowledgement`, select_one, required):
  Accept the partnership terms by selecting "I accept" or discuss further if needed.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required):
  Enter the date you agree to the partnership terms.
