# Product Impact Review Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Product Impact Review Application Form is a tool used to collect information and feedback about a product's impact on the organization that submitted it. This form is used by the product development team to evaluate the product's features, performance, and overall impact on the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill in the required information for each page.
2. For fields marked as "required", make sure to fill them in before proceeding to the next page.
3. For fields marked as "optional", you can choose to fill them in or skip them if not relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product name** (`product_impact_review_application_form_product_name`): This is the name of the product being reviewed. Please enter the name of the product in the space provided.
* **Product description** (`product_impact_review_application_form_product_description`): This is a brief description of the product. Provide a short summary of the product's features and benefits.
* **Product url** (`product_impact_review_application_form_product_url`): If the product has a website or online presence, please provide the URL here.
* **Organisation name** (`product_impact_review_application_form_organisation_name`): This is the name of the organization that submitted the product. Please enter the name of the organization.
* **Organisation email** (`product_impact_review_application_form_organisation_email`): This is the email address of the organization's representative. Please enter the email address.
* **Organisation url** (`product_impact_review_application_form_organisation_url`): If the organization has a website or online presence, please provide the URL here.
* **Impact** (`product_impact_review_application_form_impact`): This field is used to rate the product's impact on the organization. Please rate the product's impact on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating** (`product_impact_review_application_form_rating`): This field is used to rate the product's features and performance. Please rate the product's features and performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Submitter** (`product_impact_review_application_form_submitter`): This is the name of the person submitting the product review. Please enter their name.
* **Submission date** (`product_impact_review_application_form_submission_date`): This field is used to record the date the product was submitted. This will automatically be populated by the system.
* **Submitted at** (`product_impact_review_application_form_submitted_at`): This field is used to record the time the product was submitted. This will automatically be populated by the system.
* **Product type** (`product_impact_review_application_form_product_type`): Select the type of product being reviewed from the options provided.
* **Product category** (`product_impact_review_application_form_product_category`): Select the category of product being reviewed from the options provided.
* **Product status** (`product_impact_review_application_form_product_status`): Select the status of the product being reviewed from the options provided.
* **Review comments** (`product_impact_review_application_form_review_comments`): This is a field for any additional comments or feedback about the product. Please use this space to provide any additional information or thoughts.
* **Email** (`product_impact_review_application_form_email`): This is the email address of the reviewer. Please enter their email address.
* **Phone** (`product_impact_review_application_form_phone`): This is the phone number of the reviewer. Please enter their phone number.
* **Address** (`product_impact_review_application_form_address`): This is the address of the reviewer. Please enter their address.
* **Country** (`product_impact_review_application_form_country`): This is the country of the reviewer. Please select the country from the options provided.
* **State** (`product_impact_review_application_form_state`): This is the state or province of the reviewer. Please select the state or province from the options provided.
* **City** (`product_impact_review_application_form_city`): This is the city of the reviewer. Please enter their city.
