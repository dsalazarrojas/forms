# Submission Count Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and track various types of submissions from customers or clients. This may include feedback, inquiries, or other types of submissions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Select the submission date and time.
2.  Choose the type of submission (submission, inquiry, or feedback).
3.  Enter your name and email address.
4.  Specify the department related to your submission.
5.  Describe the purpose of your submission.
6.  Add any additional comments you may have (optional).

* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): This field is for selecting the date of the submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required): This field is for selecting the time of the submission.
* **Submission Type** (`submission_type`, `select_one`, required): This field is for specifying the type of submission (submission, inquiry, or feedback).
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): This field is for entering your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): This field is for entering your email address.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): This field is for specifying the department related to your submission.
* **Purpose of Submission** (`purpose_of_submission`, `text`, required): This field is for describing the purpose of your submission.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional): This field is for providing additional comments or information related to your submission (optional).
