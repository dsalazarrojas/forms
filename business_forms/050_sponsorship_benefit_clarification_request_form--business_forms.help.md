# Sponsorship Benefit Clarification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsorship Benefit Clarification Request Form is used to request clarification on the benefits received from a sponsorship. It helps ensure that the sponsor's expectations are met and that the benefits are accurately documented.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Be specific about the benefits you are requesting clarification on.
3. Ensure that you are submitting the form for the correct sponsorship.
4. If you are unsure about any part of the form, do not hesitate to reach out to the project team or the sponsor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the Sponsorship Benefit** (`sponsorship_benefit_clarification_request`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is where you describe the specific benefit you are requesting clarification on. Be as detailed as possible, so the project team can better understand your request.
* **Sponsorship Benefit Amount** (`sponsorship_benefit_amount`, number, required: false):
	+ This field is where you enter a numerical value for the benefit. For example, if you are requesting clarification on a monetary benefit, enter the exact amount.
* **Sponsorship Benefit Frequency** (`sponsorship_benefit_frequency`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is where you describe how often the benefit was received. For example, if the benefit was received monthly, enter "monthly".
* **Sponsorship Benefit Type** (`sponsorship_benefit_type`, select multiple, required: false):
	+ This field allows you to select one or more benefit types from the options provided. Choose the benefits that best apply to your request.
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is where you enter the name of the sponsor who provided the sponsorship.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false):
	+ This field is where you enter your email address so the project team can contact you for further clarification.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is where you enter your phone number so the project team can contact you for further clarification.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is where you can provide any additional information that may be helpful for the project team to understand your request.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is where you enter your name so that we can trace the origin of the request.
 
## Tips

* Be clear and concise in your description of the benefit you are requesting clarification on.
* Ensure that you are submitting the correct sponsorship for the benefit you are requesting clarification on.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, do not hesitate to reach out to the project team or the sponsor.
