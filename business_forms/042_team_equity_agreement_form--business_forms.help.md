# Team Equity Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team Equity Agreement Form is used to record and document agreements between team members regarding equity distribution. This form ensures clarity and transparency in the allocation of ownership and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting your role within the team from the provided options (Founder, Employee, Advisor, or None).
2. Enter the percentage of equity you are entitled to in the Equity Percentage field. Please note that this field requires a numerical value.
3. Choose the date when the agreement becomes effective. This will be the date when the agreement takes effect.
4. If applicable, enter the date you signed the agreement.
5. You may be asked to provide a signature to confirm your agreement. This is a text area for your signature.
6. If necessary, have your manager or team lead sign and date in their respective signature fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Member Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role within the team (Founder, Employee, Advisor, or None).
* **Equity Percentage** (`equity_percentage`, `number`, required): Enter the percentage of equity you are entitled to. Please ensure this is a numerical value between 0 and 100.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date when the agreement becomes effective.
* **Agreement Signed Date** (`agreement_signed`, `date`, optional): If you have signed the agreement, enter the date.
* **Team Member Signature** (`signature`, `note`, optional): Type your signature to confirm your agreement.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, `note`, optional): If required, your manager's signature to confirm their approval.
* **Team Lead Signature** (`team_lead_signature`, `note`, optional): If required, your team lead's signature to confirm their approval.

## Tips
* Be accurate when selecting your role and equity percentage to ensure correct ownership distribution.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If any field is not applicable to you, do not enter a value or select an option. Simply move on to the next field.
* Remember to save the completed form for future reference.
