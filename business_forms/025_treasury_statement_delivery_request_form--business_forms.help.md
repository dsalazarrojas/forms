# treasury_statement_delivery_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The treasury statement delivery request form is used to request a treasury statement delivery for a specific account. This form is used by treasury department staff to gather necessary information for the delivery process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by selecting the **delivery_method** field and choose how you would like to receive your statement (bank statement, email, or phone).
2.  Select the **delivery_period** field and choose how frequently you would like to receive your statement (daily, weekly, biweekly, or monthly).
3.  Fill in the **account_number** field with the account number for which you are requesting a statement delivery.
4.  Enter your **contact_email**, **contact_phone**, and **contact_name** for communication purposes.
5.  Provide the **contact_department**, **contact_title**, and **request_id** fields with the relevant information.
6.  Select the **account_statement** field and choose how you would like to receive your statement (statement period, statement frequency, or statement status).
7.  Select the **delivery_status** field and choose the status of your delivery request (pending, in progress, or completed).
8.  You can add any additional notes or comments in the **notes** field.
9.  Confirm your information in the **confirm** field.
10. Confirm your email and phone number in the **confirm_email** and **confirm_phone** fields.
11. Enter the **treasury_department**, **treasury_team**, and **finance_department** fields with the relevant information.
12. Enter the **request_id** field with the request ID.
13. Enter the **form_version** field with the form version.
14. Click the **submit** button to complete the form.
15. Enter the **submit_date** and **submit_time** fields with the date and time of submission.
16. Enter the **treasury_statement_delivery** field with the treasury statement delivery information.
17. Enter the **notes_treasury** field with any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **delivery_request_form** (Page 1): This is the first page of the form.
* **delivery_method**: Select how you would like to receive your statement (bank statement, email, or phone).
*   • **delivery_period**: Select how frequently you would like to receive your statement (daily, weekly, biweekly, or monthly).
*   • **account_number**: Enter the account number for which you are requesting a statement delivery.
*   • **contact_email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   • **contact_phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
*   • **contact_name**: Enter your name for communication purposes.
*   • **contact_department**: Enter the department for which you are requesting a statement delivery.
*   • **contact_title**: Enter your title or position for communication purposes.
*   • **request_id**: Enter the request ID for your statement delivery.
*   • **form_version**: Enter the form version for your statement delivery.
*   • **submit**: Click this button to complete the form.
*   • **submit_date**: Enter the date of submission.
*   • **submit_time**: Enter the time of submission.
*   • **treasury_statement_delivery**: Enter the treasury statement delivery information.
*   • **notes_treasury**: Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully and accurately.
*   Review your form carefully before submission to ensure all fields are complete and correct.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact your treasury department staff for assistance.
