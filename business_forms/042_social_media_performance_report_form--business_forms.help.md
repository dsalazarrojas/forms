# social_media_performance_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social media performance report form is used to collect data on social media platforms and allow users to provide additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the social media platforms you wish to report on.
2. Choose which metrics you want to report on (e.g., engagement, followers, reach, etc.).
3. Enter the values for each selected metric.
4. Provide any additional comments you have about your social media performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Social Media Platforms**: 
    * Select one or more social media platforms you use (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, TikTok).
* **Which platform**: 
    * Choose the platform you selected in the previous field.
* **Engagement**: 
    * Enter the engagement metric (e.g., number of likes, comments, shares).
* **Followers**: 
    * Enter the number of followers on the selected platform.
* **Reach**: 
    * Enter the reach metric (e.g., number of people who viewed your content).
* **Impressions**: 
    * Enter the number of impressions (e.g., number of times your content was viewed).
* **Clicks**: 
    * Enter the number of clicks on your content.
* **Shares**: 
    * Enter the number of shares on the selected platform.
* **Comments**: 
    * Enter the number of comments on the selected platform.
* **Likes**: 
    * Enter the number of likes on the selected platform.
* **Saves**: 
    * Enter the number of saves on the selected platform.
* **Interactions**: 
    * Enter the total number of interactions on the selected platform.
* **Additional Comments**: 
    * Provide any additional comments about your social media performance.
* **Submit**: 
    * Click to submit your report.

## Tips
- Make sure to select relevant social media platforms and metrics.
- Enter accurate values for each metric.
- Provide clear and concise additional comments if needed.
