# Vendors Participation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from vendors who want to participate in an event or collaboration. The form is divided into multiple pages, each with various text fields for the vendors to fill in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out all the required fields on each page. The fields marked as "required" should not be left blank.
2.  Be clear and concise in your answers to provide accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page** (`main_page`, text, required): The main page is the starting point of the form. Fill out your name, title, and company name in this field.
* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_info`, text, optional): This field is for additional information about the vendor, such as address and contact numbers.
* **Vendors Products** (`vendor_products`, text, optional): List the products or services that your company provides.
* **Vendors Services** (`vendor_services`, text, optional): List the services offered by your company.
* **Event Information** (`event_info`, text, optional): If the event is related to your company, provide the event details.
* **Vendors Payment Information** (`vendor_payment_info`, text, optional): Enter your payment information for the collaboration or event.
* **Vendors License Information** (`vendor_license_info`, text, optional): Provide any licensing information related to your company.
* **Main Page 2** (`main_page_2`, text, optional): This page is for any additional comments or information.
* **Vendors Agreement** (`vendor_agreement`, text, optional): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the collaboration or event.
* **Vendors Signature** (`vendor_signature`, text, optional): Sign the agreement to confirm your acceptance.
* **Vendors Note** (`vendor_note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your company.
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, text, optional): Add any notes or comments about the event.
* **Vendors Payment Information 2** (`vendor_payment_info_2`, text, optional): Enter additional payment information.
* **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`, text, optional): Check the status of your agreement.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, text, optional): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Time** (`agreement_time`, text, optional): Enter the time of the agreement.
* **Agreement Note** (`agreement_note`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the agreement.
* **Vendors Note 2** (`vendor_note_2`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from the vendor.
* **Vendors Note 3** (`vendor_note_3`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from the vendor.
* **Vendors Note 4** (`vendor_note_4`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes from the vendor.
* **Vendors Agreement 2** (`vendor_agreement_2`, text, optional): Review and agree to the revised terms and conditions.
* **Vendors Signature 2** (`vendor_signature_2`, text, optional): Sign the revised agreement to confirm your acceptance.
* **Vendors License Info 2** (`vendor_license_info_2`, text, optional): Provide any additional licensing information.
* **Vendors License Info 3** (`vendor_license_info_3`, text, optional): Provide any additional licensing information.
* **Agreement Status 2** (`agreement_status_2`, text, optional): Check the status of your revised agreement.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your answers for any errors or inconsistencies.
* Be clear and concise in your answers to avoid any misunderstandings.
