# Web Development Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Web Development Inquiry form is used to gather information from users about their web development needs. This form helps our development team to better understand your requirements and provide more accurate and tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **What kind of web development services do you need?**: Please select the type of web development service you are looking for from the options provided. You can choose one or more services that apply to your project.
2. **What specific web development services do you need?**: Provide more details about the specific services you need, such as Frontend, Backend, Database, Security, or a combination of these.
3. **What is your email address?**: Enter your email address so our development team can contact you about your project.
4. **What is your phone number?**: Enter your phone number so our team can contact you for any further discussions or questions.
5. **Any comments?**: Provide any additional information or comments about your project.
6. **Allow our development team to contact you?**: Select whether you would like our team to contact you by email or phone.
7. **When do you need the project completed by?**: Choose a date for when you need the project completed by.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What kind of web development services do you need? (web_development_inquiry)**: This field is used to ask for the type of web development service you are looking for. Please select the options that apply to your project.
* **What specific web development services do you need? (web_development_inquiry_services)**: This field is used to provide more details about the specific services you need. You can select multiple services from the provided options.
* **What is your email address? (web_development_inquiry_email)**: This field is used to collect your email address so our team can contact you about your project. Please enter a valid email address.
* **What is your phone number? (web_development_inquiry_phone)**: This field is used to collect your phone number so our team can contact you for any further discussions or questions. Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Any comments? (web_development_inquiry_comments)**: This field is used to provide any additional information or comments about your project.
* **Allow our development team to contact you? (web_development_inquiry_allow_contact)**: This field is used to determine whether you would like our team to contact you by email or phone.
* **When do you need the project completed by? (web_development_inquiry_date)**: This field is used to choose a date for when you need the project completed by. Please select a valid date.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have provided all the necessary information to allow our team to better understand your web development needs.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Our development team will review your submission and respond to your inquiry as soon as possible.
