# Smart Process Automation Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Smart Process Automation Pilot Application Form" is used to collect information from potential participants in the pilot program. Please fill out the form accurately to help the program team assess your eligibility and tailor the experience to your needs.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are interested in participating in the pilot program.
2. Fill in your name, email address, and other demographic details as requested.
3. Provide your contact information, phone number, and any additional details that may be useful for communication.
4. Enter your date of birth for identification and age verification purposes.
5. Enter your address, which will be used to send any necessary information or invitations.
6. Select your company name and position to help the program team understand your context and requirements.
7. Enter your department and job title for further context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form (Select "Yes" or "No")**: 
    - This field is used to indicate your interest in participating in the pilot program.
* **Name**: 
    - A required field to collect your full name for identification purposes.
* **Email**:
    - A required field to collect your valid email address for communication.
* **Contact Information**: 
    - Please provide a way for the program team to contact you, e.g., phone number or office hours.
* **Phone**: 
    - Enter a phone number where you can be reached for further questions or discussions.
* **Date of Birth**:
    - A required field for age verification and identification purposes.
* **Address**:
    - Enter a valid address where you can receive any necessary information or invitations.
* **Company**:
    - A field to collect your company's name to provide context and facilitate communication.
* **Position**:
    - Enter your job title to help the program team understand your role and requirements.
* **Department**:
    - Provide the name of your department to further contextualize your application.
* **Job Title**:
    - Enter your job title to provide additional context and information about your role.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth experience.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the program team for assistance.
- You can always reach out to us if you need help or need clarification on any part of the form.
