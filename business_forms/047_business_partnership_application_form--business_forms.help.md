# Business Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from businesses interested in forming partnerships with our organization. The data collected will be used to evaluate potential collaborations and assess the suitability of each applicant.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Provide your company's name and address.
3. Select your industry and years in business.
4. Choose the type of partnership you are seeking and your desired annual revenue.
5. Specify the objectives you aim to achieve through the partnership.
6. Describe the unique value proposition of your business.
7. Select the markets you serve.
8. Provide relevant experience and resources you can contribute to the partnership.
9. Indicate your availability to start the partnership and your decision-making authority.
10. Briefly describe your proposal and provide professional references (optional).
11. Add any additional information that might be relevant to the partnership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Please provide your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's legal name.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter your company's full address.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Choose the primary industry of your business.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, required): Enter the number of years your business has been operating.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, required): Enter the total number of employees in your company.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, required): Choose the approximate gross revenue of your business.
* **Partnership Type Desired** (`partnership_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of partnership you are seeking.
* **Partnership Objectives** (`partnership_objectives`, select_multiple, required): Choose the objectives you aim to achieve through the partnership.
* **Your Value Proposition** (`value_proposition`, text, required): Describe the unique value your business offers.
* **Target Markets** (`target_markets`, select_multiple, required): Select the markets your business serves.
* **Relevant Experience** (`relevant_experience`, text, required): Provide a description of your relevant experience.
* **Previous Partnership Experience** (`previous_partnerships`, select_one, required): Indicate your level of partnership experience.
* **Resources to Contribute** (`resources_to_contribute`, select_multiple, required): Choose the resources you can contribute to the partnership.
* **Partnership Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Select the desired start date for the partnership.
* **Decision Authority** (`decision_authority`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are the final decision maker for your company.
* **Partnership Proposal Summary** (`proposal_summary`, text, required): Briefly describe your proposal.
* **Professional References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide 2-3 professional references (optional).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Add any other relevant details about your business.
