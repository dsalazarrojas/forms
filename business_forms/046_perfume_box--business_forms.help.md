The purpose of this form is to collect information from users about their perfumery preferences. The form aims to understand their lifestyle, subscription type, payment method, perfume selection, confidence, perfect fit, and moment-to-moment experiences.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

## Purpose
To help us better understand your perfumery preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the page titled "Life situations".
2. Choose one or more of the following options:
	* Monthly
	* One time
3. Select one of the following:
	* Subscription
	* Payment
4. Select one or more of the following:
	* Yes
	* No
5. Enter the following information:
	* Confidence: (a brief description of your confidence level)
6. Enter the following information:
	* Perfect fit: (a brief description of your perfect fit)
7. Enter the following information:
	* Moment to moment: (a brief description of your moment-to-moment experiences)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Life situations** (`life_situations`, text, required/optional): Describe your life situations, such as daily routines, social events, or travel.
- **Subscription type** (`subscription_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you subscribe monthly or one time.
- **Payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select the payment method that best suits your needs, whether subscription or payment.
- **Perfume selection** (`perfume_selection`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether you have a perfume selection, yes or no.
- **Confidence** (`confidence`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your confidence level, such as "High", "Medium", or "Low".
- **Perfect fit** (`perfect_fit`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your perfect fit, such as "Relaxed" or "Formal".
- **Moment to moment** (`moment_to_moment`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your moment-to-moment experiences, such as "Calm" or "Energetic".
