# Color Street Sample - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your contact information, nail preferences, and other details to provide you with personalized sample nail polish options from Color Street.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Your Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Your Email" field.
3. Provide your mailing address in the "Your Mailing Address" field.
4. Select your favorite colors or styles in the "What are your favorite colors or styles?" field. (e.g., Pinks, Blues, Glitters, Solids)
5. Indicate if you have tried Color Street before in the "Have you ever tried Color Street before?" field.
6. Choose your current nail routine from the options in the "What is your current nail routine?" field.
7. Select if you would like to join Color Street's VIP mailing list for deals in the "Would you like to join my VIP mailing list for deals?" field.
8. If applicable, mention any special requests for your sample in the "Any special requests for your sample?" field.
9. Lastly, provide any additional information about how you heard about Color Street in the "How did you hear about me?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Full Name** (sample_requester_name): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
- **Your Email** (sample_requester_email): Enter your email address where you would like to receive information and samples from Color Street.
- **Your Mailing Address** (sample_requester_address): Provide your mailing address where you would like to receive samples and other mailings from Color Street.
- **What are your favorite colors or styles?** (favorite_colors): List your favorite colors or styles, for example, Pinks, Blues, Glitters, Solids.
- **Have you ever tried Color Street before?** (have_tried_before): Select 'True' if you have previously tried Color Street, 'False' otherwise.
- **What is your current nail routine?** (current_nail_routine): Choose your current nail routine from the options: Salon Manicures, DIY Polish at Home, Gel/Acrylics, Nothing/Natural.
- **Would you like to join my VIP mailing list for deals?** (join_mailing_list): Select 'True' to join Color Street's VIP mailing list for exclusive deals, 'False' otherwise.
- **Any special requests for your sample?** (sample_notes): Provide any additional information about your sample preferences or requests.
- **How did you hear about me?** (referral_source): Mention any information about how you heard about Color Street, such as social media, friend referral, etc.
- **-- Request Your Color Street Sample --** (section_sample_intro, section_preferences): Introduction sections, no action required from the user. 
- **-- Stay Connected --** (section_followup): Note section, no action required from the user. 

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled in order to receive a complete sample.
* If you experience any issues with the form, contact Color Street customer support.
* Color Street will use the information provided to tailor a sample selection to your preferences.
