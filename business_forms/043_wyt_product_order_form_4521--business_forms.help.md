# WYT Product Order Form 4521 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The WYT Product Order Form 4521 is a critical form used to gather essential information about a product order. It serves as a centralized platform for capturing customer data, product details, and order-specific information, enabling the business to efficiently process orders and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and effectively, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Product Name` field with the name of the product being ordered.
2. Enter the `Price` of the product being ordered.
3. Select the `Order Date` for the product order.
4. Provide your `Customer Name` for identification purposes.
5. Enter your `Customer Email` address to ensure efficient communication.
6. Enter your `Customer Phone` number for contact purposes.
7. If applicable, enter your `Customer Company` information.
8. Add any additional `Order Note` that may be relevant to the order.
9. Enter the `Form ID` for reference and tracking purposes.
10. If necessary, specify the `Assigned Tool` being used for this order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Price** (2, number, required): Enter the price of the product being ordered.
* **Order Date** (3, date, required): Select the date for the product order.
* **Customer Name** (4, text, required): Provide your name for identification purposes.
* **Customer Email** (5, email, required): Enter your email address for efficient communication.
* **Customer Phone** (6, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Customer Company** (7, text, optional): If applicable, provide your company information.
* **Order Note** (8, note, optional): Add any additional note relevant to the order.
* **Form ID** (9, text, optional): Enter the form ID for reference and tracking purposes.
* **Assigned Tool** (10, text, optional): If necessary, specify the assigned tool being used for this order.
