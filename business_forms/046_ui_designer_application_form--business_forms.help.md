# ui_designer_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect application information from applicants for various positions within the organization. It is intended for internal use and will be reviewed by relevant departments to assess the applicant's qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "application_form" field with a brief description of your application.
2. Provide your "applicant_information" in the designated field.
3. Describe your "portfolio" in the "portfolio" field.
4. List your relevant "experience" in the "experience" field.
5. Indicate whether you are able to "upload_files" by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
6. If you are able to "upload_file", please provide the file in the "upload_file" field.
7. If you are able to "upload_portfolio", please provide the portfolio in the "upload_portfolio" field.
8. If you are able to "upload_resume", please provide the resume in the "upload_resume" field.
9. If you are able to "upload_cover_letter", please provide the cover letter in the "upload_cover_letter" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **application_form (Text)**: A brief description of your application.
	+ Please provide a concise and clear statement of your interest in the position.
* **applicant_information (Text)**: Your personal information.
	+ Please provide your full name, email address, and phone number.
* **portfolio (Note)**: A brief description of your portfolio.
	+ Please describe any relevant projects or work that you have completed.
* **experience (Text)**: A list of your relevant experience.
	+ Please list any relevant job experience, training, or education.
* **upload_files (Select Multiple)**: Indicate if you are able to upload files.
	+ Please select "Yes" if you have files to upload, "No" otherwise.
* **upload_file (Text)**: A field for uploading files if selected in "upload_files".
	+ Please provide any relevant files you need to upload.
* **upload_portfolio (Text)**: A field for uploading portfolio if selected in "upload_files".
	+ Please provide your portfolio.
* **upload_resume (Text)**: A field for uploading resume if selected in "upload_files".
	+ Please provide your resume.
* **upload_cover_letter (Text)**: A field for uploading cover letter if selected in "upload_files".
	+ Please provide your cover letter.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
* Please do not upload any confidential or sensitive information.
* Please do not upload any files that are not relevant to your application.
