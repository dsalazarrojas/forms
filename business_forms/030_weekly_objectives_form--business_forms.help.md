# weekly_objectives_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The weekly_objectives_form is a tool used to track and manage employee weekly objectives. It helps departments within a company to monitor progress and set priorities for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the objective or goal of the task in the "Objective" field.
3. Select the status of the task in the "Status" dropdown menu:
	* In Progress
	* On Hold
	* Completed
4. You may add any additional notes about the task in the "Notes" field.
5. Enter the date the objective was assigned to the employee in the "Assigned Date" field.
6. Enter the date the objective is due to be completed in the "Due Date" field.
7. Enter the name of the supervisor responsible for the objective in the "Supervisor Name" field.
8. Set the priority level of the objective by selecting from the "Priority" dropdown menu:
	* High
	* Medium
	* Low

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee responsible for the objective.
* **Objective** (`objective`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the objective or goal.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the objective:
	+ In Progress: The objective is currently being worked on.
	+ On Hold: The objective has been paused or delayed.
	+ Completed: The objective has been finished.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the objective.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the objective was assigned to the employee.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the objective is due to be completed.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supervisor responsible for the objective.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Set the priority level of the objective:
	+ High: High priority objective.
	+ Medium: Medium priority objective.
	+ Low: Low priority objective.
