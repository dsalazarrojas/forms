# Smart Gate Access Upgrade Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Gate Access Upgrade Consent Form is used to request permission to access more advanced features or increased permissions within the system. To complete this form, follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your property's name in the "Property Name" field.
4. Enter the gate number for the property in the "Gate Number" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, which is required for notifications.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
8. Enter your manager's email address in the "Manager Email" field.
9. Enter the approval date for the upgrade in the "Approval Date" field.
10. Enter the approval time for the upgrade in the "Approval Time" field.
11. Describe the reason for the upgrade in the "Upgrade Reason" field.
12. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Property Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your property's name.
* **Gate Number** (Number, Optional): Enter the gate number for the property.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address for notifications. Please be aware that this is not optional, as it is required for notifications.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Manager Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Approval Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the approval date for the upgrade.
* **Approval Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the approval time for the upgrade.
* **Upgrade Reason** (Text, Optional): Describe the reason for the upgrade.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes.

## Tips
* Please review your access needs before submitting this form to avoid requesting unnecessary upgrades.
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your manager or system administrator for clarification.
