# workplace_housekeeping_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "workplace_housekeeping_checklist" is a form used to assess the cleanliness and quality of a workplace, promoting a safe and healthy environment for employees and visitors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and answer all required questions.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your observations.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Cleanliness (1)** (`number`, **optional**): This field is used to rate the overall cleanliness of the general workplace area.
	+ Please rate the area on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is very dirty and 10 is very clean.
* **Waste Disposal (2)** (`select_one`, **optional**): This field is used to check which type of waste disposal is available in the area.
	+ Select the type of waste disposal that is present in the area, such as "Trash Bins" or "Recycling Bins".
* **Chemical Storage (3)** (`select_multiple`, **optional**): This field is used to select which chemicals are stored in the area.
	+ Select all chemicals that are stored in the area, such as "Chemical A" or "Chemical B".
* **Bathroom Cleanliness (4)** (`time`, **optional**): This field is used to record when the bathroom was last cleaned.
	+ Enter the date and time when the bathroom was last cleaned.
* **Floor Cleanliness (5)** (`note`, **optional**): This field is used to add any comments about the cleanliness of the floor.
	+ Enter any comments about the floor cleanliness, such as "The floor is very dirty" or "The floor is well-maintained".
* **Water Quality (6)** (`date`, **optional**): This field is used to record when the water quality was last tested.
	+ Enter the date when the water quality was last tested.
* **Air Quality (7)** (`number`, **optional**): This field is used to rate the air quality in the area.
	+ Please rate the air quality on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is very poor and 10 is very good.
