# AI Strategy Development Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help organizations assess and develop their AI strategy. It's a comprehensive survey aimed at understanding the current state of AI adoption and planning for its future integration in the organization. By completing this form, you'll be able to identify areas of improvement, prioritize AI initiatives, and develop a clear strategy for AI-driven innovation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the form carefully**: Before starting, take a few minutes to familiarize yourself with the form's layout and questions.
2. **Answer each question honestly**: Be as accurate as possible when providing information about your organization's current state and goals.
3. **Provide examples or supporting details**: When applicable, provide concrete examples or descriptions to help illustrate your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of the Department/Division** (`department_name`, **text**, Required):
	+ This field is asking for the name of the department or division within your organization that this form applies to.
* **Current level of AI understanding in your leadership team** (`leadership_ai_understanding`, **select_one**, Required):
	+ This question helps us understand the level of AI awareness and initiative-taking within your leadership team. Please select one of the provided options:
		- High (Actively driving AI initiatives)
		- Moderate (Aware of AI, but no clear roadmap)
		- Basic (Interested but limited knowledge)
		- None (AI is not yet on the radar)
* **What are your top 3 business goals for the next 12-24 months?** (`business_goals`, **text**, Required):
	+ This field asks you to list your top 3 business goals for the next 12-24 months. These goals will help us understand your organization's priorities and how AI can support them.
* **Where do you see the most potential for AI impact?** (`potential_impact_areas`, **select_multiple**, Required):
	+ This question invites you to identify the areas where you see the greatest potential for AI-driven impact. Select all that apply:
		+ Operational Efficiency (Process Automation)
		+ Customer Experience (Personalization/Support)
		+ Product/Service Innovation
		+ Risk Management & Compliance
		+ Sales & Marketing Optimization
		+ Supply Chain & Logistics
* **Describe a specific process or task that is currently manual or inefficient.** (`inefficient_process_description`, **text**, Required):
	+ This field is asking you to describe a process or task that could benefit from AI-driven automation or improvement.
* **Do you have a clear data strategy in place?** (`data_strategy_status`, **select_one**, Required):
	+ This question helps us understand your organization's data handling and availability. Please select one of the options:
		+ Yes
		+ No
		+ Under Development
* **Internal AI Expertise Available** (`internal_ai_expertise`, **select_one**, Required):
	+ This field assesses the availability of internal AI expertise within your organization. Select one of the provided options:
		- Dedicated AI/Data Science Team
		- A few experts scattered in IT/Data
		- Relying primarily on external partners
		- No internal expertise currently
* **Available Technical Infrastructure** (`available_infrastructure`, **select_multiple**, Required):
	+ This question asks you to identify the type of technical infrastructure your organization has available for AI initiatives. Select all that apply:
		+ On-premise Servers/GPU Clusters
		+ Public Cloud (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud)
		+ SaaS-based AI Platforms
		+ Hybrid Environment
* **Budget availability for AI initiatives** (`ai_budget_status`, **select_one**, Required):
	+ This field helps us understand your organization's financial readiness for AI projects. Please select one of the options:
		- Budget is secured for the next fiscal year
		- Budget is in the planning/approval stage
		- No budget currently allocated
* **Key AI risks of primary concern** (`key_ai_risks`, **select_multiple**, Required):
	+ This question invites you to identify the key AI risks that you're concerned about. Select all that apply:
		+ Data Privacy & Security
		+ Algorithmic Bias & Fairness
		+ Regulatory Compliance (e.g., EU AI Act)
		+ Job Displacement/Change Management
		+ Intellectual Property & Copyright
		+ System Reliability & Hallucinations
* **What does success look like for an AI project in your organization?** (`success_criteria_kpis`, **text**, Required):
	+ This field asks you to define the key performance indicators (KPIs) that will measure the success of an AI project in your organization.
* **Target date for AI Strategy completion** (`target_completion_date`, **date**, Optional):
	+ This field is asking for the target date to complete your AI strategy. You can provide a specific date or leave it open if it's not yet defined.
* **Additional comments or strategic priorities** (`additional_comments`, **text**, Optional):
	+ This field provides an opportunity to add any additional information or comments that might be relevant to your organization's AI strategy.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough when answering each question.
- If you're unsure about a particular question, don't hesitate to add additional comments or explanations.
- This form is designed to be a starting point for your organization's AI strategy development. Feel free to modify it to suit your needs.
