# Vendor Payment Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to monitor and track vendor payments for your business. Please fill out the form to record payments made to vendors, including the date of the payment, the status of the payment, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vendor's name in the "vendor_name" field.
2. Enter the date of the payment in the "payment_date" field.
3. Select the status of the payment from the "payment_status" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the amount of the payment in the "payment_amount" field.
5. Select the method of the payment (e.g., bank transfer, cash, check, or wire transfer) from the "payment_method" dropdown menu.
6. Enter any relevant notes or comments about the payment in the "note" field.
7. Enter the business contact email address in the "email" field (if applicable).
8. Enter the business contact phone number in the "phone" field (if applicable).
9. Enter the business name in the "business_name" field (if applicable).
10. Enter the vendor's email address in the "vendor_email" field (if applicable).
11. Enter the vendor's phone number in the "vendor_phone" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (vendor_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the vendor or business partner.
* **Payment Date** (payment_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the payment in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Payment Status** (payment_status, select_one, optional): Select the status of the payment (e.g., Pending, Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid).
* **Payment Amount** (payment_amount, number, optional): Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_multiple, optional): Select the method of the payment (e.g., Bank Transfer, Cash, Check, Wire Transfer).
* **Note** (note, note, optional): Enter any relevant notes or comments about the payment.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the business contact (if applicable).
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the business contact (if applicable).
* **Business Name** (business_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the business.
* **Vendor Email** (vendor_email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the vendor.
* **Vendor Phone** (vendor_phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the vendor.
