# Product Demo Technician Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Demo Technician Booking Form is designed to help customers book a demo session for a specific product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Choose a date and time for the demo session in the "Date" field.
* Choose a time slot for the demo session in the "Time" field.
* Specify the subject of the demo session in the "Subject" field.
* Select the product(s) you are interested in from the "Product" field. You can select multiple products if applicable.
* Click the "Submit" button to send your booking request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name to help the demo technician identify you.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name to help the demo technician identify you.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive communication about your demo session.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number to enable communication with the demo technician.
* **Date** (date, optional): Choose a date for the demo session. If no date is selected, the default date will be used.
* **Time** (time, optional): Choose a time slot for the demo session. If no time is selected, the default time will be used.
* **Subject** (text, optional): Briefly describe the subject of the demo session to help the technician prepare.
* **Product** (select_multiple, optional): Select the product(s) you are interested in to help the technician prepare specific materials.
* **Submit** (text, required): Click this button to send your booking request to the demo team.
