# Acquisition Letter Of Intent Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Acquisition Letter Of Intent Submission form is used to document and record the details of potential business acquisitions, including the proposed purchase price, acquisition type, and other relevant information. This form is an essential tool for buyers and sellers to formalize their intentions and create a binding agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Gather the required information
Before filling out the form, ensure you have all the necessary details ready. This includes the potential buyer's name, target company name, proposed purchase price, acquisition type, financing status, proposed closing date, LOI expiration date, and other relevant information such as key contingencies and exclusivity period requested.

### Step 2: Fill out the form fields
1. **Potential Buyer Name** (<label>): Enter the name of the individual or entity submitting the acquisition letter of intent.
2. **Target Company Name** (<label>): Enter the name of the business being acquired.
3. **Contact Email** (<label>): Enter the contact email for the potential buyer.
4. **Proposed Purchase Price** (<label>): Enter the proposed purchase price in USD.
5. **Acquisition Type** (<label>): Select the type of acquisition (Asset Purchase, Stock Purchase, or Merger).
6. **Financing Status** (<label>): Choose the current state of funding (Fully Funded, Financing Contingent, or Private Equity Backed).
7. **Proposed Closing Date** (<label>): Enter the target date for finalization of the acquisition.
8. **LOI Expiration Date** (<label>): Enter the date after which this offer is void.
9. **Key Contingencies** (<label>): Optionally, list any key contingencies (e.g., due diligence, financing, board approval).
10. **Exclusivity Period Requested** (<label>): Choose the duration of the no-shop provision (30 Days, 60 Days, or 90 Days).
11. **Confidentiality** (<label>): This is a note field for any additional information or notes.
12. **NDA Signed?** (<label>): Check if the non-disclosure agreement has been signed (True, False, or In Progress).
13. **Submitter Title and Role** (<label>): Enter the title and role of the submitter (e.g., CEO of Buyer Entity).
