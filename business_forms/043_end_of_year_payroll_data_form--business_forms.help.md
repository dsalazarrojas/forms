# End Of Year Payroll Data Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The End Of Year Payroll Data Form is designed to collect information about employees' salaries, benefits, and payroll data for the year. This form will help HR or accounting departments track and calculate payroll expenses and taxes for the year.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the employee's name in the first field.
2.  Enter the company name for tax purposes in the second field.
3.  If applicable, enter the year of service in the third field.
4.  Enter the employee's monthly salary in the fourth field.
5.  Enter the insurance premium in the fifth field.
6.  Enter the vision care premium in the sixth field (if applicable).
7.  Enter the dental care premium in the seventh field (if applicable).
8.  Enter the long term care premium in the eighth field (if applicable).
9.  Enter the health care premium in the ninth field (if applicable).
10. Enter the date of the last payroll in the tenth field.
11. Enter the date of this payroll in the eleventh field.
12. Enter the payroll amount in the twelfth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee.
*   **Company Name**: Enter the company name for tax purposes.
*   **Year of Service**: Enter the year the employee has been with the company (optional).
*   **Monthly Salary**: Enter the employee's monthly salary.
*   **Insurance Premium**: Enter the insurance premium paid by the employee.
*   **Vision Care Premium**: Enter the vision care premium paid by the employee (if applicable).
*   **Dental Care Premium**: Enter the dental care premium paid by the employee (if applicable).
*   **Long Term Care Premium**: Enter the long term care premium paid by the employee (if applicable).
*   **Health Care Premium**: Enter the health care premium paid by the employee (if applicable).
*   **Date of Last Payroll**: Enter the date of the last payroll.
*   **Date of This Payroll**: Enter the date of this payroll.
*   **Payroll Amount**: Enter the total payroll amount.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate payroll calculations.
*   Enter accurate dates for payroll and tax purposes.
*   If any premium fields are not applicable, leave them blank.
