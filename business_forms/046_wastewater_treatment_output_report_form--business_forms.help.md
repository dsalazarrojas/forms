# Wastewater Treatment Output Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document performance and quality data from wastewater treatment plants. It's meant for operators or managers to report and monitor key data points.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form on a computer or mobile device.
* Start by selecting the correct page (Plant Performance Data, Treatment Process Data, Water Quality Data, or Contact Details).
* Enter your information in the designated fields for each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Plant Performance Data** (`plant_performance_data`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the plant's performance, such as treatment efficiency rates or operational notes.
* **Treatment Process Data** (`treatment_process_data`, text, optional): Describe the current treatment process, including any modifications or changes made to the process.
* **Water Quality Data** (`water_quality_data`, text, optional): Report water quality measurements, such as pH levels, turbidity, or other relevant metrics.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide contact information for plant personnel, including names, titles, and contact methods.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out each field to ensure accurate reporting.
* Review and edit your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the form's purpose and the field labels for clarification.
