# Gym Signboard Replacement Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order new or replacement gym signboards with various specifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

* Select the type of order you wish to place.
* Choose the type of signboard you are ordering.
* Select the type of signboard.
* Enter the location where the signboard will be placed.
* Enter the contact person's information.
* Select the design and specifications of the signboard.
* Enter the quantity and dimensions of the signboard.
* Select the material, color, and finish of the signboard.
* Select the font type, size, and color of the signboard.
* Enter the date and time of the order.
* Enter the date and time of the delivery (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **new_order**: Select the type of order you wish to place (New, Replacement, or Both).
* **order_type**: Choose the type of signboard you are ordering (New, Existing, or Both).
* **sign_type**: Select the type of signboard (Indoor, Outdoor, or Both).
* **location**: Enter the location where the signboard will be placed.
* **contact_first_name**: Enter the contact person's first name.
* **contact_last_name**: Enter the contact person's last name.
* **contact_email**: Enter the contact person's email address.
* **contact_phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **message**: Enter any additional comments or special instructions for the order.
* **order_date**: Enter the date of the order (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **order_time**: Enter the time of the order (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **delivery_date**: Enter the date of the delivery (MM/DD/YYYY) (optional).
* **delivery_time**: Enter the time of the delivery (HH:MM AM/PM) (optional).
* **delivery_note**: Enter any additional comments or special instructions for the delivery (optional).
* **signboard_design**: Select the design of the signboard (Standard, Custom, or Both).
* **quantity**: Select the quantity of signboards you wish to order.
* **height**: Select the height of the signboard.
* **width**: Select the width of the signboard.
* **material**: Select the material of the signboard (Standard, Premium, or Both).
* **color**: Select the color of the signboard (White or Color).
* **finish**: Select the finish of the signboard (Matte, Glossy, or Textured).
* **font_type**: Select the font type of the signboard (Arial, Times New Roman, or Comic Sans).
* **font_size**: Select the font size of the signboard (1-24).
* **font_color**: Select the font color of the signboard (Black, White, or Red).
* **special_instructions**: Enter any additional comments or special instructions for the delivery (optional).
