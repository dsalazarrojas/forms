# Contact Sustainable Development Officer - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed for end-users to contact the Sustainable Development Officer for sustainability-related inquiries, questions, or proposals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Contact name" field.
2. Provide your organization or affiliation in the "Organization" field (if applicable).
3. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field. This will be used for follow-up communication.
4. If you have a preferred phone number, enter it in the "Phone number" field.
5. Choose the type of inquiry you have by selecting the relevant option from the "Inquiry type" dropdown list.
6. Briefly describe the project or issue related to sustainability in the "Project or issue description" field.
7. Select the desired outcome from the "Desired outcome" dropdown list (if applicable).
8. You can optionally attach supporting documents via email to the address provided.
9. Leave any additional comments or context that might be helpful for the sustainability team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Organization**: Provide your organization or affiliation if it's relevant to your inquiry.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone number**: Enter a phone number if you have one.
* **Inquiry type**: Select the type of inquiry you have (Partnership, Reporting request, Complaint, or General question).
* **Project or issue description**: Briefly describe the project or issue related to sustainability.
* **Desired outcome**: If applicable, select the desired outcome (Information, Collaboration, Investigation, or Other).
* **Attach supporting documents via email**: Optionally, send reports or files to the provided email address.
* **Additional comments**: Leave any other context that might be helpful for the sustainability team.
## Tips

* Be clear and concise in your inquiry or description.
* Ensure your contact information is accurate.
* If you have supporting documents, attach them to the email address provided.
* If you're unsure about the form or the process, contact the sustainability team for guidance.
