# AD Customer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather your feedback and satisfaction with our product or service. Your input helps us understand how we're doing and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by reviewing the form's sections to understand what we're asking you about.
2.  Answer all required fields, marked with an \*.
3.  Provide as much detail as possible in the open-ended fields.
4.  Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your contact email address for follow-up communication.
*   **Phone number** (`contact_phone`, `phone`, optional): Include your country code (e.g., +1 for North America) in the phone number field.
*   **Customer segment** (`customer_segment`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your interaction with our product or service.
*   **Product/Service version** (`product_version`, `text`, optional): Enter the specific version of our product or service you used (e.g., 2.4.1 or "Web app").
*   **Date of interaction** (`interaction_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you last used our product or service.
*   **Approximate time of interaction** (`interaction_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the approximate time of day you last used our product or service.
*   **How often do you use our product/service?** (`usage_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you use our product or service.
*   **Overall satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Select how satisfied you are with our product or service.
*   **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, `integer`, required): Rate our product or service from 1 to 10, where 1 is the worst and 10 is the best.
*   **Best aspects of the product/service** (`best_aspects`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the aspects that you found particularly useful or enjoyable.
*   **Areas to improve** (`areas_to_improve`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select areas where you think we could improve our product or service.
*   **Problems or issues encountered** (`problems_encountered`, `text`, optional): Describe any errors, crashes, or confusing flows you experienced.
*   **Would you recommend us to others?** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Select how likely you are to recommend us to others.
*   **Permission to follow up about your responses** (`follow_up_permission`, `select_one`, required): Grant permission to follow up about your responses, and we'll contact you using your preferred method.
*   **Preferred contact method (if follow-up permitted)** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred contact method if we need to follow up about your responses.
*   **Additional comments or suggestions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions to help us improve.
