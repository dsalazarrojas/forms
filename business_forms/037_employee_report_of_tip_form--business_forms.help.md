# Employee Report Of Tip Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employee information and details about a tip received for reporting and tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee information in the first field.
2. Select the date the tip was received from the calendar or enter it in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Enter the total tip amount in the third field.
4. Choose the type of tip from the options provided in the fourth field.
5. Enter the total amount of tips received in the fifth field.
6. Provide any additional note or comment about the tip in the sixth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Information** (`Employee Information`, `text`, required): Enter your employee ID, name, or any other relevant information.
- **Date Received** (`Date Received`, `date`, required): Enter the date the tip was received, using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
- **Tip Amount** (`Tip Amount`, `number`, required): Enter the total tip amount received.
- **Tip Type** (`Tip Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of tip from the provided options (e.g., 'Option A', 'Option B', etc.). The specific meaning and purpose of these options are not specified here.
- **Total Tips** (`Total Tips`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of tips received.
- **Note** (`Note`, `text`, required): Enter any additional note or comment about the tip.
