# Apartment Lease Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from you as a tenant about your current apartment lease and your preferences for renewal. The data collected will help your landlord make an informed decision about the renewal of your lease.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the form by following these steps:

1. Fill in your renewal request date.
2. Enter your full name and contact email and phone number.
3. Specify any additional tenants living with you.
4. Enter your current lease start and end dates, as well as the current monthly rent.
5. Indicate your intention to renew and preferred lease term.
6. Specify the proposed renewal start date.
7. Choose a maximum rent increase you are willing to accept.
8. List any desired changes to the lease.
9. Describe any requested improvements to the property.
10. Rate your satisfaction with the property and management.
11. Indicate if you experienced any issues during the lease and describe them.
12. Indicate if any issues were resolved.
13. Specify your payment history with the property.
14. Describe the condition of the property at the end of the lease.
15. Indicate if you had any neighbor complaints.
16. Specify if you had pets during the lease.
17. Specify your vehicle information (if applicable).
18. Describe why you want to renew (or not renew) the lease.
19. Specify any alternative housing options you are considering.
20. Specify when you need a response from the landlord.
21. Choose a time of day for the landlord to contact you.
22. Add any additional comments.
23. Sign and date the form digitally.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Renewal Request Date** (`renewal_request_date`): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Tenant Full Name** (`tenant_name`): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`tenant_email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`tenant_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Additional Tenants** (`additional_tenants`): If there are other tenants living with you, list their names and contact information (if applicable).
* **Rental Property Address** (`property_address`): Enter the address of the current rental property.
* **Unit Number** (`unit_number`): Enter the apartment number or unit number.
* **Current Lease Start Date** (`current_lease_start`): Enter the start date of your current lease.
* **Current Lease End Date** (`current_lease_end`): Enter the end date of your current lease.
* **Current Monthly Rent** (`current_monthly_rent`): Enter the current monthly rent amount.
* **Do you intend to renew** (`renewal_intent`): Indicate your intention to renew the lease (Yes, definitely renew; Yes, likely to renew; Undecided; Probably not renew; No, will not renew).
* **Preferred Lease Term** (`preferred_lease_term`): Select the length of the new lease (6 months; 9 months; 12 months; 18 months; 24 months; Month-to-month; Other).
* **Proposed Renewal Start Date** (`proposed_move_in_date`): Enter the date you would like the new lease to begin.
* **Maximum Rent Increase Acceptable** (`rent_increase_acceptable`): Choose the maximum rent increase you are willing to accept (Up to 3 percent; Up to 5 percent; Up to 7 percent; Up to 10 percent; Market rate; Other).
* **Desired Changes to Lease** (`desired_changes`): List any desired changes to the lease (if applicable).
* **Requested Improvements** (`requested_improvements`): Choose any requested improvements to the property (None; New appliances; Updated flooring; Fresh paint; Bathroom updates; Kitchen updates; HVAC upgrade; Security upgrade; Parking addition; Storage addition; Other).
* **Satisfaction with Current Property** (`satisfaction_with_property`): Rate your satisfaction with the property (Very satisfied; Satisfied; Neutral; Dissatisfied; Very dissatisfied).
* **Satisfaction with Management** (`satisfaction_with_management`): Rate your satisfaction with property management (Very satisfied; Satisfied; Neutral; Dissatisfied; Very dissatisfied).
* **Did you experience any issues** (`issues_during_lease`): Indicate if you experienced any issues during the lease (No issues; Minor issues; Moderate issues; Significant issues; Major issues).
* **Issues Description** (`issues_description`): Describe any issues you experienced during the lease (if applicable).
* **Were issues resolved** (`issues_resolved`): Indicate if any issues were resolved (Yes, completely; Yes, partially; No, not resolved; Not applicable).
* **Payment History** (`payment_history`): Specify your payment history with the property (Always on time; Occasionally late; Sometimes late; Frequently late; Had payment issues).
* **Property Condition** (`property_condition`): Describe the condition of the property at the end of the lease (Excellent condition; Good condition; Fair condition; Some damage; Significant damage).
* **Any Neighbor Complaints** (`neighbors_feedback`): Indicate if you had any neighbor complaints (No complaints; One complaint; A few complaints; Multiple complaints; Ongoing issues).
* **Did you have pets during lease** (`pets_during_lease`): Indicate if you had pets during the lease (No pets; Yes, approved pets; Yes, unapproved pets).
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_information`): If you have a vehicle, please specify your vehicle information (if applicable).
* **Why do you want to renew (or not renew)** (`reason_for_renewing`): Explain why you want to renew (or not renew) the lease.
* **Why might you not renew** (`reason_for_not_renewing`): Explain why you might not renew the lease (if applicable).
* **Are you considering other options** (`alternative_housing`): Indicate if you are considering other housing options (No, committed to renewing; Maybe, exploring options; Yes, actively looking; Yes, found alternative).
* **When do you need a response** (`landlord_response_needed`): Specify when you need a response from the landlord.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_contact_time`): Choose a time of day for the landlord to contact you (Morning; Midday; Afternoon; Evening; Weekend).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any additional comments.
* **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`): Sign the form digitally.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): Date and sign the form digitally.
* **Landlord Response Needed** (`landlord_response_needed`): This field is only for the landlord's reference and should not be filled in by the tenant.
