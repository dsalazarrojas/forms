# Inspection Readiness Confirmation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inspection Readiness Confirmation Form is used to collect information from users to confirm their readiness for an inspection. This form is essential to ensure that users have the necessary information and equipment to proceed with the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First name** (required) in the field labeled "First name".
2. Fill in your **Last name** (required) in the field labeled "Last name".
3. Enter your **Email** (required) in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your **Phone number** (required) in the field labeled "Phone".
5. (Optional) Enter your **Address** (not required) in the field labeled "Address".
6. Choose a valid **Date** (required) for the inspection in the field labeled "Date".
7. Choose a valid **Time** (required) for the inspection in the field labeled "Time".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (First name, text, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled.
* **Last name** (Last name, text, required): Enter your last name as it is spelled.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address in the format of "username@domain.com" (e.g., "john.doe@example.com").
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" (e.g., "123-456-7890").
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter your physical address if you have one. This is not required.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Choose a valid date for the inspection from the calendar.
* **Time** (Time, time, required): Choose a valid time for the inspection from the available options.
