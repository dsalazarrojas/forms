# Team Collaboration Insights Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your collaboration experiences, communication patterns, and project status within the team. Your responses will help us understand what works well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Enter your job title.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Choose how often you collaborate with your team members.
6. Select how frequently you communicate with your team members.
7. Indicate how often you participate in team work.
8. Choose your preferred method of communication.
9. Indicate how often you use each collaboration channel (e.g., chat, forum, email).
10. Select the current status of your projects.
11. Identify any challenges or barriers you face while collaborating.
12. Add any additional comments about your collaboration experiences.
13. Enter the date you submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is displayed on your official records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is displayed on your official records.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title as it is displayed on your official records.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Collaboration Experience**: Choose how often you collaborate with your team members:
	+ High: You often work closely with your team members.
	+ Low: You seldom work with your team members.
* **Communication Frequency**: Select how frequently you communicate with your team members:
	+ Daily: You communicate daily with your team members.
	+ Weekly: You communicate weekly with your team members.
	+ Monthly: You communicate monthly with your team members.
* **Teamwork Frequency**: Indicate how often you participate in team work:
	+ Always: You participate in team work always.
	+ Sometimes: You participate in team work sometimes.
	+ Never: You never participate in team work.
* **Communication Method**: Choose your preferred method of communication:
	+ Video call: You prefer video calls for communication.
	+ Phone call: You prefer phone calls for communication.
	+ Email: You prefer email for communication.
	+ Instant Message: You prefer instant messaging for communication.
* **Collaboration Channels**: Indicate which collaboration channels you use:
	+ Chat: You use chat for collaboration.
	+ Forum: You use forum for collaboration.
	+ Email: You use email for collaboration.
* **Project Status**: Select the current status of your projects:
	+ In progress: Your projects are in progress.
	+ On hold: Your projects are on hold.
	+ Complete: Your projects are complete.
* **Collaboration Barriers**: Describe any challenges or barriers you face while collaborating.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your collaboration experiences.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted this form.
