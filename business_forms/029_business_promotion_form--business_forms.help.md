# Business Promotion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Promotion Form is a form designed to gather information about your shopping habits, interests, and demographics. This information helps us better understand our audience and tailor our marketing and product offers to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Step 1: Provide Your Contact Information**
	* Enter your First Name (required field)
	* Enter your Last Name (required field)
	* Enter your Email Address (required field)
	* Enter your Phone Number (optional field)
2. **Step 2: Share Your Shopping Habits**
	* Choose how often you visit us (required field)
	* Select your favorite stores or departments (optional field)
3. **Step 3: Indicate Your Interests**
	* Check all product categories that interest you (required field)
4. **Step 4: Tell Us About Your Shopping Experience**
	* Rate your overall experience with our store (optional field)
	* Provide suggestions for improvement (optional field)
	* Add any additional comments or feedback (optional field)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, `text`, optional): Enter your state or province.
* **Zip or Postal Code** (`zip_postal`, `text`, optional): Enter your postal code.
* **How Often Do You Visit** (`visit_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you visit our store.
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Occasionally
	+ First time
* **Favorite Stores or Departments** (`favorite_stores`, `text`, optional): Enter your favorite stores or departments.
* **Product Categories of Interest** (`product_interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Check all product categories that interest you.
	+ Clothing and Fashion
	+ Electronics
	+ Home and Garden
	+ Health and Beauty
	+ Food and Groceries
	+ Sports and Recreation
	+ Books and Media
	+ Other
* **What Would You Like to Buy Today** (`what_to_buy`, `text`, optional): Enter products you are looking for.
* **How Did You Hear About Our Store** (`how_heard_about_us`, `select_one`, optional): Select source of awareness.
	+ Word of Mouth
	+ Social Media
	+ Advertisement
	+ Website Search
	+ Referral
	+ Promotion Email
	+ Other
* **Interested in Our Promotions** (`interested_promotions`, `select_one`, optional): Would you like to hear about special offers?
	+ Yes, sign me up
	+ Maybe
	+ No, thanks
* **Overall Store Experience** (`store_experience`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your shopping experience.
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Very Good
	+ Excellent
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any other feedback.
