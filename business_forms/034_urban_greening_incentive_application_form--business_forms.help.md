# Urban Greening Incentive Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urban Greening Incentive Application Form is a tool designed to help businesses apply for urban greening initiatives, which aim to improve the environment and quality of life in urban areas. By filling out this form, businesses can provide the necessary information to be considered for these incentives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you are applying for an existing urban greening initiative. If you are not familiar with the initiative, please refer to our Urban Greening Incentives webpage for more information.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Review each field carefully and provide the most up-to-date information.
4. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields (marked as "required").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Urban Greening Incentive Application Form (Page 1)** (`urban_greening_incentive_application_form`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is the title of the form, which is pre-populated for your convenience.
* **Funding Request Type (Page 1)** (`funding_request_type`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select "Yes" if you are seeking financial support for your urban greening initiative, and "No" if you are not.
* **Name (Page 1)** (`name`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your business name as it appears on your business card or other official documents.
* **Email (Page 1)** (`email`, email, required: false)
	+ Enter your business email address.
* **Address (Page 1)** (`address`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your business address.
* **Phone (Page 1)** (`phone`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Details (Page 1)** (`business_details`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide a brief description of your business and its connection to the urban greening initiative.
* **Funding Request (Page 1)** (`funding_request`, text, required: false)
	+ Describe the specific funding request you are making for your urban greening initiative.
* **Submission Date (Page 1)** (`submission_date`, date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date you are submitting this application.

## Tips

* Ensure you have reviewed and understood the urban greening initiative and its requirements before filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
* This form should take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete.
* Once you have submitted this form, you will receive an email confirmation with further instructions.
