# Digital Communication Policy Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Communication Policy Agreement is a form used to ensure that employees understand and agree to the company's digital communication policies. Completing this form is a necessary step for employees to demonstrate their awareness and commitment to adhering to these policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your name in the "Name" field.
* Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
* Enter your department in the "Department" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* If applicable, select your time zone from the "Time Zone" field or enter a custom time zone in the "Other Time Zone" field.
* If applicable, select your last interaction date and time from the "Date Of Last Interaction" and "Time Of Last Interaction" fields.
* Acknowledge that you are aware of the company's digital communication policies by selecting one or more options from the "Acknowledgement" field.
* If you have a preferred time zone for agreements, select it from the "Time Zone Agreement" field or enter a custom time zone in the "Other Time Zone Agreement" field.
* Finally, sign the form by entering your name in the "Signed By" field.
