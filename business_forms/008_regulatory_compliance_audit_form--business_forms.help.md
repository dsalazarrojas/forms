# Regulatory Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess and document the compliance status of a particular business process or policy within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and fields.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field based on your assessment of the business process or policy.
3. Ensure that all fields are answered accurately and completely.
4. If a field does not apply, select "N/A" or "Not Applicable".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Compliance Audit Form** (`regulatory_compliance_audit_form_1`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to indicate if the business process or policy is compliant with regulatory requirements. Select "Yes" if it is, "No" if it is not.
* **Review Standards** (`regulatory_compliance_audit_form_2`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to document any standards or guidelines that have been reviewed in assessing compliance. Select all applicable standards.
* **Policies** (`regulatory_compliance_audit_form_3`, text, required: false): This field is used to document any relevant policies related to the business process or policy. Enter the full policy name or description.
* **Audit Process** (`regulatory_compliance_audit_form_4`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to indicate if the audit process was thorough and accurate in assessing compliance. Select "Yes" if it was, "No" if it was not.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are answered accurately and completely.
* If a field does not apply, select "N/A" or "Not Applicable".
* Review and understand the context and relevance of each field before completing the form.
