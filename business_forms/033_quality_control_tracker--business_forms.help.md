# Quality Control Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Control Tracker is a form used to track and document issues found during quality control inspections. This form helps ensure that all quality control issues are properly recorded, reported, and addressed in a timely manner. It serves as a centralized repository for tracking issues, their categories, and the status of corrective actions taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field.
2.  Enter the location of the inspection in the "Inspection Location" field.
3.  Describe the issue found during the inspection in the "Issue Description" field.
4.  Select the category of the issue from the "Issue Category" dropdown menu.
5.  If applicable, provide a description of the corrective action taken in the "Corrective Action" field.
6.  If applicable, provide a link to evidence or supporting document related to the issue in the "Evidence Link" field.
7.  Select the status of the corrective action from the "Corrective Action Status" dropdown menu.
8.  If applicable, assign the issue to a team member or department in the "Assigned To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date (1)**: Enter the date the inspection was performed.
* **Inspection Location (2)**: Enter the location where the inspection was performed.
* **Issue Description (3)**: Describe the issue found during the inspection.
* **Issue Category (4)**: Select the category of the issue (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Corrective Action (5)**: If applicable, describe the action taken to address the issue.
*   **Evidence Link (6)**: If applicable, provide a link to supporting documents related to the issue.
* **Corrective Action Status (7)**: Select the status of the corrective action (e.g., "Active" or "Inactive").
* **Assigned To (8)**: If applicable, assign the issue to a team member or department for follow-up.

## Tips
*   Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly, as it will help in maintaining a clear and up-to-date record of quality control issues.
*   Use the "Issue Category" dropdown menu to select the most appropriate category for the issue.
*   Use the "Corrective Action Status" dropdown menu to accurately reflect the status of the corrective action taken.
*   Assign issues to team members or departments for follow-up and to ensure that issues are properly addressed.
