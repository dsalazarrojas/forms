# Waxing Consent Cancellation Policy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waxing Consent Cancellation Policy Form is used to document the cancellation of a waxing service. This form ensures that all relevant information is collected and recorded for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required and optional fields as necessary.
2. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission**: Enter the details about the original consent and waxing service, including date and time of the consent.
	+ If the original consent is not recorded, please contact your supervisor or manager for guidance.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date when the original consent was given for the waxing service.
	+ This date should match the date recorded in the service agreement or other relevant documents.
* **Cancellation Date**: Enter the date when the cancellation is being made.
	+ This date should be the current date.
* **Service Agreement**: Enter a brief description of the original service agreement, if applicable.
	+ If the original service agreement is not recorded, please contact your supervisor or manager for guidance.
* **Cancellation Reason**: Select one of the provided options or enter a custom reason for cancellation.
	+ Please select "Option 1" for "Client Change of Heart"
	+ Select "Option 2" for "Service Not Needed"
	+ Select "Option 3" for "Other" (and enter a custom reason in the "Other Cancellation Reason" field)
* **Other Cancellation Reason (if applicable)**: Enter a brief description of the reason for cancellation if you selected "Option 3".
	+ This field is only required if you selected "Option 3" for "Cancellation Reason".
