# disbursement_correction_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to correct discrepancies or errors in disbursements, ensuring accurate financial records and compliance with company policies. This form serves as a tool to identify and rectify any mistakes or issues that have arisen during the disbursement process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, providing all required information.
2. Enter the correct organization name in the "organization" field.
3. Enter the correct amount in the "amount" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **organization** (organization, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to select the organization associated with the disbursement correction.
	+ Please enter the correct name of the organization that is associated with the disbursement correction.
* **amount** (amount, number, optional):
	+ This field is used to correct the disbursement amount.
	+ Enter the correct amount that should be recorded for the disbursement correction.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the corrections are applied correctly.
- If you are unsure about any information, please double-check with the relevant department or team before submitting the form.
- This form should only be used for corrections, not for new disbursements.
