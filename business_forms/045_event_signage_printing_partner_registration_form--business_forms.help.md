# Event Signage Printing Partner Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Event Signage Printing Partner Registration Form is used to register and partner with signage printing providers for events. This form gathers essential details about the partner's services, pricing, and contact information to ensure smooth communication and partnership management.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please read through the form carefully before starting to ensure you understand each field's purpose and requirements.
- Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, providing clear and concise information about your services, pricing, and other relevant details.
- Review your answers for completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration** (`text`, optional): This is the title of your registration.
* **Partner Details** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief description about your partner details.
* **Services** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose all the services you offer for events from the available options. These services will help us better understand your expertise.
* **Pricing** (`text`, optional): Provide details about your pricing and payment structures.
* **Partners** (`select_one`, required): Select your business name from the available options.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your official email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Event Name** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`text`, optional): Enter the location of the event.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- If you experience any issues with the form, please contact us for assistance.
- You will receive a confirmation email after successfully submitting the form.
