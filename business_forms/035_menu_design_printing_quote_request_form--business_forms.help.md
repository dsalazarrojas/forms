# Menu Design Printing Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a quote for printing your menu designs. Please fill out the form below to provide us with the necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Name (Text, Required):** Enter the name of your restaurant.
* **Contact Person (Text, Optional):** Enter the name of the contact person for your restaurant.
* **Phone Number (Text, Optional):** Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email Address (Email, Optional):** Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Menu Design File (Text, Optional):** Upload your menu design file (e.g. PDF, JPEG).
* **Menu Size (Select One, Optional):** Choose the size of your menu (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Color Scheme (Select Multiple, Optional):** Choose the color scheme for your menu (Color 1, Color 2, Color 3).
* **Print Quality (Select One, Optional):** Choose the print quality for your menu (High, Medium, Low).
