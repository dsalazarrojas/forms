# Fall 2020 Color Street - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall 2020 Color Street form is used to capture and process customer orders for the 2020 Color Street collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product you would like to order.
2. Choose the color you prefer.
3. Input the quantity of the selected product.
4. Select a date and time for your order.
5. Enter your name as the customer.
6. Input your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. If applicable, add a note to your order.
9. Select the person to be assigned to your order (in this case, "chatjimmy").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product (Product 1, Product 2, Product 3)**: Select the product you would like to order from the available options.
* **Color (red, blue, green)**: Choose the color you prefer for your order (multiple colors can be selected).
* **Size (no options)**: Although this field is not used in the provided form, it's likely an optional field for selecting the size of the product.
* **Quantity**: Input the quantity of the selected product.
* **Order Date**: Select a date for your order.
* **Order Time**: Select a time for your order.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as the customer.
* **Email**: Input your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Note (optional)**: Add any additional comments or instructions related to your order.
* **Assigned to (chatjimmy, Other)**: Select the person to be assigned to your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure proper processing of your order.
