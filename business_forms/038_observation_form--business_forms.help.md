# observation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The observation form is designed to gather specific, concrete information from observers or reviewers in a business setting. It helps track and improve processes, communication, and performance within teams, departments, or organizations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Complete the form for each observation or event individually**. Avoid filling out the form more than once for the same observation.
2. **Fill out each field accurately and completely**. Make sure to include all required information.
3. **Use the provided hints as a guide** to help you fill out the form correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (<employee_name>): Enter the name of the employee you are observing or reviewing.
* **Employee Email** (<employee_email>): Enter the email address of the employee you are observing or reviewing.
* **Manager Name** (<manager_name>): Enter the name of the manager or supervisor of the employee you are observing or reviewing.
* **Observation Date** (<observation_date>): Enter the date of the observation or review.
* **Observation Time** (<observation_time>): Enter the time of the observation or review.
* **Feedback** (<feedback>): Provide specific, actionable feedback on the observation or review.
* **Suggestions** (<suggestions>): Share any suggestions or recommendations you have for improvement.
* **Action Items** (<action_items>): Outline any action items or next steps required for implementation.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* Use the feedback, suggestions, and action items to drive improvements and changes within the organization.
