# sublease_agreement_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sublease Agreement Declaration Form is a document used to outline the terms and conditions of a sublease, a secondary or temporary rental agreement between a tenant and a landlord.

## How To Complete This Form

* Read the form carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
* Fill in the required information for the sublease, including the start and end dates, rent, deposit, and other relevant details.
* Review and sign the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Information (1)** (`tenant_information`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the tenant, including their name and contact details.
* **Landlord Information (2)** (`landlord_information`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the landlord, including their name and contact details.
* **Sublease Details (3)** (`sublease_details`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of the sublease, including its start and end dates, rent, and other relevant details.
* **Agreement Terms (4)** (`agreement_terms`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the terms and conditions of the sublease from a list of options.
* **Lease Start Date (5)** (`lease_start_date`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date (6)** (`lease_end_date`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the end date of the lease.
* **Sublease Start Date (7)** (`sublease_start_date`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the start date of the sublease.
* **Sublease End Date (8)** (`sublease_end_date`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the end date of the sublease.
* **Rent (9)** (`rent`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the rent amount for the sublease.
* **Deposit (10)** (`deposit`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the deposit amount for the sublease.
* **Security Deposit (11)** (`security_deposit`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the security deposit amount for the sublease.
* **Sublease End Use (12)** (`sublease_end_use`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select the allowed uses for the subleased property.
* **Lease Type (13)** (`lease_type`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the type of lease from a list of options.
* **Notice Period (14)** (`notice_period`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the notice period for the sublease.
* **Penalty Charge (15)** (`penalty_charge`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the penalty charge for the sublease.
* **Notice Fee (16)** (`notice_fee`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the notice fee for the sublease.
* **Sublease Agreement (17)** (`sublease_agreement`, text, optional): This field is used to enter a brief description of the sublease agreement.
* **Lease Agreement (18)** (`lease_agreement`, text, optional): This field is used to enter a brief description of the main lease agreement.
* **Landlord Contact (19)** (`landlord_contact`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the landlord's contact details.
* **Tenant Signature (20)** (`tenant_signature`, text, optional): This field is used to obtain the signature of the tenant.
* **Landlord Signature (21)** (`landlord_signature`, text, optional): This field is used to obtain the signature of the landlord.
* **Agreement Date (22)** (`agreement_date`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date the sublease agreement was signed.
* **Signed By (23)** (`signed_by`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the names of the parties who signed the agreement.
* **Date Signed (24)** (`date_signed`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Tenant Name (25)** (`tenant_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the tenant.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before completing it.
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
* Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
