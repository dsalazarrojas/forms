# Instructor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Instructor Registration Form is designed to collect personal and contact information of instructors to facilitate internal communication and coordination within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Provide your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Input your email address in the "Email" field, following the standard email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
4. Select your role from the "Role" dropdown menu (Administrator, Instructor, or Other).
5. Enter your department in the "Department" field (if applicable).
6. Fill in your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Input your preferred date of availability in the "Date" field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., `2022-01-01`).
8. Select your preferred time of availability in the "Time" field (if applicable).
9. Add any additional comments or instructions in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address in this field.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following roles: Administrator, Instructor, or Other.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you belong to or work with.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., `2022-01-01`).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select your preferred time of availability (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions here.
