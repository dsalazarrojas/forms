# Office Lease Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renew office leases for existing tenants. It gathers the necessary information from the tenant and landlord to update the lease terms, including start and end dates, lease term lengths, and rental amounts. This form is typically used by the office management team to facilitate the renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the tenant and landlord to identify the lease holders.
2. Provide the start and end dates of the lease, including the current and new lease terms.
3. Select the desired lease term length from the options provided.
4. Choose the lease term type (month-to-month or year-to-year).
5. Enter the new rental amount, if applicable.
6. Provide a brief reason for the lease renewal, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tenant_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the tenant responsible for the lease.
* **landlord_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the landlord associated with the lease.
* **lease_start_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the lease, including the day, month, and year.
* **lease_end_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the lease, including the day, month, and year.
* **lease_term_length** (Select One, Optional): Select the desired lease term length from 6 months, 12 months, or 24 months.
* **lease_term** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the lease term type, either month-to-month or year-to-year.
* **rental_amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the new rental amount, if the lease is being renewed with a new rate.
* **renewal_reason** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief explanation for renewing the lease, if desired.

## Tips
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access and update the lease information.
- Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If the lease is being renewed with a new term length or rental amount, make sure to update the relevant fields accordingly.
