# Easter Brunch Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information from customers who want to order Easter brunch for their guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with the necessary information and click on the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Choose the category of your order from the options "Eggs", "Mains", or "Sides".
* **Guest Name**: Type in the name of the person(s) you are ordering for.
* **Quantity**: Select the number of items you want to order.
* **Order Time**: Choose the time of day you want to order.
* **Date**: Choose the date you want to order.
* **Phone**: Type in your phone number.
* **Email**: Type in your email address.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, type them in here.
* **Easter Egg Color**: Choose the color of the Easter egg(s) you want to order from the options "Pink", "Blue", "Green".
* **Easter Egg Size**: Choose the size of the Easter egg(s) you want to order from the options "Small", "Large", "Extra Large".

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields carefully, especially the category field on the second page.
* You can choose any combination of options for each field, depending on your preferences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
