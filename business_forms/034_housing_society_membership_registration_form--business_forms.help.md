# housing_society_membership_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or organizations to register for membership in the housing society. It collects essential information for the society's records and communication.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through the form and read each field's explanation below.
2. Fill in all the required fields (marked as required).
3. For optional fields, provide as much information as possible.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Membership Details
* **Member Details** (`Member Details`, `text`, required): Fill in your name and any other relevant details about yourself or your organization.
* **Member Type** (`Member Type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of member you are (Residential, Commercial, or Corporate).
* **Membership Start Date** (`Membership Start Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you wish to start your membership.
* **Membership End Date** (`Membership End Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you wish to end your membership.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, optional): Provide your contact details, including address, phone number, and email.

### Membership Attributes
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, `text`, optional): If applicable, specify the tool assigned to you.
* **Output File** (`Output File`, `text`, optional): If you need to attach additional documents, specify the file name and path.

### Signature
* **Member Signature** (`Member Signature`, `note`, optional): Sign and date your membership application to confirm your commitment.

Note: Ensure you review each field carefully and fill in all the required fields before submitting the form. If you're unsure about any information, feel free to contact the housing society for guidance.
