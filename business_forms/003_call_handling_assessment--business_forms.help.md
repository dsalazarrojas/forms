# Call Handling Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the performance of representatives in handling customer calls. It assesses their ability to effectively address customer issues, demonstrate technical knowledge, and provide excellent customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the provided options for each field and select the most accurate response to evaluate the representative's performance.
2. Fill out each field honestly and accurately, providing specific examples or feedback where possible.
3. Ensure that the representative's name is entered correctly in the Assessor Name field.
4. Review your responses for completeness and ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Call Information (Section 1)
* **Representative Name** (`representative_name`, text, required): This field is for the name of the representative who handled the call.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): This field is for the date of the call assessment.
* **Call Duration** (`call_duration`, number, required): This field is for the duration of the call in minutes.
* **Customer Satisfaction Level** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_one, required): This field is for rating the customer's satisfaction level.
* **Call was answered professionally and courteously** (`call_answered_professionally`, select_one, required): This field is for rating whether the representative answered the call professionally.
* **Representative understood customer issue clearly** (`issue_understood_clearly`, select_one, required): This field is for rating whether the representative understood the customer's issue clearly.
* **Active listening and empathy demonstrated** (`active_listening_demonstrated`, select_one, required): This field is for rating whether the representative demonstrated active listening and empathy.
* **Technical knowledge and product expertise** (`technical_knowledge`, select_one, required): This field is for rating the representative's technical knowledge.
* **Problem was effectively resolved or escalated** (`problem_resolution`, select_one, required): This field is for rating whether the problem was effectively resolved or escalated.
* **Call pace was appropriate and not rushed** (`call_pace_appropriate`, select_one, required): This field is for rating whether the call pace was appropriate.
* **Follow-up or next steps were clearly communicated** (`follow_up_offered`, select_one, required): This field is for rating whether follow-up or next steps were clearly communicated.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`overall_performance_rating`, select_one, required): This field is for rating the overall performance of the representative.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): This field is for the name of the person assessing the representative's performance.

### Performance Assessment (Section 2)
* This section is for providing feedback on the representative's performance and areas for improvement.

* **Key Strengths Demonstrated** (`strengths_demonstrated`, text): This field is for noting any strengths demonstrated by the representative.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text): This field is for noting any areas where the representative needs improvement.
* **Training or Development Recommendations** (`training_recommendations`, text): This field is for providing recommendations for training or development.

### Note: This form is for internal use only. Please ensure that the representative's performance is kept confidential.
