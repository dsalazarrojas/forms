# Hotel Booking Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to process hotel booking payments. It collects important information required for payment processing and confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Guest Name**.
2. Enter the **date of arrival** and **date of departure** for your stay.
3. Select the type of **room** you have booked (Hotel Room, Suite, or Family Room).
4. Enter the **total cost** of your stay.
5. Choose your preferred **payment method** (Card or Bank Transfer).
6. If paying by **Card**, provide the **card number** and your **name on card**.
7. Enter your **email address**, **phone number**, and any additional **note** regarding your booking.
8. Confirm your payment details by typing **confirm_payment** and the **confirm_date** of your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of the guest booking the room.
* **date_arrival**: Select the date of arrival for your stay.
* **date_departure**: Select the date of departure for your stay.
* **room_type**: Choose the type of room you have booked (Hotel Room, Suite, or Family Room).
* **total_cost**: Enter the total cost of your stay.
* **method_payment**: Choose your preferred payment method (Card or Bank Transfer).
* **card_number**: If paying by Card, enter the card number.
* **name_on_card**: If paying by Card, enter your name as it appears on the card.
* **address**: Enter your address for payment confirmation.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **note**: Provide any additional information or note regarding your booking.
* **confirm_payment**: Confirm your payment details by typing this field.
* **confirm_date**: Confirm the date of your booking for payment purposes.

Note: All fields are optional, but please provide accurate and complete information for a smooth payment processing process. If a field is marked as required, please fill it in accordingly.
