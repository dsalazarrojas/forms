# real_estate_seller_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a real estate seller, including contact details, listing details, and property specifics. It is intended for use when listing a property for sale or rent.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Ensure that your Phone and Email are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Enter your Address, City, State, Zip, and Listing Date.
4. Specify the Listing Price and Expiration Date of the property.
5. Select the Realty Type and Property Type from the respective dropdown menus.
6. Enter a detailed description of the property.
7. Finally, review and submit the form to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the property or your contact address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where the property is located.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required): Select the state where the property is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, required): Enter the zip code of the property's location.
* **Listing Price** (`listing_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the listing.
* **Listing Date** (`listing_date`, date, required): Enter the date the property was listed.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the date the listing is set to expire.
* **Realty Type** (`realty_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property you are selling (e.g. Single Family, Multi Family, Commercial).
* **Realty Description** (`realty_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property (e.g. Residential, Commercial).
* **Property Description** (`property_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the property.
## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to complete this form.
* If you are having trouble completing any of the fields, please contact your system administrator for assistance.
