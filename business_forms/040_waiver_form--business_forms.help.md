# Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waiver Form is used to collect information about jumpers, including their name, date, age, guardian's name and email, to ensure proper identification and service provision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the jumper's name in the "Jumper name" field.
2. Select the date of the jumper's activity from the calendar provided.
3. Enter the jumper's age.
4. Enter the name of the jumper's guardian.
5. Enter the guardian's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Jumper name** (`jumper_name`, text, required: false): Enter the jumper's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the jumper's activity.
* **Jumper age** (`jumper_age`, number, required: false): Enter the jumper's age as of the date of their activity.
* **Guardian name** (`guardian_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the jumper's legal guardian or parent.
* **Guardian email** (`guardian_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the jumper's guardian or parent.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately to ensure proper service provision.
- Ensure the jumper is aware of the form's purpose and provides consent before filling it out.
- If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant personnel for clarification.
