# New Partner Engagement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Partner Engagement Form collects essential information about new partners, including basic contact details and partner-specific data. This form helps our team to onboard new partners efficiently and build strong business relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form in its entirety, or go to the next page when you're ready to continue.
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- Review the terms and conditions to ensure you understand the partnership agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **new_partner_form (text, Optional)**:
	+ Enter a brief description of the new partner engagement.
* **Basic Information (text, Optional)**:
	+ Provide a brief introduction to the partner, including their role or position.
* **Contact Information (text, Optional)**:
	+ Enter the partner's contact details, such as their phone number or email.
* **partner_type (Select One, Optional)**:
	+ Select one of the provided options that best describes the partner's type.
* **partner_name (text, Optional)**:
	+ Enter the partner's name or title.
* **address (text, Optional)**:
	+ Enter the partner's address or location.
* **email (email, Optional)**:
	+ Enter the partner's email address.
* **phone (text, Optional)**:
	+ Enter the partner's phone number.
* **partner_since (date, Optional)**:
	+ Select the date when the partner started working with you (if applicable).
* **partner_since_time (time, Optional)**:
	+ Select the time when the partner started working with you (if applicable).
* **partner_since_note (note, Optional)**:
	+ Enter any additional notes about the partner's engagement start.
* **partner_since_date (date, Optional)**:
	+ Select the date when the partner started working with you (if applicable).
* **partner_since_month (Select One, Optional)**:
	+ Select the month when the partner started working with you (if applicable).
* **partner_since_year (Select One, Optional)**:
	+ Select the year when the partner started working with you (if applicable).
* **terms and conditions (Select Multiple, Optional)**:
	+ Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the partnership.
* **save (text, Optional)**:
	+ Click to save the form and submit the partner information.
* **back (text, Optional)**:
	+ Click to go back to the previous page and make changes.

Please note that some fields are optional, but providing accurate and up-to-date information is crucial for efficient partner onboarding.
