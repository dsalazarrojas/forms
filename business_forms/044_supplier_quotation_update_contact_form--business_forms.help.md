# Supplier Quotation Update Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update supplier quotations with the buyer. Please fill out the form with the required information to facilitate the update process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Select the correct update method (Phone, Email, In-person, or Other) to indicate how the update was made.
3. Select all relevant options for "Updated by" to specify who made the update.
4. Enter the updated quotation date and time.
5. Select the updated quotation status (if applicable).
6. Add any additional notes about the update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company making the update.
* **Quotation Number** (`quotation_number`, text, required): Enter the original quotation number.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the supplier making the update.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the supplier or a contact person.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the supplier or a contact person.
* **Updated Quotation** (`updated_quotation`, text, required): Enter the updated quotation details.
* **Update Method** (`update_method`, select_one, required): Select how the update was made (Phone, Email, In-person, or Other).
*   *Update Method Other* (`update_method_other`, text, optional): If "Other" was selected, enter the alternative update method.
*   *Updated by* (`updated_by`, select multiple, required): Select all applicable individuals involved in making the update.
*   *Updated Quotation Date* (`updated_quotation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the update.
*   *Updated Quotation Time* (`updated_quotation_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the update.
*   *Updated Quotation Status* (`updated_quotation_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the updated quotation (if applicable).
*   *Notes* (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the update.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your internal support team for assistance.
