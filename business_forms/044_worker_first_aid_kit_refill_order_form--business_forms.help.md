# Worker First Aid Kit Refill Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a refill of your first aid kit, which is essential for providing immediate medical assistance in emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the first aid supply location where you are currently situated.
2. Select the type of first aid kit you need to be refilled.
3. If necessary, enter the quantity of the first aid kit you need.
4. If you have any specific requirements for the refill (e.g., due date), enter the first aid kit renewal date.
5. Enter the cost associated with the refill (this will be used for internal purposes).
6. Select the status of your first aid kit (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
7. If you have already received a full supply of first aid kits, enter the total quantity supplied.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your first aid kit (e.g., issues or concerns).
9. Finally, select the tool assigned to you to ensure that the refill request is processed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first aid supply location**: Enter the location where your first aid supply is currently situated.
* **first aid kit type**: Select the type of first aid kit you need to be refilled. This will help us understand what type of supplies you require.
* **first aid kit quantity**: If you need a specific quantity of first aid kits, enter the number here.
* **first aid kit renewal date**: If you have any specific requirements for the refill, such as a due date, enter the date here.
* **first aid kit cost**: This is an optional field for internal purposes only. Enter the cost associated with the refill if you have this information available.
* **first aid kit status**: Select the status of your first aid kit (Active, Inactive, or Pending) to help us understand your current situation.
* **first aid kits supplied total**: If you have already received a full supply of first aid kits, enter the total quantity here.
* **notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your first aid kit. For example, if you have any issues or concerns regarding the current supply.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to you to ensure that the refill request is processed correctly.
