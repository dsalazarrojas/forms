# Entrepreneurial Support Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Entrepreneurial Support Appointment Form is a tool used by the business support team to gather information from business owners or entrepreneurs who are seeking support. This form will help the team determine the level of support needed and schedule an appointment accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you need business support or not.
2. Choose your preferred business advisor.
3. Fill in your preferred appointment date and time.
4. Select the duration of the appointment.
5. Choose your preferred language.
6. Schedule an appointment.
7. Provide your contact email and phone number.
8. Add any additional comments.
9. Confirm your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Support** (business_support_form, select_multiple, required: false): 
This field allows you to indicate whether you need business support or not. Please select 'Yes' if you require support and 'No' if you do not.
* **Select Business Advisor** (select_business_advisor, select_one, required: false): 
This field is used to select your preferred business advisor. Please choose the advisor that best suits your needs.
* **Select Appointment Date and Time** (select_datetime, date, required: false): 
Please select your preferred appointment date and time. If you have specific requirements for the appointment, please include them in the additional comments field.
* **Appointment Duration** (select_appointment_duration, select_one, required: false): 
Select the duration of your appointment. This will help the business support team prepare for the meeting.
* **Preferred Language** (select_preferred_language, select_multiple, required: false): 
Please select your preferred language for the appointment. This will ensure that the conversation is conducted in a language you are comfortable with.
* **Schedule Appointment** (schedule_appointment, select_one, required: false): 
Please select 'Yes' if you would like to schedule an appointment and 'No' if you do not.
* **Business Phone** (select_business_phone, select_one, required: false): 
This field is optional but please provide your business phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
* **Additional Comments** (select_additional_comments, note, required: false): 
If you have any additional comments or requirements for the appointment, please include them here.
* **Confirm Appointment** (confirm_appointment, select_one, required: false): 
Please select 'Yes' to confirm your appointment and 'No' if you change your mind.
