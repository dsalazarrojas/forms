# Legal Benefits Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Benefits Order Form is a document that records and tracks legal benefits received by a business. This form helps ensure that all benefits are properly recorded and reported to relevant parties, reducing the risk of errors and maintaining compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you're familiar with each field and its purpose.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, using the provided fields and options.
3. Double-check your answers to ensure they're correct and consistent with company policies and procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Form Title** (`legal_benefits_order_form_1`): Enter the title of the business benefit being documented. This field is not required.
* **Legal Benefits Title** (`business_legal_benefits_title`): Enter a descriptive title for the legal benefit, if applicable. This field is not required.
* **Legal Benefits Description** (`business_benefits_description`): Describe the legal benefit in detail, if applicable. This field is not required.
* **Legal Benefit Type** (`business_legal_benefits_type`): Select the type of legal benefit from the options: Legal, Insurance, or Financial.
* **Legal Benefit Frequency** (`business_legal_benefits_frequency`): Select how often the legal benefit is received: Once, Repeatedly, or As needed.
* **Legal Benefit Start Date** (`business_legal_benefits_start_date`): Enter the start date of the legal benefit, if applicable.
* **Legal Benefit End Date** (`business_legal_benefits_end_date`): Enter the end date of the legal benefit, if applicable.
* **Legal Benefit Status** (`business_legal_benefits_status`): Select the status of the legal benefit from the options: Open, Closed, or Pending.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers to ensure they're accurate and complete.
- If you're unsure about any field or option, contact the relevant authority or seek clarification from your supervisor.
- Save a copy of the completed form for future reference and compliance purposes.
