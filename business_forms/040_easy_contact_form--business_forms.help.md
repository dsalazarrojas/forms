# Easy Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easy Contact Form is a general-purpose form for contacting your company or team. It's intended to be used for legitimate inquiries, feedback, or other non-sensitive information sharing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name, email address, and phone number (if desired).
2. Select the category that best describes the purpose of your message.
3. Choose the relevant tags that pertain to your inquiry.
4. Enter your message in the message field.
5. If desired, select a date and time for your inquiry.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Message** (`message`, `note`, required: false): Enter your message or inquiry.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the category that best describes the purpose of your message.
* **Tags** (`tags`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the relevant tags that pertain to your inquiry.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select a date that's relevant to your inquiry (if applicable).
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Select a time that's relevant to your inquiry (if applicable).
