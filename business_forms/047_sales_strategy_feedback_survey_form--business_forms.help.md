# Sales Strategy Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Strategy Feedback Survey Form is designed to collect information from sales team members regarding their sales strategy, helping to identify areas of improvement and best practices. This form is intended to be a one-time submission, and you should only focus on sharing your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each page of the form, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Answer each question truthfully, using the provided fields to explain your sales strategy.
3. Do not repeat the same information across multiple fields.
4. Use the "Additional Comments" field to provide any further information that may not fit within the other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Strategy Feedback** (`sales_strategy_form`, text, required: false): This field is where you can provide an overview of your current sales strategy. Please describe your approach to sales, including any notable successes or challenges you've faced.
* **How many sales calls do you make per week?** (`sales_strategy_form_1`, number, required: false): Please indicate the average number of sales calls you make per week. This will help us understand your sales volume and potential opportunities.
* **What time of day do you make these calls?** (`sales_strategy_form_2`, time, required: false): Describe the time of day when you make your sales calls. This will help us understand your sales rhythm and potential scheduling conflicts.
* **What are the top industries you focus on?** (`sales_strategy_form_3`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the industries your sales team focuses on, such as Software, Hardware, or Other.
* **Sales Strategy Feedback** (`sales_strategy_form_4`, text, required: false): This field is another opportunity to provide detailed feedback on your sales strategy, if you haven't already done so in the first field.
* **Additional Comments** (`sales_strategy_form_5`, note, required: false): If you have any further information or thoughts about your sales strategy, please provide them here. This is an optional but recommended field for any additional feedback.
