# Vendor Vetting Initiative Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Vetting Initiative Registration Form is designed to collect information about vendors who are interested in participating in the vetting program.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out all required fields.
2.  Enter your information accurately and thoroughly.
3.  If a field is not relevant, leave it blank or use the provided options to select an appropriate response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (1, Text, Required): Please enter your business name as it is known to the public.
* **Email Address** (2, Email, Required): Enter your business email address. This will be used to communicate with you about your vetting status.
* **Phone Number** (3, Text, Required): Enter your business phone number. This will be used to contact you in case we need to reach you.
* **Business Type** (4, Text, Required): Enter a brief description of your business, e.g. "Manufacturer," "Retailer," etc.
* **Business Description** (5, Text, Required): Enter a detailed description of your business. 
* **Business Category** (6, Select Multiple, Required): Select the category that best describes your business. Choose from: Manufacturing, Construction, Retail, Service.
* **Location** (7, Text, Required): Enter the physical address of your business. This will be used to geolocate your business.
* **Vetting Requestor** (8, Text, Required): Enter the name of the person or organization requesting vetting on your behalf. This could be a business partner or representative.
* **Company Name** (9, Text, Required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Company URL** (10, Text, Optional): Enter the URL of your company's website, if applicable.
* **Company Email** (11, Email, Optional): Enter the email address of your company.
* **Business Since** (12, Date, Optional): If your business has been in operation for more than 2 years, enter the date it was established.
* **Business Status** (13, Select One, Required): Select the current status of your business. Choose from: Active, Inactive.
* **Vetting Date** (14, Date, Required): Enter the date you are requesting vetting services.
* **Vetting Type** (15, Text, Required): Enter a brief description of the vetting services you are requesting.
* **Vetting Requestor Phone** (16, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the person or organization requesting vetting on your behalf.
* **Vetting Requestor Email** (17, Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the person or organization requesting vetting on your behalf.
* **Vetting Requestor Business Name** (18, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the business requesting vetting on your behalf.
* **Vetting Requestor Business Description** (19, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the business requesting vetting on your behalf.
* **Vetting Requestor Business Category** (20, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the category that best describes the business requesting vetting on your behalf. Choose from: Manufacturing, Construction, Retail, Service.
* **Vetting Requestor Location** (21, Text, Optional): Enter the location of the business requesting vetting on your behalf.
* **Vetting Requestor Requesting Vetting** (22, Text, Required): Enter a brief description of why you are requesting vetting services.
* **Business Address** (23, Text, Required): Enter the physical address of your business.
* **Business Country** (24, Text, Required): Enter the country of your business.
* **Business State** (25, Text, Required): Enter the state of your business.
