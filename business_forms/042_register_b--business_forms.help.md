# register_b - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a business within the system, allowing it to be recognized and interacted with.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Go to the main page of the form, labeled "register_b".
2. Enter your company's name in the "company name" field.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company name`, text, required/optional): Enter your company's name as it is known and recognized in the industry or market.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that will be associated with your business registration. This will be used for communication and updates from the system.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for your company name and email address.
* Double-check your email address for formatting and syntax errors to ensure submission is successful.
