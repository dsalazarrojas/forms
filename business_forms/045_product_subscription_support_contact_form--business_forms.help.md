# Product Subscription Support Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for customers who require support for their product subscriptions. You can use this form to report any issues or ask questions related to your subscription.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Name** field with your full name or the name of the person requesting support.
2. Provide your **email** address to help us contact you back.
3. In the **phone** field, you can optionally enter your phone number so we can contact you more easily.
4. Clearly describe the **subject** of your support request in brief.
5. In the **message** field, explain the problem or question you are facing in as much detail as possible.
6. Select your **subscription type** from the dropdown menu to specify the type of subscription you have.
7. If you know the start and end dates of your subscription, you can optionally select them in the **subscription_start_date** and **subscription_end_date** fields.
8. Click the **Submit** button to send your support request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: This is your full name or the name of the person requesting support. Please enter it as you would like to be addressed.
* **email**: This is the email address we will use to contact you back. Please ensure it is correct and up-to-date.
* **phone**: This is an optional field where you can enter your phone number for us to contact you more easily. If you don't have a phone number or prefer not to share, you can leave this field blank.
* **subject**: Briefly describe the purpose or topic of your support request.
* **message**: Please describe the problem or question you are facing in as much detail as possible so we can assist you better.
* **subscription_type**: Select the type of subscription you have, which will help us identify your issue more efficiently.
* **subscription_start_date** and **subscription_end_date**: If you know the start and end dates of your subscription, please enter them to help us track your subscription history. However, if you are unsure or don't know these dates, you can leave these fields blank.
* **message**: This is where you provide detailed information about the issue or question you are facing with your subscription. Be as clear and concise as possible so we can assist you better.
