# Expense Payment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Payment Requisition Form is a tool to collect and track employee expenses for reimbursement. It helps employees submit their expense claims, and administrators to process and manage these claims efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate information about your expense claim, including the type, amount, date, and other relevant details.
2. Select the correct expense type and category from the provided options.
3. Attach any required documents or attachments to support your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expense Types**: Select the type of expense you are claiming (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Amount**: Enter the total amount of the expense.
* **Date**: Enter the date the expense was incurred.
* **Time**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Expense Description**: Provide a brief description of the expense.
* **Expense Status**: Indicate the status of the expense (e.g., 'Active', 'Inactive').
* **Expense Category**: Select the category of the expense (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Attachments**: Attach any supporting documents or files to your claim.
* **Expense Sub-Category 1-10**: Select the sub-category for each expense (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Submit**: Confirm that your claim is accurate and complete before submitting.

Note: Some fields may not be required, depending on the specific expense claim. Please ensure you have understood the requirements for each field before submitting your claim.
