# Treasury Fee Waiver Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a fee waiver from the Treasury department. It gathers information about the applicant to process the request efficiently. Please complete this form if you need to waive or reduce a fee that is currently due with the Treasury department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
*   Select the applicable service type from the dropdown menu (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name Of Applicant**: Enter the name of the person or organization making the request.
*   **Email Of Applicant**: Provide the email address of the applicant.
*   **Contact Number Of Applicant**: Enter the phone number of the applicant to facilitate further contact.
*   **Service Type**: Select one or more options from the dropdown list to specify the type of service for which the waiver is requested. Options include:
    *   Account Fee Waiver
    *   Bond Fee Waiver
    *   Other (please specify in the "Other" option)

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible. If you have any doubts or questions about the form, feel free to contact the Treasury department for assistance.
