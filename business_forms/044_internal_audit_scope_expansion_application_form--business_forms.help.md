# Internal Audit Scope Expansion Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The internal audit scope expansion application form is used to collect information from users when they want to expand the scope of an internal audit. This form is filled out by internal auditors or audit team members who need to gather additional data or modify the current audit scope.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with the necessary information for your scope expansion request.
- Make sure you have the necessary permissions to fill out this form.
- Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **user-id** (1/5)
* The user-id field is an unique identifier for the user filling out the form.

### **date** (2/5)
* The date field is used to select the date when the scope expansion is needed.

### **time** (3/5)
* The time field is used to select the time when the scope expansion is needed.

### **reason** (4/5)
* The reason field is used to provide a brief explanation for the scope expansion request.

### **description** (5/5)
* The description field is used to provide a detailed explanation for the scope expansion request.
