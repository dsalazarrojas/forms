# supply_chain_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The supply_chain_audit_form is a tool for gathering information related to supply chain auditing processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the introduction page to understand the context and purpose of the form.
2. Enter supplier information in the supplier_info field.
3. In the assessment section, select all applicable options from the list.
4. Choose the relevant compliance option.
5. Enter detailed information about the supplier's performance in the performance field.
6. Add any relevant notes or comments in the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **supplier_info** (text): Enter a brief description of the supplier, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **assessment** (select multiple): Evaluate the supplier's performance based on the provided options.
* **compliance** (select one): Check the relevant compliance checkbox to indicate whether the supplier meets the required standards.
* **performance** (text): Provide a detailed description of the supplier's performance, including any strengths or weaknesses.
* **notes** (note): Add any additional comments or notes about the supplier, such as future actions or next steps.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use the assessment and compliance sections to provide a detailed evaluation of the supplier.
* Keep the performance and notes fields concise and clear.
* Review the introduction page before starting the form to understand the context and purpose.
