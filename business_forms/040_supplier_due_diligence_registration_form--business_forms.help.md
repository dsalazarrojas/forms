# Supplier Due Diligence Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for suppliers to provide necessary information for due diligence purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your company name in the **Company Name** field.
2. Choose your supplier type from the **Supplier Type** dropdown menu.
3. For **Business Structure**, select all applicable options that apply to your business structure.
4. In **Contact Person**, enter the name of the contact person for your company.
5. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
6. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
7. Enter your physical address in the **Address** field.
8. Enter your zip code in the **Zip Code** field.
9. If applicable, enter your date of incorporation in the **Date of Incorporation** field.
10. If applicable, enter your date of establishment in the **Date of Establishment** field.
11. Enter your tax ID in the **Tax Id** field.
12. Enter your registration number in the **Registration Number** field.
13. If applicable, enter your website in the **Website** field.
14. Enter a brief description of your company in the **Description** field.
15. Enter the name of your bank in the **Bank Name** field.
16. Enter your account number in the **Account Number** field.
17. Enter the country for your tax ID in the **Tax Id Country** field.
18. Enter your IBAN number in the **IBAN** field.
19. Enter your SWIFT number in the **SWIFT** field.
20. Enter your business license in the **Business License** field.
21. Enter your tax license in the **Tax License** field.
22. If applicable, enter your business registration date in the **Business Registration Date** field.
23. If applicable, enter your tax registration date in the **Tax Registration Date** field.
24. Enter any additional notes in the **Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: A text field where you can enter your company name.
* **Supplier Type**: A dropdown menu to select your supplier type (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, or Corporation).
* **Business Structure**: A multiple-choice dropdown menu for your business structure (S-Corporation, C-Corporation, Partnership, or Limited Liability Company).
* **Contact Person**: A text field for the contact person's name.
* **Email**: A text field for the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone**: A text field for the contact person's phone number.
* **Address**: A text field for the physical address of your company.
* **Zip Code**: A text field for the zip code of your company.
* **Date of Incorporation**: A date field for the date of incorporation, if applicable.
* **Date of Establishment**: A date field for the date of establishment, if applicable.
* **Tax Id**: A text field for the tax ID number.
* **Registration Number**: A text field for the registration number.
* **Website**: A text field for the website of your company.
* **Description**: A text field for a brief description of your company.
* **Bank Name**: A text field for the name of your bank.
* **Account Number**: A text field for the account number of your bank.
* **Tax Id Country**: A text field for the country of your tax ID.
* **IBAN**: A text field for the IBAN number of your bank.
* **SWIFT**: A text field for the SWIFT number of your bank.
* **Business License**: A text field for the business license of your company.
* **Tax License**: A text field for the tax license of your company.
* **Business Registration Date**: A date field for the business registration date, if applicable.
* **Tax Registration Date**: A date field for the tax registration date, if applicable.
* **Note**: A text field for any additional notes.

## Tips
