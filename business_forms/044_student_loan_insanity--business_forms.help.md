# Student Loan Insanity - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect demographic and contact information from students for unknown purposes, likely related to a student loan or financial service. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your information is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Confirm your email and phone number to ensure accuracy.
3. Select your country of residence.
4. Agree to the terms and conditions.
5. Choose whether you want to download something or not.
6. Optionally, you can opt out of email communications.
7. Confirm your opt out of email communications.
8. Select whether you want to download something or not (optional).
9. Fill out order details if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, optional):
	+ Re-enter your email address to confirm accuracy.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Re-enter your phone number to confirm accuracy.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required):
	+ Select your country of residence.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions** (`agree`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Accept the terms and conditions.
* **Download** (`download`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose whether you want to download something or not.
* **Opt out of Email** (`email_optout`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose whether you don't want to receive email communications.
* **Confirm Opt out of Email** (`confirm_email_optout`, select_one, optional):
	+ Reconfirm your opt out of email communications.
* **Confirm Download** (`confirm_download`, select_one, optional):
	+ Reconfirm your download choice.
* **Order Name** (`order_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name for the order.
* **Order Email** (`order_email`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the email address for the order.
* **Order Phone** (`order_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number for the order.
* **Order Country** (`order_country`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the country of residence for the order.
* **Order Address** (`order_address`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the address for the order.
* **Order State** (`order_state`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the state for the order.
* **Order City** (`order_city`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the city for the order.
* **Order Postal** (`order_postal`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the postal code for the order.
* **Order Zip** (`order_zip`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the zip code for the order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional):
	+ Enter notes for the order.
* **Order File** (`order_file`, text, optional):
	+ Enter a file name or link for the order.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Enter comments for the order.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required):
	+ Finalize your submission.
* **Confirm Submission** (`confirm_submision`, select_one, required):
	+ Confirm your submission to complete the form.
