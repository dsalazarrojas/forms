# Warranty Tracking Dashboard - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Warranty Tracking Dashboard form is designed to track and manage warranty information for customers. This form helps business users to enter and update details about customer warranties, claims, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant information for each field to provide accurate and complete data.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as required) and leave the optional fields blank if not applicable.
3. Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Warranty Info (1)** (`warranty_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the warranty or note.
* **Claim Info (2)** (`claim_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the claim or issue.
* **Customer Info (3)** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the customer or their purchase.
* **Customer Details (4)** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the customer, such as their contact details (e.g., address, phone number).
* **Warranty Status (5)** (`warranty_status`, select_one, optional): Select whether the warranty is Active or Inactive.
* **Customer Contact (6)** (`customer_contact`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the customer has provided contact information (Yes or No).
* **Claim Status (7)** (`claim_status`, select_one, optional): Select whether the claim is Active or Inactive.
* **Claim Details (8)** (`claim_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the claim or issue.
* **Warranty Start Date (9)** (`warranty_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the warranty.
* **Warranty End Date (10)** (`warranty_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the warranty.
* **Warranty Duration (11)** (`warranty_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the warranty in days or months.
* **Claim Date (12)** (`claim_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the claim or issue.
* **Claim Amount (13)** (`claim_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the claim.
* **Customer Email (14)** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address (if provided).
* **Customer Phone (15)** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number (if provided).
* **Customer Phone (16)** (redundant field, text, optional): This field seems to be a redundant field and can be ignored. Please enter in **Customer Phone (15)** instead.
* **Notes (17)** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the customer or warranty.

## Tips

* Please ensure to keep the form up-to-date and accurate for each customer.
* Review and update warranty status regularly to prevent expired or inactive warranties.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
