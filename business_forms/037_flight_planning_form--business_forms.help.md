# Flight Planning Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Flight Planning Form is used to collect information for planning and organizing flights. This form helps to gather essential details such as flight plan, route start and end points, dates, and times, as well as notes and assigned users. It is designed to be completed by users to provide a clear and concise overview of flight plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Route Start**: Enter the starting point of the flight.
	* **Route End**: Enter the ending point of the flight.
	* **Date**: Enter the date of the flight.
	* **Time**: Enter the time of the flight.
	* **Flight Plan Details**: Enter a detailed description of the flight plan.
2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* **Flight Plan**: Enter a brief summary of the flight plan.
	* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or observations about the flight.
	* **Assigned Users**: Select users who will be assigned to this flight plan.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Route Start**: Enter the starting point of the flight. This is a crucial field and should reflect the actual starting point of the flight.
* **Route End**: Enter the ending point of the flight. This field is also critical as it defines the destination of the flight.
* **Start Point**: Enter the specific starting location of the route, such as an airport code (e.g., IAD) or a geographical location (e.g., New York City).
* **Date**: Enter the date of the flight in standard date format (e.g., 2023-02-15).
* **Time**: Enter the time of the flight in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Flight Plan Details**: Enter a detailed description of the flight plan, including any important information about the flight.
* **Flight Plan**: Enter a brief summary of the flight plan.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or observations about the flight.
* **Date**: This field is used to store the date of the flight.
* **Time**: This field is used to store the time of the flight.
* **Assigned Users**: Select users who will be assigned to this flight plan. You can select multiple users by checking the boxes next to their names.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields as they are essential for planning and organizing flights.
* Use the **Date** and **Time** fields to enter precise information for accurate flight planning.
* The **Flight Plan Details** field is a crucial field that provides a detailed description of the flight plan.
* The **Assigned Users** field allows you to assign multiple users to the flight plan.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
