# Leadership Readiness Consulting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Readiness Consulting Request Form is a tool to collect information from users requesting leadership consulting services. This form is used to assess the user's consulting needs and provide guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please provide your contact information, including the Contact Person, Company, Job Title, and Department.
* Enter your email address in the Email field.
* Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
* Select the date and time you are available for the consulting session.
* Choose the leadership consulting need that best describes your requirement (Executive Coaching, Leadership Development, Change Management, Talent Management, or Other).
* If you have any additional needs or requirements not listed above, please describe them in the Other field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and title in this field.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's name in this field.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title in this field.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Enter the department you belong to in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address in this field.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you are available for the consulting session.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time you are available for the consulting session.
* **Leadership Consulting Need** (`leadership_consulting_need`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the leadership consulting need that best describes your requirement.
	+ Executive Coaching: You need executive coaching to improve your leadership skills.
	+ Leadership Development: You need guidance on leadership development to enhance your team's performance.
	+ Change Management: You need help with managing change in your organization.
	+ Talent Management: You need guidance on talent management to improve employee engagement.
	+ Other: Select this option if you have any other specific consulting needs.
* **Other** (`other`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional needs or requirements not listed above, please describe them in this field.
