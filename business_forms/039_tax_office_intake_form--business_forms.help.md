# Tax Office Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Office Intake Form is a crucial document that helps the Tax Office gather essential business details for official purposes. Businesses are required to complete this form to provide their office information, ensuring that their records are accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of business you represent (e.g., Business A or Business B) from the "business_type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your office name in the "office_name" field.
3. Provide your office address in the "office_address" field.
4. Enter your office phone number in the "office_phone" field.
5. If you have a referral request, select the date and time of the request in the "referral_request_date" and "referral_request_time" fields.
6. Provide your email address for communication with the Tax Office in the "tax_office_email" field.
7. Enter your phone number for the Tax Office to contact you in the "tax_office_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of business you represent (e.g., Business A or Business B).
* **Office Name** (`office_name`, text, required): Enter your office name accurately and completely.
* **Office Address** (`office_address`, text, required): Provide your office address for official purposes.
* **Office Phone** (`office_phone`, text, required): Enter your office phone number for communication with the Tax Office.
* **Referral Request Date** (`referral_request_date`, date, optional): If you have a referral request, enter the date of the request.
* **Referral Request Time** (`referral_request_time`, time, optional): If you have a referral request, enter the time of the request.
* **Tax Office Email** (`tax_office_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for official communication with the Tax Office.
* **Tax Office Phone** (`tax_office_phone`, text, optional): Enter your Tax Office phone number for communication purposes.
