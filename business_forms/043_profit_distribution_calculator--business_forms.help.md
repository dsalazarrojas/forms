# Profit Distribution Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Profit Distribution Calculator is designed to help businesses or individuals distribute profits among multiple partners or stakeholders in a fair and transparent way.

## How To Complete This Form
To use the Profit Distribution Calculator, follow these steps:
1. Input the names of all partners or stakeholders in the corresponding fields (Partner 1, Partner 2, ..., Partner 10).
2. Enter the allocation percentage for each partner in the corresponding fields (Partner 1 Allocation, Partner 2 Allocation, ..., Partner 10 Allocation).
3. Click on the "Calculate" button to calculate the total allocation, remaining allocation, and profit.

## Tips
* Make sure to input all partner names and allocation percentages correctly to avoid errors.
* Double-check the calculations before closing the form.
* This form is designed to handle a maximum of 10 partners. If you have more partners, you will need to create multiple instances of this form or use a different method to distribute profit.
