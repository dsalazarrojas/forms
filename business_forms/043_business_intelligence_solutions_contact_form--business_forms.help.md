# Business Intelligence Solutions Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and project requirements from businesses interested in business intelligence solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the options for each field based on your preferences.
3. Ensure that you've selected all relevant fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`prospect_full_name_bi`): Enter your full name as it appears on any official documents or correspondence.
- **Professional Title** (`prospect_professional_title_bi`): Provide your professional title or job function, e.g., Data Analyst, IT Manager, Director of Operations.
- **Company** (`prospect_company_name_bi`): Enter the name of your company.
- **Current BI Solution in Use** (`current_bi_solution_in_use_choice`): Select the current business intelligence solution you're using, such as Spreadsheets only, Legacy System, or Modern SaaS Tool.
- **Data Integration Interest** (`data_integration_interest_choice`): Indicate the level of focus on data consolidation, e.g., High, Moderate, or Low.
- **Data Volume** (`estimated_data_volume_category`): Select the estimated size of your total dataset for analysis, e.g., Small, Medium, Large, or Big Data.
- **Preferred Features** (`preferred_bi_features_selection`): Select the most important capabilities you're looking for in a business intelligence solution, e.g., Automated Reporting, Real-time Dashboards, Predictive Analytics / AI, Mobile Access, Self-service Analytics, or Embedding Capabilities.
- **Timeline for Implementation** (`implementation_timeline_expectation`): Choose the expected timeline for implementing the business intelligence solution, e.g., Immediately, 1 - 3 Months, 3 - 6 Months, or Just researching.
- **Best Time for Discovery Call** (`discovery_call_time_preference`): Select the best time slot for a discovery call, e.g., Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
- **Brief Project Summary** (`bi_project_summary_brief_text`): Provide a brief overview of your specific project goals and requirements.
- **Submission Date** (`bi_contact_form_date_completed`): Ensure you select the correct submission date.
- **How did you hear about our solutions** (`bi_prospect_referral_source`): If you'd like to share, tell us how you heard about our business intelligence solutions.
