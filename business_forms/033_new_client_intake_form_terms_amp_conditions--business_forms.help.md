# new_client_intake_form_terms_amp_conditions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from new clients about the terms and conditions that apply to their use of services. This form ensures that clients understand and agree to these terms and conditions before proceeding with our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each field carefully and provide the required information.
2. Ensure you understand the meaning of each field before submitting your response.
3. Fill in the form accurately and completely to avoid any issues with your application or services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **business_form** (`business_form`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your business in a few words. Please enter a short description of your business, such as its name or type of industry.
* **business_forms** (business_forms, text, optional): This field is for you to list the specific forms or contracts that you have already signed or are planning to sign. Please provide a list of the forms or contracts that you are referring to.
* **business_forms** (business_forms, text, optional): This field is another instance of "business forms" field and will likely be removed after review and validation. Please treat it as if it were the same field as "business_form".

**Note**: There are three instances of "business_forms" with different IDs (4, 5, 6), but they serve the same purpose. To avoid confusion, it is recommended to fill only the first field "business_form" and leave the others blank.
* **_no_label_** (no_label, text, optional): This field does not have a label, and its purpose is not clear. It is likely an error in the form design and can be ignored for now.
