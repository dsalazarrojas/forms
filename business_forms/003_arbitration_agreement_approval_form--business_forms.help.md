# Arbitration Agreement Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for parties to provide approval and acknowledgment of their involvement in the arbitration process. It ensures that both parties have agreed on the terms and conditions of the arbitration agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "First Party Name" field (Field 2).
2. Select your role in the arbitration process from the "First Party Role" dropdown menu (Field 3).
3. Enter the name of the other party involved in the arbitration (Field 4).
4. Select the role of the other party from the "Second Party Role" dropdown menu (Field 5).
5. Briefly describe the dispute in the "Dispute Description" field (Field 7).
6. Select the method of arbitrator selection from the "Arbitrator Selection Method" dropdown menu (Field 9).
7. Enter the date the arbitration agreement is being approved (Field 8).
8. Choose the jurisdiction or governing law for the arbitration (Field 10).
9. Select your approval status for the agreement from the "First Party Approval" dropdown menu (Field 12).
10. Select the approval status for the other party from the "Second Party Approval" dropdown menu (Field 13).
11. If applicable, indicate if the agreement has been reviewed by legal counsel (Field 14).
12. Add any additional notes or conditions in the "Additional Notes" field (Field 15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Party Name** (Field 2, text): Enter the name of the party submitting the arbitration agreement for approval.
* **First Party Role** (Field 3, select_one): Select the role of the party submitting the agreement (Claimant, Respondent, Plaintiff, Defendant, or Other Party).
* **Second Party Name** (Field 4, text): Enter the name of the other party involved in the arbitration.
* **Second Party Role** (Field 5, select_one): Select the role of the other party (Claimant, Respondent, Plaintiff, Defendant, or Other Party).
* **Dispute Description** (Field 7, text): Briefly describe the matter in dispute.
* **Agreement Date** (Field 8, date): Enter the date the arbitration agreement is being approved.
* **Arbitrator Selection Method** (Field 9, select_one): Select the method of arbitrator selection (Mutually agreed arbitrator, Single arbitrator appointed, Three arbitrator panel, or To be determined).
* **Jurisdiction** (Field 10, text): Enter the geographic area or governing law for the arbitration.
* **First Party Approval** (Field 12, select_one): Select your approval status for the agreement (Approved, Pending, Rejected, or Conditional).
* **Second Party Approval** (Field 13, select_one): Select the approval status for the other party (Approved, Pending, Rejected, or Conditional).
* **Legal counsel has reviewed agreement** (Field 14, select_one): Indicate if the agreement has been reviewed by legal counsel (True, False, or Not Applicable).
* **Additional Notes** (Field 15, text): Add any additional notes or conditions related to the agreement.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by both parties involved in the arbitration process. The fields marked as "required" must be completed.
