# Trademark Search Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trademark Search Inquiry Form is designed for businesses to submit a query for a trademark search. This form helps the trademark office process your request efficiently and accurately. Please fill out this form with the required information to ensure a successful search.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the required fields marked with an *.
2.  Select the correct options for the "Products/Services" and "Trademark Status" fields.
3.  Make sure to enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name**: <mark>Enter the name of your business as it appears on official documents and records.</mark>
* **First Name**: <mark>Enter your first name.</mark>
* **Last Name**: <mark>(Optional) Enter your last name.</mark>
* **Email**: <mark>Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.</mark>
* **Phone**: <mark>Enter your business's phone number where you can be reached.</mark>
* **Description**: <mark>Provide a brief description of your business.</mark>
* **Categories**: <mark>Select the categories that best describe your business. You can choose multiple categories.</mark>
*   <list>
      *   Electronics
      *   Food and Beverage
      *   Fashion
      *   Home Goods
      *   Medical Equipment
      *   Software
      *   Toys
      *   Transportation
  </list>
* **Products/Services**: <mark>Choose the type of product or service your business provides.</mark>
*   <list>
      *   Products
      *   Services
  </list>
* **Location**: <mark>Enter your business's location or the location where you operate.</mark>
* **Trademark Status**: <mark>Select the current status of your trademark application or registration.</mark>
*   <list>
      *   Applied
      *   Registered
      *   Cancelled
  </list>

## Tips
*   Please double-check all fields before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that all mandatory fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays.
*   If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please contact our support team for assistance.
