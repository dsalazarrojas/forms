# Business Expense Policy Review Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Expense Policy Review Registration form is designed to collect information from business expense policy attendees, such as their details, preferences, and interests, to ensure they receive a tailored experience. This form helps the organizers understand who will be attending, what they want to learn, and what accommodations may be needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Attendee Details, including your first name, last name, business email, phone number, company name, department, job title, role type, and preferred session date.
2. Choose your preferred session time: Morning (9am-12pm), Afternoon (1pm-4pm), Full Day, or No Preference.
3. Select the delivery format you prefer: In-Person, Virtual, Hybrid, or No Preference.
4. Indicate which policy topics interest you: Expense Policy Overview, Travel Expenses, Meal and Entertainment, Reimbursement Process, Technology Tools, Compliance and Auditing, or Fraud Prevention.
5. Select your experience level with expense policies: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
6. If you manage budgets, choose 'True', 'False', or 'Partial Responsibility'.
7. If you have any special accommodations, such as accessibility needs, please describe them.
8. If you have specific learning goals, please describe them.
9. If there are other attendees from your company, indicate how many.
10. If you have dietary preferences for catered meals, select Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, or None.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company name.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter your department name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
* **Role Type** (`role_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose your role type: Manager, Employee, Finance, HR, Executive, or Other.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required): Choose a preferred session date.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, `select_one`, required): Choose a preferred session time: Morning (9am-12pm), Afternoon (1pm-4pm), Full Day, or No Preference.
* **Delivery Format** (`delivery_format`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred delivery format: In-Person, Virtual, Hybrid, or No Preference.
* **Policy Topics of Interest** (`policy_review_topics`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the policy topics that interest you.
* **Experience with Expense Policies** (`policy_experience`, `select_one`, required): Choose your experience level with expense policies: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
* **Budget Responsibility** (`budget_responsibility`, `select_one`, optional): If you manage budgets, choose 'True', 'False', or 'Partial Responsibility'.
* **Special Accommodations** (`special_needs`, `text`, optional): If you have any special accommodations, such as accessibility needs, please describe them.
* **Learning Goals** (`learning_goals`, `text`, optional): If you have specific learning goals, please describe them.
* **Additional Attendees from Company** (`additional_attendees`, `number`, optional): If there are other attendees from your company, indicate how many.
* **Dietary Preferences** (`dietary_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have dietary preferences for catered meals, select Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, or None.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the Business Expense Policy Review Registration form accurately and effectively.
