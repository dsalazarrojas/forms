# Work From Home Activity Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work From Home Activity Report Form is designed to collect information from employees about their work from home activities, allowing HR or management to track and review their work from home status, meetings, and work hours.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Business Trips" field to indicate if you have any business trips planned or completed.
2. Choose your "Working From Home" status for the given period.
3. Provide any personal notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
4. Enter the date you are reporting for.
5. Enter the total hours you worked.
6. Enter the number of breaks you took.
7. Select the "Business Meetings" field to indicate if you have any business meetings during this period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Trips** (`business_trips`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all business trips you have planned or completed during the reported period.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have any business trips scheduled or completed.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not have any business trips.
- **Working From Home** (`working_from_home`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you were working from home during the reported period.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you were working from home.
	+ Select 'No' if you were not working from home.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Write any personal notes or comments about your work from home experience.
	+ Be concise and clear in your notes.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you are reporting for.
	+ Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you worked.
	+ Use the format HH:MM AM/PM.
- **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, required/optional): Enter the total hours you worked.
	+ Use numbers only (e.g., 8, 10, 12).
- **Breaks** (`breaks`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of breaks you took.
	+ Use numbers only (e.g., 2, 3, 5).
- **Business Meetings** (`business_meetings`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all business meetings you have during the reported period.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have any business meetings scheduled or completed.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not have any business meetings.
