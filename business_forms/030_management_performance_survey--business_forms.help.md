# Management Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Management Performance Survey is a form used to gather feedback on the performance of employees who have been assigned tasks or projects. This form helps assessors evaluate an employee's strengths and weaknesses, providing valuable insights for employee development and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with the required information, making sure to complete all fields marked as required (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2.  Select the correct performance level for the employee from the options provided in the "Performance Level" field.
3.  Provide specific examples or descriptions of the employee's strengths and weaknesses in the corresponding fields.
4.  Add any additional comments or feedback you may have for the employee.
5.  Enter the employee's email address and phone number for further contact.
6.  Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee being assessed.
*   **Performance Level** (`performance_level`, select_one, required): Select one of the options provided to indicate the employee's performance level. The options are:
    *   Below Average
    *   Average
    *   Above Average
    *   Excellent
    *   Exceptional
*   **Strengths** (`strengths`, text, optional): Describe the employee's strengths, providing specific examples or characteristics that demonstrate their abilities.
*   **Weaknesses** (`weaknesses`, text, optional): Describe the employee's weaknesses, providing specific examples or characteristics that may hinder their performance.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any general comments or feedback for the employee.
*   **Assessor** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person assessing the employee's performance.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the employee's email address for further contact.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the employee's phone number for further contact.
*   **Additional Comments** (`comments_1`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback for the employee.
