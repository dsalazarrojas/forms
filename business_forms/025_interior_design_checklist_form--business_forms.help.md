# interior_design_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for interior design projects, ensuring that all necessary details are captured and communicated effectively between clients and designers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field. Note that some fields are optional, and you can skip them if not applicable.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, optional): A brief description of the project, including its scope and objectives.
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, `number`, optional): Enter the client's contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses.
* **Budget** (`budget_info`, `number`, optional): Enter the project's budget.
* **Style** (`style_info`, `select_one`, optional): Select the desired style for the project (Modern, Contemporary, Traditional, or Mid-Century).
* **Goals** (`goals`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all goals that apply to the project (Improve functionality, Aesthetics, Budget-friendliness, or Timeless design).
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, `date`, optional): Enter the project's start and end dates.
* **Preferred Designers** (`preferred_designers`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select preferred designers for the project (Designer A, Designer B, or Designer C).

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your designer or project manager for clarification.
* This form is designed to be flexible and adaptable to various project requirements. If you need to add or modify fields, please consult with your designer or project manager first.
