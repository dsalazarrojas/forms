# Employee Productivity Innovation Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Productivity Innovation Grant Application Form is designed to collect information from employees about their ideas for innovative projects that can improve productivity in the workplace. This form is used to assess the viability of each project and determine whether it aligns with the company's goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Project Details section.
2. Provide a brief description of your project, including its expected outcomes.
3. Specify how much funding is needed to support your project.
4. Enter your contact information in the Applicant Information section.
5. Choose the location where your project will be based (New York, San Francisco, or Chicago).
6. Enter the start and end dates of your project.
7. Finally, estimate the number of hours you expect to spend on your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): A brief description of your project and its expected outcomes.
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, optional): Describe what you expect to achieve with your project.
* **Funding Needs** (`funding_needs`, text, optional): Specify the funding required for your project.
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, title, and contact information.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, select_one, optional): Choose the city where your project will be based (New York, San Francisco, or Chicago).
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Project Hours** (`project_hours`, number, optional): Estimate the number of hours you expect to spend on your project.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide clear and concise information about your project, including its expected outcomes and funding needs.
* Choose the correct location for your project from the options provided.
* Use dates according to your company's date format.
* Estimate accurately the number of hours you expect to spend on your project.
* Complete all required fields, but feel free to add extra information if you think it will help assess your project's viability.
* Proofread your form carefully before submitting it to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
