# Auto Repair Invoice Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Repair Invoice Form is a tool used by shops to gather the necessary information for billing and accounting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately and efficiently, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Shop Name** and **Invoice Number** in fields 2 and 3, ensuring they are correct and unique.
2. Input the **Invoice Date** in field 4.
3. Enter the **Customer Name**, **Vehicle Make, Model, and Year**, and **VIN or License Plate** in fields 6-8, ensuring the vehicle information is correct.
4. Describe the **Description of Service** performed on the vehicle in field 10.
5. Enter the **Price of Parts**, **Labor Hours**, and **Labor Rate per Hour** for each service in fields 11-13.
6. Calculate the **Parts Total**, **Labor Total**, and **Tax Amount** in fields 15-17.
7. Ensure the **Grand Total Due** is accurate, including tax.
8. Select the **Payment Status** of the invoice in field 19, indicating whether it has been paid, due on receipt, or has a net payment term.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shop Name** (Field 2, Text, Required): Enter the name of your service facility.
* **Invoice Number** (Field 3, Text, Required): Enter the unique billing ID for this invoice.
* **Invoice Date** (Field 4, Date, Required): Enter the date the invoice was issued.
* **Customer Name** (Field 6, Text, Required): Enter the name of the person or business being billed.
* **Vehicle Make, Model, and Year** (Field 7, Text, Required): Enter the correct vehicle details.
* **VIN or License Plate** (Field 8, Text, Required): Enter the vehicle's identification number for accurate verification.
* **Description of Service** (Field 10, Text, Required): Describe the work performed on the vehicle.
* **Price of Parts** (Field 11, Number, Required): Enter the total cost of parts for the service.
* **Labor Hours** (Field 12, Number, Required): Enter the total hours spent on the service.
* **Labor Rate per Hour** (Field 13, Number, Required): Enter the standard shop rate.
* **Parts Total** (Field 15, Number, Required): Calculate the sum of all parts costs.
* **Labor Total** (Field 16, Number, Required): Calculate the sum of all labor costs.
* **Tax Amount** (Field 17, Number, Required): Enter the applicable sales tax.
* **Grand Total Due** (Field 18, Number, Required): Ensure the total amount due, including tax.
* **Payment Status** (Field 19, Select One, Required): Select the current state of the invoice: Paid, Due on Receipt, Net 15, or Net 30.
