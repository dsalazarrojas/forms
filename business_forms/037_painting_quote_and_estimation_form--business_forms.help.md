# Painting, Quote And Estimation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Painting, Quote And Estimation Form is a tool used to gather information from customers about their painting project needs. It will help the customer service team to understand the project scope, provide a quote and estimation for the project, and assign the appropriate tools and resources needed to complete the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "User Input" field with your project details.
2. Select your preferred contact time for the customer service team.
3. Provide additional project details if needed.
4. Select the service details required for the project.
5. Choose the project type.
6. Indicate whether you would like to be contacted about the project details.
7. Select the assigned tool for the project.
8. Upload or provide the output file.
9. If applicable, select the category for the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (text, required/optional: optional)
	+ This field is used to capture any additional information you would like to provide about your project.
* **Preferred Contact Time** (text, required/optional: optional)
	+ Please select your preferred time of day for the customer service team to contact you about your project.
* **Project Details** (text, required/optional: optional)
	+ Add any additional details about your project that you think will help us better understand your needs.
* **Service Details** (text, required/optional: optional)
	+ Provide details about the services you need for your project.
* **Project Type** (text, required/optional: optional)
	+ Select the type of project you are working on.
* **Contact Details** (select_multiple, required/optional: optional)
	+ Indicate whether you would like to be contacted about your project details.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_one, required/optional: optional)
	+ Select whether you would like to assign a specific tool to your project.
* **Output File** (text, required/optional: optional)
	+ Upload or provide the output file for your project.
* **Form ID** (number, required/optional: optional)
	+ Enter a unique number for your project.
* **Category** (select_one, required/optional: optional)
	+ Select the category for your project.
* **Category** (select_one, required/optional: optional)
