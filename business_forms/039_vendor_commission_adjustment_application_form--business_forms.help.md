# Vendor Commission Adjustment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Commission Adjustment Application Form is used to request adjustments to vendor commissions. This form should only be used for valid reasons and to avoid duplication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Select the correct commission type that applies to the adjustment.
3. Provide a clear and concise reason for the adjustment.
4. Enter the amount of commission to be adjusted.
5. Enter the date the adjustment was made.
6. Enter any additional comments about the adjustment (optional).
7. Select the status of the commission adjustment (approved, denied, or pending).
8. Enter the date the commission adjustment was submitted (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Commission Type** (`commission_type`, select_one): Select the type of commission that applies to the adjustment.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text): Enter the name of the vendor associated with the commission adjustment.
* **Reason for Adjustment** (`reason_for_adjustment`, text): Provide a clear and concise reason for the adjustment.
* **Commission Amount** (`commission_amount`, number): Enter the amount of commission to be adjusted.
* **Adjustment Amount** (`adjustment_amount`, number): Enter the amount of the adjustment.
* **Date Adjustment Made** (`date_adjustment_made`, date): Enter the date the adjustment was made.
* **Comment** (`comment`, note): Enter any additional comments about the adjustment (optional).
* **Vendor Commission Adjustment Approval Date** (`vendor_commission_adjustment_approval_date`, date): Enter the date the commission adjustment was approved (if applicable).
* **Vendor Commission Adjustment Status** (`vendor_commission_adjustment_status`, select_one): Select the status of the commission adjustment (approved, denied, or pending).
* **Form Page 2 Submission Date** (`form_page_2_submission_date`, date): Enter the date the form was submitted (if applicable).
