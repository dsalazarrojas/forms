# outdoor_generator_soundproofing_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users apply for soundproofing solutions for their outdoor generators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the "Main" page, which is the starting point for the form.
2. Fill in the required fields as you navigate through the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main (text)** (`Main`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the outdoor generator and its location.
* **Photos (Select Multiple)** (`photos`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, upload photos of the outdoor generator to support your application. Choose 'Yes' to indicate that you have photos, and select 'No' to indicate that you do not have photos.
* **Site Details (text)** (`site_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the site where the outdoor generator is located.
