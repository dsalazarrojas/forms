# tattoos_consent_2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect consent for tattooing procedures. It is designed to gather information from clients about their personal details and tattoo specifics, and to obtain their agreement to proceed with the tattooing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "User Details" page.
2. Enter the specifics of the tattoo you would like to get, in the "Tattoo Details" page.
3. Read and understand the "Consent" page, and sign below to indicate your consent for the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details (Page 1)** (`User Details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name and any relevant personal details you'd like to share with us.
* **Tattoo Details (Page 2)** (`Tattoo Details`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the type and specifics of the tattoo you'd like to get. This includes any design, size, color, and location on your body.
* **Consent (Page 3)** (`Consent`, `note`, required): Please read the consent statement carefully, and sign below to indicate that you understand and agree to the terms. This is a crucial step before proceeding with the tattooing process.
