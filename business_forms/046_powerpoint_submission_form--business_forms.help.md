# PowerPoint Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a PowerPoint submission, including the form title, description, category, and various supporting details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with a brief and descriptive title of the PowerPoint presentation.
2. Provide a detailed **Description** of the PowerPoint presentation in the text area.
3. Select the **Category** of the form from the dropdown menu, either **Business Forms** or **Personal Forms**.
4. Indicate whether you are uploading **Files** associated with the presentation.
5. Enter any relevant information for **File 1**, **File 2**, and **File 3** if needed.
6. If applicable, enter the **Upload** information for any additional files.
7. If you are associated with the presentation, enter your **Phone** number.
8. Enter your **Email** address for contact purposes.
9. Choose the **Date** and **Time** of the presentation.
10. Enter any additional **Notes** regarding the presentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (1, text, required): Enter a brief and descriptive title for the PowerPoint presentation.
* **Description** (2, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the PowerPoint presentation.
* **Category** (3, select_one, required): Select the category for the PowerPoint presentation as either **Business Forms** or **Personal Forms**.
* **Files** (4, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you are uploading files associated with the presentation.
* **File 1** (5, text, required): Enter any relevant information for the first file.
* **File 2** (6, text, required): Enter any relevant information for the second file.
* **File 3** (7, text, required): Enter any relevant information for the third file.
* **Upload** (8, text, optional): Enter any additional upload information if needed.
* **Phone** (9, text, optional): Enter your phone number if associated with the presentation.
* **Email** (10, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Date** (11, date, optional): Choose the date of the presentation.
* **Time** (12, time, optional): Choose the time of the presentation.
* **Note** (13, note, optional): Enter any additional notes regarding the presentation.
* **Notes** (14, note, optional): Enter any additional notes regarding the presentation.
* **Submit** (15, text, optional): Once you have filled in all the required fields, click **Submit** to complete and submit the form.
