# Animal Facility Design Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from businesses or organizations that wish to partner with us on animal facility design projects. It helps us assess your company's qualifications, experience, and capabilities in providing design services related to animal facilities. Please answer the questions accurately and thoroughly to demonstrate your suitability for partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the guidelines below:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Answer questions clearly and concisely, providing examples or supporting documentation when necessary.
3. Select all relevant options that apply to your business or organization.
4. Ensure your answers are accurate and up-to-date.
5. Proofread your form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person Full Name (\*):** Enter the name of the primary contact person for your business or organization.
* **Email Address (\*):** Enter your company's email address, which will be used for communication and correspondence.
* **Phone Number (\*):** Enter your company's phone number, including the country code.
* **Company Name (\*):** Enter your company's name, which will be used to identify your business or organization.
* **Company Website:** Enter your company's website URL, if applicable.
* **Animal Facility Design Partnership Application:** This is a free-form text field where you can describe your interest in partnering with us on animal facility design projects.
* **Company Address (\*):** Enter your company's street address, city, state, and country.
* **Type of Organization (\*):** Select the type of organization you represent (e.g., Architecture firm, Design studio, etc.).
* **Years in Business (\*):** Select the number of years your business or organization has been in operation.
* **Company Size (\*):** Select the number of employees in your business or organization.
* **Type of Partnership Sought (\*):** Select the type of partnership you seek (e.g., Design collaboration, Construction partnership, etc.).
* **Types of Animal Facilities You Have Designed (\*):** Select all types of animal facilities you have designed (e.g., Veterinary clinics, Animal shelters, etc.).
* **Relevant Portfolio Projects:** Describe your relevant portfolio projects, including examples and details.
* **Design Philosophy for Animal Facilities:** Explain your approach to design for animal facilities.
* **Animal Welfare Knowledge (\*):** Select your level of knowledge with animal welfare standards.
* **Relevant Certifications:** If applicable, list any relevant certifications or professional credentials.
* **Services Offered (\*):** Select all services you can provide (e.g., Conceptual design, Architectural design, etc.).
* **Geographic Areas Served (\*):** Enter the geographic areas you serve.
* **Annual Project Capacity (\*):** Select the number of projects you can handle annually.
* **Typical Project Budget Range:** Select the typical budget range for your projects.
* **Typical Project Timeline:** Select the typical duration of your projects.
* **Sustainable Design Focus:** Select whether you incorporate sustainable design practices.
* **Technology Integration Experience:** Select your level of experience with technology integration in facilities.
* **Client References:** List any client references or testimonials.
* **Why Partner with Us:** Explain why you want to partner with us.
* **Value Proposition:** Describe the strengths and value you bring to partnerships.
* **Availability to Start Partnership:** Select when you can start a partnership.
* **Additional Information:** If necessary, provide any additional information that may be helpful for our review process.
* **Applicant Signature (\*):** Sign your name, typing it as your full name.
* **Date:** Date of submission.

## Tips

* Be honest about your experience and qualifications.
* Proofread your form before submission to ensure accuracy.
* Provide supporting documentation or examples when necessary.
* Answer questions clearly and concisely.
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
