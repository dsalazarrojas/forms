# hazardous_material_handling_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the handling and declaration of hazardous materials.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the business name in the `Business Name` field. This is the name of your company or organization that handles hazardous materials.
2. Enter the contact person's name and email address in the `Contact Person` and `Email` fields, respectively.
3. Enter the phone number in the `Phone` field.
4. Enter the address of the location where the hazardous materials are handled in the `Address` field.
5. Indicate whether hazardous materials are handled in the `Hazardous Materials` field.
6. Enter any relevant handling instructions in the `Handling Instructions` field.
7. Enter the certification information in the `Certification` field.
8. Enter the date of the declaration in the `Declaration Date` field.
9. Enter the name of the person submitting the declaration in the `Submitted By` field.
10. Enter the date the declaration was submitted in the `Submitted On` field.
11. Enter the business ID in the `Business ID` field.
12. Enter a brief description of the hazardous materials being handled in the `Hazardous Materials Description` field.
13. Enter the location where the hazardous materials are being handled in the `Handling Location` field.
14. Enter the capacity for handling hazardous materials in the `Handling Capacity` field.
15. Indicate whether a declaration has been made in the `Declaration Type` field.
16. Enter the declaration number in the `Declaration Number` field.
17. Enter the business license number in the `Business License` field.
18. Indicate the status of the declaration in the `Declaration Status` field.
19. If applicable, enter any additional notes about the declaration in the `Notes` field.
20. Enter the certification number in the `Certification Number` field.
21. Enter the date of submission in the `Submission Date` field.
22. Enter the certification expiration date in the `Certification Expiration Date` field, if applicable.
23. Indicate the status of the certification in the `Certification Status` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (1): Enter the name of your company or organization that handles hazardous materials.
* **Contact Person** (2): Enter the name of the person who will be handling the hazardous materials.
* **Email** (3): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (4): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Address** (5): Enter the address of the location where the hazardous materials are handled.
* **Hazardous Materials** (6): Indicate whether hazardous materials are handled.
* **Handling Instructions** (7): Enter any relevant handling instructions for the hazardous materials.
* **Certification** (8): Enter the certification information.
* **Declaration Date** (9): Enter the date of the declaration.
* **Submitted By** (10): Enter the name of the person submitting the declaration.
* **Submitted On** (11): Enter the date the declaration was submitted.
* **Business ID** (12): Enter the business ID.
* **Hazardous Materials Description** (13): Enter a brief description of the hazardous materials being handled.
* **Handling Location** (14): Enter the location where the hazardous materials are being handled.
* **Handling Capacity** (15): Enter the capacity for handling hazardous materials.
* **Declaration Type** (16): Indicate whether a declaration has been made.
* **Declaration Number** (17): Enter the declaration number.
* **Business License** (18): Enter the business license number.
* **Declaration Status** (19): Indicate the status of the declaration.
* **Notes** (20): If applicable, enter any additional notes about the declaration.
* **Certification Number** (21): Enter the certification number.
* **Submission Date** (22): Enter the date of submission.
* **Certification Expiration Date** (23): Enter the certification expiration date, if applicable.
* **Certification Status** (24): Indicate the status of the certification.
