# Vendor Order Confirmation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from vendors about their order confirmation, ensuring accurate record-keeping and streamlined communication in business settings. Fill out this form to provide detailed information about your order, and our team will review and process it promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Order Details** section with all necessary information about your order, such as order number, description, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your **Vendor Info** in the designated field, including your company name, contact name, and any other relevant contact information.
3. Select a **Delivery Date** for your order from the date picker or enter a specific date in the format MM/DD/YY.
4. Enter any **Payment Terms** in the text field, detailing any special payment instructions or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (`order_details`, text, optional):** Enter all necessary information about your order, such as order number, description, and any other relevant details.
* **Vendor Info (`vendor_info`, text, optional):** Provide your company name, contact name, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Delivery Date (`delivery_date`, date, optional):** Select a date for your order from the date picker or enter a specific date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Payment Terms (`payment_terms`, text, optional):** Enter any special payment instructions or requirements for your order.
