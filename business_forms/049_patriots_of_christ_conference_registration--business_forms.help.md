# patriots_of_christ_conference_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect registrations for the Patriots of Christ Conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the name of the conference organizer from the "Organizer" field.
5. Select additional organizers (if any) from the "Organisers" field.
6. Choose the date of the conference from the "Date" field.
7. Choose the time of the conference from the "Time" field.
8. Leave a note about your attendance (if any) in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Organizer** (`select_one_organiser`, select_one, required: false): Select the name of the conference organizer.
* **Organisers** (`select_multiple_organisers`, select_multiple, required: false): Select additional conference organizers (if any).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Choose the date of the conference.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Choose the time of the conference.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Leave a note about your attendance (if any).
