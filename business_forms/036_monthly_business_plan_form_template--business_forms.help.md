# Monthly Business Plan Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a monthly business plan. It consists of several sections that guide you through the process of setting up and tracking your business goals, strategies, and progress.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Choose the month for which you want to create a business plan. Select a month from the "Monthly Plan" field.
2. List the goals you want to achieve in the "Goals" field. Select all the goals that apply to you.
3. Describe the strategies you plan to use to achieve your goals in the "Strategies" field.
4. Note any progress you've made towards your goals in the "Progress" field. Enter a numerical value to track your progress.
5. Add any additional notes about your business plan in the "Notes" field.
6. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field, including your email address and phone number.
7. Categorize your business plan in the "Category" field. Choose from "Business Forms" or "Tracking Forms" (or other options that may become available in the future).
8. Finally, enter the tool you've assigned to work on your business plan in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Plan** (`id: 1`, `label: Business Plan`):
	* This is the main section of the form, where you can enter your business plan.
* **Monthly Plan** (`id: 2`, `label: Monthly Plan`):
	* Choose the month for which you want to create a business plan.
* **Goals** (`id: 3`, `label: Goals`):
	* List the goals you want to achieve for the selected month.
* **Progress** (`id: 4`, `label: Progress`):
	* Note any progress you've made towards your goals.
* **Strategies** (`id: 5`, `label: Strategies`):
	* Describe the strategies you plan to use to achieve your goals.
* **Notes** (`id: 6`, `label: Notes`):
	* Add any additional notes about your business plan.
* **Contact Information** (`id: 7`, `label: Contact Information`):
	* Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Category** (`id: 9`, `label: Category`):
	* Categorize your business plan. Choose from "Business Forms" or "Tracking Forms" (or other options that may become available in the future).
* **Assigned Tool** (`id: 10`, `label: Assigned Tool`):
	* Enter the tool you've assigned to work on your business plan.

## Tips

* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form to ensure that your business plan is easily understandable.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you need help with any part of the form, don't hesitate to ask for assistance.
