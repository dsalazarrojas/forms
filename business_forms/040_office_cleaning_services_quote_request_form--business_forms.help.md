# Office Cleaning Services Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a quote for office cleaning services. It gathers information about the contact person, company details, and services required. The form is used to create a quote for the requested services, which will be used for billing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information. The fields marked as required must be filled out.
2. For the "Service Type", select the type of service you are requesting. 
3. Choose the frequency of the service you need (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
4. Select the services you need (Cleaning Supplies, Trash Removal, Equipment Washing, Disinfection, Recycling).
5. Fill out your contact information and quote request details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person who will be responsible for the quote.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Email**: Enter your company email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your company phone number.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you are requesting (Cleaning, Maintenance, Specialized).
* **Frequency**: Choose the frequency of the service you need (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
* **Services Requested**: Select the services you need (Cleaning Supplies, Trash Removal, Equipment Washing, Disinfection, Recycling).
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like the service to start.
* **Start Time**: Enter the time you would like the service to start.
* **End Date**: Enter the date the service should end.
* **End Time**: Enter the time the service should end.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests you have.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the service.
* **Preferred Contact**: Select your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Quote Requested By**: Select who requested the quote (Manager, Supervisor, Staff).
* **Contact Method**: Select the method of contact you prefer (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Quote Delivery**: Select how you would like to receive the quote (Email, Phone, Email and Phone).
* **Payment Terms**: Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer (Check, Wire Transfer, Credit Card).
* **Payment Terms Details**: Enter any payment terms details.
* **Tax ID Number**: Enter your tax ID number.
* **Business Name**: Enter your business name.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address.
* **City State**: Enter your city and state.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code.
