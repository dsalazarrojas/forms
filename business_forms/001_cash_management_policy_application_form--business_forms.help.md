# Cash Management Policy Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cash Management Policy Application Form is used to gather information for cash management policy applications within the organization. This form helps to ensure that all necessary details are captured for the effective implementation and review of cash management policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and employee ID number to identify yourself as the applicant.
2. Select your current department from the dropdown list to indicate which team you are representing.
3. Provide your company email address and contact phone number to ensure we can contact you about your application.
4. Enter the date of your application to help track progress and deadlines.
5. Specify the type of cash management policy you are requesting to ensure accurate processing.
6. Explain why you need this policy, describing the business justification and expected benefits.
7. Briefly describe the current cash management process if you wish to highlight areas for improvement.
8. If necessary, provide a proposed implementation timeline.
9. Identify key stakeholders and compliance requirements.
10. If applicable, provide an estimated budget impact assessment.
11. Confirm whether manager approval is required.
12. Enter the name of your approving manager if applicable.
13. Add any additional comments or context to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`Applicant Full Name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name to identify yourself as the applicant.
* **Employee ID Number** (`Employee ID Number`, number, required): Your company employee identification number.
* **Department** (`Department`, select_one, required): Select your current department from the list to indicate which team you are representing.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Your company email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Application Date** (`Application Date`, date, required): Date of this application for tracking progress and deadlines.
* **Policy Type Requested** (`Policy Type Requested`, select_one, required): Choose the type of cash management policy you are seeking.
* **Business Justification** (`Business Justification`, text, required): Explain why this policy is needed, including the expected benefits.
* **Current Process Description** (`Current Process Description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the current cash management process if you wish to highlight areas for improvement.
* **Expected Benefits** (`Expected Benefits`, text, required): Describe the improvements you expect from this policy.
* **Proposed Implementation Timeline** (`Proposed Implementation Timeline`, text, optional): Suggest when this policy should take effect.
* **Key Stakeholders** (`Key Stakeholders`, text, optional): List departments or individuals affected by this policy.
* **Compliance Requirements** (`Compliance Requirements`, text, optional): Specify any regulatory or internal compliance needs.
* **Budget Impact Assessment** (`Budget Impact Assessment`, text, optional): Estimate any cost or savings associated with this policy.
* **Manager Approval Required** (`Manager Approval Required`, select_one, required): Indicate whether manager sign-off is required for this policy.
* **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your approving manager if necessary.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant information to support your application.

Note: Fields marked as optional (false) can be skipped if not applicable. However, it is crucial to fill out all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate application.
