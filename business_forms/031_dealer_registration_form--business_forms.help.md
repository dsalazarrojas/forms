# Dealer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dealer Registration Form is used to collect information from dealers who want to register themselves with our company. This form is required for dealers to be listed in our database and to receive updates on our business activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are a registered dealer and have not already filled out this form.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Choose your dealer type (physical or online) from the options provided.
4. Enter your name and other contact information, including email and phone numbers.
5. Provide your dealer address and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information** (Page 1): This section is where you can enter a brief description of your registration.
* **Name**: Enter your personal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Dealer Name**: Enter the name of your dealership or business.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used as your contact email.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication.
* **Address**: Enter your business address.
* **City**: Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State**: Enter the state where your business is located.
* **ZIP**: Enter your ZIP code.
* **Dealer Type**: Select whether your business is physical or online.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
