# Financial Department Data Usage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from users for the usage of their financial data within the financial department of an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your User Information, including any relevant details that you feel comfortable sharing with the financial department.
2. Provide your Financial Information, such as relevant financial data that you are willing to share with the financial department.
3. Read and agree to the terms of the financial data usage agreement.
4. Add your signature to indicate your consent for financial data usage.
5. Fill in the Submission Date and Submission Time fields to record the date and time of your consent.
6. If you have any additional notes or comments, feel free to include them in the Note field.
7. Enter your Email address for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (user_info): Fill in your name, title, department, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Financial Information** (financial_data): Enter any financial data that you are willing to share with the financial department, such as account numbers, balances, or other relevant financial information.
* **Agreement** (agreement): Read and agree to the terms of the financial data usage agreement, which is usually a standard agreement that outlines the usage and protection of your financial data.
* **Signature** (signature): Add your digital signature to indicate your consent for financial data usage. This is usually a simple signature or a checkbox.
* **Submission Date** (submission_date): Fill in the date on which you are submitting this form.
* **Submission Time** (submission_time): Fill in the time on which you are submitting this form.
* **Note** (note): If you have any additional notes or comments, feel free to include them here. This field is optional.
* **Email** (email): Enter your Email address for contact purposes, so that the financial department can reach out to you if needed.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields, especially Submission Date and Submission Time.
- Review the Agreement carefully before signing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the financial department.
- You can use the Note field to provide additional context or comments if needed.
