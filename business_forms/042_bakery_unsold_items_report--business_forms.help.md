# Bakery Unsold Items Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by bakery staff to report unsold items on a daily basis. The report helps the bakery track inventory, manage waste, and identify contributing factors to unsold items.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the report date from the calendar.
2. Enter your name as the staff member completing the report.
3. Select the shift time during which the report is being completed.
4. Enter the quantities of unsold items for each category (e.g., bread, pastries, cakes).
5. Select the disposition of each unsold item (e.g., donated, employee discount, composted).
6. Enter the estimated value of waste, if applicable.
7. Identify contributing factors to unsold items, if applicable.
8. Provide suggestions for improvement to reduce waste.
9. Select "Completed" when finished with the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date**: Select the date of the report.
* **Staff Member Name**: Enter your name as the person completing this report.
* **Shift Time**: Choose the time during which the report is being completed (e.g., Early Morning, Day Shift, Evening Shift, Night Shift).
* **Bread Loaves - Unsold Quantity**: Enter the quantity of unsold bread loaves.
* **Rolls - Unsold Quantity**: Enter the quantity of unsold rolls.
* **Specialty Bread - Unsold Quantity**: Enter the quantity of unsold specialty bread.
* **Croissants - Unsold Quantity**: Enter the quantity of unsold croissants.
* **Muffins - Unsold Quantity**: Enter the quantity of unsold muffins.
* **Donuts - Unsold Quantity**: Enter the quantity of unsold donuts.
* **Cakes - Unsold Quantity**: Enter the quantity of unsold cakes.
* **Cupcakes - Unsold Quantity**: Enter the quantity of unsold cupcakes.
* **Cookies - Unsold Quantity**: Enter the quantity of unsold cookies.
* **Total Unsold Count**: Enter the total quantity of unsold items.
* **Disposition of Unsold Items**: Select the disposition of each unsold item (e.g., donated, employee discount, composted).
* **Estimated Value of Waste**: Enter the estimated value of waste, if applicable.
* **Contributing Factors**: Identify contributing factors to unsold items, if applicable.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Provide suggestions for reducing waste.
* **Report Completed and Submitted**: Select "Completed" when finished with the report.
