# Great_Writers / Rareon.GW Partners / Agents Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from agents of the Great Writers / Rareon.GW Partners organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **last name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address. This will be used for official communication.
* **phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **date of birth** (5, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard "YYYY-MM-DD" format.
* **agent type** (6, select_one, required): Indicate your agent type by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **agent since** (7, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select the years of experience you have as an agent. This field is not required.
* **note** (8, note, optional): If you wish to add a note about yourself, you can do so here. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to ensure the registration process is successful.
* Use a valid email address that can be used for official communication.
* Be accurate when entering your date of birth.
* Select "Yes" or "No" for the "agent type" field accurately.
* If you have experience as an agent, select the corresponding years in the "agent since" field.
* If you wish to add a note, keep it brief and relevant to your registration information.
