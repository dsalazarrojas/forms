# Expense Reimbursement Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to audit and verify the accuracy of expense reimbursement claims submitted by employees. It helps ensure that expenses are properly recorded, categorized, and approved in accordance with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the submission date for the expense.
2. Choose the submission type from the provided options.
3. Indicate whether the employee was reimbursed or not.
4. Provide a description of the expense (optional).
5. Enter the total amount of the expense (optional).
6. Select the date the expense occurred.
7. Enter a brief reason for the expense (optional).
8. Choose the approval status from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date the expense was submitted for reimbursement.
- **Submission Type** (`submission_type`): Choose the type of submission from the provided options.
- **Employee Reimbursed** (`employee`): Indicate whether the employee was reimbursed for the expense or not.
- **Expense Description** (`expense_description`): Provide a detailed description of the expense (optional).
- **Total Amount** (`total_amount`): Enter the total amount of the expense (optional).
- **Expense Date** (`expense_date`): Select the date the expense occurred.
- **Expense Reason** (`expense_reason`): Enter a brief reason for the expense (optional).
- **Approval Status** (`approval_status`): Choose the approval status from the provided options.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check your submissions for errors before saving.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative for assistance.
