# Business Expense Program Renewal Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Expense Program Renewal Application form is a tool used to submit a renewal application for your business's expense program. This form collects necessary information about your organization, program details, and financial information to evaluate your eligibility for program renewal.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the completed form to the relevant authorities for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (Organization Name, text, **required**): Enter the legal name of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (Organization Type, select_one, **required**): Choose the type of organization you represent (e.g., Corporation, Non-Profit, Government Agency, Educational Institution, Other).
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, **required**): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your organization.
* **Contact Title** (Contact Title, text, **required**): Enter the title of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, **required**): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, **required**): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Program Name** (Program Name, text, **required**): Enter the name of your expense program.
* **Current Enrollment** (Current Enrollment, number, **required**): Enter the number of participants currently enrolled in your program.
* **Program Inception Date** (Program Inception Date, date, **required**): Enter the date when your program started.
* **Current Expiration** (Current Expiration, date, **required**): Enter the date when the current term of your program ends.
* **Requested Renewal Period** (Requested Renewal Period, select_one, **required**): Choose the desired length of the new program term (e.g., 1 Year, 2 Years, 3 Years, 5 Years, Other).
* **Program Changes or Modifications** (Program Changes or Modifications, text, **optional**): Enter any changes or modifications you would like to make to your program.
* **Program Utilization Rate** (Program Utilization Rate, number, **required**): Enter the percentage of active use of your program.
* **Participant Satisfaction Level** (Participant Satisfaction Level, select_one, **required**): Choose how satisfied participants are with your program (e.g., Very Dissatisfied, Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, Very Satisfied).
* **Annual Expenses** (Annual Program Expenses, number, **required**): Enter the total annual expense of your program.
* **Budget Justification** (Budget Justification, text, **required**): Explain why your program requires renewal.
* **Expected Renewal Benefits** (Expected Renewal Benefits, text, **required**): Describe the benefits you expect from program renewal.

Note: The form fields are designed to guide you through the process, but please ensure that you review the information carefully before submission.
