# Printing Service Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for printing service quotations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the business name of the company or organization requesting the quotation.
2. Indicate whether the requested printing services are required.
3. Enter the name of the contact person for the business.
4. Provide the email address of the contact person.
5. Enter the phone number of the contact person.
6. Choose the date on which the quotation is requested.
7. Enter a brief description of the services required for the quotation.
8. Choose the time on which the quotation is requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (text): Enter the name of the company or organization requesting the quotation.
* **Requested Printing Services** (select multiple): Indicate which printing services are required (Yes/No).
* **Contact Name** (text): Enter the name of the contact person for the business.
* **Contact Email** (email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Requested Quotation Date** (date): Choose the date on which the quotation is requested.
* **Quotation Description** (text): Enter a brief description of the services required for the quotation.
* **Requested Quotation Time** (time): Choose the time on which the quotation is requested.
