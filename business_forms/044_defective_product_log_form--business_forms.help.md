# Defective Product Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document information about defective products to ensure efficient defect identification and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and select from the provided options where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product ID**: Enter the unique identifier of the defective product. This field is not required but is useful for tracking purposes.
    - **Date Received**: Enter the date you received the defective product.
    - **Product Name**: Enter the name of the defective product.
    - **Description**: Describe the defect in detail.
    - **Category**: Select 'Yes' if the product is a specific category or 'No' otherwise.
    - **Defect Type**: Select 'Yes' if the product has a defect or 'No' otherwise.
    - **Status**: Select the status of the defect (Active or Inactive).
    - **Priority**: Select the priority level of the defect (Yes or No).
    - **Severity**: Select the severity level of the defect (Yes or No).
    - **Resolution Status**: Select the resolution status of the defect (Active or Inactive).
    - **Assigned To**: Select 'Yes' if the defect is assigned to a specific person or 'No' otherwise.
    - **Assigned By**: Select 'Yes' if the defect is assigned by a specific person or 'No' otherwise.
    - **Closed Date**: Enter the date the defect was closed.
    - **Closed By**: Select 'Yes' if the defect was closed by a specific person or 'No' otherwise.
    - **Remarks**: Enter any additional remarks or comments about the defect.
    - **Attachments**: Enter any attachments related to the defect.
    - **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the defect.
    - **Email**: Enter a contact email.
    - **Phone**: Enter a contact phone number.
    - **Created By**: Select 'Yes' if the product was created by a specific person or 'No' otherwise.
    - **Updated By**: Select 'Yes' if the product was updated by a specific person or 'No' otherwise.
    - **Created At**: Enter the date the product was created.
    - **Updated At**: Enter the date the product was last updated.
    - **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the product.
    - **Created Date**: Enter the date the product was created.

Please ensure that you fill out all the required fields and provide as much detail as possible to ensure efficient defect resolution.
