# Room Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Room Data Collection Form is used to document and manage detailed information about a room in a building. This includes its characteristics, safety features, and maintenance history.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To fill out this form, start by selecting the room you want to collect data for.
2.  Fill out the required fields (marked as "Required" in the form), which include:
    *   **Room Number**: Enter the room's number or ID.
    *   **Room Type**: Select whether the room is active or inactive.
    *   **Building Number**: Enter the building where the room is located.
    *   **Floor Number**: Enter the floor number of the building where the room is located.
    *   **Room Occupancy**: Confirm if the room is occupied or not.
    *   **Fire Supervision**: Confirm if the room has fire supervision features.
    *   **Accessibility Features**: Select the accessibility features the room has.
    *   **Fire Alarm System**: Confirm if the room has a fire alarm system.
    *   **Emergency Exit Signs**: Confirm if the room has emergency exit signs.
    *   **Emergency Exit Lamps**: Confirm if the room has emergency exit lamps.
    *   **Fire Safety Equipment**: Select the fire safety equipment the room has.
    *   **Fire Alarm**: Confirm if the room has a fire alarm.
    *   **Fire Extinguisher**: Confirm if the room has a fire extinguisher.
    *   **Fire Safety Equipment 2**: Select the fire safety equipment 2 the room has.
    *   **Fire Safety Equipment 3**: Confirm if the room has fire safety equipment 3.
    *   **Emergency Exits**: Confirm if the room has emergency exits.
3.  For optional fields (not marked as "Required" in the form), you can provide any additional information you'd like to add to the room's record. Some of these fields may be important for specific situations or room types, so be sure to check the field's description or ask your administrator if you need help deciding what to fill out.
