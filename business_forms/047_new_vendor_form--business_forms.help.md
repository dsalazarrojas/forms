# New Vendor Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The New Vendor Form is a tool used to collect information about new vendors, allowing the purchasing team to verify and validate their details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Double-check your contact information before submitting the form.
3. If you're unsure about any field, contact the purchasing team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`new_vendor_form_1`, text, required/optional): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the business.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_1`, text, required/optional): Enter the address line 1 of the business.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_2`, text, required/optional): Enter the address line 2 of the business.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of the business.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required/optional): Select the state where the business is located.
* **Zip/Postal Code** (`zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the business.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required/optional): Enter the country of the business.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the business.
* **Fax** (`fax`, text, required/optional): Enter the fax number of the business.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required/optional): Enter the website URL of the business.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Bank Info** (`bank_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the bank information of the business.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the account number of the business.
* **Routing Number** (`routing_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the routing number of the business.
* **Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of vendor.
* **Vendor Status** (`vendor_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the status of the vendor.
* **Vendor Level** (`vendor_level`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the level of the vendor.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the vendor.
* **Agreement Signed** (`agreement_signed`, text, required/optional): Indicate if an agreement has been signed.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the signed agreement.

Please note that some fields may be optional, but it's recommended to fill them out for a smooth onboarding process. If you're unsure about any field, please contact the purchasing team for assistance.
