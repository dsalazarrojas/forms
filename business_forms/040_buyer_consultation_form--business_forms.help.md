# Buyer Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Buyer Consultation Form is designed to gather information about your business needs and preferences for a potential purchase. This form helps us understand your requirements, expectations, and constraints to provide you with the best possible consultation experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer according to your true intentions.
* Fill in all the required fields (marked as "required") with the most accurate information.
* You can choose from multiple options for select fields.
* Make sure to provide any additional information you think is relevant to your consultation.
