# Courier Service Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Courier Service Quotation Form is a tool designed to gather essential information from users for a courier service quotation. It collects user details, service request, pick-up and drop-off details, payment method, special instructions, and estimated pick-up time to provide a quote for the courier service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, address, and contact information in the "User Details" section.
2. Describe your service request in the "Service Request" section.
3. Provide details about where you would like to be picked up from in the "Pick Up Details" section.
4. Specify where you would like to be dropped off in the "Drop Off Details" section.
5. Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" options.
6. Enter any special instructions or requirements in the "Special Instructions" section.
7. If you would like to schedule a pick-up, enter the estimated pick-up time in the "Estimated Pick Up" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, required: false): This section is for you to enter your name, address, and contact information. Please ensure that you enter accurate and complete information to ensure smooth communication.
* **Service Request** (`service_request`, text, required: false): Describe your service request in this section. Be as detailed as possible so that the courier service can understand your needs accurately.
* **Pick Up Details** (`pick_up_details`, text, required: false): Provide details about where you would like to be picked up from. This includes your exact location or a nearby landmark for easy identification.
* **Drop Off Details** (`drop_off_details`, text, required: false): Specify the location where you would like to be dropped off.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided. You can select one of the options: Cash, Bank Transfer, or Cheque.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, required: false): If you have any special requirements or requests, enter them here. This could include specific items to be delivered or any other information that may affect the service.
* **Estimated Pick Up** (`estimated_pick_up`, date, required: false): If you would like to schedule a pick-up, enter the estimated time. This will help the courier service plan their schedule accordingly.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Be clear and concise in your descriptions to help the courier service understand your needs better.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the courier service directly for clarification.
