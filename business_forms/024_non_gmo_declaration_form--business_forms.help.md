# Non GMO Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about non-GMO products and to ensure that they meet the required standards and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) or indicated as "required".
- If a field is not required, you can leave it blank if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### product_name 
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product being declared as non-GMO.

### declaration_statement 
* **Declaration Statement** (`declaration_statement`, text, optional): Provide a brief statement about why this product is non-GMO, e.g., "This product is a naturally grown and harvested crop."

### gmo_statement 
* **GMO Statement** (`gmo_statement`, text, optional): If the product contains GMOs, describe the steps taken to remove them, e.g., "We have implemented a double sorting process to ensure no GMOs are present."

### certifying_officer 
* **Certifying Officer** (`certifying_officer`, text, optional): Enter the name and title of the person responsible for certifying this product as non-GMO.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
