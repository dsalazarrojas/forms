# subcontractor_information_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from subcontractors, including their general contact information, business details, and working history with the company. It helps us understand their background and assess their ability to work with us on projects.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields:
	* General info (page 1)
	* Business name (page 2)
	* Contact email (page 2)
	* Contact number (page 2)
	* Subcontractor status (page 3)
	* Payment terms (page 3)
2. Select the correct options for the following fields:
	* License info (page 2)
	* Business type (page 2)
	* Work location (page 3)
	* Work start date (page 3)
	* Work end date (page 3)
	* Work hours (page 3)
3. Leave the optional fields blank if not applicable:
	* Subcontractor address (page 1)
	* Experience (page 3)
4. Ensure you enter valid email and date values in the correct format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General info** (Text, Required)
    This field is a brief description of the subcontractor, providing context for further reference.
*   **Subcontractor address** (Text, Optional)
    Enter the address of the subcontractor's business or location.
*   **Contact number** (Number, Optional)
    Enter a valid phone number for the subcontractor's contact person.
*   **Contact email** (Email, Optional)
    Enter a valid email address for the subcontractor's contact person.
*   **Business name** (Text, Required)
    Enter the name of the subcontractor's business.
*   **License info** (Select One, Optional)
    Select 'Yes' if the subcontractor has the required license or 'No' otherwise.
*   **Business type** (Select Multiple, Optional)
    Select the types of business that apply to the subcontractor (e.g., 'Yes' for 'Construction', 'No' for 'Engineering').
*   **Experience** (Number, Optional)
    Enter the number of years the subcontractor has been in business.
*   **Work location** (Select Multiple, Optional)
    Select the locations where the subcontractor's work is typically performed.
*   **Work start date** (Date, Optional)
    Enter the date when the subcontractor started working with the company.
*   **Work end date** (Date, Optional)
    Enter the date when the subcontractor's work ended with the company.
*   **Work hours** (Time, Optional)
    Enter the typical hours of work performed by the subcontractor.
*   **Subcontractor status** (Select One, Optional)
    Select 'Active' if the subcontractor is currently working with the company or 'Inactive' otherwise.
*   **Payment terms** (Number, Optional)
    Enter any special payment terms or conditions for the subcontractor.
*   **Tax info** (Note, Optional)
    Enter any relevant tax information or notes about the subcontractor.
