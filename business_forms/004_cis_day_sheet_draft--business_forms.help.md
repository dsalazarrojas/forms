# CIS Day Sheet Draft - Help Guide
## Purpose

The CIS Day Sheet Draft is a daily report card for sales or service staff to document their activities and performance. This form helps management track team productivity, client engagement, and revenue generation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by selecting the correct date** for your report.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked as `**Required**`, for accurate reporting.
3. Use the optional fields for additional details or comments, if needed.
4. **Double-check your input** before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team member name** (`Team member name`):
  • A brief text field where you should type your name as it appears on your company ID or personnel record.
* **Date** (`Date`):
  • Enter the date for which you're reporting your activities, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Submitted at** (`Submitted at`):
  • Enter the time you're submitting this report, in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Tasks completed** (`Tasks completed`):
  • A brief list of key tasks or accomplishments you've completed today.
* **Clients seen** (`Clients seen`):
  • Enter the number of clients or appointments you've completed today.
* **Revenue collected** (`Revenue collected`):
  • Enter the total amount of cash or payments collected today.
* **Issues or notes** (`Issues or notes`):
  • Any incidents or notes for management to review, such as challenges you faced or successes.
* **Follow-up actions** (`Follow-up actions`):
  • Tasks to be completed next business day or follow-up activities.
* **Supervisor name** (`Supervisor name`):
  • A text field for your supervisor to sign-off, indicating approval.
* **Supervisor signature** (`Supervisor signature`):
  • A text field for your supervisor to sign, indicating approval. 

Please note that some fields are marked as required, so ensure you fill those out for a complete and accurate report.
