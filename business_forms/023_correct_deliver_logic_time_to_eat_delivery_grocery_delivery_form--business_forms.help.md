# CORRECT Deliver Logic Time To Eat Delivery Grocery Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for their grocery delivery order, ensuring a smooth and personalized delivery experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Customer Name**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, and **Delivery Address** fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select your **City** and **State** for delivery purposes.
3. Choose your **Preferred Delivery Date** and **Preferred Delivery Time** based on your schedule.
4. Select your **Time To Eat Preference** from the options provided.
5. Choose all applicable **Grocery Categories Needed** for your order.
6. Indicate any **Dietary Restrictions** you may have.
7. List the specific **Grocery List** items you need, including quantities if possible.
8. Estimate the number of people you need to feed with the **Quantity Estimate** field.
9. Select your estimated **Budget Range** for the order.
10. If applicable, specify any **Brand Preferences** you may have.
11. Choose whether **Substitutions Are Allowed** in case items are unavailable.
12. Select the **Delivery Type** that suits your needs (e.g., contactless, hand delivery, or leave at door).
13. Choose your **Payment Method** (e.g., credit card, debit card, digital wallet, cash, or SNAP EBT).
14. Optionally, choose a **Tip Amount** or select "No tip" if you prefer not to give a gratuity.
15. If this is for a special occasion, select the corresponding option from **Special Occasion** (e.g., birthday, holiday, or party/gathering).
16. Finally, provide any **Additional Notes** about your order, such as special requests or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Provide your best contact number for any updates or queries.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required): Enter your complete street address for delivery purposes.
* **City** (`delivery_city`, `text`, required): Select the city for delivery purposes.
* **State** (`delivery_state`, `text`, required): Choose the state or province for delivery purposes.
* **ZIP Code** (`delivery_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the postal code for delivery purposes.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, `text`, optional): Provide any gate codes, building access information, or special delivery instructions.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (`preferred_delivery_date`, `date`, required): Choose a date for the delivery.
* **Preferred Delivery Time** (`preferred_delivery_time`, `select_one`, required): Select a time window for the delivery (morning, afternoon, or evening).
* **Time To Eat Preference** (`time_to_eat_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose when you plan to eat the delivered items (immediately, within 2 hours, etc.).
* **Grocery Categories Needed** (`grocery_category`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable categories from the options provided.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate any dietary restrictions or allergies you may have.
* **Grocery List** (`grocery_list`, `text`, required): List specific items you need, including quantities if possible.
* **Quantity Estimate** (`quantity_estimate`, `number`, required): Estimate the number of people you need to feed.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Select your estimated budget range for the order.
* **Brand Preferences** (`brand_preferences`, `text`, optional): Specify any specific brands you prefer.
* **Substitutions Allowed** (`substitution_allowed`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether substitutions are allowed in case items are unavailable.
* **Delivery Type** (`delivery_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the delivery type (contactless, hand delivery, or leave at door).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your payment method (credit card, debit card, digital wallet, cash, or SNAP EBT).
* **Tip Amount** (`tip_amount`, `select_one`, optional): Choose an optional tip amount or select "No tip" if you prefer not to give a gratuity.
* **Special Occasion** (`special_occasion`, `select_one`, optional): If this is for a special occasion, select the corresponding option.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information or comments about your order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid any issues with your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
* Review your order before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
