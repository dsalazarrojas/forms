# Process Optimization Roadmap Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Optimization Roadmap Request Form is designed to help the organization identify areas of improvement and create a plan to optimize business processes. It collects information about the process, area of operation, optimization goal, objective, scope, and timeline.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by giving a title for the form submission.
2. Select the area of operation related to the process you want to optimize (e.g., Business, IT, Engineering, etc.).
3. Briefly describe the optimization goal.
4. Define the objective of the optimization.
5. Specify the scope of the process to be optimized.
6. Enter a rough timeline for the optimization.
7. Add any additional notes that might be helpful for the optimization process.
8. Finally, submit the form to the designated authority for review.

* **Form Title** (`process_roadmap_title`, text, optional): Give a title for the form submission that describes the process being optimized.
* **Operation Area** (`operation_area`, select_one, optional): Select the area of operation related to the process you want to optimize (e.g., Business, IT, Engineering, etc.).
* **Optimization Goal** (`process_roadmap_goal`, text, optional): Briefly describe the optimization goal for the process.
* **Objective** (`process_roadmap_objective`, text, optional): Define the objective of the optimization.
* **Scope** (`process_roadmap_scope`, text, optional): Specify the scope of the process to be optimized.
* **Timeline** (`process_roadmap_timeline`, date, optional): Enter a rough timeline for the optimization.
* **Notes** (`process_roadmap_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes that might be helpful for the optimization process.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, text, optional): Submit the form to the designated authority for review.
