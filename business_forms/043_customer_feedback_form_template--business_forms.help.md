# Customer Feedback Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers, allowing them to express their experiences and opinions about our products or services. Please provide honest and detailed feedback to help us improve our offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the contact details of the customer, including their email address and name.
2. Select the feedback type (Positive, Negative, or Neutral) that best describes their experience.
3. Optionally, provide additional comments to elaborate on their feedback.
4. Select the product or service they are providing feedback on.
5. Rate their experience on a scale of 1-5.
6. If desired, select any additional feedback categories.
7. Optionally, provide more details about their feedback.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send their feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Feedback Form** (`customer_feedback_form`, text, required): Enter a brief description of their experience.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, email, required): Enter the customer's contact information, including their email address and name.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of their experience.
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, select multiple, required): Select the type of feedback they wish to provide (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or details about their experience.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Phone Number** (`customer_phone_number`, text, optional): If available, enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): If available, enter the customer's address.
* **Customer Company** (`customer_company`, text, optional): If available, enter the customer's company name.
* **Customer Job Title** (`customer_job_title`, text, optional): If available, enter the customer's job title.
* **Product or Service** (`product_or_service`, select one, required): Select the product or service they are providing feedback on.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select one, required): Rate their experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback Category** (`feedback_category`, text, optional): If desired, select any additional feedback categories.
* **Feedback Details** (`feedback_details`, select multiple, optional): If desired, select any additional feedback details.
* **Customer Phone Number** (`customer_phone_number`, text, optional): If available, enter the customer's phone number.
* **Product A** (`feedback_details`, select multiple, optional): If desired, select any additional feedback details for product A.
* **Product B** (`feedback_details`, select multiple, optional): If desired, select any additional feedback details for product B.
* **Product C** (`feedback_details`, select multiple, optional): If desired, select any additional feedback details for product C.
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, select multiple, optional): Select the "Submit" button to send their feedback.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate contact information to ensure we can follow up with the customer.
- Select the feedback type that best describes their experience.
- If applicable, provide additional comments or details to help us improve.
- Ensure to rate their experience honestly and fairly.
- Select the correct product or service they are providing feedback on.
- Double-check their contact information before submitting.
