# Web Design Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Web Design Service Request Form is a simple form designed to gather essential information from clients requesting web design services. Please follow these steps to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information and project details in the "Client Info" field.
2. Select the type of project you are requesting (Logo Design, Web Design, or Print Design).
3. Provide your budget for the project.
4. Choose the specific services you need (Logo Design, Web Design, or Print Design).
5. If you have any additional requests or specifications, please provide them in the "Custom Requests" field.
6. Finally, choose whether you would like to subscribe or unsubscribe from our newsletter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info** (`1`, `text`, required): Fill in your contact information, including your name and email address.
* **Project Type** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of project you are requesting (Logo Design, Web Design, or Print Design).
* **Budget** (`3`, `number`, required): Enter your budget for the project.
* **Budget Range** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the range of your budget (30000-50000, 50000-100000, or 100000-500000).
* **Services** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the specific services you need (Logo Design, Web Design, or Print Design).
* **Custom Requests** (`6`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional requests or specifications, please provide them here.
* **Subscribe to Newsletter** (`7`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you would like to subscribe or unsubscribe from our newsletter.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing your budget and services needed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
* We will review your request and get back to you as soon as possible.
