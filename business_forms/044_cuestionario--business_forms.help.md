# Cuestionario - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects basic user information to register or inquire about a service provided by a medical practice or healthcare service. Please fill in the form with your details to ensure that our service can better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with your information, making sure to provide accurate details.
2. Select the correct type of consultant from the options provided.
3. Add any additional observations or comments in the 'Observaciones' field.
4. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: Enter your name to help us understand who you are.
* **Nombre (Name)**: Enter your full name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Fecha de nacimiento (Date of Birth)**: Enter your date of birth to verify your identity.
* **Telefono (Phone)**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to stay in touch with us.
* **Tipo de consultor (Type of consultant)**: Choose the type of consultant that best describes your needs. Select either 'Dentista' (Dentist), 'Pediatria' (Pediatrician), or 'Psicologo' (Psychologist).
* **Observaciones (Observations)**: Add any additional comments or details you want to share with us.
* **Tipo de consultor 2 (Type of consultant 2)**: Choose one or multiple types of consultants that apply to your needs. Select either 'Bébé' (Baby) or 'Infantil' (Child).
* **Telefono (Phone)**: Enter your phone number for any further contact or communication.
