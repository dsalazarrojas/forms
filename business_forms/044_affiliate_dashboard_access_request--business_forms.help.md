# Affiliate Dashboard Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Affiliate Dashboard Access Request form is designed for individuals or organizations interested in promoting our business as affiliates. This form allows you to provide us with your contact information, marketing plans, and preferences for collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To request access to the affiliate dashboard, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.
3. Review the affiliate agreement and select "I Agree" to confirm you understand our terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your personal name that will be displayed in the affiliate dashboard.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name that will be displayed in the affiliate dashboard.
* **Business Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your business email address that will be used as your login username.
* **Business Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Primary Website URL** (`website_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of your website where you will promote us.
* **Social Media Profiles** (`social_media`, text, optional): Provide links to your social media profiles, if applicable.
* **Primary Traffic Source** (`traffic_source`, select_one, required): Select how you drive traffic to your website (e.g., SEO, Paid Ads, Social Media, etc.).
* **Monthly Visitors/Reach** (`monthly_visitors`, select_one, required): Estimate your approximate audience size.
* **Promotion Plan** (`promotion_plan`, text, required): Briefly describe how you plan to market our products.
* **Preferred Payout Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to receive your payouts (e.g., PayPal, Bank Transfer, etc.).
* **PayPal Email (if applicable)** (`paypal_email`, email, optional): Enter your PayPal email address, if you selected PayPal as your payout method.
* **Affiliate Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, required): Select "I Agree" to confirm you understand our affiliate program terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Double-check your email address for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* If you are not sure about any field, feel free to contact us for clarification.
