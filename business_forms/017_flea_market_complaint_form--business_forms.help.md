# Flea Market Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for flea market vendors and customers to report and manage complaints related to the flea market. It helps to track and resolve issues efficiently, improving the overall experience for both vendors and customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor involved in the complaint.
   - **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer who reported the complaint.
   - **Market Name**: Enter the name of the flea market where the incident occurred.
   - **Complaint Date**: Select the date the complaint was made.
   - **Complaint Description**: Describe the issue or problem you experienced.
   - **Complaint Type**: Select the type of complaint (Vendor, Customer, or Market).
   - **Complaint Category**: Choose the category of the complaint (Vendor, Customer, or Market).
   - **Complaint Priority**: Set the priority level of the complaint (High, Medium, or Low).
   - **Assigned To**: Select the person responsible for handling the complaint (chatjimmy or Other).
   - **Complaint Status**: Set the status of the complaint (New, In Progress, or Resolved).
   - **Complaint ID**: Leave this field blank; it will be automatically generated.

2. If necessary, fill in additional fields:
   - **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's contact phone number.
   - **Vendor Phone**: Enter the vendor's contact phone number.
   - **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
   - **Vendor Email**: Enter the vendor's email address.
   - **Photo Description**: Add a description for any attached photo related to the complaint.
   - **Vendor Response**: Add any response from the vendor related to the complaint.
   - **Customer Response**: Add any response from the customer related to the complaint.
   - **Market Manager Comments**: Add any comments from the market manager related to the complaint.
   - **Action Taken**: Add any actions taken to address the complaint.
   - **Next Steps Propects**: Add any next steps or actions planned for future reference.
   - **Resolution Notes**: Add any resolution notes related to the complaint.
   - **Customer Signature**: Add the customer's signature, if applicable.
   - **Vendor Signature**: Add the vendor's signature, if applicable.
   - **Market Manager Signature**: Add the market manager's signature, if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Use the "Complaint Type" and "Complaint Category" fields to categorize the complaint correctly.
* Set the "Complaint Priority" accordingly based on the severity of the issue.
* Choose the correct person to "Assign To" based on the type of complaint.
