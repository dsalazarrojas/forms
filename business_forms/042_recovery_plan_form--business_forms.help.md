# Recovery Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recovery Plan Form is designed to guide business owners or managers in creating a recovery plan after a disruption or crisis. It helps identify key objectives, resources, and risks involved in the recovery process and serves as a template for evaluating progress and reviewing the outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Recovery Plan Form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the introduction page and consider the current situation.
2.  Answer the objectives question to determine the key goals for recovery.
3.  Indicate whether resources are available to support the recovery process.
4.  Choose a timeline for the recovery efforts.
5.  Enter any risk that may impact the recovery process.
6.  Identify the measures to mitigate or address those risks.
7.  Describe the expected response from employees, customers, or other stakeholders.
8.  Outline the recovery process and key milestones.
9.  Evaluate the outcome of the recovery efforts.
10.  Review the final status of the recovery process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief description of the current situation and the need for a recovery plan.
* **Objectives**: Select one or more of the options to determine the key goals for the recovery process. (Yes, No)
* **Resources**: Indicate whether resources are available to support the recovery process. (Yes, No)
* **Timeline**: Choose a date for the recovery efforts to be completed by.
* **Risk**: Describe any risks that may impact the recovery process.
* **Mitigation**: Describe the measures to address or mitigate those risks.
* **Response**: Describe the expected response from employees, customers, or other stakeholders.
* **Recovery**: Outline the recovery process and key milestones.
* **Evaluation**: Evaluate the outcome of the recovery efforts. (Yes, No)
* **Review**: A brief summary of the current status of the recovery process.
* **Status**: Choose the current status of the recovery process. (Active, Inactive)
* **Final**: Enter the number of days taken for the recovery process.
* **Status**: Describe the overall status of the recovery process.
*   **Response** and **Recovery** fields are intended to allow for a brief description of the expected response and process, while **Risk** and **Mitigation** fields allow for more detailed information about the risks and mitigation measures. **Evaluation** field helps in determining the progress of the recovery efforts.
