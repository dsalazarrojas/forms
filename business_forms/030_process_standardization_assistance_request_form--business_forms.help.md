# Process Standardization Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Standardization Assistance Request Form is designed to collect information about process standardization requests from end-users. The form aims to capture essential details that will guide the process of reviewing and addressing these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Provide a brief overview of your request in the **Request Details** field.
2.  Specify the primary goal of your request in the **Goal** field.
3.  Indicate the level of urgency for the standardization process in the **Urgency** field.
4.  Describe the changes needed to standardize the process in the **Change Needs** field.
5.  Select the potential impact of standardizing the process in the **Impact** field.
6.  Identify the operational unit affected by the standardization request in the **Operational Unit** field.
7.  Specify the submitting team that is requesting the standardization assistance in the **Submitting Team** field.
8.  Add your contact information in the **Contact Info** field to enable follow-up and collaboration.
9.  Select the person or team responsible for handling your request in the **Assigned To** field.
10.  Review and update the **Review Status** field to track the progress of your request.
11.  Add any additional comments or information in the **Comments** field.
12.  Select the tool assigned to handle your request in the **Assigned Tool** field.
13.  Input your email address in the **Submitted By** field to help identify you as the submitter.
14.  Input the date and time you submitted the request in the **Submitted Date** and **Submitted Time** fields.
