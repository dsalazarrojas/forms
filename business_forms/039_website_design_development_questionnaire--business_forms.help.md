# Website Design & Development Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or companies seeking web design and development services. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary details for internal stakeholders to assess and process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name
2. Enter your Last Name
3. Enter your Email Address
4. Enter your Phone Number
5. Enter your Company Name (if applicable)
6. Enter your Company URL (if applicable)
7. Enter your Position

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your full name. This is the first step in identifying who is requesting the service.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. This is also part of identifying you or your company.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This is the preferred contact method for internal stakeholders.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This will allow internal stakeholders to reach out to you directly.
* **Company Name**: If you are representing a company, enter the name of the company.
* **Company URL**: If you are representing a company, enter the company URL (website address) for easier reference.
* **Position**: Enter your title or position within the company. This will help internal stakeholders understand your role within the organization. 

Note: All fields are optional, but please provide as much information as possible to help internal stakeholders provide better service.
