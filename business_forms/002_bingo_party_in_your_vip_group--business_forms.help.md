# Bingo Party In Your VIP Group - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Bingo Party In Your VIP Group" form is designed to gather information from customers interested in participating in a private online bingo party, hosted by a specific VIP group. The form helps in creating a list of attendees and their contact information, which will be used to send sample packs and offer exclusive deals to the members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the welcome note to understand the purpose of the form and what to expect.
2. Fill out your full name, email address, and phone number in the required fields.
3. Enter your mailing address and zip code accurately to ensure proper delivery of sample packs.
4. Select the correct party host from the given options.
5. Choose the products you are interested in for the party (if not a member of the VIP group).
6. If you wish to join the private VIP group for exclusive offers, select "Yes - send me the link!".
7. If you were referred to the party by someone, provide their name in the "Who referred you to this party?" field.
8. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to identify you as an attendee of the party.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive sample packs and updates on exclusive offers.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number to ensure contactability in case of any issues with sample packs or updates.
* **Mailing Address for Sample Pack** (`mailing_address`, `text`, required): Enter your accurate mailing address to receive the sample packs.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city you reside in to ensure correct delivery of sample packs.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code for proper addressing of sample packs.
* **Who is your Bingo Party Host?** (`host_name`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the provided options for the host of the party.
* **What products are you most interested in?** (`product_interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the products you are interested in for the party. This is only required if you are not a member of the VIP group.
* **Would you like to join our private VIP group for exclusive offers?** (`join_vip_request`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes - send me the link!" to join the private VIP group.
* **Who referred you to this party?** (`referral_source`, `text`, optional): If someone referred you, enter their name here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Review your entries before submitting the form to ensure all information is correct.
* If you experience any issues with sample pack delivery or exclusive offers, use your provided contact information to get assistance.
