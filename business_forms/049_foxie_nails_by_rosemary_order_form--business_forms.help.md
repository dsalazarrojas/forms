# foxie_nails_by_rosemary_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foxie Nails by Rosemary Order Form is used to collect customer information, order details, payment information, shipping information, and confirm order details for a single order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Customer Information**: Please fill in the customer's name and contact information.
2.  **Order Details**: Enter the order details such as services or products ordered and quantity.
3.  **Payment Information**: Enter the payment method and amount.
4.  **Shipping Information**: Enter the shipping address and any special instructions.
5.  **Confirm Order**: Confirm that the order details are accurate.
6.  **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**
	+ **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's full name.
	+ **Contact Information**: Enter the customer's email address and phone number.
* **Order Details**
	+ **Order Description**: Enter a brief description of the services or products ordered.
	+ **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the services or products ordered.
* **Payment Information**
	+ **Payment Method**: Select the payment method (e.g., credit card, cash, etc.).
	+ **Amount**: Enter the total amount due.
* **Shipping Information**
	+ **Address**: Enter the shipping address.
	+ **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions for the shipment.
* **Confirm Order**
	+ **Confirm Order Details**: Confirm that the order details are accurate.
* **Notes**
	+ **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
