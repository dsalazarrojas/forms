# Event Insurance Partner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Insurance Partner Registration Form is designed to collect information from potential event insurance partners, allowing them to register and be listed as partners for events. This form helps us ensure that all partners are authorized and prevents duplication of registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you are an authorized representative of the organization you are registering.
2.  Enter your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Organization as per your details.
3.  Fill out each field as required.
4.  Review the information you have entered before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter your email address in this field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
* **Phone** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Organization** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter your organization's name in this field.
