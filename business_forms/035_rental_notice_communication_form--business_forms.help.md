# Rental Notice Communication Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Rental Notice Communication Form is designed to facilitate the communication process between tenants, landlords, and administrators. It helps in maintaining a record of rental notices, their status, and the people involved in the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Data** field with a brief description of the notice.
2. Select the **Notice Type** that best describes the nature of the notice (e.g., Issue, Maintenance, Rent Payment).
3. Enter the **Notice Date** when the notice was created or is due.
4. Write a detailed description of the **Notice Text** to explain the reason for the notice.
5. Sign the **Notice Signature** to confirm that the notice has been acknowledged.
6. If applicable, ask the **Tenant Signature** to confirm receipt of the notice.
7. Similarly, ask the **Landlord Signature** to confirm their acknowledgment of the notice.
8. Select the **Notice Category** to classify the notice (e.g., Active, Inactive).
9. Add any additional **Notice Details** that are relevant to the notice.
10. Enter your name as the **Form Submitter** to record your role in the process.
11. Finally, select the **Notice Status** to indicate whether the notice is Active or Inactive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data**: A brief description of the notice.
* **Notice Type**: Select the type of notice (e.g., Issue, Maintenance, Rent Payment).
* **Notice Date**: Enter the date when the notice was created or is due.
* **Notice Text**: Write a detailed description of the notice to explain the reason for the notice.
* **Notice Signature**: Sign to confirm that the notice has been acknowledged.
* **Tenant Signature**: If applicable, ask the tenant to sign to confirm receipt of the notice.
* **Landlord Signature**: If applicable, ask the landlord to sign to confirm their acknowledgment of the notice.
* **Notice Category**: Select a category to classify the notice (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Notice Details**: Add any additional details relevant to the notice.
* **Form Submitter**: Enter your name to record your role in the process.
* **Notice Status**: Select the status of the notice (Active or Inactive).
