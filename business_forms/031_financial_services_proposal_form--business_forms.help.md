# Financial Services Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document financial services proposals from customers. It helps our team understand the customer's financial needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete the Contact Details section with the customer's name and contact information.
2. Indicate if the customer requires financial services (yes, no, or other).
3. Enter the submission date and time for the proposal.
4. Check if the customer has financial advisors involved in their financial decisions (yes, no, or other).
5. Select the financial institutions the customer is associated with (yes, no, or other).
6. Enter a brief proposal for the financial services the customer is requesting.
7. Check the submission status of the proposal (yes, no, or other).
8. Add any additional notes about the proposal.
9. Enter the customer's email address.
10. Enter the customer's phone number.
11. Enter the submission date and time (if applicable).
12. Check if the customer has financial advisors involved in their financial decisions (if applicable).
13. Select the financial institutions the customer is associated with (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): This section is for the customer's name and contact information.
* **Financial Services** (`financial_services`, select_multiple, required: false): Check if the customer requires financial services (yes, no, or other).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the proposal was submitted.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the proposal was submitted.
* **Financial Advisors** (`financial_advisors`, select_one, required: false): Check if the customer has financial advisors involved in their financial decisions (yes, no, or other).
* **Financial Institutions** (`financial_institutions`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the financial institutions the customer is associated with (yes, no, or other).
* **Financial Services Proposal** (`financial_services_proposal`, text, required: false): Enter a brief proposal for the financial services the customer is requesting.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required: false): Check the submission status of the proposal (yes, no, or other).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the proposal.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Submission Date 2** (`submission_date_2`, date, required: false): Enter the second submission date (if applicable).
* **Submission Time 2** (`submission_time_2`, time, required: false): Enter the second submission time (if applicable).
* **Financial Advisors 2** (`financial_advisors_2`, select_one, required: false): Check if the customer has financial advisors involved in their financial decisions (if applicable).
* **Financial Institutions 2** (`financial_institutions_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the financial institutions the customer is associated with (if applicable).
