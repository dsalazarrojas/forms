# Gift Card Seller Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for sellers to register with a marketplace or platform for selling gift cards. It captures basic contact information and ensures that the seller agrees to the platform's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Read through the terms and conditions carefully and select "Yes" if you agree.
3. Leave a note with your signature in the "Signature" field.
4. Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marketplace Name** (`marketplace_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the marketplace or platform you are selling gift cards on.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your full name as the seller.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your address.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for displaying the terms and conditions of the platform. Read through it carefully and select "Yes" in the "Do you agree with the terms and conditions?" field if you agree.
* **Do you agree with the terms and conditions?** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select "Yes" if you agree with the terms and conditions.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required: `false`): Leave a note with your signature.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of your business.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the address of your business.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the phone number of your business.
