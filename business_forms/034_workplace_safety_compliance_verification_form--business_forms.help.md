# Workplace Safety Compliance Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Safety Compliance Verification Form is used to ensure that your workplace is compliant with all necessary safety regulations and standards. It's essential to complete this form to maintain a safe working environment for yourself and your colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure that you understand each section and question.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you're unsure about any question or section, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, Optional): Please provide any general information that you think is relevant to your workplace safety compliance. This can include any additional comments or notes that you'd like to share.

### Safety Practices
* **Safety Practices** (`safety_practices`, text, Optional): Describe your workplace safety practices, such as the procedures for reporting injuries, maintaining safety equipment, and conducting workplace inspections.

### Employee Training
* **Employee Training** (`employee_training`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose all the training programs that you've completed related to workplace safety:
	+ Safety Training - Fire Evacuation Procedures
	+ Safety Training - Hazardous Materials
	+ Safety Training - Personal Protective Equipment

### Safety Equipment
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, number, Optional): If you have safety equipment, please specify the number of each type of equipment you have.

### Reporting Injuries
* **Reporting Injuries** (`reporting_injuries`, select_one, Optional): Please answer 'True' if you've reported any injuries that occurred in the workplace, 'False' otherwise.

### Safety Standards
* **Safety Standards** (`safety_standards`, text, Optional): Describe your workplace's safety standards and procedures.

### Emergency Procedures
* **Emergency Procedures** (`emergency_procedures`, time, Optional): Specify the time allocated for emergency procedures, such as fire drills or first aid training.

### Safety Meeting Minutes
* **Safety Meeting Minutes** (`safety_meeting_minutes`, text, Optional): Please provide a brief summary of your recent safety meeting minutes, including any important discussions or decisions made.

### Workplace Inspection
* **Workplace Inspection** (`workplace_inspection`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose all the areas of the workplace that you've inspected recently:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
