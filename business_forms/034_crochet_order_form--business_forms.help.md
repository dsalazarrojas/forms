# Crochet Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers provide accurate and complete information about their crochet orders. It includes fields for customer contact details, order specifics, payment information, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the pattern you would like to order.
3. Specify the quantity and color of the pattern.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments about your order.
5. Choose your payment method.
6. Select the shipping method.
7. Complete any additional fields as required or desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your address.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to place your order.
* **Pattern Selected**: Select the pattern you would like to order from the options provided.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the pattern you would like to order.
* **Color**: Enter the color of the pattern you would like to order.
* **Thread Color**: Enter the color of the thread or material you would like to use.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method from the options provided (Payment by Credit Card, Payment by Check, or Payment by Cash).
* **Shipping Method**: Select your preferred shipping method from the options provided (UPS, USPS, or FedEx).
* **Customer Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Pattern Price**: Enter the price of the pattern you selected.
* **Shipping Cost**: Enter the shipping cost.
* **Tax Rate**: Enter the tax rate applicable to your order.
* **Total Cost**: The total cost of your order, automatically calculated.
* **Order Status**: Select the status of your order from the options provided (In Progress, Complete, or Cancelled).
* **Order Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Customer Order Number**: Enter your customer order number.
* **Customer ID**: Enter your customer ID.
* **Crochet Site URL**: Enter the URL of the crochet site.
* **Order Number**: Enter your order number.
* **Order Number Prefix**: Enter your order number prefix.
* **Order Number**: Enter your order number, if applicable.
* **Pattern Image**: Enter any additional image or information about the pattern you selected.

Note: All fields marked as required must be completed before submitting the form.
