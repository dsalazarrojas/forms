# real_estate_sales_manager_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for a real estate sales manager position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Applicant Information section with your basic details.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Info section.
3. Share your employment details in the Employment Details section.
4. Describe your work experience in the Experience section.
5. Specify your sales manager experience in the Sales Manager Experience section.
6. List your educational background in the Education section.
7. Provide references for your work experience.
8. Add any additional information you feel is relevant in the Additional Info section.

* **Applicant Information** (required/optional): This section is for you to provide your name, title, and other basic details.
* **Contact Info** (required/optional): This is where you provide your contact information for future communication.
* **Employment Details** (required/optional): Share your current or past employment information.
* **Experience** (required/optional): Describe your work experience, especially in sales.
* **Sales Manager Experience** (required/optional): Provide specific details about your experience in sales management.
* **Education** (required/optional): List your educational background.
* **References** (required/optional): List your professional references.
* **Additional Info** (required/optional): Provide any other relevant information you feel is important.
