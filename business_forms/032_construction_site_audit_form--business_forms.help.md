# Construction Site Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the overall condition and compliance of a construction site. It will guide auditors in evaluating various aspects of the site, including safety, quality, and environmental compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the audit date and time from the calendar fields.
2. Enter the name of the person conducting the audit and their company.
3. Enter the name of the construction project and its address.
4. Identify the general contractor and site manager.
5. Select the current construction phase from the options provided.
6. Enter the total number of workers on site.
7. List any subcontractors present on site.
8. Rate the overall site security, housekeeping, material storage, waste management, equipment condition, safety compliance, environmental compliance, quality assurance, schedule adherence, and budget status.
9. Identify any issues found during the audit and describe each issue.
10. Provide corrective actions required for each issue.
11. Indicate if a follow-up inspection is required and schedule a date if applicable.
12. Make a final determination of the site's overall condition.
13. Add any additional comments or notes from the audit.
14. Sign and date the form as the auditor and site manager (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

Date of site audit

### 2. **Audit Time** (id: 2, time, required)
Time audit started

### 3. **Auditor Name** (id: 3, text, required)

### 4. **Auditor Company** (id: 4, text, required)

### 5. **Project Name** (id: 5, text, required)
Name of construction project

### 6. **Project Address** (id: 6, text, required)

Primary contractor company

Name of site manager
