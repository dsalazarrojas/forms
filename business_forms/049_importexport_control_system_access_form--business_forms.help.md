# ImportExport Control System Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to import and export systems or areas within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the necessary clearance to access the systems or areas you are requesting access to.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information and the information of the business form requestor.
3. Select the correct "access level" and "approval status" for your request.
4. Enter the correct "date import request" and "date export request" for your activity.
5. Enter the correct "time import request" and "time export request" for your activity.
6. Provide a brief reason for your request in the "reason for request" field.
7. Enter any additional notes in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Form Requester** (`business_form_requester`, text, optional): This field is for the person or department requesting access.
* **Business Form Requestor** (`business_form_requestor`, text, optional): This field is for the person or department that will be accessing the system or area.
* **Access Level** (`access_level`, select_one, optional): Select the appropriate access level for your request. Options are: high, low.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of your request. Options are: approved, pending.
* **Date Import Request** (`date_import_request`, date, optional): Enter the date of the import request.
* **Date Export Request** (`date_export_request`, date, optional): Enter the date of the export request.
* **Time Import Request** (`time_import_request`, time, optional): Enter the time of the import request.
* **Time Export Request** (`time_export_request`, time, optional): Enter the time of the export request.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Provide a brief reason for your request.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Business Form Requestor Notes** (`business_form_requestor_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the business form requestor.
* **Business Form Requester Notes** (`business_form_requester_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the business form requester.
