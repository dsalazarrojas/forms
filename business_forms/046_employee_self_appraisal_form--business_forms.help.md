# Employee Self Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help employees evaluate their own performance and provide feedback on their job performance. This form is a self-assessment tool that allows employees to reflect on their strengths and weaknesses, and to identify areas where they need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide honest and accurate information about your performance.
2. Use the options provided to select the most relevant ratings and comments.
3. Make sure to sign the form with your signature to confirm that you have completed it accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, required: false): This section is for general information about yourself, such as your name, job title, and department.
* **Self Rating** (`self_rating`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the ratings that best describe your performance. You can choose one or more of the options provided.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, select_one, required: false): Choose one rating that best describes your performance.
* **Job Performance Comments** (`job_performance_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any comments about your job performance.
* **Development Goals** (`development_goals`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the goals that you have accomplished or are working towards.
* **Job Performance Level** (`job_performance_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose one level that best describes your job performance.
* **Job Performance Comments** (`job_performance_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any comments about your job performance.
* **Overall Comments** (`overall_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any general comments about your overall performance.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required: false): This is for your manager's signature, which confirms that they have reviewed your appraisal form.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required: false): This is for your signature, which confirms that you have completed the form accurately.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you submitted this form.
* **Date Revised** (`date_revised`, date, required: false): Enter the date when this form was revised.
* **Reviewer Notes** (`reviewer_notes`, text, required: false): This is for your manager's notes, which can be used for future reference.
* **Job Performance Rubric** (`job_performance_rubric`, text, required: false): This is a rubric that can be used to evaluate your job performance.
* **Self Rating Rubric** (`self_rating_rubric`, text, required: false): This is a rubric that can be used to evaluate your self-assessment.
* **Performance Raters** (`performance_raters`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the ratings that best describe your performance.
* **Job Performance Level Rubric** (`job_performance_level_rubric`, text, required: false): This is a rubric that can be used to evaluate your job performance level.
* **Self Rating Justification** (`self_rating_rubric_justification`, text, required: false): Provide any justification for your self-assessment.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, text, required: false): This is for your manager's comments, which can be used for feedback.
* **Overall Comments** (`overall_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any general comments about your overall performance.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are optional and can be filled out or left blank at the discretion of the employee.
