# Travel Authorization Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request travel authorization for business or personal trips. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your travel request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked as required in the form.
2. Fill out the optional fields with the necessary information.
3. Review your form carefully before submitting it for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Authorization Request Form** (text, text, required): Enter a brief description of your travel request.
* **Category** (Category 1, Category 2, Category 3, optional): Select the category of your travel, if applicable.
* **Description** (text, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your travel.
* **Employee** (Employee 1, Employee 2, Employee 3, optional): Select the employee(s) traveling.
* **Start Date** (date, date, optional): Enter the start date of your travel.
* **End Date** (date, date, optional): Enter the end date of your travel.
* **Start Time** (time, time, optional): Enter the start time of your travel.
* **End Time** (time, time, optional): Enter the end time of your travel.
* **Travel Type** (Domestic, International, optional): Select the type of travel (domestic or international).
* **Travel Purpose** (Business, Leisure, Vacation, optional): Select the purpose of your travel (business, leisure, or vacation).
* **Travel Destination** (text, text, optional): Enter the destination of your travel.
* **Travel Details** (text, text, optional): Provide any additional details about your travel.
* **Approved By** (text, text, optional): Enter the name of the person authorizing your travel.
* **Approval Date** (date, date, optional): Enter the date of the approval.
* **Approved At** (date, date, optional): Enter the date the request was approved.
* **Status** (Pending, Approved, Rejected, optional): Select the status of your travel request (pending, approved, or rejected).

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in all fields.
* Use the optional fields only if necessary.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it for approval.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your travel administrator.
