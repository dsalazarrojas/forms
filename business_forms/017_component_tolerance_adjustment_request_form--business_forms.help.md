# Component Tolerance Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request adjustments to component tolerances, ensuring that all necessary information is gathered for a thorough review and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, including:
	* Request ID
	* Request Date
	* Requester Name
	* Requester Title
	* Department
	* Email Address
	* Phone Number
	* Component Name
	* Component Part Number
	* Current Tolerance
	* Requested Tolerance
	* Tolerance Type
	* Adjustment Reason
	* Impact Assessment
	* Cost Impact
	* Quality Impact
	* Implementation Timeline
	* Approval Status
2. Select relevant options for:
	* Department (Engineering, Manufacturing, Quality Assurance, etc.)
	* Tolerance Type (Dimensional, Geometric, Surface Finish, etc.)
	* Adjustment Reason (Manufacturing Capability, Design Change, Cost Reduction, etc.)
	* Impact Assessment (No Impact, Minor Impact, Moderate Impact, etc.)
	* Cost Impact (Cost Reduction, No Cost Impact, Minor Cost Increase, etc.)
	* Quality Impact (Quality Improvement, No Change, Minor Quality Risk, etc.)
	* Implementation Timeline (Immediate, Next Production Run, etc.)
	* Approval Status (Pending Review, Under Engineering Review, etc.)
3. Provide a detailed explanation for the request in the "Detailed Justification" field.
4. If applicable, attach supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request ID (1)**: A unique identifier for the request, ensuring that each submission is distinguishable and trackable.
* **Request Date (2)**: The date the request was submitted, helping to track the progression of requests.
* **Requester Name (3)**: The full name of the person making the request, providing accountability.
* **Requester Title (4)**: The job title or position of the requester, ensuring that their authority is acknowledged.
* **Department (5)**: The department responsible for the component, facilitating review and approval.
* **Email Address (6)**: The work email address of the requester, for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (7)**: The contact phone number of the requester, for further questions or clarifications.
* **Component Name (8)**: The name of the component requiring adjustment, clearly identifying the subject.
* **Component Part Number (9)**: The manufacturer's part number of the component, ensuring accurate identification.
* **Current Tolerance (10)**: A description of the current tolerance specification, providing context.
* **Requested Tolerance (11)**: A description of the requested tolerance change, detailing the modification.
* **Tolerance Type (12)**: The type of tolerance adjustment being requested, e.g., dimensional, geometric, etc.
* **Adjustment Reason (13)**: The reason for the requested adjustment, explaining the necessity of the change.
* **Impact Assessment (14)**: An evaluation of the potential impact of the change, considering its severity.
* **Cost Impact (15)**: An estimation of the cost implications of the change, including potential increases or decreases.
* **Quality Impact (16)**: An evaluation of the potential effect on product quality, considering risks or improvements.
* **Implementation Timeline (17)**: A plan for when the change should be implemented, ensuring a smooth transition.
* **Approval Status (18)**: The current status of the request, tracking progress and decisions.
* **Additional Comments (19)**: Any further information that may be relevant to the request.
* **Supporting Documents (20)**: Attach any supporting documents that may be required, such as design files or test results.
* **Detailed Justification (16)**: A detailed explanation of the request, providing context and supporting the reasons for the adjustment.
* **Affected Assemblies (17)**: A list of assemblies or products affected by the change, ensuring comprehensive understanding.
* **Approval Status (18)**: The current approval status, tracking the progress of the request.
* **Impact Assessment (14)**: A reassessment of the potential impact, ensuring that the change is properly evaluated.
* **Cost Impact (15)**: A revised estimation of the cost implications, accounting for new information.
* **Quality Impact (16)**: A revised evaluation of the quality implications, ensuring that the change does not compromise quality.
* **Implementation Timeline (17)**: A revised plan for implementation, considering the new context.
* **Additional Comments (19)**: Further comments or information that may be required, as needed.
* **Supporting Documents (20)**: A revision of supporting documents, as necessary.
* **Supporting Documents (22)**: An optional field for attaching supporting documents, if applicable.
* **Approval Status (23)**: A final status update, confirming approval or rejection.
* **Additional Comments (24)**: Final comments or information that may be relevant to the request.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Attach supporting documents when applicable.
* Provide a detailed explanation for the request in the "Detailed Justification" field.
* Select relevant options for each field, considering the specific context of the request.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors or omissions.
