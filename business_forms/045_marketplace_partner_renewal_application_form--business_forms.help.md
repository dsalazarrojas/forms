# Marketplace Partner Renewal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketplace Partner Renewal Application Form is used by existing partners to submit their application for renewal. This form is only intended for partners that already have an established partnership with the marketplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, especially the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) .
3. Submit the form once you've completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, ): Enter your company's name as it appears on all your marketing materials.
* **Address** (`address`, text, ): Provide your company's full address, including street number and name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, ): Enter your company's email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, ): Enter your company's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Fax** (`fax`, text, ): Enter your company's fax number for contact purposes.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, ): Enter your company's zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, text, ): Select your company's country of operation.
* **Date of Renewal** (`date_of_renewal`, date, *): Choose the date of the renewal term.
* **Renewal Term** (`renewal_term`, number, *): Select the renewal term (e.g. 1, 2, 3).
* **Renewal Status** (`renewal_status`, select_one, *): Choose between 'Active' or 'Inactive' for the renewal status.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, ): Provide any additional comments or information about your partnership or renewal.
