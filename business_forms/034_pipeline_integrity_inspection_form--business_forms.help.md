# Pipeline Integrity Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and manage pipeline integrity inspection data. It ensures that critical information on pipeline inspections is recorded, making it easier to identify patterns, trends, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields (those marked as "required" in the form).
2. For non-required fields, provide any additional information you deem necessary.
3. Review and update your answers as needed.
4. Submit the form to save your data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Location**: This field is where you should type in the specific location of the pipeline inspection, helping you and others track inspections across the pipeline's length.
* **Pipeline ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the pipeline, ensuring accurate tracking and identification of the pipeline inspected.
* **Inspector**: Please provide the name or identifier of the person or entity conducting the inspection.
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection, using the format: YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection, using 24-hour format: HH:MM.
* **Notes**: Add any relevant notes about the inspection, such as observations or issues found.
* **Inspection Status**: Select the status of the inspection (Pass, Fail, Pass/Conditionally Pass, or Pass/Conditionally Fail) to provide a clear indication of the pipeline's condition.
* **Remarks**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
* **Comments**: Add any further comments or thoughts about the inspection.
* **Next Inspection Due Date**: If applicable, enter the date when the next inspection is due.
* **Next Inspection Due Time**: Enter the time when the next inspection is due, using 24-hour format.
* **Next Inspection Frequency**: Select the frequency of the next scheduled inspection (Annually, Bi-Annually, Semi-Annuually, Quarterly, Monthly, or As-Required).
* **Inspection Frequency**: Select the frequency of the inspections (Annually, Bi-Annually, Semi-Annuually, Quarterly, Monthly, or As-Required) to track recurring inspections.
* **Inspection Frequency Unit**: Choose the unit for the inspection frequency (Years, Months, or Days).
* **Inspection Frequency Interval**: Select the interval for the frequency (Once, Twice, Thrice, Four times, Five times, Six times, Seven times, or Eight times).
* **Last Inspection Date**: If applicable, enter the date of the last inspection.
* **Last Inspection Frequency**: Select the frequency of the last inspection (Annually, Bi-Annually, Semi-Annuually, Quarterly, Monthly, or As-Required).
* **Last Inspection Frequency Unit**: Choose the unit for the last inspection frequency (Years, Months, or Days).
* **Last Inspection Interval**: Select the interval for the last inspection (Once, Twice, Thrice, Four times, Five times, Six times, Seven times, or Eight times).
* **Last Inspection Interval 2**: Select the interval for the last inspection (Once, Twice, Thrice, Four times, Five times, Six times, Seven times, or Eight times).
* **Last Inspection Frequency 2**: Select the frequency of the last inspection (Annually, Bi-Annually, Semi-Annuually, Quarterly, Monthly, or As-Required).
* **Last Inspection Frequency 2 Unit**: Choose the unit for the last inspection frequency (Years, Months, or Days).
* **Last Inspection Interval 2**: Select the interval for the last inspection (Once, Twice, Thrice, Four times, Five times, Six times, Seven times, or Eight times).
