# Stock Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track information about a store's stock inventory, including stock type, quantity, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields as prompted.
2. Select the correct options for each field where applicable.
3. Review and ensure all fields are accurately filled before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stock Form Label** (`stock_form_label_1`, text, optional): Enter a brief label for this stock form.
* **Store Size** (`store_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of the store.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, optional): Enter the number of employees in the store.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the store.
* **Stock Type** (`stock_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of stock being managed.
* **Stock Quantity** (`stock_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the current stock quantity.
* **Store Capacity** (`store_capacity`, number, optional): Enter the store's capacity.
* **Store Inventory Date** (`store_inventory_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the store's inventory.
* **Store Inventory Time** (`store_inventory_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the store's inventory.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the store's phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter the store's email address.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the store's stock.
