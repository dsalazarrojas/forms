# New Vendor Registration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Vendor Registration Request Form is used to create a new vendor account in the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vendor's details, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the vendor's address, city, and zip code.
3. Select the vendor type and contract start and end dates.
4. Choose the contract duration and assign a tool to the vendor.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.
6. Review your information for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (vendor_name, text, required): Enter the name of the new vendor.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter the vendor's email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter the vendor's phone number.
* **Address** (address, text, required): Enter the vendor's address.
* **City** (city, text, required): Enter the city of the vendor.
* **State** (state, text, required): Enter the state of the vendor.
* **Zip** (zip, number, required): Enter the vendor's zip code.
* **Contact First Name** (contact_first_name, text, required): Enter the contact person's first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (contact_last_name, text, required): Enter the contact person's last name.
* **Vendor Type** (vendor_type, select multiple, required): Choose the type of vendor (e.g. Option 1 or Option 2).
* **Contract Start Date** (contract_start_date, date, required): Choose the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (contract_end_date, date, required): Choose the end date of the contract.
* **Contract Duration** (contract_duration, select one, required): Choose the contract duration (e.g. 1, 2, or 3).
* **Notes** (notes, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the vendor.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool, text, required): Assign a tool to the vendor.
* **Category** (category, text, required): Enter the category of the vendor.
* **Form ID** (form_id, text, required): Enter the form ID.
* **Form Title** (form_title, text, required): Enter the form title.
* **Description** (description, text, required): Enter a description of the form.
* **Output File** (output_file, text, required): Enter the output file.
* **Created By** (created_by, text, required): Enter the person who created the form.
* **Updated By** (updated_by, text, required): Enter the person who updated the form.
* **Created Date** (created_date, date, required): Enter the date the form was created.
* **Updated Date** (updated_date, date, required): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Status** (status, text, required): Enter the status of the form.
