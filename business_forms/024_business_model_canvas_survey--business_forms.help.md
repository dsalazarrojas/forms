This form is designed to help business owners, entrepreneurs, and teams understand and visualize their business model. It's based on the Business Model Canvas, a widely-used business model framework. The form will guide users through a series of questions to identify and document their key business model components, making it easier to innovate, pivot, or transform their business as needed. The form is suitable for businesses of all sizes and stages, from idea development to maturity.

# Business Model Canvas Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help business owners, entrepreneurs, and teams understand and visualize their business model using the Business Model Canvas framework.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, using your business model as a reference.
2. Choose the correct answers for each question based on your business's characteristics.
3. Be specific and concise in your responses, providing clear and detailed answers.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they accurately reflect your business model.
5. Use the form as a reference point to identify areas for improvement and innovation in your business model.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): This is the name of your business. Please enter the name of your company or product.
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): This is your name and contact information, which will help us identify you as the respondent.
* **Your Role** (`respondent_role`, select_one, required): Choose the role that best describes your position in the company:
	+ Founder
	+ Co-founder
	+ CEO
	+ Executive
	+ Manager
	+ Employee
	+ Consultant
	+ Other
* **Value Proposition** (`value_proposition`, text, required): Describe the unique value your business provides to its customers.
* **Customer Segments** (`customer_segments`, select_multiple, required): Select all the customer segments that are most important to your business:
	+ Mass market
	+ Niche market
	+ Segmented
	+ Diversified
	+ Multi-sided platforms
	+ B2B
	+ B2C
	+ Government
* **Customer Segment Description** (`customer_segment_description`, text, required): Describe your primary customer segments in detail.
* **Channels** (`channels`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the channels you use to reach your customers:
	+ Website
	+ Social media
	+ Email marketing
	+ Direct sales
	+ Retail stores
	+ Distributors
	+ Partners
	+ Mobile app
* **Most Effective Channel** (`channel_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Choose the channel that brings the most customers:
	+ Website
	+ Social media
	+ Direct sales
	+ Referrals
	+ Advertising
	+ Events
	+ Other
* **Customer Relationships** (`customer_relationships`, select_one, required): Describe the type of relationship you maintain with your customers:
	+ Personal assistance
	+ Dedicated personal assistance
	+ Self-service
	+ Automated services
	+ Communities
	+ Co-creation
* **Revenue Streams** (`revenue_streams`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the revenue streams used by your business:
	+ Asset sale
	+ Usage fee
	+ Subscription fees
	+ Licensing
	+ Brokerage fees
	+ Advertising
	+ Rental
	+ Freemium
* **Primary Revenue Stream** (`primary_revenue_stream`, select_one, required): Choose the largest source of revenue for your business:
	+ Product sales
	+ Service fees
	+ Subscriptions
	+ Licensing
	+ Advertising
	+ Commission
	+ Other
* **Key Resources** (`key_resources`, select_multiple, required): Select all the key resources essential to your business:
	+ Physical assets
	+ Intellectual property
	+ Human resources
	+ Financial resources
	+ Digital assets
	+ Brand
	+ Network
* **Key Activities** (`key_activities`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the key activities that drive your business:
	+ Production
	+ Problem solving
	+ Platform management
	+ Marketing
	+ Sales
	+ Research and development
	+ Customer support
* **Key Partnerships** (`key_partnerships`, text, required): Describe the key partners and suppliers that contribute to your business.
* **Partnership Types** (`partnership_types`, select_multiple, required): Select all the partnership types used by your business:
	+ Strategic alliances
	+ Coopetition
	+ Joint ventures
	+ Buyer-supplier relationships
	+ None
* **Cost Structure** (`cost_structure`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the major cost drivers for your business:
	+ Fixed costs
	+ Variable costs
	+ Economies of scale
	+ Economies of scope
	+ Labor costs
	+ Marketing costs
	+ Technology costs
* **Largest Expense Category** (`largest_expense`, select_one, required): Choose the biggest expense category for your business:
	+ Salaries and wages
	+ Rent and facilities
	+ Marketing and advertising
	+ Technology and software
	+ Materials and inventory
	+ Professional services
	+ Other
* **Competitive Advantage** (`competitive_advantage`, text, required): Describe what makes your business different from competitors.
* **Business Model Stage** (`business_model_stage`, select_one, required): Choose the current stage of your business model:
	+ Idea stage
	+ Startup
	+ Growth
	+ Maturity
	+ Pivot or transformation
* **Biggest Business Model Challenges** (`biggest_challenges`, text, required): Describe the biggest challenges facing your business model.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): Describe areas where your business model can be improved.

Please note that the form is designed to be self-explanatory, and you can use it to identify areas for improvement and innovation in your business model.
