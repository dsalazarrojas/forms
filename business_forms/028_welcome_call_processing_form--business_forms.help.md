# Welcome Call Processing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Welcome Call Processing Form is used to collect information from guests during the welcome call to facilitate a smooth onboarding process and provide personalized support. This form will be filled out by guests to supply the necessary details required to process their welcome call.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
- Start by filling in the required fields, which are indicated by a red asterisk (*) in the label.
- Enter your information into the corresponding fields. If a field has an optional label, you have the freedom to leave it blank.
- Once you've filled out all the required fields, submit the form.

- **<none>** (welcome_call_processing_form_1): This field is currently empty. However, it might have been intended for some initial information about the guest's experience or reason for the call.
* **Guest Details 1** (welcome_call_processing_form_2): Enter any relevant details about the guest, such as their name or contact information.
* **Guest Details 2** (welcome_call_processing_form_3): Another opportunity to capture any extra information about the guest.
* **Welcome Call Details** (welcome_call_processing_form_4): This field seems to be intended for capturing the details of the welcome call itself. You might want to record the purpose of the call, the outcome, or some other important points discussed during the conversation.
* **Additional Notes** (welcome_call_processing_form_5): A place to jot down any additional notes or next steps for the guest's onboarding process.
* **Confirmation** (welcome_call_processing_form_6): This field might be intended to capture the guest's confirmation or acknowledgment of some agreement or next step.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask your contact person for assistance.
* The more accurate and detailed information you provide, the better we can tailor our support to suit your needs.
