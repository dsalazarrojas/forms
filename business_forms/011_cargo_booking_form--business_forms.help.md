# Cargo Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about cargo shipments, including shipper and consignee details, cargo description, weight, and logistics. It is essential for accurate and efficient cargo handling and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Shipper Information**: Select "Shipper's Full Name", enter the shipper's full name, and provide their company name. Please ensure that the email address and phone number of the shipper are accurate.
2. **Consignee Information**: Enter the consignee's full name and provide their delivery address, including street, city, and country.
3. **Cargo Details**: Select the type of cargo, describe the goods being shipped, and provide the total weight, volume, and number of packages. Choose the packaging type and ensure the cargo description is detailed but concise.
4. **Logistics**: Select the port of origin, destination, and preferred mode of transport. Please provide the target shipment date if applicable.
5. **Insurance and Special Instructions**: Indicate if insurance is required and provide any special handling instructions or requests.
6. **Confirmation**: Confirm that the cargo details are accurate and agree to the shipping terms by selecting "I confirm the cargo details are correct" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipper's Full Name** (required): Enter the full name of the shipper.
* **Company Name** (optional): Enter the company name of the shipper.
* **Email Address** (required): Enter the shipper's email address.
* **Phone Number** (required): Enter the shipper's phone number.
* **Consignee's Full Name** (required): Enter the full name of the consignee.
* **Consignee Delivery Address** (required): Enter the full delivery address of the consignee, including street, city, and country.
* **Type of Cargo** (required): Select the type of cargo from the dropdown options.
* **Detailed Description of Goods** (required): Briefly describe the goods being shipped.
* **Total Weight (kg)** (required): Provide the total gross weight of the cargo.
* **Total Volume (m3)** (optional): Provide the total volume of the cargo if applicable.
* **Number of Packages** (required): Enter the total count of items being shipped.
* **Packaging Type** (required): Select the packaging type from the dropdown options.
* **Port of Origin** (required): Enter the city and country of departure.
* **Port of Destination** (required): Enter the city and country of arrival.
* **Preferred Mode of Transport** (required): Select the preferred mode of transport from the dropdown options.
* **Target Shipment Date** (optional): Provide the target shipment date.
* **Is Insurance Required?** (required): Indicate if insurance is required.
* **Handling Instructions or Special Requests** (optional): Provide any special handling instructions or requests.
* **I Confirm the Cargo Details Are Correct** (required): Confirm that the cargo details are accurate and agree to the shipping terms.

Note: Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues with cargo handling and shipping.
