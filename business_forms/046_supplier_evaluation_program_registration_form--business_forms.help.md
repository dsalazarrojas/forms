# supplier_evaluation_program_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture and organize essential information about suppliers for evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each field carefully and fill in the required information.
* Ensure you only fill out this form once for each supplier.
* Double-check your input for accuracy and consistency.
* Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name**: Enter the name of the supplier you are evaluating.
* **Contact Reported To**: Enter the name of the person or department you are reporting the supplier contact to.
* **Contact Reported By**: Enter your name and title or position in the company.
* **Supplier Type**: Select the type of supplier (e.g., material supplier, service provider, etc.).
* **Supplier Location**: Enter the supplier's location or address.
* **Business Details**: Briefly describe the business details of the supplier.
* **Documentation**: Upload any relevant documents or links to documents related to the supplier.
* **Supplier Status**: Indicate the current status of the supplier (e.g., active, inactive, pending).
* **Evaluation Status**: Indicate the current evaluation status of the supplier (e.g., pending, ongoing, completed).
* **Evaluation Period**: Specify the evaluation period for the supplier.
* **Evaluation Cycle**: Specify the evaluation cycle for the supplier.
* **Consent to Participate**: Confirm that you are willing to participate in the evaluation process.
* **Vendor Manager Name**: Enter the name of the vendor manager.
* **Vendor Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the vendor manager (must be a valid email address).
