# Curb Adapter Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from end-users about the Curb Adapter, allowing us to understand their experience and make improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each page and field. If you need help or clarification, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Installation Details**: (`install_details`, text, required) Please describe your installation experience with the Curb Adapter, including any challenges or successes you encountered.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: (`rating`, number, optional) Rate your overall satisfaction with the Curb Adapter, using a numerical scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Curb Adapter Performance**: (`performance_comments`, note, optional) Provide any comments or feedback about the Curb Adapter's performance, such as improvements or areas for improvement.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible in the Installation Details field.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please provide specific examples or steps for reproduction.
* Keep your comments in the Curb Adapter Performance field clear and concise.
