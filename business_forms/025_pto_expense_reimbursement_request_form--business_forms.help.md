# PTO Expense Reimbursement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PTO Expense Reimbursement Request Form is designed to facilitate the process of submitting expense reimbursement claims related to Paid Time Off. It helps employees to provide necessary information about their expenses, allowing the company to process and verify their claims efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Employee Info section with your details to identify yourself as the requestor.
2. In the Travel Dates section, enter the dates of your travel period.
3. In the Expense Details section, provide a brief description of your expenses.
4. In the Total Expense section, enter the total cost of your expenses.
5. In the Expense Breakdown section, select the categories where your expenses were incurred.
6. In the Total Expense Breakdown section, calculate the total cost of your selected expense categories.
7. Select the Approval Level of your manager's approval.
8. If required, fill out your Manager's Info for automatic information retrieval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Info (1, Employee Info, required):** Enter your name and employee ID to identify you as the requestor.
* **Travel Dates (2, travel_dates, optional):** Enter your travel dates in a date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Expense Details (3, expense_details, optional):** Briefly describe your expenses, including any relevant receipts or proofs of purchase.
* **Total Expense (4, total_expense, optional):** Enter the total cost of your expenses.
* **Expense Breakdown (5, expense_breakdown, optional):** Select all categories where your expenses were incurred.
* **Total Expense Breakdown (6, total_expense_breakdown, optional):** Calculate the total cost of your selected expense categories.
* **Approval Level (7, approval_level, optional):** Select your manager's approval level, which will be used for verification and processing.
* **Manager Info (8, manager_info, optional):** If your manager's info is not filled out automatically, enter their name, title, and department for further verification.

## Tips
- Double-check your details for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure that your manager's info is up to date and correct.
- Keep receipts and proofs of purchase for all expenses to support your claim.
- Review your company's internal policies and procedures for specific guidelines on PTO expense reimbursement.
