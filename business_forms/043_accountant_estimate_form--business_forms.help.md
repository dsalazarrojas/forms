# Accountant Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accountant Estimate Form is designed to gather essential information from clients to provide an accurate estimate of accounting services needed. This form is divided into sections for client information, service request, timeline and budget, and additional details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of business you are representing (individual, LLC, partnership, etc.).
3. Choose the accounting services you require (tax preparation, bookkeeping, etc.).
4. Select the frequency of the service (one-time, monthly, etc.).
5. Indicate your approximate annual revenue or income.
6. If applicable, enter the number of employees in your organization.
7. If you are already using an accounting software, select the type from the provided options.
8. Select the preferred start date for the service.
9. Choose a budget range for the service.
10. Indicate the urgency of the service request.
11. Provide any additional details or requirements for the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (client_name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (email, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Business Name (if applicable)** (business_name, text, optional): If you are representing a business, enter its name.
* **Business Type** (business_type, select_one, required): Select the type of business you are representing from the provided options.
* **Accounting Services Needed** (services_needed, select_multiple, required): Select all the accounting services you require from the provided options.
* **Service Frequency** (frequency, select_one, required): Select the frequency of the service from the provided options.
* **Approximate Annual Revenue or Income** (annual_revenue, select_one, required): Select the approximate annual revenue or income from the provided options.
* **Number of Employees (if applicable)** (number_of_employees, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of employees in your organization.
* **Current Accounting Software** (current_accounting_software, select_one, optional): If you are already using an accounting software, select the type from the provided options.
* **Preferred Start Date** (preferred_start_date, date, required): Select the preferred start date for the service.
* **Budget Range** (budget_range, select_one, optional): Choose a budget range for the service.
* **How Urgent Is This Request** (urgency, select_one, required): Indicate the urgency of the service request from the provided options.
* **Additional Details or Special Requirements** (additional_details, text, optional): Provide any additional details or requirements for the service.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (how_did_you_hear, select_one, optional): Select how you heard about us from the provided options.
