# Business Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Expense Claim Form is designed to collect information about business expenses incurred by employees. This form helps employees report their business expenses accurately and efficiently, ensuring that the company can process and reimburse them correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, employee ID, department, and manager's name.
2. Provide the date of the submission and the expense period.
3. Select the primary expense category.
4. Enter details about each expense, including date, description, amount, and receipts attached.
5. Calculate the total claim amount.
6. Confirm that all receipts are attached and select the reimbursement method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, required): Enter your employee ID number as assigned by HR.
* **Department** (`Department`, required): Select your department for expense tracking.
* **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, required): Enter your manager's name for review and approval.
* **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, required): Enter the date when submitting this claim.
* **Expense Period** (`Expense Period`, required): Enter the period when these expenses were incurred.
* **Primary Expense Category** (`Primary Expense Category`, required): Select the type of expense (e.g., Travel, Meals and Entertainment).
* **Project Code or Cost Center** (`Project Code or Cost Center`, optional): Enter the associated project or cost center (if applicable).
* **Purpose of Expenses** (`Purpose of Expenses`, required): Briefly explain why these expenses were incurred.
* **Business Justification** (`Business Justification`, required): Describe how this expense benefits the company.
* **Expense 1 - Date** (`Expense 1 - Date`, required): Enter the date of the first expense.
* **Expense 1 - Description** (`Expense 1 - Description`, required): Describe the item purchased.
* **Expense 1 - Amount** (`Expense 1 - Amount`, required): Enter the amount in currency.
* **Expense 2 - Date** (`Expense 2 - Date`, optional): Enter the date of the second expense (if applicable).
* **Expense 2 - Description** (`Expense 2 - Description`, optional): Describe the item purchased (if applicable).
* **Expense 2 - Amount** (`Expense 2 - Amount`, optional): Enter the amount in currency (if applicable).
* **Total Claim Amount** (`Total Claim Amount`, required): Calculate the sum of all expenses.
* **Receipts Attached** (`Receipts Attached`, required): Confirm that all receipts are attached.
* **Reimbursement Method** (`Reimbursement Method`, required): Select how you want to receive reimbursement (e.g., Direct Deposit, Check).
