# tlu_petty_cash_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tlu_petty_cash_request form is used to collect information for petty cash requests from users. This form will help the finance department to track and manage these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the "User" field to enter your name.
2. Enter the date you are making the request for in the "Date" field.
3. Enter the amount you are requesting in the "Amount" field.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Choose whether the request has been assigned to someone else by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Assigned to" field.
8. Select the current status of the request as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Status" field.
9. If required, select any applicable categories that this request falls under in the "Category" field.
10. Enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
11. Finally, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Submit" field to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User** (`User`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the user making the request.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date you are making the request for.
* **Amount** (`Amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you are requesting.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the request.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned to** (`Assigned to`, select_one, required): Choose whether the request has been assigned to someone else by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Status** (`Status`, select_multiple, required): Select the current status of the request as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Category** (`Category`, select_multiple, required/optional): If required, select any applicable categories that this request falls under.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments about the request.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to submit the form.
