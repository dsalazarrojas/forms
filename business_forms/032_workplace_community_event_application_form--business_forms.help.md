# Workplace Community Event Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Community Event Application Form is designed for employees to submit applications for events that will take place in the workplace community. This form is used to gather necessary information for the event planning and execution process. It is essential that you complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your event application is successful.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review the form carefully to ensure you are applying for a valid event.
*   Fill out the required fields accurately and completely.
*   Make sure to select the correct options for your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Event Details**: Enter the title of your event, a brief description of what the event is about, and any other relevant details.
*   **Event Start Date**: Enter the start date of your event in the correct format (MM/DD/YY). This will help us plan for the necessary arrangements and scheduling.
*   **Event End Date**: Enter the end date of your event in the correct format (MM/DD/YY). This will help us plan for the necessary arrangements and scheduling.
*   **Event Description**: Enter a detailed description of your event, including the purpose, expected number of attendees, and any other relevant information.
*   **Event Location**: Enter the location of your event, including the address and any other relevant details.
*   **Event Capacity**: Enter the expected number of attendees for your event.
*   **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for your event.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for your event.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for your event.
*   **Event Sponsor**: Select 'Yes' if your event has a sponsor, otherwise select 'No'.
