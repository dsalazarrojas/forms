# Border Patrol Facility Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the inspection details of a border patrol facility. It will help border patrol agents to assess the conditions, security, and overall rating of the facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Facility Name** of the border patrol facility being inspected.
2. Provide the **Facility Location / Sector** where the inspection took place.
3. Enter your **Inspector Name and ID** for reference.
4. Choose the **Date of Inspection** and **Time of Inspection** for the form submission.
5. Evaluate the **Exterior Grounds and Perimeter Security** condition of the facility by selecting one of the options: Satisfactory, Needs Maintenance, or Urgent Repair Required.
6. Assess the **Interior Cleanliness and Safety** condition of the facility by selecting one of the options: Satisfactory, Needs Maintenance, or Urgent Repair Required.
7. Evaluate the **Utilities - Power, Water, HVAC** condition of the facility by selecting one of the options: All Operational, Partial Issues, or Major Outage.
8. Select all the **Security Systems Verified** that apply to the facility, such as Surveillance Cameras, Access Control Locks, Perimeter Fencing, or Communication Radios.
9. Choose the **Fire Safety and Emergency Equipment** condition by selecting one of the options: Compliant or Non-Compliant - items missing or expired.
10. Provide a **Description of Required Repairs or Maintenance** if any issues are found.
11. Choose the **Overall Facility Rating** by selecting one of the options: Pass, Conditional Pass, or Fail - Immediate Action Needed.
12. Enter the **Supervisor Reviewer Name** to sign off on the inspection report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name** (required): Enter the name of the border patrol facility being inspected.
* **Facility Location / Sector** (required): Provide the location where the inspection took place.
* **Inspector Name and ID** (required): Enter your name and ID for reference.
* **Date of Inspection** (required): Choose the date of the inspection.
* **Time of Inspection** (required): Choose the time of the inspection.
* **Exterior Grounds and Perimeter Security** (required): Evaluate the condition of the facility's exterior grounds and perimeter security.
* **Interior Cleanliness and Safety** (required): Assess the condition of the facility's interior cleanliness and safety.
* **Utilities - Power, Water, HVAC** (required): Evaluate the condition of the facility's utilities.
* **Security Systems Verified** (required): Select all applicable security systems verified, such as surveillance cameras, access control locks, and more.
* **Fire Safety and Emergency Equipment** (required): Choose the condition of the facility's fire safety and emergency equipment.
* **Description of Required Repairs or Maintenance** (optional): Provide a description of any required repairs or maintenance.
* **Overall Facility Rating** (required): Choose the overall facility rating based on the inspection findings.
* **Supervisor Reviewer Name** (required): Enter the name of the supervisor reviewer to sign off on the inspection report.
