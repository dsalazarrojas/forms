# Entertainment Standards Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entertainment Standards Acknowledgment form is used to gather information about a project in the entertainment industry, including its title, description, roles involved, partner and client contact information, and acknowledgement of entertainment standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering the **Project Title**, which should clearly describe the project.
2. In the **Project Description** field, provide a brief description of the project.
3. For **Roles**, select all the roles involved in the project, such as Client, Casting Director, or Crew.
4. Enter the **Partner Name** and **Partner Contact Info**, which should include any relevant contact details for the partner involved.
5. Enter the **Dates** for the project, which should include any relevant start and end dates.
6. If necessary, enter the **Signatures** for the project.
7. Enter the **Client Signature** and **Client Contact Info**, which should include any relevant contact details for the client.
8. In the **Acknowledge** field, enter a brief message acknowledging the entertainment standards.
9. Select the **Client Acknowledgement** option indicating whether you have read and agreed to the entertainment standards.
10. Finally, enter any **Cast Credit** information for the cast members.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (text, required): Enter the title of the project.
* **Project Description** (text, required): Provide a brief description of the project.
* **Roles** (select_multiple, required): Select all the roles involved in the project, such as Client, Casting Director, or Crew.
* **Partner Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the partner involved.
* **Partner Contact Info** (text, optional): Enter the contact information for the partner.
* **Dates** (date, optional): Enter the dates for the project.
* **Signatures** (time, optional): Enter the signatures for the project.
* **Client Signature** (text, optional): Enter the signature of the client.
* **Client Contact Info** (text, optional): Enter the contact information for the client.
* **Acknowledge** (text, optional): Enter an acknowledgement message.
* **Client Acknowledgement** (select_one, optional): Select whether you have read and agreed to the entertainment standards.
* **Cast Credit** (text, optional): Enter any credits for the cast members.
