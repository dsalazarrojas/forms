# Maintenance Cost Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Cost Report Form is used to track and report maintenance costs associated with specific forms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form title and ID fields with the relevant information.
*   Select the "Cost Submitter" field to indicate whether the cost was submitted by the original submitter or a different person.
*   Choose the "Form Type" field to select the type of form being reported.
*   Enter the total cost in the "Total Cost" field.
*   If desired, select the "Submitted By" field to indicate who submitted the form.
*   Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
*   If the form has a unique ID, enter it in the "Form ID" field.
*   Select the date range for the report in the "Date Range" field.
*   Select the "Cost Submitter 2" field to indicate whether the cost was submitted by a different person.
*   If desired, enter the second date range in the "Date Range 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Maintenance Form ID** (`maintenance_form_id`, text, required): Enter the unique identifier for the form being reported.
*   **Cost Submitter** (`cost_submitter`, select_one, optional): Check this box if the cost was submitted by the original submitter.
*   **Form Type** (`form_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of form being reported, such as "Maintenance Form" or "Report Form".
*   **Date Range** (`date_range`, date, optional): Enter the date range for the report.
*   **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, required): Enter the total cost for the form.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Check this box if the form was submitted by a different person.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for the report.
*   **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the form.
*   **Date Range 2** (`date_range_2`, date, optional): Enter the second date range for the report.
*   **Cost Submitter 2** (`cost_submitter_2`, select_one, optional): Check this box if the cost was submitted by a different person.
