# Material Traceability Verification Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track the origin, quantity, and condition of materials received from suppliers, ensuring that materials are properly accounted for in inventory management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete the form for each material shipment, providing all required information.
2. Enter the **Form Number**, which should be unique for each shipment.
3. Provide the **Customer Name**, the individual or organization that received the material.
4. Enter the **Date Received**, when the material was received.
5. Enter the **Supplier Name**, the individual or organization that provided the material.
6. Describe the **Material Description**, including any relevant details about the material.
7. Enter the **Batch Number**, if applicable.
8. Record the **Quantity Received**, the amount of material received.
9. Enter the **Supplier Contact**, the person or organization to contact for more information.
10. Record the **Received By**, the person who received the material.
11. Select the **Verified By**, the person who verified the material (e.g., John Doe, Jane Doe, or Admin).
12. Enter your **Signature**, to confirm that you received and verified the material.
13. Enter the **Material Traceability ID**, a unique identifier for the material (e.g., '123', '456', or '789').
14. Record any **Notes**, regarding the material or its condition.
15. Enter your **Email**, for contact purposes.
16. Provide **Additional Comments**, any further details or concerns about the material.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Number** (`form_number`, Number, Required): A unique identifier for each shipment.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Required): The name of the individual or organization receiving the material.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, Date, Required): The date the material was received.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, Text, Required): The name of the individual or organization providing the material.
* **Material Description** (`material_description`, Text, Optional): A description of the material, including any relevant details.
* **Batch Number** (`batch_number`, Number, Optional): The batch or lot number of the material.
* **Quantity Received** (`quantity_received`, Number, Required): The amount of material received.
* **Supplier Contact** (`supplier_contact`, Text, Optional): The person or organization to contact for more information.
* **Received By** (`received_by`, Text, Required): The person who received the material.
* **Verified By** (`verified_by`, Select, Optional): The person who verified the material (e.g., John Doe, Jane Doe, or Admin).
* **Signature** (`signature`, Time, Required): Your signature confirming that you received and verified the material.
* **Material Traceability ID** (`material_traceability_id`, Select, Required): A unique identifier for the material (e.g., '123', '456', or '789').
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Any additional comments or details about the material or its condition.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Any further details or concerns about the material.
