# custom_solar_system_inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The custom solar system inquiry form is used to collect information from prospects about their solar system requirements. This form is designed to be completed by sales or business development teams to gather insights into a prospect's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the prospect's details, such as their name and contact information on **Page 1: Enter Prospect Details**.
2. Provide details about the property or location of the solar system, on **Page 2: Property Details**.
3. Select up to 3 preferred installers for the solar system on **Page 3: Preferred Installers**.
4. Report the energy usage in units of kilowatt-hours (kWh) on **Page 4: Energy Usage** and **Page 5: Energy Usage 2**.
5. Choose a single option for their energy preferences on **Page 6: Energy Preferences**.
6. Enter the date for the solar system installation on **Page 7: Date**.
7. Enter the time for the solar system installation on **Page 8: Time**.
8. Add any additional notes about the prospect's requirements or concerns on **Page 9: Note**.
9. Enter the prospect's email address on **Page 10: Email**.
10. Enter the prospect's phone number on **Page 11: Phone**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Prospect Details**: Enter the prospect's name, contact information, and any other relevant details to identify them.
* **Property Details**: Enter the address or location of the property where the solar system will be installed.
* **Preferred Installers**: Select up to 3 companies from the list that you would prefer to install the solar system.
* **Energy Usage** and **Energy Usage 2**: Report the energy usage in units of kilowatt-hours (kWh).
* **Energy Preferences**: Choose a single option that best describes their energy preferences.
* **Date**: Enter the date for the solar system installation.
* **Time**: Enter the time for the solar system installation.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the prospect's requirements or concerns.
* **Email**: Enter the prospect's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the prospect's phone number.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access and submit this form.
* Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
* This form is designed to be completed by sales or business development teams.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your sales or business development materials for guidance.
