# corporate_partner_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate_partner_information_form is designed to collect essential information about a corporate partner's details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the company name in the field labeled "company_name".
2. Enter the year the company was established in the field labeled "year_established".
3. Enter the name of the partner who will be the point of contact.
4. Enter the job title of the partner.
5. Fill in the address of the company in the fields labeled "address_1", "address_2", "city", "state", and "postal_code".
6. Enter the phone number of the partner.
7. Enter the email address of the partner.
8. Enter the website of the partner.
9. Finally, select the type of partner from the dropdown options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **company_name**: Enter the name of the corporate partner's company.
* **year_established**: Enter the year the company was established.
* **partner_name**: Enter the name of the partner who will be the point of contact.
* **job_title**: Enter the job title of the partner.
* **address_1**: Enter the street address of the company.
* **address_2**: Enter the apartment or suite number of the company (if applicable).
* **city**: Enter the city of the company's location.
* **state**: Enter the state of the company's location.
* **postal_code**: Enter the postal code of the company's location.
* **phone**: Enter the phone number of the partner.
* **email**: Enter the email address of the partner.
* **website**: Enter the website of the partner.
* **partner_type**: Select the type of partner from the dropdown options.
