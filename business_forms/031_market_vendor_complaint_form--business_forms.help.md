# Market Vendor Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to report issues or complaints about their market vendor experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply fill in your contact information (Customer Name and Customer License) if you wish to be contacted for follow-up.
2. Describe your issue with the vendor in the "Complaint" field.
3. Select the reason for your complaint from the "category" dropdown list (Market Vendor, Quality of Service, Price, or Communication).
4. Click the "submit" button with a date and time stamp, indicating when you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or as you would like to be identified. This is an optional field.
* **vendor name (2)**: Enter the name or code of the vendor you are filing a complaint about. This is an optional field.
* **vendor license (3)**: Enter the license number or permit number of the vendor if you have it. This is an optional field.
* **Complaint (4)**: Describe the issue you are experiencing with the vendor in as much detail as possible. Be as clear and concise as you can be.
* **Category (5)**: Select the reason for your complaint from the list. The options are:
	+ Market Vendor
	+ Quality of Service
	+ Price
	+ Communication
* **Message (6)**: Enter any additional information or comments about your complaint. This is an optional field.
* **Submit (7)**: Click the button to submit the form with the current date and time.
* **Follow-up (8)**: Enter any additional information or comments about your complaint if you wish to be contacted by the vendor or internal staff. This is an optional field.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when describing your issue.
* If you are reporting multiple issues, you can submit multiple forms.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.
