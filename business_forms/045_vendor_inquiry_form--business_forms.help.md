# Vendor Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Inquiry Form is used to gather information about a vendor. This form helps you to quickly and easily collect the necessary details about the vendor.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the vendor you wish to inquire about.
2. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
3. Ensure that you are not duplicating an inquiry for the same vendor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendors Name**: Enter the name of the vendor. If you are unsure about the vendor's name, you can also use this field to provide any additional information that might help identify the vendor.
* **Address**: Type in the street address of the vendor's location.
* **Contact Person**: Choose the person in charge of the vendor's account or operations. This will help us to direct your inquiry to the right contact.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the vendor's contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the vendor's contact person.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the vendor's company.
* **Company Description**: Provide a brief description of the vendor's business or services.
* **Website URL**: Enter the URL of the vendor's website.
* **Industry**: Enter the industry or category of the vendor's business.
* **Tips**: Be as specific and accurate as possible when filling out this form. This will help us to provide the best possible service and to avoid duplicate inquires.
