# Business Insurance Client Data Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from businesses for the purpose of determining their insurance needs and providing quotes for commercial insurance policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your business' legal name in the "Business Legal Name" field.
2. Provide your business' primary physical address in the "Primary Physical Address" field.
3. Select your industry classification from the "Industry Classification" dropdown menu.
4. Choose your annual gross revenue from the "Annual Gross Revenue" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the total number of employees in the "Total Number of Employees" field.
6. Select your building ownership status from the "Building Ownership Status" dropdown menu.
7. Choose all applicable security measures in place at your primary location from the "Security Measures in Place" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the size of your commercial fleet, if applicable.
9. Enter the valuation of your specialized equipment, if applicable.
10. Enter the name and contact information of your primary contact person for claims.
11. If you have existing policies, provide a brief summary of your current policies and their expiration dates.
12. Enter the date of the data collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Legal Name** (Required): Enter your business' legal name as it appears on your business registration.
* **Primary Physical Address** (Required): Enter your business' primary physical address where the insurance policy would cover.
* **Industry Classification**: Select the category that best fits your business operations from the provided options.
* **Annual Gross Revenue**: Select your business' total revenue from the provided options.
* **Total Number of Employees**: Enter the total number of employees, including full-time and part-time staff.
* **Building Ownership Status**: Select the status of your building ownership from the provided options.
* **Security Measures in Place**: Select all applicable security measures in place at your primary location.
* **Commercial Fleet Size**: Enter the number of commercial vehicles used for business purposes, if applicable.
* **Specialized Equipment Valuation**: Enter the total replacement value of specialized machinery, if applicable.
* **Primary Contact for Claims**: Enter the name and contact information of your primary contact person for claims.
* **Existing Policy Details**: If you have existing policies, provide a brief summary of your current policies and their expiration dates.
* **Data Collection Date**: Enter the date of data collection.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate insurance quotes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
* This form should only be completed by authorized business representatives.
