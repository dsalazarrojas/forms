## Investment Research Log Form - Help Guide
### Purpose
This form is used to document and track investment research projects. It captures essential information about each project, including its company, project, research status, and contact details. This form helps streamline the research process and enables efficient tracking of projects' progress and status.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields marked as mandatory, which are:
	* Company Name
	* Project Name
	* Research Start Date
	* Research End Date
	* Research Status (if applicable)
	* Research Type
	* Investment Amount
	* Investment Return
	* Project Location
	* Project Manager
	* Research Team
	* Research Lead
	* Research Members
	* Research Status Note
	* Company Status (if applicable)
	* Project Status (if applicable)
	* Project Notes
2. For fields with options (select_one, select_multiple), choose the relevant values from the dropdown menu.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company associated with the investment research project.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Research Start Date** (`research_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the research project started.
* **Research End Date** (`research_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the research project ended.
* **Research Status** (`research_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the current research status from the dropdown menu:
	+ Active: The project is currently active and being researched.
	+ Inactive: The project has been inactive for some time.
	+ Cancelled: The project has been cancelled.
* **Research Type** (`research_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the type of research from the dropdown menu:
	+ Equity
	+ Debt
	+ Other
	+ Real Estate
* **Research Notes** (`research_notes`, `note`, required): Provide a brief summary or any additional notes about the research project.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the project or the company's point of contact.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the project or the company's point of contact.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, required): Enter the website of the company associated with the investment research project.
* **Project Website** (`project_website`, `text`, required): Enter the website related to the project.
* **Investment Amount** (`investment_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount invested or expected to be invested in the project.
* **Investment Return** (`investment_return`, `number`, required): Enter the expected investment return or actual return on investment.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location associated with the investment research project.
* **Research Category** (`research_category`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the category of research from the dropdown menu:
	+ Equity
	+ Debt
	+ Other
	+ Real Estate
* **Research Priority** (`research_priority`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the priority level of the research from the dropdown menu:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the project manager responsible for the investment research project.
* **Research Team** (`research_team`, `text`, required): Enter a list of names or a description of the research team members.
* **Research Lead** (`research_lead`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the lead researcher or point of contact.
* **Research Members** (`research_members`, `text`, required): Enter a list of names or a description of the research team members.
* **Research Status Note** (`research_status_note`, `note`, required): Provide any additional notes about the research project's status.
* **Company Status** (`company_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of the company from the dropdown menu:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ Cancelled
* **Company Notes** (`company_notes`, `note`, required): Provide any additional notes about the company associated with the investment research project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of the project from the dropdown menu:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ Cancelled
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, `note`, required): Provide any additional notes about the project.

### Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about the field labels or options, refer to the form's instructions or consult with the project team.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness to ensure that the form is filled out correctly.
