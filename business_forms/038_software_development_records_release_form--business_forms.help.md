# software_development_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the release of software development records.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Determine Release Request

* Select "Yes" or "No" from the "Release Request" field to indicate whether you are requesting to release records or not.

### Step 2: Provide Project Information

* Enter the name of the project related to your request in the "Project Name" field.

### Step 3: Add Developer Information

* Enter any additional information about yourself as the developer in the "Developer Info" field.

### Step 4: Select Record Details

* Select the type of record you are requesting access to in the "Record Details" field.

### Step 5: Specify Record Type

* Select the type of record being released in the "Record Type" field.

### Step 6: Set Access and Release Dates

* Enter the date when access was granted or will be granted in the "Access Date" field.
* Enter the date when access will be released in the "Release Date" field.

### Step 7: Select Data Sharing Method

* Choose the method of data sharing in the "Data Sharing Method" field.

### Step 8: Confirm Consent and Record Retention

* Select "Yes" or "No" from the "Consent Given" field to indicate consent for access.
* Select "Yes" or "No" from the "Record Retention" field to indicate the record retention status.

### Step 9: Provide Contact Information

* Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Contact Email" field.
* Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Contact Phone" field.

### Step 10: Add Additional Notes and Signatures

* Enter any additional comments or notes related to your request in the "Additional Comments" field.
* Sign the form in the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release Request**: Select "Yes" to request release of records, or "No" otherwise.
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project related to your request.
* **Developer Info**: Enter any additional information about yourself as the developer.
* **Record Details**: Select the type of record you are requesting access to.
* **Record Type**: Select the type of record being released.
* **Access Date**: Enter the date when access was granted or will be granted.
* **Data Sharing Method**: Select the method of data sharing.
* **Consent Given**: Select "Yes" to indicate consent for access, or "No" otherwise.
* **Record Retention**: Select "Yes" to indicate the record retention status, or "No" otherwise.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes related to your request.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Date of Contact**: Enter the date of the contact.
* **Contact Address**: Enter the address of the contact person.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Company**: Enter the company name of the contact person.
* **Contact Job Title**: Enter the job title of the contact person.
* **Record Release Type**: Select the type of record release.
* **Access Granted By**: Select "Yes" to indicate who granted the access, or "No" otherwise.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date when access was granted or will be granted.
* **End User Agreement**: Select "Yes" to indicate the end user agreement status, or "No" otherwise.
* **Record Status**: Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the record status.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes related to your request.
* **Record Destruction Date**: Enter the date when the record will be destroyed.
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm the request.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions or approvals to request record access.
* Review all fields for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.
* This form is for requesting access to records, and should be used responsibly.
