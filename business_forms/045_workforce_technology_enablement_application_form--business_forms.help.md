# Workforce Technology Enablement Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Technology Enablement Application Form is used to gather information from employees about their technology enablement needs. This information will be used by the IT department to identify the tools and technologies that employees need to perform their job functions effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manager Info** (`manager_info`, select_one, required): This field asks if you are a manager or not. Select "Yes" if you are a manager, otherwise select "No".
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): If you are a manager, please enter the name of your manager.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address.
* **Justification** (`justification`, note, optional): Provide a brief explanation of why you need technology enablement.
* **Tool Type** (`tool_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all the tools and technologies you need. This may include software, hardware, or any other technology that is not already available to you.
* **Policy Acknowledgment** (`policy_acknowledgment`, text, required): Confirm that you understand and will comply with the company's policies regarding technology use.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Enter your name as the tool requestor.
* **Tool Requester** (`tool_requester`, text, optional): Enter your name as the tool requestor. This is optional, but please enter your name if you are not a manager.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): This is the submit button. Please review all the information you've entered before submitting the form.
