# Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Off Request Form is used by employees to submit requests for time off from work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
2. Enter your email address as it appears in your personnel record.
3. Enter your phone number as it appears in your personnel record.
4. Select the date you wish to take off from work.
5. Select the date you will return to work after your time off.
6. Choose the type of time off you wish to take (Full Day Off or Half Day Off).
7. Add any additional comments or details about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as it appears in your personnel record.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as it appears in your personnel record.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Select the date you wish to take off from work.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select the date you will return to work after your time off.
* **Time Interval** (`time_interval`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of time off you wish to take (Full Day Off or Half Day Off).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or details about your request.

## Tips
* Be sure to discuss your request with your manager before submitting this form.
* Ensure that your request is for a legitimate reason, such as a medical appointment or vacation day.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
