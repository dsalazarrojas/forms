# Supplier Credit Refill Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Credit Refill Order Form is designed to collect necessary information from suppliers for the purpose of requesting a credit refill.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your business name to identify the supplier.
2. Select the desired credit refill request date.
3. Enter the amount of credit to be refilled.
4. Provide contact information for the supplier, including supplier contact info and customer name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your business to identify the supplier.
* **Credit Refill Request Date** (`credit_refill_request_date`, date, required): Select the date on which you would like the credit to be refilled.
* **Credit Amount** (`credit_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of credit to be refilled.
* **Supplier Contact Info** (`supplier_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the contact information for the supplier, including any necessary details.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer associated with this credit refill request.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer.
