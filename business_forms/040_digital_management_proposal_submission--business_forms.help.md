# Digital Management Proposal Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for submitting digital management proposals to the business organization. It is intended to collect relevant information about the vendor and submitter.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the required fields filled out.
2. Fill in the vendor information:
	* Vendor Name: Enter the name of the vendor submitting the proposal.
	* Contact Email: Enter the contact email of the vendor.
	* Contact Phone (optional): Enter the contact phone of the vendor. (Note: This field is optional, but highly recommended for communication purposes.)
3. Fill in the submitter information:
	* Suggester Name: Enter the name of the person submitting the proposal.
	* Suggester Email: Enter the email of the person submitting the proposal.
	* Suggester Title: Enter the title of the person submitting the proposal.
	* Suggester Department: Enter the department of the person submitting the proposal.
	* Suggester Company: Enter the company of the person submitting the proposal.
	* Suggester Position: Enter the position of the person submitting the proposal.
	* Suggester Address: Enter the address of the person submitting the proposal.
4. Add any additional comments or notes as needed.
5. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name**: Vender Name is the name of the vendor submitting the proposal.
* **Proposal Date**: Proposal Date is the date the proposal was submitted.
* **Contact Email**: Contact Email is the email of the person submitting the proposal.
* **Contact Phone**: Contact Phone is the contact phone of the vendor. (Note: This field is optional, but highly recommended for communication purposes.)
* **Project Description**: Project Description is a field to enter a brief description of the project.
* **Project Status**: Project Status is the current status of the project.
* **Project Status Date**: Project Status Date is the date the project status was updated.
* **Submitter Name**: Suggester Name is the name of the person submitting the proposal.
* **Submitter Email**: Suggester Email is the email of the person submitting the proposal.
* **Submitter Title**: Suggester Title is the title of the person submitting the proposal.
* **Submitter Department**: Suggester Department is the department of the person submitting the proposal.
* **Submitter Company**: Suggester Company is the company of the person submitting the proposal.
* **Submitter Position**: Suggester Position is the position of the person submitting the proposal.
* **Submitter Address**: Suggester Address is the address of the person submitting the proposal.
* **Submitter Comments**: Suggester Comments is an optional field to add any comments or notes.
* **Submitter Signature**: Suggester Signature is a field to sign and date the proposal.
* **Vendor Comments**: Vendor Comments is an optional field for the vendor to add any comments or notes.
* **Vendor Signature**: Vendor Signature is a field to sign and date the proposal.
* **Submitter Notes**: Suggester Notes is an optional field to add any notes or comments.
* **Submitter Phone**: Suggester Phone is optional and recommended for communication purposes.
* **Submitter Proposal Type**: Suggester Proposal Type is the type of proposal being submitted (Digital or Team).
