# Customer Retention Metrics Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Customer Retention Metrics Monitoring Form" is designed to help businesses track and analyze customer retention metrics, including customer satisfaction, acquisition, and retention rates. This form collects data on various aspects of customer behavior and sentiment, enabling businesses to make informed decisions to improve customer retention.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete each field according to the instructions below.
2.  Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
3.  Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and consistency.
