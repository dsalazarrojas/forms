# Inside Sales Daily Calls Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inside Sales Daily Calls Report Form is used to record inside sales team activities, including customer calls, tasks completed, and tasks reach outs. This form helps the inside sales team track their daily progress, identify areas of improvement, and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter the date** in the "Date" field. This is a required field and will help us track daily activity over time.
2. **Select the sales person responsible** for the activity in the "Sales Person" field. This ensures that the correct sales representative's progress is recorded.
3. **Enter the number of customer calls** made today in the "Customer Calls" field. This includes all calls made to customers, including both successful and unsuccessful calls.
4. **Enter the number of tasks completed** today in the "Tasks Completed" field. This includes all tasks completed during the current day.
5. **Enter the number of tasks reach outs** made today in the "Tasks Reach Outs" field. This includes all tasks scheduled or attempted during the current day.
6. **Enter any sales calls made in the last 30 days**, if applicable, in the "Sales Calls Last 30 Days" field. This helps track sales activity over the past month.
7. **Enter any tasks completed in the last 30 days**, if applicable, in the "Tasks Completed Last 30 Days" field. This helps track task completion rates over the past month.
8. **Enter any tasks reach outs made in the last 30 days**, if applicable, in the "Tasks Reach Outs Last 30 Days" field. This helps identify potential trends in task scheduling and completion.
9. **Enter the customer's outlook on the current state of sales**, if known, in the "Customer Calls Outlook" field.
10. **Enter the customer's outlook on sales over the last 30 days**, if known, in the "Customer Calls Outlook Last 30 Days" field.
11. **Enter the tasks' outlook**, if known, in the "Tasks Outlook" field.
12. **Enter the tasks' outlook over the last 30 days**, if known, in the "Tasks Outlook Last 30 Days" field.
13. **Select the next steps for the current sales activity** in the "Next Steps" field. This could be Customer, Internal, Both, or None, depending on the activity's status.
14. **Select the next steps for sales activity in the last 30 days** in the "Next Steps Last 30 Days" field, if applicable.
15. **Enter the current sales status**, if known, in the "Sales Status" field. This could be New, In Progress, Lost, or Won.
16. **Enter the sales status over the last 30 days**, if known, in the "Sales Status Last 30 Days" field, if applicable.
17. **Submit the form** after filling out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Calls (1)**: Enter the number of customer calls made today.
* **Customer Calls Last 30 Days (2)**: Enter the number of customer calls made in the last 30 days.
* **Tasks Completed (3)**: Enter the number of tasks completed today.
* **Tasks Reach Outs (4)**: Enter the number of tasks scheduled or attempted today.
* **Tasks Reach Outs Last 30 Days (5)**: Enter the number of tasks scheduled or attempted in the last 30 days.
* **Sales Calls (6)**: Enter the number of sales calls made today. (Optional)
* **Sales Calls Last 30 Days (7)**: Enter the number of sales calls made in the last 30 days. (Optional)
* **Customer Calls Outlook (8)**: Enter the customer's current outlook on sales, if known.
* **Customer Calls Outlook Last 30 Days (9)**: Enter the customer's outlook on sales over the last 30 days, if known.
* **Tasks Outlook (10)**: Enter the tasks' current outlook, if known.
* **Tasks Outlook Last 30 Days (11)**: Enter the tasks' outlook over the last 30 days, if known.
* **Next Steps (12)**: Select the next steps for the current sales activity. This could be Customer, Internal, Both, or None, depending on the activity's status.
* **Next Steps Last 30 Days (13)**: Select the next steps for sales activity in the last 30 days.
* **Sales Status (14)**: Enter the current sales status, if known. This could be New, In Progress, Lost, or Won.
* **Sales Status Last 30 Days (15)**: Enter the sales status over the last 30 days, if known.
* **Sales Person (16)**: Select the sales person responsible for the activity.
* **Sales Person Last 30 Days (17)**: Select the sales person responsible for the activity in the last 30 days.
* **Date (18)**: Enter the date of the activity.
* **Time (19)**: Enter the time of the activity.
* **Remarks (20)**: Enter any additional remarks or comments about the activity.
* **Remarks Last 30 Days (21)**: Enter any additional remarks or comments about the activity in the last 30 days.
