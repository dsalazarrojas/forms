# Tattoo Inquiry Form DTP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals looking to inquire about a tattoo, including their contact information and details about their desired tattoo design or theme.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the purpose of your inquiry from the options provided.
2. Fill in your name.
3. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
4. Provide your phone number.
5. Describe your desired tattoo design or theme.
6. Enter your date of birth.
7. Choose whether you would like to schedule a consultation or an appointment.
8. Select a time for the appointment (if scheduling an appointment).
9. Confirm the date for the appointment.
10. Confirm your availability for the scheduled appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is the purpose of your inquiry? (inquiry_purpose)**: Select the reason you are inquiring about a tattoo, which can be a Consultation, Tattoo Design, or General Inquiry.
- **What is your name? (name)**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on your tattoo.
- **Please provide your email for contact (email)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for further inquiries or updates.
- **What is your phone number? (phone)**: Enter your phone number to ensure you can be contacted for further discussion or updates.
- **Describe your desired tattoo design or theme (description)**: Write a brief description of the design or theme you are looking for in your tattoo.
- **What is your date of birth? (date_of_birth)**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Would you like to schedule a consultation or an appointment? (consultation_or_appointment)**: Choose between Consultation and Appointment to schedule a meeting with a tattoo artist.
- **What time would you like to schedule the consultation or appointment? (appointment_time)**: Select a time for the scheduled appointment, if applicable.
- **What date would you like to schedule the consultation or appointment? (appointment_date)**: Enter a date for the scheduled appointment.
- **Are you available for the scheduled appointment? (appointment_availability)**: Confirm your availability for the scheduled appointment.
- **Are there any specific dates of death or death-related information you would like to share? (date_of_death_information)**: Check the relevant boxes if there are any dates or death-related information you would like to disclose.
- **Do you have any further comments or questions? (comments)**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have regarding your tattoo inquiry.
