# Video Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Video Request Form is used by project team members or managers to gather information about video requests. The form helps to ensure that all requests are collected in one place and that the project team has a clear understanding of what videos are needed and why.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the project you are requesting videos for.
2. Describe the video request in the provided text field.
3. Indicate if the request is for a "Yes" or "No" project type.
4. Enter a budget for the video request (optional).
5. Provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you with questions or updates.
6. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
7. Enter your phone number for easy contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the project you are requesting videos for.
- **Description** (`description`, text, required): Describe the video request in this text field. This will help us understand what the video is needed for.
- **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_multiple, required): Check "Yes" if the request is for a project that requires video content. "No" if it's not.
- **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): If you have a budget in mind for the video, enter it here.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information so we can reach out to you with questions or updates.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.

**Tips**
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (Project Title, Description, Project Type, and Email).
- Be as detailed as possible in the description to ensure that we understand the request correctly.
- If you have any questions or need updates, we will contact you through the email address you provided.
- We will review your request before processing it and notify you if it's a duplicate.
