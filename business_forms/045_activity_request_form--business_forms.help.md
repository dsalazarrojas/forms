# Activity Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Activity Request Form is used to submit and manage activity requests. It helps in streamlining the process of tracking and completing tasks, ensuring that activities are prioritized and completed on time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in. These are marked as "required" in the form.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for the activity description and additional notes fields.
4. Choose the correct priority level and approval required for your activity request.
5. Submit the form when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, text, required)

* This field is for the full name of the person submitting the activity request.
* Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.

### 2. **Requestor Department** (`requestor_department`, text, required)

* This field is for the department of the person submitting the activity request.
* Enter the department name as it is known within the organization.

### 3. **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required)

* This field is for the date when the activity request is submitted.
* Enter the date in the format DD/MM/YYYY.

### 4. **Activity Title** (`activity_title`, text, required)

* This field is for the brief title of the activity being requested.
* Provide a concise and descriptive title for the activity.

### 5. **Activity Description** (`activity_description`, text, required)

* This field is for the detailed description of the activity.
* Provide as much detail as possible about the activity, including any specific requirements or constraints.

### 6. **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, select_one, required)

* This field is for the level of urgency of the activity.
* Choose from the following options:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
	+ Critical

### 7. **Requested Completion Date** (`requested_completion_date`, date, required)

* This field is for the date when the activity needs to be completed.
* Enter the date in the format DD/MM/YYYY.

### 8. **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, required)

* This field is for the name of the employee or team assigned to complete the activity.
* Enter the name of the person or team responsible for completing the activity.

### 9. **Activity Category** (`activity_category`, select_one, required)

* This field is for the type of activity being requested.
* Choose from the following options:
	+ Administrative
	+ Project Task
	+ Meeting
	+ Research
	+ Client Service
	+ Maintenance
	+ Event Planning
	+ Other

### 10. **Required Resources** (`required_resources`, text, optional)

* This field is for any additional resources needed to complete the activity.
* Provide any specific resources, tools, or equipment required for the activity.

### 11. **Estimated Duration** (`estimated_duration`, number, optional)

* This field is for the estimated time needed to complete the activity.
* Enter the estimated duration in hours.

### 12. **Approval Required** (`approval_required`, select_one, required)

* This field is for whether special approval is needed for the activity.
* Choose from the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 13. **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional)

* This field is for any additional information about the activity request.
* Provide any extra details or comments about the activity.

## Tips

* Double-check that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Be as detailed as possible in the activity description and additional notes fields.
* Ensure that the requested completion date is realistic and achievable.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact the activity request team for assistance.
