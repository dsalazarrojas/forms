# Trade Amendment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Amendment Form is used to update existing trade agreements. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the requested information.
2. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
3. If a field is not required, you can skip it.
4. If you have any questions or need help, contact your administrator or support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trade Details**: This field is for providing a brief description of the trade agreement being amended. This could include any relevant context or background information.
* **What are the changes**: In this field, describe the specific changes you wish to make to the trade agreement. This could include new terms, updated deadlines, or any other changes.
* **Trade Parties**: Select all the parties involved in the trade agreement. 
* **Trade Amendment Date**: Enter the date of the trade amendment.
* **Trade Agreement ID**: This field is for entering the ID of the trade agreement being amended.
* **Reason for Trade Amendment**: Explain the reason for the trade amendment. This could include a brief justification or explanation for the changes being made.
## Tips
* Make sure to review all fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check that all required fields are filled out.
* If you need help or clarification on any field, contact your administrator or support team.
