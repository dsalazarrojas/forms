# Property Management Software Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Management Software Contact Form is designed to help users get in touch with the support team for assistance, feedback, or to submit inquiries about the software.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Click on the "First Page" section and enter your name, email, phone number, and a brief message with your inquiry, feedback, or issue.
2. Click on the "Additional Details" section and provide more information about your inquiry, feedback, or issue.
3. Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, SMS, or Other) to ensure the support team contacts you promptly.
4. In the "Lead Capturer Name" field, enter your name as it appears on your business card or as you would like to be addressed.
5. Enter your email address in the "Lead Capturer Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Lead Capturer Phone" field.
7. In the "Lead Capturer Message" field, provide a detailed description of your issue or feedback.
8. Select the topic(s) you are interested in from the "Lead Capturer Interests" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the location related to your issue or feedback.
10. In the "Lead Capturer Notes" field, add any additional notes or information about your issue or feedback.
11. In the "Lead Capturer Features" field, mention any features you would like to see added or modified in the software.
12. Select your preferred contact method from the "Lead Capturer Preferred Contact Method" dropdown menu.
13. Enter the URL of the property management software you are using in the "Property Management Software URL" field.
14. In the "Assigned Tool" field, enter the name of the tool you are using for property management.
15. In the "Lead Capturer Comments" field, add any additional comments about your issue or feedback.
16. In the "Lead Capturer Agreement" field, select "I understand and agree to the terms and conditions".
17. In the "Lead Capturer Terms and Conditions" field, enter "I understand and agree to the terms and conditions".
18. Select "I understand and agree" from the "Lead Capturer Checkbox Terms and Conditions" dropdown menu.
19. In the "Lead Capturer Checkbox Email" field, select "Yes" if you have a valid email address.
20. In the "Lead Capturer Checkbox Phone" field, select "Yes" if you have a valid phone number.
21. Click on the "Submit" button to send your inquiry, feedback, or issue to the support team.
