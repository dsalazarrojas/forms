# Utility Cost Reporting Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for capturing consent and other details related to utility cost reporting for tenants. It's an optional process, used for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully, and answer each question based on your utility cost collection and reporting preferences.
2. Select your utility type (e.g., electricity, gas, or water).
3. Choose your collection period and preferred reporting frequency.
4. If needed, specify your collection date and time.
5. Add any comments or notes in the "Reporting note" field.
6. Provide consent to report your utility costs in the "Utility cost reporting consent" field.
7. Provide your contact number and email address for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Consent**: This field is for your consent to report your utility costs.
	+ This field is optional, and it's for you to agree or disagree to the reporting process.
* **Utility type**: Please select the type of utility you would like to report (e.g., electricity, gas, water).
	+ This field helps us know which utility type you would like to report.
* **Collection period**: Select a timeframe for collecting and reporting your utility costs.
	+ Options include daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and yearly.
* **Reporting frequency**: Choose how frequently you would like to report your utility costs.
	+ Options include one-time reporting, every 3 months, quarterly, and bi-monthly.
* **Collection date**: If needed, please input the specific date for utility cost collection in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
	+ This field is optional and only necessary if you select a specific collection period.
* **Collection time**: If necessary, please record the time for utility cost collection in the format HH:mm.
	+ This field is also optional and only required if you select a specific collection time.
* **Reporting note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the reporting process.
	+ This field is optional and for your reference.
* **Utility cost reporting consent**: Please provide your consent to report your utility costs.
	+ This field is optional and for your agreement to the reporting process.
* **Contact number**: Provide your contact number for follow-up purposes.
	+ This field is optional and for our records.
* **Email address**: Provide your email address for communication and follow-up purposes.
	+ This field is optional and for our records.
