# ColorStreet Nail Polish Strips - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to order nail polish strips from ColorStreet. It is organized into sections to make the ordering process easier to follow.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
5. Select the type of strips you are ordering from the "What type of strips are you ordering?" field.
6. List the names of the sets you want to purchase in the "Names of sets to purchase" field.
7. Enter the total number of sets you are purchasing in the "Total Number of Sets" field.
8. Select your preferred payment method from the "Preferred Payment" field.
9. Choose your shipping speed from the "Shipping Choice" field.
10. Add any additional comments or notes about your order in the "Additional Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (order_name)** (`order_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address (order_email)** (`order_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive order updates and notifications.
* **Shipping Address (order_address)** (`order_address`, text, required): Enter your shipping address, including street, city, and state.
* **Phone Number (order_phone)** (`order_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which can be useful for communication if needed.
* **What type of strips are you ordering? (strip_type)** (`strip_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of strips you are ordering (e.g. Solids, Glitters, etc.).
* **Names of sets to purchase (sets_to_purchase)** (`sets_to_purchase`, text, required): List the names of the sets you want to purchase.
* **Total Number of Sets (sets_total_count)** (`sets_total_count`, number, required): Enter the total number of sets you are purchasing.
* **Preferred Payment (billing_method)** (`billing_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method (e.g. PayPal, Venmo, etc.).
* **Shipping Choice (delivery_speed)** (`delivery_speed`, select_one, required): Select your preferred shipping speed (e.g. Standard, Priority, etc.).
* **Additional Notes (order_comments)** (`order_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
