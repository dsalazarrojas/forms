# Soda Spill Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soda Spill Incident Report form is used to document and report incidents involving spills, which can include fires, spills, or other types of accidents that occur in a business or industrial setting. This form helps ensure that incidents are properly documented, investigated, and reported to relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have permission to complete the form on behalf of your organization or department.
2. Choose the relevant incident type (Fire, Spill, or Other) from the Incident Type field.
3. Fill in the Spill Amount field with a brief description of the spill amount.
4. Enter the location where the incident occurred in the Spill Location field.
5. Provide a detailed description of what happened in the Spill Description field.
6. Describe the effects of the incident in the Spill Effects field.
7. List the consequences of the incident in the Spill Consequences field.
8. Outline any preventative actions taken before or after the incident in the Preventative Action field.
9. Describe the response to the incident in the Response field.
10. Outline the clean-up action taken after the incident in the Clean-up Action field.
11. List any safety measures taken during or after the incident in the Safety Measures field.
12. Describe the investigation conducted into the incident in the Investigation field.
13. Document any follow-up actions taken after the incident in the Follow-up field.
14. Add any additional notes or comments about the incident in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date** (1, date): The date the incident occurred.
* **Location** (2, text): The location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (3, select_one): The type of incident (Fire, Spill, or Other).
* **Spill Amount** (4, number): A brief description of the spill amount.
* **Spill Location** (5, text): The location where the spill occurred.
* **Spill Description** (6, text): A detailed description of what happened.
* **Spill Time** (7, time): The time the incident occurred.
* **Spill Causes** (8, text): The reasons or causes of the incident.
* **Spill Effects** (9, text): The effects or impact of the incident.
* **Spill Consequences** (10, text): The consequences or outcomes of the incident.
* **Preventative Action** (11, text): Any actions taken to prevent or mitigate the incident.
* **Response** (12, text): A description of the response to the incident.
* **Clean-up Action** (13, text): A description of the clean-up action taken after the incident.
* **Safety Measures** (14, text): Safety measures taken during or after the incident.
* **Investigation** (15, text): A description of the investigation conducted into the incident.
* **Follow-up** (16, text): Any follow-up actions taken after the incident.
* **Notes** (17, text): Additional notes or comments about the incident.
* **Submit** (18, select_multiple): Options to submit or cancel the form.
* **Submitted By** (19, select_one): The person submitting the form (Manager, Employee, or Other).
* **Submitted Date** (20, date): The date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (21, time): The time the form was submitted.
* **Assigned To** (22, select_one): The person responsible for investigating or resolving the incident (chatjimmy or Other).
* **Assigned Date** (23, date): The date the incident was assigned to.
* **Submitted By Notes** (24, text): Additional notes or comments about the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted By User** (25, text): Additional information about the person submitting the form.
