# Cardinal Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cardinal Approval Form is a standardized document used to request approval for various types of requests across different departments. It helps ensure that all necessary information is provided for approvers to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the requester's information, including name, email address, department, and phone number (optional).
2. Provide a brief description of the request title and the date of submission.
3. Select the type of approval request (e.g., budget approval, project approval).
4. Describe the request in detail, including the expected outcome.
5. If applicable, specify the financial amount and priority level.
6. Identify the primary and secondary approvers (if needed).
7. Add any relevant documentation or files.
8. Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name**: The name of the person requesting the approval.
* **Email address**: The email address of the person requesting the approval.
* **Department**: The department the request is related to.
* **Phone number**: The phone number of the person requesting the approval (optional).
* **Request title**: A brief description of what is being requested.
* **Request submission date**: The date the request was submitted.
* **Type of approval request**: The type of approval needed (e.g., budget approval, project approval).
* **Request category**: The category the request falls under (e.g., finance, operations).
* **Detailed description of request**: A comprehensive description of what is being requested, including the expected outcome.
* **Financial amount (if applicable)**: The financial amount involved in the request.
* **Priority level**: The level of urgency or importance of the request (e.g., urgent, high, medium, low).
* **Requested completion/decision date**: The expected date for completion or decision.
* **Primary approver name**: The name of the person responsible for reviewing and approving the request.
* **Primary approver email**: The email address of the person responsible for reviewing and approving the request.
* **Secondary approver name (if needed)**: The name of the secondary person involved in the approval process (optional).
* **CC list**: A list of people to be notified of the approval process.
* **Attached documents or files**: A list of files or documents attached to the request.
* **Budget source or funding**: The source or funding for the request (optional).
* **Business justification for request**: A description of why the approval is needed and the expected outcome.
* **Potential impact of approval**: A description of how the request will affect operations, budget, or staff.
* **Authorization confirmation**: Confirmation that the information provided is accurate and complete.
