# Covid 19 Attendance Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Covid 19 Attendance Record Form is designed to collect and store information about patient visits, consultations, and appointments. This form helps healthcare staff gather necessary data for patient records, communication, and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Enter the doctor's name in the **Doctor Name** field.
3. Enter the date of the appointment in the **Appointment Date** field.
4. Enter the time of the appointment in the **Appointment Time** field.
5. Enter the patient's date of birth (DOB) in the **Patient DOB** field.
6. Enter the patient's email address, if applicable, in the **Patient Email** field.
7. Enter the patient's phone number in the **Patient Phone** field.
8. Select the doctor's department from the options in the **Doctor Department** field.
9. Enter the duration of the appointment in the **Appointment Duration** field.
10. Choose the status of the appointment from the options in the **Appointment Status** field.
11. Sign and date the form with your doctor's signature in the **Doctor Signature** field.
12. Sign the form with the patient's signature in the **Patient Signature** field.
13. Enter the doctor's license number in the **Doctor License Number** field.
14. Enter the patient's license number, if applicable, in the **Patient License Number** field.
15. Enter the appointment note in the **Appointment Note** field.
16. Enter the doctor's National ID (NID) number in the **Doctor NID** field.
17. Enter the patient's NID number in the **Patient NID** field.
18. Select the patient's gender from the options in the **Patient Gender** field.
19. Select the doctor's specialty from the options in the **Doctor Specialty** field.
20. Enter the patient's medication information, if applicable, in the **Patient Medication** field.
21. Enter the doctor's medication information, if applicable, in the **Doctor Medication** field.
22. Enter the appointment date and time in the **Appointment Date/Time** field.
23. Enter the follow-up date for the patient in the **Follow-up Date** field.
24. Enter the date of the next appointment, if applicable, in the **Next Appointment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (text, required)**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Doctor Name (text, required)**: Enter the name of the doctor performing the consultation or appointment.
* **Appointment Date (date, required)**: Enter the date of the appointment in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Appointment Time (time, required)**: Enter the time of the appointment in the format of HH:MM:SS.
* **Patient DOB (date, required)**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Patient Email (email, optional)**: Enter the patient's email address, if provided.
* **Patient Phone (text, required)**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Doctor Department (select_one, optional)**: Select the doctor's department from the options provided.
* **Appointment Duration (number, required)**: Enter the duration of the appointment in minutes.
* **Appointment Status (select_one, required)**: Choose the status of the appointment from the options provided.
* **Doctor Signature (note, required)**: Sign and date the form with the doctor's signature.
* **Patient Signature (note, required)**: Sign the form with the patient's signature.
* **Doctor License Number (text, required)**: Enter the doctor's license number.
* **Patient License Number (text, optional)**: Enter the patient's license number, if required.
* **Appointment Note (text, required)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the appointment.
* **Doctor NID (number, required)**: Enter the doctor's National ID (NID) number.
* **Patient NID (number, required)**: Enter the patient's NID number.
* **Patient Gender (select_multiple, required)**: Select the patient's gender from the options provided.
* **Doctor Specialty (select_one, optional)**: Select the doctor's specialty from the options provided.
* **Patient Medication (text, optional)**: Enter the patient's medication information, if applicable.
* **Doctor Medication (text, optional)**: Enter the doctor's medication information, if applicable.
* **Appointment Date/Time (date, required)**: Enter the date and time of the appointment in the format of YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS.
* **Follow-up Date (date, required)**: Enter the date for the follow-up appointment.
* **Next Appointment (date, optional)**: Enter the date of the next appointment, if scheduled.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant medical staff or refer to the medical guidelines and policies.
