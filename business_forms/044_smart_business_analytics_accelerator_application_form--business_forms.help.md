# Smart Business Analytics Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Business Analytics Accelerator Application Form is designed to collect relevant business information from applicants for the accelerator program. The form is divided into several sections, each containing specific fields that require your input. Please follow the instructions below to complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages and fields carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Enter your business details accurately and completely, as incomplete or incorrect information may lead to delays or errors in the evaluation process.
3. For select fields, choose the most relevant option from the available choices.
4. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_1** (`form_1`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your business in a few words.
* **form_2** (`form_2`, number, optional): Enter the number of employees in your company.
* **form_3** (`form_3`, number, optional): Enter the average revenue of your business over the last 6 months.
* **form_4** (`form_4`, select_one, optional): Select the option that best describes your business focus area:
	+ Option 1: Data-driven decision making
	+ Option 2: Customer experience improvement
* **form_5** (`form_5`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Option 1: Data visualization
	+ Option 2: Predictive analytics
* **form_6** (`form_6`, date, optional): Enter the date you started your business.
* **form_7** (`form_7`, time, optional): Enter the time you are most available to discuss your application.
* **form_8** (`form_8`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about your business.
* **form_9** (`form_9`, email, optional): Enter your email address for further contact.
* **form_10** (`form_10`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your business in a few words (optional, but recommended).

Note: If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to contact us. We are here to help you complete the application smoothly.
