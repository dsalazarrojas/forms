# Franchise Energy Transition Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Franchise Energy Transition Application Form is a document used to collect information from franchisees who are seeking to transition to a new energy source. The form is used to gather relevant data on the franchisee's energy needs, application details, and contact information.

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Franchise Information section with details about the franchise you are seeking to transition from.
2.  Enter your location and contact information in the Location and Contact Information section.
3.  Provide your motivation for the energy transition in the Motivation section.
4.  Enter your contact phone number in the Contact Phone Number field.
5.  Select the energy source you are interested in transitioning to in the Energy Source field.
6.  Provide your reason for the motivation in the Motivation Reason field.
7.  Enter information on the sustainability of the franchise in the Franchise Sustainability field.
8.  Specify the energy needs of your franchise in the Franchise Energy field.
9.  Provide the contact person's name and job title in the Contact Person and Contact Job fields.
10. Enter your external contact information in the Contact Ext fields.
11.  Add any additional notes about the franchise in the Franchise Notes field.
12. Select the status of your application in the Application Status field.
13.  Enter the date and time of your application in the Application Date and Application Time fields.
14.  Add any comments from the franchisee in the Franchisee Comments field.
15.  Select your agreement to the terms and conditions in the Franchisee Agreement fields.
