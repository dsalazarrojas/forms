# price_consistency_clarification_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help customers clarify inconsistencies in pricing and contact the relevant customer support team for assistance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* Customer Support
	* Product Service
	* Help Desk
	* Support Team
	* Assistance
	* Help
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
3. Review your information before submitting the form to ensure it's correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name for our records.
* **Email** (`2`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Message** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Describe your concern or issue with the price of the product or service.
* **Customer Service** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all relevant customer service categories that apply to your issue, such as Customer Support, Support, Assistance, etc.
* **Product Name** (`6`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, enter the name of the product or service that's causing the price issue.
* **Price** (`7`, `number`, required: false): Enter the price that's causing concern.
* **Date** (`8`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you noticed the price issue.
* **Time** (`9`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you noticed the price issue.
* **Additional Message** (`10`, `text`, required: false): If there's anything else you'd like to add to your message.
* **Customer Support** (`11`, `select_one`, required: true): Confirm that you're reaching out for customer support.
* **Product Service** (`12`, `select_one`, required: true): Confirm that the issue is with a product or service.
* **Support Service** (`13`, `select_one`, required: true): Confirm that the issue is with a support service.
* **Select One** (`14`, `select_one`, required: false): Not applicable in this form.
* **Services** (`15`, `select_one`, required: false): Not applicable in this form.
* **Help Desk** (`16`, `select_one`, required: true): Confirm that you're reaching out to the help desk.
* **Support Team** (`17`, `select_one`, required: true): Confirm that you're looking for support from the support team.
* **Assistance** (`18`, `select_one`, required: true): Confirm that you need assistance.
* **Help** (`19`, `select_one`, required: false): Not applicable in this form.
* **Service** (`20`, `select_one`, required: false): Not applicable in this form.
* **Help** (`21`, `select_one`, required: false): Not applicable in this form.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure your issue is properly understood.
* If you have any additional information or evidence, you can attach it to your message.
* If your issue is resolved while filling out the form, you can contact us via another channel.
