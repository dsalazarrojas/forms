# farm_business_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a farm business, including its name, address, contact details, and business type. The form aims to gather essential data for registration and verification purposes. Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the farm business name and address on Page 1.
2. Provide the farm phone number, email, and website (if applicable) on Page 2.
3. Select the type of farm business (e.g., dairy, livestock, organic, etc.) on Page 3.
4. Indicate if your farm business has any certifications, licenses, or insurance coverage on Page 4.
5. Confirm or deny any bankruptcy, liability, or fines associated with your farm business on Page 5.
6. Add any additional information or notes about your farm business on Page 6 (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Enter Farm Name** (`enter_farm_name`, text, required): Please enter the official name of your farm business.
- **Enter Farm Address** (`enter_farm_address`, text, required): Provide the physical address of your farm business.
- **Enter Farm City** (`enter_farm_city`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the city where your farm is located.
- **Enter Farm State** (`enter_farm_state`, select_one, required): Select the state where your farm business operates from the list of available options.
- **Enter Farm Zip** (`enter_farm_zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code of your farm's location.
- **Enter Farm Phone** (`enter_farm_phone`, text, required): Provide your farm's phone number for contact purposes.
- **Enter Farm Email** (`enter_farm_email`, email, required): Enter your farm's official email address.
- **Enter Farm Website** (`enter_farm_website`, text, optional): If your farm business has a website, provide the URL.
- **Enter Farm Fax** (`enter_farm_fax`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the fax number of your farm business.
- **Additional Notes** (`farm_business_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your farm business.
- **Farm Business Type** (`what_kind_of_farm`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of farm business (e.g., dairy, livestock, organic, etc.).
- **Farm Licenses** (`enter_farm_licenses`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if your farm business has any licenses or certifications.
- **Farm Certifications** (`enter_farm_certifications`, select_multiple, optional): Select any certifications related to your farm business (e.g., USDA, NOP, etc.).
- **Farm Insurance** (`enter_farm_insurance`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm or deny if your farm business has insurance coverage.
- **Farm Bankruptcy** (`enter_farm_bankruptcy`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm or deny if your farm business has any bankruptcy records.
- **Farm Liability** (`enter_farm_liability`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm or deny if your farm business has any liability issues.
- **Farm Fines** (`enter_farm_fines`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm or deny if your farm business has any fine-related issues.
- **Enter Farm Other** (`enter_farm_other`, text, optional): Add any other relevant information about your farm business.
- **Additional Notes 2** (`enter_farm_notes2`, note, optional): Add any further comments or notes about your farm business.
