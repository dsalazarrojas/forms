# Driver PrePost Trip Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Driver PrePost Trip Inspection Form is designed to collect information about the condition of a vehicle before and after a trip, allowing you to document any pre-existing issues or concerns that may affect the vehicle's performance or safety during the trip.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and answer accurately to ensure the information gathered is accurate and reliable.
2. For each field, provide clear and detailed descriptions of the vehicle's condition.
3. Be as thorough as possible and include any relevant information that may be useful for the inspection.
4. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, text, required: false): Describe the overall condition of the vehicle, including any existing damage, wear, or issues.
- **Engine Oil Condition** (`engine_oil_condition`, text, required: false): Describe the condition of the engine oil, including its level, color, and consistency.
- **Tire Condition** (`tire_condition`, text, required: false): Describe the condition of the tires, including their wear, tread depth, and any visible signs of damage.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when completing this form to ensure that any issues or concerns with the vehicle are properly documented.
* If you need to make any corrections or updates to the form, please refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact [contact email: form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
