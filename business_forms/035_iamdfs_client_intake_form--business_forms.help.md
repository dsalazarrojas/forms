# iamdfs-client-intake-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their business needs and preferences. It is intended to gather essential details that will help in the process of providing services or products tailored to their specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client information in the "Client Info" section.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
3. Share your credit history, if applicable, in the "Credit History" section.
4. Enter your account details in the "Account Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Info** (optional): This section is for you to provide details about yourself or your organization.
- **Contact Info** (optional): Please enter your name, phone number, and email address so we can get back to you.
- **Credit History** (optional): Share any relevant credit history or financial information with us, if applicable.
- **Account Info** (optional): Enter your account details, such as your account number or login credentials.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields for a complete and accurate submission.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
- Please be aware that the information provided will be used to improve our services or products tailored to your specific needs.
