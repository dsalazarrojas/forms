# Partner Program Qualification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Partner Program Qualification Form is a comprehensive document used to collect and evaluate necessary information from potential partners for our business partner program. This form helps us assess your suitability and eligibility for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out each section carefully, making sure to provide accurate and relevant information.
2. Start with the "Partner Information" section, where you'll provide basic details about your company or organization.
3. In the "Contact Person" section, enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for managing your partnership.
4. The "Category" field allows you to select the type of service or product you offer (Software, Hardware, Consulting, etc.).
5. In the "Location" field, enter your company's location or address.
6. In the "Description" field, provide a brief description of your services or products offered by your company.
7. For the "Status" field, select the relevant status of your partnership (Active or Inactive).
8. In the "Notes" field, enter any additional information that might be helpful for us to know about your partnership.
9. Finally, provide your company's phone number in the "Partner Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Information** (partner_info, text, required: false): This section is where you'll provide basic details about your company or organization.
* **Contact Person** (contact_info, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for managing your partnership.
* **Email** (contact_email, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (contact_phone, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Category** (partner_category, select_one, required: false): Select the type of service or product you offer (Software, Hardware, Consulting, etc.).
* **Location** (partner_location, text, required: false): Enter your company's location or address.
* **Description** (partner_description, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your services or products offered by your company.
* **Status** (partner_status, select_multiple, required: false): Select the relevant status of your partnership (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes** (partner_notes, text, required: false): Enter any additional information that might be helpful for us to know about your partnership.
* **Partner Phone** (partner_phone, text, required: false): Provide your company's phone number.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Use a consistent format for your address and phone number (e.g., "123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345" and "123-456-7890").
- Make sure to save your work before navigating away from the page, in case you need to go back and make changes.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask our support team for clarification.
