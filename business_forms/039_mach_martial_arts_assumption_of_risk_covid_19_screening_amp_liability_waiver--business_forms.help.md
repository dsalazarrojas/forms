# Mach Martial Arts Assumption Of Risk, COVID-19 Screening & Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and medical information from individuals who wish to participate in martial arts activities at Mach Martial Arts, as well as to obtain an assumption of risk from the individual.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
5. If you have any medical conditions, list them in the "Medical Conditions" field.
6. Provide the first and last name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact First Name" and "Emergency Contact Last Name" fields, respectively.
7. Enter the email address of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Email" field.
8. Describe your relationship with your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
9. If you have any medical screening, select the corresponding option in the "Medical Screening" field.
10. Enter the date and time of your screening in the "Screening Date" and "Screening Time" fields, respectively.
11. Enter the location of your screening in the "Screening Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone (Text)**: Please enter your phone number.
* **Date Of Birth (Date)**: Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Condition (Text)**: If you have any medical conditions, list them here.
* **Emergency Contact First Name (Text)**: Please enter the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name (Text)**: Please enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email (Email)**: Please enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (Text)**: Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Medical Screening (Select One)**: Please select whether you have any medical screening.
* **Screening Date (Date)**: Please enter the date of your screening.
* **Screening Time (Time)**: Please enter the time of your screening.
* **Screening Location (Text)**: Please enter the location of your screening.
* **Signature (Note)**: Please sign this form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Risk Assumption (Text)**: Please enter a brief description of your risk assumption.
* **Medical Conditions (Select Multiple)**: Please select any medical conditions you have that may affect your participation in martial arts activities.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Text)**: Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Phone)**: Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Screening Medical Conditions (Select One)**: Please select whether or not you have medical screening.
* **Screening Location (Text)**: Please enter the location of your screening.

## Tips
Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask our staff for assistance.
