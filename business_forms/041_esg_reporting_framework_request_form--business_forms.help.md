# ESG Reporting Framework Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The ESG Reporting Framework Request Form is designed to help you get the most out of your ESG reporting experience. This form collects essential information to provide you with the right guidance and data to support your ESG reporting needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please review the form fields and provide the required information for a successful submission.
2.  Ensure that you understand the purpose of each field and the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   *Client Information (client_info)*: A text field for you to provide your company name, organization name, or any other relevant details.
*   *Reporting Guidance (reporting_guidance)*: Select one or more from the three options:
    • ESG Reporting Guidance for Beginners: For those new to ESG reporting or seeking fundamental guidance.
    • ESG Reporting Guidance for Intermediate: For those already familiar with ESG reporting looking for more in-depth information.
    • ESG Reporting Guidance for Advanced: For experienced users seeking highly specialized guidance.
*   *Preferred Standards (preferred_standards)*: Choose one from the two options:
    • Global Reporting Standards: For global ESG reporting requirements.
    • Local Reporting Standards: For local or regional ESG reporting requirements.
*   *Focus Areas (focus_areas)*: Select one or more from the three options:
    • Climate Change: Focus on ESG reporting related to environmental impact.
    • Social & Labor Practices: Focus on ESG reporting related to social and labor practices.
    • Human Rights: Focus on ESG reporting related to human rights.
*   *ESG Data Request (esg_data_request)*: Choose one from the three options:
    • ESG Data for Beginners: For fundamental ESG data needs.
    • ESG Data for Intermediate: For more advanced ESG data requirements.
    • ESG Data for Advanced: For highly specialized ESG data needs.
*   *Additional Comments (additional_comments)*: A text field for you to provide any additional information or comments.
*   *ESG Reporting Team (esg_reporting_team)*: A text field for you to provide information about your ESG reporting team.
*   *Contact Information (contact_info)*: A text field for you to provide your contact information.

## Tips
If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us directly. This form is designed to help you get the right ESG reporting guidance and data for your needs.
