# Import Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for referral management within an organization, helping to track and evaluate referrals for businesses.

## How To Complete This Form
Complete this form to provide information about a business referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`import_specialist_referral_form_business_name`): Enter the name of the business that has been referred.
* **Business Website** (`import_specialist_referral_form_business_website`): Enter the website of the business that has been referred.
* **Referrals** (`import_specialist_referral_form_referrals`): Select whether the business has any referrals.
* **Referral Form** (`import_specialist_referral_form_referral_form`): Select whether the business has any referral forms.
* **Email** (`import_specialist_referral_form_email`): Enter the email of the business or the person responsible for the referral.
* **Phone** (`import_specialist_referral_form_phone`): Enter the phone number of the business or the person responsible for the referral.
* **Address** (`import_specialist_referral_form_address`): Enter the address of the business that has been referred.
* **Country** (`import_specialist_referral_form_country`): Select the country where the business is located.
* **State** (`import_specialist_referral_form_state`): Select the state where the business is located.
* **Zip** (`import_specialist_referral_form_zip`): Enter the zip code of the business that has been referred.
* **Comments** (`import_specialist_referral_form_comments`): Provide any additional comments about the referral.
* **Business Category** (`import_specialist_referral_form_business_category`): Enter the category of the business that has been referred.
* **Referral Date** (`import_specialist_referral_form_referral_date`): Select the date of the referral.
* **Referral Time** (`import_specialist_referral_form_referral_time`): Select the time of the referral.
* **Referral Source** (`import_specialist_referral_form_referral_source`): Enter the source of the referral, such as a website or a person.
* **Referral Status** (`import_specialist_referral_form_referral_status`): Select the status of the referral, such as active or inactive.
* **Referral Date 2** (`import_specialist_referral_form_referral_date_2`): Select the date of the second referral.
* **Referral Time 2** (`import_specialist_referral_form_referral_time_2`): Select the time of the second referral.
* **Referral Source 2** (`import_specialist_referral_form_referral_source_2`): Enter the source of the second referral.
* **Referral Source Verified** (`import_specialist_referral_form_referral_source_verified`): Select whether the referral source has been verified.
* **Importer Website** (`import_specialist_referral_form_importer_website`): Enter the website of the importer who referred the business.
* **Referral Source Verified By** (`import_specialist_referral_form_referral_source_verified_by`): Enter the person responsible for verifying the referral source.
* **Referral Source Verified By Title** (`import_specialist_referral_form_referral_source_verified_by_title`): Enter the title of the person responsible for verifying the referral source.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* Double-check the referral status for consistency.
* Verify the referral source before selecting it as a verified source.
