# global_expansion_consulting_partner_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential partners that are interested in global expansion.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about yourself and your company.
- Make sure to answer all the required questions.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please reach out to our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: true): Enter your business email.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company name.
* **Company website** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company website.
* **Job Title** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Company size** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company size.
* **Company type** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company type.
* **What are you looking for?** (`10`, `select_one`, required: true): Select one of the following options:
	+ International expansion
	+ Business consulting
	+ Market research
* **Current location** (`11`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your current location:
	+ USA
	+ Europe
	+ Asia
* **Target location** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all locations you are interested in expanding to:
	+ Americas
	+ Europe
	+ Africa
* **Are you planning to expand?** (`13`, `select_one`, required: true): Select one of the following options:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Why are you looking to expand?** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all reasons for expansion:
	+ Increase market share
	+ Decrease production costs
	+ Increase profit margin
* **How long have you been a partner?** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one of the following options:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-5 years
	+ More than 5 years
* **When do you plan prophets expansion?** (`16`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you plan to start your expansion.
* **Where do you plan to expand?** (`17`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all locations you plan to expand to:
	+ USA
	+ Europe
	+ Asia
* **What is your budget for expansion?** (`18`, `number`, required: false): Enter your budget for expansion.
* **How many employees do you plan to hire in your new market?** (`19`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of employees you plan to hire.
* **When do you plan to start your expansion?** (`20`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you plan to start your expansion.
* **Can you provide more details on your expansion plans?** (`21`, `text`, required: false): Provide more details on your expansion plans.
* **Can you provide information about your partner?** (`22`, `text`, required: false): Provide more information about your partner.
* **Can you provide more details about your company?** (`23`, `text`, required: false): Provide more details about your company.
