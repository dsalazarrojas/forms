# Retailer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retailer Information Form is designed to collect and store data about retailers, which can be used to update or add new retailer information for internal use within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the store address on **store_address**.
2. Specify the store's hours of operation on **store_hours_of_operation**.
3. Enter the store's phone number on **phone_number**.
4. Enter the store's email address on **email_address**.
5. Enter the store's website on **store_website**.
6. Select the store's social media presence from the options provided on **social_media**.
7. Choose the store category from the options provided on **store_category**.
8. Enter a brief description of the store on **store_description**.
9. Enter any additional notes about the store on **store_notes** (note: this field does not support multiple values).
10. Enter the store's location on **store_location**.
11. Enter the name of the store manager on **store_manager**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **store_address**: Enter the store's address, including street number, city, state, and zip code.
* **store_hours_of_operation**: Specify the store's hours of operation, including opening and closing times, days of operation (e.g., Monday to Sunday).
* **phone_number**: Enter the store's phone number.
* **email_address**: Enter the store's email address.
* **store_website**: Enter the store's website (e.g., https://example.com).
* **social_media**: Select the store's social media presence from the options provided. This can be one or multiple options (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter).
* **store_category**: Choose a category for the store from the options provided. This can be one category at a time.
* **store_description**: Enter a brief description of the store.
* **store_notes**: Enter any additional notes about the store. This field does not support multiple values.
* **store_location**: Enter the store's location.
* **store_manager**: Enter the name of the store manager.
