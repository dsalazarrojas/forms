This form is designed to collect information about an organization's risk and compliance strategy, allowing for a thorough understanding of their current state and areas for improvement. The form's purpose is to facilitate the development of a comprehensive risk and compliance strategy advisory application. This guide will walk you through each step to complete the form accurately and effectively.

## Purpose
The main objective of this form is to gather essential information from the organization about their risk and compliance strategy. The information collected will be used to assess the organization's risk posture and provide tailored guidance on how to improve their compliance strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by answering the questions on page 1, "What is your role in the organization?"
2.  Proceed to page 2 to provide a brief description of the organization's risk and compliance background.
3.  On page 3, describe the organization's current compliance strategy.
4.  For page 4, select the level of risk associated with this compliance issue (High, Medium, or Low).
5.  Page 5 is for entering the date of the last review of the compliance issue (if applicable).
6.  On page 6, enter the time of the last review of the compliance issue (if applicable).
7.  Page 7 is to enter your contact information (email, phone number, etc.).
8.  For page 8, select how often you review the risk and compliance issues in the organization (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
9.  On page 9, select how often the risks associated with this issue occur (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely).
10.  Page 10 is for selecting the severity of the risk associated with this issue (High, Medium, Low).
11.  Finally, on page 11, describe your plan to mitigate the risk associated with this issue.
12.  Optionally, you can add any additional comments on page 12.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is your role in the organization?** (`introduction`, text, Required): Please enter your position or title within the organization to provide context for your role in the risk and compliance strategy.
*   **Please provide a brief description of the organization's risk and compliance background** (`background_info`, text, Required): Give a concise overview of the organization's risk and compliance situation, including any relevant policies, procedures, or regulatory requirements.
*   **Please describe the organization's current compliance strategy** (`compliance_strategy`, text, Required): Describe the current approach and methods used by the organization to manage and mitigate risks and ensure compliance.
*   **What is the level of risk associated with this compliance issue?** (`risk_level`, select_one, Required): Choose the level of risk (High, Medium, Low) associated with this compliance issue to help prioritize the risk.
*   **Date of last review** (`last_review_date`, date, Optional): If applicable, enter the date of the last review of the compliance issue to help track progress.
*   **Time of last review** (`last_review_time`, time, Optional): If applicable, enter the time of the last review of the compliance issue to track the timing.
*   **Please enter your contact information (email, phone number, etc.)** (`contact_info`, email, Required): Enter your contact information so that the team can reach out to you for follow-up questions or further discussions.
*   **How often do you review the risk and compliance issues in the organization?** (`review_frequency`, select_multiple, Required): Select how frequently you review risk and compliance issues in the organization to demonstrate your ongoing commitment to these areas.
*   **How often do the risks associated with this issue occur?** (`frequency_of_risk`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose how frequently the risks associated with this issue occur to further assess their impact.
*   **How severe is the risk associated with this issue?** (`severity_of_risk`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the severity level of the risk associated with this issue (High, Medium, Low) to understand its impact.
*   **Please describe your plan to mitigate the risk associated with this issue** (`mitigation_plan`, text, Required): Develop a plan to minimize the risk and its associated impact.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, Optional): Add any additional insights or concerns related to the risk and compliance strategy.
