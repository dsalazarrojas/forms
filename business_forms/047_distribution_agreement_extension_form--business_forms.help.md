# Distribution Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distribution Agreement Extension Form is used to request an extension to an existing Distribution Agreement. It is a straightforward form used to provide essential information about the extension request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the agreement number, which is a unique identifier for the agreement you are requesting an extension for.
2. Provide the reason for the extension request in the "reason_for_extension" field.
3. Enter the effective date of the extension, which is the date when the extension will start.
4. Enter the end date of the extension, which is the date when the extension will end.
5. Select the status of the extension, which can be either Active or Inactive.
6. Enter any important terms related to the agreement that need to be mentioned.
7. Enter the effective date of the extension.
8. Provide an assessment of the potential business impact of the extension.
9. Select the status of the review, which can be either Active or Inactive.
10. Enter any comments related to the review.
11. Enter the name of the person who approved the extension.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Number** (`agreement_number`, number, required): Enter a unique identifier for the agreement you are requesting an extension for.
* **Reason for Extension** (`reason_for_extension`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the reason for the extension request.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the extension will start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the extension will end.
* **Extension Status** (`extension_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the extension, which can be either Active or Inactive.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, text, optional): Enter any important terms related to the agreement that need to be mentioned.
* **Extension Effective Date** (`extension_effective_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the extension will be effective.
* **Potential Business Impact** (`potential_business_impact`, text, optional): Assess the potential business impact of the extension.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the review, which can be either Active or Inactive.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments related to the review.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who approved the extension.

## Tips

* Please make sure to be clear and concise in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the authorized personnel.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
