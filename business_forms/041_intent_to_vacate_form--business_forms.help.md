# Intent To Vacate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intent To Vacate Form is a document used to notify a tenant that their lease agreement will be ending, and they will need to vacate the premises on a specified date. It's essential to complete this form accurately to avoid any confusion or disputes regarding the lease termination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Intent To Vacate Form, follow these steps:

1. **Tenant Choice** (`tenant_choice`, select_one, optional):
	* Select 'Yes' if you are the tenant who is vacating the premises.
	* Select 'No' if you are not the tenant or are not vacating the premises.
2. **Reason for Vacating** (`reason_for_vacating`, text, optional):
	* Provide a brief explanation for the reason you're vacating the premises.
	* This field is not required, but it can be helpful for future reference.
3. **Date of Vacation** (`date_of_vacation`, date, optional):
	* Enter the date you plan to vacate the premises.
	* Please format the date in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2023-02-15).
4. **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional):
	* Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
	* This field is not required, but it can be useful for providing context.
5. **Tenant Details** (`tenant_details`, select_multiple, optional):
	* Select any applicable details related to your tenancy, such as 'Yes' for 'Yes, I will return the keys'.
	* Select 'No' if none of the options apply.
6. **Landlord Contact** (`landlord_contact`, email, optional):
	* Enter the email address of the landlord or property manager.
	* This field is not required, but it's essential for communication purposes.
7. **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, date, optional):
	* Enter the date you plan to vacate the premises.
	* Please format the date in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2023-02-15).

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult the relevant authorities or seek advice from a professional.
* Complete the form as soon as possible to ensure a smooth transition.
