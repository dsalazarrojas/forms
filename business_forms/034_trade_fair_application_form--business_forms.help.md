# Trade Fair Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary information for a trade fair application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section of the form carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to include all required information, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Use the provided hints for guidance on what information is needed for each field.
4. If you're unsure about any field, please ask our support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`1`, `text`, required): Please fill out your name and contact information in this section.
* **Product Details** (`2`, `text`, required): Describe your product in this section.
* **Contact Information** (`3`, `text`, required): Provide your contact information, including phone and email.
* **Company Information** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your company's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Product Description** (`5`, `text`, required): Write a detailed description of your product.
* **Product Image** (`6`, `text`, required): Upload a high-quality image of your product.
* **Trade Fair Information** (`7`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional trade fair information.
* **Business Hours** (`8`, `time`, required): Enter your business hours of operation.
* **Trade Fair Location** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have a trade fair location.
* **Trade Fair Date** (`10`, `date`, required): Enter the trade fair date.
* **Trade Fair Time** (`11`, `time`, required): Enter the trade fair time.
* **Trade Fair Product** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable trade fair products.
* **Application Status** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your application status.
* **Trade Fair Category** (`14`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your trade fair category.
* **Trade Fair Priority** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your trade fair priority.
* **Trade Fair Contact** (`17`, `email`, required): Enter your trade fair contact information.
* **Phone** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Fax** (`19`, `text`, optional): Enter your fax number.
* **Website** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter your website URL.
* **Application ID** (`21`, `text`, optional): Enter your application ID (if applicable).
* **Trade Fair Address** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter your trade fair address.
* **Trade Fair Notes** (`23`, `note`, optional): Add any additional trade fair notes.
* **Applicant ID** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter your applicant ID (if applicable).
* **Assigned** (`25`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether the application is assigned to a specific person.

Note: This help guide is a general explanation of the form fields. Please refer to the provided YAML for specific field labels and options.
