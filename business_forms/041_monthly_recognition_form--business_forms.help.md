# Monthly Recognition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a standardized way to acknowledge and show appreciation for employees' monthly achievements, milestones, or outstanding service within the business setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the category of recognition from the dropdown menu, which is currently set to "Business Forms". 
2.  Enter a brief description of the recognition or achievement being acknowledged. 
3.  Provide the name of the business or organization being recognized. 
4.  Enter the name of the employee being recognized. 
5.  Choose the date of the recognition or achievement. 
6.  Enter any relevant employee ID number or identifier, if applicable. 
7.  Describe the performance area or department being recognized.

*   **Date**: Choose the date of the recognition or achievement.

## Tips

*   Be brief but specific when describing the recognition or achievement. 
*   Use clear and concise language to ensure easy understanding of the recognition or achievement. 
*   Ensure the date of recognition is accurate and correct. 
*   Verify the employee ID, if applicable, to ensure accuracy. 
*   Be specific when describing the performance area or department being recognized.
