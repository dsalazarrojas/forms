# Vendor Payment Hold Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vendor Payment Hold Request Form is used to request a payment hold on a vendor's invoice. This form should be used when a vendor has not met the expected payment terms or conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with accurate information about the payment delay. Please make sure to provide sufficient reason for the payment delay, so that the vendor can be notified and understand the delay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Details**: Enter a brief description of the payment delay.
* **Invoice Date**: Enter the date of the invoice related to the payment delay.
* **Payment Delay Reason**: Enter the reason for the payment delay. This should be a clear and concise explanation of why the payment cannot be made at this time.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor related to the delayed payment.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount of the payment that is being delayed.
* **Requested By**: Enter your name or title to indicate who is requesting the payment hold.
* **Reason for Requesting**: Enter a brief description of why you are requesting the payment hold.
* **Vendor Email**: Enter the email address of the vendor to be notified about the payment delay.

## Tips
* Be accurate and clear when filling out the form to ensure the vendor is properly notified and understands the delay.
* Only fill out this form when a payment hold is truly warranted.
* Make sure to provide sufficient reason for the payment delay to avoid confusion and potential disputes.
