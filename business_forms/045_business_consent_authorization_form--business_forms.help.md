# Business Consent Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect business contact information and consent preferences from an individual or a business entity. It is part of the onboarding process or updating existing contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client Information** (`Client Information`, `note`, not required): This is a brief note section for you to provide any relevant information about your client or organization.
2. **Full Name** (`Full Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on official documents.
3. **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter the email address where you can be contacted.
4. **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
5. **Company Name** (`Company Name`, `text`, not required): If you represent a business, enter the name of your organization.
6. **Position** (`Position`, `text`, not required): Enter your job title or position within the organization.
7. **Mailing Address** (`Mailing Address`, `text`, not required): Enter your full mailing address.
8. **Consent Preferences** (`Consent Preferences`, `note`, not required): Briefly describe how you prefer to be contacted.
9. **Email Consent** (`Email Consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you consent to being contacted by email. Options:
	* Yes - I Consent
	* No - I Do Not Consent
10. **Email Purposes** (`Email Purposes`, `select_multiple`, not required): If you consent to being contacted by email, choose the purposes for which you are willing to be contacted. Options:
	* Service Updates
	* Marketing Communications
	* Newsletter
	* Event Invitations
	* Product Announcements
	* Surveys and Feedback
	* Account Notifications
11. **Phone Consent** (`Phone Consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you consent to being contacted by phone. Options:
	* Yes - I Consent
	* No - I Do Not Consent
12. **Phone Purposes** (`Phone Purposes`, `select_multiple`, not required): If you consent to being contacted by phone, choose the purposes for which you are willing to be contacted. Options:
	* Service Updates
	* Sales Calls
	* Customer Support
	* Appointment Reminders
	* Surveys and Feedback
	* Account Notifications
13. **SMS Consent** (`SMS Consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you consent to being sent text messages. Options:
	* Yes - I Consent
	* No - I Do Not Consent
14. **SMS Purposes** (`SMS Purposes`, `select_multiple`, not required): If you consent to being sent text messages, choose the purposes for which you are willing to be contacted. Options:
	* Appointment Reminders
	* Service Updates
	* Promotional Offers
	* Account Notifications
	* Security Alerts
15. **Postal Mail Consent** (`Postal Mail Consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you consent to being contacted by postal mail. Options:
	* Yes - I Consent
	* No - I Do Not Consent
16. **Postal Mail Purposes** (`Postal Mail Purposes`, `select_multiple`, not required): If you consent to being contacted by postal mail, choose the purposes for which you are willing to be contacted. Options:
	* Marketing Materials
	* Product Catalogs
	* Newsletters
	* Event Invitations
	* Account Statements
17. **Contact Frequency** (`Contact Frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you can be contacted. Options:
	* Daily
	* Weekly
	* Monthly
	* Quarterly
	* Only When Necessary
	* Never
18. **Third-Party Sharing** (`Third-Party Sharing`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you consent to sharing your information with third-party partners. Options:
	* Yes - I Consent
	* No - I Do Not Consent
19. **Data Processing** (`Data Processing`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you consent to data processing. Options:
	* Yes - I Consent
	* No - I Do Not Consent
20. **Consent Duration** (`Consent Duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose how long your consent is valid for. Options:
	* Until I Revoke
	* 1 Year
	* 2 Years
	* 3 Years
	* 5 Years
21. **Revocation Understanding** (`Revocation Understanding`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you understand that you can revoke your consent. Options:
	* Yes - I Understand
	* No - Please Explain
22. **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you agree to the terms and conditions. Options:
	* Yes - I Accept
	* No - I Do Not Accept
23. **Privacy Policy** (`Privacy Policy`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you have read the privacy policy. Options:
	* Yes - I Have Read
	* No - I Will Read
24. **Signature** (`Signature`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
25. **Signature Date** (`Signature Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of signing.
26. **Witness Name** (`Witness Name`, `text`, not required): If applicable, enter the name of a witness.
27. **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, not required): You can provide any additional information or preferences here.

## Tips
* Ensure your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Be aware of your consent preferences to avoid unwanted contact.
* Read and agree to the terms and conditions and privacy policy.
* Understand that you can revoke your consent at any time.
