# Free 5 Day Trial Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to create a 5-day trial period for an application or service. It gathers information that will be used to manage and track the trial period.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the trial form in the "Free 5 Day Trial Form" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the trial in the "Trial Description" field.
3. Select the category for the trial form from the "Category" field.
4. Choose the subcategory for the trial form from the "Subcategory" field.
5. Enter a description of the trial form in the "Description" field.
6. Upload an output file if necessary in the "Output File" field.
7. Select the "Assigned Tool" field if this trial form is assigned to a specific tool.
8. Enter the email address of the person responsible for the trial form in the "Email" field.
9. Enter a phone number for the person responsible for the trial form in the "Phone" field.
10. Enter a unique form ID in the "Form ID" field.
11. Choose the start date and time for the trial period in the "Start Date" and "Start Time" fields.
12. Choose the end date and time for the trial period in the "End Date" and "End Time" fields.
13. Enter the trial duration in days in the "Trial Duration" field.
14. Select the trial limit type from the "Trial Limit Type" field.
15. Choose the unit for the trial limit in the "Trial Limit Unit" field.
16. Choose the trial limit frequency from the "Trial Limit Frequency" field.
17. Choose the unit for the trial limit frequency in the "Trial Limit Frequency Unit" field.
18. Select the type for the trial limit frequency in the "Trial Limit Frequency Type" field.
19. Enter the time until the trial limit in the "Trial Limit Until" field.
20. Choose the unit for the trial limit until in the "Trial Limit Until Unit" field.
21. Select "Yes" or "No" for submitting the form in the "Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Free 5 Day Trial Form** (`form_title`, text, false): Enter a title for the trial form.
* **Trial Description** (`trial_description`, text, true): Provide a brief description of the trial form.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, false): Select the category for the trial form.
* **Subcategory** (`subcatagory`, select_one, false): Choose the subcategory for the trial form.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, true): Enter a description of the trial form.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, false): Upload an output file if necessary.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, false): Select the tool assigned to the trial form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, true): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the trial form.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, false): Enter a phone number for the person responsible for the trial form.
* **Form ID** (`form_ids`, number, true): Enter a unique form ID.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, true): Choose the start date for the trial period.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, true): Choose the start time for the trial period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, true): Choose the end date for the trial period.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, true): Choose the end time for the trial period.
* **Trial Duration** (`trial_duration`, number, true): Enter the trial duration in days.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, false): Select the category of the trial form.
* **Subcategory** (`subcatagory`, select_one, false): Choose the subcategory of the trial form.
* **Trial Limit Type** (`trial_limit_type`, select_one, false): Select the trial limit type.
* **Trial Limit Unit** (`trial_limit_unit`, select_one, false): Choose the unit for the trial limit.
* **Trial Limit Frequency** (`trial_limit_frequency`, select_one, false): Choose the trial limit frequency.
* **Trial Limit Frequency Unit** (`trial_limit_frequency_unit`, select_one, false): Choose the unit for the trial limit frequency.
* **Trial Limit Frequency Type** (`trial_limit_frequency_type`, select_one, false): Select the type for the trial limit frequency.
* **Trial Limit Until** (`trial_limit_until`, time, true): Enter the time until the trial limit.
* **Trial Limit Until Unit** (`trial_limit_until_unit`, select_one, false): Choose the unit for the trial limit until.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, false): Select "Yes" or "No" for submitting the form.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out all the required fields (marked as true).
- Enter accurate and relevant information for each field.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
