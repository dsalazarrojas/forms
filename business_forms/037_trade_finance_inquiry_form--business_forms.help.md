# Trade Finance Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect basic company information from trade finance inquiries. Fill out this form to provide the necessary details for our team to process your request.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with the required information.
2.  Make sure to enter a valid email address in the email field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your company.
    *   This field is for the name of your company.
*   **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of your company.
    *   This field is for the address of your company.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact person's name.
    *   This field is for the name of the person we should contact for further information.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for our team to contact you.
    *   This field is for the email address we will use to contact you.

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
*   If you have any further questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to reach out to us.
*   Our team will be in touch with you shortly after receiving this form.
