# Project Transition Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Transition Plan Form is used to document and manage project transitions, including key deliverables, approval processes, and next steps. This form helps ensure that projects are properly transferred between team members, departments, or phases, reducing errors and increasing efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your project's name and a brief description.
2. Enter the name and role of the project owner or responsible party.
3. Select the start date and end date of the project.
4. Determine the risk level and provide a brief description of the risks involved.
5. Describe the approval process and any relevant comments or next steps.
6. Select the key deliverables that need to be transitioned.
7. Choose the status of the approval (Approved, Pending, or Rejected).
8. Provide any comments or feedback from the approval process.
9. Specify the next steps or actions to be taken.
10. Confirm the person responsible for transitioning the project and the date and time of transition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your project.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the project's owner or responsible party.
* **Owner Role** (`owner_role`, text, required): Enter the role of the project's owner or responsible party.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the project.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the project.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, text, optional): Indicate the level of risk involved in the project.
* **Risk Description** (`risk_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the risks involved.
* **Approval Process** (`approval_process`, text, required): Describe the approval process and any relevant comments or next steps.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of approval.
* **Transition Notes** (`transition_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the transition.
* **Key Deliverables** (`key_deliverables`, text, optional): List the key deliverables that need to be transitioned.
* **Key Deliverables List** (`key_deliverables_list`, select_multiple, required): Select the key deliverables that need to be transitioned.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the approval (Approved, Pending, or Rejected).
* **Approval Comment** (`approval_comment`, text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback from the approval process.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Specify the next steps or actions to be taken.
* **Transitioned By** (`transitioned_by`, text, required): Confirm the person responsible for transitioning the project.
* **Transitioned Date** (`transitioned_date`, date, required): Enter the date of transition.
* **Transitioned Time** (`transitioned_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of transition.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Created Date** (`created_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was created.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who updated the form.
* **Updated Date** (`updated_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was updated.
* **Updated Time** (`updated_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was updated.
* **Deleted Date** (`deleted_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was deleted.

## Tips
* Be thorough when filling out the form to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Use the form as a guide to help you manage project transitions effectively.
