# Business Education Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Education Fund Donation Form is designed for businesses or individuals who wish to donate to a business education fund. The form collects necessary information for processing the donation, including donor details, donation preferences, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Donor Type**: Select the type of donor you are - Individual, Business/Corporation, Foundation, Non-Profit, Government, or Other.
2.  **First Name**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
3.  **Last Name**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
4.  **Email Address**: Enter your email for confirmation and future correspondence.
5.  **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number.
6.  **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate.
7.  **Currency**: Select the currency of your donation - USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, INR, or Other.
8.  **Donation Frequency**: Choose how often you wish to donate - One-time, Monthly, Quarterly, Annual, or Other.
9.  **Fund Allocation Preference**: Choose how your donation should be allocated - Scholarships, Educational Programs, Faculty Development, Research Initiatives, Infrastructure, or Unrestricted Use.
10.  **Educational Focus Area**: If you wish to specify a focus area for your donation, enter it here.
11.  **Payment Method**: Select how you wish to pay - Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Check, Wire Transfer, PayPal, or Other.
12.  **Card Type**: If paying by card, select the type of card - Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, or Other.
13.  **Billing Address**: Enter the address to be billed.
14.  **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
15.  **State/Province**: Enter the state or province where you reside.
16.  **Postal Code**: Enter your postal or ZIP code.
17.  **Country**: Enter your country of residence.
18.  **Donor Recognition**: Select how you wish to be recognized - Public Recognition, Anonymous Donation, Named Scholarship, or No Preference.
19.  **Send Tax Receipt**: Decide if you would like a tax receipt sent to you after the donation - True or False.
