# lease_agreement_compliance_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a lease agreement compliance report, helping to identify potential issues and ensure that both the landlord and tenant are aware of their responsibilities and obligations under the lease agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Tenant Details** page with the tenant's name and contact information.
2. Proceed to the **Property Info** page and provide details about the property being leased.
3. On the **Lease Terms** page, enter the terms of the lease agreement, including the duration, rent, and any specific conditions or requirements.
4. If there are any issues that have arisen during the lease term, list them on the **Compliance Issues** page.
5. Finally, add any additional notes or information on the **Notes** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Details** (Name, type: text, required: false): This field asks for the details of the tenant, such as their name and contact information.
* **Property Info** (Address, type: text, required: false): This field asks for details about the property, such as its address and other relevant information.
* **Lease Terms** (Duration, Rent, type: text, required: false): This field asks for the terms of the lease agreement, including the duration of the lease and the rent.
* **Compliance Issues** (type: text, required: false): This field asks for any issues that have arisen during the lease term, such as late payments, damage to the property, etc.
* **Notes** (type: text, required: false): This field provides a space for additional information and notes about the lease agreement.

## Tips
This form is designed to be completed by both landlords and tenants. Please ensure that all relevant information is accurate and up-to-date to ensure a thorough compliance report.
