# Expense Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Report Form is used to report expenses related to a particular project or task.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether the expense was submitted by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the "Expense Report" dropdown list.
2. Input the name of the company that the expense relates to.
3. Enter the date of the expense in the "Expense Date" field.
4. Input the amount of the expense in the "Expense Amount" field.
5. Choose the type of expense from the "Expense Type" dropdown list.
6. Provide a brief description of the expense in the "Expense Description" field.
7. If applicable, attach expense receipts to the "Expense Receipts" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expense Report** (Select Multiple, optional): Select whether the expense was submitted by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the list.
* **Company Name** (Text, optional): Input the name of the company that the expense relates to.
* **Expense Date** (Date, optional): Enter the date of the expense in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Expense Amount** (Number, optional): Input the amount of the expense.
* **Expense Type** (Select One, optional): Choose the type of expense from the list.
* **Expense Description** (Text, optional): Provide a brief description of the expense.
* **Expense Receipts** (Text, optional): Attach expense receipts if applicable.
* **Submit** (Text, optional): Click this button to submit the form.
