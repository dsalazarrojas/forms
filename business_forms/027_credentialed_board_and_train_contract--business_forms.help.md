# Credentialed Board And Train Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Credentialed Board And Train Contract form is used to capture information about a client's training program and agreement with a trainer. This form helps track client-trainer agreements and training progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering the client's name in the Client Name field.
2. Enter the trainer's name in the Trainer Name field.
3. Select the training program from the drop-down menu.
4. Enter the start date of the training in the Start Date field.
5. Enter the end date of the training in the End Date field.
6. If applicable, enter the contract date and contract duration for the training program.
7. Check the boxes for Behavior Professionals, LIMA and Training, and Science and Behavior as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the client's full name.
* **Trainer Name**: Enter the trainer's full name.
* **Training Program**: Select the training program from the drop-down menu to indicate whether the client is participating in a training program.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the training in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the training in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-02-28).
* **Contract Date**: This field is optional and not required. It is intended to be used if there is a need to document a specific date related to the contract, but its purpose is unclear without further context.
* **Contract Duration**: Enter the duration of the contract in days or hours.
* **Behavior Professionals**: Check the box if the client has behavior professionals involved in the training.
* **LIMA and Training**: Check the box if the training includes LIMA (Learning and Improving Movement Abilities) and Training components.
* **Science and Behavior**: Check the box if the training involves science and behavior components.
