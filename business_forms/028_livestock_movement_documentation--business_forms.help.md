# Livestock Movement Documentation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the movement of livestock from one location to another, including the origin and destination locations, dates and times of departure and arrival, mode of transport, type of livestock, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Origin** field with the location where the livestock is being moved from.
2. Enter the **Destination** where the livestock is being moved to.
3. Provide the **Origin Date** and **Origin Time** of the livestock movement.
4. Enter the **Destination Date** and **Destination Time** of the livestock movement.
5. Answer the **Transporters** question by selecting the mode of transport used (e.g., truck, plane, etc.).
6. Check the **Vehicles** box if vehicles are involved in the movement.
7. Select the type of **Livestock** being moved (e.g., cows, chickens, etc.).
8. Provide any additional **Movement Details** in the text field.
9. Enter the **Total Moving** number of animals being moved.
10. Enter the **Origin Weight** of the livestock being moved.
11. Select the **Origin Weight Unit** (e.g., kg, ton) of the livestock being moved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Origin**: The location from which the livestock is being moved.
* **Destination**: The location to which the livestock is being moved.
* **Origin Date**: The date on which the livestock departed from the origin location.
* **Origin Time**: The time of day when the livestock departed from the origin location.
* **Destination Date**: The date on which the livestock arrived at the destination location.
* **Destination Time**: The time of day when the livestock arrived at the destination location.
* **Transporters**: Check "Yes" if vehicles or humans are involved in transporting the livestock, "No" otherwise.
* **Vehicles**: Check "Yes" if vehicles are used for transport, "No" otherwise.
* **Livestock**: Select the type of animal being moved (e.g., cows, chickens, etc.).
* **Movement Details**: Provide any additional details about the livestock movement, such as route taken or notable events.
* **Total Moving**: Enter the total number of animals being moved.
* **Origin Weight**: Enter the weight of the livestock being moved.
* **Origin Weight Unit**: Select the unit of weight (e.g., kg, ton) of the livestock being moved.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled in completely and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
* If you have already submitted a form for the same movement, do not re-submit this form.
