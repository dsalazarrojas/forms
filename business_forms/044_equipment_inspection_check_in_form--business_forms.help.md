# Equipment Inspection Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Equipment Inspection Check In Form is used to collect information about individual equipment inspections. This form provides a standardized way to record general information, equipment status, condition report, photo upload, notes, assigned personnel, equipment inspection checklist, inspection date/time, inspection completion status, and equipment type.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the general information section with the necessary details about the equipment being inspected.
*   Provide an update on the equipment status, indicating if it's in good or bad condition.
*   Submit a condition report with any relevant information about the equipment's condition.
*   Upload a photo of the equipment if necessary.
*   Add any additional notes about the inspection.
*   Select the assigned personnel who will be completing the inspection.
*   Check the equipment inspection checklist to verify that all necessary steps were taken.
*   Record the inspection date and time.
*   Mark the inspection as completed or incomplete if necessary.
*   Indicate if the equipment is of type 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information** (ID: 1): This field is for providing overall information about the equipment being inspected.
*   **Equipment Status** (ID: 2): Check this field to indicate the condition of the equipment, whether it's in good or bad condition.
*   **Condition Report** (ID: 3): Submit a detailed report about the equipment's condition, including any issues or problems found.
*   **Photo Upload** (ID: 4): Upload a photo of the equipment for visual reference or inspection evidence.
*   **Notes** (ID: 5): Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
*   **Assigned To** (ID: 6): Select the person responsible for the equipment inspection.
*   **Equipment Inspection Checklist** (ID: 7): Verify that all necessary steps were taken during the inspection by checking the checklist provided.
*   **Inspection Date/Time** (ID: 8): Record the date and time of the inspection.
*   **Inspection Completed** (ID: 9): Mark the inspection as completed, incomplete, or in progress.
*   **Equipment Type** (ID: 10): Indicate if the equipment is of type 'Yes' or 'No'.
