# vendor_invoice_submission_form_denver_janitorial_company - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vendor_invoice_submission_form_denver_janitorial_company is a form used by vendors to submit invoices for payment. It gathers essential information for processing and approving invoices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the date the invoice is due (Due Date).
2. Enter the date the invoice is to be received (Invoice Date).
3. Enter the name of the vendor submitting the invoice (Vendor Name).
4. Enter the email address of the vendor (Vendor Email).
5. Enter the phone number of the vendor (Phone Number).
6. Enter the invoice number (Invoice Number).
7. Enter the total amount of the invoice (Total Amount).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Invoice Date** (`invoice_date`, date, required: false): This is the date the invoice is to be received and processed.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, required: false): This is the date the invoice is due for payment.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required: false): This is the name of the vendor submitting the invoice.
* **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`, email, required: false): This is the email address of the vendor.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): This is the phone number of the vendor.
* **Invoice Number** (`invoice_number`, number, required: false): This is the invoice number.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, number, required: false): This is the total amount of the invoice.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate dates for both the invoice date and due date.
* Double-check the contact information for the vendor.
* Verify the total amount is correct to avoid errors.
