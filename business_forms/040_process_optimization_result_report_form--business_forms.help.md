# Process Optimization Result Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data related to process optimization results for business or operational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team name in the "Team Name" field.
2. Enter the project name in the "Project Name" field.
3. Enter the project ID (if applicable) in the "Project ID" field.
4. Describe the process improvement efforts in the "Process Improvement" field.
5. List metrics collected in the "Metrics Collected" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team or department responsible for the process optimization.
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project being optimized.
* **Project ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the project (if applicable).
* **Process Improvement**: Describe any improvements made to the process.
* **Metrics Collected**: List any metrics collected during the process optimization.
* **Metric 1-18**: Enter the values for each metric (e.g., Metric 1, Metric 2, etc.).

Note: If you are unsure about a field, please refer to your project manager or the form's creator for clarification.
