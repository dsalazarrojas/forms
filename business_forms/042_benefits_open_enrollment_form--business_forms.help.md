# Benefits Open Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Benefits Open Enrollment Form is used to collect information from employees for their benefits enrollment. This form is designed to gather personal details, benefits selections, and acknowledgments to ensure that employees are enrolled in the correct benefits plans according to their needs and eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your personal details: Enter your employee ID, employee full name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Select your employment type: Choose your employment type from the available options (Full-Time, Part-Time, Temporary, or Contract).
3.  Provide household information: Enter your marital status and the number of dependents.
4.  Select your benefits: Choose your preferred health insurance plan, coverage tier, and whether you want to enroll in dental, vision, and life insurance.
5.  Make FSA and HSA selections: Decide whether you want to enroll in a flexible spending account (FSA) or health savings account (HSA).
6.  Enter FSA annual election amount (if applicable): If you're enrolling in an FSA, enter the desired annual election amount.
7.  Acknowledge that you've reviewed benefit materials: Confirm that you've reviewed the benefit materials provided.
8.  Understand your elections: Confirm that you understand your benefit elections.
9.  Acknowledge the enrollment deadline: Confirm that you're aware of the enrollment deadline.
10.  Select the date of enrollment: Choose the date you'd like to be enrolled in the benefits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your work email address.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Department**: Enter the department you belong to or the team you're working with.
*   **Employment Type**: Choose your employment type (e.g., Full-Time, Part-Time, Temporary, or Contract).
*   **Marital Status**: Select your marital status from the available options (Single, Married, Domestic Partnership, Divorced, or Widowed).
*   **Number of Dependents**: Enter the number of dependents you have.
*   **Dependent Details**: Enter the names and ages of your dependents.
*   **Health Insurance Plan Selection**: Choose your preferred health insurance plan from the available options.
*   **Coverage Tier**: Select your coverage tier from the available options (Individual, Employee Plus Spouse, Employee Plus Child, Family, or Waive).
*   **Dental Insurance**: Decide whether you want to enroll in dental insurance or waive it.
*   **Vision Insurance**: Decide whether you want to enroll in vision insurance or waive it.
*   **Life Insurance Coverage Amount**: Choose the amount of life insurance coverage you want (1x, 2x, 3x, or 5x Salary).
*   **FSA Election**: Decide whether you want to enroll in a Flexible Spending Account (FSA) or waive it.
*   **HSA Election**: Decide whether you want to enroll in a Health Savings Account (HSA) or waive it.
*   **FSA Annual Election Amount**: Enter the desired annual FSA election amount if you're enrolling.
*   **I Have Reviewed Benefit Materials**: Confirm that you've reviewed the benefit materials.
*   **I Understand My Benefit Elections**: Confirm that you understand your benefit elections.
*   **Enrollment Deadline Acknowledged**: Confirm that you're aware of the enrollment deadline.
*   **Enrollment Date**: Choose the date you'd like to be enrolled in the benefits.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and detailed when filling out the form to ensure that your benefits selections are correct.
*   Review the benefit materials carefully before selecting your benefits.
*   Understand your employment type and its implications on your benefits.
*   Confirm that you've reviewed all the sections before submitting the form.
