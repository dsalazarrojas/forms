# Year End Business Review - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Year End Business Review form is designed to collect and document the performance of a business at the end of each year. This form is intended to be completed by business stakeholders and reviewed by reviewers to provide an accurate and comprehensive assessment of the business's progress, challenges, and future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the business name in the "Enter Business Name" field (Field 1).
2. Select the review year from the "Review Year" field (Field 2).
3. Enter the business's revenue for the review year in the "Revenue" field (Field 3).
4. Select the key metrics that the business achieved in the review year from the "Key Metrics" field (Field 4).
5. Identify the key challenges the business faced in the review year from the "Key Challenges" field (Field 5).
6. Choose the areas for improvement for the business in the review year from the "Key Improvements" field (Field 6).
7. Rate the business's overall performance on a scale of 1-5 in the "Overall Rating" field (Field 7).
8. Enter any review comments for the business in the "Review Comments" field (Field 8).
9. Enter the business's email address in the "Email" field (Field 9).
10. Enter the business's phone number in the "Phone" field (Field 10).
11. Enter the business's website in the "Business Website" field (Field 11).
12. Enter the business's address in the "Business Address" field (Field 12).
13. Enter the contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field (Field 13).
14. Select the review date from the "Review Date" field (Field 14).
15. Select the review time from the "Review Time" field (Field 15).
16. Select the review status from the "Review Status" field (Field 16).
17. Select the submitter from the "Submitter" field (Field 17).
18. Select the reviewer from the "Reviewer" field (Field 18).
19. Select the review owners from the "Review Owners" field (Field 19).
20. Select the submission date from the "Submission Date" field (Field 20).
21. Select the submission time from the "Submission Time" field (Field 21).
22. Select the submission status from the "Submission Status" field (Field 22).
23. (Optional) Enter any additional review type or notes from the "Review Type" field (Field 23) and "Submission Notes" field (Field 24).
24. (Optional) Enter any reviewer's notes from the "Reviewer Notes" field (Field 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Business Name** (Field 1, text, required): Enter the name of the business being reviewed.
* **Review Year** (Field 2, number, required): Select the year being reviewed.
* **Revenue** (Field 3, number, required): Enter the business's revenue for the review year, including the currency symbol.
* **Key Metrics** (Field 4, select_multiple, required): Select the key metrics achieved by the business, such as Growth, Profit, or Customer Satisfaction.
* **Key Challenges** (Field 5, select_multiple, required): Identify the key challenges the business faced in the review year.
* **Key Improvements** (Field 6, select_multiple, required): Choose the areas for improvement for the business in the review year.
* **Overall Rating** (Field 7, number, required): Rate the business's overall performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Review Comments** (Field 8, note, required): Enter any review comments or observations about the business.
* **Email** (Field 9, email, required): Enter the business's email address.
* **Phone** (Field 10, text, required): Enter the business's phone number.
* **Business Website** (Field 11, text, required): Enter the business's website.
* **Business Address** (Field 12, text, required): Enter the business's address.
* **Contact Person** (Field 13, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Review Date** (Field 14, date, required): Select the date of the review.
* **Review Time** (Field 15, time, required): Select the time of the review.
* **Review Status** (Field 16, select_one, required): Select the review status, such as Approved, In Progress, or Revised.
* **Submitter** (Field 17, select_one, required): Select the person who submitted the review.
* **Reviewer** (Field 18, select_one, required): Select the person who reviewed the business.
* **Review Owners** (Field 19, select_one, required): Select the owner of the review.
* **Submission Date** (Field 20, date, required): Select the date the review was submitted.
* **Submission Time** (Field 21, time, required): Select the time the review was submitted.
* **Submission Status** (Field 22, select_one, required): Select the submission status, such as Approved, Rejected, or Pending.
* **Review Type** (Field 23, text, optional): Enter any additional review type or notes.
* **Submission Notes** (Field 24, note, optional): Enter any additional submission notes.
* **Reviewer Notes** (Field 25, note, optional): Enter any additional reviewer's notes.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a comprehensive review.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the instructions above or contact the relevant department for clarification.
* This form is designed to facilitate a thorough and objective review of the business's performance.
* The review process is a valuable tool for businesses to reflect on their performance and identify areas for improvement.
