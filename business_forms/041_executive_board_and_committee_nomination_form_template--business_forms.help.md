# Executive Board And Committee Nomination Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate individuals for positions on the executive board and committees within the organization. It captures key information about the candidate, nominator, and nominating details to facilitate a smooth and organized nomination process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the candidate's name, job title, email address, and contact information, including their phone number and department.
2. Select the committee that the candidate is being nominated for.
3. Enter the date the candidate would start in the position and the date they would end (leave blank if no end date is known).
4. Enter the nominator's name, email address, phone number, job title, and department.
5. Review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the individual being nominated.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the job title of the individual being nominated.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the individual being nominated.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the individual being nominated.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the individual would start in the position.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the individual would end in the position (leave blank if no end date is known).
* **Committee** (`committee`, text, required: false): Select the committee that the individual is being nominated for.
* **Committee Position** (`committee_position`, text, required: false): Enter the position on the committee that the individual is being nominated for.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the individual being nominated.
* **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person nominating the individual.
* **Nominator Contact Info** (`nominator_contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter additional contact information for the nominator.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the nominator.
* **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the nominator.
* **Nominator Job Title** (`nominator_job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the job title of the nominator.
* **Nominator Department** (`nominator_department`, text, required: false): Enter the department of the nominator.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, or the form will not be submitted.
- If no end date is known, leave the end date field blank.
- Select the committee that is most relevant to the nominee's role.
