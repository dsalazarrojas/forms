# Cross Border Tax Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for businesses that operate across national borders and need to declare their tax registrations. It helps to gather essential details about a business entity's tax registrations, ensuring accurate and consistent information for tax compliance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information about your business entity.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for the country and business entity registrations type.
3. Enter the correct date and time for the business entity's registrations.
4. Double-check all the fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Entity Name** (`business_entity_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your business entity.
* **Business Entity Address** (`business_entity_address`, text, required): Enter the address of your business entity.
* **Business Entity City** (`business_entity_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your business entity.
* **Business Entity Postal Code** (`business_entity_postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code of your business entity.
* **Business Entity Country** (`business_entity_country`, select_one, required): Select the country where your business entity is registered.
* **Business Entity Phone** (`business_entity_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your business entity.
* **Business Entity Email** (`business_entity_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your business entity.
* **Business Entity Fax** (`business_entity_fax`, text, optional): Enter the fax number of your business entity (if applicable).
* **Business Entity Website** (`business_entity_web`, text, required): Enter the website of your business entity.
* **Business Entity Registrations Type** (`business_entity_registrations_type`, select_one, required): Indicate if your business entity has registrations ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Business Entity Registrations Value** (`business_entity_registrations_value`, number, required): Enter the value of your business entity's registrations.
* **Business Entity Registrations Frequency** (`business_entity_registrations_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate if your business entity has regular registrations ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Business Entity Registrations Note** (`business_entity_registrations_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your business entity's registrations (if applicable).
* **Business Entity Registrations Date** (`business_entity_registrations_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your business entity's registrations.
* **Business Entity Registrations Time** (`business_entity_registrations_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your business entity's registrations (if applicable).

## Tips

* Make sure to check your information carefully before submitting the form.
* Double-check your country and business entity registrations type selections to ensure accuracy.
* Enter the correct date and time for your business entity's registrations.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your tax professional or authority for assistance.
