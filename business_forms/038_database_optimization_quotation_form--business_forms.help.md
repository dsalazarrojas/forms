# Database Optimization Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their database optimization needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter your client's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your client's phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the type of service you need from the "Service Type" dropdown menu.
5. Choose the frequency of the service from the "Service Frequency" dropdown menu.
6. Select the type of database you are working with from the "Database Type" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the size of your database in the "Database Size" field.
8. Select the level of database optimization service you need from the "Database Optimization Service Level" dropdown menu.
9. Choose the level of service you need from the "Service Level" dropdown menu.
10. Enter the date you would like the service to start in the "Service Start Date" field.
11. Enter the date you would like the service to end in the "Service End Date" field.
12. Estimate the cost of the service in the "Estimated Cost" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your client's name to help us understand who you are working with.
* **Client Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your client's email address to help us stay in touch with you.
* **Client Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your client's phone number so we can contact you about your service.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of service you need from our options (Option 1, 2, 3).
* **Service Frequency** (`service_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the frequency of the service from our options (Option 4, 5, 6, 7).
* **Database Type** (`database_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of database you are working with from our options (Option 8, 9, 10).
* **Database Size** (`database_size`, `number`, optional): Enter the size of your database.
* **Database Optimization Service Level** (`database_optimization_service_level`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the level of database optimization service you need from our options (Option 12, 13, 14, 15, 16).
* **Service Level** (`service_level`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the level of service you need from our options (Option 17, 18, 19, 20, 21).
* **Service Start Date** (`service_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you would like the service to start.
* **Service End Date** (`service_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you would like the service to end.
* **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, `number`, optional): Estimate the cost of the service.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, `note`, optional): This is a text field for any additional information you'd like to provide.

Note that each field is optional unless marked as required. You can fill in the form as much or as little as you need to provide relevant information about your database optimization needs. 

If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
