# Corporate Recovery Strategy Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Recovery Strategy Course Registration form is used to collect registration information for the Corporate Recovery Strategy Course. Please use this form to register participants for the course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are registering for the correct course and participant(s).
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Corporate Recovery Strategy Course** (`title`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the course you are registering for.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the participant(s) registering for the course.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the participant(s) or the person registering on behalf of the participant(s).
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the participant is registering with a specific role, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company the participant(s) is/are affiliated with.
* **Course Details** (`course_details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional course details or requirements.
* **Course Start Date** (`course_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the course.
* **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, select_multiple, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the participant(s) is/are registering with a specific course duration, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the course.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* Double-check the information entered before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and relevance.
* Only submit one registration per participant to prevent duplicate registrations.
* If you are unsure about any field or require further assistance, contact the course administrators.
