# mixed_use_revelopment_funding_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect project details for mixed-use redevelopment funding applications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Upload any supporting documents as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Project Leader** (`project_leader`, text, optional): Enter the name of the project lead.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the project.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of project (e.g. "Project Type" or "Project Type 2").
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of your project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, optional): Enter the budget for your project.
* **Project Funding** (`project_funding`, select_multiple, optional): Select any applicable funding options (e.g. "Funding Option 1" or "Funding Option 2").
* **Support Documents** (`support_documents`, text, optional): Attach any supporting documents for your project.
* **Submission Reviewers** (`submission_reviewers`, note, optional): Enter any reviewers for your project submission.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, note, optional): Enter any reviewer comments.
* **Form Submitter** (`form_submitter`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Project Lead Email** (`project_lead_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the project lead.
* **Project Lead Phone** (`project_lead_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the project lead.
* **Project Lead Address** (`project_lead_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the project lead.
* **Project Lead Notes** (`project_lead_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes about the project lead.
* **Project Lead Title** (`project_lead_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the project lead.
* **Project Lead Company** (`project_lead_company`, text, optional): Enter the company name of the project lead.
* **Project Lead Department** (`project_lead_department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the project lead.
* **Project Lead Job** (`project_lead_job`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the project lead.
* **Job Title** (`project_lead_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the project lead.
* **Job Description** (`project_lead_job_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the project lead's job.
* **Job Level** (`project_lead_job_level`, text, optional): Enter the job level of the project lead.
* **Job Salary** (`project_lead_job_salary`, number, optional): Enter the salary of the project lead.
* **Job Type** (`project_lead_job_type`, text, optional): Enter the job type of the project lead.
* **Project Lead Agreement** (`project_lead_agreement`, text, optional): Enter any agreements or terms related to the project lead.
