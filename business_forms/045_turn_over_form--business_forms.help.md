# turn_over_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The turn_over_form is designed to collect information from customers about their product returns. It helps customer service representatives process returns efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, fill in the **product_return_request** field with a brief description of the product you would like to return.
2.  Provide a detailed description of **reason_for_return** to help us understand the reason for the return.
3.  Select the **product_condition** of the product you are returning (e.g., New, Used, Refurbished, or Defective).
4.  Fill in your **customer_name** so we can contact you about the return.
5.  Enter your **customer_email** to ensure we can reach you for any updates or additional information.
6.  Finally, enter your **customer_phone** number so we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **product_return_request (text)**: A brief description of the product you would like to return.
*   **reason_for_return (text)**: A detailed description of the reason for the return.
*   **product_condition (select)**: Select the condition of the product you are returning (New, Used, Refurbished, or Defective).
*   **customer_name (text)**: Enter your name as the customer.
*   **customer_email (email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for updates.
*   **customer_phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for any further questions or updates.
