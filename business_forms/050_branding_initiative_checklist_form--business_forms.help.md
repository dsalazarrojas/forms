# Branding Initiative Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track and manage a Branding Initiative's progress, from discovery and research to execution and launch. It allows project managers and team members to keep track of tasks completed, progress, and resources involved in each phase of the initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with the necessary information.
2. Select all applicable tasks for each phase by checking the relevant boxes.
3. Update the status of each phase as you progress through the initiative.
4. Enter any external partners or agencies involved in the initiative.
5. Record any current roadblocks or delays that may impact the initiative's progress.
6. Enter the last updated date for the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Initiative Name** (initiative_name_val): Enter the name of the branding initiative.
* **Project Manager Name** (pm_name): Enter the name of the project manager responsible for the initiative.
* **Initiative Start Date** (initiative_start_dt): Enter the date the initiative started.
* **Target Completion Date** (completion_dt_target): Enter the target date for completing the initiative.
* **Discovery Tasks Completed** (p1_tasks_select): Select all tasks completed in Phase 1, including Market Research, Competitor Analysis, Stakeholder Interviews, and Brand Audit.
* **Phase 1 Overall Status** (p1_status_select): Update the status of Phase 1 as Not Started, In Progress, or Completed.
* **Strategy Tasks Completed** (p2_tasks_select): Select all tasks completed in Phase 2, including Brand Narrative Defined, Visual Concept Selection, Messaging Framework, and Positioning Statement.
* **Phase 2 Overall Status** (p2_status_select): Update the status of Phase 2 as Not Started, In Progress, or Completed.
* **Execution Tasks Completed** (p3_tasks_select): Select all tasks completed in Phase 3, including Asset Production, Website Update, Internal Announcement, and External Campaign Launch.
* **Phase 3 Overall Status** (p3_status_select): Update the status of Phase 3 as Not Started, In Progress, or Completed.
* **External Agencies / Partners Involved** (partners_external): Enter any external agencies or partners involved in the initiative.
* **Current Spend to Date** (spend_to_dt): Enter the current spend to date for the initiative.
* **Identify any current roadblocks or delays** (roadblock_desc): Record any current roadblocks or delays that may impact the initiative's progress.
* **Last Updated Date** (last_upd_dt): Enter the date and time the form was last updated.
