# PPE Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track the status of various aspects of personal protective equipment (PPE) in your workplace, including:
* List of personal protective equipment available.
* Training received on PPE safety.
* Frequency of employee safety inspections.
* Maintenance of PPE.
* Usage policy for PPE.
* Storage of PPE.
* Frequency of PPE inspections.
* Emergency equipment available.
* Disposal of PPE.
* Reporting and recording of PPE usage and inspections.
* Status of PPE checklists.
* Employee involvement in PPE safety.
* Frequency of PPE checklist reviews.
* PPE supervisor.
* Frequency of PPE checklist status reviews.
* PPE reviewer.
* Frequency of emergency response training.
* PPE reviewer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Go through each section of the form, and select the status of each aspect of PPE safety.
2. For each field, select the correct option from the provided list.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields, indicated by the "Required" tag.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and update the form regularly to ensure accurate information.
* Keep the form in a safe and accessible location.
* Make sure to involve all employees and supervisors in PPE safety practices.
* Review the form with employees and supervisors to ensure they understand the importance of accurate reporting.
* Be consistent in the frequency and format of the form reviews.
* Use the collected data to identify areas of improvement and implement changes to improve PPE safety in your workplace.
