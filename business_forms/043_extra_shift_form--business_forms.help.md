# Extra Shift Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Extra Shift Form is a data collection tool used to record extra shifts worked by employees. This form helps management or HR to monitor and manage employee schedules and overtime.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Extra Shift Form, follow these steps:

1. **Extra Shift Date** (Extra Shift Form 1): Enter the date on which you worked the extra shift.
2. **Employee Name** (Employee Name): Enter your name as it appears on your employee record.
3. **Shift Date** (Shift Date Label): Enter the date of the extra shift you are reporting.
4. **Shift Time** (Shift Time Label): Enter the time you worked the extra shift.
5. **Shifts Available**: Select the days you are available to work extra shifts.
6. **Shifts Worked** (Shifts Worked Label): Enter the number of extra shifts you have worked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Extra Shift Form 1** (`extra_shift_form_1`, text, required): Enter the date on which you worked the extra shift.
	+ This field is required and should be entered in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee record.
* **Shift Date** (`shift_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the extra shift you are reporting.
* **Shift Time** (`shift_time`, time, required): Enter the time you worked the extra shift.
* **Shifts Available** (`shifts_available`, select_multiple, optional): Select the days you are available to work extra shifts.
	+ Options: Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun
* **Shifts Worked** (`shifts_worked`, text, required): Enter the number of extra shifts you have worked.

## Tips

* Be accurate when entering the date and time of the extra shift.
* If you are unsure which days you are available to work extra shifts, select all the days you are available.
* If you have worked extra shifts, enter the correct number of shifts you have worked.
