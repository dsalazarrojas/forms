# Process Improvement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Improvement Form is designed to collect and track process improvements within your business or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Suggest an Improvement" field with your process improvement idea.
2. Select a Category for your improvement from the drop-down menu.
3. Choose the Priority Level for your improvement.
4. Select the Current Status of your improvement.
5. Describe the Impact on the Business and the Impact on Customers.
6. Set the Implementation Date and Implementation Time.
7. Indicate who will implement the improvement.
8. Enter any additional Notes.
9. Select who is Assigned To implement the improvement.
10. Set the Due Date for the improvement.
11. Set the Follow-up Date and Follow-up Time.
12. Indicate who will follow-up on the improvement.
13. Enter any Follow-up Notes.
14. Select the Follow-up Status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Suggest an Improvement** (`business_processes`, text, required): Describe your process improvement idea.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select a category for your improvement.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, select_multiple, required): Choose the priority level for your improvement.
* **Current Status** (`implementation_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of your improvement.
* **Impact on Business** (`impact_on_business`, text, required): Describe the impact of your improvement on the business.
* **Impact on Customers** (`impact_on_customers`, text, required): Describe the impact of your improvement on customers.
* **Implementation Date** (`implementation_date`, date, required): Set the date for the implementation of your improvement.
* **Implementation Time** (`implementation_time`, time, required): Set the time for the implementation of your improvement.
* **Implemented By** (`implemented_by`, text, required): Indicate who will implement the improvement.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select who is assigned to implement the improvement.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, required): Set the date for assignment.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your improvement.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, required): Set the due date for the improvement.
* **Followup Date** (`followup_date`, date, required): Set the follow-up date for the improvement.
* **Followup Time** (`followup_time`, time, required): Set the follow-up time for the improvement.
* **Followup By** (`followup_by`, text, required): Indicate who will follow-up on the improvement.
* **Notes Followup** (`notes_followup`, note, required): Enter any follow-up notes.
* **Followup Status** (`followup_status`, select_one, required): Select the follow-up status of your improvement.
