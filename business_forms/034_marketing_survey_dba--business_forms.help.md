# marketing_survey_dba - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect data about database administrators' (DBAs) work habits, preferences, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form consists of five questions, each designed to gather specific information about your work as a DBA.
2. Please read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
3. Enter your responses truthfully and accurately.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **What is your average work hours per week?** (required)
* **Label:** What is your average work hours per week?
* **Type:** number
* **Purpose:** This question is designed to understand your workload and schedule.

*   Please enter the average number of hours you work per week.

### 2. **What is your favorite database management system?** (optional)
* **Label:** What is your favorite database management system?
* **Type:** select_multiple
* **Purpose:** This question is designed to understand your preferences for DBMS.

*   Choose from the list of options provided:

     * MySQL
     * PostgreSQL
     * Microsoft SQL Server
     * Oracle

### 3. **How often do you attend training sessions or workshops?** (optional)
* **Label:** How often do you attend training sessions or workshops?
* **Type:** select_one
* **Purpose:** This question is designed to understand your commitment to professional development.

*   Choose one of the following options:

     * Daily
     * Weekly
     * Monthly
     * Rarely

### 4. **What is your preferred work time?** (optional)
* **Label:** What is your preferred work time?
* **Type:** time
* **Purpose:** This question is designed to understand your work schedule.

*   Enter your preferred work time in the HH:MM format (e.g., 08:00).

### 5. **Have you ever used cloud-based services for database management?** (optional)
* **Label:** Have you ever used cloud-based services for database management?
* **Type:** select_one
* **Purpose:** This question is designed to understand your experience with cloud-based services.

*   Choose from the following options:

     * Yes
     * No
