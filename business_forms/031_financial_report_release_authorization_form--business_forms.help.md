# Financial Report Release Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Report Release Authorization Form is used to obtain authorization from a user to release a financial report. This form ensures that the report is shared with the correct individual or department within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your email address in the **User Email** field. This will be used to identify you within the organization.
2. Choose a valid date in the **Authorization Date** field. This date should reflect when you are authorizing the release of the financial report.
3. Enter the ID of the financial document in the **Financial Document ID** field. This will help the system track the report.
4. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Consent Given**. If you are unsure, you can choose **No** and include a reason for your response in the **Reason for Request** field.
5. Select one or more departments from the **Department** field. This will determine who is authorized to receive the report.
6. Enter the name of the employee who has requested the report release in the **Employee Name** field.
7. Enter the job title of the employee who has requested the report release in the **Job Title** field.
8. Enter the name of the manager who has approved the report release in the **Manager Name** field.
9. Enter the title of the manager who has approved the report release in the **Manager Title** field.
10. Enter the email address of the manager who has approved the report release in the **Manager Email** field.
11. Enter the phone number of the manager who has approved the report release in the **Manager Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to identify you within the organization.
* **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, required: false): Choose a valid date for authorization.
* **Financial Document ID** (`financial_document_id`, number, required: false): Enter the ID of the financial document.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, required: false): Select **Yes** if you consent to release the report, or **No** if you do not.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, required: false): If you select **No** for **Consent Given**, enter a reason for your response.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more departments authorized to receive the report.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the employee who has requested the report release.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the job title of the employee who has requested the report release.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the manager who has approved the report release.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the manager who has approved the report release.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the manager who has approved the report release.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the manager who has approved the report release.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary authorization to request the release of the financial report.
* Double-check the information entered in this form for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the relevant department or contact person for assistance.
