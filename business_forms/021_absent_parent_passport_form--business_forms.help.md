# Absent Parent Passport Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Absent Parent Passport Form is designed to gather essential information from an individual (parent or guardian) who is submitting documents on behalf of their child. This form helps ensure that the necessary details are provided accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the personal details of the absent parent or guardian, including their name, phone number, email address, and current address.
2. Provide information about the child, including their full name, date of birth, place of birth, and gender.
3. Specify the relationship of the absent parent or guardian to the child.
4. Choose the correct reason why the other parent is absent or unable to consent.
5. If applicable, provide additional explanation for the reason of absence.
6. Indicate whether a court order granting sole custody or travel authority is available.
7. Declare that the information provided is true and correct to the best of your knowledge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name of parent or guardian** (`applicant_full_name`): Please enter the full name of the individual submitting this form.
* **Relationship to the child** (`applicant_relationship`): Choose how the parent or guardian is related to the child (Mother, Father, Legal guardian, or Other).
* **Phone number** (`applicant_phone`): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Email address** (`applicant_email`): Provide the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Current residential address** (`applicant_address`): Enter the current residential address of the parent or guardian.
* **Government-issued ID number** (`applicant_id_number`): Enter a government-issued ID number (Passport number, driver license, or national ID).
* **Full name of the child** (`child_full_name`): Enter the full legal name of the child as it should appear on the passport.
* **Child's date of birth** (`child_date_of_birth`): Enter the date of birth of the child.
* **Child's place of birth** (`child_place_of_birth`): Enter the city, state, and country where the child was born.
* **Child's gender** (`child_gender`): Choose the child's gender (Male or Female).
* **Reason for absence** (`reason_for_absence`): Choose the reason why the other parent is absent or unable to consent (Deceased, Sole custody granted by court, Unknown whereabouts, Incarcerated, or Other).
* **Additional explanation** (`absence_explanation`): If the reason for absence is "Other", provide additional explanation.
* **Court order availability** (`court_order_available`): Indicate whether a court order granting sole custody or travel authority is available.
* **Declaration agreement** (`declaration_agreement`): Declare that the information provided is true and correct to the best of your knowledge.
* **Date of submission** (`submission_date`): Enter the date the form is submitted.
