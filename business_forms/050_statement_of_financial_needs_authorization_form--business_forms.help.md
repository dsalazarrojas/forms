# Statement Of Financial Needs Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a person's financial needs and the level of authorization required to meet those needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form in the "Statement Of Financial Needs Authorization Form" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your financial needs in the "form description" field.
3. Enter the specific financial needs you are requesting in the "Financial Needs" field. This field is a numeric value.
4. Describe the resources needed to meet your financial needs in the "resource" field.
5. Select the appropriate authorization level (Low, Medium, or High) that aligns with your needs in the "Authorization Level" field.
6. If applicable, choose one or multiple authorized individuals (John, Jane, or Jim) in the "Authorized By" field.
7. Sign the form by providing your signature as requested in the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Statement Of Financial Needs Authorization Form** (`form_title`, text, no): Enter the title of the form in a few words.
* **form description** (`form_description`, text, no): Provide a brief description of your financial needs.
* **Financial Needs** (`financial_needs`, number, no): Enter a numeric value representing your financial needs.
* **resource** (`resource`, text, no): Describe the resources needed to meet your financial needs.
* **Authorization Level** (`authorization_level`, select_one, no): Select the authorization level that aligns with your needs (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Authorized By** (`authorized_by`, select_multiple, no): If applicable, choose one or multiple authorized individuals (John, Jane, or Jim).
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, no): Sign the form by providing your signature as requested.
* **Another Field** (`another_field`, text, no): This field is not required and can be used for additional information, but its purpose is not clearly defined. 

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible in the "Financial Needs" field.
* Select the correct authorization level based on your needs.
* Make sure to sign the form after completing all other fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, it is recommended to contact the responsible party for clarification.
