# Vendor Networking Mixer RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from vendors who plan to attend a networking mixer event. The form helps event organizers track RSVPs and gather essential details about the attendees. Please note that this form is not a registration form, and attendees are expected to already have an existing relationship or interest in attending the event. The form aims to provide valuable insights for event organizers to plan and prepare the event accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **RSVP Form** section with your name, as it will help identify you as an attendee.
2. In the **Attendee Details** section, provide your business information, such as your company name and contact details.
3. Specify your **Networking Goals** to help event organizers understand your objectives and tailor the event experience accordingly.
4. Mention any **Dietary Needs** if you have specific requirements or restrictions.
5. If you have any **Additional Info** or specific requests, feel free to share them in the provided text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RSVP Form**: This is your name, which will be used to identify you as an attendee.
* **Attendee Details**: Please provide your company name and contact details (e.g., email address, phone number) so that event organizers can get in touch with you.
* **Business Information**: Share a brief description of your business or your role within your company. This will help event organizers get a better understanding of your background and interests.
* **Networking Goals**: Specify your objectives for attending the event, such as seeking partnerships, learning about the industry, or simply expanding your professional network. This will help event organizers tailor the event experience to suit your needs.
* **Dietary Needs**: If you have any dietary restrictions or requirements (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free), please let event organizers know so that they can arrange for suitable food and beverage options.
* **Additional Info**: Use this text box to share any additional information or requests you may have, such as specific presentation topics, special equipment needs, or any other details that may impact the event planning.
