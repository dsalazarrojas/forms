# Payment Approval Hierarchy Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Approval Hierarchy Application Form is designed to track and manage payment approvals by capturing relevant details and submitting them for review at different levels of hierarchy. This form ensures that all necessary information is gathered and reviewed systematically, facilitating accurate and efficient payment approval decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the type of business for which you are requesting payment approval.
2.  Enter the name of the business making the request.
3.  Enter the email address of the person making the request.
4.  Choose the approver level for each step in the approval hierarchy.
5.  Select the payment method and terms applicable to the request.
6.  Enter the payment amount and payment frequency (if applicable).
7.  Specify the payment status and payment date.
8.  Choose the submitted by field for the person submitting the request.
9.  Add any additional comments or notes about the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of business for which you are requesting payment approval. This field is not mandatory.
* **Requestor Business Name** (`requestor_business_name`, select_one, optional): Enter the name of the business making the request.
* **Requestor Email** (`requestor_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person making the request.
* **Requestor Phone** (`requestor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person making the request.
* **Approver Level 1** (`approver_level_1`, select_one, optional): Choose the approver level for the first step in the approval hierarchy.
* **Approver Level 2** (`approver_level_2`, select_one, optional): Choose the approver level for the second step in the approval hierarchy.
* **Approver Level 3** (`approver_level_3`, select_one, optional): Choose the approver level for the third step in the approval hierarchy.
* **Approver Level 4** (`approver_level_4`, select_one, optional): Choose the approver level for the fourth step in the approval hierarchy.
* **Approver Level 5** (`approver_level_5`, select_one, optional): Choose the approver level for the fifth step in the approval hierarchy.
* **Approver Level 6** (`approver_level_6`, select_one, optional): Choose the approver level for the sixth step in the approval hierarchy.
* **Approver Level 7** (`approver_level_7`, select_one, optional): Choose the approver level for the seventh step in the approval hierarchy.
* **Approver Level 8** (`approver_level_8`, select_one, optional): Choose the approver level for the eighth step in the approval hierarchy.
* **Approver Level 9** (`approver_level_9`, select_one, optional): Choose the approver level for the ninth step in the approval hierarchy.
* **Approver Level 10** (`approver_level_10`, select_one, optional): Choose the approver level for the tenth step in the approval hierarchy.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or details about the request.
* **Request Amount** (`request_amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount for the request.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method applicable to the request.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_multiple, optional): Select the payment terms applicable to the request.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the payment frequency for the request.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the payment status of the request.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the payment date for the request.
* **Payment Date End** (`payment_date_end`, date, optional): Enter the end date for the payment request.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, optional): Enter the payment time for the request.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the request.
