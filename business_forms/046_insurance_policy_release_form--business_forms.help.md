# insurance_policy_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the release of an insurance policy. It captures information about the policyholder and the agent involved in the release process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the policyholder's information, including their policy number, policy date, and address.
2. Enter the agent's information, including their address.
3. Select the policy status (Active, Inactive, or Canceled).
4. Select whether the policyholder and agent have signed the policy release.
5. Enter any release notes or reason for the release.
6. Select the type of policy (Type A, Type B, or Type C).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policyholder Information** (`policy_number`, `title`, `policy_date`, `address`): Enter the policyholder's information, including their policy number, policy date, and address.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`): Enter the policy number of the insurance policy.
* **Policy Date** (`policy_date`): Enter the date when the policy was originally issued.
* **Agent Information** (`agent_info`): Enter the agent's name and address.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`): Enter the date when the policy was released.
* **Policy Status** (`policy_status`): Select the current status of the policy (Active, Inactive, or Canceled).
* **Policy Notes** (`policy_notes`): Enter any notes or comments about the policy release.
* **Policyholder Signature** (`policyholder_signature`): Indicate whether the policyholder has signed the policy release.
* **Agent Signature** (`agent_signature`): Indicate whether the agent has signed the policy release.
* **Release Reason** (`release_reason`): Enter the reason for the policy release.
* **Policyholder Address** (`policyholder_address`): Select the address where the policyholder can be contacted.
* **Agent Address** (`agent_address`): Select the address where the agent can be contacted.
* **Release Notes** (`release_notes`): Enter any notes or comments about the policy release.
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`): Select the type of policy (Type A, Type B, or Type C).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth release process.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, please contact the insurance company for clarification.
* This form is intended for internal use only and should not be shared with external parties.
