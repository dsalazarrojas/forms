# Net Profit Margin Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Net Profit Margin Calculator form is designed to calculate key financial metrics related to profit margins, helping business owners, accountants, or financial analysts evaluate the profitability of a company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the revenue generated by your business in the "Revenue" field.
2. Enter the cost of goods sold in the "Cost of Goods Sold" field.
3. Select the operating expenses relevant to your business from the "Operating Expenses" dropdown menu. You may also enter other operating expenses in the "Other Operating Expenses" field.
4. Calculate the gross profit by subtracting the cost of goods sold from the revenue.
5. Calculate the net profit by subtracting the operating expenses from the gross profit.
6. Enter the net profit margin percentage in the "Profit Margin Percentage" field.
7. Enter the operating expense percentage in the "Operating Expense Percentage" field.
8. The net profit margin percentage will be calculated automatically and displayed in the "Net Profit Margin Percentage" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Revenue** (`revenue`, `number`, required): The total revenue generated by your business, including sales revenue, service revenue, or any other revenue streams.
* **Cost of Goods Sold** (`cost_of_goods_sold`, `number`, required): The total cost of goods sold, including the cost of raw materials, labor, and other direct costs associated with producing and selling your products or services.
* **Operating Expenses** (`operating_expenses`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the operating expenses relevant to your business from the dropdown menu. Examples include rent, salaries, marketing expenses, etc.
* **Other Operating Expenses** (`operating_expense_other`, `number`, optional): Enter any other operating expenses not accounted for in the "Operating Expenses" dropdown menu.
* **Gross Profit** (`gross_profit`, `number`, required): The difference between revenue and cost of goods sold, calculated automatically.
* **Net Profit** (`net_profit`, `number`, required): The difference between gross profit and operating expenses, calculated automatically.
* **Profit Margin** (`profit_margin`, `number`, required): The profit margin, calculated automatically as the net profit divided by revenue.
* **Profit Margin Percentage** (`profit_margin_percentage`, `number`, required): The profit margin percentage, calculated automatically as the profit margin divided by revenue.
* **Net Profit Margin** (`net_profit_margin`, `number`, required): The net profit margin, calculated automatically as the net profit divided by revenue.
* **Margin Analysis** (`margin_analysis`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of margin analysis to perform, such as analyzing profit margin by product or department.
* **Operating Expense Percentage** (`operating_expense_percentage`, `number`, required): The percentage of operating expenses compared to revenue.
* **Net Profit Margin Percentage** (`net_profit_margin_percentage`, `number`, required): The net profit margin percentage, calculated automatically as the net profit divided by revenue.

## Tips
* Be careful when entering revenue and cost of goods sold, as these fields are required for accurate calculations.
* Select only the operating expenses relevant to your business to ensure accurate results.
* Margin analysis can be used to compare the profitability of different departments or products within your business.
* The operating expense percentage is calculated based on the operating expenses and revenue entered.
