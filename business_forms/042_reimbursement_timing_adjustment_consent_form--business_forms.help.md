# reimbursement_timing_adjustment_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employees to request a change in the timing of their reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Department in the second field.
3. Select the Date Of Change in the third field.
4. Enter the Timing Adjustment Amount in the fourth field.
5. Provide a clear Reason For Change in the fifth field.
6. Enter the Start Date of the new timing in the sixth field.
7. Enter the End Date of the new timing in the seventh field.
8. Sign the Manager Signature field with your manager's signature.
9. Sign the Employee Signature field with your own signature.
10. Sign the Department Head Signature field with the department head's signature.
11. Finally, select the Date in the eleventh field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
  - **Department** (Department, text, required): Enter your department as it appears in the company directory.
  - **Date Of Change** (Date Of Change, date, required): Select the date you are requesting the timing change to be effective from.
  - **Timing Adjustment Amount** (Timing Adjustment Amount, number, required): Enter the amount of the timing adjustment you are requesting.
  - **Reason For Change** (Reason For Change, text, required): Explain the reason for the timing adjustment, e.g., "Change in project scope" or "Client request".
  - **Start Date** (Start Date, date, required): Enter the new start date of the timing adjustment.
  - **End Date** (End Date, date, required): Enter the new end date of the timing adjustment.
  - **Manager Signature** (Manager Signature, text, required): Sign with your manager's signature.
  - **Employee Signature** (Employee Signature, text, required): Sign with your own signature.
  - **Department Head Signature** (Department Head Signature, text, required): Sign with the department head's signature.
  - **Date** (Date, date, required): Select the date you sign this form.
