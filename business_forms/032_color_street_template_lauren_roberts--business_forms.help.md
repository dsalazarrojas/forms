# Color Street Template - Lauren Roberts - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to place an order with Lauren Roberts for her Color Street Template.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
4. List the set names you want to order in the "List the set names you want to order" field.
5. Specify the total number of sets you want to order in the "Total number of sets" field.
6. Indicate if you have B3G1 status by selecting "True" or "False" in the "B3G1 applied?" field.
7. Select your payment method, either Venmo, PayPal, or Facebook Pay.
8. Enter your payment username in the "Payment Username" field.
9. Choose your delivery option, either Standard Shipping ($4.00), Priority Shipping ($8.50), or Lauren's Porch Pick-up (Free).
10. If this is for a party, enter the hostess's name in the "Hostess Name" field.
11. Indicate if you're interested in the Stylist opportunity by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Interested in the Stylist opportunity?" field.
12. Add any message for Lauren in the "Message for Lauren" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`lauren_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to confirm your identity.
- **Email** (`lauren_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to enable communication.
- **Shipping Address** (`lauren_shipping`, text, required): Provide your shipping address to ensure accurate delivery of your order.
- **List the set names you want to order** (`lauren_sets_list`, text, required): Type the names of the sets you want to purchase from Lauren's collection.
- **Total number of sets** (`lauren_total_sets`, number, required): Specify the total number of sets you want to order.
- **B3G1 applied?** (`lauren_b3g1_status`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have B3G1 status by selecting "True" or "False".
- **How will you pay?** (`lauren_payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method (Venmo, PayPal, or Facebook Pay).
- **Payment Username** (`lauren_payment_user`, text, required): Enter your payment username associated with your chosen payment method.
- **Delivery Choice** (`lauren_delivery`, select_one, required): Select your preferred delivery option (Standard Shipping ($4.00), Priority Shipping ($8.50), or Lauren's Porch Pick-up (Free)).
- **Hostess Name** (`lauren_host_credit`, text, optional): If this is for a party, enter the hostess's name to acknowledge their credit.
- **Interested in the Stylist opportunity?** (`lauren_business_interest`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you're interested in the Stylist opportunity by selecting "True" or "False".
- **Message for Lauren** (`lauren_comments`, text, optional): Add any message or comment for Lauren in this field.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Double-check your payment method and delivery choice to avoid any issues with order fulfillment.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact Lauren directly.
