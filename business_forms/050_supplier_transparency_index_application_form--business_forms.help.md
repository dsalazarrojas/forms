# Supplier Transparency Index Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Supplier Transparency Index Application Form is designed to collect information from suppliers about their transparency and ethics. The form is used to assess the supplier's commitment to transparency and ethics in their business practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide basic supplier information (Name, Contact Information, etc.)
2. Answer ethics-related questions
3. Select certifications and other relevant credentials
4. Enter date of birth (if applicable)
5. Enter contact information (email address)
6. Add any additional comments or notes (if necessary)
7. Review and submit the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Information** (`supplier_info`, text, Optional): This field is for entering the supplier's basic information such as name and any other relevant details.
* **Ethics Info 1** (`ethics_info_1`, text, Optional): This field is for providing more information about the supplier's ethics. Please provide additional details about their ethics practices.
* **Ethics Info 2** (`ethics_info_2`, text, Optional): This field is for providing more information about the supplier's ethics. Please provide additional details about their ethics practices. 
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, select_one, Optional): Select one of the available certifications that the supplier has obtained.
* **Certifications 2** (`certifications_2`, select_multiple, Optional): Select multiple certifications that the supplier has obtained.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Optional): Enter the supplier's date of birth (if applicable).
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, Optional): Enter the supplier's contact information, such as phone number or address.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the supplier.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Supplier Comments** (`supplier_comments`, note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the supplier.

**Tips**

- Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
- Be as detailed as possible when providing information about the supplier's ethics practices.
- Select relevant certifications that the supplier has obtained.
- Enter correct date of birth (if applicable).
- Review and proofread the form before submitting it.
