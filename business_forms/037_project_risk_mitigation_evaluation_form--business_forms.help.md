# Project Risk Mitigation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Risk Mitigation Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess and mitigate potential risks associated with projects. It helps project managers, stakeholders, and team members identify, evaluate, and prioritize risks, and develop strategies to minimize their impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the project category from the dropdown menu.
2. Assess the risk level of the project.
3. Describe the potential risk.
4. Enter the risk probability and impact.
5. Develop a mitigation strategy.
6. Rate the risk and determine your acceptance of it.
7. Specify the risk action plan and items.
8. Select the project status.
9. Enter the review date and details.
10. Identify the reviewer and their contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Category** (`project_category`): Select the category of the project (Project, Program, Portfolio, or Portfolio and Program).
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`): Choose the level of risk from the options (Low, Moderate, High).
* **Risk Description** (`risk_description`): Enter a brief description of the potential risk.
* **Risk Probability** (`risk_probability`): Enter a number between 0 and 100 indicating the likelihood of the risk occurring.
* **Risk Impact** (`risk_impact`): Enter a number between 0 and 100 indicating the potential impact of the risk if it occurs.
* **Mitigation Strategy** (`mitigation_strategy`): Describe your approach to mitigate the risk.
* **Risk Rating** (`risk_rating`): Rate the risk from the options (Very Low, Low, Medium, High).
* **Risk Acceptance** (`risk_acceptance`): Indicate whether you accept or do not accept the risk.
* **Risk Action Plan** (`risk_action_plan`): Enter a detailed plan to address the risk.
* **Risk Action Items** (`risk_action_items`): Select whether specific actions are taken to address the risk.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`): Select the current status of the project (Active, Completed).
* **Review Date** (`review_date`): Enter the date of the last review.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`): Enter the email of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`reviewer_phone`): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Select who the risk is assigned to (Chatjimmy or Other).
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`): Select the status of the review (Open, In Progress, Closed).
* **Review Type** (`review_type`): Select the type of review (Formal, Informal).
* **Risk Type** (`risk_type`): Select the type of risk (Technical, Operational).
* **Priority** (`priority`): Select the priority of the risk (Low, Medium, High).
* **Risk Category** (`risk_category`): Select the category of the risk (High, Medium, Low).
* **Risk Frequency** (`risk_frequency`): Select the frequency of the risk (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Risk Last Update** (`risk_last_update`): Enter the date of the last update.
