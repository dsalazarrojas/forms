# Resource Management Training Acknowledgement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a simple way for users to acknowledge their participation in Resource Management Training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Session Id field with the unique identifier for the session you attended.
2. Enter the Date you attended the session in the Date field.
3. Briefly describe the Topic covered during the session in the Topic field.
4. Enter your Name in the Name field.
5. Provide your digital or handwritten signature in the Signature field.
6. Select your role from the list of available options (CEO, Manager, Member) in the Role field.
7. In the Feedback field, provide any additional comments, questions, or concerns related to the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Id** (session_id): A unique identifier for the session you attended.
* **Date** (date): The date you attended the session.
* **Topic** (topic): Briefly describe the main topic covered during the session.
* **Name** (name): Enter your name.
* **Signature** (signature): Provide your digital or handwritten signature.
* **Role** (role): Select your role from the list of available options (CEO, Manager, Member).
* **Feedback** (feedback): Provide any additional comments, questions, or concerns related to the session.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Provide a clear and concise feedback in the Feedback field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to form@gic.mx.
