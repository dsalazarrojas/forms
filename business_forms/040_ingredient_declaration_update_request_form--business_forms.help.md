# Ingredient Declaration Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect updates on ingredients that were previously declared. This form helps the submitter to provide detailed information about the changes made to the ingredient declaration, which will then be reviewed by the relevant authorities. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. Provide accurate information.
4. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the updated ingredient declaration?** (`updated_ingredient`, text, required): This field is used to provide an updated declaration of the ingredient. Please enter the new or updated information about the ingredient.
* **Why was the previous declaration rejected?** (`reason_for_rejection`, text, required): This field is used to provide the reason why the previous declaration of the ingredient was rejected. Please enter the reason for the rejection.
* **What are the updated details?** (`updated_details`, text, required): This field is used to provide additional details about the updated ingredient declaration. Please enter the new or updated information about the ingredient.
* **Contact details of the submitter** (`submitter_details`, text, required): This field is used to provide contact information of the person submitting the form. Please enter your name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Review status?** (`review_status`, select_one, required): Please select the status of the review: Approved, Rejected, or Pending.
* **Review comments?** (`review_comments`, text, required): This field is used to provide any comments from the review.
* **Submitter's notes** (`submitter_notes`, text, required): This field is used to provide any additional notes from the submitter.
* **Review date?** (`review_date`, date, required): This field is used to enter the review date.
* **Submitter's email?** (`submitter_email`, text, required): This field is used to enter the submitter's email address.
* **Submitter's phone number?** (`submitter_phone`, text, required): This field is used to enter the submitter's phone number.
* **Comments from the submitter?** (`submitter_comments`, text, required): This field is used to provide any comments from the submitter.
* **Repeat review status?** (`review_status_repeated`, select_one, required=False): This field is optional and is used to repeat the review status if needed. Please select the status of the review: Approved, Rejected, or Pending.
* **Review Date Repeated** (`review_date_repeated`, date, required=False): This field is optional and is used to repeat the review date if needed.
* **Submitter's notes repeated?** (`submitter_notes_repeated`, text, required=False): This field is optional and is used to provide any repeated notes from the submitter.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* Provide accurate and clear information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant authorities before submitting the form.
