# Business Continuity Audit Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
<thinking>This form is designed to collect information about your organization's business continuity audit needs. It will help us understand your organization's size, industry, continuity plan, and specific requirements to provide you with personalized audit consulting services. Please answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly, and we will use the information to tailor our services to meet your needs.</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, answering all the questions in the required fields.
- For select_one and select_multiple fields, select only one or multiple options that apply to your organization.
- For text fields, provide detailed information in the space provided.
- Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`Organization Name`, `text`, required): Enter the official name of your organization.
* **Industry** (`Industry`, `select_one`, required): Select the industry sector your organization operates in (e.g., Technology, Finance, etc.).
* **Organization Size** (`Organization Size`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of employees in your organization (e.g., 1-50, 51-200, etc.).
* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Position** (`Position`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Do You Have a Continuity Plan?** (`Current Continuity Plan`, `select_one`, required): Select your current plan status (e.g., Yes - Comprehensive, In Progress, etc.).
* **Previous Continuity Audits** (`Previous Audits`, `select_one`, required): Select how recently you have had a continuity audit (e.g., Within 1 Year, Never, etc.).
* **Audit Scope** (`Audit Scope`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the specific areas of the audit you are interested in (e.g., Full Continuity Plan, Risk Assessment, etc.).
* **Audit Objectives** (`Audit Objectives`, `text`, required): Briefly describe what you hope to achieve with the audit.
* **Compliance Requirements** (`Compliance Requirements`, `select_one`, required): Select whether your organization has strict or voluntary compliance requirements.
* **Specific Standards** (`Specific Standards`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any specific standards that apply to your organization (e.g., ISO 22301, HIPAA, etc.).
* **Current Pain Points** (`Pain Points`, `text`, optional): Describe any concerns or pain points you are currently experiencing.
* **Timeline** (`Timeline`, `select_one`, required): Select when you need the audit (e.g., Immediately, Within 1 Month, etc.).
* **Budget Range** (`Budget Range`, `select_one`, optional): Select your expected investment range (e.g., Under $10,000, More than $100,000, etc.).
* **Key Stakeholders** (`Stakeholders`, `text`, optional): List key stakeholders involved in the audit process.
* **Decision Timeline** (`Decision Timeline`, `select_one`, required): Select when you expect to make a decision about the audit (e.g., Within 1 Week, More than 1 Month, etc.).
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`Referral Source`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you heard about our services (e.g., Website, Referral, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all required fields.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask us for clarification.
- Provide detailed and accurate information to ensure we can provide you with the best possible services.
