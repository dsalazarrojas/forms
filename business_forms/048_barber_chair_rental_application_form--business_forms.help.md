# Barber Chair Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your experience, qualifications, and commitment to our shop's standards and policies in order to assess your eligibility for renting a chair in our barbershop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out all required fields and answer all questions truthfully. The form has two main sections: Personal Information and Business Information. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`application_date`, Date, Required): This is the date of your application.
* **Applicant First Name** (`applicant_first_name`, Text, Required): Your first name.
* **Applicant Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`, Text, Required): Your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Your date of birth.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Required): Your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Required): Your contact phone number.
* **Barber License Number** (`barber_license_number`, Text, Required): Your barber license ID number.
* **License State** (`license_state`, Text, Required): The state where your license is issued.
* **License Expiration Date** (`license_expiration`, Date, Required): The date your license expires.
* **Years of Barbering Experience** (`years_experience`, Number, Required): How many years you have been cutting hair.
* **Specializations or Skills** (`specializations`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the skills you possess, such as Fade Cuts, Beard Trimming, etc.
* **Previous Shop Experience** (`previous_shop_experience`, Text, Optional): Where you have worked in the past.
* **Professional References Available** (`references_available`, Select One, Required): If you have references, check the box to indicate they are available.
* **Preferred Rental Duration** (`rental_duration`, Select One, Required): How long you would like to rent a chair.
* **Expected Days Per Week** (`days_per_week`, Number, Required): How many days per week you will work.
* **Expected Hours Per Day** (`hours_per_day`, Number, Optional): Average hours you will work per day.
* **Do You Have Clientele Base?** (`clientele_base`, Select One, Required): If you bring your own clients or need to build a base.
* **Liability Insurance** (`liability_insurance`, Select One, Required): If you have personal insurance or not.
* **Background Check Authorization** (`background_check`, Select One, Required): Consent to a background check.
* **Commitment to Shop Standards** (`cleanliness_commitment`, Select One, Required): Will you follow shop cleanliness standards.
* **Conflict Resolution Willingness** (`conflict_resolution`, Select One, Required): Willingness to follow shop policies.
* **Previous Rent or Legal Issues** (`previous_rent_issues`, Select One, Required): If you have had any issues in the past.
* **Issue Description (if applicable)** (`issue_description`, Text, Required): Explain if there were issues.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, Text, Optional): Anything else we should know.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely, as this will help us assess your qualifications and experience. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
