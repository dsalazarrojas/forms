# email_drafting_guide_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The email drafting guide form is a tool to help users create a professional email.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the email you want to draft, such as the recipient's name and email address.
2. Outline the steps you want to include in your email, if any.
3. Write the content of the email, including the main message or points you want to convey.
4. If needed, specify the email subject and send email button.
5. Add the email body where you can type the email content.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info**: A text field to input general information about the email, such as the recipient's name and email address.
* **Email Drafting Steps**: A text field to outline the steps you want to include in your email.
* **Email Content**: A text field to write the content of the email, including the main message or points you want to convey.
* **Send Email**: A text field to specify the email subject and send the email.
* **Email Subject**: A text field to input the email subject.
* **Email Body**: A text field to type the email content.

## Tips

* Be clear and concise in your email content.
* Use proper grammar and spelling.
* Proofread your email for errors before sending.
* Customize the form according to your specific needs.
* Use a professional tone and language in your email content.
* Keep the email content relevant to the recipient and purpose of the email.
