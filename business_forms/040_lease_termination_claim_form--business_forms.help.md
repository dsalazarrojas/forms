# Lease Termination Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lease Termination Claim Form is used to submit a claim for lease termination. It provides a structured way to present your case and gather the necessary information to process your claim.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Lease Termination Claim Form**: This is the main form page. Please provide a clear and concise description of the reason for your claim.
2. **Lease Start Date**: Please enter the date when the lease started.
3. **Lease End Date**: Please enter the date when the lease ended.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lease Termination Claim Form** (`lease_termination_claim_form`):
	+ This field is a text area where you can provide a detailed description of the reason for your claim.
	+ Please be as specific as possible, including relevant dates, details, and supporting evidence.
* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`):
	+ This field is a date field where you enter the date when the lease started.
	+ Please use the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY) to ensure accurate recording.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`):
	+ This field is a date field where you enter the date when the lease ended.
	+ Please use the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY) to ensure accurate recording.

## Tips
- Double-check the information you provide to ensure it is accurate and complete.
- If you have any supporting evidence or documents, please attach them separately and reference them in the form.
- If you are unsure about any aspect of the form or process, contact us for assistance.
