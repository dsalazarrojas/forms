# Business Launch Prep School Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Launch Prep School Application form is designed to gather information about your business idea, experience, and commitment to the program. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly to ensure a smooth evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully and answer all required questions.
- Provide clear and concise responses to each question, and avoid using jargon or overly technical terms.
- Make sure to save your progress as you fill out the form, as you will not be able to go back and edit your responses after submission.
- Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Applicant Profile
• **Full Name** (`blps_applicant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
• **Current Employment Status** (`current_employment_status_blps`, select_one, required): Select your current employment status, which can be:
	+ Full-time Employed
	+ Part-time Employed
	+ Self-employed
	+ Student
	+ Unemployed

### Business Concept
• **Business Concept Summary** (`business_concept_summary_blps`, text, required): Briefly describe your business idea, explaining what your business will do.
• **Target Audience** (`target_audience_blps_desc`, text, required): Identify your ideal customers and describe who they are.
• **Why Are You Ready to Launch Now?** (`readiness_statement_blps`, text, required): Explain why this is the right time for your business to launch.

### Commitment to Program
• **Commitment to Program Hours** (`commitment_to_program_hours_check`, select_one, required): Confirm your ability to commit at least 10 hours per week to the program:
	+ Yes, I can commit
	+ No, I have limited time

### Experience and Goals
• **Previous Business Experience** (`previous_business_experience_level`, select_one, required): Select your level of business experience:
	+ None (First business)
	+ Some (Side projects/freelance)
	+ Experienced (Previous founder)
• **Goals for the Program** (`blps_program_goals_selection`, select_multiple, required): Select all the goals that apply to your business:
	+ Product Validation
	+ Business Planning
	+ Financial Literacy
	+ Marketing Strategy
	+ Networking / Mentorship

### Additional Information
• **How Did You Hear About Business Launch Prep School?** (`referral_source_blps_application`, text, optional): Share how you heard about the program.
• **Date of Application** (`application_submission_date_blps`, date, required): Enter the date you submit your application.

### Terms and Conditions
• **I Agree to the Program Terms and Conditions** (`blps_terms_agreement_check`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the program's terms and conditions:
	+ I Agree
	+ Other

Please note that this is a sample form and you should adjust the content and structure based on your specific needs and requirements.
