# Product Certification Proof Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request certification proof for a product. The purpose of this form is to gather information from the applicant about the product, including its certification types, uploaded documents, and the applicant's signature. The form is used by [internal team] to evaluate the product's certification status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your details in the Applicant Details section.
2. Provide information about the product in the Product Information section.
3. Select the relevant certification types from the Certification Types dropdown menu.
4. Upload any supporting documents for the product in the Uploaded Documents field.
5. Choose your signature from the Applicant Signature dropdown menu.
6. Select the current status of the product's certification in the Product Certification Status field (multiple selection allowed).
