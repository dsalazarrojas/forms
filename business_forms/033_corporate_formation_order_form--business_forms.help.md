# Corporate Formation Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Formation Order Form is used to collect information from corporate clients who wish to form a new company. This form helps in processing the formation of the new company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required".
2. Select the correct business structure and type from the options provided.
3. Enter the details of the business, including its name, address, and contact person.
4. Add information about the owners of the company, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
5. Specify the number of owners and select the preferred payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Structure Select** (`Business Structure Select`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct business structure for your company (LLC, Corporation).
* **Business Name** (`Business Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of your business.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, `text`, required): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Business Type** (`Business Type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the type of business you wish to form (Sole-Proprietorship, Partnership, Corporation).
* **Number of Owners** (`Number of Owners`, `number`, required): Enter the number of owners.
* **Owner 1 Name** (`Owner 1 Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the first owner.
* **Owner 1 Address** (`Owner 1 Address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the first owner.
* **Owner 1 Phone** (`Owner 1 Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the first owner.
* **Owner 1 Email** (`Owner 1 Email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the first owner.
* **Owner 2 Name** (`Owner 2 Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the second owner (if applicable).
* **Owner 2 Address** (`Owner 2 Address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the second owner (if applicable).
* **Owner 2 Phone** (`Owner 2 Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the second owner (if applicable).
* **Owner 2 Email** (`Owner 2 Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the second owner (if applicable).
* **Owner 3 Name** (`Owner 3 Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the third owner (if applicable).
* **Owner 3 Address** (`Owner 3 Address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the third owner (if applicable).
* **Owner 3 Phone** (`Owner 3 Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the third owner (if applicable).
* **Owner 3 Email** (`Owner 3 Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the third owner (if applicable).
* **Formation Fees** (`Formation Fees`, `number`, required): Enter the formation fees for the company.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the preferred payment method (Bank Transfer, Online Payment).
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required): Click the submit button to complete the form and process the company formation.
