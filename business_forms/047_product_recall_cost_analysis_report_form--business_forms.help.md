# Product Recall Cost Analysis Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Recall Cost Analysis Report Form is designed to collect data and information related to product recalls, helping you to understand the financial and operational impact of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by providing product recall information on **page 1**, including any relevant details about the recall event.
- Move on to **page 2** to report estimated costs associated with the recall, including financial data such as revenue loss, replacement costs, and any other relevant expenses.
- Finally, on **page 3**, describe the analysis and results of the recall, including what was done to mitigate the effects of the recall and what actions were taken to prevent similar future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1: Product Recall Information**:
	+ **Product Recall Information** (`product_recall_information`, text, required): Provide detailed information about the product recall, including the product name, description, and any other relevant details.
* **Page 2: Costs**:
	+ **Costs** (`costs`, text, required): Report estimated costs associated with the recall, including revenue loss, replacement costs, and any other expenses incurred during the recall.
* **Page 3: Recall Analysis**:
	+ **Recall Analysis** (`recall_analysis`, text, required): Describe the analysis and results of the recall, including actions taken to mitigate the effects of the recall and what was done to prevent similar future events.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately to provide a clear picture of the product recall event.
- Use specific numbers and examples whenever possible to support your analysis.
