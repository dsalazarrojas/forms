# Consultancy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consultancy Registration Form is a comprehensive form used to gather essential details about your consultancy business for our records and to facilitate communication with potential clients.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the required fields, such as Business Name, Business Type, Business Registration Number, and others marked as required.
- Ensure you fill out all sections completely before submission, using the provided options or inputting your own values if not provided.
- Be sure to select the correct industry for your consultancy services, and choose the best category to describe your expertise.
- You may need to refer to your business documents or previous records if you're unsure about any information.
- Double-check your answers before submitting your application to ensure accuracy and correctness.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the legal name of your consulting business.
* **2. Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Select your business structure from the options provided (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Limited Liability Company, Corporation, Nonprofit, or Other).
* **3. Business Registration Number** (`business_registration_number`, text, required): Enter your official registration or tax ID number.
* **4. Year Established** (`year_established`, number, optional): If your business was not yet established, you can leave this field blank or select a future year.
* **5. Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of your primary contact person.
* **6. Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of your primary contact person.
* **7. Contact Position** (`contact_position`, text, required): Enter the job title of your primary contact person.
* **8. Business Email** (`business_email`, email, required): Enter your business email address for communication.
* **9. Business Phone Number** (`business_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary business contact number.
* **10. Business Street Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter your business's physical address.
* **11. City** (`business_city`, text, required): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **12. State or Province** (`business_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where your business is located.
* **13. ZIP or Postal Code** (`business_zip`, text, required): Enter your business postal code.
* **14. Country** (`business_country`, text, required): Enter the country where your business is located.
* **15. Business Website** (`business_website`, text, optional): If your business has a website, enter the URL here.
* **16. Consulting Specialization** (`consulting_specialization`, select_one, required): Select your primary area of consulting expertise from the options provided.
* **17. Services Offered** (`services_offered`, select_multiple, required): Select all services your consultancy provides from the options given.
* **18. Target Industries** (`target_industries`, select_multiple, required): Select the industries your consultancy primarily serves from the options provided.
* **19. Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, select_one, optional): Enter the total number of employees in your business.
* **20. Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, optional): Select your business's annual revenue range from the options provided.
* **21. Professional Certifications** (`certifications`, text, optional): List any relevant professional certifications you hold.
* **22. Insurance Coverage** (`insurance_coverage`, select_one, optional): Select if you have professional liability insurance.
* **23. Can You Provide References** (`references_available`, select_one, optional): Select if you can provide client references.
* **24. Business Description** (`business_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your consultancy business.
* **25. Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this registration submission.

## Tips
- Be as accurate and precise as possible in filling out this form.
- Double-check your submissions before submitting to avoid errors.
- You can save your progress and return later if needed.
- If you need help, please contact our support team.
