# Sponsor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsor Application Form is a tool used to gather information from potential sponsors about their event, project, or activity. It helps the sponsor to provide accurate and relevant details to support their application for sponsorship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Sponsor Info section with your personal or organization details.
2. Provide contact information in the Contact Info section.
3. Describe your event or project in the Event Info section.
4. Specify the sponsor requirements you are seeking.
5. Review and accept the Sponsorship Terms.
6. Add any Additional Info or Notes related to your application.
7. Finally, submit the form to proceed with your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Info** (`sponsor_info`, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of your organization as the sponsor.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including email, phone number, and address.
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, text, optional): Describe your event, project, or activity in detail.
* **Sponsor Requirements** (`sponsor_requirements`, text, optional): Specify the requirements you are seeking from the sponsor.
* **Sponsorship Terms** (`sponsorship_terms`, text, optional): Review and accept the Sponsorship Terms before proceeding.
* **Sponsorship Details** (`sponsorship_details`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the sponsorship you are seeking.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any extra information that supports your application.
* **Event Dates** (`event_dates`, text, optional): Enter the dates of your event or project.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, text, optional): Enter the time of your event or project.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event or project (Football, Baseball, Golf, Tennis, or Other).
* **Sponsorship Type** (`sponsorship_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of sponsorship you are seeking (Title Sponsor, Event Sponsor, Co-Sponsor, Supporting Sponsor, or Other).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city name.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state name.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country name.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your website URL.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your sponsor.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your event or project.
* **Event Description** (`event_desc`, text, optional): Describe your event or project in detail.
* **Sponsor Contact** (`sponsor_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of your sponsor.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click the submit button to complete your application.

Note: Fields marked as optional are not required to be filled out.
