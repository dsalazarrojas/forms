# Business Expo Mentor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Expo Mentor Registration Form is a crucial part of the business expo's mentoring program. It is designed to gather information about your personal and professional background, areas of expertise, and availability and preferences for mentoring.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your personal information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Describe your professional background, including your company name, job title, and years of experience.
3. Choose your areas of expertise, such as Business Strategy, Finance and Accounting, Marketing and Sales, and more.
4. Indicate your mentoring experience, including whether you have mentored before and what type of mentoring you are interested in.
5. Select your preferred mentoring format, including in-person, virtual, or both.
6. Specify your availability, including the expo date and hours you are available.
7. Choose your preferred session length and the maximum number of mentees you can handle.
8. Finally, confirm that you agree to mentor at the expo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`id: 2`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`id: 3`): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`id: 4`): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 5`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (`id: 7`): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Job Title** (`id: 8`): Enter your position in the company.
* **Industry** (`id: 9`): Enter your industry sector.
* **Years of Experience** (`id: 10`): Enter your professional experience in years.
* **Areas of Expertise** (`id: 11`): Select all the areas where you have expertise, such as Business Strategy, Finance and Accounting, and more.
* **Mentoring Background** (`id: 12`): Indicate whether you have mentored before and what type of mentoring you are interested in.
* **Mentoring Interests** (`id: 14`): Select all the types of mentoring you are interested in, such as Startup Founders, Business Growth, and more.
* **Preferred Mentee Profile** (`id: 15`): Enter the type of mentee you prefer to mentor.
* **Expo Date** (`id: 17`): Enter the date of the expo.
* **Available Hours** (`id: 18`): Enter the hours you are available for mentoring.
* **Preferred Session Length** (`id: 19`): Select the length of your preferred mentoring session.
* **Maximum Number of Mentees** (`id: 20`): Enter the maximum number of mentees you can handle.
* **Mentoring Format Preference** (`id: 21`): Select your preferred mentoring format, including in-person, virtual, or both.
* **Agreement** (`id: 22`): Confirm that you agree to mentor at the expo.

## Tips
Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and truthful. This form is only for mentors who are committed to participating in the business expo's mentoring program.
