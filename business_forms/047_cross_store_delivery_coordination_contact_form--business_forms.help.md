# Cross Store Delivery Coordination Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate effective cross-store delivery coordination between stores and customers. It allows customers to request delivery of items from a specific store, providing necessary details for smooth delivery execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the store where you would like to request delivery from.
2. Provide a brief message to describe your delivery request, including any specific details or instructions.
3. Select the date and time you would like to receive your delivery.
4. Enter your preferred delivery location.
5. Add any additional comments or messages for our delivery team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store** (`customer`, `select_one`): Select the store where you would like to request delivery from. This will be the store that will fulfill your delivery request.
* **Delivery Request** (`delivery_request`, `text`): Briefly describe the items you would like to be delivered, including any specific details or instructions.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`): Select the date you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`): Select the time you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, `text`): Enter the specific location where you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, `text`): Add any additional comments or messages for our delivery team.

Note: Fields marked as `optional` can be left blank, but providing information will improve the effectiveness of your delivery request.
