# Product Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Inventory Information Record Form is a tool for businesses to collect and store product data, including product details, quantity, location, status, and other metadata. This form is used to create, update, and manage product records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields (none of the fields are required based on the provided YAML, but this might be due to the design not reflecting the actual business logic).
2. Enter the product ID (unique identifier for the product).
3. Enter the product description to provide a brief summary of the product.
4. Specify the product location where it is stored or available.
5. Choose the product status from the provided options (e.g., in stock, out of stock, etc.).
6. Enter the product price.
7. Set the product reorder level, which might be used for restocking or reordering purposes.
8. Select the product category and type (if applicable).
9. Enter the product name (if not already included in the description).
10. Enter the product image and alt text (for image descriptions).
11. Add any additional notes about the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product ID** (1): A unique identifier for the product. Enter the product's ID number.
- **quantity** (2): The current quantity of the product in stock.
- **Product Description** (3): A brief summary of the product. Describe the product in a few words.
- **Product Location** (4): The location where the product is stored or available.
- **Product Status** (5): The current status of the product. Choose from the provided options.
- **Product Price** (6): The price of the product.
- **Product Reorder Level** (7): The level at which the product should be reordered or restocked.
- **Created By** (8): The person or team responsible for creating the product record.
- **Updated By** (9): The person or team that last updated the product record.
- **Updated At** (10): The date and time the product record was last updated.
- **Updated Time** (11): The time the product record was last updated.
- **Product Name** (12): The name of the product.
- **Product Category** (13): The category of the product (if applicable).
- **Product Type** (14): The type of the product (if applicable).
- **Created At** (15): The date the product record was created.
- **Created By** (16): The person or team responsible for creating the product record.
- **Product Image** (17): A text field for the product image URL or path.
- **Product Image Alt Text** (18): A text field for the alt text of the product image.
- **Notes** (19): Any additional notes or comments about the product.
- **Product Status** (20): The status of the product (select multiple options if applicable).
