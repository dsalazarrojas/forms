# Regional Manager Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and recommendations from regional managers on their regional performance, goals, and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please answer the questions on each page as thoroughly as possible.
* Review your responses before submitting to ensure accuracy and consistency.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on a particular field, please contact the form administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Regional Manager Recommendation Form 1 Label** (`regional_manager_recommendation_form_1_label`, optional): This field is the title of the form, which serves as a header for the form. Please enter your regional team name or a brief description of your region.
* **Regional Manager Recommendation Form 2 Label** (`regional_manager_recommendation_form_2_label`, optional): This field is not applicable for this form as it doesn't exist in the pages provided.
* **Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the current date in the format YYYY-MM-DD or MM-DD-YYYY, depending on your preference.
 
  Note: If you're unsure about the date format, please refer to your regional calendar or system of records for guidance.
