# lease_agreement_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and share key information about a lease agreement, typically for a landlord or property owner to provide to a tenant or renter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out each section with the requested information.
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete details for each field.
* Review and submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord Info (1)**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Rent Info (2)**: Provide details about the rent amount, payment frequency, and any other rent-related information.
* **Tenant Info (3)**: Enter the tenant's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Lease Terms (4)**: Describe the terms of the lease, including any specific rules or restrictions.
* **Lease Dates (5)**: Enter the start and end dates of the lease, as well as any renewal or termination dates.
* **Property Details (6)**: Provide information about the property, including its address, size, and any other relevant details.
* **Lease Agreement (7)**: Enter the main terms and conditions of the lease, including any addendums or attachments.

## Tips
* Make sure to proofread your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, try to provide the best information available, but don't hesitate to ask for clarification if needed.
* The information collected on this form will be shared with the tenant or renter, so be sure to keep it accurate and clear.
