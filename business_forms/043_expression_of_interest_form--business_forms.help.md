# Expression Of Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to gather information for a job expression of interest for potential employment opportunities at our company. It is intended for individuals who wish to be considered for a position and are interested in submitting an expression of interest. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
3. Describe your educational background in the "Educational Background" field.
4. Enter your job title and category in the "Job Title and Category" field.
5. Select your job status from the available options in the "Job Status" field.
6. Enter your level of experience (if applicable) in the "Job Level" field.
7. Choose your job type from the available options in the "Job Type" field.
8. Enter your job location in the "Job Location" field.
9. Enter the start and end dates of your employment in the "Job Start and End Dates" field.
10. Provide an update about the status of your job search in the "Job Status Update" field.
11. Describe the reason for your job status update in the "Job Status Reason" field.
12. Attach your resume for review in the "Resume" field.
13. Attach a cover letter in the "Cover Letter" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name in this field.
* **Contact Information** (2, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
* **Educational Background** (3, text, required): Describe your educational background, including your degree, institution, and relevant courses.
* **Job Title and Category** (4, text, required): Enter your job title and category.
* **Job Status** (5, select_one, required): Choose from one of the following job status options: option e, option f, option g, or option h.
* **Job Level** (6, number, required): Enter your level of experience. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Job Type** (7, select_one, required): Choose from one of the following job type options: option i, option j, or option k, or option l.
* **Job Location** (8, text, required): Enter your job location.
* **Job Start and End Dates** (9, text, required): Enter the start and end dates of your job.
* **Job Status Update** (10, text, required): Provide an update about your job status.
* **Job Status Reason** (11, text, required): Describe the reason for your job status update.
* **Resume** (12, text, required): Attach your resume for review.
* **Cover Letter** (13, text, required): Attach a cover letter for review.

# Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Please attach your resume and cover letter in the required format.
* Ensure that your resume and cover letter are relevant to your expression of interest.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact us.
* The information gathered in this form will be used for the purpose of considering your expression of interest for potential employment opportunities.
