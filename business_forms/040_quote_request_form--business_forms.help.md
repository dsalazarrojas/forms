# Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quote Request Form is used to collect information from customers or clients requesting a quote for a service or product. This form will help us better understand their needs and provide an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact name.
2. Provide your email address.
3. Enter your contact phone number.
4. Select the type of quote you are requesting.
5. Describe your quote request.
6. Choose a date for the quote request.
7. Choose a time for the quote request.
8. Enter the name of the person or company requesting the quote.
9. Enter the phone number of the person or company requesting the quote.
10. Enter the email of the person or company requesting the quote.
11. Enter the address of the person or company requesting the quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quote Request Form** (`quote_request_form`, text, optional)
  * This is the main input field for your quote request. Please provide a brief description of your needs.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional)
  * Please enter your name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional)
  * Enter your business email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional)
  * Enter your business phone number.
* **Quote for** (`quote_for`, select_multiple, optional)
  * Choose the type of quote you are requesting. This field will be populated with options such as "Quote for Service A", "Quote for Service B", etc.
* **Quote Description** (`quote_description`, text, optional)
  * Provide a detailed description of your quote request.
* **Quote Request Date** (`quote_request_date`, date, optional)
  * Choose the date you would like to have the quote completed by.
* **Quote Request Time** (`quote_request_time`, time, optional)
  * Choose the time you would like to have the quote completed.
* **Quote Requester Name** (`quote_requester`, text, optional)
  * Please enter the name of the person or company requesting the quote.
* **Quote Requester Phone** (`quote_requester_phone`, text, optional)
  * Enter the phone number of the person or company requesting the quote.
* **Quote Requester Email** (`quote_requester_email`, email, optional)
  * Enter the email of the person or company requesting the quote.
* **Quote Requester Address** (`quote_requester_address`, text, optional)
  * Enter the address of the person or company requesting the quote.

Note: The optional fields are required only if you want to provide more information to facilitate the quote process.
