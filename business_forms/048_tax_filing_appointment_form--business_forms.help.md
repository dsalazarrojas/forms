### # Tax Filing Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tax Filing Appointment Form is designed to schedule appointments for tax filing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's name.
2. Enter the appointment date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. If scheduling an appointment, select the schedule type (Individual or Business).
4. Enter the tax professional's name and phone number.
5. Select the tax filing type (Income Tax, Sales Tax, Business Tax, or State Tax).
6. Choose the payment method (Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Draft).
7. Set the appointment status (Scheduled, Rescheduled, or Canceled).
8. Enter the appointment ID.
9. Enter the client's email address.
10. Select the tax year.
11. Enter the meeting duration (optional).
12. Select the meeting frequency (Once, Recurring, Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
13. Select the appointment type (In-Office, Phone, or Video Conference).
14. Set the report status (Draft, Completed, or Pending).
15. Enter the report due date (optional).
16. Select the meeting type (Consultation, Interview, or Meeting).
17. Set the meeting status (Pending, Completed, or Canceled).
18. Select the report frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
19. Select the appointment frequency (Once, Recurring, Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
