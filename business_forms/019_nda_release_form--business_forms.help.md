# NDA Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NDA Release Form is a critical document that formalizes the release of confidentiality and non-disclosure agreements between the company and its partners or investors. It is essential to complete this form accurately and honestly to ensure the company's sensitive information remains secure.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide Company Information**: Enter the company's name and address in the "Company Name" and "Address" fields, respectively.
2. **Specify Project Details**: Enter the project name and select the relevant partner or investor type in the "Project Name" and "Partner/Investor" fields.
3. **Provide Contact Information**: Enter your contact details, including your name, email address, phone number, and date and time in the "Phone", "Email", "Date", and "Time" fields.
4. **Sign the Form**: Sign the form with your signature to acknowledge your commitment to upholding the company's confidentiality and non-disclosure policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the company.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project you are involved in.
* **Partner/Investor** (`partner_investor`, select_one, required): Select whether you are a partner or investor.
* **Address** (`address`, text, not required): Enter your company's address.
* **City** (`city`, text, not required): Enter the city where your company is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, not required): Enter the state where your company is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your company's zip code.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you are signing the form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you are signing the form.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your involvement with the company.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign the form with your signature to acknowledge your commitment to upholding the company's confidentiality and non-disclosure policies.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, not required): Enter the tool you are assigned to work with.
* **Category** (`category`, text, not required): Enter the category of the project.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, not required): Enter the user assigned to work with.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, not required): Enter the ID of the form.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, not required): Enter the output file.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, not required): Enter the version of the form.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, not required): Enter the person who created the form.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, text, not required): Enter the date and time the form was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, text, not required): Enter the date and time the form was updated.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, text, not required): Enter the date and time the form was deleted.
* **Deleted By** (`deleted_by`, text, not required): Enter the person who deleted the form.
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes.

Note: All fields with "not required" labels should be filled in only if necessary or relevant to your situation.
