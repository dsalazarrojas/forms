# RPA Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RPA Questionnaire is designed to collect information on an organization's Robotic Process Automation (RPA) initiatives. This form helps organizations gather data on various aspects of RPA, including the type of automation used, the process being automated, and the challenges and solutions faced.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and identify the questions that apply to your organization's RPA initiatives.
2. Select the relevant answers for each question, taking into account the provided options.
3. Provide detailed answers to questions with text fields.
4. Ensure that the answers are accurate and concise.
