# Service Directory Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who want to submit a service to the Service Directory. It helps the directory team understand the details of the service, including contact information, service description, and any relevant additional information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Submission Form section with a brief description of your service.
2.  Provide your contact information in the Contact Form section.
3.  Add any service details in the Service Details section.
4.  If you have any additional information, you can include it in the Additional Information section.
5.  Select whether you agree with the terms and conditions in the Agreement section.
6.  Finally, fill in the Review Details section with any relevant information about your service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Form** (Submission Form, text, required): This is the main section where you'll provide a brief description of your service.
* **Contact Form** (Contact Form, text, optional): Please fill in your name, email, and phone number so we can contact you about your submission.
* **Service Details** (Service Details, text, optional): Describe the details of your service, including its features and benefits.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, optional): Press this button to submit your form, and make sure to check your email for confirmation.
* **Review Form** (Review Form, text, optional): This section is for you to provide any additional information about your service that you think would be helpful for others to know.
* **Additional Information** (Additional Information, text, optional): If you have any extra details or information that you think would be relevant, you can include it here.
* **Agreement** (Agreement, select multiple, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you agree to the terms and conditions of the Service Directory.
* **Review Details** (Review Details, text, optional): This section is for you to provide any additional information about your service that you think would be helpful for reviewers to know.
* **Submission Status** (Submission Status, text, optional): This field will be filled in automatically after your submission has been reviewed.
