# Coaching Questionnaire AmandaV - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coaching Questionnaire AmandaV is designed to help you and your coach understand your coaching needs and preferences. It's a comprehensive form that will guide the coaching process and ensure that you get the most out of your coaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. For select-one questions, choose the option that best describes your situation.
3. For text fields, provide a brief but detailed explanation.
4. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by "required: true").
5. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask your coach for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): This is your legal full name.
* **2. Email Address** (`email`, email, required): This is your primary contact email address.
* **3. Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): This is your contact phone number.
* **4. Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): This is the name of your organization (if applicable).
* **5. Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): This is your position.
* **6. Industry** (`industry`, text, optional): This is the industry you work in.
* **7. Years in Current Role** (`years_in_current_role`, select_one, required): This question asks about your experience in your current position.
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 3-5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ Over 10 years
* **8. Total Work Experience** (`total_work_experience`, select_one, required): This question asks about your total professional experience.
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 3-5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ Over 10 years
* **9. Area of Coaching Interest** (`coaching_interest`, select_one, required): This question asks about the type of coaching you're interested in.
	+ Leadership Development
	+ Career Transition
	+ Work-Life Balance
	+ Performance Improvement
	+ Communication Skills
	+ Confidence Building
	+ Strategic Thinking
	+ Other
* **10. Primary Challenge You Face** (`primary_challenge`, text, required): This is the biggest challenge you're currently facing.
* **11. Coaching Goals** (`coaching_goals`, text, required): This is what you want to achieve through coaching.
* **12. Ideal Outcome from Coaching** (`ideal_outcome`, text, required): This is what you consider a successful outcome from coaching.
* **13. Current Stress Level** (`current_stress_level`, select_one, required): This question asks about your current stress level.
	+ Low
	+ Moderate
	+ High
	+ Very High
* **14. Work-Life Balance Satisfaction** (`work_life_balance`, select_one, required): This question asks about your satisfaction with your work-life balance.
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **15. How Would You Describe Your Leadership Style** (`leadership_style`, text, optional): This is a brief description of your leadership approach.
* **16. Size of Team You Lead** (`team_size`, select_one, optional): This question asks about the number of direct reports you have.
	+ No Direct Reports
	+ 1-5 People
	+ 6-10 People
	+ 11-20 People
	+ Over 20 People
* **17. Management Challenges** (`management_challenges`, text, optional): This is a list of challenges you face in managing your team.
* **18. Communication Strengths** (`communication_strengths`, text, optional): This is a list of your communication skills and strengths.
* **19. Communication Weaknesses** (`communication_weaknesses`, text, optional): This is a list of areas where you'd like to improve your communication skills.
* **20. Decision-Making Style** (`decision_making_style`, select_one, optional): This question asks about your decision-making approach.
	+ Quick and Decisive
	+ Analytical and Deliberate
	+ Collaborative
	+ Intuitive
	+ Depends on Situation
* **21. How Do You Receive Feedback** (`feedback_reception`, text, optional): This is your relationship with feedback.
* **22. What Motivates You** (`motivation_sources`, text, required): This is what drives you to achieve your goals.
* **23. Core Personal Values** (`values`, text, required): This is a list of your core values that guide your decisions and actions.
* **24. Preferred Learning Style** (`learning_style`, select_one, optional): This question asks about how you learn best.
	+ Visual
	+ Auditory
	+ Reading and Writing
	+ Kinesthetic
	+ Mixed
* **25. Previous Professional Development** (`previous_development`, text, optional): This is a description of any past training or coaching you've received.
* **26. Time Available for Coaching** (`time_availability`, select_one, required): This question asks about your available time for coaching.
	+ Less than 2 hours per week
	+ 2-5 hours per week
	+ 5-10 hours per week
	+ Over 10 hours per week
* **27. Preferred Session Format** (`preferred_session_format`, select_one, required): This question asks about your preferred way to meet for coaching sessions.
	+ In Person
	+ Video Call
	+ Phone Call
	+ Hybrid
* **28. Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): This is any additional information you'd like to share with your coach.

# Tips

* Please answer all required fields (indicated by "required: true").
* Take your time to answer each question carefully.
* If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask your coach for clarification.
* This form is intended to be a starting point for your coaching journey, and your coach will work with you to tailor your coaching experience to your needs.
