# Process Reengineering Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Reengineering Consultation Request Form is designed to gather information about your project objectives, challenges, and preferred meeting schedule. This form will help us understand your needs and facilitate a consultation to provide guidance and support for your project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details (page 1).
2. Describe your project's objectives (page 1).
3. Share the challenges you are facing with your project (page 1).
4. Select your preferred meeting time (page 1).
5. Choose your preferred meeting date (page 1).
6. Review and agree to the submission agreement (page 1).

* **Objectives** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false)

* **Challenges** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false)

* **Preferred Meeting Time** (`<name>`, `time`, required: false)
	+ Select a suitable time slot for our consultation, taking into account your availability.
* **Preferred Meeting Date** (`<name>`, `date`, required: false)
	+ Choose a date and time for our consultation, keeping in mind your schedule.

	+ Review and agree to our submission agreement, which ensures we can work together effectively.
