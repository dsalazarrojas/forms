# Internship Sponsor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide information about the internship the applicant is applying for and their relevant skills. It is used by the internship program to assess the suitability of the applicant for the internship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the relevant skills that you have for the internship.
3. Choose your preferred communication method for the internship.
4. Provide the required information about the internship start and end dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter the name of the company or person sponsoring the internship.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where the sponsor can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where the sponsor can contact you.
* **Internship Start Date**: Enter the date when the internship is expected to start.
* **Internship End Date**: Enter the date when the internship is expected to end.
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job that you are applying for.
* **Job Description**: Briefly describe the job and your role in the internship.
* **Required Skills**: Select all relevant skills that you have for the internship. Please choose from the options:
	+ Internship Sponsor Application Form
	+ Skill 1
	+ Skill 2
	+ Skill 3
	+ Skill 4
* **Preferred Communication Method**: Choose how you would like to communicate with the sponsor. Please select one of the following:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ In-person
* **Sponsor Location**: Enter the location where the sponsor is based.
* **Sponsor Type**: Enter the type of sponsor (e.g. company, individual).
* **Sponsor Experience**: Enter the number of years of experience you have with the sponsor.
* **Job Opening ID**: Enter the ID number of the job opening.
* **Sponsor Name**: Select the name of the sponsor from the options:
	+ Sponsor 1
	+ Sponsor 2
	+ Sponsor 3
* **Sponsor Company**: Enter the name of the company that the sponsor represents.
* **Sponsor Website**: Enter the website of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Logo**: Enter the logo of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Email**: Enter the email of the sponsor.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in all fields.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact us.
* This form should only be filled out once by each applicant, and not duplicated or submitted multiple times.
