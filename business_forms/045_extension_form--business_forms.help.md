# Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Extension Form is designed to collect information for extensions related to business operations. This form is intended for users to provide details about an extension, which is likely used for business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by selecting the relevant page (business_form) and inputting the required information for the business extension.
2. Proceed to the next page and enter the title of the extension (extension_form) in the text box.
3. Finally, select the date of extension (extension_date) from the calendar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **extension_form** (`extension_form`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of the extension in this field. This is the primary identifier for the extension.
* **business_form** (`business_form`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the business information in this field, which is used to categorize and track the extension.
* **extension_date** (`extension_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of extension from the calendar, which is essential for scheduling and record-keeping purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure that the title of the extension is unique to avoid duplicate submissions.
* Verify that the business information is accurate to ensure correct categorization and tracking of the extension.
* Date of extension should be a valid date to prevent errors in scheduling and record-keeping.
