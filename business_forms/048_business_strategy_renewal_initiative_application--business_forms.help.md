# Business Strategy Renewal Initiative Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Strategy Renewal Initiative Application form is designed to collect information from applicants regarding their initiative proposals. Each field is crafted to gather essential details about the proposal, ensuring a comprehensive evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal information, which includes your name, title or position, company name, email address, and contact phone number.
2.  Provide a detailed description of your initiative, including its title, description, and the business area it will affect.
3.  Specify the expected duration of the initiative, selecting one of the provided timeframes.
4.  Outline the primary objectives and the measurable outcomes of the initiative.
5.  Detail the expected impact, including financial or operational benefits.
6.  Explain how this initiative aligns with the company's strategy.
7.  Describe your plan to implement the initiative, including key milestones and resource requirements.
8.  Identify any potential risks and outline strategies for mitigating them.
9.  Lastly, consider any additional information you think would be beneficial for the evaluation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Your full name.
* **Title or Position** (`applicant_title`, `text`, required): Your role in the organization.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Your organization's name.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, `text`, required): Your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, `text`, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Initiative Title** (`initiative_title`, `text`, required): Name of the renewal initiative.
* **Initiative Description** (`initiative_description`, `text`, required): What is this initiative about.
* **Business Area Affected** (`business_area`, `text`, required): Which part of the business this initiative will affect.
* **Expected Initiative Duration** (`initiative_duration`, `select_one`, required): Timeline for completion.
* **Primary Objectives** (`primary_objectives`, `text`, required): Main goals of the initiative.
* **Measurable Outcomes** (`measurable_outcomes`, `text`, required): How will success be measured.
* **Expected Impact** (`expected_impact`, `text`, required): Financial or operational benefits.
* **Alignment with Company Strategy** (`strategic_alignment`, `text`, required): How does this support the company's strategy.
* **Implementation Plan** (`implementation_approach`, `text`, required): How will this be executed.
* **Key Milestones** (`key_milestones`, `text`, required): Important phases or checkpoints.
* **Resource Requirements** (`resource_requirements`, `text`, required): Budget, people, tools needed.
* **Critical Success Factors** (`critical_success_factors`, `text`, required): What must go right for success.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Any other relevant details.
