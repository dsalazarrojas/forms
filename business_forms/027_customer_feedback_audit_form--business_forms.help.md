# Customer Feedback Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer feedback and audit customer experience. It helps to gather data about a customer's satisfaction level, category of the issue, and any additional comments they may have. This information is valuable for improving services and products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the issue or concern you're providing feedback on.
2. Choose your level of satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 5.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
4. Enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
5. Enter the details of the auditor who conducted the audit, including their name, company, and position.
6. Select the date the feedback was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Feedback Audit (1)
* **Customer Feedback Audit** (`customer_feedback_audit`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to provide any feedback or comments about your experience.

### Category (2)
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the category of the issue or concern you're providing feedback on. You can choose from the following options:
		- Business Forms

### Customer Satisfaction Level (3)
* **Customer Satisfaction Level** (`customer_satisfaction_level`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose your level of satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 5.

### Experience Level (4)
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose your level of satisfaction with the experience:
		- Bad
		- Average
		- Good
		- Excellent

### Feedback (5)
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.

### Comments (6)
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or feedback you have.

### Customer Name (7)
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name.

### Customer Email (8)
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address.

### Customer Phone (9)
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number.

### Customer Company (10)
* **Customer Company** (`customer_company`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of your company.

### Customer Position (11)
* **Customer Position** (`customer_position`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your position or role.

### Customer Department (12)
* **Customer Department** (`customer_department`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of your department.

### Auditor Name (13)
* **Auditor Name** (`auditor_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the auditor who conducted the audit.

### Auditor Company (14)
* **Auditor Company** (`auditor_company`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the auditor's company.

### Auditor Position (15)
* **Auditor Position** (`auditor_position`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the position of the auditor.

### Auditor Department (16)
* **Auditor Department** (`auditor_department`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the auditor's department.

### Auditor Email (17)
* **Auditor Email** (`auditor_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email address of the auditor.

### Auditor Phone (18)
* **Auditor Phone** (`auditor_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the auditor.

### Submitted (19)
* **Submitted** (`submitted_at`, date, optional):
	+ Select the date the feedback was submitted.
