# workforce_productivity_consulting_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The workforce productivity consulting contact form is designed to gather information from users who want to contact the consulting service to discuss their productivity challenges and schedule a follow-up meeting or call.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your email address in the Email field.
2. Enter your name in the Name field.
3. Provide your phone number in the Phone field.
4. Type in the name of your organization in the Organization field.
5. Describe your productivity challenges in the Challenge Description field.
6. Select a date for the follow-up meeting or call using the Date field.
7. Choose a suitable time for the follow-up meeting or call using the Scheduling a follow-up field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email** (Email, Email, Required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Name** (Name, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Organization** (Organization, Text, Required): Type the name of your company or organization.
* **Challenge Description** (Productivity Challenges, Note, Required): Describe your current productivity challenges, e.g., managing a team, workflow optimization, time management, etc.
* **Date** (Date, Date, Required): Select a date for the follow-up meeting or call.
* **Scheduling a follow-up** (Scheduling a follow-up, Time, Required): Choose a time slot for the follow-up meeting or call.
