# Third Party Vendor Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for granting access to third-party vendors, allowing them to access certain tools and resources within our organization. Fill out this form to request access for a vendor, specifying their contact information, access level, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that the vendor's access is correctly granted.
2. Select the correct access level and contact details for the vendor.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments that may be relevant for the vendor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Third Party Vendor Access Form 1 (Page 1)**: This is the first page of the form, where you will enter the vendor's contact details.
* **Contact Details**: 
  + <label> Vendor Manager, Vendor Contact, Account Owner</label> (`select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more of these roles to indicate who the vendor's contact information is for.
* **Access Level**:
  + <label> Level 1, Level 2, Level 3</label> (`select_one`, required): Choose the appropriate access level for the vendor.
* **Access Expiration Date**:
  + <label> Access Expiration Date</label> (`date`, required): Enter the date when the vendor's access is set to expire, if applicable.
* **Access Expiration Time**:
  + <label> Access Expiration Time</label> (`time`, required): Enter the time when the vendor's access is set to expire, if applicable.
* **Notes**: 
  + <label> Notes</label> (`note`, required): Enter any additional comments or information for the vendor.
* **Vendor Email**:
  + <label> Vendor Email</label> (`email`, required): Enter the vendor's email address.
* **Vendor Phone**:
  + <label> Vendor Phone</label> (`text`, required): Enter the vendor's phone number.
* **Vendor Address**:
  + <label> Vendor Address</label> (`text`, required): Enter the vendor's address.
* **Assigned Tool**:
  + <label> chatjimmy, Other</label> (`select_one`, required): Choose the tool assigned to the vendor.
* **Notes for Assigned Tool**:
  + <label> Notes for Assigned Tool</label> (`note`, required): Enter any additional comments or information for the assigned tool.
* **Vendor Type**:
  + <label> Vendor Type</label> (`text`, required): Enter the type of vendor.
* **Vendor Status**:
  + <label> Active, Inactive, Pending</label> (`select_one`, required): Choose the status of the vendor.
* **Vendor Contact Details (Page 2)**: This is the second page of the form, where you will enter more details about the vendor.
* **Vendor Type**: 
  + <label> Vendor Type</label> (`text`, required): Enter the type of vendor.
* **Vendor Status**:
  + <label> Active, Inactive, Pending</label> (`select_one`, required): Choose the status of the vendor.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have the necessary information and permissions before filling out this form.
* Be accurate and clear in your input to avoid any potential issues.
* If you're unsure about any field or option, please reach out to your administrator for guidance.
