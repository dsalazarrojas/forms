# Restaurant Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Food Order Form is designed to collect order details from customers, including food name, quantity, and payment information. This form is used to facilitate the ordering process and store customer information for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the order details, including food name, quantity, and price per unit.
2. Enter the customer's information, including name, phone number, email, and address.
3. Select the payment method and enter the receipt number.
4. Calculate the subtotal, tax, and total amount due.
5. Set the form status as Open or Closed.
6. Fill in the submission date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details**: Enter the food order details such as food name, quantity, and price per unit.
* **Ordered Items**: Enter the items ordered by the customer.
* **Food Name**: Enter the name of the food item.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the ordered food item.
* **Price per Unit**: Enter the price per unit of the food item.
* **Total Amount**: Calculate the total amount due.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the order.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the order.
* **Payment Details**: Enter the payment details, including method and receipt number.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method (Credit Card, Cash, Bank Transfer).
* **Receipt Number**: Enter the receipt number.
* **Subtotal**: Calculate the subtotal of the order.
* **Tax**: Enter the tax amount.
* **Total Amount Due**: Calculate the total amount due.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's address.
* **Customer Order Notes**: Enter any notes about the order.
* **Customer Signature**: Enter the customer's signature.
* **Staff Name**: Enter the staff member's name.
* **Staff Email**: Enter the staff member's email address.
* **Staff Phone**: Enter the staff member's phone number.
* **Staff Signature**: Enter the staff member's signature.
* **Total**: Calculate the total amount due.
* **Tax Rate**: Enter the tax rate.
* **Payment Status**: Set the payment status (Pending, Paid).
* **Form Status**: Set the form status (Open, Closed).
* **Submission Date**: Enter the submission date.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the submission time.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Double-check calculations for correct totals.
* Set the form status to Open or Closed as necessary.
* Store customer information for future reference.
