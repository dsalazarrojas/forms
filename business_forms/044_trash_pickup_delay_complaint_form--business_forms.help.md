# Trash Pickup Delay Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Trash Pickup Delay Complaint Form" is a tool used to report any delays or issues with trash pickup services. Please use this form to report any incidents that have caused inconvenience or disruption to your trash collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name as per the "User Name" field.
2. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
3. If you have an email address, enter it in the "Email Address" field. This is optional.
4. Select the type(s) of trash affected from the list provided in the "Type Of Trash Affected" field.
5. Enter the date the delay occurred using the "Delay Date" field.
6. Choose the duration of the delay using the "Delay Time" field.
7. Describe the situation in detail using the "Description" field.
8. If you want to provide any additional comments, use the "Submitter Comments" field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (User Name, text, required)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like to be contacted.
* **Contact Number (Contact Number, number, required)**: Enter your contact number to ensure our team can reach out to you for further information.
* **Email Address (Email Address, email, optional)**: If you have an email address, please enter it so we can contact you via this method if needed.
* **Type Of Trash Affected (Type Of Trash Affected, select multiple, required)**: Select all the types of trash affected by the delay. You can choose more than one option.
* **Delay Date (Delay Date, date, required)**: Enter the date the delay occurred in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Delay Time (Delay Time, time, required)**: Choose the duration of the delay using the format HH:MM.
* **Description (Description, text, required)**: Describe the situation in detail, including any relevant information about the delay.
* **Submitter Comments (Submitter Comments, text, optional)**: If you want to provide any additional comments or information, please use this field.
