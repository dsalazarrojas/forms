# Audit Review Session Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audit Review Session Application Form is used to gather information for the review of an audit. This form helps ensure that all relevant details are captured and communicated accurately. Please make sure that this form is filled out correctly to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out the form, ensure that you have all necessary information and documents ready. Here are the steps to complete the form:

*   Fill in your full name and email address as the applicant.
*   Enter your job title and department.
*   Select the date the application is submitted.
*   Enter the name and period of the audit to be reviewed.
*   Choose the type of review you are requesting.
*   Enter the objectives of the review.
*   Specify your preferred dates for the review.
*   Choose the expected duration of the review.
*   List the participants and materials for the review.
*   Enter any special requirements if applicable.
*   Choose your preferred review format (e.g., in-person meeting, video conference, etc.).
*   Select the required technology for the review.
*   Enter the expected outcomes of the review.
*   If follow-up is required, indicate this.
*   Confirm if your manager has approved this request.
*   Sign the form with your full name as electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Applicant Full Name**: Your legal name as the applicant.
*   **Email Address**: Your contact email address.
*   **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
*   **Department**: Your department or team you belong to.
*   **Job Title**: Your current job title.
*   **Application Date**: Date when you submitted this application.
*   **Audit Name**: Name of the audit to be reviewed.
*   **Audit Period**: Period covered by the audit.
*   **Review Type**: Type of review requested (e.g., Preliminary, Final, Quality, etc.).
*   **Review Objectives**: Objectives of this review.
*   **Preferred Review Dates**: Date and time you would like the review to take place.
*   **Review Duration**: Expected duration of the review (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, half-day, full day, etc.).
*   **Participants**: List all who will participate in the review.
*   **Materials to Review**: List all materials to be reviewed (e.g., audit report, working papers, etc.).
*   **Special Requirements**: If any special requirements are needed for the review (e.g., specific software, equipment, etc.).
*   **Review Format**: Preferred format for the review (e.g., in-person meeting, video conference, etc.).
*   **Technology Needs**: List the required technology for the review (e.g., projector, screen sharing, etc.).
*   **Expected Outcomes**: Expected outcomes from the review.
*   **Follow-Up Required**: If follow-up is required after the review.
*   **Manager Approval**: Confirmation if your manager has approved this request.
*   **Applicant Signature**: Electronic signature of the applicant.
