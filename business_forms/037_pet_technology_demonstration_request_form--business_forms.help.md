# Pet Technology Demonstration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in pet technology demonstrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your relevant details. Please make sure to provide valid information for each field to ensure a smooth demo experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use for correspondence.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title or position.
* **Interested in Demo**: Select the type of demo you are interested in (Demo 1, Demo 2, or Demo 3).
* **Demo Preferences**: Enter any specific preferences or requirements you have for the demo experience.
* **Demo Date**: Select a date for the demo (optional, but recommended for scheduling purposes).
