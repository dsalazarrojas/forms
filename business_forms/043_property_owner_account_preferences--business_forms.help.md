# Property Owner Account Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide helps property owners customize their account settings to receive updates and notifications from their Homeowners Association (HOA). Property owners can set their preferred contact methods and other details to ensure they stay informed about their property-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each field carefully to understand the purpose and requirements of each section.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. Save your changes before moving to the next field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Contact Details** (`owner_contact_details`, text, optional): This field is for the property owner to provide their contact information, such as their full name and contact number.
* **Primary Contact Method** (`primary_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted by the HOA. Select one of the options: Email, Phone, or Mail.
* **Owner Communication Preferences** (`owner_communication_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to be communicated with by the HOA. Select multiple options from Email, Mail, or Phone.
* **Emergency Contact Details** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, optional): If you want someone else to be contacted in case of an emergency, fill in this field with their name and contact information.
* **Owner Address** (`owner_address`, text, optional): Enter your property address to help the HOA keep your contact information up-to-date.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive notifications and updates from the HOA.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to receive calls or text messages from the HOA.
* **Owner Note** (`owner_note`, note, optional): This field is for any additional information or notes you would like to share with the HOA.
* **HOA Communication Preferences** (`hoa_communication_preferences`, select_one, optional): If you would like to receive emails from the HOA, select `True`. If you would like to opt-out, select `False`.
* **Property ID** (`property_id`, number, optional): This field is not currently used and is left empty.
