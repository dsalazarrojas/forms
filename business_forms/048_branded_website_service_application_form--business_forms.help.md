# Branded Website Service Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your business and website needs. It's essential to provide as much information as possible to ensure we can deliver the best possible solution for your business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your client and business profile information (questions 1-4).
2. Select the primary goals for your new website (question 7).
3. Describe your target audience (question 8).
4. Choose your preferred design aesthetic (question 9).
5. Select your preferred CMS platform (question 11).
6. Specify the approximate number of pages required (question 12).
7. Choose the key features required for your website (question 13).
8. Set a target launch date and estimated budget for your project (questions 15 and 16).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`Client Name`, `text`, `true`): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Business Name** (`Business Name`, `text`, `true`): Enter your company or business name as it appears on public records.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, `true`): Enter your primary email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Current Website URL** (`Current Website URL`, `text`, `false`): If you have an existing website, enter its URL.
* **What are the Primary Goals for your new website?** (`Select all that apply`, `select_multiple`, `true`): Choose the primary goals for your new website from the following options:
	+ Sell Products (E-commerce)
	+ Lead Generation
	+ Brand Awareness
	+ Portfolio Showcase
	+ Information Hub
* **Describe your target audience** (`Describe your target audience`, `text`, `true`): Provide a brief description of your target audience.
* **Preferred Design Aesthetic** (`Select one`, `select_one`, `true`): Choose your preferred design aesthetic from the following options:
	+ Minimalist / Modern
	+ Bold / Colorful
	+ Professional / Corporate
	+ Creative / Artistic
* **Do you have a preferred CMS platform?** (`Select one`, `select_one`, `true`): Choose your preferred Content Management System (CMS) platform:
	+ WordPress
	+ Shopify
	+ Webflow
	+ Custom Development
	+ No Preference
* **Approximate number of pages required** (`Approximate number of pages required`, `number`, `true`): Enter the number of pages you expect your website to have.
* **Key Features Required** (`Select all that apply`, `select_multiple`, `true`): Choose the key features you require for your website from the following options:
	+ Contact Form
	+ Blog Section
	+ Online Booking
	+ User Accounts
	+ Social Media Integration
* **Target Launch Date** (`Target Launch Date`, `date`, `true`): Set a target launch date for your website project.
* **Estimated Budget for Website Project** (`Estimated Budget for Website Project`, `number`, `true`): Enter your estimated budget for the project in your local currency.
