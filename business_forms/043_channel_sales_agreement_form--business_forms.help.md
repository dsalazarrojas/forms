# Channel Sales Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Channel Sales Agreement Form is used to document the terms and conditions of a sales agreement between a channel partner and a vendor or manufacturer.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Read each field's description and explanation carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Review the form thoroughly before submitting it for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Number** (`agreement_number`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the agreement.
* **Vendor or Manufacturer Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required): Enter the company offering the product or service.
* **Vendor Contact Information** (`vendor_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact details for the vendor or manufacturer.
* **Channel Partner Company Name** (`channel_partner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the reseller or distributor.
* **Partner Contact Information** (`partner_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact details for the channel partner.
* **Type of Channel Partner** (`partner_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of channel partner (Reseller, Distributor, System Integrator, VAR, Agent, or Other).
* **Territory or Region** (`territory`, text, optional): Enter the geographic area covered by the partnership.
* **Products or Services Covered** (`products_services`, text, required): Enter the products or services being sold through the partnership.
* **Discount Structure** (`discount_structure`, text, required): Enter the discount structure for the partnership.
* **Partner Margin Percentage** (`margin_percentage`, number, optional): Enter the markup allowed to the partner.
* **Minimum Purchase Commitment** (`minimum_purchase_commitment`, number, optional): Enter the annual minimum or quota.
* **Support Requirements** (`support_requirements`, text, optional): Enter the support requirements for the partner.
* **Agreement Term Length** (`term_length`, select_one, required): Choose the length of the agreement (1 Year, 2 Years, 3 Years, or Indefinite).
* **Renewal Terms** (`renewal_terms`, text, optional): Enter the terms for renewing the agreement.
* **Termination or Exit Clause** (`termination_clause`, text, optional): Enter the terms for terminating the agreement.
* **Pricing Conditions** (`pricing_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any price guarantees or conditions.
* **Intellectual Property Rights** (`intellectual_property`, text, optional): Enter the ownership of IP rights.
* **Exclusive Arrangement** (`exclusive_arrangement`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the arrangement is exclusive, non-exclusive, or partially exclusive.
* **Both Parties Accept This Agreement** (`agreement_acceptance`, select_one, required): Confirm that both parties accept the agreement terms.
