# Architecture Planning Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients and users for architecture planning appointments. It is intended to be filled out by clients or users who are interested in discussing their project plans with our team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your client information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide details about your project, including its type and a brief description.
3. Choose your preferred appointment format (in-person, video call, or phone call).
4. Select a preferred appointment date and time.
5. If you have any additional information or questions, please let us know in the "Questions or Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Please enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your appointment.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your appointment.
* **Company or Organization**: If applicable, please enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Project Type**: Select the type of project you are interested in discussing (e.g. Residential, Commercial, etc.).
* **Brief Project Description**: Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Preferred Appointment Format**: Choose how you would like to conduct your appointment (in-person, video call, or phone call).
* **First Preferred Date**: Enter your preferred date for the appointment.
* **Preferred Time for First Date**: Enter your preferred time for the appointment.
* **Second Preferred Date**: Enter your second preferred date for the appointment (in case the first one is not available).
* **Preferred Time for Second Date**: Enter your preferred time for the second appointment date.
* **Meeting Location (if in-person)**: If you have chosen to meet in-person, please enter the location where you would like to meet.
* **Questions or Additional Notes**: If you have any questions or additional information to share, please let us know here.
* **Best Time to Contact You**: If you are not available at any specific time, please let us know so we can plan accordingly.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form as completely as possible to ensure we have all the necessary information to properly plan your appointment.
* If you have any specific requirements or requests, please let us know in the "Questions or Additional Notes" field.
* We will do our best to accommodate your preferred appointment dates and times, but please be flexible if we are unable to meet your exact preferences.
