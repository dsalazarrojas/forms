# Climate Resiliency Project Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register your climate resiliency project with us. Please fill out this form to provide us with necessary information about your project, including organization details, project goals, location, and implementation plan.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organization's information, including name, type, and contact person details.
2. Provide a detailed description of your climate resiliency project, including its goals and expected outcomes.
3. Specify the type of project you are undertaking and the climate hazards it addresses.
4. Select the geographic scale of your project's location and the population it targets.
5. Estimate the number of beneficiaries and the budget allocated for the project.
6. Define your project's implementation timeline, duration, and readiness.
7. Outline the expected resilience improvements and the key metrics for measuring success.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, required, true): Enter your organization's name, which will be associated with this project.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, required, true): Select the type of organization you represent (e.g., Government Agency, Non-Profit, etc.).
* **Primary Contact Person** (`contact_person`, required, true): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your organization.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, required, true): Enter the email address of your primary contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, required, true): Enter the phone number of your primary contact person.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, required, true): Enter a descriptive name for your climate resiliency project.
* **Detailed Project Description** (`project_description`, required, true): Describe your project in detail, including its goals and expected outcomes.
* **Type of Resiliency Project** (`project_type`, required, true): Select the type of project you are undertaking (e.g., Infrastructure Hardening, Ecosystem Restoration, etc.).
* **Primary Climate Hazards Addressed** (`climate_hazards`, required, true): Select all climate hazards addressed by your project (e.g., Flooding, Wildfire, etc.).
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, required, true): Specify the location of your project (e.g., city, region, country).
* **Geographic Scale** (`geographic_scale`, required, true): Select the geographic scale of your project's location (e.g., Local Community, Regional, etc.).
* **Vulnerable Populations Targeted** (`vulnerable_populations`, required, true): Select all vulnerable populations targeted by your project (e.g., Low-Income Communities, Farmers, etc.).
* **Estimated Number of Beneficiaries** (`beneficiary_count`, required, false): Estimate the number of people your project aims to benefit.
* **Project Duration** (`project_duration`, required, true): Specify the length of time your project will run (in months).
* **Project Start Date** (`start_date`, required, true): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`end_date`, required, false): Enter the end date of your project (if known).
* **Total Project Budget** (`total_budget`, required, true): Enter the total budget for your project (in currency units).
* **Budget Allocation by Category** (`budget_breakdown`, required, false): Describe how you plan to allocate your project budget.
* **Percentage of Funding Secured** (`funding_secured`, required, false): Estimate the percentage of funding secured so far.
* **Funding Sources** (`funding_sources`, required, false): List the sources of funding for your project (e.g., grants, loans, etc.).
* **Type of Support or Partnerships Needed** (`support_needed`, required, false): Specify any support or partnerships you require for your project.
* **Current Implementation Readiness** (`implementation_readiness`, required, true): Select the current stage of your project's implementation (e.g., Planning, Design, etc.).
* **Expected Outcomes and Resilience Improvements** (`expected_outcomes`, required, true): Describe the expected outcomes and resilience improvements from your project.
* **Key Success Indicators or Metrics** (`success_indicators`, required, true): Specify the key indicators or metrics to measure your project's success.

## Tips

* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
