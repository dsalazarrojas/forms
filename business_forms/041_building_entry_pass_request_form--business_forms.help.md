# Building Entry Pass Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from visitors to your building for the purpose of creating an entry pass. The information collected will be used to verify the visitor's identity, ensure their presence is known, and manage access permissions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields (marked as **Required**).
2. Enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth processing of your request.
3. Make sure to provide the correct building address and name, as this is crucial for navigation.
4. Choose the correct visit date and time, and select the correct duration of your visit.
5. Select the correct purpose of your visit and provide a brief description of your visit.
6. Answer "Yes" to the terms and conditions to indicate your agreement to follow all rules and regulations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor Full Name** (`visitor_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your first and last name.
* **Visitor Email** (`visitor_email`, email, **Required**): Provide your contact email address.
* **Visitor Phone** (`visitor_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, **Not Required**): If you are visiting on behalf of your organization, enter your company name.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date you are making this request.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, **Required**): Choose the date you plan to visit the building.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, **Required**): Select the expected time of your arrival.
* **Expected Duration** (`duration`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the length of your visit.
* **Building Name** (`building_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the building you plan to visit.
* **Building Address** (`building_address`, text, **Required**): Enter the full address of the building.
* **Floor or Suite** (`floor_or_suite`, text, **Not Required**): If applicable, provide the specific floor or suite number.
* **Host Name** (`host_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the person you are visiting.
* **Host Department** (`host_department`, text, **Not Required**): If applicable, enter the department name of the host.
* **Purpose of Visit** (`visit_purpose`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the reason for your visit.
* **Visit Description** (`visit_description`, text, **Required**): Provide a brief description of your visit.
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`, text, **Not Required**): If you are bringing a vehicle, provide make, model, and license plate number.
* **Parking Needed** (`parking_needed`, select_one, **Required**): Mark if you need parking.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, **Not Required**): If you have any special needs, such as accessibility requirements, describe them here.
* **Items Being Brought** (`items_brought`, text, **Not Required**): If you are bringing any equipment or materials, describe them here.
* **Identification Type** (`identification_type`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the type of identification you will present.
* **Previous Visitor** (`previous_visitor`, select_one, **Required**): Indicate if you have visited before.
* **Background Check Status** (`background_check`, select_one, **Not Required**): If background checks are required for your visit, indicate the status.
* **Health Screening** (`health_screening`, select_one, **Not Required**): If health screening is required for your visit, indicate the status.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, **Required**): Confirm that you agree to follow all rules and regulations.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, text, **Not Required**): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, **Not Required**): Provide the contact phone number of the emergency contact person.
