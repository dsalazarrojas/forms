# workers_profile_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The workers_profile_form is a form designed for workers to provide their personal and professional information for internal business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your First Name in the first_name field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the last_name field.
4. Enter your job title in the job_title field.
5. Select your department from the department field.
6. Enter your email address in the email field.
7. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
8. Enter the date you started work in the start_date field.
9. Enter the date you ended your employment in the end_date field.
10. If you are currently assigned to a specific role or project, select 'Yes' in the assigned_to field.
11. If you have a specific tool assigned to you, select 'Yes' in the assigned_tool field.
11. Enter your manager's name in the manager_name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: <u>First Name</u> (Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: <u>Last Name</u> (Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **job_title**: <u>Job Title</u> (Text, Optional): Enter your job title.
* **department**: <u>Department</u> (Text, Optional): Select your department.
* **email**: <u>Email</u> (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone**: <u>Phone Number</u> (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **start_date**: <u>Start Date</u> (Date, Optional): Enter the date you started work.
* **end_date**: <u>End Date</u> (Date, Optional): Enter the date you ended your employment.
* **assigned_to**: <u>Assigned to</u> (Select One, Optional): If you are currently assigned to a specific role or project, select 'Yes'.
* **assigned_tool**: <u>Assigned Tool</u> (Select One, Optional): If you have a specific tool assigned to you, select 'Yes'.
* **manager_name**: <u>Manager's Name</u> (Text, Optional): Enter your manager's name.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or HR department.
