# process_improvement_certification_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Improvement Certification Application form is designed to gather relevant information from applicants for the purpose of evaluating and certifying their process improvement skills and knowledge. It helps to verify the applicant's eligibility and suitability for the certification program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Applicant Information** on page 1, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your **Company Information** on page 1.
3. In the **Work History** section (page 1), provide a brief description of your work history.
4. In the **Education History** section (page 1), provide a brief description of your educational background.
5. On page 2, select your **Job Title** (Yes or No) and indicate whether you have a **Certification** (Yes or No).
6. On page 2, choose from the options for **Work History 2** and **Education History 2** (Yes or No).
7. On page 3, fill in your **Contact Information**, including your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Information
* **Applicant Information** (Applicant Information, text, required: false): This section is meant to capture your name and any other essential information about you as the applicant.

* **Company Info** (company_info, text, required: false): This field is for you to enter details about the company you're applying with.

* **Work History** (work_history, note, required: false): Provide a brief description of your work experience, if any.

* **Education History** (education_history, note, required: false): Describe your educational background, if any.

* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required: false): Enter your email address.

* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.

* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required: false): This field is meant for you to fill in any other essential contact details.

* **Work Title** (job_title, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' if you have a job title, 'No' otherwise.

* **Certification** (certification, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' if you have a certification, 'No' otherwise.

* **Work History 2** (work_history_2, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you have work history in any of the options listed.

* **Education History 2** (education_history_2, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you have an education history in any of the options listed.

### Contact
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your email address.

* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Make sure to include all relevant information about yourself and your background.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
* Ensure you meet the eligibility criteria for the process improvement certification program.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
