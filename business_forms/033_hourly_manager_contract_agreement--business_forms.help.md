# Hourly Manager Contract Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hourly Manager Contract Agreement form is used to collect information for a formal agreement between an employee and their manager regarding job scope, payment terms, compensation, and termination conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete the form in collaboration with your manager to ensure clear understanding and expectations.
- Ensure that you accurately fill out each field according to your agreement.
- Review the form carefully before signing to confirm that all terms are agreed upon.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information**: This section is for your personal details as an employee.
* **Manager Information**: This section is for your manager's contact information.
* **Job Scope**: Describe your job responsibilities and tasks in this field.
* **Payment Terms**: Select one of the options to agree upon payment terms (e.g., hourly rate, commission, etc.).
* **Work Schedule**: List your work schedule, including days and hours you are expected to work.
* **Compensation**: Enter your compensation amount or rate for the agreed-upon work.
* **Termination Conditions**: This field is for any conditions that would lead to contract termination.
* **Manager Signature**: Your manager's signature confirms they have reviewed and agree to the terms.
* **Employee Signature**: Your signature confirms you have reviewed and agree to the terms.
* **Contract Start Date**: Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date**: Enter the end date of the contract.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
- Review the form carefully before signing to avoid any misunderstandings.
- Keep a copy of the signed contract for future reference.
