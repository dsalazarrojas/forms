# operational_risk_management_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The operational risk management contact form is designed to help assess and document potential risks that may impact business operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each category and field, and provide a brief description of the risk. The form has multiple pages with fields that require selection or text input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select one of the two options: "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the risk category is relevant to your area of responsibility.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the staff member responsible for reporting the risk.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select one of the two options: "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the risk is specific to a department.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the risk.
* **Risk Type** (`risk_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the two options: "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the risk is related to operational risk.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one, optional): Select one of the two options: High or Low to indicate the level of risk.
* **Risk Category** (`risk_category`, select_one, optional): Select one of the two options: "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the risk is related to risk category.
* **Risk Frequency** (`risk_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select one of the two options: "Yes" or "No" to indicate the frequency of the risk.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are only reporting potential risks that you are reasonably certain about.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please select "Yes" for the relevant categories.
* You can submit this form multiple times if you need to report multiple risks.
* If you have already submitted a similar risk assessment, please review the previous form and ensure that you are not duplicating your effort.
