# General Meeting Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The General Meeting Voting Form is used to gather feedback from meeting attendees on the agenda items presented during the meeting. This form helps the meeting organizers to understand the level of support or opposition to each agenda item, allowing them to make informed decisions and adjust their strategy accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the agenda items presented during the meeting.
2. For each agenda item, select the option that best represents your opinion:
	* "Yes" to indicate that you support the item.
	* "No" to indicate that you do not support the item.
3. Complete all agenda items by selecting your opinion for each one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **agenda_item_1** (Select Multiple): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your opinion on the first agenda item.
* **agenda_item_2** (Select Multiple): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your opinion on the second agenda item.
* **agenda_item_3** (Select Multiple): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your opinion on the third agenda item.
* ...
* **agenda_item_25** (Select Multiple): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your opinion on the 25th agenda item.

Note: All agenda items are identical and have the same label and options. The only difference is the ID number.
