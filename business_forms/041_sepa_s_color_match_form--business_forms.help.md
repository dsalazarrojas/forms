# Sepa’s Color Match Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help us better understand your color preferences and physical characteristics to provide more accurate product recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your color matching preferences on the "Color Match" page.
2. For each color matching question, choose either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your match status.
3. On the "Physical Characteristics" page, enter your skin type, skin tone, hair color, hair type, and face shape.
4. For each physical characteristic, provide your actual value (e.g., "Fair", "Light", "Brown", etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Color Match**: On this page, you'll be asked to select which colors you match with. Choose the colors that you typically match with or are attracted to.
* **Match Color 1**: Select "Yes" if you match with Color 1, "No" otherwise.
* **Match Color 2**: Select "Yes" if you match with Color 2, "No" otherwise.
* **Skin Type**: Enter your skin type (e.g., "Fair", "Light", "Medium", "Dark", etc.).
* **Skin Tone**: Enter your skin tone (e.g., "Fair", "Light", "Medium", "Dark", etc.).
* **Hair Color**: Enter your hair color (e.g., "Blonde", "Brown", "Black", etc.).
* **Hair Type**: Enter your hair type (e.g., "Straight", "Curly", "Wavy", etc.).
* **Face Shape**: Select "Yes" if you have an oval face shape, "No" otherwise.
* **Face Shape 1**: This field is not necessary to complete, but you can select "Yes" if you have an oval face shape.
* **Skin Type, Skin Tone, Hair Color, Hair Type, Face Shape, and Face Shape 1 are all optional and intended for users who want to provide additional information. The fields are not required.**
