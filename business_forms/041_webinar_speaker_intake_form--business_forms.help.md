# Webinar Speaker Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Webinar Speaker Intake Form is designed to gather essential information from speakers about their availability and willingness to present in an upcoming webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your speaker details in the **Speaker Details** field.
2. Select your topics of interest for the webinar in the **Speaker Topics** field.
3. Indicate your availability for the webinar in the **Webinar Availability** field.
4. Provide your availability in the **Availability** field if you have specific time constraints.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker Details** (`speaker_details`, text, required): This field is where you should enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself as a speaker.
* **Speaker Topics** (`speaker_topics`, select_multiple, optional): Select the topics you are interested in presenting about during the webinar. You can choose from the available options or suggest your own topic.
* **Webinar Availability** (`webinar_availability`, select_one, optional): Indicate your availability for the webinar by selecting 'Yes' if you are available or 'No' if you are not.
* **Availability** (`speaker_availability`, time, optional): If you have specific time constraints, provide them in this field.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and carefully to ensure that we can schedule and prepare for the webinar effectively.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Make sure to select your topics of interest and availability carefully, as they will help us in preparing for the webinar.
