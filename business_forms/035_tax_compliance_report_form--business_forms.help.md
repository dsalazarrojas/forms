# Tax Compliance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Compliance Report Form is designed to collect and record tax compliance information from businesses and business entities. It is meant to be completed by business owners or tax professionals to report their tax compliance status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of business entity you represent or manage.
2. If applicable, select the tax year for which you are reporting compliance.
3. Choose the type of tax return you are filing.
4. Select your tax filing status.
5. Enter the due date for the tax return.
6. If applicable, enter the date the tax return was filed.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the tax compliance status.
8. Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Entity** (`business_entity`, select_one): Select the type of business entity you represent or manage, such as Individual, Business Corporation, Partnership, or Limited Liability Company (LLC).
* **Business Entity Type** (`business_entity_type`, select_multiple): Select any additional specific types of business entity you want to report, such as Sole Proprietorship or Non-US Citizen. 
* **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, number): Enter the tax year for which you are reporting compliance. This is an optional field.
* **Tax Return Type** (`tax_return_type`, select_one): Select the type of tax return you are filing, such as Federal, State, or Local.
* **Tax Filing Status** (`tax_filing_status`, select_one): Select your tax filing status, such as Single, Married, Joint, or Head of Household.
* **Tax Return Due Date** (`tax_return_due_date`, date): Enter the due date for the tax return. This is an optional field.
* **Tax Return Filing Date** (`tax_return_filing_date`, date): Enter the date the tax return was filed. This is an optional field.
* **Note** (`note`, note): Add any additional comments or notes about the tax compliance status.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
