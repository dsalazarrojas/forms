# Pet Training Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place new orders for pet training products. It collects information about the customer, the order details, and the status of the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First name** in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone number** in the "Phone number" field.
5. Select the type of **Product** you would like to order from the list of options: "Pet Training Products", "Training Sessions", or "Treats".
6. Enter the quantity of the product you would like to order in the "Product quantity" field.
7. Select the date of the order from the calendar.
8. Enter the time of the order.
9. Add any additional **Notes** about your order.
10. Confirm your **Email** by selecting "Confirm" from the dropdown menu.
11. Confirm your **Phone number** by selecting "Confirm" from the dropdown menu.
12. Select the current **Order status** of your order from the dropdown menu: "New order", "In progress", or "Completed".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your order.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Product type**: Select the type of product you would like to order.
* **Product quantity**: Enter the quantity of the product you would like to order.
* **Order date**: Select the date of the order.
* **Order time**: Enter the time of the order.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Confirm email**: Confirm that the email address entered is correct.
* **Confirm phone**: Confirm that the phone number entered is correct.
* **Order status**: Select the current status of your order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the email address and phone number entered are correct to avoid any issues with communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your order, please contact our support team.
* This form is for new orders only. If you would like to place a duplicate order, please contact our support team to inquire about the possibility.
