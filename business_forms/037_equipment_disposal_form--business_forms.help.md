# Equipment Disposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of equipment being disposed of, including its description, category, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Equipment Description field with a brief description of the equipment being disposed of.
2. Enter the Category ID to identify the equipment's category.
3. Select the Asset Status to indicate how the equipment was disposed of (e.g. Sold, Donated, Discarded).
4. Enter the Purchase Date and Purchase Price if applicable.
5. Enter the Disposal Date to record the date of disposal.
6. Enter the Authorized By field with the name of the person responsible for the disposal.
7. Enter the Category ID 2 field if necessary.
8. Enter the Asset Tag field to track the equipment's unique identifier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Description** (equipment_disposal_form, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the equipment being disposed of.
* **Category ID** (category_id, number, required/optional): Enter the category ID of the equipment.
* **Asset Type** (asset_type, text, required/optional): Enter the type of the asset (e.g. computer, chair, etc.).
* **Purchase Date** (purchase_date, date, required/optional): Enter the date the equipment was purchased, if applicable.
* **Purchase Price** (purchase_price, number, required/optional): Enter the purchase price of the equipment, if applicable.
* **Disposal Date** (disposal_date, date, required/optional): Enter the date the equipment was disposed of.
* **Authorized By** (authorized_by, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for the disposal.
* **Asset Status** (asset_status, select_multiple, required/optional): Select how the equipment was disposed of (e.g. Sold, Donated, Discarded).
* **Category ID 2** (category_id_2, number, required/optional): Enter the category ID of the equipment, if necessary.
* **Asset Tag** (asset_tag, text, required/optional): Enter the unique identifier of the equipment.
