# remote_work_policy_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The remote_work_policy_audit_form is designed to assess the remote work policies of an organization. It gathers information on various aspects of remote work, including policy conformance, risk, confidence, and approval status. This form helps in making informed decisions and improving policy effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections carefully to understand what each field represents.
2. Answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.
3. Choose the most suitable options for each field according to your knowledge and understanding of the remote work policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Remote Work Policy Audit Form Questions** (`remote_work_policy_audit_form_questions`, text, not required): Please enter a brief description of the reason for the audit. This can include any concerns, changes, or updates to the remote work policy.
- **Remote Work Policies** (`remote_work_policies`, select multiple, not required): Select all the remote work policies currently in place. Options are `Yes` and `No`.
- **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, not required): Enter the number of hours worked for the remote work policy.
- **Work Locations** (`work_locations`, select one, not required): Choose the primary location where remote work takes place. Options are `New York`, `Los Angeles`, and `Chicago`.
- **Work Frequency** (`work_frequency`, select one, not required): Choose the frequency of remote work. Options are `Full-time`, `Part-time`, and `Remote`.
- **Remote Worker Information** (`remote_worker_info`, text, not required): Provide any additional information about the remote worker.
- **Remote Work Policies Conformance** (`remote_work_policies_conformance`, date, not required): Enter the date of the last policy review or conformance check.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, select one, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the remote work policy. Options are `Very Good`, `Good`, `Fair`, and `Poor`.
- **Remote Work Policies Comments** (`remote_work_policies_comments`, text, not required): Provide any comments or suggestions on the remote work policy.
- **Remote Work Policies Suggestions** (`remote_work_policies_suggestions`, text, not required): Suggest any changes or improvements to the remote work policy.
- **Remote Work Policies Compliance** (`remote_work_policies_compliance`, select one, not required): Assess the overall compliance level of the remote work policy. Options are `Fully Compliant`, `Mostly Compliant`, and `Partially Compliant`.
- **Remote Work Policies Risk** (`remote_work_policies_risk`, select one, not required): Identify any potential risks associated with the remote work policy. Options are `Low`, `Medium`, and `High`.
- **Remote Work Policies Confidence** (`remote_work_policies_confidence`, select one, not required): Evaluate your confidence in the remote work policy. Options are `High`, `Medium`, and `Low`.
- **Remote Work Policies Adequacy** (`remote_work_policies_adequacy`, select one, not required): Assess the adequacy of the remote work policy. Options are `Adequate`, `Inadequate`.
- **Remote Work Policies Compliance Status** (`remote_work_policies_compliance_status`, select one, not required): Determine the current compliance status of the remote work policy. Options are `Fully Compliant`, `Partially Compliant`, and `Non-Compliant`.
- **Remote Work Policies Review Date** (`remote_work_policies_review_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the last policy review.
- **Remote Work Policies Review Time** (`remote_work_policies_review_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the last policy review.
- **Remote Work Policies Reviewer** (`remote_work_policies_reviewer`, text, not required): Provide the name of the person who reviewed the policy.
- **Remote Work Policies Review Comments** (`remote_work_policies_review_comments`, text, not required): Enter any comments from the last policy review.
- **Remote Work Policies Reviewed** (`remote_work_policies_reviewed`, select one, not required): Confirm if the policy has been reviewed. Options are `True` and `False`.
- **Remote Work Policies Status** (`remote_work_policies_status`, select one, not required): Determine the status of the remote work policy. Options are `Active` and `Inactive`.
- **Remote Work Policies Version** (`remote_work_policies_version`, text, not required): Enter the current version of the remote work policy.
- **Remote Work Policies Notes** (`remote_work_policies_notes`, text, not required): Provide any additional notes on the remote work policy.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when answering each question.
- Use clear and concise language when providing additional information.
- Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out for assistance.
