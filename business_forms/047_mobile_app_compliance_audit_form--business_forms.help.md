# Mobile App Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile App Compliance Audit Form is a tool to ensure that your mobile app meets the company's standards and regulations. This form will guide you through the necessary information required for a comprehensive audit of your app.

## How To Complete This Form

*   **Step 1: Fill out the form with your app's information**. Start by entering the name of your app in the "App Name" field.
*   **Step 2: Choose the category that best describes your app**. Select the correct category from the dropdown menu.
*   **Step 3: Provide details on your app's policies**. Answer the "Privacy Policies" field with a brief description of how your app protects user data.
*   **Step 4: Describe your app's data security measures**. In the "Data Security" field, explain how your app safeguards user data.
*   **Step 5: List the compliance standards your app adheres to**. In the "Compliance Standards" field, mention any relevant industry standards or regulations your app follows.
*   **Step 6: Explain your testing processes**. Describe the testing methods you use to ensure your app's security and stability.
*   **Step 7: List the security measures your app takes**. In the "Security Measures" field, outline any measures you have in place to protect user data and app security.
*   **Step 8: Describe your reporting processes**. Explain how you report and address any security incidents or vulnerabilities.
*   **Step 9: Explain your data protection policies**. Describe how you safeguard user data and protect against unauthorized access.
*   **Step 10: Specify your data retention policies**. Explain how long you retain user data and how it is disposed of when necessary.
*   **Step 11: Provide information on your app's store listing**. If your app is listed on a digital store, provide details on how you ensure the listing accurately represents your app and complies with store policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **App Name (App Name)** (`app_name`, text, optional):
    *   A field to enter your app's name.
*   **Category (Category)** (`category`, select_one, optional):
    *   A dropdown menu listing the categories your app fits into.
*   **Privacy Policies (privacy_policies)** (text, optional):
    *   A field to describe your app's privacy policies and data protection methods.
*   **Data Security (data_security)** (text, optional):

*   **Compliance Standards (compliance_standards)** (text, optional):

*   **Testing Processes (testing_processes)** (text, optional):
    *   A field to describe your app's testing methods.

*   **Reporting Processes (reporting_processes)** (text, optional):

*   **Data Protection (data_protection)** (text, optional):
