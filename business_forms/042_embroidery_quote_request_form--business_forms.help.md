# Embroidery Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for requesting a quote for an embroidery service. Please fill out the form below to provide us with the necessary information to give you an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and provide any additional information that may be relevant to your request. You can save your progress and come back later to complete the form if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Name** (`form_name`, text, optional): Enter a brief name for your quote request. This will help us identify your request.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name or organization name.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person requesting the quote.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person requesting the quote.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person requesting the quote.
* **Design Name** (`design_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the design or product for which you are requesting a quote.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the design or product you are requesting a quote for.
* **Colors** (`colors`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the colors you would like to use for the design. Please select all colors that apply.
	+ Yes: Select this option if the design includes this color.
* **Thread Color** (`thread_color`, select_one, optional): Choose the thread color you would like to use for the design.
	+ Yes: Select this option if you would like to use this thread color.
* **Design File** (`design_file`, note, optional): Attach any relevant design files or images to support your request.

**Tips**

* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to help us provide an accurate quote.
* If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact us at [insert contact info].
* Make sure to save your progress if you need to leave the form and come back later.
* If you are not sure what any of the fields mean, please contact us and we will be happy to help.
