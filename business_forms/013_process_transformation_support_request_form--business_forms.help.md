# Process Transformation Support Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for process transformation support. A process transformation involves modifying or replacing existing business processes to improve their efficiency and effectiveness. To be eligible for support, your request should be specific, measurable, and aligned with the company's business goals.

Before submitting this form, please ensure that:

* Your request is not a duplicate of an existing process transformation project.
* Your request is not a personal request for training or support on a process that is not part of the process transformation program.
* Your request is not a request for general process improvement, but rather for a specific, transformative change.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Submit your request**: Fill out the form with the required information, including your name and team members involved.
2. **Provide detailed description**: Write a clear and concise description of the process you want to transform, including its current state and the desired state.
3. **Attach relevant attachments**: If necessary, attach relevant documents or files to support your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Information**: Enter a brief description of your process transformation request. This will help us understand the purpose and scope of your request.
* **Team Members**: Select the team members involved in the process transformation project. This will ensure that we contact the right people for further discussion and collaboration.
* **Project Information**: Provide a brief description of the project or process you want to transform. This will help us understand the context and complexity of your request.
* **Process Description**: Describe the current state and desired state of the process you want to transform. This will help us understand the specific changes you need to make and the reasons behind your request.

**Tips**

* Be specific about the changes you need to make to the process.
* Provide clear goals and objectives for the process transformation project.
* Attach relevant documentation or evidence to support your request, if possible.
