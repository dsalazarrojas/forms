# Budget Limit Settings - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Budget Limit Settings form is used to establish and manage budget limits for administrators within the organization. This form is essential for controlling expenses and ensuring that spending stays within allocated limits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked as **required** in each field's label.
2. Select the correct options for each field, such as the budget period and override approval process.
3. Be sure to review and understand the implications of your settings on the organization's financials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Administrator Name** (`Administrator Name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the person setting the limits.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person setting the limits.
* **Department or Cost Center** (`Department or Cost Center`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the department or cost center being managed.
* **Fiscal Year** (`Fiscal Year`, `text`, required): Enter the fiscal year for which the limits are being set.
* **Overall Budget Limit** (`Overall Budget Limit`, `number`, required): Enter the total spending cap for the budget period.
* **Budget Period** (`Budget Period`, `select_one`, required): Select the budget period for which the limits are being set (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, Custom Period).
* **Budget Category** (`Budget Category`, `text`, required): Enter the category for which the limits are being set (e.g., Personnel, Operations, Travel, etc.).
* **Category Spending Limit** (`Category Spending Limit`, `number`, required): Enter the cap for this specific category.
* **Per-Transaction Limit** (`Per-Transaction Limit`, `number`, optional): Enter the maximum single purchase amount (optional).
* **Daily Spending Limit** (`Daily Spending Limit`, `number`, optional): Enter the maximum per-day spending limit (optional).
* **Responsible Party** (`Responsible Party`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for managing this budget.
* **Authorized Approver for Exceptions** (`Authorized Approver for Exceptions`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who can approve over-limit spending.
* **Escalation Threshold** (`Escalation Threshold`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount requiring additional approval (optional).
* **Override Approval Process** (`Override Approval Process`, `select_one`, required): Select the process for approving over-limit spending (e.g., Department Head Approval Required, Finance Director Approval Required, Executive Sign-Off Required, Not Permitted).
* **Alert Notifications** (`Alert Notifications`, `select_one`, required): Select when to send alerts for the escalation threshold (e.g., At 50 percent, At 75 percent, At 90 percent, At 100 percent).
* **Alert Recipients** (`Alert Recipients`, `text`, required): Enter the names of people to receive the notifications.
* **Unused Budget Rollover** (`Unused Budget Rollover`, `select_one`, required): Select what happens to unused funds (e.g., Rolls over to next period, Reverts to general fund, Forfeited, Other).
* **Budget Limit Review Date** (`Budget Limit Review Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when limits should be reviewed.

Please note that some fields may have specific requirements or dependencies, so review each field carefully to ensure accurate completion.
