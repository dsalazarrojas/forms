# Data Management Innovation Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Management Innovation Program Application form is designed to collect information about potential projects and teams seeking to innovate and improve data management practices within the organization. The form requires applicants to provide details about their project, team members, and project objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Project Details**: Enter a brief description of your project idea. This is an opportunity to showcase your concept, and we encourage you to be as clear and concise as possible.
2. **Team Members**: Select all team members who will be working on the project. You can choose from "Yes" or "No" to indicate if they are participating.
3. **Project Idea**: Briefly describe your project idea, highlighting its key aspects and objectives.
4. **Project Goal**: Describe the specific goal of your project, explaining what you aim to achieve.
5. **Team Lead**: Enter your name and job title to represent your team's leadership.
6. **Team Memberships**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if all team members are participating.
7. **Team Member Names**: List the names of all team members involved in the project.
8. **Team Member Roles**: Describe the roles and responsibilities of each team member.
9. **Project Start Date**: Enter the date when your project is expected to start.
10. **Project End Date**: Enter the date when your project is expected to conclude.
11. **Team Lead Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your application.
12. **Project Impact**: Describe the potential impact of your project on the company or industry.
