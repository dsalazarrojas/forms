# it_governance_compliance_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about your organization's compliance status for internal IT governance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Start by filling out the form completely and accurately.
* Review each field carefully before proceeding.
* For fields with options, select all applicable choices that apply to your organization.
* Make sure to submit the form only once for your entire organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form (<name>, <type>, required/optional>):**
	+ This is the first page of the form where you will enter your organization's registration details.
* **Organization Details (<name>, <type>, required/optional>):**
	+ Enter the details about your organization, including its name, and any other relevant information.
* **Compliance Areas (<name>, select_multiple, required/optional>):**
	+ Select the compliance areas that apply to your organization.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Documentation Upload (<name>, <type>, required/optional>):**
	+ Upload any necessary documentation supporting your compliance status.
* **Governance Data (<name>, <type>, required/optional>):**
	+ Enter any additional governance information relevant to your organization's compliance status.
