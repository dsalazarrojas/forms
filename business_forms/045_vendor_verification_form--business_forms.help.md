# Vendor Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Verification Form is designed to collect essential information about a vendor, including their contact details, business details, and verification information. This form helps to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly, ensuring that the vendor's information is up-to-date and consistent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Vendor Verification Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vendor's information in the **Vendor Information** section.
2. Provide the vendor's contact information in the **Contact Information** section.
3. Enter the vendor's business details in the **Business Information** section.
4. Confirm the vendor's verification information in the **Verification Information** section.
5. Review and agree to the terms of the agreement in the **Agreement Information** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Information** (Vendor Information, text, required):
	+ This field is used to collect the vendor's name and basic details.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required):
	+ This field is used to collect the vendor's contact details, such as email and phone number.
* **Business Information** (Business Information, text, required):
	+ This field is used to collect the vendor's business details, such as business name and address.
* **Verification Information** (Verification Information, text, required):
	+ This field is used to confirm the vendor's verification information, such as ID number or certification.
* **Agreement Information** (Agreement Information, text, required):
	+ This field is used to confirm the vendor's agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check your answers to ensure they are correct and consistent.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the form administrator for assistance.
