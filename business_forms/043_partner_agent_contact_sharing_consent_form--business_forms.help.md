# Partner Agent Contact Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from partners to share their contact information with a designated partner agent.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select one of the options in the "Partner Agent Contact Sharing Consent" field to indicate the level of access you are willing to grant to the partner agent.
2. Fill in your contact name and email address in the "Contact Name" and "Email Address" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field, if you wish to grant address sharing.
5. Enter the name of the partner agent who will have access to your contact information.
6. Enter the email address of the partner agent.
7. Enter the phone number of the partner agent.
8. Enter the address of the partner agent, if applicable.
9. Select the specific permissions you are granting to the partner agent in the "Sharing Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Agent Contact Sharing Consent**: This field determines the level of access you are willing to grant to the partner agent. Select one of the options to indicate:
	+ Allow partner agent to access my contact information
	+ Do not allow partner agent to access my contact information
* **Contact Name**: This is your contact name that will be shared with the partner agent.
* **Email Address**: This is your email address that will be shared with the partner agent.
* **Phone Number**: This is your phone number that will be shared with the partner agent.
* **Address**: This field is optional and allows you to enter your address if you wish to grant address sharing.
* **Partner Agent Name**: This is the name of the partner agent who will have access to your contact information.
* **Partner Agent Email**: This is the email address of the partner agent.
* **Partner Agent Phone Number**: This is the phone number of the partner agent.
* **Partner Agent Address**: This field is optional and allows you to enter the partner agent's address, if applicable.
* **Sharing Consent**: This field allows you to select the specific permissions you are granting to the partner agent:
	+ Allow partner agent to access my email address
	+ Allow partner agent to access my phone number
	+ Allow partner agent to access my contact address

**Tips:**

* Be sure to select the level of access you are willing to grant to the partner agent.
* Double-check that you have entered the correct contact information for yourself and the partner agent.
* Review the "Sharing Consent" field carefully to ensure you are granting only the permissions you intend to grant.
