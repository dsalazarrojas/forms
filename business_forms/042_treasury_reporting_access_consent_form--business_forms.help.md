# Treasury Reporting Access Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Reporting Access Consent Form is a document used to obtain consent from employees for accessing treasury reporting. It is essential for ensuring that all employees understand and agree to the terms of reporting access, including the methods used for reporting. This form is a crucial step in maintaining the confidentiality and security of sensitive financial data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your Email in the format of `username@example.com` in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your Job Title in the field labeled "Job Title".
5. Indicate your departmental affiliation in the field labeled "Department".
6. Choose whether you have access to treasury reporting by selecting one of the options "Yes" or "No" in the field labeled "Reporting Access".
7. Choose which methods you have access to for reporting by selecting one of the options "Yes" or "No" in the field labeled "Reporting Methods".
8. Confirm your consent to access treasury reporting by selecting "True" or "False" in the field labeled "Consent".
9. If you are granting consent, enter the date you are granting consent in the field labeled "Consent Date".
10. Finally, provide any additional notes or comments about your reporting access in the field labeled "Notes".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email in the format of `username@example.com`.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Indicate your departmental affiliation.
* **Reporting Access** (`reporting_access`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose whether you have access to treasury reporting by selecting one of the options "Yes" or "No".
* **Reporting Methods** (`reporting_methods`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose which methods you have access to for reporting by selecting one of the options "Yes" or "No".
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm your consent to access treasury reporting by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required/optional): If you are granting consent, enter the date you are granting consent.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your reporting access.
