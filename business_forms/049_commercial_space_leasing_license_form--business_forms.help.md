# Commercial Space Leasing License Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commercial Space Leasing License Form is used to document the commercial space leasing agreement between the licensor (the owner of the premises) and the licensee (the renter or user of the premises). This form captures essential information to ensure a smooth and lawful leasing process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by ensuring that you have all required information and documents ready to be filled out in each field.
2. Fill out the required fields for the license holder and licensee details.
3. Provide a description of the licensed space or area.
4. Specify the permitted use of the space or area.
5. Select the license effective and expiration dates.
6. Choose whether or not a renewal option is available.
7. Fill out the financial information, including the monthly license fee amount and security deposit amount.
8. Ensure that the licensee has provided their official contact email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Licensor Entity Name** (`licensor_legal_name`): Enter the name of the entity that is licensing the space to you.
* **Licensee Entity Name** (`licensee_legal_name`): Enter the name of the entity that is taking on the lease.
* **Description of Licensed Space/Aren** (`licensed_premises_description`): A brief description of the space or area being licensed.
* **Permitted Use of Space** (`licensed_use_description`): Specify the allowed use of the space or area, e.g., office, store, restaurant, etc.
* **License Effective Date** (`license_effective_date`): Enter the date when the lease is to take effect.
* **License Expiration Date** (`license_expiration_date`): Enter the date when the lease is to expire.
* **Is a renewal option available?** (`renewal_option_available`): Choose if an option for renewal is available at the end of the lease term.
* **Monthly License Fee Amount** (`monthly_license_fee`): Enter the amount of the monthly license fee.
* **Security Deposit Amount** (`security_deposit_amount`): Enter the amount of the security deposit required.
* **Licensee Contact Email** (`licensee_contact_email`): Enter the email address of the contact person for the licensee.
* **Licensee Contact Phone** (`licensee_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the licensee.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or authorized personnel.
