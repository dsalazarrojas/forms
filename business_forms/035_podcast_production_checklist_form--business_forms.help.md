# podcast_production_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you plan and track podcast episodes, ensuring consistency and accuracy in your production process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide a brief description of each episode in the "Episode Description" field.
3. Choose the target audience for each episode in the "Target Audience" field.
4. Select the release schedule for each episode in the "Release Schedule" field.
5. Enter the episode's duration and format in the "Episode Duration" and "Audio Format" fields, respectively.
6. Add any additional details about the episode in the "Episode Title", "Episode Tagline", and "Podcast Description" fields.
7. Specify the release date and time for each episode in the "Release Date" and "Release Time" fields.
8. Enter the recording date and time for each episode in the "Recording Date" and "Recording Time" fields.
9. Choose the social media platforms to share the episode on in the "Social Media" field.
10. Select the podcast host(s) for each episode in the "Podcast Host" field.
11. Specify the recording equipment used for each episode in the "Recording Equipment" field.
12. Choose the location where each episode was recorded in the "Recording Location" field.
