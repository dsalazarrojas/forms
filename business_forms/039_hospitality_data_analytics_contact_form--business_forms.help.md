# Hospitality Data Analytics Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospitality Data Analytics Contact Form is a support request form used to collect contact information and details about analytics support requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely.
2. Provide a brief description of your support request in the **Contact Message** field.
3. Select your preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone, or mail) in the **Preferred Contact Method** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Form**: This field is the name of the form.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person or team member submitting the form.
* **Contact Organization**: Enter the name of the organization or company related to the support request.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person or team member submitting the form.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or team member submitting the form.
* **Contact Message**: Provide a brief description of your support request.
* **Contact Analytics**: Select the analytics partner related to your support request (e.g., Analytics Partner A, B, or C).
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select one or more preferred contact methods (e.g., email, phone, or mail).
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information or context related to your support request.
