# Corporate Credit Limit Adjustment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting adjustments to a company's corporate credit limit.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name and title to identify who is submitting the request.
2. Enter a justification for the credit limit adjustment. Please provide a clear explanation for the change.
3. Select any supporting documents that are relevant to your request.
4. Specify whether you are requesting an increase or decrease in the credit limit.
5. Select a reason for the change.
6. Choose the appropriate finance approval option.
7. Select the credit committee review option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Corporate Credit Limit Adjustment Request Form** (`business_form`): 
    - This is the primary field for entering your name and title.
* **Justification** (`justification`): 
    - Please enter a clear and concise explanation for the credit limit adjustment.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`):
    - Select any relevant documents that support your credit limit adjustment request.
* **Credit Limit Increase/Decrease** (`credit_limit_increase`, `credit_limit_decrease`):
    - If you are requesting an increase, select the option that best describes the increase. If you are requesting a decrease, select the option that best describes the decrease.
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`):
    - Select a reason from the available options for the credit limit adjustment.
* **Finance Approval** (`finance_approval`):
    - Select the finance approval option that best aligns with your credit limit request.
* **Credit Committee Review** (`credit_committee_review`):
    - Select the credit committee review option that best aligns with your credit limit request.

## Tips

* Please be clear and concise in your justification and reason for change.
* Ensure that you select the correct supporting documents to support your request.
* Review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure that all necessary information is provided.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the relevant department for clarification.
