# Infrastructure Maintenance Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Infrastructure Maintenance Tracking Form** is used to collect information about maintenance tasks, schedules, and costs related to the upkeep of infrastructure. This form helps track and record essential details to ensure that maintenance activities are completed on time and within budget.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete the form, start by selecting the "General Information" page.
2. Fill in the relevant details under "General Information", including any relevant notes or comments.
3. Move on to the "Maintenance Tasks" page and list any maintenance tasks that need to be completed.
4. On the "Maintenance Schedules" page, enter the schedules or frequencies of maintenance tasks.
5. For "Notes", provide any additional comments or observations related to the maintenance activities.
6. In the "Maintenance Costs" field, enter the cost associated with each maintenance task.
7. On the "Contact Information" page, enter the contact details of the person responsible for the maintenance task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`1`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the maintenance task, such as the location, issue, or problem being addressed.
* **Maintenance Tasks** (`2`, text, required: false): List all maintenance tasks that need to be completed for the upkeep of the infrastructure.
* **Maintenance Schedules** (`3`, text, required: false): Enter the schedule or frequency of maintenance tasks, such as daily, weekly, or monthly checks.
* **Notes** (`4`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or observations related to the maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance Costs** (`5`, number, required: false): Enter the cost associated with each maintenance task, including labor, materials, and any other expenses.
* **Contact Information** (`6`, text, required: false): Enter the contact details of the person responsible for the maintenance task, including name, email, and phone number.
