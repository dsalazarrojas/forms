# Material Traceability Innovation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register and track new materials, substances, or products that have been developed or improved upon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the Main Page and enter the title and description of the innovation.
2. Proceed to the Contact Info Page and provide your contact details.
3. Move to the Innovation Info Page and enter additional information about the innovation.
4. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Material Traceability Innovation Registration Form (title)** (`<title>`, required): Enter the title of your innovation in this field.
* **Innovation Description (description)** (`<description>`, optional): Provide a brief description of your innovation.
* **Contact Name (name)** (`<name>`, required): Enter the name of the individual or organization submitting this form.
* **Contact Email (email)** (`<email>`, required): Enter the email address of the individual or organization submitting this form.
* **Innovation Type (type)** (`<type>`, required): Select the category of innovation (e.g., material, substance, or product).
* **Innovation Description (description)** (`<description>`, required): Enter a detailed description of your innovation.
* **Benefits (benefits)** (`<benefits>`, optional): List the benefits of your innovation.
* **Characteristics (characteristics)** (`<characteristics>`, optional): List the characteristics of your innovation.
* **Submit (submit)** (`<submit>`, required): Once you have filled out all the required fields, click this button to submit your form.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are not duplicating an existing innovation.
* Fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact form@gic.mx.
