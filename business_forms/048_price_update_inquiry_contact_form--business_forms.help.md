# Price Update Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Price Update Inquiry Contact Form is used to gather information from customers about their need for price updates for a particular product or service. This form helps the sales team to understand the customer's urgency level and provide a timely response to their inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have a pressing need for price updates.
2. Choose the urgency level of your need from the options: Urgent, High, Medium, or Low.
3. If necessary, attach a file to support your inquiry.
4. If you have additional questions or concerns related to pricing, provide them in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Form (1)**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you have a pressing need for price updates. Select "No" if you do not have a pressing need.
* **Urgency Level (2)**: Choose the level of urgency you experience for price updates, from Urgent to Low.
* **File Attachment (3)**: If you have a document or file that supports your inquiry, attach it here.
* **File Attachment 2 (4)**: If you have additional supporting documents, attach them here.
* **Pricing Questions (5)**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you have questions related to pricing. Select "No" if you do not have questions.
* **Question 1 (6)**: Provide any questions you have related to pricing in this field.
* **Question 2 (7)**: Provide any additional questions you have related to pricing in this field.
* **Question 3 (8)**: Provide any further questions you have related to pricing in this field.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing information in the text fields.
* If you have any attachments, ensure they are in the correct format and easily accessible.
* The sales team will respond to your inquiry as soon as possible, based on the information provided.
