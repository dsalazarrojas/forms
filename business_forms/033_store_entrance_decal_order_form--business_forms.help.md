# Store Entrance Decal Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information for store entrance decal orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the decal type by ticking the relevant boxes.
2. Enter the decal size.
3. Fill in your first name and last name.
4. Enter your company's name (if applicable).
5. Enter your address, city, state, and zip code.
6. Indicate the type of order you are making.
7. Choose the quantity of decals you require.
8. Select the decal color.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Enter your phone number.
11. Add any additional notes.
12. Enter your store name.
13. Specify the date and time of the order.
14. Enter your store's hours of operation.
15. Enter your store's URL.
16. Enter a URL for the decal image (if applicable).
17. Select your business license status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Entrance Decal Type** (`store_entrance_decal_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of decal you require.
* **Decal Size** (`decal_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of the decal you need.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, enter its name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you are located.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state/region.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your postal/zip code.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the type of order you are making.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Specify the quantity of decals you require.
* **Decal Color** (`decal_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select the color of the decal.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, optional): Enter your store's name.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Specify the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Specify the time of the order.
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, text, optional): Enter your store's hours of operation.
* **Store URL** (`store_url`, text, optional): Enter your store's URL.
* **Decal Image URL** (`decal_image_url`, text, optional): Enter a URL for the decal image (if applicable).
* **Business License ID** (`business_license_id`, select_one, optional): Indicate your business license status.
