# Product Co Development Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Co Development Registration Form is used to collect information about product co-development collaborations. This form helps us understand the specifics of each collaboration, including category, contact details, and collaboration status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by filling out the required fields, which are indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your category name in the "Category Name" field.
3. Provide your contact name and email address in the corresponding fields.
4. If you have a contact phone number, enter it in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Select your collaboration level from the "Collaboration Level" dropdown menu.
6. Choose the applicable status of your collaboration from the "Collaboration Status" dropdown menu.
7. If applicable, provide a brief description of your collaboration in the "Collaboration Description" field.
8. Enter the date of your collaboration in the "Collaboration Date" field.
9. If you have any comments or additional information, use the "Form Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category Name**: Enter the name of the category your collaboration falls under.
- *Contact Name*: Enter your name as the contact person for the collaboration.
- *Email*: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
- *Collaboration Level*: Choose the level of your collaboration from the dropdown menu:
  + Option 1
  + Option 2
  + Option 3
- *Collaboration Status*: Select the current status of your collaboration from the dropdown menu:
  + Option 1
  + Option 2
  + Option 3
- **Collaboration Description**: Provide a brief description of your collaboration.
- *Collaboration Date*: Enter the date of your collaboration.
- **Form Comments**: Use this field to add any additional comments or information about your collaboration. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (\*) before submitting the form.
- Use the provided hints if you need help with a specific field.
- Ensure that your contact information is accurate to facilitate smooth communication.
