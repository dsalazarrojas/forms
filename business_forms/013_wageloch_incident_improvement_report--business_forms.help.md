# Wageloch Incident Improvement Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wageloch Incident Improvement Report form is used to capture and document incidents that have occurred in the organization, aiming to improve incident response and mitigation processes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if an incident has already been reported. If it has, do not fill out this form.
2. Identify the type of incident (Physical, Cyber, Other).
3. Choose the severity of the incident (High, Medium, Low).
4. Choose the status of the incident (Open, Closed, Pending).
5. Select the priority of the incident (High, Medium, Low).
6. Choose the tool assigned to the incident (chatjimmy, Other).
7. Fill out any additional fields as necessary for further information and context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Incident** (1, Select Multiple): Choose the type of incident that has occurred (Yes, No).
- **Nonconformance** (2, Select Multiple): Identify if the incident resulted in a nonconformance (Yes, No).
- **Opportunity** (3, Select One): If applicable, specify the opportunity for improvement or lesson learned from the incident (Yes, No).
- **Notes** (4, Note): Provide any additional comments or notes related to the incident.
- **Email** (5, Email): Enter your email address for contact purposes, if desired.
- **Phone** (6, Text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes, if desired.
- **Category** (7, Select One): Choose the category of the incident (ISO 27001, ISO 9001, Other).
- **Category Other** (8, Text): If category is Other, specify the category here.
- **Description** (9, Text): Briefly describe the incident.
- **Date** (10, Date): Enter the date of the incident.
- **Time** (11, Time): Enter the time of the incident.
- **Location** (12, Text): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
- **Type** (13, Select One): Reiterate the type of incident (Physical, Cyber, Other).
- **Severity** (14, Select One): Choose the severity of the incident (High, Medium, Low).
- **Status** (15, Select One): Choose the status of the incident (Open, Closed, Pending).
- **Priority** (16, Select One): Choose the priority of the incident (High, Medium, Low).
- **Escalation** (17, Text): If applicable, specify the details of any escalation that occurred.
- **Reopened** (18, Text): If applicable, specify the reopened status and reason.
- **Close Date** (19, Date): If the incident is closed, enter the close date.
- **Assigned Tool** (20, Select One): Choose the tool assigned to the incident (chatjimmy, Other).
- **Reported By** (21, Text): Enter your name for reporting the incident.
- **Owners** (22, Text): List the owners of the incident, if any.
- **Respondents** (23, Text): List any individuals who responded to the incident, if any.
- **Notes** (24, Note): Add any additional comments or notes related to the incident.
- **Reviewers** (25, Text): List any reviewers of the incident, if any.

Note: Fields with optional hints are not explicitly required but might be useful for further context and information.
