# Safety Visit Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Visit Plan form is used to collect information about a safety visit to a location. This includes details about the person visiting, the visit date, location, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information before starting the form.
2. Fill in the contact person's details, including their first name, last name, and email address.
3. Enter the date of the visit and the visit location.
4. Select the visit duration and frequency from the options provided.
5. Choose the correct frequency of the visit (first, second, third, or fourth).
6. Add any comments about the visit, if necessary.
7. Assign the visit to a person or department within your organization.
8. Set the status of the visit as completed, in progress, or draft.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form 1**: This is the main form title.
* **First Name** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Last Name** (`contact_person_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Email** (`contact_person_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`contact_person_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Visit Location** (`visit_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the visit.
* **Duration** (`visit_duration`, time, required): Enter the duration of the visit.
* **Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select the visit frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly).
* **What** (`visit_frequency_what`, select_one, required): Select the visit frequency (first, second, third, or fourth).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Add any comments about the visit.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, text, required): Assign the visit to a person or department within your organization.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Set the status of the visit as completed, in progress, or draft.
