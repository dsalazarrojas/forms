# one_on_one_meeting_focus_sheet_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and track progress of one-on-one meetings between employees, managers, or other stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form for each meeting, providing the required information.
* Ensure that you are not duplicating an existing record or meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Focus Sheet**: A brief description of the meeting, including the topic, outcome, and next steps.
	+ Enter a short summary of the meeting, including the discussion and key points.
* **Meeting Participants**: Identify the participants in the meeting, including yourself.
	+ Select 'Yes' if the participant is present, 'No' otherwise.
* **Meeting Duration**: Enter the duration of the meeting in minutes.
	+ Enter the total minutes spent on the meeting.
* **Meeting Topic**: A brief description of the meeting's subject or main topic.
	+ Enter a short description of the meeting's main topic or subject.
* **Meeting Outcome**: Indicate if the meeting outcome was positive or negative.
	+ Select 'Yes' for a positive outcome, 'No' otherwise.
* **Meeting Notes**: Any additional comments or observations from the meeting.
	+ Enter any relevant notes or comments from the meeting.
* **Meeting Next Steps**: List any action items or tasks assigned to you or other participants.
	+ Enter any tasks or action items assigned to you or other participants.
* **Meeting Next Steps Details**: Provide a detailed description of the next steps, including deadlines and responsible parties.
	+ Enter a detailed description of the next steps, including deadlines and responsible parties.
* **Meeting Next Steps Action Due Date**: Enter the date by which the next steps are due.
	+ Enter the date by which the next steps are due.
* **Meeting Next Steps Responsible**: Identify the person responsible for the next steps.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are responsible, 'No' otherwise.
* **Meeting Next Steps Responsible (if not yourself)**: If you are not responsible, select the person responsible.
	+ Select the person responsible for the next steps if it is not you.
