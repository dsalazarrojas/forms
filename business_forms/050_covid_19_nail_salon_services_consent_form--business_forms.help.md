# covid 19 nail salon services consent form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "covid 19 nail salon services consent form" is a tool for collecting user information, service preferences, and scheduling details. It helps nail salons manage client interactions and services during the COVID-19 pandemic, ensuring a safe and efficient experience for both clients and staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your user information (name, contact details, and any additional notes)
2.  Select the services you are interested in (Nail Shaping, Nail Coloring, Nail Engraving, or Nail Art)
3.  Fill in your contact and scheduling details (phone number and date/time)
4.  Provide a note about your visit
5.  Read the terms and conditions and select "Yes" to indicate you've read them
6.  Give your consent to receive services from the nail salon

### 1. **User Info** (User Info, text, required: false)
*   Enter your name and any additional notes about yourself.

### 2. **Services** (Services, select_multiple, required: false)
*   Select all services you are interested in:
    *   **Nail Shaping**
    *   **Nail Coloring**
    *   **Nail Engraving** 
    *   **Nail Art**

### 3. **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required: false)
*   Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your appointment

### 4. **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false)
*   Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your appointment

### 5. **Email** (Email, email, required: false)
*   Enter your email address so we can contact you about your appointment

### 6. **Date** (Date, date, required: false)
*   Select a date and time for your appointment

### 7. **Time** (Time, time, required: false)
*   Select a time for your appointment

### 8. **Note** (Note, note, required: false)
*   Enter any notes about your visit

### 9. **Terms** (Terms, note, required: false)
*   Read and acknowledge the terms and conditions of your visit

### 10. **Consent** (Consent, select_one, required: false)
*   Select "Yes" if you give your consent to receive services from the nail salon

### 11. **Have you read the terms and conditions** (Have you read the terms and conditions, select_one, required: false)
*   Select "Yes" if you have read and understand the terms and conditions

## Tips
*   Fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient experience for both you and our staff.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
*   Make sure to review the terms and conditions before giving your consent.
