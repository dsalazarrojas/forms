# Kitchen Vent Filter Recycling Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kitchen Vent Filter Recycling Order Form is used for customers to report their kitchen vent filter recycling orders. This form helps us ensure that we collect accurate and complete information for processing and delivering the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Order Number**. This is a unique identifier for your order.
2. Provide your **Customer Name**. Please enter your name as it appears on your account or invoice.
3. Indicate whether you have a **Filter Model**. Select "Yes" if you have a filter that you would like to recycle, or "No" if you do not have a filter.
4. Select the **Filter Size**. Choose all that apply from the list.
5. Enter your **Shipping Address**. This is where we will send your recycled filter.
6. Enter your **City**. This is where you are located.
7. Enter your **State**. This is the state where you are located.
8. Enter your **Postal Code**. This is the zip code where we will ship to.
9. Indicate whether you have **Filter Materials**. Select "Yes" if you have materials that need to be recycled, or "No" if you do not.
10. Enter any **Special Instructions** you have for your order.
11. Enter the **Total Weight** of the filter you are recycling.
12. Select the **Order Date**. This is the date you would like to place the order for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Number**: Enter a unique identifier for your order. 
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your account or invoice.
* **Filter Model**: Check if you have a filter that needs to be recycled.
* **Filter Size**: Check all that apply from the list.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where we will ship the recycled filter.
* **City**: Enter the city where you are located.
* **State**: Enter the state where you are located.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the zip code where we will ship to.
* **Filter Materials**: Check if you have materials that need to be recycled.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions for your order.
* **Total Weight**: Enter the weight of the filter you are recycling.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to place the order for.
