# Renewable Energy Utilization Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renewable Energy Utilization Report Form is designed to gather information from companies about their use of renewable energy sources in their operations. This information will be used to assess the company's sustainability efforts and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of several pages with different fields. Please follow the instructions below to complete each page:

### Page 1: Company Information
* **Company Information** (`company_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your company.
* **Company Name** (`page_1`, text, optional): Enter your company's name.
* **Contact Information** (`page_1`, text, optional): Provide your company's contact information.

### Page 2: Renewable Energy Information
* **Renewable Energy Information** (`renewable_energy_info`, text, optional): Describe the renewable energy sources your company is currently using.
* **Renewable Energy Sources** (`page_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the specific renewable energy sources your company is using (e.g., solar, wind, hydro, etc.).

### Page 3: Sustainability Initiatives
* **Sustainability Initiatives** (`sustainability_initiatives`, select_multiple, optional): Describe the company's sustainability initiatives, such as reducing greenhouse gas emissions, using renewable energy sources, and implementing sustainable practices.
* **Company Goals** (`company_goals`, text, optional): Specify the company's goals and objectives related to renewable energy utilization.

### Page 4: Contact Information 1
* **Contact Information 1** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information.
* **Contact Information 2** (`contact_info_2`, text, optional): Enter your company's contact information.

### Page 5: Contact Information 2
* **Contact Information 2** (`contact_info_2`, text, optional): Enter your company's contact information.

### Page 6: Submission Details
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **Submission Note 2** (`submission_note_2`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your submission.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or the person responsible for this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
