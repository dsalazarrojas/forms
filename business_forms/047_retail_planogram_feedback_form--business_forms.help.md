# Retail Planogram Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from store staff about retail planograms, which are visual displays of products in a retail store. The goal is to help retail managers understand how effectively planograms are presented and used in the store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the retail planogram and take note of your observations and concerns.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field based on your feedback.
3. Fill in the required fields with your comments and feedback.
4. Submit the form to provide your feedback to the retail manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Retail Planogram Feedback** (`retail_planogram_feedback_form_page_1`): This is the main field where you can enter your overall feedback about the retail planogram. Be as detailed as possible, describing what you liked or disliked about the planogram.
* **Store Layout** (`store_layout`): Describe the store's layout, including the location of the planogram and any other relevant details about how it's presented in the store.
* **Product Placement** (`product_placement`): Select the option that best represents where the products are placed in the store, in relation to the planogram.
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`): If you have any comments or concerns about customer feedback, enter them in this field.
* **Store Staff** (`store_staff`): Select the store staff members who were involved in creating or maintaining the planogram.
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`): If you have any feedback about the store hours, select the time that best represents when you observed the planogram.
* **Retail Manager Name** (`retail_manager_name`): Enter the name of the retail manager responsible for the planogram.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): Enter the email address of the retail manager responsible for the planogram.
