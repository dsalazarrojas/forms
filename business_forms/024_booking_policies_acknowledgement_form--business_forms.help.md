# Booking Policies Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to ensure that customers acknowledge and agree to the various policies related to their booking, including cancellation, refund, payment terms, liability clause, privacy policy, terms and conditions, and marketing communication consent.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field.
3. If you have a **Phone Number**, enter it in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
4. Enter your **Booking Reference Number** in the "Booking Reference Number" field.
5. Select the **Booking Date** from the calendar or enter it manually in the "Booking Date" field.
6. Choose the type of **Service** you are booking in the "Service Type" field.
7. Select one of the options in the "Cancellation Policy" field to acknowledge your understanding of the policy.
8. Choose one of the options in the "Refund Policy" field to indicate your understanding of the refund conditions.
9. Select one of the options in the "Payment Terms" field to agree to the payment terms.
10. Choose one of the options in the "Liability Clause" field to acknowledge your understanding of the liability clause.
11. Select one of the options in the "Privacy Policy" field to indicate your understanding of the policy.
12. Choose one of the options in the "Terms and Conditions" field to agree to all terms and conditions.
13. Select one of the options in the "Marketing Communication Consent" field to decide if you want to receive promotional communications (optional).
14. If you have any **Special Conditions or Modifications**, enter them in the "Special Conditions or Modifications" field (optional).
15. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address that you use to communicate with us.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Booking Reference Number** (Text, Required): Enter the reference number provided to you for this booking.
* **Booking Date** (Date, Required): Select the date of this booking from the calendar or enter it manually.
* **Service Type** (Text, Required): Choose the type of service you are booking.
* **Cancellation Policy** (Select One, Required): Acknowledge your understanding of the cancellation policy by selecting one of the options.
* **Refund Policy** (Select One, Required): Choose one of the options to indicate your understanding of the refund conditions.
* **Payment Terms** (Select One, Required): Agree to the payment terms by selecting one of the options.
* **Liability Clause** (Select One, Required): Acknowledge your understanding of the liability clause by selecting one of the options.
* **Privacy Policy** (Select One, Required): Indicate your understanding of the privacy policy by selecting one of the options.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Select One, Required): Agree to all terms and conditions by selecting one of the options.
* **Marketing Communication Consent** (Select One, Optional): Decide if you want to receive promotional communications by selecting one of the options.
* **Special Conditions or Modifications** (Text, Optional): Enter any special conditions or modifications that apply to this booking.
* **Acknowledgement Date** (Date, Required): This field is automatically populated with the current date.

Please take your time to review and complete the form carefully. Your acknowledgment of these policies is crucial for a smooth and successful booking process.
