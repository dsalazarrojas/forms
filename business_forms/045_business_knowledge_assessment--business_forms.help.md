# Business Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Knowledge Assessment is a self-assessment form designed to evaluate your level of understanding of various business concepts. This form will ask you questions about your knowledge, skills, and competencies in areas such as basic accounting, company strategy, compliance requirements, market competitors, customer service principles, business ethics, and more. Completing this form will help us understand your current level of business acumen and identify areas where you may need further development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. For numerical questions, select a value between 1 and 5.
3. If you are unsure of an answer, choose the option that best represents your current understanding.
4. Please complete the form in one session, if possible, to ensure continuity and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: This is where you enter your name as an employee. This information will help us identify you as the assessor.
	+ Type: Text input
* **Department**: This is the department you belong to. Enter the name of your department.
	+ Type: Text input
* **Years of Business Experience**: This is a number representing the number of years you have been working in the business world.
	+ Type: Number input
* **Level of Basic Accounting Knowledge**: This question assesses your understanding of basic accounting concepts such as profit and loss statements, balance sheets, and budgeting.
	+ Type: Select from: Expert, Proficient, Basic, None
* **Understanding of Company Strategy**: This question evaluates your knowledge of the company's current 3-year plan. Rate your understanding from 1 (little to no understanding) to 5 (very good understanding).
	+ Type: Number input
* **Knowledge of Industry Compliance Requirements**: This question assesses your understanding of compliance requirements such as safety, data privacy, and financial regulations.
	+ Type: Select from: Expert, Proficient, Basic, None
* **Awareness of Market Competitors**: This question asks you to rate your knowledge of the company's top 3 competitors from 1 (little to no understanding) to 5.
	+ Type: Number input
* **Understanding of Customer Service Principles**: This question assesses your understanding of customer service principles that support business objectives.
	+ Type: Select from: Expert, Proficient, Basic, None
* **Knowledge of Business Ethics and Conduct**: This question evaluates your understanding of the company's ethical standards from 1 (little to no understanding) to 5.
	+ Type: Number input
* **Identified Training Needs Description**: This is a free-text field where you can describe any areas where you need more professional development.
	+ Type: Text input
* **Assessment Date**: This is the date on which you completed this assessment.
	+ Type: Date input
* **I Certify that I completed this assessment independently**: This is a checkbox that confirms you completed the assessment without external help.
	+ Type: Checkbox

Please note that this form is designed to be completed independently, without external help. If you need assistance or clarification on any question, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
