# Warehouse Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Check In Form is a tool for warehouse workers to report on inventory levels and provide feedback on stock management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Complete each field accordingly:
	* Enter the current stock levels for each warehouse section.
	* Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if there are any discrepancies or issues with inventory management.
	* Select multiple options if multiple issues exist.
	* Enter any additional comments or feedback you have on warehouse management.
	* Enter any relevant dates and times.
	* Enter any relevant contact information (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **warehouse_check_in_form_1**: Enter the current stock levels for each warehouse section. (Text input)
* **warehouse_check_in_form_2**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if there are any discrepancies or issues with inventory management. (Number input)
* **warehouse_check_in_form_3**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if there are any discrepancies or issues with inventory management. (Select one option)
* **warehouse_check_in_form_4**: Select multiple options if multiple issues exist. (Select multiple options)
* **warehouse_check_in_form_5**: Enter any relevant dates and times. (Date input)
* **warehouse_check_in_form_6**: Enter any relevant dates and times. (Time input)
* **warehouse_check_in_form_7**: Enter any comments or feedback on warehouse management. (Note input)
* **warehouse_check_in_form_8**: Enter any relevant contact information (e.g. email). (Email input)
* **warehouse_check_in_form_9**: Enter any additional comments or feedback. (Text input)
* **warehouse_check_in_form_10**: Enter any additional comments or feedback. (Text input)

## Tips
* Be thorough when reporting on stock levels and discrepancies to ensure accurate inventory management.
* Use the correct format for dates and times (e.g. YYYY-MM-DD for dates, HH:MM for times).
* Keep your comments and feedback clear and concise to ensure effective communication with management.
* Review your completed form before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
