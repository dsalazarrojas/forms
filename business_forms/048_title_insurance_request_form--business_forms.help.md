# Title Insurance Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Title Insurance Request Form is used to request title insurance for a property. It collects necessary information from the client, including their contact details, property information, and payment details. This form helps our team to understand the client's requirements and process the title insurance request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's details, including their name, phone number, and email address.
2. Select the property type (Residential or Commercial).
3. Provide a brief description of the property.
4. Choose the method of shipment (Email, Mail, or Download).
5. Select the type of document required (Title Report or Title Commitment).
6. Add any additional notes or comments.
7. Sign and date the form.
8. Provide the client's statement or remarks.
9. Fill in the title agency name and address.
10. Enter the title agent's name and contact information.
11. Select the payment term (One Time or Periodic).
12. Choose the payment status (Paid or Unpaid).
13. Select the payment method (Cash, Check, or Online).
14. Enter the payment date.
15. Enter the payment amount.
16. Enter the order date.
17. Enter the order number.
18. Enter any additional client information.
19. Provide the title insurance policy details.
20. Select the document status (Draft or Final).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, optional): This section is for the client's name, phone number, and email address.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose between Residential and Commercial.
* **Property Description** (`property_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the property.
* **Shipment Method** (`shipment_method`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how you want the documents to be shipped (Email, Mail, or Download).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Document Type** (`document_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose between Title Report and Title Commitment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, optional): Sign and date the form.
* **Client Statement** (`client_statement`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's statement or remarks.
* **Title Agency** (`title_agency`, `text`, optional): Enter the title agency name and address.
* **Title Agent** (`title_agent`, `text`, optional): Enter the title agent's name and contact information.
* **Payment Term** (`payment_term`, `select_one`, optional): Choose between One Time and Periodic.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose between Paid and Unpaid.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose between Cash, Check, and Online.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the payment date.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of order.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the order number.
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional client information.
* **Title Insurance Policy** (`title_insurance_policy`, `text`, optional): Provide the title insurance policy details.
* **Document Status** (`document_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose between Draft and Final.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can fill in as much or as little information as necessary. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask our support team for assistance.
