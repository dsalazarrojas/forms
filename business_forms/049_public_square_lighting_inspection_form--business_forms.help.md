# public_square_lighting_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The public square lighting inspection form is designed to report any issues or concerns about the lighting conditions in a public square. Fill out this form to provide details about your experience or observation and help the authorities identify and address any problems.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and provide a clear description of your experience or observation.
2. Fill out each text field with relevant information about the lighting conditions.
3. Be concise and accurate in your response to ensure that the information is helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Field 1: Public Square Lighting Inspection Form Page 1

* **Public Square Lighting Inspection Form Page 1** (`public_square_lighting_inspection_form_page_1`, text, optional)
	+ Please provide a brief description of your experience or observation about the lighting conditions in the public square.
	+ Be as clear and concise as possible in your response.

### Field 2: Public Square Lighting Inspection Form Page 2

* **Public Square Lighting Inspection Form Page 2** (`public_square_lighting_inspection_form_page_2`, text, optional)
	+ Provide any additional information or details about the lighting conditions in the public square.
	+ This could include any issues you have encountered or maintenance requests you would like to make.

### Field 3: Public Square Lighting Inspection Form Page 3

* **Public Square Lighting Inspection Form Page 3** (`public_square_lighting_inspection_form_page_3`, text, optional)
	+ If you have any further comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
	+ This could include any other information that you think is relevant to the lighting conditions in the public square.

## Tips

* Please be clear and concise in your responses to ensure that the information you provide is helpful.
* If you are unsure about what to write, provide any information that you think is relevant to the lighting conditions in the public square.
* You can add or remove pages as needed to suit your requirements.
* Do not hesitate to reach out if you need further assistance or clarification.
