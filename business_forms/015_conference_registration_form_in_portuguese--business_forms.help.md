# Conference Registration Form In Portuguese - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for a conference in a Portuguese language setting. It gathers personal and professional details, preferences, and needs to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your complete name (Nome Completo) in the "Nome Completo" field.
2. Enter your email address (Endereço de Email) in the "Endereço de Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number (Número de Telefone) in the "Número de Telefone" field.
4. Enter your organization or company name (Organização ou Empresa) in the "Organização ou Empresa" field.
5. Select your professional title or position (Cargo ou Posição) in the "Cargo ou Posição" field.
6. Choose your country of residence (País) from the dropdown menu.
7. Enter your city (Cidade) of residence.
8. Select the type of registration you are applying for (Tipo de Inscrição) from the dropdown menu.
9. Choose the days you plan to participate in the conference (Dias de Participação).
10. Select your areas of interest (Trilhas de Interesse) for the conference.
11. Choose your dietary preference for the coffee break (Preferência Alimentar).
12. If necessary, specify any food allergies or restrictions (Restrições Alimentares).
13. If you need accommodation, select "Sim Preciso" (Yes I need).
14. If applicable, choose the type of room you need (Tipo de Quarto).
15. Select your transportation needs from the airport (Transporte do Aeroporto).
16. If you want to participate in networking events (Eventos de Networking), select your preference.
17. Choose whether you need a participation certificate (Certificado de Participação).
18. If you have any accessibility needs, specify them in the "Necessidades de Acessibilidade" field.
19. Select whether you want to receive newsletters (Receber Newsletter) for future updates.
20. Choose your payment method (Forma de Pagamento).
21. If you have a promotional code, enter it in the "Código Promocional" field.
22. Finally, confirm that you accept the terms and conditions (Aceito os Termos e Condições).

* **Nome Completo (Full Name)**: This field is for your complete name, as seen on official documents.

* **Preferência Alimentar (Dietary Preference)**: Choose your dietary preference for the coffee break.
* **Restrições Alimentares (Food Allergies or Restrictions)**: If necessary, specify any food allergies or restrictions.
* **Necessita de Hospedagem (Accommodation Needed)**: If you need accommodation, select "Sim Preciso" (Yes I need).
* **Tipo de Quarto (Type of Room)**: If applicable, choose the type of room you need.
* **Transporte do Aeroporto (Airport Transportation)**: Select your transportation needs from the airport.
* **Eventos de Networking (Networking Events)**: If you want to participate in networking events, select your preference.
