# ppe_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ppe_order_form" is used to collect information about a specific PPE order, enabling teams to make informed decisions during the ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed. Some fields are required while others are optional.
2. Enter your details in the provided text fields.
3. Choose from the options in the select_one and select_multiple fields.
4. Ensure you provide accurate information to ensure the order is processed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **PPE Order Form** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is the title of the form, and it is currently set to "PPE Order Form". You can enter any title you like.
* **PPE Purpose** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): This field is intended for the purpose of the PPE order.
* **PPE Date** (`3`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date for the PPE order in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **PPE Time** (`4`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time for the PPE order in the correct format (HH:MM).
* **PPE Phone** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **PPE Email** (`6`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **PPE Comments** (`7`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any comments or notes related to the PPE order.
* **PPE Status** (`8`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the available options for the status of the PPE order.
* **PPE Status 2** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose multiple available options for the status of the PPE order.
* **PPE Status 3** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose multiple available options for the status of the PPE order.
* **PPE Status 4** (`11`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the available options for the status of the PPE order.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all required fields to ensure the order is processed correctly.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification if possible.
