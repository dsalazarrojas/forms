# Microfinance Loan Application And Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for microfinance loan applications and agreements. It is used to gather details from borrowers and lenders for the purpose of loan processing and agreement validation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form information (Page 1: form_information).
2. Provide your borrower's details (Page 2: borrower_details).
3. Specify the loan details (Page 3: loan_details).
4. Confirm the loan terms (Page 4: loan_terms).
5. Enter your borrower's income and loan amount (Page 5: borrower_income and loan_amount).
6. Confirm the loan interest (Page 6: loan_interest).
7. Enter additional loan terms (Page 7: loan_terms_2).
8. Sign as the borrower (Page 8: borrower_signature).
9. Sign as the lender (Page 9: lender_signature).
10. Enter the date and time of the agreement (Page 10: date and time).
11. (Optional) Enter your contact phone number (Page 11: phone).
12. Enter your email address (Page 12: email).
13. Add any additional notes (Page 13: note).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. form_information** (`form_information`, text, required/optional: false): This is the introductory page for the form. Please enter any additional information that you would like to provide.
* **2. borrower_details** (`borrower_details`, text, required/optional: true): Please enter your borrower's details in this field.
* **3. loan_details** (`loan_details`, select_multiple, required/optional: true): Select all options that apply to confirm loan details.
	+ Please select all that apply to confirm loan details.
* **4. loan_terms** (`loan_terms`, select_one, required/optional: true): Confirm the loan terms by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
	+ Confirm the loan terms by selecting 'Yes' if the loan terms are agreed upon.
* **5. borrower_income** (`borrower_income`, number, required/optional: true): Please enter your monthly income in numerical format.
* **6. loan_amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required/optional: true): Please enter the loan amount you are applying for.
* **7. loan_interest** (`loan_interest`, select_one, required/optional: true): Confirm the loan interest by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
	+ Confirm the loan interest by selecting 'Yes' if the loan interest is agreed upon.
* **8. loan_terms_2** (`loan_terms_2`, text, required/optional: false): Please enter any additional loan terms you would like to specify.
* **9. borrower_signature** (`borrower_signature`, text, required/optional: true): Please sign as the borrower.
* **10. lender_signature** (`lender_signature`, text, required/optional: true): Please sign as the lender.
* **11. date** (`date`, date, required/optional: true): Please enter the date of the agreement.
* **12. time** (`time`, time, required/optional: false): (Optional) Please enter the time of the agreement.
* **13. phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional: false): (Optional) Please enter your contact phone number.
* **14. email** (`email`, email, required/optional: true): Please enter your email address.
* **15. note** (`note`, text, required/optional: false): Please add any additional notes you would like to specify.

Note: This form is for microfinance loan applications and agreements. Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
