# Final Grant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for submitting grant applications to the organization. It collects necessary information for the evaluation and review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the application form with your project details.
2. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
3. Provide a brief project summary.
4. Enter your project budget breakdown.
5. (Optional) Select the grant status if you have already received a grant or have one pending.
6. (Optional) Enter a grant amount if you have received a grant.
7. Add any additional notes about your project.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Application Form** (Page 1): This is the main application form where you can provide a detailed description of your project.
  - Type: Text field
  - Description: Enter your project details in this field.

- **Contact Information** (Page 1): Enter your name and contact details here.
  - Type: Text field
  - Description: Enter your name and contact information, including your email address and phone number.

- **Project Summary** (Page 1): Provide a brief summary of your project.
  - Type: Text field with a character limit of 100 characters
  - Description: Describe your project in 100 characters or less.

- **Budget** (Page 1): Enter your project budget breakdown.
  - Type: Number field
  - Description: Enter your project budget.

- **Time Frames** (Page 1): Select a time frame for your project.
  - Type: Time field
  - Description: Select a time frame for your project.

- **Grant Amount** (Page 1): Enter the grant amount you have received (if any).
  - Type: Number field
  - Description: Enter the grant amount you have received.

- **Grant Status** (Page 1): Select the grant status (Awarded, Denied, or Pending).
  - Type: Select one option field
  - Description: Select the current grant status of your project.

- **Notes** (Page 1): Add any additional notes about your project.
  - Type: Note field
  - Description: Enter any additional comments or information about your project.

- **Email** (Page 1): Enter your email address.
  - Type: Email field
  - Description: Enter your email address.

- **Phone** (Page 1): Enter your phone number.
  - Type: Text field
  - Description: Enter your phone number.

- **Submit** (Page 1): Review and submit your application.
  - Type: Text field
  - Description: Review your application before submitting it.
