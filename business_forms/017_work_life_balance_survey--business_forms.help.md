# Work Life Balance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees and managers better understand and balance their work-life balance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant answers for the questions on each page.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to provide clear and concise answers for the comments fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, number, required: false): This field allows you to rate your current work life balance on a scale from 0-10.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, number, required: false): This field asks you to rate how satisfied you are with your job on a scale from 0-10.
* **Work Life Balance Score** (`work_life_balance_score`, number, required: false): This field asks you to rate how important it is for you to have a good work life balance on a scale from 0-10.
* **Life Satisfaction** (`life_satisfaction`, number, required: false): This field asks you to rate how satisfied you are with your life outside of work on a scale from 0-10.
* **Work Satisfaction** (`work_satisfaction`, number, required: false): This field asks you to rate how satisfied you are with your job on a scale from 0-10.
* **Is Work Life Balance Important** (`work_life_balance_important`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks you to select from the list of options how important you think work life balance is to you. Select all that apply.
* **Time Spent Working** (`time_work`, time, required: false): This field asks you to report the amount of time you spend working on a daily basis.
* **Time Spent Outside Work** (`time_life`, time, required: false): This field asks you to report the amount of time you spend outside of work on a daily basis.
* **Work Life Balance Comments** (`work_life_balance_comments`, note, required: false): This field is for providing any additional comments or feedback you have about your work life balance.
* **Work Life Balance Recommendations** (`work_life_balance_recommendations`, note, required: false): This field is for providing any recommendations you have for improving your work life balance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This field is for providing your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This field is for providing your phone number.

## Tips

* Please be honest and clear in your answers.
* Make sure to answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* If you have any suggestions or feedback, please use the comments fields to provide them.
