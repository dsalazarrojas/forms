# Class Proposal Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Class Proposal Template" form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in proposing a class or course to be offered by the institution. Completing this form will help the institution evaluate and review the proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information (Proposer Full Name, Mailing Address, Email Address, and Phone Number) to enable us to contact you.
2. Provide a descriptive title for the proposed class.
3. Choose the correct category for the class (Fitness, Arts and Crafts, Educational, etc.).
4. Describe what will be taught in the class.
5. Select the target age group and skill level for the class.
6. Choose a preferred location for the class.
7. Select the days and time of day you prefer for the class.
8. Choose the duration of each session.
9. Decide on the number of sessions for the class.
10. Specify the maximum number of participants allowed for the class.
11. If applicable, list equipment or materials required for the class.
12. (Optional) Mention any materials or instructor fees.
13. Check if you have relevant certifications for teaching the class.
14. Provide details about your certifications.
15. Confirm if you have liability insurance for teaching the class.
16. List professional references if applicable.
17. Add any additional comments about the proposed class.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Proposer Full Name** (`proposer_name`, text, required): Fill in your complete name as the proposer of the class.
* **Mailing Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your street, city, state, and ZIP to provide a contact address.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Provide your email address for correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Proposed Class Name** (`class_name`, text, required): Enter a descriptive title for the proposed class.
* **Type of Class** (`class_type`, select_one, required): Choose the correct category for the class (Fitness, Arts and Crafts, Educational, etc.).
* **Class Description** (`class_description`, text, required): Describe what will be taught in the class.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select the target age group for the class.
* **Skill Level** (`skill_level`, select_one, required): Choose the required skill level for the class (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, etc.).
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred location for the class (Community center, Park or outdoor, etc.).
* **Preferred Days** (`preferred_days`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable days for the class.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, select_one, required): Choose the time of day for the class (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, etc.).
* **Class Length** (`class_length`, select_one, required): Select the duration of each session.
* **Number of Classes** (`number_of_classes`, select_one, required): Decide on the number of sessions for the class.
* **Maximum Participants** (`max_participants`, select_one, required): Specify the maximum number of participants allowed.
* **Equipment Needed** (`equipment_needed`, text, optional): If applicable, list any equipment or materials required for the class.
* **Materials Fee** (`materials_fee`, number, optional): (Optional) Mention any materials or instructor fees.
* **Instructor Fee Requested** (`instructor_fee`, number, optional): (Optional) Mention any instructor fees requested.
* **Do You Have Relevant Certifications** (`certifications`, select_one, required): Check if you have relevant certifications for teaching the class.
* **Please List Certifications** (`certifications_details`, text, optional): If yes, provide details about your certifications.
* **Do You Have Liability Insurance** (`insurance`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have liability insurance for teaching the class.
* **Professional References** (`references`, text, optional): List professional references if applicable.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the proposed class.
