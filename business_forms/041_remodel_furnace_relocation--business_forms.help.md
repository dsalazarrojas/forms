# Remodel Furnace Relocation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist in the planning and coordination of furnace relocation tasks. It guides users through the process of uploading relevant files, scheduling renovations, and selecting the areas to be renovated.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Upload a file**: Select a file related to the furnace relocation project.
2. **Schedule renovations**: Choose a date for the scheduled renovations.
3. **Select renovations**: Choose the specific area where the furnace will be relocated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload a file**: Upload a file that contains information about the furnace relocation project. This could be a blueprint, a document, or any other relevant file. 
* **Schedule renovations**: Enter the date when the furnace will be relocated. This is likely the date when the actual work will begin. This field is a date type, so ensure you format it according to the correct date format. 
* **Select renovations**: Choose the specific area where the furnace will be relocated. Select one or multiple options from the dropdown list, which includes "Furnace area" and "Other area". This will help in identifying where the furnace needs to be moved.
