# Invoice Search Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users quickly and efficiently search for specific invoices based on several criteria. Enter the relevant details in the fields below to find the invoice you need.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the invoice number to search for a specific invoice.
2. If you know the date range of the invoice, enter the start and end dates to narrow down your search.
3. Enter the customer's name or email to search for invoices associated with that customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Search Invoice**: Enter a keyword or phrase to search for an invoice by its title or description.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: Try to be as specific as possible for better results.
* **Date Range**: Choose a date range to search for invoices between two specific dates.
	+ Type: Date Range
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: You can select any date in the past or future for the start and end dates.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name to find invoices associated with that customer.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: Try to spell the name as accurately as possible for best results.
* **Invoice Number**: Enter the invoice number to search for a specific invoice.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: This can be a numeric or alphanumeric code.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email to find invoices associated with that customer.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: Enter the exact email address used by the customer.
