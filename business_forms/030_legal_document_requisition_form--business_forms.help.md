# legal_document_requisition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The legal document requisition form is used to request legal documents from an organization for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Organization Name.
2. Choose the Document Type.
3. Upload or enter a file if required.
4. Select the Legal Document Date.
5. Provide a brief Document Description.
6. Enter the Organization Address.
7. Enter the Contact Name.
8. Enter the Contact Email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization requesting the legal document.
* **Document Type** (select one, required/optional): Choose the type of document being requested (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **File Upload** (text, required/optional): Upload or enter a file related to the legal document request.
* **Legal Document Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date related to the legal document request.
* **Document Description** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the document being requested.
* **Organization Address** (text, required/optional): Enter the address of the organization requesting the document.
* **Contact Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the email of the contact person.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
- Double-check the document type and date before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or the relevant department for guidance.
