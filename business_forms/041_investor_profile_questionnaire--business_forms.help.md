# Investor Profile Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an individual investor's profile, including their contact information, financial goals, investment preferences, and risk tolerance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact info" page.
2. Select your risk tolerance in the "Risk tolerance" page.
3. Choose your investment preferences in the "Investment preferences" page.
4. Identify your financial goals in the "Financial goals" page.
5. Indicate your relationship with a financial institution in the "Financial institution" page.
6. Select your investment profile in the "Investment profile" page.
7. Choose how often you would like to be contacted in the "Contact frequency" page.
8. Select your preferred communication channels in the "Communication channels" page (optional).
9. Indicate your risk level in the "Risk level" page (optional).
10. Indicate your relationship with an investment institution in the "Investment institution" page (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact info** (Page 1, Required): Enter your name and contact details.
* **Risk tolerance** (Page 2, Required): Select your risk tolerance level (High, Low).
* **Investment preferences** (Page 3, Required): Select whether you are interested in investing (Yes, No).
* **Financial goals** (Page 4, Required): Select all that apply to your financial goals.
*	+ No financial goals
*	+ Save for emergencies
*	+ Wealth creation
*	+ Retirement planning
*	+ Other (Please specify)
* **Financial institution** (Page 5, Required): Select all that apply to your relationship with a financial institution.
*	+ Yes, I am a client
*	+ Yes, I am an advisor
*	+ Yes, I am a financial institution
* **Investment profile** (Page 6, Required): Select all that apply to your investment profile.
*	+ High-risk investor
*	+ Medium-risk investor
*	+ Low-risk investor
* **Contact frequency** (Page 7, Required): Select how often you would like to be contacted.
*	+ Daily
*	+ Weekly
*	+ Monthly
*	+ Quarterly
* **Communication channels** (Page 8, Optional): Select all that apply to your preferred communication channels.
*	+ Email
*	+ Phone call
*	+ SMS
*	+ Meetings
* **Risk level** (Page 9, Optional): Select all that apply to your risk level.
*	+ High
*	+ Low
* **Investment institution** (Page 10, Optional): Select all that apply to your relationship with an investment institution.
*	+ Yes, I am a client
*	+ Yes, I am an advisor
*	+ Yes, I am a financial institution

## Tips
* Please make sure to answer all required fields.
* Select all that apply to your financial goals, investment preferences, and risk tolerance to provide a comprehensive profile.
* Be accurate and truthful in your answers to ensure we can provide you with the best possible service.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
