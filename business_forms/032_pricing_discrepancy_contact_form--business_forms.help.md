# Pricing Discrepancy Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pricing Discrepancy Contact Form is used to report any discrepancies or issues with pricing-related matters within the business. This form is designed to gather necessary information to resolve these discrepancies efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your contact information:
   - Fill out the Contact Number field with your contact number.
   - Enter your Contact Email address in the Contact Email field.
   - Optionally, provide more contact details in the Contact Details field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Form**: This is the main section of the form where you will describe the issue or discrepancy you are reporting.
  - Enter a detailed description of the pricing issue you are experiencing.
  - Be as clear and concise as possible to help facilitate a swift resolution.
- **Attachments**: If you have any supporting files or documents related to the issue, attach them here.
  - Ensure that the attachments are relevant to the issue being reported.
- **Issue Description**: Provide a clear and concise description of the pricing issue.
  - Focus on the specific details about the product and the discrepancy you are experiencing.
- **Price Mismatch**: Enter the price you believe is incorrect or inconsistent.
  - If you're unsure, try to provide the expected price or a range of prices you've seen.
- **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product associated with the pricing issue.
  - This will help our team quickly identify the product and resolve the issue faster.
- **Contact Number**: Enter your contact information so we can get back to you.
  - This ensures we have a way to reach out to you with updates and questions.
- **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about the issue.
  - We will use this to send updates and follow-up questions.
- **Contact Details**: If you have any additional contact information or context, provide it here.
  - This will help us to contact you if needed.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when providing information about the issue.
- If you're unsure about any part of the form, reach out to us for clarification.
- We'll use this information to investigate and resolve the pricing issue as quickly as possible.
