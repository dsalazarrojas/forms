# Weekly Meetings Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from employees about their experience in weekly team meetings. This form is used to collect data on whether employees have attended the meeting and their thoughts on the meeting minutes, action items, and next steps. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you attended the meeting.
2. Enter the date of the meeting.
3. Type a brief summary of the meeting in the Meeting Minutes field.
4. Select any action items that apply to your role or responsibilities.
5. Describe the action items you selected, if desired.
6. Enter any additional comments or next steps that you think are important.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Have you attended the meeting** (`meeting_attended`): This question is used to track attendance. Select "Yes" if you attended the meeting.
* **Meeting Attendee Label** (`meeting_attended_label`): This field is for you to label your attendance, e.g., "As a team member", "As a manager", etc.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_attended_date`): Enter the date of the meeting, using the format MM/dd/yyyy (e.g., 02/15/2024).
* **Meeting Minutes** (`meeting_minutes`): Type a brief summary of the meeting, highlighting key points and takeaways.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`): Select any action items that apply to your role or responsibilities.
* **Action Items Description** (`action_items_description`): Provide a brief description of the action items you selected.
* **Next Steps** (`meeting_next_steps`): Enter any additional comments or next steps that you think are important.
* **Meeting Comments** (`meeting_comments`): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the meeting.
