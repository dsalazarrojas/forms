# Nepotism Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and report instances of nepotism within the company. Nepotism is a form of bias in decision-making that occurs when a person in a position of power favors a family member or friend in their professional decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "employee_name" field.
2. Enter the employee's ID number in the "employee_id" field.
3. Provide the job title of the employee in the "job_title" field.
4. Enter the name of the employee's manager in the "manager_name" field.
5. Select the level of nepotism (First Level, Second Level, or Third Level) in the "nepotism_level" field.
6. Choose the type of nepotism (Nepotism, Conflict of Interest, or Other) in the "nepotism_type" field.
7. Enter the date and time of the incident in the "date_of_incident" and "time_of_incident" fields.
8. Provide a brief description of the incident in the "details_of_incident" field.
9. Enter the email address of the manager involved in the "manager_email" field.
10. Optionally, enter the phone number of the manager in the "manager_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name**: Enter the name of the employee allegedly involved in the nepotism incident.
* **employee_id**: Enter the identification number of the employee allegedly involved in the nepotism incident.
* **job_title**: Enter the job title of the employee allegedly involved in the nepotism incident.
* **manager_name**: Enter the name of the manager allegedly involved in the nepotism incident.
* **nepotism_level**: Choose the level of nepotism: First Level, Second Level, or Third Level. First Level nepotism typically involves a direct report or manager, Second Level nepotism involves a higher-level manager, and Third Level nepotism involves a top-level executive.
* **nepotism_type**: Choose the type of nepotism: Nepotism, Conflict of Interest, or Other.
* **date_of_incident**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **time_of_incident**: Enter the time of the incident.
* **details_of_incident**: Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **manager_email**: Enter the email address of the manager allegedly involved in the nepotism incident.
* **manager_phone**: Enter the phone number of the manager allegedly involved in the nepotism incident.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a thorough investigation.
* Be as detailed as possible in the "details_of_incident" field to help with the investigation.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
