# Production Waste Tracking Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Production Waste Tracking Report Form is designed to help production team members report and track production waste during the manufacturing process. This form will guide you through the process of identifying and documenting production waste, allowing you to focus on addressing and reducing waste in your production line.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Production Waste Tracking Report Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for the following fields:
	* **Production Line**: Choose the production line where the waste occurred.
	* **Production Shift Supervisor**: Select the supervisor in charge of the production line where the waste occurred.
	* **Shifts Involved**: Choose all shifts that were involved in the production process where the waste occurred.
	* **Production Waste Form 23**: Select the production waste form that best describes the waste.
	* **Production Shift**: Choose the production shift where the waste occurred.
	* **Production Supervisor**: Select the production supervisor who is responsible for the production shift where the waste occurred.
2. Fill in the required information:
	* **Shift**: Choose the correct shift for the production waste report.
	* **Production Supervisor**: Select the production supervisor who is responsible for the production waste report.
	* **Team Member**: Fill in the name of the team member who reported the production waste.
3. Provide additional details:
	* **Notes**: Describe the production waste incident in as much detail as possible.
	* **Phone**: If you have any concerns or questions, provide your contact information.
	* **Email**: Leave this field blank if you don't have email.
