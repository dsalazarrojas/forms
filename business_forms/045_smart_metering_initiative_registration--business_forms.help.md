# Smart Metering Initiative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is for completing the Smart Metering Initiative Registration form. The form is used to record participant registration and details for the Smart Metering Initiative program. Please note that this form is for program participants to register and provide necessary information. If you are not a participant, please do not fill out this form. This guide will explain each field clearly, so you will know exactly what to fill in.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **Smart Metering Initiative Registration** form by following the instructions below.
2. Ensure that you have read and understand the purpose of this form before filling it out.
3. Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
4. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Smart Metering Initiative Registration** (Text): This is the title of the form. Please do not change this field.
* **Participant Name** (Text): Enter the name of the participant who is registering.
* **Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the participant. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Address** (Text): Enter the address of the participant.
* **City** (Text): Enter the city of the participant's address.
* **State** (Text): Enter the state of the participant's address.
* **Consent** (Select One): Indicate whether you consent to participate in the program.
* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Title** (Text): Enter your title or position.
* **Department** (Text): Enter your department.
* **Company** (Text): Enter your company.
* **Job Title** (Text): Enter your job title.
* **Additional Notes** (Note): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Participant Agreement** (Select One): Indicate whether you have read and agreed to the terms.
* **Participant Termination** (Select Multiple): Check if you have been terminated from the program.
* **Reason for Termination** (Text): Enter the reason for termination if applicable.
* **Termination Date** (Date): Enter the date of termination if applicable.
* **Program Status** (Select Multiple): Check the status of the program.
* **Additional Notes** (Note): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text): Enter the tool assigned to you.
* **Assigned Date** (Date): Enter the date assigned if applicable.
