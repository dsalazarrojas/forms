# Stock Forecasting Data Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Forecasting Data Request Form is designed to collect information for stock forecasting data requests. The form helps users provide comprehensive data on stocks to aid in making informed investment decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
2.  For fields with options, select the most suitable option that best describes the data you are providing.
3.  If a field is not required, you can skip it if it's not applicable to your data request.
4.  Double-check your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stock Symbols**: This field is required and should be filled with the stock symbol of the company.
* **Forecast Period**: This field is required and should be the period for which you are forecasting the stock data (e.g., weekly, monthly, or quarterly).
* **Urgency**: This field is required and should be marked according to the urgency level of your data request.
*   *High*: The data is urgently required for making investment decisions.
*   *Low*: The data is not urgently required.
*   *Medium*: The data is moderately required.
*   *Very High*: The data is very urgently required.
*   *Very Low*: The data is not required at all.
* **Intended Use**: This field is required and should be marked according to the intended use of the data.
*   *Analysis*: The data will be used for analysis.
*   *Reporting*: The data will be used for reporting.
*   *Strategy*: The data will be used for strategy development.
* **Stock Request Details**: This field is not required and is an optional text area for providing any additional information about your data request.
* **Stock Price Change**: This field is not required and should be marked according to the stock price change.
*   *Up*: The stock price is increasing.
*   *Down*: The stock price is decreasing.
*   *Stable*: The stock price is stable.
* **Stock Volatility**: This field is not required and should be marked according to the stock volatility.
*   *High*: The stock volatility is high.
*   *Low*: The stock volatility is low.
*   *Medium*: The stock volatility is moderate.
*   *Very High*: The stock volatility is very high.
*   *Very Low*: The stock volatility is very low.
* **Stock Forecast Period Start**: This field is required and should be the start date of the forecast period.
* **Stock Forecast Period End**: This field is required and should be the end date of the forecast period.
* **Stock Forecast Period Duration**: This field is required and should be the duration of the forecast period.
* **Investment Team**: This field is required and should be the name of the investment team responsible for the stock forecasting.
* **Analyst Notes**: This field is not required and is an optional text area for the analyst to note any additional information.
* **Stock Symbol**: This field is required and should be the stock symbol of the company.
* **Stock Price**: This field is required and should be the current stock price.
* **Stock Volume**: This field is not required and should be the current stock volume.
* **Stock Open**: This field is not required and should be the opening stock price.
* **Stock High**: This field is not required and should be the highest stock price.
* **Stock Low**: This field is not required and should be the lowest stock price.
* **Stock Close**: This field is not required and should be the closing stock price.
* **Stock Average Price**: This field is not required and should be the average stock price.
* **Stock Beta**: This field is not required and should be the stock beta.
* **Stock Risk**: This field is not required and is an optional text area for the stock risk level.
* **Stock Sector**: This field is not required and should be the stock sector classification.
* **Stock Industry**: This field is not required and should be the stock industry classification.
* **Stock Sector Classification**: This field is not required and should be the stock sector classification.
*   *Industry*: The stock is classified as an industry.
*   *Sector*: The stock is classified as a sector.
*   *Classification*: The stock classification is unknown or not applicable.
* **Stock Market Cap**: This field is not required and is an optional text area for the stock market capitalization.
* **Stock Exchange**: This field is not required and is an optional text area for the stock exchange listing.
* **Stock IPO Date**: This field is not required and is an optional date field for the stock initial public offering date.
* **Stock Status**: This field is not required and should be marked according to the stock status.
*   *Active*: The stock is active.
*   *Inactive*: The stock is inactive.
*   *Pending*: The stock status is pending.
*   *Cancelled*: The stock has been cancelled.
*   *Suspended*: The stock has been suspended.
*   *Unknown*: The stock status is unknown.
* **Stock Type**: This field is not required and should be marked according to the stock type.
*   *Common*: The stock is common stock.
*   *Preferred*: The stock is preferred stock.
*   *Convertible*: The stock is convertible stock.
*   *ETF*: The stock is exchange-traded fund (ETF).
*   *Index*: The stock is index stock.

## Tips

*   Please double-check all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that you have filled in all the fields accurately and completely.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanations above.
*   If you need to request more information or clarification, please contact the administrator.
*   Your feedback on the form is always welcome.
