# HandMade Bag Order Form   Worldpay UK Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place orders for handmade bags and provide payment information. The form is likely used for internal purposes within a business or for external customer-facing interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select the relevant payment information (country, method, and value).
3. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).
4. Choose a payment date and time (if applicable).
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Bag Order Form
* **Bag Order Form** (`bag_order_form`, note, not required): This is the main page of the form where you will enter your order details.

### Payment Info
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, select_multiple, not required): Select your payment country (UK or US).

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, not required): Select whether you have a payment method (Yes or No).

### Select One
* **Select One** (`payment_method_value`, select_one, not required): Select the payment method value (UK or US). This field is redundant with "Payment Method", and you may skip it.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.

### Payment Date
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, not required): Choose the payment date (if applicable).

### Payment Time
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, not required): Choose the payment time (if applicable).

### Payment Value
* **Payment Value** (`payment_value`, number, not required): Enter the payment value.

### Business Name
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, not required): Enter the business name (if applicable).

Tips: 
* Ensure you have all the necessary information before completing the form.
* Double-check your payment information before submitting the form.
* If you encounter any issues, contact the relevant support team for assistance.
