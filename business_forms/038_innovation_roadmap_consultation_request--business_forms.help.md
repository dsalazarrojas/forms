# Innovation Roadmap Consultation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innovation Roadmap Consultation Request form is designed to collect information from end users to facilitate a consultation meeting for potential innovation projects. This form will help us better understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to tailor our consultation to suit your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the **First name** field.
2. In the **innovation_goals** field, describe the goals and objectives of your innovation project.
3. In the **challenges** field, list any challenges or obstacles you are currently facing with your innovation project.
4. Select your preferred meeting times from the **preferred_meeting_times** field (check all that apply).
5. Choose the area of expertise you would like to consult with (e.g., Artificial Intelligence) from the **expert_specialties** field.
6. In the **meeting_length** field, indicate the length of time you would like to allocate for our consultation meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name for our records and reference.
* **innovation_goals** (`innovation_goals`, `text`, required: false): Describe the goals and objectives of your innovation project. Be as specific as possible so we can better understand your requirements.
* **challenges** (`challenges`, `text`, required: false): List any challenges or obstacles you are currently facing with your innovation project. This will help us understand your pain points and provide more effective solutions.
* **preferred_meeting_times** (`preferred_meeting_times`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the times of the week you are available for our consultation meeting. Please select multiple options if you are available at different times.
* **expert_specialties** (`expert_specialties`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the area of expertise you would like to consult with (e.g., Artificial Intelligence, Web Development). This will help us match you with the right expert.
* **meeting_length** (`meeting_length`, `number`, required: false): Indicate the length of time you would like to allocate for our consultation meeting. This will help us schedule the meeting accordingly.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure we understand your needs correctly.
* If you have any additional information or supporting materials, please attach them to the form or mention them in the **innovation_goals** field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us before or after filling out the form.

(Note: The form will be reviewed and contacted with you if any additional information is required.)
