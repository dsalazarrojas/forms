# stakeholder_information_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store stakeholder information for internal purposes, such as maintaining accurate records and meeting regulatory requirements. If you're unsure about this form's purpose, please verify with the relevant teams or stakeholders before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the stakeholder's name in the **Stakeholder Name** field.
2. Enter the stakeholder's email address in the **Email** field.
3. Provide the stakeholder's phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Enter the stakeholder's organization in the **Organization** field.
5. Select the stakeholder's job title in the **Job Title** field.
6. Enter the stakeholder's department in the **Department** field.
7. If applicable, provide the stakeholder's manager's name, email, and phone number.
8. Add any additional notes about the stakeholder in the **Notes** field.
9. Choose the stakeholder's relationship with the organization (e.g., Client, Colleague, Partner, Supplier, Other).
10. Select the frequency of communication with the stakeholder (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, As Needed).
11. Choose the primary contact for the stakeholder.
12. If necessary, select additional contacts (Secondary or Tertiary).
13. Add any other contact information for the stakeholder.
14. Provide any additional comments about the stakeholder.
15. Confirm whether the stakeholder has agreed to the terms and conditions.
16. Sign the form to confirm completion.
17. If necessary, select the form version and date.
