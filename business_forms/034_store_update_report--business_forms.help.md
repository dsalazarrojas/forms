# store_update_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by authorized personnel to update and manage store data, ensuring accuracy and consistency across the system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information:
	* Store Name: Enter the name of the store to be updated.
	* Report Date: Enter the date of the report.
	* Store Manager: Select the store manager responsible for the update.
	* Submitter: Select the person submitting the update.
2. Optional fields are:
	* Location: Enter the location of the store.
	* Time: Enter the time of the report.
	* Sales: Enter any relevant sales information.
	* Profit: Enter any relevant profit information.
	* Notes: Enter any additional notes or comments.
	* Team: Select any relevant teams associated with the store.
	* Email: Enter the email address of the submitter.
	* Phone: Enter the phone number of the submitter.
	* Submitter Location: Enter the location of the submitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Store Name** (text): Enter the name of the store to be updated.
- **Location** (text): Enter the location of the store (optional).
- **Report Date** (date): Enter the date of the report (required).
- **Time** (time): Enter the time of the report (optional).
- **Sales** (number): Enter any relevant sales information (optional).
- **Profit** (number): Enter any relevant profit information (optional).
- **Notes** (note): Enter any additional notes or comments (optional).
- **Store Manager** (select_one): Select the store manager responsible for the update.
- **Team** (select_multiple): Select any relevant teams associated with the store (optional).
- **Email** (email): Enter the email address of the submitter (optional).
- **Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the submitter (optional).
- **Submitter** (select_one): Select the person submitting the update.
- **Submitter Email** (email): Enter the email address of the submitter (optional).
- **Submitter Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the submitter (optional).
- **Submitter Location** (text): Enter the location of the submitter (optional).
- **Submitter** (select_one): Select the person submitting the update.
