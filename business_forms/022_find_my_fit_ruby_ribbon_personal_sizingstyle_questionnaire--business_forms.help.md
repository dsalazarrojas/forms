# find-my-fit-ruby-ribbon-personal-sizingstyle-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The find-my-fit-ruby-ribbon-personal-sizingstyle-questionnaire is a form designed to collect customer information and body measurements for fit purposes. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure the best results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Customer Info** (Page 1)
	* Enter your name, email, and phone number to provide your contact information.
2. **Size Preference** (Page 2)
	* Select your size preference from the available options.
3. **Date of Birth** (Page 3)
	* Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. **Contact Information** (Page 4)
	* Enter your email address.
5. **Body Measurements** (Page 5)
	* Enter your measurements for Height, Waist, Hips, and other relevant body parts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (Page 1, `customer_info`)
	+ (`customer_info` is a text field for your name)
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
	+ (`email` is a text field for your email)
	+ Enter a valid email address.
	+ (`phone` is a text field for your phone number)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Size Preference** (Page 2, `size_preference`)
	+ Select your preferred size (Yes or No).
* **Date of Birth** (Page 3, `date_of_birth`)
	+ Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Contact Information** (Page 4, `email`)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Body Measurements** (Page 5)
	+ (`height` is a number field for your height)
	+ Enter your height in inches.
	+ (`waist` is a number field for your waist measurement)
	+ Enter your waist measurement in inches.
	+ (`hips` is a number field for your hips measurement)
	+ Enter your hips measurement in inches.
* (`bust_measurement` is a number field for your bust measurement)
	+ Enter your bust measurement in inches.
* (`hip_waist_ratio` is a number field for your hip to waist ratio)
	+ Enter your hip to waist ratio as a decimal value.
* (`girth` is a number field for your girth measurement)
	+ Enter your girth measurement in inches.
* (`inseam` is a number field for your inseam measurement)
	+ Enter your inseam measurement in inches.
* (`inseam_hem` is a number field for your inseam hem measurement)
	+ Enter your inseam hem measurement in inches.
* (`inseam_waist` is a number field for your inseam waist measurement)
	+ Enter your inseam waist measurement in inches.
* (`sleeve_length` is a number field for your sleeve length)
	+ Enter your sleeve length in inches.
* (`sleeve_slope` is a number field for your sleeve slope)
	+ Enter your sleeve slope measurement in inches.
* (`shoulder_slope` is a number field for your shoulder slope)
	+ Enter your shoulder slope measurement in inches.
* (`arm_slope` is a number field for your arm slope)
	+ Enter your arm slope measurement in inches.
* (`sleeve_cap` is a number field for your sleeve cap)
	+ Enter your sleeve cap measurement in inches.
* (`sleeve_hem` is a number field for your sleeve hem)
	+ Enter your sleeve hem measurement in inches.
* (`arm_hole` is a number field for your arm hole)
	+ Enter your arm hole measurement in inches.
* (`neck_slope` is a number field for your neck slope)
	+ Enter your neck slope measurement in inches.
* (`neck_hole` is a number field for your neck hole)
	+ Enter your neck hole measurement in inches.

## Tips
* Double-check your input before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the administrator.
* If you're unsure about any measurement, please refer to your local tailor or fashion expert.
