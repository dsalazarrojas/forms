# office-visitor-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about visitors who are visiting our office. The purpose of this form is to ensure that our office is prepared for visitors and to provide a safe and welcoming environment for them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) as you arrive at our office.
2. If you are not sure about any of the fields, you can ask our staff for assistance.
3. Be sure to answer the "visitor-purpose" field accurately, as this will help us direct you to the correct person or area of the office.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Welcome to Our Office**: This field is for the title of this form. Please don't fill it out.
- **Office Rules**: This is a list of rules or guidelines that we follow to ensure a safe and respectful environment in the office. It's required to provide this information. 
- **COVID-19 Precautions**: This field is to provide any relevant information about COVID-19 related rules or precautions.
- **Emergency Contact**: This field is for emergency contact information in case of an unexpected event. It's required to provide this information. 
- **Email**: This field is for your email address. It's not required but it's a good idea to provide it for communication purposes.
- **Visitor Info**: This field is for any additional information about you or your visit. It's not required but it's a good idea to provide any relevant details.
- **Visitor Purpose**: This field is to select the reason for your visit (e.g. Meeting with someone, Tour, Interview, etc.). It's required to select a valid reason. 
- **Visitor Name**: This field is for your name. It's required to provide this information.
