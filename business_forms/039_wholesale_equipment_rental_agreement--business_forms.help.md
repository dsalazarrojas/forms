# Wholesale Equipment Rental Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document the details of a wholesale equipment rental agreement between the equipment supplier and the client. It is a critical document for both parties to understand and agree upon the terms of the rental.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the basic details of the rental, including the rental date, equipment ID, and equipment name.
2. Select the rental term from the options provided.
3. Choose the rental amount and terms according to your agreement.
4. Fill in the client's contact information, including name, phone number, and email.
5. Accept the terms and conditions of the rental by selecting the correct option.
6. Review and confirm the rental contract and terms by selecting the correct options.
7. Sign the form by selecting the correct option.
8. Add any relevant notes or comments to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rental Date** (`rental_date`, date): Enter the date of the rental.
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, number): Enter the ID of the equipment being rented.
* **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`, text): Enter the name of the equipment being rented.
* **Rental Term** (`rental_term`, select_one): Select the rental term from the options provided.
* **Rental Start Date** (`rental_start_date`, date): Enter the start date of the rental.
* **Rental End Date** (`rental_end_date`, date): Enter the end date of the rental.
* **Rental Amount** (`rental_amount`, number): Enter the rental amount according to your agreement.
* **Rental Terms** (`rental_terms`, select_multiple): Select the rental terms according to your agreement.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date): Enter the date of the signature.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one): Sign the agreement by selecting the correct option.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text): Enter the client's name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Enter the client's contact phone number.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text): Enter the client's address.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Enter the client's email address.
* **Terms Accepted** (`terms_accepted`, select_one): Accept the terms and conditions by selecting the correct option.
* **Rental Agreement** (`rental_agreement`, select_multiple): Select the rental agreement terms according to your agreement.
* **Contract Reviewed** (`contract_reviewed`, select_one): Confirm that the contract has been reviewed by selecting the correct option.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text): Sign the form by selecting the correct option.
* **Client Representative** (`client_representative`, select_multiple): Select the client representative according to your agreement.
* **Client Contact Info** (`client_contact_info`, text): Enter the client's contact information.
* **Rental Contract** (`rental_contract`, select_multiple): Select the rental contract terms according to your agreement.
* **Rental Terms and Conditions** (`rental_terms_and_conditions`, text): Enter any relevant notes or comments about the rental terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and understand the rental agreement terms and conditions before filling out this form.
* Double-check your contact information and make sure it is accurate.
* Sign the form by selecting the correct option to confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions.
