# Equipment Retrofit Rebate Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Retrofit Rebate Application Form is a tool designed to help customers apply for rebates on energy-efficient equipment. The form guides you through providing necessary information to ensure accurate and efficient submission of the rebate application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of your project in the **Project Details** field.
2. Attach any relevant project documents in the **Project Documents** field.
3. Enter your and your company's information in the **Applicant Information** and **Business Information** fields, respectively.
4. Enter details about the equipment and its energy-saving features in the **Energy Information** field.
5. Describe the retrofitting process and expected outcomes in the **Retrofit Information** field.
6. Estimate the total project cost in the **Project Cost** field.
7. Specify the project's current status in the **Project Status** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (text): A brief description of your project.
* **Project Documents** (text): Attach any relevant project documents, such as photos or reports.
* **Applicant Information** (text): Enter your name, address, and contact details.
* **Business Information** (text): Enter your company's name, address, and contact details.
* **Energy Information** (text): Describe the energy-efficient equipment and its features.
* **Retrofit Information** (text): Describe the retrofitting process and expected outcomes.
* **Project Cost** (text): Estimate the total project cost.
* **Project Status** (text): Specify the project's current status (e.g., planned, in progress, completed).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Attach any supporting documents to help us assess your project.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact us for assistance.
