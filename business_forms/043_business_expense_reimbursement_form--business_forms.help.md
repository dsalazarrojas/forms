# Business Expense Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about business expenses incurred by employees. This includes details about employee information, expense type, dates, and reimbursement details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Step 1: Employee Information**
	* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official employee ID.
	* **Employee ID**: Enter your official employee ID number.
	* **Department**: Enter your department name.
	* **Direct Manager Name**: Enter your direct manager's name.
2. **Step 2: Reimbursement Information**
	* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this claim.
	* **Reimbursement Period**: Enter the period covered by this claim.
3. **Step 3: Expense Details**
	* **Type of Expense**: Select from the list of expense categories (Travel - Airfare, Travel - Ground Transportation, etc.).
	* **Project Code**: If applicable, enter the associated project code.
	* **Business Purpose**: Describe why this expense was incurred.
	* **Trip Destination or Location**: Enter where the trip was taken or location visited.
4. **Step 4: Expense Breakdown**
	* **Expense Date**: Enter the date each expense was incurred.
	* **Vendor or Merchant Name**: Enter the name of the vendor or merchant where you made the purchase.
	* **Description of Expense**: Describe what was purchased or what the expense was for.
	* **Expense Amount**: Enter the total amount of each expense.
	* **Currency**: If applicable, enter the currency type.
5. **Step 5: Reimbursement Details**
	* **Total Reimbursement Requested**: Enter the total sum of all expenses.
	* **Reimbursement Method**: Select the method of payment you prefer (Direct Deposit, Check, etc.).
	* **Bank Account for Direct Deposit**: If applicable, enter your bank account details.
