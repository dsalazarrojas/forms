# logistics_carbon_report_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The logistics_carbon_report_request_form is used to request a logistics carbon report from the logistics company. This report will provide information about the carbon emissions associated with a specific customer or shipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company (Company)
* **Company** (`Company`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company name in this field.

### Name (Name)
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name in this field.

### Email (Email)
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address in this field.

### Phone (Phone)
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number in this field.

### Address (Address)
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address in this field.

### City (City)
* **City** (`City`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city of your location in this field.

### Zip (Zip)
* **Zip** (`Zip`, `text`, required: false): Enter your zip code in this field.

### Country (Country)
* **Country** (`Country`, `text`, required: false): Enter the country of your location in this field.

### State (State)
* **State** (`State`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state of your location in this field.

### Date (Date)
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date in this field. The date should be in the format of YYYY-MM-DD. 

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact the logistics company for assistance.
