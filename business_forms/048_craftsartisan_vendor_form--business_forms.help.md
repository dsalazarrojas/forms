# craftsartisan_vendor_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The `craftsartisan_vendor_form` is used to collect information from artisans to populate a directory on a business's website. This form helps to provide users with accurate and up-to-date information about local artisans.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Vendor Name and any other relevant information about yourself or your business.
2. Provide your contact Email and Phone number.
3. Enter your Business Name and any other relevant information about your business.
4. Choose the type of business you are (Artisan or Business).
5. Enter your Business Location (city, address, state, postal code, and country).
6. Enter your Business Website and any other relevant information about your business.
7. Provide some information about when you started your business (Vendor Since).
8. Write a brief description about your business (Vendor About).
9. Provide your social media links (Facebook, Instagram, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it is to be displayed on the website.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
* **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): Enter your business name.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required: true): Choose between Artisan or Business.
* **Business Location** (`business_location`, text, required: false): Enter your business location (city, address, etc.).
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required: false): Enter your business address.
* **Business City** (`business_city`, text, required: false): Enter your city.
* **Business State** (`business_state`, text, required: false): Enter your state.
* **Business Postal Code** (`business_postal_code`, text, required: false): Enter your business postal code.
* **Business Country** (`business_country`, text, required: false): Enter your business country.
* **Business Website** (`business_website`, text, required: false): Enter your business website.
* **Vendor Since** (`vendor_since`, text, required: false): Enter when you started your business.
* **Vendor About** (`vendor_about`, note, required: false): Write a brief description about your business.
* **Contact Facebook** (`contact_facebook`, text, required: false): Enter your Facebook link.
* **Contact Instagram** (`contact_instagram`, text, required: false): Enter your Instagram link.

Note: All fields are required for a basic submission, except where indicated as "required: false".
