# Customer Loyalty Data Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to customer loyalty data for specific purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk (*))
2. Select the correct data access type that aligns with your request
3. Choose the correct frequency of access (one time or recurring)
4. Check the box to indicate that you have read and understood the terms and conditions
5. Enter any additional notes or comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that is associated with your work account.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Data Access Request** (`data_access_request`, text, required): Describe the specific purpose of your data access request.
* **Data Access Reason** (`data_access_reason`, text, required): Explain why you need to access the requested data.
* **Data Access Type** (`data_access_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of data you need access to:
	+ Customer Loyalty Data
	+ Customer Contact Information
	+ Customer Purchase History
	+ Customer Loyalty Program Data
	+ Customer Marketing Data
	+ Customer Financial Data
	+ Customer Support Data
	+ Customer Other Data
* **Data Access Request Frequency** (`data_access_request_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select how often you need access to the data:
	+ One time
	+ Recurring
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required): Check the box to indicate that you have read and understood the terms and conditions:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select who will be assigned to handle your request:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`, select_one, required): Select who will be assigned to handle your request:
	+ Admin
	+ Supervisor
	+ Support
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Select who will be submitting the request:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Date the request was submitted in the format (YYYY-MM-DD)
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select who will be assigned to handle your request:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips

* Make sure to read and understand the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have discussed your needs with your supervisor or support team before filling out the form.
* Be clear and concise in your data access request.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or support team.
