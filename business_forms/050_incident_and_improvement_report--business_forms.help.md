# Incident And Improvement Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document incidents and improvements related to your business. It helps to gather information about the client, category, and actions taken to address the incident, including any further actions to be taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant client(s) affected by the incident.
2. Choose the category related to the incident.
3. Provide a date for the category details.
4. Add any notes about the actions taken to address the incident.
5. If necessary, provide further actions to be taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client (client)**:
	+ Select all the clients affected by the incident.
	+ This field is required to ensure that the incident is associated with the correct client.
* **Category (category)**:
	+ Choose the category related to the incident.
	+ This field is required to help categorize and track the incident.
* **Actions**:
	+ Add any notes about the actions taken to address the incident.
	+ This field is optional and provides space for detailed notes.
* **Category Details (category_details)**:
	+ Enter the date of the category details.
	+ This field is required to record the date of the category details.
* **Further Actions (further_actions)**:
	+ Add any further actions to be taken to address the incident.
	+ This field is optional and provides space for detailed notes.
