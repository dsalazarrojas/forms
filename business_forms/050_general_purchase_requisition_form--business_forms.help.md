# General Purchase Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for submitting a general purchase request. The form is required for all purchase requests, and the information provided will be used to process and fulfill your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and detailed responses to ensure the purchase request is processed correctly.
3. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: This field is used to collect information about the user submitting the form. Please provide your name, email, and department.
* **Purchase Information**: This field is used to provide details about the purchase request. Please include the item(s) you are requesting to purchase and the quantity needed.
* **Pricing Information**: This field is used to provide pricing information about the item(s) being purchased. Please include the cost and any relevant details.
* **Additional Information**: This field is used to provide any additional information that may be relevant to the purchase request.
* **Vendor Information**: This field is used to provide information about the vendor supplying the item(s). Please include their name and contact details.
* **Purchase Request Information**: This field is used to provide any other relevant information about the purchase request, such as the reason for the purchase or the expected delivery date.
