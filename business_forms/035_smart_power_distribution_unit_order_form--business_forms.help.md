# Smart Power Distribution Unit Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for ordering a Smart Power Distribution Unit (PDU), a device used to distribute power in a controlled environment. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure we can process your order accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide the name of the person who will be contacting you, in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `yourname@example.com`).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. Please use a standard phone number format (e.g., `123-456-7890`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company or organization that will be using the PDU.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Provide the name of the person who will be contacting you regarding this order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in a standard format (e.g., `123-456-7890`).
