# Monthly Business Expense Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help small businesses track and report on their business expenses on a monthly basis. It allows businesses to input their specific information and track their expenses in a customizable and organized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the expense you are reporting on (e.g. Business Forms, Report Forms)
2. Enter a brief description of the expense (optional)
3. If applicable, enter the Form ID for the expense (e.g. 5341, 65713)
4. Indicate whether the expense was assigned to a tool (optional)
5. Determine whether you want to output a file for the report (optional)
6. Choose the categories you want to report on (optional)
