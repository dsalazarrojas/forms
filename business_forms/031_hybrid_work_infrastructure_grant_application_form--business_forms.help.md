# Hybrid Work Infrastructure Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from organizations applying for the Hybrid Work Infrastructure Grant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

* Read the form carefully and answer all required fields.
* Enter your organization's name in the Organization Name field.
* Provide the name and title of the contact person responsible for the application in the Contact Person field.
* Enter your contact person's email address in the Email field.
* Enter your contact person's phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of your organization as it appears on official documents or business cards.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name and title of the person responsible for this application, such as a CEO or Director of Finance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's work phone number.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that you answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your email address is correctly formatted before submitting.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
