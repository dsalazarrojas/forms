# Client Relationship Audit Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for the client relationship audit process. The form helps to document client details, identify potential issues, and provide a clear understanding of the client's overall relationship status.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Review and Confirm Client Details

*   Ensure that the client's information is up-to-date and accurate in the system.
*   Verify that the most recent interactions and issues with the client are recorded.
*   Confirm that all required fields are filled out.

### Step 2: Complete Form Sections

*   Enter the **Audit Date** (Date of the audit).
*   Enter the **Auditor Name** and **Auditor Department** (responsible for the audit).
*   Select the **Client Company Name** and **Client ID or Account Number** from the database.
*   Identify the **Primary Contact Name**, **Primary Contact Title**, and **Primary Contact Email**.
*   Select the **Services Currently Utilized** (Core Platform, Premium Features, Support Services, etc.).
*   Evaluate the **Overall Relationship Health** (Excellent, Good, Fair, At Risk, Critical).
*   Record the **Client Satisfaction Score** (1-10 scale).
*   Summarize **Recent Interactions** (last 30-60 days).
*   Identify any **Outstanding Issues or Concerns**.
*   Confirm if there have been any **Escalations in the Past Year**.
*   If yes, describe the escalation details.
*   Identify **Growth Opportunities** (Upsell, cross-sell possibilities).
*   Identify any **Risk Factors** that could threaten the relationship.
*   Indicate if the client is using a **Competitor's Product**.
*   If yes, describe the competitor details.
*   Record the **Action Items from the Audit**.
*   Schedule the **Follow-up Date**.
*   Record the **Next Steps** (Immediate actions required).
*   Enter any **Additional Notes** about the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Audit Date**: The date when the audit is performed.
*   **Auditor Name**: The person responsible for performing the audit.
*   **Auditor Department**: The department responsible for the audit.
*   **Client Company Name**: The name of the client company.
*   **Client ID or Account Number**: The client's ID or account number.
*   **Primary Contact Name**: The name of the primary contact person.
*   **Primary Contact Title**: The title of the primary contact person.
*   **Primary Contact Email**: The email of the primary contact person.
*   **Services Currently Utilized**: The services currently used by the client.
*   **Overall Relationship Health**: The overall health of the client relationship.
*   **Client Satisfaction Score**: A scale from 1 to 10 indicating the client's satisfaction level.
*   **Recent Interactions**: A summary of recent interactions with the client.
*   **Outstanding Issues or Concerns**: Any outstanding issues or concerns with the client.
*   **Escalations in the Past Year**: If yes, describe the escalation details.
*   **Growth Opportunities**: Upsell, cross-sell possibilities.
*   **Risk Factors**: Any factors that could threaten the relationship.
*   **Competitor Exposure**: If the client is using a competitor's product.
*   **Competitor Details**: If yes, describe the competitor details.
*   **Action Items from the Audit**: What needs to be done after the audit.
*   **Follow-up Date**: The date for the next follow-up.
*   **Next Steps**: Immediate actions required.
*   **Additional Notes**: Any other relevant information.

### Tips

*   Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   Use clear and concise language when filling out fields.
*   Review and confirm all information before submission.
*   If you need help or clarification on any field, please contact your supervisor or support team.
