# Fuel Expense Reimbursement Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and process fuel expense reimbursement requests from employees. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the necessary information for the employee, such as their name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the business form ID associated with this expense reimbursement.
3. Enter the date of the trip using the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Record the total mileage driven during the trip.
5. Enter the price of the fuel used.
6. Optionally, add any comments about the expense.
7. Select the user who submitted this form from the provided options.
8. Select the date this form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter the name of the employee who is submitting this form. This is the primary identifier for the submission.
* **Business Form ID** (Business Form ID, text, required): Select the unique identifier for the business form associated with this expense reimbursement.
* **Date Of Trip** (Date Of Trip, date, required): Enter the date when the trip occurred using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Mileage** (Mileage, number, required): Record the total mileage driven during the trip.
* **Fuel Price** (Fuel Price, number, required): Enter the cost of the fuel used for the trip.
* **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): Add any relevant comments or notes about the expense.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By, select_one, optional): Select the user who submitted this form from the provided options. This helps track who is responsible for the submission.
* **Submitted On** (Submitted On, date, optional): Select the date this form was submitted. This helps track when the submission was made.

## Tips
- Only one submission per employee will be processed.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If there are any issues with the submission, please contact the administrator for assistance.
