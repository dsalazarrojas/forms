# Resident Complaint Summary Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track resident complaints, ensuring that all relevant information is collected and progress is made towards resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the resident complaint status from the options provided.
2. Choose the type of complaint (Yes/No).
3. Enter the date the complaint was reported (if applicable).
4. Provide a brief description of the complaint.
5. Enter any additional details about the complaint.
6. Enter the email address of the person responsible for addressing the complaint.
7. Enter any follow-up actions taken.
8. Enter any comments from the person responsible for addressing the complaint.
9. Enter any actions taken by the person responsible for addressing the complaint.
10. Enter the updated status of the complaint.
11. Select if the complaint is a priority (Yes/No).
12. Select if the complaint has been assigned to an individual (Yes/No).
13. Enter the email address of the individual assigned to address the complaint.
14. Enter any phone number of the individual assigned to address the complaint.
15. Enter the department of the individual assigned to address the complaint.
16. Enter any designations or titles of the individual assigned to address the complaint.
17. Enter the due date for the complaint resolution.
18. Enter the follow-up date for the complaint.
19. Enter the follow-up time for the complaint.
20. Enter any follow-up notes.
21. Select if a follow-up action is required (Yes/No).
22. Enter the last updated time for the complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. <i class="fa fa-file-text" aria-hidden="true"></i> Resident Complaint Summary Request Form
* **Resident Complaint Status**: Select the current status of the resident complaint. Options: Active, Inactive.
* **Resident Complaint Type**: Choose if the complaint is applicable. Options: Yes, No.
* **Resident Complaint Date**: Enter the date the complaint was reported (if applicable).
* **Resident Complaint Description**: Provide a brief description of the complaint.
* **Resident Complaint Details**: Enter any additional details about the complaint.
* **Resident Complaint Respondent**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for addressing the complaint.
* **Resident Complaint Resolution**: Enter any resolution or outcome of the complaint.
* **Resident Complaint Follow-up Actions**: Enter any follow-up actions taken.
* **Resident Complaint Respondent Comments**: Enter any comments from the person responsible for addressing the complaint.
* **Resident Complaint Respondent Actions**: Enter any actions taken by the person responsible for addressing the complaint.
* **Resident Complaint Status Updated**: Enter the updated status of the complaint.
* **Resident Complaint Category**: Select if the complaint is a priority (Yes/No).
* **Resident Complaint Priority**: Select if the complaint has been assigned to an individual (Yes/No).
* **Resident Complaint Assignee**: Enter the email address of the individual assigned to address the complaint.
* **Resident Complaint Assignee Phone**: Enter any phone number of the individual assigned to address the complaint.
* **Resident Complaint Assignee Department**: Enter the department of the individual assigned to address the complaint.
* **Resident Complaint Assignee Designation**: Enter any designations or titles of the individual assigned to address the complaint.
* **Resident Complaint Due Date**: Enter the due date for the complaint resolution.
* **Resident Complaint Follow-up Date**: Enter the follow-up date for the complaint.
* **Resident Complaint Follow-up Time**: Enter the follow-up time for the complaint.
* **Resident Complaint Follow-up Notes**: Enter any follow-up notes.
* **Resident Complaint Follow-up User**: Select if a follow-up action is required (Yes/No).
* **Resident Complaint Last Updated**: Enter the last updated time for the complaint.
