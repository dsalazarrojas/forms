# Invoice Processing Time Extension Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Processing Time Extension Application Form is designed to allow businesses or individuals to request an extension for invoice processing due to certain reasons. This form is intended to help gather necessary information for the processing and extension of the invoice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your business name in the "Business Name" field.
2. Provide your contact person's information in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Describe the reason for the requested extension in the "Extension Reason" field.
6. Specify the requested extension date in the "Requested Extension Date" field.
7. Specify the actual extension date in the "Actual Extension Date" field.
8. Choose the payment term from the "Payment Terms" dropdown menu.
9. Select the payment frequency from the "Payment Frequency" dropdown menu.
10. Add any additional notes or comments about the request in the "Notes" field.
11. Select the category for the extension request in the "Category" dropdown menu.
12. Choose the assigned tool for the request in the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu.
13. Enter the output file name in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name (text)**: Enter your business name to identify yourself.
* **Contact Person (text)**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for this request.
* **Contact Email (email)**: Enter your contact email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone (text)**: Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Extension Reason (text)**: Describe the reason why you need an extension.
* **Requested Extension Date (date)**: Specify the date you would like to request an extension.
* **Actual Extension Date (date)**: Specify the actual date when the extension will be used.
* **Payment Terms (select_one)**: Choose from the following payment terms: 7 days, 14 days, or 21 days.
* **Payment Frequency (select_multiple)**: Choose how often payments will be made: weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
* **Notes (text)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about this request.
* **Category (select_one)**: Select the category for this request: category 1, 2, or 3.
* **Assigned Tool (select_one)**: Choose the tool assigned for this request: chatjimmy or Other.
* **Output File (text)**: Enter the output file name for this request.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to avoid any delays or complications in the processing of your request.
- If you are unsure about any field or option, please consult with the relevant personnel for assistance.
- This form should only be used for legitimate requests and not for duplicate or unnecessary submissions.
