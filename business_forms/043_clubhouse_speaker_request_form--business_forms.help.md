# Clubhouse Speaker Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a speaker for a specific event or seminar. Fill out the form to provide essential information about the event and what the organizer is looking for in a speaker.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name and contact information of the event organizer.
2. Provide details about the event, including the date, location, and expected attendance.
3. Specify the desired topic, speaker qualifications, and target audience type.
4. Indicate whether you are able to offer a speaker honorarium and budget the amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Organizer Name** (`organizer_name`, required): Enter the name of the person coordinating the event.
- **Organizer Email** (`organizer_email`, required): Enter the email address of the event organizer.
- **Organizer Phone** (`organizer_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
- **Request Date** (`request_date`, required): Enter the date the speaker request was made.
- **Organization or Institution Name** (`organization_name`, required): Enter the name of the school, club, business, or other institution related to the event.
- **Event or Seminar Name** (`event_name`, required): Enter the name of the event or seminar.
- **Proposed Event Date** (`event_date`, required): Enter the date of the event or seminar.
- **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, required): Enter the start time of the event.
- **Speaking Duration Needed** (`speaking_duration`, required): Select the desired duration of the speaker's presentation.
- **Event Location** (`event_location`, required): Enter the location of the event.
- **Preferred Topic or Subject** (`topic_preference`, required): Specify the topic or subject the speaker should cover.
- **Speaker Qualifications or Expertise Needed** (`speaker_qualifications`, required): Describe the qualifications or expertise required for the speaker.
- **Target Audience Type** (`audience_type`, required): Select the type of audience the speaker will be speaking to.
- **Expected Attendance** (`expected_attendance`, required): Estimate the number of people attending the event.
- **Speaker Honorarium Available** (`honorarium_available`, required): Indicate whether you are able to offer a speaker honorarium.
- **Speaker Honorarium Amount** (`honorarium_amount`, optional): If you are able to offer a speaker honorarium, enter the budget for it.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of the provided information to ensure you have entered the correct details about the event and the requirements for the speaker.
- If you are unable to offer a speaker honorarium, you can indicate this by selecting "False" in the `Speaker Honorarium Available` field.
- If you need help or have questions, contact the internal staff or support team for assistance.
