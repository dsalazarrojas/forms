# prophets - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "prophets" form is used to document the destruction of records. This form helps in tracking the type and method of destruction, as well as other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

- Select the **Records Destruction Form**: Choose one of the available options from the dropdown list.
- Fill out the **Destroyed By** field: Select the type of destruction from the list (Electronic Destruction, Physical Destruction, or Other).
- Fill out the **Who Destroyed Records** field: Enter the person or entity responsible for the destruction.
- Fill out the **Date Destroyed** field: Enter the date the records were destroyed.
- Fill out the **Method Destroyed** field: Select the method of destruction from the list (Pulped (pulled apart), Shredded, Burned, or Other).
- Fill out the **Type of Records Destroyed** field: Select multiple types of records destroyed from the list (Paper documents, Electronic documents, Audio/video files, or Other documents).
- Fill out the **Reason For Destruction** field: Enter the reason for the destruction.
- Fill out the **Number of Records Destroyed** field: Enter the quantity of records destroyed.
- Fill out the **Number of Records Remaining** field: Enter the quantity of records remaining.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Destroyed By**: The type of destruction, which can be Electronic Destruction, Physical Destruction, or Other.
* **Who Destroyed Records**: The person or entity responsible for destroying the records.
* **Date Destroyed**: The date the records were destroyed.
* **Method Destroyed**: The method of destruction, which can be Pulped (pulled apart), Shredded, Burned, or Other.
* **Type of Records Destroyed**: Multiple types of records destroyed, which can be Paper documents, Electronic documents, Audio/video files, or Other documents.
* **Reason For Destruction**: The reason the records were destroyed.
* **Number of Records Destroyed**: The quantity of records destroyed.
* **Number of Records Remaining**: The quantity of records remaining.
