# Factory Material Flow Study Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Material Flow Study Request Form is a tool used to request a material flow study at your factory. This form helps gather necessary information to plan and conduct the study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your factory's address in the "Factory Address" field.
2. Provide the contact person's name and email address in the "Contact Person" and "Contact Email" fields, respectively.
3. Describe the objective of the study in the "Objective" field.
4. Select the type of study you are requesting in the "Study Request Type" field. Options include Material Flow Study, Material Flow Analysis, and Process Study.
5. Specify your preferred study date, start date, and end date in the "Preferred Study Date", "Preferred Start Date", and "Preferred End Date" fields, respectively. Note that these dates are not guaranteed and may be subject to change.
6. Select the duration of the study in the "Study Duration" field.
7. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
8. Enter your factory's hours of operation in the "Factory Hours" field.
9. Enter any additional contact information in the "Contact Extension" and "Contact Fax" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Factory Address**: Enter your factory's physical address.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for contact.
* **Objective**: Describe the purpose or objective of the study.
* **Study Request Type**: Select the type of study you are requesting (Material Flow Study, Material Flow Analysis, or Process Study).
* **Preferred Study Date**: Enter the date you prefer for the study to take place.
* **Preferred Start Date**: Enter the start date of the study.
* **Preferred End Date**: Enter the end date of the study.
* **Study Duration**: Enter the number of hours or days you would like the study to last.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email address of the person responsible for contact.
* **Factory Hours**: Enter your factory's hours of operation.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Extension**: Enter any additional contact information (e.g. department or office).
* **Contact Fax**: Enter your factory's fax number.
* **Study Location**: Enter the location where the study will take place.
