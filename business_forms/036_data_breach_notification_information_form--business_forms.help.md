# Data Breach Notification Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about data breaches or unauthorized access to sensitive information, helping to facilitate investigation and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

- Begin by filling out the form completely and honestly.
- Enter your organization's name to identify the affected entity.
- Provide the first name, last name, date of birth, and phone number for each affected individual.
- Enter your social security number (if applicable) for verification purposes.
- Indicate the number of affected individuals involved.
- Provide a detailed description of the breach incident, including the date and time it occurred.
- Select the method of breach notification and the date and time of notification.
- Specify whether the breach has been resolved and provide a date for resolution.
- Describe the resolution process.
