# Customer Lounge Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Lounge Inspection Form is designed to assess the cleanliness and comfort levels of a customer lounge in your establishment. This form helps to ensure that the lounge is meeting your standards and provides a space for customers to provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the customer lounge you are inspecting.
2. Evaluate the cleanliness and comfort levels of the lounge, using the provided scores.
3. Answer the "Overall Experience" question with either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the lounge meets your standards.
4. Provide any customer feedback you have, using the "Customer Feedback" field.
5. Record the date and time you inspected the lounge.
6. Enter the name and title of the person who inspected the lounge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Lounge Inspection** (`customer_lounge_inspection_form`): Enter the name of the customer lounge you are inspecting.
* **Cleanliness Score** (`cleanliness_score`): Rate the cleanliness level of the lounge, using a numerical score.
* **Comfort Score** (`comfort_score`): Rate the comfort level of the lounge, using a numerical score.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`): Answer "Yes" if the lounge meets your standards, "No" otherwise.
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the lounge.
* **Date Inspected** (`date_inspected`): Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Inspected** (`time_inspected`): Enter the time of the inspection in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Inspected By** (`inspected_by`): Enter the name and title of the person who inspected the lounge.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the results are reliable.
* Use the scores and feedback to identify areas for improvement in the customer lounge.
* Review the form regularly to track changes and trends over time.
