# Store Inspection Checklist Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for store inspections, including store details, contact information, inspection schedule, inspection type, store location, inspectors, checklist items, and report comments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields as indicated by the *required* label.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the relevant information using the provided options.
3. Enter any additional comments or details as necessary in the provided text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Details** (`store_inspection_checklist_order_form_page_1`): Enter the name or description of the store being inspected.
* **Contact Information** (`store_inspection_checklist_order_form_page_2`): Provide the contact information for the store or the person responsible for the inspection.
* **Inspection Schedule** (`store_inspection_checklist_order_form_page_3`): Enter the date and time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Type** (`store_inspection_checklist_order_form_page_4`): Select the type of inspection being performed (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Store Location** (`store_inspection_checklist_order_form_page_5`): Enter the location of the store being inspected.
* **Inspectors** (`store_inspection_checklist_order_form_page_6`): Enter the names or IDs of the inspectors conducting the inspection.
* **Checklist** (`store_inspection_checklist_order_form_page_7`): Select the checklist items to be inspected (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Checklist Items** (`store_inspection_checklist_order_form_page_8`): Enter any additional checklist items not listed in the provided options.
* **Checklist Status** (`store_inspection_checklist_order_form_page_9`): Select the status of each checklist item (Option A or Option B).
* **Report Comments** (`store_inspection_checklist_order_form_page_10`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
