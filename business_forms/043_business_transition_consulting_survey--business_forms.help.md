# Business Transition Consulting Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help your organization assess its transition plans, readiness, and potential challenges. Please answer each question carefully to provide a comprehensive overview of your business transition situation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, one section at a time.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields (indicated by *) to provide the most helpful information.
3. Use the hints and options provided to guide your answers.
4. For open-ended questions, please provide as much detail as possible to help us better understand your concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (id: 1, `name: respondent_name`, text, required)
	+ Enter your full name.
* **Your Role in the Organization** (id: 2, `name: respondent_role`, text, required)
	+ Describe your position or title within the organization.
* **Company Name** (id: 3, `name: company_name`, text, required)
	+ Enter your organization's name.
* **Survey Date** (id: 4, `name: survey_date`, date, required)
	+ Enter the date of this survey.
* **Type of Transition Planned** (id: 5, `name: transition_type`, select_one, required)
	+ Select the type of transition your organization is undergoing or planning:
		- Ownership Change
		- Leadership Succession
		- Merger or Acquisition
		- Management Transition
		- Organizational Restructuring
		- Other
* **Transition Timeline** (id: 6, `name: transition_timeline`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose the timeline for the transition:
		- Within 6 months
		- 6-12 months
		- 1-2 years
		- 2+ years
		- Not Yet Decided
* **Overall Organizational Readiness** (id: 7, `name: readiness_overall`, select_one, required)
	+ Evaluate your organization's readiness for the transition:
		- Fully Ready
		- Well Prepared
		- Adequately Prepared
		- Needs Work
		- Not Ready
* **Leadership Continuity Plan** (id: 8, `name: leadership_continuity`, select_one, required)
	+ Assess the level of development of your leadership continuity plan:
		- Well Developed
		- In Development
		- Preliminary Ideas
		- Not Started
		- No Plan
* **Knowledge Transfer Readiness** (id: 9, `name: knowledge_transfer`, select_one, required)
	+ Evaluate your organization's knowledge transfer readiness:
		- Excellent Plan
		- Good Plan
		- Some Planning
		- Minimal Planning
		- No Plan
* **Employee Retention Concerns** (id: 10, `name: employee_retention`, select_one, required)
	+ Consider the risk of losing key staff:
		- No Concerns
		- Minor Concerns
		- Moderate Concerns
		- Significant Concerns
		- Critical Concerns
* **Stakeholder Communication Strategy** (id: 11, `name: stakeholder_communication`, text, required)
	+ Describe your plan for communicating the transition to stakeholders.
* **Cultural Continuity Concerns** (id: 12, `name: cultural_fit`, select_one, required)
	+ Consider potential cultural concerns:
		- No Concerns
		- Minor Concerns
		- Moderate Concerns
		- Significant Concerns
		- Critical Concerns
* **Anticipated Financial Impact** (id: 13, `name: financial_impact`, select_one, required)
	+ Anticipate the expected financial effects:
		- Positive Impact
		- Neutral Impact
		- Minor Negative Impact
		- Significant Impact
		- Unknown
* **Customer Impact Concerns** (id: 14, `name: customer_impact`, text, optional)
	+ Consider the potential effects on customers (not required, but may be important to consider).
* **Regulatory and Compliance Issues** (id: 15, `name: regulatory_compliance`, text, optional)
	+ Identify any regulatory or compliance concerns (not required, but may be important to consider).
* **Key Risks Identified** (id: 16, `name: key_risks`, text, required)
	+ List the primary risks or concerns.
* **Support or Consulting Needs** (id: 17, `name: support_needs`, text, required)
	+ Describe the support or consulting needs you have.
* **Budget Available for Transition** (id: 18, `name: budget_available`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the budget available for the transition (if applicable).
* **Additional Concerns or Comments** (id: 19, `name: additional_concerns`, text, optional)
	+ List any other concerns or comments.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* This form is a helpful tool for us to understand your organization's transition situation.
