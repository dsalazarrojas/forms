# Payment Term Adjustment Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Payment Term Adjustment Application Form is used by customers to request changes to their payment terms. This form collects information necessary for processing payment term adjustments, including customer and vendor details, payment term changes, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information (Customer Name, Customer Email, Customer Phone)
2. Indicate if you would like to make any adjustments to your payment terms (Payment Terms)
3. Specify the reason for your payment term change (Reason for Payment Term Change)
4. Enter the effective date for your payment term change (Effective Date of Payment Term Change)
5. Add any additional notes or comments regarding your payment term change (Notes)
6. Provide your contact information (Customer Rep Name, Customer Rep Email, Customer Rep Phone, Comments)
7. Sign and date the form (Customer Signature, Date)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information**:
	+ Customer Name (text): Please enter your name as it appears on your records.
	+ Customer Email (email): Enter your email address.
	+ Customer Phone (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Adjustment Request** (text): Describe the adjustments you would like to make to your payment terms.
* **New Payment Terms** (text): Enter the new payment terms you would like to implement.
* **Comments** (text): Add any additional comments regarding your payment term change.
* **Customer Signature** (text): Sign and date the form to confirm your request.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date the form is submitted.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time the form is submitted.
* **Payment Terms** (select_one): Indicate if you would like to make any adjustments to your payment terms (Yes/No).
* **Reason for Payment Term Change** (text): Enter the reason for your payment term change.
* **Effective Date of Payment Term Change** (date): Enter the effective date for your payment term change.
* **Note** (text): Add any additional notes or comments regarding your payment term change.
* **Customer Rep Name** (text): Enter your customer representative's name.
* **Customer Rep Email** (email): Enter your customer representative's email address.
* **Customer Rep Phone** (text): Enter your customer representative's phone number.
* **Customer Rep Comments** (text): Add any comments regarding your customer representative.
* **Vendor Name** (text): Enter the vendor name.
* **Vendor Email** (email): Enter the vendor's email address.
* **Vendor Phone** (text): Enter the vendor's phone number.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your payment term adjustments are processed correctly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your customer representative for assistance.
