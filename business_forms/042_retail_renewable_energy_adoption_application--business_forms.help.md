# Retail Renewable Energy Adoption Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Renewable Energy Adoption Application form is designed to collect information from retail businesses interested in adopting renewable energy solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact details, including your name, email, phone number, and email.
2.  Provide your business details, such as business name, address, and type.
3.  Specify your motivations for adopting renewable energy, which can be multiple select options such as Energy Efficiency, Cost Savings, Sustainability, Government Incentives, Community Expectations, and Other (please specify).
4.  Enter your website, fax number, and business nature.
5.  Provide more detailed business information, including number of employees, business owners, and business type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of business you represent, which is a crucial field as it will help in providing tailored support and resources to aid in your transition to renewable energy.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, not required): Enter your name, email, and phone number to get in touch with you.
*   **Motivations** (`motivations`, select_multiple, not required): Specify your motivations for adopting renewable energy, which can be multiple select options such as Energy Efficiency, Cost Savings, Sustainability, Government Incentives, Community Expectations, and Other (please specify).
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address to get in touch with you.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number to get in touch with you.
*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, not required): Enter your business name.
*   **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, not required): Enter your business address.
*   **Zip** (`business_zip`, text, not required): Enter your zip code.
*   **City** (`business_city`, text, not required): Enter your city.
*   **State** (`business_state`, text, not required): Enter your state.
*   **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name.
*   **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name.
*   **Role** (`role`, text, not required): Enter your role in the business.
*   **Department** (`department`, text, not required): Enter your department in the business.
*   **Title** (`title`, text, not required): Enter your title in the business.
*   **Phone Ext.** (`phone_extension`, text, not required): Enter your phone extension.
*   **Area Code** (`phone_area`, text, not required): Enter your area code.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number to get in touch with you.
*   **Fax** (`contact_fax`, text, not required): Enter your fax number.
*   **Contact Website** (`contact_website`, text, not required): Enter your business website.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address to get in touch with you.
*   **Business Nature** (`business_nature`, text, not required): Enter your business nature.
*   **Employees** (`business_employees`, text, not required): Enter the number of employees in your business.
*   **Owners** (`business_owners`, text, not required): Enter the number of owners in your business.
*   **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of business you represent, which is a crucial field as it will help in providing tailored support and resources to aid in your transition to renewable energy.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
*   If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, please do not hesitate to contact us.
*   The information collected through this form will be used to provide you with tailored support and resources to aid in your transition to renewable energy.
*   Please note that the business type is a required field and must be selected from the given options.
*   The other fields are optional and can be filled in as per your business's needs and preferences.
