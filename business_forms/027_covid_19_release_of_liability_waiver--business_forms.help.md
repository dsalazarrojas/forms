# COVID 19 Release Of Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Release Of Liability Waiver form is designed to protect both the participant and the organization from potential liabilities associated with COVID-19 exposure during activities or services provided by the organization. It ensures that participants understand and accept the risks involved and release the organization from any claims related to COVID-19 exposure.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your **Participant Full Name** in the first field. This is your full name as it should be written on any identification or official documents.
*   Provide your **Business or Organization Name** in the second field. This is the name of the organization or business you represent.
*   Describe the **Activity or Service Description** in the third field. This should be a brief explanation of the activity or service you are participating in or utilizing.
*   Enter the **Date of Waiver** in the fourth field. This is the date when you are signing this waiver.
*   In the fifth field, select "I acknowledge" to confirm that you understand the contagious nature of COVID-19 and the risk of exposure.
*   In the sixth field, select "I assume the risk" to confirm that you are aware of and accept the risk of exposure to COVID-19.
*   In the seventh field, select "I release liability" to confirm that you understand you are releasing the organization from any liability related to COVID-19 exposure.
*   In the eighth field, select "I agree" to confirm that you are willing to indemnify and hold harmless the organization from any claims related to COVID-19 exposure.
*   In the ninth field, select "I certify" to confirm that you do not currently have COVID-19 symptoms.
*   Enter the **Emergency Contact Name** and **Emergency Contact Phone Number** in the 11th and 12th fields, respectively. This is the name and contact information of someone who can be reached in case of an emergency involving you.
*   Finally, sign the form by typing your **Electronic Signature (Full Name)** in the last field. This is your electronic signature affirming the completion of this waiver form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Full Name**: Your full name as it should be written on any identification or official documents.
    *   Type your full name in this field.
*   **Business or Organization Name**: The name of the organization or business you are representing.
    *   Enter your business or organization name here.
*   **Activity or Service Description**: A brief explanation of the activity or service you are participating in or utilizing.
    *   Provide a brief description of the service.
*   **Date of Waiver**: The date when you are signing this waiver.
    *   Enter the date using the "Date" format.
*   **I acknowledge the contagious nature of COVID-19 and the risk of exposure**: Confirm that you understand the contagious nature of COVID-19 and the risk of exposure.
    *   Select "I acknowledge" to confirm understanding.
*   **I voluntarily assume the risk that I may be exposed to COVID-19 by participating**: Confirm that you are aware of and accept the risk of exposure.
    *   Select "I assume the risk" to confirm acceptance.
*   **I release the Organization from any and all liability related to COVID-19**: Confirm that you are releasing the organization from any liability related to COVID-19 exposure.
    *   Select "I release liability" to confirm.
*   **I agree to indemnify and hold harmless the Organization from any claims**: Confirm that you are willing to indemnify and hold harmless the organization from any claims related to COVID-19 exposure.
    *   Select "I agree" to confirm.
*   **I certify that I do not currently have COVID-19 symptoms**: Confirm that you do not currently have COVID-19 symptoms.
    *   Select "I certify" to confirm.
*   **Emergency Contact Name**: The name of someone who can be reached in case of an emergency involving you.
    *   Enter the name of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: The phone number of your emergency contact.
    *   Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Electronic Signature (Type Full Name)**: Your electronic signature confirming the completion of this waiver form.
    *   Type your full name to sign the form electronically.
