# Social Media Engagement Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about social media engagement metrics for a given social media platform. The goal is to gather information on the time range, platform name, username, engagement type, engagement level, and other relevant metrics to perform a comprehensive audit of social media engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the social media platform you want to audit from the options listed under "Which social media platform" (Field 1).
2. Choose the time range for your audit by entering the start and end dates in the corresponding fields (Fields 2-4).
3. Enter the name of the platform you selected in the "Platform name" field (Field 5).
4. Enter the platform's username associated with your account (Field 6).
5. Enter the platform's URL (Field 7).
6. Select the types of engagement you want to track (Field 8).
7. Choose the engagement level for your platform (Field 9).
8. Enter the engagement rate and total followers for your platform (Fields 10 and 11).
9. Enter the total views and total engagement for your platform (Fields 12 and 13).
10. Enter the engagement per post for your platform (Field 14).
11. Enter the average engagement time and average engagement days for your platform (Fields 15-16).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which social media platform** (`social_media_platform`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the social media platform you want to audit from the list.
* **Select time range** (`time_range`, date, required):
	+ Choose the start and end dates for your audit.
* **Starting date** (`start_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the start date for your audit.
* **Ending date** (`end_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the end date for your audit.
* **Platform name** (`platform_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the platform you selected.
* **Platform username** (`platform_username`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the platform's username associated with your account.
* **Platform url** (`platform_url`, text, required):
	+ Enter the platform's URL.
* **Engagement type** (`engagement_type`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select the types of engagement you want to track (e.g., Posts, Stories, Videos, Comments, Messages, Reviews).
* **Engagement level** (`engagement_level`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Choose the engagement level for your platform (e.g., Low, Medium, High, Very High).
* **Engagement rate** (`engagement_rate`, number, required):
	+ Enter the engagement rate for your platform.
* **Total followers** (`total_followers`, number, required):
	+ Enter the total number of followers for your platform.
* **Total views** (`total_views`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the total number of views for your platform.
* **Total engagement** (`total_engagement`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the total engagement for your platform.
* **Engagement per post** (`engagement_per_post`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the engagement per post for your platform.
* **Average engagement time** (`average_engagement_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the average engagement time for your platform.
* **Average engagement days** (`average_engagement_days`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the average engagement days for your platform.
