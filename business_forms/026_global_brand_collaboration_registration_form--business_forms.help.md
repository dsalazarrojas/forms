# global_brand_collaboration_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The global brand collaboration registration form is a tool for employees to submit requests for collaboration with internal or external brands.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Brand Name field with the name of the brand you're requesting collaboration with.
2. Enter the contact person's name and email in the Contact Person and Email fields respectively.
3. Enter the contact person's phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select the Collaboration Type from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter the Start and End Dates of the collaboration.
6. If applicable, add any additional notes in the Notes field.
7. Select the Assigned Tool.
8. Enter the Category and Sub Category of the collaboration.
9. Add any relevant Tags.
10. Select the collaborators and their email addresses.
11. Submit the form once you've reviewed and filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Brand Name** (`brand_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the brand you're requesting collaboration with. This field is mandatory.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person for the brand.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Collaboration Type** (`collaboration_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of collaboration. This can be one-time, ongoing, or other.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the collaboration.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the collaboration.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about the collaboration.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Select the assigned tool for the collaboration.
* **Category** (`category`, `text`, required): Enter the category of the collaboration.
* **Sub Category** (`sub_category`, `text`, required): Enter the sub category of the collaboration.
* **Tags** (`tags`, `text`, required): Add any relevant tags for the collaboration.
* **Collaborators** (`collaborators`, `text`, required): Select the collaborators involved in the collaboration.
* **Collaborators Email** (`collaborators_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email addresses of the collaborators.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person or team you're submitting the collaboration request to.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, `time`, required): Enter the time you're submitting the collaboration request.
* **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, `text`, required): Select the team member assigned to manage the collaboration.
* **Assigned At** (`assigned_at`, `time`, required): Enter the time the collaboration was assigned to.
* **Other (Collaboration Type)** (`collaboration_type_other`, `text`, optional): If you selected other as the collaboration type, enter a brief description of the type.
* **One-Time Collaboration** (`collaboration_type_one_time`, `select_one`, optional): Select yes or no for one-time collaboration.
* **Ongoing Collaboration** (`collaboration_type_ongoing`, `select_one`, optional): Select yes or no for ongoing collaboration.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): Select the assigned tool for the collaboration.

Note that all fields with an asterisk (\*) mark are required fields. 

## Tips
* Ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double check your email addresses for correctness before submitting.
* Be clear and concise in your submission, especially in the collaboration type and assigned tool fields. 
* If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to reach out to your team lead or assigned team member for clarification.
