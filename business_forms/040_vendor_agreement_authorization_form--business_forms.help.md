# Vendor Agreement Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Agreement Authorization Form is used to collect information about a vendor's agreement with the company. This form is designed to gather essential details such as the vendor's name, agreement type, start and end dates, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields for your vendor agreement.
2. Enter the vendor's name and details in the designated text fields.
3. Choose the agreement type (Yes or No).
4. Enter the start and end dates of the agreement.
5. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Agreement Form** (`vendor_agreement_form`, text, optional): This field is for entering a brief description or notes about the vendor's agreement. Enter any relevant details about the agreement, such as the purpose or scope of the agreement.
* **Vendor Details** (`vendor_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the vendor, such as their name, address, or contact details.
* **Agreement Type** (`agreement_type`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the agreement is 'Yes' or 'No' (e.g., yes to a contract or no to a contract). Select 'Yes' if the agreement is in place, and 'No' if it is not.
* **Agreement Start Date** (`agreement_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the agreement, using the standard date format.
* **Agreement End Date** (`agreement_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the agreement, using the standard date format.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you need to add or edit vendor agreements, contact the relevant department for assistance.
