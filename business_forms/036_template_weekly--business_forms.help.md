# Template Weekly - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Template Weekly" form is a collection of various fields used to gather information about businesses. This form can be used for internal purposes, such as generating reports, or for external use, such as data collection for research or business analysis.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the business from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the name of the business.
3. Enter any relevant questions and answers (select multiple options for question 1 and question 5).
4. Enter the prices (numerical values) for price1, price2, price3, and price4.
5. Enter any other relevant business details, such as email, phone numbers, dates, and times.
6. Add any additional notes (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (category)**: Choose the category that best describes the business.
* **Business name (name)**: Enter the name of the business.
* **Question 1 (question1)**: Select one or more options to answer this question (multiple selection allowed).
* **Price 1 (price1)**: Enter the numerical value of price 1.
* **Question 2 (question2)**: Enter the answer to this question (text).
* **Price 2 (price2)**: Enter the numerical value of price 2.
* **Business type (business_type)**: Choose the type of business (optional).
* **Email (email)**: Enter the business's email address (optional).
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter the business's phone number (optional).
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date (optional).
* **Time (time)**: Enter the time (optional).
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the business (optional).
* **Question 3 (question3)**: Enter the answer to this question (text).
* **Price 3 (price3)**: Enter the numerical value of price 3.
* **Question 4 (question4)**: Enter the answer to this question (text).
* **Price 4 (price4)**: Enter the numerical value of price 4.
* **Question 5 (question5)**: Select one or more options to answer this question (multiple selection allowed).
* **Business name (business_name)**: Enter the name of the business (optional).
* **Business address (business_address)**: Enter the business's address (optional).
* **Employee name (employee_name)**: Enter the name of the employee (optional).
* **Employee name 2 (employee_name2)**: Enter the name of the second employee (optional).
* **Business phone (business_phone)**: Enter the business's phone number (optional).
* **Business phone 2 (business_phone2)**: Enter the business's second phone number (optional).
* **Business email (business_email)**: Enter the business's email address (optional).
