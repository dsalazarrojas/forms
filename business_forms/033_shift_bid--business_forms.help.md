# Shift Bid - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shift Bid form is used to submit bids on available shifts in the company's scheduling system. This form helps employees express their availability and preferences for shifts they can work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Select the available dates you are able to work in the "Available Dates" field.
3. Choose the shifts you are available to work in the "Preferred Hours" field.
4. Enter the job code for the shifts you are interested in in the "Job Code" field.
5. Select the shift code for the shift you are bidding on in the "Shift Code" field.
6. Briefly describe your shift preferences in the "Shift Description" field.
7. Enter the shift rate you expect to earn in the "Shift Rate" field.
8. Select the number of hours you are available to work in the "Available Hours" field.
9. Choose the priority level of the shift you are bidding on in the "Shift Priority" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Available Dates** (date): Select one or multiple dates you are available to work.
* **Preferred Hours** (select_multiple): Choose the shifts you are available to work, such as "Shift 1", "Shift 2", "Shift 3".
* **Job Code** (text): Enter the job code for the shifts you are interested in.
* **Shift Code** (select_one): Select the shift code "S1", "S2", "S3" for the shift you are bidding on.
* **Shift Description** (text): Briefly describe your shift preferences.
* **Shift Rate** (number): Enter the shift rate you expect to earn.
* **Available Hours** (text): Select the number of hours you are available to work.
* **Shift Priority** (select_one): Choose the priority level of the shift you are bidding on, such as "High", "Medium", "Low".
* **Employee Name** (text): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number.
