# Event Marketing Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Marketing Consultation Form is designed to collect information about a potential or ongoing event, allowing us to tailor our approach and provide effective consultation to our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the client.
2. Describe your business need for the event.
3. Select the type of event you are planning (e.g., meeting, webinar, seminar, conference, or trade show).
4. Choose your preferred communication channel for consultation (e.g., email, phone, video call, or in-person).
5. Specify your preferences for follow-up communication (e.g., email, phone, video call, or in-person).
6. Provide any additional next steps or details about the event.
7. Enter the name of the person you would like the event to be assigned to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (1)**: Enter your name as the client.
* **Business Need (2)**: Describe the purpose or goal of the event, such as a product launch or team-building activity.
* **Event Type (3)**: Select the type of event you are planning, such as a meeting, webinar, seminar, conference, or trade show.
* **Preferred Communication Channel (4)**: Choose how you would like to communicate with us about the event, such as via email, phone, or video call.
* **Follow-up Preference (5)**: Specify how you would like to be contacted for follow-up communication, such as via email, phone, or video call.
* **Next Steps (6)**: Provide any additional information or next steps you would like us to take regarding the event.
* **Assigned To (7)**: Enter the name of the person you would like the event to be assigned to.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when filling in the "Business Need" field, as this helps us understand your requirements and provide better consultation.
* If you have any specific preferences or requirements for communication, please specify them in the "Preferred Communication Channel" and "Follow-up Preference" fields.
* For the "Next Steps" field, you can provide any relevant information or deadlines that we need to be aware of.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, feel free to ask, and we'll be happy to assist you.
