# Customer Incident Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Incident Reporting Form is a tool designed to collect detailed information about incidents reported by customers. This form helps business or technical teams to track and address customer complaints efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the form with as much detail as possible about the incident.
2.  Select the correct options for the incident category, type, and priority.
3.  Choose the status of the incident (Open, Closed, or Pending).
4.  Provide any necessary feedback or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Customer Info (id: 1)**: Enter your name and contact information.
*   **Incident Date (id: 2)**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Time (id: 3)**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Description (id: 4)**: Provide a detailed description of what happened.
*   **Incident Details (id: 5)**: Add any additional information about the incident.
*   **Incident Cause (id: 6)**: Identify the reason for the incident.
*   **Incident Effects (id: 7)**: Describe how the incident affected you.
*   **Incident Location (id: 8)**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Resolution (id: 9)**: Explain how the incident was resolved (if applicable).
*   **Incident Status (id: 10)**: Select the current status of the incident (Open, Closed, or Pending).
*   **Customer Feedback (id: 11)**: Share any feedback or comments about the incident.
*   **Incident Manager (id: 12)**: Enter the name of the manager responsible for the incident.
*   **Report Status (id: 13)**: Enter the status of your report.
*   **Customer Comments (id: 14)**: Add any additional comments or information.
*   **Incident Priority (id: 15)**: Categorize the incident as High, Medium, or Low.
*   **Incident Type (id: 16)**: Select the type of incident (Hardware, Software, Network).
*   **Incident Category (id: 17)**: Choose a category for the incident (Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
*   **Incident Tags (id: 18)**: Add any relevant tags or keywords.
*   **Incident Reporter (id: 19)**: Enter your name as the reporter.
*   **Customer Contact Info (id: 20)**: Enter your contact information.
*   **Incident Note (id: 21)**: Add any additional notes.
*   **Manager Note (id: 22)**: Add any notes from the manager.
*   **Resolution Date (id: 23)**: Enter the date the incident was resolved.
*   **Resolution Time (id: 24)**: Enter the time the incident was resolved.
*   **Report Date (id: 25)**: Enter the date the report was submitted.
*   **Report Time (id: 26)**: Enter the time the report was submitted.
