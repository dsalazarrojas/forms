# Appraisal Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Appraisal Waiver Form is used to record a waiver of a physical property inspection, providing an alternative method for evaluating and pricing a property. This form captures details about the property, owner information, and the reason for waiving the physical inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in the property address and city**.
2. **Enter the owner's name and email address**. Please ensure these fields are accurate to ensure proper communication.
3. **Select the type of waiver** you are requesting and provide a brief reason for the waiver.
4. **Enter the waiver effective date** when the waiver will take effect.
5. **Confirm your understanding of the implications** of waiving the physical inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details** (`Property Details`, `note`, optional): This field provides a space for any additional information about the property that may be relevant to the waiver request.
* **Property Address** (`Property Address`, `text`, required): Enter the property's address in the correct format (e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, USA).
* **City** (`City`, `text`, required): Enter the city where the property is located.
* **County** (`County`, `text`, optional): Enter the county where the property is located (if applicable).
* **Property Owner Information** (`Property Owner Information`, `note`, optional): This field can be used to provide any relevant information about the property owner.
* **Owner Name** (`Owner Name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the property owner.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter the property owner's email address to ensure proper communication.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter the property owner's phone number.
* **Appraisal Waiver Details** (`Appraisal Waiver Details`, `note`, optional): This field can be used to provide any additional context or information about the waiver request.
* **Type of Waiver** (`Type of Waiver`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Physical Appraisal Not Required
	+ Interior Inspection Waived
	+ Exterior Inspection Waived
	+ Full Waiver
* **Reason for Waiver** (`Reason for Waiver`, `text`, required): Enter a brief explanation for the waiver request.
* **Waiver Effective Date** (`Waiver Effective Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the waiver will take effect.
* **I understand the implications of this waiver** (`I understand the implications of this waiver`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the implications of waiving the physical inspection.
