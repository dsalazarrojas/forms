# Brand Strategy Advisory Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Brand Strategy Advisory Contact Form is designed to collect information about your brand's needs and challenges, and to connect you with our experienced brand strategists for guidance on how to address those challenges. This form will help us understand your priorities and tailor our advice to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form with accurate and honest information about your brand.
*   Make sure to complete all required fields.
*   Select all applicable options for each dropdown field.
*   If you have any questions or need further clarification, don't hesitate to reach out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your business cards or official documents.
*   **Company Name** (`company_name_val`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company.
*   **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your company email address where we can contact you.
*   **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your primary phone number.
*   **Briefly describe your current brand situation** (`brand_status_desc`, `text`, required): Describe your current brand state, including challenges you're facing.
*   **Primary brand challenges** (`challenges_select`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the challenges you're currently facing.
*   **Top 3 goals for a brand strategy advisory** (`goal_list`, `text`, required): List your top 3 goals for the brand strategy advisory.
*   **Approximate Annual Revenue** (`revenue_range`, `number`, optional): Provide an estimate of your annual revenue.
*   **Desired Timeframe for Implementation** (`urgency_select`, `select_one`, required): Choose when you'd like to start working on your brand strategy.
*   **Are you ready to book a 15-minute discovery call?** (`call_interest_select`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your interest in booking a discovery call.
*   **Anything else we should know before we talk?** (`extra_context`, `text`, optional): Share any additional context or information that might help us better understand your needs.
*   **Date of Inquiry** (`inq_dt`, `date`, required): Provide the date you're inquiring about this service.

## Tips

*   The more information you provide, the better we can serve you.
*   Be as specific as possible in your answers, as this will help us tailor our advice to your needs.
*   If you have any questions or need further guidance, don't hesitate to reach out.
