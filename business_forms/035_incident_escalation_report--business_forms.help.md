# Incident Escalation Report - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Incident Escalation Report form is used to document and report incidents that have been escalated within the organization. It helps in tracking and analyzing incidents, and ensuring that escalation processes are followed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the following fields:
	* **Incident Id**: Enter the unique identifier for the incident.
	* **Date of incident**: Enter the date of the incident.
	* **Time of incident**: Enter the time of the incident.
	* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident.
	* **Incident type**: Choose whether the incident was active or inactive.
	* **Incident category**: Choose the category for the incident (multiple choices can be selected).
	* **Assigned to**: Choose who the incident was assigned to.
	* **Incident status**: Choose the status for the incident (active or inactive).
	* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the incident.
	* **Escalation date**: Enter the date the incident was escalated.
	* **Escalation time**: Enter the time the incident was escalated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Id** (`incident_id`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the incident.
* **Date of incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Time of incident** (`time_of_incident`, time, required): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): Choose whether the incident was active or inactive.
* **Incident category** (`incident_category`, select_multiple, required): Choose the category for the incident (multiple choices can be selected).
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Choose who the incident was assigned to.
* **Incident status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status for the incident (active or inactive).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes about the incident.
* **Escalation date** (`escalation_date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident was escalated.
* **Escalation time** (`escalation_time`, time, required): Enter the time the incident was escalated.

Note: The fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled in for the form to be considered complete.
