# Establishment Configuration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Establishment Configuration Survey form is designed to collect essential information about a business, including its location, operating hours, and service offerings. This information will help surveyors conduct surveys accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form carefully**: Take your time to fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. **Provide accurate information**: Make sure to enter accurate information for each field to ensure that the survey is conducted correctly.
3. **Use the provided options**: Select the correct options for each field from the available choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the establishment.
* **Location Address** (`location_address`, text, required): Enter the physical address of the business.
* **Operating Hours** (`operating_hours`, date, required): Enter the operating hours of the business. This field accepts date format.
* **Operating Days** (`operating_days`, select_one, required): Select the days the business operates.
* **Operating Start Time** (`operating_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of business operations.
* **Operating End Time** (`operating_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of business operations.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the business.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Service Requirements** (`service_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any specific service requirements of the business.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of business (Retail, Restaurant, Service, or Other).
* **Services Offered** (`services`, select_multiple, required): Select the services offered by the business (delivery, pickup, cash, credit).
* **Business Hours** (`business_hours`, text, optional): Enter any additional operating hours information.
* **Parking** (`parking`, text, optional): Enter parking information for the business.
* **Accessibility** (`accessibility`, text, optional): Enter accessibility information for the business.
* **Business Notes** (`business_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional business notes.
* **Franchise Name** (`franchise_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the franchise or chain the business belongs to.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact Notes** (`contact_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional contact notes.
* **Surveyor Notes** (`surveyor_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional surveyor notes.
* **Surveyor Comments** (`surveyor_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional surveyor comments.
* **Services 2** (`services_2`, select_multiple, required): Select additional services offered by the business (delivery, pickup, cash, credit).
* **Business Hours 2** (`business_hours_2`, text, optional): Enter additional operating hours information.
* **Services 3** (`services_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select additional services offered by the business (cash, credit).

Note: Please ensure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely to ensure correct survey results.
