# Digital Marketing Campaign Quotation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Marketing Campaign Quotation Form is used to collect client-specific information for each digital marketing campaign quotation. This information will help the agency's project team to create a tailored proposal for the client.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the client's name, email, and contact number in the **Client Information** section. 
2. Select the services required for the project in the **Services** section. 
3. Enter a budget for the project if applicable. 
4. Specify the proposal date and any additional project details in the **Project Details** section. 
5. Optionally add client comments or any other relevant information for the agency team to review. 
6. Provide your agency's name and address in the **Agency Information** section. 
7. Enter your agency's phone number and email address. 
8. Assign a project manager and account manager for the project if applicable. 
9. Provide the client contact person's name and contact information. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information**: Enter the client's name, email, and contact number to ensure that the agency team can contact the client effectively.
* **Services**: Select the services required for the project from the dropdown menu. This will help the agency team to create a comprehensive proposal.
* **Budget**: If a budget is required, enter it here. Please be as specific as possible to help the agency team in providing an accurate quote.
* **Proposal Date**: Specify the date when the agency plans to submit the proposal. 
* **Project Details**: Enter a brief description of the project, including any specific details that the agency team needs to know to complete it.
* **Client Comments**: Optionally add any relevant comments or information that the client would like to include in the proposal.
* **Agency Name**: Enter the name of your agency.
* **Agency Address**: Enter the physical address of your agency.
* **Agency Phone**: Enter your agency's phone number.
* **Agency Email**: Enter your agency's email address.
* **Project Manager**: If a project manager is assigned, enter their name here.
* **Account Manager**: If an account manager is assigned, enter their name here.
* **Sales Manager**: If a sales manager is assigned, enter their name here.
* **Client Contact**: Enter the client's contact person's name and contact information.
