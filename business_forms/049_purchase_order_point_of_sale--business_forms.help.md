# Purchase Order Point Of Sale - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Purchase Order Point Of Sale form is designed to collect all the necessary information for a single purchase order from a customer. It includes fields for customer details, product information, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter the customer's information in the "customer_info" field.
2. Enter the customer's name in the "customer_name" field.
3. Enter the customer's email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter the customer's phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter a brief description of the product or service being purchased in the "purchase_order_info" field.
6. Enter the name of the product being purchased in the "product" field.
7. Enter the price of the product in the "product_price" field.
8. Enter the quantity of the product being purchased in the "quantity" field.
9. Enter any additional payment details, such as payment method or terms, in the "payment" field.
10. Enter the total amount due for the purchase in the "purchase_order_total" field.
11. Enter any additional notes or comments about the order in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_info** (text): Enter a brief description of the customer's information, such as their business or address.
* **customer_name** (text): Enter the name of the customer.
* **email** (email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **phone** (text): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **purchase_order_info** (text): Enter a brief description of the product or service being purchased.
* **product** (text): Enter the name of the product or service being purchased.
* **product_price** (number): Enter the price of the product or service.
* **quantity** (number): Enter the quantity of the product or service being purchased.
* **payment** (text): Enter any additional payment details, such as payment method or terms.
* **purchase_order_total** (number): Enter the total amount due for the purchase.
* **notes** (text): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
