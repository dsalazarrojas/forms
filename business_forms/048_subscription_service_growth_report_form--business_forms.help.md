# Subscription Service Growth Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subscription Service Growth Report Form is used to collect and analyze data about subscription services, including user engagement, customer retention, and billing details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields for your subscription service.
2. Enter the necessary information for each field, such as user engagement, subscription status, and billing details.
3. Use the options provided for each field to select the most accurate information.
4. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the user input or engagement.
* **User Engagement** (`user_engagement`, text, optional): Enter details about user interactions, such as sign-ups, logins, etc.
* **Customer Retention** (`customer_retention`, text, optional): Enter information about customer retention, such as repeat business or loyalty programs.
* **Subscription Status** (`subscription_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Active**: The subscription is currently active.
	+ **Inactive**: The subscription is inactive or paused.
	+ **Cancelled**: The subscription has been cancelled.
* **Subscription Start Date** (`subscription_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the subscription.
* **Subscription End Date** (`subscription_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the subscription.
* **Subscription Duration** (`subscription_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the subscription in days or months.
* **Billing Cycle** (`billing_cycle`, text, optional): Enter the billing cycle or frequency.
* **Billing Frequency** (`billing_frequency`, text, optional): Enter the billing frequency or cycle.
* **Subscription Type** (`subscription_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Monthly**: The subscription is monthly-based.
	+ **Yearly**: The subscription is yearly-based.
	+ **Quarterly**: The subscription is quarterly-based.
* **Billing Status** (`billing_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Paid**: The billing is paid.
	+ **Unpaid**: The billing is unpaid.
	+ **Pending**: The billing status is pending.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Credit Card**: The payment method is credit card.
	+ **Bank Transfer**: The payment method is bank transfer.
	+ **PayPal**: The payment method is PayPal.
* **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`, text, optional): Enter the subscription plan details.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter the billing address.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Enter the payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Active**: The payment status is active.
	+ **Inactive**: The payment status is inactive.
	+ **Cancelled**: The payment status is cancelled.
* **Subscription Start Date Range** (`subscription_start_date_range`, text, optional): Enter a range of start dates for the subscription.
* **Subscription End Date Range** (`subscription_end_date_range`, text, optional): Enter a range of end dates for the subscription.
* **Billing Cycle Range** (`billing_cycle_range`, text, optional): Enter a range of billing cycles.
* **Billing Frequency Range** (`billing_frequency_range`, text, optional): Enter a range of billing frequencies.
* **Payment Terms Range** (`payment_terms_range`, text, optional): Enter a range of payment terms.
* **Payment Status Range** (`payment_status_range`, text, optional): Enter a range of payment status.
* **Subscription Type Range** (`subscription_type_range`, text, optional): Enter a range of subscription types.
* **Billing Status Range** (`billing_status_range`, text, optional): Enter a range of billing status.
* **Billing Address Range** (`billing_address_range`, text, optional): Enter a range of billing addresses.
