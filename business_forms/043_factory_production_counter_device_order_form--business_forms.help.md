# Factory Production Counter Device Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Production Counter Device Order Form is a form used to place orders for production counter devices in the factory. This form is designed to collect necessary information from production team members or authorized personnel for accurate and efficient production.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the details of the device you want to order.
2. Provide the production date and time for the device.
3. Add any additional details about the production counter.
4. Enter the customer's name and email address if available.
5. Select the type of production counter device you want to order.
6. Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Device Details** (Device Details) (`device_details`, text, optional): Enter the name and description of the device you want to produce.
* **Production Date** (Production Date) (`production_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you want the production to take place.
* **Production Time** (Production Time) (`production_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when you want the production to take place.
* **Production Counter Details** (Production Counter Details) (`production_counter_details`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the production counter, such as serial numbers or specific requirements.
* **Customer Details** (Customer Details) (`customer_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the customer, such as their contact number or order reference.
* **Order Details** (Order Details) (`order_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the order, such as the reason for the order or special instructions.
* **Submit** (Submit) (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit the form for processing.
* **Production Counter Devices** (Production Counter Devices) (`production_counter_devices`, select_one, optional): Select the type of production counter device you want to order, such as "1", "2", or "3".
* **Customer Name** (Customer Name) (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Email** (Customer Email) (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the customer.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields before submitting to avoid errors or delays in production.
* Make sure to select the correct production counter device type.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or production team lead for clarification.
* This form is not intended for test or demonstration purposes only.
