# Background Information Sheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your business background, challenges, and needs, allowing us to provide you with more effective and personalized solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form sections and questions.
2. Provide detailed and honest answers to each question, ensuring you select all applicable options for multiple-choice questions.
3. Be as specific as possible when describing your challenges and goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company/Organization Name** (`client_company_name_sheet`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
- **Primary Contact Person** (`contact_person_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your organization.
- **Industry or Business Sector** (`industry_type`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your industry or business sector from the provided options.
  - **Technology**
  - **Healthcare**
  - **Finance/Banking**
  - **Education**
  - **Manufacturing**
  - **Retail/E-commerce**
  - **Non-Profit**
  - **Other**
- **Number of Employees** (`company_size_employees`, select_one, required): Select the number of employees in your organization from the provided options.
  - **1-10**
  - **11-50**
  - **51-200**
  - **201-500**
  - **501+**
- **What are your primary business challenges currently?** (`current_challenges_biz`, select_multiple, required): Select all the challenges that apply to your business from the provided options.
  - **Operations/Efficiency**
  - **Growth/Scaling**
  - **Technology/Digital Transformation**
  - **Marketing/Sales**
  - **Compliance/Regulatory**
  - **Human Resources/Talent**
- **What are your key goals for the next 12 months?** (`goals_description_biz`, text, required): Describe your key goals for the next 12 months in detail.
- **Describe any previous solutions or vendors used for this challenge** (`previous_solutions_tried_biz`, text, optional): If you have used any solutions or vendors related to your current challenges, provide a brief description of what worked and what didn't.
- **Estimated Budget Range for this Project** (`budget_range_estimate`, select_one, required): Select the estimated budget range for your project from the provided options.
  - **Under $5,000**
  - **$5,000 - $15,000**
  - **$15,001 - $50,000**
  - **$50,001 - $100,000**
  - **Over $100,000**
- **Desired Timeframe for Implementation** (`project_timeframe_biz`, select_one, required): Select the desired timeframe for implementation from the provided options.
  - **Immediately (0-3 months)**
  - **Near Term (3-6 months)**
  - **Long Term (6-12+ months)**
- **Are there any specific technical or regulatory requirements?** (`specific_requirements_biz`, text, optional): If you have any specific technical or regulatory requirements, describe them in this field.
- **Best time for a follow-up consultation** (`preferred_consultation_time`, text, optional): Provide your preferred time for a follow-up consultation.
- **How did you hear about our services?** (`how_did_you_hear_about_us`, text, optional): Let us know how you found out about our services.
