# Affordable Property Innovation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Affordable Property Innovation Application Form is a tool for organizations to submit their innovative ideas and solutions related to affordable property. This form helps us understand the details of your innovation, its potential impact, and your needs for further development and implementation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form in sections, starting from the top and moving down.
2.  Provide accurate and concise information for each field, as it will be used to evaluate your application.
3.  Be honest and thorough in your responses, as they will help us assess the feasibility and potential of your innovation.
4.  Make sure to attach supporting documents as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Organization Name
 * **Organization Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your organization.

### 2. Organization Type
 * **Organization Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of your organization from the following options:
     *   Non-profit Organization
     *   Government Agency
     *   Private Company
     *   Academic Institution
     *   Research Organization
     *   Community Group
     *   Other

### 3. Primary Contact Name
 * **Primary Contact Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for this application.

### 4. Primary Contact Title
 * **Primary Contact Title** (text, required): Enter the job title of the primary contact person.

### 5. Primary Contact Email
 * **Primary Contact Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.

### 6. Primary Contact Phone
 * **Primary Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.

### 7. Innovation Title
 * **Innovation Title** (text, required): Enter the title of your affordable property innovation.

### 8. Innovation Description
 * **Innovation Description** (text, required): Provide a detailed description of your innovation.

### 9. Innovation Category
 * **Innovation Category** (select_one, required): Select the category of your innovation from the following options:
     *   Construction Technology
     *   Building Materials
     *   Financing Models
     *   Design Approaches
     *   Energy Efficiency
     *   Smart Home Technology
     *   Community Planning
     *   Property Management
     *   Other

### 10. Target Market
 * **Target Market** (text, required): Enter the target market for your innovation.

### 11. Housing Affordability Problem
 * **Housing Affordability Problem** (text, required): Describe the specific housing affordability problem your innovation addresses.

### 12. Problem Scope
 * **Problem Scope** (text, required): Describe how widespread this problem is.

### 13. Solution Approach
 * **Solution Approach** (text, required): Describe how your innovation solves the identified problem.

### 14. Innovation Uniqueness
 * **Innovation Uniqueness** (text, required): Explain what makes your innovation unique compared to existing solutions.

### 15. Implementation Feasibility
 * **Implementation Feasibility** (text, required): Describe how feasible it is to implement your innovation.

### 16. Expected Impact
 * **Expected Impact** (text, required): Describe the expected impact of your innovation on housing affordability.

### 17. Impact Metrics
 * **Impact Metrics** (text, required): Explain how you will measure the success of your innovation.

### 18. Cost Reduction Potential
 * **Cost Reduction Potential** (text, required): Estimate the potential cost reduction of your innovation.

### 19. Scalability Potential
 * **Scalability Potential** (text, required): Explain how scalable is your innovation across different markets.

### 20. Current Development Stage
 * **Current Development Stage** (select_one, required): Select the current development stage of your innovation from the following options:
     *   Conceptual
     *   Prototype
     *   Pilot Testing
     *   Early Implementation
     *   Mature Implementation

### 21. Proof of Concept
 * **Proof of Concept** (select_one, required): Confirm whether you have proof of concept or pilot results:
     *   True
     *   False
     *   In Progress

### 22. Pilot Results
 * **Pilot Results** (text, optional): Summarize the pilot results if you have them.

### 23. Funding Requirements
 * **Funding Requirements** (text, required): Specify the funding needed to further develop and implement your innovation.

### 24. Partnership Opportunities
 * **Partnership Opportunities** (text, required): Describe the partnerships that would be beneficial for your innovation.

### 25. Implementation Timeline
 * **Implementation Timeline** (text, required): Enter the expected timeline for full implementation.

### 26. Supporting Documents
 * **Supporting Documents** (text, optional): List any supporting documents included with your application.

### 27. Authorization Signature
 * **Authorization Signature** (text, required): Enter the electronic signature of an authorized representative.

### 28. Supporting Documents
 *   This field is optional. If you have supporting documents, please attach them with your application.

Note: Please make sure to review your responses carefully and attach any necessary documents before submitting your application.
