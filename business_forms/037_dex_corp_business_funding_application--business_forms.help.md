# dex_corp_business_funding_application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **dex_corp_business_funding_application** form is designed to gather essential information about a company's funding needs. This form helps us understand the company's financial situation, business type, and other relevant details to provide tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company's name.
2. Enter the company's email address.
3. Enter the company's phone number.
4. Enter the company's website (if applicable).
5. Enter the owner's name.
6. Enter the owner's email address.
7. Enter the owner's phone number.
8. Enter the owner's website (if applicable).
9. Select the business type (Retail, Service, Manufacturing, Technology, or Other).
10. Enter the business address.
11. Enter the desired funding amount.
12. Select the funding term (30, 60, or 90 days).
13. Enter a brief description of the business (if needed).
14. Select the funding source (Bank, Investor, Friend, or Family).
15. Enter the name of the bank (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company you are representing.
* **Company Email**: Enter the official email address of the company.
* **Company Phone**: Enter the company's phone number.
* **Company Website**: If the company has an official website, enter the URL.
* **Business Owner Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the business.
* **Business Owner Email**: Enter the email address of the business owner.
* **Business Owner Phone**: Enter the phone number of the business owner.
* **Business Owner Website**: If the business owner has an official website, enter the URL.
* **Business Type**: Select the type of business (Retail, Service, Manufacturing, Technology, or Other).
* **Business Address**: Enter the address of the business.
* **Funding Amount**: Enter the desired amount of funding.
* **Funding Term**: Select the funding term (30, 60, or 90 days).
* **Business Description**: If necessary, enter a brief description of the business.
* **Funding Source**: Select the source of funding (Bank, Investor, Friend, or Family).
* **Bank Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the bank.

## Tips

* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
* Ensure that you have the authority to represent the company.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact us for clarification.
