# Maid Worker Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended to collect personal and professional information from individuals interested in applying to be a maid worker. The purpose of this form is to provide the necessary details required by the employer to assess the applicant's eligibility and qualifications for the position. Please ensure that you are using this form for its intended purpose only and not for any other reason.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review the form to understand each section and its requirements.
2. Enter your information accurately and completely in each of the form fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details (1)** (`Personal Details`, text, required/optional): This section is meant to capture your basic personal information, such as your name, address, and contact details.
* **Contact Information (2)** (`Contact Information`, text, required/optional): Here, you will need to provide your contact information, including your email and phone number, so that we can get in touch with you for further discussions.
* **Availability (3)** (`Availability`, text, required/optional): Please describe your availability, including your work schedule and any other relevant information that may impact your work as a maid worker.
* **Experience (4)** (`Experience`, text, required/optional): Share your relevant work experience, including any previous work as a maid or in a related field.
* **Skills (5)** (`Skills`, text, required/optional): List any relevant skills or qualifications you possess that may be beneficial to the position, such as languages spoken or proficiency in specific cleaning techniques.
* **References (6)** (`References`, text, required/optional): Provide the contact information of at least two professional references, who can vouch for your skills and work ethic.
* **Benefits (7)** (`Benefits`, text, required/optional): If you are an existing employee, please list any benefits you are entitled to, such as health insurance or paid time off.
* **Additional Information (8)** (`Additional Information`, text, required/optional): Here, you can provide any additional information that you think would be helpful for us to know about your application.

## Tips

* Ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information in each field.
* If you are having trouble completing any field, please do not hesitate to ask for help.
* Please review your form before submission to ensure it is complete and accurate.
