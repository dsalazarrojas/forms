# equipment_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Equipment Delivery Form" is designed to document the delivery of equipment, allowing for accurate tracking and record-keeping of this business transaction. This form is intended for use by businesses or organizations to provide information about the equipment being delivered, including the business name, delivery address, delivery date, and delivery time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the business name in the "Business Name" field.
2. Provide the delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
3. Enter the delivery date in the "Delivery Date" field.
4. Enter the delivery time in the "Delivery Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the business involved in the equipment delivery transaction.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required: false): Provide the address where the equipment is being delivered.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the equipment delivery in a standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the equipment delivery in a 12-hour clock format (HH:MM AM/PM).
