# marketing_budget_expense_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to enable users to submit marketing expenses for review and approval by the marketing department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **User Input** field with your name or username.
2. Enter the **Marketing Budget** for the expense in the corresponding field.
3. Select the **Category** for the expense from the dropdown options (Marketing, Advertising, Social Media, or Other).
4. Choose the **Expense Type** for the expense from the available options (Travel, Entertainment, Training, Other, or Miscellaneous).
5. Enter the **Date** of the expense using the date picker or manually entering the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
6. Provide a brief **Description** of the expense.
7. Enter the **Amount** of the expense.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input (text, optional)
* **User Input**: A field for users to enter their name or username to identify themselves.

### Marketing Budget (number, optional)
* **Marketing Budget**: Enter the budget amount for the expense. This will help the marketing team to track and manage expenses effectively.

### Category (select_one, optional)
* **Category**: Select the category of the expense from the available options (Marketing, Advertising, Social Media, or Other). This will help the marketing team to categorize and prioritize expenses.

### Expense Type (select_multiple, optional)
* **Expense Type**: Choose the type of expense from the available options (Travel, Entertainment, Training, Other, or Miscellaneous). This will help the marketing team to track and analyze different types of expenses.

### Date (date, optional)
* **Date**: Enter the date of the expense using the date picker or manually entering the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD. This will help the marketing team to track the timeline of expenses.

### Description (text, optional)
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the expense. This will help the marketing team to understand the context and purpose of the expense.

### Amount (number, optional)
* **Amount**: Enter the amount of the expense. This will help the marketing team to track and manage expenses effectively.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are submitting valid and accurate information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the marketing department for assistance.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not share with external parties.
