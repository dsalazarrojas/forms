# refund_claim_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Refund Claim Release Form" is used to document and process refund claims from customers. This form helps to gather necessary information from the customer to investigate and process their refund request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Customer Details" field and enter the customer's name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
2. In the "Order Details" field, provide a brief description of the order that is being refunded.
3. In the "Reason for Refund" field, select or type the reason for the refund from the options listed below:
	* Refund for returned items
	* Refund for cancelled order
	* Other (please specify)
4. Enter the refund amount in the "Refund Amount" field, using the required format for monetary values.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the customer's name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required: false): A brief description of the order that is being refunded.
* **Reason for Refund** (`reason_for_refund`, `text`, required: false): Select or type the reason for the refund from the provided options, or choose "Other" to provide a custom reason.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the refund amount using the required format for monetary values.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, but be careful not to provide excessive information.
- Ensure that the refund amount is accurate and reflects the true value of the refund.
- If you are having trouble with a refund, contact your supervisor or administrator for assistance.
