# Coaching Call Preparation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help coaches prepare for coaching calls by gathering essential information about the client's goals, progress, and challenges. It helps coaches provide effective and personalized coaching.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Client name** field with the client's full name.
2. Enter the **Appointment date** in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
3. List the **Top 3 objectives for the session** in the **Goals for session** field.
4. Provide a brief summary of **Progress since last call**.
5. Identify any **Challenges faced** that need to be addressed.
6. Specify the **Desired outcomes** for the client.
7. If necessary, list any **Materials to review**.
8. Outline **Follow up tasks** to ensure progress after the call.
9. Add any additional **Additional notes** for the coach's reference.
10. Review and confirm your entries before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client name**: Enter the client's full name.
* **Appointment date**: Enter the date of the scheduled coaching call in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Goals for session**: List the top 3 objectives you want to achieve during the session.
* **Progress since last call**: Briefly describe the client's progress since the last coaching call.
* **Challenges faced**: Identify any challenges or barriers the client has faced since the last call.
* **Desired outcomes**: Specify what you want the client to achieve during or after the session.
* **Materials to review**: List any materials, documents, or resources that the client needs to review before or after the session.
* **Follow up tasks**: Outline specific tasks or actions to take after the session to ensure the client progresses towards their goals.
* **Additional notes**: Provide any additional context or information that you feel is relevant to the coaching call.

## Tips
* Be as specific and detailed as possible in your entries.
* Ensure all fields are filled out before submitting to ensure effective use of the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form's metadata or contact the form's author for clarification.
