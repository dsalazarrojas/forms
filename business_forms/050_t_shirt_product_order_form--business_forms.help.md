This form is for customers to order T shirts from a company, allowing them to provide their personal information, order details, shipping information, payment details, and receive a summary of their order. It is not a duplicate of any existing form and is created based on public metadata about T-shirt products and industries. 

# T Shirt Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The T Shirt Product Order Form is a form designed to gather information from customers for T shirt orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your information in the **User Info** section, including your name, email, and any other relevant details.
2. In the **Order Details** section, specify the type of T shirt you want to order, including the design, size, and color.
3. In the **Shipping Info** section, enter your shipping address and any special shipping instructions.
4. In the **Payment Info** section, enter your payment method and any relevant payment information.
5. Review the **Order Summary** to ensure all information is accurate before submitting your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Specify the type of T shirt you want to order, including design, size, and color.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address and any special shipping instructions.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your payment method and any relevant payment information.
* **Order Summary** (`order_summary`, text, required/optional): Review the summary of your order to ensure all information is accurate before submitting.
