# New Partner Onboarding Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about new partners during the onboarding process. It is essential to provide accurate and complete details to ensure smooth integration and a successful partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
3. Ensure you have permission from the partner before filling out any sensitive information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Partner Information** (`new_partner_onboarding_form_page_1`, text, required:false): This field is intended for the new partner's name and business details.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required:false): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the partner's account.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required:false): Provide the name of the business or company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required:false): Enter the business email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required:false): Enter the business phone number.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required:false): Provide the website URL of the business.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required:false): Briefly describe the nature of the business.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required:false): Enter the business address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required:false): Enter the zip code of the business location.
* **City** (`city`, text, required:false): Enter the city of the business location.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required:true): Select the country where the business is based.
* **State** (`state`, select_multiple, required:false): Select the state(s) where the business operates.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required:true): Choose the type of business (e.g., Network Marketing, Direct Sales).
* **Date of Registration** (`date_of_registration`, date, required:false): Enter the date the business was registered.
* **Start of Service** (`start_of_service`, time, required:false): Enter the start date and time of the service.
* **End of Service** (`end_of_service`, time, required:false): Enter the end date and time of the service.
* **Business Partner** (`business_partner`, select_one, required:true): Indicate if the business is a partner.
* **Integration Status** (`integration_status`, select_multiple, required:false): Select the integration status of the business.
* **Integration Date** (`integration_date`, date, required:false): Enter the date of integration.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required:false): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Integration Issues** (`integration_issues`, select_multiple, required:false): Select any integration issues.
* **Comments on Integration** (`comments_on_integration`, text, required:false): Add any comments on integration.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required:false): Add any additional notes.
* **Integration Notes** (`integration_notes`, note, required:false): Add any integration notes.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required:false): Add any additional comments.

Remember to review the form carefully and provide accurate information to ensure smooth integration and a successful partnership.
