# Food Order Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer data and order details for food orders, allowing businesses to gather necessary information for processing and fulfilling customer requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact information on the "Customer Information" page.
2.  Provide details about your order on the "Order Information" page.
3.  Include any relevant event information on the "Event Information" page.
4.  Enter payment information on the "Payment Information" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information**: Enter your name, contact number, or any other relevant details to help the business understand who you are and how to contact you.
* **Order Information**: Describe your order, including the type of food you want to order, any special requests, or other relevant details.
* **Event Information**: If your order is for a specific event or occasion, provide details about the event, such as the date, time, and location.
* **Payment Information**: Enter your payment method and any other relevant payment details to complete the order.
