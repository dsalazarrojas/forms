# Finance Leadership Meeting Sign In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record attendance and participation information for finance leadership meetings. It helps in tracking attendance, reasons for partial attendance, and any comments or feedback from participants. This form is intended for internal use within the organization, and the data collected will be used to facilitate future meeting planning and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Select your role within the finance leadership team in the "role" field.
3. Enter your department in the "department" field.
4. Provide the location of the meeting in the "location" field.
5. Select your attendance status from the options provided in the "Attendance Status" field.
6. If you attended the meeting partially, provide a reason for your partial attendance in the "Reason for Partially Attended" field.
7. Add any comments or feedback you have about the meeting in the "Comments" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Sign In**: Enter the title of the meeting you attended.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Role**: Select your role within the finance leadership team, such as Director, Manager, or Team Member.
* **Department**: Enter the department you belong to within the finance leadership team.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the meeting, including the room number or building name.
* **Attendance Status**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Attended
	+ Not Attended
	+ Partially Attended
* **Reason for Partially Attended**: If you selected "Partially Attended", provide a brief explanation for your partial attendance.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or feedback about the meeting, such as suggestions for future improvements.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are filling out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your meeting invitation or discussion with your colleagues.
* If you have any issues or concerns, reach out to the meeting organizer or support team.
