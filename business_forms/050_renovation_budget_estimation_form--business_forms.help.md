# Renovation Budget Estimation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renovation Budget Estimation Form is designed to help you estimate the budget for a renovation project. The form is intended to gather information about the project, including its scope, location, and expected costs, to provide a comprehensive budget estimation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Enter your information in the spaces provided.
3. Review and submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Renovation Budget Estimation Form Field 1** (`renovation_budget_estimation_form_field_1`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the renovation project, including its purpose and scope.
* **Renovation Budget Estimation Form Field 2** (`renovation_budget_estimation_form_field_2`, text, optional): Describe the location of the renovation project (e.g., address, city, or region).
* **Renovation Budget Estimation Form Field 3** (`renovation_budget_estimation_form_field_3`, text, optional): Enter the estimated budget range for the project (e.g., $10,000 - $50,000).
* **Renovation Budget Estimation Form Field 4** (`renovation_budget_estimation_form_field_4`, text, optional): Describe the scope of the project, including the areas to be renovated and the materials to be used.
* **Renovation Budget Estimation Form Field 5** (`renovation_budget_estimation_form_field_5`, text, optional): Enter the expected completion date for the project.
* **Renovation Budget Estimation Form Field 6** (`renovation_budget_estimation_form_field_6`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the project.
* **Renovation Budget Estimation Form Field 7** (`renovation_budget_estimation_form_field_7`, text, optional): List any specific requirements or preferences for the renovation (e.g., specific materials, colors, or features).
* **Renovation Budget Estimation Form Field 8** (`renovation_budget_estimation_form_field_8`, text, optional): Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.

Note: All fields are optional, and you can leave a field blank if you do not have information to provide.
