# Supplier Payment Optimization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register a supplier for payment optimization and capture necessary information to facilitate payment processing. Please fill out this form if you are a registered supplier. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your supplier information in the "Supplier Information" page.
2. Select your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" page.
3. Enter the frequency of payments in the "Payment Frequency" page.
4. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" page.
5. Provide contact information in the "Contact Information" page.
6. Select the contact person for your account in the "Contact Person" page.
7. Enter any additional information about your payments in the "Additional Info" page.
8. Confirm your supplier status in the "Supplier Status" page.
9. Choose your payment schedule in the "Payment Schedule" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Information** (Supplier Information, Text, Required: No):
  Enter your company or supplier name and any other relevant information we may need to process your payments.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, Select Multiple, Required: No):
  Select how you would like to receive payments from us:
  - **Check**: A payment method that requires you to physically mail us a check.
  - **Wire Transfer**: A payment method that involves a direct electronic transfer of funds.
  - **Online Payment Portal**: A payment method that allows you to pay online through a secure platform.
* **Payment Frequency** (Payment Frequency, Number, Required: No):
  Enter how often you would like to receive payments from us. This will help us schedule payments for you.
* **Payment Amount** (Payment Amount, Number, Required: No):
  Enter the amount you would like to receive from us for each payment period.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, Text, Required: No):
  Provide any relevant contact information we may need to contact you about your payments or account.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, Select One, Required: No):
  Select the contact person for your account from our list of options (John Doe, Jane Doe, Other). If your contact person is not listed, select "Other" and enter the name in the "Supplier Information" field.
* **Payment Terms** (Payment Terms, Note, Required: No):
  Enter any specific payment terms or conditions you have regarding payment processing.
* **Additional Info** (Additional Info, Text, Required: No):
  Enter any additional information you would like to share about your payments.
* **Supplier Status** (Supplier Status, Select One, Required: No):
  Confirm your current supplier status:
  - **Active**: You are an active supplier with us.
  - **Inactive**: You are an inactive supplier with us.
* **Payment Schedule** (Payment Schedule, Select One, Required: No):
  Confirm your payment schedule:
  - **Immediate**: Payment will be made immediately.
  - **Bi-Weekly**: Payments will be made every 2 weeks.
  - **Monthly**: Payments will be made on a monthly basis.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are an active supplier before filling out this form. 
- Ensure that you select your preferred payment method and frequency correctly. 
- Enter accurate contact information so that we can contact you about your payments or account. 
- Enter any additional information that may be helpful in processing your payments.
