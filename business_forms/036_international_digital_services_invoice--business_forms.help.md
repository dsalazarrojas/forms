# international_digital_services_invoice - Help Guide
## Purpose

The international digital services invoice is a form used to create professional invoices for services provided to customers. It collects information related to the billing process, customer details, products or services offered, payment terms, payment method, payment information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the billing process from the "Billing Process" page.
2. Enter the customer details on the "Customer Details" page.
3. Enter the billing address on the "Billing Address" page.
4. List the products or services offered on the "Products/Services" page.
5. Specify the payment terms on the "Payment Terms" page.
6. Select the payment method on the "Payment Method" page.
7. Enter the payment information on the "Payment Info" page.
8. Take notes on the "Notes" page.
9. Enter the contact information on the "Email" and "Phone" pages.
10. Enter the total amount and payment status on the "Total Amount" and "Payment Status" pages.
11. Specify the payment date and time on the "Payment Date" and "Payment Time" pages.
12. Assign the tool used on the "Assigned Tool" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Billing Process
* **Billing Process** (`text`, required: false): Select the process that will be used to generate the invoice. Please choose the most relevant process for your needs.

### Customer Details
* **Customer Details** (`select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' to confirm that the customer details will be entered in this form.

### Billing Address
* **Billing Address** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' to confirm that the billing address will be entered in this form.

### Products/Services
* **Products/Services** (`text`, required: false): Enter the products or services provided to the customer.

### Payment Terms
* **Payment Terms** (`text`, required: false): Specify the payment terms that will be applied to the services provided.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' to confirm that the payment method will be specified in this form.

### Payment Info
* **Payment Info** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' to confirm that the payment information will be entered in this form.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the services provided.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number.

### Total Amount
* **Total Amount** (`number`, required: false): Enter the total amount due to the customer.

### Payment Status
* **Payment Status** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the payment status, either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.

### Payment Date
* **Payment Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of the payment.

### Payment Time
* **Payment Time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time of the payment.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`text`, required: false): Enter the tool used to generate the invoice.

## Tips

* Please ensure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to generate a professional invoice.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to contact the billing team.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
