# Custom Furniture Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for custom furniture delivery services. It is intended for customers who wish to schedule a custom delivery of furniture items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information, including name and any other relevant details.
2. Choose a delivery date for your custom furniture delivery.
3. Select a delivery time that suits you best.
4. Decide on whether you would like to opt for our delivery service.
5. If you have any special instructions for the delivery, please provide them in the "Customer Instructions" field.
6. Enter your contact number so we can reach you for any updates or questions.
7. Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on the status of your delivery.
8. If you would like to add any additional notes or details about your delivery, please use the "Delivery Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required): This field is used to collect basic customer information. Please enter your name and any other relevant details you would like us to know.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Please select a date for the delivery of your custom furniture items. Our team will use this date to plan and schedule the delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required): Choose a time for the delivery of your custom furniture items. This will ensure that our team is aware of the best time to deliver your items.
* **Delivery Service** (`delivery_service`, select_one, required): If you would like to opt for our delivery service, please select "Yes". If you prefer not to use our service, please select "No".
* **Customer Instructions** (`customer_instructions`, text, optional): If you have any special instructions or requests for the delivery, please provide them in this field.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Please enter your contact number so we can reach you for any updates or questions.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on the status of your delivery.
* **Address** (`address`, note, optional): If you have any additional details or notes about your delivery, please enter them here.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes about your delivery, please enter them in this field.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): If you would like to sign this form, please enter your signature below.
* **Delivery Signature** (`delivery_signature`, text, optional): If you would like to sign this form, please enter your signature below.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
- Our team will review and process your form submission promptly to arrange for the delivery of your custom furniture items.
