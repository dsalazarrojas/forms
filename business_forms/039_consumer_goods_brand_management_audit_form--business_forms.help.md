# Consumer Goods Brand Management Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data from auditors on their experience and observations during an audit of consumer goods stores. The data collected will be used to help stores identify areas for improvement and provide guidance on best practices for consumer goods management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in the order presented. Start by entering the auditor's name and ID number, and then move on to the store information.
2. Enter the date and time of the audit.
3. Select the store name and number, and provide the street address and city and state.
4. Rate the store's overall performance based on your observations.
5. Answer questions about the products, including the brand, category, shelf presence, stock level, and condition.
6. Note any out-of-stock items and any issues found during the audit.
7. Rate the store's overall score based on your observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Auditor Name**: Enter your name as the auditor.
* **Auditor ID**: Enter your ID number.
* **Audit Date**: Enter the date of the audit.
* **Audit Time**: Enter the time of the audit.
* **Store Name**: Enter the name of the store.
* **Store Number**: Enter the store's identification number (if available).
* **Store Address**: Enter the street address of the store.
* **Store City**: Enter the city where the store is located.
* **Store State**: Enter the state or province where the store is located.
* **Brand Name**: Enter the name of the brand being audited.
* **Product Category**: Select the category of products being audited (e.g. Food and Beverage, Personal Care, etc.).
* **Shelf Presence**: Rate the shelf presence of the products (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Shelf Placement**: Select the shelf placement level (Eye Level, Above Eye Level, Below Eye Level, Floor Level, End Cap, Checkout Area).
* **Number of Facings**: Enter the number of product facings.
* **Stock Level**: Rate the current stock level (Fully Stocked, Well Stocked, Adequate, Low Stock, Out of Stock).
* **Out of Stock Items**: List any out-of-stock items.
* **Pricing Display**: Rate the pricing display (Yes, Clear and Accurate, Yes, But Some Issues, No, Missing Prices, No, Incorrect Prices).
* **Promotional Materials**: Rate the display of promotional materials (Yes, Well Displayed, Yes, But Limited, No Promotions, Expired Promotions).
* **Signage Condition**: Rate the condition of signage (Excellent, Good, Average, Damaged, Missing).
* **Competitor Presence**: Note any competitor activity observed.
* **Planogram Compliance**: Rate the store's compliance with the planogram (Fully Compliant, Mostly Compliant, Partially Compliant, Not Compliant, No Planogram).
* **Product Condition**: Rate the condition of products (All Products Perfect, Minor Issues, Some Damage, Significant Damage, Major Issues).
* **Area Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness of the display area (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Photos Taken**: Indicate if photos were taken during the audit.
* **Issues Found**: Describe any issues found during the audit.
* **Recommendations**: Suggest any actions to improve store performance.
* **Follow-up Required**: Indicate if follow-up is required.
* **Overall Score**: Rate the overall score of the store (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
