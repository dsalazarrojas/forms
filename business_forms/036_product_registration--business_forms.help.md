# Product Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering a product with details such as purchase date, serial number, and product code. The collected data can be used for tracking, inventory management, or customer service purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product name in the "Product Name" field.
2. Enter the date the product was purchased in the "Purchase Date" field.
3. Input the serial number of the product.
4. Enter the product code, if applicable.
5. Provide the customer's email address if it's required for your product registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the product being registered.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the product was purchased.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, `text`, required/optional): Input the serial number of the product.
* **Product Code** (`product_code`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the product code, if applicable.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address if it's required for your product registration process.
