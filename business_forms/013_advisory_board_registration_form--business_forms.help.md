# Advisory Board Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you register as a member of our advisory board. By filling out this form, you will be able to share your expertise, skills, and experience with our organization and contribute to our decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading through each section carefully to ensure you understand what we are asking for.
2. Fill out your profile information, including your full name, current job title, and company/organization you are affiliated with.
3. Provide a valid LinkedIn profile URL if applicable.
4. Share your primary industry expertise and the skills you are offering to our advisory board.
5. Indicate your previous board experience and motivation for joining our advisory board.
6. Confirm your availability to commit time for meetings and provide your best contact email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Current Job Title**: Briefly describe your current job title and role in your organization.
* **Company / Organization**: Enter the name of your current company or organization.
* **LinkedIn Profile URL**: If you have a LinkedIn profile, please share the URL so we can connect with you.
* **Primary Industry Expertise**: Select the area where you have the most knowledge and experience.
* **Skills Offered**: Choose all the skills that you can bring to our advisory board.
* **Previous Board Experience**: Indicate if you have served on a board before and how recent it was.
* **Motivation for Joining**: Share with us why you want to join our advisory board.
* **Ability to Commit Time**: Confirm if you can commit to 4 meetings per year.
* **Best Contact Email**: Enter your best contact email address.
* **Mobile Phone**: Enter your mobile phone number.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
- Once submitted, your application will be reviewed and we will be in touch with you soon.
