# Karmic Kreative Photography Session Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Photography Session Contract used by Karmic Kreative to collect necessary information and agreement from clients before a photography session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: **Client**, **Session Date**, **Start Time**, and **End Time**.
2. Optionally, fill in **Location** if you want to specify the session location.
3. Read and agree to the terms in the **Client Agreement** field.
4. Review your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client**: Enter your name and contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Photographer**: Enter your photographer's name and contact information.
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of the photography session.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the session.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the session.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the session, if different from the default location.
* **Client Agreement**: Read and agree to the terms of the session by typing "I agree" in the field.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact Karmic Kreative.
