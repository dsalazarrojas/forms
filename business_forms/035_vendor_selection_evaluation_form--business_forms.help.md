The Vendor Selection Evaluation Form is used to evaluate and select a vendor for a business project. This form allows users to choose multiple vendors from a list, rate their pricing, quality, and overall rating, and add any additional notes or details about the vendor. The form is designed to be flexible and adaptable to different types of projects and vendor relationships.

# Vendor Selection Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate and select a vendor for a business project. It helps users to assess multiple vendors and choose the best fit for their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Vendor List" page and select the vendors that you are evaluating.
2. On the "Pricing" page, enter the vendor's pricing information, if required.
3. On the "Quality" page, enter your assessment of the vendor's quality.
4. On the "Rating" page, rate the vendor's overall performance (1-10).
5. On the "Notes" page, add any additional comments or details about the vendor.
6. On the "Date" page, select the date of the vendor evaluation (if required).
7. On the "Time" page, select the time of the vendor evaluation (if required).
8. On the "Phone" page, enter the vendor's phone number.
9. On the "Email" page, enter the vendor's email address.
10. On the "Selected Vendor" page, select the vendor that you have chosen.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor List (vendor_list)**: 
	+ Select the vendors that you are evaluating from the list.
* **Pricing (pricing)**: 
	+ If required, enter the vendor's pricing information. If not required, skip this field.
* **Quality (quality)**: 
	+ Enter your assessment of the vendor's quality.
* **Rating (rating)**: 
	+ Rate the vendor's overall performance on a scale of 1-10.
* **Notes (notes)**: 
	+ Add any additional comments or details about the vendor.
* **Date (date)**: 
	+ Select the date of the vendor evaluation (if required).
* **Time (time)**: 
	+ Select the time of the vendor evaluation (if required).
* **Phone (phone)**: 
	+ Enter the vendor's phone number.
* **Email (email)**: 
	+ Enter the vendor's email address.
* **Selected Vendor (selected_vendor)**: 
	+ Select the vendor that you have chosen.
* **Phone (phone)** and **Email (email)**: 
	+ Enter the vendor's contact information.
* **Date (date) and Time (time)**: 
	+ Select the evaluation timing (if required).
