# Aesthetics Advertising Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a template for creating an advertising agreement between an aesthetic clinic and a publisher/agency. It outlines essential details for a successful campaign, including the parties involved, campaign duration, ad format, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all necessary information for the required fields.
2. Enter the Aesthetic Clinic Name and Clinic Representative's name in the designated fields.
3. Enter the Publisher/Agency Name and the Publisher/Agency Representative's name.
4. Choose the ad format where the ads will appear (e.g., Digital Display Banners, Social Media Sponsored Posts, etc.).
5. Describe the specifics of what is being purchased (deliverables).
6. Select the payment schedule (e.g., 100% Upfront, 50% Deposit, Monthly Installments, etc.).
7. Choose how you acknowledge compliance with medical advertising regulations.
8. Sign the form to confirm the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Aesthetic Clinic Name (Clinic Name)**: Enter the name of the clinic.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
- **Clinic Representative (Clinic Contact)**: Enter the name of the authorized person representing the clinic.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
- **Publisher / Agency Name (Publisher / Agency Name)**: Enter the name of the publisher or agency.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
- **Publisher / Agency Representative (Publisher / Agency Contact)**: Enter the name of the authorized person representing the publisher or agency.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
- **Campaign Start Date (Start Date)**: Enter the date when the campaign is expected to start.
  - Type: date
  - Required: true
- **Campaign End Date (End Date)**: Enter the date when the campaign is expected to end.
  - Type: date
  - Required: true
- **Ad Format / Placements (Ad Format / Placements)**: Choose where the ads will appear (e.g., Digital Display Banners, Social Media Sponsored Posts, etc.).
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Required: true
  - Options: Digital Display Banners, Social Media Sponsored Posts, Magazine Full Page, Email Newsletter Sponsorship, Influencer Promotion
- **Deliverables Description (Deliverables)**: Describe the specifics of what is being purchased (e.g., ad designs, ad space, etc.).
  - Type: text
  - Required: true
- **Total Contract Value ($)(Contract Value)**: Enter the total value of the contract.
  - Type: number
  - Required: true
- **Payment Schedule (Payment Schedule)**: Choose the payment schedule (e.g., 100% Upfront, 50% Deposit, Monthly Installments, etc.).
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true
  - Options: 100% Upfront, 50% Deposit, 50% on Completion, Monthly Installments, Net 30
- **Compliance Acknowledgment (Compliance)**: Choose how you acknowledge compliance with medical advertising regulations (e.g., I Acknowledge, Other).
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true
  - Options: I Acknowledge, Other
- **Authorized Signature (Clinic Signature)**: Sign the form to confirm the agreement.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true
  - Options: Signed, Other
- **Date (Date)**: Enter the date when the document was signed.
  - Type: date
  - Required: true
