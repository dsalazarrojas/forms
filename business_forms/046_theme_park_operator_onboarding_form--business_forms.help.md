# Theme Park Operator Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for the onboarding process of theme park operators. It collects essential information from both the operator and provider sides to ensure a smooth and efficient onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter the **Onboarding Title** to give a brief description of the onboarding process.
2.  Enter your **Provider Name** and provide your **Provider Address**.
3.  Input your **Email** for communication purposes.
4.  Attach a **Documentation Link** if applicable (optional).
5.  Select your **Onboarding Status** from the options: **Active**, **Inactive**, or **Pending**.
6.  Upload your **Operator Document** for verification.
7.  Provide **Additional Comments** if any.
8.  For **Provider Comments**, **Additional Documents**, and **Provider Documents**, you can add any relevant information but these are not required.
9.  Confirm your agreement with the **Operator Agreed Terms**, **Provider Agreed Terms**, and subsequent "Agreed Terms" fields by selecting **I agree** or **I do not agree**.
10. Finally, click **Submit** to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Onboarding Title**: A brief description of the onboarding process.
*   **Provider Name**: Enter your company or organization name.
*   **Provider Address**: Enter your physical address for correspondence purposes.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
*   **Documentation Link**: If applicable, provide a link to any relevant documentation.
*   **Onboarding Status**: Select your current onboarding status from **Active**, **Inactive**, or **Pending** options.
*   **Operator Document**: Upload your verification document.
*   **Additional Comments**: Provide any extra information or comments.
*   **Operator Agreed Terms**: Confirm your agreement with the terms by selecting **I agree** or **I do not agree**.
*   **Provider Agreed Terms**: Confirm your agreement with the terms by selecting **I agree** or **I do not agree**.
*   **Additional Documents**: You can add any additional documents that are relevant to your onboarding process.
*   **Provider Documents**: You can add any additional documents that are relevant to your provider information.
*   **Operator Agreed Terms 1**, **Provider Agreed Terms 1**, **Operator Agreed Terms 2**, **Provider Agreed Terms 2**, **Operator Agreed Terms 3**, **Provider Agreed Terms 3**, **Operator Agreed Terms 4**, and **Provider Agreed Terms 4**: Confirm your agreement with the terms by selecting **I agree** or **I do not agree**.
*   **Operator Notes**: Add any extra notes or comments from the operator side.
*   **Provider Notes**: Add any extra notes or comments from the provider side.
*   **Submit**: Click to complete and submit the form.
