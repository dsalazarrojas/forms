# Tour Guide Employment Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tour Guide Employment Contract Form is used to document the employment contract between a tour guide and their employer. This form outlines the terms and conditions of the employment agreement, including job title, start and end dates, job type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company's name and contact information in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the job title and job type of the tour guide.
3. Select the job status (Active or Inactive) and enter any job responsibilities.
4. Provide the employee's signature and date for the employee signature field.
5. Provide the employer's signature and date for the employer signature field.
6. Enter the contract date and end date, if applicable.
7. Add any additional contract terms or conditions.
8. Enter the employee's address and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your company's contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the tour guide's job.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the tour guide's employment.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the tour guide's employment.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of job (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the job (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Responsibilities** (`job_responsibilities`, text, optional): Enter any special job responsibilities or duties.
* **Job Compensation** (`job_compensation`, text, optional): Enter the tour guide's compensation or payment details.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, optional): Provide the signature of the tour guide.
* **Employer Signature** (`employer_signature`, text, optional): Provide the signature of the employer.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, text, optional): Enter any additional contract terms or conditions.
* **Contract Terms 2** (`contract_terms2`, text, optional): Enter any additional contract terms or conditions.
* **Company Logo** (`company_logo`, text, optional): Enter your company's logo or branding information.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter your company's address.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter your company's website URL.
* **Contract Version** (`contract_version`, text, optional): Enter the version of the contract.
* **Employee Address** (`employee_address`, text, optional): Enter the tour guide's address.
* **Company Fax** (`company_fax`, text, optional): Enter your company's fax number.
* **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes or additional details about the tour guide.
* **Company Notes** (`company_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes or additional details about the company.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the contract.
