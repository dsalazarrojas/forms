# Contract Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contract Request Form is a tool used to collect information about contracts that are being created, modified, or terminated within the company. This form helps to ensure that all necessary parties and stakeholders are informed and involved in the contract process. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to facilitate a smooth and efficient contract process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review and submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Requestor Title** (`requestor_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department you belong to.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the request.
* **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of agreement.
* **Contract Title** (`contract_title`, text, required): Enter the name of the contract.
* **Contract Description** (`contract_description`, text, required): Enter a brief summary of the contract.
* **Other Party Name** (`other_party_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the counterparty.
* **Other Party Type** (`other_party_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of entity.
* **Other Party Contact** (`other_party_contact`, text, required): Enter the email and phone number of the counterparty.
* **Estimated Value** (`estimated_value`, number, required): Enter the dollar amount.
* **Contract Duration** (`contract_duration`, select_one, required): Select the length of the agreement.
* **Proposed Start Date** (`proposed_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Business Objectives** (`business_objectives`, text, required): Enter the goals of the contract.
* **Key Terms and Conditions** (`key_terms_and_conditions`, text, required): Enter the essential terms of the contract.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required): Enter the terms of payment.
* **Deliverables** (`deliverables`, text, required): Enter the items to be delivered.
* **Performance Metrics** (`performance_metrics`, text, optional): Enter the metrics to measure success.
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, select_one, required): Select the risk level.
* **Budget Code** (`budget_code`, text, required): Enter the cost center.
* **Funding Source** (`funding_source`, text, required): Enter the source of funding.
* **Approvals Required** (`approvals_required`, select_multiple, required): Select the approvers.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Select the urgency level of the contract.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, date, required): Enter the date when the contract is needed.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other information.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled out to ensure that the form is submitted correctly. The form will not be processed if any required fields are left blank.
