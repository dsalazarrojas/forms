# Home Office Deduction Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to provide information about your home office, which is a crucial component in determining your tax deductions. Please fill out this form accurately and honestly to ensure you receive the correct tax benefits. The form is divided into several pages for your ease and convenience.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the form, make sure you have all necessary information readily available. This includes your name and details about your home office. Follow these steps:

* Go to the first page and fill out your name in the "name" field.
* On the second page, enter your home office address in the "address" field.
* On the third page, input your phone number in the "phone" field.
* On the fourth page, enter your fax number in the "fax" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **prophets_office** (required): This field is actually the "address" field, which is the address of your home office.
* **phone** (required): Enter your home office phone number.
* **fax** (required): Enter your home office fax number.
