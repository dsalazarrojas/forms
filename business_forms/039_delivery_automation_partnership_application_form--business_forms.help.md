# Delivery Automation Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect information from potential partners interested in forming a business partnership with us. It will help us understand your background, goals, and interests to assess potential partnership opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly. If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Information (Text)**: Enter a brief description of your partner company.
* **Name (Text)**: Enter the name of your company or the name of the person submitting this form.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your company email address or the email address of the person submitting this form.
* **Partner Type (Select One)**: Choose the type of partner you are interested in being:
	+ Delivery Partner: A partner that offers delivery services.
	+ Sales Partner: A partner that offers sales or marketing services.
	+ Support Partner: A partner that offers support services.
* **Partner Since (Date)**: Enter the date you have been in business.
* **Contact Person (Text)**: Enter the name and title of the person responsible for this partnership.
* **Role (Select One)**: Choose the role of the contact person:
	+ Account Manager
	+ Support Manager
	+ Sales Manager
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Partner Location (Text)**: Enter the location of your company.
* **Partner Industry (Select One)**: Choose the industry of your partner company:
	+ Automotive
	+ Logistics
	+ Retail
* **Partner Description (Text)**: Enter a brief description of your company.
* **Partnership Goals (Text)**: Enter your goals and expectations for this partnership.
* **Terms and Conditions (Note)**: Read and agree to our terms and conditions of partnership.
* **Signature (Note)**: This is a placeholder for a digital signature, please add your name and date.
* **Submission Date (Date)**: This field is automatically populated when the form is submitted and is not editable.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
