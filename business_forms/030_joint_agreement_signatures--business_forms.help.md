# Joint Agreement Signatures - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Joint Agreement Signatures form is designed to collect and document information related to joint agreements between parties. It captures the base information, agreement details, signatures, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the base information for each party by providing the required details.
2. Enter the agreement details, including the date signed and the signers list.
3. Select the agreement signing party and date for each party.
4. Repeat the base information and agreement details for each additional party involved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Base Information** (text): This field captures the basic information about the parties involved in the agreement.
* **Party One** (text): Enter the name or identifier of Party One.
* **Party Two** (text): Enter the name or identifier of Party Two.
* **Agreement Details** (text): Provide a brief description of the agreement.
* **Date Signed** (date): Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Signers List** (select multiple): Check the box to indicate if the signers have agreed to the terms of the agreement.
* **Signature Block** (note): This field is intended for any additional comments or notes related to the signature.
* **Agreement Signed** (select one): Select whether the agreement has been signed or not.
* **Reason Not Signed** (text): If the agreement has not been signed, provide a reason for not signing.
* **Agreement Expiration** (date): Enter the date the agreement is set to expire.
* **Signature Block 2** (note): Another field for additional comments or notes related to the signature.
* **Agreement Signing Party** (select one): Choose the party that signed the agreement.
* **Agreement Signing Date** (date): Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Repeat Base Information** (text): Repeat the base information for each additional party involved.
* **Repeat Party** (text): Enter the name or identifier of the additional party.
* **Repeat Agreement Details** (text): Repeat the agreement details for each additional party.
* **Repeat Date Signed** (date): Repeat the date the agreement was signed for each additional party.
* **Repeat Signers List** (select multiple): Repeat the signers list for each additional party.
* **Repeat Signature Block** (note): Repeat the signature block for each additional party.
* **Repeat Agreement Signed** (select one): Repeat whether the agreement has been signed or not for each additional party.
* **Repeat Reason Not Signed** (text): If the agreement has not been signed by the additional party, provide a reason for not signing.
* **Repeat Agreement Expiration** (date): Repeat the date the agreement is set to expire for each additional party.
* **Repeat Signature Block 2** (note): Another field for additional comments or notes related to the signature.
* **Repeat Agreement Signing Party** (select one): Choose the party that signed the agreement for each additional party.
* **Repeat Agreement Signing Date** (date): Repeat the date the agreement was signed for each additional party.
