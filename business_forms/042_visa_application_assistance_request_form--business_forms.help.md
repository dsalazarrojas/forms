# visa-application-assistance-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from you to assist with your visa application. Please answer each section carefully and accurately to provide the best assistance for your application.

## How To Complete This Form
*   Start by filling out the form in the designated sections below.
*   Review each field and fill it out as requested.
*   Submit your completed form for assistance with your visa application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Personal Information (Page 1)** (`visa_application_assistance_request_form_page1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your personal details for the purpose of the visa application.
- **Contact Information (Page 2)** (`visa_application_assistance_request_form_page2`, `text`, required: false): Provide your contact information for the support team to get in touch with you.
- **Travel Information (Page 3)** (`visa_application_assistance_request_form_page3`, `text`, required: false): Share your travel plans and any specific requirements for your visa application.
- **Travel Information (Page 4)** (`visa_application_assistance_request_form_page4`, `text`, required: false): Repeat the travel information section for accurate and complete travel details.
- **Visa Information (Page 5)** (`visa_application_assistance_request_form_page5`, `text`, required: false): Enter visa-specific details for your application.
- **Passport Information (Page 6)** (`visa_application_assistance_request_form_page6`, `text`, required: false): Enter your passport details for your visa application.
- **Contact Information (Page 7)** (`visa_application_assistance_request_form_page7`, `text`, required: false): Enter a contact method for support team to get in touch with you about your application.

## Tips
*   Double-check your information for accuracy to avoid delays or complications with your visa application.
*   Use clear and legible handwriting or typing for better clarity.
*   Submit your completed form for assistance with your visa application.
*   Be patient and allow the support team sufficient time to review and process your application.
