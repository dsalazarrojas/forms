# Work Method Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Method Statement Form is designed to collect information about a project's work method, risks, and controls. It's intended to be filled out by project managers, supervisors, or team leads to provide a comprehensive overview of the project's work approach, potential hazards, and risk management strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the project's basic information.
2.  Describe the tasks and activities involved in the project.
3.  Identify any potential hazards associated with the project.
4.  Outline the risk control measures taken to mitigate those hazards.
5.  Provide an overview of the project's work planning and scheduling.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Project Info (Page 1)
*   **Project Info** (`project_info`, text, required: false): Describe the project's basic information, including its name, location, and duration.

### Task Info (Page 2)
*   **Task Info** (`task_info`, text, required: false): Describe the tasks and activities involved in the project, including their scope and objectives.

### Risk Info (Page 3)
*   **Risk Info** (`risk_info`, text, required: false): Identify any potential risks or hazards associated with the project.

### Task Hazards (Page 4)
*   **Task Hazards** (`task_hazards`, text, required: false): Describe the hazards associated with each task or activity.

### Risk Control (Page 5)
*   **Risk Control** (`risk_control`, text, required: false): Outline the risk control measures taken to mitigate those hazards.

### Work Planning (Page 6)
*   **Work Planning** (`work_planning`, text, required: false): Provide an overview of the project's work planning and scheduling.

## Tips
*   This form is designed to be a general guide for project teams to document their work method, risks, and controls. It's not a substitute for more comprehensive risk assessments or project management tools.
*   Please note that the fields are not strictly required, but providing more detailed information will help to create a more comprehensive project overview.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to the form's metadata for further assistance.
