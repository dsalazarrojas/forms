# Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Assessment Form is designed to evaluate a team's performance in various aspects, such as productivity, quality, and collaboration. It helps identify key strengths and areas for improvement, providing an objective assessment for the team being evaluated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Department or Team Being Assessed: Enter the name of the team being assessed.
	* Assessment Officer Name: Enter the name of the officer conducting the assessment.
	* Date of Assessment: Enter the date of the assessment.
	* Productivity and Output Rating: Rate the team's productivity from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
	* Quality Standards Compliance: Rate the team's adherence to quality standards from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
	* Team Collaboration and Communication: Rate the team's collaboration and communication from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
	* Adherence to Deadlines and Timelines: Rate the team's adherence to deadlines and timelines from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
	* Key Strengths Identified: Describe what the team is doing well.
	* Primary Opportunities for Improvement: Identify areas for growth.
	* Overall Assessment Score - Percentage: Calculate the overall score based on the ratings above.
2. Review and verify your answers:
	* Ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
	* Confirm that you have assessed the team's performance accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department or Team Being Assessed**: This field is required to indicate which team is being assessed.
* **Assessment Officer Name**: This field is required to identify the officer conducting the assessment.
* **Date of Assessment**: Enter the date the assessment was conducted.
* **Productivity and Output Rating**: Rate the team's productivity on a scale of 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Quality Standards Compliance**: Rate the team's adherence to quality standards on a scale of 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Team Collaboration and Communication**: Rate the team's collaboration and communication on a scale of 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Adherence to Deadlines and Timelines**: Rate the team's adherence to deadlines and timelines on a scale of 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Key Strengths Identified**: Describe what the team is doing well.
* **Primary Opportunities for Improvement**: Identify areas for growth.
* **Resources Required for Enhanced Performance**: Optional field for listing additional resources required for the team's enhanced performance.
* **Overall Assessment Score - Percentage**: Calculate the overall score based on the ratings above.
* **Assessment Certified and Signed**: Confirm that you have assessed and certified the team's performance.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling in the ratings.
- Describe specific examples or scenarios to support your answers.
- Review and verify your answers before submitting the form.
- If unsure, consult with the assessment officer or team lead for guidance.
