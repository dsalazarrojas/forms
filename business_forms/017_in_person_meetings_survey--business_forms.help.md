# In Person Meetings Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The In Person Meetings Survey form is designed to collect data about in-person meetings between employees and their managers. This data will be used to identify trends and areas for improvement in the meeting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose the meeting frequency from the options provided.
2. Select the meeting length in hours.
3. Choose the location of the meeting.
4. Select the attendees of the meeting.
5. Provide any comments about the meeting.
6. Select any additional meeting suggestions.
7. Choose the manager's response.
8. Select the follow-up plan for the meeting.
9. Enter the next steps for the meeting.
10. Enter any manager notes.
11. Enter the manager's conclusion.
12. Enter the employee's conclusion.
13. Select the manager's recommendations.
14. Select the employee's recommendations.
15. Enter the date of the last meeting.
16. Enter the date of the next meeting.
17. Enter the meeting start and end times.
18. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meetings**: Enter the name of the meeting.
* **Frequency of Meetings**: Choose how often meetings are held, e.g., daily, weekly, monthly.
* **Meeting Length**: Enter the duration of the meeting in hours.
* **Meeting Location**: Choose the location of the meeting, e.g., office, conference room, home.
* **Meeting Attendance**: Select the attendees of the meeting.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the meeting.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments from the employee or manager.
* **Employee Feedback**: Choose the employee's feedback about the meeting, e.g., positive, negative.
* **Manager Feedback**: Choose the manager's feedback about the meeting, e.g., positive, negative.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter the manager's comments about the meeting.
* **Employee Suggestions**: Select any suggestions from the employee for future meetings.
* **Manager Suggestions**: Select any suggestions from the manager for future meetings.
* **Manager Response**: Enter the manager's response to the employee's suggestions.
* **Follow-up Plan**: Choose the follow-up plan for the meeting.
* **Next Steps**: Enter the next steps for the meeting.
* **Manager Notes**: Enter any notes from the manager about the meeting.
* **Manager Conclusion**: Enter the manager's conclusion about the meeting.
* **Employee Conclusion**: Enter the employee's conclusion about the meeting.
* **Manager Recommendations**: Select any recommendations from the manager for future meetings.
* **Employee Recommendations**: Select any recommendations from the employee for future meetings.
* **Date of Last Meeting**: Enter the date of the last meeting.
* **Date of Next Meeting**: Enter the date of the next meeting.
* **Meeting Frequency**: Enter the frequency of the meeting, e.g., daily, weekly, monthly.
* **Meeting Start**: Enter the start time of the meeting.
* **Meeting End**: Enter the end time of the meeting.
