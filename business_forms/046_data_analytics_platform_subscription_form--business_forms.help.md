# data_analytics_platform_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The data_analytics_platform_subscription_form is used to subscribe to services offered by the data analytics platform. The form helps you provide your name, email, phone number, payment information, and subscription details to complete the subscription process. Please ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting the form, as some fields are optional and others are required for a successful subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Choose your preferred service from the "Subscribe to Service" dropdown list.
2. Enter your name in the "name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Provide your payment information in the "Payment Information" field. This field is a note field where you can enter any relevant payment details.
6. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown list.
7. If you have selected "Credit Card" as your payment method, select the type of credit card you have from the "Card Type" dropdown list.
8. Choose the start date of your subscription from the "Start Date" field.
9. Choose the end date of your subscription from the "End Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscribe to Service** (Select Multiple, required): Select one or more services you want to subscribe to from the provided list.
* **Name** (Text, required): Enter your name to confirm your identity.
* **Email** (Text, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
* **Phone** (Text, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Payment Information** (Note, required): Provide any relevant payment details, such as payment method and card type.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, required): Select your preferred payment method. Currently, the available options are Credit Card and PayPal.
* **Card Type** (Select One, required if Payment Method is Credit Card): Select the type of credit card you have.
* **Start Date** (Date, required): Choose the date your subscription will begin.
* **End Date** (Date, required): Choose the date your subscription will end.
* **Phone** (Text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Note**: There is no field explanation for this field as it is not applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting the form.
* Check the required fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact our support team.
