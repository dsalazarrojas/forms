# Pet Purchase Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Purchase Agreement is a form used to formalize the purchase of a pet between a buyer and a seller, ensuring both parties understand the terms and conditions of the sale.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Pet Breed Details**: Enter the breed and any relevant information about the pet being sold, if applicable.
2. **Seller Information**: Enter your name and contact information as the seller.
3. **Buyer Information**: Enter the buyer's name and contact information.
4. **Payment Details**: Enter the payment method and amount.
5. **Date Of Sale**: Enter the date of the sale.
6. **Sale Price**: Enter the price of the sale.
7. **Payment Method**: Select one of the available payment methods (PayPal, Credit/Debit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check).
8. **Terms Of Sale**: Enter any additional terms or conditions of the sale.
9. **Agreement Date**: Enter the date the agreement is signed.
10. **Seller Signature**: Enter your signature, as the seller, to confirm the sale.
11. **Buyer Signature**: Enter the buyer's signature to confirm their acceptance of the agreement.
12. **Contract Status**: Select the status of the contract (Draft, Signed, Cancelled, or Completed).
13. **Contract Date**: Enter the date the contract is signed or modified.
14. **Contract Status Explanation**: Select an optional explanation for the contract status change.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Breed Details**: The buyer's name, age, breed, and any other relevant information about the pet being sold.
* **Seller Information**: Information about the seller, including their name, address, and contact details.
* **Buyer Information**: Information about the buyer, including their name, address, and contact details.
* **Payment Details**: Information about the payment method, amount, and any other relevant details.
* **Date Of Sale**: The date the pet was sold or transferred to the buyer.
* **Sale Price**: The price of the pet at the time of sale.
* **Payment Method**: The method by which the payment was made (PayPal, Credit/Debit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check).
* **Terms Of Sale**: Any additional terms or conditions of the sale, including ownership transfer and any other relevant details.
* **Agreement Date**: The date the agreement was signed or modified.
* **Seller Signature**: A numerical value representing the seller's signature.
* **Buyer Signature**: A numerical value representing the buyer's signature.
* **Contract Status**: The status of the contract, indicating whether it is Draft, Signed, Cancelled, or Completed.
* **Contract Date**: The date the contract was signed or modified.
* **Contract Status Explanation**: An optional field to provide an explanation for changes to the contract status.
