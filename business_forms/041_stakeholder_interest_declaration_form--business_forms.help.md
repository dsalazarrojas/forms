# stakeholder_interest_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to capture the interests and commitments of stakeholders involved in a project. It is crucial to understand the stakeholder's name, position, interests, commitments, and comments to ensure effective collaboration and communication within the project team and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, starting from the first name field.
2. Provide your first name in the first name field.
3. Enter your last name in the last name field.
4. Enter your email address in the email field.
5. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
6. Enter your address in the address field.
7. Enter the name of the stakeholder with whom you are interacting.
8. Enter the position of the stakeholder.
9. Describe the stakeholder's interests.
10. Enter the name of the project with which you are involved.
11. Enter the location of the project.
12. Enter the start date of the project.
13. Enter the end date of the project, if applicable.
14. Describe your commitments to the project and the stakeholder.
15. List the stakeholder's expectations from you or the project.
16. Describe the contributions you or the project team plans to make to the stakeholder.
17. Describe your relationship with the stakeholder.
18. Enter any additional stakeholder interests (if not already mentioned).
19. Provide details about the additional stakeholder interests entered.
20. (Optional) Enter any additional comments or information about your interests or commitments.
