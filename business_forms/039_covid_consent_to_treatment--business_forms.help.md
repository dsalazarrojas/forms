# COVID Consent To Treatment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain patient consent for COVID-19 treatment. It collects necessary information about the patient, their medical history, and their understanding of the treatment process. The form is crucial for healthcare providers to ensure they have the necessary consent to proceed with treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully to understand what information is required.
- Fill in your personal details (Full Name, Date of Birth, Medical Record Number, Email Address, and Phone Number) in the "Patient Information" section.
- Enter your current address in the "Current Address" field, if required.
- In the "Healthcare Provider Information" section, enter the name of your treating healthcare provider and the name of the healthcare facility.
- Select the date of the treatment in the "Date of Treatment" field.
- In the "Understanding COVID-19 Treatment" section, confirm your understanding of your COVID-19 diagnosis, the treatment procedure, benefits, and risks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`treatment_patient_name`, text, required): Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`treatment_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Medical Record Number** (`treatment_medical_record`, text, optional): Enter your patient ID or Medical Record Number, if available.
* **Email Address** (`treatment_email`, email, required): Enter your active email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`treatment_phone`, phone, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Current Address** (`treatment_address`, text, optional): Enter your current street address.
* **Treating Healthcare Provider Name** (`treatment_provider_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your treating healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Facility Name** (`treatment_facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare facility treating you.
* **Date of Treatment** (`treatment_treatment_date`, date, required): Select the date when treatment will be provided.
* **I Understand I Have COVID-19** (`treatment_understand_diagnosis`, select_one, required): Confirm you understand you have been diagnosed with COVID-19.
* **I Understand the Treatment Procedure** (`treatment_understand_procedure`, select_one, required): Confirm you understand the treatment process.
* **I Understand the Benefits of Treatment** (`treatment_understand_benefits`, select_one, required): Confirm you understand the expected benefits of treatment.
* **I Understand the Risks of Treatment** (`treatment_understand_risks`, select_one, required): Confirm you understand potential risks and side effects of treatment.
* **Known Allergies** (`treatment_allergies`, text, optional): List any known allergies or sensitivities.
* **Current Medications** (`treatment_current_medications`, text, optional): List your current medications and dosages.
* **Chronic Medical Conditions** (`treatment_chronic_conditions`, text, optional): List any ongoing medical conditions.
* **Previous COVID-19 Hospitalization** (`treatment_hospitalization_history`, select_one, optional): Confirm if you have had a previous hospitalization due to COVID-19.
* **This Consent is Voluntary** (`treatment_voluntary_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm you are providing consent freely and without coercion.
* **I Authorize COVID-19 Treatment to Proceed** (`treatment_authorization`, select_one, required): Confirm you authorize the treatment to proceed.
* **Date of Consent** (`treatment_consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are providing consent.

## Tips
- Please ensure you complete the form accurately and honestly.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
- Review each field carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled.
