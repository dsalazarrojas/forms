# Fuel Expense Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees to request reimbursement for fuel expenses incurred during work-related activities. It collects essential details to review and approve fuel expenses accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all necessary information and documents before filling out the form.
2.  Enter your employee name, ID, and other contact information in the "Employee Name", "Employee ID", and "Submitted By" fields.
3.  Provide details about your vehicle, including "Vehicle Make" and "Vehicle Model", in the respective fields.
4.  Enter the "Mileage" and "Fuel Type" used for the fuel expense.
5.  Fill in the "Fuel Amount" and "Total Fuel Used" fields accurately, based on your records.
6.  Record the "Start Time" and "End Time" of your activity, if applicable.
7.  Enter the "Odometer Reading" before and after the fuel expense to ensure accuracy.
8.  Enter the "Total Miles" driven during the fuel expense period.
9.  If applicable, mark the "Approval Status" as "Approved", "Denied", or "Pending".
10. In the "Notes" field, provide any additional comments or details about the fuel expense.
11. Sign the "Employee Signature" and "Manager Signature" fields once you and your manager have reviewed and approved the expense, respectively.
12. Ensure that you and your manager have filled out their respective signature fields accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name**: This is where you enter your name for work-related reference and communication purposes.
    *   **Employee ID**: Please enter your unique employee ID for identification purposes.
    *   **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle you used for the fuel expense, e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.
    *   **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle you used for the fuel expense, e.g., Camry, Mustang, etc.
    *   **Mileage**: Record the mileage of the vehicle at the start and end of the fuel expense period to ensure accuracy.
    *   **Fuel Type**: Select the type of fuel used for the expense, e.g., gasoline, diesel, etc.
    *   **Fuel Amount**: Accurately report the total amount of fuel used for the expense.
    *   **Total Fuel Used**: Record the total amount of fuel used during the expense period.
    *   **Start Time** and **End Time**: Record the start and end times of the activity for which you are requesting reimbursement.
    *   **Odometer Reading**: Record the odometer reading before and after the fuel expense period for accuracy.
    *   **Total Miles**: Record the total miles driven during the fuel expense period.
    *   **Approval Status**: Mark the approval status as "Approved", "Denied", or "Pending" based on your manager's review.
    *   **Notes**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or details about the fuel expense.
    *   **Employee Signature**: Sign this field once you have reviewed and agree with the submitted expense.
    *   **Manager Signature**: Sign this field once you have reviewed and approved the expense.
    *   **Approved By**: Enter the name of the manager who reviewed and approved the expense.
    *   **Approval Date**: Record the date the manager approved the expense.
    *   **Comments**: Use this field to record any comments or feedback from your manager.
    *   **Submitted By**: Enter your name for reference and record-keeping purposes.
    *   **Submitted On**: Record the date you submitted the expense request.
    *   **Submitted By Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID for identification purposes.
