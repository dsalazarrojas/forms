# stevedore_service_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The stevedore_service_referral_form is a form used to refer a service to other businesses or organizations. It is designed to collect relevant information about the service provided and the contact person or organization involved. This form helps in maintaining a database of services and contacts for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Make sure you have all the necessary information about the service before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields with the correct information.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business ID**: This is the ID of the business or organization that is being referred.
	+ **Type**: number
	+ **Required**: false
	+ **Explanation**: Enter the unique ID of the business or organization.
- **Contact Name**: The name of the contact person.
	+ **Type**: text
	+ **Required**: false
	+ **Explanation**: Enter the name of the person who provided the service.
- **Contact Email**: The email address of the contact person.
	+ **Type**: email
	+ **Required**: false
	+ **Explanation**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the contact person.
	+ **Type**: text
	+ **Required**: false
	+ **Explanation**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Service Type**: Select the type of service provided.
	+ **Type**: select multiple
	+ **Required**: false
	+ **Explanation**: Choose one or more options for the type of service provided.
- **Service Details**: A brief description of the service provided.
	+ **Type**: note
	+ **Required**: false
	+ **Explanation**: Enter a short description of the service provided.
- **Stevedore Name**: The name of the person who provided the service.
	+ **Type**: text
	+ **Required**: false
	+ **Explanation**: Enter the name of the person who provided the service.
- **Stevedore Email**: The email address of the person who provided the service.
	+ **Type**: email
	+ **Required**: false
	+ **Explanation**: Enter the email address of the person who provided the service.
- **Stevedore Phone**: The phone number of the person who provided the service.
	+ **Type**: text
	+ **Required**: false
	+ **Explanation**: Enter the phone number of the person who provided the service.
- **Date of Service**: The date when the service was provided.
	+ **Type**: date
	+ **Required**: false
	+ **Explanation**: Enter the date when the service was provided.
- **Time of Service**: The time when the service was provided.
	+ **Type**: time
	+ **Required**: false
	+ **Explanation**: Enter the time when the service was provided.
- **Stevedore Address**: The address of the person who provided the service.
	+ **Type**: text
	+ **Required**: false
	+ **Explanation**: Enter the address of the person who provided the service.
