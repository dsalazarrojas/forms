# Supply Chain Automation Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Chain Automation Contact Form is a tool used by customers to reach out to the supply chain automation department for assistance. Customers can use this form to inquire about various topics such as automation level, industry, company size, lead type, lead status, and lead source related to their supply chain operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in your **Name**: This field is required and should be a brief but accurate representation of your name.
2.  Enter your **Company**: This field is not required, but if you want to provide additional context to our team, please fill it in.
3.  Provide your **Email**: This field is required and should be a valid email address where we can contact you.
4.  Enter your **Phone**: This field is not required, but if you have a preferred method of communication, please provide it.
5.  In **Message**, describe your concern or question: This field is required and should be a clear and concise statement of the issue you're facing.
6.  Select your **Automation Level**: Choose between "High" or "Low" to indicate the level of automation you need assistance with.
7.  Choose your **Industry**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether your company is in the supply chain industry.
8.  Select your **Company Size**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether your company is large or small.
9.  Choose your **Lead Type**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you're looking for a new lead or not.
10. Select your **Lead Status**: Choose "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of your lead.
11. Choose your **Lead Source**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you're reaching out from a certain source.
12. Select the person in charge or responsible for the **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person who will be handling the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (text, required): Enter your name for us to address you.
- **company** (text, optional): If you'd like to provide your company's name for context.
- **email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
- **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for alternative contact.
- **message** (note, required): Type a clear and concise description of the issue you're facing.
- **automation_level** (select_multiple, required): Choose between "High" or "Low" level of automation.
- **industry** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate supply chain industry relevance.
- **company_size** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate large or small company size.
- **lead_type** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate new lead or not.
- **lead_status** (select_one, required): Choose "Active" or "Inactive" lead status.
- **lead_source** (select_one, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate lead source.
- **assigned_to** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for handling the issue.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask our team for clarification.
- If you have any attachments or additional context, consider including them with your message.
