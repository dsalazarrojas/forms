# Data Compliance Certification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Compliance Certification Request Form is a document that certifies an organization's or individual's commitment to data compliance. The form is used to gather information about the request for data compliance certification, which is a critical component in ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of sensitive data. This form helps in tracking and verifying the certification status of an organization or individual, which is essential for maintaining a secure and compliant data environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the request for data compliance certification.
2. Select the relevant category for the request (e.g., "Business Forms").
3. Attach any relevant supporting documents to justify the certification request.
4. Indicate your consent to the certification request (Accept or Decline).
5. Choose the current status of the certification request (Open, In Progress, or Closed).
6. Enter the name and contact email of the reviewer assigned to the certification request.
7. Review and confirm the information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Data Compliance Certification Request Form** (`form_title`, text, required: false): This field is the title of the request for data compliance certification. It is a free-text field where you can enter a brief description of the request.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category for the request from the list of available options. This field helps us understand the context and relevance of the request.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the request for data compliance certification.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, required: false): Attach any relevant documents that justify the certification request. You can select multiple documents from the list of options.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate your acceptance or decline of the certification request. If accepted, it implies your commitment to data compliance.
* **Certification Status** (`certification_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the current status of the certification request from the available options (Open, In Progress, or Closed).
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the reviewer assigned to the certification request.
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the reviewer assigned to the certification request. This will help us contact them for further information or clarification.

## Tips

* Make sure to attach all relevant supporting documents to justify the certification request.
* Clearly indicate the consent and certification status to ensure the request is processed accordingly.
* Enter the reviewer's name and email correctly to ensure they receive the request for review.
* Double-check the information provided before submitting the form to avoid errors.
