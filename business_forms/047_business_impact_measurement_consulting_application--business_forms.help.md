# Business Impact Measurement Consulting Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Impact Measurement Consulting Application is a form designed to collect information from organizations about their impact measurement needs. This application aims to help organizations identify and address their specific needs related to impact measurement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to provide accurate and relevant information.
2. Review each section carefully, ensuring that you have provided all necessary details.
3. If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out for support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, Text, **Required**): Please enter your name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Text, **Required**): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, **Required**): Enter your phone number.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter your organization's name.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, Select One, **Required**): Please select the type of organization you represent.
	+ For-Profit
	+ Non-Profit
	+ Social Enterprise
	+ Government
	+ Other
* **Mission Statement** (`mission_statement`, Text, **Required**): Enter your organization's mission statement.
* **Years Established** (`years_established`, Number, **Optional**): Enter the number of years your organization has been operating.
* **Primary Impact Focus** (`impact_focus`, Select One, **Required**): Select the primary impact focus of your organization.
	+ Environmental
	+ Social
	+ Economic
	+ Health
	+ Education
	+ Community Development
	+ Other
* **Project or Program Name** (`project_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter the name of the project or program you want to measure impact for.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, Text, **Required**): Describe the project or program.
* **Target Beneficiaries** (`target_beneficiaries`, Text, **Required**): Who does the project or program serve?
* **Number of Beneficiaries** (`beneficiary_count`, Number, **Optional**): Enter the number of people affected by the project or program.
* **Current Impact Measurement** (`current_measurement`, Text, **Optional**): What impact measurement methods are you currently using?
* **Measurement Challenges** (`measurement_challenges`, Text, **Required**): Describe the difficulties you face with impact measurement.
* **Desired Metrics or Outcomes** (`desired_metrics`, Text, **Required**): What metrics or outcomes would you like to measure?
* **Goals for Impact Measurement** (`measurement_goals`, Text, **Required**): What do you want to achieve through impact measurement?
* **Type of Consulting Support Needed** (`consulting_support_needed`, Select Multiple, **Required**): Select all the types of consulting support you require.
	+ Framework Development
	+ Data Collection Strategy
	+ Analysis and Reporting
	+ Technology Implementation
	+ Staff Training
	+ Strategic Planning
* **Budget Range for Consulting** (`budget_range`, Select One, **Optional**): Select the budget range for consulting.
	+ Less than $10,000
	+ $10,000 - $25,000
	+ $25,000 - $50,000
	+ $50,000 - $100,000
	+ More than $100,000
* **Project Timeline** (`timeline`, Select One, **Required**): Select the project timeline.
	+ Within 3 months
	+ Within 6 months
	+ Within 1 year
	+ Flexible
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, Text, **Optional**): If you have any further information, please share it with us.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have provided accurate and relevant information.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out.
