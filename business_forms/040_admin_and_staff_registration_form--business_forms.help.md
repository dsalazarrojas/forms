# Admin And Staff Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Admin And Staff Registration Form is designed to collect personal and professional information from employees or staff members for HR record-keeping purposes. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are gathered accurately and efficiently, making it easier for HR teams to manage staff records.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow the instructions on each page carefully.
- Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
- Review each field's explanation for specific guidance on what to enter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, required): Provide your current position within the organization.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Specify the business unit or team you belong to.
* **Start date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter your employment start date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your company email address (if available).
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your work or mobile phone number for communication.
* **Access requirements** (`access_requirements`, text, required): Specify any systems, software, or building access you need for your role.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any other onboarding details or notes about your role.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Use clear and concise language when filling in fields like "Emergency contact" and "Additional notes".
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to your HR representative or supervisor for guidance.
