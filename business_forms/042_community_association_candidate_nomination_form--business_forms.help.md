# Community Association Candidate Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to nominate candidates for various positions within the community association. It gathers information about the nominee, their background, and their qualifications for the desired position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the Nomination Information section with details about the nomination.
3. Enter the Nomination Date and Nomination ID in the respective fields.
4. Select the Position Title for which the candidate is being nominated.
5. Provide the Candidate Information, including their full name, email address, phone number, address, city, state, and ZIP code.
6. Indicate the candidate's Residency Status and the number of years they have lived in the community.
7. Enter the Nominator Information, including their full name, email address, phone number, and relationship to the candidate.
8. Specify the Nominator's relationship to the candidate.
9. Provide the Candidate's qualifications and previous experience.
10. Enter the Candidate Statement and Acceptance status.
11. If desired, provide additional comments or information about the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Information**: This section provides details about the nomination. It is a free-form text field where you can provide any additional information about the nomination.
* **Nomination Date**: Enter the date of this nomination.
* **Nomination ID**: Enter a unique reference number for this nomination.
* **Position Title**: Select the position for which the candidate is being nominated from the list provided.
* **Candidate Information**: This section provides details about the nominated candidate, including their full name, email address, phone number, address, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Candidate Name**: Enter the full name of the candidate.
* **Candidate Email**: Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Candidate Phone**: Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Candidate Address**: Enter the candidate's street address.
* **Candidate City**: Enter the candidate's city.
* **Candidate State**: Enter the candidate's state.
* **Candidate ZIP Code**: Enter the candidate's ZIP code.
* **Residency Status**: Indicate whether the candidate is a resident of the community. Select "Yes - Homeowner", "Yes - Renter", or "No - Non-Resident".
* **Years in Community**: Indicate how long the candidate has lived in the community, selecting from "Less than 1 year", "1-3 years", "3-5 years", "5-10 years", or "10+ years".
* **Nominator Information**: This section provides details about the person making the nomination, including their full name, email address, phone number, and relationship to the candidate.
* **Nominator Name**: Enter the full name of the person making the nomination.
* **Nominator Email**: Enter the email address of the person making the nomination.
* **Nominator Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person making the nomination.
* **Nominator Relationship**: Indicate the relationship of the person making the nomination to the candidate. Select from "Self-Nomination", "Community Member", "Family Member", "Neighbor", "Friend", or "Colleague".
* **Candidate Qualifications**: Provide a description of what qualifies the candidate for this position.
* **Previous Experience**: Indicate whether the candidate has previous board experience. Select "Yes - Current Board Member", "Yes - Former Board Member", "Yes - Committee Experience", or "No - First Time".
* **Candidate Statement**: Provide a brief statement from the candidate.
* **Candidate Acceptance**: Indicate whether the candidate has accepted the nomination. Select "Yes - Accepted", "Pending", or "No - Declined".
* **Supporting Members**: Enter the number of community members supporting this nomination.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information about the nomination.

Note: This guide is intended to be a general help guide, not a comprehensive rulebook. If you have specific questions or concerns, please contact the community association for further assistance.
