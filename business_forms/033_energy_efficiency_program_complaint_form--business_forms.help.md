# Energy Efficiency Program Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect complaints or inquiries related to the energy efficiency program. Please fill out this form if you have experienced an issue with your energy consumption, billing, or any other aspect of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Describe your complaint or inquiry in detail, including any relevant dates and times.
3. Identify the contact person responsible for handling your complaint or inquiry.
4. Select the type of complaint you are experiencing (heating, cooling, lighting, etc.).
5. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (1): This field is optional and asks for your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Complainant Details** (2): Enter a brief description of who is filing the complaint.
* **Complaint Description** (3): A detailed description of your complaint or inquiry, including any relevant dates and times.
* **Contact Person** (4): Choose from the list the contact person responsible for handling your complaint or inquiry.
* **Contact Phone** (5): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email** (6): Enter your email address.
* **Complaint Type** (7): Select one or more of the following options: Heating, Cooling, Lighting.
* **Complaint Date** (8): Enter the date of your complaint or inquiry.
* **Complaint Time** (9): Enter the time of your complaint or inquiry.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you have not already submitted a similar complaint or inquiry before filling out this form.
* Double-check that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the energy efficiency program staff for assistance.
