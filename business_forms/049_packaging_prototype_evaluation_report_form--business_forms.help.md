# Packaging Prototype Evaluation Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Packaging Prototype Evaluation Report Form is designed to collect feedback and opinions on packaging prototypes. This form will help users evaluate and compare different packaging prototypes, providing valuable insights for product designers and developers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the overall impression of the packaging prototype.
2. Select the ease of use and functionality of the packaging prototype.
3. Choose the visual appeal of the packaging prototype.
4. Provide any comments or feedback you may have on the packaging prototype.
5. Please add your signature as one of the signers, as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Impression** (`What is the overall impression of the packaging?`): Select one of the options: Very good, Good, Average, Poor.
	+ This field is used to gather the overall opinion of the packaging prototype. Please select the option that best describes your impression.
* **Ease of Use and Functionality** (`Ease of use and functionality`): Select all that apply:
	+ Very easy
	+ Easy
	+ Average
	+ Difficult
	+ Very difficult
	+ This field is used to evaluate the ease of use and functionality of the packaging prototype.
* **Visual Appeal** (`Visual appeal`): Select all that apply:
	+ Highly visually appealing
	+ Moderately visually appealing
	+ Not visually appealing
	+ Not at all visually appealing
	+ This field is used to gather feedback on the visual appeal of the packaging prototype.
* **Comments (if any)**: Please provide any comments or feedback you may have on the packaging prototype.
	+ This field is a free text field for you to provide any additional information or comments on the packaging prototype.
* **Signer 1** (`Signer 1`): Please add your signature here.
	+ This field is for you to sign as one of the signers.
* **Signer 2** (`Signer 2`): Please add your signature here.
	+ This field is for you to sign as one of the signers.
* **Signer 3** (`Signer 3`): Please add your signature here.
	+ This field is for you to sign as one of the signers.

## Tips
* Please complete the form carefully and honestly.
* Make sure to select the options that best describe your opinion on the packaging prototype.
* You can add your signature in any format, but please make sure to include your name and title/position.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
