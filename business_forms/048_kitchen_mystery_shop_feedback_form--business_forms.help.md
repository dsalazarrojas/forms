# Kitchen Mystery Shop Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our kitchen mystery shop. Your input is valuable to us and will help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill in your name, which is the name we should use to address your feedback.
* Enter your email address, which is how we will contact you for any follow-up questions or to confirm receipt of your feedback.
* Choose a rating score that reflects your overall experience with our shop (e.g. 1-5).
* Provide any additional comments about your experience in the "Comment" field. This is your chance to share any positive or negative experiences, and any suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (1): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our feedback records.
* **Email** (2): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your feedback.
* **Rating** (3): Choose a score between 1 and 5 that reflects your overall experience with our shop. A score of 1 indicates a very poor experience, while 5 indicates an excellent experience.
* **Comment** (4): Share any additional comments or suggestions about your experience with our shop. This will help us understand what we are doing well and where we can improve.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form. Your feedback is valuable to us.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will keep your feedback confidential and use it only to improve our services.
