# Seller Readiness Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Seller Readiness Assessment is a form designed to evaluate the readiness of a seller to perform sales activities. It assesses the seller's sales skills, customer interaction, sales strategy, and business process. This form helps to identify areas of improvement and provides a score based on the seller's readiness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the relevant information. For each page, you will find a list of fields with corresponding labels. Please select the most accurate answer for each field and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Business Form** (`seller_readiness_assessment`, number, required: false): This field is used to select the type of business form. Please choose from the following options: Business Forms, Assessment Forms.
* **2. Categories** (`categories`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select the categories related to the business form. Please choose from the following options: Business Forms, Assessment Forms.
* **3. Description** (`description`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide a brief description of the business form.
* **4. Seller Readiness** (`seller_readiness`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to select the level of seller readiness. Please choose from the following options: Not prepared, Prepared, Somewhat prepared, Not at all prepared.
* **5. Sales Efforts** (`sales_efforts`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select the sales efforts. Please choose from the following options: In-store, Online, Phone, Email, Social media.
* **6. Sales Channels** (`sales_channels`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select the sales channels. Please choose from the following options: Online, Offline, Social media.
* **7. Sales Target** (`sales_target`, number, required: false): This field is used to set a sales target.
* **8. Sales Goal** (`sales_goal`, number, required: false): This field is used to set a sales goal.
* **9. Sales Pain Points** (`sales_pain_points`, text, required: false): This field is used to describe the sales pain points.
* **10. Sales Pain Points Hints** (`sales_pain_points_hints`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide more information about the sales pain points.
* **11. Sales Success Factors** (`sales_success_factors`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select the sales success factors. Please choose from the following options: Sales skills, Marketing skills, Communication skills, Presentation skills, Product knowledge.
* **12. Sales Success Factors Hints** (`sales_success_factors_hints`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide more information about the sales success factors.
* **13. Customer Interaction** (`customer_interaction`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select the customer interaction methods. Please choose from the following options: In-person, Online, Phone, Email, Chat.
* **14. Customer Interaction Hints** (`customer_interaction_hints`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide more information about the customer interaction.
* **15. Sales Strategy** (`sales_strategy`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select the sales strategy. Please choose from the following options: Product-led, Solution-led, Consultative, Needs-led.
* **16. Sales Strategy Hints** (`sales_strategy_hints`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide more information about the sales strategy.
* **17. Business Process** (`business_process`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select the business process. Please choose from the following options: Formal, Informal, Documented, Unstructured.
* **18. Business Process Hints** (`business_process_hints`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide more information about the business process.
* **19. Sales Process** (`sales_process`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select the sales process. Please choose from the following options: Formal, Informal, Documented, Unstructured.
* **20. Sales Process Hints** (`sales_process_hints`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide more information about the sales process.
* **21. Sales Process Organization** (`sales_process_organiz`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select the sales process organization. Please choose from the following options: Centralized, Decentralized, Hierarchical, Flat.
* **22. Sales Process Organization Hints** (`sales_process_organiz_hints`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide more information about the sales process organization.
* **23. Sales Control** (`sales_control`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select the sales control. Please choose from the following options: High, Medium, Low.
* **24. Sales Control Hints** (`sales_control_hints`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide more information about the sales control.
* **25. Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required: false): This field is used to describe the next steps for improvement.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to get a reliable assessment.
* If you are unsure about any field, please provide more information in the corresponding hints field.
* This form is designed to provide a score based on your answers. Please take the score as a guide to improve your sales readiness.
* If you have any questions or need help, please reach out to the support team.
