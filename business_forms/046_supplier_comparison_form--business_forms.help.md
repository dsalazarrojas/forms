# Supplier Comparison Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about suppliers for the purpose of comparison and decision-making. It is recommended to only fill out this form for active suppliers with an active review status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Go through each page of the form, one page at a time.
* Start by filling out the "supplier info" page with general information about the supplier.
* On the "quotes" page, list the quotes provided by the supplier.
* On the "decision notes" page, provide any additional notes about the decision-making process for this supplier.
* If the supplier has been selected, mark "Yes" for "vendor selection".
* Fill out the "contact info" page with any relevant contact information for the supplier.
* Enter any terms of the quote on the "quote terms" page.
* On the "additional info" page, provide any additional information about the supplier.
* Mark the review status of the supplier on the "review status" page.
* Enter the date and time of the review on the "review date" and "review time" pages.
* Enter the phone number and email of the contact on the "phone" and "email" pages.
* On the "notes" page, provide any additional notes about the supplier.
* On the second "review notes" page, provide any additional review notes.
* On the "supplier status" page, mark the status of the supplier as active or inactive.
* On the "decision notes" page, provide any additional decision notes.
* On the "review notes2" page, provide any additional review notes.
* On the "review status2" page, mark the review status of the supplier as active or inactive.
* Enter the date and time of the second review on the "review date2" and "review time2" pages.
* Enter the phone number and email of the contact on the "phone2" and "email2" pages.
* On the "review notes3" page, provide any additional review notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Info**: This is the first page of the form where you will enter general information about the supplier.
  * Enter the name and description of the supplier.
* **Quotes**: On this page, list the quotes provided by the supplier.
* **Decision Notes**: This page is for providing additional notes about the decision-making process for this supplier.
* **Vendor Selection**: Mark "Yes" if this supplier has been selected.
* **Contact Info**: Enter any relevant contact information for the supplier.
* **Quote Terms**: Enter any terms of the quote provided by the supplier.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about the supplier.
* **Review Status**: Mark the review status of the supplier as active or inactive.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Time**: Enter the time of the review.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact.
* **Email**: Enter the email of the contact.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the supplier.
* **Review Notes**: Enter any additional review notes.
* **Supplier Status**: Mark the supplier status as active or inactive.
* **Decision Notes**: Enter any additional decision notes.
* **Review Notes 2**: Enter any additional review notes.
* **Review Status 2**: Mark the review status of the supplier as active or inactive.
* **Review Date 2**: Enter the date of the second review.
* **Review Time 2**: Enter the time of the second review.
* **Phone 2**: Enter the phone number of the contact.
* **Email 2**: Enter the email of the contact.
* **Review Notes 3**: Enter any additional review notes.
