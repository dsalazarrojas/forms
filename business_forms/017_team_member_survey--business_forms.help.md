# Team Member Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Member Survey form is designed to collect feedback from employees about their work experience, departments, and overall satisfaction. This survey aims to gather insights for future development and employee engagement initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Employee Name.
2. Enter your email address for us to contact you in case of any issues.
3. Select your Department from the list of options.
4. Enter your Job Title.
5. Enter your Years of Service.
6. Fill in the name of your immediate Manager.
7. Fill in the name of your Team Leader.
8. For each question, select the response that best describes your level of satisfaction.
   * Team Member Satisfaction: Satisfied or Dissatisfied
   * Department Satisfaction: Satisfied or Dissatisfied
   * Job Performance: Yes or No
   * Job Satisfaction: Satisfied or Dissatisfied
   * Engagement: Yes or No
   * Team Satisfaction: Satisfied or Dissatisfied
   * Team Leadership: Yes or No
   * Job Duties: Yes or No
   * Company Culture: Yes or No
   * Benefits: Yes or No
   * Compensation: Yes or No
   * Work Life Balance: Yes or No
   * Career Growth: Yes or No
   * Team Work: Yes or No
   * Feedback: Yes or No
   * Communication: Yes or No
   * Job Security: Yes or No
