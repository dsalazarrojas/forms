# Project Management Board - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Management Board form is designed to provide project management capabilities, specifically for managing and tracking projects within an organization. This form helps project managers, team leaders, or coordinators centralize project information and facilitate collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project title in the **Project Management Board** field.
2. In the **Task Management** field, describe the tasks related to your project.
3. In the **Deadline Management** field, set and track the deadlines for your project.
4. In the **Progress Tracking** field, track your project's progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Management Board**
	+ (text): This is the main section for your project title.
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: Enter the name of your project to track it.
* **Task Management**
	+ (text): Use this field to describe the tasks related to your project.
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: List or describe the tasks you need to complete for your project.
* **Deadline Management**
	+ (text): Set and track the deadlines for your project tasks and milestones.
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: Enter the deadlines for your project's tasks and milestones.
* **Progress Tracking**
	+ (text): Track your project's progress by entering updates and status.
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: Enter updates on your project's progress and status.
