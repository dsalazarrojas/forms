# Candidate Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a candidate, including their contact details, job search preferences, experience, and other relevant details. It helps recruiters to get a comprehensive view of the candidate's profile and identify the most suitable candidates for a job opening.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the candidate's basic details, including full name, email address, phone number, and current location.
2. Provide their LinkedIn profile URL if applicable.
3. Indicate their current status (e.g., actively looking, open to offers, not looking, employed, etc.).
4. List the job titles they are interested in pursuing.
5. Select the industries they prefer to work in.
6. Specify their desired location (e.g., remote, hybrid, or a specific city).
7. Enter their minimum expected salary (annual amount and currency).
8. Select the duration of their notice period.
9. Add a summary of their experience, if applicable.
10. Enter their total years of professional experience.
11. List their top 3 core competencies or skills.
12. Enter the name of their current or last employer.
13. Provide internal recruiter information if applicable.
14. Check if the candidate was referred by an internal employee.
15. Enter the name of the agency that referred the candidate, if applicable.
16. Add any notes for interviewers.
17. Enter the date of entry into the company database.
18. Review and submit the form to ensure all required fields are filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Candidate Details Form, text, required): Enter the candidate's full name.
* **Email Address** (Candidate Details Form, email, required): Provide the candidate's primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (Candidate Details Form, text, required): Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Current Location** (Candidate Details Form, text, required): Specify the candidate's current city and country.
* **LinkedIn Profile URL** (Candidate Details Form, text, optional): Add the candidate's LinkedIn profile URL if applicable.
* **Current Status** (Candidate Details Form, select_one, required): Choose the candidate's current status (e.g., actively looking, open to offers, not looking, employed, etc.).
* **Target Job Titles** (Candidate Details Form, text, required): List the job titles the candidate is interested in pursuing.
* **Preferred Industries** (Candidate Details Form, select_multiple, required): Select the industries the candidate prefers to work in.
* **Desired Location** (Candidate Details Form, text, required): Specify the candidate's desired location (e.g., remote, hybrid, or specific city).
* **Minimum Expected Salary** (Candidate Details Form, number, optional): Enter the candidate's minimum expected salary (annual amount and currency).
* **What is your notice period** (Candidate Details Form, select_one, required): Select the duration of the candidate's notice period.
* **Experience Summary** (Candidate Details Form, note, optional): Add a summary of the candidate's experience, if applicable.
* **Total Years of Experience** (Candidate Details Form, number, required): Enter the candidate's total years of professional experience.
* **Core Competencies** (Candidate Details Form, text, required): List the candidate's top 3 core competencies or skills.
* **Current or Last Employer** (Candidate Details Form, text, required): Enter the name of the candidate's current or last employer.
* **Recruiter Information** (Candidate Details Form, note, optional): Provide internal recruiter information if applicable.
* **Assigned Recruiter Name** (Candidate Details Form, text, optional): Enter the name of the internal recruiter managing this candidate.
* **Internal Referral** (Candidate Details Form, select_one, required): Check if the candidate was referred by an internal employee.
* **Agency Name** (Candidate Details Form, text, optional): Enter the name of the agency that referred the candidate, if applicable.
* **Notes for Interviewers** (Candidate Details Form, text, optional): Add any notes for interviewers.
* **Date of Entry** (Candidate Details Form, date, required): Enter the date of entry into the company database.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
- Review and submit the form once all required fields are filled.
- Double-check the candidate's details for accuracy and completeness.
