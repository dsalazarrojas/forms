# Shelf Compliance Audit Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help store owners and managers ensure their shelves are in compliance with established standards. The form is used to assess the current state of the shelf and identify areas that require improvement. It will help store owners take corrective actions to maintain a high level of quality and safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. For multiple-choice questions (e.g., store_standards_checklist and photos), select all the options that apply.
4. For text fields (e.g., store_conditions and follow_up_actions), provide a brief but detailed description of the current state of the shelf and any corrective actions you plan to take.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **store_conditions** (`store_conditions`, text, required/optional): Describe the current condition of the shelf, including any issues or concerns you've encountered. This is a free-text field where you can provide as much detail as necessary.
* **store_standards_checklist** (`store_standards_checklist`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the store standards that apply to your store, indicating if they are currently met or not.
* **photos** (`photos`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if the store has any photos or visual aids to support their compliance, such as labels, signs, or equipment certifications.
* **follow_up_actions** (`follow_up_actions`, text, required/optional): Describe any follow-up actions you plan to take to address any issues or concerns identified during the audit, such as training staff, purchasing new equipment, or updating policies.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields or questions, contact the compliance officer for guidance.

Please note that this form is a self-assessment tool and is not a substitute for a full compliance audit. If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the compliance officer for further guidance.
