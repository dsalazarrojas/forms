# Insurance Policy Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Policy Complaint Form is designed to collect information for policyholder complaints about their insurance policies. This form is intended for policyholders and their representatives to submit complaints related to their account or policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine if your complaint is already being handled by another department or channel. If it is, please contact them directly.
2.  If your complaint is related to your account or policy, proceed to fill out the form.
3.  Answer the complaint type question: Do you have a "Yes" or "No" for this complaint?
4.  Enter your policy number, which is usually found on your policy documents.
5.  Provide your name as the policyholder or representative.
6.  Enter the date of the complaint.
7.  Describe the details of your complaint in the designated field.
8.  Include any relevant contact information for the complaint.
9.  (Optional) Attach any photos or evidence related to the complaint.
10.  Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Complaint type** (select_one, required): This field asks you to confirm if you are experiencing a complaint.
    Please choose "Yes" if you are submitting a complaint, or "No" if this form is not applicable to you.
*   **Policy number** (text, required): This is usually found on your policy documents, such as your ID card.
*   **Policy holder name** (text, required): This is your name as the policyholder or the name of the person representing you.
*   **Complaint date** (date, required): Enter the date when the complaint occurred.
*   **Complaint details** (text, required): Describe the issue with your policy. Be as specific as possible to help us better understand your concern.
*   **Complaint contact info** (text, required): Provide any relevant contact information for the complaint, such as phone numbers or email addresses.
*   **Complaint photo** (text, optional): If you have any supporting photos or evidence related to your complaint, please attach them here.
