# partnership-agreement-verification-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to verify and document the terms and conditions of a partnership agreement between two or more parties. It helps ensure that all parties involved are aware of their responsibilities, expectations, and obligations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the partnership terms and conditions**: Carefully read the Partner Terms and Conditions section (Page 1) and ensure you understand the agreement's scope, duration, and any other relevant details.
2. **Enter partnership details**: On Page 2, fill in the Partnership Duration field with the agreed-upon duration of the partnership.
3. **Identify the involved parties**: On Page 3, select the relevant Option(s) from the Partnership Agreement Verification Form Parties field to indicate which parties are involved in the agreement.
4. **Set start and end dates**: Enter the Start Date and End Date of the partnership on Page 4.
5. **Add business address**: On Page 5, enter the business address of the partnership (optional).
6. **List partner names**: On Page 6, enter the names of all partners involved (optional).
7. **Verify partner signatures**: On Page 7, select the relevant Option(s) from the Partner Signatures field to indicate which partner(s) have signed the agreement.
8. **Review the agreement**: On Page 8, use the Agreement Review field to note any changes, revisions, or concerns with the partnership agreement.
9. **Sign the agreement**: On Page 9, select the relevant Option from the Agreement Signature field to indicate which partner has signed the agreement.
10. **Date the agreement signature**: On Page 10, enter the date on which the agreement was signed.
11. **Review and date the agreement**: On Page 11, enter the date on which the agreement was reviewed.
12. **Upload the agreement file**: On Page 12, select the relevant Option from the Agreement File Upload field to indicate which type of file to upload (optional).
13. **Upload the agreement**: On Page 13, upload the partnership agreement file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Partner Terms and Conditions** (`partner_terms_and_conditions`, text, required): Briefly describe the terms and conditions of the partnership, including its scope, duration, and any other relevant details.
- **Partnership Duration** (`partnership_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the partnership in months or years.
- **Partnership Agreement Verification Form Parties** (`partnership_agreement_verification_form_parties`, select_one, required): Select the party involved in the partnership agreement (e.g., Company A and Company B).
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the partnership began.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the partnership will end or has ended.
- **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): Enter the business address of the partnership (e.g., company headquarters).
- **Partner Names** (`partner_names`, text, optional): List the names of all partners involved.
- **Partner Signatures** (`partner_signatures`, select_multiple, optional): Select the partner(s) who have signed the partnership agreement.
- **Agreement Review** (`agreement_review`, note, required): Briefly note any changes, revisions, or concerns with the partnership agreement.
- **Agreement Signature** (`agreement_signature`, select_one, optional): Select the partner who has signed the agreement.
- **Agreement Signature Date** (`agreement_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the agreement was signed.
- **Agreement Review Date** (`agreement_review_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the agreement was reviewed.
- **Agreement File Upload** (`agreement_file_upload`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of file to upload for the partnership agreement (e.g., PDF, Docx).
- **Agreement Upload** (`agreement_upload`, text, optional): Upload a file containing the partnership agreement.
