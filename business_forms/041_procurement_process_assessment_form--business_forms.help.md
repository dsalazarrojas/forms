# Procurement Process Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help procurement managers evaluate the effectiveness of the procurement process within their organization. It will help identify areas for improvement and ensure a smooth and efficient procurement process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about your company.
2. Select the appropriate options for each stage of the procurement process.
3. Evaluate each section carefully and provide honest feedback.
4. Complete the form in its entirety to ensure a comprehensive assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Information (1)
* **Company Name** (`company_info`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Company Name is a required field to identify your company. Please ensure it is accurate.**

* **Procurement Manager's Name** (`procurement_manager_info`, text, required): Enter your name.
