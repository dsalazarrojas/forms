# Management Consultant Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about management consultants, including their personal and professional details, services offered, availability, and other relevant information. This data will help us understand their qualifications, skills, and fit for consulting projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each field carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field, taking into account the options provided.
3. Double-check your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.
4. Review your form submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Management Consultant Information**: A brief description of your management consulting practice or service.
* **First name**: A required field for your first name.
* **Last name**: A required field for your last name.
* **Email**: A required field for your professional email address.
* **Phone number**: A required field for your phone number.
* **Address**: A required field for your contact address.
* **Date of birth**: An optional field for your date of birth.
* **Date of birth** (again): This field seems to be a duplicate of the above, please only fill in one of them.
* **Age**: An optional field for your age.
* **Service areas**: A required field to select one or more areas you offer services in (e.g., IT, Marketing, HR, Operations).
* **Preferred contact method**: A required field to select your preferred contact method (e.g., Phone, Email, Chat, Video call).
* **Availability**: A required field to select your availability in 24-hour format.
* **Start time**: A required field to select your start time.
* **End time**: A required field to select your end time.
* **Work experience**: A required field to select your level of work experience.
* **Education**: A required field to select your level of education (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, Ph.D., Other).
* **Experience**: A required field to select one or more areas of experience (e.g., Consulting, Project management, Financial analysis, Market research).
* **Languages**: A required field to select one or more languages you are proficient in (e.g., English, Spanish, French, Other).
* **Other languages**: An optional field to specify any additional languages.
* **Language proficiency**: A required field to select your level of language proficiency (e.g., Native, Advanced, Basic).
* **Additional availability**: An optional field for any additional availability details.
* **Consultant experience**: An optional field to describe your consultant experience.
* **Consultant notes**: An optional field for any additional consultant notes.
* **Onboarding process**: An optional field to describe your onboarding process.
* **Onboarding process additional**: An optional field for any additional onboarding process details.
