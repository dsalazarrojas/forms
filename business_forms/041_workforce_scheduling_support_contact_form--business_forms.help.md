# Workforce Scheduling Support Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Scheduling Support Contact Form is used to submit requests for scheduling conflicts or support issues. Use this form to communicate your concerns and get help from the support team.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the type of support issue you are experiencing by choosing from the options provided in the "Support Issue" field.
2. Fill in your name, email, and phone number in the "Name", "Email", and "Phone" fields respectively.
3. Enter the department you are a part of in the "Department" field.
4. Choose a date for the shift you are experiencing an issue with in the "Shift Date" field.
5. Enter the start and end times of your shift in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields.
6. Provide any additional comments or information about your issue in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workforce Scheduling Support Contact Form**: This field is the title of the form, and is only for informational purposes.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or on your company records.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so the support team can contact you if needed.
* **Department**: Enter the department you are a part of, as this will help us direct your request to the correct team.
* **Shift Date**: Choose the date of the shift you are experiencing an issue with.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your shift.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Support Issue**: Select the type of support issue you are experiencing, such as a scheduling conflict or time-off request.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional information or comments about your support issue.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can assist you properly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the support team after submitting your form.
* The support team will contact you regarding your support issue as soon as possible.
* Please note that the form is not a guaranteed way to get immediate support, but rather a complementary tool to help us get started on addressing your concerns.
