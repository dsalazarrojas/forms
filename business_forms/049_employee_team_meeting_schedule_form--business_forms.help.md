# Employee Team Meeting Schedule Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help team managers to schedule, plan, and track employee team meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the meeting date and time in the corresponding fields. This will help you to schedule the meeting on the desired date and time.
2. Select the meeting duration in the "Meeting Duration" field. This is the length of time you expect the meeting to last.
3. Choose the meeting location in the "Meeting Location" field. This could be a specific room or a virtual meeting space.
4. Enter the meeting topic in the "Meeting Topic" field. This is a brief description of what you want to discuss during the meeting.
5. Add or remove attendees by selecting them from the "Attendees" field. If an attendee is unable to join, select "No" to indicate this.
6. Set a reminder by selecting "Yes" in the "Reminder Sent" field. If you haven't sent a reminder, select "No".
7. If you've sent a reminder, enter the reminder time in the "Reminder Time" field. This is not required if you select "No" in the "Reminder Sent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, required: false): Choose the location of the meeting.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the length of the meeting in minutes.
* **Meeting Topic** (`meeting_topic`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the purpose of the meeting.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the team members who are attending the meeting. If an attendee cannot join, select "No".
* **Reminder Sent** (`reminder_sent`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you've sent a reminder, "No" otherwise.
* **Reminder Time** (`reminder_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you sent the reminder if you've selected "Yes" in the "Reminder Sent" field.
