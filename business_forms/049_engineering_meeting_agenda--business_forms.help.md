# engineering_meeting_agenda - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create a meeting agenda for engineering meetings, capturing key details such as date, time, topics, and action items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the meeting date in the "Meeting Date" field.
2. Enter the meeting time in the "Meeting Time" field (optional).
3. Select the agenda topics that will be discussed in the "Agenda Topics" field (multiple selections are allowed).
4. Identify the participant(s) attending the meeting in the "Participants" field (optional).
5. Choose the action item(s) to be addressed in the "Action Items" field (single selection allowed).
6. Enter any relevant comments or notes in the "Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the meeting (if applicable).
* **Agenda Topics** (`agenda_topics`, select_multiple, required): Select the topics to be discussed during the meeting. Choose as many as needed from the list.
* **Participants** (`participants`, text, optional): Enter the name(s) of the participant(s) attending the meeting.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, select_one, required): Choose the action item(s) to be addressed during the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the meeting (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the meeting.
