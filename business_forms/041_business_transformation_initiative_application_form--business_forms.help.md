# Business Transformation Initiative Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Transformation Initiative Application Form is used to submit a comprehensive business transformation proposal to the relevant department or management level. This form helps to ensure that all necessary information is provided and that the proposal is well-structured for review and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal and company information (Applicant Name, Company Name, Contact Email, and Contact Phone).
2. Provide details about the transformation initiative (Initiative Title, Initiative Description, and Business Area Being Transformed).
3. Describe the current state and desired future state of the business area being transformed.
4. Outline the scope of the transformation (Enterprise-wide, Department-wide, Process-specific, Pilot Program, or Other).
5. Identify the key business objectives and success metrics for the transformation.
6. Outline the proposed timeline and budget required for the transformation.
7. Specify the resources needed (people, technology, or assets) and potential risks associated with the transformation.
8. Summarize the expected benefits and outcomes of the transformation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name (1)** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as the person submitting this form.
* **Your Role or Title (2)** (`applicant_role`, text, required): Enter your role or title within your organization.
* **Company Name (3)** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Email (4)** (`company_email`, email, required): Enter your company's business email address.
* **Contact Phone (5)** (`company_phone`, text, required): Enter your company's business phone number.
* **Application Submission Date (6)** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Initiative Title (7)** (`initiative_title`, text, required): Enter a brief name for the transformation initiative.
* **Initiative Description (8)** (`initiative_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the proposal.
* **Business Area Being Transformed (9)** (`business_area`, select_one, required): Select the area of the business being transformed (Operations, Technology, Sales and Marketing, Customer Service, Finance, Human Resources, Supply Chain, Multiple Areas, or Other).
* **Scope of Transformation (10)** (`transformation_scope`, select_one, required): Select the scope of the transformation (Enterprise-wide, Department-wide, Process-specific, Pilot Program, or Other).
* **Current State Assessment (11)** (`current_state`, text, required): Describe the current state of the business area being transformed.
* **Desired Future State (12)** (`desired_future_state`, text, required): Describe the desired future state of the business area being transformed.
* **Business Objectives (13)** (`business_objectives`, text, required): Specify the business objectives for the transformation.
* **Success Metrics (14)** (`success_metrics`, text, required): Identify the metrics to measure the success of the transformation.
* **Proposed Timeline (15)** (`timeline`, text, required): Outline the proposed timeline for the transformation, including start and completion dates.
* **Budget Required (16)** (`budget_required`, number, required): Enter the total investment required for the transformation.
* **Resource Requirements (17)** (`resource_requirements`, text, required): Specify the resources needed (people, technology, or assets) for the transformation.
* **Risks and Mitigation Strategies (18)** (`risks_identified`, text, optional): Identify potential challenges and mitigation strategies for the transformation.
* **Expected Benefits (19)** (`expected_benefits`, text, required): Specify the expected benefits and outcomes of the transformation.
* **Internal Recommendation (20)** (`approval_recommendation`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select whether you recommend the initiative (Strongly Recommend, Recommend, Neutral, or Do Not Recommend).

This form is designed to guide you through the process of submitting a business transformation proposal. Please fill in the required fields and answer the questions to ensure your proposal is well-structured and easy to review. 

**Tips:** Before submitting, review your answers carefully to ensure you provide a complete and accurate proposal. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact your department or manager.
