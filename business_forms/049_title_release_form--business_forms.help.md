### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **seller** (`seller`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the seller from the dropdown menu to indicate the party transferring the title.
* **seller address** (`seller address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the seller's address for title release purposes.
* **buyer** (`buyer`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the buyer from the dropdown menu to indicate the party receiving the title.
* **buyer address** (`buyer address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the buyer's address for title release purposes.
* **vehicle year** (`vehicle year`, `number`, required: false): Enter the year of the vehicle for title release purposes.
* **vehicle make** (`vehicle make`, `text`, required: false): Enter the make of the vehicle for title release purposes.
* **vehicle model** (`vehicle model`, `text`, required: false): Enter the model of the vehicle for title release purposes.
* **vehicle VIN** (`vehicle VIN`, `text`, required: false): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) for title release purposes.
* **title number** (`title number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title number for title release purposes.
* **date signed** (`date signed`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the form was signed for title release purposes.

**Tips:**
Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely for a smooth title release process. If you encounter any issues, please contact us for assistance.
