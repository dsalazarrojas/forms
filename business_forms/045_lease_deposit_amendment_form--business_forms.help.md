# lease_deposit_amendment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lease deposit amendment form is used to make changes to a lease deposit agreement between a landlord and a tenant. This form is used to update the deposit amount, payment method, or payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the name of the landlord and tenant involved in the lease deposit agreement.
2. Enter the new deposit amount and select the method of payment.
3. Enter the date of payment and select whether the amended terms include payment terms.
4. Confirm whether the landlord and tenant agree to the amended terms.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes related to the lease deposit amendment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord** (`Landlord`, text, required): Enter the name of the landlord involved in the lease deposit agreement.
* **Tenant** (`Tenant`, text, required): Enter the name of the tenant involved in the lease deposit agreement.
* **Deposit Amount** (`Deposit Amount`, number, required): Enter the new deposit amount.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_multiple, optional): Select the method of payment for the deposit, such as cash, check, or bank transfer.
* **Payment Date** (`Payment Date`, date, required): Enter the date of payment for the amended terms.
* **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, select_one, optional): Select whether the amended terms include payment terms.
* **Deposit Refund** (`Deposit Refund`, text, optional): Enter any information related to the deposit refund.
* **Amended Terms** (`Amended Terms`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the amended terms include any other changes.
* **Agreement Date** (`Agreement Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the agreement to the amended terms.
* **Landlord Confirmation** (`Landlord Confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that the landlord agrees to the amended terms.
* **Tenant Confirmation** (`Tenant Confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that the tenant agrees to the amended terms.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes related to the lease deposit amendment.
