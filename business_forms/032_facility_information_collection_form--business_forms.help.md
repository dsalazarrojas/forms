# Facility Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Information Collection Form is used to collect and store information about various facilities, including hotels, resorts, motels, and other establishments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information for the selected facility.
2. Ensure that you only submit new or updated information for each facility.
3. Double-check your submissions for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Facility Name
• **Facility Name** (`facility_info_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the facility.

### Facility Address
• **Facility Address** (`facility_address_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the facility.

### Facility Location
• **Facility Location** (`facility_location_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of the facility.

### Facility Phone
• **Facility Phone** (`facility_phone_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the facility.

### Facility Email
• **Facility Email** (`facility_email_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the facility.

### Facility Type
• **Facility Type** (`facility_type_1`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of facility (Hotel, Resort, Motel).

### Facility Size
• **Facility Size** (`facility_size_1`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the size of the facility.

### Facility Opening Date
• **Facility Opening Date** (`facility_opening_date_1`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the opening date of the facility.

### Facility Rating
• **Facility Rating** (`facility_rating_1`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the rating of the facility (1 Star, 2 Stars, 3 Stars, 4 Stars, 5 Stars).

### Facility Description
• **Facility Description** (`facility_description_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the facility.

### Facility Owner
• **Facility Owner** (`facility_owner_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the facility owner.

### Facility Manager
• **Facility Manager** (`facility_manager_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the facility manager.

### Facility Hours
• **Facility Hours** (`facility_hours_1`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the operating hours of the facility.

### Facility Website
• **Facility Website** (`facility_website_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the website of the facility.

### Facility Social Media
• **Facility Social Media** (`facility_social_media_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the social media links of the facility.

### Facility Image
• **Facility Image** (`facility_image_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the image URL of the facility.

### Facility Video
• **Facility Video** (`facility_video_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the video URL of the facility.

### Facility Facebook
• **Facility Facebook** (`facility_facebook_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the Facebook link of the facility.

### Facility Twitter
• **Facility Twitter** (`facility_twitter_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the Twitter link of the facility.

### Facility Instagram
• **Facility Instagram** (`facility_instagram_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the Instagram link of the facility.

### Facility Youtube
• **Facility Youtube** (`facility_youtube_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the Youtube link of the facility.

### Facility LinkedIn
• **Facility LinkedIn** (`facility_linkedin_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the LinkedIn link of the facility.

### Facility Google Maps
• **Facility Google Maps** (`facility_googlemaps_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the Google Maps link of the facility.

### Facility Map
• **Facility Map** (`facility_map_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the map URL of the facility.

### Facility Notes
• **Facility Notes** (`facility_notes_1`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the facility.

Please note that all fields are optional unless specified as required.
