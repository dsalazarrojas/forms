# Electrical Work Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a quotation for electrical work, providing a detailed estimate of costs and services for a specific job. It is typically used by electricians or electrical contractors to provide a clear and concise quote for the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled out.
* Enter your customer name and job address in the respective fields.
* Provide a clear and detailed description of the job in the "Job Details" field.
* Select the services required for the job from the "Services" dropdown menu.
* Enter the quantity and rate for each service selected.
* Calculate the total cost by multiplying the quantity and rate for each service.
* Enter the total cost in the "Total Cost" field.
* Select the date for the job.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Job Address** (`job_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the job location.
* **Job Details** (`job_details`, `text`, required): Provide a clear and detailed description of the job.
* **Services** (`services`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the services required for the job from the dropdown menu. Available services:
	+ Electrical Service 1
	+ Electrical Service 2
	+ Electrical Service 3
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity required for each selected service.
* **Rate** (`rate`, `number`, optional): Enter the rate per unit for each selected service.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, `number`, optional): Calculated automatically based on the selected services, quantity, and rate. 
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Select the date for the job.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out to provide a complete quotation.
- Use the "Services" field to select all relevant services for the job.
- Double-check the calculations for the "Total Cost" field to ensure accuracy.
