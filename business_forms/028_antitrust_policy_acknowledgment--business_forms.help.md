# Antitrust Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm employee understanding and acknowledgment of the company's antitrust policy. It is essential to complete this form to demonstrate your understanding and commitment to comply with the policy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **Employee Full Name** in the first text field.
2. Enter your **Employee ID** number in the second text field.
3. Enter your **Work Email** address in the third text field.
4. Select your **Department** from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter your **Job Title** in the fourth text field.
6. Enter your **Manager's Name** in the fifth text field.
7. Select your **Employment Type** from the dropdown menu.
8. Enter the date you started **Hire Date** in the seventh text field.
9. Enter the **Policy Version** number in the eighth text field.
10. Select the date the **Policy Effective Date** in the ninth text field.
11. Read the **Policy Acknowledgment** and confirm that you have received and understand the policy. (Note: This is not a field to fill in, but rather a section to read and acknowledge.)
12. Select **Yes, I have received it** to confirm that you have received the policy.
13. Select **Yes, I have read it** to confirm that you have read the policy.
14. Select **Yes, I understand** to confirm that you understand the policy.
15. Select **Yes, completed** (if applicable) to confirm that you have completed training. If not, select **No, not yet** or **Not required for my role**.
16. Select the date you completed **Training Completion Date** in the seventeenth text field (if applicable).
17. Select **Yes, I understand** to confirm that you understand that price-fixing is prohibited.
18. Select **Yes, I understand** to confirm that you understand that bid-rigging is prohibited.
19. Select **Yes, I understand** to confirm that you understand that market allocation is prohibited.
20. Select **Yes, I understand** to confirm that you understand the rules about competitor communication.
21. Select **Yes, I agree** to confirm that you agree to comply with antitrust laws.
22. Select **Yes, I will report** to confirm that you will report potential violations.
23. Select **No, I have no questions** if you do not have any questions about the policy.
   If you have questions, select **Yes, I have questions** and describe them in the next field.
24. Describe your questions in the **Question Description** text field (if applicable).
25. Select **No, not aware of any** to confirm that you are not aware of any violations.
   If you are aware of any violations, select **Yes, I am aware** and describe them in the next field.
26. Describe any violations you are aware of in the **Violation Description** text field (if applicable).
27. Enter any **Additional Comments** you may have.
28. Enter your **Employee Signature** in the thirtieth text field.
29. Select the **Signature Date** in the thirty-first text field.
30. Enter the name of your **Witness Name** (if required).
31. Enter the **Witness Signature** (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your company ID or personnel file.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique ID number assigned by the company.
* **Work Email**: Enter your company email address that is associated with your account.
* **Department**: Select your department from the list of options provided.
* **Job Title**: Enter your official job title within the company.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your supervisor's name who is responsible for your department or function.
* **Employment Type**: Select your type of employment from the list of options provided.
* **Hire Date**: Enter the date you started your employment with the company.
* **Policy Version**: Enter the version number of the policy you are acknowledging.
* **Policy Effective Date**: Enter the date the policy took effect.
* **Policy Acknowledgment**: This section is for you to read and understand the policy. Please confirm that you have received, read, and understand it.
* **I have received the antitrust policy**: Select this option to confirm that you have received the policy.
* **I have read the antitrust policy**: Select this option to confirm that you have read the policy.
* **I understand the antitrust policy**: Select this option to confirm that you understand the policy.
* **Antitrust Training Completed**: If you have completed training, select **Yes, completed**. If not, select **No, not yet** or **Not required for my role**.
* **Training Completion Date**: If you completed training, select the date you completed it.
* **I understand price-fixing is prohibited**: Select this option to confirm that you understand that price-fixing is prohibited.
* **I understand bid-rigging is prohibited**: Select this option to confirm that you understand that bid-rigging is prohibited.
* **I understand market allocation is prohibited**: Select this option to confirm that you understand that market allocation is prohibited.
* **I understand rules about competitor communication**: Select this option to confirm that you understand the rules about competitor communication.
* **I agree to comply with antitrust laws**: Select this option to confirm that you agree to comply with antitrust laws.
* **I will report potential violations**: Select this option to confirm that you will report potential violations.
* **Do you have questions about the policy**: Select **No, I have no questions** if you do not have any questions. If you have questions, select **Yes, I have questions** and describe them in the next field.
* **Question Description**: Describe your questions about the policy.
* **Are you aware of any violations**: Select **No, not aware of any** if you are not aware of any violations. If you are aware of any violations, select **Yes, I am aware** and describe them in the next field.
* **Violation Description**: Describe any violations you are aware of.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any other comments you may have.
* **Employee Signature**: Enter your digital signature to confirm that you have completed and understood the policy.
* **Signature Date**: Select the date you completed the acknowledgment.
* **Witness Name**: Enter the name of your witness (if required).
* **Witness Signature**: Enter the signature of your witness (if required).
* **HR Representative**: Enter the name of your HR representative (if required).

Note: This help guide is a general explanation of the form and its fields. Please refer to your company's antitrust policy for specific guidelines and procedures.
