# industrial_automation_equipment_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about industrial automation equipment, including its condition, maintenance, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant equipment ID number.
2. Enter the equipment name and location.
3. Enter the serial number of the equipment.
4. Assess the condition of the equipment (New, Good, Fair, or Poor).
5. Enter the maintenance date.
6. Enter any additional notes about the equipment.
7. Select the person assigned to inspect the equipment.
8. Enter the date assigned to inspect the equipment.
9. Enter the due date for inspection.
10. Select the priority level of the inspection (High, Medium, or Low).
11. Select the status of the inspection (Open, In Progress, or Closed).
12. Indicate whether the form has been submitted.
13. Select the person who submitted the form.
14. Enter the date the form was submitted.
15. Indicate whether the form has been completed.
16. Select the person who completed the form.
17. Enter the date the form was completed.
18. Indicate whether the form has been revised.
19. Select the person who revised the form.
20. Enter the date the form was revised.
21. Indicate whether the form has been issued.
22. Select the person who issued the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header**: Enter a brief title or description of the form.
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the equipment being inspected.
* **Equipment Name**: Enter the name of the equipment being inspected.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the equipment is installed or used.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the equipment being inspected.
* **Condition**: Assess the condition of the equipment as New, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Maintenance Date**: Enter the date of the last maintenance performed on the equipment.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the equipment.
* **Assigned To**: Select the person assigned to inspect the equipment.
* **Assigned Date**: Enter the date the inspection was assigned to.
* **Due Date**: Enter the date by which the inspection should be completed.
* **Priority**: Select the priority level of the inspection (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Status**: Select the status of the inspection (Open, In Progress, or Closed).
* **Form Submitted**: Indicate whether the form has been submitted.
* **Form Submitted By**: Select the person who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted Date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Form Completed**: Indicate whether the form has been completed.
* **Form Completed By**: Select the person who completed the form.
* **Form Completed Date**: Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Form Revised**: Indicate whether the form has been revised.
* **Form Revised By**: Select the person who revised the form.
* **Form Revised Date**: Enter the date the form was revised.
* **Form Issued**: Indicate whether the form has been issued.
* **Form Issued By**: Select the person who issued the form.
