# Problem Solving And Decision Making Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a structured approach to problem analysis and decision making, helping users to identify potential solutions, plan actions, and track outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Answer each question honestly and thoughtfully, providing as much detail as you need**:
	* Take your time to thoroughly think through each question and provide clear, concise answers.
	* Don't worry about the length of your answers - the more information you provide, the better.
2. **Review and refine your answers as you go**:
	* As you complete each section, review your answers to ensure they align with your thought process.
	* Make adjustments as needed to ensure accuracy and clarity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Problem Statement (Page 1)**:
	+ **Problem Statement** (`problem_statement`, text, optional): Describe the problem you're trying to solve.
	+ This field is for you to concisely state the problem you're trying to address.
* **Solutions (Page 2)**:
	+ **Solutions** (`solutions`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more solutions to the problem.
	+ Select one or more potential solutions from the options provided.
* **Chosen Solution (Page 3)**:
	+ **Chosen Solution** (`chosen_solution`, select_one, optional): Choose the solution you believe is best.
	+ Select the solution you've chosen from the options provided.
* **Goals And Actions (Page 5)**:
	+ **Goals And Actions** (`goals_and_actions`, note, optional): Plan your goals and actions for solving the problem.
	+ Write down your goals and the actions you'll take to achieve them.
* **Problem Analysis (Page 6)**:
	+ **Problem Analysis** (`problem_analysis`, text, optional): Analyze the problem with the chosen solution.
	+ Write down your analysis of the problem and how it relates to the chosen solution.
* **Outcomes (Page 7)**:
	+ **Outcomes** (`outcomes`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more outcomes to track.
	+ Select one or more outcomes from the options provided.
* **Tracked Outcomes (Page 8)**:
	+ **Tracked Outcomes** (`tracked_outcomes`, text, optional): Track the outcomes of your actions.
	+ Record any outcomes you observe as a result of your actions.
* **Problem Solving And Decision Making Form (Page 9)**:
	+ **Problem Solving and Decision Making Form** (`problem_solving_and_decision_making_form`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or thoughts.
	+ Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about the form or your problem-solving process.
* **Problem Prophets (Page 10)**:
	+ **Problem Prophets** (`problem_prophets`, text, optional): Identify who or what the problem is.
	+ Write down anyone or anything that is responsible for the problem.
* **Problem Description (Page 11)**:
	+ **Problem Description** (`problem_description`, text, optional): Describe the problem in detail.
	+ Write down a detailed description of the problem you're trying to solve.
* **Solutions Description (Page 12)**:
	+ **Solutions Description** (`solutions_description`, text, optional): Describe your solutions.
	+ Write down a description of your solutions.
* **Chosen Solution Description (Page 13)**:
	+ **Chosen Solution Description** (`chosen_solution_description`, text, optional): Describe your chosen solution.
	+ Write down a description of your chosen solution.
* **Solution Prophets Description (Page 14)**:
	+ **Solution Prophets Description** (`solution_prophets_description`, text, optional): Describe the solution prophets.
	+ Write down a description of the solution prophets.
* **Problem Solving And Decision Making Form Description (Page 15)**:
	+ **Problem Solving and Decision Making Form Description** (`problem_solving_and_decision_making_form_description`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or thoughts.
	+ Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about the form or your problem-solving process.

## Tips
* Take your time and answer each question thoroughly.
* Don't worry about mistakes - you can always revise and refine your answers later.
* Use this form as a tool to improve your problem-solving skills and decision-making processes.
