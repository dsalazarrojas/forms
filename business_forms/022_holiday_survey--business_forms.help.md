# Holiday Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This holiday survey form is designed to collect information about your holiday preferences, contact details, and travel plans. It helps gather feedback and understanding of your needs and preferences for future travel and tourism development.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the "Holiday Survey" page with any relevant details about your holiday.
2.  Move on to the "Contact Information" page to enter your contact details.
3.  On the "Vacation Preferences" page, let us know how you prefer to spend your holiday.
4.  Provide information about your travel plans on the "Travel Details" page.
5.  Add any additional comments or feedback on the "Additional Comments" page.
6.  Review the information you've entered and finalize your responses.
7.  Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Holiday Survey** (`holiday_survey`, text): This is the main question for the survey. It's a blank field where you can input your holiday preferences.
*  **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text): Enter your name, email, and any other contact information here.
*  **Vacation Preferences** (`vacation_preferences`, text): Indicate how you'd like to spend your holiday (e.g., beach, city, nature).
*  **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text): Provide information about your travel plans (e.g., destination, duration).
*  **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions about your holiday.
*  **Review** (`review`, text): Review your responses and make any final adjustments before submitting the form.
*  **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text): Let us know how you'd like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, mail).
*  **Contact Information 2** (`contact_information_2`, text): Another page for more contact information.
*  **Travel Preferences** (`travel_preferences`, text): Indicate what you'd like to do during your holiday (e.g., relax, explore, dine).
*  **Holiday Destination** (`holiday_destination`, text): Specify the destination for your holiday.
