# Financial Statement Access Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Statement Access Registration Form is a tool designed to collect essential details from users who need access to financial statements. This form helps the form administrator to verify the identity of the requestor, their contact information, the type of financial statement they are requesting, and other relevant company details. The collected data will aid in the efficient and secure access to financial statements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the registration details in the designated field. Although not required, it can be useful to provide additional information about the reason for requesting access.
2.  Enter your contact details in the designated field. This is a required field as it enables the form administrator to get in touch with you.
3.  Select the type of financial statement you are requesting access to. This ensures that the request is properly routed to the relevant department.
4.  Provide your company details in the designated field. This can be helpful for the form administrator to verify your company's identity and to ensure that you have the authority to request access.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Details** (registration_details, text, required/optional): A text field where you can enter your registration details. If you don't want to fill this field, feel free to skip it.
- **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, required): This field is required and should contain your name, email address, and/or phone number. It will be used to contact you regarding your request.
- **Request Type** (request_type, select_one, required): Select the type of financial statement you need access to, which can be Financial Statement, Bank Statement, or Tax File.
- **Company Details** (company_details, text, optional): This field is not required but can be useful to provide additional details about your company for verification purposes.
