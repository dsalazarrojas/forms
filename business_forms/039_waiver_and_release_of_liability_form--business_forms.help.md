# Waiver And Release Of Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Waiver And Release Of Liability Form is designed to collect information about a participant's activities, medical conditions, and emergency contact information. This form is essential to ensure that participants are aware of their liabilities and responsibilities before engaging in an activity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please fill in the required fields with the correct information. 

- Enter the activity name and date.
- Confirm whether you are releasing your liability for the activity (yes/no).
- Provide a brief description of the activity.
- Confirm whether you are agreeing to the activity (yes/no).
- Sign the release statement as a participant, a parent or guardian, and release representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Activity Name** (`activity_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the activity you will be participating in.
* **Date of Activity** (`activity_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the activity.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_or_guardian_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your parent or guardian (if applicable).
* **Parent or Guardian Contact Information** (`parent_or_guardian_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the contact information of your parent or guardian.
* **Release Statement** (`release_statement`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you are releasing your liability for the activity (yes/no).
* **Liability** (`liability`, text, required): Enter any known liabilities or concerns related to the activity.
* **Activity Description** (`activity_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the activity.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm whether you are agreeing to the activity's terms (yes/no).
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, optional): Sign your name as a participant.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_or_guardian_signature`, note, optional): Sign your parent or guardian's name (if applicable).
* **Release Signature** (`release_signature`, note, optional): Sign your release representative's name.
* **Activity Location** (`activity_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the activity.
* **Activity Duration** (`activity_duration`, time, required): Enter the duration of the activity.
* **Anticipated Start Time** (`activity_anticipated_start_time`, time, required): Enter the anticipated start time of the activity.
* **Participant Emergency Contact Information** (`participant_emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact information.
* **Participant Emergency Relationship** (`participant_emergency_relationship`, text, optional): Enter your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Participant Emergency Phone** (`participant_emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Participant Emergency Address** (`participant_emergency_address`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Participant Emergency Email** (`participant_emergency_email`, email, optional): Enter your emergency contact's email.
* **Participant Medical Condition** (`participant_medical_condition`, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions you have.
* **Participant Medical Allergies** (`participant_medical_allergies`, text, optional): Enter any allergies you have.
* **Participant Medical Equipment** (`participant_medical_equipment`, text, optional): Enter any medical equipment you need to use.
* **Participant Medical Notes** (`participant_medical_notes`, text, optional): Enter any medical notes relevant to the activity.
* **Release Agreement Date** (`release_agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the release agreement.

Please ensure that all required fields are filled accurately and completely.
