# Homeownership Readiness Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the homeownership readiness of an individual or couple by collecting information on their financial capabilities and creditworthiness.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select all that apply for each section.
2.  Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
3.  If you are unsure about any question, please contact us for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First time homebuyer** (form_section_one, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply to determine if you are a first-time homebuyer, a first-time investor, or both.
* **Gross Income** (gross_income, select_one, required: false): Select your gross income from the provided options.
* **Liquid Assets** (assets, number, required: false): Enter all liquid assets, such as stocks, bonds, and cash accounts.
* **Debts** (debts, number, required: false): Enter all debts.
* **Credit Score** (credit_score, select_one, required: false): Select your credit score from the provided options.
* **Employment Status** (employment_status, select_one, required: false): Select your employment status.
* **Co-Borrower Income** (co_borrower_income, number, required: false): Enter the income of your co-borrower.
* **Primary Residence** (primary_residence, select_one, required: false): Select the type of property you are interested in.
* **Property Value** (property_value, number, required: false): Enter the value of the property, including any other assets.
* **Property Mortgage** (property_mortgage, select_one, required: false): Select if you have a property mortgage.
* **Rental Income** (rental_income, number, required: false): Enter the gross rental income, if any.
* **Down Payment** (down_payment, number, required: false): Enter the down payment.
* **Closing Costs** (closing_costs, number, required: false): Enter closing costs.
* **Closing Date** (closing_date, date, required: false): Select the closing date.
* **Credit Score 2** (credit_score_2, select_one, required: false): Select your credit score 2.
* **Co-Borrower Income 2** (co_borrower_income_2, number, required: false): Enter the income of your co-borrower 2.
* **Primary Use** (primary_use, select_one, required: false): Select the primary use of the property.
* **Primary Use 2** (primary_use_2, select_one, required: false): Select the primary use of the property 2.
* **Debt-to-Income Ratio** (debt_to_income, number, required: false): Enter the debt-to-income ratio.
* **Cash Flow** (cash_flow, number, required: false): Enter the cash flow.
* **Co-Borrower Income 3** (co_borrower_income_3, number, required: false): Enter the income of your co-borrower 3.
* **Debt-to-Income Ratio 2** (debt_to_income_2, number, required: false): Enter the debt-to-income ratio 2.
* **Co-Borrower Income 4** (co_borrower_income_4, number, required: false): Enter the income of your co-borrower 4.

## Tips

*   Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for guidance.
*   This form is intended to be used as a comprehensive tool for evaluating homeownership readiness. Please ensure that you have the necessary information and authority to complete this form accurately.
*   Please be aware that some questions may have multiple answers, select all that apply.
*   Please ensure that you have the necessary financial information to complete this form accurately.
