# Trademark Infringement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trademark Infringement Report Form is designed to collect information from parties reporting potential trademark infringements. This form helps us gather necessary details to evaluate the case and take appropriate action.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form's general information sections, including the reported party's and reporting party's details.
2. Select the correct trademark and infringement type from the provided options.
3. Provide accurate descriptions and URLs if applicable.
4. Enter the correct trademark status and reported date/time.
5. Complete the reporting party's contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Form**:
  - **Reported Party**: Enter the name or entity that is allegedly infringing on the trademark (e.g., a company or individual).
  - **Reported Party Address**: Enter the address of the reported party.
  - **Trademark**: Choose the correct trademark from the list (e.g., Trademark 1, Trademark 2, etc.).
  - **Trademark Type**: Select the type of trademark (e.g., Goods, Service, Service & Goods).
  - **Trademark Description**: Enter a brief description of the trademark.
* **Reporting Party**:
  - **Reporting Party**: Enter the name or entity filing the report (e.g., a company or individual).
  - **Reporting Party Address**: Enter the address of the reporting party.
  - **Reporting Party Email**: Enter the email address of the reporting party.
  - **Reporting Party Phone**: Enter the phone number of the reporting party.
  - **Reporting Party Fax**: Enter the fax number of the reporting party.
* **Reported Infringement Details**:
  - **Infringement Type**: Choose the type of infringement (e.g., Counterfeiting, Dilution, etc.).
  - **Infringement Details**: Enter a detailed description of the infringement.
  - **Trademark Status**: Select the status of the trademark (e.g., Registered, Applied, Abandoned, etc.).
  - **Reported Date/Time**: Enter the date and time of the reported infringement.
  - **Reported Time Zone**: Enter the time zone of the reported time.
  - **Trademark Registration Number**: Enter any applicable trademark registration number.
  - **Trademark Image**: Attach or enter the URL for the trademark image.
  - **Reported Item Description**: Enter a description of the item being reported.
  - **Reported Item Image**: Attach or enter the URL for the reported item image.
  - **Reported Item URL**: Enter the URL of the reported item.
