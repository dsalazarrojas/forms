# New Account Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "New Account Information Form" is designed to collect essential details from new account holders.
This form helps our team create a new account and set up the necessary services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Phone Number, if applicable.
3. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting.
4. Submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text, required: false)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (text, required: false)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (email, required: false)**: Enter your email address for communication and account verification.
* **Phone Number (text, required: false)**: If you have a phone number, please enter it for our team's convenience.
*   **Phone Number** is optional and not required to complete the form.
