# Concerned Stakeholder Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information and concerns from stakeholders who are raising issues or concerns that may require further discussion or resolution. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary information to address your concerns effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields: Stakeholder name, Contact email, and Nature of concern.
2. Optional fields: Organization, Contact phone, and Impact description can be filled out if you have additional information to provide.
3. Choose your consent for us to follow up with you for more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stakeholder name** (`Stakeholder name`, text, required): This field collects the full name of the person submitting the form.
* **Organization** (`Organization`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, please provide the name of the organization.
* **Contact email** (`Contact email`, email, required): Provide your email address so we can reach out to you for further discussion or to gather more information.
* **Contact phone** (`Contact phone`, text, optional): If you have a preferred phone number, please provide it.
* **Nature of concern** (`Nature of concern`, text, required): Describe the issue or concern you are raising in detail.
* **Impact description** (`Impact description`, text, optional): Explain how the concern affects you or your organization.
* **Desired outcome** (`Desired outcome`, text, optional): Specify what resolution would satisfy you.
* **Consent to follow up** (`Consent to follow up`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you are willing to be contacted for further discussion or resolution, "False" otherwise.
