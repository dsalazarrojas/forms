# Fashion Affiliate Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Affiliate Agreement Form is used to document and manage relationships between businesses and their affiliates or partners. It collects information about affiliates or partners, including their contact details, affiliation status, agreement terms, and contract specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your Email address in the Email field.
3. Select your Affiliation Type (Affiliate or Partner) in the Affiliation Type field.
4. Enter the Affiliation Start Date in the Affiliation Start Date field.
5. Enter the name of the Affiliated Brand in the Affiliated Brand field.
6. Set the Contract Duration in the Contract Duration field.
7. Enter the Contract Date in the Contract Date field.
8. Select the Agreement Status (Active or Inactive) in the Agreement Status field.
9. Enter the Termination Date in the Termination Date field.
10. Enter any additional Notes in the Notes field.
11. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
12. Enter the Agreement URL in the Agreement URL field.
13. Enter the Agreement Version in the Agreement Version field.
14. Select the Contract Type (Affiliate or Partner) in the Contract Type field.
15. Enter the Contract Start Date in the Contract Start Date field.
16. Enter the Contract End Date in the Contract End Date field.
17. Select the Contract Status (Active or Inactive) in the Contract Status field.
18. Enter any additional Contract Note in the Contract Note field.
19. Enter any additional Agreement Note in the Agreement Note field.
