# Auction Consignment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auction Consignment Form is a necessary step in the process of listing your item(s) for an auction. This form requires you to provide detailed information about the item, its category, and your contact details. The goal is to create a comprehensive listing that will attract potential buyers and facilitate the auction process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed accurately.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure you understand the information being requested.
3. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consignor Name** (`consignor_name`, Required): Enter your name or the name of the business/organization consigning the item.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, Optional): If the consignor name is different from the contact person's name, enter it here.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Required): Enter the email address where you can be reached for inquiries or updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Required): Enter your contact phone number for communication and updates.
* **Mailing Address** (`address`, Required): Enter the address where you would like to receive updates and communications about the item.
* **Item Category** (`item_category`, Required): Choose the category that best describes the item from the list: Fine Art, Antiques, Jewelry, Collectibles, Furniture, or Other.
* **Item Title - Short Description** (`item_title`, Required): Enter a short description of the item, including its title and any notable features.
* **Detailed Description and Condition** (`detailed_description`, Required): Provide a detailed description of the item, including any flaws, damages, or restorations.
* **Provenance and History** (`provenance_history`, Optional): If applicable, describe the item's origin, past ownership, or any notable events in its history.
* **Dimensions** (`item_dimensions`, Optional): Provide the item's dimensions, including height, width, and depth.
* **Estimated Value** (`estimated_value`, Optional): Enter an estimated market value for the item.
* **Reserve Price** (`reserve_price`, Required): Enter the minimum amount you're willing to accept for the item. Set to 0 for no reserve price.
* **Agreed Commission Rate (%)** (`commission_rate_agreed`, Required): Enter the agreed commission rate with our auction house (typically 10-50%).
* **I accept the Consignment Agreement Terms** (`contract_acceptance`, Required): Choose "Yes, I Accept" to agree to our terms and conditions.

Tips:

* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask for assistance.
* Review and understand the consignment agreement before signing it.
