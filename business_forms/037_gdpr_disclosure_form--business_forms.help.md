# gdpr_disclosure_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help your organization provide transparency about how personal data will be collected, used, stored, and protected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each section.
2. Fill in the required information, if applicable.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`User Information`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you are an individual whose personal data is being collected.
* **How information will be used** (`How information will be used`, select_multiple, required): Choose the reasons why your personal data is being collected (e.g., to improve services, sell to third parties, or for marketing purposes).
* **Information shall be shared** (`Information shall be shared`, select_multiple, required): Select where your personal data will be shared (e.g., internal teams, external partners, or both).
* **Data collection** (`Data collection`, number, required): Enter the number of times data will be collected (e.g., once, multiple times, or continuously).
* **Data retention** (`Data retention`, text, required): Describe how long data will be retained (e.g., for a specified period or until further notice).
* **Contact Preferences** (`Contact Preferences`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, both, or not at all).
* **Data Storage** (`Data Storage`, text, required): Describe where data will be stored (e.g., on servers, in the cloud, or both).
* **Data Security** (`Data Security`, text, required): Explain how data will be secured (e.g., with encryption, access controls, or regular audits).
* **Data Transfer** (`Data Transfer`, select_one, required): Select whether data will be transferred (e.g., yes or no).
* **GDPR Policy** (`GDPR Policy`, text, required): Provide a reference to your organization's GDPR policy document or describe how it can be accessed.
* **GDPR Contact** (`GDPR Contact`, email, required): Enter the contact email address for GDPR-related inquiries or complaints.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, select_one, required): Accept the terms and conditions of data handling, including GDPR rules.
