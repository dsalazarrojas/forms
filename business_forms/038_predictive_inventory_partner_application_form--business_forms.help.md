# Predictive Inventory Partner Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for businesses to apply to become predictive inventory partners of our company. It collects information about the company, its integration details, and the expected level of integration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company name.
2. Provide your company address.
3. Enter your contact name and email.
4. Describe your integration details.
5. Specify your collaboration requirements.
6. Choose the integration technologies you have experience with.
7. Specify your partnership expectations.
8. Choose your current integration status.
9. Enter your business details.
10. Provide your contact phone number.
11. Enter your company website.
12. Attach a logo if applicable.
13. Enter a brief business description.
14. Select your application type (Technology Vendor, Retailer, or Logistics Company).
15. Select your business sector.
16. Attach any additional form attachments (e.g., resume, business card).
17. Enter any notes or comments.
18. Select your integration level (Low, Mid, or High).
19. Enter any additional partnership notes.
20. Select any additional integration technologies you have experience with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (1): A general field for any additional information you'd like to attach to your application.
* **Company Name** (2): Your company's name.
* **Address** (3): Your company's address.
* **Contact Name** (4): The name of the person completing this form.
* **Contact Email** (5): The email address of the person completing this form.
* **Integration Details** (6): A description of your integration experience and capabilities.
* **Collaboration Requirements** (7): A description of what you're looking for in a partnership.
* **Integration Technologies** (8): Select any integration technologies you have experience with (e.g., Amazon S3, API Gateway, Azure Functions).
* **Partnership Expectations** (9): A description of what you expect from the partnership.
* **Integration Status** (10): Your current integration status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Business Details** (11): A description of your business.
* **Contact Phone** (12): Your contact phone number.
* **Company Website** (13): Your company's website.
* **Logo Attachment** (14): An optional attachment of your company logo.
* **Business Description** (15): A brief description of your business.
* **Application Type** (16): Select your application type (Technology Vendor, Retailer, or Logistics Company).
* **Business Sector** (17): Select your business sector (Manufacturing, Distribution, or Services).
* **Additional Attachments** (18): Attach any additional form attachments (e.g., resume, business card).
* **Notes** (19): Any additional comments or notes.
* **Integration Level** (20): Select your integration level (Low, Mid, or High).
* **Partnership Notes** (21): Any additional partnership notes.
* **Integration Technologies 2** (22): Select any additional integration technologies you have experience with.
* **Business Details 2** (23): A description of your business (if different from business description).
* **Integration Technologies 3** (24): Select any additional integration technologies you have experience with.
* **Notes 2** (25): Any additional comments or notes.
