# real_estate_investment_guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about users' investment preferences and financial details to generate a real estate investment guide or provide personalized investment recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one or more options from the "Investment Preferences" dropdown menu that describe your investment preferences.
2. Choose your "Financial Status" from the available options.
3. Select your primary "Investment Goals" from the options provided.
4. If you would like, enter your "Income" in the numerical field.
5. If you would like, enter your "Investment Amount" in the numerical field.
6. Choose your "Risk Tolerance" from the available options.
7. Select your desired "Time Frame" from the calendar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investment Preferences**: This field allows you to select one or more of your investment preferences. Choose the options that best describe your investment goals and risk tolerance.
* **Financial Status**: This field asks for your current financial status. Please select the option that accurately describes your financial situation.
* **Investment Goals**: This field is for selecting your primary investment goals. Choose the option that best represents your investment objectives.
* **Income**: If you would like to provide your income, enter it in the numerical field. If you do not have a specific income, you may leave this field blank.
* **Investment Amount**: If you would like to provide your investment amount, enter it in the numerical field. If you do not have a specific amount, you may leave this field blank.
* **Risk Tolerance**: This field asks you to select your risk tolerance level. Choose the option that best represents your comfort level with potential losses.
* **Time Frame**: This field asks you to select your desired time frame for the investment. Please choose the option that best describes your expected time horizon.
