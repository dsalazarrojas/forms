# Trade Season Pass Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Trade Season Pass Registration form is used to collect information for registering a Trade Season Pass. This pass allows users to access various benefits or services for a specific period.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name in the Name field, which is the primary identifier for your registration.
2. Provide your email address in the Email field, which will be used for communication and confirmation of your registration.
3. Enter your phone number in the Phone field, which is used for contact purposes.
4. Select the type of pass you wish to register for in the Pass Type field. This will help in determining the specific benefits or services you will be granted access to.
5. Enter the start date of your pass in the Start Date field, which is the date when your pass will become active.
6. Enter the end date of your pass in the End Date field, which is the date when your pass will expire.
7. Enter your business name in the Business Name field, which is necessary for business registrations.
8. Enter your address in the Address field, which will help in verifying your identity and location.
9. Provide the name of the contact person in the Contact Person field, which is essential for communication and verification purposes.
10. (Optional) You can select additional pass types in the Pass Type 1, Pass Type 2, and Pass Type 3 fields, as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and registration confirmation.
* **Phone** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Pass Type** (4, select_one, required): Select the type of pass you wish to register for.
* **Start Date** (5, date, required): Enter the start date of your pass when it will become active.
* **End Date** (6, date, required): Enter the end date of your pass when it will expire.
* **Business Name** (7, text, required): Enter your business name for registration (only if registering as a business).
* **Address** (8, text, required): Enter your address for verification and location purposes.
* **Contact Person** (9, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for your business or yourself.
* **Pass Type 1** (10, select_multiple, optional): Select additional pass types as needed (if applicable).
* **Pass Type 2** (11, select_multiple, optional): Select additional pass types as needed (if applicable).
* **Pass Type 3** (12, select_multiple, optional): Select additional pass types as needed (if applicable).

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have selected the correct pass type for your registration.
* If you are registering as a business, ensure that you have entered the correct business name and address.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact our support team for assistance.
