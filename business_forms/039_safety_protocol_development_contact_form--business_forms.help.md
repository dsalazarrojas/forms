# Safety Protocol Development Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from customers, staff, or other stakeholders related to safety protocol development. It is used for reporting incidents, requesting support, or other purposes. Please fill out this form to provide your contact information and details about your issue or concern.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Attach any necessary documents or files to support your issue or concern.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`safety_protocol_development_contact_form_1`, text, optional): Enter your name, phone number, or any other relevant contact details.
* **staff** (`staff_id`, number, optional): Enter your staff ID or employee number.
* **name** (`staff_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the person you're representing.
* **department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department that corresponds to your role or affiliation.
	+ Safety
	+ Security
	+ HR
* **issue** (`issue`, text, optional): Describe the issue or concern you're experiencing.
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident or issue.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident or issue.
* **note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your issue.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your issue.
* **attachment** (`attachment`, text, optional): Enter the path to any attached file or document.
* **attachment_data** (`attachment_data`, text, optional): Enter any additional data related to your attachment.
* **signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Enter your electronic signature or approval code.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact your IT department or form administrators for clarification.
- You can attach any necessary documents or files to support your issue or concern.
