# Merchant Terminal Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Merchant Terminal Information Request Form is used to collect terminal location and contact information from merchants who request access to the merchant terminal. This information will be used to provide support and resources to the merchant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information about your terminal location and contact person. Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate communication and support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Merchant Terminal Information Request Form**: This field is a text input where you can enter any additional information about your terminal request.
* **Merchant Name**: This field is where you can enter the name of your company or business.
* **Terminal Address**: Enter the physical address of your terminal location.
* **Terminal City**: Enter the city where your terminal is located.
* **Terminal State**: Enter the state where your terminal is located.
* **Terminal Zip**: Enter the zip code of your terminal location.
* **Terminal Country**: Enter the country where your terminal is located.
* **Terminal Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person who will be the primary contact for your terminal.
* **Terminal Email**: Enter the email address of the person who will be the primary contact for your terminal.
* **Terminal Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who will be the primary contact for your terminal.
* **Terminal Fax**: Enter the fax number of the person who will be the primary contact for your terminal.
* **Terminal Cellphone**: Enter the cell phone number of the person who will be the primary contact for your terminal.
* **Terminal Notes**: This field is a text area where you can enter any additional notes or comments about your terminal.
* **Additional Notes**: This field is another text area where you can enter any additional information about your terminal.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for support.
* If you need to update your information, you can submit a new form with the updated details.
