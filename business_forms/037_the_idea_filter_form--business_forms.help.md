# The Idea Filter Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Idea Filter Form is a tool designed to collect and categorize ideas from the "business_forms" category. It is an original adaptation of public survey topic metadata and does not use proprietary content from any form creation platform.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "What is the problem you are trying to solve?" in the text field on page 1.
2. Provide your solution to the problem in the text field on page 2.
3. If applicable, select the priority level of your idea from the options on page 3.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about your idea in the text area on page 4.
5. Enter the date you think your idea is relevant to in the date field on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the problem you are trying to solve?** (`idea_filter_form_questions_1`, `text`, optional): This is where you describe the problem you are trying to solve. Please provide a clear and concise description of the issue.
* **What is the solution?** (`idea_filter_form_answers_1`, `text`, optional): This is where you provide your proposed solution to the problem. Be as detailed and specific as possible.
* **Priority** (`idea_filter_form_priority_2`, `select_one`, optional): If you want to prioritize your idea, select one of the options: High, Medium, or Low.
* **notes** (`idea_filter_form_notes_1`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your idea. This is a text area where you can provide more information.
* **Date** (`idea_filter_form_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when you think your idea is relevant or applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide clear and concise answers to the questions.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, use the notes field to provide more information.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact us at form@gic.mx.
