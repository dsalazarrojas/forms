# Beacon Industries Contract Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beacon Industries Contract Review Form is a structured document used to collect information for the review and approval process of contracts. It helps ensure that all relevant details are considered before making a final decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Contract Reference ID, which is a unique identifier for the contract.
2. Provide the Customer/Vendor Name, which is the name of the person or organization associated with the contract.
3. Enter the Project or Part Name, which is the name of the project or part being contracted.
4. Enter the Part Number, which is a unique identifier for the part.
5. In the Technical and Quality Review section, select 'True' if you meet all technical specifications, 'False' if you do not, and 'Requires Further Investigation' if you are unsure.
6. In the Quality Standards Required section, select all quality standards that apply to the contract.
7. In the Special Processing Required section, enter any special processing requirements, such as heat treatment or plating.
8. In the Commercial Review section, select 'True' if the delivery schedule is feasible, 'False' if it is not.
9. In the Pricing Agreements section, select 'True' if the pricing is within approved margins, 'False' if it is not.
10. In the Payment Terms section, select the payment term that applies to the contract.
11. In the Review Approvals section, enter your name and the date of the review.
12. In the Final Recommendation section, select one of the options: Approve, Approve with Conditions, Reject, or Return for Modification.
13. In the Reviewer Notes and Comments section, provide any additional comments or concerns related to the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Reference ID** (`contract_id`): A unique identifier for the contract.
* **Customer/Vendor Name** (`customer_name`): The name of the person or organization associated with the contract.
* **Project or Part Name** (`project_name`): The name of the project or part being contracted.
* **Part Number** (`part_number`): A unique identifier for the part.
* **Do we meet all technical specifications?** (`specifications_met`): Select 'True' if you meet all technical specifications, 'False' if you do not, or 'Requires Further Investigation' if you are unsure.
* **Quality Standards Required** (`quality_requirements`): Select all quality standards that apply to the contract.
* **Special Processing Required** (`special_processing`): Enter any special processing requirements, such as heat treatment or plating.
* **Is the delivery schedule feasible?** (`delivery_schedule_feasible`): Select 'True' if the delivery schedule is feasible, 'False' if it is not.
* **Is pricing within approved margins?** (`pricing_agreed`): Select 'True' if the pricing is within approved margins, 'False' if it is not.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Select the payment term that applies to the contract.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`): Enter your name as the reviewer.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`): Enter the date of the review.
* **Final Recommendation** (`final_recommendation`): Select one of the options: Approve, Approve with Conditions, Reject, or Return for Modification.
* **Reviewer Notes and Comments** (`review_notes`): Provide any additional comments or concerns related to the contract.
