# Item Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from buyers related to an item order. It helps our customer service or sales team to understand the buyer's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your buyer name in the **Buyer Name** field.
2. Describe the **Item Ordered** in the **Item Ordered** field.
3. Choose the desired **Desired Delivery Date** for your ordered item.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Buyer Name
* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as the buyer for the item order.

### Item Ordered
* **Item Ordered** (`item_ordered`, text, optional): Describe the item you are interested in purchasing or would like to order.

### Desired Delivery Date
* **Desired Delivery Date** (`desired_delivery_date`, date, optional): Choose the date you would like to receive the ordered item by. Please use the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
