# Non Profit Donation Template WorldPay US - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donor information and donation details for non-profit organizations in the United States using WorldPay US as the payment gateway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Donor Information**
	* Fill in the donor's name, address, and any other relevant details.
	* Ensure that the donor's Tax ID (SSN or EIN) is entered correctly.
2. **Donation Amount**
	* Enter the amount the donor wishes to donate.
3. **Frequency of Giving**
	* Select the frequency of the donation (Monthly, Quarterly, or One-time).
4. **Payment Method**
	* Choose the payment method (Credit Card, Bank Account, or PayPal).
5. **Billing Information (optional)**
	* If paying by credit card, enter the card number, expiry date, and security code.
6. **Email**
	* Enter the donor's email address.
7. **Phone Number (optional)**
	* Enter the donor's phone number.
8. **Notes (optional)**
	* Add any additional comments or notes about the donation.
9. **Confirm Donation**
	* Select Confirm to complete the donation.
10. **Confirm Payment**
	* Select Confirm to complete the payment processing.
11. **Confirm Card**
	* Select Confirm to complete the payment processing (if paying by credit card).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information**
	+ **Donor Name**: Enter the name of the donor to be associated with the donation.
	+ **Donor Address**: Enter the donor's address for correspondence and tax purposes.
	+ **Tax ID**: Enter the donor's Social Security Number (SSN) or Employer Identification Number (EIN) for tax purposes.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount the donor wishes to donate.
* **Frequency of Giving**:
	+ **Monthly**: Select to donate a recurring amount each month.
	+ **Quarterly**: Select to donate a recurring amount every three months.
	+ **One-time**: Select to donate a single amount.
* **Payment Method**:
	+ **Credit Card**: Select to pay by credit card (requires card number, expiry date, and security code).
	+ **Bank Account**: Select to pay by bank transfer or ACH.
	+ **PayPal**: Select to pay by PayPal (requires PayPal account).
* **Billing Information (optional)**: Enter the credit card details if paying by credit card.
	+ **Card Number**: Enter the 16-digit credit card number.
	+ **Expiry Date**: Enter the credit card expiry date (MM/YY).
	+ **Security Code**: Enter the 3-digit credit card security code.
* **Email**: Enter the donor's email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number (optional)**: Enter the donor's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Notes (optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the donation.
* **Confirm Donation**: Confirm to complete the donation.
* **Confirm Payment**:
	+ **Confirm**: Select to complete the payment processing.
	+ **Cancel**: Select to cancel the donation.
* **Confirm Card**:
	+ **Confirm**: Select to complete the payment processing (if paying by credit card).
	+ **Cancel**: Select to cancel the donation.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have the donor's permission to complete this form on their behalf.
* Verify the donor's identity and ensure that they are a legitimate donor.
* Review and update your organization's donor database regularly to avoid duplication of efforts.
