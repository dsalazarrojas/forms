# Net Weight Control Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Net Weight Control Report is used to track and monitor the weight control process in the production line, ensuring that the weight of the products being produced falls within the acceptable tolerance range.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Product Weight** of the product being produced.
2. Select the **Production Date** of the product.
3. Enter the **Production Time** of the product.
4. Select the **Production Line** where the product was produced.
5. Enter the **Weight Tolerance** of the product.
6. Enter any other relevant details, such as **Customer Name**, **Customer Address**, and **Lot Number**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Weight**: Enter the weight of the product being produced (required, number)
* **Production Date**: Enter the date when the product was produced (required, date)
* **Start Weight**: Enter the starting weight of the product (optional, number)
* **Finish Weight**: Enter the finishing weight of the product (optional, number)
* **Lot Number**: Enter the lot number of the product (required, text)
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer who received the product (optional, text)
* **Customer Address**: Enter the address of the customer who received the product (optional, text)
* **Production Time**: Enter the time when the product was produced (required, time)
* **Production Line**: Select the production line where the product was produced (required, select_one)
* **Net Weight Difference**: Enter the difference between the expected and actual weight of the product (optional, number)
* **Weight Tolerance**: Enter the acceptable weight tolerance range for the product (required, number)
* **Product Weight Variance**: Enter the variance in the weight of the product (optional, number)
* **Start Net Weight**: Enter the starting net weight of the product (optional, number)
* **Finish Net Weight**: Enter the finishing net weight of the product (optional, number)
* **Net Weight Tolerance**: Enter the net weight tolerance range for the product (optional, number)
* **Weight Variance**: Enter the variance in the weight of the product (optional, number)

Please note that some fields may have a hint or a specific type, which will guide you through the expected input format. If you're unsure, please review the instructions above or contact your supervisor for further guidance.
