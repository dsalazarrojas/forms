# Business Process Audit Consulting Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Process Audit Consulting Application form is designed for businesses to submit an application for a business process audit consulting service. This service aims to help companies improve their processes, identify areas of improvement, and increase their efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Application Date**: Select the date you are submitting this application.
2. **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
3. **Company Size**: Choose the approximate number of employees in your company.
4. **Primary Contact Name**: Enter the full name of your primary contact person.
5. **Contact Title**: Enter the title or position of your primary contact person (optional).
6. **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of your primary contact person.
7. **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your primary contact person.
8. **Industry Sector**: Select the primary industry sector your company operates in.
9. **Audit Scope**: Describe which processes you would like to audit.
10. **Specific Process Challenges**: Describe the specific challenges you are facing related to these processes.
11. **Expected Outcomes**: Describe what you hope to achieve through the audit.
12. **Additional Information**: Provide any further relevant details or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this application.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Choose the approximate number of employees in your company:
	+ Small (1-50)
	+ Medium (51-250)
	+ Large (251-1000)
	+ Enterprise (1000+)
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your primary contact person.
* **Contact Title** (`primary_contact_title`, text, optional): Enter the title or position of your primary contact person (optional).
* **Email Address** (`primary_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your primary contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`primary_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your primary contact person.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, select_one, required): Select the primary industry sector your company operates in:
	+ Technology
	+ Finance
	+ Healthcare
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Retail
	+ Services
	+ Other
* **Audit Scope** (`audit_scope`, text, required): Describe which processes you would like to audit.
* **Specific Process Challenges** (`specific_challenges`, text, required): Describe the specific challenges you are facing related to these processes.
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, required): Describe what you hope to achieve through the audit.
* **Key Stakeholders** (`key_stakeholders`, text, optional): List the people who should be involved in the audit (optional).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any further relevant details or information (optional).
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Select an estimated budget for the audit:
	+ Under $5,000
	+ $5,000 - $15,000
	+ $15,000 - $30,000
	+ $30,000 - $50,000
	+ Over $50,000
* **Desired Timeline** (`desired_timeline`, text, optional): Enter the date and time when you would like the audit to occur (optional).
* **Previous Audits Conducted** (`previous_audits`, select_one, optional): Have you had audits conducted before?
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unsure
* **Process Documentation Status** (`process_documentation`, select_one, required): Indicate the status of your process documentation:
	+ Fully Documented
	+ Partially Documented
	+ Minimal Documentation
	+ No Documentation
* **Audit Priority** (`audit_priority`, select_one, required): Choose the level of urgency for the audit:
	+ Critical
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Current Systems and Tools** (`current_systems`, text, optional): List the systems and tools you are currently using (optional).
