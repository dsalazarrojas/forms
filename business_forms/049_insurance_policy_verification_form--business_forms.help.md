# insurance_policy_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for users to verify insurance policies in an efficient and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field carefully and accurately to ensure that the information collected is precise.
2. Select the relevant insurance type from the options provided.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the policy as required.
4. Indicate the status of the policy as it currently stands.
5. Provide detailed information about the policy coverage and coverage areas.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy Number** (`insurance_policy_verification_form_page_1`, text): Enter the number of your insurance policy.
* **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, select_one): Select the type of insurance policy you hold, such as Health, Vehicle, Home, or Other.
* **Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, date): Enter the start date of your insurance policy.
* **End Date** (`policy_end_date`, date): Enter the end date of your insurance policy.
* **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, select_multiple): Indicate the current status of your policy, which can be Active, Inactive, or Unknown.
* **Policy Coverage** (`policy_coverage`, text): Enter a brief description of your policy coverage, including any specific features or conditions.
* **Policy Coverage Details** (`coverage_details`, text): Provide detailed information about your policy coverage, such as coverage areas and any specific requirements.
* **Coverage Areas** (`policy_coverage_areas`, select_multiple): Indicate the areas of coverage for your policy, such as Home, Business, or Auto.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Double-check your entries for any errors or discrepancies.
* Use this form as a template or a starting point for your own customized form needs.
