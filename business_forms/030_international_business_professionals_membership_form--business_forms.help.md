# International Business Professionals Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from International Business Professionals who want to become members of the association. The purpose of this form is to gather all necessary details for membership registration.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please ensure you have all the required information ready before starting to fill out the form.
- Read each field's label carefully to understand what information is being requested.
- Enter accurate and complete information for each field.
- Review your form before submission to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Member Information
* **New Member Information** (id: 1, text, required: false): Please provide your name and any other relevant new member information.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (id: 2, text, required: false): Provide your contact information, such as email address and phone number, to ensure smooth communication with the association.

### Membership Information
* **Membership Information** (id: 3, select_one, required: true): Please select 'Yes' if you are interested in becoming a member of the association. This will allow us to process your membership application.

### Company Information
* **Company Information** (id: 4, text, required: false): If you are representing a company, please provide your company's name and any relevant details.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (id: 5, select_multiple, required: false): Please select any additional information you would like to provide about yourself or your company (e.g., areas of expertise, services offered, etc.).

### Upload Files (id: 6, date, required: false): This field is not used in this form and can be ignored.

Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the association's administrators.
- Membership is subject to approval after this form is submitted.
