# Painting Commission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Painting Commission Form is used to gather information about a painting project. This form will ask for your name, contact details, and project description to facilitate communication between the art team and the commissioning party.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Painting Commission Form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Provide your email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your contact number in the field labeled "Contact Number".
5. Describe your project in the field labeled "Description".
6. Enter the name of the artist who will be working on the project in the field labeled "Artist Name".
7. Add any additional details about the commission in the field labeled "Commission Details".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your given name. This will help us know who is commissioning the painting.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your surname. This will help us know who is commissioning the painting.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address. This is where we will contact you about your painting project.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Enter your phone number. This will allow us to get in touch with you about your project.
* **Description** (`description`, text, not required): Describe your painting project in detail. This will help the art team understand what you are looking for.
* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the artist who will be working on your project. This will help us know who will be creating your painting.
* **Commission Details** (`commission_details`, text, not required): Add any additional details about your commission, such as the size of the painting, the location where it will be displayed, or any specific requirements you have.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about your project to ensure that the art team can fulfill your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will get back to you within 24 hours to confirm receipt of your form and discuss further details about your project.
