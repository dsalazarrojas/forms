# Travel Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Expense Claim Form is a tool for employees to claim travel expenses for reimbursement. This form is used to capture essential information about the travel expense, including the date, employee name, department, and expense details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the travel date for the expense you are claiming.
2. Enter your employee name as it appears on your employee ID.
3. Choose your department from the list provided.
4. Select 'Yes' if the expense was for business purposes, or 'No' if it was for personal reasons.
5. Enter the expense amount, using numerical values only.
6. Provide a clear and concise description of the reason for the expense, including any additional details that may be relevant to reimbursement.
7. Describe the business purpose of the expense in a brief but clear manner.
8. Enter any additional details about the expense, such as receipts or other supporting information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Travel Date** (1, date, required/optional): Please select the date of the travel expense you are claiming.
- **Employee Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
- **Department** (3, text, required/optional): Select your department from the list provided.
- **Expense Type** (4, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether the expense was for business or personal purposes.
- **Expense Amount** (5, number, required/optional): Enter the numerical value of the expense, without any currency symbols or units (e.g., $ or %).
- **Reason for Expense** (6, text, required/optional): Provide a clear and concise description of the reason for the expense.
- **Business Purpose** (7, text, required/optional): Describe the business purpose of the expense, if applicable.
- **Expense Details** (8, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the expense, such as receipts or other supporting information.

## Tips

- Ensure you only submit one form per travel expense claim.
- If you have multiple expenses to claim, please submit separate forms for each expense.
- Attach any relevant receipts or documentation to support your claim.
- Review and edit your form before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
