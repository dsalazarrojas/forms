# Director Termination Notice Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to formalize the termination of a director's employment with the company, providing a clear and concise record of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that the form is completed accurately and truthfully, as it will be used as a formal record of the termination.
2. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field, ensuring that it matches their official company records.
3. Enter the date the employment ended in the "Date Employment Ended" field.
4. Provide a clear and concise reason for the termination in the "Reason for Termination" field. You may select one of the provided options or enter a custom reason in the "Reason Other" field.
5. If applicable, enter the number of days' notice given to the director in the "Notice Period" field.
6. Enter the effective date of the termination in the "Effective Date Termination" field.
7. Select the reason for the termination from the provided options, or enter a custom reason in the "Reason Other" field.
8. Enter the date the termination became effective in the "Termination Date" field.
9. Sign and date the form in the "Signature Director" field, confirming that the termination is acknowledged.
10. Obtain a signature from the manager in the "Manager Signature" field, indicating that they are aware of the termination.
11. Enter the manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false):
    + Enter the name of the director being terminated.
* **Date Employment Ended** (`date_employment_ended`, date, required: false):
    + Enter the date the director's employment ended.
* **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, text, required: false):
    + Select one of the provided options or enter a custom reason for the termination.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, number, required: false):
    + Enter the number of days' notice given to the director.
* **Effective Date Termination** (`effective_date_termination`, date, required: false):
    + Enter the date the termination became effective.
* **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, select_one, required: false):
    + Select the reason for the termination from the provided options or enter a custom reason.
* **Reason Other** (`reason_other`, text, required: false):
    + Enter a custom reason for the termination.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, required: false):
    + Enter the date the termination became effective.
* **Signature Director** (`signature_director`, text, required: false):
    + Sign and date the form, acknowledging the termination.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required: false):
    + Obtain a signature from the manager, indicating that they are aware of the termination.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false):
    + Enter the manager's name.

Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully, as this form will be used as a formal record of the director's termination.
