# Retail Product Placement Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this Retail Product Placement Feedback Form is to collect feedback and data about placed products in a retail setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form title to begin.
2. Provide your feedback and details about the product placement in the "Feedback" field.
3. Select the placement details in the "Placement" field, e.g., where the product was placed in the store.
4. Enter the name of the product in the "Product" field.
5. Select the relevant tag for the product in the "Tag" field, e.g., a color or material.
6. Choose the category for the product in the "Category" field.
7. Select the status of the product in the "Status" field, e.g., in stock or discontinued.
8. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required): This is the main text field where you can write your feedback and comments about the product placement.
* **Placement** (`placement`, text, required): Here, you can specify where the product was placed, e.g., near the entrance or on a specific shelf.
* **Product** (`product_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the product you are referring to.
* **Tag** (`product_tag`, select_one, required): Choose a tag that best describes the product, e.g., "Color", "Material", or "Brand".
* **Category** (`product_category`, select_multiple, required): Select all the categories that apply to the product, e.g., "Clothing", "Electronics", or "Home Decor".
* **Status** (`product_status`, select_one, required): Indicate the status of the product, e.g., "In Stock", "Discontinued", or "Coming Soon".
* **Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name so we can associate your feedback with your identity.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any updates or follow-ups.
* **Phone** (`prophets`, text, required): Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you for further clarification.
