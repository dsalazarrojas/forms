# Daily Technician Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to document daily activities and tasks performed by technicians, providing valuable insights into their work and helping with process optimization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Start Date** (page 1) and **End Date** (page 2) to specify the duration of your work period.
2. Provide your **Technician** name and ID (page 3) to identify yourself.
3. Enter the **Maintenance Task** (page 4) to detail the tasks performed.
4. Add any relevant **Notes** (page 5) to describe your activities.
5. Specify whether the task was **Assigned To** (page 6) you or not, and choose a status from the provided options (Active, Inactive).
6. Categorize your task as **Yes** or **No** (page 7) in the given categories.
7. Choose whether the task is located in a specific **Location** (page 8).
8. Enter the **Equipment ID** (page 9) if applicable.
9. Choose the type of **Equipment** (page 10) related to the task.
10. Enter the **Serial Number** (page 11) if applicable.
11. Choose the **Model** (page 12) of the equipment if applicable.
12. Select whether any **Prophets** were used (page 13).
13. Enter the **Location ID** (page 14) if applicable.
14. Enter your **Technician** name (page 15).
15. Specify whether a specific **Assigned Tool** (page 16) was used.
16. Enter the **Assigned User** (page 17) who was assigned to.
17. Add additional **Notes** (page 18-21) for further description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your work period.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your work period.
* **Technician**: Enter your name to identify yourself as the technician completing the form.
* **Maintenance Task**: Describe the task or activity you performed.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the task.
* **Assigned To**: Specify whether you or someone else was assigned to perform the task.
* **Status**: Select the status of the task as Active or Inactive.
* **Category**: Categorize your task as Yes or No in the given categories.
* **Location**: Specify the location of the task.
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the equipment ID number if applicable.
* **Equipment Type**: Choose the type of equipment related to the task.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number if applicable.
* **Model**: Choose the model of the equipment if applicable.
* **Prophets**: Select whether any prophets were used.
* **Location ID**: Enter the location ID number if applicable.
* **Technician**: Enter your name as the technician completing the form.
* **Assigned Tool**: Specify whether a specific tool was assigned to you.
* **Assigned User**: Enter the user who was assigned to the task.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes for further description.

Note: This guide will help ensure accurate and complete reporting of technician activities and tasks, promoting effective operations and maintenance in the business.
