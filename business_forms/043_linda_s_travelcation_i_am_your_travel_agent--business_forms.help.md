# linda_s_travelcation_i_am_your_travel_agent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for travel agents to gather contact information from clients or customers. It's likely used to establish communication or provide travel-related services.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the **first_name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **last_name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **first_name** (Text, Required: False): This is where you'll enter your first name.
- **last_name** (Text, Required: False): This is where you'll enter your last name.
- **email** (Email, Required: False): Enter your email address here to allow the travel agent to contact you.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly for proper delivery of communication from the travel agent.
* Double-check your first and last name for accuracy, as this information will be used to address you.
* If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
