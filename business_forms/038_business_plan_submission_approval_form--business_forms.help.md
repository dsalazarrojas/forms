# Business Plan Submission Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture and evaluate business plans submitted by team members. It provides a structured way to collect necessary information, evaluate the plan's quality, and make an informed approval decision.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Submission Date**: Please select the date the plan was submitted.
2. **Submitted By**: Enter the name and email address of the person submitting the plan.
3. **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company or business.
4. **Plan Title**: Enter a brief title of the plan.
5. **Plan Version**: If the plan has multiple versions, enter the version number (e.g., 1.0, 2.0, etc.).
6. **Review Period**: Specify the period covered by the plan (e.g., quarter, year, etc.).
7. **Plan Document Attached**: Indicate whether a plan document is attached.
8. **Supporting Documents Attached**: Indicate whether any supporting documents (e.g., financial statements, market research) are attached.
9. **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the person reviewing the plan.
10. **Reviewer Email**: Enter the email address of the reviewer.
11. **Reviewer Role**: Enter the title or position of the reviewer.
12. **Content Completeness**: Rate the plan's content completeness (1-5).
13. **Financial Analysis Quality**: Rate the plan's financial analysis quality (1-5).
14. **Market Analysis Quality**: Rate the plan's market analysis quality (1-5).
15. **Operational Plan Quality**: Rate the plan's operational plan quality (1-5).
16. **Presentation Quality**: Rate the plan's presentation quality (1-5).
17. **Overall Quality Score**: Enter the overall quality score (1-10).
18. **Approval Decision**: Select the approval decision (Approved, Approved with Revisions, Conditional Approval, Rejected, Needs Further Review).
19. **Required Revisions**: If applicable, specify any required revisions.
20. **Reviewer Feedback**: Enter any overall comments or feedback.
21. **Next Steps**: Describe the next steps to be taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Date**: The date the plan was submitted to be reviewed.
* **Submitted By**: The name and email address of the person submitting the plan.
* **Company Name**: The name of the company or business.
* **Plan Title**: A brief title of the plan.
* **Plan Version**: The version number of the plan (if multiple versions exist).
* **Review Period**: The period covered by the plan (e.g., quarter, year, etc.).
* **Plan Document Attached**: Check if a plan document is attached.
* **Supporting Documents Attached**: Check if any supporting documents are attached.
* **Reviewer Name**: The name of the person reviewing the plan.
* **Reviewer Email**: The email address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Role**: The title or position of the reviewer.
* **Content Completeness**: Rate the plan's content completeness on a scale of 1-5.
* **Financial Analysis Quality**: Rate the plan's financial analysis quality on a scale of 1-5.
* **Market Analysis Quality**: Rate the plan's market analysis quality on a scale of 1-5.
* **Operational Plan Quality**: Rate the plan's operational plan quality on a scale of 1-5.
* **Presentation Quality**: Rate the plan's presentation quality on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Quality Score**: Enter the overall quality score on a scale of 1-10.
* **Approval Decision**: Select the approval decision (Approved, Approved with Revisions, Conditional Approval, Rejected, Needs Further Review).
* **Required Revisions**: Specify any required revisions if applicable.
* **Reviewer Feedback**: Enter any overall comments or feedback.
* **Next Steps**: Describe the next steps to be taken.
