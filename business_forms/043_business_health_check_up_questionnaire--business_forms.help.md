# Business Health Check Up Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate a business's key areas of concern, including its vision and goals, revenue stability, profit margins, customer satisfaction, market competitiveness, employee engagement, operational efficiency, innovation focus, financial health, strategic planning, risk management, and areas of improvement. By completing this form, users will get a comprehensive picture of their business's strengths and weaknesses, providing valuable insights for decision-making and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your business information, including its name and contact details in the **Business Information** section.
2. Assess your business's current state by selecting the answers to the questions in the **Health Check Questions** section, using the scale provided.
3. Identify your business's key strengths and areas for improvement in the **Strengths and Opportunities** and **Areas for Improvement** sections, respectively.
4. If necessary, provide any additional information or comments in the **Type of Support You Need** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Please enter your business's name as it is registered with the government.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter your name as the contact person for your business.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your business's official email address.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your business's official phone number.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, required): Select the type of business you operate in.
* **Clear Business Vision and Goals** (`vision_clarity`, select one, required): Answer if your business's goals are clearly defined.
	+ No: Goals are not clearly defined.
	+ Somewhat: Goals are somewhat defined, but unclear.
	+ Mostly: Goals are mostly defined, but some areas of ambiguity.
	+ Yes: Goals are clearly defined and well-articulated.
	+ Fully: Goals are very clear and well-communicated throughout the organization.
* **Revenue is Stable and Predictable** (`revenue_stable`, select one, required): Answer if your business's revenue is stable and predictable.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the **Key Strengths**, **Competitive Advantages**, **Growth Opportunities**, and **Top Areas Needing Improvement** fields.
