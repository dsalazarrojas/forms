# Public Record Access Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Public Record Access Authorization Form is used to request access to a public record within the system. This form is essential for obtaining the necessary permissions to view, edit, or delete a record. It's a required step before accessing restricted data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide all required information for a successful submission.
3. Submit the form to your supervisor or manager for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Access** (`Request Access`, Text, Required/Optional): This field is used to briefly describe the reason you need to access the public record.
* **Record Details** (`Record Details`, Text, Required/Optional): Here, enter a detailed description of the record you need access to.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your contact information so that the record owner can reach out to you.
* **Contact Type** (`Contact Type`, Select One, Required/Optional): Choose the method you prefer to contact you: email, phone, or other.
* **Contact** (`Contact`, Text, Required/Optional): If your preferred contact method is email or phone, enter your contact information here. Otherwise, describe the alternative contact method.
* **Record Request** (`Record Request`, Text, Required/Optional): In this field, provide any additional information you'd like to know about the record you're requesting access to.

Note: Make sure you submit the form to your supervisor or manager for review. They will guide you through the approval process. If you have questions, don't hesitate to reach out.
