# BeBody Health And Fitness Coronavirus Self-Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess an individual's health status and eligibility to attend a fitness class during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and email address in the relevant fields.
2. Answer the questions in the Symptom and Exposure Check section, taking care to answer all questions truthfully.
3. Select the correct answers to the questions, using the options provided.
4. Sign the Digital Signature field to confirm that you have answered truthfully.
5. Date and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`client_full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Fitness Class Name and Time** (`class_name_and_time`, text, required): Please enter the name and time of the fitness class you wish to attend.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address where you can be reached in case of any issues or updates.
* **-- Symptom and Exposure Check --** (`assessment_questions_header`, note, required): This section contains questions about your health status and exposure history. Please answer all questions truthfully.
* **Have you had a fever of 100.4 F or higher in the last 24 hours?** (`has_fever`, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you have experienced a fever of 100.4 F or higher in the last 24 hours.
* **Are you experiencing a new or worsening cough or shortness of breath?** (`has_respiratory_symptoms`, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you are experiencing a new or worsening cough or shortness of breath.
* **Have you experienced sudden loss of taste or smell, or unusual fatigue?** (`has_other_symptoms`, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you have experienced sudden loss of taste or smell, or unusual fatigue.
* **Have you been in close contact with someone who tested positive for COVID-19?** (`positive_contact`, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you have been in close contact with someone who tested positive for COVID-19 in the last 14 days.
* **Have you traveled internationally or to a high-risk area recently?** (`travel_history`, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you have traveled internationally or to a high-risk area in the last 14 days.
* **-- Facility Safety Agreement --** (`safety_agreement_header`, note, required): This section contains agreements related to facility safety protocols. Please review carefully before answering.
* **I agree to wear a mask in all common areas of the facility** (`mask_policy_ack`, select_one, required): Answer "I Agree" to confirm that you will wear a mask in all common areas of the facility.
* **I agree to maintain social distancing of 6 feet from other members** (`distancing_ack`, select_one, required): Answer "I Agree" to confirm that you will maintain social distancing of 6 feet from other members.
* **I certify that my answers above are truthful to the best of my knowledge** (`truthful_reporting_ack`, select_one, required): Answer "I Certify" to confirm that your answers are truthful.
* **Digital Signature (Type Name)** (`signature_name`, text, required): Please enter your name to confirm that you have answered truthfully.
* **Assessment Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you are submitting this form.
