# Litigation Expense Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Litigation Expense Authorization Form is used to provide authorization for expenses related to litigation cases. This form is meant to be completed by employees who have incurred expenses in relation to a legal dispute or lawsuit. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee who incurred the expense by choosing an option from the "Employee" dropdown list.
2. Enter the date on which the expense was incurred in the "Expense Date" field.
3. Choose the type of expense by selecting an option from the "Expense Type" dropdown list.
4. Enter a brief description of the expense in the "Expense Details" field.
5. Enter the total amount of the expense in the "Total Expense" field.
6. Select the current approval status of the expense by choosing an option from the "Approval Status" dropdown list.
7. Enter the date on which the expense was approved by the relevant authority in the "Approval Date" field.
8. If applicable, enter the name of the person who approved the expense in the "Approved By" field.
9. If you have any additional comments, please enter them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form** (text, not applicable): This field is the header of the form and does not require input.
* **Employee** (select one, optional): Choose the employee who incurred the expense. This will help identify the person who is responsible for the expense.
* **Expense Date** (date, optional): Enter the date on which the expense was incurred.
* **Expense Type** (select one, optional): Choose the type of expense. This will help track the specific type of expense.
* **Expense Details** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the expense, including any relevant context or information.
* **Total Expense** (number, optional): Enter the total amount of the expense.
* **Approval Status** (select one, optional): Select the current status of the expense: Approved, Not Approved, or Pending.
* **Approval Date** (date, optional): Enter the date on which the expense was approved.
* **Approved By** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the person who approved the expense.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or context related to the expense.

## Tips

* Make sure to only use this form for one specific expense report period.
* Ensure that you have already submitted your expense report for the relevant period.
* If the expense is pending approval, please enter the current approval status and date.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact your supervisor or HR department.
