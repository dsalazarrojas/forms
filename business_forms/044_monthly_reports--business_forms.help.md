# Monthly Reports - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect monthly report information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the report name in the "Report Name" field.
2. Select the month of the report in the "Month" field.
3. Enter the year of the report in the "Year" field.
4. If you want to specify a date range for the report, enter it in the "Date Range" field.
5. Provide any additional notes about the report in the "Notes" field.
6. If you have a contact person for the report, enter their name in the "Contact Person" field.
7. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Phone Number" field.
8. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Email" field.
9. If you want to specify the report type, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Report Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Name** (`report_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the report.
* **Month** (`month`, select_one, required): Select the month of the report. This field is required.
* **Year** (`year`, number, required): Enter the year of the report. This field is required.
* **Date Range** (`date_range`, date, optional): If you want to specify a date range for the report, enter it here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes about the report.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): If you have a contact person for the report, enter their name here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person. This field is required.
* **Report Type** (`report_type`, select_one, optional): If you want to specify the report type, select "Yes" or "No" here.
