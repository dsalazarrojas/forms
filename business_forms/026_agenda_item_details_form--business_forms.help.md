# Agenda Item Details Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information for submitting agenda items to be discussed in an upcoming meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and department or team affiliation in the "Name of Submitter" field.
2. Enter the name of the specific meeting committee or event in the "Meeting Title" field.
3. Select the correct meeting date from the calendar.
4. Provide a clear and concise title for the proposed agenda topic.
5. Enter the expected duration of the meeting item in the "Time Requested" field.
6. Select the purpose of the agenda item from the options (Discussion, Decision, Information Only, or Workshop/Activity).
7. Provide a brief background or context for the item.
8. Specify the resources needed for the meeting item (if any).
9. If applicable, enter the desired outcome or action required.
10. Select the priority level of the agenda item (Low, Medium, High).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Meeting Agenda Item Submission --**: This section is an introduction that provides the context and purpose of the form.
* **Name of Submitter**: Enter your name as the person submitting this agenda item.
* **Department or Team**: Enter your department or team affiliation to help identify your role in submitting this item.
* **Meeting Title**: Enter the name of the specific meeting committee or event.
* **Meeting Date**: Select the correct meeting date from the calendar.
* **Proposed Agenda Topic**: Enter a clear and concise title for the proposed agenda item.
* **Time Requested**: Enter the expected duration of the meeting item in minutes.
* **Item Type**: Select the purpose of the agenda item from the options (Discussion, Decision, Information Only, or Workshop/Activity).
* **Brief Background or Context**: Provide a brief background or context for the item.
* **Desired Outcome**: Enter the desired outcome or action required (if any).
* **Equipment or Resources Needed**: Select all the resources needed for the meeting item (if any).
* **Priority Level**: Select the priority level of the agenda item (Low, Medium, High).

Note: All fields are required unless specified otherwise. If you're unsure about any field, please contact the meeting organizers or support team for assistance.
