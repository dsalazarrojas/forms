# product_listing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to create a product listing with various information such as product name, description, price, image, and more. The form is designed to help you quickly and accurately provide the necessary details for product listing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Product Name** field with the name of the product you want to list.
2. Provide a brief **Product Description** of the product in the text field.
3. Enter the **Product Price** of the product.
4. Upload an **Product Image** by clicking on the image upload button.
5. Add **Product Tags** to help with search and categorization.
6. Enter the **Product URL** where the product can be found.
7. Select the **Product Status** from the dropdown menu (e.g., available, discontinued, new, etc.).
8. Enter the **Product Brand** name.
9. Enter the **Product Color** (if applicable).
10. Enter the **Product Size** (if applicable).
11. Enter the **Product Weight** (if applicable).
12. Enter the **Product Code** (if applicable).
13. Select the **Product Category** and **Subcategory** (if applicable).
14. Add any additional **Product Tags** (if applicable).
15. Enter the **Price** of the product.
16. Select the **Status** of the product.
17. Enter the **Url** of the product.
18. Enter the product **Image** details.
19. Add any additional **Tags** for the product.
