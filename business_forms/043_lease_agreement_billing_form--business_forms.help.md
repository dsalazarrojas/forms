# lease_agreement_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and record lease agreement billing details for a tenant's lease. It helps property managers or administrators to accurately track and report on lease payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Tenant ID** to identify the tenant's account.
2. Enter the **Unit ID** to specify the unit number associated with the lease.
3. Enter the **Landlord Name** if it's available or leave it optional.
4. Select the **Lease Start Date** and **Lease End Date** of the agreement.
5. Enter the **Rent Amount** due for the lease.
6. Choose the **Payment Method** used for the lease payment (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Check).
7. Select the **Payment Status** of the lease (e.g., Paid, Partially Paid, Unpaid).
8. Add any additional **Notes** about the lease agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Id** (Number, Required): Enter the tenant's ID to identify their account.
* **Unit Id** (Number, Required): Enter the unit number associated with the lease.
* **Landlord Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the landlord or leave blank if not available.
* **Lease Start Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the lease agreement.
* **Lease End Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the lease agreement.
* **Rent Amount** (Number, Required): Enter the total rent amount due for the lease.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Choose the payment method used for the lease payment (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Check).
* **Payment Status** (Select One, Required): Select the payment status of the lease (e.g., Paid, Partially Paid, Unpaid).
* **Notes** (Text Area, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the lease agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (Tenant ID, Unit ID, Rent Amount, Payment Method, and Payment Status).
* Double-check the dates entered for the lease start and end dates.
* Use the correct payment method and status to accurately record the lease agreement.
