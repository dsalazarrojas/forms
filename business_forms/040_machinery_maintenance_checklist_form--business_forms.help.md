# Machinery Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Machinery Maintenance Checklist Form is used to document and track maintenance activities performed on machinery within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and access to complete this form.
2. Select the relevant machinery ID (equipment_id) from the options provided.
3. Enter the date of the maintenance activity (issue_date).
4. Provide a brief description of the work performed or issues identified during the maintenance.
5. Select the current status of the repair activity (repair_status).
6. Indicate if a repair is scheduled (repair_scheduled).
7. Enter the name and email of the technician responsible for the maintenance activity (technician).
8. Add any comments or notes about the maintenance activity (comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **equipment_id** (required): Enter the ID of the machinery being maintained, which can be a number or a name.
* **issue_date** (required): Enter the date of the maintenance activity.
* **description** (required): Provide a brief description of the work performed or issues identified during the maintenance.
* **repair_status** (optional): Select the current status of the repair activity:
	+ Active: The repair is currently active and ongoing.
	+ Inactive: The repair is completed or not ongoing.
* **repair_scheduled** (optional): Indicate if a repair is scheduled:
	+ Yes: A repair is scheduled.
	+ No: No repair is scheduled.
* **technician** (required): Enter the name and email address of the technician responsible for the maintenance activity.
* **comments** (optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be skipped if not relevant to the maintenance activity.
