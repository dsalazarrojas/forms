# Color Street Sample Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Color Street Sample Request form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in receiving free nail polish samples. This form helps us understand your contact details and preferences to provide you with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your mailing address in the "Mailing Address" field.
4. If you'd like to receive phone calls, you can enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. In the "What style of nail polish do you prefer?" field, select your preferred style from the provided options.
6. Let us know about your biggest struggle with traditional liquid polish.
7. If you're interested in hosting an online party for free nails, select "Yes, I'm interested."
8. If you'd like to learn about the Stylist opportunity, select "Yes, I'm interested."
9. If you'd like to be tagged when we post about your samples on social media, provide your social media handle in the "Your Facebook or Instagram handle" field.
10. You can add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`request_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`request_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Mailing Address** (`request_address`, text, required): Enter your street, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Phone Number** (`request_phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to receive phone calls, enter your phone number.
* **What style of nail polish do you prefer?** (`preferred_style`, select_multiple, required): Select all the styles you like (Solids, Glitters, Designs, French Manicure).
* **What is your biggest struggle with traditional liquid polish?** (`polish_troubles`, select_one, optional): Choose one from the list (Chipping, Dry Time, Messy Application, Smell).
* **Would you be interested in hosting an online party for free nails?** (`interested_in_party`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you're interested.
* **Would you be interested in learning about the Stylist opportunity?** (`interested_in_business`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you're interested.
* **Your Facebook or Instagram handle** (`social_media_handle`, text, optional): Enter your social media handle so we can tag you when posting about your samples.
* **Additional Comments** (`sample_request_comments`, text, optional): If you have any other questions or comments, enter them here.
