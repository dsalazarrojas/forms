# Covid 19 Pre Appointment Form Flawless - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients for a pre-appointment consultation with a healthcare professional. It contains three simple text input fields to gather essential details about the patient and their contact information, as well as appointment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Patient Info" field with your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
2. Enter your contact details in the "Contact" field.
3. Provide information about your appointment in the "Appointment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info (Patient Info)** (`patient_info`, text, not required): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact (Contact)** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter your contact details, such as your phone number or email address.
* **Appointment (Appointment)** (`appointment_info`, text, not required): Enter information about your appointment, such as the date, time, or reason for the visit.
