# Freight Broker Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freight Broker Referral Form is a tool for collecting information about a referral for a freight broker.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the First Name of the individual or representative from the referred company.
2. Enter the Last Name of the individual or representative.
3. Enter the Company name and/or affiliation with the referred company.
4. Enter the Job Title of the individual or representative.
5. Enter the Phone number where you can be reached.
6. Enter the Email address of the individual or representative.
7. Enter the Address of the referred company.
8. Enter the City of the referred company.
9. Enter the State of the referred company.
10. Enter the Zip code of the referred company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the individual or representative from the referred company.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the individual or representative.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter the company name and/or affiliation with the referred company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the job title of the individual or representative.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the individual or representative.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the referred company.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter the city of the referred company.
* **State** (`state`, text, required: false): Enter the state of the referred company.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required: false): Enter the zip code of the referred company.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
* If any field is not applicable, please leave it blank or provide a note explaining the reason for not filling it out.
