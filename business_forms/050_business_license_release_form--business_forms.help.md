# Business License Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business License Release Form is used to formally release a business license. This form is used to provide all necessary information for the release of an existing business license. It is essential to ensure that the license is up to date and there are no outstanding fees or issues before proceeding with the release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Select the correct type of license being released.
3. Enter the name of the individual or entity holding the license.
4. Enter the business registration number associated with the license.
5. Select the reason for the release request.
6. Enter the proposed release date.
7. Acknowledge any outstanding fees.
8. Confirm the final inspection status.
9. Enter the forwarding address for the final notice.
10. Sign and date the form to authorize the release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **License Holder Name** (`license_holder_full_name_official`, Text, required): Enter the full name of the individual or entity holding the license.
* **Business Registration Number** (`business_reg_number_release_ref`, Text, required): Enter the business registration number associated with the license.
* **Type of License** (`release_license_type_selection`, Select One, required): Select the type of license being released.
* **Reason for Release Request** (`reason_for_release_request_choice`, Select One, required): Choose the reason for the release request.
* **Proposed Release Date** (`proposed_license_release_date`, Date, required): Enter the date when the license should become inactive.
* **Acknowledgment of Fees** (`acknowledgment_of_fees_check`, Select One, required): Confirm that you understand any outstanding fees must be paid before release.
* **Final Inspection Status** (`final_inspection_status_selection`, Select One, required): Confirm if a final site inspection has been completed.
* **Forwarding Address for Final Notice** (`forwarding_address_final_notice_text`, Text, required): Enter the address where the confirmation of release should be sent.
* **Authorized Representative Signature** (`release_auth_signature_name_full`, Text, required): Enter the full name of the authorized representative signing the form.
* **Date Signed** (`release_form_date_completed_official`, Date, required): Date and sign the form to authorize the release.
