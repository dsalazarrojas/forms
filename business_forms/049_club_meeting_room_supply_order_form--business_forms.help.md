# Club Meeting Room Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from meeting organizers and requesters to determine the necessary supplies and equipment needed for their meetings.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete each section in a sequential manner, with each field building upon the previous one.
2.  Provide accurate and complete information in each field.
3.  Review each field carefully to ensure that you provide the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Full Name** (Requester Name, Text, Required): Enter the full name of the person requesting the supplies and equipment.
*  **Club Name** (Club Name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the club or organization hosting the meeting.
*  **Email Address** (Email Address, Email, Required): Enter the email address of the person requesting the supplies and equipment.
*  **Phone Number** (Phone Number, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the person requesting the supplies and equipment.
*  **Meeting Date** (Meeting Date, Date, Required): Enter the date of the meeting in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*  **Meeting Start Time** (Meeting Start Time, Time, Required): Enter the start time of the meeting in the format HH:MM.
*  **Expected Meeting Duration - Hours** (Expected Meeting Duration - Hours, Number, Required): Enter the expected duration of the meeting in hours.
*  **Meeting Room Number or Location** (Meeting Room Number or Location, Text, Required): Enter the room number or location of the meeting.
*  **Expected Number of Attendees** (Expected Number of Attendees, Number, Required): Enter the expected number of attendees for the meeting.
*  **Beverages Needed** (Beverages Needed, Select One, Required): Check the box if beverages are needed.
*  **Beverage Type - Quantity** (Beverage Type - Quantity, Text, Optional): Enter the type and quantity of beverages needed.
*  **Food or Snacks Needed** (Food or Snacks Needed, Select One, Required): Check the box if food or snacks are needed.
*  **Food or Snack Description - Type** (Food or Snack Description - Type, Text, Optional): Enter the type of food or snack needed.
*  **Dietary Accommodations Needed** (Dietary Accommodations Needed, Text, Optional): Enter any dietary accommodations needed.
*  **Other Supplies Needed** (Other Supplies Needed, Select Multiple, Optional): Check the boxes for any additional supplies needed.
*  **Additional Items - Specify** (Additional Items - Specify, Text, Optional): Enter any other supplies not listed.
*  **AV Equipment Needed** (AV Equipment Needed, Select One, Required): Check the box if AV equipment is needed.
*  **Type of AV Equipment** (Type of AV Equipment, Text, Optional): Enter the type of AV equipment needed.
*  **Special Furniture Arrangement Needed** (Special Furniture Arrangement Needed, Text, Optional): Enter any special furniture arrangement needed.
*  **Setup or Delivery Assistance Needed** (Setup or Delivery Assistance Needed, Select One, Required): Check the box if setup or delivery assistance is needed.

## Tips

*  Please complete each section carefully and accurately.
*  Review each field before moving on to the next.
*  If you need help or have questions, contact the meeting organizers or event planners.
*  This form will be reviewed and actioned upon by the meeting planners to ensure a successful meeting.
