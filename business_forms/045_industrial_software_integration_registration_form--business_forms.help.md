# Industrial Software Integration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration tool for companies to provide information about their industrial software integration needs. Fill out this form to help us understand your company's requirements and preferences for integration services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your company's details in the "Company Details" section.
2. In the "Technical Environment" section, describe your company's current technical setup and infrastructure.
3. In the "Integration Requirements" section, list your company's specific software integration needs and requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Details** (`Company Details`, `text`, required/optional: false): Provide a brief description of your company, including its name, industry, and contact information.
* **Technical Environment** (`Technical Environment`, `text`, required/optional: false): Describe your company's current technical setup, including operating system, server configuration, and any relevant software or systems in place.
* **Integration Requirements** (`Integration Requirements`, `text`, required/optional: false): List the specific software integration requirements your company is looking to achieve, including any API keys, authentication methods, or data formats required.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible for each field to ensure we can accurately understand your company's needs.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents that can aid in understanding your company's requirements, you can attach them to this form.
