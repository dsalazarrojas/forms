# Color Street On Hand Stock - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the current on-hand stock of Color Street merchandise, including stylists' stock, stock check dates, and the quantity of various sets of products. The form is essential for inventory management and helps to assess the total retail value of the stock.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Stylist Name** field with the name of the stylist who is currently in stock.
2. Enter the **Stock Check Date** in the format MM/DD/YY.
3. For each category of sets (Glitter, Solid, Art/Design, French, Pedicure, and Seasonal/Limited Edition), enter the quantity of sets that are currently in stock.
4. In the **Stock Value Assessment** field, provide any comments or notes about the current stock, such as any discrepancies or issues.
5. Finally, enter the **Estimated Total Retail Value** of the stock.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stylist Name**: Enter the name of the stylist who is currently in stock.
* **Stock Check Date**: Enter the date on which the stock was last checked.
* **-- Current On Hand Stock --**: This field is a note field for stylist to provide any information about the stock, such as "On hand: 1000 sets" or "On hand: 500 sets, 200 on the way".
* **Glitter Sets Quantity**: Enter the number of Glitter sets currently in stock.
* **Solid Sets Quantity**: Enter the number of Solid sets currently in stock.
* **Art/Design Sets Quantity**: Enter the number of Art/Design sets currently in stock.
* **French Sets Quantity**: Enter the number of French sets currently in stock.
* **Pedicure Sets Quantity**: Enter the number of Pedicure sets currently in stock.
* **Seasonal/Limited Edition Quantity**: Enter the number of Seasonal/Limited Edition sets currently in stock.
* **-- Stock Value Assessment --**: This field is a note field for stylist to provide any comments or notes about the current stock.
* **Estimated Total Retail Value**: Enter the estimated retail value of the stock.
* **Any discrepancies noted?**: If there are any issues with the stock, such as missing or damaged items, enter a brief description.
