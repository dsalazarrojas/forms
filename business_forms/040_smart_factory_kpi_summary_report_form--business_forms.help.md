# Smart Factory KPI Summary Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to summarize the Key Performance Indicators (KPI) of a smart factory, providing an overview of its operational status, production capacity, and other relevant metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the factory location in the `factory location` field.
2. Select the start and end dates for the period you want to analyze using the `start date` and `end date` fields.
3. Provide the total production hours worked during the selected period in the `production hours` field.
4. Enter the production capacity for the factory during the selected period in the `production capacity` field.
5. Enter the actual production values for the selected period in the `production actuals` field.
6. Provide the total production costs for the selected period in the `production costs` field.
7. Select the factory's operational status (`operational`, `not operational`, or `idle`) in the `factory operational status` field.
8. Choose the current production status (`in progress`, `complete`, or `on hold`) for each production task in the `production status` field.
9. Enter any machine IDs that are relevant to the analysis in the `machine ids` field (as a comma-separated list).
10. Describe the statuses of the machines used in the production process in the `machine statuses` field.
11. Select the production planning status (`on schedule`, `ahead of schedule`, `behind schedule`, or `not planned`) in the `production planning status` field.
12. List any issues related to production that have arisen during the selected period in the `production issues` field.
13. Provide additional comments or observations about the production process in the `comments` field.
14. Select the created date for the report in the `created at` field (this will be automatically populated).
15. Select the updated date for the report in the `updated at` field (this will be automatically populated).
16. Enter the name of the person who created the report in the `created by` field (this will be automatically populated).
17. Enter the name of the person who updated the report in the `updated by` field (this will be automatically populated).
18. Enter the factory ID number in the `factory id` field.
19. Enter the name of the factory in the `factory name` field.
20. Enter the address of the factory in the `factory address` field.
21. Enter the city of the factory in the `factory city` field.
22. Enter the state of the factory in the `factory state` field.
23. Enter the country of the factory in the `factory country` field.
24. Enter the post code of the factory in the `factory post code` field.
