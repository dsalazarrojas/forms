# Deal Memo Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Deal Memo Submission form is used to document and track business deals, including their details, terms, and status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant options for **Deal Type** (Sales, Purchase, or Lease) and **Deal Status** (Active or Inactive).
2. Enter the **Sales Contact** and **Legal Contact** details.
3. Describe the **Deal Details**, including any relevant information about the deal.
4. Add or modify the **Deal Terms** as necessary.
5. Choose whether to **Acknowledge** the terms and conditions, and select the corresponding **Acknowledgment Date**.
6. Enter the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Deal Type** (`deal_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of deal (Sales, Purchase, or Lease) to categorize the submission.
* **Sales Contact** (`sales_contact`, text, optional): Enter the sales contact information (name, email, etc.).
* **Legal Contact** (`legal_contact`, text, optional): Enter the legal contact information (name, email, etc.).
* **Deal Details** (`deal_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the deal.
* **Deal Terms** (`deal_terms`, text, optional): Add or modify any deal terms as necessary.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms`, text, optional): Enter any additional terms and conditions related to the deal.
* **Acknowledgment** (`acknowledgment`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you acknowledge the terms and conditions (Yes or No).
* **Acknowledgment Date** (`acknowledgment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of acknowledgment (if applicable).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Deal Value** (`deal_value`, number, optional): Enter the value of the deal.
* **Deal Status** (`deal_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the deal (Active or Inactive).
* **Deal Terms Notes** (`deal_terms_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the deal terms.
* **Submission By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user submitting the deal memo.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission (automatically populated).
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of submission (automatically populated).

**Tips:** Make sure to review and verify all information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
