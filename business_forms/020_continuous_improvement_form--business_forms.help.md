# Continuous Improvement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect continuous improvement ideas from employees for process, quality, cost, and other areas where they believe the organization can be better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your employee name, employee ID (if applicable), and select your department.
2.  Provide the date of submission.
3.  Choose the category of the improvement you are proposing.
4.  Describe the current process and the problem you are trying to address.
5.  Propose a solution and expected benefits.
6.  Select the expected benefits of the improvement.
7.  Estimate the implementation complexity, resources needed, and implementation timeline.
8.  Identify any potential risks and stakeholders involved.
9.  Define success metrics for the improvement.
10. Enter any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as an employee.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, optional): If you have an ID, please enter it.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select your department from the list.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting this proposal.
* **Improvement Category** (`improvement_category`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category of improvement you are proposing.
* **Current Process** (`current_process`, `text`, required): Describe the current situation or process.
* **Problem Statement** (`problem_statement`, `text`, required): State the issue you are trying to address.
* **Proposed Solution** (`proposed_solution`, `text`, required): Describe your proposed improvement idea.
* **Expected Benefits** (`expected_benefits`, `text`, required): Explain the benefits of your proposal.
* **Benefit Type** (`benefit_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all expected benefits that apply.
* **Estimated Cost Savings** (`estimated_cost_savings`, `text`, optional): If applicable, estimate the annual savings.
* **Implementation Complexity** (`implementation_complexity`, `select_one`, required): Rate the difficulty of implementation.
* **Resources Needed** (`resources_needed`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the resources needed for the improvement.
* **Estimated Timeline** (`estimated_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Choose the implementation timeline.
* **Stakeholders Involved** (`stakeholders_involved`, `text`, optional): Identify any stakeholders involved in the improvement.
* **Potential Risks** (`potential_risks`, `text`, optional): Mention any potential risks or challenges.
* **Success Metrics** (`success_metrics`, `text`, required): Define how success will be measured.
* **Similar Improvements** (`similar_improvements`, `text`, optional): If you have seen this improvement elsewhere, describe.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `text`, optional): Identify team members who can help implement the improvement.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, `select_one`, required): Rank the priority level of the improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information.
