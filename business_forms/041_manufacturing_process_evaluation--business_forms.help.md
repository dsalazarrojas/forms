# Manufacturing Process Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Process Evaluation form is designed to assess the effectiveness and risks associated with a company's manufacturing process. This form helps users evaluate the process steps, safety checklist, quality control, critical control points, process risk, safety training, equipment list, critical control points details, quality control details, process steps details, and safety checklist details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the process steps and select all the steps that are part of the manufacturing process.
2. Evaluate the safety checklist and select all the items that are checked during the process.
3. Assess the quality control and select the level of quality control in place (Good, Poor).
4. Identify critical control points and select all the critical points in the process.
5. Evaluate the process risk by entering a numerical value.
6. Check if safety training is provided for the process.
7. List the equipment used in the process.
8. Provide details about critical control points.
9. Enter details about quality control.
10. Enter details about the process steps.
11. Enter details about the safety checklist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Process Steps**: Select all the steps that are part of the manufacturing process. This will help you assess the overall process.
* **Safety Checklist**: Evaluate the safety checklist and select all the items that are checked during the process.
* **Quality Control**: Assess the quality control in place and select the level of quality control (Good, Poor).
* **Critical Control Points**: Identify all the critical control points in the process.
* **Process Risk**: Enter a numerical value to evaluate the process risk.
* **Safety Training**: Check if safety training is provided for the process.
* **Equipment List**: List the equipment used in the process.
* **Critical Control Points Details**: Enter details about critical control points.
* **Quality Control Details**: Enter details about quality control.
* **Process Steps Details**: Enter details about the process steps.
* **Safety Checklist Details**: Enter details about the safety checklist.

## Tips

* Make sure to evaluate each field carefully to ensure accurate results.
* Double-check your answers to ensure they reflect the actual process.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the process owner or team for guidance.
* This form is designed to help you evaluate the manufacturing process, so be honest and thorough in your evaluation.
