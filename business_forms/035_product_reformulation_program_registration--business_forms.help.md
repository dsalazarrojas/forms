# Product Reformulation Program Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from manufacturers or suppliers who want to participate in our Product Reformulation Program. The program aims to help companies reformulate their products to be more sustainable, eco-friendly, and compliant with regulatory requirements. By registering here, you will be providing us with essential details about your product and contact information, which will be reviewed and assessed for eligibility to join the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **General Information**, including any relevant details about your company or organization.
2. Provide your **Product Details**, including information about the product you would like to reformulate.
3. Fill in your **Contact Information**, including your name, email, and phone number.
4. Enter the **Submission Details**, such as any supporting documents you have for your product reformulation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)** (`text`, required): This field is meant for you to provide any relevant details about your company or organization that will help us understand your product reformulation goals and objectives.
* **Product Details (2)** (`text`, required): Here, you will describe the product you would like to reformulate, including its current formulation, intended use, and any relevant sustainability or eco-friendliness features.
* **Contact Information (3)** (`text`, required): Please fill in your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you about your product reformulation.
* **Submission Details (4)** (`text`, required): This is where you can attach any supporting documents, such as certificates of analysis, product datasheets, or other relevant information that will aid in the assessment of your product reformulation.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible in your responses, as this will help us better understand your product reformulation goals and objectives.
* Ensure that all fields are filled in, as this will help us review your submission efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us, and we will get in touch with you as soon as possible.
