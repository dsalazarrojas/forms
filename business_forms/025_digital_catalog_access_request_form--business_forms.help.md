# Digital Catalog Access Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Digital Catalog Access Request Form is used by users to request access to the digital catalog for a specific purpose. This form helps ensure that users understand and agree to the terms and conditions of accessing the catalog.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user details
2. Select the purpose of your access request
3. Agree to the terms and conditions

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`user_details`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter your name and any other relevant information as requested.
* **Catalog Selection** (`catalog_selection`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select the reason for your access request from the provided options.
* **Purpose of Access** (`purpose_access`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the specific purpose of your access request.
* **Terms Agreement 1-22** (`terms_agreement_1-22`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Agree to the terms and conditions for accessing the digital catalog. Select 'Yes' for each term you agree to.

**Tips**

* Make sure to carefully read and understand each term before selecting 'Yes'.
* Ensure you are not duplicating requests or submitting incomplete information.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it for approval.
