# ประก Lighting Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report issues with the lighting in your area. Please use this form to provide detailed information about your complaint, including your contact number and the level of lighting you are experiencing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact number in the "user_input" field.
2. Provide the address where the lighting issue is located in the "address" field.
3. Describe the issue you are experiencing in the "issue_description" field.
4. Specify the location of the issue in the "location" field.
5. Enter the date and time of the complaint in the "date_of_complaint" field.
6. Select the time of day when you first noticed the issue in the "time_of_complaint" field.
7. Rate the lighting level at the location in the "lighting_level" field, using the options: Very Good, Somewhat Good, Fair, or Poor.
8. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_input** (`user_input`, text, required): Enter your name and contact number for us to get in touch with you.
* **address** (`address`, text, required): Provide the address where the issue is located.
* **issue_description** (`issue_description`, text, required): Describe the issue you are experiencing with the lighting.
* **location** (`location`, text, required): Specify the location where the issue is occurring, such as a street or intersection.
* **date_of_complaint** (`date_of_complaint`, date, required): Enter the date you first noticed the issue.
* **time_of_complaint** (`time_of_complaint`, time, required): Select the time of day when you first noticed the issue.
* **lighting_level** (`lighting_level`, select_one, required): Rate the lighting level at the location, using the options: Very Good, Somewhat Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number so we can get in touch with you for further information or updates.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure that we can efficiently address your issue.
* If you have already submitted a complaint for the same issue, please do not resubmit.
