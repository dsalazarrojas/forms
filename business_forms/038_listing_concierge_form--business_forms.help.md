# Listing Concierge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a property for listing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Read each section carefully and fill in the required fields.
- Answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
- Select the correct options for each field as per the options provided.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is the property address?** (`property_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the property.
- **What is the contact info?** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact information related to the property.
- **Is the property Yes/No?** (`is_property`, select_one, true): Indicate if the property is Yes or No.
- **What type of property details is this?** (`property_details_type`, select_one, false): Select the type of property details.
- **Property Details Type 2** (`property_details_type_2`, select_one, false): Select the second type of property details.
- **What is the property status?** (`property_status`, select_one, false): Choose the status of the property (Active or Inactive).
- **What is the status list of this property?** (`property_status_list`, select_multiple, false): Select all applicable statuses for this property.
- **What is the subtype list?** (`property_subtype_list`, select_multiple, false): Select all applicable subtypes for this property.
- **What is the status date?** (`property_date`, date, false): Enter the status date of the property.
- **What status date is this property?** (`status_date`, date, false): Enter the status date of this property.
- **What is the status time for this property?** (`property_time`, time, false): Enter the status time of this property.
- **What status time is this property?** (`status_time`, time, false): Enter the status time of this property.
- **What is the property price range?** (`property_price`, text, false): Enter the price range of the property.
- **From what price range is the property priced?** (`property_price_from`, number, false): Enter the minimum price range.
- **What price range is the property priced?** (`property_price_to`, number, false): Enter the maximum price range.
- **Is this property yes or no?** (`minimalist_form_fields_0`, select_one, false): Indicate if this property is yes or no.
- **Minimalist Form Fields 1** (`minimalist_form_fields_1`, select_one, false): Select the first field of this property.
- **Minimalist Form Fields 2** (`minimalist_form_fields_2`, select_one, false): Select the second field of this property.
