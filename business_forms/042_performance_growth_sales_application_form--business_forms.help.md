# Performance Growth Sales Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Growth Sales Application Form is designed to collect sales performance data for a specific sales period. This form gathers information on sales applicants, contact details, sales performance, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Sales Applicant Info field with your sales name and title.
2.  Provide your Contact Details, including your name, phone number, and email.
3.  Enter your Sales Performance Info, including any relevant data or comments.
4.  Select your Submission Date.
5.  Choose your Sales Region from the available options.
6.  Select the Sales Channel(s) you used for sales.
7.  Choose the Sales Product you worked with.
8.  Select the Sales Quarter that corresponds to the sales period.
9.  Select the Sales Year that corresponds to the sales period.
10. Enter your Sales Manager's name and any additional comments.
11. Enter your Sales Area of responsibility.
12. Enter your Sales Salesman's name (if applicable).
13. Enter your Sales Region Manager's name (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Sales Applicant Info** (1, text, required: false): Enter your full name and title as the sales applicant.
*   **Contact Details** (2, text, required: false): Provide your name, phone number, and email to contact you for further information.
*   **Sales Performance Info** (3, text, required: false): Enter any relevant sales performance data or comments.
*   **Submission Date** (4, date, required: false): Choose the date this form was submitted.
*   **Sales Region** (6, select_one, required: false): Select the sales region that corresponds to your sales activities.
*   **Sales Channel** (7, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable sales channels used for sales.
*   **Sales Product** (8, select_one, required: false): Choose the product you sold.
*   **Sales Quarter** (9, select_one, required: false): Select the sales quarter that corresponds to the sales period.
*   **Sales Year** (10, select_one, required: false): Select the sales year that corresponds to the sales period.
*   **Sales Manager** (11, text, required: false): Enter your sales manager's name.
*   **Sales Area** (12, text, required: false): Enter your sales area of responsibility.
*   **Sales Salesman** (13, text, required: false): Enter the name of the salesperson responsible for sales.
*   **Sales Region Manager** (14, text, required: false): Enter the name of the sales region manager.
