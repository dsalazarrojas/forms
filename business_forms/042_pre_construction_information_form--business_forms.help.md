# Pre Construction Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Construction Information Form is used to collect essential details about a construction project, including contact information, project specifics, and contractor details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Ensure that you enter accurate information in each field.
3. Review the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Address**: Enter the address of the construction project site.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person in charge of the project.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person or project representative.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person or project representative.
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the project site.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the project.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the project.
* **Project Type**: Select the type of project (Construction, Renovation, or Remodeling).
* **Project Size**: Select the size of the project (Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large).
* **Project Status**: Select the current status of the project (Active, In Progress, On Hold, or Completed).
* **Contractor Details**: Enter the details of the contractor, including their name, address, and contact information.
* **Contractor License**: Select whether the contractor has a valid license.
* **Contractor Insurance**: Select whether the contractor has insurance.
* **Contractor Experience**: Enter the number of years the contractor has experience.
* **Contractor Vehicle**: Enter the details of the contractor's vehicle.
* **Contractor Equipment**: List any equipment the contractor will be bringing on site.
* **Contractor Supervise**: Select whether the contractor will supervise the project.
* **Supervision**: Enter the number of supervisors.
* **Contractor Supervision 2**: Enter the second supervision detail (if applicable).
* **Contractor Supervision 3**: Enter the third supervision detail (if applicable).
* **Insurance**: Select whether the project has insurance.
* **Owner Name**: Enter the name of the project owner.
* **Owner Contact Details**: Enter the contact details of the project owner.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the project manager or responsible person for assistance.
