# Small Business Loan Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your business loan inquiry, allowing us to understand your needs and provide the best possible loan solution for your business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit Your Loan Inquiry" button to start the process.
2. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields, which are marked as such.
3. Review your submission before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit Your Loan Inquiry**: A text field where you can type in a brief message about your loan inquiry.
* **Loan Details**: A text field where you can provide more information about your loan needs.
* **Loan Terms**: A select multiple field where you can choose the type of loan you're looking for (Term Length, Collateral Required, Interest Rate).
* **Interest Rate**: A number field where you can input the desired interest rate for your loan.
* **Term Length**: A number field where you can input the desired term length for your loan.
* **Collateral Required**: A select one field where you can choose whether collateral is required for your loan (Yes, No).
* **Loan Amount**: A number field where you can input the desired loan amount.
* **Business Name**: A text field where you can input your business name.
* **Business Address**: A text field where you can input your business address.
* **Business Phone**: A text field where you can input your business phone number.
* **Business Email**: A email field where you can input your business email address.
* **Business Website**: A text field where you can input your business website.
* **Business Fax**: A text field where you can input your business fax number.
* **Business Tax ID**: A text field where you can input your business tax ID number.
* **Business License Number**: A text field where you can input your business license number.
* **Business Owner**: A text field where you can input the name of your business owner.
* **Business Description**: A text field where you can provide a brief description of your business.
* **Years in Business**: A number field where you can input the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Business Revenue**: A number field where you can input your business revenue.
* **Business Assets**: A text field where you can input a description of your business assets.
* **Business Debt**: A text field where you can input a description of your business debt.
* **Business Liabilities**: A text field where you can input a description of your business liabilities.
* **Business Equipment**: A text field where you can input a description of your business equipment.
* **Business Property**: A text field where you can input a description of your business property.
* **Notes**: A note field where you can provide any additional notes about your business or loan inquiry.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, as the information will be used to evaluate your loan request.
* If you're unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
* We'll be in touch soon to discuss your loan options and answer any questions you may have.
