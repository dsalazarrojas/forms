# Market Potential Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to help us understand your market potential. Please answer the questions as accurately as possible to provide us with valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of each page to proceed to the next page or to submit your answers.
2. For select questions, choose one or multiple options from the list.
3. For date and time questions, enter the date or time in the format specified.
4. For text questions, enter your answer in the text box.
