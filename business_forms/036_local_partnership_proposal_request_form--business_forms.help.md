# local_partnership_proposal_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your local partnership proposal, including business details, contact information, proposal title, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your business's official name in the "business_name" field.
2. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number, in the "contact_info" field.
3. Describe your proposal in the "proposal_description" field.
4. Enter a title for your proposal in the "proposal_title" field.
5. Select the type of partnership you are seeking (e.g., local, national, regional) in the "partnership_type" field.
6. Enter the type of business you represent in the "business_type" field.
7. Specify the industry your business is in in the "business_industry" field.
8. Enter your partner's name in the "partner_name" field.
9. Provide your partner's contact information, including email and phone number, in the "partner_email" and "partner_phone" fields.
10. Enter your partner's address in the "partner_address" field.
11. Enter the proposal submission date and time in the "submit_date", "submit_time", and "submit_date_time_zone" fields.
12. Add any additional submission notes in the "submission_notes" field.
13. Enter your submitter's name in the "submitter_name" field.
14. Attach any relevant files or documents to the "proposal_file" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business's official name.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Proposal Description** (`proposal_description`, text, required): Describe your proposal.
* **Proposal Title** (`proposal_title`, text, required): Enter a title for your proposal.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, required): Enter your business's email address.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, required): Enter your business's phone number.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter your business's address.
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required): Enter your partner's name.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, required): Enter your partner's email address.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, required): Enter your partner's phone number.
* **Partner Address** (`partner_address`, text, required): Enter your partner's address.
* **Partnership Type** (`partnership_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of partnership you are seeking (e.g., local, national, regional).
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, text, required): Enter the type of business you represent.
* **Business Industry** (`business_industry`, text, required): Specify the industry your business is in.
* **Proposal File** (`proposal_file`, text, required): Attach any relevant files or documents.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required): Enter the proposal submission date.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, required): Enter the proposal submission time.
* **Submit Date Time Zone** (`submit_date_time_zone`, select_one, required): Select the time zone for your proposal submission (e.g., EST, EDT, CDT).
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, note, required): Add any additional submission notes.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your submitter's name.
