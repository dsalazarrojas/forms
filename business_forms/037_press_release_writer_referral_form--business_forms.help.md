# Press Release Writer Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect referrals for press release writers who can assist with writing press releases for your company or business. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information page.
2. Enter the press release writer's name and company or business name.
3. Provide their email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`press_release_writer_referral`, text, required/optional): This is the first page of the form, where you can enter your contact information. Please fill out this page completely, but note that it is not required.
* **Press Release Writer Name** (`press_release_writer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the press release writer you are referring, so we can get in touch with them.
* **Company or Business Name** (`company_or_business_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company or business this press release writer works for.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the press release writer. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the press release writer. Please format it as you normally would for phone numbers.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you've already submitted a referral for this person, do not submit another one to avoid duplication.
