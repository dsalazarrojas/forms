# Business Funding Review Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about your business, including your name, email address, phone number, business details, funding requirements, and financial information. The purpose of this form is to help the funding review process, so we can better understand your business needs and assess your eligibility for funding.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields marked with an * (asterisk). These are necessary for us to process your request.
2. For dropdown fields (Business Type and Industry Sector), select one option that best describes your business.
3. Ensure you answer all questions truthfully and accurately, as your responses will be used to evaluate your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your business email address or your personal email address if you don't have a business email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your business phone number or your personal phone number if you don't have a business phone number.
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business.
* **Business Type**: Select the type of business that best describes your company (e.g., Startup, Established Business, Non-Profit, Social Enterprise).
* **Years in Operation**: Enter the number of years your business has been operating.
* **Industry Sector**: Select the industry sector that best describes your business (e.g., Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Manufacturing, Retail, Services, Education, Other).
* **Funding Amount Requested**: Enter the amount of funding you are seeking.
* **Type of Funding Sought**: Select the type of funding you are looking for (e.g., Equity Investment, Debt Financing, Grant, Crowdfunding, Government Funding, Other).
* **Use of Funds**: Describe how you plan to use the funds.
* **Funding Timeline**: Enter the date by which you need the funds.
* **Current Annual Revenue**: Enter your business's current or projected annual revenue.
* **Profitability Status**: Select the current financial status of your business (e.g., Profitable, Break-even, Pre-revenue, Loss (improving), Loss (significant)).
* **Existing Debt**: Enter the total amount of outstanding debt.
* **Existing Investors**: If you have current investors, select "Yes"; if not, select "No".
* **Serves Underserved Community**: If your business serves a disadvantaged community, select "Yes"; if not, select "No".
* **Social or Environmental Impact**: If your business creates social or environmental impact, select "Yes"; if not, select "No".
* **Business Plan Ready**: If you have a business plan, select "Yes"; if not, select "No" (or "In development" if it's in progress).
* **Additional Information**: Enter any other information that might be helpful for the funding review process.
* **I consent to funding review process**: Select "Yes" to agree to be evaluated according to the funding review process.
