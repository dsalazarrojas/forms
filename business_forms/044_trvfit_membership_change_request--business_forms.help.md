# trvfit_membership_change_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The TRVFIT membership change request form is used to update a member's information, such as their membership type, reason for change, and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (member_name and submitter_name) and the optional fields as necessary.
2. Select the current membership type from the options provided (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
3. If you selected Option 3, provide the reason for change in the reason_for_change field.
4. Enter any additional comments or explanations in the comments field.
5. Enter your email address in the submitter_email field.
6. Enter your phone number in the submitter_phone field.
7. Enter your department in the submitter_department field.
8. Enter your job title in the submitter_job_title field.
9. Enter the date and time of submission in the submitter_date and submitter_time fields.
10. Enter any additional comments or explanations in the submitter_comments field.
11. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **member_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the member whose membership is being changed.
* **member_number** (number, required): Enter the member's number.
* **membership_type** (select_one, required): Select the new membership type (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **other_membership_type** (text, optional): If you selected Option 3, provide a custom membership type.
* **reason_for_change** (text, optional): Enter a reason for the membership change.
* **comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations.
* **submitter_name** (text, required): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **submitter_email** (email, required): Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **submitter_phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number as the submitter.
* **submitter_department** (text, optional): Enter your department as the submitter.
* **submitter_job_title** (text, optional): Enter your job title as the submitter.
* **submitter_date** (date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **submitter_time** (time, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **submitter_comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations as the submitter.
