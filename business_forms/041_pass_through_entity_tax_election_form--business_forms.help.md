# Pass Through Entity Tax Election Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pass Through Entity Tax Election Form is a document used to elect a tax authority for entities that are required to do so. Completing this form ensures accurate and timely tax reporting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined below:

1. Select your state from the provided options.
2. Enter the tax year for which you are electing the tax authority.
3. Choose the type of entity you are electing the tax authority for.
4. Select the state tax authority you are electing.
5. Enter any additional information required.
6. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select State (`state`):** Choose the state where your entity is located.
* **Year (`year`):** Enter the tax year for which you are electing the tax authority. Please enter the year as a four-digit number (e.g., 2022).
* **Pass-through entity type (`entity_type`):** Select the type of entity you are electing the tax authority for (Type A, Type B, or Type C).
* **State tax authority (`state_tax_authority`):** Select all state tax authorities that apply to your entity.
* **Tax year (`tax_year`):** Enter the tax year for which you are electing the tax authority. Please enter the year as a four-digit number (e.g., 2022).
* **Entity EIN (`entity_ein`):** Enter your entity's Employer Identification Number (EIN).
* **Tax election (`tax_election`):** Select the type of tax election you are making (Type A, Type B, or Type C).
* **Signature (`signature`):** Sign and date the form to confirm your identity.
* **Date (`date`):** Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Phone number (`phone_number`):** Enter your phone number.
* **Address (`address`):** Enter the address of your entity.
* **Name (`name`):** Enter the name of your entity.
* **Email (`email`):** Enter the email address of your entity.
* **Tax authority (`tax_authority`):** Enter the name of the tax authority you are electing.
* **Entity address (`entity_address`):** Enter the address of your entity.

Please make sure to complete each field accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth tax election process.
