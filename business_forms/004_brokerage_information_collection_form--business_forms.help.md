# Brokerage Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals looking to buy or sell a property. It helps us understand their needs and preferences, allowing us to provide personalized guidance and support throughout the brokerage process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and submit it. Make sure to answer each question accurately and thoroughly to ensure we can provide the best possible service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents. (Required)
* **Email Address**: Enter your active email address where you can be reached for updates and communications. (Required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your preferred phone number where we can contact you for discussions and follow-ups. (Required)
* **Type of Property You are Interested In**: Select all the types of properties you are interested in, such as Single Family Home, Condo, or Land. (Required)
* **Are you a Buyer or Seller**: Indicate whether you are looking to buy or sell a property. (Required)
* **Approximate Price Range**: Select the approximate price range you are looking to buy or sell in. (Optional)
* **Transaction Timeline**: Choose when you are looking to buy or sell, such as Immediate, 1-3 months, or Flexible. (Required)
* **Preferred Location or City**: Enter the city or location you are interested in buying or selling in. (Optional)
* **Number of Bedrooms Needed**: If you are buying, indicate the number of bedrooms you need. (Optional)
* **Financing Assistance**: Indicate whether you need financing assistance, are pre-approved, or will be paying cash. (Optional)
* **Previous Experience**: Confirm if you have worked with a real estate agent before. (Required)
* **How did you hear about us**: Indicate how you found out about our brokerage services. (Optional)
* **Any special requirements or preferences**: Enter any additional information we should know about your situation. (Optional)

Note: All required fields are marked as (Required) for your reference.
