# web_development_client_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients who are seeking web development services. It is used to understand the client's needs and preferences for a web development project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with the required information. Make sure to fill out all the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **ClientDetails**: 
  Enter the name of the client.
* **ContactInfo**:
  Enter the contact information of the person submitting the form, including their email address.
* **ProjectRequirements**:
  Enter a brief summary of the project requirements, including the desired features, functionality, and any specific requirements.
* **ClientRelationships**:
  List any existing relationships the client may have with other organizations, companies, or individuals, such as partnerships, contracts, or alliances.
* **TechnicalRequirements**:
  List any technical requirements or constraints for the project, such as programming languages, databases, or software used.
* **Services**:
  Select the services needed for the project, such as design, development, or hosting.
* **AdditionalComments**:
  Enter any additional comments or notes about the project or client.
* **Email**:
  Enter the email address of the contact person submitting the form.
* **Phone**:
  Enter the phone number of the contact person submitting the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that the web development team can understand the client's needs accurately.
* If you are not sure about any field, please ask the web development team for clarification.
* The information collected from this form will be used to create a project proposal or plan for the client's web development project.
