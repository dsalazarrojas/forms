# Property Automation Council Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from property owners who wish to register their properties with the Property Automation Council. It is essential to ensure that the property details are correct and complete to facilitate proper registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the property name and address details accurately.
2. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) to enable the council to contact you.
3. Provide the event date and time for the property.
4. Specify the registration type for the property.
5. Enter the details of the property organiser, including name, email, phone number, and address.
6. Add any additional information about the property.
7. Finally, select 'Yes' to submit the form.
