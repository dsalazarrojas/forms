# Pricing And Commission Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pricing And Commission Agreement form is designed to document the terms of a business agreement between a business and an agency. It includes essential details such as pricing and commission structures, payment terms, and other necessary conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form in its entirety.
2. Enter the title and date of the agreement.
3. Select relevant commission and pricing structures.
4. Choose the agreement terms and conditions.
5. Specify payment terms, including payment schedules and frequencies.
6. Define total commission amounts.
7. Choose the agent and business contacts.
8. Sign and date the agreement.
9. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
