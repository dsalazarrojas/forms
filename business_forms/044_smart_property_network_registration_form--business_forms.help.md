# Smart Property Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Property Network Registration Form is a tool to collect information about a property's details, including type, subtypes, services, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, which is marked as required in the form.
2. Select the correct property type and subtypes from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter your contact information, including first name, last name, email, phone number, and street address.
4. Select the services you wish to be associated with the property.
5. Enter the date and time you are available to be contacted.
6. Add any additional notes about the property.
7. Confirm that you have an assigned tool.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your business or personal email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property you are registering.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Enter the street address of the property.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city of the property location.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state of the property location.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code of the property location.
* **Property Subtype** (`select_one_property_subtype`, select_one, required): Select the subtype of the property.
* **Services** (`select_multiple_property_services`, select_multiple, required): Select the services you wish to be associated with the property.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you are available to be contacted.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you are available to be contacted.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, required): Add any additional information about the property.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have an assigned tool.
