# rope_access_daily_safety_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the daily safety checklists required for rope access work. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all necessary safety protocols are in place and followed during rope access operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the general section to ensure that all necessary safety protocols are in place.
2. Check the personnel section to ensure that all workers have received the necessary training and are qualified for rope access work.
3. Verify that all necessary equipment is available and in good working condition.
4. Confirm that the work at height is being done safely.
5. Ensure that fall protocols are being followed.
6. Confirm that the rescue team is available and equipped.
7. Verify that all necessary safety equipment is available.
8. Review the notes section for any additional safety concerns or issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General** (`general`, text, optional): This section is used to provide any additional information or comments about the rope access operation.
- **personnel** (`personnel`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if all workers have received the necessary training and are qualified for rope access work.
- **equipment** (`equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Select all necessary equipment that will be used during the operation. This may include harnesses, ropes, and fall protection equipment.
- **Work at height** (`work_at_height`, date, required): This is the date and time of the rope access operation.
- **Fall protocols** (`fall_protocols`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate that fall protocols are being followed.
- **Tie off** (`tie_off`, time, optional): If tie-off procedures are being followed, select the time of tie-off.
- **Rescue team** (`rescue_team`, select_multiple, optional): Select all necessary members of the rescue team.
- **Safety equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select_multiple, required): Select all necessary safety equipment that will be used during the operation.
- **Harness** (`harness`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that harnesses are being used.
- **Rope access safety checklist items** (`rope_access_safety_checklist_items_1` to `rope_access_safety_checklist_items_16`, note, optional): These fields are used to capture any additional safety concerns or issues that need to be addressed during the operation.
