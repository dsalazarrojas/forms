# tax_prep_class_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who wish to enroll in a tax preparation class. It's intended for administrative purposes, such as keeping records and following up with students. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Application Details, including any relevant information about your application.
2. Provide your Student Info, including your name, ID, or other relevant details.
3. Select your chosen courses from the Course Selection field, indicating which tax preparation classes you'd like to enroll in.
4. Enter the Application Date, the date you submit your application.
5. Enter your Contact Phone Number, a way for us to contact you for more information.
6. Enter your Contact Email, so we can reach out to you about your application and course progress.
7. Add any relevant Notes, if you'd like to include additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Application Details (`application_details`)
* **Application Details** (Required/Optional): This field is where you can add any relevant information about your application, such as a brief description or reference number.

### Student Info (`student_info`)
* **Student Info** (Optional): Enter your name, student ID, or any other relevant details to identify yourself as a student.

### Course Selection (`course_selection`)
* **Course Selection** (Required/Optional): Select the tax preparation classes you'd like to enroll in. You can choose one or multiple classes.

### Application Date (`application_date`)
* **Application Date** (Required/Optional): Enter the date you submit your application.

### Contact Phone (`contact_phone`)
* **Contact Phone** (Optional): Enter a phone number where we can contact you about your application and course progress.

### Contact Email (`contact_email`)
* **Contact Email** (Optional): Enter an email address where we can reach out to you about your application and course progress.

### Notes (`notes`)
* **Notes** (Optional): Add any additional information you'd like to include with your application.

### Submit (`submit`)
* **Submit** (Required/Optional): Click this button to submit your application.
