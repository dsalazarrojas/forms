# Ethical Business Practices Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethical Business Practices Program Application is a form used by businesses to apply for the program. The program aims to recognize and support businesses that demonstrate a commitment to ethical practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields with your company's details.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Provide accurate and detailed information about your company's current practices and motivations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Address** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your company's address.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for your company.
* **Current Practices** (`4`, `text`, required): Describe your company's current practices and policies.
* **Motivations** (`5`, `text`, required): Explain your motivations for applying to the program.
* **Name** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter your name as a representative of the company.
* **Job Title** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position in the company.
* **Contact Number** (`8`, `text`, required): Enter a contact number for your company.
* **Application Date** (`9`, `date`, required): Enter the date you submitted this application.
* **Submitted By** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who submitted this application.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
* Review the program's requirements and guidelines before submitting your application.
