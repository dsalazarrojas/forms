# Reusable Container Program Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Reusable Container Program Request Form is a one-time request for customers to collect reusable containers for various purposes. Fill out this form to provide your contact information and details about your container request.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please ensure that you have not submitted a container request in the past. If you have already made a request, please contact the relevant team directly.
- Read each section carefully and fill out all required fields (identified as "required" in the form).
- You can enter any additional information as needed in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Request Details**: Describe the purpose and scope of your container request.
* **Pick-Up Information**: If you need to schedule a pickup, enter the date and time you would like to pick up the containers.
* **Container Request**: List all the items you wish to collect from this request.
* **Schedule Pickup/Delivery**: If you need to schedule a pickup or delivery, enter the date and time you would like to pick up or deliver the containers.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you quickly.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you quickly.
* **Address**: Enter your address so we can deliver the containers to you or send them to a third party.
* **Notes**: Additional information about your request, such as any specific needs or requirements.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" are mandatory.
