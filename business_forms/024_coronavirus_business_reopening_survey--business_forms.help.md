# coronavirus_business_reopening_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The coronavirus_business_reopening_survey form is used to gather information from businesses that are impacted by the COVID-19 pandemic. This form helps businesses to report their current status, needs, and any government assistance or other forms of help they may be seeking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select one of the options for "can_reopen" field.
2.  For "need_help" field, select any of the relevant options.
3.  Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5.  Select the reopen date in the "reopen_date" field.
6.  Select the reopen time in the "reopen_time" field.
7.  Select the "reopening_status" field.
8.  For "government_help_needed" field, select "True" or "False".
9.  For "financial_help_needed" field, select "True" or "False".
10. For "other_help_needed" field, select "True" or "False".
11.  For "need_emergency_loan", "need_emergency_grant", "need_emergency_liquidity", "need_emergency_insurance", "need_emergency_other" fields, select "True" or "False".
12.  Enter your comments in the "comments" field.
13.  Enter your business details (ID, Name, Address, City, State, and Zip).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **business_status**: This field is used to report the current status of your business during the pandemic.
* **can_reopen**: This field indicates whether your business is able to reopen.
* **need_help**: Select any of the options if you need help with financial assistance, government help, or other.
* **email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **reopen_date**: Select the date on which your business is reopening.
* **reopen_time**: Select the time on which your business is reopening.
* **reopening_status**: This field indicates the current status of your business after the reopening.
* **government_help_needed**: Select "True" if you need help from the government.
* **financial_help_needed**: Select "True" if you need financial help.
* **other_help_needed**: Select "True" if you need other help.
* **need_emergency_loan**, **need_emergency_grant**, **need_emergency_liquidity**, **need_emergency_insurance**, **need_emergency_other**: Select "True" if you need emergency loan, grant, liquidity, insurance, or other forms of help.
* **comments_business**: This field is for any additional comments about your business during the pandemic.
* **comments_government**: This field is for any additional comments about government help during the pandemic.
* **business_id**: Enter your business ID.
* **business_name**: Enter your business name.
* **business_address**: Enter your business address.
* **business_city**: Enter your business city.
* **business_state**: Enter your business state.
* **business_zip**: Enter your business zip code.

## Tips
* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Make sure to enter your email address and phone number where you can be contacted.
* Select the correct options for "can_reopen", "need_help", "government_help_needed", "financial_help_needed", "other_help_needed", "need_emergency_loan", "need_emergency_grant", "need_emergency_liquidity", "need_emergency_insurance", "need_emergency_other" fields.
* Be honest and provide accurate information about your business during the pandemic. 
* If you need help or have questions, contact us at your email or phone number.
