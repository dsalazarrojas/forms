# Project Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Submission Form is a tool designed to collect project-related information from users. This form gathers essential details about a project, including its title, ideas, name, description, materials, and supplies required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by filling in the **Project Title** field with a brief description of your project.
*   In the **Idea** field, elaborate on your project's concept, goals, or unique aspects.
*   The **Project Name** field is where you should enter a concise name for your project.
*   The **Project Description** field is for providing a detailed explanation of your project, including its scope, objectives, and any relevant background information.
*   In the **Materials** field, list any resources, equipment, or materials you plan to use or have used for your project.
*   Finally, in the **Supplies** field, mention any materials, tools, or resources you expect to need for the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Project Title** (project_submission_form_input, text, required): Enter a short and descriptive title for your project.
*   **Idea** (project_submission_form_ideas, text, required): Briefly explain the main idea behind your project.
*   **Project Name** (project_submission_form_name, text, required): Enter a concise name for your project.
*   **Project Description** (project_submission_form_description, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your project, including its scope and objectives.
*   **Materials** (project_submission_form_materials, text, required): List any resources or equipment you plan to use or have used for your project.
*   **Supplies** (project_submission_form_supplies, text, required): Mention any materials, tools, or resources you expect to need for the project.
