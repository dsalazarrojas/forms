# Program Quality Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from customers about a program's quality, service, and overall experience. Your input will help the program administrators improve and develop the program to better meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the answers to each question based on your honest opinion of the program's performance.
2. Fill in the open-ended fields with any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
3. Make sure to submit the form once you have answered all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Quality** (`id: 1`): Rate the overall quality of the program.
	+ Select one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
* **Service Quality** (`id: 2`): Rate the quality of service offered by the program.
	+ Select one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
* **Staff Responsiveness** (`id: 3`): Rate the responsiveness of the program's staff.
	+ Select one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
* **Communication Quality** (`id: 4`): Rate the communication from the program's staff.
	+ Select one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`id: 5`): Suggest ways the program could improve.
	+ Enter your suggestions in the text field below.
* **Likes/Dislikes** (`id: 6`): Describe anything you liked or disliked about the program.
	+ Enter your thoughts in the text field below.
* **Expectations Met** (`id: 7`): Rate whether the program met your expectations.
	+ Select 'True' if the program met your expectations, 'False' otherwise.
* **Visits Count** (`id: 8`): Enter the number of times you have visited this program (optional).
* **Recommendations** (`id: 9`): Enter the number of times you would recommend this program to others (optional).
* **Staff Knowledge** (`id: 10`): Rate the program's staff knowledge of the subject matter.
	+ Select one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
* **Responsiveness** (`id: 11`): Rate the program's responsiveness to suggestions.
	+ Select one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
* **Overall Quality Again** (`id: 12`): Rate the overall quality of the program (again).
	+ Select one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
* **Staff Friendliness** (`id: 13`): Check if the program's staff is friendly.
	+ Select 'True' if the staff is friendly, 'False' otherwise.
* **Open-Ended 1** (`id: 14`): Suggest ways the program could improve (optional).
	+ Enter your suggestions in the text field below.
* **Open-Ended 2** (`id: 15`): Enter any additional comments or suggestions (optional).
	+ Enter your thoughts in the text field below.
* **Overall Opinion** (`id: 16`): Express your overall opinion of the program (optional).
	+ Enter your thoughts in the text field below.
* **Last Question** (`id: 17`): Enter any other comments or suggestions (optional).
	+ Enter your thoughts in the text field below.
