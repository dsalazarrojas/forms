# Admin Weekly Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Admin Weekly Report is a tool used by administrators or managers to report on key activities and performance metrics during the week. It is used by leadership and management teams to track progress, identify blockers and issues, and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the Monday of the reporting week in the `Week start date` field.
2. Provide your name and contact information as the `Prepared by` field.
3. List the key activities and tasks completed during the week in the `Key activities completed` field.
4. Describe any obstacles or issues that need attention in the `Blockers and issues` field.
5. List the priorities and tasks planned for the next week, including the owner or responsible person, in the `Priorities for next week` field.
6. Mention any additional resources or tools needed in the `Resource needs` field.
7. Include any key numbers or KPIs that summarize the week's performance in the `Metrics summary` field.
8. Identify any items that require managerial approval in the `Approvals required` field.
9. Add any additional comments for leadership in the `Additional comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Report Metadata** (`report_metadata`): This field is just a note section for any general comments or information.
  - **Week start date** (`week_start_date`): Enter the date of the Monday of the reporting week.
  - **Prepared by** (`prepared_by`): Provide your name and contact information.
  - **Key activities completed** (`key_activities`): List the key activities and tasks completed during the week. Use bullet points to make it easy to read.
  - **Blockers and issues** (`blockers`): Describe any obstacles or issues that need attention.
  - **Priorities for next week** (`priorities_next_week`): List the priorities and tasks planned for the next week, including the owner or responsible person.
  - **Resource needs** (`resource_needs`): Mention any additional resources or tools needed.
  - **Metrics summary** (`metrics_summary`): Include any key numbers or KPIs that summarize the week's performance.
  - **Approvals required** (`approvals_required`): Identify any items that need managerial approval.
  - **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any additional comments or thoughts for leadership.

## Tips
- Please be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask someone for help.
- The data collected will be used to inform business decisions, so please keep it concise and clear.
