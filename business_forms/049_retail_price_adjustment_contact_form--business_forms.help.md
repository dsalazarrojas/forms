# retail_price_adjustment_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for retailers to contact the pricing team regarding price adjustments for their retail products. It is used to gather information about products, pricing strategies, and any additional comments or supporting documents that may be relevant to the price adjustment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Retailer Information" to provide your store name, address, and contact details.
2. In "Pricing Information", describe the product(s) you are inquiring about, including the price adjustment you are seeking.
3. In "Pricing Team Information", provide the contact details of the person responsible for pricing in your organization.
4. In "Additional Comments", you can add any additional information or context that may be helpful for the pricing team.
5. If you have any supporting documents such as sales records or product descriptions, attach them as "Supporting Document 1" and "Supporting Document 2" respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1**: 
    * **Page 1**: This page is just for information purposes. Please do not fill this page. You will not need to enter any information here.
* **Retailer Information** (required): Enter your store name, address, and contact details.
* **Pricing Information** (required): Describe the product(s) you are inquiring about, including the price adjustment you are seeking.
* **Pricing Team Information** (required): Provide the contact details of the person responsible for pricing in your organization.
* **Additional Comments**: You can add any additional information or context that may be helpful for the pricing team.
* **Supporting Document 1**: If you have any supporting documents such as sales records or product descriptions, attach them here.
* **Supporting Document 2**: If you have any additional supporting documents, attach them here.
* **Review and Approval** (required): This is a review and approval section for the pricing team to assess and validate the price adjustment request. Please provide any comments or feedback here.
