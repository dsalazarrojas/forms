# Final Record Retention Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Final Record Retention Approval Form is used to record and track the process of retaining or destroying company records. This form is used for internal purposes only and is an essential part of the record management process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields, including:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Department
	* Role (if applicable)
	* Date Of Approval
	* Date Of Destruction
	* Reason For Approval
	* Reason For Destruction
	* Signature
	* Date Signed
	* Supervisor Approval
	* Manager Approval
	* Disposition Status
	* Reason For Disposition Status
	* Destruction Status
2. Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and completely before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Department** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Select your department from the list.
* **Role** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter your role or job title.
* **Date Of Approval** (`6`, `date`, required): Enter the date of approval for retaining the records.
* **Date Of Destruction** (`7`, `date`, required): Enter the date the records are scheduled for destruction.
* **Reason For Approval** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason for approval.
* **Reason For Destruction** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason for destruction.
* **Signature** (`10`, `text`, required): Sign the form to confirm approval.
* **Date Signed** (`11`, `date`, required): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Employee Name** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter your name (if different from first and last name).
* **Supervisor Approval** (`13`, `number`, required): Enter the supervisor's approval code.
* **Manager Approval** (`14`, `number`, required): Enter the manager's approval code.
* **Date Of Removal** (`15`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of removal of the records.
* **Date Of Deaccession** (`16`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of deaccessioning the records.
* **Date Of Archiving** (`17`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of archiving the records.
* **Date Of Disposition** (`18`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of disposition of the records.
* **Reason For Archiving** (`19`, `select_one`, optional): Select the reason for archiving.
* **Reason For Disposition** (`20`, `select_one`, optional): Select the reason for disposition.
* **Disposition Status** (`21`, `text`, required): Enter the disposition status.
* **Reason For Disposition Status** (`22`, `text`, required): Enter the reason for disposition status.
* **Disposition Notes** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes for disposition.
* **Retention Period** (`24`, `text`, required): Enter the retention period for the records.
* **Destruction Status** (`25`, `text`, required): Enter the destruction status.
