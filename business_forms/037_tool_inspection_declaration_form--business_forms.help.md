# Tool Inspection Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information about the tool and its condition after inspection. It ensures that the inspector's declaration is recorded accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the relevant information about the tool being inspected.
2. Select the tool being inspected from the options provided in the "Assigned Tool" field.
3. Enter the inspector's name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
4. Choose the tool's type from the options provided in the "Tool Type" field.
5. Select any applicable conditions from the options in the "Condition" field.
6. Enter the date and time of inspection in the "Date Inspected" and "Time Inspected" fields respectively.
7. Enter any additional remarks or comments about the tool in the "Remarks" field.
8. Sign the form by providing your name and signature in the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Id** (`company_id`, number, not required): Enter the company ID number if applicable.
* **Tool Id** (`tool_id`, number, not required): Enter the tool ID number if applicable.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, not required): Select the tool being inspected from the options provided.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Inspector Email** (`inspector_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Inspector Phone** (`inspector_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Tool Name** (`tool_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the tool being inspected.
* **Tool Type** (`tool_type`, select_one, not required): Choose the type of tool being inspected from the options provided.
* **Condition** (`condition`, select_multiple, not required): Select any applicable conditions from the options provided.
* **Date Inspected** (`date_inspected`, date, not required): Enter the date of inspection.
* **Time Inspected** (`time_inspected`, time, not required): Enter the time of inspection.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, note, not required): Enter any additional remarks or comments about the tool.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments about the tool.
* **Tool Location** (`tool_location`, text, not required): Enter the location of the tool.
* **Equipment Location** (`equipment_location`, text, not required): Enter the location of the equipment.
* **Safety Standards** (`safety_standards`, text, not required): Enter any applicable safety standards or guidelines.
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of certification.
* **Next Inspection Date** (`next_inspection_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the next inspection.
* **Inspector Comments** (`inspector_comments`, note, not required): Enter any comments from the inspector.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, not required): Sign the form by providing your name and signature.
* **Certification Status** (`certification_status`, select_one, not required): Choose the certification status from the options provided.
* **Certified On** (`certified_on`, date, not required): Enter the date of certification.
* **Next Inspection Due** (`next_inspection_due`, date, not required): Enter the date of the next inspection.
* **Inspector Note** (`inspector_note`, note, not required): Enter any additional comments from the inspector.
* **Tool Owner** (`tool_owner`, text, not required): Enter the name of the tool owner.
