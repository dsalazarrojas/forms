## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **company_details** (`company_details`, text, optional): Enter your company's basic details such as name, address, and other relevant information.
* **leadership_structure** (`leadership_structure`, text, optional): Describe your company's leadership structure, including the number of executives and board members.
* **decision_making** (`decision_making`, text, optional): Explain how your company makes decisions, including who is involved and how decisions are communicated.
* **accountability** (`accountability`, text, optional): Describe your company's accountability system, including how performance is measured.
* **board_of_directors** (`board_of_directors`, text, optional): Provide details on your company's board of directors, including members' backgrounds and roles.
* **executive_compensation** (`executive_compensation`, text, optional): State your company's policy on executive compensation and bonuses.
* **conflicts_of_interest** (`conflicts_of_interest`, text, optional): Explain how your company handles potential conflicts of interest and their impact on decision-making.
* **audit_committee** (`audit_committee`, text, optional): Describe the function and role of your company's audit committee.
* **external_auditors** (`external_auditors`, text, optional): List any external auditors your company engages with for financial or other audits.
* **code_of_conduct** (`code_of_conduct`, text, optional): Summarize your company's code of conduct policy, including its principles and values.
* **ethics_policy** (`ethics_policy`, text, optional): Outline your company's ethics policy, including how it guides decision-making and behavior.
* **board_meetings** (`board_meetings`, text, optional): Report the frequency and nature of board meetings, including their agenda and outcomes.
* **voting_processes** (`voting_processes`, text, optional): Describe how voting occurs in your company's board meetings.
* **board_meeting_minutes** (`board_meeting_minutes`, text, optional): Provide minutes from recent board meetings, including discussions and decisions made.
* **financial_controls** (`financial_controls`, text, optional): Explain your company's financial controls and risk management practices.
* **risk_management** (`risk_management`, text, optional): Describe your company's risk management practices and strategies.
* **internal_controls** (`internal_controls`, text, optional): List any internal controls or auditing processes your company employs.
* **external_controls** (`external_controls`, text, optional): Describe how your company engages with external auditors or controls.
