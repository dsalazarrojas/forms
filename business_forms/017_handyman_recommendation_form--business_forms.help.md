# Handyman Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Handyman Recommendation Form is a document used to gather information about a service provider for potential handyman services. It is intended for internal use by our company's procurement team to evaluate and consider recommendations for future projects. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required".
2. Enter accurate and clear information for each field.
3. Ensure that you have permission from the service provider to share their contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Service Name
* **Service Name**: A text field for the service provider's name, e.g., John Doe Handyman Service.

### Service Address
* **Service Address**: A text field for the service provider's address, e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, USA.

### Service Phone
* **Service Phone**: A text field for the service provider's phone number, e.g., 555-123-1234.

### Service Email
* **Service Email**: A text field for the service provider's email address, e.g., [john.doe@ServiceEmail.com](mailto:john.doe@ServiceEmail.com). Ensure that you enter an address that can receive messages, not just a generic placeholder.

### Additional Tips
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to the procurement team.
* Be honest and provide accurate information, as we take pride in building relationships with our service providers.
* Do not submit incomplete forms. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submission.
