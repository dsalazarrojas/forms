# AI Pricing Engine Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Pricing Engine Partnership Application Form is designed to collect information about your company, its needs, and expectations for our AI pricing engine. This form will help us understand how we can best support your business and tailor our solution to meet your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the required information before starting to fill it out.
2. Enter your company name, contact person's name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the correct company size and industry sector from the provided options.
4. Choose the type of partnership you're interested in and select any relevant options for technical integration complexity and specific integration requirements.
5. Provide estimated annual transaction volume and monthly revenue from the pricing engine (if applicable).
6. Specify your desired implementation timeline and compliance requirements (if any).
7. Describe your expectations from this partnership and any specific integration requirements or constraints.
8. Answer questions about your previous partnership experience and whether you're the primary decision maker.
9. Add any additional contacts involved in the partnership.
10. Finally, click the submit button to send the form to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your organization's legal name as it appears on your business cards or official documents.
* **Contact Person Full Name** (`contact_person_name`, `text`, **required**): The name of the person responsible for this partnership inquiry, as well as any other stakeholders involved.
* **Contact Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, **required**): The email address we'll use for all partnership communications.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, **required**): Include your country code if international.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the number of employees in your company.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the primary industry your company operates in.
* **Current Pricing Solution Used** (`current_pricing_solution`, `text`, **required**): What pricing engine or system do you currently use?
* **Type of Partnership Interested In** (`partnership_type`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Choose the type of partnership you're interested in (reseller, technology integration, etc.).
* **Expected Annual Volume** (`expected_annual_volume`, `integer`, **optional**): Estimated number of transactions per year.
* **Estimated Monthly Revenue** (`estimated_monthly_revenue`, `decimal`, **optional**): Estimated monthly revenue from pricing engine in thousands of USD.
* **Desired Implementation Timeline** (`implementation_timeline`, `date`, **optional**): When you'd like to start using our solution.
* **Technical Integration Complexity** (`technical_integration_complexity`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select the complexity level of the integration process.
* **Specific Integration Requirements** (`integration_requirements`, `text`, **optional**): Any technical requirements or constraints specific to your company or integration.
* **Compliance Requirements** (`compliance_requirements`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select any regulatory or compliance standards applicable to your company.
* **Partnership Expectations** (`partnership_expectations`, `text`, **required**): Describe your goals and expectations from this partnership.
* **Previous Partnership Experience** (`previous_partnership_experience`, `select_one`, **optional**): Have you partnered with similar tech providers before?
* **Are You the Primary Decision Maker** (`decision_maker`, `select_one`, **optional**): Are you the person who'll make the partnership decisions.
* **Additional Contacts** (`additional_contacts`, `text`, **optional**): List any other stakeholders involved in this partnership.
* **Application Submission Date** (`application_submission_date`, `date`, **required**): The date you're submitting this application.
* **Additional Comments or Notes** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **optional**): Any other information you'd like to share with us.
