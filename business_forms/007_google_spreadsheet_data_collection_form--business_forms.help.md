# Google Spreadsheet Data Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data related to business forms in a standardized format. It helps to ensure that all relevant information is captured consistently, reducing the risk of missing details and errors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title to clearly identify the form.
2. Provide a brief description of the form.
3. Select the relevant category for the form (e.g., Information Forms, Request Forms).
4. Enter the business unit or department where the form is being used.
5. Enter the email address of the form owner or administrator.
6. Enter the phone number of the form owner or administrator.
7. Enter the date the form was created or is due to be completed.
8. Enter any comments or additional information about the form.
9. Select the assigned tool or software used to create the form.
10. Enter any form IDs or unique identifiers.
11. Select the priority level of the form (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
12. Indicate the follow-up frequency (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
13. Add any relevant tags or keywords for the form.
14. Enter any notes or additional comments about the form.
15. Select any attachments or files related to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Google Spreadsheet Data Collection Form**: Enter a title for the form that clearly identifies it.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the form.
* **Category**: Select the category that best fits your form (e.g., Information Forms, Request Forms).
* **Business Unit**: Enter the business unit or department where the form is being used.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the form owner or administrator.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the form owner or administrator.
* **Date**: Enter the date the form was created or is due to be completed.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the form.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool or software used to create the form.
* **Form IDs**: Enter any form IDs or unique identifiers.
* **Priority**: Select the priority level of the form (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Follow-up**: Indicate the follow-up frequency (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Tags**: Add relevant tags or keywords for the form.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the form.
* **Attachments**: Select any attachments or files related to the form.

Note: All fields are optional unless specified otherwise.
