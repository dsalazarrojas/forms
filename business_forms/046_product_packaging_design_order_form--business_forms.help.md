# Product Packaging Design Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for product packaging design orders from clients. Please fill in the required fields accurately to ensure efficient production and delivery of your ordered product packaging design.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product name and type.
2. Select the required material, color, and design requests.
3. Choose the production plan and deadline for your order.
4. Select the shipping method for your order.
5. Provide your contact information (contact person, phone number, and email address) for communication.
6. Provide any additional production notes if required.
7. Review all fields to ensure accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product for which you are requesting packaging design services.
* **Product Type**: Select the type of product from the options provided. This will help the production team to understand the specific requirements of your product.
* **Material Request**: Provide any specific material requirements you have for the packaging of your product.
* **Color Request**: Select the required color(s) for your product packaging.
* **Design Request**: Provide any specific design requirements you have for the product packaging.
* **Production Plan**: Select the production plan that suits your order. This will help the production team to schedule the production accordingly.
* **Production Deadline**: Provide the deadline for the production of your packaging design.
* **Production Location**: Provide the location where the production should take place.
* **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping method you prefer for the delivery of your product packaging.
* **Contact Person**: Provide the name and contact information of the contact person responsible for communication related to this order.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the contact person's phone number for efficient communication.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the contact person for receiving updates and instructions.
* **Production Note**: If you have any additional information about your order, provide it in the production note field.
