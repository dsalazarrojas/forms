# Ambers Color Street Sample Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ambers Color Street Sample Request Form is designed to allow customers to request free samples from the company. This form helps us understand your product preferences and ensures that we get the correct samples to you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "Requester name" and "Email address" fields respectively.
2. Select the type of samples you are requesting in the "Product samples requested" field.
3. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping address" field.
4. If you wish, provide a brief description of the purpose of your sample request in the "Purpose of sample request" field.
5. You can choose to opt-in to receive marketing offers and news in the "Opt in to marketing" field.
6. If you have a phone number, you can enter it in the "Phone number" field, but this is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on any communication we send to you.
* **Email address** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Product samples requested** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Choose the type of samples you are requesting (e.g. nail strips, polish, accessories).
* **Shipping address** (`text`, `required`): Enter the address where you would like to receive your samples.
* **Purpose of sample request** (`text`, `optional`): Enter a brief description of why you are requesting samples (e.g. for personal use, for a demo or event).
* **Opt in to marketing** (`select_one`, `optional`): Choose whether or not you would like to receive marketing offers and news from us.
* **Phone number** (`text`, `optional`): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
