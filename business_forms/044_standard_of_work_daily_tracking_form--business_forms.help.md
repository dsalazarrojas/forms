# Standard Of Work Daily Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Standard Of Work Daily Tracking Form is designed to track daily work activity, including tasks completed, work status, and work duration. This information helps management monitor progress and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Tasks Completed" field and choose the tasks you completed from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date you completed the tasks in the "Tasks Completed Date" field.
3. Choose your current work status from the "Work Status" dropdown menu.
4. Enter any additional notes about your work status in the "Work Status Note" field.
5. Enter the start and end times of your work in the "Work Start Time" and "Work End Time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tasks Completed** (`tasks_completed`, select_multiple, optional): Select all tasks you have completed from the dropdown menu.
* **Tasks Completed Date** (`tasks_completed_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the tasks.
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your current work status from the dropdown menu (In Progress, On Hold, or Done).
* **Work Status Note** (`work_status_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your work status.
* **Work Start Time** (`work_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your work.
* **Work End Time** (`work_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your work.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete this form daily to accurately track your work progress.
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and consistency.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your work supervisor or manager for clarification.
