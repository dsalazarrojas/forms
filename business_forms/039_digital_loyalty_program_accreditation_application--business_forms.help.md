# Digital Loyalty Program Accreditation Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Loyalty Program Accreditation Application is a form used to submit an application for accreditation of a digital loyalty program. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information from the applicant to assess their digital loyalty program's eligibility for accreditation. This form helps the reviewer to evaluate the program's compliance with the required standards and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Application Details** section with a brief description of your digital loyalty program.
2. Select the type of program you are applying for (Digital Program, Loyalty Program, or Program Accreditation).
3. Choose the type of your digital loyalty program (Digital Loyalty, Loyalty, or Other).
4. Provide your contact details (name, email, phone number, and address).
5. Enter your email address and phone number in the respective fields.
6. Enter your physical address, city, state, zip code, and country.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Details** (Application Details, text, required/optional): A brief description of your digital loyalty program.
* **Organization** (Organization, text, optional): The name of your organization or company.
* **Program Details** (Program Details, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Digital Program
	+ Loyalty Program
	+ Program Accreditation
* **Program Type** (Program Type, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Digital Loyalty
	+ Loyalty
	+ Other
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, optional): A text field for you to enter any additional contact information.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): A valid email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): A phone number.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): The physical address of your organization.
* **City** (City, text, optional): The city of your organization.
* **State** (State, text, optional): The state of your organization.
* **Zip** (Zip, text, optional): The zip code of your organization.
* **Country** (Country, text, optional): The country of your organization.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any delay in the application process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to reviewing your application.
