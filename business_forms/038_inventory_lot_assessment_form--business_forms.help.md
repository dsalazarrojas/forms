# inventory_lot_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The inventory_lot_assessment_form is used to assess and record the value and condition of a specific lot or collection of items. It provides a standardized and structured way to collect information about a lot, making it easier to track and manage inventory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Go through each page of the form, noting any relevant information about the lot on each page.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, using the provided fields to guide you.
3. If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact your supervisor or the relevant department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lot Information** (`Lot Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the lot, including its contents and any notable features.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the lot, such as its condition or any relevant historical or contextual information.

# Tips

* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact your supervisor or the relevant department.
* If the lot has multiple items, you can break up your answers across the two pages, using the "Lot Information" and "Additional Information" fields to provide a clear and structured description.
