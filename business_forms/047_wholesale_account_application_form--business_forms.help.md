# wholesale_account_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers or businesses apply for wholesale accounts with our company. To complete this form, please provide us with your contact information and account details. We will use this information to review your application and get back to you soon.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the correct "Account Type" from the dropdown menu. This will help us determine the type of wholesale account you are applying for.
2. Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field. This is a required field, so please make sure to fill it out.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This will be our primary contact method.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This will help us contact you for further information.
5. Select the "Account Officer" from the dropdown menu. This will determine who will be handling your account.
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field. This will help us determine your shipping and billing information.
7. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field. This is a required field for shipping and billing purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the type of wholesale account you are applying for. The options are: Type 1, Type 2, and Type 3.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company name to help us identify your business.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Account Officer** (`account_officer`, select_one, required/optional): Select the account officer who will be handling your account.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your business address to help us determine your shipping and billing information.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, required): Enter your zip code to help us determine your shipping and billing information.
