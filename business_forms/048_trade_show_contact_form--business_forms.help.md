# Trade Show Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect and store contact information for trade shows that your company participates in or plans to participate in. It will help you to track progress, send reminders, and evaluate the effectiveness of the trade show participation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields: **Show Name**, **Contact Name**, **Contact Email**, and **Contact Phone**.
2. Add any additional details as needed in the **Show Notes** field.
3. Select the relevant options for **Follow Up**, **Show Type**, **Follow Up Method**, and **Follow Up Frequency**.
4. If required, fill in the **Show Start Date** and **Show End Date** fields.
5. Complete any other relevant fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Show Name** (`show_name`, text, required): The name of the trade show.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): The name of the person in charge of the trade show.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): The phone number of the person in charge of the trade show.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): The email address of the person in charge of the trade show.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): The name of the company participating in the trade show.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): The department within the company participating in the trade show.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): The job title of the person in charge of the trade show.
* **Show Start Date** (`show_start_date`, date, optional): The start date of the trade show.
* **Show End Date** (`show_end_date`, date, optional): The end date of the trade show.
* **Show Location** (`show_location`, text, optional): The location of the trade show.
* **Company Website** (`company_url`, text, optional): The website of the company participating in the trade show.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): The address of the company participating in the trade show.
* **City** (`company_city`, text, optional): The city of the company participating in the trade show.
* **State** (`company_state`, text, optional): The state of the company participating in the trade show.
* **Zip** (`company_zip`, text, optional): The zip code of the company participating in the trade show.
* **Show Notes** (`show_notes`, text, optional): Any additional notes about the trade show.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): The method for follow-up (e.g. phone call, email, etc.).
* **Show Type** (`show_type`, select_one, optional): The type of trade show (e.g. conference, exhibition, etc.).
* **Follow Up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_one, optional): The method for follow-up (e.g. phone call, email, etc.).
* **Follow Up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_one, optional): The frequency of follow-up (e.g. daily, weekly, etc.).
* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): The time for follow-up.
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): The date for follow-up.
* **Follow Up Note** (`follow_up_note`, text, optional): Any additional note for follow-up.
* **Show Status** (`show_status`, select_one, optional): The status of the trade show (e.g. completed, planned, etc.).
* **Next Follow Up** (`follow_up_next_follow_up`, select_one, optional): The next follow-up action to take.
