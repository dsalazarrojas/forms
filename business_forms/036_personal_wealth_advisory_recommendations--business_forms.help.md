# Personal Wealth Advisory Recommendations - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Wealth Advisory Recommendations form is designed to collect information about your financial goals, risk tolerance, and investment preferences. This data will help our team provide you with tailored recommendations to achieve your personal financial objectives. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure you receive the most accurate and relevant advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have a clear understanding of each field.
2. Fill out the form with your answers, making sure to select the correct options for each field.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
4. Submit the form to initiate the wealth advisory process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Goals** (1): Describe your short-term and long-term financial goals, such as saving for a down payment on a house or retirement.
* **Risk Tolerance** (2): Indicate your level of comfort with investment risk, with lower numbers indicating a lower risk tolerance.
* **Investment Preferences** (3): Specify your preferred investment type, such as stocks, bonds, or real estate.
* **Investment Amount** (4): Enter the total amount you are willing to invest.
* **Investment Timeframe** (5): Select how often you want to invest, such as one-time, monthly, quarterly, semiannually, or annually.
* **Risk Freeze** (6): Identify which types of risk you are willing to accept or freeze, such as false or true.
* **Financial Institutions** (7): Select the types of financial institutions you would like to work with, such as banks, brokerages, or insurance companies.
* **Investment Objects** (8): Specify the types of investments you are interested in, such as stocks, bonds, real estate, or other.
* **Risk Freeze Period** (9): If applicable, enter the period during which you would like to freeze risk.
* **Emergency Contact** (10): Enter the email address of someone who should be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Alternate** (11): Enter an alternate email address for emergency contact.
* **Risk Freeze Frequency** (12): Specify how often you would like to freeze risk, such as never, monthly, quarterly, semiannually, or annually.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and clear information to ensure the most effective results.
- You may not need to fill out all fields if not applicable.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
