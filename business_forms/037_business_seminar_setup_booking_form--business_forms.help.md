# Business Seminar Setup Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information to help our team set up and prepare for your upcoming business seminar. The data collected will be used to ensure that your seminar is well-planned and executed according to your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your organizer's information (name, company/organization, phone number, and email).
2. Enter the seminar title, date, start time, and duration in hours.
3. Specify the expected number of attendees.
4. Choose the venue name and type, and select the room setup style.
5. Indicate if any equipment is required.
6. List any special technology requirements.
7. Determine if catering is needed and specify the type of catering.
8. If applicable, indicate any dietary restrictions.
9. Optionally, add any additional requirements such as signage, parking, or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organizer Name (2)**: Enter the name of the person organizing the seminar.
* **Company or Organization (3)**: Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter the contact phone number for the seminar organizer.
* **Email Address (5)**: Enter the contact email for the seminar organizer.
* **Seminar Title (7)**: Enter a descriptive name for the seminar event.
* **Seminar Date (8)**: Enter the date on which the seminar will be held.
* **Start Time (9)**: Enter the start time of the seminar.
* **Duration in Hours (10)**: Enter the length of the seminar in hours.
* **Expected Number of Attendees (11)**: Enter the estimated number of attendees.
* **Venue Name (13)**: Enter the name of the location where the seminar will be held.
* **Room Type Needed (14)**: Choose the type of room that fits your seminar needs (e.g., Classroom, conference room, etc.).
* **Room Setup Style (15)**: Select the setup arrangement for the room (e.g., Theater Style, Classroom Style, etc.).
* **Equipment Needed (17)**: Choose any necessary equipment for the seminar (e.g., Projector and Screen, Sound System, etc.).
* **Special Technology Requirements (18)**: Indicate any specific technology needs or requirements.
* **Catering Needed (20)**: Choose if you need catering services (e.g., No catering, coffee and light snacks, etc.).
* **Dietary Restrictions (21)**: Enter any dietary restrictions or preferences of the attendees.
* **Signage Requirements (23)**: Optionally, specify any signage needs or requirements.
* **Parking Requirement (24)**: Enter the number of parking spaces needed.
* **Special Requests or Notes (25)**: Add any additional requirements or notes.
