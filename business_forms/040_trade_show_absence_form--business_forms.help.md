# Trade Show Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Show Absence Form is designed for employees to report their absence from trade shows, allowing for accurate and comprehensive information to be collected. This form helps ensure that all necessary data is available to supervisors for planning and coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Trade Show Absence Form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your employee's name in the **employee_name** field.
2.  Select the date you will be absent from the trade show in the **absence_date** field.
3.  Provide a clear explanation for your absence in the **absence_reason** field.
4.  Enter your supervisor's email address in the **supervisor_email** field.
5.  Choose the trade show you will be absent from in the **trade_show** field.
6.  If you have any additional information you'd like to provide, use the **trade_show_absence_form_additional_info_1** and **trade_show_absence_form_additional_info_2** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **employee_name**: Please provide your employee's name as it appears on their badge or in the company directory.
*   **absence_date**: Select the date you will be absent from the trade show. This is a required field and must match the date of the trade show you selected in the **trade_show** field.
*   **absence_reason**: Explain in detail why you will be absent from the trade show. Be as specific as possible to help your supervisor understand your situation.
*   **supervisor_email**: Enter your supervisor's email address so they can contact you directly with any questions or concerns.
*   **trade_show**: Select all trade shows you will be absent from. If you're absent from multiple trade shows, please select all relevant trade shows.
*   **trade_show_absence_form_additional_info_1**: If you have any additional information you'd like to provide, please use this field.
*   **trade_show_absence_form_additional_info_2**: If you have any further additional information, please use this field.
