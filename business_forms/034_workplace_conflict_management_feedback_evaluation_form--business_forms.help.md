# Workplace Conflict Management Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback and insights on conflict resolution practices in the workplace. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and enhance our conflict management processes.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, carefully read each section and provide honest and accurate feedback on your experiences with conflict resolution in the workplace.
- Please answer each question to the best of your ability, and if you need clarification on any field, do not hesitate to ask for help.
- If you have completed this form previously, please be aware that your responses will be reviewed in conjunction with your previous feedback to ensure continuity and consistency in our evaluation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Insights** (employee_insights, text, optional): This section is an open-ended field where you can share your thoughts and experiences regarding conflicts that you have been involved in. Please provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your perspective.
* **Conflict Resolution Practices** (conflict_resolution_practices, text, optional): In this section, describe the conflict resolution practices that you have experienced or been a part of in the workplace. Be as specific as possible about the actions taken or strategies employed during conflicts.
* **Conflict Resolution Effectiveness** (conflict_resolution_effectiveness, number, optional): Rate your perception of the effectiveness of conflict resolution in your workplace on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is 'Not Effective At All' and 10 is 'Very Effective'. Please consider the overall approach, communication, and outcome of conflicts when making your rating.

## Tips
- Please be honest and respectful in your feedback, as it will help us create a better work environment.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
- Your responses will be reviewed anonymously to ensure your confidentiality.
