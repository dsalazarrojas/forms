# Corporate Sponsorship Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Sponsorship Consent Form is a document that confirms an individual's or company's acceptance of a sponsorship agreement. It is essential for us to have a clear understanding of your consent and terms of the sponsorship for our records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the format of [name@example.com] in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Company Name in the "Company Name" field.
5. Enter your Company name in the "Company" field. (Note: This field is optional and may be the same as your Company Name.)
6. Enter your role in the organization in the "Role" field.
7. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
8. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
9. Select whether you have accepted the terms of the sponsorship by ticking the "Terms Accepted" field. Please select "True" if you have accepted the terms, and "False" if you have not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your personal last name.
* **Email**: Enter your work or personal email address in the format [name@example.com].
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Company**: Optional field for entering your company name (if different from your personal name).
* **Role**: Enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Address**: Enter your work or home address.
* **Terms Accepted**: Select "True" to indicate that you have accepted the terms of the sponsorship, or "False" if you have not.
