# Story Creation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Story Creation Agreement form is used to collect information about a story creation project. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that all project requirements are captured and understood by all stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the "project_lead" field and choose the person responsible for the project.
2.  Enter the project details in the "project_description" field.
3.  Select the delivery date in the "delivery_date" field.
4.  Provide the client requirements in the "client_requirements" field.
5.  Check the "client_requirements" field again to ensure that you have not missed any essential requirements.
6.  Choose the "submit" option to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **project_lead** (Select One, required): The person responsible for the project.
* **project_description** (Text, optional): A brief description of the project.
* **delivery_date** (Date, optional): The date when the project is expected to be delivered.
* **client_requirements** (Text, optional): A detailed description of the client's requirements.
* **compensation_terms** (Text, optional): The terms of compensation for the project.
* **terms_and_conditions** (Text, optional): The terms and conditions of the project.
* **submit** (Select One, required): Choose this option to submit the form.
* **form_id** (Text, optional): A unique ID for the form.
* **form_category** (Text, optional): The category of the form.
* **form_title** (Text, optional): The title of the form.
* **form_description** (Text, optional): A description of the form.
* **assigned_tool** (Text, optional): The tool assigned to the project.
* **form_id** (Text, optional): A unique ID for the form.
* **created_by** (Text, optional): The person who created the form.
* **form_version** (Text, optional): The version of the form.
