# loan_agreement_renewal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The loan agreement renewal form is designed to update the terms and status of an existing loan agreement. This form is not intended for initial loan applications or new loan requests. It's a tool for current borrowers to renew their loan agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields.
3. Select the loan details and status as necessary.
4. Add any comments or comments related to the renewal.
5. Sign and date the form as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **loan Agreement Renewal** (`loan_agreement_renewal_form`, note, not required): This is the title of the form. Please do not fill in this field.
* **borrower info** (`borrower_info`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the applicable lender names from the list. This field is not required but is used to ensure accuracy.
* **Loan details** (`loan_details`, number, not required): Enter the loan amount and other relevant details as necessary.
* **Loan term** (`loan_term`, select_one, not required): Select the loan term from the options: 6 months, 1 year, or 2 years.
* **Loan status** (`loan_status`, select_one, not required): Select the current status of the loan: active, inactive, or expired.
* **Borrower Signature** (`borrower_signature`, note, not required): Please sign and date this field to confirm your commitment.
* **Lender Signature** (`lender_signature`, note, not required): Please sign and date this field to confirm your agreement.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, not required): This field is not required but is used for record keeping.
* **Date Renewed** (`date_renewed`, date, not required): This field is not required but is used for record keeping.
* **comments** (`comments`, note, not required): Add any comments or explanations related to the loan renewal.
