# Party Stylist Job Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Party Stylist Job Registration Form is designed to collect information from potential party planners and stylists who want to offer their services to clients. This form will help us verify the contact information and qualifications of these individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready, including your first name, last name, email, phone number, and availability.
2. Fill out the form with your relevant details, making sure to provide accurate information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (text)**: Please enter your first name as per your identification documents.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name as per your identification documents.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date Of Birth (date)**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Available Hours (select_one)**: Indicate if you are available to work during the following periods:
	+ Yes: You are available to work at any time.
	+ No: You are not available to work at all.
* **Work Experience (text)**: Briefly describe your experience in the party planning industry, including any relevant certifications or training.
* **Availability (select_multiple)**: Indicate your availability for jobs by selecting:
	+ Yes: You are available for jobs.
	+ No: You are not available for jobs.
* **Experience Level (select_multiple)**: Select your level of experience in the industry:
	+ High: You have extensive experience in the industry.
	+ Low: You have limited experience in the industry.
* **Experience Years (number)**: Enter the number of years you have been working in the industry.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Make sure to provide clear and concise information for each field.
