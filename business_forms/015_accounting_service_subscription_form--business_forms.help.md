**Accounting Service Subscription Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

This form is designed to facilitate the subscription process for the accounting service offered by [Company Name]. It collects essential information to determine the subscription details, such as the chosen plan, billing cycle, and payment method. The form is divided into two main sections: **Service Subscription Plan** and **Payment Method**.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select a subscription plan that suits your business needs from the provided options.
2. Enter your business's legal name as it appears on official documents.
3. Choose your preferred billing cycle (monthly or annually with a 10% discount).
4. For optional add-on services, select the services you'd like to add to your subscription.
5. Provide your primary billing contact information (name and email).
6. Select your preferred payment type (credit card, ACH transfer, or wire transfer).
7. Set your subscription start date.
8. Accept the terms and conditions of the service.
9. Finally, type your authorized signature to confirm the subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Legal Name** (text, required): Enter your business's legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Subscription Tier** (select one, required): Choose a subscription plan: Basic Bookkeeping, Standard Finance, or Premium CFO Services.
* **Billing Cycle** (select one, required): Select whether you'd like to be billed monthly or annually (with a 10% discount for annual billing).
* **Add-on Services** (select multiple, optional): Select any additional services you'd like to add to your subscription, such as payroll processing, tax filing, or inventory management.
* **Primary Billing Contact** (text, required): Enter your primary billing contact's name.
* **Billing Email** (email, required): Enter the email address where you'd like to receive invoices.
* **Payment Type** (select one, required): Choose how you'd like to pay: credit card, ACH transfer, or wire transfer.
* **Subscription Start Date** (date, required): Set the date when you'd like your subscription to start.
* **Terms and Conditions Agreement** (select one, required): Agree to the service terms and conditions.
* **Authorized Subscriber Signature** (text, required): Type your name to authorize the subscription.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your selected subscription plan carefully, as the base monthly fee is estimated based on the chosen tier.
* Ensure you've entered accurate billing contact and email information to receive timely invoices.
* Choose a payment method that suits your business needs and financial arrangements.
* Read and agree to the service terms and conditions before authorizing the subscription.
* Double-check your subscription start date to ensure it aligns with your business schedule.
