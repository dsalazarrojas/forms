# Equipment Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Maintenance Request Form is designed to track and manage maintenance requests for office equipment, helping to ensure that all relevant information is captured and communicated effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. Submit Request
Fill out the form by selecting "Yes" or "No" as to whether the request is submitted or not.
Select one of the options to indicate the status of the request.

### 2. Equipment Type
Choose the correct equipment type from the provided options (General, Equipment, or Other).
This field is essential for categorizing the equipment and ensuring that the maintenance request is directed to the correct team member.

### 3. Equipment Description
Enter a clear and concise description of the equipment that requires maintenance.
This field helps the maintenance staff understand the equipment's make, model, and any other relevant details.

### 4. Location
Enter the location of the equipment, such as the room or building it is housed in.
This field ensures that the maintenance request is directed to the correct location and helps the maintenance staff plan the maintenance schedule accordingly.

### 5. Issue Description
Provide a detailed description of the issue with the equipment, including any error messages or error codes.
This field aids in troubleshooting and ensures that the maintenance staff have a clear understanding of the problem.

### 6. Request Date
Enter the date when the maintenance request was initially made.
This field helps in scheduling the maintenance request and ensuring that it is not missed.

### 7. Maintenance Type
Select the type of maintenance required (Repair, Inspection, Preventative Maintenance).
This field determines the type of maintenance that is needed and ensures that the correct team member is assigned to the task.

### 8. Maintenance Date
Enter the date when the maintenance is scheduled to be completed.
This field is used for planning and scheduling purposes.

### 9. Completed
Check this field when the maintenance request has been completed.
This field is used to track the status of the maintenance request.

### 10. Assigned User
Select the team member who is assigned to perform the maintenance.
This field ensures that the maintenance request is directed to the correct team member.

### 11. Comments
Enter any additional comments or notes about the maintenance request.
This field provides extra information that can be helpful for the assigned team member.

### 12. Attachment 1
Attach any relevant documents or files related to the maintenance request.
This field is used to provide supporting evidence or documentation.

Please ensure that you have attached any necessary documentation or supporting evidence before submitting the form.
