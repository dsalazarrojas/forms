# cybersecurity_incident_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cybersecurity Incident Check-in Form is used to collect and track information about cybersecurity incidents. This form helps the Incident Response Team to quickly gather essential details about each incident, enabling them to respond and resolve issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form fields with accurate and clear information about the incident.
2. Select the correct options for the Incident Type, Affected Assets, and Incident Status fields.
3. Include a date and time for when the incident was reported and when it was resolved.
4. Describe the resolution, incident response, and follow-up activities in the Incident Response, Incident Resolution, and Incident Follow-up fields.
5. Select the Review By and Closed By fields with the correct team members.
6. Add any relevant comments or learnings from the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Info**: This field is for providing a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident (Security Incident, Non-Security Incident, or Other).
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
* **Affected Assets**: Select all affected assets (Server, Network, Database, System, Application).
* **Incident Impact**: Choose the level of impact (High, Medium, Low).
* **Incident Status**: Select the status of the incident (Open, Closed, In Progress).
* **Incident Reported**: Enter the date and time the incident was reported.
* **Incident Escalation**: Enter the date and time the incident was escalated.
* **Incident Response**: Describe the response activities taken during the incident.
* **Incident Resolution**: Describe the resolution of the incident.
* **Incident Learning**: Describe any learnings from the incident.
* **Incident Review**: Describe the review process and outcome.
* **Incident Actions**: Describe the actions taken during and after the incident.
* **Incident Follow-up**: Describe the follow-up activities.
* **Reviewed By**: Select the team member who reviewed the incident (Incident Response Team, Security Team, Management).
* **Reviewed Date**: Enter the date the incident was reviewed.
* **Closed Date**: Enter the date the incident was closed.
* **Closed By**: Select the team member who closed the incident (Incident Response Team, Security Team, Management).
* **Incident Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the incident.
* **Category**: Select the category of the incident (Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
* **Tag**: Select all relevant tags (Tag 1, Tag 2, Tag 3).
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes about the incident.
* **Comment**: Enter any additional comments about the incident.
