# hagadone_newspaper_wedding_announcement_form_2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "hagadone_newspaper_wedding_announcement_form_2" is a form designed to submit details about a wedding event to be published in the newspaper.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), to provide details about the wedding event.
3. Select the correct options for the event type and wedding planners as applicable.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Details** (`wedding_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the wedding event.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact information for the wedding planners or individuals submitting the form.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional details about the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of event (Wedding, Funeral, or Other).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the event.
* **Venue** (`venue`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the event.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the event.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the event.
* **Wedding Planner** (`wedding_planner`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the wedding planners involved in the event.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the event.
