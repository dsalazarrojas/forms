# Process Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a comprehensive evaluation of business processes. It ensures that all necessary information is gathered for evaluation, improvement, and optimization.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Introduction**: Briefly introduce the process being evaluated.
2.  **Process Map**: Describe the process being evaluated.
3.  **Evaluation Criteria**: Select the criteria used to evaluate the process.
4.  **Feedback Form**: Provide feedback on the process.
5.  **Review Process**: Fill in the date and time of the review meeting.
6.  **Process Improvement Plan**: Develop a plan for improving the process.
7.  **Implementation Plan**: Develop a plan for implementing process improvements.
8.  **Process Owner**: Identify the person responsible for the process.
9.  **Review Schedule**: Plan the review schedule.
10.  **Evaluation Summary**: Summarize the evaluation.
11.  **Evaluation Status**: Indicate the status of the evaluation (active or inactive).
12.  **Process Owner Email**: Enter the email address of the process owner.
13.  **Process Owner Phone**: Enter the phone number of the process owner.
14.  **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
15.  **Reviewer Email**: Enter the email address of the reviewer.
16.  **Reviewer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
17.  **Evaluator Name**: Enter the name of the evaluator.
18.  **Evaluator Email**: Enter the email address of the evaluator.
19.  **Evaluator Phone**: Enter the phone number of the evaluator.
20.  **Review Date**: Fill in the date of the review.
21.  **Review Time**: Fill in the time of the review.
22.  **Review Duration**: Indicate the duration of the review.
23.  **Evaluation Score**: Enter the score given to the process.
24.  **Evaluation Comment**: Provide a comment on the evaluation.
25.  **Evaluation Status**: Indicate the status of the evaluation (active or inactive).
