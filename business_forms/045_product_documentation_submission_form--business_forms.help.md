# Product Documentation Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect product documentation submission information from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your personal information, including your first name, last name, and email.
2. Enter your phone number to enable easy contact with you.
3. Provide the date and time of the product's launch.
4. Enter a short description of the product.
5. Select the category that the product falls under.
6. Select the tools assigned to the product.
7. Upload any output files associated with the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the product's launch in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the product's launch in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a short description of the product.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category that the product falls under from the list provided.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the tools assigned to the product from the list provided.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, required: false): Upload any output files associated with the product.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your submission aligns with the product's current status.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
* Please submit only one product documentation at a time.
