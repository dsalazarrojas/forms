# Business Identification Number Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Identification Number Survey is a tool designed to collect business identification information for official records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and documentation ready before starting the form.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what is required.
3. Enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Official Entity Name** (`legal_entity_registered_name`, text, required): Enter the name as registered with the government.
* **Registered Address** (`business_registered_address_full`, text, required): Enter the complete address including City, State, and Zip Code.
* **Federal Tax ID / EIN** (`federal_tax_id_ein`, text, required): Enter the Federal Tax ID/EIN number, e.g. 12-3456789.
* **Business Registration Number** (`local_business_registration_number`, text, required): Enter the State or local registration ID.
* **State or Province of Registration** (`state_of_incorporation_registration`, text, required): Enter the state or province where the business is registered.
* **Year of Incorporation** (`year_of_incorporation_official`, number, required): Enter the year the business was incorporated.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`official_contact_person_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person authorized to handle business identity matters.
* **Contact Email** (`official_contact_email_address`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`official_contact_phone_number`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Business Website** (`company_website_domain`, text, optional): Enter the business website, e.g. www.business.com.
* **Industry Classification** (`industry_classification_naics_sic`, select_one, required): Choose the primary business activity category from the dropdown list.
* **I certify that the identification numbers provided are accurate** (`verification_acknowledgment_id`, select_one, required): Choose from the dropdown list to confirm the accuracy of the identification numbers provided.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date_recorded`, date, required): Enter the date the survey was completed.

Note: Fields with a `*` symbol are required.
