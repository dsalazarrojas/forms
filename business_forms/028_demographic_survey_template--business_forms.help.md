# Demographic Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your demographics. It will help us better understand your background, preferences, and circumstances. Your participation will aid us in providing more relevant and personalized services and support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the pages you want to complete, one by one.
2. Fill in the required fields with your information.
3. Choose your preferred options for select fields (e.g., gender, income level, education level).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
*   **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Choose how you identify your gender.
*   **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age.
*   **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional): Enter your profession or occupation.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (e.g., example@example.com).
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
*   **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you currently reside.
*   **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where you currently reside.
*   **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the country where you currently reside.
*   **Income Level** (`income_level`, select_one, optional): Choose your current income level.
*   **Education Level** (`education`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your educational background (e.g., high school, some college, bachelor's degree).
*   **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your current marital status.
*   **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your current employment status.
*   **Household Income** (`household_income`, number, optional): Enter your household income.
*   **Household Members** (`household_members`, number, optional): Enter the number of household members.
*   **Education Level 2** (`education_level2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your educational background (e.g., high school, some college, bachelor's degree).
*   **Income Level 2** (`income_level2`, select_one, optional): Choose your current income level.
*   **Occupation 2** (`occupation`, text, optional): Enter your profession or occupation.
*   **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, text, optional): Enter your employment type (e.g., full-time, part-time).
*   **Income Source** (`income_source`, text, optional): Enter your income source (e.g., salary, freelance).
*   **Occupation Type** (`occupation_type`, text, optional): Enter your occupation type (e.g., IT, marketing).
*   **Employment Status 2** (`employment_status2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your current employment status.
*   **Household Income 2** (`household_income2`, number, optional): Enter your household income.
*   **Household Members 2** (`household_members2`, number, optional): Enter the number of household members.

Note that each field is optional. You may choose to skip fields you don't wish to answer or don't have information about. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us!
