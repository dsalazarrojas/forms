# cryptocurrency_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about users' perceptions, behaviors, and preferences related to cryptocurrency. The data collected will help us better understand the users' attitudes towards cryptocurrency and their current practices in the market.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the survey.
2. Answer each question carefully, making sure to select the option that best matches your opinion or current behavior.
3. Fill in the required fields (denoted by \*).
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`title: text` , not required): Please provide your user ID or name so we can identify you in case there are any issues with your responses.
* **What is your current job role?** (`question_1` , select_one, required: no): Indicate your current job title or position at the company.
* **Have you ever heard of cryptocurrency?** (`question_2` , select_one, required: no): Choose whether you have any knowledge or experience with cryptocurrency.
* **How do you feel about cryptocurrency?** (`question_3` , select_one, required: no): Select how likely you are to invest in or use cryptocurrency.
* **How likely are you to invest in cryptocurrency?** (`question_4` , select_one, required: no): Choose how likely you are to invest in cryptocurrency.
* **What is your current knowledge about cryptocurrency?** (`question_5` , select_multiple, required: no): Select all that apply from the provided options to describe your current knowledge level about cryptocurrency.
* **What is your level of concern about cryptocurrency?** (`question_6` , select_one, required: no): Select how concerned you are about cryptocurrency.
* **Which cryptocurrency exchange do you primarily use?** (`question_7` , select_multiple, required: no): Select all that apply from the provided options as your primary cryptocurrency exchange.
* **What is your current investment strategy for cryptocurrency?** (`question_8` , select_multiple, required: no): Select all that apply from the provided options to describe your current investment strategy.
* **How often do you trade cryptocurrency?** (`question_9` , select_one, required: no): Indicate how frequently you trade cryptocurrency.
* **How much money have you invested in cryptocurrency?** (`question_10` , number, required: no): Enter the amount you have invested in cryptocurrency.
* **When do you think you will stop investing in cryptocurrency?** (`question_11` , date, required: no): Enter a date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" when you expect to stop investing in cryptocurrency.
* **At what time do you usually trade cryptocurrency?** (`question_12` , time, required: no): Enter the time when you usually trade cryptocurrency.
* **What is your average investment in cryptocurrency per trade?** (`question_13` , number, required: no): Enter the amount you average invest per trade.
* **How often do you consult with a financial advisor about cryptocurrency?** (`question_14` , select_one, required: no): Select how often you consult a financial advisor.
* **Do you use a cryptocurrency wallet?** (`question_15` , select_one, required: no): Indicate whether you use a cryptocurrency wallet.
* **How many cryptocurrency wallets do you have?** (`question_16` , number, required: no): Enter the number of wallets you use.
* **What type of wallet do you use?** (`question_17` , select_multiple, required: no): Select all that apply from the provided options as the type of wallet you use.
* **Do you use two-factor authentication?** (`question_18` , select_one, required: no): Indicate whether you use two-factor authentication.
* **Do you have a recovery seed for your wallet?** (`question_19` , select_one, required: no): Indicate whether you have a recovery seed.
* **Do you use a hardware wallet?** (`question_20` , select_one, required: no): Select whether you use a hardware wallet.
* **Do you use a paper wallet?** (`question_21` , select_one, required: no): Select whether you use a paper wallet.
* **Do you use a mobile wallet?** (`question_22` , select_one, required: no): Select whether you use a mobile wallet.
* **Do you use a desktop wallet?** (`question_23` , select_one, required: no): Select whether you use a desktop wallet.
* **Do you use a cryptocurrency exchange?** (`question_24` , select_one, required: no): Indicate whether you use a cryptocurrency exchange.
