# First Aid Training Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for first aid training registration. It's intended for individuals who want to sign up for first aid training sessions. Please fill out the form to provide necessary details for the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of training you are interested in.
2. Enter your date of training (if known).
3. Provide your name and contact information (email and phone number).
4. Indicate if you have any medical conditions that need to be considered during the training.
5. Select whether you have an emergency contact and provide their name, phone number, and email (if applicable).
6. Choose your training status and level (high/low).
7. Select the type of training you are interested in (if applicable).
8. Choose your training location (if applicable).
9. Select whether you have a first aid certification and if you are interested in additional types of training.
10. If you have any other comments or information, please add them in the 'Comments' section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Type**: Choose the type of training you are interested in. You can select multiple options.
* **Date of Training**: Enter the date you are interested in taking the training.
* **Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **E-Mail**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Training Location**: Enter the location where you would like to take the training (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact E-Mail**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their email address (if applicable).
* **Medical Condition**: Indicate if you have any medical conditions that need to be considered during the training.
* **Training Time**: Enter the time you are interested in taking the training (if applicable).
* **Instructor**: Choose whether you have an instructor or are not sure.
* **Training Class**: Select the type of training class you are interested in (if applicable).
* **Training Date**: Enter the date of the training you are taking (if applicable).
* **Training Hour**: Enter the hour of the training you are taking (if applicable).
* **Location**: Enter the location where the training is taking place (if applicable).
* **First Aid Certification**: Indicate if you have a first aid certification or if you are interested in obtaining one.
* **Training Type 2**: Select the type of training you are interested in (if applicable).
* **Training Level**: Choose your desired training level (high/low).
* **Training Status**: Choose your training status (active/inactive).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship you have with your emergency contact.
* **Training Venue**: Enter the location where the training is taking place (if applicable).
