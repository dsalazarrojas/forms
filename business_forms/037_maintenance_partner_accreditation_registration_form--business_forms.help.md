# Maintenance Partner Accreditation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Partner Accreditation Registration Form is used to register a maintenance partner with our organization for the purpose of maintaining our facilities. This form is not intended for emergency repairs or urgent maintenance requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2.  Fill out the form completely, paying attention to the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3.  Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: 
    *   This field is the title of the form. Please enter the name of your business or organization.
*   **Vendor Name**: 
    *   Enter the name of the vendor or company you are representing.
*   **Partner Name**: 
    *   Enter the name of the maintenance partner who will be performing the maintenance work.
*   **Contact Email**: 
    *   Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you or your organization.
*   **Contact Phone Number**: 
    *   Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you or your organization.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you are unsure about your registration status, contact our team to verify.
*   Please note that emergency or urgent maintenance requests should not be submitted through this form. Contact us directly for these types of requests.
