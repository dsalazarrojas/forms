# supply_chain_green_transition_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The supply chain green transition application form is designed to collect information from suppliers who are transitioning to a more sustainable business model. This form helps the organization assess the supplier's commitment to environmental sustainability and their current status in the supply chain.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about your company.
2. Ensure that you are authorized to fill out this form on behalf of your company.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplyer Name** (1): Enter the name of your company as the supplier.
* **Email** (2): Enter your company's email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your company's phone number where you can be reached.
* **Sustainability Programs** (4): Select all the sustainability programs that your company is currently participating in (e.g., Green Transition, Sustainable Practices, Environmental Stewardship).
* **Company Name** (5): Enter your company's name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Company Address** (6): Enter your company's address.
* **Contact Person** (7): Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **Contact Phone** (8): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (9): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Company Representative** (10): Enter the name of the person who will be the main contact for your company.
* **Company Representative Role** (11): Enter the role of the company representative.
* **Certification** (12): Check 'True' if your company has a valid certification, 'False' otherwise.
* **Application Date** (13): Enter the date when this application was submitted.
* **Submission Date** (14): Enter the date when this application was submitted.
* **Company Type** (15): Select the type of your company (Private, Public, Government).
* **Industry** (16): Enter the industry your company is in.
* **Company Size** (17): Enter the number of employees in your company.
* **Number of Locations** (18): Enter the number of locations your company has.
* **Annual Revenue** (19): Enter your company's annual revenue.
* **Website** (20): Enter your company's website URL.
* **Notes** (21): Enter any additional comments or notes about your company.
* **Certification Body** (22): Enter the name of the certification body.
* **Certification Number** (23): Enter the number of your company's certification.
* **Application Status** (24): Select the status of your application (Pending, Accepted, Rejected).
* **Submission Status** (25): Select the status of your submission (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Certification Type** (26): Select the type of certification your company has (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3).
