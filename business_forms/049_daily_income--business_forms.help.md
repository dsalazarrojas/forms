# Daily Income - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Daily Income" form is used to record a user's daily income and expenses. This form is essential for tracking financial data and providing a clear picture of a user's financial situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **date of birth** in the "date of birth" field. This will help us understand your age and demographic information.
2.  Enter your **income** in the "income" field. This is the amount of money you earn or receive daily.
3.  Select all applicable **expenses type** from the list. This will help us understand the types of expenses you have.
4.  Enter the **expense amount** for each selected expense type. This will help us calculate your total expenses.
5.  Add any additional **notes** about your income or expenses. This is an optional field for any additional information you want to provide.
6.  If you have any other contact information, you can optionally enter your **email** and **phone number**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth_field`, date, required): A field to provide your date of birth, which helps us understand your age and demographic information.
*  **Income** (`income_field`, number, required): Enter the amount of money you earn or receive daily.
*  **Select All Expenses Type** (`select_all_expenses_type`, select multiple, required): Choose from the options rent, utilities, or other to select the types of expenses you have.
*  **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, number, required for selected expenses): Enter the amount for each selected expense type.
*  **Notes** (`notes_field`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your income or expenses.
*  **Email** (`email_input`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to share additional contact information.
*  **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to share additional contact information.

## Tips
*   Make sure to select only the expenses types that apply to you.
*   Enter the correct amount for each selected expense type.
*   Use the "notes" field to provide any additional information that may not fit in the other fields.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact us.
