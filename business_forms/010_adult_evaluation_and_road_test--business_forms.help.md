# Adult Evaluation And Road Test - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult Evaluation And Road Test form is used to evaluate a candidate's driving skills and performance during a road test. The form helps to assess their ability to operate a vehicle safely and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the candidate's first name, last name, email, phone number, test date, and test time.
2. Choose the type of vehicle used during the test.
3. Select the class of driver's license being tested.
4. Enter the result of the vision test.
5. Score the candidate's performance for each driving skill, using the options provided.
6. Enter any evaluator's comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the candidate's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the candidate's last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter the candidate's contact email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the candidate's contact phone number.
* **Test Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Test Time**: Enter the start time of the evaluation.
* **Evaluator Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the evaluation.
* **Vehicle Type Used**: Select the type of vehicle used during the test.
* **License Class Being Tested**: Select the class of driver's license being tested.
* **Pre-test Vision Check**: Select the result of the vision test.
* **Starting Procedure Score**: Rate the candidate's ability to start the vehicle.
* **Steering Control Score**: Rate the candidate's ability to steer the vehicle.
* **Acceleration and Braking Score**: Rate the candidate's ability to accelerate and brake smoothly.
* **Gear Shifting Score**: Rate the candidate's ability to shift gears smoothly.
* **Clutch Control Score** (if applicable): Rate the candidate's ability to operate the clutch.
* **Signal and Turn Usage Score**: Rate the candidate's use of signals and turns.
* **Mirror Checking Score**: Rate the frequency of mirror checks.
* **Lane Discipline Score**: Rate the candidate's ability to maintain their position in the lane.
* **Traffic Awareness Score**: Rate the candidate's awareness of other vehicles.
* **Parking Score**: Rate the candidate's ability to park.
* **Overall Performance Rating**: Enter the final assessment of the candidate's performance.
* **Evaluator Comments**: Add any additional comments from the evaluator.

Note: Fields marked as "required" must be filled out. Fields marked as "optional" can be left blank.
