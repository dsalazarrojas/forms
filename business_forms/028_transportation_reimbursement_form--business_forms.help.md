# Transportation Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Reimbursement Form is used to document and manage employee transportation expenses, such as travel dates, origin and destination details, mode of travel, mileage, and reimbursement amounts, as well as approvals and approval dates.

## How To Complete This Form

Before filling out this form, please ensure that you have not already submitted this form for the same travel period and purpose. 

1. Select the travel date of the trip from the calendar.
2. Provide the origin of your trip.
3. Select the destination of your trip.
4. Choose the mode of travel (e.g., Car, Train, Bus, Bike, or Other).
5. Enter the total mileage of the trip.
6. Enter the expense date of the trip.
7. Enter the purpose of the trip.
8. Enter the total amount of expenses incurred.
9. Enter the reimbursement amount you are requesting.
10. Select the person who approved your reimbursement (e.g., John or Jane).
11. Select the date the reimbursement was approved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Date**: Select the date you traveled using the calendar. The date should be in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Origin**: Enter the location where your trip started.
* **Destination**: Enter the location where your trip ended.
* **Mode of Travel**: Choose how you traveled to your destination (e.g., Car, Train, Bus, Bike, or Other).
* **Mileage**: Enter the total distance you traveled.
* **Expense Date**: Enter the date you incurred the expenses for the trip.
* **Purpose**: Enter the reason you traveled, such as a business meeting or conference.
* **Total Amount**: Enter the total amount of expenses incurred for the trip.
* **Reimbursement Amount**: Enter the amount you are requesting as reimbursement for the trip expenses.
* **Approved By**: Select the person who approved your reimbursement (e.g., John or Jane).
* **Approved Date**: Enter the date the reimbursement was approved.

## Tips

* Be accurate when filling out the form to avoid errors and delays in processing.
* Ensure that you have all necessary supporting documentation, such as receipts, before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact your supervisor or HR department for assistance.
