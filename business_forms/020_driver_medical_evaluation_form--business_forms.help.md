# driver_medical_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The driver medical evaluation form is designed to collect necessary medical evaluation information from drivers. This information is vital for medical professionals to accurately assess the driver's fitness to operate a vehicle safely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read through each page carefully, and ensure you understand the information requested before proceeding.
2.  Fill out your full name, including first, middle, and any suffix (e.g., Jr., Sr., etc.) in the "driver's name" field.
3.  Select the correct medical certification status for the "medical_certification" field.
4.  Enter the date of your medical certification, if applicable, in the "medical_certification_date" field.
5.  Enter the medical examination date, if applicable, in the "medical_exam_cert_date" field.
6.  Enter the medical examination expiry date, if applicable, in the "medical_exam_cert_expiry" field.
7.  Select the type of medical examination you completed in the "medical_exam_cert_type" field.
8.  Enter the number of your medical examination certificate in the "medical_exam_cert_num" field.
9.  Select the type of medical certification you hold in the "certification" field.
10.  Enter the number of your medical certification in the "medical_cert_num" field.
11.  Enter your license number in the "license_number" field.
12.  Enter the date your license was issued in the "license_date" field.
13.  Enter the date your license was issued in the "issue_date" field.
14.  Enter the date your license expires in the "expiry_date" field.
15.  Enter any medical conditions you have in the "medical_condition" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **driver's_name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name, including first, middle, and any suffix (e.g., Jr., Sr., etc.)
*   **medical_certification** (Select one, Required): Select "True" if you hold a medical certification, "False" otherwise
*   **medical_certification_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of your medical certification, if applicable
*   **medical_exam_cert_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of your medical examination, if applicable
*   **medical_exam_cert_expiry** (Date, Optional): Enter the expiry date of your medical examination, if applicable
*   **medical_exam_cert_type** (Select one, Required): Select the type of medical examination you completed (Class 1, Class 2, Class 3)
*   **medical_exam_cert_num** (Text, Optional): Enter the number of your medical examination certificate
*   **certification** (Select one, Required): Select the type of medical certification you hold (Medical, Vision, Hearing)
*   **medical_cert_num** (Text, Optional): Enter the number of your medical certification
*   **license_number** (Text, Optional): Enter your license number
*   **license_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date your license was issued
*   **issue_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date your license was issued
*   **expiry_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date your license expires
*   **medical_condition** (Text, Optional): Enter any medical conditions you have

## Tips

*   Always select the correct date for each field, if applicable.
*   Be accurate when filling out your medical certification and examination details.
*   Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are complete before submission.
*   If you are unsure about any field, consult the medical professional who completed the examination.
