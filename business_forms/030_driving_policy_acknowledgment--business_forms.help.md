# driving_policy_acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "driving_policy_acknowledgment" form is used to gather information about a vehicle's owner/operator, vehicle details, and driver details, and ensure that the driver or vehicle operator is aware of and agrees to the driving policy requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all necessary documents, such as driver's license, vehicle registration, and vehicle make/model/year information.
* Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* Review each field carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_details**: This field is for capturing general information about the employee who is completing the form. It is not typically required but is useful for administrative purposes.
* **driver_details**: This field is used to capture information about the driver or vehicle operator's details.
* **vehicle_details**: This field is used to capture information about the vehicle's details.
* **policy_acknowledgment**: Select one of the options to indicate whether you have read and understand the driving policy requirements.
* **consent**: Select all applicable options to indicate your relationship to the vehicle (owner/operator, passenger, etc.).
* **signature**: This is a mandatory field where you need to sign the form to confirm your agreement to the driving policy requirements.
* **contact_details**: Select all applicable options to indicate your preferred contact method (Primary, Secondary, Emergency).
* **vehicle_make**: Select the make of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Honda, Ford).
* **vehicle_model**: Select the model of the vehicle (e.g., Corolla, Civic, F150).
* **driver_license_state**: Select the state where the driver's license was issued (e.g., CA, WA, FL).
* **driver_license_expiration**: Enter the expiration date of the driver's license.
* **vehicle_registration_expiration**: Select the status of the vehicle registration (Not Specified, Specified).
* **vehicle_year**: Enter the year the vehicle was made.
* **vehicle_make_year**: Enter the year the vehicle's make was made.
* **vehicle_color**: Enter the color of the vehicle (e.g., Red, Blue, Green).
* **emergency_contact_method**: Select all applicable contact methods (Phone, Email, SMS).
* **emergency_contact_person**: Select the primary contact person.
* **contact_method**: Select all applicable contact methods (Phone, Email, SMS).
* **vehicle_status**: Select the status of the vehicle (Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **vehicle_make_year**: Enter the year the vehicle's make was made.
* **vehicle_model_year**: Enter the year the vehicle's model was made.
* **driver_license_number**: Enter the driver's license number.
* **vehicle_color**: Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_type**: Select the type of vehicle (e.g., Car, Truck, Van).
* **signature2**: This is a mandatory field where you need to sign the form to confirm your agreement to the driving policy requirements.

Please note that some fields may be mandatory and must be completed accurately to ensure the form is properly submitted. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or the form administrator for assistance.
