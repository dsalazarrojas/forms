# Vehicle Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vehicle Release Form is used to document the release of a vehicle from an organization. This form is required for various business and regulatory reasons, such as reporting to authorities, accounting, and record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the vehicle's **Vehicle Number**, which is the unique identifier assigned to the vehicle.
2.  Enter the **Vehicle Year**, which is the year the vehicle was manufactured.
3.  Record the **Odometer Reading**, which is the current mileage reading of the vehicle.
4.  Enter the **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)**, which is a unique identifier assigned to each vehicle.
5.  Select the **Date of Sale**, which is the date the vehicle was sold or transferred.
6.  Enter the **Sale Price**, which is the amount paid for the vehicle.
7.  Choose the **Vehicle Status**, which indicates whether the vehicle is **Active**, **Sold**, or **Leased**.
8.  Enter the **Release Date**, which is the date the vehicle was released from the organization.
9.  Record the **Release Price**, which is the amount paid for the release of the vehicle.
10.  Enter the name of the person or entity the vehicle was released to, known as the **Released To**.
11.  Provide a brief explanation for the **Reason for Release**, which should include the details of the release.
12.  Sign the form with your **Signature** and date it with the **Date Signed**.
13.  Print your **Printed Name** and **Printed Title**, which are used to verify your identity.
14.  Record the date you printed the form with the **Printed Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vehicle Number** (Text, Required): A unique identifier assigned to the vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Year** (Number, Required): The year the vehicle was manufactured.
*   **Odometer Reading** (Number, Required): The current mileage reading of the vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)** (Text, Required): A unique identifier assigned to each vehicle.
*   **Date of Sale** (Date, Required): The date the vehicle was sold or transferred.
*   **Sale Price** (Number, Required): The amount paid for the vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Status** (Select One, Required): Indicates whether the vehicle is **Active**, **Sold**, or **Leased**.
*   **Release Date** (Date, Required): The date the vehicle was released from the organization.
*   **Release Price** (Number, Required): The amount paid for the release of the vehicle.
*   **Released To** (Text, Required): The name of the person or entity the vehicle was released to.
*   **Reason for Release** (Note, Required): A brief explanation for the release.
*   **Signature** (Text, Required): Your signature, which confirms you filled out the form.
*   **Date Signed** (Date, Required): The date you signed the form.
*   **Printed Name** (Text, Required): Your printed name, used for verification.
*   **Printed Title** (Text, Required): Your printed title, used for verification.
*   **Printed Date** (Date, Required): The date you printed the form.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly, as the information will be used for official purposes.
