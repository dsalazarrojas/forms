# Stock Replenishment Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Replenishment Report Form is used to document and track stock requests from customers or internal stakeholders. This form is designed to gather essential information for the stock replenishment team to fulfill stock requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields accurately.
3. Use the provided options for select fields to ensure your selection is correct.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **stock_replenishment_report** (1)
* **Label:** stock_replenishment_report
* **Type:** text
* **Purpose:** This field is the main stock request description.
* **Instructions:** Enter a brief description of the stock request.

### **stock_request_form** (2)
* **Label:** stock_request_form
* **Type:** select_one
* **Options:** option 1, option 2, option 3
* **Purpose:** Select the type of stock request form.
* **Instructions:** Choose the correct option for the type of stock request.

### **stock_replenishment_date** (3)
* **Label:** stock_replenishment_date
* **Type:** date
* **Purpose:** This field captures the date of the stock request.
* **Instructions:** Enter the date of the stock request in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).

### **stock_request_time** (4)
* **Label:** stock_request_time
* **Type:** time
* **Purpose:** This field captures the time of the stock request.
* **Instructions:** Enter the time of the stock request in the standard time format (HH:MM AM/PM).

### **stock_request_note** (5)
* **Label:** stock_request_note
* **Type:** note
* **Purpose:** Additional information or notes about the stock request.
* **Instructions:** Enter any additional information or notes about the stock request.

### **stock_request_email** (6)
* **Label:** stock_request_email
* **Type:** email
* **Purpose:** Contact email of the stock requester.
* **Instructions:** Enter the email address of the person requesting stock.

### **stock_request_phone** (7)
* **Label:** stock_request_phone
* **Type:** text
* **Purpose:** Contact phone of the stock request.
* **Instructions:** Enter the phone number of the person requesting stock.

### **stock_requester_full_name** (8)
* **Label:** stock_requester_full_name
* **Type:** text
* **Purpose:** Full name of the person requesting stock.
* **Instructions:** Enter the full name of the person requesting stock.
