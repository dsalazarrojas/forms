# Admin Staff Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Admin Staff Details Form is a tool used to collect and record essential information about admin staff members. This form helps ensure accurate and up-to-date records, facilitating effective communication and collaboration within the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, simply follow these steps:

* Review the form's pages and sections to ensure you're filling out the correct information.
* Fill out the form completely and accurately, following the guidelines and hints provided for each field.
* Make sure to select the correct option for the contract type field.
* If an emergency contact is provided, ensure it includes both the name and phone number of the contact person.
* Use the notes section to add any additional details that might be helpful for staff members.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (employee_name)**: Enter the full name of the staff member as it appears on their payroll records.
* **Employee ID (employee_id)**: Enter the internal identifier assigned to the staff member.
* **Position (position)**: Enter the staff member's job title or position within the organization.
* **Manager Name (manager)**: Enter the name of the staff member's direct supervisor.
* **Office Location (office_location)**: Indicate whether the staff member works in a specific office building, on a floor, or remotely.
* **Workstation Needs (workstation_needs)**: Describe the staff member's workstation requirements, such as hardware or software needs.
* **Contract Type (contract_type)**: Select whether the staff member is on a Permanent, Temporary, or Contractor contract.
* **Emergency Contact (emergency_contact)**: If applicable, provide the name and phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Notes (notes)**: Use this field to add any additional details or comments about the staff member.
