# Staff Whereabouts Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Whereabouts Form is a tool for tracking and managing staff whereabouts. This form helps in keeping accurate records of staff attendance, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the correct staff location from the "Staff Location" field. This is a mandatory field and must be filled before proceeding.
2. Enter the start and end time of the staff's shift using the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields respectively.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
4. Enter the name and phone number of the staff's manager in the "Manager Name" and "Manager Phone" fields.
5. Enter the staff's email address in the "Email" field.
6. Click on the "Submit" button to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form 1
* **Form 1** (text): This is the title of the form.
### Form 2
* **Location Name** (text): Enter the name of the location where the staff is present.
### Form 3
* **Location Address** (text): Enter the address of the location where the staff is present.
### Form 4
* **Staff Location** (select_one): Select the location from the dropdown menu where the staff is present. 
### Form 5
* **Start Time** (date): Enter the start time of the staff's shift.
### Form 6
* **Start Time** (time): Enter the start time of the staff's shift. 
### Form 7
* **End Time** (time): Enter the end time of the staff's shift.
### Form 8
* **Notes** (note): Provide any additional notes or comments about the staff's whereabouts.
### Form 9
* **Manager Name** (text): Enter the name of the staff's manager. 
### Form 10
* **Manager Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the staff's manager. 
### Form 11
* **Email** (email): Enter the email address of the staff. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all mandatory fields before proceeding.
- Use the "Submit" button to save your changes.
- You can edit and delete any submissions after they have been saved.
