# prophets_dispatch_instruction_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "prophets_dispatch_instruction_form" is a form used to record and document instructions for dispatching items to a customer or location. It gathers information on the customer's details, the items being dispatched, and the dispatch date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "customer_name" field.
2. Fill in the customer's address in the "customer_address" field.
3. Check if the items will be dispatched to the customer's "points of service" by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "points_of_service" field.
4. Record the items that will be dispatched in the "items_received" field.
5. Select the date of dispatch from the calendar in the "date_of_service" field.
6. Sign the "customer_signature" field to acknowledge receipt of the document.
7. Optionally, enter the "business_id" to reference the company's internal business ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **dispatch_instruction_form** (`dispatch_instruction_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief note or instruction for the dispatch process. This is where you can include any important details or reminders for the person sending the items.
* **points_of_service** (`points_of_service`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if the items are being dispatched to the customer's points of service.
* **customer_info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the customer, such as contact details or account number.
* **items_received** (`items_received`, text, optional): Record the items that will be dispatched to the customer, including any specific details or instructions.
* **date_of_service** (`date_of_service`, date, optional): Select the date for dispatching the items.
* **customer_signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): Sign here to acknowledge receipt of this document.
* **customer_name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name for reference.
* **customer_address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address for reference.
* **business_id** (`business_id`, text, optional): Enter the company's internal business ID for reference.
* **date_dispatched** (`date_dispatched`, date, optional): Select the date the items were dispatched.

## Tips
