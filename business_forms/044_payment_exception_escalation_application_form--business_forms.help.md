# payment_exception_escalation_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assist users in reporting or requesting help with payment-related issues or discrepancies. It's intended to gather information about the payment exception, including customer details, payment method, and status updates. This information will help relevant teams or individuals identify and address the issue efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the payment exception details and fill in any relevant information about the issue.
2. Enter the customer's name and email address.
3. Select the correct account type.
4. Provide the date of the payment exception.
5. Specify the amount involved.
6. Choose the payment methods affected.
7. Select the current payment status.
8. Choose the resolution status.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about the issue.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Exception Details** (`payment_exception_details`): Please describe the payment exception issue in as much detail as possible.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter the customer's full name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Account Type** (`account_type`): Select the type of account that's relevant to the payment exception.
* **Date of Exception** (`date_of_exception`): Enter the date when the payment exception occurred.
* **Amount of Exception** (`amount_of_exception`): Enter the amount involved in the payment exception.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select all payment methods that were affected by the exception.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Select the current status of the payment.
* **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`): Select the status of the resolution efforts.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or explanations about the issue.
* **Chatjimmy** (`chatjimmy`): This field is currently not required and its purpose is unclear. Please do not fill this field.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible in the "Payment Exception Details" field to help relevant teams understand the issue.
* Ensure that you have entered the correct email address in the "Customer Email" field.
* If the payment method is not listed, select "Other" as a workaround.
* If the resolution status is unclear, choose "Not Applicable" and note the reason in the "Notes" field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all relevant information is provided.
