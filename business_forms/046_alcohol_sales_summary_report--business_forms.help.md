# Alcohol Sales Summary Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alcohol Sales Summary Report is a periodic report for store managers or reporting officers to provide a summary of alcohol sales data for a specific period. This report helps monitor and evaluate the store's alcohol sales performance over time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Reporting Officer/Manager Name** field with your full name and title.
2. Enter the **Store/Venue ID** number associated with your location.
3. Select the **Period Covered** from the list of options (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual).
4. Fill in the **Date of Report** with the date you are submitting this report.
5. Enter the sales figures for each category (Beer/Cider, Wine, Spirit/Liquor, and Premixed/RTD) in the respective fields.
6. Enter the **Total Number of Alcohol Transactions** for the period.
7. Identify the **Best Selling Item for Period** for the current reporting period.
8. Fill in the **Inventory Variance/Shrinkage** amount, if applicable.
9. Record the **Number of Refusals or ID Incidents** for age verification issues.
10. Add any manager notes or explanations for sales performance and inventory issues.
11. Finally, select **Verified and Signed** to confirm that the report data is accurate and matches the POS records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reporting Officer/Manager Name (required)**: Enter your full name and title for reporting purposes.
* **Store/Venue ID (required)**: Enter the unique ID number for your store location.
* **Date of Report (required)**: Enter the date you are submitting this report.
* **Period Covered (required)**: Select the reporting period (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual).
* **Total Beer/Cider Sales ($)**: Enter the total sales amount for beer and cider products.
* **Total Wine Sales ($)**: Enter the total sales amount for wine products.
* **Total Spirit/Liquor Sales ($)**: Enter the total sales amount for spirit and liquor products.
* **Total Premixed/RTD Sales ($)**: Enter the total sales amount for premixed and ready-to-drink products.
* **Gross Alcohol Sales Total ($)**: This field is automatically calculated and shows the sum of all categories.
* **Total Number of Alcohol Transactions**: Enter the total number of sales transactions for the period.
* **Best Selling Item for Period (optional)**: Identify the best-selling item for the current reporting period.
* **Inventory Variance/Shrinkage ($)**: Enter any inventory discrepancies or variances for the period.
* **Number of Refusals or ID Incidents (required)**: Record any instances of age verification issues.
* **Manager Notes and Variance Explanations (optional)**: Add any additional comments or explanations for sales performance and inventory issues.
* **I verify that this sales data is accurate and matches the POS records (required)**: Select **Verified and Signed** to confirm that the report data is accurate and matches the POS records.
