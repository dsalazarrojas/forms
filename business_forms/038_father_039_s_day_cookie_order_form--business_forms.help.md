# Fathers Day Cookie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an order of cookies for Father's Day. It's used to gather details such as the orderer's name, order message, cookie size and shape, order total, contact email, and delivery date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "orderer_name" field.
2. Write a message in the "order_message" field.
3. Select your preferred cookie size and shape in the "cookie_size" and "cookie_shape" fields.
4. If you'd like a special message printed on the cookie, select it from the "message_on_cookie" field.
5. Enter the total cost of your order in the "order_total" field.
6. Enter your contact email in the "contact_email" field.
7. Select the date you'd like to receive your order in the "order_date" field.
8. If you'd like to assign your order to someone, select 'Yes' or 'No' in the "assigned_to" field.
9. Finally, confirm your order by selecting "Submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Orderer Name (`orderer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to help us get your order right.**
* **Message (`order_message`, text, required): Type in a brief message for your father's special day.**
* **Cookie Size (`cookie_size`, select_multiple, required): Choose from Large, Medium, or Small to get the right size for your gift.**
* **Cookie Shape (`cookie_shape`, select_multiple, required): Select from Heart, Star, or Animal to add some fun to your cookie.**
* **Message on Cookie (`message_on_cookie`, select_one, optional): If you'd like a personalized message on the cookie, choose one of the options.**
* **Order Summary (`order_summary`, note, optional): Enter a brief summary of your order.**
* **Order Total (`order_total`, number, required): Enter the total cost of your order.**
* **Contact Email (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email to help us get in touch with you.**
* **Contact Phone (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.**
* **Submit (`submit`, select_one, required): Confirm your order by selecting "Submit".**
* **Order Date (`order_date`, date, required): Select the date you'd like to receive your order.**
* **Order Time (`order_time`, time, optional): Select the time you'd like to receive your order.**
* **Assigned To (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Decide if you'd like to assign your order to someone else.**
* **Order Instructions (`order_instructions`, note, optional): Enter any special instructions for your order.**
