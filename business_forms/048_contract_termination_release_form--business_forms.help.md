# Contract Termination Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by releasing parties to document the termination of a contract, including details about the releasing party, the released party, the contract terms, and any outstanding obligations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and documents before filling out this form.
2. Fill out each field with the accurate and complete information for your situation.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Releasing Party Name** (`releasing_party_name`, required): Enter the full name of the entity or individual releasing the contract.
* **Releasing Party Type** (`releasing_party_type`, required): Select the type of entity or individual releasing the contract (e.g. Corporation, LLC, Partnership, Individual).
* **Releasing Party Address** (`releasing_party_address`, required): Enter the full address of the releasing party.
* **Released Party Name** (`released_party_name`, required): Enter the full name of the entity or individual the contract was with.
* **Released Party Type** (`released_party_type`, required): Select the type of entity or individual the contract was with (e.g. Corporation, LLC, Partnership, Individual).
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, required): Enter the date the contract was released.
* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, required): Enter the contract reference number or identifier.
* **Contract Title** (`contract_title`, required): Enter the name of the contract being released.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, required): Enter the original contract date.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, required): Enter the date the contract was terminated.
* **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, required): Select the reason for the contract's termination (e.g. Mutual agreement, Completion of terms, Breach of contract).
* **Outstanding Obligations** (`outstanding_obligations`, required): Select whether there are any remaining commitments or obligations.
* **Obligations Description** (`obligations_description`, optional): Describe any remaining obligations in detail.
* **Financial Settlement** (`financial_settlement`, optional): Select the status of financial settlement (e.g. Fully settled, Partially settled, Pending settlement).
* **Settlement Amount** (`settlement_amount`, optional): Enter the dollar amount of financial settlement (if applicable).
* **Property Return** (`property_return`, required): Select whether any property was returned or is pending.
* **Property Description** (`property_description`, optional): Describe any property that was returned or is pending.
* **Confidentiality Survival** (`confidentiality_survival`, required): Select whether confidentiality terms survive the contract's termination.
* **Non Compete Survival** (`non_compete_survival`, optional): Select whether non-compete terms survive the contract's termination (not required but may be relevant).
* **Release Scope** (`release_scope`, required): Select the scope of the release (e.g. Full and complete release, Partial release, Conditional release).
* **Future Claims** (`future_claims`, required): Select whether future claims are waived.
* **Governing Law** (`governing_law`, required): Select the state law that applies to the contract.
* **Releasing Party Signature** (`releasing_party_signature`, required): Sign the form as the releasing party.
* **Released Party Signature** (`released_party_signature`, required): Sign the form as the released party (if present).
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, optional): Sign the form as a witness (if required).
* **Notarization Required** (`notarization_required`, required): Select whether notarization is required for this form submission.
* **Property Return** (`property_return`, optional): Select whether any property was returned or is pending.
