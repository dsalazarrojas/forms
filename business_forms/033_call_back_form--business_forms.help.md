# Call Back Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect contact information and specific details for a callback request. Fill out this form to schedule a callback with our team, and our representatives will contact you at the preferred time and contact method specified.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact information: Enter your name, phone number, and email address in the designated fields.
2. Specify the subject of your inquiry: Choose from the list of subjects or enter a custom subject related to your question or concern.
3. Select the type of inquiry: Choose from the options for the type of inquiry you have, such as Sales, Support, or Information.
4. Provide a message: Type in a detailed message about your inquiry, including any specific questions or concerns you have.
5. Schedule the callback: Choose a preferred date and time for the callback, and select a time zone if different from your local time zone.
6. Specify any additional account information: If applicable, enter your account number and customer ID.
7. Confirm your contact preferences: Choose how you would like to be contacted, such as phone, email, or text.
8. Indicate the urgency of your request: Select the level of urgency for your inquiry, such as Routine, Important, or Urgent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (Required):
	+ A text input field to enter your full name.
* **Phone Number** (Required):
	+ A text input field to enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address** (Required):
	+ An email input field where you can provide your email address.
* **Subject of Inquiry** (Required):
	+ A text input field to specify the subject of your inquiry.
* **Type of Inquiry** (Required):
	+ A dropdown menu to select the type of inquiry from options: Sales, Support, Information, Complaint, or Other.
* **Message or Details** (Required):
	+ A text input field to provide a detailed message about your inquiry.
* **Preferred Date for Callback** (Required):
	+ A date input field to schedule the callback on a preferred date.
* **Preferred Time** (Required):
	+ A dropdown menu to select the preferred time for the callback, such as Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Your Time Zone** (Optional):
	+ A dropdown menu to select your time zone, such as Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific, or Other.
* **Account Number (if applicable)** (Optional):
	+ A text input field to enter your account number if applicable.
* **Customer ID** (Optional):
	+ A text input field to enter your customer ID if applicable.
* **Are You an Existing Customer?** (Required):
	+ A dropdown menu to confirm if you are an existing customer, Yes or No.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Required):
	+ A dropdown menu to select how you would like to be contacted, such as Phone, Email, or Text.
* **How Urgent Is Your Request?** (Optional):
	+ A dropdown menu to select the level of urgency for your inquiry, such as Routine, Important, or Urgent.
* **-- Contact Information --** (Optional):
	+ A note field to enter any additional contact information.
* **-- Reason for Callback --** (Optional):
	+ A note field to specify the reason for the callback.
* **-- Account Information --** (Optional):
	+ A note field to enter any additional account information.
* **-- Contact Preferences --** (Optional):
	+ A note field to specify any contact preferences.
* **-- Preferred Callback Time --** (Optional):
	+ A note field to enter any preferred callback time.

Note: This help guide assumes that users have already navigated to the form page.
