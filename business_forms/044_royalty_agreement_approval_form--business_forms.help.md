# Royalty Agreement Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and manage royalty agreements between Company A and Company B. It captures the essential details of the agreement, including the agreement date and time, royalty rate, payment terms, and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Company A and Company B fields with the relevant details.
2. Enter the agreement date and time in the Agreement Date and Agreement Time fields, respectively.
3. Enter the royalty rate in the Royalty Rate field.
4. If necessary, add any payment terms in the Payment Terms field.
5. Fill in the Approval Date and Approval Time fields with the date and time of approval.
6. Enter the name of the person approving the agreement in the Approved By field.
7. Choose the Approval Status from the dropdown menu (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
8. Add any comments in the Comments field (optional).
9. Sign the form with your signature in the Signature field.
10. Fill in your name and role in the Name and Role fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Royalty Agreement Approval Form** (1): This is the title of the form, which should be filled in with the name of the agreement.
* **Company A** (2): Enter the name of Company A, the party entering into the agreement.
* **Company B** (3): Enter the name of Company B, the party entering into the agreement.
* **Agreement Date** (4): Enter the date on which the agreement was made.
* **Agreement Time** (5): Enter the time on which the agreement was made.
* **Royalty Rate** (6): Enter the rate at which royalties will be paid to Company B.
* **Payment Terms** (7): If payment terms are applicable, enter them here (optional).
* **Approval Date** (8): Enter the date on which the agreement was approved.
* **Approval Time** (9): Enter the time on which the agreement was approved.
* **Approved By** (10): Enter the name of the person approving the agreement.
* **Approval Status** (11): Choose whether the agreement is Approved, Rejected, or Pending.
* **Comments** (12): Add any comments about the agreement (optional).
* **Signature** (13): Sign and date your signature.
* **Name** (14): Enter your name.
* **Role** (15): Enter your role in the organization.
* **Department** (16): Enter your department.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the form is properly approved and documented.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure all details are accurate.
* Save a copy of the signed form for future reference.
