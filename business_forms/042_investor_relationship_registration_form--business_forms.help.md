## Investor Relationship Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Investor Relationship Registration Form is used to register investor relationships with the company, providing a clear and organized overview of each investor's details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter the investor's details in the "User Input" field.
3. Select the appropriate "Company Type" from the given options (Public, Private, or Limited).
4. Choose the "Relationship Type" from the provided options (Partner, Customer, or Supplier).
5. Select the "Investment Type" from the options (Equity, Debt, or Hybrid).
6. Enter the investment amount in the "Investment Amount" field.
7. Enter the investment date in the "Investment Date" field.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (id: 1): Enter a brief description of the investor relationship.
- **First Name** (id: 2): Enter the first name of the investor.
- **Last Name** (id: 3): Enter the last name of the investor.
- **Company** (id: 4): Enter the name of the investor's company.
- **Job Title** (id: 5): Enter the job title of the investor.
- **Email** (id: 6): Enter the investor's email address.
- **Phone** (id: 7): Enter the investor's phone number.
- **Company Type** (id: 8): Choose the type of company the investor represents (Public, Private, or Limited).
- **Relationship Type** (id: 9): Select the type of relationship the investor has with the company (Partner, Customer, or Supplier).
- **Investment Type** (id: 10): Select the type of investment made (Equity, Debt, or Hybrid).
- **Investment Amount** (id: 11): Enter the investment amount.
- **Investment Date** (id: 12): Enter the date of the investment.
- **Investment Amount Goals 1** (id: 13): Enter the investment amount for the specified goal 1.
- **Notes** (id: 14): Enter any additional notes or comments about the investor relationship.
