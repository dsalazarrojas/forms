# digital_ecosystem_strategy_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The digital ecosystem strategy contact form is a general contact form used to gather information from visitors who are interested in collaborating with the company. It's a minimal form that collects essential details for follow-up conversations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Specify your company name in the "Company" field.
6. Mention your position or title in the "Position" field.
7. Select your department or team in the "Department" field.
8. Enter your location in the "Location" field.
9. Write a brief message to express your interest in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your personal last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for follow-up calls.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Specify the name of your company or organization.
* **Position** (`position`, text, required: false): Mention your position or title within your company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Select the department or team you're working with.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter your office or work location.
* **Message** (`message`, note, required: false): Write a brief message to express your interest in collaborating with the company.
* **Confirm E-mail** (`confirm_email`, email, required: false): Verify the email address entered in the "Email" field to prevent typos or errors.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required: false): Confirm your phone number to prevent typos or errors.

## Tips
Please double-check the information you provide before submitting the form. The company will use the details you enter to follow up with you.
