# Risk Mitigation Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Mitigation Consultation Request Form is a tool used to identify, assess, and prioritize potential risks within a business environment. This form helps stakeholders to provide detailed information about risks, their impact, and mitigation plans, facilitating informed decision-making and proactive risk management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant sections that apply to your risk mitigation consultation request.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields with accurate and detailed information about the risk.
3. Ensure that you provide all necessary information for each field, including risk type, level, and probability.
4. Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Risk Mitigation Form
* **Risk Mitigation Form** (`risk_mitigation_form`, text, optional): This is the main field where you can describe the risk in detail.
### Page 2: Business Information
* **Business Information** (`business_info`, text, optional): Provide information about the business related to the risk, such as business name and address.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the business or organization.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the business.
### Page 3: Risk Information
* **Risk Information** (`risk_info`, text, optional): Describe the risk in detail, including its impact, frequency, and type.
* **Risk Type** (`risk_type`, text, optional): Specify the type of risk (e.g., operational, financial, strategic).
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, number, optional): Assess the risk level on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Risk Probability** (`risk_probability`, number, optional): Estimate the likelihood of the risk occurring (e.g., 0-1).
### Page 4: Mitigation
* **Mitigation Steps** (`mitigation_steps`, text, optional): Outline the steps taken to mitigate the risk.
* **Mitigation Approach** (`mitigation_approach`, text, optional): Describe the approach used to mitigate the risk.
* **Mitigation Status** (`mitigation_status`, text, optional): Status of the mitigation plan (e.g., not started, in progress, completed).
* **Mitigation Plan** (`mitigation_plan`, text, optional): Outline the mitigation plan.
### Page 5: Risk Status
* **Risk Status** (`risk_status`, text, optional): Status of the risk (e.g., identified, assessed, mitigated).
* **Risk Mitigation Status** (`risk_mitigation_status`, select_multiple, optional): Status of the risk mitigation (e.g., not mitigated, mitigated, unknown).
### Page 6: Additional Information
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to mitigate the risk (e.g., Chat, API, Other).
* **Risk Mitigation Date** (`risk_mitigation_date`, date, optional): Date the risk was mitigated.
* **Risk Mitigation Time** (`risk_mitigation_time`, time, optional): Time the risk was mitigated.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Additional notes about the risk.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Contact phone number.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Outline the next steps to take.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): The user assigned to handle the risk.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the "Risk Mitigation Consultation Request Form" accurately and effectively. If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to ask.
