# Affiliate Marketing Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for an affiliate marketing audit, which is a crucial process for evaluating the performance of an affiliate marketing program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form carefully, ensuring that you provide accurate and relevant information.
2. Start by providing the audit overview, including your name and the date of the audit.
3. Then, move on to the program performance section, where you'll input metrics such as total affiliate revenue, total commission paid, active affiliates, conversion rate, and more.
4. Next, focus on the compliance section, where you'll assess the program's compliance with branding and messaging.
5. Finally, in the top partners analysis section, identify the top performers and their trends.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Auditor Name**: The name of the person performing the audit. This is a required field that helps identify who completed the audit.
  (ID: 2, Type: text, required: true)
- **Date of Audit**: The date on which the audit was performed. This is also a required field to track the audit's timeframe. (ID: 3, Type: date, required: true)
- **Period Under Review**: The time period being audited, for example, Q1 2024. This field is necessary to provide context for the metrics. (ID: 4, Type: text, required: true)
- **Total Affiliate Revenue ($)**: The total revenue generated by affiliates. This is a required field to assess the program's financial performance. (ID: 6, Type: number, required: true)
- **Total Commission Paid ($)**: The cost of sales to the program. This is another required field to evaluate the program's financials. (ID: 7, Type: number, required: true)
- **Number of Active Affiliates**: The number of affiliates with at least one sale. This field helps identify the program's reach and engagement. (ID: 8, Type: number, required: true)
- **Affiliate Conversion Rate (%)**: The ratio of sales to clicks. This is a required field to evaluate the program's effectiveness. (ID: 9, Type: number, required: true)
- **Fraudulent Activity Detected?**: Check if any fraudulent activity has been detected, such as self-referrals or bot traffic. This field is required to identify potential issues. (ID: 11, Type: select_one, required: true)
- **Brand Compliance Check**: Assess the affiliates' use of correct branding and messaging. This field is necessary to ensure the program's integrity. (ID: 12, Type: select_one, required: true)
- **Top Performer 1 Name**: Identify the top-performing affiliate. This field helps highlight successful performers. (ID: 14, Type: text, required: false)
- **Top Performer Trend**: Evaluate the trend of the top performer's performance. This field helps identify growth or decline. (ID: 15, Type: select_one, required: false)
- **Audit Recommendations**: Provide action items to improve the program. This is a required field for program optimization. (ID: 16, Type: text, required: true)
