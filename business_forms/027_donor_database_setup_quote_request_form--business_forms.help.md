# Donor Database Setup Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who are requesting a quote for setting up their donor database. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide your organization with a quote that meets their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field (optional).
4. Enter your non-profit organization's information in the "non_profit_organization" field.
5. Provide a detailed description of your donor database requirements in the "donor_database_description" field.
6. Indicate whether you prefer to use a "yes" or "no" for donor management system in the "preferred_donor_management_system" field.
7. If you have any custom requirements, enter them in the "custom_requirements" field (optional).
8. Add any additional information you feel is necessary in the "additional_info" field (optional).
9. Select the donor management tool you would like to be assigned from the "assigned_tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (`text`, required): Your name.
* **email** (`email`, required): Your email address.
* **phone_number** (`text`, optional): Your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).
* **non_profit_organization** (`text`, required): Information about your non-profit organization.
* **donor_database_description** (`text`, required): A detailed description of your donor database requirements.
* **preferred_donor_management_system** (`select_one`, required): Choose "Yes" if you prefer to use a donor management system or "No" if not.
* **custom_requirements** (`text`, optional): Any additional requirements you may have.
* **additional_info** (`note`, optional): Any additional information you'd like to provide.
* **assigned_tool** (`text`, required): The donor management tool you'd like to be assigned.
