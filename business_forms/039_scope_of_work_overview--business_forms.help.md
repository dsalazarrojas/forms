# Scope Of Work Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scope Of Work Overview form is a project management tool to define and communicate project expectations, deliverables, and timelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide the required information for your project.
2. Ensure you answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Use the available space to provide detailed explanations and notes where necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Expectations
* **Project Expectations** (`project_expectations`, text, Optional): Briefly describe the project's overall objectives, goals, and expectations. What needs to be achieved, and what is expected from the team.

### Deliverables
* **Deliverables** (`deliverables`, text, Optional): List the specific products, services, or results that the project aims to produce or deliver. Be as detailed as possible to ensure everyone knows what is expected.

### Timeline
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, date, Optional): Provide the start and end dates for the project, including any significant milestones or deadlines. Use the correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### Team Responsibilities
* **Team Responsibilities** (`team_responsibilities`, text, Optional): Describe the roles and responsibilities of each team member, including their tasks, duties, and any specific expectations.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to avoid misunderstandings.
* Use clear and concise language to ensure everyone understands the project scope.
* Review and revise the form as needed to reflect changes in the project's scope or timeline.
* Save a copy of the completed form for future reference and updates.
