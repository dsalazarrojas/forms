# simple_business_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow business owners to submit their contact information and a message to the platform. It is intended for internal use, and the data collected will be used for communication purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and submit. You can leave fields blank if they are not applicable to your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form 1 (form_1)**: This is the first field of the form, where you can enter any information you think is relevant to your business. It is not a required field.
- **Business Name (business_name)**: Enter the name of your business.
- **Email (email)**: Enter your business email address.
- **Phone (phone)**: Enter your business phone number.
- **Description (description)**: Enter a brief description of your business.
- **Message (message)**: Enter a message for the platform.
- **Submit (business_forms_submit)**: This is the submit button, click it to send your data to the platform.
- **Agree (business_forms_agree)**: Select 'Yes' if you agree to use this form for communication purposes only.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your data is accurately submitted.
* You can enter any additional information in the first field.
* If you select 'No' for the "Agree" field, your submission will not be processed.
* If you need to make changes to your submission, you can simply re-submit the form.
