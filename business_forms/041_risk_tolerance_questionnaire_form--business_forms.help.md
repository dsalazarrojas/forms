# Risk Tolerance Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Tolerance Questionnaire Form is a tool used to understand your investment preferences and risk tolerance, helping you make informed decisions about your investments. This form will guide you through a series of questions that will help you determine your:
* Investment goals
* Risk tolerance
* Investment style
* Expected returns
* Liquidity requirements
* Experience with investments

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question honestly, using the options provided.
2. Select all applicable options for each question.
3. Review and submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **investment_goal** (`investment_goal`, text, not required):
	+ Enter a brief description of your investment goals, such as "Conservative growth" or "Emergency fund for a down payment on a house."
* **time_horizon** (`time_horizon`, select one, not required):
	+ Select the timeframe for your investment, such as "1 year" or "5 years".
* **risk_tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, select multiple, not required):
	+ Choose the risks you are willing to take on your investments, such as "High" or "Medium".
* **asset_allocation** (`asset_allocation`, select multiple, not required):
	+ Select the types of investments you are interested in, such as "Stocks" or "Real Estate".
* **investment_style** (`investment_style`, select one, not required):
	+ Choose the style of your investments, such as "Aggressive" or "Conservative".
* **return_expectation** (`return_expectation`, number, not required):
	+ Enter a specific expected return on your investment, such as "7%".
* **liquidity_requirement** (`liquidity_requirement`, number, not required):
	+ Enter a specific liquidity requirement for your investment, such as "6 months".
* **risk_free_return** (`risk_free_return`, select one, not required):
	+ Indicate whether you expect a risk-free return on your investment, such as "True" or "False".
* **investment_experience** (`investment_experience`, select multiple, not required):
	+ Select your level of experience with investments, such as "Beginner" or "Advanced".

## Tips
* Answer honestly and accurately to ensure the results are representative of your investment preferences.
* Select options that best match your investment goals and expectations.
* Review and submit your responses carefully to ensure accuracy.
