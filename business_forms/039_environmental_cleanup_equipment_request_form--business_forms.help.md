# Environmental Cleanup Equipment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request equipment for environmental cleanup projects. It helps in efficiently managing equipment requests, ensuring that the right equipment is available at the right time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to provide accurate and sufficient information.
2. Select the correct equipment type (Heavy Machinery, Light Equipment, or Specialized Equipment) from the dropdown list.
3. Enter your contact person's details, including their name and email address.
4. Provide a delivery date for the equipment if you need it by a specific date.
5. Specify the equipment you need in the "Equipment Requested" field.
6. Add any additional contact details or notes in the "Contact Details" field.
7. If assigned to a specific tool, enter the tool's name in the "Assigned Tool" field.
8. Add any project-specific details in the "Project Details" field.
9. Indicate the project's status (Approved, Declined, or Pending Review) in the "Project Status" field.
10. Enter the name of the person or team assigned to the project in the "Assigned To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization**: Enter the name of your organization requesting the equipment.
* **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment you need (Heavy Machinery, Light Equipment, or Specialized Equipment).
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for coordinating the equipment request.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Delivery Date**: If you need the equipment by a specific date, enter the date.
* **Equipment Requested**: Specify the exact equipment you need, including any additional details or notes.
* **Contact Details**: Add any additional contact information or notes about the request.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the name of the tool assigned to the project if applicable.
* **Notes**: Add any additional project-specific details or information.
* **Project Details**: Enter any project details that may be relevant to the equipment request.
* **Project Status**: Indicate the current status of the project (Approved, Declined, or Pending Review).
* **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person or team assigned to the project.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* Ensure that you're not duplicating a request that has already been made.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
