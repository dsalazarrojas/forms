# Supplier Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Contract Form is a standard tool for capturing essential information about a supplier's contract, including their name, contract terms, start and end dates, contact details, and other relevant details. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and recorded accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Supplier Name
	* Contract Terms
	* Contract Start Date
	* Contract End Date
	* Supplier Contact Email
	* Supplier Contact Phone Number
2. If applicable, fill in the optional fields:
	* Supplier Address
	* Contract Value
	* Payment Terms
	* Assigned Tool
	* Assigned Tool 2
	* Category of Business Form
	* Category of Business Forms
	* Form Identifiers
	* Output File
	* Description
3. Review and ensure all fields are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (1): Enter the name of the supplier.
* **Contract Terms** (2): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the contract terms.
* **Contract Start Date** (3): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (4): Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Supplier Address** (5): Enter the supplier's address (optional).
* **Contract Value** (6): Enter the contract value (optional).
* **Payment Terms** (7): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the payment terms.
* **Supplier Contact Email** (9): Enter the supplier's contact email (required).
* **Supplier Contact Phone Number** (10): Enter the supplier's contact phone number (optional).
* **Assigned Tool** (11): Enter the assigned tool for the supplier (optional).
* **Assigned Tool 2** (17): Enter the assigned tool 2 for the supplier (optional).
* **Category of Business Form** (12): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the category of business form.
* **Category of Business Forms** (13): Select multiple categories of business forms (optional).
* **Form Identifiers** (14): Enter the form identifiers (optional).
* **Output File** (15): Enter the output file (optional).
* **Description** (16): Enter a brief description of the supplier's contract (optional).
* **Category** (18): Select multiple categories for the supplier's contract (optional).

## Tips

* Double-check the accuracy and completeness of all fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about a field, you can contact your supervisor or the form owner for assistance.
