# Entity Tax Preparation Status - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the status of entity tax preparation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant entity and its tax year.
2. Choose the current status of the entity's tax preparation.
3. Enter any next steps or actions needed for the entity's tax preparation.
4. Indicate the frequency of follow-up actions.
5. Record the user who last updated the entity's tax preparation status.
6. Record the date and time the status was last updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the entity's organization.
* **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, number, optional): Enter the tax year for the entity's tax preparation.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following statuses: Not Started, In Progress, Completed.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Describe any next steps or actions needed for the entity's tax preparation.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`followup_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following frequencies: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly.
* **Last Updated By** (`last_updated_by`, text, optional): Record the user who last updated the entity's tax preparation status.
* **Last Updated At** (`last_updated_at`, date, optional): Record the date and time the status was last updated.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, optional): Record the user who updated the entity's tax preparation status.
