# Auto Insurance Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Insurance Billing Form is a critical tool for processing auto insurance payments. It gathers essential information from policyholders for successful billing and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all required fields filled out correctly.
2. Enter the policyholder's full name exactly as it appears on their policy.
3. Provide the active insurance policy number.
4. Enter the billing email address for payment confirmation.
5. Enter the best contact phone number for billing purposes.
6. Choose the payment method from the provided options.
7. Enter the payment amount in USD.
8. Select the billing cycle from the options provided.
9. Enter the complete mailing address for billing purposes.
10. Choose whether to enroll in automatic payments.
11. Provide any additional billing notes or references.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policyholder Full Name** (`policyholder_full_name`, text, required): Enter the policyholder's full name as it appears on their policy.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (`insurance_policy_number`, text, required): Enter your active policy ID.
* **Billing Email** (`billing_email`, email, required): Enter the email address used for payment confirmation.
* **Billing Phone** (`billing_phone`, text, required): Enter your best contact number for billing purposes.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_one, required): Select your payment method: Credit Card, Debit Card, or Electronic Check - ACH.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount in USD.
* **Payment Plan** (`payment_plan`, select_one, required): Select your billing cycle: Monthly Installment, Semi-Annual, or Full Annual Payment.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Enter the mailing address for billing purposes.
* **City, State, and Zip** (`city_state_zip`, text, required): Enter the complete address details.
* **Automatic Payments Enrollment** (`automatic_payments_enrollment`, select_one, required): Choose whether to enroll in automatic payments.
* **Billing Notes or Reference** (`billing_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information for your payment.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submission.
- Use a clear and consistent format for your address entry.
- Select the correct payment method and billing cycle to avoid any payment discrepancies.
