# Debit Card Expense Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about employee expenses related to debit card transactions. It is essential for tracking and recording these expenses accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any other relevant information in the **Merchant Name** field.
2. Enter your debit card number in the **Card Number** field.
3. Select the type of your debit card from the **Card Type** dropdown menu.
4. Enter the date of the transaction in the **Date** field.
5. Enter the amount of the transaction in the **Amount** field.
6. Enter a description of the transaction in the **Description** field.
7. Enter the city where the transaction occurred in the **Merchant City** field.
8. Enter the state where the transaction occurred in the **Merchant State** field.
9. Enter the country where the transaction occurred in the **Merchant Country** field.
10. Enter the ZIP code of the merchant in the **Merchant ZIP** field.
11. Enter the address of the merchant in the **Merchant Address** field.
12. Enter the name of the person who submitted the expense in the **Submitted By** field.
13. Enter the name of the person who approved the expense in the **Approver Name** field.
14. Enter the email of the person who approved the expense in the **Approver Email** field.
15. Enter the phone number of the person who approved the expense in the **Approver Phone** field.
16. Enter any comments from the approver in the **Approver Comments** field.
17. Enter the date when the expense was submitted for approval in the **Submitted Date** field.
18. Enter the time when the expense was submitted for approval in the **Submitted Time** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission** (Text): Briefly describe the purpose of your expense submission.
* **Card Number** (Text): Enter your debit card number to track your expenses accurately.
* **Card Type** (Select One): Select the type of your debit card from the dropdown menu (e.g. Visa, MasterCard, American Express, etc.).
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date of the transaction.
* **Amount** (Number): Enter the amount of the transaction.
* **Receipt** (Text): Enter any additional information about the transaction, such as receipt number or reference number.
* **Receipt Date** (Date): Enter the date when the receipt was received.
* **Receipt Time** (Time): Enter the time when the receipt was received.
* **Category** (Text): Enter a category for your expense, such as "Food" or "Transportation".
* **Description** (Text): Briefly describe the expense.
* **Merchant Name** (Text): Enter the name of the merchant where the transaction occurred.
* **Merchant City** (Text): Enter the city where the transaction occurred.
* **Merchant State** (Text): Enter the state where the transaction occurred.
* **Merchant Country** (Text): Enter the country where the transaction occurred.
* **Merchant ZIP** (Text): Enter the ZIP code of the merchant.
* **Merchant Address** (Text): Enter the address of the merchant.
* **Submitted By** (Text): Enter your name.
* **Submitted To** (Text): Enter the name of the person you submitted the expense to.
* **Submitted Date** (Date): Enter the date when you submitted the expense.
* **Submitted Time** (Time): Enter the time when you submitted the expense.
* **Approver Name** (Text): Enter the name of the person who approved the expense.
* **Approver Email** (Email): Enter the email of the person who approved the expense.
* **Approver Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the person who approved the expense.
* **Approver Comments** (Text): Enter any comments from the approver.
* **Approver Date** (Date): Enter the date when the expense was approved.
* **Approver Time** (Time): Enter the time when the expense was approved.

## Tips

* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure that it is accurate.
* Avoid submitting duplicate expenses to ensure that all expenses are properly tracked and approved.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your approver or the person who submitted the expense to you.
