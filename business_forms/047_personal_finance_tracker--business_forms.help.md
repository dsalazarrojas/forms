# Personal Finance Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Finance Tracker is a form designed to track personal financial information, including income and expenses. It helps users to keep track of their financial data, which can be useful for budgeting, planning, and making informed financial decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Input your income sources (personal or business) in the "User Income" field.
2. Input your business income in the "Business Income" field.
3. Input your total income in the "Total Income" field.
4. Input your regular expenses in the "Regular Expenses" field.
5. Input your savings in the "Savings" field.
6. Input your personal expenses in the "User Expenses" field.
7. Input your business expenses in the "Business Expenses" field.
8. Indicate whether the income is from a personal or business source in the "Income Source" field.
9. Indicate whether the expense is from a personal or business source in the "Expense Source" field.
10. Input the date of the financial data in the "Date" field.
11. Input the month of the financial data in the "Month" field.
12. Input the year of the financial data in the "Year" field.
13. Input the time of the financial data in the "Time" field.
14. Input any additional notes about the financial data in the "Notes" field.
15. Input your email address in the "Email" field.
16. Input your phone number in the "Phone" field.
17. Indicate whether the business is a personal or business expense in the "Business Category" field.
18. Input any additional tags or labels for the financial data in the "Tags" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Income** (`User Income`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to input the user's income.
* **Business Income** (`Business Income`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to input the user's business income.
* **Total Income** (`Total Income`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to input the total income of the user.
* **Regular Expenses** (`Regular Expenses`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to input the user's regular expenses.
* **Savings** (`Savings`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to input the user's savings.
* **User Expenses** (`User Expenses`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to input the user's personal expenses.
* **Business Expenses** (`Business Expenses`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to input the user's business expenses.
* **Income Source** (`Income Source`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to indicate whether the income is from a personal or business source.
* **Expense Source** (`Expense Source`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to indicate whether the expense is from a personal or business source.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required: false): This field is used to input the date of the financial data.
* **Month** (`Month`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to indicate the month of the financial data.
* **Year** (`Year`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to input the year of the financial data.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required: false): This field is used to input the time of the financial data.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required: false): This field is used to input any additional notes about the financial data.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): This field is used to input the user's email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to input the user's phone number.
* **Business Category** (`Business Category`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to indicate whether the business is a personal or business expense.
* **Tags** (`Tags`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to input any additional tags or labels for the financial data.
