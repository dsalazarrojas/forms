# Product Expiry Monitoring Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to monitor and report product expirations. It helps track the status, frequency, and categorization of products that are about to expire or have already expired. The form ensures that businesses can keep track of their products' shelf life and make informed decisions about inventory management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category that best describes the form's purpose: **Order Forms** or **Business Forms**.
2. Indicate the product's **Expiry Status**: choose between **Expiring Now** or **Not Expiring**.
3. Enter the product's **Expiry Date** (if it's expired) or **Expiry Frequency** (if it's not yet expired).
4. Add any additional **Expiry Note** for the product.
5. Select the **Expiry Frequency**: choose from **Weekly**, **Biweekly**, or **Monthly**.
6. Enter the **Product Expiry Frequency Note** for further explanation.
7. Add any **Customer Note** for the product.
8. Optionally, enter the **Assigned Tool** and **Assigned User** for further reference.
