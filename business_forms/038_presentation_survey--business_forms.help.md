# Presentation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users about a presentation they attended. Please take a few minutes to provide your thoughts, and help us improve our presentations for you!

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a page with a question and start typing your answers in the corresponding field.
2. Answer each question accurately, providing as much detail as you can.
3. If a question doesn't apply to you, just leave it blank or type "N/A".
4. Once you've completed all the pages, review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: What do you think about the presentation?
* **What do you think about the presentation?** (text): Answer this question with your overall thoughts about the presentation. Be as detailed as you can, sharing your impressions and feelings.

### Page 2: What did you learn?
* **What did you learn?** (text): Share what new information or knowledge you gained from the presentation. Be specific and provide examples if possible.

### Page 3: What did you like?
* **What did you like?** (select multiple): Choose all the aspects that you found pleasing or enjoyable about the presentation. You can select multiple options.
	+ The presenter was engaging and enthusiastic.
	+ The presentation was well-organized and logical.
	+ The content was informative and interesting.
	+ The presentation was well-represented with images and slides.
	+ The presenter was knowledgeable and expert in the field.
	+ The presentation was not well-organized and logical.
	+ The content was not informative and interesting.
	+ The presentation was not well-represented with images and slides.
	+ The presenter was not knowledgeable and expert in the field.
