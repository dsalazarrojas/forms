# return_authorization_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization from the customer for returns or exchanges of products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the customer information fields, including name, phone number, and email.
2. Provide details about the order, including the order number and any other relevant information.
3. Select the reason for the return from the available options.
4. Add any additional return notes.
5. Sign the form to acknowledge that you have authorized the return.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: Enter the customer's name, phone number, and email address.
* **Order Details**: Describe the order that you are returning, including the order number and any other relevant information.
* **Return Reason**: Select the reason for the return from the provided options:
	+ Product Defective
	+ Order Not Received
	+ Order Not as Described
	+ Order Not Wanted
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Return Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the return.
* **Customer Signature**: Sign the form to acknowledge that you have authorized the return.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address for contact purposes.
* **Authorized Signature**: Sign the form as the customer's representative or authorized person.
* **Date Returned**: Enter the date the product was returned.
* **Date Received**: Enter the date the product was received.
* **Product ID**: Enter the product ID number, if applicable.
