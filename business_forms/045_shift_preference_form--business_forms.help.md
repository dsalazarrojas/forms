# Shift Preference Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about employee shifts, preferences, and submission status. It's essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure efficient scheduling and management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section with the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
3. Select your department from the options provided.
4. Choose your shift preference from the available options.
5. Add any additional notes if necessary.
6. If applicable, select the employee's status from the options provided.
7. Enter the employee's ID number.
8. Enter the form's ID number.
9. Select the person who submitted the form.
10. Choose the submission date and time.
11. Select the submission status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_input`, text, *): Please enter your name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, *): Select your department.
* **Manager** (`manager_input`, number): Enter the ID of your manager.
* **Shift** (`shift_input`, select_one, *): Choose your shift preference.
* **Notes** (`notes_input`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your shift preference.
* **Employee** (`employee_input`, select_one, *): Select whether you are an employee or not.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, *): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, *): Enter the form's ID number.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by_input`, select_multiple, *): Select who submitted this form (Option A or Option B).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, *): Enter the submission date.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, *): Enter the submission time.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, *): Select the submission status (Active or Inactive).
* **Submission Time 2** (`submission_time_2`, time, *): Enter the submission time (if different from the initial submission time).

Note: Asterisks (\*) denote required fields.
