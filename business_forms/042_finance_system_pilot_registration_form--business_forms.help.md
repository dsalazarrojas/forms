# Finance System Pilot Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for the finance system pilot program. The form collects information about the participant's details, objectives, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information and details at hand.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, taking care to provide complete and correct information.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
4. Take your time to fill out the form carefully, as rushing through it may lead to errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Finance System Pilot Registration Form** (`title`, text, required): Enter the title of your finance system pilot program.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Enter your participant details, including your name, role, and other relevant information.
* **Pilot Participants Objectives** (`pilot_participants_objectives`, text, required): Enter your objectives for participating in the pilot program.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your consent to participate in the pilot program.
* **Finance System Pilot Registration Form Notes** (`finance_system_pilot_registration_form_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your participation in the pilot program.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Finance System Pilot Registration Form Phone** (`finance_system_pilot_registration_form_phone`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have a phone number.
* **Finance System Pilot Registration Form Participant Objectives** (`finance_system_pilot_registration_form_participant_objectives`, text, optional): Enter your participant objectives.
* **Finance System Pilot Registration Form Participant Objectives Objectives** (`finance_system_pilot_registration_form_participant_objectives_objectives`, text, optional): Enter your objectives for participant objectives.
* **Finance System Pilot Registration Form Participant Objectives Participants** (`finance_system_pilot_registration_form_participant_objectives_participants`, text, optional): Enter the participants for your objectives.
* **Finance System Pilot Registration Form Participant Objectives Start Date** (`finance_system_pilot_registration_form_participant_objectives_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date for your objectives.
* **Finance System Pilot Registration Form Participant Objectives Start Time** (`finance_system_pilot_registration_form_participant_objectives_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time for your objectives.
* **Finance System Pilot Registration Form Participant Objectives End Date** (`finance_system_pilot_registration_form_participant_objectives_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date for your objectives.
* **Finance System Pilot Registration Form Participant Objectives End Time** (`finance_system_pilot_registration_form_participant_objectives_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time for your objectives.
* **Finance System Pilot Registration Form Participant Objectives Notes** (`finance_system_pilot_registration_form_participant_objectives_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your participant objectives.
* **Finance System Pilot Name** (`finance_system_pilot_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the finance system pilot.
* **Finance System Pilot Role** (`finance_system_pilot_role`, text, optional): Enter your role in the finance system pilot.
* **Finance System Pilot Department** (`finance_system_pilot_department`, text, optional): Enter the department for the finance system pilot.
* **Finance System Pilot Location** (`finance_system_pilot_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the finance system pilot.
* **Finance System Pilot Phone** (`finance_system_pilot_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number for the finance system pilot.
* **Participant Company** (`participant_company`, text, optional): Enter the company that you are representing.
* **Participant Role** (`participant_role`, text, optional): Enter your role as a participant.
* **Participant Department** (`participant_department`, text, optional): Enter the department you are from.
* **Participant Location** (`participant_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your company.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
