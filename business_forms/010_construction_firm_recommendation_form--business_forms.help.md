# Construction Firm Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Firm Recommendation Form is a tool for clients to provide feedback and recommendations on the performance of a construction firm. This form helps the firm to identify areas of improvement and provides valuable insights for future projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct project and client information in the **Client and Project Identification** section.
2. Enter your name and job title/position in the **Signer Name** field.
3. Provide feedback in the **Quality of Workmanship**, **Adherence to Schedule**, **Budget and Financial Management**, and **Communication and Responsiveness** sections, using the provided rating scales.
4. Enter your **Would you recommend this firm to others?** choice, as well as your official **Recommendation or Testimonial Statement**.
5. If applicable, mention **Areas where the firm could improve** in the **Improvement Suggestion** field.
6. Grant permission to use your testimonial for marketing purposes in the **Consent to use this testimonial for marketing** field.
7. Finally, indicate whether you would be willing to act as a reference for the firm in the future in the **Consent to use as a reference** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name or Organization**: Enter the name of the client or organization you worked with.
- **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person you worked with from the firm.
- **Name of Completed Project**: Enter the name of the project you're providing feedback on.
- **Project Completion Date**: Enter the date when the project was completed.
- **Quality of Workmanship (1-5 Stars)**: Rate the firm's overall quality of workmanship, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
- **Adherence to Schedule**: Select whether the firm was **Ahead of Schedule**, **On Time**, **Slightly Delayed**, or **Significantly Delayed**.
- **Budget and Financial Management**: Select whether the firm was **Under Budget**, **Exactly on Budget**, **Minor Cost Overruns**, or **Major Cost Overruns**.
- **Communication and Responsiveness**: Select whether the firm's communication was **Excellent**, **Good**, **Fair**, or **Poor**.
- **Professionalism of Staff and Subcontractors**: Select whether the firm's staff and subcontractors were **Extremely Professional**, **Professional**, **Neutral**, or **Unprofessional**.
- **Would you recommend this firm to others?**: Choose whether you would recommend the firm **Yes - Without Hesitation**, **Yes - With Conditions**, or **No**.
- **What was the firm's primary strength during this project?**: Enter a brief description of what you felt was the firm's strongest point.
- **Areas where the firm could improve**: Enter any areas where you think the firm could improve.
- **Official Recommendation or Testimonial Statement**: Enter a brief testimonial statement about your experience with the firm.
- **Consent to use this testimonial for marketing**: Indicate whether you agree to let the firm use your testimonial for marketing purposes.
- **Consent to use as a reference**: Indicate whether you would be willing to be used as a reference for the firm in the future.
- **Title of Person Completing This Form**: Enter your job title or position.
- **Date of Recommendation**: Enter the date when you're completing this form.

Note: The form is designed to be completed online, and the required fields are marked with an asterisk (*) in the form's UI. Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
