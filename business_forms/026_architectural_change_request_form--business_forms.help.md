# Architectural Change Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Architectural Change Request Form is a tool used to submit a request for architectural changes to a property. This form is essential for homeowners, residents, or property owners to inform the relevant authorities about planned changes to a property's structure, design, or other physical attributes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your name and email.
2. Provide your phone number for easy contact.
3. Enter your resident's or property owner's name (if different from yours).
4. Enter the property address where the change is requested.
5. If applicable, enter the unit or lot number.
6. Select the type of change request (e.g., Addition, Alteration, etc.).
7. Describe the change or modification in detail.
8. Explain the reason behind the change request.
9. If applicable, include design or sketch attachments.
10. Describe the materials and colors proposed for the change.
11. Requested completion date for the change.
12. Estimate the timeline for the change.
13. If relevant, indicate whether the change will impact neighboring properties.
14. Explain how the change will impact neighboring properties.
15. Enter the date of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident or Homeowner Name** (id: 2, text, required): Enter your full name as a resident or homeowner of the property.
* **Email Address** (id: 3, email, required): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (id: 4, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Property Address** (id: 6, text, required): Enter the property address where the change is requested.
* **Unit or Lot Number** (id: 7, text, optional): If applicable, enter the unit or lot number of the property.
* **Property Owner Name** (id: 8, text, optional): If different from the resident, enter the property owner's name.
* **Type of Change Requested** (id: 10, select_one, required): Choose the type of change requested (e.g., Addition, Alteration, etc.).
* **Detailed Description of Changes** (id: 11, text, required): Describe the change or modification in detail.
* **Reason for Change** (id: 12, text, optional): Explain the reason behind the change request.
* **Architect or Designer Name** (id: 14, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the architect or designer.
* **Design or Sketch Attached** (id: 15, select_one, optional): Indicate if a design or sketch attachment is included.
* **Materials and Colors Proposed** (id: 16, text, optional): Describe the proposed materials and colors for the change.
* **Requested Completion Date** (id: 18, date, required): Enter the requested completion date for the change.
* **Timeline** (id: 17, note, optional): Estimate the timeline for the change.
* **Does this change impact neighboring properties?** (id: 20, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the change will impact neighboring properties.
* **How will it impact neighbors?** (id: 21, text, optional): Explain how the change will impact neighboring properties.
* **Date of Request** (id: 22, date, required): Enter the date of your request.

Note: Some fields are marked as optional, but it is highly recommended to fill them in for better and more accurate information.
