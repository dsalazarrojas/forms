# Laboratory Space Lease Letter Of Intent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and manage laboratory space lease letters of intent, which outlines the terms of a lease agreement between a laboratory and its tenant or landlord.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the start date of the lease term.
2.  Enter the end date of the lease term.
3.  Specify the duration of the lease term.
4.  Enter the name of the tenant or user.
5.  Enter the email address of the tenant or user.
6.  Enter the phone number of the contact person.
7.  Enter the name of the landlord or property owner.
8.  Enter the phone number of the landlord or property owner.
9.  Enter the email address of the landlord or property owner.
10.  Select the status of the lease term (active or inactive).
11.  Enter the name of the contact person.
12.  Enter the contact phone number.
13.  Enter the email address of the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the start date of the lease term?** (`lease_term_start_date`, `date`, required): The start date of the lease term. Enter the date when the lease begins.
* **What is the end date of the lease term?** (`lease_term_end_date`, `date`, required): The end date of the lease term. Enter the date when the lease ends.
* **How long is the lease term?** (`lease_term_duration`, `number`, required): The duration of the lease term in months. Enter the number of months the lease is active.
* **What is the name of the tenant?** (`tenant_name`, `text`, required): The name of the tenant or user renting the space.
* **What is the email address of the tenant?** (`tenant_email`, `email`, required): The email address of the tenant or user.
* **What is the phone number of the contact person?** (`tenant_phone`, `text`, required): The phone number of the contact person or tenant representative.
* **What is the name of the landlord?** (`landlord_name`, `text`, required): The name of the landlord or property owner.
* **What is the phone number of the landlord?** (`landlord_phone`, `text`, required): The phone number of the landlord or property owner.
* **What is the email address of the landlord?** (`landlord_email`, `email`, required): The email address of the landlord or property owner.
* **What is the current status of the lease term (active or inactive)?** (`lease_term_status`, `select_one`, required): The status of the lease term (active or inactive).
* **What is the name of the contact person?** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): The name of the contact person or representative.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): The phone number of the contact person or representative.
* **What is the email address of the contact person?** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): The email address of the contact person or representative.

Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
