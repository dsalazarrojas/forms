# Fire Safety Equipment Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow employees to report and claim any damage or issues with fire safety equipment. It's essential to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the necessary actions are taken to rectify the situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, enter your input in the provided field. This is the User Input section, where you can provide any additional information about the claim.
2. On the second page, describe the damage or issue with the fire safety equipment in the Damage Claim field.
3. On the third page, select the current status of your claim from the Claim Status dropdown menu.
4. On the fourth page, select any relevant Supporting Documents you would like to attach to your claim. You can choose multiple options here, as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant information about the claim in this field.
* **Damage Claim** (`damage_claim`, text, required: false): Describe the damage or issue with the fire safety equipment in this field. Be as detailed as possible to ensure that the maintenance team can understand the problem.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the current status of your claim. Options are In Progress, Completed, and Cancelled.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_document_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose any relevant supporting documents you would like to attach to your claim. Options are Photos, Documents, and Video.
