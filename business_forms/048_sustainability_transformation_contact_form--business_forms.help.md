# Sustainability Transformation Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and project details for sustainability transformation projects. It helps in gathering essential data for project management, consulting, or other related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user input and contact details in the "User Input" section.
2. Provide your organization's details in the "Organization Details" section.
3. Describe your project in the "Project Description" section.
4. Specify the project location in the "Project Location" section.
5. Enter your contact information in the "Project Contact" section.
6. Select the lead source in the "Lead Source" section.
7. Choose follow-up methods in the "Follow up" section.
8. Update the lead status in the "Lead Status" section.
9. Set a follow-up time in the "Follow up time" section (optional).
10. Assign the lead to a user in the "Assigned to" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required: false): This is where you can enter any relevant information about yourself as a user.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Provide your contact details such as name, email, phone number, etc.
* **Organization Details** (`organization_details`, text, required: false): Enter your organization's name, title, phone number, and email.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the project and its goals.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, required: false): Specify the location of the project.
* **Project Contact** (`project_contact`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information for the project.
* **Project Phone** (`project_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the project phone number.
* **Project Email** (`project_email`, text, required: false): Enter the project email address.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the consultant's name.
* **Consultant Title** (`consultant_title`, text, required: false): Enter the consultant's title.
* **Consultant Phone** (`consultant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the consultant's phone number.
* **Consultant Email** (`consultant_email`, text, required: false): Enter the consultant's email address.
* **Consultant Organization** (`consultant_organization`, text, required: false): Enter the consultant's organization.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required: false): Enter the organization's name.
* **Organization Title** (`organization_title`, text, required: false): Enter the organization's title.
* **Organization Phone** (`organization_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the organization's phone number.
* **Organization Email** (`organization_email`, text, required: false): Enter the organization's email address.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Choose a date for the project.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Choose a time for the project.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, select_one, required: false): Select the source of the lead.
* **Follow up** (`follow_up`, select_multiple, required: false): Select methods to follow up with the lead.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the lead.
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, select_one, required: false): Update the status of the lead.
* **Follow up time** (`follow_up_time`, time, required: false): Set a follow-up time for the lead.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required: false): Assign the lead to a user.
