# Factory Planning Consultant Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to contact the factory planning consultant with their personal details for potential consultation or collaboration opportunities. The purpose of this form is to facilitate communication and provide a means for users to share their contact information with the factory planning consultant. Please ensure you fill out this form correctly to help the consultant reach you efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each field with your relevant personal and company details. Ensure you provide accurate information, and don't hesitate to add any additional comments or attachments if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1, text, required: false): This field is used to enter your given name.
  - Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed in communication with the factory planning consultant.
- **Last Name** (2, text, required: false): This field is used to enter your surname.
  - Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed in communication with the factory planning consultant.
- **Email** (3, email, required: false): This field is used to enter your email address.
  - Enter a valid email address that you use to receive messages from the factory planning consultant.
- **Phone** (4, text, required: false): This field is used to enter your contact phone number.
  - Enter your phone number that the factory planning consultant can use to contact you.
- **Company** (5, text, required: false): This field is used to enter your company name.
  - Enter the name of your company or organization as you would like it to be displayed in communication with the factory planning consultant.
- **Job Title** (6, text, required: false): This field is used to enter your job title.
  - Enter your job title or position within your company to help the factory planning consultant understand your role.

## Tips
- Double-check your entered information before submitting the form.
- Use a valid email address that you check regularly.
- Ensure your phone number is correctly formatted.
- Attach any relevant documents or files to support your consultation request.
