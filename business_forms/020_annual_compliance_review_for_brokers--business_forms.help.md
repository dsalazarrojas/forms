# Annual Compliance Review For Brokers - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from brokers on their compliance status and ensure they are meeting all regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
- Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Broker Name (Full Name) (<name>)
The broker's full name.

* **Employee ID (<name>)**: The broker's employee identification number.

* **Email Address (<name>)**: The broker's work email address.

* **Phone Number (<name>)**: The broker's best contact number.

* **Branch Office (<name>)**: The location of the primary office.

* **CRD Number (<name>)**: The Central Registration Depository number.

* **Review Year (<name>)**: Select the year this review is for.

* **Registration Status (<name>)**: Current registration status.

* **Licenses Held (<name>)**: Select all licenses held by the broker.

* **Continuing Education Completed (<name>)**: Have you completed all required continuing education?

* **Regulatory Disclosures (<name>)**: Has the broker had any regulatory actions or disclosures?

* **Disclosure Details (<name>)**: Provide details if regulatory actions or disclosures are yes.

* **Customer Complaints (<name>)**: Has the broker had any customer complaints this year?

* **Complaint Details (<name>)**: Provide details if customer complaints are yes.

* **Outside Business Activities (<name>)**: Does the broker have any outside business activities?

* **Outside Activity Details (<name>)**: Describe outside activities if they are yes.

* **Financial Disclosures (<name>)**: Has the broker had any financial disclosures or liens?

* **Financial Details (<name>)**: Provide details if financial disclosures or liens are yes.

* **Criminal Disclosures (<name>)**: Has the broker had any criminal charges or convictions?

* **Criminal Details (<name>)**: Provide details if criminal charges or convictions are yes.

* **Compliance Training Completed (<name>)**: Has the broker completed all required compliance training?

* **Training Topics Completed (<name>)**: Select all compliance training completed.

* **Policy Acknowledgment (<name>)**: Has the broker reviewed all firm policies?

* **Attestation (<name>)**: I certify the information is accurate.

* **Signature Date (<name>)**: Date of attestation.

* **Supervisor Review Status (<name>)**: Has the supervisor reviewed this form?

* **Additional Comments (<name>)**: Any other relevant information.
