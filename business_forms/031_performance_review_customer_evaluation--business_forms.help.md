# performance_review_customer_evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers on their experience with their representative, allowing for performance evaluation and improvement opportunities. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your honest feedback on your experience with the representative. 
2. Complete each field, providing detailed information where requested.
3. If a field is not relevant, you can skip it.
4. Double-check your answers before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Evaluation**: Describe your experience with the representative. Provide any comments or feedback you have about their performance.
* **Experience with Representative**: Provide detailed feedback on your experience with the representative. Describe any positive or negative interactions you've had.
* **Customer Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
* **Communication**: Describe your experience with the representative's communication style and effectiveness.
* **Professionalism**: Provide feedback on the representative's level of professionalism, including punctuality, courtesy, and respect for your time.
* **Customer Service**: Describe your experience with the representative's customer service skills, including their response time, accuracy, and courtesy.
* **Timeliness**: Rate the representative's timeliness in meeting deadlines and providing updates on your case.
* **Organization**: Check if you believe the representative's work was organized and well-structured.
* **Employee**: Rate whether the representative was an employee or not.
