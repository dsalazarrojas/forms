# weekly_schedule_and_bonus_options - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and bonus preferences from employees or users for administrative and accounting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact details fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Indicate your availability and any relevant details that you would like to declare.
3. If desired, provide any bonus preferences, such as bonus frequencies or types.
4. If you have any additional comments or notes, use the note field to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, required): Fill out this field with your accurate and up-to-date contact information, including your name, email, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, text, required): Indicate your availability and any relevant details that you would like to declare.
* **Bonus Preferences** (`Bonus Preferences`, text, optional): If you have any bonus preferences, such as bonus frequencies or types, use this field to declare them.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or notes that you would like to include.
