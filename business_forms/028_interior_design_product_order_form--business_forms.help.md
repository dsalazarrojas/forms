# interior_design_product_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for interior design product orders. It will help you place orders for custom furniture or design services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email**.
2. Provide your **Phone Number**.
3. Select the **Order Date** and **Order Time** you would like to place the order.
4. Choose the interior design **Product List** you wish to order. You can select multiple products using the dropdown menu.
5. Add any additional **Notes** about your order, if needed.
6. Select your preferred **Payment Method** and enter the **Payment Amount**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to place the order.
- **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required): Select the time you would like to place the order.
- **Product List** (`product_list`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more products from the dropdown menu.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order, if needed.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
- **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount for your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure the form is complete and valid.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any issues with your order, please contact our support team.
