# custom_furniture_order_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The custom_furniture_order_intake_form is designed to collect information from customers to create custom furniture orders. It captures essential details such as customer contact information, order specifics, and custom preferences to ensure accurate and timely delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Customer Information** section with your basic contact details.
2. Select your **Contact Preferences** from the drop-down menu.
3. Input specific **Order Details** about the item you are ordering.
4. If applicable, provide **Custom Furniture Details** to specify your custom order.
5. If required, fill out the **Shipping and Delivery** details.
6. If necessary, input **Payment Terms** for your order.
7. Review and confirm your **Submission Confirmation** to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Input your name, company name, and any other identifying information.
* **Contact Preferences** (`Contact Preferences`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or other).
* **Order Details** (`Order Details`, `text`, required): Enter the specific details about the item you are ordering, such as type of furniture, quantity, and color.
* **Custom Furniture Details** (`Custom Furniture Details`, `text`, optional): Add any additional details about your custom order, if applicable.
* **Shipping and Delivery** (`Shipping and Delivery`, `text`, optional): Specify any specific shipping or delivery instructions.
* **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, `text`, optional): Input any specific payment terms or instructions.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`Submission Confirmation`, `text`, required): Confirm that you have submitted your order details correctly.
