The "Account Modification Request Form" is intended to collect information from users who need to make modifications to their account. This form is used by users to request updates to their personal information, password, contact information, permissions, account type, payment method, or other changes as needed. To ensure that the form is filled out correctly, users should only fill out one form per modification request. Users with existing requests are advised to review their previous submissions before creating new ones.

**Purpose**
The "Account Modification Request Form" is used to collect information from users who want to make modifications to their account.

**How To Complete This Form**

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information for each field.
2. Review your submission before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Attach any required supporting documentation or evidence as needed.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Your full name.
* **Requester Email** (`requester_email`, email, required): Your email address.
* **Requester Phone** (`requester_phone`, text, required): Your phone number.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, required): The number of your account.
* **Current Account Holder Name** (`current_account_holder_name`, text, required): The name currently on the account.
* **Modification Type** (`modification_type`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to specify the type of modification.
* **Specific Changes Requested** (`specific_changes_requested`, text, required): Describe the specific changes you want to make.
* **Reason for Modification** (`reason_for_modification`, text, required): Explain why you need to make these changes.
* **Current Password** (`current_password`, text, optional): For security verification, your current account password.
* **New Information** (`new_information`, text, required): Enter the new information you want to update with.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, required): Specify when you want these changes to take effect.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Select the urgency level of this modification.
* **Business Impact** (`business_impact`, text, required): Describe the business impact of this modification.
* **Authorization Required** (`authorization_required`, select_one, required): Check if additional authorization is needed.
* **Secondary Contact Name** (`secondary_contact_name`, text, optional): Name of secondary contact for verification.
* **Secondary Contact Phone** (`secondary_contact_phone`, text, optional): Phone number of secondary contact.
* **Security Questions Answers** (`security_questions_answers`, text, optional): Answer to your security questions for verification.
* **Compliance Requirements** (`compliance_requirements`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable compliance requirements.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other information about your modification request.
* **Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature`, text, required): Type your full name to sign this modification request.

**Tips**

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Review your submission for completeness and correctness before submitting.
* Ensure you attach all required supporting documentation.
* If you're unsure about any field, reach out to your point of contact for clarification.
