# Supplier Quotation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from suppliers about their quotation requests. It's intended for internal use within the organization, for authorized personnel to review and manage quotation requests from suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your supplier's name in the "Supplier Name" field.
2. Provide your contact number and email address in the "Contact Number" and "Email" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the date you would like to receive the quotation in the "Quotation Request Date" field.
4. Provide any additional notes about the quotation request in the "Supplier Notes" field.
5. Select the items you would like to include in the quotation request in the "Requested Items" field.
6. Enter the quantity you would like to order for each item in the "Requested Quantity" field.
7. Enter your supplier's price for each item in the "Supplier Price" field.
8. Calculate the total price for all items by multiplying the "Supplier Price" by the "Requested Quantity" for each item and summing the results.
9. Enter the total price for all items in the "Total Price" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name (Text)**: Enter your company's or personal name as the supplier.
* **Contact Number (Text)**: Enter your contact number that we can use to contact you.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address that we will use to communicate with you.
* **Quotation Request Date (Date)**: Enter the date you would like to receive the quotation.
* **Supplier Notes (Note)**: Enter any additional notes about the quotation request.
* **Requested Items (Select Multiple)**: Select the items you would like to include in the quotation request.
* **Requested Quantity (Number)**: Enter the quantity you would like to order for each item.
* **Supplier Price (Number)**: Enter the supplier's price for each item.
* **Total Price (Number)**: Calculate the total price for all items by multiplying the "Supplier Price" by the "Requested Quantity" for each item and summing the results.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us.
* If you've already submitted a similar quotation request, please do not submit a new one.
