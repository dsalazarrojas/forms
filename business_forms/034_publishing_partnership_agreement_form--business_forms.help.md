# Publishing Partnership Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Publishing Partnership Agreement Form is designed to create a partnership agreement between a publisher and an author. This form is a crucial document that outlines the terms and conditions of the partnership, ensuring that both parties are aware of their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Select the correct options for each field, as indicated below.
3. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title**: This field is not applicable as it is a metadata field.
- **Author Information (2)**: Select 'Yes' if the author has provided their information, and 'No' if they have not.
- **Publisher Information (3)**: Select 'Yes' if the publisher has provided their information, and 'No' if they have not.
- **Terms and Conditions (4)**: Provide a brief description of the terms and conditions of the partnership.
- **Contact Information (5)**: Select 'Yes' if the publisher has provided their contact information, and 'No' if they have not.
- **Agreement Terms 1-20 (6-25)**: Provide the detailed terms and conditions of the partnership.
- **Submit (26)**: Click the submit button to save your changes.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*). Ensure that you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
