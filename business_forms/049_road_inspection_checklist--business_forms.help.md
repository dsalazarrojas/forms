# Road Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Road Inspection Checklist is used by road inspectors to document the results of their road inspections. This form helps them collect and record necessary information to assess the condition of the roads inspected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the inspector's details: provide your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the date and time of the inspection.
3. Review each page of the road inspection checklist, which will guide you through the inspection process.
4. Answer the questions and check the relevant boxes on each page.
5. Once you have completed all the pages, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspector Name** (text): Enter your name as the inspector performing the road inspection.
* **Inspector Email** (email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Inspector Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date and Time** (date): Select the date and time of the road inspection.
* **Page 2-18** (text): Each page is a checklist for specific aspects of the road inspection. Follow the instructions on each page to answer the questions and check the relevant boxes.
* **Weather Conditions** (text): Record the weather conditions at the time of the inspection, if applicable.
* **Road Surface** (text): Describe the condition of the road surface.
* **Road Geometry** (text): Describe the geometry of the road, including its shape and alignment.
* **Page 19-26** (text): Continue with the remaining pages of the road inspection checklist, following the same instructions as above.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete each page thoroughly and accurately.
* Use your discretion when selecting the weather conditions and describing the road conditions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or the road maintenance department.
