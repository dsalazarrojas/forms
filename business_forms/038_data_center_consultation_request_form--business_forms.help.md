## Data Center Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for users to submit a consultation request for data center-related services, such as design, installation, or management. We will use the information provided to assess your needs and respond accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and contact information in the "Contact Person" field.
2.  Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
3.  Specify the location of the data center in question in the "Data Center Location" field.
4.  Select the services you require from the list in the "Services Required" field.
5.  Choose a preferred date and time of day for the consultation in the "Preferred Date" and "Time of Day" fields.
6.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7.  Optionally, add any additional comments or instructions in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person (Contact Person, Text, Optional):** Enter the name and title of the person submitting the request.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details, Text, Optional):** Provide a contact number and any other relevant contact information for the person submitting the request.
* **Data Center Location (Data Center Location, Text, Optional):** Specify the address or location of the data center.
* **Services Required (Services Required, Select Multiple, Optional):** Select one or more services you require from the list.
	+ Data Center Design
	+ Data Center Installation
	+ Data Center Management
* **Preferred Date (Preferred Date, Date, Optional):** Choose a date for the consultation.
* **Time of Day (Time of Day, Time, Optional):** Choose a time of day for the consultation.
* **Email (Email, Email, Optional):** Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Phone, Text, Optional):** Enter a phone number for contact.
* **Note (Note, Note, Optional):** Add any additional comments or instructions about the request.
* **Submission (Submission, Text, Optional):** This is not a field for users to fill in. It's a placeholder for the form submission.
