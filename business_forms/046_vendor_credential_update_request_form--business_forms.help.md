# Vendor Credential Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update vendor credentials, such as certifications, licenses, and permits, for your organization's vendors. It helps to keep vendor information up-to-date and compliant with relevant regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Vendor ID** associated with the vendor you want to update.
2. Enter the **Vendor Name**.
3. Select the type of **Credential Type** you are updating (Certs, Licenses, or Permits).
4. Provide the **Document Type** and **Document Number** for the updated credential.
5. Enter the **Update Date** of the updated credential.
6. (Optional) Attach a **Supporting Document** to verify the update.
7. Enter any additional **Notes** about the update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor ID**: Enter the ID associated with the vendor you want to update. This is a required field.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor you want to update. This is a required field.
* **Credential Type**: Select the type of credential you are updating (Certs, Licenses, or Permits). This field is required.
* **Document Type**: Enter the type of document associated with the updated credential (e.g., certification, license, permit).
* **Document Number**: Enter the number of the document associated with the updated credential.
* **Update Date**: Enter the date the credential was updated.
* **Supporting Document**: (Optional) Attach a document that supports the update, such as a scanned copy of the updated credential.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about the update.
