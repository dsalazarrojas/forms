# Home Insurance Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect home insurance information, including address, coverage provider, policy start and end dates, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your address information in the **Street Address** field, including your house number and street name.
2. In the **City** field, enter the name of the city where your home is located.
3. Enter the state or province where your home is located in the **State** field.
4. Provide the zip code of your home in the **Zip** field.
5. Enter the estimated value of your home in the **Home Value** field.
6. Enter the year your home was built in the **Year Built** field.
7. Enter the square footage of your home in the **Square Footage** field.
8. Enter the number of bedrooms in your home in the **Number of Bedrooms** field.
9. Enter the number of bathrooms in your home in the **Number of Bathrooms** field.
10. Select the type of property you have from the options provided in the **Property Type** field.
11. Select the provider of your home insurance from the options provided in the **Home Insurance Provider** field.
12. Enter the start date of your home insurance policy in the **Policy Start Date** field.
13. Enter the end date of your home insurance policy in the **Policy End Date** field.
14. Select the status of your policy from the options provided in the **Policy Status** field.
15. If applicable, enter the name of your insurance agent in the **Agent Name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Street Address** (id: 1, text, required: false): Enter your house number and street name.
* **City** (id: 2, text, required: false): Enter the name of the city where your home is located.
* **State** (id: 3, text, required: false): Enter the state or province where your home is located.
* **Zip** (id: 4, text, required: false): Enter the zip code of your home.
* **Home Value** (id: 5, number, required: false): Enter the estimated value of your home.
* **Year Built** (id: 6, number, required: false): Enter the year your home was built.
* **Square Footage** (id: 7, number, required: false): Enter the square footage of your home.
* **Number of Bedrooms** (id: 8, number, required: false): Enter the number of bedrooms in your home.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (id: 9, number, required: false): Enter the number of bathrooms in your home.
* **Property Type** (id: 10, select_one, required: false): Select the type of property you have (Single Family, Multi Family, Condo).
* **Home Insurance Provider** (id: 11, select_multiple, required: false): Select the provider of your home insurance (State Farm, Allstate, Geico).
* **Policy Start Date** (id: 12, date, required: false): Enter the start date of your home insurance policy.
* **Policy End Date** (id: 13, date, required: false): Enter the end date of your home insurance policy.
* **Policy Status** (id: 14, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your policy (Active, Cancelled, Inactive).
* **Agent Name** (id: 15, text, required: false): If applicable, enter the name of your insurance agent.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for help or clarification.
* This form should only be used for official purposes and by authorized personnel.
