# venture-capital-analyst-offboarding-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and assign tasks for the offboarding process of analysts who are leaving the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are filling out this form for an analyst who is leaving the company.
2. Enter the analyst's exit date.
3. Provide any feedback you have for the offboarder (the person offboarding the analyst).
4. Enter any comments related to the offboarding process.
5. Enter the analyst's email address.
6. Enter the analyst's phone number.
7. Select the category of the venture capital analyst (e.g. Venture Capital, Private Equity, Real Estate, or Other).
8. If you select "Other" for the category, provide a description of the other category.
9. Select the team members who will be assigned to the offboarding process.
10. Select the tools that will be assigned to the offboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: `venture-capital-analyst-offboarding-form` - This is the title of the form, which is displayed at the top.
* **Exit Date**: This is the date when the analyst will be leaving the company. (Type: date)
* **Feedback**: This is a field for you to provide any feedback you have for the offboarder. (Type: text)
* **Comments**: This is a field for you to provide any comments related to the offboarding process. (Type: text)
* **Email**: This is the analyst's email address. (Type: email)
* **Phone**: This is the analyst's phone number. (Type: text)
* **Category**: This is the category of the venture capital analyst (e.g. Venture Capital, Private Equity, Real Estate, or Other). (Type: select_one)
*   **Other**: If you select "Other" for the category, provide a description of the other category. (Type: text)
* **Assigned To**: This is the team members who will be assigned to the offboarding process. (Type: select_multiple)
*   **Other**: If you select "Other" for the assigned to field, please provide the name of the team member. (Type: text)
* **Assigned Tool**: This is the tools that will be assigned to the offboarding process. (Type: select_multiple)
*   **Other**: If you select "Other" for the assigned tool field, please provide the name of the tool. (Type: text)

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that the offboarding process is completed successfully.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, please ask the offboarder for assistance.
* This form is only for analysts who are leaving the company, and not for other types of employees.
