# Lead Generation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Generation Survey is a form used to collect data on lead generation metrics and gather feedback on the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer each one accurately to the best of your knowledge.
2. Enter your responses in the fields provided, using the options available for select fields.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Generation** (`lead_generation_survey_lead_generation`): This field is used to track the number of leads generated.
* **Leads In Progress** (`lead_generation_survey_leads_in_progress`): This field is used to track the number of leads that are currently in progress.
* **Opportunities In Pipeline** (`lead_generation_survey_opportunities_in_pipeline`): This field is used to track the number of opportunities that are currently in the pipeline.
* **Opportunities Converted** (`lead_generation_survey_opportunities_converted`): This field is used to track the number of opportunities that have been converted.
* **Opportunities Lost** (`lead_generation_survey_opportunities_lost`): This field is used to track the number of opportunities that were lost.
* **Time Spent On Leads** (`lead_generation_survey_time_spent_on_leads`): This field is used to track the amount of time spent on leads.
* **Leads Source** (`lead_generation_survey_leads_source`): This field is used to select the source of the lead, such as Social Media, Email Marketing, Referral, or Other.
* **Lead Generation Comments** (`lead_generation_survey_lead_generation_comments`): This field is used to provide any additional comments or feedback on the lead generation process.
* **Lead Generation Rating** (`lead_generation_survey_lead_generation_rating`): This field is used to rate the quality of the lead generation process, with options Very Good, Good, Average, or Poor.
* **Lead Generation Comment** (`lead_generation_survey_lead_generation_comment`): This field is used to provide any additional comments on the lead generation process.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all questions accurately and honestly to provide valuable feedback.
* If you are unsure of an answer, please select "Other" for the Leads Source field and provide a brief explanation in the Lead Generation Comments field.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the Lead Generation Comments field.
