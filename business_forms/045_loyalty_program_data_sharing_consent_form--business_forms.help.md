# Loyalty Program Data Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain your consent for sharing your loyalty program data with our partners and affiliates. This information will help us better understand your preferences and tailor our programs to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and implications of sharing your data.
2. Select "1" on the "i_accept_to_sharing_my_data" field to indicate your consent.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Click the "Submit" button to save your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **i_accept_to_sharing_my_data** (number, required): Select "1" to indicate that you consent to sharing your loyalty program data with our partners and affiliates.
* **Email (Email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about our loyalty programs and updates.
* **Phone (Text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your loyalty program participation and offers.

## Tips
Please note that your email and phone number will only be used for loyalty program related communications. You can unsubscribe from these communications at any time by contacting us directly.
