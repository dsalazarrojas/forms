# Engineering Drawing Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect details from users who need to request an engineering drawing. It helps to gather necessary information from the user for efficient processing and response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name as the Contractor.
2. Provide your address as the Contractor.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contractor** (<type:text>): Enter your name as the contractor submitting the request.
- **Contractor** (<type:text>): Enter your address as the contractor submitting the request.
- **Submit** (<type:text>): Click this button to submit your request.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill in both "Contractor" fields correctly with accurate information.
- Double-check your details before submitting your request.
- If you have any questions or clarifications, contact us at form@gic.mx.
