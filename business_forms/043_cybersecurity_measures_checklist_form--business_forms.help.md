# Cybersecurity Measures Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help organizations assess and document their current cybersecurity measures. It covers various aspects of cybersecurity, including risk assessment, security controls, incident response, security standards, training programs, monitoring, and backup practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review each page and fill out the fields as instructed.
2. Be honest and thorough in your answers.
3. Use the options provided to choose the most relevant answers for your organization.
4. If an option is not available, select "N/A" or add a custom answer in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1):
	+ This page is an introduction to the form and is not a required field.
	+ It's a good idea to review the form's purpose and instructions before proceeding.
* **Risk Assessment** (Page 2):
	+ This field is for performing a comprehensive assessment of potential security risks.
	+ Please provide a detailed description of the risks you've identified.
* **Security Controls** (Page 3):
	+ This field is for selecting the security controls your organization has implemented.
	+ Choose all the controls that apply to your organization.
	+ Examples: Firewalls, Encryption, Access Controls.
* **Incident Response** (Page 4):
	+ This field is for selecting the incident response procedures your organization follows.
	+ Choose one or more of the following:
		- Investigate
		- Contain
		- Eradicate
		- Recover
* **Security Standards** (Page 5):
	+ This field is for selecting the security standards your organization follows.
	+ Choose all that apply to your organization.
	+ Examples: ISO 27001, NIST, COBIT.
* **Training Program** (Page 6):
	+ This field is for selecting relevant training courses for employees.
	+ Choose all the courses that apply to your organization.
	+ Examples: Cybersecurity Fundamentals, Phishing and Social Engineering, Incident Response.
* **Monitoring** (Page 7):
	+ This field is for selecting the types of monitoring your organization performs.
	+ Choose all the monitoring types that apply to your organization.
	+ Examples: Network Monitoring, System Monitoring, User Activity Monitoring.
* **Backup** (Page 8):
	+ This field is for selecting the types of backup your organization performs.
	+ Choose all the backup types that apply to your organization.
	+ Examples: Local Backup, Cloud Backup, External Backup.

## Tips
* Be thorough and honest in your answers.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure your organization's cybersecurity posture remains strong.
* Consult with your IT team or cybersecurity expert if you're unsure about any field.
* Store the completed form as a reference document for future reviews and updates.
