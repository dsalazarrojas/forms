# Franchise Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Franchise Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect feedback from franchisees about their experience with the franchisor. It helps in identifying areas of improvement and areas of success, allowing us to better serve our franchisees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Read the survey questions carefully and answer them truthfully.
2. Provide your feedback in the required fields.
3. Select the most accurate response for each multiple-choice question.
4. Enter your email address to receive a copy of the survey response.
5. Enter your phone number (optional) to receive a phone follow-up call.
6. Provide any additional comments you have about your experience with the franchisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, `note`, not required): This section is for general comments or any specific questions about your experience with the franchisor. Please provide your honest feedback.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the franchisor's service, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Franchise** (`franchise`, `text`, not required): If you have any specific concerns or issues with the franchise, please mention them here.
* **Franchisee Name** (`franchisee_name`, `text`, not required): If you'd like to provide your name or contact information, please do so here.
* **Communication with Franchisor** (`communication_with_franchisor`, `select_multiple`, not required): How frequently do you communicate with the franchisor? Select 'Yes' if you communicate regularly, 'No' otherwise.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, `select_one`, not required): How effective do you think your communication with the franchisor is? Select 'Yes' if you find it effective, 'No' otherwise.
* **Responsiveness** (`responsiveness`, `number`, not required): Rate the franchisor's responsiveness to your needs or concerns, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Support** (`support`, `select_one`, not required): How would you rate the franchisor's support, on a scale of 1-5? Select 'Yes' if you receive adequate support, 'No' otherwise.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, not required): Rate your overall experience with the franchisor, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the franchisor.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address to receive a copy of the survey response and to receive any communication from the franchisor.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number to receive a phone follow-up call from the franchisor.
* **Email Sent** (`email_sent`, `text`, not required): This field is for internal use by the franchisor. Please do not fill it out.
* **Email Received** (`email_received`, `text`, not required): This field is for internal use by the franchisor. Please do not fill it out.
* **Phone Sent** (`phone_sent`, `text`, not required): This field is for internal use by the franchisor. Please do not fill it out.
* **Phone Received** (`phone_received`, `text`, not required): This field is for internal use by the franchisor. Please do not fill it out.
* **Follow-up Sent** (`follow_up_sent`, `text`, not required): This field is for internal use by the franchisor. Please do not fill it out.
* **Follow-up Received** (`follow_up_received`, `text`, not required): This field is for internal use by the franchisor. Please do not fill it out.
* **Communication with Franchiseor** (`communication_with_franchiseor`, `select_multiple`, not required): How frequently do you communicate with the franchiseor? Select 'Yes' if you communicate regularly, 'No' otherwise.
* **Communication Effectiveness Franchiseor** (`communication_effectiveness_franchiseor`, `select_one`, not required): How effective do you think your communication with the franchiseor is? Select 'Yes' if you find it effective, 'No' otherwise.
* **Responsiveness Franchiseor** (`responsiveness_franchiseor`, `number`, not required): Rate the franchiseor's responsiveness to your needs or concerns, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Support Franchiseor** (`support_franchiseor`, `select_one`, not required): How would you rate the franchiseor's support, on a scale of 1-5? Select 'Yes' if you receive adequate support, 'No' otherwise.
* **Overall Rating Franchiseor** (`overall_rating_franchiseor`, `number`, not required): Rate your overall experience with the franchiseor, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments Franchiseor** (`comments_franchiseor`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the franchiseor.
* **Email Franchiseor** (`email_franchiseor`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address to receive any communication from the franchiseor.
* **Phone Franchiseor** (`phone_franchiseor`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number to receive a phone follow-up call from the franchiseor.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your feedback is honest and constructive, as it will help us improve our service.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please provide them in the 'Comments' field.
* If you have any questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
