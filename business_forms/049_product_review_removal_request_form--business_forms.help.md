# Product Review Removal Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Product Review Removal Request Form is used to submit a request for the removal of a product review that is deemed inappropriate or false. This form helps authorized personnel identify and handle such issues in a structured and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have a legitimate reason for the removal of the review.
2.  Fill in the form with the relevant information.
3.  Select the correct review link from the provided options.
4.  Provide any additional evidence that supports your request.
5.  Submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Review Removal Request** (`review_removal_request`, text, optional): Briefly explain why the review should be removed.
*   **Reason for Removal** (`reason_for_removal`, text, optional): Provide a detailed explanation of the reason for requesting the review's removal.
*   **Link to Review** (`link_to_review`, select_one, required): Choose the platform where the review was published (Amazon, eBay, Facebook).
*   **Evidence** (`evidence`, text, optional): Attach any relevant evidence that supports your request.
*   **Review Link** (`review_link`, select_one, required): Choose the link to the review (Amazon, eBay, Facebook).
*   **Product Title** (`product_title`, text, optional): Provide the title of the product associated with the review.
*   **Product ID** (`product_id`, number, optional): Enter the product ID associated with the review.
*   **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the product.

Note: The fields marked as "required" must be filled in to submit the form, while the fields marked as "optional" can be left empty if not applicable.
