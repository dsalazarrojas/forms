# Business Process Compliance Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Process Compliance Contact Form is designed to collect information about business process compliance inquiries. This form helps to identify and address potential issues related to business process compliance, ensuring that the organization maintains a culture of compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Select the type of inquiry you want to make by choosing from the options provided in the "Type of Inquiry" field.
3. Provide your name, contact information, and department in the corresponding fields.
4. If applicable, select your job title and direct manager's name from the dropdown options.
5. Choose the policy area related to your inquiry from the options provided.
6. Describe your inquiry in the "Detailed Description" field.
7. If you have supporting documentation, indicate it by selecting "True" in the "Relevant Documentation Attached" field.
8. Select the level of urgency for your inquiry from the options provided.
9. If you have previously encountered similar issues, indicate it by selecting "True" in the "Previous Similar Issues" field.
10. Choose your preferred contact method from the options provided.
11. Provide any additional notes or information relevant to your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Date (Required)
* **Contact Date**: Date of this submission
* Type: Date
* Purpose: The date on which the form is submitted.

### Your Name (Required)
* **Your Name**: Full name
* Type: Text
* Purpose: Your name for contact purposes.

### Email Address (Required)
* **Email Address**: Contact email
* Type: Email
* Purpose: Your email address for contact purposes.

### Phone Number (Optional)
* **Phone Number**: Contact phone
* Type: Text
* Purpose: Your phone number for contact purposes. This field is optional.

### Department (Required)
* **Department**: Your department
* Type: Text
* Purpose: The department you belong to for context.

### Job Title (Optional)
* **Job Title**: Your position
* Type: Text
* Purpose: Your job title for context.

### Direct Manager (Optional)
* **Direct Manager**: Supervisor's name
* Type: Text
* Purpose: The name of your direct manager for context.

### Type of Inquiry (Required)
* **Type of Inquiry**: The type of inquiry you are making (e.g. policy clarification, compliance question, etc.)
* Type: Select One
* Purpose: To categorize the type of inquiry being made.

### Policy Area (Required)
* **Policy Area**: The specific policy area related to your inquiry
* Type: Select One
* Purpose: To identify the policy area related to your inquiry.

### Specific Policy or Process (Required)
* **Specific Policy or Process**: The name of the specific policy or process related to your inquiry
* Type: Text
* Purpose: To specify the policy or process being inquired about.

### Detailed Description (Required)
* **Detailed Description**: A description of your question or concern
* Type: Text
* Purpose: To provide context and description of your inquiry.

### Business Impact (Optional)
* **Business Impact**: How does this affect operations?
* Type: Text
* Purpose: To describe the potential business impact of your inquiry.

### Urgency Level (Required)
* **Urgency Level**: Select the level of urgency for your inquiry
* Type: Select One
* Purpose: To prioritize your inquiry based on its urgency.

### Relevant Documentation Attached (Optional)
* **Relevant Documentation Attached**: If you have supporting documentation, select "True"
* Type: Select One
* Purpose: To indicate if you have supporting documentation.

### Related Regulation or Standard (Optional)
* **Related Regulation or Standard**: Any regulatory reference relevant to your inquiry
* Type: Text
* Purpose: To provide regulatory references.

### Proposed Solution (Optional)
* **Proposed Solution**: Any suggested solution or action
* Type: Text
* Purpose: To provide a suggested solution or action.

### Previous Similar Issues (Optional)
* **Previous Similar Issues**: Has this happened before?
* Type: Select One
* Purpose: To indicate if similar issues have occurred before.

### Preferred Contact Method (Optional)
* **Preferred Contact Method**: How should we reach you?
* Type: Select One
* Purpose: To identify your preferred contact method.

### Additional Notes (Optional)
* **Additional Notes**: Any other information relevant to your inquiry
* Type: Text
* Purpose: To provide additional information or context.

**Tips**
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your inquiry is processed efficiently.
* If you have any supporting documentation, please attach it with your submission.
* If you have previously encountered similar issues, please select "True" in the "Previous Similar Issues" field.
