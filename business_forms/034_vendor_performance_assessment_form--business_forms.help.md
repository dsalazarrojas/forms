# vendor_performance_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vendor performance assessment form is a tool used to evaluate the performance of vendors in the organization, helping to make informed decisions about vendor relationships and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form and understand its purpose and fields.
2. Ensure you have a valid reason for completing this form and a basic understanding of the vendor's performance.
3. Fill out the form accurately and carefully, paying attention to each field's purpose and description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Performance Assessment Form**:
	+ `vendor_form` (Text Field): This field is where you can type a brief description of the vendor's performance, such as their strengths and weaknesses.
* **Performance Rating**:
	+ `performance_rating` (Number Field): Rate the vendor's performance on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Quality of Service**:
	+ `quality_of_service` (Text Field): Describe the vendor's service quality, including any notable achievements or areas for improvement.
* **Value for Money**:
	+ `value_for_money` (Number Field): Rate the vendor's value for money on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
