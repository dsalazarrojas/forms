# Intellectual Property Use Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intellectual Property Use Authorization Form is used to collect permission from users to use or share the company's intellectual property. This form ensures that individuals or organizations have the necessary authorization to use or share the company's proprietary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your User IP**: This field is not required and is not used as a validation check, so feel free to fill it in if you need to identify your user IP.
2. **Name**: Fill in your name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address.
4. **Phone**: Provide your phone number.
5. **Permission Type**: Select whether the usage is for **Public** or **Private** purposes.
6. **Permission Date**: Enter the date you received permission to use the intellectual property.
7. **Usage Term**: Briefly describe the term or period of usage you're authorized for.
8. **Usage Agreement**: Check the box to acknowledge that you've read and agreed to the usage terms.
9. **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your signature.
10. **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User IP**: This field is used to identify your user IP, but it is not required.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to contact you for further communication.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Permission Type**: Select the type of permission you're requesting, either **Public** or **Private**.
* **Permission Date**: Enter the date you received permission to use the intellectual property.
* **Usage Term**: Describe the term or period of usage you're authorized for, e.g., "one-time use only".
* **Usage Agreement**: Check this box to confirm you've read and agreed to the usage terms.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your signature, which confirms the authorization.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm authorization.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have the necessary approvals and permissions from the relevant stakeholders before filling out this form.
* Be accurate and honest when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the relevant department for clarification.
