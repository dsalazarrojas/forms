# Therapist Hiring Stages - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to guide the process of hiring a therapist in various stages, from initial assessment to the final stages of the process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the Initial Assessment field, which is used for free-form notes or comments.
2. Proceed to the Job Description field, where you can enter a brief description of the job requirements.
3. In the Job Requirements field, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the job requires specific qualifications.
4. In the Other (job requirements) field, provide any additional job requirements that are not listed in the previous field.
5. In the Salary Range field, enter the salary range for the job posting.
6. In the Job Title field, enter the title of the job posting.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" in the Job Type field to indicate the type of job posting.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" in the Experience Required field to indicate whether experience is required for the job.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" in the Location field to indicate the location of the job posting.
10. Select "Active" or "Inactive" in the Job Status field to indicate the status of the job posting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Initial Assessment** (`Initial Assessment`, text, optional): This field is used for free-form notes or comments about the job posting.
* **Job Description** (`Job Description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job requirements.
* **Job Requirements** (`Job Requirements`, select_one, true): Select "Yes" if the job requires specific qualifications, and "No" otherwise.
* **Other (job requirements)** (`Other (job requirements)`, text, false): Enter any additional job requirements that are not listed in the previous field.
* **Salary Range** (`Salary Range`, number, true): Enter the salary range for the job posting.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, false): Enter the title of the job posting.
* **Job Type** (`Job Type`, select_one, true): Select "Yes" if the job is a full-time or part-time position, and "No" otherwise.
* **Experience Required** (`Experience Required`, select_one, true): Select "Yes" if experience is required for the job, and "No" otherwise.
* **Location** (`Location`, select_one, false): Select "Yes" if the job is located in a specific location, and "No" otherwise.
* **Job Status** (`Job Status`, select_one, false): Select "Active" if the job is currently available, and "Inactive" otherwise.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields.
* Use the Other (job requirements) field for any additional job requirements that are not listed in the previous field.
* If you select "Yes" in the Job Requirements field, be sure to enter the specific qualifications required for the job.
