## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about research projects and their participants. It helps to ensure that all necessary details are collected accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as follows:

1. **Research Proposal**: Provide a brief summary of the research proposal.
2. **Participant Details**: Enter the participant's name, email, and any other relevant details.
3. **Project Start Date**: Enter the start date of the project.
4. **Project End Date**: Enter the end date of the project.
5. **Participant Contact Information**: Enter the participant's phone number, institution, and other contact details.
6. **Project Information**: Enter the project's institution, type, and funding sponsor.
7. **Research Area**: Enter the specific area of research.
8. **Project Budget**: Enter the budget for the project.
9. **Participant Role**: Select the participant's role in the project.
10. **Participant Other Role**: If the participant's role is not in the list, enter it here.
11. **Participant Affiliation**: Enter the participant's institution and affiliation.
12. **Participant Title**: Enter the participant's title.
13. **Project Lead**: Enter the project lead's name and email.
14. **Project Lead Contact Information**: Enter the project lead's phone number.
15. **Participant Education**: Enter the participant's education background.
16. **Participant Employment Status**: Enter the participant's employment status.
17. **Participant Current Institution**: Enter the participant's current institution.
18. **Participant Agency**: Enter the participant's agency.
