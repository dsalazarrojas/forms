# Vendor Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Satisfaction Survey Form is designed to collect data on the satisfaction level of vendors regarding various aspects of the business relationship. This data will help evaluate and improve the partnership between your business and its vendors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each question's purpose and relevance.
2. Answer each question accurately and truthfully, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you're unsure about a question or have a concern, please contact the specified contact person.
4. Complete all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
5. If a field is optional, feel free to provide as much information as you're comfortable sharing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vendor_satisfaction_survey_form_page1** (`text`, required): Briefly describe your experience with our business.
* **vendor_satisfaction_survey_form_page1_questions_1** (`select_one`, required): How satisfied are you with our service? (Very good, Good, Fair, Poor)
* **vendor_satisfaction_survey_form_page1_questions_2** (`text`, required): What was the most impressive aspect of our service?
* **vendor_satisfaction_survey_form_page1_questions_3** (`text`, required): What was the most disappointing aspect of our service?
* **vendor_satisfaction_survey_form_page1_questions_4** (`date`, required): When did you first engage with our business? (Format: DD/MM/YY)
* **vendor_satisfaction_survey_form_page1_questions_5** (`time`, required): What time of day did you first engage with our business? (HH:MM format)
* **vendor_satisfaction_survey_form_page2** (`text`, required): Briefly describe your recent experience with our business.
* **vendor_satisfaction_survey_form_page2_questions_1** (`select_multiple`, required): Which aspects of our service were you particularly satisfied with? (Quality of service, Communication, Punctuality, Value for money)
