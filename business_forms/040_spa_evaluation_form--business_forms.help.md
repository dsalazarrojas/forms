# spa_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The spa_evaluation_form is used to evaluate spas and collect information about the experience of the user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Go through each page and fill in the required fields. The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) next to the label.
* Fill in your answer to each question or field according to the label description.
* Make sure to review each field for accuracy before submitting the form.
