# piercing_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The piercing_request_form is used to collect information from individuals who have been pierced or are interested in getting pierced to submit their piercing requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide your correct contact information so that we can reach out to you with any questions or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Piercing Request** (`piercing_request`, text, optional): This is the field where you can describe your piercing request in detail.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name to help us identify you and contact you about your piercing request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your piercing request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number in case we need to reach out to you about your piercing request.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose the date you are interested in getting your piercing.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time you prefer for your piercing.
* **Piercing Type** (`piercing_type`, text, optional): Describe the type of piercing you are interested in.
* **Desired Location** (`desired_location`, text, optional): Specify the location where you would like to get your piercing.
* **Reason** (`reason`, note, optional): If there's anything specific you'd like to share about your piercing request, this is the place to do so.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted by us about your piercing request (e.g., Phone, Email, etc.).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you chose to be contacted by phone.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you chose to be contacted by email.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
* We'll review your request and be in touch with you soon.
