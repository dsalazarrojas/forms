# Safety Equipment Installation Record - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to record and track the installation of safety equipment within the organization. It helps ensure that all safety equipment is properly installed and documented to prevent accidents and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in the following order:
	* Date of installation (page 1)
	* Type of equipment installed (page 2)
	* Status of the installed equipment (page 3)
	* Location where the equipment is installed (page 4)
	* Person who performed the installation (page 5)
	* Notes (page 6)
	* Safety inspections completed (page 7)
	* Serial numbers of the equipment (page 8)
	* Time of installation (page 9)
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Install Date** (`installation_date`): Enter the date of the installation. This includes the day and month. The format is MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Select the type of safety equipment installed, such as 'safety equipment' or 'first aid kits.' This field is optional and will be used for categorization purposes.
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`): Choose the status of the installed equipment. Options are:
	+ Installed: The equipment is properly installed.
	+ Functional: The equipment is functioning correctly.
	+ Not Installed: The equipment is not installed yet.
	+ Not Functional: The equipment is not functioning correctly.
* **Installation Location** (`installation_location`): Enter the exact location where the equipment is installed, such as 'Main Office' or 'Room 101.'
* **Installed By** (`installed_by`): Enter the name of the person who performed the installation. This is typically the name of the person who physically installed the equipment.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any relevant notes or comments about the installation, such as specific instructions or observations.
* **Safety Inspections Completed** (`safety_inspections_completed`): Check if safety inspections have been completed for the installed equipment. This field is optional and will help with compliance and record-keeping.
* **Equipment Serial Numbers** (`safety_equipment_serial_numbers`): Enter the serial numbers of the safety equipment, if applicable. This is useful for tracking and identifying specific equipment.
* **Installed At** (`installed_at`): Enter the time of the installation, including the hours and minutes. The format is HH:MM.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use clear and concise language when entering notes or comments.
* If you're unsure about the installation date or time, check with the person who performed the installation or refer to the equipment's documentation.
* This form is an essential tool for tracking and ensuring the safety and compliance of your organization's safety equipment.
