# rental_checklist_check_out_check_in - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for checking out and checking in rental equipment. It helps to record the date the equipment is checked out, the date it is returned, the type of equipment, its ID, name, contact name, and contact number. The form ensures that all necessary information is provided and maintained correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the check out date of the equipment on the first page.
2. Fill in the return date of the equipment on the second page.
3. Select the type of equipment from the dropdown on the third page.
4. Fill in the ID of the equipment on the fourth page.
5. Fill in the name of the equipment on the fifth page.
6. Fill in the name of the contact person on the sixth page.
7. Fill in the contact number of the person on the seventh page.
8. Fill in any additional comments on the eighth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, date, required): Enter the date the equipment is being checked out by the user.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required): Enter the date the equipment is being returned to the user.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, required): Select the category of the equipment being checked out (e.g., Equipment, Machinery).
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, number, required): Enter the unique ID of the equipment being checked out.
* **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the equipment being checked out.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the equipment.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the person responsible for the equipment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the equipment.
