# Smart Equipment Systems Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Equipment Systems Contact Form is a vital tool for reporting issues or asking questions about smart equipment systems. This form allows you to provide contact information, describe the problem or inquiry, and track the status of your submission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (marked as `*`) with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct options for `contact_person`, `contact_department`, `equipment_type`, `inquiry_type`, `assigned_to`, `route_to`, and `submission_status` fields.
3. Enter a brief description of the issue or inquiry in the `equipment_description` field (if required).
4. Provide a valid `contact_email` and `contact_phone` for further communication.
5. Select the correct `created_by` and `updated_by` fields to indicate who submitted and last updated the form.
6. Fill in the `date_submitted`, `time_submitted`, and `created_date` fields with the corresponding dates and times.
7. Click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_info (Text, *):** Enter your name, title, and contact information (e.g., name, department, and contact number).
* **contact_person (Select One, *):** Choose whether you are a customer or a technical person.
* **contact_department (Select Multiple, *):** Select all departments that apply to your inquiry.
* **contact_email (Email, *):** Enter a valid email address for further communication.
* **contact_phone (Text, *):** Enter a valid phone number for further communication.
* **equipment_type (Select Multiple, *):** Choose the type(s) of equipment related to your inquiry.
* **equipment_description (Text, optional):** Provide a brief description of the issue or inquiry.
* **inquiry_type (Select One, *):** Choose the type of inquiry (e.g., report issue, ask question).
* **comment (Note, optional):** Add any additional comments or details about the issue or inquiry.
* **assigned_to (Select One, *):** Choose who should be assigned to handle your submission.
* **route_to (Select Multiple, *):** Choose how your submission should be routed (e.g., to a technical team or sales team).
* **date_submitted (Date):** Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **time_submitted (Time):** Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **submission_status (Select Multiple, *):** Choose the status of your submission (e.g., Active or Inactive).
* **created_by (Select One, *):** Choose who created the submission.
* **created_date (Date):** Enter the date the submission was created.
* **updated_by (Select One, optional):** Choose who updated the submission.
* **updated_date (Date, optional):** Enter the date the submission was last updated.
