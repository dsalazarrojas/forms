## Purpose

The purpose of this questionnaire is to gather information from potential carriers or subcontractors. This information will be used to assess their qualifications and suitability for partnership.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subcontractor Legal Name**: Enter the name of your company as registered with the FMCSA (Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration).
* **Parent Company**: If applicable, enter the name of your parent company.
* **DOT Number**: Enter your unique DOT number.
* **Years in Operation**: Enter the number of years your business has been active.
* **Total Number of Trucks in Fleet**: Enter the total number of trucks in your fleet.
* **Types of Trailers**: Select all trailer types you operate (Dry Van, Reefer, Flatbed, Drop Deck, Intermodal).
* **Team Drivers**: Select 'True' if you have team drivers available for long-haul shipments, or 'False' if not.
* **Double-Brokering Policy**: Select 'True' if you do not engage in unauthorized double-brokering, or 'False' if you do.
* **Subcontracting Drivers**: Select 'Employees', 'Independent Contractors', or 'Both' based on your driver classification.
* **Driver Background Screening**: Describe your process for vetting your drivers.
* **Current FMCSA Safety Score**: Enter your current safety score from the FMCSA dashboard.
* **Dedicated Safety Officer Name**: Enter the name of your primary safety officer.
* **Insurance Limits Check**: Select 'True' if you meet the minimum 1,000,000 dollar Liability / 100,000 dollar Cargo requirement, or 'False' otherwise.
* **Financial Stability**: Select 'Excellent', 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Poor' based on your self-reported credit score range.
* **Average Monthly Freight Volume**: Select 'Under 50 loads', '50 to 200 loads', or 'Over 200 loads' based on your average monthly shipment volume.
* **Primary Service States**: List the major service areas you serve.
* **I Certify**: Type your name as a digital signature to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the current date.

Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful partnership.
