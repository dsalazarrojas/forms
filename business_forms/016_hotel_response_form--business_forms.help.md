# Hotel Response Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Response Form is designed to collect information and responses from customers and hotels for various purposes, such as customer service, management, and communication. It covers details like customer and hotel contact information, response types, and follow-up schedules, making it a versatile tool for hotel staff to stay organized and respond effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter the hotel name**: Provide the name of the hotel where the response is being made.
2. **Enter the date and time of arrival and departure**: Enter the dates and times when the customer is expected to arrive and depart from the hotel.
3. **Select the response type**: Choose the type of response you are making, such as booking, quote, or cancellation.
4. **Provide customer contact information**: Enter the customer's email, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
5. **Add any attachments or notes**: If necessary, attach files or add notes related to the response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hotel Name** (`hotel_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the hotel where the response is being made.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, optional): Enter the customer's contact number for communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address for communication.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Add any additional comments related to the response.
* **Date Of Arrival** (`date_arrival`, date, optional): Enter the date when the customer is expected to arrive at the hotel.
* **Time Of Arrival** (`time_arrival`, time, optional): Enter the time when the customer is expected to arrive at the hotel.
* **Date Of Departure** (`date_departure`, date, optional): Enter the date when the customer is expected to depart from the hotel.
* **Time Of Departure** (`time_departure`, time, optional): Enter the time when the customer is expected to depart from the hotel.
* **Hotel Address** (`hotel_address`, text, optional): Enter the hotel's address for reference.
* **Hotel Phone** (`hotel_phone`, text, optional): Enter the hotel's phone number for contact.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name for identification.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address for communication.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number for contact.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, text, optional): Add any additional customer notes or comments.
* **Hotel Note** (`hotel_note`, text, optional): Add any additional hotel notes or comments.
* **Response Type** (`response_type`, select one, optional): Select the type of response you are making, such as booking, quote, or cancellation.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select multiple, optional): Select the frequency at which you would like to follow up with the customer, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the name of the staff member assigned to handle the response.

* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments related to the response.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, select multiple, optional): Attach any relevant files or links to the response.
