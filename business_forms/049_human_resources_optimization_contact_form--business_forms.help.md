# Human Resources Optimization Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for business contacts to provide their company information and HR priorities for the purpose of optimizing HR services. It will help us understand your business needs and improve our services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the required fields (marked as "required") and leave the optional fields blank if not necessary.
2. For the "Company Size" field, select one of the three options: small, medium, or large.
3. For the "HR Priorities" field, select all the relevant options that apply to your business needs.
4. Enter your company's website in the "Company Website" field if your company has one.
5. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
6. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): This is your personal name that will be used to identify you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): This is your last name that will be used to identify you.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): This field helps us understand the size of your company. Select one of the three options: small, medium, or large.
* **HR Priorities** (`hr_priorities`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select the areas where HR services are most important to your business. Select all the options that apply to your company needs.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): If your company has a website, please enter the URL here.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): This is the email address you would like to be contacted on.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): This is your contact phone number where you can be reached.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
- Double-check the fields before submitting the form.
- If you need help or have questions, contact us at [Your Contact Information].
