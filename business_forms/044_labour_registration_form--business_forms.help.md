# Labour Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Labour Registration Form is a tool used to capture and store information about labourers or employees for internal business use. This form is intended to be filled out by administrators or HR personnel who have access to this information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the Labour Registration Form, follow these steps:
	* Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
	* Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
	* For each field, provide accurate and complete information.
2. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Labour Records (Page 1)
* **labour_records** (Note): This is a note field where you can add any additional information about the labourer or employee.

### Labour Type (Page 2)
* **labour_type** (Select One): Choose the type of labour or employee category (e.g., '1', '2', or '3').

### Labour Date (Page 3)
* **labour_date** (Date): Enter the date of the labour or employment event.

### Labour Time (Page 4)
* **labour_time** (Time): Enter the time of the labour or employment event.

### Labourer Name (Page 5)
* **laborer_name** (Text): Enter the name of the labourer or employee.

### Business Email (Page 6)
* **business_email** (Email): Enter the email address of the business or company.

### Business Phone (Page 7)
* **business_phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the business or company.

### Address (Page 8)
* **address** (Text): Enter the address of the business or company.

### City (Page 9)
* **city** (Text): Enter the city of the business or company.

### State (Page 10)
* **state** (Text): Enter the state of the business or company.

### Country (Page 11)
* **country** (Text): Enter the country of the business or company.

### Zip (Page 12)
* **zip** (Text): Enter the ZIP code of the business or company.

### Labourer Count (Page 13)
* **laborer_count** (Number): Enter the number of labourers or employees.

### Labourer Salary (Page 14)
* **laborer_salary** (Number): Enter the salary of the labourer or employee.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* This form is intended for internal use within the company, so be mindful of sensitive information.
