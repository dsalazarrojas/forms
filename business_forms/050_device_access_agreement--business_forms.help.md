# Device Access Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Device Access Agreement form is designed to collect information related to device access and usage within an organization. This form will help in ensuring that employees are aware of their responsibilities and limitations when accessing company devices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the User Information section with your employee details.
2. Provide the necessary information about the device you need access to, including the device type and status.
3. Check the User Acknowledgement section to confirm your understanding of the device access agreement.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel file.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your department or team name.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job function or role within the company.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your company email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company phone number.

### Device Information
* **Device Type** (`device_type`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the type of device you need access to (e.g., laptop, mobile phone, etc.).
* **Device ID** (`device_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the device ID number.
* **Device Status** (`device_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the device (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Device Notes** (`device_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the device.

### Usage Terms
* **User Acknowledgement** (`user_acknowledgement`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the boxes to confirm your understanding of the device access agreement.
* **Other Reasons** (`user_acknowledgement_other`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional reasons for your acknowledgement.
* **Date of Agreement** (`date_of_agreement`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you are agreeing to the device access terms.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you signed the agreement.
* **User Signature** (`user_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your signature.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your manager's signature.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date your manager signed the agreement.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time your manager signed the agreement.
* **Printed Name** (`printed_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your printed name.
* **Printed Title** (`printed_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your printed title.
