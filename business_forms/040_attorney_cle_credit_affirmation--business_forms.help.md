# Attorney CLE Credit Affirmation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a means for attorneys to affirm their completion of Continuing Legal Education (CLE) courses, which helps ensure compliance with their state's attorney certification requirements.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your basic contact information (Attorney Name, Bar Number, Bar State, Email Address, and Law Firm/ Organization Name).
2.  Provide details about the CLE course, including the title, date, provider, and location.
3.  Choose the correct accreditation and credit types (General CLE, Ethics, Professionalism, Diversity, Cybersecurity, or Other).
4.  Confirm your attendance, retention of course materials, receipt of a completion certificate, and absence of compensation received.
5.  Finally, affirm that the information provided is truthful and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Attorney Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your license.
*   **Bar Number**: Enter your state bar admission number.
*   **Bar State**: Enter the state(s) in which you are admitted to the bar.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your professional email address.
*   **Law Firm Name**: Enter the name of your current law firm or organization.
*   **Course Title**: Enter the official name of the CLE course you attended.
*   **Course Date**: Enter the date on which you completed the course.
*   **Course Provider**: Enter the organization that offered the CLE course.
*   **Course Format**: Choose the format in which the course was delivered (Live In-Person, Live Virtual, Recording/Webinar, Self-Study, or Hybrid).
*   **Course Location**: If applicable, enter the city or venue where the course was held.
*   **Approved Jurisdiction**: Enter the state bar(s) that approved this CLE course.
*   **CLE Credits Claimed**: Enter the number of credit hours completed.
*   **Ethics Credits**: Enter the number of credits earned towards ethics requirements.
*   **Professionalism Credits**: Enter the number of credits earned towards professionalism or diversity requirements.
*   **Type of Credit**: Choose the category of credit (General CLE, Ethics, Professionalism, Diversity, Cybersecurity, or Other).
*   **I Attended This Course In Its Entirety**: Confirm that you attended the course in its entirety.
*   **I Retained Course Materials**: Confirm that you retained course materials for records.
*   **Certificate of Completion Received**: Confirm whether you received a completion certificate.
*   **No Compensation Disclosure**: Affirm that you did not receive compensation for attending the course.
*   **Truthfulness Affirmation**: Affirm that the information provided is truthful and accurate.
*   **Submission Date**: Enter the date this form is being submitted.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and truthfully to avoid any potential issues with your certification.
*   If you have any doubts or questions, please contact your state bar association or the CLE provider for clarification.
*   Keep a record of your completed forms for future reference.
