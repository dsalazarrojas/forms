# cultural-transformation-consulting-contact-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers or clients interested in our cultural transformation consulting services. It will help us understand their needs and goals, which will aid us in providing tailored solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field according to your preferences. Make sure to complete all required fields. If a field is optional, feel free to skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (2)**: Enter your name as you would like it to be referred to in our communication.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Contact Preference (5)**: Select all contact preferences that apply to you, such as phone, email, or in-person meetings.
* **Transformation Goals (6)**: Describe your goals and expectations for our cultural transformation consulting services.
* **Notes (7)**: Add any additional information or context that may be helpful for us to understand your needs.
* **Assigned Tool (8)**: Select the tool that you would like us to use for your project, which is mandatory.
* **Form ID (9)**: This is an internal field and can be left blank.
