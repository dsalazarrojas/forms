# Internship Supervisor Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate an intern's performance during their internship period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the intern's performance during their internship period.
2. Choose the relevant fields that you feel are applicable to the intern's performance.
3. Provide a rating for each selected field using the scale provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Supervisor Rating** (`supervisor_rating`, Number, Not Required): This field allows you to rate the intern's overall performance as a supervisor. Rate the intern's ability to work effectively with you and the rest of the team.
* **Intern Progress** (`intern_progress`, Number, Not Required): This field is used to rate the intern's progress during the internship period. Rate how well the intern met the goals and objectives set during the internship.
* **Work Quality** (`work_quality`, Number, Not Required): This field is used to rate the intern's work quality. Evaluate the intern's ability to produce high-quality work.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, Number, Not Required): This field is used to rate the intern's communication skills. Rate the intern's ability to communicate effectively with you and the rest of the team.
* **Teamwork** (`teamwork`, Number, Not Required): This field is used to rate the intern's ability to work effectively with the team.
* **Punctuality** (`punctuality`, Number, Not Required): This field is used to rate the intern's punctuality.
* **Adaptability** (`adaptability`, Number, Not Required): This field is used to rate the intern's adaptability.
* **Professionalism** (`professionalism`, Number, Not Required): This field is used to rate the intern's professionalism.
* **Leadership** (`leadership`, Number, Not Required): This field is used to rate the intern's leadership skills.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text, Not Required): This field is used to provide any additional feedback you have for the intern. This can be any comments or suggestions you have for areas of improvement. 

## Tips
* Be objective and fair when evaluating the intern's performance.
* Ensure that you are evaluating the intern's performance based on their actual abilities and not based on potential.
* Choose only the fields that you feel are relevant to the intern's performance.
