## pos_setup_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to request setup and configuration of a Point of Sale (POS) system in a business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the business name and address.
2. Enter the phone number and email of the business.
3. Specify the number of equipment required for the setup.
4. Choose the type of POS system to be set up (POS or EPOS).
5. Identify any issues that may be encountered during setup (Hardware issues, Software issues, Hardware/Software issues, Configuration issues).
6. Provide additional comments or notes about the setup.
7. Enter the name and email of the person who will be submitting the request.
8. Enter the name and email of the store manager.
9. Add any additional notes.
