# Business Intelligence Roadmap Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from stakeholders to create a business intelligence roadmap. The form is used to understand the current state of the organization's analytics capabilities, identify areas for improvement, and determine the strategic vision and goals for the next business intelligence roadmap.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and clear information.
2. Provide the stakeholder's name and organization name in the respective fields.
3. Select the current BI maturity level to describe your organization's current analytics capabilities.
4. Choose the primary data sources that are relevant to the roadmap.
5. Describe the current challenges with analytics.
6. Select the top three strategic goals for the next roadmap.
7. Choose the desired planning horizon for the roadmap.
8. List the key performance indicators (KPIs) that are important for your business.
9. Indicate the budget availability for the BI roadmap.
10. Provide the name of the executive project sponsor.
11. Enter the date of request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stakeholder Name** (`roadmap_stakeholder_name`): Enter the name of the stakeholder submitting this form.
* **Organization Name** (`roadmap_org_name`): Enter the name of the organization.
* **Current BI Maturity Level** (`current_bi_maturity_level`): Describe your current analytics capabilities, from "Basic" (ad-hoc reporting, mostly spreadsheets) to "Prescriptive" (automated optimization, what should we do).
* **Primary Data Sources** (`primary_data_sources_selection`): Select all the data sources that are relevant to the roadmap, such as CRM Data, Financial / ERP Systems, etc.
* **Current Challenges with Analytics** (`current_analytics_challenges_text`): Describe the challenges you are facing with analytics.
* **Top 3 Strategic Goals for BI** (`strategic_bi_goals_selection`): Select the top three strategic goals for the next roadmap, such as Better Decision Making Speed, Operational Cost Reduction, etc.
* **Desired Planning Horizon** (`desired_planning_horizon_roadmap`): Choose the desired planning horizon for the roadmap, from 6 Months to 3-5 Years.
* **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to Track** (`key_performance_indicators_list`): List the most important metrics for your business.
* **Budget Availability for BI Roadmap** (`roadmap_budget_availability_choice`): Indicate if there is an allocated budget for the BI roadmap.
* **Executive Project Sponsor** (`roadmap_project_sponsor_name`): Enter the name of the person championing this initiative.
* **Date of Request** (`roadmap_request_date_official`): Enter the date of request.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by the appropriate person in the organization, and any submitted form will be reviewed to ensure it is valid and relevant.
