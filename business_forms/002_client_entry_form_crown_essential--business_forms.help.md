# Client Entry Form - Crown Essential - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to enter new client information for onboarding and efficient client management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form header to begin.
2. Fill in the client's given name in the "Client First Name" field.
3. Provide the client's surname in the "Client Last Name" field.
4. Enter the client's company or organization name, if applicable, in the "Company" field.
5. Enter the client's email address in the "Email Address" field.
6. Optionally, provide the client's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Select the primary service required from the "Service Required" dropdown.
8. If the client was referred by someone else, select the source of the referral from the "Referral Source" dropdown.
9. If desired, choose a time that is most convenient for contacting the client in the "Preferred Contact Time" field.
10. Leave any additional context or notes about the client in the "Intake Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client First Name (text, Required)**: Enter the client's given name.
* **Client Last Name (text, Required)**: Enter the client's family name.
* **Company (text, Optional)**: Enter the client's company or organization name, if applicable.
* **Email Address (Email, Required)**: Enter the client's business or personal email address.
* **Phone Number (text, Optional)**: Enter the client's best contact number.
* **Service Required (Select One, Required)**: Select the primary service required by the client.
* **Referral Source (Select One, Optional)**: Select how the client heard about you.
* **Preferred Contact Time (Time, Optional)**: Choose a time that is most convenient for contacting the client.
* **Intake Notes (Text, Optional)**: Leave any additional context or notes about the client.
