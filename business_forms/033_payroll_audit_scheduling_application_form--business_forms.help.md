# payroll_audit_scheduling_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for payroll audits from employees. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the audit process is well-organized and efficient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field in the format of `your_name@example.com`.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the reason for your audit request from the "Audit Request" dropdown menu.
6. Choose a preferred date for the audit in the "Preferred Date" field. Please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
7. Optionally, you can provide additional details in the "Details" field about your audit request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format `your_name@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the standard format (###-###-####).
* **Audit Request** (`audit_request`, select_one, required): Select the reason for your audit request from the dropdown menu.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Choose a date for the audit in the standard format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Details** (`details`, text, optional): If you have any additional information about your audit request, enter it here.
