# Internet Speed Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their internet speed experience. It helps our team assess the current state of internet services provided by different companies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the "User Input" tab and enter your name in the required field.
2. Provide the location where you experience the internet services in the "location" field.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" if you receive internet services from more than one provider.
4. Choose the type of internet service provider you are using from the options below (if applicable).
5. Select "Yes" or "No" if you have experienced issues with download or upload speeds.
6. If you have experienced issues with download or upload speeds, choose the "Yes" or "No" options for the specific speed type.
7. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter it in the "User Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input (Text)**: Enter your name to help us identify you in our survey results.
* **Location (Text)**: Please enter the place where you experience the internet services.
* **Internet Service Provider (Text)**: If you have internet services from more than one provider, select one of the options below. This field is not applicable if you only have one provider.
* **Router Type (Text)**: Enter the type of router you are using.
* **Place (Text)**: Enter any additional details about the place you are in.
* **User Comments (Note)**: Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the form.
* **Internet Service Provider 2 (Select Multiple)**: Select all internet service providers that apply.
	+ "Yes" for providers that you use and have experienced issues with download or upload speeds.
* **Download Speed 2 (Select One)**: Select "Yes" if you have experienced issues with download speeds from the selected provider.
* **Upload Speed 2 (Select One)**: Select "Yes" if you have experienced issues with upload speeds from the selected provider.
