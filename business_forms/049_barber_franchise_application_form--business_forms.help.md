# Barber Franchise Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Barber Franchise Application Form is a comprehensive form designed to gather essential information from potential franchisees who are interested in opening a barber franchise. This form is a crucial step in the application process, as it helps the franchise evaluate the applicant's background, experience, and business plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide detailed and accurate information about yourself and your business plan. Make sure to answer all required fields, and if you're unsure about any field, please provide a brief explanation or note that you're unsure. If you have any issues or concerns, you can also provide additional information in the "Issue Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): The date you submitted your application.
* **Applicant First Name** (`applicant_first_name`, text, required): Your first name.
* **Applicant Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`, text, required): Your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, text, required): Your current residential address.
* **Years of Business Experience** (`business_experience`, number, required): Total business experience.
* **Years of Barbering Experience** (`barbering_experience`, number, required): How long you've been in barbering.
* **Previous Business Ownership** (`previous_business_ownership`, select_one, required): If you've owned a business before, select 'Yes', 'No', or 'Partial Ownership'.
* **Previous Business Details** (`previous_business_details`, text, required if previous business ownership is 'Yes' or 'Partial Ownership'): Describe your previous business.
* **Preferred Location for Franchise** (`preferred_location`, text, required): The city or region you'd like to open the franchise.
* **Desired Franchise Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): The date you'd like to start the franchise.
* **Available Investment Capital** (`investment_capital`, number, required): Total funds available to invest.
* **Financing Plan** (`financing_plan`, select_one, required): How you plan to finance the franchise, select from 'All Cash', 'Bank Loan', 'SBA Loan', 'Investor Partnership', 'Mixed Financing', or 'Still Planning'.
* **Credit Score Range** (`credit_score_range`, select_one, not required): Your approximate credit score, select from 'Excellent (750+)', 'Good (700-749)', 'Fair (650-699)', 'Poor (below 650)', or 'Prefer Not to Say'.
* **Existing Business Debt** (`existing_debt`, select_one, required): If you have outstanding debt, select 'Yes', 'No', or 'Minor Amount'.
* **If Yes, Approximate Debt Amount** (`debt_amount`, number, required if existing debt is 'Yes' or 'Minor Amount'): The total outstanding debt amount.
* **Management Team in Place** (`management_team`, select_one, required): If you have a management team, select 'Yes - Full Team', 'Partial Team', or 'No - Need to Hire'.
* **Management Team Details** (`team_details`, text, required if management team is 'Yes - Full Team' or 'Partial Team'): Names and roles of your management team members.
* **Marketing and Sales Experience** (`marketing_experience`, select_one, required): Your experience level in marketing and sales, select from 'Extensive', 'Moderate', 'Limited', or 'None'.
* **Commitment to Brand Standards** (`commitment_level`, select_one, required): Your commitment level to following the brand standards, select from 'Fully Committed', 'Committed', or 'Will Discuss'.
* **Interest in Multiple Locations** (`multiple_locations`, select_one, not required): If you're interested in opening multiple locations, select 'Single Location Only', 'Possible Future Expansion', or 'Yes - Multiple Units Planned'.
* **Any Legal or Regulatory Issues** (`legal_issues`, select_one, required): If you have any legal or regulatory issues, select 'Yes', 'No', or 'Prefer Not to Disclose'.
* **Issue Description (if applicable)** (`issue_description`, text, not required): Explain any legal or regulatory issues, if applicable.
