# legal_documentation_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the legal documentation needs for a business or individual.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of legal document needed from the "Legal Document Type" field.
2. Specify the purpose of the document from the "Legal Document Purpose" field.
3. Enter the required information for the document, including the issuer's contact details and the document's effective date and time.
4. Review and update the revision details if necessary.
5. Select the status of the document's revision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Legal Document Needs
* **Business Legal Document Needs** (`business_legal_document_needs`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the legal document needs.
### Legal Document Type
* **Legal Document Type** (`legal_document_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of legal document needed.
*   - Legal Document
*   - Business Registration
*   - Non-Resident Alien
*   - Will
*   - Living Will
*   - Power of Attorney
*   - Other
### Legal Document Purpose
* **Legal Document Purpose** (`legal_document_purpose`, select_multiple, required): Select the purpose of the document.
*   - To create a will
*   - To create a trust
*   - To create a power of attorney
*   - To create a business registration
*   - Other
