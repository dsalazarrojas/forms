# Internet Services Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and confirm billing information from customers for internet services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Company Name field with the customer's business name.
2. Enter the Billing Date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Enter the Billing Amount for the services rendered.
4. Select the Payment Gateway (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, Bank Transfer) from the dropdown menu.
5. Choose the Payment Method (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Account, PayPal) from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter the Due Date for the payment in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
7. Provide any additional Notes about the billing.
8. Enter the Customer's Email address.
9. Enter the Customer's Phone number.
10. Enter the Customer's Address.
11. Enter the Customer's Address 2 (if applicable).
12. Enter the City the customer is from.
13. Select the State where the customer is from from the dropdown menu.
14. Enter the Zip code for the customer's address.
15. Select the Country from which the customer is from from the dropdown menu.
16. Confirm the Payment Gateway selected in step 4.
17. Confirm the Payment Method selected in step 6.
18. Confirm the Due Date entered in step 7.
19. Confirm any Notes entered in step 8.
20. Confirm the Email address entered in step 9.
21. Confirm the Phone number entered in step 10.
22. Confirm the Address entered in step 11.
23. Confirm the Address 2 entered in step 12.
24. Confirm the City entered in step 13.
25. Confirm the State selected in step 14.
26. Confirm the Zip code entered in step 15.
27. Confirm the Country selected in step 16.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (1)**: Enter the full name of the customer's business.
* **Billing Date (2)**: Enter the date when the customer's bill is due.
* **Billing Amount (3)**: Enter the amount of the customer's bill.
* **Payment Gateway (4)**: Select how the customer's payment will be processed (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Method (5)**: Select the method of payment the customer has chosen (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Account, PayPal).
* **Due Date (6)**: Enter the date when the customer's payment is due.
* **Notes (7)**: Enter any additional comments or details about the billing.
* **Email (8)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone (9)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Address (10)**: Enter the customer's address.
* **Address 2 (11)**: Enter the customer's secondary address (if applicable).
* **City (12)**: Enter the city where the customer is from.
* **State (13)**: Select the state where the customer is from from the dropdown menu.
* **Zip (14)**: Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Country (15)**: Select the country from which the customer is from from the dropdown menu.
* **Confirm Payment Gateway (16)**: Confirm the Payment Gateway selected in step 4.
* **Confirm Payment Method (17)**: Confirm the Payment Method selected in step 5.
* **Confirm Due Date (18)**: Confirm the Due Date entered in step 6.
* **Confirm Notes (19)**: Confirm any additional comments or details about the billing.
* **Confirm Email (20)**: Confirm the customer's email address.
* **Confirm Phone (21)**: Confirm the customer's phone number.
* **Confirm Address (22)**: Confirm the customer's address.
* **Confirm Address 2 (23)**: Confirm the customer's secondary address (if applicable).
* **Confirm City (24)**: Confirm the city where the customer is from.
* **Confirm State (25)**: Confirm the state where the customer is from.
* **Confirm Zip (26)**: Confirm the customer's zip code.
* **Confirm Country (27)**: Confirm the country from which the customer is from.
