# Minimalist Company Letterhead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Minimalist Company Letterhead Form is a basic template for creating a company letterhead, designed to collect essential information for official purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields as follows:

- **Company Name**: Enter your company's name.
- **Address**: Enter your company's address.
- **City**: Enter your city or location.
- **State**: Enter your state or region.
- **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
- **Phone**: Enter your company's phone number.
- **Email**: Enter your company's email address.
- **Date**: Enter the date (in the format "MM/DD/YYYY").
- **Time**: Enter the time (in the format "HH:MM AM/PM").
- **Signature**: Add your digital or handwritten signature for official approval or signing.
