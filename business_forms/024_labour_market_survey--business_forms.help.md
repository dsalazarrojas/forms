# labour_market_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Labour Market Survey form is a tool to gather data and feedback from individuals or organizations about their experiences and insights on the local employment market. The information collected will help inform business decisions and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read through each page carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2.  For the "Survey Part One" section, enter the number of people you have seen looking for a job in the past 4 weeks.
3.  In the "Survey Part Two" section, select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have seen any changes in the local employment market.
4.  In the "Employment Issues" section, select all the relevant options that apply to your experiences.
5.  In the "Notes" section, provide any additional comments or thoughts you have.
6.  In the "Contact Info" section, enter your contact information if you wish to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **This is an introduction** (Introduction): A short introduction to the form. Please read and understand the purpose and scope of this form.
* **How many people have you seen in the past 4 weeks looking for a job?** (Survey Part One, Number): Report the number of people you have seen looking for a job in the past 4 weeks. This helps us understand local job market trends.
* **Have you seen any changes in the local employment market?** (Survey Part Two, Select One): Select "Yes" if you have seen any changes, and "No" if not. This helps us understand changes in the local employment market.
* **What are some of the biggest employment issues you have faced?** (Employment Issues, Select Multiple): Select all the relevant options that apply to your experiences. These questions help us understand specific employment issues.
* **Do you have any further comments or notes?** (Notes): Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have.
* **How did you contact us?** (Contact Info): Enter your contact information if you wish to be contacted.
* **Email** (Contact Email): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted through email.
* **Name** (Contact Name): Enter your name if you wish to be contacted.
* **Phone** (Contact Phone): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted through phone.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any further comments or questions, please use the "Notes" section.
* If you wish to be contacted, please enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
