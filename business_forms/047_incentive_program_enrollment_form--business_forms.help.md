# Incentive Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incentive Program Enrollment Form is used to enroll employees in company incentive programs. This form collects essential information from employees, their managers, and the submitter to facilitate the enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your Employee Email in the "Employee Email" field.
3. Enter your Company Name, Company Address, and Department in their respective fields.
4. Select your Program Type from the list of available options.
5. Choose the Enrollment Date and Start Date for the program.
6. Enter your Manager's Name, Manager's Email, and Manager's Phone in their respective fields.
7. Select the Manager's Status of the program (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
8. Enter any additional Notes for the program.
9. Choose the Submitter (John, Jane, or Joe) and enter their Email, Phone, and Department.
10. Enter any additional Comments or Notes for the Submitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as an employee.
* **Employee Email** (Email, Required): Enter your work email address.
* **Company Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your company's name.
* **Company Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your company's address.
* **Program Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of incentive program you are enrolling in.
* **Enrollment Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date of program enrollment.
* **Start Date** (Date, Optional): Select the start date of the program.
* **Manager Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager's Status** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the manager's status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Program Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any notes about the program.
* **Submitter** (Select One, Optional): Choose the submitter (John, Jane, or Joe).
* **Submitter Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the submitter's phone number.
* **Submitter Department** (Text, Optional): Enter the submitter's department.
* **Submitter Title** (Text, Optional): Enter the submitter's title.
* **Submitter Comments** (Note, Optional): Add any comments from the submitter.
* **Company Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes about the company.
* **Submitter Additional Comments** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments from the submitter.
