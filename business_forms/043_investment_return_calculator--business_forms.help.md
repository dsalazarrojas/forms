# Investment Return Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Return Calculator is designed to help users estimate the potential return on investment based on the amount invested, investment period, and interest rate. This form collects information about the investment amount, time period, and rate to provide a quick calculation of the expected return.

## How To Complete This Form
To use the Investment Return Calculator, follow these steps:

1. Enter the investment amount in the "investment_amount" field. This is the total amount you plan to invest.
2. Select the investment period in the "investment_period" field. This is the time frame over which you expect the investment to grow.
3. Enter the interest rate in the "investment_rate" field. This is the annual percentage rate of return on investment.
4. Click the "submit" button to calculate the expected return on investment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investment Amount**: Enter the total amount you plan to invest. For example, "10000" for $10,000.
* **Investment Period**: Select the time frame over which you expect the investment to grow. For example, "1 year", "5 years", "10 years", etc.
* **Investment Return**: Enter the annual percentage rate of return on investment. For example, "5" for 5% or "10" for 10%. 
* **Investment Rate**: Enter the interest rate at which the investment grows. For example, "5" for 5% or "10" for 10%. 

Note: The investment amount, period, and rate fields are all optional, but are required to provide a meaningful calculation. If any of these fields are left blank, the calculator will not produce a result.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the investment amount, period, and rate accurately to ensure the most accurate calculation.
* The investment return is calculated based on the formula: A = P * (1 + r)^n, where A is the amount of money earned, P is the principal amount, r is the annual interest rate, and n is the number of years.
