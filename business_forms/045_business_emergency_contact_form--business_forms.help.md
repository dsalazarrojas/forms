# Business Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Emergency Contact Form is a critical tool to gather information for emergency situations. This form is not for general employee information, only for emergency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form carefully and accurately, using the correct information.
- Ensure that the form is completed in case of an actual emergency or when instructed by HR or management.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your complete name as it is recorded on your official records.
* **Employee ID or Badge Number**: Enter your employee identification number or badge number, if applicable.
* **Department**: Specify your department or division where you work.
* **Position Title**: Provide your official job title.
* **Work Phone**: Enter your office phone number for contact.
* **Work Email**: Enter your company email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your primary emergency contact, such as a spouse or family member.
* **Relationship to You**: Choose how you are related to your emergency contact (e.g., Spouse, Parent, Sibling, etc.).
* **Primary Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your primary emergency contact.
* **Secondary Contact**: Enter the name of your secondary emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Secondary Contact Name**: Enter the name of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Secondary Relationship**: Specify the relationship of your secondary contact to you.
* **Secondary Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your secondary contact.
* **Blood Type**: If known, enter your blood type (e.g., O+, A-, etc.).
* **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have (e.g., medications, foods).
* **Medical Conditions**: Specify any medical conditions that may affect your response or well-being.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Physician Name**: Enter the name of your physician or healthcare provider.
* **Physician Phone**: Enter the contact number of your physician.
* **Special Emergency Instructions**: Provide any special care instructions or needs that should be followed in an emergency.
