# Risk Assessment Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Assessment Report Form is designed to help employees identify and report potential risks that may impact the organization. It is used to gather information on risks that have a significant impact on the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the risk category that best describes the risk you are reporting.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of the risk.
3. Enter a number for the likelihood of the risk (with 1 being the highest likelihood and 5 being the lowest likelihood).
4. Enter a number for the impact of the risk (with 1 being the highest impact and 5 being the lowest impact).
5. If applicable, provide recommendations for mitigating the risk.
6. If applicable, add any comments about the risk.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Risk Category
* **Risk Category** (`risk_category`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes the risk. Choose from: High, Medium, or Low.

### Description
* **Description** (`risk_description`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise description of the risk. Please ensure this field is not empty.

### Likelihood
* **Likelihood** (`risk_likelihood`, number, required): Enter a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is the highest likelihood and 5 is the lowest likelihood.

### Impact
* **Impact** (`risk_impact`, number, required): Enter a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is the highest impact and 5 is the lowest impact.

### Risk Score
* **Risk Score** (`risk_score`, number, optional): If you want to provide a score for the risk, enter a number between 1 and 5, but only if you have a clear understanding of what that score represents.

### Recommendations
* **Recommendations** (`risk_recommendations`, text, optional): If you have recommendations on how to mitigate or reduce the risk, enter them here.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`risk_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments about the risk, enter them here.

Note: All fields with asterisks are mandatory. If you are unsure about the purpose or use of any field, please contact the relevant personnel.
