# Marketing Campaign Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Campaign Checklist Form is a tool to evaluate key factors before launching a marketing campaign to ensure success.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and select the relevant fields that apply to your campaign.
2. Fill out each field according to the information required.
3. Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to ensure all necessary information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marketing Objectives** (page 3, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to indicate whether your marketing campaign has specific marketing objectives, such as increasing brand awareness, generating leads, or driving sales.
* **Target Audience** (page 4, select_one, required: false): Choose the target audience for your campaign. Select 'Yes' if your campaign targets a specific group, or 'No' if your campaign targets a general audience.
* **Marketing Channels** (page 5, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to select the marketing channels you plan to use for your campaign, such as social media, email, or advertising.
* **Content Strategy** (page 6, select_one, required: false): Indicate the quality of your content strategy for the campaign, with options 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **Content Distribution Channels** (page 7, select_one, required: false): Choose the channels through which you plan to distribute your content, such as 'Yes' for social media or 'No' for email.
* **Timelines** (page 8, date, required: false): Enter the start and end dates for your marketing campaign.
* **Marketing Campaign Checklist Form** (page 1, text, required: false): This field is a general description of your marketing campaign. Provide a brief overview of your campaign's purpose and objectives.
* **Campaign Planning** (page 2, note, required: false): Note any additional details or considerations for your campaign that don't fit into the other categories. This field is a free text field for you to add any relevant information.

Note: The required status of each field is 'false', meaning it's not mandatory to fill out each section. However, it's essential to fill out the relevant fields to get an accurate assessment of your campaign's readiness.
