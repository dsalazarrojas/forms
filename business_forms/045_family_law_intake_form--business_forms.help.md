# Family Law Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Law Intake Form is designed to collect essential information about family law cases, helping the organization's staff to understand the client's needs and manage the case effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill in the form accurately and thoroughly, using the correct information about the client and their case.
- Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. Client Info
* **Client Info** (text): Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
### 3. Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (number): Enter the client's contact phone number.
### 4. Family Info
* **Family Info** (select_one): Check "Yes" if you are collecting information about family members, or "No" if not.
### 5. Case Details
* **Case Details** (select_multiple): Check "Yes" for each relevant case detail you are collecting information about (e.g., divorce, child custody, adoption).
### 6. Client Documents
* **Client Documents** (text): Attach or upload any relevant documents or files related to the case.
### 7. Communication Preferences
* **Communication Preferences** (select_one): Check "Yes" to indicate that you want to keep the client updated on the case status, or "No" if not.
### 8. Case Status
* **Case Status** (select_one): Select "Active" to indicate that the case is currently being managed, or "Inactive" if it has been closed or resolved.
### 9. Next Steps
* **Next Steps** (text): Describe the next actions or steps planned for the case.

## Tips

* Always check with the client to ensure that the information collected is accurate and complete.
* Use this form as a guide to ensure that all necessary information is collected.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, consult with a supervisor or colleague for clarification.
* Make sure to keep the form up-to-date and accurate to reflect any changes in the case status or client information.
