# Excavation Permit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Excavation Permit Form is used to collect necessary information for regulatory approval of excavation projects. It gathers details about the project, including project location, site description, and safety precautions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in the fields with accurate and relevant information about your project.
3. Ensure you have answered all required fields, as indicated by the "Required" tag.
4. If you are unsure about any field, ask for assistance from your supervisor or contact the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Details
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the project, including the project name, location, and any relevant details.

### Project Location
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, Optional): Enter the exact location of the project, including the street address or coordinates.

### Permit Type
* **Permit Type** (`permit_type`, select_one, Optional): Select the type of permit required for the project from the following options:
	+ Permit A
	+ Permit B
	+ Permit C

### Site Description
* **Site Description** (`site_description`, text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of the site, including any relevant environmental or geographical features.

### Start Date
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, Optional): Enter the expected start date of the project.

### End Date
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, Optional): Enter the expected end date of the project.

### Excavated Area
* **Excavated Area** (`excavated_area`, number, Optional): Enter the total area to be excavated, including any underground or above-ground areas.

### Excavated Area Unit
* **Excavated Area Unit** (`excavated_area_unit`, select_one, Optional): Select the unit of measurement for the excavated area from the following options:
	+ Square Meters
	+ Square Feet
	+ Square Centimeters
	+ Square Kilometers

### Safety Precautions
* **Safety Precautions** (`safety_precautions`, text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of any safety precautions that will be taken during the project, including any necessary personal protective equipment, emergency procedures, and contingency plans.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and understand all relevant regulations and laws before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, do not hesitate to ask for assistance.
