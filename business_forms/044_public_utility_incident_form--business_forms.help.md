# Public Utility Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Utility Incident Form is a tool for utility companies to report and track incidents related to their services. This form helps to gather relevant information about each incident, allowing the company to respond and resolve issues quickly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Report Details** section with a brief description of the incident.
2. Enter the **Location** where the incident occurred.
3. Select the **Utility Company** responsible for the service affected by the incident.
4. Enter the **Date** and **Time** of the incident.
5. Enter the **Report Date** and **Report Time** (if available).
6. Provide a detailed **Description** of the incident.
7. Select the **Status** of the incident (Active or Inactive).
8. Indicate whether the incident is **Assigned** to a specific user (Yes or No).
9. Select the **Type** of incident (if applicable).
10. Enter the contact information for the person reporting the incident, including **Contact Name**, **Email**, and **Phone Number**.
11. Add any additional **Incident Note** as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Details**: Enter a brief description of the incident, such as a power outage or water leak.
* **Location**: Enter the street address, city, or other specific location where the incident occurred.
* **Utility Company**: Select the company responsible for providing the affected service (e.g., electricity, water, etc.).
* **Date**: Enter the date of the incident in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the incident in the format 12:00 AM (HH:MM).
* **Report Date**: Enter the date when the incident was reported (if different from the incident date).
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time when the incident was reported (if different from the incident time).
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details.
* **Status**: Select whether the incident is currently Active or Inactive.
* **Assigned User**: Indicate whether the incident is assigned to a specific user (Yes or No).
* **Type**: Select the type of incident (if applicable).
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person reporting the incident.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person reporting the incident.
* **Incident Note**: Add any additional notes or information about the incident as necessary.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible when filling out the form.
* Use the correct format for dates (YYYY-MM-DD) and times (12:00 AM).
* If you are unsure about the status of the incident, select "Active" by default.
* If you are not sure about the type of incident, select "Yes" by default (if applicable).
