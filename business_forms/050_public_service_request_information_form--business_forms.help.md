# public_service_request_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about public service requests, including details about the service requested, supporting information, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the "Public_Service_Request" field on Page 1, providing a brief description of the public service request.
2. On Page 2, provide more details about the service request in the "Service_Request_Details" field.
3. On Page 3, select one of the options in the "Service_Request_Dropdown" field to categorize the request.
4. On Page 4, enter the date of the public service request in the "Public_Service_Request_Date" field.
5. On Page 5, enter the time of the public service request in the "Public_Service_Request_Time" field.
6. On Page 6, enter your email address in the "Email" field to contact you about the request.
7. On Page 7, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field to contact you about the request.
8. On Page 8, you may add a note about the public service request in the "Public_Service_Request_Note" field.
9. On Page 9, enter any additional information about the request in the "Public_Service_Request_Hint" field.
10. On Page 10, you may enter a secondary email address for contact purposes.
11. On Page 11, enter a phone number for contact purposes.
12. On Page 12, you may re-enter the date of the public service request if it's different from the one entered on Page 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Public_Service_Request** (`Page 1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the public service request.
* **Service_Request_Details** (`Page 2`, `text`, required/optional): Provide more details about the service request.
* **Service_Request_Dropdown** (`Page 3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the options to categorize the request.
* **Public_Service_Request_Date** (`Page 4`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the public service request.
* **Public_Service_Request_Time** (`Page 5`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the public service request.
* **Email** (`Page 6`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`Page 7`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Public_Service_Request_Note** (`Page 8`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the request.
* **Public_Service_Request_Hint** (`Page 9`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any further information about the request.
* **Public_Service_Request_Email** (`Page 10`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a secondary email address for contact purposes.
* **Public_Service_Request_Phone** (`Page 11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a phone number for contact purposes.
* **Public_Service_Request_Date_2** (`Page 12`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the public service request, if different from Page 4.
