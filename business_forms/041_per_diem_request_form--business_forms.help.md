# Per Diem Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Per Diem Request Form is designed to gather information about your business-related travel or other work-related activities that require Per Diem expense reimbursements. This form must be completed thoroughly and accurately to ensure that your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Per Diem Date of your trip or work-related activity.
2. Enter the Per Diem Start and End Time for your activity.
3. Provide the Per Diem Destination where you will be traveling or conducting work.
4. Enter the Per Diem Allowance amount for your trip or activity.
5. Enter the Per Diem Amount you are requesting for reimbursement.
6. Specify the Per Diem Expense associated with your trip or activity.
7. Enter the Per Diem Rate applicable to your trip or activity.
8. Provide a brief description of the Per Diem Reason for your request.
9. If you have an agenda for your trip or activity, please provide details about it on Page 2.
10. If you have any additional information or comments, please provide them in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Per Diem Date**: The date on which you will be traveling or conducting your work-related activity. This should be in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Per Diem Start Time**: The start time of your activity. This should be in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Per Diem End Time**: The end time of your activity. This should be in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Per Diem Destination**: The location where you will be traveling or conducting your work-related activity.
* **Per Diem Allowance**: The amount of Per Diem Allowance you are eligible for.
* **Per Diem Amount**: The total amount you are requesting for reimbursement.
* **Per Diem Expense**: A description of the expenses associated with your trip or activity.
* **Per Diem Rate**: The rate applicable to your trip or activity.
* **Per Diem Reason**: A brief description of the reason for your request.
* **Per Diem Agenda**: If you have an agenda for your trip or activity, please provide details about it on Page 2.
* **Per Diem Agenda Organizer**: If you have an agenda organizer for your trip or activity, please specify.
* **Per Diem Agenda Details**: If you have details about your agenda, please provide them.
* **Per Diem Agenda Date**: If you have a specific date for your agenda, please specify.
* **Per Diem Agenda Time**: If you have a specific time for your agenda, please specify.
* **Per Diem Agenda Location**: If you have a specific location for your agenda, please specify.
* **Per Diem Agenda Note**: If you have any additional notes about your agenda, please provide them.
* **Per Diem Agenda Organizer 2**: If you have an agenda organizer for your trip or activity, please specify.
* **Per Diem Agenda Note 2**: If you have any additional notes about your agenda, please provide them.
* **Per Diem Agenda Note 3**: If you have any additional notes about your agenda, please provide them.
* **Per Diem Agenda Note 4**: If you have any additional notes about your agenda, please provide them.

Note: This form must be completed in the correct manner to ensure that your request is processed correctly. If you are unsure about any field, please consult your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
