# Maintenance Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about maintenance reports from owners of various assets. This includes details about the asset's owner, location, purpose, equipment used, and work performed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the owner's information: Provide the name and title/position of the person completing the form.
2. Enter the asset's location and purpose: Specify the location where the asset is being used and its intended purpose.
3. Describe the work performed: Provide a brief description of the maintenance work done.
4. Specify the equipment used: List the equipment used during the maintenance work.
5. Record the work hours: Enter the total number of hours spent on the maintenance work.
6. Enter the work date and time: Provide the date and time the maintenance work was completed.
7. Add any additional comments or attachments: If necessary, add any additional comments or attach supporting documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **owner_info** (text, optional): This field is for the owner's name and title/position.
  - Enter the name of the asset's owner.
  - Include their title/position for reference.

- **owner_organization** (text, optional): This field is for the owner's organization/unit.
  - Enter the name of the asset's owner's organization or unit.

- **designation** (text, optional): This field is for the asset's designation or ID number.
  - If the asset has a unique designation or ID number, enter it here.

- **location** (text, optional): This field is for the asset's location.
  - Specify where the asset is being used.

- **purpose** (text, optional): This field is for the asset's intended purpose.
  - Briefly describe the asset's purpose.

- **equipment** (text, optional): This field is for the equipment used during maintenance.
  - List the tools or machines used for the maintenance work.

- **description** (text, optional): This field is for a brief description of the work performed.
  - Provide a short summary of the maintenance work done.

- **work_details** (text, optional): This field is for additional work details.
  - If necessary, add any additional comments or information about the work performed.

- **work_hours** (text, optional): This field is for the total hours spent on maintenance.
  - Enter the total number of hours spent on the maintenance work.

- **work_date** (date, optional): This field is for the date the maintenance was done.
  - Enter the date the maintenance was completed.

- **work_time** (time, optional): This field is for the time the maintenance was done.
  - Enter the time the maintenance was completed.

- **owner_signature** (text, optional): This field is for the owner's signature.
  - Sign here to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

**Tips**

* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* Use standard business format for dates and times.
* If you're unsure about any information, contact your authorized personnel.
* Make sure to keep the form for your records for future reference.
