# medical_reimbursement_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for medical reimbursement applications, which are typically submitted by patients or their representatives to request reimbursement for medical expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions in the "Form Instructions" section above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Medical Reimbursement Form** (`Medical Reimbursement Form`): Select if the medical procedure was performed (Yes/No).
    *   This field is used to indicate if the medical procedure was performed.
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter the patient's name.
    *   This field is used to enter the patient's name.
*   **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`): Select all medical procedures that were performed.
    *   This field is used to select all medical procedures that were performed.
*   **Medical Provider Name** (`medical_provider_name`): Enter the name of the medical provider who performed the procedure.
    *   This field is used to enter the name of the medical provider who performed the procedure.
*   **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`): Select if the patient has an insurance provider (Yes/No).
    *   This field is used to select if the patient has an insurance provider.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of the medical procedure.
    *   This field is used to enter the start date of the medical procedure.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of the medical procedure.
    *   This field is used to enter the end date of the medical procedure.
*   **Claim Status** (`claim_status`): Select the current claim status (Active/Inactive).
    *   This field is used to select the current claim status.
*   **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`): Enter the medical record number (if applicable).
    *   This field is used to enter the medical record number (if applicable).
*   **Billing Doctor** (`billing_doctor`): Select if the billing doctor is involved (Yes/No).
    *   This field is used to select if the billing doctor is involved.
*   **Facility Name** (`facility_name`): Enter the name of the facility where the procedure was performed.
    *   This field is used to enter the name of the facility where the procedure was performed.
*   **Claim Date** (`claim_date`): Enter the claim date.
    *   This field is used to enter the claim date.
*   **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`): Enter the claim amount.
    *   This field is used to enter the claim amount.
*   **Patient Address** (`patient_address`): Enter the patient's address (if applicable).
    *   This field is used to enter the patient's address (if applicable).
