# Factory Design Architect Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for Factory Design Architect professionals to gather information and contact details from clients. Fill out this form to send a query or provide feedback about a design project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a project from the dropdown menu, and enter the project's details in the "Project Details" field.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
3. You can upload files related to the project, if needed.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required):
	+ Enter a brief description of the project you are inquiring about.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required):
	+ Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **File Uploads** (`file_uploads`, text, required):
	+ You can upload files related to the project (e.g., project plans, photos, or 3D models).
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required):
	+ Click this button to send your message.
* **Custom Text 1** (`custom_text_1`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for additional comments or information (not required).
