# New Matter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about new matters, such as client details, matter information, case specifics, contact methods, and any additional notes. The form can be customized to fit your organization's specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing client information in the "Client Information" page.
2. In the "Matter Information" page, enter details about the matter.
3. Move on to the "Case Details" page and provide any relevant case-specific information.
4. On the "Contact Info" page, select one or more methods to contact the client (Email, Phone, or Fax).
5. Finally, add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Information** (`client_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's name, company, or any other relevant details.
- **Matter Information** (`matter_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the matter, including its nature and objective.
- **Case Details** (`case_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional case-specific information, such as the case type or status.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more methods to contact the client (Email, Phone, or Fax).
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the matter.

## Tips
- Make sure to keep the form concise and focused on the essential information.
- Use clear and legible writing for all fields.
- Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
- Save the form as a draft or submit it for review and approval.
- Customize the form as needed to fit your organization's specific requirements.
