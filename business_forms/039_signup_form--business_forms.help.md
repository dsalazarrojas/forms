# Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Signup Form is used for users to sign up and provide information about themselves and their company. This includes your name, email address, company name, role, phone number, website, and any additional notes. The information collected will be used to create or update a user account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please make sure to use the correct format (e.g. `username@example.com`).
4. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
5. Select your role from the list in the "Role" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter your website in the "Website" field.
8. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
9. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field by re-entering the same email address you entered in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Role**: Select your role (e.g. CEO, CTO, CMO).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Website**: Enter your website URL.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about yourself or your company.
* **Confirm Email**: Re-enter your email address to confirm it is correct.
