# Carrier Packet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Carrier Packet form is a document used by carriers to provide information about themselves and their operations. It is designed to gather relevant data that can be used for various purposes such as compliance, safety, and financial purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Carrier Packet form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out each section of the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is current and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Legal Name** (`company_legal_name`): Enter the name of your company as registered with the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA).
- **MC and DOT Number** (`mc_dot_number`): Enter your MC and DOT numbers for identification and verification purposes.
- **Authorized Contact Person** (`primary_contact`): Enter the name of the authorized contact person for your company.
- **Business Email Address** (`primary_email`): Enter your business email address where we can contact you.
- **Primary Business Phone** (`primary_phone`): Enter your primary business phone number.
- **Years in Business** (`years_in_operation`): Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
- **Total Number of Units** (`total_equipment_units`): Enter the total number of active trucks or units you are operating.
- **Fleet Specialty** (`fleet_specialty`): Select the main focus of your fleet (e.g., Flatbed, Dry Van, Reefer, Specialized, Intermodal).
- **States of Operation** (`states_authorized`): List all states where you are authorized to operate.
- **Operating Authority Document Available** (`authority_doc_status`): Indicate if your operating authority document is available.
- **Certificate of Insurance (COI) Available** (`insurance_cert_status`): Indicate if your COI is available.
- **Current W-9 Document Status** (`w9_doc_status`): Indicate if your current W-9 document is available.
- **Latest FMCSA Safety Rating** (`safstat_rating`): Select your carrier's safety rating from the available options (Satisfactory, Conditional, Unsatisfactory, Unrated).
- **Carrier Confirms No Major Safety Violations** (`accident_history_ack`): Indicate if you confirm that your carrier has not had any major safety violations in the last 12 months.
- **Interested in QuickPay** (`quickpay_interest`): Indicate if you are interested in the QuickPay option (1.5 to 5 percent accelerated payment).
- **Bank Account Type** (`bank_account_type`): Select the type of bank account you use (Checking, Savings, Business, Factor Account).
- **Factoring Company Name** (`factoring_company`): Enter the name of your factoring company (if applicable).
- **Packet Acknowledgment** (`packet_acknowledgment`): Confirm that all documents in this packet are current and accurate.
- **Digital Signature of Authorized Officer** (`digital_signature`): Enter your name as the digital signature of the authorized officer.
