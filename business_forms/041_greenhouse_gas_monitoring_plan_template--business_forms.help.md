# Greenhouse Gas Monitoring Plan Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Greenhouse Gas Monitoring Plan Template is a standardized form used to track and report greenhouse gas emissions from facilities. It ensures that emissions data is collected and reported in a consistent and reliable manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the facility name and address.
2. Select the type of gas to be monitored.
3. Choose the sampling frequency and method.
4. Enter the monitoring start and end dates.
5. Select the reporting frequency.
6. Enter the contact information for the monitoring team.
7. Add any additional remarks or comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility.
* **Gas Type** (`gas_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of gas to be monitored. Options: CO2, CH4, N2O, O3, Other.
* **Sampling Frequency** (`sampling_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the frequency of sampling. Options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly.
* **Sampling Method** (`sampling_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select the method of sampling. Options: Air Sample, Water Sample, Soil Sample.
* **Monitoring Start Date** (`monitoring_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of monitoring in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Monitoring End Date** (`monitoring_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of monitoring in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Reporting Frequency** (`reporting_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the frequency of reporting. Options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly.
* **Monitoring Location** (`monitoring_location`, text, optional): Enter the location name or facility ID.
* **Gas Emissions Value** (`gas_emissions_value`, number, optional): Enter gas emissions values in units (e.g., tons CO2-eq/year).
* **Monitoring Team** (`monitoring_team`, text, optional): Enter contact information for the monitoring team.
* **Reporting Responsible Party** (`reporting_responsible_party`, text, optional): Enter contact information for the party responsible for reporting.
* **Reporting Date** (`reporting_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of reporting in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, optional): Enter the facility address.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter a 10-digit phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, optional): Optional remarks.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Optional comments.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Enter signature.
* **Certification** (`certification`, text, optional): Enter certification.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of review in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Title** (`reviewer_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Contact** (`reviewer_contact`, text, optional): Enter contact information for the reviewer.
* **Review Date-Time** (`review_date_time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of review in YYYY-MM-DD format.
