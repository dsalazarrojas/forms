# Business Performance Modeling Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from business requestors to create a business performance model that addresses their specific needs and requirements. The model will help requestors make data-driven decisions and improve business performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details: * Requestor Name * Email Address * Phone Number * Company Name
2. Specify your performance modeling requirements: * Select the number of employees in your company * Choose your industry * Identify the metrics you already track * Select how much historical data you have available
3. Define your performance modeling objectives: * Choose the reasons you want to achieve with performance modeling * Identify specific questions you want the model to answer
4. Determine your performance modeling scope: * Select the areas of your business that need modeling * Identify any other specific performance metrics
5. Specify your timeline and budget: * Choose how soon you need the model * Select your available budget for the project

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Name**: Your full name.
* **Email Address**: Your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Company Name**: The name of your organization.
* **Company Size**: The number of employees in your company.
	+ 1 to 10
	+ 11 to 50
	+ 51 to 200
	+ 201 to 500
	+ 500 plus
* **Industry**: Your primary industry.
	+ Technology
	+ Healthcare
	+ Finance
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Retail
	+ Professional services
	+ Other
* **Request Date**: The date you are submitting this request.
* **Modeling Objectives**: The reasons you want to achieve with performance modeling.
	+ Revenue forecasting
	+ Cost optimization
	+ Resource planning
	+ Risk assessment
	+ Scenario analysis
	+ Growth planning
	+ Investment analysis
	+ Operational efficiency
* **Current Metrics Available**: The metrics you currently track.
	+ Revenue
	+ Profit margins
	+ Customer acquisition cost
	+ Customer lifetime value
	+ Employee productivity
	+ Operational costs
	+ Cash flow
	+ Inventory turnover
	+ None currently
* **Data Availability**: The amount of historical data you have available.
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1 to 2 years
	+ 2 to 5 years
	+ 5 plus years
	+ No historical data
* **Key Business Drivers**: What drives your business performance.
* **Modeling Scope**: The areas of your business that need performance modeling.
	+ Financial performance
	+ Sales performance
	+ Operational performance
	+ Marketing performance
	+ HR performance
	+ Customer performance
	+ Full business model
* **Specific Questions to Answer**: Any specific questions the model should answer.
* **Timeline Expectations**: When you need the model.
	+ Within 2 weeks
	+ Within 1 month
	+ Within 3 months
	+ Within 6 months
	+ Flexible
* **Target Completion Date**: The desired completion date of the model (optional).
* **Budget Range**: The available budget for the project (optional).
* **Key Stakeholders**: Who will use the model.
* **Technical Capability**: How comfortable your team is with performance modeling.
	+ Very comfortable - advanced users
	+ Comfortable - intermediate users
	+ Somewhat comfortable - basic users
	+ Not comfortable - need training
* **Software Preferences**: Your preferred tools or platforms.
	+ Excel
	+ Google Sheets
	+ Tableau
	+ Power BI
	+ Python
	+ R
	+ Custom solution
	+ No preference
* **Documentation Needs**: The type of documentation you need.
	+ User guide
	+ Technical documentation
	+ Training materials
	+ Video tutorials
	+ Executive summary
	+ Minimal documentation
* **Ongoing Support Needs**: Whether you need ongoing support.
	+ Yes - regular updates
	+ Yes - occasional support
	+ Yes - one-time training
	+ No - self-sufficient
* **Additional Requirements**: Any other requirements you have.
