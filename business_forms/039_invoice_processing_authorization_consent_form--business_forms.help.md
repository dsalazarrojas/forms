# invoice_processing_authorization_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about the authorized business that will be processing invoices on behalf of the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, enter the **Business Name** of the authorized business in the text field.
2. On the second page, select the status of the authorized business from the dropdown list. You can choose between "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the business is authorized.
3. If the business is authorized, enter their **Authorized Business Address**, **Authorized Business Phone**, and **Authorized Business Email** in the text fields.
4. Enter the **Invoice Frequency** in the text field, which indicates how frequently the business processes invoices.
5. Enter the **Invoice Amount** in the numeric field.
6. Optionally, enter any additional notes about the authorized business in the **Authorized Business Notes** field.
7. Optionally, enter any additional comments about the client in the **Client Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the authorized business in this field.
* **Authorized Business Name**: Select "Yes" if the business is authorized, or "No" otherwise.
* **Authorized Business Address**: Enter the address of the authorized business in this field.
* **Authorized Business Phone**: Enter the phone number of the authorized business in this field.
* **Authorized Business Email**: Enter the email address of the authorized business in this field.
* **Invoice Frequency**: Enter the frequency at which the business processes invoices, e.g., weekly, monthly, etc.
* **Invoice Amount**: Enter the amount of the invoices processed by the business.
* **Authorized Business Notes**: Enter any additional comments about the authorized business in this field (optional).
* **Client Notes**: Enter any additional comments about the client in this field (optional).
