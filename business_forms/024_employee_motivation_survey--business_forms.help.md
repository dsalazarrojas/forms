# Employee Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Motivation Survey" is a tool used to gather information about employee motivation and identify areas for improvement in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the first field.
2. Answer the three "What motivates you?" questions with a brief description.
3. Choose how often you receive recognition, feedback, and opportunities from the provided options.
4. If desired, provide any additional information about what would motivate you more.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: This is the field where you can enter your name as an employee.
* **What motivates you?** (question_1, text): This is an open-ended question where you can describe what motivates you as an employee.
* **What motivates you?** (question_2, text): This is another open-ended question to gather more information about your motivations.
* **What motivates you?** (question_3, text): This is the third open-ended question to help us understand your motivations better.
* **How often do you receive recognition?** (question_4, select_one): Choose how often you receive recognition, which can be very often, sometimes, or rarely.
* **How often do you receive feedback?** (question_5, select_one): Select how often you receive feedback, which can be very often, sometimes, or rarely.
* **How often do you receive opportunities?** (question_6, select_one): Choose how often you receive opportunities, which can be very often, sometimes, or rarely.
* **What would motivate you more?** (question_7, text): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information about what would motivate you more.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any trouble understanding a question, feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Remember, the purpose of this survey is to help us better understand your needs and improve the workplace experience for everyone.
