# 1099 NEC Tax Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects tax-related information from both the contractor (payee) and payer (company) sides of a payment transaction. It includes details such as contractor and payer contact information, payment amounts and methods, and certification from the preparer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contractor's information, including name, address, phone number, and tax ID number.
2. Enter the payer's information, including company name, contact name, address, and EIN number.
3. Select the tax year for reporting and the non-employee compensation amount paid.
4. Choose the primary payment type and select all payment methods used.
5. Answer whether backup withholding was applied and if the payment will be reported to the IRS on Form 1099-NEC.
6. Finally, enter the certification section, including the preparer's name, email, and signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contractor (Payee) Information**
	+ Full name of contractor: Enter the legal name of the individual or business owner.
	+ Business name / DBA (if applicable): Leave blank if not applicable.
	+ Contractor SSN or EIN: Enter SSN (XXX-XX-XXXX) or EIN (XX-XXXXXXX).
	+ Contractor mailing address: Enter the street, city, state, and ZIP.
	+ Contractor email address: Enter the email address used for confirmation and follow-up.
	+ Contractor phone number: Enter the mobile or business contact number.
* **Payer (Company) Information**
	+ Payer (company) name: Enter the company name that paid the contractor.
	+ Payer contact name: Enter the primary contact for questions about this payment.
	+ Payer mailing address: Enter the street, city, state, and ZIP.
	+ Payer EIN: Enter the Employer Identification Number.
* **Payment & Reporting Details**
	+ Tax year for reporting: Enter the four-digit year (e.g., 2025).
	+ Non-employee compensation: Enter the total amount paid, in USD.
	+ Date of last payment in tax year: Enter the date of the most recent payment reported.
	+ Primary payment type: Select the category that best describes the payments (e.g., Contract labor).
	+ Payment methods used (select all that apply): Choose all methods used to pay the contractor (e.g., Check, ACH / Wire transfer).
	+ Was backup withholding applied?: Select Yes if backup withholding was withheld on these payments.
	+ Will this payment be reported to the IRS on Form 1099-NEC?: Select Yes if the payer will file the form.
* **Certification & Preparer Information**
	+ Name of person completing this form: Enter the full name of the preparer or authorized representative.
	+ Preparer email address: Enter the email address for follow-up questions.
	+ Electronic signature (type full name to certify): Type your name to certify the information is accurate.
	+ Date signed: Enter the date the preparer signed this form.
	+ Additional comments or instructions: Use this field for clarifications, special payment details, or notes.

## Tips
Please fill out the form carefully and accurately. If you have any questions or need help, contact us for assistance.
