# Returning Client Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Returning Client Form is used by the business operations team to collect information from clients about their returning engagements. This form helps to gather data on client's name, email address, phone number, previous engagement, client's service provider, and whether they want to update their details or not.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's name in the text field.
2. Enter the client's email address in the email field. **Please note that this field is required.**
3. Provide the client's phone number in the text field.
4. Answer the question about the client's previous engagement.
5. Select the client's service provider from the dropdown list.
6. Decide whether the client wants to update their details or not in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_name**: Enter the client's full name in this field.
* **email_address**: Enter the client's email address. **Please note that this field is required.**
* **phone_number**: Enter the client's phone number in this format: XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **previous_engagement**: Describe the client's previous engagement with our business. This field is used to track their history with us.
* **client_service_provider**: Select the client's service provider from the list. This field helps us to identify the client's service provider.
* **update_details**: If the client wants to update their details, type 'Yes'. Otherwise, type 'No'.
