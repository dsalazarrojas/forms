# package_delivery_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The package delivery authorization form is used to document and track package delivery details, ensuring accuracy and efficiency in the package shipping process.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out this form carefully and thoroughly, providing accurate information for each field.
- Review and validate information with relevant stakeholders before submitting the form.
- Avoid duplication by ensuring that the same form is not filled out for the same package more than once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **package_details** (`package_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the package being delivered, including its contents, dimensions, or any special handling instructions.
* **recipient_details** (`recipient_details`, text, required: false): Provide the recipient's name, address, and any relevant contact information, such as phone number or email.
* **delivery_date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Enter the expected delivery date for the package, following the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **package_weight** (`package_weight`, number, required: false): Enter the weight of the package in kilograms (kg) or pounds (lbs).
* **delivery_time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): Enter the expected delivery time for the package, following the standard time format (HH:MM:SS).
* **delivery_location** (`delivery_location`, text, required: false): Enter the delivery location, including the city, state, or country.
* **shipping_service_provider** (`shipping_service_provider`, select_one, required: false): Choose the shipping service provider used for this delivery, such as USPS, UPS, or FedEx.
* **shipping_service_provider_options** (`shipping_service_provider_options`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any additional services or options used for this shipment, such as insurance, signature upon delivery, or express shipping.
* **shipping_service_provider_2** (`shipping_service_provider_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any additional services or options used for this shipment, such as tracking, express shipping, or signature upon delivery.
* **shipping_service_provider_3** (`shipping_service_provider_3`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any additional services or options used for this shipment, such as insurance, signature upon delivery, or express shipping.

## Tips

* When in doubt, refer to internal standards and best practices for package delivery procedures.
* Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the package delivery team or shipping coordinator for guidance.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals to access the package and shipping information.
