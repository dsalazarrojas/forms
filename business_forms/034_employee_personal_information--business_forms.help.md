# Employee Personal Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Personal Information" form is used to collect personal details about an employee, which can be utilized for various internal purposes such as HR management, benefits administration, or performance tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter your date of birth using the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter your physical address in the "Address" field.
8. Enter the name of the city where you live in the "City" field.
9. Enter the name of the state where you reside in the "State" field.
10. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field to enter your last name.
* **Job Title**: A text field to enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Date of Birth**: A date field in the format MM/DD/YYYY to enter your date of birth.
* **Email**: An email field to enter your email address.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number.
* **Address**: A text field to enter your physical address.
* **City**: A text field to enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **State**: A text field to enter the name of the state where you reside.
* **Zip**: A text field to enter your zip code.

Note: All fields are optional unless specified as required in the field description.
