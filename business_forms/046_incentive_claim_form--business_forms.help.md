# Incentive Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incentive Claim Form is used for employees to submit claims for incentives they have earned. This form helps to track and verify employee incentives, ensuring accurate and timely processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with \*. Ensure you have the necessary information before submitting the form.
2. Enter your company address in the Company Address field.
3. Enter your name and manager's name in the respective fields.
4. Select your employee status (Active, Inactive, Unknown, Retired, Resigned, or Deceased).
5. Enter the claim date and claim amount.
6. Provide details about your claim in the Claim Details field.
7. Select the claim type (Bonus, Stock, Profit-Sharing, Stock Options, or Stock Options and Bonus).
8. Enter the claim value.
9. Enter your company email and phone number, if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Address** (text): Enter your company's address.
* **Employee Name** (text): Enter your full name.
* **Claim Date** (date): The date when the incentive was earned.
* **Claim Amount** (number): The amount of the incentive.
* **Claim Details** (text): Briefly describe the reason for your claim.
* **Manager Name** (text): Enter your manager's full name.
* **Manager Phone** (text): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Employee Status** (select_one):
	+ **Active**: You are currently an active employee.
	+ **Inactive**: You are not an active employee.
	+ **Unknown**: Your status is unknown.
	+ **Retired**: You are retired.
	+ **Resigned**: You have resigned from the company.
	+ **Deceased**: You are deceased.
* **Claim Reason** (text): Briefly describe the reason for your claim.
* **Company Name** (text): Enter your company's name.
* **Department** (text): Enter your department.
* **Claim Type** (select_one):
	+ **Bonus**: A bonus-based incentive.
	+ **Stock**: A stock-based incentive.
	+ **Profit-Sharing**: A profit-sharing incentive.
	+ **Stock Options**: Stock options-based incentive.
	+ **Stock Options and Bonus**: A combination of stock options and bonus-based incentives.
* **Claim Value** (number): The value of the claim.
* **Company Email** (email): Enter your company's email address.
* **Company Phone** (text): Enter your company's phone number.
