# tax_reconciliation_disclosure_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "tax_reconciliation_disclosure_consent_form" is a form used to capture a client's disclosure consent and tax information for reconciliation purposes. It is designed to gather essential details about a client's tax status, reported address, phone number, consent, reported tax year, reported tax period, tax amount, revised amount, tax deductions, revised deductions, tax refund, reconciliation refund, reconciliation fee, tax reported tax refund, reported tax deductions, reported tax amount, reported tax fee, and reported tax status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form completely.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
3. Review your answers carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_name**: Enter the name of the client.
* **phone**: Enter the phone number of the client.
* **reported_address**: Enter the reported address of the client.
* **physically_resides**: Select 'True' if the client physically resides in the reported address, 'False' otherwise.
* **consent**: Select 'True' if the client has given consent, 'False' otherwise.
* **reported_year**: Enter the reported tax year.
* **reported_period**: Select the reported tax period from the options provided.
* **reported_tax_amount**: Enter the reported tax amount.
* **revised_amount**: Enter the revised tax amount.
* **tax_deductions**: Select 'True' if the client has tax deductions, 'False' otherwise.
* **revised_deductions**: Enter the revised tax deductions.
* **revised_tax_deductions**: Enter the revised tax deductions.
* **revised_tax_refund**: Enter the revised tax refund.
* **revised_tax_reconciliation_refund**: Enter the revised tax reconciliation refund.
* **revised_tax_reconciliation_fee**: Enter the revised tax reconciliation fee.
* **tax_refund**: Enter the reported tax refund.
* **reconciliation_refund**: Enter the reported tax reconciliation refund.
* **reported_tax_refund**: Enter the reported tax refund.
* **reported_tax_deductions**: Select 'True' if the client has reported tax deductions, 'False' otherwise.
* **reported_tax_amount**: Enter the reported tax amount.
* **reported_tax_fee**: Enter the reported tax fee.
* **reported_tax_status**: Select the reported tax status from the options provided.
* **reported_tax_reported_tax**: Select 'True' if the client has reported tax, 'False' otherwise.
