# Postpaid Service Ownership Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Postpaid Service Ownership Change Form is used to document and facilitate the transfer of ownership of a postpaid service from one party to another.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the transfer date in the "Transfer Date" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
2. Enter the transfer time in the "Transfer Time" field in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
3. Provide information about the current owner in the "Current Owner Info" field.
4. Enter information about the new owner in the "New Owner Info" field.
5. Describe the service being transferred in the "Service Info" field.
6. Enter the new owner's contact information in the "New Owner Contact" field.
7. Enter the preferred transfer date in the "Preferred Transfer Date" field, if applicable.
8. Enter the preferred transfer time in the "Preferred Transfer Time" field, if applicable.
9. Enter the service provider's name in the "Service Provider" field.
10. Enter the service provider's email address in the "Service Provider Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Transfer Request**:
	+ A text field for entering any additional account transfer request information.
* **Transfer Date**:
	+ A date field for entering the transfer date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Transfer Time**:
	+ A time field for entering the transfer time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Current Owner Info**:
	+ A text field for providing information about the current owner.
* **New Owner Info**:
	+ A text field for providing information about the new owner.
* **Service Info**:
	+ A text field for describing the service being transferred.
* **New Owner Contact**:
	+ A text field for entering the new owner's contact information.
* **Preferred Transfer Date**:
	+ A date field for entering the preferred transfer date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Preferred Transfer Time**:
	+ A time field for entering the preferred transfer time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Service Provider**:
	+ A text field for entering the name of the service provider.
* **Service Provider Email**:
	+ An email field for entering the service provider's email address.
* **New Owner Address**:
	+ A text field for entering the new owner's address.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete transfer of ownership.
* Double-check the information provided to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the service provider for assistance.
* This form is for internal use only and should not be shared with unauthorized parties.
