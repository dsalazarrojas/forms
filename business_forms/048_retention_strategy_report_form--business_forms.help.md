# Retention Strategy Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retention Strategy Report Form is designed to help employees gather and share information essential for developing and improving customer retention strategies. This form serves as a platform to collect and document various aspects of customer interaction, satisfaction, and data analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
* Fill in each field as per the instructions provided.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use the provided options and hints to aid in your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Retention Strategy** (`Retention Strategy`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the retention strategy being discussed or proposed.
* **Customer Retention** (`customer_retention`, number, optional): If applicable, enter a numerical value or ratio representing the customer's level of retention.
* **Customer Base** (`customer_base`, text, optional): Describe the customer base, including demographics or any other relevant details.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, number, optional): If applicable, enter a numerical value or ratio representing the customer's satisfaction level.
* **Data Sources** (`data_sources`, select_multiple, optional): Check all sources that apply from the list provided, such as 'Yes' for data sources you've used or 'No' for sources you haven't.
* **Analysis** (`analysis`, text, optional): Describe the analysis conducted or proposed, including any insights or findings.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, select_one, optional): Check 'Yes' if you have recommendations or suggestions for improvement or 'No' otherwise.
* **Implementation** (`implementation`, text, optional): Describe the proposed or implemented strategy for addressing customer retention.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, date, optional): Enter the date for any follow-up activities or actions taken.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Describe the next steps for customer retention, including any proposed actions or activities.
* **Customer Base Notes** (`customer_base_notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about the customer base.
* **Customer Satisfaction Notes** (`customer_satisfaction_notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about customer satisfaction.
* **Retention Strategy Notes** (`retention_strategy_notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about the retention strategy.
* **Retention Strategy Recommendations** (`retention_strategy_recommendations`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about retention strategy recommendations.
* **Implementation Notes** (`implementation_notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about implementation.
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about follow-up activities.
* **Next Steps Notes** (`next_steps_notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about next steps.
* **Retention Strategy Form Notes** (`retention_strategy_form_notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about the form itself.
* **Data Sources Notes** (`data_sources_notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about data sources.
* **Analysis Notes** (`analysis_notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about analysis.
* **Recommendations Notes** (`recommendations_notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about recommendations.
* **Retention Strategy Form Comments** (`retention_strategy_form_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the form.
* **Data Sources Comments** (`data_sources_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about data sources.
* **Analysis Comments** (`analysis_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about analysis.
* **Recommendations Comments** (`recommendations_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about recommendations.
