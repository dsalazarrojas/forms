# Governance Review Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Governance Review Appointment Form is a tool for scheduling and documenting business meetings, consultations, or other types of appointments for governance-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for scheduling a consultation.
2. Fill in the "Organization" field with the name of the organization or company related to the meeting.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the meeting in the respective "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
4. Enter the time of the meeting in the "Time" field.
5. Provide the location of the meeting in the "Location" field.
6. Add any additional note about the meeting in the "Note" field.
7. Enter the email address of the person to be contacted in the "Email" field.
8. Enter the phone number of the person to be contacted in the "Phone" field.
9. Select the tool assigned to handle the meeting from the options provided in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule A Consultation** (`schedule_consultation`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" to schedule a consultation or "No" if it's not required.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization or company related to the meeting.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the meeting.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the meeting.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional note about the meeting.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person to be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person to be contacted.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Select the tool assigned to handle the meeting from the options provided.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all the required fields before submitting.
* Double-check the information provided before finalizing your submission.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the support team.
