# Anna Pugh Made To Match Hire Wish List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Anna Pugh Made To Match Hire Wish List" form is designed to collect information from customers about their event, including personal details and specific items they wish to hire.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the form to begin.
2. Enter your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
3. Select the event date and location.
4. Choose the items you wish to hire from the list provided.
5. Add any custom requests or additional comments you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address, e.g., `example@example.com`.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of your event using the calendar picker.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the venue name or address.
* **Select Items for Hire** (`wish_list_items`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the items that apply to your wish list from the provided list.
* **Custom Requests** (`custom_requests`, text, optional): Add any specific items or variations you are looking for.
* **Delivery Preference** (`delivery_preference`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to receive the items: Delivery and Setup, Delivery Only, or Pick up from Warehouse.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other information that you think we should know.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you have selected all the correct items from the wish list.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
