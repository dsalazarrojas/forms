This form is intended for businesses and individuals to provide contact information and project details to potential smart energy equipment providers. The form will help providers assess and respond to leads in a structured and organized manner. 

This form will guide users through the process of submitting a lead, providing all necessary information for the provider to understand the project requirements and provide accurate quotes or responses.

Please note that the form is not a commitment, and submitting a lead does not guarantee a response or a project. The form is designed to facilitate communication and provide a convenient way for providers to gather information about potential projects.

# Smart Energy Equipment Provider Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help smart energy equipment providers gather contact information and project details from potential leads. It allows providers to assess and respond to leads in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the lead details section with your name, company, and contact information.
2. Provide a contact person and their email address and phone number.
3. Specify the project requirements and details.
4. Answer the project location question (Yes/No).
5. Identify the lead qualifier (Yes/No).
6. Choose the project status (Active/Inactive).
7. Identify the lead owner.
8. Provide a description of the lead.
9. Enter any additional project details.
10. Choose the project deadline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Lead Details
* **Lead Details** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): This section is intended to collect your name and contact information. Please provide your name and company name as well as your phone number and email address.

### Contact Person
* **Contact Person** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide the name of the contact person who will be handling the project.

### Contact E-mail
* **Contact E-mail** (`email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

### Project Requirements
* **Project Requirements** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the project requirements.

### Project Location
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please answer Yes or No to indicate if the project location is on-site or off-site.

### Lead Qualifier
* **Lead Qualifier** (`lead_qualifier`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Identify if the project is a qualifier or not by selecting Yes or No.

### Project Status
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the project status by selecting Active or Inactive.

### Lead Owner
* **Lead Owner** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Identify the owner of the lead.

### Lead Description
* **Lead Description** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a description of the lead.

### Project Details
* **Project Details** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional project details.

### Project Deadline
* **Project Deadline** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the project deadline in a date format.

### Additional Notes
* **Additional Notes** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the project.
