# AF Membership Cancellation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you cancel your AF membership. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide the necessary information for a smooth cancellation process. Please note that your cancellation will be processed in accordance with our membership terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To cancel your AF membership, please fill out this form carefully and completely. Make sure to provide all the required information, including your name, membership ID or account number, email address, and other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your membership records.
- **Membership ID or account number** (`membership_id`, text, required): Enter the alphanumeric ID from your membership documents.
- **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): We will send a cancellation confirmation to this address. Make sure to enter a valid email address to receive important updates.
- **Phone number** (`phone_number`, phone, optional): If you have a phone number, please include your country code for international numbers.
- **Mailing address** (`mailing_address`, text, optional): Enter your street address, city, state or province, and postal code for our records.
- **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If you are providing your date of birth, please ensure it matches the one on your membership records.
- **Current membership tier** (`membership_type`, select_one, required): Select the tier that matches your current membership level as shown on your account.
- **Membership start date** (`membership_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date your membership began.
- **Last payment amount** (`last_payment_amount`, decimal, optional): If you are requesting a refund, enter the amount of your last payment.
- **Date of last payment** (`last_payment_date`, date, optional): If you are requesting a refund, enter the date of your last payment.
- **Requested cancellation effective date** (`cancellation_effective_date`, date, optional): If you want your cancellation to take effect on a specific date, enter it here. If left blank, your cancellation will be processed immediately.
- **Primary reason for cancellation** (`cancellation_reason`, select_one, required): Choose the reason that best fits your decision to cancel.
- **Additional reasons** (`additional_reasons`, select_multiple, optional): Select any other factors that influenced your decision to cancel.
- **Request refund for unused period?** (`refund_requested`, select_one, required): Choose whether you are requesting a refund for the unused period of your membership.
- **Refund amount requested** (`refund_amount_requested`, decimal, optional): If you are requesting a refund, enter the amount you are requesting.
- **Preferred refund method / account details** (`refund_method_details`, text, optional): If you are requesting a refund, enter your preferred bank account, PayPal email, or refund instruction.
- **I confirm I want to cancel my AF membership and understand the terms** (`consent_and_confirmation`, select_one, required): Selecting "Yes, I confirm" constitutes an electronic signature.
- **Would you consider rejoining in the future?** (`would_you_consider_returning`, select_one, optional): This will help us tailor retention offers.
- **Preferred contact method for follow-up** (`preferred_contact_for_followup`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up purposes.
- **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments or notes that might help us improve or process your request.
- **Staff processing notes** (`staff_use_only`, note, internal): This field is for staff use only and should not be edited by members.
