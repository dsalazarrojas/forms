# Global Market Strategy Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Market Strategy Program Application is designed to gather information from potential candidates about their interests, qualifications, and experience for the Global Market Strategy Program. This program is intended for individuals who are interested in developing their skills and knowledge in market research, business development, and strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with the requested information. Please note that all fields are optional, except where noted as required. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the program administrators for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`1`): This is where you can provide your personal details, such as your name, job title, and company name. This information helps us understand who you are and how you fit into the context of the program.
* **Contact Details** (`2`): Please provide your contact details, including phone and email. This ensures that we can get in touch with you for further information.
* **Program Interest** (`3`): Choose the program area you are interested in (Strategy, Market Research, or Business Development). This helps us understand your motivation for applying.
* **Program Type** (`4`): Select the type of program you are interested in (In-House or Partnered). This determines the type of program you will be eligible for.
* **Company Size** (`5`): Select your company size category. This helps us understand your business context.
* **Company Industry** (`6`): Choose the industries your company operates in (Technology, Finance, Healthcare, Retail, or Other). This helps us understand your business focus.
* **Company Website** (`7`): Please provide your company website. This helps us verify your identity.
* **Job Title** (`8`): Provide your job title. This helps us understand your role in the company.
* **Company Address** (`9`): Provide your company address. This helps us understand your location.
* **Resume** (`10`): Please attach your resume or CV. This helps us understand your qualifications and experience.
* **Job Description** (`11`): Briefly describe your job description. This helps us understand your role.
* **Salary** (`12`): If requested, please provide your current salary. This helps us understand your compensation.
* **Email** (`13`): Please provide your email address. This helps us ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`14`): Provide your phone number. This ensures we can contact you.
* **Confirm Email** (`15`): Re-enter your email address for verification purposes. 
* **Confirm Phone** (`16`): Re-enter your phone number for verification purposes. 
* **Submit** (`17`): Please click submit once you have completed all necessary fields.
* **Agreement** (`18`): Please agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Company Name** (`19`): Please provide your company name. This helps us understand your affiliation with the company.
* **Location** (`20`): Please provide your location. This helps us understand your geographical context.
* **Job Type** (`21`): Select your job type (Full Time, Part Time, or Freelance). This helps us understand your employment status.
* **Job Status** (`22`): Select your job status (Active or Inactive). This helps us understand your current employment status.
* **Job Experience** (`23`): If requested, please provide your job experience.
* **Experience Years** (`24`): Please provide your experience years if requested. This helps us understand your work history.
* **Education** (`25`): Please describe your education background.

Note: The order of fields may change based on program requirements and updates. Always refer to the official program application page for the latest information.
