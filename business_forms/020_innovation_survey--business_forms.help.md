# Innovation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your perception of innovation within your department. Your input will help us understand your thoughts and concerns, and inform decisions to drive improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form by providing your responses to each question.
* Each question is optional, but your participation is greatly appreciated.
* Please answer each question based on your genuine experience and perception.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Innovation Survey (form_1)**:
	+ This is an optional text field where you can provide any comments or suggestions related to innovation within your department.
* **Question 1 (form_2)**:
	+ Select 'Yes' if you believe your department is innovative, 'No' if you have concerns.
* **Question 2 (form_3)**:
	+ Select 'Yes' if you see opportunities for innovation, 'No' if you don't.
* **Question 4 (form_4)**:
	+ This field is for a date, but its exact purpose is unclear. Please provide the date you think best represents your answer to the question.
* **Question 5 (form_5)**:
	+ This field is for a time, but its exact purpose is unclear. Please provide the time you think best represents your answer to the question.
* **Question 6 (form_6)**:
	+ This field is for a note, where you can provide any additional information you think is relevant to your answer.
* **Question 7 (form_7)**:
	+ This field is for an email address, but its exact purpose is unclear. Please provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Question 8 (form_8)**:
	+ This is an optional text field where you can provide any further comments or thoughts.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate with your responses.
* Your participation is greatly appreciated, and your input will help shape our department's future decisions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out.
