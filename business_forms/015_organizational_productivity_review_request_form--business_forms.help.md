# Organizational Productivity Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help team members reflect on their work and productivity, and to identify areas for improvement. It helps them set goals and focus areas that will help them stay on track and meet expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please ensure you have a clear understanding of what you're looking for in a review. Review the form carefully and make sure you're not duplicating an existing review request.
*   Complete each section thoroughly and honestly, as this will help the reviewer gain a complete understanding of your work and challenges.
*   Be specific when answering questions and providing details about your focus areas and goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Review Form** (1/10): This section should be a brief summary of the areas you'd like to be reviewed. Please provide a clear and concise overview of your work and the areas you'd like to focus on.
*   **Requester** (2/10): In this section, please provide your name and any other relevant contact information that the reviewer may need to get in touch with you.
*   **Reviewee** (3/10): In this section, please provide the name of the team member or group you'd like to be reviewed. Be sure to spell their name correctly to ensure the correct person receives the review.
*   **Focus Areas** (4/10): This select multiple box allows you to identify specific areas where you'd like the review to focus. Please select all relevant focus areas, as these will help the reviewer understand the key challenges and goals you're trying to achieve.
*   **Preferred Timeline** (5/10): Choose when you'd like the review to take place, whether you prefer it to be done ASAP, or if you have a specific date in mind.
*   **Review Goal** (6/10): In this section, please provide a clear and specific goal you'd like to achieve during the review. Be sure to include any relevant metrics or targets you'd like to reach.
*   **Review Focus** (7/10): This field allows you to provide a more detailed explanation of what you'd like to focus on during the review. Please be as specific as possible.
*   **Review Timeline** (8/10): Choose the date by which you'd like the review to take place.
*   **Reviewer Comments** (9/10): This is the space for you to provide any additional comments or information that the reviewer should know before starting the review.
*   **Review Status** (10/10): Please select the status of your review, whether it's Active or Inactive.
*   **Reviewer Name** (11/10): In this section, please provide the name of the person conducting the review.
