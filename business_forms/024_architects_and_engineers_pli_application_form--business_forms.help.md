# Architects And Engineers PLI Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for Architects And Engineers to provide information about their firm's details and experience in the architecture and engineering industry. It gathers firm information such as firm name, address, number of employees, and annual revenue, as well as professional details like primary discipline, specializations, and number of licensed professionals. It also asks about claims history and coverage information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your firm's name and address in the **Firm Name** and **Firm Address** fields, respectively.
2. Select the correct **Firm Type** (Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, or Other).
3. Enter the year your firm was established.
4. Enter the number of employees in your firm.
5. Select your firm's **Primary Discipline** (Architecture, Engineering, Structural engineering, MEP engineering, Landscape architecture, or Other).
6. Choose your firm's **Specializations** (Commercial, Residential, Industrial, Healthcare, Education, Hospitality, or Mixed-use).
7. Enter the number of licensed professionals in your firm.
8. Answer whether you have had **Professional liability claims in the past 5 years**.
9. Answer whether you have any **pending claims or lawsuits**.
10. If you have claims, provide a brief description in the **Description of Claims** field.
11. Select whether you **currently have professional liability insurance**.
12. If you do, enter your desired **Coverage Amount** (250K, 500K, 1M, 2M, 5M, or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address (e.g., [user@example.com](mailto:user@example.com)).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
* **Firm Name**: Enter your firm's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Firm Address**: Enter your firm's street address and city, state, and zip code.
* **Firm Type**: Select the type of firm you represent (Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, or Other).
* **Year Firm Established**: Enter the year your firm was established.
* **Number of Employees**: Enter the total number of people employed in your firm.
* **Primary Discipline**: Select the main field of practice for your firm (Architecture, Engineering, Structural engineering, MEP engineering, Landscape architecture, or Other).
* **Specializations**: Select the areas where your firm specializes (Commercial, Residential, Industrial, Healthcare, Education, Hospitality, or Mixed-use).
* **Number of Licensed Professionals**: Enter the total number of licensed professionals in your firm.
* **Professional liability claims in past 5 years**: Answer whether you have had any professional liability claims in the past 5 years.
* **Any pending claims or lawsuits**: Answer whether you have any pending claims or lawsuits.
* **Description of Claims**: Provide a brief description of any claims or lawsuits if applicable.
* **Currently have professional liability insurance**: Answer whether you currently have professional liability insurance.
* **Desired Coverage Amount**: If you have insurance, select the desired coverage amount.
