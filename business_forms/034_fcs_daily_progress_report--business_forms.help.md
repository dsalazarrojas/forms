# fcs_daily_progress_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fcs_daily_progress_report form is used to record daily activities, progress, and comments for business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the report in the "fcs_daily_progress_report" field.
2. Select the date of the report using the calendar icon.
3. Enter the time of the report using the time picker.
4. Describe your progress in the "form_progress" field.
5. Add any comments or observations in the "form_comments" field.
6. Check if the report is assigned to someone in the "form_assigned_to" field.
7. Enter any phone numbers in the "form_phone" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **fcs_daily_progress_report (Title)** (<name>, <type>, required/optional>): Enter a brief title for the report, describing the activity or task being reported.
* **form_date (Date)** (<name>, <type>, required/optional>): Select the date of the report using the calendar icon.
* **form_time (Time)** (<name>, <type>, required/optional>): Choose the time of the report using the time picker.
* **form_progress (Progress)** (<name>, <type>, required/optional>): Describe your progress in detail, including any challenges faced or successes achieved.
* **form_comments (Comments)** (<name>, <type>, required/optional>): Add any comments or observations related to the report.
* **form_assigned_to (Assigned to)** (<name>, <type>, required/optional>): Check if the report is assigned to someone, if yes, enter their name.
* **form_phone (Phone)** (<name>, <type>, required/optional>): Enter any phone numbers (if applicable).
