# Home Staging Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Home Staging Checklist Form is a tool used to gather information about a property's address, state, and date of inspection. This form is intended to be completed by a specific user group (e.g., home inspectors, property managers) to record and track relevant details about a property. It does not appear to be designed for public use or as a public-facing form, as the required fields (such as state and zip code) are not necessarily required for public completion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field according to your knowledge or information about the property.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your completed form for any errors or missing fields before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address Line 1** (`address_field`, text, required: false): Enter the first line of the property's address.
* **City** (`city_field`, text, required: false): Enter the city where the property is located.
* **State** (`state_field`, select_one, required: false): Select the state where the property is located from the list of available options. Note that while this field is not explicitly required, the selected state will be used as an attribute of the property.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, required: false): Enter the property's zip code.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_field`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the property's inspection. This field is used to track the date when the inspection was conducted.
