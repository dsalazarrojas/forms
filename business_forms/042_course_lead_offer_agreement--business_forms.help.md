# Course Lead Offer Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Course Lead Offer Agreement form is used to document the terms and agreements between a course lead and the provider of an online course. It ensures that both parties are on the same page regarding the course lead's obligations and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review and discuss the terms of the lead offer with your partner (course provider) to ensure you both have a clear understanding of the agreements.
2.  Fill out the form with the agreed-upon terms, making sure to complete all required fields.
3.  Ensure that you have obtained signatures from both parties for the terms to be effective.
4.  Store the completed form for future reference and compliance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Define Lead Offer Terms** (`lead_offer_terms`, text, optional): This field is where you define the terms of the lead offer, including any specific details or conditions agreed upon with your partner.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): This field is for the date when the signature was obtained, marking the agreement's effective date.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): This field is for the signature of the course lead, confirming their agreement to the terms.
*   **Partner Signature** (`partner_signature`, date, optional): This field is for the signature of the partner, confirming their agreement to the terms.
*   **Course Creation Date** (`course_creation_date`, date, optional): This field is for the date when the course was created, which can be important for tracking and record-keeping purposes.
*   **Lead Creation Date** (`lead_creation_date`, date, optional): This field is for the date when the lead was created, which can be important for tracking and record-keeping purposes.
*   **Course Creation Date 2** (`course_creation_date_2`, date, optional): This field is for an additional course creation date, which can be used for tracking changes or updates to the course.

## Tips

*   Be sure to keep the form up-to-date and accurate as terms change or are updated.
*   Store the completed form securely and in accordance with your organization's data retention policies.
*   Review the form with your partner regularly to ensure the terms remain mutually understood and accepted.
