# Supply Chain Digitalization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Chain Digitalization Registration Form is designed to collect data on the digitalization state of your supply chain management. This form is used to gather information on various aspects of digital transformation in the supply chain, including registration information, digital transformation programs, and qualifying criteria.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the correct options for digital transformation programs and qualifying criteria.
3. Choose a registration date for your digitalization efforts.
4. Add any additional comments or notes, if necessary.
5. Review your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information (1)** (`supply_chain_registration_form`, text, required): This section captures the fundamental details about your organization's supply chain digitalization efforts.
* **Organization name (2)** (`organization_name`, text, required): Please enter your organization's name accurately.
* **Organization address (3)** (`organization_address`, text, required): Enter the address of your organization for better contact and reference.
* **Contact Person (4)** (`contact_person`, text, required): Provide the contact person's name and any additional details.
* **Phone (5)** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number for future communication.
* **Email (6)** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Digital Transformation Programs (7)** (`digital_transformation_programs`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you have any digital transformation programs running, 'No' otherwise.
* **Qualifying Criteria (8)** (`qualifying_criteria`, select_multiple, required): Choose the qualifying criteria that apply to your organization.
* **Registration Date (9)** (`registration_date`, date, required): Choose a date for your digitalization efforts' registration.
* **Notes (10)** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for reference.
* **Assigned Tool (11)** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If there's a specific tool assigned to your digitalization efforts, enter its name.
* **Form IDs (12)** (`form_ids`, text, optional): Enter any form IDs related to your digitalization registration.
* **Category (13)** (`category`, text, optional): Enter the category of your digitalization efforts, if applicable.
* **Description (14)** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your digitalization efforts.
* **Output File (15)** (`output_file`, text, optional): If you have an output file related to your digitalization efforts, enter its name.
* **Category 2 (16)** (`category_2`, text, optional): Enter the category of your digitalization efforts, if applicable.
* **Title (17)** (`title`, text, optional): Provide a title for your digitalization efforts.
* **Assigned Tool 2 (18)** (`assigned_tool_2`, text, optional): If there's a specific tool assigned to your digitalization efforts, enter its name.
* **Category 3 (19)** (`category_3`, text, optional): Enter the category of your digitalization efforts, if applicable.
* **Description 2 (20)** (`description_2`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your digitalization efforts.
* **Title 2 (21)** (`title_2`, text, optional): Provide a title for your digitalization efforts.
* **Form IDs 2 (22)** (`form_ids_2`, text, optional): Enter any form IDs related to your digitalization registration.
* **Assigned Tool 3 (23)** (`assigned_tool_3`, text, optional): If there's a specific tool assigned to your digitalization efforts, enter its name.
* **Category 4 (24)** (`category_4`, text, optional): Enter the category of your digitalization efforts, if applicable.
* **Form ID (25)** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter any form ID related to your digitalization registration.

## Tips
When filling out this form, make sure to:

* Double-check the accuracy of your information.
* Use clear and concise language when providing descriptions.
* Be precise with dates and categories.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask for clarification.

Please ensure you've read this guide carefully and understand the form before proceeding with registration.
