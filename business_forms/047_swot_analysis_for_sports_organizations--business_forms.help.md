# SWOT Analysis For Sports Organizations - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SWOT analysis is a strategic planning tool that helps sports organizations identify their internal and external strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats. By filling out this form, you'll be able to:
* Identify your organization's internal and external strengths and weaknesses
* Recognize potential opportunities for growth and development
* Anticipate and prepare for potential threats

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. For each section, provide a brief description of your sports organization's:
	* Internal strengths
	* Weaknesses
	* Opportunities
	* Threats
2. For each of these sections, consider both internal and external factors that impact your organization.
3. Be as specific as possible with your answers, providing concrete examples or examples from past experiences.
4. Review each section carefully to ensure that you've covered all relevant points.
