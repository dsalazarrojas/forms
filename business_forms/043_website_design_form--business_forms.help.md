# Website Design Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Website Design Form is designed to gather essential information from customers about their website design projects. This form serves as a crucial starting point for the design team to understand the project requirements and deliver a high-quality outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category for your website design project.
2. Enter a brief description of your project.
3. Choose your preferred payment method (if applicable).
4. Add any additional project details.
5. Provide your name and contact information (name, email, and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (2)**: Select the type of form this project belongs to, either "Business Forms" or "Personal Forms".
* **Description (3)**: Enter a brief description of your website design project, including its goals, objectives, and any specific requirements.
* **Payment In Advance (4)**: If you have a preferred payment method or would like to pay in advance, select the corresponding option.
* **Project Details (5)**: Add any additional project details or notes that will help the design team understand your requirements.
* **Customer Name (6)**: Enter your name as the customer or project owner.
* **Customer Email (7)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Customer Phone (8)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any project updates or clarifications.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure the design team can create an accurate and effective website.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary information is provided.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for further assistance.
