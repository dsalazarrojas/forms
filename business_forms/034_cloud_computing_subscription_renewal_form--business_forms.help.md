# Cloud Computing Subscription Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cloud Computing Subscription Renewal Form is used to renew your subscription to Cloud Computing services. It captures necessary information for the renewal process, ensuring a smooth transition to the new subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read and fill out each section of the form.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, required): Enter your company's legal name.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, required): Provide the existing account or subscription number.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact`, required): Enter the full name of your main contact.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required): Enter the email address for renewal correspondence.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter your phone number for renewal inquiries.
* **Current Subscription Details** (`section_current`, optional): Provide information about your existing subscription.
* **Current Cloud Plan or Service** (`current_plan`, required): Enter the name of your current subscription.
* **Current Monthly Cost** (`current_monthly_cost`, required): Enter the amount in dollars or your local currency.
* **Current Number of Users** (`current_users`, required): Enter the number of users on your current plan.
* **Current Storage Allocation** (`current_storage`, optional): Enter the storage allocation for your current plan in GB or TB.
* **Current Renewal or Expiration Date** (`current_renewal_date`, required): Enter the date of your current subscription's renewal or expiration.
* **Renewal Plan Details** (`section_new`, optional): Describe what you want for the new subscription.
* **Are you Renewing the Same Plan?** (`renew_same_plan`, required): Choose whether you're renewing the same plan, upgrading, or downgrading.
* **New Cloud Plan or Service** (`new_plan`, optional): If different from current, enter the new plan or service.
* **Number of Users Needed** (`new_users`, required): Enter the number of users needed for the new subscription.
* **Storage Allocation Needed** (`new_storage`, optional): Enter the storage allocation needed for the new subscription in GB or TB.
* **Additional Features or Add-ons Needed** (`additional_features`, optional): Select additional features or add-ons needed for the new subscription.
* **Payment Information** (`section_payment`, optional): Provide billing and payment details.
* **Preferred Billing Cycle** (`billing_cycle`, required): Choose your preferred billing cycle (Monthly, Quarterly, Annual, or Multi-year).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, required): Choose your payment method (Credit card, Bank transfer, Purchase order, Invoice, or Other).
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, required): Enter your full billing address.
* **Renewal Preferences** (`section_preferences`, optional): Describe your preferences for the renewal.
* **Enable Auto-Renewal** (`auto_renewal`, required): Choose whether you want to enable auto-renewal.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`renewal_notes`, optional): Enter any specific requirements for the renewal.
* **Agree to the Renewal Terms and Conditions** (`agreed_to_terms`, required): Confirm your agreement to the renewal terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submission.
- Ensure all required fields are completed.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your Cloud Computing representative for assistance.
