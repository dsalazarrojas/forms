# Franchise Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who are interested in learning more about our franchise opportunities. It allows us to collect their contact information and understand their reasons for inquiring about our franchise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields and select the appropriate options where applicable. Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can best contact and assist you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your personal name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to receive communication from us.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your company name or business name if applicable.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who will be in touch with us.
* **Email (Business)** (`email_business`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for the contact person.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact number of the person who will be in touch with us.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the website of your business if applicable.
* **Franchise Name** (`franchise_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the franchise you are interested in.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of your business or where you are located.
* **Reason Inquiring** (`reason_inquiring`, `text`, required/optional): Tell us why you are inquiring about our franchise opportunities.
* **How Did You Find Us** (`how_did_you_find_us`, `text`, required/optional): Describe how you found out about our franchise opportunities.
* **Inquiry Method** (`inquiry_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how you are inquiring about our franchise opportunities. Options: 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can best contact and assist you.
* If you have any further questions or would like to discuss our franchise opportunities, please contact us at [insert contact information].
