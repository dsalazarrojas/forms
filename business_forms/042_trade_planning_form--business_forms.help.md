# Trade Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Planning Form is used to collect information for trade planning and decision-making in business settings. Fill out this form to gather insights and data for strategic trades, ensuring you have all necessary details to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Trade Planning Form, follow these steps:

* Fill out all the required fields (indicated by a * symbol)
* Enter your trade planning details in the **Trade Planning Form Entry** field (Field 1)
* Specify your target for this trade in the **Trade Planning Form Target** field (Field 2)
* Set your stop loss percentage for this trade in the **Trade Planning Form Stop Loss** field (Field 3)
* Choose the size of your trade in the **Trade Planning Form Size** field (Field 4)
* Provide a brief rationale for this trade in the **Trade Planning Form Rationale** field (Field 5)
* Specify the discipline for this trade in the **Trade Planning Form Discipline** field (Field 6)
* Review and update your trade plan in the **Trade Planning Form Review** field (Field 7)
* Choose data-driven factors for this trade in the **Trade Planning Form Data-Driven Decision Making** field (Field 8)
* Select the discipline for this trade (Field 9)
* Select one stop loss for this trade (Field 10)
* Choose the size for this trade (Field 11)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trade Planning Form Entry** (`trade_planning_form_entry`, text, optional): Enter a description or details about this trade.
* **Trade Planning Form Target** (`trade_planning_form_target`, number, optional): Enter your target value for this trade.
* **Trade Planning Form Stop Loss** (`trade_planning_form_stop_loss`, number, optional): Enter your stop loss percentage for this trade.
* **Trade Planning Form Size** (`trade_planning_form_size`, number, optional): Choose the size of your trade.
* **Trade Planning Form Rationale** (`trade_planning_form_rationale`, text, optional): Briefly explain the reasoning behind this trade.
* **Trade Planning Form Discipline** (`trade_planning_form_discipline`, text, optional): Select one discipline for this trade.
* **Trade Planning Form Review** (`trade_planning_form_review`, text, optional): Review and update your trade plan.
* **Trade Planning Form Data-Driven Decision Making** (`trade_planning_form_data_driven_decision_making`, text, optional): Choose data-driven factors for this trade.
* **Trade Planning Form Discipline 2** (`trade_planning_form_discipline_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple disciplines for this trade.
* **Trade Planning Form Stop Loss 2** (`trade_planning_form_stop_loss_2`, select_one, optional): Select one stop loss for this trade.
* **Trade Planning Form Size 2** (`trade_planning_form_size_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple sizes for this trade.

Note: Fields marked as `optional` can be left blank, but it's recommended to fill them out for better trade planning.
