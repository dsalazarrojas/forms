# Unconditional Waiver & Release On Progress Payment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain an unconditional waiver and release from clients or parties involved in a progress payment agreement, acknowledging that they waive any claims or liabilities related to the payment.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  **Client Details**: Provide the client's information, including their name, contact details, and any relevant notes.
2.  **Waiver and Release**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the client agrees to the waiver and release.
3.  **Employee Injuries**: If the client has suffered any injuries, please select the type of injuries (Physical, Emotional, or Both).
4.  **Employee Agent Injuries**: If the client has suffered injuries through an employee agent or subordinate, select the type of injuries (Physical, Emotional, or Both).
5.  **Employee Injuries Description**: Provide a brief description of the injuries suffered by the client or employee.
6.  **Form Signed By**: Enter the name of the person signing the form.
7.  **Date Signed**: Enter the date the form was signed.
8.  **Form Version**: Enter the version number of the form.
9.  **Employee Signature**: Sign the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Details**: Enter the name and contact information of the client.

*   **Employee Injuries**: If applicable, select the type of injuries suffered by the employee.
*   **Employee Agent Injuries**: If applicable, select the type of injuries suffered by the employee agent or subordinate.
*   **Employee Injuries Description**: Provide a brief description of the injuries suffered by the employee.
*   **Form Signed By**: Enter the name of the person signing the form.
*   **Date Signed**: Enter the date the form was signed.
*   **Form Version**: Enter the version number of the form.
