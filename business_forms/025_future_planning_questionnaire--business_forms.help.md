# future_planning_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information on your future plans and goals, including career and personal aspirations, financial support, risk tolerance, and self-care habits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. For questions with multiple options, choose the option that best describes your current thoughts and feelings.
3. If you're unsure about any question, respond based on your best understanding.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Page 1):
	+ **Introduction** (Note type): This section is an open-ended note field where you can provide an introduction to your future plans and goals.
* **Goals** (Page 2):
	+ **Goals** (Select Multiple): Indicate which goals you have for your future, such as career goals or personal aspirations.
* **Time Frame** (Page 3):
	+ **Time Frame** (Select One): Choose whether you expect your goals to be achieved within a short-term or long-term timeframe.
* **Priority Level** (Page 4):
	+ **Priority Level** (Select One): Indicate the level of priority you assign to your goals, such as High, Low, or other.
* **Time Commitment** (Page 5):
	+ **Time Commitment** (Select Multiple): Indicate how much time you are committed to achieving your goals.
* **Risk Tolerance** (Page 6):
	+ **Risk Tolerance** (Select Multiple): Indicate your level of risk tolerance for achieving your goals.
* **Financial Support** (Page 7):
	+ **Financial Support** (Select Multiple): Indicate whether you need financial support to achieve your goals.
* **Career Goals** (Page 8):
	+ **Career Goals** (Select Multiple): Indicate which career goals you have, such as advancing in your career or changing careers.
* **Personal Relationships** (Page 9):
	+ **Personal Relationships** (Select Multiple): Indicate which personal relationships are important for achieving your goals.
* **Personal Identity** (Page 10):
	+ **Personal Identity** (Select Multiple): Indicate which personal qualities or characteristics you value for achieving your goals.
* **Self-Care** (Page 11):
	+ **Self-Care** (Select One): Indicate whether you prioritize self-care for achieving your goals.
* **Self-Care Goals** (Page 12):
	+ **Self-Care Goals** (Select Multiple): Indicate which self-care goals you have, such as practicing mindfulness or exercising regularly.
* **Self-Care Commitment** (Page 13):
	+ **Self-Care Commitment** (Select Multiple): Indicate how committed you are to self-care practices.
* **Self-Care Support** (Page 14):
	+ **Self-Care Support** (Select Multiple): Indicate which self-care activities you need support for, such as seeking help from a therapist.
* **Self-Care Identity** (Page 15):
	+ **Self-Care Identity** (Select Multiple): Indicate which self-care practices help you feel good about yourself, such as meditation or journaling.

Please note that this is a general guidance and you may not be able to complete all pages of the form. If a question does not apply to you, please mark it as "No" or skip it.
