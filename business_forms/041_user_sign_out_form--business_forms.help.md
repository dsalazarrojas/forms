# User Sign Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The User Sign Out Form is designed to help employees sign out of the system for a variety of reasons. This form will be used to track and manage employee sign outs, ensuring that the process is smooth and efficient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reasons for your sign out using the "Sign Out Reasons" field.
2. Confirm your sign out using the "Confirmation" field.
3. Provide the time you will sign out using the "Sign Out Time" field.
4. Provide the date of your sign out using the "Sign Out Date" field.
5. Confirm your sign out status using the "Sign Out User" field.
6. Add any additional information using the "Notes" field.
7. Specify how often you sign out using the "Sign Out Frequency" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sign Out Reasons**: Select the reasons for your sign out.
	+ Choose one or more options from the list of available reasons.
* **Confirmation**: Confirm your sign out to ensure that you have reviewed the form accurately.
	+ Select 'True' to confirm your sign out.
* **Sign Out Time**: Provide the time you will sign out.
	+ Use the format HH:MM.
* **Sign Out Date**: Provide the date of your sign out.
	+ Use the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Sign Out User**: Confirm your sign out status.
	+ Select 'True' to confirm your sign out status.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about your sign out.
	+ Provide a brief description.
* **Sign Out Frequency**: Specify how often you sign out.
	+ Choose one or more options from the list of available frequencies.
