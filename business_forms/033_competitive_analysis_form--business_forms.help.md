# Competitive Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Competitive Analysis Form is designed to gather essential data about competitors in the market. This form helps in understanding the company's competitive profile, including their target audience, revenue range, core competitive advantage, distribution channels, recent news or strategic moves, pricing model, website and social media presence, and a brief SWOT analysis summary. By completing this form, you can gain valuable insights into competitors' strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about your competitor.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field, ensuring that you choose the best description of the data or information you have about the competitor.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled in as they play a crucial role in providing a comprehensive competitive analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Competitor Company Name** (`competitor_company_legal_name`):
	* This field is required and asks for the full legal name of the competing entity.
	* Please fill in the exact name as it appears in public records or official documents.

### 2. **Headquarters Location** (`competitor_hq_location`):
	* This field is not required but helps in understanding the geographical presence of the competitor.
	* Enter the city and country where the competitor's headquarters is located.

### 3. **Primary Target Audience** (`primary_target_audience_comp`):
	* This field is required and asks for the specific audience the competitor is trying to sell to.
	* Be as specific as possible, providing details about their target market or customer base.

### 4. **Estimated Annual Revenue** (`estimated_annual_revenue_range`):
	* This field is required and asks for the estimated annual revenue of the competitor.
	* Choose the revenue range based on publicly available data or estimates.

### 5. **Core Competitive Advantage** (`core_competitive_advantage_text`):
	* This field is required and asks for what makes the competitor unique in the market.
	* Briefly describe their competitive edge or unique selling proposition.

### 6. **Distribution Channels Used** (`distribution_channels_multi`):
	* This field is required and asks for the distribution channels the competitor uses.
	* Select all that apply from the list to describe how they reach their customers.

### 7. **Recent News or Strategic Moves** (`recent_competitor_news`):
	* This field is not required but helps in understanding the competitor's recent strategies.
	* Provide any significant news or moves made by the competitor.

### 8. **Product Pricing Model** (`product_pricing_model_type`):
	* This field is required and asks for how the competitor charges their customers.
	* Select the pricing model from the provided options.

### 9. **Website and Social Media Presence Rating** (`digital_presence_rating`):
	* This field is required and asks for the quality of the competitor's online engagement.
	* Rate their website and social media presence as excellent, good, average, or poor.

### 10. **SWOT Summary (Brief)** (`swot_summary_brief`):
	* This field is required and asks for a brief summary of each SWOT category.
	* Identify one key factor for each category.

### 11. **Date Collected** (`competitor_analysis_date`):
	* This field is required and asks for the date when this analysis was collected.
	* Select today's date for accurate tracking of when the data was collected.
