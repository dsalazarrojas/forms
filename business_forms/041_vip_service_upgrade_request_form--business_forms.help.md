# Vip Service Upgrade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vip Service Upgrade Request Form is designed to gather necessary information from customers for their VIP service upgrade requests. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review your input carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the international format (e.g., `+1 1234567890`).
* **VIP Package** (`vip_package`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred level of service, which can be Basic, Premium, or VIP.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any specific requirements for your upgrade, describe them here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or details about your request.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your company or organization's address in the correct format.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where your company or organization is located.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter the country where your company or organization is located.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your company or organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your company or organization.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or details about your request.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Be as detailed as possible in the comments section for a smoother handling process.
* If you need any assistance or clarification during the process, reach out to our support team.
