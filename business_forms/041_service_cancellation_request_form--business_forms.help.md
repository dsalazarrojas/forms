# Service Cancellation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the cancellation of a service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Carefully read the instructions and purpose of the form.
2. Select a reason for cancellation from the available options in the "Reason for Cancellation" field.
3. Enter the desired cancellation date in the "Cancellation Date" field.
4. Enter the desired cancellation time in the "Cancellation Time" field.
5. If necessary, provide additional information about your cancellation reason in the "Reason for Cancellation Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`Reason for Cancellation`, `select_multiple`, **required**): 
  Please select a reason for cancellation from the available options. Options include:
  - Cancellation Due to Dissatisfaction
  - Cancellation Due to Unreliability
  - Cancellation Due to Miscommunication
  - Other
* **Cancellation Date** (`Cancellation Date`, `date`, **required**): 
  Enter the date on which you would like the service to be cancelled.
* **Cancellation Time** (`Cancellation Time`, `time`, **optional**): 
  Enter the time on which you would like the service to be cancelled.
* **Reason for Cancellation Details** (`Reason for Cancellation Details`, `text`, **optional**): 
  Provide additional information about your cancellation reason, if necessary.
