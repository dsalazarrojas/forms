# System Upgrade Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for system upgrade quotations from customers. It is an internal tool for authorized personnel to gather details about system upgrades.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. On the first page, provide a brief description of the system upgrade details.
2. On the second page, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the system upgrade is desired.
3. Enter the customer's details on the third page, including their email, phone number, name, company, position, job title, address, and any additional notes.
4. On the fourth page, enter the upgrade date, start time, and end time.
5. On the fifth page, enter the total hours, labor costs, equipment costs, material costs, and any other costs related to the upgrade.
6. On the final page, add any additional comments or remarks about the upgrade.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **system_upgrade_details** (`system_upgrade_details`, text): Briefly describe the system upgrade details.
- **system_upgrades** (`system_details`, select_one): Select "Yes" to indicate the system upgrade is desired.
- **customer_details** (`customer_info`, text): Enter the customer's email, phone number, name, company, position, job title, address, and any additional notes.
- **upgrade_date** (`upgrade_date`, date): Enter the date of the system upgrade.
- **start_time** (`start_time`, time): Enter the start time of the system upgrade.
- **end_time** (`end_time`, time): Enter the end time of the system upgrade.
- **total_hours** (`total_hours`, number): Enter the total hours required for the system upgrade.
- **labor_costs** (`labor_costs`, select_one): Select "Yes" to indicate if labor costs are included.
- **equipment_costs** (`equipment_costs`, number): Enter any equipment costs related to the upgrade.
- **material_costs** (`material_costs`, number): Enter any material costs related to the upgrade.
- **other_costs** (`other_costs`, number): Enter any other costs related to the upgrade.
- **comments** (`comments`, note): Add any additional comments or remarks about the upgrade.
- **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, text): Enter the tool or equipment assigned to the customer.
- **form_id** (`form_id`, text): Enter a unique form ID (not required for customer use).
- **category** (`category`, text): Enter the category of the upgrade (e.g., hardware, software, etc.).
- **description** (`description`, text): Enter a brief description of the upgrade.
- **customer_email** (`customer_email`, email): Enter the customer's email address.
- **customer_phone** (`customer_phone`, text): Enter the customer's phone number.
- **customer_name** (`customer_name`, text): Enter the customer's name.
- **customer_company** (`customer_company`, text): Enter the customer's company name.
- **customer_position** (`customer_position`, text): Enter the customer's position.
- **customer_job_title** (`customer_job_title`, text): Enter the customer's job title.
- **customer_address** (`customer_address`, text): Enter the customer's address.
- **customer_notes** (`customer_notes`, text): Enter any additional customer notes.
- **customer_signature** (`customer_signature`, text): Enter the customer's signature.
