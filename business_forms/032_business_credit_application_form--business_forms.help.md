# Business Credit Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Credit Application Form is used by businesses to apply for credit facilities such as lines of credit, term loans, equipment financing, and working capital loans. The form collects information about the business, its owner, and the credit request to help the lender make an informed decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the business name and federal tax ID (EIN) to identify the business.
2. Select the type of business (e.g., sole proprietorship, partnership, corporation, etc.).
3. Enter the number of years the business has operated.
4. Enter the owner's name and social security number for personal guarantee.
5. Enter the owner's phone number and email address for direct contact.
6. Enter the ownership percentage (if the business is not fully owned by the applicant).
7. Select the type of credit requested (e.g., line of credit, term loan, etc.).
8. Enter the credit amount requested.
9. Enter the purpose of the credit.
10. Enter the repayment terms in months.
11. Enter the annual revenue and profit for the last 12 months.
12. Enter the total business assets and liabilities.
13. (Optional) Enter the existing credit lines and collateral available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Enter the legal name of the business.
* **Business Type**: Select the type of business (e.g., sole proprietorship, partnership, corporation, etc.).
* **Federal Tax ID (EIN)**: Enter the Employer Identification Number.
* **Years in Business**: Enter the number of years the business has operated.
* **Owner/Principal Name**: Enter the full legal name of the owner.
* **Owner Social Security Number**: Enter the social security number for personal guarantee.
* **Owner Phone Number**: Enter the direct contact number of the owner.
* **Owner Email Address**: Enter the business email address.
* **Ownership Percentage**: Enter the percentage of ownership.
* **Type of Credit Requested**: Select the type of credit requested (e.g., line of credit, term loan, etc.).
* **Credit Amount Requested**: Enter the total amount of credit needed.
* **Purpose of Credit**: Enter the reason for the credit request.
* **Requested Repayment Terms**: Enter the preferred repayment terms in months.
* **Annual Revenue**: Enter the revenue for the last 12 months.
* **Annual Net Profit**: Enter the net profit for the last 12 months.
* **Total Business Assets**: Enter the total value of the business assets.
* **Total Business Liabilities**: Enter the total debt/liabilities of the business.
* **Existing Credit Lines**: (Optional) Enter the current credit facilities.
* **Collateral Available**: (Optional) Enter the assets available for security.

**Tips**

* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the information entered to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the lender for assistance.
