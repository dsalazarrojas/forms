# Lash Studio Franchise Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Studio Franchise Application Form is a tool used to collect information from potential franchisees who are interested in joining the Lash Studio franchise family. It is essential to provide accurate and honest answers to ensure a smooth evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date details.
3. Submit the completed form to the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Franchise Application Form Data** (`franchise_application_form_data`, text, optional): This field is used to collect any additional information you would like to share with Lash Studio about your application. You can provide a brief description of your business or any other relevant details.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): In this field, please provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. This helps Lash Studio reach out to you for further evaluation or discussion.
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, text, optional): If you have a physical store, please provide your store's operating hours in this field. This information will be used to understand your store's availability and schedule.
* **Business License** (`business_license`, text, optional): If you have a business license, please upload or provide a scan of it in this field. This helps Lash Studio verify your business credentials.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, text, optional): In this field, enter the physical address of your store, including the city and state where you plan to operate.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of your store, which should match your business name.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, optional): Enter the physical address of your store, including the street address, apartment number, or suite number.
* **Store City** (`store_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where your store is located.
* **Store Postal Code** (`store_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter the postal code of your store's location.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your store.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy.
* If you have any attachments or documents, please upload them separately and reference them in the relevant fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will be in touch with you shortly after submitting this form.
