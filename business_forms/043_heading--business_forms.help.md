# Heading - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a simple request form for customers to inquire about babysitting services. It is used to gather information about the customer's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to enter your details in the required fields.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **babysitting_request_form** (`1`, `number`, required: false): This is the main field for the form. It is used to collect information about the customer's specific request for babysitting services.
* **parent_name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect the name of the parent or guardian responsible for the child.
* **phone_number** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect the contact phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **goals** (`goals`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect any specific goals or requests the customer may have for the babysitting service.
