# Maritime Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maritime Incident Report Form is designed to collect information about maritime incidents that occur on vessels or in ports. This form is intended for use by anyone involved in or witnessing an incident, as well as those responsible for investigating and resolving the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have read and understood the form purpose.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about the incident.
3. Provide a detailed description of the incident, including the date and time it occurred.
4. Specify the location where the incident occurred.
5. Describe any damage caused by the incident.
6. Provide contact information for the person who witnessed or was involved in the incident.
7. Specify the status of the incident (e.g., Open, Closed).
8. Add any additional notes about the incident.
9. Confirm your name and contact information as the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date**: The date of the incident. Select the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: The time of the incident. Select the time of day when the incident occurred.
* **Location**: The place where the incident occurred. Enter the location in text.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Type**: The type of incident that occurred. Select "Fire" if the incident was a fire, or "Other" if it was another type of incident.
* **Damage Description**: Describe any damage caused by the incident. Enter the details of the damage in text.
* **Damage Amount**: The amount of damage caused by the incident. Enter the monetary value of the damage in numerical form.
* **Contact Person**: Name of the contact person for the incident. Enter the name of the person responsible for the incident or who witnessed it.
* **Contact Info**: Contact information for the incident. Enter any necessary contact details.
* **Incident Status**: The status of the incident. Select "Open" if the incident is ongoing, or "Closed" if it has been resolved.
* **Notes**: Additional notes about the incident. Enter any relevant details.
* **Form Submitter**: Name of the person submitting the form. Confirm your name.
* **Submitter Info**: Contact information for the person submitting the form. Enter your contact details.
* **Incident Report Date**: The date the form was submitted. This field will be automatically filled in.
* **Submit Date**: The date the form was submitted. This field will be automatically filled in, but can be overridden if necessary.
* **Form Version**: The version of the form being used. This field will be automatically filled in.
* **Form UUID**: The unique identifier for the form being submitted. This field will be automatically filled in.
*   **Notes**: This is an optional field where you can add additional information about the incident. 

Please note that all fields marked as "required" must be filled out before the form can be submitted. The remaining fields are optional and can be filled out at your discretion. 

If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or the incident investigation team. 

You can submit this form as many times as necessary, as it will be automatically saved each time it's submitted. 

Please ensure that you have permission to submit this form before doing so. 

Thank you for taking the time to submit this incident report form. Your promptness and accuracy will aid the efficient resolution of the incident.
