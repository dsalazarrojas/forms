# solar_referral_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for referrals from healthcare providers to other healthcare providers. It's intended to be used for various referral purposes, including new and existing patient visits, and billing inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the necessary information regarding the patient and the referral purpose.
2. Enter the contact information of the healthcare provider who is referring the patient.
3. Provide the medical information of the patient.
4. Select the desired appointment time and date.
5. Choose the contact purpose from the options provided.
6. Enter any additional follow-up information, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**:
	+ `contact_info` (`text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the healthcare provider who is referring the patient.
* **Patient Info**:
	+ `patient_info` (`text`, optional): Enter the patient's name and any other relevant information.
* **Medical Info**:
	+ `medical_info` (`text`, optional): Enter the patient's medical information, such as diagnosis, treatment, or any other relevant details.
* **Appointment Time**:
	+ `appointment_time` (`date`, optional): Select the desired appointment time for the referral.
* **Appointment Date**:
	+ `appointment_date` (`date`, optional): Select the desired appointment date for the referral.
* **Referral Info**:
	+ `referral_info` (`text`, optional): Enter any additional referral information, such as the patient's condition or the type of care needed.
* **Doctor Info**:
	+ `doctor_info` (`text`, optional): Enter the doctor's name and contact information who is referring the patient.
* **Contact Purpose**:
	+ `contact_purpose` (select multiple, optional): Select the reason for contacting the other healthcare provider, such as "Contact for new patient visit" or "Contact for billing".
* **Follow Up Info**:
	+ `follow_up_info` (`text`, optional): Enter any additional follow-up information, such as next steps or follow-up appointments.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Use the form for its intended purpose, which is referrals between healthcare providers.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
