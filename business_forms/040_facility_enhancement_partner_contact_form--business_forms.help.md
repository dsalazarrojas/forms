# Facility Enhancement Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Enhancement Partner Contact Form is a straightforward tool designed to collect essential contact information from interested partners.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the fields according to the instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form 1** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): This is the first page of the form. It is not required to be filled out but is optional.
* **project_details** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Select one of the available options to specify the project details.
	+ Please choose the correct project details that best match your needs.
* **Form 2** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): This is the second page of the form. It is not required to be filled out but is optional.
* **phone** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): This is a text field where you can enter your phone number.
	+ Please enter your phone number in the required format (XXX-XXX-XXXX).
* **Form 3** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): This is the third page of the form. It is not required to be filled out but is optional.
* **message** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide to support your contact.
	+ This is a text box where you can type any additional information about your interest in the project.
* **Form 4** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): This is the fourth page of the form. It is not required to be filled out but is optional.
* **email** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
	+ Please enter a real email address where you can be reached.
* **Form 5** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): This is the last page of the form. It is not required to be filled out but is optional.
* **date** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Select a date from the available options.
	+ Please choose a date for the project timeline.
