# Visitor Center Vendor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for vendors to apply to be listed in the Visitor Center's directory. Fill it out to increase your chances of successful listing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with your most up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to review your answers for accuracy.
3. Check the required fields.
4. Submit the form once you've completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Details
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, optional): 
    This field is for your personal details, which could be your name, address, etc.
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): 
    This field is for your contact information, such as phone number, email, etc.
### Business Information
* **Business Information** (`business_info`, text, optional): 
    This field is for your business information, such as business description, etc.
### Social Media Accounts
* **Social Media Accounts** (`social_media`, text, optional): 
    This field is for your social media profiles, such as Facebook, Twitter, etc.
### Type of Products
* **Type of Products** (`products`, select_multiple, optional): 
    Select all the types of products your business offers (e.g., 'Yes' for products you offer and 'No' for products you don't).
### Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): 
    This field is for you to agree or acknowledge the terms and conditions of listing in the Visitor Center's directory.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
