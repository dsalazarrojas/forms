# Slime Shop - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Slime Shop form is a crucial tool for collecting customer information to provide tailored services or products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Birth Year in the Birth Year field.
4. Enter your Tormead Year in the Tormead Year field.
5. Select your Can Buy option, either True or False, in the Can Buy field.
6. Enter your Email in the Email field.
7. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
8. If applicable, add notes in the Note field.
9. You may leave the Form ID and Assigned Tool fields blank if not provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your given name, for example, John.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your surname, for example, Doe.
* **Birth Year** (`Birth Year`, number, required): Enter the year you were born.
* **Tormead Year** (`Tormead Year`, number, required): Enter the year the Tormead event occurred (assuming Tormead is an event related to Slime Shop's context).
* **Can Buy** (`Can Buy`, select_multiple, required): Select True if you are eligible to buy from the Slime Shop, False otherwise.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address in a format like name@example.com.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Add any additional information if you feel it's necessary for your interaction with the Slime Shop.
* **Form ID** (`Form ID`, number, optional): If provided, enter a unique identifier for this form submission.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, text, optional): If provided, enter the tool assigned to you for this form submission.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- The Can Buy field is crucial in determining the products/services you are eligible for.
- The Email and Phone fields will be used for communication and potential follow-up actions.
