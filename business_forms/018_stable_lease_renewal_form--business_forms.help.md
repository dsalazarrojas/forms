# Stable Lease Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stable Lease Renewal Form is used to gather information about the lease renewal, such as the lease status, start and end dates, lease terms, and contact information for both the landlord and tenant.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the lease status from the available options.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the lease.
3. Enter the lease terms, such as the lease term and length.
4. Enter the contact information for the landlord, including their name, address, phone number, and email address.
5. Enter the contact information for the tenant, including their name, address, phone number, and email address.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments about the lease.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lease Status**: Select the current status of the lease from the available options.
* **Lease Terms**: Enter the lease term and length.
* **Lease Start Date**: Enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date**: Enter the end date of the lease.
* **Lease Security Deposit Amount**: Enter the lease security deposit amount.
* **Lease Rent**: Enter the lease rent amount.
* **Landlord Details**: Enter the name, address, phone number, and email address of the landlord.
* **Tenant Details**: Enter the name, address, phone number, and email address of the tenant.
* **Landlord Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the landlord.
* **Tenant Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the tenant.
* **Landlord Address**: Enter the address of the landlord.
* **Landlord Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the landlord.
* **Landlord Email Address**: Enter the email address of the landlord.
* **Tenant Address**: Enter the address of the tenant.
* **Tenant Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the tenant.
* **Tenant Email Address**: Enter the email address of the tenant.
* **Email Alternate**: Enter an alternate email address for the landlord.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person for the landlord.
* **Lease Length Months**: Enter the lease length in months.
* **Fax Number**: Enter the fax number of the landlord.
* **Lease End Date Lease Status**: Enter the status of the lease end date.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the lease.
