# Audit Document Download Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audit Document Download Request Form is designed to collect information for requesting access to audit documents. This form is used to gather details about the requester, the audit documents they are seeking, and the purpose of the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and email address in the "Requester Information" section.
2. In the "Audit Details" section, select the type of audit document you are requesting, and provide any relevant details such as the document reference number or start and end dates of the audit period.
3. In the "Access Level" section, select the type of access permission you require for the documents.
4. In the "Authorization" section, enter the name and email address of the person authorizing this request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Email Address** (Required): Use your professional organization email.
* **Department** (Required): Enter your current department or unit.
* **Job Title** (Required): Enter your official position title.
* **Audit Type** (Required): Select the category of audit documents requested (Internal Audit, External Audit, Compliance Audit, Financial Audit).
* **Document Reference Number** (Optional): Enter the specific ID or reference number if known.
* **Audit Period Start Date** (Required): Enter the beginning of the timeframe for the requested records.
* **Audit Period End Date** (Required): Enter the end of the timeframe for the requested records.
* **Document Types Requested** (Required): Select all that apply (Financial Statements, Audit Report, Working Papers, Risk Assessment, Governance Documentation).
* **Purpose of Request** (Required): Provide a brief justification for accessing these documents.
* **Required By Date** (Required): Enter the date by which you need access to these documents.
* **Access Level Requested** (Required): Select the type of access permission needed (Read Only, Download, Full Access).
* **Supervisor Name** (Required): Enter the name of the person authorizing this request.
* **Supervisor Email** (Required): Enter the authorized supervisor email address.

Note: Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays in processing your request.
