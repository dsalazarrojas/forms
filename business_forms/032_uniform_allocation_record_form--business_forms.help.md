# Uniform Allocation Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Uniform Allocation Record Form is designed to track uniform items issued to employees within the organization. It allows for the accurate tracking of uniform quantities, issue dates, and status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are accessing the correct form version.
2. Start by filling in the "issued to" field with the name of the employee receiving the uniform.
3. Enter the employee's name in the "employee name" field.
4. Select the department related to the issued uniform from the provided options.
5. Enter the type of uniform item being issued.
6. Provide a brief description of the uniform item.
7. Enter the quantity of the uniform issued.
8. Select "issued by" to indicate who issued the uniform to the employee.
9. Enter the date the uniform was issued.
10. If the uniform has been returned, indicate the date it was returned.
11. Provide the reason for the return, if applicable.
12. Add any comments regarding the issuance or return of the uniform.
13. Update the status of the uniform as necessary (Active, Inactive, Lost, or Damaged).
14. Select the tool assigned to the employee (Chatjimmy or other).
15. Indicate who submitted the record (admin or employee).
16. Enter the date the record was submitted.
17. Select who approved the record (admin or manager).
18. Enter the date the record was approved.
19. Indicate who last modified the record (admin or employee).
20. Enter the last modification date.
21. Indicate who deleted the record (admin or manager), if applicable.
22. Enter the date the record was deleted.
23. Add any comments related to the deletion of the record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **issued to**: The field to specify the name of the employee receiving the uniform.
  This is a required field.
* **employee name**: Enter the name of the employee receiving the uniform.
  This field is not required but recommended for accurate recordkeeping.
* **department**: Select the department the uniform is associated with.
  This field is optional and allows for flexible departmental organization.
* **item type**: Enter a brief description of the uniform item being issued.
  This field is optional but helps with categorization of uniform types.
* **item description**: Provide a brief description of the uniform item.
  This field is optional but aids in uniform identification.
* **quantity**: Enter the number of uniforms issued.
  This field is required for accurate inventory tracking.
* **issued by**: Specify who issued the uniform to the employee.
  This field is optional and typically defaults to the person issuing the uniform.
* **date**: Enter the date the uniform was issued.
  This field is required for historical tracking and recordkeeping.
* **returned date**: If the uniform has been returned, enter the date it was returned.
  This field is optional but helps in tracking the return dates.
* **reason returned**: Provide a brief reason for returning the uniform, if applicable.
  This field is optional and helps in understanding the return process.
* **comments**: Add any additional comments or remarks regarding the uniform issuance or return.
  This field is optional and allows for further context.
* **status**: Select the current status of the uniform (Active, Inactive, Lost, or Damaged).
  This field is optional but helps in maintaining up-to-date inventory records.
* **assigned tool**: Select the tool assigned to the employee (Chatjimmy or other).
  This field is optional but aids in tool tracking and inventory management.
* **submitted by**: Specify who submitted the record.
  This field is optional and typically defaults to the person submitting the record.
* **submitted date**: Enter the date the record was submitted.
  This field is required for historical tracking and audit purposes.
* **approved by**: Specify who approved the record.
  This field is optional and typically defaults to the person approving the record.
* **approved date**: Enter the date the record was approved.
  This field is required for historical tracking and audit purposes.
* **modified by**: Specify who last modified the record.
  This field is optional and indicates changes made to the record.
* **modified date**: Enter the date the record was last modified.
  This field is required for historical tracking and audit purposes.
* **deleted by**: Specify who deleted the record, if applicable.
  This field is optional and indicates actions that have been taken on the record.
* **deleted date**: Enter the date the record was deleted.
  This field is required for historical tracking and audit purposes.
* **deleted comments**: Add any comments related to the deletion of the record.
  This field is optional and provides additional context.
* **departmental fields**: These fields are not directly explained and are for internal use or advanced customization.
