# Lost Luggage Recovery Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lost Luggage Recovery Request Form is a form designed to collect information from passengers who have lost their luggage during travel. To submit this form, passengers (or their authorized representatives) should only submit the form once for each lost item.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your passenger information (name, contact number, and email).
2. Provide your contact information (name, contact number, and email).
3. Enter details about the flight you were on, including the flight number and date.
4. Describe your baggage in detail, including its make, model, size, and any distinctive features.
5. If applicable, provide information about the airline you were traveling with, including their flight number and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Passenger Information** (`passenger_info`, `text`, optional): Please fill in your name, contact number, and email address.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, contact number, and email address, as well as a brief explanation of why you are submitting this form on behalf of the passenger.
* **Flight Information** (`flight_info`, `text`, optional): Please enter your flight number and date to help us verify your flight details.
* **Baggage Details** (`baggage_details`, `text`, optional): Describe your baggage in detail, including make, model, size, and any distinctive features it may have.
* **Airline Information** (`airline_info`, `text`, optional): Provide the name of the airline and their contact information.
