# Organizational Engagement Strategy Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from businesses looking to engage with our company for a specific service or purpose. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to help us better understand your business needs and provide tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully and fill out all required fields.
- Ensure you have permission to represent the business, if applicable.
- Enter the business name as it appears on official documents or marketing materials.
- Provide a clear and concise description of your business.
- Answer the questions accurately and honestly.
- Select relevant payment frequencies and methods for the services you're seeking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Name
• **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter your business name exactly as it appears on official documents or marketing materials, to avoid any confusion with other businesses.

### Contact Person
• **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for this engagement, who will be the primary point of contact.

### Business Address
• **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required): Enter your business address, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.

### Business Description
• **Business Description** (`business_description`, `text`, required): Provide a clear and concise description of your business, its products or services, and its main goals.

### Years in Business
• **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years your business has been operating.

### Revenue Range
• **Revenue Range** (`revenue_range`, `number`, required): Estimate your business annual revenue.

### Engagement Strategy
• **Engagement Strategy** (`engagement_strategy`, `text`, required): Explain how you plan to engage with our company, what service or purpose you're seeking, and any specific expectations.

### Communication Frequencies
• **Communication Frequencies** (`communication_frequencies`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how often you would like to communicate with our team, from:
  • Monday
  • Tuesday
  • Wednesday
  • Thursday
  • Friday

### Communication Channels
• **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how you would like to communicate with our team, from:
  • Email
  • Phone
  • In-person
  • Video call
  • Chat

### Payment Frequency
• **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how often you would like to make payments for our services, from:
  • Monthly
  • Quarterly
  • Yearly

### Payment Method
• **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how you would like to make payments, from:
  • Credit card
  • Bank transfer
  • Invoice

### Payment Amount
• **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you are willing to pay for our services.

### Additional Info
• **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): If there's anything else you'd like to share or discuss, please include it here.

Tips:
- Be honest and clear in your answers to ensure we can tailor our support to your business needs.
- Ensure you have all necessary permissions and approvals before filling out this form.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask our support team for guidance.
