# Golf Course Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Course Maintenance Checklist Form is a tool used to gather and track information about the maintenance status and schedule of a golf course. This form will help you keep your golf course in top condition by monitoring maintenance activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Course Name** of the golf course you are maintaining.
2. If you're the **Maintenance Manager**, enter your name in this field.
3. Select the **Maintenance Date** when the maintenance activity was or will be performed.
4. Choose the current **Maintenance Status**:
	* **Active**: The course is currently undergoing maintenance.
	* **Inactive**: The course is not currently undergoing maintenance.
	* **Scheduled**: Maintenance is scheduled but not yet performed.
5. Enter any **Notes** about the maintenance activity in the text box.
6. List **Maintenance Tasks** to be completed in the text box.
7. Enter the **Contact Email** of the person responsible for maintenance.
8. Enter the **Contact Phone** number of the person responsible for maintenance.
9. Select the **Assigned To** responsible person from the dropdown list:
	* **chatjimmy**, **chatjane**, **chatbob**.
10. Enter the **Tool Assigned** for the maintenance activity.
11. Select the **Maintenance Frequency**:
	* **Daily**
	* **Weekly**
	* **Bi-Weekly**
	* **Monthly**
12. Choose the **Course Condition**:
	* **Good**
	* **Poor**
	* **Fair**
	* **Excellent**
13. Select the **Course Status**:
	* **Open**
	* **Closed**
	* **Scheduled**
14. Enter the **Last Maintenance** date if applicable.
15. Review and update this form as needed to reflect changes in maintenance status, schedule, and other relevant data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the golf course you are maintaining.
* **Maintenance Manager**: If you are the manager, enter your name here. Otherwise, this field is optional.
* **Maintenance Date**: Enter the date when maintenance was or will be performed.
* **Maintenance Status**: Select the current maintenance status of the golf course.
* **Notes**: Enter any comments or notes about the maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance Tasks**: List any maintenance tasks to be completed.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the person responsible for maintenance.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for maintenance.
* **Assigned To**: Select the person responsible for maintenance from the dropdown list.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned for the maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance Frequency**: Select the frequency of maintenance activities.
* **Course Condition**: Choose the current condition of the golf course.
* **Course Status**: Select the current status of the golf course.
* **Last Maintenance**: If applicable, enter the last maintenance date.

Note: All fields marked as **required** are mandatory and must be filled in for the form to be considered complete.
