# End Of Shift Report Form S&T Cover - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report the completion of a shift for a specific job and work period. It collects information about the employee's work hours, status, and any notes or comments about the shift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the end-of-shift date.
2. Enter your employee ID number.
3. Enter the total hours worked for the shift.
4. Enter any notes or comments about the shift.
5. Enter your employee name.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Enter your supervisor's ID number.
8. Enter your supervisor's name.
9. Enter your supervisor's phone number.
10. Select the status of the shift completion (Complete, Not Complete, or Partial).
11. Enter any additional notes or comments about the shift.

* **Shift Supervisor ID (#7)**: Your supervisor's ID number.
