# Customer Account Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to create or update their account information. To avoid duplication, check our database for existing customer accounts before filling this form out.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out all required fields. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
2.  Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
3.  Ensure that your business name and address are accurate and up-to-date.
4.  If you have any issues or concerns, contact our customer support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address that you wish to use for your customer account. Please be aware that this email will be used for account notifications and communications.
*   **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format "###-####-####".
*   **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your mailing address to receive any mail that may be sent to you from us.
*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your business (if applicable).
*   **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your business (if applicable).
*   **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your business (if applicable).
*   **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments you would like to include about your account.

## Tips

*   Please keep your account information up-to-date to ensure you receive important notifications and communications.
*   If you have any issues or concerns, contact our customer support team.
