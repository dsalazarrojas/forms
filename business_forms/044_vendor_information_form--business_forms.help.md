# Vendor Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect comprehensive information about vendors for our records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Vendor Information Form" to access the form.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for the optional fields, if applicable.
4. Review your filled form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of the vendor company.
* **name** (`name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person in the vendor company.
* **address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address of the vendor company.
* **contact_person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person in the vendor company.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the vendor company.
* **email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter the email address of the vendor company.
* **vendor_type** (`vendor_type`, select_one, required): Select whether the vendor is "Yes" or "No".
* **vendor_status** (`vendor_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the vendor is "Active" or "Inactive".
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time in the format `HH:MM`.
* **note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any notes about the vendor.
* **contact_person_phone** (`contact_person_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person in the vendor company.
* **vendor_type_other** (`vendor_type_other`, select_one, optional): Select whether the vendor is "Yes" or "No", for additional information.
* **vendor_status_other** (`vendor_status_other`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the vendor is "Active" or "Inactive", for additional information.
* **company_other** (`company_other`, text, optional): Enter additional information about the company.
* **address_other** (`address_other`, text, optional): Enter additional information about the address.
* **contact_person_other** (`contact_person_other`, text, optional): Enter additional information about the contact person.
* **phone_other** (`phone_other`, text, optional): Enter additional information about the phone number.
* **email_other** (`email_other`, text, optional): Enter additional information about the email address.
* **vendor_type_other** (`vendor_type_other`, select_one, optional): Select additional information about the vendor type.
* **vendor_status_other** (`vendor_status_other`, select_multiple, optional): Select additional information about the vendor status.
* **date_other** (`date_other`, text, optional): Enter additional information about the date.
* **time_other** (`time_other`, time, optional): Enter additional information about the time.
* **note_other** (`note_other`, note, optional): Enter additional information about the vendor.
