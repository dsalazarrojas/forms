# Trade Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Registration Form is used to collect information about a business or individual's trade or profession. This form is required for registration with the relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of business or industry you are registering under.
3. Provide your address and zip code.
4. Enter your date of registration and license number (if applicable).
5. Select your trade license status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format "example@example.com".
* **Type of Business**: Select the type of business or industry you are registering under (e.g. Limited Liability Company (LLC), Corporation, Sole Proprietor, etc.).
* **Address Line 1**: Enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2**: Enter your address line 2.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your 5-digit zip code.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format "1234567890".
* **Date of Registration**: Enter the date of your registration (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **License Number**: Enter your license number (if applicable).
* **License State**: Select the state of your license.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Industry**: Select the industries your business operates in (e.g. Agriculture and Forestry, Arts and Culture, etc.).
* **Trade License**: Enter your trade license (if applicable).
* **Trade License Number**: Enter your trade license number (if applicable).
* **Report**: Enter any additional comments or reports (if applicable).
* **Trade Employees**: Enter the number of employees in your trade or business.
* **Years in Business**: Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Industry Description**: Enter a brief description of your industry or business.
* **Address Line 3**: Enter your address line 3.
* **Trade License Status**: Enter your trade license status (e.g. Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Report Date**: Enter the date of your report (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Report Time**: Enter the time of your report (format: HH:MM).
* **Report Status**: Select the status of your report (e.g. Active, Inactive, Pending).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
