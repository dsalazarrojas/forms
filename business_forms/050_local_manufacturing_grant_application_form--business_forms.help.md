## Local Manufacturing Grant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Local Manufacturing Grant Application Form is a tool to help us evaluate your project's eligibility for a grant. Please provide accurate and detailed information to ensure a smooth review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your company name, address, and contact information in the relevant fields.
2. Provide your contact email address and phone number in the required fields.
3. If your company has a website, please include the URL.
4. Enter the name of the applicant and project summary/goals and objectives.
5. Enter the project budget and grant amount requested.
6. Upload any supporting documents that are relevant to your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (Required)**: Enter the name of your company, as registered with the relevant authorities.
* **Address (Required)**: Enter your business address where the project will be carried out.
* **Contact Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the person to contact regarding this application.
* **Contact Email (Required)**: Enter the email address of the person to contact.
* **Contact Phone (Required)**: Enter the phone number of the person to contact.
* **Company Website (Optional)**: If your company has a website, please include the URL.
* **Applicant Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the person submitting this application.
* **Project Summary / Goals & Objectives (Required)**: Provide a detailed description of the project, including its goals and objectives. Please note that this is a critical section and will be assessed closely.
* **Project Budget (Required)**: Enter the total budget for the project.
* **Grant Amount (Required)**: Enter the amount of grant requested.
* **Upload Document 1 (Optional)**: If required, upload a relevant document to support your application.
* **Upload Document 2 (Optional)**: If required, upload another relevant document to support your application.
* **Upload Document 3 (Optional)**: If required, upload a third relevant document to support your application.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
