# tourism_policy_review_council_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for members of the tourism industry to submit their applications for review and approval to join a review council.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the Application Form (Page 1).
2. On Page 2, select all roles that apply to your position in the tourism industry.
3. On Page 3, select all motivations that align with your reasons for joining the review council.
4. On Page 4, enter your role(s) in the tourism industry.
5. On Page 5, enter your motivation(s) for joining the review council.
6. On Page 6, enter your name.
7. On Page 7, enter your email address.
8. On Page 8, enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, required: false): This is the first section of the form where you can enter any additional information that you think is relevant to your application.
* **Select Roles** (`roles`, select_multiple, required: false): This section asks you to select all roles that you hold or have experience in within the tourism industry. This could include positions such as Tour Guide, Hotel Manager, or Tour Operator.
* **Select Motivations** (`motivations`, select_multiple, required: false): This section asks you to select all motivations that align with why you want to join the review council. For example, you may be motivated by a passion for travel, a desire to improve industry standards, or a wish to network with other professionals.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required: false): This field asks you to enter a specific role that you hold or have experience in. For example, you may enter "Tour Guide" or "Tour Operator".
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, text, required: false): This field asks you to enter a specific motivation for why you want to join the review council. For example, you may enter "To improve industry standards" or "To network with other professionals".
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): This field asks you to enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This field asks you to enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This field asks you to enter a phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, but you may leave optional fields blank if not applicable.
* Use the "Select Roles" and "Select Motivations" sections to provide specific examples of your roles and motivations.
* Double-check your entries for spelling and grammar errors before submitting.
