# Webinar Materials Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help participants submit materials associated with webinars, such as handouts, videos, or presentations. Fill out this form to share your materials with the host or other participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Choose the type of material you are submitting (e.g., Handouts, Videos, Presentations).
3. Enter a brief description of the material.
4. If applicable, upload the material file.
5. Enter any additional details required for the material, such as file size or license information.
6. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Webinar Host**: Enter the name of the host who will be presenting the webinar.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as the participant submitting the material.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address for correspondence and communication.
* **Material Type**: Select the type of material you are submitting (e.g., Handouts, Videos, Presentations).
* **Material URL**: Provide a link to the material, if applicable.
* **Upload Materials**: Upload the material file, if available.
* **Material Description**: Enter a brief description of the material.
* **Material Size**: If applicable, enter the size of the material file.
* **Material Format**: Select the format of the material (e.g., PDF, DOC, TXT).
* **Upload File**: Upload the material file, if available.
* **File Size**: If applicable, enter the size of the material file.
* **Material License**: Indicate if the material is free or paid.
* **Material License Note**: Enter any additional license notes or details.
* **Material License Fee**: If applicable, enter the fee associated with the material license.
* **Participant Organization**: Enter your organization or affiliation.
* **Participant Title**: Enter your title or position.
* **Participant Organization 2**: Enter your organization or affiliation, if applicable.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Email 2**: Enter your email address for correspondence and communication, if applicable.
* **Participant Title 2**: Enter your title or position, if applicable.
* **Material License Fee 2**: If applicable, enter the fee associated with the material license, if applicable.
* **Material License Fee 4**: If applicable, enter the fee associated with the material license, if applicable.

Note: Some fields may not be applicable depending on the material type or other selections made.
