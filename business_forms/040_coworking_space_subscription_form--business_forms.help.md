# Coworking Space Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coworking Space Subscription Form is designed for individuals or businesses looking to book a private coworking space for a specific term. This form will gather essential details about your subscription preferences, which will be used to provide a personalized experience tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form correctly, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name, Last name, address, email, and phone number.
2. Choose your preferred Subscription term (3 months, 6 months, or 12 months).
3. Provide your Start date and End date for the subscription term.
4. Select your preferred Payment method (Stripe or PayPal).
5. Choose your desired Subscription status (Active, Pending, or Inactive).
6. Add any additional Notes about your subscription, if necessary.
7. Select your preferred Business hours (e.g., 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM).
8. Choose which Meeting room you'd like to book ( Meeting room 1, 2, or 3).
9. Select your Parking preferences (Free parking or Paid parking).
10. Choose your desired Internet access options (WiFi or Ethernet).
11. Select your preferred Power outlets (AC, DC, or Both).
12. Choose whether you'd like to have a Coffee machine available.
13. Select whether you'd like to have a Whiteboard available.
14. Choose whether you'd like to have a Kitchen facility.
15. Select whether you'd like to have a Water cooler available.
16. Choose the Meeting equipment you'd like to use (Projector, Screen, or Whiteboard).
17. Select the Meeting capacity you'd like to book (1 to 6 people).
18. Choose your Cancellation policy (1 day, 7 days, or 30 days).
19. Select your Refund policy (Full or Partial).
20. Add any additional Notes about your preferences.
