# Shipping Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shipping Quote Form is used to request shipping quotes from delivery businesses for specific orders. It is designed to help customers get an early estimate of the shipping cost, allowing them to plan their logistics and finances more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the shipping quote request in the `shipping_quote_request` field, providing a brief description of the shipping needs.
2. In the `shipping_quote_details` field, provide additional information about the shipping requirements, such as any special handling or packaging instructions.
3. Enter the estimated shipping price in the `shipping_quote_price` field.
4. In the `shipping_quote_terms` field, provide any terms or conditions of the shipping quote, such as delivery deadlines or payment terms.
5. If you have any additional notes about the shipping quote, enter them in the `shipping_quote_notes` field.
6. Enter the customer's email address in the `shipping_quote_email` field.
7. If the customer has a phone number, enter it in the `shipping_quote_phone` field.
8. Finally, select the date for the shipping quote in the `shipping_quote_date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **shipping_quote_request**: A text field where you enter the shipping quote request, including any specific details or instructions.
  - This field is required and should be completed in a brief and concise manner.
  - Enter a clear and accurate description of the shipping needs.

- **shipping_quote_details**: A text field where you provide additional information about the shipping requirements.
  - This field is required and should be completed in a detailed and accurate manner.
  - Examples might include special handling instructions, packaging needs, or other relevant details.

- **shipping_quote_price**: A numerical field where you enter the estimated shipping price.
  - This field is required and should be completed with a numerical value (e.g., 10.99).
  - Enter the estimated cost of shipping, including any additional fees or charges.

- **shipping_quote_terms**: A text field where you enter any terms or conditions of the shipping quote.
  - This field is required and should be completed with any relevant terms or conditions.
  - Examples might include delivery deadlines, payment terms, or other important details.

- **shipping_quote_notes**: A note field where you enter any additional notes or comments about the shipping quote.
  - This field is optional and should be completed if there are any additional notes or comments.
  - Enter any additional information that might be helpful for the delivery business to know.

- **shipping_quote_email**: An email field where you enter the customer's email address.
  - This field is required and should be completed with a valid email address.
  - Enter the customer's email address to ensure they receive the shipping quote and any updates.

- **shipping_quote_phone**: A text field where you enter the customer's phone number (if applicable).
  - This field is optional and should be completed if the customer has a phone number.
  - Enter the customer's phone number for additional contact information.

- **shipping_quote_date**: A date field where you select the date for the shipping quote.
  - This field is required and should be completed with the date the shipping quote will be delivered.
  - Enter the date to ensure the delivery business knows when to expect the shipping quote.
