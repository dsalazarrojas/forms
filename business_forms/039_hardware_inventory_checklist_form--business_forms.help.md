# Hardware Inventory Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and update information about hardware assets in a business setting. It helps ensure that all necessary details are recorded consistently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form header at the top for any important notes or instructions.
2. Enter the serial number of the asset in the "serial number" field. This is a unique identifier for each asset.
3. Indicate whether the asset is currently active or inactive in the "hardware type" field.
4. Select the current status of the asset from the list in the "asset status" field. Common statuses include "Active" and "Inactive".
5. Enter the location where the asset is currently stored or located in the "location" field.
6. Check the "asset condition" field to indicate if the asset is in good condition or not.
7. Record the date of the last update in the "last update" field.
8. Enter the name or initials of the person who updated the asset in the "updated by" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (`form_header`, `note`, required: false): This section provides any important notes or instructions for filling out the form.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the unique serial number of the asset.
* **Hardware Type** (`hardware_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether the asset is active or inactive.
* **Asset Status** (`asset_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the current status of the asset. Common statuses include "Active" and "Inactive".
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where the asset is currently stored or located.
* **Asset Condition** (`asset_condition`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check "Yes" if the asset is in good condition or "No" if it's not.
* **Last Update** (`last_update`, `date`, required: false): Record the date of the last update.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name or initials of the person who updated the asset.
* **Form Footer** (`form_footer`, `note`, required: false): This section provides any final notes or instructions for filling out the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Update the form regularly to ensure that asset information is current and consistent.
