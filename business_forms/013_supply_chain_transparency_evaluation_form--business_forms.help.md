# Supply Chain Transparency Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Chain Transparency Evaluation Form is designed to evaluate the suppliers' data, business information, contact data, address data, product information, supply chain information, certification information, and compliance information. This form assesses the suppliers' transparency and compliance with industry standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form instructions and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for each field to indicate the suppliers' level of transparency and compliance.
3. If required, enter your comments in the text fields.
4. Enter dates for the relevant fields.
5. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Main** (`main`, `select_one`, Required): This field asks if you have access to the main information.
* **2. Suppliers Data** (`suppliers_data`, `note`, Optional): This field is a text area for entering suppliers' data.
* **3. Business Data** (`business_data`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the level of business data transparency.
* **4. Contact Data** (`contact_data`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the level of contact data transparency.
* **5. Address Data** (`address_data`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the level of address data transparency.
* **6. Product Info** (`product_info`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the level of product information transparency.
* **7. Supply Chain** (`supply_chain`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the level of supply chain transparency.
* **8. Certification Info** (`certification_info`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the level of certification information transparency.
* **9. Compliance Info** (`compliance_info`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the level of compliance information transparency.
* **10. Supplier Comments** (`supplier_comments`, `text`, Optional): Enter any comments or additional information related to the suppliers.
* **11. Buyer Comments** (`buyer_comments`, `text`, Optional): Enter any comments or additional information related to the buyer.
* **12. Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **13. Date Completed** (`date_completed`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date when the form was completed.
* **14. Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date when the form was reviewed.
* **15. Date Certified** (`date_certified`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date when the form was certified.
* **16. Form Version** (`form_version`, `text`, Optional): Enter the form version number.

## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* Ensure the form is completed by the supplier.
* Review and evaluate the form based on the provided information.
