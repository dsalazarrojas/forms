# production_automation_grant_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for companies to submit applications for grants related to production automation projects. Only authorized representatives from companies should submit this form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's name.
2. Provide your company's address.
3. Enter your company's phone number.
4. Add your contact email address.
5. Choose the type of production automation project you are applying for (Robotics, Automation, or Machine Vision).
6. Select the type of grant you are interested in (Grant 1, Grant 2, or Grant 3).
7. Enter the project status (Active, Inactive, or On Hold).
8. Choose the start date for your project.
9. Choose the end date for your project.
10. Add any additional project notes.
11. Provide the name of the person assigned to this project.
12. Confirm that you are submitting this form on behalf of your company.
13. Enter the date you submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **company_name** (Text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **company_address** (Text, optional): Provide your company's address.
* **company_phone** (Text, optional): Enter your company's phone number.
* **contact_email** (Email, optional): Add your contact email address.
* **production_type** (Select One, optional): Choose the type of production automation project you are applying for:
	+ Robotics
	+ Automation
	+ Machine Vision
* **grant_type** (Select Multiple, optional): Select the type of grant you are interested in:
	+ Grant 1
	+ Grant 2
	+ Grant 3
* **grant_amount** (Number, optional): Enter the grant amount (not applicable if you select "Grant 1").
* **project_status** (Select One, optional): Choose the project status:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ On Hold
* **start_date** (Date, optional): Enter the start date for your project.
* **end_date** (Date, optional): Enter the end date for your project.
* **notes** (Note, optional): Add any additional project notes.
* **assigned_to** (Text, optional): Provide the name of the person assigned to this project.
* **submitted_by** (Text, required): Confirm that you are submitting this form on behalf of your company.
* **date_submitted** (Date, required): Enter the date you submitted this form.
