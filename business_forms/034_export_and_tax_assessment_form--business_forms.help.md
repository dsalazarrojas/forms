# Export And Tax Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Export And Tax Assessment Form is a comprehensive tool designed to collect essential information about exports, focusing on declared values, shipping details, contact information, and supporting documents. This form is likely used for tax assessment, record-keeping, or other regulatory purposes related to export activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form in a new tab or window** to avoid losing progress in case of errors or interruptions.
2. **Start with the export details section**, where you'll enter information about the export, such as the declared value.
3. **Provide shipping details**, including any relevant information about the shipping method, carrier, or tracking numbers.
4. **Enter contact information** for the export's recipient, such as their name, address, and phone number.
5. **Add any additional documents** that support the export, such as receipts or invoices.
6. **Repeat the process for subsequent exports**, as the form allows for multiple sections for different types of exports or shipments.
