# 3D Photo Download Request - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is designed to walk you through the 3D Photo Download Request form, step-by-step, to help you complete it efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Begin by filling out your name and organization or individual status, as well as your contact email address. This information will be used for delivery of the download links and any further communication.
2.  Enter your phone number, including the country code if you're not located within the local region.
3.  Provide a short project or campaign name for the asset you're requesting.
4.  Enter the asset ID or filename you're requesting access to.
5.  Select the intended use of the asset from the options provided.
6.  Choose the preferred format for download.
7.  Select the resolution and fidelity level you require for your asset.
8.  Choose the license type based on your use case.
9.  Enter the number of assets you're requesting.
10. Select the date you need access to the asset by, or leave blank for flexibility.
11. Agree to the terms and fees for usage.
11. Enter any additional notes or instructions for delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requestor Name** (`Requestor Name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your full name for identification.
*   **Requestor Organization** (`Organization / Studio`, `text`, **Optional**): If you're representing an organization, enter the name. Otherwise, leave blank.
*   **Contact Email** (`Contact email`, `email`, **Required**): Enter your email address for delivery of download links and further communication.
*   **Contact Phone Number** (`Contact phone number`, `phone`, **Optional**): Include your country code if you're outside the local region.
*   **Project or Campaign Title** (`Project or usage title`, `text`, **Required**): Enter a brief name for the project or campaign you're working on.
*   **Asset ID or Filename** (`Asset ID or filename`, `text`, **Required**): Provide the 3D photo ID or filename for the asset you're requesting.
*   **Intended Use of the Asset** (`Intended use of the asset`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the reason for your request based on the provided options.
*   **Preferred Download Format** (`Preferred download format`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose one format for the asset delivery.
*   **Resolution / Fidelity Required** (`Resolution / fidelity required`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the level of resolution and fidelity you need.
*   **License Requested for Use** (`License requested for use`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose the license type based on your intended use.
*   **Number of Assets Requested** (`Number of assets requested`, `integer`, **Required**): Enter the whole number of assets you're requesting.
*   **Access Date Needed By** (`Access date needed by`, `date`, **Optional**): Enter the date by which you need access to the asset, or leave blank for flexibility.
*   **Agree to Terms** (`Do you agree to the usage terms and fees?`, `select_one`, **Required**): Agree to the usage terms and fees by selecting true.
*   **Additional Notes or Delivery Instructions** (`Additional details or delivery instructions`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter any additional comments or requests for the delivery.
