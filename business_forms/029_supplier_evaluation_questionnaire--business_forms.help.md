# Supplier Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Evaluation Questionnaire is used to collect feedback on a supplier's performance. This form asks questions about the supplier's information, contact details, evaluation criteria, and overall rating. It's a crucial tool to help purchasing teams make informed decisions about their suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Read through each question carefully** to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.
2. **Fill in the necessary fields**, including supplier information, contact details, and evaluation criteria.
3. **Choose a rating** for each of the supplier's performance criteria, such as quality, price, communication, and overall rating.
4. **Add any additional comments** if you have any feedback or concerns about the supplier.
5. **Indicate whether the supplier is recommended or not** and add a reason for your recommendation.
6. **Provide a scale rating** for each performance criteria, if applicable.
