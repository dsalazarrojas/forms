# Mission Statement Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mission Statement Collection Form is a tool used to collect mission statements and other relevant information from team members. This form helps to gather data that can be used to improve team performance, communication, and overall company goals. It is essential to complete this form accurately and comprehensively to ensure that your mission statements and other input are recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully before submitting your mission statement and other details. Ensure that all fields are accurately filled out.
2.  Enter your name and department to identify yourself as a team member.
3.  Select the correct options for your mission vision and team lead.
4.  Enter the dates and times for when you created and last updated your mission statement.
5.  Add your email address, phone number, name, and other contact information.
6.  Enter the location and description of your team.
7.  Enter any additional information as needed, such as the team lead's name and your role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Mission Statement** (`mission_statement`, text, optional): Enter a short description of your personal or team mission statement.
*   **Team Members** (`team_members`, select multiple, optional): Select all team members who should be included in the mission statement collection.
*   **Mission Vision** (`mission_vision`, select one, optional): Choose your personal or team mission vision.
*   **Date Created** (`date_created`, date, optional): Enter the date you created your mission statement.
*   **Date Modified** (`date_modified`, date, optional): Enter the date you last updated your mission statement.
*   **Time Created** (`time_created`, time, optional): Enter the time you created your mission statement.
*   **Time Modified** (`time_modified`, time, optional): Enter the time you last updated your mission statement.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address or location of your team or organization.
*   **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your team or organization.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your team or organization.
*   **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who created the mission statement.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the mission statement.
*   **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department or team you belong to.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or role in the organization.
*   **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your team.
*   **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, text, optional): Enter the name of your team lead.
*   **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Enter your role or position in the organization.
*   **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of your reporting manager.
*   **Mission Statement 2** (`mission_statement_2`, text, optional): Enter a short description of your personal or team mission statement.
*   **Mission Statement 3** (`mission_statement_3`, text, optional): Enter a short description of your personal or team mission statement.
*   **Mission Statement 4** (`mission_statement_4`, text, optional): Enter a short description of your personal or team mission statement.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are accurately filled out to guarantee that your mission statement and other details are recorded correctly.
*   You can select multiple team members who should be included in the mission statement collection.
*   If you have any questions or need help completing the form, please reach out to your team lead or supervisor.
