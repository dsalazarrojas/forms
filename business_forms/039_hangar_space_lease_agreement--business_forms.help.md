# Hangar Space Lease Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist landlords and tenants in creating a comprehensive lease agreement for renting a hangar space. It collects relevant information to establish a clear understanding of the terms and conditions of the lease.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the required and optional fields.
2. Fill in the general information about the hangar space.
3. Provide tenant information as required.
4. Specify the lease term, rental fees, and any other relevant details.
5. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
6. Sign and date the agreement to confirm your commitment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (Page 1)**:
	+ **General Information (text)**: Fill in the details about the hangar space, including its location, size, and any specific features.
* **Tenant Information (Page 2)**:
	+ **Tenant Information (text)**: Provide the tenant's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Lease Term (Page 3)**:
	+ **Lease Term (text)**: Specify the start and end dates of the lease, including any renewal or termination conditions.
* **Rental Fees (Page 4)**:
	+ **Rental Fees (text)**: List the rental fees, including any additional charges or penalties.
* **Terms and Conditions (Page 5)**:
	+ **Terms and Conditions (text)**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the lease, which should be reviewed carefully before signing.
* **Agreement Review (Page 6)**:
	+ **Agreement Review (text)**: Review the agreement carefully to ensure it accurately reflects the terms of the lease.
* **Tenant Signature (Page 7)**:
	+ **Tenant Signature (text)**: Sign and date the agreement to confirm your commitment as the tenant.
* **Lessor Signature (Page 8)**:
	+ **Lessor Signature (text)**: Sign and date the agreement to confirm your commitment as the landlord.
* **Contact Information (Page 9)**:
	+ **Contact Information (text)**: Provide the contact information for the tenant and landlord.
* **Lease End Date (Page 10)**:
	+ **Lease End Date (date)**: Specify the end date of the lease, including any renewal or termination conditions.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure all necessary information is included.
* Make sure to read and agree to the terms and conditions carefully before signing the agreement.
* Keep a copy of the signed lease agreement for future reference.
* Update the form as needed to reflect changes in the lease terms or conditions.
