# vat_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The VAT registration form is designed to collect business-related data for VAT registration purposes. This form is used to gather information about a business's name, address, VAT number, type, phone number, email, VAT registration date, registration status, and any additional notes or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the business's name in the "Business Name" field.
2. Enter the business's address in the "Business Address" field.
3. Enter the business's VAT number in the "VAT Number" field.
4. Select the type of business from the "Business Type" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the business's phone number in the "Business Phone" field.
6. Enter the business's email address in the "Business Email" field.
7. Select the date of VAT registration from the calendar input or enter it manually in the "VAT Registration Date" field.
8. Select the VAT registration status from the dropdown menu in the "VAT Registration Status" field.
9. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
10. Optionally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Business Name** (Business Name, text, required: false)
Enter the name of your business.

### 2. **Business Address** (Business Address, text, required: false)
Enter the address of your business.

### 3. **VAT Number** (vat_number, number, required: false)
Enter your VAT number.

### 4. **Business Type** (business_type, select_one, required: false)
Select the type of business you are. Options include: 
  * Sole Trader
  * Limited Company
  * Partnership
  * Other

### 5. **Business Phone** (business_phone, text, required: false)
Enter the phone number of your business.

### 6. **Business Email** (business_email, email, required: false)
Enter the email address of your business.

### 7. **VAT Registration Date** (vat_registration_date, date, required: false)
Enter the date of VAT registration or select it from the calendar.

### 8. **VAT Registration Status** (vat_registration_status, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the status of your VAT registration from the dropdown menu. Options include:
  * Active
  * Inactive
  * Pending

### 9. **Notes** (notes, note, required: false)
Provide any additional notes or comments about your business.

### 10. **VAT Registration Form Submitted** (vat_registration_form_submitted, time, required: false)
This field is not currently used in the form, so you can ignore it.

**Tips:**

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact our support team.
