# Treasury Audit Schedule Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Audit Schedule Request Form is designed to facilitate the process of requesting a treasury audit. Completing this form ensures that all necessary information is provided to schedule and complete the audit efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, making sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you understand its purpose and requirements.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide or consult with the relevant personnel.
4. Submit the form once you have completed it to initiate the audit process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Name (`company_name`)
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company requesting the audit.
* **Required**: This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide the company name for reference purposes.

### Audit Details (`audit_details`)
* **Audit Details**: Provide a brief explanation of the audit you're requesting. This field is required, so please make sure to include a clear and concise description of the audit.

### Preferred Date (`preferred_date`)
* **Preferred Date**: Indicate the date you would like to schedule the audit. This field is required.

### Supporting Documents (`supporting_documents`)
* **Supporting Documents**: If you have any supporting documents (e.g., receipts, invoices, or other relevant evidence), select "Yes" to indicate that you'll provide them. This field is required.

### Date Requested (`date_requested`)
* **Date Requested**: Indicate the date you would like to schedule the audit. This field is required.

### Date Completed (`date_completed`)
* **Date Completed**: If the audit has been completed, enter the date it was completed. This field is not required, as it may not always be applicable.

### Audit Type (`audit_type`)
* **Audit Type**: Select "Yes" if this is a routine audit, and "No" if it's a special audit. This field is required.

### Notes (`notes`)
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or explanations related to the audit. This field is not required, but it's helpful for auditors to understand the context.

### Auditor Name (`auditor_name`)
* **Auditor Name**: If an auditor is assigned to this audit, enter their name. This field is not required.

### Assigned To (`assigned_to`)
* **Assigned To**: If a specific person is assigned to this audit, enter their name. This field is not required.

### Status (`status`)
* **Status**: Select "Active" if the audit is active, and "Inactive" if it's not. This field is required.

### Priority (`priority`)
* **Priority**: Select "Yes" if the audit has high priority, and "No" if it's not. This field is required.

### Category (`category`)
* **Category**: Enter any relevant category or classification related to the audit. This field is not required.

### Notes for Status (`notes_for_status`)
* **Notes for Status**: Add any additional comments or explanations related to the audit status. This field is required.

### Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool`)
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool or equipment assigned to this audit. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Double-check the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
* Review the fields carefully to ensure you understand their purpose and requirements.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide or consult with the relevant personnel.
* Make sure to include any supporting documents when necessary.
