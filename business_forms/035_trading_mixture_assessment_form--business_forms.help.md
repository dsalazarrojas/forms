# trading_mixture_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the trading mixture of a specific substance. It collects relevant information about the mixture's components, hazards, internal review, safer trading practices, and other essential details. This form aims to ensure that the trading mixture meets the required standards and regulations for safe handling, storage, and transportation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Mixture Details**: Provide a brief description of the trading mixture, including its name and any relevant details.
2. **Intended Use**: Describe the intended use of the mixture, if applicable.
3. **Components**: Select the components that make up the mixture. You can choose multiple components from the list of options.
4. **Hazards**: Identify any potential hazards associated with the mixture.
5. **Internal Review**: Provide an internal review of the mixture, including any relevant notes or comments.
6. **Safer Trading**: Choose the safer trading practice for the mixture (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
7. **Lab Info**: Enter any relevant information about the lab or facility handling the mixture.
8. **Distributor Info**: Enter any relevant information about the distributor or supplier of the mixture.
9. **Component 6**: Select the specific component 6 associated with the mixture.
10. **Component 5**: Select the specific components 5 associated with the mixture.
11. **Component 4**: Select the specific components 4 associated with the mixture.
12. **Component 2**: Select the specific components 2 associated with the mixture.

14. **Component 1**: Select the specific components 1 associated with the mixture.
15. **Manufacturer Info**: Enter any relevant information about the manufacturer of the mixture.
