# Corporate Life And Confidentiality Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Life And Confidentiality Agreement Form is a legally binding document that ensures employees understand their obligations and responsibilities regarding confidential information. This form helps the employer to verify that the employee has read, understood, and agrees to maintain confidentiality of sensitive business information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your Business Name, which is the name of your company or organization.
2. Enter the Agreement Date, which is the date you are signing the agreement.
3. Provide your Employee ID, which is your unique identifier within the company.
4. Select the terms you agree to maintain confidentiality from the list of Confidentiality Terms.
5. Sign your Employee Signature, which confirms your agreement to the terms.
6. Sign your Employer Signature, which confirms your employer's agreement to the terms.
7. Enter the Date Signed, which is the date you complete and sign the agreement.
8. Fill in your Employee Name, which is your name as an employee.
9. Enter your title and position within the department.
10. Enter your department within the company.
11. Enter your Employee Email, which is your official email address.
12. Enter your Employee Phone, which is your official phone number.
13. Enter your Employee Address, which is your official mailing address.
14. Enter your Employer Name, which is the name of your company or organization.
15. Enter your Employer Address, which is the official address of your company or organization.
16. Enter your Employer Phone, which is the official phone number of your company or organization.
17. Fill in the Agreement Terms, which is a free-text field for any additional terms or conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are signing this agreement.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your unique identifier within the company.
* **Confidentiality Terms** (`confidentiality_terms`, select_multiple, required): Select the terms you agree to maintain confidentiality from the list.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): Sign your name to confirm your agreement to the terms.
* **Employer Signature** (`employer_signature`, text, required): Sign your employer's name to confirm their agreement to the terms.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date you complete and sign the agreement.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as an employee.
* **Title Position** (`title_position`, text, required): Enter your title and position within the department.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required): Enter your official phone number.
* **Employee Address** (`employee_address`, text, required): Enter your official mailing address.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, required): Enter the official address of your company or organization.
* **Employer Phone** (`employer_phone`, text, required): Enter the official phone number of your company or organization.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, text, required): Fill in any additional terms or conditions not listed.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields carefully and accurately.
* Review the Agreement Terms section to ensure you understand your obligations and responsibilities.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
* Once completed, please sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
* This form should be kept in your personnel file for reference.
