# Customer Service Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your customer service complaint so that our team can effectively investigate and resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Describe the issue you are experiencing in the "Description of the Issue" field.
2. Provide as much detail as possible about what happened in the "Incident Details" field.
3. Specify the date and time of the incident in the "Date of Incident" and "Time of Incident" fields, respectively.
4. Enter your name and email address in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Email" fields.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
6. Choose how you would like to be contacted for updates and resolution in the "How would you like to be contacted?" field.
7. Select your preference for resolution in the "Resolution Preference" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Description of the Issue** (Customer Service Complaint Form/Description of the Issue, Text, **Required**):
  Please provide a clear description of the problem or issue you are experiencing.
* **Incident Details** (Customer Service Complaint Form/Incident Details, Text, **Required**):
  Please provide as much information as possible about what happened, including any details that may be relevant to understanding the issue.
* **Date of Incident** (Customer Service Complaint Form/Date of Incident, Date, **Required**):
  Enter the date of the incident in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time of Incident** (Customer Service Complaint Form/Time of Incident, Time, **Required**):
  Enter the time of the incident in 12-hour AM/PM format.
* **Customer Name** (Customer Service Complaint Form/Customer Name, Text, **Required**):
  Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Customer Email** (Customer Service Complaint Form/Customer Email, Email, **Required**):
  Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you for updates and resolution.
* **Customer Phone** (Customer Service Complaint Form/Customer Phone, Text, **Required**):
  Enter your phone number so we can contact you for updates and resolution.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (Customer Service Complaint Form/How would you like to be contacted?, Select Multiple, **Required**):
  Choose how you would like to be contacted for updates and resolution. Select all applicable options, such as Email and Phone.
* **Resolution Preference** (Customer Service Complaint Form/Resolution Preference, Select One, **Required**):
  Choose your preference for the resolution of the issue. Options include Immediate Resolution and Follow-up.
