# Office Furniture Purchase Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Office Furniture Purchase Form is designed to enable employees to submit orders for office furniture. This form collects the required information to process orders efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Select the office furniture items you would like to purchase and their corresponding colors.
3. Choose a payment method (PayPal or other) for the order.
4. Optionally, enter a note about your order.
5. Click 'Submit' to send the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (<code>first_name</code>, <code>text</code>, required: <code>false</code>): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Email (<code>email</code>, <code>email</code>, required: <code>false</code>): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone Number (<code>phone_number</code>, <code>text</code>, required: <code>false</code>): Enter your work phone number.
* **Street Address (<code>street_address</code>, <code>text</code>, required: <code>false</code>): Enter your street address.
* **Products (<code>products</code>, <code>select_multiple</code>, required: <code>false</code>): Select the office furniture items you would like to purchase.
*   Select one or more items from the list to add them to your order.
* **Color (<code>product_color</code>, <code>text</code>, required: <code>false</code>): Select the color of the furniture you've chosen.
*   Please note that this field is only available for select items with color options.
* **Submit (<code>submit</code>, <code>select_one</code>, required: <code>false</code>): Select 'Select One' to submit your order or 'Other' to indicate a custom order.
*   Please note that this field is only available to select between two options.
* **Note (<code>note</code>, <code>text</code>, required: <code>false</code>): Enter any additional information or comments about your order.
* **Payment Method (<code>payment_method</code>, <code>select_one</code>, required: <code>false</code>): Select 'PayPal' or 'Other' for your payment method.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
*   Select only the items you truly need.
*   If you select 'Other' for payment method, please contact the purchasing team for further instructions.
