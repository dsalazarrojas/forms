# Tour Operator Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tour Operator Agreement Extension Form is used to document changes or updates to an existing Tour Operator Agreement, ensuring that all parties involved are aware of the updates and their implications.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of extension you need to make to the agreement from the "Tour Operator Agreement" field.
2. Enter the original Agreement ID for reference.
3. Choose the type of change or update from the "Agreement Type" field.
4. Enter the start and end dates for the extension.
5. On the next page, enter the customer name, email, phone number, and any other relevant details as prompted.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the agreement extension in the "Agreement Notes" field.
7. Assign the form to a person or team for handling.
8. Record the date and time of form creation and last update.
