# Organizational Transparency Initiative Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from organizations applying for the Organizational Transparency Initiative. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Applicant Info section with your organization's details.
2. Provide your organization's contact information in the Contact Info section.
3. Select the relevant program(s) that your organization is interested in applying for in the Program Selection section.
4. Confirm whether you need to provide supporting documents for your application.
5. Add any additional information or comments in the Application Review section.
6. Enter your email address in the Email field.
7. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
8. If required, fill in the Date and Time fields for events or deadlines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, text, required/optional): 
	+ This section asks for basic details about your organization, including the organization's name, address, and other essential contact information.
* **Organization Details** (`organization_details`, text, required/optional): 
	+ Here, you can enter any additional information about your organization that would help us understand its structure, size, and relevance to the initiative.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): 
	+ Please provide your organization's primary contact person's name, title, and contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Select Program** (`program_selection`, select_one, required/optional): 
	+ Choose the programs you are interested in applying for. This will help us understand your organization's goals and objectives.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
	+ Confirm whether you need to provide additional documents to support your application. If so, we will contact you for more information.
* **Application Review** (`application_review`, note, required/optional): 
	+ Add any additional comments, questions, or information about your application.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): 
	+ Enter your organization's email address for official communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): 
	+ Provide your organization's primary contact person's phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): 
	+ If relevant, enter the date of an event or deadline related to your application.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): 
	+ If relevant, enter the time of an event or deadline related to your application.
* **Additional Information** (`note_field`, note, required/optional): 
	+ Provide any additional information that would help us understand your application.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide all the required fields.
* If you need assistance with any field, please contact us.
* This form is for internal use only.
