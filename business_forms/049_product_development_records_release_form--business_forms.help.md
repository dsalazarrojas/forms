# Product Development Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Development Records Release Form is designed to collect essential information for releasing product development records. This form helps track project progress, ensure accurate data, and facilitate smooth communication within the development team and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Project ID** (Field 1) to identify the specific project being worked on.
2. Provide the **Product Title** (Field 2) to accurately label the product being released.
3. Enter the **Team Lead ID** (Field 3) to associate the project with the team lead responsible.
4. Fill in the **Team Lead Name** (Field 4) for easy reference.
5. Enter the **Team Lead Email** (Field 6) for contact purposes.
6. Enter the **Team Lead Phone** (Field 7) for additional contact information.
7. Provide the **Product Description** (Field 5) to describe the product being released.
8. Enter the **Product Version** (Field 8) to track versioning history.
9. Set the **Release Date** (Field 9) for record-keeping.
10. Enter the **Reviewer Name** (Field 10) to acknowledge the reviewer's involvement.
11. Enter the **Reviewer Title** (Field 11) for additional context.
12. Enter the **Reviewer Email** (Field 12) for easy contact.
13. Enter the **Reviewer Phone** (Field 13) for additional contact information.
14. Provide the **Reviewer Signature** (Field 14) as proof of approval.
15. Select the **Project Status** (Field 15) to update project status (Released, Approved, Pending, Rejected).
16. Choose the **Release To** (Field 16) to determine the recipient of the release (Team Lead, Product Team, Reviewers, External Parties).
17. Add any additional **Notes** (Field 17) for future reference.
17. Enter **Comments** (Field 18) for any additional feedback or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project ID** (Field 1): Enter the project ID to associate the record with the correct project.
* **Product Title** (Field 2): Accurately label the product being released.
* **Team Lead ID** (Field 3): Enter the team lead's ID for easy reference.
* **Team Lead Name** (Field 4): Provide the team lead's name for easy reference.
* **Team Lead Email** (Field 6): Enter the team lead's email address for contact purposes.
* **Team Lead Phone** (Field 7): Enter the team lead's phone number for additional contact information.
* **Product Description** (Field 5): Describe the product being released in detail.
* **Product Version** (Field 8): Enter the product version number for tracking changes.
* **Release Date** (Field 9): Set the release date for record-keeping.
* **Reviewer Name** (Field 10): Enter the reviewer's name for acknowledgement.
* **Reviewer Title** (Field 11): Provide the reviewer's title for additional context.
* **Reviewer Email** (Field 12): Enter the reviewer's email address for easy contact.
* **Reviewer Phone** (Field 13): Enter the reviewer's phone number for additional contact information.
* **Reviewer Signature** (Field 14): Provide a signature as proof of approval.
* **Project Status** (Field 15): Update the project status (Released, Approved, Pending, Rejected).
* **Release To** (Field 16): Determine the recipient of the release (Team Lead, Product Team, Reviewers, External Parties).
* **Notes** (Field 17): Add any additional notes for future reference.
* **Comments** (Field 18): Enter any comments or feedback for the release process.
