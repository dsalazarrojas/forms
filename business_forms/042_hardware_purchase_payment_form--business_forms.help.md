# Hardware Purchase Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hardware Purchase Payment Form is used to provide detailed information about a hardware purchase payment. This includes payment information, product details, customer info, shipping information, and any additional notes or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields. If a field is marked as required, it's essential to provide a valid value for that field.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure that you've provided accurate information.
3. Double-check the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the payment, including any relevant details or notes.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the payment was made or will be made. This helps in tracking payment timelines.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount paid for the hardware purchase. Please ensure that this is accurate to avoid any discrepancies.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the hardware product, including any relevant details or notes.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if the payment has been made via this method, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the customer, including their name and contact details (if required).
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, optional): Enter the shipping details, including the shipping address and contact information (if required).
* **Payment Note** (`payment_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the payment.
* **Customer E-Mail** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address (if required).

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and consistency before submission.
* Ensure that you've covered all the necessary details for payment processing.
* If you're unsure about any field or have further questions, don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
