# Logistics Optimization Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Logistics Optimization Grant Application Form is designed to help us understand the details of your project and assess its potential for grant funding. The information collected will be used to evaluate the feasibility and viability of your project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your contact information (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone).
* Provide details about your organization (if applicable).
* Choose your business type and project location.
* Specify the duration, budget, and status of your project.
* Describe your project, its objectives, outcome, impact, and benefit.
* Provide information about any relevant documents and funding proposals.
* Choose the status of your project approval and the source of external funding (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name to help us identify you.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name to help us identify you.
### Email
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
### Phone
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
### Organization
* **Organization** (text): If you represent an organization, enter its name.
### Business Type
* **Business Type** (select_one): Choose 'Yes' if your project is a business-related project; otherwise, choose 'No'.
### Business Size
* **Business Size** (select_multiple): Choose the size of your business (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
### Project Duration
* **Project Duration** (date): Enter the duration of your project.
### Project Budget
* **Project Budget** (number): Enter the budget for your project.
### Project Location
* **Project Location** (text): Enter your project location.
### Project Status
* **Project Status** (select_one): Choose the current status of your project (e.g., 'Active', 'Inactive').
### Relevant Documents
* **Relevant Documents** (select_multiple): Choose 'Yes' if you have relevant documents; otherwise, choose 'No'.
### Project Description
* **Project Description** (text): Briefly describe your project.
### Project Objectives
* **Project Objectives** (text): Specify the objectives of your project.
### Project Outcome
* **Project Outcome** (text): Describe the expected outcome of your project.
### Project Impact
* **Project Impact** (text): Describe the impact of your project on your business or organization.
### Project Benefit
* **Project Benefit** (text): Describe the benefits of your project.
### Project Risk
* **Project Risk** (text): Identify any risks associated with your project.
### Project Approval Status
* **Project Approval Status** (select_one): Choose the current status of your project approval (e.g., 'Active', 'Inactive').
### External Funding Source
* **External Funding Source** (text): If you have external funding, specify its source.
### Agreement Term
* **Agreement Term** (text): Specify the term of your agreement (if applicable).
### Review Status
* **Review Status** (note): This field is for internal review purposes only.

Note: The fields marked as 'required' are mandatory, while the others are optional.
