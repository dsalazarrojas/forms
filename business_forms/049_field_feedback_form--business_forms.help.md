# Field Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Feedback Form is a collection of questions and fields meant to gather information about your experiences and opinions regarding a specific sector. This form helps us understand your thoughts and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Sector Name** (sector_name): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your presence in this sector. This will help us know if you are a part of this specific sector.
*   **Sector Type** (sector_type): Provide a brief description of the sector you are in or think you belong to. This will help us get a better understanding of the context.
*   **Comment** (comment): Write down any additional information or thoughts you have about the sector. This is an open-ended field, so feel free to share as much as you need to.
*   **Feedback** (feedback): If you have any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve, share them here.
*   **Rating** (rating): Give this sector a score out of 5, where:
    \[rating_label] (1-5)
*   **Email** (email): We will use this email address to contact you if needed. If you do not want to share your email, you can simply leave it blank.
*   **Submit** (submit): Once you've filled out all the fields, click this button to submit your feedback.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all the required fields (marked as required in the form), and use a valid email address if you choose to share it. Your input will help us understand your opinions and make necessary changes to improve our services.
