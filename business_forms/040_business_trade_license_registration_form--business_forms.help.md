# Business Trade License Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering a business trade license, which is a requirement for businesses engaged in trade.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your details in the following sections:
	* **Applicant Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
	* **Personal Address**: Enter your current residential address.
	* **Business Name**: Enter the legal name of your business.
	* **Type of Business**: Enter a brief description of the type of business you are engaged in.
	* **Business Description**: Enter a detailed description of the business's activities.
	* **Business Address**: Enter the physical location of your business.
	* **City**: Enter the city where your business is located.
	* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where your business is located.
	* **Postal Code**: Enter the ZIP or postal code of your business location.
	* **Business Phone Number**: Enter your business contact phone number.
	* **Business Email Address**: Enter your business contact email address.
	* **Business Website**: (Optional) Enter the URL of your business website.
	* **Government ID Type**: Select the type of government identification you hold (e.g., Driver License, Passport, etc.).
	* **Government ID Number**: Enter the number of your government ID.
	* **Years of Experience in Trade**: (Optional) Enter the number of years you have been engaged in trade.
	* **Number of Employees**: (Optional) Enter the number of people you will employ.
	* **Business Startup Date**: Enter the date when your business will start.
	* **Estimated Annual Revenue**: (Optional) Enter your projected first-year revenue.
	* **Application Submission Date**: This will be automatically populated with today's date.
