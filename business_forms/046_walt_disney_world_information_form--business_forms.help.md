# Walt Disney World Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your visit to Walt Disney World. This data will be used for internal purposes to improve our services. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure we receive the correct information.

## How To Complete This Form

2. Provide your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This will be used for updates and information about your visit.
4. If you have a phone number, please provide it in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter the date of your visit in the "Date" field, in the format dd-mm-yyyy.
6. If you have been assigned a tool, select the correct option in the "Assigned Tool" field.

8. If you want to provide more information, please type it in the "Form Description" field.
9. If you need to categorize your form, type the relevant category in the "Form Category" field.
10. Finally, enter a unique ID in the "Form ID" field.
11. You can also specify the page number of your form in the "Page Number" field.
12. If you want to save your form output, type it in the "Form Output File" field.
