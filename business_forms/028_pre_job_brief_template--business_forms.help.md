# pre_job_brief_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used to collect key information about job-related activities, including company details, project descriptions, job status, job type, and assigned personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter company name**: This field is not required but provides context about the company associated with the job.
2. **Job Description**: This field is also not required, but it helps to briefly describe the job or project in question.
3. **Project Details**: Similar to Job Description, this field is not required and provides additional context for the job or project.
4. **Job Status**: Select one of the options available (Active, Completed, or Cancelled) to indicate the current status of the job.
5. **Job Type**: Choose the type of job (Sales, Service, Consulting, or Other) to categorize the job accurately.
6. **Job Date**: Enter the date when the job was created or started.
7. **Job Time**: Enter the time of day when the job was created or started.
8. **Assigned User**: Select the user who is assigned to this job (John, Jane, or Jim).
9. **Assigned Date**: Enter the date when the job was assigned to the selected user.
10. **Notes**: This field is not required but can be used to store any additional comments or information about the job.
11. **Email**: This is not a required field, but it can be used for internal communication or reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: A text input field to enter the name of the company associated with the job. 
* **Job Description**: A text input field to briefly describe the job or project in question. 
* **Project Details**: Similar to Job Description, this field provides additional context for the job or project. 
* **Job Status**: Selects the current status of the job (Active, Completed, or Cancelled). 
* **Job Type**: Categorizes the job (Sales, Service, Consulting, or Other) for easy reference. 
* **Job Date**: Enters the date when the job was created or started. 
* **Job Time**: Enters the time of day when the job was created or started. 
* **Assigned User**: Selects the user assigned to the job (John, Jane, or Jim). 
* **Assigned Date**: Enters the date when the job was assigned to the selected user. 
* **Notes**: A text input field for storing any additional comments or information about the job. 
* **Email**: An email input field for internal communication or reference purposes. 

## Tips
* Be sure to fill in all required fields for accurate data collection.
* The form is designed to be a template, so you can modify it as needed to suit your specific business needs.
