# Business Premises Photo Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and upload photos of business premises for inspection, insurance, or other related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date of the photo upload.
2.  Provide your contact information (name, email, phone number).
3.  Enter the property's name and address.
4.  Select the type of property (office, warehouse, retail store, etc.).
5.  Choose the type of inspection (insurance, property, routine review, etc.).
6.  Enter the date of inspection.
7.  Indicate whether exterior, interior, roof condition, HVAC system, and electrical system photos are included.
8.  Check if any damage was observed during the inspection.
9.  Describe any damage found.
10.  Indicate if maintenance is needed.
11.  Enter the total number of photos uploaded.
12.  Rate the photo quality (excellent, good, acceptable, poor).
13.  Provide any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Upload Date** (upload_date, date, required): Enter the date of the photo upload.
*   **Your Name** (uploader_name, text, required): Provide your full name for contact purposes.
*   **Email Address** (uploader_email, email, required): Enter your contact email for communication.
*   **Phone Number** (uploader_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
*   **Property Name** (property_name, text, required): Enter the name of the location.
*   **Property Address** (property_address, text, required): Enter the full address of the location.
*   **Property Type** (property_type, select_one, required): Select the type of property (office, warehouse, retail store, etc.).
*   **Type of Inspection** (inspection_type, select_one, required): Choose the type of inspection (insurance, property, routine review, etc.).
*   **Inspection Date** (inspection_date, date, required): Enter the date of inspection.
*   **Exterior Photos Provided** (exterior_photos, select_one, required): Indicate if exterior photos are included.
*   **Interior Photos Provided** (interior_photos, select_one, required): Indicate if interior photos are included.
*   **Roof Condition Photos** (roof_condition_photos, select_one, required): Indicate if roof condition photos are included.
*   **HVAC System Photos** (hvac_photos, select_one, required): Indicate if HVAC system photos are included.
*   **Electrical System Photos** (electrical_photos, select_one, required): Indicate if electrical system photos are included.
*   **Any Damage Observed** (damage_observed, select_one, required): Check if any damage was observed during inspection.
*   **Damage Description** (damage_description, text, required): Describe any damage found.
*   **Maintenance Needed** (maintenance_needed, text, required): Indicate if maintenance is needed.
*   **Total Number of Photos** (number_of_photos, number, required): Enter the total number of photos uploaded.
*   **Photo Quality** (photo_quality, select_one, required): Rate the photo quality (excellent, good, acceptable, poor).
*   **Additional Notes** (additional_notes, text, required): Provide any additional notes or observations.
