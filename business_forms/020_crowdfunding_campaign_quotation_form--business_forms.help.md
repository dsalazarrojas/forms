# crowdfund_crowdfunding_campaign_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The crowdfund_crowdfunding_campaign_quotation_form is used to create a quote for a customer who wants to start a crowdfunding campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **campaign_info** (`1`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter a short description of the campaign.
* **campaign_name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the name of the campaign.
* **target_amount** (`3`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the target amount for the campaign.
* **end_date** (`4`, `date`, required/optional: false): Enter the end date of the campaign.
* **start_date** (`5`, `date`, required/optional: false): Enter the start date of the campaign.
* **description** (`6`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter a detailed description of the campaign.
* **goal_amount** (`7`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the goal amount for the campaign.
* **campaign_type** (`8`, `select_one`, required/optional: false): Select the type of campaign (option 1, option 2, or option 3).
* **backers** (`9`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the number of backers for the campaign.
* **goal_frequency** (`10`, `select_one`, required/optional: false): Select the goal frequency (option 1, option 2, or option 3).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information for each field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are complete and correct.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your customer support team for assistance.
