# Personal Tax Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients for tax-related purposes. It will be used to collect personal details and employment status to provide an overview of the client's tax situation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields. These fields are required and should be filled out accurately.
2. If applicable, enter your **Date of Birth** in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Enter your **Email** address, which will be used to contact you with any updates or queries.
4. Enter your **Phone** number, which can be in the format (123) 456-7890.
5. Provide your **Address** for contact and communication purposes.
6. Enter your **Social Security Number**, which should be in the format XXX-XX-XXXX.
7. Select your **Employment Status** from the provided options (Employed, Self-Employed, or Retired).
8. Choose your **Tax Return Status** from the provided options (Not Started, Completed, or Pending Review).
9. Select all **Income Sources** that apply (Wages, Business, Investments, Interest, or Rental Income).
10. Enter details about your **Income**, including **Tax Year** and **Total Income**.
11. Enter the number of **Dependents** you have.
12. Select your **Tax Return Filing Status** from the provided options (Single, Married, Head of Household, or Joint).
13. Enter the **Age of Dependents**.
14. Enter the **Dependent's Income**.
15. Select the **Dependent's Tax Return Filing Status** from the provided options (Not Started, Completed, or Pending Review).
16. If applicable, enter any **Additional Income** details.
17. Select the **Frequency of Additional Income** (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
18. If applicable, enter the **Amount of Additional Income**.
19. Select the **Employment Status Change** reason from the provided options (No Change, Started Working, Stopped Working, or Changed to Part Time).
20. If applicable, enter the **Employment Status Start Date**.
21. If applicable, enter the **Employment Status End Date**.
22. Enter the reason for the **Employment Status Change**.
