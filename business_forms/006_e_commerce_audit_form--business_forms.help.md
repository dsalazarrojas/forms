# e_commerce_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_commerce_audit_form is designed to help e-commerce businesses evaluate their current state and identify areas for improvement. It gathers information on various aspects of an e-commerce platform, including customer satisfaction, sales volume, inventory management, shipping policy, payment processing, customer service, marketing strategy, goals and objectives, target audience, product line, pricing strategy, and shipping frequency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, providing accurate information about your business.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the options that best describe your business.
3. For text fields, provide brief descriptions or answers.
4. Ensure you answer all required fields, as indicated by the "required: true" tag in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the name of your business?** (`business_name`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter the name of your business.
* **What category does your business fall under?** (`business_category`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Choose one of the following categories: retail, online store, e-commerce, wholesale, or other.
* **What is your current website address?** (`website`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter your website URL.
* **What is your current social media presence?** (`social_media`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Choose all the social media platforms you currently use (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, or Other).
* **What is your current customer satisfaction rating?** (`customer_satisfaction`, `number`, required)
	+ Rate your customer satisfaction on a scale of 1-10.
* **What is your current sales volume?** (`sales_volume`, `number`, required)
	+ Enter your current sales volume.
* **What is your current inventory management system?** (`inventory_management`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Choose one of the following options: manual, automated, hybrid, or other.
* **What is your current shipping policy?** (`shipping_policy`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter a brief description of your shipping policy.
* **What is your current payment processing system?** (`payment_processing`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Choose one of the following options: manual, automated, hybrid, or other.
* **What is your current customer service process?** (`customer_service`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter a brief description of your customer service process.
* **What is your current marketing strategy?** (`marketing_strategy`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Choose one of the following options: social media, email marketing, content marketing, influencer marketing, or other.
* **What are your current goals and objectives?** (`goals_objectives`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter a brief description of your goals and objectives.
* **What is your target audience?** (`target_audience`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter a brief description of your target audience.
* **What is your current product line?** (`product_line`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter a brief description of your product line.
* **What is your current pricing strategy?** (`pricing_strategy`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Choose one of the following options: dynamic, fixed, volume-based, tiered, or other.
* **What is your current shipping frequency?** (`shipping_frequency`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Choose one of the following options: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or other.
