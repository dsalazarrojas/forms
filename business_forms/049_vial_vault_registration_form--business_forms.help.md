# vial_vault_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about vials and their storage. It ensures that all vials are properly documented and accounted for in the vault.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of vial from the options provided.
2. Enter the quantity of vials being stored.
3. Choose the storage start date for the vials.
4. Enter the storage location where the vials will be stored.
5. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the vials.
6. Enter the email address of the contact person.
7. Enter the phone number of the contact person.
8. Optionally, enter any additional storage instructions for the vials.
9. Optionally, enter the address of the contact person for the vials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vial Type** (`vial_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of vial you are registering.
  - Choose from the options: 'Yes', 'No'.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the number of vials being stored.
- **Storage Start Date** (`storage_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the vials will be stored.
- **Storage Location** (`storage_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the vials will be stored.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the vials.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Storage Instructions** (`storage_instructions`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional instructions for the storage of the vials.
- **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, optional): Optionally, enter the address of the contact person for the vials.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid any issues or rejections during the registration process.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask a representative for assistance.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
