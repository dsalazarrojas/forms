# Payment Gateway Pricing Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Payment Gateway Pricing Inquiry Form" is used to collect information about payment gateway pricing and related details. This includes inquiring about payouts, payment terms, and other payment-specific information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
2.  Make sure you have all necessary authorizations and approvals before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **business_details** (`payment_gateway_pricing_inquiry_form_business_details`, Text, Optional): Enter your business details such as business name, address, or other relevant contact information.
* **payouts** (`payment_gateway_pricing_inquiry_form_payouts`, Number, Optional): Enter the total amount or frequency of payouts you are inquiring about.
* **payment_term** (`payment_gateway_pricing_inquiry_form_payment_term`, Select One, Optional): Select 'Yes' if you are inquiring about a payment term or 'No' otherwise.
* **pricing_inquiry** (`payment_gateway_pricing_inquiry_form_pricing_inquiry`, Text, Optional): Enter a detailed description of your pricing inquiry.
* **business_payout** (`payment_gateway_pricing_inquiry_form_business_payout`, Number, Optional): Enter the amount you are inquiring about for business payouts.
* **pricing_payout_frequency** (`payment_gateway_pricing_inquiry_form_pricing_payout_frequency`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select 'Yes' for inquiring about a specific payout frequency or 'No' otherwise.
* **pricing_payout_amount** (`payment_gateway_pricing_inquiry_form_pricing_payout_amount`, Number, Optional): Enter the specific amount you are inquiring about for payouts.
* **pricing_payout_date** (`payment_gateway_pricing_inquiry_form_pricing_payout_date`, Date, Optional): Select the date you are inquiring about for payouts.
* **pricing_payout_time** (`payment_gateway_pricing_inquiry_form_pricing_payout_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time you are inquiring about for payouts, if applicable.
* **pricing_payout_frequency** (`payment_gateway_pricing_inquiry_form_pricing_payout_frequency`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select 'Yes' for inquiring about specific payout frequency or 'No' otherwise.

## Tips
Please review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose before submitting it. This form is designed to gather information about payment gateway pricing and related details. If you are unsure about any field, please contact your administrator or support team for clarification.
