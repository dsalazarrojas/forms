# ppe_bulkoorder_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ppe_bulkoorder_quote_request_form is used to request a quote for a bulk order of personal protective equipment (PPE).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business or company that will be placing the order. 
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person completing this form, typically the contact person for the business. 
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address for the contact person. 
* **Contact Phone**: Enter a valid phone number for the contact person. 
* **Business Address**: Enter the address of the business or company. 
* **Quote Date**: Enter the date the quote is required by (optional, but recommended). 
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date the order is needed by (optional, but recommended). 
* **Items Needed**: Select the types of PPE items you are interested in ordering. Select all that apply. 
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of each item you wish to order (if applicable). 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure the quote is generated correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supplier for clarification.
* Once you submit this form, a quote will be generated and sent to you via email.
