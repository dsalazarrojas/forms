# Request For Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request For Proposal Form is used by clients and partners to provide information about a project they are looking to develop with our team. It is used internally to assess the project proposal and determine the best fit for our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project name, project type, and project description.
2. Provide your contact information, including your company name, contact person, email, and phone number.
3. If applicable, select the required documents that need to be submitted with the proposal.
4. Specify any submission requirements or deadlines for the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Project Name** (text, required: false)
The project name is the title of the project you are proposing.

### **Project Type** (select_one, options: Software Development, Web Development, Mobile App Development)
Select the type of project you are proposing (Software Development, Web Development, Mobile App Development).

### **Project Description** (text, required: false)
Provide a brief description of the project, including its goals, objectives, and any relevant details.

### **Company Name** (text, required: false)
Enter the name of your company or organization.

### **Contact Person** (text, required: false)
Enter the name of the person who will be your primary contact for this project.

### **Contact E-mail** (email, required: false)
Enter the email address of the contact person.

### **Contact Phone** (text, required: false)
Enter the phone number of the contact person.

### **Deadline** (date, required: false)
If there is a specific deadline for the project, enter the date.

### **Submission Date** (date, required: false)
If there is a specific submission deadline for your proposal, enter the date.

### **Required Documents** (select_multiple, options: CV, Resume, Portfolio, Other (please specify))
Select the required documents that need to be submitted with the proposal. These may include a CV, resume, and/or portfolio.

### **Submission Requirements** (text, required: false)
If there are any specific submission requirements or guidelines, enter them here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to submit accurate and complete information to ensure a timely and accurate review process.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to our team for clarification.
* We appreciate your proposal and look forward to reviewing it.
