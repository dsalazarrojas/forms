# Nail Art Strip Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help customers order nail art strips online. It collects customer and order information to facilitate the production and delivery of the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Customer Information" section with your details.
2. Select the design, size, and color options for your order in the "Order Details" section.
3. Confirm your order details in the "Order Confirmation" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Information
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): This section collects your name and contact information. Please fill out this section to get started.

### Order Details
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false): Select the design, size, and color options for your nail strip order.

### Color Options
* **Color Options** (`color_options`, text, required: false): Select the color you prefer for your nail strip order.

### Size Options
* **Size Options** (`size_options`, text, required: false): Select the nail strip size for your order.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is currently not providing any information for the user. Please disregard this field for now.

### Order Confirmation
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, required: false): Please review your order details and confirm before proceeding with the order. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all relevant fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your order, please contact us for assistance.
* We will process your order as soon as possible after receiving your submission.
