# independent_contractor_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The independent_contractor_declaration_form is a form for independent contractors to declare their business and contract details to a client or client company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) or a red asterisk. If a field is not marked, it is optional.
2. Enter your business name and address in the respective fields.
3. Provide your business email and phone number for contact purposes.
4. Enter your tax ID number, which is a unique identifier for your business.
5. Select the start and end dates of your contract with the client.
6. Specify the payment terms, frequency, and method with your client.
7. Determine your contractor type, contract status, and business type.
8. Select the payment amount and payment terms frequency.
9. Enter any additional contract terms and conditions.
10. Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business name as it appears on your business license or registration.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your business address, including street number and postal code.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, required): Enter your business email address for contact purposes.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, required): Enter your business phone number for contact purposes.
* **Tax ID Number** (`tax_id_number`, text, required): Enter your business tax ID number, which is a unique identifier for your business.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your contract with the client.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your contract with the client.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required): Specify the payment terms, including any conditions or agreements with your client.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, text, required): Determine the payment frequency, such as weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text, required): Specify the payment method, such as check or bank transfer.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount for each payment period.
* **Contractor Type** (`contractor_type`, select_one, required): Select your contractor type, either Independent Contractor, Employee, or Other.
* **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, text, required): Enter any additional contract terms or conditions.
* **Contractor Status** (`contractor_status`, select_multiple, required): Select your contractor status, either Active, Inactive, or Terminated.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_multiple, required): Select your business type, including Corporation, Partnership, Limited Liability Company (LLC), Sole Proprietorship, or Other.
* **Tax ID Type** (`tax_id_type`, select_one, required): Select your tax ID type, including Employer Identification Number (EIN), Social Security Number (SSN), Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN), or Other.
* **Payment Terms Frequency** (`payment_terms_frequency`, text, required): Specify the payment terms frequency.
* **Contract Duration** (`contract_duration`, text, required): Enter the contract duration.
* **Business Name Alias** (`business_name_alias`, text, required): Enter an alias or nickname for your business.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, required): Enter the first line of your business address.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, text, required): Enter the second line of your business address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city or location.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state or region.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your business postal code.
* **Business Hours** (`business_hours`, time, required): Enter your business operating hours.

Note: The required fields marked with an asterisk (*) or a red asterisk are essential for the form submission.
