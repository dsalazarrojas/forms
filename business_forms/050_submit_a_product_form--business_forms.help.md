# Submit A Product Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a product from users. The form is used to submit a product for internal purposes and the information will be reviewed and processed internally.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the **Product Name** field with the name of the product you are submitting.
2. Enter the **Product Image URL** field with a valid URL of the product's image.
3. Provide a detailed description of the product in the **Product Description** field.
4. Enter the price of the product in the **Product Price** field.
5. Select the **Product Categories** from the list of options provided.
6. Add any additional tags for the product in the **Product Tags** field.
7. Enter your name in the **Submitter Name** field.
8. Enter your email address in the **Submitter Email** field.
9. Enter your phone number in the **Submitter Phone** field (optional).
10. Enter your company name in the **Submitter Company** field (optional).
11. Enter your website in the **Submitter Website** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the product you are submitting.
* **Product Image URL** (Text, Required): Enter a valid URL of the product's image.
* **Product Description** (Text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of the product.
* **Product Price** (Number, Required): Enter the price of the product.
* **Product Categories** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the categories that the product belongs to.
* **Product Tags** (Text, Optional): Add any additional tags for the product.
* **Submitter Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Company** (Text, Optional): Enter your company name.
* **Submitter Website** (Text, Optional): Enter your website.
