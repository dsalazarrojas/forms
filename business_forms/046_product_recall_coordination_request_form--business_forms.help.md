# Product Recall Coordination Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help companies and product manufacturers coordinate product recalls. It helps collect relevant information about product issues that require recall action, ensuring that the recall process is initiated and managed effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief description of the product issue in the "Product Issue Description" field.
2. Enter the "Product Identifier" to help identify the product involved.
3. Enter the name of the "Contact Person" who is responsible for the issue.
4. Enter the "Contact Person's Title" to provide additional context.
5. Select the "Product Type" to help categorize the product affected.
6. Provide a detailed "Product Description" to help identify the product.
7. Enter the date when the issue was first observed in the "Issue Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Issue Description** (`product_issue_description`, text, optional):
	+ A brief description of the product issue that requires recall action.
	+ Please provide a concise summary of the problem.
* **Product Identifier** (`product_identifier`, text, optional):
	+ The unique identifier of the product involved in the issue.
	+ This helps to quickly identify the product.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person_name`, text, optional):
	+ The name of the person responsible for the issue.
	+ Enter the name of the person who reported or is affected by the issue.
* **Contact Person Title** (`contact_person_title`, text, optional):
	+ The title of the contact person.
	+ This provides additional context about the contact person's position or role.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, text, optional):
	+ The type of product affected by the issue.
	+ Choose from options like "Electronics", "Clothing", "Food", etc.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional):
	+ A detailed description of the product involved.
	+ Describe the product in detail to ensure accurate identification.
* **Issue Date** (`issue_date`, date, optional):
	+ The date when the issue was first observed.
	+ Enter the date in MM/DD format.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill in as much information as possible to help us coordinate the recall process efficiently.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask a team member for guidance.
- This form is intended for recall coordination purposes only and should not be used for other product support requests.
