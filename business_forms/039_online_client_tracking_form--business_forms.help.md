# online_client_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online_client_tracking_form is used for tracking online clients. It is essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure that the client's information is up-to-date and correct.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Enter the client's **First Name** in the first field.
2.  Enter the client's **Last Name** in the second field.
3.  Enter the client's **Email** in the third field. Make sure to enter a valid email address.
4.  Enter the client's **Phone** in the fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the client's first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the client's last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the fields accurately and correctly.
- Double-check the information you enter before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant team or department for assistance.
