# product_quality_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about any issues they have experienced with a specific product.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "Company Name" field with the name of the company that sold you the product.
2. Enter your "Email" address so that we can contact you to discuss your issue.
3. Enter the "Product ID" number, which can usually be found on the product packaging or in your order confirmation email.
4. Describe the issue you experienced with the product in the "Description" field.
5. Select the department that handles your issue from the "Department" field.
6. Enter the date and time you first experienced the issue in the "Date/Time of Complaint" field.
7. Choose the type of issue you experienced from the "Complain Type" field.
8. If you have any supporting documentation or images, attach them to this form in the "Attach File" field.
9. Enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
10. Sign and date the form with your "Signature" by writing your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name (text, required):** Enter the name of the company that sold you the product.
* **Email (email, required):** Enter your email address so that we can contact you to discuss your issue.
* **Product ID (number, required):** Enter the product ID number, which can usually be found on the product packaging or in your order confirmation email.
* **Description (text, optional):** Describe the issue you experienced with the product in as much detail as possible.
* **Department (select_one, required):** Select the department that handles your issue from the list of options provided.
* **Date/Time of Complaint (date, required):** Enter the date and time you first experienced the issue.
* **Complain Type (select_multiple, required):** Choose the type of issue you experienced from the list of options provided.
* **Attach File (text, optional):** If you have any supporting documentation or images that can help us understand the issue, attach them to this form.
* **Comments (text, optional):** Enter any additional comments you have about the issue.
* **Signature (note, required):** Sign and date the form by writing your name.
