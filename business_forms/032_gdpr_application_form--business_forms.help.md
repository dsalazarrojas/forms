# GDPR Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The GDPR Application Form is a tool used to gather information from employees regarding their job applications. This form is essential for collecting accurate and complete data about your job title, contact information, and job application details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form completely and accurately for each job application.
2. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure it reflects your current job status and details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (gdpr_application_form_first_name)** (text, not required): Your first name, as per the company's employee directory.
* **Last Name (gdpr_application_form_last_name)** (text, not required): Your last name, as per the company's employee directory.
* **Job Title (gdpr_application_form_job_title)** (text, not required): Your current job title or the title you're applying for.
* **Email (gdpr_application_form_email)** (email, not required): Your company email address associated with your job title.
* **Phone (gdpr_application_form_phone)** (text, not required): Your company phone number associated with your job title.
* **Job Application (gdpr_application_form_job_application)** (text, not required): A brief description of the job you're applying for.
* **Job Category (gdpr_application_form_job_category)** (select_one, not required): Check if your job falls under any of the given categories.
* **Job Type (gdpr_application_form_job_type)** (select_multiple, not required): Check all that apply to your job type.
* **Job Location (gdpr_application_form_job_location)** (text, not required): The location associated with your job title.
* **Resume (gdpr_application_form_resume)** (text, not required): Attach a copy of your resume to support your job application.
* **Job Status (gdpr_application_form_job_status)** (select_one, not required): Check your current job status.
* **Job Description (gdpr_application_form_job_description)** (text, not required): A brief description of your job responsibilities.
* **Date (gdpr_application_form_date)** (date, not required): The date you're submitting this application.
* **Time (gdpr_application_form_time)** (time, not required): The time you're submitting this application.
* **Note (gdpr_application_form_note)** (text, not required): Additional comments or details about your job application.
* **Agree (gdpr_application_form_agree)** (select_one, not required): Confirm that you agree with the GDPR guidelines.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely for each job application.
* Review each field carefully to avoid errors and inconsistencies.
* Make sure to attach a copy of your resume to support your job application.

By following this guide, you should be able to fill out the GDPR Application Form accurately and efficiently, ensuring that your job application is complete and submitted without any issues.
