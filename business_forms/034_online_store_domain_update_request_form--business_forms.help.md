# online_store_domain_update_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update the information associated with an online store domain. It should only be used for authorized updates and changes to the domain.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the current domain information in the `current_domain` field.
2. Enter the new domain information in the `new_domain` field.
3. Select the type of domain change in the `domain_change_type` field (optional).
4. Provide the reason for the change in the `reason_for_change` field.
5. Enter the contact person responsible for the change in the `contact_person` field.
6. Enter the contact email address of the person responsible for the change in the `contact_email` field.
7. Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for the change in the `contact_phone` field.
8. Enter the name of the business in the `business_name` field.
9. Enter the status of the business in the `business_status` field.
10. Select the type of business in the `business_type` field (optional).
11. Enter the address of the business in the `business_address` field.
12. Enter the city of the business in the `business_city` field.
13. Select the state of the business in the `business_state` field (optional).
14. Enter the postal code of the business in the `business_postal_code` field.
15. Enter the zip code of the business in the `business_zip_code` field.
16. Enter the country of the business in the `business_country` field.
17. Enter the website URL of the business in the `website_url` field.
18. Enter the IP address of the business in the `ip_address` field.
19. Enter the port number of the business in the `port` field.
20. Enter the timezone of the business in the `timezone` field.
21. Add any additional notes about the change in the `notes` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Domain Change Request** (`domain_change_request`, text, required): Enter a short description of the domain change request.
* **Current Domain** (`current_domain`, text, required): Enter the current domain name and any other relevant information about the current domain.
* **New Domain** (`new_domain`, text, required): Enter the new domain name and any other relevant information about the new domain.
* **Domain Change Type** (`domain_change_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of domain change (Domain Name, Domain Status, or Domain Type).
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`, text, required): Provide a clear explanation for the domain change.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the change.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the change.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the change.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the business.
* **Business Status** (`business_status`, text, required): Enter the status of the business.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of business (Business, Organization, or Company).
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the business.
* **Business City** (`business_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the business.
* **Business State** (`business_state`, select_one, optional): Select the state of the business (State 1 or State 2).
* **Business Postal Code** (`business_postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code of the business.
* **Business Zip Code** (`business_zip_code`, text, required): Enter the zip code of the business.
* **Business Country** (`business_country`, text, required): Enter the country of the business.
* **Website URL** (`website_url`, text, required): Enter the website URL of the business.
* **IP Address** (`ip_address`, text, required): Enter the IP address of the business.
* **Port** (`port`, text, required): Enter the port number of the business.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, text, required): Enter the timezone of the business.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the change.
