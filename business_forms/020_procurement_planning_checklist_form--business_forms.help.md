# procurement_planning_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide users through the procurement planning process, ensuring that all key steps and activities are completed to ensure a successful procurement process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form, and for each page, select the relevant options to indicate your progress and completion status.
2. On the first page, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have completed the "procurement_planning_checklist".
3. On the second page, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have completed the "procurement_process_steps".
4. On the third page, enter the details of the "procurement_process" in the text field.
5. On the fourth page, enter the number of goods or services being purchased.
6. On the fifth page, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the "vendor_qualifications" are met.
7. On the sixth page, enter the date of the "purchasing_agreement".
8. On the seventh page, enter any notes on the "contract_review".
9. On the eighth page, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether "vendor_evaluation" is complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **procurement_planning_checklist_form_page_1**: 
  **procurement_planning_checklist** (Select Multiple, Yes/No): 
  This field indicates whether you have completed the procurement planning checklist.
* **procurement_planning_checklist_form_page_2**: 
  **procurement_process_steps** (Select One, Yes/No): 
  This field confirms whether you have completed the procurement process steps.
* **procurement_planning_checklist_form_page_3**: 
  **procurement_process** (Text): 
  Enter details of the procurement process.
* **procurement_planning_checklist_form_page_4**: 
  **purchasing_goods_services** (Number): 
  Enter the number of goods or services being purchased.
* **procurement_planning_checklist_form_page_5**: 
  **vendor_qualifications** (Select One, Yes/No): 
  This field confirms whether the vendor qualifications are met.
* **procurement_planning_checklist_form_page_6**: 
  **purchasing_agreement** (Date): 
  Enter the date of the purchasing agreement.
* **procurement_planning_checklist_form_page_7**: 
  **contract_review** (Text): 
  Enter notes on contract review.
* **procurement_planning_checklist_form_page_8**: 
  **vendor_evaluation** (Select Multiple, Yes/No): 
  This field indicates whether vendor evaluation is complete.

## Tips
- Please review each page carefully to ensure all steps are completed before progressing.
- Use "Yes" or "No" to indicate completion status for fields with options.
- Enter relevant details for text fields.
- Ensure accurate completion of numerical fields.
