# Receipt Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Receipt Claim Form is a document used to submit claims for business receipts. It is designed to collect relevant information about the receipt, including date, time, amount, and other supporting details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the receipt details (date, time, amount, description, and receipt date) accurately and completely.
2. Select the expense type (yes or no) as applicable.
3. Review and submit the form once all fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **receipt_claim_form_business_forms** (`receipt_claim_form_business_forms`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the claim.
* **date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the receipt (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **time** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the receipt (e.g., HH:MM AM/PM).
* **amount** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the total amount of the receipt (e.g., $X.XX).
* **description** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of the receipt (e.g., what was purchased).
* **receipt_date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date the receipt was issued (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **expense_type** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the receipt is an expense report or 'No' otherwise.
* **submit** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name and submit the form for review.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to process the claim efficiently.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant department or team.
