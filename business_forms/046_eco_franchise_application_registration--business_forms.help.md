# Eco Franchise Application Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering eco-franchise businesses with the company. It collects personal and business details, sustainability experience, business model, franchise fee, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Address, City, State, Zip Code, and Country.
2. Provide your company details: Company Name, Location, Website (optional), and Bio.
3. Describe your business model and sustainability experience.
4. Enter your franchise fee details: Franchise Fee, Franchise Fee Frequency, Franchise Fee Status, Franchise Fee Date, Franchise Fee Amount, and Franchise Fee Currency.
5. Agree to the terms of the franchise (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of your business.
* **Sustainability Experience** (`sustainability_experience`, `text`, required): Describe your level of experience in sustainable practices.
* **Business Model** (`business_model`, `text`, required): Describe your business model.
* **Franchise Fee** (`franchise_fee`, `number`, required): Enter the franchise fee amount.
* **Franchise Fee Frequency** (`franchise_fee_frequency`, `text`, required): Enter the frequency of the franchise fee.
* **Franchise Fee Status** (`franchise_fee_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the franchise fee (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Franchise Fee Notes** (`franchise_fee_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the franchise fee.
* **Franchise Fee Date** (`franchise_fee_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the franchise fee.
* **Franchise Fee Time** (`franchise_fee_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the franchise fee (optional).
* **Franchise Fee Amount** (`franchise_fee_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of the franchise fee.
* **Franchise Fee Currency** (`franchise_fee_currency`, `select_one`, required): Select the currency of the franchise fee (USD, EUR, or GBP).
* **Franchise Fee Terms** (`franchise_fee_terms`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional terms about the franchise fee.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your company's address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of your business.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state of your business.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter the zip code of your business.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Enter the country of your business.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, optional): Enter your company's website (optional).
* **Bio** (`bio`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your company's business.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select whether you agree to the terms of the franchise (Yes or No).
