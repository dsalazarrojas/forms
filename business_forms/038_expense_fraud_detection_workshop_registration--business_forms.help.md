# Expense Fraud Detection Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Expense Fraud Detection Workshop Registration" form is used to register users for an expense fraud detection workshop. This form is intended for users who are interested in learning about fraud detection techniques and how to improve their company's internal controls.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
* Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** in the first page.
* Enter your **Phone** number, **Company**, **Job Title**, and **Role** information.
* Fill in your **Department**, **Job Function**, and **Location** on the second page.
* Enter your **Job Title 2**, **Department 2**, and **Job Function 2**.
* Fill in your **Company 2** and **Phone 2** on the second page.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field. Please enter the name by which you are known professionally.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field. Please enter the name by which you are known professionally.
* **Email**: Enter your business email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Role**: Enter your role in the company.
* **Department**: Enter the name of your department.
* **Job Function**: Enter your job function.
* **Location**: Enter the location of your workplace.
* **Job Title 2**: Enter your secondary job title.
* **Department 2**: Enter the name of your secondary department.
* **Job Function 2**: Enter your secondary job function.
* **Company 2**: Enter the name of your secondary company.
* **Phone 2**: Enter your secondary phone number.
> Note: If you have any doubts about the fields, please contact us for clarification.
## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that you receive a confirmation email with workshop details.
* If you have any issues or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* The form is available in two pages, please fill out the first page first and then move on to the second page.
* Make sure to review your information before submitting the form to avoid any mistakes.
