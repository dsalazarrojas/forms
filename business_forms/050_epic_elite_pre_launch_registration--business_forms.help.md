# epic_elite_pre_launch_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The epic_elite_pre_launch_registration form is a comprehensive registration form designed for users to provide detailed information about themselves and their job roles within the company. The form is used to collect data for internal purposes only and is not intended for public use. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your data is up-to-date and can be used effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure that you have all the necessary information required for the form.
2.  Start by filling out the form with your company name.
3.  Proceed to fill out the required fields (marked with an asterisk \*), which include:
	* First name
	* Last name
	* Email
	* Date of Birth
	* Phone Number
	* Country
	* City
	* State
	* Zip Code
	* Work Status
	* Job Category
	* Assigned Tool
4.  You can skip the optional fields (not marked with an asterisk \*) if they are not relevant to you. These include:
	* Job Title
	* Job Description
	* Job Type
	* Work Location
	* Job Description 2
	* Date of Joining
	* Job Status
	* Date
	* Time
	* Address
	* Work Status
	* Assigned Tool 2

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (text, required): Enter your company name.
* **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Job Description** (note, optional): Enter a description of your job.
* **Job Category** (select one, required): Select the category of your job, which includes Sales, Support, and Development.
* **Job Status** (select multiple, optional): Select your current job status, which includes Active, On leave, and Inactive.
* **Job Type** (select one, optional): Select the type of your job, which includes Full-time and Contract.
* **Work Location** (select one, optional): Select the location where you work, which includes New York, Los Angeles, and Chicago.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Date of Joining** (date, optional): Enter the date you joined the company.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Description 2** (note, optional): Enter an additional description of your job.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter a date (format not specified).
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter a time (format not specified).
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Country** (text, required): Enter your country of residence.
* **City** (text, required): Enter your city of residence.
* **State** (text, required): Enter your state of residence.
* **Zip Code** (text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Work Status** (select one, optional): Select your work status, which includes Active, On leave, and Inactive.
* **Assigned Tool** (select one, required): Select the tool assigned to you, which includes chatjimmy and Other.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (select one, optional): Select whether you have been assigned another tool, which includes Yes and No.
