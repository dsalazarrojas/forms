# insurance-agent-information-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather and store information about insurance agents working with your agency. It helps us keep track of their details and ensures that we have accurate and up-to-date records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields below. You can save and submit your completed form at any time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your agency where you work.
* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as an agent.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your agency's email address.
* **Insurance Lines** (`insurance_lines`, select multiple, required): Choose the types of insurance you provide, such as Life, Auto, Health, or Home.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, text, required): Enter your agent license number.
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your certification.
* **Experience Years** (`experience_years`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have experience in the insurance industry.

## Tips
- Please ensure you're filling out accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or HR department for assistance. 
- If you have any questions or issues, you can contact our support team at [support@youragency.com](mailto:support@youragency.com).
