# Evidence Chain Of Custody Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Evidence Chain Of Custody Tracking Form is designed to monitor the handling and storage of evidence in a case from collection to presentation in court.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page to understand what information is required.
2. Complete the form page by page, starting from page 1.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Page 1
* **Page 1** (page_1, text, required: false):
    This is the first page of the form where you will start documenting the chain of custody.
* **Page 2: Case Details** (case_details, text, required: false):
    On this page, provide a brief description of the case, including relevant case details.
* **Page 3: Evidence Handling** (evidence_handling, number, required: false):
    Enter the number of people handling the evidence, e.g., 1, 2, or 3.
* **Page 4: Evidence Description** (evidence_description, text, required: false):
    Describe the evidence being tracked, including its type and condition.
* **Page 5: Tracking History** (tracking_history, time, required: false):
    Record the date and time when the evidence was handled or stored.
* **Page 6: Tracking History Details** (tracking_history_details, select multiple, required: false):
    Choose multiple options that describe the events that occurred during the chain of custody:
        + option 1: Label
        + option 2: Stored
        + option 3: Transported
* **Page 7: Case Status** (case_status, select one, required: false):
    Select the current case status:
        + option 1: Open
        + option 2: Closed
        + option 3: Pending
* **Page 8: Evidence Disposition** (evidence_disposition, text, required: false):
    Specify the final disposition of the evidence, e.g., destroyed, disposed of, or stored.
* **Page 9: Case Notes** (case_notes, text, required: false):
    Add any additional comments or notes about the case.
* **Page 10: Notes** (notes, text, required: false):
    Enter any additional comments or notes about the form or case.
* **Page 11: Case Notes Status** (case_notes_status, text, required: false):
    Update the status of the case notes.

Tips:
* Be sure to review each page carefully before proceeding.
* Fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and thorough documentation.
* If unsure about a field, refer to the relevant case or form guidelines.
* Save the form regularly to ensure that your work is not lost.
* If you need help, consult with a colleague or supervisor.
