# Merchandising Request And Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their merchandising requests and orders. Fill out this form to provide your details and preferences, and our team will assist you with your merchandising needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked as such.
2. For non-required fields, you can leave them blank or fill them out as needed.
3. Ensure you provide accurate and complete information in all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer** (`Customer`, text, required: false)
Enter your name and contact information, if needed.

### 2. **Order** (`order`, text, required: false)
Describe your order, including any specific instructions or requests.

### 3. **Product** (`Product`, text, required: false)
Name or describe the product you are interested in.

### 4. **Quantity** (`Quantity`, number, required: false)
Enter the quantity of the product you want to order.

### 5. **Price** (`Price`, number, required: false)
If you have a specific price in mind, enter it here.

### 6. **Product Type** (`Product Type`, select_one, required: false)
Select the type of product you are interested in from the options provided.

### 7. **Product Categories** (`Product Categories`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select all applicable categories for your product from the options provided.

### 8. **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false)
Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted.

### 9. **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false)
Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted by phone.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
- We'll be in touch shortly after receiving your form submission to discuss your merchandising needs.
