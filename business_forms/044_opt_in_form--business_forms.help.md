# Opt In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Opt In Form is used to collect contact information and opt-in preferences from customers, allowing them to choose how they want to receive updates and offers from our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select how you would like to receive updates and offers in the "receive_updates" field. You can choose from the following options:
	* Exclusive Offers
	* Special Deals
	* Newsletters

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (Text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (Text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **email** (Email, required: false): Enter your email address. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `user@example.com`).
* **phone** (Text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **receive_updates** (Select multiple, required: false):
	+ **Exclusive Offers**: Receive exclusive offers and promotions.
	+ **Special Deals**: Receive special offers and discounts.
	+ **Newsletters**: Receive regular newsletters with company updates and news.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your contact information accurately to ensure you receive updates and offers correctly.
* You can choose multiple options for how you want to receive updates and offers.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact our support team.
