# Logo Upload And Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Logo Upload And Change Form" is designed to collect all necessary information for uploading and modifying a logo for a business or organization. This form allows you to update the logo's properties and provide additional context for the change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Upload your new logo in the "Upload new logo" field.
2. Select the logo properties you want to change, such as color, size, and orientation.
3. Provide a reason for the logo change in the "What is the reason for the logo change?" field.
4. Confirm your details by entering your email, phone, date, and time in the corresponding fields.
5. Add any additional notes about the logo change in the "Note" field.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Logo Upload**: Upload your new logo to replace the current one. (Required)
* **What is the reason for the logo change?**: Provide a brief description of why the logo needs to be updated. (Optional)
* **Upload new logo**: Upload a new logo file. (Required)
* **Why do you want to change the logo?**: This field is likely for additional context, and can be left blank if not necessary. (Optional)
* **Logo change request**: This field is probably not necessary as it is redundant with "What is the reason for the logo change?". (Optional)
* **Logo description**: Add any additional notes about the logo. (Optional)
* **Logo type**: Select whether the logo is a "Yes" or "No" type. (Optional)
* **Logo color**: Select the color of the logo, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Logo size**: Select the size of the logo, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Logo orientation**: Select the orientation of the logo, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Logo position**: Select the position of the logo, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Logo width**: Enter the width of the logo, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Logo height**: Enter the height of the logo, if applicable. (Optional)
* **File size**: Enter the file size of the uploaded logo, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Email**: Enter your email address for confirmation. (Required)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for confirmation. (Optional)
* **Date**: Enter the date of the logo change, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Time**: Enter the time of the logo change, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Note**: Add any additional notes about the logo change. (Optional)
* **Confirm email**: Confirm your email address. (Required)
* **Confirm phone**: Confirm your phone number. (Optional)
* **Confirm date**: Confirm the date of the logo change, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Confirm time**: Confirm the time of the logo change, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Confirm note**: Confirm your notes about the logo change. (Optional)
* **Submit**: Click to submit the form with all the information provided. (Required)
