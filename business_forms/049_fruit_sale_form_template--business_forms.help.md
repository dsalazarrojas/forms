# fruit_sale_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fruit sale form template is a tool for collecting customer information and order details for fruit sales transactions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in the customer's name in the customer name field.
- Select the type of fruit(s) you would like to purchase in the fruit type field. You can choose one or multiple options from the list.
- Enter the quantity of the fruit(s) you would like to purchase.
- Enter your email address in the customer email field.
- Enter your phone number in the customer phone field.
- Select the date and time of your order in the order date and time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, required): This field is where you enter your full name.
- **Fruit Type** (fruit_type, select multiple, required): Select the type(s) of fruit you would like to purchase from the available options (Apple, Banana, Orange, Mango, Papaya).
- **Quantity** (quantity, number, required): Enter the number of fruit(s) you would like to purchase.
- **Customer Email** (customer_email, email, required): Enter your email address where you would like to receive communications about your order.
- **Customer Phone** (customer_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
- **Order Date** (order_date, date, required): Select the date of your order.
- **Order Time** (order_time, time, required): Select the time of your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field accurately to ensure a smooth sales process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask our sales team.
- This form is for sales purposes only; please do not submit fake or invalid information.
