# Sponsorship Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request approval for sponsorship events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the following:
	* Enter the name of the event in the "Event Name" field.
	* Select the date of the event using the "Event Date" field.
	* Enter the name of the contact person in the "Contact Name" field.
	* Enter the contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
	* Enter the organization information in the "Organization" field.
	* Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
2. If necessary, fill in the sponsorship details in the "Sponsorship Details" field.
3. If relevant, specify the sponsorship amount in the "Sponsorship Amount" field.
4. Choose the type(s) of event(s) this sponsorship is related to in the "Event Type" field. This can be one or multiple of the provided options. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email address.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the organization name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Address** (`sponsor_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the sponsor.
* **Sponsorship Details** (`sponsorship_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the sponsorship.
* **Sponsorship Amount** (`sponsorship_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the sponsorship. 
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type(s) of event(s) this sponsorship is related to. Options: Event, Conference, Festival, Other.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill in all required fields.
- Make sure to enter accurate and relevant event and sponsorship details.
- Review and proofread the form to ensure it's complete and accurate before submitting.
