# Laundry Pickup Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request laundry service for customers. Fill out this form to let us know about your preferred service type, pickup date and time, and any other details we need to know.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select the service type you need from the options provided.
2. Enter your preferred date for pickup in the "Preferred Pickup Date" field.
3. Enter your preferred pickup time in the "Preferred Pickup Time" field (24-hour format, e.g., 14:00).
4. Enter your pickup address.
5. Fill out the contact information: First name, Last name, Contact Email, and Contact Phone number.
6. If you have any special instructions or additional information, type it in the "Special Instructions" field.
7. Finally, provide any additional information about your request in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What service type would you like?** (`laundry_service_type`, select_one, required/optional)
	+ Select one of the options: Wash, Dry, Wash & Fold, Dry & Fold, or Other.
* **Preferred pickup date** (`preferred_date`, date, required/optional)
	+ Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-07-25).
* **Preferred pickup time** (`pickup_time`, time, required/optional)
	+ Enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Pickup address** (`pickup_address`, text, required/optional)
	+ Type your full address where you would like to be picked up.
* **First name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required/optional)
	+ Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required/optional)
	+ Enter your last name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional)
	+ Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional)
	+ Enter your contact phone number.
* **Special instructions** (`laundry_instructions`, note, required/optional)
	+ If you have any special instructions or information, type it here.
* **Additional info** (`contact_message`, text, required/optional)
	+ Provide any additional information about your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are not duplicating an existing service request.
* Enter all required fields (marked as "required") for a successful submission.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
