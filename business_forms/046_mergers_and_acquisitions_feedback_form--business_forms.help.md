# Mergers And Acquisitions Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from different teams involved in a merger and acquisition (M&A) process. The feedback will help the organization understand the effectiveness of the integration process and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address or phone number so that we can contact you to discuss your feedback.
2. Provide your feedback on the company's name.
3. Enter the acquisition date of the company.
4. Enter the value of the deal.
5. Rate the satisfaction level of different teams involved in the M&A process.
6. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
7. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Form** (`1`, `text`, optional): This field is a text area where you can provide any general feedback about the M&A process.
* **Company Name** (`2`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the company involved in the merger.
* **Acquisition Date** (`3`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the acquisition.
* **Deal Value** (`4`, `number`, optional): Enter the value of the deal.
* **Finance Teams Feedback** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the satisfaction level of the finance team involved in the M&A process.
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Very Not Satisfied
* **Legal Teams Feedback** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the satisfaction level of the legal team involved in the M&A process.
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Very Not Satisfied
* **Integration Teams Feedback** (`7`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the satisfaction level of the integration teams involved in the M&A process.
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Very Not Satisfied
* **Corporate Development Teams Feedback** (`8`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the satisfaction level of the corporate development team involved in the M&A process.
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Very Not Satisfied
* **Comments** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Email** (`10`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you to discuss your feedback.
* **Phone Number** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you to discuss your feedback.
