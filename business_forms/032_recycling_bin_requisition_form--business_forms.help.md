# recycling_bin_requisition_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "recycling_bin_requisition_form" is used to request a recycling bin for a specific business unit. The form allows you to select the business unit, provide contact information, and specify the date and quantity of the bin requested.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the business unit**: Choose the correct business unit from the dropdown list. This will ensure that the request is directed to the right team.
2. **Provide contact information**: Fill out the "contact_name", "contact_email", and "contact_phone" fields with your name, email address, and phone number. This is the best way to get in touch with you.
3. **Specify the date and quantity**: Enter the "date_ordered" and "quantity_ordered" fields with the desired date and number of recycling bins you need.
4. **Add any additional notes or comments**: Use the "notes" field to provide any additional context or details about your request.
5. **Save your changes**: Click "submit" to send your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Business Unit**: `select_business_unit` (select_one, required)
	+ Select the specific business unit for which you are requesting a recycling bin.
* **Contact Name**: `contact_name` (text, required: false)
	+ Enter your name as the point of contact for this request.
* **Contact Email**: `contact_email` (email, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Phone**: `contact_phone` (text, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Date Ordered**: `date_ordered` (date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date you need the recycling bin by.
* **Quantity Ordered**: `quantity_ordered` (number, required: false)
	+ Enter the number of recycling bins you need.
* **Notes**: `notes` (note, required: false)
	+ Add any additional information about your request.
* **Comments**: `comments` (text, required: false)
	+ Add any additional comments or details about your request.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct business unit to ensure the request is directed to the right team.
* Enter accurate contact information so that we can get in touch with you.
* Specify the date and quantity of recycling bins you need to ensure timely delivery.
