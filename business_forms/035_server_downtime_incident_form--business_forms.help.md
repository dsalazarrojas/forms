# Server Downtime Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form helps us document and track server downtime incidents, making it easier for our IT team to identify and resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form

- **Server Downtime Incident Form** - Start by providing a brief title of the incident.
- **Server Downtime Incident Start Time** - Enter the exact time when you first noticed the incident occurred.
- **Server Downtime Incident End Time** - Enter the time when you last checked for the incident.
- **Server Downtime Incident Description** - Provide a concise description of what happened.
- **Incident Affected Servers** - Select the specific servers affected by the incident.
- **Priority** - Choose the priority level of the incident (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Status** - Select the current status of the incident (Open, In progress, or Resolved).
- **Comment** - Add any additional comments or details about the incident.
- **Resolved Time** - If applicable, enter the date and time when the incident was resolved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **incident_title** (Server Downtime Incident Form)
	+ A brief title of the incident.
* **incident_start_time** (Server Downtime Incident Start Time)
	+ The exact time when you first noticed the incident occurred.
* **incident_end_time** (Server Downtime Incident End Time)
	+ The time when you last checked for the incident.
* **incident_description** (Server Downtime Incident Description)
	+ A concise description of what happened.
* **incident_affected_servers** (Incident Affected Servers)
	+ Select the specific servers affected by the incident.
* **incident_priority** (Priority)
	+ Choose the priority level of the incident (High, Medium, or Low).
	+ If unsure, consult with the team leader or supervisor.
* **incident_status** (Status)
	+ Select the current status of the incident (Open, In progress, or Resolved).
* **incident_comment** (Comment)
	+ Add any additional comments or details about the incident.
* **incident_resolved_time** (Resolved Time)
	+ If applicable, enter the date and time when the incident was resolved.

## Tips

- Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to report an incident.
- Check with your team leader or supervisor if you are unsure about any field.
