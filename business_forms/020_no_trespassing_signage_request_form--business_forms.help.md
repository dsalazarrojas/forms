# no_trespassing_signage_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request signage for a specific property to deter trespasseders. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Property Owner**'s name in the **Property Owner** field.
2.  Select the correct **Property Details** (e.g. Building A, Building B, Building C) that corresponds with the property where the signage is needed.
3.  Enter the **Request Date** in the requested format (YYYY-MM-DD).
4.  Choose the correct **Request Time** (e.g., 08:00, 12:00).
5.  Choose the **Authorization** type (e.g., HOA prophets and managers, Facility team, Property Owner) that will be able to approve the request.
6.  Select the desired **Signage Type** (e.g., Official No Trespassing Sign, Warning Sign, Do Not Enter Sign).
7.  Select the **Request Status** (e.g., New, Pending prophets and managers, Completed).
8.  Choose the personnel or team that should be assigned to handle the request (e.g., Facility team, Property owner, minimalist).
9.  Enter any additional **Notes** about the request.
10. Enter your **Email** for contact purposes.
11. Enter your **Phone** number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Property Owner**: Enter the name of the owner of the property where the signage is needed.
*   **Property Details**: Select the building where the signage is required (e.g., Building A, Building B, Building C).
*   **Request Date**: Enter the date when the signage is needed.
*   **Request Time**: Choose the time of day when the signage is needed.
*   **Authorization**: Choose who should be able to approve your request (e.g., HOA prophets and managers, Facility team, Property Owner).
*   **Signage Type**: Choose the type of signage desired (e.g., Official No Trespassing Sign, Warning Sign, Do Not Enter Sign).
*   **Request Status**: Choose the status of your request (e.g., New, Pending prophets and managers, Completed).
*   **Assigned To**: Choose who should be assigned to handle your request.
*   **Notes**: Enter any additional information about your request.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
