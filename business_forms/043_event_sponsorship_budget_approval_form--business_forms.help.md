# Event Sponsorship Budget Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Sponsorship Budget Approval Form is designed to facilitate the process of seeking approval for event sponsorship budgets within the organization. It serves as a structured platform for submitting proposals and associated details for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please refer to the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Sponsorship Budget Approval Form**: This field is the title of the form and should not be edited. It is provided for reference only.
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the form submission, if required.
* **Category**: Select the category of the event you are seeking sponsorship for.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Budget Amount**: Enter the budget amount for the event.
* **Event Sponsorship Name**: Enter the name of the event's sponsor.
* **Event Sponsorship Proposal Details**: Enter any additional details related to the event sponsorship proposal.
* **Approval Request Date**: Enter the date you would like the proposal to be approved.
* **Approval Request Time**: Enter the time you would like the proposal to be approved.
* **Event Sponsorship Budget**: Enter the budget amount for the event.
* **Event Sponsorship Proposal Status**: Select the current status of your proposal from the options provided.
* **Event Sponsorship Proposal Status Details**: Enter any additional details related to the proposal status.
* **Event Sponsorship Proposal Comments**: Enter any comments about the proposal.
* **Event Sponsorship Proposal Remarks**: Enter any additional remarks about the proposal.
* **Event Sponsorship Proposal Approval**: Indicate whether you have received approval or not.
* **Event Sponsorship Proposal Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the proposal.
* **Event Sponsorship Proposal Files**: Attach any relevant proposal files that support your request.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email for follow-up communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Form Submission User**: Enter the user submitting the form for record-keeping purposes.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all necessary information and supporting documents before submitting the form.
* Review your form submission carefully before submitting it to prevent errors.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only. Do not share it with external parties without approval.
