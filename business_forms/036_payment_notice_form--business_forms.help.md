# Payment Notice Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Payment Notice Form is a document used to communicate payment notices to tenants. This form provides a structured way to record and convey payment information, including overdue rent, past due rent, or other payment-related matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Payment Notice Form, follow the steps below:
1. Select the relevant notice type (e.g., Overdue Rent, Past Due Rent, or Other).
2. Enter the payment date.
3. Enter the payment amount.
4. Choose the payment method (e.g., Online Payment, In Person, or By Mail).
5. Add any additional notes about the notice.
6. Enter the tenant's email address to send the notice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Notice Type (id: 3)** 
* **Notice Type (label: Notice Type)**: Select the type of notice being sent to the tenant. Choose from Overdue Rent, Past Due Rent, or Other.

### 2. **Payment Date (id: 4)** 
* **Payment Date (label: Payment Date)**: Enter the date of the payment that is overdue, past due, or related to the notice.

### 3. **Payment Amount (id: 5)** 
* **Payment Amount (label: Payment Amount)**: Enter the amount of the payment that is overdue, past due, or related to the notice.

### 4. **Payment Method (id: 6)** 
* **Payment Method (label: Payment Method)**: Choose the method of payment that was made or is expected to be made (e.g., Online Payment, In Person, or By Mail).

### 5. **Note (id: 7)** 
* **Note (label: Note)**: Add any additional comments or information about the payment notice.

### 6. **Email Address (id: 8)** 
* **Email Address (label: Email Address)**: Enter the email address of the tenant to whom the notice will be sent.

Remember to fill in the form accurately and completely to ensure that the notice is helpful in communicating with tenants. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to your supervisor or the support team.
