# Insurance Billing Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Billing Waiver Form is used to collect personal contact information from customers to provide them with insurance billing information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user name in the "User name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
4. Select a date for the billing period in the "Date" field.
5. Enter the amount you would like to be billed in the "Amount" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User name**: Enter your name as it is listed on your company's records.
  - **Type**: Text field.
  - **Required**: No, but it is recommended for proper billing processing.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you would like to receive billing information.
  - **Type**: Email field.
  - **Required**: No, but it is recommended for proper billing processing.
- **Phone number**: Enter your company phone number where you can be reached for billing inquiries.
  - **Type**: Text field.
  - **Required**: No, but it is recommended for proper billing processing.
- **Date**: Select a date for the billing period using the calendar dropdown.
  - **Type**: Date field.
  - **Required**: No, but it is recommended for proper billing processing.
- **Amount**: Enter the amount you would like to be billed.
  - **Type**: Number field.
  - **Required**: No, but it is required for billing processing.
