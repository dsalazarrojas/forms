# Entrepreneurial Networking Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entrepreneurial Networking Event Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from entrepreneurs and attendees of an event. It helps us understand who is attending, their role, and any other necessary details for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Double-check that your contact information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your full name.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your company or organization name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your job title or profession.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your job or profession.
* **Job Industry** (`job_industry`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the industry related to your job or profession.
* **Role At Event** (`role_at_event`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your role at the event or if you have a specific role in mind, enter "Other" and provide details in the "Job Description" field.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of the event or conference.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): Please select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false): Please select the time of the event.
* **Attendee Count** (`attendee_count`, `number`, required: false): Please enter the number of attendees you will bring along.
* **Attendee Cost** (`attendee_cost`, `number`, required: false): Please enter the cost per attendee.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the payment method you will use.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter any additional payment information.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter any extra details about your attendance.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, `text`, required: false): Please enter any details about your attendees.
* **Attendee Details 2** (`attendee_details_2`, `text`, required: false): Please enter any additional details about your attendees.
* **Payment Method 2** (`payment_method_2`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the payment method you will use for your attendees.
* **Payment Info 2** (`payment_info_2`, `text`, required: false): Please enter any additional payment information for your attendees.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Once you've completed the form, click this button to submit your information.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Be as accurate as possible when entering your information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
