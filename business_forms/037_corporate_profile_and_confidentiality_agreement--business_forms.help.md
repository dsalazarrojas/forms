## Corporate Profile And Confidentiality Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Corporate Profile And Confidentiality Agreement form is a crucial document that outlines the company's profile and a confidentiality agreement that must be filled out accurately and thoroughly. This form is used to establish a relationship of trust and confidentiality between the company and other entities. It is essential to fill out the form with the correct information to ensure that your company's profile and interests are represented accurately and without miscommunication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

*   Read through the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information required for the form before starting.
*   Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
*   Review the form for any errors or mistakes before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name (Company Name)** (`company_name`, text, required: false): This field is for the company's name. Please enter the company's full name as it appears on its official documentation.
*   **Contact Person (Contact Person)** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the company's profile and confidentiality agreement.
*   **Job Title (Job Title)** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the job title of the contact person.
*   **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Company Address (Company Address)** (`company_address`, text, required: false): Enter the physical address of the company.
*   **Job Description (Job Description)** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the job or role of the contact person.
*   **Company Industry (Company Industry)** (`company_industry`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the company is in the industry, 'No' otherwise.
*   **Company Type (Company Type)** (`company_type`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the company type is relevant, 'No' otherwise.
*   **Company Status (Company Status)** (`company_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the active or inactive status of the company.
*   **Confidentiality Agreement (Confidentiality Agreement)** (`confidentiality_agreement`, time, required: false): This field is not applicable in this form as it is set to "time" and does not have any options. However, it can be used to add any other agreement or terms related to confidentiality.
