# Credit Card Fee Refund Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Credit Card Fee Refund Form is used to process credit card fee refunds for customers. This form gathers essential information needed to verify and process the refund.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Provide your customer's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Include your customer's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your customer's account number in the "Account Number" field.
5. Select the fee date for the refund in the "Fee Date" field.
6. Enter the fee amount being refunded in the "Fee Amount" field.
7. Choose a valid reason for the refund from the "Reason for Refund" dropdown menu.
8. Attach supporting documents, such as a transaction receipt, to the "Attach File" field.
9. If required by your customer, have them sign and date the form with the "Customer Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, **Required):** Enter the customer's name as it appears on their account records.
* **Email** (Email, email, **Optional):** Enter your customer's email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, **Optional):** Enter your customer's phone number.
* **Account Number** (Account Number, text, **Required):** Enter the account number associated with the refund.
* **Fee Date** (Fee Date, date, **Required):** Select the date when the fee was charged or is due for refund.
* **Fee Amount** (Fee Amount, number, **Required):** Enter the amount of the fee being refunded.
* **Reason for Refund** (Reason for Refund, select_one, **Optional):** Choose one of the provided options for the reason of the refund.
	+ Excessive Fees: The fee was charged excessively or unfairly.
	+ Incorrect Charges: The fee was incorrectly charged or applied.
	+ Other: Other valid reason not listed here (enter a brief description).
* **Attach File** (Attach File, select_multiple, **Optional):** Upload a copy of the transaction receipt or supporting document.
* **Customer Signature** (Customer Signature, note, **Optional):** Sign and date this form, if required.
* **Date of Signature** (Date of Signature, date, **Optional):** Date the form when signed.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields correctly before submitting the form.
- Upload supporting documents to help verify the refund request.
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
