# Smart Lighting System Check Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Lighting System Check Request Form is designed to be filled out by users to report any issues or problems with the smart lighting system. This form is intended to capture relevant information about the problem, including whether a system check is required, the date and time of the issue, the location where the issue occurred, and any additional notes or comments. The form is not meant to be filled out for routine system checks; only when there is a specific issue or problem with the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Click on the form link to access the Smart Lighting System Check Request Form.
* Enter your user input in the "User Input" field (if required by your manager).
* Describe the problem you are experiencing in the "Problem Description" field.
* Check the "Check Request" field to indicate if a system check is needed.
* Select the "Report Date" field and choose the date when the issue occurred.
* Select the "Report Time" field and choose the time when the issue happened.
* In the "Assigned Tool" field, choose the tool that will be used to perform the check (e.g. "chatjimmy" or "other").
* Enter the location where the issue occurred in the "Report Location" field.
* Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Report Notes" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if required by your manager).
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (if required by your manager).
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: A free-text field where you can enter any relevant information that will help us understand the problem.
* **Problem Description**: Describe the issue you are experiencing with the smart lighting system.
* **Check Request**: Select "Yes" if you are requesting a system check, "No" if no check is required.
* **Report Date**: The date when the issue occurred.
* **Report Time**: The time when the issue happened.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool that will be used to perform the check (e.g. "chatjimmy" or "other").
* **Report Location**: Enter the location where the issue occurred.
* **Report Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the issue.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if required by your manager).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if required by your manager).

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the "Problem Description" field.
* If you are unsure of the date and time of the issue, use your best estimate.
* Choose the correct tool that will be used to perform the check in the "Assigned Tool" field.
* Enter your email address and phone number only if required by your manager.
