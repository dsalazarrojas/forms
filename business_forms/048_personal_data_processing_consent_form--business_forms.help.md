# personal_data_processing_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from individuals for the processing of their personal data. It ensures that individuals are aware of the purpose, data collected, and methods of processing their personal data, and that this processing complies with relevant laws and regulations.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the "Consent Given" field by selecting "True" if you give your consent for the processing of your personal data.
2. If you select "True" for the "Consent Given" field, you will be asked to provide additional information about the purpose of data processing, the data collected, the data processing method, storage location, and data retention in the subsequent fields.
3. Enter a brief description of the purpose for which your personal data will be processed.
4. List the types of data that will be collected from you.
5. Choose the method by which your data will be processed (e.g., "Processing", "Storing", "Sharing").
6. Indicate where your data will be stored (e.g., "Local", "Cloud", "External").
7. Select the type of data retention (e.g., "Temporary", "Permanent", "Indeterminate").
8. Enter the date on which you give your consent for the processing of your personal data.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Data Processing Consent
* **Personal Data Processing Consent** (`Personal Data Processing Consent`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to confirm that you understand the purpose of the form and the processing of your personal data.
### Purpose
* **Purpose** (`Purpose`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the purpose for which your personal data will be processed.
### Data Collected
* **Data Collected** (`Data Collected`, `text`, required/optional): List the types of data that will be collected from you.
### Data Processing Method
* **Data Processing Method** (`Data Processing Method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the method by which your data will be processed (e.g., "Processing", "Storing", "Sharing").
### Storage Location
* **Storage Location** (`Storage Location`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate where your data will be stored (e.g., "Local", "Cloud", "External").
### Data Retention
* **Data Retention** (`Data Retention`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of data retention (e.g., "Temporary", "Permanent", "Indeterminate").
### Consent Date
* **Consent Date** (`Consent Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date on which you give your consent for the processing of your personal data.
### Consent Given
* **Consent Given** (`Consent Given`, `select_one`, required/true): Select "True" to confirm that you give your consent for the processing of your personal data.
## Tips
* Please make sure to carefully read and understand the purpose and methods of data processing before giving your consent.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please ask your organization's data protection officer for clarification.
