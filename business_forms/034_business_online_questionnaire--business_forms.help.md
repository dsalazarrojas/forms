# Business Online Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your business, including its owner, operations, financials, goals, and challenges. The purpose of this form is to help us understand your business needs and provide personalized coaching and guidance to support your growth and success.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out each field with the requested information. You can type in your answers directly into the fields provided.
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* For fields with multiple options, select one or more options that best describe your business.
* You can add any additional information in the "Additional Information" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Owner Name**: Enter the full name of the person responsible for the business.
* **Business Name**: Enter the legal name of the business.
* **Business Email**: Enter the primary email address associated with the business.
* **Business Phone**: Enter the contact phone number for the business.
* **Business Website**: Enter the URL of the business website (if applicable).
* **Business Type**: Select the legal structure of the business (e.g., sole proprietorship, partnership, LLC, etc.).
* **Industry**: Select the primary industry of the business (e.g., technology, healthcare, retail, etc.).
* **Years in Operation**: Enter the number of years the business has been in operation.
* **Number of Employees**: Enter the total number of employees.
* **Current Annual Revenue**: Select the approximate annual revenue of the business (e.g., under $50K, $50K to $100K, etc.).
* **Business Stage**: Select the current stage of the business (e.g., idea stage, startup, growth, etc.).
* **Primary Products or Services**: Enter what the business primarily sells or offers.
* **Target Market**: Select who the business's primary customers are (e.g., local consumers, national consumers, B2B companies, etc.).
* **Unique Value Proposition**: Describe what makes the business different from others in the same industry.
* **Main Competitors**: List the primary competitors.
* **Current Marketing Channels**: Select the current marketing channels used by the business (e.g., social media, email marketing, etc.).
* **Biggest Challenges**: Select the main challenges faced by the business (e.g., cash flow, customer acquisition, etc.).
* **Business Goals for Next Year**: Enter the main business goals for the next year.
* **Areas Where You Need Coaching**: Select the areas where the business could use additional coaching and guidance (e.g., business strategy, marketing, sales, etc.).
* **Previous Coaching Experience**: Select whether the business has had previous coaching experience.
* **Coaching Budget**: Select the maximum monthly budget for coaching services.
* **Commitment Level**: Select how committed the business is to growth and change.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information that may be helpful in understanding the business's needs.
