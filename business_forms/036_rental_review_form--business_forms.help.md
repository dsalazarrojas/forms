# Rental Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user feedback and reviews about rental experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **name** in the text field above. This is the name of the person submitting the review.
2. Enter your **email** in the email field, so we can contact you if we need more information or to follow up on your review.
3. Enter your **phone number**, if you would like us to contact you via phone.
4. Add a **note** about your experience, including any positive or negative comments about the property.
5. Choose how you would rate your experience with the property by selecting one of the three options in the **rental-review** field:
	* Rental Review Form 1
	* Rental Review Form 2
	* Rental Review Form 3
6. Select the **date** you visited the property or when your rental period started.
7. Enter the **time** you visited the property or when you left the property.
8. Finally, select the specific option that best matches your experience from the **rental-review** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name**: A text field where you can enter your name.
* **email**: An email field to enter your contact email address.
* **phone**: A text field for your phone number.
* **note**: A text field to add notes about your experience.
* **rental-review**: A rating field to select your experience level with the property (Rental Review Form 1, Rental Review Form 2, or Rental Review Form 3).
* **date**: A date field for selecting the date you visited the property or when your rental period started.
* **time**: A time field for entering the time you visited the property or when you left the property.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will help property managers and owners improve their services.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please include them in the **note** field.
* Make sure to select the correct **rental-review** option that best reflects your experience.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
