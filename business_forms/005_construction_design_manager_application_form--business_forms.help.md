# Construction Design Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Design Manager Application Form is designed to gather necessary information from applicants for the Construction Design Manager position.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately, making sure to provide all required information.
- Double-check your email address and phone number for correctness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): This is the applicant's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Email Address (`applicant_email`, email, required): The email address of the applicant for future communication.
* **Phone Number (`applicant_phone`, text, required): The phone number of the applicant for communication and interview scheduling.
* **Home Address (`applicant_address`, text, required): The applicant's home address with city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Current or Most Recent Job Title (`current_job_title`, text, required): The title of the applicant's current or most recent job.
* **Total Years of Experience in Construction/Design (`years_experience_total`, number, required): The total number of years the applicant has experience in construction and design.
* **Total Years in a Managerial Role (`years_experience_managerial`, number, required): The total number of years the applicant has experience in a managerial role.
* **Primary Areas of Expertise (`expertise_areas`, select_multiple, required): The applicant's primary areas of expertise in construction and design (Select all that apply).
* **Software Proficiency (`software_proficiency`, select_multiple, required): The software the applicant is proficient in (Check all that apply).
* **Highest Level of Education Attained (`highest_degree`, select_one, required): The highest level of education attained by the applicant (Select one option).
* **Major or Field of Study (`major_field_of_study`, text, required): The major or field of study of the applicant's highest degree.
* **Professional Certifications (`professional_certifications`, text, optional): Any professional certifications the applicant may hold (e.g., PMP, AIA, NCARB, LEED AP).
* **Notice Period at Current Employer (`notice_period`, select_one, required): The number of weeks the applicant can provide notice if hired.
* **Desired Annual Salary (`salary_expectation`, number, required): The applicant's desired annual salary.
* **Willingness to Travel to Site Locations (`willingness_to_travel`, select_one, required): The percentage of time the applicant is willing to travel to site locations.
* **Short Summary of Why You're a Great Fit (`cover_letter_summary`, text, required): A short summary of why the applicant is a great fit for the Construction Design Manager position (Max 500 characters).
