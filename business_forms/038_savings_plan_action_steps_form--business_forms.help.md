# Savings Plan Action Steps Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Savings Plan Action Steps Form is designed to help users create a comprehensive plan to achieve their savings goals. This form guides users through a series of questions and fields to gather essential information about their savings objectives, including their target amount, savings progress, and payment schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **1. What are your savings goals?**: Answer this field with a description of your savings goals. This is a required field and should be filled in accurately.
2.  **2. What actions will you take to achieve your savings goals?**: Select all relevant actions you will take to reach your goal.
3.  **3. When do you plan to start making progress on your savings goals?**: Enter the start date for making progress on your savings goals.
4.  **4. How much will you save towards your goals?**: Enter the amount you plan to save towards your goals.
5.  **5. How often do you plan to save?**: Select the frequency of your savings, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
6.  **6. What payment method will you use to make payments towards your savings goals?**: Select the method you will use for savings payments, such as Debit Card, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer.
7.  **7. How much will you pay each time?**: Enter the amount you plan to pay each time towards your savings goals.
8.  **8. What is your savings progress so far?**: Select your current savings progress, such as On track, Behind schedule, or Not started.
9.  **9. What is your target amount to save?**: Enter the amount you aim to save.
10.  **10. How much time will you have to reach your savings goal?**: Enter the time it will take you to achieve your savings goal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. What are your savings goals?** (`savings_goal`, note, required): This field is designed to capture the user's short description of their savings goals. It should be a brief and accurate description of what the user aims to save for.
* **2. What actions will you take to achieve your savings goals?** (`action_item`, select_multiple, required): This field allows users to select multiple actions they will take to achieve their savings goals. Select all relevant actions to provide a comprehensive plan.
* **3. When do you plan to start making progress on your savings goals?** (`start_date`, date, required): This field is for entering the start date of making progress on your savings goals. Enter the date you plan to begin.
* **4. How much will you save towards your goals?** (`savings_amount`, number, required): This field is for entering the amount you plan to save towards your goals. Ensure the amount is accurate and realistic.
* **5. How often do you plan to save?** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, required): This field is for selecting the frequency of savings, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly. Choose the frequency that best suits your plan.
* **6. What payment method will you use to make payments towards your savings goals?** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): This field is for selecting the payment method you will use, such as Debit Card, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer. Choose the method that is most convenient for you.
* **7. How much will you pay each time?** (`payment_amount`, number, required): This field is for entering the amount you plan to pay each time towards your savings goals. Ensure the amount is accurate and realistic.
* **8. What is your savings progress so far?** (`progress`, select_one, required): This field is for selecting your current savings progress, such as On track, Behind schedule, or Not started. Choose the progress that best describes your situation.
* **9. What is your target amount to save?** (`target_amount`, number, required): This field is for entering the amount you aim to save. Enter a realistic and achievable target amount.
* **10. How much time will you have to reach your savings goal?** (`time_to_goal`, time, required): This field is for entering the time it will take you to achieve your savings goal. Enter a realistic and achievable time frame.

## Tips
* Review and update your progress regularly to ensure you are on track with your savings goals.
* Be realistic and accurate when filling in the form to create a reliable savings plan.
* Ensure you have a clear understanding of your savings goals and the steps to achieve them.
* If you have any issues or need assistance with your savings plan, please contact your financial advisor or relevant support team.
