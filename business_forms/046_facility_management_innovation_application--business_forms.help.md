# Facility Management Innovation Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Management Innovation Application form is designed to collect improvement ideas from staff, vendors, and innovators. 
It is meant to gather information on ideas, their status, priority, and other relevant details. 
The form is an open platform for submitting and tracking innovation ideas for the facility management team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you are submitting as a Staff, Vendor, or Innovator. 
2. Provide a clear description of your improvement idea. 
3. Choose the type of idea (Operational, Product, or Process). 
4. Indicate the current status of your idea (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed). 
5. Select the priority level of your idea (High, Medium, or Low). 
6. Enter the target start and end dates for your idea (if applicable). 
7. Enter your email address, name, title, department, role, company, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Involvement** (`staff_involvement`, number, optional): This field represents the number of staff involved in the idea. Please provide the number of staff members who will be impacted by the idea.
* **Vendor Partners** (`vendor_partners`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether your idea involves vendor partners.
* **Proposal Submitter** (`proposal_submitter`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are submitting as a Staff, Vendor, or Innovator.
* **Improvement Idea** (`improvement_idea`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your improvement idea.
* **Idea Type** (`idea_type`, select_one, optional): Indicate the type of idea (Operational, Product, or Process).
* **Description** (`idea_description`, text, optional): Please give a clear description of your idea.
* **Current Status** (`idea_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate the current status of your idea (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Select the priority level of your idea (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Target Start Date** (`target_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the target start date for your idea (if applicable).
* **Target End Date** (`target_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the target end date for your idea (if applicable).
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Title** (`submitter_title`, text, optional): Enter your title.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Submitter Role** (`submitter_role`, text, optional): Enter your role.
* **Submitter Company** (`submitter_company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

Note: All fields are optional unless specified as required.
