# Supplier Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Supplier Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from suppliers to register them in our system. This form helps us verify and validate the identity and details of suppliers, ensuring accurate and up-to-date information for our business operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your company name and contact number in the respective fields.
2.  Provide a valid email address to enable us to communicate with you.
3.  Enter your website (if applicable).
4.  Fill in your address, city, state, and country details.
5.  Enter your zip code to help us locate you on our system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, Required): Enter your company's official name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, Optional): Enter your business contact number or the number of the person responsible for communication with our team.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter your company's official email address where we can send notifications, quotes, and updates.
* **Website** (`website`, text, Optional): If your company has a website, enter the URL for our reference.
* **Address** (`address`, text, Required): Enter your company's physical address to ensure our delivery and shipping accuracy.
* **City** (`city`, text, Required): Enter the city where your company is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, Required): Enter the state or province where your company is located.
* **Country** (`country`, text, Required): Enter the country where your company is based.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, Required): Enter your company's zip or postal code for accurate delivery and shipping.
* **Region** (`region`, text, Optional): Enter a region or area your company operates in (if applicable).
