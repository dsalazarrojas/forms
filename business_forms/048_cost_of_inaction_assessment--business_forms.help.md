# Cost Of Inaction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cost Of Inaction Assessment form is a tool to evaluate the potential cost of not taking action on a project, product, or initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, start from the first page and work your way through each section, filling in the required and optional fields as needed.
- Please provide accurate and relevant information for each field, and use the available options to guide your responses.
- When finished, review your answers to ensure they are comprehensive and clear.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro** (1, text, optional): A brief introduction to the project, product, or initiative being assessed. This can include a summary of the project's purpose, scope, and current status.
* **Project Info** (2, text, optional): Additional project information, such as project name, code, or ID. This can help identify the project for reference purposes.
* **Project Status** (3, select_one, optional): The current status of the project, product, or initiative. Select from the available options: Active, Inactive.
* **Project Description** (4, text, optional): A more detailed description of the project, product, or initiative. This can include its goals, objectives, and key features.
* **Project Owner** (5, text, optional): The individual or team responsible for the project, product, or initiative. This can be a name, title, or team name.
* **Delay Period** (6, date, optional): The estimated time period for delay or inaction. This can be a specific date or a duration (e.g., weeks, months, years).
* **Delay Cost** (7, number, optional): The estimated cost associated with the delay or inaction. This can be a monetary value or a percentage value.
* **Risk Level** (8, select_multiple, optional): The potential risks associated with the delay or inaction. Select from the available options: High, Low.
* **Mitigation Strategy** (9, text, optional): A strategy or plan to mitigate the potential risks and impacts. This can include contingency plans, alternative solutions, or risk management techniques.
* **Impact** (10, text, optional): A description of the potential impact of the delay or inaction. This can include effects on project scope, timeline, budget, or other relevant aspects.
* **Conclusion** (11, text, optional): A summary of the assessment, including the key findings and recommendations.
* **Recommendation** (12, text, optional): A final recommendation or suggestion based on the assessment. This can include actions to be taken, investments to be made, or decisions to be made.
