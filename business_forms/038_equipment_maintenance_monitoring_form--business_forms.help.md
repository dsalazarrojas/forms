# equipment_maintenance_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and report on equipment maintenance activities, including schedules, performance, downtime, status, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, ensure you have the required access and permissions to the equipment being reported on. Select the relevant options from the maintenance schedules, equipment performance, and other fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Maintenance Schedules** (`maintenance_schedules`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select the maintenance schedules for the equipment being reported on. Select all applicable schedules for the correct information.
* **Equipment Performance** (`equipment_performance`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the equipment's current performance.
* **Maintenance Downtime** (`maintenance_downtime`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total maintenance downtime for the equipment.
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of the equipment (Active or Inactive).
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`, `number`, required: false): Enter the frequency of maintenance performed on the equipment.
* **Maintenance Type** (`maintenance_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of maintenance performed (Yes or No).
* **Maintenance Cost** (`maintenance_cost`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total maintenance cost for the equipment.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the equipment's current condition.
* **Maintenance History** (`maintenance_history`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the last maintenance performed.
* **Next Scheduled** (`next_scheduled`, `time`, required: false): Enter the date and time of the next scheduled maintenance.
* **Maintenance Status** (`maintenance_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of the maintenance (Active or Inactive).
* **Last Maintenance** (`last_maintenance`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the last maintenance performed.
* **Last Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency_last`, `number`, required: false): Enter the frequency of the last maintenance performed.
* **Last Maintenance Type** (`maintenance_type_last`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of last maintenance performed (Yes or No).
* **Last Maintenance Cost** (`maintenance_cost_last`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total cost of the last maintenance performed.
* **Last Schedules** (`maintenance_schedules_last`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the last maintenance schedules performed.
* **Last Downtime** (`maintenance_downtime_last`, `number`, required: false): Enter the last maintenance downtime.
* **Last Scheduled** (`next_scheduled_last`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time of the last scheduled maintenance.
* **Last Status** (`maintenance_status_last`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the last maintenance (Active or Inactive).
* **Last History** (`maintenance_history_last`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the last maintenance history.
* **Equipment Condition Last** (`equipment_condition_last`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the last equipment condition.
* **Last Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency_last_2`, `number`, required: false): Enter the last maintenance frequency.
* **Last Status Last** (`last_status_last`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the last status.
* **Maintenance Frequency Last 3** (`maintenance_frequency_last_3`, `number`, required: false): Enter the last maintenance frequency.

Note: The fields marked as required: false are optional and can be filled out if applicable. Ensure you only select the relevant options and enter accurate information to ensure the form is filled out correctly.
