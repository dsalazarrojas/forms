# Vendor Payment Cycle Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Payment Cycle Survey is designed to help vendors provide their payment information and preferences to the company. This includes details about their payment method, payment frequency, and other relevant contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the vendor information on the first page, including the vendor name, address, city, state, and ZIP.
2. Select the payment method you would like to use (Check, Direct Deposit, Electronic Funds Transfer, or None of the above).
3. Choose the payment frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, etc.).
4. Enter the date and time you would like to submit the survey.
5. Optionally, enter any additional comments or notes about the payment cycle.
6. Fill in the email address and phone number of your contact person.
7. Enter any further comments or notes on the second page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (Vendor Name, text, required): Enter your company or business name.
* **Address** (Address, text, required): Enter your business address.
* **City** (City, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (State, text, required): Enter your state.
* **ZIP** (ZIP, text, required): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method.
	+ Check
	+ Direct Deposit
	+ Electronic Funds Transfer
	+ None of the above
* **Payment Frequency** (Payment Frequency, time, required): Enter the frequency of payments.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the payment cycle.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, required): Enter the name of your contact person.
* **Submission Date** (Submission Date, date, required): Enter the date you would like to submit the survey.
* **Submission Time** (Submission Time, time, required): Enter the time you would like to submit the survey.
* **Surveyed By** (Surveyed By, text, required): Enter the person who surveyed your vendor.
* **Surveyed Date** (Surveyed Date, date, required): Enter the date the survey was surveyed.
* **Surveyed Time** (Surveyed Time, time, required): Enter the time the survey was surveyed.
* **Comments 2** (Comments 2, text, optional): Enter any further comments or notes.
* **Comments 3** (Comments 3, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Vendor Information 2** (Vendor Information 2, text, optional): Enter any additional vendor information.
* **Vendor Information 3** (Vendor Information 3, text, optional): Enter any further vendor information.

Note: The fields marked as required are mandatory and must be completed before submitting the form. The fields marked as optional can be left blank, but it's recommended to fill them in for a more comprehensive submission.
