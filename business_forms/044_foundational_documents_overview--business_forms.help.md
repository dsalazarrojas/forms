# Foundational Documents Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the foundational documents to provide a structured overview of their status, review dates, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organization's information in the "Organization" field.
2. Choose the current status of the document from the "Document Status" field.
3. Enter the review date of the document in the "Review Date" field.
4. If the document has a copy, enter the copy date in the "Copy Date" field.
5. If the document has multiple copies, enter the number of copies in the "Copies" field.
6. Enter the last review date of the document in the "Last Review Date" field.
7. Add any relevant notes about the document in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter the email address of the person responsible for the document in the "Email" field.
9. Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the document in the "Phone" field.
10. Select the person(s) assigned to work on the document from the "Assigned User" field.
11. If this is a revised version of the document, enter the version number in the "Document Version" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Document Status** (`document_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the current status of the document (Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the review date of the document.
* **Copy Date** (`copy_date`, `date`, required/optional): If the document has a copy, enter the copy date.
* **Copies** (`copies`, `number`, required/optional): If the document has multiple copies, enter the number of copies.
* **Last Review Date** (`last_review_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the last review date of the document.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any relevant notes about the document.
* **Email** (`email_address`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the document.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the document.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the person(s) assigned to work on the document.
* **Document Version** (`document_version`, `text`, required/optional): If this is a revised version of the document, enter the version number.
