# phone_exchange_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect phone exchange information. It may be used for business or professional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information:

1. Fill in your first name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Provide your last name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone Number".
4. Enter the exchange code for your phone number in the field labeled "Exchange Code".
5. If your password is required for phone exchange, enter it in the field labeled "Password". This field is optional.
6. Confirm your password in the field labeled "Confirm Password" for security verification.
7. If you have an email address associated with your phone, enter it in the field labeled "Email".
8. Provide a hint for the email address in the field labeled "Email Hint". This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number, including any necessary country codes.
* **Exchange Code** (`exchange_code`, text, required): Enter the exchange code for your phone number.
* **Password** (`password`, text, optional): If required, enter your password for phone exchange.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, text, optional): Re-enter your password for verification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if associated with your phone number.
* **Email Hint** (`email_hint`, text, optional): Provide a hint or a description for your email address.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in all required fields before submission.
- If you need to submit multiple phone exchange records, you may need to fill this form out for each individual record.
- If you have any doubts or issues with this form, please contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
