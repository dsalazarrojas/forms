# Graphic Design Services Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from potential clients who are interested in seeking graphic design services. It helps us understand their project requirements, budget, and contact information to provide them with the most suitable design solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any relevant details about yourself as the contact person.
2. Provide a brief description of your project, including any specific requirements and objectives.
3. Outline the timeline for the project, including any important deadlines.
4. Indicate your budget for the project, including any currency details.
5. Specify the services you require, such as graphic design, visual identity, branding, or print design.
6. Upload any relevant files, documents, or images related to the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Project Details**: Describe your project, including any specific requirements and objectives.
* **Timeline Details**: Outline the timeline for the project, including any important deadlines.
* **Budget Details**: Enter your budget for the project.
* **Budget Currency**: Select the currency in which your budget is in. Options include USD, EUR, GBP, and CAD.
* **Services Required**: Choose the services you require from our graphic design services, such as graphic design, visual identity, branding, or print design.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and any other relevant details about the contact person.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Message**: Leave any additional message or comments about your project.
* **File**: Select the type of file you will be uploading, such as a file, image, or document.
* **File Upload**: Upload the file you selected.
* **Budget Estimated Cost**: Enter your estimated budget for the project.
