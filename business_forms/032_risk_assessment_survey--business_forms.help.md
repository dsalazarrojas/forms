# Risk Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate potential risks within a workplace or organization. It collects data on workplace hazards, risk factors, and the likelihood and impact of accidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the following sections: 
   - Workplace Hazards: Provide a brief description of the hazards you've identified in the workplace. 
   - Risk Factors: Select all the risk factors that apply. 
   - Accident Evaluation: Enter the date of the last accident related to the hazard (if applicable). 
   - Control Measures: Describe the steps taken to mitigate or control the hazard. 
2.  Evaluate the risk level: Select the level of risk from low, medium, high. 
3.  Fill in the following fields:
   - Risk Level: Enter a number from 0 to 10 to represent the risk level. 
   - Control Level: Enter a number from 0 to 10 to represent the control level. 
   - Frequency: Enter the frequency of the hazard occurrence. 
4.  Evaluate the impact and likelihood of the risk:
   - Impact: Select the potential impact of the hazard. 
   - Likelihood: Select the likelihood of the hazard. 
   - Probability: Enter a number from 0 to 10 to represent the likelihood. 
5.  Describe the consequence: Enter a short description of the consequence of the hazard. 
   - Consequence: Enter a short description of the consequence of the hazard. 
6.  Add a risk summary: Enter a brief summary of the risk assessment. 
   - Risk Summary: Enter a brief summary of the risk assessment. 
7.  Add risk assessment comment: Enter any comments or observations regarding the risk assessment. 
   - Risk Assessment Comment: Enter any comments or observations. 
8.  Review and update the form: Review and update the form as necessary after reviewing the risk assessment. 
   - Reviewer Name: Enter the name of the reviewer. 
   - Date Reviewed: Enter the date of the review. 
   - Reviewer Comment: Enter comments or observations regarding the review. 
   - Risk Assessment Status: Select the status of the risk assessment. 
   - Risk Rationale: Enter a brief description of the risk rationale. 
   - Risk Level Status: Select the level of the risk. 
   - Control Measures Status: Select the status of the control measures. 
   - Follow-up Status: Select the status of the follow-up. 
   - Reviewer Follow-up Reported: Select whether the reviewer has reported any follow-up. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Workplace Hazards** (`workplace_hazards`, `text`, required/optional): A brief description of the hazards in the workplace.
*   **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the risk factors that apply to the hazard.
*   **Accident Evaluation** (`accident_evaluation`, `date`, required/optional): The date of the last accident related to the hazard.
*   **Control Measures** (`control_measures`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the steps taken to mitigate or control the hazard.
*   **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, `number`, required/optional): A number from 0 to 10 to represent the risk level.
*   **Control Level** (`control_level`, `number`, required/optional): A number from 0 to 10 to represent the control level.
*   **Frequency** (`frequency`, `number`, required/optional): The frequency of the hazard occurrence.
*   **Impact** (`impact`, `select_one`, required/optional): The potential impact of the hazard.
*   **Probability** (`probability`, `number`, required/optional): A number from 0 to 10 to represent the likelihood.
*   **Likelihood** (`likelihood`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): The likelihood of the hazard.
*   **Consequence** (`consequence`, `text`, required/optional): A short description of the consequence of the hazard.
*   **Risk Summary** (`risk_summary`, `note`, required/optional): A brief summary of the risk assessment.
*   **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, `text`, required/optional): Any comments or observations regarding the risk assessment.
*   **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, required/optional): Any recommendations or suggestions for mitigating the hazard.
*   **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `note`, required/optional): Any follow-up actions or observations.
*   **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, `text`, required/optional): The name of the reviewer.
*   **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, `date`, required/optional): The date of the review.
*   **Reviewer Comment** (`reviewer_comment`, `text`, required/optional): Comments or observations from the reviewer.
*   **Risk Assessment Status** (`risk_assessment_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): The status of the risk assessment.
*   **Risk Rationale** (`risk_rationale`, `text`, required/optional): A brief description of the risk rationale.
*   **Risk Level Status** (`risk_level_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): The level of the risk.
*   **Control Measures Status** (`control_measures_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): The status of the control measures.
*   **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): The status of the follow-up.
*   **Reviewer Follow-up Reported** (`reviewer_follow_reported`, `select_one`, required/optional): Whether the reviewer has reported any follow-up.
