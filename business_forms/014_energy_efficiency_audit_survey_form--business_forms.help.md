# energy_efficiency_audit_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The energy efficiency audit survey form is a tool used to assess the current state of energy efficiency practices in buildings. It helps identify areas for improvement and aids in developing a roadmap for future implementation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form correctly, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have the necessary information and access to relevant data before proceeding.
2. Review each field carefully and select the most accurate answer for each question.
3. Fill in all required fields to ensure your responses are complete and thorough.
4. Use the provided explanations for each field to clarify any doubts or uncertainties.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **energy_efficiency_audit_survey_form_first_questions**: This page contains multiple-choice questions that will help you gauge the current energy efficiency practices in place. Select all that apply.
	+ **Yes**: Indicate that the practice is currently being used or applied.
	+ **No**: Indicate that the practice is not being used or applied.
* **building_characteristics**: Select the type of building you are assessing.
	+ **Yes**: Commercial/Industrial
	+ **No**: Residential
* **energy_consumption**: Select all methods of energy consumption you currently use.
	+ **Yes**: Electricity
	+ **No**: Gas
* **energy_efficiency**: Select all methods of energy efficiency practices you currently use.
	+ **Yes**: Lighting
	+ **No**: HVAC
* **current_energy_efficiency_practices**: Select all energy efficiency practices currently being used.
	+ **Yes**: Energy-efficient lighting
	+ **No**: Energy-efficient appliances
* **future_energy_efficiency_practices**: Select all energy efficiency practices you plan to implement in the future.
	+ **Yes**: Energy-efficient building envelope
	+ **No**: Energy-efficient windows
* **energy_efficiency_objectives**: Select all energy efficiency objectives you have.
	+ **Yes**: Reduce energy consumption
	+ **No**: Reduce water consumption
* **energy_efficiency_benefits**: Select all benefits of energy efficiency practices you've experienced.
	+ **Yes**: Reduced energy bills
	+ **No**: Improved building comfort
* **roadmap**: Describe your current energy efficiency roadmap or strategy.
* **implementation**: Describe the implementation plan for your energy efficiency practices.
* **evaluation**: Describe how you evaluate the effectiveness of your energy efficiency practices.
* **monitoring**: Describe how you monitor energy consumption and efficiency.
* **revision_history**: Describe any revisions made to your energy efficiency plan.
* **surveyors**: Select all individuals involved in the survey.
	+ **Yes**: Energy auditor
	+ **No**: Architect
* **survey_dates**: Select all dates relevant to your energy efficiency survey.
	+ **Yes**: Completed in 2022
	+ **No**: Scheduled for 2023
* **survey_status**: Select the current status of your energy efficiency survey.
	+ **Active**: Currently active
	+ **Inactive**: Currently inactive
* **survey_frequency**: Enter the frequency of your energy efficiency surveys.
* **survey_comments**: Provide any additional comments or observations about your energy efficiency survey.
* **survey_attachments**: Upload any relevant attachments related to your energy efficiency survey.
* **survey_attachments_type**: Select the type of attachments.
	+ **Yes**: Energy audits
	+ **No**: Energy-efficient designs
* **survey_attachments_description**: Describe the attachments.
* **survey_attachments_comment**: Provide any comments about the attachments.
* **survey_attachments_comment_description**: Describe the attachments' comments.
* **survey_attachments_comment_comment**: Provide any additional comments about the attachments.
