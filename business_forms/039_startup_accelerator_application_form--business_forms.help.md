# Startup Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Accelerator Application Form is a tool to help you apply for the startup accelerator program. The form gathers essential information about your startup, team, and contact details to assess your eligibility for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field's description for guidance on what to enter.
3. Ensure you have all the necessary attachments and supporting documents before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <u>Startup Name</u> (1. **Startup Name**, text, required: false)
• Enter your startup's name as it is commonly known.

### <u>Business Name</u> (2. **Business Name**, text, required: false)
• Enter your business's name as it is registered.

### <u>Business Description</u> (3. **Business Description**, text, required: false)
• Provide a brief description of your business, including its products/services, target market, and competitive advantage.

### <u>Team Name</u> (4. **Team Name**, text, required: false)
• Enter the names of the key members of your team, including founders, directors, or other essential personnel.

### <u>Team Description</u> (5. **Team Description**, text, required: false)
• Provide a brief description of your team, including their roles, experiences, and skills.

### <u>Contact Email</u> (6. **Contact Email**, email, required: false)
• Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you regarding your application.

### <u>Contact Phone</u> (7. **Contact Phone**, text, required: false)
• Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you regarding your application.

Note: If you're unsure about any field, contact us at [contact information]. We'll be happy to help.
