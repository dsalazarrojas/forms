# Contractor Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Contractor Application Form is used by contractors to apply for work with our company. It gathers information about the contractor's experience, insurance status, and availability for the project. This form helps us to assess your qualifications and determine if you are a good fit for our business needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out all required fields**: Each field marked as "required" must be filled out in order to submit the form.
2. **Use correct formatting**: When entering phone numbers, format them as usual (e.g., 123-456-7890).
3. **Use official names and addresses**: Use your legal name as it appears on official documents, and enter your business address if applicable.
4. **Choose correct options**: Select the correct option for each field, especially for fields with multiple options.
5. **Upload supporting documents**: If required by our company, you may need to upload supporting documents, such as your license number and insurance information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your contracting business if applicable.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): Enter your full business address if applicable.
* **Project Details** (`section_project_details`, note, optional): This is a section for you to describe your project details, but it is not a required field.
* **Type of Project** (`project_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of project you need help with (e.g., Home Renovation, New Construction, etc.).
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, required): Enter the address where the project will be performed.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the work needed.
* **Desired Timeline** (`project_timeline`, select_one, required): Choose the desired start time for the project (e.g., As soon as possible, Within 2 weeks, etc.).
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Choose the estimated budget for this project (e.g., Under $5,000, $5,000 - $10,000, etc.).
* **Contractor Information** (`section_contractor_info`, note, optional): This is a section for you to describe your contractor information, but it is not a required field.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter how many years you have been in business.
* **Contractor License Number** (`license_number`, text, required): Enter your professional license number.
* **License State** (`license_state`, text, required): Enter the state where your license is issued.
* **Insurance Status** (`insurance_status`, select_one, required): Choose your insurance status (e.g., Yes - Fully Insured, Yes - Partially Insured, etc.).
* **Insurance Coverage Amount** (`insurance_amount`, text, optional): Enter your total coverage amount if applicable.
* **Bonding Status** (`bonding_status`, select_one, required): Choose if you are bonded or not.
* **Reference 1 Name** (`reference_1_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your first reference.
* **Reference 1 Phone** (`reference_1_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact number for your first reference.
* **Reference 2 Name** (`reference_2_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your second reference.
* **Reference 2 Phone** (`reference_2_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact number for your second reference.
* **Have a Portfolio** (`portfolio_available`, select_one, optional): Choose if you have a portfolio of past work or not.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to share.
