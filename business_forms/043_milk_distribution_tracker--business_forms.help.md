# Milk Distribution Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Milk Distribution Tracker" form is designed to track the distribution of milk products from a central hub or source to various locations, such as farms, NGOs, or other organizations. This tool helps in maintaining records of milk deliveries, including the amount, temperature, price, and location details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the milk delivery, such as the quantity delivered, temperature, and price per liter.
2. Select the type of milk product being distributed (Milk, Cheese, or Butter).
3. Provide details about where the milk is to be delivered to (e.g., a specific farm or NGO).
4. Enter the date the milk was delivered and received if it's different.
5. If applicable, choose which team or organization is responsible for delivering the milk (Logistics Team, Dairy Farm, or NGO).
6. Add any additional delivery details that are relevant.
7. Enter the price per liter of milk delivered.
8. Confirm that the form information is accurate before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Milk Distribution Form (milk_distribution_form)**: This is the first field to complete. It's where you'll enter the details about the milk delivery, including the type of milk product (Milk, Cheese, or Butter).
- **Milk Delivered**: Enter the amount of milk delivered in liters.
- **Milk Date Delivered**: Enter the date the milk was delivered.
- **Milk Date Received**: If the milk was received at a different date from delivery, enter that date here.
- **Location of Distribution (milk_location)**: Select from Milk, Cheese, or Butter to indicate the type of milk product being distributed.
- **Milk Location Details**: Enter any additional details about where the milk is being delivered to.
- **Temperature (milk_temperature)**: Enter the temperature in degrees Celsius of the milk.
- **Quantity (milk_quantity)**: Enter the total amount of milk delivered in liters.
- **Delivery Details (milk_delivery_details)**: Add any extra notes or comments about the delivery.
- **Assigned to**: Select from Logistics Team, Dairy Farm, or NGO to indicate who is responsible for the delivery.
- **Assigned at**: If you need to track when the delivery was assigned to a team or organization, enter that date here.
- **Milk Price per Liter (milk_price)**: Enter the price per liter of the milk delivered.

## Tips
- Always ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
- If a field is not applicable or not relevant, leave it blank or select "Not Applicable" as a placeholder.
- Use this form regularly to maintain accurate and consistent records of milk distribution.
- If you're unsure about any field or have questions, consult the relevant teams or personnel responsible for the distribution process.
