# Office And Gym Drop Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents or issues within the office and gym areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the incident date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields, respectively.
2. Choose whether the incident occurred in the office or gym area in the "Incident Location" field.
3. Provide a brief description of what happened in the "Incident Description" field.
4. Add any additional details about the incident in the "Details" field.
5. If there is any evidence related to the incident, you can upload it in the "Evidence" field.
6. Indicate the cleanliness condition of the office or gym area after the incident in the "Cleanliness Condition" field.
7. Indicate the safety condition of the office or gym area after the incident in the "Safety Condition" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Incident Date
* **Incident Date** (date): The date when the incident occurred.
### Incident Time
* **Incident Time** (time): The time when the incident occurred.
### Incident Location
* **Incident Location** (select_one): Choose 'Office' or 'Gym' to indicate where the incident happened.
### Incident Description
* **Incident Description** (text): A brief description of what happened.
### Details
* **Details** (text): Additional information about the incident.
### Evidence
* **Evidence** (text): Any supporting evidence related to the incident.
### Cleanliness Condition
* **Cleanliness Condition** (select_multiple): Choose all that apply, but at least one, of the cleanliness conditions: Yes or No.
### Safety Condition
* **Safety Condition** (select_multiple): Choose all that apply, but at least one, of the safety conditions: Yes or No.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing what happened and what you observed.
* Attach any supporting evidence if possible.
* Ensure you have permission to report the incident before submitting this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the relevant authorities or the help desk for guidance.
