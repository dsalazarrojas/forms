# Business Partner Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Partner Intake Form is designed to help us better understand your company and its needs, so we can assess compatibility and provide relevant support.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- Use the dropdown menus to select from the provided options if applicable.
- If you have any additional information to share, please use the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Prospect Name** (`prospect_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the primary point of contact.
- **Email Address** (`prospect_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`prospect_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's name.
- **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, optional): If your company has a website, enter the URL (optional).
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position.
- **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): Select your primary industry from the list:
	+ Technology
	+ Healthcare
	+ Finance
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Retail
	+ Professional services
	+ Education
	+ Other
- **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required): Select your company's size from the list:
	+ 1 to 10
	+ 11 to 50
	+ 51 to 200
	+ 201 to 500
	+ 500 plus
- **Current Business Challenges** (`current_challenges`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the challenges your business faces from the list:
	+ Revenue growth
	+ Customer acquisition
	+ Market expansion
	+ Technology adoption
	+ Operational efficiency
	+ Talent acquisition
	+ Competition
	+ Cash flow
- **Desired Outcomes** (`desired_outcomes`, `text`, required): Describe what you want to achieve through partnership.
- **Willingness to Invest** (`willingness_to_invest`, `select_one`, required): Select how much you are willing to invest from the list:
	+ Under $10K
	+ $10K to $25K
	+ $25K to $50K
	+ $50K to $100K
	+ Over $100K
- **Time Commitment Available** (`time_commitment`, `select_one`, required): Select how much time you can dedicate from the list:
	+ Full-time dedication
	+ Part-time - 20 plus hours weekly
	+ Part-time - 10 to 20 hours weekly
	+ Limited - under 10 hours weekly
- **Decision Timeline** (`decision_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Select when you need to make a decision from the list:
	+ Immediate
	+ Within 1 week
	+ Within 2 weeks
	+ Within 1 month
	+ Still exploring
- **Previous Partnership Experience** (`previous_partnership_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select your previous partnership experience from the list:
	+ Yes - very successful
	+ Yes - mixed results
	+ Yes - unsuccessful
	+ No - first time
- **Lessons Learned** (`lessons_learned`, `text`, optional): Share any lessons you learned from previous experiences.
- **How Do You Define Success** (`success_definition`, `text`, required): Describe what success looks like for you.
- **Key Priorities** (`key_priorities`, `text`, required): List your top 3 priorities.
- **Potential Obstacles** (`potential_obstacles`, `text`, optional): Share any potential obstacles that might prevent your success.
- **Support Needed** (`support_needed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the support you need from the list:
	+ Strategy development
	+ Marketing support
	+ Sales training
	+ Operational guidance
	+ Financial planning
	+ Technology implementation
	+ Team building
	+ Accountability
- **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you heard about us from the list:
	+ Referral
	+ Website
	+ Social media
	+ Event
	+ Advertisement
	+ Other
- **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Share any additional information about your business.

## Tips
- Be accurate and detailed in your responses to ensure a smooth assessment process.
- If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask or provide further details.
- We will review your submission carefully and respond accordingly.
