# Covid 19 Protocol Sign In For New Zealand Construction Sites - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from visitors to construction sites in New Zealand, ensuring their compliance with COVID-19 health and safety protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Site Name** (1): Enter the name of the site you are visiting.
- **Contact Name** (2): Enter your name as it should be contacted in case of emergencies.
- **Phone Number** (3): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
- **Email** (4): Enter a contact email address for emergencies.
- **Location** (5): Confirm the location of the site you are visiting.
- **Do you have flu symptoms today?** (6): Select 'True' if you are experiencing flu symptoms.
- **Have you traveled overseas in the past 14 days?** (7): Select 'True' if you have traveled overseas in the past 14 days.
- **Have you had contact with someone with a confirmed Covid-19 case?** (8): Select 'True' if you have had contact with someone with a confirmed Covid-19 case.
- **Are you currently experiencing any of the following symptoms?** (9): Select all symptoms you are currently experiencing.
  *  **Fever** 
  *  **Cough**
  *  **Fatigue**
  *  **Headache**
  *  **Sore Throat**
  *  **Runny Nose**
  *  **Shortness of breath**
- **Is the site manager aware of this?** (10): Select 'True' if the site manager is aware of your health status.
- **Is the site supervisor aware of this?** (11): Select 'True' if the site supervisor is aware of your health status.
- **Date signed in** (12): Enter the date you signed in to the site.
- **Time signed in** (13): Enter the time you signed in to the site.
- **Signature** (14): Sign the form to confirm your information.

## Tips
* Always answer the health declaration questions honestly.
* Ensure the site manager and supervisor are aware of your health status.
* Sign and date the form when you sign in to the site.
