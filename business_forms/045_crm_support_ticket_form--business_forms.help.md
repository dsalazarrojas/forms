# CRM Support Ticket Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The CRM Support Ticket Form is designed to help users report and track issues or requests related to our CRM system. Please use this form to report any problems you're experiencing with our system, and we'll do our best to assist you promptly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your contact information:
	* **Customer Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your account.
	* **Customer Email Address**: Enter your valid email address.
	* **Customer Phone Number**: Optional, but helps us to contact you if needed.
2. Select the correct **Issue Category**: Choose the type of problem you're experiencing (e.g. Technical issue, Account problem, etc.).
3. Select your **Priority Level**: Indicate how urgent your issue is (e.g. Low, Medium, High, Critical).
4. Describe your **Issue Subject or Title**: Briefly describe the problem you're experiencing.
5. Provide a detailed **Detailed Description of Issue**: Describe the steps you've taken and any relevant details to help us understand your problem.
6. If applicable, add **Steps to Reproduce Issue**: Numbered steps to help us reproduce your problem.
7. If applicable, add **Error Message or Code**: Any error message or code related to your problem.
8. Add any **Attachments** (e.g. screenshots, logs, files): This will help us to better understand your problem.
9. Indicate if you have a **Temporary Workaround Available**: If you've found a temporary solution, let us know.
10. If applicable, select your **Browser and Operating System**: This will help us to troubleshoot your issue.
11. **Attachments Available**: Indicate if you've attached any files or screenshots to help us understand your problem.
12. Select your **Desired Resolution Timeline**: When do you need this resolved by?
13. **Preferred Contact Method for Resolution**: How would you like to receive the resolution (e.g. Email, Phone, etc.)?

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ticket ID**: Auto-generated reference number.
* **Customer Name**: Your full name as it appears on your account.
* **Customer Email Address**: Your valid email address.
* **Customer Phone Number**: Optional, but helps us to contact you if needed.
* **Account or Customer Number**: If your issue is related to your account, please enter the account or customer number.
* **Date Issue Occurred**: When did the issue first occur?
* **Date Reported**: When did you submit this ticket?
* **Issue Category**: Type of problem (e.g. Technical issue, Account problem, etc.).
* **Priority Level**: How urgent is your issue (e.g. Low, Medium, High, Critical).
* **Issue Subject or Title**: Brief description of your problem.
* **Detailed Description of Issue**: Description of the steps you've taken and any relevant details.
* **Steps to Reproduce Issue**: Numbered steps to help us reproduce your problem.
* **Error Message or Code**: Any error message or code related to your problem.
* **Browser and Operating System**: What device and software are you using?
* **Temporary Workaround Available**: If you've found a temporary solution, let us know.
* **Attachments**: Screenshot, logs, or files that help us understand your problem.
* **Business Impact of Issue**: How does this affect your operations?
* **Attachments Available**: Indicate if you've attached any files or screenshots.
* **Desired Resolution Timeline**: When do you need this resolved by?
* **Preferred Contact Method for Resolution**: How would you like to receive the resolution?
