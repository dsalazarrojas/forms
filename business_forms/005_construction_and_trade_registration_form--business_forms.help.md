# Construction And Trade Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a construction or trade business, collecting essential information about the business, its credentials, and services offered. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter the Business Name**: Type in the legal name of your business.
2. **Select Business Type**: Choose your business structure from the options provided: Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Limited Liability Company (LLC), Corporation, Non-Profit, or Government Entity.
3. **Choose Trade Category**: Select your primary trade or specialty from the list.
4. **Years in Business**: Indicate how long your business has been operating.
5. **Business Address**: Enter the full street address of your business.
6. **City**: Enter the city where your business is located.
7. **State**: Enter the state or province where your business is located.
8. **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter the postal code for your business location.
9. **Primary Contact Name**: Enter the full name of your business representative.
10. **Contact Title**: Specify the job title or position of the contact person.
11. **Email Address**: Enter your business email address.
12. **Phone Number**: Enter the primary phone number for your business.
13. **Contractor License Number**: Enter your state or local contractor license number.
14. **License State**: Enter the state that issued your license.
15. **License Expiration Date**: Enter the date when your license is set to expire.
16. **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your insurance company.
17. **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter your policy or certificate number.
18. **Insurance Expiration Date**: Enter the date when your insurance is set to expire.
19. **Workers Compensation Coverage**: Select if you have workers compensation insurance coverage.
20. **Number of Employees**: Enter the total number of full-time and part-time employees.
21. **Services Offered**: Select all services your business provides from the list.
22. **Service Area**: Enter the geographic area your business serves.
23. **Certifications**: List any professional certifications or qualifications.
24. **References Available**: Select if you can provide client references.
25. **Additional Information**: Enter any other relevant details about your business.
