# Tax Filing Service Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge the client's understanding and consent of the tax filing services provided by a tax professional.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): This field is for the client's name. Enter their full name as it appears on their identification documents.
- **Service Year** (`service_year`, number, optional): This field is for the year for which the tax filing service is being provided.
- **Tax Professional Name** (`tax_professional_name`, text, optional): This field is for the tax professional's name.
- **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of tax service being provided (Individual Income Tax, Business Income Tax, or Other).
- **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): This field is for the date the client gave their consent for the service to proceed.
- **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, optional): This field is for the time the client gave their consent for the service to proceed.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This is an open field for any additional comments or notes about the client's service.
- **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): This field is for the client's email address.
- **Tax Professional Phone** (`tax_professional_phone`, text, optional): This field is for the tax professional's phone number.
- **Consent Status** (`consent_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the client's consent (Active or Inactive). 

Note: The fields marked as optional are not mandatory and can be left blank.
