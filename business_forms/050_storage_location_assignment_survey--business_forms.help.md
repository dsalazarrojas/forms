# Storage Location Assignment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assign storage areas and access levels to teams or personnel within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct storage area for each team or personnel from the available options.
2. Choose the corresponding access level (Read, Write, or Both) for each storage area.
3. Enter the name of the person assigning the storage area.
4. Enter the date and time of assignment.
