# Workplace Productivity Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from employees, managers, peers, and the employees themselves to evaluate their workplace productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with your information and feedback.
2.  Select the relevant options for each field, such as ratings and productivity levels.
3.  Make sure to include any additional comments or suggestions you may have in the "Evaluation Comments" field.
4.  Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Survey** (`employee_survey`, text, false): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your current work situation.
*   **Work Hours Worked** (`work_hours_worked`, number, false): Enter the number of hours you have worked in the current evaluation period.
*   **Work Hours Overtime** (`work_hours_overtime`, number, false): Enter the number of hours you have worked overtime in the current evaluation period.
*   **Productivity Levels** (`productivity_levels`, select_one, false): Select the level of productivity you currently exhibit:
    +   High
    +   Medium
    +   Low
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, false): This field is for any additional feedback or comments about your work situation.
*   **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, false): Share any suggestions you have for improving your productivity and work situation.
*   **Work Hours Averages** (`work_hours_averages`, time, false): Enter the average number of hours you work in a week.
*   **Work Hours Overtime Averages** (`work_hours_overtime_averages`, time, false): Enter the average number of hours you work overtime in a week.
*   **Productivity Improvement** (`productivity_improvement`, select_multiple, false): Select how you plan to improve your productivity:
    +   Increase
    +   Decrease
    +   No Change
*   **Manager Rating** (`manager_rating`, number, false): Enter the rating of your current work performance from your manager.
*   **Peer Rating** (`peer_rating`, number, false): Enter the rating of your current work performance from your peers.
*   **Self Rating** (`self_rating`, number, false): Enter the rating of your current work performance from yourself.
*   **Department Rating** (`department_rating`, number, false): Enter the rating of your current department performance.
*   **Team Rating** (`team_rating`, number, false): Enter the rating of your current team performance.
*   **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, note, false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your evaluation.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, false): Enter your work email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, false): Enter your work phone number.
*   **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, false): The date this form was submitted.
*   **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, false): The time this form was submitted.
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, false): Enter your name.
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, false): Enter your employee ID number.
*   **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, false): Enter your personal email address.
*   **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, false): Enter your personal phone number.
*   **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, false): Enter the name of your supervisor.
*   **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, false): Enter the email address of your supervisor.

Note: The fields with `required: false` are not mandatory, and you can fill them out or leave them blank if you do not have the information.
