# Invoice Reconciliation Summary Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to initiate an invoice reconciliation request. It is meant to provide information needed to verify and match transactions or accounts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the purpose of your request:
	*   "Request for Reconciliation" for an initial request
	*   "Reconciliation Review" for a review of a previous request
	*   "Reconciliation Approval" for approval of a previous request
2.  Enter the date of your request.
3.  Enter the name of the user who requested the reconciliation.
4.  Enter the invoice ID.
5.  Enter the date of the invoice.
6.  Enter the total amount of the invoice.
7.  Select the payment status of the invoice:
	*   "Paid" for invoices that have been fully paid
	*   "Partially Paid" for invoices that have partial payment
	*   "Unpaid" for invoices with no payment made
8.  Optionally, enter any additional notes about the reconciliation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **summary_request** (Select One, Optional): Select the purpose of your request for reconciliation. This will help us know how to process your request.
*   **request_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you are requesting the reconciliation.
*   **requested_by** (Text, Optional): Enter your name to associate the request with you.
*   **invoice_id** (Number, Optional): Enter the ID of the invoice.
*   **invoice_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the invoice.
*   **total_amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the total amount of the invoice.
*   **payment_status** (Select One, Required): Select the status of the payment for the invoice:
	+   "Paid" for invoices that have been fully paid
	+   "Partially Paid" for invoices that have partially paid
	+   "Unpaid" for invoices with no payment made
*   **notes** (Note, Optional): Optionally, enter any additional notes about the reconciliation. This can include context or information that may be helpful to us for processing your request.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed to the best of your ability.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to us for assistance.
*   Reconciliations are typically completed within 2-5 business days. If you need this done urgently, please contact us to make arrangements.
*   This form is meant to provide a quick and simple way to initiate a reconciliation. If you need any more detailed or complex assistance, please contact us for further guidance.
