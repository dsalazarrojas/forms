# Business Partner Contract Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Business Partner Contract Form is used to establish a partnership agreement between your company and a business partner. This form captures essential details about the partnership, including the partner manager's contact information, contract terms, and performance targets.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Partner Manager Name: Enter the full legal name of the partner manager.
	* Partner Manager Email: Enter the email address of the partner manager.
	* Partner Manager Phone: Enter the contact phone number of the partner manager.
	* Company Name: Enter the legal name of the company.
	* Company Address: Enter the full business address of the company.
	* Company Registration Number: Enter the registration or tax ID number of the company.
	* Contract Start Date: Enter the date the contract begins.
	* Contract End Date: Enter the date the contract ends.
	* Contract Type: Select the type of partnership contract (e.g., Exclusive partnership, Non-exclusive partnership, etc.).
	* Commission Rate: Enter the percentage of commission.
	* Payment Terms: Select the payment terms (e.g., Net 15 days, Net 30 days, etc.).
	* Payment Method: Select the method of payment (e.g., Bank transfer, Check, PayPal, etc.).
	* Territory Coverage: Enter the geographic area covered by the partnership.
	* Performance Targets: Describe the sales or performance targets of the partnership.
	* Contract Terms Description: Enter a description of the key terms and conditions of the contract.
	* Exclusivity Clause: Select whether this is an exclusive or non-exclusive arrangement.
	* Termination Clause: Select the termination notice period.
	* Confidentiality Agreement: Select whether you agree to confidentiality terms.
	* Non-Compete Agreement: Select whether you agree to non-compete terms.
	* Governing Law: Select the jurisdiction that governs the contract.
	* Authorized Signatory Name: Enter the name of the person signing the contract.
	* Signatory Title: Enter the title of the person signing the contract.
	* Signature Date: Enter the date of signing.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are complete and accurate.
3. Sign and date the contract.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are complete and accurate.
* Enter the correct date of signing for the authorized signatory.
* Consider having the authorized signatory sign the contract in the presence of a witness to ensure the signature is authentic.
* Store the signed contract in a secure location, such as a locked cabinet or a digital vault.
* Review the contract with the partner manager to ensure both parties are in agreement with the terms and conditions.
