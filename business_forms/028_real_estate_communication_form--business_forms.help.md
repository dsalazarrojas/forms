# Real Estate Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Communication Form is used to gather essential information from clients, including contact details, schedule a call, client feedback, and preferred contact method.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the contact details, including [Client Name, Contact Email, and Contact Phone Number](#contact_details).
2. Schedule a call with the client by selecting a date in the [Schedule A Call](#schedule_call) field.
3. Share your thoughts and feedback about our service with us in the [Client Feedback](#client_feedback) field.
4. Let us know how you prefer to be contacted by selecting one or more options in the [How do you prefer to be contacted?](#preferred_contact_method) field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Name`, `Contact Email`, `Contact Phone Number`)  
  Enter the client's contact information, including name, email, and phone number.

### Schedule A Call
* **Schedule A Call** (`Date`)  
  Select a specific date and time for the call.

### Client Feedback
* **Client Feedback** (`Client Feedback`)  
  Share any thoughts or feedback about our service.

### How do you prefer to be contacted?
* **How do you prefer to be contacted?** (`Phone, Email, Text Message`)  
  Select one or more preferred contact methods for future communication.
