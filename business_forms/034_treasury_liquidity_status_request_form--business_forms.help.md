# Treasury Liquidity Status Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by treasury managers or financial professionals to request updates on the current state of liquidity within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the "General Information" page, provide any additional context or information about your request.
2. On the "Request Details" page, describe the specific details of your request for a Treasury Liquidity update.
3. On the "Submit Request" page, select "Submit" to send your request to the relevant team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): This page allows you to provide any additional context or information about your request. You can type in any relevant details that will help the team understand your needs.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, optional): In this field, describe the specific details of your request for a Treasury Liquidity update. Be as clear and concise as possible so that the team can understand your requirements.
* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`, select_one, optional): To submit your request, select the "Submit" option. If you want to cancel your request, select "Cancel."
