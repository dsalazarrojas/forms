# Detailed Resume Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about an individual's work experiences, education, and skills. It is intended to be used by businesses and organizations to create a comprehensive resume.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by providing information in the required fields (Job Experiences, Education Level)
2. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank or fill it out if you have relevant information
3. Use the options provided in the select fields to select the most accurate answer
4. Enter dates in the date fields in the format MM/DD/YYYY

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Resume Education** (`resume_education`, `text`, required: false): 
  This field is for providing a brief description of your educational background.
- **Job Experiences** (`job_experiences`, `select_multiple`, required: true): 
  List your past work experiences, selecting all relevant job titles (Software Engineer, Data Scientist, Marketing Manager, etc.)
- **Resume Skills** (`resume_skills`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Choose one key skill that best describes your expertise (Programming, Data Analysis, Marketing, etc.)
- **Employment History** (`employment_history`, `text`, required: false): 
  This field is for providing a description of your past employment history.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter your job title for each work experience.
- **Job Employer** (`job_employer`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the name of the company or organization you worked for.
- **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, `date`, required: false): 
  Enter the start date of your job in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, `date`, required: false): 
  Enter the end date of your job in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required: false): 
  Briefly describe your job responsibilities and achievements.
- **Education Level** (`resume_education_level`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select your highest level of education (High School, Bachelor, Master, PhD).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (Job Experiences)
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
