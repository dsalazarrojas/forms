# Customer Service Skills Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Service Skills Assessment Form is used to evaluate an individual's customer service skills and experience. This form is used to determine a person's suitability for a customer service role and to gather information about their skills, experience, and certifications in customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form honestly and thoroughly, providing as much information as possible.
2.  Answer each question clearly and concisely, making sure to follow the instructions provided in each section.
3.  If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant department or team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the definition of customer service skills?** (`customer_service_skills_definition`, text, optional): Please specify the definition of customer service skills. This section is intended to help us understand your understanding of customer service skills and how you apply them in your work.
* **Which of the following is a key skill for customer service representatives?** (`key_customer_service_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: 
  • Communication skills
  • Problem-solving skills
  • Adaptability
  • Customer service skills
  • Other (please specify)
* **How would you rate your customer service skills?** (`self_assessment`, number, required): Rate your customer service skills on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Needs Improvement" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **What skills do you think are essential for a customer service representative?** (`essential_skills`, text, optional): List the essential skills for a customer service representative.
* **Can you describe your experience with problem-solving in a customer service role?** (`problem_solving_experience`, text, optional): Please describe a situation where you had to solve a difficult customer problem.
* **How would you rate your ability to adapt to changing situations?** (`adaptability_rating`, number, optional): Rate your ability to adapt on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Needs Improvement" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **What is your availability for customer service skills assessment?** (`availability`, date, required): Specify your availability for this assessment.
* **How many years have you been working in customer service?** (`years_in_customer_service`, number, required): Specify the number of years you have been working in customer service.
* **Do you have any certifications or training related to customer service?** (`customer_service_certification`, select_multiple, optional): Specify the certifications or training you have received.
* **How would you rate your communication skills?** (`communication_skills`, number, required): Rate your communication skills on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Needs Improvement" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Can you give an example of a time when you had to solve a difficult customer problem?** (`problem_solving_example`, text, optional): Describe the situation and how you handled it.
* **How would you rate your ability to work under pressure?** (`pressure_rating`, number, required): Rate your ability to work under pressure on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Needs Improvement" and 5 is "Excellent".
