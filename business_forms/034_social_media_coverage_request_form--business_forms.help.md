# Social Media Coverage Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help businesses and organizations request coverage on social media platforms for their products, services, or events. It collects information about the requestor's business and the nature of their request, allowing us to review and respond promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Business Info** section (Page 1) with your business details. This includes your business name, description, and industry.
2. In the **Request Info** section (Page 1), provide a clear and concise description of what you are requesting coverage for (e.g., a product launch, event, or service promotion).
3. In the **Contact Info** section (Page 2), enter your name and email address so we can get in touch with you to discuss your request.
4. In the **Submit Request** section (Page 2), type in a brief confirmation message to indicate that you've double-checked your submission.
5. Finally, in the **Confirm Submission** section (Page 3), type "yes" to confirm that you've carefully reviewed your submission and are ready for us to review it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Info** (`business_info`, text, optional): Enter your business name, description, and industry to help us understand your brand and what you're promoting.
* **Request Info** (`request_info`, text, optional): Describe the specific product, service, or event you are requesting coverage for. Be as detailed as possible so we can tailor our response to your needs.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your name and email address so we can get in touch with you to discuss your request.
* **Submit Request** (`submit_request`, text, optional): Type a brief "yes" or confirmation message to ensure we receive your submission.
* **Confirm Submission** (`confirm_submission`, text, optional): Type "yes" to confirm that you've carefully reviewed your submission and are ready for us to review it.
