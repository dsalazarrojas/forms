# Roof Check - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about a roof, including its characteristics and features. It will likely be used by professionals in the construction or real estate industry to assess and document roof properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of roof from the options provided (Asphalt, Clay, Cedar, or Metal).
2. Enter the height of the roof in meters or feet.
3. Enter the area of the roof in square meters or feet.
4. Choose the slope of the roof from the options provided (Flat, Gable, Hip, or Mansard).
5. Select the pitch of the roof from the options provided (Low or High).
6. If applicable, select any additional features of the roof that apply (Solar or Wind).
7. Enter any additional notes about the roof.
8. (Optional) Enter the email address associated with the roof.
9. (Optional) Enter the phone number associated with the roof.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Roof Type** (`roof_type`, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to select the type of roof (Asphalt, Clay, Cedar, or Metal).
* **Roof Height** (`roof_height`, number, required): Enter the height of the roof in meters or feet.
* **Roof Area** (`roof_area`, number, required): Enter the area of the roof in square meters or feet.
* **Roof Slope** (`roof_slope`, select_one, optional): Choose the slope of the roof from the options provided (Flat, Gable, Hip, or Mansard).
* **Roof Pitch** (`roof_pitch`, select_one, optional): Select the pitch of the roof from the options provided (Low or High).
* **Roof Features** (`roof_features`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select any additional features of the roof that apply (Solar or Wind).
* **Roof Notes** (`roof_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the roof.
* **Roof Email** (`roof_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address associated with the roof (if applicable).
* **Roof Phone** (`roof_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number associated with the roof (if applicable).
