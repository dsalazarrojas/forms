# Tax Filing Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for users who need to make a payment for tax filing services. It will help process their payment information and associated services efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select "Payment Details" page.
2. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Details" field.
3. Go to the "Client Information" page.
4. Choose either "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the client's information is accurate.
5. Proceed to the "Tax Filing Services" page.
6. Select either "Yes" or "No" to confirm the tax filing services provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter your payment information in this field. This field is used to record your payment details.
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, select_multiple, optional): Choose "Yes" if the client's information is accurate and "No" if it is not. This is to confirm the accuracy of the client's information.
* **Tax Filing Services** (`tax_filing_services`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if tax filing services were provided and "No" if not. This is to confirm if tax filing services were included in the payment.
