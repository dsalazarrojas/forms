# Car Insurance Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a car insurance quotation. Please fill out the form accurately to receive an insurance quote tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the owner's information: **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**.
2. Proceed to the **Vehicle Information** section: **Type of Vehicle**, **Vehicle Make/Model/Year**, and **Radius of Operation (Miles)**.
3. If you have a commercial vehicle or carry cargo, provide the necessary details.
4. In the **Driver Information** section, enter the number of drivers and their ages.
5. Confirm your **Desired Liability Coverage Limits** and select the desired level of coverage.
6. Verify the **Insurance Quotation Terms** by selecting the acknowledgement option.
7. Finally, sign the **Quotation Confirmation** and provide any additional comments or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (owner_full_name)**: Enter your first and last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address (owner_email_address)**: Enter the email address where you want to receive your insurance quote.
* **Phone Number (owner_phone_number)**: Enter your phone number for discussion and follow-up purposes.
* **Type of Vehicle (vehicle_type_quotation)**: Select the type of vehicle you own from the options provided.
* **Vehicle Make/Model/Year (vehicle_make_model_year)**: Provide a description of your vehicle, including make, model, and year.
* **Radius of Operation (vehicle_usage_radius)**: Indicate how far you typically drive in miles.
* **Type of Cargo Carried (type_of_cargo)**: If applicable, describe the type of cargo you carry.
* **Number of Drivers to Insure (number_of_drivers)**: Enter the number of drivers who will be covered under this insurance quote.
* **Ages of Primary Drivers (driver_ages)**: List the ages of the primary drivers separated by commas.
* **Any accidents or violations in the past 3 years? (driving_history_status)**: Select whether you have had any accidents or violations in the past 3 years.
* **Current Liability Coverage Limits (current_liability_coverage)**: If you already have liability coverage, enter the current limits.
* **Desired Liability Coverage Limits (desired_liability_coverage)**: Select your preferred liability coverage from the options provided.
* **I acknowledge the insurance quotation terms (quotation_acknowledgement)**: Select **Yes, I agree** to receive the insurance quote.
* **Quotation Confirmation (signature_confirmation_quotation)**: Sign your name for insurance quotation confirmation.
* **Any additional comments or requests? (additional_quotation_comments)**: Provide any additional details or requests for the insurance quote.
