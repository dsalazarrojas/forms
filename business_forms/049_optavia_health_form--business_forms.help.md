# Optavia Health Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on your medical history and any symptoms you're experiencing. It's essential to fill this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the data collected is helpful for your health management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Fill in your medical history in the "medical_history" field.
3. Describe your symptoms in the "symptoms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Please provide a detailed account of your medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical procedures you've undergone.
- **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, required): Describe any symptoms you're currently experiencing, such as pain, fever, or difficulty breathing. Be as specific as possible to ensure that your healthcare provider understands your condition.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any question or feel that it's not relevant to your situation, ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
- This form is designed to be completed by the user themselves, without any assistance from a healthcare professional. If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to form@gic.mx.
