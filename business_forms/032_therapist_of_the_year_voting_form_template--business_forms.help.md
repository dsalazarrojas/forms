# Therapist Of The Year Voting Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect votes for the Therapist of the Year award from users who want to cast their vote for one of three nominated therapists.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your `Form Id` to help us track your vote.
2. Enter your `Therapist Id` to identify the therapist you want to vote for.
3. Enter your `First Name` and `Last Name` to confirm your identity.
4. Enter a valid `Email` address to ensure that your vote is unique.
5. Select one of the three `Vote for Therapist 1`, `Vote for Therapist 2`, or `Vote for Therapist 3` options to cast your vote.
6. Enter your vote count for each of the three therapists by typing the number of votes you have cast for them in the `Vote Count for Therapist 1`, `Vote Count for Therapist 2`, and `Vote Count for Therapist 3` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Id (`form_id`)
* **Form Id** (`number`, required): Enter a unique ID to help us track your vote.

### Therapist Id (`therapist_id`)
* **Therapist Id** (`number`, required): Enter the ID of the therapist you want to vote for.

### First Name (`first_name`)
* **First Name** (`text`, optional): Enter your first name to confirm your identity.

### Last Name (`last_name`)
* **Last Name** (`text`, optional): Enter your last name to confirm your identity.

### Email (`email`)
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address to ensure that your vote is unique.

### Vote for Therapist 1 (`vote_for_therapist_1`)
* **Vote for Therapist 1** (`select_one`, required): Select one of the three options to cast your vote for Therapist 1.

### Vote for Therapist 2 (`vote_for_therapist_2`)
* **Vote for Therapist 2** (`select_one`, required): Select one of the three options to cast your vote for Therapist 2.

### Vote for Therapist 3 (`vote_for_therapist_3`)
* **Vote for Therapist 3** (`select_one`, required): Select one of the three options to cast your vote for Therapist 3.

### Notes (`notes`)
* **Notes** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your vote.

### Vote Count for Therapist 1 (`vote_count_therapist_1`)
* **Vote Count for Therapist 1** (`number`, required): Enter the number of votes you have cast for Therapist 1.

### Vote Count for Therapist 2 (`vote_count_therapist_2`)
* **Vote Count for Therapist 2** (`number`, required): Enter the number of votes you have cast for Therapist 2.

### Vote Count for Therapist 3 (`vote_count_therapist_3`)
* **Vote Count for Therapist 3** (`number`, required): Enter the number of votes you have cast for Therapist 3.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure that your vote is unique.
* If you have trouble casting your vote, please contact us for assistance.
* You can only vote once for each therapist.
