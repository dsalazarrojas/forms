# a_r_cleanup_quote_request--business_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from companies requesting a cleanup and reconciliation quote from an AR specialist or vendor.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, provide the required information in the following sections:

1.  Requester & Company Information: Enter your company's name, contact person's name, contact email, and contact phone number.
2.  Financial Information: Enter the current total accounts receivable balance and the number of open invoices.
3.  Aging Profile: Select all the relevant aging profile buckets that apply to your current situation.
4.  Services Required: Choose the services you need from the list provided.
5.  Desired Completion Date: Enter the target date for the completion of the cleanup process.
6.  Budget Range: If you have a budget range in mind, select it from the options provided.
7.  Additional Information: Provide any additional access requirements or information that might impact the cleanup and reconciliation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company name** (`Company name`, required): Enter your company's name as a legal entity requesting the quote.
* **Primary contact name** (`Primary contact name`, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for onboarding and the quote process.
* **Contact email** (`Contact email`, required): Provide the email for proposal and questions.
* **Contact phone** (`Contact phone`, optional): Enter the direct phone number for urgency or special requests.
* **Total AR balance** (`Total accounts receivable balance (USD)`, required): Enter the current total outstanding balance in USD.
* **Number of open invoices** (`Number of open invoices`, required): Enter the whole number of open invoices across all customers.
* **Aging profile** (`Aging profile (select all that apply)`, required): Select all the aging profile buckets that apply to your current situation (0-30 days, 31-60 days, 61-90 days, 91-180 days, and over 180 days).
* **Services required** (`Services required (select all that apply)`, required): Choose all the services you want quoted from the list (Data cleansing & validation, Customer outreach & collections, Reconciliation & dispute resolution, Reporting & dashboarding, and Ongoing AR management).
* **Desired completion date** (`Desired completion date`, optional): Enter the target date for the completion of the cleanup process.
* **Budget range** (`Budget range estimate (USD)`, optional): If you have a budget range in mind, select it from the options provided.
* **Expected access requirements** (`Expected access requirements`, optional): Enter any expected access requirements or information that might impact the cleanup and reconciliation process.
* **Additional information** (`Additional information for vendors`, optional): Provide any additional information that might impact the cleanup and reconciliation process.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate quotes.
- If you're unsure about any field, reach out to the AR specialist or vendor for clarification.
- The more information you provide, the better they can scope the proposal and quote for your company.
