# Dinner Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for attendees of a dinner seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields for the event details and attendee information.
2. Enter the attendee's name, email, phone number, and any dietary restrictions.
3. Select the method of payment and enter the payment date.
4. Confirm attendance by selecting "Yes" for "Is Attending".
5. Enter the RSVP date and time.
6. Optionally, provide any special requests or additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event or seminar.
* **Event Start Date**: Enter the date the event is scheduled to start.
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the time the event is scheduled to start.
* **Event End Time**: Enter the time the event is scheduled to end.
* **Event Location**: Enter the physical location of the event.
* **Attendee Name**: Enter the name of the individual attending the event.
* **Attendee Email**: Enter the email address of the attendee.
* **Attendee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the attendee.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: List any dietary restrictions the attendee has.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests the attendee has.
* **Is Attending**: Select "Yes" to confirm attendance.
* **RSVP Date**: Enter the date the attendee RSVPs.
* **RSVP Time**: Enter the time the attendee RSVPs.
* **Payment Option**: Select the method of payment (Cash, Check, Credit Card).
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date of payment.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment (Credit Card, Check, Cash).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the event or attendee.
* **Event Details**: Enter additional details about the event.
* **Attendee Details**: Enter additional details about the attendee.
* **Event Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the event.
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of the payment (Active, Inactive).
* **Event Status**: Select the status of the event (Active, Inactive).
* **Attendee Status**: Select the status of the attendee (Active, Inactive).
* **RSVP Status**: Select the status of the RSVP (Active, Inactive).
* **Payment RSVP Status**: Select the status of the payment RSVP (Active, Inactive).
