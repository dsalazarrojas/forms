# leadership_development_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to conduct an audit of leadership development within an organization. It is used to evaluate the effectiveness of leadership within the organization and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully to understand its purpose and the expected outcome.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. For fields with multiple options, select the most applicable option(s) for your situation.
4. If a field is marked as required, ensure that you provide a response.
5. For fields with a "select_multiple" type, you can select more than one option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction**: A brief introduction to provide context for the form.
* **leader_effectiveness**: Rate the overall effectiveness of leadership within your team or department.
*   *Scale: 1 (Low) to 10 (High)*
* **team_dynamics**: Select all that apply regarding team dynamics:
	+ Option 1: Collaborative environment
	+ Option 2: Clear goals and expectations
	+ Option 3: Regular feedback and coaching
* **leader_skills**: Select the most important leadership skill(s) for you as a leader:
	+ Skill 1: Communication
	+ Skill 2: Problem-solving
	+ Skill 3: Strategic planning
* **feedback**: Provide any relevant feedback about your team or department's leadership.
* **recommendations**: Suggest any recommendations for leadership development initiatives or training programs.
* **assigned_tool**: If a specific tool or resource was assigned to you or your team, please describe it.
* **other_assigned_tool**: If a tool or resource was assigned that is not listed, please describe it.
* **submit**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information for follow-up.
