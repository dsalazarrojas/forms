# E Commerce Transaction Compliance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Commerce Transaction Compliance Verification Form is used to collect and verify critical information about e-commerce transactions. It is a mandatory document for all online businesses to ensure the legitimacy and accuracy of transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start Form" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Complete each field as instructed, making sure to provide accurate and honest information.
3. If a field is required, it is marked as such; please ensure that you fill it out accordingly.
4. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Details
* **Order Details (#1)**: Provide a brief description of the transaction, including the product(s) purchased and the quantity.
* **Customer Info (#2)**: Fill out this field with the customer's name, title, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Shipping Address (#3)**: Enter the customer's shipping address, including the street address, city, state or province, and zip code.
* **Payment Method (#4)**: Select the payment method used for the transaction (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, JCB, or PayPal).
* **Note (#5)**: If necessary, provide additional notes or comments about the transaction.
* **Email (#6)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone (#7)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Transaction Details (#8)**: Provide any additional details about the transaction, such as the transaction date, time, or amount.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure the legitimacy and accuracy of the transaction.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to contact our support team.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your company's policies and procedures for guidance.
