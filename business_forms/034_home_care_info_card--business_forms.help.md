# Home Care Info Card - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record client information in a clear and organized manner for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's title and other relevant details on page 1.
2. On page 2, provide client contact information, including phone number, email, and contact method (phone, email, or other).
3. Enter the client's address on page 3.
4. Record the date and time of the last visit on page 4.
5. Add any additional notes or comments on page 5.
6. Enter the client's email and phone number on page 6.
7. Record any medical information, including medication and allergies, on page 7.
8. Check the home visit status on page 8.
9. Enter the follow-up status on page 9.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Care Info Card (1)**: Enter a brief title for the client's home care record.
* **Client Data (2)**: Enter any additional client information that is not already recorded.
* **Client Info (3)**: Enter any additional client information that is not already recorded.
* **Contact Method (4)**: Select the method through which you would like to contact the client (phone, email, or other).
* **Contact Method Other (5)**: If "other" is selected for contact method, enter the specific method here.
* **Address (6)**: Enter the client's home address.
* **Date (7)**: Enter the date of the last visit.
* **Time (8)**: Enter the time of the last visit.
* **Note (9)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the client.
* **Email (10)**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone (11)**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Fax (12)**: Enter the client's fax number.
* **Home Visit (13)**: Record the status of the home visit (completed, pending, not visited).
* **Home Visit Other (14)**: If "not visited" is selected, enter the reason why the visit was not made.
* **Follow Up (15)**: Record the status of the follow-up (completed, pending, not completed).
* **Follow Up Other (16)**: If "not completed" is selected, enter the reason why the follow-up was not made.
* **Next Visit (17)**: Enter the date and time of the next scheduled visit.
* **Medical Info (18)**: Record any medical information, including medication and allergies.
* **Medication (19)**: List any medications the client is currently taking.
* **Allergy (20)**: List any allergies the client has.
* **Medication Allergy (21)**: If the client has an allergy to a specific medication, list it here.
* **Medical Condition (22)**: Record any medical conditions the client has.
* **Home Visit Status (23)**: Record the status of the home visit (completed, pending, not visited).
* **Home Visit Status Other (24)**: If "not visited" is selected, enter the reason why the visit was not made.
* **Visit Details (25)**: Enter any additional details about the visit.
* **Follow Up Status (26)**: Record the status of the follow-up (completed, pending, not completed).
* **Follow Up Status Other (27)**: If "not completed" is selected, enter the reason why the follow-up was not made.

Please note that the fields marked as optional are not required and can be left blank if not applicable.
