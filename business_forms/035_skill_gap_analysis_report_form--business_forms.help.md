# Skill Gap Analysis Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees and managers assess and discuss skills gaps within the organization. It provides a platform for identifying areas where employees need improvement, recommending training and development opportunities, and tracking progress towards closing the skill gaps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and approvals before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
3. Select the relevant options for each field as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID (1)**: Enter the employee ID number.
* **Employee Name (2)**: Enter the employee's name.
* **Employee Email (3)**: Enter the employee's email address.
* **Employee Phone (4)**: Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Manager Name (5)**: Enter the manager's name.
* **Manager Email (6)**: Enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone (7)**: Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Training Department (8)**: Select the department where the training is being coordinated.
* **Skill Level (9)**: Select the level of skill required for the training.
* **Training Location (10)**: Select the location where the training will take place.
* **Date (11)**: Select the date of the training session.
* **Time (12)**: Select the time of the training session.
* **Notes (13)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the training session.
* **Employee Comments (14)**: Enter comments or feedback from the employee.
* **Manager Comments (15)**: Enter comments or feedback from the manager.
* **Training Coordinator Comments (16)**: Enter comments or feedback from the training coordinator.
* **Training Approvals (17)**: Select any relevant approvals or approvals required for the training session.
* **Training Recommendations (18)**: Select any recommended training opportunities for the employee.
* **Skill Gap Description (19)**: Enter a description of the skill gap being addressed.
* **Training Plan (20)**: Select the training plan for the employee.
* **Training Status (21)**: Select the status of the training session.

## Tips

* Be sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.

This form is designed to streamline and simplify the skill gap analysis process, and by following these instructions, you will be able to effectively use it for your needs.
