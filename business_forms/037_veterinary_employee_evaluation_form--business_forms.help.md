# veterinary_employee_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Vetinary Employee Evaluation Form" is designed to assess the performance and progress of veterinary employees. It helps evaluate their overall rating and provides feedback to colleagues and the director. This form serves as a tool for employee growth and development, allowing managers and evaluators to track progress and provide constructive feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Performance Evaluation Form**: Answer the questions and provide specific examples to assess the employee's performance over a period of time.
2. Ensure that you fill in all required fields, as indicated by the "Required" label.
3. Provide feedback to the Senior Vet, colleagues, and Director as per the employee's performance.
4. Evaluate the employee's overall rating by selecting one of the options provided.
5. Confirm that the form is completed and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performance Evaluation Form (employee_evaluation_form)**: A text field to record the employee's performance evaluation, providing specific examples and details.
* **Feedback To Senior Vet (feedback_to_senior_vet)**: A text field for providing feedback and comments to the Senior Vet, which can be completed at the evaluator's discretion.
* **Action Items Completed (action_items_completed)**: A numerical field for recording the number of action items completed by the employee.
* **Evaluation Date (veterinary_employee_evaluation_form_date)**: A date field indicating the date of the evaluation.
* **Overall Rating (overall_rating)**: A select-one field for evaluating the employee's overall performance, with options Very Good, Good, Fair, and Poor.
* **Feedback To Colleagues (feedback_to_colleagues)**: A text field for providing feedback to colleagues.
* **Feedback To Director (feedback_to_director)**: A text field for providing feedback to the Director.
* **Evaluation Form Completed (evaluation_form_completed)**: A select-one field indicating whether the form is completed or not.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Provide specific examples and details when answering the Performance Evaluation Form.
- Evaluate the employee's overall rating based on their performance.
- Confirm that the form is completed and accurate before submitting it.
