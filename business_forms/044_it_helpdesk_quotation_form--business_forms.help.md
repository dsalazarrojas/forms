# it_helpdesk_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for service requests from customers, including details about the service requested, contact information, and estimated costs. This form will help support staff to better understand and respond to customer service requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the service requested by the customer.
2. Provide the customer's name and contact information, including their email address and phone number.
3. Enter the estimated start and end dates for the service.
4. Specify the estimated duration of the service.
5. Enter the estimated cost of the service.
6. Select the type of service requested (e.g. IT Support, Network Administration, etc.).
7. Indicate the current status of the service (e.g. Open, In Progress, Completed).
8. Add any additional contact information or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Service Requested** (`service_requested`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the service requested by the customer.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
- **Service Description** (`service_description`, text, optional): Enter a more detailed description of the service requested.
- **Service Start Date** (`service_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the estimated start date for the service.
- **Service End Date** (`service_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the estimated end date for the service.
- **Service Duration** (`service_duration`, number, optional): Enter the estimated duration of the service.
- **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, number, optional): Enter the estimated cost of the service.
- **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of service requested (e.g. IT Support, Network Administration, etc.).
- **Service Status** (`service_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current status of the service (e.g. Open, In Progress, Completed).
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
- **Contact Cell** (`contact_cell`, text, optional): Enter the customer's cell phone number.
- **Contact Notes** (`contact_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure timely and effective service response.
* Use the estimated dates and costs as a guide, but adjust as needed based on actual service progression.
* Keep the contact information up-to-date to ensure easy communication with the customer.
