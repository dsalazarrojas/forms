# What If Scenario Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to analyze and document scenarios that are used for risk management and mitigation. It is a crucial tool for identifying potential risks and developing effective mitigation strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field in the form as instructed.
2. Provide detailed and accurate information in each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Planning Scenarios** (`planning_scenarios`, text, required): Describe the scenario being analyzed, including the context and the potential risks involved.
* **Compare Impacts** (`compare_impacts`, select_multiple, required): Compare the potential impacts of different risks, selecting all applicable options. Choose 'Yes' if the impact is applicable, or 'No' if not.
* **Mitigation Steps** (`mitigation_steps`, text, optional): If applicable, describe the steps taken to mitigate the risk or scenario.
* **Risks** (`risks`, select_one, required): Identify the type of risk, selecting 'Yes' if the risk is present, or 'No' if not.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, email, required): List the team members involved in the risk analysis, including their email addresses.
* **Scenario Details** (`scenario_details`, date, required): Enter the date of the scenario, including year, month, and day.
* **Impact Assessment** (`impact_assessment`, number, required): Assess the potential impact of the scenario, using numbers to evaluate the severity.
* **Mitigation Strategy** (`mitigation_strategy`, text, required): Describe the strategy or plan to mitigate the risk or scenario.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, text, optional): Track the review status of the scenario, providing any necessary updates or comments.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, optional): Include any comments or feedback from reviewers.
* **Scenario Status** (`scenario_status`, time, required): Set a deadline for reviewing and finalizing the scenario.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required): Outline the next steps to take regarding the scenario, including any necessary actions or deadlines.

**Tips**

* Be thorough and accurate when completing each field.
* Review and revise the form before submission.
* Use the form as a template for consistent and effective risk analysis and mitigation.
