# Site Visit Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Site Visit Checklist form is a tool for documenting and tracking site visits. It collects information about the site, including general details, safety hazards, inspection comments, and the inspector's notes and signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Site Visit Checklist form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's sections and fields to understand the required and optional information.
2. Complete the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. Use the hint provided for each field as a reference for filling out the form.
