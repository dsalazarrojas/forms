# Arcade Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Arcade Complaint Form is a tool used to document and report issues or incidents that occurred during a visit to our arcade. The form helps us understand and resolve customer complaints efficiently, ensuring that we can address the issues and improve our services for future visits.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your descriptions and responses.
3. Select the correct options for each field based on your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name to identify yourself as the person filing the complaint.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for follow-up communication regarding your complaint.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for follow-up communication, if desired.
* **Date of Visit** (`visit_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you visited the arcade when the issue occurred.
* **Time of Visit** (`visit_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the approximate time you visited the arcade when the issue occurred.
* **Complaint Category** (`complaint_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category that best describes the nature of your complaint.
* **Game or Machine Name (if applicable)** (`machine_name`, `text`, optional): If the issue was related to a specific game or machine, enter its name.
* **Detailed Description of Complaint** (`complaint_description`, `text`, required): Describe the issue that occurred in as much detail as possible.
* **Money Lost (if applicable)** (`money_lost`, `number`, optional): If you lost money as a result of the issue, enter the amount in dollars.
* **Were there witnesses?** (`witness_present`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether there were witnesses to the incident.
* **Desired Resolution** (`resolution_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the resolution that would satisfy you in this situation.
* **Resolution Amount** (`resolution_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the dollar amount you are requesting as a resolution.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would prefer to be contacted regarding the resolution.
* **When would you like a response?** (`timeline_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Select how quickly you would like to receive a response.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out, as they are essential for us to understand and address your complaint accurately.
