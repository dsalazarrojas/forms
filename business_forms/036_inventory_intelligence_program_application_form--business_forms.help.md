# Inventory Intelligence Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is intended for users to apply to the Inventory Intelligence Program. It collects necessary information to assess their qualifications and suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you provide your actual contact information.
3. Enter your date of birth in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
4. Select your current job title.
5. Enter your years of experience in the correct format (number).
6. Double-check your email address for correctness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (user_info):
	+ Enter your name, which will be used to identify you in the program.
* **Contact Info** (contact_info):
	+ Enter your current contact information, including your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Address** (address):
	+ Enter your current address for logistical purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth):
	+ Enter your date of birth in the format yyy-mm-dd (e.g., 1999-12-31).
* **Job Title** (job_title):
	+ Select your current job title from the available options.
* **Company** (company):
	+ Enter your current or previous company name.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number):
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (email):
	+ Enter your valid email address.
* **Job Title** (job_title):
	+ This field is a duplicate of the "Job Title" field above. Please fill out only one "Job Title" field.
* **Years of Experience** (years_of_experience):
	+ Enter your years of experience in the correct format (number).
