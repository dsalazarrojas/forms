# Facility And Production Capabilities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a facility's capabilities, including its type, production capacity, contact information, and location. The goal is to gather details that will help the organization assess and understand the facility's capabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the type of facility (Manufacturing Facility, Service Facility, or Other)
* Enter the production capacity (a numerical value)
* Provide contact information (name, address, phone number, email)
* Select the location of the facility (North America, South America, Europe, Asia, Africa)
* Enter any additional facility information as needed

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Type**: This field is used to select the type of facility. Choose one of the options: Manufacturing Facility, Service Facility, or Other.
* **Production Capacity**: This field requires a numerical value that represents the facility's production capacity. Enter a whole number only.
* **Contact Info**: Enter the name of the contact person for the facility.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the facility.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person for the facility.
* **Contact Info 2**: Enter any additional contact information for the facility.
* **Facility Location**: Select one or more of the options that represent the location of the facility: North America, South America, Europe, Asia, Africa.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Facility Info 2**: Enter any additional information about the facility.
* **Facility Info 3** (Note: This field is not visible on the form, but it's included here for consistency with the field names): This field is not visible in the form and should not be filled in.

Note: Fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled in, while those marked as optional can be left blank if not applicable.
