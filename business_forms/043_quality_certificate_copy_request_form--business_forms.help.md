# Quality Certificate Copy Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a quality certificate copy from our organization. Please fill out the form with your details and purpose of the request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide your details in the "Applicant Details" field.
3. Specify the purpose of your request in the "Purpose" field.
4. Indicate your delivery preferences in the "Delivery Preferences" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Details**: Enter your name, company, and contact information in this field. This will help us identify you and contact you regarding your request.
* **Purpose**: Specify the reason why you are requesting a quality certificate copy. This will help us understand your requirements and process your request efficiently.
* **Delivery Preferences**: Indicate how you would like to receive the certificate copy, e.g., email, physical mail, or fax. 

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that we can process your request correctly.
* If you have any additional information or documentation to support your request, please attach it to the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
