# Retail Blockchain Partnership Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register potential partners for our retail blockchain partnership program. It helps us understand their level of interest, qualifications, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete all required fields marked as **required**.
2. Select the level of interest you have in our partnership program.
3. Choose your qualifications that align with our partnership requirements.
4. Select your preferred contact methods (phone or email).
5. Add any additional notes about your partnership proposal.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter the date you are available to meet or discuss further.
8. Enter the time you are available to meet or discuss further.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Interest (High, Medium, Low)**: This field determines the level of interest you have in our partnership program. Please select the level that best describes your enthusiasm for working with us.
* **Qualification (Strong, Medium, Low)**: This field is used to evaluate your qualifications and skills relevant to our partnership. Please select all that apply from the available options.
* **Contact Details (Phone, Email)**: This field is used to gather your preferred contact information. Please select one or more of the available options to indicate your preferred method of communication.
* **Additional Notes**: This field is optional, but it's a great opportunity to provide any additional information about your partnership proposal that may be relevant to us.
* **Email**: Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your partnership proposal.
* **Date**: This field is used to schedule a meeting or discussion about your partnership proposal. Please enter the date you are available to meet or discuss further.
* **Time**: Similarly, this field is used to schedule a meeting or discussion. Please enter the time you are available to meet or discuss further.
