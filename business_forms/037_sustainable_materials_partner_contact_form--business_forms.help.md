# Sustainable Materials Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and preferences from partners who are interested in sustainability materials. It helps us understand their contact details, role, job functions, and location, allowing us to tailor our communication strategy and build relationships with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and company information in the "Partner Name", "Organization", and "Company" fields.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your role from the "Role" field.
5. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
6. Describe your department in the "Department" field.
7. Enter your location in the "Location" field.
8. Briefly describe your job functions in the "Job Functions" field.
9. Write a brief description of your company in the "About Us" field.
10. Answer the "Why Do You" field with a brief description of why you are interested in sustainability materials.
11. Answer the "What Do You" field with a brief description of what you are looking for.
12. Enter any other information you would like to share in the "What Else" field.
13. Select your preferred contact method from the "Contact Preferences" field.
14. Enter any other contact preferences in the "Other Contact" field.
15. Indicate how often you would like to be followed up on in the "Follow-up" field.
16. Select the frequency of follow-up from the "Follow-up Frequency" field.
17. Enter any next steps or follow-up actions in the "Next Steps" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role from the options provided.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter your location.
* **Job Functions** (`job_functions`, text, required): Briefly describe your job functions.
* **About Us** (`about_us`, note, required): Briefly describe your company.
* **Why Do You** (`why_do_you`, text, required): Briefly describe why you are interested in sustainability materials.
* **What Do You** (`what_do_you`, text, required): Briefly describe what you are looking for.
* **What Else** (`what_else`, text, required): Enter any other information you would like to share.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, select_one, required): Select your preferred contact method.
* **Other Contact** (`other_contact`, text, required): Enter any other contact preferences.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would like to be followed up on.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select the frequency of follow-up.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required): Enter any next steps or follow-up actions.
