# Employee Safety Violation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Safety Violation Form is used to document incidents of employee safety violations or near misses in the workplace. This form helps gather information about the incident, including the date, location, and details of the incident, as well as the type of safety violation that occurred. The information collected will help management track and analyze safety incidents, identify areas for improvement, and take corrective action to prevent future incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the incident in the `employee_safety_violation_date` field.
2. Describe the location where the incident occurred in the `incident_location` field.
3. Provide a detailed description of what happened in the `violation_details` field.
4. Select all applicable options for `employee_safety_violation_type` to indicate the type of safety violation that occurred.
5. If the incident is closed, select "Closed" in the `employee_safety_violation_status` field. If it is not closed, select "Open".
6. Identify who reported the incident by selecting "Management", "Employee", or "Supervisor" in the `incident_reporter` field.
7. Provide a brief description of the incident in the `incident_description` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Safety Violation Date** (employee_safety_violation_date, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Incident Location** (incident_location, text, required/optional): Describe the location where the incident occurred, such as a specific room or area.
* **Violation Details** (violation_details, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Employee Safety Violation Type** (employee_safety_violation_type, select multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable options that describe the type of safety violation that occurred, such as "Burn Injury" or "Muscle or Joint Injury".
* **Incident Status** (employee_safety_violation_status, select one, required/optional): Select "Closed" if the incident has been resolved, or "Open" if it is still being investigated or addressed.
* **Incident Reporter** (incident_reporter, select one, required/optional): Identify who reported the incident, such as "Management", "Employee", or "Supervisor".
* **Incident Description** (incident_description, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the incident.
