# Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for cleaners and supervisors to record and document the cleaning tasks completed at a specific location, including the cleaner's name, date, and completion time. The form helps standardize the cleaning process and ensures consistency and accuracy in reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the property address or location (ID 2) where the cleaning was performed.
2. Enter the date of the cleaning (ID 3).
3. Provide your name and title as the cleaner (ID 4).
4. Answer the questions in the kitchen and living areas sections (ID 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12).
5. Complete the bathroom tasks (ID 14, 15, 16).
6. List any supplies that are running low (ID 17) and note any client special requests (ID 18).
7. Record your completion time (ID 19).
8. Provide a supervisor's inspection signature or note (ID 20).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Address/Location (ID 2)**: Enter the address or location where the cleaning was performed.
* **Date of Cleaning (ID 3)**: Enter the date of the cleaning visit.
* **Cleaner Name (ID 4)**: Enter your name as the cleaner.
* **Countertops wiped and sanitized (ID 6)**: Choose "Done" if the countertops were wiped and sanitized.
* **Sink cleaned and polished (ID 7)**: Choose "Done" if the sink was cleaned and polished.
* **Exterior of appliances (ID 8)**: Choose "Done" if the exterior of appliances were wiped.
* **All flat surfaces dusted (ID 10)**: Choose "Done" if all flat surfaces were dusted.
* **Floors vacuumed or swept (ID 11)**: Choose "Done" if the floors were vacuumed or swept.
* **Hard floors mopped (ID 12)**: Choose "Done" if the hard floors were mopped.
* **Toilet cleaned and sanitized (ID 14)**: Choose "Done" if the toilet was cleaned and sanitized.
* **Shower and tub scrubbed (ID 15)**: Choose "Done" if the shower and tub were scrubbed.
* **Mirrors and glass cleaned (ID 16)**: Choose "Done" if the mirrors and glass were cleaned.
* **Supplies low (ID 17)**: List any supplies that are running low.
* **Client special requests (ID 18)**: Note any special requests from the client.
* **Time Finished (ID 19)**: Record the time you finished the cleaning.
* **Supervisor Inspection Sign-off (ID 20)**: Provide a note for the supervisor's inspection or signature.

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately report all completed tasks and any issues encountered.
- Be detailed in your responses, especially when listing client special requests.
- Ensure the supervisor signs off or notes the completion of the tasks.
- This form is designed to help standardize and improve cleaning efficiency and quality.
