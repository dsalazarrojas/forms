# Corporate Swag Delivery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Corporate Swag Delivery Form is designed to capture information needed to ship corporate swag to employees. This form helps to ensure accurate delivery of swag items to the correct recipients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Form ID, which is used to identify the form.
2. Provide your Employee Name, which will be used to address the swag item to you.
3. Enter your Shipping Address, which is used to determine the physical address to ship the swag item to.
4. Enter your Shipping City, which is used to further refine the shipping address.
5. Enter your Shipping State, which is used to determine the state for shipping purposes.
6. Enter your Shipping Zip, which is used to determine the postal code for shipping.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Id (1)**: Enter a unique identifier for the form, used to track its progress.
* **Employee Name (2)**: Enter your name as it appears on official company documents.
* **Shipping Address (3)**: Enter the physical address where you would like the swag item shipped to.
* **Shipping City (4)**: Enter the city where the swag item will be shipped to.
* **Shipping State (5)**: Enter the state where the swag item will be shipped to.
* **Shipping Zip (6)**: Enter the postal code for the shipping address to ensure accurate delivery.
