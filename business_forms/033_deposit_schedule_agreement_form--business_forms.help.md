# Deposit Schedule Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to establish a deposit schedule agreement between participants. It outlines the terms and frequency of payment, as well as the type of deposit and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, ensure all required fields are filled out, marked by an asterisk. 
2.  Select the correct type of deposit from the options provided.
3.  Choose the payment frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
4.  Select the payment start date and end date.
5.  Specify the payment amount.
6.  Ensure all participants have signed and selected their signature options.
7.  Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Deposit Type* (Deposit Type, select_one, required): Choose the type of deposit (Bank Deposit, Cash, Other).
* **Payment Frequency* (Payment Frequency, select_one, required): Select the frequency of payments (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Payment Start Date (Payment Start Date, date, optional): Enter the start date of payment schedule.
* **Payment End Date (Payment End Date, date, optional): Enter the end date of payment schedule.
* **Payment Amount (Payment Amount, number, optional): Enter the total payment amount of the deposit.
* **Signatures (Signatures, select_multiple, required): Select all participants who have agreed to the payment schedule.
* **Email (Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the participant or contact.
* **Phone (Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the participant or contact.
* **Notes (Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the payment schedule.
* **Date Signed (Date Signed, date, optional): Enter the date the form was signed.
