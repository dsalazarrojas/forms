# A/B Testing Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The A/B Testing Calculator form is a tool to help business users evaluate the performance of two variants of a product, feature, or experience. It allows you to input data on your experiment and analyze results to determine which variant performs better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a short identifier for your experiment in the "Experiment name" field.
2. Provide a one-sentence hypothesis describing the expected impact of your experiment.
3. Select the primary metric to measure success (e.g., conversion rate, click-through rate, etc.).
4. Enter the name of Variant A (e.g., Control or Current Experience).
5. Enter the name of Variant B (e.g., Treatment or New Variation).
6. Input the number of visitors shown to Variant A.
7. Input the number of conversions on Variant A.
8. Input the number of visitors shown to Variant B.
9. Input the number of conversions on Variant B.
10. Choose the desired significance level for your analysis (e.g., 90%, 95%, etc.).
11. Optionally, enter any calculation notes or assumptions.
12. Choose the recommended action based on your analysis results.
13. (Optional) Input the start and end dates of your experiment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Experiment name** (`Experiment name`, text, required): Enter a short identifier for this test (e.g., hero_cta_color).
- **Hypothesis** (`Hypothesis`, text, required): One-sentence hypothesis describing expected impact.
- **Primary metric to measure** (`Primary metric to measure`, select_one, required): Select the single metric used to declare success.
- **Variant A name** (`Variant A name`, text, required): e.g., Control or current experience.
- **Variant B name** (`Variant B name`, text, required): e.g., Treatment or new variation.
- **Visitors shown Variant A** (`Visitors shown Variant A`, integer, required): Whole number of unique visitors exposed to A.
- **Conversions on Variant A** (`Conversions on Variant A`, integer, required): Number of conversions (use whole number).
- **Visitors shown Variant B** (`Visitors shown Variant B`, integer, required): Whole number of unique visitors exposed to B.
- **Conversions on Variant B** (`Conversions on Variant B`, integer, required): Number of conversions (use whole number).
- **Desired significance level** (`Desired significance level`, select_one, required): Choose threshold for statistical significance.
- **Calculation notes / assumptions** (`Calculation notes / assumptions`, text, optional): Note sampling, segmentation or any caveats.
- **Recommended action** (`Recommended action`, select_one, required): Choose recommended next step based on result.
- **Experiment start date** (`Experiment start date`, date, optional): Leave blank if still running.
- **Experiment end date** (`Experiment end date`, date, optional): Leave blank if still running.
- **Analyst notes and interpretation** (`Analyst notes and interpretation`, text, optional): Free-form commentary on statistical test and context.

## Tips

* Ensure you input accurate and whole numbers for visitors and conversions.
* Choose a significance level that makes sense for your business goals and risk tolerance.
* Consider entering calculation notes or assumptions to provide context.
* Select a recommended action based on your analysis results.
* If your experiment is still running, leave the start and end dates blank.
