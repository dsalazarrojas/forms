# Smart Inventory Reorder Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Smart Inventory Reorder Application Form is used to submit a request to reorder items from your inventory. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the product you want to reorder using the "Product ID" field.
2. Enter the quantity you want to reorder.
3. Choose the "Reorder Frequency" for the product, whether it is "Yes" or "No".
4. Optionally, select the "Shipping Method" from the dropdown list.
5. Enter the "Shipping Address" if shipping method is not "UPS" or "FedEx".
6. Enter your "Contact Name" and "Contact Email" for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reorder Request** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for the reorder request.
  - This field is meant to provide additional context about the reorder request.
- **Product ID** (number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the product you want to reorder.
  - This is the product's unique identifier in your inventory system.
- **Quantity** (number, optional): Enter the quantity you want to reorder for the product.
  - This is the amount of product you want to add to your inventory.
- **Reorder Frequency** (select multiple, optional): Choose if you want to reorder the product "Yes" or "No".
  - This will help us determine if you want to continuously reorder the product or not.
- **Reorder Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you want to reorder the product.
  - This will help us determine if you have a specific date for the reorder.
- **Shipping Method** (select one, optional): Select the shipping method from "UPS", "FedEx", or "USPS".
  - This will help us determine the most efficient shipping method for your reorder.
- **Shipping Address** (text, optional): Enter the shipping address if you selected "USPS" as your shipping method.
  - This will help us determine the shipping address for the product.
- **Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter your name for communication purposes.
  - This will help us contact you with any questions or updates about your reorder request.
- **Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
  - This will help us contact you with any questions or updates about your reorder request.
