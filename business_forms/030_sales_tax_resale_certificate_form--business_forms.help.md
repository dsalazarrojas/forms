# Sales Tax Resale Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for sales tax purposes, specifically for resale certificates. It is used by sellers to provide information about their sales tax obligations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the seller's information in the **seller_info** field, which includes details about the seller's identity and contact information.
2. Enter the **purchaser_info** field with details about the purchaser, including their name, address, and contact information.
3. Enter your **resale_number**, which is a unique identifier for your sales tax accounts.
4. Enter the **resale_number_expiration** field to specify the date when the resale number expires or was revoked.
5. If you are exempt from sales tax, select a reason for exemption in the **exemption_reason** field.
6. Add any additional information to explain the exemption reason in the **exemption_reason_other** field.
7. Add any further comments about the exemption reason in the **exemption_reason_note** field.
8. Sign the form with your **seller_signature** to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
9. Sign the form with the **purchaser_signature** to confirm that they have received and reviewed the information.
10. Select the type of sales tax resale certificate you are submitting in the **sales_tax_resale_certificate** field.
11. Add any further comments about the sales tax resale certificate in the **sales_tax_resale_certificate_note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **seller_info**: Enter your business name, address, and contact information in this text field.
- **purchaser_info**: Enter the name, address, and contact information of the purchaser.
- **resale_number**: Enter your unique resale number, which is used to identify your sales tax accounts.
- **resale_number_expiration**: Enter the date when your resale number expires or was revoked.
- **exemption_reason**: Select the reason why you are exempt from sales tax.
  * Resale Number Required: You are not exempt from sales tax.
  * Resale Number Not Required: You are not required to have a resale number.
  * Resale Number Exempt: You are exempt from sales tax.
- **exemption_reason_other**: Enter any additional information to explain why you are exempt from sales tax.
- **exemption_reason_note**: Add any further comments about your exemption reason.
- **seller_signature**: Sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
- **purchaser_signature**: Sign the form to confirm that you have received and reviewed the information.
- **sales_tax_resale_certificate**: Select the type of sales tax resale certificate you are submitting.
  * Sales Tax Exempt: You are exempt from sales tax.
  * Sales Tax Resale Certificate: You are submitting a sales tax resale certificate.
- **sales_tax_resale_certificate_note**: Add any further comments about the sales tax resale certificate.
