# Sales Opportunity Tracker - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Sales Opportunity Tracker form is used to track sales opportunities from lead generation to close.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the **Sales Opportunity** field with the title of the sales opportunity.
2.  Enter the **Contact Name** of the individual or company associated with the opportunity.
3.  Select the current **Sales Stage** of the opportunity from the dropdown list.
4.  Enter the next **Action** to be taken in the opportunity in the next step.
5.  Enter the **Sales Probability** of converting the opportunity into a sale.
6.  If applicable, enter the **Last Action** taken in the opportunity.
7.  If applicable, enter the **Close Date** and **Close Time** of the opportunity.
8.  Select the methods used for **Follow-ups** from the list (Email, Phone, Meeting, Demo, Other).
9.  Enter any additional **Pipeline** information related to the opportunity.
10. Select the person **Assigned To** for the opportunity.
11. Enter any relevant **Sales Notes** about the opportunity.
12. Enter any relevant **Customer Notes** about the opportunity.
13. Enter any **Follow-up Notes** about the opportunity.
14. If applicable, enter the **Follow-up Date** and **Follow-up Time**.
15. Select the method used for **Last Follow-up** from the dropdown list.
16. Enter the **Follow-up Frequency** of the opportunity.
17. Enter the **Last Follow-up Time** of the opportunity.
18. Enter the reason for **Close Reason** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Sales Opportunity** (`Sales Opportunity`, text, required): Enter the title of the sales opportunity.
*   **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the individual or company associated with the opportunity.
*   **Sales Stage** (`Sales Stage`, select\_one, optional): Select the current stage of the sales opportunity from the dropdown list.
*   **Next Action** (`Next Action`, text, optional): Enter the next step to be taken in the opportunity.
*   **Sales Probability** (`Sales Probability`, number, optional): Enter the probability of converting the opportunity into a sale.
*   **Last Action** (`Last Action`, text, optional): Enter the last action taken in the opportunity.
*   **Close Date** (`Close Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the opportunity was closed.
*   **Close Time** (`Close Time`, time, optional): Enter the time the opportunity was closed.
*   **Follow-ups** (`Follow-ups`, select\_multiple, optional): Select the methods used for follow-ups from the list (Email, Phone, Meeting, Demo, Other).
*   **Pipeline** (`Pipeline`, text, optional): Enter any additional pipeline information related to the opportunity.
*   **Assigned To** (`Assigned To`, select\_one, required): Select the person assigned to the opportunity.
*   **Sales Notes** (`Sales Notes`, note, optional): Enter any sales notes about the opportunity.
*   **Customer Notes** (`Customer Notes`, note, optional): Enter any customer notes about the opportunity.
*   **Follow-up Notes** (`Follow-up Notes`, note, optional): Enter any follow-up notes about the opportunity.
*   **Follow-up Details** (`Follow-up Details`, select\_multiple, optional): Select the details about the follow-up (Email, Phone, Meeting, Demo, Other).
*   **Sales Stage Change** (`Sales Stage Change`, select\_one, required): Select the stage change of the opportunity from the dropdown list.
*   **Follow-up Method Change** (`Follow-up Method Change`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up method changes.
*   **Follow-up Frequency** (`Follow-up Frequency`, text, optional): Enter the follow-up frequency of the opportunity.
*   **Last Follow-up Time** (`Last Follow-up Time`, time, optional): Enter the last follow-up time of the opportunity.
*   **Close Reason** (`Close Reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for close (if applicable).

## Tips

*   Always fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to maintain a clear and up-to-date record of sales progress.
*   Use the form consistently across the company to avoid confusion.
*   Take into account any necessary changes and updates to the opportunities.
*   Keep in mind the importance of the opportunity's sales probability and stage.
*   Follow up with the customers and update the form accordingly.
*   Be aware of any deadlines and close dates for the opportunities.
