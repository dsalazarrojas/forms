# Mechanical Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mechanical Inspection Report form is used to document the findings of a mechanical inspection on a vehicle. It helps identify potential issues and maintenance needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select a date for the inspection event. This will help keep track of the time when the inspection was conducted.
2. Record the inspection data as relevant to the inspection event. This can include any specific details that were observed or data collected during the inspection.
3. Check off one of the options ('Yes' or 'No') for the vehicle's condition. This indicates whether any issues were found or if the vehicle is in good condition.
4. Enter any inspection notes that may be relevant to the inspection event. These can include comments, observations, or recommendations for maintenance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Test drive**: This field is used to record the date of the inspection event. Choose a date for the inspection.
*  **Inspection data**: This field is for recording any relevant information that was observed or collected during the inspection. Enter the details here.
*  **Vehicle condition**: This field is used to report on the general condition of the vehicle. Check off 'Yes' if any issues were found, and 'No' if the vehicle is in good condition.
*  **Inspection notes**: This field is for capturing any additional comments or recommendations for maintenance. Enter any relevant notes here.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are using this form for each inspection event separately.
* Be as detailed as possible when recording inspection data and notes.
* This form should only be completed by the person conducting the inspection.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure that it accurately reflects the vehicle's condition.
