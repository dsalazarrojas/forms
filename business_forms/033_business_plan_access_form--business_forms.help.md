# Business Plan Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Plan Access Form is a tool used by entrepreneurs and businesses to request access to their business plans and information. This form is designed to gather essential details about the business and the individual requesting access, ensuring that the information is shared securely and only with authorized parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Business Plan Access Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details as an entrepreneur or representative of a business.
2. Select the relevant sections of the business plan you would like to access.
3. Specify the duration of access you need.
4. Confirm your willingness to sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
5. Agree to the access terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Entrepreneur Name** (`entrepreneur_name`, `text`, required): Fill in your full name as the entrepreneur or representative of the business.
- **Email Address** (`entrepreneur_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address.
- **Phone Number** (`entrepreneur_phone`, `text`, required): Provide your contact phone number.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your business name.
- **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, optional): If available, provide your business website URL.
- **Business Stage** (`business_stage`, `select_one`, required): Select the current stage of your business (e.g., Idea Stage, MVP/Prototype, Pre-revenue, etc.).
- **Business Description** (`business_description`, `text`, required): Describe what your business does in a few sentences.
- **Funding Amount Sought** (`funding_sought`, `number`, required): Specify the amount of funding you are seeking.
- **Type of Requester** (`access_requester_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose who is requesting access (e.g., Investor, Business Partner, etc.).
- **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person requesting access.
- **Requester Contact Email** (`requester_email`, `email`, required): Provide the email address of the person requesting access.
- **Requester Company** (`requester_company`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the organization requesting access.
- **Interest in Business** (`requester_interest`, `text`, required): Explain why you are interested in the business plan.
- **Willing to Sign NDA** (`confidentiality_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your willingness to sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
- **Plan Sections Needed** (`plan_sections_needed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the sections of the business plan you require access to (e.g., Executive Summary, Market Analysis, etc.).
- **Duration of Access Needed** (`access_duration`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the duration of access you need (e.g., 30 days, 60 days, Ongoing).
- **Additional Requirements** (`additional_requirements`, `text`, optional): If any other conditions apply, describe them here.
- **Agree to Access Terms** (`agreement_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your agreement to the access terms.
