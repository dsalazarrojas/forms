# Vehicle Allocation And Exchange Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Allocation And Exchange Form is designed to collect information about vehicle allocation and exchange within a company or organization. This form helps track vehicle status, allocation decisions, and exchange activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and information to complete the form accurately.
2. Select the correct vehicle type from the options provided.
3. Enter the company name related to the vehicle allocation or exchange.
4. Enter the vehicle number or identifier.
5. Select the current exchange status of the vehicle (Available, In Exchange, or Allocated).
6. Enter the date of vehicle allocation, if applicable.
7. Enter the name or details of the vehicle allocated to, if applicable.
8. Enter any notes about the vehicle allocation or exchange.
9. Indicate if the vehicle has been returned, if applicable.
10. Enter the date and time of vehicle return, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Name (`company_name`)
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company or organization related to the vehicle allocation or exchange.

### Vehicle Type (`vehicle_type`)
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle being allocated or exchanged (Car, Truck, or Van).

### Vehicle Number (`vehicle_number`)
* **Vehicle Number**: Enter the vehicle's unique identifier or number.

### Exchange Status (`exchange_status`)
* **Exchange Status**: Select the current status of the vehicle (Available, In Exchange, or Allocated).

### Allocation Date (`allocation_date`)
* **Allocation Date**: Enter the date of vehicle allocation, if applicable.

### Allocated To (`allocated_to`)
* **Allocated To**: Enter the name or details of the vehicle allocated to.

### Allocation Notes (`allocation_notes`)
* **Allocation Notes**: Enter any notes about the vehicle allocation or exchange.

### Vehicle Returned (`vehicle_returned`)
* **Vehicle Returned**: Indicate if the vehicle has been returned.

### Vehicle Returned Date (`vehicle_returned_date`)
* **Vehicle Returned Date**: Enter the date of vehicle return, if applicable.

### Vehicle Returned Time (`vehicle_returned_time`)
* **Vehicle Returned Time**: Enter the time of vehicle return, if applicable.

### Vehicle Allocation Note (`vehicle_allocation_note`)
* **Vehicle Allocation Note**: Enter any notes about the vehicle allocation or exchange.
