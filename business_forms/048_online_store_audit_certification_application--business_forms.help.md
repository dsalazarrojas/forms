# Online Store Audit Certification Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online store audit certification application form is designed to help online store business owners apply for certification. The certification process is a review of the store's online presence, security practices, and customer service to ensure compliance with relevant standards and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your contact details, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide your company's details, including its name and address.
3. Select the type of business you operate (Online Store or Other).
4. If you selected "Other", provide a brief description of your business.
5. Enter your phone number and email address.
6. Enter your website URL.
7. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form** (1): Enter any additional information you would like to include in your application.
* **Contact Details** (2): Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Company Details** (3): Enter your company's name and address.
* **Business Type** (4): Select "Online Store" if you have an online store, otherwise select "Other".
* **Other (please specify)** (5): If you selected "Other", enter a brief description of your business.
* **Business Address** (6): Enter your business address.
* **Phone Number** (7): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (8): Enter your email address.
* **Website URL** (9): Enter your website URL.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact your certification authority.
