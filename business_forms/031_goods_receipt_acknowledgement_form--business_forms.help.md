# Goods Receipt Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the receipt of goods in a business setting, enabling accurate tracking and recording of deliveries.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Begin by filling out the "Business Details" section with the required information about the business or organization.
- Enter the details about the goods received, including any relevant information such as quantities, descriptions, or any other relevant details.
- Choose the current "Delivery Status" of the goods, either "Received" or "Not Received".
- Enter the date the goods were received.
- Provide any remarks or comments about the goods receipt in the "Remarks" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Details** (Business Details, text, required): Enter the name, address, and any other relevant information about the business or organization receiving the goods.
- **Goods Details** (Goods Details, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the goods received, including quantities and any other relevant information.
- **Delivery Status** (Delivery Status, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the goods delivery. Options are "Received" or "Not Received".
- **Goods Received** (Goods Received, date, required): Enter the date the goods were received.
- **Remarks** (Remarks, note, required): Provide any remarks or comments about the goods receipt.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- Review the form carefully for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure that the "Delivery Status" is up-to-date and accurate.
