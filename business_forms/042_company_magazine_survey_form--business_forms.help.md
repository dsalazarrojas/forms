# Company Magazine Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Company Magazine Survey Form is designed to collect feedback from readers on their reading habits, content preferences, and suggestions for future issues. This survey aims to improve the magazine's content and better serve its readership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name (Reader name) in the text field.
2. Select how often you read the magazine (How often do you read the magazine).
3. Rate the relevance of articles in the magazine (Content relevance).
4. Choose the sections you enjoy most (Favorite sections).
5. Provide any suggestions for future issues (Suggestions for future issues).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reader name** (`reader_name`, Text, Optional): This field is for your name. You can optionally enter your name to help us better understand who's responding to this survey.
* **How often do you read the magazine** (`readership_frequency`, Select One, Required): Select how often you read the magazine to help us understand your reading habits.
* **Content relevance** (`content_relevance`, Select One, Required): Rate the relevance of articles in the magazine. This will help us know how well we're meeting your content needs.
* **Favorite sections** (`favorite_sections`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the sections you enjoy most in the magazine. This will help us identify popular content areas.
* **Suggestions for future issues** (`suggestions`, Text, Optional): Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for future issues. This will help us improve the magazine's content and better serve you.
