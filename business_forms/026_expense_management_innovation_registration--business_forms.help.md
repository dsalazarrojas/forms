# expense-management-innovation-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register innovations related to expense management. The data collected will help categorize and filter information for future reference and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a category for your innovation from the options provided in the "Category" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your innovation in the "Description" field.
3. Assign the innovation to a tool or resource from the options provided in the "Assigned Tool" field.
4. Repeat the same category selection process for the second category.
5. Choose the person who submitted the innovation from the options provided in the "Submitted By" field.
6. Select the team or department that the innovation was submitted to from the options provided in the "Submitted To" field.
7. Enter the date when the innovation was submitted in the "Submitted On" field.
8. Generate a unique identifier for the form in the "Form Id" field.
9. Choose a file to output in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`Category`, select_multiple): Select one or more categories for your innovation. This will help categorize and filter the data later.
* **Description** (`Description`, text): Provide a brief description of your innovation. This field should be as clear and concise as possible.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, select_multiple): Select one or more tools or resources associated with your innovation.
* **Category 2** (`Category 2`, select_one): Select a category for your innovation. This field is similar to "Category" and may be used for secondary categorization.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, select_one): Choose the person who submitted the innovation. This field is used to track authorship and ownership.
* **Submitted To** (`Submitted To`, select_multiple): Select the team or department that received the submitted innovation.
* **Submitted On** (`Submitted On`, date): Enter the date when the innovation was submitted. This field helps track timelines and history.
* **Form Id** (`Form Id`, number): Generate a unique identifier for the form. This field is used for internal reference and tracking.
* **Output File** (`Output File`, select_one): Choose a file to output with your innovation. This field is used to attach relevant files or documents.
* **Category** (`Category`, select_multiple): Select one or more categories for your innovation. This field is a duplicate of the "Category" field and may be used for additional categorization.
* **Output File** (`Output File`, select_one): This field is similar to the "Output File" field and may be used for attaching additional files or documents.
