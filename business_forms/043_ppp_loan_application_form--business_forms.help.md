# ppp_loan_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paycheck Protection Program (PPP) is a loan program offered by the Small Business Administration (SBA) to provide financial support to small businesses affected by the pandemic. This form is designed to collect necessary business and owner information for the loan application process.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Review and understand the PPP requirements and eligibility criteria before proceeding.

### Step 2: Fill out each field accurately and completely.

### Step 3: Ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Loan Application Form**: This is the title of the form and a required field.
* **Business Name**: Enter your business name as it appears on your business documents. (Text, required)
* **Business Address**: Enter your business address. (Text, required)
* **Business Phone**: Enter your business phone number. (Text, required)
* **Business Email**: Enter your business email. (Email, required)
* **Date of Incorporation**: Enter the date your business was incorporated. (Date, required)
* **Business Website**: Enter your business website URL. (Text, optional)
* **Bank Account Number**: Enter your business bank account number. (Text, required)
* **Tax ID Number**: Enter your business Tax ID number. (Text, required)
* **Owner Name**: Enter your name as the owner of the business. (Text, required)
* **Owner Title**: Enter your title as the owner of the business. (Text, optional)
* **Owner Address**: Enter your address as the owner of the business. (Text, required)
* **Owner Phone**: Enter your phone number as the owner of the business. (Text, required)
* **Owner Email**: Enter your email as the owner of the business. (Email, required)
* **Owner Tax ID Number**: Enter your Tax ID number as the owner of the business. (Text, required)
* **Owner Business Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the business. (Text, optional)
* **Owner Employer Name**: Enter your employer's name. (Text, optional)
* **Owner Employer Phone**: Enter your employer's phone number. (Text, optional)
* **Owner Employer Email**: Enter your employer's email. (Email, optional)
* **Owner DBA Name**: Enter your DBA (Doing Business As) name. (Text, optional)
* **Owner DBA Phone**: Enter your DBA phone number. (Text, optional)
* **Owner DBA Email**: Enter your DBA email. (Email, optional)
* **Owner Employees**: Enter the number of employees you have. (Number, optional)
* **Owner Employees Employed Last 3 Years**: Enter the number of employees you have employed in the last 3 years. (Number, optional)

### Tips
- Ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
- Review and verify the information before submission.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the SBA for clarification.
