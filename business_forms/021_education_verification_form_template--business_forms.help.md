# Education Verification Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Education Verification Form Template" is a standard form used to verify an employee's education.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read through the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2.  Complete each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Education Verification Form (Page 1, `education_verification_form`)**: Enter your title as instructed above.
- **Employers Details** (`employer_details`): If you are an employee, ask your supervisor or HR representative for this information.
    - Enter the name and title of your supervisor or HR representative.
    - Enter your employer's address and contact number.
- **Employee Details** (`employee_details`): Enter your personal details as required by your employer.
- **Education Details** (`education_details`): Enter your educational background as follows:
    - Enter your high school diploma information (type: High School, College, University).
    - Enter your graduation date.
    - Enter your major (if applicable).
- **Employee Education** (`employee_education`): Select 'True' if your education is verified; 'False' if it is not; 'Pending' if it is still being verified.
- **Employers Education** (`employer_education`): Select the type of education your employer has (High School, College, University).
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth.
- **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`): Enter your graduation date.
- **Degree** (`degree`): Enter your degree (if applicable).
- **Major** (`major`): Enter your major (if applicable).
- **GPA** (`gpa`): Enter your GPA (if applicable).
- **Address** (`address`): Enter your address.
- **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
- **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your education.
- **Verified By** (`verified_by`): Enter the name of the person who verified your education.
- **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`): Sign the form to confirm your education.
- **Employer Signature** (`employer_signature`): Sign the form to confirm your employer's education.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Review the form with your supervisor or HR representative if you are unsure about any field.
- Sign the form to confirm your education.
