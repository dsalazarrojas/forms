# Commercial Land Use Policy Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commercial Land Use Policy Registration form is used to submit and register commercial land use policies for a particular project or development. This includes gathering information about the applicant, the proposed use and size of the property, and the required supporting documents and certifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information as the applicant.
	* Enter your name and title in the "Applicant Name" and "Applicant Title" fields.
	* Enter your contact email address in the "Email Address" field.
	* Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
2. Enter property information.
	* Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field.
	* Enter the parcel number in the "Parcel Number" field.
	* Enter the size of the property in the "Property Size" field (in acres or square feet).
3. Proposed Use and Size.
	* Select the type of commercial use from the "Proposed Use Type" dropdown menu (e.g., Retail, Office, Industrial).
	* Enter a detailed description of the proposed use in the "Proposed Use Description" field.
	* Enter the building square footage in the "Building Square Footage" field.
	* Enter the percentage of the lot to be covered in the "Lot Coverage Percentage" field.
	* Enter the number of parking spaces in the "Number of Parking Spaces" field.
4. Compliance and Certifications.
	* Confirm whether the proposal complies with zoning regulations in the "Zoning Compliance" dropdown menu.
	* Confirm whether an environmental review is required in the "Environmental Review Required" dropdown menu.
	* Confirm whether a traffic impact study is required in the "Traffic Impact Study" dropdown menu.
5. Supporting Documents.
	* Confirm whether a site plan has been submitted in the "Site Plan Submitted" dropdown menu.
	* Confirm whether architectural drawings are included in the "Architectural Drawings" dropdown menu.
	* Confirm whether an environmental assessment is included in the "Environmental Assessment" dropdown menu.
	* Confirm whether a traffic study has been completed in the "Traffic Study" dropdown menu.
6. Timeline.
	* Enter the proposed start date of the project in the "Proposed Start Date" field.
	* Enter the proposed completion date of the project in the "Proposed Completion Date" field.
7. Fees.
	* Confirm that the application fee has been paid in the "Application Fee Paid" field (a payment receipt number is optional but recommended).
8. Certifications.
	* Confirm that the information provided is accurate in the "Information Accuracy" dropdown menu.
	* Enter an authorized signature in the "Authorization Signature" field.
	* Enter the date of the authorized signature in the "Signature Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Applicant Title** (`applicant_title`, text, required): Enter your title or position in the organization.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company or organization.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the complete address of the property.
* **Parcel Number** (`parcel_number`, text, required): Enter the tax parcel ID of the property.
* **Property Size** (`property_size`, number, required): Enter the size of the property in acres or square feet.
* **Current Zoning** (`current_zoning`, text, required): Enter the current zoning designation.
* **Proposed Use Type** (`proposed_use_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of commercial use (e.g., Retail, Office, Industrial).
* **Proposed Use Description** (`proposed_use_description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the proposed use.
* **Building Square Footage** (`building_square_footage`, number, required): Enter the proposed building size.
* **Lot Coverage Percentage** (`lot_coverage`, number, required): Enter the percentage of the lot to be covered.
* **Number of Parking Spaces** (`parking_spaces`, number, required): Enter the number of proposed parking spaces.
* **Zoning Compliance** (`zoning_compliance`, select_one, required): Confirm whether the proposal complies with zoning regulations.
* **Environmental Review Required** (`environmental_review`, select_one, required): Confirm whether an environmental review is required.
* **Traffic Impact Study** (`traffic_impact`, select_one, required): Confirm whether a traffic impact study is required.
* **Site Plan Submitted** (`site_plan_submitted`, select_one, required): Confirm whether a site plan has been submitted.
* **Architectural Drawings** (`architectural_drawings`, select_one, required): Confirm whether architectural drawings are included.
* **Environmental Assessment** (`environmental_assessment`, select_one, required): Confirm whether an environmental assessment is included.
* **Traffic Study** (`traffic_study`, select_one, required): Confirm whether a traffic study has been completed.
* **Proposed Start Date** (`proposed_start_date`, date, required): Enter the proposed start date of the project.
* **Proposed Completion Date** (`proposed_completion_date`, date, required): Enter the proposed completion date of the project.
* **Application Fee Paid** (`application_fee`, number, required): Confirm that the application fee has been paid.
* **Payment Receipt Number** (`fee_receipt_number`, text, optional): Enter the payment receipt number (optional but recommended).
* **Information Accuracy** (`accuracy_certification`, select_one, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Authorization Signature** (`authorization_signature`, text, required): Enter an authorized signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the authorized signature.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other information or comments.

## Tips

* Make sure to carefully review and complete this form accurately and thoroughly.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Check that all supporting documents and certifications are included.
* Review the form before submitting to avoid errors and omissions.
