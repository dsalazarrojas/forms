# Safety Orientation Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from trainees after attending a safety orientation training session. Your input will help us evaluate the effectiveness of the training and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please select the correct pages to submit feedback on:
	+ Introduction (Page 1)
	+ Training Program (Page 2)
	+ Instructor (Page 3)
	+ Feedback (Page 4)
	+ Date (Page 5)
	+ Time (Page 6)
	+ Comments (Page 7)
	+ Training Hours (Page 8)
	+ Evaluation (Page 9)
	+ Overall Satisfaction (Page 10)
	+ Recommendations (Page 11)
	+ Date Submitted (Page 12)
	+ Time Submitted (Page 13)
	+ Submitter Name (Page 14)
	+ Submitter E-mail (Page 15)
- Fill out each page carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
- Use the options provided for select-type fields to select the most accurate answer.
- If you have no feedback or comments for a particular page, you can simply leave the field blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: Briefly introduce yourself and mention if you have any prior experience with the company or the subject matter. This is your chance to give us an idea of what you know or are familiar with before the training started.
* **Training Program**: Select 'Yes' if you found the training program engaging and well-structured. If not, select 'No'.
* **Instructor**: Select 'Yes' if your instructor was knowledgeable and effective in teaching the content. If not, select 'No'.
* **Feedback**: Share any thoughts or suggestions about the training session. Be honest and specific about what you liked and disliked.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the training session you attended.
* **Time**: Enter the start and end time of the training session you attended.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or observations about the training session.
* **Training Hours**: Enter the number of hours you spent in the training session.
* **Evaluation**: Select 'Yes' if you think the training met your expectations. If not, select 'No'.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Select 'Satisfied' if you are satisfied with the training session. If not, select 'Dissatisfied'.
* **Recommendations**: Select 'Yes' if you would recommend this training to others. If not, select 'No'.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date on which you are submitting this form.
* **Time Submitted**: Enter the time at which you are submitting this form.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as you want it to be displayed in our records.
* **Submitter E-mail**: Enter your email address to enable us to contact you if needed.
