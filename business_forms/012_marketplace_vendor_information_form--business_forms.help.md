# Marketplace Vendor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketplace Vendor Information Form is a tool designed to help vendors share their business details with potential customers. Completing this form will allow you to provide essential information about your products, services, and business operations, which will help us better promote your brand and connect you with potential buyers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with your business details.
2. Select all applicable product categories that represent your business.
3. Add a list of products you offer for sale.
4. Specify your store hours of operation.
5. Provide a description of your store.
6. Optionally, upload a store image to add visual context.
7. Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor details (1)**: Provide a brief introduction to your business, including a short description and any other relevant details.
* **Name (2)**: Enter your business name as you want it to be displayed on the marketplace.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your business email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your business phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **Address (5)**: Enter your business address for delivery and logistics purposes.
* **City (6)**: Enter the city where your business is located.
* **Country (7)**: Enter the country where your business is located.
* **Zip code (8)**: Enter the zip code of your business location.
* **Product categories (9)**: Select all applicable categories that represent your business offerings (e.g., Electronics, Fashion, Furniture, etc.).
* **Products (10)**: List all the products you offer for sale, separated by commas or paragraphs.
* **Store hours (11)**: Specify your business hours of operation, including any exceptions or variations.
* **Store description (12)**: Provide a detailed description of your store and what you offer.
* **Store image (13)**: Upload an image that represents your business, such as a logo or a photo of your storefront.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure that we can effectively promote your business.
* Use clear and descriptive language when filling out the form to help potential customers understand your offerings.
* Ensure that your store image is clear and high-quality.
