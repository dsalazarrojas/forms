# Criminal Record Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for conducting a background check on an employee. The form ensures that both the employer and employee understand the terms of the background check, including the type, frequency, and scope of the check.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2.  Sign the consent sections to confirm your agreement to the background check.
3.  Complete all required fields, which are marked as `required`.
4.  Enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth background check process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent to Conduct Background Check** (`consent_to_conduct_background_check`, text, optional): 
  This section is to obtain your consent to conduct a background check. If you agree, sign here to confirm.
* **Background Check Type** (`background_check_type`, select_one, optional): 
  Select the type of background check you want to conduct, which can be "type a", "type b", or a combination of both.
* **Consent to Share Results** (`consent_to_share_results`, text, optional): 
  This is to obtain your consent to share the background check results with the employer. Sign here to confirm.
* **Employer Identifier** (`employer_employer_identifier`, text, optional): 
  Enter the company's identifier or a unique code assigned by the HR department to identify the employer.
* **Business Entity** (`business_entity`, text, optional): 
  Enter the name of your business or the name of your company.
* **Employee Identifier** (`employee_identifier`, text, optional): 
  Enter your unique employee ID or the ID assigned by the HR department.
* **Background Check Frequency** (`background_check_frequency`, select_one, optional): 
  Select the frequency of the background check, which can be "once", "periodically", "as needed", or "unknown".
* **Criminal Record Information** (`criminal_record_information`, text, optional): 
  If you have any information related to your criminal record, please enter it here.
* **Consent to Disclose Background Check** (`consent_to_disclose_background_check`, text, optional): 
  This is to obtain your consent to disclose your background check information to the employer.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, optional): 
  Enter the address of your employer's office or location.
* **Employer Phone** (`employer_phone`, text, optional): 
  Enter the phone number of your employer's office or location.
* **Employer Email** (`employer_email`, text, optional): 
  Enter the email address of your employer's office or location.
* **Employer Contact Name** (`employer_contact_name`, text, optional): 
  Enter the name of the person handling the background check process.
* **Employer Title** (`employer_title`, text, optional): 
  Enter the title of the person handling the background check process.

Remember, it is essential to fill out this form carefully and accurately to maintain a smooth background check process.
