# Quality Nonconformance Summary Report - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Quality Nonconformance Summary Report is a tool used to gather and document information about quality incidents that occur within our organization. This report helps to ensure that incidents are properly recorded, reviewed, and analyzed to prevent future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by filling out the Incident Details section with relevant information about the incident.
- Choose the severity level of the incident by selecting one of the options provided in the Incident Severity field.
- Categorize the incident by selecting one or more of the options provided in the Incident Categorization field.
- Provide a detailed description of the incident in the Description field.
- Record any corrective actions taken in the Corrective Action field.
- Enter the date the incident occurred in the Incident Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details**: 
  - **Incident Details (text)** (required: false): This field is used to collect any additional information related to the incident that is not covered in other fields.
* **Incident Severity**: 
  - **Incident Severity (select_one)** (required: false): This field is used to determine the level of severity of the incident. Please select one of the following options: Low, Medium, or High.
* **Incident Categorization**: 
  - **Incident Categorization (select_multiple)** (required: false): This field is used to categorize the incident. Please select one or more of the following options: Defect, Non-Defect, or Other.
* **Description (text)** (required: false): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details or circumstances surrounding the event.
* **Corrective Action (text)** (required: false): Record any corrective actions taken to address the incident, including any actions that were taken or will be taken.
* **Incident Date (date)** (required: false): Enter the date the incident occurred.

## Tips

- Please ensure that you have a clear understanding of the incident details, severity, categorization, description, corrective action, and date of occurrence.
- If you are unsure about the severity or categorization of the incident, please consult with your supervisor or quality team for guidance.
- This form is a summary report and should not be used for detailed incident investigation or root cause analysis.
- Please use clear and concise language when filling out the form to ensure that the information is easy to read and understand.
