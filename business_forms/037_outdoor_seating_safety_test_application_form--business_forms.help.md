# outdoor_seating_safety_test_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an outdoor seating safety test application from restaurants. It is intended for authorized personnel from restaurants that need to conduct a safety test for their outdoor seating areas.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields with accurate information.
2. Ensure the information provided is relevant and up-to-date.
3. Check for any duplicate submissions before sending the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Name** (`Restaurant Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the restaurant that is submitting the outdoor seating safety test application.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the restaurant that is submitting the outdoor seating safety test application.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the restaurant.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person for the restaurant.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person for the restaurant.
* **Facility Manager Name** (`Facility Manager Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the facility manager for the outdoor seating area.
* **Facility Manager Phone** (`Facility Manager Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the facility manager for the outdoor seating area.
* **Facility Manager Email** (`Facility Manager Email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the facility manager for the outdoor seating area.
* **Seating Count** (`Seating Count`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of seating in the outdoor seating area.
* **Inspection Date** (`Inspection Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the intended inspection for the outdoor seating safety test.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the outdoor seating safety test application.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information provided before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the relevant authorities or support team.
