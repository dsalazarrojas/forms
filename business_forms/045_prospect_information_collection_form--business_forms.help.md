# Prospect Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prospect Information Collection Form is designed to gather essential details about potential customers or clients for business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information about the prospect.
2. Make sure to enter the prospect's email address in the correct format (e.g., [name]@domain.com).
3. Select the correct options for the prospect's Job Type (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, or Contract).
4. Choose the correct Lead Source (e.g., Referral, Website, or Advertisement).
5. Enter the follow-up and last follow-up dates in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the prospect's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the prospect's last name.
* **Email**: Enter the prospect's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the prospect's phone number.
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the prospect's company or business.
* **Position**: Enter the prospect's position or role in their company.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the prospect's company.
* **Job Function**: Select the type of job the prospect is looking for.
* **Industry**: Select the industry the prospect works in (e.g., Software Development, Finance, Marketing, or Sales).
* **Job Type**: Select all the types of jobs the prospect is looking for (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, or Contract).
* **Lead Source**: Select the source where the prospect was referred or found (e.g., Referral, Website, or Advertisement).
* **Follow-up**: Enter the date of the next follow-up with the prospect.
* **Last Follow-up**: Enter the date of the last follow-up with the prospect.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or details about the prospect (optional).
