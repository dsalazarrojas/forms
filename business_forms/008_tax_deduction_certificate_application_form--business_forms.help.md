# tax_deduction_certificate_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Deduction Certificate Application Form is a document that helps employees request and track tax deductions related to their work. This form is used to apply for tax deductions, provide details about the requestee, and attach supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the "Requestee details" field with the relevant information about the person or organization you are requesting the tax deduction for.
3. Attach any supporting documents that are relevant to your request.
4. Enter the date of the request in the "Date of request" field.
5. If applicable, enter the amount of deduction you are requesting in the "Amount of deduction" field.
6. Provide a brief reason for the deduction in the "Reason for deduction" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax Deduction Certificate Request (text, optional)**: This field is a brief summary of your tax deduction request. Please enter a concise description of the purpose of your request.
* **Requestee Details (note, optional)**: This field is a free-text area where you can provide any additional details about the person or organization you are requesting the tax deduction for.
* **Supporting Documents (text, optional)**: Attach any relevant documents that support your tax deduction request. This can include receipts, invoices, or other supporting evidence.
* **Date of Request (date, optional)**: Enter the date the tax deduction request was made.
* **Amount of Deduction (number, optional)**: If the tax deduction is a monetary value, enter the amount here.
* **Reason for Deduction (text, optional)**: Briefly explain the reason why you are requesting the tax deduction.
