# rental_contract_delivery_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The rental contract delivery form is designed to collect essential information from tenants for the successful delivery of rental contracts. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are gathered and processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are understood.
2. Enter the Tenant's name in the "Tenant Name" field.
3. Provide the Tenant's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the Tenant's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the relevant rental terms from the list provided in the "Rental Terms" field.
6. Choose the contract terms from the list provided in the "Contract Terms" field.
7. Enter the move-in date in the "Move-in Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tenant Name (id: 2)**: Enter the name of the person or entity renting the property.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Email (id: 3)**: Provide the email address of the tenant to ensure communication is effective.
  - Type: email
  - Required: false
- **Phone (id: 4)**: Enter the phone number of the tenant for quick contact.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Rental Terms (id: 5)**: Select one or more of the provided options to define the rental terms:
  - Options: Landlord, Tenant, Other
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Required: false
- **Contract Terms (id: 6)**: Choose the contract term(s) relevant to the rental:
  - Options: '30', '60', '90'
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Required: false
- **Move-in Date (id: 7)**: Enter the date the tenant is expected to move in.
  - Type: date
  - Required: false

## Tips
- Double-check all fields to ensure all necessary information is provided.
- If any fields are not applicable, please mark them as "Not Applicable" or provide a clear explanation.
