Local Business Outreach Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about local businesses for outreach and marketing purposes. To be eligible to use this form, the business should not already be listed in our database, and it's recommended that the business is not already a client of our organization. This form is intended for genuine business owners who are looking for our services or have questions about their business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all the required fields marked as "required" in the form. 
2. Select the correct category for your business from the dropdown list.
3. You can add any additional information or attachments that you feel are relevant to your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Details** (`business_details`, text, required: false): This field is not required but provides a space for you to add any additional information about your business.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): The name of your business as it appears on your signage or business card.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, required: false): This is the email address associated with your business.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, required: false): The phone number of your business.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required: false): The physical location of your business.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, text, required: false): A brief description of your business.
* **Business Category** (`business_category`, select_one, required: true): Select the category that best represents your business (e.g., Food and Beverage, Retail, Services, etc.).
* **Business Tags** (`business_tags`, text, required: false): Additional keywords or tags that describe your business (e.g., organic, eco-friendly, local-only, etc.).
* **Business Files** (`business_files`, text, required: false): Attach any relevant files or documents about your business (e.g., business license, menu, etc.).
