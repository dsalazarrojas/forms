# Mutual Fund Portfolio Addition - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to add a new mutual fund portfolio for a client. It will help investment firms to manage their client's portfolios efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct investment firm for the client's portfolio.
2. Choose the appropriate advisor for the client's portfolio.
3. Provide the client's name and contact number (if applicable).
4. Fill out the remaining fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Investment Firm** (`investment_firm`, select_one, required): Select the investment firm that will be managing the client's portfolio. This field is required.
* **Advisor** (`advisor`, select_one, required): Choose the advisor who will be working with the client. This field is required.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the client's contact number (if applicable).
* **Allocation** (`allocation`, select_multiple, optional): Select the allocation for the client's portfolio.
* **Objectives** (`objectives`, text, optional): Enter the objectives for the client's portfolio.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, number, optional): Enter the risk tolerance for the client's portfolio.
