# seint_beauty_color_match_with_tiffany - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information about a user's personal preferences for beauty products, including their favorite shampoo, conditioner, and other product details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address.
2. Select whether you like Tiffany shampoos or not.
3. Choose your preferred product types, such as conditioners, beauty products, and colors.
4. Select the product details that match your preferences.
5. Confirm whether you want color-matched beauty products or not.
6. Optionally, provide any additional comments or information in the user note field.
7. Enter your phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used to communicate with you regarding your preferences.
* **Select Tiffany Shampoos**: Check the box if you like Tiffany shampoos.
* **Select Conditioners**: Select any conditioners that match your preferences.
* **Select Beauty Products**: Choose the beauty products that suit your needs.
* **Select Product Details**: Choose the product details that match your preferences.
* **Select Color Match**: Confirm whether you want color-matched beauty products or not.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (if applicable).
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth (if applicable).
* **User Note**: Provide any additional comments or information.
* **User Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Select Tiffany Shampoo Type**: Choose the type of Tiffany shampoo you prefer.
* **Select Tiffany Product Size**: Choose the size of the Tiffany product you prefer.
* **Select Conditioner Type**: Choose the type of conditioner you prefer.
* **Select Beauty Product Type**: Choose the type of beauty product you prefer.
* **Select Beauty Product Color**: Choose the color of the beauty product you prefer.
* **Select Beauty Product Texture**: Choose the texture of the beauty product you prefer.
* **Select Beauty Product Smell**: Choose the smell of the beauty product you prefer.
* **Select Beauty Product Brand**: Choose the brand of the beauty product you prefer.
* **Select Beauty Product Category**: Choose the category of the beauty product you prefer.
* **Select Beauty Product Gender**: Choose the gender of the beauty product you prefer.
* **Select Beauty Product Age Group**: Choose the age group of the beauty product you prefer.
* **Select Beauty Product Price**: Choose the price range of the beauty product you prefer.
* **Select Beauty Product Weight**: Choose the weight of the beauty product you prefer.
* **Select Beauty Product Packaging**: Choose the packaging of the beauty product you prefer.
* **Select Beauty Product Country of Origin**: Choose the country of origin of the beauty product you prefer.
