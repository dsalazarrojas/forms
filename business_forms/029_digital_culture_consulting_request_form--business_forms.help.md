# Digital Culture Consulting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Culture Consulting Request Form is designed for clients to submit requests for consulting services related to digital culture. This form helps us understand your needs and requirements for digital culture consulting, making it easier for us to provide you with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (Company Name, Contact Number, Email, Contact Name, Service Request, and Message).
2. Provide any additional information in the Project Details field if necessary.
3. If you have any supporting files to attach (up to 15 files), please upload them.
4. Select a date for your service request if you'd like to schedule it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, Text, Required): This is the name of your company. Please fill in your company's official name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Number, Optional): This is your contact number. If you have one, please provide it so we can contact you easily.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): This is your company's official email address. We use it to communicate with you regarding your request.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text, Required): This is the name of the person submitting this request. Please fill in your name as it should appear on our records.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, Text, Optional): If you have a preferred consultant, please let us know their name so we can assign them to your project.
* **Service Request** (`service_request`, Text, Required): Describe the digital culture service you need. Please be as specific as possible with your requirements and expectations.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, Text, Optional): If you have any additional information about your project, please provide it here.
* **File 1**-**File 15** (`file1`, `file2`, etc., Text, Optional): You can upload up to 15 files supporting your request. Please make sure they are relevant to your service request.
* **Request Date** (`service_request_date`, Date, Optional): If you have a specific date in mind for your service request, please let us know.
