# Direct Sales Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Direct Sales Lead Form is used to collect information from customers or potential customers for sales purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure we have the necessary contact information.
2. Make sure you've checked the "I have permission to fill out this form" checkbox.
3. Submit the form once you've filled it out completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the contact's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the contact's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact's email address. Please use the correct format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the contact's company name (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the contact's job title (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact's phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter the contact's company name (if applicable).
* **Address 1** (`address_1`, text, required: false): Enter the contact's address (line 1).
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, required: false): Enter the contact's address (line 2).
 
## Tips
* Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're having trouble filling out the form, contact your administrator.
