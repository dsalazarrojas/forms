# Pool Access Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track complaints related to pool access issues. It helps to standardize and centralize the complaint tracking process, ensuring that your issue is properly documented and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field, which is a required field.
2. If you have a contact number that we can use for follow-up, please enter it in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Describe the issue you are experiencing with the "Complaint Description" field.
4. Indicate the current status of your resolution with the "Resolution Status" field. This is a required field and options include "Completed", "In Progress", and "Not Yet Started".
5. If your issue has been resolved, provide the resolution date and time with the "Resolution Date" and "Resolution Time" fields.
6. If a follow-up is required, enter your contact number and provide a follow-up note with the "Follow-up Contact" and "Follow-up Note" fields.
7. Finally, sign and date the form with your manager's signature and name, and enter the current date and time in the "Manager Signature", "Manager Name", and "Manager Date" and "Manager Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pool Access Complaint** (`pool_access_complaint_form`, text, required): This field is a title for the form and is not a field to be filled out. Please ignore it.
* **Name** (`name`, text, true): This is a required field where you can enter your name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, false): If you have a contact number that we can use for follow-up, enter it here.
* **Issue** (`issue`, text, true): Describe the issue you are experiencing with the pool access.
* **Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`, text, true): Provide a detailed description of the issue.
* **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, select_one, true): Indicate the current status of your resolution. Options include "Completed", "In Progress", and "Not Yet Started".
* **Resolution Date** (`resolution_date`, date, false): If your issue has been resolved, enter the date of the resolution.
* **Resolution Time** (`resolution_time`, time, false): If your issue has been resolved, enter the time of the resolution.
* **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, text, true): If a follow-up is required, enter your contact number.
* **Follow-up Note** (`follow_up_note`, text, false): Provide a follow-up note if a follow-up is required.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, false): Sign your manager's signature here.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, false): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Date** (`manager_date`, date, false): Enter the date of your manager's signature.
* **Manager Time** (`manager_time`, time, false): Enter the time of your manager's signature.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your complaint is properly documented and addressed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
