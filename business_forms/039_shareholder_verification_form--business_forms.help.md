# Shareholder Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the identity of a shareholder. Please fill out the form with your personal details to ensure accurate verification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the text field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the text field labeled "Last Name" (this field is optional).
3. Enter your SSN in the text field labeled "SSN".
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the date format (mm/dd/yyyy) in the field labeled "Date of Birth".
5. Enter your Email address in the text field labeled "Email".
6. Review your information for accuracy and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents. (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended for accurate verification.)
* **SSN** (`ssn`, text, required): Enter your Social Security Number as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
