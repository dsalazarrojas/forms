# Historical Position Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store information about an employee's past work experience, including position, responsibilities, performance, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the historical position in the "Historical Position" field.
2. List the responsibilities of the position in the "Responsibilities" field.
3. Select the job dates in the "Job Dates" field.
4. Evaluate the performance of the employee in the "Performance Raters", "Performance Rating", and "Overall Performance" fields.
5. Add any additional comments about the historical position in the "Historical Position Comments" field.
6. Select the job title, type, and classification of the position in the "Job Title", "Job Type", and "Job Classification" fields.
7. Choose the job status as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Job Status" field.
8. Select any roles or responsibilities that apply to the position in the "Role" field.
9. Confirm that the position's historical data is accurate and up-to-date in the "Historical Position Last Updated" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Historical Position** (`historical_position`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the historical position.
* **Responsibilities** (`responsibilities`, `text`, required): List the key responsibilities of the position.
* **Job Dates** (`job_dates`, `date`, required): Enter the start and end dates of the job.
* **Performance Raters** (`performance_raters`, `select_one`, required): Select how well the employee performed in the position.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, `select_one`, required): Select the overall performance of the employee.
* **Overall Performance** (`overall_performance`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the overall performance of the employee.
* **Historical Position Comments** (`historical_position_comments`, `text`, required): Add any additional comments about the historical position.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `text`, required): Select the user who filled out this form.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter the job title of the position.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `text`, required): Enter the job type of the position.
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, `text`, required): Enter the job classification of the position.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the job status as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Role** (`role`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any roles or responsibilities that apply to the position.
* **Responsibilities** (`responsibilities`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any responsibilities that apply to the position.
* **Historical Position Comments** (`historical_position_comments`, `text`, required): Add any additional comments about the historical position.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required): Select the user who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, `date`, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `text`, required): Select the user who updated the form.
* **Updated On** (`updated_on`, `date`, required): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Historical Position Form ID** (`historical_position_form_id`, `text`, required): Enter a unique ID for the historical position form.
* **Historical Position Form Version** (`historical_position_form_version`, `text`, required): Enter the version number of the historical position form.
* **Job History ID** (`job_history_id`, `text`, required): Enter the ID of the job history.
* **Historical Position Last Updated** (`historical_position_last_updated`, `date`, required): Enter the date the historical position was last updated.
* **Historical Position Last Updated By** (`historical_position_last_updated_by`, `text`, required): Enter the user who last updated the historical position.
* **Historical Position Last Updated On** (`historical_position_last_updated_on`, `date`, required): Enter the date the historical position was last updated.

Note: Fields marked as required should only be filled out if they are relevant to your specific job. If not applicable, please leave blank.
