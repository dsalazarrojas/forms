# R&D Consultant Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from business owners who are looking to refer their R&D project to an external consultant. The data collected will be used to assess the consultant's suitability for the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your business name and address in the relevant fields.
* Select the consultant you would like to refer your project to.
* Describe the R&D project in the project description field.
* Select the start and end dates of the project.
* Confirm the consultant's skills and availability.
* Enter their contact information, including their email address and phone number.
* Provide notes on their rate and fees.
* Confirm your agreement with the consultant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consultant**: Please select the external consultant you would like to refer your project to.
	+ This field should be filled with the name of the consultant or consultant company you would like to work with.
	+ Make sure to select the correct option that represents the consultant.
* **Business Owner (Text, optional)**: If you are the business owner or authorized representative, please enter your name.
* **Business Address (Text, optional)**: Enter your business address.
* **Business City (Text, optional)**: Enter the city of your business location.
* **Business State (Select One, optional)**: Select 'Yes' if you are located in the state.
* **Business Zip (Number, optional)**: Enter your business zip code.
* **Consultant Email (Email, optional)**: Enter the consultant's email address.
* **Project Description (Text, optional)**: Describe the R&D project you would like to refer to the consultant.
* **Project Start Date (Date, optional)**: Enter the start date of the project.
* **Project End Date (Date, optional)**: Enter the end date of the project.
* **Consultant Skills (Select Multiple, optional)**: Confirm the consultant's skills and expertise.
	+ Select all relevant options that apply.
* **Consultant Availability (Text, optional)**: Provide notes on the consultant's availability.
* **Consultant Rate (Text, optional)**: Provide notes on the consultant's rate and fees.
* **Consultant Hours (Text, optional)**: Provide notes on the consultant's working hours.
* **Consultant Rate Note (Text, optional)**: Provide additional notes on the consultant's rate.
* **Consultant Fee (Number, optional)**: Enter the consultant's fee.
* **Consultant Fee Note (Text, optional)**: Provide additional notes on the consultant's fee.
* **Consultant Agreement (Select One, optional)**: Confirm your agreement with the consultant.
* **Consultant Agreement Note (Text, optional)**: Provide additional notes on your agreement with the consultant.
* **Form Submission (Text, optional)**: Enter any additional information or comments about the form submission.
* **Business Name (Text, optional)**: Enter your business name.
* **Business Address Two (Text, optional)**: Enter an additional address line for your business.
* **Business State (Select One, optional)**: Select 'Yes' if you are located in the state.
* **Form Submission (Text, optional)**: Enter any additional information or comments about the form submission.
