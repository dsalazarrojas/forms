# Customer Success Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers applying for a specific service or product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Onboarding Date.
2. Select your role from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose your location from the dropdown menu.
4. Select the tools and category that best fit your needs.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.
6. Choose the tags that apply to your application.
7. Select the person who created this form (this is likely a default value).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Role**: Select your role from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand your specific needs.
* **Location**: Choose your location from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand your region or industry.
* **Company**: Enter your company name or information.
* **Onboarding Date**: Enter the date you would like to start the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Time**: Enter the time you would like to schedule the onboarding process.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool that you would like to use for the onboarding process.
* **Category**: Select the category that best fits your needs.
* **Description**: Add a brief description of your application.
* **Tags**: Choose the tags that apply to your application.
* **Created By**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be changed. It shows the person who created this form.</description>

Note: The required fields are First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Onboarding Date. If any of these fields are empty, you will not be able to submit the form.
