# Art Work Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for art work requests from internal stakeholders. It is meant to be completed by the requester to provide essential details about their desired project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name and email address in the "Your Full Name" and "Email Address" fields, respectively.
2. Optionally, include your company or organization name if the request is related to your work.
3. Select the type of art work you are requesting (e.g., Illustration, Mural, etc.) from the list.
4. Provide a detailed description of your project, including the subject matter and desired style.
5. If applicable, specify the target dimensions and material or software preferences for the project.
6. Estimate the maximum budget for the project in USD.
7. If desired, specify the deadline for the project.
8. Select the intended usage rights for the completed artwork.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Company or Organization** (Text, Optional): If the request is related to your work, provide your company or organization name. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Type of Artwork Requested** (Select One, Required): Select the type of art work you are requesting (e.g., Illustration, Mural, etc.).
* **Detailed Project Description** (Text, Required): Provide a detailed description of your project, including the subject matter and desired style.
* **Target Dimensions** (Text, Optional): If applicable, specify the target dimensions for the project (e.g., 5x7 feet, 1920x1080 pixels).
* **Material or Software Preferences** (Text, Optional): If applicable, specify the material or software preferences for the project (e.g., Oil, Watercolor, Photoshop).
* **Expected Budget Range** (Number, Required): Estimate the maximum budget for the project in USD.
* **Desired Deadline** (Date, Optional): If desired, specify the deadline for the project.
* **Intended Usage Rights** (Select One, Required): Select the intended usage rights for the completed artwork (e.g., Personal Use Only, Commercial Use, etc.).
