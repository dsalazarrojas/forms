# data_storage_billing_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The data_storage_billing_form is a form used for collecting data storage billing information for a business, specifically for a company or organization. It's crucial to fill out this form accurately to maintain reliable data and ensure the correct payment information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Fill out the "Billing Details" section (id: 1) with relevant billing information.
2.  Optionally, fill out the "Client Details" section (id: 2) for additional context.
3.  Select at least one payment method from the options provided in the "Payment Method" section (id: 3).
4.  Enter the amount of the service or product in the "Amount" field (id: 4).
5.  Enter the date of service in the "Date of Service" field (id: 5).
6.  Enter the time of service in the "Time of Service" field (id: 6).
7.  Enter the email address of the client or billing contact in the "Email" field (id: 7).
8.  Enter the phone number of the client or billing contact in the "Phone Number" field (id: 8).
9.  Select the current payment status in the "Confirm Payment Status" field (id: 9).
10. Select the subscription status in the "Subscription Status" field (id: 10).
11. Confirm the phone number entered in the "Confirm Phone Number" field (id: 11).
12. Confirm the email address entered in the "Confirm Email" field (id: 12).
13. Confirm the date of service entered in the "Confirm Date of Service" field (id: 13).
14. Confirm the time of service entered in the "Confirm Time of Service" field (id: 14).
15. Confirm the payment method selected in the "Confirm Payment Method" field (id: 15).
16. Confirm the subscription status selected in the "Confirm Subscription Status" field (id: 16).
17. Confirm the amount entered in the "Confirm Amount" field (id: 17).
18. Confirm any additional note entered in the "Confirm Note" field (id: 18).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Billing Details (id: 1)
*   **Billing Details**: Required, Text. Enter your billing information, including name and address.

### Client Details (id: 2)
*   **Client Details**: Optional, Text. Enter additional details about the client.

### Payment Method (id: 3)
*   **Payment Method**: Required, Select Multiple. Select at least one payment method: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Diners, JCB.

### Amount (id: 4)
*   **Amount**: Required, Number. Enter the amount of the service or product.

### Date of Service (id: 5)
*   **Date of Service**: Required, Date. Enter the date of service.

### Time of Service (id: 6)
*   **Time of Service**: Required, Time. Enter the time of service.

### Email (id: 7)
*   **Email**: Required, Email. Enter your email address.

### Phone Number (id: 8)
*   **Phone Number**: Required, Text. Enter your phone number.

### Confirm Payment Status (id: 9)
*   **Confirm Payment Status**: Required, Select One. Select the current payment status: Active or Inactive.

### Subscription Status (id: 10)
*   **Subscription Status**: Required, Select One. Select the subscription status: Active or Inactive.

### Confirm Phone Number (id: 11)
*   **Confirm Phone Number**: Required, Select One. Confirm the phone number entered.

### Confirm Email (id: 12)
*   **Confirm Email**: Required, Select One. Confirm the email address entered.

### Confirm Date of Service (id: 13)
*   **Confirm Date of Service**: Required, Select One. Confirm the date of service entered.

### Confirm Time of Service (id: 14)
*   **Confirm Time of Service**: Required, Select One. Confirm the time of service entered.

### Confirm Payment Method (id: 15)
*   **Confirm Payment Method**: Required, Select One. Confirm the payment method selected.

### Confirm Subscription Status (id: 16)
*   **Confirm Subscription Status**: Required, Select One. Confirm the subscription status selected.

### Confirm Amount (id: 17)
*   **Confirm Amount**: Required, Select One. Confirm the amount entered.

### Confirm Note (id: 18)
*   **Confirm Note**: Required, Select One. Confirm any additional note entered.

## Tips
*   Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
*   Make sure to confirm all selections and checks to avoid errors.
*   Review the form thoroughly to avoid missing any required fields.
