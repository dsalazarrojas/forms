# Counter Barrier System Installation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counter Barrier System Installation Survey is designed to gather information about the site details, barrier requirements, and access notes necessary for the installation and maintenance of a counter barrier system. This form aims to collect data in a standardized and efficient way, reducing the risk of errors or omissions during the installation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please answer all the questions to the best of your ability and provide as much detail as possible.
* Make sure to enter your responses in the designated fields, following any formatting instructions provided.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the questions, do not hesitate to contact the specified contact person or organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Details**:
	+ **Site Details** is the first part of the form where you need to provide information about the site where the counter barrier system will be installed.
	+ Please enter a brief description of the site, including the location, address, or any other relevant details.
* **Barrier Requirements**:
	+ **Barrier Requirements** is the second part of the form where you need to specify the requirements for the counter barrier system.
	+ Please list any specific requirements or constraints related to the barrier, such as height, width, or material specifications.
* **Access Notes**:
	+ **Access Notes** is the third and final part of the form where you need to provide any additional notes about access to the site.
	+ Please use this field to note any access restrictions, safety concerns, or other relevant information that may impact the installation process. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid any confusion or miscommunication.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the specified contact person or organization for assistance.
* The information collected through this form will be used to ensure the successful installation and maintenance of the counter barrier system.
