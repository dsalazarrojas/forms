# Cash Injection Intensive Submit Results 2019 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect business results after participating in a cash injection intensive program. It will help us understand your financial performance and your experience with the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question carefully and accurately. Make sure to provide your honest feedback about your experience with the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission ID Number** (`submission_id`, text, optional): This is a reference number that identifies your submission. If you don't have one, you can leave it blank.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): The date you are submitting this form.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, note, optional): This is a text field for you to provide any additional information about yourself or your business.
* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): This is your legal name.
* **Company or Business Name** (`company_name`, text, required): This is the name of your business or organization.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): This is your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): This is your best contact phone number.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of business you have:
	+ Retail
	+ E-commerce
	+ Services
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Technology
	+ Healthcare
	+ Restaurant
	+ Professional services
	+ Other
* **Business Size** (`business_size`, select_one, required): Please select the size of your business:
	+ Under 100K
	+ 100K to 500K
	+ 500K to 1M
	+ 1M to 5M
	+ 5M to 10M
	+ Over 10M
* **Program Information** (`program_info`, note, optional): This is a text field for you to provide any additional information about the program.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, required): The date the program began.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, required): The date the program ended.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, select_one, required): Please select the length of the program:
	+ 1 week
	+ 2 weeks
	+ 30 days
	+ 60 days
	+ 90 days
	+ Other
* **Coach or Mentor Name** (`coach_name`, text, optional): If applicable, please provide the name of your coach or mentor.
* **Baseline Metrics** (`baseline_metrics`, note, optional): This is a text field for you to provide any baseline metrics for your business.
* **Starting Monthly Revenue** (`starting_monthly_revenue`, number, required): The revenue of your business before the program.
* **Starting Cash Balance** (`starting_cash_balance`, number, required): The cash balance of your business before the program.
* **Starting Profit Margin** (`starting_profit_margin`, number, optional): The profit margin of your business before the program.
* **Challenges** (`starting_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Please select the initial challenges your business faced:
	+ Low cash flow
	+ Inconsistent revenue
	+ High expenses
	+ Pricing issues
	+ Sales challenges
	+ Collection problems
	+ No financial systems
	+ Debt burden
	+ Other
* **Results Metrics** (`results_metrics`, note, optional): This is a text field for you to provide any results metrics about your business.
* **Ending Monthly Revenue** (`ending_monthly_revenue`, number, required): The revenue of your business after the program.
* **Revenue Increase Percentage** (`revenue_increase`, number, optional): The percentage increase in revenue.
* **Ending Cash Balance** (`ending_cash_balance`, number, required): The cash balance of your business after the program.
* **Cash Increase Amount** (`cash_increase`, number, optional): The net gain in cash.
* **Ending Profit Margin** (`ending_profit_margin`, number, optional): The profit margin of your business after the program.
* **Profit Improvement** (`profit_improvement`, number, optional): The margin change.
* **Key Wins Achieved** (`key_wins`, select_multiple, required): Please select the major accomplishments of your business:
	+ Increased revenue
	+ Improved cash flow
	+ Reduced expenses
	+ Better pricing
	+ Faster collections
	+ New systems implemented
	+ Debt reduced
	+ Profit margin improved
	+ Team trained
	+ Other
* **Strategies Implemented** (`strategies_implemented`, text, required): The actions taken by your business.
* **Biggest Breakthrough** (`biggest_breakthrough`, text, required): The major insight gained by your business.
* **Challenges Overcome** (`challenges_overcome`, text, optional): The obstacles faced by your business.
* **Specific Tactics** (`specific_tactics`, note, optional): This is a text field for you to provide any additional tactics used by your business.
* **Pricing Changes** (`pricing_changes`, select_one, optional): Please select if you changed your pricing strategy:
	+ Yes - increased prices
	+ Yes - restructured
	+ Yes - added tiers
	+ No - kept same
* **Pricing Change Impact** (`pricing_impact`, text, optional): The results of the pricing change.
* **Sales Improvements Made** (`sales_improvements`, text, optional): The revenue actions taken by your business.
* **Expense Reductions Achieved** (`expense_reductions`, text, optional): The cost savings achieved by your business.
* **Systems or Processes Implemented** (`systems_implemented`, text, optional): The new procedures implemented by your business.
* **Testimonial** (`testimonial`, note, optional): This is a text field for you to provide a testimonial about your experience with the program.
* **Overall Experience Rating** (`overall_experience`, select_one, required): Please select your overall experience with the program:
	+ Excellent - exceeded expectations
	+ Very Good - great results
	+ Good - met expectations
	+ Fair - some value
	+ Poor - below expectations
* **Would You Recommend This Program** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Please select if you would recommend this program to others:
	+ Yes - definitely
	+ Yes - probably
	+ Maybe
	+ Probably not
	+ Definitely not
* **What Did You Value Most** (`program_feedback`, text, optional): The best part of your experience with the program.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`program_suggestions`, text, optional): Any feedback for improving the program.
* **Future Goals** (`future_goals`, text, optional): The next steps for your business.
* **Do You Need Ongoing Support** (`ongoing_support`, select_one, optional): Do you need continued help:
	+ Yes - interested
	+ Maybe - considering
	+ No - all set
* **May We Share Your Success Story** (`success_story_sharing`, select_one, required): Please select if you are willing to let us share your story:
	+ Yes - full story
	+ Yes - anonymized
	+ Yes - metrics only
	+ No - private
* **Would You Participate in a Case Study** (`case_study_interest`, select_one, optional): Please select if you are interested in a case study:
	+ Yes - interested
	+ Maybe - more info needed
	+ No - not interested
* **Do You Know Others Who Could Benefit** (`referral_potential`, select_one, optional): Do you know others who could benefit from the program:
	+ Yes - several people
	+ Yes - a few people
	+ Maybe - one or two
	+ No - no one
* **Referral Names or Contacts** (`referral_names`, text, optional): If any.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Anything else you would like to add.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, date, required): The date signed.

This form is designed to collect information about your business's financial performance and your experience with the program. Please fill it out carefully and accurately.
