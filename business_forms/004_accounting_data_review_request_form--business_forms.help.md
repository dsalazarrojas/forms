# Accounting Data Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accounting Data Review Request Form is used to collect information from internal departments within an organization for the purpose of reviewing and analyzing accounting data. This form helps to streamline the review process, ensuring that all necessary information is gathered accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and department in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the urgency of the review request (Low, Medium, High, or Urgent).
3. Specify the start and end dates of the data range to be reviewed.
4. Select all the documents that need to be reviewed (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement, Expense Reports, or Bank Reconciliations).
5. Briefly describe the reason for the review request.
6. If necessary, specify any specific account numbers that require attention.
7. Choose your preferred delivery method for the report (Secure Portal, Encrypted Email, or In-person Meeting).
8. Select a completion date for the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your name, as you are the person submitting the request.
*   **Department** (`department`, text, required): Select your internal department that is requesting the review.
*   **Review Priority** (`review_priority`, select_one, required): Choose the urgency of the review request (Low, Medium, High, or Urgent).
*   **Review Period Start** (`review_period_start`, date, required): Enter the start date of the data range to be reviewed.
*   **Review Period End** (`review_period_end`, date, required): Enter the end date of the data range to be reviewed.
*   **Documents to Review** (`documents_to_review`, select_multiple, required): Select all the documents that need to be reviewed (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement, Expense Reports, or Bank Reconciliations).
*   **Reason for Review Request** (`reason_for_review_request`, text, required): Briefly describe why this review is needed.
*   **Specific Account Numbers** (`specific_account_numbers`, text, optional): If specific ledgers need attention, enter their numbers here.
*   **Preferred Delivery Method** (`preferred_delivery_method`, select_one, required): Choose how the report should be shared (Secure Portal, Encrypted Email, or In-person Meeting).
*   **Requested Completion Date** (`requested_completion_date`, date, required): Select a completion date for the review.

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the accounting department for assistance.
- Make sure to provide a valid reason for the review request to ensure it's processed efficiently.
