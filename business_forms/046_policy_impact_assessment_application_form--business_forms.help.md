# policy_impact_assessment_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Policy Impact Assessment Application Form is used to assess the impact of new policies on various aspects of the organization. This form helps to gather information on new policies, their objectives, impacts, and other relevant details from submitters.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the title of the new policy in the "New Policies" field.
2. Describe the objectives of the policy in the "Objectives" field.
3. Choose whether the policy will have an impact or not in the "Impacts" field.
4. List the stakeholders involved in the policy in the "Stakeholders" field.
5. Attach any relevant supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents" field.
6. Provide any review comments in the "Review Comments" field.
7. Specify the category of the policy in the "Policy Category" field.
8. Enter the name of the organization responsible for the policy in the "Organization" field.
9. Enter the name and email of the person submitting the policy in the "Contact Person" and "Contact Email" fields.
10. Enter the contact phone number of the submitter in the "Contact Phone" field.
11. Enter the address of the organization responsible for the policy in the "Address" field.
12. Choose the review date and time in the "Review Date" and "Review Time" fields.
13. Enter any review notes in the "Review Note" field.
14. Enter the name of the person submitting the policy in the "Submitter" field.
15. Enter the email of the person submitting the policy in the "Submitter Email" field.
16. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the policy in the "Submitter Phone" field.
17. Enter the name of the organization of the person submitting the policy in the "Submitter Organization" field.
18. Enter the address of the organization of the person submitting the policy in the "Submitter Address" field.
19. Specify whether the policy version is yes or no in the "Policy Version" field.
20. Enter the date of the policy version in the "Policy Version Date" field.
21. Enter the dedication of the person submitting the policy in the "Submitter Dedication" field.
22. Choose the review status of the policy in the "Review Status" field.
23. Enter any additional review comments in the "Review Comments 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Policies** (`1`, text, required): Enter the title of the new policy.
* **Objectives** (`2`, text, optional): Describe the objectives of the policy.
* **Impacts** (`3`, select_one, required): Choose whether the policy will have an impact or not.
* **Stakeholders** (`4`, select_multiple, optional): List the stakeholders involved in the policy.
* **Supporting Documents** (`5`, text, optional): Attach any relevant supporting documents.
* **Review Comments** (`6`, text, required): Provide any review comments.
* **Policy Category** (`7`, select_one, optional): Specify the category of the policy.
* **Organization** (`8`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization responsible for the policy.
* **Contact Person** (`9`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the policy.
* **Contact Email** (`10`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person submitting the policy.
* **Contact Phone** (`11`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the submitter.
* **Address** (`12`, text, optional): Enter the address of the organization responsible for the policy.
* **Review Date** (`13`, date, optional): Choose the review date.
* **Review Time** (`14`, time, optional): Choose the review time.
* **Review Note** (`15`, text, required): Enter any review notes.
* **Submitter** (`16`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the policy.
* **Submitter Email** (`17`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person submitting the policy.
* **Submitter Phone** (`18`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the policy.
* **Submitter Organization** (`19`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization of the person submitting the policy.
* **Submitter Address** (`20`, text, optional): Enter the address of the organization of the person submitting the policy.
* **Policy Version** (`21`, select_one, optional): Specify whether the policy version is yes or no.
* **Policy Version Date** (`22`, date, optional): Enter the date of the policy version.
* **Submitter Dedication** (`23`, text, optional): Enter the dedication of the person submitting the policy.
* **Review Status** (`24`, select_one, optional): Choose the review status of the policy.
* **Review Comments 2** (`25`, text, optional): Enter any additional review comments.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a complete submission.
* Use the "Review Comments" field for any additional comments or feedback.
* Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
