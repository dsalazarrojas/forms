# Hotel Reservation Form With Skrill - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to take reservations from customers and process their payment information. It collects essential details such as reservation dates, guest name, contact information, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the reservation date you wish to make.
2. Enter the date you will check-in.
3. Enter the date you will check-out.
4. Enter your name as the guest.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Select your preferred payment method from the options (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Skrill).
8. Enter the total amount to be paid.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about your reservation.
10. Confirm that you wish to make the reservation by selecting "Yes".
11. If you wish to assign a tool or note, enter the tool or note name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation date** (1, date): This field is used to enter the date of the reservation you wish to make. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Check-in date** (2, date): This field is used to enter the date you will check-in to the hotel. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Check-out date** (3, date): This field is used to enter the date you will check-out of the hotel. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Guest name** (4, text): This field is used to enter your name as the guest.
* **Phone number** (5, text): This field is used to enter your phone number.
* **Email** (6, email): This field is used to enter your email address.
* **Room number** (7, text): This field is used to enter the room number you wish to reserve.
* **Payment method** (8, select multiple): This field is used to select your preferred payment method. Choose from Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Skrill.
* **Amount** (9, number): This field is used to enter the total amount to be paid.
* **Notes** (10, note): This field is used to add any additional notes or comments about your reservation.
* **Confirm reservation** (11, select one): This field is used to confirm that you wish to make the reservation. Select "Yes" to confirm.
* **Assigned tool** (12, text): This field is used to assign a tool or note to your reservation (optional).
* **Output file** (13, text): This field is used to save the output file of your reservation details (optional).
