The purpose of the Kaizen Improvement Report Form is to collect suggestions and ideas for improving business processes, products, or services from employees, customers, or other stakeholders. This form is designed to encourage open-ended and specific suggestions for improvement, with multiple fields to capture various aspects of the improvement idea.

Before starting, please ensure that your suggestion is not a duplicate and has not been previously submitted. If you're unsure, you can check our internal knowledge base or consult with your team lead for more information.

## Kaizen Improvement Report Form - Help Guide
### Purpose
The Kaizen Improvement Report Form is designed to collect, document, and prioritize improvement suggestions from various stakeholders in the organization. This form helps in identifying areas that require improvement and facilitates their implementation in a structured way.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant workflow, product, or service that your improvement idea is related to.
2.  Choose the product or service you are improving.
3.  Select all the services you think will be impacted.
4.  Describe your improvement idea in a concise and clear manner, ensuring it is specific and actionable.
5.  Explain how your improvement idea will impact the organization (positive, negative, or neutral).
6.  Provide a date for the intended implementation of your suggestion.
7.  If relevant, select the person who will be responsible for implementing your improvement idea.
8.  Add any additional comments or information that will help in the implementation process.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Kaizen Improvement Report Form**: The form title.
*   **Select Workflow**: Choose the relevant workflow that your improvement idea is related to.
    +   Required for submission.
*   **Select Product/Service**: Select the product or service your improvement idea is related to.
    +   Required for submission.
*   **Select Service**: Select all services that will be impacted by your improvement idea.
    +   Optional, but recommended for a comprehensive improvement suggestion.
*   **Describe Your Suggestion**: Provide a clear and concise description of your improvement idea.
    +   Required for submission. Ensure it's specific and actionable.
*   **Suggestion**: Provide a brief summary of your improvement idea.
    +   Required for submission.
*   **How Will It Impact?**: Explain the potential positive, negative, or neutral impact of your improvement idea.
    +   Required for submission.
*   **Implementation Date**: Enter the date for the intended implementation of your improvement idea.
    +   Required for submission.
*   **Assigned To**: If relevant, select the person responsible for implementing your improvement idea.
    +   Optional.
*   **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information that will aid in the implementation process.
    +   Optional.

By following these instructions and explanations, you'll be able to submit a well-rounded improvement suggestion that will help the organization achieve its goals.

Please submit your improvement idea, and we'll work together to make it a reality!

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly before submission, as incomplete submissions might be rejected for review.
