> # new_starter_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_starter_request_form is designed to gather information from employees who are new to the organization. This form will help us understand their background, position, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organization name in the first field.
2. Enter your first name in the second field.
3. Enter your last name in the third field.
4. Provide your email address in the fourth field.
5. Enter your job title in the fifth field.
6. Enter your manager's name in the sixth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **organization** (Organization, text, optional): Please enter the name of your current organization.
* **first_name** (First Name, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **last_name** (Last Name, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **email** (Email, email, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **job_title** (Job Title, text, optional): Please enter your job title.
* **manager_name** (Manager's Name, text, optional): Please enter your manager's name.
