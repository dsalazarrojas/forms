# Guide For Using Online Forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
The guide is a tool to help users understand the process of creating and using online forms. It is designed to collect information about online forms, including their title, target audience, data collection methods, form submission, analytics, and integration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by reading the introduction**, which explains the purpose and objectives of the form.
2. The form is divided into sections, each with its own set of questions. Please answer each question according to your needs.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have provided accurate information.
4. Once you have completed the form, submit it and review the analytics and integration options available to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (1): Provide a brief and descriptive title for your online form.
* **Business Objectives** (2): List the main goals and objectives of using online forms for your business or organization.
* **Target Audience** (3): Choose the category that best describes the target audience for your online form:
	+ **Customer**: The form is designed for customers or users.
	+ **Employee**: The form is designed for employees or staff members.
	+ **Other**: Select this option if your target audience is not listed.
* **Form Design** (4): Describe the overall design and layout of your online form, including any customizations or features.
* **Data Collection** (5): Specify the methods used to collect data, such as manual entry, API integration, or other automated processes.
* **Form Submission** (6): Explain how you plan to handle form submissions, including any follow-up actions or notifications.
* **Form Analytics** (7): Describe the analytics and tracking options you plan to use to measure the form's effectiveness and performance.
* **Form Integration** (8): Explain how your online form will integrate with other systems or tools, such as CRM systems or databases.
* **Form Integration** (8) is currently not being used, please ignore this field for now.
