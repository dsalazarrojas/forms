# Process Intelligence Advisory Program Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Process Intelligence Advisory Program Application form is designed to gather information from business users who wish to participate in the program. This form helps to identify areas of improvement in your business processes and provides a platform for you to request guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please provide a clear and concise title for your application.
2.  Select the correct category that best describes your form (Business Forms or Consulting Forms).
3.  Describe the objectives you want to achieve through the program.
4.  Provide details about the category challenge you're facing in your process.
5.  Offer a description of the process and its current status.
6.  Indicate the level of support you require for the process.
7.  If applicable, provide additional notes to support your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Title** (`title`, `text`, required): A brief title that describes your application.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct category for your application from Business Forms or Consulting Forms.
* **Objectives** (`objective`, `text`, optional): Describe the objectives you want to achieve through the program.
* **Category Challenge** (`category_challenge`, `text`, optional): Provide details about the category challenge you're facing in your process.
* **Process Description** (`process_description`, `text`, optional): Offer a description of the process and its current status.
* **Process Details** (`process_details`, `text`, optional): Additional details about the process.
* **Support Level** (`support_level`, `number`, optional): Indicate the level of support you require (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **Objectives Challenge** (`objectives_challenge`, `text`, optional): Describe any challenges you're facing in achieving your objectives.
* **Process Intelligence Guidance** (`process_intelligence_guidance`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional guidance or information about your process.
* **Support Type** (`support_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of support you require (Advisory Services or Technical Services).
* **Support Level 2** (`support_level_2`, `number`, optional): Indicate any additional support level (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, `text`, optional): List any additional challenges you're facing.
* **Support Level 3** (`support_level_3`, `number`, optional): Indicate any additional support level (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your application.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): If applicable, specify any tools or resources assigned to support your process.
