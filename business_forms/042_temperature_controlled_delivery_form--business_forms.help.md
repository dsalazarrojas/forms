# Temperature Controlled Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide critical information for temperature-controlled delivery services. The form helps ensure that packages are handled, stored, and delivered at optimal temperatures to maintain product quality and safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the delivery type from the options provided. This will determine the specific requirements for the delivery process.
2. Enter the temperature range for the package, if applicable. This will help ensure that the package is stored and handled at the correct temperature.
3. Enter the storage time in hours for the package. This will determine how long the package should be stored before delivery.
4. Enter the package details, including any specific instructions or notes about the package.
5. Select the delivery date for the package. This is the date when you would like to receive the delivery.
6. Choose the delivery time for the package. Select the time of day when you would like to receive the delivery.
7. Add any relevant notes or comments about the delivery.
8. Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes. This will ensure that we can reach you with any updates or concerns about the delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Delivery Type** (`delivery_type`, select_one): Select the type of delivery you require.
  * This field determines the specific requirements for the delivery process.
- **Temperature Range** (`temperature_range`, number): Enter the temperature range for the package, if applicable.
  * This field ensures that the package is stored and handled at the correct temperature.
- **Storage Time** (`storage_time`, number): Enter the storage time in hours for the package.
  * This will determine how long the package should be stored before delivery.
- **Package Details** (`package_details`, text): Enter any specific instructions or notes about the package.
  * This field provides critical information about the package.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date): Select the date when you would like to receive the delivery.
  * This is the date when you would like to receive the delivery.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time): Choose the time of day when you would like to receive the delivery.
  * This field ensures that you receive the delivery at the desired time.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note): Add any relevant notes or comments about the delivery.
  * This field provides additional information about the delivery.
- **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
  * This will ensure that we can reach you with any updates or concerns about the delivery.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
  * This will ensure that we can reach you with any urgent matters related to the delivery.
