# Website Management Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Management Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with a website. This feedback will help us understand user perspectives and improve the website's functionality and usability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your email address in the "user_email" field to ensure we can contact you for follow-up questions.
2. Enter the name of the website you are providing feedback on in the "website_name" field.
3. Select the type of feedback you would like to provide by choosing one of the options in the "feedback_type" field.
4. Enter your message in the "feedback_message" field. This is your opportunity to share your thoughts in detail.
5. If applicable, select the tools you have used on the website from the "assigned_tool" field.
6. For websites with features, select "Yes" or "No" in the "website_features" field, depending on whether you found the features helpful or not.
7. Similarly, for websites with usability, select "Yes" or "No" in the "website_usability" field, depending on your user experience.
8. Finally, submit the form to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email** (user_email, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Website Name** (website_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the website you are providing feedback on.
* **Feedback Type** (feedback_type, select_one, required: false): Select the type of feedback you would like to provide (Performance, Usability, Features).
* **Feedback Message** (feedback_message, text, required: false): Enter your detailed feedback message.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool, select_multiple, required: false): Select the tools you have used on the website (chatjimmy, jira, trello).
* **Website Features** (website_features, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" for each feature to indicate whether you found it helpful or not.
* **Website Usability** (website_usability, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" for each usability aspect to indicate your experience.
* **Submit** (submit_button, time, required: false): You can submit your feedback at your convenience.
