# fleet_management_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect details about vehicles and their owners. It's meant for fleet managers, administrators, or owners to record and track information about their vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Vehicle Info page with your vehicle's details.
2. Proceed to the Owner Info page and enter the relevant information about the vehicle's owner.
3. Finally, go to the Vehicle Registration page to add any additional registration data.
4. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vehicle Info
* **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`, text, required: no): Enter your vehicle's make, model, and any other relevant details. This will help us identify the vehicle.
### Owner Info
* **Owner Info** (`owner_info`, text, required: no): Add the owner's name, contact information, and any other pertinent details. This will help us contact the owner or provide relevant information to them.
### Vehicle Registration
* **Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, text, required: no): Enter any relevant vehicle registration information, such as license plate numbers, VIN, or other official documentation numbers. This will aid in identifying the vehicle and verifying its legitimacy.

## Tips
* Be accurate and detailed when filling out the form to ensure we have the best chance of accurately tracking and managing your vehicle.
* If you're unsure about a field or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us for guidance.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not submit any sensitive or proprietary information.
