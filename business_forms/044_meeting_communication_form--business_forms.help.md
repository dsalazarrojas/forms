# Meeting Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for meeting participants to share information, give feedback, and outline actions before, during, and after the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and fill in the required and optional fields as needed. Start by filling out the meeting agenda, then provide your feedback, and finally, describe any follow-up actions you would like to take.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Agenda** (`meeting_agenda`, text, required): This field is where you can write down your expectations, goals, or objectives for the meeting.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts, comments, or suggestions about the meeting. This is an opportunity to express your opinions and help meeting organizers improve future meetings.
* **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, text, optional): Describe any tasks or actions you plan to take after the meeting. This can include tasks assigned to yourself, others, or follow-up questions you need to investigate.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear when filling out the meeting agenda.
* Use bullet points or numbered lists to make your feedback more readable.
* Be specific about the follow-up actions you want to take. This will help meeting organizers track progress and assign tasks efficiently.
