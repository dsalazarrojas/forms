# Asset Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Asset Inventory Form is used to collect and record information about company assets, such as their identification, acquisition, and management details. This form helps track and maintain accurate records of assets, ensuring their proper management and utilization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the full name or description of the asset in the `Asset Name` field.
2.  Select the most appropriate category for the asset from the `Asset Category` dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the manufacturer's unique identifier (Serial Number) for the asset.
4.  Enter the purchase date and price of the asset.
5.  Specify the current location of the asset.
6.  Provide details about the asset's current condition (e.g., New, Good, Fair, or Poor).
7.  Optionally, select a maintenance schedule for the asset (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
8.  Enter the last inspection date (if applicable).
9.  Add any additional notes or relevant information about the asset.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Asset Name** (`asset_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name or description of the asset.
* **Asset Category** (`asset_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the most appropriate category for the asset from the available options (IT Equipment, Furniture and Fixtures, Vehicles, Machinery and Tools, or Office Supplies).
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, `text`, required): Enter the manufacturer's unique identifier for the asset.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the asset was acquired.
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, `number`, required): Enter the amount paid for the asset in the local currency.
* **Current Location** (`current_location`, `text`, required): Specify the current location of the asset.
* **Asset Condition** (`asset_condition`, `select_one`, required): Select the current physical state of the asset (New, Good, Fair, Poor, or Non-Functional).
* **Asset Category** (`asset_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the most appropriate category for the asset from the available options.
* **Maintenance Schedule** (`maintenance_schedule`, `select_one`, optional): Select a maintenance schedule for the asset (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, or As Needed).
* **Last Inspection Date** (`last_inspection_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the last inspection (if applicable).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any other relevant information about the asset.

Note: The fields marked as `required` are essential for completing the form accurately.
