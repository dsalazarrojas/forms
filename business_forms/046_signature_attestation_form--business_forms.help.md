# signature_attestation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "signature_attestation_form" is a form designed to collect information for signature attestation, ensuring the authenticity of a signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, start by filling in the Signer Name field with the name of the individual signing the document or contract. Then, enter the Title of Signature with the title or position of the individual signing. If required, enter the Date the signature was made or the document was signed. Next, insert the actual signature in the Signature field. Finally, enter the name of the individual observing or witnessing the signature in the Witness Signer Name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Signer Name**: Signer Name (signer_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the individual signing the document or contract.
* **Title of Signature**: Title of Signature (title_of_signature, text, required: false): Enter the title or position of the individual signing.
* **Date**: Date (date, date, required: false): Enter the date the signature was made or the document was signed.
* **Signature**: Signature (signature, text, required: false): Insert the actual signature.
* **Witness Signer Name**: Witness Signer Name (witness_signer_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the individual observing or witnessing the signature.
