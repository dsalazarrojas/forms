# Coaching Tool Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request coaching tools for the employees of the company. It helps the management to understand the requirements and make the necessary arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic information in the first section: Requestor Information.
2. In the Tool Details section, fill in the tool name, category, description, and quantity needed.
3. In the Justification section, explain why you need the tool and how it will improve coaching.
4. In the Budget Information section, provide the estimated cost per unit and the total estimated cost.
5. In the Timing and Priority section, select the priority level and the needed by date.
6. In the Approval section, confirm if your supervisor has approved the tool.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, `Text`, Required): Fill in your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `Email`, Required): Enter your company email address.
* **Department or Team** (`department`, `Text`, Required): Fill in the department or team you belong to.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `Date`, Required): Select the date you are requesting the tool.
* **Tool Name** (`tool_name`, `Text`, Required): Enter the name of the tool being requested.
* **Tool Category** (`tool_category`, `Select One`, Required): Choose a category for the tool from the list.
* **Tool Description** (`tool_description`, `Text`, Required): Describe the tool and its features.
* **Quantity Needed** (`quantity_needed`, `Number`, Required): Fill in the number of units required.
* **Business Justification** (`business_justification`, `Text`, Required): Explain why this tool is needed and how it will improve coaching.
* **Expected Benefits** (`expected_benefits`, `Text`, Required): Describe how this tool will improve coaching.
* **Target Users** (`target_users`, `Text`, Required): Fill in who will use this tool.
* **Estimated Cost Per Unit** (`estimated_cost`, `Number`, Required): Enter the estimated cost per unit.
* **Total Estimated Cost** (`total_cost`, `Number`, Required): Fill in the total estimated cost.
* **Budget Code or Cost Center** (`budget_code`, `Text`, Optional): If there is a budget code or cost center for the tool, fill it in.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, `Select One`, Required): Choose the priority level from the list.
* **Needed By Date** (`needed_by_date`, `Date`, Required): Select the date the tool is needed by.
* **Supervisor or Manager Name** (`supervisor_name`, `Text`, Required): Enter your supervisor or manager's name.
* **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_approval`, `Select One`, Required): Confirm if your supervisor has approved the tool.
* **Needed By Date** (`needed_by_date`, `Date`, Required): This should match the date you entered in section 9.

Please note that all the fields marked as required are necessary to be filled in. The optional fields can be skipped if not required. Ensure that you have filled in all the required fields before submitting the form.
