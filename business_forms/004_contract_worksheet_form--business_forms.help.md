# Contract Worksheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather detailed information about a contract, including its basics, financial terms, timeline, parties involved, and terms and conditions. The gathered information will help to ensure that all relevant details are captured and communicated to relevant parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the basic information about the contract, including the **Contract Name**, **Contract Description**, and **Contract Category**.
2. Specify the **Prepared By** and **Preparation Date**, which should be the person and date that completed this form.
3. Choose the **Department** responsible for this contract.
4. Fill in the **Contract Start Date** and **Contract End Date**.
5. Select the **Priority Level** of the contract (Critical, High, Medium, or Low).
6. Specify the **Total Contract Value** and **Currency** for all transactions.
7. Choose the **Payment Terms** (Lump Sum, Monthly Installments, Quarterly Payments, etc.).
8. Fill in the **Primary Vendor or Counterparty** and **Internal Contract Owner**.
9. Select the **Insurance Required** status.
10. Specify any **Termination Notice Required** and **Liability Cap** if applicable.
11. Fill in the **Current Approval Status** and **Additional Notes** as needed.
