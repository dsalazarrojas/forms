# Customer Loyalty Tier Adjustment Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Loyalty Tier Adjustment Request form is used by customers to request a change to a different loyalty tier. This could be due to various reasons such as a tier downgrade or upgrade.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the First Name and Last Name fields with your personal details.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Phone number.
4. Select the reason for your request from the dropdown menu (you can select one or multiple reasons).
5. Choose the loyalty program you are currently enrolled in.
6. If applicable, enter your Date of Birth.
7. Select your current Tier Level and the desired Tier Level.
8. Enter your current Tier Level.
9. Enter your Manager's Name (if you have one).
10. Enter your Employee Number (if you have one).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your Email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone (Text)**: Provide your Phone number where we can contact you.
* **Reason for request (Select Multiple)**: Choose one or multiple reasons for your request from the dropdown menu. You can select Tier Downgrade, Tier Upgrade, or Tier Swap.
* **Loyalty program (Select One)**: Choose the loyalty program you are currently enrolled in from the dropdown menu. You can select Rewards Program, Exclusive Program, or Standard Program.
* **Date of Birth (Date)**: [Note: This field might be optional or used for internal purposes only.]
* **Tier Level (Select Multiple)**: Select your current and desired Tier Level from the dropdown menu. You can choose from Tier 1 to Tier 8.
* **Current Tier (Select One)**: Select your current Tier Level.
* **Manager Name (Text)**: Enter your Manager's name if you have one.
* **Employee Number (Number)**: [Note: This field might be optional or used for internal purposes only.]
