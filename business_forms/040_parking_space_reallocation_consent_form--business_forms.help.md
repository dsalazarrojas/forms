# parking_space_reallocation_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The parking space reallocation consent form is used by parking administrators to obtain consent from employees for the reallocation of parking spaces. This form is essential for ensuring that employees are aware of and agree to any changes made to their parking assignments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question accurately and honestly.
2. For the "form_two" field, select "Yes" if you consent to the reallocation of your parking space.
3. For the "form_three" field, select all applicable options that indicate your availability for the reallocation of your parking space.
4. In the "contact" field, provide any additional comments or questions you have regarding the reallocation of your parking space.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_one**: This field is not visible in the form as it is on a separate page.
* **form_two**: (Yes/No) - Select "Yes" to indicate your consent to the reallocation of your parking space. If you select "No", you will not be able to proceed with the reallocation.
* **form_three**: (Select Multiple) - Select all applicable options that indicate your availability for the reallocation of your parking space. Options include:
	+ [Insert options here, e.g., "Available at a specific time", "Available during the day", "Available during the week"]
* **contact**: (Text) - Please provide any additional comments or questions you have regarding the reallocation of your parking space. This is an optional field and can be used to provide more information to the parking administrator.
