# Store Audit Scheduling Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Store Audit Scheduling Contact Form is a tool used to schedule and coordinate store audits. This form helps store owners and managers provide essential contact information and details about their store, including the preferred schedule date and time for the audit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide the necessary information about your store, including its location and name.
2. Enter the contact person's name and email address for the store.
3. Choose a preferred schedule date and time for the audit.
4. Select the reason for the audit from the options provided.
5. If the reason for the audit is 'Other - Specify', provide a brief explanation in the 'Other - Specify' field.
6. Add any additional comments about the audit in the 'Additional Comments' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Information** (`store_audit_scheduling_contact_form_page_1`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the store owner or manager to provide a brief description of their store.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person for the store.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person for the store.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, `text`, required: false): Describe the store's location, including its address or any other relevant details.
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose the preferred date for the audit from the available dates.
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the preferred time for the audit.
* **Store Manager** (`store_manager`, `text`, required: false): Provide the name of the store manager responsible for the audit.
* **Reason for Audit** (`reason_for_audit`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the reason for the audit from the available options (Store Audit, Loss Prevention, or Other - Specify).
* **Other - Specify** (`reason_for_audit_other`, `text`, required: false): If you selected 'Other - Specify' as the reason for the audit, provide a brief explanation of the reason.
* **Additional Comments** (`store_audit_scheduling_contact_form_page_2`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or information about the audit.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about your store to help the auditors conduct a thorough and efficient audit.
* Ensure that your preferred schedule date and time are realistic and feasible.
* If you select 'Other - Specify' as the reason for the audit, provide a clear and concise explanation.
* Review and proofread your form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is included.
