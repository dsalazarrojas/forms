# Vendor Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Audit Form is a tool for collecting and documenting information about vendors and their activities. This form is intended to be used by internal staff or auditors to gather relevant information about vendors in a structured and organized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Vendor Name**: This field is used to record the name of the vendor you are auditing.
2. Select the **Vendor Type**: Choose the type of vendor (Retail, Food Service, Service, Manufacturing) that best describes the vendor's business category.
3. Enter the **Audit Date**: Record the date of the audit.
4. Enter the **Audit Time**: Record the time of the audit.
5. Enter the **Audit Location**: Record the location of the audit.
6. Add **Notes**: Provide any additional information about the audit, such as observations or actions taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (vendor_name, text, required): Enter the name of the vendor being audited.
* **Vendor Type** (vendor_type, select_one, required): Select the type of vendor from the provided options (Retail, Food Service, Service, Manufacturing).
* **Audit Date** (audit_date, date, required): Record the date of the audit.
* **Audit Time** (audit_time, time, required): Record the time of the audit.
* **Audit Location** (audit_location, text, required): Record the location of the audit.
* **Notes** (notes, note, required): Add any additional information about the audit.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered in each field.
* Keep the **Notes** field for any additional context or information about the audit.
* Use this form consistently across all vendors to ensure accurate and organized documentation.
