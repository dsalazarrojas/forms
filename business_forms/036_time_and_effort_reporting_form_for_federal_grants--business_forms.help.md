# Time And Effort Reporting Form For Federal Grants - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for employees to report their time and effort on federal grants. It is used to track and document work hours and costs for each grant project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the relevant federal grant from the options provided.
2. Select the reporting period for which you are reporting.
3. Enter the total number of hours worked on the project.
4. Enter the rate per hour for the work done on the project.
5. Enter the total cost associated with the work done on the project.
6. Enter the cost per hour for the project.
7. Select the reporting frequency for the project.
8. Enter the reporting deadline for the project.
9. Enter any comments or additional information about the project.
10. Enter your contact information and phone number.
11. Enter the name of the organization and grant funding information.
12. Enter the federal awards received.
13. Select the reporting status of the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Federal Grants**: Select the specific federal grant for which you are reporting time and effort.
* **Reporting Period**: The time period for which you are reporting time and effort (e.g., month, quarter, year).
* **Total Hours Worked**: Enter the total number of hours worked on the project.
* **Rate**: Enter the rate per hour for the work done on the project.
* **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost associated with the work done on the project.
* **Cost Per Hour**: Enter the cost per hour for the project.
* **Reporting Frequency**: Select the frequency at which you will be reporting time and effort for the project (e.g., monthly, quarterly, annually).
* **Reporting Deadline**: Enter the deadline for submitting this report.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional information or comments about the project.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information (e.g., email address).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization receiving the grant funding.
* **Grant Funding**: Enter the grant funding information.
* **Federal Awards**: Enter the federal awards received.
* **Reporting Status**: Select the reporting status of the project (e.g., active, inactive, completed).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure accurate tracking and reporting of time and effort.
* Review and edit your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or support team.
* Ensure that you have the necessary information and documentation required to complete this form accurately.
