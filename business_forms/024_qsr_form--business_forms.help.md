# qsr_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and observations from restaurant staff. This form is designed to be a simple and concise tool for collecting information on their experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "restaurant_survey" field on page 1 with any relevant details about your experience at a QSR (Quick Service Restaurant).
2. Provide any feedback in the "feedback" field on page 2.
3. Enter any observations in the "observation" field on page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Survey** (`restaurant_survey`, text, **Required/Optional: Optional**,): This field is a free-text field for you to share any details or comments about your experience at the restaurant. You are not required to fill this field.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, **Required/Optional: Optional**): This field is for providing any feedback or suggestions about your experience at the restaurant.
* **Observation** (`observation`, text, **Required/Optional: Optional**): This field is for entering any observations or insights you have about your experience at the restaurant.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible in the "restaurant_survey" field.
* Keep your feedback and observations clear and concise.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
