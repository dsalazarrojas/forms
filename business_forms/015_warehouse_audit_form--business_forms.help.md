# Warehouse Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Audit Form is used to perform regular audits on warehouse facilities. This form helps you to track and document the status of warehouse facilities, including their type and current status. It's essential for ensuring compliance with regulations and maintaining a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the Facility Id of the warehouse to be audited.
2. Select the Warehouse Id of the facility being audited.
3. Fill in the Facility Name (optional).
4. Provide the Location of the facility (optional).
5. Choose the Facility Type (e.g., office, warehouse).
6. Select the Facility Status (active, inactive).
7. Choose the Audit Type (e.g., safety, compliance).
8. Enter the Start Date and End Date of the audit (if applicable).
9. Enter the name of the Inspector conducting the audit (if applicable).
10. Add any Comments related to the audit (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Id** (`facility_id`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier of the facility being audited.
* **Warehouse Id** (`warehouse_id`, number, required): Select the Warehouse Id to associate with the facility.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the facility (if not provided, it will be left blank).
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the facility (if not provided, it will be left blank).
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of facility (e.g., office, warehouse).
* **Facility Status** (`facility_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the facility (e.g., active, inactive).
* **Audit Type** (`audit_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of audit (e.g., safety, compliance).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the audit (if not provided, it will be left blank).
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the audit (if not provided, it will be left blank).
* **Inspector** (`inspector`, text, optional): Enter the name of the Inspector conducting the audit (if not provided, it will be left blank).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments related to the audit (if not provided, it will be left blank).

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information for each field to ensure the audit is completed correctly.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
* This form is designed to help you track and document warehouse facilities, please use it as intended.
