# Job Safety Observation Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect observations and feedback on workplace safety. It helps identify potential hazards and recommend actions to improve job safety.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely**: Each field is crucial in helping us collect accurate and useful information.
2. **Answer questions truthfully**: Please provide honest feedback on the observed area and safety conditions.
3. **Use the provided fields for each section**: Make sure to fill out the correct field for each section to avoid confusion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Safety** (`job_safety_observation_form_template_page_2`, text, required): Briefly describe the area you observed in relation to job safety.
* **Observed By** (`job_safety_observation_form_template_page_3`, text, required): Please enter your name and title/position as observed.
* **Date of Observation** (`job_safety_observation_form_template_page_4`, date, required): Enter the date of the observation.
* **Time of Observation** (`job_safety_observation_form_template_page_5`, time, required): Enter the time of the observation.
* **Observed Area** (`job_safety_observation_form_template_page_6`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the area observed.
* **Observation Summary** (`job_safety_observation_form_template_page_7`, note, required): Write a short summary of your observations.
* **Safety Deficiency** (`job_safety_observation_form_template_page_8`, select_one, required): Indicate the level of the safety deficiency as follows: High, Moderate, or Low.
* **Recommendation** (`job_safety_observation_form_template_page_9`, text, required): Suggest possible actions to improve job safety.
* **Recommended Action** (`job_safety_observation_form_template_page_10`, text, required): Suggest specific actions to take to address the safety deficiency.

## Tips
* Be thorough and honest when filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
