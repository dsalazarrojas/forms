# Building Maintenance Forecast Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a building's maintenance needs to prepare accurate forecasts for maintenance and repair activities. Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately to help us understand your building's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information in sections 1-5.
2. Provide details about the building, including its type, size, and maintenance history.
3. Specify the forecast scope, including the time period and priority areas.
4. Add any additional comments or requirements in the last section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`): Please enter your full name.
* **Job Title** (`title`): Enter your job title or position.
* **Company Name** (`company`): Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date of the maintenance request.
* **Property Name** (`property_name`): Enter the name of the building or property.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`): Enter the full address of the building or property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Office Building
	+ Residential Complex
	+ Retail Center
	+ Industrial Facility
	+ Mixed Use
	+ Institutional
	+ Other
* **Building Size** (`building_size`): Enter the square footage of the building.
* **Year Built** (`year_built`): Enter the construction year of the building (optional).
* **Number of Floors** (`number_of_floors`): Enter the total number of floors in the building.
* **Forecast Period** (`forecast_period`): Select one of the following options:
	+ 1 Year
	+ 2 Years
	+ 3 Years
	+ 5 Years
	+ 10 Years
	+ Custom Period
* **Forecast Scope** (`forecast_scope`): Select all applicable options:
	+ Routine Maintenance
	+ Preventive Maintenance
	+ Capital Improvements
	+ Emergency Repairs
	+ System Replacements
	+ Exterior Maintenance
	+ Interior Maintenance
	+ Landscaping
	+ All Areas
* **Current Maintenance Budget** (`current_maintenance_budget`): Enter the annual budget (optional).
* **Maintenance History** (`maintenance_history`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Excellent - Well Documented
	+ Good - Mostly Up to Date
	+ Fair - Some Gaps
	+ Poor - Limited History
	+ Unknown
* **Known Issues** (`known_issues`): Enter any current problems or issues with the building (optional).
* **Recent Improvements** (`recent_improvements`): Enter any recent work done on the building (optional).
* **Major Systems Age** (`major_systems_age`): Enter the age of major systems, such as HVAC, roof, etc. (optional).
* **Priority Areas** (`priority_areas`): Select all applicable options:
	+ Cost Reduction
	+ Preventive Planning
	+ Capital Planning
	+ Compliance
	+ Risk Management
	+ Energy Efficiency
	+ Tenant Satisfaction
	+ All Areas
* **Forecast Format** (`forecast_format`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Detailed Report
	+ Executive Summary
	+ Spreadsheet
	+ Presentation
	+ Dashboard
* **Include Recommendations** (`include_recommendations`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes - Detailed
	+ Yes - Summary
	+ No - Data Only
* **Stakeholder Review** (`stakeholder_review`): Enter the names of individuals or teams that will review the forecast (optional).
* **Deadline** (`deadline`): Enter the date when you need the forecast completed.
* **Budget for Forecast** (`budget_for_forecast`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Under $2,000
	+ $2,000 - $5,000
	+ $5,000 - $10,000
	+ $10,000 - $25,000
	+ Over $25,000
* **Additional Requirements** (`additional_requirements`): Enter any special requirements or needs.
