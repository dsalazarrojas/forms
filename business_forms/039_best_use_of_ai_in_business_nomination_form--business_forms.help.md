# Best Use of AI in Business Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to identify and recognize businesses that have effectively leveraged AI in their operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your nominator information in the first section.
2. Provide details about the business you are nominating in the second section.
3. Describe the AI solution or project used by the business in the third section.
4. Rate the business's AI implementation based on various criteria in the fourth section.
5. Submit your nomination by providing final details and verifying your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Nominator Information
* **Your Full Name** (`nominator_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the person nominating the business.
* **Your Role** (`nominator_professional_role`, text, required): Enter your job title or function.
* **Your Company** (`nominator_company_org`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Your Contact Email** (`nominator_email_addr`, email, required): Enter your work email address.

### Business Nominee Details
* **Nominated Business Name** (`nominated_business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the business being nominated.
* **Nominated Business Website** (`nominated_business_website`, text, required): Enter the website URL of the business.
* **Industry Sector** (`nominee_industry_select`, select_one, required): Select the primary industry of the business (Technology and Software, Healthcare and Pharmaceuticals, Finance and Banking, Retail and E-commerce, Manufacturing and Logistics, Marketing and Advertising, Education and E-learning, Other).

### AI Implementation Details
* **Description of AI Solution or Project** (`ai_solution_description`, text, required): Briefly explain the AI use case and how it works.
* **AI Technologies Used** (`ai_technologies_used`, select_multiple, required): Select all the AI technologies implemented (Machine Learning (ML), Natural Language Processing (NLP), Computer Vision, Generative AI (LLMs/etc.), Robotics and Automation, Predictive Analytics, Recommendation Engines, Other).
* **Duration of AI Solution** (`ai_operational_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of the AI solution in production (Less than 6 months, 6 to 12 months, 1 to 2 years, Over 2 years).
* **Measurable ROI or Business Outcome** (`ai_roi_outcome_desc`, text, required): Describe specific gains in efficiency, revenue, or results.
* **Innovation Level of the Solution** (`innovation_rating_score`, select_one, required): Rate how unique and forward-thinking the project is (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Description of AI Safety and Ethics** (`ai_ethics_safety_desc`, text, required): Describe how the business addresses bias and transparency.
* **Customer Experience Impact** (`customer_experience_impact_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how the AI improves the lives of end-users (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Scalability of the AI Solution** (`ai_scalability_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the potential for growth and wider application (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).

### Nomination Support
* **What makes this the Best Use of AI in Business?** (`best_use_ai_justification`, text, required): Summarize your argument for the award.
* **Future Vision for AI in this Business** (`future_ai_vision_desc`, text, optional): Describe where the project is headed next.
* **Professional Reference for Verification** (`professional_verification_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and email of someone familiar with the project.
* **Date of Nomination** (`nomination_final_date_input`, date, required): Enter the date of the nomination.

Please note that all fields are required unless specified as optional.
