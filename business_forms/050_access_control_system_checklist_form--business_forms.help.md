# Access Control System Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for inspecting and documenting the status of access control systems in facilities or buildings. It helps ensure that all systems are functioning correctly and that any issues are identified and addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Select the status of each access control system based on your inspection.
3. Add any notes or comments about system issues or conditions.
4. Rate the overall condition of the access control system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector or technician name**: Enter your name, as this will be used to identify you as the person who conducted the inspection.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and follow-up.
* **Employee or badge ID**: Enter your employee or badge ID, which is used for identification purposes.
* **Date of inspection**: Enter the date when you inspected the access control systems.
* **Time of inspection**: Enter the time when you inspected the access control systems.
* **Facility or building name**: Enter the name of the facility or building being inspected.
* **Physical locks and doors**: Select the status of physical locks and doors, including whether they are functioning properly or not.
* **Door locks status**: Select whether all door locks are functioning properly, or if there are issues.
* **Notes on door lock issues (if any)**: Enter any notes or comments about door lock issues.
* **Card readers and badge scanners status**: Select the status of card readers and badge scanners, including whether they are operational or not.
* **Biometric scanners status (fingerprint, facial recognition)**: Select the status of biometric scanners, including whether they are operational or not.
* **Keypads and PIN entry systems status**: Select the status of keypads and PIN entry systems, including whether they are operational or not.
* **Intrusion alarm system status**: Select the status of the intrusion alarm system, including whether it is operational or disarmed.
* **Fire alarm system status**: Select the status of the fire alarm system, including whether it is operational or malfunctioning.
* **Surveillance system status**: Select the status of the surveillance system, including whether CCTV cameras are operational or offline.
* **Video recording and storage system status**: Select the status of the video recording and storage system, including whether it is recording normally or not.
* **Summary of all issues found**: Enter a summary of any issues found during the inspection.
* **Corrective actions taken**: Enter any corrective actions taken on site.
* **Maintenance or repair needed**: Select whether maintenance or repair is needed for any systems.
* **Overall access control system condition**: Select the overall condition of the access control system, based on your inspection.
* **Date inspection was completed**: Enter the date when the inspection was completed.
