# Agent Credentials Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Agent Credentials Verification Form is designed to ensure the authenticity of an agent's credentials by verifying their professional licenses, identity, and background information.

<thinking>
This form is used for the following purpose:
- To collect and verify the agent's primary field of expertise (e.g., real estate, insurance, or finance)
- To collect the agent's date of birth and license expiry date
- To collect the agent's license number and issuing authority
- To collect uploaded documents for verification
- To collect background information about the agent's license status (suspension or revocation)
- To collect references from industry peers for validation of professional standing

This form is required for agents seeking to be listed on our platform. Please ensure that you have all necessary documentation and information before completing this form.

Note: Please answer all required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible. Incomplete or incorrect information may delay the verification process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Provide your full name in the "Applicant Full Name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Select your primary field of expertise from the dropdown menu in the "Primary License Type" field.
5. Enter your license number as issued by the state or authority in the "License Number" field.
6. Enter the expiry date of your license in the "License Expiry Date" field.
7. Enter the issuing authority of your license in the "Issuing Authority" field.
8. Upload the required documents for verification in the "Documents uploaded for verification - ID, License, Certifications" field.
9. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have ever had a license suspended or revoked in the "Have you ever had a license suspended or revoked?" field.
10. If you answered "Yes", provide details about the circumstances in the "If yes, please provide details" field.
11. Enter the names and contact information of two industry peers for reference in the "References for professional standing" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`dob`, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Primary License Type** (`license_type`, Select One, Required): Select your primary field of expertise from the dropdown menu (e.g., real estate, insurance, or finance).
* **License Number** (`license_number`, Text, Required): Enter your license number as issued by the state or authority.
* **License Expiry Date** (`expiry_date`, Date, Required): Enter the expiry date of your license.
* **Issuing Authority** (`issuing_authority`, Text, Required): Enter the issuing authority of your license (e.g., Department of Insurance).
* **Documents uploaded for verification - ID, License, Certifications** (`uploaded_docs`, Select Multiple, Required): Select and upload the required documents for verification (e.g., Government Photo ID, Professional License Copy).
* **Have you ever had a license suspended or revoked?** (`suspension_check`, Select One, Required): Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have ever had a license suspended or revoked.
* **If yes, please provide details** (`suspension_details`, Text, Optional): If you answered "Yes", provide details about the circumstances.
* **References for professional standing** (`professional_references`, Text, Required): Enter the names and contact information of two industry peers for reference.
