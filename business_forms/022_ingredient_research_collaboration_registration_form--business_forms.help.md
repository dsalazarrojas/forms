# Ingredient Research Collaboration Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals or organizations interested in collaborating on ingredient research projects. By filling out this form, you will be able to register your interest in collaboration and provide necessary contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your contact information, including name, company, and email address.
3. Select your role in the collaboration (e.g., researcher, industry partner, etc.).
4. Choose one or multiple research interests that align with your area of expertise.
5. Answer yes or no to questions about specific research interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form 1** (`form_1`): Enter a brief description of your research interests or goals.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Enter your name and any additional contact information (e.g., company, phone number).
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name.
* **Company** (`company`): Enter the name of your company.
* **Role** (`role`): Select your role in the collaboration (e.g., researcher, industry partner, etc.).
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date you are interested in starting the collaboration.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the collaboration will take place.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`): Enter a brief description of your research project or goals.
* **Research Area** (`research_area`): Select your area of research interest.
* **Research Interests** (`research_interests`): Select one or multiple research interests that align with your area of expertise.
* **Research Interest 1** (`research_interests_1`): Select one specific research interest (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Research Interest 2** (`research_interests_2`): Select one specific research interest (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Research Interest 3** (`research_interests_3`): Select one specific research interest (e.g., "Yes" or "No").

## Tips
Please fill out the form completely and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
