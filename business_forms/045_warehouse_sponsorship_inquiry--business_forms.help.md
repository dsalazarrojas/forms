# Warehouse Sponsorship Inquiry - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to inquire about warehouse sponsorship opportunities for your company. Please fill out the form below to provide necessary information about your company and your interest in warehouse sponsorship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company name and contact information, including phone number and email address.
2. Select your company type (Manufacturing, Service Provider, or Other) and the type of inquiry you have (General, Product Inquiry, or Support).
3. Choose how you would like to be contacted (Online, Phone, or Email).
4. Provide any additional comments or questions you have.
5. Finally, confirm the resolution status of your inquiry and provide a closed date if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (1)** (`company_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Name (2)** (`contact_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **Email Address (3)** (`email_address`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number (4)** (`phone_number`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Company Type (5)** (`company_type`, Select One, Required): Choose the type of your company (Manufacturing, Service Provider, or Other).
* **Inquiry Type (6)** (`inquiry_type`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the type of inquiry you have (General, Product Inquiry, or Support).
* **Contact Method (7)** (`contact_method`, Select One, Required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Online, Phone, or Email).
* **Inquiry Details (8)** (`inquiry_details`, Text, Required): Enter any additional information about your inquiry.
* **Additional Comments (9)** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Follow Up Question (10)** (`follow_up_question`, Text, Optional): Enter any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Follow Up Answer (11)** (`follow_up_answer`, Text, Optional): Enter any follow-up answers or comments.
* **Next Steps (12)** (`next_steps`, Text, Optional): Describe the next steps for your inquiry.
* **JIRA Ticket (13)** (`jira_ticket`, Text, Optional): Enter any JIRA ticket information related to your inquiry.
* **Status (14)** (`status`, Text, Optional): Enter the resolution status of your inquiry.
* **Resolution (15)** (`resolution`, Select One, Required): Confirm the resolution status of your inquiry (Resolved, Not Resolved, or In Progress).
* **Closed Date (16)** (`closed_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date when your inquiry is resolved.
* **Created By (17)** (`created_by`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person who created the inquiry.
* **Updated By (18)** (`updated_by`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person who updated the inquiry.
* **Updated Date (19)** (`updated_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the inquiry was last updated.
* **Created At (20)** (`created_at`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the inquiry was created.
* **Updated At (21)** (`updated_at`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the inquiry was last updated.
* **Deleted At (22)** (`deleted_at`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the inquiry was deleted.
* **Deleted By (23)** (`deleted_by`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person who deleted the inquiry.
* **Deleted Reason (24)** (`deleted_reason`, Text, Optional): Enter the reason the inquiry was deleted.
* **Archived At (25)** (`archived_at`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the inquiry was archived.

Note: Fields with `Optional` labels can be left blank if not applicable.
