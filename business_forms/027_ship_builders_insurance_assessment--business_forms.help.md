# Ship Builders Insurance Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a shipyard's or ship builder's insurance needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a clear and concise answer to each question in the text fields.
2. Ensure you fill in all required fields, as marked with a red asterisk (\*).
3. If a question asks for a specific type of information (e.g. contact information), provide the requested type of information in the corresponding field.
4. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ship Builders Assessment (<name>: ship_builders_insurance_assessment, <type>: text, required: false>):**
	+ This field is a general assessment of the shipyard's or ship builder's insurance needs.
* **Risk Details (<name>: risk_details, <type>: text, required: false>):**
	+ This field is for providing additional risk details or information that may be relevant to the insurance assessment.
* **Insurance Requirements (<name>: insurance_requirements, <type>: text, required: false>):**
	+ This field is for listing the specific insurance requirements or needs of the shipyard or ship builder.
* **Underwriting Questions (<name>: underwriting_questions, <type>: text, required: false>):**
	+ This field is for answering any underwriting questions or providing additional information that may be relevant to the insurance assessment.
* **Coverage Discussions (<name>: coverage_discussions, <type>: text, required: false>):**
	+ This field is for discussing or clarifying any coverage-related questions or concerns.
* **Operational Details (<name>: operational_details, <type>: text, required: false>):**
	+ This field is for providing detailed information about the shipyard's or ship builder's operations, such as their business operations, management, or other relevant information.
* **Contact Info (<name>: contact_info, <type>: text, required: false>):**
	+ This field is for providing contact information about the person or company submitting the form.
* **Shipyard Details (<name>: shipyard_details, <type>: text, required: false>):**
	+ This field is for providing detailed information about the shipyard, such as its location, size, or type.
* **Marine Contractors Info (<name>: marine_contractors_info, <type>: text, required: false>):**
	+ This field is for providing information about any marine contractors or subcontractors involved in the shipbuilding process.
* **Risk Factors (<name>: risk_factors, <type>: text, required: false>):**
	+ This field is for listing any risk factors or concerns that may be relevant to the insurance assessment.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
- If you're unsure about any field or question, refer back to the instructions above.
- This form is designed to be used by shipyards or ship builders to provide information about their insurance needs and requirements.
- The information provided will be used by an insurance company or risk management team to assess and tailor insurance policies accordingly.
