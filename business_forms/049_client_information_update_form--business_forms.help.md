# Client Information Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Information Update Form is used to update client information, including contact details, address, insurance provider, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form to begin.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Provide accurate information for each field, as it may affect downstream processes.
4. Review the form before submission to ensure all fields are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (client_first_name)** (`text`, *): Your legal first name.
* **Last Name (client_last_name)** (`text`, *): Your legal last name.
* **Email Address (client_email)** (`email`, *): Primary contact email.
* **Phone Number (client_phone)** (`text`, *): Best contact number.
* **Client ID or Account Number (client_id_number)** (`text`, optional): Your account identifier.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)** (`date`, optional): For verification purposes.
* **Type of Update (update_type)** (`select_one`, *): Select the type of update you are making.
* **Previous Address (old_address)** (`text`, optional): If you are updating your address.
* **New Address (new_address)** (`text`, optional): Complete new address details.
* **New City (new_city)** (`text`, optional): New city name.
* **New State (new_state)** (`text`, optional): New state name.
* **New ZIP Code (new_zip)** (`text`, optional): New ZIP code.
* **New Email Address (new_email)** (`email`, optional): New contact email (if changing).
* **New Phone Number (new_phone)** (`text`, optional): New contact number (if changing).
* **New Insurance Provider (new_insurance_provider)** (`text`, optional): New insurance provider company name.
* **New Policy Number (new_policy_number)** (`text`, optional): New policy ID.
* **New Emergency Contact Name (new_emergency_contact_name)** (`text`, optional): Full name of emergency contact.
* **New Emergency Contact Phone (new_emergency_contact_phone)** (`text`, optional): New contact number for emergency.
* **New Emergency Contact Relationship (new_emergency_contact_relationship)** (`text`, optional): Relationship with emergency contact.
* **New Payment Method (new_payment_method)** (`select_one`, optional): Select a new payment method if changing.
* **New Medical Conditions (new_medical_conditions)** (`text`, optional): Any new medical conditions.
* **New Medications (new_medications)** (`text`, optional): New prescriptions.
* **New Allergies (new_allergies)** (`text`, optional): New allergies.
* **Communication Preferences (communication_preferences)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select how you want to be contacted.
* **Reason for Update (update_reason)** (`text`, optional): Explain why you are making this change.
* **When Should This Take Effect? (effective_date)** (`date`, *): Desired effective date for the update.
* **I Confirm All Information Is Accurate (information_accuracy)** (`select_one`, *): Confirm all inputted information is accurate.
* **Update Date (update_date)** (`date`, *): Date of the update submission.

Please note that fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required fields. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
