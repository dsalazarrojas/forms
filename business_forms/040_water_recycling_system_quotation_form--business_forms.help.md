# water_recycling_system_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The water recycling system quotation form is used to request a quotation for a water recycling system installation project. This form helps us understand your requirements and provides us with the necessary information to create an accurate quotation for your project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your quotation request, providing a brief description of your requirements.
2. Provide your customer information, including your name and contact details.
3. Enter the contact person's name and phone number for further communication.
4. Enter your email address for us to contact you.
5. Select the type of system you are interested in (System A, B, or C).
6. Specify the system capacity.
7. Enter the location where the system will be installed.
8. Choose a quotation deadline.
9. Enter the expected quotation price.
10. Select the current quotation status (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
11. Add any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quotation request** (`quotation_request`, **note**, optional): A brief description of your project requirements.
* **Customer information** (`customer_info`, **text**, optional): Enter your name, company name, and any other relevant customer information.
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`, **text**, optional): Enter the name and phone number of the person we should contact for further communication.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, **text**, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, **text**, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **System type** (`system_type`, **select_one**, optional): Select the type of system you are interested in (System A, B, or C).
* **System capacity** (`system_capacity`, **number**, optional): Enter the capacity of the system you need.
* **System location** (`system_location`, **text**, optional): Enter the location where the system will be installed.
* **Quotation deadline** (`quotation_deadline`, **date**, optional): Choose a deadline for the quotation.
* **Quotation price** (`quotation_price`, **number**, optional): Enter the expected quotation price.
* **Quotation status** (`quotation_status`, **select_one**, optional): Select the current status of the quotation (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, **text**, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your project.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure we have enough information to create an accurate quotation.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
