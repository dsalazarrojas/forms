# Corporate Governance Evaluation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Governance Evaluation Request Form is used to collect and track information related to corporate governance and review requests. This form will help you to understand the status and progress of your review requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the request form (page 1) with your basic information and request details.
2. On page 1, choose your review request status (Active/Inactive) to help us track your request.
3. On pages 2-3, select the review period and preferred timeline for your request.
4. On pages 4-6, add any supporting documents and provide details about them.
5. On pages 7-9, fill in the board member details and review request status.
6. On pages 10-13, select the review request due date, completed date, closed date, and reopened date.
7. On page 14, select the review request closed status (Active/Inactive).
8. On page 15, select the review request reopened status (Active/Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request Form (1)**: Enter a brief description of your review request.
- **Review Request (2)**: Indicate if you have a review request (Yes/No).
- **Review Period (3)**: Select the duration of the review period for your request.
- **Preferred Timeline (4)**: Select your preferred completion timeline for the review request.
- **Supporting Documents (5)**: Provide any supporting documents related to your review request.
- **Review Request Details (6)**: Add any additional details about your review request.
- **Supporting Documents Details (7)**: Provide more details about your supporting documents.
- **Board Member Details (8)**: Fill in the details of the board member related to your review request.
- **Review Request Status (9)**: Select the current status of your review request.
- **Review Request Due Date (10)**: Enter the due date for your review request.
- **Review Request Completed Date (11)**: Enter the date your review request was completed.
- **Review Request Closed Date (12)**: Enter the date your review request was closed.
- **Review Request Reopened Date (13)**: Enter the date your review request was reopened.
- **Review Request Closed Status (14)**: Select the status of your review request (Active/Inactive).
- **Review Request Reopened Status (15)**: Select the status of your review request after it was reopened (Active/Inactive).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate tracking and evaluation of your review request.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact your administrator for assistance.
