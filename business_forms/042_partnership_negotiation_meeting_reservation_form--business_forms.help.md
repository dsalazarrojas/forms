# Partnership Negotiation Meeting Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Partnership Negotiation Meeting Reservation Form is used to reserve a meeting with a partner. This form helps you to schedule and organize meetings efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Meeting Date field with the date of the meeting.
2. Enter the Time field with the time of the meeting.
3. In the Partner field, type the name of the partner you're meeting with.
4. Use the Meeting Details field to provide any additional details about the meeting.
5. Enter the Location of the meeting in the Location field.
6. Set the Duration field to the length of the meeting (in minutes).
7. Choose the Type of meeting from the available options (likely 'Yes' or 'No' indicating whether it's a yes or no meeting type).
8. In the Purpose field, type in the reason for the meeting.
9. For the Status field, select the status of the meeting (Active or Inactive).
10. Finally, add any Notes about the meeting in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Time** (`meeting_time`, number, required: false): Enter the time of the meeting in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Partner** (`partner_name`, text, required: false): Type in the name of the partner you're meeting with.
* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, note, required: false): Add any additional details about the meeting.
* **Location** (`meeting_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the meeting (e.g., office, conference room).
* **Duration** (`meeting_duration`, number, required: false): Set the length of the meeting in minutes.
* **Type** (`meeting_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose from the available options (likely 'Yes' or 'No' indicating whether it's a yes or no meeting type).
* **Purpose** (`meeting_purpose`, text, required: false): Type in the reason for the meeting.
* **Status** (`meeting_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the status of the meeting (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes** (`meeting_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the meeting.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask your colleagues or superiors for clarification.
* This form is meant to be used for partnership negotiation meetings, so please keep the purpose and type relevant to the meeting topic. 
* The status field is likely used to track whether the meeting is active or inactive, so select accordingly. 
* Notes can be used for any additional information that you need to remember about the meeting.
