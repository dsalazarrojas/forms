# Byrna Reseller Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Byrna Reseller Agreement is used to collect information from resellers who wish to sell Byrna products on their own channels. This guide will walk you through each field and provide a better understanding of the required fields and the purpose of the form.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow the steps below to complete the form:

* Ensure you have all necessary documents and information available, such as business license numbers and tax IDs.
* Fill out all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
* Double-check that you have filled out the correct information for your business.
* Review the agreement terms and conditions to ensure you understand and agree to them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Reseller Business Name (1)
* **Reseller Business Name** (`reseller_name`, text, required): Enter your official business name as it appears on your government-issued documents, such as your business license.

### Business Address (2)
* **Business Address** (`reseller_address`, text, required): Enter your full business address, including the street address and any additional address lines.

### City (3)
* **City** (`reseller_city`, text, required): Enter the city where your business is located.

### State or Province (4)
* **State or Province** (`reseller_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where your business is located.

### ZIP or Postal Code (5)
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`reseller_zip`, text, required): Enter the ZIP or postal code associated with your business address.

### Contact Person Name (6)
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your business.

### Contact Email (7)
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.

### Contact Phone (8)
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the business phone number of the contact person.

### Type of Business (9)
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best describes your business type:
	+ Retail
	+ Online Store
	+ Distributor
	+ Security Company
	+ Law Enforcement
	+ Other

### Years in Business (10)
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, required): Enter the number of years your business has been established.

### Business License Number (11)
* **Business License Number** (`business_license`, text, required): Enter your official business license number.

### Tax ID or EIN (12)
* **Tax ID or EIN** (`tax_id`, text, required): Enter your federal tax identification number (EIN) or state tax ID.

### Resale Certificate Number (13)
* **Resale Certificate Number** (`resale_certificate`, text, optional): If you have a resale certificate, enter the number here.

### Sales Experience with Similar Products (14)
* **Sales Experience with Similar Products** (`sales_experience`, text, optional): Share any relevant background or experience you have with similar products.

### Territory or Market (15)
* **Territory or Market** (`market_territory`, text, required): Enter the geographic area you will serve with your business.

### Estimated Annual Sales (16)
* **Estimated Annual Sales** (`estimated_annual_sales`, number, optional): Project your annual sales revenue for your business.

### I Agree to Reseller Terms and Conditions (17)
* **I Agree to Reseller Terms and Conditions** (`agreement_terms`, select_one, required): Choose to accept the reseller terms and conditions:
	+ Yes, I accept
	+ No, I do not accept

### Agreement Date (18)
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): This field will be automatically populated with the current date.

## Tips
* Double-check that you have filled out all the required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have provided accurate and up-to-date information.
* Review the agreement terms and conditions carefully before accepting them.
