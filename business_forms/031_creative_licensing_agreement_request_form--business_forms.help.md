# Creative Licensing Agreement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The creative licensing agreement request form is used to collect essential information from both the artist and the client when entering into a licensing agreement. It helps establish a clear understanding of the project's terms and details.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Read the form carefully and understand the purpose and fields.
*   Fill in the project details, artist information, project terms, and license information on the corresponding pages.
*   Sign and date both the artist and client signature fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Project Details** (Project Details, text, required: false):
    This field is where you will enter the details of the project, including any relevant information about the project's scope, objectives, and expectations.
*   **Artist Info** (Artist Info, text, required: false):
    In this field, you will enter information about the artist, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
*   **Project Terms** (Project Terms, text, required: false):
    This field is where you will outline any specific terms or conditions of the project, including deadlines, payment terms, and any other important details.
*   **License Info** (License Info, text, required: false):
    Here, you will specify the type of license or agreement being made, including any specific terms or conditions that apply.
*   **Artist Signature** (Artist Signature, text, required: false):
    The artist must sign and date this field to confirm their agreement to the licensing terms.
*   **Client Signature** (Client Signature, text, required: false):
    The client must sign and date this field to confirm their agreement to the licensing terms.

## Tips
*   Make sure to read and understand each field carefully before filling it out.
*   Ensure that both the artist and client sign and date their respective signature fields.
*   Review the completed form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.
