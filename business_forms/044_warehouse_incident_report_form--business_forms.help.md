# Warehouse Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Incident Report Form is a document used to record and report incidents that occur in the warehouse environment. It helps to gather information about the incident, including the date it occurred, the type of incident, and the actions taken. This form is used for internal record-keeping and may be used for auditing, investigation, or review purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Accident Date" field with the date the incident occurred.
2. Select the type of incident from the options provided in the "Incident Type" dropdown menu.
3. Provide a detailed description of what happened in the "Incident Description" field.
4. Enter the location where the incident took place in the "Location" field.
5. List any witnesses to the incident in the "Witness List" field.
6. Assess the level of damage caused by the incident in the "Damage Assessment" dropdown menu.
7. Enter any actions taken as a result of the incident in the "Action Taken" field.
8. Enter a detailed report of what happened in the "Incident Report" field.
9. Sign the incident report with your name in the "Incident Report" field.
10. Sign and date the report with your supervisor's name in the "Supervisor" field.
11. Confirm that the incident report is completed and accurate in the "Incident Report Completed" dropdown menu.
12. Confirm that the report has been reviewed in the "Incident Report Review" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Accident Date** (date): Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (select_one): Choose the type of incident from the options provided:
	+ Fire
	+ Equipment Malfunction
	+ Natural Disaster
* **Incident Description** (text): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Location** (text): Enter the location where the incident took place.
* **Employee Name** (text): Enter your name as the person involved in the incident.
* **Department** (text): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Witness List** (text): List any witnesses to the incident.
* **Damage Assessment** (select_one): Assess the level of damage caused by the incident:
	+ Minor
	+ Major
* **Action Taken** (text): Enter any actions taken as a result of the incident.
* **Incident Report** (text): Enter a detailed report of what happened.
* **Incident Report** (text): Sign and date the report with your name and date.
* **Supervisor** (text): Sign and date the report with your supervisor's name and date.
* **Incident Report Date** (date): Enter the date the report was completed.
* **Incident Report Completed** (select_one): Confirm that the report is completed and accurate:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Incident Report Review** (select_one): Confirm that the report has been reviewed:
	+ True
	+ False
