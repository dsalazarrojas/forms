# Innovation Ecosystem Audit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your organization's innovation ecosystem audit request. It will help us understand your organization's goals, objectives, and preferred schedule for the audit.

## How To Complete This Form
- Take a few minutes to read through this guide and fill out each field accurately.
- Fill in all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
- Use the provided options for select multiple fields.
- Double-check your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization Details** (1, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your organization. This could include your company name, location, and type of organization (e.g., startup, non-profit, corporation).
- **Objectives** (2, text, optional): List the specific objectives you want to achieve through the innovation ecosystem audit. Be as detailed as possible to help us provide accurate results.
- **Preferred Audit Schedule** (3, select multiple, optional): Choose the schedule that best fits your needs. You can select one or multiple options, such as Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annually, or Yearly. This will help us tailor the audit process to your organization's needs.
 
## Tips
- If you have any doubts or need help with this form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
