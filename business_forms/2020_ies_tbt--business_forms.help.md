# 2020 Ies Tbt - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and satisfaction data from users about their experience with 2020 Ies Tbt. The form aims to understand how users feel about the platform, what they like or dislike about it, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email address" field. This will be used to contact you if needed.
3. Select your department from the "Department" dropdown menu.
4. Choose how satisfied you are with 2020 Ies Tbt from the "How satisfied are you with 2020 Ies Tbt?" dropdown menu.
5. Enter your overall rating of 2020 Ies Tbt, from 1 (worst) to 10 (best).
6. Select how often you use 2020 Ies Tbt from the "How often do you use 2020 Ies Tbt?" dropdown menu.
7. If needed, select the areas that applied to your experience with 2020 Ies Tbt from the "Which aspects applied to your experience with 2020 Ies Tbt?" dropdown menu.
8. Provide detailed feedback on which area of 2020 Ies Tbt needs improvement.
9. If needed, describe your experience with 2020 Ies Tbt in the "Additional comments or notes" field.
10. Finally, provide your overall feedback on 2020 Ies Tbt in the "Open feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, optional): Enter your complete name.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Company name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, enter your organization's name.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): This field is currently not used, please enter a date if you want to provide it.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select your department from the dropdown menu.
	+ Sales
	+ Marketing
	+ Operations
	+ IT
	+ Finance
	+ HR
	+ Other
* **How satisfied are you with 2020 Ies Tbt?** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with 2020 Ies Tbt from the dropdown menu.
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, integer, required): Rate 2020 Ies Tbt from 1 (worst) to 10 (best).
* **Which aspects applied to your experience with 2020 Ies Tbt?** (`experience_aspects`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the areas that applied to your experience with 2020 Ies Tbt from the dropdown menu.
	+ Easy to use
	+ Fast
	+ Reliable
	+ Missing feature
	+ Confusing
	+ Well designed
* **How often do you use 2020 Ies Tbt?** (`usage_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you use 2020 Ies Tbt from the dropdown menu.
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
	+ First time user
* **Primary use case** (`primary_use_case`, text, optional): Describe your primary use case for 2020 Ies Tbt.
* **Did you require support during your experience?** (`support_needed`, select_one, required): Choose if you required support during your experience with 2020 Ies Tbt.
	+ Yes, frequently
	+ Yes, occasionally
	+ No, rarely
	+ No, never needed
* **Quality of support received** (`support_quality`, integer, optional): Rate the quality of support you received from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent).
* **How likely are you to recommend 2020 Ies Tbt?** (`recommend_likelihood`, integer, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend 2020 Ies Tbt from 1 (not likely) to 10 (extremely likely).
* **Which area needs most improvement?** (`improvement_priority`, select_one, optional): Select the area that needs most improvement with 2020 Ies Tbt from the dropdown menu.
	+ User interface
	+ Performance
	+ Features
	+ Documentation
	+ Customer support
	+ Integration
* **Open feedback** (`open_feedback`, text, optional): Provide your overall feedback on 2020 Ies Tbt.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about 2020 Ies Tbt.

## Tips

* Please provide honest and detailed feedback about your experience with 2020 Ies Tbt.
* If you selected "Rarely" or "First time user" in the "How often do you use 2020 Ies Tbt?" field, please explain your reason for selecting this option.
* If you are representing a company, please ensure that you are authorized to provide feedback on behalf of your organization.
