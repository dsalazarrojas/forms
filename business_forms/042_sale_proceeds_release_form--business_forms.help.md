# Sale Proceeds Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sale Proceeds Release Form is a critical document used to record and confirm the terms of a property sale or transfer. It ensures that all parties involved are aware of the details of the transaction, including the sale price, client shares, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Transaction Date** (`transaction_date`): Enter the date of the transaction in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
2.  **Client Name** (`client_name`): Type the name of the client involved in the transaction.
3.  **Client Address** (`client_address`): Enter the address of the client.
4.  **Sale Price** (`sale_price`): Enter the total sale price of the property.
5.  **Our Share** (`our_share`): Fill in the organization's share of the sale or transfer.
6.  **Client Share** (`client_share`): Enter the client's share of the sale or transfer.
7.  **Net Proceeds** (`net_proceeds`): Calculate the net proceeds (sale price minus the organization's share) and enter the amount.
8.  **Release Date** (`release_date`): Enter the date when the sale proceeds are to be released to the client.
9.  **Sale Type** (`sale_type`): Choose the type of sale or transfer from the options provided (Sold, Leased, or Transferred).
10. **Property Details** (`property_details`): Provide any additional details about the property.
11.  **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the transaction.
12.  **Email** (`email`): Enter the client's email address.
13.  **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the client's contact phone number.

Note: Ensure that all fields with a `*` are required, and please fill out the form carefully, double-checking the accuracy of the information provided. This form is essential for maintaining transparency and compliance in the sale or transfer of a property.
