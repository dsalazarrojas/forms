# inventory_rotation_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The inventory rotation checklist is a crucial tool for inventory management, helping organizations keep track of their stock levels, usage cycles, and other essential details. By using this form, you can ensure accurate and up-to-date inventory information, preventing stock discrepancies and optimizing business operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Product Name** in the "Product Name" field. This is a required field and should be filled out with the exact name of the product being tracked.
2. Provide the current **Stock Level** of the product in the "Product Stock Level" field. This is a numerical value representing the quantity of the product currently in stock.
3. Select the **Usage Cycle** of the product from the options provided. This field allows you to specify the usage cycle of the product, such as "monthly", "quarterly", or "as needed".
4. Enter the **Date Last Updated** in the "Date Last Updated" field. This is a required field and should be filled out with the date the inventory information was last updated.
5. (Optional) Add any additional **Notes** about the product or inventory issues in the "Notes" field.
6. Enter the **Prophets** name in the "Prophets" field. This field is for internal use and should be filled out with the name of the person responsible for the product.
7. Enter the **Phone** number of the person responsible for the product in the "Phone" field. This field is for communication purposes and should be filled out with the phone number of the person responsible for the product.
8. Enter the email address of the person responsible for the product in the "Email" field. This field is for communication purposes and should be filled out with the email address of the person responsible for the product.
9. Select the assigned **Tool** from the options provided in the "Assigned Tool" field. This field is used for internal purposes and should be filled out with the assigned tool or equipment.
10. (Optional) Enter any additional information about the assigned **Tool** 2 in the "Assigned Tool 2" field. This field is not required and should be filled out only if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (text, required): Enter the exact name of the product being tracked.
* **Product Stock Level** (number, required): Enter the current quantity of the product in stock.
* **Usage Cycle** (select multiple, required): Select the usage cycle of the product from the provided options.
* **Date Last Updated** (date, required): Enter the date the inventory information was last updated.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the product or inventory issues.
* **Prophets** (text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the product.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the product.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the product.
* **Assigned Tool** (select one, required): Select the assigned tool or equipment from the provided options.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about the assigned tool 2.
