# Vendor Sustainability Compliance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Sustainability Compliance Report Form is designed to gather information about a vendor's sustainability practices and certifications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you are authorized to fill out this form on behalf of your company.
2. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
3. For fields with multiple options (like sustainability practices), select all applicable options.
4. For fields with single-select options (like certifications), select only one option.
5. For uploaded documents, please ensure that you upload relevant and up-to-date documents.
6. Finally, submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, not required)
	+ This field is used to gather the vendor's contact information.
	+ Please fill out this field with your company's contact information.
* **Company Information** (Company Information, text, not required)
	+ This field is used to gather general information about the company.
	+ Please fill out this field with your company's information.
* **Sustainability Practices** (Sustainability Practices, select multiple, not required)
	+ This field allows you to select multiple sustainability practices that your company employs.
	+ Please select all applicable sustainability practices that your company is committed to.
* **Certifications** (Certifications, select one, required)
	+ This field is used to select a certification that your company holds.
	+ Please select only one certification that your company holds.
* **Documents** (Documents, text, required)
	+ This field is used to describe the uploaded documents that support your company's sustainability practices.
	+ Please type out a brief description of the documents you have uploaded.
* **Upload Documents** (Upload Documents, text, required)
	+ This field is used to upload supporting documents that demonstrate your company's sustainability practices.
	+ Please upload relevant and up-to-date documents that support your sustainability practices.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, not required)
	+ This field is used to add any additional notes or comments about your company's sustainability practices.
	+ Please use this field to add any additional information that may be helpful in assessing your company's sustainability performance.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required)
	+ This field is used to submit the form once you have completed all required fields.
	+ Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are authorized to fill out this form on behalf of your company.
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your company's information is up-to-date and consistent.
- For fields with multiple options, select all applicable options.
- For fields with single-select options, select only one option.
- For uploaded documents, please ensure that you upload relevant and up-to-date documents.
- Please submit the form once you have completed all required fields.
