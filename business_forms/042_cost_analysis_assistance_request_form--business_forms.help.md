# Cost Analysis Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to support cost analysis requests for projects. Please fill it out to provide a detailed overview of your project and its related costs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Project Details" section with the relevant information about your project, including its ID, name, and description.
2. If your project has any files attached that are relevant to your cost analysis request, upload them in the "File Attachments" section.
3. Answer the questions in the "Project Questions" section to provide context about your project's expected outcome, data use, and potential risks.
4. In the "Cost Review" section, provide information about the cost amount, frequency, and currency involved.
5. Finally, submit your request by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project ID** (`project_details_project_id`): A unique identifier for your project.
* **Project Name** (`project_details_project_name`): The name of your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_details_project_description`): A brief description of your project.
* **Files** (`file_attachments_files`): Attach any relevant files related to your project.
* **Expected Outcome** (`project_questions_question_0`): Describe the expected outcome of your project.
* **Data Use** (`project_questions_question_1`): Explain how you plan to use the data collected in your project.
* **Potential Risks or Obstacles** (`project_questions_question_2`): Identify any potential risks or obstacles that may affect your project.
* **Cost Amount** (`cost_review_cost_amount`): Enter the cost amount involved in your project.
* **Cost Frequency** (`cost_review_cost_frequency`): Select the frequency of the cost involved (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Cost Currency** (`cost_review_cost_currency`): Choose the currency of the cost involved (USD, EUR, JPY, CNY, INR).
* **Cost Description** (`cost_review_cost_description`): Provide a description of the cost involved.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`): Click this button to submit your request.
