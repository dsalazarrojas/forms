# Digital Asset Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Asset Release Form is a tool used to track and manage digital assets within the company. It helps ensure that assets are properly documented and released to prevent misuse or unauthorized use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Asset Owner field with the name of the individual or organization responsible for the digital asset.
2. Enter the name of the asset in the Asset Name field.
3. Specify the type of asset in the Asset Type field.
4. Enter the date the asset was created or used in the Date Used field.
5. Select all applicable permissions for the asset in the Permission field.
6. Enter the release date in the Release Date field.
7. Specify the release version number in the Release Version field.
8. Enter any relevant details about the asset's status in the Asset Status field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Asset Owner** (`asset_owner`, text, optional): Enter the name of the individual or organization responsible for the digital asset.
- **Asset Name** (`asset_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the digital asset.
- **Asset Type** (`asset_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of digital asset (e.g. "image", "video", "document", etc.).
- **Date Used** (`date_used`, date, optional): Enter the date the asset was created or used (YYYY-MM-DD format).
- **Permission** (`use_permission`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable permissions for the asset (e.g. "Internal Use", "External Use", "Public Use", etc.).
- **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the asset was released (YYYY-MM-DD format).
- **Release Version** (`release_version`, number, optional): Enter the version number of the asset that has been released.
- **Asset Status** (`asset_status`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the asset's status (e.g. "Available", "Released", "In Progress", etc.).
