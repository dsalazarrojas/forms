# popup-contact-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect contact information from customers or clients, enabling your business to communicate with them effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" or "Send" button to open the form in a pop-up window.
2. Enter your name in the "Contact Name" field.
3. If you want to provide your email address, enter it in the "Contact Email" field.
4. If you want to provide your phone number, enter it in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Review your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name (Contact Name)**: This is where you provide your name to be contacted. 
* **Contact Email (Contact Email)**: This is an optional field where you can provide your email address so we can contact you. 
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)**: This is an optional field where you can provide your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
