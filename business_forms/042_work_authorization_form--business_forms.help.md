# Work Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Authorization Form is used to collect information about a person's work authorization status, which includes their nationality, visa status, employment status, and other relevant details. This form is used for administrative or bureaucratic purposes, such as verifying a person's eligibility to work in a specific country or industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following fields with the requested information:

*   Enter your first name in the field labeled "first_name".
*   Enter your last name in the field labeled "last_name".
*   Enter your date of birth in the field labeled "date_of_birth".
*   Enter your country of birth in the field labeled "country_of_birth".
*   Enter your current country of residence in the field labeled "country_of_residence".
*   Select your current visa status from the options in the field labeled "current_visa".
*   Select the options that apply to you from the field labeled "authorized_to_work".
*   Select your employment status from the options in the field labeled "employment_status".
*   Enter your work authorization number in the field labeled "work_authorization_number".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **first_name (Text)**: Enter your first name.
*   **last_name (Text)**: Enter your last name.
*   **date_of_birth (Date)**: Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **country_of_birth (Text)**: Enter the country where you were born.
*   **country_of_residence (Text)**: Enter the current country you reside in.
*   **current_visa (Select One)**: Select your current visa status from the options provided.
*   **authorized_to_work (Select Multiple)**: Select the options that apply to you.
*   **employment_status (Select One)**: Select your current employment status from the options provided.
*   **work_authorization_number (Text)**: Enter your work authorization number.
