# gmp_audit_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for conducting a general management system (GMP) audit. It helps auditors assess an organization's adherence to GMP standards and guidelines by collecting data on various aspects of their management system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the company name and audit date to provide context for the audit.
2. Answer the questions regarding the audit type, regulatory body, and compliance status to ensure the audit is properly scoped and aligned with relevant regulations.
3. Assess the inspection status of the facility, equipment, personnel, procedures, documents, records, training and qualifications, regulatory control, deviations and complaints, product quality, and customer service. Select the appropriate status based on your observations.
4. Evaluate the cleanliness of the facility and the status of record retention, management systems, and review cycles.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`Company Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company being audited.
* **Audit Date** (`date_audit`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the audit.
* **Auditor Name** (`auditor_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the auditor.
* **Audit Type** (`audit_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of audit being conducted.
* **Regulatory Body** (`regulatory_body`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all regulatory bodies relevant to the audit.
* **Compliance Status** (`compliance_status`, select_one, required: false): Assess the compliance status of the organization with relevant regulations.
* **GMP Version** (`gmp_version`, text, required: false): Record the GMP version in use.
* **Facility Inspection Status** (`facility_inspection_status`, select_one, required: false): Assess the inspection status of the facility.
* **Equipment Inspection Status** (`equipment_inspection_status`, select_one, required: false): Assess the inspection status of equipment used in the facility.
* **Personnel Inspection Status** (`personnel_inspection_status`, select_one, required: false): Assess the inspection status of personnel working in the facility.
* **Procedure Inspection Status** (`procedure_inspection_status`, select_one, required: false): Assess the inspection status of procedures in place.
* **Document Review Status** (`document_review_status`, select_one, required: false): Assess the review status of documents.
* **Record Review Status** (`record_review_status`, select_one, required: false): Assess the review status of records.
* **Training and Qualifications** (`training_and_qualifications`, select_one, required: false): Assess the status of training and qualifications for personnel.
* **Regulatory Control** (`regulatory_control`, select_one, required: false): Assess the regulatory control in place.
* **Deviation and Complaints** (`deviation_and_complaints`, select_one, required: false): Assess the status of deviations and complaints.
* **Product Quality** (`product_quality`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate the product quality.
* **Product Presentation** (`product_presentation`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate the product presentation.
* **Packaging and Labeling** (`packaging_and_labeling`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate the packaging and labeling.
* **Shipping and Distribution** (`shipping_and_distribution`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate the shipping and distribution.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate the customer service.
* **Facility Cleanliness** (`facility_cleanliness`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate the facility cleanliness.
* **Record Retention** (`record_retention`, select_one, required: false): Assess the record retention status.
* **Management System** (`management_system`, select_one, required: false): Assess the management system in place.
* **Review Cycle** (`review_cycle`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate the review cycle status.

## Tips
- Be thorough when completing this form to ensure accurate and comprehensive data for the audit.
- If unsure of any status, select 'Unknown' or 'Not Applicable' as necessary.
- Review the form thoroughly before submitting it to avoid errors.
