# Step By Step Company Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about companies, which can be used for various business purposes such as registration, profiling, or other corporate needs. Fill out this form to provide accurate and up-to-date information about your company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the "company_name" field, which requires your company's name.
2. Next, provide your company's address in the "company_address" field.
3. If your company has a website, enter it in the "company_website" field.
4. Enter your company's email address in the "company_email" field.
5. Fill out the "company_phone" field with your company's phone number.
6. Provide your name and contact information in the "contact_name", "contact_phone", and "contact_email" fields.
7. Specify your contact position in the "contact_position" field.
8. Add any additional information about your company in the "additional_info" field.
9. Review the "terms_of_service" field, which is a note field for terms of service.
10. Choose your preferred service options from the "agreement" field.
11. Select the date and time you want to collaborate from the "collaboration", "date", and "time" fields.
12. Finally, select the type of company and partnership you have in the "company_type", "partnership_type", "service_request", and "inquiry_type" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **company_name** (text): Enter your company's name.
* **company_address** (text): Enter your company's address.
* **company_website** (text): If your company has a website, enter the URL.
* **company_email** (email): Enter your company's email address.
* **company_phone** (text): Enter your company's phone number.
* **contact_name** (text): Enter your name and title.
* **contact_phone** (text): Enter your personal phone number.
* **contact_email** (email): Enter your personal email address.
* **contact_position** (text): Specify your position in the company.
* **additional_info** (text): Add any additional information about your company.
* **terms_of_service** (note): Review and acknowledge the terms of service.
* **agreement** (select_multiple): Choose all preferred service options (Option A, Option B, or Option C).
* **date** (date): Select the date you want to collaborate.
* **time** (time): Select the time you want to collaborate.
* **collaboration** (select_one): Select the type of collaboration (Option ประก, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **company_type** (select_one): Select the type of company (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **partnership_type** (select_one): Select the type of partnership (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **service_request** (select_one): Select the service request (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **inquiry_type** (select_one): Select the inquiry type (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
