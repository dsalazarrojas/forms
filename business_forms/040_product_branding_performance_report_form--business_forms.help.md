# product_branding_performance_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "product_branding_performance_report_form" is a form designed to collect information about the performance of a product's branding efforts. This form helps marketing and product teams to evaluate and report on the effectiveness of a product's branding strategies and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the product's current branding performance and consider its strengths and weaknesses.
2. Answer each question in a clear and concise manner, providing specific examples or anecdotes to support your responses.
3. Be thorough but concise in your answers, avoiding unnecessary details or repetitive information.
4. Use the form's sections and questions as a guide to ensure you cover all essential aspects of the product's branding performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **product_branding_performance_review** (`product_branding_performance_review`, text, required/optional): 
This section is for a brief overview of the product's current branding performance. Please provide a brief description of your experience with the product's branding.
* **product_branding_performance_strengths** (`product_branding_performance_strengths`, text, required/optional): 
List the product's branding strengths, such as its unique selling proposition, brand identity, or messaging consistency.
* **product_branding_performance_weakness** (`product_branding_performance_weakness`, text, required/optional): 
Identify the product's branding weaknesses, such as inconsistent messaging, lack of brand awareness, or poor customer engagement.
* **product_branding_performance_improvement_ideas** (`product_branding_performance_improvement_ideas`, text, required/optional): 
Share your ideas for improving the product's branding performance, such as rebranding, repositioning, or adjusting messaging.
* **product_branding_category** (`product_branding_category`, select_one, required/optional): 
Choose the category that best describes the product's branding, such as Brand Name, Product Name, Tagline, or Logo.
* **product_branding_key_message_points** (`product_branding_key_message_points`, text, required/optional): 
Explain the product's key message points, such as its unique value proposition or brand positioning.
* **product_branding_performance_rating** (`product_branding_performance_rating`, number, required/optional): 
Rate the product's branding performance on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **product_branding_performance_date** (`product_branding_performance_date`, date, required/optional): 
Enter the date when you last evaluated the product's branding performance.
* **product_branding_target_audience** (`product_branding_target_audience`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
Select the target audience(s) for the product's branding, such as Women's, Men's, Kids', Unisex, or Business.
* **product_branding_channel** (`product_branding_channel`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
Choose the channel(s) where you currently experience the product's branding, such as Social Media, Email, In-store, Online, or Offline.
* **product_branding_performance_notes** (`product_branding_performance_notes`, note, required/optional): 
Add any additional comments or notes about the product's branding performance.
* **product_branding_performance_email** (`product_branding_performance_email`, email, required/optional): 
Enter your email address for follow-up questions or additional information.
* **product_branding_performance_phone** (`product_branding_performance_phone`, text, required/optional): 
Enter your phone number for follow-up questions or additional information.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective when assessing the product's branding performance.
* Use specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
* Keep your answers concise and clear, avoiding jargon or overly technical terms.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have covered all essential aspects of the product's branding performance.
