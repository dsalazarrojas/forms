# Contact A Factory Efficiency Specialist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your contact details, factory information, and efficiency challenges. This data will help our factory efficiency specialists provide you with tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with your contact information, factory details, and efficiency challenges.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for your industry type, facility size, and specific challenges.
3. Clearly describe your efficiency challenges in detail.
4. Select your preferred consultation method and availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Company Name**: Name of your manufacturing facility.
* **Job Title**: Your role in the organization.
* **Email Address**: Business email address.
* **Phone Number**: Direct phone number.
* **Facility Location**: City and state of your facility.
* **Industry Type**: Select the type of manufacturing you do (e.g., Automotive, Electronics, etc.).
* **Facility Size**: Select the size of your workforce.
* **Primary Efficiency Challenges**: Select your main efficiency challenges.
* **Current Production Capacity Utilization**: Select the percentage of capacity currently used.
* **Efficiency Improvement Goals**: Select the improvements you target.
* **Project Timeline**: Select when you want to start improvements.
* **Budget Authority**: Select your decision-making authority level.
* **Previous Improvements**: Select if you have implemented efficiency programs before.
* **Specific Challenges Description**: Describe your efficiency challenges in detail.
* **Preferred Consultation Method**: Select how you prefer to consult.
* **Availability for Consultation**: Select your availability for consultation.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any other details about your needs.
