# Utility Account Consolidation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from the customer for the consolidation of their utility accounts. This form ensures that the customer is aware and consents to the consolidation of their accounts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information.
2. Indicate if you give authorization for account consolidation (yes or no).
3. Select the accounts you want to merge (yes or no).
4. Enter the dates of the accounts you want to merge.
5. Review your answers to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Customer Authorization**: Indicate if you are authorized for account consolidation by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Account to Merge**: Select "Yes" or "No" for each account you want to merge, indicating your consent.
* **Utility Account 1-4**: Enter the account details for each utility account you want to merge. This may include account numbers or other relevant information.
* **Date Merged**: Enter the date of the consolidation (if applicable) or a future date for the merge to occur. If you do not know the exact date, you can enter a date in the future (e.g., the current date + 30 days).
