# real_estate_buyer_consultation_checklist_sarah_desamours - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from the user to determine their loan requirements for a real estate transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Contact Information" section with your first and last name.
2. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the type of property from the "Property Type" dropdown list.
4. Select the loan term from the "Loan Term" dropdown list.
5. Enter the loan amount in the "Loan Amount" field.
6. Enter the interest rate in the "Interest Rate" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`general_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your first and last name.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to allow us to contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of property you are buying or selling, such as residential or commercial.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the property.
* **Property Value** (`property_value`, number, required/optional): Enter the value of the property.
* **Loan Requirement** (`loan_requirement`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of loan you are seeking, such as cash sale or loan.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, select_one, required/optional): Select the length of time you are willing to commit to the loan, such as 1-10 years.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of the loan you are seeking.
* **Interest Rate** (`interest_rate`, number, required/optional): Enter the annual interest rate of the loan.
* **Loan Type** (`loan_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of loan you are seeking, such as fixed rate or adjustable rate.
* **Collateral** (`collateral`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of collateral you will be using for the loan, such as cash or real estate.
* **Other Collateral** (`other_collateral`, text, required/optional): Enter any other collateral you may have.
* **Prepayment Clause** (`prepayment_clause`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the prepayment clause type, such as 1% per annum or 2% per annum.
* **Balloon Payment** (`balloon_payment`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the balloon payment type, such as one time lump sum or gradually increasing.
* **Collateral Type** (`collateral_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of collateral, such as first mortgager or other.
* **Payout Rate** (`prepayment_rate`, number, required/optional): Enter the prepayment rate.
* **Prepayment Term** (`prepayment_term`, select_one, required/optional): Select the prepayment term, such as 1-10 years.
* **Prepayment Type** (`prepayment_clause_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the prepayment type, such as flat or hybrid.
* **Balloon Payment Type** (`balloon_payment_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the balloon payment type, such as one time lump sum or gradually decreasing.
* **Other Collateral** (`other_collateral`, text, required/optional): Enter any other collateral you may have.
