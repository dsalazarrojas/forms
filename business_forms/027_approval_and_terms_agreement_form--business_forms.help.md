# Approval And Terms Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain approval and terms agreement from a signatory, which is typically a client, customer, or partner. The form is used to confirm their consent to the terms and conditions of a particular agreement or project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the signatory's information in the fields as requested.
2. Confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" for both "Do you accept the terms and conditions" and "I have read and understand all terms".
3. Select all applicable acknowledgments that apply to your situation.
4. Sign and date the form by entering the current date in the "Signature Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Signatory Information** (Note): This field is not required but provides a note for the signatory's information.
* **Full Name**: Enter the signatory's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Job Title or Position**: Enter the signatory's job title or position.
* **Email Address**: Enter the signatory's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the signatory's phone number.
* **Company or Organization**: Enter the signatory's company or organization name.
* **Agreement Details**: This field is not required but provides a note for agreement details.
* **Agreement Title**: Enter a brief title for the agreement.
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Do you accept the terms and conditions**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and understand all the terms and conditions.
* **I have read and understand all terms**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and understood all the terms.
* **Acknowledgments**: Select all applicable acknowledgments that apply to your situation. These are:
	+ Understand obligations
	+ Accept liability
	+ Agree to confidentiality
	+ Consent to monitoring
	+ Accept all terms
* **Signature Date**: Enter the current date to sign and date the form.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the agreement.
