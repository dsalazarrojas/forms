# Oto_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to input personal details, including contact information and demographic data. Please follow the instructions below to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Input your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Provide your address in the "address" field.
6. Enter the name of the city in the "city" field.
7. Choose your state of residence from the "state" field.
8. Input your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
9. Input the time of birth in the "time_of_birth" field.
10. Input any additional note in the "note" field.
11. Confirm your email address in the "email_confirmed" field.
12. Confirm your phone number in the "phone_confirmed" field.
13. Confirm your address in the "address_confirmed" field.
14. Confirm your state of residence in the "state_confirmed" field.
15. Confirm your first name in the "first_name_confirmed" field.
16. Confirm your last name in the "last_name_confirmed" field.
17. Confirm your date of birth in the "date_confirmed" field.
18. Confirm the time of birth in the "time_confirmed" field.
19. Confirm any additional note in the "note_confirmed" field.
20. Select a category for your registration (e.g., Food, Drink, Music, Theatre, Other) from the "category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, Email, Optional): Input your email address.
* **Phone** (4, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (5, Text, Optional): Provide your address.
* **City** (6, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the city.
* **State** (7, Select One, Optional): Choose your state of residence.
* **Date of Birth** (8, Date, Optional): Input your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (9, Time, Optional): Input the time of birth.
* **Note** (10, Note, Optional): Input any additional note.
* **Email Confirmed** (11, Email, Optional): Confirm your email address.
* **Phone Confirmed** (12, Text, Optional): Confirm your phone number.
* **Address Confirmed** (13, Text, Optional): Confirm your address.
* **State Confirmed** (14, Select One, Optional): Confirm your state of residence.
* **First Name Confirmed** (15, Text, Optional): Confirm your first name.
* **Last Name Confirmed** (16, Text, Optional): Confirm your last name.
* **Date Confirmed** (17, Date, Optional): Confirm your date of birth.
* **Time Confirmed** (18, Time, Optional): Confirm the time of birth.
* **Note Confirmed** (19, Note, Optional): Confirm any additional note.
* **Category** (20, Select One, Optional): Select a category for your registration (e.g., Food, Drink, Music, Theatre, Other).
