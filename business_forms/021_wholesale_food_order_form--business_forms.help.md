# Wholesale Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for wholesale food orders. It consists of several sections that guide the customer through the order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the wholesale food order on page 1.
2. Provide details of your order on page 2.
3. Enter your payment information on page 3.
4. Confirm your order on page 4.
5. Add your contact information on page 5.
6. Enter the total value of your order on page 6.
7. (Optional) Fill in any additional information required on pages 7-11.
8. Finally, review and submit your order on page 12.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wholesale Food Order (Page 1)** (`wholesale_food_order`, text, optional): Enter the product(s) you wish to order in the wholesale quantity.
* **Order Details (Page 2)** (`order_details`, text, optional): Describe the specific items or products you are ordering.
* **Payment Information (Page 3)** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Enter your payment details, such as your payment method and amount.
* **Order Confirmation (Page 4)** (`order_confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm that you have placed the order correctly.
* **Contact Information (Page 5)** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
* **Order Total (Page 6)** (`order_total`, text, optional): Enter the total value of your order.
* **Additional Information 1-11 (Pages 7-11)** (`wholesale_food_order_form_page_7_`, `wholesale_food_order_form_page_10_`, `wholesale_food_order_form_page_8_`, `wholesale_food_order_form_page_9_`, `wholesale_food_order_form_page_11_`, text, optional): Enter any additional information related to your order.

Note: All fields are optional and can be filled in or left blank as per the customer's preference. However, it is essential to provide accurate and clear information to ensure a smooth order process.
