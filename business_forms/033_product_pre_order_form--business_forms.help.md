## Product Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Product Pre Order Form is designed to help customers pre-order products before they are available for sale. By filling out this form, customers can express their interest in purchasing a product and provide important details about their preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the product name you are interested in.
2. Choose a shipping method that suits your needs (Ground, Two-Day, or One-Day).
3. Select a payment option (Bank, PayPal, or Cash).
4. Enter the quantity you wish to purchase.
5. Provide your email address for communication and updates.
6. Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
7. Select the date and time you would like to receive your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the product you are interested in purchasing.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, options: Ground, Two-Day, One-Day): Choose the shipping method you prefer. This will affect the estimated delivery date and time.
* **Payment Option** (`payment_option`, select_one, options: Bank, PayPal, Cash): Select your preferred payment method. This will influence the payment process and any associated fees or costs.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the number of products you wish to purchase.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and communication about your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact and confirmation of your order details.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Choose the time you would like to receive your order.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have entered your email address and phone number correctly to ensure smooth communication and follow-up contact.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your order, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review and confirm your order details with you before processing your payment.
* Please note that available quantities and shipping times are subject to change; we will notify you of any changes.
* You may be contacted by our sales team to clarify any missing or incorrect information.
