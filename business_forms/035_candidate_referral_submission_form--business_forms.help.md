# Candidate Referral Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide us with information about a potential candidate for an open position. This form is used to refer an individual who you believe has the skills and qualifications necessary to excel in our company. Your input will help us evaluate and contact the candidate for potential interviews.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about the candidate.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referrer Name**: (`referrer_name_field`, text, required) Your full name.
* **Referrer Email**: (`referrer_email_field`, email, required) The email address of the person submitting this form, used to contact you.
* **Referrer Phone**: (`referrer_phone_field`, text, optional) Your phone number.
* **Are you a current employee**: (`referrer_status`, select_one, required) Choose "True" if you are a current employee, or "False" if you are not.
* **Candidate Full Name**: (`referred_name`, text, required) The candidate's full name.
* **Candidate Email**: (`referred_email`, email, required) The candidate's email address, used to contact them.
* **Candidate Phone**: (`referred_phone`, text, optional) The candidate's phone number.
* **LinkedIn or Resume Link**: (`candidate_profile_link`, text, optional) A link to the candidate's professional profile or resume.
* **Position you are referring them for**: (`target_position_name`, text, required) The job title or position you believe the candidate is best suited for.
* **Relationship to the candidate**: (`relationship_description`, text, required) Explain how you know the candidate, e.g. colleague, friend, mentor, etc.
* **Candidate's top skills**: (`candidate_skills_summary`, text, required) List 2-3 of the candidate's most important skills.
* **Why do you recommend this candidate**: (`recommendation_rationale`, text, required) Explain why you think the candidate is well-suited for our team.
* **How well does the candidate fit our company culture**: (`culture_fit_rating_internal`, number, required) Rate the candidate's fit from 1 to 5.
* **I have obtained the candidate's consent**: (`candidate_sharing_consent`, select_one, required) Confirm that you have the candidate's consent to share their information.
* **Notification preference for referral status**: (`status_update_preference`, select_one, required) Choose your preferred method of notification (email, SMS, no notification).
* **Submission Date**: (`submission_date_field`, date, required) The date and time you submit this form.

Please ensure you have the necessary information and consent from the candidate before submitting this form.
