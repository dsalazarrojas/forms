**Customs Clearance Declaration Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is used to declare goods for customs clearance. It is required for all businesses importing or exporting goods, and must be completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure smooth customs clearance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your business name and contact information in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide a detailed description of the goods being declared, including their value and weight.
3. Enter the date of the customs declaration.
4. Enter any other relevant information as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customs Clearance Declaration** (`customs_clearance_declaration`, text, optional):
	+ A brief description of the goods being declared.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional):
	+ The name of your business or company.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional):
	+ The name of the contact person responsible for the declaration.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional):
	+ The email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional):
	+ The phone number of the contact person.
* **Declaration Date** (`customs_declaration_date`, date, optional):
	+ The date of the customs declaration.
* **Total Value** (`total_value`, number, optional):
	+ The total value of the goods being declared.
* **Goods Description** (`goods_description`, text, optional):
	+ A detailed description of the goods being declared.
* **Package Weight** (`package_weight`, number, optional):
	+ The weight of the package being declared.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as "required" in the form schema.
