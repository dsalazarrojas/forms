# Web Developer/Programmer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about web developers and programmers interested in applying for a job or collaboration opportunity. The form helps us understand your background, skills, and experience in web development and programming. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information on the first page.
2. Provide your contact information on the second page.
3. Select your relevant skills from the list on the third page.
4. List your current and past projects on the fourth page.
5. Describe your apps or projects that you've built and maintained on the fifth page.
6. Share your Github profile link on the sixth page.
7. Enter your website or blog URL (if applicable) on the seventh page.
8. Attach a link to your portfolio (if applicable) on the eighth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, not required): Please provide your full name and a brief introduction.
  - Enter your name and a short paragraph about yourself.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Share your email address and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
  - Enter your email address and phone number.
- **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, not required): Choose your relevant programming skills.
  - Select all the skills that you have proficiency in.
- **Projects** (`projects`, text, not required): List your current and past projects.
  - Enter a list of your projects with a brief description.
- **Apps** (`apps`, text, not required): Describe your apps or projects that you've built and maintained.
  - Enter a list of your apps or projects with a brief description.
- **Github Profile** (`github_profile`, text, not required): Share your Github profile link so we can review your code.
  - Enter your Github profile link.
- **Website** (`website`, text, not required): Share your website or blog URL (if applicable).
  - Enter your website or blog URL.
- **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, not required): Attach a link to your portfolio (if applicable).
  - Enter a link to your portfolio.
