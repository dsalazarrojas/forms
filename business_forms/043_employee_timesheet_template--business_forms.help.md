# Employee Timesheet Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Timesheet Template is a form designed to record employee work hours for accurate tracking, payroll, or time-off purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `start_date` field with the starting date of the work period.
2. Fill in the `end_date` field with the ending date of the work period.
3. Enter the total hours worked in the `total_hours` field.
4. Add any relevant notes about the work period in the `notes` field (optional).
5. Review and submit the form for accurate time tracking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **start_date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): The date when the employee's work period starts.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): The date when the employee's work period ends.
* **total_hours** (`total_hours`, number, required/optional): The total number of hours worked by the employee during the work period.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): A field for any additional information or comments about the work period.

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately record the start and end dates for each work period.
- Enter the actual total hours worked, taking into account any breaks or time-off.
- Use the `notes` field to provide context or details about the work period, if needed.
