# Circular Economy Lodging Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Circular Economy Lodging Program Registration Form is designed to gather information from businesses or property owners interested in participating in the program. This form will help us understand your business's specific needs and interests, and guide you through the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
* Ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting.
* Read and understand each field's purpose.
* Answer all fields truthfully and thoroughly.
* Save your progress frequently.
* Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business or Property Name** (`business_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your lodging establishment. This will be used to identify your business in the program.
* **Contact Person Full Name** (`contact_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the person filling out this form, typically a representative of your business.
* **Contact Person Title** (`contact_title`, Text, Required): Enter the title or position of the person filling out this form.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter the contact email address for your business or property.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter a valid phone number for your business or property.
* **Business Address** (`address`, Text, Required): Enter the street address of your lodging establishment.
* **Type of Lodging Property** (`property_type`, Select One, Required): Select the type of lodging property you have (e.g., Hotel, Motel, Resort, etc.).
* **Number of Rooms** (`number_rooms`, Number, Required): Enter the total number of rooms in your lodging establishment.
* **Average Occupancy Rate** (`occupancy_rate`, Select One, Required): Select the average occupancy rate of your lodging property (e.g., Under 50%, 50-60%, etc.).
* **Current Sustainability Initiatives** (`sustainability_initiatives`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose all the sustainability initiatives currently implemented by your business or property (e.g., Recycling program, Energy efficient lighting, etc.).
* **Current Waste Management Practices** (`waste_management`, Select One, Required): Select the current waste management practices of your lodging establishment (e.g., Comprehensive recycling, Basic recycling, etc.).
* **Primary Energy Source** (`energy_source`, Select One, Required): Select the primary energy source used by your lodging property (e.g., Grid electricity, Natural gas, Solar, etc.).
* **Why Are You Interested in This Program** (`program_interest`, Text, Required): Briefly explain why you are interested in participating in the Circular Economy Lodging Program.
* **What Are Your Sustainability Goals** (`goals`, Text, Required): Describe your business or property's sustainability goals and priorities.
* **What Challenges Do You Face** (`challenges`, Text, Required): Share any challenges or obstacles you face in achieving your sustainability goals.
* **Budget Available for Improvements** (`budget_available`, Select One, Required): Select the budget available for sustainability improvements (e.g., Under $5,000, $5,000-$25,000, etc.).
* **Implementation Timeline** (`timeline`, Select One, Required): Select when you would like to start implementing sustainability improvements (e.g., Immediately, Within 3 months, etc.).
* **Interested in Sustainability Certification** (`certification_interest`, Select One, Required): Indicate your interest in obtaining sustainability certification for your lodging establishment.
* **Need Staff Training** (`staff_training`, Select One, Required): Select whether you need staff training to support sustainability initiatives.
* **Any Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please share them here.
