# Cleaning Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information necessary for a cleaning proposal. It includes company information, client details, property characteristics, proposed services, pricing information, and terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will need to provide the following:

*   Select a company name and enter contact details
*   Fill in client details (name, organization, address, phone, and email)
*   Choose a type of property to be cleaned and enter approximate square footage
*   Select the type of cleaning service and frequency of service
*   List services included in the proposal
*   Enter base cleaning fee and additional service fees (if applicable)
*   Choose payment terms and accepted payment methods
*   Select preferred days and time of service
*   Choose a start date for service
*   Enter cancellation policy, satisfaction guarantee, and insurance coverage
*   Set an expiration date for the proposal
*   Sign the proposal with your company representative name and signature

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Company Information

*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, required): Enter your company's name
*   **Company Address** (`company_address`, required): Enter your company's business address
*   **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, required): Enter your company's phone number
*   **Company Email** (`company_email`, required): Enter your company's email address
*   **Company Website** (`company_website`, optional): Enter your company website URL

### Client Information

*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, required): Enter the client or organization name
*   **Client Organization** (`client_organization`, optional): Enter the client or organization name
*   **Client Address** (`client_address`, required): Enter the client's service address
*   **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, required): Enter the client contact phone number
*   **Client Email** (`client_email`, required): Enter the client contact email address

### Property Details

*   **Property Type** (`property_type`, required): Select the type of property to be cleaned
*   **Approximate Square Footage** (`square_footage`, required): Enter the size of the property
*   **Number of Rooms** (`number_of_rooms`, optional): Enter the total rooms or areas
*   **Number of Bathrooms** (`number_of_bathrooms`, optional): Enter the number of bathrooms if applicable

### Proposed Services

*   **Service Type** (`service_type`, required): Select the type of cleaning service
*   **Service Frequency** (`service_frequency`, required): Choose how often service will be provided
*   **Services Included** (`services_included`, required): Select all services included in this proposal
*   **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, optional): List any additional services
*   **Base Cleaning Fee** (`base_cleaning_fee`, required): Enter the standard cleaning fee per visit
*   **Additional Service Fees** (`additional_service_fees`, optional): List any additional fees
*   **Supplies Fee** (`supplies_fee`, optional): Enter the fee for cleaning supplies if applicable

### Pricing Information

*   **Total Cost Per Visit** (`total_per_visit`, required): Enter the total cost for each cleaning visit
*   **Estimated Monthly Cost** (`estimated_monthly_cost`, optional): Enter the total cost based on frequency
*   **Discount Applied** (`discount_applied`, optional): Enter any discounts being offered
*   **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, required): Choose when payment is due
*   **Accepted Payment Methods** (`payment_methods`, required): Select how the client can pay

### Proposed Schedule

*   **Preferred Days of Service** (`preferred_days`, required): Select the preferred days of service
*   **Preferred Time of Day** (`preferred_time`, required): Choose the time of day for service
*   **Proposed Start Date** (`start_date`, required): Enter when service can begin

### Terms and Conditions

*   **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, required): Describe your cancellation policy
*   **Satisfaction Guarantee** (`satisfaction_guarantee`, required): Describe your satisfaction guarantee
*   **Insurance Coverage** (`insurance_coverage`, required): Describe your insurance coverage
*   **Proposal Valid Until** (`proposal_valid_until`, required): Enter the expiration date of the proposal
*   **Additional Notes or Comments** (`additional_notes`, optional): Enter any other information

### Proposal Acceptance

*   **Client Acceptance** (`client_acceptance`, optional): Choose whether you accept this proposal
*   **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, optional): Enter client electronic signature
*   **Acceptance Date** (`acceptance_date`, optional): Enter the date of acceptance

### Proposal Submission

*   **Company Representative Name** (`company_representative`, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the proposal
*   **Company Representative Signature** (`company_representative_signature`, required): Enter the electronic signature of the company representative
*   **Proposal Valid Until** (`proposal_valid_until`, required): Enter the expiration date of the proposal
