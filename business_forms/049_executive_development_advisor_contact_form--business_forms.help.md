# executive_development_advisor_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The executive development advisor contact form is a tool to gather information from clients who are seeking executive development advice. This form captures essential details about the client, such as their contact information and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide your valid email address in the "Email" field, as we will use this to communicate with you.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Specify the service you are inquiring about in the "Service Inquired" field.
5. Select your preferred contact method in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
6. Choose the best time to contact you in the "Best Time to Contact" field.
7. Provide any additional information you think will help us better serve you in the "Additional Information" field.
8. Select the assigned agent you would like to work with from the "Assigned Agent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name**: Client Name (<client_name>, text, required: false) 
    Enter your name as it appears on your business card or documents.
- **Email**: Email (<email>, email, required: true) 
    Enter your valid email address. This is how we will communicate with you.
- **Phone**: Phone (<phone>, text, required: false) 
    Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Service Inquired**: Service Inquired (<service_inquired>, text, required: true) 
    Specify the service you are inquiring about. This will help us to better serve you.
- **Preferred Contact Method**: Preferred Contact Method (<preferred_contact_method>, select multiple, required: false) 
    Select one or more methods you would like to contact you, such as Phone, Email, or Text.
- **Best Time to Contact**: Best Time to Contact (<best_time_to_contact>, time, required: false) 
    Choose a time that works best for you to be contacted.
- **Additional Information**: Additional Information (<additional_information>, note, required: false) 
    Provide any additional information you think will help us to better serve you.
- **Assigned Agent**: Assigned Agent (<assigned_agent>, select one, required: false) 
    Select the assigned agent you would like to work with from the provided options.
