# Check Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Check Release Form is used to verify and release funds from an account holder's account. It ensures that all necessary information is collected to prevent errors and fraud in the check release process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Verify the provided information to ensure it is correct.
3. Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Processing Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who processed the check release.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee ID of the person who processed the check release.
* **Processing Date**: Enter the date the check was processed.
* **Check Number**: Enter the number of the check being released.
* **Check Amount ($)**: Enter the amount of the check being released.
* **Payer Legal Name**: Enter the name of the person or entity that wrote the check.
* **Payee Legal Name**: Enter the name of the person or entity receiving the check.
* **Identification Method Used**: Select the method used to identify the account holder (Driver License, Passport, State ID, or Existing Customer Database).
* **Deposit Account Number**: Enter the account number of the account to be credited with the check amount.
* **Funds Hold Applied**: Select the type of funds hold applied to the account (No Hold, Standard (2 Business Days), or Extended (5+ Business Days)).
* **Approval Status**: Select the approval status of the check release (Approved for Deposit, Flagged for Review, or Rejected).
* **Was a manager override required**: Select whether a manager override was required for approval.
* **Internal Audit Notes**: Enter any internal audit notes related to the check release process.
* **Digital Acknowledgment of Check Release**: Select the status of the digital acknowledgment of the check release (Processed and Verified, or Other).

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely before submitting the form for processing. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department for assistance.
