# Business Recovery Planning Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Recovery Planning Application Form is designed to help businesses affected by a disruption or impact recover and rebuild. The form gathers information about the business, its impact, and its recovery needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary information and permissions.
2. Complete each section of the form according to the instructions provided.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
4. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness.
5. Submit the form to your support team for review and support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Business Information Section
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): The official name of your business.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, text, required): The industry or sector of your business.
* **Years in Operation** (`years_operating`, number, required): The number of years your business has been operating.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): The full address of your business location.

### Contact Information Section
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): The name of the primary contact person.
* **Title or Position** (`contact_title`, text, required): The title or position of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): The email address of the primary contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): The phone number of the primary contact person.

### Impact Assessment Section
* **Type of Impact or Disruption** (`impact_event`, select_one, required): The type of impact or disruption that affected your business (e.g., Natural Disaster, Economic Crisis, etc.).
* **Date of Impact** (`impact_date`, date, required): The date when the disruption occurred.
* **Description of Impact** (`impact_description`, text, required): A description of how your business was affected.
* **Estimated Financial Impact** (`financial_impact`, number, optional): The estimated financial impact of the disruption (in dollars).

### Support Needs Section
* **Types of Support Needed** (`support_types_needed`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable support types (e.g., Financial Assistance, Business Consulting, etc.).
* **Immediate Recovery Needs** (`immediate_needs`, text, required): What you need right away to recover.
* **Expected Recovery Timeline** (`recovery_timeline`, select_one, optional): The expected recovery timeline.

### Recovery Plan Section
* **Recovery Strategy** (`recovery_strategy`, text, required): Your plan to recover and rebuild your business.
* **Current Employee Status** (`employee_status`, text, optional): The current status of your employees (e.g., active, laid off, etc.).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Any other relevant details about your business and its recovery needs.
