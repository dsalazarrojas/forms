# Process Efficiency Grant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Efficiency Grant Registration Form is used to register for a process efficiency grant. It helps the system administrators track the registration process and ensure that users do not register multiple times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 12/25/1995).
4. Select your business name from the drop-down menu in the "Business name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field.
6. Fill in your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
7. Provide a brief description of your registration in the "Description" field.
8. Select the tool assigned to you from the drop-down menu in the "Assigned tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Your first name is the first name you use to refer to yourself.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Your last name is the last name you use to refer to yourself.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Provide your date of birth in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 12/25/1995).
* **Business name** (`business_name`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your business name from the list of available options.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in the format of `username@domain.com`.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in the format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your registration.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Select the tool assigned to you from the list of available options.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your administrator for assistance.
* You can only register once for this grant, so make sure to fill in your details correctly.
