# travel Expense Pre-Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel Expense Pre-Approval Request Form is used to request pre-approval for business travel expenses. This form is typically used by employees to request approval for travel-related expenses before they incur them, ensuring that all necessary costs are accounted for and approved before the trip takes place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the option that best describes the purpose of your trip on **Page 1**.
2. Enter the destination of your trip in the **Destination** field.
3. Enter the start and end dates of your trip in the **Start Date** and **End Date** fields, respectively.
4. Provide the purpose of your trip in the **Purpose** field.
5. Enter the budgeted cost of your trip in the **Budgeted Cost** field.
6. Enter the actual cost of your trip in the **Actual Cost** field (if applicable).
7. Fill in the **Traveler Name**, **Traveler Email**, and **Traveler Phone** fields with your contact information.
8. Enter the approver's name, email, and phone number in the **Approver Name**, **Approver Email**, and **Approver Phone** fields.
9. Select the status of your request (pending, approved, or denied) in the **Status** field.
10. Enter the name of the travel agency you are using, if applicable.
11. Enter the flight number and departure city for your trip.
12. Enter the arrival city for your trip.
13. Enter any comments or notes from the traveler in the **Traveler Comments** field.
14. Enter any comments or notes from the approver in the **Approver Comments** field.
15. Add any additional comments or notes from the approver with their signature in the **Approver Comments** and **Approver Signature** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1**: Select the option that best describes the purpose of your trip.
* **Destination**: Enter the city or location of your destination.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you will begin your trip.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you will end your trip.
* **Purpose**: Briefly describe the purpose of your trip.
* **Budgeted Cost**: Enter the estimated cost of your trip, up to the nearest dollar.
* **Actual Cost**: Enter the actual cost of your trip, if different from the budgeted cost.
* **Traveler Name**: Enter your name as the traveler.
* **Traveler Email**: Enter your work email address.
* **Traveler Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Approver Name**: Enter the name of the person who will be approving your request.
* **Approver Email**: Enter the email address of the person who will be approving your request.
* **Approver Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who will be approving your request.
* **Status**: Select the status of your request (pending, approved, or denied).
* **Travel Agency**: Enter the name of the travel agency you are using.
* **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number associated with your trip.
* **Departure City**: Enter the city where you will depart from.
* **Arrival City**: Enter the city where you will arrive at.
* **Traveler Comments**: Enter any comments or notes from the traveler.
* **Approver Comments**: Enter any comments or notes from the approver.
* **Approver Signature**: Enter the signature of the person who approved your request.
* **Travel Agency Fee**: Enter any additional fees from the travel agency.
* **Tax Included**: Indicate whether tax is included in your trip costs.
