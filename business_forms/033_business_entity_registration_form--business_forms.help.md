# Business Entity Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering business information for various entities, such as sole proprietors, partnerships, corporations, and LLCs. It's essential to provide accurate and complete details to ensure proper registration and taxation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the form. This includes your business name, address, contact details, owner's name, and any relevant identification documents.
2. Fill in the form with the correct business name, DBA (if applicable), and business type (sole proprietor, partnership, corporation, or LLC).
3. Provide accurate date of incorporation and tax ID or EIN information.
4. Classify your primary industry and provide a brief business description.
5. Enter your owner's name, ownership percentage (if applicable), and Social Security Number (last four digits).
6. Enter your business address, city, state, and zip code.
7. Provide your business phone number, email address, and website (if applicable).
8. Estimate your annual revenue (if applicable).
9. Fill in the Number of Employees.
10. Review your form carefully to ensure all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, Text, required): Enter your business's legal name.
* **Doing Business As (DBA)** (`business_dba`, Text, optional): If your business operates under a different name, enter it here.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, Select One, required): Select your business structure (sole proprietor, partnership, corporation, LLC, non-profit, or other).
* **Date of Incorporation** (`incorporation_date`, Date, required): Enter the date your business was formed.
* **Tax ID or EIN** (`tax_id`, Text, required): Enter your federal tax ID or Employer Identification Number.
* **Primary Industry** (`industry_classification`, Text, required): Describe your main business activity.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, Text, required): Briefly explain what your business does.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, Text, required): Enter the full name of the owner.
* **Ownership Percentage** (`ownership_percentage`, Number, optional): Enter the owner's percentage of ownership.
* **Owner SSN (Last 4 Digits)** (`owner_ssn`, Text, optional): Enter the last four digits of the owner's Social Security Number.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, Text, required): Enter your business's physical address.
* **City** (`city`, Text, required): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State** (`state`, Text, required): Enter the state where your business is located.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, Text, required): Enter your business's zip code.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Website** (`website`, Text, optional): Enter your business website URL (if applicable).
* **Number of Employees** (`employees_count`, Number, required): Enter the total number of employees.
* **Estimated Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, Number, optional): Estimate your business's annual revenue (if applicable).

## Tips
- Double-check your form for any required fields before submitting.
- Ensure all information is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a professional.
