# Vendor Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a vendor. Please fill out this form to provide details about the vendor's contact information, services offered, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill out the contact information section with the vendor's details.
3. Enter the vendor's full name and email address in the corresponding fields.
4. Provide a phone number for the vendor.
5. Select the services offered by the vendor from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: This section is for the vendor's basic contact information.
* **Full Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the vendor.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person for the vendor.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person for the vendor.
* **Services Offered**: Select all the services offered by the vendor from the list. You can choose multiple services.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the information for accuracy before submission.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask the vendor for clarification.
* This form is for internal use, so please keep the information confidential.
* If you need to make changes to the form in the future, please update the fields accordingly.
