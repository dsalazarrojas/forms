# Business Service Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Service Agreement Form is designed to document the terms and conditions of services provided by a business to a client. It ensures that both parties have a clear understanding of the scope, timeline, and payment terms of the service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Service Provider Information section. Enter your business name, contact person, email, phone number, and business address.
2. Move on to the Client Information section, where you'll enter the client's name, contact person, email, and phone number.
3. In the Service Details section, provide a description of the services to be provided, a detailed breakdown of the scope of work, and the start and end dates of the service.
4. In the Pricing and Payment section, enter the total contract value and payment terms, including the payment schedule and method.
5. In the Terms and Conditions section, specify the key deliverables, project timeline, and support duration after completion.
6. Finally, confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes, I accept" and sign the agreement on the specified date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Provider Name**: Enter your business name.
* **Contact Person**: Enter your name as the contact person.
* **Email Address**: Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Business Address**: Enter your business address.
* **Client Name**: Enter the organization receiving services.
* **Client Contact Person**: Enter the client's contact person's name.
* **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Description of Services**: Briefly describe the services to be provided.
* **Scope of Work**: Provide a detailed breakdown of the deliverables and timeline.
* **Service Start Date**: Enter the date services begin.
* **Service End Date**: Enter the date services end.
* **Total Contract Value**: Enter the full cost of services.
* **Payment Terms**: Specify the payment schedule and milestones.
* **Payment Method**: If not required, enter the method of payment (e.g., bank transfer, invoice).
* **Key Deliverables**: List the essential deliverables.
* **Project Timeline and Milestones**: Outline the project timeline, including key dates and phases.
* **Support Duration After Completion**: Specify how long support will be provided after completion.
* **Termination or Cancellation Terms**: Outline the conditions under which this agreement can be terminated.
* **Agreement Acceptance**: Confirm agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Agreement Date**: Sign and date the agreement.

Note: This form is a general template and may need to be adapted or modified based on specific business needs. It is essential to ensure that both parties have a clear understanding of the services, scope, and terms before signing the agreement.
