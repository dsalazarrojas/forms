# Broker Dealer Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Broker Dealer Change Form is designed to help brokerage firms update their business information, including address, contact details, and other relevant changes. This form is used to notify regulatory bodies and ensure that the firm's information is up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure that all necessary sections are completed.
3. Attach any supporting documentation required for the changes being reported.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Firm Legal Name** (`firm_name`): Enter the full legal name of the brokerage firm.
* **DBA Name** (`dba_name`): Enter the doing business as name if different from the firm's legal name.
* **CRD Number** (`crd_number`): Enter the Central Registration Depository number.
* **SEC File Number** (`sec_file_number`): Enter the Securities and Exchange Commission file number (if applicable).
* **Street Address** (`street_address`): Enter the firm's primary business address.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city of the firm's business address.
* **State** (`state`): Enter the state of the firm's business address.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`): Enter the firm's ZIP code.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact_name`): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Title** (`primary_contact_title`): Enter the title or position of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address for official communications.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the firm.
* **Category of Change** (`change_category`): Select the category of change (e.g., address change, contact information change, etc.).
* **Effective Date of Change** (`change_effective_date`): Enter the date when the change takes effect.
* **Reason for Change** (`change_reason`): Explain the reason for the change.
* **Previous Street Address** (`previous_street_address`): Enter the firm's previous street address (if applicable).
* **Previous City** (`previous_city`): Enter the firm's previous city (if applicable).
* **Previous State** (`previous_state`): Enter the firm's previous state (if applicable).
* **Previous ZIP Code** (`previous_zip`): Enter the firm's previous ZIP code (if applicable).
* **Previous Contact Name** (`previous_contact_name`): Enter the previous contact name (if applicable).
* **New Street Address** (`new_street_address`): Enter the firm's new street address (if applicable).
* **New City** (`new_city`): Enter the firm's new city (if applicable).
* **New State** (`new_state`): Enter the firm's new state (if applicable).
* **New ZIP Code** (`new_zip`): Enter the firm's new ZIP code (if applicable).
* **New Contact Name** (`new_contact_name`): Enter the firm's new contact name (if applicable).
* **FINRA Notified** (`finra_notification`): Check if you have notified FINRA about the change.
* **SEC Notified** (`sec_notification`): Check if you have notified the SEC about the change.
* **State Regulator Notified** (`state_notification`): Check if you have notified the state regulator about the change.
* **Documents Attached** (`documents_attached`): Select any supporting documentation attached.
* **I Certify** (`certification_statement`): Check to certify that the information provided is accurate.
* **Authorized Signature** (`authorized_signature`): Enter the name of the authorized signatory.
* **Title** (`signature_title`): Enter the title of the authorized signatory.
* **Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date of submission.

## Tips
* Ensure that all attachments are complete and accurate before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully to avoid errors or omissions.
* If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the relevant documentation or contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
