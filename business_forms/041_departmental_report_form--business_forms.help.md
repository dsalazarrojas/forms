# Departmental Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from departmental teams for internal reporting and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your department from the department field.
2. List any internal issues that you'd like to report.
3. Upload any relevant documents related to your report.
4. Enter your manager's email address.
5. Enter your manager's phone number.
6. Select your manager's name from the list of options.
7. Enter the date and time of your report.
8. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **department**: This field asks you to select your department or team that you are reporting from. Please choose the department that is most relevant to your report.
* **internal issues**: This field is for listing any internal issues that you'd like to report. Please provide as much detail as possible so that your manager can understand the situation.
* **document_upload**: This field is for uploading any relevant documents related to your report. Please ensure that the file is in a compatible format (e.g., PDF, DOCX, JPEG).
* **manager_email**: This field is for entering your manager's email address. Please ensure that this is a valid email address.
* **manager_phone**: This field is for entering your manager's phone number. Please ensure that this is a valid phone number.
* **manager**: This field is for selecting your manager's name from the list of options. Please choose the manager who is most relevant to your report.
* **date**: This field is for entering the date of your report. Please use the standard date format (DD/MM/YY).
* **time**: This field is for entering the time of your report. Please use the standard time format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **notes**: This field is for adding any additional notes or comments. Please keep this field concise and focused on the most important information.
* **payroll**: This field is not currently used, but it's likely intended for reporting any payroll-related issues. Please check with your manager for further guidance.
* **issues**: This field is the same as "internal issues" and is used to list any internal issues that you'd like to report.
