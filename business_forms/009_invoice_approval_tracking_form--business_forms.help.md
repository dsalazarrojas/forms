# Invoice Approval Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Approval Tracking Form is a tool used for tracking and recording invoices, their status, and essential information related to their collection. It helps businesses in maintaining accurate financial records and avoiding missed payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus for "order_forms" and "tracking_statuses" fields to indicate the order type and the current status of the invoice.
2. Enter any essential information in the "essential_info" field as required.
3. Choose the "approved_by" field to indicate who approved the invoice.
4. Select the "organized_documentation" field to choose how the documentation is organized.
5. If more information is needed, enter it in the "essential_info_2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Forms** (`business_forms`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information related to the form type (e.g., purchase order, sales order, etc.).
* **Order Forms** (`order_forms`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information related to the order (e.g., order number, customer name, etc.).
* **Approval Forms** (`approval_forms`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information related to the approval process (e.g., approval date, approval status, etc.).
* **Tracking Statuses** (`tracking_statuses`, select one, optional): Select the current status of the invoice:
	+ Invoices Approved
	+ Invoices Rejected
	+ Invoices Pending
* **Essential Information** (`essential_info`, number, optional): Enter any numerical data essential for the invoice (e.g., invoice total, payment amount, etc.).
* **Collection Date** (`collection_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the collection was made or due.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, select one, optional): Select who approved the invoice:
	+ Manager
	+ Team Member
	+ External Partner
* **Organized Documentation** (`organized_documentation`, select multiple, optional): Select how the documentation is organized:
	+ Organized in a spreadsheet
	+ Organized in a database
	+ Other
* **Essential Information 2** (`essential_info_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional essential information related to the invoice.

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately select the correct status of the invoice to maintain up-to-date records.
- Enter accurate and detailed information for each field to ensure smooth invoice tracking and payment processes.
- Regularly review and update the form to reflect any changes in business operations or policies.
