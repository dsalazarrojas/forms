# Patent Application Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patent Application Approval Form is a critical component of the patent application process. It captures the necessary information required to process and review patent applications submitted for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Applicant Information section with the name and contact details of the applicant.
2. Provide the contact details of the person submitting the application.
3. Enter the application details, including the patent type, category, and status.
4. Select the patent filing country.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the application.
6. Attach any necessary documents or attachments.
7. Enter the email and phone number of the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_information`, text, required: false):
  Enter the name and contact details of the person submitting the patent application.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false):
  Enter the contact details of the person submitting the patent application.
* **Application Details** (`application_details`, text, required: false):
  Enter the application details, including the patent type, category, and status.
* **Patent Details** (`patent_details`, select_multiple, required: false):
  Select the patent type, category, and status from the options provided.
* **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, text, required: false):
  Enter any additional details about the submission.
* **Inventor Details** (`inventor_details`, text, required: false):
  Enter the details of the inventor(s) of the patent application.
* **Patent Filing** (`patent_filing`, select_one, required: false):
  Select the country where the patent will be filed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false):
  Add any additional notes or comments about the application.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, text, required: false):
  Attach any necessary documents or files related to the patent application.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
  Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false):
  Enter the phone number of the contact person.

Note: This form is not intended for external use, so please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete to avoid delays or rejection of the application.
