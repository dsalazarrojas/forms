# Shift Swap Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shift Swap Request Form is a tool for employees to request a shift swap with their colleagues. It helps employees adjust their schedules as needed, promoting work-life balance and team collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Request Date** (field 1) as the date you are requesting the shift swap for.
2. Enter the **Employee Name** (field 2) to identify yourself.
3. Select the **Old Shift** (field 3) to which you are currently assigned.
4. Select the **New Shift** (field 4) you wish to swap with.
5. If necessary, provide additional **Swap Details** (field 5) to support your request.
6. Choose a **Manager Approval** (field 6) who will review and approve or decline your request.
7. Set the **Submit Date** (field 7) as the date you are submitting the form.
8. Your **Manager Note** (field 8) will provide additional context or comments for the manager reviewing your request.

* **Request Date (1)**: The date for which you are requesting the shift swap.
* **Employee Name (2)**: Enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Old Shift (3)**: Select the shift you currently work. This field allows you to choose between:

	+ Option 2
* **New Shift (4)**: Select the shift you wish to swap with. This field allows you to choose between:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Swap Details (5)**: Provide any additional information or context about your shift swap request, if needed.
* **Manager Approval (6)**: Choose your manager's approval. Select between:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Submit Date (7)**: The date you are submitting your shift swap request.
* **Manager Note (8)**: Add any additional comments or notes for your manager to review.

Tips:

* Make sure to submit your request as soon as possible to avoid last-minute shifts swaps.
* Be clear and concise in your **Swap Details** field to facilitate manager review.
* Ensure that your manager's approval is selected correctly to guarantee prompt processing of your request.
