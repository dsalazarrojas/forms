# corporate_governance_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate governance quotation form is used to collect information for corporate governance quotations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Review the form for completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. company_id
* **company_id** (`number`, required: false): This field is used to identify the company associated with the quotation.
### 2. service_type
* **service_type** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the type of service provided by the company.
### 3. employee_name
* **employee_name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the employee providing the service.
### 4. employee_email
* **employee_email** (`email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the employee providing the service.
### 5. contact_number
* **contact_number** (`text`, required: false): Enter the contact number of the employee providing the service.
### 6. address
* **address** (`note`, required: false): Enter the address associated with the service.
### 7. quote_price
* **quote_price** (`number`, required: false): Enter the price of the quotation.
### 8. quote_terms
* **quote_terms** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional terms or conditions associated with the quotation.
### 9. agreement_terms
* **agreement_terms** (`text`, required: false): Enter the terms and conditions of the agreement.
### 10. signature
* **signature** (`text`, required: false): Enter the signature of the employee providing the service.
### 11. signature_date
* **signature_date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of the signature.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form for completeness before submission.
* Ensure that all entered information is correct and up-to-date.
* Save a copy of the completed form for record-keeping purposes.
