# Contract Preparation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Contract Preparation Form is used to collect information to prepare a contract between the company and an external entity. It helps to gather details about the requestor, the other party, and the contract terms to ensure that the contract is comprehensive and meets the necessary requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out your information, including your name, title, and department.
2.  Select the type of contract you are preparing.
3.  Fill out the details about the other party, including their name, type, address, and contact information.
4.  Provide details about the contract, including its value, duration, and type.
5.  Specify who needs to approve the contract and the urgency level of the request.
6.  Fill out any additional comments or information that may be relevant to the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requestor Name** (1, `text`, required): This is your name as the person requesting the contract.
*   **Requestor Title** (2, `text`, required): This is your title or position within the company.
*   **Department** (3, `select_one`, required): Select the department you belong to.
*   **Request Date** (4, `date`, required): This is the date the request is being made.
*   **Contract Type** (5, `select_one`, required): Select the type of contract you are preparing.
*   **Contract Title** (6, `text`, required): This is the name of the contract.
*   **Other Party Name** (7, `text`, required): This is the name of the other party involved in the contract.
*   **Other Party Type** (8, `select_one`, required): Select the type of the other party.
*   **Other Party Address** (9, `text`, required): This is the full address of the other party.
*   **Other Party Contact** (10, `text`, required): This is the contact information of the other party.
*   **Contract Value** (11, `number`, required): This is the total dollar amount of the contract.
*   **Contract Duration** (12, `select_one`, required): Select the length of the agreement.
*   **Effective Date** (13, `date`, required): This is the start date of the contract.
*   **Expiration Date** (14, `date`, not required): This is the end date of the contract (leave blank if not applicable).
*   **Auto Renewal** (15, `select_one`, required): Select whether the contract should auto-renew.
*   **Payment Terms** (16, `text`, required): Describe how payment will work.
*   **Deliverables** (17, `text`, required): Specify what will be delivered under the contract.
*   **Key Obligations** (18, `text`, required): List the main responsibilities under the contract.
*   **Special Terms** (19, `text`, not required): Include any unique conditions or terms.
*   **Template to Use** (20, `select_one`, required): Select the preferred template to use for the contract.
*   **Previous Contract Reference** (21, `text`, not required): Provide any previous contract reference if applicable.
*   **Approvals Needed** (22, `select_multiple`, required): Select who needs to approve the contract.
*   **Urgency Level** (23, `select_one`, required): Select how urgent the request is.
*   **Deadline** (24, `date`, required): This is the date by which the contract is needed.
*   **Additional Comments** (25, `text`, not required): Provide any additional comments or information about the contract.
