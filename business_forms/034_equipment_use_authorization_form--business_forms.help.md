# equipment_use_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment_use_authorization_form is designed to collect information from users regarding the use of equipment within the organization. This form is crucial for maintaining an accurate record of equipment usage and ensuring compliance with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your user information, including your name and ID (if applicable).
2. Provide details about the equipment being used, such as its description, model number, or other relevant information.
3. Enter the date on which you intend to use the equipment.
4. Specify the start and end times of usage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (1)**: This field collects your personal details, including your name and ID (if applicable). Enter your name as it appears on your identification or employee card.
* **Equipment Details (2)**: Provide a clear and concise description of the equipment being used. This may include its model number, serial number, or any other relevant details that can help identify the equipment.
* **Usage Date (3)**: Enter the date on which you plan to use the equipment. Use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD) to ensure accuracy.
* **Start Time (4)**: Specify the start time of equipment usage. Enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **End Time (5)**: Specify the end time of equipment usage. Enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

## Tips
- Ensure that you have reviewed the equipment use policy before filling out this form.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information provided in each field.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, refer to your supervisor or the equipment management team.
- Remember to submit the form before using the equipment to ensure that it is properly recorded and authorized.
