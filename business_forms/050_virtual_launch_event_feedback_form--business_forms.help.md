# Virtual Launch Event Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from attendees after a virtual or in-person launch event. It helps organizers understand the event's success, attendee satisfaction, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the event information to ensure you are filling out the correct form.
2. Select the type of event you attended by choosing "Virtual" or "In-person" from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the number of attendees you met or interacted with.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-10.
5. Check whether you consider the event successful or not.
6. Provide any specific areas for improvement you experienced during the event.
7. Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_type**: Select the type of event you attended from the dropdown menu. This helps us understand the type of event you experienced.
* **attendees_number**: Enter the number of attendees you met or interacted with during the event. This information will help us understand the event's reach and impact.
* **attendees_satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-10. This will help us understand how well the event met your expectations.
* **event_success**: Check whether you consider the event successful or not. This will help us evaluate the event's effectiveness.
* **area_for_improvement**: Share any specific areas that could be improved upon during the event. This will help us identify potential issues or opportunities for growth.
* **comment**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about the event. This will help us understand your thoughts and ideas about the event.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and thorough as possible when filling out the form.
* Double-check your answers to ensure they are accurate and complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the event organizers.
