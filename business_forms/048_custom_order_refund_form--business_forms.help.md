# Custom Order Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers who wish to request a refund for their custom orders. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to initiate the refund request process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form in one of two steps: **Customer Information**, **Refund Information**, and **Order Details**.
- Fill in the **Customer Information** step with the customer's details as requested.
- Fill in the **Refund Information** step by stating the reason for the refund and the amount of refund required.
- In the **Order Details** step, select the refund method and add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: This section is where you should enter the customer's name, address, and contact information. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Request Reason**: Describe the reason for the refund request in this field.
* **Refund Amount**: Enter the exact amount of refund required.
* **Refund Method**: Select one of the available refund methods (Cash or Credit) to indicate how you would like to receive the refund.
* **Order Number**: Enter the number of the order for which you are requesting a refund.
* **Refund Comments**: Add any additional comments or information related to the refund request in this field.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer service team for assistance.
- Refund requests will be processed as soon as possible after form submission.
