# Pricing Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pricing Inquiry Form is a tool for businesses to inquire about pricing for a specific product or service. This form helps to gather relevant information from the customer to facilitate a smooth pricing discussion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your business information, including your name and contact details.
2. Provide the name of the product or service you are inquiring about pricing for.
3. Indicate your preferred contact method (email, phone, or meeting).
4. Confirm your email address and phone number for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): Enter your business name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who will be handling the pricing inquiry.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number that we can contact you on.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the product or service you are inquiring about pricing for.
* **Price Point** (`price_point`, number, required: false): If you have a specific price point in mind, please enter it here.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): If you are inquiring about a quantity, enter the number here.
* **Date Required** (`date_required`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you require the product or service.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): If you have a preferred delivery time, enter it here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your pricing inquiry.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, select_one, required: false): Confirm your email address for communication.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, select_multiple, required: false): Confirm your preferred phone contact method (Home, Cell, or Work).
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or Meeting).
