# Short Stay Property Alliance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering properties for the Short Stay Property Alliance program. It is meant to collect information about property owners or their authorized representatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address, phone number, and address.
3. Select your language and timezone.
4. Check if you have a manager, and if so, provide their contact information.
5. Enter your property name and address.
6. Select your property's timezone.
7. Verify that all required fields are filled in before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address, e.g., `john.doe@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number, e.g., `123-456-7890`.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address, e.g., `123 Main St`.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city of your property, e.g., `New York City`.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state of your property, e.g., `New York`.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, required): Enter the zip code of your property, e.g., `10001`.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter the country of your property, e.g., `USA`.
* **Time Zone** (`timezone`, select_one, required): Select the timezone of your property, e.g., `Pacific Time Zone`.
* **Language** (`language`, select_multiple, required): Select the language you speak, e.g., `English`.
* **Manager** (`manager`, select_one, required): Check if you have a manager, and if so, select `Yes`.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter your manager's email address, e.g., `manager@example.com`.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter your manager's phone number, e.g., `123-456-7890`.
* **Manager Address** (`manager_address`, text, optional): Enter your manager's address, e.g., `123 Main St`.
* **Manager City** (`manager_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your manager's address, e.g., `New York City`.
* **Manager State** (`manager_state`, text, required): Enter the state of your manager's address, e.g., `New York`.
* **Manager Zip** (`manager_zip`, number, required): Enter the zip code of your manager's address, e.g., `10001`.
* **Manager Country** (`manager_country`, text, required): Enter the country of your manager's address, e.g., `USA`.
* **Property Name** (`property_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your property, e.g., `My Hotel`.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your property, e.g., `123 Main St`.
* **Property City** (`property_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your property, e.g., `New York City`.
* **Property State** (`property_state`, text, required): Enter the state of your property, e.g., `New York`.
* **Property Zip** (`property_zip`, number, required): Enter the zip code of your property, e.g., `10001`.
* **Property Country** (`property_country`, text, required): Enter the country of your property, e.g., `USA`.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your manager's and property's addresses, as they are also required fields.
* The timezone selection should match the timezone of your property's location.
* If you have a manager, make sure to enter their email address and phone number.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your administrator for assistance.
