# Club Director Candidate Profile Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for candidates who are interested in becoming the club director. It will help the selection committee evaluate their qualifications, experience, and vision for the club. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Describe your background and history with the club, including how long you've been involved.
3. List your previous roles or positions within the club.
4. Briefly describe your leadership experience and accomplishments.
5. Evaluate your club attendance record using the provided options.
6. Explain why you're qualified and what skills you bring to the role.
7. Select the key strengths you possess as a leader, such as communication and team building.
8. List any relevant professional qualifications, skills, or certifications.
9. Describe your educational background, including schools and degrees earned.
10. Outline your vision for the club as director and specific goals for your term.
11. Explain how you plan to address current challenges faced by the club.
12. Indicate your willingness to commit time and energy to the director role.
13. Confirm your availability and flexibility for the role.
14. Provide two professional references, with their names and contact information.
15. Add any additional information the selection committee should know.
16. Finally, sign and print your name to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Background Information** (4, note, required): Briefly describe your history with the club and any relevant experience.
* **Years of Club Involvement** (5, number, required): Enter the number of years you've been involved with the club.
* **Previous Club Roles or Positions** (6, text, required): List any positions you've held in the club in the past.
* **Leadership Experience** (7, text, required): Briefly describe your leadership experience and accomplishments.
* **Club Attendance Record** (8, select_one, required): Evaluate your attendance record as a member of the club.
* **Qualifications and Skills** (9, note, required): Explain why you're qualified for the role and what skills you bring.
* **Key Strengths as a Leader** (10, select_multiple, required): Select the leadership skills you possess, such as communication and team building.
* **Relevant Skills or Certifications** (11, text, required): List any relevant professional qualifications, skills, or certifications.
* **Education Background** (12, text, required): Describe your educational background, including schools and degrees earned.
* **Vision for the Club as Director** (14, text, required): Outline your vision for the club and its future direction.
* **Specific Goals for This Term** (15, text, required): Set concrete objectives for your term as director.
* **How Will You Address Club Challenges** (16, text, required): Explain how you plan to address current challenges faced by the club.
* **Time Commitment** (18, number, required): Enter the number of hours you can commit per week to the role.
* **Willing to Serve Full Term** (19, select_one, required): Confirm your willingness to serve for the full term.
* **General Availability** (20, select_one, required): Evaluate your availability for the role.
* **Reference 1 Name and Contact** (22, text, required): Provide the name and contact information of a professional reference.
* **Reference 2 Name and Contact** (23, text, required): Provide the name and contact information of another professional reference.
* **Additional Information** (24, text, required): Add any additional information you think the selection committee should know.
* **Candidate Signature** (25, text, required): Sign and print your name to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
