# Collective Bargaining Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Collective Bargaining Survey is designed to gather information on employees' opinions and experiences with their current Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA). The survey aims to collect data on various aspects of the CBA, including union membership, job satisfaction, and concerns about working conditions, wages, benefits, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
3. If you are a union member, select the corresponding option for "Union Membership Status".
4. If you are not a union member but eligible to join, select "Yes Member" for "Union Membership Status".
5. For "Years with Company" and "Years as Union Member" questions, select the corresponding option that best matches your experience.
6. For "How Familiar Are You with Current CBA" and other similar questions, select the option that best reflects your current level of understanding or satisfaction.
7. Please select all that apply for "Priority Issues for Next Negotiation" and "Additional Comments or Suggestions".
