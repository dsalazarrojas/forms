# Speed Meeting Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a speed meeting with team members or external contacts. It helps to collect essential information about the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the meeting date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
2. Select a time slot for the meeting.
3. Enter your name and email address.
4. Provide your phone number.
5. Specify the meeting location.
6. Set the meeting duration in minutes.
7. Enter a brief description of the meeting topic.
8. Add any additional meeting notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date (1)** (`meeting_date`, required: false): Enter the date of the meeting in "YYYY-MM-DD" format.
* **Meeting Time (2)** (`meeting_time`, required: false): Choose a time slot for the meeting.
* **Attendee Name (3)** (`attendee_name`, required: false): Enter your name and title.
* **Attendee Email (4)** (`attendee_email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Attendee Phone (5)** (`attendee_phone`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Meeting Location (6)** (`meeting_location`, required: false): Specify the location of the meeting.
* **Meeting Duration (7)** (`meeting_duration`, required: false): Set the meeting duration in minutes.
* **Meeting Topic (8)** (`meeting_topic`, required: false): Briefly describe the meeting topic.
* **Meeting Notes (9)** (`meeting_notes`, required: false): Add any additional meeting notes.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the meeting details before submitting the form.
* If you need to book a meeting with multiple attendees, please submit separate forms for each attendee.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the meeting administrator.
