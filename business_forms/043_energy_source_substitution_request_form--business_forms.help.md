This form is used to gather information about energy source substitution requests from customers. It helps the business process manage and handle these requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Customer Name** field with the name of the customer making the request.
2. Enter the **Customer Email** field with the customer's email address for communication purposes.
3. Provide the **Property Owner Name** and **Property Owner Phone** fields with the relevant information about the property related to the energy source substitution request.
4. Select the **Request Date** for the request.
5. In the **Description** field, provide a brief description of the substitution request.
6. In the **File Upload** field, attach relevant files or documents related to the request.
7. Select the **Energy Source Type** for the energy source being substituted.
8. In the **File Relevant Info** field, add any relevant information about the energy source.
9. Add **Notes** and **Customer Comments** for any additional comments or information.
10. Repeat steps 3-9 for any additional energy sources being substituted (if applicable).
