# Call For Abstract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Call For Abstract form is designed to collect information about a presentation or research abstract to be presented at a conference. It is intended to gather essential details about the presenter, including their contact information and presentation preferences. The form also requires an abstract text and keywords for categorization purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**. These details will help us contact you regarding your presentation.
2. Enter your **Title of Abstract** and provide a detailed **Abstract Text** of up to 500 words. This abstract will be used for review and categorization purposes.
3. Choose your **Track or Category** from the available options.
4. Select your preferred **Presentation Format** (Oral presentation, Poster, Workshop, Panel discussion, or No preference).
5. Choose your preferred **Presentation Duration** (15 minutes, 30 minutes, 45 minutes, 60 minutes, or Other).
6. Indicate your **Audio-Visual Needs** (Projector and screen, Microphone, Audio equipment, or None).
7. If applicable, enter the names of your **Co-Authors** in a comma-separated list.
8. Confirm that the work is **original, unpublished**, and agree to present if accepted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your professional email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Title of Abstract**: Enter a clear and concise title of your abstract.
* **Abstract Text**: Write a detailed abstract of up to 500 words that summarizes your presentation.
* **Keywords**: Enter up to 5 keywords separated by commas that describe your abstract.
* **Track or Category**: Choose the category that best fits your abstract (Research, Practice, Innovation, Case Study, or Other).
* **Preferred Presentation Format**: Select the format in which you would like to present (Oral presentation, Poster, Workshop, Panel discussion, or No preference).
* **Preferred Duration**: Select the duration you would like for your presentation (15 minutes, 30 minutes, 45 minutes, 60 minutes, or Other).
* **Audio-Visual Needs**: Indicate what equipment you need for your presentation (Projector and screen, Microphone, Audio equipment, or None).
* **Co-Authors**: If you have co-authors, enter their names in a comma-separated list.
* **I confirm this is original, unpublished work**: Confirm that the work you are presenting is original and unpublished.
* **I agree to present if accepted**: Agree to present your work if accepted by the conference committee.
* **Names of Co-Authors**: Only required if you have co-authors.
