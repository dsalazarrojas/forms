# digital_commerce_innovation_fund_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from businesses applying for the digital commerce innovation fund. It is used to gather details about the business, project, team, financials, and contact information of the applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your business details, including a brief description of your company and its history.
2. Describe your project, including its goals, objectives, and expected outcomes.
3. Introduce your team members, including their roles and experience.
4. Share your financial information, including your revenue model and funding requirements.
5. Specify your project location and status.
6. State your funding request and contact details.
7. Provide a description of your project.
8. Share your business experience and financial capacity.
9. Specify your project start and end dates.
10. Provide the size of your team and its formation date.
11. Identify your team lead role and their experience.
12. Finally, review and submit your application.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and submit your application carefully to avoid errors.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure it is accurate.
* Clearly describe your project and its goals.
* Provide realistic financial information.
* Ensure your team is well-structured and experienced.
* Be prepared to answer questions and provide additional information if required.
