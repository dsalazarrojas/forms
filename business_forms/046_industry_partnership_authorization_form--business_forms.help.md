# Industry Partnership Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about industry partnerships between your organization and external business partners. The form will help you understand the nature of the partnership, identify key stakeholders, and gather contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select your partnership category**: Choose the category that best describes the partnership, such as "Business", "Academic", or "Government".
2. **Enter the institution involved**: Type the name of the institution related to the partnership.
3. **List your business partners**: Enter the names of the business partners participating in the partnership.
4. **Provide contact information**: Enter the contact details for the business partner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category (Select Category)**: Choose the category that best describes the partnership type. This field is optional.
	+ Options: Business, Academic, Government
* **Institution**: Type the name of the institution related to the partnership.
* **Business Partners (List Business Partners)**: Enter the names of all business partners participating in the partnership.
* **Contact**: Enter the contact details for the business partner. This includes any additional relevant information for communication. 

Note: The category, institution, business partners, and contact information fields are all optional and can be completed or left blank as needed.
