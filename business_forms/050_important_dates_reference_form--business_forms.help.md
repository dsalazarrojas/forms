# important_dates_reference_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "important_dates_reference_form" is a simple form designed to collect and reference important dates associated with events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on each page (if the form has multiple pages).
*   Enter the event name, date, start time, end time, and location in the respective fields.
*   You can leave the "required" fields blank if you don't have the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Event Name** (Event Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the event. This field is meant to hold the title or name of the event that you wish to record.
*   **Event Date** (event_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the event in the format you prefer. Please note that this field accepts dates, which could include future or past dates.
*   **Start Time** (start_time, time, optional): Enter the start time of the event. This could be in 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM) or 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
*   **End Time** (end_time, time, optional): Enter the end time of the event. This could be in 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM) or 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
*   **Location** (location, text, optional): Enter the location of the event. This field is meant to capture the venue or place where the event took place, or is taking place.

## Tips
*   Ensure that you fill in all fields that apply to your event to maintain accurate and comprehensive information.
*   If you're unsure about the format or content of a field, try referencing the help documentation for further clarification.
*   Remember that the "important_dates_reference_form" is intended to be a simple reference guide, not a comprehensive survey tool.
