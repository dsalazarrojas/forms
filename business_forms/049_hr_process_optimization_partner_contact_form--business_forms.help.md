# HR Process Optimization Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Process Optimization Partner Contact Form is designed for businesses seeking to partner with the HR department for improvement of HR processes. It collects contact details, focuses on HR-related areas of interest, and outlines partnership goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your contact information in the "Contact Details" field.
2.  Select all relevant HR-related areas of interest in the "HR Focus Areas" field (e.g., HR, Recruitment, Training, Development, or Other).
3.  Indicate your partnership goals in the "Partnership Goals" field (e.g., Improve Operational Efficiency, Reduce Costs, Improve Compliance, or Other).
4.  Click "Submit" to send your completed form to the HR department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Details
*   **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required): This field collects your name, email address, and phone number to contact you for follow-up or any further discussion on potential partnership opportunities.

### HR Focus Areas
*   **HR Focus Areas** (`HR Focus Areas`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is for you to choose one or multiple areas of interest related to HR, such as Human Resources, Recruitment, Training, Development, or Other. Select all that apply to help us understand your areas of focus.

### Partnership Goals
*   **Partnership Goals** (`Partnership Goals`, `select_one`, required): This field is for you to specify your main goal for the partnership, such as improving operational efficiency, reducing costs, or improving compliance. Choose the option that best describes your main objective.

### Submit
*   **Submit** (`Submit`, `note`, required): Click this field to send your completed form to the HR department and initiate further discussion on potential partnership opportunities.

## Tips
*   Please be specific about your partnership goals and HR focus areas to help the HR department serve you better.
*   If you're unsure of your areas of interest or partnership goals, feel free to reach out to the HR department for guidance and assistance.
