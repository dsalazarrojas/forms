# Daily Activity Planner - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Activity Planner is a form designed to help you plan and organize your daily activities. This form collects information about your tasks, priority levels, and other relevant details to help you stay on track and manage your time effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of activity you will be performing: Task List or Notes.
2. Choose the priority level of your activity: High or Low.
3. Enter any additional Time Management information (optional).
4. Select the Date for your activity.
5. Enter the Time for your activity.
6. Provide any additional Notes about your activity (optional).
7. Enter your Email (optional).
8. Enter your Phone number (optional).
9. Enter any additional information in the Main Form fields (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Activity** (Select One): This field allows you to choose between Task List and Notes.
* **Task Priority** (Select One): This field helps you prioritize your activities as High or Low.
* **Time Management** (Text): This field is for any additional time-related information.
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date for your activity.
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time for your activity.
* **Notes** (Text): Add any additional notes about your activity (optional).
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Main Form 1-25** (Text): These fields are for any additional information you want to provide about your activity.
