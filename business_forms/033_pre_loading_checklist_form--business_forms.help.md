# Pre Loading Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Loading Checklist Form is designed to ensure that all necessary checks are performed before loading vehicles with goods or equipment. This form helps confirm critical aspects of the loading process, including arrival, equipment, location, and other related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the "Confirm Arrival" question by selecting "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the vehicle has arrived at the loading location.
2. For "Is The Vehicle Equipped", select all equipment types that apply to the loaded vehicle.
3. Enter the correct "Location Is Correct" in the text field to confirm the loading location.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Confirm Status" to indicate the current status of the vehicle.
5. Enter the loading time using the "Confirm Time" field in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
6. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Phone" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the loading process in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Email" field.
9. Enter the address of the loading location in the "Address" field.
10. Select the date of the loading event using the "Date" field.
11. Enter any final comments or messages about the loading process in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Confirm Arrival**: Select "Yes" if the vehicle has arrived at the loading location, or "No" otherwise.
* **Is The Vehicle Equipped**: Select all equipment types that are present in the loaded vehicle.
* **Location Is Correct**: Enter the correct location where the vehicle is being loaded.
* **Confirm Status**: Select "Yes" if the vehicle's status is as expected, or "No" otherwise.
* **Confirm Time**: Enter the loading time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the loading process.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the loading location.
* **Date**: Select the date of the loading event.
* **Message**: Enter any final comments or messages about the loading process.
