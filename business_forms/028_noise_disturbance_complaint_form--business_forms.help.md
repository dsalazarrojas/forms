Noise Disturbance Complaint Form - Help Guide
=====================================

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect complaints about noise disturbances in the community. It helps authorities to identify the source and severity of the noise and take necessary actions to resolve the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of noise that was disturbing the area. You can choose from "Noise from nearby construction", "Noise from a nearby business", or "Noise from a residential area".
2. Describe the noise disturbance in your own words.
3. Specify how much the noise affected you, on a scale from High to Low.
4. Provide the address or street name where the noise was happening.
5. Share your contact information, so that authorities can reach out to you for further questions or updates.
6. Report the date and time when the noise was disturbing you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Noise Type
* **Noise Type** (`noise_disturbance_form_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of noise that was disturbing the area. This will help authorities to understand the source of the noise.

### Noise Description
* **Noise Description** (`noise_disturbance_form_3`, `text`, required: false): Describe the noise disturbance in your own words. Try to be as detailed as possible so that authorities can understand the issue better.

### Noise Impact
* **Noise Impact** (`noise_disturbance_form_4`, `select_one`, required: false): Specify how much the noise affected you. Choose from High, Medium, or Low to indicate the severity of the issue.

### Location
* **Noise Disturbance Location** (`noise_disturbance_form_5`, `text`, required: false): Provide the address or street name where the noise was happening. This will help authorities to pinpoint the location of the disturbance.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`noise_disturbance_form_6`, `email`, required: false): Share your contact information, so that authorities can reach out to you for further questions or updates.

### Time of Disturbance
* **Time of Disturbance** (`noise_disturbance_form_7`, `date`, required: false): Report the date and time when the noise was disturbing you. This will help authorities to understand when the issue occurred.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form to help authorities understand the issue better.
* If you are reporting on behalf of someone else, make sure to provide their contact information as well.
* If you have any supporting evidence, such as photos or videos, feel free to attach them to the form.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
