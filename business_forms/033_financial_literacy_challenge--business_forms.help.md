# Financial Literacy Challenge - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Literacy Challenge is a form designed to help users improve their financial knowledge and decision-making skills. It collects personal and financial information to provide users with personalized financial recommendations and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name and last name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
5. If applicable, enter your monthly income and savings amounts in the "income" and "savings" fields.
6. Select your areas of financial interest from the "financial_knowledge" field.
7. Select multiple areas of financial interest from the "financial_knowledge_2" field.
8. If you have any specific time constraints, enter them in the "time" field.
9. If you have any additional information, enter it in the "note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name (text)**: Enter your first name.
* **email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **date_of_birth (date)**: Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **income (number)**: If applicable, enter your monthly income.
* **savings (number)**: If applicable, enter your current savings.
* **financial_knowledge (select_one)**: Select one area of financial interest.
*   - Financial Literacy: Basic financial knowledge, such as budgeting and investing.
*   - Financial Planning: Long-term planning and goal-setting.
*   - Financial Analysis: In-depth financial analysis and assessment.
*   - Investment Management: Wealth management and investment strategies.
* **financial_knowledge_2 (select_multiple)**: Select multiple areas of financial interest.
*   - Financial Planning: Long-term planning and goal-setting.
*   - Financial Analysis: In-depth financial analysis and assessment.
*   - Investment Management: Wealth management and investment strategies.
* **time (time)**: If applicable, enter specific time constraints.
* **note (note)**: Enter any additional information you would like to share.
