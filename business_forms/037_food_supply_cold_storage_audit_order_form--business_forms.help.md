# Food Supply Cold Storage Audit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Supply Cold Storage Audit Order Form is designed to collect data on cold storage audits for food supply chain management. This form will help in ensuring that products stored in cold storage facilities are maintained at safe storage conditions, which is critical for food safety and quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your input in the designated fields.
2. Select the date of the audit from the calendar picker.
3. Enter the current temperature reading in the temperature field.
4. Enter the current humidity reading in the humidity field.
5. Enter the storage location where the audit is being performed.
6. Add any notes or comments about the audit in the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input (<name: user_input>, <type: text>, <required: false>):** Enter any relevant input or information about the storage area being audited.
* **Date (<name: date, <type: date, <required: false>):** Enter the date of the audit using the calendar picker.
* **Temperature (<name: temperature, <type: number, <required: false>):** Enter the current temperature reading in the storage area.
* **Humidity (<name: humidity, <type: number, <required: false>):** Enter the current humidity reading in the storage area.
* **Location (<name: location, <type: text, <required: false>):** Enter the specific location within the storage facility where the audit is being performed.
* **Notes (<name: note, <type: note, <required: false>):** Enter any notes or comments about the storage conditions, any issues found, or recommendations for improvement.
