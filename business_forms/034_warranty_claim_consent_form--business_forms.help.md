# Warranty Claim Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warranty Claim Consent Form is a document that you'll need to fill out to submit a warranty claim to us. This form is used to gather essential information from you, our customer, to process and handle your claim efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information and any additional details you'd like to provide.
2. Ensure you have the correct Product Serial Number, as this is the key identifier for your product.
3. Select the correct Product Type to help us understand the nature of your issue.
4. Enter the date when you first noticed the issue (Issue Date).
5. Describe the details of the issue (Issue Details).
6. Sign the form digitally to confirm your identity and acknowledge that you are the rightful owner of the product.
7. Provide your contact information (Customer Name, Email, and Phone) so we can contact you regarding your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Warranty Claim Consent Form**: This is the title of the form, which you'll need to acknowledge before proceeding.
* **Product Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of your product as printed on the product or packaging. This is crucial for identifying your product and processing your claim.
* **Product Type**: Select the type of product that is experiencing issues (e.g., 'Yes' for yes, or 'No' for no, depending on whether the product has a warranty).
* **Issue Date**: Enter the date when you first noticed the issue with your product. This helps us track the timeline of your claim.
* **Issue Details**: Describe the problem or issue you're experiencing with your product in as much detail as possible.
* **Customer Signature**: Sign this form digitally to confirm that you are the rightful owner of the product and acknowledge that you've filled out the form accurately.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents or records.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address where we can contact you for updates on your claim.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if we need additional information or clarification.
