# Club Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register new or renewing members to a club or organization. Fill out the form to provide essential information required for your membership and any additional details we need to know about you and your family.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your registration date in the `Registration Date` field.
2. Fill in your `First Name` and `Last Name` fields.
3. Provide your `Email Address` and `Phone Number` for contact purposes.
4. Select the `Club of Interest` you're registering for.
5. Choose your `Registration Type` (e.g., New Member, Renewal, Guest, or Alumni).
6. Select your `Membership Level` (e.g., Basic, Standard, Premium, or Lifetime).
7. Enter the `Membership Fee` amount.
8. If applicable, select any events you're interested in participating in from the `Interested in Event Participation` field.
9. Choose your `Payment Method` (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, Check, Cash, or Bank Transfer).
10. If other family members are joining, provide their details in the `Additional Family Members` field.
11. If needed, mention any accessibility accommodations in the `Special Accommodations` field.
12. Let us know how you heard about us in the `How Did You Hear About Us` field (optional).
13. Finally, review and agree to the membership terms and conditions by selecting `I Agree` in the `I Agree to Membership Terms` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date**: Enter the date you're registering for the club. (Required)
* **First Name**: Enter your first name. (Required)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. (Required)
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes. (Required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. (Required)
* **Club of Interest**: Select the club or organization you're interested in joining. (Required)
* **Registration Type**: Choose your registration type (e.g., New Member, Renewal, Guest, or Alumni). (Required)
* **Membership Level**: Select your membership level (e.g., Basic, Standard, Premium, or Lifetime). (Required)
* **Membership Fee**: Enter the membership fee amount. (Required)
* **Interested in Event Participation**: Select any events you'd like to participate in (e.g., Monthly Meetings, Annual Gala, Workshops, Networking Events, or Volunteer Opportunities). (Optional)
* **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, Check, Cash, or Bank Transfer). (Required)
* **Additional Family Members**: If other family members are joining, provide their details here. (Optional)
* **Special Accommodations**: Mention any accessibility accommodations you need. (Optional)
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Let us know how you heard about us (e.g., Word of Mouth, Social Media, Website, Email, or Advertisement). (Optional)
* **I Agree to Membership Terms**: Review and agree to the membership terms and conditions. (Required)

By following this guide, you'll be able to complete the Club Registration form successfully and register yourself or your family to the club. If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to contact us.
