# Water Filtration Efficiency Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Filtration Efficiency Report Form is used to collect information on the performance of water filtration systems. This includes the water flow rate, pressure drop, and water temperature. The form is meant to be a quick and easy way to report the efficiency of these systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on "Page 1" to access the form fields.
2. Enter the water flow rate in the "Water Flow Rate" field.
3. Select the units for the flow rate from the "Flow Rate Units" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the water inlet pressure in the "Water Inlet Pressure" field.
5. Enter the pressure drop in the "Pressure Drop" field.
6. Enter the water temperature in the "Water Temperature" field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Water Flow Rate
* **Water Flow Rate (1)**: Enter the water flow rate of the filtration system. This is the volume of water that flows through the system per unit time (e.g., m3/h).

### Flow Rate Units
* **Flow Rate Units (2)**: Select the unit of measurement for the flow rate. Options include m3/h, gpm, and ft^3/s.

### Water Inlet Pressure
* **Water Inlet Pressure (3)**: Enter the pressure of the water inlet to the filtration system.

### Pressure Drop
* **Pressure Drop (4)**: Enter the pressure drop across the filtration system.

### Water Temperature
* **Water Temperature (5)**: Enter the temperature of the water entering the filtration system. Note: The unit of measurement is °C.

### Notes
* **Notes (6)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the filtration system's performance.

## Tips
* Be sure to select the correct unit of measurement for the flow rate.
* Enter all values in the correct unit of measurement.
* Keep the form concise and clear, but feel free to add any additional comments or explanations in the "Notes" field.
