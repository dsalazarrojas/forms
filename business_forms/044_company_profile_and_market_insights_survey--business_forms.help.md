# Company Profile And Market Insights Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your company's profile, market insights, and challenges. This data will help us gain a better understanding of your business and provide valuable insights to support your growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name in the **Company Name** field.
2. Enter your company website URL in the **Company Website** field.
3. Enter your name and title in the **Your Name** and **Your Title** fields, respectively.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email Address** field.
5. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field (optional).
6. Provide basic company information in the **Company Information** field (optional).
7. Enter the year your company was founded in the **Year Founded** field.
8. Enter your headquarters location in the **Headquarters Location** field.
9. Enter the number of locations your company has worldwide in the **Number of Locations** field.
10. Select the geographic presence of your company in the **Geographic Presence** field.
11. Choose the number of employees in the **Number of Employees** field.
12. Select the percentage change of your employee growth in the **Employee Growth (YoY)** field (optional).
13. Select the **Primary Industry** of your company.
14. Enter the sub-industry or specialization of your company in the **Sub-Industry/Specialization** field (optional).
15. Select the **Business Model** of your company.
16. Select the **Primary Revenue Model** of your company.
17. Choose the approximate revenue range of your company in the **Annual Revenue Range** field.
18. Select the percentage change of your revenue growth in the **Revenue Growth (YoY)** field (optional).
19. Select the **Market Share** of your company in the **Estimated Market Share** field (optional).
20. Choose your **Market Position** in the market.
21. Select the **Competitive Advantage** of your company.
22. Select the **Target Market** of your company.
23. Enter the approximate number of customers your company has in the **Approximate Number of Customers** field (optional).
24. Choose the percentage of customer retention rate of your company in the **Customer Retention Rate** field (optional).
25. Enter your **Net Promoter Score (NPS)** if known.
26. List the products or services offered by your company in the **Products/Services** field (optional).
27. Enter the number of product or service lines your company has.
28. Select the rate of new product or service introduction in the **New Product Introduction Rate** field (optional).
29. Identify the area of innovation focus of your company in the **Innovation Focus Areas** field (optional).
30. Select the **R&D Spending as % of Revenue** of your company.
31. List the areas of innovation focus of your company in the **Innovation Focus Areas** field (optional).
32. Identify the main market challenges faced by your company in the **Main Market Challenges** field.
33. Elaborate on the main market challenges faced by your company in the **Please elaborate on your main challenge** field (optional).
34. Identify the main growth opportunities of your company in the **Main Growth Opportunities** field.
35. Elaborate on the main growth opportunities of your company in the **Please elaborate on your main opportunity** field (optional).
36. Identify the key trends affecting your industry in the **Key Trends** field.
37. Provide your perspective on how technology is impacting your industry in the **Technology Impact** field (optional).
38. Enter the date of survey completion in the **Survey Completion Date** field.
