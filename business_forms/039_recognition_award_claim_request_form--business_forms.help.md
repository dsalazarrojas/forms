# recognition_award_claim_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The recognition award claim request form is used to request recognition and awards within the company. This form is used to submit requests for recognition and awards for work performed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the award type that best describes the reason for your request.
2. Provide a justification for your award request.
3. Drag-and-drop supporting documents relevant to your request and add any additional notes.
4. Enter the date and time you submitted the request.
5. Enter your employee ID and name.
6. Enter your manager's name, email address, and department.
7. Enter your contact email address and phone number.
8. Enter the department and team you belong to.
9. Enter your manager's email address, phone number, and title.
10. Select whether the manager is a team lead.
11. Enter your manager's location and team.
12. Enter the title of your manager.
13. Enter the recognition date.
14. Select the recognition status.
15. Add any additional notes.
16. Select whether you are the submitter of the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Award Type** (`award_type`, select_multiple): Select one or more types of awards that best describe the reason for your request.
* **Justification** (`justification`, select_one): Provide a brief explanation of why you are requesting recognition and an award.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text): Drag-and-drop supporting documents relevant to your request and add any additional notes to help explain your submission.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time): Enter the time you submitted this form (optional).
* **Name** (`name`, text): Enter your name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text): Enter your manager's name.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, text): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Manager Department** (`manager_department`, text): Enter your manager's department.
* **Location** (`location`, text): Enter your location.
* **Team** (`team`, text): Enter the team you belong to.
* **Manager E-mail** (`manager_email`, email): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, select_one): Select whether your manager is a team lead.
* **Manager Location** (`manager_location`, text): Enter your manager's location.
* **Manager Team** (`manager_team`, text): Enter your manager's team.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text): Enter your manager's title.
* **Recognition Award** (`recognition_award`, text): Enter the recognition award you are requesting.
* **Recognition Date** (`recognition_date`, date): Enter the date of the recognition award.
* **Recognition Status** (`recognition_status`, select_one): Select the recognition status of the award.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Add any additional notes to help explain your submission.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_one): Select whether you are the submitter of the request.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have the necessary supporting documents before submitting this form.
- Check that your manager's information is accurate before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you select the correct award type and justification for your request.
- Add any additional notes to help explain your submission.
