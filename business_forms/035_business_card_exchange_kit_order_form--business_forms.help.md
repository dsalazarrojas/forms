# Business Card Exchange Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to create and ship their custom business cards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Information**, including your full name, company name, position, email address, phone number, shipping address, city, state, and zip code.
2. Choose your **Kit Selection** from the available options: Starter Kit (50 cards), Professional Kit (100 cards), Executive Kit (250 cards), Premium Kit (500 cards), or Bulk Kit (1000 cards).
3. Select your preferred **Card Style** from the available options: Classic, Modern, Minimalist, Bold, Elegant, or Custom Design.
4. Choose your **Card Size** from the available options: Standard (3.5 x 2 inches), Square (2.5 x 2.5 inches), Rounded Corners, Mini (2 x 1.5 inches), or European (85 x 55 mm).
5. Select your preferred **Card Material** from the available options: Standard Cardstock, Premium Cardstock, Recycled Paper, Plastic, Metal, or Wood.
6. Choose your **Card Finish** from the available options: Matte, Glossy, Satin, Soft Touch, or Textured.
7. Select your **Print Sides** from the available options: Single Sided or Double Sided.
8. Choose your **Color Option** from the available options: Full Color, Black and White, or Spot Color.
9. Select any **Special Features** you'd like to add to your cards, such as QR Code, Foil Stamping, Embossing, Die Cut, Spot UV, or Rounded Corners.
10. Choose how you will **Provide Your Design** from the available options: I Have a Ready Design, Need Design Help, Use Template, or Custom Design Service.
11. If you have a custom design, describe how you will **Submit Your Design File**.
12. Enter the **Quantity** of kits you'd like to order.
13. Choose your **Rush Order** preference from the available options: No - Standard, Yes - 3 Day Rush, Yes - 2 Day Rush, or Yes - Overnight.
14. Select your **Delivery Preference** from the available options: Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, Overnight Shipping, or International Shipping.
15. Choose your **Payment Method** from the available options: Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Invoice.
16. If you have a **Promo Code**, enter it here.
17. Enter any **Special Instructions** you have, such as specific printing requirements or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your business card.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name as it appears on your business card.
* **Position**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where you'd like to ship your kit.
* **City**: Enter the city where you'd like to ship your kit.
* **State/Province**: Enter the state or province where you'd like to ship your kit.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code where you'd like to ship your kit.
* **Country**: Enter the country where you'd like to ship your kit.
* **Kit Selection**: Choose the quantity and type of kit you'd like to order.
* **Card Style**: Choose the style of your business card.
* **Card Size**: Choose the size of your business card.
* **Card Material**: Choose the material of your business card.
* **Card Finish**: Choose the finish of your business card.
* **Print Sides**: Choose whether you'd like to print on one or both sides of your card.
* **Color Option**: Choose the color scheme for your business card.
* **Special Features**: Choose any additional features you'd like to add to your business card.
* **Provide Your Design**: Choose how you will provide your custom design.
* **Submit Your Design File**: Describe how you will submit your design file, if applicable.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of kits you'd like to order.
* **Rush Order**: Choose whether you'd like to expedite your order.
* **Delivery Preference**: Choose your shipping method.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method.
* **Promo Code**: Enter any promotional code you have.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions or requests you have.
