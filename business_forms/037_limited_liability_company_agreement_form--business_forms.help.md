# Limited Liability Company Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a Limited Liability Company (LLC), a type of business structure that provides personal liability protection for its owners. It will gather data on the LLC's name, address, ownership structure, operational details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). All fields marked as required must be completed.
2.  Enter the LLC's name in the "Llc Name" field.
3.  Provide the LLC's address in the "Llc Address" field.
4.  Enter the name of the member or member(s) in the "Member Name" field.
5.  Specify the member's percentage or ownership share in the "Member Percentage" field (if applicable).
6.  For each additional Llc Owners field (e.g., "Llc Owners 2" to "Llc Owners 10"), select whether the LLC has that owner. This field is optional and only necessary if there are multiple owners.
7.  Enter the LLC's management structure (text) in the "Management Structure" field.
8.  Describe the profit distribution (text) in the "Profit Distribution" field.
9.  Specify the LLC's term (number of years) in the "Llc Term" field.
10. Enter the date the LLC started (date) in the "Llc Start Date" field.
11. Enter the date the LLC will end (date) in the "Llc End Date" field.
12. Enter any additional address information for the LLC (text) in the "Llc Address 2" field (if applicable).
13. For each additional Llc Owners field (e.g., "Llc Owners 2" to "Llc Owners 10"), select whether the LLC has that owner (if applicable).
14. Enter any additional notes about the LLC (text) in the "Llc Notes" field (if applicable).
15. Enter any other relevant information about the LLC (text) in the "Llc Other Information" field (if applicable).
16. Enter any notes about the member (text) in the "Member Notes" field (if applicable).
17. Select the status of the agreement (active/inactive) from the "Agreement Status" field.
18. Enter the date the agreement was signed (date) in the "Agreement Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Llc Name**: The name of the Limited Liability Company.
 	* Required field.
- **Llc Address**: The address of the Limited Liability Company.
 	* Required field.
- **Member Name**: The name of the member(s) of the LLC.
 	* Required field.
- **Member Percentage**: The percentage or ownership share of the member.
 	* Required if applicable (if multiple members exist).
- **Llc Owners**: Select whether the LLC has the specified owner.
 	* Optional field, only for multiple owners.
- **Management Structure**: A description of the LLC's management structure.
 	* Required field.
- **Profit Distribution**: A description of how the profit is distributed.
 	* Required field.
- **Llc Term**: The term of the LLC (number of years).
 	* Required field.
- **Llc Start Date**: The date the LLC started.
 	* Required field.
- **Llc End Date**: The date the LLC will end.
 	* Required field.
- **Llc Address 2**: Additional address information for the LLC.
 	* Optional field.
- **Llc Owners 2** to **Llc Owners 10**: Select whether the LLC has the specified owner.
 	* Optional fields for multiple owners.
- **Llc Notes**: Any additional notes about the LLC.
 	* Optional field.
- **Llc Other Information**: Any other relevant information about the LLC.
 	* Optional field.
- **Member Notes**: Any additional notes about the member.
 	* Optional field.
- **Agreement Status**: Select the status of the agreement (active/inactive).
 	* Required field.
- **Agreement Date**: The date the agreement was signed.
 	* Required field.
