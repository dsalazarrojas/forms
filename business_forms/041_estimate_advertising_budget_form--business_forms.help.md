# estimate_advertising_budget_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The estimate_advertising_budget_form is a template for estimating an advertising budget. It's a collection of fields that gather information about the budget's details, such as duration, budget amount, and allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the start and end dates of the estimated budget period.
2. Enter the total budget amount for the estimated period.
3. Select the frequency of the ad budget (e.g., "Yes" for weekly, monthly, etc.).
4. Choose the type of ad frequency (e.g., "Yes" for daily, weekly, etc.).
5. Select the channels for the ad budget (e.g., "Yes" for social media, email, etc.).
6. Choose the type of ad budget channel (e.g., "Yes" for video, image, etc.).
7. Select the type of budget (e.g., "Yes" for fixed, variable, etc.).
8. Choose the allocation of the ad budget (e.g., "Yes" for specific departments, etc.).
9. Select the target audience for the ad budget (e.g., "Yes" for males, females, etc.).
10. Choose the location for the ad budget (e.g., "Yes" for national, international, etc.).
11. Select the language for the ad budget (e.g., "Yes" for English, Spanish, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Estimate Budget** (`estimate_budget`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total budget amount for the estimated period.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the start date of the estimated budget period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the end date of the estimated budget period.
* **Ad Budget** (`ad_budget`, `number`, required: false): Enter the ad budget amount for the estimated period.
* **Ad Budget Frequency** (`ad_budget_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the frequency of the ad budget (e.g., "Yes" for weekly, monthly, etc.).
* **Ad Frequency Type** (`ad_frequency_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of ad frequency (e.g., "Yes" for daily, weekly, etc.).
* **Ad Budget Channel** (`ad_budget_channel`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the channels for the ad budget (e.g., "Yes" for social media, email, etc.).
* **Ad Budget Channel Type** (`ad_budget_channel_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of ad budget channel (e.g., "Yes" for video, image, etc.).
* **Budget Type** (`budget_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of budget (e.g., "Yes" for fixed, variable, etc.).
* **Ad Budget Allocation** (`ad_budget_allocation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the allocation of the ad budget (e.g., "Yes" for specific departments, etc.).
* **Ad Budget Target Audience** (`ad_budget_target_audience`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the target audience for the ad budget (e.g., "Yes" for males, females, etc.).
* **Ad Budget Location** (`ad_budget_location`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the location for the ad budget (e.g., "Yes" for national, international, etc.).
* **Ad Budget Language** (`ad_budget_language`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the language for the ad budget (e.g., "Yes" for English, Spanish, etc.).
