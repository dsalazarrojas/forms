# Employee Contract Review Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Contract Review Form is used to review and manage employee contracts. It helps administrators to keep track of employee contracts, including contract details, contract status, termination reasons, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields, such as contract number, contract date, and contract status.
3. Select the termination reason and termination status from the dropdown menus.
4. Provide additional comments and notes as necessary.
5. Review the form carefully before submitting.
