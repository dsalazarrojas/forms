# Specialty Products Insurance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Specialty Products Insurance Form is used to capture and store detailed information about a product's insurance needs. This form is specifically designed for products that require customized coverage and policy details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing the necessary product details.
2. Select the correct product type from the available options.
3. Specify the coverage location where the product will be used or stored.
4. Enter the coverage date and time.
5. Provide contact information for the product owner or contact person.
6. Enter the policy details, including amount, duration, and status.
7. Select the coverage type (e.g., property, liability, casualty).
8. Choose the coverage area.
9. Enter the coverage value.
10. Specify the policy effective and end dates and times.
11. Enter the policy end date and time.
12. Specify the coverage period.
13. Enter the policy amount.
14. Specify the coverage start date and time.
15. Enter a description of the policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your product** (Product Details): This field is used to describe the type of product being insured. Please be specific and provide a brief description.
* **Product Type** (Select One): Choose the type of product you are insuring. Options include Automobile, Aircraft, Home, and Other.
* **Coverage Location** (Select One): Indicate where the product will be used or stored. Options include Home, Business, and Other.
* **Coverage Date** (Date): Enter the date on which coverage begins.
* **Coverage Time** (Time): Enter the time on which coverage begins.
* **Contact Name** (Text): Provide the name of the person or organization contacting us.
* **Contact Phone** (Text): Enter the contact phone number for the person or organization.
* **Contact E-mail** (Email): Enter the contact email address.
* **Additional Information** (Note): Enter any additional information about the product or policy.
* **Policy Details** (Text): Provide a description of the policy, including any relevant details.
* **Policy Amount** (Number): Enter the amount of the policy.
* **Policy Duration** (Time): Enter the duration of the policy.
* **Policy Status** (Select One): Select the status of the policy. Options include Active, Inactive, and Pending.
* **Coverage Type** (Select Multiple): Choose the type of coverage required for the product. Options include Property, Liability, and Casualty.
* **Coverage Area** (Select One): Select the area where the product will be used or stored. Options include Home, Business, and Other.
* **Coverage Value** (Number): Enter the value of the coverage.
* **Policy Effective Date** (Date): Enter the date on which the policy becomes effective.
* **Policy End Date** (Date): Enter the date on which the policy ends.
* **Policy Effective Time** (Time): Enter the time on which the policy becomes effective.
* **Policy End Time** (Time): Enter the time on which the policy ends.
* **Coverage Period** (Time): Select the period of coverage.
* **Policy Amount** (Number): Enter the amount of the policy.
* **Coverage Start Date** (Date): Enter the date on which coverage begins.
* **Coverage Start Time** (Time): Enter the time on which coverage begins.
* **Policy Description** (Text): Enter a description of the policy.
