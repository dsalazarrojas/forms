# Team Collaboration Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Collaboration Check Out Form is a tool used to record and track team collaboration events, ensuring accurate documentation and reducing potential conflicts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for every team collaboration event, including check out events.
2. Enter the Team Name to identify the team that the resource belongs to.
3. Select the type of resource being checked out (Resources, Equipment, or Materials).
4. Enter the name of the resource being checked out.
5. Enter the quantity of the resource checked out.
6. Choose a Date Out for when the resource is checked out.
7. Choose a Time Out for when the resource is checked out.
8. Enter the User Name of the person checking out the resource.
9. Enter the User Department of the person checking out the resource.
10. Enter the Project Name if the collaboration event is related to a specific project.
11. Enter the Project Manager if the collaboration event is related to a project.
12. Enter the Team Size of the collaborating team.
13. Enter any relevant Notes about the collaboration event.
14. Choose the Check In Date when the resource is checked back in.
15. Choose the Check In Time when the resource is checked back in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, **optional**): Enter the name of the team that the resource belongs to.
* **Resource Type** (`resource_type`, select_one, **optional**): Select the type of resource being checked out (Resources, Equipment, or Materials).
* **Resource Name** (`resource_name`, text, **optional**): Enter the name of the resource being checked out.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, **optional**): Enter the number of resources being checked out.
* **Date Out** (`date_out`, date, **optional**): Choose the date when the resource is checked out.
* **Time Out** (`time_out`, time, **optional**): Choose the time when the resource is checked out.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, **optional**): Enter the name of the user checking out the resource.
* **User Department** (`user_department`, text, **optional**): Enter the department of the user checking out the resource.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, **optional**): Enter the name of the project related to the collaboration event, if applicable.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, text, **optional**): Enter the name of the project manager, if applicable.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, **optional**): Enter the size of the team collaborating.
* **Location** (`location`, text, **optional**): Enter the location of the collaboration event.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, **optional**): Enter any additional notes about the collaboration event.
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, date, **optional**): Choose the date when the resource is checked back in.
* **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`, time, **optional**): Choose the time when the resource is checked back in.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to fill out every field to ensure accurate documentation.
* Double-check the dates and times to ensure they are correct.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your team's collaboration guidelines or ask your team lead for guidance.
