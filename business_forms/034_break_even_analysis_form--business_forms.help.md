# Break Even Analysis Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Break Even Analysis Form is a tool used to determine the minimum price of a product or service that a company must charge to break even, which is the selling price at which the total revenue from sales equals the total costs (fixed and variable) of production and sales.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your business information, including your business name, contact name, and email.
2. Provide the date of the analysis you are performing.
3. Enter the name of the product or service being analyzed.
4. Enter the selling price per unit and the variable cost per unit for the product or service.
5. Enter the total fixed costs for the month, including any additional costs such as rent, salaries, utilities, insurance, marketing, and other costs.
6. Enter the breakdown of the fixed costs, listing each category and its corresponding cost.
7. Select the stage of your business (Startup, Early Growth, Established, or Expanding).
8. Select the industry your business operates in (Retail, Manufacturing, Services, Technology, Food and Beverage, Healthcare, or Other).
9. Select the purpose of the analysis (New Product Launch, Business Planning, Pricing Decision, Investment Decision, Loan Application, or Regular Review).
10. Enter any additional notes you want to include in the analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for analysis results.
* **Analysis Date** (`analysis_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the analysis.
* **Product or Service Name** (`product_service_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product or service being analyzed.
* **Selling Price Per Unit** (`selling_price_per_unit`, number, required): Enter the price you charge per unit.
* **Variable Cost Per Unit** (`variable_cost_per_unit`, number, required): Enter the cost to produce each unit.
* **Total Fixed Costs Monthly** (`fixed_costs_monthly`, number, required): Enter the total monthly fixed expenses.
* **Fixed Costs Breakdown** (`fixed_costs_breakdown`, text, required): List each fixed cost category and its corresponding cost.
* **Rent or Lease** (`rent_lease`, number, optional): Enter the monthly rent amount (if applicable).
* **Salaries and Wages** (`salaries`, number, optional): Enter the monthly payroll costs (if applicable).
* **Utilities** (`utilities`, number, optional): Enter the monthly utility costs (if applicable).
* **Insurance** (`insurance`, number, optional): Enter the monthly insurance premiums (if applicable).
* **Marketing Costs** (`marketing_costs`, number, optional): Enter the monthly marketing budget (if applicable).
* **Other Fixed Costs** (`other_fixed_costs`, number, optional): Enter any additional fixed expenses (if applicable).
* **Current Sales Volume** (`current_sales_volume`, number, optional): Enter the current units sold per month (if applicable).
* **Target Sales Volume** (`target_sales_volume`, number, optional): Enter the goal for units sold per month (if applicable).
* **Business Stage** (`business_stage`, select_one, required): Select the stage of your business (Startup, Early Growth, Established, or Expanding).
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select the industry your business operates in (Retail, Manufacturing, Services, Technology, Food and Beverage, Healthcare, or Other).
* **Purpose of Analysis** (`analysis_purpose`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of the analysis (New Product Launch, Business Planning, Pricing Decision, Investment Decision, Loan Application, or Regular Review).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional relevant information.
