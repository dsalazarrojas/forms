# Corporate Housing Operations Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Housing Operations Contact Form is a tool used by the Corporate Housing Operations team to collect contact information and inquiries from clients. This form is essential for our team to understand client needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by filling out the "Client Name" field with the correct name of the person or company you are contacting us about.
2.  Next, enter the email address of the client in the "Email" field. This should be the email address we will use to communicate with them.
3.  Enter the client's phone number in the "Phone" field. This will help us reach out to them for further discussion or clarification.
4.  Select the type of inquiry from the dropdown menu in the "Type of Inquiry" field. This will help us understand the nature of their question or concern.
5.  Finally, type in your message to the client in the "Message" field. Be as detailed as possible to ensure we can address their issue accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name** (Client Name, text, required): Enter the name of the person or company you are contacting us about.
*   **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter the email address we will use to communicate with the client.
*   **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
*   **Type of Inquiry** (Type of Inquiry, select_one, required): Select the type of inquiry from the dropdown menu.
	+ Option 1: General Inquiry
	+ Option 2: Reservation or Accommodation Question
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify)
*   **Message** (Message, text, required): Type in your message to the client, being as detailed as possible to ensure accurate understanding of their inquiry.
