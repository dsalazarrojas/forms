# mock-interview-evaluation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "mock-interview-evaluation-form" is designed to collect feedback on mock interviews, allowing users to provide an assessment of the candidate's performance, the duration of the interview, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the "Company Name" field with the name of the company where the mock interview took place.
2. Enter the date of the mock interview in the "Interview Date" field.
3. Select the time of the mock interview from the "Interview Time" field.
4. Enter the duration of the mock interview in the "Interview Duration" field.
5. Provide feedback and suggestions in the "Comments and Suggestions" field.
6. Evaluate the candidate's performance for each of the 17 mock interview questions, using the corresponding "Mock Interview Score" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (text, required)**: Enter the name of the company where the mock interview took place.
* **Interview Date (date, required)**: Select the date of the mock interview.
* **Interview Time (time, required)**: Select the time of the mock interview.
* **Interview Duration (number, required)**: Enter the duration of the mock interview.
* **Comments and Suggestions (note, required)**: Provide any feedback or suggestions for improvement.
* **Mock Interview Questions**: Evaluate the candidate's performance for each of the 17 mock interview questions using the corresponding "Mock Interview Score" fields.
  * **Mock Interview Score 1-17 (number, optional)**: Evaluate the candidate's performance for each question on a scale of 1-17.
