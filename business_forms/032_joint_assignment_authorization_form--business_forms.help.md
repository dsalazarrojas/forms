# Joint Assignment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document the authorization and effective dates of a joint assignment between two parties. It ensures that both parties are aware of their responsibilities and agree to the terms of the assignment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the name of Party One and their address in the respective fields.
2. Enter the name and address of Party Two.
3. Enter the effective start and end dates of the assignment.
4. Confirm that Party One has consented to the assignment by selecting "Yes" in the dropdown menu.
5. Confirm that Party Two has consented to the assignment by selecting "Yes" in the dropdown menu.
6. If necessary, enter the date and sign of Party One and Party Two.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_header**: This is a note field that serves as a header for the form.
* **party_one_name**: Enter the name of Party One.
* **party_one_address**: Enter the address of Party One.
* **party_one_phone**: Enter the phone number of Party One.
* **party_two_name**: Enter the name of Party Two.
* **party_two_address**: Enter the address of Party Two.
* **party_two_phone**: Enter the phone number of Party Two.
* **assignment_effective_date_start**: Enter the effective start date of the assignment.
* **assignment_effective_date_end**: Enter the effective end date of the assignment.
* **party_one_consent**: Confirm that Party One has consented to the assignment by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **party_two_consent**: Confirm that Party Two has consented to the assignment by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **signature_date**: Enter the date of the signature (if applicable).
* **party_one_signature**: Enter the signature of Party One (if applicable).
* **party_two_signature**: Enter the signature of Party Two (if applicable).
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the assignment.

## Tips

* Please ensure that both parties have read and understood the terms of the assignment before completing this form.
* If either party has questions or concerns, please contact the relevant department or person for clarification.
* This form is not intended to be used for any other purpose than documenting the authorization and effective dates of a joint assignment.
