# Contact Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Information Collection Form is a crucial step in our registration process that provides us with essential details about you, allowing us to tailor our communication and services to your specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and enter your full name, email address, and phone number accurately.
2. Choose the registration type you are interested in (e.g. Upcoming Event, Resource Download).
3. Select all the areas of interest that apply to you (e.g. Technology, Business, Science, Art, Education).
4. Choose your preferred contact method (e.g. Email, SMS, Phone).
5. Accept the terms for receiving communications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on any official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address to receive important communications from us.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can reach you via phone calls or SMS.
* **Organization / Company** (`organization`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a business or organization, enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a business or organization, enter your job title.
* **Registering for** (`registration_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the reason for registering (e.g. Upcoming Event, Resource Download).
* **Areas of Interest** (`interest_areas`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the areas of interest that apply to you.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you prefer to be contacted (e.g. Email, SMS, Phone).
* **I agree to receive communications** (`terms_acceptance`, `select_one`, required): Accept the terms for receiving communications from us.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can contact you effectively.
* Select only relevant registration types and areas of interest to avoid being contacted unnecessarily.
* Choose your preferred contact method carefully to match your preferences.
* Accepting the terms is mandatory for this form submission.
