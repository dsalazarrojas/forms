# Kristine's Knockout Nails Ordering Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your nail preferences for a customized nail service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Answer each field according to your preferences.
3. Fill in the nail length and color fields by selecting the desired options.
4. In the nail quantity field, type in the number of nails you would like.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **knockout_nails_form**: This field is a text field where you can provide any additional information about your nail preferences.
* **nail_length**: This field is a select field where you can choose whether you want a long or short nail length.
* **nail_color**: This field is a select field where you can choose multiple colors for your nail color preference.
* **nail_quantity**: This field is a text field where you can type in the number of nails you would like. For example, if you want 10 nails, you would type "10".
