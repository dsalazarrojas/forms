# Tax Deduction Detail Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Deduction Detail Request Form is a document used to request tax deductions for specific business expenses. It is a crucial tool for employees to obtain approvals from relevant team members for reimbursement and tax deduction purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined below:

1. Ensure you have not already received approval for the expense you are requesting tax deduction for.
2. Select the correct request date from the calendar.
3. Choose the type of request (Accounting Firms, Finance Teams, or Individuals).
4. Provide any supporting documents related to the expense.
5. Enter detailed information about the request in the text field.
6. Select the current status of the request (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
7. Choose the relevant team member to assign the request to.
8. Select the review date from the calendar.
9. Enter any comments regarding the request (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you first submitted the request.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of request (Accounting Firms, Finance Teams, or Individuals).
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, required: false): Upload any relevant documents or files related to the expense.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text, required: false): Provide detailed information about the request, including the expense amount, purpose, and any other relevant context.
* **Request Status** (`request_status`, select_one, required: true): Choose the current status of the request (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Assignee** (`assignee`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the team member you would like to assign the request to.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required: false): Choose the review date for the request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback regarding the request (optional).

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Upload only relevant supporting documents to facilitate quick review and processing.
- Clearly provide detailed information about the request for faster approval.
- If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to reach out to your manager or the assigned team member.
