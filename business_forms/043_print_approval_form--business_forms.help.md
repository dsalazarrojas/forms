# print_approval_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The print approval form is used for customers to approve or disapprove print jobs, including weddings, and provide additional comments or feedback. The form allows customers to indicate their approval status and add comments or notes to the print job.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the approval status for the print job from the options provided. You can choose from "Wedding", "Not Approved", or "Pending".
2. Choose the customers who have approved or disapproved the print job, if any. You can select multiple customers from the options provided.
3. Select the print status of the job, whether it has been printed or not.
4. Enter any comments or additional notes about the print job.
5. Enter the customer's name.
6. Enter the customer's email address.
7. If you need to contact the customer further, select their phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Approve Printings** (Wedding, Not Approved, Pending):
	+ This field allows you to select the approval status of the print job. Choose the option that best describes the current state of the print job.
* **Customer Approval** (Customer 1, Customer 2, Customer 3):
	+ This field allows you to select the customers who have approved or disapproved the print job. You can choose one or multiple customers from the options provided.
* **Print Status** (Printed, Not Printed):
	+ This field is used to indicate whether the print job has been printed or not.
* **Comments**:
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback about the print job.
* **Customer Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the customer who is approving or disapproving the print job.
* **Customer Email**:
	+ Enter the email address of the customer who is approving or disapproving the print job.
* **Customer Phone** (Phone, Not Contacted):
	+ If you need to contact the customer, select their phone number. If you do not need to contact them, select "Not Contacted".
* **Notes**:
	+ Enter any additional notes or information about the print job.
