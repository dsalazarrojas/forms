# Warranty Claim Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warranty Claim Information Form is used to collect information from customers or clients who are making a warranty claim against a product or service provided by the company. This form helps the company understand the reason for the claim, gather details about the product or service in question, and gather any supporting documents or attachments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "First name" and "Last name" fields.
2. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
4. Describe the product or service in question in the "Product information" field.
5. Briefly explain the reason for your warranty claim in the "Reason for claim" field.
6. Provide a detailed description of your issue with the product or service in the "Description" field.
7. Add any supporting details or information that will help the company understand your claim in the "Claim details" field.
8. If you have attached any documents or images that support your claim, select "Claim attachments" and upload them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached regarding your claim.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you have a phone number, enter it to help us contact you regarding your claim.
* **Product information** (`product_info`, text, required: false): Describe the product or service in question, including the product code, model number, or any other relevant details.
* **Reason for claim** (`reason_for_claim`, text, required: false): Briefly explain the reason why you are making a warranty claim.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your issue with the product or service.
* **Claim details** (`claim_details`, text, required: false): Add any supporting details or information that will help us understand your claim.
* **Claim attachments** (`claim_attachments`, text, required: false): Select and upload any documents or images that support your claim.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible in your responses.
- If you are unable to find the product information, please contact our support team.
- If you have any supporting documents or images, please make sure they are clear and readable.
