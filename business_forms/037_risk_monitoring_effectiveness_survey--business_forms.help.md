# risk_monitoring_effectiveness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The risk monitoring effectiveness survey is a tool used to evaluate the success or effectiveness of risk monitoring activities within an organization. It collects information about contact details, risk types, impact, and mitigation strategies to provide a comprehensive assessment of risk monitoring processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary contact details.
2. Select the relevant risk type and frequency of the risk.
3. Assess the risk impact on the organization.
4. Provide a risk score for the risk.
5. Enter any relevant mitigation strategies.
6. Describe the follow-up plan and frequency.
7. Indicate if the risk has been reviewed and by whom.
8. Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Header**: This field is a note field where you can add any additional information about the survey.
* **Contact Details**: Please enter your contact information, including name, email, and phone number. This information will be used to reach out to you for follow-up discussions.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your official email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Department**: Please select whether you are in a department that monitors risk.
* **Position**: Enter your position or role within the department.
* **Department (2)**: This field seems to be a redundant question and can be skipped. However, if you must enter a department, please select it from the options provided.
* **Risk Type**: Select the type of risk that requires monitoring.
* **Risk Frequency**: Select how frequently this risk occurs or is likely to occur.
* **Risk Impact**: Assess the potential impact of the risk on the organization.
* **Risk Score**: Assign a numerical score to the risk, representing its level of severity.
* **Mitigation Control**: Describe any existing mitigation strategies or controls in place to mitigate the risk.
* **Mitigation Description**: Provide a detailed description of the mitigation strategies.
* **Follow-up Plan**: Describe any follow-up actions to be taken on this risk.
* **Follow-up Frequency**: Indicate how frequently follow-up actions should be taken.
* **Next Action**: Enter the next action required based on the follow-up plan.
* **Reviewed By**: Indicate if the risk has been reviewed by someone in the organization.
* **Risk Reviewed By**: Select the person who reviewed the risk.
* **Reviewed By Date**: Enter the date when the risk was reviewed.
* **Reviewed**: Indicate if the risk has been reviewed again.
* **Risk Reviewed By 2**: Select the person who reviewed the risk again.
* **Risk Monitoring Effectiveness**: Evaluate the effectiveness of the risk monitoring process.
* **Overall Monitored**: Indicate if the risk is overall monitored.
