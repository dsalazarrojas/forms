# Parking Cost Estimator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parking Cost Estimator form is designed to help users estimate the cost of parking in different cities. This form is intended to be a quick and easy way to calculate the parking cost, providing users with an estimate based on their input.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the location where you will be parking from the list provided.
2.  Enter the total number of hours you will be parking.
3.  Enter the total number of days you will be parking.
4.  Choose whether the parking is free or paid.
5.  Enter the parking rate per hour.
6.  You will be provided with an estimated total parking cost.
7.  You can also enter the estimated number of hours and days you will be parking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Parking Location** (`parking_location`, select_one, Optional): Select the location where you will be parking. This will help the form provide accurate estimates based on the location's parking rate.
*   **Parking Hours** (`parking_hours`, number, Optional): Enter the total number of hours you will be parking. This will help the form estimate the parking cost based on the hours you will be parking.
*   **Parking Days** (`parking_days`, number, Optional): Enter the total number of days you will be parking. This will help the form estimate the parking cost based on the days you will be parking.
*   **Parking Rate** (`parking_rate`, select_one, Optional): Choose whether the parking is free or paid. This will help the form provide an accurate estimate of the parking cost.
*   **Parking Fee** (`parking_fee`, number, Optional): Enter the parking rate per hour. This will help the form calculate the total parking cost based on the parking rate and the number of hours you will be parking.
*   **Total Parking Cost** (`parking_cost`, number, Optional): This is the estimated total parking cost based on your input.
*   **Estimated Parking Hours** (`estimated_parking_hours`, number, Optional): Enter the estimated number of hours you will be parking. This will help the form provide a more accurate estimate of the parking cost.
*   **Estimated Parking Days** (`estimated_parking_days`, number, Optional): Enter the estimated number of days you will be parking. This will help the form provide a more accurate estimate of the parking cost.

## Tips
*   Make sure to select the correct location and enter the correct hours and days to get an accurate estimate of the parking cost.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can always contact us for assistance.
*   The estimated total parking cost is provided based on the input you have entered, so make sure to double-check your answers.
