# Company Profile Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your company for submission. Please fill out this form to provide your company's details for inclusion in our directory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company name in the `Company name` field.
2. Enter the name of the contact person in the `Contact person name` field.
3. Type in the contact email in the `Contact email` field.
4. You can optionally add your company's website URL in the `Website` field.
5. Provide a brief summary of your company's description in the `Company description` field.
6. List your company's key products or services in the `Key products or services` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, required, text): This is the official name of your company.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`, required, text): The name of the representative for this company submission.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required, email): This is the email address we will use for follow-up communication.
* **Website** (`website`, optional, text): Your company's website URL (if you have one).
* **Company Description** (`company_description`, required, text): A brief summary of your company for our directory listing.
* **Key Products or Services** (`key_products`, optional, text): A short list of your key products or services.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can process your submission correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
