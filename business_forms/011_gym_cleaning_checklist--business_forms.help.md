# Gym Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Cleaning Checklist is a form used to assess the cleanliness and emergency preparedness of a gym. This guide will help you understand what each field is intended to capture and how to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are in the designated area for inspection (e.g. the gym itself, or a specific section within the gym).
2. Review each field carefully and answer accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Each field is designed to gather specific information about your assessment. Here's what each field is intended to capture:

* **Bathroom Areas** (`bathroom_areas`, text, required): Describe the condition of the bathrooms, including cleanliness and any maintenance needs.
* **Locker Rooms** (`locker_rooms`, text, optional): Note any concerns or issues with locker rooms.
* **Common Areas** (`common_areas`, text, required): Evaluate the overall cleanliness and organization of common areas such as the lobby or main workout area.
* **Exercise Equipment** (`exercise_equipment`, text, required): Inspect and report on the state of exercise equipment, including cleanliness and functionality.
* **First Aid Kits** (`first_aid_kits`, text, optional): Identify the location and accessibility of first aid kits.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (`fire_extinguishers`, select_one, optional): Indicate if fire extinguishers are present and in working order.
* **Safety Signs** (`safety_signs`, text, required): Verify that safety signs are posted and visible in designated areas.
* **Exit Signs** (`exit_signs`, text, required): Confirm that exit signs are posted and functioning properly.
* **Emergency Exit Signs** (`emergency_exit_signs`, text, required): Check that emergency exit signs are clearly posted.
* **Fire Doors** (`fire_doors`, text, optional): Note the condition and accessibility of fire doors.
* **Emergency Exit Routes** (`emergency_exit_routes`, text, optional): Map out emergency exit routes.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Provide contact information for emergency situations.
* **Emergency Exit Signs in Dark** (`emergency_exit_signs_in_dark`, text, optional): Check if exit signs are visible in low-light conditions.
* **Fire Extinguisher Locations** (`fire_extinguisher_locations`, text, optional): Document the location of fire extinguishers.
* **Emergency Exit Signs on Stairs** (`emergency_exit_signs_on_stairs`, text, optional): Verify exit signs are posted on stairs.
* **First Aid Kits Locations** (`first_aid_kits_locations`, text, optional): Identify the location of first aid kits.
* **Emergency Exit Signs Outside** (`emergency_exit_signs_outside`, text, optional): Confirm exit signs are posted near exits.
* **Fire Suppression System** (`fire_suppression_system`, text, optional): Evaluate the state of the fire suppression system.
* **Safety Hazards** (`safety_hazards`, text, optional): Report any safety hazards or concerns.
* **Emergency Exit Signs at Floor** (`emergency_exit_signs_at_floor`, text, optional): Check if exit signs are posted on each floor.
* **Emergency Exit Signs on Doors** (`emergency_exit_signs_on_doors`, text, optional): Verify exit signs on doors.
* **Exit Routes from Stairs** (`exit_routes_from_stairs`, text, optional): Document exit routes from stairs.
* **Emergency Exit Signs in Rooms** (`emergency_exit_signs_in_rooms`, text, optional): Check for exit signs in each room.
* **Emergency Exit Signs at Windows** (`emergency_exit_signs_at_windows`, text, optional): Confirm exit signs at windows.
* **Emergency Exit Signs on Entrances** (`emergency_exit_signs_on_entrances`, text, optional): Verify exit signs at entrances.
