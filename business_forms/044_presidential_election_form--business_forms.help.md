# presidential_election_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information related to the presidential election, specifically about whether the respondent is a voter and their candidate choice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for "voter_choice" to indicate whether you are a voter.
2. For "candidate_choice", select all applicable candidates you support. You can choose from the options "Yes" or "No" for each candidate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **voter_choice** (`voter_choice`, select_one, yes): Indicates whether you are a voter. Select "Yes" to confirm your status as a voter.
* **candidate_choice** (`candidate_choice`, select_multiple, yes/no): Indicates your candidate choice for the presidential election. Select "Yes" to choose each candidate you support.
