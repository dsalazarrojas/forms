# prophets - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a requisition request for internal use within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Submit" to start filling out the form.
2. Select your department from the "Department" field.
3. Enter the location of the requisition in the "Location" field.
4. Provide a detailed description of the equipment in the "Equipment Description" field.
5. Enter your department head in the "Department Head" field.
6. Select the material type in the "Material Type" field.
7. Enter the material description in the "Material Description" field.
8. Enter the quantity of the equipment or materials required in the "Quantity" field.
9. Enter the unit price of the equipment or materials in the "Unit Price" field.
10. Enter the total cost of the requisition in the "Total Cost" field.
11. Select the date of the requisition in the "Requisition Date" field.
12. Indicate if the requisition has been approved in the "Approved By" field.
13. Add any comments regarding the requisition in the "Comments" field.
14. Attach any relevant documents or attachments to the "Attachment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header**: This field is a header section that provides a brief title and introduction to the form.
* **Department**: Select the department that is requesting the requisition.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the equipment or materials will be used.
* **Equipment Description**: Provide a detailed description of the equipment or materials being requisitioned.
* **Department Head**: Enter the name of the department head who will be responsible for approving the requisition.
* **Material Type**: Select the type of material or equipment being requisitioned.
* **Material Description**: Provide a detailed description of the material or equipment being requisitioned.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the equipment or materials required.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the unit price of the equipment or materials.
* **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of the requisition.
* **Requisition Date**: Select the date of the requisition.
* **Approved By**: Indicate if the requisition has been approved.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or remarks regarding the requisition.
* **Attachment**: Attach any relevant documents or files to the requisition.
