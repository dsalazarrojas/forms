# Operational Risk Reduction Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operational Risk Reduction Request Form is a tool for employees to report and document operational risks that need to be addressed within the organization. This form helps to gather information about the risk, its category, potential impact, and suggested solutions, allowing the risk management team to prioritize and mitigate risks effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of risk you are reporting: Choose the type of risk you are reporting, such as "Request for risk assessment" or "Request for risk mitigation."
2. Identify the risk category: Select one or more categories that best describe the risk, such as "Operational," "Strategic," "Financial," "Regulatory," "Technical," or "Security."
3. Describe the risk: Provide a brief description of the risk in the "Risk Description" field.
4. Quantify the risk impact: If applicable, estimate the potential impact of the risk in the "Risk Impact" field.
5. Determine the frequency of risk occurrence: Estimate the frequency of the risk occurrence in the "Impact Frequency" field.
6. Prioritize the risk: Select the priority of the risk, from "High" to "Low," in the "Risk Priority" field.
7. Identify the responsible party: Select the party responsible for the risk, such as "Risk Management Team," "Department," or "Individual."
8. Suggest solutions: If applicable, suggest a possible solution to mitigate or address the risk in the "Risk Suggestion" field.
9. Add comments: Provide any additional comments or information about the risk in the "Risk Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Type** (`request_type`): Select the type of risk you are reporting, such as "Request for risk assessment" or "Request for risk mitigation."
* **Risk Category** (`risk_category`): Select one or more categories that best describe the risk, such as "Operational," "Strategic," "Financial," "Regulatory," "Technical," or "Security."
* **Risk Description** (`risk_description`): Provide a brief description of the risk.
* **Risk Impact** (`risk_impact`): Estimate the potential impact of the risk, if applicable.
* **Impact Frequency** (`impact_frequency`): Estimate the frequency of the risk occurrence.
* **Risk Priority** (`risk_priority`): Select the priority of the risk, from "High" to "Low."
* **Responsible Party** (`risk_responsible`): Select the party responsible for the risk, such as "Risk Management Team," "Department," or "Individual."
* **Risk Suggestion** (`risk_suggestion`): Suggest a possible solution to address the risk, if applicable.
* **Risk Comments** (`risk_comments`): Provide any additional comments or information about the risk.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* Use the "Risk Category" field to help prioritize the risk.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult your department or risk management team for guidance.
* You can submit the form multiple times if you have a new risk to report, but please ensure that you are not duplicating a previously reported risk.
