# Background Check Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Background Check Authorization Form is a legal document that allows the employer to conduct a background investigation on the individual. This form is used to gather information about the individual's identity, including their full legal name, social security number, date of birth, and current residential address. The employer may use this information for employment purposes, such as verifying an individual's identity, employment history, and education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read the disclosure and authorization statement carefully, and if you have any questions, ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
2.  Fill out your full legal name, including any middle names.
3.  Provide your social security number in the format XXX-XX-XXXX.
4.  Enter your date of birth.
5.  Provide your current residential address.
6.  List your previous addresses for the past 7 years, including City, State, and Zip.
7.  If you have a driver's license, provide your driver's license number and the state it was issued in.
8.  Select "I authorize and consent" for the background investigation.
9.  Acknowledge receipt of the Summary of Your Rights under the FCRA.
10. Sign the form digitally by selecting "This is my legal signature".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Legal Name** (`applicant_full_legal_name`): Please fill out your full legal name, including any middle names.
* **Other Names Used (Maiden name, aliases)** (`other_names_used`): If you have used other names in the past, please provide them here.
* **Social Security Number Disclosure** (`ssn_disclosure_note`): This is a note explaining that your SSN is required for accurate identity verification and will be handled securely.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`): Please provide your social security number in the format XXX-XX-XXXX.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth_bg`): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Current Residential Address** (`current_residential_address_bg`): Provide your current residential address.
* **Previous Addresses (Last 7 Years)** (`previous_addresses_7years`): List your previous addresses for the past 7 years, including City, State, and Zip.
* **Driver's License Number** (`drivers_license_number_bg`): If you have a driver's license, provide your driver's license number.
* **Driver's License Issuing State** (`drivers_license_state`): If you have a driver's license, provide the state it was issued in.
* **I hereby authorize the company to conduct a background investigation** (`authorization_statement_text`): Select "I authorize and consent" to allow the employer to conduct the background investigation.
* **I acknowledge receipt of the Summary of Your Rights under the FCRA** (`fcra_acknowledgment`): Select "I acknowledge receipt" to confirm that you have received the Summary of Your Rights under the FCRA.
* **Digital Signature Acknowledgment** (`signature_confirmation_bg`): Select "This is my legal signature" to digitally sign the form.
* **Date Signed** (`date_of_signature_bg`): Enter the date you signed the form in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
