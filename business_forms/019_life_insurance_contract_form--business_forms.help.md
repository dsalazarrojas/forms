# life_insurance_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "life_insurance_contract_form" is a comprehensive form used to create, update, or modify a life insurance contract. This form will guide you through the essential details of the policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "life_insurance_contract_form", follow these steps:

1.  Start on the **Basic Info** page and enter the **Basic Info** information. This page includes fields for the contract title and description.
2.  Proceed to the **Applicant Info** page and fill out the **Applicant Info** section, which includes information about the insured person.
3.  On the **Contact Info** page, enter the contact details for the policy owner or beneficiary.
4.  The **Insurance Type** page allows you to specify whether the insurance is active or inactive.
5.  The **Policy Type** page is used to select the type of policy.
6.  The **Policy Status** page determines the status of the policy (active or inactive).
7.  On the **Policy Status Options** page, select multiple options that apply to the policy status.
8.  Enter the **Policy Terms** and **Policy Effectiveness Date** information.
9.  Fill out the **Policy End Date** and **Insurance Coverage** information.
10. On the **Policy Amount** page, enter the policy amount.
11. The **Payment Schedule** and **Premium Payment Frequency** fields are optional and depend on the policy specifics.
12. Enter the **Premium Info** and **Premium Frequency** details, if applicable.
13. Complete the **Beneficiary Info**, **Address Info**, **Payment Info**, and **Payment Terms** fields, if applicable.
14. On the final page, select the **Policy Status** and **Payment Status** options for the policy.
15. Add any relevant **Policy Notes**.
