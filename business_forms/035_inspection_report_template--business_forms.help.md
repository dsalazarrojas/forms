# Inspection Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information during the inspection of a building for any potential issues, defects, or damage. The form aims to collect data on the building's condition, any damage or repairs required, and the inspector's findings and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Building info**: Enter a brief description of the building, including its address and any relevant information about its condition.
2. **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
3. **Inspector details**: Select whether the inspector has been present or not during the inspection.
4. **Client details**: Select whether the client is present or not during the inspection.
5. **Building condition**: Select the current building condition (Good, Fair, Poor).
6. **Damage description**: Provide a detailed description of any damage or defects found during the inspection.
7. **Repair description**: If applicable, provide a description of any repairs or actions needed to fix the issues found.
8. **Recommendation**: Provide any recommendations for future maintenance or repairs.
9. **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or observations from the inspection.
10. **Additional comments**: Enter any further comments or information that may be relevant to the inspection.
11. **Signed by**: Select who signed the report (Inspector, Manager, or Supervisor).
12. **Signature**: Select whether the document has been signed or not.
13. **Inspection Date 2**: Enter the date of the second inspection (if applicable).
14. **Inspection time**: Enter the time of the inspection (if applicable).
15. **Inspector Details 2**: Select whether the second inspector was present or not during the second inspection.
16. **Client Details 2**: Select whether the client was present or not during the second inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Building info** (Building info, Note, Required): A brief description of the building, including its address and any relevant information about its condition.
* **Inspection Date** (Inspection Date, Date, Required): The date of the inspection in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Inspector details** (Inspector details, Select One, Required): Select whether the inspector was present or not during the inspection.
* **Client details** (Client details, Select Multiple, Required): Select whether the client is present or not during the inspection.
* **Building condition** (Building condition, Select One, Required): Select the current building condition (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Damage description** (Damage description, Text, Required): A detailed description of any damage or defects found during the inspection.
* **Repair description** (Repair description, Text, Required): If applicable, provide a description of any repairs or actions needed to fix the issues found.
* **Recommendation** (Recommendation, Text, Required): Provide any recommendations for future maintenance or repairs.
* **Notes** (Notes, Text, Required): Enter any additional comments or observations from the inspection.
* **Additional comments** (Additional comments, Text, Required): Enter any further comments or information that may be relevant to the inspection.
* **Signed by** (Signed by, Select One, Required): Select who signed the report (Inspector, Manager, or Supervisor).
* **Signature** (Signature, Select One, Required): Select whether the document has been signed or not.
* **Inspection Date 2** (Inspection Date 2, Date, Required): Enter the date of the second inspection (if applicable).
* **Inspection time** (Inspection time, Time, Required): Enter the time of the inspection (if applicable).
* **Inspector Details 2** (Inspector details 2, Select One, Required): Select whether the second inspector was present or not during the second inspection.
* **Client Details 2** (Client details 2, Select Multiple, Required): Select whether the client was present or not during the second inspection.

Note: The required fields are marked as required in the form, but in this guide, all fields are marked as required to ensure that users understand the importance of filling all fields.
