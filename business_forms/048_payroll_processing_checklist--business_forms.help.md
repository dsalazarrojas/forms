# Payroll Processing Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Payroll Processing Checklist is a form used to collect and verify the necessary information before processing each new or updated payroll period. This form ensures that all relevant details are accurate and complete to prevent errors and ensure timely and accurate payment.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select the Payroll Period
- Select the correct **payroll_period** from the options: Payroll Period, Weekly, Bi-weekly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual, or Annual.

### Step 2: Select Payroll Frequency (if applicable)
- Choose all applicable **payroll_frequency** options: Daily, Bi-weekly, Weekly, Semi-Monthly, or Monthly.

### Step 3: Select Payroll Method
- Choose the correct **payroll_method** from: Paper or Online.

### Step 4: Enter Payroll Date
- Enter the **payroll_date** in the required format (MM/DD/YY).

### Step 5: Enter Payroll Time (if applicable)
- Enter the **payroll_time** in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### Step 6: Enter Payroll Address (if applicable)
- Enter the **payroll_address** in the required format.

### Step 7: Add Note (if applicable)
- Enter any additional **payroll_note** related to the payment.

### Step 8: Enter Payroll Email (if applicable)
- Enter the **payroll_email** of the employee or payee.

### Step 9: Enter Payroll Phone Number (if applicable)
- Enter the phone number associated with the **payroll_phone**.

### Step 10: Select Payroll Status
- Choose the status of the payment from: Pending, Approved, or Rejected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **payroll_period**: Select the correct period for which the payroll is being processed.
* **payroll_frequency**: Choose all applicable frequencies for the payroll.
* **payroll_method**: Select the method used to process the payroll (Paper or Online).
* **payroll_date**: Enter the date on which the payroll is due or was processed.
* **payroll_time**: If applicable, enter the time when the payroll is processed or due.
* **payroll_address**: Enter the address where the payroll is to be delivered or sent.
* **payroll_note**: Enter any additional details or notes related to the payroll.
* **payroll_email**: Enter the email of the individual or entity receiving the payroll.
* **payroll_phone**: Enter the phone number associated with the payroll.
* **payroll_status**: Select the current status of the payroll: Pending, Approved, or Rejected.
