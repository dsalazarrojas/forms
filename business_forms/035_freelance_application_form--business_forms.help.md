# Freelance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freelance Application Form is designed for potential freelancers to submit their applications. This form collects general information about the freelancer, including their contact details, skills, work experience, and portfolio.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Freelance Application Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields:
	* Provide your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your contact information in the "contact" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. List your skills in the "skills" field.
5. Describe your work experience in the "work experience" field.
6. Provide information about your availability in the "availability" field.
7. Upload your portfolio in the "portfolio" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: This is a required field where you can enter your full name.
* **Contact**: Enter your contact information, such as your phone number or physical address.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Skills**: List your relevant skills and experience in this field.
* **Work Experience**: Describe your relevant work experience in this field.
* **Availability**: Provide information about your availability for work, including your schedule and preferences.
* **Portfolio**: Upload your portfolio, which can be a link to your online portfolio or a PDF document.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your application is complete.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the fields, especially the "skills" and "work experience" fields.
* If you have any issues uploading your portfolio, contact the form administrator for assistance.
* The form will be reviewed and responded to in a timely manner after submission.
