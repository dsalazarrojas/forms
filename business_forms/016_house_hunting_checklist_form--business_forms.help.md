# house-hunting-checklist-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for clients who are looking to buy or rent a new home. It will help you gather essential information about the client's preferences, contact details, and relationship with the broker. The form is intended to be completed by the client, and the information collected will be used by the broker to provide personalized services and tailor their marketing efforts accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your email and phone number.
2. Choose your preferred language for communication.
3. Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or text).
4. Provide your name and relationship with the client.
5. Enter the client's contact information, including their email, phone number, and address.
6. Select the client's relationship with you (friend, family, colleague, etc.).
7. Enter the client's name and address.
8. Choose the services you would like the broker to provide (full-service, limited-service, or none).
9. Select the features you are looking for in a new home (yard, pool, parking, etc.).
10. Specify the search area and radius.
11. Add any additional notes for the broker.
12. Finally, review and submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (required): Enter your email address and phone number so that the broker can contact you.
* **Contact Email** (required): Enter the email address the broker will use to contact you.
* **Contact Name** (required): Enter your name as the broker's contact person.
* **Client Name** (required): Enter the name of the client who is looking for a new home.
* **Client Email** (required): Enter the email address of the client.
* **Client Phone** (required): Enter the phone number of the client.
* **Client Relationship** (required): Choose how you know the client (friend, family, colleague, etc.).
* **Broker Name** (required): Enter your name as the broker.
* **Broker Email** (required): Enter your email address as the broker.
* **Broker Phone** (required): Enter your phone number as the broker.
* **Broker Address** (required): Enter your address as the broker.
* **Search Area** (required): Enter the area you would like to search for a new home.
* **Search Radius** (required): Enter the radius of the search area.
* **Budget Range** (required): Enter the budget for the new home.
* **Home Type** (optional): Choose the type of home you are looking for (single, multi-unit, condo, etc.).
* **Home Size** (required): Enter the size of the new home.
* **Move-in Date** (required): Enter the date you would like to move into the new home.
* **Move-out Date** (optional): Enter the date you would like to move out of your current home.
* **Broker Services** (optional): Choose the level of service you would like the broker to provide (full-service, limited-service, or none).
* **Broker Notes** (optional): Add any additional notes for the broker.
* **Home Features** (optional): Choose the features you are looking for in a new home (yard, pool, parking, etc.).
* **Preferred Language** (optional): Choose your preferred language for communication.
* **Contact Method** (optional): Choose your preferred contact method (phone, email, or text).
* **Client-Broker Relation** (optional): Enter any additional information about your relationship with the client.
* **Notes** (optional): Add any additional notes or comments for the broker.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled in.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the broker.
* The information collected from this form will be used to provide personalized services to you and your client.
