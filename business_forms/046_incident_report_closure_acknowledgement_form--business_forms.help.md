# Incident Report Closure Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Incident Report Closure Acknowledgement Form is used to acknowledge the closure of an incident report, ensuring that all relevant stakeholders are informed and on the same page. This form provides a formal record of the incident's closure status and any accompanying comments or actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the relevant incident report ID and any necessary information to complete the form.
2. Select the correct closure status (Closed, Pending, or Review) and enter the closure date.
3. If the closure status is Pending or Review, enter the review due date.
4. Choose the current incident status (Active, Resolved, or Cancelled).
5. Select the reviewer who will review the incident report.
6. Enter any comments or notes related to the incident report.
7. Enter any closure comments or notes related to the closing of the incident report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Report Closure Acknowledgement Form** (Page 1): This is the title of the form, which is displayed at the top of the page.
* **Date Closed** (Page 2):
	+ Label: Date Closed
	+ Description: Enter the date the incident report was closed.
* **Incident ID** (Page 2):
	+ Label: Incident ID
	+ Description: Enter the ID number of the incident report.
* **Closure Status** (Page 3):
	+ Label: Closure Status
	+ Description: Select the current status of the incident report's closure (Closed, Pending, or Review).
* **Closure Date** (Page 3):
	+ Label: Closure Date
	+ Description: Enter the date the incident report was closed.
* **Review Due Date** (Page 4):
	+ Label: Review Due Date
	+ Description: Enter the date the incident report needs to be reviewed by the next person in the review process.
* **Incident Status** (Page 4):
	+ Label: Incident Status
	+ Description: Select the current status of the incident report (Active, Resolved, or Cancelled).
* **Reviewers** (Page 5):
	+ Label: Reviewer
	+ Description: Select the person who will review the incident report.
* **Comments** (Page 5):
	+ Label: Comments
	+ Description: Enter any comments or notes related to the incident report.
* **Closure Comments** (Page 6):
	+ Label: Closure Comments
	+ Description: Enter any comments or notes related to the closure of the incident report.

## Tips
* Ensure the closure status is accurate and up-to-date.
* Enter any comments or notes that are relevant to the incident report's closure.
* Review and verify all information entered before submitting the form.
* If another team member has already filled out this form for the same incident report, do not duplicate effort.
