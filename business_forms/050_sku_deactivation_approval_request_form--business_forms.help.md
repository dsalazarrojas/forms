# sku_deactivation_approval_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by a manager to approve or reject a SKU deactivation request. The form helps to collect information about the request and the person requesting deactivation, and includes fields for approver comments and the status of the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select whether the deactivation request is approved or not.
2. Enter the S KU ID of the product being deactivated.
3. Provide a reason for deactivation.
4. Optionally, add any comments about the request.
5. Enter the name and email address of the person who will be approving this request.
6. (Optional) Enter the name and contact information for the person who submitted the request.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Details** (Select One):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you approve the request, or "No" if you reject it.
* **S KU ID** (Number):
	+ Enter the unique identifier of the product being deactivated.
* **Reason For Deactivation** (Text):
	+ Provide a clear and concise explanation for deactivating the product.
* **Comments** (Note):
	+ Add any additional information or comments about the request.
* **Approver Name** (Text):
	+ Enter the name of the person approving this request.
* **Approver E-mail** (Email):
	+ Enter the email address of the person approving this request.
* **Approver Phone** (Text):
	+ Enter the phone number of the person approving this request.
* **Date Approved** (Date):
	+ The date when the approval was made (optional).
* **Time Approved** (Time):
	+ The time when the approval was made (optional).
* **Approver Notes** (Note):
	+ Add any additional notes or information about the approval process.
* **Submitter ID** (Number):
	+ (Optional) Enter the identifier of the person who submitted the request.
* **Submitter E-mail** (Email):
	+ (Optional) Enter the email address of the person who submitted the request.
* **Submitter Phone** (Text):
	+ (Optional) Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the request.
* **Submitter Name** (Text):
	+ (Optional) Enter the name of the person who submitted the request.
* **Submitter Department** (Text):
	+ (Optional) Enter the department of the person who submitted the request.

## Tips
* Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you are entering the correct information for the approver and submitter.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or the submitter for clarification.
