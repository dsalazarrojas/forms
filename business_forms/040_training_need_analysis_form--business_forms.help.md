# Training Need Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for analyzing and addressing training needs within the organization. It helps to track, report, and manage training requirements, ensuring that employees receive the necessary training and development opportunities to enhance their skills and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Business Form ID" field with the unique identifier assigned to this form by the system.
2. Enter a clear and concise title for the form in the "Form Title" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the form in the "Form Description" field.
4. Select the relevant "Form Category" from the provided options (e.g., "Business Forms").
5. Choose the user to whom the form is assigned from the available options (e.g., "chatjimmy").
6. Enter the version number of the form in the "Form Version" field.
7. Select the time and date when the form was created and last updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Form ID (ID 1)**: A unique identifier assigned to this form by the system. Enter the exact ID as provided by the system.
* **Form Title (ID 2)**: A clear and concise title for the form that summarizes its purpose and content.
* **Form Description (ID 3)**: A brief description of the form, explaining what it is used for and its relevance.
* **Form Category (ID 4)**: Select the relevant category for the form (e.g., "Business Forms").
* **Form Assigned To (ID 5)**: Choose the user to whom the form is assigned (e.g., "chatjimmy").
* **Form Version (ID 6)**: Enter the version number of the form, indicating changes or updates made to it.
* **Form Created At (ID 7)**: Select the date and time when the form was created.
* **Form Updated At (ID 8)**: Select the date and time when the form was last updated.
* **Form Status (ID 9)**: Select the status of the form, indicating whether it is active or inactive.
* **Comments (ID 10)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback related to the form.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and consistently.
* Double-check the form for completeness and correctness before submitting.
* If unsure about any field, consult with the relevant user or department.
* Review and update the form as necessary to reflect changes in the organization's training needs.
