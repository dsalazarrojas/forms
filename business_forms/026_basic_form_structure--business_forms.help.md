# Basic Form Structure - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Basic Form Structure" is a registration form used to gather information from businesses or organizations seeking services or support from our agency. It collects details about the organization, its contact and address information, and the purpose of the submission or request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in the Organization Name**: Enter the name of your organization in the text field labeled "Organization name".
2. **Select Organization Type**: Choose the type of organization you represent from the dropdown menu labeled "Organization type".
3. **Enter Contact Information**: Fill in the fields for the contact person's name, title, email, and phone number.
4. **Enter Address Information**: Fill in the fields for street address, city, state or province, and postal code.
5. **Select Purpose and Details**: Choose the purpose of your submission from the dropdown menu labeled "Purpose of this submission".
6. **Enter Submission Date**: Select the date of your submission using the date picker.
7. **Enter Details Description**: Describe the purpose of your submission in the text field labeled "Detailed description of your need".
8. **Fill in Additional Fields (if applicable)**: If your submission requires additional information, select your priority level, budget range, preferred contact method, and provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, required): Choose the type of organization you represent from the dropdown menu.
* **Industry or Sector** (`industry`, optional): Enter the industry or sector your organization operates in.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Title** (`contact_title`, optional): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, required): Enter the street address of your organization.
* **City** (`city`, required): Enter the city of your organization's address.
* **State/Province** (`state`, required): Enter the state or province of your organization's address.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, required): Enter the postal code of your organization's address.
* **Purpose of this Submission** (`form_purpose`, required): Choose the purpose of your submission from the dropdown menu.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, required): Select the date of your submission using the date picker.
* **Detailed Description of your Need** (`details_description`, required): Describe the purpose of your submission in the text field.
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, optional): Select the priority level of your submission from the dropdown menu.
* **Budget Range (if applicable)** (`budget_range`, optional): Select the budget range of your submission from the dropdown menu.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, optional): Select your preferred contact method from the dropdown menu.
* **Additional Notes or Comments** (`additional_notes`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your submission.
* **I Confirm the Information Provided is Accurate** (`form_confirmation`, required): Select "I Confirm" to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
