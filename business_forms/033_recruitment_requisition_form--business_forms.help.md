# Recruitment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Requisition Form is a tool used to create and manage job openings within the organization. This form is essential for ensuring that job postings are accurately and completely filled out, which will help streamline the hiring process and reduce errors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out each field in the form as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct job type and category.
3. Enter the job title, description, and requirements in the required fields.
4. Select the job location and opening and closing dates.
5. Choose the required skills and job status.
6. Enter the name and date of the person assigned to the job.
7. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the job opening.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of job, such as Manager or Non-Manager.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, `select_one`, optional): Select the category of the job, such as Sales, Marketing, or Engineering.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`, `text`, required): Enter the specific position or role of the job.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required): Enter a detailed description of the job, including the responsibilities and requirements.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, `text`, required): Enter any specific requirements for the job.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the job will be based.
* **Job Opening Date** (`job_opening_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the job will be open.
* **Job Closing Date** (`job_closing_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the job will be closing.
* **Required Skills** (`required_skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the required skills for the job.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the job as Open, Closed, or Inactive.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person assigned to the job.
* **Job ID** (`job_id`, `text`, optional): Enter a custom ID for the job (not required but useful).
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the job was assigned.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the job was submitted.

Tips:
- Make sure to review and proofread your submission before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant department or team for assistance.
