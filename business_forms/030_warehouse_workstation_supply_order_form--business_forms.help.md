# Warehouse Workstation Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Workstation Supply Order Form is a tool used to submit orders for supplies to be restocked or reordered at a specific warehouse.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form**: A text field for entering additional details or instructions related to the order.
* **Select Warehouse**: Choose the warehouse where the supplies should be restocked or reordered from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Item**: Choose the specific item you need to order from the dropdown menu.
* **Quantity**: Enter the amount of the item needed for the order.
* **Date**: Enter the date when the order should be fulfilled.
* **Time**: Enter the time when the order should be fulfilled.
* **Notes**: Include any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Confirm Order**: Confirm that the order is correct by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
