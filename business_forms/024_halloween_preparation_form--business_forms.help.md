# Halloween Preparation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Preparation Form is a tool to help event organizers and coordinators gather information and prepare for Halloween events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by providing event details in the "Event Details" field.
2. Enter the "Event Date" in the correct date format.
3. Enter "Venue Details" to provide information about the event location.
4. Provide your "Contact Number" so that the event organizers can reach out to you.
5. Finally, enter "Business Contact" information to ensure that the correct person is aware of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of the event, including any relevant details such as theme, dress code, or expected number of attendees.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the Halloween event in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Venue Details** (`venue_details`, text, optional): Provide information about the event location, including the address and any other relevant details.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter a contact number where the event organizers can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
- **Business Contact** (`business_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and title of the business contact person responsible for the event.
