# Driver Expense Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help drivers and fleet managers track expenses related to driving activities, including fuel costs, tolls, and parking fees. It aims to make it easy to log and report business-related driving expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you are filling out the correct category for each expense.
2. Fill out each field with the required information for the specific expense type.
3. Use the "Additional Information" field to provide any supporting details or comments about the expense.
4. Click on the "Submit" button to save your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Driver Information** (`driver_expense_tracking_form`, text, optional): Enter your name, company name, and any other relevant information about yourself as a driver.
* **Expense Information** (`driver_expense_tracking_form`, text, optional): Describe the type of expense you are tracking, such as fuel, tolls, or parking fees.
* **Additional Information** (`driver_expense_tracking_form`, text, optional): Provide any additional details or comments about the expense, such as the date, time, or location of the expense.
* **Submit** (`driver_expense_tracking_form`, note, optional): This is the final step to save your information. You can add any final comments or notes about the form, but this field is not required for submission.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form to ensure the accuracy and relevance of the data collected.
* Use the form for business-related driving expenses only.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, contact the form administrator at form@gic.mx.
