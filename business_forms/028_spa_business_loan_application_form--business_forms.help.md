# Spa Business Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about your spa business and its owners to evaluate your loan application. Please complete it accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful loan processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the required fields for your business and its owners. This includes:
    *   Business Name
    *   Contact Email
    *   Contact Phone
    *   Business Type
    *   Owner Role
    *   Business Years in Operation
    *   Monthly Revenue
    *   Owner Phone 2
    *   Loan Amount
    *   Loan Term
    *   Documents (you will need to confirm if you have the necessary documents)
2.  For each "Owner", complete the corresponding owner fields: Owner Name 2, Owner Phone 2, Owner Address 2, and Owner Document 2 (if applicable).
3.  Confirm the "Documents 2" and "Owner Document 3" and "Owner Document 4" and "Owner Document 5" fields accurately, as they are crucial for the loan application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Owner Name**: Please enter the name of the owner(s) of the business.
*   **Business Name**: Enter your spa business name as it appears on its license or registration.
*   **Address**: Enter your business address.
*   **Loan Amount**: Specify the amount you are applying for.
*   **Loan Term**: Confirm whether you are offering a loan with a term (yes/no).
*   **Documents**: Confirm if you have the necessary documents.
*   **Business Details**: Enter any additional business details.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Business Type**: Confirm the type of business you are applying for.
*   **Owner Role**: Confirm the role of the owner(s).
*   **Business Years in Operation**: Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
*   **Monthly Revenue**: Enter the monthly revenue of your business.
*   **Owner Name 2**: Enter the name of the second owner.
*   **Owner Role 2**: Confirm the role of the second owner (if applicable).
*   **Owner Phone 2**: Enter the phone number of the second owner.
*   **Owner Address 2**: Enter the address of the second owner.
*   **Owner Document 2**: Confirm if you have the necessary documents for the second owner.
*   **Owner Document 3**: Confirm if you have the necessary documents for the second owner.
*   **Owner Document 4**: Confirm if you have the necessary documents for the second owner.
*   **Owner Document 5**: Confirm if you have the necessary documents for the second owner.

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid delays in processing your loan application.
*   Ensure that you have all the required documents before submitting this form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our loan officer for assistance.
