# Daily Leads Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Leads Report Form is used to document and report on the daily activities related to leads, providing accurate and up-to-date information for data-driven decision-making and performance evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the relevant options for each field based on the day's activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assigned Leads** (Select Multiple, id: 1): Select all the leads assigned to you for the day.
* **Actions Taken** (Select Multiple, id: 2): Select all the actions you took for the assigned leads.
* **Lead Status** (Select One, id: 3): Choose the current lead status, such as Lead Qualified, New Lead, or Lead Qualified (Follow up).
* **Lead Source** (Select One, id: 4): Choose how the lead was sourced, such as Email, Phone, or Web.
* **Lead Source Details** (Text, id: 5): Provide additional details about the lead source, if applicable.
* **Lead Owner** (Number, id: 6): Enter the ID of the lead owner, if applicable.
* **Follow-up Details** (Text, id: 7): Provide any follow-up details related to the leads, if applicable.

## Tips

* Be sure to select all relevant options for each field based on your daily activities.
* Ensure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for data-driven decision-making and performance evaluation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the support team.
