# Sales Call Documentation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document sales calls, including details about the client, call type, next steps, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Sales Call" tab.
2. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Choose the type of call you had with the client from the "Call Type" select menu.
4. If you had a follow-up call, enter the date in the "Follow-up Date" field.
5. Choose the next steps for the client from the "Next Steps" select menu.
6. Enter any additional notes about the call in the "Notes" field.
7. If you had a meeting with the client, enter the date and type of meeting (e.g. "This week").
8. Choose the status of the meeting (e.g. "Done", "Scheduled", "Follow-up", etc.).
9. If you had a trial with the client, choose whether it was completed or not.
10. Enter any trial details in the "Trial Details" field.
11. Choose the current sales stage of the client (e.g. "Qualified", "Active", "Closed", etc.).
12. Enter the lead stage of the client (e.g. "Qualified", "Re-qualified", etc.).
13. Enter the lead ID number.
14. If you had a follow-up call, enter the follow-up user's name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Call** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the sales call.
* **Client Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the client.
* **Call Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of call you had with the client (e.g. "Call", "Follow-up", "Demo", "Meeting", etc.).
* **Call Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the call.
* **Next Steps** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the next steps for the client (e.g. "Follow-up Call", "Meeting Requested", "Next Meeting", etc.).
* **Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the call.
* **Follow-up Call** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the follow-up call.
* **Meeting Requested** (Select One, Optional): Choose whether a meeting was requested.
* **Next Meeting** (Select One, Optional): Choose the next meeting type (e.g. "This week", "Next week", "Later", etc.).
* **Other** (Text, Optional): Enter any other information about the meeting.
* **Trial Details** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose whether the trial was completed (e.g. "True", "False", "Maybe", etc.).
* **Sales Stage** (Select One, Optional): Choose the current sales stage of the client (e.g. "Qualified", "Active", "Closed", etc.).
* **Lead Stage** (Select One, Optional): Enter the lead stage of the client (e.g. "Qualified", "Re-qualified", etc.).
* **Lead ID** (Number, Optional): Enter the lead ID number.
* **Follow-up User** (Text, Optional): Enter the follow-up user's name.
* **Follow-up Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the follow-up date.
* **Follow-up Date Status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the status of the follow-up date (e.g. "Done", "Scheduled", "Follow-up", etc.).
* **Follow-up User Status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the status of the follow-up user (e.g. "Active", "Not Active", etc.).
* **Trial User** (Text, Optional): Enter the trial user's name.
* **Follow-up User Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the follow-up user's name.
* **Follow-up Date User** (Text, Optional): Enter the follow-up user's name.
* **Lead Status** (Select One, Optional): Enter the lead status (e.g. "New", "Active", "Disqualified", etc.).
* **Lead Stage** (Select One, Optional): Enter the lead stage (e.g. "Qualified", "Re-qualified", etc.).
* **Trial Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the trial date.
* **Follow-up User Status** (Select One, Optional): Enter the status of the follow-up user (e.g. "Active", "Not Active", etc.).
* **Lead Manager** (Text, Optional): Enter the lead manager's name.
* **Follow-up Date Status** (Select One, Optional): Enter the status of the follow-up date (e.g. "Done", "Scheduled", etc.).
* **Trial User** (Text, Optional): Enter the trial user's name.
* **Follow-up Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the follow-up date.

## Tips
* Make sure to select only one sales call per client.
* Use this form to document only sales calls with defined outcomes or next steps.
* Enter any additional notes or comments about the call in the "Notes" field.
