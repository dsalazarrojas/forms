# Catering Partner Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for catering businesses to register themselves with the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the business details, including:
	* Business Name: Enter your business's name as it appears on your license or marketing materials.
	* Contact Person and Title: Enter the title of the person responsible for your business.
	* Primary Business Address: Enter your business address where you operate.
	* Business Email Address: Enter your business email address where you can be reached.
	* Business Phone Number: Enter your business phone number.
2. Provide compliance and certification details:
	* Food Handler/Catering License Number: Enter your license number for food handling and catering operations.
	* Liability Insurance Provider: Enter the name of your liability insurance provider.
	* Coverage Amount (General Liability): Enter the amount of coverage for general liability.
	* Health Department Permit Expiry Date: Enter the date your health department permit expires.
3. Select your service capability:
	* Primary Cuisine Styles: Select all cuisine styles your business offers.
4. Provide event capacity and transportation details:
	* Maximum Guest Capacity for Single Event: Enter the maximum number of guests you can accommodate for a single event.
	* Do you own refrigerated transport: Select whether you own refrigerated transport or rent/contract it.
5. Provide professional references:
	* Professional References: List 2 names and contact numbers of professionals who can vouch for your business.
6. Agree to partner terms:
	* I agree to follow the partner code of conduct and safety standards: Select "I Agree" to indicate your agreement.
7. Sign and date the form:
	* Registrant Signature (Type Name): Sign your name as the registrant.
	* Date of Registration: Enter the date you registered with the platform.
