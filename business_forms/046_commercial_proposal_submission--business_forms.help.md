# Commercial Proposal Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from companies or entities interested in submitting a commercial proposal for a project. It captures key details about the proposing entity, project title, proposed budget, and supporting documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about your company and proposal.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Attach any required technical documentation as specified in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Name of Proposing Company** (`proposing_company_name`): Enter the full, official name of your company.
* **Company Headquarters Address** (`proposer_address`): Provide the address of your company's headquarters.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`): Enter the URL of your company's website, if applicable.
* **Project Title or RFP Reference Number** (`rfp_project_title`): Enter the title or reference number of the project you are proposing for.
* **Proposal Category** (`proposal_category`): Select the category that best describes your proposal (e.g., Construction Services, IT Infrastructure, etc.).
* **Executive Summary of Proposal** (`proposal_executive_summary`): Provide a concise overview of your proposed solution, highlighting key points and benefits.
* **Total Proposed Budget (USD)** (`estimated_project_budget`): Enter the total budget for the proposed project in US dollars.
* **Estimated Project Duration** (`proposed_completion_timeline`): Enter the estimated duration for the project in weeks or months.
* **Have you attached the required technical documentation?** (`supporting_docs_check`): Indicate if you have attached all necessary technical documentation or if you will be sending it separately.
* **Full Name** (`lead_contact_name`): Enter the full name of the lead contact person.
* **Professional Email Address** (`lead_contact_email`): Enter the professional email address of the lead contact person.
* **I certify that I am authorized to submit this proposal on behalf of the company** (`submission_acknowledgment`): Confirm that you are authorized to submit the proposal on behalf of your company.
