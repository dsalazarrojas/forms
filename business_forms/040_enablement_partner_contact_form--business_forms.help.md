# Enablement Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information and inquiries from our Enablement Partners. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that your message reaches our team and we can address your concerns promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Contact Information" page with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your Enablement Partner from the list provided.
3. If your partner is not listed, enter the partner name in the "Other (if not listed)" field.
4. Choose the "Inquiry Type" from the options provided (Sales, Support, or Other).
5. Provide a description of your inquiry in the "Description of Inquiry" field.
6. Add any additional notes you would like to share with us.
7. Submit the form to send your inquiry to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: This is the contact information we need to get in touch with you. Please fill out your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number accurately.
* **Enablement Partner**: This is the name of your partner. If your partner is listed, select it from the options provided. If not, please enter the partner name in the "Other (if not listed)" field.
* **Other (if not listed)**: This field is for entering the name of your partner if it's not listed in the options provided.
* **Inquiry Type**: This field allows you to choose the type of inquiry you have (Sales, Support, or Other).
* **Description of Inquiry**: This is a description of your inquiry. Please provide as much detail as possible so we can understand your concern.
* **Notes**: This is an optional field for you to add any additional notes or information you would like to share with us.
* **Phone Number**: This is your phone number for our team to contact you with updates.
* **Email**: This is your email address for our team to contact you with updates.
* **First Name**: This is your first name.
* **Last Name**: This is your last name.
