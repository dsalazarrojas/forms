# corporate_vendor_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate vendor application form is used to collect information about your company and services to be considered as a corporate vendor. It helps us understand your company's details and assess its suitability as a vendor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Read through each field and its explanation to understand what information is required.
- Enter your company's name, address, contact details, and other relevant information in the corresponding fields.
- Choose the correct product category that best describes your company's services.
- Provide a clear and concise description of your services.
- Specify the locations where your services will be provided.
- Accept the agreement terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): Enter your company's official name.
- **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter your company's physical address.
- **Contact** (Contact, text, optional): Enter your company's contact person and their contact information.
- **Vendor Name** (Vendor Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the vendor representative.
- **Website** (Website, text, optional): Enter your company's website URL.
- **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your company's official email address.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number.
- **Product Category** (Product Category, text, required): Choose the category that best describes your company's services.
  - Example: If your company provides IT services, select "Software Development" or "IT Consultancy".
- **Service Description** (Service Description, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise description of your company's services.
- **Service Location** (Service Location, text, required): Specify the locations where your services will be provided. This is crucial in helping us understand your service reach and coverage.
- **Agreement** (Agreement, note, required): Read and accept the terms of the agreement to proceed with the application.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled accurately and completely.
- Double-check your information for spelling and grammar errors.
- Use clear and concise language in the Service Description field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
