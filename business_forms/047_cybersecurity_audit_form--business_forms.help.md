This help guide aims to assist end-users in filling out the Cybersecurity Audit Form accurately and effectively. The form is designed to gather information for cybersecurity assessments and audits. Before proceeding, please note that this form does not duplicate any other form within the company, as it's a distinct tool used for auditing and reporting purposes.

The purpose of the form is to collect data on an organization's or company's cyber posture, including their risk level, vulnerability exposure, and implemented security controls.

To complete the form, follow these steps:

### How to Complete This Form

1.  The form consists of several fields that require you to provide information on your organization's cybersecurity posture.
2.  Please ensure that you fill out all the mandatory fields.
3.  For fields marked as `select_multiple` or `select_one`, choose the options that best describe your organization's situation.
4.  Enter accurate and relevant information in the `text` fields.
5.  Enter a valid date and time in the `date` and `time` fields.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Organization** (`text`, Required): Enter the name of your organization. This is the primary field for identifying your organization's name.
*   **Risk Level** (`number`, Not Required): Enter a numerical value to describe your organization's risk level, with higher values indicating higher risks.
*   **Vulnerability** (`select_multiple`, Not Required): Select all the vulnerabilities that apply to your organization.
*   **Date** (`date`, Not Required): Enter the date of the audit or assessment.
*   **Time** (`time`, Not Required): Enter the time of the audit or assessment.
*   **Note** (`text`, Not Required): Add any relevant notes or comments about your organization's cyber posture.
*   **Email** (`email`, Not Required): Enter the email address of the contact person for this audit or assessment.
*   **Phone** (`text`, Not Required): Enter a valid phone number for the contact person.
*   **Security Controls** (`select_multiple`, Not Required): Select all the implemented security controls relevant to your organization.
*   **Comments** (`text`, Required): Enter a detailed description of your organization's current cyber posture and areas for improvement.
*   **Security Measures** (`select_multiple`, Not Required): Select all the security measures taken or planned to mitigate risks.
*   **Action Plan** (`text`, Not Required): Enter a detailed plan of action to improve your organization's cyber posture.
*   **Recommendations** (`text`, Not Required): Enter any recommendations or suggestions for improving your organization's cyber posture.
*   **Review Status** (`select_one`, Not Required): Choose the status of your review (Active or Inactive).

Note that some fields are not mandatory, but it is recommended to fill out all fields to provide a comprehensive picture of your organization's cyber posture.

### Tips

*   Ensure that you accurately fill out all mandatory fields.
*   Use valid dates and times in the `date` and `time` fields.
*   Use the correct format for email addresses in the `email` field.
*   Select all applicable options in the `select_multiple` and `select_one` fields.
*   Enter clear and concise text in the `text` fields.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the Cybersecurity Audit Form effectively and accurately.
