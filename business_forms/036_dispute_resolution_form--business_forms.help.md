# Dispute Resolution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dispute Resolution Form is a tool used to collect and document information about disputes, helping to clarify the situation and identify the best course of action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Dispute Resolution Description** field with a clear and concise summary of the dispute.
2. Select the **Dispute Resolution Type** from the options provided (e.g. Conflict of Opinion, Breach of Contract, Non-Payment).
3. If applicable, select all the parties involved in the dispute from the options (e.g. Party A, Party B, both Party A and B).
4. Choose the expected outcome of the dispute resolution process (e.g. Resolved, Unresolved).
5. Select the method of resolution used (e.g. Mediation, Arbitration).
6. Enter the date the incident occurred.
7. Enter the time the incident occurred.
8. Add any additional comments or details about the dispute resolution process.
9. Enter any additional details about the outcome of the dispute resolution process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dispute Resolution Description**: A brief summary of the dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Type**: The type of dispute, such as Conflict of Opinion, Breach of Contract, or Non-Payment.
* **Dispute Resolution Parties Involved**: The parties involved in the dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Outcome**: The expected outcome of the dispute resolution process.
* **Dispute Resolution Resolution Method**: The method used to resolve the dispute.
* **Dispute Resolution Date of Incident**: The date the incident occurred.
* **Dispute Resolution Time of Incident**: The time the incident occurred.
* **Dispute Resolution Comments**: Additional comments or details about the dispute resolution process.
* **Dispute Resolution Outcome Details**: Additional details about the outcome of the dispute resolution process.
