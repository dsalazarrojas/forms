# Plant Operation License Renewal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plant Operation License Renewal Application Form is designed to help plant operations renew their licenses. This form is part of the company's regular maintenance and compliance process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company information: Provide your company's name and address.
2. Enter the license number: Enter the number of your plant operation license.
3. Choose the license type: Select the type of license your plant operation has, which can be "Plant", "Business", or "Other".
4. Provide the license expiration date and renewal date: Enter the dates for your license expiration and renewal.
5. Add contact information: Provide the name, email, and phone number of the person responsible for the license renewal process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name as it appears on your license.
* **Company Address (`company_address`, text, required): Enter your company's address as it appears on your license.
* **License Number (`license_num`, text, required): Enter the number of your plant operation license.
* **License Expiration Date (`license_expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the date your license is set to expire.
* **License Renewal Date (`license_renewal_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to renew your license.
* **License Type (`license_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of license your plant operation has, which can be "Plant", "Business", or "Other".
* **Contact Name (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the license renewal process.
* **Contact Email (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the person responsible for the license renewal process.
* **Contact Phone (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the license renewal process.
* **Submit (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your application.
