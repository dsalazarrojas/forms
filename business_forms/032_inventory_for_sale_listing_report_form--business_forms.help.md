# Inventory For Sale Listing Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Inventory For Sale Listing Report Form" is used to report sales information on existing products in the inventory. This form should be used by sales team members to provide an accurate representation of the current state of products for sale in the inventory.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the relevant information about the product being sold.
2. Enter the "store name" of the product's location.
3. Enter the "product name" and "product description" of the product being sold.
4. Enter the "product price" and "sale quantity" of the product.
5. Select the "inventory status" of the product, which can be "Available", "Out of Stock", or "Special Order".
6. Enter the "sale date" and "sale time" of the product.
7. Enter any additional "notes" about the product sale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Store name** (required: false)
* The "store name" is the name of the location where the product is sold.
* Enter the name of the store where the product is sold.

### 2. **Product name** (required: false)
* The "product name" is the name of the product being sold.
* Enter the name of the product you are reporting on.

### 3. **Product description** (required: false)
* The "product description" is a brief description of the product being sold.
* Enter a brief description of the product.

### 4. **Product price** (required: false)
* The "product price" is the price of the product being sold.
* Enter the price of the product.

### 5. **Sale quantity** (required: false)
* The "sale quantity" is the number of products sold.
* Enter the number of products sold.

### 6. **Inventory status** (required: false)
* The "inventory status" indicates the current status of the product in the inventory.
* Select "Available", "Out of Stock", or "Special Order" to indicate the product's status.

### 7. **Sale date** (required: false)
* The "sale date" is the date the product was sold.
* Enter the date the product was sold.

### 8. **Sale time** (required: false)
* The "sale time" is the time the product was sold.
* Enter the time the product was sold.

### 9. **Notes** (required: false)
* The "notes" field is for any additional information about the product sale.
* Enter any relevant notes or comments about the sale.

### Tips
* Be accurate and detailed when filling out this form to ensure that the inventory is up-to-date.
* Use this form for products that are currently being sold, and avoid duplicating sales reports already submitted.
* If you are unsure about any field or need further guidance, contact the sales team for assistance.
