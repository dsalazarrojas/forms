# project_scalability_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help project managers and team members evaluate the scalability of a project. It guides users through a series of questions to assess the project's potential for growth, resource allocation, and risk management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the project title and goals in the **project_title** and **project_goals** fields, respectively.
2. Provide your assessment on the project's **scalability_assessment** by selecting multiple options from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter your thoughts on **resource_planning** and **resource_challenges** in the text fields.
4. Choose your answer for **risk_management** by selecting one option from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter your thoughts on **project_management** in the text field.
6. Finally, fill in any additional comments or thoughts in the **resource_allocation** field, which is a numerical field for resource allocation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **project_title** (`project_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the project.
* **project_goals** (`project_goals`, text, required: false): Enter the goals of the project.
* **resource_planning** (`resource_planning`, text, required: false): Describe how you plan to allocate resources for the project.
* **scalability_assessment** (`scalability_assessment`, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple options to assess the project's scalability.
	+ Select 'Yes' if the project has a high potential for growth.
	+ Select 'No' if the project has a low potential for growth.
* **resource_allocation** (`resource_allocation`, number, required: false): Enter a numerical value for resource allocation.
* **resource_challenges** (`resource_challenges`, text, required: false): Describe any resource challenges you've encountered while working on the project.
* **risk_management** (`risk_management`, select_one, required: false): Select one option to assess the project's risk management.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have a risk management plan in place.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not have a risk management plan in place.
* **project_management** (`project_management`, text, required: false): Enter your thoughts on project management.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling out each field.
- Use the **scalability_assessment** field to get a comprehensive view of the project's scalability.
- Use the **resource_challenges** field to highlight any resource challenges you've faced.
- Use the **project_management** field to provide insights on project management practices.
- Save your answers and use them for future project planning and assessment.
