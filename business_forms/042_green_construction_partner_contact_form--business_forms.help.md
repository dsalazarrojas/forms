# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the "<string>" form, which is used to collect information from potential partners or collaborators for business projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you.
3. Provide your Phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
4. Select your area of expertise, such as Sustainable Building, Green Building, or Energy Efficiency.
5. Indicate your collaboration interest by selecting Partnership, Collaboration, or Opportunity.
6. Choose a Project Manager from the list.
7. Select the department you are interested in collaborating with.
8. Choose the type of partner you are, such as Partner, Collaborator, or Associate.
9. Enter your company name and location.
10. Enter your partner's website and email address.
11. Enter your partner's phone number.
12. Confirm your partner details by selecting a project manager and department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as per your official name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as per your official name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your official email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your official phone number.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the project you are interested in.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your location or where you operate from.
* **Expertise** (`expertise`, `select_one`, required/true): Select one area of expertise, such as Sustainable Building, Green Building, or Energy Efficiency.
* **Collaboration Interest** (`collaboration_interest`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select one or more of your collaboration interests, such as Partnership, Collaboration, or Opportunity.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose a project manager from the list.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the department you are interested in collaborating with.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required/true): Enter your company name as per your official name.
* **Partner Type** (`partner_type`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose the type of partner you are, such as Partner, Collaborator, or Associate.
* **Partner Location** (`partner_location`, `text`, required/true): Enter your partner's location.
* **Partner Website** (`partner_website`, `text`, required/true): Enter your partner's website.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, `email`, required/true): Enter your partner's email address.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, `text`, required/true): Enter your partner's phone number.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, optional/true): Enter a message, but this field is optional.
* **Message**: You can use this field to provide any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Project Manager**: Choose a project manager from the list.
* **Department**: Select the department you are interested in collaborating with.
* **Collaboration Interest**: Select one or more of your collaboration interests, such as Partnership, Collaboration, or Opportunity.
