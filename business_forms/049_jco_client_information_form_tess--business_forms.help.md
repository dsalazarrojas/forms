# JCOClientsInformationFormTess - Help Guide
## Purpose
The JCOClientsInformationFormTess is designed to collect essential information from clients who are booking a service or appointment with your organization. This form gathers details such as name, contact information, and booking details to ensure a smooth and efficient booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Provide your Email in the "Email" field in the correct format (e.g., john.doe@example.com).
5. Enter your Contact Number in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Enter your Address in the "Address" field, if required.
7. Select your Date Of Birth in the "Date Of Birth" field in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
8. Select your Time Of Booking in the "Time Of Booking" field in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., john.doe@example.com).
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number without any special characters or spaces.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your full address, if required.
- **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Select your Date Of Birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Time Of Booking** (`time_of_booking`, `time`, required): Select your Time Of Booking in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or notes about your booking.

## Tips
- Please double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields or have specific questions, please don't hesitate to contact our support team for assistance.
- If you have already submitted a similar request in the past, please check your previous submissions to see if you have already provided the necessary information.
