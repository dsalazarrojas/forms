# Crane Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crane Safety Inspection Form is used to document and record the results of a crane safety inspection, which is essential for identifying and addressing potential hazards and risks associated with crane usage in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and materials before starting the inspection.
2. Complete the **General Information** section by providing a brief overview of the crane, including its type, location, and any other relevant details.
3. In the **Crane Details** section, enter a detailed description of the crane, including its make, model, serial number, and any relevant specifications.
4. In the **Inspector Information** section, enter your name, contact number, and email address, as well as any other relevant contact information.
5. Select the **Inspection Date** and enter the date and time of the inspection.
6. Select the **Inspection Time** and enter the time of the inspection.
7. Choose one of the three available **Inspector Signature** options to confirm that you conducted the inspection.
8. In the **Inspector Name** field, enter your full name as the inspector.
9. In the **Inspector Phone** field, enter your contact phone number.
10. In the **Inspector Email** field, enter your email address.
11. In the **Comments** field, enter any additional comments or observations related to the crane and its condition.
12. In the **Certification** field, select all the relevant certifications that apply to the crane.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (General Information):
	+ A text field for providing a brief overview of the crane.
* **Crane Details** (Crane Details):
	+ A text field for entering a detailed description of the crane.
* **Inspector Information** (Inspector Information):
	+ A text field for entering your name, contact number, and email address.
* **Inspection Date** (Inspection Date):
	+ A date field for entering the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (Inspection Time):
	+ A time field for entering the time of the inspection.
* **Inspector Signature** (Inspector Signature):
	+ A dropdown field with three options for selecting the inspector's signature.
* **Inspector Name** (Inspector Name):
	+ A text field for entering your full name.
* **Inspector Phone** (Inspector Phone):
	+ A text field for entering your contact phone number.
* **Inspector Email** (Inspector Email):
	+ An email field for entering your email address.
* **Comments** (Comments):
	+ A text field for entering additional comments or observations.
* **Certification** (Certification):
	+ A dropdown field with multiple options for selecting relevant certifications.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all necessary permissions and permissions before conducting the inspection.
* Be thorough and accurate when documenting the crane's details and condition.
* Use standard format for dates and times.
* Use the available options in the **Inspector Signature** field only.
* Enter your full name and contact information in the **Inspector Information** field.
* Keep the **Comments** field concise and focused on important observations.
* Review and update your selected certifications regularly.
* Store this form in a secure location for easy reference.
* This form is intended for internal use only; please do not share it with others without permission.
