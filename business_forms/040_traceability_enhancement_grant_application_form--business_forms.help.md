# Traceability Enhancement Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Traceability Enhancement Grant Application Form is a tool used to help businesses apply for a grant to enhance their traceability. This form is used to gather information about the business and its project, and will help determine the suitability of the grant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field and its purpose.
2. Fill in the general information section with your business details.
3. Provide information about your company structure and management structure.
4. Indicate whether you have management structure positions and provide details.
5. Share your company history.
6. Enter your financials and funding request.
7. Select whether you have a budget in place.
8. Indicate whether you have a key metric for measuring project success.
9. Provide information about your project team.
10. Enter the project timeline.
11. Describe your traceability solution.
12. Provide details about the project leader and other key team members.
13. Check the project status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter your business name, address, and other general information.
* **Company Structure** (`company_structure`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to describe your company structure, including its type and any other relevant details.
* **Management Structure** (`management_structure`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to describe your management structure, including how your company is organized.
* **Management Structure Positions** (`management_structure_positions`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This field is used to indicate whether you have management structure positions and provide details about them.
* **Company History** (`company_history`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to share your company history, including its founding date, key milestones, and any other relevant information.
* **Financials** (`financials`, number, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter your financial information, including your revenue and expenses.
* **Funding Request** (`funding_request`, number, required: false)
	+ This field is used to specify the amount of funding you are requesting.
* **Budget** (`budget`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field is used to indicate whether you have a budget in place.
* **Key Metric** (`key_metric`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field is used to indicate whether you have a key metric for measuring project success.
* **Project Team** (`project_team`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to describe your project team, including their names and roles.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, date, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the project timeline, including its start and end dates.
* **Traceability Solution** (`traceability_solution`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to describe your traceability solution, including its details and benefits.
* **Project Leader** (`project_leader`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the project leader's name and details.
* **Project Coordinator** (`project_coordinator`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the project coordinator's name and details.
* **Project Administrator** (`project_administrator`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the project administrator's name and details.
* **Project Sponsor** (`project_sponsor`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is used to enter the project sponsor's name and details.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field is used to check the project status, whether active or inactive.
