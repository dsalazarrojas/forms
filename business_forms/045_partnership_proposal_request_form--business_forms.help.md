# Partnership Proposal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Partnership Proposal Request Form is used by businesses and organizations to submit partnership proposals to your company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Partnership Proposal Request** field with a brief description of your proposal.
2. Enter your **Business Name** as it appears on your company records.
3. Provide your **Contact Email** so we can get in touch with you.
4. Optionally, you can provide additional information in the **Additional Info** field.
5. Select one of the following options under **Submit**:
	* Option 1: Partner for a specific project
	* Option 2: Collaborate on a long-term partnership
	* Option 3: Explore potential partnership opportunities

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partnership Proposal Request** (1): A brief description of your proposal.
* **Business Name** (2): Your business name as it appears on your company records.
* **Contact Email** (6): A valid email address where we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (7): Your business contact phone number (optional).
* **Additional Info** (8): Any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Submit** (9, 10, 11, 12):
	+ **Submit** (9): Select one of the following options:
		- Option 1: Partner for a specific project
		- Option 2: Collaborate on a long-term partnership
		- Option 3: Explore potential partnership opportunities
	+ **Submit** (10): Select one or more of the following options:
		- Option 1: Partner for a specific project
		- Option 2: Collaborate on a long-term partnership
		- Option 3: Explore potential partnership opportunities
		- Option 4: Provide further information for evaluation
		- Option 5: Schedule a follow-up meeting
	+ **Submit** (11): Select a specific date to submit your proposal.
	+ **Submit** (12): Select a specific time to submit your proposal.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your internal representative.
* This form is for official partnership proposals only. Any other requests will not be accepted.
