# Data Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect data from users for various purposes such as data analysis, surveys, or other business needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your details.
2. For fields marked as 'select_one', choose one option from the list.
3. Ensure all fields that are marked as 'required' are filled in.
4. Double-check your details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, select_one, optional): Choose your job title from the list.
* **Job start date** (`job_start_date`, date, optional): Enter your job start date.
* **Job end date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional): Enter your job end date.
* **Years of experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have worked in your profession.
* **Years of service** (`years_of_service`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been serving in your profession.
* **Job title 1** (`job_title_1`, select_one, optional): Choose your previous job title from the list.
* **Job start date 1** (`job_start_date_1`, date, optional): Enter your previous job start date.
* **Job end date 1** (`job_end_date_1`, date, optional): Enter your previous job end date.
* **Years of experience 1** (`years_of_experience_1`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you had worked in your previous job.
* **Job title 2** (`job_title_2`, not available, not used): Note: This field is not currently used.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in all required fields.
- Choose one option for fields marked as 'select_one'.
- Double-check your details before submitting the form to avoid errors.
