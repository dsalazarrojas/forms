# Tone Frequency Adjustment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to adjust the tone and calibration of a system to optimize its performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the requestor's name in the "requestor" field if required.
2. Enter the specific request or change needed in the "request" field.
3. Choose the type of change needed ("tone" or "calibration") in the "change_type" dropdown menu.
4. Select which type(s) of request(s) you are making in the "change_request_type" field. This field is optional.
5. If necessary, upload any additional context or files in the "upload_file" field.
6. Add any additional notes about the request in the "notes" field.
7. Click the "submit" button to finalize your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tone Frequency Adjustment Form** (`tone_frequency_adjustment_form`, `text`, false): Enter a brief description of this form to aid in identification.
* **Requestor** (`requestor`, `text`, false): Enter the name of the person or organization making the request.
* **Request** (`request`, `text`, false): Enter a clear description of the change requested.
* **Change Type** (`change_type`, `select_one`, true): Choose between "tone" or "calibration" to specify the type of adjustment needed.
* **Change Request Type** (`change_request_type`, `select_multiple`, false): This field is optional and allows you to select multiple types of requests.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, `text`, false): Upload any additional files or context to support your request.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, false): Enter any additional information about the request.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, false): Click this button to finalize your request and submit it for review.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are submitting a well-documented request to help facilitate the review process.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary information is included.
