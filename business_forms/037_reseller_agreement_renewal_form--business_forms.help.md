# Reseller Agreement Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by resellers to provide information about their agreement renewal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as prompted. You will need to provide details about your current agreement, including your business details, contact information, and renewal dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reseller Agreement Renewal Form**: This is the form title.
* **Reseller Agreement Renewal Form (page 1)**: This is the first page of the form.
* **Customer Details**: Provide your business details, including your name and any other relevant information.
* **Agreement Term**: Enter the length of your current agreement term.
* **Renewal Date**: Enter the date of the last renewal or expected renewal date.
* **Reseller Name**: Enter your business name.
* **Contact Details**: Enter any contact information for your company, such as a phone number or email.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person who will be handling contact.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the person who will be handling contact.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who will be handling contact.
* **Agreement Signed Date**: Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Last Payment Date**: Enter the date of the last payment.
* **Next Payment Date**: Enter the expected next payment date.
* **Last Renewal Date**: Enter the date of the last renewal.
* **Current Term Length**: Enter the length of your current agreement term.
* **Remaining Term Length**: Enter the remaining length of your current agreement term.
* **Renewal Term Length**: Enter the length of the new term.
* **Last Renewal Status**: Select the status of the last renewal (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Reason for Renewal**: Enter the reason for the renewal.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the renewal.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Enter any terms and conditions associated with the renewal.
