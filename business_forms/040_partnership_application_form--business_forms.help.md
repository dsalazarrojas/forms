# Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from businesses interested in exploring partnership opportunities with our company. It will help us understand your business needs, interests, and requirements, allowing us to evaluate potential partnerships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and business name as required for the application.
2. Provide a valid email address to ensure we can contact you for further information.
3. Enter your phone number to enable us to reach you for any follow-up questions.
4. Provide your business address to give us a better understanding of your location.
5. Enter your partner's address (if applicable) to facilitate communication and logistics.
6. Double-check all fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partnership Application Form** (`id: 1`): This is the main title of the form.
* **Name** (`id: 2`): Please fill in your name as it appears on your ID or business card.
* **Business Name** (`id: 3`): Enter your business name as it appears on your ID or business card.
* **Email** (`id: 4`): Use a valid email address we can use to contact you for any further questions or information.
* **Phone** (`id: 5`): Enter a contact phone number where we can reach you.
* **Business Address** (`id: 6`): Provide your business address for a better understanding of your location.
* **Partner Address** (`id: 7`): If you have a partner, enter their address for easier communication and logistics.

## Tips
* Make sure all fields are filled in correctly to ensure a smooth processing of your application.
* If you have any supporting documents or additional information, attach them to the email we will use to contact you.
* We will review your application form and may follow up with further questions or requests for more information before proceeding with the partnership.
