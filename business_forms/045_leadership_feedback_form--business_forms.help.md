# Leadership Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from team members or employees about their leaders, managers, or supervisors. This form is designed to gather information about the leadership style, communication, and overall performance of the person in a leadership position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
- Fill out your first and last name, which will help identify you as a contributor to the feedback.
- Choose your job title, which will help categorize you as a team member.
- For each field:
	+ Select 'Yes' or 'No' for department and category questions, depending on whether you have a positive or negative experience.
	+ Type your feedback in the 'Feedback' field. This is an open-ended space for you to write anything you'd like.
	+ Choose your date and time, if applicable.
	+ Provide your email address and phone number, if you're willing to share this information.
- Rate your overall experience using the 'Rating (1-5)' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (text)**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name (text)**: Please enter your last name.
* **Job Title (select_one)**: Choose your job title from the options provided.
* **Department (select_multiple)**: Check all the departments that you have had experience with the leader, such as 'Yes' for 'Yes, this is correct' or 'No' for 'No, this is not correct'. 
* **Feedback (note)**: Write anything you'd like to share with the leader about your experience.
* **Date (date)**: If you're providing feedback about a specific event or event range, use this field to select the date(s) involved.
* **Time (time)**: If you're providing feedback about a specific event or event range, use this field to select the time(s) involved.
* **Category (select_one)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' depending on whether you agree or disagree with a particular leadership trait or action.
* **Email (email)**: If you're willing to share your email address, provide it here. This will allow the leader to contact you for further discussion.
* **Phone (text)**: If you're willing to share your phone number, provide it here. This will allow the leader to contact you for further discussion.
* **Category 2 (select_multiple)**: Check all the categories that you agree or disagree with, such as 'Yes' for 'Yes, this is correct' or 'No' for 'No, this is not correct'. 
* **Rating (1-5)**: Rate your overall experience with the leader using a 1-5 scale, with 1 being 'Very Bad' and 5 being 'Excellent'. 

# Tips
- Be honest and objective when providing feedback.
- Use specific examples and incidents to support your comments.
- Keep your feedback respectful and professional.
