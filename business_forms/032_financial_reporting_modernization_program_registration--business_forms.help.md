# Financial Reporting Modernization Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants enrolling in the Financial Reporting Modernization Program. The program aims to modernize financial reporting processes, making it easier for organizations to manage and report their financial data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Review each field carefully before filling in any information.
2. Ensure you are filling in the correct information for each field.
3. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Enrollment (Page 1)
* **Enrollment** (`enrollment_form`, text, optional): This is the title of the form, which you can leave blank if not applicable.
* **Participant Details** (participant_details_form, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about the participant that is not covered by other fields.
* **Expectations** (expectations_form, text, optional): Describe your expectations from the program, if any.
* **Submit** (submit, text, optional): This button will submit your registration.
* **Back** (back, text, optional): This button will take you back to the previous page.

### Participant Details
* **Participant Organization** (participant_organization_form, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant's organization.
* **Name** (participant_name_form, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Position** (participant_position_form, text, optional): Enter the participant's position or title in the organization.
* **Email** (participant_email_form, text, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone** (participant_phone_form, text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your submission carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the program administrators for assistance.
* You can reach out to the program administrators at form@gic.mx.
