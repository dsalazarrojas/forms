# Building Service Vendor Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback about the quality of service provided by a vendor or service provider. It is meant to help evaluate their performance in areas such as timeliness, professionalism, and customer service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Date:** The evaluation date is the date on which you are submitting this form. This is a required field and should be filled out in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
2. **Vendor or Service Provider Name:** Enter the name of the vendor or service provider being evaluated.
3. **Type of Service:** Select the type of service that was provided from the list of options.
4. **Date Service Was Provided:** Enter the date the service was provided in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. **Contact Person or Supervisor:** Enter the name of the contact person or supervisor you worked with. (Optional)
6. **Your Name:** Enter your name as the person completing the evaluation.
7. **Your Role:** Enter your role or title within the organization. (Optional)
8. **Quality of Work:** Rate the quality of work as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very Poor.
9. **Timeliness and Punctuality:** Rate the timeliness and punctuality as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very Poor.
10. **Professionalism:** Rate the professionalism as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very Poor.
11. **Communication Skills:** Rate the communication skills as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very Poor.
12. **Customer Service:** Rate the customer service as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very Poor.
13. **Problem Solving Ability:** Rate the problem-solving ability as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Did Not Need Problem Solving. (Optional)
14. **Cleanliness After Work:** Rate the cleanliness after work as Spotless, Clean, Acceptable, Messy, or Very Messy.
15. **Value for Cost:** Rate the value for cost as Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value, or Very Poor Value.
16. **Safety and Compliance:** Rate the safety and compliance as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Not Observed. (Optional)
17. **Would You Use This Vendor Again:** Rate your likelihood of using the vendor again as Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Unsure, Probably Not, or Definitely Not.
18. **Would You Recommend to Others:** Rate your willingness to recommend the vendor to others as Highly Recommend, Recommend, Neutral, Do Not Recommend, or Strongly Advise Against.
19. **Vendor Strengths:** Enter any strengths or positive aspects of the vendor's service. (Optional)
20. **Areas for Improvement:** Enter any areas where the vendor could improve. (Optional)
21. **Additional Comments:** Enter any additional comments or feedback about the vendor's service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Date:** When is this evaluation submitted (MM/DD/YYYY)
* **Vendor or Service Provider Name:** Company being evaluated
* **Type of Service:** What service was provided (e.g. Cleaning, Maintenance, etc.)
* **Date Service Was Provided:** When did they work (MM/DD/YYYY)
* **Contact Person or Supervisor:** Who did you work with (Optional)
* **Your Name:** Person completing evaluation
* **Your Role:** Manager, employee, tenant, etc. (Optional)
* **Quality of Work:** How well was the work done
* **Timeliness and Punctuality:** Were they on time and efficient
* **Professionalism:** How professional was the team
* **Communication Skills:** How clear was their communication
* **Customer Service:** How helpful were they
* **Problem Solving Ability:** Could they solve issues (Optional)
* **Cleanliness After Work:** Did they clean up properly
* **Value for Cost:** Was pricing reasonable
* **Safety and Compliance:** Did they follow safety procedures (Optional)
* **Would You Use This Vendor Again:** Would you hire them again
* **Would You Recommend to Others:** Would you suggest to colleagues
* **Vendor Strengths:** What they did well (Optional)
* **Areas for Improvement:** What could be better (Optional)
* **Additional Comments:** Any other feedback
