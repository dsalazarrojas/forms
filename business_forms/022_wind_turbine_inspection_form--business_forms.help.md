# Wind Turbine Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the maintenance and inspection of wind turbines. It helps track and document the status of wind turbines, ensuring they are properly maintained and inspected on a regular basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **General Information** section, which includes basic details about the wind turbine.
2. Then, move on to the **Turbine Details** section, where you can provide more specific information about the turbine.
3. Next, fill out the **Maintenance Schedule** section, which should help you keep track of when the turbine was last maintained and when it's due for maintenance.
4. Finally, report any **Issues Reported** in the respective section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, not required): This field is for entering any general information about the wind turbine. You can use this field to add any notes or comments about the turbine.

### Turbine Details
* **Turbine Details** (`turbine_details`, text, not required): This field is for entering more specific details about the turbine, such as its location, type, or manufacturer.

### Maintenance Schedule
* **Maintenance Schedule** (`maintenance_schedule`, text, not required): This field is for documenting the turbine's maintenance schedule, including when it was last maintained and when it's due for maintenance.

### Issues Reported
* **Issues Reported** (`issues_reported`, text, not required): This field is for reporting any issues that have been observed or experienced with the turbine. This can include problems with the turbine's performance, safety concerns, or other issues that need to be addressed. 

## Tips
* Be sure to keep the form up to date with the latest information.
* Use this form as a starting point for more in-depth inspections or documentation.
* Review the form regularly to ensure that the turbine is being properly maintained and inspected.
