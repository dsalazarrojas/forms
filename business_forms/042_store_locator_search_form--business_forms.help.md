# Store Locator Search Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users find stores based on their search preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **store ID** (optional) to search for a specific store.
2. Enter the **location**, either by typing an address or a zip code of the location you wish to search.
3. Specify the **distance** you wish to search within.
4. Choose the **category** of stores you are interested in (Retail, Service, etc.).
5. Select the **language** of the store's location (English, Spanish, French, etc.).
6. Enter the **phone number** of the store (optional).
7. Enter the **address** of the store (optional).
8. Enter the **postal code** of the store (optional).
9. Select the **country** of the store (United States, Canada, etc.).
10. Choose the **state** of the store (optional).
11. Enter the **street** of the store (optional).
12. Enter the **city** of the store (optional).
13. Enter the **latitude** and **longitude** of the store (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **store_locator_search_form_store_id** (`store_locator_search_form_store_id`, Number, Optional): Enter the ID of the store you wish to search for.
* **search_location** (`search_location`, Text, Optional): Enter the address or zip code of the location you wish to search.
* **search_distance** (`search_distance`, Number, Optional): Enter the distance within which to search for stores.
* **search_radius** (`search_radius`, Number, Optional): Enter the search radius in meters.
* **store_locator_search_form_category** (`store_locator_search_form_category`, Select One, Optional): Choose the category of stores you are interested in (Retail, Service, etc.).
* **search_radius_unit** (`search_radius_unit`, Select One, Optional): Choose the unit of the search radius (miles, kilometers, etc.).
* **store_locator_search_form_location_type** (`store_locator_search_form_location_type`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the type of store (Store, Service Station, etc.).
* **store_locator_search_form_language** (`store_locator_search_form_language`, Select One, Optional): Choose the language of the store's location (English, Spanish, French, etc.).
* **store_locator_search_form_phone_number** (`store_locator_search_form_phone_number`, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the store.
* **store_locator_search_form_address** (`store_locator_search_form_address`, Text, Optional): Enter the address of the store.
* **store_locator_search_form_postal_code** (`store_locator_search_form_postal_code`, Text, Optional): Enter the postal code of the store.
* **store_locator_search_form_country** (`store_locator_search_form_country`, Select One, Optional): Choose the country of the store (United States, Canada, etc.).
* **store_locator_search_form_state** (`store_locator_search_form_state`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the state of the store (Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, etc.).
* **store_locator_search_form_street** (`store_locator_search_form_street`, Text, Optional): Enter the street of the store.
* **store_locator_search_form_city** (`store_locator_search_form_city`, Text, Optional): Enter the city of the store.
* **store_locator_search_form_zip** (`store_locator_search_form_zip`, Text, Optional): Enter the zip code of the store.
* **store_locator_search_form_latitude** (`store_locator_search_form_latitude`, Text, Optional): Enter the latitude of the store.
* **store_locator_search_form_longitude** (`store_locator_search_form_longitude`, Text, Optional): Enter the longitude of the store.
* **store_locator_search_form_submit** (`store_locator_search_form_submit`, Text, Optional): Submit the search form to get the results.
