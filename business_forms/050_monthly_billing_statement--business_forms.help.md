# Monthly Billing Statement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Billing Statement form is used to collect information for monthly billing purposes, including customer details, payment methods, charges, balances, and other relevant information. This form helps ensure accuracy and completeness of the billing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure you have all necessary information available.
2. Fill out the Customer Information section with the customer's name and contact details.
3. Provide the billing information, including the charges and balance.
4. Select the payment method and payment terms.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.
6. Enter the customer's email address.
7. Enter the customer's phone number.
8. Enter the date and time of the billing.
9. Enter the billing address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Fill in the customer's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Billing Info** (`billing_info`, text, optional): Enter the billing details, including the charges and balance.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select whether the payment method is 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Charges Info** (`charges_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the charges.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the payment terms are 'Yes' or 'No', if applicable.
* **Balance** (`balance`, number, optional): Enter the current balance.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the billing statement.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the billing statement.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the billing statement.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the billing address.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or the customer for clarification.
* Review the form for any errors before submitting it.
