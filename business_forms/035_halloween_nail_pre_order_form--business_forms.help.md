# halloween_nail_pre_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Halloween Nail Pre-Order Form" is designed to collect customer information for a pre-order of halloween-themed nail services. This form is meant to be completed by customers who want to book a nail appointment for a halloween-themed nail design.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information in the "Customer Information" section.
2. Choose the services you are interested in from the "Services" dropdown menu.
3. Select the products you want to order from the "Products" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your order details in the "Order Details" field.
5. Provide your date and time for the appointment in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
6. Enter your phone number and email address in the "Phone" and "Email" fields.
7. If you have any special instructions or requests, type them in the "Special Instructions" field.
8. Finally, type any additional customer message in the "Customer Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_information`, text, required/optional): Please fill in your full name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Services** (`service_options`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of service you want to book, such as "Nail Pre-Order Form" or "Other".
* **Products** (`product_options`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the products you want to order from our Halloween-themed collection.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Type in any specific details about your order.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for your appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, required/optional): Provide any special instructions or requests for your appointment.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, required/optional): Type any additional customer message or note.
