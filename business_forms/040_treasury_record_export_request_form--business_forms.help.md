# Treasury Record Export Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request the export of treasury records. It is used to gather information for the export of financial data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the purpose of the export request.
2. Select the date range for the export.
3. Choose the type of records to export.
4. Enter any additional information or details as required.
