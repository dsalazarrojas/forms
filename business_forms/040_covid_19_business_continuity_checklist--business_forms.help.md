# COVID 19 Business Continuity Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Business Continuity Checklist is designed to help businesses assess their readiness and response strategies to the pandemic, ensuring they are well-equipped to maintain continuity and minimize disruptions in the event of a pandemic.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete the form by selecting the relevant options for each section, indicating your business's current state of readiness for each area.
2. Fill in the necessary information in the text fields (e.g., name, address, contact details).
3. Review and revise the sections as needed based on your business's specific situation.
4. Submit the completed form for evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (1, text, required): This field is for your business's legal name.
* **Business Address** (2, text, required): This field is for your business's complete address.
* **Contact Person** (3, text, required): This field is for your business's contact person's full name.
* **Contact Title** (4, text, required): This field is for your business's contact person's title.
* **Contact Email** (5, email, required): This field is for your business's contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (6, text, required): This field is for your business's contact phone number.
* **Assessment Date** (7, date, required): This field is for the date the assessment was completed.
* **Industry** (8, select_one, required): This field is for your business's sector.
* **Number of Employees** (9, number, required): This field is for your business's total workforce size.
* **-- Health and Safety Protocols --**
* **Written Safety Plan** (11, select_one, required): This field indicates if you have a written safety plan in place to address COVID 19.
* **Employee Health Screening** (12, select_one, required): This field indicates if you implement employee health screening measures.
* **Mask Policy** (13, select_one, required): This field indicates if you require masks for all or specific employees.
* **Social Distancing Measures** (14, select_one, required): This field indicates if you have measures in place to maintain physical distancing.
* **Hand Hygiene Stations** (15, select_one, required): This field indicates if you provide hand hygiene stations.
* **Enhanced Cleaning Protocol** (16, select_one, required): This field indicates if you have an enhanced cleaning schedule.
* **PPE Availability** (17, select_one, required): This field indicates if you have a supply of personal protective equipment.
* **Ventilation Improved** (18, select_one, required): This field indicates if you have improved ventilation in your workspace.
* **-- Workforce Management --**
* **Remote Work Policy** (20, select_one, required): This field indicates if you have a remote work policy.
* **Sick Leave Policy** (21, select_one, required): This field indicates if you have a sick leave policy for employees.
* **Vaccination Policy** (22, select_one, required): This field indicates if you require vaccinations for all or specific employees.
* **Testing Available** (23, select_one, required): This field indicates if you provide on-site testing for employees.
* **Contact Tracing Plan** (24, select_one, required): This field indicates if you have a plan for contact tracing.
* **-- Operations Continuity --**
* **Capacity Limits** (26, select_one, required): This field indicates if you have capacity limits.
* **Contactless Services** (27, select_one, required): This field indicates if you offer contactless services.
* **Supply Chain Contingency** (28, select_one, required): This field indicates if you have alternative suppliers.
* **Inventory Management** (29, select_one, required): This field indicates if you manage your stock levels.
* **Financial Reserves** (30, select_one, required): This field indicates if you have financial reserves.
* **-- Communication --**
* **Employee Communication Plan** (32, select_one, required): This field indicates if you have a plan for regular employee updates.
* **Customer Communication** (33, select_one, required): This field indicates if you have a policy for customer notifications.
* **Safety Signage** (34, select_one, required): This field indicates if you have safety signage posted.
* **-- Compliance --**
* **Local Guidelines Followed** (36, select_one, required): This field indicates if you follow local guidelines.
* **State Guidelines Followed** (37, select_one, required): This field indicates if you follow state guidelines.
* **Federal Guidelines Followed** (38, select_one, required): This field indicates if you follow federal guidelines.
* **Documentation Maintained** (39, select_one, required): This field indicates if you keep records of compliance.
* **Overall Pandemic Readiness** (40, select_one, required): This field indicates your level of preparedness for the pandemic.
* **Priority Improvements Needed** (41, text, required): This field is for indicating areas that need improvement.
* **Additional Resources Needed** (42, text, optional): This field is for indicating resources that would be helpful for pandemic readiness.
* **Assessor Signature** (43, text, required): This field is for your full name.
* **Date Completed** (44, date, required): This field is for the date the assessment was completed.
