# Envelope Efficiency Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Envelope Efficiency Registration Form is designed to gather information from individuals to analyze and improve envelope distribution and usage within the company. By completing this form, you are contributing to a comprehensive data set used to optimize envelope-related processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields (marked as "required").
2. Enter accurate and valid information in each field.
3. Review and verify your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is displayed on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is displayed on your official documents.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or institution.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your work or business phone number.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other (type "Other" and describe your reason)
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the provided options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other (type "Other" and describe your reason)
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes related to your submission (this field is not required).
