# Customer Arrival Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers upon their arrival at the business. It is used to understand the reason for their visit, provide any necessary contact information, and gather feedback on their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the "Reason of visit" field with a brief description of the purpose of your visit.
2. If you have a reservation number, please enter it in the corresponding field.
3. If you wish to provide feedback on your experience, select one or more of the options provided in the "Rate your experience" field.
4. If you have any additional comments or instructions for our staff, please enter them in the "Additional comments" field.
5. You may also leave any instructions for our staff in the "Instruction to our staff" field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reason of visit** (`reason_of_visit`, Text, Optional): Briefly describe the purpose of your visit.
- **Reservation number** (`reservation_number`, Text, Optional): Enter your reservation number, if applicable.
- **Rate your experience** (`rate_your_experience`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or more of the options to rate your experience. The options are:
  • Good
  • Fair
- **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or information you wish to share with our staff.
- **Instruction to our staff** (`instruction_to_our_staff`, Text, Optional): Leave any instructions or information for our staff, if needed.

## Tips
- Please be brief and concise in your responses.
- If you have a reservation number, please ensure it is accurate to receive any applicable benefits or services.
- If you wish to provide feedback, please select all applicable options.
