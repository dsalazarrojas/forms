# market_feasibility_research_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The market feasibility research request form is designed to collect information about market feasibility research projects from businesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide clear and concise information in the required fields.
3. Double-check the form for spelling and grammar errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Research Brief** (Field 2): A brief description of the research project.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field is optional and should be a short description of the research project.
* **Market Feasibility Research** (Field 3): A description of the market feasibility research project.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should provide a detailed description of the research project.
* **Research Objectives** (Field 4): The objectives of the research project.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should describe the goals and objectives of the research project.
* **Research Questions** (Field 5): A list of research questions to be answered by the research project.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should list the specific questions that need to be answered by the research project.
* **Research Brief Details** (Field 6): Additional details about the research project.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should provide any additional information about the research project.
* **Target Market** (Field 7): A description of the target market.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should describe the target market for the research project.
* **Research Methodology** (Field 8): The approach used to conduct the research project.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should describe the methodology used to conduct the research project.
* **Research Timeframe** (Field 9): The timeframe for the research project.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should describe the start and end dates of the research project.
* **Research Benefits** (Field 10): The benefits of the research project.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should describe the expected benefits of the research project.
* **Research Risks** (Field 11): The potential risks of the research project.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should describe any potential risks associated with the research project.
* **Budget Allocation** (Field 12): The budget allocated for the research project.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should describe the budget allocated for the research project.
* **Project Status** (Field 13): The current status of the research project.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should describe the current status of the research project.
* **Project Deadline** (Field 14): The deadline for the research project.
	+ Type: Date.
	+ This field should indicate the start and end dates of the research project.
* **Contact Person** (Field 15): The contact person for the research project.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should provide the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (Field 16): The email of the contact person.
	+ Type: Email.
	+ This field should be the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Field 17): The phone number of the contact person.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should provide the phone number of the contact person.
* **Company** (Field 18): The company conducting the research project.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should provide the name of the company.
* **Department** (Field 19): The department conducting the research project.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should provide the department conducting the research project.
* **Job Title** (Field 20): The job title of the contact person.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should provide the job title of the contact person.
* **Address** (Field 21): The address of the contact person.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should provide the physical address of the contact person.
* **Zip Code** (Field 22): The zip code of the contact person's address.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should provide the postal code of the contact person's address.
* **City** (Field 23): The city of the contact person's address.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should provide the city of the contact person's address.
* **State** (Field 24): The state of the contact person's address.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should provide the state of the contact person's address.
* **Country** (Field 25): The country of the contact person's address.
	+ Type: Text.
	+ This field should provide the country of the contact person's address.
