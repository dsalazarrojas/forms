# Aircraft Brokerage Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aircraft Brokerage Agreement Form is a crucial document for brokers and clients to formalize their partnership and ensure a smooth transaction process. It outlines the terms and conditions of their collaboration, including brokerage fees, client responsibilities, and dispute resolution methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information on each page.
2. Provide your brokerage firm's or individual's name and details under "Broker Name".
3. Enter the client's name and contact information under "Client Name".
4. Describe the aircraft, including its make, model, year, and registration, under "Aircraft Description".
5. If applicable, enter the proposed sale price or valuation under "Listing Price".
6. Specify the commission terms, if agreed upon.
7. If applicable, define the timeframe for the exclusive brokerage under "Exclusivity Period".
8. List the client's obligations under the agreement under "Client Obligations".
9. Choose a method for resolving disputes, if necessary.
10. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm mutual understanding and acknowledgement.
