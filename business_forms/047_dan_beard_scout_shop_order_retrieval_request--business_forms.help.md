# dan-beard-scout-shop-order-retrieval-request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to request updates on their existing orders, allowing them to communicate with our team for order status inquiries or related concerns.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information. Please note that some fields are optional, but providing accurate details will help us serve you better.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your order documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, optional): Your contact number for us to reach out to you directly.
* **Order Id** (`order_id`, number, optional): Enter your order ID for order status updates or inquiries.
* **Select One Field** (`select_one_field`, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you have an order inquiry or concern related to this field.
* **Select Multiple Field** (`select_multiple_field`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable options that describe your order concerns or status updates.
* **Date Field** (`date_field`, date, optional): Enter the date relevant to your order inquiry or concern.
* **Time Field** (`time_field`, time, optional): Enter the time relevant to your order inquiry or concern.
* **Note Field** (`note_field`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or questions you have regarding your order.
* **Email Field** (`email_field`, email, optional): Your email address for order updates or direct communication.
* **Phone Field** (`phone_field`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for quick contact in case we need to reach out to you.
## Tips
* For accurate order status updates, please ensure you've entered the correct Order ID.
* Please select the appropriate options for Select One Field and Select Multiple Field according to your specific order concerns.
* For any inquiries, provide as much detail as possible in the Note Field for efficient order resolution.
