# Mall Security Breach Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and document mall security breaches, ensuring that incidents are properly investigated and responded to in a timely and effective manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Breach Description**: Provide a clear and concise description of the incident, including any relevant details such as the circumstances surrounding the breach.
2. **Date of Incident**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
3. **Time of Incident**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
4. **Location of Incident**: Select the location where the incident took place, such as Mall Entrance, Mall Aisle, or Mall Exit.
5. **Description of Incident**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
6. **Number of Victims**: Enter the number of people affected by the incident.
7. **Number of Injuries**: Enter the number of people injured during the incident.
8. **Number of Deaths**: (Optional) Enter the number of people who lost their lives during the incident.
9. **Witness Statements**: Provide any witness statements or testimonies related to the incident.
10. **Video Footage**: If available, upload any relevant video footage of the incident.
11. **Photo Footage**: If available, upload any relevant photos of the incident.
12. **Investigation Status**: Select the current status of the investigation, such as In Progress, Completed, or Pending.
13. **Security Response**: Choose the team responsible for responding to the incident, such as Security Response Team, Mall Management, or Mall Security Team.
14. **Incident Reporting Officer**: Select the officer who reported the incident, such as John Doe, Jane Doe, or Security Manager.
15. **Incident Report Date**: Enter the date the incident was reported.
16. **Incident Report Time**: Enter the time the incident was reported.
17. **Incident Reported By**: Enter the email address of the person who reported the incident.
18. **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the person who reported the incident.
19. **Incident Reporter Name**: Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
