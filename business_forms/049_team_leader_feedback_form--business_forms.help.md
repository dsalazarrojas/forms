# team_leader_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The team leader feedback form is a tool designed to facilitate feedback and communication within the team, allowing team members to provide constructive feedback to their colleagues, team leads, and management. This form is meant to be used when a team member wants to express their thoughts, ideas, or concerns to their team lead or management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing the Employee Name, which should be the name of the team member submitting the feedback.
2.  Enter the feedback_date to specify the date the feedback was given or is due to be given.
3.  In the Feedback Details section, describe the specific feedback or concern you have, providing as much detail as possible.
4.  If applicable, indicate the Employee Feedback section for your personal feedback or thoughts.
5.  In the Feedback Given To section, specify to whom the feedback is being given (Team Lead or Management).
6.  Select the Feedback Type from the options provided to categorize the feedback.
7.  If your team is organized into departments, enter the Department ID in the Department field.
8.  Enter any relevant information about the Team Lead in the Team Lead field, if it's applicable.
9.  For the Feedback Status, select the relevant status from the options (In Progress or Completed).
10.  In the Additional Comments section, you can enter any further information you'd like to share with the team lead or manager.
11.  Finally, submit the form for review and consideration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name (text)**: This is the name of the team member submitting the feedback.
*   **Feedback Date (date)**: This field captures the date the feedback was given or is scheduled to be given.
*   **Feedback Details (text)**: Use this section to provide a detailed explanation of the feedback or concern you have.
*   **Employee Feedback (text)**: If you have personal feedback or thoughts, enter them here.
*   **Feedback Given To (text)**: Indicate to whom the feedback is being directed (Team Lead or Management).
*   **Feedback Type (select multiple)**: Select from the provided options to categorize the feedback (Leadership, Management, Team Management).
*   **Department (number)**: If your team is organized into departments, enter the Department ID here.
*   **Team Lead (text)**: Provide any relevant information about the Team Lead, if applicable.
*   **Feedback Status (select one)**: Choose the relevant status from the options (In Progress or Completed).
*   **Follow Up (text)**: Enter any additional comments or follow-up information.
*   **Additional Comments (text)**: Enter any further thoughts or information you'd like to share with the team lead or manager.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out the form thoroughly and accurately, ensuring all required fields are completed.
*   Be specific and clear in your feedback, providing concrete details and examples.
*   Proofread your submission for spelling and grammar errors before submitting.
