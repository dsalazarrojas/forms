## international_missions_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The international_missions_form is used to capture and organize information about international missions. This form is intended to provide a standardized and structured way to collect and store mission details, dates, times, locations, descriptions, goals, team members, and contact numbers for international activities. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the mission details, including any relevant information about the mission.
2. Select a date for the mission.
3. Choose a time for the mission.
4. Provide the location of the mission.
5. Enter a brief description of the mission.
6. Define the mission goals.
7. Indicate whether team members are present during the mission.
8. Enter the contact number for the mission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mission Details** (id: 1, text, required: false): Enter the key details about the mission, including any relevant notes or information.
* **Mission Date** (id: 2, date, required: false): Select a date for the mission from the calendar. Please use the format of year, month, day (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Mission Time** (id: 3, time, required: false): Choose a time for the mission from the available options. Please use 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Mission Location** (id: 4, text, required: false): Enter the location of the mission, including city, state, or region.
* **Mission Description** (id: 5, text, required: false): Write a brief description of the mission.
* **Mission Goals** (id: 6, text, required: false): Enter the key goals and objectives of the mission.
* **Team Members** (id: 7, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether team members are present or not by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'. If no team members are present, you can submit the form without making a selection.
* **Contact Number** (id: 8, text, required: false): Enter the contact number for the mission, which can be a phone number or email address.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information for each field to ensure accurate mission records.
* If team members are not present, you can still submit the form without selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the Team Members field.
* Use the format YYYY-MM-DD for the Mission Date and 24-hour clock format (HH:MM) for the Mission Time.
