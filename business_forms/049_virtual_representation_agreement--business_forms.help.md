# Virtual Representation Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about a virtual representation agreement between a user and a client. The purpose of this form is to document the details of the agreement, including user and client information, agreement specifics, and signatures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the user's details, including their name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide the agreement name and date.
3. Enter the client's name and address.
4. Indicate whether the user and client have signed the agreement.
5. Enter the date the agreement was signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User Details** (User Details, text, required)
Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.

### 2. **Agreement Name** (Agreement Name, text, required)
Enter a name for the virtual representation agreement.

### 3. **Representation Date** (Representation Date, date, required)
Enter the date when the representation will take place.

### 4. **Client Name** (Client Name, text, required)
Enter the name of the client with whom you are entering into the agreement.

### 5. **Client Address** (Client Address, text, required)
Enter the address of the client.

### 6. **User Signature** (User Signature, select_one, required)
Indicate whether you have signed the agreement by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 7. **Client Signature** (Client Signature, select_one, required)
Indicate whether the client has signed the agreement by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 8. **Date Signed** (Date Signed, date, required)
Enter the date when the agreement was signed.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you need to make any changes to the agreement, update the relevant fields accordingly.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is correct.
