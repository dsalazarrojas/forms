# Export Control Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Export Control Screening Questionnaire is a form used to collect information from users to determine if an export is subject to any export control regulations. It is designed to be completed by export administrators to ensure that all relevant export control regulations are considered before exporting goods or services.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Select the correct country (or countries) from the export control list on pages 2 and 3.
3. Enter any additional notes if required.
4. Enter the date and time of the export.
5. Enter your email and phone number for contact information.
6. Review your form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant general information about the export.

### Export Control List (Page 2)
* **Select One** (`export_control_list_1`, select_one, optional): Select one country from the export control list. This will help us determine if the export is subject to any export restrictions. The options are:
	+ **us**: United States
	+ **uk**: United Kingdom

### Export Control List (Page 2)
* **Select Multiple** (`export_control_list_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple countries from the export control list. This will help us determine if the export is subject to any export restrictions. The options are:
	+ **us**: United States
	+ **uk**: United Kingdom
	+ **au**: Australia
	+ **ca**: Canada

### Date and Time (Page 3)
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the export.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the export.

### Notes (Page 4)
* **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the export.

### Contact Information (Page 5)
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact information.
