# Request A Free Estimate - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers or potential customers to request a free estimate for our services. Our team will review the information you provide and get back to you with a detailed estimate of the cost and time required for your project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the type of service you need from the options provided (Plumbing, Electrical, or Carpentry).
2. Enter your name, contact number, and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your address in the provided field.
4. Add any additional information you'd like us to know about your project in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What service type do you need?** (`service_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of service you're looking for (Plumbing, Electrical, or Carpentry). This will help us understand what kind of expertise we'll need to provide for your project.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name so we can address you directly and get back to you with your estimate.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact number so we can reach you easily.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can get back to you with your estimate and any other updates.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your address so we can know where to send your estimate and any other relevant documents.
* **Additional Information** (`message`, note, required: false): Add any additional details about your project that you think we should know, like any specific materials or equipment needed.
