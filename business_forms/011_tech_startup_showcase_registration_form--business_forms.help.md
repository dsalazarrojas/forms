# Tech Startup Showcase Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Startup Showcase Registration Form is a simple form designed to collect information about your tech startup and its upcoming events. This form is used by tech startups to register for events, conferences, and meetups, and to showcase their presence in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details: 
	* First Name
	* Last Name
2. Enter your contact information:
	* Email (a valid email address)
	* Phone (optional)
3. Share your company details:
	* Company Name
4. Add your event information:
	* Event Name
	* Event Date (in date format, e.g., 2024-02-20)
	* Event Time (in 24-hour format, e.g., 14:00)
	* Event Location
	* Event Description (a brief description of your event)
	* Event Type (e.g., Tech Conference, Trade Show, Workshop, Webinar)
	* Event Fee (optional, e.g., Free, Paid)
	* Event Link (optional)
	* Event Notes (additional notes about your event)
5. Add any additional notes about your startup:
	* Additional Notes (any additional information about your startup)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, optional): Enter your company website.
* **Industry Type** (`industry_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the industry type of your company (Software and Services, Hardware and Electronics, Media and Entertainment, Other).
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select the size of your company (1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-500, 501+).
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your event in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of your event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of your event (Tech Conference, Trade Show, Workshop, Webinar, Other).
* **Event Fee** (`event_fee`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select whether your event is Free or Paid.
* **Event Link** (`event_link`, `select_one`, optional): Select the link to your event (https://example.com, https://example2.com).
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your event.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about your startup.
