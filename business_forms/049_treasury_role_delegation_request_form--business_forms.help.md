# Treasury Role Delegation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Role Delegation Request Form is used to request delegation of a role within the treasury department. This form is intended to facilitate role delegation when a role holder is unable to perform their duties due to absence, other responsibilities, or other unforeseen circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your user information, including your name and email address.
2.  Provide your phone number for further contact, if necessary.
3.  Select the role you wish to delegate.
4.  Indicate whether the new delegate will take on the role permanently or only temporarily, i.e., whether the new delegate will take on the role until further notice or just for a specific period of time.
5.  Choose the start and end dates for the delegation period, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information (`user_info`):** This field asks for your basic user information. Please enter your full name to help ensure accurate handling of the role request.
    *   **Label:** User Information
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** Yes
*   **Email (`email`):** Enter your official email address for communication related to your role delegation request.
    *   **Label:** Email
    *   **Type:** Email
    *   **Required:** Yes
*   **Phone (`phone`):** Provide your contact number for any further communication.
    *   **Label:** Phone
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** No
*   **Role to Delegate (`role_to_delegate`):** Select the specific role you wish to delegate. Please choose from the available options (Role A, Role B, Role C).
    *   **Label:** Role to Delegate
    *   **Type:** Select One
    *   **Options:** Role A, Role B, Role C (select one only)
*   **New Delegate Role (`new_delegate_role_to_delegate`):** Specify whether the new delegate will take on the role permanently or just for a limited time. Choose 'Yes' for permanent delegation or 'No' for limited time delegation.
    *   **Label:** New Delegate Role
    *   **Type:** Select One
    *   **Options:** Yes, No
*   **Start Date (`start_date`):** If the delegation is for a limited time, enter the start date of the delegation period.
    *   **Label:** Start Date
    *   **Type:** Date
    *   **Required:** No
*   **End Date (`end_date`):** If the delegation is for a limited time, enter the end date of the delegation period.
    *   **Label:** End Date
    *   **Type:** Date
    *   **Required:** No

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate information so that the role delegation can be handled efficiently.
- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to delegate the specific role you are requesting.
- If your role delegation request is for a limited time, make sure to provide both the start and end dates to clarify the delegation period.
