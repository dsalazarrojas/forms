# Workforce Culture Innovation Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register participants for the Workforce Culture Innovation Challenge, a program aimed at driving cultural innovation within organizations. This form captures essential details to ensure proper registration and participant engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Registration Form (Page 1) with the required details.
2. Provide Participant Details (Page 2), including your name, title, and department/role.
3. Share your Innovation Ideas (Page 3) for the challenge.
4. Add your Email and Phone numbers (Page 4) for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form (Page 1)** (`registration_form`, text, required: false): This page is the initial registration form where you provide a brief description of your registration.
* **Participant Details (Page 2)** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): In this section, please enter your name, title, and department/role to help us understand your affiliation and position within the organization.
* **Innovation Ideas (Page 3)** (`innovation_ideas`, text, required: false): Describe your proposed innovation idea, including a brief overview of the solution, its benefits, and any relevant supporting materials.
* **Email (Page 4)** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address for communication and updates about the challenge and program.
* **Phone (Page 5)** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for additional contact and discussion purposes.
