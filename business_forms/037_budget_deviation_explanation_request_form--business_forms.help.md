# Budget Deviation Explanation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an explanation for a budget deviation, which is a significant variance between the actual and budgeted amounts for a project or expense.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address as the requestor.
2. Select your department from the drop-down list.
3. Enter the date of the request.
4. Specify the fiscal period for which the deviation applies.
5. Provide the budget line item affected.
6. Enter the original budgeted amount and the actual amount spent.
7. Calculate the deviation amount by subtracting the actual amount from the budgeted amount.
8. Enter the percentage deviation, if applicable.
9. Select the type of deviation (Over Budget or Under Budget).
10. Provide a clear explanation for the deviation.
11. Identify any primary cost drivers that contributed to the deviation.
12. Propose a corrective action plan.
13. Indicate whether any adjustments to the project timeline are necessary.
14. Describe any potential impact on other areas or projects.
15. Document any lessons learned to prevent future deviations.
16. Indicate the status of your supervisor's review.
17. Confirm whether supporting documentation (receipts, quotes, etc.) are attached.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Requester Name** (`requester_name`): Enter your full name.
- **Email Address** (`requester_email`): Enter your departmental email address.
- **Department** (`requester_department`): Select your department from the drop-down list.
- **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date of the request.
- **Fiscal Period** (`fiscal_period`): Specify the fiscal period for which the deviation applies.
- **Budget Line Item** (`budget_line_item`): Enter the budget line item affected.
- **Original Budget** (`budgeted_amount`): Enter the original budgeted amount.
- **Actual Amount Spent** (`actual_amount`): Enter the actual amount spent.
- **Deviation Amount** (`deviation_amount`): This is calculated by subtracting the actual amount from the budgeted amount.
- **Deviation Percentage** (`deviation_percentage`): Enter the percentage deviation, if applicable.
- **Type of Deviation** (`deviation_type`): Select the type of deviation (Over Budget or Under Budget).
- **Reason for Deviation** (`deviation_reason`): Provide a clear explanation for the deviation.
- **Primary Cost Drivers** (`cost_drivers`): Identify any primary cost drivers that contributed to the deviation.
- **Corrective Action Plan** (`corrective_action`): Propose a corrective action plan.
- **Timeline Adjustment Needed** (`timeline_adjustment`): Indicate whether any adjustments to the project timeline are necessary.
- **Impact on Other Areas** (`impact_assessment`): Describe any potential impact on other areas or projects.
- **Lessons Learned** (`lesson_learned`): Document any lessons learned to prevent future deviations.
- **Supervisor Review Status** (`supervisor_review`): Indicate the status of your supervisor's review.
- **Supporting Documentation Attached** (`supporting_docs`): Confirm whether supporting documentation (receipts, quotes, etc.) is attached.

## Tips
- Please provide clear and concise explanations for all fields.
- Ensure that supporting documentation is attached where applicable.
- Review and finalize this form with your supervisor before submission.
