# A3 Problem Solving Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The A3 Problem Solving Form is a structured worksheet designed to help teams identify and address problems in their work processes. It provides a framework for documenting and solving problems in a methodical and transparent way.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. **Write a concise problem statement** in the "Problem statement" field, describing the issue and its impact.
2. **Provide background and context** in the "Background and context" field, including key facts and history about the problem.
3. **Describe the current condition** of the problem in the "Current condition (observed)" field, focusing on measured performance or behaviors.
4. **Identify the target condition** in the "Target condition (goal)" field, outlining the desired outcome after implementing countermeasures.
5. **Choose probable root causes** in the "Probable root causes (select all that apply)" field, selecting all applicable causes from the options provided.
6. **Propose countermeasures** in the "Proposed countermeasures" field, listing actions and explaining why they help.
7. **Create an implementation plan** in the "Implementation plan summary" field, outlining steps, owners, and deadlines.
8. **Assign a countermeasure owner** in the "Countermeasure owner" field, identifying the individual responsible for execution.
9. **Set an implementation start date** in the "Implementation start date" field, if applicable.
10. **Estimate an expected completion date** in the "Expected completion date" field, if applicable.
11. **Define success metrics and targets** in the "Success metrics and targets" field, outlining how improvement will be measured.
12. **Schedule a follow-up review date** in the "Follow-up review date" field, if applicable.
13. **Document results and lessons learned** in the "Results and lessons learned" field, summarizing outcomes after the follow-up review.
14. **Select the current status** in the "Current status" field, choosing from the options: "Planned", "In progress", "Completed", or "On hold".
15. **Add any additional comments** in the "Additional comments" field, providing open-ended notes for the team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Problem statement** (Required): A concise description of the problem and its impact.
* **Background and context** (Optional): Key facts and history about the problem.
* **Current condition (observed)** (Required): Describe measured performance or behaviors now.
* **Target condition (goal)** (Required): The desired outcome after implementing countermeasures.
* **Probable root causes (select all that apply)** (Required): Choose causes identified during analysis.
* **Proposed countermeasures** (Required): List corrective actions and explain why they help.
* **Implementation plan summary** (Required): Brief steps, owners, and deadlines.
* **Countermeasure owner** (Required): Individual responsible for execution.
* **Implementation start date** (Optional): Date to start implementation.
* **Expected completion date** (Optional): Estimated completion date.
* **Success metrics and targets** (Required): Define how improvement will be measured (e.g., defect rate).
* **Follow-up review date** (Optional): Date to evaluate results.
* **Results and lessons learned** (Optional): Summarize outcomes after follow-up review.
* **Current status** (Required): Select from options: "Planned", "In progress", "Completed", or "On hold".
* **Additional comments** (Optional): Open-ended notes for the team.
