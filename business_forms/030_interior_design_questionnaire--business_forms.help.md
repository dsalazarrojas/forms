# Interior Design Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client information and project details for an interior design project. It will help the client to provide all necessary details about their project, preferences, and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the form completely and accurately, ensuring that you provide all required information.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the explanations below.
- Review your submission carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Information**: This field is used to capture the client's details such as name, address, and other relevant contact information.
	+ Please provide your full name and contact information.
- **Contact Details**: This field is used to gather any additional contact information or communication preferences.
	+ Provide your email address, phone number, or any other way we can contact you.
- **Design Questions**: This field is intended for you to ask us any design-related questions.
	+ Be as specific as possible to help us understand your design preferences.
- **Client Preferences**: This field is used to gather any specific preferences you have regarding the project timeline, design style, or other project requirements.
	+ Provide clear and detailed information to help us better understand your expectations.
- **Space Dimensions**: This field is used to provide dimensions of the space where the design will be applied.
	+ Measure or provide exact dimensions to help us better understand the space.
- **Project Deadline**: This field is used to set a deadline for the project completion.
	+ Provide a specific date or timeframe to help us plan the project.
- **Communication Preferences**: This field is used to gather how you prefer to communicate with us about the project.
	+ Choose your preferred method of communication (e.g., email, phone, or in-person).
- **Payment Details**: This field is used to provide payment information for the project.
	+ Provide payment details or any other relevant payment information.
- **Project Status**: This field is used to update the project status.
	+ Please update the status as needed to help us track the project progress.
- **Project Notes**: This field is used to provide any additional information about the project.
	+ Add any relevant project notes that might be helpful for the project team.
- **Contact Information**: This field is used to gather any additional contact information.
	+ Provide any other relevant contact information.
- **Additional Notes 1-8**: These fields are used to capture any additional information about the project.
	+ Use them as needed to provide any extra context or details.
- **Submission**: This field is used to submit the form.
	+ Once you've filled in all the required information, click the "Submit" button.
- **Back to Form**: This field is used to return to the form for further editing or corrections.
	+ Use it to review or modify any fields before submitting.
- **Reset**: This field is used to reset the form to its original state.
	+ Use it to clear any filled-in fields before resubmitting.
- **Submit**: This field is used to submit the form once all fields are filled in.
	+ Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting.

Note: The provided form fields are mostly text fields. Please provide clear and concise text responses to ensure accurate and efficient processing of your project details.
