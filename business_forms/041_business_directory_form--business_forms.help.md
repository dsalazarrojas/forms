## Business Directory Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit your business information for listing in a directory that will display your company name, description, contact information, and location to help customers or partners find and contact you.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your company name and description in the provided fields. This will help potential customers know what your business does and what services or products you offer.
*   Choose your primary industry or sector from the list provided. This will help categorize your business and make it easier for customers to find.
*   Select the type of business entity (e.g., Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, etc.) you are.
*   Enter the year your business was established.
*   Provide contact information, including the name of the main contact person, their title, phone number, email address, and website (if applicable).
*   Enter the street address, suite number, city, state or province, postal code, and country of operation.
*   If you have social media profiles, enter the links so customers can connect with you on these platforms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the legal name of your business.
*   **Company Description** (`company_description`, text, required): Briefly describe what your business does or offers.
*   **Industry Category** (`industry_category`, text, required): Select the primary industry or sector your business operates in.
*   **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of business entity (e.g., Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, etc.).
*   **Year Established** (`year_established`, number, required): Enter the year your business was founded.
*   **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the main contact person for your business.
*   **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the primary contact person.
*   **Business Phone Number** (`business_phone`, text, required): Enter the main phone number for your business.
*   **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, required): Enter the main email address for your business.
*   **Website URL** (`website_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your company website (if applicable).
*   **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Enter the street address of your business location.
*   **Suite/Unit Number** (`suite_number`, text, optional): Enter the suite or unit number of your business location (if applicable).
*   **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city location of your business.
*   **State/Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Enter the state or province of operation.
*   **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter the ZIP or postal code of your business location.
*   **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter the country of operation.
*   **Social Media Links** (`social_media_links`, text, optional): Enter links to your social media profiles for customers to connect with you.

Note that some fields are optional and only require a value if you have them.

Note: This guide is a general help guide, please adjust fields according to your specific use case.
