# Online Survey Customization Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your online survey customization needs, so our team can tailor the survey to suit your requirements. Please fill out this form to request a customized survey that meets your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of survey you are requesting: Internal or External.
2. Choose the purpose of your survey: Get Feedback, Conduct Research, or Test.
3. Enter your organization's contact information: Contact Number and Email Address.
4. Specify the desired features for your survey: Survey design, Survey theme, and Question types.
5. Choose the desired data format: CSV, JSON, or SQL.
6. (Optional) Upload any relevant files.
7. Provide additional comments or information about your survey needs.
8. (Optional) Specify any custom fields you would like to include in your survey.
