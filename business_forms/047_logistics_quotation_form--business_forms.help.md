# logistics_quotation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The logistics_quotation_form is a tool for creating or editing shipment quotations. It captures critical information about the shipment, including details about the shipment, the shipping method, package details, and more. This form is used by logistics staff or administrators to manage shipments, track shipments, or communicate with customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Shipment Details**: Enter a brief description of the shipment, such as the package contents and dimensions.
2. **Shipping Method**: Select the method of transportation or delivery (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
3. **Package Details**: Add any additional information about the package, such as weight or special care requirements.
4. **Shipment Status**: Choose the current status of the shipment from the available options (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
5. **Shipment Service Charge**: Enter any extra service fees or charges (if applicable).
6. **Shipment Handling**: Enter any extra handling charges (if applicable).
7. **Shipment Distance**: Enter the distance of the shipment.
8. **Shipment Weight**: Enter the weight of the shipment.
9. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or remarks about the shipment (if necessary).
10. **Assigned User**: Choose the team member or user assigned to handle the shipment.
11. **Shipment Date**: Enter the expected or actual date of the shipment.
12. **Shipment Time**: Enter the expected or actual time of the shipment.
13. **Customer Email**: Enter the contact email of the customer.
