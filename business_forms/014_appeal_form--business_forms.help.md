# Appeal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Appeal Form is a tool used to submit an appeal against a decision made by the organization. This form helps the appellant to clearly and concisely present their case, providing relevant information and evidence to support their appeal.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Appeal Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information in the **Appellant Information** field, providing your full name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your email address and phone number in the **Email Address** and **Phone Number** fields respectively.
3. If applicable, provide your employee or account ID in the **Employee or Account ID** field.
4. In the **Date of Original Decision** field, select the date on which the original decision was made.
5. Choose the type of decision being appealed in the **Type of Decision Being Appealed** field.
6. Explain why you are appealing the decision in the **Reason for Appeal** field.
7. Provide a detailed description of your appeal in the **Detailed Description of Appeal** field.
8. If you have any supporting documentation, select **True** in the **Do you have supporting documentation?** field and provide a description of the evidence or documentation in the **Describe the evidence or documentation** field.
9. If there are any witnesses who can support your appeal, select **True** in the **Are there any witnesses to support your appeal?** field and provide their names and contact information in the **Witness names and contact information** field.
10. Finally, describe the desired outcome you are seeking in the **What resolution are you seeking?** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Employee or Account ID**: If you are an employee, enter your employee ID. If you are not an employee, enter any other relevant ID.
* **Date of Original Decision**: Select the date on which the original decision was made.
* **Type of Decision Being Appealed**: Choose the type of decision being appealed from the list provided.
* **Reason for Appeal**: Select the reason why you are appealing the decision from the list provided.
* **Detailed Description of Appeal**: Provide a clear and detailed explanation of why you believe the decision should be reconsidered.
* **Do you have supporting documentation?**: Select **True** if you have any supporting documentation and provide a description of the evidence or documentation.
* **Describe the evidence or documentation**: Provide a description of the evidence or documentation supporting your appeal.
* **Are there any witnesses to support your appeal?**: Select **True** if there are any witnesses who can support your appeal.
* **Witness names and contact information**: If you selected **True** for the previous field, enter the names and contact information of the witnesses.
* **What resolution are you seeking?**: Describe the desired outcome you are seeking from the appeal.
