# Staff Induction - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Induction form is used by aviation businesses to induct employees in the field. It is designed to collect information about new employees and their assigned tools.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form title: "Pilot and Front Line Induction Form".
2. Scroll through the form's pages and fill out the required fields. The first page is "Pilot and Front Line Induction Form", which is a text field.
3. On the next page, fill out "Used by" with the name of the business or organization that will be using this form.
4. On the next page, confirm that "No coding involved" is accurate.
5. In the "Form Description" field, provide a brief description of the form's purpose and usage.
6. Select the assigned tool from the dropdown menu, such as "Chatjimmy" or "Other".
7. Finally, select the category of the form, either "Business Forms" or "Other".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pilot and Front Line Induction Form** (`pilot_and_front_line_induction_form`, text, optional):
	+ This field is a text input field where you can enter any additional information about the form.
* **Used by** (`used_by_aviation_businesses`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the business or organization that will be using this form.
* **No coding involved** (`no_coding_involved`, text, optional):
	+ Confirm that no coding is involved with this form.
* **Form description** (`form_description`, text, optional):
	+ Provide a brief description of the form's purpose and usage.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the tool assigned to the employee from the dropdown menu.
* **Form category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the category of the form, either "Business Forms" or "Other".
