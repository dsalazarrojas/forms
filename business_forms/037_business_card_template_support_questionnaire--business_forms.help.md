# Business Card Template Support Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from customers about their experience with business card templates, specifically to understand their support needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Specify the type of support you need, such as Template Download Issue, Editing Problem, or Printing Issue.
3. Provide details about the template you are using, including its name or ID, and where you got it from.
4. Describe the issue you are experiencing with the template, and any error messages you may have encountered.
5. Let us know how many business cards you need and if you have any customization requests.
6. Indicate your level of comfort with design software and your preferred support method.
7. Specify when you need the issue resolved and how you would like to be contacted.
8. If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them in the text box at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (1)**: Please provide your basic information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
* **Full Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it should appear on your business card.
* **Email Address (3)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company Name (5)**: If you are representing a company, please enter its name.
* **Issue Type (6)**: Specify the type of support you need. This can be Template Download Issue, Editing Problem, Printing Issue, or Design Customization.
* **Template Information (7)**: Provide details about the template, including its name or ID.
* **Template Name or ID (8)**: Enter the name or ID of the template you are using.
* **Template Source (9)**: Indicate where you got the template from, such as Website Download or Email Attachment.
* **Software Used (10)**: Specify the design software you are using, such as Adobe Photoshop or Microsoft Word.
* **Issue Description (11)**: Describe the issue you are experiencing with the template in detail.
* **When Did Issue Occur (12)**: Let us know when you first noticed the problem.
* **Steps Taken (13)**: Mention any steps you have taken to resolve the issue so far.
* **Error Messages (14)**: If you have any error messages, please enter them here.
* **Template Usage (15)**: Describe how you will be using the template.
* **Intended Use (16)**: Specify what the template is intended for, such as Personal Business Cards or Client Work.
* **Quantity Needed (17)**: Indicate how many business cards you need.
* **Customization Needs (18)**: If you need any customizations, select the options that apply.
* **Technical Skill Level (19)**: Indicate your level of comfort with design software.
* **Deadline (20)**: Specify when you need the issue resolved.
* **Preferred Support Method (21)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted, such as Email or Phone Call.
* **Best Time to Contact (22)**: Indicate the best time to contact you.
* **Previous Experience with Templates (23)**: If you have used templates before, let us know your level of experience.
* **Additional Comments (24)**: Enter any additional comments or information that may help us assist you better.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible about the issue you are experiencing.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Our team will review your submission and respond as soon as possible.
* We appreciate your patience and understanding as we work to resolve your issue.
