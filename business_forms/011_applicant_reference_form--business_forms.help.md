# Applicant Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from a reference about an applicant's professional performance, work habits, and character. It is an essential part of the hiring process, as it provides valuable insights into the applicant's strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  **Start by reading the form carefully**: Before filling out the form, make sure you understand what each section is asking.
2.  **Fill out the form completely and accurately**: Answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
3.  **Choose the correct options for each question**: Select the best option that describes the applicant's performance, work habits, or character.
4.  **Provide additional comments if necessary**: If there's anything else you'd like to mention about the applicant, use the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name (Reference)** (<name>: Please fill in your full name as the reference.
* **Your Current Job Title** (<name>): Enter your current job title or role.
* **Organization** (<name>): Enter the name of the organization or company you work for.
* **Your Email Address** (<name>): Enter your work email address.
* **Your Phone Number** (<name>): Enter your work phone number.
* **Name of the Applicant** (<name>): Enter the name of the applicant you are referring to.
* **What is your relationship to the applicant?** (<name>): Choose the relationship you have with the applicant (e.g., former supervisor, current supervisor, peer, teacher, etc.).
* **How long have you known the applicant?** (<name>): Estimate the length of time you've known the applicant.
* **Reliability and Dependability** (<name>): Rate the applicant's reliability and dependability (Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Ability to Work in a Team** (<name>): Rate the applicant's ability to work in a team (Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Problem Solving and Initiative** (<name>): Rate the applicant's problem-solving and initiative (Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Technical Competence in the Field** (<name>): Rate the applicant's technical competence in the field (Excellent, Above Average, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Would you recommend this applicant for the position?** (<name>): Select whether you highly recommend, recommend, recommend with reservations, or do not recommend the applicant.
* **Additional Comments** (<name>): If you have any additional comments or insights about the applicant, please provide them here.
* **I certify that the above information is accurate to the best of my knowledge** (<name>): Confirm that the information you provided is true to the best of your knowledge.
