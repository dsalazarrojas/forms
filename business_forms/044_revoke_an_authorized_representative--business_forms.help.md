# Revoke An Authorized Representative - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Revoke An Authorized Representative form is used to remove or replace an authorized representative, documenting the reason for revocation and providing a date and time for the removal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter the authorized representative's name in the "Authorized Representative" field.
3. Enter the reason for revoking the representative's access in the "Reason for Revocation" field.
4. Select the date and time of the removal in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
5. Confirm the removal in the "Confirm Removal" field by selecting one of the provided options.
6. Enter the name of the tool or system the representative is currently assigned to in the "Tool" field.
7. Provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (Text, Required)**: Enter the name of the client associated with the authorized representative.
* **Authorized Representative (Text, Required)**: Enter the name of the authorized representative to be removed or replaced.
* **Reason for Revocation (Text, Required)**: Enter a brief description of the reason for revoking the representative's access.
* **Date (Date, Required)**: Enter the date of the removal in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time (Time, Required)**: Enter the time of the removal in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Confirm Removal (Select Multiple, Required)**: Select one of the provided options to confirm the removal.
* **Tool (Text, Required)**: Enter the name of the tool or system the representative is currently assigned to.
* **Comments (Text, Required)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the removal.

## Tips

* Ensure you are using this form for its intended purpose and not duplicating efforts.
* Double-check the information entered to ensure accuracy.
