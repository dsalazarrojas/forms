# Sidewalk Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the condition of sidewalks, assign maintenance tasks, and track the status and priority of repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "sidewalk condition" field with a brief description of the condition of the sidewalk, including any issues or hazards found.
2. Select the "inspection date" to record the date of the inspection.
3. If necessary, select a "repair priority" to indicate the urgency of the repair.
4. Choose the "repair status" to indicate the current state of the repair, whether it's complete, ongoing, none, or partial.
5. Enter any relevant "repair notes" to provide additional context.
6. Select who is "assigned to" perform the repair, ensuring that the correct person is aware of their task.
7. Enter any "inspection notes" to include any observations or comments from the inspector.
8. Include "inspection comments" to capture any further thoughts or recommendations.
9. If applicable, enter the number of "photo count" taken during the inspection.
10. If applicable, enter the URLs for "photo urls" taken during the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **sidewalk condition**: A brief description of the condition of the sidewalk, including any issues or hazards found.
* **inspection date**: The date of the inspection, which should be accurate and up-to-date.
* **repair priority**: A measure of the urgency of the repair, indicating how quickly it should be addressed.
* **repair status**: The current state of the repair, whether it's complete, ongoing, none, or partial.
* **repair notes**: Additional context or information about the repair, such as the cause of the issue or the proposed solution.
* **assigned to**: The person or team responsible for performing the repair.
* **inspection notes**: Any observations or comments made during the inspection.
* **inspection comments**: Further thoughts or recommendations for improvement.
* **photo count**: The number of photos taken during the inspection.
* **photo urls**: The URLs of any photos taken during the inspection.
