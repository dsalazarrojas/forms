# Luxury Business Gift Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for businesses to place orders for luxury gifts. It gathers information about the business, the gift order process, and other relevant details to facilitate the placement of the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the appropriate options for the **Order Forms** field to indicate the type of gift order being placed.
2.  Choose the relevant **Gift Order Process** options to indicate how the gift order will be processed within the business.
3.  Answer the **Gift Details** field with any relevant information about the gift order.
4.  Choose the **Gift Options** to select any additional options or features for the gift.
5.  Enter any **Special Instructions** for the gift order.
6.  Provide **Delivery Information** for the gift order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **1. Business Forms** (business_forms, text, not required): This field is used for any additional information about the business that the gift is being ordered for.
*   **2. Order Forms** (order_forms, text, not required): This field is used to specify the type of order being placed (e.g., "Client Gift," "Employee Appreciation," etc.).
*   **3. Gift Order Process** (gift_order_process, select_multiple, not required): Choose whether the gift order process will be 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if it will be a straightforward process or if additional steps are required.
*   **4. Clients and Employees** (clients_and_employees, select_one, not required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the gift order is for clients or employees.
*   **5. Gift Details** (gift_details, note, not required): Enter any relevant details about the gift order in this field.
*   **6. Gift Options** (gift_options, select_multiple, not required): Select any additional options or features for the gift, such as "Monetary Gift" or "Personalized Message."
*   **7. Special Instructions** (special_instructions, text, not required): Enter any special instructions for the gift order, such as "Gift wrapping is required" or "Please include a card."
*   **8. Delivery Information** (delivery_info, text, not required): Enter any delivery information for the gift, including the address and shipping instructions.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure the gift order is processed correctly.
*   Use the **Gift Options** field to customize the gift with any special features or services you require.
*   If you have any special requests or instructions for the gift order, enter them in the **Special Instructions** field.
