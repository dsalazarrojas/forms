# Vendor Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and manage information about vendors and their agreements with your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by providing the required information for each field.
2. Review each page carefully to ensure you have completed all required fields.
3. Submit the form when you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Vendor Information
* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_information`, `text`, required/optional: false): This field is for entering information about the vendor.
* **Hint:** None

### Page 2: Business Details
* **Business Details** (`business_details`, `text`, required/optional: false): This field is for entering business details about the vendor.
* **Hint:** None

### Page 2: Vendor Agreement
* **Vendor Agreement** (`vendor_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required/optional: false): This field is for selecting whether the vendor has agreed to the terms of the agreement.
* **Options:** 
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Page 2: Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, required/optional: false): This field is for entering any additional terms and conditions of the agreement.
* **Hint:** None

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure accurate and complete vendor information.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your team or the form's creators for clarification.
