# Consignment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consignment Form is a document used to outline the terms and conditions of the consignment agreement between the consignor (the person or business providing the goods) and the consignee (the business selling the goods). This form helps to establish a clear understanding of the consignment period, payment terms, commission rates, and other essential details to avoid misunderstandings and ensure a smooth consignment process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Consignment Form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date of the consignment agreement.
2.  Provide a unique identifier for this agreement.
3.  Fill in the name, address, phone number, and email of the consignor (the person or business providing the goods).
4.  Enter the consignor's tax identification number (if applicable).
5.  Enter the name and address of the business selling the goods (consignee).
6.  Provide the name and phone number of the consignee.
7.  Enter the email address of the consignee.
8.  Describe the items being consigned in detail.
9.  Choose the category of the items (e.g., artwork, jewelry, clothing, etc.).
10.  If other is selected, specify the category.
11.  Enter the quantity of items being consigned.
12.  Select the condition of the items (e.g., new, like new, excellent, etc.).
13.  Enter the retail price per item.
14.  Enter the minimum price that the consignee must pay for each item.
15.  Choose the commission rate for the consignee.
16.  Enter the percentage of the commission for the consignor.
17.  Select the consignment period (e.g., 30 days, 60 days, etc.).
18.  Choose when the consignment starts and ends.
19.  Select the payment terms for the consignor (e.g., upon sale, within 15 days, etc.).
20.  Choose how the consignor will be paid (e.g., by check, bank transfer, etc.).
21.  Choose what happens to unsold items (e.g., return to consignor, donate to charity, etc.).
22.  Enter the date by which unsold items must be picked up (if applicable).
23.  Choose who is responsible for insuring the items (e.g., consignee, consignor, etc.).
24.  Describe what happens if items are damaged or lost.
25.  Choose if items can be marketed and photographed (with or without approval).
26.  Choose if this is an exclusive agreement or not.
27.  Describe how the agreement can be terminated.
28.  Enter any additional terms and conditions.
29.  Sign and date the form as the consignor and consignee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Agreement Date** (`id: 1`, `title: Agreement Date`, `type: date`): Date of consignment agreement.
*   **Agreement Number** (`id: 2`, `title: Agreement Number`, `type: text`): Unique identifier for this agreement.
*   **Consignor Name** (`id: 3`, `title: Consignor Name`, `type: text`): Name of person or business providing goods.
*   **Consignor Address** (`id: 4`, `title: Consignor Address`, `type: text`): Mailing address.
*   **Consignor Phone** (`id: 5`, `title: Consignor Phone`, `type: text`): Contact phone number.
*   **Consignor Email** (`id: 6`, `title: Consignor Email`, `type: email`): Contact email address.
*   **Consignor Tax ID** (`id: 7`, `title: Consignor Tax ID`, `type: text`): Tax identification number (if applicable).
*   **Consignee Name** (`id: 8`, `title: Consignee Name`, `type: text`): Name of business selling goods.
*   **Consignee Address** (`id: 9`, `title: Consignee Address`, `type: text`): Business address.
*   **Consignee Phone** (`id: 10`, `title: Consignee Phone`, `type: text`): Business phone number.
*   **Consignee Email** (`id: 11`, `title: Consignee Email`, `type: email`): Business email address.
*   **Item Description** (`id: 12`, `title: Item Description`, `type: text`): Detailed description of items.
*   **Item Category** (`id: 13`, `title: Item Category`, `type: select_one`): Type of items (e.g., artwork, jewelry, clothing, etc.).
*   **Other Category** (`id: 14`, `title: Other Category`, `type: text`): Specify if other category selected.
*   **Quantity** (`id: 15`, `title: Quantity`, `type: number`): Number of items.
*   **Item Condition** (`id: 16`, `title: Item Condition`, `type: select_one`): Condition of items (e.g., new, like new, excellent, etc.).
*   **Retail Price** (`id: 17`, `title: Retail Price`, `type: number`): Selling price per item.
*   **Minimum Acceptable Price** (`id: 18`, `title: Minimum Acceptable Price`, `type: number`): Lowest price consignor will accept.
*   **Commission Rate** (`id: 19`, `title: Commission Rate`, `type: number`): Percentage to consignee.
*   **Consignor Percentage** (`id: 20`, `title: Consignor Percentage`, `type: number`): Percentage to consignor.
*   **Consignment Period** (`id: 21`, `title: Consignment Period`, `type: select_one`): How long will items be displayed (e.g., 30 days, 60 days, etc.).
*   **Start Date** (`id: 22`, `title: Start Date`, `type: date`): When does consignment begin.
*   **End Date** (`id: 23`, `title: End Date`, `type: date`): When does consignment end.
*   **Payment Terms** (`id: 24`, `title: Payment Terms`, `type: select_one`): When consignor will be paid (e.g., upon sale, within 15 days, etc.).
*   **Payment Method** (`id: 25`, `title: Payment Method`, `type: select_one`): How consignor will be paid (e.g., by check, bank transfer, etc.).
*   **Unsold Items Policy** (`id: 26`, `title: Unsold Items Policy`, `type: select_one`): What happens to unsold items (e.g., return to consignor, donate to charity, etc.).
*   **Return Deadline** (`id: 27`, `title: Return Deadline`, `type: date`): When must unsold items be picked up (if applicable).
*   **Insurance Coverage** (`id: 28`, `title: Insurance Coverage`, `type: select_one`): Who insures the items (e.g., consignee, consignor, etc.).
*   **Damage or Loss Policy** (`id: 29`, `title: Damage or Loss Policy`, `type: text`): What happens if items are damaged or lost.
*   **Marketing Authorization** (`id: 30`, `title: Marketing Authorization`, `type: select_one`): Can items be photographed and marketed (with or without approval).
*   **Is This an Exclusive Agreement** (`id: 31`, `title: Is This an Exclusive Agreement`, `type: select_one`): Is this an exclusive agreement or not.
*   **Termination Terms** (`id: 32`, `title: Termination Terms`, `type: text`): How can agreement be terminated.
*   **Additional Terms** (`id: 33`, `title: Additional Terms`, `type: text`): Any other agreements.
*   **Consignor Signature** (`id: 34`, `title: Consignor Signature`, `type: text`): Type full name as signature.
*   **Consignee Signature** (`id: 35`, `title: Consignee Signature`, `type: text`): Type full name as signature.
*   **Agreement Status** (`id: 36`, `title: Agreement Status`, `type: select_one`): For administrative use (e.g., draft, active, etc.).

## Tips

*   Read through all fields carefully before signing.
*   Make sure the consignor and consignee have a mutual understanding of all terms.
*   Review the contract before signing to avoid any last-minute disputes.
*   Be sure to have a clear and concise agreement that covers all aspects of the consignment.
*   Sign both the consignor and consignee copies of the agreement for record-keeping and reference.
