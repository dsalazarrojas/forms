# Digital Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Emergency Contact Form is designed to collect essential information from individuals who are experiencing an emergency or have an emergency situation that requires attention from our internal teams. This information will be reviewed and addressed promptly to ensure adequate support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Provide your **Email** address in the field labeled "Email". Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the field labeled "Phone".
5. If applicable, enter your **Company Name** and **Company Website**.
6. Provide the **Location** where the emergency or situation is occurring.
7. Enter the name of the **Emergency Contact** person who can be reached for further assistance.
8. If applicable, enter the **Contact Name** and **Relationship** with the Emergency Contact.
9. Enter the **Contact Phone Number** and **Contact Email** of the Emergency Contact.
10. Add any additional **Contact Notes** regarding the emergency or situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: A valid email address is required. This will be used to communicate with you regarding the emergency or situation.
* **Phone**: Enter a phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Company Name**: If you are representing a company, enter the name of the company.
* **Company Website**: If applicable, enter the website of the company you represent.
* **Location**: Enter the specific location where the emergency or situation is occurring.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Name**: If you have already entered an Emergency Contact, this field will be used to enter their name.
* **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the Emergency Contact, if applicable.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the Emergency Contact.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the Emergency Contact.
* **Contact Notes**: Add any additional information regarding the emergency or situation that the Emergency Contact can assist with.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are completed accurately to ensure prompt and adequate support.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for assistance.
* The information provided will be reviewed and addressed by our internal teams promptly.
* This form is designed to support individuals in emergency or critical situations. If you are experiencing a non-emergency issue, it is recommended to contact us through our general contact form or support channels.
