# AI Solution Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information necessary to generate a quotation for AI services. Please provide your details to help us understand your project and tailor a solution that meets your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the company information section with your company's details.
2. Select your company's industry from the given options.
3. Provide your contact person's name, email, and phone number.
4. Describe your project and its objectives in the project description field.
5. Choose the services you require for your project.
6. Select the expected project duration from the given options.
7. Provide an estimated budget range for your project.
8. Choose your preferred start date for the project.
9. Select your current technology stack and data availability.
10. Specify any specific technical constraints or requirements.
11. Select your preferred communication method for the quote.
12. Finally, let us know how you heard about our AI services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your company's industry from the given options.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the project.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Service(s) Required** (`services_required`, select_multiple, required): Select all the services you require for your project.
* **Project Description & Objectives** (`project_description`, text, required): Briefly describe the problem you want to solve with AI.
* **Expected Project Duration** (`project_duration`, select_one, required): Select the expected project duration from the given options.
* **Estimated Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Select the estimated budget range for your project.
* **Preferred Start Date** (`preferred_start_date`, date, required): Choose your preferred start date for the project.
* **Current Technology Stack** (`tech_stack`, select_multiple, required): Select the primary technologies you use.
* **Data Availability & Readiness** (`data_readiness`, select_one, required): Select the status of your data availability.
* **Specific Technical Constraints or Requirements** (`technical_constraints`, text, required): Specify any specific technical constraints or requirements.
* **Preferred Communication Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred communication method for the quote.
* **How Did You Hear About Our AI Services?** (`referral_source`, text, required): Let us know how you heard about our AI services.
