# Work Input Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Input Form is designed to collect relevant information about employee work inputs. This form is used to record updates and activities related to projects, allowing for easy tracking and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the projects you have worked on by checking all the projects that apply from the "daily_activities" field.
2. Enter any updates or changes for the selected projects in the "project_updates" field.
3. Enter any relevant details about the projects, including tasks completed, hours worked, or any other important information, in the "project_details" field.
4. Enter your employee ID, if applicable, in the "employee_id" field.
5. Enter the date and time of completion in the "date" and "time" fields, respectively.
6. Optionally, add any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Activities** (`daily_activities`, select multiple, required): Select all the projects you have worked on by checking the applicable boxes. This helps us track your time and effort across multiple projects.
* **Project Updates** (`project_updates`, text, optional): Enter any updates or changes you have made to the projects. This could include new information, changes, or any other relevant details.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the projects, such as tasks completed, hours worked, or any other important details.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID, if applicable. This helps us to track your contributions and contributions across different projects.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you completed the work.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time when you completed the work.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the work.
