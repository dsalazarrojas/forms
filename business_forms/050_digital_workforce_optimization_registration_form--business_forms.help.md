# Digital Workforce Optimization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Workforce Optimization Registration Form is used to collect and record information about employees and their work details in a business setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First name.
2. Fill in your Last name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Select your Job title.
6. Select your Department.
7. Enter your Start date (if applicable).
8. Enter your End date (if applicable).
9. Fill in any additional Notes.
10. Enter your Manager's First name.
11. Enter your Manager's Email address.
12. Enter your Manager's Phone number.
13. Select your Reporting line.
14. Select your Team.
15. Select your Job type.
16. Enter your Salary range (if applicable).
17. Enter your Manager's salary range (if applicable).
18. Select your Work location.
19. Select your Work hours.
20. Enter your available Vacation days.
21. Enter your Sick leave days.
22. Enter your Paid holidays.
23. Select your Performance review schedule.
24. Fill in Performance review comments (if applicable).
25. Enter your Submission date and time (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your Email address in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Phone**: Enter your Phone number in the correct format (e.g., `123-456-7890`).
* **Job title**: Select your job title from the list (e.g., Software Engineer, Data Scientist, Operations Manager).
* **Department**: Select your department from the list (e.g., Engineering, Finance, Sales).
* **Start date**: If applicable, enter the date you started your job in the correct format (e.g., `MM/DD/YYYY`).
* **End date**: If applicable, enter the date you ended your job in the correct format (e.g., `MM/DD/YYYY`).
* **Notes**: Fill in any additional comments or details about your job or work experience.
* **Manager name**: Enter your Manager's first name.
* **Manager email**: Enter your Manager's Email address in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Manager phone**: Enter your Manager's Phone number in the correct format (e.g., `123-456-7890`).
* **Reporting line**: Select your reporting line from the list (e.g., Direct, Indirect).
* **Team**: Select your Team from the list (e.g., Marketing, Sales, Support).
* **Job type**: Select your job type from the list (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Contract).
* **Salary range**: Enter your salary range in the correct format (e.g., `50000-60000`).
* **Manager salary range**: Enter your Manager's salary range in the correct format (e.g., `60000-70000`).
* **Work location**: Select your work location from the list (e.g., On site, Remote, Hybrid).
* **Work hours**: Select your work hours from the list (e.g., 8 am - 5 pm, 10 am - 7 pm).
* **Vacation days**: Enter your available Vacation days.
* **Sick leave days**: Enter your available Sick leave days.
* **Paid holidays**: Enter your available Paid holidays.
* **Performance reviews**: Select your Performance review schedule from the list (e.g., Quarterly, Bi-annually, Annually).
* **Performance review comments**: Fill in any comments or details about your performance reviews.
* **Submission date**: If applicable, enter the date and time you submitted the form in the correct format (e.g., `MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM`).
