# union_negotiation_appointment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and document meetings between union representatives and management representatives for union negotiations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select a date for the meeting in the "Meeting Date" field.
2. Choose the time of the meeting in the "Meeting Time" field.
3. Indicate whether union representatives will be attending the meeting by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Union Representatives" field.
4. Similarly, choose whether management representatives will be attending the meeting in the "Management Representatives" field.
5. Enter the expected duration of the meeting in the "Meeting Duration" field.
6. Provide the location of the meeting in the "Meeting Location" field.
7. Outline the agenda for the meeting in the "Meeting Agenda" field.
8. Enter any comments from the union in the "Union Comments" field.
9. Enter any comments from management in the "Management Comments" field.
10. Select the type of meeting (e.g. "Yes" or "No") in the "Meeting Type" field.
11. Enter the topic of discussion in the "Meeting Topic" field.
12. Record any notes from the meeting in the "Meeting Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date (`meeting_date`, date, required: false):** Enter the date on which the meeting will take place.
* **Meeting Time (`meeting_time`, time, required: false):** Choose the time of the meeting.
* **Union Representatives (`union_representatives`, select_multiple, required: false):** Indicate whether union representatives will be attending the meeting. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate their attendance.
* **Management Representatives (`management_representatives`, select_multiple, required: false):** Choose whether management representatives will be attending the meeting. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate their attendance.
* **Meeting Duration (`meeting_duration`, number, required: false):** Enter the expected duration of the meeting in minutes.
* **Meeting Location (`meeting_location`, text, required: false):** Provide the location where the meeting will take place.
* **Meeting Agenda (`meeting_agenda`, text, required: false):** Outline the agenda for the meeting.
* **Union Comments (`union_comments`, text, required: false):** Enter any comments from the union.
* **Management Comments (`management_comments`, text, required: false):** Enter any comments from management.
* **Meeting Type (`meeting_type`, select_one, required: false):** Select the type of meeting (e.g. "Yes" or "No").
* **Meeting Topic (`meeting_topic`, text, required: false):** Enter the topic of discussion for the meeting.
* **Meeting Notes (`meeting_notes`, text, required: false):** Record any notes from the meeting.
