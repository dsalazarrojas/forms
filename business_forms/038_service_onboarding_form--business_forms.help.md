# Service Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Onboarding Form is designed to collect information about a service's onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
* Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
* For fields not required, you can leave them blank or add notes if you wish.
* Submit the form once you have finished filling it out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Service Details (required: false)
* **Service Details**: A text field to enter a brief description of the service.
*   This field is optional, but it will help the service provider understand the nature of the service you are onboarding.

### 2. Contact Info (required: false)
* **Contact Info**: A text field to enter contact information about the person or team responsible for the service.
*   This field is optional, but it will help the service provider communicate with the right person.

### 3. Service Terms (required: false)
* **Service Terms**: A text field to enter any terms or conditions related to the service.
*   This field is optional, but it will help the service provider understand any specific requirements or constraints.

### 4. Service Agreement (required: false)
* **Service Agreement**: A text field to enter any agreements or contracts related to the service.
*   This field is optional, but it will help the service provider understand any commitments or obligations.

### 5. Service Status (required: false)
* **Service Status**: A text field to enter the current status of the service.
*   This field is optional, but it will help the service provider track the service's progress.

### 6. Service Plan (required: false)
* **Service Plan**: A text field to enter any plans or schedules related to the service.
*   This field is optional, but it will help the service provider understand the timeline for the service.

### 7. Service Pricing (required: false)
* **Service Pricing**: A text field to enter any pricing information related to the service.
*   This field is optional, but it will help the service provider understand the financial aspects of the service.

### 8. Service Notes (required: false)
* **Service Notes**: A text field to enter any additional notes or comments about the service.
*   This field is optional, and you can use it to add any relevant details or reminders.

# Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Use accurate and complete information in the required fields.
* Take your time to fill out the form if needed, as it is for your convenience.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
