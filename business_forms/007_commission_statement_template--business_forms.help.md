# Commission Statement Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Commission Statement Template is designed to generate a comprehensive statement outlining the commission earnings of a sales representative for a specified period. It calculates commissions, overrides, and other adjustments, providing an accurate and transparent representation of the representative's earnings.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Enter Representative Information**
    - Fill in the **Sales Representative Full Name** field with the sales representative's full name.
    - Enter the **Representative ID Number** in the corresponding field.
    - Input the representative's **Email Address** for digital statement delivery.
2.  **Define the Statement Period**
    - Enter the **Statement Period Start Date** and **Statement Period End Date** to specify the period for which you want to generate the statement.
3.  **Enter Sales Summary**
    - Input the **Total Number of Sales in Period** to calculate total sales volume.
    - Enter the **Total Gross Sales Volume** (exclusive of taxes and returns).
    - Enter the **Returns or Cancellations (Deductions)** if applicable.
4.  **Calculate Commission**
    - Enter the **Base Commission Rate (%)** applied to net sales.
5.  **Compute Earnings**
    - Calculate the **Base Commission Amount Earned** using the previously entered fields.
    - Enter any **Overrides or Tiered Bonuses** if applicable.
    - Input any **Other Adjustments or Reimbursements** (e.g., travel allowance or marketing credits).
6.  **Review and Authorize**
    - Confirm that the statement is accurate by indicating you **Acknowledge receipt of this statement and agree with the calculations**.
    - Enter the **Statement Prepared By** for payroll or manager verification.
    - Set the **Date Statement was Prepared**.
7.  **Finalize**
    - Click "Submit" or "Generate" to produce the commission statement.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Sales Representative Full Name**: Enter the full name of the sales representative for whom the commission statement is being generated.
*   **Representative ID Number**: Enter the representative's ID number for identification.
*   **Email Address**: Input the representative's email address for digital statement delivery.
*   **Statement Period Start Date**: Enter the starting date of the period for which you want to generate the commission statement.
*   **Statement Period End Date**: Enter the ending date of the period for which you want to generate the commission statement.
*   **Total Number of Sales in Period**: Enter the total number of sales made during the specified period.
*   **Total Gross Sales Volume**: Enter the total sales volume, excluding taxes and returns.
*   **Returns or Cancellations (Deductions)**: Enter the returns or cancellations (deductions) if any.
*   **Base Commission Rate (%)**: Enter the base commission rate applied to net sales.
*   **Base Commission Amount Earned**: Calculate the base commission amount earned based on the previously entered fields.
*   **Overrides or Tiered Bonuses**: Enter any overrides or tiered bonuses if applicable.
*   **Other Adjustments or Reimbursements**: Enter any other adjustments or reimbursements (e.g., travel allowance or marketing credits).
*   **Total Payable Commission for Period**: Calculate the total payable commission for the period after applying all adjustments.
*   **Statement Prepared By**: Enter the full name of the payroll or manager preparing the statement.
*   **Date Statement was Prepared**: Set the date the statement was prepared.
*   **Acknowledge receipt of this statement and agree with the calculations**: Confirm that you acknowledge receipt of the statement and agree with the calculations.
*   **Acknowledge Date**: Enter the date you acknowledged receipt of the statement and agreed with the calculations.
