# Tax Receipt Configuration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Receipt Configuration Form is used to generate or manage tax receipts. It collects necessary information to create accurate and official tax receipts.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **client's name** in the "Client Name" field. This is the name of the person or business receiving the tax receipt.
2.  Enter the **client's address** in the "Client Address" field. This is the physical address where the tax receipt will be sent to.
3.  Enter the **client's postal code** in the "Client Postal Code" field. This is the postal code or zip code associated with the client's address.
4.  Select the **tax receipt date** in the "Tax Receipt Date" field. This is the date when the tax was paid or is due. **This field is required.**
5.  Enter the **tax receipt amount** in the "Tax Receipt Amount" field. This is the total amount of the tax paid or due. **This field is required.**
6.  Add any additional **tax receipt notes** in the "Tax Receipt Notes" field if necessary. This can be used for any extra comments or details about the tax receipt.
7.  Enter the **email** of the person or business receiving the tax receipt in the "Email" field. **This field is required.**
8.  If applicable, enter the **phone number** of the person or business receiving the tax receipt in the "Phone" field.
9.  Enter the **business name** and **business address** and **business postal code** if this is a business-related tax receipt.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Tax Receipt Date (tax_receipt_date)**: The date when the tax was paid or is due. This field is required.
*   **Tax Receipt Amount (tax_receipt_amount)**: The total amount of the tax paid or due. This field is required.
*   **Client Name (client_name)**: The name of the person or business receiving the tax receipt. 
*   **Client Address (client_address)**: The physical address of the person or business receiving the tax receipt.
*   **Client Postal Code (client_postal_code)**: The postal code or zip code associated with the client's address.
*   **Email (email)**: The email of the person or business receiving the tax receipt. This field is required.
*   **Phone (phone)**: The phone number of the person or business receiving the tax receipt.
*   **Business Name (business_name)**: The name of the business receiving the tax receipt.
*   **Business Address (business_address)**: The address of the business receiving the tax receipt.
*   **Business Postal Code (business_postal_code)**: The postal code or zip code associated with the business address.
*   **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)**: Not applicable for end-users but possibly used for internal purposes.
*   **Tax Receipt Notes (tax_receipt_notes)**: Any extra comments or details about the tax receipt.
