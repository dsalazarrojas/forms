# Supplier Ethics Declaration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Ethics Declaration Registration Form is a form used to collect and manage information about suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter supplier data.
2. Enter registration number (if applicable).
3. Select the business entity status.
4. Enter business name and contact person details.
5. Provide contact email and phone number.
6. Make an ethics declaration (optional).
7. Choose declaration deadline and status.
8. Add any additional information.
9. Review and agree to terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Data**: Enter the supplier's name and any other relevant details.
* **Registration Number**: Enter the supplier's registration number (if applicable).
* **Business Entity**: Select whether the supplier is a business entity or not.
* **Business Name**: Enter the supplier's business name.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the supplier's contact person's name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the supplier's contact email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the supplier's contact phone number.
* **Ethics Declaration**: Make an ethics declaration (optional).
* **Declaration Deadline**: Choose the deadline for the ethics declaration.
* **Declaration Status**: Select the status of the ethics declaration (Active or Inactive).
* **Declaration Notes**: Add any additional notes about the declaration.
* **Supplier Address**: Enter the supplier's address (not used in this form).
* **Country**: Select whether the supplier is from a specific country or region.
* **Region**: Select whether the supplier is from a specific region.
* **City**: Enter the supplier's city (not used in this form).
* **Supplier Type**: Select the type of supplier (e.g., service, product, etc.).
* **Supplier Category**: Select the category of supplier (e.g., electronics, furniture, etc.).
* **Supplier Subcategory**: Select the subcategory of supplier (e.g., smartphone, chair, etc.).
* **Supplier Industry**: Select the industry of supplier (e.g., tech, furniture, etc.).
* **Supplier Website**: Enter the supplier's website (not used in this form).
* **Supplier Phone**: Enter the supplier's phone number (not used in this form).
* **Supplier Email**: Enter the supplier's email address (not used in this form).
* **Additional Info**: Add any additional information about the supplier.
* **Agreement**: Select whether the supplier has agreed to terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
