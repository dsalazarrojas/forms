# Leadership Coaching Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture details about a leadership coaching engagement, including client information, services provided, rates, and terms of payment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely.
2. Select all applicable options for each field.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (<client_name>, text, required: false): This field is for the client's name.
*   **Service Type** (<service_type, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of service you will be providing to the client.
*   **Start Date** (<start_date, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the coaching engagement.
*   **End Date** (<end_date, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the coaching engagement.
*   **Hours** (<hours, number, required: false): Enter the total hours to be worked on the engagement.
*   **Minutes** (<minutes, number, required: false): Enter the total minutes to be worked on the engagement.
*   **Rate Per Hour** (<rate_per_hour, number, required: false): Enter the rate per hour for the engagement.
*   **Total Amount** (<total_amount, number, required: false): This field will automatically calculate the total amount based on the hours and rate per hour.
*   **Payment Method** (<payment_method, select_one, required: false): Select the payment method you will use for the engagement.
*   **Email** (<email, email, required: false): Enter the client's email address.
*   **Phone** (<phone, text, required: false): Enter the client's phone number.
*   **Signature** (<signature, note, required: false): Enter your signature.
*   **Signature Date** (<signature_date, date, required: false): Enter the date of your signature.
*   **Client Notes** (<client_notes, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes for the client.
*   **Coach Notes** (<coach_notes, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes for the coach.
*   **Services** (<services, select_multiple, required: false): Select the services to be provided to the client.
*   **Service Description** (<service_description, text, required: false): Enter a description of the services to be provided.
*   **Client Signature** (<client_signature, note, required: false): Enter the client's signature.
*   **Coach Signature** (<coach_signature, note, required: false): Enter your signature as the coach.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (<terms_conditions, text, required: false): Enter any terms and conditions for the engagement.
*   **Date Created** (<date_created, date, required: false): This field will automatically be populated with the date the form was created.
*   **Created By** (<created_by, text, required: false): This field will automatically be populated with the name of the person who created the form.
*   **Date Updated** (<date_updated, date, required: false): This field will automatically be populated with the date the form was last updated.
*   **Updated By** (<updated_by, text, required: false): This field will automatically be populated with the name of the person who last updated the form.

Note: Fields with a "required: false" label are not mandatory, but it is recommended to fill them out for completeness.
