# international_sales_action_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "international_sales_action_checklist" form is a tool designed to help sales teams and professionals in the international sales industry streamline their processes and ensure that all necessary steps are taken when engaging in international sales activities. This form serves as a checklist to facilitate review and verification of critical documents, compliance, and logistics procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by carefully reading the form and understanding the purpose and sections that follow.
2. Start by filling out the "Review documentation" section, which is used to record your initial review of relevant documents.
3. Once you have reviewed the documentation, move on to the "Check Compliance" section, where you will verify that all necessary compliance procedures have been met.
4. Finally, document the "Logistics Steps" section, which is used to record the necessary steps for successful logistics and delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Review documentation** (`Review documentation`, text, **optional**):
    This field is for noting and recording your initial review of relevant documents, such as contracts, agreements, and other sales-related documents.
* **Check Compliance** (`Check Compliance`, text, **optional**):
    In this field, you will verify that all necessary compliance procedures have been met, such as checking for any local laws or regulations that may apply to your sales activities.
* **Logistics Steps** (`Logistics Steps`, text, **optional**):
    This field is used to document the necessary steps for successful logistics and delivery of products or services.
