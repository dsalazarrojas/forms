# Enhancement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enhancement Request Form is designed to collect user feedback and suggestions for improving our business processes, products, or services. This form helps us understand your needs and ideas to enhance our offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and email address to help us identify you and respond to your feedback.
2. Select your department (if applicable) to help us understand your role and context.
3. Provide a clear and concise description of the enhancement request, including any specific details or suggestions.
4. Specify the priority level of your request (high, medium, or low).
5. Choose a due date for your request, if applicable.
6. Select the user who will be responsible for working on your request.
7. Choose the status of your request (Active or Inactive).
8. Add any additional comments or context to support your enhancement request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (`user_name`, text, optional)
The First Name field is where you can enter your first name for identification purposes.

*   **Email** (`user_email`, email, optional)
    Enter your email address to enable us to contact you with updates and responses.

*   **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional)
    Select the department related to your role or context to help us understand your specific area of interest.

*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional)
    Enter your job title to provide context and relevance to your enhancement request.

*   **Description** (`description`, text, required)
    Provide a clear and concise description of the enhancement request, including any specific details or suggestions.

*   **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional)
    Choose the priority level of your request (high, medium, or low) to help us understand its relative importance.

*   **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, optional)
    Select a due date for your request, if applicable, to help us track progress and follow-up.

*   **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, optional)
    Select the user who will be responsible for working on your request.

*   **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional)
    Choose the status of your request (Active or Inactive) to help us track its progress.

*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional)
    Add any additional comments or context to support your enhancement request.

This guide will help you complete the Enhancement Request Form accurately and effectively, ensuring that your feedback and suggestions are properly evaluated and implemented to enhance our business processes, products, or services.
