# transaction_record_access_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The transaction record access application form is designed to request access to specific company transaction records. This form helps employees access the necessary information to complete their work tasks efficiently and maintain data accuracy and integrity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information and authorization to access the records.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name to identify yourself in the system.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name to identify yourself in the system.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth to ensure you are authorized to access the records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your company email address to receive notifications and updates about your request.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your company phone number for internal communication.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required/optional): Select your department from the list provided to determine which team is responsible for the transaction.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title to confirm your role within the company.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your job duties to ensure you are authorized to access the records.
* **Transaction ID** (`transaction_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the ID of the transaction record you are requesting access to.
* **Transaction Date** (`transaction_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the transaction to ensure you are accessing the correct record.
* **Access Duration** (`access_duration`, number, required/optional): Select the duration of time you need access to the record.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief explanation for your request to ensure the request is processed correctly.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the approval status from the list provided to indicate the manager's decision.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, required/optional): Click this button to submit your request.
