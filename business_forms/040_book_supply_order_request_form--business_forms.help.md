# Book Supply Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for book supply orders from various types of organizations, including schools, libraries, bookstores, universities, and corporations. It helps us process your order efficiently and ensure timely delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your full name, email address, and phone number to enable us to contact you regarding your order.
2. Enter your company or organization name and select the type of your organization.
3. Provide your shipping address, city, state, and postal code to facilitate smooth delivery.
4. Specify the date you would like to receive your order and the date you would like us to fulfill the order.
5. Fill out the title, author, and quantity of each book you'd like to order. For each book, select the desired format (e.g., Hardcover, Paperback).
6. If you have more than three items to order, repeat the process for each additional item.
7. Specify your estimated budget for the order.
8. Choose your preferred shipping method and payment method (e.g., Purchase Order, Credit Card).
9. If you have a purchase order number, enter it here.
10. Enter any special instructions or requests you may have.
11. Finally, confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes I agree."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents. This will enable us to contact you regarding your order.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address we will use to send you confirmation of your order and any updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for any additional questions or inquiries.
* **Company or Organization Name**: Enter the name of your school, library, bookstore, or corporate entity.
* **Company Type**: Select the type of your organization from the provided options.
* **Department**: If applicable, enter the department within your organization responsible for the order.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address where you would like the books to be delivered.
* **City**: Enter the city where you would like the books to be delivered.
* **State**: Enter the state where you would like the books to be delivered.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code where you would like the books to be delivered.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Requested Delivery Date**: Enter the date you would like us to fulfill the order.
* **Item 1 Title**: Enter the title of the first book you'd like to order.
* **Item 1 Author**: Enter the author of the first book you'd like to order.
* **Item 1 ISBN**: If available, enter the ISBN of the first book you'd like to order.
* **Item 1 Quantity**: Enter the number of copies of the first book you'd like to order.
* **Item 1 Format**: Select the desired format for the first book (e.g., Hardcover, Paperback).
* **Item 2 Title** (if applicable): Enter the title of the second book you'd like to order.
* **Item 2 Author** (if applicable): Enter the author of the second book you'd like to order.
* **Item 2 ISBN** (if applicable): If available, enter the ISBN of the second book you'd like to order.
* **Item 2 Quantity** (if applicable): Enter the number of copies of the second book you'd like to order.
* **Item 2 Format** (if applicable): Select the desired format for the second book (e.g., Hardcover, Paperback).
* **Item 3 Title** (if applicable): Enter the title of the third book you'd like to order.
* **Item 3 Author** (if applicable): Enter the author of the third book you'd like to order.
* **Item 3 ISBN** (if applicable): If available, enter the ISBN of the third book you'd like to order.
* **Item 3 Quantity** (if applicable): Enter the number of copies of the third book you'd like to order.
* **Item 3 Format** (if applicable): Select the desired format for the third book (e.g., Hardcover, Paperback).
* **Total Number of Items**: Enter the total number of books you'd like to order.
* **Estimated Budget**: Enter your estimated budget for the order.
* **Shipping Method**: Select your preferred shipping method (e.g., Standard, Expedited, Overnight).
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (e.g., Purchase Order, Credit Card).
* **Purchase Order Number** (if applicable): Enter any purchase order number associated with your order.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special requests or instructions you may have.
* **I Agree to Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the order.
