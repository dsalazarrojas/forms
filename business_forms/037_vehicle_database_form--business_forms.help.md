# Vehicle Database Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store information about vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle registration information.
2. Select the type of vehicle you are registering (Vehicle, Boat, or Plane).
3. Enter the make and model of the vehicle.
4. Enter the year of the vehicle.
5. Enter the color of the vehicle.
6. Enter the owner's name and contact information (phone number and email).
7. Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
8. Enter the vehicle's current location.
9. Add any additional notes about the vehicle.
10. Enter the vehicle's phone number and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, text, required: false): Enter the vehicle's registration information.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of vehicle (Vehicle, Boat, or Plane).
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required: false): Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required: false): Enter the vehicle's model.
* **Year** (`year`, number, required: false): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, required: false): Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Owner** (`vehicle_owner`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the vehicle's owner.
* **Vehicle License Plate** (`vehicle_license_plate`, text, required: false): Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
* **Vehicle Location** (`vehicle_location`, text, required: false): Enter the vehicle's current location.
* **Vehicle Notes** (`vehicle_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Phone** (`vehicle_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the vehicle's phone number.
* **Vehicle Email** (`vehicle_email`, email, required: false): Enter the vehicle's email address.
* **Vehicle Address** (`vehicle_address`, text, required: false): Enter the vehicle's address.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately.
* Double-check the vehicle type to ensure it is correct.
* Enter the vehicle's contact information accurately.
* Use the notes field to add any additional information about the vehicle.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is correct.
