# Responsive Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Responsive Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants for a responsive workshop. The form helps us gather data about the workshop, participants, and their preferences, which will be used to tailor the workshop experience to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the workshop you are interested in.
2. Provide a brief description of the workshop content.
3. Enter your name and contact information (email and phone number).
4. Make any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the workshop.
5. Review your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Workshop Content Description** (`workshop_content_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the workshop content.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Make Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Share any additional suggestions or comments you may have about the workshop.
* **Add Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Make any additional comments about the workshop.

## Tips

* Please fill out as much information as possible for us to get a better understanding of your needs and preferences.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
