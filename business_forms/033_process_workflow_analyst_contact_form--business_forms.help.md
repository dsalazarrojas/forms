**Process Workflow Analyst Contact Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a contact point for individuals seeking assistance or guidance from the Process Workflow Analyst team. The form is used to collect information about the user, such as their name, contact details, and category of inquiry, to facilitate efficient communication and issue resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your First Name.
2.  Enter your Last Name.
3.  Provide a valid Email address for contact purposes.
4.  Enter your Phone number.
5.  Add a Description of your issue or question.
6.  Select the Category related to your issue or question (e.g., Finance, Business, Marketing).
7.  If applicable, specify the Priority of your issue or question.
8.  Indicate the Expected Resolution Time.
9.  Select the Category Options related to your issue or question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
    *   This field is for your first name, which is a required field.
*   **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
    *   This field is for your last name, which is also a required field.
*   **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
    *   This field is for your email address, which is required for communication and issue resolution.
*   **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact information.
    *   This field is optional and used for additional contact information.
*   **Description** (note, optional): Add a description of your issue or question.
    *   This field is for detailed information about your issue or question.
*   **Category** (text, optional): Select the category related to your issue or question.
    *   This field helps categorize and prioritize your issue or question for efficient resolution.
*   **Category Options** (select one, optional): Select one option from the list provided.
    *   This field helps narrow down the category for your issue or question.
*   **Priority** (number, optional): Indicate the priority level of your issue or question.
    *   This field helps in prioritizing your issue or question for resolution.
*   **Expected Resolution Time** (time, optional): Indicate the expected resolution time for your issue or question.
    *   This field is for planning and tracking resolution progress.
*   **Category Options** (select one, optional): Select one option from the list provided.
    *   This field helps narrow down the category for your issue or question.
