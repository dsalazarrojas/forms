# reuse_and_refill_program_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for the re-use and refill program application. It is intended for business owners or individuals who wish to participate in this program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For each field, please provide accurate and complete information as requested.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (*) 
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).

### Last Name (*) 
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).

### Email (*) 
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address. Please ensure it is correct for any communications related to your application.

### Phone 
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Business Name (*) 
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business or organization.

### Business Address (*) 
* **Business Address**: Enter the address of your business or organization.

### City (*) 
* **City**: Enter the city where your business or organization is located.

### Business State (*) 
* **State**: Select the correct state where your business or organization is located from the provided options.

### Zip (*) 
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of your business or organization.

### Date of Birth (*) 
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/2000).

### Reason for Refill (*) 
* **Reason for Refill**: Select the reasons why you are applying for the re-use and refill program from the provided options.

### Comments 
* **Comments**: This field is optional. If you have any additional comments or information, you can enter them here.

### Signature (*) 
* **Signature**: Please provide your electronic signature to confirm your application.

### Date Submitted (*) 
* **Date Submitted**: This field is automatically populated with the current date upon form submission.

Tips:

- Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact the relevant authorities or support staff for guidance.
