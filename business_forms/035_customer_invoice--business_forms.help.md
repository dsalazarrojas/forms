# Customer Invoice - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Customer Invoice" form is designed to create and view customer invoices for our business. This form allows you to input customer information and generate an invoice that can be used for billing and payment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "customer_name" field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "customer_email" field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "customer_phone" field.
4. Select the date of service in the "Date" field.
5. Click "submit" to generate the invoice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Invoices Form (Text)**: This is the main form where you input customer information. Please enter the relevant details for the customer.
* **Customer Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter the customer's name as it appears on their invoice.
* **Customer Email (Email, Optional)**: Enter the customer's email address for communication purposes.
* **Customer Phone (Text, Optional)**: Enter the customer's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date (Date, Optional)**: Select the date of service to be included on the invoice.

## Tips
* Make sure to input accurate and up-to-date information to ensure correct invoicing.
* Double-check for spelling errors and formatting issues before submitting the form.
* If you need to make any changes to the invoice, you can modify the form fields before submitting.
