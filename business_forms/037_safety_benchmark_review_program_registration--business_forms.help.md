**Safety Benchmark Review Program Registration - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Safety Benchmark Review Program Registration form is used to collect information from your company to help us evaluate and improve our safety services. Please note that only one program choice can be selected for this registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's name in the **Company Name** field.
2. Select the type of program your company is interested in participating in (e.g., Safety, Quality, Health) in the **Program Choice** field.
3. Choose the start and end dates of the program in the **Start Date** and **End Date** fields, respectively.
4. Enter your company's goals for participating in the program in the **Goal** field (optional).
5. Select your preferred days for the program in the **Preferred Dates** field (multiple selections allowed).
6. Share your goals with us in the **Share Goals** field.
7. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number in the **Contact Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** fields, respectively.
8. Enter your company's address in the **Address** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (Company Name)**: Enter your company's name to associate this registration with your company.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: No
* **Program Choice (Program Choice)**: Select the type of program your company is interested in participating in.
	+ Type: `select_one`
	+ Required: No
* **Start Date (Start Date)**: Enter the start date of the program.
	+ Type: `date`
	+ Required: Yes
* **End Date (End Date)**: Enter the end date of the program.
	+ Type: `date`
	+ Required: Yes
* **Goal (Goal)**: Enter your company's goals for participating in the program (optional).
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: No
* **Preferred Dates (Preferred Dates)**: Select your preferred days for the program.
	+ Type: `select_multiple`
	+ Required: No
* **Share Goals (Share Goals)**: Share your goals with us to help us better understand your company's objectives.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: Yes
* **Contact Name (Contact Name)**: Enter your name so we can contact you.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: Yes
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
	+ Type: `email`
	+ Required: Yes
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: Yes
* **Address (Address)**: Enter your company's address (optional).
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: No

## Tips
Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure proper registration and follow-up communication. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
