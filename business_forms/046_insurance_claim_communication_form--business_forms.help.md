# Insurance Claim Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Claim Communication Form is used to notify and inform parties involved in the insurance process about the progress and status of an insurance claim. This form helps to ensure that all parties are aware of and can take necessary actions on the claim.

## How To Complete This Form
This form has six fields in total. Please follow these steps to complete it:

1. Enter the Company Name of the insured party.
2. Provide the Policy Number of the insured party.
3. If applicable, enter the date of the claim (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
4. If applicable, enter the name of the adjuster assigned to the claim.
5. Enter the total Claim Amount.
6. Select all relevant communication channels for the claim communication (Phone, Email, Fax).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company or entity involved in the claim.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, `number`, required): Enter the policy number associated with the claim.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date of the claim in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Adjuster Name** (`adjuster_name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the adjuster assigned to the claim.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of the claim.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all relevant communication channels for the claim communication (Phone, Email, Fax).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
- Use the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD) when filling out the date field.
- Select all relevant communication channels for efficient communication.
- This form is designed to collect information from multiple fields, so make sure to fill out all relevant fields to provide a complete picture of the claim.
