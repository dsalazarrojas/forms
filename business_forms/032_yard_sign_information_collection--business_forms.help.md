# Yard Sign Information Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about yard signs, including the sign shop that created it, the size of the yard it was installed in, the artwork or design of the sign, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the sign shop that created the yard sign in the **Sign Shop Name** field.
2. Check the **Yard Size** field to select the correct size of the yard the sign was installed in.
3. Enter a brief description of the artwork or design of the sign in the **Artwork** field.
4. Add any additional details or specifications about the sign in the **Specs** field (if necessary).
5. Provide information about the installation details, including any relevant notes or comments, in the **Installation Details** field.
6. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the yard sign in the **Contact Person** field.
7. Enter the email address of the contact person in the **Email** field.
8. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the **Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sign Shop Name**: This is the name of the sign shop that created the yard sign. This field is required.
* **Yard Size**:
	+ Select 'Yes' to indicate a large yard size.
	+ Select 'No' to indicate a small yard size.
* **Artwork**: This is a brief description of the artwork or design of the sign. This field is required.
* **Specs**: This field is for additional details or specifications about the sign. If you need to add more information, you can use this field. (Optional)
* **Installation Details**: This is a field for notes or comments about the installation of the sign. This field is required.
* **Contact Person**: This is the name of the person responsible for the yard sign. This field is required.
* **Email**: This is the email address of the contact person. This field is required.
* **Phone**: This is the phone number of the contact person. This field is not required but recommended for easy contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information in each field to ensure proper tracking and maintenance of the yard sign.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant contact person for clarification.
* This form is intended to be used for yard signs, so please ensure that the information entered is related to a yard sign.
