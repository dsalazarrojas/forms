# Retail Sustainability Collaboration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Sustainability Collaboration Registration Form is designed to help retail businesses collaborate on sustainability initiatives with their partners. By filling out this form, you will be able to:
* Express your interests in sustainability initiatives
* Share your sustainability goals and focus areas
* Provide contact information
* Describe your current partnerships
* Identify areas for potential collaboration

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in sections, answering each question in turn.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by *).
3. Use the provided options for select fields or enter free-form text for text fields.
4. Upload any relevant attachments to support your submission (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Details** (`partner_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of your business and its relationship with the retail partner.
* **Retailer Interests** (`partner_interests`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all interests that apply:
		- Sustainability
		- Environmental
		- Social
* **Sustainability Goals** (`goals`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one goal that best represents your sustainability priorities:
		- Reduce Carbon Footprint
		- Increase Efficiency
		- Enhance Sourcing
* **Contact Information** (`partner_contact`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for your business.
* **Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the person completing the form.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email address of the person completing the form.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the person completing the form.
* **Title** (`contact_title`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the title of the person completing the form.
* **Sustainability Commitments** (`partner_sustainability_commitments`, text, optional):
	+ Describe your business's sustainability commitments and achievements.
* **Current Partnerships** (`partner_partnerships`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all current partnerships that apply:
		- Existing partnership
		- New Partnership
* **Industry** (`partner_industry`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the industry your business operates in:
		- Retail
		- Manufacturing
		- Services
* **Sector** (`partner_sector`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the sector your business operates in.
* **Sustainability Focus** (`partner_sustainability_focus`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all focus areas for sustainability:
		- Environmental
		- Social
		- Governance
* **Sustainability Goal** (`partner_sustainability_goal`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one goal that best represents your priorities:
		- Reduce
		- Increase
		- Enhance
* **Sustainability Status** (`partner_sustainability_status`, text, optional):
	+ Describe your business's current sustainability status.
* **Additional Information** (`partner_additional_info`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information about your business and its sustainability initiatives.
* **Sustainability Comments** (`partner_sustainability_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Add any comments or notes about your submission.

Note: Fields marked as optional are not required but are encouraged for a more detailed submission.
