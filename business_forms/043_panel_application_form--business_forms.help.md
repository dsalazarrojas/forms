# Panel Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Panel Application Form is a form used to collect information for panel applications. This form is used to gather essential details about the applicant, including their name, email, phone number, application date, and other relevant information. The form helps ensure that the application process is completed accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the applicant before starting the form.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. If a field is optional, feel free to include or leave it blank as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, \*): Enter the date the application was submitted.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, \*): Enter the applicant's full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, \*): Enter the applicant's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, \*): Enter the applicant's phone number.
* **Panel Type** (`panel_type`, select_one, \*): Select the type of panel the applicant is applying for (Panel A, Panel B, or Panel C).
* **Panel Location** (`panel_location`, select_multiple, \*): Select all applicable locations for the applicant (Location A, Location B, or Location C).
* **Application Reason** (`application_reason`, text, \*): Enter a brief reason for the application.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): If applicable, attach a resume for the applicant.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, optional): If applicable, attach a portfolio for the applicant.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): If applicable, enter names and contact information of professional references.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, \*): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, \*): Sign the form to confirm submission.
