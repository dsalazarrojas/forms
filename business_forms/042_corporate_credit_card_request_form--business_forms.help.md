# corporate_credit_card_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a corporate credit card from the IT department. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the company information at the top of the form.
2. Enter the employee's name.
3. Select the employee's position within the company.
4. Enter the employee's department.
5. Choose the credit card type (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover).
6. Enter the credit card number.
7. Enter the CVV (Card Verification Value) for the credit card.
8. Enter the employee's email address.
9. If applicable, provide the date of the last credit card request, the date it was approved, the date it was denied, and the reason for denial.
10. Enter any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Employee**: Enter the name of the employee requesting the credit card.
* **Position**: Enter the employee's job title or position within the company.
* **Department**: Enter the employee's department within the company.
* **Credit Card Type**: Choose the type of credit card the employee needs (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover).
* **Credit Card Number**: Enter the number of the credit card.
* **CVV**: Enter the Card Verification Value for the credit card.
* **Employee Email**: Enter the employee's email address.
* **Date of last Request**: If the employee has previously requested a credit card, enter the date of the last request.
* **Last Request Approved Date**: If the employee's previous request was approved, enter the date it was approved.
* **Last Request Denied Date**: If the employee's previous request was denied, enter the date it was denied.
* **Last Request Denied Reason**: If the employee's previous request was denied, enter the reason for denial.
* **Last Request Status**: Select the status of the previous request (Pending, Approved, Denied).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the request.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the credit card information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor or the IT department for assistance.
* This form is for corporate credit card requests only, and personal credit card information should not be submitted here.
