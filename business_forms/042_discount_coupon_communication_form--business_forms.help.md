# discount_coupon_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to send targeted communications to customers regarding special offers or deals from businesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the business sending the communication.
2. Enter a valid email address for the recipient(s).
3. Enter a phone number, if applicable.
4. Enter a valid email address for your confirmation and reply-to address.
5. Make sure all fields are filled out and correct before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your business.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address to which the communication will be sent.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter a phone number to contact the customer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your own email address for confirmation and reply purposes.

Note: This form can be completed multiple times for different customers or communications, but be mindful of the business name and email fields as they are intended to be unique and specific for each use case.
