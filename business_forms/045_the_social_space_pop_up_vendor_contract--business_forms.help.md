# The Social Space Pop Up Vendor Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Space Pop Up Vendor Contract form is used to store and manage the details of vendor contracts for the social space. This form is a crucial part of maintaining accurate and up-to-date information about vendor agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in all the required fields as accurately and completely as possible. Some fields may have specific formats or requirements, please review those fields carefully.
* Review and ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorization to access and update vendor contracts.
* If you are unsure about any field or requirement, please contact the relevant department for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Contact** (`popup_vendor_contract`):
	+ Enter the contact information for the vendor, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Social Space Name** (`social_space_name`):
	+ Enter the name of the social space that the vendor is providing services or goods for.
* **Email** (`popup_vendor_email`):
	+ Enter the vendor's email address, which will be used for communication and notifications.
* **Phone** (`popup_vendor_phone`):
	+ Enter the vendor's phone number, which will be used for communication and emergency contact.
* **Notes** (`popup_vendor_notes`):
	+ Enter any additional information about the vendor or their services/goods that are not captured in other fields.
* **Date** (`popup_vendor_date`):
	+ Enter the date the contract was created or started.
* **Time** (`popup_vendor_time`):
	+ Enter the time the contract was created or started.
* **Contract Terms** (`popup_vendor_contract_terms`):
	+ Indicate if the contract has terms that need to be agreed upon.
* **Contract Notes** (`popup_vendor_contract_notes`):
	+ Enter any notes or details about the contract that are not captured in other fields.
* **Contract Expires** (`popup_vendor_contract_expires`):
	+ Enter the date the contract is set to expire.
* **Contract Start** (`popup_vendor_contract_start`):
	+ Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End** (`popup_vendor_contract_end`):
	+ Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Status** (`popup_vendor_status`):
	+ Indicate the status of the contract (Active or Inactive).
* **Terms Accepted** (`popup_vendor_contract_terms_accepted`):
	+ Indicate if the contract terms have been accepted by the vendor.
* **Notes** (`popup_vendor_contract_notes`):
	+ Enter any additional notes or details about the contract that are not captured in other fields.
* **Signed By** (`popup_vendor_contract_signed_by`):
	+ Enter the name of the person who signed the contract.
* **Signed Date** (`popup_vendor_contract_signed_date`):
	+ Enter the date the contract was signed.
* **Signed Time** (`popup_vendor_contract_signed_time`):
	+ Enter the time the contract was signed.
* **Signed At** (`popup_vendor_contract_signed_at`):
	+ Enter the date and time the contract was signed.
* **Contract Type** (`popup_vendor_contract_type`):
	+ Indicate if the contract is for goods or services.
* **Status** (`popup_vendor_contract_status`):
	+ Indicate the status of the contract (Active or Inactive).
* **Expires At** (`popup_vendor_contract_expires_at`):
	+ Enter the date the contract is set to expire.
* **Start At** (`popup_vendor_contract_start_at`):
	+ Enter the start date of the contract.
* **End At** (`popup_vendor_contract_end_at`):
	+ Enter the end date of the contract.

## Tips
* Review the fields carefully before filling out the form to ensure you have all the necessary information.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorization to access and update vendor contracts.
* If you are unsure about any field or requirement, please contact the relevant department for assistance.
