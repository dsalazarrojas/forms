# healthcare_consulting_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from both the consultant and client sides for healthcare consulting services. It will help generate quotes and invoices for services rendered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information (First name, Last name, Email, Phone number).
2. Choose the service quote that best describes your needs.
3. Provide your consultant's contact information (name, phone, email).
4. Enter the client's contact information (name, email, phone).
5. Select the service type and billing frequency.
6. Enter any additional billing address information if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Service Quote** (`service_quote`, `select_one`, optional): Select the service quote that best describes your needs.
* **Service Quote Date** (`service_quote_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date for the service quote.
* **Consultant name** (`consultant_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the consultant's name.
* **Consultant Phone** (`consultant_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the consultant's phone number.
* **Consultant Email** (`consultant_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the consultant's email address.
* **Client name** (`client_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's name.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Note** (`client_note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about the client.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the service type(s) you require.
* **Other Service Type** (`service_type_other`, `text`, optional): Enter any other service type not listed.
* **Billing frequency** (`billing_frequency`, `date`, optional): Enter the billing frequency for the services.
* **Billing Cycle** (`billing_cycle`, `select_one`, optional): Select the billing cycle that best describes your needs.
* **Other Billing Cycle** (`billing_cycle_other`, `text`, optional): Enter any other billing cycle not listed.
* **Billing Cycle Frequency** (`billing_cycle_frequency`, `text`, optional): Enter the billing cycle frequency.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the billing address.
* **Consultant Address** (`consultant_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the consultant's address.
* **Consultant Address 2** (`consultant_address_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the consultant's address 2.
* **Consultant Address 3** (`consultant_address_3`, `text`, optional): Enter the consultant's address 3.
* **Consultant Address 4** (`consultant_address_4`, `text`, optional): Enter the consultant's address 4.
* **Consultant Address 5** (`consultant_address_5`, `text`, optional): Enter the consultant's address 5.
