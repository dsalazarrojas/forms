# Case Settlement Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Settlement Discharge Form is used to formalize the discharge of claims after a settlement has been reached. This form provides a detailed record of the settlement details, including the parties involved, the scope of release, and the terms of the discharge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the basic information section, including the case title, case number, and court name.
2. Specify the date of discharge.
3. Enter the settlement details, including the settlement amount and payment terms.
4. Identify the parties involved, including the releasing party and released party.
5. Determine the type of release and scope of release.
6. If applicable, include any exceptions to the release and any continuing obligations.
7. Specify the confidentiality terms and any non-disclosure agreements.
8. If necessary, include additional terms or conditions.
9. Sign and date the form, including the releasing and released parties' signatures and the witness's signature.
10. If required, notarize the form and include the notary's signature and commission number.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
* Ensure all parties have agreed to the terms of the discharge.
* If notarization is required, ensure the notary has signed and dated the form.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for reference.
