# maintenance_contract_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update or modify existing maintenance contracts, including their extensions. It's essential to ensure accurate and up-to-date contract information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have permission to access and update maintenance contracts.
2. Familiarize yourself with the contract details, extension date, and other relevant information.
3. Fill in the required fields, marked with a `*`. Some fields have specific types (e.g., `date`, `select_one`, `text`) that require corresponding input formats.
4. Review and verify the accuracy of your input before submitting.
