# Membership Renewal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for membership renewal purposes within your organization. It allows users to update their membership information, including contact details and membership status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your current contact information.
2. Review and update your membership status as needed.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
4. Submit the form to update your membership status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Please enter your current contact information, including name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your current email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current mailing address.
* **Membership Number** (`membership_number`, text, optional): Enter your membership number, if applicable.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to renew your membership.
* **Renewal Year** (`renewal_year`, number, optional): Enter the year you would like to renew your membership.
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current membership status: Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes related to your membership.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, optional): Select the user responsible for your membership.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, select_one, optional): Select the user who created your membership.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, select_one, optional): Select the user who last updated your membership.
* **Updated Date** (`updated_date`, date, optional): The date your membership was last updated.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, optional): The date your membership was assigned.
