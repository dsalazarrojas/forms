# Commercial Property Insurance Subscription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Commercial Property Insurance Subscription Form is designed to collect information necessary for determining your commercial property insurance coverage needs. Please complete this form to provide details about your business, property, and coverage requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please provide your business and entity profile information, including your business name, nature of activities, and contact details.
2. Answer questions about the property to be insured, such as its address, year built, construction type, and square footage.
3. Indicate the desired coverage amounts for building and business personal property, as well as general liability.
4. Disclose any previous loss history, and provide a brief description of the losses.
5. Enter your contact information, including an email address for quote delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Name of Insured Business** (`insured_business_name`): Enter your business name as it appears on all business documentation, tax records, and licenses.
* **Nature of Business Activities** (`nature_of_business`): Provide a brief description of your business activities to help us understand your coverage needs.
* **Building Address to be Insured** (`building_address`): Enter the physical address of the property to be insured.
* **Year Building was Constructed** (`year_built`): Enter the year the building was constructed.
* **Primary Construction Type** (`construction_type`): Select the primary construction type of the building (e.g., Frame, Masonry, Non-Combustible, Fire Resistive, Mixed).
* **Total Building Square Footage** (`square_feet_total`): Enter the total square footage of the building.
* **Fire Protection Systems Present** (`fire_protection_status`): Select the fire protection systems present in the building (e.g., Full Sprinkler System, Partial Sprinkler System, Fire Alarm (Monitored), Smoke Detectors, Fire Extinguishers).
* **Is there a fire hydrant within 500 feet?** (`proximity_to_fire_hydrant`): Indicate whether there is a fire hydrant within 500 feet of the building.
* **Desired Building Coverage Limit (Replacement Cost)** (`replacement_cost_value`): Enter the desired coverage limit for the building (e.g., $1M, $2M, $3M, etc.).
* **Business Personal Property Limit (Inventory/Equip)** (`business_personal_property_limit`): Enter the desired coverage limit for business personal property (e.g., $1M, $2M, $3M, etc.).
* **Desired General Liability Limit** (`general_liability_limit`): Select the desired general liability limit (e.g., $1M / $2M, $2M / $4M, Higher).
* **Have you had any property losses in the last 3 years?** (`previous_loss_history`): Indicate whether you have had any property losses in the last 3 years.
* **If yes, please describe losses** (`loss_history_description`): Provide a brief description of any previous losses.
* **Contact Email for Quote Delivery** (`primary_agent_email`): Enter the email address where you would like to receive quotes and policy documents.
* **Additional Risks or Special Instructions** (`additional_insurance_notes`): Enter any additional risks or special instructions not covered by the above fields.
