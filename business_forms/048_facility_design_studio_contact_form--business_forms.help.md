# Facility Design Studio Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used for project inquiries and facility design studio submissions. The form is used to collect information about a project and gather contact details from the user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant project information from the fields provided.
2. Fill in any applicable contact information.
3. Provide any additional details or notes about the project.
4. Choose the submission date and time format that suits your needs.
5. Select the facility designer(s) involved in the project.
6. Select the project status.
7. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Inquiry** (`project_inquiry`, text, required/optional): This field is intended for users to briefly describe their project inquiry.
- **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the project.
- **Facility Size** (`facility_size`, number, required/optional): Enter the size of the facility. The unit of measurement will be displayed in the field below.
- **Facility Type** (`facility_type`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the facility type.
- **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, required/optional): Enter the project budget.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Fill in any contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of submission.
- **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of submission.
- **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the current status of the project from the provided options.
- **Facility Designer** (`facility_designer`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the facility designer(s) involved in the project from the provided options.
- **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the submission.
- **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address for form submission.
- **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the project manager.
- **Facility Location** (`facility_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the facility.
- **Submission Date/Time** (`submission_date_time`, date, required/optional): Enter the date and time of submission.
- **Facility Size Unit** (`facility_size_unit`, text, required/optional): Enter the unit of measurement for the facility size.
- **Submission Date Format** (`submission_date_format`, select_one, required/optional): Select the format of the submission date from the provided options.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check your contact information for correct formatting.
- Ensure the submission date and time are correct and in the chosen format.
- Select the correct facility designer and project status.
- Proofread your submission note for any errors.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to your project manager or contact us.
