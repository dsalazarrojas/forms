# Fuel Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fuel Reimbursement Claim Form is used to document and process fuel claims for employees who use company vehicles for business purposes. It gathers essential information about the claim, including the employee's details, trip dates, mileage, fuel cost, and business purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee ID to identify yourself.
2. Select the Start Date and End Date of the trip or period during which you used company fuel.
3. Record the Odometer Reading Start and Odometer Reading End to calculate the total miles driven.
4. Enter the total Miles Driven during the claim period.
5. Provide a brief description of the Business Purpose for the trip.
6. Enter the Fuel Cost associated with the claim.
7. Select the person who Submitted this claim (e.g., Manager, Department Head, HR, or Supervisor).
8. Choose the Submit To option (e.g., Finance, Accounts Payable, HR, or Manager).
9. Select the Approval Level for this claim (e.g., Manager, Department Head, Director, or CEO).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee ID** (1): Enter your employee ID number to identify yourself.
- **Start Date** (2): Select the date you started the trip or period during which you used company fuel.
- **End Date** (3): Select the date you ended the trip or period during which you used company fuel.
- **Odometer Reading Start** (4): Record the odometer reading at the start of the trip or period.
- **Odometer Reading End** (5): Record the odometer reading at the end of the trip or period.
- **Miles Driven** (6): Enter the total miles driven during the claim period.
- **Fuel Cost** (7): Enter the total fuel cost associated with this claim.
- **Business Purpose** (8): Provide a brief description of the business purpose for the trip.
- **Reimbursement Amount** (9): Enter the total reimbursement amount requested.
- **Submitted By** (10): Select the person who submitted this claim (e.g., Manager, Department Head, HR, or Supervisor).
- **Submitted To** (11): Choose the department or person to whom this claim is submitted (e.g., Finance, Accounts Payable, HR, or Manager).
- **Approval Level** (12): Select the approval level required for this claim (e.g., Manager, Department Head, Director, or CEO).

## Tips
- Ensure that you complete the form accurately and truthfully.
- Attach any supporting documents or receipts that may be required for verification.
- Submit the form to the designated person or department to initiate the review and approval process.
- Be prepared to provide additional information or clarification if requested during the review process.
