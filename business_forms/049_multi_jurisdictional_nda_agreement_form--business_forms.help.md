# Multi Jurisdictional NDA Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for Multi Jurisdictional Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) forms, which are essential for international business operations or collaborations. The form helps ensure that all parties involved understand and agree to keep confidential information within their respective jurisdictions or regions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the first field.
2. Enter your job title in the second field.
3. Type your location in the third field.
4. Select your jurisdiction, region, or country from the dropdown list in the fourth field (USA, UK, CA, Australia, Germany, France, Japan, or China).
5. Enter the date you signed the agreement in the fifth field.
6. Indicate whether you signed the agreement by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the sixth field.
7. Choose whether a signature is required from the seventh field.
8. Add your signature in the eighth field (if a signature is required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on the agreement.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within your organization.
* **Location**: Type your physical or business location.
* **Jurisdiction/Region/Country**: Select the area where you operate or reside. This helps ensure the agreement is applied to the correct jurisdiction.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the agreement.
* **Agreement Signed**: Indicate if you have signed the agreement by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'. This confirms your acceptance of the agreement's terms.
* **Signature Required**: Choose if a signature is required for the agreement, usually for official documents.
* **Signature**: Add your signature if required. This is for official verification purposes.

## Tips

* Ensure you understand the agreement terms before signing.
* Verify that you're selecting the correct jurisdiction/region/country for your location.
* If a signature is required, please add your actual signature.
* Review the agreement before signing to ensure it's applicable and compliant with your jurisdiction's laws.
* Keep the agreement confidential and secure.
