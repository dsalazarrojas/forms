# Telecommunications Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telecommunications Service Request Form is designed to collect information from customers or companies requesting telecommunications services such as installation, repair, or maintenance. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Provide a brief description of the service you are requesting in the **Service Request** field.
2. Enter your **Customer Info** details, including name, contact information, and company information.
3. Select the **Service Type** that best describes the reason for the request (Installation, Repair, or Maintenance).
4. Enter the date and time of the service request in the **Service Request Date** and **Service Request Time** fields.
5. Provide a detailed description of the service request in the **Service Request Details** field.
6. Enter your contact information, including **Contact Name**, **Contact Email**, **Contact Phone**, **Company Name**, **Company Website**, and **Company Address**.
7. Identify the **Contact Person** and their **Contact Person Title**.
8. Select the **Company Type** (Business, Non-profit, or Residential).
9. (Optional) If the company type is "Other", you can provide a detailed description in the **Other Company Type** field.
10. Select the **Assigned Tool** (Chatjimmy or Other) that will be used to address the request.
11. (Optional) If "Other" is selected, provide a detailed description of the other tool in the **Other Assigned Tool** field.
12. Enter any **Customer Comments** or concerns.
13. Provide any additional **Internal Notes** for the internal team to reference.
14. (Optional) If applicable, provide a signed copy of the **Customer Agreement** and **Internal Agreement**.
15. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms of the **Customer Agreement** and **Internal Agreement**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Request**: A brief description of the service you are requesting.
* **Customer Info**: Details about the customer or company making the request.
	+ **Contact Name**: The name of the person making the request.
	+ **Contact Email**: The email address of the person making the request.
	+ **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the person making the request.
	+ **Company Name**: The name of the company making the request.
	+ **Company Website**: The website of the company making the request.
	+ **Company Address**: The physical address of the company making the request.
* **Service Type**: The reason for the service request (Installation, Repair, or Maintenance).
* **Service Request Date**: The date you are requesting the service.
* **Service Request Time**: The time of the service request.
* **Service Request Details**: A detailed description of the service you are requesting.
* **Contact Person**: The name of the person making the request.
* **Contact Person Title**: The title of the person making the request.
* **Company Type**: The type of company (Business, Non-profit, or Residential).
* **Other Company Type**: A detailed description of the company type if it is not one of the listed options.
* **Assigned Tool**: The tool that will be used to address the request (Chatjimmy or Other).
* **Other Assigned Tool**: A detailed description of the other tool if selected.
* **Customer Comments**: Any additional comments or concerns from the customer.
* **Internal Notes**: Any additional notes for the internal team to reference.
* **Customer Agreement**: A signed copy of the customer's agreement.
* **Internal Agreement**: A signed copy of the internal agreement.
* **Customer Agreement Signed**: Confirmation that the customer has signed the agreement.
* **Internal Agreement Signed**: Confirmation that the internal team has signed the agreement.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have all necessary information to complete the form accurately.
- If you are filling out the form multiple times for the same reason, please use a single instance of the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the internal team for assistance.
