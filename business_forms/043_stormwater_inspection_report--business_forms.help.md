# Stormwater Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The stormwater inspection report is used to document the condition and performance of stormwater systems, including pumps, catch basins, and other infrastructure. This form helps in monitoring and managing these systems, ensuring they are functioning correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorization to access and fill out this form.
2. Fill out the form fields accurately and thoroughly, especially the required fields.
3. Provide clear and concise information for each field to facilitate easy understanding and reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Info** (Page 1, **id: 1**):
	+ This field is not required but provides a general description of the site.
* **Inspector Name** (Page 1, **id: 2**):
	+ Required field to record the name of the inspector who performed the inspection.
* **Inspector Signature** (Page 1, **id: 3**):
	+ A field for the inspector's signature.
* **Date Completed** (Page 1, **id: 4**):
	+ Required field for the date of the inspection completion.
* **Stormwater Pumps** (Page 1, **id: 5**):
	+ Select whether stormwater pumps are present ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Sump Pumps** (Page 1, **id: 6**):
	+ Select whether sump pumps are present ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Catch Basins** (Page 1, **id: 7**):
	+ A numeric field to record the number of catch basins.
* **DSS Pumps** (Page 1, **id: 8**):
	+ A numeric field to record the number of DSS pipes.
* **DSS Valves** (Page 1, **id: 9**):
	+ Select whether DSS valves are present ('Yes' or 'No').
* **DDR Pumps** (Page 1, **id: 10**):
	+ A numeric field to record the number of DDR pumps.
* **DDR Valves** (Page 1, **id: 11**):
	+ Select whether DDR valves are present ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Cistern Pumps** (Page 1, **id: 12**):
	+ Select whether cistern pumps are present ('Yes' or 'No').
* **DSS Pumping Capacity** (Page 1, **id: 13**):
	+ A numeric field to record the DSS pumping capacity.
* **DDR Pumping Capacity** (Page 1, **id: 14**):
	+ A numeric field to record the DDR pumping capacity.
* **DSS Pumping Rate** (Page 1, **id: 15**):
	+ A numeric field to record the DSS pumping rate.
* **DDR Pumping Rate** (Page 1, **id: 16**):
	+ A numeric field to record the DDR pumping rate.
* **Sump Pumping Capacity** (Page 1, **id: 17**):
	+ A numeric field to record the sump pumping capacity.
* **Sump Pumping Rate** (Page 1, **id: 18**):
	+ A numeric field to record the sump pumping rate.
* **Sump Pumping Volume** (Page 1, **id: 19**):
	+ A numeric field to record the sump pumping volume.
* **Inspection Notes** (Page 1, **id: 20**):
	+ A field for recording any inspection notes.
* **Action Items** (Page 1, **id: 21**):
	+ A field to list any action items.
* **Photo Evidence** (Page 1, **id: 22**):
	+ A field for uploading photo evidence.
* **Notes** (Page 1, **id: 23**):
	+ A field for any additional notes.
* **Site Map** (Page 1, **id: 24**):
	+ A field to upload a site map.
* **Site Visit Date** (Page 1, **id: 25**):
	+ A field to record the site visit date.

Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, and if you have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities or technical support for clarification.
