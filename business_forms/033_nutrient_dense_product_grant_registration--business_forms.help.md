# Nutrient Dense Product Grant Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help businesses register their nutrient-dense products for grants. The form collects project details, project impact, funding plans, contact information, and additional information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project details, including any relevant information about your project.
2. Describe the project's impact and how it will benefit the organization or community.
3. Provide your funding plans, including any relevant budgets or financial details.
4. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
5. Add any additional information that is relevant to your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details**: This field is where you should provide an overview of your project, including its name, description, and any relevant dates or timelines.
* **Project Impact**: Describe how your project will benefit the organization or community. What are the expected outcomes, and how will it make a positive impact?
* **Funding Plans**: Outline your funding plans, including any budgets, financial projections, or other relevant details.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number so that we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Additional Information**: Use this field to add any additional details that are relevant to your project. This could include references, supporting documents, or other information that will help us evaluate your application.
