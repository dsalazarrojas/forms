# Product Lifecycle Tracking Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Lifecycle Tracking Registration Form is a tool for tracking the lifecycle stages of a product. This form helps to register products and their corresponding lifecycle stages for easier management and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form purpose and ensure you're not duplicating an existing product.
2.  Enter all the required information for the product, including its details and lifecycle stages.
3.  Fill in any relevant notes about the product.
4.  Designate the team members assigned to the product.
5.  Choose the product manager responsible for the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Details**: This field is for entering the product's name, description, or any other relevant information.
* **Lifecycle Stages**: Select all the lifecycle stages that apply to the product. This field is for indicating whether the product is in a specific lifecycle stage or not.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about the product.
* **Assigned Team Members**: Select the team members who are responsible for the product. This field is for specifying the team members who will be handling the product.
* **Product Manager**: Choose the product manager who will be overseeing the product's lifecycle. This field is for assigning the person responsible for making key decisions about the product.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all relevant information for accurate product tracking.
* Review the product's lifecycle stages regularly to ensure it's up to date.
* Use the product manager field to assign a key person for product decision-making.
* Avoid assigning duplicate team members to avoid confusion.
* Ensure that the form is filled out correctly to prevent errors in the product's lifecycle tracking.
