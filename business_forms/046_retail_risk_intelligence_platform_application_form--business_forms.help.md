# Retail Risk Intelligence Platform Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from businesses applying to our retail risk intelligence platform.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question accurately and completely.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form Data** (id: 1, text): Please provide a brief description of your business and its purpose.
* **Business Details** (id: 2, text): Share your business's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Risk Challenges** (id: 3, text): Describe the specific risk challenges your business faces.
* **Risk Analytics** (id: 4, select multiple): Select the risk analytics you require for your business from the list.
* **Qualification Criteria** (id: 5, text): Explain the qualifications your business needs to meet for a successful onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Process** (id: 6, text): Describe your preferred onboarding process.
* **Notes** (id: 7, note): Add any additional notes that would help us better understand your application.
* **Assigned To** (id: 8, select one): Select the person assigned to your account.
* **Email** (id: 9, email): Enter your business's email address.
* **Phone** (id: 10, text): Enter your business's phone number.
* **Business Category** (id: 11, select one): Select the category your business falls under (Retail, Finance, or Technology).
* **Description** (id: 12, text): Provide a description of your business and its services.
* **Submission Date** (id: 13, date): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Submission Time** (id: 14, time): Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Form Version** (id: 15, text): Enter the current version of this form.
* **Submitted By** (id: 16, text): Enter your name as the person submitting this form.
* **Submitted At** (id: 17, date): Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Submitted By Name** (id: 18, text): Enter your name as the person submitting this form.
* **Submitted By Email** (id: 19, email): Enter your email address.
* **Submitted By Phone** (id: 20, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitted By Company** (id: 21, text): Enter your company's name.
* **Submitted By Department** (id: 22, text): Enter your department name.
* **Submitted By Title** (id: 23, text): Enter your title.
* **Submitted By Position** (id: 24, text): Enter your position.
* **Submitted By Job Title** (id: 25, text): Enter your job title.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us. We're here to help!
