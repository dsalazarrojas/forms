# Factory Resilience Consultant Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals seeking assistance with factory resilience. It collects contact information and other relevant details to facilitate internal communication and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
* Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
* Specify your position or role in the "Position" field.
* Identify the department you belong to in the "Department" field.
* Write a brief message in the "Message" field, describing your needs or requests.
* Select the tool you would like to be assigned (if applicable) in the "Assigned Tool" field.
* Choose the category related to your request in the "Category" field.
* Enter the path to the output file (if applicable) in the "Output File" field.
* Provide a brief description of your request in the "Description" field.
* If applicable, select the date when your request was created.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is spelled.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for additional communication.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Position**: Describe your role or position within the company.
* **Department**: Identify the department you belong to.
* **Message**: Write a brief message describing your needs or requests.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select a tool that you think would be relevant to your request (if applicable).
* **Category**: Choose a category related to your request (if applicable).
* **Output File**: If you have an output file related to your request, enter its path here (if applicable).
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your request.
* **Created At**: If you're reporting back on an existing request, select the date when it was created (if applicable).

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
