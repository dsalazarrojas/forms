# Corporate Budgeting Alignment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Budgeting Alignment Survey is a crucial tool used to align individual and departmental budgets with the company's overall financial goals. This form helps to capture essential information for budget planning, tracking, and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure you've included all the necessary details.
3. Submit the form to ensure your input is recorded and reviewed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person in charge of the company.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department that this budget aligns with (e.g., Department A, Department B, Department C).
* **Budget Alignment** (`budget_alignment`, select_multiple, required): Choose the relevant budget alignment options for your department (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions for improving the budgeting process.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): Select the date you're submitting the form (this will auto-populate if not selected).
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, optional): Select the time of submission (this will auto-populate if not selected).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about the submission.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): Choose relevant category options for the budget (e.g., Category A, Category B, Category C).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool being used for budgeting (e.g., chatjimmy, JotForm, Other).
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Enter the name of the output file generated by the survey (if applicable).
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the budget and its alignment.
