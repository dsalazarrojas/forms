# Office Liquidation Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to office liquidation inquiries. It is used to gather details about the location, inspection schedule, and asset value for liquidation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
3. Provide the address and location details of the office to be liquidated.
4. Specify the type of inspection required (on-site, off-site, or other).
5. Choose the type of asset (asset, non-asset, or other) and provide its value.
6. Schedule the inspection date and time.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection or asset.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information (1)**: Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
  - Type: text, required: true
- **Address (11)**: Provide the address of the office to be liquidated.
  - Type: text, required: true
- **Location (12)**: Specify the location of the office.
  - Type: text, required: true
- **Sell-Off Date (23)**: Schedule the date of the sell-off.
  - Type: date, required: true
- **Contact Person (9)**: Enter the name of the contact person.
  - Type: text, required: false
- **Company Name (10)**: Enter the name of the company.
  - Type: text, required: true
- **Inspection Date (14)**: Schedule the inspection date. This can be left blank if not applicable.
  - Type: date, required: false
