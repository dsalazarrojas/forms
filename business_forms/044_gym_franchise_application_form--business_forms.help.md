# Gym Franchise Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Franchise Application Form is a tool used by the company to collect information from individuals interested in becoming a gym franchisee. This form is essential in the application process, ensuring that the company receives all necessary details to review and consider the application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the relevant options for each field according to your circumstances.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
4. Ensure that you have attached any required supporting documents before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Applicant Info (Required)
* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, `text`, Required): This field is used to capture your personal details, such as your name and contact information. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. Experience (Optional)
* **Experience** (`experience`, `Select One`, Optional): This field asks about your relevant work experience in the fitness industry. Please select the option that best describes your background.

### 3. Investment (Optional)
* **Investment** (`investment`, `Number`, Optional): This field is used to determine your financial investment capacity. Please enter the amount you are willing and able to invest in the franchise.

### 4. Location (Optional)
* **Location** (`location`, `Text`, Optional): This field is used to identify your preferred location for the gym. Please enter the address or name of the city where you would like to establish the gym.

### 5. Franchise Lead (Optional)
* **Franchise Lead** (`franchise_lead`, `Select Multiple`, Optional): This field is used to identify your marketing and sales approach for the gym. Please select the options that best describe your strategy.

### 6. Other Info (Optional)
* **Other Info** (`other_info`, `Note`, Optional): This field is for any additional information you would like to share about yourself or your plans for the gym. Please use this space to provide any relevant details not covered in the other fields.

### 7. Confirm Info (Optional)
* **Confirm Info** (`confirm_info`, `Select One`, Optional): This field is used to confirm your understanding of the application process. Please select the option that best describes your level of commitment.

### 8. Confirm Location (Optional)
* **Confirm Location** (`confirm_location`, `Select Multiple`, Optional): This field is used to confirm your preferred location for the gym. Please select the options that best describe your preferred location.

### 9. Agreement Terms (Optional)
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, `Select Multiple`, Optional): This field is used to confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions of the franchise agreement. Please select the options that best describe your understanding of the agreement.

Please ensure that you have reviewed and understood all the fields before submitting the form.
