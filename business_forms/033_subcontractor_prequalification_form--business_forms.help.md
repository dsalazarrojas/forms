# Subcontractor Prequalification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a prequalification tool used to assess subcontractors' suitability for potential partnerships. It helps us understand your business, experience, and qualifications to determine if you're a good fit for our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. Most fields are optional, but please make sure to provide your first name, last name, and contact number (if required) to ensure we can contact you back.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact number if you have one.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your company's name.
* **Company Address 1** (`company_address_1`, `text`, optional): Enter your company's address line 1.
* **Company Address 2** (`company_address_2`, `text`, optional): Enter your company's address line 2.
* **Company Address 3** (`company_address_3`, `text`, optional): Enter your company's address line 3.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your company email address.
* **Business Experience** (`business_experience`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your business experience level (5-10 years, 5-15 years, or More than 15 years).
* **Business License** (`business_license`, `select_one`, optional): Select your business license type (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Subcontractor Type** (`subcontractor_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select your subcontractor type (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select your company size (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, optional): Select your location (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the services you offer (Service A, Service B, Service C, or Service D, Service E).
* **Equipment Needed** (`equipment_needed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any equipment you need (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of job you offer (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Certification** (`certification`, `select_one`, optional): Select your certification type (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your payment terms (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Insurance** (`insurance`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your insurance type (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Bonding** (`bonding`, `select_one`, optional): Select your bonding type (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Workers Compensation** (`workers_compensation`, `select_one`, optional): Select your workers compensation type (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **General Liability** (`general_liability`, `select_one`, optional): Select your general liability type (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Umbrella** (`umbrella`, `select_one`, optional): Select your umbrella type (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Equipment Cost** (`equipment_cost`, `number`, optional): Enter the equipment cost (number).
* **Labor Cost** (`labor_cost`, `number`, optional): Enter the labor cost (number).
