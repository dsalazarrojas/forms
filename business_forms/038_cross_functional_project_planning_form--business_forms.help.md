## cross-functional-project-planning-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for project management and planning purposes. It is used to track and manage projects from start to finish, including project goals, progress, and team members. This form will help you plan and execute your projects more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

Before you start, make sure you have all the necessary information ready. Each field is explained in detail below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Management (1)**: Enter a brief description of your project, including its goals and objectives. If this field is left blank, your project will not be recorded in our system.
* **Project Goals (2)**: Enter the specific goals and objectives of your project. This will help us and others track the project's progress and ensure it meets its objectives.
* **Task Assignment (3)**: Indicate whether each task has been assigned to a team member. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Progress Tracking (4)**: Enter the date when your project started and ended. This will help you track progress and identify milestones.
* **Meeting Schedule (5)**: Enter the date and time of the next meeting. This will help you stay on top of project discussions and updates.
* **Team Members (6)**: Indicate whether each team member has been assigned to the project. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Project Status (7)**: Select the current status of your project. Options are 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Project Duration (8)**: Enter the number of days or weeks the project will take to complete.
* **Team Role (9)**: Indicate whether each team member has a specific role in the project. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Task Description (10)**: Enter a brief description of each task, including its goals and objectives.
* **Deadline (11)**: Enter the deadline for task completion.
* **Start Date (12)**: Enter the start date of your project.
* **End Date (13)**: Enter the end date of your project.
* **Priority (14)**: Indicate whether each task has a high or low priority. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Task Status (15)**: Select the current status of each task. Options are 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Project Manager (16)**: Indicate whether you are the project manager. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Assigned To (17)**: Indicate whether each task is assigned to a team member. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Task Assignment Status (18)**: Select the current status of task assignment. Options are 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Progress Percentage (19)**: Enter the progress percentage of your project.
* **Progress Note (20)**: Enter any notes about the progress of your project.
* **Meeting Notes (21)**: Enter any notes from your meetings.
* **Task Completion Status (22)**: Select the current status of task completion. Options are 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Task Completion Date (23)**: Enter the date when each task is completed.
* **Next Meeting Date (24)**: Enter the date of the next meeting.
* **Last Meeting Date (25)**: Enter the date of the last meeting.

This guide is designed to help you understand how to complete this form and make the most out of it. Please let us know if you have any questions or need further clarification.
