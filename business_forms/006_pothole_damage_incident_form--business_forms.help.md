# pothole_damage_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pothole damage incident form is designed to collect information about a pothole damage incident reported by drivers. This form will help gather accurate details about the incident, including the date, vehicle information, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine if the incident is genuine and not a duplicate. If it is not, do not proceed.
2.  Select "Yes" or "No" for each field as it applies to your situation.
3.  Fill out the relevant fields with accurate information about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required): Choose "Yes" if you have submitted the incident report. This confirms that you are submitting the form as a report.
- **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the incident. If the incident happened on a date in the past, select the correct date.
- **Vehicle Damage Description** (`vehicle_damage_description`, `text`, required): Describe the damage to your vehicle caused by the pothole incident.
- **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Provide the location where the pothole was found.
- **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether your vehicle was involved in the incident.
- **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, `text`, required): Enter the color of your vehicle.
- **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the first name of the contact person.
- **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the last name of the contact person.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact email of the contact person. This is optional but recommended for easier communication.
- **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `text`, required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
- **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required): Enter the make of your vehicle.
- **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model of your vehicle.
- **Vehicle License Plate** (`vehicle_license_plate`, `text`, required): Enter the license plate number of your vehicle.
- **Damage Type** (`damage_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose "Yes" or "No" indicating whether your vehicle was damaged or not.
- **Photo or Video** (`photo_or_video`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose "Yes" or "No" indicating whether you have taken a photo or video of the incident.
- **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information you think might be helpful to the incident investigation.
- **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you have completed the report accurately and truthfully.
