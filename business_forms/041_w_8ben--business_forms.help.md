# W 8Ben - Help Guide
## Purpose
The W 8Ben form is a certification document for businesses that have international tax implications. It helps businesses provide accurate information for tax purposes, ensuring compliance and avoiding any potential issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your business name.
2. Select your country.
3. Enter your date of birth (if required).
4. Enter your address (if required).
5. Enter your phone number (if required).
6. Select the type of business you are (yes/no).
7. Enter your business ID (if required).
8. Enter your W-8BEN certification number.
9. Enter the date of your W-8BEN certification.
10. Enter the issue date of your W-8BEN certification.
11. Select your business status (active/inactive).
12. Enter the name of the entity that issued your W-8BEN certification.
13. Select the status of your W-8BEN certification (active/inactive).
14. Select whether you are a yes/no for W-8BEN tax year.
15. Select whether you are a yes/no for W-8BEN certification period.
16. Select whether you are a yes/no for W-8BEN certification type.
17. Select whether you are a yes/no for W-8BEN certification country.
18. Enter the date of your W-8BEN certification (certificate).
19. Enter the issue date of your W-8BEN certification (issuer).
20. Enter the date of your W-8BEN certification (issuer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name (1)** (`business_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your business.
* **Country (2)** (`country`, text, required: false): Select the country where your business is located.
* **Date of Birth (3)** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth (if required).
* **Address (4)** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your business address (if required).
* **Phone (5)** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your business phone number (if required).
* **Business Type (6)** (`business_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of business you are (yes/no).
* **Business ID (7)** (`business_id`, text, required: false): Enter your business ID (if required).
* **W-8BEN Cert. No. (8)** (`w8ben_cert_no`, text, required: false): Enter your W-8BEN certification number.
* **W-8BEN Cert. Date (9)** (`w8ben_cert_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your W-8BEN certification.
* **W-8BEN Issue Date (10)** (`w8ben_cert_issue_date`, date, required: false): Enter the issue date of your W-8BEN certification.
* **Business Status (11)** (`business_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your business status (active/inactive).
* **W-8BEN Issuer (12)** (`w8ben_issuer`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the entity that issued your W-8BEN certification.
* **W-8BEN Cert. Status (13)** (`w8ben_cert_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your W-8BEN certification (active/inactive).
* **Business Address (14)** (`business_address`, text, required: false): Enter your business address (if required).
* **W-8BEN Cert. Number (15)** (`w8ben_cert_number`, text, required: false): Enter your W-8BEN certification number.
* **W-8BEN Tax Year (16)** (`w8ben_tax_year`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you are a yes/no for W-8BEN tax year.
* **W-8BEN Cert. Period (17)** (`w8ben_cert_period`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you are a yes/no for W-8BEN certification period.
* **W-8BEN Cert. Type (18)** (`w8ben_cert_type`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you are a yes/no for W-8BEN certification type.
* **W-8BEN Cert. Country (19)** (`w8ben_cert_country`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you are a yes/no for W-8BEN certification country.
* **W-8BEN Cert. Date (Cert.) (20)** (`w8ben_cert_date_cert`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your W-8BEN certification (certificate).
* **W-8BEN Cert. Date (Issuer) (21)** (`w8ben_cert_date_issuer`, date, required: false): Enter the issue date of your W-8BEN certification (issuer).
* **W-8BEN Cert. Issue Date (Cert.) (22)** (`w8ben_cert_issue_date_cert`, date, required: false): Enter the issue date of your W-8BEN certification (certificate).
* **W-8BEN Cert. Issue Date (Issuer) (23)** (`w8ben_cert_issue_date_issuer`, date, required: false): Enter the issue date of your W-8BEN certification (issuer).
* **W-8BEN Cert. Date (Issuer) (24)** (`w8ben_cert_date_issuer`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your W-8BEN certification (issuer).
* **W-8BEN Cert. Status (Issuer) (25)** (`w8ben_cert_status_issuer`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your W-8BEN certification (active/inactive).
