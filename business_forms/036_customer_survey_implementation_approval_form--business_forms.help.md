# Customer Survey Implementation Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and manage customer survey implementations within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Category" of the form (e.g., "Business Forms") to categorize the survey.
2. Enter the "Form Name" and provide a brief description in the "Description" field.
3. Enter the "Launch Date" of the survey.
4. Provide any additional context or information in the "Notes" field.
5. Update the "Approval Status" to "Approved", "Denied", or "Pending" as per the approval process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Name**: Enter the name of the survey implementation.
* **Category**: Select the type of form (e.g., "Business Forms") to categorize and filter the survey.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the survey.
* **Launch Date**: Enter the date when the survey was launched or implemented.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or explanations about the survey (required).
* **Approval Status**: Select the status of the survey (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Form Footer**: This field is for providing additional information or context. It's not a required field.
