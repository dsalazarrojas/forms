# Resticcing automation quote request form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resticcing automation quote request form is designed to collect information from customers for quote requests. This form helps us understand their needs and provide an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the customer.
2. Choose the suppliers you would like to receive a quote from.
3. Enter any additional notes or details about the quote request.
4. Enter the email address of the person who should receive the quote.
5. Fill in the phone number of the person who should receive the quote.
6. Select the status of the quote.
7. Enter any additional details about the quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quote Request** (`form_data`, text, optional): Fill in a brief description of the quote request.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_data`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the customer, such as their name, company name, or contact person.
* **Quote Date** (`quote_date`, date, optional): If a specific quote date is required, enter it here.
* **Quote Amount** (`quote_amount`, number, optional): Enter the estimated quote amount.
* **Suppliers** (`supplier_list`, select multiple, optional): Choose from the available suppliers you would like to receive quotes from.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or details about the quote request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who should receive the quote.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Fill in the phone number of the person who should receive the quote.
* **Quote Status** (`quote_status`, select one, optional): Select the status of the quote.
* **Quote Details** (`quote_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the quote.
* **Quote Details** (`quote_details`, text, optional) [Duplicate field, can be ignored]
