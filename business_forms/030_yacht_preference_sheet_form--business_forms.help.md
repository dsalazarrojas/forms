# Yacht Preference Sheet Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Yacht Preference Sheet Form is a tool designed to collect information about your preferences for a yacht charter experience. This form helps us understand your needs and tailor your experience to your liking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each field and provide your answers according to your preferences.
2. For questions with multiple options, select the relevant options that suit your needs.
3. For text fields, provide as much detail as you can.
4. If a field is optional, you can skip it if you don't have a preference or opinion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Yacht Preference Sheet Form
* No specific field explanation needed.

* Please provide any personal preferences or needs you have while on the yacht (e.g., dietary restrictions).

### 3. Preferred Yacht Size
* Choose the size of yacht you prefer for your charter (e.g., small, medium, large).

### 4. Preferred Yacht Type
* Choose the type of yacht that suits your style (e.g., Monohull, Catamaran, Speedboat).

### 5. Preferred Yacht Location
* Select all locations you're interested in visiting or would like to avoid (e.g., Mediterranean Sea, Red Sea, Caribbean Sea).

### 6. Preferred Yacht Cabin Type

### 7. Preferred Yacht Meal Time

### 8. Preferred Yacht Meal Frequency

### 9. Preferred Yacht Bar Service

### 10. Preferred Yacht Cocktails

### 12. Preferred Yacht Diet

### 13. Preferred Yacht Smoking

### 14. Preferred Yacht Music

### 15. Preferred Yacht Entertainment

### 18. Preferred Yacht Tipping

### 19. Preferred Yacht Tour

### 20. Preferred Yacht Comments

### 21. Preferred Yacht Signature

### 22. Preferred Yacht Additional Comments
* Please provide any additional comments or requests.

### 23. Preferred Yacht Departure Time
* If applicable, select a departure time that suits your schedule.

### 24. Preferred Yacht Arrival Time
