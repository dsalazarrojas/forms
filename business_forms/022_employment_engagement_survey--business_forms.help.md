# Employment Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their overall happiness and satisfaction within the company. The survey is meant to gauge how employees feel about their work environment, tasks, and overall well-being. It's essential to fill out this form to help the management understand your concerns and improve the work experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions honestly, as this is a confidential and anonymous survey.
2. Choose the options that best fit your feelings for each question.
3. Don't hesitate to provide any additional comments or insights in the text fields.
4. Make sure to submit your response before the deadline, so your feedback can be considered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall happiness** (`overall_happiness`, number, optional): This question asks you to rate your overall happiness on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is unhappy and 5 is very happy.
* **Satisfaction level** (`satisfaction_level`, number, optional): Similar to the previous question, this one asks you to rate your satisfaction level with the company on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Perception** (`perception`, number, optional): This question also asks you to rate your perception of the company, on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **question 4** (`question_4`, text, optional): This question is an open-ended text field where you can provide any thoughts or comments you'd like to share about your experience.
* **Question 5** (`question_5`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the options that apply to you from the list:
	+ Option 1: Option 1
	+ Option 2: Option 2
	+ Option 3: Option 3
* **Question 6** (`question_6`, date, optional): Enter the date of a specific event or situation that relates to your answer.
* **Question 7** (`question_7`, time, optional): Enter the time of a specific event or situation that relates to your answer.
* **Question 8** (`question_8`, note, optional): This is a note field where you can add any additional comments or notes about your answer.
* **Question 9** (`question_9`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Question 10** (`question_10`, text, optional): This question is another open-ended text field where you can provide any final thoughts or comments.
