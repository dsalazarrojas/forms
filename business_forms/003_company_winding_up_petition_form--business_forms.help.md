# Company Winding Up Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by an individual or company to submit a petition to wind up a company that is currently operational but has reached a point where it can no longer continue to do business. The form is meant to be a concise and straightforward way to initiate the process of winding up the company, which involves shutting down the company's operations and distributing its assets to creditors or other parties.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that you are using the correct format for each field.
3. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Petitioner Name** (<name of petitioner>): This field is required and should be filled in with the name of the individual or company that is filing the petition.
* **Contact Email** (<email>): This field is required and should be filled in with a valid contact email address. This will be used as the primary contact method for communication regarding the petition.
* **Contact Phone** (<phone>): This field is not required but is recommended for additional contact information.
* **Company Name** (<company name>): This field is required and should be filled in with the name of the company that is subject to winding up.
* **Company Registration Number** (<registration number>): This field is not required but is recommended for providing additional information about the company.
* **Reason for Petition** (<reason>): This field is required and should be filled in with a brief summary of the grounds for winding up the company.
* **Petition Date** (<date>): This field is required and should be filled in with the date of submission of the petition.
* **Supporting Documents List** (<list>): This field is not required but is recommended for listing any additional documents that support the petition.
* **Representative Name** (<name>): This field is required and should be filled in with the name of the person filing on behalf of the petitioner.
