# Audit Review Summary Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audit Review Summary Submission form is used to collect information about the results of an audit review. It's essential to fill out the form accurately, as it helps ensure the audit process is thorough, efficient, and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and supporting documents.
2. Fill out the form in the required sections, providing accurate and clear information.
3. Review each section carefully to ensure you haven't missed any crucial details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reviewer Full Name** (1): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (2): Provide your official email address for contact purposes.
* **Reviewer Title** (3): Type your job title as it appears on your official records.
* **Department** (4): Select the department that conducted the audit.
* **Submission Date** (5): Enter the date you're submitting this review.
* **Audit Name** (6): Enter the name of the audit being reviewed.
* **Audit Reference** (7): Enter the reference number of the audit.
* **Audit Period** (8): Describe the period covered by the audit.
* **Review Date** (9): Enter the date the review was conducted.
* **Review Type** (10): Choose the type of review conducted (e.g., Preliminary, Final, Quality, Peer, Management).
* **Overall Assessment** (11): Select the overall assessment of the audit (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Key Findings Summary** (12): Summarize the key findings of the audit.
* **Findings Count** (13): Enter the total number of findings.
* **High Priority Findings** (14): Enter the number of high-priority findings.
* **Recommendations Summary** (15): Summarize the recommendations made.
* **Compliance Status** (16): Select the overall compliance status (e.g., Fully Compliant, Mostly Compliant, Partially Compliant, Non-Compliant, Not Assessed).
* **Strengths Identified** (17): Describe the strengths identified during the audit.
* **Weaknesses Identified** (18): Describe the weaknesses identified during the audit.
* **Management Response** (19): If applicable, provide a summary of the management response.
* **Action Items** (20): List key action items from the review.
* **Follow-Up Required** (21): Select whether follow-up is needed and if so, within which timeframe.
* **Supporting Documents** (22): List any supporting documents.
* **Reviewer Signature** (23): Type your full name as an electronic signature.

Note: Fields marked as required must be filled out accurately and completely.
