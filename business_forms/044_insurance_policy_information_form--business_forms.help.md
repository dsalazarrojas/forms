# Insurance Policy Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document essential information about an insurance policy. It's necessary for maintaining accurate records and ensuring that policy holders are contacted and updated correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Ensure that you enter the correct policy number, holder's name, and coverage start and end dates.
3. Choose the correct coverage status from the options provided.
4. If available, provide the policy holder's address, phone number, and email for future contact.
5. Add any additional notes about the policy holder or policy in the notes field.
6. Once you've completed all fields, click "Form End" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **policy_number** (Text, Required): Enter the unique number assigned to the insurance policy.
* **policy_type** (Text, Optional): If known, provide the type of insurance policy (e.g., health, life, auto).
* **policy_holder_name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the policy holder.
* **coverage_start_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date when the policy coverage started.
* **coverage_end_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date when the policy coverage ends.
* **coverage_status** (Select One, Required): Choose the current status of the policy (Active, Inactive, Canceled, or Expired).
* **policy_holder_address** (Text, Optional): If available, enter the policy holder's address for future contact.
* **policy_holder_phone** (Text, Optional): If available, enter the policy holder's phone number for future contact.
* **policy_holder_email** (Email, Optional): If available, enter the policy holder's email for future contact.
* **policy_holder_notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes about the policy holder or policy.
* **form_end** (Text, Optional): Once you've completed all fields, click this button to submit the form.
