# Employer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employer Survey form is a data collection tool designed to gather information about your company and job postings. This form is used by our organization to better understand your business needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are an authorized representative of your company.
2. Review the form carefully to understand the fields and their corresponding options.
3. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
4. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's official name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your company's email address, if applicable.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number, if applicable.
* **Working Experience** (`experience`, select_one, optional): Select the amount of working experience your company has:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ More than 10 years
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the official job title of the position being posted.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the job duties and responsibilities.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable job types:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Freelance
	+ Intern
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours the job requires per week.
* **Salary** (`salary`, number, optional): Enter the salary range for the position.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of your company:
	+ 1-10 people
	+ 11-50 people
	+ 51-200 people
	+ More than 200 people
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select the job level:
	+ Entry-level
	+ Mid-level
	+ Senior-level
	+ Executive
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select the job type:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Freelance
	+ Intern
* **Date Employed** (`date_employed`, date, optional): Enter the date you were employed.
* **Date Hired** (`date_hired`, date, optional): Enter the date you were hired.

Please fill out this form accurately and completely, and do not hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
