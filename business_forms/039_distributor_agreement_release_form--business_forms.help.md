# Distributor Agreement Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distributor Agreement Release Form is used to track and manage the release status of business agreements between the company and its distributors. This form ensures that all necessary information is captured correctly for efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Agreement ID" field with the unique identifier assigned to this agreement.
2. Enter the "Distributor Name" as the name of the distributor associated with this agreement.
3. Select the "Release Date" as the date of the release.
4. Provide a brief reason for the release in the "Release Reason" field, if required.
5. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field, if needed.
6. Enter the distributor's contact information (email and phone number) in the respective fields.
7. Select the person "Assigned To" from the provided list.
8. Enter the "Assigned Date" for the agreement.
9. Choose the "Release Status" as either "Approved" or "Not Approved".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agreement ID** (`agreement_id`, number, required): A unique identifier for this agreement.
* **Distributor Name** (`distributor_name`, text, required): The name of the distributor associated with this agreement.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required): The date of the release.
* **Release Reason** (`release_reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief reason for the release, if required.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for this agreement.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): The distributor's contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): The distributor's contact phone number.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select the person assigned to handle this agreement.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, required): Enter the assigned date for this agreement.
* **Release Status** (`release_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the release status as either "Approved" or "Not Approved".

## Tips
- Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in before proceeding.
- Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.
- If unsure about any field, contact the assigned person for assistance.
