# Business Setup Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to guide you through the essential steps of setting up your business, covering key aspects such as business information, legal and financial setup, operational processes, marketing and branding, staffing and HR, and overall status.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please answer each field based on the current status of your business setup. If a task is not applicable, select "Not started" and continue to the next field.
* Fill in your business details in the corresponding fields.
* Select the correct status for each field based on your current progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter your business name.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the business owner.
* **Planned Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date you plan to launch your business.
* **Business Structure Selected** (`business_structure_selected`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of business structure you have chosen for your business (e.g., sole prop, LLC, etc.).
* **Business License Obtained** (`business_license_obtained`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your business license (e.g., local or state business license).
* **EIN (Employer ID Number) Applied** (`ein_applied`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your EIN application (e.g., not started, in progress, completed).
* **DBA (Doing Business As) Registered** (`dba_registered`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your DBA registration (e.g., not needed, not started, in progress, completed).
* **Business Bank Account Opened** (`business_bank_account`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your business bank account (e.g., not started, in progress, completed).
* **Accounting System Setup** (`accounting_system`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your accounting system setup (e.g., not started, in progress, completed).
* **Business Insurance Obtained** (`business_insurance`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your business insurance (e.g., liability, property, etc.).
* **Business Location Secured** (`location_secured`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your business location (e.g., office, storefront, home base).
* **Essential Equipment Purchased** (`equipment_purchased`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your essential equipment purchases (e.g., not started, in progress, completed).
* **Software and Systems Set Up** (`software_systems`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your software and systems setup (e.g., not started, in progress, completed).
* **Branding Developed** (`branding_developed`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your branding development (e.g., not started, in progress, completed).
* **Website Created** (`website_created`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your business website (e.g., not needed, not started, in progress, completed).
* **Social Media Accounts Set Up** (`social_media_setup`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your social media accounts (e.g., not needed, not started, in progress, completed).
* **Payroll System Set Up** (`payroll_system`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your payroll system setup (e.g., not needed, not started, in progress, completed).
* **HR Policies Developed** (`hr_policies`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your HR policies development (e.g., not needed, not started, in progress, completed).
* **Overall Setup Status** (`overall_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your overall business setup (e.g., just starting, 25% complete, 50% complete, 75% complete, ready to launch).
