# Facility Efficiency Analysis Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Efficiency Analysis Report Form is designed to collect data for the analysis and improvement of facilities within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each facility to get a comprehensive view of its efficiency.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization**: The name of the facility's organization.
* **Energy Usage**: The average energy usage (in units) for the facility.
* **Maintenance Frequency**: The frequency of maintenance (Low, Medium, High) performed on the facility.
* **Space Utilization**: The percentage of space utilized (0-100) for the facility.
* **Assigned Tool**: The tool or equipment assigned to the facility.
* **Assigned User**: The user assigned to the facility.
* **Assigned Date**: The date the form was assigned to the facility.

## Tips
1. Make sure to fill out all required fields correctly.
2. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to this help guide for clarification.
