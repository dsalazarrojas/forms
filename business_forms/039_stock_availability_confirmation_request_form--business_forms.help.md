# Stock Availability Confirmation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Availability Confirmation Request Form is used to request confirmation of stock availability from suppliers. It helps ensure that the business has accurate information about stock levels of their products and can make informed decisions about orders and stock management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your business name
* Enter the stock level of the product
* Enter a 5-digit product code
* Enter the supplier's name
* Enter the supplier's email address
* Enter the supplier's phone number
* Enter the order date (in date format, e.g., MM/DD/YY)
* Confirm that you have received this request
* Optionally, add any additional notes about the request

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (required): Enter the name of your business as it appears on your business card or invoice.
* **Stock Level** (required): Enter the current stock level of the product, using numbers only (e.g., 10, 20, 30).
* **Product Code** (required): Enter a 5-digit product code for the product you are requesting confirmation on.
* **Supplier Name** (required): Enter the name of the supplier you are requesting confirmation from.
* **Supplier Email** (required): Enter the email address of the supplier's contact person.
* **Supplier Phone** (required): Enter the phone number of the supplier's contact person.
* **Order Date** (required): Enter the date of the order or request, in date format (e.g., MM/DD/YY).
* **Confirm** (required): Confirm that you have received this request.
* **Notes** (optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the request.
