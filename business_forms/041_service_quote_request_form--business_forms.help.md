# service_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "service_quote_request_form" is a form used to request quotes for services from a business. It is used to gather information from the customer about their service needs and preferences. This form is designed to help businesses provide accurate quotes for services by collecting relevant details about the customer's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Enter the company name in the "business_name" field.
	* Select "Yes" or "No" for "service_requests".
	* Select "Yes" or "No" for "requested_services".
	* Enter your estimated time in the "estimated_time" field.
	* Enter your estimated budget in the "estimated_budget" field.
2. Provide any additional comments in the "additional_comments" field if needed.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Quote Form**: This is the main section of the form that collects information about your service requests.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Service Requests**: Select "Yes" if you need assistance with a service, otherwise select "No".
* **Requested Services**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in receiving services, otherwise select "No".
* **Estimated Time**: Estimate the time required for the service.
* **Estimated Budget**: Enter an estimated budget for the service.
* **Service Type**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in specific service types, otherwise select "No".
* **Additional Comments**: Leave any additional comments about your service request.
