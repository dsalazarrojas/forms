# Fuel Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fuel Delivery Form is used to collect delivery information from customers for fuel services. This form ensures that customers can provide their delivery details accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your customer information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide your delivery address and the date and time you'd like the fuel to be delivered.
3. Specify whether fuel is required or not for your vehicle.
4. Add any special delivery instructions or comments.
5. Enter the quantity of fuel you need to be delivered.
6. Leave any additional comments or notes about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name, as the customer who is requesting the fuel delivery.
- **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required: false): Enter the exact address where you'd like the fuel to be delivered.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date when you'd like the fuel to be delivered.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): Select the time when you'd like the fuel to be delivered.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Verify that the name provided matches the name on the address.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address for confirmation and future communication.
- **Fuel Type** (`fuel_type`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you need fuel for your vehicle, or 'No' otherwise.
- **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, note, required: false): Add any special instructions or messages for the delivery team.
- **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of fuel you need to be delivered.
- **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, required: false): Leave any additional comments or notes about your order.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
* Ensure that the delivery address is correct and up to date.
* Keep the delivery instructions clear and concise.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the fuel delivery team.
