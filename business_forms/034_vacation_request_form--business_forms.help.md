# Vacation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vacation Request Form is a tool for employees to submit their vacation requests to their managers for approval. The form captures essential information about the employee's request, including their name, ID, dates of absence, duration, reason, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name and ID in the respective fields.
2. Provide the start and end dates of your absence.
3. Specify the duration of your vacation.
4. Enter a brief reason for your vacation.
5. Leave a note for your manager if needed.
6. Select the status of your request (Active or Inactive).
7. Update the status of your request as needed.
8. Enter your manager's name and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, Optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Number, Optional): Enter your employee ID number as issued by the company.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the start date of your absence in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the end date of your absence in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Duration** (`duration`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of days or weeks you would like to take off.
* **Reason** (`reason`, Text, Optional): Briefly explain the purpose of your vacation.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, Note, Optional): Leave a note for your manager if needed.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, Select One, Optional): Select Active or Inactive to indicate the status of your request.
* **Status Update** (`status_update`, Text, Optional): Enter an update on the status of your request.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your manager's email address. 

Please note that some of these fields are not required to be filled but it is recommended that they be filled to ensure a smooth and efficient processing process.
