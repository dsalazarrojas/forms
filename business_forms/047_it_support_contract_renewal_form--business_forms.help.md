# IT Support Contract Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Support Contract Renewal Form is used to provide information about your company's current contract with our IT support service and to facilitate the renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely and accurately with your company's information.
2.  Make sure to select the correct contract status, renewal status, and renewal type from the available options.
3.  Enter the correct dates for the contract start date, contract end date, and contract expiration date.
4.  If applicable, enter the renewal reason and renewal notes to help us understand your needs.
5.  Finally, review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
