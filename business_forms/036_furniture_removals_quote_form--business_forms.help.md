# Furniture Removals Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a quote for furniture removal services. Please provide the required information to ensure that our team can give you an accurate estimate for your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the **Home Address** field with the address where the furniture will be removed from.
2.  Enter your **Contact Name** and **Contact Email** so our team can contact you with any updates or questions.
3.  Provide your **Contact Phone** number for us to contact you if needed.
4.  Select the type of **Job Type** from the dropdown menu (House, Apartment, Flat, or Office).
5.  Enter the desired **Pickup Date** and **Pickup Time** for when you would like the furniture to be removed.
6.  Enter the desired **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time** for when you would like the furniture delivered.
7.  Specify the **Removals Location** where the furniture will be delivered to.
8.  Estimate the **Removals Size** of the furniture to be removed.
9.  Provide any **Quote Details** about your specific needs.
10. Enter the **Quote Price** if you know of any additional costs or fees associated with the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Home Address**: Please enter the address where the furniture will be removed from.
*   **Contact Name**: Enter your name and contact information so our team can reach out to you.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you with any updates or questions.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
*   **Job Type**: Select the type of location where the furniture will be removed from (House, Apartment, Flat, or Office).
*   **Pickup Date**: Enter the date on which you would like to have your furniture removed.
*   **Pickup Time**: Enter the time of day on which you would like to have your furniture removed.
*   **Delivery Date**: Enter the date on which you would like to have your furniture delivered.
*   **Delivery Time**: Enter the time of day on which you would like to have your furniture delivered.
*   **Removals Location**: Specify the location where the furniture will be delivered to.
*   **Removals Size**: Estimate the amount of furniture to be removed.
*   **Quote Details**: Provide any additional information about your quote request.
*   **Quote Price**: If you know of any additional costs or fees associated with the service, enter them here.
