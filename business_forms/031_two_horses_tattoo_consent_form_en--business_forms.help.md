# Two Horses Tattoo Consent Form (EN) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Two Horses Tattoo Consent Form is a document that confirms a client's consent for a tattoo to be administered. This form is used to gather essential information from the client, including their name, contact details, and consent for the tattoo.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. Please use the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY or MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Describe the tattoo design you'd like to get in the "Tattoo Design" field.
7. Indicate the tattoo artist who will administer the tattoo in the "Tattoo Artist" field.
8. Select one of the options in the "Consent" field, which confirms that you've read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the tattoo.
9. Sign and date the "Client Signature" field to confirm that you've read and agreed to the form.
10. Sign and date the "Artist Signature" field to confirm that the artist has read and agreed to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name in this field. This will help us identify you correctly.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name in this field. This will help us identify you correctly.
* **Date of Birth (Date)**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY or MM/DD/YYYY). This will help us confirm your age and ensure that you're eligible for a tattoo.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address in this field. This will help us contact you about your appointment and any changes to your tattoo.
* **Phone Number (Text)**: Enter your phone number in this field. This will help us contact you about your appointment and any changes to your tattoo.
* **Tattoo Artist (Text)**: Indicate the artist who will administer your tattoo. Please ensure you've agreed on the design with your chosen artist.
* **Tattoo Design (Text)**: Describe the design you'd like to get tattooed in this field. This will help us plan and prepare your tattoo.
* **Consent (Select One)**: Select one of the options in this field to confirm that you've read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the tattoo.
	+ **Client**: I, the client, have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the tattoo.
	+ **Artist**: I, the artist, have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the tattoo.
	+ **Manager**: I, the manager, have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the tattoo.
* **Client Signature (Note)**: Sign and date this field to confirm that you've read and agreed to the form.
* **Artist Signature (Note)**: Sign and date this field to confirm that the artist has read and agreed to the form.
