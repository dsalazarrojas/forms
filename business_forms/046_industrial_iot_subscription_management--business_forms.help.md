# Industrial IoT Subscription Management - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers who wish to subscribe to Industrial IoT services. It helps manage customer subscriptions and provides a platform for users to specify their plan preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Request Form** field with a brief message or description of your subscription request.
2. Enter your **Customer Details** in the subsequent field, providing any relevant information about yourself or your organization.
3. Select your preferred **Plan Selection** from the available options (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request Form** (`request_form`, text, optional): A short description of your subscription request.
- **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about yourself or your organization.
- **Plan Selection** (`plan_selection`, text, optional): Choose your preferred subscription plan from the available options (if any).

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately to avoid any issues with your subscription request.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact our support team for assistance.
- This form is designed to be user-friendly, but if you have any accessibility requirements, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for accommodations.
- You can modify the form fields as needed, but ensure that you're not duplicating any existing information.
