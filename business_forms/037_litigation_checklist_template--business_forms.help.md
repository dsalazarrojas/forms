# Litigation Checklist Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This template is designed to help users create a comprehensive checklist for a litigation matter. It includes fields to collect details on the parties involved, case information, legal representation, and financial data. This form is intended to aid in organizing and keeping track of various aspects of a litigation case.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To get started, simply fill in the required fields and leave the optional ones blank if not applicable.
2. The form is organized into several sections, each with a specific category of information.
3. Fill in the fields in order, from top to bottom, to ensure accurate and consistent data entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Litigation Checklist Template** (`title`, `text`, required: false): This is the title of the form, which serves as a label and identifier.
* **Party Involvement** (`party_involvement`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the party involved in the litigation.
* **Case Details** (`case_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe the case in as much detail as possible.
* **Legal Representation** (`legal_representation`, `text`, required: false): Enter information on the legal representation involved in the case.
* **Plaintiff Info** (`plaintiff_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter information on the plaintiff, including name, address, and contact number.
* **Defendant Info** (`defendant_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter information on the defendant, including name, address, and contact number.
* **Plaintiff Party Name** (`plaintiff_party_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the party filing the lawsuit.
* **Defendant Party Name** (`defendant_party_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the opposing party.
* **Litigation Details** (`litigation_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe the litigation matter in detail.
* **Court Location** (`court_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the court where the case will be heard.
* **Litigation Status** (`litigation_status`, `text`, required: false): Indicate the current status of the case.
* **Plaintiff Address** (`plaintiff_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the plaintiff.
* **Defendant Address** (`defendant_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the defendant.
* **Plaintiff Contact Number** (`plaintiff_contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number of the plaintiff.
* **Defendant Contact Number** (`defendant_contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number of the defendant.
* **Plaintiff Attorney** (`plaintiff_attorney`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the plaintiff's attorney.
* **Defendant Attorney** (`defendant_attorney`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the defendant's attorney.
* **Case Start Date** (`case_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the case started.
* **Case End Date** (`case_end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the case ended.
* **Plaintiff Side** (`plaintiff_side`, `text`, required: false): Indicate which side of the case the plaintiff is on.
* **Defendant Side** (`defendant_side`, `text`, required: false): Indicate which side of the case the defendant is on.
* **Plaintiff Attorney Fees** (`plaintiff_attorney_fees`, `text`, required: false): Enter the fees charged by the plaintiff's attorney.
* **Defendant Attorney Fees** (`defendant_attorney_fees`, `text`, required: false): Enter the fees charged by the defendant's attorney.
* **Total Litigation Cost** (`total_litigation_cost`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total cost of the litigation.
* **Court Costs** (`court_costs`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional costs associated with the court.
