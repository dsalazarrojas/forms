# Omnichannel Retail Grant Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help retailers apply for grants related to their projects, providing a structured and organized way to submit their proposals, contact information, funding requests, supporting documents, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2.  Select the relevant options for each field as prompted.
3.  Fill in any required fields with accurate and complete information.
4.  Ensure you have all necessary supporting documents attached or linked as requested.
5.  Read and agree to the consent terms, indicating your permission for grant data collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Retailer Project Proposals
*   **Retailer Project Proposals** (`retailer_project_proposals`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the proposals that apply to your project. This field allows you to choose multiple options that best represent your project's goals and objectives.

### Retailer Contact Info
*   **Retailer Contact Info** (`retailer_contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and any other relevant contact details. This field is used to ensure we can get in touch with you for any further information or follow-up questions.

### Funding Requests
*   **Funding Requests** (`funding_requests`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you are requesting funding for your project. This field helps us understand your project's budget and financial requirements.

### Supporting Documents
*   **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, required: false): Attach or link any relevant documents that support your project proposal, such as contracts, invoices, or certificates. This field is essential for a comprehensive review of your project.

### Consent
*   **Consent** (`consent`, `note`, required: false): Read and agree to the consent terms, indicating your permission for grant data collection. This is a required field, so please take a moment to understand and agree to the terms.

### Project Location
*   **Project Location** (`project_location`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the location where your project will be conducted. This field helps us understand the geographical scope of your project.

### Project Start Date
*   **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the expected start date of your project. This field is used to schedule and plan our review process.

## Tips

- Make sure to answer all required fields accurately and completely.
- Attach all necessary supporting documents.
- Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact us for assistance.
