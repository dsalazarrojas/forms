# Meeting Summary Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you efficiently capture and summarize meeting information, ensuring that essential details are documented and tracked.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the meeting for which you'd like to create a summary report.
2. Provide a brief summary of the meeting discussion and key outcomes.
3. Indicate if there were any action items, follow-up tasks, or discussion points that arose during the meeting.
4. Note any relevant meeting details, such as the attendees, meeting schedule, or next steps.
5. Review and edit your summary as needed to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Summary** (`meeting_summary_report_form`, text, optional): Briefly describe the main discussion, outcomes, and key takeaways from the meeting.
	+ This field should capture the essence of what was discussed and decided during the meeting.
	+ Keep the summary concise and focused on the essential points.
* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, note, optional): Include any relevant meeting details, such as attendees, meeting schedule, or next steps.
	+ Be as specific as possible while keeping the information brief.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, select multiple, optional): Indicate if there were any action items or follow-up tasks arising from the meeting.
	+ Choose 'Yes' if action items were assigned or agreed upon.
* **Discussion Points** (`discussion_points`, note, optional): Note any notable discussion points or topics that arose during the meeting.
	+ Keep this field for future reference and follow-up.
* **Meeting Outcomes** (`meeting_outcomes`, select multiple, optional): Indicate the outcomes of the meeting, such as decisions made or next steps agreed upon.
	+ Choose 'Yes' for each outcome that applied.
* **Follow-up Tasks** (`follow_up_tasks`, select multiple, optional): Identify any follow-up tasks that were assigned or agreed upon during the meeting.
	+ Choose 'Yes' for each task that applies.
* **Meeting Schedule** (`meeting_schedule`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the meeting.
	+ Ensure the date and time are within the past 7 days for accurate meeting summary reports.
