# Sustainable Partnership Innovation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Partnership Innovation Application Form is a tool used to gather information about innovative projects and partnerships within the organization. This form helps to track and manage project details, partner interactions, and funding status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields below and fill in the required information for each field.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
3. Double-check that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
4. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (Text, Optional): This field is for entering the title of the project you want to submit for review.
* **Organization Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the organization associated with this project.
* **Project Description** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the project.
* **Partner Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the partner involved in the project.
* **Partner Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the partner's email address.
* **Project Start Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the project.
* **Project End Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the project.
* **Funding Status** (Select One, Optional): Select the funding status of the project (Active or Inactive).
* **Project Location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of the project.
* **Project Tags** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant tags or keywords associated with the project.
* **Partner Contact Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the partner's contact number.
* **Partner Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the partner's phone number.
* **Partner Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the partner's address.
* **Project URL** (Text, Optional): Enter the URL of the project.
* **Partner URL** (Text, Optional): Enter the partner's URL.
* **Project Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the project.
* **Form Category** (Text, Optional): Select the category of the form (e.g., "Innovation", "Partnership", etc.).
* **Organization Type** (Text, Optional): Enter the type of organization.
* **Partner Type** (Text, Optional): Enter the type of partner.
* **Partner Level** (Text, Optional): Enter the level of partner engagement.
* **Project Budget** (Number, Optional): Enter the budget for the project.
* **Partner Budget** (Number, Optional): Enter the partner's budget.
* **Funding Source** (Text, Optional): Enter the source of funding.
* **Funding Amount** (Text, Optional): Enter the amount of funding.
* **Submit** (Select One, Optional): Select "Yes" to submit the form or "No" if not ready to submit.

Note: All fields marked as "Optional" are not required to be filled, but it's highly recommended to fill them for a complete and accurate application.
