# Travel Reimbursement Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request reimbursement for business travel expenses incurred by employees. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth reimbursement process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the start and end dates of the travel.
2. Enter your name and title.
3. Specify the purpose of your travel.
4. Provide the location of your travel.
5. Enter the reimbursement amount.
6. Choose the method of reimbursement (if applicable).
7. Confirm that you have submitted this form.
8. Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Date** (travel_date): Enter the date you started and ended your travel. This field is used to determine the duration of your travel.
* **Employee Name** (employee_name): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Travel Purpose** (travel_purpose): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the travel was for business purposes. This field helps us determine the validity of the reimbursement request.
* **Travel Start Date** (travel_start_date): Enter the date you started your travel.
* **Travel End Date** (travel_end_date): Enter the date you ended your travel.
* **Travel Location** (travel_location): Enter the location of your travel.
* **Travel Description** (travel_description): Briefly describe your travel.
* **Reimbursement Amount** (reimbursement_amount): Enter the total amount of reimbursement you are seeking.
* **Reimbursement Method** (reimbursement_method): Choose 'Yes' if you are seeking reimbursement by a specific method (e.g., check, credit card).
* **Submitted By** (submitted_by): Select 'Yes' to confirm that you are submitting this form.
* **Submitted By Email** (submitted_by_email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Submitted By Phone** (submitted_by_phone): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Travel Date 2** (travel_date_2): Enter the date you will be returning from your travel (if different from the travel end date).
* **Travel Type** (travel_type): Enter the type of travel (e.g., business, personal).
* **Travel Category** (travel_category): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the travel was for business purposes.
* **Business Purpose** (business_purpose): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the travel was for business purposes.
* **Travel Expense** (travel_expense): Briefly describe any travel expenses.
* **Travel Expense Type** (travel_expense_type): Enter the type of travel expenses (e.g., meals, lodging).
* **Travel Expense Amount** (travel_expense_amount): Enter the total amount of travel expenses.
* **Reimbursement Status** (reimbursement_status): Select 'Active' to indicate that the reimbursement is active.
* **Reimbursement Date** (reimbursement_date): Enter the date of the reimbursement.
* **Submitted By Name** (submitted_by_name): Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Submitted By Title** (submitted_by_title): Enter your job title for identification purposes.
* **Submitted By Department** (submitted_by_department): Enter your department for identification purposes.
* **Submitted By Job Title** (submitted_by_jobtitle): Enter your job title for identification purposes.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any errors or delays in the reimbursement process.
* If you have any travel expenses, attach receipts or invoices to support your claim.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR department.
