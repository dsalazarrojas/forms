# Garage Door Opener Installation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers for garage door opener installation surveys. The purpose of this form is to collect customer details, preferences, and requirements for their garage door opener installation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your customer details, including your name and email address.
2. Select the type of garage door you have or want to install.
3. Specify the date and time you would like for the installation to take place.
4. Briefly describe your job requirements and any specific features you are interested in.
5. If applicable, enter your preferred time for the installation.
6. Provide your phone number so we can contact you.
7. Finally, select any additional features you would like to be included with your garage door opener installation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (customer_details, text, required):
	+ Please enter your name and any other relevant customer details.
* **door_type** (door_type, select_one, optional):
	+ Please select the type of garage door you have or want to install.
* **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, optional):
	+ Please enter your customer name (if different from your customer details).
* **Installation Date** (installation_date, date, optional):
	+ Please enter the date you would like for the installation to take place.
* **Start Time** (start_time, time, optional):
	+ Please enter the start time you would like for the installation to take place.
* **Job Description** (job_description, text, optional):
	+ Please briefly describe your job requirements and any specific features you are interested in.
* **Preferred Start Time** (preferred_install_time, text, optional):
	+ If applicable, please enter your preferred start time for the installation.
* **Customer Email** (customer_email, email, optional):
	+ Please enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Customer Phone** (customer_phone, text, optional):
	+ Please enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Door Features** (door_features, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Please select any additional features you would like to be included with your garage door opener installation.
