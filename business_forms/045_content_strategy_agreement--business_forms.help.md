# Content Strategy Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Strategy Agreement form is used to document and formalize the terms and conditions of a content strategy engagement between a client and an agency. This form outlines the scope of work, payment terms, and other key aspects of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client and agency information, including client name, email, and address.
2. Select the agreement type, project scope, and deliverables.
3. Define the content types, target platforms, and content frequency.
4. Specify the agreement duration, start and end dates, monthly fee, and total contract value.
5. Choose the payment terms and additional costs.
6. Determine the revision policy, approval process, and ownership rights.
7. Agree on confidentiality and termination clause.
8. Add any additional terms or conditions.
9. Sign and date the form to finalize the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (1)** (`text`, `required`): Enter the client's full name or company name.
* **Client Email (2)** (`email`, `required`): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone (3)** (`text`, `optional`): Enter the client's contact phone number, if desired.
* **Client Address (4)** (`text`, `required`): Enter the client's mailing address.
* **Agency Name (5)** (`text`, `required`): Enter the agency's name.
* **Agency Email (6)** (`email`, `required`): Enter the agency's email address.
* **Agreement Date (7)** (`date`, `required`): Enter the date of this agreement.
* **Agreement Type (8)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the type of agreement (e.g., Content Strategy Development, Content Marketing Services, etc.).
* **Project Scope (9)** (`text`, `required`): Describe the scope of work in detail.
* **Deliverables (10)** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Select the deliverables (e.g., Content Strategy Document, Content Calendar, etc.).
* **Content Types (11)** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Select the types of content to be created (e.g., Blog Articles, Social Media Posts, etc.).
* **Target Platforms (12)** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Select the target platforms (e.g., Company Website, Facebook, etc.).
* **Content Frequency (13)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the content frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.).
* **Agreement Duration (14)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the agreement duration (e.g., 3 Months, 12 Months, etc.).
* **Start Date (15)** (`date`, `required`): Enter the start date of the agreement.
* **End Date (16)** (`date`, `required`): Enter the end date of the agreement.
* **Monthly Fee (17)** (`number`, `required`): Enter the monthly retainer amount.
* **Total Contract Value (18)** (`number`, `required`): Enter the total value of the agreement.
* **Payment Terms (19)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the payment terms (e.g., Monthly in Advance, Monthly in Arrears, etc.).
* **Additional Costs (20)** (`text`, `optional`): If there are any additional costs, describe them here.
* **Revision Policy (21)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the number of revisions allowed (e.g., 1 Round of Revisions, 3 Rounds of Revisions, etc.).
* **Approval Process (22)** (`text`, `required`): Describe how content will be approved.
* **Ownership Rights (23)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select who owns the content (e.g., Client Owns All Content, Agency Retains Some Rights, etc.).
* **Confidentiality (24)** (`select_one`, `required`): Agree on confidentiality terms.
* **Termination Clause (25)** (`text`, `required`): Describe the conditions for termination.
* **Additional Terms (26)** (`text`, `optional`): Add any additional terms or conditions.
* **Client Signature Date (27)** (`date`, `required`): Sign and date to finalize the agreement for the client.
* **Agency Signature Date (28)** (`date`, `required`): Sign and date to finalize the agreement for the agency.

**Tips**

- Make sure to review and understand each field before completing the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Use the form as a guide to discuss and agree on terms with your client.
- Save a copy of the signed agreement for future reference.
