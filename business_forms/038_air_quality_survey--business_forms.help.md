# Air Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information on perceived air quality, symptoms related to air quality, and the impact of air quality on work performance. It will help identify areas where air quality is affecting workspaces and suggest possible measures to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question based on your real-life experiences with air quality.
2. Be honest and provide accurate information for each question.
3. Use the provided options for `perceived_air_quality`, `reported_symptoms`, and `work_impact` questions, or type in your own answer if it does not fit in the provided options.
4. Use the `contact_email` field if you want to be contacted for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`): Optional field for providing your name for anonymous responses.
* **Workplace location** (`workplace_location`): Enter the name or address of your workplace.
* **Perceived air quality** (`perceived_air_quality`): Rate your perceived air quality on an ordinal scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Reported symptoms** (`reported_symptoms`): Select any symptoms you have experienced due to air quality issues (e.g., Cough, Sore throat, Headache, Eye irritation, None).
* **Impact on work performance** (`work_impact`): Indicate the level of impact air quality has on your work performance (No impact, Mild, Moderate, Severe).
* **Mitigation measures** (`mitigation_measures`): Describe any measures taken or suggested to improve air quality (e.g., ventilation, filters, masks).
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions`): Provide any suggestions you have to improve air quality.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`): Optional field for providing your email address for follow-up.

## Tips
- If you experience air quality issues, please report them as accurately as possible.
- Use the provided options for questions with multiple options, or type in your own answer if it does not fit in the provided options.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [contact email].
