# Customer Sentiment Analysis Pilot Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and sentiment from customers about a pilot application of a product or service. It helps businesses understand their customers' perceptions and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your role in the company from the options provided in question 1.
2. Enter your job title or position in question 2.
3. Select all the products or services that interest you from question 3.
4. Describe your sentiment about the product or service in question 4.
5. Answer questions 5-7 if you have thoughts or comments, but feel free to skip them if you don't have anything to add.
6. Select the primary benefit you hope to gain from our product or service in question 8.
7. Enter your contact information in question 9.
8. Let us know if you are interested in being contacted by our team in question 10.
9. Share how you found out about this pilot program in question 11.
10. If you have anything else to add, please do so in question 12.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **1. What is your role in the company? (Select One)**: This question helps us understand your position within the company to better tailor our response to your specific needs.
- **2. What is your job title or position? (Text)**: Please enter your official job title or position to give us a better understanding of your background.
- **3. What is the primary product or service you are interested in? (Select Multiple)**: This question helps us determine which product or service you are most interested in, allowing us to provide more relevant information.
- **4. What is your sentiment about the product or service? (Text)**: Describe your feelings or opinions about our product or service in as much detail as possible.
- **5. What do you like about the product or service? (Text)**: If you have any positive thoughts or comments about our product or service, please share them here.
- **6. What do you not like about the product or service? (Text)**: Similarly, if you have any negative thoughts or comments, please add them here.
- **7. What do you wish the product or service offered? (Text)**: If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please add them here.
- **8. What is the primary benefit you hope to gain from our product or service? (Select One)**: This helps us understand what you hope to achieve or gain from using our product or service.
- **9. What is your contact info? (Note)**: Please enter your name and email or phone number so we can get back to you.
- **10. Are you interested in being contacted by our team? (Select One)**: Let us know if you are open to our team reaching out to you for further discussion or clarification.
- **11. How did you find out about this pilot program? (Text)**: Share with us how you came across this pilot program.
- **12. Is there anything else you'd like to tell us? (Text)**: If you have any final thoughts or comments, please add them here.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions thoroughly and honestly.
- If you have any issues or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us through the contact information provided.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and provide your feedback!
