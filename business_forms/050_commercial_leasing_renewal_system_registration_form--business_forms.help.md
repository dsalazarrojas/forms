# Commercial Leasing Renewal System Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commercial Leasing Renewal System Registration Form is designed to gather information from business tenants about their current lease and renewal intentions. This form will help us understand your needs and preferences for the renewal process, allowing us to provide you with tailored services and support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, making sure to provide accurate and complete information about your current lease and renewal intentions.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, and do not leave any fields blank.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or contact us for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Name**: Please enter your company's legal business name.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for your company.
* **Contact Title**: Describe the position held by the primary contact person.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Current Property Address**: Enter the address of the currently leased space.
* **Suite Number**: Enter the suite or unit number of the leased space.
* **Current Landlord**: Enter the name of the current landlord or management company.
* **Current Lease Start Date**: Enter the date when the current lease began.
* **Current Lease End Date**: Enter the date when the current lease expires.
* **Current Monthly Rent**: Enter the current monthly rent amount.
* **Current Square Footage**: Enter the size of the leased space in square feet.
* **Interest in Renewal**: Choose one of the options to indicate your interest in renewing your lease.
* **Preferred Renewal Term**: Choose the preferred length of the new lease term.
* **Desired Move-in Date**: Enter the date when you would like the new lease to begin. (Optional)
* **Space Size Change**: Choose one of the options to indicate if you need to change the space size.
* **Additional Space Needed**: If you need more space, enter the amount in square feet.
* **Location Preference**: Choose one of the options to indicate your location preference for the new lease.
* **Required Amenities**: Choose all the amenities you require for the new lease.
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any accessibility requirements, enter a brief description.
* **Budget Range**: Choose one of the options to indicate your budget range for the new lease.
* **Tenant Improvements Needed**: Choose one of the options to indicate if you need any tenant improvements.
* **Decision Timeline**: Choose one of the options to indicate your decision timeline for renewing the lease.
* **Current Space Satisfaction**: Choose one of the options to indicate your level of satisfaction with your current space.
* **Reason for Considering Change**: If you are considering a change, enter a brief description of your reason.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information about your renewal needs and preferences.
* **Current Rent Budget**: If you are interested in staying within the same budget, choose the option "Same as Current".
* **Decision Timeline**: Choose one of the options to indicate how quickly you would like to make a decision about your lease.
