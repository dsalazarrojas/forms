# digital_production_coordinator_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The digital production coordinator contact form is used to gather contact information and project details from customers or stakeholders for internal coordination and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Contact Us" button to access the form.
2. Fill out your contact and project details, including full name, email, phone number, project title, project type, and any other relevant information.
3. If necessary, select the required fields for project deadline, project start and end dates, and project status.
4. Use the "How Can We Help?" field to specify the type of assistance needed.
5. Upload any relevant files using the "File Upload" field.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" and "Project Notes" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Us** (Contact Form): This is the main contact form section where you can enter your name and other contact information.
* **Full Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name to be contacted by our team.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for internal communication purposes.
* **Email (2)** (`email`, email, required: false): Please note that this field is a duplicate of the `contact_email` field. If you fill this field out, it will not affect your contact information but you may receive duplicate emails.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to be contacted by our team.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the project for our internal reference.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, required: false): Specify the type of project you have. Please choose one of the provided options.
* **Production Company** (`production_company`, text, required: false): If you represent a production company, enter the name of your company.
* **Message** (`contact_message`, text, required: false): Enter a message to our team to describe your query.
* **How Can We Help?** (`contact_request`, select_multiple, required: false): Specify the type of assistance needed by selecting multiple options.
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, date, required: false): Enter the expected project deadline.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the project start date.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the project end date.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required: false): Specify the project status. Please choose one of the provided options.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, required: false): Enter the project budget.
* **Time Zone** (`contact_request_time`, time, required: false): Enter your time zone for our team to coordinate with you.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, required: false): Upload relevant files for our team to review.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, note, required: false): Enter project-specific notes.
* **Additional Notes** (`contact_request_note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Contact Request Email** (`contact_request_email`, email, required: false): Enter a contact email for our team to reach out to you.
* **Phone** (`contact_request_phone`, text, required: false): Enter a phone number for our team to contact you.
* **Address** (`contact_request_address`, text, required: false): Enter your address for our team to contact you.
* **Contact Request Message** (`contact_request_message`, text, required: false): Enter a message to our team to describe your query.
* **Additional Comments** (`contact_request_message_2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes.
