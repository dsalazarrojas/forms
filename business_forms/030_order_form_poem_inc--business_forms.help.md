# Order Form Poem Inc - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their orders, including delivery dates and times, customer contact information, and product details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Enter your delivery date and time in the "delivery date" and "delivery time" fields.
3. Enter your customer name, email, and phone number in the "customer name", "customer email", and "customer phone" fields, respectively.
4. Enter the name and description of the product you are ordering in the "product name" and "product description" fields.
5. Add any comments about your order in the "order comments" field.
6. Add any delivery notes in the "delivery notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form** (order_form_poem_inc, text, optional): This field is the main section of the form where you will enter your order details.
* **Delivery Date** (delivery_date, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time** (delivery_time, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email** (customer_email, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Customer Phone** (customer_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Product Name** (product_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the product you are ordering.
* **Product Description** (product_description, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the product you are ordering.
* **Order Comments** (order_comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your order.
* **Delivery Notes** (delivery_notes, text, optional): Add any notes about your delivery preferences.
