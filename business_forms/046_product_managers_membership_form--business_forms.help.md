# product_managers_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about product managers who are interested in joining a membership program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field and should be a valid email address.
4. If you are not a product manager, enter your Job Title in the "Job Title" field. If you are a product manager, leave this field blank.
5. Enter your department in the "Department" field.
6. Enter your Company in the "Company" field.
7. Check the "Role" field to select your role within the company.
8. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Check the "Team Members" field to indicate if you are a team member. If you are a team member, select "Yes".
10. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Team Member 1" and "Team Member 2".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as you would like it to appear on our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to appear on our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department if you have one.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title if not a product manager.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Select your role within the company.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Other** (`job_title_other`, text, optional): Enter your job title if it's not a product manager.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select multiple, optional): Check if you are a team member.
* **Team Member 1** (`team_member_one`, select one, optional): Check if you are a team member 1.
* **Team Member 2** (`team_member_two`, select one, optional): Check if you are a team member 2.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly, as it will be used for communication.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* We will review your submission and follow up with you if needed.
