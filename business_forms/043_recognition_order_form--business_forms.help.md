# Recognition Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recognition Order Form is used to submit a request for a recognition order, such as an award or gift, to acknowledge and show appreciation for an individual's achievements or contributions to the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of award or gift you would like to order (Award, Certificate, Gift, or Other).
2. Briefly describe the item you would like to order in the item description field.
3. Enter the quantity of the item you would like to order.
4. Select the date of the event or occasion that the recognition is for.
5. Enter the date the recognition will be given.
6. Enter the name of the recipient of the recognition.
7. Enter the email address of the recipient.
8. Select the department of the recipient (if applicable).
9. Select whether manager approval is required.
10. If manager approval is required, list any necessary manager approvals.
11. Enter the date the order is to be completed.
12. Enter your name and email address as the submitter.
13. Enter your department and job title as the submitter.
14. Enter your work phone number and work location as the submitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Award Type** (`award_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of award or gift you would like to order (Award, Certificate, Gift, or Other).
* **Item Description** (`item_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the item you would like to order in 2 sentences or less.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the item you would like to order.
* **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, date, optional): Select the date of the event or occasion that the recognition is for.
* **Date of Recognition** (`date_of_recognition`, date, optional): Enter the date the recognition will be given.
* **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the recipient.
* **Recipient Email** (`recipient_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the recipient.
* **Recipient Department** (`recipient_department`, select_one, optional): Select the department of the recipient (if applicable).
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, optional): Select whether manager approval is required.
* **Manager Approvals** (`manager_approvals`, select_multiple, optional): If manager approval is required, list any necessary manager approvals.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the order is to be completed.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the submitter.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, optional): Enter your department as the submitter.
* **Submitter Title** (`submitter_title`, text, optional): Enter your title as the submitter.
* **Submitter Job Title** (`submitter_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title as the submitter.
* **Submitter Work Email** (`submitter_work_email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Work Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number as the submitter.
* **Submitter Work Department** (`submitter_work_department`, text, optional): Enter your work department as the submitter.
* **Submitter Work Location** (`submitter_work_location`, text, optional): Enter your work location as the submitter.
* **Submitter Work Title** (`submitter_work_title`, text, optional): Enter your work title as the submitter.
