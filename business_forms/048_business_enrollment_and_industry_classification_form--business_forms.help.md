# Business Enrollment And Industry Classification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Enrollment And Industry Classification Form is used to provide information about your business operations and classification for administrative and regulatory purposes. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that there are no errors or discrepancies.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Enter your business's legal name.
* **Doing Business As (DBA)**: If your business has a different name from its legal name, enter it here.
* **Business Type**: Select the structure of your business from the provided options (Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, S-Corporation, Non-Profit, Government Agency, Other).
* **Federal Tax ID (EIN)**: Enter your Employer Identification Number.
* **Business Address**: Enter your business's street address.
* **City**: Enter your business's city location.
* **State/Province**: Enter your business's state or province.
* **ZIP/Postal Code**: Enter your business's postal code.
* **Country**: Enter the country where your business operates.
* **Owner Name**: Enter the full name of the owner.
* **Owner Social Security Number**: Enter the SSN of the owner for identification purposes.
* **Owner Address**: Enter the owner's residential address.
* **Owner Phone Number**: Enter the owner's contact number.
* **Owner Email**: Enter the owner's email address.
* **Primary Industry Classification**: Select the main industry category of your business from the provided options.
* **NAICS Code**: Enter the North American Industry Classification Code for your business (optional).
* **Business Description**: Provide a detailed description of your business operations.
* **Number of Employees**: Enter the current workforce size of your business.
* **Estimated Annual Revenue**: Select the estimated annual revenue range of your business from the provided options.
* **Business Startup Date**: Enter the date your business was started.
* **Enrollment Confirmation**: Confirm that the provided information is accurate.

Note: Fields marked as required are necessary to complete the form; others can be left blank if not applicable.
