# Retail Partner Exit Notice Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a formal notice of your intention to exit your partnership with our company. This form will help us process your exit smoothly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the date of the notice in the "notice_date" field.
2. Select the type of notice you wish to give in the "notice_type" field.
3. Provide the details of the notice in the "notice_details" field.
4. Fill in the name of your business in the "business_name" field.
5. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
7. Provide the reason for your exit in the "reason_for_exit" field.
8. Enter your partner ID in the "partner_id" field.
9. Fill in the name of your store in the "store_name" field.
10. Enter the name of your partner in the "partner_name" field.
11. Enter the address of your store in the "store_address" field.
12. Enter the date of your exit in the "exit_date" field.
13. Select the status of the notice in the "notice_status" field.
14. Enter your name as the submitter in the "submitter" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Notice**: A field for providing a formal notice of your intention to exit your partnership with our company.
* **Date**: A field for entering the date of the notice.
* **Notice Type**: A field to select the type of notice you wish to give (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Notice Details**: A field for providing the details of the notice.
* **Business Name**: A field for entering the name of your business.
* **Contact Number**: A field for entering your contact number.
* **Email**: A field for entering your email address.
* **Reason for Exit**: A field for providing the reason for your exit.
* **Partner ID**: A field for entering your partner ID.
* **Store Name**: A field for entering the name of your store.
* **Partner Name**: A field for entering the name of your partner.
* **Store Address**: A field for entering the address of your store.
* **Exit Date**: A field for entering the date of your exit.
* **Notice Status**: A field for selecting the status of the notice (e.g., Option 1 or Option 2).
* **Submitter**: A field for entering your name as the submitter of the notice.

Note: This form is for your formal notice of your intention to exit your partnership with our company. Please complete it carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth exit process.
