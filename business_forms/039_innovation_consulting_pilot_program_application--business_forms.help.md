# Innovation Consulting Pilot Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innovation Consulting Pilot Program is a unique opportunity for employees to develop their innovation consulting skills and knowledge. By participating in this program, you will work closely with experienced innovation consultants to enhance your abilities in identifying and solving business challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide a valid date of birth to help us verify your identity.
3. Enter your current address to allow us to contact you for further instructions.
4. Describe your current work challenge, if any, to help us understand your motivation for participating in the program.
5. State your objective, i.e., what you hope to achieve through this program.
6. Select your innovation team from the list provided.
7. Add any additional information that you would like us to consider in the evaluation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_input_1`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`user_input_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`user_input_3`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`user_input_4`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`user_input_5`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth to help us verify your identity.
* **Address** (`user_input_6`, text, required/optional): Enter your current address to allow us to contact you for further instructions.
* **Challenge Description** (`user_input_7`, text, required/optional): Describe your current work challenge, if any, to help us understand your motivation for participating in the program.
* **Objective** (`user_input_8`, text, required/optional): State your objective, i.e., what you hope to achieve through this program.
* **Innovation Team** (`user_input_9`, select_one, required/optional): Select your innovation team from the list provided. This will determine who you will be working with during the program.
* **Additional Information** (`user_input_10`, text, required/optional): Add any additional information that you would like us to consider in the evaluation process.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor/HR representative.
* We will review your application and contact you for further instructions.
