# Driver Scoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Scoring Form is a tool used to collect data on a driver's performance, including scoring, incident details, and comments.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the driver's name and score in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide incident details, including whether the incident occurred on a date or time.
3. Add any necessary comments or feedback for the driver.
4. Choose whether the driver was involved in an incident (Yes or No).
5. Select the status of the vehicle (Active or Inactive).
6. Provide the instructor's name and any comments.
7. Enter the date and time of the incident, if applicable.
8. Report any vehicle damage, if applicable.
9. Choose whether the instructor was present during the incident (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Driver Scoring Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the incident, if applicable.
* **Driver Score**: Enter the driver's score, with higher scores indicating better performance.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or feedback for the driver.
* **Trainee**: Choose whether the driver was involved in an incident (Yes or No).
* **Incident Details**: Select the options to report the details of the incident.
* **Instructor Comments**: Add any comments or feedback from the instructor.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date of the incident, if applicable.
* **Time of Incident**: Enter the time of the incident, if applicable.
* **Vehicle Damage**: Report any damage to the vehicle, with higher numbers indicating more damage.
* **Instructor Name**: Choose whether the instructor was present during the incident (Yes or No).
* **Vehicle Status**: Select the status of the vehicle (Active or Inactive).
