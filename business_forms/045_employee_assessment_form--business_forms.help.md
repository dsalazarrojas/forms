# Employee Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess employees' performance and provide feedback for improvement. It is typically used by HR representatives to collect information about an employee's job responsibilities, performance, and areas for growth. The form is essential for employee development and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information about the employee, such as their company name, role, and employee ID.
2. Select the correct assessment date and time.
3. Provide an evaluation score out of a scale (e.g., 1-5).
4. Review and complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (1)** (company_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company where the employee works.
* **Role (2)** (role, text, required: false): Enter the employee's job title or position.
* **Employee ID (3)** (employee_id, number, required: false): Enter the employee's unique ID number.
* **Assessment Date (4)** (assessment_date, date, required: false): Select the date when the assessment was conducted.
* **Assessment Time (5)** (assessment_time, time, required: false): Select the time when the assessment was conducted.
* **Evaluation Score (6)** (evaluation_score, number, required: false): Enter a score out of the scale to evaluate the employee's performance.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed.
* Use a consistent format for entering dates and times.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to HR for further clarification.
