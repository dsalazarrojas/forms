# Rental Income Analysis Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Income Analysis Report Form is used to collect and analyze rental income data for a property. This form is essential for property managers or financial analysts to assess the financial performance of rental properties.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the rent period, income source, and rent frequency for the property.
2. Enter the rent amount, property type, and lease start and end dates.
3. Provide the property address, renter details, and property value.
4. Select the rent amount currency.
5. Indicate whether the property is insured and whether there are any property management fees.
6. Enter any other income related to the property.
7. Set the tax rate and frequency.
8. Choose the tax rate currency.
9. Add any submission comments or notes.
10. Complete the submission details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rent Period**: Select the period for which you want to analyze the rental income.
* **Income Source**: Choose the source of the rental income.
* **Rent Frequency**: Indicate how often the rent is collected (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, or Semi-Annually).
* **Rent Amount**: Enter the rent amount for the selected rent period.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property (Apartment, Single Family Home, Townhouse, Condo, or Duplex).
* **Lease Start Date**: Enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date**: Enter the end date of the lease.
* **Property Address**: Enter the full address of the property.
* **Renter Details**: Provide any details about the renter(s).
* **Rent Amount Currency**: Select the currency of the rent amount.
* **Property Insurance**: Indicate whether the property is insured.
* **Property Management Fees**: Enter any property management fees.
* **Other Income**: Enter any other income related to the property.
* **Tax Rate**: Enter the tax rate applied to the rent amount.
* **Tax Frequency**: Indicate how often the tax is applied (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, or Semi-Annually).
* **Tax Rate Currency**: Select the currency of the tax rate.
* **Notes**: Add any comments or notes about the submission.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name or email of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted At**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Updated By**: Enter the name or email of the person updating the form.
* **Updated At**: Enter the date the form was updated.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Updated Date**: Enter the date the form was updated.
* **Submission Comments**: Add any comments or notes about the submission.
