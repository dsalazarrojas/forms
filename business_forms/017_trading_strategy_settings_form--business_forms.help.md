# Trading Strategy Settings Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trading Strategy Settings Form is a tool used to collect data from traders regarding their trading strategy, risk tolerance, and other relevant information. This form aims to gather insights into traders' risk management practices and strategies, which can be used to inform and refine trading policies and decisions within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Trader Name** field with your name as it appears on your official records.
2. Select your **Trading Strategy** from the dropdown menu, indicating which strategy you employ for your trades.
3. If applicable, enter your **Risk Tolerance** level, indicating your comfort level with taking risk.
4. Choose the **Time Frame** for your trades from the dropdown menu.
5. Optionally, add any additional **Review Comments** in the text area provided.
6. If necessary, specify the **Review Date** and **Review Time** of your last review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trader Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Trading Strategy**: Select the trading strategy you employ for your trades.
* **Risk Tolerance**: Enter your comfort level with taking risk. Please note that this field is optional and not required.
* **Time Frame**: Choose the time frame for your trades. This could be daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Market Data**: This field is currently not in use and will not affect the functionality of the form.
* **Review Comments**: Add any comments about your trading strategy or practices.
* **Review Date** and **Review Time**: These fields are used to record the date and time of your last review. Please note that these fields are optional.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have reviewed and filled out this form accurately and truthfully.
* Make sure to update your trading strategy and risk tolerance regularly to reflect changes in your trading practices.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please reach out to your supervisor or trading representative.
