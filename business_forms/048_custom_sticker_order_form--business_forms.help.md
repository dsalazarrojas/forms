# custom_sticker_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The custom sticker order form is used to collect information from customers to place a custom sticker order. This form is likely used for sales teams, customer support, or marketing teams to collect necessary details for a successful order placement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your company information (if applicable).
2. Select the desired sticker design from the available options.
3. Choose the type of sticker you need.
4. Enter the number of stickers you require.
5. Enter your shipping details.
6. Provide your contact information (email and phone number).
7. Include any additional notes or comments.
8. If you need custom sticker design, provide a description of your custom design.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_info**: This field is used to provide your company or organization's information, which will be used for communication and order placement purposes. (Text, optional)
* **product_design**: Select the pre-designed sticker you want from the available options to ensure the correct design is ordered.
* **sticker_type**: Choose the type of sticker you require (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **sticker_quantity**: Enter the quantity of stickers you need. This field requires a numerical value.
* **shipping_info**: Provide your shipping address and any special instructions for shipping.
* **email**: Enter your company or organization's email address for communication purposes.
* **phone**: Enter your company or organization's phone number for contact purposes.
* **note**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **business_info**: This field is used to provide more detailed information about your business or organization.
* **custom_design**: If you need a custom design, provide a description of your design requirements.
* **custom_sticker_info**: Describe your custom sticker requirements, including any specific details you need to be included in the sticker.

## Tips
- Ensure you complete the form accurately to ensure the correct sticker order is placed.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you've provided all necessary information.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the form instructions or contact the sales team for assistance.
