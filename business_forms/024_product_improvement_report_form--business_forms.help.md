# Product Improvement Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Product Improvement Report Form is a tool for users to report issues or propose improvements to a product. This form helps the development team understand user needs and prioritize product development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "Required" in the field descriptions below).
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the other fields (optional).
3. Submit your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Product Id (Product Id)
* Product Id (id: 2): Enter the ID of the product you are reporting issues or proposing improvements for.
### 2. Improvement Suggestion (improvement_suggestion)
* Improvement Suggestion (id: 3): Provide a clear and concise description of your improvement suggestion.
### 3. Submit Issue (submit_issue)
* Submit Issue (id: 4): Enter a brief description of the issue you are reporting.
### 4. Product Category (product_category)
* Product Category (id: 5): Choose the category of the product you are reporting issues or proposing improvements for.
### 5. Subcategory (product_subcategory)
* Subcategory (id: 6): Choose the subcategory of the product you are reporting issues or proposing improvements for.
### 6. Issue Description (issue_description)
* Issue Description (id: 7): Provide a detailed description of the issue you are reporting.
### 7. Suggest Improvements (suggest_improvement)
* Suggest Improvements (id: 8): Provide a detailed description of the improvements you are proposing.
### 8. Submitter Name (submitter_name)
* Submitter Name (id: 9): Enter your name as the person submitting this report.
### 9. Submitter Email (submitter_email)
* Submitter Email (id: 10): Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you for follow-up questions or to discuss your report.
### 10. Submitter Phone (submitter_phone)
* Submitter Phone (id: 11): Enter your phone number (optional).
### 11. Issue Type (issue_type)
* Issue Type (id: 12): Choose the type of issue you are reporting (Technical Issue, Product Feature, Service Issue).
### 12. Issue Priority (issue_priority)
* Issue Priority (id: 13): Choose the level of priority for your issue (High, Medium, Low).
### 13. Submit Date (submit_date)
* Submit Date (id: 14): This field will be automatically filled in when the form is submitted.
### 14. Issue Category (issue_category)
* Issue Category (id: 15): Choose the category of the issue you are reporting (Bug, Feature Request, Design Issue, Service Issue).
### 15. Submit Time (submit_time)
* Submit Time (id: 16): This field will be automatically filled in when the form is submitted.
### 16. Submitter IP (submitter_ip)
* Submitter IP (id: 17): This field will be automatically filled in when the form is submitted.
### 17. Submitter Browser (submitter_browser)
* Submitter Browser (id: 18): This field will be automatically filled in when the form is submitted.
### 18. Issue Submitter (issue_submitter)
* Issue Submitter (id: 19): Choose whether you are submitting this issue on your own behalf (Yes, No).
### 19. Product Version (product_version)
* Product Version (id: 20): Enter the version of the product you are reporting issues or proposing improvements for.
### 20. Product Version Type (product_version_type)
* Product Version Type (id: 21): Enter the type of product version you are reporting (e.g., beta, release).
### 21. Comments (submitter_comments)
* Comments (id: 22): Enter any additional comments or information you have about your report.
### 22. Agree (submitter_agree)
* Agree (id: 23): Choose whether you agree to the terms and conditions (Yes, No).
### 23. Other (submitter_agree_other)
* Other (id: 24): If you chose "Yes" for the previous question, enter the reason why you agree (optional).
### 24. Reason (submitter_agree_other_reason)
* Reason (id: 25): Enter the reason for your agreement (optional).

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when describing your issues or proposals.
* If you have additional comments or information, use the "Comments" field.
* If you are reporting an issue, choose the correct "Issue Type" and "Issue Category" to help the development team understand your concern.
* If you are proposing an improvement, provide as much detail as possible about the change you are proposing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the development team.
