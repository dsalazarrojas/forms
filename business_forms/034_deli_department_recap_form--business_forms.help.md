# Deli Department Recap Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Deli Department Recap Form is designed to gather information about employees in the deli department, including their names, contact information, and department affiliations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the text fields with the employee's first and last name.
2. Enter the employee's phone number in the "phone_number" field.
3. Enter the employee's email address in the "email_address" field.
4. Select the department(s) the employee is affiliated with from the "department" and "department_2" fields.
5. Select the department the employee is primarily affiliated with from the "department_3" field.
6. Fill in the "date" field with the start and end date of the employee's shift, including the month and year.
7. Fill in the "time" field with the start and end time of the employee's shift, including AM/PM.
8. Use the note fields ("additional_info", "more_info", "note_2", and "note_3") for any additional comments or notes about the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (text, required: false): Enter the employee's first name.
* **last_name** (text, required: false): Enter the employee's last name.
* **phone_number** (text, required: false): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **email_address** (email, required: false): Enter the employee's email address.
* **department** (select_one, required: false): Select the primary department the employee is affiliated with.
* **date** (date, required: false): Enter the start and end date of the employee's shift.
* **time** (time, required: false): Enter the start and end time of the employee's shift.
* **additional_info** (note, required: false): Use this field for any additional comments or notes about the employee.
* **more_info** (note, required: false): Use this field for any additional comments or notes about the employee.
* **department_2** (select_multiple, required: false): Select any additional departments the employee is affiliated with.
* **department_3** (select_one, required: false): Select the department the employee is primarily affiliated with.
* **note_2** (note, required: false): Use this field for any additional comments or notes about the employee.
* **note_prophets** (note, required: false): Use this field for any additional comments or notes about the employee.
* **department_4** (select_one, required: false): Select the department the employee is affiliated with.
* **department_5** (select_multiple, required: false): Select any additional departments the employee is affiliated with.
* **department_6** (date, required: false): Enter the date the employee's shift starts and ends.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the information is up-to-date and correct.
* Use the note fields for any additional comments or notes about the employee.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor for assistance.
