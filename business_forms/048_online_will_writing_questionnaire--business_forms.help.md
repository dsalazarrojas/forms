# Online Will Writing Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for the creation of an online will, helping individuals to document their estate planning and testamentary wishes. It is recommended to have a clear understanding of the form's purpose and content before completing it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the person's information (First Name, Last Name, Date Of Birth, Address, Phone Number, Email) which will help in establishing the identity of the will maker.
2. If applicable, provide details about the person's family, including whether they have children and their names (Children Names).
3. Identify the assets the person would like to pass down or include in the will, including the type (Cash, Property, Other), and the amount or value.
4. If the person has other beneficiaries in addition to family, select the 'Yes' or 'No' options for Other Beneficiaries.
5. Provide the name and information of the other beneficiaries.
6. Choose the percentage of the asset that the other beneficiary is to receive.
7. Consider charitable giving, selecting 'Yes' or 'No' for Charitable Donor, and if so, specify the amount.
8. Finally, review and confirm all provided information by signing and dating the will.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Person Info**: Enter the person's first and last name, date of birth, address, phone number, and email to identify the will maker.
* **First Name**: Enter the person's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the person's last name.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter the person's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Enter the person's address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the person's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the person’s email address.
* **Family Info**: If the person has family, enter their details here.
* **Children Names**: Select all children's names if the answer to Family Info is 'Yes'.
* **Children Count**: Only visible if the answer to Family Info is 'Yes'.
* **Assets Info**: Describe the assets to be included in the will.
* **Asset Type**: Select the type of asset to be included (Cash, Property, Other).
* **Asset Amount/Value**: Enter the amount or value of the asset.
* **Witness**: Provide the name and information of the witness (if any).
* **Executor**: Provide the name and information of the executor (if any).
* **Testamentary Counsel**: Provide the name and information of the testamentary counsel (if any).
* **Other Beneficiaries**: Select 'Yes' if the person has other beneficiaries, and enter their names and information.
* **Other Beneficiary Info**: Enter the information about the other beneficiaries.
* **Other Beneficiary Percentage**: Enter the percentage that the other beneficiary is to receive.
* **Charitable Donor**: Select 'Yes' if the person would like to be a charitable donor.
* **Charitable Amount**: Enter the amount to be donated.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of signing.
* **Signature**: Enter your signature.

Note: This help guide is for informational purposes only and does not cover all the intricacies and complexities of the will writing process. It's recommended that you consult a legal professional for a complete and accurate understanding of the will making process.
