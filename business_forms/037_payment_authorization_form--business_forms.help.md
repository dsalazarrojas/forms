# Payment Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment-related information for authorization purposes within a company or organization. It helps ensure that payments are properly processed and approved by the correct personnel.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please provide all required information (indicated by a red asterisk *) and follow these steps:
*   Enter the company's name as it should be recorded in our system.
*   Enter the first name and last name of the contact person responsible for this payment.
*   Provide the contact email address for the company or individual.
*   Enter the contact phone number for additional contact information.
*   Enter the total amount of the payment.
*   Choose a date for the payment or authorization.
*   Indicate who authorized the payment or transaction.
*   Enter the date of authorization.
*   Specify the method used for authorization (e.g., verbal, written, etc.).
*   Choose a payment status (paid, partially paid, or overdue).
*   If necessary, include any additional comments about the payment.
*   The form ID is for reference and tracking purposes; it is not required to be filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company or organization making the payment.
*   **Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the person responsible for this payment.
*   **Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the person responsible for this payment.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person or company.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person or company.
*   **Amount**: Enter the total amount of the payment.
*   **Date**: Enter the date for the payment or authorization.
*   **Authorized By**: Indicate who authorized the payment or transaction (e.g., a manager, department head, etc.).
*   **Authorization Date**: Enter the date when the payment was authorized.
*   **Authorization Method**: Specify the method used to authorize the payment (e.g., verbal, written, etc.).
*   **Payment Status**: Choose one of the following options: Paid, Partially Paid, or Overdue.
*   **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about the payment.
*   **Form ID**: This field is for internal reference and tracking purposes; it is not required to be filled.

## Tips

*   Ensure you fill out all required fields.
*   Double-check the payment status to ensure it accurately reflects the current state of the payment.
*   If you need help or have questions, feel free to reach out to our support team.
*   This form is used for internal purposes only; do not share it externally.
