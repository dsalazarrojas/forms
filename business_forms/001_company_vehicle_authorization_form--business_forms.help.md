# Company Vehicle Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Company Vehicle Authorization Form is a document that requests authorization to use a company vehicle for business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your personal information, including your first and last name.
2. Enter your employee identification number.
3. Select your primary department.
4. Enter the vehicle identification or plate number.
5. Specify the purpose of vehicle use for business tasks or assignments.
6. Choose the start date when work on the vehicle begins.
7. Enter the end date or estimated end date when work on the vehicle ends (if applicable).
8. Enter the name of your approver, typically your manager or fleet administrator.
9. Provide the contact information (phone or email) of your approver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee full name** (employee_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **employee identification number** (employee_id, text, required): Enter your unique employee ID as issued by the company.
* **department** (department, select_one, required): Choose your primary department from the options provided, which include Operations, Sales, Human Resources, Maintenance, and Other.
* **vehicle identification or plate** (vehicle_id, text, required): Enter the vehicle's identification number or license plate number.
* **purpose of vehicle use** (authorization_purpose, text, required): Briefly describe the business task or assignment that requires vehicle use.
* **authorization start date** (start_date, date, required): Enter the date when work on the vehicle begins.
* **authorization end date** (end_date, date, optional): Enter the end date when work on the vehicle ends, or an estimated end date if the vehicle usage is ongoing.
* **approver name** (authorized_by, text, required): Enter the name of your manager or fleet administrator who approved this vehicle usage.
* **approver contact** (approver_contact, text, optional): Provide the contact information (phone or email) of your approver in case of any questions or issues.
