# Community Planning Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Planning Complaint Form is designed to collect information from individuals reporting complaints related to community planning issues. This form is used to gather details about the complaint, including contact information, issue description, location, and preferred resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information, including First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Select the Type of Complaint (e.g., Zoning Violation, Building Code Violation, Noise Complaint).
3. Provide a detailed Description of the complaint.
4. Choose the Date and Time of the incident (if applicable).
5. Select the desired Resolution for your complaint.
6. Indicate the level of Urgency for the complaint.
7. Choose whether you wish to remain anonymous.
8. Provide any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complainant Information**: Your contact details, including First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email Address**: Your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Your Address**: The address where the issue is located.
* **City**: The city where the issue is located.
* **State**: The state where the issue is located.
* **ZIP Code**: The zip code where the issue is located.
* **Complaint Details**: A description of the issue, including any supporting materials.
* **Type of Complaint**: The type of complaint (e.g., zoning, building code, noise).
* **Detailed Description**: A thorough description of the complaint.
* **Date of Incident**: When the issue occurred.
* **Time of Incident**: If applicable, the time the issue occurred.
* **Location Information**: More details about the location.
* **Property Address**: The address where the issue is located.
* **Property Owner Name**: If known, the property owner's name.
* **Parcel or Lot Number**: If known, the parcel or lot number.
* **Have You Reported This Before**: If you have previously reported this issue.
* **Previous Report Date**: If applicable, the date of the previous report.
* **Evidence and Documentation**: Supporting materials for your complaint.
* **Do You Have Photos**: If you have attached photos.
* **Do You Have Supporting Documents**: If you have attached documents.
* **Are There Witnesses**: If there are others who can confirm the issue.
* **Desired Resolution**: The preferred outcome for your complaint.
* **Urgency Level**: The level of urgency for your complaint (e.g., emergency, high, medium, low).
* **Do You Want to Remain Anonymous**: If you wish to remain anonymous.
* **Additional Comments**: Any other relevant information.

**Tips** 
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing the issue.
* Attach any supporting documents or photos to help us understand the complaint better.
* Choose the correct Type of Complaint to help us address the issue correctly.
* Be sure to indicate the correct Date and Time of the incident.
* Select the desired Resolution for your complaint.
* Indicate the level of Urgency for your complaint.
* If you have any additional comments or information, please include them in the Additional Comments field.
