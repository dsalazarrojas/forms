# 90 Day Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 90 Day Employee Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the performance of new employees during their 90-day probationary period. This form helps supervisors or HR representatives to evaluate an employee's overall performance, strengths, areas for improvement, and recommend next steps for their growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name, position, and evaluator's name in the respective fields.
2. Select the evaluation date when the performance was assessed.
3. Rate the employee's overall performance on a scale of 1-10.
4. Select the core competencies that best match the employee's performance (e.g., Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, Needs improvement, Unsatisfactory).
5. Describe the employee's key strengths observed during the evaluation period.
6. Identify areas for improvement or development plans for the employee.
7. Rate the employee's attendance and punctuality on a scale of 1-5.
8. If applicable, rate the employee's remote work adaptability (Highly adaptable, Adequate, Needs support, Not applicable).
9. Select the recommended next steps for the employee (e.g., Continue in role, Extend probation with performance plan, Reassign responsibilities, Termination recommended, Further training required).
10. Summarize the evaluator's final comments and decision rationale.
11. Sign the form with your full name to serve as an electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee name**: Enter the employee's first and last name as per company records.
* **Position/Job title**: Enter the employee's current job title and position.
* **Evaluator name**: Enter your full name as the supervisor or HR representative conducting the evaluation.
* **Evaluation date**: Enter the date when the performance was assessed.
* **Overall performance rating**: Rate the employee's performance on a scale of 1-10, with 10 being exceptional.
* **Core competencies assessment**: Select the best match from the provided options (Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, Needs improvement, Unsatisfactory).
* **Key strengths**: Describe specific behaviors or outcomes that demonstrate the employee's strengths.
* **Areas for improvement/Development plan**: Identify specific actions or measures to help the employee improve.
* **Attendance & punctuality**: Rate the employee's attendance and punctuality on a scale of 1-5, with 5 being excellent.
* **Remote work adaptability**: If applicable, rate the employee's remote work adaptability (Highly adaptable, Adequate, Needs support, Not applicable).
* **Recommended next steps**: Select the next steps for the employee (e.g., Continue in role, Extend probation with performance plan, Reassign responsibilities, Termination recommended, Further training required).
* **Final evaluator comments**: Summarize your decision rationale and expectations for the employee's growth and development.
* **Evaluator signature**: Type your full name to serve as an electronic signature.

Remember to provide specific, concrete, and objective feedback to help the employee grow and improve. This form is a tool to support the employee's development, not to criticize or demote them.
