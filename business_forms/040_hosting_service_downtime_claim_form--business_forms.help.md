# Hosting Service Downtime Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hosting Service Downtime Claim Form is used to document and report instances of hosting service downtime. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Hosting Service Downtime Claim" page to begin.
2. Enter a brief description of the service interruption in the "Service Intermittent" field.
3. Select the "Downtime Start Date" and "Downtime End Date" to record the duration of the service outage.
4. Choose the level of "Impact" the outage had on your system, with options being High, Medium, or Low.
5. Upload any "Supporting Files" that are relevant to the incident.
6. Enter any additional "Comments" about the outage.
7. Assign the form to a specific "Assigned To" if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hosting Service Downtime Claim** (`hosting_service_downtime_claim_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the service outage, including its nature and any relevant details.
* **Service Intermittent** (`service_interruption_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the service interruption.
* **Claim Amount** (`compensation_claim_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you are claiming for the service outage.
* **Downtime Start Date** (`downtime_start_date`, date, optional): Select the date the service went down.
* **Downtime End Date** (`downtime_end_date`, date, optional): Select the date the service came back up.
* **Reason for Intermittent** (`reason_for_interruption`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the service interruption.
* **Impact** (`impact`, select_one, optional): Select the level of impact the outage had, with options being High, Medium, or Low.
* **Supporting Files** (`supporting_files`, text, optional): Upload any supporting files related to the incident.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the outage.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Assign the form to a specific person or team if desired.
