# retail_innovation_lab_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Retail Innovation Lab Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in the lab, including their contact details, job titles, and innovation descriptions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the type of registration you want to submit (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
2. Fill in your first name in the first name field.
3. Enter your last name in the last name field.
4. Provide your date of birth in the date field (mm/dd/yyyy format).
5. Enter your email address in the email field (e.g., example@example.com).
6. Provide your phone number in the phone field (no spaces or special characters).
7. Enter your company name in the company field.
8. Select your role in the company from the options provided in the role field.
9. Enter your department in the department field.
10. Enter your location in the location field.
11. Select the team you belong to from the options provided in the team field.
12. Enter your job title in the job title field (e.g., Sales Manager).
13. Enter your innovation description in the innovation description field (e.g., "Our company is developing a new product...").
14. Enter the name of the company representative in the company representative field.
15. Select your job type from the options provided in the job type field.
16. Select your industry from the options provided in the industry field (multiple selections allowed).
17. Select your job level from the options provided in the job level field.
18. Select your job type 1 from the options provided in the job type 1 field.
19. Select your job type 2 from the options provided in the job type 2 field.
20. Select your job type 3 from the options provided in the job type 3 field.
21. Enter your job title 2 in the job title 2 field (e.g., Marketing Analyst).
22. Enter your job title 3 in the job title 3 field (e.g., Software Engineer).
23. Enter your job title 4 in the job title 4 field (e.g., Product Manager).
24. Enter your job title 5 in the job title 5 field (e.g., Data Scientist).
25. Enter your job title 6 in the job title 6 field (e.g., IT Manager).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (e.g., example@example.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number without spaces or special characters.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Select your role in the company from the options provided.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department name.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter your location.
* **Team** (`team`, text, optional): Select the team you belong to from the options provided.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title (e.g., Sales Manager).
* **Innovation Description** (`innovation_description`, text, optional): Enter your innovation description (e.g., "Our company is developing a new product...").
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company representative.
* **Company Representative** (`company_representative`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company representative.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select your job type from the options provided.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_multiple, optional): Select your industry from the options provided (multiple selections allowed).
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, optional): Select your job level from the options provided.
* **Job Type 1** (`job_type_1`, select_one, optional): Select your job type 1 from the options provided.
* **Job Type 2** (`job_type_2`, select_one, optional): Select your job type 2 from the options provided.
* **Job Type 3** (`job_type_3`, select_one, optional): Select your job type 3 from the options provided.
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, optional): Enter your job title 2 (e.g., Marketing Analyst).
* **Job Title 3** (`job_title_3`, text, optional): Enter your job title 3 (e.g., Software Engineer).
* **Job Title 4** (`job_title_4`, text, optional): Enter your job title 4 (e.g., Product Manager).
* **Job Title 5** (`job_title_5`, text, optional): Enter your job title 5 (e.g., Data Scientist).
* **Job Title 6** (`job_title_6`, text, optional): Enter your job title 6 (e.g., IT Manager).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Select the correct options for job type, job level, industry, and job type fields.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Clear and concise innovation descriptions and job titles are recommended.
