# Sign On Letter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sign On Letter Form is designed to help participants create a simple sign-on letter for an organization and a group they are participating in. It collects information necessary to make a concise and accurate letter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Organization Name field with the official name of the organization you are representing or involved with.
2. Fill in the Group Name field with the name of the group you are participating in.
3. In the Participation Information field, provide any relevant details about your participation, such as dates, roles, or accomplishments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the official name of the organization you are representing or involved with.
* **Group Name** (`group_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the group you are participating in.
* **Participation Information** (`participation_information`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any relevant details about your participation, such as dates, roles, or accomplishments.
