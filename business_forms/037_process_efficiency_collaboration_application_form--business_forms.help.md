# Process Efficiency Collaboration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect collaboration and efficiency improvement ideas from users to enhance business processes. Please only submit if you have a genuine idea or input.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields. Required fields are marked as 'required'.
2. Select multiple ideas in the "collaboration ideas" field if you have more than one idea.
3. Choose one improvement idea in the "process improvement ideas" field.
4. Enter your email and phone number.
5. Optionally, provide a date and time for reference.
6. If you have any comments, type them in the "comments" field.
7. Finally, select "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief title to describe your idea or input.
- **Collaboration Ideas** (`collaboration_ideas`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more ideas that can be improved or changed. 
- **Process Improvement Ideas** (`process_improvement_ideas`, `select_one`, required): Choose one idea that you believe can improve processes.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts to your submission.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Optionally, select a date for reference.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Optionally, select a time for reference.
- **Review** (`review`, `note`, optional): Add any last-minute thoughts or comments.
- **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required): Select "Submit" to finalize your submission.
