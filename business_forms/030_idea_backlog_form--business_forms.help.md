# Idea Backlog Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Idea Backlog Form is a tool used to collect and organize ideas, tasks, and other relevant information related to business operations. It helps track and manage ideas from their inception to completion.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the title of your idea or task in the first field.
2. Select the due date and time for the idea or task on the second field.
3. Choose the priority level (High, Low, Medium) for your idea or task.
4. Select the assignee (person responsible for the task) from the options provided.
5. Choose the status (Open, In Progress, Closed) of your idea or task.
6. Enter any additional comments for your idea or task.
7. Enter your email address for contact.
8. (Optional) Enter your phone number.
9. Repeat the steps above for the remaining pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title** (`idea_backlog_form_page`, text, required): Enter the title of your idea or task.
  - This field is used to label the idea or task.
  - It should be a brief and descriptive title.
- **Due Date** (`idea_backlog_form_page_3`, date, required): Select a due date for the idea or task.
  - This field is used to set a deadline for the completion of the idea or task.
  - Choose a date that is realistic and achievable.
- **Priority** (`idea_backlog_form_page_4`, select_one, required): Choose the priority level for your idea or task.
  - This field is used to determine the importance and urgency of the idea or task.
  - Select High for high-priority tasks, Low for low-priority tasks, and Medium for tasks with moderate priority.
- **Assignee** (`idea_backlog_form_page_5`, select_one, required): Select the person responsible for the task.
  - This field is used to assign ownership and accountability for the idea or task.
