# Supply Chain Mapping Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Chain Mapping Request Form is used to collect information about companies that participate in the supply chain. This form helps to understand how the supply chain is structured and how information flows between companies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields for the company you are representing. If you are not sure about any field, please ask your contact person for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Company Address**: Enter the address of your company.
* **City**: Enter the city where your company is located.
* **State**: Select the state where your company is located from the list. Currently, we support California, New York, and Texas.
* **Country**: Select the country where your company is located from the list. Currently, we support USA, Canada, and Mexico.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and title of the contact person for your company.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Company Type**: Enter a brief description of your company type.
* **Industry**: Enter a brief description of your industry.
* **Supply Chain Map**: Select the maps that apply to your company. This helps us to understand the supply chain structure.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about your company.
* **Company Name (2)**: Enter the name of another company in your supply chain.
* **Company Address (2)**: Enter the address of another company in your supply chain.
* **City (2)**: Enter the city where the other company is located.
* **State (2)**: Select the state where the other company is located from the list. Currently, we support California, New York, and Texas.
* **Country (2)**: Select the country where the other company is located from the list. Currently, we support USA, Canada, and Mexico.
* **Contact Person (2)**: Enter the name and title of the contact person for the other company.
* **Contact Email (2)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (2)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Company Type (2)**: Enter a brief description of the other company type.
* **Notes (2)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the other company.
