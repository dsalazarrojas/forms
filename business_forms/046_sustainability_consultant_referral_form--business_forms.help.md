# Sustainability Consultant Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Consultant Referral Form is used to refer a sustainability consultant to work with a business on a specific project. This form collects the required information for the referral process and helps in assigning the correct consultant for the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the name of the business that needs the sustainability consultant.
2. Enter the email address of the business contact person.
3. Enter the phone number of the business contact person.
4. Enter the address of the business.
5. Enter the name of the expert that will be working with the business.
6. Choose the type of referral (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3) that best describes the project.
7. Provide any comments or additional information about the project.
8. Select the tool that will be assigned to the consultant for the project (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
9. Attach any relevant output files or documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the business that needs the sustainability consultant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the business contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the business contact person.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the business.
* **Expert Name** (`expert_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the expert that will be working with the business.
* **Referral** (`referral`, select_one, optional): Select the type of referral that best describes the project. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any comments or additional information about the project.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool that will be assigned to the consultant for the project. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Attach any relevant output files or documents.

**Tips**

* Ensure that each field is filled in as relevant and accurately before submitting the form.
* Double-check that the selected options are correct and match the project requirements.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the support team for assistance.
