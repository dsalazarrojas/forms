# Facility Management Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Facility Management Audit Form is designed to collect information and feedback from facility management teams to assess and improve the effectiveness of your facility's operations. This form aims to gather data on various aspects of facility management, including equipment maintenance, safety procedures, and employee training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review each page carefully to ensure you have provided complete and correct information.
3. Use the available space to provide any additional comments or explanations when necessary.
4. If you are unsure about any question or need clarification, please contact the facility management team for guidance.
