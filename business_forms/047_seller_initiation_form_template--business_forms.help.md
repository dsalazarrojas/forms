# Seller Initiation Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seller Initiation Form Template is designed to help sellers initiate their business activities by collecting essential information about their store and product details. This form is intended to be filled out by sellers to provide a clear and concise overview of their business operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the `seller_onboarding_process` field on page 1.
2.  Proceed to page 2 and complete the `seller_details` field.
3.  On page 3, fill out the `store_info` field.
4.  Next, complete the `product_info` field on page 4.
5.  Move on to page 5 and fill out the `seller_info` field.
6.  On page 6, complete the `payment_terms` field.
7.  Finally, fill out the `legal_terms` field on page 7.
8.  Lastly, complete the `agreement` field on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Seller Onboarding Process** (`seller_onboarding_process`): This field is used to provide an overview of the seller's onboarding process.
*   **Seller Details** (`seller_details`): This field is used to capture information about the seller's personal details.
*   **Store Information** (`store_info`): This field is used to collect information about the seller's store, such as its name and address.
*   **Product Information** (`product_info`): This field is used to gather details about the products the seller offers.
*   **Seller Information** (`seller_info`): This field is used to capture information about the seller's background and expertise.
*   **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): This field is used to outline the seller's payment terms and conditions.
*   **Legal Terms** (`legal_terms`): This field is used to document the seller's legal terms and conditions.
*   **Agreement** (`agreement`): This field is used to record the seller's agreement to the terms and conditions.

Note: All fields are optional, but filling them out will provide a more comprehensive overview of the seller's business operations.
