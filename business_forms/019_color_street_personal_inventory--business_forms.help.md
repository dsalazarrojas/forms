# Color Street Personal Inventory - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your personal jewelry inventory, including the quantity of each type of item you have in your collection. This will help us track and manage your inventory levels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date of your last inventory update in the "Date of Inventory" field.
2. Enter the quantity of each type of item in your personal inventory, using the following fields:
	* Solids: the number of solid-colored items
	* Glitters: the number of glitter-coated items
	* Designs/Art: the number of items with unique designs or art
	* French: the number of French-colored items
	* Holiday/Limited: the number of holiday-themed or limited-edition items
	* Pedicure: the number of pedicure sets
	* Samples/Twosies: the number of samples or twosies
3. Enter any additional notes about your personal inventory in the "Personal Inventory Notes" field.
4. Review and submit the form to update your personal inventory levels.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Inventory (Date)**: Enter the date of your last inventory update.
* **Solids (Personal)**: Enter the number of solid-colored items in your personal collection.
* **Glitters (Personal)**: Enter the number of glitter-coated items in your personal collection.
* **Designs/Art (Personal)**: Enter the number of items with unique designs or art in your personal collection.
* **French (Personal)**: Enter the number of French-colored items in your personal collection.
* **Holiday/Limited (Personal)**: Enter the number of holiday-themed or limited-edition items in your personal collection.
* **Pedicure (Personal)**: Enter the number of pedicure sets in your personal collection.
* **Samples/Twosies (Personal)**: Enter the number of samples or twosies in your personal collection.
* **Total Personal Stock Value** (Optional): Enter the total value of your personal collection.
* **Personal Inventory Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your personal inventory.
