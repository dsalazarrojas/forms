# Design Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients or customers about their design projects. It helps gather contact information, project details, and specific design requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information about the client or customer.
2. Provide a brief description of the project, including its name and any relevant details.
3. Specify the design services required for the project.
4. Choose the relevant design files related to the project.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about the project.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person to contact about the project.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the person to contact about the project.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person to contact about the project.
* **Contact Website** (`contact_website`, `text`, required: false): Enter the website of the person or company to contact about the project.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the project, including its name.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required: false): Add a brief description of the project.
* **Design Services** (`design_services`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the design services required for the project from the available options (Yes, No).
* **Other Design Services** (`design_services_other`, `text`, required: false): If the required design services are not listed, enter the specific services needed for the project.
* **Budget** (`budget`, `number`, required: false): Enter the budget for the project.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, `date`, required: false): Enter the deadline for the project.
* **Design Files** (`design_files`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the relevant design files related to the project from the available options (file1, file2, file3).
* **Other Design Files** (`design_files_other`, `text`, required: false): If the required design files are not listed, enter the specific files needed for the project.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the project.
* **Design Notes** (`design_notes`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional notes or information about the project.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields if they are marked as required.
* Use the available options for the select_multiple and select_one fields to choose the relevant design services or files.
* If the project requires specific design services or files not listed, enter the details in the corresponding text fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary information is provided.
