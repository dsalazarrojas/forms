# Facility Management Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit maintenance requests for facilities and equipment within your workplace. Please use this form to provide accurate and complete information to help the maintenance team understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of maintenance required from the options provided. You can choose one or multiple options as needed.
2. Enter the date of the maintenance request, if applicable.
3. Provide a clear description of the maintenance request in the description field.
4. Select the facilities affected by the maintenance request from the options provided.
5. Enter the contact information and email address of the person requesting maintenance.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the maintenance request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Maintenance Request Type** (`Maintenance Requests`, `type: select_multiple`): Select the type(s) of maintenance required. Please choose one or multiple options from the dropdown list.
	+ Request for maintenance of facilities: Select this option for maintenance needs related to the physical structure or building itself.
	+ Request for maintenance of equipment: Select this option for maintenance needs related to office machines, appliances, or other equipment within the facility.
	+ Other: Choose this option if your maintenance request doesn't fit into the other two categories.
- **Date of Maintenance Request** (`Date of Maintenance Request`, `type: date`): Enter the date the maintenance request is required or was noticed. This field is optional but helpful for the maintenance team to plan and schedule maintenance effectively.
- **Description of Maintenance Request** (`Description of Maintenance Request`, `type: text`): Provide a clear and concise description of the maintenance request, including any specific details or requirements.
- **Service Required** (`Service Required`, `type: select_one`): Select the service required for the maintenance request.
	+ Request for maintenance of facilities: This service option is for maintenance related to the physical structure or building itself.
	+ Request for maintenance of equipment: This service option is for maintenance related to office machines, appliances, or other equipment within the facility.
	+ Other: This option is for maintenance requests not listed above.
- **Which Facility** (`Which Facility`, `type: select_multiple`): Select the facilities affected by the maintenance request from the available options. You can choose one or multiple facilities.
- **Contact Information** (`Contact`, `type: text`): Enter your name and title or position within the organization.
- **Email** (`Email`, `type: email`): Enter a valid email address of the person requesting maintenance.
- **Note** (`Note`, `type: note`): Add any additional comments or notes about the maintenance request. This field is optional but can provide helpful context for the maintenance team.
