# spa_session - Help Guide
## Purpose
The spa_session form is designed to collect necessary information for a spa session, enabling the spa business to efficiently manage their operations and provide excellent customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the spa_session form, follow these steps:

1. Select the service type you are offering (e.g., Massage, Facial, Manicure, or Pedicure).
2. Choose the date of the service.
3. Specify the time of the service.
4. Enter the customer's name.
5. Enter the customer's email address.
6. Enter the customer's phone number.
7. Enter the name of the therapist performing the service.
8. Provide any additional service details in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Spa Session (1)**: This section is a note section for general session information.
* **Service Type (2)**: Select the type of service you are providing, such as Massage, Facial, Manicure, or Pedicure.
* **Service Date (3)**: Choose the date on which you will be providing the service.
* **Service Time (4)**: Specify the time of the service in hours and minutes.
* **Customer Name (5)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer E-mail (6)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone (7)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Therapist (8)**: Enter the name of the therapist who will be providing the service.
* **Service Details (9)**: Provide any additional information about the service, such as any special requests or requirements.
