# Zero Plastic Facility Commitment Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zero Plastic Facility Commitment Registration form is used to gather information about facilities that have committed to reducing plastic usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the facility details, including the name, location, and address of the facility.
2. Enter the contact information for the facility, including phone number and email.
3. Describe the sustainability initiatives being implemented at the facility.
4. Provide a brief description of the commitment made by the facility.
5. Specify the status of the commitment (Active or Inactive).
6. Indicate whether the facility is part of a sustainability program (Yes or No).
7. Confirm whether the facility has been authorized to commit to reducing plastic usage (Yes or No).
8. Enter the name and contact information for the facility owner and manager.
9. Enter any additional notes about the facility's commitment.
10. Enter the date and time of the commitment.
11. Enter the type of facility (e.g., warehouse, office building, etc.).
12. Enter the size of the facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Details** (`facility_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the facility.
* **Facility Location** (`facility_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the facility.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the facility.
* **Facility Phone** (`facility_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the facility.
* **Facility Email** (`facility_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the facility.
* **Sustainability Initiatives** (`sustainability_initiatives`, text, required): Describe the sustainability initiatives being implemented at the facility.
* **Initiative Description** (`initiative_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the commitment made by the facility.
* **Commitment Status** (`commitment_status`, select_one, required): Specify the status of the commitment (Active or Inactive).
* **Sustainability Programs** (`sustainability_programs`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether the facility is part of a sustainability program (Yes or No).
* **Authorized Commitment** (`authorized_commitment`, select_one, required): Confirm whether the facility has been authorized to commit to reducing plastic usage (Yes or No).
* **Facility Owner** (`facility_owner`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information for the facility owner.
* **Facility Manager** (`facility_manager`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information for the facility manager.
* **Facility Operations** (`facility_operations`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about the facility's commitment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about the facility.
* **Facility Commitment Date** (`facility_commitment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the commitment.
* **Facility Commitment Time** (`facility_commitment_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the commitment.
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`, text, required): Enter the type of facility (e.g., warehouse, office building, etc.).
* **Facility Size** (`facility_size`, number, required): Enter the size of the facility.

Note: Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field, as this form will be used to track progress and identify areas for improvement.
