# Penetration Testing Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track information about penetration testing audits, focusing on risk assessments, remediation activities, and compliance testing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the test target field with the specific system, application, or network being tested.
2. Choose the frequency of the test (Low, Medium, or High) and select the types of tests performed (Penetration, Vulnerability, or Compliance).
3. Determine the type of test target (External, Internal, or External/Internal).
4. List any risk risk factors found during the test.
5. Select the severity of the risk factors (Low, Medium, or High).
6. Indicate whether risk factors have been remediated (True or False).
7. Choose the status of remediation activities (In Progress, Done, or Not Started).
8. Enter the date when remediation activities were completed or are due.
9. Add any additional comments about remediation activities.
10. For compliance testing, select the status of testing activities (In Progress, Done, or Not Started).
11. Enter the date of the last and next compliance tests.
12. Add any comments about compliance testing activities.
13. Finally, include any additional notes about the test or remediation activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Test Target**: This field is used to specify the system, application, or network being tested. Please enter the name or description of the test target.
* **Frequency Tested**: Choose the frequency of the test, indicating how often the system or application is tested (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Date Last Tested**: Enter the date when the last test was performed.
* **Date Next Test**: Enter the date when the next test is scheduled.
* **Test Frequency**: Select the frequency of the test (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Test Type**: Choose the type of test performed (Penetration, Vulnerability, or Compliance).
* **Test Target Type**: Select the type of test target (External, Internal, or External/Internal).
* **Risk Risk Factors**: List any risk factors found during the test.
* **Risk Risk Factor Comments**: Add any comments about risk risk factors.
* **Risk Risk Factor Severity**: Select the severity of risk risk factors (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Risk Risk Factors Found**: Indicate whether risk factors have been found.
* **Risk Risk Factors Remediated**: Indicate whether risk factors have been remediated.
* **Remediation Status**: Choose the status of remediation activities (In Progress, Done, or Not Started).
* **Remediation Date Completed**: Enter the date when remediation activities were completed.
* **Remediation Date Due**: Enter the date when remediation activities are due.
* **Remediation Comments**: Add any comments about remediation activities.
* **Compliance Testing Status**: Select the status of compliance testing activities (In Progress, Done, or Not Started).
* **Compliance Testing Last Test**: Enter the date of the last compliance test.
* **Compliance Testing Next Test**: Enter the date of the next compliance test.
* **Compliance Testing Comments**: Add any comments about compliance testing activities.
* **Notes**: Include any additional notes about the test or remediation activities.
