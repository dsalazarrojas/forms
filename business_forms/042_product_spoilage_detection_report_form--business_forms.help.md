# Product Spoilage Detection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report product spoilage detection in a business setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, enter a brief description of the product being reported (Product Spoilage Detection Report Form page 1).
2. On the second page, choose the type of spoilage detected (Spoilage Type: Spoilage Type, Defect Type, Damage Type).
3. Describe the spoilage in detail on the third page (Describe Spoilage).
4. On the fourth page, capture any relevant evidence (Capture Evidence).
5. On the final page, submit the report by selecting "Submit" or "Other" (Submit).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Spoilage Detection Report Form** (`product_spoilage_detection_report_form_page_1`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the product being reported.
* **Spoilage Type** (`spoilage_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of spoilage detected:
	+ Spoilage Type
	+ Defect Type
	+ Damage Type
* **Describe Spoilage** (`spoilage_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the spoilage.
* **Capture Evidence** (`evidence_capture`, text, optional): Capture any relevant evidence or photos of the spoilage.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, select_one, optional): Choose to submit the report by selecting "Submit" or "Other" for further action or review.
