# Asset Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track information about company assets, ensuring accurate records and effective asset management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have a clear and accurate understanding of the asset's purpose, usage, and location.
2. Enter the internal reference identifier for the asset (asset id).
3. Provide a descriptive name for the asset (asset name).
4. Specify the department or business unit responsible for the asset (owning department).
5. Identify the physical location or site where the asset is kept (location).
6. Select the current lifecycle stage of the asset (lifecycle stage).
7. (Optional) Enter an estimated value for the asset for tracking purposes (estimated value).
8. If applicable, specify the date when the warranty for the asset expires (warranty expiration date).
9. Identify the primary contact person responsible for the asset (custodian name).
10. Specify the recommended frequency of audits for the asset (audit frequency).
11. Enter any additional management instructions or context for the asset (notes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Asset id** (Required): The internal reference identifier for the asset.
* **Asset name** (Required): A descriptive name used in records.
* **Owning department** (Required): Department or business unit responsible for the asset.
* **Location** (Required): Physical location or site where the asset is kept.
* **Lifecycle stage** (Required): The current stage in the asset lifecycle (Active, Maintenance, Decommissioned, or Pending disposal).
* **Estimated value** (Optional): Estimated monetary value for tracking purposes.
* **Warranty expiration date** (Optional): Date when the warranty for the asset expires, if applicable.
* **Custodian name** (Optional): The primary contact responsible for the asset.
* **Recommended audit frequency** (Required): How often the asset should be audited (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, or As needed).
* **Notes** (Optional): Additional management instructions or context for the asset.

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately and completely fill out all required fields.
- Use the correct lifecycle stage to reflect the asset's current status.
- Ensure that the estimated value is realistic and accurate for tracking purposes.
- If applicable, specify the warranty expiration date for assets with active warranties.
- Be specific about the asset's location to avoid confusion.
- Regularly review and update the asset's lifecycle stage to reflect changes.
- Use the recommended audit frequency to ensure the asset is properly audited and managed.
