# Energy Company Regulatory Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Company Regulatory Compliance Audit Form is a tool for energy companies to track and record their regulatory compliance status. This form helps identify potential issues and provides recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide audit details, including the audit date (if applicable).
2.  Enter your company information, including name and address.
3.  Identify potential compliance issues and select the relevant options.
4.  Document any findings and recommendations related to your company's energy operations.
5.  Set action items and next audit due dates as per your company's procedures.
6.  Select regulatory requirements and energy production, consumption, and distribution statuses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Audit Details** (id: 1)
    *   (`audit_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the audit.
*   **Company Name** (id: 2)
    *   (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your energy company.
*   **Address** (id: 3)
    *   (`address`, text, optional): Enter your company's address.
*   **Location** (id: 4)
    *   (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your company.
*   **Audit Date** (id: 5)
    *   (`audit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the audit.
*   **Compliance Issues** (id: 6)
    *   (`compliance_issues`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have compliance issues, otherwise select 'No'.
*   **Findings** (id: 7)
    *   (`findings`, note, optional): Document any findings related to your company's energy operations.
*   **Recommendations** (id: 8)
    *   (`recommendations`, note, optional): Provide recommendations for improvement related to your company's energy operations.
*   **Action Items** (id: 9)
    *   (`action_items`, select_multiple, optional): Select the action items to be taken to address compliance issues, if any.
*   **Regulatory Requirements** (id: 10)
    *   (`regulatory_requirements`, select_multiple, optional): Select regulatory requirements applicable to your company.
*   **Compliance Status** (id: 11)
    *   (`compliance_status`, select_one, optional): Select the compliance status of your company, either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
*   **Energy Consumption** (id: 12)
    *   (`energy_consumption`, select_one, optional): Select if your company is involved in energy consumption, either 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Energy Production** (id: 13)
    *   (`energy_production`, select_multiple, optional): Select energy production types relevant to your company, e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Energy Storage** (id: 14)
    *   (`energy_storage`, select_one, optional): Select if your company is involved in energy storage, either 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Energy Distribution** (id: 15)
    *   (`energy_distribution`, select_multiple, optional): Select energy distribution types relevant to your company, e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Energy Consumption Type** (id: 16)
    *   (`energy_consumption_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select energy consumption types relevant to your company, e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Energy Source** (id: 17)
    *   (`energy_source`, select_multiple, optional): Select energy sources relevant to your company, e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Energy Storage Method** (id: 18)
    *   (`energy_storage_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select energy storage methods relevant to your company, e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Audit Results** (id: 19)
    *   (`audit_results`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the audit results are satisfactory, otherwise select 'No'.
*   **Next Audit Due Date** (id: 20)
    *   (`next_audit_due_date`, date, optional): Enter the next audit due date.
