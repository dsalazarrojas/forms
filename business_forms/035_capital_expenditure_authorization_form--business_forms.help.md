# Capital Expenditure Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Capital Expenditure Authorization Form is used to request authorization for capital expenditures such as equipment purchases, technology upgrades, or facility improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined in the previous section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (first_name): Your first name.
- **Last Name** (last_name): Your last name.
- **Email Address** (email_address): Your work email address.
- **Phone Number** (phone_number): Your contact number.
- **Department** (department): Your department.
- **Position Title** (position_title): Your job title.
- **Direct Manager Name** (manager_name): Your direct manager's name.
- **Request Date** (request_date): Date of the request.
- **Type of Expenditure** (expenditure_type): Select one from the options: Equipment purchase, Technology/IT, Infrastructure, Facility improvement, Vehicle, Software/License, Furniture, Other.
- **Project Title** (project_title): Brief project title.
- **Project Description** (project_description): Detailed project description.
- **Business Justification** (business_justification): Why is this expenditure needed?
- **Expected Benefits and ROI** (expected_benefits): Describe benefits.
- **Estimated Total Cost** (estimated_cost): Dollar amount.
- **Cost Breakdown** (cost_breakdown): Itemized costs.
- **Funding Source** (funding_source): Select one from the options: Operating budget, Capital budget, Grant funding, Loan/Financing, External funding, Other.
- **Budget Status** (budget_available): Select one from the options: Fully funded from existing budget, Partially funded, request allocation, Not yet budgeted, Contingency allocation.
- **Cost Center/Account Code** (cost_center): For tracking purposes.
- **Number of Vendor Quotes Obtained** (vendor_quotes): Select one from the options: 1 quote, 2 quotes, 3 plus quotes, Not yet obtained.
- **Preferred Vendor** (preferred_vendor): Vendor name.
- **Procurement Method** (procurement_method): Select one from the options: Competitive bidding, Sole source, Emergency purchase, Blanket order, Other.
- **Requested Start/Purchase Date** (requested_start_date): When is this needed?
- **Expected Completion/Installation Date** (completion_date): When should it be completed?
- **Priority Level** (priority_level): Select one from the options: Critical - Immediate, High, Medium, Low.
- **Manager Approval Status** (manager_approval_status): Select one from the options: Approved, Pending approval, Not yet requested.
- **Budget Officer Input/Review** (budget_officer_input): Optional notes.
- **Does This Require Executive Approval?** (authorization_limit): Select one from the options: Yes, requires executive approval, No, manager approval sufficient, Unsure.
- **Any Insurance Implications?** (insurance_implications): Select one from the options: Yes, No, Unsure.
- **Additional Notes or Comments** (additional_notes): Optional comments.
- **I Confirm that all information provided is accurate** (confirmation): Select one from the options: Yes, I confirm, No.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled and that the information provided is correct and up-to-date.
