# Travel Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Travel Request Form is used to gather information about business travels, including travel dates, destinations, and reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the requested information for the travel request, client name, start and end dates, and travel destination.
- Choose the travel reason and duration from the provided options.
- Provide flight and hotel details if applicable.
- Enter the travel date and return date for the trip.
- Check the "Business Trip" option if the trip is for business purposes.
- Add any meeting details if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Travel Request (Required: False)** (`business_travel_request`, Text): Enter a brief description of the travel request, including the purpose and goals of the trip.
* **Client Name (Required: False)** (`client_name`, Text): The name of the client or organization the employee is visiting.
* **Start Date (Required: False)** (`start_date`, Date): The date the employee starts their travel.
* **End Date (Required: False)** (`end_date`, Date): The date the employee ends their travel.
* **Travel Destination (Required: False)** (`travel_destination`, Select One): Choose the city or location of the travel destination.
	+ Options: New York, Los Angeles, Chicago
* **Travel Reason (Required: False)** (`travel_reason`, Text): A brief description of the reason for the travel.
* **Travel Duration (Required: False)** (`travel_duration`, Number): The number of days the travel will last.
* **Flight Details (Required: False)** (`flight_details`, Text): Provide any additional information about flight arrangements.
* **Hotel Details (Required: False)** (`hotel_details`, Text): Provide any additional information about hotel arrangements.
* **Travel Date (Required: False)** (`travel_date`, Date): The date the employee is expected to travel.
* **Return Date (Required: False)** (`return_date`, Date): The date the employee is expected to return.
* **Return Travel Duration (Required: False)** (`return_travel_duration`, Number): The number of days the return travel will last.
* **Business Trip (Required: False)** (`business_trip`, Select One): Check if the trip is for business purposes.
	+ Options: True, False
* **Meeting Details (Required: False)** (`meeting_details`, Text): Provide any additional information about meetings during the trip.
* **Meeting Time (Required: False)** (`meeting_time`, Text): Provide any additional information about meeting times.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required and optional fields accurately and truthfully.
* Check the "Business Trip" option if the trip is for business purposes.
* Double-check travel dates and durations to ensure accuracy.
