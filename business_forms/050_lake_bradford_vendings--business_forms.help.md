# Lake Bradford Vendings - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from lake vendors participating in the "Lake Bradford Vendings" event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your lake vendor's name in the "lake_braddock_vendings" field.
2. Select the date of the event from the calendar provided.
3. Select the start and end times of your participation in the event.
4. Enter your email address and phone number.
5. Indicate if you have a machine to vend and select the type (if applicable).
6. Indicate if you are vending at multiple locations.
7. If you have any comments, enter them in the "form_comments" field.
8. Confirm that you have a valid vendor license.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **lake_braddock_vendings** (`lake_braddock_vendings`, text, required): Enter your lake vendor's name.
* **event_date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event from the calendar provided.
* **start_time** (`start_time`, time, required): Select the start time of your participation in the event.
* **end_time** (`end_time`, time, required): Select the end time of your participation in the event.
* **lake_braddock_vendings_vendor_name** (`lake_braddock_vendings_vendor_name`, text, required): Enter your vendor's name.
* **lake_braddock_vendings_email** (`lake_braddock_vendings_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **lake_braddock_vendings_phone** (`lake_braddock_vendings_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **lake_braddock_vendings_machine_type** (`lake_braddock_vendings_machine_type`, select one, required): Indicate if you have a machine to vend and select the type (if applicable).
* **lake_braddock_vendings_machine_quantity** (`lake_braddock_vendings_machine_quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of machines you have to vend.
* **lake_braddock_vendings_booth_location** (`lake_braddock_vendings_booth_location`, select multiple, required): Indicate if you are vending at multiple locations.
* **lake_braddock_vendings_form_comments** (`lake_braddock_vendings_form_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments you have about your participation in the event.
* **lake_braddock_vendings_vendor_license** (`lake_braddock_vendings_vendor_license`, select multiple, required): Confirm that you have a valid vendor license.

## Tips
* Ensure that your entry is accurate and up-to-date as the form data will be used for reference during the event.
* If you are experiencing any issues during the event, please contact the event organizers with the information provided in this form.
* Only one entry per vendor is allowed.
