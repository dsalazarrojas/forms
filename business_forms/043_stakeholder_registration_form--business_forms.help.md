# Stakeholder Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stakeholder Registration Form is designed to collect information about stakeholders involved in events, meetings, or conferences. This includes their personal details, company information, and roles within the organization. The form is essential for keeping track of stakeholders, assigning tasks, and setting priorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "First name" and "Last name" fields with the stakeholder's personal details.
2. Provide the "Company name" if applicable.
3. Enter the stakeholder's "Email" address.
4. Add the "Phone number" and "Address" if available.
5. Select the "Job title" of the stakeholder.
6. Choose the "Role" of the stakeholder (Stakeholder, Key personnel, or Other).
7. Select the "Company" type (Sales, Marketing, IT, or Other).
8. If attending an event, select the "Event date" and "Event time".
9. Add any "Stakeholder details" or "Other details" as needed.
10. If applicable, select the "Event attendees", "Event organizer", "Stakeholder type", and "Event type".
11. Choose the "Priority" level (High, Low, or Medium).
12. Select the "Status" (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
13. Add "Additional notes" about the stakeholder.
14. Enter any other information in the "Notes" field.
15. Select the "Event type" (Meeting, Conference, Seminar, or Workshop).
16. Add the "Location" and "Attendees" information as needed.
17. Finally, select the "Created by" option (Self, Admin, or User).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name (1)
* First name: Enter the stakeholder's first name.

### Last name (2)
* Last name: Enter the stakeholder's last name.

### Company name (3)
* Company name: Enter the name of the company the stakeholder is associated with (if applicable).

### Email (4)
* Email: Enter the stakeholder's email address.

### Phone number (5)
* Phone number: Enter the stakeholder's phone number (if available).

### Address (6)
* Address: Enter the stakeholder's address (if available).

### Job title (7)
* Job title: Enter the stakeholder's job title.

### Role (8)
* Role: Choose the stakeholder's role within the organization (Stakeholder, Key personnel, or Other).

### Company (9)
* Company: Select the type of company the stakeholder is associated with (Sales, Marketing, IT, or Other).

### Event date (10)
* Event date: Enter the date of the event the stakeholder is involved in.

### Event time (11)
* Event time: Enter the time of the event the stakeholder is involved in.

### Stakeholder details (12)
* Stakeholder details: Add any additional details about the stakeholder.

### Other details (13)
* Other details: Add any other information about the stakeholder.

### Assigned to (14)
* Assigned to: Select the person or team the stakeholder is assigned to.

### Priority (15)
* Priority: Choose the priority level of the task assigned to the stakeholder (High, Low, or Medium).

### Status (16)
* Status: Choose the status of the stakeholder (Active, Inactive, or Pending).

### Notes (17)
* Notes: Add any additional notes about the stakeholder.

### Additional notes (18)
* Additional notes: Add any additional information about the stakeholder.

### Event type (19)
* Event type: Choose the type of event the stakeholder is involved in (Meeting, Conference, Seminar, or Workshop).

### Location (20)
* Location: Enter the location of the event the stakeholder is involved in.

### Attendees (21)
* Attendees: Enter the attendees of the event the stakeholder is involved in.

### Event attendees (22)
* Event attendees: Select the attendees of the event the stakeholder is involved in (Stakeholder, Key personnel, or Other).

### Event organizer (23)
* Event organizer: Enter the person or team organizing the event.

### Stakeholder type (24)
* Stakeholder type: Choose the type of stakeholder (Individual, Organization, or Group).

### Created by (25)
* Created by: Choose the person or team who created the stakeholder registration (Self, Admin, or User).

### Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* You can add more details about the stakeholder in the "Stakeholder details" field.
* You can also add more information about the event in the "Event details" field.
* If you need help with filling in the form, feel free to ask your admin or support team for assistance.
