# private_investigator_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The private investigator interview form is designed to collect and document information about a case, including client details, services needed, and investigation status.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's information in the **Client Information** section.
2. Specify your contact preferences and details in the **Contact Preferences** section.
3. Choose the contact method you prefer to communicate with the client in the **Contact Method** section.
4. Provide the client's contact details in the **Contact Details** section.
5. Select the services you need to perform on the case in the **Services Needed** section.
6. Enter the case information and status in the **Case Information** and **Case Status** sections.
7. Enter the investigator's information in the **Investigator Information** section.
8. Update the total hours worked and total cost in the **Total Hours Worked** and **Total Cost** sections.
9. Specify the payment status and method in the **Payment Status** and **Payment Method** sections.
10. Add any additional case notes in the **Case Notes** section.
11. Sign and date the form to finalize it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Page 1)**: Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Preferences (Page 1)**: Specify how you would like to be contacted by the client (e.g., phone, email, or text message).
* **Contact Method (Page 1)**: Choose the method you prefer to contact the client.
* **Contact Details (Page 1)**: Enter the client's contact information.
* **Investigator Information (Page 2)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Services Needed (Page 2)**: Select the services you need to perform on the case.
* **Case Information (Page 2)**: Enter the case details and status.
* **Case Status (Page 2)**: Update the case status and provide any necessary notes.
* **Client Name (Page 2)**: Enter the client's name.
* **Investigator Name (Page 2)**: Enter your name.
* **Investigation Start Date (Page 2)**: Enter the date the investigation started.
* **Investigation End Date (Page 2)**: Enter the date the investigation ended.
* **Total Hours Worked (Page 3)**: Enter the total hours worked on the case.
* **Total Cost (Page 3)**: Enter the total cost of the services provided.
* **Payment Status (Page 3)**: Specify the payment status of the case.
* **Payment Method (Page 3)**: Choose the payment method used (e.g., cash, check, or credit card).
* **Case Notes (Page 3)**: Enter any additional case notes.
* **Client Signature (Page 3)**: Sign and date the client's signature.
* **Investigator Signature (Page 3)**: Sign and date the investigator's signature.
* **Case Status Notes (Page 3)**: Enter any case status notes.
* **Additional Services (Page 4)**: Select any additional services needed.
* **Other (Specify) (Page 4)**: Enter any other additional services.
* **Additional Cost (Page 4)**: Enter the cost of the additional services.
* **Additional Services Notes (Page 4)**: Enter any notes about the additional services.
* **Review Status (Page 4)**: Specify the final review status of the case.

## Tips

* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals to collect and store the data.
* Verify that you are not duplicating data collection efforts.
* Use the correct format for dates and numbers.
* Enter accurate and complete information.
* Review the form thoroughly before finalizing it.
* Keep the form for future reference and use it to track case progress and payments.
