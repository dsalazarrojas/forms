# Newsletter Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Newsletter Details Form is a tool used to collect information about an individual or organization interested in receiving a newsletter. It is used to gather personal and company information to better understand who the newsletter is being sent to, and what they are interested in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Interest: Select your interest from the options provided or type in your own answer.
2. Enter your Email: Type in your email address where you would like to receive the newsletter.
3. Enter your Phone Number: Type in your phone number if you would like to be reached by phone.
4. Choose your Newsletter Frequency: Select how often you would like to receive the newsletter (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
5. Choose your Newsletter Format: Select the format you prefer for the newsletter (e.g. HTML, Plain Text, RSS, Audio, Video).
6. Choose a Category: Select a category of content you are interested in (e.g. Business, Entertainment, Sports, etc.).
7. Enter your First Name: Type in your first name.
8. Enter your Last Name: Type in your last name.
9. Enter your Company: Type in your company name.
10. Enter your Job Title: Type in your job title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Interest (1)**: Select your interest from the options provided or type in your own answer.
* **Email (2)**: Type in your email address where you would like to receive the newsletter.
* **Phone (3)**: Type in your phone number if you would like to be reached by phone.
* **Newsletter Frequency (4)**: Select how often you would like to receive the newsletter (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Newsletter Format (5)**: Select the format you prefer for the newsletter (e.g. HTML, Plain Text, RSS, Audio, Video).
* **Newsletter Category (6)**: Select a category of content you are interested in (e.g. Business, Entertainment, Sports, etc.).
* **First Name (7)**: Type in your first name.
* **Last Name (8)**: Type in your last name.
* **Company (9)**: Type in your company name.
* **Job Title (10)**: Type in your job title.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure you receive the newsletter.
* If you have any specific preferences for the newsletter format, please let us know in the comments section.
* You can always update your preferences later if your interests or preferences change.
