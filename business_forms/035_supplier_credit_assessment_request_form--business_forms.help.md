# Supplier Credit Assessment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Credit Assessment Request Form is a tool designed to collect information about a supplier's credit assessment request for business purposes. This form is used to gather data on the supplier's contact details, credit limit, payment terms, bank account, and bank routing number.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for this form (e.g., a reference number or a description of the form).
2. **Category**: Select the category of your business (Local, International, or Both).
3. **Address**: Enter the supplier's address in a format that includes street, city, state, and zip.
4. **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person you would like to contact for business purposes.
5. **Contact Number**: Enter a valid phone number that can be used to contact the supplier.
6. **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact the supplier.
7. **Supplier Type**: Select the type of supplier you are dealing with (Local, International, or Both).
8. **Credit Limit**: Enter the supplier's credit limit, which is the maximum amount of credit they can be granted.
9. **Payment Terms**: Enter the payment terms and conditions that apply to your business agreement with the supplier.
10. **Bank Account**: Enter the supplier's bank account details.
11. **Bank Routing Number**: Enter the supplier's bank routing number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form ID**: This field is required to identify the form for reference and tracking purposes.
* **Category**: This field categorizes the supplier's business as local, international, or both.
* **Address**: This field captures the supplier's address, which is essential for communication and delivery purposes.
* **Contact Person**: This field is used to identify the person you would like to contact for business purposes.
* **Contact Number**: This field is used to contact the supplier for communication and confirmation purposes.
* **Contact Email**: This field is used to contact the supplier for email communication and confirmation.
* **Supplier Type**: This field categorizes the supplier as local, international, or both, which helps in assessing their scope of business.
* **Credit Limit**: This field captures the supplier's credit limit, which is the maximum amount of credit they can be granted.
* **Payment Terms**: This field captures the payment terms and conditions that apply to your business agreement with the supplier.
* **Bank Account**: This field captures the supplier's bank account details.
* **Bank Routing Number**: This field captures the supplier's bank routing number.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use a valid and up-to-date address format.
* Enter a valid contact number and email address.
* Select the correct category for the supplier's business.
* Enter a valid bank account and routing number.
* Review the payment terms carefully to ensure they align with your business agreement.
