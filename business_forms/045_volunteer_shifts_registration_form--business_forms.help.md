# Volunteer Shifts Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Volunteer Shifts Registration Form" is a tool for volunteers to register their availability and preferences for specific shifts. By filling out this form, volunteers can indicate their interest in working on certain dates, times, and shifts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your answer to "Volunteer Shifts"
	* If you are available to work shifts, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
2. Enter your event ID (optional)
	* This is the ID of the event you are registering for. If you don't know the ID, you can leave this field blank.
3. Choose your shift dates
	* Select the dates you are available to work.
4. Select your start and end times
	* Choose the start and end times you are available to work.
5. Select your shifts
	* Choose the shifts you are available to work.
6. Select your available shifts
	* Choose the shifts you are available to work.
7. Select your shift preferences
	* Choose the shifts you have preferences for.
8. Enter your volunteer name
	* This is your name as a volunteer.
9. Enter your volunteer email
	* This is your email address as a volunteer.
10. Enter your volunteer phone number
	* This is your phone number as a volunteer.
11. Add volunteer notes (optional)
	* This is a field for any additional information or comments you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **volunteer_shifts** (Yes/No): Indicate whether you are available to work shifts.
* **event_id** (Event ID): This is the ID of the event you are registering for. If you don't know the ID, you can leave this field blank.
* **shift_dates** (Date): Choose the dates you are available to work.
* **start_time** (Time): Choose your start time for the shift.
* **end_time** (Time): Choose your end time for the shift.
* **shifts** (Select Multiple): Choose the shifts you are available to work.
* **available_shifts** (Select Multiple): Choose the shifts you are available to work.
* **shift_preferences** (Select Multiple): Choose the shifts you have preferences for.
* **volunteer_name** (Text): Enter your name as a volunteer.
* **volunteer_email** (Email): Enter your email address as a volunteer.
* **volunteer_phone** (Text): Enter your phone number as a volunteer.
* **volunteer_notes** (Note): Add any additional information or comments you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your availability and preferences before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
