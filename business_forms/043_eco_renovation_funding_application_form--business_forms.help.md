# Eco Renovation Funding Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your eco-renovation project to support your application for funding. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your application is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the first page with your project details.
2. Provide your project's name and description.
3. Indicate the project's location.
4. Select your project's funding source (Government, Private, or Other).
5. Enter the project's start and end dates.
6. Select the project's status (Active, Inactive, or Complete).
7. Provide your contact information, including name, phone number, and email.
8. Upload any supporting documents required for your application.
9. Review and submit the form to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (`project_name`): A brief title of your eco-renovation project. This will be used to identify your project for the funding application.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`): A detailed description of your project, including its objectives and goals.
* **Location** (`project_location`): The location where your project will take place.
* **Funding Source** (`funding_source`): Select the funding source for your project (Government, Private, or Other).
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`): The date your project is expected to start.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`): The date your project is expected to end.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`): Indicate the current status of your project (Active, Inactive, or Complete).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): The name of the person responsible for the project.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): The phone number of the person responsible for the project.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): The email address of the person responsible for the project.
* **Supporting Documents 1-12** (`supporting_documents_1`, `supporting_documents_2`, ..., `supporting_documents_12`): You are required to upload any additional supporting documents related to your project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`): The budget allocated for your project.
* **Project Cost** (`project_cost`): The total cost of your project.
* **Project Funding Amount** (`project_funding_amount`): The amount of funding you are requesting.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to complete your application. 

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Upload any additional supporting documents that are relevant to your project.
* Review your application before submitting it to ensure everything is correct.
* Contact the business owner if you are unsure about any field or requirement.
