# Maritime Accident Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Maritime Accident Incident Form is a tool used to report and document maritime accidents or incidents that have occurred. It is essential to provide accurate and detailed information about the incident to help prevent future occurrences and ensure a swift resolution of the incident.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by entering the Incident Date, which is the date on which the accident or incident occurred.
2. Next, enter the Incident Time, which is the time the accident or incident occurred.
3. Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any injuries or damages sustained, in the Incident Description field.
4. If the cause of the incident is known, enter a description of it in the Incident Cause field.
5. List the parties involved in the incident, including their roles and affiliations, in the Parties Involved field.
6. Provide a detailed description of the damages sustained in the Damages Description field.
7. Enter the Incident Location, which is the physical location where the incident occurred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which the accident or incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the accident or incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false): A detailed description of the incident, including any injuries or damages sustained.
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`, text, required: false): The cause of the incident, if known.
* **Parties Involved** (`parties_involved`, select_multiple, required: false): List the parties involved in the incident, including their roles and affiliations.
* **Damages Description** (`damages_description`, text, required: false): A detailed description of the damages sustained.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required: false): The physical location where the incident occurred.
