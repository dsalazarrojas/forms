# Business Growth Consulting Partnership Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your business growth consulting partnership interests. Please fill out this form to share your contact information, business details, and partnership expectations. We'll use this information to determine the potential fit between our businesses and discuss further.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each field, using the hint text as a guide.
3. Use the select options provided for fields with dropdown choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (`<name>`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or correspondence.
* **Email Address** (`<email_address>`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address for business inquiries.
* **Phone Number** (`<phone_number>`, text, required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Company Name** (`<company_name>`, text, required): Enter the name of your business or consulting firm.
* **Company Website** (`<company_website>`, text, optional): Enter your business website URL.
* **Company Size** (`<company_size>`, select_one, optional): Select the number of employees in your business.
* **Services Offered** (`<services_offered>`, text, required): Describe the consulting services your business provides.
* **Years of Experience** (`<years_of_experience>`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been in business consulting.
* **Key Areas of Expertise** (`<key_expertise>`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant areas of expertise (e.g. Strategy and Planning, Growth and Scaling).
* **Industries Served** (`<industries_served>`, text, optional): Enter the industries your business works with.
* **Target Business Size** (`<business_size_focus>`, select_one, optional): Select the size of clients your business targets.
* **Type of Partnership Sought** (`<partnership_type>`, select_one, required): Select the type of partnership you are seeking (e.g. Referral Partnership, Joint Ventures).
* **Expected Partnership Benefits** (`<partnership_benefits>`, text, required): Describe the benefits you hope to gain from a partnership.
* **Potential Client Overlap** (`<client_overlap>`, text, optional): If you have similar target clients to our business, describe them.
* **Service Complementarity** (`<service_complement>`, text, optional): Describe how your services complement ours.
* **Consulting Methodology** (`<methodology>`, text, optional): Describe your approach to consulting.
* **Key Business Values** (`<key_values>`, text, optional): Describe the values that drive your business.
* **Recent Success Cases** (`<success_cases>`, text, optional): Share examples of successful engagements.
* **Preferred Contact Frequency** (`<contact_frequency>`, select_one, optional): Select how often you'd like to stay in touch (e.g. Monthly check-in).

Please note that we value your time and will do our best to respond promptly to your inquiry. Thank you for considering a partnership with us!
