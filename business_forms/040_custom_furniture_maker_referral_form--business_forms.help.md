# Custom Furniture Maker Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The custom_furniture_maker_referral_form is used to collect information from business partners or potential partners to a custom furniture making business. It is used to assess potential partnerships and collaborations in the custom furniture making industry.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out the form, please make sure you are representing the business or individual you are referring. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department for assistance. The form can be filled out online or on paper and submitted to the relevant department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your business phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter the name of the company.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter the department you are from.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter your job title.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter the name of the contact person.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter the company website.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, `text`, required): Please select the reason why you are referring this business or individual.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Please select the type of business or individual you are referring. Options include:
	+ Company
	+ Partner
	+ Freelance
* **Business Location** (`business_location`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the location of the business or individual. Options include:
	+ New York
	+ Chicago
	+ Los Angeles
	+ Miami
	+ Dallas
	+ Houston
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, `text`, optional): If you are referring a job opening, please enter the job function.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, optional): If you are referring a job opening, please enter the industry.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, optional): If you are referring a job opening, please enter a brief description of the job.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, optional): If you are referring a job opening, please enter the number of years of experience required.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, required): Please select the level of the job opening. Options include:
	+ Director
	+ Manager
	+ Assistant Manager
	+ Team Lead
	+ Team Member
	+ Intern
	+ Entry Level
* **Job Salary** (`job_salary`, `number`, optional): If you are referring a job opening, please enter the salary of the job.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the status of the job opening. Options include:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Contractor
	+ Freelance
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the type of job opening. Options include:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Contractor
	+ Freelance
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the start date of the job opening.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, `date`, optional): If the job opening is for a limited time, please enter the end date of the job opening.
* **Job Title Notes** (`job_title_notes`, `text`, optional): If you are referring a job opening, please enter any additional notes about the job title.
* **Job Title Notes 2** (`job_title_notes_2`, `text`, optional): If you are referring a job opening, please enter any additional notes about the job title.
* **Job Notes** (`job_notes`, `text`, optional): If you are referring a job opening, please enter any additional notes about the job.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department for assistance.
