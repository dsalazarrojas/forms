# industrial_equipment_sales_team_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for customers and clients of the industrial equipment sales team to contact and get in touch with the team. It helps the team to quickly and efficiently gather information about customer inquiries, feedback, and other messages related to sales and support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately as it will help the sales team to better understand and respond to your request.
2. Select the type of contact you want to make, either "sales" or "support", in the "Contact Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_info** (customer info, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any relevant information about yourself as a customer or client.
* **message_info** (message info, text, optional): Here, you can type in your message or question about the sales team or industrial equipment.
* **sales_info** (sales info, text, optional): You can provide any additional information related to a sales inquiry or question.
* **contact_info** (contact info, select multiple, required): This is where you select the type of contact you want to make with the sales team. Please choose "sales" if you have a sales inquiry or need assistance with a purchase, or "support" for technical issues or product questions.
* **submit** (submit, text, optional): This is where you will submit your completed form for review by the sales team.
