# Packaging Materials Testing Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register testing and materials for packaging materials. It collects information on testing conditions, methods, dates, times, test results, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the test materials section: test_materials
2. Choose the test method: test_method
3. Enter the testing date and time: testing_date and testing_time
4. Enter the testing duration: testing_duration
5. Select the test results: results
6. Add any additional notes: notes
7. Enter the contact information: email, phone, and customer_address

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Test Materials**: This is the first section of the form. Enter the name of the materials being tested.
* **Test Method**: Choose the method used to test the materials. This will help determine the testing process.
* **Testing Date**: Enter the date of the testing. This is a date field, so enter it in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Testing Time**: Enter the time of the testing. This is a time field, so enter it in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Testing Duration**: Enter the length of the testing. This can be a numerical value.
* **Results**: Select one or multiple test results from the list of options.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the testing.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person or organization being tested.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or organization being tested.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer or person being tested.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email address of the customer or person being tested.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the customer or person being tested.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the address of the customer or person being tested.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the testing in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time**: Enter the time of the testing in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Test Type**: Choose the type of test being conducted.
* **Testing Group**: Choose the group the testing belongs to.
* **Testing Status**: Choose the status of the testing.
* **Testing Lab**: Choose the testing lab where the testing was conducted.
* **Testing Equipment**: Choose the equipment used for testing.
* **Testing Location**: Choose the location of the testing.
* **Sample ID**: Enter the ID of the sample being tested.
* **Batch ID**: Enter the batch ID of the sample being tested.
* **Testing Status**: Not applicable (there are multiple fields with this label)
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the testing.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
* Double-check the fields to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact your local testing administrator.
* This form is for packaging materials testing registration only. If you need to register other types of testing, please use the relevant form.
