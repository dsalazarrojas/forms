# Client Strategic Plan Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store client strategic plan information for reference and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information:
	* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client.
	* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company or organization.
	* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
	* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
2. Specify your strategic plan details:
	* **Strategic Plan Title**: Enter the name or title of the strategic plan.
	* **Plan Period**: Choose the time frame for this strategic plan (1 year, 3 years, 5 years, or other).
	* If you selected "Other", provide the specific time frame in the next field.
	* **Strategic Focus Areas**: Select the main areas covered in the plan (Growth, Market expansion, Product development, Cost reduction, Operational efficiency, Risk management, or Other).
	* **Target Outcomes or Goals**: Enter the key objectives for this plan period.
3. Describe your budget or resource allocation:
	* **Budget or Resource Allocation**: Explain how resources are allocated in the plan.
4. Attach your strategic plan document and supporting files:
	* **Primary Strategic Plan Document**: Upload the main plan document.
	* **Supporting Documents**: Attach any additional files (financial forecasts, market analysis, etc.).
5. Provide a brief overview:
	* **Executive Summary**: Write a brief summary of the strategic plan.
6. Identify key risks and metrics:
	* **Key Risks Identified**: List the risks that might affect plan execution.
	* **Success Metrics**: Explain how you will measure plan success.
7. Review and update the plan:
	* **Plan Review Frequency**: Choose how often this plan will be reviewed (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annually, Annually, or As needed).
