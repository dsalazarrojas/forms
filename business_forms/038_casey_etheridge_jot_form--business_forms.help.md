# Casey Etheridge Jot Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Casey Etheridge Jot Form is designed to collect information from customers or clients about their orders, including product selection, delivery details, and payment information. It's used in a business setting, likely for an online store or e-commerce platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Product Selection**
	* Select the type of product you're ordering (e.g. Apparel, Accessories, Home goods, etc.)
	* Specify the name of the product you'd like to order
2. **Customer Information**
	* Enter your full name
	* Enter your email address for order confirmation
	* Enter your phone number for best contact
3. **Order Details**
	* Enter the date of the order
	* Check if the item needs customization
	* If applicable, describe the customization options
	* Check if you've checked the stock availability of the item
4. **Shipping Information**
	* Enter your shipping address
	* Select the shipping method (e.g. Standard shipping, Expedited shipping, Overnight shipping, Local pickup, Will call)
	* Enter the shipping cost (if any)
5. **Payment Information**
	* Select the payment method (e.g. Credit card, Debit card, PayPal, etc.)
	* Check if you've paid in full or have made a deposit
6. **Order Tracking**
	* Leave this field blank
7. **Communication Preference**
	* Select how you'd like to be notified about order updates (e.g. Email updates, Text message updates, Phone calls)
8. **Order Completion Date**
	* Leave this field blank, but note that it's the estimated completion date
9. **Terms and Conditions**
	* Check if you agree to the terms and conditions
