# Individual Self-Assessment Form For Program Management Office - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect individual information from program management office members, providing accurate and up-to-date self-assessment data for internal use.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately and completely as possible.
2. Review each field for accuracy before submitting the form.
3. Use the available space to provide any additional comments or notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Name**: (`form_name`, text, optional)
	+ This field is for entering a brief name for the form, which will be used for reference purposes.
* **Name**: (`name`, text, optional)
	+ Please enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email**: (`email`, email, optional)
	+ Enter your official email address, which will be used for internal communication.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your contact phone number, which will be used for internal communication.
* **Manager Name**: (`manager_name`, text, optional)
	+ If you have a manager, enter their full name as it appears on their official records.
* **Manager Email**: (`manager_email`, email, optional)
	+ If you have a manager, enter their official email address, which will be used for internal communication.
* **Manager Phone**: (`manager_phone`, text, optional)
	+ If you have a manager, enter their contact phone number, which will be used for internal communication.
* **Start Date**: (`start_date`, date, optional)
	+ If you are new to the program management office, please enter your start date of employment or appointment.
	+ If you are not new, please enter the current date.
* **End Date**: (`end_date`, date, optional)
	+ If applicable, please enter your expected or actual end date of employment or appointment.
* **Project Name**: (`project_name`, text, optional)
	+ If you are working on a specific project, please enter its name for reference purposes.
