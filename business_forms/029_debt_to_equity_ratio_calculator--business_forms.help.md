# Debt To Equity Ratio Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Debt-to-Equity Ratio Calculator is a simple tool used to estimate the Debt-to-Equity Ratio of a company or organization. This ratio is an important metric in finance that helps investors and analysts understand a company's leverage and risk.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Total Debt value in the first field. This represents the total amount of debt a company or organization has.
2. Enter the Total Equity value in the second field. This represents the total amount of equity a company or organization has.
3. The form will calculate and display the Debt-to-Equity Ratio in the third field. You do not need to take any further action for this field as it will be populated automatically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Total Debt** (`debt`, number, required/optional: optional): Enter the total amount of debt a company or organization has. This can be a one-time payment, a loan, or a bond.
* **Total Equity** (`equity`, number, required/optional: optional): Enter the total amount of equity a company or organization has. This represents the difference between a company's assets and liabilities.
* **Debt-to-Equity Ratio** (number, required/optional: optional): This field will be populated automatically with the calculated Debt-to-Equity Ratio. You do not need to take any action for this field.
