# online_screen_addiction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online_screen_addiction_survey is a tool designed to assess your online screen use habits, focusing on both time spent and potential harms associated with excessive screen use.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question as accurately and honestly as possible.
2. Please select all options that apply to your online behaviors.
3. Do not leave any questions blank, even if you feel unsure or uncomfortable answering them.
4. Take your time, but be as concise as possible in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Screen Use** (`screen_use`, number, required: false): Please report the average amount of time you spend on screens per day, including time spent on computers, smartphones, tablets, gaming consoles, and televisions.
- **Screen Type** (`screen_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all types of screens you regularly use, including computers, smartphones, tablets, gaming consoles, and televisions.
- **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, number, required: false): Report the total time you typically spend on screens per day, including work, entertainment, social media, and other activities.
- **Screen Use Harms** (`screen_use_harms`, select_one, required: false): Check the potential harms you experience or are concerned about due to screen use, such as Screen addiction, Social media addiction, Online game addiction, Mobile phone addiction, Internet addiction, Video game addiction, or Computer addiction.
- **Other screen addiction** (`screen_use_harms`, select_one, required: false): If you do not experience any of the listed harms or are unsure, please check "Other screen addiction" and describe the specific issue you are concerned about.
- **Online Behaviors** (`online_behaviors`, note, required: false): If you would like to elaborate on your online behaviors or provide any additional information not covered in previous questions, please use this field.
- **Family Involvement** (`family_involvement`, text, required: false): If you would like to report how your family members are involved in your online activities or how they might be affected by your screen use, please provide a brief description.

## Tips
- If you are unsure or uncomfortable answering any questions, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
- If you would like to report any specific screen-related issues or concerns not covered in this form, please use the "Online Behaviors" field.
- If you would like to provide additional information or ask questions, please use the "Online Behaviors" field.

Note: This guide is for the online_screen_addiction_survey form. Please keep in mind that it is not a definitive diagnostic tool for screen addiction, but rather a general assessment tool to promote healthy online habits.
