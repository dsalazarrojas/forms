# facility_upgrade_advisor_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in upgrading their facility. It will guide the user through a series of questions to gather essential details about their facility and provide personalized guidance and advice on upgrading it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This can be a work or personal email address.
4. Select the type of facility you are looking to upgrade.
5. Choose the status of your facility.
6. Select the type(s) of inquiry you have regarding your facility.
7. You can add any additional comments or questions you have about your facility.
8. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`facilty_upgrade_advisor_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`facility_upgrade_advisor_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`facilty_upgrade_advisor_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`facility_upgrade_advisor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Facility type** (`facility_upgrade_advisor_facility_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of facility you are looking to upgrade. Options include 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Facility size** (`facility_upgrade_advisor_facility_size`, select_multiple, required): Select the size of your facility. Options include 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Facility status** (`facility_upgrade_advisor_facility_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your facility. Options include Active and Inactive.
* **Inquiry type** (`facility_upgrade_advisor_inquiry_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type(s) of inquiry you have regarding your facility. Options include 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Additional comments** (`facility_upgrade_advisor_additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about your facility.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* We will review your submission and provide personalized guidance and advice on upgrading your facility.
