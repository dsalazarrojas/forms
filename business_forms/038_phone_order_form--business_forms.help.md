# Phone Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phone Order Form is a tool used to collect information for phone orders from customers. This information will be used to facilitate sales and customer service within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
2. Double-check the form for any errors before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Phone (#1)** (`Company Phone`, number, optional): Enter the phone number of the company that will be receiving the order.
* **Customer Name (#2)** (`Customer Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer placing the order.
* **Customer Email (#3)** (`Customer Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the customer, if applicable.
* **Customer Phone (#4)** (`Customer Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer, if applicable.
* **Customer Address (#5)** (`Customer Address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address, if applicable.
* **Order Type (#6)** (`Order Type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of order being placed, such as "Mobile", "Phone", or "Tablet".
* **Order Quantity (#7)** (`Order Quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of items being ordered.
* **Order Notes (#8)** (`Order Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Business Name (#9)** (`Business Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the business associated with the order, if applicable.
