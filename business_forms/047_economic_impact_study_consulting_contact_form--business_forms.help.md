# Economic Impact Study Consulting Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather contact information from customers interested in consulting services related to economic impact studies. It helps us understand your needs and requirements, enabling us to provide the best possible support and guidance for your project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your contact information, including your email address.
* Provide your project information, such as budget, timeline, and description.
* Select the consulting inquiries that interest you.
* Choose the consulting services you require for your project.
* Review and agree to the terms and conditions. (Please note that there are multiple "Agreed to Terms and Conditions" fields. While they appear redundant, they are essential for us to ensure your understanding of our terms and conditions.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact email address. This is the address we will use to communicate with you about your project.
* **Project Information** (`form_project_information`): Fill in the project details, including budget and timeline. This will help us understand your project requirements.
* **Budget and Timeline** (`form_budget_and_timeline`): Enter your project budget and timeline. This will help us estimate the scope and duration of your project.
* **Email** (`form_email_input`): Enter your email address. This is where we will send you further information and updates about your project.
* **Consulting Inquiries** (`form_consulting_inquiries`): Select one or more consulting inquiries that interest you. This will help us understand your needs and provide you with the most relevant information.
* **Consulting Service** (`form_consulting_service`): Select one or more consulting services you require for your project.
* **Additional Consulting Services** (`form_consulting_service_2`): If you require additional services for your project, select one or more from the options.
* **Project Description** (`form_project_description`): Fill in a brief description of your project. This will help us understand its scope and requirements.
* **Agreed to Terms and Conditions** (Multiple): Please review and agree to each of the terms and conditions. This is essential for us to ensure we are working together under clear and understood expectations.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`): Click this button to submit your form. This will send us your information, and we will review it for further communication and collaboration.
