# Newsletter Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users about the content of a newsletter. It is an optional form that allows users to share their thoughts, opinions, and suggestions about the newsletter's quality, content, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit Feedback" button to access the form.
2. Answer each field according to your thoughts and opinions about the newsletter. You can type freely in the text fields.
3. If you are submitting feedback about a specific newsletter, please enter the name of the newsletter in the "What is the name of the newsletter you are subscribed to?" field.
4. You can provide additional comments or thoughts in the "Please enter your comments" field.
5. If you want to contact us directly, please enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field.
6. You can also provide a phone number for further communication in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think about this newsletter?** (`What do you think about this newsletter?`, text, required: false): Answer this question freely, providing your thoughts and opinions about the newsletter's content, quality, and overall experience.
* **What is the name of the newsletter you are subscribed to?** (`What is the name of the newsletter you are subscribed to?`, text, required: false): If you are providing feedback about a specific newsletter, please enter its name. This will help us to identify which newsletter you are referring to.
* **Please enter your comments** (`Please enter your comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the newsletter. You can type freely and provide as much information as you want.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, required: false): If you want to contact us directly for further communication, please enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required: false): If you would like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number.
