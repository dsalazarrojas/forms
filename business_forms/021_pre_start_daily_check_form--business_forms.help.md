# pre_start_daily_check_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre_start_daily_check_form is a quick and efficient tool for employees to report their workday activities, including work hours, completed tasks, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your weekday status from the "Weekday" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your working hours in the "Working Hours" field.
3. Report the number of hours you worked in the "Hours Worked" field.
4. Check "Yes" or "No" for each completed task in the "Completed Tasks" field.
5. Add any additional notes about your workday in the "Notes" field.
6. Set your start and end times using the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields.
7. Enter the date of the workday in the "Date" field.
8. Enter your name in the "Submitted By" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Weekday** (`Weekday`, select_one): Select "Yes" if you are working on a weekday, "No" otherwise.
* **Working Hours** (`Working Hours`, text): Enter the hours you worked during the workday.
* **Hours Worked** (`Hours Worked`, number): Enter the number of hours you worked during the workday.
* **Completed Tasks** (`Completed Tasks`, select_multiple): Check "Yes" for each task you completed during the workday.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, text): Add any additional notes about your workday.
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, time): Set the time you started work.
* **End Time** (`End Time`, time): Set the time you ended work.
* **Date** (`Date`, date): Enter the date of the workday.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, text): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`Email`, text): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
