# Flood Management Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about flood management complaints, which are reviewed and prioritized by designated authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the flood management issue in the "Description" field.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact" field.
4. (Optional) If you have an email address, enter it in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the date and time of the flood incident in the "Date" and "Time" fields respectively.
6. Indicate the location where the flood occurred in the "Location" field.
7. (Optional) If you have attached a photo related to the incident, add it to the "Photo" field.
8. Select the category of the flood management issue (e.g., Flood, Drainage Issue, Water Quality) in the "Category" field.
9. Select all relevant types of flood (e.g., Storm, Flash, Coastal) in the "Type of Flood" field.
10. Indicate the affected area in the "Affected Area" field.
11. If you submitted this complaint, please enter your name in the "Submitted By" field.
12. Enter the date and time when you submitted this form in the "Submitted Date" and "Submitted Time" fields.
13. Select the priority of the issue (e.g., 1-3) in the "Priority" field.
14. Select the authority that will review and take action on this complaint in the "Assigned To" field.
15. Select the status of the complaint (e.g., Open, Closed, In Progress, On Hold) in the "Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required): Enter your address to help us locate the flood incident.
* **Description** (`Description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the flood incident.
* **Contact** (`Contact`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): If you have an email address, enter it here.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the flood incident.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the flood incident.
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, required): Indicate the location where the flood occurred.
* **Photo** (`Photo`, `note`, optional): (Optional) If you have attached a photo related to the incident, add it here.
* **Category** (`Category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of the flood management issue (e.g., Flood, Drainage Issue, Water Quality).
* **Type of Flood** (`Type of Flood`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant types of flood (e.g., Storm, Flash, Coastal).
* **Affected Area** (`Affected Area`, `text`, required): Indicate the affected area.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, `note`, optional): If you submitted this complaint, enter your name here.
* **Submitted Date** (`Submitted Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you submitted this form.
* **Submitted Time** (`Submitted Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time when you submitted this form.
* **Priority** (`Priority`, `number`, required): Select the priority of the issue (e.g., 1-3).
* **Assigned To** (`Assigned To`, `select_one`, required): Select the authority that will review and take action on this complaint.
* **Status** (`Status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the complaint (e.g., Open, Closed, In Progress, On Hold).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional information or comments.
* **Notes** (`Submit Time`, `time`, optional): (Optional) You may not need this, the field is optional.
* **Submit Time** (`Submit Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time when you submitted this form, this field should only be used if it's really necessary
