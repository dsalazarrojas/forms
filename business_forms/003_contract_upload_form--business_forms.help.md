# Contract Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to upload and manage contracts. It is crucial to fill out all required fields accurately and completely to ensure data integrity.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering your name and email address as the submitter. This is necessary for internal tracking and communication purposes.
2. Enter the title and type of the contract. This will help categorize and classify the contract for easy reference.
3. Provide the contract date and effective and expiration dates if applicable.
4. Enter the contract value (if applicable).
5. Upload the signed contract document.
6. If applicable, upload any supporting documents related to the contract.
7. Enter the counterparty's name and type.
8. Classify the contract as public, internal use only, confidential, or highly confidential.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Name** (`Submitter Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the person uploading the contract.
* **Submitter Email** (`Submitter Email`, email, required): Enter your email address for confirmation and updates.
* **Submitter Phone** (`Submitter Phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for urgent matters.
* **Department** (`Department`, select_one, required): Select your department or division (e.g., Legal, Human Resources, Finance, etc.).
* **Contract Title** (`Contract Title`, text, required): Enter the official title or name of the contract.
* **Contract Type** (`Contract Type`, select_one, required): Select the category of the contract (e.g., Vendor Agreement, Client Contract, etc.).
* **Contract Date** (`Contract Date`, date, required): Enter the date the contract was executed or signed.
* **Effective Date** (`Effective Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the contract takes effect.
* **Expiration Date** (`Expiration Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the contract expires.
* **Contract Value** (`Contract Value`, number, optional): Enter the total monetary value of the contract.
* **Counterparty Name** (`Counterparty Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the other party to the contract.
* **Counterparty Type** (`Counterparty Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of entity the counterparty is (e.g., Corporation, LLC, etc.).
* **Internal Stakeholders** (`Internal Stakeholders`, text, optional): Enter the names of internal team members involved.
* **Contract Document** (`Contract Document`, text, required): Upload the signed contract file.
* **Supporting Documents** (`Supporting Documents`, text, optional): Upload any additional related documents.
* **Document Version** (`Document Version`, text, optional): Enter the version number of the uploaded document.
* **Confidentiality Level** (`Confidentiality Level`, select_one, required): Classify the contract as public, internal use only, confidential, or highly confidential.
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, text, optional): Enter any comments or special instructions related to the contract.
