### Library Daily Attendance Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Library Daily Attendance Tracking Form is used to track the daily attendance of visitors at the library. This form collects information about visitors, their attendance, and any additional comments or notes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, select the "Visitor Information" field and enter the visitor's name and any other relevant details.
2. Next, select the days of the week the visitor attended the library using the "Attendance Log" field. You can choose multiple days if needed.
3. Finally, provide any additional comments or notes about the visitor's visit using the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Information** (`Visitor Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the visitor's name and any other relevant details.
* **Attendance Log** (`Attendance Log`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the days of the week the visitor attended the library. You can choose multiple days if needed.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `text`, required/optional): Leave any other comments or notes about the visitor's visit.

### Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information for each field to ensure accurate tracking of visitor attendance.
* If a visitor had multiple visits, you can select multiple days in the "Attendance Log" field.
* If you have any additional comments or notes about a visitor's visit, use the "Additional Information" field to provide more details.
