# Commercial Investigation Reference Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Commercial Investigation Reference Form is designed to gather information from a reference or professional contact about a subject's performance, conduct, and any known issues. This form helps assess the subject's professionalism, integrity, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Select the relevant options for each field according to your knowledge and relationship with the subject.
3. Provide accurate and concise answers to the questions.
4. Sign and date the form electronically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investigator Name** (`investigator_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the investigator or the person completing the form.
* **Investigation Date** (`investigation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the investigation or the date when you last interacted with the subject.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, required): Enter the reference number of the investigation case.
* **Subject Name** (`subject_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the subject being investigated.
* **Subject Company** (`subject_company`, text, required): Enter the name of the subject's company or organization.
* **Subject Position** (`subject_position`, text, required): Enter the subject's job title or role.
* **Relationship to Subject** (`relationship_to_subject`, select_one, required): Select how you know the subject:
	+ Former Supervisor
	+ Former Co-worker
	+ Former Employee
	+ Business Partner
	+ Client or Customer
	+ Vendor
	+ Professional Associate
	+ Other
* **Relationship Duration** (`relationship_duration`, select_one, required): Select how long you worked with the subject:
	+ Less than 6 months
	+ 6 months - 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 3-5 years
	+ 5+ years
* **Reference Name** (`reference_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person providing the reference.
* **Reference Company** (`reference_company`, text, required): Enter the name of the reference's company or organization.
* **Reference Position** (`reference_position`, text, required): Enter the reference's job title or role.
* **Reference Email** (`reference_email`, email, required): Enter the reference's email address.
* **Reference Phone** (`reference_phone`, text, required): Enter the reference's phone number.
* **Would You Rehire** (`would_rehire`, select_one, required): Select if you would work with the subject again:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Not Sure
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
* **Subject Strengths** (`strengths`, text, required): Enter the subject's strengths or positive qualities.
* **Subject Weaknesses** (`weaknesses`, text, optional): Enter the subject's weaknesses or areas for improvement (optional).
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, select_one, required): Select the subject's overall performance:
	+ Top Performer
	+ Above Average
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor Performer
* **Known Performance Issues** (`known_issues`, select_one, required): Select any known performance issues with the subject:
	+ None
	+ Minor Issues
	+ Moderate Issues
	+ Significant Issues
	+ Prefer Not to Say
* **Disciplinary Actions** (`disciplinary_actions`, select_one, required): Select any disciplinary actions taken against the subject:
	+ None
	+ Verbal Warning
	+ Written Warning
	+ Suspension
	+ Termination
	+ Prefer Not to Say
* **May We Follow Up** (`follow_up_permission`, select_one, required): Select if you agree to be contacted for clarification:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Reference Signature** (`reference_signature`, text, required): Enter the reference's electronic signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form was signed.

Note: This help guide is for the reference or professional contact completing the form. Ensure that the information provided is accurate and relevant to the commercial investigation.
