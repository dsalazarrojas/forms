# equipment_rental_authorization_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Equipment Rental Authorization Form is used to obtain authorization for the rental of company equipment. This form is completed by the customer to provide information about the equipment being rented, such as the rental date, equipment description, type, term, term length, and status. The form also captures the customer's signature, ensuring that the customer acknowledges the rental terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the rental date for the equipment being rented.
2. Provide a detailed description of the equipment being rented.
3. Select the equipment type from the list of options provided.
4. Choose the rental term that applies to the equipment (e.g., short-term, long-term, etc.).
5. Enter the length of the rental term (optional).
6. Select the current status of the equipment (e.g., available, in use, etc.).
7. Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understand the rental terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rental Date** (id: 1, <name>): Enter the date when the equipment is to be delivered or taken in for rental. This is the starting date of the rental period.
* **Equipment Description** (id: 2, <name>): Provide detailed information about the equipment being rented, including its brand, model, and any other relevant details.
* **Equipment Type** (id: 3, <name>): Select the type of equipment from the list of options provided. This helps us ensure that the correct equipment is matched with the correct rental agreement.
* **Rental Term** (id: 4, <name>): Choose the rental term that applies to the equipment. This could be short-term (e.g., a day or week), long-term (e.g., a month or year), or another term specified by the company.
* **Rental Term Length** (id: 5, <name>): If you selected a rental term, you can optionally specify its length. For example, if you chose long-term, you can enter the exact number of months or years.
* **Equipment Status** (id: 6, <name>): Select the current status of the equipment from the list of options provided. This helps us track whether the equipment is available, in use, or not available for rental.
* **Customer Signature** (id: 7, <name>): Please sign and date this form to acknowledge that you have read and understand the rental terms and conditions.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays in processing the rental request. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
