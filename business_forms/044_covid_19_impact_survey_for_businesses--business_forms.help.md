# COVID 19 Impact Survey For Businesses - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess the COVID-19 impact on businesses, gathering information on operational, workforce, financial, and customer and market impacts. This will help in understanding how businesses are faring during this crisis and what kind of support they might need to recover.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing your business's name and type.
2.  Enter your name, email address, and the date of the survey.
3.  Answer each section according to your business's current situation.
   *   Section 1: **Operational Impact**
     *   **Current Operations Status**: Select the status of your business's operations during the pandemic.
     *   **Operating at What Capacity**: If your business is partially operating, enter the percentage or number of employees currently working.
     *   **Revenue Change Compared to Pre-COVID**: Enter the percentage or numerical change in revenue compared to pre-COVID.
   *   Section 2: **Workforce Impact**
     *   **Number of Employees Before COVID-19**: Enter the number of employees before COVID-19.
     *   **Current Number of Employees**: Enter the number of employees currently working.
     *   **Employees Working Remotely**: Enter the percentage or number of employees working remotely.
   *   Section 3: **Financial Impact**
     *   **Main Financial Challenges**: Select all the challenges your business is currently facing.
     *   **Government or Financial Assistance Received**: Select whether you have received assistance.
     *   **Type of Assistance**: If you have received assistance, enter the type.
   *   Section 4: **Customer and Market Impact**
     *   **Customer Demand Change**: Select the change in customer demand.
     *   **Implemented Digital Solutions**: Select whether you have implemented digital solutions.
   *   Section 5: **Future Outlook**
     *   **Expected Recovery Timeline**: Select your expected recovery timeline.
     *   **Business Viability Outlook**: Select your business's viability outlook.
     *   **What Support Would Help Most**: Enter any additional support that would be helpful.
     *   **Additional Feedback**: Enter any additional feedback.
