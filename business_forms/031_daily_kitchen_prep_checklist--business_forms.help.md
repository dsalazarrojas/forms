# Daily Kitchen Prep Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Daily Kitchen Prep Checklist" form is used to gather information about kitchen preparations and settings on a daily basis. It helps kitchen staff or management track and record important details about morning routines, lunch breaks, dinner preparations, kitchen cleanliness, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the "Kitchen Type" to indicate whether the kitchen is commercial, residential, or other.
* Choose the "Kitchen Location" where the kitchen is located.
* Select the "Kitchen Equipment" used in the kitchen.
* Enter the "Kitchen Capacity" of the kitchen.
* Enter the "Kitchen Size" of the kitchen.
* Describe the "Kitchen Layout".
* Select the "Morning Routine" to indicate the daily morning preparations.
* Select the "Lunch Break" to indicate whether a lunch break is taken during the day.
* Enter the "Dinner Prep" to describe the evening preparations.
* Select the "Kitchen Cleaning" to indicate when the kitchen is cleaned (before, after, or both).
* Select the "Trash/Recycling" to indicate whether the kitchen has trash and recycling.
* Enter any "Notes" about the kitchen or preparations.
* Select who the "Assigned To" is responsible for the kitchen preparations.
* Enter the "Date" of the kitchen preparations.
* Enter the "Time" of the kitchen preparations.
* Select who "Submitted By" to indicate who submitted the form.
* Select who "Notes Submitted By" to indicate who submitted notes about the kitchen.
* Select the "Kitchen Status" to indicate whether the kitchen is active, inactive, or unknown.
* Enter any "Comments" about the kitchen or preparations.
* Enter the "Email" and "Phone Number" of the kitchen staff or management.
* Enter the "Address" of the kitchen.
* Enter the "Kitchen Number".
* Enter the "Kitchen Area".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Morning Routine**: Select one of the following options to indicate the daily morning routines: Morning Routine 1, Morning Routine 2, etc.
* **Lunch Break**: Select one or multiple options to indicate whether a lunch break is taken: Break 1, Break 2, Break 3, or No Break.
* **Dinner Prep**: Enter the description of the evening preparations.
* **Kitchen Cleaning**: Select one of the following options to indicate when the kitchen is cleaned: Before Prep, After Prep, or Both.
* **Trash/Recycling**: Select whether the kitchen has trash and recycling: Yes or No.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about the kitchen or preparations.
* **Assigned To**: Select the person responsible for the kitchen preparations: Jim, Jane, or Other.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the kitchen preparations.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the kitchen preparations.
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the form: Jim, Jane, or Other.
* **Notes Submitted By**: Select who submitted notes about the kitchen: Jim, Jane, or Other.
* **Kitchen Status**: Select one of the following options to indicate the kitchen status: Active, Inactive, or Unknown.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the kitchen or preparations.
* **Email**: Enter the email of the kitchen staff or management.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the kitchen staff or management.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the kitchen.
* **Kitchen Number**: Enter the kitchen number.
* **Kitchen Area**: Enter the kitchen area.
* **Kitchen Type**: Select one of the following options to indicate the type of kitchen: Commercial, Residential, or Other.
* **Kitchen Location**: Enter the location of the kitchen.
* **Kitchen Equipment**: Enter the kitchen equipment used.
* **Kitchen Capacity**: Enter the capacity of the kitchen.
* **Kitchen Size**: Enter the size of the kitchen.
* **Kitchen Layout**: Enter the layout of the kitchen.
