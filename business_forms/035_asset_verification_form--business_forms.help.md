# Asset Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Asset Verification Form is used to confirm the details of assets owned or held by a policy holder, ensuring that they match the information stored in the database. This form helps ensure accurate and up-to-date asset information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, including the verification date, verifier name, and asset details such as asset ID, description, and type.
2. Verify the asset's location, acquisition date, original purchase price, and current declared value.
3. Confirm if any modifications have been made to the asset and provide a description of the modifications.
4. Attach any supporting documents such as photos, appraisal reports, and insurance policies.
5. Confirm the ownership and value of the asset, and if any discrepancies exist.
6. Provide a verification result, which will be one of four options: Verified - All Confirmed, Verified with Notes, Partially Verified, or Not Verified.
7. Confirm if any discrepancies exist and provide a description of the issues.
8. Provide any recommendations for further action.
9. Sign and date the form to confirm the verification process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Verification Date** (`1`, `date`, required): Enter the date of verification.
* **Verifier Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the verifier.
* **Verifier Title** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter the job title of the verifier.
* **Company Name** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter the company or insurance name.
* **Policy Number** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter the insurance policy ID.
* **Policy Holder Name** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter the insured party's name.
* **Policy Holder Address** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the insured party's address.
* **Contract Reference** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter the agreement number (if applicable).
* **Asset ID** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter the asset identifier.
* **Asset Description** (`10`, `text`, required): Describe the asset.
* **Asset Type** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of the asset.
*   Available options: Real Property, Vehicle, Equipment, Jewelry, Art or Collectibles, Business Equipment, Inventory, Other.
* **Asset Location** (`12`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the asset.
* **Acquisition Date** (`13`, `date`, required): Enter the date the asset was obtained.
* **Original Purchase Price** (`14`, `number`, required): Enter the original purchase price.
* **Current Declared Value** (`15`, `number`, required): Enter the current declared value.
* **Last Appraisal Date** (`16`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the last appraisal (if applicable).
* **Appraiser Name** (`17`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the appraiser (if applicable).
* **Appraiser Credentials** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter the appraiser's qualifications (if applicable).
* **Current Condition** (`19`, `select_one`, required): Select the current state of the asset.
*   Available options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Damaged.
* **Condition Notes** (`20`, `text`, optional): Describe the state of the asset (if applicable).
* **Have Modifications Been Made** (`21`, `select_one`, required): Select if modifications have been made to the asset.
*   Available options: Yes, No.
* **Modifications Description** (`22`, `text`, optional): Describe the modifications (if applicable).
* **Number of Photos Attached** (`23`, `number`, optional): Attach documentation (if applicable).
* **Supporting Documents** (`24`, `select_multiple`, required): Select supporting documents attached.
*   Available options: Purchase Receipt, Appraisal Report, Title Document, Insurance Policy, Photos, Video, Other.
* **Has Ownership Been Verified** (`25`, `select_one`, required): Confirm ownership.
*   Available options: Yes - Documents Reviewed, Yes - Visually Confirmed, No - Pending.
* **Is Declared Value Confirmed** (`26`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if the declared value is accurate.
*   Available options: Yes - Matches Appraisal, Yes - Within Range, No - Needs Adjustment, Unable to Verify.
* **Verification Result** (`27`, `select_one`, required): Select the overall verification status.
*   Available options: Verified - All Confirmed, Verified with Notes, Partially Verified, Not Verified.
*   **Are There Discrepancies** (`28`, `select_one`, required): Select if discrepancies exist.
*   Available options: Yes, No.
*   **Discrepancies Description** (`29`, `text`, optional): Describe the discrepancies (if applicable).
*   **Recommendations** (`30`, `text`, optional): Suggest further actions (if applicable).
*   **Policy Holder Signature** (`31`, `text`, required): Enter the policy holder's name to confirm.
*   **Verifier Signature** (`32`, `text`, required): Enter the verifier's name to sign and date the form.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Attach supporting documents and photos as applicable.
* Verify the asset's details to ensure accuracy.
* If any discrepancies exist, provide a clear description of the issues.
* Confirm the declared value and ownership of the asset.
* Sign and date the form to confirm the verification process.
