# Business Coaching Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from business owners or managers who are interested in coaching services. It will help us understand their business situation and goals, so we can tailor our coaching approach to their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the client information section, which includes your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Proceed to provide details about your business, such as company name, position, industry, and years in operation.
3. Answer the questions about your business model, revenue, and goals, including your primary goal and specific challenges you face.
4. Select the frequency and format of the sessions, as well as the preferred session duration.
5. Finally, provide any additional information that you think will help us understand your business and coaching needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: This is the first section of the form. Please provide your full name, email address, and phone number.
* **Full Name**: This is a text field where you can enter your full name, including your title or position.
* **Email Address**: This is an email address where we can contact you for further discussions.
* **Phone Number**: Please provide your contact phone number so we can reach out to you for sessions or discussions.
* **Company Name**: This is the name of your business, including any relevant details.
* **Your Position**: This is a text field where you can describe your title or role in the company.
* **Business Information**: This is an optional section where you can provide more details about your business.
* **Industry**: Please select the industry that your business operates in.
* **Years in Business**: This is a select-one field where you can choose how long your business has been operating.
* **Number of Employees**: This is a select-one field where you can select the current size of your team.
* **Annual Revenue**: This is a select-one field where you can approximate your annual revenue.
* **Business Model**: This is a select-one field where you can choose how your business generates income.
* **Coaching Goals**: This is an optional section where you can describe your goals for coaching.
* **Primary Goal**: This is a select-one field where you can choose your main objective for coaching.
* **Specific Challenges**: This is a text field where you can describe the specific challenges you face in your business.
* **Biggest Obstacle**: This is a text field where you can describe what is holding you back the most.
* **Goals Timeline**: This is a select-one field where you can choose when you want to achieve your goals.
* **Success Metrics**: This is an optional section where you can describe how you will measure the success of the coaching sessions.
* **Coaching History**: This is a select-one field where you can choose if you have had a coach before.
* **Previous Coaching Experience**: This is an optional section where you can describe your experience with coaching.
* **Coaching Preferences**: This is an optional section where you can describe how you want to work with a coach.
* **Preferred Session Frequency**: This is a select-one field where you can choose how often you want to meet with a coach.
* **Preferred Session Format**: This is a select-one field where you can choose how you want to meet with a coach.
* **Preferred Session Duration**: This is a select-one field where you can choose how long the sessions should be.
* **Best Availability**: This is a select-multiple field where you can choose the days and times you are available to meet.
* **Investment Readiness**: This is a select-one field where you can choose what you are willing to invest in the coaching program.
* **Commitment Level**: This is a select-one field where you can choose how committed you are to the coaching process.
* **Additional Information**: This is an optional section where you can provide any additional information that will help us understand your business and coaching needs.

## Tips

Please fill out this form completely and honestly, and do not hesitate to reach out if you need any clarification on any of the questions.
