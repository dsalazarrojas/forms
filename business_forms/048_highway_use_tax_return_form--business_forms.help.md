# Highway Use Tax Return Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Highway Use Tax Return Form is a data collection form designed to gather information about a vehicle's highway usage. This form is typically used for tax reporting and auditing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the make of your vehicle.
2. Enter the model of your vehicle.
3. Enter your vehicle's license plate number.
4. Select the start date of your vehicle's usage.
5. Select the end date of your vehicle's usage.
6. Enter the total number of miles driven.
7. Enter the average miles per gallon.
8. Enter the total liters of gas used.
9. Enter the total cost of gas.
10. Enter the total tolls and fees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required): Enter the make of your vehicle, i.e., Toyota, Ford, Honda, etc.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model of your vehicle, i.e., Camry, Mustang, Accord, etc.
* **License Plate Number** (`license_plate_number`, `text`, required): Enter your vehicle's license plate number as it appears on your vehicle's registration.
* **Highway Use Tax Return Start Date** (`highway_use_tax_return_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of your vehicle's usage.
* **Highway Use Tax Return End Date** (`highway_use_tax_return_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of your vehicle's usage.
* **Total Highway Miles Driven** (`total_highway_miles_driven`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of miles your vehicle was driven on highways.
* **Average Miles Per Gallon** (`average_miles_per_gallon`, `number`, required): Enter your vehicle's average miles per gallon.
* **Total Liters of Gas Used** (`total_liters_of_gas_used`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of gas used in liters.
* **Total Gas Cost** (`total_gas_cost`, `number`, required): Enter the total cost of gas consumed.
* **Total Tolls and Tolls Fees** (`total_tolls_and_tolls_fees`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of tolls and fees paid.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to report your vehicle's usage correctly. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to your vehicle's registration or usage records for accurate information.
