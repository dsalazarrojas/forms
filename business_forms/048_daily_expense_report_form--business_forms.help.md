# Daily Expense Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Expense Report Form is designed to collect information about employee expenses. It should be used to report daily expenses, such as office supplies, travel, or meals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the expense in the `date` field.
2. Select the type of expense from the `expenseType` field.
3. Enter the amount of the expense in the `amount` field.
4. Describe the expense in the `description` field.
5. Select a category for the expense from the `category` field.
6. Provide any additional comments or notes about the expense in the `notes` field.
7. If you want to attach a file to the expense report, you can do so in the `fileUpload` field.
8. Finally, click the `submit` button to save your expense report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **date**: Enter the date of the expense, in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`. This will help track the expenses over time.
* **expenseType**: Select the type of expense, such as office supplies, travel, or meals. This will help categorize and track the types of expenses.
* **amount**: Enter the amount of the expense, as a numeric value. This will help track the total expenses.
* **description**: Enter a brief description of the expense, such as "Coffee for a meeting".
* **category**: Select a category for the expense, such as "Office supplies" or "Travel".
* **notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the expense, such as "I will need to purchase more coffee soon".
* **fileUpload**: If you want to attach a file to the expense report, such as a receipt, you can do so here.
* **submit**: Click this button to save your expense report and submit it for processing.
