# Franchise Training Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Franchise Training Material Request Form is a structured format for requesting training materials from franchises. This form is designed to gather essential information to fulfill material requests in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title with a brief description of the materials you are requesting.
2. Enter your contact information, including first name, last name, and email address.
3. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Indicate the type of material you need (True or False).
5. Select the urgency of your request (True or False).
6. State your goals for requesting the materials.
7. Specify the location where you will be using the materials.
8. Add any additional comments or details about your request.
9. Select whether you want to submit the form (True or False).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the materials you are requesting.
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, select multiple, required): Select the type of material you need (True or False).
* **Urgency** (`material_urgency`, select one, required): Select the urgency of your request (True or False).
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, required): Describe your goals for requesting the materials.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Specify the location where you will be using the materials.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or details about your request.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, select one, required): Select whether you want to submit the form (True or False).
* **Additional Documents** (`additional_documents`, text, optional): Attach any additional documents related to your request. 

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to ensure your request is processed efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your franchise representative for assistance.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals before submitting the form.
