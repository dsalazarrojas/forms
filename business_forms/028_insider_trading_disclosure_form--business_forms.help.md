# Insider Trading Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insider Trading Disclosure Form is designed to gather information about trades made by company insiders. This form helps to ensure that all trades are properly documented and reported, as required by applicable laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter your name and the name of the company you represent in the "Trader Name" and "Company Name" fields, respectively.
*   Select the date of the trade from the calendar provided.
*   Choose the type of trade (e.g., Short Selling, Long Selling, Long Buying, or Short Buying) from the drop-down menu.
*   Enter the number of shares involved in the trade.
*   Enter the purchase price and sale price of the shares, if applicable.
*   Calculate the profit or loss from the trade, if applicable.
*   Select the date on which you will make the disclosure.
*   Enter the name of the reporting manager who will review your submission.
*   Confirm that you have made the required certification.
*   If desired, provide any additional comments or remarks about the trade.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Trader Name**: Enter the name of the person making the trade.
*   **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company that owns the shares being traded.
*   **Trade Date**: Select the date on which the trade was made.
*   **Trade Type**: Choose the type of trade made (e.g., Short Selling, Long Selling, Long Buying, or Short Buying).
*   **Number of Shares**: Enter the total number of shares involved in the trade.
*   **Purchase Price**: Enter the price at which the shares were purchased, if applicable.
*   **Sale Price**: Enter the price at which the shares were sold, if applicable.
*   **Profit/Loss**: Enter the profit or loss from the trade, if applicable.
*   **Disclosure Date**: Select the date on which you will make the disclosure.
*   **Reporting Manager**: Enter the name of the reporting manager who will review your submission.
*   **Certification**: Confirm that you have made the required certification.
*   **Remarks**: Provide any additional comments or remarks about the trade, if desired.
*   **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about the trade, if desired.
*   **Submitted By**: Enter your name and title, as a record of who submitted the form.
