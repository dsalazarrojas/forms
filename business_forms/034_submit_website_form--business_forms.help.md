# Submit Website Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Submit Website Form is a tool for users to submit their website information to our system. This form is designed to collect essential details about the website, which will then be analyzed and processed for various business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have not submitted this form previously or that the website information submitted is up-to-date and accurate.
2. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information about your website.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Submit Website Form** (submit_website_form, text, optional)
Enter a brief description of your website, including its title and a short summary of its purpose.

### **User Data** (user_data, text, optional)
Provide any additional information about your website that you think is relevant to our analysis and processing.

### **Keywords** (keywords, text, optional)
Enter a list of relevant keywords related to your website, separated by commas.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear in your descriptions to ensure accurate analysis and processing of your website information.
* If you have any doubts or questions about the form, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
