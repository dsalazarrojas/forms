# Corporate Turnaround Strategy Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from individuals interested in discussing a corporate turnaround strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Contact Information" page with your details, including your name, email, and other contact information.
2. Choose a "Contact Reason" to let us know why you're contacting us.
3. If you have any additional information to share, use the "Additional Information" text field.
4. If you need to attach any files, click on the "File Upload" field.
5. Select a "Submission" option to choose how you'd like to proceed with your contact.
6. Finally, provide any comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information**: A field for you to enter your contact details.
	+ Required: false
* **Contact Reason**: A dropdown menu to select why you're contacting us.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
	+ Required: false
* **Additional Information**: A text field for any additional comments or information.
	+ Required: false
* **File Upload**: A field to attach any files you'd like to share.
	+ Required: false
* **Date**: A field to choose a date.
	+ Required: false
	+ Format: dd/mm/yyyy
* **Time**: A field to choose a time.
	+ Required: false
	+ Format: hh:mm AM/PM
* **Submission**: A dropdown menu to choose how you'd like to proceed.
	+ Options: Select 1, Select 2
	+ Required: false
* **Comments**: A text field for any final comments or feedback.
	+ Required: false

Note: The required fields are not explicitly marked as required in the form configuration, which means that all fields are optional. This form is designed to gather general information, and filling it out is entirely up to the user's discretion.
