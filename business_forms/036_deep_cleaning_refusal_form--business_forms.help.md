# Deep Cleaning Refusal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a client's refusal of a deep cleaning service in a clinical setting. It provides a space to input the client's response, reason for refusal, acknowledgment, and other relevant details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Ensure the client understands the implications of refusing the service.
2.  Fill out the "Client Refusal" field with a clear and concise response.
3.  Provide a suitable reason for refusal in the "Reason for Refusal" field.
4.  Sign the "Acknowledgment" field to confirm understanding.
5.  Ensure the "Signature" field is filled out with a physical signature.
6.  Input the client's name, contact details, email, and phone number accurately.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Client Refusal** (1, Client Refusal, required): The client's response to the deep cleaning service.
*   **Reason for Refusal** (2, Reason for Refusal, required): The client's reason for refusing the service.
*   **Acknowledgment** (3, Acknowledgment, required): A field to confirm the client's understanding of their refusal.
*   **Signature** (4, Signature, required): A field for the client's physical signature.
*   **Client Name** (5, Client Name, required): The client's name for identification purposes.
*   **Contact Details** (6, Contact Details, required): Additional contact details for the client.
*   **Email** (7, Email, required): The client's email address for communication.
*   **Phone** (8, Phone, required): The client's phone number for contact.
## Tips
*   Please ensure the client's response and reason for refusal are clear and concise.
*   Ensure the "Acknowledgment" field is filled out with a clear and accurate understanding of the client's refusal.
*   Ensure the "Signature" field is filled out with a physical signature.
*   Input the client's name, contact details, email, and phone number accurately.
*   This form is intended for use in a clinical setting, so ensure accuracy and attention to detail are maintained.
