# political_contribution_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The political contribution form is used to record and report political contributions made to a campaign or election-related activity. This form is essential for tracking and monitoring financial transactions that may be subject to regulatory requirements and reporting obligations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. On the first page, enter the details of the political activity (e.g., "Campaign Event" or "Political Rally").
3. On the subsequent pages, fill out the required information for the candidate, contributor, and contribution details.
4. Ensure that the contributor's address and email are accurate and complete.
5. Select the correct contribution type (e.g., Cash, In-Kind, Service, or Other).
6. Enter the amount contributed and date of the contribution.
7. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Political Activity
* **political_activity** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the political activity, such as a campaign event or political rally.

### Candidate Name
* **candidate_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the candidate or political organization receiving the contribution.

### Contributor Name
* **contributor_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the individual or entity making the contribution.

### Contributor Address
* **contributor_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the contributor, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.

### Contributor Email
* **contributor_email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the contributor.

### Contribution Type
* **contribution_type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of contribution made (e.g., Cash, In-Kind, Service, or Other).

### Amount Contributed
* **amount_contributed** (Number, Optional): Enter the amount contributed.

### Contribution Date
* **contribution_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the contribution.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of your entries before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* Review the form carefully to avoid missing any important information.
