# disaster_recovery_plan_renewal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the purpose and usage of the disaster recovery plan renewal form. The form is designed to collect essential information from organizations to review and update their disaster recovery plans.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reviewing the form and ensuring you have all the necessary information required.
2. Fill in the organization name in the "Organization" field (page 1).
3. Provide the contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field (page 2).
4. Enter a brief description of your disaster recovery plan in the "Recovery Plan" field (page 4).
5. Specify the date for the review in the "Review Date" field (page 5).
6. Select the review time for the specified date in the "Review Time" field (page 6).
7. Add your digital signature as the submitter in the "Submitter" field (page 7).
8. Add the approver's digital signature in the "Approver" field (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (Organization, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the disaster recovery plan.
* **Recovery Plan** (Recovery Plan, text, required): Provide a brief description of your disaster recovery plan.
* **Review Date** (Review Date, date, required): Specify the date for the review of the disaster recovery plan.
* **Review Time** (Review Time, time, required): Select the review time for the specified date.
* **Submitter** (Submitter, text, required): Add your digital signature as the submitter of the form.
* **Approver** (Approver, text, required): Add the approver's digital signature in this field.
* **Review Date** (Review Date, date, required): Specify the date for the review of the disaster recovery plan.
* **Review Time** (Review Time, time, required): Select the review time for the specified date.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check the information provided to avoid errors.
* Only submit one instance of the form per organization to avoid duplication of efforts.
