# Financial Counseling Referral Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Counseling Referral Intake Form is a tool used by financial advisors to gather essential information about a client's financial situation, goals, and objectives. This form helps the advisor to identify areas that require attention and create a tailored plan to assist the client in achieving their financial goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Review each page carefully**: This form is divided into sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of a client's financial situation.
2. **Answer each question thoroughly**: Provide as much detail as possible, especially when answering questions about your financial assets, income, and goals.
3. **Select relevant options**: When prompted to choose from options, select the ones that best describe your situation.
4. **Double-check your answers**: Review each page to ensure all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (Form Header, Note, Required: False)
	+ This section serves as a brief introduction to the form and explains its purpose.
* **Client Name** (Client Name, Text, Required: False)
	+ Please provide your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Client Email** (Client Email, Email, Required: False)
	+ Enter your email address where we can contact you with updates and information about your account.
* **Client Phone** (Client Phone, Text, Required: False)
	+ Provide your phone number where we can reach you to discuss your account.
* **Business Name** (Business Name, Text, Required: False)
	+ If applicable, provide the name of your company or business.
* **Business Address** (Business Address, Text, Required: False)
	+ If applicable, provide the address of your business.
* **Business Phone** (Business Phone, Text, Required: False)
	+ If applicable, provide the phone number of your business.
* **Financial Advisor** (Financial Advisor, Select One, Required: False)
	+ Please select the financial advisor you have been referred to.
* **Referral Source** (Referral Source, Text, Required: False)
	+ Indicate who referred you to our financial counseling services.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, Date, Required: False)
	+ Provide your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Social Security Number** (Social Security Number, Text, Required: False)
	+ Enter your social security number for identification purposes.
* **Financial Assets** (Financial Assets, Select Multiple, Required: False)
	+ Select all assets that apply to your current financial situation.
* **Employment Status** (Employment Status, Select One, Required: False)
	+ Indicate your current employment status.
* **Employment Income** (Employment Income, Number, Required: False)
	+ Enter your monthly or annual income from your employment.
* **Employment Frequency** (Employment Frequency, Text, Required: False)
	+ Describe your employment schedule (full-time, part-time, etc.).
* **Financial Goals** (Financial Goals, Text, Required: False)
	+ Specify your short-term and long-term financial goals.
* **Financial Objectives** (Financial Objectives, Text, Required: False)
	+ Describe your financial objectives and priorities.
* **Financial Institutions** (Financial Institutions, Select Multiple, Required: False)
	+ Select all financial institutions you are affiliated with.
* **Financial Planning** (Financial Planning, Text, Required: False)
	+ Describe your financial planning approach and preferences.
* **Risk Tolerance** (Risk Tolerance, Select One, Required: False)
	+ Indicate your risk tolerance level.
* **Investment Assets** (Investment Assets, Text, Required: False)
	+ Describe your investment assets.
* **Investment Frequency** (Investment Frequency, Text, Required: False)
	+ Describe your investment frequency.
* **Financial Instruments** (Financial Instruments, Text, Required: False)
	+ Describe the financial instruments you are interested in.
* **Assets Invested** (Assets Invested, Text, Required: False)
	+ Indicate the assets you have invested.
* **Financial Status** (Financial Status, Select One, Required: False)
	+ Indicate your current financial status.

Please note that all fields are optional unless indicated as required.
