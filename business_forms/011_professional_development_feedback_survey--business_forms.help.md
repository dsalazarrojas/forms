# Professional Development Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants on the relevance and effectiveness of a seminar or workshop they have attended. It aims to gather information on the participant's perception of the seminar or workshop, including its overall rating, relevance, and any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have the necessary information ready.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your abilities.
3. Select the most accurate options for each question.
4. Provide clear and concise answers in the text fields.
5. Click "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seminar or Workshop** (`seminar_workshop`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have attended a seminar or workshop, 'No' if you haven't.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, optional): Select one of the two options: 'Good' or 'Fair'. Please provide a subjective rating based on your overall experience.
* **Relevance** (`relevance`, select_multiple, optional): Choose 'Yes' for the topics that you found relevant to your professional development, and 'No' for the topics that you did not find relevant. Select multiple topics if they apply.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience of the seminar or workshop on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and comments about the seminar or workshop.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, optional): Share any feedback or suggestions you have on how the seminar or workshop can be improved.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have on the seminar or workshop.

## Tips

* Please be honest and truthful in your answers.
* Try to provide specific examples or examples when answering the questions.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* All answers will be kept confidential and used to improve the seminar or workshop offerings.
