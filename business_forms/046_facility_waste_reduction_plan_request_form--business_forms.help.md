# Facility Waste Reduction Plan Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information for a business facility's waste reduction plan request.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your facility's name in the **Facility Name** field.
2. Provide a brief description of your sustainability project proposal in the **Sustainability Project Proposal** field.
3. Add a valid date for your proposal in the **Proposal Date** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Add a valid time for your proposal in the **Proposal Time** field in the format 08:30.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility making the request.
* **Sustainability Project Proposal**: Describe the proposed project or plan to reduce waste at your facility.
* **Facility Waste Reduction Plan**: This field might be optional, please use it to add additional details about the plan.
* **Proposal Date**: Enter the date when the proposal was made.
* **Proposal Time**: Enter the time when the proposal was made.

## Tips
Please ensure that all the required fields are correctly filled out. If a field is not required but still needed for your proposal, please enter it. If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant department or team.
