# vehicle-repair-billing-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle-repair-billing-form is used to document vehicle repairs and billing information for customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the first page of the form, which is the initial contact and vehicle information section.
2. Proceed to the second page to enter repair details and cost information.
3. Finally, review and confirm your entries on the third page before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Vehicle Information
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of vehicle being repaired, such as car, truck, or motorcycle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of the vehicle, e.g., Toyota, Ford, or Honda.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the vehicle, e.g., Corolla, F-150, or Civic.

### Repair Details
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, text, optional): Enter the date of the service or repair.
* **Repair Description** (`repair_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the repair, including parts replaced or services performed.
* **Parts Replaced** (`parts_replaced`, text, optional): List the parts replaced during the repair, if any.
* **Labor Charges** (`labor_charges`, text, optional): Enter any labor charges associated with the repair.

### Billing Information
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer or vehicle owner.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address for billing purposes.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the customer's contact number for communication.

### Confirm
* **Submit** (submit, optional): Review and confirm the entered information before submitting the form.
