# wholesale_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for wholesale applications, gathering information from applicants such as store names, addresses, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out each page with the required information.
2.  Start from the first page (Page 1) and move through each subsequent page in order.
3.  Fill out all the text fields with the relevant information.
4.  For dropdown options, select the most accurate option for each choice.
5.  Ensure that you fill out all required fields, as marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1: wholesale_application_form** (`wholesale_application_form`, `text`, not required): Enter the title of your wholesale application.
* **Page 2: store_name** (`store_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of your store.
* **Page 3: store_address** (`store_address`, `text`, not required): Enter the address of your store.
* **Page 4: store_city** (`store_city`, `text`, not required): Enter the city where your store is located.
* **Page 5: store_province** (`store_province`, `text`, not required): Enter the province where your store is located.
* **Page 6: store_zip** (`store_zip`, `number`, not required): Enter the zip code of your store's location.
* **Page 7: store_phone** (`store_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the phone number of your store.
* **Page 8: store_email** (`store_email`, `email`, not required): Enter the email of your store's contact person.
* **Page 9: company_name** (`company_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
* **Page 10: business_number** (`business_number`, `text`, not required): Enter your business number (if applicable).
* **Page 11: wholesale_application_form_page_11** (`wholesale_application_form_page_11`, `select_one`, not required): Select the country in which your store is located (Canada, USA, Mexico).
* **Page 12: wholesale_application_form_page_12** (`wholesale_application_form_page_12`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select any additional countries in which you have a presence or operate.
* **Page 13: wholesale_application_form_page_13** (`wholesale_application_form_page_13`, `date`, not required): Enter a date relevant to your wholesale application.
* **Page 14: wholesale_application_form_page_14** (`wholesale_application_form_page_14`, `time`, not required): Enter a time relevant to your wholesale application.
* **Page 15: wholesale_application_form_page_15** (`wholesale_application_form_page_15`, `note`, not required): Enter any additional information or comments relevant to your wholesale application.
* **Page 16: wholesale_application_form_page_16** (`wholesale_application_form_page_16`, `number`, not required): Enter a numerical value relevant to your wholesale application.
* **Page 17: wholesale_application_form_page_17** (`wholesale_application_form_page_17`, `select_one`, not required): Select an option relevant to your wholesale application.
* **Page 18: wholesale_application_form_page_18** (`wholesale_application_form_page_18`, `text`, not required): Enter a piece of information relevant to your wholesale application.
* **Page 19: wholesale_application_form_page_19** (`wholesale_application_form_page_19`, `text`, not required): Enter a further piece of information relevant to your wholesale application.
* **Page 20: wholesale_application_form_page_20** (`wholesale_application_form_page_20`, `select_one`, not required): Select a final option relevant to your wholesale application.
* **Page 21: wholesale_application_form_page_21** (`wholesale_application_form_page_21`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select multiple options relevant to your wholesale application.
* **Page 22: wholesale_application_form_page_22** (`wholesale_application_form_page_22`, `select_one`, not required): Select a single option relevant to your wholesale application.
* **Page 23: wholesale_application_form_page_23** (`wholesale_application_form_page_23`, `select_one`, not required): Select a final option relevant to your wholesale application.
* **Page 24: wholesale_application_form_page_24** (`wholesale_application_form_page_24`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select multiple options relevant to your wholesale application.
* **Page 25: wholesale_application_form_page_25** (`wholesale_application_form_page_25`, `select_one`, not required): Select the last option relevant to your wholesale application.
