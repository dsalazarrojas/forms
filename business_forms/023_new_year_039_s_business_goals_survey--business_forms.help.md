# New Year's Business Goals Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Year's Business Goals Survey is a tool used to gather information on the company's business goals and objectives for the next year, and to identify the processes and strategies in place to achieve them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form carefully and answer all questions thoroughly.
2. Fill out each field with as much detail as possible.
3. Select all relevant options for each question that apply to your business.
4. Be sure to explain your answers to any questions that require a text response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What are your main business goals for the next year? (`new_years_business_goals`)
* This field is where you should list your main business goals for the next year. Be as specific as possible and try to identify no more than 3-5 key goals.

### What are your top business objectives? (`business_objectives`)
* This field is where you select the top business objectives that align with your main business goals. Select all objectives that apply.

### What are the challenges you expect to face in the next year? (`expected_challenges`)
* This field is where you list any challenges you expect to face in the next year. Select all challenges that apply.

### How do you see your business goals aligning with your organizational mission? (`business_goals`)
* This field is where you explain how your business goals align with your organizational mission.

### How will you measure and track progress towards your business goals? (`performance_indicators`)
* This field is where you select the methods you will use to measure and track progress towards your business goals.

### How do you see your business goals aligning with your organizational mission? (`goal_alignment`)
* This field is where you explain how your business goals align with your organizational mission.

### What are your long-term business aspirations? (`business_aspirations`)
* This field is where you list your long-term business aspirations.

### What is your goal-setting process? (`goal_setting_process`)
* This field is where you select the process you will use to set your business goals.

### How will you ensure that all relevant stakeholders are informed and aligned with your business goals? (`communication_planning`)
* This field is where you select the methods you will use to communicate your business goals to all relevant stakeholders.

### How will you ensure that you achieve your business goals? (`business_goals_achievement`)
* This field is where you select the methods you will use to achieve your business goals.

### What are some of the key performance indicators (KPIs) you will use to measure business progress? (`performance_improvement`)
* This field is where you select the KPIs you will use to measure business progress.

### How will you resource your business goals? (`resource_allocation`)
* This field is where you list the resources needed to achieve your business goals.

### What potential risks do you foresee in achieving your business goals? (`risk_management`)
* This field is where you list any potential risks that may affect achieving your business goals.

### How will you manage change and adaptation throughout the year? (`change_management`)
* This field is where you select the methods you will use to manage change and adaptation throughout the year.

### What is your current level of organizational capacity for change management? (`organizational_capacity`)
* This field is where you list your current level of organizational capacity for change management.

### What organizational structure do you think will be most effective in supporting your business goals? (`organizational_structure`)
* This field is where you select the organizational structure that will be most effective in supporting your business goals.

### How will you involve stakeholders in achieving your business goals? (`stakeholder_involvement`)
* This field is where you select the methods you will use to involve stakeholders in achieving your business goals.

### What resources (financial, personnel, etc.) do you think will be required to achieve your business goals? (`resource_commitment`)
* This field is where you list the resources needed to achieve your business goals.

### How will you allocate your budget to support business goals? (`budget_allocation`)
* This field is where you select the budget allocation method you will use to support your business goals.

### What is your budgeting process? (`budgeting`)
* This field is where you select the budgeting process you will use to achieve your business goals.
