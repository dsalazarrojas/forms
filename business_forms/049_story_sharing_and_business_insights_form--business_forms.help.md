# Story Sharing And Business Insights Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the effectiveness of story sharing and its impact on business growth. It is intended to gather data on customer stories, business insights, data collection, and other related metrics.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please complete each field carefully and accurately, based on your experience and knowledge.
- Make sure to select all applicable options in the "customer_stories" and "effective_data_collection" fields.
- Choose the correct response in the "effective_data", "engagement_growth", and "driving_growth" fields based on your understanding of the story sharing and business growth.
- Enter your email address and phone number in the "email" and "phone" fields, if applicable.
- Use the "notes" field for any additional information that you want to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **story_sharing**: A short text describing the story sharing process. (text, required: false)
    * This field is intended to describe the story sharing process, if applicable. Please keep it brief.
    * Examples: "We have an in-house story sharing platform.", "We use a third-party tool for story sharing."

- **business_insights**: A short text describing the business insights gained from the story sharing process. (text, required: false)
    * This field is intended to describe the business insights gained from the story sharing process, if applicable. Please keep it brief.
    * Examples: "Better alignment between teams.", "More accurate customer data collection."

- **customer_stories**: A list of customer stories that have been shared. (select_multiple, required: false)
    * Select all applicable customer stories from the list.
    * Examples: "Customer A", "Customer B", "Customer C"

- **business_growth**: A numerical value indicating the growth of the business. (number, required: false)
    * Enter the growth value of the business.
    * Examples: "10%", "20%"

- **customer_insights**: A selection of customer insights gained from the story sharing process. (select_one, required: false)
    * Select the customer insights that apply.
    * Examples: "Yes", "No"

- **effective_data_collection**: A list of effective data collection methods. (select_multiple, required: false)
    * Select all applicable data collection methods.
    * Examples: "Yes", "No"

- **engagement_growth**: A selection of engagement growth indicators. (select_one, required: false)
    * Select the engagement growth indicators that apply.
    * Examples: "Yes", "No"

- **driving_growth**: A date of the story sharing process. (date, required: false)
    * Enter the date of the story sharing process.
    * Examples: "2022-01-01"

- **effective_data**: A selection of effective data. (select_one, required: false)
    * Select the effective data that apply.
    * Examples: "Yes", "No"

- **email**: The email address of the user. (email, required: false)
    * Enter your email address.
    * Examples: "john.doe@example.com"

- **phone**: The phone number of the user. (text, required: false)
    * Enter your phone number.
    * Examples: "123-456-7890"

- **notes**: Any additional information. (note, required: false)
    * Use this field for any additional information or comments.
    * Examples: "Additional comments or information."

- **assigned_tool**: A selection of the assigned tool. (select_one, required: false)
    * Select the tool that was assigned.
    * Examples: "Yes", "No"

- **created_by**: A selection of who created the story sharing process. (select_one, required: false)
    * Select who created the story sharing process.
    * Examples: "Yes", "No"

- **updated_by**: A selection of who updated the story sharing process. (select_one, required: false)
    * Select who updated the story sharing process.
    * Examples: "Yes", "No"

- **created_at**: A date of when the story sharing process was created. (date, required: false)
    * Enter the date of when the story sharing process was created.
    * Examples: "2022-01-01"

- **updated_at**: A date of when the story sharing process was updated. (date, required: false)
    * Enter the date of when the story sharing process was updated.
    * Examples: "2022-01-01"

- **created_by_team**: A selection of who created the story sharing process. (select_one, required: false)
    * Select who created the story sharing process.
    * Examples: "Yes", "No"
