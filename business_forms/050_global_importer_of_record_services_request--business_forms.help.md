# Global Importer Of Record Services Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from a company's representatives to request Global Importer Of Record (GIR) services for their products. This includes details about the company, contact person, shipment, and terms of agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about your company.
2. Provide the name of the contact person responsible for the shipment.
3. Enter the company's address and email address where the shipment will be received.
4. Enter the shipment date and time.
5. Enter the product description, weight, and value.
6. Confirm the shipment terms and conditions.
7. Review the agreement terms and conditions and confirm you have read them.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the shipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter the name of your company as it appears on official documents.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, Text, **Required**): Enter the full address where the shipment will be received.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, Text, **Required**): Enter the name of the person responsible for the shipment.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, **Required**): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, **Required**): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Shipment Route** (`shipment_route`, Text, **Required**): Enter the details about the shipment route, including any specific delivery instructions.
* **Shipment Date** (`shipment_date`, Date, **Required**): Enter the date the shipment is expected to arrive.
* **Shipment Time** (`shipment_time`, Time, **Required**): Enter the time the shipment is expected to arrive.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, Text, **Required**): Enter a detailed description of the products being shipped.
* **Product Weight** (`product_weight`, Number, **Required**): Enter the weight of the products being shipped.
* **Product Value** (`product_value`, Number, **Required**): Enter the value of the products being shipped.
* **Terms Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, Select One, **Required**): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the shipment.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, Text, **Optional**): If you have any comments or concerns about the terms and conditions, enter them here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Text, **Optional**): Enter any additional comments or information regarding the shipment.
* **Shipment Terms** (`shipment_terms`, Select Multiple, **Optional**): Confirm any additional shipment terms or requirements.
