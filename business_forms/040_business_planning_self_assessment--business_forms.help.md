# Business Planning Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Planning Self-Assessment is designed to evaluate your business's performance in various areas, providing an overview of its strengths and weaknesses. This tool helps identify areas that need improvement and provides guidance for business planning and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question truthfully, using the provided scale of 1-5 or free-text fields.
2. Complete all required fields.
3. Answer each question based on your current business situation and knowledge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`): This is the date you're completing this assessment.
	+ Please enter the date in `mm/dd/yyyy` format.
* **Business Owner Name** (`business_owner_name`): Your full name as the business owner.
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your business records.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`): The name of your business.
	+ Enter the name of your business as it appears on your business records or public-facing materials.
* **Business Age** (`business_age`): The number of years your business has been in operation.
	+ Enter the number of years since your business started.
* **Clarity of Business Objectives** (`objective_clarity`): Rate how clear your business objectives are.
	+ Use the scale of 1 (not clear at all) to 5 (very clear).
* **Team Alignment on Objectives** (`objective_alignment`): Rate how well your team is aligned on your business objectives.
	+ Use the scale of 1 (not aligned at all) to 5 (very well aligned).
* **Understanding of Target Market** (`market_understanding`): Rate your knowledge of your target market.
	+ Use the scale of 1 (not well understood at all) to 5 (very well understood).
* **Quality of Market Analysis** (`market_analysis_quality`): Rate the quality of your market analysis.
	+ Use the scale of 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Knowledge of Competitors** (`competitor_knowledge`): Rate your knowledge of your competitors.
	+ Use the scale of 1 (not well known at all) to 5 (very well known).
* **Confidence in Financial Projections** (`financial_projections_confidence`): Rate your confidence in your financial projections.
	+ Use the scale of 1 (not confident at all) to 5 (very confident).
* **Financial Management Competence** (`financial_management_competence`): Rate your financial management competence.
	+ Use the scale of 1 (not competent at all) to 5 (very competent).
* **Marketing Strategy Clarity** (`marketing_strategy_clarity`): Rate how clear your marketing strategy is.
	+ Use the scale of 1 (not clear at all) to 5 (very clear).
* **Confidence in Sales Projections** (`sales_projection_confidence`): Rate your confidence in your sales projections.
	+ Use the scale of 1 (not confident at all) to 5 (very confident).
* **Operational Readiness** (`operational_readiness`): Rate your business's operational readiness.
	+ Use the scale of 1 (not ready at all) to 5 (very ready).
* **Resource Availability** (`resource_availability`): Rate your availability of necessary resources.
	+ Use the scale of 1 (not available at all) to 5 (very available).
* **Team Capability and Experience** (`team_capability`): Rate your team's capability and experience.
	+ Use the scale of 1 (not capable at all) to 5 (very capable).
* **Risk Awareness and Mitigation** (`risk_awareness`): Rate your understanding of risks and ability to mitigate them.
	+ Use the scale of 1 (not aware at all) to 5 (very aware).
* **Realistic Planning Timeline** (`planning_timeline`): Rate your planning timeline's realism.
	+ Use the scale of 1 (not realistic at all) to 5 (very realistic).
* **Overall Business Readiness** (`overall_readiness`): Rate your business's overall readiness.
	+ Use the scale of 1 (not ready at all) to 10 (very ready).
* **Key Planning Strengths** (`key_strengths`): List your business's key planning strengths.
	+ Enter up to 3 key strengths, separated by commas (e.g., "Strong customer relationships, efficient operations, competitive pricing").
* **Areas of Concern** (`areas_of_concern`): List areas that concern you about your business.
	+ Enter up to 3 areas, separated by commas (e.g., "Competition, funding, market fluctuations").
* **Areas Needing Improvement** (`improvement_areas`): List areas that need improvement in your business.
	+ Enter up to 3 areas, separated by commas (e.g., "Sales strategy, employee retention, supply chain efficiency").
* **Types of Support Needed** (`support_needed`): List any types of support you need for your business.
	+ Enter up to 3 types, separated by commas (e.g., "Financial guidance, marketing expertise, mentorship").
* **Next Planning Steps** (`next_steps`): Describe your next steps for your business.
	+ Enter a brief description of your next steps, including any specific actions or timelines.

## Tips

* Be honest when answering each question, and provide specific examples when possible.
* Use the scales provided for numerical fields to rate your answers objectively.
* For free-text fields, be concise and clear in your descriptions.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask your business advisor for guidance.
* Review your answers and ensure they accurately reflect your business situation before submitting the assessment.
