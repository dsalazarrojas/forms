# Procurement Payment Audit Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate accurate and efficient payment auditing for procurements. Its completion is crucial for reviewing and verifying payments to suppliers. Only authorized personnel should fill out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form carefully and accurately to provide essential details about the payment.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions and clearance to access and complete this form.
- Review each field for its intended purpose and fill it out accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supplier associated with the payment.
- **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the payment was made.
- **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, text, optional): Input the amount paid to the supplier.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select one, optional): Select the method used to make the payment (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
- **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select multiple, optional): Indicate the status of the payment (e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive').
- **Reviewer Comment** (`payment_comment`, note, optional): Enter any comments or observations about the payment.
- **Reference Number** (`reference_number`, text, optional): Enter any relevant reference number for the payment.
- **Reviewer Comment** (`reviewer_comment`, note, optional): Enter any comments or observations about the audit process.
- **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, text, optional): Enter your reviewer signature.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select one, optional): Indicate whether a specific tool was used to make the payment (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
- **Review Type** (`review_type`, select one, optional): Indicate the type of review conducted (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
