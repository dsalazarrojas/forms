# Safety & Compliance Officer Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow employees or other stakeholders to report or inquire about safety and compliance concerns or incidents, or to contact safety and compliance officers or reporting officers for follow-up or further assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in any applicable text fields with the relevant information (e.g., incident report, safety concerns, or contact information).
* Select the correct options for the "Safety Concerns" field, indicating whether you are reporting a concern, unsure, or neither.
* If contacting a reporting officer, select the relevant contact method (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* If reporting an incident, provide the necessary details in the "Incident Report" field.
* If you have compliance questions, provide them in the "Compliance Questions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Form** (Text, Optional): A text field for any general comments or information.
* **Safety Concerns** (Select One, Optional): Indicate whether you are reporting a safety concern, unsure, or neither.
* **Incident Report** (Text, Optional): Provide details about the incident, including any relevant information or circumstances.
* **Compliance Questions** (Text, Optional): If you have questions or concerns about company policies or procedures, provide them here.
* **Contact Information** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* **Follow-up** (Date, Optional): If you are expecting a follow-up from the safety and compliance officers, select a date for when you would like to be contacted.
* **Reporting Officer Name** (Text, Optional): Provide the name of the reporting officer who should be contacted.
* **Reporting Officer Phone** (Text, Optional): If you have the reporting officer's phone number, provide it here.
* **Reporting Officer Email** (Email, Optional): If you have the reporting officer's email address, provide it here.
* **Reporting Officer Location** (Text, Optional): If you have the reporting officer's location, provide it here.
* **Reporting Officer Department** (Select One, Optional): If you know the reporting officer's department, select it here.
* **Reporting Officer Job Title** (Text, Optional): If you have the reporting officer's job title, provide it here.
* **Contact Method** (Select Multiple, Optional): If you are contacting a reporting officer, choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).

## Tips
* Be as specific and detailed as possible when providing information in the text fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, select the "Unsure" option in the "Safety Concerns" field.
* Make sure to select the correct contact method if you are expecting a follow-up from the safety and compliance officers.
* This form is intended for internal use within the organization. If you are not an employee or a stakeholder, please do not use this form.
