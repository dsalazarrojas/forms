# Non Debarment Certification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non Debarment Certification Form is used by government agencies to collect information from companies that have been debarred or excluded from receiving government contracts due to non-compliance with regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review the form instructions and ensure that all fields are completed accurately and completely.
2. Fill in your company's contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Fill in the date of certification in the "Date of Certfication" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Non-Debarment Form**: This field is the title of the form and is not editable. Please leave this field as it is.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your company's contact information, including name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the individual completing the form or the name of the company representative.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your company or contact person.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person or company representative.
* **Date of Certfication**: Enter the date on which the debarment certification was issued. This field is a date field and accepts date formats in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and consistently.
* This form is intended for debarment certification purposes only, and any other uses may be subject to rejection or correction.
* Please review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors or rejections.
