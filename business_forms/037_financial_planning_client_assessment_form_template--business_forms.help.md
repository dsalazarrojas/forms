<thinking>Financial Planning Client Assessment Form Template - Help Guide

The purpose of this form template is to collect essential information about a client's financial situation, goals, and preferences. This form is designed to help the client and financial advisor understand each other's expectations and create a tailored financial plan that meets the client's needs.

Before filling out this form, please ensure that you have reviewed and understood the provided instructions and the client's previous responses to ensure that the information is accurate and complete. This form is intended to be used as a starting point for financial planning discussions and may be modified or expanded upon as needed.

If you are a financial advisor, please review the form carefully and make sure to explain each field and section to the client before proceeding.

If you are a client, please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. The more accurate and complete the information provided, the more effective the financial planning process will be.

If you have any questions or concerns while filling out this form, do not hesitate to ask for clarification or assistance.

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each section carefully, making sure to provide as much detail as possible.
2. If a field is marked as required, it is essential that you provide a response to ensure the financial planning process can proceed.
3. If you are unsure about any field or section, please ask for clarification or assistance.
4. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully to ensure that all fields and sections have been filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Planning Form (financial_planning_form)**: This is the main section of the form where you will provide general financial information, such as income, expenses, financial goals, and risk tolerance.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: Please provide your contact information, including name, address, phone number, and email address. This will help the financial advisor communicate with you effectively.
* **Income (income)**: Please enter your monthly income. This information is essential for understanding your financial situation and creating a comprehensive financial plan.
* **Expenses (expenses)**: Enter your monthly expenses. This will help the financial advisor understand your financial obligations and create a plan that takes into account your expenses.
* **Financial Goals (financial_goals)**: Please select the financial goals that apply to you. This will help the financial advisor understand your priorities and create a plan that aligns with your goals.
* **Risk Tolerance (risk_tolerance)**: Please select your risk tolerance. This will help the financial advisor understand your comfort level with risk and create a plan that suits your risk profile.
* **Financial History (financial_history)**: Please enter your financial history, including any notable financial events or milestones.
* **Assets (assets)**: List your assets, such as savings accounts, investments, and real estate.
* **Liabilities (liabilities)**: List your liabilities, such as debts, loans, and credit cards.
* **Financial Institutions (financial_institutions)**: Please select the financial institutions you have a relationship with, such as banks, insurance companies, or investment firms.
* **Financial Partners (financial_partners)**: Please select your financial partners, such as spouse, business partner, or family members.
* **Tax Identification (tax_identification)**: Please select your tax identification status.
* **Employment Status (employment_status)**: Please select your employment status.
* **Income Frequency (income_frequency)**: Please select your income frequency.
* **Financial Institutions (financial_institutions)**: Please select your financial institutions.
* **Investment Goals (investment_goals)**: Please select your investment goals.
* **Income Streams (income_streams)**: Please select your income streams.
* **Assets Owned (assets_owned)**: Please select your assets owned.
* **Financial Institutional Investments (financial_institutional_investments)**: Please select your financial institutional investments.
* **Risk-Free Investment (risk_free_investment)**: Please select your risk-free investment.
* **Alternative Investment (alternative_investment)**: Please select your alternative investment.
* **Financial Institutional Investment Type (financial_institutional_investments_type)**: Please select your financial institutional investment type.
* **Risk-Free Investment Type (risk_free_investment_type)**: Please select your risk-free investment type.

* **Financial Planning Recommendations (financial_planning_recommendations)**: Please select any additional financial planning recommendations you have.
