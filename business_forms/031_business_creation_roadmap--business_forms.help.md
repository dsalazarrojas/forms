# Business Creation Roadmap - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Creation Roadmap form is designed to guide entrepreneurs through a structured process of defining their business vision, goals, and plans.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a different aspect of your business. Please answer each question carefully and thoroughly, ensuring that you provide as much detail as possible. 

1. Please provide your contact information (Entrepreneur Name, Email Address, and Phone Number) so that we can get in touch with you.
2. Select your level of business experience.
3. Enter your business idea and proposed business name.
4. Describe your business concept, industry, and target market.
5. Define your competitive advantage and business model.
6. Set goals and milestones for the first year and three years.
7. Plan your launch date and major milestones.
8. Determine your funding requirements and planned sources of capital.
9. Calculate your estimated startup cost and current funding gap.
10. Project your first year revenue.
11. Describe your team structure and current team members.
