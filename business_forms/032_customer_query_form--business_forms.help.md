# Customer Query Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the form is to enable customers to submit their queries or feedback regarding any aspect of our business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Customer Query Form" from the list of available forms.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and clear information.
3. Press the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the customer's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you regarding your query.
* **Query** (`query`, text, required: false): Describe your query or concern in this field.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you if required.
* **Email or Phone** (`email_phone`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide an alternative contact method if you have not provided an email or phone number.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form with accurate and clear information to facilitate efficient processing.
* You can always contact the support department directly if you face any issues during the submission process.
