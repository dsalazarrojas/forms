# Smart Real Estate Compliance Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Smart Real Estate Compliance Application Form is designed to gather essential information from real estate agents or brokers who wish to apply for smart real estate compliance. This form will help us assess your eligibility and ensure that you meet the necessary requirements for compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill out the **Applicant Information** page with your name, agency name, and contact details.
2.  On the **Property Information** page, provide the property address and details about the property you are applying for.
3.  The **Agreement** page is where you can outline the terms and conditions of your agreement with the client.
4.  The **Contact Information** page is where you can provide additional contact details for follow-up or communication.
5.  Finally, provide any additional **Scheduling Information** on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information (Applicant Information)** (`title`, `text`, required): This is where you should enter your name and agency name to identify yourself.
* **Property Information (Property Information)** (`property_info`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the property you are applying for.
* **Agreement (Agreement)** (`agreement`, `text`, required): This is where you outline the terms and conditions of your agreement with the client.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Provide additional contact details for follow-up or communication.
* **Scheduling Information (Scheduling Information)** (`scheduling`, `text`, required): If there are any scheduling requirements or restrictions, enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields with accurate and complete information.
* Ensure that you are eligible to fill out the form before submitting.
* Review your application before submitting it to ensure everything is correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
