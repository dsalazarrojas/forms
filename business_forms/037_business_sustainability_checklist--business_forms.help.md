# Business Sustainability Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a company's business sustainability practices across various categories. It's meant to be a self-assessment tool for companies to evaluate their own sustainability performance, identify areas for improvement, and make informed decisions to reduce their environmental, social, and governance (ESG) impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company's name in the "Company Name" field (Field 1).
2. Select the date of the assessment in the "Assessment Date" field (Field 2).
3. Answer each of the questions in the remaining fields, selecting the best option that describes your company's current practices.
4. In the "Top Sustainability Priorities" field (Field 19), enter your company's top sustainability priorities as a text response.
5. In the "Resource Constraints" field (Field 20), describe any resource constraints that might limit your company's sustainability efforts as a text response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Field 1): Enter your company's name to identify the company completing the assessment.
* **Assessment Date** (Field 2): Select the date when this sustainability assessment is being completed.
* **Energy Efficiency Initiatives** (Field 3): Describe the level of energy efficiency initiatives your company has implemented, such as Comprehensive Program, Active Initiatives, Some Measures, Minimal Effort, or None.
* **Waste Reduction Program** (Field 4): Indicate the level of waste reduction efforts your company has in place, such as Comprehensive Program, Active Program, Some Measures, Minimal, or None.
* **Water Conservation** (Field 5): Select the level of water conservation practices your company has implemented, such as Active Measures, Some Measures, Minimal Measures, or No Measures.
* **Carbon Emissions Reduction** (Field 6): Describe the level of carbon emissions reduction efforts your company has, such as Active Reduction Program, Some Initiatives, Minimal Efforts, or No Action.
* **Sustainable Materials Use** (Field 7): Indicate the level of sustainable materials use in your company, such as Extensive Use, Regular Use, Some Use, Minimal, or None.
* **Employee Health and Wellness** (Field 8): Select the level of employee health and wellness initiatives your company promotes, such as Comprehensive Program, Active Program, Some Initiatives, Minimal, or None.
* **Diversity and Inclusion Practices** (Field 9): Describe the level of diversity and inclusion practices your company has in place, such as Comprehensive Program, Strong Program, Some Efforts, Minimal, or None.
* **Community Engagement** (Field 10): Indicate the level of community engagement your company participates in, such as Active Engagement, Regular Activities, Some Participation, Minimal, or None.
* **Fair Labor Practices** (Field 11): Select the level of fair labor practices your company has, such as Comprehensive Standards, Strong Policies, Adequate Standards, Minimal Standards, or None.
* **Transparency and Disclosure** (Field 12): Describe the level of transparency and disclosure your company has, such as Full Transparency, High Transparency, Adequate, Limited, or Minimal.
* **Business Ethics and Compliance** (Field 13): Indicate the level of business ethics and compliance policies your company has in place, such as Strong Policies, Good Policies, Adequate, Limited, or None.
* **Board Diversity** (Field 14): Select the level of board diversity, such as Excellent Diversity, Good Diversity, Adequate, Limited Diversity, or No Diversity.
* **Accountability and Oversight** (Field 15): Describe the level of accountability and oversight systems your company has, such as Comprehensive Systems, Strong Systems, Adequate, Limited, or None.
* **Sustainable Supply Chain** (Field 16): Indicate the level of sustainable supply chain assessment your company conducts, such as Comprehensive Assessment, Regular Assessment, Some Assessment, Minimal, or None.
* **Sustainability Reporting and Metrics** (Field 17): Select the level of sustainability reporting and metrics your company tracks, such as Comprehensive Reporting, Regular Reporting, Some Metrics, Minimal, or None.
* **Formal Sustainability Goals** (Field 18): Describe the level of formal sustainability goals your company has set, such as Clear Goals with Targets, Well-defined Goals, General Goals, Vague Goals, or No Goals.
* **Top Sustainability Priorities** (Field 19): Enter your company's top sustainability priorities as a text response.
* **Resource Constraints** (Field 20): Describe any resource constraints that might limit your company's sustainability efforts as a text response.

## Tips
* Answer each question with the option that best describes your company's current practices.
* If you're unsure about an answer, choose the option that most closely aligns with your company's practices.
* Review and revise your answers as needed, considering your company's specific circumstances.
