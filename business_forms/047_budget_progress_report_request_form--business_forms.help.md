# Budget Progress Report Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a budget progress report from the finance department. The report will provide an overview of the current budget status for a specific project or department, and will include the requested information and analysis to help stakeholders make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address to identify yourself as the requester.
2. Select the department or project for which you are requesting the report.
3. Choose the type of report you need (e.g. Project Budget Status, Department Budget Status, etc.).
4. Select the reporting period (e.g. month, quarter, etc.).
5. Enter the total budget amount for the selected project or department.
6. Choose the focus areas for the report (e.g. Spending vs Budget, Variance Analysis, etc.).
7. Select your preferred report format (e.g. Excel Spreadsheet, PDF Report, etc.).
8. Identify who needs to receive the report.
9. Specify how quickly you need the report (e.g. immediate, urgent, normal).
10. Optionally, enter any specific questions or concerns you have.
11. Choose whether or not you need budget recommendations.
12. Finally, enter the date by which you need the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Department**: Enter the department or project name for which you are requesting the report.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date on which you are requesting the report.
* **Type of Report Needed**: Select the type of report you need (e.g. Project Budget Status, Department Budget Status, etc.).
* **Project or Department Name**: Enter the name of the project or department for which you are requesting the report.
* **Reporting Period**: Enter the period for which you are requesting the report (e.g. month, quarter, etc.).
* **Total Budget Amount**: Enter the total budget amount for the selected project or department.
* **Report Focus Areas**: Select all areas of focus for the report (e.g. Spending vs Budget, Variance Analysis, etc.).
* **Preferred Report Format**: Select your preferred report format (e.g. Excel Spreadsheet, PDF Report, etc.).
* **Distribution List**: Enter the names of those who need to receive the report.
* **Report Urgency**: Select how quickly you need the report (e.g. immediate, urgent, normal).
* **Specific Questions to Address**: Optionally, enter any specific questions or concerns you have.
* **Include Comparative Data**: Choose whether or not to include comparative data (e.g. compare to previous periods).
* **Budget Recommendations Needed**: Choose whether or not you need budget recommendations.
* **Report Due Date**: Enter the date by which you need the report.
