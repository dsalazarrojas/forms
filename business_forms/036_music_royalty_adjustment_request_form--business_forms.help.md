**Music Royalty Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

This form is used to submit requests for adjustments to music royalties. It provides a structured format for users to describe the issue they are experiencing with their royalty payments, and for the company's adjuster to assess and process the request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the relevant fields as prompted, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Choose the correct adjustment type and select multiple music types as applicable.
3. Select the performing rights holder and payment method.
4. Enter any additional information or comments that may be relevant to the request.
5. Review the form carefully before submitting it for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Royalty Discrepancy Claim** (music_royalty_adjustment_request_form_data, text, required): Describe the discrepancy you are experiencing with your royalty payments in this field.
* **Upload Supporting Documents** (upload_screenshots, text, optional): If you have any supporting documentation or screenshots that can help with your request, upload them here.
* **Submit Adjustment Request** (submit_request, note, required): This is a confirmation step to ensure that you are ready to submit your request for processing.
* **Adjustment Type** (adjustment_type, select_one, required): Choose from the options to select the type of adjustment you are requesting.
* **Description** (description, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the adjustment request.
* **Music Type** (music_type, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable music types (e.g. Album, Single, Song).
* **Release Date** (release_date, date, required): Enter the release date of the music in question.
* **Release Year** (release_year, number, required): Enter the release year of the music in question.
* **Performing Rights Holder** (performing_rights_holder, select_one, required): Choose from the options to select the performing rights holder.
* **Record Label** (record_label, text, required): Enter the record label associated with the music.
* **Royalty Rate** (royalty_rate, number, required): Enter the royalty rate associated with the music.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, required): Choose from the options to select the method for payment.
* **Payment Amount** (payment_amount, number, required): Enter the payment amount requested.
* **Upload Supporting Documents** (upload_screenshots, text, optional): If you have any additional supporting documentation, upload them here.
* **Submit Adjustment Request** (submit_request, note, optional): This is a confirmation step to ensure that you are ready to submit your request for processing.
* **Status** (status, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the request.
* **Comment** (comment, text, required): Enter any comments or additional information about the request.
* **Additional Information** (additional_information, text, required): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the request.
* **Adjuster** (adjuster, text, required): Enter the name of the adjuster processing the request.
* **Submitter** (submitter, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Assigned Adjuster** (assigned_adjuster, text, required): Enter the name of the adjuster assigned to process the request.
* **Created Date** (created_date, date, optional): Enter the date the request was created.
* **Updated Date** (updated_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the last update to the request.
* **Created By** (created_by, text, required): Enter the name of the person who created the request.
* **Modified By** (modified_by, text, required): Enter the name of the person who last modified the request.
