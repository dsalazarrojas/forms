# Civil Litigation Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details on civil litigation cases, including case specifics, procedural status, and related parties. It's a tool for legal staff or attorneys to provide accurate and timely information on ongoing cases.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering the **Law Firm Name**, which should be the name of the law firm handling the case.
2. Provide the **Official Case Name** (e.g., Smith v. Jones), which should include the names of the parties involved.
3. Enter the **Case Index or File Number**, which is the unique identifier assigned to the case.
4. Identify the **Lead Attorney**, who is the primary attorney handling the case.
5. Specify the **Court/Jurisdiction**, where the case is currently being handled.
6. Select the **Type of Civil Litigation**, which determines the nature of the case (e.g., Personal Injury, Breach of Contract, etc.).
7. Choose the **Current Procedural Status**, which indicates the current stage of the case (e.g., Initial Pleadings, Discovery Phase, etc.).
8. Enter the **Scheduled Trial Date** (if set), which is the planned date for the trial.
9. Provide the **Immediate Next Action Item**, which is the essential step to be taken next in the case.
10. Enter the **Discovery Completion Deadline**, which is the date by which discovery should be completed.
11. Enter the **Dispositive Motion Deadline**, which is the deadline for dispositive motions.
12. Enter the **Opposing Party Name**, which is the name of the opposing party.
13. Enter the **Opposing Counsel Name**, which is the name of the opposing counsel.
14. Enter the **Last Updated** date, which is the date the case information was last updated.
15. Rate the **Case Complexity Level**, which indicates the level of complexity of the case (Standard, Moderate, Complex, Highly Complex).
16. Enter the **Probability of Settlement** before trial, which is a percentage indicating the likelihood of settlement.
17. Enter any **Strategic Case Summary**, which is a confidential note on the case strategy.
18. Enter any **Next Action Item**, which is the essential step to be taken next in the case.
