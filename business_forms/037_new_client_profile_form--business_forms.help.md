# new_client_profile_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The new_client_profile_form is a form designed to collect information from new clients in a single, efficient process. This form is meant for genuine new clients, and not for existing users or other non-client purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Enter your company name in the **Company** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Please enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Please enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Please enter your email address to ensure you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Please enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you.
* **Company** (`company`, text, not required): Please enter the name of the company you represent or work for.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can best serve you.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
* Please review our data privacy and security policies to understand how we handle sensitive data.
