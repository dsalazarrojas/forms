# swimming_pool_chemical_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help pool operators or managers collect and record information on the chemical levels and maintenance actions performed on a pool.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the chemical levels to be inspected, including pH, Alkalinity, Total Alkalinity, Stabilizer, Calcium Hardness, Cyanuric Acid, Chlorine, and Chlorine Free.
2. Choose the maintenance actions performed on the pool, such as Water Test Kit, Visual Inspection, Test Kit Calibration, or Other (if not listed).
3. If selecting "Other" under Maintenance Actions, provide a brief description of the other maintenance action taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **pH** (Chemical Levels)
* A measure of the pool's acidity, with lower values indicating a more alkaline or base-like condition.

### **Alkalinity** (Chemical Levels)
* A measure of the pool's ability to neutralize acids, indicating the amount of alkalinity in the water.

### **Total Alkalinity** (Chemical Levels)
* The sum of alkalinity and non-volatile alkalinity in the water, indicating the pool's total alkalinity capacity.

### **Stabilizer** (Chemical Levels)
* A type of water conditioner used to stabilize the pool water against changes in pH and water hardness.

### **Calcium Hardness** (Chemical Levels)
* A measure of the amount of calcium ions in the water, which can affect the pool's water quality.

### **Cyanuric Acid** (Chemical Levels)
* A disinfectant often used in pools, also known as cyanuric acid or CYA for short.

### **Chlorine** (Chemical Levels)
* A disinfectant often used in pools, typically in the form of chlorine gas or liquid chlorine solutions.

### **Chlorine Free** (Chemical Levels)
* A measure of the amount of chlorine present in the water, with lower values indicating a more chlorine-free condition.

### **Water Test Kit** (Maintenance Actions)
* A tool used to test and monitor the pool's water quality.

### **Visual Inspection** (Maintenance Actions)
* A check of the pool's equipment, surroundings, and general condition.

### **Test Kit Calibration** (Maintenance Actions)
* A process to ensure the accuracy and reliability of the test kit readings.

### **Additional Actions** (Maintenance Actions)
* Any other actions taken on the pool that are not listed in the other options.

### **Other** (Maintenance Actions)
* A text box to describe any other maintenance actions taken on the pool, if not listed in the other options.
