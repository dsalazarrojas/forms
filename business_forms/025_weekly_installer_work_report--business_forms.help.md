# Weekly Installer Work Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Installer Work Report form is used by installers to report their work activities, hours worked, and notable incidents during a specific week. This form helps to track and document the installer's work, providing a clear and concise record of their activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the week start date for the period you are reporting on.
2. Choose which job sites you visited during the week.
3. Enter the total hours worked during the week.
4. Select which job activities you performed during the week.
5. Check any incidents that occurred during your work.
6. Provide any additional work notes or comments.
7. Summarize your week's work in the "Weekly Summary" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Week Start** (`week_start`, date, optional): Select the date when your work week started.
- **Job Sites** (`job_sites`, select_multiple, optional): Choose which job sites you visited during the week.
  - This field allows you to select multiple job sites, so you can report on all the locations you worked at.
- **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, optional): Enter the total number of hours you worked during the week.
- **Job Activities** (`job_activities`, select_one, optional): Select which job activities you performed during the week.
  - This field allows you to select one or more activities from the list provided.
  - Activities may include tasks such as installation, maintenance, or other work-related activities.
- **Incidents** (`incidents`, select_multiple, optional): Check any incidents that occurred during your work.
  - This field allows you to report multiple incidents, which may be useful if you experienced more than one issue.
- **Work Notes** (`work_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or explanations about your work during the week.
  - Use this field to include any details that didn't fit in the other fields.
- **Weekly Summary** (`weekly_summary`, text, optional): Summarize your week's work in a few sentences.
  - Keep this field concise, but be as detailed as needed to give a clear overview of your week's activities.
