# Canyon RA Scheduling - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule meetings between residents and their Resident Director (RD) for various purposes such as initial check-ins, roommate mediations, community concerns, personal support, event planning, policy questions, and emergency meetings.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form with your personal details and meeting preferences.
2.  Select the relevant options for your meeting type, preferred date, time, duration, location, and attendees.
3.  Provide a brief description of the meeting topic.
4.  Choose how you would like to be confirmed about your meeting.
5.  If you have any accessibility needs, please indicate them.
6.  If you have met with your RD before, select the correct option.
7.  Add any additional notes that your RD should be aware of.
8.  Submit the form to schedule your meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Information** (Note): This is a placeholder field, but it will be replaced with a text field to capture any additional information about the resident.
* **Resident Name** (Text, **Required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Room Number** (Text, **Required**): Enter your room number in Canyon Hall.
* **Contact Email** (Email, **Required**): Enter your preferred email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, **Required**): Enter your phone number for emergency situations.
* **Resident Director Information** (Note): This field is optional, but you can use it to add any additional information about your RD assignment.
* **Assigned Resident Director** (Select One, **Required**): Select the RD who will be attending your meeting from the list provided.
* **Scheduling Request** (Note): This field is optional, but you can use it to describe your scheduling preferences or any specific requests.
* **Meeting Type** (Select One, **Required**): Choose the type of meeting you need to schedule.
* **Preferred Date** (Date, **Required**): Enter your preferred date for the meeting.
* **Preferred Time** (Time, **Required**): Enter your preferred time for the meeting.
* **Expected Duration** (Select One, **Required**): Choose the expected duration of the meeting.
* **Preferred Location** (Select One, **Optional**): Select the location for your meeting, or choose "No preference" if you don't have a preference.
* **Additional Attendees** (Select One, **Required**): Choose who will be attending the meeting with you.
* **Topic Details** (Text, **Required**): Briefly describe the topic you'd like to discuss with your RD.
* **Urgency Level** (Select One, **Required**): Choose the urgency level of your meeting.
* **Accessibility Needs** (Select Multiple, **Optional**): Check any accessibility needs you may have.
* **Previous Meetings with RD** (Select One, **Required**): Choose if you have met with your RD before.
* **Additional Notes** (Text, **Optional**): Add any additional information that your RD should be aware of.
* **Confirmation Preference** (Select One, **Required**): Choose how you would like to be confirmed about your meeting.
