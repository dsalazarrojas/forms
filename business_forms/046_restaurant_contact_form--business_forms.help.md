# restaurant_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The restaurant contact form is designed to collect contact information and messages from clients or customers of a restaurant. It is a simple form intended for casual, one-off interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the `Name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `Email` field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
3. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
4. Enter your message in the `Message` field.
5. For the `Select One` field, choose either `Yes` or `No` as required.
6. In the `Select Multiple` field, select multiple options by holding down the `Ctrl` key and clicking on multiple items.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments in the `Note` field.
8. If you need to select a specific date, use the `Date` field.
9. If you need to select a specific time, use the `Time` field.
10. Enter any additional email hints in the `Email Hint` field.
11. Enter any additional phone hints in the `Phone Hint` field.
12. Select a `Select Date` option, either `Yes` or `No`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Form**: This field has a title but no label.
* **Name (Name)**: Enter your full name here.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Message (Message)**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Select One (Select One)**: Choose either "Yes" or "No" as required.
* **Select Multiple (Select Multiple)**: Select multiple options by holding down the `Ctrl` key and clicking on multiple items.
* **Note (Note)**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Date (Date)**: Select a specific date if required.
* **Time (Time)**: Select a specific time if required.
* **Email Hint (Email Hint)**: Enter any additional hints regarding your email address.
* **Phone Hint (Phone Hint)**: Enter any additional hints regarding your phone number.
* **Select Date (Select Date)**: Choose either "Yes" or "No" as required.
