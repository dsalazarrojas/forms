# Club Visit Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Visit Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from visitors about their experience at our club. This form helps us understand your thoughts, opinions, and suggestions on various aspects of your visit, which we will use to make improvements and provide better services for our members and visitors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information:
   - **Your Name**: Please enter your name as you would like to be known.
   - **Email Address**: This is optional but recommended for us to get in touch with you regarding your feedback. 
2. Select the date you visited our club.
3. Choose the location you visited.
4. Indicate the type of visit you made (e.g., first-time visit, regular member, guest of a member, etc.).
5. Rate your experience with our staff in terms of friendliness and courtesy.
6. Rate the cleanliness of our facilities.
7. Rate the quality and availability of our amenities.
8. Rate the quality of our programs or classes offered.
9. (Optional) Rate the value for money of our services and facilities.
10. Rate the condition of our equipment.
11. Rate your overall satisfaction with your experience.
12. Indicate your likelihood to return or join our club.
13. Share any suggestions for improvement with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name** (1, `text`, Required): Enter your name as you would like to be known.
- **Email Address** (2, `email`, Optional): This is optional but helps us get in touch with you regarding your feedback. If you provide your email address, we will only use it for this purpose.
- **Date of Visit** (4, `date`, Required): Select the date you visited our club.
- **Club Location Visited** (5, `text`, Required): Choose the location you visited.
- **Type of Visit** (6, `select_one`, Required): Indicate the type of visit you made (e.g., first-time visit, regular member, guest of a member, etc.).
- **Staff Friendliness and Courtesy** (7, `select_one`, Required): Rate the staff's friendliness and courtesy.
- **Facility Cleanliness** (8, `select_one`, Required): Rate the cleanliness of our facilities.
- **Amenities and Facilities** (9, `select_one`, Required): Rate the quality and availability of our amenities.
- **Program or Class Quality** (10, `select_one`, Required): Rate the quality of our programs or classes offered.
- **Pricing and Value for Money** (11, `select_one`, Optional): Rate the value for money of our services and facilities.
- **Equipment Condition and Maintenance** (12, `select_one`, Optional): Rate the condition and maintenance of our equipment.
- **Overall Satisfaction** (13, `select_one`, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your experience.
- **Likelihood to Return or Join** (14, `select_one`, Required): Indicate your likelihood to return or join our club.
- **Suggestions for Improvement** (15, `text`, Optional): Share any suggestions or improvements you have.
