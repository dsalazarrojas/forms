This form serves as a tool for employees to assess and record data privacy practices within an organization. The purpose of this form is to help ensure that the organization adheres to data privacy standards and policies. By using this form, employees can identify areas of improvement and document their efforts to protect sensitive customer information.

# Data Privacy Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Data Privacy Checklist is a form designed to help you evaluate and document your organization's data privacy practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Choose one of the following options for "Data Privacy Checklist" to indicate if your organization has implemented data privacy practices.
	* Select 'Yes' if your organization has implemented data privacy practices in place.
	* Select 'No' if your organization does not have data privacy practices in place.
2. Enter a brief description of your organization's data handling practices in the "Data Handling Practices" field. This can include details about how your organization collects, stores, and uses customer data.
3. Select all that apply from the list for "Customer Information Safeguarding" to indicate how your organization safeguards customer information.
	* Select 'Yes' for each practice your organization implements.
4. In the "Description" field, provide a brief explanation of your organization's data privacy policies and procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Data Privacy Checklist** (`Data Privacy Checklist`, select_one, required): Indicate if your organization has implemented data privacy practices.
* **Data Handling Practices** (`Data Handling Practices`, text, required): Describe your organization's data handling practices.
* **Customer Information Safeguarding** (`Customer Information Safeguarding`, select_multiple, required): Select all the ways your organization safeguards customer information.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your organization's data privacy policies and procedures.

## Tips

* Ensure that your responses are accurate and up-to-date to reflect your organization's current data privacy practices.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of data privacy, consult with your organization's data privacy officer or policy owner.
* Please note that this form is not a substitute for thorough policy documentation and training on data privacy practices.
