# Service Plan Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess and document a service plan for a business. It gathers information on the service provider, client needs, plan comparison, data collection, form submission, submission tracking, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Service Provider**: Select all applicable service providers from the dropdown menu.
2. **Client Needs**: Select the primary client needs from the dropdown menu.
3. **Plan Comparison**: Enter the planned duration of the service (in numeric format).
4. **Data Collection**: Select all applicable data collection methods from the dropdown menu.
5. **Form Submission**: Select the submission status of the form.
6. **Submission Tracking**: Enter the email address of the person tracking the submission.
7. **Submission Notes**: Enter any additional notes regarding the submission.
8. **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
9. **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
10. **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client.
11. **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business.
12. **Business Location**: Enter the location of the business.
13. **Business Address**: Enter the address of the business.
14. **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
15. **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
16. **Service Plan Start Date**: Enter the start date of the service plan.
17. **Service Plan End Date**: Enter the end date of the service plan.
18. **Service Plan Duration**: Enter the duration of the service plan.
19. **Plan Cost**: Enter the cost of the plan.
20. **Payment Method**: Enter the payment method used.
21. **Payment Status**: Select the payment status of the plan.
22. **Payment Details**: Enter any additional payment details.
23. **Submission Status**: Select the submission status of the form.
24. **Submission Notes 2**: Enter any additional submission notes.
25. **Submission Comments**: Enter any additional comments regarding the submission.
