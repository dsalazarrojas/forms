## Purpose
The Disaster Recovery Plan Form is designed to help organizations and individuals assess their disaster recovery plans in the event of an unexpected event. This form gathers information about the plan's scope, goals, and strategies for recovery.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, provide detailed and clear responses.
3. If you're unsure about any of the questions, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: What is a Disaster Recovery Plan?

* **What is a disaster recovery plan?**: Please describe the plan's purpose, scope, and objectives. This is an open-ended question, so feel free to provide as much detail as necessary.

### Page 2: What are Your Recovery Strategies?

* **What are your recovery strategies?**: Explain the methods and procedures you'll use to recover from a disaster. This can include procedures for emergency services, critical services, or other services. Provide a clear and concise description of each strategy.

### Page 3: Services Recovery

* **What services will be restored first?**: Please select all the services that you want to restore immediately after a disaster. You can choose from:
	+ Emergency services
	+ Critical services
	+ Other services
* **Which services will be minimized during the recovery?**: Select all the services that you want to minimize or suspend during the recovery process. You can choose from:
	+ Emergency services
	+ Critical services
	+ Other services

### Page 4: Time to Recover

* **How much time do you expect to take to recover?**: Estimate the number of hours, days, or weeks it will take to fully recover from a disaster. This will help us understand your recovery timeline.

### Page 5: Personnel Involved

* **Who is involved in the recovery process?**: List the names and positions of all personnel who will be part of the recovery team. This can include team leaders, support staff, or other employees.
