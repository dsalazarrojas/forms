# foundation-damage-incident-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about foundation damage incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information about the incident.
- Ensure that all fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Summary**: Enter a brief summary of the incident, including a clear description of what happened.
* **Date Found**: Enter the date the incident was discovered or reported.
* **Location Found**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Damage Description**: Enter a detailed description of the damage caused by the incident.
* **Contractor Information**: Enter any relevant information about the contractor(s) involved in the repair process.
* **Repair Status**: Select the current status of the repair (Pending, In Progress, Completed).
* **Insurance Claim**: If an insurance claim has been filed, enter the relevant details.
* **Insurance Provider**: If an insurance claim has been filed, enter the name of the insurance provider.
* **Claim Status**: If an insurance claim has been filed, select the current status of the claim (Pending, Approved, Denied).
* **Claim Date**: If an insurance claim has been filed, enter the date the claim was submitted.
* **Claim Total**: If an insurance claim has been filed, enter the total amount of the claim.
* **Incident Images**: If relevant images of the incident are available, upload them here.
* **Incident Video**: If a video of the incident is available, upload it here.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling in the fields to ensure that all relevant information is collected.
- Ensure that you have permission to submit information on behalf of others, especially if you are filling in fields related to insurance claims.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields or their requirements, please contact the relevant personnel for assistance.
