# Covid 19 Workplace Safety Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Workplace Safety Checklist Form is a tool used to gather essential information from employees and supervisors to ensure a safe working environment during the Covid-19 pandemic. This form helps identify potential risks and provides a standardized way to record and track workplace safety measures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee information section (fields 1-8) with your personal details.
2. Enter the shift start and end times (fields 3-4) for your shift.
3. Indicate your supervisor's ID and your own ID status in the supervisor and employee ID fields (fields 5-6).
4. Enter the number of people present at your workplace (field 7) and your personal details in fields 8-12.
5. Fill in the supervisor's details in fields 13-15.
6. Provide the capacity of your workplace (field 16).
7. Enter your department (field 17) and your personal details in field 18.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments in field 25.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Employee Information (fields 1-8)
* **Employee Information (1, text, required)**: Enter your name and personal details.
* **Workplace Location (2, text, required)**: Enter the location of your workplace.
* **Shift Start Time (3, date, required)**: Enter the start time of your shift.
* **Shift End Time (4, date, required)**: Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Supervisor ID (5, select_one, required)**: Indicate if you have a supervisor or not.
* **Employee ID (6, select_multiple, required)**: Indicate if you have an ID or not.
* **Workplace Capacity (7, number, optional)**: Enter the capacity of your workplace.
* **Employee Name (8, text, required)**: Enter your name.

### Supervisor and Employee Details (fields 9-12)
* **Employee Name 2 (9, text, required)**: Enter the name of the second employee on your team.
* **Employee Phone 2 (10, text, required)**: Enter the phone number of the second employee.
* **Supervisor Name 2 (11, text, required)**: Enter the name of the second supervisor.
* **Employee Department 2 (12, select_multiple, required)**: Indicate if the second employee is in your department.

### Supervisor and Workplace Details (fields 13-16)
* **Supervisor Name (13, text, required)**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone (14, text, required)**: Enter the phone number of your supervisor.
* **Workplace Email (15, email, optional)**: Enter the email of your workplace.
* **Workplace Capacity 2 (16, number, optional)**: Enter the capacity of your workplace.

### Additional Details (fields 17-25)
* **Employee Name 2 (17, text, required)**: Enter the name of the second employee on your team.
* **Employee Phone 2 (18, text, required)**: Enter the phone number of the second employee.
* **Supervisor Name 2 (19, text, required)**: Enter the name of the second supervisor.
* **Workplace Email (20, email, optional)**: Enter the email of your workplace.
* **Employee Department 2 (21, select_multiple, required)**: Indicate if the second employee is in your department.
* **Notes (22, note, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes.

### Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use the correct format for dates (MM/dd/yyyy).
* If you have any doubts or issues, contact your supervisor or HR department.
