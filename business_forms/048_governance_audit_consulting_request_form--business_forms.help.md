# Governance Audit Consulting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients who are requesting governance audit consulting services. The information collected will be used to tailor the audit to the client's specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your contact information and project details in the "Client Info" section.
2. Select the services you are requesting from us in the "Services Selected" section.
3. If you selected "Service A", fill in the "Service A" section with the required information.
4. If you selected "Service B", fill in the "Service B" section with the required information.
5. If you selected "Service C", fill in the "Service C" section with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Info
• **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, optional): Please fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and any other relevant details.

### Project Details
• **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): In this section, please describe your project and provide any additional information that will help us understand your needs.

### Services Selected
• **Services Selected** (`services_selected`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the services you are requesting from us. You can choose multiple services by holding down the control key.

  • **Service A** (`service_a`, text, optional): If you selected "Service A", fill in this section with the required information.

  • **Service B** (`service_b`, text, optional): If you selected "Service B", fill in this section with the required information.

  • **Service C** (`service_c`, text, optional): If you selected "Service C", fill in this section with the required information.
