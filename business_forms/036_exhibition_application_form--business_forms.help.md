# Exhibition Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide applicants through the process of applying for an exhibition, collecting information about their exhibitions, contact details, product information, and any additional details they might want to provide.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first page with your exhibition title and any relevant details.
2. On the second page, provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. On the third page, describe your exhibition in detail, including its theme, dates, and location.
4. On the fourth page, provide information about your products or services, including their names, descriptions, and images.
5. On the fifth page, add any additional information you think is relevant to your exhibition.
6. Finally, review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1 - Exhibition
* **Exhibition** (`exhibition_application_form_first_page`, text, required): Please enter your exhibition title and any other relevant details.
* **Exhibition Application Form** (`exhibition_application_form_second_page`, text, optional): This is an optional field for any additional information you might want to provide.
### Page 2 - Contact Info
* **Name** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business cards or ID.
* **Email** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your email address associated with your business or organization.
* **Phone** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your business phone number.
### Page 3 - Exhibition Details
* **Exhibition Name** (`exhibition_details`, text, required): Enter your exhibition name or title.
* **Exhibition Theme** (`exhibition_details`, text, optional): If your exhibition has a specific theme, please describe it here.
* **Exhibition Dates** (`exhibition_details`, text, optional): Enter the dates of your exhibition.
* **Exhibition Location** (`exhibition_details`, text, optional): Enter the location of your exhibition.
### Page 4 - Product Info
* **Product Name** (`product_info`, text, optional): Enter the name of your product.
* **Product Description** (`product_info`, text, optional): Describe your product in detail.
* **Product Image URL** (`product_info`, text, optional): If you have an image URL for your product, please enter it here.
### Page 5 - Additional Info
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Use this space to add any additional information about your exhibition.
### Page 6 - Submission
* **Exhibition Application Form** (`exhibition_application_form_sixth_page`, text, optional): Please review and confirm your application before submitting.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
* The form is designed to be simple and straightforward; if you need further assistance, reach out to the form administrators.
