# Prospect List Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prospect List Request Form Template is designed to gather essential information from clients, prospects, or customers for prospect list requests. This form helps in organizing and streamlining the process of collecting relevant details, making it easier to manage and prioritize prospect lists.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the form's title and any other relevant details, as needed.
2. In the "Contact Info" field, provide the necessary contact information for the prospect list request, such as name, email, phone number, or other relevant contact details.
3. If applicable, add any additional questions or information you would like to share about the prospect list request in the "Additional Questions" field.
4. Finally, upload any relevant attachments that support the prospect list request in the "Upload Attachment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prospect List Request Form (ID 1)**: Enter a brief description of the prospect list request, including the purpose and scope of the request.
* **Contact Info (ID 2)**: Provide the contact information of the person or organization making the prospect list request, such as name, email, phone number, or other relevant contact details.
* **Additional Questions (ID 3)**: Add any additional questions or information you would like to share about the prospect list request, such as specific requirements or special requests.
* **Upload Attachment (ID 4)**: Attach any relevant documents or files that support the prospect list request, such as images, spreadsheets, or other relevant files.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear when filling out the form to ensure the prospect list request is processed efficiently.
* Use the "Contact Info" field to ensure the prospect list request reaches the correct person or organization.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, feel free to add them in the "Additional Questions" field.
* Attach relevant supporting documents or files in the "Upload Attachment" field to facilitate processing of the prospect list request.
