# Travel Expense Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to facilitate the reimbursement process of employee travel expenses. It collects relevant information about the employee's trip, including dates, destinations, and expense details. Completing this form accurately will help ensure that your travel expenses are processed promptly and correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring that all required fields are completed before submission.
2.  Enter your travel date, which includes the date you departed and returned.
3.  Enter your employee name as it is recorded in our HR system.
4.  Provide a brief description of the business purpose of your trip.
5.  Enter the destination of your trip.
6.  Enter the date you returned from your trip.
7.  Enter the amount of the reimbursement for your expenses.
8.  Select whether or not you are requesting reimbursement for your expenses.
9.  Enter the total amount of your expenses.
10.  Select the status of your reimbursement request.
11.  Enter the date you submitted this form.
12.  Enter the time you submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **travel_date**: Enter the date you departed for your trip. (Required)
*   **employee_name**: Enter your full employee name as it is recorded in our HR system. (Required)
*   **business_purpose**: Describe the purpose of your business trip in a few sentences. (Required)
*   **travel_destination**: Enter the destination of your trip. (Optional)
*   **travel_date_return**: Enter the date you returned from your trip. (Optional)
*   **reimbursement_amount**: Enter the total amount of your reimbursement request. (Required)
*   **currency**: Select the currency of your reimbursement request. (Required)
*   **expense_description**: Provide a description of your expenses, if applicable. (Optional)
*   **expense_item**: Select the types of expenses you are requesting reimbursement for. (Required)
*   **expense_amount**: Enter the total amount of your expenses. (Required)
*   **expense_rate**: Enter the expense rate, if applicable. (Optional)
*   **total_expense**: Enter the total amount of your expenses. (Optional)
*   **reimbursement_status**: Select the status of your reimbursement request. (Required)
*   **submission_date**: Enter the date you submitted this form. (Optional)
*   **submission_time**: Enter the time you submitted this form. (Optional)

## Tips

*   Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   Double-check your expense amounts and descriptions for accuracy.
*   Review your form for completeness and consistency before submitting.
