# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer and event information for business purposes. It is used to gather details about customers, attorneys, and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields as indicated by the asterisk (\*).
* For each field, enter the corresponding information.
* Review each field for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (first_name, text, optional): Enter the first name of the customer or person.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, optional): Enter the last name of the customer or person.
* **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, optional): Enter the full name of the customer.
* **Customer Email** (customer_email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone** (customer_phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Attorney** (attorney, text, optional): Enter the name of the attorney.
* **Attorney Email** (attorney_email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the attorney.
* **Attorney Phone** (attorney_phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the attorney.
* **Address** (address, text, optional): Enter the customer's address.
* **City** (city, text, optional): Enter the city of the customer's address.
* **State** (state, text, optional): Enter the state of the customer's address.
* **Zip** (zip, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the customer's address.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Schedule** (schedule, text, optional): Enter the schedule of the event.
* **Event Type** (event_type, select_one, optional): Select the type of event from the options provided.
* **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the event.
* **Submitted By** (submitted_by, text, optional): Enter the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On** (submitted_on, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted At** (submitted_at, time, optional): Enter the time the form was submitted.

Note: All fields are optional, but filling out as much information as possible will help ensure accurate and complete information is captured.
