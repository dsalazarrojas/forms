# wiremold_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wiremold_request_form is used to request information about Wiremolds. This form helps gather relevant data about the product and the person making the request. It is a critical tool for our internal team to assist with your Wiremold-related needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form with accurate and clear information about the Wiremold you are requesting.
2. Make sure to select the correct "Requested By" option, indicating who is making the request.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Code (Product Code)** (`product_code`, `text`, optional): Enter the product code of the Wiremold you are requesting.
* **Product Name (Product Name)** (`product_name`, `text`, optional): Provide the name of the Wiremold product you are requesting.
* **Requested By (Requested By)** (`requested_by`, `select_one`, required): Select who is making the request (Wiremold or another option).
* **Contact Name (Contact Name)** (`contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person or department requesting the Wiremold.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address for the person or department requesting the Wiremold.
* **Address (Address)** (`address`, `text`, optional): Provide the contact address of the person or department making the request.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or department making the request.
* **Comments (Comments)** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments about the Wiremold request.

## Tips
- Make sure all required fields are filled out to avoid delays in processing your request.
- If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team for assistance.
- This form will be used to improve our internal process and enhance your experience with Wiremold products and services.
