# Client Call Log 5 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Client Call Log 5" form is designed to record and document client interactions, providing a structured format for staff members to log details about their interactions with clients, including employee information, call details, and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee who conducted the call from the list of available staff members.
2. Enter the client or company name as it was mentioned during the call.
3. Provide a brief description of the person spoken to during the call.
4. Record the date and start and end times of the call.
5. Select the call direction (inbound or outbound).
6. Briefly describe the subject matter of the call.
7. Summarize the detailed conversation that took place during the call.
8. Choose the call outcome from the list of available options.
9. Enter any action items or next steps required.
10. Add any internal employee notes about the call.
11. Check if a manager's review is requested.
12. Confirm if the data has been synced to the CRM system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Employee Name**
- This field is required to be filled out and is used to identify the staff member who conducted the call.
- Please enter the name of the employee who spoke with the client.
### 2. **Employee Department**
- This field is required and is used to determine which department the call relates to (e.g., Sales, Customer Support, etc.).
- Please select the department that conducted the call.
### 3. **Client or Company Name**
- This field is required and is used to record the name of the client or company mentioned during the call.
- Please enter the client or company name as accurately as possible.
### 4. **Person Spoken To**
- This field is required and is used to record the name of the person spoken to during the call.
- Please enter the name of the person spoken to.
### 5. **Date of Call**
- This field is required and is used to record the date the call took place.
- Please select the date of the call using the date picker.
### 6. **Start Time**
- This field is required and is used to record the start time of the call.
- Please select the start time of the call using the time picker.
### 7. **End Time**
- This field is required and is used to record the end time of the call.
- Please select the end time of the call using the time picker.
### 8. **Call Direction**
- This field is required and is used to determine if the call was inbound or outbound.
- Please select the call direction from the list of options.
### 9. **Subject Matter**
- This field is required and is used to briefly describe the reason for the call.
- Please enter a brief description of the subject matter.
### 10. **Detailed Conversation Summary**
- This field is required and is used to provide a detailed summary of the conversation.
- Please enter a detailed summary of the conversation.
### 11. **Call Outcome**
- This field is required and is used to determine the outcome of the call.
- Please select the call outcome from the list of available options.
### 12. **Action Items and Next Steps**
- This field is optional and is used to record any action items or next steps required after the call.
- Please enter any relevant action items or next steps.
### 13. **Internal Employee Notes**
- This field is optional and is used for internal employee notes about the call.
- Please enter any internal employee notes.
### 14. **Manager Review Requested**
- This field is required and is used to determine if a manager's review is requested for the call.
- Please select if a manager's review is requested.
### 15. **Data Synced to CRM**
- This field is optional and is used to confirm if the data has been synced to the CRM system.
- Please select if the data has been synced to the CRM system.

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately fill out the form to ensure accurate record-keeping and future reference.
- Use the call summary to provide a detailed account of the conversation.
- Please be concise in your description of the subject matter.
- If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your team lead or support staff for guidance.
