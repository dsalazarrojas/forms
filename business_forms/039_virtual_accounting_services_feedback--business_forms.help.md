# Virtual Accounting Services Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients about the virtual accounting services provided by the company. The feedback will be used to assess the quality of service, identify areas of improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance the services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose a category that best describes the service you are providing feedback on (e.g., Accounting, Financial Planning, Tax Planning, or Audit and Accounting Services).
2. Provide your feedback about the service. This is your opportunity to share your thoughts, concerns, or suggestions about the service you received.
3. If you have any comments or additional information, please share them in the space provided.
4. Enter your email address (if you have one) so we can contact you for follow-up questions or discussions.
5. Provide your phone number (if you have one) so we can reach out to you for further information.
6. Select the assigned user (e.g., Jimmy, Joe, or Jane) who provided you with the service.
7. Please note that the form ID and creation date are automatically generated and do not require input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Accounting Services Feedback**: This field is the main content of the feedback form. Please provide a detailed and honest account of your experience with the service. You can share your likes, dislikes, and suggestions.
* **Category**: This field allows you to select the category that best describes the service you are providing feedback on. Choose the option that best matches your experience.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or information about your experience, please share them here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you have one) so we can contact you for follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can reach out to you for further information.
* **Assigned User**: This field indicates the person who provided you with the service. Select the name of the person who worked with you.
* **Form ID**: This field is automatically generated and is for internal use only. Please do not enter any values in this field.
* **Created at**: This field displays the date and time when the form was created. It is for reference only and is not editable.

## Tips
* Please provide specific examples or details about your experience when giving feedback.
* Be honest and objective in your feedback, and avoid using jargon or technical terms that may be difficult to understand.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us through the email address provided in the form.
