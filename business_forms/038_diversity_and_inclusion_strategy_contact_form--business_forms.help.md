# diversity_and_inclusion_strategy_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The diversity and inclusion strategy contact form is a tool to help us better understand your needs and challenges regarding diversity and inclusion within our organization. Your feedback is essential to tailor our strategy to serve the needs of all stakeholders.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
- Fill in your first name.
- Fill in your last name.
- Enter your organization's name.
- Enter your email address.
- Enter your phone number (optional).
- Provide a brief description of your goal or challenge.
- If your challenge is not listed, please provide a brief description of it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (first name, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (last name, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Organization** (organization, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
- **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
- **Goal** (goal, text, required): Provide a brief description of your goal or challenge.
- **Challenge** (challenge, text, optional): Select a challenge that best describes your experience or concern. If not listed, provide a brief description of it.
- **Challenge Other** (challenge other, text, optional): If your challenge is not listed, provide a brief description of it.

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide accurate information.
- Please be as specific as possible when describing your goal or challenge.
- If you have any questions or need help, feel free to reach out to us.
