# Client Case Progress Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to track progress on a specific case, allowing you to monitor milestones, interactions, and other relevant information. It's intended to provide a centralized and structured way to document the case's status, progress, and updates.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**: Make sure to provide all required information to ensure the progress is properly tracked.
2. **Enter a Case/Project ID**: This is a unique identifier for the case. Enter the correct ID to link your progress tracking to the correct case.
3. **Enter the Client Name**: Provide the name of the client associated with the case.
4. **Set the Current Case Status**: Select the current status from the list of options provided (e.g., Discovery/Intake, Active (Phase 1), etc.). This helps track the overall progress of the case.
5. **Enter the Estimated Progress**: Provide the estimated progress percentage completed for the case.
6. **Record the Date of Last Client Interaction**: Enter the date of the most recent client interaction.
7. **Specify the Type of Last Interaction**: Choose the type of interaction that occurred (e.g., Meeting, Phone Call, etc.).
8. **Enter the Most Recent Milestone Achieved**: Describe the most recent milestone achieved in the case.
9. **Set the Target for Next Milestone**: Indicate what the next milestone target is.
10. **Identify any Roadblocks or Risks**: If applicable, mention any roadblocks or risks that have been encountered.
11. **Specify Additional Resources or Input Required**: If additional resources or input are needed, describe them here.
12. **Determine if Progress Should be Notified to the Client**: Choose how progress should be communicated to the client (e.g., via email, portal, or internal update only).
13. **Update By**: Enter the name of the staff member responsible for updating the case information.
14. **Date of Update**: Enter the date of the update.
15. **Internal Case Manager Summary**: Provide a brief internal summary of the case progress.
16. **Set the Priority Level**: Indicate the priority level of the case (Low, Medium, High, or Urgent).

**Tips**:

* Use this form to regularly track progress and updates on cases to ensure accurate and efficient tracking.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely to avoid missing critical information.
* This form is designed to be used in conjunction with other tracking and management tools to streamline your work process.
