# Vehicle Inspection Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers schedule a vehicle inspection appointment at our facility. It collects essential information from the client, including their name, contact number, email, preferred time, and date of birth (optional). This form is not a formal vehicle inspection report, but rather a way to initiate the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Client Name** in the designated field.
2. Provide your **Contact Number** so we can reach you to confirm the appointment.
3. Enter your **Email** address for communication purposes.
4. Choose a **Preferred Time** for the inspection appointment.
5. If you are scheduling the appointment for someone else, please provide their **Date of Birth**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Inspection Form** (`vehicle inspection form`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the vehicle, such as make, model, and type.
* **Client Name** (`client name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact number`, text, optional): Enter a phone number where we can reach you to confirm the appointment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for us to send appointment reminders and confirmation messages.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred time`, time, optional): Choose a time slot for the inspection appointment. You can select from a list of available time slots.
* **Date of Birth** (`date of birth`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the person scheduling the inspection, if different from your own.
