# Employee Payroll Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Payroll Change Form is used to update an employee's payroll information, specifically their employee ID, title, and salary. This form helps HR or payroll administrators to efficiently make changes to employee records in the company's database.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's **Employee ID** in the "Employee ID" field. This is the unique identifier assigned to each employee in the company's database.
2. Enter the employee's new **Title** in the "Title" field. This field should reflect the employee's current position or role within the company.
3. Enter the **Old Salary** in the "Old Salary" field. This field is used to record the employee's current salary before the change.
4. Enter the **New Salary** in the "New Salary" field. This field should reflect the updated salary of the employee after the change.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's unique identifier assigned in the company's database.
* **Title**: Enter the employee's current position or role within the company.
* **Old Salary**: Enter the employee's current salary before the change.
* **New Salary**: Enter the employee's updated salary after the change.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the employee's ID, title, and salary.
* Double-check the employee's current and updated salary to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the HR or payroll department for assistance.
