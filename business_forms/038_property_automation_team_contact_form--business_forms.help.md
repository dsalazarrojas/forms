# Property Automation Team Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Automation Team Contact Form is a contact form for customers or employees to send messages, questions, or issues related to property automation to the Property Automation Team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name field with your name.
2. Enter your Email address for us to contact you if needed.
3. Enter your Company name for identification purposes.
4. Select your Department from the drop-down list to let us know which team to direct your message to (e.g., Automation or Support).
5. Choose the Subject of your message from the provided options or type in your own (e.g., Property automation issue or Property automation question).
6. Enter a brief Description of your message.
7. Enter your Phone number for us to contact you if needed.
8. Select a Date and Time for the issue or meeting.
9. You can add any additional information in the Note field, if needed.
10. Click the submit button to send your message to the Property Automation Team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your name, a required field for us to address you by name. 
* **Email**: A field where you should enter your contact Email address for us to get back to you if needed.
* **Company**: Enter your company name for identification purposes.
* **Department**: Select the department you're working with or the issue is related to (e.g., Automation or Support).
* **Subject**: The main topic of your message, which should be one of our provided options or your own choice.
* **Description**: Briefly describe the issue or question you're submitting.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
* **Date**: Select the date related to your issue or meeting.
* **Time**: Select the time for your issue or meeting.
* **Note**: If you have any additional information, you can add it here.
