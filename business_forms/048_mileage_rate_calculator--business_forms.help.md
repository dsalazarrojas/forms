# Mileage Rate Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Mileage Rate Calculator form is designed to help businesses track and record their mileage expenses, including type, start and end dates and times, distance traveled, fuel type, and fuel cost.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of mileage (Business, Private, or Personal) that applies to your submission.
2. Enter the start date and start time of your trip.
3. Enter the end date and end time of your trip.
4. Enter the distance traveled for the trip.
5. Choose the type of fuel used (Gas, Diesel, or Alternative).
6. Enter the fuel cost associated with the trip.
7. Specify the reimbursement status (Approved, Declined, or Pending).
8. Optionally, add any comments about the trip or submission.
9. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
10. Enter the business name, email, and website (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Mileage Type**: Select the type of mileage this submission pertains to (Business, Private, or Personal). 
*   **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your trip. Please format the date in the standard MM/DD/YY format. 
*   **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your trip. Please format the time in the 12-hour format (e.g., 02:30 PM). 
*   **End Date**: Enter the end date of your trip. Please format the date in the standard MM/DD/YY format. 
*   **End Time**: Enter the end time of your trip. Please format the time in the 12-hour format (e.g., 03:30 PM). 
*   **Distance**: Enter the distance traveled for the trip. 
*   **Fuel Type**: Select the type of fuel used for the trip (Gas, Diesel, or Alternative). 
*   **Fuel Cost**: Enter the cost of the fuel used for the trip. 
*   **Reimbursement Status**: Specify the reimbursement status of the submission (Approved, Declined, or Pending). 
*   **Comments**: Optionally, add any comments or notes about the submission. 
*   **Submitter Name**: Enter your name for the submission. 
*   **Submitter Contact**: Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. 
*   **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business related to this submission. 
*   **Email**: Enter the business email address. 
*   **Phone Number**: Enter the business phone number. 
*   **Business Email**: Enter the business email address. 
*   **Business Address**: Enter the business address. 
*   **Business Postal**: Enter the business postal/zip code. 
*   **Business Website**: Enter the business website URL.
