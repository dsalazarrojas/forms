# <string> - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about deliveries, including delivery details, status, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options for each field from the available choices.
2. Enter any necessary additional details in the `delivery_details` field.
3. Provide the required date fields for delivery dates.
4. Enter the `contract_number` and `contract_date` fields if applicable.
5. Update the `delivery_status` and `delivery_status_2` fields as necessary.
6. Select the assigned users from the available options.
7. Enter any additional notes in the `notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client** (`client`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select multiple options to indicate the client(s) involved in the delivery.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the delivery.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional details about the delivery.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the signature.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the signature.
* **Document Type** (`document_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of document involved in the delivery.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the delivery address from the available options.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the delivery method from the available options.
* **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, select_one, required/optional): Update the delivery status as necessary.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, required/optional): Select the assigned user from the available options.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date assigned to the user.
* **Assigned Time** (`assigned_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time assigned to the user.
* **Delivery Date 2** (`delivery_date_2`, date, required/optional): Enter the second delivery date.
* **Delivery Method 2** (`delivery_method_2`, select_one, required/optional): Select the second delivery method from the available options.
* **Assigned User 2** (`assigned_user_2`, select_one, required/optional): Select the second assigned user from the available options.
* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, select_one, required/optional): Select the contract number from the available options.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the contract date.
* **Delivery Status 2** (`delivery_status_2`, select_one, required/optional): Update the second delivery status as necessary.
* **Delivery Date 3** (`delivery_date_3`, date, required/optional): Enter the third delivery date.
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the contract status from the available options.
* **Assigned User 3** (`assigned_user_3`, select_one, required/optional): Select the third assigned user from the available options.
* **Contract Number 2** (`contract_number_2`, select_one, required/optional): Select the second contract number from the available options.
* **Contract Date 2** (`contract_date_2`, date, required/optional): Enter the second contract date.
