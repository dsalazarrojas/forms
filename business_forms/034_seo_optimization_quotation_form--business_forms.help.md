# seo_optimization_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The seo_optimization_quotation_form is a business form used to collect information about SEO services quotations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's information on page 1 (client_info).
2. Select the services offered on page 2 (services).
3. Choose the relevant keywords for the project on page 3 (keywords).
4. Provide any expected results or notes on page 4 (expected_results).
5. Add any additional comments on page 5 (additional_comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_info (text, on page 1, optional)**: Enter the client's basic information, such as name, contact details, etc.
* **services (select multiple, on page 2, optional)**: Select all services applicable to the quotation, such as "Yes" for services that will be offered and "No" for those that won't.
* **keywords (select one, on page 3, optional)**: Choose the most relevant keyword for the project, such as "keyword1", "keyword2", or "keyword3".
* **expected_results (note, on page 4, optional)**: Enter any expected results or notes about the project.
* **additional_comments (text, on page 5, optional)**: Add any additional comments or information about the project.

## Tips

* Ensure you select the correct services and keywords relevant to the project.
* Be concise and clear in your expected results and additional comments.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid any errors or missing information.
