# job_description_revision_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The job description revision request form is used to submit a formal request for changes to job titles, job descriptions, or other job-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's name in the `Company` field.
2. Enter the job title that needs to be changed or updated in the `Job Title` field.
3. Describe the changes that need to be made to the job description in the `Job Description` field.
4. If applicable, provide the specific changes requested in the `Requested Changes` field.
5. Enter the reason for the request in the `Reason for Request` field.
6. Provide your contact email address in the `Contact Email` field.
7. Provide your phone number in the `Contact Phone` field.
8. Enter the name of the manager who will be working with you on this request in the `Manager Name` field.
9. Enter the manager's email address in the `Manager Email` field.
10. Enter the manager's phone number in the `Manager Phone` field.
11. If applicable, enter the department affected by the job description changes in the `Department` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title that needs to be changed or updated.
* **Job Description**: Describe the changes that need to be made to the job description.
* **Requested Changes**: If applicable, provide specific changes requested.
* **Reason for Request**: Enter the reason for submitting this request.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding this request.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you regarding this request.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager who will be working with you on this request.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the manager's email address so that we can contact them regarding this request.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter the manager's phone number so that we can contact them regarding this request.
* **Department**: If applicable, enter the department affected by the job description changes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid delays in processing the request.
* Ensure that the request is legitimate and not for minor adjustments or updates.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, contact your manager or HR representative for guidance.
