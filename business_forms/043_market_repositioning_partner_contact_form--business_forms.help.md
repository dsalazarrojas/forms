# market_repositioning_partner_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The market repositioning partner contact form is used to collect information from partners that will aid in the repositioning process of a market. This information will help us better understand the partner's needs, goals, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you are filling this form for the purpose of repositioning a market.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Double-check your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or business cards.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents or business cards.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your professional email address where you can be reached.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Please type in your company name or name of your organization.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether you are a partner or not.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position within your company.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter a contact number that can be reached at your company.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information or message you would like to share about your role in the repositioning process.

## Tips
- Ensure accuracy and completeness in filling out the form.
- Review your information before submitting to ensure it's correct.
- If you have any issues or questions, please contact the relevant department.
