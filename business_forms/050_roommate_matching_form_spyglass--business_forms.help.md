# Roommate Matching Form SpyGlass - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to collect user preferences and characteristics to match them with potential roommates.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your information, including user input, roommates' name, roommates' presence, age range, room size, preferred room size, gender, age, contact email, and contact phone.
2. Select your preferred room size, languages, interests, room size liked, preferred roommate type, and interests liked.
3. Indicate whether you liked or disliked roommates, room sizes, languages, interests, and preferred roommate types.
4. Enter your contact email and phone number to be contacted by potential matchers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself that you feel is important to share with potential roommates.
* **Roommate Name** (`roommate_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who will be sharing a space with you.
* **Roommates** (`roommates`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you want roommates, none, or all roommates.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred age range.
* **Room Size (sqft)** (`room_size_sqft`, text, optional): Enter your preferred room size in square feet.
* **Preferred Room Size (sqft)** (`preferred_room_size_sqft`, text, optional): Enter the room size you prefer in square feet.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred gender(s) for roommates.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Roommate Match Score** (`roommate_match_score`, number, optional): Enter your preferred match score.
* **Roommates Liked** (`roommates_liked`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you liked or disliked roommates.
* **Preferred Languages** (`preferred_languages`, select_multiple, optional): Select languages you prefer to be spoken with roommates.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select interests you prefer to have with roommates.
* **Room Size (sqft) Liked** (`room_size_sqft_liked`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you liked or disliked room sizes.
* **Preferred Roommate Type** (`preferred_roommate_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of roommates you prefer.
* **Room Size (sqft) Disliked** (`room_size_sqft_disliked`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you liked or disliked room sizes.
* **Preferred Roommate Type Disliked** (`preferred_roommate_type_disliked`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of roommates you dislike.
* **Interests Disliked** (`interests_disliked`, select_multiple, optional): Select interests you dislike.
* **Roommates Disliked** (`roommates_disliked`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you liked or disliked roommates.
* **Interests Liked** (`interests_liked`, select_multiple, optional): Select interests you like.
* **Preferred Roommate Type Liked** (`preferred_roommate_type_liked`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of roommates you like.
* **Room Size (sqft) Disliked Liked** (`room_size_sqft_disliked_liked`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you liked or disliked room sizes.
* **Age Range Liked** (`age_range_liked`, select_multiple, optional): Select age ranges you like.
* **Preferred Roommate Type Disliked** (`preferred_roommate_type_disliked`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of roommates you dislike.
* **Interests Disliked** (`interests_disliked`, select_multiple, optional): Select interests you dislike.
* **Roommates Liked** (`roommates_liked`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you liked or disliked roommates.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure the most suitable match.
* Be open-minded and flexible with your preferences.
* Remember that the form is for your own preferences and characteristics.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always choose "No" or "Unknown".
