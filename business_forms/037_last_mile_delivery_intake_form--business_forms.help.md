# last_mile_delivery_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The last mile delivery intake form is used to capture information for the last mile delivery process. This form ensures accurate and efficient delivery of packages and packages.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the customer who is receiving the package.
* **email** (email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **phone** (text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **delivery_date** (date, required): Enter the date of the delivery.
* **delivery_time** (time, required): Enter the time of the delivery.
* **delivery_location** (text, required): Enter the delivery location (e.g. building, floor, room number).
* **package_weight** (number, required): Enter the weight of the package.
* **package_size** (text, required): Enter the size of the package (e.g. large, medium, small).
* **delivery_address** (text, required): Enter the delivery address (including street, city, state, and zip code).
* **delivery_status** (select_one, optional): Select the current status of the delivery (In Progress, Completed, Cancelled, or On Hold).
* **note** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the delivery.
* **note2** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the delivery.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the customer's contact information is accurate.
* Double-check the delivery date and time to ensure it is correct.
* Use the delivery status field to keep track of the delivery's progress.
* Use the notes fields for any additional information that may be relevant to the delivery.
