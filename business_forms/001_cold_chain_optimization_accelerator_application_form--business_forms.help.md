# Cold Chain Optimization Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cold Chain Optimization Accelerator Application Form is designed to help businesses optimize their cold chain operations. By providing information about your business and your optimization goals, you will be able to access the Cold Chain Optimization Accelerator program, which will help you improve your supply chain visibility, reduce costs, and enhance compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company's name and registration number.
2. Select your business's primary industry sector.
3. Provide your approximate total storage capacity, annual revenue range, and number of employees.
4. Choose the primary cold chain challenges you are facing.
5. Select your main optimization goals.
6. Briefly describe your current technology systems and any existing challenges.
7. Provide your estimated budget for optimization.
8. Select your preferred implementation timeline.
9. Describe your optimization project.
10. Provide your contact information (name, email, phone number, and job title).
11. Select the date of application.
12. Finally, confirm that you agree to the program terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's legal registered name.
* **Business Registration Number** (`business_registration_number`, text, required): Enter your company's registration number.
* **Primary Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, select_one, required): Select the sector your business operates in:
	+ Pharmaceutical
	+ Food and Beverage
	+ Healthcare
	+ Logistics
	+ Retail
	+ Other
* **Current Cold Chain Capacity** (`current_cold_chain_capacity`, number, required): Approximate total storage volume.
* **Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, required): Select your annual revenue range:
	+ Under 1 million
	+ 1-5 million
	+ 5-20 million
	+ 20-50 million
	+ 50+ million
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, required): Enter the number of full-time equivalent staff.
* **Primary Cold Chain Challenges** (`primary_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Temperature Control
	+ Energy Efficiency
	+ Supply Chain Visibility
	+ Equipment Maintenance
	+ Regulatory Compliance
	+ Cost Optimization
* **Main Optimization Goals** (`optimization_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Reduce Operating Costs
	+ Improve Product Quality
	+ Expand Capacity
	+ Increase Sustainability
	+ Enhance Compliance
* **Current Technology Systems** (`current_technology_systems`, text, optional): Describe any existing monitoring or management systems.
* **Estimated Budget for Optimization** (`budget_allocation`, number, optional): Enter your estimated budget for optimization in your local currency.
* **Preferred Implementation Timeline** (`implementation_timeline`, select_one, required): Select your preferred implementation timeline:
	+ Immediate (1-3 months)
	+ Short-term (3-6 months)
	+ Medium-term (6-12 months)
	+ Long-term (12+ months)
* **Describe Your Optimization Project** (`project_description`, text, required): Provide details about what you want to optimize.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person_name`, text, required): Enter your primary contact person's name.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number, including your country code.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Select the date of application.
* **I agree to the program terms and conditions** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, required): Select "Yes". 
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any other relevant information.
