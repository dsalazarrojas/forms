# Service Listing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Listing Form is used to collect information about a service provider, including their contact details, social media links, and descriptions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Service Listing Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the service listing (Service Listings or Listings).
2. Enter the name of the service provider.
3. Select the type of service (Service Type or Other).
4. Enter the description of the service.
5. Enter the service address.
6. Enter the service phone number.
7. Enter the service email address.
8. Enter the service website.
9. Enter any additional social media links for the service provider (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, Yelp, and LinkedIn).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Listings/Listing**: Select the category of the service listing from the options "Service Listings" or "Listings".
* **Service Provider Name**: Enter the name of the service provider. This is a required field.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service from the options "Service Type" or "Other".
* **Service Description**: Enter a brief description of the service. This is a required field.
* **Service Address**: Enter the address of the service. This is a required field.
* **Service Phone**: Enter the phone number of the service. This is a required field.
* **Service Email**: Enter the email address of the service. This is a required field.
* **Service Website**: Enter the website of the service. This is a required field.
* **Facebook**: Enter the service provider's Facebook link. (Optional)
* **Twitter**: Enter the service provider's Twitter link. (Optional)
* **Instagram**: Enter the service provider's Instagram link. (Optional)
* **Yelp**: Enter the service provider's Yelp link. (Optional)
* **LinkedIn**: Enter the service provider's LinkedIn link. (Optional)
* **Service Provider Phone 2**: Enter any additional phone number of the service provider. (Optional)
* **Service Provider Email 2**: Enter any additional email address of the service provider. (Optional)
* **Service Provider Website 2**: Enter any additional website of the service provider. (Optional)
* **Service Provider Facebook 2**: Enter any additional Facebook link of the service provider. (Optional)
* **Service Provider Twitter 2**: Enter any additional Twitter link of the service provider. (Optional)
* **Service Provider Instagram 2**: Enter any additional Instagram link of the service provider. (Optional)
* **Service Provider Yelp 2**: Enter any additional Yelp link of the service provider. (Optional)
* **Service Provider LinkedIn 2**: Enter any additional LinkedIn link of the service provider. (Optional)
* **Service Provider Description 2**: Enter any additional description of the service provider. (Optional)

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the service listing is complete and effective.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the service provider directly for clarification.
* This form is intended to be filled out by authorized representatives of the service provider.
