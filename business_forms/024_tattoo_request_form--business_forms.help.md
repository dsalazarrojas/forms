# Tattoo Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tattoo Request Form is used to collect information from customers regarding their tattoo requests. This includes their customer information, contact information, tattoo details, preferred designer, design description, design file, tattoo type, tattoo size, and tattoo color.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, if possible. However, if you are unable to complete a field, you can leave it blank.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and relevant information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanations below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Info
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): This field is for the customer to provide their name, address, and any other contact information they are willing to share.
### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is for the customer to provide their contact details, such as phone number and email address.
### Tattoo Details
* **Tattoo Details** (`tattoo_details`, text, required: false): In this field, the customer can describe the design and details of the tattoo they are requesting.
### Preferred Designer
* **Preferred Designer** (`preferred_designer`, select_one, required: false): The customer can choose whether they have a preferred designer or not. Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
### Design Description
* **Design Description** (`design_description`, note, required: false): This field is for the customer to provide a description of the design they are looking for.
### Design File
* **Design File** (`design_file`, text, required: false): If the customer has a design file, they can upload it here.
### Tattoo Type
* **Tattoo Type** (`tattoo_type`, select_one, required: false): The customer can choose whether the tattoo is 'Yes' or 'No'. Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
### Tattoo Size
* **Tattoo Size** (`tattoo_size`, number, required: false): This field is for the customer to indicate the size of the tattoo in inches.
### Tattoo Color
* **Tattoo Color** (`tattoo_color`, select_multiple, required: false): The customer can choose the color of the tattoo from the available options. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your input for accuracy before submission.
* If you need to provide additional information or clarification, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for guidance.
