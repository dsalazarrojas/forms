# Tax Preparer Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from tax preparers and clients for a tax filing service agreement. The form is designed to outline the terms and conditions of the service, including client and tax preparer information, service details, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each section of the form carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to review and agree to the terms of the service before signing.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`client_info`): This section is for the client to provide their details, including name, address, and contact information.
* **Tax Preparer Information** (`tax_preparer_info`): This section is for the tax preparer to provide their details, including name, address, and contact information.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`): This section is for the tax preparer to describe the services they will provide, including the type of tax service and any additional information relevant to the service.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`): This section is for the tax preparer to describe the terms of the service, including any limitations or conditions of the service.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): This section is for the tax preparer to provide their contact information, including name, address, and phone number.
* **Review and Acceptance** (`review`): This section is for the client to review and accept the terms of the service.
* **Date** (`review_date`): This section is for the client to select the date they are reviewing and accepting the terms of the service.
* **Tax Filing Service Agreement** (`tax_filing_service_agreement`): This section is for the tax preparer to provide a statement of the service agreement.
* **Tax Return** (`tax_return`): This section is for the tax preparer to provide any additional information about the tax return.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): This section is for the tax preparer to describe the payment terms of the service.
* **Signature** (`signature`): This section is for the tax preparer to sign the agreement.
* **Payment** (`payment`): This section is for the tax preparer to specify the payment method.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`): This section is for the tax preparer to confirm their agreement to the terms of the service.
* **Review Agreement** (`review_agreement`): This section is for the tax preparer to review and agree to the terms of the service.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): This section is for the tax preparer to specify the method of payment.

## Tips
* Make sure to read and understand the terms of the service before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
