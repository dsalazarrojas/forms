# credit_dispute_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "credit_dispute_report_form" is a tool designed to help consumers report inaccuracies or disputes with their lenders. This form gathers essential information to assist in the dispute resolution process, ensuring that issues are addressed promptly and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Select the "Consumer Information" field and choose your correct name from the dropdown list.
*   Select the "Report Type" field and choose one or more of the available options (Credit Dispute, Account Inaccuracy, or Identity Theft).
*   Provide a "Dispute Description" explaining the accuracy issue you're reporting.
*   Enter the "Date of Dispute" when the issue occurred.
*   Select the "Resolution Status" field and choose between Disputed, Resolved, or In Progress.
*   If necessary, add "Additional Comments" to provide further context.
*   Finally, submit your report by selecting "Submit Now" or "Save for Later" to review and edit your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Report Inaccuracy (Page 1)**:
    *   A text field for describing the inaccuracy you're reporting.
*   **Consumer Information (Page 2)**:
    *   Choose your correct name from the dropdown list to identify yourself as the consumer.
*   **Report Type (Page 3)**:
    *   Select one or more of the available options to describe the type of issue you're experiencing.
*   **Date of Dispute (Page 4)**:
    *   Enter the date when the issue occurred.
*   **Dispute Description (Page 5)**:
    *   Provide a detailed description of the inaccuracy you're reporting.
*   **Resolution Status (Page 6)**:
    *   Select Disputed, Resolved, or In Progress to indicate the current status of your issue.
*   **Contact Lender (Page 7)**:
    *   Enter any contact information for the lender related to the dispute.
*   **Submit Report (Page 8)**:
    *   Select "Submit Now" to submit your report immediately or "Save for Later" to review and edit before submission.
*   **Additional Comments (Page 9)**:
    *   Provide any additional context or information about your issue.
*   **Submit (Page 10)**:
    *   Select "Submit Now" to finalize your submission or "Other" for alternative submission options.

## Tips
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
*   Review each field carefully to avoid submission errors.
*   Save and review your submission before finalizing it.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authority for further assistance.
