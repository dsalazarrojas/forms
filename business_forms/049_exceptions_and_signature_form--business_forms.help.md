# Exceptions and Signature Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by employees to request exceptions, such as time off, travel, or other work-related exceptions. The form will be reviewed and approved by supervisors or managers, and will serve as a record of the exception request and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Exception Request field with a brief description of the exception being requested.
2. Enter the Employee Name, Exception Date, and Exception Description fields as needed.
3. Select all applicable approvers for the exception in the Signature Approvals field.
4. Select the Approver and Approver Email fields as needed for approval process.
5. Enter any comments or notes in the Approver Comments field.
6. Set the Exception Status to Approved, Denied, Pending, or In Progress.
7. Enter the Employee Email and Approver Phone fields as needed.
8. Enter the Exception Category and Exception Subcategory fields as needed.
9. Enter the Exception Description field as needed.
10. Enter the Approver Department field as needed.
11. Enter the Approver Title field as needed.
12. Finalize the form by clicking the Submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exception Request**: A short description of the exception being requested. (text)
* **Employee Name**: The name of the employee requesting the exception. (text, required)
* **Exception Date**: The date of the exception request. (date)
* **Justification**: A brief description of the exception request. (text)
* **Signature Approvals**: Select all applicable approvers for the exception. (select multiple)
* **Approved By**: Select the approver who approved the exception. (select one, required)
* **Signature**: A signature from the employee. (text)
* **Comments**: Any comments or notes related to the exception. (text)
* **Approver Comments**: Any comments or notes from the approver. (text)
* **Approval Date**: The date of approval. (date)
* **Exception Status**: The status of the exception (Approved, Denied, Pending, or In Progress). (select one)
* **Employee Email**: The email of the employee requesting the exception. (email)
* **Approver Email**: The email of the approver. (email)
* **Exception Type**: A description of the exception type. (text)
* **Reason for Exception**: A reason for the exception. (text)
* **Exception ID**: A unique identifier for the exception. (text)
* **Employee Title**: The title of the employee requesting the exception. (text)
* **Exception Description**: A description of the exception. (text)
* **Exception Category**: The category of the exception. (text)
* **Exception Subcategory**: The subcategory of the exception. (text)
* **Approver**: The name of the approver. (text)
* **Approver Email**: The email of the approver. (email)
* **Approver Phone**: The phone number of the approver. (text)
* **Approver Department**: The department of the approver. (text)
* **Approver Title**: The title of the approver. (text)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or manager.
