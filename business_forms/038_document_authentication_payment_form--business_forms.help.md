# Document Authentication Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Document Authentication Payment Form is designed to collect information related to document authentication and payment from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select relevant options for the "Select One" field (Page 2).
2. Choose multiple options for the "Select Multiple" field (Page 2).
3. Enter a date for the "Document Authentication Payment Form Date" field (Page 5).
4. Enter a time for the "Document Authentication Payment Form Time" field (Page 5).
5. Enter notes or comments in the "Document Authentication Payment Form Note" and "Document Authentication Payment Form Note 2" fields (Pages 3 and 7).
6. Enter an email address for the "Document Authentication Payment Form Email" field (Page 8).
7. Enter a phone number for the "Document Authentication Payment Form Phone" field (Page 8).
8. Enter a numerical value for the "Document Authentication Payment Form Integer" field (Page 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Document Authentication Payment Form Page** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the main page of the form. Please fill in this field only if instructed to do so.
* **Select One** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one option from the list provided. Select only one option.
* **Select Multiple** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose multiple options from the list provided. Select all applicable options.
* **Document Authentication Payment Form Date** (`4`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date related to the document authentication and payment.
* **Document Authentication Payment Form Time** (`5`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time related to the document authentication and payment.
* **Document Authentication Payment Form Note** (`6`, `note`, required: false): Enter additional notes or comments about the document authentication and payment.
* **Document Authentication Payment Form Email** (`7`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address related to the document authentication and payment.
* **Document Authentication Payment Form Phone** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number related to the document authentication and payment.
* **Document Authentication Payment Form Note 2** (`9`, `note`, required: false): Enter additional notes or comments about the document authentication and payment.
* **Select Multiple 2** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose multiple options from the list provided. Select all applicable options.
* **Document Authentication Payment Form Integer** (`11`, `number`, required: false): Enter a numerical value related to the document authentication and payment.

## Tips
* Double-check your selections and entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the form owner or contact support for clarification.
