# Order Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Submission Form is a crucial tool for customers to submit their orders to our company. This form allows customers to provide essential details about themselves and their orders, ensuring that we receive accurate and complete information for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Enter your phone number for us to contact you with updates on your order.
3. Provide a brief description of your order in the "order details" field.
4. Specify the item(s) you are ordering in the "order item" field.
5. Indicate the status of your order and the total amount you are paying for it in the "order status" and "order total" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your active email address where we can contact you with updates on your order.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates on your order.
- **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Briefly describe your order, e.g., "I'm ordering a custom product for delivery next week."
- **Order Item** (`order_item`, text, required): Specify the item(s) you are ordering, e.g., "Product A" or "Product B."
- **Order Status** (`order_status`, text, required): Indicate the status of your order, e.g., "pending," "shipped," or "delivered."
- **Order Total** (`order_total`, text, required): Enter the total amount you are paying for your order.
