# Business Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Registration Form is designed to collect essential information about a business, including its details, owner information, contact details, startup data, and compliance status. This form is intended for businesses that have not been previously registered.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in sections, starting from the top and working your way down.
2. Ensure you have all required information available before starting.
3. Enter your answers accurately and completely.
4. Review your answers for any errors or inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Details
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the official name of your business.
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose the type of business you are registering (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Limited Liability Company, Corporation, Non-Profit Organization, Cooperative, Other).
* **Industry or Sector** (`business_industry`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the primary industry or sector of your business.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, `text`, **Required**): Provide a brief overview of what your business does.

### Owner Information
* **Owner First Name** (`owner_first_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the owner's first name.
* **Owner Last Name** (`owner_last_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the owner's last name.
* **Owner Date of Birth** (`owner_date_of_birth`, `date`, **Optional**): Enter the owner's date of birth, if applicable.
* **Owner ID Number** (`owner_id_number`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the owner's social security number or business ID, if applicable.

### Business Location
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the business street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the city where the business is located.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the state or province where the business is located.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the postal or zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the country of operation.

### Contact Information
* **Primary Phone Number** (`primary_phone`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the main business phone number.
* **Secondary Phone Number** (`secondary_phone`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the alternate contact number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, **Required**): Enter the business email address.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the business website URL, if applicable.

### Startup Information
* **Business Start Date** (`startup_date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date the business was launched.
* **Startup Capital** (`startup_capital`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the initial investment amount.
* **Expected Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the current or planned employee count.

### Compliance Acknowledgment
* **Have You Applied for Tax ID** (`tax_id_applied`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose whether you have applied for a tax ID (True, False, In Progress).
* **Required Licenses or Permits Obtained** (`licenses_permits`, `text`, **Optional**): List any required licenses or permits obtained.
* **I Agree to Comply with All Regulations** (`agreement_acceptance`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose whether you agree to comply with all regulations (Yes, I agree, No, I do not agree).
