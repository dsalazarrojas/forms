It allows users to evaluate and document key aspects of each opportunity, including its status, description, location, and associated contact information.

1. Fill in the form with the required information for each opportunity.
2. Ensure that you are using the form for its intended purpose and do not use it for other purposes to avoid duplication of effort.

* **Opportunity ID** (`opportunity_id`, number, required: false): Enter a unique identifier for the opportunity.
* **Opportunity Name** (`opportunity_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the opportunity.
* **Opportunity Status** (`opportunity_status`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the status of the opportunity from the options: New, Open, Closed.
* **Opportunity Description** (`opportunity_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the opportunity.
* **Opportunity Location** (`opportunity_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the opportunity.
* **Opportunity Date** (`opportunity_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date related to the opportunity.
* **Opportunity Time** (`opportunity_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time related to the opportunity.
* **Opportunity Notes** (`opportunity_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the opportunity.
* **Opportunity Email** (`opportunity_email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact email for the opportunity.
* **Opportunity Phone** (`opportunity_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number for the opportunity.
* **Opportunity Website** (`opportunity_website`, text, required: false): Enter the website related to the opportunity.
* **Opportunity Address** (`opportunity_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address related to the opportunity.
