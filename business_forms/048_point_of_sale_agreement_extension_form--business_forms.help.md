# Point Of Sale Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Point Of Sale Agreement Extension Form is used to collect information and agreement terms for clients and businesses, including business details, payment terms, payment status, extension type, contract terms, and more. This form helps ensure that all parties involved are on the same page and understand their responsibilities and obligations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the client and business.
2. Select the correct agreement type (Agreement or Non-agreement).
3. Choose the payment terms, frequency, and method that apply to the agreement.
4. Enter any additional payment notes as needed.
5. Provide the client's and business's contact information.
6. Select the payment status and extension type.
7. Enter any contract terms that apply to the agreement.
8. Review the terms of service and enter any additional comments or notes as needed.

* **business_details**: Enter a brief description of the client's business or project.

* **agreement_end_date**: Enter the end date of the agreement.
* **agreement_term**: Enter a description of the agreement term.
* **client_details**: Enter a brief description of the client's details or project.
* **payment_terms**: Select any payment terms that apply to the agreement (e.g., Payment term 1, Payment term 2, Payment term 3).
* **payment_frequency**: Select the payment frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **payment_amount**: Enter the payment amount.
* **payment_method**: Select the payment method (e.g., Credit card, Cash, Check).
