# Innovation Consulting Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals or organizations interested in joining the Innovation Consulting Network. It's a crucial step for registration and inclusion in the network.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field. This is a required field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
4. If you want to be contacted, fill in your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
5. Enter your Company name in the "Company" field (optional).
6. Enter your Job Title in the "Job Title" field (optional).
7. Select your expertise areas from the "Expertise Areas" dropdown menu (optional).
8. Describe your interests in the "Interests" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text, Required/Optional)**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (Text, Required/Optional)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents. This is a required field.
* **Email (Email, Required/Optional)**: Enter a valid email address that you use for communication. This is a required field.
* **Phone Number (Text, Required/Optional)**: If you want to be contacted, enter your phone number.
* **Company (Text, Required/Optional)**: Enter your company name if you represent a organization.
* **Job Title (Text, Required/Optional)**: If applicable, enter your job title within your company or organization.
* **Expertise Areas (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select all areas where you have expertise to help the network understand your capabilities.
* **Interests (Text, Optional)**: Describe any topics or areas where you have interests that can help drive the network's innovation.
