# Manufacturing Process Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for manufacturing process partners to provide their contact information and details about their manufacturing capabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your company in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide your company's website in the "Company Website" field.
3. Enter the name of the person who will be the point of contact for your company.
4. If you offer any of the listed manufacturing processes, select them in the "Manufacturing Process Partners" field. You can select multiple processes.
5. If you selected a manufacturing process, provide a brief description in the "Select at least one type" field to help the company understand your capabilities.
6. If you don't offer any of the listed manufacturing processes, leave the "Manufacturing Process Partners" field blank.
7. Enter any other information you want to provide in the "Manufacturing Capabilities" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`Company Details`, required/optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, optional): Enter the name of the person who will be the point of contact for your company.
* **Company Website** (`Company Website`, optional): Enter your company's website.
* **Email** (`Email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Manufacturing Capabilities** (`Manufacturing Capabilities`, optional): Enter any other information you want to provide about your manufacturing capabilities.
* **Manufacturing Process Partners** (`Manufacturing Process Partners`, optional): Select "Yes" if you offer at least one of the listed manufacturing processes. If you don't offer a process, leave this field blank.
* **Select at least one type** (`Manufacturing Process Partners Option Value`, optional): Provide a brief description of the manufacturing process you selected to help the company understand your capabilities.
* **What do you manufacture?** (`Manufacturing Process Partners Options`, required): Select at least one of the manufacturing processes you offer. This will help the company understand your capabilities and potentially identify opportunities for collaboration.
* **Select at least one type** (`Manufacturing Process Partners Option Label`, optional): Not applicable as it's a redundant field.
