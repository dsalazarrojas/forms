# Emergency Role Assignment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Emergency Role Assignment Form is a critical document used to record and manage emergency contact information and role assignments for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Emergency Contact Name
	* Manager Name and Phone (if assigned to Manager)
	* Team Member Name and Phone (if assigned to Team Member)
	* Other Name and Phone (if assigned to Other)
2. Select the emergency contact relationship from the dropdown menu:
	* Mother or Father
	* Brother or Sister
	* Son or Daughter
	* Spouse or Partner
	* Other
3. If assigned to Manager, Team Member, or Other, select the relevant option from the dropdown menu:
	* Manager
	* Team Member
	* Other
4. Fill in the other fields as needed:
	* Emergency Contact Phone (if applicable)
	* Emergency Contact Email (if applicable)
	* Date of Last Emergency (if applicable)
	* Time of Last Emergency (if applicable)
	* Number of Emergencies (if applicable)
	* Total Emergency Calls (if applicable)
	* Status (if applicable)
	* Comments (if applicable)
5. Review and submit the form to update the emergency contact information and role assignments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: The first name of the employee or emergency contact person.
* **Last Name (2)**: The last name of the employee or emergency contact person.
* **Email (3)**: The email address of the employee or emergency contact person.
* **Phone (4)**: The phone number of the employee or emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name (5)**: The name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (6)**: The relationship between the employee and emergency contact person (e.g., mother, father, brother, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone (7)**: The phone number of the emergency contact person (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Email (8)**: The email address of the emergency contact person (if applicable).
* **Assigned To (9)**: The person or entity assigned to in case of an emergency (e.g., Manager, Team Member, Other).
* **Date of Last Emergency (10)**: The date of the last emergency (if applicable).
* **Time of Last Emergency (11)**: The time of the last emergency (if applicable).
* **Number of Emergencies (12)**: The number of emergencies experienced by the employee or emergency contact person (if applicable).
* **Total Emergency Calls (13)**: The total number of emergency calls made to the employee or emergency contact person (if applicable).
* **Emergency Role Assignment Form Status (14)**: The status of the form (e.g., In Progress, Completed, Not Started, On Hold).
* **Emergency Role Assignment Form Comments (15)**: Additional comments or notes about the form.
* **Manager Name (16)**: The name of the manager assigned to in case of an emergency.
* **Manager Phone (17)**: The phone number of the manager assigned to in case of an emergency.
* **Manager Email (18)**: The email address of the manager assigned to in case of an emergency.
* **Team Member Name (19)**: The name of the team member assigned to in case of an emergency.
* **Team Member Phone (20)**: The phone number of the team member assigned to in case of an emergency.
* **Team Member Email (21)**: The email address of the team member assigned to in case of an emergency.
* **Other Name (22)**: The name of the person or entity assigned to in case of an emergency.
* **Other Phone (23)**: The phone number of the person or entity assigned to in case of an emergency.
* **Other Email (24)**: The email address of the person or entity assigned to in case of an emergency.
* **Assigned To Other (25)**: The person or entity assigned to in case of an emergency (if applicable).
* **Form Submitted (26)**: Indicates whether the form has been submitted or not (if applicable).

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and relevantly to avoid confusion and ensure that emergency contact roles are properly managed.
