# Order Fulfillment Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track the status and progress of customer orders. It helps to confirm that orders are being accurately and efficiently fulfilled, which can improve customer satisfaction and overall business performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the correct options for the "Payment Confirmation" field, which confirms whether payment has been received for the order.
2. Enter the shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
3. Enter the shipping tracking number in the "Shipping Tracking Number" field.
4. Select the status of the order in the "Order Status" field.
5. Enter the shipping date and time in the "Shipping Date" and "Shipping Time" fields, respectively.
6. Enter any relevant contact information for the shipping contact person in the "Shipping Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Payment Confirmation** (`payment_confirmation`, select_one, Optional): Confirm whether payment has been received for the order. Select "Yes" if payment is confirmed, "No" otherwise.
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, Optional): Enter the shipping address for the order.
- **Shipping Tracking Number** (`shipping_tracking_number`, text, Optional): Enter the tracking number for the shipment.
- **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, Optional): Select the current status of the order (Active or Inactive).
- **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date of shipment.
- **Shipping Time** (`shipping_time`, time, Optional): Enter the time of shipment.
- **Shipping Contact** (`shipping_contact`, text, Optional): Enter any relevant contact information for the shipping contact person.
