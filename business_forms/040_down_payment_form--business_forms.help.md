# Down Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Down Payment Form is used to capture down payment information from clients. This includes details such as client name, down payment date, payment method, amount paid, payment status, notes, assigned tool, client email, and payment date/time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Select the down payment date using the calendar icon.
3. Choose the payment method from the options "Visa", "Mastercard", "Amex", or select "Other Payment Method" if none of the above apply.
4. Enter the amount paid in the "Amount Paid" field.
5. Select the payment status from the options "Paid", "Unpaid", or "Partially Paid".
6. Add any additional notes about the payment in the "Notes" field.
7. Assign a tool to the client in the "Assigned Tool" field.
8. Enter the client's email address in the "Client Email" field.
9. (Optional) Select the payment date and time using the time picker.
10. (Optional) If "Other Payment Method" is selected, choose additional payment methods from the options "Bank Transfer", "Online banking", "Check".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the client.
* **Down Payment Date** (date, required): Select the date of the down payment.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required): Choose a payment method from "Visa", "Mastercard", "Amex", or "Other Payment Method".
* **Amount Paid** (number, required): Enter the amount paid.
* **Payment Status** (select_multiple, required): Select the payment status from "Paid", "Unpaid", or "Partially Paid".
* **Notes** (text, optional): Add any additional notes about the payment.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, required): Assign a tool to the client.
* **Client Email** (email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Payment Date/Time** (time, optional): Select the payment date and time.
* **Other Payment Method** (select_multiple, optional): If "Other Payment Method" is selected, choose additional payment methods from "Bank Transfer", "Online banking", "Check".
