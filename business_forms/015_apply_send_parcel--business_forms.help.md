# Apply Send Parcel - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about sending a parcel, allowing users to provide details about the sender and recipient, parcel characteristics, and shipping preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the submission date of the parcel.
2. Provide the full name, email address, company (if applicable), phone number, address, city, state, and country of the sender.
3. Enter the name, email address (if applicable), phone number, address, city, state, and country of the recipient.
4. Specify the type of parcel being sent (e.g., document, small package, medium package, etc.).
5. Describe the contents of the parcel.
6. Enter the weight of the parcel in pounds or kilograms.
7. If applicable, provide the parcel's dimensions (length x width x height).
8. Declare the value of the contents of the parcel.
9. Choose the desired shipping speed (e.g., standard, express, overnight, same day, economy).
10. Select the preferred pickup date and time (if different from the submission date).
11. Decide if insurance coverage is required.
12. Specify if a signature is required on delivery.
13. Add any special handling instructions.
14. Choose the payment method (e.g., credit card, debit card, account billing, cash on pickup, PayPal).
15. Add any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you'd like to submit the parcel.
* **Sender Name** (`sender_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the sender.
* **Sender Email** (`sender_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the sender.
* **Sender Company** (`sender_company`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the company name of the sender.
* **Sender Phone** (`sender_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the sender.
* **Sender Address** (`sender_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of the sender.
* **Sender City** (`sender_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the sender's address.
* **Sender State** (`sender_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of the sender's address.
* **Sender Zip** (`sender_zip`, text, required): Enter the postal or zip code of the sender's address.
* **Sender Country** (`sender_country`, select_one, required): Select the country of origin of the sender.
* **Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the recipient.
* **Recipient Company** (`recipient_company`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the company name of the recipient.
* **Recipient Email** (`recipient_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the recipient (if applicable).
* **Recipient Phone** (`recipient_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the recipient.
* **Recipient Address** (`recipient_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of the recipient.
* **Recipient City** (`recipient_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the recipient's address.
* **Recipient State** (`recipient_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of the recipient's address.
* **Recipient Zip** (`recipient_zip`, text, required): Enter the postal or zip code of the recipient's address.
* **Recipient Country** (`recipient_country`, select_one, required): Select the destination country of the recipient.
* **Parcel Type** (`parcel_type`, select_one, required): Specify the type of parcel being sent (e.g., document, small package, etc.).
* **Parcel Description** (`parcel_description`, text, required): Describe the contents of the parcel.
* **Parcel Weight** (`parcel_weight`, number, required): Enter the weight of the parcel in pounds or kilograms.
* **Parcel Dimensions** (`parcel_dimensions`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the dimensions of the parcel (length x width x height).
* **Parcel Value** (`parcel_value`, number, required): Declare the value of the contents of the parcel.
* **Shipping Speed** (`shipping_speed`, select_one, required): Choose the desired shipping speed (e.g., standard, express, overnight, same day, economy).
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required): Select the preferred pickup date.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, select_one, optional): Select the preferred pickup time (if different from the submission date).
* **Insurance Required** (`insurance_required`, select_one, required): Decide if insurance coverage is required.
* **Signature Required** (`signature_required`, select_one, required): Specify if a signature is required on delivery.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special handling instructions.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose the payment method (e.g., credit card, debit card, account billing, cash on pickup, PayPal).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.

## Tips

* Be sure to review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Make sure to enter all required fields to avoid incomplete submissions.
* Use the correct format for dates, phone numbers, and email addresses.
* If you have any special handling instructions or requirements, be sure to include them in the "Special Instructions" field.
* Double-check the shipping speed and pickup date and time to ensure they meet your needs.
* Choose the correct payment method to suit your payment preferences.
* Enter any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.
