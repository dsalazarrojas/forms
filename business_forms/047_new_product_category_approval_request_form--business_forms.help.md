# New Product Category Approval Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The New Product Category Approval Request Form is used to request approval for new product categories in your business. This form is used by product owners to submit their product category requests, and approvers to review and approve or reject these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the product category information page (page 1).
2. Select the product category you want to request approval for.
3. Fill out the product details page (page 2).
4. Fill out the approver and submitter information (pages 3-4).
5. Add any additional notes or comments as needed.
6. Review your submission for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Category** (`new_product_category_approval_request_form_page1`, Select One, Required): Select the product category you want to request approval for. This will be one of Apple, Banana, or Carrot.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the product you are requesting approval for.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the product you are requesting approval for.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, Number, Optional): Enter the price of the product you are requesting approval for, if applicable.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, Text, Optional): Attach any relevant images of the product.
* **Product Video** (`product_video`, Text, Optional): Attach any relevant videos of the product.
* **Product Document** (`product_document`, Text, Optional): Attach any relevant documents related to the product.
* **Product URL** (`product_url`, Text, Optional): Enter the URL of the product, if applicable.
* **Product Tags** (`product_tags`, Text, Optional): Add any relevant tags or keywords for the product.
* **Product Status** (`product_status`, Select One, Required): Select the status of the product, which can be Approved, Pending, or Rejected.
* **Product Status Note** (`product_status_note`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the product status.
* **Product Approver** (`product_approver`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select who will be the approver for the product.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select who will be the submitter for the product.
* **Submitter Note** (`submitter_note`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional notes or comments from the submitter.
* **Approver Note** (`approver_note`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional notes or comments from the approver.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, Date, Optional): Select the date of submission, if applicable.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, Time, Optional): Select the time of submission, if applicable.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional notes or comments from the submitter.
* **Approver** (`approver`, Select One, Required): Select the approver who will review and approve or reject the request.
* **Approver Note 2** (`approver_note_2`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional notes or comments from the approver.
* **Submission Date 2** (`submission_date_2`, Date, Optional): Select the date of submission, if applicable.
* **Submission Time 2** (`submission_time_2`, Time, Optional): Select the time of submission, if applicable.
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional notes or comments from the submitter.
* **Approver Confirmation** (`approver_confirmation`, Select One, Required): Select whether the approver has confirmed or not confirmed the request.
