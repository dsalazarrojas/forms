# governance_and_transparency_partner_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "governance_and_transparency_partner_contact_form" is a form designed to collect contact information from partners and stakeholders for governance and transparency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your contact information.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Submit the form once you have reviewed and double-checked your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (Name)** (`text`, required):
	+ Enter your name as it appears on official documents or communication.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, required):
	+ Enter your email address that you use for professional or official purposes.
* **Message (Message)** (`text`, required):
	+ Provide a clear and concise message regarding your inquiry or concern.
* **Partner Name (Partner Name)** (`text`, required):
	+ Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Partner Email (Partner Email)** (`email`, required):
	+ Enter the email address associated with your organization or company.
* **Partner Message (Partner Message)** (`text`, required):
	+ Describe your inquiry or concern related to your organization or company.
