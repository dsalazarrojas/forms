# EPW Financial Fact Find - Help Guide
## Purpose

The EPW Financial Fact Find form is designed to gather information about businesses and their financial facts. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure the EPW Financial Fact Find process can proceed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the business type from the dropdown menu on page 1.
2. If the business type is "Yes", proceed to page 2 and complete the address, city, zip code, phone number, email, and date established fields.
3. Continue to complete each page in sequence, providing accurate information for each field.
4. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the business is eligible for the EPW Financial Fact Find process.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the business address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city where the business is located.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, required/optional): Enter the business zip code.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the business phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the business email.
* **Date Established** (`date_established`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the business was established.
* **Employee Count** (`employee_count`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of employees in the business.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, required/optional): Enter the business industry.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the business location.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, number, required/optional): Enter the business annual revenue.
* **Employee Range** (`employee_range`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the business has an employee range that fits within the criteria.
* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the business is a financial institution.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of employees in the business.
* **Employee Range 2** (`employee_range_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the business has an employee range that fits within the criteria.
* **Business Sector** (`business_sector`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the business is in a specific sector.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required/optional): Enter the business website.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, text, required/optional): Enter the business social media handles.
* **Employee Range 3** (`employee_range_3`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the business has an employee range that fits within the criteria.
* **Company Description** (`company_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the business.
* **CEO Name** (`ceo_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the CEO's name.
* **CEO Title** (`ceo_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the CEO's title.
* **Employee Range 4** (`employee_range_4`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the business has an employee range that fits within the criteria.
* **CEO Email** (`ceo_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the CEO's email.
* **CEO Phone Number** (`ceo_phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the CEO's phone number.

## Tips

* Double-check each field before proceeding to the next page.
* Ensure that you have permission to enter accurate and up-to-date information about the business.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
