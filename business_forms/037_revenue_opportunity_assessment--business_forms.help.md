# Revenue Opportunity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Revenue Opportunity Assessment form is designed to gather information about a 
revenue opportunity, including its name, close probability, value, sales revenue, 
customer contact information, and sales team involvement. This form helps sales 
teams assess and track revenue opportunities in a structured and standardized way.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Opportunity Name**: Enter the name of the revenue opportunity. This is a 
    required field that cannot be left blank.
2.  **Close Probability**: Enter the close probability as a decimal value between 0 
    and 100%. This is a required field that cannot be left blank.
3.  **Value**: Enter the value of the revenue opportunity. This is a required 
    field that cannot be left blank.
4.  **Sales Revenue**: Enter the sales revenue for the opportunity. This is a 
    required field that cannot be left blank.
5.  **Customer Contact**: Enter the contact information for the customer, including 
    their name, company, and phone number. These fields are required.
6.  **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer. This is a required field 
    that cannot be left blank.
7.  **Customer Company**: Enter the name of the customer's company. This is a 
    required field that cannot be left blank.
8.  **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number. This is a required field 
    that cannot be left blank.
9.  **Sales Team**: Select all applicable sales teams involved in the 
    opportunity. This field is not required.
10. **Opportunity Status**: Select the status of the opportunity, such as New, 
    Lead, or Closed - Lost. This is a required field that cannot be left blank.
11.  **Sales Stage**: Select the sales stage of the opportunity, such as Stage 
    1, Stage 2, or Stage 3. This is a required field that cannot be 
    left blank.
12.  **Close Date**: Enter the close date of the opportunity. This is a 
    required field that cannot be left blank.
13.  **Close Time**: Enter the close time of the opportunity. This field is not 
    required.
14.  **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's address. This field is not 
    required.
15.  **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the opportunity. This field is 
    not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Opportunity Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the revenue 
    opportunity.
*  **Close Probability** (2, number, required): Enter the close probability as a 
    decimal value between 0 and 100%.
*  **Value** (3, number, required): Enter the value of the revenue 
    opportunity.
*  **Sales Revenue** (4, number, required): Enter the sales revenue for the 
    opportunity.
*  **Customer Contact** (5, text, required): Enter the contact 
    information for the customer, including their name, company, and phone 
    number.
*  **Customer Name** (21, text, required): Enter the name of the 
    customer.
*  **Customer Company** (22, text, required): Enter the name of the 
    customer's company.
*  **Customer Phone** (24, text, required): Enter the customer's phone 
    number.
*  **Sales Team** (7, select_multiple, not required): Select all 
    applicable sales teams involved in the opportunity.
*  **Opportunity Status** (8, select_one, required): Select the status of 
    the opportunity, such as New, Lead, or Closed - Lost.
*  **Sales Stage** (9, select_one, required): Select the sales stage of 
    the opportunity, such as Stage 1, Stage 2, or Stage 3.
*  **Close Date** (10, date, required): Enter the close date of the 
    opportunity.
*  **Close Time** (11, time, not required): Enter the close time of 
    the opportunity.
*  **Sales Manager** (12, text, not required): Enter the sales manager's 
    name.
*  **Region** (13, text, not required): Enter the region.
*  **Territory** (14, select_one, not required): Select the territory.
*  **Account Manager** (15, text, not required): Enter the account 
    manager's name.
*  **Sales Rep** (16, text, not required): Enter the sales rep's 
    name.
*  **Sales Manager Email** (17, email, not required): Enter the sales 
    manager's email.
*  **Region Manager** (18, text, not required): Enter the region 
    manager's name.
*  **Account Manager Email** (19, email, not required): Enter the 
    account manager's email.
*  **Sales Rep Email** (20, email, not required): Enter the sales rep's 
    email.
*  **Customer Address** (23, text, not required): Enter the customer's 
    address.
*  **Notes** (25, text, not required): Enter any additional notes about 
    the opportunity.

## Tips
*  Before filling out this form, make sure to gather all relevant 
    information about the revenue opportunity.
*  If you are unsure about a field, please do not leave it blank, as this 
    may affect the accuracy of the opportunity assessment.
*  Please make sure to select all applicable sales teams involved in 
    the opportunity.
*  Use the customer contact information to reach out to the customer and 
    discuss the opportunity.
*  Keep the customer address field blank if it is not necessary to track.
*  Use the notes field to add any additional information about the 
    opportunity.
*  Review and update the opportunity regularly to reflect changes in its 
    status and sales stage.
