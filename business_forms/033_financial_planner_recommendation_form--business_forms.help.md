# financial_planner_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "financial_planner_recommendation_form" is designed to collect information about your financial situation to provide personalized recommendations for investment planning and advice. This form will help the financial advisor understand your financial goals, risk tolerance, and other relevant factors to provide tailored guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please complete each field carefully, providing as much detail as possible. Ensure that all required fields are completed, and if you are unsure about any option, choose the most accurate answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, **Required**): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Birth Date** (`birthdate`, date, **Not Required**): If you are filling out this form, it is recommended to provide your birthdate for demographic information.
- **Income** (`income`, number, **Required**): Enter your current annual income.
- **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, **Not Required**): Choose one of the following:
  - Employed
  - Self-Employed
  - Unemployed
  - Retired
- **Income Source** (`income_source`, select_multiple, **Not Required**): Select all applicable sources of your income:
  - Business
  - Investments
  - Other (please specify)
- **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, number, **Required**): Choose your risk tolerance level:
  - 1 (Lowest)
  - 2
  - 3 (Medium)
  - 4
  - 5 (Highest)
- **Investment Assets** (`investment_assets`, number, **Required**): Enter the total value of your current investment assets.
- **Risk Awareness** (`risk_awareness`, select_multiple, **Required**): Select all applicable risk awareness levels:
  - Medium
  - High
  - Medium High
  - High High
- **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`, select_multiple, **Required**): Choose your financial goals:
  - Short Term (less than 1 year)
  - Long Term (5+ years)
  - Medium Term (1-5 years)
- **Investment Expertise** (`investment_expertise`, select_multiple, **Not Required**): Select all levels of your investment expertise:
  - Beginner
  - Intermediate
  - Advanced
- **Investment Frequency** (`investment_frequency`, select_multiple, **Not Required**): Select your investment frequency:
  - Infrequent
  - Occasional
  - Frequent
  - Very Frequent
- **Financial Planner** (`financial_planner`, select_one, **Not Required**): Choose your financial planning preference:
  - Financial Advisor
  - Financial Planner
  - Planner Advisor
- **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, **Not Required**): Choose your preferred contact method:
  - Phone
  - Email
  - Mail
- **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, select_multiple, **Not Required**): Select your contact preferences:
  - Morning
  - Afternoon
  - Evening
- **Email** (`email`, email, **Not Required**): Enter your email address (if applicable).
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Not Required**): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
- **Investment Assets Value** (`investment_assets_value`, number, **Not Required**): Enter the value of your investment assets (if applicable).
- **Financial Institute** (`financial_institute`, select_multiple, **Not Required**): Select all applicable financial institutions:
  - Fidelity
  - Charles Schwab
  - T.D. Apex
- **Investor Type** (`investment_investor_type`, select_one, **Not Required**): Choose your investor type:
  - Individual
  - Joint
  - Trust
- **Investor Status** (`investment_investor_status`, select_one, **Not Required**): Choose your investor status:
  - Active
  - Inactive
- **Account Type** (`account_type`, select_one, **Required**): Choose your account type:
  - Individual
  - Joint
  - Trust
- **Account Status** (`account_status`, select_one, **Not Required**): Choose your account status:
  - Active
  - Inactive
- **Account Investor** (`account_investor`, select_one, **Not Required**): Choose your account investor type:
  - Individual
  - Joint
  - Trust
- **Account Investor Type** (`account_investor_type`, select_one, **Not Required**): Choose your account investor type:
  - Active
  - Inactive

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact a financial advisor for guidance.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the financial planner directly.
