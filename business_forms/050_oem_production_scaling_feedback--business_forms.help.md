# oem_production_scaling_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from OEMs on their experiences with our production scaling processes. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions to scale our production effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review and submit your form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **oem_partner** (`oem_partner`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your company or the name of the partner we are working with.
* **production_capacity** (`production_capacity`, number, optional): If applicable, please provide the current production capacity of your company or partner.
* **scaling_challenges** (`scaling_challenges`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more challenges you have experienced with our production scaling processes:
	+ Scaling Capacity
	+ Quality Issues
	+ Production Delays
* **support_needs** (`support_needs`, select_one, required): Please select the type of support you need from the following options:
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Scheduling
	+ Quality
* **comments** (`comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **oem_contact_info** (`oem_contact_info`, text, optional): If you have any contact information for your company or partner, please provide it here.
* **partner_feedback** (`partner_feedback`, text, optional): If you have any feedback for our partner or vendor, please provide it here.
* **vendor_contact_info** (`vendor_contact_info`, text, optional): If you have any contact information for our vendor, please provide it here.
