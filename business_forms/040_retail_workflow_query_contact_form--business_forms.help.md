# Retail Workflow Query Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather queries, feedback, and contact information from customers, employees, or partners regarding retail-related issues or questions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, provide your contact information:
	* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
2. Select one or more options that best describe your query or issue:
	* Choose "Yes" or "No" in the "Select Multiple" field, depending on your query or issue.
3. Provide a specific date for your query or issue (if applicable):
	* Enter the date related to your query or issue in the "Date" field.
4. Add any additional information you would like to provide:
	* Select one of the options in the "Additional Information" field to provide further context.
5. Provide a brief description of your query or issue:
	* Enter a brief summary in the "Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address associated with your query or issue.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate the status of your query or issue.
* **Date** (`date`, required/optional): Provide a specific date related to your query or issue.
* **Note** (`note`, required/optional): You can add any additional comments or details in this field.
* **Additional Information** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the options to provide further context about your query or issue.
* **Submit** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your query or issue to submit the form.
