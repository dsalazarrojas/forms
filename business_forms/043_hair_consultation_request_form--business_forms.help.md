# Hair Consultation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to request hair consultation services. It captures information about the customer's hair type, concerns, desired services, and other relevant details to help our stylists better understand their needs and provide personalized advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information, including your name, hair type, and preferred contact details.
2. Select your hair type from the options provided.
3. Choose any hair concerns you're experiencing, such as dandruff or dryness.
4. Select the services you desire from our stylists, such as a haircut or color.
5. If you have any specific hair length, you can enter it in the corresponding field.
6. Choose your preferred hair color.
7. Select a date and time for your appointment, if you have a specific preference.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your request to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information**: Please enter your name, phone number, and email address so we can contact you to confirm your appointment and provide updates.
- **Hair Type**: Select the type of hair you have from the options provided (Blonde, Brown, or Red).
- **Hair Concerns**: Choose any hair concerns you're experiencing, such as dandruff, dryness, or oilyness.
- **Desired Services**: Select the services you want from our stylists, such as a haircut, color, or style.
- **Hair Length**: If you have a specific hair length, please enter it in this field.
- **Hair Color**: Choose your preferred hair color (Red, Brown, or Blonde).
- **Appointment Date**: Select a date for your appointment, or leave it blank if you're not sure yet.
- **Preferred Time**: Choose a time for your appointment, or leave it blank if you're flexible.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you to confirm your appointment and provide updates.
- **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with appointment reminders and updates.
- **Message**: If you have any specific details or questions, please enter them here.
- **Submit**: Click this button to send your request to our team.
