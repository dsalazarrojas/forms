# Government Service Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Government Service Waiver Form is used to waive or exempt an individual or organization from a specific government service or fee. This form is used to request a waiver or exemption due to various reasons, such as financial hardship, exceptional circumstances, or other valid grounds.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please select "Individual" or "Organization"**: Choose whether you are submitting on behalf of an individual or an organization.
2. **Enter your Name**: Provide your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or business registration.
3. **Enter your Address**: Provide your physical address or the address of the organization you represent.
4. **Enter your Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
5. **Enter your Phone Number**: Provide your phone number where you can be reached.
6. **Select Service Type**: Choose the type of service you are requesting a waiver or exemption for (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
7. **Enter Reason for Waiver**: Describe the reason you are requesting a waiver or exemption (optional).
8. **Select Signature**: Choose the type of signature (Option 1 or Option 2).
9. **Enter Date**: Provide the date you are requesting the waiver or exemption.
10. **Enter Time**: Provide the time you are requesting the waiver or exemption (optional).
11. **Enter Service Fee**: Provide the amount of service fee you are requesting a waiver or exemption for.
12. **Enter Waiver Amount**: Provide the amount of waiver or exemption you are requesting.
13. **Select Government Agency**: Choose the government agency providing the service (Option 1 or Option 2).
14. **Enter Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information or comments regarding your request.
15. **Enter Service Fee Proof**: Provide any supporting documentation or proof for your service fee.
16. **Enter Waiver Letter**: Upload any relevant documents or letters supporting your waiver request.
17. **Enter Service Fee Certificate**: Upload any relevant certificates or documents supporting your service fee.
18. **Enter Additional Documents**: Upload any additional documents or evidence supporting your waiver request.
19. **Enter Government Agency Address**: Provide the address of the government agency providing the service.
20. **Enter Service Fee Certificate Date**: Provide the date the service fee certificate was issued.
21. **Enter Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information or comments regarding your request.
22. **Enter Service Fee Certificate Number**: Provide the number of the service fee certificate.
23. **Enter Additional Comments 2**: Provide any additional information or comments regarding your request.
24. **Enter Service Fee Certificate Issuer**: Provide the issuer of the service fee certificate.
25. **Enter Additional Comments 3**: Provide any additional information or comments regarding your request.
