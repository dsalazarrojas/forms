# Pool House Service Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pool House Service Contract form is designed to gather information about the services provided for a pool house. This includes details about the service description, service provider, service provider contact information, and service contract terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Service Description** in the text field.
2. Select the **Service Provider** from the list.
3. Enter the **Service Provider Contact Info** in the text field.
4. Enter a **Service Description** in the text field.
5. Read and agree to the **Service Contract Terms and Conditions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pool House Service Contract** (`pool_house_service_contract_form_1`, text, optional): Enter the title of the service contract, which should briefly describe the services provided.
- **Service Description** (`pool_house_service_contract_form_2`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the services provided, including any specific details or expectations.
- **Service Provider** (`pool_house_service_contract_form_3`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options to indicate if the service is provided by 'Yes' or 'No', or if other arrangements are made, select 'Other'.
- **Service Provider Contact Info** (`pool_house_service_contract_form_4`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the service provider, including their name, phone number, and email address.
- **Service Description** (`pool_house_service_contract_form_5`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the services provided, including any specific details or expectations.
- **Service Contract Terms and Conditions** (`service_contract_terms`, text, optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the service contract, which outlines the responsibilities and expectations of both the service provider and customer.
