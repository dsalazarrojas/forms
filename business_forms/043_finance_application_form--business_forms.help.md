# Finance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Finance Application Form is used to collect information for finance applications, such as loan requests, from individuals. This form gathers personal and employment-related information, financial data, and loan-specific details.
## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by filling in the personal details: enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Proceed to the Employment Status section, selecting either Active Employee, Self-Employed, or Unemployed.
3. Move on to the financial details: input your Income and Credit Score. If you're employed, you may be asked for your loan terms (e.g., 12, 24, 36, or 48 months).
4. For loan applications, select the type (Personal, Commercial, or Business Loan) and provide your Bank Name and Contact Person details.
5. Input your Loan Amount and Loan Term.
6. Enter the Loan Date and Loan Time.
7. Select the status of your loan (Approved, Denied, or Approved with Conditions).
8. If your loan is denied, provide a Reason Denied. If approved, offer a Reason Approved.
9. For loans with conditions, enter a Reason Condition.
10. Optionally, add Additional Comments.
11. Lastly, review and provide Review Comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2000-01-01).
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Provide your current address or any other relevant address information.
* **Employment Status** (Select One, Required): Select your employment status from the given options: Active Employee, Self-Employed, or Unemployed.
* **Income** (Number, Required): Enter your monthly income.
* **Credit Score** (Number, Optional): Enter your current credit score.
* **Loan Amount** (Number, Required): Enter the amount you're applying for.
* **Loan Term** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the term of your loan from the given options: 12 months, 24 months, 36 months, or 48 months.
* **Loan Type** (Select One, Required): Select the type of loan you're applying for: Personal, Commercial, or Business Loan.
* **Bank Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the bank you're applying to.
* **Contact Person** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person you're contacting.
* **Contact Number** (Text, Required): Enter the contact number of the person you're contacting.
* **Contact Email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the person you're contacting.
* **Loan Agreement** (Note, Required): This field is for a note about your loan agreement.
* **Loan Terms Accepted** (Select Multiple, Required): Confirm your understanding of the loan terms by selecting the options that apply to your situation.
* **Loan Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of your loan application.
* **Loan Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time of your loan application.
* **Loan Status** (Select One, Required): Select the status of your loan: Approved, Denied, or Approved with Conditions.
* **Reason Denied** (Text, Optional): If your loan is denied, provide a reason for the denial.
* **Reason Approved** (Text, Optional): If your loan is approved, provide a reason for the approval.
* **Reason Condition** (Text, Optional): If your loan is approved with conditions, provide a reason for the condition.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your loan application.
* **Review Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide review comments about your loan application.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Use the correct format for dates (YYYY-MM-DD) and times (HH:MM) as specified.
- Select the correct employment status and loan type.
- Understand the implications of your loan terms and status.
- Review your application carefully before submitting it.
