# Meat And Seafood Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meat And Seafood Pre Order Form is used to collect information from customers for their meat and seafood pre orders. This form helps to gather the necessary details such as customer information, delivery date, order details, contact phone number, email, and order total.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your customer information in the "customer" field. This is not required, but it's helpful for our team to know who is placing the order.
2. Enter the delivery date when you would like to receive your order. Please use the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Provide a brief description of the order in the "order_details" field. This will help us to prepare your order correctly.
4. Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
6. Finally, enter the total cost of your order in the "order_total" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer** (`text`, optional):
	+ This field is used to record the name of the customer.
* **delivery_date** (`date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **order_details** (`text`, optional):
	+ Provide a brief description of your order.
* **customer_phone** (`text`, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **customer_email** (`email`, optional):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **order_total** (`number`, optional):
	+ Enter the total cost of your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter all the required information accurately.
- Double-check the delivery date to ensure it aligns with your availability.
- Clearly describe your order in the "order_details" field to avoid any mistakes.
