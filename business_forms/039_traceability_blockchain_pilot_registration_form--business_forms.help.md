# Traceability Blockchain Pilot Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Traceability Blockchain Pilot Registration Form is used to register and track the status of traceability blockchain pilot projects. It is intended to gather information about the project's name, location, contact person, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name in the "company_name" field.
2. Enter the contact person's name and other relevant details in the "contact_person" field.
3. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "email" field.
4. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "phone" field.
5. Enter the company address in the "company_address" field.
6. Enter the city in the "city" field.
7. Enter the state in the "state" field.
8. Enter the zip code in the "zip" field.
9. Enter the country in the "country" field.
10. Select the industry type in the "industry" field.
11. Select the category in the "category" field.
12. Enter the project description and submitted date and time.
13. Select who submitted the project.
14. Select the status of the submission.
15. Enter any comments or remarks.
16. Select if attachments were uploaded.
17. Select the status of the project.
18. Enter any additional remarks.
19. Select if the project has attachments.
20. Enter the signature of the contact person.
21. Enter the time when the attachments were uploaded.
