# Dealer Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dealer Signup Form is intended for dealers to provide their information to be associated with a business. The form is used to collect basic contact and dealer information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Read through each page carefully and fill in the required fields.
- Make sure to fill in all fields marked as "required".
- For fields with a "select_one" type, choose the correct option that best describes your information.
- Double-check all fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Dealer info** (`dealer_info`, text, optional): This is the main text area for dealer information. You can provide any relevant details about the dealer here.
  - **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the dealer.
  - **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the dealer.
  - **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the dealer.
  - **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the dealer.
  - **Dealer type** (`dealer_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of dealer (New Dealer, Existing Dealer, or Other).
  - **Dealer name** (`dealer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the dealer.
  - **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name.
  - **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
  - **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
  - **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's address.
  - **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or information about the dealer.
  - **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to the dealer (Chatjimmy or None).
  - **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit the form.
