# Maintenance Building Meeting Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Maintenance Building Meeting Feedback form is designed to gather feedback from users after a meeting has taken place. It is intended to capture essential details such as the date and time of the meeting, the attendees, the location, and any additional comments or feedback that may be relevant to the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the meeting from the calendar.
2. Enter the time of the meeting.
3. List the attendees who attended the meeting.
4. Enter the location where the meeting took place.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Meeting Date
- **Meeting Date** (`Date`, required: false): Enter the date of the meeting. This field is not required but it's recommended to provide a record of when the meeting occurred.

### Meeting Time
- **Meeting Time** (`Time`, required: false): Enter the time of the meeting. This field is not required but it's recommended to provide a clear understanding of the meeting's schedule.

### Meeting Attendees
- **Meeting Attendees** (`Text`, required: false): List the names of all attendees who participated in the meeting. This field is not required but it's recommended to keep track of who was present.

### Meeting Location
- **Meeting Location** (`Text`, required: false): Enter the location where the meeting took place. This field is not required but it's recommended to provide a reference point for future meetings.

### Feedback
- **Feedback** (`Note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the meeting. This field is not required but it's recommended to capture any observations or insights that may be beneficial for future improvements.

### Additional Comments
- **Additional Comments** (`Text`, required: false): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the meeting. This field is not required but it's recommended to provide any extra details that may be helpful for future reference.
