# Performance Measurement Advisory Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Measurement Advisory Program Application Form is designed to collect information from organizations interested in participating in the Performance Measurement Advisory Program. The form helps to gather essential details about the organization, its performance, and its certification status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name and title of the Performance Measurement Advisory Program Application in the **Performance Measurement Advisory Program Application** field.
2. Provide your organization's name in the **Organization** field.
3. Enter your organization's address in the **Address** field.
4. Enter your organization's email address in the **EMail** field.
5. Enter your organization's phone number in the **Phone** field.
6. Enter your organization's fax number in the **Fax** field.
7. Enter your organization's URL in the **URL** field.
8. Enter your contact person's name in the **Contact Person** field.
9. Select your organization type from the drop-down list in the **Organization Type** field.
10. Enter a brief description of your organization in the **Organization Description** field.
11. Enter the year your organization was established in the **Year In Business** field.
12. Enter the number of employees in your organization in the **Number of Employees** field.
13. Enter your organization's annual revenue in the **Annual Revenue** field.
14. Select whether your organization participates in Performance Measurement Programs from the drop-down list in the **Performance Measurement Programs** field.
15. Select whether your organization has certification in the **Has Certification** field.
16. Select whether your organization has ISO 9001 certification from the drop-down list in the **Has ISO 9001 Certification** field.
17. Select whether your organization has ISO 14001 certification from the drop-down list in the **Has ISO 14001 Certification** field.
18. Select whether your organization has ISO 27001 certification from the drop-down list in the **Has ISO 27001 Certification** field.
19. Select the frequency of ISO 27001 audit from the drop-down list in the **ISO 27001 Audit Frequency** field.
20. Select whether your organization has certification in the **Has Certification** field.
21. Enter the date of submission in the **Date** field.
22. Enter the time of submission in the **Time** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performance Measurement Advisory Program Application**: Enter the title of your submission.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization's name.
* **Address**: Enter your organization's address.
* **EMail**: Enter your organization's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your organization's phone number.
* **Fax**: Enter your organization's fax number.
* **URL**: Enter your organization's URL.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **Organization Type**: Select the type of your organization (Private, Public, Non-Government, or Other).
* **Organization Description**: Provide a brief description of your organization.
* **Year In Business**: Enter the year your organization was established.
* **Number of Employees**: Enter the number of employees in your organization.
* **Annual Revenue**: Enter your organization's annual revenue.
* **Performance Measurement Programs**: Select whether your organization participates in Performance Measurement Programs (Yes or No).
* **Has Certification**: Select whether your organization has certification (Yes or No).
* **Has ISO 9001**: Select whether your organization has ISO 9001 certification (Yes or No).
* **Has ISO 14001**: Select whether your organization has ISO 14001 certification (Yes or No).
* **Has ISO 27001**: Select whether your organization has ISO 27001 certification (Yes or No).
* **ISO 27001 Audit Frequency**: Select the frequency of ISO 27001 audit (Annual, Bi-Annual, Quarterly, Monthly, or Other).
* **Has Certification**: Select whether your organization has certification (Yes or No).
* **Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Time**: Enter the time of submission.
