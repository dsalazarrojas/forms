# Business Registration Certificate Amendment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Registration Certificate Amendment Form is used to make changes to an existing business registration certificate. This includes updating the business name, address, owner or manager information, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as `required` in the form.
2. For fields marked as `note`, review the section headers to understand which fields are applicable to your amendment.
3. If you are making changes to the business name, ensure you provide the new name and the reason for the change.
4. If you are making changes to the address, provide the current and new addresses.
5. If you are making changes to the owner or manager information, provide the current and new owner or manager details.
6. If you are making changes to contact information, provide the current and new contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Current Registered Business Name**: Your business name as shown on the current certificate.
* **Registration Number**: The registration number from your current certificate.
* **Original Registration Date**: The date when the business was first registered.
* **Type of Amendment**: Select the type of amendment you are making. This includes:
	+ Business Name Change
	+ Address Change
	+ Owner or Manager Change
	+ Business Type Change
	+ Contact Information Update
	+ Partnership Change
	+ Legal Structure Change
* **New Business Name**: If changing the business name, enter the new name.
* **Reason for Name Change**: If changing the business name, explain the reason for the change.
* **Current Registered Address**: Your present business address.
* **New Business Address**: If moving, enter the new address.
* **Date Change is Effective**: If applicable, enter the date when the amendment takes effect.
* **Current Registered Owner**: If making changes to the owner or manager information, enter the current owner's name.
* **New Owner Name**: If changing the owner or manager, enter the new owner's name.
* **New Owner ID Number**: If changing the owner or manager, enter the new ID number (if required).
* **Current Contact Phone**: Your current contact phone number.
* **New Contact Phone**: If changing the contact phone number, enter the new number.
* **Current Contact Email**: Your current contact email address.
* **New Contact Email**: If changing the contact email, enter the new address.
* **Submitted By - Name**: Enter your name.
* **Submitted By - Title**: Enter your role or position.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the current date.
* **Supporting Documents Attached**: List any attached documentation supporting your amendment application.
