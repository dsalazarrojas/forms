# New Flavor Concept Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to submit new flavor concept review requests. It gathers contact information and details about the flavor concept, including priority level and submit request.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately with your name and contact information.
2.  Describe your new flavor concept in the description field.
3.  Choose your priority level (Low, Medium, High) to indicate the urgency of the review request.
4.  Once you have filled out all required and optional fields, click on the "Submit" button to send in your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name to help us identify you in case we need to contact you.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name, this can be your full name or a nickname, as you prefer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your business email to allow us to contact you with more information about your review request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your business phone number to enable us to contact you quickly.
* **Flavor Concept** (`flavor_concept`, text, optional): Describe your new flavor concept in detail, including your ideas and any supporting materials you have.
*   **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Add a brief description of why this flavor concept deserves review and what sets it apart from other similar flavors.
*   **Priority Level** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Select your priority level for the review request, this will influence how quickly we review and respond to your concept.
    *   Low: We'll review your concept, but there may be a longer wait time.
    *   Medium: We'll review your concept within a few days of receiving it.
    *   High: We'll review and respond to your concept as quickly as possible.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Enter the words "Review Request" to submit your new flavor concept for review.

## Tips
*   Please be clear and concise in your description to help us better understand and process your submission.
*   Please ensure you provide a valid email address so we can contact you with more information about your review request.
*   If you have any supporting materials like images, videos, or documents, please attach them with your submission.
