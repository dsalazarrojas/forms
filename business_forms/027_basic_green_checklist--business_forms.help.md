# Basic Green Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Basic Green Checklist is a tool to evaluate a property's sustainability and environmental quality in six areas: Property Information, Energy Efficiency, Water Conservation, Waste Reduction, Indoor Environmental Quality, and Sustainable Transportation. The form provides an overview of the property's compliance with green standards, helping property owners or managers identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions about sustainable practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all required fields to ensure accurate scoring.
2. Review each section carefully, and choose the correct answer for each field.
3. Select "True" for fields that the property has the specified feature or practice.
4. Select "False" for fields where the property does not have the feature or practice.
5. For fields with "Needs Upgrade" or "Some" options, choose "Needs Upgrade" or "Some" as necessary.
6. Enter any recommendations for improvement in the "Recommendations for improvement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Property Information --**: This section is a header and should be left blank.
* **Property or business name** (2): Enter the name of the property or business being evaluated.
* **Property address** (3): Enter the physical address of the property or business.
* **Inspection date** (4): Enter the date of the property's inspection.
* **Inspector name** (5): Enter the name of the inspector or person conducting the evaluation.
* **HVAC system is energy efficient** (7): Choose "True" if the property's HVAC system is energy-efficient.
* **Insulation is adequate** (8): Choose "True" if the property's insulation is adequate.
* **Windows and doors are energy efficient** (9): Choose "True" if the property's windows and doors are energy-efficient.
* **Lighting uses LED or CFL bulbs** (10): Choose "True" if the property's lighting uses LED or CFL bulbs.
* **-- Water Conservation --**: This section is a header and should be left blank.
* **Low-flow water fixtures installed** (12): Choose "True" if the property has low-flow water fixtures.
* **Water usage monitoring system present** (13): Choose "True" if a water usage monitoring system is present.
* **-- Waste Reduction --**: This section is a header and should be left blank.
* **Recycling program implemented** (15): Choose "True" if the property has a recycling program.
* **Waste reduction measures in place** (16): Choose "True" if the property has waste reduction measures in place.
* **Composting or organic waste management** (17): Choose "True" if the property has compositing or organic waste management.
* **-- Indoor Environmental Quality --**: This section is a header and should be left blank.
* **Indoor air quality systems present** (19): Choose "True" if the property's indoor air quality systems are present.
* **Non-toxic materials used** (20): Choose "True" if the property uses non-toxic materials.
* **Adequate natural lighting available** (21): Choose "True" if the property has adequate natural lighting.
* **-- Sustainable Transportation --**: This section is a header and should be left blank.
* **Public transit access available** (23): Choose "True" if public transit access is available.
* **Bike storage or facilities provided** (24): Choose "True" if bike storage or facilities are provided.
* **-- Summary --**: This section is a header and should be left blank.
* **Overall green standards compliance** (26): Choose one of the provided options based on your assessment of the property's compliance with green standards.
* **Recommendations for improvement** (27): Enter any recommendations for improvement in this field.
