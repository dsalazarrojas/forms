# Equipment Inventory Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about the equipment inventory, including its condition, location, and maintenance schedules. This data will help the organization make informed decisions and ensure the optimal use of its assets.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant fields and fill them out according to the required information. Note that some fields are optional and can be completed at your discretion.
2. Double-check your entries for accuracy and consistency before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, optional): Enter the unique serial number assigned to the equipment by the manufacturer or vendor.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, text, optional): Indicate the type of equipment being recorded, e.g., computer, printer, or vehicle.
* **Model Number** (`model_number`, text, optional): Enter the model number of the equipment, as provided by the manufacturer.
* **Asset Location** (`asset_location`, text, optional): Specify the current location of the equipment, including the room or department it is housed in.
* **Asset Location 2** (`asset_location_2`, text, optional): (Optional) Provide an additional location where the equipment can be found or accessed.
* **Asset Status** (`asset_status`, select_one, optional): Choose from the following options:
	+ active: The equipment is currently in use and functional.
	+ retired: The equipment is no longer in use but still maintained.
	+ lost: The equipment is missing or misplaced.
	+ stolen: The equipment has been stolen.
	+ awaiting replacement: The equipment is awaiting replacement or repair.
* **Acquisition Date** (`acquisition_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the equipment was acquired or purchased.
* **Maintenance Schedule** (`maintenance_schd`, text, optional): Record any scheduled or planned maintenance for the equipment.
* **Asset Tag 1** (`asset_tag`, text, optional): Provide a unique identifier or tag for the equipment, as assigned by the organization.
* **Asset Tag 2** (`asset_tag_2`, text, optional): (Optional) Enter another asset tag or identifier for the equipment.
* **Asset Tag 3** (`asset_tag_3`, text, optional): (Optional) Enter any additional asset tags or identifiers for the equipment.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): Specify the name of the equipment vendor or manufacturer.
* **Vendor Part Number** (`vendor_part_number`, text, optional): Enter the part number of the equipment as provided by the vendor.
* **Vendor Serial Number** (`vendor_serial_number`, text, optional): Enter the serial number of the equipment as provided by the vendor.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the equipment, such as its condition or known issues.
