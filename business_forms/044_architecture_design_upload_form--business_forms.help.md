# Architecture Design Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Architecture Design Upload Form is a tool used to submit projects for review and feedback. Please use this form to provide detailed information about your project, including submitter information, project details, and design files.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the submitter information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide the project name, project type, and project location.
3. Enter a brief description of the project and the completion date (if applicable).
4. Upload design drawings, 3D renderings, and other relevant files for review.
5. Add any additional media or details about the project, such as notable features or awards.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the submitter of the project.
* **Email Address** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and feedback.
* **Phone Number** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Firm or Studio Name** (`firm_or_studio`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your firm or studio.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the project.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of project (e.g., Residential, Commercial, Institutional, etc.).
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the city, region, or address of the project location.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Project Completion Date** (`completion_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the completion date of the project (if applicable).
* **Design Drawings** (`design_drawings`, `text`, required): Upload CAD, PDF, or image files for review.
* **3D Renderings or Visualizations** (`renderings`, `text`, optional): Upload high-quality images or videos for review.
* **Floor Plans or Layouts** (`floor_plans`, `text`, optional): Upload PDF or image files for review.
* **Elevations and Sections** (`elevation_sections`, `text`, optional): Upload detailed building views for review.
* **Project Photos** (`project_photos`, `text`, optional): Upload construction or completed project photos for review.
* **Site Plans or Context** (`site_plans`, `text`, optional): Upload geographic location mapping for review.
* **Technical Documents** (`technical_documents`, `text`, optional): Upload specifications, reports, etc. for review.
* **Design Approach or Concept** (`design_approach`, `text`, optional): Enter an explanation of your design philosophy.
* **Notable Features or Innovations** (`notable_features`, `text`, optional): Enter what makes this project special.
* **Awards or Recognition** (`awards_recognition`, `text`, optional): Enter any notable achievements.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Provide high-quality uploads for design drawings, 3D renderings, and other media.
* Proofread and review your submission before submitting for review.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please contact us for assistance.
