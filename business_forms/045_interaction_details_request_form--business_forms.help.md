# interaction_details_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect interaction details from customers. It is intended to be used by customer support teams to gather information about customer interactions, such as whether they had an interaction with the company, when it occurred, and what it was about. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's information on page 1.
2. Choose whether the customer had an interaction with the company on page 2.
3. Select a date and time for the interaction on page 3.
4. Enter the details of the interaction on page 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info (customer_info, text, optional)**: Enter the customer's name or any other relevant details about them.
* **Interaction Type (interaction_type, select_one, optional)**: Choose 'Yes' if the customer had an interaction with the company, or 'No' if they did not.
* **Interaction Date (interaction_date, date, optional)**: Enter the date on which the customer interaction occurred.
* **Interaction Time (interaction_time, time, optional)**: Enter the time when the customer interaction took place.
* **Interaction Details (interaction_details, text, optional)**: Provide any additional details about the customer's interaction, such as what was discussed or what was asked. 

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much information as possible about the customer's interaction.
* If the customer did not have an interaction with the company, select 'No' for the interaction type.
* If the customer had an interaction, select a specific date and time for when it occurred.
* Enter a detailed description of the customer's interaction in the interaction details field.
