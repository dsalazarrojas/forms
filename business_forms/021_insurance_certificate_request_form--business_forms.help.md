# Insurance Certificate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request insurance certificates for businesses or companies. It collects necessary information to process the request, including business details and dates related to the certificate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of business or company you represent.
2. Choose the type of insurance you are requesting for your business.
3. Fill in the relevant dates for the certificate.
4. Upload any required supporting documents or proof of insurance.
5. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **is your business or company** (`user_business`, `text`, required): This field is for entering your business or company name.
* **what type of vendor or entity** (`vendor_type`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is for selecting whether you are a business or another type of vendor or entity. Select 'Yes' if your business is a vendor or entity, otherwise select 'No'.
* **upload your certificate** (`upload_certificate`, `text`, required: false): This field is not currently being used. You can skip it.
* **supporting documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, required: false): This field is not currently being used. You can skip it.
* **upload additional documents** (`upload_additional_documents`, `text`, required: false): This field is not currently being used. You can skip it.
* **business name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of your business.
* **what kind of insurance** (`insurance_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is for selecting the type of insurance you are requesting. You can select multiple options if necessary.
* **certificate request** (`certificate_request`, `date`, required): This field is for entering the date you are requesting the certificate.
* **certificate issue date** (`certificate_issue_date`, `date`, required): This field is for entering the date the certificate was issued.
* **certificate effective date** (`certificate_effective_date`, `date`, required): This field is for entering the date the certificate is effective from.
* **certificate expire date** (`certificate_expire_date`, `date`, required): This field is for entering the date the certificate expires.
* **upload proof of insurance** (`upload_proof_of_insurance`, `text`, required: false): This field is not currently being used. You can skip it.
* **upload proof of insurance 2** (`upload_proof_of_insurance2`, `text`, required: false): This field is not currently being used. You can skip it.
* **upload proof of insurance 3** (`upload_proof_of_insurance3`, `text`, required: false): This field is not currently being used. You can skip it.
* **upload proof of insurance 4** (`upload_proof_of_insurance4`, `text`, required: false): This field is not currently being used. You can skip it.
