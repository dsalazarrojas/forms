# Service Agreement For Support Services - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Service Agreement For Support Services form is designed to gather essential information from clients regarding their support services. This form ensures that all necessary details are collected accurately and efficiently, helping in delivering effective support services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the service terms that apply to the client**: Choose the service terms from the dropdown menu that best describe the services offered to the client.
2. **Enter the client's information**: Select the client's name from the list of available options.
3. **Describe the service**: Provide a brief description of the services offered.
4. **Specify the payment terms**: Enter the payment terms in a numerical format (e.g., 10% of the total amount).
5. **Choose the payment method**: Select the payment method from the available options (e.g., Bank transfer, Credit card, etc.).
6. **Enter the billing frequency**: If applicable, specify how frequently the services will be billed (e.g., weekly, bi-monthly, etc.).
7. **Schedule the payment due date**: If applicable, enter the date when the payment is due.
8. **Specify the payment amount**: Enter the total amount to be paid.
9. **Select the payment status**: Choose the status of the payment from the available options (e.g., Paid, Due, Pending, etc.).
10. **Enter the service provider's information**: Provide a brief description of the service provider.
11. **Enter the client contact information**: Provide the client's contact information for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Agreement For Support Services**: This field is not visible for input but is included to confirm that you are on the correct form.
* **Client Info** (`Client A`, `Client B`, `Client C`, required: false): Select the client's name from the available options. This field is not required, but it's essential to choose the correct client for accurate service delivery.
* **Service Description**: This field is where you describe the services offered to the client in a brief and concise manner.
* **Service Terms** (`Term 1`, `Term 2`, `Term 3`, required: false): Select the service terms from the available options. This field is not required but is crucial for defining the scope of services.
* **Payment Terms** (`10`, required: true): Enter the payment terms in a numerical format (e.g., 10% of the total amount). This field is required to ensure accurate payment processing.
* **Payment Method** (`Method 1`, `Method 2`, `Method 3`, required: false): Select the payment method from the available options (e.g., Bank transfer, Credit card, etc.).
* **Billing Frequency**: If applicable, specify how frequently the services will be billed (e.g., weekly, bi-monthly, etc.).
* **Payment Due Date**: If applicable, enter the date when the payment is due.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the total amount to be paid.
* **Payment Status** (`Status A`, `Status B`, required: false): Select the payment status from the available options (e.g., Paid, Due, Pending, etc.).
* **Service Provider**: Provide a brief description of the service provider.
* **Client Contact**: Provide the client's contact information for further communication.
