# Construction Bid Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist contractors in submitting their construction bids to clients. It provides a structured and organized format for presenting bid details, ensuring that all necessary information is included and easily understood by the client.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information:
	* Date of bid submission (Bid Date)
	* Client details (Client Name, Client Address)
	* Project details (Project Name, Project Location, Project Description)
	* Contractor details (Contractor Name, Contractor Address, License Number)
	* Project scope and costs (Scope of Work, Materials Included, Labor Costs, Material Costs, Equipment Costs, Overhead Costs, Contingency Amount)
	* Payment terms and conditions (Payment Terms)
	* Project timeline and start date (Project Timeline, Available Start Date)
	* Warranty and bid validity (Warranty Included, Bid Validity Period)
	* Additional information (Exclusions, Special Notes or Conditions, Authorized By)
2. Review and ensure all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
3. Select the correct payment terms and warranty options from the dropdown menus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bid Date** (`bid_date`): Date of bid submission.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`): Name of project owner or client.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`): Project or client address.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`): Official project name.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`): Full address of project site.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`): Brief description of scope.
* **Contractor Name** (`contractor_name`): Your company name.
* **Contractor Address** (`contractor_address`): Your business address.
* **License Number** (`contractor_license`): Contractor license number.
* **Scope of Work** (`scope_of_work`): Detailed description of work included.
* **Materials Included** (`materials_included`): List materials provided in bid.
* **Labor Costs** (`labor_costs`): Total labor cost amount.
* **Material Costs** (`material_costs`): Total material cost amount.
* **Equipment Costs** (`equipment_costs`): Equipment rental or usage costs. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Overhead Costs** (`overhead_costs`): Administrative and overhead expenses. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Contingency Amount** (`contingency_amount`): Contingency fund if applicable. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Total Bid Amount** (`total_bid_amount`): Sum of all costs.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Proposed payment schedule. (Select from dropdown menu.)
	+ 50% upfront, 50% on completion
	+ 30% upfront, 40% midpoint, 30% completion
	+ Monthly progress payments
	+ Net 30 days
	+ Custom terms
* **Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`): Estimated start to finish duration.
* **Available Start Date** (`start_date_available`): Earliest possible start date.
* **Warranty Included** (`warranty_included`): Is warranty provided. (Select from dropdown menu.)
	+ Yes, 1 Year
	+ Yes, 2 Years
	+ Yes, 5 Years
	+ False
* **Bid Validity Period** (`bid_validity`): How long this bid remains valid. (Select from dropdown menu.)
	+ 30 days
	+ 60 days
	+ 90 days
	+ 120 days
* **Exclusions** (`exclusions`): What is NOT included in this bid.
* **Special Notes or Conditions** (`special_notes`): Any additional terms or notes.
* **Authorized By** (`authorized_by`): Name of authorized signatory.
* **Equipment Costs** (`equipment_costs`): This field is optional.
* **Overhead Costs** (`overhead_costs`): This field is optional.
* **Contingency Amount** (`contingency_amount`): This field is optional.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely before submission.
* Use the dropdown menus to select the correct payment terms and warranty options.
* Review and revise the form as needed before submitting the bid.
* Keep a record of the bid submission for future reference.
