# vendor_invoice_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vendor invoice submission form is a tool used for submitting invoices from external vendors to the company. It captures essential details about the vendor, their company, and the invoice details, facilitating efficient and accurate tracking and processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Vendors Name" field with the name of the vendor.
2. In the "Vendor Company Name" field, enter the name of the vendor's company.
3. Provide the address of the vendor in the "Address" field.
4. Enter the email of the vendor in the "Email" field.
5. If the vendor has a phone number, enter it in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Select the invoice date from the calendar in the "Invoice Date" field.
7. Enter the total amount of the invoice in the "Invoice Total" field.
8. Choose the assigned user from the dropdown list in the "Assigned User" field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about the invoice in the "Notes" field.
9. Click on the "Submitted Date" field to select the date the invoice was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendors Name**: Enter the name of the vendor.
* **Vendor Company Name**: Enter the name of the vendor's company.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the vendor.
* **Email**: Enter the email of the vendor.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the vendor, if applicable.
* **Invoice Date**: Select the date the invoice was issued.
* **Invoice Total**: Enter the total amount of the invoice.
* **Assigned User**: Choose the user assigned to manage this invoice.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the invoice.
* **Submitted Date**: Select the date the invoice was submitted.

## Tips
* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant department or contact the support team.
* Always check for existing submissions from the same vendor before resubmitting.
