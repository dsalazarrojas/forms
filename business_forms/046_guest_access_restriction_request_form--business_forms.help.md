# Guest Access Restriction Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request restrictions on guest access for a particular area or resource. Fill out this form to inform our team about your need for restriction and we will take it into consideration for approval.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
* Ensure you have a clear reason for the restriction request.
* Provide your information (Name, Email, etc.) in the "Guest Info" field.
* Specify the date and time you are requesting to restrict access.
* Mention the duration for which you would like to have access restricted.
* Explain the reason for the restriction (if not clear from the above points).
* Indicate if you are requesting restrictions on specific areas or resources.
* Specify if the restriction is during specific days or hours.
* Choose the status of the request (Active or Inactive).
* If approved, indicate who approved the request.
* If restricted, indicate who restricted the request.
* Optionally, choose a date until which the restriction is to be kept in place.
* Leave any additional comments about your request.
* Finally, confirm that you are submitting the request (or not).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Form**: A space to describe your request for guest access restriction.
* **Guest Info**: A field to provide your basic information (name, email, etc.) so that we can reach out to you about your request.
* **Request Date**: A date field to specify the date when you are requesting to restrict access.
* **Request Time**: A time field to specify the time for which you would like to restrict access.
* **Duration**: A field to specify how long you want the access restriction to be in place for.
* **Restriction Reason**: A field to give an explanation for the restriction request.
* **Restricted Area**: A field to indicate if the access restriction is for a specific area or resource.
* **Restricted Hours**: A field to specify if the restriction is during specific hours.
* **Restricted Days**: A field to specify if the restriction is during specific days.
* **Approval Status**: A field to specify the status of the request (Active or Inactive).
* **Approved By**: A field to indicate who has approved the request.
* **Restricted By**: A field to indicate who restricted the request.
* **Restricted Until**: A field to specify until when the access restriction should be kept in place.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments you have about your restriction request.
* **Submitter**: A field to confirm if you are submitting the request on your own behalf.
* **Form ID**: Not applicable in this form.
