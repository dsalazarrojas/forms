# Risk Culture Consulting Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Risk Culture Consulting Program Application Form is designed to gather information about an organization's risk culture and goals. It is used to assess the current state of risk culture within an organization and gather details about the applicant's goals and aspirations for the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide Company Information**: Fill out the Company Name field with your organization's name.
2. **Identify Your Contact Person**: Enter the name and title of the person responsible for the application in the Contact Person field.
3. **Describe Current Risk Culture**: In the Current Risk Culture field, describe the current state of risk culture within your organization. This should include any specific risks or concerns that are currently being addressed.
4. **Set Goals for the Program**: In the Goals field, describe your organization's goals and aspirations for the program.
5. **Identify Your Program Applicant**: Select the type of program applicant you are (e.g., CEO/President, COO, Director/Manager, etc.).
6. **Provide Contact Information**: Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable) to ensure that you can be contacted by the Risk Culture Consulting Program team.
7. **Add Additional Information**: If you have any additional information that you would like to share with the program team, please use the Additional Info field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`Company Name`, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, text, required): Enter the name and title of the person responsible for the application.
* **Current Risk Culture** (`Current Risk Culture`, text, required): Describe the current state of risk culture within your organization.
* **Goals** (`Goals`, text, required): Describe your organization's goals and aspirations for the program.
* **Program Applicant** (`Program Applicant`, select_one, required): Select the type of program applicant you are (e.g., CEO/President, COO, Director/Manager, etc.).
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, note, optional): Provide any additional information that you would like to share with the program team.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing the current state of risk culture within your organization.
* Clearly state your organization's goals and aspirations for the program.
* Ensure that you select the correct type of program applicant.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, do not hesitate to reach out to the Risk Culture Consulting Program team.
