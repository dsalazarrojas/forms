# Business Partner Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Partner Application Form is a comprehensive document designed to gather information from potential business partners. This form is used to assess your business's eligibility, goals, and capabilities to ensure a successful partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and contact information in fields 1-3.
2. Enter your business's name, address, and website (if applicable) in fields 4-6.
3. Select your primary industry (field 8).
4. Provide information about your business's size and revenue (fields 9-11).
5. Choose the type of partnership you're interested in (field 12).
6. Outline your partnership goals (field 13).
7. Describe your value proposition (field 14).
8. Indicate the markets you serve (field 15).
9. Share relevant experience and previous partnership experience (fields 16-17).
10. List any relevant resources you can contribute (field 18).
11. Specify the timeline for the partnership (field 19).
12. Confirm your decision-maker status (field 20).
13. Summarize your proposal (field 21).
14. Provide professional references (optional, field 22).
15. Add any additional information (optional, field 23).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Provide your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your business's legal name.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter your business's full address.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, required): Provide your business's website URL.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, required): Enter your business's contact phone number.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your primary industry from the options provided.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, required): Enter the number of years your business has been operating.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, required): Enter the total number of employees in your company.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, required): Select your business's approximate annual revenue range.
* **Partnership Type Sought** (`partnership_type_sought`, select_one, required): Choose the type of partnership you're interested in.
* **Partnership Goals** (`partnership_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select all goals you hope to achieve through the partnership.
* **Your Value Proposition** (`value_proposition`, text, required): Describe the value you bring to the partnership.
* **Target Markets** (`target_markets`, select_multiple, required): Select all markets your business serves.
* **Relevant Experience** (`relevant_experience`, text, required): Describe your relevant experience.
* **Previous Partnership Experience** (`previous_partnerships`, select_one, required): Indicate your experience with previous partnerships.
* **Resources Available** (`resources_available`, select_multiple, required): Select all resources your business can contribute to the partnership.
* **Partnership Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Specify when you want to start the partnership.
* **Decision Maker Status** (`decision_maker`, select_one, required): Confirm your decision-maker status.
* **Partnership Proposal Summary** (`proposal_summary`, text, required): Briefly describe your proposal.
* **Professional References** (`references`, text, optional): List 2-3 professional references.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant details.

Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate and detailed answers, and don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need further clarification.
