# prophets_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prophets Membership Form is used to collect contact and sales information from distributors, which are individuals or businesses that sell or distribute products on behalf of the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the distributor's name in the "distributor_name" field.
2. Fill in the distributor's address in the "address" field.
3. Enter the city and state in the "city" and "state" fields, respectively.
4. Enter the distributor's email in the "email" field.
5. Enter the distributor's phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Enter the distributor's fax number in the "fax" field.
7. Enter the zip code in the "zip_code" field.
8. Select the type of distributor from the "distributor_type" field (Distributors, Dealers, Retailers, Wholesalers, or Other) and enter any additional information in the "distributor_type_other" field if Other is selected.
9. Enter the sales representative's name in the "sales_rep_name" field.
10. Enter the sales representative's email in the "sales_rep_email" field.
11. Enter the sales representative's phone number in the "sales_rep_phone" field.
12. Enter the sales representative's fax number in the "sales_rep_fax" field.
13. Enter the sales representative's extension in the "sales_rep_extention" field.
14. Enter the contact person's name in the "contact_person" field.
15. Enter the contact person's email in the "contact_email" field.
16. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
17. Enter the contact person's fax number in the "contact_fax" field.
18. Enter the contract number in the "contract_number" field.
19. Enter the contract date in the "contract_date" field.
20. Enter any additional notes in the "notes" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **distributor_name**: Enter the name of the distributor (required).
* **address**: Enter the address of the distributor (required).
* **city**: Enter the city of the distributor (required).
* **state**: Enter the state of the distributor (required).
* **email**: Enter the email of the distributor (required).
* **phone**: Enter the phone number of the distributor.
* **fax**: Enter the fax number of the distributor.
* **zip_code**: Enter the zip code of the distributor.
* **distributor_type**: Select the type of distributor (Distributors, Dealers, Retailers, Wholesalers, or Other) and enter any additional information in the "distributor_type_other" field if Other is selected.
* **distributor_type_other**: Enter any additional information if "distributor_type" is set to Other (optional).
* **sales_rep_name**: Enter the name of the sales representative (optional).
* **sales_rep_email**: Enter the email of the sales representative (optional).
* **sales_rep_phone**: Enter the phone number of the sales representative (optional).
* **sales_rep_fax**: Enter the fax number of the sales representative (optional).
* **sales_rep_extention**: Enter the sales representative's extension (optional).
* **contact_person**: Enter the name of the contact person (optional).
* **contact_email**: Enter the email of the contact person (optional).
* **contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person (optional).
* **contact_fax**: Enter the fax number of the contact person (optional).
* **contract_number**: Enter the contract number (optional).
* **contract_date**: Enter the contract date (optional).
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the information entered for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the sales team for assistance.
