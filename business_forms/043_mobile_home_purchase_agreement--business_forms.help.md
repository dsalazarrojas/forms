# Mobile Home Purchase Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mobile Home Purchase Agreement is a document that outlines the terms of a mobile home purchase, including details such as seller and buyer information, property address, asking and purchase prices, payment terms, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the seller's name and contact information in the "Seller Name" and "Seller Contact Info" fields.
2. Enter the buyer's name and contact information in the "Buyer Name" and "Buyer Contact Info" fields.
3. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field.
4. Enter the asking price of the mobile home in the "Asking Price" field.
5. Enter the purchase price of the mobile home in the "Purchase Price" field.
6. Specify the payment terms and method in the "Payment Terms" and "Payment Method" fields.
7. If applicable, enter any financing terms and provider information in the "Financing Terms" and "Financing Provider" fields.
8. Specify any inspection terms in the "Inspection Terms" field.
9. Enter the seller's representatives' information in the "Seller Representatives" field.
10. Sign and date the agreement in the "Seller Signature" and "Date Signed" fields.
11. Sign and date the agreement by the buyer in the "Buyer Signature" field.
12. Review and agree to the terms and conditions in the "Terms and Conditions" field.
13. Enter any additional comments or notes from the buyer and seller in the "Buyer Note" and "Seller Note" fields.
14. Enter the buyer's email address and phone number in the "Buyer Email" and "Phone Number" fields.
15. Enter the seller's email address and phone number in the "Seller Email" and "Fax Number" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seller Name** (`seller_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the seller.
* **Seller Contact Info** (`seller_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the seller's contact information, such as address, phone number, and email.
* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the buyer.
* **Buyer Contact Info** (`buyer_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the buyer's contact information, such as address, phone number, and email.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the mobile home.
* **Asking Price** (`asking_price`, number, required): Enter the asking price of the mobile home.
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, required): Enter the purchase price of the mobile home.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Specify the payment terms, such as payment schedule and method.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text, optional): Specify the payment method, such as cash or financing.
* **Financing Terms** (`financing_terms`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any financing terms and conditions.
* **Financing Provider** (`financing_provider`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the financing provider's information.
* **Inspection Terms** (`inspection_terms`, text, optional): Specify any inspection terms and conditions.
* **Seller Representatives** (`seller_representatives`, text, optional): Enter any information for seller representatives.
* **Seller Signature** (`seller_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the agreement by the seller.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Date and sign the agreement by the seller.
* **Buyer Signature** (`buyer_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the agreement by the buyer.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Buyer Representatives** (`buyer_representatives`, text, optional): Enter any information for buyer representatives.
* **Buyer Note** (`buyer_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes from the buyer.
* **Seller Note** (`seller_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes from the seller.
* **Buyer Email** (`buyer_email`, email, optional): Enter the buyer's email address.
* **Seller Email** (`seller_email`, email, optional): Enter the seller's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the buyer.
* **Fax Number** (`fax_number`, text, optional): Enter the fax number of the seller.
