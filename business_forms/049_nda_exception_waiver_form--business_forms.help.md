# Nda Exception Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nda Exception Waiver Form is used to create a document that waives any outstanding exceptions to agreements. This form is typically used when an exception has been agreed upon by all parties involved, and it's necessary to formalize this agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the user input fields as required or optional by your company's policies. Typically, you'll need to enter the description and exception type in the first section.
2. If applicable, enter the start and end dates of the exception period.
3. Provide the reason for the exception in the reason field.
4. Add any relevant contact information and signature.
5. Upload any necessary files related to the exception.
6. Enter the agreement number and name if it's applicable.
7. Fill out the agreement term and signature term fields as needed.
8. Choose the exception reason and term if they apply.
9. Select the submit options (submit or submit2) and submit the form when ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **user_input** (User input, text, required)
Enter a brief description of the exception being waived.

### 2. **description** (Description, text, optional)
Provide a detailed description of the exception.

### 3. **exception_type** (Exception type, select_one, optional)
Select the type of exception being waived.

### 4. **start_date** (Start date, date, optional)
Enter the start date of the exception period.

### 5. **end_date** (End date, date, optional)
Enter the end date of the exception period.

### 6. **reason** (Reason, text, optional)
Enter the reason for the exception.

### 7. **contact_info** (Contact info, text, optional)
Provide any relevant contact information related to the exception.

### 8. **signature** (Signature, text, optional)
Sign the document to confirm your agreement to the exception.

### 9. **agreement_number** (Agreement number, text, optional)
Enter the number of the agreement being waived.

### 10. **fileupload** (File upload, text, optional)
Upload any files related to the exception.

### 11. **agreement_name** (Agreement name, text, optional)
Enter the name of the agreement being waived.

### 12. **agreement_date** (Agreement date, date, optional)
Enter the date of the agreement being waived.

### 13. **exception_date** (Exception date, date, optional)
Enter the date of the exception being waived.

### 14. **end_reason** (End reason, text, optional)
Enter the reason for ending the exception.

### 15. **signature_date** (Signature date, date, optional)
Enter the date of your signature.

### 16. **agreement_term** (Agreement term, text, optional)
Enter the term of the agreement being waived.

### 17. **term_date** (Term date, date, optional)
Enter the date of the agreement term.

### 18. **exception_reason** (Exception reason, text, optional)
Enter the reason for the exception.

### 19. **agreement_type** (Agreement type, select_one, optional)
Select the type of agreement being waived.

### 20. **agreement_status** (Agreement status, select_one, optional)
Select the status of the agreement being waived.

### 21. **agreement_term_date** (Agreement term date, date, optional)
Enter the date of the agreement term.

### 22. **term** (Term, text, optional)
Enter the term of the agreement.

### 23. **signature_term** (Signature term, text, optional)
Enter the term of the signature.

### 24. **exception_term** (Exception term, text, optional)
Enter the term of the exception.

### 25. **submit** (Submit, select_multiple, optional)
Select the submit option to finalize the waiver.

## Tips
- Please make sure to review and fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- This form is meant to waive exceptions, so make sure you have the necessary approvals and agreements before submitting it.
- Upload any necessary files related to the exception to support your submission.
- Review the form carefully before signing and submitting to ensure everything is accurate.
