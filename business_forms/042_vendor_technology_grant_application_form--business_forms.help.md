# Vendor Technology Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by vendors to apply for technology grants. It helps gather information about the vendor and their technology grant requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To fill out this form, click on the "Submit" button on the top right corner of the page.
2. The form is divided into two main sections: Vendor Information and Grant Details.
3. Please fill out all required fields and provide as much information as possible in the optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grant Application Form**: This field is for entering a brief description of the grant request.
* **Vendor Info**: This field is for entering the name, address, and other relevant details about the vendor.
* **Grant Request**: Please select "Yes" if you are requesting a grant, "No" otherwise.
* **Grant Uses**: Please select "Yes" if the grant will be used for the intended purpose, "No" otherwise.
* **Grant Amount**: Enter the amount of the grant being requested.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the grant.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the grant.
* **Tech Request Details**: Please describe the specific equipment or services you are requesting.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number.
* **Tech Request**: Please list the equipment and services you are requesting.
* **Grant Status**: Please select the current status of the grant.
* **Submitted By**: Please select "Yes" if the grant has been submitted, "No" otherwise.
* **Submitted At**: Enter the date and time the grant was submitted.
* **Updated By**: Please select "Yes" if the grant has been updated, "No" otherwise.
* **Updated At**: Enter the date and time the grant was updated.
* **Submitted To**: Please select "Yes" if the grant has been submitted to the approver, "No" otherwise.
* **Updated To**: Enter the date and time the grant was updated to the approver.
* **Grant Type**: Please select "Yes" if the grant is for a specific type, "No" otherwise.
