# Agriculture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Agriculture Survey form is designed to collect information from businesses in the agriculture sector. The form aims to gather insights on various aspects of these businesses, such as their main challenges, technology use, employment, and more. This data will help us better understand the sector and identify areas for improvement or development.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete the form, simply follow the prompts for each page.
2.  Fill out all required fields (*). Some fields are optional and can be left blank.
3.  Be accurate when providing information, but if you are unsure, provide the best available data.
4.  When finished, review your submission for any mistakes before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name** (1, text, Optional): This field is intended to capture the name of the person completing the survey. If you prefer to remain anonymous, you do not need to fill this out.
* **Business type** (2, select_one, Required): Choose the type of business you represent. This includes Farm, Cooperative, Input supplier, Service provider, or Research.
* **Main challenges faced** (3, text, Optional): Describe the main challenges your business is currently facing. This can include economic, climatic, logistical, or other challenges.
* **Support needs** (4, text, Optional): List any services or resources that you, as a business, would find beneficial or helpful.
* **Use of technology** (5, select_multiple, Optional): Indicate which technologies you are currently using in your operations. This can be Mobile apps, Mechanization, Soil sensors, Irrigation systems, or other technologies.
* **Number of employees** (6, number, Optional): Approximately how many full and part-time staff members your business has.
* **Estimated annual turnover** (7, number, Optional): Your approximate total income for the year, in local currency.
* **Willingness to participate** (8, select_one, Optional): Indicate your willingness to participate in trainings or pilots. This can be True, False, or Maybe.
* **Preferred contact method** (9, select_one, Optional): Select the best way for us to contact you, whether by Email, Phone, or In-person.
* **Additional comments** (10, text, Optional): Add any further information that you think would be helpful for us to know.
