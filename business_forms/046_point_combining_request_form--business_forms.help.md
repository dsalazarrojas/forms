# Point Combining Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Point Combining Request Form is designed to collect information from customers about their request for combining points from different sources or accounts. This form aims to gather essential details to process and fulfill their points combination requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the necessary details in the Request Form section.
2. If you wish to include your customer details, fill in the Customer Details section.
3. Add more context to your request in the Request Details section.
4. If necessary, provide account information in the Account Details section.
5. Review and confirm your request in the Review And Confirm section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form** (`Request Form`, `text`, optional):
    This section is for providing the core information about your request.
* **Customer Details** (`Customer Details`, `text`, optional):
    This section is for collecting your customer details, though not required. If you choose to fill this, provide your full name, email, and any other relevant customer information.
* **Request Details** (`Request Details`, `text`, optional):
    This section is meant for providing more details about your request, such as the reason for the request or any additional context.
* **Account Details** (`Account Details`, `text`, optional):
    If you have an account associated with your request, please provide the account information in this section. This could be your account ID, username, or any other relevant account details.
* **Review And Confirm** (`Review And Confirm`, `text`, optional):
    This section is for reviewing and confirming your request. Please ensure that all the details are accurate and complete before submitting.
