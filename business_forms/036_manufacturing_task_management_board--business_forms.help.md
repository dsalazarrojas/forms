# Manufacturing Task Management Board - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Task Management Board form is used to collect and track information related to production tasks, including assignment, priority, due dates, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting whether the task is to be assigned or not.
2. Choose the task priority level to indicate its urgency and importance.
3. Enter the due date for the task, which is crucial for meeting deadlines.
4. Select the production status to reflect its current state.
5. Provide a brief description of the task to summarize its nature.
6. Enter the production plan number if applicable.
7. Select the production team status to indicate whether it's active or inactive.
8. Enter the production supervisor's and responsible's name(s) if applicable.
9. Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the task.
10. Select the escalation status to indicate whether it needs escalation or not.
11. Enter the escalation email and phone number if necessary.
12. Enter any notes about escalations.
13. Add attachments related to the task, if necessary.
14. Select the task assignee to indicate who is responsible for the task.
15. Choose whether the task priority order is to be followed or not.
16. Enter the production team status and comments if applicable.
17. Enter any additional comments or notes about the task assigner.
18. Finally, enter any additional production notes.
