# Resume Client Files - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resume Client Files form is a crucial document that allows you to collect and store essential information about your clients' resumes. This form will help you create a comprehensive profile for each client, making it easier to manage and track their career history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Resume Client Files" form.
2.  Fill in the required fields with the relevant information about the client's resume.
3.  Review the form carefully to ensure that all the fields are filled in accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Resume Name** (`resume_client_files_name`, text, **required:** false): This field is used to input the name of the client. 
*  **Job Title** (`resume_client_files_job_title`, text, **required:** false): This field is for inputting the client's job title or position.
*  **Company** (`resume_client_files_company`, text, **required:** false): Enter the name of the client's company where they worked.
*  **Location** (`resume_client_files_location`, text, **required:** false): Input the location where the client worked or resides.
*  **Start Date** (`resume_client_files_start_date`, date, **required:** false): Enter the start date of the client's job or position.
