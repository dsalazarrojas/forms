# Subcontractor Warranty Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect warranty information from subcontractors, ensuring they are aware of their responsibilities and commitments. It helps to standardize the process, ensure compliance, and reduce misunderstandings. Please use it to gather accurate and relevant information from your subcontractors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your first name in the field labeled "First name."
2. Enter your last name in the field labeled "Last name."
3. Provide your business email address in the field labeled "Email."
4. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone."
5. Select the duration of the warranty term (e.g., "1 week", "2 weeks", "3 months", etc.) in the field labeled "Warranty term."
6. Indicate the location where the work will be performed in the field labeled "Work location."
7. Enter the date when the warranty begins in the format "MM/DD/YY" in the field labeled "Warranty date."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Provide your business email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Warranty term** (Number, Optional): Select the duration of the warranty term for which you commit to providing your services.
* **Work location** (Text, Optional): Specify the location where the work will be performed.
* **Warranty date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date when the warranty begins.
