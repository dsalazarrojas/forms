# Smart Warehouse Robotics Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Warehouse Robotics Application Form is designed to collect information from users to assist in the development of a robotics solution tailored to their warehouse operations. This form will help us understand your needs and provide a suitable proposal for your robotics solution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), as they are necessary to provide a complete and accurate proposal.
2. Ensure that the email and phone fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. If you have any additional comments or notes, please enter them in the note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or business card.
- **Email (2)**: Enter your valid email address. This will help us contact you with any questions or concerns.
* **Job Title (3)**: Provide your current job title, if applicable.
- **Robotics Solution Proposal (4)**: Describe your current issues with warehouse operations and what you hope to achieve with a robotics solution.
- **Robot Type (5)**: Select the type of robot you are interested in implementing in your warehouse.
- **Warehouse Size (6)**: Choose the size of your warehouse from the available options.
- **Operation Location (7)**: Select all relevant locations where the robot will be operating.
- **Note (8)**: Additional comments or notes about your proposal.
- **Note 1 (9)**: More details about your robot solution proposal.
- **Date (10)**: Enter the date you would like to implement the robotics solution.
- **Time (11)**: Enter the time of day you would like to schedule the implementation.
- **Phone (12)**: Enter your contact phone number.
- **Email 2 (13)**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your email or phone number.
