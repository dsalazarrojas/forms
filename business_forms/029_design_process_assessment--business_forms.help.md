# design_process_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the design process, including the design team, team lead, stakeholders, and feedback or comments received.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Introduction" page to begin the assessment.
2. On the "design_team" page, select up to three options for your design team from the dropdown menu.
3. On the "team_lead" page, select one option for your team lead from the dropdown menu.
4. On the "stakeholder" page, enter any relevant stakeholders' names or details.
5. On the "feedback" page, enter any feedback or comments related to the design process.
6. On the "Email" page, enter your email address to receive any further instructions or feedback.
7. On the "File upload" page, upload any relevant files related to the design process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page provides an overview of the design process and serves as a starting point for the assessment.
* **design_team**: Select up to three options for your design team from the dropdown menu. This field helps identify the team members involved in the design process.
	+ **type:** select_multiple
	+ **options:** option 1, option 2, option 3
* **team_lead**: Select one option for your team lead from the dropdown menu. This field helps identify the person leading the design process.
	+ **type:** select_one
	+ **options:** option 1, option 2
* **stakeholder**: Enter any relevant stakeholders' names or details. This field helps identify any external parties involved in the design process.
	+ **type:** text
	+ **required:** false
* **feedback**: Enter any feedback or comments related to the design process. This field helps gather insights for future improvements.
	+ **type:** text
	+ **required:** false
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive any further instructions or feedback.
	+ **type:** email
	+ **required:** false
* **File upload**: Upload any relevant files related to the design process.
	+ **type:** text
	+ **required:** false
