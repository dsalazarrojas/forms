3. Enter your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.

8. Choose the type of lead, 'Qualified' or 'Unqualified', based on the lead's quality.
9. Select the lead source, 'Referral', 'Advertisement', or 'Other', to indicate how the lead was generated.
10. Enter any additional notes about the project.
11. If applicable, assign a user to follow up on the project.
12. Set a date for follow-up activities.
13. Update the follow-up status, 'Scheduled', 'In Progress', or 'Completed', as needed.
14. Add any follow-up notes for reference.
15. Enter the follow-up user's contact information.

17. Enter the follow-up email.
18. Select the lead type for follow-up activities, 'Qualified' or 'Unqualified'.

20. Add any follow-up notes.
21. Set a date for the next follow-up activity.
