# Service Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Service Order Form is designed to collect information from customers about their service requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of service you are requesting.
2. Choose the location where the service will take place.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the service period.
4. Provide a brief description of the service required.
5. Enter your contact email address.
6. Enter your contact phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Order** (select_one): Please select "Yes" to indicate that you are requesting a service.
* **Service Type** (select_multiple): Select the type of service you are requesting, such as "Yes" or "No". You can select multiple options if needed.
* **Service Location** (select_one): Select the location where the service will take place.
* **Start Date** (date): Enter the start date of the service period.
* **End Date** (date): Enter the end date of the service period.
* **Description** (note): Please provide a brief description of the service required.
* **Contact Email** (email): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the service.
* **Contact Phone** (text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the service.
