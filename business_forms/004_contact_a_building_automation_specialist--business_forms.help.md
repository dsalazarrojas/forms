# Contact A Building Automation Specialist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your building automation needs. It will help us understand your requirements and provide you with a tailored solution to automate your facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and select relevant options. Please answer each question as thoroughly as possible to provide us with a clear understanding of your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Inquiry Information (1)**: A brief introduction to your building automation needs.
* **First Name (2)**: Your first name.
* **Last Name (3)**: Your last name.
* **Company Name (4)**: Name of your organization.
* **Job Title (5)**: Your position or role.
* **Email Address (6)**: Best email to reach you.
* **Phone Number (7)**: Best phone number to reach you.
* **Building Type (8)**: Type of facility you need automation for (e.g. Office Building, Hospital, School).
* **Building Size (9)**: Approximate square footage of your facility.
* **Automation Systems Needed (10)**: Select all systems you are interested in (e.g. HVAC, Lighting, Security).
* **Current Building Automation System (11)**: Do you have an existing BAS? If yes, what's your current system like?
* **Project Timeline (12)**: When do you plan to start your project (e.g. Immediate, 1-3 months).
* **Estimated Budget Range (13)**: Your budget for this project (optional, but helps us tailor our solution).
* **Primary Project Goals (14)**: Select all main objectives of your project (e.g. Energy Efficiency, Cost Reduction).
* **Project Description (15)**: Describe your project and specific requirements.
* **Preferred Contact Method (16)**: How would you like to be contacted (e.g. Email, Phone Call).
* **Best Time to Contact (17)**: When are you available for contact (optional, but helps us schedule meetings).
* **Additional Comments (18)**: Any other information you'd like to share about your project.

Please fill out the form, and we'll be in touch to discuss your building automation needs and provide a tailored solution.
