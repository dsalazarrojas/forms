# Business Partner Store Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the performance of business partner stores based on various criteria to ensure they meet the expected standards of the business partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form completely, making sure to answer all required questions.
2. Be honest and objective in your assessment, focusing on specific observations and facts.
3. Use the provided scales or options to rate each question accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the evaluator of this form.
* **Email Address** (`evaluator_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`evaluator_phone`, `text`, optional): If you need to be contacted for further questions or clarification, please enter your phone number.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the store being evaluated.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, `text`, required): Enter the full address of the store.
* **Store Manager Name** (`store_manager`, `text`, optional): If you met or interacted with a store manager, enter their name.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your visit to the store.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your visit to the store.
* **Store Type** (`store_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of retail location from the provided options (Flagship store, Standard retail, Outlet store, Pop-up location, Partner location, Other).
* **Store Size** (`store_size`, `select_one`, required): Choose the approximate square footage of the store from the provided options (Under 1000 sq ft, 1000 to 2500 sq ft, 2500 to 5000 sq ft, 5000 to 10000 sq ft, Over 10000 sq ft).
* **Exterior Appearance Rating** (`exterior_appearance`, `select_one`, required): Rate the outside appearance of the store on a scale of Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Interior Cleanliness Rating** (`interior_cleanliness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the cleanliness of the store on a scale of Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Product Display Rating** (`product_display`, `select_one`, required): Rate the product presentation on a scale of Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Signage Quality Rating** (`signage_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of signage and wayfinding in the store on a scale of Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Staff Greeting** (`staff_greeting`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you were greeted by staff, and if so, if it was promptly and warmly or not.
* **Staff Knowledge Rating** (`staff_knowledge`, `select_one`, required): Rate the staff's product knowledge on a scale of Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor, Not applicable (if not applicable).
* **Inventory Availability** (`inventory_availability`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether the product was available in the store on a scale of Fully stocked, Mostly stocked, Partially stocked, Limited inventory, Poorly stocked.
* **Pricing Visibility** (`pricing_visibility`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether prices were clearly displayed on a scale of Yes - all prices visible, Yes - most prices visible, Partially - some prices missing, No - prices not visible.
* **Checkout Experience Rating** (`checkout_experience`, `select_one`, required): Rate the checkout process on a scale of Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor, Did not checkout.
* **Brand Compliance** (`brand_compliance`, `select_one`, required): Answer how well the store follows the brand standards on a scale of Fully compliant, Mostly compliant, Somewhat compliant, Not compliant.
* **Issues Identified** (`issues_identified`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all issues you observed in the store.
* **Issues Description** (`issues_description`, `text`, optional): Describe any issues you identified in detail, if applicable.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, required): Provide recommendations or improvements you suggest to the store.
* **Overall Store Rating** (`overall_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the store's overall performance on a scale of Excellent - exceeds standards, Very good - above standards, Good - meets standards, Fair - below standards, Poor - needs immediate attention.
* **Follow-up Required** (`follow_up_required`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether follow-up is required on a scale of Yes - urgent, Yes - routine, No - all satisfactory.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any other observations or comments about the store.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering questions.
* Use concrete examples or incidents to support your ratings or answers.
* Keep the form clear and concise, but thorough in your assessment.
* If unsure or need clarification, please reach out to the business partner for further questions or guidance.
