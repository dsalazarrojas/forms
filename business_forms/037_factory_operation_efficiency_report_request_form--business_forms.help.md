# Factory Operation Efficiency Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Operation Efficiency Report Request Form is designed to gather information about various aspects of factory operations. This form is intended for employees who need to report on their shifts, including start and end times, equipment used, and production metrics. The data collected from this form will help factory managers and supervisors understand how to optimize factory operations for improved efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the necessary details.
2. Select the correct options for each question, if applicable.
3. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Information** (`id: 1`): Enter a brief description of the report.
* **Select Shift** (`id: 2`): Check if this is a report about your shift. This will help us track which shifts are being reported on.
* **Select Shift End** (`id: 3`): Check if this is the end of your shift. This will help us track the duration of your shift.
* **Equipment Type** (`id: 4`): Check if you used any equipment during your shift.
* **Equipment Type** (`id: 5`): This field is identical to the previous one, but if you select "Yes" here, it will be considered as a separate instance of equipment usage.
* **Shift Start Time** (`id: 6`): Enter the start time of your shift in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
* **Shift End Time** (`id: 7`): Enter the end time of your shift in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00).
* **Shift Duration** (`id: 8`): Enter the duration of your shift in hours.
* **Shift Start Date** (`id: 9`): Enter the start date of your shift in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Shift End Date** (`id: 10`): Enter the end date of your shift in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Total Production** (`id: 11`): Enter the total production achieved during your shift.
* **Material Loss** (`id: 12`): Enter any material loss during your shift.
* **Total Loss** (`id: 13`): Enter any total loss during your shift.
* **Report Notes** (`id: 14`): Add any additional notes about your report.
* **Email** (`id: 15`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 16`): Enter your phone number.
* **Name** (`id: 17`): Enter your name.
* **Surname** (`id: 18`): Enter your surname.
* **Company** (`id: 19`): Enter your company or department name.
* **Department** (`id: 20`): Enter your department name.
* **Job Title** (`id: 21`): Enter your job title or position.
* **Job Position** (`id: 22`): Enter your job position or role.
* **Supervisor** (`id: 23`): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Department** (`id: 24`): Enter the name of your department.
* **Submit** (`id: 25`): Select "Yes" if you are ready to submit the form.

Note: This form is optional, but submitting a completed form will help improve factory operations.
