# Software Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their software development habits, preferences, and needs. Your input will help us improve our product and better serve our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each page carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Software Survey Form Page 1: Welcome to our software survey**: This page is an optional introduction to the form.
	+ You can choose to skip this page if you're not interested in the form.
* **What do you currently use for software development? (Page 2)**: Choose one of the listed options for the Integrated Development Environment (IDE) you currently use.
	+ Please select one of the provided options: "IDE: Visual Studio", "IDE: Eclipse", or "IDE: Other".
* **How often do you use your software? (Page 3)**: If you have an opinion on how often you use our software, enter a number between 1 and 10.
	+ This question is optional, but your input is appreciated.
* **What feature(s) would you like to see added to our software? (Page 4)**: Choose one or more of the listed features that you'd like to see added to our software.
	+ Please select all features that apply to you.
* **Would you like to be contacted by a representative? (Page 5)**: If you're interested in being contacted by one of our representatives, please select a time of day that works best for you.
	+ This question is optional, but it helps us better understand your availability.

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
* Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form and help us improve our product!
