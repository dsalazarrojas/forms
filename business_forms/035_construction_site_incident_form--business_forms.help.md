# Construction Site Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to document and report incidents that occur on construction sites. It is essential to provide accurate and detailed information about the incident to ensure a prompt and effective response. This form will help the site management and safety team to investigate, record, and prevent future incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2.  Enter the incident date and time to record the exact time of the incident.
3.  Enter the project name and address to identify the site location.
4.  Provide details about the incident, including the location, type, and severity.
5.  List any witnesses to the incident.
6.  Describe the medical treatment provided.
7.  Identify the treatment provider if applicable.
8.  Document any equipment involved.
9.  Report the weather and lighting conditions at the time of the incident.
10.  Indicate if personal protective equipment (PPE) was in use and if it prevented further injury.
11.  Specify the immediate action taken.
12.  Confirm if the supervisor was notified.
13.  Document if photos were taken.
14.  Identify the apparent root cause.
15.  Suggest preventive measures to prevent recurrence.
16.  Enter your name and signature as the reporter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, `required`): Enter the time of the incident.
*   **Report Date** (`reported_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date this report is filed.
*   **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the construction project.
*   **Project Address** (`project_address`, `text`, `required`): Enter the site location.
*   **Specific Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, `required`): Enter the exact location on the site where the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the category of the incident from the provided options.
*   **Severity Level** (`severity_level`, `select_one`, `required`): Select how severe the incident was from the provided options.
*   **Injured Person Name** (`injured_person_name`, `text`, `optional`): If applicable, enter the name of the injured person.
*   **Injured Person Company** (`injured_person_company`, `text`, `optional`): If applicable, enter the injured person's employer.
*   **Injured Person Job Title** (`injured_person_job_title`, `text`, `optional`): If applicable, enter the injured person's job title.
*   **Type of Injury** (`injury_type`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select all the types of injury that occurred.
*   **Body Parts Affected** (`body_parts_affected`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select all the body parts affected by the injury.
*   **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the medical treatment provided.
*   **Treatment Provider** (`treatment_provider`, `text`, `optional`): If applicable, enter the name of the treatment provider.
*   **Detailed Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, `required`): Describe exactly what happened during the incident.
*   **Were There Witnesses?** (`witnesses_present`, `select_one`, `required`): Select if anyone saw the incident.
*   **Witness Names and Contact Info** (`witness_names`, `text`, `optional`): List all the witnesses' names and contact information.
*   **Equipment Involved** (`equipment_involved`, `text`, `optional`): Describe any equipment involved.
*   **Weather Conditions** (`weather_conditions`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the weather conditions at the time of the incident.
*   **Lighting Conditions** (`lighting_conditions`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the lighting conditions at the time of the incident.
*   **PPE In Use** (`ppe_in_use`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select the type of PPE being worn.
*   **Did PPE Prevent Further Injury?** (`ppe_effectiveness`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select if PPE prevented further injury.
*   **Immediate Action Taken** (`immediate_action_taken`, `text`, `required`): Describe the immediate action taken after the incident.
*   **Was Supervisor Notified?** (`supervisor_notified`, `select_one`, `required`): Select if the supervisor was notified.
*   **Photos Taken?** (`photos_taken`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select if photos were taken.
*   **Apparent Root Cause** (`root_cause`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the apparent root cause of the incident.
*   **Preventive Measures Recommended** (`preventive_measures`, `text`, `required`): Suggest preventive measures to prevent recurrence.
*   **Reporter Name** (`reporter_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your name as the reporter.
*   **Reporter Signature** (`reporter_signature`, `text`, `required`): Enter your name and signature as the reporter.

## Tips

*   Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the report.
*   Enter accurate and detailed information to aid in the investigation and response process.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, seek guidance from the site management or safety team.
