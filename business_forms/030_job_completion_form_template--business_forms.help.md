# Job Completion Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the completion of a job or task, ensuring accurate and timely recording of key details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with the accurate information about the job completion.
2. Enter the completion date and time for both the employee and the employer, if applicable.
3. Check the job completion status and choose the corresponding option from the select box.
4. Add any relevant notes or comments, if necessary.
5. Ensure that both the employee and employer signatures are accurate and dated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Completion Form Template** (Job Completion Form Template): This is the title of the form.
* **Job Completion Date** (job_completion_date): Enter the date on which the job was completed.
* **Job Completion Time** (job_completion_time): Enter the time on which the job was completed. (Optional)
* **Job Title** (job_title): Enter a brief description of the job or task completed.
* **Job Hours Worked** (job_hours_worked): Enter the number of hours worked on the job.
* **Job Employer Signature** (job_employer_signature): Enter the employer's signature, if applicable.
* **Job Employee Signature** (job_employee_signature): Enter the employee's signature, if applicable.
* **Job Completion Date Employer** (job_completion_date_employer): Enter the date on which the employer acknowledged the job completion. (Optional)
* **Job Completion Time Employer** (job_completion_time_employer): Enter the time on which the employer acknowledged the job completion. (Optional)
* **Job Completion Status** (job_confirmation): Choose the status of the job completion from the select box.
* **Job Completion Notes** (job_completion_notes): Enter any additional notes or comments about the job completion, if necessary.
* **Job Completion Signature Employer** (job_completion_signature_employer): Enter the signature of the employer, if applicable.
* **Job Completion Signature Employee** (job_completion_signature_employee): Enter the signature of the employee, if applicable.
* **Job Completion Signature Date Employer** (job_completion_signature_date_employer): Enter the date on which the employer signed, if applicable.
* **Job Completion Signature Time Employer** (job_completion_signature_time_employer): Enter the time on which the employer signed, if applicable.
* **Job Completion Signature Date Employee** (job_completion_signature_date_employee): Enter the date on which the employee signed, if applicable.
* **Job Completion Signature Time Employee** (job_completion_signature_time_employee): Enter the time on which the employee signed, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurately completed before signing off on the job completion.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors or omissions.
* Store a copy of the completed form for future reference.
