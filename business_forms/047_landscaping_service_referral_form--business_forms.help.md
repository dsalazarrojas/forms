# Landscaping Service Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the referrer and the referree (the person being referred) to track and measure the effectiveness of the referral process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* `first_name`
	* `referrer_name`
	* `email`
	* `referrer_email`
	* `employed_at_referrer`
	* `referrer_rating`
	* `how_long_referral`
	* `how_did_you_meet_referrer`
	* `referrer_rating_repeat`
	* `how_long_referral_repeat`
2. Choose an option for `employed_at_referrer` from the list provided:
	* 1 year
	* 2 years
	* 3 years
	* 4 years
	* 5 years
	* Less than 1 year
	* More than 5 years
3. Choose an option for `referrer_rating` from the list provided:
	* 'Yes'
	* 'No'
	* Other
4. Choose an option for `how_long_referral` from the list provided:
	* 'Yes'
	* 'No'
	* Other
5. Choose an option for `how_did_you_meet_referrer` from the list provided:
	* I was referred by a friend or family member
	* I saw an ad or sign
	* I saw the referrer's work on social media
	* I saw their website or marketing materials
	* Other (please describe)
6. Add any additional comments in the `note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name**: The name of the person completing this form.
* **referrer_name**: The name of the person who referred them.
* **email**: The email address of the person completing this form.
* **referrer_email**: The email address of the person who referred them.
* **employed_at_referrer**: The length of time the person who referred them has worked at Landscaping Service Referral Form.
* **referrer_rating**: The rating of the person who referred them (out of 5 stars).
* **how_long_referral**: The length of time they were referred to Landscaping Service Referral Form.
* **how_did_you_meet_referrer**: How they met the person who referred them.
* **referrer_rating_repeat**: The rating of the person who referred them (again, out of 5 stars).
* **how_long_referral_repeat**: The length of time they were referred to Landscaping Service Referral Form (again).
* **note**: Any additional comments they want to add.
* **referrer_employed_repeat**: The length of time the person who referred them has worked at Landscaping Service Referral Form (again).
* **referrer_phone_repeat**: The phone number of the person who referred them (not actually used in the form).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Choose an option for `employed_at_referrer` that is accurate to the best of your knowledge.
* Take your time to provide a thoughtful answer to `how_did_you_meet_referrer`.
* Use the `note` field to add any additional comments or details you think are important.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us at [insert contact information].
