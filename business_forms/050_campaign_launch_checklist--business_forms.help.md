# Campaign Launch Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campaign Launch Checklist is a form designed to help ensure that all necessary steps are taken before launching a campaign. It helps you gather key information and confirm that all tasks are completed and ready for launch.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Enter the Campaign Project Name**: In the text field, type the name of the campaign project.
2. **Choose the Lead Campaign Manager**: Select the person responsible for managing the campaign.
3. **Set the Targeted Launch Date**: Choose the date when the campaign is expected to launch.
4. **Define the Primary Campaign Objective**: Type in the objective of the campaign (e.g., Brand Awareness, Product Launch, Lead Gen).
5. **Check Assets and Collateral**: Select the status of visual creative assets, copywriting and messaging, landing pages, and email templates.
6. **Select the Distribution Channels**: Choose the channels through which the campaign will be distributed (e.g., Paid Search, Social Media Ads, Organic Social Media).
7. **Confirm Budget Allocation Status**: Select the status of the budget allocation for the campaign.
8. **Set the Total Campaign Budget Estimate**: Enter an estimate of the total campaign budget.
9. **Verify KPI and Tracking Setup**: Confirm that KPI and tracking setup is complete and verified.
10. **Get Final Approval**: Enter the name of the person approving the campaign launch.
11. **Set the Checklist Completion Date**: Enter the date when the checklist is completed.
12. **Schedule Post-Launch Review**: Optional - Schedule a post-launch review date.
