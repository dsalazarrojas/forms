# Remote Work Policy Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback on the company's remote work policy. Your input will help us understand your experiences and perceptions, and inform policy development and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply go through each section and answer the questions according to your experience and opinion. You can select all that apply from the multiple-choice options or enter your free-form text answers. If you have no opinion or experience with a particular question, please select the "No" option or leave the field blank. Don't worry about writing a long essay – your brief answers are just fine!

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, `text`, required/optional): Please share your general feedback about your remote work experience with the company. What have you enjoyed or disliked about working remotely? Do you have any suggestions for improvements?
* **Remote Work Policies** (`remote_work_policies`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): How have you experienced the company's remote work policies? Select all that apply:
	+ Yes (you've had a positive experience)
	+ No (you've had a negative experience)
* **Work Arrangements** (`work_arrangements`, `select_one`, required/optional): How do you prefer to work? Select one of:
	+ Yes (yes, you prefer to work remotely)
	+ No (no, you prefer to work in the office)
* **Communication** (`communication`, `text`, required/optional): How has communication been while working remotely? For example, have you had trouble getting in touch with team members or managers?
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, `text`, required/optional): How has collaboration been while working remotely? For example, have you had trouble working with others on projects or sharing files?
* **Work From Home** (`work_from_home`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Are you able to work from home? Select all that apply:
	+ Yes (yes, you've worked from home)
	+ No (no, you haven't worked from home)
* **Work From Anywhere** (`work_from_anywhere`, `select_one`, required/optional): How do you prefer to work? Select one of:
	+ Yes (yes, you prefer to work from anywhere)
	+ No (no, you prefer to work in the office)
* **Impact on Business** (`impact_on_business`, `text`, required/optional): Do you think the remote work policy has had a positive or negative impact on the business? Please explain.
* **Impact on Individual** (`impact_on_individual`, `text`, required/optional): Do you think the remote work policy has had a positive or negative impact on you as an individual? Please explain.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required/optional): Do you have any suggestions for improving the remote work policy? Please share them!
* **Company Response** (`company_response`, `text`, required/optional): Do you have any additional comments or questions about the remote work policy?
