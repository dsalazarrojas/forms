# H₂S Safety Equipment Sign Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by H₂S personnel to sign out of safety equipment used during work activities. It is essential to complete this form accurately and honestly, as it helps the organization keep track of equipment usage, availability, and potential maintenance needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and title in the "user" field, this is your identification information.
2. Select the type of safety equipment you are using from the "equipment_type" field, this ensures that we can track which type of equipment is being used.
3. Enter the date you are signing out of the equipment in the "date_of_sign_out" field.
4. Enter the time you are finishing using the equipment in the "time_of_sign_out" field.
5. Provide the location from where you are signing out the equipment in the "location_of_sign_out" field.
6. Describe the status of the equipment you are signing out in the "equipment_status" field, e.g., "in good condition", "needs maintenance", "broken".
7. You may add any additional comments about the equipment usage in the "additional_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User** (user, text, optional):
	+ This field is your identification information, please fill it in with your full name and title.
* **Equipment Type** (equipment_type, select one, optional):
	+ Select the correct type of safety equipment you are using.
* **Date of Sign Out** (date_of_sign_out, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date you are signing out of the equipment.
* **Time of Sign Out** (time_of_sign_out, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time you are finishing using the equipment.
* **Location of Sign Out** (location_of_sign_out, text, optional):
	+ Provide the location where you are signing out the equipment.
* **Equipment Status** (equipment_status, text, optional):
	+ Describe the status of the equipment you are signing out.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, text, optional):
	+ You can add any additional comments about the equipment usage.
