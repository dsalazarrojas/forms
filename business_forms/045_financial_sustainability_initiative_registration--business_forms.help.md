# financial_sustainability_initiative_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register financial sustainability initiatives with accurate and complete information to avoid duplication and ensure the initiative is properly represented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to review each field carefully to avoid duplication and ensure the initiative is accurately represented.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of your financial sustainability initiative. This will be the name that appears on the form and in any subsequent references to the initiative.
* **Organization Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact details of the organization associated with the financial sustainability initiative.
* **Initiative Details** (`initiative_details`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the financial sustainability initiative, including its purpose, goals, and objectives.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the financial sustainability initiative is taking place or will be implemented.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter a contact number for the organization or individual associated with the initiative.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the organization or individual associated with the initiative.
