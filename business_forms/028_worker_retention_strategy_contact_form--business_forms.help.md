# Worker Retention Strategy Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and feedback from employees on the company's worker retention strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, department, and email address.
2. Enter your feedback or concerns about the company's worker retention strategies.
3. Select the department you belong to from the provided options.
4. Optionally, provide your company name if you are not already employed with us.
5. If you have suggestions for improving the company's retention initiatives, please include them in the "Suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information**: This is where you can enter your contact details. Please fill in your name, email address, and phone number so that we can contact you back.
- **Employee Name**: This is your name, which will be used to identify you for future references.
- **Department**: This is your department at the company. Please select the department from the provided options.
- **Feedback**: Share your concerns, issues, or suggestions regarding worker retention strategies.
- **Email**: This is where you can enter your email address so that we can contact you.
- **Phone**: If you have a phone number, please enter it so that we can contact you via phone.
- **Company**: If you are an employee of another company, please enter your company name here.
- **Retention Initiatives**: Share your thoughts on how we can improve our worker retention initiatives.
- **Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions on how we can improve worker retention, please include them here.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill in all required fields for accurate processing of your feedback.
- If you are experiencing difficulties with the form, please reach out to the HR team for assistance.
- Please note that your feedback is treated with confidentiality and is used only for the purpose of improving worker retention.
