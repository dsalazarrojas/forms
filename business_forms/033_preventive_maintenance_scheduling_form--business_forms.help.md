# Preventive Maintenance Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for scheduling preventive maintenance on vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the date field with the date you are scheduling the maintenance.
2. Enter the location where the vehicle is currently stationed.
3. Provide details about the vehicle, including any relevant information about the type, model, and make.
4. Specify the required services for the scheduled maintenance.
5. Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date**: Enter the date you are scheduling the maintenance in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Station Location**: Enter the location where the vehicle is currently stationed, such as a warehouse, depot, or a specific site.
* **Vehicle Information**: Provide details about the vehicle, including the type, model, and make. This will help the maintenance team understand the specifics of the vehicle for efficient maintenance.
* **Service Requirements**: Specify the required services for the scheduled maintenance, such as oil change, tire rotation, or software updates. 

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and relevant information to help the maintenance team perform the required services efficiently.
* Double-check the information you provide to prevent errors or misunderstandings.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the maintenance team or the form owner for clarification.
