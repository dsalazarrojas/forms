# Retail Onboarding Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help new users understand the key concepts and terminology used in the retail industry. It's a quick and easy way to get familiar with the main ideas and strategies that are essential for success in retail.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each question and its corresponding options or text boxes.
- Fill out each question thoroughly, providing as much information as you can.
- Do not hesitate to ask for help if you're unsure about any of the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Satisfaction (1)
* **Customer Satisfaction** (number): This question asks you to rate your level of satisfaction with the current state of your retail business. Please use the provided scale to indicate your level of satisfaction.

### Sales Tactics (2)
* **Sales Tactics** (select multiple): Please select all the sales tactics that you currently use in your retail business. These tactics can include things like offering discounts, bundling products, or using sales teams.

### Customer Engagement (3)
* **Customer Engagement** (number): This question asks you to rate your level of engagement with your customers. Please use the provided scale to indicate your level of engagement.

### Customer Satisfaction 2 (4)
* **Customer Satisfaction 2** (number): This question is similar to the first question about customer satisfaction. However, it's a follow-up question that explores your satisfaction with your customers in a different context. Please use the provided scale to indicate your level of satisfaction.

### Onboarding Strategy (5)
* **Onboarding Strategy** (text): This question asks you to describe your current onboarding strategy for new customers. Please provide a brief description of your strategy, including any specific methods or tools you use.

### Retail Channels (6)
* **Retail Channels** (select multiple): Please select all the retail channels you use to reach your customers. These can include things like social media, email, or in-store promotions.
