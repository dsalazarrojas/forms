# Industrial Heat Shield Panel Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Industrial Heat Shield Panel Order Form is designed to gather essential information from customers to place orders for industrial heat shield panels. This form helps us to understand your requirements and process your order accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information section (Field 1: customer_info) with your details, including your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. Provide a brief description of your order in the order details section (Field 2: order_details).
3. Select the type of panel you are interested in ordering (Field 3: panel_type) from the options provided (Heat, Non-Heat, or Hybrid).
4. Specify the panel size (Field 4: panel_size) and thickness (Field 5: panel_thickness) you require.
5. Choose the material you would like the panel to be made of (Field 6: panel_material) from the options provided (Steel, Aluminum, or Wood).
6. Finally, enter your customer name and address in the corresponding fields (Fields 7: customer_name and 8: customer_address).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_info (text)**: Please provide your name and contact information for us to get back to you.
* **order_details (text)**: Describe your order in a few words. This will help us understand your requirements.
* **panel_type (select_one)**: Choose the type of panel you are ordering: Heat, Non-Heat, or Hybrid.
* **panel_size (number)**: Enter the size of the panel you need. Make sure to provide the correct measurements.
* **panel_thickness (number)**: Specify the thickness of the panel you require.
* **panel_material (select_one)**: Select the material you would like the panel to be made of: Steel, Aluminum, or Wood.
* **customer_name (text)**: Enter your name as the customer.
* **customer_address (text)**: Provide your address for delivery purposes.

## Tips
* Double-check your details carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us for further assistance.
* Please note that some fields are required for us to process your order accurately.
