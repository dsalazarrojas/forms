# Angel Investment Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from angel investors to request a refund for their investments in a startup or company. The form ensures that all necessary details are gathered for the refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as an investor.
2. Enter the name of the startup or company you invested in.
3. Provide your email address for contact purposes.
4. Specify the total original investment amount.
5. Select the date of the original investment.
6. Enter the investment agreement reference number (if available).
7. Select or describe the primary reason for the refund request.
8. Provide a detailed explanation for the refund request (if applicable).
9. Enter the name of the bank where the refund should be sent.
10. Enter the account number or IBAN for the receiving bank.
11. Enter the SWIFT/BIC code (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Investor Full Name** (`investor_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the investor.
* **Startup / Company Name** (`company_startup_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the startup or company you invested in.
* **Investor Email Address** (`investor_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Total Original Investment Amount** (`total_investment_amount`, number, required): Specify the total amount of the original investment.
* **Date of Original Investment** (`investment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the original investment.
* **Investment Agreement Reference Number** (`investment_agreement_reference_number`, text, optional): Enter the reference number of the investment agreement (if available).
* **Amount of Refund Requested** (`refund_amount_requested`, number, required): Enter the amount of refund requested.
* **Primary Reason for Refund** (`reason_for_refund`, select_one, required): Select the primary reason for the refund request from the given options.
* **Detailed Explanation for Refund Request** (`detailed_reason_explanation`, text, required): Provide a detailed explanation for the refund request.
* **Receiving Bank Name** (`bank_name_for_refund`, text, required): Enter the name of the bank where the refund should be sent.
* **Account Number / IBAN** (`account_number_iban`, text, required): Enter the account number or IBAN for the receiving bank.
* **SWIFT / BIC Code** (`swift_bic_code`, text, optional): Enter the SWIFT/BIC code for international refunds (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to ensure the refund process is efficient.
* If you need help or have questions about the refund process, contact our support team.
* Please note that the refund process may take some time to complete.
