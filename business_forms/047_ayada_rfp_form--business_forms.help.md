# Ayada Request for Proposal (RFP) Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your upcoming event to provide you with the best possible solutions and services. Please provide the required information in the following fields to help us understand your needs accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the contact information section, which includes your full name, email address, phone number, and the name of your company or organization.
2. Next, provide details about your event, including the type, start date, estimated number of attendees, and any special requirements.
3. Specify your audiovisual and technical needs, such as projector and screen, microphones, and Wi-Fi.
4. Provide an estimated total budget for your event.
5. Choose how you heard about Ayada, and include any special requirements or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name_rfp`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address_rfp`, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number_rfp`, required): Enter your phone number for any follow-up questions.
* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_name_rfp`, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Type of Event** (`event_type_rfp`, required): Select the primary category of your event from the options provided.
* **Requested Event Start Date** (`event_date_rfp`, required): Enter the date you would like the event to start.
* **Flexible Dates** (`flexible_dates_rfp`, required): Choose how flexible you are with your event dates.
* **Estimated Number of Attendees** (`guest_count_rfp`, required): Enter an approximate headcount for your event.
* **Number of Sleeping Rooms Required** (`rooms_required_rfp`, optional): If you need rooms for your guests, enter the total number of rooms required.
* **Number of Nights** (`nights_count_rfp`, optional): Enter the number of nights your guests will be staying.
* **Meeting Spaces Needed** (`space_requirements_rfp`, required): Select all the meeting spaces you require for your event.
* **Catering Requirements** (`catering_needs_rfp`, optional): Choose all the catering options you need for your event.
* **Audiovisual and Tech Needs** (`av_requirements_rfp`, optional): Select all the audiovisual and technical requirements you need for your event.
* **Estimated Total Budget** (`budget_estimate_rfp`, required): Enter your estimated total budget for the event.
* **How Did You Hear About Ayada?** (`referral_source_rfp`, required): Choose how you heard about Ayada.
* **Special Requirements or Comments** (`special_requirements_rfp`, optional): Provide any special requirements or comments you may have for your event.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact us for clarification.
* This form is designed to be a starting point for a successful event planning process.
