# Sewer Testing Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sewer Testing Log is a form for documenting and tracking the results of sewer tests. Its purpose is to provide a standardized way to collect and store data about the testing frequency, location, type, and results of the tests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Test Date by using the calendar icon or typing the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
2. Choose the Testing Frequency by selecting one of the available options.
3. Choose the Testing Location by selecting all applicable options from the dropdown menu.
4. Select the Test Type by choosing one of the available options.
5. Enter the Inspector Name and Inspector Organization.
6. Choose the Test Result by selecting one of the available options.
7. Add any additional Notes about the test.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the test.
* **Testing Frequency** (`testing_frequency`, number, required: false): Choose the frequency of the test, e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.
* **Testing Location** (`testing_location`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable locations where the test was performed.
* **Test Type** (`test_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of test performed, e.g., Manhole Inspection, Catch Basin Inspection, etc.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the inspector who performed the test.
* **Inspector Organization** (`inspector_organization`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the organization or company conducting the test.
* **Test Result** (`test_result`, select_one, required: false): Choose the result of the test, e.g., Passed, Failed, In Progress.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the test.

## Tips
* Make sure to choose the correct Test Type and Test Result to ensure that the data is accurate and complete.
* Use the correct date format when entering the Test Date.
* Enter the correct Inspector Name and Inspector Organization to ensure that the data is attributed to the correct person or organization.
* Use the Notes field to provide additional context and information about the test.
