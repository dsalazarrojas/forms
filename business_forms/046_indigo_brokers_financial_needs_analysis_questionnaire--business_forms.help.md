# indigo_brokers_financial_needs_analysis_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to analyze the financial needs of brokers, providing an overview of the current state of the business entity's finances. It is designed to collect specific information about the business entity's ID, type, contact email, and contact phone. The goal is to get a better understanding of the entity's financial situation, which can be used for various purposes, such as providing financial services or making informed business decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the business entity ID in the corresponding field.
2. Select the type of company from the available options (if any).
3. Enter the contact email of the business entity.
4. Enter the contact phone number of the business entity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Entity ID** (`business_entity_id`, number, required: false): This field is used to identify the unique ID of the business entity. Enter the correct ID number to associate this form response with the correct entity.
- **Company Type** (`company_type`, text, required: false): This field is used to specify the type of company, such as Corporation, Partnership, or Sole Proprietorship. Select the type that best describes the business entity.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): This field is used to collect the email address of the contact person or representative of the business entity.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect the phone number of the contact person or representative of the business entity.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in the fields accurately and completely to ensure the form is completed correctly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department or person for clarification.
- This form is designed to collect information, not to create new business entities or update existing ones.
