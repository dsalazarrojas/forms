# Utility Bill Clarification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers report issues or discrepancies with their utility bills and allow utility providers to track and manage these inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name for identification purposes.
2. Provide your utility bill number for reference.
3. Select a date when the issue occurred.
4. Describe the issue you are experiencing with your bill.
5. Choose your preferred method of contact for follow-up (Phone, Email, or In Person).
6. Select the method of contact you would like to use (Phone, Email, or In Person).
7. Enter your contact number.
8. Select the status of the issue (Open, Closed, or In Progress).
9. Select the resolution of the issue (Not Applicable, Accepted, Rejected, or In Progress).
10. (Optional) Provide any follow-up details and number.
11. (Optional) Select a follow-up method (Phone, Email, or In Person).
12. (Optional) Select a follow-up date and status.
13. (Optional) Enter any comments or follow-up details.
14. (Optional) Enter the name of the customer service agent you spoke with.
15. (Optional) Enter the next steps or actions taken.
16. (Optional) Enter any follow-up details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Utility Bill Number**: Enter your utility bill number for reference.
* **Bill Date**: Select the date when the issue occurred.
* **Description**: Describe the issue you are experiencing with your bill.
* **Issue Details**: Enter the details about the issue.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose your preferred method of contact for follow-up (Phone, Email, or In Person).
* **Contact Method**: Select the method of contact you would like to use (Phone, Email, or In Person).
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
* **Issue Status**: Select the status of the issue (Open, Closed, or In Progress).
* **Issue Resolution**: Select the resolution of the issue (Not Applicable, Accepted, Rejected, or In Progress).
* **Follow-up Details**: (Optional) Provide any additional details about the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Number**: (Optional) Enter any follow-up number.
* **Follow-up Method**: (Optional) Select a follow-up method (Phone, Email, or In Person).
* **Follow-up Date**: (Optional) Select a follow-up date.
* **Follow-up Status**: (Optional) Select a follow-up status (Not Applicable, Completed, or In Progress).
* **Follow-up Comments**: (Optional) Enter any comments or details about the follow-up.
* **Customer Service Agent**: (Optional) Enter the name of the customer service agent you spoke with.
* **Next Steps**: (Optional) Enter any next steps or actions taken.
* **Follow-up Details**: (Optional) Enter any follow-up details.
