# Phase Agreement Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phase Agreement Confirmation form is used to gather information on project phases and participant agreements. This form is essential for ensuring that all participants are informed and agree to the project phase details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter the project phase details in the "project_phase_details" field.
3. Select the participant information, including their name, email, phone number, and note, in the respective fields.
4. Confirm the participant's consent by selecting "Yes" in the "participant_consent" field.
5. Select the participant's electronic signature(s) by choosing "Yes" from the dropdown list.
6. Enter the project status by selecting "Active" or "Inactive" in the "project_status" field.
7. Optionally, enter additional participant information and comments in the "participant_note" field.
8. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **project_phase_details**: This field is used to enter project phase details. Enter a brief description of the project phase.
* **participant_informations**: This field is used to enter participant information. Enter a number to indicate how many participants are involved.
* **participant_consent**: This field is used to confirm the participant's consent. Select "Yes" to indicate that the participant agrees to the project phase details.
* **electronic_signatures**: This field is used to select participant electronic signatures. Choose "Yes" to indicate that the participant has provided an electronic signature.
* **project_phase_status**: This field is used to enter the project phase status. Enter a date to indicate the status of the project phase.
* **participant_name**: This field is used to enter the participant's name.
* **participant_email**: This field is used to enter the participant's email address.
* **participant_phone**: This field is used to enter the participant's phone number.
* **participant_note**: This field is used to enter any additional notes or comments.
* **participant_signature**: This field is used to confirm the participant's signature. Select "Yes" to indicate that the participant has provided their signature.
* **project_status**: This field is used to enter the project status. Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the project phase.
