# HR Consulting Quotation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HR Consulting Quotation Form is designed for clients to provide information about their project and services they are interested in. This form allows HR consulting firms to generate quotes that accurately reflect the project's specifics and clients' requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your client's details, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the project's start and end dates.
3. Select the services you require.
4. Choose the quotations you are interested in.
5. Enter your company's name.
6. Indicate whether the client is new or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
* **Client Information** (1): Enter your client's name, email, and phone number. This information will be used to contact the client and confirm project details.

### Contact
* **Contact** (2): Enter the client's email and phone number. This will allow us to get in touch with the client and provide them with project updates.

### Project Details
* **Project Details** (3): Enter the project start and end dates to give us a clear understanding of the project's scope and timeline.

### Services
* **Services** (4): Select the services you are interested in, such as HR consulting, project management, or data analysis. This will help us tailor our quotation to your specific needs.

### Quotations
* **Quotations** (5): Select the quotations you are interested in, such as hourly rate or flat fee. This will allow us to provide you with an accurate quote for your project.

### Company Information
* **Company Information** (6): Enter your company's name to associate your project with your company. This will help us track your company's projects and provide you with more personalized service.

### New Client
* **New Client** (7): Check this box if this client is new to us. This will help us prioritize our services and provide you with more efficient support.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we can provide you with the most accurate and comprehensive quote.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
* Remember to review your quote carefully before committing to a project, as this will ensure that both you and the client are aware of the project scope and requirements.
