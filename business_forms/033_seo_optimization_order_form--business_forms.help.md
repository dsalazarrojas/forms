# SEO Optimization Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your business and website needs for our SEO optimization services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. Make sure to provide accurate and detailed answers to help us better understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (Client Name, text, optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed in our communication.
* **Business Name** (Business Name, text, optional): Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Website URL** (Website URL, text, optional): Provide the URL of your website for our team to access.
* **Services Requested** (Services Requested, select_multiple, optional): Select the SEO services you are interested in, such as SEO Analysis, Keyword Research, Content Creation, Link Building, Social Media Optimization, or Local SEO.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, optional): Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to communicate with you.
* **Message** (Message, text, optional): Share any additional information or requests you have.
* **Business Address** (Business Address, text, optional): Enter your business address if different from your contact address.

Note: Fields with an asterisk (*) are required.
