# Luggage Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to log information related to luggage for inventory management or tracking purposes within our business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting to fill out the form.
2. Start by filling in the `travel_form` field with a brief description of the luggage or travel-related information.
3. Enter the `date` field with the relevant date related to the luggage or travel.
4. Enter the `time` field with the relevant time related to the luggage or travel.
5. In the `luggage_type` field, select the type of luggage or item being logged.
6. Enter the `storage_id` field with the corresponding storage ID, if applicable.
7. Enter the `user_id` field with the ID of the user responsible for the luggage or travel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **travel_form** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the luggage or travel-related information.
* **date** (date, required): Enter the relevant date related to the luggage or travel.
* **time** (time, required): Enter the relevant time related to the luggage or travel.
* **luggage_type** (text, optional): Select the type of luggage or item being logged.
* **storage_id** (number, optional): Enter the corresponding storage ID, if applicable.
* **user_id** (number, required): Enter the ID of the user responsible for the luggage or travel.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant authorities or seek clarification before proceeding.
- This form is designed to be completed by users who are familiar with the business operations and procedures.
