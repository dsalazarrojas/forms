# Night Shift Task Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Night Shift Task Audit Form is used to document and audit night shift tasks, identifying any incidents that occurred during that time period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the incident details section (fields 1-5) for information about the incident.
2.  In the timing section (fields 6-8), select the correct dates and times for the incident, audit, and review.
3.  In the supervisor comments and approval section (fields 9-18), enter comments and approval from the supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Night Shift Audit Form**: This is the title of the form.
*   **Night Shift Activities**: A text field for describing any relevant activities that occurred during the night shift.
*   **Incidents**: A select one field for selecting the type of incident that occurred (no incident, incident reported, incident investigation, or incident review).
*   **Incident Description**: A text field for providing a description of the incident.
*   **Incident Cause**: A text field for explaining the cause of the incident.
*   **Incident Action Taken**: A text field for documenting the action taken as a result of the incident.
*   **Incident Outcome**: A text field for describing the outcome of the incident.
*   **Comments**: A text field for any additional comments about the incident.
*   **Date and Time of Incident**: A date field for selecting the date and time of the incident.
*   **Date and Time of Audit**: A date field for selecting the date and time of the audit.
*   **Date and Time of Review**: A date field for selecting the date and time of the review.
*   **Supervisor Comments**: A text field for comments from the supervisor.
*   **Supervisor Action Taken**: A text field for the action taken by the supervisor.
*   **Supervisor Comments 2**: A text field for additional comments from the supervisor.
*   **Supervisor Approval**: A select one field for the supervisor's approval (Yes or No).
*   **Supervisor Comments 3**: A text field for final comments from the supervisor.
*   **Supervisor Action Taken 2**: A text field for the second action taken by the supervisor.

Note: The field labels and descriptions are based on the provided YAML and are subject to change based on the actual business logic and requirements.
