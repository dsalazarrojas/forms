# Promoter And Business Partner Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from business promoters and partners about their relationship with the business, their income, work hours, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the fields below, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the table below.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What Is Your Relationship With The Business** (business_partner_quiz, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to describe your relationship with the business. Please provide a brief answer.
* **Do You Own The Business** (do_you_own_business, select_one, optional)
	+ Select one of the options:
		- True
		- False
* **Who Owns The Business** (business_partner_name, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to identify the owner of the business. Please provide the name.
* **What Do You Earn Per Hour** (earn_money, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select multiple options from the list:
		- Below Minimum Wage
		- Slightly Above Minimum Wage
		- Above Minimum Wage
* **How Many Hours Do You Work** (hours_worked, number, optional)
	+ Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to describe your job title within the business. Please provide a brief answer.
* **Business Hours Per Week** (business_hours_per_week, text, optional)
	+ Enter the number of hours the business operates per week.
* **Money Earned In Last 7 Days** (money_earned, number, optional)
	+ Enter the amount earned in the last 7 days.
* **How Much Do You Earn** (how_much, select_one, optional)
	+ Select one of the options:
		- $10-$19
		- $20-$29
		- $30-$39
		- $40-$49
		- $50-$59
		- $60-$69
		- $70-$79
		- $80-$89
		- $90-$99
		- $100 or more
* **Hours Per Day** (hours_per_day, number, optional)
	+ Enter the number of hours you work per day.
* **Days Worked Per Week** (days_per_week, number, optional)
	+ Enter the number of days you work per week.
* **Weekly Hours** (weekly_hours, number, optional)
	+ Enter the total weekly hours worked.
* **Weekly Hours Per Day** (weekly_hours_per_day, number, optional)
	+ Enter the number of hours worked per day.
* **Name Of Business** (business_name, text, optional)
	+ Enter the name of your business.
* **Business Phone** (business_phone, text, optional)
	+ Enter the phone number of your business.
* **Business Address** (business_address, text, optional)
	+ Enter the address of your business.
* **Business Email** (business_email, email, optional)
	+ Enter the email address of your business.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities.
* If you need to complete this form multiple times, please ensure that each form is filled out accurately and thoroughly to avoid duplication of effort.
