# Carbon Credit Claim Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a request for carbon credits to be evaluated and certified by our organization. Please fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about your project to help us assess your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your organization's information, project details, and carbon reduction data.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information to ensure a smooth evaluation process.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
4. Ensure that you have all the required documentation and evidence ready for submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Organization type** (`organization_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of organization you represent (e.g., Private Company, Public Company, Non-profit, etc.).
* **Business registration number** (`registration_number`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your organization's business registration number.
* **Tax identification number** (`tax_id`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your organization's tax identification number.
* **Contact person name** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your project.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication with your organization.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number for communication with your organization.
* **Project name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your project.
* **Project type** (`project_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of project (e.g., Renewable Energy, Reforestation, etc.).
* **Project description** (`project_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Project start date** (`project_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project end date** (`project_end_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the end date of your project.
* **Project location** (`project_location`, text, required): Enter the location of your project (country, state, region).
* **Estimated carbon reduction** (`estimated_reduction`, number, required): Enter the estimated carbon reduction of your project in metric tons CO2e.
* **Verified carbon reduction** (`verified_reduction`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the verified carbon reduction of your project in metric tons CO2e.
* **Reduction methodology** (`reduction_methodology`, text, required): Describe the methodology used to calculate the carbon reduction of your project.
* **Documentation available** (`documentation_available`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have documentation and evidence ready for submission.
* **Certification standard** (`certification_standard`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select the certification standard used for your project (e.g., ISO 14064, VCS, etc.).
* **Third-party verification** (`third_party_verification`, select_one, optional): Indicate if third-party verification has been completed for your project.
* **Verifier name** (`verifier_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the verifier or organization.
* **Claim submission date** (`claim_submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting your claim.

Please note that all fields marked as `required` are mandatory and must be filled out correctly to ensure a smooth evaluation process.
