# Process Change Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Process Change Assessment Form is a tool used to document and assess process changes within the organization. It ensures that all relevant information about the change is captured and communicated effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each process change you want to document and assess.
2. Enter the "Process Change" title in the first field.
3. Answer the "Category" question by selecting one of the options provided.
4. Provide a detailed description of the change in the "Description" field.
5. Assess the impact of the change in the "Impact" field.
6. Evaluate the risk associated with the change in the "Risk" field.
7. Describe the benefits of the change in the "Benefit" field.
8. Provide any relevant recommendations or suggestions for the change in the "Recommendation" field.
9. Add any comments or reviews about the change in the "Review Comment" field.
10. Click the "Submit" button to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Process Change** (`process_change`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise title for the process change.
* **Category** (`change_category`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options to categorize the process change.
* **Description** (`change_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the change, including its scope and any relevant context.
* **Impact** (`change_impact`, text, required): Assess the potential impact of the change on the organization or its processes.
* **Risk** (`change_risk`, text, required): Evaluate the level of risk associated with the change.
* **Benefit** (`change_benefit`, text, required): Describe the benefits of the change, including any positive outcomes.
* **Recommendation** (`change_recommendation`, text, required): Provide any relevant recommendations or suggestions for the change.
* **Review Comment** (`review_comment`, text, required): Add any comments or reviews about the change.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have all necessary information before filling out this form.
* Avoid duplication of effort by only filling out this form once for each process change.
* Be clear and concise in your answers to ensure effective communication.
* Proofread your form before submitting to ensure accuracy.
