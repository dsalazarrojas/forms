# Freight Shipment Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freight Shipment Check Out Form is a business tool designed to gather crucial information from the shipper for efficient and secure checkout processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your name as the shipper in the "Shipper Name" field.
* Provide your 10-digit contact number for communication and verification in the "Contact Number" field (if required).
* Select the payment gateway used for the shipment payment in the "Payment Gateway" field (required).
* Enter the weight of the shipment in the "Freight Weight" field.
* Select your preferred payment method for the shipment in the "Payment Method" field (optional).
* Add any relevant notes or comments about the shipment in the "Notes" field (optional).
* Provide the date and time the shipment was sent in the "Date Shipped" and "Time Shipped" fields (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipper Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name as the shipper.
* **Contact Number** (2, text, optional): Enter a 10-digit phone number for communication and verification.
* **Payment Gateway** (3, select_one, required): Select the payment gateway used for the shipment payment.
* **Freight Weight** (4, number, optional): Enter the weight of the shipment.
* **Payment Method** (5, select_one, optional): Select your preferred payment method for the shipment.
* **Notes** (6, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about the shipment.
* **Date Shipped** (7, date, optional): Provide the date the shipment was sent.
* **Time Shipped** (8, time, optional): Provide the time the shipment was sent.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed to avoid hindering the checkout process or causing inaccuracies in the shipment's tracking.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the logistics operator for assistance.
