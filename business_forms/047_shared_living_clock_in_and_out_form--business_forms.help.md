# Shared Living Clock In And Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the clocking in and out of employees or personnel within a shared living arrangement, including time clocked in, time clocked out, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are accessing a valid version of the form.
2. Fill in the driver name in the **Driver Name** field.
3. Enter the clock in and clock out dates and times in the **Clock In**, **Clock Out**, **Clock In Date**, and **Clock In Time** fields. These fields should be filled out for each shift.
4. Enter any additional start and end times and notes for each shift, as necessary.
5. Select the assigned individual or team for each shift from the **Assigned To** dropdown menu.
6. Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the individual clocking in and out.
* **Clock In** (`clock_in`, date, required): Enter the date of the shift start.
* **Clock In Time** (`clock_in_time`, text, required): Enter the start time of the shift.
* **Clock Out** (`clock_out`, time, required): Enter the date and time of the shift end.
* **Clock Out Time** (`clock_out_time`, text, required): Enter the end time of the shift.
* **Clock In Date** (`clock_in_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the shift start, if different from the main clock in date.
* **Clock In Time 2** (`clock_in_time2`, text, optional): Enter the start time of the second shift, if applicable.
* **Clock Out Time 2** (`clock_out_time2`, text, optional): Enter the end time of the second shift, if applicable.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the shift.
* **Notes 2** (`notes2`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the second shift.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Select the individual or team assigned to work the shift.
* **Start Time 2** (`start_time2`, text, optional): Enter the start time of the second shift, if applicable.
* **End Time 2** (`end_time2`, text, optional): Enter the end time of the second shift, if applicable.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, text, optional): Enter the start time of the shift, if different from the main clock in time.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, text, optional): Enter the end time of the shift, if different from the main clock out time.
* **Clock In Time 3** (`clock_in_time3`, text, optional): Enter the start time of the third shift, if applicable.
* **Clock Out Time 3** (`clock_out_time3`, text, optional): Enter the end time of the third shift, if applicable.
* **Notes 3** (`notes3`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the third shift.
* **Assigned To 3** (`assigned_to3`, select_one, optional): Select the individual or team assigned to work the third shift.
* **Clock In 2** (`clock_in2`, date, optional): Enter the date of the second shift, if different from the main clock in date.
* **Clock In 4** (`clock_in4`, date, optional): Enter the date of the fourth shift, if applicable.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling out the form to ensure correct records are kept.
* Ensure that you are filling out the correct version of the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to the form's instructions or consult with your supervisor.
