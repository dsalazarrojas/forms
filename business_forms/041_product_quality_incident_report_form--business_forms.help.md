# Product Quality Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report and document product quality incidents, allowing organizations to identify and address issues affecting their products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as accurately and completely as possible.
2. Be honest and clear in your description of the incident.
3. Select the correct defect type and priority level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Incident Date (1)**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
- **Incident Time (2)**: Enter the time the incident occurred (if known).
- **Product Name (3)**: Enter the name of the product affected by the incident.
- **Description (4)**: Provide a detailed description of the incident.
- **Defect Type (5)**: Select the type of defect that occurred (Defect, Flaw, or Issue).
- **Defect Description (6)**: Enter a detailed description of the defect.
- **Priority (7)**: Choose the priority level of the incident (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Follow-up Action (8)**: Enter any follow-up actions taken or planned to address the incident.
- **Follow-up Date (9)**: Enter the date of the follow-up action (if applicable).

## Tips
- If you are experiencing multiple incidents related to the same product, please submit separate forms.
- Please ensure that you are submitting the form only once per incident.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the form creators for guidance.
