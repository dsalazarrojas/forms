# Hiring Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hiring Inquiry Form is used by HR personnel to collect contact information and qualifications from job seekers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the field labeled "first name".
2. Enter your email address in the field labeled "email".
3. Fill in your last name in the field labeled "last name".
4. Type in your job title in the field labeled "job title".
5. Provide your phone number in the field labeled "phone".
6. You may upload your resume or paste its URL in the field labeled "resume" (optional).
7. Optionally, provide additional notes in the field labeled "note" (text area).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **first name** (text, required): Type your first name in this field.
- **email** (email, required): Enter your email address here, which will be used to contact you.
- **last name** (text, required): Type your last name in this field.
- **job title** (text, required): Briefly describe your job title or position.
- **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number in this field.
- **resume** (text, optional): You may upload your resume or paste the URL of your online resume.
- **note** (note, optional): Provide any additional information you'd like to share with us.
