# cybersecurity-threat-mitigation-assessment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cybersecurity Threat Mitigation Assessment Form is designed to help you identify and assess potential security threats to your business. This form collects information on company details, threat descriptions, mitigation strategies, and security controls to help you understand and address potential risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form header with your company information.
2. Provide your company's name in the "company-name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the level of risk your company is currently facing ("Low", "Medium", or "High").
6. Describe the threat you are concerned about in the "Threat Description" field.
7. Outline your mitigation strategy in the "Mitigation Strategy" field.
8. List the security measures you have implemented or plan to implement in the "Security Measures" field.
9. Select the security controls you have in place from the list ("Firewalls", "Vulnerability Scanning", or "Incident Response").
10. Enter a numerical value for the vulnerability score (if applicable).
11. Enter the date of the assessment in the "Assessment Date" field.
12. Outline the next steps you plan to take in the "Next Step" field.
13. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
14. Enter the date of the review in the "Review Date" field.
15. Select who reviewed this form ("Manager", "Auditor", or "Security Officer").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: This is a note field for you to enter any additional information about your form submission.
* **Company Name (company-name)**: Enter your company's name.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Risk Level (risk_level)**: Select the level of risk your company is currently facing. Choose from "Low", "Medium", or "High".
* **Threat Description (threat_description)**: Describe the threat you are concerned about.
* **Mitigation Strategy (mitigation_strategy)**: Outline your mitigation strategy.
* **Security Measures (security_measures)**: List the security measures you have implemented or plan to implement.
* **Security Controls (security_controls)**: Select the security controls you have in place. Choose from "Firewalls", "Vulnerability Scanning", or "Incident Response".
* **Vulnerability Score (vulnerability_score)**: Enter a numerical value for the vulnerability score (if applicable).
* **Assessment Date (assessment_date)**: Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Next Step (next_step)**: Outline the next steps you plan to take.
* **Comments (comments)**: Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Review Date (review_date)**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Reviewed By (reviewed_by)**: Select who reviewed this form. Choose from "Manager", "Auditor", or "Security Officer".
