# Estimation & Project Management Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information related to project estimation and project management. Please fill out this form to ensure comprehensive data for your project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section as instructed, ensuring you answer all required fields marked as **Required**.
2. Select the options for each field according to your project's needs.
3. Provide accurate and clear information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Project Scope** (1)
* A brief description of what your project aims to achieve.

### **Project Requirements** (2)
* A list of essential features and functionalities of your project.

### **Team and Organization** (3)
* Check 'Yes' if your project has a dedicated team or organization, or 'No' otherwise.

### **Risk Level** (4)
* Select all applicable risk levels:
	+ High: The project has significant risks that could impact its success.
	+ Low: The project has minimal risks or risks are well-managed.

### **Estimation Method** (5)
* Select all applicable estimation methods:
	+ 'Yes': Your project uses one or more estimation methods (e.g., top-down, bottom-up, or expert judgment).
	+ 'No': Your project does not use estimation methods.

### **Project Schedule** (6)
* The start date and end date of your project. Use the date format: `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### **Project Budget** (7)
* Enter the total budget for your project, including all expenses and costs. Use the format: `123.45`.

### **Project Resources** (8)
* A detailed list of resources and personnel involved in the project, including their roles and contact information (if necessary).

### **Communication Plan** (9)
* A brief description of how communication among team members and stakeholders will be managed, including the frequency and method of updates.

### **Project Status** (10)
* Select the current status of your project:
	+ Active: The project is ongoing and has not been completed.
	+ Inactive: The project has been completed or cancelled.

## Tips
- Ensure you answer each question according to your project's specific needs and details.
- Double-check the required fields marked with **Required**.
- If you are unsure about any question, refer to your project manager or relevant documentation.
- This form will help project managers and team members understand project scope, requirements, risks, and other essential aspects of your project.
