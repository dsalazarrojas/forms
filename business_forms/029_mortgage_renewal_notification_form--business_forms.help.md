# mortgage_renewal_notification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **mortgage_renewal_notification_form** is a form used to collect information from homeowners about their mortgage renewal intentions, loan details, and contact preferences. This form helps us understand their needs and preferences for mortgage renewal, allowing us to provide personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide homeowner details, including their name, address, and contact information.
2.  Enter loan details such as loan amount, loan term, and interest rate.
3.  Specify the type of loan, whether fixed, adjustable, or variable.
4.  Indicate the contact method for follow-up communications (phone, email, or text).
5.  Enter the next follow-up date for the mortgage renewal.
6.  Select the assigned user responsible for handling the mortgage renewal.
7.  Provide any additional comments or notes about the mortgage renewal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Homeowner Details (1)**: This field is used to gather information about the homeowner, including their name and contact information.
*   **Loan Details (2)**: Enter the loan amount, loan term, and interest rate to provide context for the mortgage renewal.
*   **Contact Info (3)**: Specify the contact method (phone, email, or text) and enter the homeowner's contact details.
*   **Renewal Intentions (4)**: Describe the reasons for renewing the mortgage.
*   **Follow-up Details (5)**: Enter any additional details about the follow-up process.
*   **Upcoming Renewals (6)**: Enter the date for the next mortgage renewal.
*   **Assigned User (8)**: Select the user responsible for handling the mortgage renewal.
*   **Mortgage Status (9)**: Choose the status of the mortgage (active, inactive, or pending).
*   **Renewal Date (10)**: Enter the date for the mortgage renewal.
*   **Loan Term (11)**: Enter the loan term.
*   **Property Type (12)**: Select the type of property (single-family home, condo, or townhouse).
*   **Loan Amount (13)**: Enter the loan amount.
*   **Interest Rate (14)**: Enter the interest rate.
*   **Loan Type (16)**: Select the type of loan (fixed, adjustable, or variable).
*   **Contact Method (17)**: Specify the method for contact (phone, email, or text).
*   **Contact Details (18)**: Enter additional contact details.
*   **Next Follow-up Date (19)**: Enter the date for the next follow-up.
*   **Follow-up Method (20)**: Select the method for follow-up (phone, email, or text).
*   **Follow-up Details (21)**: Enter additional follow-up details.
*   **Follow-up Status (22)**: Choose the status of the follow-up (active, inactive, or pending).
*   **Assigned Tool User (23)**: Select the user responsible for using the tool.
*   **Follow-up Notes (24)**: Enter any follow-up notes.
*   **Notes (25)**: Enter any additional notes about the mortgage renewal.

The **mortgage_renewal_notification_form** is designed to help us understand the homeowners' needs and preferences for mortgage renewal. By completing this form, you will be able to provide the necessary information to our team, enabling us to assist with the mortgage renewal process efficiently and effectively.
