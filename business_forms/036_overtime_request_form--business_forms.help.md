# Overtime Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Overtime Request Form is used to request overtime for an employee when they have worked beyond their regular working hours. This form is intended to facilitate communication between the employee and their manager for approval and tracking of overtime requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure that you only submit actual overtime requests, not other types of requests such as vacation or travel requests.
2.  Fill in your employee information, including your name and any additional information as needed.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the overtime request, including the date and duration of the overtime.
4.  Select your manager's name from the options for manager approval. Ensure that you have checked with your manager first for approval before submitting the form.
5.  Enter your manager's email and phone number to facilitate communication.
6.  Leave space for any additional information that you need to share with your manager, or for your manager to leave notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Information (`employee_info`):**
    This field is for you to provide your personal information, including your name and any other details you want to share with your manager.
*   **Request Details (`request_details`):**
    Provide a detailed description of the overtime request, including the date and duration of the overtime.
*   **Manager Approval (`manager_approval`):**
    Select 'Yes' if your manager has approved the overtime request, otherwise select 'No'. Please ensure that you have already checked with your manager for approval before submitting the form.
*   **Manager Email (`manager_email`):**
    Enter your manager's email address, so they can communicate with you about the overtime request.
*   **Manager Phone (`manager_phone`):**
    Enter your manager's phone number, so they can reach out to you about the overtime request.
*   **Employee Notes (`employee_notes`):**
    Add any additional comments or information that you need to share with your manager about the overtime request.
*   **Manager Notes (`manager_notes`):**
    Your manager will use this space to leave notes or comments about the overtime request.

## Tips
*   Before submitting the form, check with your manager to ensure they have not already approved or denied the request.
*   Ensure that your manager's email and phone number are up-to-date in the system to facilitate communication.
*   This form is for actual overtime requests only, not for other types of requests such as vacation or travel requests.
