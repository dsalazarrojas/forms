# Sales Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from sales representatives on their performance, focusing on various aspects such as sales performance, communication skills, teamwork, product knowledge, initiative-taking, and sales tactics. The evaluations aim to provide valuable insights for professional development and future improvement opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field (page 1).
2. Choose the evaluation date in the "Evaluation Date" field (page 2).
3. Evaluate the employee's sales performance by rating it from 1 to 100 (page 3).
4. Provide a detailed assessment of the sales performance, focusing on "Sales Performance (hands on)" (page 4).
5. Rate the employee's communication skills, choosing from "Strong", "Fair", or "Poor" (page 5).
6. Select how well the employee works with the team, choosing from "Works well with team", "Does not work well with team", or "Neutral" (page 6).
7. Assess the employee's product knowledge, selecting from "Excellent", "Fair", or "Poor" (page 7).
8. Evaluate the employee's initiative-taking, choosing from "Always", "Sometimes", or "Rarely" (page 8).
9. Rate the effectiveness of the employee's sales tactics, selecting from "Effective", "Neutral", or "Poor" (page 9).
10. Provide any additional comments regarding the employee's sales tactics (page 10).
11. Rate the overall performance of the sales representative (page 11).
12. Finally, add any comments on the sales tactics used by the representative (page 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the sales representative being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Choose the date of the evaluation.
* **Sales Performance** (`sales_performance`, number, required): Rate the sales performance of the representative, from 1 to 100.
* **Sales Performance (hands on)** (`sales_performance_hands_on`, text, required): Provide a detailed assessment of the sales performance.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_one, required): Rate the employee's communication skills, choosing from "Strong", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Teamwork** (`teamwork`, select_multiple, required): Select how well the employee works with the team, choosing from "Works well with team", "Does not work well with team", or "Neutral".
* **Product Knowledge** (`productknowledge`, select_multiple, required): Assess the employee's product knowledge, selecting from "Excellent", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Initiative Taking** (`initiative_taking`, select_one, required): Evaluate the employee's initiative-taking, choosing from "Always", "Sometimes", or "Rarely".
* **Sales Tactics** (`sales_tactics`, select_multiple, required): Rate the effectiveness of the employee's sales tactics, selecting from "Effective", "Neutral", or "Poor" (Note: This field is optional).
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments regarding the employee's performance.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate the overall performance of the sales representative.
* **Sales Tactics Comments** (`sales_tactics_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments on the sales tactics used by the representative.

**Tips**

* Ensure that you are evaluating the sales representative fairly and honestly, based on their actual performance.
* Be specific and provide concrete examples when providing feedback.
* Use the rating scale consistently to maintain fairness and accuracy.
* Keep the comments and evaluations professional and respectful to maintain a positive work environment.
