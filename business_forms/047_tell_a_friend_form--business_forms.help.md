# tell_a_friend_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `tell_a_friend_form` is designed to facilitate communication between you and your colleagues or friends about a specific product or topic. This form allows you to select a product, send a message, and suggest it to a friend.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On **page 1**, select "Suggest Product" to indicate whether you want to suggest a product to your friend. You can choose from two options: "Yes" or "No".
2. Enter your friend's email address in the **Friend Email** field.
3. Type a personal message to your friend in the **Your Message** field. This field is a free-form text area where you can enter any text.
4. On **page 2**, select "Suggest Friend" to indicate whether you want to suggest a friend. Again, you can choose from two options: "Yes" or "No".
5. Enter the product URL where your friend can access the product information if you chose "Yes" for "Suggest Product" on the previous page.
6. Finally, enter your friend's name in the **Friend Name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Suggest Product**: Select "Yes" if you want to suggest a product to your friend, or "No" if you do not want to suggest a product.
* **Friend Email**: Enter your friend's email address where you will send the message. This is a required field.
* **Your Message**: Type a personal message to your friend about the product. You can include any information or details you want to share.
* **Suggest Friend**: Select "Yes" if you want to suggest a friend, or "No" if you do not want to suggest a friend.
* **Product Url**: If you chose to suggest a product, enter the URL where your friend can find the product information.
* **Friend Name**: Enter your friend's name. This field is optional but helps you keep a personal touch in your message.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter a clear and concise message in the **Your Message** field to effectively communicate with your friend.
* If you are suggesting a product, ensure that the product URL is correct to direct your friend to the right location.
* You can customize the message to fit your personal style and tone to better connect with your friend.
