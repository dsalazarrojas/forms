# Budget Reporting Authorization Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Budget Reporting Authorization Consent Form is used to obtain authorization from employees to access budget reporting information. This form is essential in ensuring that employees are aware of their rights and responsibilities related to data sensitivity, confidentiality, and authorized use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Employee Full Name**, **Employee ID**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**.
2. Choose the **Type of Budget Reports to Access**, which determines what type of budget information you will be able to view.
3. Acknowledge the **Data Sensitivity Level** of the data you will be accessing.
4. Confirm that you have a **Confidentiality Agreement Understood**, indicating that you will keep the data confidential.
5. Agree to use the data for **Authorized Use Only**, for job-related purposes.
6. Select **No Sharing or Distribution Clause**, to ensure you do not share the data with unauthorized parties.
7. Confirm that your **Manager has Authorized this Access**.
8. Confirm that you have completed **Budget Reporting Training**, if applicable.
9. Agree to **Security Best Practices**, such as password protection and logout.
10. Acknowledge that your **Access Will Be Audited**.
11. Sign and date the form by selecting a date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or personnel document.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee identification number or unique identifier.
* **Email Address**: Enter your official email address on record with the company.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your official phone number on record with the company.
* **Type of Budget Reports to Access**: Choose the type of budget reports you need to access:
	+ View Own Department Budget: Allows you to view budget reports related to your department.
	+ View Multiple Departments: Allows you to view budget reports from multiple departments.
	+ View All Company Budgets: Allows you to view all company-wide budget reports.
	+ Management and Approvals: Allows you to view budget reports for management and approval purposes.
	+ Executive Dashboard: Allows you to view executive-level budget reports.
* **Data Sensitivity Level**: Acknowledge the sensitivity level of the data you will be accessing:
	+ Public: The data is publicly available.
	+ Internal Use Only: The data is for internal use only.
	+ Confidential: The data is confidential and should not be shared with external parties.
	+ Highly Confidential: The data is highly confidential and should not be shared with anyone.
* **Confidentiality Agreement Understood**: Confirm that you understand and agree to keep the data confidential.
* **Authorized Use Only**: Agree that you will use the data for job-related purposes only.
* **No Sharing or Distribution Clause**: Confirm that you will not share the data with unauthorized parties.
* **Manager Approval for Access**: Confirm that your manager has authorized this access.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name as it appears on their ID or personnel document.
* **Budget Reporting Training Completed**: Confirm that you have completed budget reporting training, if applicable.
* **Agree to Security Best Practices**: Agree to follow security best practices, such as password protection and logout.
* **Acknowledge Access Will Be Audited**: Confirm that you understand access will be audited.
* **Consent Date**: Sign and date the form by selecting a date.

Note: Completing this form is a one-time requirement and does not guarantee continuous access to budget reports. Management reserves the right to revoke access at any time.
