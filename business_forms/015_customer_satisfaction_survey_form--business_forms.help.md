# Customer Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to measure customer satisfaction with the company's services or products.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly to provide a clear picture of your experience.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as such.
3. Select the relevant options for each question.
4. Provide additional comments or suggestions where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Survey**: This field is a text box where you can describe your experience with the company's services or products.
* **Rating Scale**: Choose a rating from the scale below:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **What are you thinking?**: Provide additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the company.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up.
* **Satisfaction Scale**: Choose how you feel about the quality of service:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Time**: Enter the time you spent interacting with the company.
* **What can we improve?**: Provide suggestions on how the company can improve its services or products.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Choose all that apply for how satisfied you are with the overall experience:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your answers.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please provide them in the "What can we improve?" or "Additional Comments" fields.
* If you would like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up, enter your email address in the "Email" field.
