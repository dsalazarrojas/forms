# corporate_governance_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on corporate governance practices, compliance standards, data collection, management processes, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Start by filling out the required fields (indicated by a *) on each page.
3. For fields with multiple options, select the relevant option that best represents your response.
4. Use the available space provided for fields marked as "note" to provide additional information or explanations.
5. Enter your email address and phone number in the respective fields.
6. Enter the date and time in the "date" and "time" fields respectively.
7. Finally, review your entries carefully and ensure they are accurate before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Governance Practices** (`corporate_governance_checklist_form_page`, text, true): Briefly describe the current governance practices in your organization.
* **Compliance Standards** (`corporate_governance_checklist_form_page_2`, multiple select, true): Check the boxes that apply to your organization's compliance standards.
* **Data Collection** (`corporate_governance_checklist_form_page_3`, number, true): Enter the number of data points collected for compliance purposes.
* **Management Processes** (`corporate_governance_checklist_form_page_4`, note, true): Describe the management processes in place for data collection and governance.
* **Email** (`corporate_governance_checklist_form_page_5`, email, true): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`corporate_governance_checklist_form_page_6`, text, true): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date** (`corporate_governance_checklist_form_page_7`, date, true): Enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time** (`corporate_governance_checklist_form_page_8`, time, true): Enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Comments** (`corporate_governance_checklist_form_page_9`, note, false): Provide any additional comments or explanations if required.
* **Corporate Governance Checkpoint** (`corporate_governance_checklist_form_page_21`, text, true): Enter the checkpoint description.
* **Corporate Governance Risk Assessment** (`corporate_governance_checklist_form_page_22`, text, true): Enter the risk assessment details.
* **Corporate Governance Score** (`corporate_governance_checklist_form_page_23`, text, true): Enter the score details.
* **Corporate Governance Scorecard** (`corporate_governance_checklist_form_page_24`, text, true): Enter the scorecard details.
* **Corporate Governance Review Date** (`corporate_governance_checklist_form_page_25`, date, true): Enter the review date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
