# social_media_audit_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The social media audit form is designed to collect vital information about social media performance and demographics. This data will aid in decision-making and help identify potential improvements for social media presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the social media audit form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you're filling out the form for a unique social media presence, as duplication of data will lead to inaccurate insights.
2. Fill out the form with the required information for each field, marked as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### page_1 (Text, Optional)
* **page_1_label** (`page_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the social media presence being audited, e.g., Facebook, Twitter, or Instagram.

### engagement_metrics (Select Multiple, Optional)
* **engagement_metrics_label** (`engagement_metrics_label`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or more metrics to analyze, such as "likes", "shares", or "comments" to gauge social media engagement.

### audience_demographics (Select One, Optional)
* **audience_demographics_label** (`audience_demographics_label`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the demographic information of the social media presence, either "male", "female", or "unknown" to understand the audience.

### email_address (Email, Optional)
* **email_address_label** (`email_address_label`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address associated with the social media presence, if applicable.

### phone_number (Text, Optional)
* **phone_number_label** (`phone_number_label`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number associated with the social media presence, if applicable.

### date_of_birth (Date, Optional)
* **date_of_birth_label** (`date_of_birth_label`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of birth of the social media presence, if applicable.

### time_zone (Time, Optional)
* **time_zone_label** (`time_zone_label`, `time`, required/optional): Choose the time zone associated with the social media presence, if applicable.

### notes (Note, Optional)
* **notes_label** (`notes_label`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional information or notes about the social media presence being audited.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields for accurate insights.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and consistency.
* Use the form for every unique social media presence to avoid duplication of data.
