# virtual_office_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect subscription information for a virtual office setting, including basic contact details, email addresses, phone numbers, and other relevant data to facilitate communication and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Company Name** in the "Company Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose a date for your virtual office subscription.
6. Choose a time for your virtual office subscription.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Select One" field.
8. Select one or multiple options for the "Select Multiple" field.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
10. Enter an alternate email address in the "Email 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name to be associated with your subscription.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name for organizational purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive important updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date** (`date_field`, date, optional): Choose a date for your virtual office subscription.
* **Time** (`time_field`, time, optional): Choose a time for your virtual office subscription.
* **Select One** (`select_one_field`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your subscription status.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple_field`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple options for your subscription.
* **Note** (`note_field`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your subscription.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, email, optional): Enter an alternate email address for your subscription.
