# Stallholder-operations-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and manage operations performed on stallholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access and complete this form.
2. Fill in the required fields (operation type, operation date, and operation time) as accurately as possible.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments in the notes field.
4. Enter the stallholder's email address in the email field, if applicable.
5. Enter the stallholder's phone number in the phone field, if applicable.
6. Select the tool assigned to the stallholder from the dropdown list.
7. Review your completed form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name (`event_name`, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the event or operation being performed on the stallholder.
* **Operation Type (`operation_type`, select_one, required)**: Select the type of operation performed (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Operation Date (`operation_date`, date, optional)**: Enter the date of the operation.
* **Operation Time (`operation_time`, time, optional)**: Enter the time of the operation.
* **Notes (`notes`, note, optional)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the operation.
* **Email (`email`, email, optional)**: Enter the stallholder's email address, if applicable.
* **Phone (`phone`, text, optional)**: Enter the stallholder's phone number, if applicable.
* **Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional)**: Select the tool assigned to the stallholder from the dropdown list.
