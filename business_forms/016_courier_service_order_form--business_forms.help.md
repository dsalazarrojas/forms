## Purpose
The Courier Service Order Form is a digital form designed to collect information from customers for a courier service. This form helps the service provider to efficiently gather necessary details for the order processing and delivery. It is crucial to ensure that the customer's requirements are accurately reflected in the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the necessary information about the customer, including their name, address, and contact details in the **Customer Details** section.
2. Enter the delivery details in the **Delivery Details** section, specifying the location and any special requirements.
3. Choose the time of day for the delivery in the **Delivery Time** section.
4. Select the date of the delivery in the **Delivery Date** section.
5. If the delivery date has a specific time component, enter it in the **Delivery Date 2** section.
6. Add any additional delivery instructions in the **Delivery Instructions** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the customer's name, address, and contact information for efficient communication.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, `text`, required: false): Specify the delivery location and any special requirements for a smooth delivery process.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `number`, required: false): Choose the time of day for the delivery, e.g., 9:00 AM or 3:00 PM.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date for the delivery.
* **Delivery Date 2** (`delivery_date_2`, `time`, required: false): If the delivery has a specific time component, select it here, e.g., 12:00 PM or 8:00 PM.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional instructions for the delivery, such as parking instructions or access restrictions.
