# Annual Trust Meeting Minutes Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Annual Trust Meeting Minutes Form" is designed to document the events of an annual trust meeting, recording key details such as meeting date, time, and location, as well as key actions and decisions taken.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field to ensure you provide accurate information for your organization's meeting minutes.
2. Start by entering the meeting date in the "Meeting Date" field, followed by the meeting time in the "Meeting Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date (1)** (`meeting_date`, `date`, required): The date of the meeting, including day, month, and year.
* **Meeting Time (2)** (`meeting_time`, `time`, required): The start time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location (3)** (`meeting_location`, `text`, optional): The physical location or virtual details of the meeting.
* **Trust or Organization Name (4)** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): The official name of the trust or organization.
* **Minutes Recorder Name (5)** (`minutes_recorder`, `text`, required): The name of the person recording the meeting minutes.
* **Recorder Title (6)** (`recorder_title`, `text`, optional): The title of the recorder.
* **Recorder Email (7)** (`recorder_email`, `email`, required): The contact email of the recorder.
* **Meeting Chairperson (8)** (`chairperson`, `text`, required): The person who presided over the meeting.
* **Attendees Present (9)** (`attendees`, `text`, required): The names of all attendees.
* **Authorized Absences (10)** (`absences`, `text`, optional): The names of those excused from the meeting.
* **Quorum Status (11)** (`quorum_status`, `select_one`, required): Select "Quorum present" if quorum was achieved, otherwise select "Quorum not present".
* **Time Called to Order (12)** (`meeting_called_to_order`, `time`, optional): The actual start time of the meeting.
* **Prior Meeting Minutes Approval (13)** (`prior_minutes_approval`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the previous meeting minutes.
* **Agenda Items Discussed (14)** (`agenda_items`, `text`, required): List the topics covered during the meeting.
* **Key Decisions Made (15)** (`key_decisions`, `text`, required): Record any key resolutions or votes taken.
* **Discussion Highlights (16)** (`discussion_highlights`, `text`, optional): A summary of major discussions.
* **Financial Report Presented (17)** (`financial_report`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of any financial report presented.
* **Financial Status (18)** (`financial_status`, `text`, optional): A summary of the financial status.
* **Action Items Assigned (19)** (`action_items`, `text`, optional): Tasks assigned to specific individuals.
* **Action Item Deadlines (20)** (`action_deadlines`, `text`, optional): Due dates for assigned tasks.
* **Next Meeting Date (21)** (`next_meeting_date`, `date`, required): The date of the next scheduled meeting.
* **Meeting Adjourned Time (22)** (`meeting_adjourned`, `time`, optional): The time the meeting ended.
* **Approval Process for Minutes (23)** (`minutes_approval_note`, `select_one`, optional): How the minutes will be approved.
* **Additional Notes or Attachments (24)** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Any other relevant information.

Note: The "required" fields are marked with `**`, which indicates that they are required to be filled out for the form to be completed. "Optional" fields are marked with `*` but are not strictly necessary for the meeting minutes to be recorded. However, providing accurate information in these fields can enhance the completeness and accuracy of the meeting minutes.
