# cost_audit_support_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cost audit support request form is used to request support for the cost audit process. It helps gather necessary information from users who need assistance with cost classification or tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Select the type of request you are submitting (cost audit or support request).
2. Enter your organization's name.
3. Choose the relevant cost category (category a, b, or c).
4. Provide an estimate of the cost involved.
5. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you.
6. Optionally, add any additional notes.
7. Click the "submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **request_type** (select one): This field allows you to select the type of request you are submitting. Choose "cost audit" if you need help with cost classification, or choose "support request" if you need assistance with any other cost-related issue.
* **organization_name** (text): Enter your organization's name here. This will help us understand which department or team the request is related to.
* **cost_category** (select multiple): Choose the relevant cost category (category a, b, or c) that aligns with your request.
* **cost_estimate** (number): Provide an estimate of the cost involved in your request.
* **notes** (note): Add any additional notes or comments about your request.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further questions or updates.
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further questions or updates.
* **submit** (time): Click this button to complete and submit your request.
* **submitted_by** (text): Enter your name as it will appear on the submitted request.
* **submitted_at** (date): This field will automatically be populated with the date and time the request was submitted.
* **submitted_via** (text): This field will automatically be populated with the method you used to submit the request.
* **submitted_by_user** (text): Enter your name as it will appear on the submitted request.
* **submitted_by_title** (text): Enter your job title or position as it will appear on the submitted request.
* **submitted_by_department** (text): Enter your department or team as it will appear on the submitted request.
* **submitted_by_phone** (text): Enter your phone number as it will appear on the submitted request.
* **submitted_by_email** (email): Enter your email address as it will appear on the submitted request.
* **submitted_by_notes** (note): Add any additional notes or comments that will appear on the submitted request.

* **submitted_by_email_1** (email): This field is a duplicate of the "email" field and should be avoided.
