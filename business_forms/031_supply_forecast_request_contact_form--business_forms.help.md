# Supply Forecast Request Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a supply forecast request in a business setting. It gathers details about the user's contact information and forecast information to facilitate communication and planning.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), as these are necessary for the form to be submitted successfully.
2.  Enter your email address in the "email" field. This will be the contact information for your forecast request.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4.  Select a date for your forecast request in the "date" field.
5.  Enter the time for your forecast request in the "Time" field.
6.  Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
7.  Enter the name of the contact person who will handle your forecast request in the "Contact Name" field.
8.  Enter the title of the contact person who will handle your forecast request in the "Contact Title" field.
9.  Enter the department of the contact person who will handle your forecast request in the "Contact Department" field.
10. Enter the email address of the contact person who will handle your forecast request in the "Contact Email" field.
11. Enter the phone number of the contact person who will handle your forecast request in the "Contact Phone" field.
12. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Notes" field.
13. Click the "Submit" button to complete and send your forecast request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **email** (required): Enter your email address to be used as the contact information for your forecast request.
*   **phone** (required): Enter your phone number so that others can contact you regarding your forecast request.
*   **date** (required): Select a date for your forecast request.
*   **Time** (required): Select a time for your forecast request.
*   **Notes** (optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your forecast request.
*   **contact\_name** (optional): Enter the name of the contact person who will handle your forecast request.
*   **contact\_title** (optional): Enter the title of the contact person who will handle your forecast request.
*   **contact\_department** (optional): Enter the department of the contact person who will handle your forecast request.
*   **contact\_email** (required): Enter the email address of the contact person who will handle your forecast request.
*   **contact\_phone** (required): Enter the phone number of the contact person who will handle your forecast request.
*   **Additional Notes** (optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your forecast request.
*   **Submit**: Click this button to complete and send your forecast request.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by a user in a business setting to request a supply forecast. The fields are labeled to provide clear instructions on what information to enter in each field.
