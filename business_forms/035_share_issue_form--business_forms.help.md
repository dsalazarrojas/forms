# Share Issue Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Share Issue Form is designed to collect and record details about a share issue event, providing an easy-to-use interface for users to input the required information. This form is essential for maintaining a clear and accurate record of share issuances, helping to ensure that all relevant details are captured and accessible at all times.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form to understand the required and optional fields.
2. Fill out the required fields with the relevant information.
3. Enter any relevant additional information in the optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Issue Details**: <label> (`share_issue_form_1`, `text`, required/optional: false) - Enter a brief description of the issue, such as the name of the share issue.
* **Company Information**: <label> (`share_issue_form_2`, `text`, required/optional: false) - Enter any relevant company details, such as the company name or address.
* **Share Allocation**: <label> (`share_issue_form_3`, `text`, required/optional: false) - Provide any allocation information, if applicable.
* **Share Price**: <label> (`share_issue_form_4`, `number`, required/optional: false) - Enter the share price.
* **Number of Shares**: <label> (`share_issue_form_5`, `number`, required/optional: false) - Enter the number of shares.
* **Issued Shares**: <label> (`share_issue_form_6`, `number`, required/optional: false) - Enter the total number of shares issued.
* **Remaining Shares**: <label> (`share_issue_form_7`, `number`, required/optional: false) - Enter the remaining number of shares.
* **Allocation Date**: <label> (`share_issue_form_8`, `date`, required/optional: false) - Enter the allocation date.
* **Shareholder Contact**: <label> (`share_issue_form_9`, `text`, required/optional: false) - Enter any contact information for the shareholder, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the entered information for any errors or inaccuracies.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
