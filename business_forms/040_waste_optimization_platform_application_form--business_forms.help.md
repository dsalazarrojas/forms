# Waste Optimization Platform Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waste Optimization Platform Application Form is a tool designed to collect information from businesses interested in optimizing their waste management processes. The form helps the platform understand the business's needs and provide tailored solutions to improve waste management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organization's details in the `Organization Name` field.
2. Select your answer to `Organization Details` (yes or no).
3. Choose your `Waste Types` (yes or no).
4. Enter your contact information, including your email address in the `Contact Info` field and your telephone number in the `Contact Telephone` field.
5. Enter your goals and descriptions for waste optimization in the `Goals Description` field.
6. Choose your submission confirmation (yes or no) and submit the form.
7. Provide a valid date and time for the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name**: Enter your business name as it appears on official documents.
* **Organization Details**: Select "Yes" if your organization has waste optimization needs, "No" otherwise.
* **Waste Types**: Select "Yes" if your organization is interested in waste management services, "No" otherwise.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your work email address.
* **Contact Telephone**: Enter your work telephone number.
* **Goals Description**: Describe your organization's goals for waste optimization.
* **Goals Metric**: Enter your target metric or KPI for waste reduction.
* **Submit**: Confirm that you are submitting this form. Select "Yes" to complete the form, "No" to cancel.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter a valid date for the submission.
* **Time Submitted**: Enter a valid time for the submission.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
