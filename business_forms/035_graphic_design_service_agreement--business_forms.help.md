# Graphic Design Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive agreement for graphic design services. It outlines the scope of work, payment terms, and other essential details for the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read through each section carefully to understand the requirements and expectations for the project.
2.  Fill in the form according to the instructions and guidelines provided.
3.  Make sure to review and agree on the scope of work, payment terms, and other essential details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): A brief introduction to the project and the scope of work.
	+ This field is used to describe the project in a brief and concise manner.
* **Scope of Work** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe the project scope including services provided.
	+ This field is used to outline the services to be provided by the designer.
* **Payment Schedule** (`3`, `number`, required: `false`): Payment terms for the project.
	+ This field is used to specify the payment schedule for the project.
* **Payment Terms** (`4`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ This field is used to determine when payment is due. Options include:
		- Payment due on receipt of invoice
		- Payment due 14 days after project completion
		- Payment due on project completion
* **Deliverables** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): List the deliverables included in the project.
	+ This field is used to list the final products or services to be delivered to the client.
* **Copyright Protection** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe copyright protection for the work.
	+ This field is used to outline the copyright protection for the project.
* **Communication** (`7`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe how often you'd like to hear from each other.
	+ This field is used to establish communication expectations between the designer and client.
* **Payment Terms Notes** (`8`, `note`, required: `false`): Additional notes for payment terms.
	+ This field is used to provide additional context or notes for payment terms.
* **Agreement Signatures** (`9`, `number`, required: `false`): Number of signatures required.
	+ This field is used to specify the number of signatures required for the agreement.
* **Contract Review** (`10`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ This field is used to determine who reviews the contract. Options include:
		- Both parties
		- Designer only
		- Client only
		- Both client and designer
* **Contract Review Comments** (`11`, `text`, required: `false`): Additional comments for contract review.
	+ This field is used to provide additional context or comments for contract review.
* **Date Signed** (`12`, `date`, required: `false`): Date signed by the client.
	+ This field is used to record the date the agreement was signed.
* **Signed by Client** (`13`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ This field is used to record the client's signature. Options include:
		- John Doe
		- Jane Doe
