# Company Meeting Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and insights from attendees about company meetings. It helps management and HR assess the effectiveness of meetings, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Meeting Title** (`meeting_title`, text, required): Enter the name or purpose of the meeting.
2. **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required): Fill in the date of the meeting.
3. **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): If you're completing this form as a follow-up, enter your name here.
4. **Meeting Effectiveness** (`meeting_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate how effective the meeting was using the options provided:
	* Very effective
	* Effective
	* Neutral
	* Ineffective
5. **Key Takeaways** (`key_takeaways`, text, optional): Describe the main outcomes or key points discussed during the meeting.
6. **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Share ideas to make future meetings more productive and efficient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **Meeting Title**: The name or purpose of the meeting, providing context to the rest of the form.
• **Meeting Date**: The date the meeting took place, helping to track progress and schedule upcoming events.
• **Attendee Name**: Your name, if you're completing this form as a follow-up, for reference and identification.
• **Meeting Effectiveness**: Rate the meeting's effectiveness, helping management and HR assess its success.
• **Key Takeaways**: Share the main points or outcomes from the meeting, summarizing the discussion.
• **Improvement Suggestions**: Offer ideas to enhance future meetings, making them more productive and efficient.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- Provide clear and concise answers to help management and HR make data-driven decisions.
- If you've already completed a similar form, try to provide new insights rather than duplicating previous responses.
