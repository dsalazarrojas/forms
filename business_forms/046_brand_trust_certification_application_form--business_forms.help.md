# Brand Trust Certification Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from brands and companies applying for Brand Trust Certification. It gathers data on company policies and practices related to trust building, transparency, and social responsibility.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  Complete all required fields to increase your chances of a successful certification process.
3.  Select all applicable transparency measures in question 7.
4.  Enter your current customer data protection practices in question 9.
5.  Provide a brief summary of your Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives in question 11.
6.  Enter your annual investment in community/social projects in question 12 (if applicable).
7.  Indicate your internal audit status in question 14.
8.  Enter your contact details for the certification process in question 15.
9.  Select the date of application in question 16.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Legal Name** (`legal_company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name as per your legal records.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector_val`, text, required): Identify the industry your company operates in.
* **Corporate Email Address** (`corp_email`, email, required): Enter your company's email address.
* **Corporate Phone Number** (`corp_phone`, text, required): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Transparency Measures** (`transparency_measures_select`, select multiple, required): Select all transparency measures you currently implement, such as Public Financial Reporting, Open Supply Chain Data, Customer Feedback Loops, Ethical Sourcing Audits, and Clear Privacy Policies.
* **Primary Objectives for Seeking Brand Trust Certification** (`trust_objectives_desc`, text, required): Describe your primary objectives for seeking Brand Trust Certification.
* **Customer Data Protection Practices** (`data_protection_rating`, select one, required): Rate your current customer data protection practices as Industry Leading, Fully Compliant, Improving, or Initial Stages.
* **Brief Summary of CSR Initiatives** (`csr_summary_text`, text, required): Provide a concise summary of your CSR initiatives.
* **Annual Investment in Community/Social Projects** (`social_invest_amt`, number, optional): Enter your annual investment in community/social projects (if applicable).
* **Internal Audit Status** (`internal_audit_status`, select one, required): Indicate whether an internal trust-focused audit has been completed in the last 12 months.
* **Primary Point of Contact for Certification Process** (`cert_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the certification process.
* **Date of Application** (`app_dt`, date, required): Select the date of your application.
