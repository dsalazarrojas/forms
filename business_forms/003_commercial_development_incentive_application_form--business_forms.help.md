# Commercial Development Incentive Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary details from businesses seeking commercial development incentives. It aims to gather accurate and complete information to assess eligibility and suitability for these incentives.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a successful application, please follow these steps:

- Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
- Ensure that your business name and project details are correct.
- Enter a clear and concise description of your project and its objectives.
- Provide your contact information for correspondence and any relevant documents supporting your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business name as it is registered in the relevant authorities.
- **Project Address** (`project_address`, text, required): Provide the exact address of the proposed development project.
- **Applicant Contact Name** (`applicant_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for this application.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for application correspondence.
- **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): This field is optional but may be helpful for direct contact or follow-up communication.
- **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Briefly describe the project, including its objectives, and expected outcomes.
- **Estimated Total Cost** (`estimated_total_cost`, number, required): Enter the total estimated cost of the project, using whole numbers in the local currency.
- **Incentive Requested** (`incentive_requested`, text, required): Describe the type and amount of incentive you are seeking.
- **Number of Jobs Expected** (`number_of_jobs_created`, number, optional): Estimate the number of jobs you anticipate will be created as a result of this project, if applicable.
- **Anticipated Start Date** (`anticipated_start_date`, date, optional): Provide the expected start date of the project.
- **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, note, optional): List any supporting documents, such as plans, permits, or financing details, that can enhance your application.
- **Certification and Signature** (`certification_and_signature`, select_one, required): Indicate that you certify the accuracy of the provided information and agree to the terms of this application.
