# Rural Contracting Policy Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Rural Contracting Policy Agreement form is a standardized template used to document the terms and conditions of a rural contracting arrangement between a client and a contractor. This form ensures that all necessary information is collected to establish a clear understanding of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you are aware of all terms and conditions before signing the form.
2.  Enter your details in the designated fields.
3.  Review the scope of work and ensure you have a clear understanding of the work to be completed.
4.  Sign the form to acknowledge your acceptance of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (ID 1, Required): Enter the client's details, including their name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Scope of Work** (ID 2, Required): Describe the scope of work to be completed under the agreement.
* **Agreement Period** (ID 3, Required): Specify the period of the agreement.
* **Start Date** (ID 4, Required): Enter the start date of the agreement.
* **End Date** (ID 5, Required): Enter the end date of the agreement.
* **Client Signature** (ID 6, Optional): If applicable, include the client's signature.
* **Contractor Signature** (ID 7, Required): Enter the contractor's signature.
* **Scope of Work Details** (ID 8, Optional): Provide any additional details about the scope of work.
* **Consent** (ID 9, Required): Confirm your consent to the terms and conditions.
* **Contract Review** (ID 10, Required): Indicate if you have read and understand the contract.
* **Contract Version** (ID 11, Required): Specify the version of the contract.
* **Contract Notes** (ID 12, Optional): Include any additional notes about the contract.
* **Client Address** (ID 13, Optional): Enter the client's address.
* **Client Phone** (ID 14, Optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Contractor Address** (ID 15, Optional): Enter the contractor's address.
* **Contractor Phone** (ID 16, Optional): Enter the contractor's phone number.
* **Agreement Location** (ID 17, Optional): Specify the location of the agreement.
* **Contract Type** (ID 18, Optional): Specify the type of contract.
* **Contract Start Date** (ID 19, Optional): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (ID 20, Optional): Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Contract Notes 2** (ID 21, Optional): Include any additional notes about the contract.
* **Client Email** (ID 22, Optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Contractor Email** (ID 23, Optional): Enter the contractor's email address.
* **Client Name** (ID 24, Optional): Enter the client's name.
* **Contractor Name** (ID 25, Optional): Enter the contractor's name.

Please note that while this form includes several optional fields, it is still essential to complete the required fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful collaboration between the client and contractor.
