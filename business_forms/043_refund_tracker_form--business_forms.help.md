# refund_tracker_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The refund_tracker_form is used to track refund information for orders. It allows customer support staff to update the status of refunds, enter refund details, and keep track of customer communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the current status of the refund (Active or Inactive) in the "Order Status" field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Enter a unique tracking ID in the "Tracking ID" field (optional).
4. Enter the URL associated with the tracking ID in the "Tracking URL" field (optional).
5. Enter the estimated refund amount in the "Estimated Refund" field (optional).
6. Enter the actual refund amount in the "Actual Refund" field (optional).
7. Enter the refund amount in the "Refund Amount" field, which is required.
8. Choose the date of the refund in the "Refund Date" field, which is required.
9. Add any additional notes regarding the refund in the "Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Status (refund_status)**: Select the current status of the refund from the options (Active or Inactive).
* **Customer Email (customer_email)**: Enter the email address of the customer associated with the refund.
* **Tracking ID (tracking_id)**: Enter a unique ID associated with the tracking URL (optional).
* **Tracking URL (tracking_url)**: Enter the URL associated with the tracking ID (optional).
* **Estimated Refund (estimated_refund)**: Enter the estimated refund amount (optional).
* **Actual Refund (actual_refund)**: Enter the actual refund amount (optional).
* **Refund Amount (refund_amount)**: Enter the total amount of the refund, which is required.
* **Refund Date (refund_date)**: Choose the date of the refund, which is required.
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional notes regarding the refund (optional).

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct status of the refund.
* Enter accurate and complete information to ensure accurate tracking and communication.
* Use the "Tracking ID" and "Tracking URL" fields to keep track of external tracking information.
* Use the "Estimated Refund" and "Actual Refund" fields to track refund progress.
* Use the "Notes" field to record any important details regarding the refund.
