# Annual Business Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Business Planning Form helps businesses create a comprehensive plan for the upcoming year. This form gathers essential information about your business, including its name, contact details, goals, and strategic planning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your business details, including its official name, contact person's name, and email address.
2. Select the planning year for which you are creating this plan.
3. Indicate the current stage of your business and the industry it operates in.
4. Enter your mission statement, which should be a brief summary of your business purpose and values.
5. Outline your top priorities, such as revenue growth, profit improvement, and customer retention.
6. Identify your target market and the key factors that set you apart from competitors.
7. Describe your marketing strategies, including digital marketing, social media, and content marketing.
8. Allocate a budget for marketing activities.
9. Plan for staffing changes, including hiring or restructuring.
10. Outline your technology investments, including upgrades or new purchases.
11. Determine the capital requirements needed for the business.
12. Identify the sources of funding for your business.
13. Assess key risks to the plan.
14. Define success metrics to measure progress.
15. Establish a timeline with key milestones.
16. Add any additional notes or details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (id: 1, label: Business Name, type: text, required: true): Enter the official name of your business.
* **Contact Person Name** (id: 2, label: Contact Person Name, type: text, required: true): Enter the name of the primary contact person for this plan.
* **Email Address** (id: 3, label: Email Address, type: email, required: true): Enter the contact email address for this person.
* **Phone Number** (id: 4, label: Phone Number, type: text, required: false): Enter the best contact phone number for this person.
* **Planning Year** (id: 5, label: Planning Year, type: select_one, required: true): Select the year this plan is for (2026, 2027, 2028, or Multi-Year).
* **Business Stage** (id: 6, label: Business Stage, type: select_one, required: true): Select the current stage of your business (Startup, Growth, Established, Expansion, Maturity, Turnaround).
* **Industry** (id: 7, label: Industry, type: select_one, required: true): Select the industry in which your business operates (Technology, Retail, Manufacturing, Healthcare, Food Service, Professional Services, Construction, Real Estate, Education, Other).
* **Mission Statement** (id: 8, label: Mission Statement, type: text, required: true): Enter a brief summary of your business's purpose and values.
* **Vision Statement** (id: 9, label: Vision Statement, type: text, required: false): Enter a statement of your business's vision for the future (optional).
* **Core Values** (id: 10, label: Core Values, type: text, required: false): Enter a list of your core values (optional).
* **Revenue Goal** (id: 11, label: Revenue Goal, type: number, required: true): Enter the target revenue for the year.
* **Profit Goal** (id: 12, label: Profit Goal, type: number, required: false): Enter the target profit for the year (optional).
* **Target Growth Rate** (id: 13, label: Target Growth Rate, type: number, required: false): Enter the target growth rate (optional).
* **Key Objectives** (id: 14, label: Key Objectives, type: select_multiple, required: true): Select your top priorities (Revenue Growth, Profit Improvement, Market Expansion, Product Development, Customer Retention, Brand Awareness, Operational Efficiency, Team Building, Technology Upgrade, Cost Reduction, Other).
* **Target Market** (id: 15, label: Target Market, type: text, required: true): Enter the primary target market for your business.
* **Competitive Advantage** (id: 16, label: Competitive Advantage, type: text, required: true): Enter a description of what sets your business apart from competitors.
* **Marketing Strategies** (id: 17, label: Marketing Strategies, type: select_multiple, required: true): Select your planned marketing activities (Digital Marketing, Social Media, Content Marketing, Email Campaigns, Events and Trade Shows, Partnerships, Traditional Advertising, Public Relations, Referral Programs, Other).
* **Marketing Budget** (id: 18, label: Marketing Budget, type: number, required: false): Enter the allocated marketing budget.
* **Staffing Plan** (id: 19, label: Staffing Plan, type: select_one, required: true): Select the planned staffing changes (No Changes, Hiring Key Roles, Team Expansion, Restructuring, Outsourcing, Other).
* **Technology Investments** (id: 20, label: Technology Investments, type: text, required: false): Enter descriptions of planned technology upgrades or purchases (optional).
* **Capital Requirements** (id: 21, label: Capital Requirements, type: number, required: false): Enter the funding needed for your business (optional).
* **Funding Sources** (id: 22, label: Funding Sources, type: select_multiple, required: true): Select the sources of funding for your business (Internal Cash Flow, Bank Loan, Investors, Venture Capital, Crowdfunding, Government Grants, Other, Not Needed).
* **Risk Assessment** (id: 23, label: Risk Assessment, type: text, required: false): Enter a description of key risks to the plan (optional).
* **Success Metrics** (id: 24, label: Success Metrics, type: text, required: true): Define how you will measure success.
* **Timeline and Milestones** (id: 25, label: Timeline and Milestones, type: text, required: false): Enter key dates and milestones.
* **Additional Notes** (id: 26, label: Additional Notes, type: text, required: false): Enter any other planning details.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Review and edit your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy and clarity.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the support team for assistance.
