# Chekya Website Self Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is designed to walk you through the fields and options on the Chekya Website Self Survey Form. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to ensure we can provide the best possible service for your website needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and accurately to help us understand your business needs.
2. Select the most appropriate option for each field, using the provided options or typing in a custom answer if provided.
3. Make sure to scroll through each page (section) and fill out all relevant fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_legal_name_chekya`, text, required): Enter the official name of your business.
* **Industry Sector** (`business_industry_sector`, select_one, required): Select the industry your business operates in from the provided options (e.g. Professional Services, E-commerce / Retail, etc.).
* **Primary Purpose of the Website** (`primary_website_purpose`, select_one, required): Choose the primary purpose of your website from the provided options (e.g. Brand Awareness / Information, Selling Products (E-commerce), etc.).
* **Expected Monthly Visitors** (`expected_monthly_traffic_range`, select_one, required): Select the estimated number of visitors you expect to your website each month from the provided options.
* **Do you need e-commerce capabilities** (`feature_req_ecommerce`, select_one, required): Choose whether you need e-commerce capabilities, and if so, if it's a required feature.
* **Do you need blogging / content management** (`feature_req_blogging`, select_one, required): Choose whether you need blogging and content management capabilities.
* **Do you need a private customer portal / login** (`feature_req_customer_portal`, select_one, required): Select whether you need a private customer portal or login.
* **Monthly Budget for Website Management ($)** (`estimated_website_budget_range`, select_one, required): Choose the estimated budget you have for website management per month from the provided options.
* **Preferred Design Aesthetic** (`design_aesthetic_preference`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred design aesthetic for your website from the provided options (e.g. Minimalist and Modern, Bold and Creative, etc.).
* **Which version are you leaning towards** (`recommended_version_match`, select_one, required): Select the version of Chekya that best fits your needs based on your research and answers.
* **Email Address for Result Summary** (`contact_person_email_chekya`, email, required): Enter your email address where we can send the results of your assessment.
* **I have completed the self-survey** (`survey_completion_ack_chekya`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have completed the survey and are ready to proceed.

## Tips

- Please answer all required fields to ensure we can provide accurate assessment.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- We will use this information to tailor our services to meet your specific needs and preferences.
