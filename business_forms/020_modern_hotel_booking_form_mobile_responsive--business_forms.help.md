# Modern Hotel Booking Form Mobile Responsive - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a hotel booking process. It is intended for demonstration purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Information** field with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your **Contact Information** in the next field, including your email address and phone number.
3. Provide your **Booking Details**, including the date, time, and any other relevant information for your reservation.
4. Specify your **Arrival and Departure** dates and times in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Choose your preferred **Pickup Options** from the available options (if any).
6. If you have any **Special Requests**, such as extra towels or a specific room type, enter them in this field.
7. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
8. Finally, enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact details, including email address and phone number.
* **Booking Details** (`booking_details`, text, required: false): Enter your booking date, time, and any other relevant details.
* **Arrival and Departure** (`arrival_departure`, text, required: false): Enter your arrival and departure dates in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Pickup Options** (`pickup_option`, text, required: false): Choose your preferred pickup options (if available).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required: false): Enter any special requests, such as extra towels or a specific room type.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
