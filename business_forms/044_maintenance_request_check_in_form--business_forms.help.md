# Maintenance Request Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from employees about their maintenance requests. It is used to create a clear and concise description of the issue, and to gather information that will help our team address it effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required information, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Describe the issue in as much detail as possible in the `Issue Description` field.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documentation or ID.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documentation or ID.
* **Email** (3): Enter your official email address associated with your work account.
* **Issue Description** (4): Describe the issue you are experiencing, including any steps taken to try to resolve it. Be as specific as possible, and include any relevant details that will help our team understand the problem.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understood the purpose of this form before proceeding.
* If you are unsure whether a request has already been submitted, please search our database or contact your supervisor/manager for verification.
* Please fill out the form in a clear and concise manner, and avoid using jargon or technical terms that may be unfamiliar to our team.
* If you have any attachments or additional information that may be relevant to your request, please attach them to the form.
