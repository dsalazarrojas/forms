# tire_repair_service_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about referrals to a tire repair service. This form is used to track client visits, status, contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form information section with the relevant details about the client's visit.
2. Select the client's information from the options provided.
3. Enter the client's contact information in the required fields.
4. Select the lead status and provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Information** (id: 1, text, not required): Enter the name of this form to identify it uniquely.
* **Client Information** (id: 2, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the client has visited our service before, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Contact Information** (id: 3, text, not required): Enter the client's contact information, such as their phone number and email address.
* **Lead Information** (id: 4, select_multiple, required): Select the type(s) of lead that this client represents (e.g., New, Returning, etc.).
* **Date of Visit** (id: 5, date, required): Enter the date of the client's visit in MM/dd/yyyy format.
* **Time of Visit** (id: 6, time, required): Enter the time of the client's visit.
* **Phone Number** (id: 7, text, required): Enter the client's phone number in 123-123-1234 format.
* **Email** (id: 8, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Lead Status** (id: 9, select_one, required): Select the current status of this lead (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments** (id: 10, note, not required): Enter any additional comments about this lead.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure that the client's status is up-to-date.
* If a client has visited multiple times, fill out the form for each visit separately.
* If you have any additional comments or notes about this lead, enter them in the comments field.
