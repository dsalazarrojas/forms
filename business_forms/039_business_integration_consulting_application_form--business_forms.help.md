Business Integration Consulting Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Integration Consulting Application Form is designed to gather information from businesses seeking integration consulting services. This form helps us understand your company's integration needs and goals, allowing us to provide personalized support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that we have a clear understanding of your company's integration needs.
2. Be as specific as possible when answering questions, especially when describing your current data challenges and desired integration goals.
3. Select all applicable systems and services you are interested in.
4. Provide accurate contact information for your project lead.
5. Submit the form to initiate the application process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (integration_client_company_name)**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Primary Integration Goal (primary_integration_goal_selection)**: Choose the main outcome you seek from the integration process. Options include:
	+ Streamline Workflows: Improve efficiency and productivity by integrating systems and automating tasks.
	+ Data Synchronization: Align data between systems for a more comprehensive view.
	+ Legacy System Modernization: Update outdated systems for better performance.
	+ Scalability Enhancement: Prepare for growth and expansion.
	+ Cost Reduction: Minimize costs by streamlining processes.
* **Systems Targeted for Integration (systems_to_be_integrated_selection)**: Select all systems you are interested in integrating:
	+ CRM (e.g. Salesforce, HubSpot): Customer relationship management systems.
	+ ERP (e.g. SAP, Oracle): Enterprise resource planning systems.
	+ Marketing Automation: Automate marketing workflows.
	+ E-commerce Platform: Online shopping and sales platforms.
	+ Cloud Infrastructure (AWS/Azure): Cloud-based infrastructure and services.
	+ On-premise Databases: In-house databases and data storage systems.
* **Current Data or Integration Challenges (current_data_challenges_description)**: Describe any data silos, manual entry issues, or data quality problems you are facing.
* **Status of Technical Documentation (existing_documentation_status_choice)**: Choose how well your current system is documented:
	+ Fully documented: Complete ERDs, APIs, and workflows are available.
	+ Partially documented: Some documentation is available but incomplete.
	+ Minimal / Outdated documentation: Limited or outdated documentation.
	+ No documentation: No documentation is available.
* **Expected Timeframe for ROI (expected_project_roi_timeframe)**: Choose when you expect to see the benefits of integration:
	+ Within 3 Months: Rapid results and benefits.
	+ 3 to 6 Months: Gradual results and benefits.
	+ 6 to 12 Months: Long-term results and benefits.
	+ 12+ Months: Extended results and benefits.
* **Estimated Integration Budget (integration_budget_bracket_selection)**: Choose your estimated budget for the integration:
	+ Under 25,000: A limited budget.
	+ 25,001 - 75,000: A moderate budget.
	+ 75,001 - 200,000: A higher budget.
	+ 200,000+: A large budget.
* **Project Lead Name (project_lead_contact_name_consulting)**: Enter the name of your project lead.
* **Lead Email Address (project_lead_contact_email_consulting)**: Enter the email address of your project lead.
* **Desired Consulting Services (desired_consulting_services_selection)**: Select the services you are interested in:
	+ Strategy and Planning: Develop a strategy and plan for integration.
	+ Technical Architecture Design: Design the technical architecture for integration.
	+ Development and Implementation: Implement the integration.
	+ Post-integration Support: Support after integration.
	+ Training and Onboarding: Train users on new systems and processes.
* **Date of Application (consulting_application_submission_date)**: Enter the date you submit this application.
