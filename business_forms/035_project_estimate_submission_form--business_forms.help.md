# Project Estimate Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information about a project's estimate submission. It is used to gather details about the project, such as the submitter's name, company, job title, and contact information. This form is not a formal estimate submission form but rather a supplementary document that provides a brief overview of the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the First Name field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the Last Name field.
3. Type the name of your **Company** in the Company field.
4. Enter your **Job Title** in the Job Title field.
5. Enter the number of **Prophets** (if applicable).
6. Provide a brief description of the **Budget Information** in the Budget Information field.
7. Enter the start and end times of the **Job Start Time** and **Job End Time** respectively.
8. Enter your **Email** address in the Email field.
9. Enter your **Phone Number** in the Phone Number field.
10. Enter your **Address** in the Address field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A field for entering your first name.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Last Name**: A field for entering your last name.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Company**: A field for entering the name of your company.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Job Title**: A field for entering your job title.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Prophets**: A field for entering the number of prophets (if applicable).
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
* **Budget Information**: A field for providing a brief description of the budget.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Job Start Time**: A field for entering the start time of the job.
	+ Type: Time
	+ Required: No
* **Job End Time**: A field for entering the end time of the job.
	+ Type: Time
	+ Required: No
* **Email**: A field for entering your email address.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: No
* **Phone Number**: A field for entering your phone number.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Address**: A field for entering your address.
	+ Type: Note
	+ Required: No

## Tips
- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
- If you have any issues or concerns while filling this form, please contact the relevant authorities.
- This form is for internal use only, and any sensitive or confidential information should not be disclosed.
