# Videography Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for videography contracts. It is designed to be filled out by clients or videographers who are looking to book a videography service for a specific project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Project Details section with information about the project, including the project name and a brief description.
2. Provide your contact information in the Client Info section.
3. Select the payment terms that apply to your project.
4. Enter the shoot schedule, including the start and end dates and times.
5. Enter the shoot location where the videography will take place.
6. List the equipment needed for the shoot in the Equipment List section.
7. Enter your contact information, including your email and other relevant contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, required): This is the main section where you can enter the name and description of your project. Be as detailed as possible to help the videographer understand the scope of the project.
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, `number`, required): This section is used to capture your contact details, including your phone number. Please enter your contact number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" without any special characters.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_one`, required): This field allows you to select the payment terms that apply to your project. Please choose one of the available options to indicate your payment terms.
* **Shoot Details** (`shoot_details`, `date`, required): This field is used to enter the start and end dates of the shoot.
* **Shoot Schedule** (`shoot_schedule`, `time`, required): This field is used to enter the start and end times of the shoot.
* **Shoot Location** (`shoot_location`, `text`, required): This section is used to enter the location where the shoot will take place.
* **Equipment List** (`equipment_list`, `note`, required): This is a free-form text field where you can list the equipment needed for the shoot. Be as detailed as possible to help the videographer prepare for the shoot.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `email`, required): This section is used to enter your email address and other relevant contact details. Please ensure that your email is formatted correctly to ensure it can be used for communication.
