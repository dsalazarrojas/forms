# Insurance Claim Submission Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document information related to insurance claims, facilitating efficient processing and communication between parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Claim details" field with a brief description of your insurance claim.
2. Provide your own information in the "Claimant information" field.
3. Enter the policy holder's information in the "Policy holder information" field.
4. Enter your contact information in the "Claimant contact info" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Claimant address" field.
6. If applicable, upload relevant documents in the "Document upload" field.
7. If you have already experienced a loss, enter the date of the loss in the "Date of loss" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim details** (`claim_details`, text, optional): A brief description of your insurance claim.
* **Claimant information** (`claimant_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
* **Policy holder information** (`policy_holder_info`, text, optional): Enter the policy holder's name, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
* **Claimant contact info** (`claimant_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your email address, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
* **Claimant address** (`claimant_address`, text, optional): Enter your address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Claim details** (`claimant_claim_details`, text, optional): A detailed description of your claim, including any supporting evidence or documentation.
* **Document upload** (`claimant_document_upload`, text, optional): Upload any relevant documents related to your claim.
* **Date of loss** (`claimant_date_of_loss`, date, optional): The date of the loss or event that gave rise to your claim. If you're unsure, please enter the date you discovered the loss or event.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information in each field.
* If you have any questions or need help filling out this form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* If you have already experienced a loss, try to provide as much detail as possible about the event.
* This form is for your convenience, and we will use the information provided to process your insurance claim.
