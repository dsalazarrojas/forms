# toasting-2020 - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form, "Toasting-2020," is a tool for capturing and reporting information about districts, divisions, and club activities. It's used to collect data for analysis and administration purposes within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the form, please follow these steps:

*   Select the relevant information for each field.
*   Ensure you enter the correct data for each required field (marked as required).
*   Review your inputs for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **District-Id** (`district_id`, Number, required): Enter a unique number identifying the district.
*   **Division-Name** (`division_name`, Text, not required): Type the name of the division within the district.
*   **Year** (`year`, Number, required): Specify the year related to the report or record.
*   **Reporting-Period-Start** (`reporting_period_start`, Date, required): Enter the start date of the reporting period.
*   **Reporting-Period-End** (`reporting_period_end`, Date, required): Enter the end date of the reporting period.
*   **District-Division** (`district_division`, Text, required): Possibly enter another identifier for the district or division.
*   **Report-By** (`report_by`, Text, required): Identify the person responsible for preparing this report or record.
*   **Club-Id** (`club_id`, Number, required): Enter a unique number identifying the club.
*   **Club-Name** (`club_name`, Text, not required): Enter the name of the club.
*   **Member-Name** (`member_name`, Text, required): Enter the name of the club member.
*   **Club-Name** (`club_name_2`, Text, not required): Possibly enter another name for the club.
*   **Reporting-Period** (`reporting_period`, Text, not required): Enter the time frame of the report, possibly for historical context.
*   **Division-Director** (`division_director`, Text, required): Enter the name of the person in charge of the division.

## Tips

*   Double-check your inputs for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authority or support team.
*   Use the form for its intended purpose to ensure accurate and consistent data collection.
