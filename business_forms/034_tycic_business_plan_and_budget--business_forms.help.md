# Tycic Business Plan And Budget - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a comprehensive business plan and budget for your business. It will help you gather all the necessary information to make informed decisions and stay organized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Business Plan` section with a detailed description of your business plan.
2. In the `Budget` section, select all the items that apply to your business needs.
3. In the `Details` section, provide as much detail as possible about your business plan.
4. Enter the `Date` for your business plan in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., 2023-02-20).
5. Choose a `Time` for your business plan in the format `HH:MM:SS` (e.g., 14:30:00).
6. Select the `Category` of your business (e.g., Business Forms, Personal Forms, School Forms).
7. Choose the `Priority` of your business (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
8. Optionally, add any additional `Notes` about your business plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Plan
* **Business Plan** (`1`, text, required): A detailed description of your business plan.
### Budget
* **Budget** (`2`, select_multiple, required): Select all the items that apply to your business needs, such as equipment, marketing, travel, etc.
### Details
* **Details** (`3`, text, required): Provide as much detail as possible about your business plan, such as target market, competition, etc.
### Date
* **Date** (`4`, date, required): Enter the date for your business plan in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
### Time
* **Time** (`5`, time, required): Choose a time for your business plan in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
### Category
* **Category** (`6`, select_one, required): Select the category of your business (e.g., Business Forms, Personal Forms, School Forms).
### Priority
* **Priority** (`7`, select_one, required): Choose the priority of your business (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
### Notes
* **Notes** (`8`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your business plan.
