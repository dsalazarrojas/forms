# feminism_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The feminism survey is a tool designed to collect feedback and opinions on one's perception of feminism. It is meant to be a self-assessment tool to help users understand their views and provide information that can be used to develop and improve feminist theories and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read through the introduction section, which provides a brief overview of the survey.
2. Answer the questions regarding your gender identity and what you think feminism means.
3. Express your level of agreement or disagreement with statements about feminism.
4. Share your personal beliefs and thoughts about feminism.
5. Indicate how different social influences have affected your views on feminism.
6. Describe what actions you plan to take to support or engage with feminism.
7. Provide your next steps or plans regarding feminism.
8. Leave any additional comments or feedback you have.
9. Finally, provide your contact information so that we can get in touch with you for further discussions or follow-ups.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): This section provides a brief introduction to the survey and its purpose. Please read through this section to understand the context of the survey.
* **gender_identity** (`2`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Please select your gender identity (Male, Female, or Other).
* **what_is_feminism** (`3`, `text`, required: `true`): Share your thoughts on what you think feminism means.
* **how_much_do_you_agree** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Indicate your level of agreement or disagreement with statements about feminism. Select all the options that apply.
* **personal_beliefs** (`5`, `note`, required: `false`): Share your personal beliefs and thoughts about feminism. You can write anything that comes to your mind.
* **social_influence** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Identify how different social influences have affected your views on feminism. Select all the options that apply.
* **what_will_you_do** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Describe what actions you plan to take to support or engage with feminism. Select all the options that apply.
* **what_next** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide your next steps or plans regarding feminism.
* **comments** (`9`, `text`, required: `false`): Leave any additional comments or feedback you have about the survey.
* **demographics** (`10`, `note`, required: `false`): Note down any demographic information you are willing to share.
* **contact_information** (`11`, `text`, required: `true`): Provide your contact information so that we can get in touch with you for further discussions or follow-ups.
* **contact_email** (`12`, `email`, required: `true`): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further discussions or follow-ups.
