# Sponsor Promotion Material Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsor Promotion Material Request Form is a simple form used to request sponsorship materials from a sponsor. The form collects relevant information about the materials and the sponsor, and allows the marketing team to track and manage the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you need sponsorship materials.
2. Choose the material type that you need.
3. Enter the date you requested the materials.
4. Enter the date you expect to receive the materials.
5. Enter the sponsor's name and contact information.
6. Enter any additional notes about the request.
7. If applicable, attach supporting files or documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Request Form**: This is where you select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you need sponsorship materials.
* **Material Type**: Choose the type of material you are requesting, such as brochures or flyers.
* **Material Request Date**: Enter the date you requested the materials.
* **Material Delivery Date**: Enter the date you expect to receive the materials.
* **Sponsor Promotion Name**: Enter the name of the sponsor who provided the materials.
* **Sponsor Promotion Email**: Enter the email address of the sponsor who provided the materials.
* **Sponsor Promotion Phone**: Enter the phone number of the sponsor who provided the materials.
* **Sponsor Promotion Address**: Enter the address of the sponsor who provided the materials.
* **Sponsor Promotion Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the request, such as special instructions or requests.
* **Sponsor Promotion Attachment**: If applicable, attach any supporting files or documents that are relevant to the request.
* **Material Status Update**: Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the request.
* **Sponsor Promotion Status**: Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of the sponsor's promotion.
