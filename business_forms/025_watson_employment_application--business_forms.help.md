# Watson Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Watson Employment Application form is a tool for collecting employment-related information from employees. It helps gather essential details about job applications, employee status, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Job Application page with details about your current or desired job role.
2. Select your Job Title and confirm if you have any experience or education related to the job.
3. Provide your contact information, including phone numbers and email address.
4. Fill out your work and personal address details.
5. Enter your emergency contact information, including name, phone number, and email address.
6. Select your employment status and type.
7. Fill out the job notes and employment start and end dates, if applicable.
8. Enter your job salary details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Application** (`job_application`, text, required): Fill out a brief description of your current or desired job role.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_multiple, required): Select your job title from the given options.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have experience or not in your selected job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your job.
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, required): Enter your experience years in the selected job title.
* **Education** (`education`, select_multiple, required): Select your educational background related to the job.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Attach your resume or CV for reference.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, required): Select your relevant skills and qualifications.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have a contact person for emergency purposes.
* **Availability** (`availability`, time, required): Enter your availability for work.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required): Enter your job start date.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, required): Enter your job end date.
* **Work Address** (`work_address`, text, required): Provide your work address.
* **Personal Address** (`personal_address`, text, required): Provide your personal address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, required): Enter your emergency contact person's email address.
* **Emergency Relation** (`emergency_relation`, text, required): Enter your relation with your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone2** (`emergency_phone2`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Confirm your employment status.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, text, required): Enter your employment type.
* **Job Notes** (`job_notes`, text, required): Enter any relevant job notes.
* **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, required): Enter your employment start date.
* **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, date, required): Enter your employment end date.
* **Job Salary** (`job_salary`, number, required): Enter your job salary details.
