# tax_policy_amendment_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tax policy amendment approval form is used for submitting tax policy changes for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields with accurate and honest information. Make sure to follow the guidance in the relevant policy documentation and contact the relevant parties if you have any questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### policy_changes 
• **Policy Changes** (Policy Changes, text, not required): This is where you can describe the tax policy changes you want to make. Be as clear and concise as possible.

### approval_process 
• **Approval Process** (Approval Process, text, not required): This is where you can describe the approval process you will follow for the tax policy amendment. This could include any relevant meetings, discussions, or other procedures.

### stakeholders_feedback 
• **Stakeholders Feedback** (Stakeholders Feedback, text, not required): This is where you can gather feedback from stakeholders about the tax policy amendment. You can include any comments or suggestions they may have.

### consent 
• **Consent** (Consent, text, not required): This is where you can indicate that you have received consent from all relevant parties to make the tax policy amendment. 

### form_submission 
• **Form Submission** (Form Submission, select_multiple, not required): This is where you can indicate whether the form is being submitted or not. You can select 'Yes' if you are submitting the form, and 'No' if you are not submitting it.
