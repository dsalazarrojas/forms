# General Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Membership Form is a document used to collect information from individuals or businesses interested in becoming members of a general membership program. The form helps to streamline the membership process and ensures that all necessary information is gathered in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by navigating through each page of the form, starting from the first page.
2. For each page, read the label and hint provided for each field.
3. Fill out the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
4. Double-check the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Membership Form** (`general_membership_form`, text, required: false): This is the title of the form, and it is not required to be filled out.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required: false): This field is intended to capture any additional payment details, such as payment methods or frequencies.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture the contact information of the individual or business interested in becoming a member.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required: false): Another instance of this field, which may be considered for consolidation or removal.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture any additional information that the individual or business would like to provide.
* **Member Signature** (`member_signature`, text, required: false): This field is intended to capture the signature of the individual or representative of the business interested in becoming a member.
* **Member Signature Date** (`member_signature_date`, date, required: false): This field is used to capture the date of the member signature.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This field is used to capture the email address of the individual or business.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture the phone number of the individual or business.
* **Fax** (`fax`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture the fax number of the individual or business.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture the physical address of the individual or business.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture the city where the individual or business is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture the state where the individual or business is located.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture the zip code of the individual or business.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture any payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Terms Date** (`payment_terms_date`, date, required: false): This field is used to capture the date of the payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Amount** (`payment_terms_amount`, number, required: false): This field is used to capture the amount of the payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Fees** (`payment_terms_fees`, number, required: false): This field is used to capture the fees associated with the payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Frequency** (`payment_terms_frequency`, select_one, required: true): This field is used to select the frequency of payment terms, which can be Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, or Bi-Monthly.
* **Payment Terms Frequency Hint** (`payment_terms_frequency_hint`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide a hint for the payment terms frequency selection.
* **Payment Terms Note** (`payment_terms_note`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture any additional notes or comments about the payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Note Hint** (`payment_terms_note_hint`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide a hint for the payment terms note.
* **Payment Terms Terms Note** (`payment_terms_terms_note`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture any additional terms or conditions related to the payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Terms Note Hint** (`payment_terms_terms_note_hint`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide a hint for the payment terms terms note.

## Tips
* Double-check the form for any errors before submitting.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the provided hints or contact the membership team for assistance.
