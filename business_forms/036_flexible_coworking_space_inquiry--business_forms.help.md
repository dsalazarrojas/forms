# Flexible_Coworking_Space_Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information about the requirements and needs of potential clients or users for a coworking space. Please fill out this form thoroughly to ensure that your space needs are accurately communicated to us.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the introduction section, providing a brief overview of your space needs and requirements.
2. In the Team Details section, enter your team's name, job title, and other relevant contact information.
3. Specify the workspace needs in the Workspace Needs section, choosing one or more options that best suit your requirements.
4. If applicable, provide a budget for the requested space in the Budget Details section.
5. Select the preferred layout for the coworking space in the Preferred Layout section.
6. Enter your follow-up contact information, including email, phone number, and any follow-up notes.
7. Agree to the terms of service and sign the agreement to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
