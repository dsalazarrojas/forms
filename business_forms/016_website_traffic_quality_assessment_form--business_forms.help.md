# Website Traffic Quality Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the quality of website traffic on our platform. The information collected through this form will help us understand how often users visit websites, their behavior on our platform, and their demographics. This data will be used to improve the user experience and tailor our services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select all that apply for the "What type of behavior do you exhibit on our website?" question.
3. Choose your demographic category that best represents you.
4. Enter your age range if it is not listed in the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form** (1): This is an introductory note that provides background information about the form.
* **How often do you visit websites?** (2): Answer this question based on your actual behavior. You can respond with a numerical value.
* **What type of behavior do you exhibit on our website?** (3): Select all that apply:
	+ Browsing: This refers to casually browsing through our website without a specific goal in mind.
	+ Searching: This refers to actively looking for something specific on our website.
	+ Scrolling: This refers to scrolling through our website's pages.
	+ Purchasing: This refers to making a purchase or completing a transaction on our website.
* **What type of demographics do you identify with?** (4): Choose one or more of the following:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ 16-25
	+ 26-35
	+ 36-45
	+ 46-55
	+ 56-65
	+ Other (Please specify in the text field)
* **16-25** (5): Select this option if you fall within this age range.
* **26-35** (6): Select this option if you fall within this age range.
* **36-45** (7): Select this option if you fall within this age range.
* **46-55** (8): Select this option if you fall within this age range.
* **56-65** (9): Select this option if you fall within this age range.
* **Other** (10): Choose this option if you are outside the listed age ranges, and enter your age range in the text field provided.
* **What are the conversion rates on your website?** (11): Respond with:
	+ High: This refers to high conversion rates, meaning you are likely to take action on our website.
	+ Medium: This refers to moderate conversion rates, meaning some users may take action but it's not guaranteed.
	+ Low: This refers to low conversion rates, meaning few users take action on our website.
