# Burger Restaurant Shift Swap Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate shift swaps between employees in the Burger Restaurant. A shift swap occurs when an employee swaps shifts with a colleague, and both employees agree to take each other's shifts. This form helps to document the swap request and ensure that it is properly reviewed and approved by the relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on the roster.
2. Enter your employee ID number as a 4 or 5-digit staff code.
3. Select the date of the original shift you want to swap.
4. Select the start time of the original shift you want to swap.
5. Choose the valid reason for the shift swap (Medical Appointment, Family Emergency, Educational Commitment, Personal Reasons, or Travel).
6. Enter the name of the colleague you are swapping with.
7. Confirm that the colleague has agreed to take your shift.
8. If applicable, enter the date and start time of the new shift you are taking.
9. Review the notice regarding manager approval and roster update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on the roster.
* **Employee ID Number**: Enter your 4 or 5-digit employee ID number as assigned by your supervisor.
* **Date of Original Shift**: Select the date of the original shift you want to swap.
* **Start Time of Original Shift**: Select the start time of the original shift you want to swap.
* **Reason for Shift Swap**: Choose a valid reason for the shift swap (Medical Appointment, Family Emergency, Educational Commitment, Personal Reasons, or Travel).
* **Name of Colleague Swapping With**: Enter the name of the colleague you are swapping with.
* **Has the Colleague Confirmed this Swap**: Confirm that the colleague has agreed to take your shift.
* **Digital Signature**: Type your full name to acknowledge the request.
