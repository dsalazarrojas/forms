# Financial Forecast Revision Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request revisions to existing financial forecasts. Please use this form to provide detailed information about the revisions you need, and our finance team will review and update the forecasts accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields with the following information:

1. **Financial Forecast Revision Request Form** (`financial_forecast_revision_request`, text, optional): Briefly describe the reason for the revision request.
2. **Revision Date** (`revision_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the revision. Please note that this field is not required, but it's helpful for tracking revisions over time.
3. **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Provide a detailed explanation of the reason for the revision request.
4. **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Attach any relevant documents that support your revision request.
5. **Revision Details** (`revision_details`, text, optional): Describe the specific changes you need to make to the forecast.
6. **Finance Team Review** (`finance_team_review`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to request that the finance team reviews your request, indicating that you need their input before proceeding with the revision.
7. **Select Finance Team Members** (`finance_team_review_hint`, select_multiple, optional): If you selected "Yes" for the previous field, choose the finance team members you'd like to review your request.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling in the fields, especially the "Reason" and "Revision Details" fields, to ensure that your request is properly understood and addressed.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your supervisor or the finance team for guidance.
* The finance team will review and update the forecasts accordingly, and you'll receive updates on the status of your request.
