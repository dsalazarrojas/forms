# Construction Site Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Site Check In Form is designed to collect information from visitors, including their name, purpose of visit, areas to be visited, and other relevant details for site management and visitor safety.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Check the boxes for each field as you complete it.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check-In Date** (`check_in_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your site entry.
* **Check-In Time** (`check_in_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your arrival.
* **Full Name** (`visitor_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your employer or organization's name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your position or role.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Visitor Type** (`visitor_type`, select_one, required): Choose your visitor category: Employee, Subcontractor, Vendor or Supplier, Client or Owner, Inspector or Official, Delivery Personnel, Guest, or Other.
* **Purpose of Visit** (`purpose_of_visit`, select_one, required): Choose the reason for your visit: Regular Work, Delivery, Meeting, Inspection, Service or Maintenance, Tour, Emergency, or Other.
* **Areas You Will Visit** (`visiting_area`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas you plan to visit on site, such as Office Trailer, Main Building, Foundation Area, Framing Area, Roof, Parking Area, Storage Yard, or Other.
* **Host/Contact on Site** (`host_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you are meeting or reporting to.
* **Expected Duration** (`expected_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the expected length of your visit: Less than 1 hour, 1 to 2 hours, 2 to 4 hours, 4 to 8 hours, Full Day, or Multiple Days.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle's brand if applicable.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle's model if applicable.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle's color if applicable.
* **License Plate Number** (`license_plate`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle's license plate number and state if applicable.
* **Safety Orientation Completed** (`safety_orientation_completed`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have completed site safety orientation today or previously.
* **PPE Required for Your Visit** (`ppe_required`, select_multiple, required): Select any necessary Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) you will need for your visit, such as Hard Hat, Safety Glasses, High-Visibility Vest, Steel-Toed Boots, Hearing Protection, Respirator, Safety Harness, or Gloves.
* **PPE Provided** (`ppe_provided`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you received all necessary PPE for your visit.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter a contact name in case of an emergency if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact phone number if applicable.
* **Relevant Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical conditions that the site should be aware of.
* **Site Rules Acknowledged** (`site_rules_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to follow all site rules.
* **Liability Waiver Accepted** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required): Confirm that you accept the liability waiver.
* **Visitor Signature** (`visitor_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as a signature.
* **Security Guard Name** (`security_guard_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the security guard processing your check-in, but this is not required.

## Tips

* Please take a few minutes to review and fill out the form accurately.
* If you are unsure about a field, contact the person processing your check-in.
* This form is for the safety and well-being of all site visitors, so please take your responsibility seriously.
