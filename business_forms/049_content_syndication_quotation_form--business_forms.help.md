# Content Syndication Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Syndication Quotation Form is designed to collect necessary information to provide you with the best content syndication services that meet your business needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out all the required fields. The form has multiple pages with different sections, each containing questions or fields that require your input. You can scroll down or navigate through the pages using the provided buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (1, text, required): Enter your company name as it appears on your official documents or website.
- **Contact Name** (2, text, required): Enter the full name of the contact person responsible for this request.
- **Contact Email** (3, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Contact Phone** (4, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Quotation Date** (5, date, required): Select the date for this quotation request.
- **Content Type** (6, select_one, required): Choose the type of content you need syndication services for.
  * Articles
  * Blog Posts
  * Videos
  * Infographics
  * Podcasts
  * News Content
  * Multiple Types
- **Content Volume** (7, select_one, required): Indicate the volume of content you need to syndicate.
  * 1-10 pieces per month
  * 11-25 pieces per month
  * 26-50 pieces per month
  * 51-100 pieces per month
  * Over 100 pieces per month
- **Target Audience** (8, text, required): Describe your target audience or the people you are trying to reach with your content.
- **Industry Focus** (9, select_one, required): Choose the industry your content is related to.
  * Technology
  * Healthcare
  * Finance
  * Education
  * Retail
  * Manufacturing
  * Media
  * Multiple Industries
- **Geographic Targeting** (10, select_one, required): Choose where your content should be distributed.
  * Local
  * Regional
  * National
  * International
  * Worldwide
- **Distribution Channels** (11, select_multiple, required): Select all distribution channels where you want your content to be syndicated.
  * News Websites
  * Industry Blogs
  * Social Media Platforms
  * Email Newsletters
  * Partner Websites
  * Content Aggregators
  * Mobile Apps
  * Print Publications
- **Syndication Duration** (12, select_one, required): Choose the length of the syndication period.
  * 3 Months
  * 6 Months
  * 12 Months
  * 24 Months
  * Ongoing
- **Exclusivity Requirements** (13, select_one, required): Indicate your exclusivity requirements.
  * Exclusive Rights
  * Non-Exclusive
  * Semi-Exclusive
  * Not Sure
- **Content Customization** (14, select_one, required): Choose whether you need content customized.
  * Yes, full customization
  * Yes, minor edits
  * No, as-is syndication
  * Not Sure
- **SEO Requirements** (15, select_one, required): Choose your SEO preferences.
  * Yes, full SEO
  * Yes, basic SEO
  * 'False'
  * Not Sure
- **Analytics and Reporting** (16, select_one, required): Select your analytics and reporting preferences.
  * Comprehensive analytics
  * Basic metrics
  * Monthly reports
  * Real-time dashboard
  * No reporting needed
- **Budget Range** (17, select_one, required): Indicate your budget range.
  * Under $5,000/month
  * $5,000-$10,000/month
  * $10,000-$25,000/month
  * $25,000-$50,000/month
  * Over $50,000/month
- **Pricing Model Preference** (18, select_one, required): Choose your pricing model preference.
  * Per Article
  * Monthly Retainer
  * Performance-Based
  * Revenue Share
  * Custom Agreement
- **Desired Start Date** (19, date, required): Select the desired start date for the syndication service.
- **Current Syndication** (20, select_one, required): Indicate if you are currently syndicating content.
  * Yes, with one provider
  * Yes, with multiple providers
  * No, this is our first time
  * Previously, but not now
- **Current Challenges** (21, text, optional): Describe any current challenges you are facing with content syndication.
- **Success Metrics** (22, text, required): Describe how you will measure success with our content syndication services.
- **Additional Requirements** (23, text, optional): List any additional requirements or preferences you have for our services.
- **Quotation Validity** (24, select_one, optional): Choose how long your quotation should be valid.
  * 30 days
  * 60 days
  * 90 days
  * No preference

## Tips
- Double-check your answers to ensure you provide accurate information.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you have any questions, contact our team for assistance.
