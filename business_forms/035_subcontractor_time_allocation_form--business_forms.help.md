# subcontractor_time_allocation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and report time allocated for a subcontractor on a specific project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the project from the "Project" field.
2. Enter the total hours worked on the project in the "Hours Worked" field.
3. Choose the week(s) the work was done using the "Week" field.
4. Select the subcontractor(s) involved from the "Subcontractor" field.
5. Enter the start date of the work in the "Start Date" field.
6. Enter the end date of the work in the "End Date" field.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project (1)** (`title`, required): Enter the title of the project you're reporting on.
* **Hours Worked (2)** (`hours_worked`, required): Enter the total number of hours spent on the project.
* **Week (3)** (`week`, required): Select the week(s) the work was completed.
* **Subcontractor (4)** (`subcontractor`, required): Choose the subcontractor(s) who worked on the project.
* **Start Date (5)** (`start_date`, required): Enter the start date of the work.
* **End Date (6)** (`end_date`, required): Enter the end date of the work.
* **Notes (7)** (`notes`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the work.
* **Email (8)** (`email`, optional): Enter the subcontractor's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone (9)** (`phone`, optional): Enter the subcontractor's phone number (if applicable).
* **Comments (10)** (`comments`, optional): Add any additional comments about the work.
* **Submit (11)** (`submit_button`, required): Click this button to submit the form.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out to avoid submission errors.
- Double-check the accuracy of your input, especially dates and hours worked.
- Use the "Comments" field to add any context or references that might be helpful for future reference.
