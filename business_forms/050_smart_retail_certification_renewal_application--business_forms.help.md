# Smart Retail Certification Renewal Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Retail Certification Renewal Application form is designed to help retailers renew their certifications in a straightforward and efficient manner. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of certification renewal you are requesting: Request a Renewal, Renewal for Change of Business Name, or Renewal for Change of Business Ownership.
2. Upload any necessary documents.
3. Confirm that you have uploaded all required documents.
4. Provide your business details.
5. Select the type and status of your certification.
6. Enter your contact information (optional).
7. Confirm that you have submitted the renewal request correctly.
8. Select "Submit Request" to submit your renewal application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request a Certification** (`certification_request`, select_one, required): 
  Select the type of certification renewal you are requesting.
  - Request a Renewal: 
    Renew your existing certification.
  - Renewal for Change of Business Name:
    Renew your certification due to a change in your business name.
  - Renewal for Change of Business Ownership:
    Renew your certification due to a change in your business ownership.
- **Upload Documents** (`document_upload`, note, optional): 
  Upload any necessary documents to support your certification renewal request.
  You can attach multiple documents, and they will be displayed below.
- **Confirm Upload** (`document_confirm`, select_multiple, required): 
  Confirm that you have uploaded all required documents.
  Select "True" if you have uploaded all documents, or "False" otherwise.
- **Business Details** (`business_details`, text, required): 
  Enter your business details, such as address and contact information.
- **Certification Details** (`certification_details`, select_multiple, required): 
  Select the type and status of your certification.
  - Type:
    Select your certification type.
  - Status:
    Select your current certification status.
  - Issue Date:
    Enter the date your current certification expires or was issued.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): 
  Enter your contact information, such as name and phone number.
  This is optional, but recommended in case we need to contact you.
- **Confirm Renewal** (`confirm_renewal`, select_one, required): 
  Confirm that you have submitted the renewal request correctly.
  Select "True" to confirm.
- **Submit Request** (`submit_request`, select_one, required): 
  Select "Submit Request" to submit your certification renewal application.
  Select "Save for Later" to save your progress and submit later.

## Tips
- Make sure to upload all required documents to ensure a successful submission.
- Double-check your business and certification details for accuracy.
- Confirm that you have selected the correct certification type and status.
- If you have any issues with the form, contact the relevant authority for assistance. 
- Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid mistakes.
