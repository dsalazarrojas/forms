# equipment_borrowing_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit and track equipment borrowing requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill out the user information section, including your name and any relevant details.
2.  Provide the requested date for the equipment you want to borrow.
3.  Specify the equipment you want to borrow and its description.
4.  Enter the location where the equipment will be used.
5.  Set the return date for the borrowed equipment.
6.  Select the status and assignee of the equipment.
7.  Optionally, add any additional comments or notes.
8.  Sign and date the form when you have returned the equipment or updated its status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Request**: Enter a brief description of why you need to borrow the equipment.
* **Requested Date**: Enter the date you plan to start using the equipment.
* **Equipment Borrowed**: Describe the equipment you are borrowing.
* **Borrower Name**: Enter your name for borrowing records.
* **Borrower Info**: Add any additional information about yourself, such as department or contact information.
* **Item Location**: Specify where the equipment will be used.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date you plan to return the equipment.
* **Status**: Select whether the equipment is currently Active or Inactive.
* **Assigned To**: Choose the person or team who will be using the equipment.
* **Status Update**: Add any updates or comments regarding the equipment status.
* **Item Condition**: Select the condition of the equipment (New, Used, or Damaged).
* **Equipment Returned**: Indicate if the equipment has been returned.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the equipment borrowing.
* **Borrower Comments**: Add comments from the borrower regarding the equipment usage.
* **Admin Comments**: Add comments from the administrator regarding the equipment usage.
* **Borrower Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm return of equipment.
* **Admin Signature**: Sign and date the form as the administrator to confirm reception of the form.
* **Date Borrowed**: Enter the actual date the equipment was taken.
* **Date Returned**: Enter the actual date the equipment was returned.
* **Duration Borrowed**: Enter the total number of days the equipment was borrowed.
* **Equipment Returned Date**: Enter the date the equipment was returned (if applicable).
* **Total Hours Borrowed**: Enter the total hours the equipment was borrowed.
* **Borrower Type**: Select the type of borrower (Employee or Guest).
* **Status Color**: Select the status color (Red, Yellow, or None).
