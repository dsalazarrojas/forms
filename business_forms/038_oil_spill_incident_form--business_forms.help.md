# Oil Spill Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oil Spill Incident Form is designed to collect information about oil spills for reporting and recordkeeping purposes. This form should be completed after an incident has occurred.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Oil Spill Incident Form" from the available forms.
2. Click on the "Start" or "Next" button to begin filling out the form.
3. Fill out the form in the following sections:
   - **Header**: Provide a brief header or title for the incident.
   - **Incident ID, Date, and Time**: Record the incident ID, date, and time of the spill.
   - **Spill Details**: Enter information about the spill, including quantity, type, location, and description.
   - **Spill Quantities**: Record the quantities of different types of spills.
   - **Report Details**: Enter information about who reported the spill, when they reported it, and other relevant details.
   - **Submission**: Record the details of who submitted the report, including their name, email, phone number, and organization.
4. Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident ID**: An identification number assigned to the spill.
* **Incident Date and Time**: Record the date and time the spill occurred.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the spill occurred.
* **Incident Description**: A brief description of the spill.
* **Spill Quantity**: Record the quantity of the spill.
* **Spill Type**: Choose the type of spill that occurred (e.g., oil, chemical, other).
* **Spill Location**: Enter the location of the spill.
* **Spill Quantity (Chemical)**: If the spill was chemical, record the quantity involved.
* **Spill Quantity (Oil)**: If the spill was oil, record the quantity involved.
* **Spill Quantity (Other)**: If the spill was of an unknown or other type, record the quantity involved.
* **Report Date and Time**: Record the date and time the report was made.
* **Report Source**: Choose who reported the spill (e.g., environmental agency, oil company, other).
* **Report Date Note**: Provide any additional notes about the report date.
* **Submitted By**: Record the name of the person who submitted the report.
* **Submitted By Email**: Record the email of the person who submitted the report.
* **Submitted By Phone Number**: Record the phone number of the person who submitted the report.
* **Submitted By Department**: Enter the department of the person who submitted the report.
* **Submitted By Position**: Record the position of the person who submitted the report.
* **Submitted By Organization**: Record the organization of the person who submitted the report.
* **Submitted By Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the submission.

Note: The fields that are not required may be left blank.
