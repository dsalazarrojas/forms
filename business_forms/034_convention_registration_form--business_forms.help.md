# Convention Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for attendees to register for the convention, providing essential information about their attendance, preferences, and needs. Please fill it out carefully, as it will be used to plan and organize the event accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you have access to a stable internet connection.
2. Carefully review each field and ensure the information is accurate.
3. Fill out all required fields.
4. If a field is not required, you may leave it blank.
5. Use the options provided to select the correct answer for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email address for communication with the convention organizers.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number for any last-minute inquiries.
* **Organization**: If applicable, enter your company or institution name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your position or role, if applicable.
* **Convention Name**: Choose the convention you are attending.
* **Registration Type**: Select the type of registration that suits your needs (e.g., Full Conference, Single Day, Virtual Only).
* **Days Attending**: Choose all the days you plan to attend.
* **Preferred Track**: Select the track that interests you the most.
* **Workshops to Attend**: Choose all the workshops you plan to attend.
* **Meal Plan**: Select which meals you need (e.g., No Meals, Lunch Only, Dinner Only, All Meals).
* **Dietary Requirements**: Select any special dietary needs you have (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free).
* **Accommodation Needed**: If you need accommodation, select 'True' or 'False'.
* **Room Type Preference**: If you need accommodation, select your preferred room type (e.g., Single Room, Double Room).
* **Check-in Date**: Enter your arrival date (if applicable).
* **Check-out Date**: Enter your departure date (if applicable).
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you plan to pay (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer).
* **Registration Fee**: Enter the amount due (if not paid).
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of your payment (e.g., Paid, Pending, To Be Invoiced).
* **Invoice Address**: If you're paying by invoice, enter your address.
* **Networking Events to Attend**: Choose all the networking events you plan to attend.
* **Interested in Topic Meetups**: Select whether you're interested in topic meetups.
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any special accessibility requirements, enter them here.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Choose your T-Shirt size (if you need one).
* **How did you hear about this Convention**: Select how you became aware of the convention.
* **First-time Attendee**: Select whether this is your first time attending the convention.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any other requirements, enter them here.
* **I accept the terms and conditions**: Select 'True' to confirm you've read and accepted the terms.
* **Registration Date**: Enter the date of your registration.

Note: Ensure you review and submit the form carefully, as it will be used to personalize your experience and provide you with the necessary resources and accommodations.
