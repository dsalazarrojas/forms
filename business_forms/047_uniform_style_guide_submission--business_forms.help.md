# uniform_style_guide_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit uniform style guides for business purposes. It is intended for employees who need to submit their uniform style guides for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Select the relevant options for the fields that require selection (e.g., Style, Colors, Assigned To).
3. Add any additional notes or comments if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Uniform Purpose**: A brief description of the purpose of the uniform style guide.
* **Style**: Select the relevant style for the uniform from the available options.
* **Colors**: Select multiple colors that will be used for the uniform (up to 3).
* **Branding**: A text field for any branding information related to the uniform.
* **Team Size**: The number of team members who will be wearing the uniform.
* **School Name**: The name of the school associated with the uniform.
* **Company Name**: The name of the company associated with the uniform.
* **Department Name**: The name of the department associated with the uniform.
* **Sizing Guidelines**: Any special sizing information for the uniform.
* **Uniform Image**: A text field for uploading or referencing a uniform image.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or notes about the uniform.
* **Email**: The email address of the person submitting the uniform style guide.
* **Phone**: A text field for the person's phone number.
* **Assigned To**: Select the person responsible for the uniform style guide from the available options.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out correctly before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or the relevant department for guidance.
* This form is for submitting uniform style guides only; please do not use it for other purposes.
