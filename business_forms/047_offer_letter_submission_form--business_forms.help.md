# Offer Letter Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees submit their offer letters, which are essential documents that outline the terms and conditions of an employee's employment. The form collects information about the employee's personal details, job title, department, and employment status, as well as the reason for their leave (if applicable). The purpose of this form is to provide a standardized and efficient way to collect offer letter information from employees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information and documents ready before starting the form.
2. Enter your employee name in the `Employee Name` field.
3. Enter your job title in the `Job Title` field.
4. Enter the date you were hired in the `Date of Hire` field.
5. Enter the date you started your job in the `Job Start Date` field.
6. Attach a copy of your offer letter in the `Employee Offer Letter` field.
7. Select your department from the options provided in the `Department` field.
8. Enter the name of your supervisor in the `Supervisor Name` field.
9. Indicate your current employment status in the `Employee Status` field.
10. Enter your job position in the `Job Position` field.
11. If applicable, enter the reason for your leave in the `Reason for Leave` field.
12. Leave the `Manager Name` field blank, as it is not required.
13. Sign the form digitally with your employee signature in the `Employee Signature` field.
14. Sign the form digitally with your supervisor's signature in the `Manager Signature` field.
15. Sign the form digitally with your department's signature in the `Departmental Signature` field.
16. Enter the date in the `Date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Employee Name
* **Employee Name**: This field is where you will enter your full name as an employee.

### 2. Job Title
* **Job Title**: This field is where you will enter your job title or position within the company.

### 3. Date of Hire
* **Date of Hire**: This field is where you will enter the date you were hired by the company.

### 4. Job Start Date
* **Job Start Date**: This field is where you will enter the date you started your job in the company.

### 5. Employee Offer Letter
* **Employee Offer Letter**: This field is where you will attach a copy of your offer letter, which is an essential document that outlines the terms and conditions of your employment.

### 6. Department
* **Department**: This field is where you will select the department you belong to, which can be Accounting, Sales, Marketing, or HR.

### 7. Supervisor Name
* **Supervisor Name**: This field is where you will enter the name of your supervisor.

### 8. Employee Status
* **Employee Status**: This field is where you will indicate your current employment status, which can be Active or Inactive.

### 9. Job Position
* **Job Position**: This field is where you will enter your job position.

### 10. Reason for Leave
* **Reason for Leave**: This field is where you will enter the reason for your leave, if applicable. If not applicable, leave this field blank.

### 11. Manager Name
* **Manager Name**: This field is not required and can be left blank.

### 12. Employee Signature
* **Employee Signature**: This field is where you will sign the form digitally with your employee signature.

### 13. Manager Signature
* **Manager Signature**: This field is where you will sign the form digitally with your supervisor's signature.

### 14. Departmental Signature
* **Departmental Signature**: This field is where you will sign the form digitally with your department's signature.

### 15. Date
* **Date**: This field is where you will enter the current date.
