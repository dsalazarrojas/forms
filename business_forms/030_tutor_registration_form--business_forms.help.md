# tutor_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from individuals who want to become tutors. Please fill out the form with accurate details to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Double-check that your email address is correctly formatted as it will be used as your login username.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as you would like it to appear on official documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as you would like it to appear on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that you will use as your login username. This will be used to send you important information and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number for future reference.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your contact address for future reference.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out the form to ensure a smooth registration process.
* Ensure all fields are filled out correctly to avoid any issues during the registration process.
