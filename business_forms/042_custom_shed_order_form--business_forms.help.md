# custom_shed_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The custom shed order form is used to collect information from customers interested in purchasing a custom-made shed. It helps the business to understand the customer's preferences and requirements for their shed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the size of your shed from the options provided.
2. Choose the material you prefer for your shed from the available options.
3. Select the color for your shed from the options provided.
4. Choose the style of your shed from the available options.
5. Select the type of door you prefer for your shed from the options provided.
6. Select the type of roof you prefer for your shed from the available options.
7. Enter the number of windows you would like to have in your shed.
8. If you have any additional information or specific requests, please provide them in the "Additional information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the size of your shed? (custom_shed_size)**: Please select the size of your shed from the options provided.
* **What material would you prefer? (material)**: Please choose the material you prefer for your shed from the options: Oak, Pine, or Cedar.
* **What color would you prefer? (color)**: Select the color you prefer for your shed from the options: Red, Blue, or Green.
* **What style would you prefer? (style)**: Choose the style of your shed from the options: Modern, Traditional, or Rustic.
* **What type of door would you prefer? (door_style)**: Select the type of door you prefer for your shed from the options: Sliding, Hinged, or Double.
* **What type of roof would you prefer? (roof_style)**: Choose the type of roof you prefer for your shed from the options: Gable, Hip, or Shed.
* **How many windows? (windows)**: Enter the number of windows you would like to have in your shed.
* **Additional information (additional_info)**: Please provide any additional information or specific requests you may have for your shed.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are essential for the business to fulfill your order effectively. Please ensure you complete these fields as accurately as possible.
