# gdpr_breach_reporting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report incidents related to potential GDPR breaches in a company or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant options for each field.
2. Fill in the date in the correct format for the "Incident Date" field.
3. Choose the correct "Report Type" from the options provided.
4. Provide a clear and concise description in the "Description" field.
5. Select the correct "Breach Location" from the options provided.
6. Report the number of people affected by the breach in the "Affected People" field.
7. Report the status of the incident in the "Incident Status" field.
8. Select the category of the incident from the options provided in the "Incident Category" field.
9. Provide additional notes about the incident category in the "Incident Category Note" field.
10. Report the assets affected by the breach in the "Affected Assets" field.
11. Select the type of incident from the options provided in the "Incident Type" field.
12. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
13. Report the location of the incident in the "Incident Location" field.
14. Select the second category of the incident from the options provided in the "Incident Category 2" field.
15. Provide additional notes about the second incident category in the "Incident Category 2 Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (date): The date of the incident.
* **Report Type** (select_one): The type of report.
* **Affected Data** (text): A detailed description of the data affected by the breach.
* **Breach Location** (select_one): The location of the breach.
* **Affected People** (text): The number of people affected by the breach.
* **Incident Status** (select_one): The status of the incident.
* **Incident Category** (select_one): The category of the incident.
* **Incident Category Note** (text): Additional notes about the incident category.
* **Affected Assets** (text): The assets affected by the breach.
* **Incident Type** (select_one): The type of incident.
* **Incident Description** (text): A detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (text): The location of the incident.
* **Incident Category 2** (select_one): The second category of the incident.
* **Incident Category 2 Note** (text): Additional notes about the second incident category.
