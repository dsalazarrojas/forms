# Facility Energy Benchmarking Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Energy Benchmarking Registration form is a crucial tool in tracking and analyzing energy consumption patterns of facilities. It provides us with valuable data to understand and improve energy consumption habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields below. Each field is marked as required, ensuring that we have the necessary information to process your registration. Please make sure to double-check your inputs for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the facility you are registering. Please enter the name of the facility as it appears on official documents or records.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the facility, including any additional details that may be useful in identifying it.
* **Facility Location** (`facility_location`, `text`, required): Provide the specific location of the facility, including any relevant geographical information.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the contact person responsible for the facility.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the program or initiative the facility is associated with.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the program or initiative.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the program or initiative.
* **Building Square Feet** (`building_sqft`, `number`, required): Enter the total square feet of the building.
* **Building Type** (`building_type`, `text`, required): Specify the type of building (e.g. office, residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Building Energy Use** (`building_energy_use`, `text`, required): Describe the primary energy use of the building (e.g. heating, cooling, electricity, etc.).
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the participant involved in the energy benchmarking process.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the participant.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Facility Details** (`facility_details`, `text`, required): Provide any additional details about the facility that may be helpful in understanding its energy consumption habits.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, required): Check the provided terms and conditions to ensure you understand and agree with them before proceeding with your registration.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the input data to ensure a smooth and error-free registration process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for assistance.
