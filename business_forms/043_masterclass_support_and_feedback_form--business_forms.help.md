# Masterclass Support And Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather support and feedback from users for the Masterclass program. Fill out this form to provide detailed descriptions of any issues you're facing and the support you require. You can also share attachments to help us understand your concerns better.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the corresponding fields to help us know who you are and how to get in touch with you.
2. Describe the issue you're facing in the "Issue Description" field, being as detailed as possible.
3. If you have any specific feedback or suggestions, please type them in the "Feedback" field.
4. If you're attached to a file that helps us better understand your concern, upload it in the "Attachment" field.
5. If you want us to follow up with you, choose how you'd like to be contacted from the "Follow Up" field.
6. Finally, select "True" if you need immediate support or "False" if you don't require support at this time.
7. If you'd like to specify a date for follow-up, you can select it from the calendar in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business** (`business_form`, text, optional): 
This field is for you to describe your business context, such as your company or department. This is an optional field and can be left blank if not applicable.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): 
Please fill in your name in this field so we know who you are.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): 
Provide your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): 
Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, optional): 
Describe the issue you're facing, being as detailed as possible. This field is optional but recommended for us to better understand your concern.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): 
If you have any specific feedback or suggestions, please type them in this field. This is an optional field but helps us understand your thoughts better.
* **Attachment** (`attachment_file`, text, optional): 
If you have any attachments, such as screenshots or documents, that can help us understand your issue better, upload them here. This field is optional but recommended if it's applicable.
* **Support** (`support`, select_one, optional): 
Choose "True" if you require immediate support or "False" if you don't need support at this time.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select_multiple, optional): 
Choose how you'd like to be followed up (via phone, email, or mail) if you require support.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): 
If you'd like to specify a date for follow-up, you can choose a date from the calendar provided. This is optional but helps us plan follow-up actions better.
