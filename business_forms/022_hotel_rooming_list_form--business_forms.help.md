<thinking>Hotel Rooming List Form - Help Guide

This form is designed to collect information from hotel guests for rooming purposes. It is essential to ensure that all required fields are completed to ensure accurate and efficient processing of guest requests. This form should not be used to collect sensitive or confidential information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the "Form Name" to help us track and identify each guest's form submission.
2. Enter the "Guest Name" as the name of the guest submitting the form.
3. Enter the "Company" name if the guest is traveling for business purposes.
4. Enter the "Job Title" of the guest.
5. Enter the "Guest Address" where the guest can be reached.
6. Select the "Arrival Date" and "Departure Date" of the guest's stay.
7. Select the "Arrival Time" and "Departure Time" of the guest's stay (if applicable).
8. Enter the "Guest Phone" number where the guest can be reached.
9. Enter the "Guest Email" address for communication.
10. Enter the "Room Number" where the guest will be staying.
11. Enter the "Total Guests" expected to stay in the room.
12. Enter the "Room Rate" for the guest's room.
13. Select additional "Room Services" that the guest requires (e.g., Wi-Fi, TV, etc.).
14. Enter any "Special Requests" the guest may have.
15. Add any additional comments about the guest's stay.
16. Select the team member or person assigned to handle the guest's request.
17. Select the status of the form submission (if applicable).
18. Enter the date and time of the form submission.

* **Form Name**: Enter the name of the form to help us track and identify each guest's submission.
* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of the guest submitting the form.

* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the guest.
* **Guest Address**: Enter the address where the guest can be reached.
* **Arrival Date**: Select the date the guest is expected to arrive.
* **Departure Date**: Select the date the guest is expected to depart.
* **Arrival Time**: Select the time the guest is expected to arrive (if applicable).
* **Departure Time**: Select the time the guest is expected to depart (if applicable).
* **Guest Phone**: Enter the phone number where the guest can be reached.
* **Guest Email**: Enter the email address where the guest can be reached.
* **Room Number**: Enter the room number where the guest will be staying.
* **Total Guests**: Enter the total number of guests expected to stay in the room.
* **Room Rate**: Enter the rate for the guest's room.
* **Room Services**: Select additional services the guest requires (e.g., Wi-Fi, TV, etc.).
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests the guest may have.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the guest's stay.
* **Assigned To**: Select the team member or person assigned to handle the guest's request.
* **Status**: Select the status of the form submission (if applicable).
* **Date**: Enter the date of the form submission.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the form submission.
* **Room Services Options**: Select additional services the guest requires (if applicable).
* **Room Rate Notes**: Enter any notes about the room rate.
* **Room Number Notes**: Enter any notes about the room number.
* **Special Requests Notes**: Enter any notes about the guest's special requests.
* **Assigned To**: Select the team member or person assigned to handle the guest's request.
* **Status**: Select the status of the form submission.

This form should be completed in the following scenarios:
- When a guest is making an inquiry or request for a room.
- When a guest is making a reservation for a room.
- When a team member needs to track and follow up on guest's requests or stay.

Note: This form is for general information collection and tracking purposes. If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact the relevant team member or person assigned to handle the guest's request.
