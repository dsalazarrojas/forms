# Sponsorship Letter Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsorship Letter Submission Form is used to request funding or support for a specific event, project, or non-profit initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the following fields:
	* Organization Name
	* Name of Requester
	* Date of Request
	* Sponsorship Type (select one of the options: Event, Non-Profit, or Business)
	* Email
2. Provide additional information as needed in the following fields:
	* Name of Sponsor
	* Phone Number
	* Reason for Sponsorship Request
	* Sponsorship Letter (attach a file with the letter)
	* Terms and Conditions (attach a file with the terms and conditions)
3. Make sure to review and submit the form before hitting the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Name of Requester** (Text, required): Enter your name as the requester.
* **Date of Request** (Date, required): Enter the date of the sponsorship request.
* **Sponsorship Type** (Select One, required): Select the type of sponsorship request you are making (Event, Non-Profit, or Business).
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter the email address associated with your organization or project.
* **Name of Sponsor** (Text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the sponsor supporting your request.
* **Phone Number** (Number, optional): If applicable, enter the phone number associated with your organization or project.
* **Reason for Sponsorship Request** (Text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your sponsorship request.
* **Sponsorship Letter** (Note, optional): Attach a file with the sponsorship letter, including all necessary details about your request.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Note, optional): Attach a file with the terms and conditions of the sponsorship request.

# Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide or contact our internal teams for clarification.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling in the non-essential fields to provide a clear and comprehensive picture of your request.
