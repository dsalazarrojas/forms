# Competitor Analysis Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and assessment from trainees after a competitor analysis training session. It aims to evaluate their understanding of the training content, the effectiveness of the session, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Trainee Name**, which is the person who attended the training session.
2. Select the **Department/Team** that the trainee belongs to, e.g., Sales, Product, Marketing.
3. Enter the **Date of Training**, when the session occurred.
4. Rate your **Understanding of our Primary Competitor** by selecting one of the options: 1 - No Knowledge, 2, 3, 4, or 5 - Expert Knowledge.
5. Rate your **Understanding of our Unique Selling Proposition (USP) against others** by selecting one of the options: Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, or Unclear.
6. Select your **Confidence in handling competitor-related objections from customers** with one of the options: High Confidence, Moderate Confidence, or Low Confidence.
7. Choose the **most valuable part of the training**, e.g., Battlecards, Live demo, Roleplay.
8. Provide any **competitor or topics we missed** in the text area.
9. Rate the **quality of the training materials (Slides, Handouts)** by selecting one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
10. Select your **Overall satisfaction with the training session** with one of the options: Highly Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
11. Enter the **Date of Feedback**, which is today's date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trainee Name** (`trainee_full_name`, text, required): Enter the person who attended the training session.
* **Department/Team** (`department_team_trainee`, text, required): Select the department or team the trainee belongs to.
* **Date of Training** (`training_date_comp`, date, required): Enter the date when the training session occurred.
* **Understanding of our Primary Competitor** (`knowledge_rating_top_competitor`, select_one, required): Rate your understanding of our primary competitor, selecting one of the options: 1 - No Knowledge, 2, 3, 4, or 5 - Expert Knowledge.
* **Understanding of our Unique Selling Proposition (USP)** (`usp_understanding_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your understanding of our Unique Selling Proposition (USP) against others, selecting one of the options: Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, or Unclear.
* **Confidence in handling competitor-related objections from customers** (`objection_handling_confidence`, select_one, required): Select your confidence in handling competitor-related objections from customers with one of the options: High Confidence, Moderate Confidence, or Low Confidence.
* **Most valuable part of the training** (`most_valuable_training_part`, text, required): Choose the most valuable part of the training, e.g., Battlecards, Live demo, Roleplay.
* **Competitors or topics we missed** (`training_gaps_identified`, text, optional): Provide any competitor or topic suggestions we missed in the text area.
* **Rate the quality of the training materials (Slides, Handouts)** (`training_materials_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the training materials by selecting one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Overall satisfaction with the training session** (`trainee_satisfaction_rating`, select_one, required): Select your overall satisfaction with the training session with one of the options: Highly Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
* **Date of Feedback** (`training_eval_date`, date, required): Enter the date of feedback, which is today's date.
