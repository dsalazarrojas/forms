# Pain Disability Test Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pain Disability Test Form is designed to assess an individual's experience of pain, specifically regarding their perceived level of pain intensity, pain frequency, and related symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question based on your personal experience. The questions are designed to be self-explanatory, so no additional instructions are required. Simply select the answer that best describes your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, text, optional): A brief introduction to get you comfortable with the form.
* **Pain Question 1** (`pain_question_1`, text, optional): Rate your current level of pain intensity on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is no pain and 10 is extreme pain.
* **Pain Question 2** (`pain_question_2`, text, optional): How often do you experience pain, on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is never and 10 is all the time.
* **Pain Question 3** (`pain_question_3`, text, optional): Describe any physical symptoms you experience due to pain.
* **Pain Question 4** (`pain_question_4`, text, optional): Describe any emotional symptoms you experience due to pain.
* **Pain Question 5** (`pain_question_5`, text, optional): Describe any cognitive symptoms you experience due to pain.
* **Pain Question 6** (`pain_question_6`, text, optional): Describe any social symptoms you experience due to pain.
* **Pain Question 7** (`pain_question_7`, text, optional): Describe any work/school related symptoms you experience due to pain.
* **Pain Question 8** (`pain_question_8`, text, optional): Describe any financial symptoms you experience due to pain.
* **Pain Question 9** (`pain_question_9`, text, optional): Describe any other symptoms you experience due to pain.

## Tips
* Please answer each question based on your personal experience.
* If you are unsure about a question, please answer to the best of your ability.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact form@gic.mx.
