# Online Inventory Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a simple and efficient way for customers to place new orders for inventory items. Fill out the form below to ensure accurate and timely processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Company Name (if applicable) in the respective fields.
2.  Provide your valid Email address for contact and communication purposes.
3.  Enter your Contact Number for easy communication.
4.  Select the items you wish to order from the dropdown menu.
5.  Specify the Quantity of each item you want to order.
6.  Enter the Date Ordered and Time Ordered for your convenience.
7.  Add any Comments or special instructions for the order.
8.  Fill in the Shipping Address, Shipping City, Shipping State, and Shipping Zip for the items to be sent.
9.  Enter the Billing Address, Billing City, Billing State, and Billing Zip for payment and invoicing purposes.
10.  Calculate the Total Cost and confirm the Date Received for record-keeping.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Enter your first name as you want it to appear in our records.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name as you want it to appear in our records.
*   **Company Name**: If you are placing an order on behalf of a company, enter the name here.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address for us to contact you.
*   **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number for us to communicate with you about your order.
*   **Order Items**: Select all the items you wish to order from the dropdown menu.
*   **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of each item you wish to order.
*   **Date Ordered**: Enter the date you wish to place the order.
*   **Time Ordered**: Enter the time you wish to place the order.
*   **Comments**: Add any comments or special instructions for your order.
*   **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where you want the items shipped to.
*   **Shipping City**: Enter the city of the shipping address.
*   **Shipping State**: Enter the state of the shipping address.
*   **Shipping Zip**: Enter the zip code of the shipping address.
*   **Billing Address**: Enter the address where you want to receive invoices and payment reminders.
*   **Billing City**: Enter the city of the billing address.
*   **Billing State**: Enter the state of the billing address.
*   **Billing Zip**: Enter the zip code of the billing address.
*   **Total Cost**: Calculate the total cost of your order.
*   **Date Received**: Enter the date you received the order.

This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to use. If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
