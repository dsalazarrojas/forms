# loan_application_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a form for loan applications, used to gather essential information from applicants for the purpose of communication and loan processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the required fields, which are marked as such with an asterisk (\*).
2.  Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your abilities.
3.  Use the provided options for the "contact_frequency" field to select how often you want to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This field is for your contact information, such as your name, address, and phone number.
* **Loan Details** (`loan_details`, text, optional): Enter the specifics of the loan you are applying for, including the loan amount and purpose.
* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, optional): Provide some details about yourself, such as your occupation and income.
* **Documents** (`documents`, text, optional): List any documents you have that are required for the loan application process.
* **Review Details** (`review_details`, text, optional): This field is for any comments or feedback you have on the review process.
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, text, optional): Indicate how often you would like to be contacted, either daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Contact Frequency** (`contact_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the frequency at which you would like to be contacted by the lender, with options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
- You can use the provided options for "contact_frequency" to indicate your preferred contact schedule.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the lender for assistance.
