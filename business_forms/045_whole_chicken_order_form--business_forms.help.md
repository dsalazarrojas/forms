# Whole Chicken Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Whole Chicken Order Form is used to collect information from customers for their whole chicken orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer details, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the type of chicken you would like to order (yes or no).
3. Specify the number of chickens you would like to order.
4. Choose your preferred delivery time (yes or no).
5. Enter your address where the chicken will be delivered.
6. Provide your email address and phone number for communication.
7. Add any notes about your order (optional).
8. Select the payment method for your order (yes or no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (Customer Details, text, optional):
	+ Please enter your name, email, and phone number so we can contact you.
* **Chicken Type** (Chicken Type, select_one, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you would like to order whole chickens.
* **Chicken Count** (Chicken Count, number, optional):
	+ Enter the number of chickens you would like to order.
* **Delivery Time** (Delivery Time, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you prefer to receive your chickens on time.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional):
	+ Enter the address where your chickens will be delivered.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional):
	+ Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional):
	+ Add any notes or comments about your order.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you would like to pay for your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth order processing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the email or phone number provided.
