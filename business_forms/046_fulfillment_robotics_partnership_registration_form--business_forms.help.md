# Fulfillment Robotics Partnership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential partners or partners of Fulfillment Robotics to assess their interest in collaborating with our company. The gathered data will be used to evaluate the suitability of potential partnerships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1. Fill in the General Info section with your basic contact information.
2. Provide your company's details in the Company Info section.
3. Enter the contact person's details in the Contact Person section.
4. Enter the address of your company in the Address section.
5. Enter your email address in the Email section (make sure it's a valid email address).
6. Enter your phone number in the Phone section.
7. Select a date and time for a potential meeting or meeting slot in the Date section.
8. Select a time slot for the potential meeting or meeting slot in the Time section.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the Note section.
10. In the Select One and Select Multiple sections, choose one or multiple options that best describe your company's area of expertise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Fill in your basic contact information, such as name and title.
* **Company Info** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's name and details.
* **Contact Person** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact person's name and details.
* **Address** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's address.
* **Email** (`5`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address of your company's contact person.
* **Phone** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Date** (`7`, `date`, required: false): Select a date for a potential meeting or meeting slot.
* **Time** (`8`, `time`, required: false): Select a time slot for a potential meeting or meeting slot.
* **Note** (`9`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Select One** (`10`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose one option that best describes your company's area of expertise, such as Robotics and Automation, Machine Vision, or Artificial Intelligence.
* **Select Multiple** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Choose one or multiple options that best describe your company's area of expertise.
* **Email 2** (`12`, `email`, required: false): This is a secondary email address for your company's contact person.
* **Phone 2** (`13`, `text`, required: false): This is a secondary phone number for your company's contact person.
* **Date 2** (`14`, `date`, required: false): This is a secondary date for a potential meeting or meeting slot.
* **Time 2** (`15`, `time`, required: false): This is a secondary time slot for a potential meeting or meeting slot.
* **Note 2** (`16`, `note`, required: false): This is a secondary note or comment section.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check that you have not registered with us before.
* In case of any questions or issues, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Please note that internal checks will be performed to avoid duplicate registrations.
