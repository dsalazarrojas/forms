# Research Media Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by researchers who want to inquire about research media, such as images, videos, or documents, to get more information or request access to specific research media.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields.
2. If you have a specific role (e.g., Director, Manager, Researcher) within your organization, select the correct role from the options.
3. Indicate whether or not you have a department that you would like to contact (optional).
4. Specify the type of research media you are inquiring about (yes/no options).
5. Describe the research topic or project you are inquiring about in the Research Topic field.
6. If you want to provide a reason for your contact, you can do so in the Contact Reason field (optional).
7. Finally, choose the method you would like to be contacted (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Researchers Name**: Please fill in your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Department**: If you are a researcher from a specific department, please select it from the options. If not applicable, leave this field empty.
* **Role**: Select your role within your organization (Director, Manager, Researcher, Other).
* **Inquiry Type**: Indicate if you are inquiring about research media that is available (Yes) or not available (No).
* **Research Topic**: Describe the research topic or project you are inquiring about.
* **Contact Reason**: Provide a reason for your contact, if applicable.
* **Contact Method**: Choose the method you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, mail).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have checked that the research media you are inquiring about is not already publicly available.
* If you are inquiring about a specific research project, include all relevant information in the Research Topic field.
* If you have any general inquiries or experience technical difficulties, please contact us through our support channels.
