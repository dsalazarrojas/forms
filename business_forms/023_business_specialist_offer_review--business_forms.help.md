# Business Specialist Offer Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help business specialists review and evaluate offers or proposals. It assesses various aspects of the offer, including alignment with marketing strategy, competitive positioning, profit margin impact, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your reviewer's name in the "Reviewer Name" field (page 1).
2. Provide the date of the review in the "Review Date" field (page 1).
3. Enter the title of the offer or proposal in the "Offer or Proposal Title" field (page 1).
4. Briefly describe the offer or proposal in the "Offer Description" field (page 1).
5. Identify the target audience or segment for the offer (page 2).
6. Specify the offer's value or discount amount (page 2).
7. Determine the offer's validity period (page 2).
8. Assess the offer's alignment with the company's marketing strategy (page 3).
9. Evaluate the offer's competitive positioning (page 3).
10. Consider the expected impact on profit margins (page 3).
11. List any necessary resources for implementation (page 4).
12. Assess the feasibility of implementation (page 4).
13. Identify any risks associated with the offer (page 4).
14. Rate the offer's appeal to customers (page 5).
15. Make a recommendation for proceeding with the offer (page 5).
16. Provide any additional comments or suggestions (page 5).
17. Specify the approval status of the offer (page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, required): Enter your name as the reviewer of this offer.
* **Department** (`reviewer_department`, optional): Identify your department or team.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, required): Enter the date of this review.
* **Offer or Proposal Title** (`offer_title`, required): Name of the offer being reviewed.
* **Offer Description** (`offer_description`, required): Brief description of the offer.
* **Target Audience or Segment** (`target_audience`, optional): Who this offer is for.
* **Offer Value or Discount** (`offer_value`, optional): What is the offer worth or discount amount.
* **Offer Validity Period** (`validity_period`, optional): How long the offer is valid.
* **Alignment with Marketing Strategy** (`marketing_alignment`, required): How well does this offer align with our marketing strategy? Choose from: Excellent Alignment, Good Alignment, Adequate Alignment, Poor Alignment, Not Aligned.
* **Competitive Positioning** (`competitive_positioning`, required): How competitive is this offer? Choose from: Highly Competitive, Competitive, Adequately Competitive, Less Competitive, Not Competitive.
* **Profit Margin Impact** (`profit_margin`, required): Expected impact on profit margins? Choose from: Significantly Positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, Significantly Negative.
* **Resource Requirements** (`resource_requirements`, optional): What resources are needed for implementation.
* **Implementation Feasibility** (`implementation_feasibility`, required): Is this feasible to implement? Choose from: Highly Feasible, Feasible, Moderately Feasible, Difficult, Not Feasible.
* **Risks Identified** (`risks_identified`, optional): List any risks associated with the offer.
* **Customer Appeal Rating** (`customer_appeal`, required): How appealing is this to customers? Choose from: Highly Appealing, Appealing, Moderately Appealing, Not Very Appealing, Unappealing.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, required): Should we proceed with this offer? Choose from: Strongly Recommend, Recommend, Neutral, Do Not Recommend, Strongly Oppose.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, optional): Additional comments or suggestions.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, optional): What is the status of approval? Choose from: Approved, Pending Review, Conditional Approval, Rejected.
