# Procurement Request Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Procurement Request Communication Form is a tool used to communicate with internal stakeholders and customers about procurement requests. Fill out this form to provide details about the items or services being requested, including contact information, delivery dates and times, and other relevant details. This form helps ensure that procurement requests are properly documented and tracked for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Provide the required information in the "Request details" field, if necessary.
* Select the contact information for the individual or department that will be handling the procurement request.
* Choose the item or service being requested from the "Requested item" dropdown menu.
* Enter the purchase order number, quantity, and unit price for the requested item.
* Enter the total cost and delivery date and time for the requested item.
* Enter the contact person's email address, phone number, and name.
* Select the tool assigned to handle the request.
* Enter your name and the date and time of submission.
* Add any relevant comments or notes about the request.
* If attachments are necessary, select them from the "Attachments" dropdown menu.
* Check if you would like to allow attachments to be uploaded.
* If there are any issues with attachment downloads, enter details in the "Attachment download issues" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request details**: A field to provide a brief description of the procurement request.
* **Contact info**: A field to select the individual or department handling the procurement request.
* **Contact person**: The individual or department responsible for handling the procurement request.
* **Requested item**: A dropdown menu to select the item or service being requested.
* **Purchase order number**: A field to enter the purchase order number for the requested item.
* **Quantity**: A field to enter the quantity of the requested item.
* **Unit price**: A field to enter the unit price of the requested item.
* **Total cost**: A field to calculate the total cost based on the quantity and unit price.
* **Delivery date**: A field to enter the delivery date for the requested item.
* **Delivery time**: A field to enter the delivery time for the requested item.
* **Contact email**: A field to enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact phone**: A field to enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact person**: A field to enter the contact person's name.
* **Assigned tool**: A dropdown menu to select the tool assigned to handle the request.
* **Submitter**: A field to enter your name.
* **Submission date**: A field to enter the date and time of submission.
* **Submission time**: A field to enter the submission time.
* **Comments**: A field to add any relevant comments or notes about the request.
* **Attachments**: A field to select any attachments necessary for the request.
* **Attachment uploads**: A field to indicate whether you would like to allow attachment uploads.
* **Attachment comments**: A field to add any comments about the attachments.
* **Attachment notes**: A field to add any notes about the attachments.
* **Attachment downloads**: A field to select any issues with attachment downloads.
* **Attachment upload errors**: A field to enter any errors that occurred during attachment upload.
* **Attachment download issues**: A field to enter any issues that occurred during attachment download.
