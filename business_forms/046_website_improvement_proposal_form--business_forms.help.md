# Website Improvement Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit proposals for website improvement suggestions. Please use it to provide your ideas on how we can improve our website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Be as specific as possible when describing your proposal.
3. Attach any relevant files or documents that support your proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Requirements** (Note): This is a note field where you can provide any additional information that may be relevant to your proposal.
* **Project Name** (*): Enter the name of your project proposal.
* **Project Description** (*): Provide a brief description of your proposal.
* **Project Timeline** (*): Enter the expected completion date or timeline for your proposal.
* **Project Budget** (*): Enter the estimated budget for your proposal.
* **Project Location** (*): Enter the location of the project (e.g. internal, external, etc.).
* **Project Status** (*): Select the current status of your proposal (In Progress, Not Started, On Hold, Completed).
* **Contact Person** (*): Select the person who will be responsible for your proposal (Jim, John, Jane, Joe).
* **Contact Email** (*): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (*): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Project Type** (*): Select the type of project (Website Development, Website Maintenance, Web Design).
* **Submission Notes** (*): Enter any additional notes about your proposal.
* **Project Urgency** (*): Select the urgency level of your proposal (High, Medium, Low).
* **Project Priority** (*): Select the priority level of your proposal (High, Medium, Low).
* **Website URL** (*): Enter the URL of the website related to your proposal (if applicable).
* **Project Notes** (*): Enter any additional comments or notes about your proposal.
* **Project Files** (*): Upload any relevant files or documents that support your proposal.
* **Project File Attachment**: This field is not required and is only used for optional file attachments.
* **Submission Notes 2** (*): Enter any final comments or notes about your submission.
