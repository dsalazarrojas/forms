# Home Damage Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Damage Incident Form is used to document home damage incidents in a structured and consistent manner. This form captures essential information about the incident, including the date and time it occurred, the location, type of damage, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Complete the form in a step-by-step manner**. Start by filling in the mandatory fields, ensuring all required fields are accurate and complete.
2. **Enter the incident date and time**. Select the date and time the incident occurred using the date and time pickers.
3. **Specify the location**. Enter the specific location where the incident occurred.
4. **Identify the type of damage**. Select the type of damage from the options provided.
5. **Provide a detailed description** of the damage incident.
6. **Enter the damage amount**. Enter the monetary value of the damage.
7. **Enter contact information**. Provide the contact name and phone number.
8. **Enter contact email** (optional). If the contact has an email address, enter it here.
9. **Attach an image (optional)**. If an image is available, upload it to provide visual evidence.
10. **Specify the incident status**. Select whether the incident is active or inactive.
11. **Specify follow-up status**. Select whether follow-up is active or inactive.
12. **Enter notes** (optional). Add any additional information or comments about the incident.
13. **Enter incident ID**. Select whether the incident is active or inactive.
14. **Assign a tool**. Select the tool assigned to the incident.
15. **Enter updated information** (optional). If you updated the form, enter your name and the updated time.
16. **Enter created information**. Enter your name and the created time.
17. **Enter form ID**. Enter the form's ID.
18. **Specify the category**. Select the category of the incident.
19. **Assign a tool name and ID** (optional). If a tool was assigned, enter its name and ID.
20. **Specify the form version**. Select whether the form is active or inactive.
