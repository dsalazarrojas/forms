# regional_operations_office_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The regional_operations_office_contact_form is designed to collect contact information and notes for regional office locations, enabling users to manage office details in a centralized location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the location of the office you wish to input information about.
2. Choose the region associated with the office.
3. Enter the contact person's name and email address.
4. Input the contact person's phone number.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the office or region.
6. Enter any notes about the office location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **office_location (Text)**: Enter the name or address of the office location.
* **region (Select One)**: Choose the region associated with the office from the available options.
* **contact_person (Text)**: Enter the name of the person to contact for the office.
* **contact_email (Email)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone (Text)**: Input the phone number of the contact person.
* **contact_note (Note)**: Add any additional information or comments about the contact person.
* **office_note (Note)**: Add any notes or comments about the office location.
* **region_note (Note)**: Add any notes or comments about the region associated with the office.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the required fields for accurate information submission.
- Review and verify the information entered before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
