# Production Capacity Expansion Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Production Capacity Expansion Request Form is used to request an increase in production capacity. It is essential to fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure your request is processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the details of your request in the "request_details" field.
2. Specify who made the request in the "requested_by" field.
3. Enter the amount of capacity increase you are requesting in the "increase" field.
4. Provide a justification for your request in the "justification" field.
5. Choose the status of your request in the "request_status" field.
6. Date and time of when the request is made in the "date" and "time" fields.
7. Add any additional comments about your request in the "comments" field.
8. Specify the approval status of your request in the "approvals" field (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **request_details**: A brief description of the reasons for your production capacity expansion request. Please provide detailed information about the capacity increase you are seeking.
* **requested_by**: The name of the person or department making this request.
* **increase**: The amount of production capacity you are requesting to be added. Enter a numerical value (e.g., 10, 20, etc.).
* **justification**: A clear and concise explanation for why this production capacity increase is necessary.
* **request_status**: The current status of your request (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected, etc.).
* **date**: The date when the request is made.
* **time**: The time when the request is made.
* **comments**: Any additional information or comments about your request.
* **approvals**: The approvals required for this request (e.g., Manager, Team Leader, etc.).
