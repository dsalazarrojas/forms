# Industrial Hazard Control Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Industrial Hazard Control Report Form is used to document and track industrial hazards and incidents that occur on site. This information helps our team identify and mitigate potential safety risks, ensuring a safer working environment for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the necessary information in each field, making sure to fill out all required fields.
2. Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form, as this information helps us understand the hazard or incident more effectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`employee`, text, required: false): Enter the employee's name who reported the hazard or incident.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter the department where the hazard or incident occurred.
* **Hazard** (`hazard`, text, required: false): Describe the hazard or incident.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the hazard or incident occurred.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the hazard or incident.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the hazard or incident.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, required: false): Enter a contact person's name and phone number for further investigation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required: false): Select the person who will investigate and mitigate the hazard or incident.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the tool or equipment used to mitigate the hazard or incident.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional information about the hazard or incident.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required: false): If you have any supporting files or documents related to the hazard or incident, upload them here.

## Tips

* Always fill out the form for every hazard or incident that occurs on site.
* Make sure to be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
