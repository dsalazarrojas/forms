# digitalEngagementPlatformApplicationForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals applying to the digital engagement platform. It will be used to create a user profile and may be used for marketing and analytics purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the date of birth field (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
6. Enter your address in the address field.
7. Select your employment status (Active or Inactive).
8. Indicate whether you hold a job title (Yes or No).
9. Indicate whether you belong to an industry (Yes or No).
10. Indicate whether you work as an independent contractor (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address in this field. Please note that your email will be used as a unique identifier.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter your address in this field.
* **Employment Status** (select multiple, required): Select your employment status (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Title** (select one, required): Indicate whether you hold a job title (Yes or No).
* **Industry** (select one, required): Indicate whether you belong to an industry (Yes or No).
* **Job Type** (select one, required): Indicate whether you work as an independent contractor (Yes or No).
