# B2B Contract Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The B2B Contract Agreement Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a business-to-business partnership. It's essential to create this form to establish a clear understanding between the parties involved, ensuring a smooth and successful collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the company name and primary contact person's name.
2. Enter the contact email address for contract communications.
3. Provide the contact phone number for contract queries (optional).
4. Specify the effective date and end date of the agreement (if applicable).
5. Describe the services to be provided under the agreement.
6. Choose the payment terms (e.g., Net 30, Net 45, Upon receipt, or Milestone-based).
7. Select whether the confidentiality agreement is included (Yes or No).
8. Add any additional notes or instructions for signing the contract (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the legal name of the company entering into this agreement.
* **Primary contact name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person signing this contract on behalf of the company.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address for contract-related communications.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number for contract queries (if applicable).
* **Contract start date** (`contract_start_date`, date, required): Enter the effective date of the agreement.
* **Contract end date** (`contract_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the agreement will end (leave blank for ongoing agreements).
* **Services to be provided** (`services_description`, text, required): Describe the services or scope of work to be provided under the agreement.
* **Payment terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, required): Select the payment terms for the services provided (e.g., Net 30, Net 45, Upon receipt, or Milestone-based).
* **Confidentiality agreement included** (`confidentiality_clause`, select_one, required): Select whether the standard confidentiality agreement is included (Yes or No).
* **Signatures and notes** (`signatures_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or instructions for signing the contract (if applicable).
