# quarterly_business_review_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The quarterly business review form is used to collect information on a business's quarterly review process. This includes details on review date, client name, review topic, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the review date for the current quarter.
2. Enter the client's name.
3. Choose the review period from the available options.
4. Select relevant review topics.
5. Enter the review outcome.
6. Provide review recommendations.
7. Outline the next steps.
8. Add any review comments.
9. Enter the business lead's name.
10. Enter the business email address.
11. Provide the business address.
12. Enter the contact name.
13. Enter the contact phone number.
14. Enter the contact email address.
15. Choose the review frequency.
16. Enter the assigned tool.
17. Enter the form ID (if necessary).
18. Enter the created by name.
19. Enter the form category.
20. Enter the form description.
21. Enter the output file (if applicable).
22. Choose the form status.
23. Enter the form version.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, **required: false**): This is the date for the current quarter's review. Please select the date when the review will take place.
  - **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, **required: false**): This is the name of the client.
  - **Review Period** (`review_period`, `select_one`, **required: false**): This is the period for the review, which can be Quarter 1, 2, 3, or 4.
  - **Review Topic** (`review_topic`, `select_multiple`, **required: false**): Select one or more relevant topics for the review, such as Business Goals, Business Strategies, Client Feedback, or Goal Alignment.
  - **Review Outcome** (`review_outcome`, `text`, **required: false**): This is the expected outcome of the review.
  - **Review Recommendations** (`review_recommendations`, `text`, **required: false**): Provide any recommendations that can be implemented.
  - **Review Next Steps** (`review_next_steps`, `text`, **required: false**): Outline the next steps for the review.
  - **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, `note`, **required: false**): Add any comments about the review.
  - **Business Lead** (`business_lead`, `text`, **required: false**): This is the name of the business lead or contact person.
  - **Business Email** (`business_email`, `email`, **required: false**): Enter the business email address.
  - **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter the business address.
  - **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, **required: false**): This is the contact person's name.
  - **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, **required: false**): This is the contact phone number.
  - **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, **required: false**): Enter the contact email address.
  - **Review Frequency** (`review_frequency`, `text`, **required: false**): Choose the frequency of the review.
  - **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter the tool assigned for the review.
  - **Form ID** (`form_id`, `number`, **required: false**): Enter the form ID.
  - **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter the name of the person who created this form.
  - **Form Category** (`form_category`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter the form category.
  - **Form Description** (`form_description`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter a description of the form.
  - **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter the output file (if applicable).
  - **Form Status** (`form_status`, `select_one`, **required: false**): Choose the status of the form, which can be Active, Inactive, or Disabled.
  - **Form Version** (`form_version`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter the form version.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you select the correct review date and period for the current quarter.
- Double-check the review topics to ensure they are relevant to the review.
- Enter the business lead's and contact person's name correctly.
- Enter the correct business and contact email addresses.
- Enter the correct business and contact phone numbers.
- Double-check the form status to ensure it is Active, Inactive, or Disabled.
