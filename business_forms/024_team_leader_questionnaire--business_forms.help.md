# Team Leader Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help team leaders evaluate their team's performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer based on your experience and knowledge of your team.
2. Choose the answers that best fit your team's situation.
3. Be honest and thorough in your answers.
4. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **team_leadership** (1): This question is an open-ended text field where you can describe your team leadership approach.
* **team_decision_making** (2): This question asks you to select all the ways in which your team makes decisions. Choose all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **team_conflict_resolution** (3): This question asks you to select the best approach to resolving conflicts within your team. Choose one:
	+ Collaborative approach
	+ Assertive approach
	+ Avoidant approach
* **team_motivation** (4): This question asks you to select all the ways in which your team is motivated. Choose all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **team_emotional_intelligence** (5): This question is an open-ended text field where you can describe your team's emotional intelligence.
* **team_communication_skills** (6): This question asks you to select the level of communication skills your team has. Choose one:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Poor
* **team_workload_management** (7): This question is an open-ended text field where you can describe how your team manages its workload.
* **team_dynamics** (8): This question asks you to select all the ways in which your team's dynamics are managed. Choose all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **team_leadership_style** (9): This question asks you to select the leadership style you use with your team. Choose one:
	+ Autocratic approach
	+ Democratic approach
	+ Laissez-faire approach
* **team_motivation** (10): This question is an open-ended text field where you can describe your team's motivation.
* **team_conflict_resolution** (11): This question is an open-ended text field where you can describe how your team resolves conflicts.
* **team_workload** (12): This question asks you to input the current team workload.
* **team_satisfaction** (13): This question asks you to select the level of team satisfaction. Choose one:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **team_leadership_skills** (14): This question is an open-ended text field where you can describe your team leadership skills.
* **team_leadership_experience** (15): This question is an open-ended text field where you can describe your team leadership experience.
* **team_leadership_development** (16): This question is an open-ended text field where you can describe your team's leadership development.
