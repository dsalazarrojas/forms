# service_provider_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details from service providers, including provider information, business details, contact information, and permission for data collection.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2.  Fill out the form by providing the requested information in the relevant fields.
3.  Ensure that you have filled out all the required fields before submitting the form.
4.  Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **provider_info** (Text, Optional): This field is for you to enter your service provider company or individual name, as it appears on your business cards or public records.
*   **business_info** (Text, Optional): This field is for you to provide a brief description of your business, including your services offered and relevant details.
*   **contact_info** (Text, Optional): This field is for you to provide your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and address, so that customers or clients can reach out to you.
*   **permission_info** (Text, Optional): This field is for you to grant permission for the data collection and use of your information. Please review the terms of service before granting permission.

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
*   If you are unsure about any field, you can contact the form creators for clarification.
*   Ensure that you have permission from your employer or company to complete this form.
*   Review the terms of service before granting permission for data collection.
