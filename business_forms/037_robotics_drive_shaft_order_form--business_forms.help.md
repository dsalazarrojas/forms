# robotics_drive_shaft_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "robotics_drive_shaft_order_form" is a form used to order custom drive shafts for robotics projects. The form collects information about the customer, their preferred shaft type, length, and material, and payment details. It's designed to guide the user through the ordering process, ensuring accurate and efficient transmission of the required information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide a brief description of the project and your company in the "form_info" field.
2.  Enter your customer's information, including their name and a phone number, in the "customer_info" field.
3.  Select the desired shaft type from the options provided in the "shaft_type" field.
4.  Enter the required shaft length in the "shaft_length" field.
5.  Enter the desired shaft diameter in the "shaft_diameter" field.
6.  Select the material type(s) for the shaft from the options provided in the "material_type" field. You can choose one or multiple materials.
7.  Enter your payment information in the "payment_info" field.
8.  Provide your contact information, including an email address, in the "contact_info" field.
9.  Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
10. If you have any special instructions or requests, enter them in the "special_instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Info (text)**: A brief description of your project and your company.
* **Customer Info (number)**: Enter your customer's name and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Shaft Type (Select One)**: Choose from the options - Cycloidal Shafts, Helical Shafts, or Planetary Shafts.
* **Shaft Length (number)**: Enter the required shaft length.
* **Shaft Diameter (number)**: Enter the desired shaft diameter.
* **Material Type (Select Multiple)**: Choose from the options - Aluminum, Steel, or Copper. You can select one or multiple materials.
* **Payment Info (text)**: Enter your payment details, such as your bank account or credit card number.
* **Contact Info (email)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Special Instructions (text)**: Enter any special instructions or requests you may have.
* **Confirm Shaft Type (Select One)**: Confirm the selected shaft type.
* **Confirm Material Type (Select Multiple)**: Confirm the selected material type.
 
Note that fields marked as required must be completed, while non-required fields are optional.
