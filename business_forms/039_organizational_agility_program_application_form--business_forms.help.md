# Organizational Agility Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Agility Program Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants about their company, project, and goals in relation to the Organizational Agility Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Application Form section with a brief description of your application.
2.  Provide your details in the Applicant Details section, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
3.  Enter your company's information in the Company Information section.
4.  Describe your project's objectives, scope, and goals in the Project Information section.
5.  Provide technical details about your project in the Technical Information section.
6.  Describe your project's goals and objectives in the Project Goals section.
7.  Identify any risks or challenges associated with your project in the Risk Factors section.
8.  Provide a project timeline in the Timeline section.
9.  Leave comments and feedback for the reviewer in the Review section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Application Form** (`application_form`, `text`, `no`): A section for the applicant to describe their application. Enter a brief description of your application.
*   **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, `text`, `no`): A section where the applicant can provide their name, contact information, and any other relevant details. Enter your name and contact information.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, `no`): A section for the applicant to provide their contact information. Enter your contact information.
*   **Company Information** (`company_info`, `text`, `no`): A section for the applicant to provide their company's name and details. Enter your company's name and details.
*   **Project Information** (`project_info`, `text`, `no`): A section where the applicant can describe their project's objectives, scope, and goals. Enter your project's objectives, scope, and goals.
*   **Technical Information** (`technical_info`, `text`, `no`): A section for the applicant to provide technical details about their project. Enter technical details about your project.
*   **Project Goals** (`project_goals`, `text`, `no`): A section where the applicant can describe their project's goals and objectives. Enter your project's goals and objectives.
*   **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, `text`, `no`): A section where the applicant can identify any risks or challenges associated with their project. Enter any risks or challenges associated with your project.
*   **Timeline** (`timeline`, `text`, `no`): A section where the applicant can provide a project timeline. Enter your project's timeline.
*   **Review** (`review`, `text`, `no`): A section for comments and feedback from the reviewer. Leave comments and feedback for the reviewer.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all sections are filled out accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful application
*   Review the form carefully to ensure a proper submission
*   Be honest and clear in your application to increase the chances of acceptance
