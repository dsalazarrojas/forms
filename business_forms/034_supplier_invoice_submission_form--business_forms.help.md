# Supplier Invoice Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Supplier Invoice Submission Form is a tool for suppliers to submit their invoices to the company for payment. This form helps the company track and manage invoices submitted by suppliers, making it easier to process payments and maintain accurate financial records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please provide the following information:
	* **Supplier Invoice Submission Form**: This is a summary of the form, which is not a field for input.
2. **Supplier Name**: Enter your company name or the name of your business. This field is optional.
3. **Supplier Number**: Enter your supplier number or ID, if applicable.
4. **Invoice Date**: Select the date the invoice is for. If you're unsure, leave it blank.
5. **Payment Terms**: Choose how you'd like to be paid from the options provided. This field is not required.
6. **Supplier Address**: Enter your business address. This field is optional.
7. **Invoice Number**: Enter the invoice number or reference number. This field is optional.
8. **Supplier Contact Info**: Provide any additional contact information or notes about your company. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Invoice Submission Form**: This is a summary of the form, which is not a field for input.
* **Supplier Name**: A text field to enter your company name or business name.
* **Supplier Number**: A text field for your supplier number or ID, if applicable.
* **Invoice Date**: A date field to select the invoice date.
* **Payment Terms**: A select multiple field with options for payment terms (Payment Term A, Payment Term B, Payment Term C).
* **Supplier Address**: A text field for your business address.
* **Invoice Number**: A text field to enter the invoice number or reference number.
* **Supplier Contact Info**: A text field for any additional contact information or notes about your company.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your invoice.
* If you're unsure about any field, it's okay to leave it blank, and we'll try our best to assist you.
* You can contact us at `form@gic.mx` if you have any questions or need further assistance.
