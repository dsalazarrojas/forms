# creative_agency_contract_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant for creative agencies to extend their contracts with clients, helping to discuss and possibly modify existing agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information about the extension request.
2. Fill in the "extension_request" field with a clear and concise description of the extension.
3. In the "contract_terms" field, provide a brief summary of the changes needed to be made to the contract.
4. For the "client_name" field, enter the name of the client related to the extension request.
5. In the "agency_terms" field, enter any additional terms or conditions the agency wants to impose.
6. The "deliverables" field is used to outline the specific items that will be added to or modified in the contract.
7. If necessary, enter the "review_date" field with a specific date when the revised contract will be reviewed and discussed with the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **extension_request** (extension_request, text, optional): A clear and concise description of the extension request.
* **contract_terms** (contract_terms, text, optional): A brief summary of the changes needed to be made to the contract.
* **client_name** (client_name, text, optional): The name of the client related to the extension request.
* **agency_terms** (agency_terms, text, optional): Any additional terms or conditions the agency wants to impose.
* **deliverables** (deliverables, text, optional): The specific items that will be added to or modified in the contract.
* **review_date** (review_date, date, optional): A specific date when the revised contract will be reviewed and discussed with the client.
