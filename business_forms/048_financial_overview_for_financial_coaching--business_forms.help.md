# Financial Overview For Financial Coaching - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about your financial situation for financial coaching purposes. It will help the financial coach understand your income, expenses, financial goals, and other relevant details to provide personalized advice and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and the questions being asked.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the provided options for select fields, selecting the most appropriate answer for each question.
4. Do not duplicate efforts or provide redundant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Income Statement** (`income_statement`, text, required: false)
Provide a detailed description of your income, including your salary or wages, investments, and any other sources of income. Be specific about the amounts and frequencies of each income source.
* **Expense Statement** (`expense_statement`, text, required: false)
Describe your expenses, including regular bills, rent, utilities, and other recurring expenses. Be detailed about the amounts and frequencies of each expense.
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`, text, required: false)
Specify your short-term and long-term financial goals, such as saving for a down payment on a house, paying off debt, or investing in a specific asset class. Be specific about your goals and timelines.
* **Financial Instruments** (`financial_instruments`, select_multiple, required: false)
Indicate which financial instruments you are currently using or plan to use for your financial goals, such as stocks, bonds, or mutual funds. Select all that apply.
* **Financial Instruments List** (`financial_instruments_list`, select_one, required: false)
Choose one primary financial instrument that you are most interested in for your financial goals.
* **Assets** (`assets`, text, required: false)
List your assets, including cash, savings, investments, and other valuable items. Be specific about the amounts and types of each asset.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when providing information in this form.
* Do not hesitate to ask for clarification if you are unsure about any question or field.
* This form is intended to be completed by one person at a time, so please ensure you are providing information about yourself and your situation only.
* If you need assistance or have questions, contact the relevant authorities or supervisors for guidance.
