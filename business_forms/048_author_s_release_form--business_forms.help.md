# Author's Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the necessary information for an author to release their work to a publishing or media organization. It collects details such as release date, author's name and contact information, publication medium and date, copyright ownership, and other publishing agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow the instructions below to complete the form:

- Start by filling in your release date.
- Ensure that you have entered your full name as the author.
- Provide your valid email address and phone number for contact.
- Enter your pen name or publishing name, if different from your legal name.
- Fill in the title of the work being released.
- Provide a brief description of the work.
- Select the type of work (e.g. article, story, poetry, etc.).
- Choose the publication medium (e.g. print, online, magazine, etc.).
- Enter the name of the publishing organization.
- Specify the planned publication date.
- Select who will own the copyright.
- Indicate whether this is an exclusive publication or not.
- Determine the republication and reuse rights.
- Decide on translation rights.
- Specify adaptation and derivative work rights.
- Indicate if the author is being paid and the amount of payment.
- Choose how the author will be credited.
- Determine if moral rights are reserved.
- Select the indemnification clause.
- Specify the termination rights of this agreement.
- Confirm that you authorize this release.
- If applicable, add any additional conditions or restrictions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Release Date (`release_date`)
**Release Date** (`release_date`, `date`, `required`): This is the date on which this release agreement takes effect.

*   Please note that you should enter the date on which this agreement is signed.

### 2. Author Full Name (`author_name`)
**Author Full Name** (`author_name`, `text`, `required`): This is your legal full name as the author.

*   Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### 3. Author Email (`author_email`)
**Author Email** (`author_email`, `email`, `required`): This is your email address for contact.

*   Ensure that this is a valid and active email address where you can be reached.

### 4. Author Phone (`author_phone`)
**Author Phone** (`author_phone`, `text`, `required`): This is your contact phone number.

*   Please enter your valid phone number where you can be contacted.

### 5. Pen Name or Publishing Name (`pen_name`)
**Pen Name or Publishing Name** (`pen_name`, `text`, `optional`): If your pen name or publishing name is different from your legal name.

*   Please enter your pen name or publishing name if it is not your legal name.

### 6. Work Title (`work_title`)
**Work Title** (`work_title`, `text`, `required`): This is the title of the work being released.

*   Please enter the title of the work that will be released to the publishing organization.

### 7. Work Description (`work_description`)
**Work Description** (`work_description`, `text`, `required`): This is a brief description of the work being released.

*   Please provide a short description of the work to help the publishing organization understand it.

### 8. Type of Work (`work_type`)
**Type of Work** (`work_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the type of work being released (e.g. article, story, poetry, etc.).

*   Please select the appropriate type of work being released to the publishing organization.

### 9. Publication Medium (`publication_medium`)
**Publication Medium** (`publication_medium`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the medium of publication (e.g. print, online, magazine, etc.).

*   Please select the medium in which the work will be published.

### 10. Publishing Organization Name (`publishing_organization`)
**Publishing Organization Name** (`publishing_organization`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the publishing organization.

*   Please enter the name of the publishing organization handling the release of your work.

### 11. Planned Publication Date (`publication_date`)
**Planned Publication Date** (`publication_date`, `date`, `required`): This is the planned date of publication.

*   Please enter the date when the work will be published.

### 12. Copyright Ownership (`copyright_ownership`)
**Copyright Ownership** (`copyright_ownership`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose who will own the copyright.

*   Please select who will own the rights to the work being released.

### 13. Exclusive Publication Rights (`exclusive_publication`)
**Exclusive Publication Rights** (`exclusive_publication`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate whether this is an exclusive publication.

*   Please select if this is an exclusive publication with one organization.

### 14. Republication and Reuse Rights (`republication_rights`)
**Republication and Reuse Rights** (`republication_rights`, `select_one`, `required`): Determine the republication and reuse rights.

*   Please decide on the republication and reuse rights for your work.

### 15. Translation Rights (`translation_rights`)
**Translation Rights** (`translation_rights`, `select_one`, `optional`): Decide on translation rights.

*   Please select if translation rights are applicable and if so, what level of control you want.

### 16. Adaptation and Derivative Works (`adaptation_rights`)
**Adaptation and Derivative Works** (`adaptation_rights`, `select_one`, `optional`): Specify adaptation and derivative work rights.

*   Please determine if adaptation and derivative work rights are applicable.

### 17. Compensation or Payment (`compensation_payment`)
**Compensation or Payment** (`compensation_payment`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate if you are being compensated or not.

*   Please select if you are being paid for your work.

### 18. Payment Amount (`payment_amount`)
**Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, `optional`): Enter the payment amount, if applicable.

*   Please enter the payment amount, if applicable.

### 19. Author Credit/Byline (`author_credit`)
**Author Credit/Byline** (`author_credit`, `select_one`, `required`): Decide on how the author will be credited.

*   Please select how you want to be credited in the publication.

### 20. Moral Rights Reserved (`moral_rights`)
**Moral Rights Reserved** (`moral_rights`, `select_one`, `optional`): Determine if moral rights are reserved.

*   Please select if moral rights are reserved.

### 21. Indemnification Clause (`indemnification`)
**Indemnification Clause** (`indemnification`, `select_one`, `optional`): Specify the indemnification clause.

*   Please select the indemnification clause.

### 22. Termination Rights (`termination_clause`)
**Termination Rights** (`termination_clause`, `select_one`, `required`): Determine the termination rights of this agreement.

*   Please select the termination rights of this agreement.

### 23. Author Authorization (`author_signature`)
**Author Authorization** (`author_signature`, `select_one`, `required`): Confirm that you authorize this release.

*   Please confirm that you authorize this release.

### 24. Additional Conditions or Restrictions (`additional_conditions`)
**Additional Conditions or Restrictions** (`additional_conditions`, `text`, `optional`): Add any special requirements.

*   Please enter any additional conditions or restrictions if applicable.
