# Application For Computer Operator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and professional data from individuals applying for a computer operator role.

## How To Complete This Form
- Enter your full name in the "Applicant Name" field.
- Enter a valid email address in the "Email Address" field.
- Enter a phone number in the "Phone Number" field, but this is not required.
- Describe your key qualifications, certifications, or training relevant to the role in the "Qualifications" field.
- Enter the whole number of years of experience you have in similar roles in the "Years of Experience" field.
- Specify your technical skills, including operating systems, software, and hardware in the "Technical Skills" field.
- Select your shift availability (Day, Night, Rotating, or Any) in the "Shift Availability" field.
- Provide two professional references (name, title, and contact information) in the "References" field.
- If you have uploaded a resume, mention the filename in the "Resume Filename" field.
- Add any additional notes that are relevant to your application in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as per your legal documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter a phone number that can be used for contact purposes.
* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, text, required): Describe your key qualifications, certifications, or training relevant to the role.
* **Years of Experience** (`experience_years`, number, required): Enter the whole number of years of experience you have in similar roles.
* **Technical Skills** (`technical_skills`, text, required): Specify your technical skills, including operating systems, software, and hardware.
* **Shift Availability** (`shift_availability`, select_one, required): Select your shift availability (Day, Night, Rotating, or Any).
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide two professional references (name, title, and contact information).
* **Resume Filename** (`resume_filename`, text, optional): If you have uploaded a resume, mention the filename.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes that are relevant to your application.
