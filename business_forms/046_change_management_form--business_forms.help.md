# Change Management Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used for requesting and tracking changes within the organization. It gathers information about the change, its priority, and its approval status. It helps ensure that changes are properly assessed, approved, and implemented.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Enter the request date and the date of approval.
3. Provide the full name, email, and phone number of the person requesting the change.
4. Select the department and category of the change.
5. Describe the change, its priority, and its expected benefits.
6. Identify the affected systems, processes, and stakeholders.
7. Specify the resources and estimated cost of the change.
8. Outline the implementation timeline, risks, and mitigation plan.
9. Provide the success metrics and rollback plan.
10. Enter the current approval status and the name of the approving authority.
11. Sign the form with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, required): The date when the change request was submitted.
* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): The full name of the person requesting the change.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, `email`, required): The email address of the requester for correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, `text`, optional): The contact phone number of the requester.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): The department of the requester.
* **Change Title** (`change_title`, `text`, required): A brief title for the change.
* **Change Description** (`change_description`, `text`, required): A detailed description of the proposed change.
* **Change Category** (`change_category`, `select_one`, required): The type of change (e.g., process improvement, technology change).
* **Change Priority** (`change_priority`, `select_one`, required): The priority level of the change (e.g., critical, high, medium, low).
* **Business Justification** (`business_justification`, `text`, required): The reason why the change is needed.
* **Expected Benefits** (`expected_benefits`, `text`, required): The expected benefits of the change.
* **Affected Systems** (`affected_systems`, `text`, optional): The systems that will be affected by the change.
* **Affected Processes** (`affected_processes`, `text`, optional): The processes that will be affected by the change.
* **Key Stakeholders** (`stakeholders`, `text`, required): The people who will be impacted by the change.
* **Resource Requirements** (`resource_requirements`, `text`, optional): The resources needed for the change.
* **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, `text`, optional): The approximate cost of the change.
* **Implementation Timeline** (`implementation_timeline`, `text`, required): The timeline for implementing the change.
* **Risks Identified** (`risks_identified`, `text`, optional): The potential risks of the change.
* **Mitigation Plan** (`mitigation_plan`, `text`, optional): The plan to mitigate the risks.
* **Rollback Plan** (`rollback_plan`, `text`, optional): The plan to roll back the change if needed.
* **Success Metrics** (`success_metrics`, `text`, optional): The metrics to measure the success of the change.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, optional): The current status of approval (e.g., pending review, under evaluation, approved).
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, `text`, optional): The name of the approving authority.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, `date`, optional): The date of approval.
* **Requester Signature** (`requester_signature`, `text`, required): The full name of the person requesting the change.

Note: Fields marked as "optional" can be filled out if relevant, but are not required.
