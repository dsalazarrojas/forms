# Interview Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interview Booking Form is a tool used to book and plan interviews. It allows users to make informed decisions about the need for interviews and gather essential information to facilitate smooth and efficient interactions with candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the desired interview options from the "Select Interview" page.
2.  Provide necessary candidate details on the "Candidate details" page.
3.  Fill in the "Interview Details" page with relevant information.
4.  Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Select Interview** (Select Multiple, required: false): Choose whether an interview is desired or not.
*   **Candidate details** (Text, required: false): Enter the candidate's name, phone number, or any other relevant details.
*   **Interview Details** (Text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the interview or any additional information required.
*   **Submit** (Text, required: false): Complete the form to initiate the booking process.

## Tips
*   Please ensure you've made an informed decision about the need for an interview before proceeding.
*   Be as specific as possible when providing candidate and interview details to facilitate efficient communication.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required information is included.
