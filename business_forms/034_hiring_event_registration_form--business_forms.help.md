# Hiring Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for business or organization registration for a hiring event. Please fill it out accurately to ensure you don't miss any necessary details for the event you're registering for.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
2. Review each field carefully to make sure it's relevant to the event you're registering for.
3. Use the available space for any additional information that's not covered by the existing fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Details** (`Business Details`, `text`, required/optional): Fill this field with a brief description of your organization or event. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Business Profile** (`Business Profile`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of your organization or event.
* **Workforce** (`Workforce`, `text`, required/optional): If your organization or event has a specific workforce (e.g., number of employees), fill this field with the relevant information.
* **Business Plans** (`Business Plans`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your organization or event's plans, especially those related to the hiring event.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required/optional): Fill this with your organization's or event's contact information for communication and follow-up.
* **Business Address** (`Business Address`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your organization's or event's physical address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the appropriate option for whether you have a business phone number or not.
* **Email** (`Email Address`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your organization's or event's email address.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a specific job title for the event, fill it in here.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
* Be as detailed as possible, especially in the business details section.
* Review each field to ensure it's correctly filled out.
