# Product Catalog - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Catalog form is used to manage product information, including product details, shipping options, and inventory management.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, navigate to the "Product Page" section, where you can add or edit product information.
2.  Fill out the required fields, such as product name, description, and price.
3.  Select the product tags and shipping options as needed.
4.  Set the product status and availability.
5.  Manage the product inventory and reorder level.
6.  Add shipping estimates and notes as necessary.
7.  Review and save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Page** (text): Enter the product name and description.
* **Product Details** (note): Add any additional information about the product.
* **Product Images** (text): Add images of the product.
* **Product Videos** (text): Add videos about the product.
* **Product Tags** (select one): Choose whether the product has tags.
* **Product Price** (number): Enter the product price.
* **Product Weight** (number): Enter the product weight.
* **Product Dimensions** (number): Enter the product dimensions.
* **Product Weight Units** (select one): Choose the units for the product weight.
* **Product Shipping** (select multiple): Select the shipping options for the product.
* **Product Shipping Type** (select one): Choose the type of shipping for the product.
* **Product Shipping Address** (select one): Choose whether the product has a shipping address.
* **Product Shipping Email** (select one): Choose whether the product has a shipping email.
* **Product Description** (text): Enter a detailed description of the product.
* **Product Status** (select one): Set the product status.
* **Product Availability** (select multiple): Set the product availability.
* **Product Inventory** (number): Set the product inventory level.
* **Product Reorder Level** (number): Set the product reorder level.
* **Product Reorder Frequency** (select one): Choose whether the product has a reorder frequency.
* **Product Shipping Cost** (select multiple): Choose the shipping cost options for the product.
* **Product Shipping Estimated Delivery Time** (date): Enter the shipping estimated delivery time.
* **Product Shipping Estimated Delivery Time Range** (date): Enter the shipping estimated delivery time range.
* **Product Shipping Method** (select one): Choose the shipping method for the product.
* **Product Shipping Notes** (note): Add any shipping notes for the product.
* **Product Comments** (note): Add any comments about the product.
