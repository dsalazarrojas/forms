# Author Bio Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Author Bio Form is designed to collect biographical information from authors, which will be used to create standardized bios for publications and online presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit Date" field and select the date of submission.
2.  Enter your full name in the "Author Name" field.
3.  Enter your professional email in the "Email Address" field.
4.  Select your primary writing genre in the "Primary Writing Genre" field.
5.  Enter the number of years you have been writing in the "Years of Writing Experience" field.
6.  Optionally, enter the number of books you have published.
7.  Enter the websites, journals, and other publications where your work has appeared.
8.  Enter any literary awards or recognition you have received.
9.  Enter your educational background or training.
10. Enter any professional credentials you hold.
11. Enter areas of expertise or interest.
12. Write a short, 50-word bio for author boxes.
13. Optionally, write a medium bio for publications, up to 150 words.
14. Optionally, write a detailed bio if needed, up to 300 words.
15. Enter the URL of your personal website or blog.
16. Enter your Twitter, LinkedIn, and other social media handles.
17. If you have a photo, select "Yes - Will Attach" and ensure it meets the required size and resolution.
18. Select "Yes - Permitted" to allow usage of your photo in bios.
19. Select "Yes - Contact Me" to allow the team to reach you about opportunities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, required): Enter the date of submission of the bio form.
* **Author Name** (`author_name`, required): Enter your full name or pen name as it appears on your work.
* **Email Address** (`author_email`, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (`author_phone`, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Primary Writing Genre** (`writing_genre`, required): Select the primary genre in which you primarily write.
* **Years of Writing Experience** (`years_writing`, required): Enter the number of years you have been writing.
* **Number of Books Published** (`books_published`, optional): Enter the number of books you have published.
* **Publications Where Work Appears** (`publications_featured`, optional): Enter the publications where your work appears.
* **Awards and Recognition** (`awards_recognition`, optional): Enter any literary awards or recognition you have received.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, optional): Enter your relevant degrees or training.
* **Professional Credentials** (`professional_credentials`, optional): Enter any professional credentials you hold.
* **Professional Interests and Expertise** (`professional_interests`, required): Enter areas of expertise or interest.
* **Short Bio** (`bio_short`, required): Write a short, 50-word bio for author boxes.
* **Medium Bio** (`bio_medium`, optional): Optionally, write a medium bio for publications, up to 150 words.
* **Long Bio** (`bio_long`, optional): Optionally, write a detailed bio if needed, up to 300 words.
* **Personal Website or Blog** (`website_url`, optional): Enter the URL of your personal website or blog.
* **Twitter Handle** (`social_media_twitter`, optional): Enter your Twitter/X handle.
* **LinkedIn Profile** (`social_media_linkedin`, optional): Enter the URL of your LinkedIn profile.
* **Instagram Handle** (`social_media_instagram`, optional): Enter your Instagram username.
* **Other Social Media** (`social_media_other`, optional): Enter other professional profiles.
* **Author Photo Available** (`author_photo`, required): Select whether you have a photo available.
* **Photo Usage Permission** (`photo_permission`, optional): Select whether you permit the photo to be used in bios.
* **Contact Permission** (`contact_consent`, required): Select whether you permit contact from the team.
