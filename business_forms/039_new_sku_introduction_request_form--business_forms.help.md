# New SKU Introduction Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a new stock keeping unit (SKU) for your product catalog. Fill out this form to provide information about the new SKU, including its description, product name, price, image URL, and other relevant details. This form is used by your team to onboard new SKUs and ensure accurate and up-to-date product information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about the new SKU.
2. Double-check that the information you provide is correct and complete.
3. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New SKU (1)** (`new_sku`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the new SKU.
	+ This field is required to create a new SKU.
* **Description (2)** (`description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the product.
	+ This field is required to help your team understand the product's characteristics and features.
* **Product Name (3)** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product.
	+ This field is required to accurately categorize and list the product in the catalog.
* **Price (4)** (`price`, text, required): Enter the price of the product.
	+ This field is required to ensure accurate pricing for the product.
* **Image URL (5)** (`image_url`, text, required): Provide the URL of the product image.
	+ This field is required to display the product image in the catalog.
* **Stock Count (6)** (`stock_count`, text, required): Enter the available stock count of the product.
	+ This field is required to track inventory levels.
* **Weight oz (7)** (`weight_oz`, text, optional): Enter the weight of the product in ounces.
	+ This field is optional and provides additional product details.
* **Weight Unit (8)** (`weight_unit`, text, optional): Select the unit of measurement for the weight (e.g., oz, lbs, etc.).
	+ This field is optional and provides more accurate product weight information.
* **Dimensions (9)** (`dimensions`, text, optional): Enter the dimensions of the product.
	+ This field is optional and provides additional product details.
* **Vendor SKU (10)** (`vendor_sku`, text, optional): Enter the SKU of the product from the supplier.
	+ This field is optional and helps your team verify product information.
* **Supplier (11)** (`supplier`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's name or company.
	+ This field is optional and provides additional product sourcing information.
* **Supplier Email (12)** (`supplier_email`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's email address.
	+ This field is optional and helps your team contact the supplier.
* **Shipping Carrier (13)** (`shipping_carrier`, text, optional): Enter the shipping carrier used for this product.
	+ This field is optional and tracks shipping details.
* **Shipping Carrier Tracking (14)** (`shipping_carrier_tracking`, text, optional): Enter the tracking number for the shipping carrier.
	+ This field is optional and provides additional shipping tracking information.

## Tips

* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact your team or [support email](link to your support email).
