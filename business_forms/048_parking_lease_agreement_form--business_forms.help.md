# Parking Lease Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parking Lease Agreement Form is a document used to outline the terms and conditions of a parking lease between the lessee (tenant) and the lessor (landlord). It is a crucial document that ensures both parties are on the same page regarding the lease agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the parking address on page 1 of the form.
2. Check the "Lessee Information" and "Lessor Information" checkboxes on page 2 of the form to indicate whether you are the lessee or lessor.
3. Select the start and end dates of the lease on pages 3 and 4 of the form.
4. Enter the rental amount on page 5 of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Parking Lease Agreement** (`lease_agreement_form`, text, not required)
This field is the title of the parking lease agreement form. It is not a required field, but it is recommended to fill it in for reference purposes.

### 2. **Parking Address** (`parking_address`, text, not required)
Enter the address of the parking location. Please make sure to enter the exact address where the parking is located.

### 3. **Lessee Information** (`lessee_information`, select_multiple, not required)
Check "Yes" if you are the lessee (tenant), and "No" if you are not.

### 4. **Lessor Information** (`lessor_information`, select_multiple, not required)
Check "Yes" if you are the lessor (landlord), and "No" if you are not.

### 5. **Lease Term** (`lease_term`, date, not required)
Select the start date of the lease. Please ensure that you select the correct date for the start of the lease.

### 6. **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, not required)
Select the end date of the lease. Please ensure that you select the correct date for the end of the lease.

### 7. **End Date** (`end_date`, date, not required)
Enter the rental amount for the parking lease. Please ensure that you enter the correct amount for the lease.

### 8. **Rental Amount** (`rental_amount`, number, not required)
Please note that the fields on this form are not required, but it is recommended to fill them in for a complete and accurate parking lease agreement.
