# fuels_and_emissions_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "fuels_and_emissions_assessment" form is designed to collect detailed information about a vehicle's characteristics, including its fuel type, emissions level, and other relevant attributes. This form helps ensure that accurate and consistent data is collected, which is vital for informed business decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that the vehicle you are assessing is within your company's scope of operations or business interests.
2. Fill out the form accurately and consistently, as duplicated or inaccurate data can lead to incorrect business insights.
3. Select the correct vehicle type and fuel type from the provided options.
4. Specify the vehicle's emissions level, taking into account its fuel type and other relevant characteristics.
5. Provide any additional comments or information that may be relevant to the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This field is a text box where you can provide a brief overview of the vehicle assessment. This section is optional.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company that owns or operates the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle from the options provided (Car, Truck, or Other). This field is required.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle. This field is optional.
* **Vehicle Fuel Type**: Select the fuel type of the vehicle from the options provided (Petrol, Diesel, or Other). This field is required.
* **Vehicle Fuel Consumption**: Enter the fuel consumption of the vehicle. This field is optional.
* **Fuel Type**: Select the fuel type of the fuel consumed by the vehicle from the options provided (Petrol, Diesel, or Other). This field is required.
* **Fuel Consumption**: Enter the fuel consumption of the vehicle. This field is optional.
* **Emissions**: Select the emissions level of the vehicle from the options provided (High, Low, or Unknown). This field is required.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information that may be relevant to the assessment. This field is optional.
* **Company Name 2**: This field is not currently used in the form and is left blank for potential future use.
