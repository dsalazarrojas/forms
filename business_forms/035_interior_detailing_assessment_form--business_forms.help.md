# interior_detailing_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The interior detailing assessment form is used to gather information about a vehicle's interior detailing needs. It is designed to be completed by business users to estimate the cost and time required for interior detailing services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the make of the vehicle from the list.
2. Enter the year of the vehicle.
3. Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
4. Select the condition of the vehicle's interior and exterior.
5. Choose the interior and exterior detailing services required.
6. Enter the estimated cost and time required for the services.
7. Select the type of vehicle being assessed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, select_one): Select the make of the vehicle from the list: Honda, Toyota, Ford, Nissan, Kia, Volkswagen, Mazda, or other.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Mileage** (`vehicle_mileage`, number): Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, select_multiple): Select the condition of the vehicle's interior and exterior from the list: Fair, Good, Excellent, Poor, Dirty, Clean, or Somewhat Dirty.
* **Interior Condition** (`interior_condition`, select_one): Select the condition of the vehicle's interior from the list: Clean, Dirty, or Somewhat Dirty.
* **Exterior Condition** (`exterior_condition`, select_one): Select the condition of the vehicle's exterior from the list: Clean, Dirty, or Somewhat Dirty.
* **Interior Detailing Services** (`interior_detailing_services`, select_multiple): Select the interior detailing services required from the list: Interior Cleaning, Interior Detailing, Interior Odor Removal, Interior Protection, or Interior Protection and Cleaning.
* **Exterior Detailing Services** (`exterior_detailing_services`, select_multiple): Select the exterior detailing services required from the list: Exterior Cleaning, Exterior Detailing, Exterior Odor Removal, Exterior Protection, or Exterior Protection and Cleaning.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number): Enter the estimated total cost of the services.
* **Labor Cost** (`labor_cost`, number): Enter the estimated labor cost of the services.
* **Estimated Time** (`estimated_time`, time): Enter the estimated time required for the services.
* **Total Value** (`total_value`, number): Enter the estimated total value of the vehicle based on its condition and type.
* **Estimated Value** (`estimated_value`, number): Enter the estimated value of the vehicle based on its condition and type.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one): Select the type of vehicle from the list: Sedan, SUV, Truck, Van, Bus, Motorcycle, ATV/UTV, or Boat.

## Tips
- Be accurate when selecting the vehicle make, year, and mileage.
- Select the most accurate condition of the vehicle's interior and exterior.
- Choose the services required based on the vehicle's condition.
- Estimate the cost and time required based on the services selected.
- Select the correct type of vehicle.
