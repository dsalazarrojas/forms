# Brand Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you evaluate and document the risks associated with a brand's activities or actions. It will guide you through the process of identifying potential risks, assessing their severity, and recommending mitigation actions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and role as the assessor in the "Assessor Name and Role" field.
2. Enter the name of the brand being assessed in the "Business Unit / Brand Name" field.
3. Select the date of the assessment in the "Date of Assessment" field.
4. Evaluate the potential risks associated with the brand's activities or actions and select the corresponding risk levels in the "Potential for Reputational Damage", "Regulatory and Legal Compliance Exposure", and "Risk from Competitor Activity" fields.
5. Describe the identified risks in detail in the "Detailed Description of Identified Risk" field.
6. Select the channels at risk in the "Channels at Risk" field.
7. Describe any existing mitigation actions taken in the "Existing Mitigation Actions" field.
8. Assess the residual risk after mitigation in the "Residual Risk after Mitigation" field.
9. Recommend new mitigation actions in the "Recommended New Mitigation Actions" field.
10. Schedule a review date for the next assessment in the "Scheduled Next Review Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessor Name and Role** (`assessor_info`, text, required): Enter your name and role as the assessor.
* **Business Unit / Brand Name** (`brand_name_val`, text, required): Enter the name of the brand being assessed.
* **Date of Assessment** (`assess_dt`, date, required): Select the date of the assessment.
* **Potential for Reputational Damage** (`rep_risk_lvl`, select_one, required): Select the level of reputational damage risk associated with the brand.
* **Regulatory and Legal Compliance Exposure** (`legal_risk_lvl`, select_one, required): Select the level of regulatory and legal compliance risk associated with the brand.
* **Risk from Competitor Activity** (`market_risk_lvl`, select_one, required): Select the level of risk from competitor activity associated with the brand.
* **Detailed Description of Identified Risk** (`risk_desc`, text, required): Describe the identified risks in detail.
* **Channels at Risk** (`risk_channels`, select_multiple, required): Select all the channels at risk associated with the brand.
* **Existing Mitigation Actions** (`mitigation_plan_existing`, text, optional): Describe any existing mitigation actions taken.
* **Residual Risk after Mitigation** (`residual_risk`, select_one, required): Assess the residual risk after mitigation.
* **Recommended New Mitigation Actions** (`new_actions`, text, required): Recommend new mitigation actions to address the risks.
* **Scheduled Next Review Date** (`review_dt`, date, required): Schedule a review date for the next assessment.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct risk levels and describe the identified risks accurately.
* Ensure that you select all the relevant channels at risk associated with the brand.
* Provide clear and actionable recommendations for mitigation actions.
* Schedule a review date for the next assessment to ensure continuous monitoring and improvement.
