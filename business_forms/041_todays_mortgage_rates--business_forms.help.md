# Todays Mortgage Rates - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Todays Mortgage Rates" form is designed to gather information about a lead's mortgage needs and preferences. This information will be used by mortgage professionals to better understand the lead's requirements and provide personalized guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your lead's information in the "Lead Information" section.
2.  Provide details about the property the lead is interested in in the "Property Information" section.
3.  Select your lead's mortgage preferences in the "Mortgage Preferences" section.
4.  Enter your lead's income details in the "Income Details" section.
5.  Provide your contact information in the "Contact Details" section.
6.  Select your lead's status in the "Lead Status" section.

*   **Mortgage Preferences** (`Mortgage Preferences`, text, required/optional): Select your lead's mortgage preferences, such as the loan term and interest rate.
*   **Income Details** (`Income Details`, text, required/optional): Enter your lead's income and expenses, such as their annual income and debt-to-income ratio.
*   **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, required/optional): Enter your lead's contact information, such as their name and email address.
