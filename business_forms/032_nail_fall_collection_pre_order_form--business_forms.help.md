# Nail Fall Collection Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-order form is designed to collect information from customers interested in pre-ordering the Nail Fall Collection. Please fill out this form to express your interest in purchasing a product from the collection.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select a collection option that matches your preference.
2.  Choose the type of nail product you're interested in (almond, gel, powder, shell, or chrome).
3.  If desired, select a date for your order (if you're looking for a specific date for your order).
4.  Complete the form by clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Pre Order Form** (`pre_order_form`, text, optional): A brief description of your interest in pre-ordering the Nail Fall Collection. Please provide a short message explaining your selection or any specific requests.
*   **collection** (`collection`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more collections that interest you from the provided options (e.g., 'Yes', 'No'). If you select 'Yes', you may be contacted with further information.
*   **order_date** (`order_date`, date, optional): If you've selected a collection and nail type, please enter the date you'd like to place the order. This is not required but can help ensure timely fulfillment.
*   **nail_type** (`nail_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of nail product you're interested in purchasing from the available options (almond, gel, powder, shell, or chrome).
    Please ensure your selection is accurate to avoid any confusion during the order process.
