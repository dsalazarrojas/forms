# Programming equipment order form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the programming equipment a company needs to order. It helps the IT department or procurement team to understand the company's requirements and place the order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the form with the required information about your company:
	* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
	* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person to contact.
	* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
2. Select the programming language you need for the equipment:
	* **Programming Language**: Choose the programming language required for the equipment.
3. Specify the equipment needed:
	* **Equipment Needed**: Enter a detailed description of the equipment you need.
4. Select the order date and time:
	* **Order Date**: Enter the date when you would like to receive the equipment.
	* **Order Time**: Enter the time when you would like to receive the equipment.
5. Add any additional comments or notes:
	* **Comments**: Enter any additional information or comments about the order.
6. Enter your company's address and contact information:
	* **Company Address**: Enter the address of your company.
	* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool or equipment you are assigning to the contact person.
7. Finally, enter your company's phone number:
	* **Company Phone**: Enter the phone number of your company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Text, Required: false): Enter the name of your company to help us understand who is ordering the equipment.
* **Contact Person** (Text, Required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the person to contact for this order.
* **Contact Email** (Email, Required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Programming Language** (Select One, Required: false): Choose the programming language required for the equipment.
	+ Options: Python, Java, C++, C#, JavaScript
* **Equipment Needed** (Text, Required: false): Enter a detailed description of the equipment you need.
* **Order Date** (Date, Required: false): Enter the date when you would like to receive the equipment.
* **Order Time** (Time, Required: false): Enter the time when you would like to receive the equipment.
* **Comments** (Note, Required: false): Enter any additional information or comments about the order.
* **Company Address** (Text, Required: false): Enter the address of your company.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text, Required: false): Enter the tool or equipment you are assigning to the contact person.
* **Company Phone** (Text, Required: false): Enter the phone number of your company.

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill in all required fields to help the IT department or procurement team process your order efficiently.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact the IT department or procurement team.
* The form is designed to be completed by the person who ordered the equipment, to ensure accurate and efficient processing of the order.
