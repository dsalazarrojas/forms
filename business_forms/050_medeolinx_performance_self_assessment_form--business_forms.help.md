# MedeoLinx Performance Self-Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MedeoLinx Performance Self-Assessment Form is designed for employees to provide their performance self-assessment and for managers to provide feedback to employees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out your name and title (fields 2 and 7) as they appear on your company's organizational chart.
*   Select the start date of your performance evaluation period (field 3).
*   Select the evaluation scale that best describes your performance during this period (field 4).
*   Provide any comments from your manager (field 5).
*   Select the reviewer's name and title (fields 6 and 7) as they appear on the organizational chart.
*   Select the date for the performance evaluation (field 8).
*   Provide a brief description of your performance status (field 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Input (field 1)
*   **User Input**: This field is where you provide your self-assessment of your performance during the evaluation period.

### 2. Manager Name (field 2)
*   **Manager Name**: Fill out your manager's name as it appears on the company's organizational chart.

### 3. Start Date (field 3)
*   **Start Date**: Select the start date of your performance evaluation period in YYYY-MM-DD format.

### 4. Evaluation Scale (field 4)
*   **Evaluation Scale**: Select one of the following options:
    *   **Excellent**: You met all performance goals and expectations.
    *   **Good**: You met most performance goals and expectations.
    *   **Fair**: You met some performance goals and expectations.
    *   **Poor**: You did not meet performance goals and expectations.

### 5. Manager Comments (field 5)
*   **Manager Comments**: Your manager will provide any comments or feedback they have for you.

### 6. Reviewer Name (field 6)
*   **Review Name**: Fill out the reviewer's name as it appears on the company's organizational chart.

### 7. Reviewer Title (field 7)
*   **Review Name**: Fill out the reviewer's title as it appears on the company's organizational chart.

### 8. Date (field 8)
*   **Date**: Select the date for the performance evaluation in YYYY-MM-DD format.

### 9. Performance Status (field 9)
*   **Performance Status**: Provide a brief description of your performance status, such as "Meets all performance goals and expectations" or "Needs improvement".
