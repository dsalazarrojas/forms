# Internal Controls Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internal Controls Certification Registration Form is used to register internal controls certifications for various roles or positions within an organization. This form helps ensure that individuals with certified internal controls are documented and tracked, which is essential for maintaining accurate records and meeting regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "date of birth" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Enter the date of completion of your internal controls certification in the "date of completion" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Select your certification level (High or Low) in the "certification level" field.
8. Select the programs or services you're certified in (Yes or No) in the "program" field.
9. Enter your department name in the "department" field.
10. Enter the date you submitted the certification in the "date" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
11. Enter the time you submitted the certification in the "time" field in the format of HH:MM.
12. Select whether the certification was submitted (Yes or No) in the "submit" field.
13. Add any additional comments or notes about your certification in the "comments" field (optional).
14. Add any additional information about your certification in the "additional info" field (optional).
15. If required, upload any relevant documents, videos, audio files, images, or files related to your certification.
16. Finally, review and submit the form by selecting "submit".
