# Company Name Change Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Company Name Change Request Form is a document that enables you to submit a formal request to change the name of a company. This form ensures that all relevant information is collected and reviewed before the name change is processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the current company name in the "Current company name" field. This is the legal name of the company as it is currently registered.
2.  Enter the proposed new company name in the "Requested new company name" field.
3.  Provide a brief explanation for the name change in the "Reason for name change" field.
4.  Enter the name of the person authorizing the change in the "Authorization contact name" field.
5.  (Optional) Enter the desired effective date for the name change in the "Desired effective date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Current company name** (`current_company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the current, legal registered name of your company.
* **Requested new company name** (`requested_new_name`, `text`, required): Enter the proposed new name for your company, as it will appear in the new registration.
* **Reason for name change** (`reason_for_change`, `text`, required): Describe the reason for the name change, providing a brief explanation of the justification for the request.
* **Authorization contact name** (`authorization_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person authorizing the name change.
* **Desired effective date** (`effective_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the name change should take effect, if desired (note that the name change will not be processed until the specified date).
