# lean_operations_strategy_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information and specific details from clients or customers who need help with Lean Operations strategies. It helps us understand their needs and provide tailored support or solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address.
2. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
3. Provide your company name and URL (if applicable).
4. Select the type of inquiry you are making (Consult, Process Improvement, or Strategy Support).
5. Describe the details of your request.
6. If you have a specific tool assigned, select 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, `text`, required: false): A description of your request or problem you are trying to solve.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): A field to enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): A field to enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): A field to enter your phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): A field to enter your company name.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`, `text`, required: false): A field to enter your company URL.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, `select_one`, required: false): A field to select the type of inquiry you are making (Consult, Process Improvement, or Strategy Support).
	+ Consult: For consultation on a specific topic.
	+ Process Improvement: For help improving a business process.
	+ Strategy Support: For strategic support or guidance.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: false): A field to select if you have been assigned a specific tool.
	+ 'Yes': I have been assigned a tool.
	+ 'No': I do not have a tool assigned.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, `text`, required: false): A field to describe the details of your request.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to help us respond to your inquiry effectively.
- If you are having trouble with the form, contact us for assistance.
