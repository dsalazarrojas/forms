# Digital Culture Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge employees' digital culture within an organization. It collects information about their technology usage, work habits, and engagement levels to provide insights for management and HR teams to understand and improve the company's digital culture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each section and answer the questions accordingly. If a question has multiple options, select all that apply. If a question is not relevant to you, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This section is where you can briefly introduce yourself and your role in the organization. It's not required, so feel free to skip it if you're short on time.
* **Questions Section**: This section has a list of questions about your technology usage and work habits. Select all the options that apply to you.
	+ **What is your role in the organization?**: This question helps us understand your position within the company.
	+ **What is your job title?**: This question provides additional context about your role and responsibilities.
	+ **How long have you been with the organization?**: This question helps us understand your level of familiarity with company policies and culture.
	+ **What are your primary work hours?**: This question helps us understand your work schedule and availability.
	+ **Which technology platforms do you use most frequently?**: This question is about the tools and platforms you use for work.
	+ **How often do you use email?**: This question is about your email frequency and usage.
	+ **How often do you use instant messaging or chat apps?**: This question is about your real-time communication habits.
	+ **Do you access the organization's intranet or internal communication platform?**: This question helps us understand your level of interaction with company-wide communication channels.
	+ **Do you have a work computer or laptop?**: This question is about your device usage for work.
	+ **Do you use a virtual private network (VPN)?**: This question is about your online security practices.
* **Employee Engagement**: This section asks about your level of engagement with the company. Choose one option that best describes your current level of engagement.
* **Technology Adoption**: This section has questions about your adoption and usage of technology within the company. Select all the options that apply to you.
	+ **High**: You frequently use and rely on company-provided technology and tools.
	+ **Moderate**: You use company-provided technology and tools occasionally.
	+ **Low**: You rarely or never use company-provided technology and tools.
* **Technology Adoption 2**: This section is similar to the previous one, but with slightly different options.
	+ **High**: You frequently use and rely on company-provided technology and tools.
	+ **Moderate**: You use company-provided technology and tools occasionally.
	+ **Low**: You rarely or never use company-provided technology and tools.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure about any question, try your best to provide a reasonable answer.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
