# Key Account Planning Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help business users document and plan key accounts. It captures important information about the account, including the key objectives, stakeholders, challenges, and potential growth opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the account manager responsible for the account from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the key objectives for the account from the available options (e.g., Revenue Growth, Customer Acquisition, Retention).
3. Identify the key stakeholders involved in the account (e.g., CEO, Sales Team, Other).
4. Describe the key challenges facing the account.
5. Select the potential growth opportunities for the account (e.g., Increase Revenue, Enhance Customer Experience, Other).
6. Enter a customer profile number, which may be used for internal reference.
7. Enter an account value, which represents the account's value to the organization.
8. Assess the account's risk level (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
9. Choose the desired sales outcomes for the account (e.g., Won, Lost, Other).
10. Provide any additional comments or next steps for the account.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Manager**: Select the individual responsible for managing the account from the dropdown menu. This will help ensure that the correct person is involved in the account planning process.
* **Objectives**: Choose the key objectives for the account from the available options. These objectives should align with the organization's overall business goals.
* **Stakeholders**: Identify the key stakeholders involved in the account, such as the CEO, Sales Team, or Other. This will help ensure that everyone involved is aware of their role and responsibilities.
* **Challenges**: Describe the key challenges facing the account. Be as specific as possible to ensure that these challenges are understood by all parties involved.
* **Growth Opportunities**: Select the potential growth opportunities for the account from the available options. These opportunities should align with the organization's overall business goals.
* **Customer Profile**: Enter a customer profile number, which may be used for internal reference and tracking.
* **Account Value**: Enter an account value, which represents the account's value to the organization.
* **Account Risk**: Assess the account's risk level (e.g., High, Medium, Low). This will help prioritize efforts and resources for the account.
* **Sales Outcomes**: Choose the desired sales outcomes for the account (e.g., Won, Lost, Other). This will help determine the next steps and actions for the account.
* **Next Steps**: Provide any additional comments or next steps for the account. This is an opportunity to outline specific actions or follow-up tasks.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure that the account planning process is thorough and accurate.
* Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in the account's status or circumstances.
