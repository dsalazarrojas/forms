# zoom_liability_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a participant in a Zoom meeting or other Zoom-related activity, including contact information and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Participant Name** in the text field provided.
2. Provide the **Participant Contact Info** in the text field.
3. Enter the **Emergency Contact Name** in the text field.
4. Provide the **Emergency Contact Phone** in the text field.
5. Select the **Relationship** between the participant and the emergency contact from the dropdown list.
6. If the relationship is not listed, you can specify **Other** in the text field and enter a brief description.
7. Enter the **Phone** number for the emergency contact in the text field.
8. Sign and date the **Participant Signature** field as an acknowledgment of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Contact Info** (`Participant Contact Info`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information for the participant.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`Relationship`, select_one, required: false): Select the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact from the dropdown list.
* **Other (if not listed above)** (`Other (if not listed above)`, text, required: false): If the relationship is not listed, enter a brief description.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number for the emergency contact.
* **Participant Signature** (`Participant Signature`, note, required: false): Sign and date as an acknowledgment of the information provided.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the participant and emergency contact.
* Use a pen to sign the **Participant Signature** field.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
