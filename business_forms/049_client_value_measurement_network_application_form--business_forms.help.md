# Client Value Measurement Network Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Value Measurement Network Application Form is designed to collect information about potential members of the network, including their background, interests, and availability. The form is used to assess an individual's fit and suitability for the network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the personal contact information: 
  * **Full Name**: Enter your full name in this field.
  * **Current Title or Position**: Enter your current job title or position.
  * **Company or Organization Name**: Enter the name of the company or organization you are associated with.
  * **Email Address**: Enter your professional email address.
  * **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
  * **LinkedIn Profile URL**: Enter your LinkedIn profile URL, if you have one.
2. Next, provide details about your background and experience:
  * **Years of Professional Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been in your profession.
  * **Relevant Experience with Value Measurement**: Describe your experience with value measurement.
3. Specify your interests and participation expectations:
  * **Areas of Interest**: Select topics that interest you most (Value proposition, Client success metrics, Business outcomes measurement, ROI analysis, Performance management, or Other).
  * **How Would You Like to Participate?**: Select the roles that interest you (Attend events, Share insights, Collaborate on projects, Mentor others, Lead initiatives, or Other).
4. Finally, provide information about your motivation and availability:
  * **Why Are You Interested in Joining?**: Explain why you want to join the network.
  * **Current Challenges in Your Field**: Describe any challenges you're currently facing in your field.
  * **Goals and Objectives**: Outline what you hope to achieve by joining the network.
  * **Availability for Participation**: Select how much time you can commit (Very flexible, Several hours per month, Once per month, A few times per year, or Limited availability).
  * **Agreement and Acceptance**: Confirm that you agree to the network's terms and conditions.

Note: The form is divided into sections to help you navigate and complete it more efficiently. Please fill out each section before proceeding to the next. If you have any questions or need help, please contact the network administrators.
