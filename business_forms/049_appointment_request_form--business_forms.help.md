# Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to request an appointment with the company. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure your appointment request is processed accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
2. Select the type of appointment you are requesting in the "Type of Appointment" field.
3. Choose your preferred date and time for the appointment in the "Preferred Date" and "Preferred Time" fields.
4. Specify the expected duration of the appointment in the "Expected Duration" field (optional).
5. Choose the meeting format you prefer in the "Meeting Format" field (optional).
6. In the "Purpose of Appointment" field, describe the reason for your appointment.
7. If necessary, provide any relevant background information or specific needs or requirements.
8. Choose how you would like to be confirmed about your appointment in the "How should we confirm your appointment?" field (optional).
9. Finally, accept the appointment terms and conditions by selecting "I agree" in the "Terms and Conditions" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Contact Information, id: 2)** (`Full Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it should be displayed on any communication.
* **Email Address (Contact Information, id: 3)** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive confirmation and updates about your appointment.
* **Phone Number (Contact Information, id: 4)** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number to receive phone call confirmations or updates about your appointment.
* **Company (Contact Information, id: 5)** (`Company Name`, `text`, optional): If you are contacting us on behalf of a company, please enter the company name.
* **Type of Appointment (Appointment Scheduling, id: 7)** (`Type of Appointment`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of appointment you are requesting.
* **Preferred Date (Appointment Scheduling, id: 8)** (`Preferred Date`, `date`, required): Choose the date you prefer for your appointment.
* **Preferred Time (Appointment Scheduling, id: 9)** (`Preferred Time`, `select_one`, required): Choose the time you prefer for your appointment.
* **Expected Duration (Appointment Scheduling, id: 10)** (`Expected Duration`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the expected duration of your appointment.
* **Meeting Format (Appointment Scheduling, id: 11)** (`Meeting Format`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the meeting format you prefer.
* **Purpose of Appointment (Purpose and Details, id: 13)** (`Purpose of Appointment`, `text`, required): Describe the reason for your appointment.
* **Background Information (Purpose and Details, id: 14)** (`Background Information`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant background information or details about your appointment.
* **Specific needs or requirements (Purpose and Details, id: 15)** (`Specific needs or requirements`, `text`, optional): Specify any specific needs or requirements for your appointment.
* **How should we confirm your appointment? (Confirmation Method, id: 17)** (`How should we confirm your appointment?`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you would like to be confirmed about your appointment.
* **I agree to the appointment terms and conditions (Confirmation Method, id: 18)** (`I agree to the appointment terms and conditions`, `select_one`, optional): Accept the appointment terms and conditions by selecting "I agree".
