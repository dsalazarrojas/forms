# poster_design_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The poster_design_quotation_form is a form designed to collect information from clients about their poster design quotation requests. This form aims to capture essential details about the client, the design, quotation, submission, and payment requirements. By providing the correct information, clients can ensure that their quotation request is accurately processed and submitted successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your client's details in the "Client Details" field.
2. Provide design specifications and requirements in the "Design Details" field.
3. Enter quotation details and requirements in the "Quotation Details" field.
4. Specify the submission details in the "Submission Details" field.
5. Enter designer's details in the "Designer Details" field.
6. Add any additional client requirements in the "Client Requirements" field.
7. Select the current status of the quotation in the "Quotation Status" field.
8. Enter payment details in the "Payment Details" field.
9. Add any additional requirements in the "Additional Requirements" field.
10. Enter designer's comments and notes in the "Designer Comments" and "Submission Notes" fields.
11. Enter client's comments and notes in the "Client Comments" field.
12. Select the status of the submission in the "Submission Status" and "Payment Status" fields.
13. Capture client's and designer's signatures in the "Client Signature" and "Designer Signature" fields.
14. Confirm submission and quotation in the "Submission Confirmation" and "Quotation Confirmation" fields.
15. Confirm payment in the "Payment Confirmation" field.
16. Enter the dates and times for submission, quotation, and payment confirmations in the "Submission Confirmation Date", "Quotation Confirmation Date", and "Payment Confirmation Date" fields.
17. Enter the times for submission, quotation, and payment confirmations in the "Submission Confirmation Time", "Quotation Confirmation Time", and "Payment Confirmation Time" fields.
