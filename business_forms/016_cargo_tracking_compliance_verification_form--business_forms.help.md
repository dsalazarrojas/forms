# Cargo Tracking Compliance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify that a shipment meets all necessary requirements and regulations for cargo tracking. It is an important tool for logistics and transportation companies to ensure timely and accurate delivery of goods.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your Verification ID, which is a unique internal code for this check.
2. Enter the date of the verification in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter your professional title or position as the verifier.
4. Choose your verifier role from the options provided.
5. Enter the shipment or tracking number for the cargo being tracked.
6. Enter the manifest number associated with the shipment.
7. Enter the company name and vehicle ID of the carrier.
8. For each checklist item, select the corresponding answer based on the actual conditions of the shipment.
9. Enter the ID of the GPS or tracking device, if equipped.
10. Select the current tracking signal status.
11. Check if the tracking data is synced with the central system.
12. List any discrepancies or violations observed.
13. Enter a compliance score out of 100.
14. Choose the final verification status (Approved, Rejected, or Conditional Approval).
15. Enter your full name as a digital signature to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Verification ID** (Text, Required): A unique internal code for this check.
* **Date of Verification** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the verification in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Verifier Name** (Text, Required): Your professional title or position.
* **Verifier Role** (Select One, Required): Choose your role from the options provided.
* **Shipment or Tracking Number** (Text, Required): Enter the shipment or tracking number.
* **Manifest Number** (Text, Required): Enter the manifest number associated with the shipment.
* **Carrier Details** (Text, Required): Enter the company name and vehicle ID of the carrier.
* **Are all required shipping documents complete?** (Select One, Required): Choose True if all documents are complete, False if not, or In Progress.
* **Are all cargo labels compliant with regulations?** (Select One, Required): Choose True if labels are compliant, False if not, or N/A if not applicable.
* **Is the hazardous material declaration accurate?** (Select One, Required): Choose True if accurate, False if not, or N/A if not applicable.
* **Does actual weight match manifest weight?** (Select One, Required): Choose True if weights match, False if not.
* **Is the shipment routing compliant with restricted zones?** (Select One, Required): Choose True if compliant, False if not, or N/A if not applicable.
* **GPS or Tracking Device ID** (Text, Optional): Enter the ID of the GPS or tracking device, if equipped.
* **Current tracking signal status** (Select One, Required): Choose Online, Weak, Offline, or Not Equipped.
* **Is tracking data synced with the central system?** (Select One, Required): Choose True if synced, False if not.
* **List any discrepancies or violations** (Text, Optional): List any issues observed during the verification process.
* **Compliance Score** (Number, Required): Enter a compliance score out of 100.
* **Verification Status** (Select One, Required): Choose Approved, Rejected, or Conditional Approval.
* **Verification Status** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as a digital signature to confirm.
