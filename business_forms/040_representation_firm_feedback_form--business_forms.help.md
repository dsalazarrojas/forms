# Representation Firm Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Representation Firm Feedback Form is a tool for firms to provide feedback on their experience with the representation of certain events or services. This form helps internal personnel to gather insights and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have not already submitted feedback on the same topic.
2. Select the relevant fields to provide feedback, using the options provided in the form.
3. Complete all required fields, indicated by a asterisk (*) in the field labels.
4. If you have any additional comments or notes, use the "notes" field to provide more detailed feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (`page_1`, text, not required): Provide a brief description of your feedback regarding the representation firm experience.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the representation firm experience, using a scale of 1-5.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, not required): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): If you want to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, provide your phone number.
* **Category** (`category`, select multiple, not required): Select all categories that apply to your feedback.
	+ Please select all categories that are relevant to your feedback.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, select one, not required): Select the name of the person who submitted the feedback.
	+ Please select the name of the person who submitted the feedback.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, not required): The date and time when the feedback was submitted.
	+ This field will be automatically populated by the system.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, not required): The time when the feedback was submitted.
	+ This field will be automatically populated by the system.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): If you have any additional comments or notes, please provide them here.
* **Submitted By Name** (`submitted_by_name`, text, not required): If you want to be identified by name, please provide your name.
* **Firm** (`firm`, select one, not required): Select the name of the firm that provided the feedback.
	+ Please select the name of the firm that provided the feedback.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback to help us better understand your experience.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Your feedback is highly valued and will help us improve our services.
