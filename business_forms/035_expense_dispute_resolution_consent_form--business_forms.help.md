# Expense Dispute Resolution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Dispute Resolution Consent Form is designed to collect information related to an expense dispute between an employee and the management. The form is used to track and document the process of resolving the dispute, ensuring that all necessary details are captured correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of dispute from the options provided (Dispute on expense, Dispute on reimbursement, or Dispute on payment method).
2. Provide a brief description of the dispute in the Dispute Description field.
3. Choose the date of the dispute from your calendar.
4. Enter the amount of the disputed expense.
5. Enter the name and email address of the employee involved in the dispute.
6. Enter the name and email address of the manager involved in the dispute.
7. Select the current resolution status of the dispute (Dispute resolved, Dispute pending, or Dispute ongoing).
8. Optionally, add any additional notes related to the dispute.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dispute Type** (`Dispute Type`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select the type of dispute you are experiencing (e.g., Dispute on expense, Dispute on reimbursement, or Dispute on payment method).
* **Dispute Description** (`dispute_description`, text, not required):
	+ Provide a brief description of the dispute, including details about the expense and the issue you are disputing.
* **Dispute Date** (`dispute_date`, date, not required):
	+ Choose the date of the dispute from your calendar.
* **Dispute Amount** (`dispute_amount`, number, not required):
	+ Enter the amount of the disputed expense.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, not required):
	+ Enter the name of the employee who is involved in the dispute.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, not required):
	+ Enter the email address of the employee who is involved in the dispute.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, not required):
	+ Enter the name of the manager who is involved in the dispute.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, not required):
	+ Enter the email address of the manager who is involved in the dispute.
* **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select the current resolution status of the dispute (e.g., Dispute resolved, Dispute pending, or Dispute ongoing).
* **Dispute Notes** (`dispute_notes`, note, not required):
	+ Optionally, add any additional notes related to the dispute.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct dispute type that accurately reflects the issue at hand.
* Ensure that you provide a clear and brief description of the dispute.
* Enter accurate dates and amounts to avoid any confusion.
* Check the resolution status regularly to reflect any updates to the dispute.
* Use the dispute notes field to provide any additional context or information related to the dispute.
