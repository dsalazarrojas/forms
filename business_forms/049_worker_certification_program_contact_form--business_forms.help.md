# Worker Certification Program Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Certification Program Contact Form is designed to collect contact details and certification interests from workers interested in participating in certification programs. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please do the following:

1.  Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" section, including any relevant information that will help our team reach you.
2.  In the "Certification Interests" section, select all the certification areas that interest you.
3.  In the "Questions" section, provide any questions or concerns you have about the certification program.
4.  If you have an email address, please enter it in the "Email" field to ensure you receive updates and information about the certification program.
5.  If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone" field so we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Details
*   <label>Contact Details</label> (`<name>`: contact_details, `<type>`: text, required/optional: false)
    Please provide your contact details, including your name, company, and any other relevant information that will help our team reach you.

### Certification Interests
*   <label>Certification Interests</label> (`<name>`: certification_interests, `<type>`: select_multiple, required/optional: false)
    In this field, please select all the certification areas that interest you. You can choose more than one certification area.

### Questions
*   <label>Questions</label> (`<name>`: questions, `<type>`: note, required/optional: false)
    If you have any questions or concerns about the certification program, please provide them in this field.

### Email
*   <label>Email</label> (`<name>`: email, `<type>`: email, required/optional: false)
    Please enter your email address if you have one. This will ensure you receive updates and information about the certification program.

### Phone
*   <label>Phone</label> (`<name>`: phone, `<type>`: text, required/optional: false)
    If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
*   Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure you receive the correct information and support.
*   You can choose more than one certification area in the "Certification Interests" field.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please enter them in the "Questions" field.
*   You can also provide your phone number in the "Phone" field for additional contact options.
