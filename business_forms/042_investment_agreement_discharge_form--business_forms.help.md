# Investment Agreement Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to formalize the termination or discharge of an investment agreement, providing a structured way to capture essential information about the agreement and the parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the "Agreement Details" page:
	* Enter the Agreement Number, Investment Amount, Reason for Discharge, and Termination Date.
2. Fill out the "Parties Involved" page:
	* Check the box for the parties involved (Yes/No)
	* Enter the Obligations Terminated date
	* Enter the Signed By name
	* Enter the Signed Date and time
	* Enter the Signed Position
	* Enter the Company Name
	* Enter the Company Position
	* Enter the Company Address
	* Enter the Company Zip
	* Enter the Company City
	* Enter the Company State
	* Enter the Company Country
	* Enter the Company Phone (optional)
	* Enter the Agreement Signed By

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agreement Number**: Enter the unique number of the investment agreement.
* **Investment Amount**: Enter the total amount invested in the agreement.
* **Reason for Discharge**: Provide a clear and concise explanation for the termination of the agreement.
* **Termination Date**: Enter the date on which the agreement was terminated.
* **Parties Involved (Yes/No)**: Check if there are parties involved in the agreement.
* **Obligations Terminated**: Enter the date on which the obligations were terminated.
* **Signed By**: Enter the name of the person who signed the agreement.
* **Signed Date**: Enter the date on which the agreement was signed.
* **Signed Time**: Enter the time at which the agreement was signed.
* **Signed Position**: Enter the position or title of the person who signed the agreement.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company involved in the agreement.
* **Company Position**: Enter the position or title of the person signing for the company.
* **Company Address**: Enter the address of the company.
* **Company Zip**: Enter the zip code of the company address.
* **Company City**: Enter the city where the company is located.
* **Company State**: Enter the state where the company is located.
* **Company Country**: Enter the country where the company is located.
* **Company Phone**: Enter the phone number of the company (optional).
* **Agreement Signed By**: Enter the name of the person who signed the agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the relevant documentation or seek guidance from the relevant authorities.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure accuracy and completeness.
