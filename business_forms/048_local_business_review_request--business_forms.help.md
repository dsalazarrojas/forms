# Local Business Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to collect feedback from customers about their experience with local businesses. Please provide honest and accurate information about the business you have interacted with.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the business you have interacted with.
2. Provide the address of the business.
3. Enter the city where the business is located.
4. Enter the zip code of the business location.
5. Enter the business phone number (if available).
6. Enter your contact email address (if you want to receive a response).
7. Rate your experience with the business (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Terrible).
8. Choose the date you visited the business.
9. Select the time you visited the business.
10. Share any comments or feedback you have about your experience.
11. You can also provide a second rating (if you have a different experience with the same business).
12. Finally, add any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business you have interacted with.
* **Address**: Enter the physical address of the business.
* **City**: Enter the city where the business is located.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the business location.
* **Phone**: Enter the business phone number (if available).
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address (if you want to receive a response).
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with the business (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Terrible).
* **Review Date**: Choose the date you visited the business.
* **Review Time**: Select the time you visited the business.
* **Comments**: Share any comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Rating 2**: If you have a different experience with the same business, rate it again (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Terrible).
* **Comments 2**: Add any additional comments or feedback about your second experience.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Use the correct format for dates (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* If you have multiple experiences with the same business, please use the "Comments 2" field to provide separate feedback for each experience.
* If you want to receive a response from the business, please enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
