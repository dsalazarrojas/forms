# BP MINI FUNKO POP Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BP MINI FUNKO POP Order Form is designed for customers to place orders for Mini Funko Pops, collectible items, by providing us with essential information for order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure accuracy and efficiency in processing your order, please follow these steps:
*   Fill in your full name, as we'll need this for communication and order processing.
*   Enter your email address, which we'll use to contact you regarding your order.
*   Provide your phone number, which we'll use to communicate any updates or issues with your order.
*   Enter your shipping address, including your full street address.
*   Specify the city where you reside or would like us to ship to.
*   Provide your state or province of residence.
*   Enter your ZIP or postal code.
*   Select your country of residence.
*   Choose the Funko POP character you'd like to order.
*   Select the Product Line (e.g., Marvel, DC Comics, Star Wars, Disney, Animation, Movies, TV Shows, or Games) from the available options.
*   Specify the quantity of the item you wish to order.
*   Enter the unit price in dollars.
*   If applicable, select the condition of the item (Mint in Box, Out of Box, Used/Loose, or No preference).
*   Choose the shipping method (Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, Overnight, or Local Pickup).
*   If desired, add any special requests or notes, such as a gift message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your full name for order processing and communication purposes.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, Required): Provide a valid email address so we can contact you regarding your order.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, Required): Enter your phone number for any order updates or issues.
*   **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, Required): Include your full street address for accurate shipping.
*   **City** (`city`, `text`, Required): Specify the city where you reside or where you would like us to ship to.
*   **State or Province** (`state_province`, `text`, Required): Provide your state or province of residence.
*   **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, Required): Enter your ZIP or postal code for accurate shipping.
*   **Country** (`country`, `text`, Required): Select your country of residence.
*   **Funko POP Character** (`funko_pop_character`, `text`, Required): Choose the Funko POP character you would like to order.
*   **Product Line** (`product_line`, `select_one`, Required): Select from the available product lines (e.g., Marvel, DC Comics, Star Wars, Disney, Animation, Movies, TV Shows, Games, or Other).
*   **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, Required): Specify the number of items you would like to order.
*   **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, `number`, Required): Enter the unit price in dollars.
*   **Subtotal** (`subtotal`, `number`, Optional): This is automatically calculated based on your order details.
*   **Condition Preferred** (`condition`, `select_one`, Optional): If applicable, select the condition of the item (Mint in Box, Out of Box, Used/Loose, or No preference).
*   **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, `select_one`, Required): Choose your preferred shipping method (Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, Overnight, or Local Pickup).
*   **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, `number`, Optional): This is calculated based on your selected shipping method.
*   **Total Amount Due** (`total_amount`, `number`, Optional): This is the subtotal plus shipping costs.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, Required): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, Check, or Cash on Delivery).
*   **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, `text`, Optional): Add any special requests or notes, such as a gift message.
