# Change Management Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an organization's readiness for a change initiative. It will help identify potential risks, barriers, and required resources to ensure successful implementation of the change.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the form completely and accurately.
2. For each field, provide clear and concise answers.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date** (1): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Assessor Name** (2): Enter your name as the person conducting the assessment.
* **Department** (3): Enter the department being assessed.
* **Change Initiative** (4): Enter the name of the change project.
* **Initiative Start Date** (5): Enter the date when the change initiative began.
* **Expected End Date** (6): Enter the date when the change is expected to be complete. (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Change Type** (7): Select the category of change (e.g. Process change, Technology implementation, etc.).
* **Change Scope** (8): Select the scope of the change (e.g. Individual, Team, Department, etc.).
* **Organizational Readiness Level** (9): Select how ready the organization is for the change.
* **Stakeholder Support Level** (10): Select the level of support from stakeholders.
* **Resource Availability** (11): Select whether resources are adequate for the change.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (12): Select the effectiveness of communication.
* **Training Needs Assessment** (13): Select the training needs for the change.
* **Risk Level** (14): Select the risk level of the change.
* **Identified Risks** (15): List key risks (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Impact on Operations** (16): Select the impact on operations.
* **Impact on Employees** (17): Select the impact on employees.
* **Success Criteria** (18): Enter how success will be measured.
* **Key Milestones** (19): List major milestones (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Barriers Identified** (20): List barriers that exist (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Recommendations** (21): Enter recommended actions (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Assessment Conclusion** (22): Select the overall assessment conclusion.
* **Assessor Signature** (23): Enter your full name as the signature.
