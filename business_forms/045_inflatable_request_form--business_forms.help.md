# Inflatable Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a party's inflatable rental needs for events like parties, corporate events, or other gatherings.

## How To Complete This Form
- To get started, select the correct page ( Party Details, Guest Count, Date and Time, etc.) from the form sections below.
- Review each field carefully, making sure to provide accurate and complete information.
- Fill out all required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (\*) in the field labels.
- Submit the completed form to the designated contact for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Details** (text): Enter a brief description of the party or event.
* **Guest Count** (number): Enter the number of guests expected at the party.
* **Date and Time** (date): Enter the date and time of the party.
* **Inflatable Type** (text): Specify the type of inflatable(s) needed for the party.
* **Contact Info** (text): Enter your contact information or the contact information of the person responsible for the party.

## Tips
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the event planning team or the inflatable rental company.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- This form should be filled out for each new event or party to ensure correct and timely ordering of inflatables. 

Note: The form will guide you through the process of selecting the correct options and providing the necessary information for the party's inflatable needs.
