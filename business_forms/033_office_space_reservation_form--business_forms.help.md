# office_space_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The office space reservation form is used to reserve a space in the office for a specific period. This form is used to request a space for meetings, events, or personal use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as instructed by your supervisor or the person requesting the space.
2. Provide the date and start and end times for the reserved period.
3. Choose the type of space you need (Office, Meeting Room, or Open Space).
4. If necessary, select the size of the space you require.
5. Fill in the name of the person reserving the space and the purpose for which it is being reserved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **office_space_reservation** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): The title of the requested space reservation.
* **date** (`2`, `date`, required/optional): The date on which you need the space.
* **start_time** (`3`, `time`, required/optional): The start time of the space reservation.
* **end_time** (`4`, `time`, required/optional): The end time of the space reservation.
* **space_type** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): The type of space you need (Office, Meeting Room, or Open Space).
* **space_size** (`6`, `number`, required/optional): The size of the space you need (if applicable).
* **reserved_by** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): The name of the person reserving the space.
* **reserved_for** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): The purpose for which the space is being reserved.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and clearly to avoid confusion.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, check with your supervisor or the person requesting the space.
* If the space needs to be reserved for a recurring event, indicate the recurrence in the reserved_for field.
* Be respectful of other's space reservations and avoid overbooking.
