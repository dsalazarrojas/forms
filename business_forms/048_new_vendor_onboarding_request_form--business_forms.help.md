# New Vendor Onboarding Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Vendor Onboarding Request Form is used to request the addition of a new vendor to your organization. This form helps us gather all necessary information for successful onboarding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that the vendor is not already in our system by reviewing the vendor list.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Provide detailed descriptions for fields that have a "Description" label.
4. Use the "Submit" button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name** (*): Enter the name of the new vendor.
* **Email**: Enter the vendor's email address.
* **Website**: Enter the vendor's website (if applicable).
* **Phone**: Enter the vendor's phone number.
* **Address**: Enter the vendor's address.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person at the vendor.
* **Goals**: Enter the goals and objectives of the vendor relationship.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the vendor and their services.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the vendor (optional).
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your request.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary information and authority to complete this form.
* Double-check your submissions for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or issues, please reach out to your point of contact for assistance.
