## Distribution Route Optimization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distribution Route Optimization Registration Form is designed to collect information about a customer's distribution route. This form is intended for customers who want to register a new distribution route with their company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name and contact information (name, email, phone number).
2. Enter the company name and website (if applicable).
3. Provide details about the route, including the starting point, destination, distance, and duration.
4. Choose the type of vehicle used for the route.
5. Add any additional notes about the route.
6. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): The customer's name is the primary contact person for the route.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): The customer's email address is used for communication and updates.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): The customer's phone number is used for urgent matters.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): The company name associated with the route.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): The company website (if applicable).
* **Route Starting Point** (`route_starting_point`, text, required): The location where the route starts.
* **Route Destination** (`route_destination`, text, required): The location where the route ends.
* **Route Distance** (`route_distance`, number, optional): The distance of the route (if applicable).
* **Route Duration** (`route_duration`, number, optional): The duration of the route (if applicable).
* **Route Vehicle Type** (`route_vehicle_type`, select one, optional): The type of vehicle used for the route (Truck, Van, Car, or Motorcycle).
* **Route Notes** (`route_notes`, note, optional): Any additional notes about the route.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): The customer's signature confirms that they have registered this route.

Note: The fields marked as optional are only required if the information is applicable to the customer's route.
