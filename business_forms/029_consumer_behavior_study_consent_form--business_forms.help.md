# Consumer Behavior Study Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather informed consent from participants in a consumer behavior study. It's a crucial step in ensuring that you, as a participant, understand the study's purpose, procedures, and potential risks and benefits. Please take a few minutes to read this document carefully and ask questions if you're unsure about anything.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your personal details, including your first and last name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Read through and understand the study's title, purpose, procedures, duration, and potential risks and benefits.
3. Provide your informed consent for data sharing and audio/video recording (if applicable).
4. Sign the form to confirm your participation and agreement to the terms.
5. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian will need to sign the form as well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (participant_first_name)**: Please type your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (participant_last_name)**: Please type your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address (participant_email)**: Enter your primary email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number (participant_phone)**: Provide a contact phone number where we can reach you.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter your birthdate in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Street Address (address)**: Enter your mailing address.
* **City (city)**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State or Province (state)**: Enter your state or province of residence.
* **ZIP or Postal Code (zip_code)**: Enter your postal code.
* **Study Title (study_title)**: Briefly describe the research study's title.
* **Principal Investigator (principal_investigator)**: Enter the name of the lead researcher.
* **Study Purpose (study_purpose)**: Read through and understand the study's purpose.
* **Study Procedures (study_procedures)**: Understand what you will be asked to do during the study.
* **Study Duration (study_duration)**: Learn about the estimated duration of participation.
* **Potential Risks (potential_risks)**: Understand any potential risks associated with participation.
* **Potential Benefits (potential_benefits)**: Learn about any potential benefits of participation.
* **Compensation (compensation)**: Check if you'll receive any compensation for participation.
* **Confidentiality (confidentiality)**: Understand how your data will be protected.
* **Data Usage (data_usage)**: Learn how your data will be used.
* **Voluntary Participation (voluntary_participation)**: Confirm that your participation is voluntary.
* **Right to Withdraw (right_to_withdraw)**: Understand that you can withdraw at any time.
* **Contact for Questions (contact_information)**: Know who to contact for any questions.
* **Audio Recording Consent (audio_recording_consent)**: Provide consent for audio recording (if applicable).
* **Video Recording Consent (video_recording_consent)**: Provide consent for video recording (if applicable).
* **Data Sharing Consent (data_sharing_consent)**: Consent to share anonymized data.
* **Participant Signature (participant_signature)**: Type your full name as a signature.
* **Signature Date (signature_date)**: Enter the date of signing.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature (parent_guardian_signature)**: Required if under 18, type the parent or guardian's signature.

## Tips
- Please review each section carefully to ensure you understand the study's implications.
- Ask questions or seek clarification if you're unsure about anything.
- Sign and date the form to confirm your informed consent.
- If you're under 18, make sure a parent or guardian signs the form as well.
