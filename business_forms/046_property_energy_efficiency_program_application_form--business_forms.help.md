# Property Energy Efficiency Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for property owners to submit an application for the Property Energy Efficiency Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information and permissions required to complete the form.
2. Fill out the form by providing the following information:
	* First Name: Enter your first name.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name.
	* Email: Enter your email address.
	* Street Number: Enter the number of the property's street address.
	* Street Name: Enter the name of the property's street address.
	* City: Enter the city of the property's address.
	* State: Select the state where the property is located (optional).
	* Upgrade Type: Select the type of energy efficiency upgrade you are applying for (Solar, LED Lighting, HVAC, Windows, Doors, Roofing).
	* Name: Enter the name of the property owner or the person submitting the application.
	* Phone Number: Enter the property owner's phone number.
	* Email: Enter the property owner's email address.
	* Electricity Usage: Enter the property's electricity usage in kilowatt-hours (kWh).
	* Gas Usage: Enter the property's gas usage in therms.
	* Water Usage: Enter the property's water usage in gallons.
	* Insulation: Enter the insulation rating of the property (optional).
	* Solar Panels: Enter the number and type of solar panels installed on the property (optional).
	* Solar Tubes: Enter the number and type of solar tubes installed on the property (optional).
	* Energy Efficient Appliances: Enter a list of energy-efficient appliances in the property (optional).
	* Energy Efficient Lighting: Enter a list of energy-efficient lighting features (optional).
	* Energy Efficient Window Treatment: Enter a description of the energy-efficient window treatment (optional).
	* Plan Description: Enter a brief description of the proposed energy efficiency plan.
	* Agreement: Enter your agreement to the program's terms and conditions.
