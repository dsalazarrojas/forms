# service_resource_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The service resource report form is used to collect information about a service resource for reporting and tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the service resource you wish to report on.
2. Choose the date of your report.
3. Indicate whether the report is a yes or no report.
4. Enter the resource count (e.g., number of units available).
5. Enter the resource value (e.g., amount spent on the resource).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **service_resource** (`Number`, `optional`): This field allows you to select the service resource you are reporting on. Enter the corresponding number from your inventory or database.
* **report_date** (`Date`, `optional`): Enter the date you are reporting on. Use the calendar icon to select the date if you need help with formatting.
* **report_type** (`Select One`, `optional`): Indicate whether the report is a 'Yes' or 'No' report. This helps us track the type of report you are submitting.
* **resource_count** (`Number`, `optional`): Enter the count of resources available or used. For example, if you have 10 units available, enter 10.
* **resource_value** (`Number`, `optional`): Enter the value or amount spent on the resource being reported. For example, if you spent $100 on the resource, enter 100.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately select the service resource you are reporting on to avoid confusion.
* Use the calendar icon to help format the report date correctly.
* Enter the report type as 'Yes' or 'No' to ensure we understand the nature of your report.
* Double-check your resource count and value entries for accuracy.
* This form should only be completed by authorized personnel to maintain data integrity.
