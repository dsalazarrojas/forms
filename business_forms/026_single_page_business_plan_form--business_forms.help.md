# single_page_business_plan_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help entrepreneurs, startups, or small business owners create a one-page business plan that outlines their business goals, target industry, location, and team composition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
* Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to make sure you haven't missed any crucial details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Goal** (`business_goal`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your business's main objective or goal. This could be a mission statement, a brief summary of your product or service, or a description of your target market. 
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more of the provided industries that best represent your business. This will help us understand your business's nature and scope.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Enter the city, state, zip, or region where your business operates or plans to operate. This is essential for us to understand your business's geographical presence.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of team members you have or plan to have for your business. This will help us understand your business's manpower and resource allocation.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering each question.
* Use clear and concise language to ensure your answers are easily understandable.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
