# funding_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about potential funding requests. It gathers details such as company name, individual's name, email, address, a brief description of the project, the amount of money requested, the expected date of the project, and the time frame for completion. The form is intended to help assess and process funding proposals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter the company name of the applicant in the `company_name` field.
2. Provide your personal name in the `name` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
4. Enter the address of the location for the project in the `address` field.
5. Describe the project in the `description` field.
6. Enter the amount of money requested in the `amount` field.
7. Select a date for the project timeline in the `date` field.
8. Enter the time frame for the project completion in the `time` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company or organization requesting funding.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your personal name as the representative of the company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted about your funding proposal.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the physical address of the location of the project.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the project that is being funded.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of money requested for the project.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for the project timeline.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time frame for the project completion.
