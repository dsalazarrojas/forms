The Spa Franchise Inquiry Registration Form is designed to gather contact and business information from potential spa franchises. This form can be completed by individuals interested in franchising a business or learning more about our franchise opportunities. The form's purpose is to gather necessary details to further assess the viability of a potential franchise and provide more information to help them move forward with the franchise process.

Before continuing, please note that this form is intended for internal use within your organization. Please ensure that you're not submitting the form multiple times or for different franchise opportunities.

Below is a step-by-step guide on how to fill out this form:

## Purpose
The Spa Franchise Inquiry Registration Form is designed to gather contact and business information from potential spa franchises to help assess their viability and provide more information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select your preferred contact method**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted: Phone, Email, or Both.
2. **Enter your spa details**: Choose your location, business name, type, and select one of our available spa brands.
3. **Enter your contact information**: Provide your contact person, contact number, email, and any additional notes.
4. **Enter your business information**: Confirm your business license status, franchise fee (if applicable), and franchise fee currency.
5. **Additional details**: Select the method you'd like to be followed up with (Phone, Email, or Mail) and schedule (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
6. **Enter your website and address**: Provide your spa franchise's website and address.
7. **Enter your spa franchise details**: Enter your spa franchise's name, phone number, email, and any additional notes.
