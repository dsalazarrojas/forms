# Labor Cost Reconciliation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Labor Cost Reconciliation Request Form is used to identify and correct discrepancies between actual and reported labor costs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select the project**: From the project dropdown list, select the project for which you want to complete the labor cost reconciliation.
2. **Select the period**: Choose the period for which you want to complete the labor cost reconciliation.
3. **Choose who reconciled the cost**: Select the person or team responsible for the labor cost reconciliation.
4. **Add any additional notes**: If needed, enter any additional notes about the labor cost reconciliation.
5. **Check the project status**: Select the status of the project, if it is active or inactive.
6. **Enter total labor cost**: Enter the total labor cost for the project and period.
7. **Enter reconciled cost**: Enter the cost that has been reported.
8. **Calculate the difference**: The system will automatically calculate the difference between the total labor cost and the reconciled cost.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project**: Select the project that the labor cost reconciliation is for.
*  **Period**: Choose the period that the labor cost reconciliation is for. This could be a month or quarter of the year.
*  **Reconciled By**: Choose who performed the labor cost reconciliation. This could be a person or a team.
*  **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the labor cost reconciliation.
*  **Project Status**: Select the status of the project, if it is active or inactive.
*  **Total Labor Cost**: Enter the total labor cost for the project and period.
*  **Reconciled Cost**: Enter the cost that has been reported.
*  **Difference**: This field will automatically be filled in with the calculated difference between the total labor cost and the reconciled cost.
*   **Tips**
	+ Ensure that you are selecting the correct project and period to avoid incorrect labor cost reconciliations.
	+ Double-check the total labor cost and reported cost to ensure accuracy.
	+ If you have any questions or concerns, contact the accounting department.
