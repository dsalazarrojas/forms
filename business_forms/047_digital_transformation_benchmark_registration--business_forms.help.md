# Digital Transformation Benchmark Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Transformation Benchmark Registration form is designed to collect data from organizations that are undergoing or planning digital transformation initiatives. This information will help us understand their current state and provide tailored recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your organization's name and type (if applicable).
2. Indicate your organization's current digital maturity stage.
3. Specify your goals for the benchmark program.
4. Answer whether you have engaged or plan to engage with consultants, research teams, or tech providers.
5. Provide your email address and phone number (if applicable).
6. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter your organization's full name.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if your organization is a public or private company.
* **Digital Maturity Stage** (`digital_maturity_stage`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Goals for Benchmark Program** (`goals_for_benchmark_program`, text, required): Describe your organization's goals for the benchmark program.
* **Consultant** (`consultant`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if your organization has or plans to engage with a consultant.
* **Research Team** (`research_team`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if your organization has or plans to engage with a research team.
* **Tech Provider** (`tech_provider`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if your organization has or plans to engage with a tech provider.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your organization's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your organization's phone number (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your organization's digital transformation journey.
