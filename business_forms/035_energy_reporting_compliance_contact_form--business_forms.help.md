# Energy Reporting Compliance Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients and customers about their energy-related concerns, questions, or issues. Please fill out this form for each type of energy and company separately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant type of energy for which you want to submit your contact information.
2. Fill out the required information for the selected type of energy, which may include your contact details, company name, location, and other relevant fields.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for your company type, industry, and location.
4. Describe your question or issue clearly in the "What is your question?" field.
5. If you have any additional information, please provide it in the "Additional Information" field.
6. You can also provide additional information for each type of energy and company separately, if needed.
7. Finally, make sure to review your completed form carefully before submitting it.
