# Digital Delivery Partner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for digital delivery partners to register their information for collaboration and partnership opportunities with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your contact and vehicle information.
2. If you have multiple vehicles, fill in the additional vehicle information fields.
3. Select the insurance provider and insurance expiration date for your vehicle(s).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User Name**
* (`user_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. **Email**
* (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your work email address associated with the partnership.

### 3. **Phone**
* (`phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.

### 4. **Address**
* (`address`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your work address for partnership purposes.

### 5. **Company Name**
* (`company_name`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your company name, if applicable.

### 6. **Job Title**
* (`job_title`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your job title at your company.

### 7. **Vehicle Type**
* (`vehicle_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of vehicle you drive (Car, Motorcycle, or Bicycle).

### 8. **Vehicle Make**
* (`vehicle_make`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the make of your vehicle.

### 9. **Vehicle Model**
* (`vehicle_model`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the model of your vehicle.

### 10. **Vehicle Year**
* (`vehicle_year`, `number`, `optional`): Enter the year of your vehicle.

### 11. **Vehicle Color**
* (`vehicle_color`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the color of your vehicle.

### 12. **Vehicle Registration**
* (`vehicle_registration`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your vehicle registration information.

### 13. **Driver License**
* (`driver_license`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your driver's license number.

### 14. **Social Security Number**
* (`social_security_number`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your social security number (for identification purposes).

### 15. **Insurance Provider**
* (`insurance_provider`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you have insurance for your vehicle.

### 16. **Insurance Expiration**
* (`insurance_expiration`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the expiration date of your insurance.

### 17. **Vehicle Insurance**
* (`vehicle_insurance`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your vehicle insurance information.

### 18. **Vehicle License**
* (`vehicle_license`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your vehicle license number.

### 19. **License Number**
* (`license_number`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your license number.

### 20. **Vehicle Type 2**
* (`vehicle_type_2`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select any additional vehicles you may have, if applicable.

### 21. **Vehicle Make 2**
* (`vehicle_make_2`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the make of your additional vehicle, if applicable.

### 22. **Vehicle Model 2**
* (`vehicle_model_2`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the model of your additional vehicle, if applicable.

### 23. **Vehicle Year 2**
* (`vehicle_year_2`, `number`, `optional`): Enter the year of your additional vehicle, if applicable.

### 24. **Vehicle Color 2**
* (`vehicle_color_2`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the color of your additional vehicle, if applicable.

### 25. **Vehicle Registration 2**
* (`vehicle_registration_2`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the registration of your additional vehicle, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure your information is up-to-date and accurate.
* You can add additional vehicles if you have multiple vehicles registered.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [support email or phone number].
