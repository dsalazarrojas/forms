# Training Development Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Training Development Plan" form is used to create a comprehensive plan for training programs, including details on goals, objectives, target audience, and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the training program.
2. On "Training Goals", describe the main objectives of the training program.
3. On "Target Audience", select the relevant options that apply to your training program.
4. Enter the training period using the date field.
5. Enter the training duration in number of hours or days.
6. Select the training schedule from the options provided.
7. On "Training Methods", describe the methods and approaches that will be used to deliver the training program.
8. On "Training Evaluation", estimate the time required for the evaluation process.
9. Enter the estimated cost of the training program.
10. Add any additional comments or notes about the training program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Goals** (text): Describe the main objectives of the training program.
* **Target Audience** (select multiple): Select the relevant options that apply to your training program (e.g., employees, customers, managers).
* **Training Period** (date): Enter the start and end dates of the training program.
* **Training Duration** (number): Enter the number of hours or days the training program will run.
* **Training Schedule** (select one): Select the training schedule from the options provided (e.g., daily, weekly).
* **Training Methods** (text): Describe the methods and approaches that will be used to deliver the training program.
* **Training Evaluation** (time): Estimate the time required for the evaluation process.
* **Training Cost** (number): Enter the estimated cost of the training program.
* **Comments** (note): Add any additional comments or notes about the training program.
* **Email** (email): Enter the email address of the trainer or training manager.
* **Additional Fields** (text): These fields can be used to capture additional information about the training program, but are not mandatory.
