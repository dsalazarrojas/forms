# Epicor Job Role Calibration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Epicor Job Role Calibration Form is a standardized form designed to collect job role information for new roles within an organization. This form helps in maintaining consistency and accuracy in job role definitions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields: Job Title, Job Function, and Reporting Manager Email.
2. Select the appropriate options for Team Members, Role Level, and Job Classification.
3. Provide the necessary information for Reporting Manager, Reporting Manager Email, and Department.
4. Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the job role in the provided text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Role** (`new_role`, text, required): This field should be filled out with the name of the new job role.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the role.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the manager of the role.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the manager of the role.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required): Select whether the role involves team members. Options: Yes or No.
* **Role Level** (`role_level`, select_one, required): Select the role level: Junior, Mid-Level, or Senior.
* **Reporting Line** (`reporting_line`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the reporting line.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, required): Enter the job function of the role.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Email** (`reporting_manager_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the reporting manager.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the team lead.
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the job classification.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the department.
* **Reporting Manager Title** (`reporting_manager_title`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the reporting manager's title.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the job level.
* **Reporting Line Level** (`reporting_line_level`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the reporting line.
* **Role Level Important** (`role_level_important`, text, optional): Enter any information about why the role level is important.
* **Reporting Manager Job Function** (`reporting_manager_job_function`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the reporting manager's job function.
* **Job Classification Important** (`job_classification_important`, text, optional): Enter any information about why the job classification is important.
* **Job Title Important** (`job_title_important`, text, optional): Enter any information about why the job title is important.
* **Job Function Important** (`job_function_important`, text, optional): Enter any information about why the job function is important.
* **Team Lead Name** (`team_lead_name`, text, optional): Enter any information about the team lead's name.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" should be filled out. Optional fields can be left blank unless additional information is necessary for the job role.
