# Partnership Agreement Amendment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Partnership Agreement Amendment Form is a document used to record changes to a partnership agreement. It ensures that all parties are aware of the modifications made to the original agreement and maintains a record of the agreement's history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the agreement date, which is the date the original partnership agreement was signed.
2. Choose the amending party from the provided options (Partner 1, Partner 2, or Partner 3).
3. Enter the details of the amending party, providing a brief description of their role or affiliation.
4. Describe the amended terms, detailing the changes made to the original agreement.
5. Provide a brief description of the amended terms.
6. State the reason for the amendment.
7. Describe the amended terms in more detail.
8. Indicate when the amended terms are effective.
9. Specify the person or party making the amendment (Client or Lawyer).
10. Enter any additional details regarding the amendment, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Date**: The date the original partnership agreement was signed.
* **Amendment Date**: The date the amendment to the partnership agreement is being made.
* **Amending Party**: Select the party making the amendment (Partner 1, Partner 2, or Partner 3).
* **Amending Party Details**: Briefly describe the amending party's role or affiliation.
* **Amended Terms**: Enter the changes made to the original partnership agreement.
* **Amended Terms Description**: Briefly describe the changes.
* **Reason for Amendment**: State the reason for the change.
* **Amendment Description**: Describe the changes in more detail.
* **Effective Date**: The date when the amended terms become effective.
* **Amended By**: Specify the person or party making the amendment (Client or Lawyer).
* **Amended By Details**: Enter any additional details regarding the amendment.
