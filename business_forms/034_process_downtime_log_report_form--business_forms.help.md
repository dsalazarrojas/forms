# Process Downtime Log Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to log and report manufacturing process downtimes, providing a centralized record of events and their corresponding details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date and time of the downtime event.
2.  Specify the duration of the downtime event.
3.  Select the manufacturing team responsible for the downtime.
4.  Choose the shift during which the downtime occurred.
5.  Classify the downtime event and provide any additional relevant details.
6.  Identify the production line and equipment affected by the downtime.
7.  Specify the cause of the downtime event.
8.  Add any additional comments or notes about the event.
9.  Document the current production status and any actions taken.
10. Summarize the root cause of the downtime event.
11. Provide recommendations for future prevention or improvement.
12. Document any follow-up actions or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manufacturing Team**: The team responsible for the downtime event. This can be a specific team or "N/A" if not applicable.
	+ Select the team from the options provided.
* **Downtime Event Date**: The date of the downtime event.
	+ Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Downtime Start Time**: The time when the downtime event started.
	+ Enter the time in the format 12:00 AM.
* **Downtime Duration**: The length of time the downtime event lasted.
	+ Enter the duration in minutes.
* **Description**: A brief description of the downtime event.
	+ Enter a short but detailed description of what happened.
* **Downtime Classification**: Classification of the downtime event.
	+ Select "Yes" or "No" for the classification options or provide a custom classification.
* **Downtime Classification Options**: Additional details about the classification.
	+ Provide any relevant details or explanations about the classification.
* **Production Line**: The production line affected by the downtime event.
	+ Select from the options provided.
* **Equipment**: The equipment affected by the downtime event.
	+ Select "Yes" or "No" for the equipment options or provide a custom selection.
* **Downtime Cause**: The reason for the downtime event.
	+ Select from the options provided.
* **Other Downtime Cause**: If the downtime cause is not listed, provide an explanation.
	+ Enter a detailed explanation of the downtime cause.
* **Shift**: The shift during which the downtime occurred.
	+ Select from the options provided.
* **Production Status**: The current production status.
	+ Select from the options provided.
* **Action Taken**: Any actions taken during or after the downtime event.
	+ Enter a detailed explanation of the actions taken.
* **Preventive Action**: Preventive actions taken to avoid future downtimes.
	+ Enter a detailed explanation of the preventive actions.
* **Root Cause**: A brief explanation of the root cause of the downtime.
	+ Enter a short but detailed explanation.
* **Recommendations**: Recommendations for future prevention or improvement.
	+ Enter a detailed explanation of the recommendations.
* **Follow-up Actions**: Follow-up actions or notes.
	+ Enter a detailed explanation of the follow-up actions or notes.
* **Notes**: Additional comments or notes about the downtime event.
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes.
