# Business Group Membership Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Group Membership Application Form is a comprehensive application for businesses to join your organization. It is used to gather necessary information to process your application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information: Provide your name, email, and phone number in the "Primary Contact" section.
2. Enter your business information: Provide your business name, type, years in operation, and address in the "Business Information" section.
3. Select your industry: Choose your industry from the list provided in the "Industry Classification" section.
4. Choose your membership type: Select your preferred membership level from the "Membership Type" section.
5. Specify your payment method: Choose how you would like to pay your membership dues in the "Payment Method" section.
6. Provide any additional information: Answer any other questions that apply to you and your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Contact Name** (2): Enter your name so we can contact you about your application.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you.
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Title/Position** (5): Enter your role in your organization.
* **Business Name** (7): Enter the name of your business.
* **Business Type** (8): Select the type of business you are.
* **Years in Business** (9): Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Business Address** (10): Enter your business address.
* **City** (11): Enter the city of your business.
* **State/Province** (12): Enter the state or province of your business.
* **Postal Code** (13): Enter your postal code.
* **Industry Classification** (14): Select the industry of your business.
* **Company Size** (15): Select the number of employees in your business.
* **Membership Type** (17): Select your preferred membership level.
* **Membership Goals** (18): Enter your reasons for joining our organization.
* **Previously a Member?** (19): Select if you have been a member before.
* **How did you hear about us?** (20): Select who referred you or how you heard about our organization.
* **Committee or Working Group Interest** (22): Enter any committees or groups you are interested in participating in.
* **Volunteer Participation Interest** (23): Select if you would be willing to volunteer.
* **Payment Method** (25): Select how you would like to pay your membership dues.
