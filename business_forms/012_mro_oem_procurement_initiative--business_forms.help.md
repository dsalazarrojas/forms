# MRO & OEM Procurement Initiative - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MRO & OEM Procurement Initiative form is designed to guide users through the process of identifying and implementing procurement objectives, budget allocation, supplier selection criteria, and timelines for MRO and OEM procurement processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Overview of MRO and OEM Procurement Processes**: On the first page, write a brief description of the MRO and OEM procurement processes.
2. **Procurement Objectives and Goals**: On the second page, describe the procurement objectives and goals for the initiative.
3. **Budget Allocation for MRO and OEM Procurement**: On the third page, specify the budget allocated for the MRO and OEM procurement processes. This field is **required**.
4. **Supplier Selection Criteria**: On the fourth page, select the criteria for choosing suppliers, such as **Price**, **Quality**, or **Delivery Time**. This field is **required**.
5. **Procurement Timelines and Deadlines**: On the fifth page, enter the procurement timelines and deadlines for the initiative. This field is **not required**.
6. **Procurement Budget Tracking**: On the sixth page, track the procurement budget for the initiative. This field is **required**.
7. **Additional Information**: On the seventh page, enter any additional information related to the procurement initiative.
8. **Supplier Details**: On the eighth page, select the suppliers involved in the procurement process. You can select multiple suppliers.
9. **Contact Information**: On the ninth page, enter your contact information, including your email address. This field is **required**.
10. **Notes**: On the tenth page, enter any additional notes or comments about the procurement initiative. This field is **not required**.
11. **Attachments**: On the eleventh page, attach any relevant documents or files related to the procurement initiative. This field is **not required**.
11. **Review and Approval**: On the twelfth page, review and approve the procurement initiative by selecting one of the options, such as **Option A** or **Option B**. This field is **required**.
