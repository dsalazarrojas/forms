# Furniture Inventory Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Furniture Inventory Checklist is a form used to track and record detailed information about furniture items in inventory. This includes asset type, status, description, room name, price, purchase date and time, and inventory number. By using this form, you can efficiently manage your inventory and make it easier to identify and categorize items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Furniture Asset Type** from the dropdown menu. This will help categorize the item as a chair, desk, or fridge, for example.
2. Choose the **Status** of the item by selecting all that apply from the dropdown menu. You can select one or multiple statuses, such as New, Used, or Broken.
3. Enter a brief **Description** of the item to provide more context and details.
4. Enter the **Room Name** where the item is located.
5. Enter the **Item Price**, if applicable.
6. Select the **Date Purchased** in mm/dd/yyyy format.
7. Select the **Time Purchased** if you want to record the exact time of purchase.
8. Enter the **Furniture Inventory Number**, a unique identifier for the item.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Furniture Asset Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of furniture asset, such as chair, desk, or fridge.
* **Status** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all that apply to indicate the item's status, such as New, Used, or Broken.
* **Description** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the item to provide more context.
* **Room Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the room where the item is located.
* **Item Price** (Number, Optional): Enter the price of the item, if applicable.
* **Date Purchased** (Date, Optional): Select the date the item was purchased in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Time Purchased** (Time, Optional): Select the time the item was purchased, if applicable.
* **Furniture Inventory Number** (Text, Optional): Enter a unique identifier for the item in your inventory.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if applicable.
* Use the dropdown menus to quickly select options.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Save your form regularly to avoid losing your progress.
