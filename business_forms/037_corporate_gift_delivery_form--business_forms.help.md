# Corporate Gift Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Gift Delivery Form is used to request a gift delivery on behalf of a team or department within the company. This form helps to gather essential information about the gift recipient and the gift details to ensure smooth and efficient delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name in the "company name" field.
2. Enter the recipient's name in the "recipient name" field.
3. Select a delivery date in the "delivery date" field (optional).
4. Select a delivery time in the "delivery time" field (optional).
5. Enter the recipient's delivery address in the "delivery address" field.
6. If applicable, enter the gift item name and description in the "gift item name" and "gift item description" fields respectively.
7. Enter the gift value in the "gift value" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the company that will be sending the gift.
* **Recipient name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the person who will receive the gift.
* **Delivery date** (3, date, optional): Select a date for the gift delivery. If not selected, the default date will be used.
* **Delivery time** (4, time, optional): Select a time for the gift delivery. If not selected, the default time will be used.
* **Delivery address** (5, text, required): Enter the address where the gift will be delivered.
* **Gift item name** (6, text, optional): Enter the name of the gift being delivered (if applicable).
* **Gift item description** (7, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the gift being delivered (if applicable).
* **Gift value** (8, number, required): Enter the value of the gift being delivered.
