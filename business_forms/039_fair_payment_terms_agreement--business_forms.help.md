# Fair Payment Terms Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect business and payment information from clients. It is essential for understanding the terms and conditions of the agreement between your business and the client, including payment details and timeline of the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required".
3. Enter your business information (page 1) and payment information (page 2) accurately.
4. Select a date for the project timeline (page 3).
5. Enter the client's email address (page 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Business Information (Page 1)
* **Business Information** (`business_info`, text, optional): Enter your business's name, address, and any other relevant details.
### Payment Information (Page 2)
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount or details.
### Timeline (Page 3)
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, date, optional): Select the date when the payment is due.
### Client Information (Page 4)
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address to keep them informed about project updates.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication with your clients.
* Double-check that all fields are filled in correctly before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions about the form, please contact your supervisor or support team for assistance.
* This form is for internal business use only. Do not share it with external parties without permission.
