# Business Ethics Advisory Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Ethics Advisory Workshop Registration Form is designed to gather information from attendees who wish to participate in the Business Ethics Advisory Workshop. This form helps us understand your expectations, preferences, and needs to tailor the workshop experience to your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your basic information, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, company name, job title, and department (if applicable).
2. Select your preferred workshop date and session (Morning or Afternoon).
3. Choose your preferred format (In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid).
4. Indicate your expertise level in business ethics (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
5. Select the topics you are interested in learning about.
6. Briefly describe your learning goals and objectives.
7. If you have any dietary restrictions or special accommodations, please let us know.
8. If there are additional attendees from your company, please let us know how many.
9. Indicate your company's commitment to ethics (Strong, Moderate, Developing, or Just Starting).
10. Finally, if you have any questions or comments, please share them in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email Address**: Your company email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your work phone number.
* **Company Name**: The name of your organization.
* **Job Title**: Your position within your company.
* **Department**: Your department within your company (if applicable).
* **Preferred Workshop Date**: The date you prefer to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Session**: You can choose from Morning (9am-12pm) or Afternoon (1pm-4pm) sessions.
* **Preferred Format**: You can choose from In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid formats.
* **Ethics Expertise Level**: Indicate your level of experience in business ethics (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
* **Topics of Interest**: Select all the topics you are interested in learning about.
* **Learning Goals**: Briefly describe what you hope to achieve from the workshop.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, etc.), please select them.
* **Special Accommodations**: If you have any special needs or accommodations, please let us know.
* **Additional Attendees**: If there are additional attendees from your company, please specify how many.
* **Company Commitment to Ethics**: Indicate your company's level of commitment to ethics (Strong, Moderate, Developing, or Just Starting).
* **Questions or Comments**: If you have any other questions or comments, please share them with us.
