# Vendor Access Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Access Authorization Form is used to grant access to a company's resources to a vendor. This form helps ensure that vendors have the necessary access to perform their tasks efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Company Name field with the name of the company you are authorizing access for.
2. Provide the necessary vendor information in the vendor_info field.
3. Select the desired Access Type from the dropdown menu (Read-Only or Read-Write).
4. Enter the User ID of the user you are authorizing access for.
5. Enter the Password for the user.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (text, required): Fill in the name of the company you are authorizing access for.
* **vendor_info** (text, optional): Provide any additional information about the vendor, such as their contact details or relevant notes.
* **Access Type** (select_one, optional): Choose whether the vendor should have Read-Only or Read-Write access to the company's resources.
* **User ID** (number, optional): Enter the ID of the user you are authorizing access for.
* **Password** (text, optional): Enter the password of the user.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the vendor information and access type to avoid any potential issues with access.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor for assistance.
* This form should only be filled out when it's actually needed and not duplicated.
