# Medical Academy Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather personal information from applicants to the Medical Academy. Please complete this form thoroughly and accurately to increase the chances of being accepted into the Medical Academy.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have read and understood the form purpose and instructions above.
2. Fill out the form in a new browser tab or window.
3. Complete all required fields with accurate information.
4. Double-check your submission before sending.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yy).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required: false): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant medical history or conditions that may impact your application.
* **Medical Academy ID** (`medical_academy_id`, text, required: false): Enter your Medical Academy ID if you have one.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your submission before sending.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the Medical Academy staff.
