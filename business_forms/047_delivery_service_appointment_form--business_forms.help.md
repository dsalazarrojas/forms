# Delivery Service Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create a delivery service appointment for an order, allowing customers to provide their preferred delivery date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your address and contact information in **delivery_service_appointment_form_1_label**.
2. Select your preferred delivery date from **delivery_service_appointment_form_2_label**.
3. Choose your preferred delivery time from **delivery_service_appointment_form_9_label**.
4. Fill in any additional comments or notes in **delivery_service_appointment_form_7_label**.
5. Enter your email address for contact purposes in **delivery_service_appointment_form_6_label**.
6. Fill in any further information or comments in **delivery_service_appointment_form_8_label**.
7. Select the preferred delivery option from **delivery_service_appointment_form_3_label** (not used in this case).
8. Fill in the expected delivery date in **delivery_service_appointment_form_4_label**.
9. Fill in the expected delivery time in **delivery_service_appointment_form_5_label**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **delivery_service_appointment_form_1_label** (text): Enter your address details for delivery purposes.
* **delivery_service_appointment_form_2_label** (select one): Choose your preferred delivery date from the available options.
* **delivery_service_appointment_form_3_label** (select one): Choose your preferred delivery option (not used in this case).
* **delivery_service_appointment_form_6_label** (email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **delivery_service_appointment_form_7_label** (text): Fill in any additional comments or notes.
* **delivery_service_appointment_form_8_label** (text): Fill in any further information or comments.
* **delivery_service_appointment_form_9_label** (select one): Choose your preferred delivery time.
* **delivery_service_appointment_form_4_label** (date): Enter the expected delivery date.
* **delivery_service_appointment_form_5_label** (text): Fill in the expected delivery time.
* **delivery_service_appointment_form_6_label** (text): Enter a note or comment about the delivery.
