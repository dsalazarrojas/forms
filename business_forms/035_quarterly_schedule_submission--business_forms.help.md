# Quarterly Schedule Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Quarterly Schedule Submission form is a tool used to collect and review employee schedules and plans for the current quarter. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your quarterly plans in the "Quarterly Plans" field.
3. Enter your weekly schedules in the "Weekly Schedules" field.
4. Add any risk notes or concerns in the "Risk Notes" field.
5. If applicable, describe any special department planning considerations in the "Department Planning" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official employee identification.
* **Quarterly Plans** (`quarterly_plans`, text, required/optional): Describe your goals and objectives for the quarter. Be specific and provide details on what you aim to accomplish and how you will achieve them.
* **Weekly Schedules** (`weekly_schedules`, text, required/optional): Outline your weekly tasks and activities. Include due dates and any relevant deadlines.
* **Risk Notes** (`risk_notes`, text, required/optional): List any potential risks or concerns that may impact your schedule or progress.
* **Department Planning** (`department_planning`, text, required/optional): Provide any specific planning considerations that are relevant to your department or team.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure that your information is up-to-date and correct.
- Use clear and concise language when describing your plans and schedules.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form or its purpose, please contact the relevant department or manager for assistance.
