# Site Safety Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Site Safety Inspection Report is a form used to collect information about site safety inspections. It is designed to help site managers, safety officers, and other personnel responsible for site safety gather the necessary data to identify and address potential hazards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Site Location" field with the specific location where the safety inspection was conducted.
2. Enter the "Date Of Inspection" and "Time Of Inspection" fields with the dates and times when the inspection took place.
3. Describe the site conditions in the "Site Conditions" field, including any notable hazards or concerns.
4. In the "Site Inspection Report" field, provide a detailed account of the inspection, including any findings, observations, and recommendations.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
6. Assign the report to a specific person or group in the "Assigned To" field.
7. Record the date the report was assigned in the "Assigned Date" field.
8. If the report has been reviewed, enter the name of the person who reviewed it in the "Reviewed By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Location** (`site_location`, Text, required): Enter the specific location where the safety inspection was conducted, such as a warehouse, construction site, or office building.
* **Date Of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`, Date, required): Enter the date when the safety inspection was conducted.
* **Time Of Inspection** (`time_of_inspection`, Time, required): Enter the time when the safety inspection was conducted.
* **Site Conditions** (`site_conditions`, Text, required): Describe the site conditions, including any notable hazards or concerns.
* **Site Inspection Report** (`site_inspection_report`, Text, required): Provide a detailed account of the inspection, including any findings, observations, and recommendations.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, Text, optional): Assign the report to a specific person or group.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, Date, optional): Record the date the report was assigned.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, Text, optional): If the report has been reviewed, enter the name of the person who reviewed it.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, Date, optional): Record the date the report was assigned.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Be clear and concise in the descriptions and comments provided.
* Review the report carefully before submitting it to ensure all relevant information is included.
* Make sure to assign the report to the correct person or group and record the assigned date accurately.
* If the report has been reviewed, ensure the reviewer is aware of any changes or updates made to the report.
