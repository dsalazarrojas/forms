# Document Retrieval Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture employee information for document retrieval purposes, such as requesting or confirming access rights for documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* If you are submitting a document retrieval request, enter your personal details.
	* If you are confirming document access rights, enter the employee's information.
2. Review and confirm the form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Document Retrieval Agreement** (`document_retrieval_agreement`): Enter a brief description of the document retrieval request or confirmation.
* **Organization** (`organization`): Select 'Yes' if you are affiliated with the organization, 'No' otherwise.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter the name of the employee.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter the department of the employee.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): Enter the email address of the employee's manager.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Document Types** (`document_types`): Select the types of documents you are requesting access to.
* **Document Retrieval Agreement 1** (`document_retrieval_agreement_1`): Enter the number of documents requested.
* **Document Retrieval Agreement 2** (`document_retrieval_agreement_2`): Enter the number of documents requested (if applicable).
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`): Enter the phone number of the employee's manager.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Document Date** (`document_date`): Enter the date of the document (if applicable).
* **Document Time** (`document_time`): Enter the time of the document (if applicable).
