# Vendor Risk Review Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to review and assess the risks associated with a particular vendor or third-party entity. It helps ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and provides an easy and streamlined process for internal teams and vendors to review and complete the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide accurate and truthful information for each field. If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant team or vendor before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form carefully, as each field is crucial in determining the vendor's risk level.
3. Ensure all fields are completed before submitting the form.
4. Review and understand the form instructions and field explanations provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Vendor Details** (`vendor_details`, text, optional)
* Provides a brief description of the vendor, including their name, address, and a brief overview of their services or products.

### 2. **Risk Category** (`risk_category`, select_one, optional)
* Selects the risk category that best describes the level of risk associated with the vendor. Options include Low, Medium, and High.

### 3. **Certifications** (`risk_certifications`, select_multiple, optional)
* Selects the relevant certifications or standards the vendor has achieved, such as ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and GDPR.

### 4. **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional)
* Provides contact information for the vendor, including name, job title, and department.

### 5. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
* The vendor's email address for communication purposes.

### 6. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
* The vendor's phone number for communication purposes.

Note: The phone field is a text field and not a numerical keypad. Enter the full phone number in the format (XXX) XXX XXXX.
