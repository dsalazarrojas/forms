# Tax Compliance Confirmation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request confirmation of tax compliance from a business. It helps ensure that the business has met the required tax standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for "What is the current tax status" field, depending on whether the business is in compliance or not.
2. If the business is not in compliance, provide a brief explanation in the notes section.
3. If you have supporting documents, upload them in the "Supporting Documents" field.
4. Fill out the "Individual Business Name", "Business Type", "Business License Number", "Address", "Contact Name", "Phone", "Email", and "Notes" fields with the relevant information.
5. Upload any additional supporting documents in the "Upload Supporting Documents" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the current tax status** (`tax_status`, select_one, required): Select "In Compliance", "Not Compliant", or "Unknown" to indicate the business's tax status.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you sign this form in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, not required): Enter your signature.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, note, not required): Upload supporting documents if applicable.
* **Individual Business Name** (`individual_business_name`, text, not required): Enter the individual business name.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, not required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the business is an individual or not.
* **Business License Number** (`business_license_number`, text, not required): Enter the business license number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, not required): Enter the business address.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, not required): Enter the contact name.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter the business phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter the business email.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes.
* **Upload Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents_upload`, text, not required): Upload supporting documents if applicable.
* **File Name** (`file_name`, text, not required): Enter the file name of the uploaded document.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have reviewed the business's tax status before filling out this form.
* If the business is not in compliance, please provide a brief explanation in the notes section.
* Upload supporting documents if applicable to demonstrate the business's compliance.
* Fill out all fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the form is complete and useful.
