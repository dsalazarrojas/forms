# VSM Item Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The VSM Item Record Form is used to create a new VSM item or update an existing one. This form collects essential details about an item, including its ID, name, description, category, tags, status, and priority.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (Item ID, item_name, and due_date).
2. Select the relevant options for category, tags, and status.
3. Enter any additional comments or descriptions about the item.
4. Click 'Submit' to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Item ID** (`vsm_item_id`, number, required): A unique identifier for the VSM item. This will be used to track and reference the item within your VSM repository.
* **Item Name** (`vsm_item_name`, text, required): A descriptive title for the VSM item, which should be short and concise.
* **Item Description** (`vsm_item_description`, text, required): A brief explanation of what the item is about.
* **Item Category** (`vsm_item_category`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the item has a category or not ('Yes' or 'No'). This field will help you group similar items within your VSM.
* **Item Tags** (`vsm_item_tags`, select_multiple, required): Add any relevant tags or keywords to help you filter and search for similar items.
* **Item Status** (`vsm_item_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the item ('Active' or 'Inactive'). This will determine its visibility and availability within your VSM.
* **Due Date** (`vsm_item_due_date`, date, required): Enter the date by which the item is due or should be completed.
* **Item Priority** (`vsm_item_priority`, number, optional): Assign a numerical value to indicate the item's priority, with higher values indicating higher priority.

## Tips
- Ensure that you provide accurate and relevant information to get the most out of your VSM repository.
- Review your entries before submitting to avoid errors or inaccuracies.
- Use the 'Update' feature to edit or modify existing VSM items.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your administrator or colleague for assistance.
