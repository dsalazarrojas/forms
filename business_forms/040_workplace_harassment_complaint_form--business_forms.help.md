# Workplace Harassment Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Harassment Complaint Form is a tool to report and document incidents of harassment that occur within the workplace. This form helps ensure a clear understanding of the incident, the actions taken by the organization, and the support offered to the complainant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Report a Complaint** (Page 1)
   - Click on the "Report a Complaint" page to start the complaint process.
2. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Description of Harassment**: Provide a detailed account of what happened, including the circumstances surrounding the incident.
   - **Incident Location**: Specify the location where the harassment occurred.
   - **Date of Incident**: Enter the date when the harassment took place.
   - **Date of Report**: Record the date you are submitting this complaint.
   - **Name of Complainant**: Enter your name as the person filing the complaint.
   - **Name of Respondent**: Identify the person involved in the harassment, if known.
   - **Description of Harassment**: Give further details about the incident, including the actions taken by the respondent.
   - **Incident Details**: Provide any additional information that might be relevant to the investigation.
   - **Impact of Harassment**: Describe how the incident affected you, including emotional and professional impact.
   - **Prevention of Further Harassment**: Suggest what actions can be taken to prevent future incidents.
   - **Support Offered**: Specify any support services or actions you've taken or received to address the situation.
   - **Resolution Status**: Choose the current status of the complaint: Ongoing, Closed, or In Progress.
   - **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information that might be helpful.
3. Sign with your **Signature of Complainant**: This confirms you are the person filing the complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report a Complaint**: This is the initial page to start the complaint process.
* **Description of Harassment** (`description_of_harassment`, text, required): Describe what happened, including the circumstances surrounding the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): Specify the location where the harassment occurred.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, text, required): Enter the date when the harassment took place.
* **Date of Report** (`date_of_report`, text, required): Record the date you are submitting this complaint.
* **Name of Complainant** (`name_of_complainant`, text, required): Enter your name as the person filing the complaint.
* **Name of Respondent** (`name_of_respondent`, text, optional): Identify the person involved in the harassment, if known.
* **Description of Harassment** (`description_of_harassment`, text, required): Give further details about the incident, including the actions taken by the respondent.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, required): Provide any additional information that might be relevant to the investigation.
* **Impact of Harassment** (`impact_of_harassment`, text, required): Describe how the incident affected you, including emotional and professional impact.
* **Prevention of Further Harassment** (`prevention_of_further_harassment`, text, optional): Suggest what actions can be taken to prevent future incidents.
* **Support Offered** (`support_offered`, text, optional): Specify any support services or actions you've taken or received to address the situation.
* **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the complaint: Ongoing, Closed, or In Progress.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information that might be helpful.
* **Signature of Complainant** (`signature`, text, required): This confirms you are the person filing the complaint.

## Tips

* Fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure the organization can effectively address the complaint.
* If you are unsure about any information or need help, contact your HR department or a designated support contact.
* Please note that this form is for internal use only within the organization and any information submitted will be kept confidential.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the process, do not hesitate to reach out.
