# Animal Training Innovation Grant Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Training Innovation Grant Application form is designed to collect information from applicants for the Animal Training Innovation Grant. The form helps applicants provide details about their project, including contact information, project description, goals, timeline, budget, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact and organization information (fields 1-5).
2. Describe your project in detail, including title, summary, category, and goals (fields 6-11).
3. Provide information about your project team, including team members and their qualifications (fields 23).
4. Explain your project's expected outcomes and how you will measure the impact (fields 16-17).
5. Describe your plan for sharing and disseminating your results (field 18).
6. Address any ethical considerations, particularly related to animal welfare (field 20).
7. Outline your project's timeline and budget (fields 8-10, 21-22).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Primary contact name.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Position title.
*   **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Institution or company name.
*   **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, select one, required): Select the type of organization you belong to.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Contact email.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Contact phone number.
*   **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Street address of your organization.
*   **City** (`city`, text, required): City of your organization.
*   **State or Province** (`state`, text, required): State or province of your organization.
*   **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): ZIP or postal code of your organization.
*   **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Name of your innovation project.
*   **Project Summary** (`project_summary`, text, required): Brief description of your innovation project in 200 words or less.
*   **Project Category** (`project_category`, select one, required): Select the category of your innovation project.
*   **Species Focus** (`species_focus`, select multiple, required): Select all species your project focuses on.
*   **Describe the Innovation** (`innovation_description`, text, required): Describe what makes your approach new or different.
*   **What Problem Does This Solve** (`problem_statement`, text, required): Describe the need or gap your project addresses.
*   **Project Objectives** (`objectives`, text, required): List specific measurable goals.
*   **Methodology** (`methodology`, text, required): Describe how you will accomplish your project goals.
*   **Project Timeline** (`timeline`, text, required): Outline your project timeline, including start and end dates with specific milestones.
*   **Grant Amount Requested** (`grant_amount_requested`, number, required): Enter the grant amount you are requesting in dollars.
*   **Budget Breakdown** (`budget_breakdown`, text, required): Explain how you plan to use the granted funds.
*   **Other Funding Sources** (`other_funding`, text, optional): List any additional funding sources you have or plan to seek.
*   **Project Team Members** (`team_members`, text, required): Describe your project team members and their qualifications.
*   **Principal Investigator Background** (`principal_investigator`, text, required): Provide details about your principal investigator's background, including relevant experience and credentials.
*   **Previous Related Work** (`previous_work`, text, optional): Describe any previous related work you have done.
*   **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, required): Anticipate the expected outcomes of your project.
*   **How Will Impact Be Measured** (`impact_assessment`, text, required): Explain how you will measure the impact of your project.
*   **Dissemination Plan** (`dissemination_plan`, text, required): Describe your plan for sharing and disseminating your results.
*   **Sustainability Plan** (`sustainability`, text, optional): Explain how your project will be sustained after funding.
*   **Ethical Considerations** (`ethical_considerations`, text, required): Address any ethical considerations, particularly related to animal welfare.
*   **Required Approvals** (`approvals_needed`, text, optional): If required, list any approvals or clearances you need.
*   **Potential Risks and Challenges** (`risks_challenges`, text, required): List potential risks and challenges you anticipate and how you will address them.
*   **Supporting Materials** (`supporting_materials`, text, optional): If needed, provide supporting materials for your project.
*   **Professional References** (`references`, text, required): Provide professional references with contact information.
*   **I Accept the Grant Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, select one, required): Accept or decline the grant terms and conditions.
*   **Electronic Signature** (`applicant_signature`, text, required): Type your full name.
*   **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submit your application.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   Provide clear and concise answers to each field.
*   Proofread your answers for completeness and clarity.
*   Attach any supporting materials you need to provide.
*   Make sure to provide professional references with contact information.
*   Review the grant terms and conditions before accepting or declining them.
*   Sign your application with your electronic signature.
*   Enter the correct application date.
