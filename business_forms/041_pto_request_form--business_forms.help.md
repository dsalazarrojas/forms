# PTO Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PTO Request Form is used to submit a request for paid time off to your manager. Please complete this form to provide necessary details for approval.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your request.
2.  Fill out the form with as much detail as possible to help your manager make an informed decision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **PTO Request Form** (`pto_request_form`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your request in a few words. It's a good idea to keep it concise but clear.
*   **Date of Absence** (`date_of_absence`, date, optional): Choose the date(s) you would like to take as paid time off. If you have multiple dates, separate them with commas.
*   **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): If you need to specify start time for your absence, fill in the start time. This field is only required if you're requesting a specific time slot.
*   **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): If you need to specify end time for your absence, fill in the end time. This field is only required if you're requesting a specific time slot.
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): This field is for your manager's reference. It's a good idea to include your employee ID or any other relevant details.
*   **Start Reason** (`start_reason`, text, optional): Briefly describe the reason for your start date or time of absence.
*   **End Reason** (`end_reason`, text, optional): Briefly describe the reason for your end date or time of absence.
*   **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): If you have a preferred manager for this request, fill in their name.
*   **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): If you have a preferred manager's email address, fill it in. This helps them get in touch with you.
*   **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): If you have a preferred manager's phone number, fill it in. This helps them contact you if needed.
*   **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`, note, optional): This field is for your manager to take notes on your request.
*   **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`, note, optional): This field is for you to take notes on your request, if needed.
*   **Manager Approved** (`manager_approved`, select one, optional): Your manager will use this field to indicate approval or denial of your request.
*   **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): This field is for your manager to add any comments on your request.
