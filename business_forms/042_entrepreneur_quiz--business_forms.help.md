# Entrepreneur Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help entrepreneurs evaluate their skills, experience, and background.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing specific examples or anecdotes when possible.
2. Be honest and thorough in your responses.
3. Review each question carefully to ensure you're providing the most accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for this form, if desired.
* **what_is_your_goal** (`form_question_1`, text, optional): Describe your professional goal or objective as an entrepreneur. What motivates you, and what do you want to achieve in your business?
* **what_is_your_answer** (`form_question_1_answer`, text, optional): Provide a more detailed explanation or examples of your goal or objective.
* **what_is_your_background** (`form_question_2`, text, optional): Describe your educational and professional background, including relevant work experience, skills, and achievements.
* **what_is_your_answer_2** (`form_question_2_answer`, text, optional): Provide more details about your background, including relevant courses, training, or certifications.
* **what_are_your_skills** (`form_question_3`, text, optional): List your relevant skills, such as communication, leadership, problem-solving, or time management.
* **what_is_your_answer_3** (`form_question_3_answer`, text, optional): Provide examples or anecdotes of how you've applied your skills in previous situations.
* **what_is_your_experience** (`form_question_4`, text, optional): Describe your relevant work experience, including job titles, companies, and industries.
* **what_is_your_answer_4** (`form_question_4_answer`, text, optional): Provide details about your experience, such as job responsibilities, achievements, or challenges you overcame.
* **what_is_your_education** (`form_question_5`, text, optional): List your relevant education, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **what_is_your_answer_5** (`form_question_5_answer`, text, optional): Provide details about your education, such as relevant coursework, academic achievements, or academic challenges you faced.
* **what_is_your_background_2** (`form_question_6`, text, optional): Describe your relevant hobbies, interests, or personal projects that relate to your business or professional background.
* **what_is_your_answer_6** (`form_question_6_answer`, text, optional): Provide more details about your background, including how they relate to your business or professional goals.
* **what_are_your_skills_2** (`form_question_7`, text, optional): List your additional skills, such as languages, software proficiency, or soft skills.
* **what_is_your_answer_7** (`form_question_7_answer`, text, optional): Provide examples or anecdotes of how you've applied your additional skills in previous situations.
* **what_is_your_experience_2** (`form_question_8`, text, optional): Describe your relevant non-work experience, including volunteer work, personal projects, or leadership roles.
* **what_is_your_answer_8** (`form_question_8_answer`, text, optional): Provide details about your experience, such as challenges you faced, achievements you accomplished, or lessons you learned.
* **what_is_your_education_2** (`form_question_9`, text, optional): List your relevant ongoing education or training, including courses taken or certifications earned.
* **what_is_your_answer_9** (`form_question_9_answer`, text, optional): Provide details about your education or training, such as how you applied the knowledge or skills gained.
* **what_are_your_skills_3** (`form_question_10`, text, optional): List your relevant skills or areas of interest that you're developing or improving.
* **what_is_your_answer_10** (`form_question_10_answer`, text, optional): Provide examples or anecdotes of how you're developing or applying these skills.
* **what_are_your_skills_4** (`form_question_11`, text, optional): List your relevant skills or areas of interest that you're currently learning or improving.
* **what_is_your_answer_11** (`form_question_11_answer`, text, optional): Provide examples or anecdotes of how you're learning or applying these skills.
* **what_is_your_background_3** (`form_question_12`, text, optional): Describe your relevant background or personal characteristics that influence your business or professional decisions.
* **what_is_your_answer_12** (`form_question_12_answer`, text, optional): Provide more details about your background or characteristics, including how they influence your business or professional decisions.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Review each question carefully to ensure you're providing the most accurate information.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes whenever possible.
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or manager for clarification.
