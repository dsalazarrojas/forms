# Business Automation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Business Automation Checklist form aims to gather information about your company's current state of automation, helping us understand your current processes and pain points, and identify potential opportunities for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Company Information (Page 1)**: Enter your company's name and provide a brief description.
2. **Business Details (Page 2-3)**: Select your company's industry and size, and provide your name and contact information.
3. **Automation Level and Tools (Page 4-5)**: Indicate your current automation level, the tools you're using, and the departments that are utilizing automation within your organization.
4. **Automation Needs and Goals (Page 6-7)**: Identify the processes and areas you'd like to automate, and choose the goals you aim to achieve through automation.
5. **Technical and Cultural Readiness (Page 8-9)**: Indicate your current technical and cultural readiness for automation, including your IT resources and change readiness.
6. **Additional Comments (Page 10)**: Provide any additional information or context that might be helpful for us to understand your business's unique situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Information (1)**
	+ **Company Name**: Your company's name.
	+ **About your business**: A brief description of your company.
* **Industry (3)**: Select your company's industry from the given options.
* **Company Size (4)**: Choose the number of employees in your organization.
* **Contact Name (5)**: Enter your full name.
* **Contact Email (6)**: Enter your work email address.
* **Contact Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Current Automation (8)**: Describe your current automation level and what you're currently automating.
* **Current Automation Level (9)**: Select your current automation level.
* **Current Tools (10)**: Choose the tools you're currently using for automation.
* **Departments Using Automation (11)**: Select the departments within your organization using automation.
* **Automation Needs (12)**: Describe what you'd like to automate.
* **Processes to Automate (13)**: Choose the processes you'd like to automate.
* **Pain Points (14)**: Describe your current pain points or manual tasks that are problematic.
* **Time Spent on Manual Tasks (15)**: Choose the amount of time spent on manual tasks per week.
* **Automation Goals (16)**: Select the goals you aim to achieve through automation.
* **Integration Needs (17)**: Describe any integration needs with other systems.
* **Current Software Systems (18)**: Choose the software systems you're currently using.
* **CRM System (19)**: Select your current CRM system.
* **ERP System (20)**: Select your current ERP system.
* **Budget and Timeline (21)**: Describe your expected investment and timeline for automation.
* **Budget Range (22)**: Choose your expected budget range for automation.
* **Implementation Timeline (23)**: Select when you'd like to start automation implementation.
* **IT Resources (24)**: Indicate whether you have dedicated IT staff or IT resources.
* **Change Readiness (25)**: Select your organization's change readiness for automation.
* **Additional Comments (26)**: Enter any additional comments or information to provide context.

## Tips

- Take your time to answer each question thoughtfully, as your input will help us better understand your business's unique situation.
- If you're unsure or have questions about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask.
- This form is designed to gather information, not to commit you to any specific course of action.

By following this guide, you'll be able to provide us with valuable information to understand your business's current state of automation and identify potential areas for improvement.
