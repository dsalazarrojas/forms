# Smart Queue Management Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for the management of smart queue applications. It includes details about the customer, their visit date and time, and any other relevant information to facilitate the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Details field with the customer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Select the Visit Date from the calendar provided.
3. Enter the Start and End Time of the visit.
4. Choose the Queue Length from the dropdown options (Short, Medium, Long).
5. Enter any additional Customer Details in the text field.
6. Add any notes about the application in the Note field.
7. Enter the customer's Email address.
8. Enter the customer's Phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Details
* **User Details** (`text`, `User Details`, optional): This field is for entering the customer's name and contact information. Please provide a clear and concise description of the customer, including their name, address, and any other relevant details.

### Visit Date
* **Visit Date** (`date`, `Visit Date`, optional): Select the date of the customer's visit from the calendar. Make sure to choose the correct date to ensure accurate processing.

### Start Time
* **Start Time** (`time`, `Start Time`, optional): Enter the start time of the customer's visit. This is important for scheduling and timing purposes.

### End Time
* **End Time** (`time`, `End Time`, optional): Enter the end time of the customer's visit. This is also important for scheduling and timing purposes.

### Queue Length
* **Queue Length** (`select_one`, `Queue Length`, optional): Choose the length of the queue from the dropdown options (Short, Medium, Long). This helps in prioritizing the customer's visit.

### Customer Details
* **Customer Details** (`text`, `Customer Details`, optional): Add any additional details about the customer, such as their preferences or special requests.

### Note
* **Note** (`note`, `Note`, optional): Add any notes or comments about the customer's application. This field is for any additional information that may be useful for processing.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `Email`, optional): Enter the customer's email address for easy communication.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`text`, `Phone`, optional): Enter the customer's phone number for easy communication.

The provided form fields should not be left blank as some are optional but still provide important information. Please ensure to fill in all fields as accurately as possible to facilitate efficient processing and follow-up on queue management.
