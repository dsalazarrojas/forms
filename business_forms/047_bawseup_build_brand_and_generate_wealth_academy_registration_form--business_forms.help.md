# BawseUp Build, Brand And Generate Wealth Academy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BawseUp Build, Brand And Generate Wealth Academy Registration Form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in joining the 90-day intensive business and branding program. This form serves as the primary entry point for prospective students to submit their application and enroll in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Carefully review each page to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. For fields with hints, use the information provided to help guide your input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Enrollee Full Name
* **Full Name** (`enrollee_full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.

### 2. Primary Email Address
* **Primary Email Address** (`enrollee_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address that we will use to communicate with you about the program and any updates.

### 3. Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`enrollee_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you about the program and any important updates.

### 4. Current Business Name
* **Current Business Name** (`current_business_name`, `text`, optional): If you have an existing business, please enter its name. If not, leave this field blank.

### 5. Primary Goal
* **What is your primary goal for the next 90 days?** (`primary_business_goal`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: 
  * Launch New Business
  * Rebrand Existing Business
  * Increase Revenue/Sales
  * Scale Operations
  * Establish Personal Brand

### 6. Business Challenges
* **Describe your top 2 business challenges** (`specific_business_challenges`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the two main challenges you are facing in your business, so we can better understand your needs and provide tailored support.

### 7. Academy Commitment and Access
* **I agree to the 90-day commitment and weekly video sessions** (`commitment_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: 
  * I Agree and Commit

### 8. Facebook Profile Link
* **Link to your Facebook Profile** (`facebook_profile_link`, `text`, required): Provide a link to your public Facebook profile for us to verify your identity.

### 9. Tuition Payment
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_selection`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following payment options: 
  * Credit Card (Full Payment)
  * PayPal (Full Payment)
  * Installment Plan (If pre-approved)

### 10. How did you find us?
* **How did you hear about BawseUp Academy?** (`how_did_you_find_us`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options: 
  * Instagram
  * Facebook
  * Podcast
  * Referral
  * Website

### 11. Date of Enrollment
* **Date of Enrollment** (`enrollment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are enrolling in the program.

This form is designed to be filled out in a straightforward and efficient manner, ensuring that you have all the necessary information to get started with the 90-day intensive business and branding program. If you have any questions or need further clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us.
