# Timber Frame Construction Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details and agreements of a timber frame construction project between the client and the contractor.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Project Details** section with the project's name, location, and any other relevant information.
2. Provide **Client Information** including your name, contact details (email and phone number), and address.
3. Specify the **Project Start Date** and **Project End Date** for the project timeline.
4. Describe the **Project Scope** to outline the work to be done and any specific requirements.
5. Fill out the **Contractor Information** section with their name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
6. Sign and date both the **Client Signature** and **Contractor Signature** fields to confirm agreement.
7. Enter the **Client Email**, **Contractor Email**, and **Client Phone**, and **Contractor Phone** fields with the relevant contact information.
8. Fill out the **Client Address** and **Contractor Address** fields with the addresses of the project location and the contractor's business address, respectively.
9. Specify the **Project Location** where the project will be built or constructed.
10. Fill out the **Contract Price** field with the total cost of the project.
11. Read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions** by signing and dating both the **Client Signature** and **Contractor Signature** fields.
