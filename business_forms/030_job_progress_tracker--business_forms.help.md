# job_progress_tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "job_progress_tracker" form is a tool for project managers to track the progress of a job, including general information, project tasks, deadlines, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by reviewing the fields listed below and fill them out accordingly.
2. For each field, follow the specific instructions and explanations provided in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`General Info`, text, optional): This field is for entering any general information related to the job. You can type any relevant details about the project here.
* **Project Tasks** (`Project Tasks`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes for the tasks that are currently being worked on. The options are 'Yes' and 'No'. Select 'Yes' for tasks that are active and 'No' for tasks that are not.
* **Deadlines** (`Deadlines`, date, optional): Enter the deadline for the project or any relevant dates that need to be tracked.
* **Milestone Info** (`Milestone Info`, select_one, optional): Check the box to confirm whether a milestone has been achieved or not. The options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Team Members** (`Team Members`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes for team members who are actively working on the project. The options are 'Yes' and 'No'. Select 'Yes' for team members who are working and 'No' for those who are not.
* **Task Progress** (`Task Progress`, number, optional): Enter the progress of the tasks being worked on, represented as a percentage (e.g., 50% for 50% completion).
* **Deadline** (`Deadline`, date, optional): Enter the deadline for the project or any specific dates that need to be tracked.
* **Assigned Team Lead** (`Assigned Team Lead`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team lead or project manager assigned to the job.
* **Progress Notes** (no field label, implied): This field is not explicitly listed, but it's implied as a text box for users to enter any progress notes or comments.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and according to your project's needs.
* Review and update the form regularly to reflect any changes in project progress.
* Use the form to track and discuss progress with your team and stakeholders.
