# Storage Facility Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about incidents that occur within storage facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date the incident occurred in the "Incident Date" field.
2. Provide the location where the incident took place in the "Incident Location" field.
3. Select the type of incident in the "Incident Type" field.
4. Enter a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
5. If applicable, provide an image related to the incident in the "Incident Image" field.
6. Fill in the "Reporter Name" field with your contact information.
7. Enter your email in the "Reporter Email" field.
8. Choose the status of the incident in the "Incident Status" field.
9. Select who reported the incident in the "Incident Reporter" field.
10. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Reporter Notes" field.
11. If you are not the reporter, fill in the "Reviewer Name" field with the reviewer's contact information.
12. Enter the reviewer's email in the "Reviewer Email" field (optional).
13. Select the date the reviewer completed their review in the "Review Date" field (optional).
14. Enter the reviewer's comments in the "Review Comments" field (optional).
15. Select the reviewer's decision on the incident in the "Reviewer's Decision" field.
16. Enter an incident number to uniquely identify the incident in the "Incident Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Incident Date**: The date the incident occurred.
- **Incident Location**: Enter the location of the incident.
- **Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident (e.g., Fire, Theft, Environmental, Other).
- **Incident Description**: Enter a detailed description of the incident.
- **Incident Image (optional)**: If applicable, upload an image related to the incident.
- **Reporter Name**: Your contact information.
- **Reporter Email**: Your email address.
- **Incident Status**: Choose the current status of the incident (Open or Closed).
- **Incident Reporter**: Choose who reported the incident (Facility Manager, Maintenance, Employee).
- **Reporter Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the incident.
- **Reviewer Name (optional)**: If applicable, fill in the reviewer's contact information.
- **Reviewer Email (optional)**: Enter the reviewer's email address.
- **Review Date (optional)**: Select the date the reviewer completed their review.
- **Review Comments (optional)**: Enter the reviewer's comments about the incident.
- **Reviewer's Decision (optional)**: Choose the reviewer's decision on the incident (No Action Needed, Review and Investigate, Escalate).
- **Incident Number**: A unique identifier for the incident.
