# Lean Manufacturing Consultant Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lean Manufacturing Consultant Contact Form is a means of gathering information from potential clients or customers for consultations or services provided by the consultant. This form allows users to provide their contact details and specify their specific consultation requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address accurately.
2. Enter your Company name and its Website (if applicable).
3. Choose your preferred Consultant (Jim or Jane).
4. Select the type of consultation you need (Consulting, Coaching, or Training).
5. Indicate if you require on-site services (yes or no).
6. Select the language in which you need service (yes or no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your active and valid email address for communication.
* **Company** (text, optional): If applicable, enter your company's name.
* **Company Website** (text, optional): If you have a company website, enter the URL.
* **Consultant** (select_one, optional): Choose between consulting options provided, Jim or Jane.
* **Consultation Type** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the provided consultation types, such as Consulting, Coaching, or Training.
* **Service Location** (select_one, optional): Choose whether you need on-site services (yes or no).
* **Service Language** (select_one, optional): Select the language in which you require service (yes or no).
