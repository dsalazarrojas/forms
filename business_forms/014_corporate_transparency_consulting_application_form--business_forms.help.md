# Corporate Transparency Consulting Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect general information about a company's corporate transparency, contact details, business specifics, and more. It will help us better understand your company's needs and provide you with tailored consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the general information section with a brief description of your company and its purpose.
2. Provide contact details, including your name, email, phone number, and company address.
3. Answer questions about your company's financials, risk assessment, and service details.
4. Select your company's characteristics, such as its size, industry, and type.
5. Provide additional comments or information about your company.
6. Confirm your submission by clicking on the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (Page 1)**:
	+ General Information (`general_information`, text, optional): A brief description of your company and its purpose.
* **Contact Details**:
	+ Contact Name (`contact_name`, text, optional): The name of the contact person.
	+ Contact Email (`contact_email`, email, optional): The email address of the contact person.
	+ Contact Phone (`contact_phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the contact person.
	+ Company Address (`company_address`, text, optional): The address of your company.
	+ Website (`website`, text, optional): The website of your company.
* **Business Information**:
	+ Business Hours (`business_hours`, date, optional): The date you would like to receive the service.
* **Risk Assessment**:
	+ Corporate Transparency (`corporate_transparency`, select_one, optional): How would you rate your company's corporate transparency? (Good, Fair)
* **Company Characteristics**:
	+ Company Type (`company_type`, select_multiple, optional): Is your company public (Yes, No)?
	+ Industry (`industry`, select_one, optional): Is your company in a specific industry? (Yes, No)?
	+ Company Size (`company_size`, number, optional): The number of employees in your company.
	+ Employee Count (`employee_count`, number, optional): The number of employees in your company.
	+ Employee Type (`employee_type`, select_one, optional): What type of employees do you have? (Yes, No)?
	+ Employee Title (`employee_title`, select_one, optional): What is the title of your employees? (Yes, No)
	+ Employee Salary (`employee_salary`, number, optional): The average salary of your employees.
* **Additional Comments**:
	+ Additional Comments (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any additional information you would like to provide.
* **Submission Details**:
	+ Submission Details (`submission_details`, text, optional): Any additional information about your submission.
* **Confirm Submission**:
	+ Confirm Submit 2 (`confirm_submit_2`, select_one, optional): Confirm your submission. (Yes, No)

Note: Please fill out the fields as prompted, and submit your form when finished.
