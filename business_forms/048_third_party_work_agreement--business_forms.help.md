# Third Party Work Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Third Party Work Agreement form is used to record and document the details of a working relationship between an individual or entity and the company, including the services to be provided, payment terms, and duration of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client or third party with whom you are establishing a working relationship.
2. **Work Description**: Provide a brief description of the work that will be performed by the third party.
3. **Agreement Type**: Select the type of agreement (Business, Personal, or Partnership) that best describes the nature of the relationship.
4. **Services Provided**: List the specific services that will be provided by the third party.
5. **Service Frequency**: Select the frequency at which the services will be provided (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
6. **Payment Terms**: Specify the payment terms, including any relevant details such as payment frequency or method.
7. **Payment Method**: Select the payment methods that will be used (Bank Transfer, Cheque, or Cash).
8. **Agreement Duration**: Enter the duration of the agreement in months.
9. **Agreement Start Date**: Enter the start date of the agreement.
10. **Agreement End Date**: Enter the end date of the agreement (if applicable).
11. **Agreement Status**: Select the current status of the agreement (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled).
12. **Third Party**: Enter any relevant information about the third party, such as their name and contact details.
13. **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person to contact regarding the agreement.
14. **Contact Details**: Enter any additional contact information for the person responsible for the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: The name of the client or third party with whom you are establishing a working relationship.
* **Work Description**: A brief description of the work that will be performed by the third party.
* **Agreement Type**: The type of agreement (Business, Personal, or Partnership) that best describes the nature of the relationship.
* **Services Provided**: A list of the specific services that will be provided by the third party.
* **Service Frequency**: The frequency at which the services will be provided (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Payment Terms**: The payment terms, including any relevant details such as payment frequency or method.
* **Payment Method**: The payment methods that will be used (Bank Transfer, Cheque, or Cash).
* **Agreement Duration**: The duration of the agreement in months.
* **Agreement Start Date**: The start date of the agreement.
* **Agreement End Date**: The end date of the agreement (if applicable).
* **Agreement Status**: The current status of the agreement (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled).
* **Third Party**: Additional information about the third party, such as their name and contact details.
* **Contact Person**: The name and contact information of the person responsible for the agreement.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the agreement carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is included.
* Be clear and concise in your descriptions and entries to avoid confusion.
* Keep the agreement up-to-date and make sure to update the status of the agreement as needed.
* Store the agreement in a secure location, such as a digital archive, for easy access and reference.
