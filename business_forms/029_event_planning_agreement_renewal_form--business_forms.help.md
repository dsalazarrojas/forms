# event_planning_agreement_renewal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Planning Agreement Renewal Form is used to renew an existing event planning agreement. This form is designed to collect information necessary to update and extend the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the event planning agreement and ensure it is the correct agreement to be renewed.
2. Answer the questions in the "Client Information" section to confirm the client's identity and agreement.
3. In the "Event Information" section, choose the correct event type and status.
4. Provide the date and time of the renewal in the "Date and Time" section.
5. Enter the location of the renewal in the "Location" section.
6. Write a short description of the agreement and any additional details in the "Agreement Details" section.
7. Enter any relevant event details in the "Event Details" section.
8. Confirm the agreement status in the "Agreement Status" section.
9. Select the agreement type in the "Agreement Type" section.
10. Confirm the event status in the "Event Status" section.
11. Select the event type in the "Event Type" section.
12. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" section.
13. Enter your email address in the "Email" section.
