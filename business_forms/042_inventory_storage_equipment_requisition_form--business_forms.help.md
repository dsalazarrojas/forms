# Inventory Storage Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the necessary information for requisitioning equipment from inventory. Please fill out the form below to submit your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields as follows:

1. **Requisition Form 1**:
	* Enter a brief description of the equipment you are requesting.
2. **Equipment Description**:
	* Provide a detailed description of the equipment you are requesting.
3. **Quantity**:
	* Enter the number of units of the equipment you are requesting.
4. **Location**:
	* Enter the location where the equipment will be used or stored.
5. **Priority**:
	* Enter a number indicating the level of priority for this requisition (e.g., 1 for high, 2 for medium, 3 for low).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requisition Form 1**: Enter a brief description of the equipment you are requesting. (Required)
* **Equipment Description**: Provide a detailed description of the equipment you are requesting. (Required)
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of units of the equipment you are requesting. 
* **Location**: Enter the location where the equipment will be used or stored. 
* **Priority**: Enter a number indicating the level of priority for this requisition (e.g., 1 for high, 2 for medium, 3 for low). 

## Tips
* Ensure that you are requesting equipment for a legitimate business need.
* Check that the equipment is not already duplicated in another requisition.
* If you are unsure about the equipment or the information required, contact the relevant team or department for assistance.
