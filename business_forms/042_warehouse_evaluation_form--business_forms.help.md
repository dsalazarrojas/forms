# warehouse_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The warehouse_evaluation_form is designed to gather feedback from customers about the quality of the warehouse services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate the service you received by selecting one of the options on the first page.
2. Provide your thoughts on the quality of service in the comments section.
3. Select how you got to the warehouse from the options provided.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **rate_warehouse_services** (`rate_warehouse_services`, select_one, optional): Rate the service you received by selecting one of the three options: 1, 2, or 3.
* **how_good_was_service** (`service_quality`, number, optional): Provide a numeric rating of the service quality, with higher numbers indicating better service.
* **what_was_warehouse_condition** (`warehouse_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to describe the condition of the warehouse.
* **comments** (`comment_section`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and feedback about the service you received.
* **how_did_you_get_here** (`contact_info`, select_one, optional): Select how you got to the warehouse, or choose "Other" and provide a description.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional feedback or comments you'd like to share.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **rate_services_again** (`rating_2`, select_one, optional): Rate the service again, selecting one of the three options.

## Tips
* Be honest and constructive in your feedback, as it will help us improve our services.
* Make sure to select all that apply when rating the warehouse condition.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
