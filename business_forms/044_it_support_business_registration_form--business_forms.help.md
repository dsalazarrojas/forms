# it_support_business_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The it_support_business_registration_form is a registration form for businesses to provide their details to the IT department. This form will help us understand the services provided by your business and ensure correct listing in our IT inventory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your company's email address and phone number in the respective fields.
3. Add your company's name and address in the respective fields.
4. Provide a brief description of your business and what services you offer.
5. Select the IT services you provide from the list.
6. Review your information carefully before clicking the "Submit" button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your company's email address in this field.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's phone number in this field.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's name in this field.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's address in this field.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's address in this field (if different from above).
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your business and what services you offer.
* **IT Services** (`it_services`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the services you provide from the list. You can select multiple services if applicable.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Review your information carefully and click this button to submit your registration.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, `text`, required: false): Click this button to cancel your registration and return to the previous page.

Note: The "Cancel" button is not required for submission, but it provides a way to cancel your registration if you need to start over or abandon the form.
