# intern_shift_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The intern_shift_report_form is used to collect information related to an intern's shift work, which includes start and end times, shift hours, task name, progress, comments, supervisor name, assigned tool, and their own name. This form helps track and record the details of an intern's work schedule and performance during their shifts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the shift you wish to report on.
2. Enter the start time of the shift.
3. Enter the end time of the shift.
4. Record the total hours worked during the shift.
5. Enter the name of the task assigned to you during the shift.
6. Provide a brief description of your progress on the task.
7. Add any comments about your shift, if necessary.
8. Enter the name of your supervisor who assigned the shift.
9. Specify any tool or equipment assigned to you for the shift.
10. Enter your name, as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shift Date** (id: 1, type: date, required: false)
	+ This field is used to select the date of the shift you wish to report on. Select the date from the available options.
* **Start Time** (id: 2, type: time, required: false)
	+ Enter the start time of the shift. This is typically the time you began your work on the assigned task.
* **End Time** (id: 3, type: time, required: false)
	+ Enter the end time of the shift. This is the time you completed your work or the shift ended.
* **Shift Hours** (id: 4, type: number, required: false)
	+ Record the total hours worked during the shift. This can be calculated manually or using a time-tracking system.
* **Task Name** (id: 5, type: text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the task assigned to you during the shift.
* **Progress** (id: 6, type: text, required: false)
	+ Provide a brief description of your progress on the assigned task. This can include details about what was achieved during the shift.
* **Comments** (id: 7, type: text, required: false)
	+ Add any comments or feedback about your shift. This can be used to note any issues or successes you experienced.
* **Supervisor Name** (id: 8, type: text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of your supervisor who assigned the shift. This helps track communication and accountability.
* **Assigned Tool** (id: 9, type: text, required: false)
	+ Specify any tools or equipment assigned to you for the shift. This can include software, hardware, or other resources used.
* **Intern Name** (id: 10, type: text, required: false)
	+ Enter your name, as the intern reporting on the shift. This is a required field for your identification.
