# Corporate Citizenship Strategy Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather contact information from companies interested in learning more about our corporate citizenship strategy. It will help us understand your company's needs and preferences, allowing us to provide more tailored support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as instructed below. Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to collect and share contact information from your company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Text, Required): Enter your company's official name as it appears on their website or marketing materials.
* **Company Type** (Select One, Optional): Please select the type of organization you represent. This will help us better understand your specific needs.
	+ Choose from: Company, Consultant, Nonprofit
* **Contact Person** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for corporate citizenship efforts or someone who can be contacted for more information.
* **Department** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the department within your company responsible for corporate citizenship efforts.

## Tips
- Ensure that the company's name is accurate and consistent across all marketing materials.
- If you are a representative of a consulting firm, please provide your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
- If you are a nonprofit, please provide your organization's name in the "Company Name" field.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us directly.
