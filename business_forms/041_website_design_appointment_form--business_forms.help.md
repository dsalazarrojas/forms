# Website Design Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect client and project information, as well as schedule appointments for website design services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Client Details section with your information.
2. In the Project Details section, provide a brief description of your project.
3. Select your preferred communication methods in the Preferred Communication section.
4. Choose a date and time for your appointment in the Appointment Date and Appointment Time sections.
5. Add any additional project preferences or notes in the Project Preferences and Notes sections.
6. Enter your email address in the Email field.
7. Finally, provide your phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, company, and contact information.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Give a brief description of your project, including any relevant details or context.
* **Preferred Communication** (`preferred_communication`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of day you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Project Preferences** (`project_preferences`, text, optional): Share any additional project preferences or requirements.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* If you have any specific needs or requirements for your project, add them to the Project Preferences and Notes fields.
* If you have a preferred contact method, select it in the Preferred Communication field.
* If you need to schedule an appointment, select a date and time in the Appointment Date and Appointment Time fields.
