# Price Quote Kf - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Price Quote Kf form is designed to collect customer information for quote purposes, allowing you to quickly gather essential details for quote-related tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the `Name` field.
2. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
3. Type your email address in the `Email` field. Make sure to use the correct format (e.g., example@example.com).
4. Select a date for the quote from the calendar icon next to the `Date` field.
5. Choose a time for the quote from the clock icon next to the `Time` field.
6. Enter any additional notes regarding the quote in the `Note` field.
7. Select one option from the `Select One` dropdown menu.
8. Select multiple options from the `Select Multiple` dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Business Form Fields (text)** (`1`, `text`, required: false): 
No input is required for this field, as it's an optional field for adding additional information.
### **Name (text)** (`2`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter your name, which will be used to identify you.
### **Phone (text)** (`3`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
### **Email (text)** (`4`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., example@example.com).
### **Date (date)** (`5`, `date`, required: false): 
Select a date for the quote from the calendar icon.
### **Time (time)** (`6`, `time`, required: false): 
Choose a time for the quote from the clock icon.
### **Note (note)** (`7`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter any additional notes regarding the quote.
### **Select One (select_one)** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Select one option from the dropdown menu to provide more information.
### **Select Multiple (select_multiple)** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
Choose multiple options from the dropdown menu to provide more choices.
