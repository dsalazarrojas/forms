# property_tour_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about a property, including its location, status, and type. It's intended for real estate agents or brokers to gather information from clients or clients' representatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter the property's street in the "street" field.
6. Enter the city of the property in the "city" field.
7. Choose the property's status from the "property_status" field.
8. Select all applicable property types from the "property_type" field.
9. Enter the zip code of the property in the "zip" field.
10. Enter the name of the agent or representative who will tour the property with you in the "agent_name" field.
11. Enter the agent's phone number in the "agent_phone" field.
12. Enter any property notes in the "property_notes" field.
13. Enter any agent notes in the "agent_notes" field.
14. Choose a date for the property tour from the "tour_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### first_name (Text)
Enter your first name in this field.

### last_name (Text)
Enter your last name in this field.

### email (Text)
Enter your email address in this field, which will be used for correspondence.

### phone (Text)
Enter your phone number in this field for the agent to contact you.

### street (Text)
Enter the street address of the property in this field.

### city (Text)
Enter the city where the property is located in this field.

### state (Select One)
Choose "Yes" or "No" to specify whether the property is currently available for viewing.

### zip (Text)
Enter the zip code of the property in this field.

### property_type (Select Multiple)
Select all the types that apply to the property (e.g. residential, commercial, industrial).

### property_status (Select One)
Choose "Active" or "Inactive" to specify the property's status.

### agent_name (Text)
Enter the name of the person who will be touring the property with you.

### agent_phone (Text)
Enter the phone number of the agent or representative in this field.

### property_notes (Note)
Enter any additional information or notes about the property in this field.

### agent_notes (Note)
Enter any additional information or notes for the agent or representative in this field.

### tour_date (Date)
Choose a date for the property tour in this field.
