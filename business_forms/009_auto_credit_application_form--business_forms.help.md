# Auto Credit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and financial information from customers who are applying for auto credit. The form is used to evaluate the customer's creditworthiness and determine whether they are eligible for credit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, including your full name, SSN or Tax ID, date of birth, and email address.
2. Provide your contact information, including your phone number.
3. Enter your housing information, including your current address, housing status, and years at current address.
4. Enter your employment information, including your employer name, job title, gross monthly income, and months at current job.
5. Enter your credit details, including the down payment amount, trade-in intentions, and authorization for credit check.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **SSN or Tax ID** (`ssn_tax_id`, `text`, required): Enter your Social Security Number or Tax ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`dob`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your personal or work email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your mobile or home phone number.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, `text`, required): Enter your current address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Housing Status** (`housing_status`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you own or rent your current residence.
* **Years at Current Address** (`years_at_address`, `number`, required): Enter how long you have lived at your current address.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your current employer.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Gross Monthly Income** (`gross_monthly_income`, `number`, required): Enter your income before taxes.
* **Months at Current Job** (`months_at_job`, `number`, required): Enter the time you have spent with your current employer.
* **Down Payment Amount** (`down_payment_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you are willing to pay as a down payment.
* **Trade-in Intended** (`trade_in_intended`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have a vehicle to trade in.
* **Authorize Credit Check** (`authorize_credit_check`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you authorize the dealership to check your credit history.
