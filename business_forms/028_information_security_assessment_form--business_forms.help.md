# Information Security Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your organization's security measures and potential vulnerabilities. This information will be used to assess your security posture and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information, including your name and contact details.
2. Describe your organization's details, such as its name and address.
3. List the security measures you have in place, such as firewalls, antivirus software, and access control systems.
4. Identify any potential vulnerabilities, such as phishing, brute force attacks, and zero-day exploits.
5. Rate the level of risk for each security measure, using the options provided.
6. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
7. Submit your completed form to review and update your security posture.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (1)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number to provide contact information.
* **Organization Details (2)**: Enter your organization's name and address to provide context for the assessment.
* **Security Measures (3)**: Select all the security measures you have implemented, such as firewalls, antivirus software, and access control systems.
* **Vulnerabilities (4)**: Select any potential vulnerabilities you are aware of, such as phishing attacks, brute force attacks, and zero-day exploits.
* **Security Assessment (5)**: Rate the level of risk for each security measure, using the options provided.
* **Comments (6)**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Email (7)**: Enter your email address to receive updates and communications about your security posture.
* **Phone Number (8)**: Enter your phone number to receive phone calls or notifications about your security posture.
* **Submit (9)**: Click the submit button to complete and submit your assessment.
