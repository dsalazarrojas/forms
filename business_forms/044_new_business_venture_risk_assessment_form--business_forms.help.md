# new_business_venture_risk_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the risks associated with a new business venture. It helps to identify, evaluate, and mitigate potential risks that may impact the success of the venture.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
* Fill out each field as it appears on the page, moving from one page to the next as directed.
* Use the drop-down menus to select options for fields that require choices, such as "go/no-go" and "review status".
* For text fields, provide clear and concise answers that address the question being asked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. New Business Venture Risk**: This is the main topic or question being assessed. Provide a brief description of the business venture and the risk you are evaluating.
* **2. Business Initiative**: Describe the business initiative or project being evaluated. This could include a brief summary of the venture's goals and objectives.
* **3. Risk Insights**: Share any insights or comments about the risks you have identified with the business venture.
* **4. Assessment Results**: Provide a summary of your assessment of the risks associated with the business venture.
* **5. Go/No-Go Decision**: Select "go" if you believe the venture should proceed, and "False" if you do not.
* **6. Supporting Documents**: Attach any supporting documents or evidence that support your assessment and decisions.
* **7. Next Steps**: Describe the next steps you plan to take to mitigate or address the risks identified.
* **8. Evaluation Method**: Select how you will evaluate the risks, using "qualitative", "quantitative", or both methods.
* **9. Evaluation Scale**: Enter a number value to represent the level of risk you are evaluating the business venture for.
* **10. Risk Factors**: Identify any risk factors that contribute to the risk assessment.
* **11. Mitigation Strategies**: Describe any strategies you will use to mitigate or address the risks.
* **12. Review Status**: Select the status of the review, "pending", "completed", or "reviewed".
* **13. Review Date**: Enter the date of the review, in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **14. Risk Level**: Select the level of risk, "low", "moderate", or "high".
* **15. Risk Score**: Enter a number value representing the level of risk.
* **16. Risk Description**: Provide a brief description of the risk.
* **17. Mitigation Effectiveness**: Assess the effectiveness of the mitigation strategies.
* **18. Mitigation Outcomes**: Describe the outcomes of the mitigation strategies.
* **19. Evaluation Comments**: Enter any additional comments or thoughts about the evaluation.
* **20. Business Form**: This field is not currently used. Please leave blank.
