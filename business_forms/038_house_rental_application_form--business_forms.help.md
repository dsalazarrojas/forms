# House Rental Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive application for renters who are interested in renting a property. It collects necessary information from the applicant, ensuring a smooth and efficient rental process for both the renter and the landlord/property owner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" page.
2. Share your profile in the "Renter Profile" page.
3. Specify your property preferences in the "Renter Property Preferences" page.
4. Fill out the "Property Details" page with relevant information.
5. Complete the "Renter Application" page with your application details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): This page is for you to provide your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
- **Renter Profile** (`renter_profile`, text, optional): Share a brief introduction about yourself, including your background, interests, or any relevant experience that might be helpful for the landlord/property owner to know.
- **Renter Property Preferences** (`renter_property_preferences`, text, optional): Specify your preferences for the type of property you are looking for, such as location, number of bedrooms, or amenities.
- **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, optional): Provide details about the property you are interested in renting, including the address, size, and any other relevant features.
- **Renter Application** (`renter_application`, text, optional): In this section, please provide your application and reasons why you are a good fit for the property.
