# deal_experience_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The deal_experience_booking_form is a comprehensive booking experience form used for gathering detailed information about a specific experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name and last name.
2. Enter a valid email address to contact you.
3. Add your phone number so we can reach you.
4. Select the correct date for your experience.
5. Choose the time for your experience.
6. If necessary, provide a brief note for any important details.
7. Select your experience type.
8. Indicate whether you have the experience or not.
9. Choose the current status of the deal.
10. Choose the methods of payment you agree to.
11. Select the active status of payment terms.
12. Choose the method of payment you agree to.
13. Indicate whether you agree to the terms and conditions.
14. Indicate whether you agree to the terms and conditions.
15. Indicate whether you agree to payment terms.
16. Select the type of currency involved.
17. Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
18. Confirm your agreement to payment terms.
19. Indicate the status of agreement.
20. Choose whether you confirm the payment terms.
21. Describe the experience in detail.
22. Enter the experience ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address for contact.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (Date, Required): Choose the date for your experience.
* **Time** (Time, Required): Choose the time for your experience.
* **Note** (Text, Optional): Provide any important notes.
* **Experience Type** (Select One, Required): Select the type of experience you are booking.
* **Experience** (Select Multiple, Required): Indicate whether you have the experience or not.
* **Deal Status** (Select One, Required): Choose the current deal status.
* **Payment Method** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the methods of payment you agree to.
* **Payment Status** (Select One, Required): Choose the active status of payment terms.
* **Total Price** (Number, Required): Enter the total price.
* **Price** (Number, Required): Enter the price.
* **Tax** (Number, Required): Enter the tax.
* **Discount** (Number, Required): Enter the discount.
* **Discount Status** (Select One, Required): Choose the discount status.
* **Payment Terms** (Select One, Required): Choose whether you agree to payment terms.
* **Currency** (Select One, Required): Choose the currency involved.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Text, Required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions Agreement** (Select One, Required): Indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Payment Terms Agreement** (Select One, Required): Indicate your agreement to payment terms.
* **Agreement** (Select One, Required): Choose the status of agreement.
* **Confirm Payment Terms** (Select One, Required): Confirm your agreement to payment terms.
* **Experience Description** (Text, Required): Describe the experience in detail.
* **Experience ID** (Text, Required): Enter the experience ID.
