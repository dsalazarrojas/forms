# IT Service Request Form 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Service Request Form 2 is used to submit requests for IT services. Please use this form to describe your problem or issue, and we will do our best to assist you.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  **Page 1: Request form**: Enter a clear and concise description of your problem or issue in the **Request form** field.
2.  **Page 2: Contact Information**: Provide your contact details, such as name, email, or phone number, so we can get back to you.
3.  **Page 3: Category of problem**: Select the category that best describes your problem or issue from the dropdown menu. If you're unsure, you can choose "Yes" or "No" and our team will help you determine the category.
4.  **Page 4: Further explanation**: If you need to provide additional information about your issue, use this field to add more details.
5.  **Page 5: Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share with our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Request form (Page 1)**: A free-text field where you can enter your problem or issue. Please be as clear and concise as possible.
*   **Contact Information (Page 2)**: A field to capture your contact details, such as name, email, or phone number. This will help us get back to you efficiently.
*   **Category of problem (Page 3)**: A dropdown select field with two options: 'Yes' and 'No'. This helps us understand the type of problem or issue you're experiencing.
*   **Further explanation (Page 4)**: A free-text field for providing additional details about your issue.
*   **Comments (Page 5)**: A field for adding any comments or feedback you'd like to share with our team.

## Tips
*   Be as clear and concise as possible when filling out the form.
*   If you're unsure about any field, you can contact our team for guidance.
*   Our team will review and address your issue as soon as possible.
