# Exit Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Exit Interview Questionnaire Form is a tool used to collect valuable insights from departing employees to help the company understand reasons for turnover and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly in each field.
2. Select the correct option from the dropdown menu for the "What department are you from?" field.
3. Enter your manager's name, if applicable.
4. Provide a brief description for each of the open-ended questions.
5. Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further discussions.
6. Enter any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **What department are you from?**: Please select the department you are a part of, as this will help us understand your perspective and experiences within the company.
* **Manager's Name**: If you have a manager, please enter their name so we can reach out to them for further discussion.
* **Reasons for Leaving**: Please provide a brief description of why you are leaving the company. This will help us understand your reasons and identify potential areas for improvement.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: If you have any suggestions on how the company can improve, please provide them here.
* **Contact Information**: If you would like to be contacted for further discussions or follow-ups, please enter your contact information (email address).
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience with the company can be entered here.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
- This form should not be completed in duplicate, and each employee should only submit one response.
