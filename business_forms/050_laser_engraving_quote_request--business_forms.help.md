# Laser Engraving Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect all the necessary information from clients for a laser engraving quote request. It includes details about the project, artwork files, quantities, deadline, engraving specifics, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with as much detail as possible.
2. For the "Artwork Files" field, select the files that you would like us to work with.
3. In the "Quantities" field, enter the exact number of items you would like us to engrave.
4. For the "Deadline" field, choose the date you need the project to be completed by.
5. In the "Engraving Details" field, provide any special instructions or requests you have for the engraving process.
6. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Lastly, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

(project_details, text, required: false)
This field is where you can enter any relevant project details that will help us better understand your requirements.

### **Artwork Files**
(Artwork Files, select_multiple, required: false)

### **Quantities**

### **Deadline**

### **Notes**

### **Email**

### **Phone**
