# monthly_audit_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a monthly audit assessment, used to gather data from employees for their performance assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you are filling it out as an employee, as it's not intended for any other purpose.
2. Enter your information accurately and completely.
3. Make sure to use the correct format for your phone number and email addresses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Phone Number** (text, required: false): Enter a valid phone number in the correct format.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address in the correct format.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Use a valid phone number format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* Use a valid email format (e.g., name@example.com).
