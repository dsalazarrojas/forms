# Inventory Turnover Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Turnover Report Form is designed to help businesses track and analyze their inventory levels, costs, and turnover rates over time. This form will aid in creating reports for inventory analysis and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the Period**: Choose the period for which you want to report on, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
2. **Enter Current and Previous Inventory Levels**: Enter the current and previous levels of stock for your business.
3. **Enter Current and Previous Inventory Costs**: Enter the current and previous costs of your inventory.
4. **Calculate Inventory Turnover Rate**: This field will be calculated automatically once you have entered the required fields.
5. **Submit the Report**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the report and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: Enter a title for your report, which will be displayed in the report.
* **Inventory Period**: Select the period for which you want to report on (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Current Inventory Level**: Enter the current level of stock for your business.
* **Previous Inventory Level**: Enter the previous level of stock for your business.
* **Inventory Turnover Rate**: This will be calculated automatically once you have entered the required fields.
* **Current Inventory Cost**: Enter the current cost of your inventory.
* **Previous Inventory Cost**: Enter the previous cost of your inventory.
* **Inventory Turnover Report**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the report.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the report.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address (optional).
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **Date**: Select the date you want the report to be submitted.
* **Time**: Select the time you want the report to be submitted.
* **Current Date**: Select the date for the current period (optional).
* **Current Time**: Select the time for the current period (optional).
* **Previous Date**: Select the date for the previous period (optional).
* **Previous Time**: Select the time for the previous period (optional).
* **Current Period**: Select the current period (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Previous Period**: Select the previous period (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Submitted**: Enter any final comments or notes before submitting the report.

Please note that some fields may not be required depending on your business needs and the specific configuration of the form.
