# Customer Visit Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record and document customer visits to the organization, ensuring that accurate and consistent information is captured for future reference and improvement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the visit date in the "Visit Date" field.
2. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Enter the visit location in the "Visit Location" field.
4. Enter the staff member's name in the "Staff Name" field.
5. Add any comments or notes about the visit in the "Comments" field.
6. Enter the customer's email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Choose the visit frequency from the available options in the "Visit Frequency" field.
9. Rate the overall visit experience from the available options in the "Customer Feedback" field.
10. Choose the staff member's attitude during the visit from the available options in the "Staff Attitude" field.
11. Rate the overall experience from the available options in the "Overall Experience" field.
12. Suggest areas for improvement in the "Improvement Areas" field.
13. Enter the next visit date, if applicable, in the "Next Visit Date" field.
14. Add any follow-up actions or notes in the "Follow Up Actions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the customer visit.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Visit Location** (`visit_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the customer visit.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the staff member involved in the visit.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any comments or notes about the visit.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the overall visit experience from the available options.
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the visit frequency from the available options.
* **Staff Attitude** (`staff_attendance`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the staff member's attitude during the visit from the available options.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the overall visit experience from the available options.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, required: false): Suggest areas for improvement.
* **Next Visit Date** (`next_visit_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the next visit date, if applicable.
* **Follow Up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, `text`, required: false): Add any follow-up actions or notes.

Note: All fields are optional, unless marked as required. Make sure to fill out the required fields accurately to ensure accurate and consistent information capture.
