# Bridal Gown Storage Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to provide information about their bridal gown storage needs, including details about their gown, storage requirements, and payment options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure the correct information is entered.
3. If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification or assistance if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your wedding.
* **Gown Designer or Brand** (`gown_designer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the designer or brand of your gown, if applicable.
* **Gown Style Description** (`gown_style`, text, required): Describe your gown style in a few words (e.g., strapless, mermaid, beaded).
* **Gown Color** (`gown_color`, select_one, required): Select the color of your gown from the provided options.
* **Gown Size** (`gown_size`, text, optional): Enter the size of your gown (e.g., 5' 6", 10, etc.).
* **Gown Condition Upon Drop-Off** (`gown_condition_before`, select_one, required): Rate the current condition of your gown from the provided options.
* **Special Gown Details** (`gown_special_details`, text, optional): Describe any special details about your gown, such as beading or embellishments.
* **Storage Duration Needed** (`storage_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration you need to store your gown.
* **Drop-Off Date** (`drop_off_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to bring your gown to be stored.
* **Preferred Drop-Off Time** (`drop_off_time`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred drop-off time from the provided options.
* **Required Storage Conditions** (`storage_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Select any special storage requirements for your gown, such as climate-controlled or low humidity.
* **Professional Cleaning Needed** (`cleaning_needed`, select_one, optional): Check if you require professional cleaning before storage.
* **Gown Preservation Service** (`preservation_service`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional preservation services you may need for your gown.
* **Accessories to Store** (`accessories_stored`, select_multiple, optional): Select any accessories you need to store with your gown.
* **Planned Pickup Method** (`pickup_plan`, select_one, optional): Select how you plan to pick up your gown after storage.
* **Storage Pricing Plan** (`storage_cost`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred storage pricing plan.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select how you plan to pay for storage.
* **Special Requests or Instructions** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Add any special requests or instructions for your storage.
* **Insurance Coverage Desired** (`insurance_coverage`, select_one, optional): Check if you desire insurance coverage for your gown investment.
