# External Factor Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture and analyze external factors affecting business operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields (none are currently required, but this may change as the form evolves).
2. Select the correct category from the provided options.
3. Choose an assigned tool, if applicable.
4. Enter a description (optional).
5. Select or enter relevant external factors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Political Factor** (`political_factor`, text, optional): Enter any significant political events or developments that may impact your business. 
* **Economic Factor** (`economic_factor`, text, optional): Record any relevant economic factors that may affect your business operations.
* **Social Factor** (`social_factor`, text, optional): Capture any significant social events or trends that may impact your business.
* **Technological Factor** (`technological_factor`, text, optional): Note any technological advancements or changes that may influence your business.
* **Legal Factor** (`legal_factor`, text, optional): Enter any relevant legal issues or changes that may affect your business.
* **Environmental Factor** (`environmental_factor`, text, optional): Record any environmental concerns or changes that may impact your business operations.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category under which the external factor falls (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): If applicable, choose a specific tool or resource assigned to address the external factor.
* **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Enter a brief description of the external factor, if necessary.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): This field is reserved for future development.

## Tips
* This form is designed to be adaptable and flexible. You can add or remove fields as needed.
* Please ensure that you are accurately recording and reporting external factors to aid business operations. 
* Use the category and assigned tool selections to help categorize and prioritize external factors.
* The description field can help you keep track of specific details or context for each factor.
