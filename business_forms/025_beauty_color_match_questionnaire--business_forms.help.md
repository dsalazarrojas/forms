# Beauty Color Match Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beauty Color Match Questionnaire is designed to gather information about a client's beauty preferences, color goals, and lifestyle factors that influence their color palette. This information will help our team to create a personalized color match plan for the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's information (full name, email address, phone number, and appointment date).
2. Choose the type of color service the client is interested in (Hair Color, Makeup Color, Nail Color, or Multiple Services).
3. Describe the client's color goal and what they are trying to achieve.
4. Select the color inspiration or reference images that the client likes.
5. Identify the client's personal style (Classic, Trendy, Bold, Natural, etc.).
6. Choose the skin tone range (Very Fair, Fair, Light, Medium, Tan, Very Deep).
7. Select the undertone (Warm, Cool, Neutral, Balanced).
8. Choose the skin type (Dry, Normal, Oily, Combination, Sensitive).
9. Select the natural hair color.
10. Select the natural eye color.
11. Choose the color families the client is attracted to (Warm tones, Cool tones, Earth tones, Jewel tones, etc.).
12. List any colors the client wants to avoid.
13. Select the seasonal color preference (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter, Not Applicable).
14. Choose the contrast preference (Low Contrast - Soft, Medium Contrast, High Contrast - Bold).
15. Select the desired maintenance level (Low - Minimal Upkeep, Medium - Regular Care, High - Frequent Maintenance).
16. Describe the client's hair health goals.
17. Add any additional notes or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter the client's full name.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Appointment Date** (Date, Required): Enter the client's desired appointment date.
* **Color Service Type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of color service the client is interested in (Hair Color, Makeup Color, Nail Color, or Multiple Services).
* **Describe Your Color Goal** (Text, Required): Describe what the client is trying to achieve with their color.
* **Color Inspiration or Reference Images** (Text, Not Required): List any color inspiration or reference images the client likes.
* **Personal Style** (Text, Not Required): Identify the client's personal style (Classic, Trendy, Bold, Natural, etc.).
* **Skin Tone Range** (Select One, Required): Choose the client's skin tone range (Very Fair, Fair, Light, Medium, Tan, Very Deep).
* **Undertone** (Select One, Required): Select the client's undertone (Warm, Cool, Neutral, Balanced).
* **Skin Type** (Select One, Not Required): Choose the client's skin type (Dry, Normal, Oily, Combination, Sensitive).
* **Natural Hair Color** (Select One, Required): Select the client's natural hair color.
* **Natural Eye Color** (Select One, Required): Select the client's natural eye color.
* **Color Families You Are Attracted To** (Text, Required): Choose the color families the client is attracted to (Warm tones, Cool tones, Earth tones, Jewel tones, etc.).
* **Colors You Want to Avoid** (Text, Not Required): List any colors the client wants to avoid.
* **Seasonal Color Preference** (Select One, Not Required): Select the client's seasonal color preference (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter, Not Applicable).
* **Contrast Preference** (Select One, Required): Choose the client's contrast preference (Low Contrast - Soft, Medium Contrast, High Contrast - Bold).
* **Desired Maintenance Level** (Select One, Required): Select the client's desired maintenance level (Low - Minimal Upkeep, Medium - Regular Care, High - Frequent Maintenance).
* **Hair Health Goals** (Text, Not Required): Describe the client's hair health goals.
* **Additional Notes or Preferences** (Text, Not Required): Add any additional notes or preferences.

Please note that this is just a sample guide, and actual content and explanations might need to be adjusted according to the business' specific branding and policies.
