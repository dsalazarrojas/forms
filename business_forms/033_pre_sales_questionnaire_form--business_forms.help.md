# Pre Sales Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Sales Questionnaire Form is designed to gather information from businesses about their company details, contact information, preferences, and product-related information. This form is used by sales teams or business development teams to understand potential clients or customers better before engaging with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information for each field, marked as "Required".
2. Fill in the optional fields as needed.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Company Details
* **Business Name** (text): Enter the name of your company.
* **Business Type** (select_one): Select the type of your business (Business, Personal, or Other).
* **Industry Sector** (select_multiple): Select the industry sector(s) your business operates in.
* **Business Description** (note): Provide a brief description of your business.
* **Company Website** (text): Enter the URL of your company website.
* **Company Address** (text): Enter the address of your company.
* **Contact Person** (text): Enter the contact person's name.

### 2. Contact Information
* **Contact Number** (number): Enter the contact number of your company.
* **Email Address** (email): Enter the email address of your company.
* **Company Email** (email): Enter the company email address (if different from the above email).

### 3. Preferences
* **Preference Language** (select_multiple): Select the languages you prefer to communicate in.
* **Preference Location** (select_multiple): Select the locations you prefer to operate in.

### 4. Product Information
* **Product Interest** (select_multiple): Select the product(s) you are interested in.
* **Product Pricing** (number): Enter the price of your product.
* **Product Availability** (date): Enter the date your product is available.
* **Product Description** (text): Enter a brief description of your product.
* **Product Image** (text): Enter a URL or description of the product image.

### 5. Payment and Delivery Terms
* **Payment Terms** (select_multiple): Select the payment terms you prefer (Pay Now, Pay Later).
* **Delivery Terms** (select_multiple): Select the delivery terms you prefer (Delivery Now, Delivery Later).

### 6. Other Notes
* **Other Notes** (text): Enter any additional notes or information about your business or product.
