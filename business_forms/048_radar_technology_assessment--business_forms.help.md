# radar_technology_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The radar technology assessment form is used to collect information about the performance and characteristics of a radar system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the correct system type from the options.
2. Provide a brief description of the system.
3. Rate the system's performance using a number.
4. List the strengths of the system.
5. List the limitations of the system.
6. Rate the system overall.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **System Type** (`system_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of radar system being assessed, such as Radar System, Other System, or No System.
* **System Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the radar system.
* **Performance** (`performance_rating`, number, required: false): Enter a number rating from 1 to 10 for how well the system performs.
* **Strengths** (`strength`, text, required: false): List the strengths of the radar system, including its benefits and advantages.
* **Limitations** (`limitation`, text, required: false): List the limitations of the radar system, including any drawbacks or challenges it presents.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required: false): Enter a number rating from 1 to 10 for the overall system.
