# Contingency Plan Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Contingency Plan Report Form is designed to help organizations identify and plan for potential risks and threats to their operations, assets, and reputation. This form is a tool for businesses to anticipate and mitigate potential disruptions by providing a structured framework for risk identification, impact assessment, and response planning.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the department/business unit affected by the potential risk or threat.
2. Identify the primary risk category (e.g., Natural Disaster, Cyber Attack, etc.).
3. Determine the probability of occurrence (very low to very high).
4. Estimate the maximum acceptable downtime for the business.
5. Describe the potential impact on operations.
6. Identify the primary mitigation strategy to reduce the risk.
7. Determine if an alternative work site is identified (physical location or remote work).
8. List critical resources required for recovery (e.g., generators, backup servers, specialized staff).
9. Record the date of the last simulation/test.
10. Enter the name of the report prepared by.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Department/Business Unit
* This field is required and should be completed with the name of the department or business unit affected by the potential risk or threat.

### 2. Report Creation Date
* This field is required and should be completed with the date the report is created (YYYY-MM-DD format).

### 3. Risk Category
* Select the primary risk category (e.g., Natural Disaster, Cyber Attack, etc.) from the provided options.
* This field is required and will help identify the type of risk or threat.

### 4. Probability of Occurrence
* Select the probability of occurrence (very low to very high) from the provided options.
* This field is required and will help determine the likelihood of the risk or threat materializing.

### 5. Estimated Downtime
* This field is required and should be completed with an estimated maximum acceptable downtime for the business.
* The goal is to understand how long the business can survive without this function.

### 6. Describe the Impact on Operations
* This field is required and should provide a detailed description of the potential impact on operations.
* This will help in identifying the critical functions that may be affected.

### 7. Primary Mitigation Strategy
* This field is required and should be completed with a detailed description of the primary strategy to reduce the risk.
* This will help in developing a plan to mitigate the potential impact.

### 8. Is Alternative Work Site Identified
* Select whether an alternative work site is identified (physical location or remote work) from the provided options.
* This field is required and will help in determining the plan for alternative work arrangements.

### 9. Critical Resources Required for Recovery
* This field is optional and should list critical resources required for recovery (e.g., generators, backup servers, specialized staff).
* This will help in developing a recovery plan.

### 10. Date of Last Simulation/Test
* This field is optional and should be completed with the date of the last simulation/test.
* This will help in tracking progress and updates to the plan.

### 11. Report Prepared By
* This field is required and should be completed with the name of the person preparing the report.
* This will help in tracking who is responsible for the report.
