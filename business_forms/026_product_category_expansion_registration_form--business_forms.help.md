# Product Category Expansion Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register new product categories in the business system. It is intended for business owners or category managers to submit requests to add or modify product categories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the new product category in the `product_name` field.
2. Provide a brief description of the product category in the `product_description` field.
3. Specify the status of the product category as either Active or Inactive.
4. Indicate whether the category has team members associated with it.
5. Select the category manager from the drop-down list.
6. Enter the email address of the category manager.
7. Enter the phone number of the category manager (optional).
8. Select the team lead from the drop-down list.
9. Enter the email address of the team lead.
10. Enter the phone number of the team lead.
11. Enter the title of the category manager.
12. Enter the location of the category manager.
13. Specify the business unit associated with the category.
14. Specify the type of product.
15. Specify the status of the product as either Active or Inactive.
16. Select the team members who are associated with the category.
17. Select the team leads who are associated with the category.
18. Select whether the category manager has a list or not.
19. Submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.
20. Add any additional notes or comments regarding the category registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the new product category.
* **Product Description** (text, required): Provide a brief description of the product category.
* **Category Description** (text, required): Enter a description of the category.
* **Submit** (text, required): Click to submit the form.
* **Team Members** (select multiple, required): Select whether the category has team members.
* **Category Manager** (text, required): Select the category manager.
* **Manager Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the category manager.
* **Manager Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the category manager.
* **Team Lead** (text, required): Select the team lead.
* **Team Lead Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the team lead.
* **Team Lead Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the team lead.
* **Manager Title** (text, required): Enter the title of the category manager.
* **Manager Location** (text, required): Enter the location of the category manager.
* **Business Unit** (text, required): Specify the business unit associated with the category.
* **Product Type** (text, required): Specify the type of product.
* **Product Status** (select one, required): Specify the status of the product as either Active or Inactive.
* **Category Status** (select one, required): Specify the status of the category as either Active or Inactive.
* **Team Members List** (select multiple, required): Select whether the category has a team members list.
* **Team Lead List** (select multiple, required): Select whether the category has a team leads list.
* **Manager List** (select multiple, required): Select whether the category manager has a list.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments regarding the category registration.
* **Review Comments** (date, required): Enter the date of review and comments.

Note: Fields with a red "Required" tag are mandatory fields that must be completed before submitting the form.
