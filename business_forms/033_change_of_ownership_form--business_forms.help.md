# Change Of Ownership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Change of Ownership Form is designed to capture details of ownership changes of various assets. This form is typically used when an individual or entity acquires or disposes of an asset.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the `Date of Ownership Change` field with the date on which the ownership change took effect.
2. Provide a brief description of the `Asset` being transferred in the `Description of Asset` field.
3. Select the category of the `Type of Asset` in the drop-down menu.
4. Enter the `Asset ID or Serial Number` if applicable.
5. Enter the name of the `Previous Owner` in the corresponding field.
6. Enter the address of the `Previous Owner` in the corresponding field, if applicable.
7. Enter the contact information of the `Previous Owner` in the corresponding field, if applicable.
8. Enter the name of the `New Owner` in the `New Owner Name` field.
9. Enter the address of the `New Owner` in the `New Owner Address` field, if applicable.
10. Enter the contact information of the `New Owner` in the `New Owner Contact Information` field, if applicable.
11. Select the type of `Ownership Being Transferred` from the drop-down menu.
12. Select the `Method of Transfer` from the drop-down menu.
13. If applicable, enter the `Consideration or Purchase Price` paid for the transfer.
14. If applicable, provide details on the `Payment Terms` in the corresponding field.
15. If applicable, enter any `Liabilities or Encumbrances` associated with the asset.
16. If applicable, enter any `Warranties or Representations` made by the seller.
17. Enter the `Effective Date of Transfer` when the ownership change took effect.
18. If required, enter the names of the `Witnesses` who attest to the transfer.
19. If applicable, select if the form has been `Notarized`.
20. Finally, confirm that both parties have `Acknowledged and Accepted` the terms of the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Ownership Change (change_date)**: Enter the date on which the ownership change took effect.
* **Description of Asset (asset_description)**: Briefly describe the asset being transferred.
* **Type of Asset (asset_type)**: Select the category of the asset being transferred.
* **Asset ID or Serial Number (asset_id_number)**: Enter the unique identifier for the asset, if applicable.
* **Previous Owner Name (previous_owner_name)**: Enter the name of the previous owner.
* **Previous Owner Address (previous_owner_address)**: Enter the address of the previous owner, if applicable.
* **Previous Owner Contact Information (previous_owner_contact)**: Enter the contact information of the previous owner, if applicable.
* **New Owner Name (new_owner_name)**: Enter the name of the new owner.
* **New Owner Address (new_owner_address)**: Enter the address of the new owner, if applicable.
* **New Owner Contact Information (new_owner_contact)**: Enter the contact information of the new owner, if applicable.
* **Type of Ownership Being Transferred (ownership_type)**: Select the type of ownership being transferred.
* **Method of Transfer (transfer_method)**: Select the method of transfer.
* **Consideration or Purchase Price (consideration_amount)**: Enter the amount paid for the transfer, if applicable.
* **Payment Terms (payment_terms)**: Enter the payment terms, if applicable.
* **Liabilities or Encumbrances (liabilities_assumption)**: Enter any liabilities or encumbrances associated with the asset, if applicable.
* **Warranties or Representations (warranties_representation)**: Enter any warranties or representations made by the seller, if applicable.
* **Effective Date of Transfer (effective_date)**: Enter the date on which the ownership change took effect.
* **Witnesses (witnesses)**: Enter the names of the witnesses who attest to the transfer, if required.
* **Notarized (notarization)**: Select if the form has been notarized, if applicable.
* **Both Parties Acknowledge and Accept (acknowledgment)**: Confirm that both parties have acknowledged and accepted the terms of the transfer.
