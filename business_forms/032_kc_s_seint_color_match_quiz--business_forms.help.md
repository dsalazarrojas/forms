# KCs-Seint-Color-Match-Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information and opinions for marketing or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective text fields.
2. Select your preferred color from the options provided.
3. Choose your favorite season from the list of options.
4. Select your favorite time of day from the options.
5. Choose multiple colors that you prefer from the options.
6. Select your favorite season from the options.
7. Choose a time of day when you are most active.
8. Select your favorite number from the options.

* **First name** (`user_input`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`user_input_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **What color do you prefer?** (`quiz_question_1`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose a color from the options.

* **Date** (`date_input`, `date`, required/optional): Enter a date (format not specified).

* **Email address** (`email_input`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Which of the following is your favorite color?** (`quiz_question_3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose multiple colors from the options.
* **What is your favorite season?** (`quiz_question_4`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose a season from the options.
* **Date 2** (`date_input_2`, `date`, required/optional): Enter another date (format not specified).
* **Time 2** (`time_input_2`, `time`, required/optional): Choose another time of day (format not specified).
