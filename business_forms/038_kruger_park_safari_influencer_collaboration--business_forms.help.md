# Kruger Park Safari Influencer Collaboration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kruger Park Safari Influencer Collaboration form is a tool used to gather information from potential collaborators for a collaboration with Kruger Park Safari.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you are a suitable collaborator for Kruger Park Safari.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the type of collaboration you are interested in from the "Collaboration Type" dropdown menu.
5. Fill in any additional details about your collaboration in the "Safari Details" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Safari Details** (`safari_details`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about your collaboration with Kruger Park Safari. You can enter any relevant details about your proposal, such as your ideas for content creation or promotion.
* **Collaboration Type** (`collaboration_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of collaboration you are interested in:
	+ **Influencer**: You are a social media influencer looking to collaborate with Kruger Park Safari.
	+ **Brand**: You are a brand representative looking to collaborate with Kruger Park Safari.
	+ **Community**: You are a community manager or representative looking to collaborate with Kruger Park Safari.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your collaboration proposal.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your collaboration proposal.
