# Equipment Retrofitting Expert Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and equipment details for retrofitting experts to provide their expertise to customers or clients in the equipment retrofitting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details in the `Contact Details` section.
2. Provide information about the equipment you need help with in the `Equipment Information` section.
3. Specify the retrofit requirements for your equipment in the `Retrofit Requirements` section.
4. Let us know where the equipment is located in the `Equipment Location` section.
5. Briefly describe the equipment in the `Equipment Description` section.
6. Choose how you want to be contacted in the `Contact Method` section.
7. If you have a phone number, provide it in the `Phone Number` field.
8. If you have an email address, provide it in the `Email` field.
9. If you have a preferred contact method other than phone or email, describe it in the `Other Contact Method` section.
10. Select the type of equipment you need help with in the `Equipment Type` section.
11. Specify the status of the equipment in the `Equipment Status` section.
12. Provide the estimated retrofit cost and duration in the `Retrofit Cost` and `Retrofit Duration` sections.
13. Add any submission comment in the `Submission Comment` section.
14. Choose who submitted the form in the `Submitted By` section.
15. Select the date and time the form was submitted in the `Submission Date` and `Submission Time` sections.
16. Choose who the form was assigned to in the `Assigned To` section.
17. Select the date and time the form was assigned to in the `Assigned Date` and `Assigned Time` sections.
18. Choose the status of the form in the `Status` section.
19. Add any comments in the `Comments` section.
20. Provide a URL for the submission if applicable in the `Submission URL` section.
21. Add any notes for the submission in the `Submission Note` section.
22. Attach any relevant files or attachments for the submission in the `Submission Attachment` section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`):
	+ Enter your name and contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Equipment Information** (`equipment_info`):
	+ Provide details about the equipment you need help with, such as make, model, and serial number.
* **Retrofit Requirements** (`retrofit_requirements`):
	+ Specify the specific requirements for the retrofitting process.
* **Equipment Location** (`equipment_location`):
	+ Let us know where the equipment is located.
* **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`):
	+ Briefly describe the equipment you need help with.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`):
	+ Choose how you want to be contacted (phone, email, or other).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`):
	+ If you have a phone number, provide it here.
* **Email** (`email`):
	+ If you have an email address, provide it here.
* **Other Contact Method** (`other_contact_method`):
	+ If you have a preferred contact method other than phone or email, describe it here.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`):
	+ Select the type of equipment you need help with (machinery, tools, etc.).
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`):
	+ Specify the status of the equipment (active, inactive, retired, etc.).
* **Retrofit Cost** (`retrofit_cost`):
	+ Estimate the cost of the retrofitting process.
* **Retrofit Duration** (`retrofit_duration`):
	+ Estimate the duration of the retrofitting process.
* **Submission Comment** (`submission_comment`):
	+ Add any additional comments about the submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`):
	+ Choose who submitted the form (admin, user, etc.).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`):
	+ Select the date the form was submitted.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`):
	+ Select the time the form was submitted.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`):
	+ Choose who the form was assigned to.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`):
	+ Select the date the form was assigned to.
* **Assigned Time** (`assigned_time`):
	+ Select the time the form was assigned to.
* **Status** (`status`):
	+ Choose the status of the form (new, in progress, completed, pending).
* **Comments** (`comments`):
	+ Add any comments about the form.
* **Submission URL** (`submission_url`):
	+ If applicable, provide a URL for the submission.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`):
	+ Add any notes for the submission.
* **Submission Attachment** (`submission_attachment`):
	+ Attach any relevant files or attachments for the submission.
