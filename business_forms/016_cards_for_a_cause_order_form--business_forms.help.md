# Cards For A Cause Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Cards For A Cause Order Form" is a crucial form for customers and fundraisers to place orders for custom cards, which support a good cause. The form is designed to guide users through the order process, ensuring that all the necessary information is collected accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Customer & Fundraiser Information**: Enter your name, contact email address, and contact phone number. This information is vital for order confirmations and communication.
2. Select your preferred delivery method from the options provided. Choose how you would like to receive your cards – whether it's by pickup from the organization, or by shipping to your address.
3. Select a payment method that suits your needs. You can opt for online payment through credit/debit card, PayPal, or even cash (for pickup orders).
4. If you choose to have your order shipped to your address, fill in your shipping address details, including street, city, state, and zip code.
5. **Card Box Selection**: Choose from the different types of card boxes available, each designed for specific occasions.
6. **Card Box Quantity**: Enter the total number of boxes you want to order.
7. **Delivery & Payment**: Confirm your delivery method and payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Your name as the customer or fundraiser.
*   **Contact Email Address**: We will send your order confirmation here.
*   **Contact Phone Number**: Your phone number for further communication.
*   **Organization or Fundraiser Name**: The name of the organization or fundraiser you are supporting with this purchase.
*   **Card Box Selection**: Choose from the different types of card boxes available, each designed for specific occasions.
*   **Total Quantity of Boxes**: Enter the total number of boxes you want to order.
*   **Delivery Method**: Choose how you would like to receive your cards – whether by pickup from the organization or by shipping to your address.
*   **Shipping Address (if applicable)**: If you chose shipping, enter your address details, including street, city, state, and zip code.
*   **Payment Method**: Select a payment method that suits your needs, such as online payment through credit/debit card, PayPal, or cash (for pickup orders).
*   **Special Instructions or Order Notes**: Enter any special requests or instructions for your order.
*   **Order Confirmation**: Confirm that your order details are accurate and complete.

## Tips

*   Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting your form.
*   Make sure to select the correct delivery and payment methods to ensure smooth processing of your order.
