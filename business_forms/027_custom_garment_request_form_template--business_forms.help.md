# Custom Garment Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers or users submit requests for custom garments. It collects personal information and details about the garment request, including type, color, quantity, and any special instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
   * First name
   * Last name
   * Email
   * Phone number

2. Select the type of garment you are requesting:
   * Choose from the list of available options: Dress, Shirt, Pants

3. Select the color of the garment:
   * Choose from the list of available options: White, Black, Blue, Red

4. Specify the quantity of the garment you are requesting:
   * Enter the number of garments you need

5. Provide any special instructions:
   * Enter any specific details or requests for your garment

6. Select any additional options:
   * Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4

7. Select any further options:
   * Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4

8. Select the final option:
   * Option 1, Option 2, Option 3

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: This is the first name of the person making the request.
* **Last name**: This is the last name of the person making the request.
* **Email**: This is the email address of the person making the request.
* **Phone**: This is the phone number of the person making the request.
* **Garment type**: Choose the type of garment you are requesting: Dress, Shirt, or Pants.
* **Color**: Choose the color of the garment: White, Black, Blue, or Red.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of garments you need.
* **Special instructions**: Enter any specific details or requests for your garment.
* **Custom instructions**: Enter any additional information about your garment request.
* **Date**: Enter the date you need the garment by.
* **Time**: Enter the time you need the garment by.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your request.
* **Option 1, 2, 3, 4**: Select any additional options from the list of available options.
* **Option 1, 2, 3, 4**: Select any further options from the list of available options.
* **Option 1, 2, 3**: Select the final option from the list of available options.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your request is processed correctly.
* Double-check the selected options to ensure they are accurate and relevant to your request.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is provided.
