# Receipt Submission For Projects - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Receipt Submission For Projects" form is used for submitting receipts related to projects. This form helps collect essential information about the project and the submitter, which will help in efficient management and processing of project-related receipts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project name in the "Project Name" field.
2. Enter the date of receipt in the "Receipt Date" field in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
3. Enter the receipt amount in the "Receipt Amount" field.
4. Select the status of the receipt as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Receipt Status" field.
5. Enter your name in the "Submitter Name" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Submitter Email" field in the correct format (e.g., user@example.com).
7. Enter your phone number in the "Submitter Phone" field.
8. Enter your address in the "Submitter Address" field.
9. Enter your department in the "Submitter Department" field.
10. Enter your job title in the "Submitter Job Title" field.
11. Select the date and time of submission in the "Submitter Date" field in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
12. Enter any comments you may have in the "Submitter Comments" field.
13. Select the status of the upload receipt as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Upload Receipt Status" field.
14. Enter any error message if there is an issue with uploading the receipt in the "Upload Receipt Error" field.
15. Upload the receipt attachment if required.
16. Enter a description of the receipt attachment if required.
17. Enter your ID number in the "Submitter ID Number" field.
18. Sign your name in the "Submitter Signature" field.
19. Enter your IP address and the browser used for submission in the "Submitter IP Address" and "Submitter Browser" fields, respectively.
