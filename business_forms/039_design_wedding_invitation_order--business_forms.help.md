# Design Wedding Invitation Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Design Wedding Invitation Order form is used to collect information from customers for their wedding invitation orders. This form helps sales or customer support teams gather the necessary details to fulfill the order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose whether or not to include the wedding invitation order in the customer's order.
2. Enter the customer's name.
3. Enter the customer's email address.
4. Select the contact method for the customer (phone, email, or mail).
5. Enter the customer's contact number (if phone is selected as the contact method).
6. Enter the ship date for the order.
7. Enter any additional design details.
8. Select the type of paper to be used for the invitation.
9. Enter the quantity of invitations needed.
10. Confirm the working status of the order (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Wedding Invitation Order Form (Select Multiple):** 
  This field allows you to choose whether or not to include the wedding invitation order in the customer's order. Select 'Yes' to include it, 'No' otherwise.
- **Customer Name (Text):** 
  Enter the customer's name as it appears on their address label or as you prefer it to be displayed on the invitation.
- **Customer Email (Email):** 
  Enter the customer's email address where you can contact them for any updates or issues with their order.
- **Contact Method (Select One):** 
  Choose how you would like to contact the customer: by phone, email, or mail.
- **Contact Number (Text):** 
  Enter the customer's contact number if you chose to contact them by phone.
- **Ship Date (Date):** 
  Enter the date you plan to ship the order.
- **Design Details (Text):** 
  Enter any additional information about the design or specifications for the invitation.
- **Paper Type (Select One):** 
  Choose the type of paper to be used for the invitation: Cardstock, Paper, or Foil.
- **Quantity (Number):** 
  Enter the number of invitations needed.
- **Working (Text):** 
  Confirm the working status of the order (optional).
