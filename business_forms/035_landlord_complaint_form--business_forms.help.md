# Landlord Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from tenants about their concerns or complaints regarding their landlord or property management. It provides a standardized way for tenants to report their issues and ensures that their concerns are documented and tracked for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
2. Enter your name as the tenant who is reporting the issue.
3. Provide the address of the property where the issue is occurring.
4. Describe the issue in detail, including any relevant dates and times.
5. If you have already notified the landlord or property manager about the issue, provide the dates when you first and last notified them.
6. Enter the landlord's name and contact information (if available).
7. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your name as the tenant who is reporting the issue.
* **Address** (`address`, text, **Required**): Enter the address of the property where the issue is occurring.
* **Description of Issue** (`issue_description`, text, **Required**): Describe the issue in detail, including any relevant dates, times, and circumstances.
* **Date First Notified** (`date_first_notified`, date, **Required**): If you have already notified the landlord or property manager about the issue, enter the date when you first notified them.
* **Date Last Notified** (`date_last_notified`, date, **Required**): If you have already notified the landlord or property manager about the issue, enter the date when you last notified them.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the landlord or property manager who is responsible for the issue.
* **Landlord Email** (`landlord_email`, email, **Optional**): If you have the landlord's email address, enter it here.
* **Landlord Phone** (`landlord_phone`, text, **Optional**): If you have the landlord's phone number, enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can contact the relevant authorities or our support team for guidance.
* Please note that filling out this form does not guarantee that your issue will be resolved, but it helps us to document and track your concerns for future reference.
