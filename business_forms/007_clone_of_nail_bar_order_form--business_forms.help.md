# Clone Of Nail Bar Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clone Of Nail Bar Order Form is used to capture customer orders for nail services, including contact information, product selection, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your full name in the "Customer name" field (Field 2: **Customer name**).
2. Provide your email address in the "Contact email" field (Field 3: **Contact email**).
3. Select the desired nail product from the list provided (Field 4: **Product selected**).
4. Specify the quantity of the selected product (Field 5: **Quantity**).
5. Choose between "Pickup" or "Delivery" for order fulfillment (Field 6: **Pickup or delivery**).
6. If selecting "Delivery", provide the delivery address (Field 7: **Delivery address**).
7. Select the preferred date for pickup or delivery (Field 8: **Preferred pickup or delivery date**).
8. Choose the payment method (Field 9: **Payment method**).
9. Add any custom notes or requests for the order (Field 10: **Order notes**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name** (`Customer name`, text, required): Enter your full name to help us identify you as a customer.
* **Contact email** (`Contact email`, email, required): This is the email address we will use to contact you regarding your order.
* **Product selected** (`Product selected`, text, required): Choose the nail product you would like to order.
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, number, required): Specify how many units of the selected product you would like to order.
* **Pickup or delivery** (`Pickup or delivery`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would like to pick up your order or have it delivered.
	+ **Pickup**: Select this option to pick up your order at our salon.
	+ **Delivery**: Select this option to have your order delivered to your specified address.
* **Delivery address** (`Delivery address`, text, false): If you selected "Delivery", provide your address for delivery.
* **Preferred pickup or delivery date** (`Preferred pickup or delivery date`, date, false): Choose a date for pickup or delivery.
* **Payment method** (`Payment method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to pay for your order.
	+ **Credit card**: Select this option for credit card payment.
	+ **Cash**: Select this option for in-person payment at the salon.
	+ **PayPal**: Select this option for online payment through PayPal.
* **Order notes** (`Order notes`, text, false): If you have any custom requests or allergies, please note them here.

## Tips
- Ensure that the "Contact email" field is accurate and up-to-date.
- Double-check that the selected product is correct and the quantity is appropriate.
- If selecting "Delivery", ensure that the "Delivery address" is complete and accurate.
- If you have any custom requests or allergies, please provide them in the "Order notes" field.
- Review your order carefully before submitting it to the salon.
