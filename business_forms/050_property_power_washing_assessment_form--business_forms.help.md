# Property Power Washing Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Power Washing Assessment Form is a tool used to collect information about a property's type, location, and other relevant details. This form helps us understand the property's characteristics, service needs, and estimated costs for power washing services. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure we can provide you with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of property you are assessing (e.g., Exterior, Deck/Patio, Pool, etc.).
2. Specify the location of the property (e.g., Home, Business, Apartment Building, etc.).
3. Provide the property size and age.
4. Select any additional features of the property (e.g., Gutters, Downspouts, Decks, etc.).
5. Indicate the services needed (e.g., Clean, Disinfect, Strip, etc.).
6. Specify the service area and estimated time required.
7. Choose the equipment to be used (e.g., Soft wash system, High pressure wash, etc.).
8. Provide the estimated cost of the service.
9. Enter your contact information (name, phone number, and email).
10. Add any additional comments or remarks.
11. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Type**: Select the type of property you are assessing (e.g., Exterior, Deck/Patio, Pool, etc.). This will help us understand the specific needs of the property.
* **Property Location**: Specify the location of the property (e.g., Home, Business, Apartment Building, etc.). This will help us determine the best approach for the service.
* **Property Size**: Enter the size of the property. This will help us estimate the time and resources needed for the service.
* **Property Age**: Provide the age of the property. This will help us assess the condition of the property and plan the services accordingly.
* **Property Features**: Select any additional features of the property (e.g., Gutters, Downspouts, Decks, etc.). This will help us tailor the service to the property's specific needs.
* **Service Needed**: Indicate the services needed (e.g., Clean, Disinfect, Strip, etc.). This will help us provide the best possible service.
* **Service Area**: Specify the service area to be cleaned. This will help us estimate the time and resources needed for the service.
* **Estimated Time**: Enter the estimated time required for the service. This will help us schedule the service accordingly.
* **Equipment**: Choose the equipment to be used (e.g., Soft wash system, High pressure wash, etc.). This will help us tailor the service to the property's specific needs.
* **Estimated Cost**: Provide the estimated cost of the service. This will help us provide a fair and accurate quote.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name and contact information (phone number and email).
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email.
* **Quote Message**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the service.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or remarks about the service.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
