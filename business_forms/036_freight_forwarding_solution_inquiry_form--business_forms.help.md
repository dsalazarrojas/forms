# Freight Forwarding Solution Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from potential customers or clients about their freight forwarding needs.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with your information.
2.  Select the type of service you require (domestic, international, or both).
3.  Choose your preferred delivery timelines.
4.  Enter your contact information (name, email, and phone number).
5.  If applicable, specify which services you are inquiring about (rate, service, or timeline).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Freight Forwarding Solution Inquiry Form**: This field is the title of the form and is pre-filled for your convenience.
* **Shipping Rates**: Enter the shipping rates you are interested in (e.g., 1-3, 4-6, etc.).
* **Service Options**: Select the type of service you require:
	+ **Domestic**: Services for shipping within your country.
	+ **International**: Services for shipping across different countries.
	+ **Both**: Services for both domestic and international shipping.
* **Delivery Timelines**: Choose your preferred delivery time frame.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name for follow-up contact purposes.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up contact purposes.
* **Company**: Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Inquiry Type**: Select the services you are inquiring about:
	+ **Rate**: Inquire about shipping rates.
	+ **Service**: Inquire about freight forwarding services.
	+ **Timeline**: Inquire about delivery timelines.
