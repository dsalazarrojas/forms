# Face Mask Requirement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Face Mask Requirement Survey is a form designed to gather information about the face mask requirements in the workplace. This survey aims to understand individual responses and preferences regarding face masks, which will help inform business operations and decisions related to face mask usage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best describe your response to each question.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.
3. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Forms** (`Business Forms`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field allows you to select the types of business forms you require or use. Please select all that apply.
* **Face Mask Requirement** (`Face Mask Requirement`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field asks about your face mask requirement. Please select one of the options provided.
* **Face Mask Type** (`Face Mask Type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for selecting the type of face mask you use or require. Please choose all that apply.
* **Face Mask Frequency** (`Face Mask Frequency`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks about how often you wear a face mask. Please select all that apply.
* **Work Area** (`Work Area`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for selecting the areas where you work with face masks. Please choose all that apply.
* **Face Mask Worn** (`Face Mask Worn`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks if you wear a face mask at work. Please select all that apply.
* **Face Mask Rejection Reasons** (`Face Mask Rejection Reasons`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for selecting reasons why you might reject a face mask. Please choose all that apply.
* **Face Mask Recommendation** (`Face Mask Recommendation`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field allows you to recommend face masks. Please select all that apply.
* **Face Mask Additional Comments** (`Face Mask Additional Comments`, `note`, required/optional): This field is for providing any additional comments or information about face masks.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR department.
* Your responses are valuable and will help inform business decisions and operations.
* Please review your responses carefully before submitting the form.
