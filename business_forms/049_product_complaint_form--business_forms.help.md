# Product Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Complaint Form is designed to help customers report issues or provide feedback on products. This form allows you to submit a complaint or feedback in a structured format, ensuring that we can efficiently categorize and prioritize each complaint.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information, such as your name, email, and complaint description.
2. Select the type of complaint and priority level if applicable.
3. Add any additional information, such as product images or company details, if necessary.
4. Review and submit the form once you've completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as the customer submitting the complaint.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you for further assistance.
* **Product Issue**: Describe the issue you're experiencing with the product.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the issue you're facing.
* **Customer Feedback**: Share your thoughts on your experience with the product.
* **Assigned To**: Select which team member or department should handle your complaint. This can be the Support Team or a specific person.
* **Priority**: Choose a priority level for your complaint:
	+ Low: Not urgent, but still need some assistance.
	+ Medium: Urgent, but can be handled in a few days.
	+ High: Emergency, need immediate attention.
* **Status**: The status of your complaint will be updated as it's processed:
	+ New: Initially submitted complaint.
	+ In Progress: Being worked on.
	+ Resolved: Complaint fully resolved.
* **Customer Signature**: This field is not required, but if you'd like to sign the complaint, please include your signature date.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name if you're submitting a complaint on behalf of your business.
* **Company Address**: Enter your company address if you're submitting a complaint from a business account.
* **Company Phone**: Enter your company phone number if you're submitting a complaint from a business account.
* **Company Email**: Enter your company email address if you're submitting a complaint from a business account.
* **Company Website**: Enter your company website if you're submitting a complaint from a business account.
* **Message**: Enter any additional information or comments about your complaint.
* **Product Image**: If you have an image of the issue, please upload it here.
* **Complaint Date**: If the issue occurred on a specific date, please include it here.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you're experiencing issues with.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your personal phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Type of Complaint**: Select the type of complaint:
	+ Defect: Issue with the product quality or functionality.
	+ Quality: Issue with the product's quality or performance.
	+ Price: Issue with the product's price or value.
	+ Other: General feedback or issue not listed above.
* **Product Code**: Enter the product code if you know it.
* **Customer Type**: Select your type:
	+ Individual: Personal complaint.
	+ Business: Complaint from a business account.
* **Department**: Enter the department that's handling your complaint.
* **Complaint Number**: Enter the complaint number if you have one.
* **Signature Date**: If you've signed the complaint, please include the date you signed it.
* **Company Website**: Enter your company website if you're submitting a complaint from a business account.

**Tips**: Please be as detailed and clear as possible when submitting your complaint. This will help us to efficiently handle and resolve your issue.
