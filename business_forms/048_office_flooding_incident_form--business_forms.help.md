# Office Flooding Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report office flooding incidents. Please fill out this form to provide detailed information about the incident, including the date, time, location, and cause.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information about the incident.
2. Make sure to answer all the questions accurately and thoroughly.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or maintenance team for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): The date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required): The time the incident occurred.
* **Location** (`location_address`, text, required): The address of the location where the incident occurred.
* **Floor** (`location_floor`, text, required): The floor where the incident occurred.
* **Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): A brief description of what happened.
* **Affected Areas** (`affected_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select all areas that were affected by the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of incident that occurred.
* **Cause** (`cause_source`, text, optional): A brief description of the cause of the incident.
* **Details** (`cause_source_details`, text, optional): Additional details about the cause of the incident.
* **Type** (`cause_source_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of cause.
* **Damage** (`cause_source_damage`, text, optional): A description of the damage caused by the incident.
* **Depth** (`cause_source_depth`, number, optional): The depth of the flooding.
* **Location** (`cause_source_location`, text, optional): The location of the cause.
* **Flow Rate** (`cause_source_flow_rate`, text, optional): The flow rate of the incident.
* **Level** (`cause_source_flow_level`, text, optional): The level of the incident.
* **Duration** (`cause_source_flow_duration`, time, optional): The duration of the incident.
* **Value** (`cause_source_depth_value`, number, optional): The value of the cause.
* **Reporting Person** (`incident_reporting_person`, select_one, optional): Select whether you are the person reporting the incident.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible about the incident.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or maintenance team for guidance.
* This form should only be filled out once for each incident.
