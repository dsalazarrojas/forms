# retail_loss_prevention_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about retail loss prevention incidents, which can help identify and prevent future losses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Incident Details" page and provide a brief description of the incident.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident.
3. Choose the location where the incident occurred.
4. If applicable, provide the suspect's details, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
5. Enter the total loss amount and provide a description of the loss.
6. Add any supporting images or videos if available.
7. Enter a detailed description of the incident.
8. Provide additional suspect information if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details**: A brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Date**: The date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: The time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location**: The location where the incident occurred.
* **Suspect Details**: If known, the suspect's name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Suspect Address**: The suspect's address.
* **Suspect Phone**: The suspect's phone number.
* **Suspect Email**: The suspect's email address.
* **Loss Amount**: The total amount of loss due to the incident.
* **Loss Description**: A description of the loss.
* **Suspect Image**: If available, an image of the suspect.
* **Suspect Video**: If available, a video of the suspect.
* **Incident Description**: A detailed description of the incident, including any relevant context.
* **Suspect Details Additional**: Any additional information about the suspect that may be relevant to the incident.
* **Incident Location Map**: A note field for any map reference that may be useful for investigation.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident and suspect.
* If you're unsure about any information, leave it out and mark it as unknown.
* Use clear and concise language to fill out the form.
