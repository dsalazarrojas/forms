# Supplier Standardization Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Supplier Standardization Application Form is designed to collect and standardize supplier information, allowing for easier tracking and management of supplier data within the organization. This form captures key details such as supplier contact information, certifications, and agreements, ensuring consistency and accuracy in the supplier's data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about the supplier.
2. Review and select the relevant options for certifications, supplier standardization, and other required fields.
3. Verify that all fields are filled in correctly and accurately before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Supplier Info** (`supplier_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the supplier's business.
- **Standardization Agreement** (`standardization_agreement`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the supplier agrees to standardization, "No" otherwise.
- **Certifications** (`certifications`, select_multiple, optional): Select relevant certifications held by the supplier.
- **Certifications List** (`certifications_list`, text, optional): List additional certifications held by the supplier.
- **More Certifications** (`certifications_list_2`, text, optional): List additional certifications held by the supplier.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the supplier.
- **Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Supplier Agreement** (`supplier_agreement`, text, optional): Enter the supplier agreement details.
- **Supplier Terms** (`supplier_terms`, text, optional): Enter any terms related to the supplier.
- **Supplier Info 2** (`supplier_info_2`, text, optional): Enter additional supplier information.
- **Certification Info** (`certification_info`, text, optional): Enter certification details.
- **Supplier Certification** (`supplier_certification`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the supplier has been certified, "No" otherwise.
- **Contact Info 2** (`contact_info_2`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the second contact person.
- **Contact Name 2** (`contact_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of the second contact person.
- **Contact Email 2** (`contact_email_2`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the second contact person.
- **Contact Phone 2** (`contact_phone_2`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the second contact person.
- **Supplier Certifications** (`supplier_certifications`, select_multiple, optional): Select relevant certifications held by the supplier.
- **Supplier Agreement 2** (`supplier_agreement_2`, text, optional): Enter the supplier agreement details.

Note: All fields marked as `optional` can be left blank if not applicable to the supplier. Ensure to complete all required fields before submission.
