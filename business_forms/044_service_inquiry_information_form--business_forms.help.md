# Service Inquiry Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Service Inquiry Information Form is designed to collect relevant information from customers or internal staff about a service inquiry. This form helps in gathering details such as client details, service request, and contact information. It is intended to be completed by customers or internal staff to provide a clear and concise way to submit service inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields to ensure accurate and thorough information submission.
* Review the options for each field carefully before making a selection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`service_inquiry_information_form`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the client or the person submitting the inquiry.
* **Potential Clients** (`potential_clients`, select_multiple, not required): Select all potential clients that the service may be applied to. (e.g., Yes, No)
* **Service Request** (`service_request`, select_one, not required): Choose the service requested. (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3)
* **Inquiry Details** (`inquiry_details`, text, not required): Provide a detailed description of the service inquiry.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, not required): Enter any relevant contact information for the client or internal staff submitting the inquiry.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter a phone number for contact purposes.
* **Service Inquiry** (`service_inquiry`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the service inquiry.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Select the relevant date for the service inquiry.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Select the relevant time for the service inquiry.
* **Location** (`location`, text, not required): Enter the location where the service will be applied.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, text, not required): Enter the service provider or internal staff responsible for the service.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, not required): Choose the type of service applied. (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3)
