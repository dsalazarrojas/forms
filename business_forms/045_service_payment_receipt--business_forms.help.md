# Service Payment Receipt - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment information for services rendered to clients. It's a crucial tool for business owners or administrators to accurately record payment details for accounting and financial purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification.
2. Enter the client's address to help identify the client accurately.
3. Select the payment method used for the transaction.
4. Enter the date of payment to track the payment timeline.
5. Enter the amount paid to ensure accurate accounting records.
6. Provide a brief description of the service provided (if required).
7. Obtain the client's signature to verify payment.
8. Obtain the business owner's or administrator's signature to verify the payment.
9. Record the date the form was submitted.
10. Indicate the payment status (e.g., paid, pending, or declined).
