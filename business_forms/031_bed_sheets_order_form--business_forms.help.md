# Bed Sheets Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bed Sheets Order Form is a customer-facing form designed to collect information for ordering bed sheets. It is intended for customers to provide their personal and payment details, select their preferred bed sheet options, and choose a payment method.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name, shipping address, and email address in the respective fields.
2.  Select your preferred bed sheet size from the options provided.
3.  Choose your preferred material type, thread count, and color for the bed sheet.
4.  Indicate the quantity of bed sheets you would like to order.
5.  Select your preferred payment method.
6.  If desired, provide any special delivery instructions.
7.  Check the box for gift wrapping, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`customer_full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required): Enter your street address, city, and state in the format "Street, City, State, ZIP".
*   **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and confirmation.
*   **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
*   **Sheet Size** (`sheet_size`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred bed sheet size from the options: Twin, Twin XL, Full / Double, Queen, King, or California King.
*   **Material Preference** (`material_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred material type from: 100% Egyptian Cotton, Organic Bamboo, Microfiber, Linen, Tencel, or Satin.
*   **Preferred Thread Count** (`thread_count`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred thread count from: 200 - 400, 400 - 600, 600 - 800, 800 - 1000.
*   **Color or Pattern** (`color_choice`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred color or pattern from: Classic White, Dove Grey, Navy Blue, Sage Green, Ivory / Cream, or Stripe Pattern.
*   **Quantity (Sets)** (`order_quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of bed sheets you would like to order.
*   **Payment Method** (`preferred_payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred payment method from: Credit Card, PayPal, Apple Pay, or Bank Transfer.
*   **Special Delivery Instructions** (`special_delivery_notes`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide any special delivery instructions, such as gate code or leaving at the back door.
*   **Gift Wrapping** (`gift_wrap_option`, `select_one`, optional): Check the box if you would like your order gift wrapped, with an additional $5.00 fee.
*   **Today's Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date today, in the format "MM/DD/YY", to confirm the order date.

Note: Please fill out all required fields accurately and completely to ensure timely and correct order fulfillment.
