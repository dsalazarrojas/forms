# Social Media Management Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Management Agreement is a contract that outlines the terms and conditions for managing a client's social media presence. This form ensures that both parties are on the same page and understand their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the client's details, including their name and contact information.
2. Select the services you will be providing, such as Social Media Management, Content Creation, or Ad Management.
3. Specify the duration of the agreement, which can be a fixed period or an open-ended option.
4. Choose the payment terms, such as hourly, project-based, monthly, or other.
5. Enter the amount of payment for the selected payment terms.
6. Agree to the terms and conditions of the contract.
7. Enter your business details and manager's details for record-keeping purposes.
8. Sign the contract, which confirms your commitment to the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required): Enter the client's name, email address, and other contact information.
* **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, required): Select the services you will be providing for the client, such as Social Media Management, Content Creation, or Ad Management.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the agreement, which can be in days, weeks, or months.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, select_one, required): Choose the payment terms, which can be Hourly, Project-Based, Monthly, or Other.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment`, number, required): Enter the amount of payment based on the selected payment terms.
* **Terms** (`terms`, text, required): Agree to the terms and conditions of the contract, which outlines your responsibilities and expectations.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, note, required): Sign the contract, which confirms your commitment to the agreement.
* **Business Details** (`business_details`, text, required): Enter your business contact information and any other relevant details.
* **Manager Details** (`manager_details`, text, required): Enter your manager's contact information and any other relevant details.
* **Contract Start** (`contract_start`, date, required): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End** (`contract_end`, date, required): Enter the end date of the contract or leave it open-ended.
* **Contract Review** (`contract_review`, select_one, required): Choose how often you want to review the contract, which can be Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, or Yearly.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the terms and conditions carefully before signing the contract.
* Ensure that you understand your roles and responsibilities as specified in the contract.
* Keep a copy of the signed contract for record-keeping purposes.
