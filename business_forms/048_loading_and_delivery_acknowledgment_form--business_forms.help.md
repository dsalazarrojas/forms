# Loading And Delivery Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loading And Delivery Acknowledgment Form is used to document the status of goods or materials being loaded and delivered to customers or sites.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the delivery details in the "Delivery Details" field.
2. Select the date the delivery was made in the "Date Delivered" field.
3. Provide a delivery note in the "Delivery Note" field.
4. Select the type of delivery made from the options in the "Delivery Type" field.
5. Choose the status of the delivery from the options in the "Delivery Status" field.
6. If applicable, enter the customer's signature and date in the "Signature" fields.
7. Enter the customer's email address and name in the "Customer Email" and "Customer Name" fields.
8. Enter the customer's address in the "Customer Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Details** (text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the delivery, including any relevant details such as delivery date, time, and location.
* **Date Delivered** (date, required: false): Enter the date the delivery was made.
* **Delivery Note** (text, required: false): Enter any notes or comments about the delivery.
* **Signature** (note, required: false): Provide your signature to acknowledge that the delivery was made.
* **Signature Date** (time, required: false): Enter the date you signed the acknowledgment.
* **Delivery Type** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Loading: Select this option if the goods or materials were loaded for delivery.
	+ Delivery: Select this option if the goods or materials were delivered to the customer site.
	+ Pickup: Select this option if you picked up goods or materials from the customer site.
	+ Other: Select this option if the delivery type does not fit into the other categories.
* **Delivery Status** (select_multiple, required: false):
	+ In Transit: Select this option if the delivery is en route to the customer site.
	+ Delivered: Select this option if the goods or materials were delivered to the customer site.
	+ Received: Select this option if the customer has received the delivery.
	+ On Hold: Select this option if the delivery is on hold due to issues with the order.
	+ On Site: Select this option if the delivery is at the customer site but not yet delivered.
	+ Cancelled: Select this option if the delivery was cancelled.
	+ Pending Pickup: Select this option if the delivery is pending pickup by the customer.
* **Customer Signature** (text, required: false): Provide the customer's signature to acknowledge receipt of the delivery.
* **Customer Email** (email, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Name** (text, required: false): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Address** (text, required: false): Enter the customer's address.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that delivery records are consistent and accurate. 
* If you are unsure about any part of the delivery process, consult with your supervisor or team lead for guidance. 
* If you have any issues or concerns, report them to your supervisor or team lead immediately.
