# Product Personalization Partnership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you, as a representative from a company, express your interest in our product personalization partnership opportunity. It will guide you through collecting necessary information about your company and your interest in working with us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the first field.
2. Provide your company's name in the "Company" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used as the primary contact point for communication.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field to ensure we can reach you easily.
5. Check the "Industry" field to indicate if your company is interested in product personalization.
6. Select the relevant products you are interested in from the "Product of Interest" field.
7. Enter your company's information in the "Company Info" field. This could be a brief description or a note about your company.
8. If your company has a website, enter the URL in the "Company Website" field.
9. Enter your company's social media handles for Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, and YouTube. If you don't have any of these, just leave the field blank.
10. Enter the URLs for your company's websites, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, and YouTube. Again, if you don't have any, just leave the field blank.
