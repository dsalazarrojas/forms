# policy_execution_confirmation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to confirm the execution of a policy by a staff member. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the policy has been properly executed, acknowledged, and recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options for "Executed By" (executed_by) and "Staff Acknowledgment" (staff_acknowledgment) to indicate whether the policy has been executed and acknowledged by the staff member.
2. If the policy has been executed, select the date and time of execution (date_executed and time_executed).
3. Enter any comments or notes related to the execution of the policy (staff_comment and manager_comment).
4. Select the date when the form was completed (execution_confirmation_form_completed_date and execution_confirmation_form_completed_time).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **policy_execution_confirmation** (Text Field):
	+ This is the text field where you can enter any additional comments or information related to the policy execution.
* **Executed By** (Select One: Yes/No):
	+ Select "Yes" if the policy has been executed by the staff member, or "No" otherwise.
* **date_executed** (Date Field):
	+ Enter the date when the policy was executed.
* **time_executed** (Time Field):
	+ Enter the time when the policy was executed.
* **executed_by_staff** (Select One: Yes/No):
	+ Select "Yes" if the staff member acknowledged the execution of the policy, or "No" otherwise.
* **staff_acknowledgment** (Select Multiple: Yes/No):
	+ Select all relevant staff members who acknowledged the policy execution.
* **manager_acknowledgment** (Select Multiple: Yes/No):
	+ Select all relevant managers who acknowledged the policy execution.
* **manager_acknowledgment_date** (Date Field):
	+ Enter the date when the manager acknowledged the policy execution.
* **executed_by_date** (Date Field):
	+ Enter the date when the policy was executed.
* **executed_by_time** (Time Field):
	+ Enter the time when the policy was executed.
* **staff_comment** (Note Field):
	+ Enter any comments or notes related to the policy execution by the staff member.
* **manager_comment** (Note Field):
	+ Enter any comments or notes related to the policy execution by the manager.
* **execution_confirmation_form_completed_date** (Date Field):
	+ Enter the date when the form was completed.
* **execution_confirmation_form_completed_time** (Time Field):
	+ Enter the time when the form was completed.

## Tips
* Make sure to select "Executed By" as "Yes" only if the policy has been properly executed and acknowledged.
* Enter accurate dates and times for policy execution and form completion.
* Use the Note fields for any additional comments or notes related to the policy execution.
