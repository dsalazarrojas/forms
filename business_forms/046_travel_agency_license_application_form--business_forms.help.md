# travel_agency_license_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel agency license application form is designed to collect information from travel agencies applying for a license. This form gathers essential data on agency and owner details, contact information, and license specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Complete the Agency Details section with your agency's basic information.
2.  In the Contact Details section, provide your agency's contact information, including phone and email.
3.  In the License Info section, provide any relevant license information.
4.  Complete the Agency Address and Owner Address sections with the agency and owner's respective addresses.
5.  Fill out the Business Name and Owner Name fields with the agency and owner's names.
6.  Finally, review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Agency Details (Agency Details)**: Enter your agency's name, address, and other basic details.
*   **Contact Details**: Enter your agency's contact phone number and email.
*   **License Info**: Enter any relevant license details, such as license number or type.
*   **Agency Address**: Enter your agency's full address.
*   **Business Name**: Enter your agency's name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Owner Name**: Enter your owner's full name.
*   **Owner Address**: Enter your owner's full address.
*   **Agency Phone**: Enter your agency's phone number.
*   **Agency Email**: Enter your agency's email address.
