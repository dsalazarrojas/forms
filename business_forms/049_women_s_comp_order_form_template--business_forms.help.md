# Women's Comp Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers regarding their women's compensation order. It is intended to be a template for businesses to streamline the order process and gather necessary details for fulfilling the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the "Customer Info" section.
2. Enter your order details in the "Order Details" section.
3. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info (customer_info)**: This section is for entering your personal details such as your name, address, phone number, and email address.
* **Order Details (order_details)**: In this section, describe the specific product or service you wish to purchase, including any specifications or customizations.
* **Payment Info (payment_info)**: Enter your payment method, such as credit card details or bank account information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* Double-check your input for accuracy before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
