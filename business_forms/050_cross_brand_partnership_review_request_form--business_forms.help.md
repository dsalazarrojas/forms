# Cross Brand Partnership Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your business and potential partner's information for cross-brand partnership reviews. It's essential to ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you are submitting the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the name of your business.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the name of the contact person for your business.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
	+ Enter a valid email address for your business or contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
	+ Enter a contact phone number for your business.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the name of the partner company (if applicable).
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the name of the partner company or person (if applicable).
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose the type of business that best describes your company (Retailer, Manufacturer, or Other).
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the location of your business.
* **Website URL** (`website_url`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the URL of your website (if applicable). 

## Tips
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* Ensure that your email address is valid and not a generic "info@" account.
* Enter a specific location for your business instead of a general region or country. 

Note that the form fields and their labels should correspond directly with the YAML file, but some explanations have been provided for better understanding and clarity.
