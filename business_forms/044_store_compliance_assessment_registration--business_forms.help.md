# Store Compliance Assessment Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Store Compliance Assessment Registration form is a crucial document that helps businesses demonstrate their commitment to regulatory compliance. It is a formal declaration of a store's compliance status, which can be used as evidence for audits, inspections, and other regulatory purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the store's compliance status by selecting the appropriate options in the Schedule Audit field.
2.  Identify the areas of compliance that need to be addressed by choosing relevant options in the Compliance Areas field.
3.  Enter the store's name and contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
4.  Add any additional comments or notes in the Note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Schedule Audit
* **Schedule Audit** (required/optional): Select the date and time for the scheduled audit.
*   *Note: This field is optional, but it is highly recommended to select an option to ensure accurate tracking of compliance assessment.*

### Compliance Areas
* **Compliance Areas** (required/optional): Choose all relevant compliance areas that the store needs to be assessed on, such as health and safety, environmental, or customer data protection.
*   *Note: This field is required to ensure that all relevant compliance areas are covered.*

### Store Name
* **Store Name** (required): Enter the full name of the store, as it appears on its business registration.
*   *Note: This field is required for identification purposes.*

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (required): Select the contact person responsible for the store's compliance.
*   *Note: This field is required for communication purposes.*

### Email
* **Email** (required): Enter a valid email address associated with the store.
*   *Note: This field is required for communication purposes.*

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (required): Enter a valid phone number associated with the store.
*   *Note: This field is required for communication purposes.*

### Note
* **Note** (optional): Add any additional comments or notes that may be relevant to the store's compliance assessment.
*   *Note: This field is optional, but it is highly recommended for providing further context.*
