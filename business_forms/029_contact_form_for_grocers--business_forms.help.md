## Contact Form For Grocers - Help Guide

## Purpose
The contact form is designed to gather information from individuals or businesses interested in inquiring about products, services, or partnerships with the grocer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), which include:
	* Contact Full Name
	* Email Address
	* Role in Contact
	* Subject of Inquiry
	* Date of Submission
2. Optionally, provide additional information, such as:
	* Phone Number
	* Preferred Store Location
	* Company Name
	* Primary Product Line
3. Select the relevant inquiry category that best matches your interest:
	* Product Availability
	* Refund/Exchange Question
	* Store/Service Feedback
	* Vendor Partnership Inquiry
	* Other

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Full Name** (id: 2, type: text, required: true): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (id: 3, type: email, required: true): Enter your primary email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (id: 4, type: text, required: false): Provide a phone number where we can reach you best.
* **Role in Contact** (id: 5, type: select_one, required: true): Select one of the following options:
	+ Customer
	+ Potential Vendor
	+ Supplier
	+ Media/PR
	+ Community Member
* **Inquiry Category** (id: 7, type: select_one, required: true): Select the category that best matches your inquiry:
	+ Product Availability
	+ Refund/Exchange Question
	+ Store/Service Feedback
	+ Vendor Partnership Inquiry
	+ Other
* **Specific Product Details** (id: 8, type: text, required: false): If applicable, provide specific details about the product you are inquiring about (e.g., brand, item name).
* **Preferred Store Location** (id: 9, type: select_one, required: false): Select the store location you prefer to contact:
	+ Downtown Store
	+ Northside Supermarket
	+ Westgate Market
	+ Online/Delivery Service
* **Company Name** (id: 11, type: text, required: false): If you represent a company, enter your company name.
* **Primary Product Line** (id: 12, type: text, required: false): Briefly describe the primary product line you provide.
* **Subject of Inquiry** (id: 14, type: text, required: true): Enter a brief summary of your inquiry.
* **Your Message** (id: 15, type: text, required: true): Provide more details about your inquiry.
* **Date of Submission** (id: 16, type: date, required: true): The date and time you are submitting this form will be recorded.

Please note that some fields are marked as optional but may be required depending on the purpose of your inquiry. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us directly.
