# Material Usage Tracking Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Material Usage Tracking Request Form is used to track material usage within a company. It is intended for employees to submit requests for materials, providing information about the material being requested, quantity, unit of measurement, unit requested, submission date, submitter's email, department, manager approval, and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Determine if material is being used and if you need to submit a request.
2. Select the correct option for 'Material Used' field.
3. Enter the quantity of material being used in the 'Quantity Requested' field.
4. Choose the unit of measurement used for the requested material in the 'Unit Used' field.
5. Determine if you need to request a specific unit in the 'Unit Requested' field.
6. Enter the date of submission in the 'Date Submitted' field.
7. Select the correct submitter's name in the 'Submitted By' field.
8. Enter the submitter's email address in the 'Submitter Email' field.
9. Choose the department that requested the material in the 'Department Requested' field.
10. Select the correct manager's approval in the 'Manager Approved' field.
11. Select the correct approver's name in the 'Approved By' field.
12. Enter any additional comments about the request in the 'Comments' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Material Used** (Select Multiple, id: 1): Indicate whether material is being used (Yes/No).
* **Quantity Requested** (Text, id: 3): Enter the quantity of material being used.
* **Unit Used** (Select One, id: 4): Choose the unit of measurement used for the material (e.g., meters, pounds, etc.).
* **Unit Requested** (Select One, id: 5): Determine if you need to request a specific unit (Yes/No).
* **Date Submitted** (Date, id: 6): Enter the date the request was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (Select One, id: 7): Select the employee who submitted the request.
* **Submitter Email** (Email, id: 8): Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Department Requested** (Select One, id: 9): Choose the department that requested the material (Yes/No).
* **Manager Approved** (Select One, id: 10): Select the manager's approval (Yes/No).
* **Approved By** (Select One, id: 11): Select the approver's name.
* **Comments** (Note, id: 12): Enter any additional comments about the request.
