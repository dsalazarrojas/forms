# Meeting Space Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Space Request Form is used to gather information about a meeting's details to determine the best available meeting space for the meeting attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the meeting title.
2. Select the meeting type (Yes or No).
3. Enter the meeting date.
4. Select the meeting start time.
5. Select the meeting end time.
6. Enter the meeting duration (in hours).
7. Select if the meeting requires space (Yes or No).
8. Select the space capacity required for the meeting (e.g., number of attendees).
9. Select if the meeting requires a specific meeting space capacity (Yes or No).
10. Select the attendees of the meeting (e.g., number of attendees).
11. Click submit to send the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Space** (meeting_space_request_form_1): Enter a brief description of the meeting space required (e.g., conference room, training room, etc.).
* **Meeting Type** (meeting_space_request_form_2): Select Yes if the meeting is a company event, No if it is not.
* **Meeting Time** (meeting_space_request_form_3): Enter the date of the meeting (mm/dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Meeting Duration** (meeting_space_request_form_4): Enter the duration of the meeting (in hours).
* **Space Capacity** (meeting_space_request_form_5): Enter the number of attendees that the meeting space can accommodate.
* **Meeting Date** (meeting_space_request_form_6): Enter the date of the meeting (mm/dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Meeting Start Time** (meeting_space_request_form_7): Select the start time of the meeting (hh:mm).
* **Meeting End Time** (meeting_space_request_form_8): Select the end time of the meeting (hh:mm).
* **Meeting Space** (meeting_space_request_form_9): Select Yes if the meeting requires a specific meeting space, No if not.
* **Meeting Space Capacity** (meeting_space_request_form_10): Select Yes if the meeting requires a specific meeting space capacity, No if not.
* **Meeting Space Capacity Count** (meeting_space_request_form_11): Note the capacity of the meeting space required.
* **Meeting Attendees** (meeting_space_request_form_12): Select the number of attendees expected to attend the meeting.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct meeting date and time to ensure accurate scheduling.
* If the meeting space is not available, you will receive an email with alternative options.
* If you need help with the form, please reach out to the meeting planner.
