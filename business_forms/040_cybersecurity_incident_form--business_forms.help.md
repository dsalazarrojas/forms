# Cybersecurity Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cybersecurity Incident Form is used to report and classify cybersecurity incidents that have occurred within the organization. This information helps the incident response team to assess and respond to incidents effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, but only if you have been instructed to do so by the incident response team. 
- Answer the questions according to your knowledge of the incident.
- Select the incident type and classification that best fit the incident you are reporting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Incident Details** (Text): Enter a brief description of the incident.
- **Incident Type** (Select One): Choose the type of incident you are reporting (e.g. Malware, Phishing, Ransomware).
- **Location** (Select Multiple): Select all locations affected by the incident (e.g. Local, Remote).
- **Date Occurred** (Date): Enter the date the incident occurred.
- **Reported By** (Select One): Choose who reported the incident (e.g. John, Jane, Jim).
- **Classification** (Select Multiple): Choose the classification of the incident based on its severity (e.g. Low, Medium, High).
- **Status** (Select One): Choose the status of the incident (e.g. Open, Closed).
- **Resolution** (Text): Enter a brief description of how the incident was resolved.
- **Additional Information (not shown)**: This field is not displayed to the user, but it is used internally by the system to store additional information about the incident.
