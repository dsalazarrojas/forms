# Co Working Station Reassignment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a change in your coworking station. Fill out this form to provide your manager with the necessary information to process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
- Enter your full name and work email address as they appear on your official records.
- Select your primary department from the provided options.
- Enter the current Station ID located on your desk.
- Choose a reason for requesting the reassignment, such as space requirements or team proximity.
- Provide the date you would like the change to take effect.
- If desired, you can provide additional details about your request.
- Lastly, specify a preferred station or area if known, or leave it blank if unknown.
- Finally, enter the name of your manager who approved this move.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your legal first and last name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email Address**: Enter your work email address.
* **Department**: Select your primary department from the options provided.
* **Current Station ID**: Enter the Station ID located on your desk.
* **Requested Reassignment Date**: Choose a date when you would like the change to take effect.
* **Reason for Request**: Select a primary reason for moving, such as space requirements or team proximity.
* **Additional Details**: Provide more information about your request if needed.
* **Preferred Station or Area**: Specify a preferred station or area if known, or leave it blank if unknown.
* **Manager Approval Name**: Enter the name of your manager who approved this move.
* **Approval Status**: This field is not visible to users and is used internally to track the status of your request.
