# Executive Business Review Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Business Review Program is a valuable opportunity to showcase company executives' expertise and achievements in the business world. By registering in this program, you will gain exposure to a broader audience, build your professional network, and have access to exclusive resources and events.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3.  Upload a high-quality photo to represent yourself in the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Title** (`program_title`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of the program or event you are participating in.
* **Executive Name** (`executive_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Executive Email** (`executive_email`, email, optional): This is the email address that we will use to contact you about the program.
* **Executive Phone** (`executive_phone`, text, optional): Your phone number will be used for program-related communications and invitations.
* **Executive Position** (`executive_position`, text, optional): Describe your current position within the company.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter your company name or organization name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your department or team name.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the city, state, or region you work in.
* **Executive Title** (`executive_title`, text, optional): Describe your job title or professional title.
* **Executive Bio** (`executive_bio`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your professional background and achievements.
* **Executive Photo** (`executive_photo`, text, optional): Upload a professional photo that represents you in the program.
* **Upload Photo** (`upload_photo`, text, optional): Use this field to upload your photo from your computer or device.
