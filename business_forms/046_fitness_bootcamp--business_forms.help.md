# Fitness Bootcamp - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants for the Fitness Bootcamp program. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a successful and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: First Name, Email, Date of Birth, and Select Multiple.
2. Enter a valid email address in the Email field.
3. Choose a valid date range for Select Date Range.
4. Select all applicable options for Select Multiple and Select Date Range.
5. Leave any other fields empty if not applicable or not necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as you wish to be known in the Fitness Bootcamp.
    * Type: `text`
    * Required: Yes
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive important updates and information about the Fitness Bootcamp.
    * Type: `email`
    * Required: Yes
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any emergency contact or further communication.
    * Type: `text`
    * Required: No
- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to ensure accurate age verification.
    * Type: `date`
    * Required: Yes
- **Time of Visit**: Select the time of your visit to the Fitness Bootcamp.
    * Type: `time`
    * Required: No
- **Address**: Enter your address for any potential delivery or shipping purposes.
    * Type: `text`
    * Required: No
- **Note**: Enter any additional information or comments about your visit or preferences.
    * Type: `note`
    * Required: No
- **Select One**: Choose one of the provided options.
    * Type: `select_one`
    * Required: No
- **Select Multiple**: Choose all applicable options.
    * Type: `select_multiple`
    * Required: Yes
- **Decimal Value**: Enter a decimal number to represent your progress or measurements.
    * Type: `number`
    * Required: No
- **Integer Value**: Enter an integer number to represent your progress or measurements.
    * Type: `number`
    * Required: No
- **Time Value**: Select the time of your preference for the program.
    * Type: `time`
    * Required: No
- **Select Date Range**: Choose a date range for your participation in the program.
    * Type: `select_one`
    * Required: Yes

## Tips
- Double-check the information you enter, especially email, date of birth, and select multiple options.
- Use valid format for date, time, and number fields.
- If you have any questions, contact the Fitness Bootcamp staff for further assistance.
