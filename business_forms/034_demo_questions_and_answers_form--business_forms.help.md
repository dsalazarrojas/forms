# Demo Questions And Answers Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Demo Questions And Answers Form is designed to collect demo questions and answers from users. It has 10 questions with different types of fields, such as text, select options, and date/time fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form to start filling out the questions.
2. Fill out each question by typing or selecting the answer according to the field type.
3. Review each question carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Question 1** (`question_1`, text, optional): Answer this question by typing in the text box. You can write a short sentence or phrase to answer this question.
* **Another question** (`question_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the options provided in the dropdown list. You can select as many options as you like.
* **What is your name** (`question_3`, email, optional): This is a field for your name. Please enter your name in the format of email address.
* **How old are you** (`question_4`, number, optional): Answer this question by typing a number to indicate your age.
* **What is your phone** (`question_5`, text, optional): This is a field for your phone number. Please enter your phone number in the correct format.
* **Select one of the following** (`question_6`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided in the dropdown list.
* **What is your age** (`question_7`, date, optional): This field is for your date of birth. Please select the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **What time** (`question_8`, time, optional): Select the time in the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
* **Note field** (`question_9`, note, optional): This is a free-form text field for any additional information.
* **What is your email** (`question_10`, email, optional): This field is for your email address. Please enter your email in the correct format.

## Tips
* Please answer each question carefully and accurately.
* If a field is optional, you can skip it if it's not relevant to you.
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
