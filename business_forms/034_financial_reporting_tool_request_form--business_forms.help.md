# Financial Reporting Tool Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Reporting Tool Request Form is designed to collect information from business users to request financial reporting tools and reports. This form helps us gather the necessary details to generate accurate and timely financial reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the Report Type that best suits your needs. You can choose from Balance, Cash Flow, or Income Statement reports.
2. Specify the Reporting Period for which you require the report.
3. Choose the Frequency of Reporting that suits your needs. You can select from Quarterly, Bi-Annual, or Annually.
4. If a specific tool has been assigned to you, select the correct option.
5. Specify the Reporting Frequency (Daily or Weekly).
6. Add any additional Comments regarding your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request for Financial Reporting** (`request_for_financial_reporting`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for you to describe the purpose of your financial reporting request.
* **Report Type** (`reporting_tool_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of report you need, such as Balance, Cash Flow, or Income Statement.
* **Reporting Period** (`reporting_period`, `date`, required/optional): Indicate the period for which you require the report, e.g., Q1, Q2, etc.
* **Frequency of Reporting** (`frequency_of_reporting`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the frequency of reporting that suits your needs, such as Quarterly, Bi-Annual, or Annually.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required/optional): If a specific tool has been assigned to you, select the correct option.
* **Reporting Frequency** (`reporting_frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the reporting frequency that suits your needs, such as Daily or Weekly.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information regarding your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you only submit one request per form.
* If you need to make multiple requests, please create a new form for each request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [support email/phone number].
