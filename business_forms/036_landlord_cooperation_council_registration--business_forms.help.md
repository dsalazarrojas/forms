# Landlord Cooperation Council Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landlord Cooperation Council Registration form is used to collect information from landlords regarding their interest and contact details. This form helps us understand their level of cooperation and facilitates communication with them for any future collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with providing your landlord details, which includes basic contact information and other relevant data.
2. Specify your interest in renting out or buying a property.
3. Indicate your level of consent for us to keep this information private.
4. Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
5. Add a brief description of your current property address (if applicable).
6. Enter your phone number for easy contact.
7. Add any additional notes about yourself or your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord Details** (`landlord_details`, Text, required): Enter your name and other basic contact information.
* **Landlord Interests** (`landlord_interests`, Select Multiple, required): Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Landlord is interested in renting out his property.
	+ Landlord is interested in buying a property.
	+ Landlord is interested in both renting out and buying a property.
* **Landlord Consent** (`landlord_consent`, Select One, required): Choose whether we should keep this information private.
* **Landlord Contact** (`landlord_contact`, Email, required): Enter a valid email address for us to contact you.
* **Landlord Address** (`landlord_address`, Text, optional): Enter a brief description of your current property address (if applicable).
* **Landlord Phone** (`landlord_phone`, Text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Landlord Notes** (`landlord_notes`, Text, optional): Add any additional notes about yourself or your situation.
