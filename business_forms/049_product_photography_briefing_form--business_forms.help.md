# Product Photography Briefing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect detailed information about a product from a customer. It ensures that the customer's photography needs are met by providing essential details about the product and the photo shoot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select a category for your product.
2. Enter the name of your product.
3. Provide a brief description of the product.
4. Choose the type of photography needed for your product.
5. Select the target audience for your product.
6. Specify the number of product images required.
7. Determine if a shoot location is needed.
8. Enter the product availability date.
9. Provide details about the product size and dimensions.
10. Specify the product color(s).
11. Choose a product finish.
12. Specify the product material.
13. Enter the product weight.
14. Enter the name and contact information of the contact person.
15. Enter the name of the company.
16. Choose a shoot date.
17. Choose a shoot time.
18. Provide more details about the shoot location.
19. Enter any additional photo shoot notes.
20. List all photo shoot requirements.
21. List the photography equipment needed.
22. Choose the image capture type.
23. Determine if retouching is required.
24. Enter any retouching notes.
25. Enter the date for reviewing the shoot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Select a category for your product from the provided options.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of your product.
* **Product Description**: A brief description of the product.
* **Photography Type**: Select the type of photography needed for your product.
* **Target Audience**: Select the target audience for your product.
* **Number of Product Images**: Specify the number of images required for the product.
* **Shoot Location**: Determine if a shoot location is needed.
* **Product Date**: Enter the product availability date.
* **Product Size**: Provide details about the product size and dimensions.
* **Product Color**: Specify the product color(s).
* **Product Finish**: Choose a product finish.
* **Product Material**: Specify the product material.
* **Product Weight**: Enter the product weight.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the contact person.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company.
* **Shoot Date**: Choose a shoot date.
* **Shoot Time**: Choose a shoot time.
* **Shoot Location Details**: Provide more details about the shoot location.
* **Photo Shoot Notes**: Enter any additional photo shoot notes.
* **Photo Shoot Requirements**: List all photo shoot requirements.
* **Photography Equipment**: List the photography equipment needed.
* **Image Capture**: Choose the image capture type.
* **Retouching**: Determine if retouching is required.
* **Retouching Notes**: Enter any retouching notes.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date for reviewing the shoot.
