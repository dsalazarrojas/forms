# Commercial Executive Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commercial Executive Membership Application Form is designed for current or prospective business leaders to apply for executive membership in a professional organization. This form gathers essential information about the applicant, their professional background, business details, and membership preferences. It helps the organization's membership committee evaluate the suitability of the applicant and provide a personalized membership experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the required fields**: Ensure you complete all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) or indicated as required in the form.
2. **Choose your professional background**: Select your primary industry from the drop-down menu.
3. **Share your experience and education**: Choose your years of experience and highest level of education achieved.
4. **Select your membership type**: Choose the membership level that suits your needs.
5. **Explain why you want to join**: Briefly describe why you are interested in joining the organization.
6. **Provide business details**: Enter your company's size and annual revenue.
7. **List your professional affiliations**: Share your current memberships and board positions.
8. **Express your commitment**: Indicate your willingness to volunteer and participate in committees.
9. **Make a payment commitment**: Choose your payment method and frequency.
10. **Agree to the code of ethics and bylaws**: Confirm your agreement to abide by the organization's code of ethics and bylaws.
11. **Authorize a background check**: Agree to allow the organization to perform a background check.
12. **Add any additional comments**: Provide any further information that you think would be helpful for the membership committee to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Professional Title**: Enter your current job title.
* **Company Name**: Enter your current employer's name.
* **Company Address**: Enter your company's business address.
* **Email Address**: Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your business phone number.
* **Industry**: Select your primary industry from the provided list.
* **Years of Experience**: Choose your level of experience based on the provided options.
* **Education Level**: Select your highest level of education achieved.
* **Membership Type**: Choose the membership level that suits your needs.
* **Reason for Applying**: Briefly describe why you are interested in joining the organization.
* **Referred By**: If applicable, provide the name of the member who referred you.
* **Company Size**: Select your company's size based on the provided options.
* **Company Annual Revenue**: Choose your company's annual revenue range.
* **Company Website**: Enter your company's website URL if applicable.
* **Current Memberships**: List any other professional memberships you hold.
* **Board Positions**: List your current or past board positions.
* **Professional Certifications**: List any professional certifications you hold.
* **Volunteer Interest**: Indicate your willingness to volunteer and participate in committees.
* **Committee Preference**: Select the committees that interest you.
* **Expected Event Attendance**: Choose how many events you expect to attend per year.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you will make payment for your membership dues.
* **Payment Frequency**: Select your payment frequency.
* **Code of Ethics Agreement**: Confirm your agreement to abide by the organization's code of ethics.
* **Bylaws Acceptance**: Confirm your acceptance of the organization's bylaws.
* **Background Check Authorization**: Agree to allow the organization to perform a background check.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information you think would be helpful for the membership committee to know.

Please review each field carefully and ensure accurate and complete information is provided to increase the chances of your membership application being approved.
