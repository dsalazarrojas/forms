# Legal Retainer Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help collect information for the payment of legal retainers. It's an original creation, not affiliated with any form platform, and its content is based on public survey topics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and fields to understand what's required.
2. Fill in the necessary fields:
	* **What is the name of the client** (`first_question`): Enter the name of the client as it appears in your records.
	* **Client company** (`client_company`): Enter the name of the client's company.
	* **Client name** (`client_name`): Enter the name of the client as it appears on their identification documents.
3. Review and submit the form to send the payment request to the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the name of the client** (`first_question`): This field asks for the name of the client as it appears in your records.
* **Client company** (`client_company`): Enter the official name of the client's company.
* **Client name** (`client_name`): Enter the full name of the client as it appears on their identification documents.

## Tips

* Make sure to use the correct name of the client as it appears in your records to avoid confusion.
* Double-check the accuracy of the client's company name to ensure proper payment processing.
* If you're unsure about any information, consult with the client or your supervisor before submitting the form.
* This form is meant to simplify the legal retainer payment process; use it as a helpful tool, but not a substitute for professional legal consultation.
