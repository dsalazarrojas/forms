# Team Strength Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team Strength Assessment form is designed to help teams evaluate their performance and identify areas for improvement. This form is meant to be completed by team members to provide an honest assessment of their strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To get started, select the **Survey Questions** page and provide your honest answers to the questions in the text input field. This section is meant to gather information about your team's goals, challenges, and concerns.
2.  Next, proceed to the **Performance Indicators** page. Here, select up to three **Performance Indicators** that best represent your team's performance. Choose from the options listed.
3.  Finally, on the **Team Size** page, enter the number of members in your team. This information will help us better understand the dynamics of your team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Survey Questions**: This text input field is where you can provide detailed information about your team's goals, challenges, and concerns. Please answer these questions honestly and thoroughly.
*   **Performance Indicators**: Select up to three **Performance Indicators** that best represent your team's performance. These indicators help us understand how you measure and track your team's progress.
*   **Team Size**: Enter the number of members in your team. This information is important for us to understand the team's dynamics and structure.
