# Multiple Choice Recurring Subscriptions - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Multiple Choice Recurring Subscriptions form is used to collect data from customers regarding their subscription preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all the subscriptions you are currently subscribed to by choosing from the available options on the "Select a Recurring Subscription" page.
2. Choose the relevant subscription plan on the "Subscription Plan" page.
3. Specify your preferred payment method and frequency on the "Payment Method" and "Payment Frequency" pages, respectively.
4. Enter your preferred payment due date on the "Payment Due Date" page.
5. Enter your customer details on the "Customer" page.
6. Enter your phone number on the "Phone" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select a Recurring Subscription** (`recurring_subscription`, select_multiple, **Required**): Select all the subscriptions you are currently subscribed to. Choose all the relevant options from the list provided.
* **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`, select_one, **Required**): Select the relevant subscription plan that applies to your subscription. Choose the option that best describes your subscription plan.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, **Required**): Specify your preferred payment method. Choose all the methods that apply to you.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, **Required**): Specify your preferred payment frequency. Choose the frequency that suits your subscription.
* **Payment Due Date** (`payment_date`, date, **Required**): Enter your payment due date. This is the date on which the subscription payment is due.
* **Customer** (`customer`, text, **Required**): Enter your name as the customer.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Ensure you are selecting all relevant subscriptions on the "Select a Recurring Subscription" page.
- Double-check your subscription plan on the "Subscription Plan" page.
- Verify your payment method and frequency on the "Payment Method" and "Payment Frequency" pages.
- Enter your payment due date accurately on the "Payment Due Date" page.
- Enter your name correctly on the "Customer" page.
- Enter your phone number correctly on the "Phone" page.
