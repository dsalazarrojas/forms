# Automation Supplier Directory Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to register your company in the automation supplier directory, providing a centralized platform for companies to showcase their products and services. By registering, you will be able to:

* Showcase your company's products and services to a targeted audience of potential customers
* Increase your online visibility and credibility
* Establish yourself as a trusted supplier in the industry

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your company information, including company name, website, description, and address
2. Provide details about your company, such as year established, company size, and annual revenue
3. Add contact information, including contact name, title, email, and phone number
4. Specify the industries you serve and the products or services you offer
5. Select the types of listings you would like to have in the directory (Basic Free, Standard, Premium, or Featured)
6. Upload your company logo and any additional marketing materials
7. Review and agree to the directory guidelines, terms and conditions, and marketing consent
8. Provide a digital signature and any additional comments or comments
