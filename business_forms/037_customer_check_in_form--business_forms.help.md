# Customer Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about customers checking in at your business. It includes fields for reservation information, reason for visit, name, and email. The form is designed to be quick and easy to complete, and the information collected can be used to improve customer service and follow-up interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Reservation Information page.
2. Provide your reason for visiting on the Reason For Visit page.
3. Enter your name on the Name page.
4. Enter your email address on the Email page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Information**: This is the first page of the form. If you have a reservation number or code, you can enter it here. Otherwise, select the "No Reservation" option.
* **Reason For Visit**: Provide a brief explanation of why you are visiting our business. This can be for shopping, dining, or any other reason.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address where we can contact you about your visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly, as this is how we will contact you if we need to follow up about your visit.
* If you have any special requests or instructions for your reservation, please let us know in the Reason For Visit field.
* If you are unable to complete any of the fields, please ask a staff member for assistance.
