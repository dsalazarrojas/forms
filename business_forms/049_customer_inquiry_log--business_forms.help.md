# Customer Inquiry Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Inquiry Log form is used to allow customers to submit inquiries or requests for information, support, or other business-related matters. This form helps us track and manage these inquiries effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your company name
* Provide your contact number
* Choose your preferred inquiry type (phone, email, or in-person)
* Type in your message
* If you would like to be followed up on, please select 'Yes'
* If you would like to be contacted by email, please provide your email address

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (Company)**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number where we can reach you.
* **Inquiry Type**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or in-person).
* **Message**: Type in your inquiry or request for information.
* **Contact Name**: If you would like to be contacted by a specific person, please enter their name.
* **Customer Message**: If you have any additional comments or details about your inquiry.
* **Business Response**: This field is for us to respond to your inquiry.
* **Follow Up**: If you would like to be followed up on, please select 'Yes'. (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Date Submitted**: This field will be automatically populated with the date and time you submit this form. (This field is not editable by the user.)
* **Time Submitted**: This field will be automatically populated with the time you submit this form. (This field is not editable by the user.)
* **Email**: If you would like to be contacted by email, please provide your email address. 

Note: The Date and Time fields are automatically populated and cannot be edited by the user. They are intended to track when the form was submitted, but are not relevant to the user's interaction with the form.
