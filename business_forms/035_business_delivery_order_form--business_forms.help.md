# Business Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for business delivery orders, ensuring accurate and timely delivery of products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your contact details, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the preferred delivery date and time for the order.
3. Specify the product type, quantity, and any special requirements.
4. Enter the unit price and item total for the order.
5. If applicable, provide any additional items to be ordered.
6. Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, debit card, cash on delivery, PayPal, or bank transfer).
7. Review the subtotal, delivery fee, tax amount, and total amount to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required): Enter your full name as the customer or the name of the company or business placing the order.
* **Email Address** (Customer Email, email, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (Customer Phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for any further communication.
* **Delivery Address** (Delivery Address, text, required): Enter the street address where the delivery should be made.
* **City** (City, text, required): Enter the city where the delivery should be made.
* **State/Province** (State/Province, text, required): Enter the state or province where the delivery should be made.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (ZIP/Postal Code, text, required): Enter the postal code for the delivery address.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (Preferred Delivery Date, date, required): Select the date you would like to receive the delivery.
* **Preferred Delivery Time** (Preferred Delivery Time, select one, required): Select your preferred delivery time: Morning (8am-12pm), Afternoon (12pm-5pm), Evening (5pm-9pm), or Flexible.
* **Product Type** (Product Type, text, required): Specify the type of product being ordered.
* **Quantity** (Quantity, number, required): Enter the number of units ordered.
* **Product Specifications** (Product Specifications, text, optional): Enter any special requirements or specifications for the product.
* **Unit Price** (Unit Price, number, required): Enter the price per unit for the product.
* **Item Total** (Item Total, number, optional): This field will be auto-calculated, but you can review and edit the total if necessary.
* **Additional Items** (Additional Items, text, optional): If applicable, enter any other products to be ordered.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select one, required): Select how you will pay for the order: Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash on Delivery, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Subtotal** (Subtotal, number, required): This field will be auto-calculated, but you can review and edit the total if necessary.
* **Delivery Fee** (Delivery Fee, number, optional): Enter the shipping or delivery cost.
* **Tax Amount** (Tax Amount, number, optional): This field will be auto-calculated, but you can review and edit the total if necessary.
* **Total Amount** (Total Amount, number, required): This field will be auto-calculated, but you can review and edit the total if necessary.
