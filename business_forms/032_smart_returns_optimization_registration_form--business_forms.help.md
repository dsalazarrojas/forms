# Smart Returns Optimization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect business information for smart returns optimization. Businesses can use this form to provide their contact details, return volumes, and challenges they face in their operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the business details section with your business information.
2. Select the relevant challenges that you face in your operations.
3. Provide return volumes for each relevant return type.
4. Fill in the contact information with your business contact details.
5. Add any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Details (1)**: Enter your business name and other relevant details.
* **Return Volumes (2)**: Enter the number of returns you have experienced in the past month.
* **Challenges (3)**: Describe the challenges you face in your business operations.
* **Challenges List (4)**: Select all the challenges that apply to your business.
* **Business Details 1-5 (5-9)**: Enter additional business details if required.
* **Challenge 1-5 (10-14)**: Describe the challenges you face for each challenge type.
* **Return Volumes 1-4 (15-18)**: Enter the number of returns you have experienced for each return type.
* **Submit (15, 19, 20, 21)**: Click to submit your business information.
* **Email (22)**: Enter your business email address.
* **Phone (23)**: Enter your business phone number.
* **Address (24)**: Enter your business address.
* **Notes (25)**: Add any additional notes if necessary.
* **Contact Email (26)**: Enter the email address of the person to contact.
* **Phone 1 (27)**: Enter the phone number of the person to contact.
* **Contact Address (28)**: Enter the address of the person to contact.
* **Contact Notes (29)**: Add any additional contact notes if necessary.
