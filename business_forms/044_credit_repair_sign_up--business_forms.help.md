# credit_repair_sign_up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The credit_repair_sign_up form is designed to collect personal and business information from users who want to sign up for credit repair services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first page with your personal details.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, Business Name, Address, and Date of Birth.
3. Enter your Email (if you have one).
4. If you have a Phone number, enter it.
5. Add any additional Note if you have any specific requirements.
6. Review your information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Business Name** (Text): Enter the name of your business (if applicable).
* **Address** (Text): Enter your business or residential address.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your business or personal email (if you have one, otherwise leave blank).
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Date of Birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Note** (Note): Add any additional information you would like to share with us.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to avoid delays in processing your form.
* If you have any issues with your form submission, please contact us.
* This form is intended for internal use within the company and should not be reused or filled out multiple times.
