# Employee Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Daily Report Form is designed to gather daily information from employees, helping management and team leads track work progress, identify potential issues, and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name under "employee_name".
2. Enter the date you are submitting the report.
3. Record your activities, tasks, and any notable events under "employee_activities".
4. List any tasks assigned to you under "employee_tasks".
5. Provide a brief update on your productivity under "productivity".
6. Add any additional comments or feedback under "comments".
7. Select your current status under "status" (e.g., Active, On leave, On break).
8. Identify who the report is assigned to (e.g., Team Lead, Manager, Director) under "assigned_to".
9. Specify who you would like to receive comments from (e.g., Employee, Manager, HR) under "comments_to".
10. If applicable, provide any tools or resources assigned to you under "assigned_tool".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name** (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
* **date** (date): Select the date you are submitting this report.
* **time** (time): Record the time you completed this report.
* **employee_activities** (text): Describe your main activities and tasks performed today.
* **employee_tasks** (text): List any tasks assigned to you or tasks you completed today.
* **productivity** (text): Provide a brief update on your productivity and any challenges faced.
* **comments** (text): Add any additional comments or feedback on your tasks, projects, or work environment.
* **status** (select one): Select your current status:
	+ Active
	+ On leave
	+ On break
* **assigned_to** (select multiple): Identify who this report is assigned to:
	+ Team Lead
	+ Manager
	+ Director
* **comments_to** (select multiple): Specify who you would like to receive comments from:
	+ Employee
	+ Manager
	+ HR
* **assigned_tool** (text): Provide any tools or resources assigned to you for this task or project.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form in a clear and concise manner.
* Ensure you have permission from your assigned contact before submitting your report.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* Review and update your report before submitting it.
* Submit your report at the end of each workday or as instructed by your supervisor.
