# Agent Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Performance Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the performance of an agent or sales representative during a given evaluation period. It helps evaluators provide feedback on key aspects of the agent's job and sets goals for future evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Evaluator Name**: Enter your name as the evaluator.
2. **Agent Name**: Enter the name of the agent being evaluated.
3. **Evaluation Period Start**: Select the start date of the evaluation period.
4. **Evaluation Period End**: Select the end date of the evaluation period.
5. **Sales Goal Achievement**: Choose how well the agent met their sales targets (Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Below Expectations, or Not Applicable).
6. **Quality of Customer Interactions**: Rate the quality of the agent's customer interactions (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
7. **Adherence to Company Policies and Processes**: Rate the agent's adherence to company policies and processes (Fully Compliant, Mostly Compliant, Frequent issues, or Not applicable).
8. **Collaboration and Teamwork**: Rate the agent's collaboration and teamwork skills (Strong Team Player, Collaborative, Needs Improvement, or Individualistic).
9. **Key Accomplishments**: List major successes achieved during the evaluation period.
10. **Areas Requiring Development and Training**: Specify areas where the agent needs improvement and training.
11. **Overall Performance Rating**: Provide a final performance rating (5 - Outstanding, 4 - Commendable, 3 - Effective, 2 - Marginal, or 1 - Unsatisfactory).
12. **Goals for the Next Evaluation Period**: Set three key objectives for the next evaluation period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator Name** (`Evaluator Name`, text, required): This field is for your name as the evaluator.
* **Agent Name** (`Agent Name`, text, required): This field is for the name of the agent being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Period Start** (`Evaluation Period Start`, date, required): Choose the start date of the evaluation period.
* **Evaluation Period End** (`Evaluation Period End`, date, required): Choose the end date of the evaluation period.
* **Sales Goal Achievement** (`Sales Goal Achievement`, select_one, required): Rate the agent's sales goal achievement (Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Below Expectations, or Not applicable).
* **Quality of Customer Interactions** (`Quality of Customer Interactions`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the agent's customer interactions (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Adherence to Company Policies and Processes** (`Adherence to Company Policies and Processes`, select_one, required): Rate the agent's adherence to company policies and processes (Fully Compliant, Mostly Compliant, Frequent issues, or Not applicable).
* **Collaboration and Teamwork** (`Collaboration and Teamwork`, select_one, required): Rate the agent's collaboration and teamwork skills (Strong Team Player, Collaborative, Needs Improvement, or Individualistic).
* **Key Accomplishments** (`Key Accomplishments`, text, optional): This field is for listing major successes achieved during the evaluation period.
* **Areas Requiring Development and Training** (`Areas Requiring Development and Training`, text, optional): Specify areas where the agent needs improvement and training.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`Overall Performance Rating`, select_one, required): Provide a final performance rating (5 - Outstanding, 4 - Commendable, 3 - Effective, 2 - Marginal, or 1 - Unsatisfactory).
* **Goals for the Next Evaluation Period** (`Goals for the Next Evaluation Period`, text, required): Set three key objectives for the next evaluation period.

**Tips**

* Please provide specific examples and supporting evidence for each rating and goal.
* Be honest and objective in your evaluation, and avoid personal biases.
* Use this form to provide constructive feedback that helps the agent grow and improve.
