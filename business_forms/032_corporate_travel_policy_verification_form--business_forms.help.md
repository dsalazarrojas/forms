# corporate_travel_policy_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to verify an employee's adherence to the company's travel policy. It is used to gather information about business travel habits to ensure compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all required questions.
2. Provide accurate and honest information about your travel activities.
3. If you are unsure about any question or section, please reach out to your HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Travel Policy 
* **Company Travel Policy** (`Company Travel Policy`, `note`, required/optional): This field is a brief description of the company's travel policy.
* **Company Travel Policy** (`Company Travel Policy`, `note`, required/optional): This field is for any additional information about the company's travel policy.
### Travel Frequency 
* **Travel Frequency** (`travel_frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the following options to indicate how often you travel for business: Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely.
### Travel Type 
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply for the type of business travel you undertake: Business, Personal.
### Travel Reason 
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the reason for your business travel.
* **Travel Reason 1** (`travel_reason_1`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the reason for your recent business travel.
* **Travel Reason 2** (`travel_reason_2`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the reason for your recent business travel.
### Travel Frequency Description 
* **Travel Frequency 1** (`travel_frequency_1`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the frequency of your recent business travel.
* **Travel Frequency 2 Description** (`travel_frequency_2_description`, `note`, required/optional): This field is for a description of the travel frequency you entered.
* **Travel Frequency 3** (`travel_frequency_3`, `note`, required/optional): This field is for any additional notes about the travel frequency you entered.
### Travel Type 
* **Travel Type 1** (`travel_type_1`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply for the type of business travel you undertake.
* **Travel Type 2** (`travel_type_2`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply for the type of business travel you undertake.

## Tips
* Ensure you answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any question or section, please reach out to your HR representative for guidance.
* This form is intended for internal use only and is not for use by external parties.
