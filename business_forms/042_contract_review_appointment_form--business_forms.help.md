# Contract Review Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contract Review Appointment Form is a tool used by clients to schedule a contract review appointment with a lawyer. This form collects information about the client's contract, including the type of agreement, appointment date and time, and other relevant details. The lawyer uses this information to prepare for the meeting and provide personalized service to the client.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*)
2. Enter your contact information, including your full name, email, and phone number
3. Select the type of agreement you are reviewing (e.g., Service agreement, Vendor contract)
4. Choose a date and time for the appointment that suits you best
5. Select a meeting format that suits your preference (e.g., In-person, Video conference)
6. Indicate the urgency level of the appointment (e.g., Routine, Urgent)
7. Provide any additional comments or concerns you may have

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, enter the name of the organization.
* **Client Title** (`client_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or role in the organization.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date you would like to schedule the appointment.
* **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of agreement you are reviewing (e.g., Service agreement, Vendor contract).
* **Contract Title** (`contract_title`, text, required): Enter the name of the contract or agreement.
* **Contract Value** (`contract_value`, number, optional): Enter the value of the contract, if applicable.
* **Review Type** (`review_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of review you need (e.g., Initial draft review, Revision review).
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`preferred_appointment_date`, date, required): Select the date and time you prefer for the appointment.
* **Alternative Appointment Date** (`alternative_appointment_date`, date, optional): Enter an alternative date and time in case the preferred date is not available.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, select_one, required): Select the time of day you prefer for the appointment (e.g., Morning, Afternoon).
* **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of the appointment (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour).
* **Meeting Format** (`meeting_format`, select_one, required): Select the meeting format that suits your preference (e.g., In-person, Video conference).
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Indicate the urgency level of the appointment (e.g., Routine, Urgent).
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, date, required): Enter the deadline for the review.
* **Documents to Review** (`documents_to_review`, text, required): List any relevant documents for review.
* **Specific Concerns** (`specific_concerns`, text, optional): Enter any specific concerns or issues you would like the lawyer to focus on during the review.
* **Prior Legal Review** (`prior_legal_review`, select_one, optional): Indicate if the contract has been reviewed by a lawyer before.
* **Budget for Review** (`budget_for_review`, select_one, optional): Enter your estimated budget for the review.
* **Preferred Attorney** (`preferred_attorney`, text, optional): Enter the name of your preferred attorney or a preference for the lawyer who will review your contract.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Select the source of your referral.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your contract review needs.
