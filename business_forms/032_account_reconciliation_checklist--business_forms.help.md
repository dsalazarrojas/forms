# Account Reconciliation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help users reconcile account balances between the general ledger (GL) and sub-ledger or bank statement balances. It guides users through the process of verifying account balances, identifying discrepancies, and ensuring that all issues are resolved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the general information section, providing your name and the account name.
2. Enter the account number and reconciliation period in the respective fields.
3. Verify the balance per the general ledger and sub-ledger or bank statement balance in the "Balance Verification" section.
4. Complete the "Reconciliation Checklist" by selecting all items that have been verified.
5. Enter the number of outstanding reconciling items and a brief description.
6. Indicate if adjusting journal entries are required.
7. Select the reconciliation status, which indicates if the account is complete, complete with noted exceptions, or incomplete.
8. Date and time the completion of the form.
9. Add any additional notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Preparer Name**: Enter your name as the person preparing the form.
* **Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the person reviewing the form.
* **Account Name**: Enter the name of the account being reconciled.
* **Account Number**: Enter the account number associated with the account being reconciled.
* **Reconciliation Period**: Select the period for which the account balance is being reconciled (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annually, Annually).
* **Period End Date**: Enter the date of the last day of the reconciliation period.
* **General Ledger Balance**: Enter the balance per the general ledger.
* **Sub-Ledger or Bank Statement Balance**: Enter the balance per the supporting documentation (e.g., sub-ledger or bank statement).
* **Variance Amount**: Calculate the difference between the GL and sub-ledger balances.
* **Is the Variance Fully Explained**: Indicate if the variance is fully explained or not.
* **Checklist Items Completed**: Select all items that have been verified in the "Reconciliation Checklist" section.
* **Number of Outstanding Reconciling Items**: Enter the number of items that have not been reconciled.
* **Description of Outstanding Items**: Briefly describe the items that have not been reconciled.
* **Are Adjusting Journal Entries Required**: Indicate if adjusting journal entries are required.
* **Reconciliation Status**: Select the status of the account reconciliation, which indicates if it's complete, complete with noted exceptions, or incomplete.
* **Date Completed**: Date and time the form is completed.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the reconciliation process.
