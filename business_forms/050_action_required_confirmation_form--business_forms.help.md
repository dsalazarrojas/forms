# Action Required Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to confirm that a recipient has understood and accepted the instructions and will complete the required action. It is essential to ensure that the recipient acknowledges their responsibilities and commitments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by providing the required information in each field.
2. Enter the recipient's name in the `Recipient name` field.
3. Provide a brief description of the action required in the `Instruction summary` field.
4. Choose the priority level of the action from the `Priority level` dropdown menu.
5. Confirm acceptance of the instruction by selecting the corresponding option in the `Acceptance confirmation` field.
6. Provide resources needed to complete the action in the `Required resources` field (optional).
7. Enter the person to contact for questions in the `Follow up contact` field (optional).
8. Sign the form by typing your full name in the `Signature` field.
9. Date the confirmation by entering the date in the `Date signed` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Confirmation details** (`confirmation_header`): This is a note field that provides a brief description of the form purpose.
* **Recipient name** (`recipient_name`): Enter the name of the person receiving the instruction.
* **Instruction summary** (`instruction_summary`): Provide a brief description of the action required.
* **Due date** (`due_date`): Enter the date by which the action must be completed.
* **Priority level** (`priority_level`): Choose the importance of the action from the dropdown menu.
	+ **Low**: The action is not urgent.
	+ **Medium**: The action is somewhat urgent.
	+ **High**: The action is important and should be completed as soon as possible.
	+ **Critical**: The action is extremely urgent and must be completed immediately.
* **Acceptance confirmation** (`acceptance_confirmation`): Confirm that you accept the instruction by selecting the corresponding option.
	+ **I accept and will complete**: I agree to complete the action.
	+ **I acknowledge but need clarification**: I need more information or clarification before completing the action.
	+ **I decline**: I will not complete the action.
* **Required resources** (`required_resources`): Provide resources needed to complete the action (optional).
* **Follow up contact** (`follow_up_contact`): Enter the person to contact for questions (optional).
* **Signature** (`signature`): Type your full name as confirmation of the signature.
* **Date signed** (`date_signed`): Enter the date the confirmation was signed.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any extra information for the requester (optional).
