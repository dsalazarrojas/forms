# Daily Performance Goals Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Performance Goals Form is a tool designed to help employees set and track their daily performance objectives. It allows individuals to clearly define what they need to accomplish and how they will measure their progress.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your employee name.
2.  Specify the task you are focused on accomplishing.
3.  Clearly state your objective for the day.
4.  Indicate how you will measure your performance (e.g., number of tasks completed, quality of work, etc.).
5.  Record your actual performance (e.g., number of tasks completed, hours worked, etc.).
6.  Enter the date and time you completed this form.
7.  Add any additional notes or comments about your performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as an employee.
* **Task** (`task`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the task you are focused on.
* **Objective** (`objective`, text, required/optional): Clearly state what you aim to accomplish today.
* **Performance Measure** (`performance_measure`, text, required/optional): Indicate how you will measure your progress (e.g., number of tasks completed).
* **Actual Performance** (`actual_performance`, text, required/optional): Record your actual progress (e.g., number of tasks completed, hours worked).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the performance goals were set and tracked.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when you completed the form, to track your performance in a time frame.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your performance.

## Tips
- Be specific and clear when defining your objectives and performance measures.
- Track your progress throughout the day to ensure accurate reporting.
- Review your form regularly to evaluate your performance and identify areas for improvement.
