# Business Impact Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to help organizations assess their potential impact of a disruption and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your organization's name, which will be used to identify your responses.
2. Rate your organizational readiness for an unexpected disruption (Operational Readiness Rating).
3. Select the critical business functions that must be maintained at all times.
4. Rate your reliance on physical infrastructure (Infrastructure Vulnerability Assessment).
5. Choose the most likely outcome of a 48-hour disruption.
6. Rate your employees' awareness of disaster recovery procedures (Mitigation Strategy Awareness Score).
7. Select the level of dependency on supply chain partners (Supply Chain Dependency Level).
8. Rate your confidence in your current risk data (Risk Assessment Confidence).
9. Identify the primary concern that poses the biggest threat.
10. Briefly describe any major past disruptions that may be relevant.
11. Set the maximum acceptable downtime for critical functions (Recovery Time Objective).
12. Finally, submit the date and time you completed the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name**: Enter your organization's full name.
* **Operational Readiness Rating (1-5)**: Rate how prepared your organization is for an unexpected disruption (e.g., 1 = not prepared, 5 = highly prepared).
* **Critical Business Functions**: Select all the functions that must be maintained at all times, such as:
	+ IT / Communication Systems
	+ Customer Support
	+ Sales and Revenue Generation
	+ Logistics / Supply Chain
	+ Human Resources / Payroll
	+ Research and Development
* **Infrastructure Vulnerability Assessment (High, Moderate, or Low)**: Rate your reliance on physical infrastructure (e.g., High = dependent on a specific location, Moderate = partially mobile, Low = fully distributed/Cloud-based).
* **Potential Impact of 48-hour Downtime**: Select the most likely outcome of a 2-day disruption (e.g., Catastrophic financial loss, Significant operational delay).
* **Mitigation Strategy Awareness Score (1-5)**: Rate how aware employees are of disaster recovery procedures.
* **Supply Chain Dependency Level (Critical, High, Moderate, or Low)**: Select the level of dependency on supply chain partners (e.g., Critical = single failure stops operations, High = significant reliance on key vendors).
* **Risk Assessment Confidence (1-5)**: Rate your confidence in your current risk data.
* **Primary Threat Concern**: Identify the biggest threat that poses a concern.
* **Previous Disruption Experience**: Briefly describe any major past disruptions.
* **Recovery Time Objective (Hours)**: Set the maximum acceptable downtime for critical functions.
* **Survey Date**: Enter the date and time you completed the survey.
