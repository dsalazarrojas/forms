# indoor_air_quality_program_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The indoor air quality program registration form was designed to collect information from facilities about their needs and details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your facility's details, including any relevant information you want to share.
2. List your facility's contacts, including their names, titles, and contact information.
3. Select the type of service your facility requires (e.g., Indoor Air Quality, HVAC, etc.).
4. If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Details** (`facility_details`, `text`, optional)
  • This is the first page of the form where you can enter your facility's name and any relevant details.
* **Facility Contacts** (`facility_contacts`, `text`, optional)
  • This is where you can list your facility's contact information, including their names, titles, and contact details.
* **Service Needs** (`service_needs`, `select_one`, optional)
  • This is where you can select the type of service your facility requires (e.g., Indoor Air Quality, HVAC, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional)
  • If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them in this field. For example, you may want to include any specific requirements or constraints your facility has.
* **Service Needs Options**:
  • This field is a dropdown menu with the following options: Indoor Air Quality, HVAC, Other, Plumbing. Select the option that best fits your facility's needs.
