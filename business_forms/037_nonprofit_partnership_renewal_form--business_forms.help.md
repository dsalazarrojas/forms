# Nonprofit Partnership Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Partnership Renewal Form is designed to help you renew your partnership with our organization. This form ensures that we have accurate and up-to-date information about your nonprofit, which is essential for maintaining our partnership and providing ongoing support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization**: Enter the full name of your nonprofit organization. This field is used to identify your organization for our records.
* **Name**: Enter the full name of the contact person responsible for this partnership. This field is used to identify the key contact for our communication and support.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of your contact person. This field is used for communication and will be used to send updates and notifications about your partnership.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of your contact person. This field is used for phone communication and support.
* **Address**: Enter the physical address of your organization. This field is optional but recommended for record-keeping purposes.
* **Date**: Enter the date of when your partnership started. This field is used to determine the length of our partnership.
* **Time**: Enter the time of when your partnership started. This field is optional and used for scheduling purposes.
* **Contact Person**: Re-enter the full name of the contact person responsible for this partnership. This field is a duplicate of the "Name" field and is required for verification purposes.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person. This field is a duplicate of the "Email" field and is required for verification purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person. This field is a duplicate of the "Phone" field and is required for verification purposes.
* **Partnership Start Date**: Enter the date of when your partnership started. This field is used to determine the length of our partnership.
* **Partnership End Date**: Enter the date of when your partnership will end. This field is used to determine the remaining term of our partnership.
* **Partnership Status**: Select the current status of your partnership:
	+ **Active**: Your partnership is currently active and in good standing.
	+ **Inactive**: Your partnership is currently inactive due to reasons such as suspension or cancellation.
	+ **Expired**: Your partnership has expired and is no longer valid.
* **Renewal Cycle**: Enter the renewal cycle number for your partnership. This field is used to track the number of renewals for your partnership.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
