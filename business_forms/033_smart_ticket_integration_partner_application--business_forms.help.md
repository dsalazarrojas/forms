# Smart Ticket Integration Partner Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential integration partners to help us assess their qualifications and suitability for integration with our Smart Ticket system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following fields:

1. **Company Details**: This section is used to capture information about your company, including your name and a brief description.
2. **Technical Experience**: In this section, please provide information about your technical experience related to integration with our Smart Ticket system.
3. **Terms and Conditions**: This is a one-time checkbox to acknowledge that you understand and agree to our terms and conditions.
4. **Contact Information**: Here, you will enter your contact name, email address, and phone number, so we can get in touch with you if we need more information.
5. **Additional Information**: This section is optional and allows you to provide any additional details about your company or integration capabilities that you feel are relevant to our partnership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company name** (`company name`, text, required): Your company's name, which will be displayed on our platform as your partner name.
* **Contact name** (`contact name`, text, required): The name of the person filling out this form, who will be the primary contact for our communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): The email address of the person filling out this form, which we will use for communication and follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Your phone number, which we will use for follow-up questions or to schedule calls.
* **Company description** (`company description`, text, optional): A brief description of your company, which will help us understand your business and products/services.
* **Technical expertise** (`technical expertise`, text, optional): A description of your technical expertise and experience related to integration with our Smart Ticket system.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_consent`, text, checkbox, required): Please check this box to acknowledge that you understand and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Company Name** (not explicitly labeled, but implied as **company details**): This field should contain your company's name.
* **Technical Expertise** (`technical_expertise`, text): Please describe your technical expertise related to integration with our Smart Ticket system.
* **Company Description** (`company_description`, text): Please describe your company and products/services in a few sentences.
