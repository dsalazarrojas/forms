# Coastal Property Conservation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from property owners or individuals who want to conserve coastal properties. Its purpose is to understand the conservation goals, methods, and timeline for protecting these sensitive areas.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your property owner information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter your property's mailing address and county location.
3. Specify the total property acreage and the number of acres you want to conserve.
4. Identify the primary habitat type of your property.
5. Describe any sensitive or protected areas on your property.
6. Indicate the current land use of your property.
7. Specify your conservation objectives, such as habitat protection or water quality improvement.
8. Describe your conservation goals and vision for the property.
9. Choose a conservation method, such as a conservation easement or land donation.
10. Provide details about your chosen conservation method.
11. Specify your implementation timeline.
12. Enter your budget available for conservation efforts.
13. Finally, submit the form with the registration date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Name**: Enter your full name as the property owner.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for follow-up questions or updates.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your property's mailing address.
* **Property Address**: Enter your property's address.
* **County**: Enter the county where your property is located.
* **Total Property Acreage**: Enter the total acreage of your property.
* **Acres to be Conserved**: Enter the number of acres you want to conserve.
* **Primary Habitat Type**: Choose the primary habitat type of your property.
* **Sensitive or Protected Areas**: Describe any critical habitats or protected areas on your property.
* **Current Land Use**: Indicate how your property is currently used.
* **Conservation Objectives**: Choose one or more conservation objectives for your property, such as habitat protection or water quality improvement.
* **Detailed Conservation Goals**: Describe your conservation vision for the property.
* **Proposed Conservation Method**: Choose a conservation method, such as a conservation easement or land donation.
* **Method Details**: Provide specifics about your chosen conservation method.
* **Implementation Timeline**: Specify when you plan to start conservation efforts.
* **Budget Available for Conservation**: Enter your estimated budget for conservation efforts.
* **Registration Date**: Enter the current date for registration purposes.
