# Store Ownership Interest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your interest in owning a store and your level of involvement in the store's operations. It will help us understand your preferences and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your level of interest in owning a store by choosing from the available options: Very interested, Somewhat interested, or Not interested.
2. Enter the percentage of ownership you have, if any.
3. Indicate your level of involvement in the store's operations by selecting all that apply: True, False, Some.
4. Provide any additional store details in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your interest in owning a store?** (`store_ownership_interest`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This field asks you to select your level of interest in owning a store. Choose from: Very interested, Somewhat interested, or Not interested.
* **What percentage of ownership do you have?** (`store_ownership_percentage`, number, required: false)
	+ Enter the percentage of ownership you have, if any. You can type in a numerical value.
* **Involvement in store operations** (`store_ownership_involvement`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose all that apply to indicate your level of involvement in the store's operations: True, False, Some.
* **Store Details** (`store_details`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide any additional information about the store you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to include them in the Store Details field.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can always ask for clarification.
