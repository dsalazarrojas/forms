# AlumierMD X Jotform Consultation Form 2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients or customers to help AlumierMD professionals provide personalized consultations and recommendations for their skin care needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Start" button to begin the form.
2. Fill out each field as prompted.
3. Click "Submit" to submit the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for consultation confirmation and communication.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up or consultation purposes.
* **Preferred Consultation Date** (`consultation_date`, date, required): Select a date for your preferred consultation.
* **Preferred Time** (`consultation_time`, select_one, required): Choose a time slot for your consultation (9 AM - 12 PM, 12 PM - 3 PM, 3 PM - 6 PM, 6 PM - 9 PM).
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, optional): Select your age range to help us better understand your skin concerns.
	+ 18 - 25
	+ 26 - 35
	+ 36 - 45
	+ 46 - 55
	+ 56 - 65
	+ 65+
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, select_one, required): Choose your skin type (Normal, Dry, Oily, Combination, Sensitive).
* **Primary Skin Concern** (`primary_skin_concern`, select_one, required): Select the main reason for your consultation (Acne, Aging and Wrinkles, Hyperpigmentation, Dull Skin, Sensitivity, Texture Issues, Other).
* **Secondary Concerns** (`secondary_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional skin concerns you may have (Dark Circles, Redness, Oiliness, Dryness, Rosacea, Eczema, Sun Damage).
* **Describe Current Skincare Routine** (`skincare_routine`, text, optional): Share your current skincare routine and the products you use.
* **Known Product Allergies or Sensitivities** (`product_allergies`, text, optional): List any ingredients or products you are allergic or sensitive to.
* **Skincare Goals** (`skincare_goals`, text, required): Describe your skincare goals and what you hope to achieve.
* **Budget Range for Products** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Choose a budget range for your expected product investment (Under $50, $50 - $100, $100 - $200, $200 - $500, Over $500).
* **Skincare Experience** (`skincare_experience`, select_one, required): Select your level of experience with skincare products and routines (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Very Experienced).
* **Professional Treatments Received** (`professional_treatments`, text, optional): Share any professional treatments or services you have received (Facials, Peels, Lasers, etc.).
* **Current Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications that may affect your skin.
* **Sun Exposure Level** (`sun_exposure`, select_one, optional): Select your sun exposure level (Minimal, Moderate, Frequent, Very Frequent).
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`heard_about_us`, select_one, optional): Choose where you heard about AlumierMD (Google Search, Instagram, Friend Recommendation, Other Social Media, Advertisement, Other).
* **Additional Notes or Questions** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Leave any additional notes or questions for the consultation.
