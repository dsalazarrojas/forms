# Digital Signature Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Signature Authorization Form is designed to collect information about digital signatures for various business forms, legal forms, contracts, agreements, and documents. This form helps to track and manage digital signatures, ensuring that all necessary information is recorded accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Indicate your authorization status for business forms, legal forms, contracts, agreements, and documents using the provided options.
4. Upload any relevant documents related to the signature.
5. Enter your signatures as requested.
6. Provide details about the signature status, type, and level.
7. Select the expiration date and time for the signatures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Digital Signature Authorization** (`digital_signature_authorization_form`, Text, Optional): This field is used to enter your authorization status for digital signatures.
* **Business Forms** (`business_forms`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose whether you have access to business forms.
* **Legal Forms** (`legal_forms`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose whether you have access to legal forms.
* **Contracts** (`contracts`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose whether you have access to contracts.
* **Agreements** (`agreements`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose whether you have access to agreements.
* **Documents** (`documents`, Text, Optional): Please upload any relevant documents related to the signature.
* **Signatures** (`signatures`, Text, Optional): Enter your signature as requested.
* **Signature Status** (`signature_status`, Text, Optional): Enter the status of the signature.
* **Signature Type** (`signature_type`, Text, Optional): Enter the type of signature.
* **Signature Level** (`signature_level`, Select One, Optional): Choose the level of signature (High, Low).
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, Date, Optional): Select the expiration date for the signature.
* **Expiration Time** (`expiration_time`, Time, Optional): Select the expiration time for the signature.

Note: Ensure you have uploaded all required documents and entered all necessary information before submitting the form.
