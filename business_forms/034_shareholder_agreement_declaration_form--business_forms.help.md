# Shareholder Agreement Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shareholder Agreement Declaration Form is a legal document that outlines the agreement between shareholders and the company regarding the shareholding terms. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and compliant process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the step-by-step guide below:

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and implications of each field.
2. Fill in the required information for each field accurately and completely.
3. Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy and correctness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shareholding Percentage (1)**: This field is used to record the percentage of shareholding for each shareholder. Enter the percentage as a decimal value (e.g., 25.5).
* **Legal Name (2)**: This field is used to record the legal name of the shareholder. Enter the full legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Address (3)**: This field is used to record the address of the shareholder. Enter the complete address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Contact Person (4)**: This field is used to record the contact person's name and contact information. Enter the name and contact details of the person responsible for communicating with the shareholder.
* **Date Of Formation (5)**: This field is used to record the date the company was formed. Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Number Of Shares (6)**: This field is used to record the total number of shares held by the shareholder. Enter the number as a whole number (e.g., 1000).
* **Other Shares (7)**: This field is used to record any additional shares held by the shareholder. Enter any other shares held, if applicable.
* **Percentage (8)**: This field is used to record the percentage of shareholding for the additional shares. Enter the percentage as a decimal value (e.g., 5.5).
* **Shares Owned (9)**: This field is used to record the total number of shares owned by the shareholder. Enter the number as a whole number (e.g., 5000).
* **Share Capital (10)**: This field is used to record the amount of share capital. Enter the amount as a text value (e.g., "USD 100,000").
* **Profit Distribution (11)**: This field is used to select the profit distribution method. Choose one or more of the available options (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Shareholding Agreement (12)**: This field is used to record the date of the shareholding agreement. Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Agreement Date (13)**: This field is used to record the date the agreement was signed. Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Agreement Type (14)**: This field is used to record the type of agreement. Enter the type as a text value (e.g., "Shareholding Agreement").
* **Termination Date (15)**: This field is used to record the date of termination. Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Termination Type (16)**: This field is used to record the type of termination. Enter the type as a text value (e.g., "Termination of Shareholding Agreement").
* **Termination Status (17)**: This field is used to record the termination status. Enter the status as a text value (e.g., "Terminated").
* **Reason For Termination (18)**: This field is used to record the reason for termination. Enter the reason as a text value (e.g., "Shareholder Resigned").
* **Notice Period (19)**: This field is used to record the notice period for termination. Enter the number of days as a whole number (e.g., 30).
* **Share Capital Amount (20)**: This field is used to record the amount of share capital. Enter the amount as a text value (e.g., "USD 50,000").
* **Notice Period Days (21)**: This field is used to record the notice period in days. Enter the number of days as a whole number (e.g., 60).
* **Termination Fee (22)**: This field is used to record the termination fee. Enter the fee as a number (e.g., 1000).
* **Liquidation Amount (23)**: This field is used to record the liquidation amount. Enter the amount as a text value (e.g., "USD 20,000").
* **Termination Fee Currency (24)**: This field is used to record the currency of the termination fee. Enter the currency as a text value (e.g., "USD").
* **Share Capital Currency (25)**: This field is used to record the currency of the share capital. Enter the currency as a text value (e.g., "USD").
