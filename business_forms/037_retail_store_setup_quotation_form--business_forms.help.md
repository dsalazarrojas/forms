# Retail Store Setup Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a retail store's setup. It's essential for ensuring that the store's information is accurately represented in the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the retail store in the "Store Name" field.
2. Provide the store's address in the "Store Address" field.
3. Enter the store's contact email in the "Store Contact Email" field.
4. Enter the first name of the contact person in the "Contact Person First Name" field.
5. Enter the last name of the contact person in the "Contact Person Last Name" field.
6. Enter the store's hours of operation in the "Store Hours" field.
7. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact Person Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the retail store.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the store's address.
* **Store Contact Email** (`store_contact_email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter the store's contact email.
* **Contact Person First Name** (`contact_first_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the store's hours of operation.
* **Contact Person Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the contact person's phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid any errors.
* If the store is already registered in the system, please check the store's profile to review and update its information.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the system administrator for assistance.
