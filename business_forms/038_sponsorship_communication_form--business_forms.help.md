# sponsorship_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **sponsorship_communication_form** is a tool to help event organizers understand sponsors' needs and tailor communication accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and ensure you are an authorized representative of the sponsoring organization.
2. Fill in the **Event Details** field with any relevant information about the event.
3. Select your preferred method of communication in the **Communication Preferences** field.
4. Choose the **Interests** and **Sponsorship Opportunities** that apply to your organization.
5. Ensure you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: A field to provide additional information about the event (optional).
* **Sponsor Contact**: A field to provide your contact information (optional).
* **Communication Preferences**: Select how you would like to be communicated with (Email, Phone, or Mail) (required).
* **Interests**: Choose the areas your organization is interested in (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3) (required).
* **Sponsorship Opportunities**: Choose the sponsorship opportunities your organization is interested in (Opportunity 1, Opportunity 2, or Opportunity 3) (required).
* **Event Start Date**: The date of the event (optional).
* **Event Start Time**: The start time of the event (optional).
* **Event Duration**: The duration of the event (optional).

## Tips
- Please only fill out this form if you are an authorized representative of the sponsoring organization.
- Ensure you are familiar with the event details and sponsorship opportunities before completing this form.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact the event organizers directly.
