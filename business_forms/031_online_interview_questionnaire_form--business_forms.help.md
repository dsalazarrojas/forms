# online_interview_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online interview questionnaire form is used to collect information from candidates for a job posting. This form helps to gather essential details about the candidate, including their contact information, job history, and job preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide details about your job history, such as the job title, start and end dates, and job duration.
3. Select your job level, job category, and job type.
4. Add any additional notes about your job or experience.
5. Choose the interviewers you would like to be contacted by.
6. Select the preferred interview date and time.
7. Enter the location of the interview.
8. Provide details about the company you are applying to, including the company name, size, and address.
9. Enter your contact name, title, department, and job title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **job_title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the job you are applying for.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **job_start_date** (`job_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your current or previous job.
* **job_end_date** (`job_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your current or previous job.
* **job_duration** (`job_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your job in months or years.
* **job_level** (`job_level`, select_one, optional): Select your job level, which can be junior, mid-level, or senior.
* **job_category** (`job_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the job, which can be management or non-management.
* **note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your job or experience.
* **interviewers** (`interviewers`, select_multiple, optional): Select the interviewers you would like to be contacted by.
* **interviewers_2** (`interviewers_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the secondary interviewers you would like to be contacted by. (Note: This field is optional and can be used to add additional interviewers.)
* **interview_time** (`interview_time`, time, optional): Select the preferred interview time. (Note: This field is optional and can be left blank.)
* **interview_date** (`interview_date`, date, optional): Select the preferred interview date. (Note: This field is optional and can be left blank.)
* **interview_location** (`interview_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the interview. (Note: This field is optional and can be left blank.)
* **job_type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of job, which can be full-time or part-time.
* **job_status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the job, which can be active or inactive.
* **job_notes** (`job_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your job or experience.
* **company_name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company you are applying to.
* **company_size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of the company, which can be 1-10, 11-50, 51-100, or 101-1000.
* **company_address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the company.
* **contact_name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **contact_title** (`contact_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **contact_department** (`contact_department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the contact person.
* **contact_job_title** (`contact_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the contact person.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth and efficient interview process.
- Double-check your contact information to ensure it is correct.
- Select all applicable interviewers and preferred interview times to ensure you are available for the interview.
- Be clear and concise in your responses to help the interviewer understand your qualifications and experience.
