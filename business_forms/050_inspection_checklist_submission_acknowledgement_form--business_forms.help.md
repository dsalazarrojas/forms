# inspection_checklist_submission_acknowledgement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to acknowledge and submit inspection checklists. This form is used to collect information from users to track and record the completion of inspection checklists. It helps to ensure that all required steps have been taken and that the submitter is acknowledging their involvement in the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that you are accurately providing the correct information.
* Submit the form once you have filled it out completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Details** (`business_details`, `number`, required: false): Enter the business details related to the inspection checklist, if applicable.
* **Inspection Data** (`inspection_data`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant inspection data or notes.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you are submitting the inspection checklist.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the user who submitted the form.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **First Page** (`first_page`, `note`, required: false): This is a note field for any initial information or comments.
