# Lead Generation Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Generation Checklist Form is designed to gather information about leads and their preferences for updates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by entering your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the lead source from the provided options.
3. If you selected "Other" as the lead source, you can provide additional information in the "Other" field.
4. Choose whether you want to receive updates or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Generation Checklist Form** (Text): This is the title of the form, which is for display purposes only.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address to receive updates about leads.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Lead Source** (Select One): Select the source of the lead from the provided options.
* **Other** (Select Multiple): If you selected "Other" as the lead source, provide additional information about the lead source.
* **Agree To Receive Updates** (Select One): Choose whether you want to receive updates or not. 

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your information is recorded correctly.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact our support team.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that you have provided accurate information.
