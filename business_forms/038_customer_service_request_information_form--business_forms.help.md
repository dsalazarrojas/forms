This form is for customers to submit a service request to the customer service team. The form is split into three pages, each containing a specific set of fields. The fields are designed to gather information about the customer, their request, and any attachments or additional comments they would like to include.

To fill out the form, please follow the steps below.

## customer_service_request_information_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for customers to submit a service request to the customer service team. The information collected will be used to help the team understand and resolve the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by filling out the fields on the first page, which includes customer information.
- Proceed to the second page to provide more detailed information about the request.
- If additional comments or attachments are necessary, use the fields on the second page.
- Review and submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Page
* **Customer Information** (`title`, `text`, `required`): Please fill out the customer's name, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Phone** (`title`, `text`, `required`): Enter the customer's contact phone number.

### Second Page
* **Problem Description** (`title`, `text`, `required`): Briefly describe the problem or issue the customer is experiencing.
* **Request Type** (`title`, `select_one`, `required`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Service Request
	+ Order Inquiry
	+ Order Issue
* **Request Category** (`title`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Service Request
	+ Order Inquiry
	+ Order Issue
* **Request Date** (`title`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of the request.
* **Request Time** (`title`, `time`, `required`): Enter the time of the request.
* **Requester** (`title`, `text`, `required`): Enter the customer's name.
* **Requester Email** (`title`, `email`, `required`): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Requester Phone** (`title`, `text`, `required`): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Requester Location** (`title`, `text`, `required`): Enter the customer's location.
* **Requester Company** (`title`, `text`, `required`): Enter the customer's company name.
* **Additional Comments** (`title`, `text`, `required`): Enter any additional comments or information about the request.
* **Additional Notes** (`title`, `text`, `required`): Enter any additional notes or details about the request.
* **File Attachment** (`title`, `note`, `required`): Upload a file attachment if necessary.
* **File Attachment 2** (`title`, `note`, `required`): Upload another file attachment if necessary.

### Third Page
* **Service Request** (`title`, `text`, `required`): Describe the service request.
* **Service Request Description** (`title`, `text`, `required`): Briefly describe the service request.

## Tips

- Please fill out all required fields to ensure that the customer service team can efficiently process your request.
- If you have any attachments or additional comments, please upload them or include them in the form.
- Review your form before submitting it to ensure that all fields are completed correctly.
