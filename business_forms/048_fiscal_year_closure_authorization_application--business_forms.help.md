# Fiscal Year Closure Authorization Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fiscal Year Closure Authorization Application is used to request approval for the closure of a fiscal year. This form is used to gather information and obtain approval from an approver for the financial review and authorization of year-end activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields as required and as much as necessary to provide a complete and accurate submission.
2. Review the options for each field and select the correct choices that apply to your situation.
3. Ensure that you have all necessary information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **FyC Authorizaton Request** (`fyc_authorization_request`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter a brief description of the fiscal year closure request.
* **Request Reason** (`request_reason`, text, optional): Please provide the reason for requesting the closure of the fiscal year. This can include any relevant information or context that will help the approver understand your request.
* **Approval Approvals** (`approval_approvals`, select_multiple, optional): Select all approval categories that apply to your request. These include "Yes" options such as "Manager", "Department Head", "Director", and "CEO".
* **Financial Review** (`financial_review`, select_multiple, optional): Select all financial review categories that apply to your request. These include "Yes" options such as "Balance Sheet", "Income Statement", and "Cash Flow Statement".
* **Documentation** (`documentation`, select_multiple, optional): Select all documentation categories that apply to your request. These include "Yes" options such as "Year-End Report", "Financial Statements", and "Audit Report".
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who will be approving your request.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the approver.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the approver.
* **Approver Date** (`approver_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the approver expects to approve your request.
* **Approver Time** (`approver_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of day when the approver expects to approve your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all necessary information and approvals before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that you have selected all relevant options.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, please ask your approver for clarification.
* Once you have completed the form, review and submit it for approval.
