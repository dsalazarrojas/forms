# Director Information Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect director information for official purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary details required to complete the form accurately.
2. Fill in the required fields with your personal and professional information.
3. If you have additional information not covered by the standard fields, use the optional fields to provide more details.
4. Make sure to review and verify the accuracy of the information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a phone number, otherwise select 'No'.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
*   **Job Title Other** (`job_title_other`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if your job title is not listed, otherwise select 'No'.
*   **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter your department.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Time of Hire** (`time_of_hire`, `time`, optional): Enter your time of hire in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
*   **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, `note`, optional): Enter the last time you updated this information.
*   **Organisation** (`organisation`, `text`, required): Enter your organisation.
*   **Position** (`position`, `text`, optional): Enter your position in the organisation.
*   **Designation** (`designation`, `text`, optional): Enter your designation.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your address.
*   **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Enter your country.
*   **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state.
*   **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city.
*   **Pin** (`pin`, `text`, required): Enter your pin code.
*   **Other** (`other`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information not covered by the standard fields.
*   **Organisation Other** (`organisation_other`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your organisation if not listed.
*   **Designation Other** (`designation_other`, `text`, optional): Enter your other designation.
*   **Country Other** (`country_other`, `text`, optional): Enter your other country.
*   **State Other** (`state_other`, `text`, optional): Enter your other state.
*   **City Other** (`city_other`, `text`, optional): Enter your other city.
