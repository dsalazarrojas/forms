# Political Canvassing Debriefing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Political Canvassing Debriefing Form is a tool for recording and documenting interactions during political canvassing activities. It helps to gather and track essential information about individuals who have been canvassed, enabling the organization to assess the effectiveness of their efforts and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the First Name of the person you have interacted with.
2. Enter the Last Name of the person you have interacted with.
3. Enter their Email (if applicable).
4. Record their Contact Number (phone number or other contact info).
5. Select the name of the person who conducted the canvassing.
6. Choose the date and time of the interaction using the respective fields for Canvassed Date and Canvassed Time.
7. Optionally, add any additional Notes about the interaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter the first name of the person you have interacted with.
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the person you have interacted with.
* **Email**: If the person you have interacted with has an email address, enter it here. This field is not required.
* **Contact Number**: Record the contact number (phone number or other contact info) of the person you have interacted with. This field is required.
* **Canvassed By**: Select the name of the person who conducted the canvassing from the provided options. This field is required.
* **Canvassed Date**: Enter the date of the interaction. Use the calendar icon to select the date.
* **Canvassed Time**: Enter the time of the interaction. Use the clock icon to select the time.
* **Canvassed By** is actually the "Canvassed By" field that I will correct to "Select the name of the person who conducted the canvassing" in the field-by-field explanation. 
* **Notes**: Optional notes about the interaction. If you have any additional comments or details to share, enter them here.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure accurate records.
- Double-check the information provided to ensure it matches your actual interaction with the individual.
- Use the provided options for Canvassed By to select the correct person who conducted the canvassing.
- If you have any issues or concerns while filling this form, please reach out to your supervisor or support team for assistance.
