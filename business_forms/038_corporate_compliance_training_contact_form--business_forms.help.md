# Corporate Compliance Training Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from corporate users who are interested in attending corporate compliance training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the training topic that best describes your needs.
2. Choose a preferred date for the training session.
3. Enter your contact information (name, email, and phone number) so we can reach out to you to confirm your training session.
4. If applicable, select the department that will be attending the training.
5. Optionally, provide the number of training hours you require.
6. Any additional comments or requests can be added in the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Contact**: Enter the name of the contact person or team that will be receiving training.
* **Training Topic**: Select the training topic that best describes your needs (e.g. Training and Development, Regulatory Compliance, Risk Management, etc.).
* **Preferred Date**: Choose a date for the training session. If no date is selected, we will contact you to schedule a date and time.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like the training to start.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you would like the training to end.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name and title (e.g. John Smith, Compliance Officer).
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you to confirm your training session.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Department**: Select the department that will be receiving the training (e.g. Finance, HR, Operations, etc.).
* **Training Hours**: If you have a specific number of hours you require for the training, enter it here.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or requests you may have about the training session.
