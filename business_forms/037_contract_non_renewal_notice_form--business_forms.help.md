# Contract Non Renewal Notice Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to notify the other party of non-renewal of a contract. It is an essential document that outlines the reasons for non-renewal and ensures a smooth transition. Ensure you are using this form for the correct reason and in compliance with any applicable laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the sender's details:
	* **Sender Name**: Provide your full name as the individual responsible for sending the non-renewal notice.
	* **Sender Title**: Enter your job title or position.
	* **Organization**: Type in the name of your organization or company.
2. Enter contract details:
	* **Notice Date**: Specify the date of the non-renewal notice.
	* **Contract Number**: Enter the contract reference number.
	* **Contract Title**: Provide the name of the contract being non-renewed.
	* **Contract Start Date**: Enter the original start date of the contract.
	* **Contract End Date**: Enter the current expiration date of the contract.
3. Select the non-renewal reason:
	* **Non-Renewal Reason**: Choose the reason for non-renewal from the list provided.
	* **Reason Details**: Explain the reason for non-renewal in a few sentences.
4. Confirm notice compliance:
	* **Notice Period Compliance**: Indicate whether you are fully compliant with the notice requirements or if you need a waiver.
5. Transition arrangements:
	* **Transition Assistance**: Determine if you will provide any transition assistance to the other party.
	* **Transition Period**: If providing transition assistance, specify the duration of the transition period.
	* **Outstanding Obligations**: List any outstanding commitments or obligations that need to be fulfilled.
6. Payment details:
	* **Final Payment Date**: Enter the date of the final payment for the non-renewed contract.
7. Property return:
	* **Property Return**: If applicable, specify the property that needs to be returned to the other party.
8. Future relationship:
	* **Future Relationship**: Indicate if you are open to future work with the other party.
9. Notice delivery:
	* **Delivery Method**: Choose the method of notice delivery (e.g., certified mail, email, hand delivery, etc.).
10. Finalize the form:
	* **Sender Signature**: Sign your name as the sender.
	* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information that may be relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sender Name (1)**: Full name of the individual responsible for sending the non-renewal notice.
* **Sender Title (2)**: Job title or position of the individual responsible for sending the non-renewal notice.
* **Organization (3)**: Name of the company or organization sending the non-renewal notice.
* **Notice Date (4)**: Date of the non-renewal notice.
* **Contract Number (5)**: Reference number for the contract being non-renewed.
* **Contract Title (6)**: Name of the contract being non-renewed.
* **Contract Start Date (7)**: Original start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date (8)**: Current expiration date of the contract.
* **Other Party Name (9)**: Name of the counterparty or other party involved in the contract.
* **Other Party Address (10)**: Mailing address of the counterparty or other party involved.
* **Non-Renewal Reason (11)**: Select the reason for non-renewal from the provided list.
* **Reason Details (12)**: Explain the reason for non-renewal in a few sentences.
* **Notice Period Compliance (13)**: Indicate compliance with notice requirements or need for a waiver.
* **Required Notice Days (14)**: Number of days required for notice.
* **Actual Notice Days (15)**: Number of days actually given for notice.
* **Transition Assistance (16)**: Determine if transition assistance will be provided.
* **Transition Period (17)**: Duration of the transition period if assistance is provided.
* **Outstanding Obligations (18)**: List any outstanding commitments or obligations.
* **Final Payment Date (19)**: Date of the final payment for the non-renewed contract.
* **Property Return (20)**: If applicable, specify property to be returned to the other party.
* **Future Relationship (21)**: Indicate openness to future work with the other party.
* **Delivery Method (22)**: Choose the method of notice delivery.
* **Sender Signature (23)**: Sign your name as the sender.
* **Additional Comments (24)**: Provide any additional comments or information.

## Tips
* Ensure you are using this form for the correct reason and in compliance with any applicable laws and regulations.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled in accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a legal expert.
* Keep in mind that this form is a formal document, so ensure all information is accurate and complete.
