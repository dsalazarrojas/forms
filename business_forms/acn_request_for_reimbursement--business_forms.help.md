# ACN Request For Reimbursement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from users for ACN Request For Reimbursement. It is used to capture personal details, reimbursement information, and feedback on the user's experience with the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Full name**, **Email address**, and **Phone number** (if provided).
2. Select your **Department** from the list provided.
3. Enter the **Date of Expense** when the expense was incurred.
4. Choose the **Type of Expense** from the provided options.
5. Enter the **Expense Amount (USD)** for the total amount of the expense.
6. Select the **Currency** of the expense.
7. Select your level of **Satisfaction** with the form.
8. Enter your **Overall rating** of the form.
9. Select the **Aspects** of your experience that applied to you (if any).
10. Provide any **Additional comments** or **Feedback** you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Enter your legal first and last name.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid corporate email address. This will be used for verification purposes.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number with country code (e.g., +1 555 123 4567). This is optional.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your internal employee identifier (if provided).
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to from the list provided.
* **Date of Expense**: Enter the date when the expense was incurred.
* **Type of Expense**: Choose the primary category of the expense (e.g., Travel, Meals, etc.).
* **Expense Amount (USD)**: Enter the total amount of the expense in U.S. dollars.
* **Currency**: Select the currency of the expense (e.g., USD, EUR, etc.).
* **Satisfaction**: Select the level of satisfaction with the form that best reflects your experience.
* **Overall rating**: Enter a rating from 1 to 10, where 1 is the worst and 10 is the best.
* **Aspects**: Select the aspects of your experience that applied to you (e.g., Easy to use, Fast, etc.).
* **Additional comments or feedback**: Provide any other information you wish to share about your experience.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to ensure that your reimbursement is processed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* Provide specific feedback and suggestions for improvement in the **Additional comments or feedback** section.
* Fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your submission is valid.
