# Digital Process Redesign Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for redesigning a digital process. Please fill out the form with your contact information and details about the process you'd like to see redesigned.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a title for the process you'd like to see redesigned.
2. Select the category for the process (e.g., "Business Forms" or "Information Request Forms").
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your name.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Briefly describe the process you'd like to see redesigned.
7. If you have any supporting files or documents related to the process, you can attach them to this form.
8. If you have any additional comments or attachments, you can include them in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Digital Process Redesign Contact Form** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the process you'd like to see redesigned. This will help our team quickly identify your request.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): Select the category for the process you'd like to see redesigned (e.g., "Business Forms" or "Information Request Forms").
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you with updates about your request.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Question** (`question`, text, required): Briefly describe the process you'd like to see redesigned.
* **Form File** (`form_file`, text, required): If you have any supporting files or documents related to the process, you can attach them to this field.
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or attachments, you can include them in this field.

Note: The "Attachment" field is optional, but if you do choose to use it, please make sure to include any additional comments or explanations to help our team understand your request.
