# Free Brand Audit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request a free brand audit, a process to analyze and improve your business's brand image and reputation. We'll use this information to provide you with expert guidance on how to enhance your brand's online presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions accurately and concisely.
2. Select your preferred method of communication (email, phone, or chat).
3. Choose your desired frequency of updates (one-time, weekly, or bi-weekly).
4. Select any additional free resources you'd like to receive (eBook, webinar, or checklist).
5. Describe your business in a few sentences.
6. Provide any specific questions or concerns about the free brand audit process.
7. Rate your experience level with branding and marketing (1-5 or 1-10).
8. Specify your expected outcome from the free brand audit (e.g., improved brand reputation, increased sales).
9. Estimate the time required for the free brand audit process.
10. Indicate your interest in paid services after the free brand audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What type of business do you have?**: Please enter the type of business you have, such as Marketing, Consulting, E-commerce, etc.
* **2. What is the main purpose of your free brand audit request?**: Enter the primary reason you're requesting a free brand audit.
* **3. Can you describe your business in a few sentences?**: Give a brief summary of your business to help us better understand your goals and expectations.
* **4. What is your preferred method of communication?**: Select how you'd like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Chat).
* **5. Are you interested in a one-time free brand audit or would you like to receive regular updates?**: Choose from the options: One-time free brand audit, Weekly updates, Bi-weekly updates.
* **6. Would you like to receive any free resources in addition to the audit report?**: Select from the options: E-book, Webinar, Checklist.
* **7. Do you have any specific questions or concerns about the free brand audit process?**: Enter any questions or concerns you may have.
* **8. How would you rate your level of experience with branding and marketing?**: Rate your experience level on a scale of 1-5 or 1-10.
* **9. What is your expected outcome from the free brand audit?**: Enter the desired outcome of the free brand audit.
* **10. How long do you expect the free brand audit process to take?**: Provide an estimate of the time required for the free brand audit process.
* **11. Are you interested in paid services after the free brand audit?**: Select from the options: Option A, Option B.
* **12. Would you like to provide any additional information about yourself or your business?**: Use this field to add any additional details you feel are relevant to your request.
