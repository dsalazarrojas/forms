# Contract Renewal Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about contract renewals, including details about the requestor, contract terms, and other relevant factors affecting the renewal decision. The form is used to ensure accurate and thorough documentation of contract renewal requests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these general steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the contract and your request.
2. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review your responses for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1

* **Requestor Name (1)**: Enter your full name as the requestor of the contract renewal.
* **Requestor Title (2)**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Organization (3)**: Enter your company or organization's name.
* **Email Address (4)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number (5)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Request Date (6)**: Enter the date you requested this contract renewal.
* **Contract Number (7)**: Enter the contract reference number or ID.
* **Contract Title (8)**: Enter the name of the contract.

### Page 2

* **Current Contract Start (9)**: Enter the original start date of the contract.
* **Current Contract End (10)**: Enter the expiration date of the current contract.
* **Renewal Type (11)**: Select the type of renewal you are requesting (e.g., same terms, modified terms, extended duration, etc.).
* **Proposed Renewal Duration (12)**: Select the proposed duration of the contract renewal (e.g., 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, etc.).
* **Current Contract Value (13)**: Enter the current annual value of the contract.
* **Proposed Renewal Value (14)**: Enter the proposed new annual value of the contract.
* **Satisfaction Rating (15)**: Rate your satisfaction level with the service provided (e.g., very satisfied, satisfied, neutral, dissatisfied, very dissatisfied).

### Page 3

* **Reasons for Renewal (16)**: Select all reasons why you are requesting a contract renewal (e.g., satisfied with performance, cost-effective, reliable service, etc.).
* **Proposed Changes (17)**: Describe any changes you wish to make to the contract (optional).
* **Issues to Address (18)**: Identify any issues or problems with the current contract that need to be addressed (optional).
* **Alternative Options Considered (19)**: Indicate if you have considered alternative options or quotes (e.g., yes, multiple quotes; yes, one alternative; no, staying with current; not applicable).
* **Decision Timeline (20)**: Enter the date by which you need a decision on the contract renewal.
* **Budget Approved (21)**: Indicate if the renewal budget has been approved (e.g., true, false, pending approval).
* **Stakeholders (22)**: List any stakeholders involved in the contract renewal process (optional).
* **Additional Comments (23)**: Enter any additional information relevant to the contract renewal (optional).

This help guide should provide end-users with a clear understanding of the form's purpose and requirements, ensuring accurate and thorough completion of the contract renewal request form.
