# Club Funding Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Funding Request Form is used to submit a request for funding for club-related activities, events, or expenses. This form helps the club to provide detailed information about their financial needs and justify their funding request to the club's financial advisors or funding committee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your club's basic information, including the club's name, type, founding year, and contact email and phone number.
2. Describe your club's funding request, including the purpose of the request, a detailed description of how the funds will be used, and a timeline for the fund usage.
3. Provide a justification and expected impact of the funding on the club and its members.
4. If applicable, provide information about other funding sources or contributions.
5. Finally, confirm your contact and funding information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Club Name** (`club_name`, required): Enter the name of your club.
* **Club Type or Category** (`club_type`, required): Choose from the available club types or categories (e.g. Sports, Academic, Cultural, etc.).
* **Year Club Was Founded** (`founding_year`, optional): Enter the year your club was founded.
* **Club President Name** (`club_president`, required): Enter the name of your club's president.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Amount Requested - USD** (`requested_amount`, required): Enter the total amount of money requested.
* **Purpose of Funding Request** (`funding_purpose`, required): Choose the purpose of your funding request from the available options (e.g. Event or Competition, Equipment Purchase, etc.).
* **Detailed Description of How Funds Will Be Used** (`detailed_purpose`, required): Provide a detailed description of how the funds will be used.
* **Timeline for Fund Usage** (`timeline`, required): Describe when the funds will be needed and spent.
* **Current Club Membership** (`member_count`, required): Enter the number of active members in your club.
* **Expected Impact of This Funding** (`expected_impact`, required): Describe how the funding will benefit the club and its members.
* **Community or Campus Benefit** (`community_benefit`, optional): Describe how the funding will benefit the broader community or campus.
* **Itemized Budget Breakdown** (`budget_items`, required): List major expenses and costs for which the funds will be used.
* **Other Funding Sources or Contributions** (`other_funding_sources`, optional): Describe any other funding sources or contributions.
* **Current Club Fund Balance - USD** (`current_balance`, optional): Enter the approximate balance of funds available in your club.
* **Last Funding Received - Date** (`last_funding_date`, optional): Enter the date when the club last received funds.
* **How Will You Report on Fund Usage?** (`reporting_plan`, required): Describe how you will report on the fund usage.
* **Faculty Advisor Name - Approval** (`advisor_approval`, optional): Enter the name of your faculty advisor who endorses the request.
