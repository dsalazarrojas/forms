# Annual Maintenance Contract Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Maintenance Contract Request Form is a document used to gather information from clients or customers to create an annual maintenance contract. This form will help in understanding their needs, requirements, and constraints related to their equipment, systems, or properties that need maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name and contact information in the **Full Name** field.
2.  Enter your email address in the **Email Address** field.
3.  Provide your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
4.  Enter your company name in the **Company Name** field.
5.  Enter your company address in the **Company Address** field.
6.  Select the **Contract Year** for which you are requesting a maintenance contract.
7.  Choose the **Maintenance Type** required for your equipment, systems, or property.
8.  Enter any **Equipment Details** related to the maintenance needs.
9.  Provide the **Property Location** where the maintenance is needed.
10. Enter the **Square Footage** of the property if relevant.
11. Enter the **Building Age** of the property, if applicable.
12. Select the **Service Frequency** of the maintenance.
13. Choose the **Contract Duration** for which you are requesting a maintenance contract.
14. Indicate whether you already have a **Current Provider** and their name.
15. If applicable, mention any **Previous Issues** with maintenance.
16. Select the **Priority Level** of the maintenance need.
17. Select your expected **Budget Range** for the maintenance.
18. Choose the **Response Time Requirement** for the maintenance.
19. Indicate whether you need **After Hours Service** and select the options accordingly.
20. Enter any **Special Requirements** you have.
21. If necessary, mention how to access the property for maintenance.
22. Select a **Preferred Start Date** for the maintenance contract.
23. Choose your preferred **Payment Terms**.
24. Finally, add any additional comments or information in the **Additional Comments** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: The full name of the person requesting the contract.
* **Email Address**: A contact email for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number**: A contact phone number for communication and urgent issues.
* **Company Name**: The name of the company or organization requesting the contract.
* **Company Address**: The full address of the company or organization.
* **Contract Year**: The year for which the maintenance contract is being requested.
* **Maintenance Type**: The type of maintenance needed for your equipment, systems, or property.
* **Equipment Details**: A description of the equipment or systems that require maintenance.
* **Property Location**: The location of the property where maintenance is needed.
* **Square Footage**: The size of the property, if applicable.
* **Building Age**: The age of the building, if applicable.
* **Service Frequency**: The frequency of the maintenance service required.
* **Contract Duration**: The duration of the maintenance contract.
* **Current Provider**: If you have an existing maintenance provider, select the option accordingly.
* **Previous Issues**: If you have had any issues with previous maintenance, describe them here.
* **Priority Level**: The urgency level of the maintenance need.
* **Budget Range**: The expected budget range for the maintenance.
* **Response Time Requirement**: The time frame in which you need a response for the maintenance.
* **After Hours Service**: Whether you need service outside of business hours.
* **Special Requirements**: Any specific requirements you have for the maintenance.
* **Site Access**: If necessary, describe how to access the property for maintenance.
* **Preferred Start Date**: The preferred start date for the maintenance contract.
* **Payment Terms**: The preferred payment terms for the maintenance contract.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional information or comments about the maintenance contract.
