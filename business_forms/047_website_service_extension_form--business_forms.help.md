# Website Service Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Service Extension Form is designed to gather information from users about their website service extension needs. This form is used to understand users' requirements and to guide our team in providing the best possible service. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the service type that best describes your request.
2. Enter your client's name in the "Client Name" field. This is the name of the person or organization requesting the service.
3. Enter your contact email in the "Email" field. This will be our primary contact point for communication.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone" field. This will help us to reach out to you if needed.
5. If you have any additional services required beyond the standard services, please list them in the "Additional Services" field.
6. In the "Message" field, provide any specific details or instructions about your request.
7. If you have a project deadline, please select the date in the "Project Deadline" field.
8. If you need to attach any file related to your request, please do not forget to upload the file in the "File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the service type that best describes your request. This will help us to assess your needs accurately.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization requesting the service.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email in this field. This will be our primary contact point for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number in this field. This will help us to reach out to you if needed.
* **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, text, optional): If you have any additional services required beyond the standard services, please list them here.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): In this field, provide any specific details or instructions about your request.
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, date, optional): If you have a project deadline, please select the date in this field.
* **File** (`file`, text, optional): If you need to attach any file related to your request, please upload it here.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure that our team understands your needs accurately.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required information is included.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
