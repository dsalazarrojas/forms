This help guide will cover the purpose, how to complete, and a field-by-field explanation of the Investment Performance Report Form.

**Purpose**
The Investment Performance Report Form is designed to collect information from clients about their investment performance. This form will be used to track and analyze the performance of client investments, helping to identify trends and opportunities for growth. This form should only be filled out by clients and submitted for review and analysis.

**How To Complete This Form**
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name and contact information (Client Name, Client Phone, Client Email).
2. Select the investment period (6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or More than 5 years).
3. Choose the performance metric (Return on Investment (ROI), Return on Equity (ROE), or Return on Sales (ROS)).
4. Select the type of investment (Stocks, Bonds, Mutual Funds, or Real Estate).
5. Choose the status of the investment (Open, Closed, or Pending).
6. Enter the date of the investment.
7. Input the investment return and value.
8. Add any additional notes about the investment.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client.
* **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Investment Period**: Select the duration of the investment (e.g., 6-12 months).
* **Performance Metric**: Choose a metric to measure the investment's performance (e.g., Return on Investment (ROI)).
* **Investment Type**: Select the type of investment (e.g., Stocks).
* **Investment Status**: Choose the status of the investment (e.g., Open).
* **Investment Date**: Enter the date of the investment.
* **Investment Return**: Enter the return on the investment (e.g., 10%).
* **Investment Value**: Enter the value of the investment (e.g., $10,000).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the investment.
* **Submitted By**: Choose who submitted the investment report (e.g., User 1).
