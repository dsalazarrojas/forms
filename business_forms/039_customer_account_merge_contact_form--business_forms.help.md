# Customer Account Merge Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Customer Account Merge Contact Form" is designed to help customers merge their accounts with our company. This form collects relevant account and contact information to facilitate the account merging process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Double-check your details before submitting the form to avoid errors.
3. Submit the form, and our team will review and process your account merge request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Account Merge Form Data** (`customer_account_merge_form_data`): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or information relevant to the account merge process.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Enter your up-to-date contact information, including your name, email address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Account Info** (`account_info`): Fill in the required account information, such as your account ID, username, or any other unique identifier associated with your account.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date to avoid delays in the account merge process.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact our support team for assistance.
* You can submit the form multiple times if you need to update your information or if there are any changes in your account details.
