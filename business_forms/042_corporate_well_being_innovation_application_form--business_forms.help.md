# Corporate Well Being Innovation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Well Being Innovation Application Form is designed to collect business-related information for the purpose of assessing and implementing corporate well-being initiatives. This form will be reviewed and evaluated by the relevant authorities to ensure that it aligns with the company's goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for your business:
	* Business Name
	* Business Type
	* Business Website
	* Business Address
	* Business Postal Code
	* Business Country
	* Business Phone
	* Business Email
	* Business Fax
2. Select the relevant team members involved in the project:
	* Project Manager
	* Team Members (Marketing, Sales, Operations)
3. Choose the project goals:
	* Business Growth
	* Financial Gain
	* Employee Engagement
4. Specify the project budget and duration:
	* Project Budget
	* Project Duration
5. Select the project location:
	* USA
	* UK
	* Other
6. Enter the project start and end dates:
	* Project Start Date
	* Project End Date
7. If necessary, provide additional notes or files:
	* Note
	* File Upload

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Information (id: 1)**: Enter your business name, type, website, address, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Information (id: 2)**: Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.
* **Business Description (id: 3)**: Briefly describe your business and its goals.
* **Project Goals (id: 4)**: Select the goals of your project (Business Growth, Financial Gain, Employee Engagement).
* **Project Budget (id: 5)**: Enter the budget for your project.
* **Project Duration (id: 7)**: Specify the duration of your project.
* **Well-Being Initiative (id: 6)**: If you have any well-being initiatives, describe them briefly.
* **Team Members (id: 8)**: Select the teams involved in your project (Marketing, Sales, Operations).
* **Project Manager (id: 9)**: Choose the project manager for your team.
* **Project Location (id: 10)**: Select the location of your project (USA, UK, Other).
* **Project Start Date (id: 11)**: Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date (id: 12)**: Enter the end date of your project.
* **Note (id: 13)**: Provide any additional comments or notes for the project.
* **File Upload (id: 16)**: Upload any relevant files or documents related to your project.
* **Email (id: 14)**: Enter your business email address.
* **Phone (id: 15)**: Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Name (id: 17)**: Enter your business name.
* **Business Type (id: 18)**: Enter your business type.
* **Business Website (id: 19)**: Enter your business website.
* **Business Address (id: 20)**: Enter your business address.
* **Business Postal Code (id: 21)**: Enter your business postal code.
* **Business Country (id: 22)**: Enter your business country.
* **Business Phone (id: 23)**: Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Email (id: 24)**: Enter your business email address.
* **Business Fax (id: 25)**: Enter your business fax number.
