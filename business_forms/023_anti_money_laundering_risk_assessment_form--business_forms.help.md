# Anti Money Laundering Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anti Money Laundering Risk Assessment Form is used to identify and assess potential money laundering risks associated with a business or account.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of several sections, each with its own purpose. Please complete the form as follows:
1. In the "Entity name" field, enter the name of the business or account being assessed.
2. In the "Assessment date" field, select the date on which the risk evaluation is being performed.
3. In the "Customer risk factors" field, select all the risk factors that apply to the customer. This includes:
	* Politically exposed person: If the customer is a politically exposed person (PEP), select this option.
	* High risk jurisdiction: If the customer operates in a high-risk jurisdiction, select this option.
	* Unusual transaction patterns: If the customer exhibits unusual transaction patterns, select this option.
	* Complex ownership: If the customer has complex ownership structures, select this option.
	* None: If none of the above options apply, select this option.
4. In the "Product or service risk" field, select the level of risk associated with the products or services used by the customer.
	* Low: Low risk
	* Medium: Medium risk
	* High: High risk
5. In the "Transaction volume and velocity risk" field, select the level of risk associated with the customer's transaction volume and velocity.
	* Low: Low risk
	* Medium: Medium risk
	* High: High risk
6. In the "Overall risk rating" field, select the composite risk rating based on the factors above.
	* Low: Low risk
	* Moderate: Moderate risk
	* High: High risk
	* Critical: Critical risk
7. In the "Mitigation measures" field, enter any controls or measures that can be implemented to reduce identified risks.
8. In the "Reviewer name" field, enter the name of the person completing the risk assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entity name** (required): Enter the name of the business or account being assessed.
* **Assessment date** (required): Select the date on which the risk evaluation is being performed.
* **Customer risk factors** (optional): Select all the risk factors that apply to the customer.
* **Product or service risk** (required): Select the level of risk associated with the products or services used by the customer.
* **Transaction volume and velocity risk** (required): Select the level of risk associated with the customer's transaction volume and velocity.
* **Overall risk rating** (required): Select the composite risk rating based on the factors above.
* **Mitigation measures** (optional): Enter any controls or measures that can be implemented to reduce identified risks.
* **Reviewer name** (required): Enter the name of the person completing the risk assessment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that you are assessing the correct level of risk.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with a colleague or a supervisor before completing the form.
* This form is intended for internal use only and should not be shared with external parties without permission.
