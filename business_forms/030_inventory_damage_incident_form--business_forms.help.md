# Inventory Damage Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Damage Incident Form is used to report and document incidents of damage to inventory items. This form helps to capture essential information about the incident, including details about the item affected, the nature of the damage, and the circumstances of the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Incident Details" field with a brief description of the incident, including the inventory item affected and any relevant details.
2. In the "Damage Description" field, describe the nature and extent of the damage to the inventory item.
3. Select the applicable "Damage Category" from the dropdown menu to classify the type of damage.
4. Enter the "Date Incident Occurred" field with the date the incident took place.
5. Enter the "Time Incident Occurred" field with the time the incident took place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details**: Enter a brief description of the incident, including the inventory item affected and any relevant details.
* **Damage Description**: Describe the nature and extent of the damage to the inventory item.
* **Damage Category**: Select the category of damage that best matches the incident. This helps to track and report incidents more efficiently.
* **Date Incident Occurred**: Enter the date the incident took place in MM/DD/YY format (month/day/year).
* **Time Incident Occurred**: Enter the time the incident took place in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

## Tips
* Make sure to be as specific as possible when describing the incident and damage.
* If multiple items were affected, you can enter multiple incidents on separate forms.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or manager for assistance.
