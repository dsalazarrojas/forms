# Nightly Cashup Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a daily financial report used to record staff attendance, cash handling, and bank balance updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting to fill out the form.
2. Fill in the following fields:
	* Daily cashup
	* Staff sick: Select "Yes" if any staff members are absent due to sickness or injury.
	* Staff incident: Select "Yes" if any staff members have been involved in an incident.
	* Cashup: Enter the total cash balance at the end of the day.
	* Tips: Enter the total tips collected from customers.
	* Service charge: Enter any service charge applied to customer transactions.
	* Tax: Enter any tax collected from customers.
	* Total: Enter the total sales or revenue for the day.
	* Discount: Enter any discounts applied to customer transactions.
	* Net: Enter the net sales or revenue after discount.
	* Service report: Select "Yes" if any services were provided to customers.
	* Cash out: Enter the amount of cash handed out to staff or vendors.
	* Change: Enter the amount of change handed out to staff or vendors.
	* Bank fund: Enter the current bank balance.
	* Tips collected: Enter the total tips collected from customers.
	* Staff sick reason: Enter the reason for staff member's absence due to sickness or injury.
	* Staff sick date: Enter the date of staff member's absence due to sickness or injury.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily cashup** (`daily_cashup`, text, required): Enter the total cash balance at the end of the day.
* **Staff sick** (`staff_sick`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if any staff members are absent due to sickness or injury.
* **Staff incident** (`staff_incident`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if any staff members have been involved in an incident.
* **Cashup** (`cashup`, number, required): Enter the total cash balance at the end of the day.
* **Tips** (`tips`, number, required): Enter the total tips collected from customers.
* **Service charge** (`service_charge`, number, required): Enter any service charge applied to customer transactions.
* **Tax** (`tax`, number, required): Enter any tax collected from customers.
* **Total** (`total`, number, required): Enter the total sales or revenue for the day.
* **Discount** (`discount`, number, required): Enter any discounts applied to customer transactions.
* **Net** (`net`, number, required): Enter the net sales or revenue after discount.
* **Service report** (`servicereport`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if any services were provided to customers.
* **Cash out** (`cash_out`, number, required): Enter the amount of cash handed out to staff or vendors.
* **Change** (`change`, number, required): Enter the amount of change handed out to staff or vendors.
* **Bank fund** (`bankfund`, number, required): Enter the current bank balance.
* **Tips collected** (`tips_collected`, number, required): Enter the total tips collected from customers.
* **Staff sick reason** (`staff_sick_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for staff member's absence due to sickness or injury.
* **Staff sick date** (`staff_sick_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of staff member's absence due to sickness or injury.
