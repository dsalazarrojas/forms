# Retail Space Leasing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Space Leasing Request Form is designed to collect essential information from potential lessees to facilitate the leasing process for retail space within our properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are authorized to represent the company or property in the leasing process.
2. Review the form carefully to avoid any duplicate submissions.
3. Fill in the required details accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Retail Space Leasing Request Form
* **Retail Space Leasing Request Form** (`page_1`, text, not required): This is the header section of the form, and you can leave it blank if not necessary.
* **Retail Space Representative** (`page_2`, text, not required): Please enter the name and title of the representative who will be dealing with us.
* **Contact Number** (`page_3`, text, not required): Enter your contact number for any follow-up communication.
* **Contact Email** (`page_4`, text, not required): Enter your email address for any follow-up communication.
* **Budget** (`page_5`, number, not required): Specify your estimated budget for leasing, if any.
* **Rent Range** (`page_6`, number, not required): Enter the desired rent range for the leased space.
* **Description** (`page_7`, text, not required): Briefly describe your retail business and what type of space you are looking for.
* **Space Requirements** (`page_8`, text, not required): Specify any specific space requirements or needs you have.

## Tips
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- We appreciate your prompt completion of this form to facilitate a smooth leasing process.
