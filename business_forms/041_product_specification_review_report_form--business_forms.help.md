# Product Specification Review Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Specification Review Report Form is used to document the results of a product specification review and compliance check. It ensures that all product specifications and compliance status are accurately recorded and reported.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the results of your product specification review and compliance check.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and concisely.
3. Review the form thoroughly before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. product_specification_review_form
* **Product Specification Review Form** (text, optional): This field is where you can enter any relevant comments or observations about the product specification review.

### 2. product_compliance_status
* **Product Compliance Status** (select_one, optional): This field is used to indicate the compliance status of the product. Select 'True' if the product is compliant, 'False' if it is not, or 'Partially Met' if it partially meets the compliance requirements.

### 3. product_issues_recorded
* **Product Issues Recorded** (text, optional): This field is used to record any issues or problems that were encountered during the product specification review.

### 4. product_improvement_recommendations
* **Product Improvement Recommendations** (text, optional): This field is used to record any improvement recommendations for the product based on the review results.

### 5. product_compliance_comments
* **Product Compliance Comments** (text, optional): This field is used to provide any additional comments or explanations about the product's compliance status.

### 6. date
* **Date** (date, optional): This field is used to record the date of the product specification review.

### 7. engineer_comments
* **Engineer Comments** (text, optional): This field is used to record any comments or observations from the product engineer.

### 8. qa_comments
* **QA Comments** (text, optional): This field is used to record any comments or observations from the QA team.

### 9. product_manager_comments
* **Product Manager Comments** (text, optional): This field is used to record any comments or observations from the product manager.
