# Business Valuation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Business Valuation Form is designed to collect essential information about your business to help determine its value. This form is used to gather data on your business operations, finances, and performance. It's crucial to provide accurate and complete information to ensure the most accurate valuation results.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out all required fields marked as required.
2.  Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3.  Double-check your answers for accuracy and consistency.
4.  If unsure about any field, consult our business valuation experts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Business Name** (`business_name`): Enter the full name of your business.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Valuation Date** (`valuation_date`): Enter the date of this valuation.
    *   Type: `date`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Purpose of Valuation** (`valuation_purpose`): Select the reason for this valuation:
    *   Options: Financing, Acquisition, Sale Planning, Litigation, Partnership Dissolution, Insurance, Tax Assessment, Other
    *   Type: `select_one`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Business Owner or Contact** (`business_owner`): Enter the full name of the business owner or contact person.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the business owner or contact person.
    *   Type: `email`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the business owner or contact person.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Type of Business** (`business_type`): Enter the business sector or industry.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Years in Operation** (`years_in_operation`): Enter the number of years the business has been in operation.
    *   Type: `number`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`): Enter the current workforce size.
    *   Type: `number`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`): Enter the most recent annual revenue.
    *   Type: `number`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Annual Operating Expenses** (`annual_expenses`): Enter the total annual expenses.
    *   Type: `number`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Annual Net Income** (`net_income`): Enter the profit after expenses.
    *   Type: `number`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Total Assets** (`total_assets`): Enter the value of all assets.
    *   Type: `number`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Total Liabilities** (`total_liabilities`): Enter the total debts and obligations.
    *   Type: `number`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Owner Equity** (`equity_value`): [Optional] Enter the value of assets minus liabilities (if applicable).
    *   Type: `number`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **Number of Active Customers** (`customer_base`): [Optional] Enter the number of customers served.
    *   Type: `number`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **Market Position** (`market_position`): Select the competitive standing:
    *   Options: Market Leader, Strong Competitor, Established Player, Emerging Business, Niche Player
    *   Type: `select_one`
    *   Required: `true`
*   **Average Annual Growth Rate** (`growth_rate`): [Optional] Enter the percentage growth over past 3 years.
    *   Type: `number`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **Key Business Risks** (`key_risks`): [Optional] Enter the main risks facing the business.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **Additional Notes** (`valuation_notes`): [Optional] Enter any relevant information for valuation.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `false`

## Tips

*   Double-check your answers for accuracy and consistency.
*   If unsure about any field, consult our business valuation experts.
*   Provide accurate and up-to-date information for best valuation results.
*   Fill out all required fields to ensure accurate valuation.
