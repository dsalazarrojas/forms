# Content Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Content Audit Form is a tool used to evaluate the performance and alignment of content assets with marketing strategies. It helps in identifying areas of improvement and providing recommendations for updates to enhance the asset's effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the content asset for which you want to perform the audit.
2. Fill out the required fields for the selected asset, providing accurate information about its title, identification number, type, and URL (if applicable).
3. Evaluate the asset's alignment with marketing strategies and performance using the provided scales (e.g., 1-5).
4. Choose the target audience and distribution channels for the asset.
5. Provide recommendations for updates, if necessary.
6. Confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
7. Submit the audit with the required date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Content Asset Title** (`asset_title`, `text`, required)
Enter a brief and accurate title of the content asset.

### 2. **Content Asset Identification Number** (`asset_id`, `text`, required)
Enter the unique identification number for the content asset.

### 3. **Content Asset Type** (`asset_type`, `select_one`, required)
Select the type of content asset: Blog Post, White Paper, Case Study, Video, Infographic, Podcast, Webinar, E-book, or Other.

### 4. **Content Asset URL (if applicable)** (`asset_url`, `text`, optional)
Enter the URL of the content asset, if it has one.

### 5. **Alignment with Marketing Strategies** (`alignment_marketing_strategies`, `select_one`, required)
Evaluate the asset's alignment with marketing strategies on a scale of 1-5.

### 6. **Target Audience Engagement Rating** (`target_audience_engagement`, `select_one`, required)
Rate the engagement of the target audience with the asset on a scale of 1-5.

### 7. **Conversion Rate Rating** (`conversion_rate_rating`, `select_one`, required)
Rate the conversion rate of the asset on a scale of 1-5.

### 8. **Primary Target Audience** (`primary_target_audience`, `text`, required)
Enter a brief description of the primary target audience persona.

### 9. **Content Distribution Channels** (`distribution_channels`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all applicable distribution channels for the asset: Social Media, Email, Website, SEO, Paid Search, Referral, Other.

### 10. **Update Priorities and Recommendations** (`update_priorities`, `note`, optional)
Add any update recommendations, if necessary.

### 11. **Update Priority Level** (`update_priority_level`, `select_one`, required)
Set the update priority level: Low, Medium, High, or Critical.

### 12. **Specific Recommendations for Updates** (`update_recommendations`, `text`, required)
Enter specific recommendations for updates.

### 13. **Confirmation** (`audit_completed`, `select_one`, required)
Confirm that the audit has been completed.

### 14. **Any additional details or comments?** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional)
Add any additional comments or details about the audit.

### 15. **I confirm that the information provided is accurate** (`signature_confirmation`, `select_one`, required)
Confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

### 16. **Date of Audit Submission** (`audit_submission_date`, `date`, required)
Enter the date of the audit submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a thorough audit.
* Use the provided scales to evaluate the asset's alignment and performance.
* Provide specific and actionable recommendations for updates.
* Confirm the accuracy of the information provided to ensure a reliable audit.
* Submit the audit with the required date.
