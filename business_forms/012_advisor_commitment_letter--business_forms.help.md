# Advisor Commitment Letter - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Advisor Commitment Letter" is a formal agreement between the advisor and the project team, outlining the terms and expectations of the advisor's involvement. By completing this form, you will provide essential information that helps the project team understand your role, responsibilities, and commitment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill in your details in each section according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Advisor Details --**: This section captures your basic information. Please fill in the following fields:
	+ **Advisor Name**: Enter your full name as you want it to be displayed on the commitment letter.
	+ **Current Organization / Affiliation**: Type your current organization or affiliation (e.g., company name, university name, etc.).
	+ **Email Address**: Enter your email address where the project team can reach you.
	+ **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where the project team can contact you for any urgent matters.
* **Advisory Role Title**: Please choose the advisory role title from the options provided that aligns with your position and responsibilities.
* **Commitment Term**: Select the length of time you are committing to serve as an advisor. This can be 1, 2, 3 years or for the duration of the project.
* **Responsibilities Acknowledgment**: Select all the responsibilities that apply to your advisory role, such as attending meetings, providing strategic guidance, mentoring junior members, participating in annual review, and maintaining confidentiality.
* **Conflict of Interest Declaration**: Declare whether you have any known conflicts of interest. If yes, provide a detailed explanation in the "Conflict Details" field.
* **Conflict Details (if yes)**: If you have conflicts of interest, please provide a detailed explanation in this field.
* **Statement of Commitment**: Choose a statement that reflects your commitment to serving as an advisor to the best of your ability.
* **Date**: This is the date on which you submit this commitment letter.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields marked as required are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have conflicts of interest, please provide a detailed explanation to ensure transparency.
