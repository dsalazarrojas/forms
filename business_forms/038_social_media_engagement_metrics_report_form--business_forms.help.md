# Social Media Engagement Metrics Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on social media engagement metrics across various platforms. The purpose of this form is to provide a structured way to gather information on how your social media engagement metrics are performing, allowing you to track and analyze your online presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the social media platforms you'd like to track (e.g. Facebook, Instagram, Twitter).
2. Choose the engagement metrics you'd like to measure (e.g. likes, shares, comments).
3. Choose the time range for which you'd like to collect data.
4. Select the output format for the report (e.g. CSV, Excel, JSON).
5. If desired, enter your email address and preferred email notification frequency and time.
6. Finally, select the report frequency (e.g. daily, weekly, monthly).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social Media Platforms**: 
  - **(Select multiple)**
    Choose the social media platforms you'd like to track engagement metrics for.
* **Engagement Metrics:**
  - **(Select one)**
    Select the engagement metric you'd like to measure (likes, shares, comments, etc.).
* **Time Range:**
  - **(Date)**
    Enter the start and end dates for which you'd like to collect data.
* **Form Output:**
  - **(Select one)**
    Choose the format for your report (e.g. CSV, Excel, JSON).
* **Email Notification:**
  - **(Email)**
    Enter your email address to receive notifications when new reports are available.
* **Phone Number:**
  - **(Text)**
    Enter a valid phone number to receive notifications (not currently used).
* **Report Frequency:**
  - **(Select one)**
    Choose how often you'd like to receive reports (daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Email Notification Frequency:**
  - **(Select one)**
    Choose how often you'd like to receive email notifications (daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Email Notification Time:**
  - **(Time)**
    Enter the time of day you'd like to receive email notifications.
* **Report Frequency Details:**
  - **(Select multiple)**
    Choose specific days or times you'd like to receive reports (e.g. every Monday at 9:00 AM).
