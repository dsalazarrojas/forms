# personnel_activity_report_form_for_federal_grants - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report personnel activities related to federal grants. It helps ensure that your organization receives correct funding and reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct category for this report.
2. Fill in the required information for the grant, including grant number, start date, end date, grant administrator's name, and effort type.
3. Enter the hours worked, rate per hour, and total cost for the personnel involved.
4. Select the personnel affected by the grant.
5. Verify that the information is accurate before submitting the form.
