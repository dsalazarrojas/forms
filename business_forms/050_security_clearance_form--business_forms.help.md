# Security Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Clearance Form is used to collect information required for security clearance purposes. This form ensures that employees are properly vetted and cleared before being granted access to sensitive or classified information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the full name, date of birth, and social security number fields, as these are required for verification purposes.
2. Enter your address, contact information, and emergency contact information, if applicable.
3. Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
4. Choose the type of security clearance required for your role.
5. Enter the clearance date and expiration date.
6. Provide a reason for your clearance.
7. If applicable, enter the date of your background check and the status of your background check.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes about your clearance.
9. Sign and date the form, and sign it with your name and employee ID.
10. Enter your department, job title, and reporting manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, required): Enter your social security number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your mailing address.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact, if applicable.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, required): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g., Spouse, Parent, Child, Sibling, Other).
* **Clearance Type** (`clearance_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of security clearance required for your role (e.g., Top Secret, Secret, Confidential).
* **Clearance Level** (`clearance_level`, number, optional): Enter the level of security clearance you are requesting (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **Clearance Date** (`clearance_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are requesting security clearance.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the date your security clearance will expire.
* **Clearance Reason** (`clearance_reason`, text, required): Enter a reason for your security clearance request.
* **Background Check Date** (`background_check_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your background check.
* **Background Status** (`background_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your background check (e.g., Pass, Denied, Pending).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your clearance.
* **Background Clearance Agreement** (`background_clearance_agreement`, text, required): Sign the background clearance agreement.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date and sign this form.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, required): Sign this form with your name and employee ID.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, text, required): Enter your reporting manager.
* **Security Clearance Level** (`security_clearance_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of security clearance required for your role (e.g., Top Secret, Secret, Confidential).
* **Clearance Category** (`clearance_category`, select_one, required): Choose the category of security clearance required for your role (e.g., Business, Government, Other).
* **Clearance Expiration Date** (`clearance_expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the date your security clearance will expire.
