# New Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Enquiry Form is used for users to submit a new enquiry to the system. It is essential to provide accurate contact information and details about the enquiry to ensure proper tracking and registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your contact name in the "contact name" field.
* Provide your email address in the "email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
* Enter your contact address in the "contact address" field.
* Describe the details of your enquiry in the "enquiry details" field.
* Specify the next steps for the assigned team in the "next steps" field.
* Check if follow-up steps are required and select the "follow-up steps" options accordingly.
* Choose to whom the enquiry should be assigned in the "assigned to" field.
* Select the tool(s) that should be assigned to the enquiry in the "assigned tool" field.
* Set the date and time for assignment of the task in the "assigned date" and "assigned time" fields (if applicable).
* Specify the follow-up date and time in the "follow-up date" and "follow-up time" fields (if applicable).
* Optionally, select "Yes" or "No" for follow-up steps and assignment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact name**: The field for entering your contact name.
* **contact email**: The field for entering your email address.
* **phone**: The field for entering your phone number.
* **contact address**: The field for entering your contact address.
* **enquiry details**: The field for describing the details of your enquiry.
* **next steps**: The field for specifying the next steps for the assigned team.
* **follow-up steps**: The field for selecting if follow-up steps are required. Choose "Yes" or "No" for this option.
* **assigned to**: The field for choosing to whom the enquiry should be assigned. Select "Yes" or "No" for this option.
* **assigned tool**: The field for selecting the tool(s) that should be assigned to the enquiry. Select "Yes" or "No" for this option.
* **assigned date**: The field for setting the date for assignment of the task.
* **assigned time**: The field for setting the time for assignment of the task.
* **follow-up date**: The field for setting the follow-up date.
* **follow-up time**: The field for setting the follow-up time.
* **assigned tool**: The field for selecting the tool(s) assigned to the enquiry.
* **assigned to**: The field for choosing to whom the enquiry should be assigned.
