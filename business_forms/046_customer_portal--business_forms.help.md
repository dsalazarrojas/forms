# customer_portal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer portal form is designed to collect information from customers through a secure and user-friendly interface. It is intended for general use and does not require specific business logic or complex conditional fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields and select the relevant options for each field.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled out before submission.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_portal_form_1** (customer_portal_form_1_label, text, not required): This is a text field where you can enter any information related to your customer interaction.
* **customer_portal_form_2** (customer_portal_form_2_label, number, not required): This is a numerical field where you can enter a number related to your customer interaction.
* **customer_portal_form_3** (customer_portal_form_3_label, number, not required): This is another numerical field where you can enter a number related to your customer interaction.
* **customer_portal_form_4** (customer_portal_form_4_label, select_one, not required): This is a select-one field where you can choose between two options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **customer_portal_form_5** (customer_portal_form_5_label, select_multiple, not required): This is a select-multiple field where you can choose between two options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **customer_portal_form_6** (customer_portal_form_6_label, date, not required): This is a date field where you can enter a date related to your customer interaction.
* **customer_portal_form_7** (customer_portal_form_7_label, time, not required): This is a time field where you can enter a time related to your customer interaction.
* **customer_portal_form_8** (customer_portal_form_8_label, note, not required): This is a free-form text field where you can enter any additional information related to your customer interaction.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about a particular field, contact the relevant personnel for clarification.
* The form is not encrypted, so please do not enter sensitive or confidential information.
