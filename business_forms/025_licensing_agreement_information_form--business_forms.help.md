# licensing_agreement_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Licensing Agreement Information Form" is used to collect and store essential details about licensing agreements. This form helps to ensure that all necessary information is documented correctly and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Agreement Title" field with the title of the licensing agreement.
2. Provide the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Enter the date the agreement was signed in the "Date Signed" field.
4. Input the effective date of the agreement in the "Date Effective" field.
5. Describe the contract terms in the "Contract Terms" field.
6. Enter the length of the contract in the "Contract Length" field.
7. If applicable, fill out the "Renewal Date" field with the date the contract is set to be renewed.
8. Input the term for which the contract is to be renewed in the "Renewal Term" field.
9. Select the type of license from the drop-down options (License, Non-Exclusive License, or Non-Exclusive License with Revenue Sharing).
10. Enter the royalty rate in the "Royalty Rate" field.
11. Input the license fee in the "License Fee" field.
12. Choose from the payment terms options (On a monthly basis, On a quarterly basis, On a semi-annual basis, On an annual basis, or Other (specify)).
13. Provide details about the payment terms in the "Payment Term Details" field.
14. Input the frequency of payments in the "Payment Term Frequency" field.
15. Select the renewal option from the drop-down options (To renew indefinitely, To renew at the option of either party, To renew for [X] months/years, or To renew for [X] months/years and [Y] months/years).
16. Enter details about the renewal option in the "Renewal Option Details" field.
17. Describe the termination clause in the "Termination Clause" field.
18. Input the notice requirement in the "Notice Requirement" field.
19. Enter the notice period in the "Notice Period" field.
20. Input the notice fee in the "Notice Fee" field.
21. Input the termination fee in the "Termination Fee" field.
22. Select the agreement version from the drop-down options (Original, Revised, or Updated).
23. Enter the date of the agreement in the "Agreement Date" field.
24. Provide details about the agreement version in the "Agreement Version Details" field.
25. Input the license expiration date in the "License Expiration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Title** (1, text, required: false): Enter a brief title for the licensing agreement.
* **Client Name** (2, text, required: false): Provide the name of the client involved in the licensing agreement.
* **Date Signed** (3, date, required: false): Enter the date the licensing agreement was signed.
* **Date Effective** (4, date, required: false): Enter the effective date of the licensing agreement.
* **Contract Terms** (5, text, required: false): Describe the terms of the contract in detail.
* **Contract Length** (6, number, required: false): Enter the length of the contract in months or years.
* **Renewal Date** (7, date, required: false): If applicable, enter the date the contract is set to be renewed.
* **Renewal Term** (8, number, required: false): Input the term for which the contract is to be renewed.
* **License Type** (9, select_one, required: false): Select the type of license from the drop-down options (License, Non-Exclusive License, or Non-Exclusive License with Revenue Sharing).
* **Royalty Rate** (10, number, required: false): Enter the royalty rate for the licensing agreement.
* **License Fee** (11, number, required: false): Input the license fee for the agreement.
* **Payment Terms** (12, select_multiple, required: false): Choose from the payment terms options (On a monthly basis, On a quarterly basis, On a semi-annual basis, On an annual basis, or Other (specify)).
* **Payment Term Details** (13, text, required: false): Provide details about the payment terms.
* **Payment Term Frequency** (14, text, required: false): Input the frequency of payments.
* **Renewal Option** (15, select_multiple, required: false): Select the renewal option from the drop-down options (To renew indefinitely, To renew at the option of either party, To renew for [X] months/years, or To renew for [X] months/years and [Y] months/years).
* **Renewal Option Details** (16, text, required: false): Enter details about the renewal option.
* **Termination Clause** (17, text, required: false): Describe the termination clause in the agreement.
* **Notice Requirement** (18, number, required: false): Enter the notice requirement for termination.
* **Notice Period** (19, number, required: false): Input the notice period for termination.
* **Notice Fee** (20, number, required: false): Input the notice fee for termination.
* **Termination Fee** (21, number, required: false): Input the termination fee.
* **Agreement Version** (22, select_multiple, required: false): Select the agreement version from the drop-down options (Original, Revised, or Updated).
* **Agreement Date** (23, date, required: false): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Version Details** (24, text, required: false): Provide details about the agreement version.
* **License Expiration** (25, date, required: false): Input the license expiration date.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check the form for any errors or missing information before submission.
* Review and understand all the terms and conditions of the licensing agreement before proceeding.
* If any field is unclear, refer to the relevant section of the agreement for further information.
* Save the completed form for future reference and record-keeping purposes.
