# Offer Building Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Offer Building Form is a tool for creating and managing offers for businesses. This form is used to gather essential details about the offer, including its type, name, email, phone number, description, start and end dates and times, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the offer type (e.g., Offer A, Offer B, or Offer C).
2. Fill in the business name and email address.
3. Enter the phone number associated with the business.
4. Provide a short description of the offer.
5. Set the start date and time of the offer.
6. Set the end date and time of the offer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Offer Type** (`offer_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of offer you're creating (e.g., Offer A, Offer B, or Offer C).
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the business offering the product or service.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the business.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number associated with the business.
* **Description** (`description`, note, required): Provide a brief description of the offer (e.g., 1000 characters max).
* **Date Start** (`date_start`, date, required): Enter the start date of the offer.
* **Time Start** (`time_start`, time, required): Enter the start time of the offer.
* **Date End** (`date_end`, date, required): Enter the end date of the offer.
* **Time End** (`time_end`, time, required): Enter the end time of the offer.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
- Double-check your work before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your business administrator.
