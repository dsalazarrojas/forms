# Customer Advocacy Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the customer advocacy team at your company understand your role, goals, and areas of interest for the advocacy program. It will help us tailor our support to best fit your needs. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to get the most out of the advocacy program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely with your correct name, email, and other information.
- Be as specific as possible when answering questions about your role, goals, and areas of interest.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (Required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name here. (If not provided, we will use the first name for identification.)
* **Email (3)** (Required): Enter your business email address here.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your business phone number here.
* **Company (5)** (Required): Enter your company name here.
* **Role (6)** (Required): Select your role within your company from the list. Options: Team Member, Executive, Manager.
* **Department (7)** (Required): Select your department from the list. Options: Sales, Marketing, HR.
* **Company Size (8)**: Select your company size from the list. Options: 1-10 employees, 11-50 employees, 51-100 employees, 101-200 employees, 201-500 employees, 501-1000 employees, 1001+ employees.
* **Job Title (9)** (Required): Enter your job title here.
* **Job Function (10)**: Select your job function from the list. Options: Sales, Marketing, HR.
* **Manager Name (11)**: Enter your manager's name here.
* **Manager Phone (12)**: Enter your manager's phone number here.
* **Manager Email (13)**: Enter your manager's email address here.
* **Team Members (14)**: List other team members who may be interested in the advocacy program.
* **Team Manager (15)**: Enter your team manager's name here.
* **Advocacy Goals (16)** (Required): Describe your goals and objectives for participating in the advocacy program.
* **Expected Outcome (17)** (Required): Describe what you hope to achieve through the advocacy program.
* **Preferred Communication Channels (18)**: Select which communication channels you prefer. Options: Email, Phone, In-person, Video Call.
* **Primary Advocacy Area (19)** (Required): Select your primary area of interest for the advocacy program. Options: Online, Offline.
* **Secondary Advocacy Area (20)**: Select your secondary area of interest for the advocacy program. Options: Online, Offline.
* **Tertiary Advocacy Area (21)**: Select your tertiary area of interest for the advocacy program. Options: Online, Offline.
* **Quaternary Advocacy Area (22)**: Select your quaternary area of interest for the advocacy program. Options: Online, Offline.
* **Quinary Advocacy Area (23)**: Select your quinary area of interest for the advocacy program. Options: Online, Offline.
* **Senary Advocacy Area (24)**: Select your senary area of interest for the advocacy program. Options: Online, Offline.
* **Septenary Advocacy Area (25)**: Select your septenary area of interest for the advocacy program. Options: Online, Offline.

Note: This guide is based on the provided YAML form fields.
