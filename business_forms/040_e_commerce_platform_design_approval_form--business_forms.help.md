# e-commerce_platform_design_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e-commerce platform design approval form is designed to facilitate the approval process for e-commerce platform designs. It ensures that the design meets the requirements and expectations of the stakeholders and provides a clear and transparent process for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the category of the e-commerce platform design (e.g., Business Forms, Online Store, E-commerce).
2. Provide a brief description of the design.
3. Attach any relevant design files.
4. Provide any additional feedback or comments about the design.
5. Select the reviewers for the design (e.g., Manager, Team Lead, Developer, Designer).
6. Enter the reviewers' emails.
7. Enter the reviewers' phone numbers.
8. Add any review comments.
9. Select the approval status of the design (e.g., Approved, Rejected, Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **E Commerce Platform Design** (`e_commerce_platform_design`, text, required): Describe the e-commerce platform design in a few sentences.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the e-commerce platform design (e.g., Business Forms, Online Store, E-commerce).
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the design.
* **Design Files** (`design_files`, text, optional): Attach any relevant design files for the reviewer to review.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the design.
* **Review Deadline** (`review_deadline`, date, optional): Enter the deadline for the review of the design.
* **Reviewers** (`reviewers`, select_multiple, optional): Select the reviewers for the design (e.g., Manager, Team Lead, Developer, Designer).
* **Reviewers Emails** (`reviewers_emails`, email, optional): Enter the emails of the selected reviewers.
* **Reviewers Phone** (`reviewers_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone numbers of the selected reviewers.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, optional): Add any review comments.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, optional): Select the approval status of the design (e.g., Approved, Rejected, Pending).
