# Sellers Product Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on a seller's product from an evaluator. Your input will help the seller to understand the strengths and weaknesses of their product and make informed decisions to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that you are evaluating a single product at a time.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If a field is not required, feel free to skip it if you don't have the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seller Name** (`seller_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the seller who created or sold the product.
* **Product Id** (`product_id`, number, required: false): Enter the ID number of the product you are evaluating. If you don't have a product ID, you can leave this field blank.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the product you are evaluating.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the product or your experience with it.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required: false): Evaluate the product's overall quality using the rating options (Excellent, Good, Poor, Bad, Very Bad).
* **Review** (`review`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts and feedback on the product's performance, usability, and any areas for improvement.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required: false): Enter the date you submitted this evaluation (optional).
