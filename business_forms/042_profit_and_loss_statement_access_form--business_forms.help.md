## Purpose
This form is used to collect business financial data for various purposes, such as auditing, accounting, or financial analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the business or company.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the financial data starts to be recorded.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the financial data ends to be recorded.
*   **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of business, such as small, medium, or large.
*   **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, number, optional): Enter the total annual income of the business.
*   **Annual Expenses** (`annual_expenses`, number, optional): Enter the total annual expenses of the business.
*   **Net Profit** (`net_profit`, number, optional): This field is calculated automatically by subtracting the annual expenses from the annual income.
