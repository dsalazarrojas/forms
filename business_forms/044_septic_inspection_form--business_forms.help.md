# Septic Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Septic Inspection Form is designed to collect information about septic systems, including their type, inspection date, and results. This form will help inspectors and maintenance teams track septic system inspections, monitor their status, and provide insights for future planning and maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Septic Inspection Form, follow these steps:

* Enter the following required fields:
	+ **Septic System Type**: Select the type of septic system (Residential, Commercial, or Institutional) associated with the inspection.
	+ **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the septic inspection.
	+ **Inspection Result**: Choose the outcome of the inspection (Pass or Fail) and enter any additional comments if necessary.
	+ **Inspection Frequency**: Select how often the inspection is done (Every 1 year, Every 2 years, Every 3 years, Every 4 years, or Never).
	+ **Inspection Cost**: Enter the cost of the inspection, if applicable.
* Enter optional fields as needed, including:
	+ **Inspector's Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
	+ **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
	+ **Previous Inspection Result**: Enter the result of the previous inspection (if applicable).
	+ **Previous Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the previous inspection (if applicable).
	+ **Previous Inspection Cost**: Enter the cost of the previous inspection (if applicable).
	+ **Next Inspection Frequency**: Select the frequency of the next inspection (if applicable).
	+ **Next Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the next inspection (if applicable).
	+ **Next Inspection Result Comment**: Add any comments about the next inspection result (if applicable).
