# retail_store_layout_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the retail store layout and preferences to help store managers and customers understand and discuss the store's characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form one page at a time, starting with **store_layout_preferences**.
2. Answer each question carefully, and if you need clarification, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **store_layout_preferences** (`retail_store_layout_inquiry_form_page_1`, text, required: false)
This field is where you can briefly describe your store's layout preferences.

### 2. **manager_details** (`manager_details`, select_one, required: false)
Choose the manager you are working with.

### 3. **store_characteristics** (`store_characteristics`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the characteristics that apply to your store, such as its width and height.

### 4. **layout_preferences** (`layout_preferences`, select_one, required: false)
Choose your preferred layout.

### 5. **store_floor_plan** (`store_floor_plan`, note, required: false)
This field is a note field, which means you can type any relevant information about the store's floor plan.

### 6. **store_space_characteristics** (`store_space_characteristics`, number, required: false)
Enter the store's space characteristics, if applicable.

### 7. **customer_traffic_flow** (`customer_traffic_flow`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select how you would like customers to flow through your store.

### 8. **store_characteristics** (`store_characteristics`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select additional characteristics about your store, such as width and height.

### 9. **floor_plan_options** (`floor_plan_options`, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose from the available floor plans.

### 10. **manager_layout** (`manager_layout`, date, required: false)
Enter the manager's layout preferences if applicable.

### 11. **store_hours** (`store_hours`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the hours of operation for your store.

### 12. **customer_experience** (`customer_experience`, text, required: false)
Provide your feedback on customer experience.

### 13. **manager_comments** (`manager_comments`, text, required: false)
Type any comments from the manager.

### 14. **customer_comments** (`customer_comments`, text, required: false)
Type any comments from the customer.

### 15. **manager_suggestions** (`manager_suggestions`, text, required: false)
Provide any suggestions from the manager.
