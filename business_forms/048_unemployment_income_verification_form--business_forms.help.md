# Unemployment Income Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify an applicant's unemployment status or income for benefits or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Select your current employment status.
5. Enter your employer's name (if applicable).
6. Enter your employer's address (if applicable).
7. Enter the date you started your last job.
8. Enter the date you last worked.
9. Select the reason you left your last job.
10. Enter the last day you worked.
11. Select the type of unemployment benefit you are claiming.
12. Attach proof of income (e.g., paystub, letter from employer).
13. Attach any supporting documentation as needed.
14. Sign the form to confirm it's accurate.
15. Provide any comments or explanations as needed.
16. Complete the form and submit it for processing.
