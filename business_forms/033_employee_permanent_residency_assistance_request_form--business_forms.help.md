# Employee Permanent Residency Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information from employees who are seeking permanent residency assistance. It is used to gather details about the employee's permanent residency status, employment, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Request Information section with your name, date of birth, and employee ID.
2. In the Permanent Residency Information section, provide your current immigration status, country of origin, and visa type.
3. In the Employment Information section, enter your current employment status and job title.
4. In the Permanent Residency Request section, provide a clear and concise description of your permanent residency request.
5. In the Contact Information section, enter your email address and phone number.
6. In the Employee Identification section, enter your employee ID and department or team name.
7. In the Employee Signature section, sign your name as the employee making the request.
8. In the Employer Information section, enter your supervisor's name and email address.
9. In the Employer Signature section, sign your supervisor's name as the approver.
10. In the Request Details section, provide any additional information that may be helpful in processing your request.
11. In the Permanent Residency Certificate section, upload any necessary documents.
12. In the Request Confirmation section, confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the terms and conditions of the request.
13. In the Permanent Residency Request Status section, provide the current status of your request.
14. In the Additional Comments section, enter any additional information that may be helpful in processing your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Information**: This is the first section of the form where you will provide your name, date of birth, and employee ID.
*  **Permanent Residency Information**: This section is where you will provide your current immigration status, country of origin, and visa type.
*  **Employment Information**: This is where you will enter your current employment status and job title.
*  **Permanent Residency Request**: In this section, provide a clear and concise description of your permanent residency request.
*  **Contact Information**: Enter your email address and phone number in this section.
*  **Employee Identification**: Enter your employee ID and department or team name in this section.
*  **Employee Signature**: Sign your name as the employee making the request.
*  **Employer Information**: Enter your supervisor's name and email address in this section.
*  **Employer Signature**: Sign your supervisor's name as the approver.
*  **Request Details**: Provide any additional information that may be helpful in processing your request.
*  **Permanent Residency Certificate**: Upload any necessary documents in this section.
*  **Request Confirmation**: Confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the terms and conditions of the request.
*  **Permanent Residency Request Status**: Provide the current status of your request.
*  **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information that may be helpful in processing your request.
*  **Employee Email**: Enter your employee email address.
*  **Employee Phone**: Enter your employee phone number.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will ensure efficient processing of your request.
