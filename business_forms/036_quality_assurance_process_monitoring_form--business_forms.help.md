# Quality Assurance Process Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the status of quality assurance processes, identify areas for improvement, and document process changes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Provide a brief description of the process being monitored.
2. Enter the name of the process.
3. Update the status of the process using the options provided.
4. List any areas of the process that require improvement.
5. Record any actions taken to improve the process.
6. Enter the date the process was created.
7. Enter the date the process is due to be completed or reviewed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Process Description** (`process_description`, `text`, required: false): A brief description of the process being monitored.
- **Process Name** (`process_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the process.
- **Status** (`process_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of the process: In Progress, Completed, or On Hold.
- **Quality Standards** (`quality_standards`, `text`, required: false): Enter the quality standards applicable to the process.
- **Process Improvement Areas** (`process_improvement_areas`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the areas that require improvement.
- **Process Improvement Actions** (`process_improvement_actions`, `text`, required: false): Record any actions taken to improve the process.
- **Date Created** (`date_created`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the process was created.
- **Date Due** (`date_due`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the process is due to be completed or reviewed.

## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information on process status and improvement areas.
* Use the select_multiple option for Process Improvement Areas to choose all applicable areas.
* Enter dates in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* Save and submit the form after completing all required and optional fields.
