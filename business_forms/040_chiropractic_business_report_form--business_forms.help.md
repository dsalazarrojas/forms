# Chiropractic Business Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Chiropractic Business Report Form is a tool used by chiropractic practices to track and report on their business operations during a specific period of time. This includes financial data such as total revenue and operating expenses, as well as operational challenges and planned improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety. The form is divided into several fields, each representing a different aspect of your practice's performance.
2. Start by entering the "Practice Name" to identify your business or clinic.
3. Select the "Reporting Period Start" and "Reporting Period End" dates that cover the time period you are reporting on.
4. Enter the total number of patients seen during this period and the total revenue generated.
5. If applicable, report on any operational challenges or issues experienced during the period and outline potential steps to address these challenges.
6. Enter total operating expenses (if applicable).
7. Finally, outline any improvements or actions planned to address operational issues or grow your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Practice Name (practice_name, text, Required)**: Enter the name of your business or clinic.
* **Reporting Period Start (reporting_period_start, date, Required)**: Select the start date of the period you are reporting on.
* **Reporting Period End (reporting_period_end, date, Required)**: Select the end date of the period you are reporting on.
* **Total Patients Seen (total_patients, number, Required)**: Enter the total number of patients seen during this period.
* **Total Revenue (revenue, number, Required)**: Enter the total revenue generated during this period.
* **Operating Expenses (operating_expenses, number, Optional)**: Enter total expenses during this period (if applicable).
* **Key Issues and Risks (key_issues, text, Optional)**: Describe any operational challenges or issues encountered during this period.
* **Improvement Actions Planned (improvement_actions, text, Optional)**: Outline steps to address operational challenges or grow your business.

## Tips

* Ensure that you complete all required fields.
* Make sure to select correct dates for the reporting period.
* Be as accurate and specific as possible when reporting on patients seen and revenue generated.
* Describe operational challenges and planned improvements concisely but clearly.
* Double-check that all fields are filled out before submission.
