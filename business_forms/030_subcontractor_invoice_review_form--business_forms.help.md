# Subcontractor Invoice Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to review and manage invoices from subcontractors. It helps the review team to assess and verify the accuracy of invoices before processing payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Invoice Date**, which is the date the invoice was submitted.
2. Enter the **Invoice Number** to identify the specific invoice being reviewed.
3. Enter the **Client Name** and **Client Email** if provided.
4. Enter the **Invoice Total**, which is the total amount due for the services provided.
5. Select the current **Invoice Status**, which can be Pending, Approved, Denied, or Partially Approved.
6. Select the **Payment Method**, which can be Check, Wire Transfer, or Online Payment.
7. Enter any additional **Notes** if necessary.
8. If required, enter the **Submitted By** and **Reviewer Name** fields for tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Invoice Date (1)
* **Invoice Date**: Enter the date the invoice was submitted for review.
### Invoice Number (2)
* **Invoice Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the specific invoice being reviewed.
### Client Name (3)
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client or organization that submitted the invoice.
### Client Email (4)
* **Client Email**: Enter the email address of the client or organization that submitted the invoice (if available).
### Invoice Total (5)
* **Invoice Total**: Enter the total amount due for the services provided.
### Invoice Status (6)
* **Invoice Status**: Select the current status of the invoice (Pending, Approved, Denied, or Partially Approved).
### Subcontractor Name (7)
* **Subcontractor Name**: Enter the name of the subcontractor who submitted the invoice.
### Subcontractor Address (8)
* **Subcontractor Address**: Enter the address of the subcontractor who submitted the invoice (if available).
### Payment Method (9)
* **Payment Method**: Select the method used to make the payment (Check, Wire Transfer, or Online Payment).
### Notes (10)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information regarding the invoice.
### Submitted By (11)
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the invoice for review.
### Reviewer Name (12)
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the invoice (if required).
### Reviewer Comments (13)
* **Reviewer Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback regarding the invoice review process.
