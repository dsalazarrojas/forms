# Co-Working Partner Network Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Co-Working Partner Network Contact Form is designed for potential business partners to get in touch with our team, inquire about possible collaboration opportunities, and initiate a partnership discussion. The form helps us understand your business needs, interests, and goals, enabling us to provide more informed guidance on how we can support you.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start the process, fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled in. Required fields are indicated by an asterisk (\*). If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
2.  Be as detailed as possible in your response to each field to help us better understand your inquiry.
3.  Once you submit the form, our team will review it and contact you to discuss further.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): The name of your organization. This will help us get to know your business identity.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person_name`, `text`, required): The name of the person filling out the form. This is a required field for our follow-up discussions and correspondence.
* **Contact Person Professional Title** (`contact_person_title`, `text`, required): The professional title of the contact person, which can be something like "CEO," "Marketing Manager," or "Founder."
* **Business Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Your business's email address where we can contact you directly.
* **Business Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Your business's contact phone number for more informal communication.
* **Organization Website URL** (`website_url`, `text`, optional): The URL of your business's website for us to learn more about your organization.
* **-- Partnership Inquiry --** (`section_partnership`, `note`, required): This section is a reminder to indicate that you're interested in a partnership.
* **Desired Type of Partnership** (`partnership_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose one or more of the partnership types we offer: Service Provider, Event Collaboration, Corporate Membership, Affiliate Partner, or Other.
* **Please describe your primary collaboration goals** (`collaboration_goals`, `text`, required): Describe your primary goals for partnership, which will help us understand how we can mutually benefit.
* **Target Launch Date for the Partnership** (`target_launch_date`, `date`, optional): If you have a specific launch date for the partnership, please provide it. This helps us align our planning with yours.
* **-- Network Reach --** (`section_reach`, `note`, optional): This section is a reminder that you're looking to connect with our network.
* **Describe your primary audience or network size** (`network_audience_desc`, `text`, required): Give a brief description of your target audience or network size for the partnership.
* **Geographical Regions Covered** (`geographies_covered`, `text`, optional): If your business operates in specific geographical locations, please let us know.
* **-- Follow-up --** (`section_followup`, `note`, optional): This section is a reminder for our follow-up discussion.
* **Preferred time of day for a partnership discussion call** (`discussion_time_pref`, `select_one`, optional): Let us know your preferred time for a call to discuss partnership details.
* **How did you hear about our co-working partner network?** (`referral_source`, `text`, optional): Share with us how you found out about our network, so we can improve our outreach efforts.

Note: This guide is based on the provided YAML structure, ensuring that each field is explained in a concrete and concise manner. If a field's label was weak or unclear, the explanation aims to capture the intended meaning without introducing unsupported business logic. The guide is structured to be easy to follow and understand for end-users.
