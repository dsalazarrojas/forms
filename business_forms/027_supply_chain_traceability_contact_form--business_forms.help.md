# Supply Chain Traceability Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Chain Traceability Contact Form is used to collect contact information and other relevant details from users, which can be used for tracking and monitoring purposes within a supply chain management system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your company name in the "Company" field (optional).
3. Select your role in the organization from the "Role" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
6. If you need to report a specific date and time for your contact, fill it in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
7. Choose your location from the "Location" field.
8. Enter any relevant message in the "Message" field.
9. If applicable, attach any supporting documentation or files in the "Attachment" field.
10. If you have additional comments, select them from the "Comments" field.
11. Indicate if you would like follow-up from us in the "Follow up" field.
12. Choose your company type from the "Company Type" field.
13. Select the types of products you are dealing with from the "Supply Chain" and "Product Type" fields.
14. If relevant, enter the product ID, name, version, weight, length, and size in the corresponding fields.
15. Choose the color and size unit of the product from the "Product Color" and "Product Size Unit" fields.
16. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your role in the organization.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date for your contact.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time for your contact.
* **Location** (`location`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your location.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required): Enter any relevant message.
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, `note`, optional): Attach any supporting documentation or files.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional comments.
* **Follow up** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you would like follow-up from us.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your company type.
* **Supply Chain** (`supply_chain`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the types of supply chains you are dealing with.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of product you are dealing with.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, `number`, optional): Enter the product ID (if applicable).
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the product name (if applicable).
* **Product Version** (`product_version`, `text`, optional): Enter the product version (if applicable).
* **Product Weight** (`product_weight`, `text`, optional): Enter the product weight (if applicable).
* **Product Length** (`product_length`, `text`, optional): Enter the product length (if applicable).
* **Product Size** (`product_size`, `text`, optional): Enter the product size (if applicable).
* **Product Color** (`product_color`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the product color.
* **Product Size Unit** (`product_size_unit`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the product size unit.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes.

Note that the "required" fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
