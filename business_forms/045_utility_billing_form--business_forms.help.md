# Utility Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect utility billing information for customers, ensuring accurate and efficient billing for services provided by the utility company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Customer Details section with your name, address, and any other relevant information.
2. In the Account Details section, provide your account number and account status.
3. Enter your billing address in the Billing Address section.
4. Select your preferred payment method and frequency in the Payment Information section.
5. Enter the payment method status and frequency in the Payment Method Status and Billing Frequency Status sections.
6. Enter your account balance and account balance status in the Account Balance section.
7. Select your account type and billing frequency in the Account Type and Billing Frequency sections.
8. Enter your payment method note and account status note in the Payment Method Note and Account Status Note sections.
9. Finally, review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
