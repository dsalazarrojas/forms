# Account Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help us assess your qualifications and experience for the Account Manager position. Please provide accurate and complete information to increase your chances of being considered for the role.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in all the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to upload your resume and cover letter if required.
3. Answer all the questions thoroughly and truthfully.
4. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name**: Your full legal name.
* **Applicant Email**: Your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Applicant Phone**: Your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Applicant Address**: Your current address for correspondence.
* **Cover Letter**: Upload or paste your cover letter explaining why you are a good fit for the Account Manager position.
* **Resume Attachment**: Upload your resume in PDF or Word format.
* **Years of Experience**: Number of years you have worked in account management.
* **Relevant Industries**: Industries you have managed accounts in, separated by comma (e.g., Finance, Healthcare, Marketing).
* **Education Background**: Your educational qualifications relevant to the position.
* **Certifications**: Any relevant certifications you hold (e.g., CRM, sales, project management).
* **Previous Employer**: Name of your most recent employer.
* **Previous Job Title**: Your most recent job title.
* **Key Responsibilities**: Describe your key responsibilities in your previous role.
* **Achievements Examples**: Provide examples of your key achievements in account management.
* **Client Retention Rate**: Average client retention rate for accounts you managed.
* **Revenue Growth Managed**: Average revenue growth for accounts you managed.
* **CRM Proficiency**: Select all CRM systems you are proficient in (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics, SAP, Oracle, Pipedrive, Other).
* **Languages Spoken**: Languages you speak fluently.
* **Availability Start Date**: Date you would be available to start.
* **Salary Expectations**: Your expected salary for the position.

Note: Please make sure to double-check your responses before submitting the form.
