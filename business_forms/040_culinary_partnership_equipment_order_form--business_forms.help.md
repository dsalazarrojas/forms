# Culinary Partnership Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Culinary Partnership Equipment Order Form is designed for customers to provide information and details about their equipment orders. This form helps to collect essential data from customers to facilitate the processing and shipping of their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing business information on the first page.
2. Move on to the customer information page and fill out the required details.
3. Provide shipping information on the next page.
4. Enter the order details, which includes the items ordered and quantity.
5. If you have any special requests, such as customizations or delivery timeframes, please specify on the special requests page.
6. Review and agree to the terms of service on the review agreement page.
7. Finalize your order by confirming the details on the order confirmation page.
8. Sign the form to confirm your acceptance of the order terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Form Info (Page 1)**
	+ **Business Information** (text field)
		- This field is intended for business owners or representatives to enter their company or business information.
* **Customer Info (Page 2)**
	+ **Customer Information** (text field)
		- This field is designed to collect the customer's personal information, such as name and contact details.
* **Shipping Info (Page 3)**
	+ **Shipping Information** (text field)
		- Enter the delivery address and any other relevant shipping details.
* **Order Details (Page 4)**
	+ **Order Details** (text field)
		- Provide a detailed description of the items ordered and the quantity.
* **Special Requests (Page 5)**
	+ **Special Requests** (text field)
		- This field is for customers to specify any customizations, modifications, or special instructions for their order.
* **Review Agreement (Page 6)**
	+ **Review Agreement** (text field)
		- Review the terms of service carefully and accept or decline the agreement.
* **Order Confirmation (Page 7)**
	+ **Order Confirmation** (text field)
		- Verify the accuracy of your order details before submission.
* **Customer Signature (Page 8)**
	+ **Customer Signature** (text field)
		- Sign the form to confirm acceptance of the order terms and conditions.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and honestly to ensure efficient order processing and shipping.
