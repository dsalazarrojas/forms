# Angie S S Mores Usborne Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and interest from guests at a book-themed party, hosted by Angie S S Mores Usborne. The form is intended to capture overall party satisfaction, favorite book flavors, book interests, and potential future opportunities for collaboration, including hosting parties and joining a book team. The purpose of this form is to help Angie identify areas of improvement and tailor future events to her guests' preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk \*.
* For select_one, select_one multiple, and select_multiple fields, select one or more options that best fit your preferences.
* For text fields, provide a brief answer in the space provided.
* Be sure to scroll to the next page to continue filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- S Mores and Books Party Survey --** (Required)
	+ This is a header section that marks the beginning of the form. It is a note field and does not require any input.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, Text, \*)
	+ Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed for future communications.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, \*)
	+ Enter your valid email address to receive updates and information from Angie S S Mores Usborne.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Text, )
	+ Enter your phone number to be contacted for future events or collaborations.
* **-- Party Experience --** (Required)
	+ This is a header section that marks the beginning of the party feedback section.
* **Overall Party Satisfaction** (`overall_party_satisfaction`, Select One, \*)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the party, with options: Loved it, Enjoyed it, It was okay, Not for me.
* **Favorite S More Flavor Combination** (`favorite_s_more_flavor`, Select One, \*)
	+ Select your favorite S More flavor combination.
* **Which book categories interest you?** (`books_of_interest`, Select Multiple, )
	+ Choose all book categories that interest you, including Board Books, Picture Books, Activity Books, Young Adult, and Non-fiction.
* **-- Future Opportunities --** (Required)
	+ This is a header section that marks the beginning of the future opportunities section.
* **Would you like to host your own book party?** (`interest_in_hosting_a_party`, Select One, \*)
	+ Indicate your interest in hosting a book party, with options: Yes - Book me, Maybe - Send info, Not at this time.
* **Interested in joining my Usborne team?** (`interest_in_joining_my_team`, Select One, \*)
	+ Express your interest in joining Angie's book team, with options: Yes - I want to sell books, Maybe - Tell me more, No thanks.
* **-- Fundraising and Giving --** (Required)
	+ This is a header section that marks the beginning of the fundraising and giving section.
* **Interest in a book fair fundraiser?** (`interest_in_book_fair_fundraiser`, Select One, \*)
	+ Indicate your interest in a book fair fundraiser, with options: Yes for my school / org, Tell me how it works, False.
* **Referral Name - Who else would love these books?** (`referral_name_who_should_i_contact`, Text, )
	+ Enter the name and contact information of someone who might be interested in these books.
* **Additional Feedback for Angie** (`additional_feedback_for_angie`, Text, )
	+ Share any additional feedback or comments with Angie S S Mores Usborne.
