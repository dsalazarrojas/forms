# Software Product Demo Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for a software product demo. Please fill out the form below to schedule a demo at your convenience.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form fields as completely and accurately as possible.
2.  Select the correct demo type that best suits your needs.
3.  Choose a date and time that works for you.
4.  Ensure to provide a valid company URL for our team to access the necessary information.
5.  If applicable, specify the product name and version you are interested in.
6.  You can add notes about any specific questions or areas of interest for the demo.
7.  Submit the form to schedule your demo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name for our team's record.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name for our team's record.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company's name so our team can easily access the correct product version.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be reached via phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`, text, optional): Enter your company's website URL for easier access.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): If you are requesting a specific product, please let us know its name.
* **Product Version** (`product_version`, text, optional): If you are using a specific version of the product, specify it here.
* **Demo Type** (`demo_type`, select_one, required): Choose from Software Overview, Custom Demo, or Trial Version to best suit your needs.
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, required): Select a date for the demo in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Scheduled Time** (`scheduled_time`, time, required): Select the desired time for the demo in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, select_one, optional): Please select your time zone (EST, CST, MST, PST).
* **Demo Length** (`demo_length`, number, optional): If you have a specific time requirement for the demo, please specify the length in minutes.
* **Meeting Length** (`meeting_length`, number, optional): If you plan to have a meeting after the demo, please specify the length in minutes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any specific notes or questions for the demo.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to help us provide the best demo experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us via email or phone.
* We will respond to your demo request within 24-48 hours after submitting the form.
