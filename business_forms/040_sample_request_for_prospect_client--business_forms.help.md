# Sample Request For Prospect Client - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from prospective clients who are requesting services or products from our company. The form is meant to gather essential details about the client, including their contact information, request details, and any other relevant specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field (id: 2).
2. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field (id: 3).
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field (id: 4).
4. Describe your **Request Details** in the "Request Details" field (id: 5).
5. Add any additional **Contact Details** in the "Contact Details" field (id: 6).
6. If you have a **Company Name**, enter it in the "Company Name" field (id: 7).
7. If you are a contact person for a company, enter your name in the "Contact Person" field (id: 8).
8. If applicable, enter your **Job Title** in the "Job Title" field (id: 9).
9. If you are representing a company, enter the **Company** name in the "Company" field (id: 10).
10. If relevant, enter the **Department** you are from in the "Department" field (id: 11).
11. Enter the date you are requesting our services in the "Request Date" field (id: 12).
12. If applicable, select the time you are requesting our services in the "Request Time" field (id: 13).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (id: 2)**: Enter your personal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (id: 3)**: Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Phone Number (id: 4)**: Enter your work or personal phone number.
* **Request Details (id: 5)**: Describe the services or products you are requesting from us.
* **Contact Details (id: 6)**: Add any additional contact information that may be helpful for us to reach you.
* **Company Name (id: 7)**: If you are representing a company, enter the name of the company.
* **Contact Person (id: 8)**: If you are a contact person for a company, enter your name.
* **Job Title (id: 9)**: If you are representing a company, enter your job title.
* **Company (id: 10)**: If you are representing a company, enter the name of the company.
* **Department (id: 11)**: If you are representing a company, enter the department you are from.
* **Request Date (id: 12)**: Enter the date you are requesting our services.
* **Request Time (id: 13)**: Select the time you are requesting our services.

## Tips
Please fill in all required fields to help us better understand your request. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
