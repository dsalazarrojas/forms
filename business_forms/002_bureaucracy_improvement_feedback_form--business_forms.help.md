# Bureaucracy Improvement Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Bureaucracy Improvement Feedback Form is a tool for stakeholders to provide feedback on processes within the organization that need improvement. By filling out this form, you will help identify areas of inefficiency and suggest ways to simplify and improve these processes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, providing as much detail as you can.
2. Be honest and objective in your feedback, focusing on specific issues and suggestions.
3. If you have any additional comments or ideas, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Finally, confirm that you consent to being contacted for follow-up details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Stakeholder Information --** (`section_stakeholder_info`): This section is for general information about you as a stakeholder. This will help us understand your role and interaction with the process.
* **Department or Business Unit** (`department_name`): Please specify the department or business unit where this process is primarily located.
* **Your Current Role** (`stakeholder_role`): Choose your current role within the organization (e.g., Manager, Administrative Staff, External Partner).
* **Name of the Bureaucratic Process** (`target_process_name`): Specify the process you are providing feedback for.
* **How often do you interact with this process** (`process_frequency`): Select the most accurate frequency from the provided options (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually or less).
* **Identified Challenges** (`identified_challenges`): Choose all that apply to this process (Redundant paperwork, Too many approval layers, Unclear instructions, Slow response times, Lack of digital alternatives, Inconsistent requirements).
* **Ease of Navigation Score** (`ease_of_navigation_score`): Rate the ease of navigation from 1 (Very Difficult) to 5 (Very Easy).
* **Suggestions for Simplification** (`simplification_suggestions`): Describe how this process could be made more efficient.
* **Primary Bottleneck Description** (`primary_bottleneck_description`): If there is a single biggest delay in this process, describe it here.
* **Estimated Hours Lost Per Month** (`estimated_time_waste`): If you can estimate the time spent on this process, enter it here.
* **Expected Impact of Changes** (`expected_improvement_impact`): Choose the primary benefit of improving this process (Faster completion time, Cost reduction, Improved employee morale, Better data accuracy, Increased stakeholder satisfaction).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): If you have any other thoughts or ideas for improving organizational efficiency, please add them here.
* **I consent to being contacted for follow-up details** (`submission_consent`): Confirm your consent to being contacted for follow-up information.

Please ensure to review each field carefully and provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your concerns and suggestions.
