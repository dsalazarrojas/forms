# Spa Products Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Spa Products Order Form is designed to collect customer information and preferences for spa products and services. This form is used to create a record of the customer's order, which can be accessed by the spa staff to prepare for their visit and provide the best experience possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your order, including any specific requests or special instructions.
2. **Spa Information** (`form_info`, text, optional): Share any relevant information about your spa visit, such as the type of treatment you're interested in.
3. **Spa Name** (`spa_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the spa you're visiting.
4. **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address, which will be used for communication and confirmation of your order.
5. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which will be used for scheduling and confirmation of your appointment.
6. **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to have your spa products delivered.
7. **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Describe the specifics of your order, including any products or services you're interested in.
8. **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional):
	* Select "Yes" to pay by credit card or other payment method.
	* Select "No" to pay in person or by other means.
9. **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or special requests.
10. **Spa Products** (`spa_products`, select_multiple, optional):
	* Select "Yes" for each product or service you're interested in.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all relevant fields to ensure a smooth and accurate order process.
* Use the "Spa Name" field to ensure the correct spa staff receives your order.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your spa representative for guidance.
