# Supplier Financing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from suppliers who wish to apply for financing. It is designed to gather essential details to facilitate the financing application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page, "Supplier Information", and fill in the required fields with your company's information.
2. Proceed to the second page, "Contact Details", and provide your personal contact information.
3. On the third page, "Financing Requirements", describe the specific financing requirements you need for your business.
4. Finally, on the fourth page, "Order Details", enter the details of the order you are applying for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Information (supplier_information)** (`Supplier Information`, text, required): Please enter your company's name and other relevant details as requested on this page.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)** (`Contact Details`, text, required): Fill in your name, email, phone number, and other contact information.
* **Financing Requirements (financing_requirements)** (`Financing Requirements`, text, required): Describe the specific financing requirements you need for your business, including the amount you require and any specific terms you need.
* **Order Details (order_details)** (`Order Details`, text, required): Enter the details of the order you are applying for, including the date and any relevant information.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields, as indicated by the "required" label.
- If you have any questions or concerns while filling out the form, contact the relevant department or person in charge of financing applications.
