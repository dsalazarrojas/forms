# Loyalty Program Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to verify a customer's loyalty program membership details, including membership type, points balance, points earned, membership expiration date, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

*   First, carefully review the fields below and fill in the required information.
*   Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and access to view this form.
*   Complete all fields accurately and truthfully to ensure that the verification process is successful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your membership card or identification document.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your membership card or identification document.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address associated with your loyalty program membership.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number associated with your loyalty program membership.
*   **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required: false): Select your membership type from the options provided. This will help us verify your loyalty program status.
*   **Points Balance** (`points_balance`, number, required: false): Enter your current points balance as it appears on your membership card or identification document.
*   **Points Earned** (`points_earned`, number, required: false): Enter the number of points earned under your loyalty program membership.
*   **Membership Expiration Date** (`membership_expiration_date`, date, required: false): Enter your membership expiration date as it appears on your membership card or identification document.
*   **Member Since** (`member_since`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you first joined the loyalty program.
*   **Last Update** (`last_update`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you last updated your membership information.
*   **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, text, required: false): Enter your customer ID number or other unique identifier associated with your loyalty program membership.
*   **Points Balance Verification** (`points_balance_verification`, number, required: false): This field is not applicable as it's not a field for user input.

Note: This form is for verification purposes only. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your loyalty program representative for assistance.
