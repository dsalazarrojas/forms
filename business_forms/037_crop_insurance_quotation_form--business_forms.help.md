# crop_insurance_quotation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to generate a quote for crop insurance policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of your farm in the **Farm Name** field.
2. Select the type of crop you are insuring from the **crop_type** dropdown menu.
3. Select the type(s) of coverage you are interested in from the **coverage_type** dropdown menu.
4. Enter the yield (or production amount) of the crop in the **Yield** field.
5. Enter the price per unit of the crop in the **Price per Unit** field.
6. If you have a specific region or location for your farm, enter it in the **Region** field.
7. If you have any special instructions or contact information, enter it in the **Contact Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Farm Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your farm.
* **crop_type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of crop you are insuring (e.g. Corn, Soybean, etc.).
* **coverage_type** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the type of coverage you are interested in (e.g. Crop Insurance, etc.).
* **Yield** (Number, Required): Enter the yield (or production amount) of the crop.
* **Price per Unit** (Number, Required): Enter the price per unit of the crop.
* **Region** (Text, Optional): Enter the specific region or location of your farm (optional).
* **Farm Location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of your farm (optional).
* **Contact Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any special instructions or contact information.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information to get an accurate quote.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can contact our customer support for assistance.
* If you have any special circumstances or requirements for your crop insurance quote, please contact our support team for further assistance.
