# new_distributor_approval_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_distributor_approval_request_form is designed for distributor approval, allowing you to request approval for your product to be sold on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the required fields with the requested information.
2.  Make sure to review and submit the form carefully before sending it for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Distributor Details** (`distributor_details`, text, required: false): This field requires a brief description of the distributor, including their company name, business address, and any other relevant information. 
    *   Enter your distributor name and contact information in this field.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide contact information for the distributor, such as email, phone number, or other preferred contact methods. 
    *   Enter your distributor's contact information in this field.
*   **Business Details** (`business_details`, text, required: false): This field is for a brief description of your business, including your company name, address, and other relevant information. 
    *   Enter your business name and contact information in this field.
*   **Products** (`products`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for selecting which product(s) you are requesting approval for. 
    *   Choose the relevant product(s) from the dropdown list.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): This field is for any additional information or context you would like to provide about your request. 
    *   Enter any additional information about your request in this field.
