# Smtf Policies And Procedures - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track policies and procedures within the organization. It ensures that all relevant information is recorded and up-to-date, making it easier to manage and implement policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that the policy or procedure does not already exist before filling out this form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information for the new policy or procedure.
3. Select the correct category and policy type.
4. Choose the status of the policy or procedure (Active or Inactive).
5. Select who created and updated the policy or procedure.
6. Add any comments or notes as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policy Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the policy or procedure.
* **Procedure Description** (Text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of the policy or procedure.
* **Effective Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the policy or procedure comes into effect.
* **Expiration Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the policy or procedure expires or will no longer be in effect.
* **Category** (Select One, Required): Choose a category for the policy or procedure (Smtf, Other, Multiple).
* **Policy Type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose which type of policy or procedure this is (Yes, No).
* **Status** (Select One, Required): Choose the status of the policy or procedure (Active, Inactive).
* **Created By** (Select One, Optional): Choose who created the policy or procedure.
* **Updated By** (Select One, Optional): Choose who updated the policy or procedure.
* **Comments** (Note, Optional): Add any comments or notes about the policy or procedure.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Use the correct category and policy type to accurately reflect the policy or procedure.
* Update the status accordingly when a policy or procedure is modified or expires.
* Keep comments up-to-date and accurate for reference and future changes.
