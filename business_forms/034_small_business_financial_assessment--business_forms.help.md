# Small Business Financial Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess the financial situation of a small business. It gathers information about the business's income and expenses to provide a comprehensive understanding of its financial status.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your annual revenue.
2.  Project your revenue for the next year.
3.  Enter your operating expenses.
4.  Enter your capital expenses.
5.  Enter your debt expenses.
6.  Enter your miscellaneous expenses.
7.  Select your goals for the next quarter, year, and long-term.
8.  Enter the date and time you are completing this assessment.
9.  Enter your contact phone number.
10.  Enter your email address.
11.  Provide any additional notes about your business's financial situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Annual Revenue**: This field asks for the total revenue generated by your business in a year. This is a required field for accurate assessment.
* **Projected Revenue**: This field is for forecasting the revenue your business is expected to generate in the next year. This helps in identifying potential income sources and trends.
* **Operating Expenses**: These are expenses related to the day-to-day operations of your business, such as rent, utilities, and supplies. 
* **Capital Expenses**: These expenses are related to long-term investments and asset purchases that benefit the business over time.
* **Debt Expenses**: These expenses are related to repaying loans and interest on borrowed money.
* **Miscellaneous Expenses**: These are any other expenses not categorized above, such as marketing and advertising.
* **Goals 1, 2, 3**: These fields allow you to select your goals for the next quarter, year, and long-term, such as increasing revenue, reducing debt, or expanding operations. 
* **Date**: This field is for recording the date you are completing this assessment.
* **Time**: This field is for recording the time you are completing this assessment.
* **Phone**: This field is for providing your contact phone number.
* **Email**: This field is for providing your email address.
* **Note**: This field is for providing any additional notes about your business's financial situation.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
- Review the form carefully to ensure consistency and accuracy.
- If you need help or clarification, contact the relevant authorities or department.
