# Debt Collection Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify debt collection information for a debtor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the debtor's contact information in the **Contact Info** field.
2. Select the correct **Debt Status** (Active, Inactive, or Closed) and **Debtors** (Yes or No) fields.
3. Enter the **Debt Amount** and **Due Date** fields if applicable.
4. Choose the **Payment Method** (Bank Account or Credit Card) if applicable.
5. Add any **Notes** or **Additional Contact** information if required.
6. Review and submit the form once all fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Debtor Information**: This is a text field where you need to enter the debtor's details. 
- **Debt Amount**: Enter the amount of debt owed to the debtor. 
- **Payment Method**: Select the method used to make the payment (Bank Account or Credit Card). 
- **Due Date**: Enter the date when the debt is due. 
- **Account Number**: If applicable, enter the debtor's account number. 
- **Contact Info**: Enter the debtor's contact information (email address). 
- **Debt Description**: Enter a brief description of the debt. 
- **Debt Status**: Select all relevant statuses for the debt (Active, Inactive, or Closed). 
- **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the debt. 
- **Additional Contact**: Add any additional contact information. 
- **Debtors**: Select Yes or No to indicate if the debt is with one or multiple debtors. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use the correct payment method and due date for the debt.
* Use the correct contact information to ensure you can reach the debtor.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
