# hoa_document_amendment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide amendments and updates to documents related to Home Office Affairs (HOA) in the Document Management System.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief title to describe the amendment in the **form_title** field.
2. Choose whether the amendment is a Category 1 or Category 2 by selecting the correct option in the **form_category** field.
3. In the **form_description** field, enter a detailed description of the changes or updates being made.
4. Enter the ID of the document that is being amended in the **form_id** field.
5. Select the date of the amendment from the calendar in the **form_date** field.
6. Select the time of the amendment from the clock in the **form_time** field.
7. In the **hoa_document_amendment** field, enter any relevant prophets or amendments.
8. Select whether or not the amendment is a prophet amendment in the **hoa_document_amendment_reason** field.
9. Enter any additional details about the amendment in the **hoa_document_amendment_details** field.
10. Finally, select whether or not the amendment has been signed in the **hoa_document_amendment_signed** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title** (text, required: false): Enter a brief title to describe the amendment.
* **form_category** (select_one, required: false): Choose whether the amendment is a Category 1 or Category 2.
* **form_description** (text, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the changes or updates being made.
* **form_id** (number, required: false): Enter the ID of the document that is being amended.
* **form_date** (date, required: false): Select the date of the amendment from the calendar.
* **form_time** (time, required: false): Select the time of the amendment from the clock.
* **hoa_document_amendment** (text, required: false): Enter prophets of amendments.
* **hoa_document_amendment_reason** (select_multiple, required: false): Select whether or not the amendment is a prophet amendment.
* **hoa_document_amendment_details** (text, required: false): Enter any additional details about the amendment.
* **hoa_document_amendment_signed** (select_one, required: false): Select whether or not the amendment has been signed.
