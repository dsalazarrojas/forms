# Travel Agency Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Agency Incident Form is used to collect information about incidents that occurred on a trip, allowing the agency to document and report such incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of trip (Domestic, International, or Cruise) that the incident occurred on.
2. Provide the agency name and any other relevant details.
3. Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
4. Describe the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
5. Enter any additional information, such as the agency address and incident location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agency Name**: Enter the name of your travel agency, which will help us identify your agency in our records.
* **Trip Type**: Select the type of trip where the incident occurred (Domestic, International, or Cruise).
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Agency Address**: Enter the address of your travel agency.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
