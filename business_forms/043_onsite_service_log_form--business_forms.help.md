# onsite_service_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and record on-site services provided to customers. It's a crucial tool for maintaining accurate records and ensuring continuity of service.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields as per the instructions for each field.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for onsite_engineer and onsite_clinic fields accurately.
3. Enter the correct date and time in the onsite_date and onsite_time fields.
4. Provide a detailed account of the actions taken in the actions_taken field.
5. Obtain the customer's signature in the customer_signature field.
6. Add any relevant notes about the on-site service in the onsite_note field.
7. Enter the customer's email address for contact purposes.
8. Fill in your contact information in the onsite_phone field.
9. Select the current onsite_service_status from the dropdown options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **onsite_engineer** (Yes/No): Check if an engineer was present on-site.
* **onsite_clinic** (Yes/No): Check if a clinic was present on-site.
* **onsite_date** (Date): Enter the date of the on-site service.
* **onsite_time** (Time): Enter the time of the on-site service.
* **actions_taken** (Text): Describe the work done or actions taken during the on-site service.
* **customer_signature** (Text): Obtain a signature from the customer to confirm the service.
* **onsite_note** (Note): Add any additional notes about the on-site service.
* **onsite_email** (Email): Enter the customer's contact email address.
* **onsite_phone** (Text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **onsite_service_status** (Active/Inactive): Select the current status of the on-site service.
