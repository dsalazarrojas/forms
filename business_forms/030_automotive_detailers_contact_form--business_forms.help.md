# Automotive Detailers Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from customers to provide personalized detailing services. It includes fields for customer contact information, vehicle details, and service preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your **Address** (optional) in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your **Vehicle Year** in the "Vehicle Year" field.
6. Enter your **Vehicle Make** in the "Vehicle Make" field.
7. Enter your **Vehicle Model** in the "Vehicle Model" field.
8. Enter your **Vehicle Color** (optional) in the "Vehicle Color" field.
9. Select the **Type of Vehicle** you have from the options provided.
10. Choose the **Type of Detail Service Desired** from the options provided.
11. Select all services you would like to have done to your vehicle from the "Specific Services Requested" options.
12. Choose the **Current Vehicle Condition** from the options provided.
13. Enter any **Specific Issues or Concerns** with your vehicle (optional).
14. Choose a **Preferred Service Date** and **Preferred Service Time** from the options provided.
15. Select your **Service Location Preference** from the options provided.
16. Enter your **Estimated Budget** (optional).
17. Enter any **Additional Notes or Special Requests** (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your driver's license or identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address that we can contact you with.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your home or work address for billing and contact purposes (optional).
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of your vehicle, e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of your vehicle, e.g., Corolla, Mustang, etc.
* **Vehicle Color**: Enter the color of your vehicle (optional).
* **Type of Vehicle**: Select the type of vehicle you have, e.g., sedan, SUV, motorcycle, etc.
* **Type of Detail Service Desired**: Choose the level of service you need, e.g., exterior wash, interior cleaning, etc.
* **Specific Services Requested**: Check all services you would like to have done to your vehicle, e.g., hand wash, wax application, etc.
* **Current Vehicle Condition**: Select the current condition of your vehicle, e.g., excellent, good, fair, poor, etc.
* **Specific Issues or Concerns**: Enter any specific issues or concerns you have with your vehicle, e.g., stains, odors, etc.
* **Preferred Service Date**: Choose a date for your preferred service, considering we may not be able to accommodate your first choice of date.
* **Preferred Service Time**: Choose a time for your preferred service, e.g., morning, afternoon, evening, etc.
* **Service Location Preference**: Select where you would like your vehicle serviced, e.g., at our facility, your location, etc.
* **Estimated Budget**: Enter your estimated budget for the services you need.
* **Additional Notes or Special Requests**: Enter any additional notes or special requests you may have for the service.

Please note that this form collects sensitive customer information and we will handle it according to our internal policies and procedures to ensure your data is secure and private.
