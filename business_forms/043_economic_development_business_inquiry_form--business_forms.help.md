# Economic Development Business Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for users to inquire about economic development business opportunities. It helps us understand your business inquiry and provide the most suitable support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions according to your business needs.
2. Provide accurate and detailed information for all required fields.
3. Review and submit the form to initiate the inquiry process.

* **What is the nature of your business inquiry?** (`business_inquiry_form`): Briefly describe the nature of your business inquiry. This will help us understand your business needs.
* **What type of business are you inquiring about?** (`business_type`): Select the type of business you are inquiring about. For example, if you are inquiring about a new business venture, select "Yes."
* **What is the location of your business?** (`business_location`): Select all locations relevant to your business.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`): Provide any additional details about your project.

* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`): Enter your business address.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`): Enter your project budget.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`): Enter any additional project notes.
