# tax_payment_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect payment authorization from customers for tax-related activities. This form gathers information necessary for businesses to complete tax payments efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information.
2. Enter your payment details.
3. Share tax-related information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info (customer_info)**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details to identify yourself as a customer.
* **Payment Info (payment_info)**: Enter your payment method and any other payment-specific details as required.
* **Tax Info (tax_info)**: Enter tax-related information, such as your tax ID or other relevant tax details.
