# Succession Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Succession Planning Form is a tool used to identify and document key employees, assess their roles and responsibilities, and create a plan for succession to ensure the continued growth and success of your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to understand the questions and their relevance to your business.
2. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
3. Answer all questions to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): A brief description of the key employee or the current state of the succession planning process.
* **Key Employees** (`key_employees`, select_multiple, optional): Check all key employees who will be impacted by the succession planning process. 
    * This field is used to identify who will be impacted by the succession planning process.
* **Role Hierarchy** (`role_hierarchy`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the current role has a clear hierarchy and "No" if it does not.
    * This field helps identify if there is a clear succession plan in place.
* **Succession Plan** (`succession_plan`, text, optional): Describe the current state of the succession planning process.
* **Key Employees 1** (`key_employees_1`, select_multiple, optional): Check all key employees who are in the current role or will be impacted by the succession planning process.
* **Key Employees 2** (`key_employees_2`, select_multiple, optional): Check all key employees who are in the current role or will be impacted by the succession planning process.
* **Key Employees 3** (`key_employees_3`, select_multiple, optional): Check all key employees who are in the current role or will be impacted by the succession planning process.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): List the next steps to be taken in the succession planning process.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the succession planning process.
* **Submit Button** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Click this button to submit the form.

## Tips
* Ensure each field is filled out accurately and completely to create an effective succession plan.
* Review each page carefully to understand the questions and their relevance to your business.
* If a key employee is missing from the list, add them under Key Employees 1, 2, or 3.
