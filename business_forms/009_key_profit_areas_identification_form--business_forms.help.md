# Key Profit Areas Identification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you identify and track key profit areas within your business. It will guide you through the process of identifying key profit areas for each segment of your business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each segment of your business and determine whether each one is a key profit area or not.
2. For each segment, select the correct answer for each field, based on your assessment.
3. Enter relevant notes or comments for each segment if needed.
4. Repeat the process for each segment, ensuring you fill out all required and optional fields.
