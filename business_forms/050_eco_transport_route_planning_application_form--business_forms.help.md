# Eco Transport Route Planning Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Transport Route Planning Application Form is designed to help users plan their eco-friendly transport route. This form gathers information about the starting and ending locations, type of transport, and other relevant details to provide users with the most efficient and environmentally friendly route options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the starting location and city in the "start_location" and "start_city" fields respectively.
2. Enter the ending location and city in the "end_location" and "end_city" fields.
3. Select the type of transport you plan to use from the "transport_type" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the distance you plan to travel in the "distance" field.
5. Provide any additional notes about your route in the "route_notes" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **start_location**: Enter the exact address of your starting location.
* **start_city**: Enter the city of your starting location.
* **end_location**: Enter the exact address of your ending location.
* **end_city**: Enter the city of your ending location.
* **transport_type**: Select the type of transport you plan to use:
	+ Air: Flying
	+ Train: Train travel
	+ Bus: Bus travel
	+ Car: Driving
	+ Bicycle: Cycling
* **distance**: Enter the distance of your journey.
* **route_notes**: Enter any additional notes or information about your route.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate route planning.
* Double-check your input information to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
