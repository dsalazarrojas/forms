# Data Access Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Access Monitoring Form is designed to collect information about individuals and their access to certain data centers for internal purposes. This form will help monitor and track access to data centers within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields respectively.
- Select the department you belong to from the drop-down menu next to "Department".
- Choose the data center you are accessing from the drop-down menu next to "Data Center".
- If necessary, enter a date and time of access in the "Date" and "Time" fields respectively.
- Add any notes about your access request in the "Notes" field.
- Click "Submit" to submit your access request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your work email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your work phone number.
- **Department** (`department`, select_one, required/optional): Select your department (e.g. Sales, Engineering, Finance).
- **Data Center** (`data_center`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the data center you are accessing (e.g. North, Central, South).
- **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of access (optional, if not entering date, leave blank).
- **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of access (optional, if not entering time, leave blank).
- **Notes** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional information about your access request. 

## Tips
- Be as accurate as possible with your information, as this will help with data accuracy and tracking. 
- If you have any issues with the form, please contact your departmental representative for assistance.
