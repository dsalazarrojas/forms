# supply_chain_industry_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general feedback and opinions on the industry as a whole. Please provide your genuine insights and thoughts about your experiences and perceptions in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name and contact information (if you choose to provide it) in the corresponding fields.
2. Answer the assessment questions with your genuine opinions and thoughts about the industry.
3. Make sure to answer all the questions, but do not feel obligated to answer them in a specific format or length.
4. Submit the form when you are finished.
