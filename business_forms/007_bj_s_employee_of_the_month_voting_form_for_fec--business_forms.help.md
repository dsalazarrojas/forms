# Bj S Employee Of The Month Voting Form For Fec - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect nominations for the "Employee of the Month" award, allowing colleagues to recognize and reward outstanding work.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name in the "Your Name" field.
2.  Select the current date in the "Voting Date" field.
3.  Choose the month for which you're nominating the employee in the "Month for Nomination" field.
4.  Enter the name of the employee you're nominating in the "Employee You are Nominating" field.
5.  Select the department of the employee you're nominating in the "Department" field.
6.  Provide specific examples of the employee's great work in the "Why does this employee deserve Employee of the Month?" field.
7.  Choose the qualities the employee demonstrated in the "Which qualities did they demonstrate?" field.
8.  Add any additional comments about the employee in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name** (text, required): Please enter your name as a required field to identify you as the voter.
*   **Today Date** (date, required): Select the current date to ensure the vote is up-to-date.
*   **Month for Nomination** (select_one, required): Choose the month for which you're nominating the employee (e.g., January, February, etc.).
*   **Employee You are Nominating** (text, required): Enter the name of the employee you're nominating.
*   **Department** (select_one, required): Select the department of the employee you're nominating (e.g., Front Desk, Party Hosting, etc.).
*   **Why does this employee deserve Employee of the Month?** (text, required): Provide specific examples of the employee's great work.
*   **Which qualities did they demonstrate?** (select_multiple, required): Choose the qualities the employee demonstrated (e.g., Customer Service, Teamwork, etc.).
*   **Any other comments?** (text, optional): Add any additional comments about the employee you're nominating.

Note that some fields are required and should be filled out for accurate feedback.
