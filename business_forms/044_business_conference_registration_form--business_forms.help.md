# Business Conference Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect registration information from attendees of a business conference. It helps us understand your preferences, needs, and expectations for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your **Full Name**, **Position**, and **Company Name** as required fields.
2.  Select your **Industry** from the provided options.
3.  Provide your **Email Address** and **Phone Number**.
4.  Select the **Conference Name**, **Conference Date**, and **Conference Location**.
5.  Choose your **Registration Type** (e.g., Full Conference Pass, Single Day Pass, etc.).
6.  If you're attending **Day 1 Only**, **Day 2 Only**, or **All Days**, select the relevant option.
7.  Indicate your **Dietary Requirements** (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, etc.) and specify any **Dietary Notes**.
8.  If you need **Hotel Booking**, select **Yes - Need Booking**.
9.  If you have a **Hotel Preference** (e.g., Standard Room, Deluxe Room, etc.), select the relevant option.
10.  Indicate if you need **Airport Transfers** (e.g., Airport Pickup, Airport Drop-off, etc.).
11.  Express your **Networking Interest** (e.g., Yes - Very Interested, Yes - Somewhat Interested, etc.).
12.  Select if you're interested in visiting **Exhibitors**.
13.  Choose your preferred **Payment Method** (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
14.  If you have a **Promo Code**, enter it here.
15.  Mention any **Special Requests** you have.
16.  Finally, accept the **Terms and Conditions** by selecting **Yes - I Agree**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Attendee Information**: This field is a note where you can provide any additional information about yourself.
*   **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Position**: Enter your job title or position in your company.
*   **Company Name**: Enter the name of your organization.
*   **Industry**: Select the industry that best describes your field of work.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Mailing Address**: Enter your full mailing address (optional).
*   **Conference Information**: This field is a note where you can provide any additional information about the conference.
*   **Conference Name**: Enter the name of the conference you're registering for.
*   **Conference Date**: Enter the date of the conference.
*   **Conference Location**: Enter the location of the conference.
*   **Registration Type**: Select the type of registration you require (e.g., Full Conference Pass, Single Day Pass, etc.).
*   **Day Selection**: Select which day you'll be attending the conference.
*   **Session Preferences**: Choose the types of sessions you're interested in attending (e.g., Keynote Sessions, Technical Workshops, etc.).
*   **Dietary Requirements**: Select any dietary restrictions you have (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, etc.).
*   **Dietary Notes**: Provide any additional information about your dietary requirements.
*   **Accommodation**: Indicate if you need hotel booking assistance.
*   **Hotel Preference**: Select your preferred room type.
*   **Transportation**: Indicate if you need airport transfers.
*   **Networking Interest**: Express your level of interest in networking opportunities.
*   **Exhibitor Interest**: Indicate if you're interested in visiting exhibitors.
*   **Payment Information**: This field is a note where you can provide any additional payment information.
*   **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment you prefer (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
*   **Promo Code**: If you have a promo code, enter it here.
*   **Special Requests**: Provide any special requirements you have.
*   **Terms and Conditions**: Accept the terms and conditions of the conference by selecting **Yes - I Agree**.

**Tips**

*   Please fill in the form accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth registration process.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the conference organizers.
*   Review the **Terms and Conditions** carefully before accepting them.
