# Courier Partner Realignment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Courier Partner Realignment Request Form is used to request changes to your current courier partner arrangement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you wish to realign your partner.
2. If you selected "Yes", provide the address, city, state, and postal code of your partner's new address.
3. Enter the partner's name as you would like to see it displayed in the system.
4. Enter the phone number of your partner, including the area code.
5. Enter the email address of your partner.
6. Select the date and time when you would like the realignment to take effect.
7. Provide a brief reason for the realignment.
8. Add any additional notes about the realignment.
9. Select the person who will be assigned to take over your route, either "Chat Jimmy" or "Other".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Request** (Required/Optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you wish to realign your partner.
* **Address** (Text): Enter the new address of your partner if you selected "Yes" for the "Partner Request" field.
* **City** (Text): Enter the city of your partner's new address.
* **State** (Text): Enter the state of your partner's new address.
* **Postal Code** (Text): Enter the postal code of your partner's new address.
* **Partner Name** (Text): Enter the name of your partner as you would like to see it displayed in the system.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of your partner, including the area code.
* **Email** (Email): Enter the email address of your partner.
* **Realignment Date** (Date): Select the date when you would like the realignment to take effect.
* **Realignment Time** (Time): Select the time when you would like the realignment to take effect.
* **Reason for Realignment** (Text): Provide a brief reason for the realignment.
* **Notes** (Note): Add any additional notes about the realignment.
* **Assigned To** (Select One): Select the person who will be assigned to take over your route, either "Chat Jimmy" or "Other".

## Tips
- Only submit one request per partner at a time.
- Ensure that you have your partner's correct contact information entered.
- Make sure to select a valid realignment date and time.
- Keep your reason for realignment brief and concise.
- If you are reassigning your partner to "Other", please specify the name of the person in the "Notes" field.
