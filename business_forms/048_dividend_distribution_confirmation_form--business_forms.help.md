# Dividend Distribution Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dividend Distribution Confirmation Form is used to confirm the distribution of dividends to shareholders. This form helps in tracking and recording the confirmation of dividend payments, ensuring accuracy and transparency in financial transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2.  Select the correct payment method and confirm the payment details.
3.  Confirm whether the dividend distribution has been done correctly.
4.  Add any relevant notes about the distribution.
5.  Enter the email and phone number of the shareholder for contact purposes.
6.  Choose the tool assigned to the shareholder for their account.
7.  Select the category and description of the form.
8.  Enter the unique identifier of the form for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company involved in the dividend distribution.
*   **Shareholder** (`shareholder`, text, optional): Enter the name of the shareholder receiving the dividend.
*   **Distribution Date** (`distribution_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the dividend distribution.
*   **Distribution Amount** (`distribution_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of dividend distributed.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how the dividend was paid (Bank Transfer, Check, Wire Transfer).
*   **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the payment (e.g., cheque number).
*   **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the distribution has been confirmed or not (True or False).
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes about the distribution.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the shareholder for contact purposes.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the shareholder for contact purposes.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Choose the tool assigned to the shareholder's account (chatjimmy or Other).
*   **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Enter the name of the file outputted from the form.
*   **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category for the form (Business Forms or Other).
*   **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the form for reference.
*   **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the form.
