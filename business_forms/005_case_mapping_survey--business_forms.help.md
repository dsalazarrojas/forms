# Case Mapping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Mapping Survey is designed to collect structured and relevant information about cases within an organization. This data will help case managers and stakeholders track and analyze cases efficiently, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name for the purpose of tracking and follow-up.
2.  Provide the name of the organization you represent or are associated with.
3.  Fill out the case title or identifier, which should be a short and descriptive name.
4.  Choose the current phase of the case lifecycle from the provided options (Intake, Investigation, Active management, or Closed).
5.  List the main obstacles affecting the progress of the case.
6.  Specify the desired outcome or resolution of the case.
7.  Indicate the operational priority level (Low, Medium, High, or Critical) based on your organization's standards.
8.  If applicable, suggest actions and owners for the next steps in the case.
9.  List any supporting documents available for the case, including their locations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): This field is for your name, which will help us track and follow up on the case.
*   **Organization name** (`organization`, text, required): Please enter the name of your company or team to identify the organization associated with this case.
*   **Case title or identifier** (`case_title`, text, required): Provide a short and descriptive name for the case that will help identify it quickly.
*   **Case stage** (`case_stage`, select_one, required): Choose the current phase of the case from the provided options: Intake, Investigation, Active management, or Closed.
*   **Stakeholders involved** (`stakeholders_involved`, text, optional): List the teams or individuals involved in or affected by the case.
*   **Key challenges** (`key_challenges`, text, required): Describe the main obstacles hindering case progress.
*   **Expected outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, optional): Specify the desired resolution or metrics for the case.
*   **Priority level** (`priority_level`, select_one, required): Select the operational priority level from Low, Medium, High, or Critical, as defined by your organization.
*   **Recommended next steps** (`recommended_next_steps`, text, optional): Suggest actions and owners for the next steps in the case if necessary.
*   **Supporting documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): List and provide locations for any relevant documents supporting the case.
## Tips
*   Be thorough and accurate when providing information to ensure effective case management and analysis.
*   If you need help or clarification on any of the fields, refer to your organization's case management guidelines or contact the case management team.
*   Remember to review and update this form as necessary to ensure it reflects your organization's changing needs and standards.
