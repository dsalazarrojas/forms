# Getting Started With Administrative Services - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect administrative details for services provided by our organization. It helps to track and manage service requests, ensuring timely and efficient resolution. Please use this form to provide accurate and clear information about the service you require.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant contact details and service type from the provided options.
2. Choose the urgency level of your service request.
3. Provide your contact name and email (optional).
4. Add any additional service details (optional).
5. Check the consent box to acknowledge that you have read and understood the service terms.
6. Add any additional notes or comments (optional).
7. Fill in the assigned tool (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Contact Details (1)**
* `Contact Name` (`contact_name`): Your name, if you'd like to provide it.
### **Service Type (2)**
* `Service Type`: Select the type of service you are interested in. Please choose from the provided options.
### **Urgency (3)**
* `Urgency`: Indicate the level of urgency for your service request. Please select from the provided options.
### **Contact Email (4)**
* `Contact Email`: Your email address, if you'd like to provide it.
### **Service Details (5)**
* `Service Details`: Provide any additional information about the service you require.
### **Consent (6)**
* `Consent`: Check this box to acknowledge that you have read and understood the service terms.
### **Note (7)**
* `Note`: Add any additional notes or comments about your service request.
### **Assigned Tool (8)**
* `Assigned Tool`: Fill in the name of the tool assigned to your service request.
### **Category (9)**
* `Category`: Select the relevant category for your service request.
### **Description (10)**
* `Description`: Add a brief description of your service request.
### **Form ID (11)**
* `Form ID`: Enter a unique ID for your form submission.
### **Category ID (12)**
* `Category ID`: Enter the ID of the category selected in the previous field.
### **Output File (13)**
* `Output File`: Enter the name of the output file for your service request.
### **Assigned Tool 2 (14)**
* `Assigned Tool 2`: Fill in the name of the tool assigned to your service request.
### **Created By (15)**
* `Created By`: Enter your name as the creator of the service request.
### **Updated By (16)**
* `Updated By`: Enter the name of the person who updated the service request.
### **Updated On (17)**
