# Payment And Obligation Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment And Obligation Review form is designed to collect information about payments and obligations in a business or financial context. This form helps to gather essential details for accounting, invoicing, or financial reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information and context before starting the form.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* `contact_phone`: the contact phone number of the person or entity involved in the payment or obligation.
	* `email`: the email address of the person or entity involved in the payment or obligation.
3. Fill in the optional fields as needed:
	* `company_details`: a brief description of the company or entity involved in the payment or obligation.
	* `contact_details`: select one or more options to describe the contact details of the person or entity involved in the payment or obligation.
	* `payment_terms`: select one option to describe the payment terms, such as payment methods or schedules.
	* `date_of_payment`: the date when the payment was or will be made.
	* `amount_of_payment`: the amount of the payment.
	* `payment_status`: select one option to describe the payment status, such as pending or received.
	* `payment_comments`: any additional comments or notes about the payment or obligation.
4. Review and submit the form to ensure that all required fields are filled in and the information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment And Obligation Review** (`1`): This is the title of the form, which should be filled in with the name of the payment or obligation being reviewed.
* **company_details** (`2`): A brief description of the company or entity involved in the payment or obligation. This field is optional.
* **contact_details** (`3`): Select one or more options to describe the contact details of the person or entity involved in the payment or obligation.
* **payment_terms** (`4`): Select one option to describe the payment terms, such as payment methods or schedules. This field is optional.
* **date_of_payment** (`5`): The date when the payment was or will be made. This field is optional.
* **amount_of_payment** (`6`): The amount of the payment. This field is optional.
* **payment_status** (`7`): Select one option to describe the payment status, such as pending or received. This field is optional.
* **payment_comments** (`8`): Any additional comments or notes about the payment or obligation. This field is optional.
* **contact_phone** (`9`): The contact phone number of the person or entity involved in the payment or obligation. This field is required.
* **email** (`10`): The email address of the person or entity involved in the payment or obligation. This field is required.
* **contact_email** (`11`): The contact email address of the person or entity involved in the payment or obligation. This field is optional.
* **date_of_obligation** (`12`): The date when the obligation was or will be made. This field is optional.

Note: If you are unsure about any of the fields or their requirements, please refer to the form's title and the instructions above for guidance.
