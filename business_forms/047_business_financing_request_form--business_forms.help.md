# Business Financing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Financing Request Form is designed to gather essential information from businesses seeking financing. This form helps us understand your business needs, financial situation, and goals to assess your eligibility for financing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Answer all questions in the form.
2.  Enter accurate and complete information for each field.
3.  Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required):
	+ Your complete name for identification purposes.
* **SSN (Last 4 Digits)** (`ssn_last_four`, `text`, required):
	+ The last 4 digits of your social security number for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required):
	+ The email address associated with your business or company.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required):
	+ The phone number for contact purposes.
* **Home Address** (`address`, `text`, required):
	+ Your home address for identification purposes.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required):
	+ The legal name of your business or company.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required):
	+ The type of structure your business has (e.g., Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, or Startup).
* **Business Stage** (`business_stage`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Where your business is in its development cycle (e.g., Planning, Pre-Launch, Startup, Early Stage, or Growth Stage).
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, required):
	+ The industry your business operates in.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, `text`, required):
	+ A brief description of your business and its activities.
* **Amount of Financing Requested** (`amount_requested`, `number`, required):
	+ The total amount of financing you're requesting.
* **Type of Financing Requested** (`financing_type`, `select_one`, required):
	+ The type of financing you're requesting (e.g., Debt Financing, Equity Financing, Grant, Loan, Credit Line, or Other).
* **Use of Funds** (`use_of_funds`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ The specific uses for the requested funds (e.g., Startup Costs, Equipment Purchase, Marketing, Working Capital, etc.).
* **Detailed Use of Funds Description** (`use_description`, `text`, required):
	+ A detailed description of how you plan to use the requested funds.
* **Proposed Repayment Timeline** (`repayment_timeline`, `select_one`, required):
	+ The number of months you expect to take to repay the loan.
* **Year 1 Projected Revenue** (`projected_revenue`, `number`, optional):
	+ Your expected revenue for the first year.
* **Current Personal Capital** (`current_capital`, `number`, optional):
	+ The amount you plan to invest personally.
* **Estimated Credit Score** (`credit_score`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Your approximate credit score.

Note: Please provide accurate and truthful information to ensure your request is processed efficiently.
