# Simple Employee Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Employee Application form is used to collect information about employees in the company. This form helps to gather essential details such as first name, last name, email, phone number, job title, company, position, employment type, job start and end dates, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide a valid email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your job title in the Job Title field.
6. Enter the name of the company you work for in the Company field.
7. Enter your position within the company in the Position field.
8. Select your employment type (Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance) in the Employment Type field.
9. Enter the start date of your job in the Job Start Date field in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
10. Enter the end date of your job in the Job End Date field in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2023-02-28).
11. (Optional) Enter any additional notes regarding your job title in the Job Title Notes field.
12. (Optional) Enter any additional notes regarding the company in the Company Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address. This is an optional field, but it's recommended to be filled out for internal communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the company you work for.
* **Position** (`position`, `text`, optional): Enter your position within the company.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your employment type (Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance).
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of your job in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01). If you don't have a start date, please leave this field blank.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of your job in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2023-02-28). Please ensure that this field is filled out for current employees who are updating their information.
* **Job Title Notes** (`job_title_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes regarding your job title.
* **Company Notes** (`company_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes regarding the company.

## Tips

* Always ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
* If you are a new employee, please fill out all fields.
* If you are a current employee, please ensure that you have filled out the Job End Date field correctly.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative.
