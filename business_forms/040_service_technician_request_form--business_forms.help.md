# Service Technican Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Technican Request Form is designed to enable customers to submit their service requests in a structured and organized way. Please fill out this form to ensure that your service needs are captured accurately and communicated to our technical team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly to provide our technical team with all necessary information.
2. Select the correct service type from the list of available options.
3. Choose the assigned technician from the list of available options (if applicable).
4. Provide a detailed description of the service required.
5. Ensure that the customer name and contact number are accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the customer requesting the service.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, required): Enter your contact number to ensure our team can reach you for further clarification or updates.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of service you need from the provided options to ensure that our technical team is aware of the specific service required.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the service you need, including any specific requirements or constraints.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for the service, please enter it here. If not, our team will work with you to schedule a mutually convenient time.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, optional): If you have a specific time in mind for the service, please enter it here. If not, our team will work with you to schedule a mutually convenient time.
* **Technician Notes** (`technician_notes`, note, optional): If there is any additional information that you would like to share with the assigned technician, please enter it here.
* **Assigned Technician** (`assigned_technician`, select_one, optional): If you have a preferred technician, please select them from the list of available options. If not, our team will assign a suitable technician based on the service type and availability.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that our technical team has all necessary information to fulfill your service request.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to contact our support team.
- Our technical team will review and respond to your service request within a timely manner.
