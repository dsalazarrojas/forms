## Entrepreneur City Summit Speaker Information Form - Help Guide
### Purpose
The Entrepreneur City Summit Speaker Information Form is used to collect information from speakers who will be presenting at the summit. This form helps us better understand each speaker's details, presentation, and other relevant information. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure we can provide the best experience for both speakers and attendees.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by *) with your speaker details.
2. For fields with options, select the most relevant option for your presentation.
3. Make sure to proofread your submission before submitting to avoid any errors.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Details**
	+ <label> Speaker Details</label> (text, required): Please enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself as a speaker.
* **Bio**
	+ <label> Bio</label> (text, optional): Write a brief bio about yourself, including your professional background and experience.
* **Speaker Photo URL**
	+ <label> Speaker Photo URL</label> (text, optional): If you have a professional photo, please enter the URL of the photo. This will be displayed on the summit website and marketing materials.
* **Contact Email**
	+ <label> Contact Email</label> (email, optional): Enter your contact email address so that attendees can reach out to you.
* **Contact Phone**
	+ <label> Contact Phone</label> (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number so that attendees can reach out to you.
* **Social Facebook**
	+ <label> Social Facebook</label> (text, optional): Enter your Facebook profile URL so that attendees can connect with you on social media.
* **Social Instagram**
	+ <label> Social Instagram</label> (text, optional): Enter your Instagram profile URL so that attendees can connect with you on social media.
* **Social LinkedIn**
	+ <label> Social LinkedIn</label> (text, optional): Enter your LinkedIn profile URL so that attendees can connect with you on social media.
* **Bio Description**
	+ <label> Bio Description</label> (note, optional): This field is for any additional description of your bio.
* **Speaker Type**
	+ <label> Speaker Type</label> (select one, optional): Select the type of speaker you are (e.g. "Keynote", "Panelist", etc.).
* **Presentation Type**
	+ <label> Presentation Type</label> (select multiple, optional): Select the type of presentation you will be giving (e.g. "Talk", "Workshop", etc.).
* **Session Type**
	+ <label> Session Type</label> (select one, optional): Select the type of session you will be presenting (e.g. "Breakout", "Panel", etc.).
* **Session Time**
	+ <label> Session Time</label> (time, optional): Enter the time of your session.
* **Presentation Title**
	+ <label> Presentation Title</label> (text, optional): Enter the title of your presentation.
* **Presentation Abstract**
	+ <label> Presentation Abstract</label> (text, optional): Enter a brief abstract of your presentation.
* **Presentation File**
	+ <label> Presentation File</label> (text, optional): Enter the URL of your presentation file.
* **Additional Info**
	+ <label> Additional Info</label> (note, optional): Enter any additional information about your presentation.

### Tips

* Please proofread your submission before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to the summit organizers.
* Make sure to keep your speaker details up to date throughout the summit.
