# lease_process_modernization_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lease process modernization registration form is designed to collect information from participants interested in the modernization process. This form helps the initiative team to understand the participants' interests and needs, and to ensure that the participant has given their consent to proceed with the modernization process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Form Data" section to enter the initial details about the participant.
2. In the "Participant Interests" section, select all the interests that apply to the participant.
3. In the "Participant Consent" section, select "Yes" if the participant has given their consent to proceed with the modernization process.
4. In the "Contact Details" section, enter the participant's contact information.
5. In the "Participant Information" section, enter any additional information about the participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (id: 1): Enter the initial details about the participant, such as their name and contact information.
* **Participant Interests** (id: 2):
	+ Select all the interests that apply to the participant, such as "Yes" for "Yes" and "No" for "No".
* **Participant Consent** (id: 3):
	+ Select "Yes" if the participant has given their consent to proceed with the modernization process.
* **Contact Details** (id: 4): Enter the participant's contact information, such as their email and phone number.
* **Participant Information** (id: 5): Enter any additional information about the participant, such as their company name and role.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are representing the correct participant and their interests.
* The participant's consent is required to proceed with the modernization process.
* This form is not intended for participants who have already registered for the lease process modernization initiative.
