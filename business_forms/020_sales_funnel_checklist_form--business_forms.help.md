# Sales Funnel Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Funnel Checklist Form is designed to track and analyze the sales process of leads through various stages, from initial contact to conversion. This form helps sales teams monitor the progress of leads, identify potential issues, and optimize their sales strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the current lead status (Lead Status field) to indicate the current stage of the lead in the sales process.
2. Enter any follow-up tasks or notes (Follow Ups field) to record interactions with the lead.
3. Specify the lead's qualifications (Lead Qualifications field) by selecting relevant areas, such as Sales, Marketing, or Technical.
4. Choose the lead source (Lead Source field) to identify where the lead originated from (e.g., Social, Email, Website, Referral, or Phone).
5. Select the lead's current stage in the sales process (Lead Stage field).
6. Enter the frequency of follow-up tasks or interactions (Follow Up Frequency field) with the lead.
7. Record the date of the last follow-up task or interaction (Follow Up Date field).
8. Assign the lead to a specific sales owner (Lead Owner field) to track responsibility and progress.
9. Choose the current sales funnel step (Sales Funnel Step field) the lead is in.

* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`): Select the origin of the lead, e.g., Social, Email, Website, Referral, or Phone.
* **Lead Stage** (`lead_stage`): Select the current stage of the lead in the sales process, e.g., Awareness, Consideration, Decision, or Action.
* **Follow Up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`): Enter the frequency of follow-up tasks or interactions with the lead.
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`): Record the date of the last follow-up task or interaction with the lead.
* **Lead Owner** (`lead_owner`): Assign the lead to a specific sales owner to track responsibility and progress.

* Make sure to select the correct lead status to accurately track progress.
* Use the Follow Ups field for recording important interactions or tasks related to the lead.
* Be specific when selecting lead qualifications, source, stage, and sales funnel step to provide clear insights.
