# Team Building Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form aims to collect information about team building dynamics and effectiveness, focusing on key aspects such as communication, leadership, and team satisfaction. The survey is designed to be completed by team members to provide insights into their team's strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several sections, each addressing a specific aspect of team building.
2. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the options that best describe your experiences and perceptions.
4. Make sure to answer all required questions, as indicated by the "required" label.
