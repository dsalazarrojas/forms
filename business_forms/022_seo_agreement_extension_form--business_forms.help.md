# seo_agreement_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "seo_agreement_extension_form" is a form used to create or extend a search engine optimization agreement with a client. It helps to collect necessary information about the client and the extension request in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Client**: Enter the client's name and any other relevant information about them.
2. Choose the **extension type** that best describes the reason for the extension:
	* **Yes**: The agreement is being extended due to a valid reason.
	* **No**: The agreement is not being extended.
3. Enter the **contract term** (if applicable).
4. Enter the **contract start date** and **contract end date** (if applicable).
5. Provide a brief **reason for extension** if the agreement is being extended.
6. If the client approves the extension, select **client approval** accordingly:
	* **Yes**: The client has approved the extension.
	* **No**: The client has not approved the extension.
7. Enter any **reviewer notes** (additional information about the extension request).
8. Enter your **reviewer name** and **reviewer signature** (if you are reviewing and approving the extension).
9. Enter the **client name** and **client signature** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client**: Enter the name of the client who is involved in the SEO agreement.
* **Extension type**: Choose the reason for the extension (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Contract term**: Enter the term of the contract, if applicable.
* **Contract start date**: Enter the start date of the contract, if applicable.
* **Contract end date**: Enter the end date of the contract, if applicable.
* **Reason for extension**: Provide a brief explanation for the reason of extension.
* **Client approval**: Select if the client has approved the extension.
* **Reviewer note**: Enter any additional information about the extension request.
* **Reviewer name**: Enter your name if you are reviewing and approving the extension.
* **Reviewer signature**: Enter your signature if you are reviewing and approving the extension.
* **Client name**: Enter the name of the client, if applicable.
* **Client signature**: Enter the client's signature, if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review and proofread your submission before submitting.
* Ensure that the client and reviewer fields are accurately filled out.
* If you are unsure about the purpose or meaning of a field, consult with the relevant stakeholders or team members for guidance.
