# Marketplace Seller Onboarding Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for new sellers on a marketplace platform to provide accurate and truthful information about themselves and their products. It is used to onboard sellers efficiently and ensure they understand the platform's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your details accurately in the **seller_details** field.
2.  List your product categories of interest in the **product_categories** field.
3.  Confirm the accuracy of the information provided in the **consent** field.
4.  Select your agreement to the platform's terms and conditions in the **seller_agreement** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **seller_details**: This field is for entering your details (e.g., name, contact information). Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   **product_categories**: This field is for listing your product categories of interest. Select as many categories as necessary.
*   **consent**: Please confirm that all information provided is accurate and truthful. This ensures that the platform can trust your data and provide a smooth onboarding experience.
*   **seller_agreement**: This field asks you to agree or disagree to the platform's terms and conditions. Please review the agreement before making your selection.
