The purpose of this form is to collect information about tunnel accidents, including the date and time of the incident, the location and description of the incident, the ID of the tunnel involved, the type of incident, and the person reporting the incident. This form is used to gather accurate and detailed information to aid in investigations and decision-making processes. Before filling out this form, please ensure that the incident you are reporting has not already been reported or documented elsewhere.

This form is designed to be completed by individuals who are responsible for reporting accidents in tunnels. Please take the time to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, and make sure to answer all required fields.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start a new form.
2. Select the correct date and time of the incident by using the date and time picker or typing it in manually.
3. Enter a brief description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
4. Type in the ID of the tunnel involved in the incident in the "Tunnel ID" field.
5. Select the type of incident that occurred using the dropdown menu in the "Incident Type" field.
6. Enter the name or ID of the person reporting the incident in the "Incident Reporter" field.
7. Provide any additional details about the incident in the "Incident Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): The date on which the incident occurred. Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD or select it from the date picker.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): The location where the incident occurred. Enter a brief description of the location, including the tunnel ID if known.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): A brief description of what happened during the incident. Be as detailed and accurate as possible.
* **Tunnel ID** (`tunnel_id`, text, required): The ID of the tunnel involved in the incident. Enter the tunnel's ID if known.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required): The time of the incident. Enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM or select it from the time picker.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of incident that occurred. This will help in categorizing the incident for further analysis.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, required): The person reporting the incident. Enter their name or ID.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional): Additional details about the incident that may be helpful for investigation and analysis.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
* Make sure to save your progress as you fill out the form, as you can lose your work if you navigate away from the form.
