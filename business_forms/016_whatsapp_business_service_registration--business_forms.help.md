# WhatsApp Business Service Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist businesses in registering their services on WhatsApp, ensuring they can interact with customers and clients effectively. It's essential to provide accurate and truthful information to avoid any issues with your business account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **User Details**: Enter your name and other relevant details about yourself as the business owner or representative.
2.  **Permissions**: Select the permissions you'd like to have for your business service, such as 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your preferences.
3.  **Notification Preferences**: Indicate your preferences for receiving notifications, if any.
4.  **User Phone Numbers**: Provide your phone numbers, as applicable.
5.  **User Preferences**: Indicate your preferences for certain features or services.
6.  **User EMail**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
7.  **Business Name**: Enter your business name as it appears on the platform.
8.  **WhatsApp Business Account ID**: Enter your unique WhatsApp Business Account ID, if you have one.
9.  **Country**: Select your country of operation.
10.  **Region**: Choose your region of operation.
11.  **Language**: Select the language you're comfortable operating in.
12.  **Timezone**: Select your time zone of operation.
13.  **Timezone Preference**: Indicate your time zone preferences.
14.  **Business Type**: Choose your business type (e.g., product/service-based, etc.).
15.  **Business Category**: Select your category of operation (e.g., food & beverage, etc.).

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure you've selected the correct permissions and preferences for your business.
- Enter a valid email address for your User EMail field.
