# Supplier Payment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Payment Authorization Form is used to provide payment authorization to suppliers. This form is a requirement for processing payments to suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the payment method (ACH, Wire Transfer, or Check) that will be used for the payment.
2. Choose the payment terms (net 30, net 45, or net 60) that will be applied to the payment.
3. Enter the bank name and routing number associated with the payment method.
4. Select the payment status (pending, authorized, or denied) for the payment.
5. If necessary, enter any additional notes or comments about the payment.
6. Enter the email address and phone number of the supplier, if applicable.
7. Enter the name of the company, address, supervisor's name, and department, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company making the payment.
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date for which the payment is being made.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment to be used (ACH, Wire Transfer, or Check).
* **Payment Terms**: Select the payment terms that will be applied to the payment (net 30, net 45, or net 60).
* **Bank Name**: Enter the name of the bank associated with the payment method.
* **Routing Number**: Enter the routing number associated with the bank.
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of the payment (pending, authorized, or denied).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the payment.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the supplier, if applicable.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the supplier, if applicable.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company making the payment, if applicable.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the company making the payment, if applicable.
* **Supervisor's Name**: Enter the name of the supervisor, if applicable.
* **Department**: Enter the name of the department, if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct payment method and payment terms for the payment.
* Double-check the payment amount and bank information to ensure accuracy.
* Select the correct payment status to reflect the current status of the payment.
* Use the notes field to provide additional context or clarification about the payment, if necessary.
* If the supplier has provided an email address or phone number, be sure to enter it to facilitate communication.
