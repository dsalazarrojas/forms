# Executive Advisory Residency Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Advisory Residency Application form is designed to collect information from users applying to the Executive Advisory Residency program. This form will help us assess your qualifications and background to determine whether you are a good fit for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Full Name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
3. Attach your Resume in the "Resume" field.
4. Write a statement expressing your interest in the program in the "Interest Statement" field.
5. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter your City in the "City" field.
8. Enter your State in the "State" field.
9. Enter your Zip code in the "Zip" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Text, Required): Your full name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Your email address, where you can be reached by our team.
* **Resume** (Text, Required): Attach a copy of your resume, highlighting your relevant work experience and skills.
* **Interest Statement** (Text, Required): A statement explaining why you're interested in the Executive Advisory Residency program and how it will benefit you.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Your phone number, where our team can contact you.
* **Address** (Text, Required): Your physical address, including the street number and apartment number (if applicable).
* **City** (Text, Required): The city where you reside or work.
* **State** (Text, Required): The state where you reside or work.
* **Zip** (Text, Required): The zip code of your address.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your resume is in a suitable format and can be read by our team.
* Make sure your email and phone number are correct and up-to-date.
* The Interest Statement should be specific and clearly explain your motivations for applying to the program.
* Double-check your address, city, state, and zip code for accuracy.
* Please submit a valid attachment for your resume, preferably in a PDF or Word document format.
