# Investigator Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investigator Agreement Form is used to collect information from investigators about their agreement to participate in research or other business activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Investigator Agreement** (Note): This is a free-form text area where you can type any information you want to communicate to the investigator.
* **Responsibilities** (Text): Describe your expectations and responsibilities for the investigator's role.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Text): Enter any terms or conditions that apply to this investigation or agreement.
* **Contact Information** (Text): Provide your contact details, such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Investigator Agreement Form 5** (Select Multiple): This field requires you to select one or more of the following options:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Investigator Agreement Form 6** (Text): This field is intended for additional comments or notes.
* **Investigator Agreement Form 7** (Text): This field is for any other information you want to share with the investigator.
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date you expect to start or complete the investigation or activity.
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time you expect to start or complete the investigation or activity.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address, which will help us contact you for any updates or questions.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number, which will help us contact you for any updates or questions.
* **Investigator Agreement Form 12** (Select One): This field requires you to choose one of the following options:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Consent** (Text): Enter your consent for any activities or investigations. 

Note: All fields are free-form text areas, so you can enter any information you want.
