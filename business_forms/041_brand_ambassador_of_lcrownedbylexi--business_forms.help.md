# Brand Ambassador Of Lcrownedbylexi - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through each section of the form and explain each field to help you complete it successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To start, carefully read the form introduction above and make sure you're eligible to apply. If you're ready to proceed, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and fill out the required fields as instructed.
2. Answer truthfully to the questions, and provide accurate information about yourself and your social media presence.
3. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section 1: Welcome
* **-- Welcome to LCrownedbylexi Ambassador Program --**: This is a welcome message, and you can read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Message**: Please write a brief introduction or expression of interest in becoming an LCrownedbylexi ambassador. This will help us understand your motivation for applying.
* **Your Name**: This is a required field where you'll enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number where we can reach you.

### Section 2: Eligibility
* **Total Social Media Followers**: Enter your total social media follower count. Please make sure you have 1000 or more followers.
* **Your Follower Count Meets Our Minimum**: Choose "Yes, 1000 or more" if you have met the requirement. Otherwise, choose "No, less than 1000" or "Not sure" if you're unsure.
* **Instagram Handle**: Enter your Instagram username.
* **Instagram Followers**: Enter your current Instagram follower count.
* **Other Social Media Platforms**: If you have a presence on other platforms such as TikTok, Facebook, or YouTube, enter them here.

### Section 3: About
* **Tell Us About Yourself**: This is where you'll share a little about yourself, your background, and your interests.
* **Why LCrownedbylexi Resonates With You**: Share why you're drawn to our brand and what you think makes us unique.
* **Type of Content You Create**: Describe the type of content you create (e.g., blog posts, videos, social media posts, etc.).
* **Your Ideas for Promoting LCrownedbylexi**: Share your ideas on how you would represent the brand and promote our products or services.

### Section 4: Agreement
* **I Agree to Ambassador Terms and Will Represent LCrownedbylexi Professionally**: Check "I agree and apply" if you're committed to representing the brand as an ambassador. If not, choose "I do not agree."
