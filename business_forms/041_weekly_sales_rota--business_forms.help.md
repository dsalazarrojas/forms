# Weekly Sales Rota - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Sales Rota form is designed to help managers and sales leaders schedule team members and their sales leads for the week. This form allows you to set up a schedule that takes into account team members' availability, responsibilities, and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the following information:
	+ Weekly Sales Rota title (text box)
	+ Manager's name (text box)
	+ Week start date (date field)
	+ Week end date (date field)
* Select the sales team (check box) if you have a sales team associated with this schedule
* Fill in the sales leader's name and email (text boxes)
* Fill in the manager's phone number (text box)
* Select team leaders (check box) if any team leaders are associated with this schedule
* Select sales leads (check box) if any sales leads are associated with this schedule
* Select sales lead title (select one) if a sales lead title is required
* Select team members (check box) if any team members are associated with this schedule
* Select sales members (check box) if any sales members are associated with this schedule

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Weekly Sales Rota title (id: 1)**: Enter a title for the sales rota, e.g., "Weekly Sales Rota for Q1".
* **Manager (id: 2)**: Enter the name of the manager responsible for the sales team.
* **Week Start (id: 3)**: Select the start date of the week for which you want to schedule sales activities.
* **Week End (id: 4)**: Select the end date of the week for which you want to schedule sales activities.
* **Sales Team (id: 5)**: Check this box if your sales team is associated with this schedule.
* **Sales Leader Name (id: 6)**: Enter the name of the sales leader responsible for the sales team.
* **Email (id: 7)**: Enter the email address of the manager or sales leader.
* **Phone (id: 8)**: Enter the phone number of the manager or sales leader.
* **Team Manager Phone (id: 9)**: Enter the phone number of the team manager (if different from the manager's phone number).
* **Team Leaders (id: 10)**: Check this box if your team has team leaders associated with this schedule.
* **Sales Leads (id: 11)**: Check this box if your sales team has sales leads associated with this schedule.
* **Sales Lead (id: 12)**: Select whether a sales lead is required for this schedule.
* **Sales Lead Title (id: 13)**: Select the title of the sales lead (if required).
* **Team Members (id: 14)**: Check this box if your team has members associated with this schedule.
* **Sales Member (id: 15)**: Check this box if your sales team has sales members associated with this schedule.
