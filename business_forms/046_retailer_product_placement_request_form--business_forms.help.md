# Retailer Product Placement Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from retailers about their product placement requests. It helps our team process and review the requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the retail partner in the "Retailer Name" field.
2. Provide the name of the product being requested in the "Product Name" field.
3. Write a brief description of the product in the "Product Description" field.
4. Select the category of the product from the "Category Name" field.
5. Choose the placement area for the product from the "Placement Area" field.
6. Select the timing of the product placement from the "Timing" field.
7. Upload an image of the product in the "Product Image" field.
8. Enter the price of the product in the "Product Price" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retailer Name**: Enter the name of the retail partner submitting this request. This field is optional but helps our team identify the retailer.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product being requested. This field is required for accurate product identification.
* **Product Description**: Provide a brief description of the product, including its features and details. This field is optional but helps our team understand the product better.
* **Category Name**: Select the category of the product from the options provided. For example, if the product is a book, select "Book". If the product is a toy, select "Toy". This field is optional but helps our team prioritize the requests.
* **Placement Area**: Choose the placement area where the product should be placed. For example, select "In-store" for a physical product or "Online" for an e-commerce product. This field is optional but helps our team understand the product's distribution channel.
* **Timing**: Select the timing of the product placement. For example, select "Immediate" for urgent requests or "Scheduled" for future requests. This field is optional but helps our team prioritize the requests.
* **Product Image**: Upload an image of the product to help our team visualize it better. This field is optional but recommended for accurate product identification.
* **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product. This field is optional but helps our team understand the product's value.
