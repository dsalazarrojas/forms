# Stakeholder Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about stakeholders, including their name, email address, contact number, type, and company name. This form is intended to help us better understand the needs and requirements of our stakeholders, which may inform business decisions or interactions with them.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields as required. Fields marked as required are necessary to submit the form.
2. Double-check your input for accuracy, especially for fields such as email and contact number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Stakeholder Name
* **Stakeholder Name** (optional): This field is used to record the name of the individual associated with the stakeholder. You can enter the stakeholder's first and last name, or just one or the other. If the stakeholder is an individual, enter their name here. If you are unsure, just enter their name as you would normally refer to them.

### Email
* **Email** (optional): Enter the email address of the stakeholder, typically the email address they use for business or communication.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (optional): Enter the phone number of the stakeholder, including their area code or country code.

### Stakeholder Type
* **Stakeholder Type** (optional, select): Choose the category that best describes the relationship between our team and the stakeholder. This field helps us tailor our communication and interactions with them. Options include:
	+ Business Partner: A business partner or supplier that we work with.
	+ Customer: A customer or client that we have a relationship with.
	+ Employee: An employee of the company, either current or past.

### Company Name
* **Company Name** (optional): If the stakeholder is associated with a company, enter the name of that company here. This helps us understand the organization they are part of.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information for fields such as email and contact number to ensure we can get in touch with them effectively.
* If you are unsure which stakeholder type to choose, select the category that best describes their relationship with our team.
