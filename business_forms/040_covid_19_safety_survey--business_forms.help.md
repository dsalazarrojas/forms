# Covid 19 Safety Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Covid 19 Safety Survey is a form designed to collect information about your health and safety concerns related to the Covid 19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields. These are not mandatory but will help us get to know you better.
2.  Enter your Email ID and Phone Number in the text fields. These are not mandatory but will help us reach out to you if needed.
3.  In the Safety Concerns field, provide any health concerns you are experiencing related to Covid 19.
4.  For Medical History, please mention if you have any medical history that might be relevant to your Covid 19 safety.
5.  The Vaccination Status field is a critical one. Please select your current vaccination status from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand if you're at a higher risk of contracting Covid 19.
6.  In the Medical Condition field, mention any medical conditions you have that might affect your ability to fight off Covid 19.
7.  The Medication Side Effects field is not mandatory, but if you're experiencing any side effects from medications, please mention them.
8.  Please select your medical specialty from the dropdown menu. This is important for us to understand your medical background.
9.  If you have been hospitalized due to Covid 19, select True in the Hospitalization field.
10. If you're experiencing any symptoms, provide them in the Symptoms field.
11. The Treatment field is not mandatory, but if you've received any treatment for Covid 19, please mention it.
12. If you have any follow-up visits scheduled, please mention them in the Follow-up field.
13. For the Next Visit field, provide the date of your next visit.
14. Finally, if you have a doctor you're currently seeing, please mention their name in the Doctor Name field.
