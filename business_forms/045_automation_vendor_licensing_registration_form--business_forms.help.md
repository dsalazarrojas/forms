# Automation Vendor Licensing Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for automation vendors who want to register their company with us for licensing purposes. It helps us understand your company's details, products, and services to ensure a smooth and efficient partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting.
3. Review your information before submitting to ensure it's correct.

* **Company Name** (Required): Enter your company's legal name.
### 2. Company Website
* **Company Website** (Required): Enter your company's website URL.
### 3. Company Description
* **Company Description** (Required): Enter a brief description of your company.
### 4. Year Established
* **Year Established**: Enter the year your company was founded.
