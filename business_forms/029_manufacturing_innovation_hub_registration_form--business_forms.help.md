# Manufacturing Innovation Hub Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Innovation Hub Registration Form is designed to collect company and product information from manufacturers and innovators in the industry. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, navigate through each page, filling out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about your company and product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page (required)**: This is the first page of the form, where you will be asked to confirm that you are not duplicating an existing registration. If you are unsure, please contact the Manufacturing Innovation Hub administration for clarification.
* **Manufacturing Info (required)**: This field is for providing a brief description of your company or product, including the product or service offered. Be sure to keep this description concise and clear.
* **Company Info (required)**: This field is for providing detailed information about your company, including address, contact person, and contact information.
* **Contact Info (required)**: This field is for providing contact information for your company, including email, phone number, and website.
* **Product Info (optional)**: This field is for providing additional information about your product or service, including features, benefits, and any other relevant details.
* **Submit (required)**: Click this button to submit your registration form.
* **Confirm (required)**: Confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms (optional)**: Read through the terms and conditions of the Manufacturing Innovation Hub.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the Manufacturing Innovation Hub administration for clarification.
* You can always contact us for help and support at form@gic.mx.
