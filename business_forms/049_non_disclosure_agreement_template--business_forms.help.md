# Non Disclosure Agreement Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to generate a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) template for confidential information exchange between two parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the name of the company or organization providing the confidential information (Fondation Marie Claire Heureuse Felicite Bonheur Dessalines).
2. Enter the name of the person receiving the confidential information (Assignee Name).
3. Enter the email address of the person receiving the confidential information (Email).
4. Enter the phone number of the person receiving the confidential information (Phone).
5. Enter the date of the agreement (Date).
6. Enter the time of the agreement (Time).
7. Add any additional note or comment about the agreement (Note).
8. Enter the name of the person or organization providing the confidential information (Assigner Name).
9. Enter the title of the person or organization providing the confidential information (Assigner Title).
10. Enter the purpose of the agreement (Purpose).
11. Enter the terms of the confidentiality agreement (Confidentiality Agreement).
12. Enter the signature of the person receiving the confidential information.
