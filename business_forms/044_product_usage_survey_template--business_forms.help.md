# product_usage_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user feedback and information about their product usage. Please fill out this form to provide valuable insights that help us improve our product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
4. Enter the name of the product you are using in the "Product Name" field.
5. Describe your usage frequency of the product in the "Usage Frequency" field.
6. Rate your satisfaction with the product in the "Satisfaction" field (select one of the options).
7. Add any additional comments about your experience in the "Comments" field (optional).
8. Enter the product ID (a numeric identifier for the product) in the "Product ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address to help us contact you for further feedback.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you (if applicable).
* **Product Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the product you are using.
* **Usage Frequency** (text, optional): Describe how often you use the product, e.g., daily, weekly, rarely, etc.
* **Satisfaction** (select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the product, with options ranging from "Very Satisfied" to "Very Not Satisfied".
* **Comments** (note, optional): Share any additional thoughts or feedback about your experience with the product.
* **Product ID** (number, required): Enter the numeric product ID that corresponds to the product you are using.
