# 3D Sculpture Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 3D Sculpture Agreement Form is a template for clients and artists to document the terms and conditions of a 3D sculpture commission project. It ensures both parties are on the same page regarding scope, payment, and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you understand all sections and questions.
2.  Complete the form accurately and thoroughly, including all required fields.
3.  Verify your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Client full name, as it appears on official documents. (Required)
* **Client Email Address**: Client email address for contract and invoice delivery. (Required)
* **Client Phone Number**: Client phone number for communication and arrangement. (Optional)
* **Artist/Artist's Name**: Artist or sculptor's legal or business name. (Required)
* **Commission Title/Short Description**: Brief project title or description. (Required)
* **Detailed Project Description and Scope**: Describe the concept, finish, and any included services. (Required)
* **Primary Material**: Choose the main material for the sculpture. (Required, options: Bronze, Marble, Wood, Resin, Mixed media)
* **Estimated Finished Dimensions**: Provide the height, width, and depth of the sculpture in cm or inches. (Required)
* **Approximate Weight**: Provide the best estimate for shipping and installation planning. (Optional)
* **Estimated Start Date**: The date when the project is expected to start. (Required)
* **Estimated Completion Date**: The date when the project is expected to be completed, including lead time for curing and finishing. (Required)
* **Payment Terms**: Select the agreed payment schedule. (Required, options: 50% deposit, 50% on completion, Three instalments, Full payment on completion, Custom schedule)
* **Agreed Total Fee**: Enter the total fee in numeric currency without a symbol. (Required)
* **Delivery and Installation Responsibility**: Choose who arranges delivery and covers costs. (Required, options: Artist arranges and invoices, Client arranges, Shared/negotiable)
* **Additional Contract Terms or Conditions**: Include any extra rights, reproductions, or storage requirements. (Optional)
* **Client Printed Name (Contract Signature)**: Type your name to signify agreement to terms. (Required)
* **Client Signature Date**: Date you signed the contract. (Required)
* **Artist Notes/Special Considerations**: Internal notes or considerations for the artist. (Optional)

Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to [form contact information].
