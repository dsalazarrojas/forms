# Online Work From Home - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Work From Home form is designed to collect information from remote workers about their work arrangements, allowing management and HR to better understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "Contact Information" page.
2. Fill in your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Move on to the "Work Schedule" page, where you can specify your working hours and any time-off schedules.
4. On the "Work Preferences" page, let us know about your preferred communication channels and any specific needs or requirements.
5. On the "Date and Time" page, provide your available work hours and any specific dates or periods you are not available.
6. On the "Additional Information" page, use the space to provide any other relevant details about your work-from-home arrangement.
7. Finally, review the "Confirmations" page to ensure you have filled in all required information correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (contact_info_page, text, false):
	* This is the first page of the form, where you can provide your contact details. Please fill in your name, email, and phone number to help us get in touch with you.
- **Work Schedule** (work_schedule_page, text, false):
	* On this page, specify your working hours and any time-off schedules you have. This will help us plan our communication and meetings accordingly.
- **Work Preferences** (work_preferences_page, text, false):
	* Here, let us know about your preferred communication channels (e.g., email, phone, or messaging apps) and any specific needs or requirements you may have.
- **Date and Time** (date_time_page, text, false):
	* Provide your available work hours and any specific dates or periods you are not available. This will help us schedule meetings and communication with you efficiently.
- **Additional Information** (additional_info_page, text, false):
	* Use this space to provide any other relevant details about your work-from-home arrangement. This will help us better understand your situation and needs.
- **Confirmations** (confirmations_page, text, false):
	* Review the form to ensure you have filled in all required information correctly. Make sure to check your contact details, work schedule, and other preferences.

## Tips
* Please be as concise as possible when filling in each field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* This form is designed to be user-friendly, so feel free to ask for help if you need it.
