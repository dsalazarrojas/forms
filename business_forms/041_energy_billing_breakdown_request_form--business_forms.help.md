# Energy Billing Breakdown Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Billing Breakdown Request Form is designed to provide detailed information about energy consumption or billing inquiries. This form helps users submit their energy billing breakdown requests, allowing the energy provider or billing manager to address their concerns efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to understand its purpose and required fields.
2.  Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information about your energy consumption or billing inquiry.
3.  Ensure you complete all required fields (if any) and provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Billing Details** (`billing_details`, text, required): This field is used to enter your billing account number or any other relevant billing information.
*   **Account Questions** (`account_questions`, text, required): This field is where you can provide any specific questions or concerns you have about your account.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your energy billing breakdown request.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you want your energy billing breakdown request to be processed.
*   **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your energy billing breakdown request (if applicable).
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your energy billing breakdown request.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can call you about your energy billing breakdown request.
*   **Billing Inquiry** (`billing_inquiry`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options for your energy billing breakdown request:
    *   Option 1: Energy consumption breakdown.
    *   Option 2: Billing error or dispute.
    *   Option 3: Other (please specify).
*   **Multiple Billing Inquiries** (`billing_inquiry_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple billing inquiries for your energy billing breakdown request:
    *   Option 1: Energy consumption breakdown.
    *   Option 2: Billing error or dispute.
    *   Option 3: Other (please specify).
*   **Comment 1** (`comments_2`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your energy billing breakdown request.
*   **Comment 2** (`comments_3`, note, optional): This is another field for any additional comments or notes you might have.
*   **Comment 3** (`comments_4`, note, optional): This is another field for any additional comments or notes about your energy billing breakdown request.
