The monthly payment submission form is used to collect information for payments made by customers on a monthly basis. This form can be used to track and record payments received from customers for accounting and financial purposes. When filling out the form, please ensure that you are submitting the correct information for the relevant month.

The form is divided into several sections, each corresponding to a specific field.

## monthly_payment_submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
The monthly payment submission form is used to collect and record payments from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the submission date for the payment from the calendar picker.
2. Enter the payment amount.
3. Select the payment method used for the transaction.
4. Enter the customer ID.
5. Enter the customer name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, Date, Required: false): Enter the date the payment was submitted.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, Number, Required: false): Enter the amount of the payment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, Required: false): Select the method used for the payment (Bank Draft, Check, or Credit Card).
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, Number, Required: false): Enter the customer's ID number.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Required: false): Enter the customer's name.

**Tips**

* Double-check that the submission date is correct to ensure that the payment is recorded in the correct month.
* Be accurate when entering the customer ID and name to ensure that the payment is linked to the correct customer.
* If a payment method is not selected, the payment will not be recorded correctly.

Note: The form requires minimal information for simplicity and ease of use.
