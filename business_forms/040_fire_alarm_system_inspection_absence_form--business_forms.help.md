# Fire Alarm System Inspection Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the absence of a fire alarm system inspection and provide information on the reasons for the absence and other relevant details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the start date of the absence of the inspection.
2. Enter the reason for the absence (optional, but recommended if applicable).
3. Enter the start date of the absence (if applicable).
4. Enter the start time of the absence (if applicable).
5. Enter the duration of the absence (in hours).
6. Select the type of system that was absent from inspection (panel, addressable, conduit, or combination).
7. Enter the contact person's name and email address (if applicable).
8. Enter the contact person's phone number (if applicable).
9. Enter the location of the absence.
10. Indicate whether a fire alarm system inspection certificate was issued (yes or no).
11. Enter any comments or notes on the absence.
12. Indicate whether a notification will be sent to the contact person (yes or no).
13. Enter any additional comments or notes (optional).
14. Select the frequency of inspections for the system (daily, weekly, monthly).
15. Enter the how often the system inspections are performed (as text).
16. Enter the unit of measurement for the inspection frequency (hours, minutes, seconds).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Date**: Enter the date from which the fire alarm system inspection was absent.
* **Reason for Absence**: Enter the reason why the inspection was not performed (e.g., system maintenance, equipment failure, etc.) (Optional).
* **Absence Start Date**: Enter the date from which the absence of the inspection started (if applicable).
* **Absence Start Time**: Enter the time from which the absence of the inspection started (if applicable).
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of the absence (in hours).
* **System Type**: Select the type of system that was absent from inspection (panel, addressable, conduit, or combination).
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and email of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Absence Location**: Enter the location where the absence of the inspection was observed.
* **Fire Alarm System Inspection Certificate**: Indicate whether a fire alarm system inspection certificate was issued (yes or no).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes on the absence (optional).
* **Notify**: Indicate whether a notification will be sent to the contact person (yes or no).
* **System Inspection Frequency**: Select the frequency of inspections for the system (daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Inspection Frequency How Often**: Enter the how often the system inspections are performed (as text).
* **Inspection Frequency Unit**: Enter the unit of measurement for the inspection frequency (hours, minutes, seconds).
