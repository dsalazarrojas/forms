# Sales Commission Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by employees to request adjustments to their sales commissions. The form helps to gather the necessary information to review and process commission requests in a timely and accurate manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit Request" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* The reason for the commission adjustment request (Field 3: "reason").
	* The amount of the adjustment (Field 2: "request_amount").
	* The date of the request (Field 4: "request_date").
	* The employee's name (Field 5: "employee_name").
3. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Commission Request** (Field 1: "sales_commission_request"): A brief description of the commission adjustment request.
* **Request Amount** (Field 2: "request_amount"): The amount of the commission adjustment being requested. Please enter a numerical value.
* **Reason** (Field 3: "reason"): A detailed explanation of the reason for the commission adjustment request.
* **Request Date** (Field 4: "request_date"): The date of the commission adjustment request. Please enter a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employee Name** (Field 5: "employee_name"): The name of the employee requesting the commission adjustment.
