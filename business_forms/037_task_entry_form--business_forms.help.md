# Task Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Task Entry Form is a tool used to record and track tasks and activities within the organization. It is designed to provide a centralized platform for users to input and manage their tasks, including their objectives, goals, start and end dates, descriptions, assigned tools, categories, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Task Entry Form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the required and optional fields.
2. Fill in the `title` field with a brief description of the task.
3. Enter the `objective` of the task, which should be a clear and concise statement of what needs to be accomplished.
4. Add a `goal` that outlines the desired outcome or result of the task.
5. Enter the `start_date` and `end_date` fields to provide a timeline for the task.
6. Add a detailed `description` of the task, including any relevant background information or context.
7. Select the `assigned_tool` used for the task from the provided options (chatjimmy, other).
8. Choose the relevant `category` from the provided options (Business Forms, Other).
9. Enter the `form_id` and `form_version` fields if applicable, which are used for internal tracking and reference purposes.
10. Enter the `created_at` field if applicable, which records the date and time the task was created.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **title** (`title`, text, optional): A brief description of the task.
* **objective** (`objective`, text, optional): A clear and concise statement of what needs to be accomplished.
* **goal** (`goal`, text, optional): The desired outcome or result of the task.
* **start_date** (`start_date`, date, optional): The date when the task is scheduled to start.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, date, optional): The date when the task is scheduled to end.
* **description** (`description`, text, optional): A detailed description of the task, including any relevant background information or context.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): The tool assigned to perform the task (chatjimmy, other).
* **category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): The category under which the task falls (Business Forms, Other).
* **form_id** (`form_id`, number, optional): An internal tracking number used for reference purposes.
* **form_version** (`form_version`, number, optional): The version of the form used for tracking changes.
* **created_at** (`created_at`, time, optional): The date and time the task was created.

**Tips**

* Be as detailed as possible when completing the `description` field to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
* Ensure that the `assigned_tool` and `category` fields are accurate and relevant to the task.
* Use the `form_id` and `form_version` fields only if required, as they are used for internal tracking purposes.
* Double-check that the `start_date` and `end_date` fields are correct to avoid scheduling conflicts.
