# Pet Care Service Partnership Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for organizations interested in partnering with our pet care service. It will help us gather necessary information about your organization and your partnership request, which will be reviewed by our team. Please fill in the form accurately and completely to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Name**.
2. Enter your **Phone number** and **Email**.
3. Select your **Service Type**.
4. Choose the **Location(s)** we will be providing our service in.
5. Select the type(s) of **Partnership** you are interested in.
6. Add any **Notes** about your partnership request.
7. Select the **Tool** we will be using to create the output file.
8. Select the **File** type you would like the output to be.
9. Select the **Category** of your partnership request.
10. Select the **Category ID** of your partnership request.
11. Enter the **Category Name** of your partnership request.
12. Enter the **Form Category** of your partnership request.
13. Enter a **Description** of your partnership request.
14. Enter a **Form Description** of your partnership request.
15. Enter the **Form Category ID** of your partnership request.
16. Enter the **Form Category Name** of your partnership request.
17. Enter the **Assigned Tool ID** of your partnership request.
18. Enter the **Form ID** of your partnership request.
19. Enter the **Output File ID** of your partnership request.
20. Enter the path of the **Output Path** of your partnership request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: A text field where you should enter the name of your organization.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your organization's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your organization's email for communication.
* **Service Type**: Select one from the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Service Location**: Select one or multiple from the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Partnership Type**: Select one or multiple from the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about your partnership request.
* **Tool**: Select the tool we will use to create the output file: chatjimmy, other tool.
* **File Type**: Select the type of file you would like the output to be in: 026 pet care service partnership request form -- business forms, other file.
* **Category**: Select the category of your partnership request: business forms, other category.
* **Category ID**: Select the category ID of your partnership request: 8891, other id.
* **Category Name**: Enter the category name of your partnership request.
* **Form Category**: Enter the form category of your partnership request.
* **Description**: Enter a description of your partnership request.
* **Form Description**: Enter a description of the form.
* **Form Category ID**: Enter the form category ID of your partnership request.
* **Form Category Name**: Enter the form category name of your partnership request.
* **Assigned Tool ID**: Enter the assigned tool ID of your partnership request.
* **Form ID**: Enter the form ID of your partnership request.
* **Output File ID**: Enter the output file ID of your partnership request.
* **Output Path**: Enter the path of the output file.
