# sponsor_sign_up_and_payment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from sponsors who want to support a business or organization financially.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and email address in the "First Name" and "Email" fields.
2. Enter the amount you are willing to sponsor in the "Amount" field.
3. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
4. Enter any additional payment terms or conditions in the "Payment Terms" field.
5. Choose the type of sponsor you are (Sponsor, Co-Sponsor, or Donor) in the "Sponsor Details" field.
6. Enter your organization's name in the "Organization" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
8. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
9. Enter your city in the "City" field.
10. Enter your state in the "State" field.
11. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
12. Enter your country in the "Country" field.
13. Enter your tax ID (if applicable) in the "Tax ID" field.
14. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Amount** (number, required): Enter the amount you are willing to sponsor.
* **Payment Method** (select_multiple, required): Select one or more payment methods (Cash, Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, Other).
* **Payment Terms** (note, optional): Enter any additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Sponsor Details** (select_one, required): Choose the type of sponsor you are (Sponsor, Co-Sponsor, Donor).
* **Organization** (text, optional): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **City** (text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (text, optional): Enter your state or region.
* **Zip** (text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (text, optional): Enter your country of residence.
* **Tax ID** (text, optional): Enter your tax ID (if applicable).
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
