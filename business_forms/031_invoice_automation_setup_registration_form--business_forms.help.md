# Invoice Automation Setup Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Automation Setup Registration Form is used to create and manage automated invoices for your business. It's crucial to fill out this form correctly to ensure that invoices are generated and processed accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your First Name.
2.  Enter your Last Name.
3.  Enter your Email address.
4.  Enter your Business Name.
5.  Enter your Business Address.
6.  Enter your Business Phone number.
7.  Enter your Billing Address.
8.  Select your payment terms from the list provided.
9.  If applicable, add any additional Notes.
10.  Assign a Tool for the invoice.
11.  Choose the Category for the invoice.
12.  (Optional) Add a Description for the invoice.
13.  (Optional) Add a Form ID if you have one.
14.  (Optional) Specify the Output File for the invoice.
15.  (Optional) Set a Form Title.
16.  (Optional) Select the Form Category.
17.  (Optional) Set the date the invoice was Submitted.
18.  (Optional) Select who Submitted the invoice.
19.  (Optional) Set the date the invoice was Created.
20.  (Optional) Select who Created the invoice.
21.  (Optional) Set the date the invoice was Last Updated.
22.  (Optional) Select who Last Updated the invoice.
23.  (Optional) Set the date the invoice was Deleted.
24.  (Optional) Select who Deleted the invoice.
25.  Set the Status of the invoice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Business Name** (4, text, required): Enter your business name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Business Address** (5, text, required): Enter your business address as it appears on your official documents.
* **Business Phone** (6, text, required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Billing Address** (7, text, required): Enter the address where you receive bills or invoices.
* **Payment Terms** (8, select_multiple, required): Select the payment terms that apply to the invoice from the list provided.
* **Notes** (9, note, optional): Add any additional information about the invoice.
* **Assigned Tool** (10, select_one, required): Choose a tool for the invoice.
* **Category** (11, select_one, required): Choose a category for the invoice.
* **Description** (12, text, optional): Add a description for the invoice if needed.
* **Form ID** (13, text, optional): If you have an existing Form ID, enter it here.
* **Output File** (14, text, optional): Specify the output file for the invoice.
* **Form Title** (15, text, optional): Set a title for the form.
* **Form Category** (16, text, optional): Set the category for the form.
* **Submitted At** (17, date, optional): Set the date the invoice was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (18, select_one, optional): Select who submitted the invoice.
* **Created At** (19, date, optional): Set the date the invoice was created.
* **Created By** (20, select_one, optional): Select who created the invoice.
* **Updated At** (21, date, optional): Set the date the invoice was last updated.
* **Updated By** (22, select_one, optional): Select who last updated the invoice.
* **Deleted At** (23, date, optional): Set the date the invoice was deleted.
* **Deleted By** (24, select_one, optional): Select who deleted the invoice.
* **Status** (25, select_one, optional): Set the status of the invoice.
