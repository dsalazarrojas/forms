# Lease Clause Clarification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lease Clause Clarification Request Form is used to submit requests for further clarification on specific lease clauses from the landlord or tenant. This form helps to streamline communication and reduce potential disputes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Provide your name and contact information.
* Specify the type of clarification you are requesting (from Landlord, Tenant, or Other).
* Indicate the date and time you would like to clarify the lease clause.
* Clearly describe the lease clause for which you are requesting clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lease Clause Clarification Request Form** (`lease_clause_clarification_request_form`): This is the title of the form, which you cannot edit.
* **Leaseholder Name** (`landlord_name`): A text field to enter the name of the landlord from whom you are requesting clarification.
* **Your Name** (`tenant_name`): A text field to enter your name, as the person submitting the clarification request.
* **Clarification Request** (`clarification_request`): A text field to enter the specific clarification you are requesting regarding the lease clause.
* **Lease Term** (`lease_term`): A text field to enter the specific lease term or clause that requires clarification.
* **Clarification Needed From** (`clarification_needed`): A dropdown menu to select whether the clarification is requested from the Landlord, Tenant, or Other.
* **Request Date** (`clarification_request_date`): A date field to enter the date you would like to submit the clarification request.
* **Request Time** (`clarification_request_time`): A time field to enter the specific time you would like to submit the clarification request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are submitting a new request and not duplicating an existing one.
* Clearly and concisely describe the lease clause for which you are requesting clarification.
* Be specific about the type of clarification you are requesting (Landlord, Tenant, or Other).
