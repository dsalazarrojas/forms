# Organizational Design Assessment Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about organizational design assessments. The form is intended to gather essential details about the organization, contact person, and the challenge or problem being assessed. The collected data will be used to create a comprehensive assessment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the organization's name and contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Describe the challenge or problem being assessed.
3. Specify the goal of the assessment.
4. Provide the requested assessment date and deadline (if applicable).
5. Select the organization's size, industry, and challenge level.
6. Indicate the challenge type, priority, and urgency.
7. Specify the challenge location and scope.
8. Identify the contact person's role and department (if applicable).
9. Describe the challenge impact and objective.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization name** (Organization name, text, required): Enter the name of the organization for which the assessment is being conducted.
* **Contact name** (Contact name, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the assessment.
* **Contact email** (Contact email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Contact phone** (Contact phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Challenge description** (Challenge description, text, required): Describe the challenge or problem being assessed.
* **Goal description** (Goal description, text, required): Specify the goal of the assessment.
* **Assessment request date** (Assessment request date, date, required): Enter the date the assessment is requested for.
* **Assessment deadline** (Assessment deadline, date, optional): Enter the deadline for the assessment (if applicable).
* **Contact notes** (Contact notes, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the contact person.
* **Organization size** (Organization size, select_one, optional): Select the size of the organization (Small, Medium, Large, or Extra large).
* **Industry** (Industry, text, optional): Specify the industry or domain of the organization.
* **Challenge level** (Challenge level, select_one, optional): Select the level of the challenge (Low, Medium, High).
* **Priority** (Priority, select_one, optional): Select the priority of the challenge (Low, Medium, High).
* **Challenge type** (Challenge type, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of challenge (Strategic, Operational, Tactical).
* **Contact title** (Contact title, text, optional): Enter the title of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Challenge location** (Challenge location, text, optional): Specify the location of the challenge (if applicable).
* **Assessment frequency** (Assessment frequency, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of the assessment (Once, Ongoing, Regularly).
* **Challenge scope** (Challenge scope, select_multiple, optional): Select the scope of the challenge (Local, Global, Regional).
* **Contact department** (Contact department, text, optional): Enter the department of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Challenge urgency** (Challenge urgency, select_one, optional): Select the urgency of the challenge (Low, Medium, High).
* **Challenge priority** (Challenge priority, select_multiple, optional): Select the priority of the challenge (Strategic, Operational, Tactical).
* **Contact location** (Contact location, text, optional): Enter the location of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Assessment objective** (Assessment objective, text, optional): Describe the objective of the assessment.
* **Contact role** (Contact role, text, optional): Enter the role of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Challenge impact** (Challenge impact, text, optional): Describe the impact of the challenge.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
