# Mary Kay Beauty Profile By Julie Potts - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about employees or consultants working with Mary Kay Beauty. It is used to create a comprehensive profile for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field (format: `example@example.com`).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Type your company or business name in the "Company" field.
6. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
7. Provide your department in the "Department" field.
8. Enter a brief job description in the "Job Description" field.
9. Select your job type (Yes/No) in the "Job Type" field.
10. Enter the number of years you have worked in the "Years Worked" field.
11. Type the number of hours you work in a week in the "Hours Worked" field.
12. (Optional) Select the types of jobs you have done in the "Job Type" field (Yes/No).
13. Enter your date of hire or start date in the "Date Hired" field (format: `YYYY-MM-DD`).
14. Enter the amount of time you work in the "Time Worked" field (format: `HH:MM`).
15. Enter your date of birth in the "Birthday" field (format: `YYYY-MM-DD`).
16. Enter your anniversary date (if applicable) in the "Anniversary Date" field (format: `YYYY-MM-DD`).
17. Leave a note for your profile in the "Note" field.
18. Leave any additional comments for your consultant in the "Consultant Note" field.
19. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
