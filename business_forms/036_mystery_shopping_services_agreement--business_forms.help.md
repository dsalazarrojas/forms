# Mystery Shopping Services Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information and agreement terms from customers for mystery shopping services. It is intended for customers to provide details about themselves, their expectations, and the services they require.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill out each field with the required information, making sure to provide as much detail as possible.
3. Double-check that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): This field is where you can provide your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Assignment Terms** (`assignment_terms`, text, required: false): Here, you can describe the specific services you are looking for and any expectations you have regarding the assignment.
* **Confidentiality Expectations** (`confidentiality_expectations`, text, required: false): Please outline your expectations for maintaining confidentiality and any sensitive information that needs to be handled carefully.
* **Approval Process** (`approval_process`, text, required: false): Describe how you would like the approval process to be handled for your services, such as deadlines and communication methods.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, text, required: false): In this field, provide details on how you would like to be serviced, including any specific requests or preferences.
* **Review Process** (`review_process`, text, required: false): Outline the process you would like for reviewing the services provided, including any requirements or expectations.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required: false): This section is where you can discuss payment terms, conditions, and any financial expectations.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Finally, ensure you provide your contact information so that the service provider can get in touch with you for any further questions or concerns.

## Tips
* Be sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the service provider.
* The more detailed and specific you are in your answers, the better the service provider can tailor the services to your needs.
