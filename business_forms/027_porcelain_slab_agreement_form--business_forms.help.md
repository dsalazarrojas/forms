# Porcelain Slab Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Porcelain Slab Agreement Form is designed to gather crucial details about the delivery and agreement of porcelain slabs with customers. This includes general information, contact details, delivery dates and times, terms and conditions, signature, delivery addresses, and other essential data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section with the necessary details.
2. Provide contact information for communication and contact details.
3. Specify the delivery date and time.
4. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
5. Sign the form to acknowledge agreement.
6. Enter the delivery address and other relevant details.
7. Leave any customer comments or questions you may have.
8. Fill in any additional customer details, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter any general information related to the delivery.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide contact details for communication.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, text, optional): Specify the date of delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, text, optional): Specify the time of delivery.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign to acknowledge agreement.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter the delivery address.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, optional): Leave any customer comments or questions.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter customer name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, text, optional): Enter customer email.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter customer phone number.
