# Brand Integrity Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brand Integrity Audit Form is a tool designed to assess and evaluate a company's brand integrity across various aspects such as visual branding, voice and messaging, values, and operational practices. It helps identify areas of improvement and provides suggestions for enhancing brand integrity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select your role in the audit from the drop-down menu.
2. Fill in your name if you are an external auditor (optional).
3. Assess the brand's visual consistency across all platforms by selecting one of the options.
4. Evaluate the brand's voice and messaging consistency by selecting one of the options.
5. Choose the level of agreement or disagreement with the brand's values.
6. Rate the brand's ethical integrity on a scale from Very High to Very Low.
7. Identify areas where the brand did not meet its promises and suggest improvements.
8. Select all areas that need improvement in brand integrity.
9. Provide suggestions for enhancing brand integrity.
10. Finally, provide your verification as the auditor and select the date of the audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Role in the Audit**: Please select your role in the audit from the drop-down menu to ensure your response is relevant to your perspective.
* **Name (Optional)**: If you are an external auditor, please enter your name as a verification step.
* **Our visual branding (logo, colors, fonts) is consistent across all platforms**: This question evaluates the uniformity of the brand's visual identity across various touchpoints.
* **Our brand voice and messaging are consistent in all communications**: This question checks if the brand's tone and language are consistent across all messages and interactions.
* **Our actions as a business consistently align with our stated brand values**: This question assesses the brand's adherence to its values and principles.
* **How would you rate the brand's ethical integrity?**: This question rates the brand's ethics and accountability on a scale from Very High to Very Low.
* **Describe any instance where the brand did not meet its promises**: Provide specific examples of situations where the brand failed to fulfill its commitments.
* **Which areas need the most improvement in brand integrity**: Select all areas that you believe require improvement.
* **Suggestions for improving brand integrity**: Provide concrete suggestions for enhancing brand integrity.
* **Auditor Signature / Verification**: Enter your name as a verification step, and select the date of the audit.
