# customer_reward_replacement_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a replacement for a customer reward, which is a promotional item given to customers for their loyalty or purchases.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "Customer Reward Details" field with the description of the reward item you are requesting a replacement for.
2. Provide your "Customer Info" in the relevant fields, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Select the "Reward Status" of the item from the drop-down menu, indicating if it is Lost, Damaged, or Stolen.
4. Explain the "Reason For Replacement" in the text field, including any relevant details about the issue with the item.
5. Sign your "Customer Signature" to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
6. Provide a copy of your "Reward Image" if applicable.
7. Scan and attach a copy of your "Customer Signature Image" if required.
8. Enter your "Customer Email" for contact purposes.
9. Enter your "Customer Phone" number for contact purposes.
10. Provide your "Customer Address" for contact and delivery purposes.
11. Provide any "Business Info" relevant to the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Reward Details** (`customer_reward_details`, `text`, required/optional): A description of the reward item you are requesting a replacement for.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field includes the customer's name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Reward Status** (`reward_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the reward item from the drop-down menu:
	+ Lost: The item has been misplaced or lost.
	+ Damaged: The item is in a damaged or broken state.
	+ Stolen: The item has been taken or stolen from the customer.
* **Reason For Replacement** (`reason_for_replacement`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed explanation of the reason for the replacement request.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign your name to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Reward Image** (`reward_image`, `text`, required/optional): A copy of the reward item's image, if applicable.
* **Customer Signature Image** (`customer_signature_image`, `text`, required/optional): A scan of your signature, if required.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your address for contact and delivery purposes.
* **Business Info** (`business_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any relevant business information related to the request.
