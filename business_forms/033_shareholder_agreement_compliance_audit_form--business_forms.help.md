# Shareholder Agreement Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to ensure that shareholders are in compliance with certain agreements or regulations. It collects information about the shareholder's agreement date, contact details, and compliance status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Agreement Date**, which is the date the shareholder entered into their agreement.
2. Provide the **Shareholder Name**, which is the name of the shareholder.
3. Enter the **Shareholder Address**, which is the address of the shareholder.
4. Enter the **Shareholder Contact Number**, which is the phone number of the shareholder.
5. Enter the **Shareholder Email**, which is the email address of the shareholder.
6. Enter the **Shareholder Number of Shares Owned**, which is the number of shares owned by the shareholder.
7. For **Compliance Issues**, select all that apply:
	* Yes
	* No
8. Describe any **Compliance Description** if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required/optional): The date the shareholder entered into their agreement.
* **Shareholder Name** (`shareholder_name`, text, optional): The name of the shareholder.
* **Shareholder Address** (`shareholder_address`, text, optional): The address of the shareholder.
* **Shareholder Contact Number** (`shareholder_contact_number`, text, optional): The phone number of the shareholder.
* **Shareholder Email** (`shareholder_email`, email, optional): The email address of the shareholder.
* **Shareholder Number of Shares Owned** (`shareholder_number_of_shares_owned`, number, optional): The number of shares owned by the shareholder.
* **Compliance Issues** (`compliance_issues`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Compliance Description** (`compliance_description`, text, optional): A description of any compliance issues.
