# Departmental SWOT Analysis Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate departmental SWOT analysis. SWOT stands for "Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats," which are essential factors in business strategy development. The form helps the team to assess the internal and external factors affecting their department, providing valuable insights for strategic planning and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your team or department in the "Team name" field.
2. Provide the location of your team or department in the "Team location" field.
3. In the "Internal Strengths" and "Internal Weaknesses" sections, select up to 5 internal strengths and weaknesses, respectively, that are specific to your team or department.
	* Internal strengths: Identify the internal attributes, such as skills, knowledge, or resources, that contribute to your team's success.
	* Internal weaknesses: Identify the internal attributes that hinder your team's success.
4. In the "External Strengths" and "External Weaknesses" sections, select up to 5 external strengths and weaknesses, respectively, that affect your team or department.
	* External strengths: Identify external factors, such as market trends, competitor advantages, or government regulations, that contribute to your team's success.
	* External weaknesses: Identify external factors that hinder your team's success.
5. In the "Opportunities" and "Threats" sections, select up to 5 opportunities and threats, respectively, that affect your team or department.
	* Opportunities: Identify external factors that could benefit your team or department.
	* Threats: Identify external factors that could harm your team or department.
6. In the "SWOT Insights" section, provide 1-2 short paragraphs describing the team's SWOT analysis results.
7. List up to 5 team leaders responsible for departmental SWOT analysis in the "Team Leaders" field.
8. Enter the current date in the "Date" field.
9. Enter the time the form was completed in the "Time" field.
10. Enter the email address of the person completing the form in the "Email" field.

## Tips

* Be specific and concise when describing internal and external strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
* Provide detailed explanations for each selected item in the "SWOT Insights" section.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review and proofread your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and clarity.
