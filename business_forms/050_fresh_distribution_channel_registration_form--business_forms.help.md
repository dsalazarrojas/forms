# Fresh Distribution Channel Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for partners to register themselves in our distribution channel. It's a one-time registration process that helps us to get in touch with you and keep you updated about our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Partner Name** in the "Partner Name" field. This is the name of your company or the name under which you are registered.
2. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This is how we will contact you for any updates or promotions.
3. If you have a **Website**, enter it in the "Website" field. This is optional, but it helps us to know more about your online presence.
4. Fill in your **Address** in the "Address" field. This is required for us to know your location and send any shipments or updates.
5. Select your **Partner Type** from the dropdown menu. This helps us to understand the type of partner you are (e.g. Amazon, Walmart, etc.).
6. Enter the name of the **Contact Person** who will be our primary contact for communication.
7. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field. This is how we will contact you for urgent matters or updates.
8. You may add any **Note** about your partnership or requirements in the "Note" field. This is optional.
9. Fill in the **Partner Since** field with the year you have been working with us.
10. Fill in the **Partner Since Date** field with the date you started working with us.
11. Fill in the **Partner Since Month** and **Partner Since Day** fields with the month and day you started working with us.
12. If you have the exact **Partner Since Time**, fill it in. This is optional but helps us to understand your exact start time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Name** (text, required): This is the name of your company or the name under which you are registered.
* **Email** (email, optional): This is how we will contact you for any updates or promotions.
* **Website** (text, optional): This is optional, but it helps us to know more about your online presence.
* **Address** (text, required): This is required for us to know your location and send any shipments or updates.
* **Partner Type** (select_one, optional): This helps us to understand the type of partner you are (e.g. Amazon, Walmart, etc.).
* **Contact Person** (text, optional): This is the name of the person who will be our primary contact for communication.
* **Phone** (text, required): This is how we will contact you for urgent matters or updates.
* **Note** (note, optional): You may add any note about your partnership or requirements.
* **Partner Since** (date, optional): This is the year you have been working with us.
* **Partner Since Date** (date, optional): This is the date you started working with us.
* **Partner Since Month** (number, optional): This is the month you started working with us.
* **Partner Since Day** (number, optional): This is the day you started working with us.
* **Partner Since Time** (time, optional): This is the exact time you started working with us.
