# Security Supplier Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Supplier Evaluation Form is used to assess the security posture of a potential supplier, evaluating their ability to handle sensitive data, meet regulatory requirements, and provide secure communication channels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct options for each field based on your assessment of the supplier's capabilities.
2. Answer each field honestly and thoroughly, providing specific examples or explanations when necessary.
3. Review your answers carefully to ensure they accurately reflect your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **security_supplier_evaluation_form_1_label (Text)**: Enter a brief description or comment about the supplier's security posture.
* **evaluation_form_2_label (Number)**: Rate the supplier's evaluation (1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent).
* **potential_supplier_3_label (Select One)**: Choose the option that best describes the supplier's potential (A, B, C).
* **security_supplier_4_label (Select Multiple)**: Select all the options that apply to the supplier's security posture (A, B, C).
* **future_business_5_label (Text)**: Describe the potential business benefits or opportunities of working with this supplier.
* **supplier_6_label (Select Multiple)**: Choose all the options that apply to the supplier's capabilities (A, B, C).
* **evaluation_7_label (Date)**: Enter the date of your evaluation (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **security_8_label (Select One)**: Choose the level of security risk associated with this supplier (A, B, C).
* **future_9_label (Time)**: Enter the future date or deadline for the evaluation (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **business_10_label (Text)**: Describe the future business benefits or opportunities of working with this supplier.
* **evaluation_11_label (Select Multiple)**: Select all the options that apply to the supplier's security posture (A, B, C).
* **security_12_label (Text)**: Describe the supplier's security posture or concerns.
