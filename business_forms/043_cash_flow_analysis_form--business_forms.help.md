## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Analysis Header (1)**: A brief introduction to the analysis.
*   **Analysis ID Number (2)**: A unique number that helps identify the analysis for future reference.
*   **Analysis Date (3)**: The date the analysis was completed.
*   **Business Information (4)**: A brief description of the company.
*   **Business Type (6)**: Select the type of business you operate (e.g., retail, manufacturing, services, etc.).
*   **Fiscal Year (7)**: Enter the year being analyzed.
*   **Analysis Period (8)**: Select the time frame for the analysis (e.g., monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, annually, or custom period).
*   **Period Start Date (9)**: The beginning date of the analysis period.
*   **Period End Date (10)**: The ending date of the analysis period.
*   **Opening Cash Balance (11)**: The starting cash balance for the analysis.
*   **Cash Inflows (12)**: This section is for entering money coming into the business, such as sales, accounts receivable collected, interest received, etc.
*   **Operating Expenses (24)**: These are the day-to-day costs, including operating expenses, payroll and wages, rent or lease payments, etc.
*   **Cash Outflows (37)**: These are the money going out of the business, including accounts payable paid, loan payments, etc.
*   **Total Cash Inflows (22)**: The sum of all inflows.
*   **Total Cash Outflows (36)**: The sum of all outflows.
*   **Net Cash Flow (38)**: The difference between the total inflows and outflows.
*   **Closing Cash Balance (39)**: The ending cash balance for the analysis.
*   **Prepared By (50)**: The name of the person preparing the analysis.
*   **Prepared Date (51)**: The date the analysis was completed.
