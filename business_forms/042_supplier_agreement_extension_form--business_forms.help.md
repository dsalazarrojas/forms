# Supplier Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to add or modify terms and conditions of a pre-existing supplier agreement. It helps in providing a clear and organized structure for managing and documenting the changes made to the supplier agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the supplier agreement ID to associate the extension with the original agreement.
2. Enter the supplier's name.
3. Enter the supplier's email address.
4. Enter the supplier's phone number.
5. Describe the reason for the extension in the "extension_reason" field.
6. Choose the extension dates.
7. Enter the contract start date.
8. Enter the contract end date.
9. Select the managers who need to approve this extension.
10. Review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Agreement ID** (`supplier_agreement_id`, text, not required): Enter the unique identifier of the original supplier agreement.
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the supplier.
* **Supplier Email** (`supplier_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address associated with the supplier.
* **Supplier Phone** (`supplier_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number associated with the supplier.
* **Extension Reason** (`extension_reason`, text, not required): Describe the reason for the extension of the supplier agreement.
* **Extension Date** (`extension_date`, date, not required): Enter the date for which the agreement is extended.
* **Extension Terms** (`extension_terms`, text, not required): Describe the new terms and conditions of the extended agreement.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_multiple, not required): Select the manager(s) who need to approve this extension.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, not required): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, not required): Enter the end date of the contract.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that the form is complete and accurate.
* Use the "extension_reason" field to provide a clear and concise explanation for the extension.
* Double-check the "extension_date" to ensure it aligns with the actual date of the extension.
* Review the "extension_terms" field carefully to ensure it accurately reflects the new terms and conditions.
* Select the correct manager(s) who need to approve this extension in the "manager_approval" field.
* Review the "contract_start_date" and "contract_end_date" fields carefully to ensure they align with the actual dates of the contract.
