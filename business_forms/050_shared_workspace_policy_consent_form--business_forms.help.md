# Shared Workspace Policy Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Shared Workspace Policy Consent Form is used to collect information from staff members about their consent to shared workplace policies. It is a form that asks staff members to confirm their understanding and acceptance of the policies that apply to their shared workspace. This form is an essential part of ensuring that all staff members are aware of and compliant with these policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the link to access the form.
2. Review the shared workplace policies that are displayed.
3. Read and understand the policies, then confirm your consent by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the respective fields.
4. Enter your staff ID in the designated field.
5. Select the option to report to in case of any policy breaches.
6. Confirm the date and time of your last review.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes in the respective fields.
8. Finally, submit the form to confirm your consent.
