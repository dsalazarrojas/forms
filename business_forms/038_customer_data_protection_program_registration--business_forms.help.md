# Customer Data Protection Program Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register your interest in the Customer Data Protection Program. By completing this form, you will provide the necessary information to be part of the program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Provide your First Name
2.  Provide your Last Name
3.  Enter your Email
4.  Select your Role
5.  Enter your Organization
6.  Enter your Department
7.  Enter your Job Title
8.  Enter your Phone
9.  Enter your Address
10.  Select your interests (Data Security, Data Governance, or Data Compliance)
11.  Enter the Date you submit this form (in YYYY-MM-DD format)
12.  Enter the Time you submit this form (in 12-hour format, e.g., 14:30)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your First Name.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your Last Name.
* **Email**: Please enter your Email address (e.g., user@example.com).
* **Role**: Select your role from the following options:
	+ Data Protection Officer
	+ Chief Information Security Officer
	+ Chief Information Protection Officer
* **Organization**: Please enter your Organization name.
* **Department**: Enter your Department name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your Job Title.
* **Phone**: Enter your Phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your Address.
* **Interests**: Select all the relevant interests from the following options:
	+ Data Security
	+ Data Governance
	+ Data Compliance
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the Date you submit this form in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Time Submitted**: Enter the Time you submit this form in 12-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
