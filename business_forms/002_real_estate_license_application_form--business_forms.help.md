# real_estate_license_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_license_application_form is a form used to collect necessary information from real estate professionals applying for a real estate license. This form is intended to help the real estate regulatory body or government agency assess the applicant's eligibility for a license.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your basic contact information in the "Applicant Name", "Contact Email", and "Contact Phone" fields.
2. Select the type of real estate license you are applying for from the "License Type" dropdown menu.
3. Provide your existing license number, if you have one.
4. Enter your company name, address, city, state, and zip code in the respective fields.
5. Enter the amount you paid for the application fee in the "Amount Paid" field.
6. Record the date of your payment and the method used (check, bank transfer, or credit card) in the "Payment Date" and "Payment Method" fields.
7. Indicate your employment status and provide more specific employment information, including your employer's name, job title, years with your current employer, and years of experience in the real estate industry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address used for official communication.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number used for official communication.
* **License Type**: Select the type of real estate license you are applying for (Real Estate Agent, Real Estate Broker, or Real Estate Broker-Associate).
* **License Number**: If you have a current license, enter the number. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company you are working with.
* **Address**: Enter the physical address of your company or business.
* **City**: Enter the city where your company or business is located.
* **State**: Enter the state where your company or business is located.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of your company or business address.
* **Amount Paid**: Enter the amount you paid for the application fee.
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date you made the payment for the application fee.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method you used for the payment (check, bank transfer, or credit card).
* **Employment Status**: Indicate your current employment status (Full Time, Part Time, or Self-Employed).
* **Employer Name**: Enter the name of your employer.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title with your employer.
* **Years With Current Employer**: Enter the number of years you have been working with your current employer.
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been working in the real estate industry.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your contact information for errors before submitting the form.
* Review your employment status carefully, as it affects your eligibility for a real estate license.
* Attach any supporting documents required for the application process.
