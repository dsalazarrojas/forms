# Time Tracking Summary Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for time tracking and reporting purposes, specifically for business or enterprise activities. It's essential to select the correct form type to ensure accurate data categorization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct form type from the "What is the business form?" dropdown menu. This will determine the category under which the form will be stored in the system.
2. Enter the start and end times when the task was performed.
3. If necessary, record the break time taken during the task.
4. Provide a brief summary of the work done in the "Work Log" field (optional).
5. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Notes" field (optional).
6. Categorize the form as a "Business" or "Personal" form.
7. Choose whether the form is associated with a specific tool or not.
8. Submit the form to create a time tracking summary report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the business form?** (`business_form_page`, select_multiple, required): Select the correct category for the form from the provided options (Business, Enterprise).
* **Start Time** (`start_time_page`, date, required): Enter the start time of the task or activity.
* **End Time** (`end_time_page`, date, required): Enter the end time of the task or activity.
* **Break Time** (`break_time_page`, time, optional): Record the break time taken during the task (if applicable).
* **Work Log** (`work_log_page`, text, optional): Provide a brief summary of the work done.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes_page`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the task.
* **Form Category** (`form_category_page`, select_one, required): Choose the main category for the form (Business or Personal).
* **Form Sub Category** (`form_sub_category_page`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the form is associated with a specific tool or not (Yes, No).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool_page`, select_one, optional): Select the tool used during the task (if applicable).
