# Business Plan Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a business plan, including its submission date, details, and approval status. It is meant to be completed by the submitter and the reviewer (approver) to ensure that all relevant information is collected and documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your submission date and details, including your name and email, company name, and a brief title of the plan.
2. Rate the completeness, strategic alignment, financial feasibility, resource availability, and risk assessment of the plan using the provided scale.
3. Select the type of plan and indicate if it is approved or not.
4. If approved, indicate if any conditions or revisions are required.
5. Add any additional feedback or comments.
6. Record the date of review and the planned implementation start date (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Plan Submission Date** (`submission_date`)
Date submitted for approval.

### 2. **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`)
Name of the person submitting the plan.

### 3. **Email Address** (`submitted_email`)
Contact email of the submitter.

### 4. **Company Name** (`company_name`)
Name of the company submitting the plan.

### 5. **Business Plan Title** (`plan_title`)
Brief title of the plan.

### 6. **Fiscal Year** (`fiscal_year`)
What fiscal year does this plan cover? (Optional)

### 7. **Type of Plan** (`plan_type`)
Select the type of plan: Strategic, Annual Operating, Marketing, Financial, Product Launch, Expansion, or Other.

### 8. **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`)
Name of the approver reviewing the plan.

### 9. **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`)
Email of the approver reviewing the plan.

### 10. **Reviewer Title** (`reviewer_title`)
Position or role of the approver.

### 11. **Plan Completeness** (`plan_completeness`)
Rate the completeness of the plan on a scale of 1-5.

### 12. **Strategic Alignment** (`strategic_alignment`)
Rate if the plan aligns with the company's vision on a scale of 1-5.

### 13. **Financial Feasibility** (`financial_feasibility`)
Rate the financial feasibility of the plan on a scale of 1-5.

### 14. **Resource Availability** (`resource_availability`)
Rate if the resources are sufficient on a scale of 1-5.

### 15. **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`)
Rate if the risks are identified on a scale of 1-5.

### 16. **Timeline Realism** (`timeline_realism`)
Rate if the timeline is realistic on a scale of 1-5.

### 17. **Approval Status** (`approval_status`)
Select whether the plan is Approved, Approved with Conditions, Needs Revision, or Not Approved.

### 18. **Conditions or Revisions** (`conditions_or_revisions`)
If approved with conditions or needs revision, specify them.

### 19. **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`)
Any additional feedback from the reviewer.

### 20. **Date of Review** (`review_date`)
Date when the plan was reviewed.

### 21. **Planned Implementation Start** (`implementation_start_date`)
Date when the plan will be implemented (if applicable).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Review the form carefully before submitting or approving.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities.
