# shed_design_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shed_design_questionnaire form is designed to collect information about your shed design requirements. This form will help us provide an accurate quote for your project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Click on the "Next" button to navigate through the different pages of the form.
2. For each page, select the relevant options that apply to your shed design requirements.
3. Fill out all the required fields, marked as such in each page.
4. Once you have completed all the pages, click on the "Submit" button to send us your questionnaire.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the options that apply to your shed design preferences.
* **Client Requirements** (`text`, required: true): Describe your client's requirements, if any. This will help us provide a tailored solution for your shed.
* **Preferred Sizes** (`number`, required: false): Enter the preferred sizes of your shed. Please use numbers only, e.g., 6x4 or 8x12.
* **Materials Needed** (`select_one`, required: false): Select whether materials are needed for your shed design.
* **Site Details** (`select_multiple`, required: true): Select all the options that apply to your site's characteristics.
* **Quote Details** (`time`, required: false): If you have any specific time requirements for your quote, please enter them here.
