# Trial Data Meeting Scheduler - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Trial Data Meeting Scheduler" form is designed to schedule meetings for discussing trial data and reviewing meeting preferences with colleagues and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select a suitable duration for the meeting by choosing from the available options in the "meeting_duration" field.
2. List the locations you are available for the meeting in the "trial_data_locations" field.
3. Select the dates you are available for the meeting by choosing from the available options in the "available_dates" field.
4. Choose the times you are available for the meeting by selecting from the available options in the "available_times" field.
5. Select your preferred meeting schedule options in the "meeting_schedule" field.
6. Choose your preferences for the meeting by selecting from the available options in the "trial_data_review_meeting_preferences" field.
7. Select the topics you would like to discuss during the meeting by listing them in the "discussion_topics" field.
8. Select the preferred schedule for the meeting by choosing from the available options in the "preferred_meeting_schedule" field.
9. Rate your availability for the meeting by selecting a number in the "availability_rating" field.
10. Provide any additional information about the meeting in the "additional_info" field (optional).
11. Select your preferences for the meeting by choosing from the available options in the "trial_data_review_meeting_preferences_2" field.
12. Select your preferred discussion topics by choosing from the available options in the "discussion_topic_preferences" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Preferred Trial/Data Review Meeting Duration**: Select a suitable duration for the meeting.
* **Trial/Data Review Meeting Locations**: List the locations you are available for the meeting.
* **Available Dates**: Select the dates you are available for the meeting.
* **Available Times**: Select the times you are available for the meeting.
* **Meeting Schedule**: Select your preferred meeting schedule options.
* **Trial Data Review Meeting Preferences**: Select your preferences for the meeting.
* **Discussion Topics**: Select the topics you would like to discuss during the meeting.
* **Preferred Meeting Schedule**: Select the preferred schedule for the meeting.
* **Availability Rating**: Rate your availability for the meeting.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information about the meeting (optional).
* **Trial Data Review Meeting Preferences 2**: Select your preferences for the meeting.
* **Discussion Topic Preferences**: Select your preferred discussion topics.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that the meeting is scheduled efficiently and effectively.
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the fields to ensure that the meeting is scheduled accordingly.
* If you have any additional information about the meeting, please provide it in the "Additional Information" field.
