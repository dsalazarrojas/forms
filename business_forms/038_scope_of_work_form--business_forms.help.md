# scope_of_work_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The scope of work form is used to gather information on the scope of work for a particular project. This form will help project managers and team members to define the project's objectives, goals, and deliverables.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and instructions to ensure you understand the scope of work for the project.
2. Choose the correct answer for each field based on the project's requirements.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for fields that require a binary response.
4. Enter text or date responses as required.
5. Review your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **scope_of_work_form_step_1 (Yes/No)**: Select "Yes" if the project has a clear and well-defined scope of work. Select "No" if the scope of work is unclear or undefined.
* **scope_of_work_form_step_2 (Text)**: Provide a brief description of the project's objectives, goals, and deliverables. This field is used to gather more information on the project's scope.
* **scope_of_work_form_step_3 (Date)**: Enter the date when the project's scope of work was defined or revised. This field is used to track the project's timeline.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are complete and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your project manager or team leader for guidance.
* This form is used to gather information for project planning and management purposes only.
