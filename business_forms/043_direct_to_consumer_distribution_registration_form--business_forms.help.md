# Direct To Consumer Distribution Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from businesses that want to register for direct-to-consumer distribution in their region or country. It's essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to avoid delays or rejections in the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the company name and contact details (company_name, contact_first_name, contact_last_name, email, phone_number, address, and website).
2. Select the distribution type (Distribution Type, Direct To Consumer, Wholesale, or Other) that applies to your business.
3. Provide a brief description of your business using the company_abbreviation field.
4. Fill out your contact job title and any additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (company_name): This is the official name of your company.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To provide the exact name of your company.
* **Contact First Name** (contact_first_name): This is the first name of the contact person responsible for your business.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To provide the first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name** (contact_last_name): This is the last name of the contact person responsible for your business.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To provide the last name of the contact person.
* **Email** (email): This is the email address of the contact person responsible for your business.
	+ Type: Email field
	+ Purpose: To provide the email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number): This is the phone number of the contact person responsible for your business.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To provide the phone number for quick contact.
* **Address** (address): This is the physical address of your business location.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To provide the exact address of your business location.
* **Website** (website): This is the URL of your company website.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To provide the link to your company website.
* **Distribution Type** (distribution_type): This is the type of distribution your business is registered for (Distribution Type, Direct To Consumer, Wholesale, or Other).
	+ Type: Select One field
	+ Purpose: To select the correct distribution type for your business.
* **Company Abbreviation** (company_abbreviation): This is a brief description of your business (e.g., a few words that describe your company).
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To provide a brief description of your business.
* **Contact Job Title** (contact_job_title): This is the job title of the contact person responsible for your business.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To provide the job title of the contact person.

## Tips
- Please fill out as much information as possible to ensure a smooth registration process.
- Double-check your details before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or questions, please contact us at your email address provided above.
