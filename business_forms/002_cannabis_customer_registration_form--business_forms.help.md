# Cannabis Customer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cannabis Customer Registration Form is a required step to create a customer account with our business. It gathers essential information for your personal and product preferences, ensuring a smooth and secure experience for both you and our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Email Address, Phone Number, and Identification Details.
2. Select your preferred Product Types and choose your Cannabis Experience Level.
3. Indicate if you have a medical cannabis recommendation.
4. Choose how you'd like to receive marketing promotions (email, SMS, or not at all).
5. Select how you heard about us (Friend/Family, Social Media, Online Search, Advertisement, Walk-by, or Other).
6. Read and agree to our Terms and Conditions.
7. Sign with the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Required)**: Enter your legal first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name (Required)**: Enter your legal last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth (Required)**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format to ensure age verification.
* **Email Address (Required)**: Enter a valid email address where you can receive account notifications and promotions.
* **Phone Number (Required)**: Enter your primary contact number where we can reach you.
* **Identification Details (Optional)**: Provide any government-issued ID information if available.
* **ID Type (Required)**: Choose the type of government-issued ID you possess (Driver's License, State ID Card, Passport, or Military ID).
* **ID Number (Required)**: Enter the identification number from your government-issued ID.
* **State Issued (Required)**: Enter the state that issued your ID.
* **ID Expiration Date (Required)**: Enter the expiration date of your government-issued ID, ensuring it's currently valid.
* **Street Address (Required)**: Enter your current residential street address for contact and delivery purposes.
* **City (Required)**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State (Required)**: Enter your state of residence.
* **ZIP Code (Required)**: Enter your ZIP/postal code.
* **Product Preferences (Optional)**: Select all product types that interest you (Flower, Edibles, Concentrates, Vapes, Tinctures, Topicals, Pre-rolls, Accessories).
* **Cannabis Experience Level (Required)**: Choose your familiarity with cannabis products (First-time user, Beginner, Intermediate, Experienced, Expert).
* **Medical Recommendation (Required)**: Indicate if you have a medical cannabis recommendation.
* **Marketing Consent (Required)**: Select how you'd like to receive marketing communications (Email and SMS, Email only, SMS only, or No thanks).
* **How Did You Hear About Us (Optional)**: Choose how you heard about us (Friend/Family, Social Media, Online Search, Advertisement, Walk-by, Other).
* **Terms and Conditions (Required)**: Read and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Signature Date (Required)**: Sign with the current date to complete the registration process.
