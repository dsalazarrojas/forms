# Vendor Finance Office Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vendor Finance Office Contact Form is designed to collect relevant information from end-users to facilitate efficient communication and contact between them and the Vendor Finance Office.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Vendor Name of the individual or organization you are contacting.
2. Specify the Office Location of the contact person.
3. Enter the Contact E-mail of the individual or organization.
4. Provide the Contact Phone number of the contact person.
5. Type a Message detailing the purpose and context of your contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor you are contacting.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required
* **Office Location**: Enter the location of the office where the contact person is based.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required
* **Message**: Type a brief message detailing the purpose and context of your contact.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required
* **Vendor Type**: Select one of the provided options to categorize the vendor.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required
* **Select One - Vendor Type**: Select one or more of the provided options to categorize the vendor.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Optional
* **Vendor Notes**: Provide any additional context or information that might be helpful for the Vendor Finance Office.
	+ Type: note
	+ Optional

## Tips

* Be concise and clear in your message.
* Use proper contact information to ensure efficient communication.
