# lease_agreement_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "lease_agreement_records_release_form" is used to request access to specific or all records related to a property or tenant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure you have the necessary information to complete each field.
2. Select the "access_records" field and choose the type of record you wish to access (e.g., "All Records" or "Specific Record").
3. If you selected "Specific Record", enter the date range for the specific record you wish to access in the "specific_record_dates" field.
4. Select the "landlord" field and choose the landlord who will be accessing the records.
5. Select the "tenant" field and choose the tenants who will be accessed.
6. Enter any necessary additional information in the "notes" field.
7. Review your completed form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **tenant_details** (1, text, not required): This field is for you to enter your details if you are a tenant requesting access.
*   **permission_date** (2, date, not required): This field is for the date you are requesting access to the records.
*   **access_records** (3, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select the type of record you wish to access. Choose from "All Records" or "Specific Record".
*   **specific_record_dates** (4, text, not required): This field is for you to enter the date range if you selected "Specific Record".
*   **signature** (5, note, not required): This field is for you to enter any additional comments or explanations.
*   **signature_date** (6, date, not required): This field is for you to enter the date and time of your signature.
*   **landlord** (7, select_one, required): This field allows you to select the landlord who will be accessing the records.
*   **tenant** (8, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select the tenants who will be accessed.
*   **notes** (9, note, not required): This field is for you to enter any additional comments or explanations.
*   **signature_date** (10, time, not required): This field is for you to enter the date and time of your signature.
*   **tenant_email** (11, email, not required): This field is for the tenant's email address.
*   **landlord_email** (12, email, not required): This field is for the landlord's email address.
*   **tenant_phone** (13, text, not required): This field is for the tenant's phone number.
*   **landlord_phone** (14, text, not required): This field is for the landlord's phone number.
*   **tenant_address** (15, text, not required): This field is for the tenant's address.
*   **landlord_address** (16, text, not required): This field is for the landlord's address.
*   **record_type** (17, select_one, required): This field allows you to select the type of record you will be accessing (e.g., "Lease Agreement", "Lease Addendum", "Lease Renewal").
*   **reason_for_access** (18, text, not required): This field is for you to enter the reason for the access request.
*   **additional_comments** (19, text, not required): This field is for you to enter any additional comments or explanations.
*   **tenant_id** (20, text, not required): This field is for the tenant's ID.
*   **landlord_id** (21, text, not required): This field is for the landlord's ID.
*   **property_address** (22, text, not required): This field is for the property address.
*   **state** (23, text, not required): This field is for the state of the property.
*   **city** (24, text, not required): This field is for the city of the property.
*   **zip** (25, number, not required): This field is for the zip code of the property.
