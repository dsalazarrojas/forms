# Custom Cylinder Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create custom cylinder orders for your specific needs. It is intended for business-to-business use cases where you need to order cylindrical products with tailored specifications. Please ensure that all relevant fields are accurately filled out to ensure proper processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form

- To complete this form, start by selecting the Custom Cylinders page.
- Fill out the required and optional fields as necessary, providing accurate information.
- Review your selections before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Custom Cylinders** (Page 1)

* **Custom Cylinders** (`custom_cylinder_order_form`, text, Optional): Enter the description or specifications of the custom cylinder you're looking to order.

### **Material Type** (Page 1)

* **Material Type** (`material_type`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the desired material type for your custom cylinder from the provided options (Aluminum, Brass, Copper, Steel). You can choose one or multiple materials as needed.

### **Length (in mm)** (Page 1)

* **Length (in mm)** (`length_in_mm`, number, Optional): Enter the desired length of your custom cylinder in millimeters. This field is only required if you're ordering multiple cylinders with varying lengths.

### **Diameter (in mm)** (Page 1)

* **Diameter (in mm)** (`diameter_in_mm`, number, Optional): Enter the desired diameter of your custom cylinder in millimeters. This field is only required if you're ordering multiple cylinders with varying diameters.

### **Base Diameter (in mm)** (Page 1)

* **Base Diameter (in mm)** (`base_diameter_in_mm`, number, Optional): Enter the base diameter of your custom cylinder in millimeters. This field is only required if you're ordering multiple cylinders with varying base diameters.

## Tips

- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting your order.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact our sales team.
- For orders of larger quantities or specialized customizations, please contact our custom order department for further assistance.
