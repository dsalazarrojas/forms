# Customer Value Strategy Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from individuals who want to discuss their customer value strategy. The goal is to gather essential details that will help our team understand their needs and provide tailored support.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide us with your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
4. If you're reaching out from a specific department or team within your company, please let us know what department you're a part of.
5. If you'd like to provide your phone number, we'd love to have it for follow-up conversations.
6. If you have an existing company, please provide its name so we can better understand your context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the email address we can use to contact you. Make sure to enter the correct format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Department** (text, optional): If you're a member of a specific department or team within your company, please let us know.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Provide your phone number for follow-up conversations.
* **Company** (text, optional): If you're representing a company, please enter its name.
