# Internal Startup Incubator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from startups applying to the internal startup incubator program. It helps us understand your business needs and assess your eligibility for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form with your business details.
2. Provide your contact information for follow-up calls and discussions.
3. Select the relevant options for your business type, industry, and stage.
4. Add any additional comments or next steps you'd like to discuss during the application process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Name** (text): Enter your startup's name.
* **Legal Name** (text): Enter your legal name for business purposes.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for contact and follow-up.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Date Of Birth** (date): Enter your date of birth.
* **Business Name** (text): Enter your business name.
* **Business Description** (text): Briefly describe your business.
* **Industry** (select multiple):
	+ Software
	+ Finance
	+ Healthcare
	+ Technology
	+ Other
* **Business Type** (select one):
	+ Startup
	+ Partnership
	+ Other
* **Business Stage** (select one):
	+ Idea
	+ Pre-revenue
	+ Revenue
	+ Scaling
* **Location** (text): Enter your business location.
* **Team Size** (number): Enter the number of people on your team.
* **Website** (text): Enter your business website.
* **LinkedIn** (text): Enter your LinkedIn profile link.
* **Twitter** (text): Enter your Twitter profile link.
* **Instagram** (text): Enter your Instagram profile link.
* **Facebook** (text): Enter your Facebook profile link.
* **Bio** (text): Briefly describe yourself or your team.
* **How did you find us?** (select one):
	+ Social Media
	+ Referral
	+ Online Search
	+ Other
* **Why did you choose us?** (text): Explain why you're interested in the incubator program.
* **What do we offer?** (select multiple):
	+ Financial Support
	+ Mentorship
	+ Networking
	+ Marketing
* **Have you had support from us before?** (select one):
	+ True
	+ False
* **Follow-up Call** (select one):
	+ True
	+ False
* **Next Steps** (text): Provide any additional information or next steps you'd like to discuss.
* **Comments** (text): Add any additional comments or questions you have.
