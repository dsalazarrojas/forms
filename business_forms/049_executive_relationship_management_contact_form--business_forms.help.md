# Executive Relationship Management Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Relationship Management Contact Form is a tool used to gather and organize information about key contacts and relationships for business stakeholders and executives. This form helps to build and maintain professional connections, facilitate internal networking, and assign team members to specific projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide detailed contact information in the Contact Details section.
2. Select the type of relationship you have with the contact from the Relationship Type dropdown menu.
3. Indicate the contact's interests and preferred communication methods.
4. Enter the contact's email address in the Email field.
5. Enter the contact's phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: Enter a brief description of the contact, including their name, job title, and company. This field helps to identify and differentiate between multiple contacts.
* **Relationship Type**: Choose the type of relationship you have with the contact from the provided list. Options include Friend, Colleague, and Mentor. This field helps to categorize and filter contacts for future reference.
* **Interests**: Select all applicable interests for the contact from the list. This field helps to understand the contact's professional or personal inclinations. Options include Business, Technology, Sports, and Travel.
* **Communication Preferences**: Enter your preferred method of communication with the contact. This field is not currently used in this form, but is intended to be used in the future for automation and filtering.
* **Email**: Enter the contact's email address. This field is used to send messages or invitations to the contact.
* **Phone**: Enter the contact's phone number. This field is used for direct communication with the contact.

## Tips
When completing this form, be as accurate and thorough as possible to ensure that the contact information is up-to-date and useful for future reference and collaboration.
