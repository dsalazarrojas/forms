Nail Preorder Form CS - Help Guide
=============================

### Purpose
The Nail Preorder Form CS allows customers to provide order details, customer information, contact phone number and email, and specify their preferred nail type for a preorder.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Order Details** field with your desired order information.
2.  Enter your **Customer Info** in the corresponding field.
3.  Provide your **Phone** number.
4.  Enter your **Email** address.
5.  Select the desired **Preorder Details** from the dropdown list.
6.  Choose your preferred **Nail Type** from the options provided.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Order Details** (text, optional): Enter your order details, such as the type of nail service you want to preorder.
*   **Customer Info** (text, optional): Enter your customer information, including your name, address, etc.
*   **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
*   **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information or send you preorder updates.
*   **Preorder Details** (select multiple, optional): Select the specific services you want to preorder, such as nail shaping, coloring, etc.
*   **Nail Type** (select one, optional): Choose the type of nail you want, such as regular or acrylic.

### Tips

*   Please ensure that you enter all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   Double-check your selections for **Preorder Details** and **Nail Type** before submitting the form.
*   Make sure to enter a valid email address for easy contact and updates.
*   If you need further assistance or have questions, contact us at \[your contact info].
