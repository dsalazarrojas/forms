# IT Service Ticket Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Service Ticket Form is used to report issues that require assistance from the IT department. It helps to gather essential information to expedite the resolution process and categorize the issue's priority and urgency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Briefly describe the issue in the "What is the issue?" field.
2. Provide more details about the issue in the "Description of the issue" field.
3. Select all relevant categories for the issue in the "Select all relevant categories for the issue" field.
4. Enter the name of the person who reported the issue in the "Who reported the issue?" field.
5. Enter the due date for the issue resolution in the "What is the due date for the issue?" field.
6. Enter the email address of the reporter in the "What is the email address of the reporter?" field.
7. Optionally, provide comments for the reporter in the "Comments for the reporter" field.
8. Check if the reporter is available for follow-up in the "Is the reporter available for follow-up?" field.
9. Choose the method of submission (e.g., email, phone, in-person) in the "How will the issue be submitted?" field.
10. Select the type of submitter (individual or department) in the "What is the type of the submitter?" field.
11. Choose a contact method (e.g., email, phone, in-person) in the "How would you like to be contacted?" field.
12. Check if the issue is urgent in the "Is this an urgent issue?" field.
13. Check if the issue requires escalation in the "Is this issue that requires escalation?" field.
14. Check if the issue requires follow-up from the submitter in the "Is this issue that requires follow-up from the submitter?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the issue?** (Issue Description): A brief description of the issue.
* **Description of the issue**: A more detailed description of the issue.
* **Select all relevant categories for the issue**: Choose all categories that apply to the issue.
* **Who reported the issue?** (Reporter's Name): Enter the name of the person who reported the issue.
* **What is the due date for the issue?** (Due Date): Enter the due date for the issue resolution.
* **What is the email address of the reporter?**: Enter the email address of the reporter.
* **Comments for the reporter**: Any comments from the reporter.
* **Is the reporter available for follow-up?**: Check if the reporter is available for follow-up.
* **How will the issue be submitted?** (Submission Method): Choose the method of submission (e.g., email, phone, in-person).
* **What is the type of the submitter?** (Submitter Type): Choose the type of submitter (individual or department).
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (Contact Method): Choose a contact method (e.g., email, phone, in-person).
* **Is this an urgent issue?**: Check if the issue is urgent.
* **Is this issue that requires escalation?**: Check if the issue requires escalation.
* **Is this issue that requires follow-up from the submitter?**: Check if the issue requires follow-up from the submitter.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide as much detail as possible when describing the issue.
* Choose the correct categories for the issue to ensure it is properly categorized.
* Enter the correct due date for the issue resolution.
* Make sure to provide the correct email address of the reporter.
* If applicable, provide comments for the reporter.
* Check the correct contact method for follow-up.
* Be accurate when indicating if the issue is urgent or requires escalation.
