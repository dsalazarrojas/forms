# (CANVAS) Employee Onboarding - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and process essential information for new employees during the onboarding process, making it easier for them to get settled into their new role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
3. Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Preferred name** (`preferred_name`, `text`, optional): If different from your legal name, enter your preferred name to be used in informal communication.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address for payroll and scheduling purposes.
* **Primary phone number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, required): Include your country code if you're outside the US, e.g., +1 123-456-7890.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth for benefits eligibility.
* **Home address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your home address, including street, city, state/region, and postal code.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your primary emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `phone`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact, including country code if applicable.
* **Medical information** (`emergency_medical_info`, `text`, optional): List any allergies, medications, or conditions we should know about.
* **Position/role** (`position_applied`, `text`, required): Enter your position or role at CANVAS, e.g., Stylist, Assistant, Receptionist.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select the department you'll be working in, e.g., Hair, Nails, Skin, Front Desk, Management.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your official job title for payroll records.
* **Date of hire** (`hire_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you started working at CANVAS.
* **Planned arrival time on first day** (`first_day_time`, `time`, required): Enter your local time of arrival on your first day.
* **Typical availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all shifts you can regularly work, e.g., Weekday mornings, Weekday afternoons, Weekend mornings.
* **Agreed hourly rate** (`hourly_rate`, `decimal`, required): Enter your agreed-upon hourly rate in local currency, e.g., $15.00.
* **Uniform/shirt size** (`uniform_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select the size of your uniform if you're ordering one, e.g., XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL.
* **Equipment or supplies needed** (`equipment_needed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any equipment or supplies you require for your job, e.g., Scissors and shears, Clippers, Towels and linens.
* **Professional certifications/licenses** (`certifications`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any professional certifications or licenses you hold, e.g., Cosmetology license, Nail technician license, Esthetician license.
* **Payroll and tax paperwork completed** (`payroll_forms_completed`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you've completed the required payroll and tax paperwork.
* **Management rating** (`management_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your experience with management communication, e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Onboarding satisfaction** (`onboarding_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with the onboarding process, e.g., Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **Improvement suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions for training, scheduling, or support you'd like to see at CANVAS.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your onboarding experience.

Note: This guide is a general explanation of the form fields. Please refer to your HR or management team for any specific questions or concerns.
