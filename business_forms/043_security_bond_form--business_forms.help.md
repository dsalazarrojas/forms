# Security Bond Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for a security bond, a financial agreement that ensures a company or employee will fulfill their obligations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you are completing the form as the authorized representative of the company or employee.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (1)**: Enter the name of the company filling out this form.
* **Contact Name (2)**: Enter the name of the contact person for the company.
* **Address (3)**: Enter the physical address of the company.
* **City (4)**: Enter the city where the company is located.
* **State (5)**: Enter the state where the company is located.
* **Zip (6)**: Enter the postal code of the company's address.
* **Email (7)**: Enter the email address of the company's contact person.
* **Phone (8)**: Enter the phone number of the company.
* **Bond Amount (9)**: Enter the amount of the bond.
* **Payment Terms (10)**: Enter the payment terms agreed upon with the company.
* **Payment Method (11)**: Select the method of payment used for the bond (Cash, Bank Transfer, or Check).
* **Employee Name (12)**: Enter the name of the employee filling out this form.
* **Employee Position (13)**: Enter the position of the employee.
* **Employee Address (14)**: Enter the address of the employee.
* **Employee City (15)**: Enter the city where the employee is located.
* **Employee State (16)**: Enter the state where the employee is located.
* **Employee Zip (17)**: Enter the postal code of the employee's address.
* **Employee Email (18)**: Enter the email address of the employee.
* **Employee Phone (19)**: Enter the phone number of the employee.
* **Employee Signature (20)**: Enter the employee's signature.
* **Company Signature (21)**: Enter the company's signature.
* **Contract Date (22)**: Enter the date the contract was signed.
* **Contract Term (23)**: Select the term of the contract (Month to Month or Year to Year).
* **Contract Start Date (24)**: Enter the start date of the contract.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled out for the form to be considered complete. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the company or employee filling out this form.
