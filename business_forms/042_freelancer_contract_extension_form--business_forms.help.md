# Freelancer Contract Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freelancer Contract Extension Form is used to extend the existing contract between a client and a freelancer. This form ensures a smooth transition of the agreement's duration, value, or status when needed.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the form to open it in a new window.
2.  Review the project details and the terms of the contract.
3.  Update the contract start date, end date, or duration as necessary.
4.  Choose the desired contract status (Active, Inactive, or Draft).
5.  Add client notes and freelancer notes if required.
6.  Select the client acceptance and ensure it is correct.
7.  Both the freelancer and client need to sign the form.
8.  Click the submit button to save the changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Details**: Enter a brief description of the project to ensure context.
- **Extension Terms**: Enter a numerical value to extend the contract duration in days.
- **Client Consent**: Choose how the client accepts the contract extension:
  - Accept
  - Reject
  - Conditional
- **Freelancer Signature**: Enter the freelancer's identification number or signature.
- **Client Signature**: Enter the client's identification number or signature.
- **Contract End Date**: Enter the new end date of the contract.
- **Contract Start Date**: Enter the new start date of the contract.
- **Contract Duration**: Enter the extended contract duration in days.
- **Contract Value**: Enter the new contract value.
- **Client Notes**: Add any notes or comments from the client.
- **Freelancer Notes**: Add any notes or comments from the freelancer.
- **Contract Status**: Choose the status of the contract:
  - Active
  - Inactive
  - Draft
