# Office Rental Lease Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to outline the terms and conditions of a rental agreement between a landlord and a tenant. It captures essential details such as rent details, lease period, and contact information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the landlord's information in the "Landlord Information" section.
2.  Enter the tenant's information in the "Tenant Information" section.
3.  Specify the lease terms in the "Lease Terms" section.
4.  Enter the rent details in the "Rent Details" section.
5.  Select the rent period in the "Rent Period" section.
6.  Enter the security deposit amount in the "Security Deposit" section.
7.  Specify the start and end dates of the lease in the "Lease Start Date" and "Lease End Date" sections.
8.  Sign the form in the "Signature" section.
9.  Enter the contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
10.  Specify the agreement terms and date in the "Agreement Terms" and "Agreement Date" sections.
11.  Select the agreement status in the "Agreement Status" section.
12.  Enter any notes or comments in the "Lease Note" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Landlord Information (1)**: Enter the landlord's name, address, and contact information.
*   **Tenant Information (2)**: Enter the tenant's name, address, and contact information.
*   **Lease Terms (3)**: Specify the terms of the lease, such as rent, payment due date, and any other conditions.
*   **Rent Details (4)**: Enter the rent amount, payment frequency, and any other rent-related details.
*   **Rent Period (5)**: Select the rent period, which may be weekly, monthly, or yearly.
*   **Security Deposit (6)**: Enter the security deposit amount.
*   **Lease Start Date (8)**: Enter the start date of the lease.
*   **Lease End Date (9)**: Enter the end date of the lease.
*   **Signature (10)**: Sign the agreement to confirm its completion.
*   **Tenant Signature (11)**: Sign the agreement to confirm its completion by the tenant.
*   **Landlord Signature (12)**: Sign the agreement to confirm its completion by the landlord.
*   **Agreement Terms (13)**: Specify any additional agreement terms or conditions.
*   **Agreement Date (14)**: Enter the date the agreement was signed.
*   **Agreement Time (15)**: Enter the time the agreement was signed.
*   **Agreement Status (16)**: Select the status of the agreement, such as pending or active.
*   **Lease Status (17)**: Select the status of the lease, such as active or inactive.
*   **Lease Note (18)**: Enter any notes or comments about the lease.
*   **Contact Information (19)**: Enter the contact information for any parties involved.
*   **Contact Name (20)**: Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone (21)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Contact Email (22)**: Enter the email of the contact person.
*   **Contact Address (23)**: Enter the address of the contact person.
*   **Contact Note (24)**: Enter any notes or comments about the contact person.
*   **Agreement Note (25)**: Enter any notes or comments about the agreement.
