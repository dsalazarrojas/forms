# Freight Service Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about shipments and freight services. It requires you to provide your business details, contact person's information, and shipment details. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure that your shipment is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your business details in the **Business Form** field.
2. Enter the contact person's name and email in the **Contact Person** field.
3. Fill out the **Phone** field with your contact person's phone number.
4. Select whether you need a **Service Type** (yes or no) in the **Service Type** field.
5. Fill out the **Shipment Details**, **Shipment Length**, **Shipment Width**, **Shipment Height**, and **Shipment Volume** fields accordingly.
6. Select the **Shipment Service Type** (yes or no) in the **Shipment Service Type** field.
7. Choose the service type (LTL or TL) in the **Shipment Details** field.
8. In the **Ship To Address** field, enter the recipient's shipping address, including city, state, and zip.
9. In the **Ship From Address** field, enter the sender's shipping address, including city, state, and zip.
