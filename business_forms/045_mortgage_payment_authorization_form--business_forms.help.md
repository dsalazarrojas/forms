# Mortgage Payment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize mortgage payments for a specific account. It collects information about the account type, authorization method, and authorization details to facilitate the payment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your mortgage account number in the "Mortgage Account" field.
2. Select your account type from the list provided in the "Account Type" field.
3. Choose the authorization method from the list in the "Authorization Method" field.
4. Enter your authorization details in the "Authorization Details" field.
5. Select the authorization date and time in the "Authorization Date" and "Authorization Time" fields.
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mortgage Account** (Text): Enter your mortgage account number.
* **Account Type** (Select One): Select your account type from the list:
	+ Savings
	+ Checking
	+ Credit
* **Authorization Method** (Select One): Select the authorization method from the list:
	+ Online
	+ Phone
	+ Email
* **Authorization Details** (Text): Enter your authorization details.
* **Authorization Date** (Date): Select the authorization date.
* **Authorization Time** (Time): Select the authorization time.
* **Submit** (Text): Click to submit the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields correctly to avoid errors.
* Review the form before submission to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your account documentation or contact your finance department for assistance.
* This form is for authorization purposes only; do not use it for other purposes.
