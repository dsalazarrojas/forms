## Utility Cost And Vendor Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect detailed information about the utility costs and vendor details from a business.

## How To Complete This Form
- To begin, fill in the required fields such as **Vendor Name**, **Billing Address**, **Utility Expenses**, and **Form ID**.
- If applicable, select the **Utility Type** from the available options.
- Enter any relevant details in the **Vendor Address**, **Vendor Note**, and **Terms** fields.
- If the vendor is not a company, select the appropriate **Vendor Type** from the options.
- Enter the **Contact Name**, **Contact Phone**, and **Contact Email** if applicable.
- Finally, review your entries and submit the form by selecting **Submit**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data**: This field is not visible for the end user.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor providing the utility services. (Required)
* **Billing Address**: Enter the address where the utility services are billed. (Required)
* **Utility Expenses**: Enter the monthly or annual utility expenses. (Required)
* **Vendor Address**: Enter the address of the vendor providing the utility services.
* **Utility Type**: Select the type of utility service being provided (e.g., Electricity, Gas, Water, etc.). (Optional)
* **Rate**: Enter the rate of the utility service if applicable. (Optional)
* **Rate Period**: Enter the period for which the rate applies if applicable. (Optional)
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the utility service if applicable. (Optional)
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the utility service if applicable. (Optional)
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the vendor. (Optional)
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number for the vendor. (Optional)
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email for the vendor. (Optional)
* **Vendor Type**: Select the type of vendor (Company, Individual, Partnership, etc.). (Optional)
* **Other (if other please specify)**: If the vendor type is not listed, enter the alternative type. (Optional)
* **Vendor Note**: Enter any notes or comments about the vendor. (Optional)
* **Vendor Terms**: Enter any additional terms related to the vendor. (Optional)
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the form submission. (Optional)
* **Form Submit**: Select **Submit** to complete the form or **Save Draft** to save it for later submission. (Optional)
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the form submission if **Save Draft** is selected. (Optional)
* **Utility ID**: Enter the ID of the utility service if applicable. (Optional)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the utility service. (Optional)
* **Terms**: Enter any additional terms related to the utility service. (Optional)
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for the form submission. (Required)
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the form from the available options. (Optional)

Note: Some fields are not required, but it's recommended to fill them in if applicable to ensure accurate and complete information collection.
