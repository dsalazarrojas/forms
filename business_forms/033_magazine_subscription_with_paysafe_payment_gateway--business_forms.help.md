# Magazine Subscription With Paysafe Payment Gateway - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process magazine subscription information and payment details using the Paysafe payment gateway. It allows users to input subscription information such as subscription terms, payment types, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with the necessary information
* Select the desired options for subscription term, payment type, and payment method
* Enter your contact name, email, and phone number
* Choose a start date for the subscription
* Enter the payment amount

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Subscription Details (1)
* **Subscription Details**: This field is used to input any additional details about the subscription. Enter any relevant information about the subscription.

### Subscription Term (2)
* **Subscription Term**: Select the term of the subscription from the options provided. Options may include "Option 1", "Option 2", and "Option 3".

### Payment Type (3)
* **Payment Type**: Select the type of payment method to be used for the subscription. Options may include "Option 1" and "Option 2".

### Payment Method (4)
* **Payment Method**: Select the specific payment method to be used for the subscription. Options may include "Option 1" and "Option 2".

### Contact Name (5)
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the subscription.

### Contact E-mail (6)
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter the email address of the contact person for the subscription.

### Contact Phone (7)
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person for the subscription.

### Subscription Start Date (8)
* **Subscription Start Date**: Choose the start date of the subscription. This date should be in the correct format for the date field.

### Payment Amount (9)
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount to be paid for the subscription.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and submit the form carefully before confirming the subscription.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, do not hesitate to contact us.
