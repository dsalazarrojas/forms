# Brand Ambassador Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to establish a partnership between the brand and the ambassador, defining the terms and conditions of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and address as the brand ambassador.
2. Provide the name and address of the company or brand you are representing.
3. Select the start and end dates of the contract.
4. Describe the detailed scope of work and responsibilities as an ambassador.
5. Choose the territories or regions you will represent as an ambassador.
6. Specify the expected performance metrics and goals of the partnership.
7. If applicable, choose the salary or fee amount, payment frequency, and benefits provided.
8. Acknowledge that you agree to keep brand information confidential and that you will not work for competing brands.
9. Finally, confirm that you agree to all terms in the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ambassador Full Name** (`ambassador_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as the brand ambassador.
* **Ambassador Address** (`ambassador_address`, text, required): Provide your full mailing address.
* **Company or Brand Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company or brand you are representing.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the company or brand headquarters.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, required): Select the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, required): Select the end date of the contract.
* **Contract Duration** (`contract_duration`, text, optional): Describe the length of the agreement if not already specified by the contract dates.
* **Ambassador Duties and Responsibilities** (`ambassador_duties`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your work as an ambassador.
* **Territories for Brand Representation** (`brand_representation`, text, optional): Specify the geographic scope of the partnership.
* **Performance Metrics and Goals** (`performance_metrics`, text, optional): Describe the expected outcomes of the partnership.
* **Salary or Fee Amount** (`salary_amount`, number, optional): Enter the compensation amount for your services.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you are paid (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, Per deliverable).
* **Benefits Provided** (`benefits_provided`, text, optional): Describe any benefits or perks provided as part of the agreement.
* **I agree to keep brand information confidential** (`confidentiality_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you will keep brand information confidential.
* **Non-compete clause agreed upon** (`non_compete`, select_one, required): Confirm that you will not work for competing brands.
* **I agree to all terms in this contract** (`contract_signature`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to all terms and conditions of the contract.
