# Mergers & Acquisitions Consulting Panel Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mergers & Acquisitions Consulting Panel Application Form is designed for individuals or organizations interested in becoming a part of the Mergers and Acquisitions Consulting Panel. This form helps us understand your background, company details, and your role in the consulting process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you select the correct company information from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Info** (Company A, Company B, Company C, required): Select the company you represent or are associated with. This will help us understand your affiliation and connection to the mergers and acquisitions space.
* **Consultant Details** (text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your role and experience in mergers and acquisitions. This will help us assess your expertise and suitability for the consulting panel.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to avoid delays in processing your application.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact us for clarification.
* We will review your application and respond as soon as possible.
