# VIP Order Form Color Street W Kelli Independent Stylist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather contact information and order preferences from customers or clients for the business "Color Street W Kelli Independent Stylist". 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Make sure to select the desired color(s) for your order.
3. Enter your contact information for follow up.
4. Add any additional notes you'd like to include about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be known.
- **Email** (`Email`, text, optional): Provide your email address for further correspondence.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow up.
- **Color** (`Color`, select_one, optional): Choose one color for your order from the provided options: Red, Blue, or Green.
- **Colors** (`Colors`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple colors for your order, choosing from: Red, Blue, or Green.
- **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Select a date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Select a time for any scheduled appointment (format not explicitly stated, but implied as 12-hour clock, e.g., 02:30 PM).
- **Note** (`Note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or requests about your order.
