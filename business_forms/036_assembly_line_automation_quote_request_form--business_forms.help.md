# Assembly Line Automation Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from companies looking to automate their assembly line processes. It will help our team assess the suitability of our assembly line automation solutions for your business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your company name, industry, and a brief description of your current manual process.
2. Specify your desired production throughput.
3. List the types of components to be handled.
4. Select your estimated budget and desired implementation timeline.
5. If you have existing systems or software that need to be integrated, please list them.
6. Provide your contact email for us to send a quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: (`company_name_automation`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Primary Industry**: (`industry_type_automation`, select_one, required): Choose your company's primary industry from the list provided.
* **Description of Current Manual Process**: (`manual_process_desc`, text, required): Describe the primary tasks performed by your workers in the assembly line process.
* **Desired Production Throughput**: (`desired_throughput`, text, required): Specify your desired production throughput in units per hour or day.
* **Physical Space Constraints**: (`space_constraints`, text, optional): If you have specific space constraints for the assembly line installation, please provide approximate dimensions.
* **Types of Components to be Handled**: (`component_handling_needs`, text, required): List the types of components to be handled, such as small electronic parts or heavy metal frames.
* **Estimated Budget**: (`estimated_budget_automation`, select_one, required): Choose your estimated budget from the provided ranges.
* **Desired Implementation Timeline**: (`implementation_timeline`, select_one, required): Select your desired implementation timeline from the provided options.
* **Existing System Integration Needs**: (`system_integration_notes`, text, optional): If you have existing systems or software that need to be integrated, please list them.
* **Contact Email for Quote**: (`contact_email_automation`, email, required): Provide your contact email for us to send a quote.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
* This form should only be filled out once per company, so if you've already submitted this form, please do not resubmit it.
* Your information will be kept confidential and used only for assessment and potential quote purposes.
