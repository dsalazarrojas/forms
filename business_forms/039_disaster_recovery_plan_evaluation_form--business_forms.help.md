# Disaster Recovery Plan Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Recovery Plan Evaluation Form is designed to assess and document the effectiveness of a disaster recovery plan in your organization. This form will help you gather valuable information about the plan's category, description, and expected outputs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for your disaster recovery plan.
2. Enter a brief description of the plan, including its purpose and scope.
3. Describe the expected outputs from the plan.
4. Repeat steps 2 and 3 for each subsequent page of the form, ensuring you capture all relevant information about the plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`form_category_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field helps determine the type of form you are evaluating. In this case, it's likely used to distinguish business-related forms from others.
* **Description** (`form_description_8`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the disaster recovery plan, including its purpose and scope.
* **Outputs** (`form_outputs_12`, `text`, optional): Describe the expected outputs from the disaster recovery plan.
