# Event Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Photography Contract form is designed to document the agreement between a client and a photographer for an event. This form helps establish clear expectations, ensures both parties understand their roles, and maintains a record of the agreed-upon terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate information about the event:
	* Enter the correct date and time of the event.
	* Write the correct location of the event.
2. Record the client's details:
	* Provide the client's name.
	* Enter any additional event details.
3. Select the photography services to be provided.
4. Ensure both parties sign the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event, e.g., 2023-07-17.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the event, e.g., 14:00.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: false): Write the complete address or location of the event, e.g., "123 Main St, Anytown USA".
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Enter the client's full name.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details about the event, e.g., "Wedding reception" or "Company meeting".
* **Photographer Details** (`photographer_details`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant information about the photographer, e.g., "Professional photographer with 5+ years of experience".
* **Photography Services** (`photography_services`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the services you want the photographer to provide, e.g., "Service 1", "Service 2", or "Service 3".
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, note, required: false): Sign here to confirm you have read and agreed upon the terms.
* **Photographer Signature** (`photographer_signature`, note, required: false): Sign here to confirm you have read and agreed upon the terms.
