# Shift Handover Protocol Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the handover process between shifts, ensuring that the employee handing over the shift acknowledges that the handover protocol has been followed.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out this form as soon as you take over the shift.
- Make sure to answer all fields accurately and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not leave it blank. Instead, ask your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your name as the employee taking over the shift.
* **Shift Handover Date** (`shift_handover_date`, date, optional): This field is where you enter the date the handover took place.
* **Handover Protocol Acknowledged** (`handover_protocol_acknowledged`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have followed the handover protocol correctly. Select "No" if you have not.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, time, optional): This field is where you enter the start time of the shift you took over.
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, time, optional): This field is where you enter the end time of the shift you took over.
* **Employee Name of Next Shift** (`employee_name_of_next_shift`, text, optional): This field is where you enter the name of the employee taking over the next shift.
* **Contact Name of Next Shift** (`contact_name_of_next_shift`, text, optional): This field is where you enter the name and contact details of the employee taking over the next shift.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This field is where you enter any contact details for the next shift, such as phone number or email.
* **Next Shift Start Time** (`next_shift_start_time`, time, optional): This field is where you enter the start time of the next shift.
* **Handover Details** (`handover_details`, text, optional): This field is where you provide any additional details about the handover process.

## Tips

- Make sure to follow the handover protocol carefully and accurately document the handover process.
- Double-check the information you enter in each field to ensure it is correct.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
