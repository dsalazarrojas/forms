# Concierge Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a comprehensive agreement between the concierge service provider and the client. It captures essential details such as service scope, payment terms, and cancellation policies to establish a mutual understanding between both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the agreement date: Select the date of the agreement from the calendar icon.
2. Enter the agreement number: Provide the contract reference number, if applicable.
3. Service provider: Enter the name of the company providing the service.
4. Client details: Enter a brief description of the client's information (optional).
5. Client full name: Enter the client's legal name.
6. Client type: Select the client type: Individual, Corporate, or Family Office.
7. Client email: Enter the client's contact email.
8. Client phone: Enter the client's contact number.
9. Client address: Enter the full address of the client.
10. Service scope: Enter the services included in the agreement (select all that apply).
11. Services included: Select all services that apply to the agreement.
12. Service exclusions: Enter services not included in the agreement.
13. Service hours: Select the availability schedule of the provider.
14. Financial terms: Review the payment and fees terms.
15. Membership fee: Enter the monthly or annual fee.
16. Billing frequency: Select the billing frequency: Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
17. Payment due date: Select when the payment is due.
18. Cancellation policy: Select the terms for cancellation: 30 Days Notice, 60 Days Notice, or 90 Days Notice.
19. Confidentiality agreement: Select the level of privacy agreement: Full Confidentiality, Limited Disclosure.
20. Liability terms: Select the limitation of liability: Standard Liability or Limited Liability.
21. Contract duration: Select the length of the agreement: 6 Months, 1 Year, 2 Years, 3 Years, or Indefinite.
22. Renewal terms: Select the auto-renewal option: Auto Renew, Manual Renewal, or No Renewal.
23. Client signature: Select to confirm client agrees to terms.
24. Provider signature: Select to confirm provider agrees to terms.
