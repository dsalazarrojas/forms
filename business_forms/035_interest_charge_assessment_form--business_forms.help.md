# Interest Charge Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interest Charge Assessment Form is used to record and assess interest charges for financial transactions. This form provides a structured way to input necessary information about the interest charge, including principal amount, interest rate, and interest start and end dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field to ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Enter the principal amount, which is the original amount of the transaction.
3. Select the principal currency of the transaction.
4. Enter the interest rate, which is the rate at which the interest charge is applied.
5. Enter the start and end dates for the interest charge period.
6. Select the interest reason for the charge.
7. Enter the interest charge amount.
8. Select the finance team members responsible for the interest charge.
9. Select the accounting staff members involved in the interest charge.
10. Add any notes or comments about the interest charge.
11. Enter the email contact information for the person submitting the form.
12. Enter the phone number for the person submitting the form.
13. Finally, enter the name of the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Principal Amount** (`principal_amount`, number, required): Enter the original amount of the transaction. This is the amount on which the interest charge will be applied.
* **Principal Currency** (`principal_amount_currency`, select_one, optional): Select the currency of the original transaction. This will help track the currency of the interest charge.
* **Principal Amount Frequency** (`principal_amount_frequency`, date, optional): This field is currently empty, so no explanation is provided.
* **Interest Rate** (`interest_rate`, number, optional): Enter the interest rate at which the interest charge is applied.
* **Interest Start Date** (`interest_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the interest charge period.
* **Interest End Date** (`interest_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the interest charge period.
* **Interest Reason** (`interest_reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for the interest charge.
* **Interest Charge Amount** (`interest_charge_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total interest charge amount.
* **Finance Team** (`finance_team`, select_multiple, optional): Select all finance team members involved in the interest charge.
* **Accounting Staff** (`accounting_staff`, select_multiple, optional): Select all accounting staff members involved in the interest charge.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the interest charge.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email contact information for the person submitting the form.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number for the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
