# Classic Car Appraisals Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Classic Car Appraisals Form is a tool used to determine the market value of classic vehicles. It collects information about the vehicle's condition, features, and other relevant factors to provide an estimated current value.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the date of the appraisal from the calendar picker.
2. Enter the full name of the certified appraiser conducting the appraisal.
3. Enter the professional license or certification number of the appraiser.
4. Select a vehicle information page.
5. Enter the year the vehicle was manufactured.
6. Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., Ford, Chevrolet, Mercedes-Benz).
7. Enter the model name of the vehicle.
8. Enter the 17-character Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
9. Select the primary exterior color of the vehicle.
10. Select the type of vehicle body (e.g., Sedan, Coupe, Convertible).
11. Enter the current odometer reading in miles or kilometers.
12. Select the overall condition of the exterior and interior.
13. Indicate if the vehicle has undergone any restoration.
14. If restored, describe the restoration details and dates.
15. Select whether complete or partial service records are available.
16. List any unique or modified features of the vehicle.
17. Select the approach used to determine the value (e.g., Comparative Sales Analysis, Cost Approach).
18. Enter the estimated current market value in local currency.
19. Select the method used to determine the value.
20. Enter any additional notes or comments affecting the value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Appraisal Date**: The date of the appraisal, selected from a calendar picker.
* **Appraiser Name**: The full name of the certified appraiser conducting the appraisal.
* **License Number**: The professional license or certification number of the appraiser.
* **Vehicle Year**: The year the vehicle was manufactured.
* **Make**: The manufacturer of the vehicle (e.g., Ford, Chevrolet, Mercedes-Benz).
* **Model**: The specific model name of the vehicle.
* **VIN**: The 17-character Vehicle Identification Number.
* **Color**: The primary exterior color of the vehicle.
* **Body Style**: The type of vehicle body (e.g., Sedan, Coupe, Convertible).
* **Current Mileage**: The current odometer reading in miles or kilometers.
* **Exterior Condition**: The overall condition of the vehicle's exterior.
* **Interior Condition**: The overall condition of the vehicle's interior.
* **Mechanical Condition**: The condition of the vehicle's engine and mechanical components.
* **Has the vehicle been restored?**: Indicate if the vehicle has undergone any restoration.
* **Restoration Details**: If restored, describe the restoration work and dates.
* **Service Records**: Whether complete or partial service records are available.
* **Special Features**: List any unique or modified features of the vehicle.
* **Estimated Value**: The estimated current market value of the vehicle in local currency.
* **Valuation Method**: The approach used to determine the value (e.g., Comparative Sales Analysis, Cost Approach).
* **Supporting Notes**: Additional notes or comments affecting the value.
