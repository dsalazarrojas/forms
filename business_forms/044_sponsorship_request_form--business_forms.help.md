# Sponsorship Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool used to request sponsorships for projects or events. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your User information in the "User" field.
2. Enter your Company Name in the "Company Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the type of sponsorship you are requesting (Sponsor, Donator, or Contributor) from the "Sponsorship Type" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User** (Text, Optional): Enter your name and title as it appears on your business card or contact information.
* **Company Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your company as it appears on your business card or contact information.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your company email address. This will be the primary contact method for communication.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your company phone number. This will be used for follow-up calls and discussions.
* **Sponsorship Type** (Select One, Optional): This field is a multiple choice dropdown menu with three options: 
  - **Sponsor**: A person or entity that provides financial support for a project or event.
  - **Donator**: A person or entity that provides goods or services in-kind to support a project or event.
  - **Contributor**: A person or entity that provides expertise or knowledge to support a project or event.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant department or project lead.
- Ensure you are not requesting a sponsorship that is already covered.
