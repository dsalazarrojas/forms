# new-homeowner-onboarding-including-homeowner-checklist--business-forms - Help Guide

## Purpose

The New Homeowner Onboarding Including Homeowner Checklist Business Form is designed to capture important information from new homeowners, including their contact details and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and details before completing this form.
2. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (`Email`, optional): Enter your active and personal email address that you check frequently. This will be used for communication and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, optional): Enter your phone number that you can be reached at for any updates or notifications.
