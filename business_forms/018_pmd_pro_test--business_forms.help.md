# PMD Pro Test - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PMD Pro Test form is designed to collect information from users for internal tracking and recording purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required information in the **Form Name** field (`id: 1`).
2. Enter your **User Name** in the **UserName** field (`id: 2`).
3. Provide the desired **Date** in the **Date** field (`id: 3`).
4. Select the correct **Time** in the **Time** field (`id: 4`).
5. Enter your **Email** address in the **Email** field (`id: 5`).
6. Enter your **Phone** number in the **Phone** field (`id: 6`).
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the **Note** field (`id: 7`).
8. Select the relevant **Business Category** from the list (`id: 8`).
9. Choose the applicable **PMD Type** from the list (`id: 9`).
10. Enter the **Assigned Tool** name in the **Assigned Tool** field (`id: 10`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Name** (`id: 1`): A text field where you can enter a name for the form.
* **User Name** (`id: 2`): A text field where you can enter your name.
* **Date** (`id: 3`): A date field where you can select a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (`id: 4`): A time field where you can select a time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Email** (`id: 5`): An email field where you can enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 6`): A text field where you can enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`id: 7`): A text field for entering any additional notes or comments.
* **Business Category** (`id: 8`): A dropdown list where you can select a business category (Business or Other).
* **PMD Type** (`id: 9`): A multiple-select option list where you can choose one or more PMD types (Type A, Type B).
* **Assigned Tool** (`id: 10`): A text field where you can enter the name of the assigned tool.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (denoted by *) to ensure accurate submission.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
