# Service Termination Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Termination Request Form is used to request the termination of services from various service providers. This form helps gather information about the reason for termination, the date of termination request, and any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the reason for requesting service termination in the "Reason for Requesting Service Termination" field.
2.  Enter the date of the termination request in the "Date of Termination Request" field.
3.  If you have any additional comments, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
4.  Select the service providers you would like to request termination for in the "Service Providers" field.
5.  Choose the method of contact in the "Contact Method" field.
6.  If applicable, enter the service type in the "Service Type" field.
7.  Enter the name of the contact person in the "Contact Person" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Service Termination Request** (`service_termination_request_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of why you are requesting service termination.
*   **Reason for Requesting Service Termination** (`reason_for_requesting_service_termination`, text, optional): Enter a detailed explanation for why you want to terminate the service.
*   **Date of Termination Request** (`date_of_termination_request`, date, optional): Enter the date on which you are submitting this termination request.
*   **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which you expect the service to be terminated.
*   **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, text, optional): Enter the reason for termination if it is not already specified.
*   **Service Providers** (`service_providers`, select multiple, optional): Select the service providers you are requesting to terminate. You can select one or more providers from the provided list.
*   **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted after submitting this form, such as phone, email, or chat.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
*   **Service Type** (`service_type`, select one, optional): Select the type of service you are terminating, if applicable.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for your company or organization.

## Tips
*   Make sure to be specific and concise when filling out the form to help the team process your request efficiently.
*   If you are unsure about any of the information required, please contact your service provider or the internal support team for guidance.
