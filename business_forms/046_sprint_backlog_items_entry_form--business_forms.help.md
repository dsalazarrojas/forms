# Sprint Backlog Items Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sprint Backlog Items Entry Form is used to input new items into the sprint backlog, which is a collection of tasks and activities to be completed during a specific sprint. This form helps track progress and status of each item, ensuring that team members stay on track and have accurate information about the tasks at hand.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by a star (*) in the label.
2. Enter the project name where the item belongs to.
3. Select the task ID associated with the task.
4. Choose the current status of the task.
5. Enter the sprint ID where the task will be completed.
6. Specify the priority of the task.
7. Enter the estimated time required to complete the task.
8. Enter the actual time spent on the task.
9. Select the team members involved in the task.
10. Add any additional notes about the task.
11. Enter the email and phone number of the person responsible for the task.
12. Choose a category for the task (bug, feature, or other).
13. Assign the task to a team member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (Project Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the project where this task is part of.
* **Task Id** (Task Id, number, optional): Enter the unique identifier of the task.
* **Status** (Status, select one, optional): Select the current status of the task (Open, In Progress, Done).
* **Sprint Id** (Sprint Id, number, optional): Enter the unique identifier of the sprint where this task is part of.
* **Priority** (Priority, select one, optional): Choose the priority level of the task (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
* **Estimated Time** (Estimated Time, number, optional): Enter the estimated time required to complete the task.
* **Actual Time** (Actual Time, number, optional): Enter the actual time spent on the task.
* **Team Members** (Team Members, select multiple, optional): Select the team members involved in the task.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the task.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the task.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the task.
* **Category** (Category, select one, optional): Choose a category for the task (Bug, Feature, Task, Other).
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To, select one, optional): Assign the task to a team member.

## Tips
* Double-check the project name to ensure it is correct.
* Use the task ID to track the task's progress.
* Select the correct status to reflect the current progress.
* Enter accurate sprint ID to ensure the task is correctly assigned.
* Use the priority level to guide team members on the task's importance.
* Keep the estimated and actual time records up-to-date.
* Assign tasks accurately to team members to avoid confusion.
* Keep notes concise and relevant to the task.
* Ensure the email and phone number are correct and up-to-date.
