# rocket_launch_failure_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "rocket_launch_failure_incident_form" is a crucial tool for reporting and documenting failure incidents during rocket launches. It helps identify and categorize failures to improve future launches.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if the launch failed (page 1, "rocket_launch_failure_incident_form") and select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
2. Provide the launch date and time (page 2, "launch_date" and "launch_time") to help track the timeline of the failure.
3. Specify the type of rocket involved (page 3, "rocket_type") to ensure accurate identification.
4. Describe the failure type (page 4, "failure_type") to provide a clear understanding of what happened.
5. Provide a detailed description of the cause of the failure (page 6, "cause").
6. Indicate if the crew was involved (page 7, "crew_involved") and specify their involvement level.
7. If applicable, describe the damage (page 8, "damage_description").
8. Outline any corrective actions taken to prevent future failures (page 9, "corrective_actions").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rocket Launch Failure Incident Form (page 1)**: Indicates if the launch failed. Select "Yes" if the launch failed, "No" otherwise.
* **Launch Date (page 2)**: Enter the date of the launch.
* **Launch Time (page 3)**: Enter the time of the launch.
* **Rocket Type (page 4)**: Select the type of rocket involved in the failure.
* **Failure Type (page 5)**: Select the type of failure that occurred.
* **Cause (page 6)**: Provide a detailed description of the cause of the failure.
* **Crew Involved (page 7)**: Indicate if the crew was involved in the failure. Select "Yes" if they were involved, "No" otherwise.
* **Damage Description (page 8)**: If applicable, provide a detailed description of the damage caused by the failure.
* **Corrective Actions (page 9)**: Outline any corrective actions taken to prevent future failures.
