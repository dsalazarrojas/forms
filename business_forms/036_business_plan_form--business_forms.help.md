# Business Plan Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide explains each section of the Business Plan Form to help you complete it effectively and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing basic information about your business, including its name, description, mission, vision, and core values.
2. Choose the type of business structure you're planning to set up (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, etc.).
3. Describe the products or services your business will offer.
4. Identify your target market and the estimated market size.
5. Outline your competitive advantage and how you'll make money (revenue model).
6. Estimate your projected revenue for the first year and, if applicable, the third year.
7. Describe how you plan to manage your finances and when you expect to break even.
8. List your key team members and their roles.
9. Explain your marketing strategy.
10. Outline your operational plan.
11. Discuss your risk mitigation strategy.
12. Define your success metrics.
13. Set long-term goals for your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (1, text, required): Enter the official name of your business.
* **Business Description** (2, text, required): Describe what your business does.
* **Mission Statement** (3, text, required): Define your business mission.
* **Vision Statement** (4, text, required): Outline your long-term vision.
* **Core Values** (5, text, optional): List your core values guiding your business.
* **Business Structure** (6, select_one, required): Choose the type of business structure you'll set up (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, etc.).
* **Products or Services** (7, text, required): Describe the products or services your business will offer.
* **Target Market** (8, text, required): Identify your target market.
* **Target Market Size** (9, text, optional): Estimate the size of your target market.
* **Competitive Advantage** (10, text, required): Outline what makes you unique.
* **Startup Capital Required** (11, number, required): Estimate the initial investment needed to start your business.
* **Funding Sources** (12, text, optional): Describe how you plan to fund your business.
* **Revenue Model** (13, text, required): Explain how you'll make money.
* **Projected Revenue Year 1** (14, number, required): Estimate your revenue for the first year.
* **Projected Revenue Year 3** (15, number, optional): Estimate your revenue for the third year.
* **Break-Even Timeline** (16, text, optional): Describe when you expect to break even.
* **Key Team Members** (17, text, required): List your key team members and their roles.
* **Marketing Strategy** (18, text, required): Describe how you'll market your business.
* **Operational Plan** (19, text, required): Outline how you'll operate your business.
* **Risk Mitigation Strategy** (20, text, optional): Explain how you'll manage risks.
* **Success Metrics** (21, text, optional): Define how you'll measure success.
* **Long-term Goals** (22, text, optional): Set long-term goals for your business.
