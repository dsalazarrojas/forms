# Tip Pool Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to document and confirm an employee's tip pool agreement, which is an informal arrangement where employees pool their tips together for sharing or distribution. The form captures information about the tip-sharing policies, document distribution methods, and the employee's signature and contact details. It is an internal reference for the company to track and manage tip pool agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review and understand the tip-sharing policies, document distribution methods, and other relevant details.
2. Select the applicable policies and methods that apply to you.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
4. Enter your signature, email address, and phone number for contact information.
5. Optionally, add comments or remarks about the agreement or your role in the tip pool.
