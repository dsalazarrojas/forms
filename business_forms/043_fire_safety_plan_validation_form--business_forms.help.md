# Fire Safety Plan Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Plan Validation Form is designed to gather information about a company's fire safety plan to ensure that it meets the necessary standards and protocols to prevent and respond to fires.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Company Information** (`company_info`): Provide the company name, address, and contact information.
2. **Employee Information** (`employee_info`): List the name and contact information of the person responsible for implementing and maintaining the fire safety plan.
3. **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Provide the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
4. **Fire Safety Plan** (`fire_safety_plan`): Describe the company's fire safety plan, including procedures for prevention, detection, and response.
5. **Plan Approval** (`plan_approval`): Select one of the following options to indicate the status of the plan:
	* **Approved**: The plan has been approved by the relevant authorities.
	* **Not Approved**: The plan has not been approved by the relevant authorities.
	* **Pending**: The plan is pending approval.
6. **Fire Safety Plan Version** (`fire_safety_plan_version`): Enter the version number of the fire safety plan.
7. **Fire Safety Plan Status** (`fire_safety_plan_status`): Select one of the following options to indicate the status of the plan:
	* **Active**: The plan is active and currently being enforced.
	* **Inactive**: The plan is not currently being enforced.
	* **Not Applicable**: The plan is not applicable to the company or workplace.
8. **Fire Safety Plan Effectiveness** (`fire_safety_plan_effectiveness`): Select all of the following options that describe the effectiveness of the plan:
	* **Effective**: The plan is effective in preventing and responding to fires.
	* **Not Effective**: The plan is not effective in preventing and responding to fires.
	* **Pending**: The plan is pending evaluation.

## Tips

* Ensure that the company's fire safety plan is up-to-date and compliant with all relevant regulations and standards.
* Review and update the plan regularly to reflect changes in the workplace or industry.
* Train employees on the fire safety plan and ensure that they understand their roles and responsibilities.
* Conduct regular fire safety drills and training exercises to ensure that employees are prepared to respond to fires.
