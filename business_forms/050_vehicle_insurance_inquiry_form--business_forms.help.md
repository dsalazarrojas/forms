# Vehicle Insurance Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers about their vehicle insurance inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*) or are required for processing.
2. Enter your vehicle's make, year, model, color, and registration number in the respective fields.
3. Select your vehicle's insurance provider from the drop-down menu.
4. Enter your customer name, email, phone number, address, city, state, post office code, and country.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Customer note" field.
6. Sign the form with your signature in the "Customer signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle make (Vehicle make)**: Enter your vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle year (Vehicle year)**: Enter your vehicle's year of production.
* **Vehicle model (Vehicle model)**: Enter your vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle color (Vehicle color)**: Enter your vehicle's color.
* **Vehicle registration number (Vehicle registration number)**: Enter your vehicle's registration number.
* **Vehicle insurance provider (Vehicle insurance provider)**: Select your vehicle's insurance provider from the drop-down menu.
* **Other vehicle insurance provider (Vehicle insurance provider)**: Enter the name of your vehicle's insurance provider if it's not listed in the drop-down menu.
* **Customer name (Customer name)**: Enter your customer name.
* **Customer email (Customer email)**: Enter your customer's email address.
* **Customer phone (Customer phone)**: Enter your customer's phone number.
* **Customer address (Customer address)**: Enter your customer's address.
* **Customer city (Customer city)**: Enter your customer's city.
* **Customer state (Customer state)**: Enter your customer's state.
* **Customer post office code (Customer post office code)**: Enter your customer's post office code.
* **Customer country (Customer country)**: Enter your customer's country.
* **Customer note (Customer note)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the customer.
* **Customer signature (Customer signature)**: Sign the form with your signature.

## Tips

* Ensure that the vehicle registration number and insurance provider information are accurate.
* Please provide your customer's signature for verification purposes.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
