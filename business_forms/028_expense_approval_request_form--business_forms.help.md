## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company name and department.
2. If you are submitting expenses for a specific location, select the location in the corresponding field.
3. If the expense has a date, enter the date in the corresponding field.
4. Select the approver's name, title, email, and phone number from the provided options.

7. If necessary, add additional details in the subcategories or comments.

9. Finally, select the submission option to submit the form.
