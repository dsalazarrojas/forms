**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Expense Type**: Select the type of expense (e.g., travel, meal, etc.). 
* **Category**: Select the category of the expense (e.g., travel, meals, etc.).
* **Submit and Approve/Reject**: Select the option to submit and approve or reject your expense request. 

This field guide is for users who are unsure of the form purpose, usage or any unclear field labels. If any field remains unclear, please contact the approver for clarification.
