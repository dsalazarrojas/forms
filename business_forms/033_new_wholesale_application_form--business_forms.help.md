# new_wholesale_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from businesses that wish to become wholesale customers. This form will help us to understand your business details and evaluate your application for our wholesale program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your business name in the `Business Name` field.
2. Provide a detailed description of your business in the `Business Information` field.
3. Specify the main intention of your wholesale application.
4. If your business has a resellers permit, please provide the permit number in the `Resellers Permit` field.
5. Enter your address for shipping in the `Address for Shipping` field.
6. Upload your business license in the `Business License` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: 
	+ Label: Business Name
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	Enter the name of your business as it appears on your license and marketing materials.
* **Business Information**: 
	+ Label: business_information
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	Please provide a detailed description of your business, including the type of products or services offered and the scope of your operations.
* **Intention**: 
	+ Label: intention
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	Please specify the main intention of your wholesale application.
* **Resellers Permit**: 
	+ Label: resellers_permit
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	If your business has a resellers permit, please enter the permit number.
* **Address for Shipping**: 
	+ Label: address_for_shipping
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	Enter the address where you would like to receive your wholesale shipments.
* **Business License**: 
	+ Label: business_license
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	Upload your business license, so we can verify your credentials.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the fields accurately and thoroughly.
* You can upload your business license as a file or enter the license number.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
