# Product Recall Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Product Recall Verification Form" is used to collect information about a recalled product and the customer who reported it. This helps us to investigate and resolve product recall issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide details about the recalled product in the "Product Recall Information" section.
2. Enter the customer's information, including their contact number and email address in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Recall Information** (`product_recall_info`, note, required: false): This section is where you provide detailed information about the recalled product.
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's name here, if available.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's contact number here.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the customer's email address here. Make sure to enter a valid email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide detailed information about the recalled product to help us investigate and resolve the issue efficiently.
* Enter the customer's information accurately to ensure we can contact them back if needed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
