# Recycled Fabric Vendor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential vendors who want to work with us as a recycled fabric vendor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your vendor information, including your business name and address.
2. Enter your contact information, including the first and last name of the contact person and their email address.
3. Specify the type of vendor you are (Fabric Supplier, Wholesaler, Distributor, or Other).
4. Explain why you are joining as a vendor.
5. Add any additional information you would like to include about your fabric products or services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_info`, text, required: false): Enter your business name and address. 
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter the first and last name of the contact person and their email address. 
* **Fabric Information** (`fabric_info`, text, required: false): Add any additional information about your fabric products or services. 
* **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, text, required: false): Provide information about how you plan to ship your fabric products. 
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): Specify how you plan to handle payment for your fabric products. 
* **Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, select one, required: false): Choose one of the options: Fabric Supplier, Wholesaler, Distributor, or Other. 
* **Why are you joining** (`application_reason`, note, required: false): Explain why you are joining as a vendor. 
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the contact person. 
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the contact person. 
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person. 
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please contact us.
* We'll review your application and get back to you soon.
* If you have any issues with the form, please let us know.
* Thank you for your interest in working with us as a recycled fabric vendor!
