# Outlet Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Outlet Evaluation Form is used to collect information about an outlet, including its name, product offerings, and performance details. It helps track performance metrics, identify areas for improvement, and gather customer feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Provide a brief description of the outlet in the "Form Description" field.
2. Enter the name of the outlet you are evaluating.
3. Select the product offered at the outlet from the provided options.
4. Rate the outlet's performance on a scale (though the specific rating scale is not defined in the form, it's assumed to be a standard 1-5 rating system).
5. Enter the date and time of your visit to the outlet.
6. Provide any additional notes about your visit.
7. If you have any contact information for the outlet, please enter it in the relevant fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Description**: Enter a brief summary of the outlet, including its products or services offered. (~50 characters max)
* **Outlet Name**: Type the name of the outlet you are evaluating.
* **Product**: Select the product offered by the outlet.
* **Rating**: Rate the outlet's performance out of 5, where 1 is low and 5 is high. (Note: this field is not defined in the form options, but is a standard 1-5 rating system).
* **Date**: Enter the date of your visit to the outlet in the standard date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Time**: Enter the time of your visit to the outlet in the standard time format (HH:MM).
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or observations about your visit.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person completing this form, and the contact person for the outlet.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person completing this form, and the contact person for the outlet.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person completing this form, and the contact person for the outlet.
* **Contact Title**: Enter the title or position of the person completing this form, and the contact person for the outlet.
