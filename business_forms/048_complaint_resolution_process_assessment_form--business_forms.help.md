# Complaint Resolution Process Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who have completed the complaint resolution process to provide feedback on their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form only after you have received a resolution email with a unique Case Reference ID.
2. Answer each question with care, providing honest and specific feedback.
3. If you wish to provide additional information, use the "Process Improvement Suggestions" field.
4. Click on the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Case ID**: Please refer to the ID on your resolution email to fill in this field.
* **How easy was it to file your complaint?**: Rate your experience from 1 (Difficult) to 5 (Very Easy).
* **Satisfaction with the speed of our initial response**: Rate your satisfaction with how quickly we first contacted you.
* **Professionalism and empathy shown by our staff**: Rate how you were treated during the process.
* **Clarity of information provided throughout the process**: Rate how well you were kept informed of progress.
* **Fairness of the final resolution**: Rate your belief in the fairness of the outcome.
* **How likely are you to continue using our services?**: Rate your willingness to use our services after this experience.
* **Process Improvement Suggestions**: Provide any specific ideas you have for enhancing our complaint handling process.
* **Date of Assessment**: This field will automatically fill in the current date.
* **Thank you for helping us improve our service**: Write a final message to express your appreciation for your help in improving our service.
