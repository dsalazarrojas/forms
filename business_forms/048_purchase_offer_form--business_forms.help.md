# Purchase Offer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for a purchase offer between the buyer and the seller, ensuring clear communication and understanding of the terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your buyer information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Enter the product information you are interested in purchasing.
3. Specify any purchase terms you'd like to discuss.
4. Choose a payment method that suits your needs.
5. Enter your payment information (e.g., email address for electronic transfer).
6. Sign the form to confirm your intent.
7. Date and time of signing.
8. Enter your seller information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
9. Optionally, enter seller's address.
10. Sign and date the form to confirm the seller's acceptance of your offer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Buyer Information** (`purchase_offer_form_1`, text, required: false): This field is for the buyer to enter their name, which will be used as a reference for the purchase offer.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address, so the seller can get in touch with you.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, required: false): Describe the product or service you are interested in purchasing.
* **Purchase Terms** (`purchase_terms`, text, required: false): Specify any terms or conditions you'd like to discuss regarding the purchase.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required: false): Clarify any payment-related terms or conditions.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): Enter your payment information, such as your email address or bank account details, for electronic payment.
* **Buyer Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Sign this form to confirm your intent to purchase.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, number, required: false): Enter the date and time you signed this form.
* **Buyer EMail** (`buyer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication with the seller.
* **Buyer Phone** (`buyer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for the seller to get in touch with you.
* **Seller Info** (`seller_info`, text, required: false): Enter the seller's name, which will help identify them for the purchase offer.
* **Seller EMail** (`seller_email`, email, required: false): Enter the seller's email address for communication.
* **Seller Phone** (`seller_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the seller's phone number.
* **Seller Address** (`seller_address`, text, required: false): Enter the seller's address for reference.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Select a payment method that suits your needs, such as Bank Transfer, Wire Transfer, Check, or PayPal.
* **Payment Method Options:**
	+ Bank Transfer: Transfer payment directly from your bank account.
	+ Wire Transfer: Send a wire transfer for payment.
	+ Check: Pay by check.
	+ PayPal: Use a PayPal account for payment.

## Tips
1. Make sure to fill out all required fields correctly.
2. Be clear and concise in your responses.
3. Use a pen and paper for signing the form for better legibility and authenticity.
4. Keep a copy of the signed form for future reference.
