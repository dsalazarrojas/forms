# subscription_cancellation_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The subscription cancellation claim form is used to request the cancellation of a subscription. This form will gather necessary information to process the cancellation and ensure a smooth transition for the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Cancellation Request field with a clear and concise description of your request.
2. Provide the reason for your cancellation in the Reason for Cancellation field.
3. Select the subscription type that you would like to cancel.
4. Enter your customer email address in the Customer Email field.
5. Enter your customer phone number in the Customer Phone field.
6. Choose a date for the cancellation in the Cancellation Date field.
7. Provide additional details for your cancellation reason in the Reason for Cancellation Details field.
8. Enter your customer name in the Customer Name field.
9. Enter your customer ID (if applicable) in the Customer ID field.
10. Add any comments or notes in the Comments field.
11. Finally, click the submit button to complete the form and initiate the cancellation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cancellation Request**: A field to enter a concise description of your cancellation request. 
* **Reason for Cancellation**: A field to provide the reason you want to cancel your subscription. 
* **Subscription Type**: A dropdown menu to select the type of subscription you wish to cancel (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Customer Email**: A field to enter your customer email address.
* **Customer Phone**: A field to enter your customer phone number.
* **Cancellation Date**: A field to select a date for the cancellation.
* **Reason for Cancellation Details**: A field to provide additional information about your cancellation reason.
* **Customer Name**: A field to enter your customer name.
* **Customer ID**: A field to enter your customer ID (if applicable).
* **Comments**: A field to add any comments or notes about your cancellation.
