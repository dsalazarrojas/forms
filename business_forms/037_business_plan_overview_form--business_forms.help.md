# Business Plan Overview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Plan Overview Form is a tool designed to collect essential information about your business model, its development stage, target customers, key products or services, unique value proposition, and other crucial aspects. This information will help us evaluate and support your business idea, providing a comprehensive overview of your business plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill in your personal details, including your full name, contact email, and phone number.
2.  Enter your business name, select the industry sector and business type that best describe your venture.
3.  Briefly describe your business, its mission, and its current development stage.
4.  Identify your target customers, key products or services, unique value proposition, and competitive positioning.
5.  Provide details on your estimated startup costs, initial funding amount, and how you plan to use the funds.
6.  If your business is already established, provide information about your team size and their key expertise.
7.  If applicable, project your first year's revenue and the timeline for reaching profitability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Entrepreneur Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
*   **Email Address** (`<email>`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`<phone>`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Business Name** (`<business_name>`, `text`, required): Enter your business's name.
*   **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, `select_one`, required): Select the industry sector that best describes your business.
*   **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of business you are (e.g., B2B, B2C, etc.).
*   **Brief Business Description** (`brief_description`, `text`, required): Describe your business in 50-100 words.
*   **Mission Statement** (`mission_statement`, `text`, required): Write a one-sentence mission statement for your business.
*   **Business Stage** (`business_stage`, `select_one`, required): Select the current development stage of your business.
*   **Target Customers** (`target_customers`, `text`, required): Identify your ideal customers.
*   **Key Products or Services** (`key_products_services`, `text`, required): Describe the key products or services you will offer.
*   **Unique Value Proposition** (`unique_value_proposition`, `text`, required): Explain why customers should choose your business over others.
*   **Competitive Positioning** (`competitive_positioning`, `text`, required): Describe where your business fits in the market.
*   **Estimated Startup Costs** (`startup_costs`, `number`, required): Enter the estimated total capital needed for your business.
*   **Initial Funding Amount** (`initial_funding_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the initial funding amount you are raising.
*   **Use of Funds** (`use_of_funds`, `text`, required): Explain how you plan to use the funds.
*   **Team Size** (`team_size`, `number`, `false`): If your business is already established, enter the number of team members.
*   **Key Team Expertise** (`key_team_expertise`, `text`, false): If your business is already established, describe your team's strengths.
*   **First Year Revenue Target** (`first_year_revenue_target`, `number`, `false`): If applicable, project your first year's revenue.
*   **Profitability Timeline** (`profitability_timeline`, `text`, `false`): If applicable, describe when you expect to be profitable.
