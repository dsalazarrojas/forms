# Mother's Day Giveaway Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mother's Day Giveaway Form is a template designed to collect user information for a giveaway event on Mother's Day. It is meant to be filled out by users interested in participating in the giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your User Information: Fill out the User Information section with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Choose Your Gift: Select one of the gift options (A, B, or C) that you would like to receive.
3. Enter Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY) in the Date of Birth field.
4. Provide Your Email: Enter your email address to help us contact you if you win.
5. Enter Your Phone Number: Enter your phone number for further communication.
6. Add a Message: If you'd like, you can add a message to express your gratitude or share your thoughts.
7. Add a Gift Message: If you'd like, you can leave a message for the gift recipient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (user_info, text, optional): This field is where you can input your name and any other information you'd like to share.
  - **Gift Choice** (gift_choice, select_one, optional): Choose one of the three gift options (A, B, or C) that you prefer.
  - **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address to help us contact you if you win.
  - **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
  - **Message** (message, note, optional): Add a message to express your gratitude or thoughts.
  - **Gift Message** (gift_message, note, optional): If you'd like, leave a personal message for the gift recipient.

This form does not require any additional information, and users can customize it as needed for their specific giveaway event. Please ensure that you are entering accurate and relevant information for your participation.
