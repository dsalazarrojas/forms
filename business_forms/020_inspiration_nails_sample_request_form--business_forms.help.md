# <inspiration_nails_sample_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for collecting customer details and sample requests for inspiration nails.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Customer Details**.
2. Provide your **First Name** and **Last Name**.
3. Enter your valid **Email** address.
4. Enter your **Phone** number.
5. Describe the **Sample Details** you would like to request.
6. Select the **Product Name** of the inspiration nail sample you are interested in.
7. Enter the **Product Code** of the product you are interested in.
8. Select all the **Sample Request** types you would like to request (e.g. Nail Polish, Nail Lacquer, etc.).
9. Choose the **Sample Type** of the product you are interested in (e.g. Nail Lacquer, Nail Polish, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**: Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address so we can contact you about your sample request.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your sample request.
* **Sample Details**: Describe the details of the sample you are requesting (e.g. color, finish, etc.).
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you are interested in.
* **Product Code**: Enter the code of the product you are interested in.
* **Sample Request**: Select all the sample request types you are interested in (e.g. Nail Polish, Nail Lacquer, etc.).
* **Sample Type**: Choose the type of sample you are interested in (e.g. Nail Lacquer, Nail Polish, etc.).
