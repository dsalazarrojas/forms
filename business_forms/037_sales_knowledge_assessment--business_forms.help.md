# Sales Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gauge the level of sales knowledge of a specific employee or group of employees in an organization. This information helps in identifying areas that require improvement and development opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please provide the name of your sales team (field 1).
2. Check the types of sales skills you possess, such as sales process, communication, and presentation skills (fields 3, 4, 5).
3. Answer questions about your sales tactic usage, such as 'Yes' or 'No' for 'Do you use the sales tactic?' and 'Which sales tactic?' (fields 6, 7, 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Knowledge Assessment** (`sales_knowledge_assessment`, text, optional): Please provide the name of your sales team.
* **Sales Team** (`sales_team`, text, optional): Enter the name of your sales team.
* **Sales Process** (`sales_process`, select multiple, optional): Select all sales process skills you are familiar with. You can choose from:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, text, optional): Describe your communication skills.
* **Presentation Skills** (`presentation_skills`, text, optional): Describe your presentation skills.
* **Sales Tactic** (`sales_tactic`, select one, optional): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to whether you use the sales tactic.
* **Sales Tactic 2** (`sales_tactic_2`, select multiple, optional): Select all sales tactic 2 skills you use. You can choose from:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Sales Tactic 3** (`sales_tactic_3`, select one, optional): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to whether you use the sales tactic 3.

## Tips
- Please provide clear and concise answers to all fields.
- Be honest in your assessment of your sales skills and tactics.
- You can skip fields if you don't have any experience with the sales skills or tactics listed.
