# legal-case-summary-report-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The legal-case-summary-report-form is designed to gather information about a legal case in a summary format.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Case Summary** (text, required): Enter a brief summary of the legal case.
* **Case Details** (text, optional): Provide any additional information about the case.
* **Case Status** (select_one, optional): Select the current status of the case from the options "New", "Active", or "Closed".
* **Court Location** (text, optional): Enter the location of the court where the case is being heard.
* **Case Facts** (text, optional): Enter any additional facts or information about the case.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have all necessary information about the case before filling out this form.
* Use the "Case Status" field to keep track of the current status of the case.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with your supervisor or legal expert before proceeding.
