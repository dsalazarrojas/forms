# lash_training_center_partnership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for partnership applications from training centers that offer lash and beauty training services. It collects necessary details to assess the center's capabilities, interests, and qualifications for partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the business details, including any relevant information about the center.
2. Provide your contact details, including email, phone number, and name.
3. Select the specific location of your training center (e.g., Brisbane, Melbourne, Sydney).
4. Indicate your partnership interest and specify the reasons for your interest.
5. Enter the training center's operating hours.
6. Enter the training center's capacity (number of people).
7. Select any relevant equipment offered by the training center.
8. Choose the services offered by the training center (e.g., training, certification, consultation).
9. Enter the partner's contact information, including name, company, and title.
10. Review and agree to the submission terms and conditions.
11. Confirm the partner agreement.
12. Verify the training center's licensing status.
13. Confirm if the training center has insurance.
14. Select any certified professionals working at the training center.
15. Finally, submit the form to apply for partnership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Details (Page 1)
*   **Business Details** (Business Details, Text, Required): Enter a brief description of your training center.
*   **Business Details** (Business Details, Text, Required): Enter a brief description of your training center.

### Contact Details (Page 2)
*   **Contact Details** (Contact Details, Text, Required): Enter your name for contact purposes.
*   **Partner Contact** (Partner Contact, Email, Required): Enter your email address.
*   **Partner Phone** (Partner Phone, Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Partner Email** (Partner Email, Email, Required): Enter your email address.
*   **Partner Name** (Partner Name, Text, Required): Enter your name.
*   **Partner Company** (Partner Company, Text, Required): Enter your company name.
*   **Partner Title** (Partner Title, Text, Required): Enter your title.

### Training Center Details (Page 3)
*   **Training Center Location** (Training Center Location, Select One, Required): Select the location of your training center (e.g., Brisbane, Melbourne, Sydney).
*   **Partnership Interest** (Partnership Interest, Select One, Required): Indicate the reasons for your interest in partnership (e.g., General Interest, Specific Interest, Referral).
*   **Training Center Hours** (Training Center Hours, Time, Required): Enter your training center's operating hours (e.g., Monday to Friday 9-5 PM).
*   **Training Center Capacity** (Training Center Capacity, Number, Required): Enter the capacity of your training center (number of people).
*   **Training Center Equipment** (Training Center Equipment, Select Multiple, Required): Select any relevant equipment offered by the training center (e.g., Lash Lifting, Brow Lifting, Brow Tinting, Lash Tinting, Nail Art).
*   **Training Center Services** (Training Center Services, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the services offered by the training center (e.g., Training, Certification, Consultation).
*   **Training Center Licensing** (Training Center Licensing, Select One, Required): Verify the training center's licensing status (Active, Inactive, Pending).
*   **Training Center Insurance** (Training Center Insurance, Select Multiple, Required): Confirm if the training center has insurance (Yes, No).
*   **Training Center Certification** (Training Center Certification, Select Multiple, Required): Select any certified professionals working at the training center (e.g., Lash Lifting, Brow Lifting, Brow Tinting, Lash Tinting, Nail Art).

### Submission (Page 4)
*   **Submission Terms and Conditions** (Submission Terms and Conditions, Text, Required): Review and agree to the submission terms and conditions.
*   **Partner Agreement** (Partner Agreement, Text, Required): Confirm your partner agreement.
*   **Submission** (Submission, Text, Required): Submit the form to apply for partnership.
*   **Reset** (Reset, Text, Optional): Reset the form.

**Tips**

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
*   Review the submission terms and conditions carefully before submitting the form.
*   Make sure to agree to the partner agreement.
*   Double-check the training center's licensing status and insurance.
*   Select certified professionals working at the training center.
*   Submit the form carefully to avoid errors.
