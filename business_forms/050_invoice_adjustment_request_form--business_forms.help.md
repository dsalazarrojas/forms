# invoice_adjustment_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "invoice_adjustment_request_form" is a form designed to request adjustments to invoices. This form is used to provide details about the issue with the invoice, and the customer or representative who is requesting the adjustment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Select the date of the original invoice in the "Invoice Date" field.
3. Enter the overcharge amount if the issue is an overcharge, or undercharge amount if it's an undercharge.
4. Select the type of issue in the "Billing Issue" field.
5. Enter any additional details about the overcharge or undercharge in the "Overcharge Details" or "Undercharge Details" fields, depending on the issue.
6. If necessary, enter the correct invoice details in the "Correct Invoice" field.
7. Upload any relevant attachments.
8. Enter any additional information or internal notes in the "Additional Info", "Internal Note", "Requester Internal Note", "Requester Phone", "Requester Email", and "Customer Address" fields.
9. Enter the approver's name and any additional internal notes in the "Approver" and "Requester Internal Note" fields.
10. Review and submit the form.
