# cosmetics_brand_ambassador_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is for individuals interested in becoming a brand ambassador for our cosmetics company. We're looking for people to represent our brand and promote our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your contact information on the first page, including your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Address.
2. On the second page, answer questions about your work experience and the brands you have represented in the past.
3. On the third page, upload relevant files that demonstrate your interest in representing our brand.
4. Finally, write a brief bio about yourself, and optionally, add a photo to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This field is for your first name, please fill it in as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: This is your last name, please fill it in as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: This is your official email address where we can reach you. Please ensure it's accurate and up to date.
* **Phone**: This is your contact phone number, we'll use it to reach you for further discussions.
* **Address**: This is your physical address, please fill it in accurately so we can get in touch with you.
* **Work Experience**: This field allows you to choose how many years you have worked in the industry, select one of the options: 0-2 years, 3-5 years, or More than 5 years.
* **Brands Represented**: This is where you select the brands you've represented in the past, choose all that apply.
* **Bio**: This is your brief description about yourself, please keep it short and professional.
* **Photo**: This is an optional field where you can add a photo that best represents you.
* **Uploaded Files**: This is where you can upload files that demonstrate your interest in representing our brand, such as certifications, awards or other relevant documents.
* **Photo**: This is not a mandatory field, but we encourage you to add a photo that best represents you and your personality.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when filling in your information.
* Make sure the uploaded files are relevant and correctly named.
* Use your best judgment when selecting your work experience and represented brands.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
