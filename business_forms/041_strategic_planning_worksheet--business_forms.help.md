# Strategic Planning Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strategic Planning Worksheet is a tool designed to assist teams and individuals in planning and tracking their strategic objectives, obstacles, and progress. It guides the process of defining project goals, identifying potential obstacles and risk factors, and creating a plan for achieving those objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each page of the form, starting from "Team Name" and moving through to the end.
- Ensure that all relevant fields are filled out for each page.
- Be specific and concise when answering questions, especially when describing potential obstacles and risk factors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your team or the team working on this project.
* **Objective** (`objective`, text, required: false): Clearly describe the main objective or goal of this project.
* **Strategy Title** (`strategy_title`, text, required: false): Give a title to your strategy that reflects the overall approach to achieving the project objective.
* **Goals** (`goals_list`, select multiple, required: false): List all specific, measurable goals related to the project objective. Choose all relevant options from the list provided.
* **Vision** (`vision_statement`, text, required: false): Describe the long-term vision or desired outcome for this project.
* **Mission** (`mission_statement`, text, required: false): Define the specific objectives or actions needed to achieve the project vision.
* **Progress Tracker** (`progress_tracker`, date, required: false): Track progress and changes over time by selecting a date for when you last updated this information.
* **Target Date** (`target_date`, date, required: false): Set a target completion date for the project.
* **Progress Notes** (`progress_notes`, text, required: false): Record any progress or observations made during the project.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, select one, required: false): Determine the next course of action based on progress or obstacles encountered.
* **Resources Required** (`resources_required`, select multiple, required: false): Identify all resources required to achieve the project objectives.
* **Obstacles** (`obstacles`, select multiple, required: false): List all potential obstacles or challenges that might hinder the project objectives.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, select multiple, required: false): Identify all potential risk factors that might jeopardize the project.
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, select one, required: false): Assess the risks and consequences of taking action.
* **Review Schedule** (`review_schedule`, number, required: false): Decide on a review schedule for the project's progress, if necessary.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required: false): Schedule a review date for the project's progress, if necessary.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select multiple, required: false): List all team members involved in this planning cycle.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, text, required: false): Identify the team leader or contact person for this project.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Identify the person to contact for further questions or concerns.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional information or comments relevant to the project.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select one, required: false): Determine the current review status of the project, whether active or inactive.
