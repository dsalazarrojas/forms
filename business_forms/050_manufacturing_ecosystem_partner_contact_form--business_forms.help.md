# Manufacturing Ecosystem Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for manufacturers who want to get in touch with our team to collaborate on potential partnerships within the manufacturing ecosystem. It collects information about the partner's contact details, product, and submission requirements to facilitate a smooth collaboration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you are a legitimate representative of a manufacturing company. If you're not, please don't fill this form as it's intended for partner collaborations.
2.  Scroll through the form and fill in your contact information on the first page.
3.  On the second page, provide details about your products, such as product name, description, and relevant images.
4.  On the third page, specify any submission requirements or additional information you'd like us to consider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Contact Information** (`contact_info` , text, required): Please fill in your name, company name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
*  **Product Information** (`product_info` , text, required): Describe your product(s) in a few sentences, including any relevant images or links to your product pages.
*  **Submission Information** (`submission_info` , text, required): Specify any specific submission requirements, such as file formats, sizes, or deadlines.

## Tips
*   Make sure to proofread your submission before submitting it for any grammatical or factual errors.
*   Be as detailed as possible to ensure a smooth review process.
*   If you have any attachments, such as product images or documents, please ensure they're properly formatted and accessible to us.
