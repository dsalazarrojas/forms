# digital_talent_strategy_consulting_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The digital talent strategy consulting application form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in exploring digital talent strategy consulting services. This form aims to gather essential details about the applicant, their educational background, job history, and other relevant information to assess their fit for our consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the necessary information in the **Applicant Details** field.
2. Provide details about your **Education Background** in the **Education Details** field.
3. Describe your current and previous work experience in the **Job Details** and **Work History** fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Details**: This field is optional and allows you to introduce yourself and provide any necessary context for your application.
* **Education Details**: Share your educational background, including any degrees earned, institutions attended, and relevant courses taken.
* **Job Details**: Describe your current job role and responsibilities, as well as any relevant work experience.
* **Work History**: Share your work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth evaluation process.
* If you have any relevant certifications, skills, or achievements, feel free to include them in the relevant fields.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, don't hesitate to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
