## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (id: 1): Enter your first name to help us get to know you better.
* **Last Name** (id: 2): Enter your last name to complete your contact information.
* **Email** (id: 3): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you with follow-up questions or information.
* **Job Title** (id: 4): Enter your job title or position to understand your role in the collaboration.
* **Organization** (id: 5): Enter the name of your organization to help us understand your affiliation.
* **Contact Number** (id: 6): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Partner Name** (id: 7): Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Inquiry** (id: 8): Choose the type of collaboration you are interested in: Human Capital or Business Partner.
* **Message** (id: 9): Enter a brief message about your collaboration interests or questions.
* **Date and Time** (id: 10): Enter the date and time you would prefer to collaborate.
* **Time** (id: 11): Enter the time of day you prefer to collaborate.
* **Assigned To** (id: 12): Select one or multiple team members you would like to be assigned to your collaboration.
* **Follow-up** (id: 13): Select the follow-up time frame you prefer (1 week, 2 weeks, or 3 weeks).
* **Follow-up Date** (id: 14): Enter the follow-up date you would like to schedule.
