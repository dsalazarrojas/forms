## Human Capital Strategy Partner Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential partners interested in collaborating on the Human Capital Strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Fill in your contact information
* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your work email address.
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Organization** (text, optional): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.

### Step 2: Choose your collaboration details
* **Inquiry** (select_one, required): Select one of the options provided: Human Capital or Business Partner.
* **Message** (note, optional): Enter a brief message about your collaboration interests.
* **Date and Time** (date, optional): Enter the date and time you would prefer to collaborate.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of day you prefer to collaborate.
* **Assigned To** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple team members you would like to be assigned to your collaboration.
* **Follow-up** (select_one, optional): Select the follow-up time frame you prefer (1 week, 2 weeks, or 3 weeks).
* **Follow-up Date** (date, optional): Enter the follow-up date you would like to schedule.

### Tips
* Please be as specific as possible in your inquiry and message.
* If you have any additional details or questions, please include them in the message field.
* If you have any scheduling conflicts or preferences, please let us know in the Follow-up field.
* Please ensure that you select a real name and date/time to facilitate smooth communication and planning.
