# Energy Management Support Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Energy Management Support Contact Form is designed to provide a structured way for users to request support with energy management. Please use this form to provide detailed information about your energy management setup and the issues you are experiencing.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely, including selecting all relevant fields that apply to your energy management setup.
2.  Be as detailed as possible when describing your issue in the "Describe Your Issue" field.
3.  If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask a support representative for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, `text`, not required): This field is where you can enter a brief message or question you have about energy management support. Please keep your message clear and concise.
*   **What is your top priority?** (`priority`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all the areas that best describe your top priority. Please choose all that apply.
*   **What area of support do you need?** (`support_area`, `select_one`, not required): Select the area of support you need help with.
*   **Your Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, not required): Please provide a valid contact number or email address so we can contact you for further clarification or resolution.
*   **Which energy system(s) are you using?** (`energy_systems`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all the energy systems you are currently using.
*   **Describe Your Issue** (`issue_description`, `text`, not required): Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing with your energy management setup.
*   **Which energy tools are you using?** (`energy_tools`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all the energy tools you are currently using.
*   **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, not required): If there is any other relevant information you would like to share with our support team, please enter it here.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to fill out all the relevant fields to ensure that our support team can assist you effectively.
*   Be as detailed as possible when describing your issue.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask a support representative for clarification.
