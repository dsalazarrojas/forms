# Monthly Business Contribution Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Business Contribution Report is a form used to collect and track business contributions from various departments within a company on a monthly basis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the department(s) that made contributions.
2. Enter the month in which the contributions were made.
3. Fill in the total contributions made by the selected department(s).
4. Check the contribution status as 'Yes' or 'No' if you want to record it.
5. Provide a brief description of the contribution(s).
6. Enter the contribution goal(s) achieved by the department(s).
7. Enter a detailed description of the contribution(s).
8. Check the contribution success as 'Yes' or 'No' if you want to record it.
9. Enter any additional details about the contribution(s).
10. If applicable, select the contribution type and enter the contribution date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department** (Select multiple): Choose the department(s) that contributed to the monthly business goals. Select all departments that contributed to the goal.
* **Contribution Month** (Date): Enter the month in which the contributions were made (e.g., January 2024).
* **Total Contributions** (Number): Fill in the total contributions made by the selected department(s).
* **Contribution Status** (Select one): Check 'Yes' if the contribution is successful, 'No' otherwise.
* **Contribution Descriptions** (Text): Provide a brief description of the contribution(s) made by the department(s).
* **Contribution Goals** (Text): Enter the contribution goal(s) achieved by the department(s).
* **Contribution Description** (Text): Enter a detailed description of the contribution(s).
* **Contribution Success** (Select one): Check 'Yes' if the contribution is successful, 'No' otherwise.
* **Contribution Details** (Text): Enter any additional details about the contribution(s).
* **Contribution Type** (Select multiple): If applicable, select the contribution type (e.g., Marketing, Sales, Support, Development).
* **Contribution Date** (Date): Enter the date of the contribution, if applicable.
* **Contribution Goal** (Date): Enter the date of the contribution goal achievement, if applicable.
