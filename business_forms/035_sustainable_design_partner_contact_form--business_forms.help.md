# Sustainable Design Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and details about your company or organization from potential partners and collaborators in the sustainable design field. The form is used to facilitate communication and coordination with external parties interested in sustainable design projects or partnerships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company's name and address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide a brief description of your company's sustainable design focus or services offered.
3. Select the type of company you represent (Sustainable Products, Sustainable Services, or Other).
4. If applicable, provide your company's website, email, and fax number.
5. Fill in your representative's name and job title.
6. Provide your preferred contact method (email, phone, or both).
7. If necessary, indicate whether you would like to receive follow-up emails from us.
8. If you would like to receive follow-up emails, provide a message or note for us.
9. If applicable, schedule a follow-up call or meeting with a specific date and time.
10. Fill in any additional notes or comments about your company or contact preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address for communication with your company.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your company's phone number for direct calls.
* **Company Address**: Enter your company's physical address for deliveries or visits.
* **Company Description**: Provide a brief description of your company's sustainable design focus or services offered.
* **Job Title**: Enter your representative's job title for easy identification.
* **First Name**: Enter your representative's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your representative's last name.
* **Company Website**: Enter your company's website URL for easy access.
* **Company Type**: Select the type of company you represent (Sustainable Products, Sustainable Services, or Other).
* **Company Email**: Provide a valid email address for your company.
* **Company Fax**: Enter your company's fax number for faxing documents.
* **Company Representative**: Enter the name of the person representing your company.
* **Department**: Enter the department or team within your company that handles sustainable design projects.
* **Company Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your company.
* **Message**: Provide a message or note for us to know your company's interests or needs.
* **Contact Requesting**: If you're looking for a contact person or team member, indicate this here.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred contact method (email, phone, or both).
* **Follow-Up Email**: Indicate whether you would like to receive follow-up emails from us.
* **Follow-Up Message**: If you would like to receive follow-up emails, provide a message or note for us.
* **Follow-Up Time**: Suggest a specific time for a follow-up call or meeting.
* **Follow-Up Date**: Suggest a specific date for a follow-up call or meeting.
* **Form Submission ID**: This field is automatically generated and should not be filled in.
* **User Email**: This field is for internal system use and should not be filled in.
* **User Name**: This field is for internal system use and should not be filled in.
