# Freelancer Contract Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freelancer Contract Approval Form is used to obtain approval for a freelancer's contract with a client. This form is a crucial step in the process of ensuring that all necessary information and agreements are in place before commencing work on a project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the contract details and select the appropriate options from the dropdown list.
2. Fill out the freelancer information section with the relevant details.
3. Enter the contract date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Indicate whether the client information is available or not.
5. Enter the contract terms in the text field.
6. Select the date of the signature.
7. Review and submit the form once all fields are complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract details**: Select one or more of the options to indicate the type of contract you are submitting for approval.
* **Freelancer Information**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Contract Date**: Enter the date when the contract was signed or is expected to be signed, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Client Information**: Indicate whether the client information is available or not by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Contract Terms**: Enter the terms of the contract, including any relevant details or agreements.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date you expect to sign the contract, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Tips**: Please ensure that all fields are complete and accurate before submitting the form. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your approver or contact person for guidance.

Please review this form carefully and ensure that all fields are complete and accurate before submitting it to your approver.
