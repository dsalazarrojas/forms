# Office Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and evaluate office employee information for screening purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields (marked as required).
2. Double-check the information for accuracy before submitting.
3. Review the form carefully to avoid any mistakes or missing information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Employee Name
* **Employee Name**: A required text field to input the employee's full name.
### 2. Employee Email
* **Employee Email**: A required email field to input the employee's work email address.
### 3. Department
* **Department**: An optional select field to choose the employee's department.
### 4. Phone Number
* **Phone Number**: An optional text field for the employee's phone number. Format: E.123-456-7890.
### 5. Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth**: A required date field to input the employee's date of birth.
### 6. Medical History
* **Medical History**: An optional note field to input any relevant medical information.
### 7. Screening Result
* **Screening Result**: A required select field to select the outcome of the screening (Passed, Failed).
### 8. Employee Status
* **Employee Status**: A required select field to input the employee's status (Active, Inactive, Retired).
### 9. Employee Type
* **Employee Type**: An optional select multiple field to select the employee's type (Management, Non-Management, Contract).
### 10. Notes
* **Notes**: An optional note field to input any additional comments or information.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting to avoid mistakes.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submission.
* Verify the information with the employee when necessary.
