# Franchise Drop Shipping Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Franchise Drop Shipping Inquiry form is used to collect information from potential franchises interested in drop shipping opportunities.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the name of your company.
3. Enter the name of the franchise you are interested in.
4. Enter the date of your inquiry (optional).
5. Add any additional information about your message.
6. Select the franchise you are interested in from the dropdown list.
7. Describe the product you are interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info <required>**: This field is required and asks for your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Company Name <required>**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Franchise Name <required>**: Enter the name of the franchise you are interested in.
* **Email <required>**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone <required>**: Enter your phone number.
* **Location <required>**: Enter your location.
* **Inquiry Date <optional>**: This field is optional and allows you to enter the date of your inquiry.
* **Message <optional>**: Add any additional information about your message.
* **Franchise Name <required>**: Select the franchise you are interested in from the dropdown list.
* **Product <required>**: Describe the product you are interested in.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your contact information and franchise name to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any additional questions or comments, please use the "Message" field to provide more information.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please feel free to ask for clarification.
