# Survey Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Survey Participation Form" is a simple form used to collect contact information from survey participants. It helps us gather essential data about our participants, allowing us to better understand our audience and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Contact E mail" field.
3. If you are affiliated with a company, enter the name in the "Company" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional: false):
	+ A text field where you should enter your name as you prefer to be addressed.
* **Contact E mail** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional: false):
	+ A field where you should enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional: false):
	+ If you are affiliated with a company, enter the name of the company for which you work or are associated with.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional: false):
	+ A field where you should enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to help us best serve you.
* If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your participation and look forward to hearing your thoughts!
