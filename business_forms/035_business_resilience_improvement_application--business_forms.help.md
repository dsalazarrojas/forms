# Business Resilience Improvement Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the Business Resilience Improvement Application form, a tool designed to help businesses assess and improve their resilience. The form is used to collect information on the business's current state, goals, and desired outcomes, as well as its support needs and organizational commitment to improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section in the order presented, starting with the Business Information section.
2. Answer all required fields carefully, as they are essential for a comprehensive assessment.
3. Use the hints provided to help guide your answers.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (id: 2, label: Business Name, type: text, required: true): The official name of your business.
* **Type of Business** (id: 3, label: Type of Business, hint: Industry or sector, type: text, required: true): The industry or sector your business operates in.
* **Business Size** (id: 4, label: Business Size, type: select_one, options: Micro, Small, Medium, Large, required: true): The number of employees in your business, which can be Micro (1-2 people), Small (3-20), Medium (21-100), or Large (100+).
* **Years in Operation** (id: 5, label: Years in Operation, type: number, required: true): The number of years your business has existed.
* **Contact Person Name** (id: 7, label: Contact Person Name, type: text, required: true): The primary contact person for this application.
* **Title or Position** (id: 8, label: Title or Position, type: text, required: true): The role of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (id: 9, label: Email Address, type: email, required: true): The business's email address.
* **Phone Number** (id: 10, label: Phone Number, type: text, required: true): The business's phone number.
* **Current Resilience Level** (id: 12, label: Current Resilience Level, type: select_one, options: Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, Very High, required: true): The current level of resilience in your business, which can be Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, or Very High.
* **Major Business Vulnerabilities** (id: 13, label: Major Business Vulnerabilities, type: text, required: true): Your key weaknesses.
* **Areas for Resilience Improvement** (id: 15, label: Areas for Resilience Improvement, type: select_multiple, options: Supply Chain Resilience, Financial Resilience, Operational Continuity, Staff Capacity, Technology and Systems, Leadership and Planning, Customer Relations, Community Relations, required: true): Select all areas you want to strengthen in your business.
* **Specific Improvement Objectives** (id: 16, label: Specific Improvement Objectives, type: text, required: true): The specific outcomes you want to achieve.
* **Desired Timeline for Improvements** (id: 17, label: Desired Timeline for Improvements, type: select_one, options: 3 months, 6 months, 12 months, 18 months, 2 years, Flexible, required: false): The timeframe for achieving your goals.
* **Type of Support Needed** (id: 19, label: Type of Support Needed, type: select_multiple, options: Consulting Services, Training Programs, Financial Assistance, Mentoring or Coaching, Technology Support, Network Access, Planning Assistance, required: true): Select all applicable support types.
* **Consultant Experience Needed** (id: 20, label: Consultant Experience Needed, type: text, required: false): The expertise you need from consultants.
* **Budget Available for Improvements** (id: 21, label: Budget Available for Improvements, type: number, required: false): Your approximate budget range.
* **Management Commitment Level** (id: 23, label: Management Commitment Level, type: select_one, options: Not committed, Somewhat committed, Moderately committed, Highly committed, Fully committed, required: true): The level of commitment from leadership.
* **Staff Participation Level** (id: 24, label: Staff Participation Level, type: select_one, options: Unlikely, Unlikely but possible, Likely, Very likely, Definitely, required: true): The likelihood of staff involvement.
* **Additional Information** (id: 25, label: Additional Information, type: text, required: false): Any other relevant details.
