# material_nonconformance_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The material nonconformance report is a form used to document and report nonconformances, which are defects or issues that do not conform to specifications or expectations. Nonconformances can have significant business impact and are an essential part of maintaining product quality and customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Report Date field with the date the nonconformance was discovered or reported.
2. Enter your name and title in the Reported By field to identify the person submitting the report.
3. Select the appropriate option for Nonconforming Product by checking the relevant box.
4. Choose the Nonconformity Category by selecting the relevant box.
5. Provide a detailed description of the nonconformance in the Description field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Material Nonconformance Report** (`material_nonconformance_report_form`, text, optional): This is the main form field where you can enter a brief description of the nonconformance.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the nonconformance was discovered or reported.
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`, text, optional): Enter your name and title to identify yourself as the person submitting the report.
* **Nonconforming Product** (`nonconforming_product_id`, select_multiple, optional): Check the box indicating that a product does not conform to specifications or expectations.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Nonconformity Category** (`nonconformity_category`, select_multiple, optional): Check the box indicating the category of the nonconformance.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the nonconformance.

## Tips
When completing this form, be as thorough as possible and ensure all relevant fields are filled out completely. If a nonconformance has been previously reported, please ensure that the new report is not a duplicate.
