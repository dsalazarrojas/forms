# vehicle_wrap_design_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect specific details for vehicle wrap design purposes, helping to ensure accurate and complete designs for your vehicle. Please fill out the form for each unique vehicle design request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (indicated by an asterisk*) to provide the basic information about the vehicle and design preferences.
2. Choose the correct options for each field based on your vehicle and design needs.
3. Upload any necessary background images for your design.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vehicle Wrap Design
* **Vehicle Wrap Design** (`vehicle_wrap_design`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the vehicle wrap design you are requesting. This will help us understand the overall design concept.
### Vehicle Details
* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, text, required): Provide the make and model of the vehicle you are requesting a design for.
### Vehicle Type
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if your vehicle is a truck, van, or bus, and "No" otherwise.
### Vehicle Make
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable makes that your vehicle is from, e.g., Ford, GM, etc.
### Vehicle Model
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the specific model of your vehicle, e.g., Ford F-150, etc.
### Design Type
* **Design Type** (`design_type`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you are requesting a wrap with a specific design type, and "No" otherwise.
### Wrap Size
* **Wrap Size** (`wrap_size`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable wrap sizes that you need, e.g., full wrap, partial wrap, etc.
### Color Scheme
* **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, select_one, optional): Select a color scheme that suits your design, e.g., solid color, gradient, etc.
### Font Size
* **Font Size** (`font_size`, select_one, optional): Select a font size that suits your design, e.g., 8pt, 10pt, etc.
### Font Color
* **Font Color** (`font_color`, select_one, optional): Select a font color that suits your design, e.g., blue, red, etc.
### Background Image
* **Background Image** (`background_image`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the background image you would like to use.
### Upload Background Image
* **Upload Background Image** (`upload_background_image`, text, optional): Upload your background image for the design.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out each field accurately and completely to ensure the best results.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
- This form is designed to collect specific details for vehicle wrap design purposes, so only fill out the form for specific vehicle wrap design requests.
