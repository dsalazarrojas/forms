# Customer Invoice Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Invoice Checklist Form is a form designed to collect important information related to customer invoices. The form is meant to be filled out by customers to provide accurate and complete details for their invoices. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected in a structured way.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by selecting the "Customer Invoices" page.
2. Enter the relevant details for the customer's invoices, including any notes or observations.
3. Move on to the "Business Details" page and enter the basic information about the business or organization.
4. Next, provide the billing address of the customer on the "Billing Address" page.
5. Enter the Tax Identification Number of the customer on the "Tax Identification Number" page.
6. Enter the customer's details on the "Customer Details" page.
7. Specify the payment terms on the "Payment Terms" page.
8. Choose the relevant payment methods on the "Payment Methods" page.
9. Provide the shipping address of the customer on the "Shipping Address" page.
10. Enter any order details on the "Order Details" page.
11. Finally, leave any additional notes or comments on the "Notes" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Invoices (1)**: This is the main page where you will enter the details of the customer's invoices.
* **Business Details (2)**: Enter the basic information about the business or organization.
* **Billing Address (3)**: Enter the address of the customer's billing information.
* **Tax Identification Number (4)**: Enter the customer's Tax Identification Number.
* **Customer Details (5)**: Enter any additional information about the customer.
* **Payment Terms (6)**: Specify the payment terms for the customer.
* **Payment Methods (7)**: Select the payment methods that the customer uses.
* **Shipping Address (8)**: Enter the customer's shipping address.
* **Order Details (9)**: Enter any details about the customer's order.
* **Notes (10)**: Leave any notes or comments about the customer or their invoice.
