# false_alarm_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document false alarms or non-occurrences that were initially reported as incidents. It helps to clarify the situation, gather information, and prevent unnecessary investigations or actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the false alarm or non-occurrence in the "Incident Description" field.
2. Select the date and time of the incident using the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields.
3. Enter the location where the false alarm or non-occurrence occurred in the "Incident Location" field.
4. Choose the type of false alarm or non-occurrence from the "Incident Type" field.
5. Select the category and subcategory of the situation from the "Incident Category" and "Incident Subcategory" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the name of the person who reported the false alarm or non-occurrence in the "Incident Reporter" field.
7. Choose the status of the situation from the "Incident Status" field.
8. Enter the priority of the situation using the "Incident Priority" field.
9. Provide any additional comments about the situation in the "Incident Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **False Alarm Incident Form** (Form Title): This is the title of the form.
* **Incident Description** (incident_description): Enter a brief description of the false alarm or non-occurrence.
* **Incident Date** (incident_date): Select the date of the false alarm or non-occurrence.
* **Incident Time** (incident_time): Select the time of the false alarm or non-occurrence.
* **Incident Location** (incident_location): Enter the location where the false alarm or non-occurrence occurred.
* **Incident Type** (incident_type): Choose the type of false alarm or non-occurrence.
* **Incident Category** (incident_category): Select the category of the situation.
* **Incident Subcategory** (incident_subcategory): Select the subcategory of the situation.
* **Incident Reporter** (incident_reporter): Enter the name of the person who reported the false alarm or non-occurrence.
* **Incident Status** (incident_status): Choose the status of the situation.
* **Incident Priority** (incident_priority): Enter the priority of the situation.
* **Incident Comments** (incident_comments): Provide any additional comments about the situation.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the situation.
* Use the correct category and subcategory to accurately classify the false alarm or non-occurrence.
