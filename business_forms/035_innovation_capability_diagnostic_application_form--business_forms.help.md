# Innovation Capability Diagnostic Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information about a company and its representative contact details. This information will help us understand the company's innovation capabilities and provide insights for future applications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, make sure you have all the necessary information about your company and its representative. This includes the company name, address, and contact details of the person filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form with the required fields marked as "required." These include:
	* Company (page 1)
	* Email (page 2)
	* Submission (page 4)
3. You can leave out any fields that are marked as "optional" or have no specific instructions.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" field (page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company** (Page 1): Enter the name of the company.
* **Address** (Page 1): Enter the physical address of the company.
* **Date of Birth** (Page 2): Leave this field blank, as it is not relevant to the form's purpose.
* **First Name** (Page 2): Leave this field blank, as it is not relevant to the form's purpose.
* **Last Name** (Page 2): Leave this field blank, as it is not relevant to the form's purpose.
* **Email** (Page 3): Enter a valid email address of the representative.
* **Phone** (Page 3): Leave this field blank, as it is not required.
* **Company** (Page 4): Enter the name of the company again.
* **Skills** (Page 4): This field is optional, and you can enter any relevant skills or areas of expertise.
* **Submission** (Page 4): Enter a brief description of the submission.
* **Additional Comments** (Page 5): Use this field to provide any additional information or feedback.
