# Business Formation Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize a business formation, which is the process of creating a new business entity. The form requires information about the business and the person authorizing it. This will help ensure that the business is properly set up with the necessary authority to operate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields with accurate information.
2. For the "Entity Type" field, select one of the available options to indicate the structure of the business (e.g., Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, Non-Profit, or Other).
3. Enter the "Proposed Business Name" as the legal name of the business.
4. Enter the "State of Formation" where the business will be established.
5. Provide the physical address of the "Principal Place of Business".
6. Classify the "Industry" of the business.
7. Explain the "Business Purpose" in a brief description.
8. Enter the "Full Name" and "Title or Role" of the person authorizing the business.
9. Enter the "Personal Address", "Phone Number", and "Email Address" of the authorizing person.
10. If applicable, enter the "Ownership Interest" percentage.
11. Select the authorities granted to the business (e.g., File Formation Documents, Sign on Behalf of Business, Open Bank Accounts, etc.).
12. Indicate if the authorization is "Full Authority", "Limited to Specific Acts", "Temporary Authorization", or "Other".
13. If applicable, specify any "Scope Limitations" on the authority granted.
14. Enter the "Effective Date" when the authorization starts.
15. If applicable, enter the "Expiration Date" when the authorization ends.
16. Finally, certify that the information provided is accurate by selecting "True".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Proposed Business Name**: The legal name of the business.
* **Entity Type**: The structure of the business (e.g., Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, Non-Profit, or Other).
* **State of Formation**: Where the business will be established.
* **Principal Place of Business**: The physical address of the business.
* **Industry Classification**: The type of business.
* **Business Purpose**: A brief description of what the business will do.
* **Full Name**: The name of the person authorizing the business.
* **Title or Role**: The position or relationship of the person authorizing the business.
* **Personal Address**: The home or business address of the authorizing person.
* **Phone Number**: The contact phone number of the authorizing person.
* **Email Address**: The contact email of the authorizing person.
* **Ownership Interest**: The percentage of ownership of the business.
* **Authority Granted**: The authorities granted to the business (e.g., File Formation Documents, Sign on Behalf of Business, Open Bank Accounts, etc.).
* **Authorization Scope**: The type of authority granted (e.g., Full Authority, Limited to Specific Acts, Temporary Authorization, or Other).
* **Scope Limitations**: Any limitations on the authority granted.
* **Effective Date**: The date when the authorization starts.
* **Expiration Date**: The date when the authorization ends.
* **I Certify This Information is Accurate**: Confirm that the provided information is accurate.
