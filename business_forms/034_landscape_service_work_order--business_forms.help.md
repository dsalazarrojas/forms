# landscape_service_work_order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The landscape_service_work_order form is used by a business or individual to gather information from customers or clients for specific landscape services. This form is intended to collect details about service requests, appointments, contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you have a service request or not.
2. Provide a detailed description of your service request, if applicable.
3. Decide if you need to schedule an appointment or not.
4. Provide your contact information, such as your name and any relevant contact details.
5. Specify the job location where the service will be performed.
6. Choose the requested dates for the service.
7. Add any additional job notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Requests** (`service_requests`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to indicate whether you have a service request or not. Select "Yes" if you have a service request, and "No" if you don't.
* **Service Request Description** (`service_request_description`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide a detailed description of your service request. Please type a clear and concise description of the work you need to be done.
* **Schedule Appointments** (`schedule_appointments`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to choose whether you want to schedule an appointment or not. Select "Yes" if you need to schedule an appointment, and "No" if you don't.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide your contact information, such as your name and any other relevant details.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to specify the location where the service will be performed.
* **Service Request Dates** (`service_request_dates`, `date`, required: false): This field is used to select the date(s) for the service. Please select a valid date(s) according to your service request.
* **Job Notes** (`job_notes`, `note`, required: false): This field is used to add any additional job notes or details.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to specify the name of the contact person.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all relevant fields to ensure that the form is completed accurately and promptly.
* If you are unsure about any field, it's always better to ask for clarification or guidance.
