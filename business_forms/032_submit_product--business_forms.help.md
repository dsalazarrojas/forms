# Submit Product - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Submit Product" form is a simple, text-based form designed to collect product information from customers, partners, or employees. 

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a product, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the product name in the "Product name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the product in the "Product description" field.
3. Enter the product price in the "Price" field.
4. Select the currency of the product price in the "Currency" field.
5. Add relevant tags to the "Tags" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product form** (`product_form`, text, optional):
    Enter your product in the text field.
* **Product name** (`product_name`, text, optional):
    Enter a name for your product. It should be a brief description of what the product is.
* **Product description** (`product_description`, text, optional):
    Enter a short description of your product.
* **Price** (`product_price`, number, optional):
    Enter the price of your product. This can be a whole number (e.g., 10) or a decimal number with two decimal places (e.g., 10.99).
* **Currency** (`product_price_currency`, select_one, optional):
    Select the currency of your product. Options include USD, EUR, and GBP.
* **Tags** (`product_tags`, select_multiple, optional):
    Add relevant tags to your product, such as keywords or categories. This field allows for multiple selections.

## Tips
- Be concise and clear when providing product information.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask for help.
- You can add multiple tags to help your product be found in searches or categories.
- Make sure to select the correct currency for the price of your product.
