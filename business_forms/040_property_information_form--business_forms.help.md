# Property Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Information Form is used to collect information about properties, including contact details, property type, and physical characteristics. This form is designed to gather essential data for property-related transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property owner's First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter the property owner's Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter the property's address in the Address field.
4. Enter the property owner's Email in the Email field.
5. Enter the property owner's Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
6. Select the Property Type (Yes or No) to indicate whether the property is a specific type.
7. Enter the Building Age in the Building Age field.
8. Select the Property Status (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3) to indicate the property's status.
9. Enter the Year Built in the Year Built field.
10. Enter the Square Footage of the property in the Square Footage field.
11. Enter the Lot Size in the Lot Size field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter the property owner's first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter the property owner's last name.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter the property's address.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the property owner's email address.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter the property owner's phone number.
* **Property Type** (select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the property is a specific type.
* **Building Age** (number, optional): Enter the property's building age.
* **Property Status** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options to indicate the property's status.
* **Year Built** (date, optional): Enter the year the property was built.
* **Square Footage** (number, optional): Enter the property's square footage.
* **Lot Size** (number, optional): Enter the property's lot size.

## Tips
- Ensure you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
- Check for any required fields and ensure they are completed before submitting the form.
- Review each field's label and type to determine the required information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authority for clarification.
