# Financial Data Governance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form collects information about a user's asset, which includes personal details, asset characteristics, and other relevant information. The form aims to ensure accurate and consistent data registration, preventing potential duplication of records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to complete and submit your registration.
2. Fill out each field according to the guidelines below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (1)** (`user_info`, text, required): Enter your name and contact details.
* **Asset Information (2)** (`asset_info`, text, required): Describe your asset in a concise and accurate manner.
* **Ownership (3)** (`ownership`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more from the options provided.
* **Asset Location (4)** (`asset_location`, text, optional): Provide the location of your asset.
* **Asset Value (5)** (`asset_value`, number, required): Enter the value of your asset.
* **Asset Type (6)** (`asset_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of asset you possess.
* **Financial Institution (7)** (`financial_institution`, text, optional): Mention the financial institution related to your asset.
* **Asset Description (8)** (`asset_description`, text, optional): Offer a brief description of your asset.
* **Submission Date (9)** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time (10)** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Notes (11)** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your asset.
* **Email (12)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone (13)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **File Upload (14)** (`file_upload`, text, optional): Upload any supporting documents or files related to your asset.
* **Comments (15)** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about your asset.
* **File Upload 2-10 (16-24)** (`file_upload_2`, `file_upload_3`, `file_upload_4`, `file_upload_5`, `file_upload_6`, `file_upload_7`, `file_upload_8`, `file_upload_9`, `file_upload_10`, text, optional): Upload any additional files related to your asset.
* **Submit (25)** (`submit`, text, required): Review and submit your registration.
