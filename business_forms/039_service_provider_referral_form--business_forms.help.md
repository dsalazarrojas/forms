# Service Provider Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Service Provider Referral Form is used to collect information about potential new service providers that can offer valuable services to our clients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the service provider's business name in the "Business Name" field.
2. Select the service provider from the "Service Provider" dropdown list.
3. Enter the contact person's name in the "Contact Name" field.
4. Enter the contact person's email address in the "Contact Email" field.
5. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. Select the referral date from the calendar.
7. Select the type of referral from the "Referral Type" dropdown list.
8. Select the type of client the service provider works with from the "Client Type" dropdown list (optional).
9. Enter the service provider's business address in the "Business Address" field (optional).
10. Provide any additional comments about the referral in the "Referral Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the service provider's business.
* **Service Provider** (select multiple, optional): Select one or more service providers from the dropdown list.
* **Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the service provider.
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Referral Date** (date, required): Select the date of the referral.
* **Referral Type** (select one, required): Select the type of referral (e.g., new customer, new project, etc.).
* **Client Type** (select multiple, optional): Select one or more types of clients the service provider works with.
* **Business Address** (text, optional): Enter the service provider's business address.
* **Referral Comments** (note, optional): Provide any additional comments about the referral.
