# Advertiser Feedback Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advertiser Feedback Form Template is designed to collect feedback from advertisers on their experience with our services. This form helps us understand their satisfaction with the campaign performance, communication, and collaboration. The feedback received will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for future campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide accurate and helpful feedback, please answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly. You may need to consult with your team or refer to the campaign's goals and performance data to answer some questions. Please make sure to fill out all required fields, which are marked as "Required".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Advertiser Information 
- **-- Advertiser Information --** (`advertiser_information_section`, `note`, required/optional): 
  This section is an introductory note to help guide you through the form. Please ignore it and skip to the next section.

### Company Name 
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): 
  Enter the name of your company as it appears on your business card or letterhead.

### Contact Person Name 
- **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person_name`, `text`, required): 
  Enter the name of the primary contact person for the campaign.

### Contact Person Title 
- **Contact Person Title** (`contact_person_title`, `text`, required): 
  Enter the job title of the contact person.

### Email Address 
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): 
  Enter your business email address for communication. Please use the format `example@example.com`.

### Phone Number 
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): 
  Enter your business phone number.

### -- Campaign Information 
- **-- Campaign Information --** (`campaign_information_section`, `note`, required/optional): 
  This section is an introductory note to help guide you through the campaign information. Please ignore it and skip to the next section.

### Campaign Name 
- **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, `text`, required): 
  Enter the name of the campaign as it appears in the contract or agreement.

### Campaign Duration 
- **Campaign Duration** (`campaign_duration`, `text`, required): 
  Enter the start and end dates of the campaign in the format `MM/YYYY - MM/YYYY`.

### Campaign Budget 
- **Campaign Budget** (`campaign_budget`, `number`, required): 
  Enter the total budget allocated for the campaign in the format `$$$`.

### -- Campaign Performance 
- **-- Campaign Performance --** (`campaign_performance_section`, `note`, required/optional): 
  This section is an introductory note to help guide you through the campaign performance evaluation. Please ignore it and skip to the next section.

### Overall Satisfaction 
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): 
  Select one of the following options to rate your overall satisfaction:
  - Very satisfied
  - Satisfied
  - Neutral
  - Dissatisfied
  - Very dissatisfied

### ROI Expectations Met 
- **ROI Expectations Met** (`roi_expectations_met`, `select_one`, required): 
  Select one of the following options to evaluate if the campaign met your return on investment expectations:
  - Exceeded expectations
  - Met expectations
  - Below expectations
  - Not measurable

### Target Audience Reached 
- **Target Audience Reached** (`target_audience_reached`, `select_one`, required): 
  Select one of the following options to evaluate how well the campaign reached your target audience:
  - Excellent
  - Good
  - Fair
  - Poor
  - Not applicable

### -- Communication Collaboration 
- **-- Communication Collaboration --** (`communication_collaboration_section`, `note`, required/optional): 
  This section is an introductory note to help guide you through the communication and collaboration process. Please ignore it and skip to the next section.

### Response Time Satisfaction 
- **Response Time Satisfaction** (`response_time_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): 
  Select one of the following options to rate your satisfaction with response times:
  - Very satisfied
  - Satisfied
  - Neutral
  - Dissatisfied
  - Very dissatisfied

### Communication Clarity 
- **Communication Clarity** (`communication_clarity`, `select_one`, required): 
  Select one of the following options to rate the clarity of the communication:
  - Very clear
  - Clear
  - Neutral
  - Unclear
  - Very unclear

### Collaboration Effectiveness 
- **Collaboration Effectiveness** (`collaboration_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): 
  Select one of the following options to evaluate how effective the collaboration process was:
  - Very effective
  - Effective
  - Neutral
  - Ineffective
  - Very ineffective

### Improvement Suggestions 
- **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): 
  If there are any areas for improvement, please provide specific suggestions for future campaigns.

### Likelihood to Recommend 
- **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, `select_one`, required): 
  Select one of the following options to rate how likely you are to recommend our services to others:
  - Very likely
  - Likely
  - Neutral
  - Unlikely
  - Very unlikely

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your time and feedback, and it will help us improve our services.
