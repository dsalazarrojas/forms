# Expense Audit Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record an employee's participation and consent for an expense audit. It is essential for the audit process to have accurate and up-to-date information, and this form ensures that all relevant details are collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Enter your employee ID.
4. Select your department (if applicable).
5. Enter your job title (if applicable).
6. Select a date for the audit participation.
7. If applicable, select a time for the audit participation.
8. Provide a signature to indicate your consent.
9. Enter your email address (optional).
10. Enter your phone number (optional).
11. Select your agreement status regarding the audit (e.g., Agreed, Not Agreed, Unknown).
12. Select a date for your agreement.
13. Select the status of the review (e.g., In Progress, Completed, Not Started).
14. Enter the name of the last person to review the audit.
15. Enter the date of the last review.
16. Enter the time of the last review (optional).
17. Enter the name of the person who last reviewed the audit (optional).
18. Enter employee notes (optional).
19. Enter auditor notes (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Expense Audit Participation Consent Form**: This field is the title of the form, and it should be left blank as it is automatically populated.
* **First Name**: This field is required and should be completed with your first name.
* **Last Name**: This field is required and should be completed with your last name.
* **Employee ID**: This field is required and should be completed with your employee ID.
* **Department**: This field is optional and should be completed with your department name if applicable.
* **Job Title**: This field is optional and should be completed with your job title if applicable.
* **Date**: This field is required and should be completed with the date of the audit participation.
* **Time**: This field is optional and should be completed with the time of the audit participation if applicable.
* **Signature**: This field is required and should be signed to indicate your consent.
* **Email**: This field is optional and should be completed with your email address if you wish to be contacted.
* **Phone**: This field is optional and should be completed with your phone number if you wish to be contacted.
* **Agreement Status**: This field is required and should be selected to indicate your agreement status regarding the audit (e.g., Agreed, Not Agreed, Unknown).
* **Agreement Date**: This field is required and should be completed with the date of your agreement.
* **Agreement Time**: This field is optional and should be completed with the time of your agreement if applicable.
* **Submitted On**: This field is required and should be completed with the date of submission.
* **Submitted By**: This field is optional and should be completed with the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Employee Notes**: This field is optional and should be completed with any relevant notes.
* **Auditor Notes**: This field is optional and should be completed with any relevant notes from the auditor.
* **Review Status**: This field is required and should be selected to indicate the status of the review (e.g., In Progress, Completed, Not Started).
* **Last Reviewed By**: This field is optional and should be completed with the name of the last person to review the audit.
* **Last Reviewed On**: This field is optional and should be completed with the date of the last review.
* **Last Reviewed Time**: This field is optional and should be completed with the time of the last review if applicable.
* **Last Reviewed By Who**: This field is optional and should be completed with the name of the person who last reviewed the audit.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed to avoid delays or errors in the audit process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or departmental representative for guidance.
* The form should be submitted to the relevant person or department for review and processing.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
