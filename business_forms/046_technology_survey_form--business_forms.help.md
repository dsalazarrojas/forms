# Technology Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Technology Survey Form is used by IT teams to gather information about employee's usage of computer applications, technologies, and other metrics. This form will help IT teams understand the technology landscape within the company and make informed decisions to support employees' needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Fill out the form in the following pages:
	* Page 1: Computer Specs (Enter your computer specifications)
	* Page 2: Technology Survey Form (This page repeats the form name)
3. Select all the applicable applications and technologies you use in your work.
4. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
5. Fill out the form completely, as some fields are required.
6. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Computer Specs**: (`computer_specs`, text, required/optional)
	+ Enter your computer specifications, such as processor speed, RAM, and operating system.
* **Applications Used**: (`applications_used`, select_one, required/optional)
	+ Select all the applications you use in your work, such as Application A, Application B, and Application C.
* **Technology Techniques**: (`technology_techniques`, select_multiple, required/optional)
	+ Select all the technologies you use in your work, such as Technology 1, Technology 2, and Technology 3.
* **Date**: (`date`, date, required/optional)
	+ Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Time**: (`time`, time, required/optional)
	+ Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Email**: (`email`, email, required/optional)
	+ Enter your company email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, text, required/optional)
	+ Enter your phone number (if applicable).

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering questions.
* If you are unsure about any field, leave it blank or ask your IT representative for guidance.
* The form is designed to be completed by current employees of the company.
* The form will take approximately 5-7 minutes to complete.
