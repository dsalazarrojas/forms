# Soft Heel Cream - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soft Heel Cream form helps us understand common causes of heel pain and how to provide relief. Fill out this form to share your experience and get tailored advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the causes of your heel pain from the list, provide a description of your situation, and submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Soft Heel Cream Form** (`soft_heel_cream_form`, text, required): Fill in your name as it appears on your account.
* **Causes of Heel Pain** (`causes_of_heel_pain`, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply to your situation, examples include:
	+ Poorly fitting shoes
	+ Wearing high heel shoes
	+ Prolonged period on feet
	+ Overuse (long walks or marathons)
	+ Sprains
	+ Strains
	+ Trauma
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Describe your situation in detail, including any relevant medical conditions or recent activities that may have contributed to your heel pain.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Choose all causes of heel pain that apply to your situation.
* Write a clear and concise description of your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
