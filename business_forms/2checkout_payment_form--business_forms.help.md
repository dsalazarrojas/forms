# 2Checkout Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment details for 2Checkout and Verifone transactions. It captures essential information such as customer contact details, transaction specifics, and payment options.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting.
2. Enter the customer's full name in the **Customer full name** field.
3. Provide the customer's email address in the **Customer email** field.
4. Enter the customer's phone number (including the country code) in the **Customer phone number** field.
5. Enter the billing address in the **Billing address** field.
6. Describe the product or service being transacted in the **Product or service description** field.
7. Enter the invoice or order number (if applicable) in the **Invoice or order number** field.
8. Enter the total payment amount in the **Payment amount** field.
9. Select the transaction currency from the **Currency** dropdown menu.
10. Choose the payment method from the **Payment method** dropdown menu.
11. If using a Verifone terminal, enter the terminal ID in the **Verifone terminal ID** field.
12. Enter the transaction date in the **Transaction date** field.
13. Add any additional notes or instructions in the **Additional notes for the order** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer full name** (Text): Enter the customer's full name as it appears on the billing information.
* **Customer email** (Email): Enter the customer's email address where the receipt will be sent.
* **Customer phone number** (Phone): Enter the customer's phone number, including the country code.
* **Billing address** (Text): Enter the street, city, and postal code for the billing address.
* **Product or service description** (Text): Enter a short description of the product or service being transacted.
* **Invoice or order number** (Text): Enter any internal reference number for the order or invoice (optional).
* **Payment amount** (Decimal): Enter the total payment amount.
* **Currency** (Select One): Choose the transaction currency from the dropdown menu (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD).
* **Payment method** (Select One): Select the payment method used (Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Verifone Terminal, ACH / Bank Transfer).
* **Verifone terminal ID** (Text): If using a Verifone terminal, enter the terminal ID provided by the merchant.
* **Transaction date** (Date): Enter the date of the transaction.
* **Additional notes for the order** (Text): Enter any additional notes or instructions for the order.
