# Cost Allocation Mapping Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cost Allocation Mapping Application Form is used to capture information for cost allocation mapping purposes within a business organization. It gathers details about customers, their allocation requests, and associated costs to aid in budgeting and accounting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Form Title** to identify the specific form submission.
2. Enter the **Customer Name**, **Customer Email**, and **Customer Phone** to provide contact information for the customer.
3. Enter the **Customer Address** for location details.
4. Add any additional **Customer Notes** if necessary.
5. Select the **Allocation Request Type** from the provided options.
6. Select one or more **Allocation Categories** to categorize the allocation.
7. Enter the **Cost Center** to identify the associated cost center.
8. Enter the **Allocation Amount** to specify the allocation amount.
9. Select the **Allocation Period** from the provided options to specify the period for the allocation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (1, text, required/optional): A brief title to identify the specific form submission.
* **Customer Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (4, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Address** (5, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's address.
* **Customer Notes** (6, text, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the customer.
* **Allocation Request Type** (7, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the provided options for the allocation request type.
* **Allocation Category** (8, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more of the provided options for the allocation category.
* **Cost Center** (9, text, required/optional): Enter the associated cost center.
* **Allocation Amount** (10, number, required/optional): Enter the allocation amount.
* **Allocation Period** (11, date, required/optional): Select the allocation period from the provided options.

## Tips
- Please ensure to fill out all required fields accurately to avoid delays or errors in processing.
- If unsure of any field or its purpose, please refer to this help guide for further clarification.

- Take the time to carefully complete this form to ensure accurate and efficient processing.
