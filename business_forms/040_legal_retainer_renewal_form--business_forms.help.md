# Legal Retainer Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Retainer Renewal Form is a document used to track and update information about client retainers, including renewal dates, contract terms, payment methods, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field, including client name, contact person, address, email, and renewal date.
2. Select the relevant options for contract terms, renewal terms, and payment method.
3. If applicable, provide a reason for termination and notes about the client agreement.
4. Select the status of the client agreement.
5. Indicate whether the form has been submitted to the relevant party.
6. Confirm that the form has been created and updated by the relevant user.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person for the client.
* **Address**: Enter the client's address.
* **Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Renewal Date**: Enter the date the retainer is due for renewal.
* **Contract Terms**: Select whether the client agrees to the contract terms.
* **Renewal Terms**: Select the renewal terms that apply to the client.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment used for the retainer.
* **Client Agreement Status**: Select the current status of the client agreement.
* **Agreement Signed Date**: Enter the date the client agreement was signed.
* **Termination Date**: Enter the date the client retainer was terminated.
* **Reason for Termination**: Enter a reason for the termination of the retainer.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the client retainer.
* **Assigned User**: Select whether the form is assigned to a specific user.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date the form was reviewed.
* **Last Updated**: Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Status**: Select the status of the client retainer (Active or Inactive).
* **Submitted By**: Indicate whether the form was submitted to the relevant party.
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Created By**: Indicate whether the form was created by the relevant user.
* **Created On**: Enter the date the form was created.
* **Updated By**: Indicate whether the form was updated by the relevant user.
* **Updated On**: Enter the date the form was updated.
* **Submitted To**: Indicate whether the form was submitted to the relevant party.
