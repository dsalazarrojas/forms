# Public Relations Event Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Relations Event Approval Form is designed to gather essential information and facilitate the approval process for public relations events. It ensures that events are properly documented, well-structured, and aligned with business objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event title and event name accurately.
2. Select the event type, which could be In-Person, Online, or Hybrid.
3. Input the event date and time.
4. Enter the event location and number of guests (if applicable).
5. Provide your contact details and email (if you want to be reached for any updates).
6. Add any additional notes related to the event in the Event Notes field.
7. Choose the approval date and time for the event.
8. Add a note for the approval process in the Approval Note field.
9. Finally, fill in the reviewer and approver names, titles, and comments, and select the review status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: A unique identifier for the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): The date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): The time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): The venue or location of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): The type of event, which could be In-Person, Online, or Hybrid.
* **Number of Guests** (`event_guests`): The estimated number of attendees.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Your personal details for any updates or follow-up conversations.
* **Email** (`email`): Your email address for any correspondence.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Your phone number for any urgent updates.
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`): Any additional information about the event.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`): The date the approval process is completed.
* **Approval Time** (`approval_time`): The time the approval process is completed.
* **Approval Note** (`approval_note`): Any comments or notes from the approver.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`): The name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Title** (`reviewer_title`): The title of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`): Comments from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Status** (`reviewer_comments`): The status of the review, which could be Approved, Approved with Conditions, or Rejected.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`): The name of the approver.
* **Approver Title** (`approver_title`): The title of the approver.
* **Approver Comments** (`approver_comments`): Comments from the approver.
