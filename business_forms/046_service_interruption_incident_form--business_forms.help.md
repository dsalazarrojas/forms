# service_interruption_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The service interruption incident form is used to report and document service interruptions, including their impact and resolution progress. It helps to standardize the collection of incident information for effective response and recovery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reporting organization from the list of options.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident.
3. Describe the incident in the space provided.
4. Specify the services affected by the incident.
5. Choose the impact severity of the incident.
6. Select the status of the resolution.
7. Add any additional comments or notes.
8. Specify the officer responsible for reporting the incident.
9. Enter the date and time of reporting.
10. If necessary, provide contact information for further clarification.
11. If necessary, provide additional notes on recovery details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reporting Organization**: Select the organization responsible for reporting the incident from the list of options.
- **Incident Date**: Enter the date when the incident occurred.
- **Incident Time**: Enter the time when the incident occurred.
- **Incident Description**: Provide a brief description of the incident.
- **Affected Services**: Select the services that were affected by the incident.
- **Impact Severity**: Choose the level of impact on services due to the incident.
- **Resolution Status**: Select the current status of resolution progress.
- **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments on the incident.
- **Reporting Officer**: Select the officer responsible for reporting this incident.
- **Reporting Date**: Enter the date when the incident was reported.
- **Reporting Time**: Enter the time when the incident was reported.
- **Contact Name**: Provide the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Number**: Provide the contact number for further clarification.
- **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email for further clarification.
- **Additional Contact Notes**: Provide any additional contact information.
- **Service Disruption Date**: Enter the date when the service was disrupted.
- **Service Disruption Time**: Enter the time when the service was disrupted.
- **Service Disruption Duration**: Enter the duration of the service disruption.
- **Service Recovery Date**: Enter the date when the service was recovered.
- **Service Recovery Time**: Enter the time when the service was recovered.
- **Recovery Details**: Provide any details on the recovery progress.
- **Root Cause**: Identify the root cause of the incident.
- **Follow-up**: Provide any additional information on follow-up actions.
