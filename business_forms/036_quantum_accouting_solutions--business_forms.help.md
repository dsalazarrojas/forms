# Quantum Accounting Solutions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect business-related information and data entry from users. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form to start filling it out.
2. Select the relevant fields that pertain to your business.
3. Fill out each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Form** (`1`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your business.
* **Data Entry** (`2`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional data entry information.
* **Maintain Day Book** (`3`, text, required/optional): Enter data for maintaining day book.
* **Tally Data Feeding** (`4`, text, required/optional): Enter data for tally data feeding.
* **Bank Reconciliation** (`5`, text, required/optional): Enter data for bank reconciliation.
* **Debtors & Creditors** (`6`, text, required/optional): Enter data for debtors & crediters.
* **TDS Filling** (`7`, text, required/optional): Enter data for TDS filling.
* **GST Fees** (`8`, number, required/optional): Enter GST fees amount.
* **Debtors & Creditors List** (`9`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select from 'Yes' or 'No' for debtors & crediters list.
* **GST Fees List** (`10`, select_one, required/optional): Select from GST fee 1, GST fee 2, or GST fee 3 for GST fees list.
* **Bank Statement** (`11`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select from 'Yes' or 'No' for bank statement.
* **Account Details** (`12`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select from 'Yes' or 'No' for account details.
* **GST Fees Details** (`13`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional GST fees details.
* **GST Fees Amount** (`14`, number, required/optional): Enter GST fees amount.
* **GST Fees Frequency** (`15`, select_one, required/optional): Select from Daily, Weekly, or Monthly for GST fees frequency.
* **GST Fees Start Date** (`16`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date for GST fees.
* **GST Fees End Date** (`17`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date for GST fees.
* **GST Fees Total** (`18`, number, required/optional): Enter GST fees total.
* **PAN Card** (`19`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your PAN card.
* **PAN Card Number** (`20`, text, required/optional): Enter your PAN card number.
* **PAN Card Date** (`21`, date, required/optional): Enter the date your PAN card was issued.
* **PAN Card Image** (`22`, note, required/optional): Upload an image of your PAN card.
* **Upload PAN Card Image** (`23`, text, required/optional): Upload a scanned copy of your PAN card.
* **Email** (`24`, email, required/optional): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone** (`25`, text, required/optional): Enter your business phone number.
