# Best Western Plus Abbey Inn Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Western Plus Abbey Inn Employment Application form is designed to collect information from applicants interested in working at Best Western Plus Abbey Inn. This form gathers essential details about the applicant's personal and professional history, education, and availability. The information collected will be used to assess the applicant's suitability for available positions.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the application process, ensure you have all necessary documents and information ready. This includes your social security number, date of birth, and employment history. You can fill out the form in sections, as each part is clearly labeled and described. Be honest and accurate when providing information, as it will be used to evaluate your qualifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Social Security Number** (`ssn_entry`, text, required): Enter your social security number for background check purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`dob_entry`, date, required): Provide your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Current Address** (`residential_address`, text, required): Enter your street address, city, state, and ZIP for identification and background purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Position Applied For** (`desired_position_choice`, select_one, required): Select the primary role you are interested in applying for, such as Front Desk Guest Services or Hotel Management.
* **Preferred Shift** (`shift_preference`, select_multiple, required): Choose all shifts you are available to work.
* **Expected Hourly Rate** (`expected_pay_rate`, number, optional): Enter the amount you expect to be paid in USD.
* **Date Available to Start** (`start_date_availability`, date, required): Provide the date you can start working.
* **Highest Education Level** (`highest_education_attained`, select_one, required): Choose the highest level of education you have attained, such as High School Diploma or Bachelor Degree.
* **Relevant Skills or Certifications** (`relevant_skills_desc`, text, optional): List any skills or certifications that make you a good fit for this role.
* **Previous Company Name** (`prev_company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your most recent employer.
* **Position Held** (`prev_position_title`, text, required): Enter the title of your position held at your previous company.
* **Employment Duration** (`employment_duration`, text, required): Provide the duration of your employment at your previous company, including start and end dates or total months/years.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, optional): If applicable, explain the reason for your previous employment leaving.
* **Reference Name** (`ref_full_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your professional reference.
* **Reference Contact Info** (`ref_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the phone number or email of your professional reference.
* **Certify** (`accuracy_certification`, select_one, required): Confirm that all information provided is true and correct by selecting "Yes - I certify" from the dropdown menu.
* **Signature and Full Name Confirmation** (`electronic_signature`, text, required): This field is a digital signature that confirms your application, typed in your full name.
* **Date of Application** (`application_date_stamp`, date, required): The date you complete this application will be recorded automatically, but you can enter a specific date if desired.
