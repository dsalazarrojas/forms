# Champion Recognition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Champion Recognition Form is a tool used to nominate individuals for recognition within the organization. This form allows you to submit a nomination for a team member or colleague who has demonstrated exceptional performance and deserves recognition. The form is used to gather information about the nominee's achievements and provide context for the recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields:
	* Nomination Date: Enter the date the nomination is being submitted.
	* Nominator Name: Enter your full name as the person nominating the individual.
	* Nominator Email: Enter your email address as the person nominating the individual.
	* Nominee Name: Enter the full name of the person being nominated.
	* Nominee Department: Enter the department of the person being nominated.
	* Nominee Position: Enter the job title of the person being nominated.
	* Nomination Reason: Explain why you are nominating the individual.
	* Confidentiality: Indicate if the nomination should be kept confidential until decision is made.
	* Nominator Signature: Type your full name as signature.
2.  Select and enter the following:
	* Recognition Category: Choose the category for the recognition.
	* Achievement Period: Enter the time period of the achievements.
	* Specific Achievements: Describe specific accomplishments of the nominee.
	* Impact Description: Describe the impact of the nominee's work.
	* Company Values Demonstrated: Select values shown by the nominee.
	* Previous Recognition: Indicate if the nominee has been recognized before.
	* Previous Awards: List any previous awards or recognition received by the nominee.
	* Witness Statements: Indicate if others can support this nomination.
	* Witness Contacts: Enter names and emails of supporters.
	* Suggested Recognition: Choose the type of recognition suitable for the nominee.
3.  If required, upload supporting documents and witness statements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Date** (Date of Nomination): Enter the date the nomination is being submitted.
* **Nominator Name** (Full name of person making nomination): Enter your full name as the person nominating the individual.
* **Nominator Email** (Email for confirmation): Enter your email address as the person nominating the individual.
* **Nominee Name** (Full name of person being nominated): Enter the full name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Department** (Department of nominee): Enter the department of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Position** (Job title of nominee): Enter the job title of the person being nominated.
* **Recognition Category** (Recognition Category): Choose a category for the recognition (e.g., Customer Service Excellence, Innovation, etc.).
* **Achievement Period** (Time period of achievements): Enter the time period of the nominee's achievements.
* **Nomination Reason** (Why are you nominating this person): Explain why you are nominating the individual.
* **Specific Achievements** (Describe specific accomplishments): Describe specific accomplishments of the nominee.
* **Impact Description** (What impact did their work have): Describe the impact of the nominee's work.
* **Company Values Demonstrated** (Select values shown by nominee): Select the company values demonstrated by the nominee (e.g., Integrity, Excellence, etc.).
* **Previous Recognition** (Has nominee been recognized before): Indicate if the nominee has been recognized before.
* **Previous Awards** (List previous awards if known): List any previous awards or recognition received by the nominee.
* **Witness Statements** (Are there others who can support this nomination): Indicate if others can support this nomination.
* **Witness Contacts** (Names and emails of supporters): Enter names and emails of supporters.
* **Suggested Recognition** (What type of recognition is suitable): Choose the type of recognition suitable for the nominee.
* **Confidentiality** (Should this nomination be kept confidential): Indicate if the nomination should be kept confidential until decision is made.
* **Nominator Signature** (Type your full name as signature): Type your full name as signature.
