# Mediation Agreement Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mediation Agreement Release Form is used to document the details of a mediation session, including the parties involved, any agreements reached, and any notable events or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Mediation Session Details**: Enter a brief description of the mediation session, including the date, time, and location.
2. **Parties Involved**: List all parties participating in the mediation session.
3. **Agreement Reached**: Describe the agreement reached during the mediation session.
4. **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the mediation session.
5. **Location**: Enter the location of the mediation session.
6. **Mediation Agreement**: Describe the mediation agreement.
7. **Signatures**: Indicate whether all parties have signed the agreement.
8. **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the mediation session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mediation Session Details** (`mediation_agreement_release_form_mediation_session_details`): A brief description of the mediation session, including the date, time, and location.
* **Parties Involved** (`mediation_agreement_release_form_parties_involved`): A list of all parties participating in the mediation session. Select all applicable options.
* **Agreement Reached** (`mediation_agreement_release_form_agreement_reached`): A description of the agreement reached during the mediation session.
* **Date and Time** (`mediation_agreement_release_form_date_and_time`): The date and time of the mediation session. Format: MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM
* **Location** (`mediation_agreement_release_form_location`): The location of the mediation session.
* **Mediation Agreement** (`mediation_agreement_release_form_mediation_agreement`): A description of the mediation agreement.
* **Signatures** (`mediation_agreement_release_form_signatures`): Indicate whether all parties have signed the agreement. If not all parties have signed, provide the reason.
* **Additional Comments** (`mediation_agreement_release_form_additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the mediation session.

## Tips
* Ensure that all parties have signed the agreement before submitting this form.
* If there are any changes to the agreement, update this form accordingly.
* Keep this form accurate and up-to-date to ensure that the mediation process is properly documented.
