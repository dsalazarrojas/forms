# Logistics Partner Evaluation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Logistics Partner Evaluation Request Form is used to evaluate and categorize logistics partners based on their performance. It is a crucial tool for identifying strong and weak partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the logistics partner you want to evaluate.
2. Choose whether the partner fits into the category "Yes" or "No".
3. If necessary, provide a note with additional information about the partner.
4. Fill in the vendor performance rating (if you want to rate them).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **partner** (`partner`, text, **optional**): This field is where you will select the logistics partner you want to evaluate.
* **category** (`category`, select multiple, **optional**): Choose whether the partner fits into the category "Yes" or "No".
* **vendor_performance** (`vendor_performance`, number, **optional**): This field is where you can rate the partner's performance on a scale of 0-10.
* **supporting_documents** (`supporting_documents`, note, **optional**): If you have additional information about the partner, you can provide it in this field.
