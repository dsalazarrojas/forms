> # face_to_face_guidelines - Help Guide
## Purpose
The face_to_face_guidelines form is used to collect information about employees' face-to-face interactions with supervisors. This form helps to track and document important details about employee attendance, job status, and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). The job status field is a select_one field with the options Active, Inactive, Vacation, Resigned, and Retired.
2. Enter your first and last name in the First Name and Last Name fields, respectively.
3. Provide your contact email address in the Contact Email field.
4. Enter your job title in the Job Title field.
5. If the employee's job status is Inactive, provide a reason for Inactive in the Reason for Inactive field.
6. If the employee's job status is Resigned, provide a reason for Resigned in the Reason for Resigned field.
7. If the employee's job status is Retired, provide a reason for Retired in the Reason for Retired field.
8. Enter the date of your last vacation in the Date of Last Vacation field, if applicable.
9. Enter the date of your last resignation in the Date of Last Resigned field, if applicable.
10. Enter the date of your last inactive period in the Date of Last Inactive field, if applicable.
11. Enter your supervisor's job title in the Supervisor Job Title field.
12. Provide your supervisor's email address in the Supervisor Email field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Status** (Job Status, select_one, \*: This field indicates the current status of the employee's job. Select one of the following options:
	+ Active: The employee is currently working.
	+ Inactive: The employee is not currently working.
	+ Vacation: The employee is on vacation.
	+ Resigned: The employee has resigned from their position.
	+ Retired: The employee has retired.
* **Reason for Inactive** (Reason for Inactive, text, ): This field is only required if the employee's job status is Inactive. Provide a brief explanation for why the employee's job status is Inactive.
* **Reason for Resigned** (Reason for Resigned, text, ): This field is only required if the employee's job status is Resigned. Provide a brief explanation for why the employee resigned.
* **Reason for Retired** (Reason for Retired, text, ): This field is only required if the employee's job status is Retired. Provide a brief explanation for why the employee retired.
* **First Name** (First Name, text, ): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, ): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, ): Provide your contact email address.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, ): Enter your job title.
* **Supervisor First Name** (Supervisor First Name, text, ): Enter your supervisor's first name.
* **Supervisor Last Name** (Supervisor Last Name, text, ): Enter your supervisor's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, ): Enter the date of your birth.
* **Time of Birth** (Time of Birth, time, ): Enter the time of your birth.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, ): Enter the date you started working.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, ): Enter the date you ended your employment.
* **Hours Worked** (Hours Worked, number, ): Enter the number of hours you've worked.
* **Job Performance Rating** (Job Performance Rating, text, ): Enter a performance rating.
* **Work Location** (Work Location, text, ): Enter the location where you work.
* **Supervisor Job Title** (Supervisor Job Title, text, ): Enter your supervisor's job title.
* **Supervisor Email** (Supervisor Email, email, ): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Date of Last Vacation** (Date of Last Vacation, date, ): Enter the date of your last vacation.
* **Date of Last Resigned** (Date of Last Resigned, date, ): Enter the date of your last resignation.
* **Date of Last Inactive** (Date of Last Inactive, date, ): Enter the date of your last inactive period.
* **Reason for Vacation** (Reason for Vacation, text, ): This field is only required if the employee is currently on vacation. Provide a brief explanation for why you are on vacation.
* **Reason for Resigned** (Reason for Resigned, text, ): This field is only required if the employee has resigned. Provide a brief explanation for why you resigned.
* **Reason for Retired** (Reason for Retired, text, ): This field is only required if the employee has retired. Provide a brief explanation for why you retired.

Note: The following fields are not used in this form:
* date_of_birth
* time_of_birth
* hours_worked
* job_performance_rating
* work_location
