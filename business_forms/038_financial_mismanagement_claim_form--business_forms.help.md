# Financial Mismanagement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Mismanagement Claim Form is designed to collect information about a specific event or situation that has led to a claim of financial mismanagement. This form will help us understand the details of the situation and ensure that the claim is processed accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the event or situation before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
3. Review your answers carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, date, **required**): Enter the date when the event or situation that led to the claim occurred.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, **required**): Provide a detailed description of the event or situation that led to the claim.
* **Amount Claimed** (`amount_claimed`, number, **required**): Enter the amount that you are claiming as a result of the mismanagement.
* **Claimed By** (`claimed_by`, text, **required**): Please enter the name of the person or entity that is making the claim.
* **Evidence** (`evidence`, text, **required**): Provide any relevant evidence or documentation that supports your claim.
* **Evidence Description** (`evidence_description`, text, **required**): Describe the evidence provided, including any context or details about how it supports your claim.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact our support team for clarification.
