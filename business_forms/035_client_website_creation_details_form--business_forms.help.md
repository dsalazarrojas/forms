# Client Website Creation Details Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information about your client's website creation project. Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately to ensure we can provide the best possible services for your project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide detailed information about your website project in the "Website Project Details" section.
2. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Name", "Company or Business Name", "Email Address", "Phone Number" fields.
3. Specify the type and purpose of your website in the "Type of Website", "Primary Purpose of the Website", and "Target Audience" sections.
4. Describe your business in the "Brief Description of Your Business" field.
5. Provide information about your business, such as the number of employees and years in business, if applicable.
6. Answer questions about your current website, if any, and provide the URL if applicable.
7. Indicate your preferred design style and provide examples of websites that you like.
8. Select the key features that you need for your website from the list.
9. Estimate the approximate number of pages for your website.
10. Specify whether you have website content and assets ready, and indicate if you have a logo and professional photos or images.
11. Select the SEO keywords and indicate if mobile-responsive design is essential.
12. Choose a budget range and specify when you need the website launched.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Project Details**: Please provide a detailed description of your project, including any relevant details about your business or needs.
* **Contact Name**: Your full name.
* **Company or Business Name**: Your company or business name.
* **Email Address**: Your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number.
* **Type of Website**: The type of website you need, such as business/company website, e-commerce store, blog, portfolio, etc.
* **Primary Purpose of the Website**: The primary purpose of your website.
* **Target Audience**: Who will be using your website?
* **Brief Description of Your Business**: A brief description of your business.
* **Years in Business**: The number of years your business has been in operation, if applicable.
* **Number of Employees**: The number of employees in your business, if applicable.
* **Current Website**: Do you currently have a website? If yes, provide the URL.
* **Current Website URL**: If you have an existing website, provide the URL.
* **Preferred Design Style**: Your preferred design style for your website (e.g., modern, minimalist, colorful, professional, etc.).
* **Examples of Websites You Like**: Provide URLs or describe examples of websites that you like.
* **Key Features**: Select all the features that you need for your website, such as contact forms, online store, blog/news, photo gallery, video integration, appointment booking, member portal, search function, social media integration, etc.
* **Approximate Number of Pages**: Estimate the approximate number of pages for your website.
* **Do you have website content ready?**: Do you have website content ready? If yes, it will be used in the project.
* **Do you have a logo?**: Do you have a logo for your business?
* **Do you have professional photos or images?**: Do you have professional photos or images for your website?
* **Target Keywords for SEO**: Provide a comma-separated list of target keywords for SEO.
* **Mobile Responsive Design is Essential**: Do you need a mobile-responsive design for your website? If yes, it's a must-have.
* **Budget Range**: Choose your budget range for the project.
* **When do you need the website launched?**: When do you need the website launched?
