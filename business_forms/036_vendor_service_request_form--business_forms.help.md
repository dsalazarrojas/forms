# Vendor Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Service Request Form is a tool for vendors to provide their service requests to the internal team. This form captures essential information about the requested service, contact details, and any additional information the user would like to provide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of service you are requesting.
2. Briefly describe the service you are seeking.
3. Enter your name and email address so that we can get in touch with you.
4. Provide your phone number for us to contact you.
5. Add any additional information you'd like to share about your service request.
6. Specify the date you are requesting the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What service are you requesting** (`service_type`): Select the category of service you are seeking, such as Accounting and Finance, IT Consulting, Marketing and Sales, or Operations. If your service doesn't fit into one of these categories, choose "Other" to provide a custom description.
* **Describe the service** (`service_description`): Provide a brief explanation of the service you are requesting. This will help us understand your needs better.
* **Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name so we can get in touch with you.
* **Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address so we can send you updates and information about your service request.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`): Provide your phone number so we can contact you for additional information.
* **Message** (`contact_message`): Add any additional information about your service request that is not covered in the other fields.
* **Service Request Date** (`service_request_date`): Specify the date you are requesting the service.

## Tips
- Make sure to submit only one request per service.
- Be as specific as possible when describing the service you are seeking.
- Ensure your email and phone numbers are correct and up-to-date.
- Use this form for new service requests only; if you have an existing request, please review and update that one instead of submitting a new one.
