# AML Compliance Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The AML Compliance Consent Form is used to obtain consent from clients for identity verification, monitoring of account activities, and information sharing with regulatory authorities. This form is a mandatory requirement for maintaining AML compliance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Enter your full name, email address, phone number, business name (if applicable), tax identification number, residential address, source of funds, expected account activity, estimated monthly transaction volume, account purpose, beneficial ownership disclosure, consent to identity verification, consent to monitoring of activities, consent to information sharing, and electronic signature consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (Required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter a valid email address for account communications.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your contact number for account verification.
* **Business Name** (Optional): Enter the name of the business entity (if applicable).
* **Tax Identification Number** (Required): Enter your SSN, EIN, or equivalent tax ID.
* **Residential Address** (Required): Enter your current address for identity verification.
* **Source of Funds** (Required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Employment Income
	+ Business Revenue
	+ Investment Returns
	+ Gift/Inheritance
	+ Sale of Assets
	+ Other
* **Expected Account Activity** (Required): Select all applicable activities:
	+ Deposits
	+ Withdrawals
	+ Transfers
	+ International Wire Transfers
	+ Checks
	+ Electronic Payments
* **Estimated Monthly Transaction Volume** (Required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Under $1,000
	+ $1,000-$5,000
	+ $5,000-$10,000
	+ $10,000-$25,000
	+ Over $25,000
* **Account Purpose** (Required): Enter the purpose of opening this account.
* **Beneficial Ownership Disclosure** (Required): Acknowledge that beneficial ownership information may be required.
* **Consent to Identity Verification** (Required): Consent to identity verification procedures as required by AML regulations.
* **Consent to Monitoring of Activities** (Required): Consent to monitoring of your account activities for suspicious transactions.
* **Consent to Information Sharing** (Required): Consent to sharing of information with regulatory authorities as required.
* **Electronic Signature Consent** (Required): Agree that your electronic submission constitutes your legal signature.
