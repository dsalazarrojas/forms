# Retail Digitalization Funding Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Digitalization Funding Application is a form used to apply for funding for digitalization projects in the retail industry. It helps to gather information about the project details, retailer information, project location, funding request, project cost, grant programs, supporting documents, project description, business support organization, agency name, contact person, phone, email, address, city, state, zip, project status, project type, project goal, project objectives, and project timeline.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project details (Project Details, Location, Project Start Date, Project Completion Date)
2. Choose the grant programs that apply to your project (Grant Programs)
3. Select the required supporting documents for your project (Supporting Documents)
4. Enter a brief description of your project (Project Description)
5. Select the type of your project (Project Type)
6. Enter your project's goal (Project Goal)
7. Enter your project's objectives (Project Objectives)
8. Provide a project timeline (Project Timeline)
9. Fill in the retailer information (Retailer Info)
10. Enter the name of the business support organization (Business Support Organization)
11. Enter the name of the agency (Agency Name)
12. Enter the contact person's name (Contact Person)
13. Enter the contact person's phone number (Phone)
14. Enter the contact person's email address (Email)
15. Enter the contact person's address (Address)
16. Enter the city where the project will be located (City)
17. Enter the state where the project will be located (State)
18. Enter the zip code of the project location (ZIP)
19. Select the project status (Project Status)
20. Select the status note for your project (Project Status Note)
21. Enter your project's type (Project Type)
22. Provide a note for your project status (Project Status Note)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details**: Enter the name of your project. (Required: false)
* **Retailer Info**: Enter information about the retailer, such as name and address. (Required: false)
* **Project Location**: Enter the location of the project, such as city, state, and zip. (Required: false)
* **Project Start Date**: Enter the start date of the project. (Required: false)
* **Project Completion Date**: Enter the completion date of the project. (Required: false)
* **Funding Request**: Enter the amount of funding you are requesting. (Required: false)
* **Project Cost**: Enter the total cost of the project. (Required: false)
* **Grant Programs**: Select the grant programs that apply to your project. (Required: true)
	+ Options: Small Business Administration, National Retail Foundation, Retail Association
* **Supporting Documents**: Select the documents required for your project. (Required: true)
	+ Options: Business Plan, Market Analysis, Financial Statement
* **Project Description**: Enter a brief description of your project. (Required: false)
* **Business Support Organization**: Enter the name of the business support organization. (Required: false)
* **Agency Name**: Enter the name of the agency. (Required: false)
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person. (Required: false)
* **Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number. (Required: false)
* **Email**: Enter the contact person's email address. (Required: false)
* **Address**: Enter the contact person's address. (Required: false)
* **City**: Enter the city where the project will be located. (Required: false)
* **State**: Enter the state where the project will be located. (Required: false)
* **ZIP**: Enter the zip code of the project location. (Required: false)
* **Project Status**: Select the status of your project. (Required: true)
	+ Options: Active, Inactive, Pending
* **Project Status Note**: Enter a note for your project status. (Required: false)
* **Project Type**: Select the type of your project. (Required: true)
	+ Options: Digital Transformation, E-commerce, Data Analytics
* **Project Goal**: Enter the goal of your project. (Required: false)
* **Project Objectives**: Enter the objectives of your project. (Required: false)
* **Project Timeline**: Provide a timeline for your project. (Required: false)

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Review your project details carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have the necessary supporting documents before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, contact your project manager or support team.
