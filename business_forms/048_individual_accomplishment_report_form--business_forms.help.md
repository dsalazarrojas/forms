# Individual Accomplishment Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Individual Accomplishment Report Form" is a tool used to document and showcase an employee's achievements and progress in their work. It is a key component of the performance evaluation and development process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure you've covered all relevant information.
3. Proofread your submission before submitting it to your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First name
* **First name**: Enter your first name.

### Last name
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.

### Job title
* **Job title**: Enter your current job title.

### Work unit
* **Work unit**: Select the work unit you belong to (Team A, Team B, or Team C).

### Start Date
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the relevant project or period being reported.

### End Date
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the relevant project or period being reported.

### Project ID
* **Project ID**: Enter the ID of the project being reported.

### Project code
* **Project code**: Enter the code of the project being reported.

### Accomplishment
* **Accomplishment**: Describe your significant accomplishments and achievements during the reporting period.

### Impact
* **Impact**: Describe the impact of your accomplishments on the organization or team.

### Key Results
* **Key Results**: List the key results achieved during the reporting period.

### Actions
* **Actions**: Describe the actions taken to achieve your goals.

### Obstacles
* **Obstacles**: Describe any obstacles or challenges faced during the reporting period.

### Lessons Learned
* **Lessons Learned**: Summarize the lessons learned and recommendations for improvement.

### Recommendations
* **Recommendations**: Make recommendations for future improvements or actions.

### Comments
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback.

### Submit
* **Submit**: Click to submit your report.

### Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.

### Manager name
* **Manager name**: Enter your manager's name.

### Manager email
* **Manager email**: Enter your manager's email address.

### Manager phone
* **Manager phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.

### Employee name
* **Employee name**: Enter your name.

### Employee job title
* **Employee job title**: Enter your job title.

### Employee email
* **Employee email**: Enter your email address.

### Employee phone
* **Employee phone**: Enter your phone number.
