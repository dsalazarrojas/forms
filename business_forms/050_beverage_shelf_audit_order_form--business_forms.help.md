# Beverage Shelf Audit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beverage Shelf Audit Order Form is a crucial tool for stores to request an audit of their beverage shelves. It helps to gather essential information from the user, including their identity, the store's details, and the specifics of the audit request. By filling out this form accurately, users can ensure that the audit is performed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the user information**:
	* Enter your full name in the "Your Name" field.
	* Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
2. **Provide store information**:
	* Enter the name of your company in the "Company Name" field.
	* Enter the name of the store to be audited in the "Store Name" field.
	* Enter the full address of the store in the "Store Address" field.
	* Enter the city where the store is located in the "City" field.
	* Enter the state where the store is located in the "State" field.
	* Enter the ZIP code for the store location in the "ZIP Code" field.
	* Enter the name of the store contact person or manager in the "Store Contact Name" field.
	* Enter the phone number of the store contact person or manager in the "Store Contact Phone" field.
3. **Select audit specifics**:
	* Choose the type of audit required in the "Audit Type" field from the options provided.
	* Select the category of beverages to be audited in the "Beverage Category" field from the options provided.
4. **Schedule the audit**:
	* Select a preferred date for the audit in the "Preferred Audit Date" field.
	* Optionally, select a preferred time for the audit in the "Preferred Audit Time" field.
5. **Add requirements and deliverables**:
	* Specify any additional requirements or data to be collected during the audit in the "Specific Requirements" field.
	* Specify any photo requirements, such as "Full Shelf View" or "Price Tags," in the "Photo Requirements" field.
	* Choose how the audit results will be delivered in the "How Should Data Be Delivered" field from the options provided.
6. **Determine the budget and urgency**:
	* Select the urgency level of the audit, including "Emergency" or "High Priority," in the "Urgency Level" field.
	* Optionally, select a budget range for the audit in the "Budget Range" field from the options provided.
7. **Add additional comments**:
	* Provide any additional comments or information about the audit request in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (1): Enter your full name to identify yourself as the person requesting the audit.
* **Email Address** (2): Provide your email address to receive audit confirmation and results.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your phone number for audit coordination.
* **Company Name** (4): Enter the name of your company requesting the audit.
* **Store Name** (5): Enter the name of the store to be audited.
* **Store Address** (6): Enter the full address of the store to be audited.
* **City** (7): Enter the city where the store is located.
* **State** (8): Enter the state where the store is located.
* **ZIP Code** (9): Enter the postal code for the store location.
* **Store Contact Name** (10): Enter the name of the store contact person or manager.
* **Store Contact Phone** (11): Enter the phone number of the store contact person or manager.
* **Audit Type** (12): Select the type of audit required (e.g., Full Shelf Audit, Partial Audit, etc.).
* **Beverage Category** (13): Select the category of beverages to be audited (e.g., Carbonated Soft Drinks, Energy Drinks, etc.).
* **Preferred Audit Date** (14): Select a preferred date for the audit.
* **Preferred Audit Time** (15): Optionally, select a preferred time for the audit.
* **Specific Requirements** (16): Specify any additional requirements or data to be collected during the audit.
* **Photo Requirements** (17): Specify any photo requirements, such as Full Shelf View or Price Tags.
* **How Should Data Be Delivered** (18): Choose how the audit results will be delivered (e.g., Email Report, Online Dashboard, etc.).
* **Urgency Level** (19): Select the urgency level of the audit, including "Emergency" or "High Priority."
* **Budget Range** (20): Optionally, select a budget range for the audit from the options provided.
* **Additional Comments** (21): Provide any additional comments or information about the audit request.

## Tips
* Ensure that the form is filled out accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and efficient audit process.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure that all required fields are completed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the audit team for assistance.
