# Cash Flow Statement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cash Flow Statement Report Form is a document used to report cash inflows and outflows of a company over a specific period, providing a comprehensive picture of a company's cash flows.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Start by selecting the report type (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annually, or Annually).
*   Enter the report date and ensure the correct fiscal year is selected.
*   Fill out business information, including the legal business name and its relevant details.
*   Report the cash flows from different activities, such as operating, investing, and financing.
*   Ensure to enter accurate and correct data for each field.
*   Review and adjust any discrepancies in the summary and balance fields.
