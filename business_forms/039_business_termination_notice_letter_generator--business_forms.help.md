# Business Termination Notice Letter Generator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for businesses to provide formal notice of termination to other parties such as service providers or suppliers. 

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the sender information fields: "Your Name or Title", "Company Name", "Address", "Contact Email", and "Phone Number" with your details.
2.  Fill out the termination details: "Notice Date", "Recipient's Name", "Recipient's Company Name", "Address", "Type of Agreement Being Terminated", "Agreement Description", "Agreement Start Date", "Termination Effective Date", "Notice Period Provided", "Reason for Termination", "Reason Category", "Outstanding Obligations", "Final Payment Details", and "Authorized Signature" with the relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name or Title (1)**: Enter your name or title as the person sending the notice.
* **Your Company Name (2)**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Your Company Address (3)**: Enter your company's complete address.
* **Your Email Address (4)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Your Phone Number (5)**: Enter your phone number. (Optional)
* **Notice Date (6)**: Enter the date the notice is being sent.
* **Recipient's Name (7)**: Enter the name of the person or company receiving the notice.
* **Recipient's Company Name (8)**: Enter the name of the recipient's company.
* **Recipient's Address (9)**: Enter their complete address.
* **Recipient Contact Person (10)**: Enter the name of the person to address. (Optional)
* **Type of Agreement Being Terminated (11)**: Select the type of agreement being terminated. (e.g., Service Agreement, Supply Contract)
* **Agreement Description (12)**: Briefly describe the agreement or contract being terminated.
* **Agreement Start Date (13)**: If known, enter the date the agreement began. (Optional)
* **Termination Effective Date (14)**: Enter the date when the termination takes effect.
* **Notice Period Provided (15)**: Enter the notice period provided to the recipient.
* **Reason for Termination (16)**: Enter a clear and concise reason for termination.
* **Reason Category (17)**: Select the category of reason for termination. (e.g., Poor Performance, Cost Reduction)
* **Outstanding Obligations (18)**: If applicable, list any outstanding obligations that need to be completed before termination.
* **Final Payment Details (19)**: If applicable, enter any final payment details. (Optional)
* **Authorized Signature (20)**: Enter the name of the person authorized to sign the notice.
