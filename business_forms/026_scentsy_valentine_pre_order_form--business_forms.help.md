# Scentsy Valentine Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scentsy Valentine Pre Order Form is a simple form designed to collect customer information and order details for Valentine's Day gifts or cards. The form is intended for customers to place a pre-order and provide their shipping address for delivery purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Provide your customer information on the first page, including any relevant details.
2. Enter your order information on the second page, including any specific details about your order.
3. Enter your shipping address on the third page, which will be used for delivery purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`Customer Information`, text, required):
	+ This field is for you to enter your name, phone number, and any other relevant details about yourself as a customer.
* **Order Information** (`Order Information`, text, required):
	+ In this field, please enter the specific details about your order, such as gift type and message.
* **Shipping Address** (`Shipping Address`, text, required):
	+ This field is for you to enter your shipping address, which will be used to deliver your order. Please include a clear street address and apartment/ suite number.
