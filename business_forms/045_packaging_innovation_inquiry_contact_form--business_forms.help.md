# Packaging Innovation Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your Packaging Innovation Inquiry and facilitate further discussions or collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your brand or manufacturer name in the "Brand/Manufacturer" field.
2. Choose the type of inquiry you are interested in from the "Inquiry type" field by selecting one or more options.
3. Briefly describe your project in the "Project description" field.
4. If you have specific collaboration requests (e.g., partnership, joint-venture, license), select the relevant option in the "Collaboration requests" field.
5. Fill in your contact information, including your first name, last name, and email address in the "Contact first name," "Contact last name," and "Email" fields, respectively.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the submit button to send us your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Brand/Manufacturer** (Brand/Manufacturer, text, required): Enter your brand or manufacturer name to help us identify you in our database.
* **Inquiry type** (Inquiry type, select multiple, optional): Choose one or more options to indicate the type of inquiry you are interested in.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are interested in a partnership or joint-venture.
	+ Select 'No' if you are not interested in a partnership or joint-venture.
* **Project description** (Project description, text, optional): Briefly describe your project to help us understand your needs.
* **Collaboration requests** (Collaboration requests, select one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Partnership: We are interested in a partnership opportunity.
	+ Joint-venture: We are interested in a joint-venture opportunity.
	+ License: We are interested in a licensing agreement.
* **Contact first name** (Contact first name, text, required): Enter your first name to contact you.
* **Contact last name** (Contact last name, text, required): Enter your last name to contact you.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address to contact you.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number to contact you.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have not filled out this form previously to avoid duplicating efforts.
* If you are not sure about the type of inquiry or collaboration requests, please select 'No' or 'None of the above' to indicate that you are open to discussing your options with us.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact us.
