# Relocation Budget Extension Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees who need to request a relocation budget extension. It helps managers and decision-makers make informed decisions by gathering necessary details and approvals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the prompts and fill in the required fields. You can type in your answers directly into the text fields provided. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form** (<font size="1">(1)</font>): A brief description of your reason for requesting a relocation budget extension.
* **Employee Information** (<font size="1">(2)</font>): Please provide your name, job title, department, and contact information so that we can quickly contact you for further details.
* **Request Details** (<font size="1">(3)</font>): Describe your relocation plans, including the location, dates, and any other relevant details.
* **Approvals** (<font size="1">(4)</font>): Please list any necessary approvals from managers or team members that support your relocation request.
* **Attachments** (<font size="1">(5)</font>): You can attach any relevant documents, such as receipts, agreements, or photos that support your relocation request.

## Tips
- Make sure to clearly describe your reason for the relocation request.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Attach any supporting documents or approvals to strengthen your request.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
