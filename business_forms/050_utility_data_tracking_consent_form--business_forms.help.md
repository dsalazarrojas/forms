# Utility Data Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer data for utility tracking purposes, such as electricity, gas, and water consumption. The form is used to obtain consent from customers to track their utility data, which helps us improve our services and better understand their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your customer name and email address.
2. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
3. Choose the type of utility data you would like to track (electricity, gas, water, or other).
4. Select how frequently you would like to receive collections (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
5. Enter the start and end dates for the collection period.
6. If applicable, enter the ID of the assigned user.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your account.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it to enable faster communication.
* **Utility Data Type** (`utility_data_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of utility data you would like to track (electricity, gas, water, or other).
* **Collection Frequency** (`collection_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select how frequently you would like to receive collections (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
* **Collection Start Date** (`collection_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date for the collection period.
* **Collection End Date** (`collection_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date for the collection period.
* **Assigned User ID** (`assigned_user_id`, text, optional): If you have been assigned a specific user ID, enter it here (if applicable).

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for assistance.
- Your consent to track your utility data will be stored securely and used only for the purpose of improving our services.
