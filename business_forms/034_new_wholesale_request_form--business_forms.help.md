# New Wholesale Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Wholesale Request Form is a tool used to facilitate the process of creating a new wholesale request for a business or individual. This form collects necessary contact and order details to ensure that the wholesale request is properly processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address fields with your correct information.
2. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field for communication purposes.
3. Provide your Company Name in the Company field.
4. Enter your Address, City, and Zip fields with your correct address.
5. Select the products you are interested in from the Products dropdown list.
6. Enter the quantity of each product you want to purchase.
7. Choose a Date for the delivery or pickup of your order.
8. Click the Submit button to submit your wholesale request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required/Optional: Optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Company** (`company`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter your company name.
* **Address** (`address`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter your state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional)
	+ This field is where you can enter your zip code.
* **Products** (`products`, Select Multiple, Required/Optional: Optional)
	+ This field is a dropdown list of available products. Select all products you are interested in.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, Number, Required/Optional: Required for selected products)
	+ This field is where you can enter the quantity of each selected product.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Required/Optional: Optional)
	+ This field is where you can choose a delivery or pickup date for your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct products and quantities to avoid any miscommunication or misordering.
* Ensure that your address is correct to ensure proper delivery or pickup of your order.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, include them in the "Additional Information" field (not shown in this guide).
