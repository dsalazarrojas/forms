# Climate Neutral Hospitality Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Climate Neutral Hospitality Program Registration form is designed to help hospitality businesses join the program and commit to sustainable practices. This guide will walk you through each step of the form to ensure a smooth and accurate submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you understand what each field means.
2.  Fill in your property's name and address in the "Property Name" and "Property Address" fields.
3.  Select the correct property type from the "Property Type" field.
4.  Enter the number of guest rooms in the "Number of Guest Rooms" field.
5.  Provide your contact person's name, title, and email address in the "Contact Person" fields.
6.  Answer the questions related to your current sustainability initiatives, energy sources, and waste management practices.
7.  Set your climate goals and target dates for neutrality.
8.  Indicate your willingness to invest in sustainability improvements and what type of support you would find most helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Property Name** (`property_name`, text, required): Enter your property's name, e.g., "The Green Hotel."
- **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Choose your property type from the options provided (Hotel, Bed and Breakfast, Inn, etc.).
- **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter your property's street address.
- **Number of Guest Rooms** (`number_of_rooms`, number, required): Enter the number of guest rooms in your property.
- **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter your contact person's name.
- **Contact Person Title** (`contact_title`, text, optional): Enter your contact person's title, if applicable.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact person's email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact person's phone number.
- **Annual Guest Count** (`annual_guests`, number, optional): Enter the approximate annual guest count.
- **On-Site Restaurant or Food Service** (`has_restaurant`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have an on-site restaurant or food service.
- **Spa or Wellness Facilities** (`has_spa`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have a spa or wellness facilities.
- **Conference or Event Spaces** (`has_conference`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have conference or event spaces.
- **Current Sustainability Initiatives** (`current_initiatives`, select_multiple, required): Select all current sustainability initiatives your property is undertaking (e.g., Renewable Energy, Water Conservation, etc.).
- **Primary Energy Source** (`energy_source`, text, optional): Enter your primary energy source.
- **Waste Management Practices** (`waste_management`, text, optional): Describe your waste management practices.
- **Environmental Certifications or Standards** (`certifications`, text, optional): Enter any environmental certifications or standards your property holds.
- **Have You Calculated Your Carbon Footprint?** (`carbon_footprint_known`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have calculated your carbon footprint.
- **Target Year for Carbon Neutrality** (`carbon_reduction_target`, number, optional): Enter the target year for carbon neutrality.
- **Target Carbon Reduction Percentage** (`reduction_percentage`, number, optional): Enter the target carbon reduction percentage.
- **Primary Climate Goal** (`climate_goal`, select_one, required): Choose your primary climate goal from the options provided (Carbon Neutral by 2030, Carbon Neutral by 2040, etc.).
- **Willing to Invest in Sustainability Improvements** (`investments`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you are willing to invest in sustainability improvements.
- **What Type of Support Would Be Most Helpful?** (`support_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of support you would find most helpful (e.g., Technical Guidance, Financial Incentives, etc.).
- **Additional Information or Commitments** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or commitments your property would like to make.
