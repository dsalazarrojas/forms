# Pet Product Disclaimer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Product Disclaimer Form is used to collect information from pet owners who purchase pet products from your business. This form is designed to gather essential details about the user, the product they purchased, and their business contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title in the "User Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the pet product you purchased in the "Product Name" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the product in the "Product Description" field (optional).
4. Write the disclaimer text as per the product details in the "Disclaimer Text" field.
5. Sign your name in the "Signature" field.
6. Enter the date of the agreement in the "Agreement Date" field.
7. If your business has a name, enter it in the "Business Name" field (optional).
8. If your business has an address, enter it in the "Business Address" field (optional).
9. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name** (User Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
- **Product Name** (Product Name, text, required): Enter the name of the pet product you purchased.
- **Product Description** (Product Description, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the product, if applicable.
- **Disclaimer Text** (Disclaimer Text, text, required): Enter any specific details or disclaimers about the product as per the product manufacturer's instructions.
- **Signature** (Signature, text, required): Sign your name to acknowledge that you have read and understood the product details and disclaimers.
- **Agreement Date** (Agreement Date, date, required): Enter the date of the agreement in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Business Name** (Business Name, text, optional): Enter your business name, if applicable.
- **Business Address** (Business Address, text, optional): Enter your business address, if applicable.
- **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, required): Enter your contact number, including area code.
- **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you wish to be contacted for any follow-up.
