# Risk Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Risk Incident Form is used to document and report workplace incidents, including their details, causes, and consequences. This information helps to identify potential risks and take corrective actions to prevent similar incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) on the field label.
2. Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Describe the incident in the space provided.
4. Indicate if the incident involved injuries or damages.
5. Identify the cause of the incident, if known.
6. Enter any relevant comments or actions taken.
7. If you are reporting the incident, enter your name, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **workplace_risk** (text, optional): Describe the workplace where the incident occurred.
* **incident_date** (date, required\*): Enter the date of the incident in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **incident_location** (text, optional): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **incident_description** (text, required\*): Describe the incident in detail, including any circumstances leading up to it.
* **incident_reported_by** (text, optional): Enter your name if you are reporting the incident.
* **incident_type** (select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the incident involved a hazard, "No" if it did not.
* **incident_type_other** (text, optional): If you selected "Yes" above, enter an explanation.
* **incident_cause** (text, optional): Identify the cause of the incident, if known.
* **incident_injuries** (text, optional): Describe any injuries sustained during the incident.
* **incident_damages** (text, optional): Describe any damages or losses sustained during the incident.
* **incident_actions** (text, optional): Enter any actions taken to address the incident.
* **incident_comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **incident_reporter_name** (text, optional): Enter your name if you are reporting the incident.
* **incident_reporter_phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you are reporting the incident.
* **incident_reporter_email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you are reporting the incident.
* **incident_reviewed** (text, optional): Enter a note indicating if this incident has been reviewed.

Note: This help guide is a general explanation and may not cover all possible fields or options. If you have any questions or concerns, please consult with your supervisor or relevant department.
