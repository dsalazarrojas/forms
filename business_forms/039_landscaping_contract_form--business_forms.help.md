# Landscaping Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate communication between landscapers or gardening services providers and clients about upcoming projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by scrolling through the form pages to understand the structure and fields that need to be completed.
2. On each page, fill in the relevant information as prompted.

- **Project Details** (<type: text>, required: false):

	+ Enter the relevant information as needed.
- **Payment Terms** (<type: text>, required: false):
	+ This field is for outlining the payment terms and conditions for the project.

- **Timeline** (<type: text>, required: false):
	+ This field is for defining the project timeline, including start and end dates, milestones, or any relevant deadlines.

- **Client Info** (<type: text>, required: false):
	+ This field is for collecting and storing client information, such as contact details or relevant background information.
	+ Enter the necessary client information to ensure smooth communication and project execution.
