# Silent Partnership Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document the details of a silent partnership between an active partner and a silent partner. It captures information about the partners' roles, profit shares, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the details of the active partner, including their name, role, and any other relevant information.
2. Enter the details of the silent partner, including their name, role, and any other relevant information.
3. Specify the profit share for each partner.
4. Outline the contribution requirements for each partner.
5. Enter the confirmation details, including the date and time of confirmation.
6. Specify the agreement terms and conditions for the partnership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Active Partner Details
* **Active Partner Details**: Enter the name and contact information of the active partner.
* **Profit Share**: Enter the percentage of profit share for the active partner.
* **Contribution Requirements**: Specify the contribution requirements for the active partner.
* **Confirmation Details**: Enter the date and time of confirmation for the active partner.

### Silent Partner Details
* **Silent Partner Details**: Enter the name and contact information of the silent partner.
* **Profit Share**: Enter the percentage of profit share for the silent partner.
* **Contribution Requirements**: Specify the contribution requirements for the silent partner.
* **Confirmation Details**: Enter the date and time of confirmation for the silent partner.

### Partnership Agreement Details
* **Active Partnership Agreement Details**: Enter the details of the partnership agreement between the active partner and other partners.
* **Silent Partnership Agreement Details**: Enter the details of the partnership agreement between the silent partner and other partners.
* **Agreement Terms**: Outline the terms and conditions of the partnership agreement.
* **Confirmation Requirements**: Specify the confirmation requirements for the partnership agreement.

### Confirmation Details
* **Confirmation Confirmation Date**: Enter the date of confirmation for the partnership agreement.
* **Confirmation Confirmation Time**: Enter the time of confirmation for the partnership agreement.
* **Agreement Confirmation Date**: Enter the date of confirmation for the agreement.
* **Agreement Confirmation Time**: Enter the time of confirmation for the agreement.
* **Partner Confirmation Date**: Enter the date of confirmation for the partner.
* **Partner Confirmation Time**: Enter the time of confirmation for the partner.
* **Agreement Confirmation Method**: Select the method of confirmation (Email, In Person, Phone, or Video Conference).
* **Agreement Confirmation Location**: Specify the location of confirmation.
* **Silent Confirmation Date**: Enter the date of confirmation for the silent partner.
* **Silent Confirmation Time**: Enter the time of confirmation for the silent partner.
* **Silent Confirmation Method**: Select the method of confirmation (Email, In Person, Phone, or Video Conference).

### Roles and Contributions
* **Roles**: Select the roles of each partner (Partner, Active Partner, Silent Partner, Manager, or Owner).
* **Contributions**: Select the contributions of each partner (Cash, In Kind, or Services).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* Double-check the profit share and contribution requirements for each partner.
* Ensure that the agreement terms and conditions are clear and concise.
