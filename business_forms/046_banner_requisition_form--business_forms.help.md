# Banner Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a banner for various purposes such as marketing campaigns, events, and office decorations. It captures essential information about the request, including the required fields for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requisitioner Name** (`requisitioner_name`, `text`, *): Enter your name.
* **Email Address** (`requisitioner_email`, `email`, *): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`requisiton_phone`, `text`, *): Enter your contact number.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, *): Select your department from the options: Marketing, Sales, Events, Human Resources, Operations, Executive, or Other.
* **Cost Center** (`cost_center`, `text`, ): Enter your budget code if required.
* **Requisition Date** (`requisition_date`, `date`, *): Enter the date of the request.
* **Needed By Date** (`needed_by_date`, `date`, *): Enter the date when the banner is needed.
* **Banner Purpose** (`banner_purpose`, `select_one`, *): Select the purpose of the banner from the options: Marketing Campaign, Event Promotion, Trade Show, Office Decoration, Recruitment, Safety Signage, or Other.
* **Event or Campaign Name** (`event_or_campaign`, `text`, ): Enter the specific event or campaign name (optional).
* **Banner Specifications** (`banner_specifications`, `select_one`, *): Select the size and type of banner from the options: Small Vinyl (2x4 ft), Medium Vinyl (3x6 ft), Large Vinyl (4x8 ft), Fabric Banner, Retractable Banner, or Custom Specifications.
* **Custom Size** (`custom_size`, `text`, ): If custom banner size is selected, enter the specific size.
* **Quantity Requested** (`quantity_requested`, `number`, *): Enter the number of banners needed.
* **Design Requirements** (`design_requirements`, `text`, *): Describe how the banner should look like.
* **Text Content** (`text_content`, `text`, *): Enter the exact wording for the banner.
* **Brand Guidelines** (`branding_guidelines`, `select_one`, *): Select how to follow the standard branding guidelines or if there are special requirements.
* **Logo Usage** (`logo_usage`, `select_one`, *): Decide if the company logo should be included.
* **Color Requirements** (`color_requirements`, `text`, ): Enter specific colors if required.
* **Usage Location** (`usage_location`, `select_one`, *): Select where the banner will be used from the options: Indoor, Outdoor, or Both.
* **Installation Required** (`installation_required`, `select_one`, *): Decide if professional installation is needed.
* **Vendor Preference** (`vendor_preference`, `text`, ): Enter your preferred supplier if any.
* **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, `number`, ): Enter your budget estimate (optional).
* **Funding Source** (`funding_source`, `text`, ): Enter the budget source (optional).
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, `text`, *): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, *): Select the current status from the options: Approved, Pending, or Not Yet Submitted.
* **Additional Justification** (`additional_justification`, `text`, ): Enter the reason for the request (optional).
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, ): Enter any specific instructions or notes.
