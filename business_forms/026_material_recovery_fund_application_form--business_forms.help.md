# Material Recovery Fund Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Recovery Fund Application Form is designed to collect information from project teams about their projects that require funding from the Material Recovery Fund. This form is used to assess the project's eligibility and allocate resources accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with your team's details.
2. Provide project details, including the project's location and status.
3. Specify the project's funding requirements and budget.
4. Enter the expected completion date of the project.
5. Select the project status and add any additional notes.
6. Fill out the contact information for the project manager.
7. Specify the project manager's title and contact details.
8. Indicate whether the project team members are part of the project.
9. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Fill out your team's name, and contact details, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the project.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if the project is located within our region, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Funding Requirements** (`funding_requirements`, `number`, required): Enter the required funding amount for the project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, `number`, required): Enter the project's total budget.
* **Expected Completion Date** (`expected_completion_date`, `date`, required): Enter the expected completion date of the project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the project status as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Project Status Notes** (`project_status_notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes about the project status.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, `text`, required): Fill out the project manager's name.
* **Project Manager Title** (`project_manager_title`, `text`, required): Fill out the project manager's title.
* **Project Manager Email** (`project_manager_email`, `email`, required): Fill out the project manager's email.
* **Project Manager Phone** (`project_manager_phone`, `text`, required): Fill out the project manager's phone number.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'Yes' if the project team members are part of the project, or 'No' otherwise.

Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
