# safety_standards_compliance_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The safety standards compliance approval form is used to gather contact information and confirm compliance with relevant safety standards. This form is essential for ensuring that your company's safety standards are up-to-date and meet the necessary requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your company's contact information (Company Name, Contact Name, Contact Email, and Contact Phone).
* Select the relevant safety standards that apply to your company (3E+L+R, 3E+L+R+I, 3E+L+R+I+O, 3E+L+R+S, 3E+L+R+S+I).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (text, optional): Enter your company's name as it appears in official records.
* **Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact regarding this form.
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the person to contact regarding this form.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person to contact regarding this form.
* **Safety Standards** (select_one, optional): Select the relevant safety standards that apply to your company. Options include:
	+ 3E+L+R
	+ 3E+L+R+I
	+ 3E+L+R+I+O
	+ 3E+L+R+S
	+ 3E+L+R+S+I
* **Certification Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the safety standards certification (if applicable). This field is only required if you select any safety standards.
