# Service Ticket And Part Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Ticket And Part Request Form is a tool for requesting service tickets and parts from the team. It helps the team to capture and process service requests in a structured and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about the service ticket and part request.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Ticket** (`service_ticket_and_part_request_form`): Enter a brief description of the service ticket and part request.
* **Part Name** (`part_name`): Enter the name of the part or component that is required for the service ticket.
* **Description** (`description`): Provide a detailed description of the service ticket and part request.
* **Priority** (`priority`): Select the priority level of the service ticket:
	+ High: Urgent or high-priority service request.
	+ Medium: Routine service request.
	+ Low: Non-urgent or low-priority service request.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`): Enter the expected due date for the service ticket and part request, if applicable.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter the name of the customer who requested the service ticket and part.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter the contact number of the customer.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the service ticket and part request will be fulfilled.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`): Select the type of service required:
	+ Hardware: Physical equipment or device repair or replacement.
	+ Software: Software installation, update, or maintenance.
* **Part Number** (`part_number`): Enter the part number required for the service ticket and part request.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
- Use this form for new service tickets and part requests only.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the team for assistance.
