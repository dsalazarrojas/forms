# Marketing Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Contest Entry Form is a tool for users to enter a marketing contest by providing essential details about their submission. This guide will walk you through each field of the form to help you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide the correct details.
3. Submit your entry once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter** (`Submitter`, text, required: false): Please enter your name and contact information in this field. This will help the contest organizers contact you if they need more information or have questions about your submission.
* **Entry Title** (`Entry Title`, text, required: false): Enter a title that accurately represents your submission. This should be a brief and descriptive title that helps identify your entry.
* **Entry Description** (`Entry Description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your submission. This can include any necessary details or context that will help the contest judges understand your entry.
* **Entry Image** (`Entry Image`, text, required: false): If your submission includes an image, please provide the URL or upload the image here. You can also provide an alternative text for visually impaired individuals.
* **Submit Date** (`Submit Date`, date, required: false): If applicable, enter the date you submitted your entry.
* **Entry URL** (`Entry URL`, text, required: false): If your submission is a URL, provide it here.
* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category that best fits your submission. Please choose one from the provided options.
* **Tags** (`Tags`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant tags or keywords associated with your submission.
* **Submission Type** (`Submission Type`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether your submission is a 'Yes' or 'No' submission. Please clarify if you are unsure.
* **Submission Status** (`Submission Status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your submission, whether it's 'Active' or 'Inactive'. This will help the contest organizers track your entry.
* **Submission Notes** (`Submission Notes`, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or notes about your submission, please provide them here.
* **Submitter Email** (`Submitter Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Submitter Phone** (`Submitter Phone`, text, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Submitter Company** (`Submitter Company`, text, required: false): If applicable, enter your company name or affiliation.
* **Submitter Job Title** (`Submitter Job Title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title or position.
* **Submitter Department** (`Submitter Department`, text, required: false): Enter your department or team name.
* **Submitter Location** (`Submitter Location`, text, required: false): Enter your location or city.
* **Submitter Job Function** (`Submitter Job Function`, text, required: false): Enter your job function or role.
* **Submitter Job Level** (`Submitter Job Level`, text, required: false): Enter your job level or position level.
* **Submitter Category** (`Submitter Category`, text, required: false): Enter your category or affiliation.
* **Entry Image Alt Text** (`Entry Image Alt Text`, text, required: false): If your submission includes an image, provide alternative text for visually impaired individuals.
* **Entry Image Caption** (`Entry Image Caption`, text, required: false): Enter a caption for your image.
* **Submitter Comments** (`Submitter Comments`, text, required: false): If you have any comments or feedback about the contest, please provide them here.
* **Submitter Agreement** (`Submitter Agreement`, select_one, required: false): Agree or disagree with the terms and conditions of the submission.
* **Submitter Agreement Date** (`Submitter Agreement Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you agree to the terms and conditions of the submission.

Note: Please ensure that you read the form carefully and provide accurate information to increase your chances of winning. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
