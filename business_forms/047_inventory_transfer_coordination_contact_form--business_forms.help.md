# Inventory Transfer Coordination Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Transfer Coordination Contact Form is used to gather information from the contact person responsible for the inventory transfer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the contact person's name in the `contact_name` field.
2. Enter the contact person's email address in the `email` field.
3. Provide a detailed description of the inventory transfer in the `transfer_details` field.
4. Select the date of the transfer from the calendar in the `transfer_date` field.
5. Choose the time of the transfer from the time picker in the `transfer_time` field.
6. Enter the contact person's phone number in the `contact_phone` field.
7. If a specific tool is assigned to the transfer, select "Yes" in the `assigned_tool` field.
8. Add any additional notes about the transfer in the `notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contact_name**: Enter the name of the person who will be coordinating the transfer.
* **email**: Enter the email address of the person who will be coordinating the transfer. This will help the logistics or supply chain team stay in touch with them.
* **transfer_details**: Provide a detailed description of the inventory transfer, including the items being transferred, the locations involved, and any other relevant information.
* **transfer_date**: Select the date of the transfer from the calendar. Make sure to choose a date that works for both parties involved in the transfer.
* **transfer_time**: Choose the time of the transfer from the time picker. Be mindful of any time zones or scheduling conflicts.
* **contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who will be coordinating the transfer. This will allow others to contact them if needed.
* **assigned_tool**: If a specific tool is assigned to the transfer, select "Yes" in this field. This indicates that the tool is part of the transfer and should be moved with the inventory.
* **notes**: Add any additional notes about the transfer, such as any challenges or concerns that need to be addressed.
