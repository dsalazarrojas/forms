# pp_weekly_update - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pp_weekly_update form is used to submit weekly updates from team members, providing information about the date and time of the update, the type and category of the update, and its status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the pp_weekly_update form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date and time of the update from the calendar or clock input fields.
2. Choose the type of update, such as "Yes" or "No".
3. Select the categories of the update, such as "Yes" or "No".
4. Enter any additional notes about the update.
5. Select the status of the update, such as "Active" or "Inactive".
6. Enter your name as the author of the update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Update Date** (`update_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the update.
* **Time** (`update_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the update.
* **Type** (`update_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of update, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Category** (`update_category`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the categories of the update, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Notes** (`update_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional information about the update.
* **Status** (`update_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the update, such as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Author** (`update_author`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the author of the update.
* **Update 2**, **Update 3**, **Update 4**: These fields are similar to the previous ones, with the only difference being that they are additional updates for subsequent weeks.
* **Notes 2**, **Notes 3**, **Notes 4**: Enter any additional information about the update 2, 3, and 4 respectively.
