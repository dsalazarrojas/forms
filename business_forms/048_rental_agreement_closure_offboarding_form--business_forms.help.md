# rental_agreement_closure_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Rental Agreement Closure Offboarding Form" is a document used to formalize the closure of a rental agreement between a landlord and a tenant. This form is intended to be completed by the tenant to provide necessary information and formalize the offboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all required information and necessary documents before starting the form.
2.  Review the form carefully to understand what information is required and why.
3.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tenant Details** (`tenant_details`, text, optional): Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
- **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the property address where the rental agreement is to be closed.
- **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date you moved into the property.
- **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date you plan to move out of the property.
- **Move Reason** (`move_reason`, text, optional): Provide a clear explanation for why the rental agreement is to be closed.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter any relevant contact information for yourself or your representative.
- **Landlord Signature Date** (`landlord_signature_date`, date, optional): The date the landlord signs this form.
- **Landlord Signature** (`landlord_signature`, note, optional): The landlord's signature, which confirms they have reviewed and agreed to close the rental agreement.
- **Tenant Signature Date** (`tenant_signature_date`, date, optional): The date you sign this form.
- **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, note, optional): Your signature, which confirms you have reviewed and agreed to close the rental agreement.

## Tips

- Make sure to keep a copy of this form for your records.
- Ensure the landlord reviews and signs this form before proceeding with the rental agreement closure.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the landlord or your representative.
