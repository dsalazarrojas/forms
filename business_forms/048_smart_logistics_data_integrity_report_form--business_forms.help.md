# Smart Logistics Data Integrity Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Logistics Data Integrity Report Form is a tool used to identify and document logistics data issues. This form is used to report problems that disrupt the flow of goods, services, or information in the supply chain. By filling out this form, you can ensure that logistics teams can quickly identify and address issues, reducing delays and errors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of logistics data issue you are reporting. (Logistics Data Issues, required)
2. Enter the location where the issue occurred. (Location, required)
3. Describe the issue you are experiencing. (Issue Description, required)
4. If available, choose the date and time the issue started. (Date, optional)
5. If available, choose the time the issue started. (Time, optional)
6. Provide any additional location information related to the issue. (Location, optional)
7. Upload any relevant attachments related to the issue. (Attachment Upload, optional)
8. Describe the cause of the issue. (Cause, optional)
9. List any symptoms you are experiencing. (Symptoms, optional)
10. Outline the action plan to resolve the issue. (Action Plan, optional)
11. Update the follow-up status of the issue. (Follow-up Status, optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Logistics Data Issues**: Select all the issues you are experiencing, such as discrepancies, errors, or inconsistencies.
* **Location**: Enter the specific location where the issue occurred, including any details about the warehouse, dock, or other relevant site.
* **Issue Description**: Describe the problem you are experiencing, including any symptoms or effects.
* **Date**: If available, choose the date when the issue started.
* **Time**: If available, choose the time when the issue started.
* **Location**: Provide any additional location information related to the issue, such as the specific aisle or section.
* **Attachment Upload**: Upload any relevant documents, images, or videos that support your issue report.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the issue.
* **Cause**: Choose the reason why the issue occurred, if known.
* **Symptoms**: Select all the symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Action Plan**: Outline the steps you plan to take to resolve the issue.
* **Follow-up Status**: Update the status of any follow-up actions taken on the issue.
