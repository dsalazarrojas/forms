# denver_multi_page - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect various pieces of information from users for business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Enter your information in the corresponding fields.
3. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit a Classified Ad** (`submit_a_classified_ad`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the classified ad you wish to submit. This field is not required, so you can leave it blank if not applicable.
* **Enter Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company or organization. If you don't have a company or organization, you can enter your personal name.
* **Banner Images** (`banner_images`, text, optional): Enter any relevant images related to your classified ad. You can enter the URLs or file names of the images.
* **Social Media Links** (`social_media`, text, optional): Enter any social media links related to your classified ad. You can enter URLs or handles of the social media platforms.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your website, if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted via phone.
* **Banner Image Uploads** (`banner_image_uploads`, text, optional): Enter any images or files related to your classified ad. You can enter URLs or file names.
* **Other Information** (`other_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your classified ad.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your completed form.
