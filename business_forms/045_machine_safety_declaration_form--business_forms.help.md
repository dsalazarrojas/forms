# Machine Safety Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare safety information about machines within a company. It helps in documenting the usage, maintenance, and safety protocols for each machine. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the company name and address.
2. Fill in the machine information, including serial number, make, model, and location.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the machine's usage.
4. Provide the inspector's name, signature, and contact person's name.
5. Repeat steps 2-4 for any additional machines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Company Information
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company owning the machine.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where the company is located.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person to contact for further questions or concerns.

### Page 2: Machine Information 1
* **Machine Serial Number** (`machine_serial_number`, `text`, required): Enter the unique serial number of the machine.
* **Machine Make** (`machine_make`, `text`, required): Enter the make of the machine.
* **Machine Model** (`machine_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model of the machine.
* **Location Where Used** (`location_where_used`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the machine is used.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the machine started being used.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the machine stopped being used or will stop being used.

### Page 3: Machine Information 2
* **Company Name 2** (`company_name_2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company owning this machine.
* **Machine Serial Number 2** (`machine_serial_number_2`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the unique serial number of the second machine.
* **Machine Make 2** (`machine_make_2`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the make of the second machine.
* **Machine Model 2** (`machine_model_2`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the model of the second machine.
* **Location Where Used 2** (`location_where_used_2`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the location where the second machine is used.
* **Start Date 2** (`start_date_2`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date the second machine started being used.
* **End Date 2** (`end_date_2`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date the second machine stopped being used or will stop being used.

### Page 4: Inspection Details
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the inspector who performed the inspection.
* **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`, `text`, required): Enter the signature of the inspector.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person.

### Page 5: Additional Information 1
* **Machine Serial Number 2** (`machine_serial_number_2`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the serial number of any additional machines.
* **Machine Make 2** (`machine_make_2`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the make of any additional machines.
* **Machine Model 2** (`machine_model_2`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the model of any additional machines.
* **Location Where Used 2** (`location_where_used_2`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the location where any additional machines are used.
* **Start Date 2** (`start_date_2`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date any additional machines started being used.
* **End Date 2** (`end_date_2`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date any additional machines stopped being used or will stop being used.
* **Inspector Name 2** (`inspector_name_2`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the inspector for any additional machines.
* **Inspector Signature 2** (`inspector_signature_2`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the signature of the inspector for any additional machines.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of all entered information before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in completely and accurately. 
* If any field is unclear or you have further questions, contact the form administrator or your supervisor. 

This guide will help users understand the form's purpose and the required information, reducing errors and ensuring that the form is completed accurately and efficiently.
