# Audit Data Retention Authorization Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the retention of sensitive and confidential data. It requires input from the data owner or their authorized representative and must be completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the request is properly reviewed and approved.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out this form in its entirety, providing accurate and truthful information.
2.  Make sure to check the box for "I Acknowledge" to acknowledge your responsibility for the secure retention of the data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Applicant Full Name**: The full name of the person requesting the retention, as it appears on their official company ID or organizational card.
*   **Department/Unit**: The department or unit that will be responsible for the retention and management of the requested data.
*   **Audit Reference Number**: The unique identifier assigned to the audit or project that the data belongs to.
*   **Primary Type of Data to be Retained**: Select all applicable types of data that need to be retained, such as financial records, PII, or internal IT logs.
*   **Format of Data**: Choose the format in which the data is stored, such as digital cloud storage or physical media.
*   **Requested Retention Period (Years)**: Specify the total years after the audit completion date that the data needs to be retained.
*   **Justification for Retention**: Provide a clear explanation of why the data needs to be retained beyond the standard policy period.
*   **Relevant Compliance Standard**: Select the applicable compliance standard that governs the retention of this data, such as GDPR or HIPAA.
*   **Security Controls for Retention**: Describe the security measures that will be used to protect the data during its retention period.
*   **Planned Final Deletion Date**: Specify the date on which the data will be deleted or disposed of.
*   **Data Custodian Name**: Enter the name of the person or team responsible for ensuring the secure storage and disposal of the data.
*   **I Acknowledge**: Check this box to confirm that you are aware of and accept your responsibility for the secure retention of the data.
*   **Date of Application**: Enter the date on which this application is submitted.
*   **Applicant Digital Signature**: Sign this form electronically to confirm your identity and authorization.

Note: This form is designed to ensure accurate and thorough completion of data retention requests. Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and that the requested retention is in accordance with your organization's policies and applicable laws and regulations.
