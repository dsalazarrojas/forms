# Meat Inventory Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meat Inventory Management Form is used to collect and manage data related to meat inventory. This data is essential for businesses that handle meat products, such as tracking current stock levels, suppliers, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting "Current Stock" to enter the current stock levels you have available for sale or storage.
2. Select the type of suppliers you have (e.g., Cattle, Poultry, Fish, or Pork) in the "Suppliers" field.
3. Enter the address of the selected supplier in the "Supplier Address" field.
4. Choose the supplier from which you received the product from the "Supplier Name" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the purchase price of the product in the "Purchase Price" field.
6. Enter the name of the product in the "Product Name" field.
7. Enter the weight of the product in the "Product Weight" field.
8. Enter the expiration date of the product in the "Expiration Date" field. **Please note that this field is required.**
9. Enter the stock prophet (or forecast) value in the "Stock Prophet" field. **Please note that this field is required.**
10. Select the stock levels (e.g., High, Low) for the product from the "Stock Levels" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Current Stock** (`text`, required: false): Enter the current stock levels you have available for sale or storage.
* **Suppliers** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of suppliers you have (e.g., Cattle, Poultry, Fish, or Pork).
* **Supplier Address** (`text`, required: false): Enter the address of the selected supplier.
* **Supplier Name** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose the supplier from which you received the product from.
* **Purchase Price** (`number`, required: false): Enter the purchase price of the product.
* **Product Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the product.
* **Product Weight** (`text`, required: false): Enter the weight of the product.
* **Expiration Date** (`date`, required: true): Enter the expiration date of the product.
* **Stock Prophet** (`number`, required: true): Enter the stock prophet (or forecast) value for the product.
* **Stock Levels** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the stock levels (e.g., High, Low) for the product.
