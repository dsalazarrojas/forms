# Organizational Health Review Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Health Review Registration Form is designed to collect information about an organization's health status. This form is for internal use, and its purpose is to gather data that will help us make informed decisions and take actions to support organizational development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Organization Name.
4. Enter a valid Email address.
5. Enter your Phone number.
6. If applicable, enter your Date of Birth.
7. Select your Organizational Health Status from the provided options.
8. If you have any additional comments or information, enter them in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name, as displayed on your official documents or identification.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name, as displayed on your official documents or identification.
* **Organization Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the organization you represent. This should match the name on your official documents or identification.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that is actively used for communication. This will help us reach out to you with updates and information.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): If applicable, enter your date of birth. This will help us with demographic analysis.
* **Organizational Health Status** (Select, Required): Select the status of the organization you represent from the provided options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Comments** (Note, Optional): If you have any additional comments or information, enter them here. This is a free-text field where you can provide any relevant details.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Use a valid email address that is actively used for communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
