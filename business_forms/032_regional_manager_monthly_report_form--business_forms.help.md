# Regional Manager Monthly Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Regional Manager Monthly Report Form is a tool for Regional Managers to report on their monthly performance, customer count, and success rate. This form will help management track the progress of each region and identify areas for improvement. Please ensure you are using this form to report on your own region only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the Regional Manager.
2. Select the month for which you are reporting (e.g., "January").
3. Enter your success rate (out of 100%) as a number.
4. Report the total number of customers you have in your region.
5. Enter the name of your region.
6. (Optional) Provide any additional comments or feedback.
7. (Optional) Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Regional Manager** (text, required)
Enter your name as the Regional Manager for the month.
### 2. **Manager Month** (text, required)
Select the month for which you are reporting (e.g., "January").
### 3. **Success Rate** (number, required)
Enter your success rate out of 100% (e.g., 95).
### 4. **Customer Count** (number, required)
Enter the total number of customers you have in your region.
### 5. **Region** (text, required)
Enter the name of your region (e.g., "New York").
### 6. **Comments** (text, optional)
Provide any additional comments or feedback about your performance.
### 7. **Email** (email, optional)
Enter your email address for contact purposes (not required but recommended).
### 8. **Phone** (text, optional)
Enter your phone number for contact purposes (not required but recommended).

Please ensure that you are accurate and honest when filling out this form. The information you provide will be used to evaluate your performance and help us improve our services. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
