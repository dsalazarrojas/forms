# Detailed Business Finance Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a business application form for businesses seeking financial assistance. It collects necessary information to assess and process your application efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details: Enter your name and your business name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your application date: Enter the date you are applying for financial assistance.
3. Confirm your business type: Indicate whether your business is active or inactive.
4. Select your industries: Choose the industries your business operates in.
5. Provide contact details: Enter your phone number and email address.
6. Enter your business address: Provide the address of your business.
7. Write a brief description: Describe your business in a few sentences.
8. Attach required documents: Upload any necessary documents for your application.
9. Confirm financial institution involvement: Indicate whether your business has a financial institution involved.
10. Confirm status of your application: Select the status of your application.
11. Confirm submission to authorities: Indicate whether your application has been submitted to authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **John Doe** (`user_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Business Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your business name.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are applying for financial assistance.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether your business is active or inactive.
* **Industries** (`industry`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the industries your business operates in.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): Enter a contact person for your business.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, optional): Provide the address of your business.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, `text`, required): Describe your business in a few sentences.
* **Financial Statement** (`financial_statement`, `note`, optional): Attach any necessary financial documents.
* **Uploaded Documents** (`uploaded_documents`, `select_multiple`, optional): Confirm whether you have uploaded required documents.
* **Uploaded Files** (`uploaded_files`, `select_multiple`, optional): Confirm whether you have uploaded files required for your application.
* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether your business has a financial institution involved.
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your application.
* **Submitted to** (`submitted_to`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether your application has been submitted to authorities.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date your application was submitted.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the department of the reviewer.
* **Designation** (`designation`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the designation of the reviewer.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm whether you were reviewed by the authority.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your review.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether your application was approved.
* **Submitted to** (`submitted_to`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether your application was submitted to authorities.
