# Automation Design Validation Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Automation Design Validation Program Registration form is a comprehensive form designed to gather information about participants' companies, projects, and preferences. It helps program administrators understand who the participants are, what kind of automation projects they are interested in, and what kind of support and resources they may need.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and answer all required questions truthfully.
2. Enter your company information, such as company name, address, and email.
3. Provide details about your project, including project name, description, budget, and timeline.
4. Choose the type of support you need from the given options.
5. Fill in any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your current job title or position.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's legal name.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, required): Enter your company's business address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where your company is located.
* **State** (`state`, `select_one`, required): Select your state from the given options.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your company's ZIP code.
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, required): Select your country from the given options.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, optional): Enter your company's website URL if applicable.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required): Select your company's size from the given options.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): Select your company's industry from the given options.
* **Program Information** (`program_info`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional details about the program.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of automation project you are interested in.
* **Project Stage** (`project_stage`, `select_one`, required): Select the current stage of your project.
* **Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Select the expected duration of your project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, `select_one`, optional): Select your estimated project budget.
* **Team Information** (`team_info`, `note`, optional): Enter any details about your team.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of team members.
* **Team Expertise Level** (`team_expertise`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of expertise of your team.
* **Additional Participants** (`additional_participants`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional participants' names.
* **Validation Requirements** (`validation_requirements`, `note`, optional): Enter any specific validation requirements for your project.
* **Standards Compliance** (`standards_compliance`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the relevant standards for your project.
* **Documentation Needs** (`documentation_needs`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the documentation needs for your project.
* **Support Level Needed** (`support_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of support you need for your project.
* **Previous Validation Experience** (`previous_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select your previous validation experience.
* **Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`, `text`, optional): Enter your learning objectives.
* **Certification Needed** (`certification_needed`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you need certification.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `note`, optional): Enter any payment information.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method for your registration.
* **Purchase Order Number** (`purchase_order_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your purchase order number if applicable.
* **Billing Contact** (`billing_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and email of the billing contact.
* **Billing Email** (`billing_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the billing contact's email.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): Enter any special requirements.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you heard about us.
* **Referral Name** (`referral_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the referral.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, `select_one`, required): Accept the terms and conditions of the registration.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required): Enter your digital signature.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting this registration.
Note: This guide assumes that all fields marked as `required` are mandatory fields that must be filled out before submission, while fields marked as `optional` can be skipped or left blank. Please refer to the form instructions for specific requirements and guidance on completion.
