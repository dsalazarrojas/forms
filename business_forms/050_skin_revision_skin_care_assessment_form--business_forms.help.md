# Skin Revision Skin Care Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us assess your skin care needs and provide you with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each page and ensure you have provided all necessary information.
3. Once you have completed the form, submit it for assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (customer_info)**: This is the first page of the form and is required. Please provide your basic details such as name, address, and contact information.
* **Medical History (medical_history)**: This page will gather information about any medical conditions or allergies you may have. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Skin Information (skin_info)**: This page will collect details about the skin condition or issue you are experiencing. Please provide as much information as you can.
* **Consent (consent)**: This page is where you will give your consent for the assessment. Please read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions (terms)**: This page outlines the terms and conditions of the skin revision skin care service. Please read and agree to these terms.
* **Additional Information (customer_info_2)**: This page is for any further information you would like to provide. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Medical History 2 (medical_history_2)**: This page will gather any further medical history information. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Skin Care (skin_info_2)**: This page will collect skin care related information. Please provide as much information as you can.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form in a quiet and private space, away from distractions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your form and be in touch with you soon to discuss your skin care assessment.
