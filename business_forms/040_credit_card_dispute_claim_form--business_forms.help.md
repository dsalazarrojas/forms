# Credit Card Dispute Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers who want to dispute a charge on their credit card.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your details in the "User Details" field. This includes your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Select the type of dispute from the "Dispute Type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your credit card details in the "Card Details" field.
4. Enter the date of the dispute in the "Date of Dispute" field.
5. Enter the amount disputed in the "Amount Disputed" field.
6. Provide a description of the dispute in the "Dispute Description" field.
7. If applicable, attach any supporting documents to the "Supporting Documents" field.
8. Finally, enter your contact information in the "Contact Us" and "Phone Number" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **User Details**
* `User Details` (text): Enter your name, phone number, and email address to contact you.
### **Dispute Type**
* `Dispute Type` (select_one):
	+ Option 1: Select the type of dispute you are experiencing (e.g. unauthorized charge, incorrect billing address, etc.)
	+ Option 2: Select another type of dispute (if not listed, select other)
### **Card Details**
* `Card Details` (text): Enter your credit card number, expiration date, and security code.
### **Date of Dispute**
* `Date of Dispute` (date): Enter the date you first noticed the dispute.
### **Amount Disputed**
* `Amount Disputed` (number): Enter the total amount disputed.
### **Dispute Description**
* `Dispute Description` (text): Provide a detailed description of the dispute.
### **Supporting Documents**
* `Supporting Documents` (text): Attach any supporting documents that may help resolve the dispute.
### **Contact Us**
* `Contact Us` (email):
	+ Enter your email address so we can reach out to you with updates.
### **Phone Number**
* `Phone Number` (text): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you with updates.
### **Email Address**
* `Email Address` (email): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you with updates.
### **Comments**
* `Comments` (text): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about your dispute.
