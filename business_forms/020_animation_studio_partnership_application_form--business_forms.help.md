# Animation Studio Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from animation studios or production companies that are interested in partnering with us. By filling out this form, you will help us understand your studio's background, expertise, and what type of partnership you are interested in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your company's information, including your studio's name, type, website, and years in business.
2. Provide your contact person's name, title, email, and phone number.
3. Describe your company's areas of expertise and what type of partnership you are interested in.
4. Share your portfolio link and any relevant information about your past projects.
5. Indicate your typical project scope and budget, as well as your team size and capacity.
6. Specify your key equipment and software.
7. Finally, describe your motivation for partnering with us and your studio's key strengths.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter your animation studio or production company name.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`): Select the type of studio you represent (e.g. Animation Studio, Production Company, etc.).
* **Company Website** (`company_website`): Enter your company's website URL.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`): Select how long your company has been operating.
* **Primary Contact Person** (`contact_person_name`): Enter the name of your main contact person.
* **Contact Person Title** (`contact_title`): Enter your contact person's title or role.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`): Enter your company's physical location.
* **Type of Partnership Sought** (`partnership_type`): Select all that apply for the type of partnership you are interested in (e.g. Co-Production, Technical Collaboration, etc.).
* **Your Studio's Main Areas of Expertise** (`expertise_areas`): Select all that apply for your studio's main areas of expertise (e.g. 2D Animation, 3D Animation, etc.).
* **Portfolio or Showreel Link** (`portfolio_link`): Enter a link to your work samples.
* **Types of Projects You Have Completed** (`project_types`): Select all that apply for the types of projects you have completed.
* **Typical Project Scope and Budget** (`typical_project_scope`): Select your usual project size.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`): Select the number of employees in your studio.
* **Available Project Capacity** (`available_capacity`): Select your available project capacity (e.g. full capacity, some capacity, etc.).
* **Key Equipment and Software** (`equipment_software`): Describe your key equipment and software.
* **Why are you interested in partnering** (`partnership_motivation`): Describe your reasons for partnering with us.
* **Key Strengths of Your Studio** (`strengths`): Describe what makes your studio valuable as a partner.

## Tips
Please fill out the form as thoroughly and accurately as possible. If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
