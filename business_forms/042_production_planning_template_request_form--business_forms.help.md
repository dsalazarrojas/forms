# Production Planning Template Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Production Planning Template Request Form is a tool used to request a custom template for production planning purposes. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the industry that best describes your production planning needs.
2. Enter the volume of production you are planning for.
3. Choose the features you require for your template.
4. Enter any additional comments or information.
5. Select the user or group assigned to work on your template.
6. Choose the project(s) your template will be used for.
7. Select whether or not you have been assigned a task.
8. Select the status of your template request.
9. Enter the date you would like the template to be assigned to you.
10. Enter any additional information you would like to provide about your template request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **industry (industry)**: Select the industry that best describes your production planning needs.
* **volume (volume)**: Enter the volume of production you are planning for. This could be a number or a range (e.g., 1-1000).
* **features (features)**: Choose the features you require for your template. This could include things like "Features", "Customization", "Integration", "Scalability", or "Security".
