# Supplier Pricing Structure Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help suppliers evaluate and improve their pricing structure. It assesses the overall pricing score, structure score, and evaluation score of the supplier's pricing structure. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of the pricing structure.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the supplier's name and pricing structure information on pages 1 and 2.
2. On page 3, evaluate the pricing structure and provide comments on pages 4 and 5.
3. On page 6, compare the pricing structure with others and evaluate it on page 7.
4. Provide overall comments and confirm completion on pages 8 and 9.
5. Review the overall scorecard on pages 10 and 11.
6. Add any additional comments and confirm completion on pages 12 and 13.
7. Finally, confirm completion and provide additional comments on pages 14 and 15.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier** (`supplier`): This field is for the supplier's name and contact information.
* **Supplier Pricings** (`pricing_structure_form_page_16`): Enter the supplier's current pricing structure.
* **Supplier Pricings** (`supplier_pricing`): Enter additional comments or feedback on the supplier's pricing.
* **Structure Score** (`structure_score`): Provide a score between 1-10 on the structure's organization and clarity.
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`): Provide a score between 1-10 on the structure's relevance and relevance to the industry.
* **Overall Score** (`overall_score`): Enter the overall score between 1-10 on the pricing structure.
* **Overall Scorecard** (`overall_pricing_scorecard`): Review the overall scorecard with the scores above.
* **Structure Form Page** (`structure_scorecard`): Add additional comments or feedback on the structure.
* **Overall Scorecard** (`structure_scorecard`): Review the structure scorecard.
* **Evaluation Scorecard** (`evaluation_scorecard`): Review the evaluation scorecard.
* **Final Comments** (`comments_text`): Enter additional comments or feedback on the structure.
* **Confirm Completion** (`structure_form_page_23`): Confirm that you have completed the form and provide additional comments if needed.
* **Final Comments** (`structure_form_page_24`): Enter final comments or feedback.
* **Confirm Completion** (`form_confirmation`): Confirm that you have completed the form.
* **Pricing Form** (`pricing_form`): Enter additional comments or feedback on the pricing structure.
* **Form Confirmation** (`form_confirmation`): Confirm that you have completed the form.

Note: Some fields may not be required and can be left blank if not applicable.
