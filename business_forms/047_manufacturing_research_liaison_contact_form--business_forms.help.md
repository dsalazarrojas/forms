# Manufacturing Research Liaison Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from manufacturing research liaisons to help us better understand their needs and provide relevant information and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the research areas you are interested in.
3. Provide any additional message or comments you have.
4. Choose the type of manufacturing process you are involved in.
5. Select the type of manufacturing equipment you use.
6. Provide any additional details about your manufacturing technology and process control.
7. If applicable, specify other manufacturing process control methods or equipment used.
8. Select the area of your manufacturing involvement.
9. If applicable, specify any other manufacturing process control methods or equipment used.
10. Finally, indicate if you would like us to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: Please enter your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Research Areas**: Select the areas of research that interest you, such as Research and Development, Quality and Compliance, or Product Development.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact at your organization.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person to contact at your organization.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person to contact at your organization.
* **Message**: Provide any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Department**: Enter the department name of your organization.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Company Size**: Select the size of your company, such as Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large.
* **Manufacturing Process**: Select the type of manufacturing process you are involved in, such as In-house, External, Hybrid, or Other.
* **Manufacturing Equipment**: Select the type of manufacturing equipment you use, such as High-Tech, Low-Tech, Hybrid, or Other.
* **Manufacturing Technology**: Enter any additional details about your manufacturing technology.
* **Manufacturing Process Control**: Select the type of manufacturing process control you use, such as In-process, Off-line, Hybrid, or Other.
* **Other Manufacturing Process Control**: If you selected "Other", specify the type of process control you use.
* **Other Manufacturing Equipment**: If you selected "Other", specify the type of equipment you use.
* **Manufacturing Area of Involvement**: Select the area of your manufacturing involvement, such as Production, Quality, Process Development, or Other.
* **Manufacturing Process Control Other 2**: If you selected "Other", specify the type of process control you use.
* **Follow-up**: Indicate if you would like us to follow up with you.
