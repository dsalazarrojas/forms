# Student Enrollment & Release Form: Littlest Learners - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Enrollment & Release Form is designed to collect and manage student enrollment information for the Littlest Learners program. This form captures essential details about students, their parents, and program specifics to ensure efficient and accurate record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name.
2. Provide the student's email address.
3. Enter the student's phone number.
4. Select the enrollment date.
5. Enter the program name.
6. Provide the parent's name.
7. Enter the parent's email address.
8. Enter the parent's phone number.
9. Add any additional notes about the student's enrollment.
10. Choose the program type (e.g., Sprouts, Camp).
11. Select whether other programs are available.
12. Enter the time of enrollment.
13. Choose the time of day.
14. Add any other notes about the student.
15. (Optional) Enter the student's ID (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Enrollment Form**: Enter the student's name in this field.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification or as their parent prefers it to be displayed.
* **Student Email**: Provide the student's email address for communication purposes.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number for communication and contact.
* **Enrollment Date**: Select the date the student is enrolled in the program.
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program the student is enrolled in (e.g., Sprouts, Camp).
* **Parent Name**: Enter the parent's name associated with the student.
* **Parent Email**: Provide the parent's email address for communication purposes.
* **Parent Phone**: Enter the parent's phone number for communication and contact.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the student's enrollment.
* **Program**: Choose the type of program the student is enrolled in (e.g., Sprouts, Camp).
* **Other**: Select if other programs are available.
* **Time**: Enter the time of enrollment.
* **Time of Day**: Choose the time of day of the student's enrollment.
* **Other Note**: Add any other notes about the student.
* **Student ID**: (Optional) Enter the student's ID (if applicable).
