# Digital Twin Creation Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers about their digital twin quotation requirements. Fill out this form to provide details about your project, payment method, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by providing the required information in each field.
2. Ensure that all required fields (marked as required in the form) are filled out.
3. Select the appropriate payment gateway from the options provided (Stripe, PayPal, or Square).
4. Be as clear and accurate as possible when filling out each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Digital Twin Creation Quotation Form** (`digital_twin_creation_quotation_form`, text, false): A brief description of your project, explaining the purpose of the digital twin creation.
* **Quotation Details** (`quotation_details`, text, false): Provide a detailed description of your quotation requirements, including any specific needs or constraints.
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, select_one, false): Choose the payment method you would like to use for this quotation (Stripe, PayPal, or Square).
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, text, false): Describe the service you require the digital twin for (e.g. data analytics, simulation, etc.).
* **Service Cost** (`service_cost`, number, false): Enter the cost for the service you require.
* **Service Duration** (`service_duration`, time, false): Enter the duration of the service you require (e.g. days, weeks, months, etc.).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, false): Enter your name as the customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, false): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, false): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, false): Enter your address.

Note: If any field is marked as required, please ensure it is filled out correctly to avoid any issues with submitting the form.
