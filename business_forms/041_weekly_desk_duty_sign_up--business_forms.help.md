# Weekly Desk Duty Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to sign up for desk duty, a weekly rotation of tasks and responsibilities among team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Base Information**: This field is used to collect your basic contact information. (text, required: false)
* **Contact Details**: This field is used to collect your email and phone number. (text, required: true)
* **Preferred Weeks**: This field allows you to select the weeks you are available for desk duty. (select_multiple, required: true)
* **Time Slots**: This field allows you to select your time slots for desk duty. (select_multiple, required: true)
* **Notes**: This field is optional and allows you to add any additional comments or information. (note, required: false)
* **Submitted By**: This field indicates that you are submitting this form, and is used for tracking purposes. (select_one, required: true)
* **Submitted At**: This field records the date and time you submitted this form. (date, required: true)
* **Assigned To**: This field indicates whether you are assigned to perform desk duty for specific tools, users, or teams. (select_one, required: true)
* **Assigned At**: This field records the date and time you are assigned to perform desk duty. (date, required: false)
* **Assigned Tool**: This field indicates whether you are assigned to use specific tools for desk duty. (select_one, required: true)
* **Assigned User**: This field indicates whether you are assigned to work with specific users for desk duty. (select_one, required: true)
* **Assigned Group**: This field indicates whether you are assigned to a specific group for desk duty. (select_one, required: true)
* **Assigned Team**: This field indicates whether you are assigned to a specific team for desk duty. (select_one, required: true)
* **Assigned Department**: This field indicates whether you are assigned to a specific department for desk duty. (select_one, required: true)
* **Assigned Location**: This field indicates whether you are assigned to a specific location for desk duty. (select_one, required: true)
* **Submitted By Name**: This field is used to track the name of the user who submitted this form. (text, required: false)
* **Submitted IP**: This field is used to track the IP address of the user who submitted this form. (text, required: false)
* **Submitted User**: This field is used to track the username of the user who submitted this form. (text, required: false)
* **Submitted User ID**: This field is used to track the ID of the user who submitted this form. (text, required: false)
* **Submitted User Type**: This field is used to track the type of user who submitted this form. (text, required: false)
* **Submitted User Group**: This field is used to track the group of the user who submitted this form. (text, required: false)
* **Assigned Date**: This field records the date and time you are assigned to perform desk duty. (date, required: false)

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled in, while the fields marked as optional can be left empty if not applicable.
