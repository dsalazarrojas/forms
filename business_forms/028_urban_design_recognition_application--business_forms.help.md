# Urban Design Recognition Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Urban Design Recognition Application is a form used to collect information about urban design projects, including project details, descriptions, locations, and other relevant data. This form helps to identify and record urban design projects that showcase excellence and innovation in urban design.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project details in the "Project Details" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the project in the "Description" field.
3. Enter the location of the project in the "Location" field.
4. Upload any supporting files related to the project in the "Supporting Files" field.
5. Add an image of the project in the "Project Image" field.
6. Add a video related to the project in the "Project Video" field.
7. Upload a PDF related to the project in the "Project PDF" field.
8. Enter the URL of the project in the "Project URL" field.
9. Select the submission type (Award or Showcase) in the "Submission Type" field.
10. Enter a unique submission ID in the "Submission ID" field.
11. Enter the submission date in the "Submission Date" field.
12. Enter the submission time in the "Submission Time" field.
13. Enter the submission date range in the "Submission Date Range" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Details** (id: 1, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief summary of the project.
- **Description** (id: 2, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the project.
- **Location** (id: 3, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where the project was executed.
- **Supporting Files** (id: 4, `text`, required: false): Upload any files related to the project, such as reports, images, or videos.
- **Project Image** (id: 5, `text`, required: false): Add an image of the project.
- **Project Video** (id: 6, `text`, required: false): Add a video related to the project.
- **Project PDF** (id: 7, `text`, required: false): Upload a PDF related to the project.
- **Project URL** (id: 8, `text`, required: false): Enter the URL of the project.
- **Submission Type** (id: 9, `select_one`, required: false): Select the submission type (Award or Showcase).
- **Submission ID** (id: 10, `text`, required: false): Enter a unique submission ID.
- **Submission Date** (id: 11, `date`, required: false): Enter the submission date.
- **Submission Time** (id: 12, `time`, required: false): Enter the submission time.
- **Submission Date Range** (id: 13, `text`, required: false): Enter the submission date range.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
* Upload relevant files and images to support your submission.
* Use a unique submission ID to track your submission.
* Enter accurate submission dates and times.
* Review your submission before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
