# Business Profit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Profit Survey is designed to collect valuable insights into your business's performance, financials, and growth prospects. This information helps us better understand your business's strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have a clear understanding of the form's purpose and scope.
2. Verify that your business fits the criteria for filling out this form.
3. Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
4. Double-check your input for completeness and consistency.
5. Submit the form when you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name (2)**: Enter your company's name. This is a required field.
* **Business Owner (3)**: Enter the name of the person in charge of the business. This field is required.
* **Type of Business (4)**: Describe the type of business you operate. Examples include retail, services, manufacturing, etc.
* **Years in Operation (5)**: Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Annual Revenue (7)**: Report your business's last year's total revenue.
* **Annual Expenses (8)**: Enter your total operating expenses for the past year.
* **Annual Profit (9)**: If you have reported revenue and expenses, you can enter your net profit after expenses. This field is required only if you report revenue and expenses.
* **Profit Margin (10)**: If you have reported net profit, calculate and enter your profit margin (profit divided by revenue, expressed as a percentage). This field is optional.
* **Primary Revenue Source (12)**: Identify where most of your business's income comes from. Examples include online sales, offline sales, services, etc.
* **Revenue Stream Distribution (13)**: Approximate the distribution of your revenue streams by percentages. For example, "Online sales: 50%, Offline sales: 30%, Services: 20%."
* **Top 3 Business Expenses (15)**: Identify your business's three biggest expense categories. Examples include employee salaries, raw materials, rent, etc.
* **Employee Costs as Percentage of Revenue (16)**: Calculate and enter the percentage of revenue that goes to employee costs.
* **Profit Trend (18)**: Select whether your profit is significantly decreasing, decreasing, stable, increasing, or significantly increasing.
* **Annual Growth Rate (19)**: Estimate the annual growth rate of your business, expressed as a percentage.
* **Main Profit Drivers (21)**: Identify the factors that contribute to your business's profitability.
* **Profitability Challenges (22)**: Identify challenges that hinder your business's profits.
* **Opportunities for Improvement (23)**: Suggest ways to increase your business's profits.
