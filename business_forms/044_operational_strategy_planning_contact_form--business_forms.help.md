# Operational Strategy Planning Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather contact information and operational strategy planning details from users. It is used to collect relevant data for business planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name by typing in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select your organization from the "organization_name" field.
6. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
7. Enter your address in the "address" field.
8. Type in a message in the "message" field.
9. You may attach up to 4 files, including "file", "file2", "attachment3", and "attachment4", as necessary.
10. If you have any additional comments, type them in the "note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <first_name> (Text, Required)
* First name of the person completing the form.

### <last_name> (Text, Required)
* Last name of the person completing the form.

### <email> (Email, Required)
* A valid email address of the person completing the form.

### <phone> (Text, Optional)
* Phone number of the person completing the form.

### <organization_name> (Select One, Required)
* Name of the organization that the person completing the form represents.

### <contact_number> (Number, Optional)
* Contact number of the person completing the form.

### <address> (Text, Optional)
* Physical address of the person completing the form.

### <message> (Text, Required)
* Brief message or comments about the operational strategy planning.

### <file> (Note, Optional)
* File to be attached as necessary.

### <file2> (Note, Optional)
* Additional file to be attached as necessary.

### <attachment3> (Note, Optional)
* Third file to be attached as necessary.

### <attachment4> (Note, Optional)
* Fourth file to be attached as necessary.

### <date> (Date, Optional)
* Date of the operational strategy planning meeting.

### <time> (Time, Optional)
* Time of the operational strategy planning meeting.

### <additional_comments> (Note, Optional)
* Additional comments or information about the operational strategy planning.

### <email2> (Email, Optional)
* A second email address for follow-up.

### <phone2> (Text, Optional)
* A second phone number for follow-up.

### <organization2> (Select One, Optional)
* Name of the second organization that the person completing the form represents.

### <contact2> (Text, Optional)
* Second contact number of the person completing the form.

### <Address2> (Text, Optional)
* Second physical address of the person completing the form.

### <Message2> (Text, Optional)
* Second brief message or comments about the operational strategy planning.

### <attachment2> (Note, Optional)
* Second file to be attached as necessary.

### <attachment3 (2)> (Note, Optional)
* Third file to be attached as necessary.

### <attachment4> (Note, Optional)
* Fourth file to be attached as necessary.

### <attachment5> (Note, Optional)
* Fifth file to be attached as necessary.
