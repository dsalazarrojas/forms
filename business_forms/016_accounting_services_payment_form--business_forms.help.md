# Accounting Services Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accounting Services Payment Form is used for clients to make payments for various accounting services provided by the firm. This form is essential for processing payments securely and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Client Name: Type in the name of the individual or company paying for the services.
2. Enter the Invoice Number: Type in the invoice number sent by our firm.
3. Provide the Billing Email: Enter the email address where you want to receive the payment receipt.
4. Select the Payment For: Choose the service category for which you are making the payment.
5. Enter the Payment Amount: Type in the amount in USD.
6. Select the Payment Method: Choose how you would like to make the payment (Credit Card, ACH Transfer, PayPal, or Bank Wire).
7. Provide the Payment Date: Enter the date of the payment.
8. (Optional) Enter Internal Reference Code: Type in any internal reference code if required by your organization.
9. Choose Receipt Preference: Decide how you want to receive the payment receipt (via Email, Mailed Hardcopy, or No Receipt Needed).
10. Review Security Note: This section informs you that payments are processed securely and no full credit card numbers are stored on our servers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, required): Enter the name of the individual or company paying for the services.
* **Invoice Number** (`invoice_number`, required): Type in the invoice number sent by our firm.
* **Billing Email** (`billing_email`, required): Enter the email address where the receipt should be sent.
* **Payment For** (`payment_for`, required): Select the service category for which you are making the payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, required): Enter the amount in USD.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, required): Choose how you would like to make the payment (Credit Card, ACH Transfer, PayPal, or Bank Wire).
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, required): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Internal Reference Code** (`internal_reference_code`, optional): Type in any internal reference code if required by your organization.
* **Receipt Preference** (`receipt_preference`, required): Decide how you want to receive the payment receipt (via Email, Mailed Hardcopy, or No Receipt Needed).
* **Security Note** (`security_note`, not required): This section informs you that payments are processed securely and no full credit card numbers are stored on our servers.

## Tips
- Please ensure the Client Name matches the invoice name to avoid any errors.
- Make sure to choose the correct Payment Method to ensure smooth processing.
- If you have any issues, feel free to contact us.
