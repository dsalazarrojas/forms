# Simple Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from customers or internal users for simple supply orders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form title to briefly describe what this order is for.
2. Select a relevant category to help us better understand what you need.
3. Provide a short description of what you are ordering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Simple Supply Order Form** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title for this order.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required: false): Please select a category that best describes what you are ordering. For example, "Office Supplies", "IT Equipment", etc.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a short description of what you are ordering, including quantities or specific details.

## Tips
* Be as clear and concise as possible when filling in the form fields.
* If you need any help or clarification, feel free to contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
