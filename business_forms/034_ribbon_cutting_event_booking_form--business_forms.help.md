# ribbon-cutting-event-booking-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from attendees about the Ribbon Cutting event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event details in the first field, which includes any relevant information about the event.
2. Provide your contact information in the second field, which is necessary for us to reach out to you with event updates or information.
3. Select the time you plan to attend the event in the third field. Please choose a time that works for you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Details (text):** Enter the name and description of the event. This will help us know what to expect and provide relevant information.
- **Contact Info (text):** Enter your name and contact information so we can reach out to you about the event. Please include your email address and phone number if you'd like us to contact you directly.
- **Time (time):** Choose the time you plan to attend the event. Please select a time that suits you best.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately to ensure we can contact you and provide you with the right information about the event.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
