# Printing Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request printing services from the business. It collects information about the business, the printing service required, and the delivery details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form instructions and make sure you understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
2. Select the 'Yes' or 'No' option for 'business_type' to indicate whether the requested service is a one-time event or a recurring service.
3. Enter the name of the specific printing service required in the 'service_name' text field.
4. If the service is a 'Yes' for 'business_type', select the correct 'service_type' option (e.g., color printing, black and white printing, etc.).
5. Enter the delivery date and time in the 'delivery_date' and 'delivery_time' fields.
6. Enter the business address in the 'business_address' text field.
7. Enter the contact person's name and any additional contact information in the 'contact_person' text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_id** (1, number, required): A unique identifier for the form. Enter a number to identify the form.
* **business_type** (2, select_multiple, optional): A checkbox to indicate whether the requested service is a one-time event or a recurring service.
* **service_name** (3, text, optional): A text field to enter the specific type of printing service required.
* **service_type** (4, select_one, optional): A dropdown menu to select the specific service type (e.g., color printing, black and white printing, etc.).
* **delivery_date** (5, date, optional): A date field to enter the delivery date.
* **delivery_time** (6, time, optional): A time field to enter the delivery time.
* **business_address** (7, text, optional): A text field to enter the business address.
* **contact_person** (8, text, optional): A text field to enter the contact person's name and any additional contact information.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure prompt and successful delivery of the printing service.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the service provider for clarification or guidance.
* This form is intended for businesses that need printing services. If you have any other questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
