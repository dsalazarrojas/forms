# freelance_editor_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The freelance editor agreement form is used to document the terms of the working relationship between a freelancer and their client. It outlines the project details, payment terms, and other essential agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the terms and conditions of the agreement.
2. Ensure that you have discussed and agreed upon these terms with your client.
3. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully, including your name and any other required information.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Editor Agreement Form** (`editor_agreement_form`, text, optional): This field is a free-form text field where you can describe the terms of the agreement between you and your client. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Editor Agreement Terms** (`editor_agreement_terms`, text, optional): In this field, describe the terms of the agreement in your own words. This field is not intended to be a legal document, but rather a summary of your understanding of the agreement.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more options from the dropdown list to indicate the project details. This field is used to indicate whether the project is ongoing or completed, and whether you have been paid or not.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount for the project. Note that this field is not intended to be used for actual payment, but rather as a record of the agreed-upon payment terms.
* **Editor Agreement Signature** (`editor_agreement_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name in this field to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms of the agreement.
