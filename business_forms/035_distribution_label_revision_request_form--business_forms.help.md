# Distribution Label Revision Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distribution Label Revision Request Form is used to collect information for revising distribution labels. Fill out this form to ensure that all necessary data is gathered for an accurate and organized label revision process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read and understand the form's purpose and instructions.
- Fill out all required fields with accurate information.
- Ensure that each field is completed correctly as per its purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. manufacturer_name
* **Manufacturer Name**: Enter the name of the product's manufacturer to identify the product's origin.

### 2. distributor_name
* **Distributor Name**: Enter the name of the distributor that handles the product to keep track of the product's distribution.

### 3. product_name
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product for which the label is being revised.

### 4. product_description
* **Product Description**: Provide a brief description of the product to ensure accurate identification.

### 5. change_description
* **Change Description**: Explain the reason for the label change to keep track of revisions accurately.

### 6. new_label_version
* **New Label Version**: Enter the new version of the distribution label, if applicable.

### 7. files
* **Files**: Choose "Yes" if you are attaching files related to the product or label change. Choose "No" if you are not attaching files.

### 8. reason_for_change
* **Reason for Change**: Indicate whether you are making the change for "Yes" (yes, it's a change) or "No" (it's not a change).

### 9. date_of_last_revision
* **Date of Last Revision**: Enter the date of the last distribution label revision, if applicable.

### 10. date_of_next_revision
* **Date of Next Revision**: Enter the date of the next planned distribution label revision, if applicable.

## Tips
- Double-check each field for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- Review the form's purpose and instructions to guarantee that all necessary information is collected accurately.
