# fleet_maintenance_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture essential information for checking out a vehicle from a fleet maintenance process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the vehicle's unique identifier (Vehicle ID) and its Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
2. Input the vehicle's current mileage.
3. Provide the contact email and phone number of the owner or authorized personnel.
4. Enter the vehicle's location address, city, state, and zip code.
5. Select the current condition of the vehicle.
6. Choose the status of the maintenance tasks.
7. Input the name of the technician who performed the maintenance tasks.
8. Confirm that the technician has reviewed and signed off the maintenance tasks (if applicable).
9. Add any additional comments or notes about the maintenance process.
10. Input the date by which the maintenance tasks are due.
11. Input the date when the maintenance tasks were actually completed.
12. Enter any additional notes about the service process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Id** (`Vehicle ID`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the unique identifier for the vehicle.
* **VIN** (`VIN`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the 17-character Vehicle Identification Number.
* **Mileage** (`Mileage`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the contact email of the owner or authorized personnel.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number of the owner or authorized personnel.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address where the vehicle is located.
* **City** (`City`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city where the vehicle is located.
* **State** (`State`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the state where the vehicle is located.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the vehicle's location.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`Vehicle Condition`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the current condition of the vehicle from "Good", "Bad", or "Fair".
* **Maintenance Status** (`Maintenance Status`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the status of the maintenance tasks from "Completed", "Pending", or "Cancelled".
* **Technician Name** (`Technician Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the technician who performed the maintenance tasks.
* **Technician Signature** (`Technician Signature`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm that the technician has reviewed and signed off the maintenance tasks.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the maintenance process.
* **Due Date** (`Due Date`, `date`, required/optional): Input the date by which the maintenance tasks are due.
* **Actual Completion Date** (`Actual Completion Date`, `date`, required/optional): Input the date when the maintenance tasks were actually completed.
* **Vehicle Number** (`Vehicle Number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's number.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`Maintenance Frequency`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the frequency of the maintenance tasks.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the maintenance process.
* **Service Notes** (`Service Notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter additional notes about the service process.
