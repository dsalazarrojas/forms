# operational_clarity_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The operational clarity assessment form is used to gather feedback and insights on operational processes within an organization, to identify areas that need improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance operational clarity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question as thoroughly and honestly as possible.
2. For text fields, describe your thoughts and ideas in a few sentences.
3. For select_multiple fields, choose all the options that apply.
4. For select_one fields, choose only one option that best represents your answer.
5. If you're unsure about any question or need clarification, please ask your supervisor or team lead for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Goals**: Describe your current goals and objectives. What are you trying to achieve, and what would you like to accomplish in your role?
- **Roles Responsible**: Identify all the roles responsible for your current goals and objectives. This could include your supervisor, colleagues, or external partners. (Required)
- **Communication Channels**: Describe the channels you use for communication, such as email, phone, or chat. (Optional)
- **Resource Allocation**: Identify the resources you need to achieve your goals, such as time, money, or equipment. (Optional)
- **Operational Gaps**: Describe any obstacles or areas where you experience difficulties in completing your tasks or achieving your goals. (Optional)
- **Clarity Recommendations**: Share any ideas or suggestions for improving operational clarity in your team or department. Describe how these recommendations can benefit the organization. (Optional)
- **Employee Resources**: Identify any resources you need to achieve your goals, such as training, coaching, or mentorship. (Optional)
- **Management Support**: Identify any support you receive from management, such as training, coaching, or mentorship. Choose all that apply. (Optional)
- **Collaboration Tools**: Describe the collaboration tools you use, such as Slack, Trello, or Asana. (Optional)
- **Performance Metrics**: Identify any performance metrics or indicators that measure the success of your work, such as sales or customer satisfaction. (Optional)
