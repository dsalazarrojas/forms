# Fleet Management Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fleet Management Assessment Form is used to evaluate the performance and efficiency of a fleet. It helps assess the performance of a fleet manager, vehicle performance, vehicle maintenance, and driver efficiency, providing valuable insights to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Fleet Manager" field and enter the name or title of the fleet manager in charge of the fleet.
2. In the "Vehicle Performance" field, enter a numerical value indicating the overall performance of the vehicles in the fleet, on a scale of 1 to 100.
3. In the "Vehicle Maintenance" field, enter the maintenance schedule for the vehicles, including details such as frequency and type of maintenance.
4. In the "Driver Efficiency" field, enter the efficiency of the drivers in delivering goods or services, including any relevant statistics or metrics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fleet Manager**: Enter the name or title of the person responsible for managing the fleet. This field is optional, but it's helpful for keeping track of who is in charge.

* **Vehicle Performance**: Enter a numerical value from 1 to 100 to rate the overall performance of the vehicles in the fleet. For example, if the vehicles are running smoothly and efficiently, you can enter a high value (e.g., 90). If the vehicles are experiencing frequent issues, you can enter a lower value (e.g., 20).

* **Vehicle Maintenance**: Enter the maintenance schedule for the vehicles, including how often they are serviced and what type of maintenance is performed. For example, "Every 10,000 miles" or "Monthly oil checks".

* **Driver Efficiency**: Enter the efficiency of the drivers in delivering goods or services, including any relevant statistics or metrics. This field is for you to enter any relevant data that showcases the drivers' performance.
