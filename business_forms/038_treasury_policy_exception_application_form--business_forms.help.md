# Treasury Policy Exception Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Policy Exception Application Form is used to request exceptions to the company's Treasury Policy. Employees can fill out this form to seek permission to deviate from standard procedures for specific business needs or circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "form_data" field with a brief description of the exception request.
2. Enter your user information in the "User Data" field.
3. Provide a justification for the exception in the "justification" field.
4. List any risks associated with the exception in the "Risks" field.
5. Describe your mitigation plans to address these risks in the "Mitigation Plans" field.
6. Attach any supporting documents that are relevant to your exception request in the "Supporting Documents" field.
7. Enter any additional user-specific data in the "User Input Data" field.
8. Identify any risks that are specific to the user in the "User Input Risks" field.
9. Enter the exception amount in the "Exception Amount" field.
10. Select the date of the exception in the "Exception Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_data**: A brief description of the exception request. Please provide a clear and concise explanation of why the exception is needed.
* **User Data**: This field is for entering user information. Please provide your employee ID, name, and any other relevant contact information.
* **justification**: Provide a detailed explanation of why the exception is necessary. Be sure to clearly state the purpose and benefits of the exception.
* **Risks**: List any risks associated with granting the exception. This could include potential financial losses, reputational damage, or other negative consequences.
* **Mitigation Plans**: Describe the steps you will take to address the risks associated with the exception. This could include contingency plans, risk management strategies, or other mitigating actions.
* **Supporting Documents**: Attach any relevant documents that support your exception request. This could include financial reports, market research, or other evidence that supports your request.
* **User Input Data**: Enter any additional user-specific data that is relevant to your exception request. This could include personal financial information, account numbers, or other relevant details.
* **User Input Risks**: Identify any risks that are specific to the user. This could include personal risks, such as financial instability or other relevant concerns.
* **Exception Amount**: Enter the amount of the exception being requested.
* **Exception Date**: Select the date on which the exception is being requested.

## Tips
* Please only submit one exception request per form.
* Be clear and concise in your descriptions and explanations.
* Make sure to attach all relevant supporting documents.
* Be prepared to discuss your exception request and address any questions or concerns that may arise.
