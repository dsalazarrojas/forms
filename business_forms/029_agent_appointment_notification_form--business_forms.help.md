# Agent Appointment Notification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create and manage notifications for clients about upcoming appointments. It's an essential part of our agency's process to keep clients informed and organized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the client's information**: Fill in the client's name and email address to ensure they receive the notification.
2. **Schedule the meeting**: Select the date and time of the meeting, including the format (in-person, phone, or video call) and location.
3. **Provide necessary documents**: If required, select the documents the client needs to bring to the meeting.
4. **Confirm attendance**: Choose whether you, as the agent, will be attending the meeting.
5. **Submit the form**: Once you've filled out all the required fields, click submit to create the notification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agent Name**: Please enter your name as the agent who will be attending the meeting.
* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name who will be attending the meeting.
* **Client Email Address**: Enter the client's email address where they can receive the notification.
* **Client Phone Number**: Enter the client's phone number where they can receive reminders or confirmations.
* **Scheduled Date**: Select the date of the meeting.
* **Scheduled Time**: Select the time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Format**: Choose the format of the meeting (in-person, phone, or video call).
* **Meeting Location**: Enter the location of the meeting, including the address or a link to an online platform.
* **Purpose of the Appointment**: Briefly describe the purpose of the meeting.
* **Documents for Client to Prepare**: If necessary, select the documents the client needs to bring to the meeting.
* **Confirm Attendance**: Choose whether you will be attending the meeting.

Note: This form is designed to be easy to use and understand. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please refer to your agency's guidelines or consult with your supervisor for clarification.
