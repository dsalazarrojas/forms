# Warehouse Safety License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Safety License Application Form is a document that enables businesses or organizations to apply for a warehouse safety license. This form is designed to collect necessary information about the facility, its safety status, and other related aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the General Information section, which includes fields for the facility's address and other basic contact information.
2. In the Safety Details section, provide information about the safety features and procedures in place at your facility.
3. In the Emergency Contacts section, list the names, phone numbers, and email addresses of the emergency contact persons.
4. In the Declaration section, make a declaration about the facility's safety status.
5. In the Declaration Confirmation section, confirm the declaration made in the previous step.
6. Select which types of information you want to include in the Facility Details section, such as facility address and safety features.
7. In the Facility Type section, select whether the facility is a warehouse, office, or other.
8. In the License Information section, provide information about the facility's license number, status, and expiry date.
9. Finally, in the License Status and License Expiry Date sections, confirm the facility's license status and expiry date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (section 1, text): Provide the facility's address and other basic contact information.
* **Safety Details** (section 2, text): Describe the safety features and procedures in place at your facility.
* **Emergency Contacts** (section 3, text): List the names, phone numbers, and email addresses of the emergency contact persons.
* **Declaration** (section 4, text): Make a declaration about the facility's safety status. This is a critical section that ensures you understand the implications of your declaration.
* **Declaration Confirmation** (section 5, select_one): Confirm the declaration made in the previous step.
* **Section 6** (section 6, select_multiple): Select which types of information you want to include in the Facility Details section.
* **Facility Details** (section 7, text): Provide information about the facility's address and safety features.
* **Section 8** (section 8, text): This section is currently empty and can be ignored.
* **Section 9** (section 9, text): This section is currently empty and can be ignored.
* **Facility Type** (section 10, select_one): Select whether the facility is a warehouse, office, or other.
* **License Information** (section 11, text): Provide information about the facility's license number, status, and expiry date.
* **Section 12** (section 12, text): This section is currently empty and can be ignored.
* **License Status** (section 13, select_one): Confirm the facility's license status as Active or Inactive.
* **License Expiry Date** (section 14, date): Provide the expiry date of the facility's license.
* **Facility Address** (section 15, text): Provide the address of the facility.
