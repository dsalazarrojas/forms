# Procurement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Procurement Request Form is a tool used to capture user information and request data for procurement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form completely, providing as much information as possible about your request.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and relevant information.
- If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the form administrator for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your official email address.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department you belong to.
* **Procurement Type** (`procurement_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable procurement types that apply to your request.
* **Procurement Justification** (`procurement_justification`, text, required/optional): Provide a clear explanation for your request, including why you need the resource.
* **Requested Date** (`requested_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you expect to use the resource.
* **Requested Time** (`requested_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of day when you expect to use the resource.
* **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your request.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, select_multiple, optional): If you have attached any files or documents, select the relevant options.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select if you are assigned a tool for the procurement.
* **Business Forms** (`business_forms`, select_one, optional): Select if the procurement is for a business form.
* **Procurement Requests** (`procurement_requests`, select_one, optional): Select if the procurement is a request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your request.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required): Enter a unique identifier for the form submission, which will be provided by the form administrator.
