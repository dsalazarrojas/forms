# Request Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request Approval Form is used to create a new request for approval. This form is intended for internal use within your organization, and the data collected will be used to track and manage requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Request Approval Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your request in the "Request Title" field.
2. Select the category of your request from the "Category" dropdown list.
3. Enter a detailed description of your request in the "Description" field.
4. Select the tool assigned to your request from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown list.
5. Enter the date and time of your request in the "Request Date" and "Request Time" fields, respectively.
6. Enter a brief description of your request in the "Request Description" field.
7. Select the team responsible for your request from the "Assigned Team" dropdown list.
8. Select the status of your request from the "Status" dropdown list.
9. Enter any comments or notes about your request in the "Comments" field.
10. Enter the name of the person who approved your request in the "Approved By" field.
11. Select the date and time of approval in the "Approval Date" and "Approval Time" fields, respectively.
12. Enter a reason for rejecting your request in the "Rejected Reason" field, if applicable.
13. Select the request status from the "Request Status" dropdown list.
14. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
15. Select whether your request is in the "Form Category" dropdown list.
16. Select whether a user is assigned to your request from the "Assigned User" dropdown list.
17. Enter the ID of the request form in the "Request Form ID" field.
18. Enter a title for your request form in the "Request Form Title" field.
19. Enter a description of your request form in the "Request Form Description" field.
20. Enter the date and time when the request was submitted in the "Submitted At" field.
21. Enter the name of the person who submitted the request in the "Submitted By" field.
22. Enter the person or team who submitted the request to in the "Submitted To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Title** (`form_title`, text, required): A brief and descriptive title for your request.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select a category for your request from the dropdown list.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select a tool assigned to your request from the dropdown list.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your request.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your request.
* **Request Description** (`request_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Assigned Team** (`assigned_team`, select_multiple, optional): Select the team responsible for your request from the dropdown list.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your request from the dropdown list.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes about your request.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who approved your request.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of approval.
* **Approval Time** (`approval_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of approval.
* **Rejected Reason** (`rejected_reason`, text, optional): Enter a reason for rejecting your request, if applicable.
* **Request Status** (`request_status`, select_one, optional): Select the request status from the dropdown list.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional): Select whether your request is in a category from the dropdown list.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, optional): Select whether a user is assigned to your request from the dropdown list.
* **Request Form ID** (`request_form_id`, text, required): Enter a unique ID for your request form.
* **Request Form Title** (`request_form_title`, text, required): Enter a title for your request form.
* **Request Form Description** (`request_form_description`, text, required): Enter a description for your request form.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, required): Enter the date and time when the request was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the request.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, text, required): Enter the person or team who submitted the request to.
