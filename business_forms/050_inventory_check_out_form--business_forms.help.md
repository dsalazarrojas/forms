# Inventory Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Check Out Form is designed to help you track items checked out from inventory. This form captures essential details such as description, quantity, unit price, date, and other relevant information to ensure accurate inventory management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Inventory Check Out Form, follow these steps:

1.  **Fill in the form title**: Type a brief description of the item being checked out in the "Inventory Check Out Form" field.
2.  **Provide a description**: Enter a detailed description of the item being checked out in the "Description" field.
3.  **Select prophets**: Choose the "Prophets" field to select one or multiple prophets who will be handling the item (if applicable).
4.  **Enter quantity and unit price**: Enter the quantity of the item being checked out and its unit price in the "Quantity" and "Unit Price" fields, respectively.
5.  **Set dates and times**: Choose the "Date Out" and "Return Date" fields to specify the dates when the item is being checked out and expected return date, and set the "Return Time" field to indicate the time when the item should be returned.
6.  **Add contact information**: Enter your phone number and email address in the "Phone" and "Email" fields for contact purposes.
7.  **Add notes**: Use the "Notes" field to add any additional comments or details about the item being checked out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inventory Check Out Form** (1, text, required): Enter a brief title of the form, which will be used as a reference for the checked-out item.
* **Description** (2, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the item being checked out.
* **Prophets** (3, select multiple, optional): Select one or multiple prophets who will be handling the item, if applicable.
* **Quantity** (4, number, required): Enter the quantity of the item being checked out.
* **Unit Price** (5, number, required): Enter the unit price of the item being checked out.
* **Date Out** (6, date, optional): Choose the date when the item is being checked out.
* **Return Date** (7, date, optional): Choose the expected return date of the item.
* **Return Time** (8, time, optional): Specify the return time of the item.
* **Phone** (9, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (10, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Notes** (11, note, optional): Add any additional comments or details about the item being checked out.
