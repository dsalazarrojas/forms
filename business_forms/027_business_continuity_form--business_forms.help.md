# Business Continuity Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Business Continuity Form is designed to help us understand your organization's ability to maintain and recover from disruptions or crises that may impact your operations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions in this form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Use the drop-down menus to select the correct options for each question.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, required): Enter the full legal name of your organization.
* **Organization Size** (`organization_size`, required): Enter the number of employees in your organization.
* **Primary Business Function** (`primary_function`, required): Describe what your organization does.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, required): Enter the date of this continuity assessment.
* **Have Critical Functions Been Identified** (`function_identification`, required): Select "True" if critical functions have been identified, "False" if not.
* **List Critical Functions** (`critical_function_list`, required): Describe your critical operations.
* **Recovery Time Objective (RTO)** (`recovery_time_obj`, required): Enter the maximum acceptable downtime in hours.
* **Recovery Point Objective (RPO)** (`recovery_point_obj`, required): Enter the maximum acceptable data loss in minutes.
* **Critical Dependencies** (`dependencies`, required): Describe the key systems or resources needed.
* **Primary Business Risks** (`primary_risks`, required): Identify the main threats to your organization.
* **Likelihood of Severe Disruption** (`likelihood_severe`, required): Select the likelihood of severe disruption within the next 12 months.
* **Worst Case Scenario Impact** (`worst_case_scenario`, required): Describe the financial impact if critical functions stop.
* **Business Continuity Plan Status** (`plan_status`, required): Select the current state of your BC planning.
* **Plan Storage Location** (`plan_location`, required): Describe where the plan is kept.
* **Backup Facilities Available** (`backup_facilities`, required): Select whether you have alternate facilities.
* **Data Backup Systems** (`data_backups`, required): Select the current backup procedures.
* **Crisis Communication Plan** (`communication_plan`, required): Select how you will communicate during a disruption.
* **Plan Testing Schedule** (`testing_schedule`, required): Select how often the plan is tested.
* **Employee Training Program** (`training_program`, required): Select the level of staff awareness and training.
* **Improvement Needs** (`improvement_needs`, optional): Describe areas for improvement.
