# Profit And Loss Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Profit And Loss Report Form is designed to help businesses track their revenue and expenses. It provides an overview of the company's financial performance, which can be used for internal management, financial planning, and external reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "revenue_fields" section and report all revenue earned during the period.
2. Enter all expenses in the "expense_fields" section.
3. Provide the business ID in the "business_id" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **revenue_fields (Page 1)** (`text`, not required): This field allows you to report all revenue earned during the period. Include any income from sales, services, or other revenue sources.
* **expense_fields (Page 2)** (`text`, not required): Report all expenses incurred during the period. This includes costs related to materials, labor, and overheads.
* **business_id (Page 3)** (`text`, not required): Enter the unique identifier for your business. This is essential for accurate reporting and tracking.
