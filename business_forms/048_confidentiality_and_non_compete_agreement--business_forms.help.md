# Confidentiality And Non-Compete Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and ensure that employees understand their confidentiality and non-compete agreements with the company.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your full legal name in the **Employee Full Legal Name** field.
*   Enter your contact email address in the **Email Address** field.
*   Enter your contact phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
*   Enter your full address in the **Address** field.
*   Enter your city in the **City** field.
*   Enter your state in the **State** field.
*   Enter your postal code in the **ZIP Code** field.
*   Enter the name of your company in the **Company Name** field.
*   Enter your job title in the **Job Title** field.
*   Enter your department in the **Department** field (optional).
*   Enter your employment start date in the **Employment Start Date** field.
*   Enter the date of this agreement in the **Agreement Date** field.
*   Select the type of agreement you are entering into in the **Agreement Type** field.
*   Enter what is considered confidential information in the **Confidential Information Definition** field.
*   Describe your confidentiality obligations in the **Confidentiality Obligations** field.
*   Describe the scope of the non-compete in the **Non-Compete Scope** field.
*   Select the duration of the non-compete in the **Non-Compete Duration** field.
*   If the non-compete duration is other than specified, enter details in the **Non-Compete Duration Details** field (optional).
*   Select the geographic scope of the non-compete in the **Geographic Scope** field.
*   If the geographic scope is other than specified, describe the areas in the **Geographic Details** field (optional).
*   Describe the activities that are restricted in the **Restricted Activities** field.
*   List the companies that are restricted from competing in the **Restricted Competitors** field (optional).
*   Select whether or not customer and employee solicitation is included in the non-compete in the **Non-Solicitation** field.
*   Select the duration of the non-solicitation in the **Non-Solicitation Duration** field.
*   Describe the consideration you receive in the **Consideration** field.
*   Select whether or not you have received legal review for this agreement in the **Legal Review** field.
*   Enter the name and title of your legal representative (optional).
*   Select whether or not you are signing this agreement voluntarily in the **Voluntary Agreement** field.
*   Select whether or not you fully understand the terms of the agreement in the **Understanding Statement** field.
*   Select whether or not you understand that this agreement is binding in the **Enforceability Acknowledgment** field.
*   Select whether or not you understand the breach consequences in the **Breach Consequences** field.
*   If you have any questions, describe them in the **Any Questions** field (optional).
*   Select whether or not you accept the terms of the agreement in the **Agreement Acceptance** field.
*   Select whether or not you are willing to sign the agreement in the **Employee Signature** field.
*   Enter the date of your signature in the **Date of Signature** field.
*   Enter the name of your employer representative in the **Employer Representative** field.
*   Enter the title of your employer representative in the **Employer Representative Title** field.
*   Select whether or not your employer representative has signed the agreement in the **Employer Signature** field.
