# Business Growth Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Growth Application Form is designed to help businesses assess their growth potential, identify areas for improvement, and provide valuable insights for strategic planning and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your business information, growth objectives, and resource needs.
2. Answer each question accurately and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Review and submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Information
* **Business Name** (`business_name`): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`): Provide the name of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your company phone number.
* **Industry Type** (`industry_type`): Select the industry your business operates in from the provided options.

### Current Business Status
* **Years in Operation** (`years_in_operation`): Enter the number of years your business has been operating.
* **Current Annual Revenue** (`current_revenue`): Enter your most recent annual revenue.
* **Current Number of Employees** (`employee_count`): Enter the number of people working for you.
* **Market Position** (`market_position`): Describe your current market position.

### Growth Objectives
* **Growth Timeframe** (`growth_timeframe`): Select the timeframe for your growth plans from the provided options.
* **Target Growth Rate** (`growth_target`): Enter the desired percentage growth.
* **Target Revenue** (`revenue_goal`): Enter the desired revenue level.
* **Proposed Growth Strategies** (`growth_strategies`): Select all applicable growth strategies that will help achieve your growth objectives.
* **Key Challenges to Growth** (`key_challenges`): Describe the obstacles you face in achieving growth.
* **Competitive Threats** (`competitive_threats`): Describe any competition concerns you have.
* **Resource Needs** (`resource_needs`): Enter the amount of capital and other resources needed for growth.
* **Talent or Skill Gaps** (`talent_needs`): Describe the skills or talent you need to hire.
* **Type of Support Needed** (`support_type`): Select the types of support you need to achieve growth.
* **Success Metrics** (`success_metrics`): Describe how you will measure the success of your growth plan.
* **Committed to Growth Plan** (`commitment_confirmation`): Confirm your commitment to execute your growth plan.
