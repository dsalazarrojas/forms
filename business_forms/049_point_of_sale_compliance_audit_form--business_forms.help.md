# Point Of Sale Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the auditor assess a store's point of sale compliance and gather information to make decisions during the audit process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the store's information:
	* Store Name: Enter the store's name.
	* Store Address: Enter the store's address.
	* Store Phone: Enter the store's phone number.
	* Store Email: Enter the store's email address.
2. Answer the compliance questions:
	* Sales Regulations: Check if the store is compliant with sales regulations.
	* Audit Frequency: Indicate how often the store is audited (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
3. Provide additional information:
	* Auditor Information: Check if the auditor has access to any additional information (e.g., documentation, reports).
	* Comments: Add any additional comments or notes about the audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Info** (Required: false)
	* This field is a note field, where you can enter any additional information about the store that is relevant to the audit.
* **Sales Regulations** (Required: false)
	* Check the box to indicate if the store is compliant with sales regulations. This field is multiple-choice, and you can select multiple options.
* **Audit Frequency** (Required: false)
	* Select the frequency of the store's audits (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Store Name** (Required: false)
	* Enter the store's name.
* **Store Address** (Required: false)
	* Enter the store's address.
* **Store Phone** (Required: false)
	* Enter the store's phone number.
* **Store Email** (Required: false)
	* Enter the store's email address.
* **Auditor Information** (Required: false)
	* Check the box to indicate if the auditor has access to any additional information (e.g., documentation, reports).
* **Audit Date** (Required: false)
	* Enter the date of the audit.
* **Comments** (Required: false)
	* Add any additional comments or notes about the audit.

Note: Please fill out this form as thoroughly and accurately as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your auditor.
