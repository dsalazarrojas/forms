# Daily EOD Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily EOD Report Template is designed to gather information for the End of Day report, allowing users to track their work and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, review the form, enter the current date, describe your activity, add comments, and enter your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **report_date**: Enter the current date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **activity**: Describe the main task or activity you completed during the day.
* **comments**: Add any additional comments or notes related to your work.
* **user**: Enter your name or username.
