# Wholesale Partnership Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information for a wholesale partnership agreement between a business and its partner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about the partner.
2. Review and select the payment terms and conditions that apply to the partnership.
3. Choose the partner's status and termination options.
4. Select the agreement revision options if necessary.
5. Provide any necessary notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the partner.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the partner's business.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): Enter the business address of the partner.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, optional): Enter the partner's email address.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, optional): Enter the partner's phone number.
* **Terms** (`partner_terms`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available options for the partnership terms.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, number, optional): Enter the payment terms of the partnership.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply for the payment frequency.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Partner Agreement** (`partner_agreement`, note, optional): Enter any notes or comments about the partner.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Partnership Percentage** (`partnership_percentage`, number, optional): Enter the partnership percentage.
* **Partner Notes** (`partner_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the partner.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user assigned to the partner.
* **Partner Agreed** (`partner_agreed`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available options for the partner's agreement.
* **Contract Length** (`contract_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the contract.
* **Payment Terms 2** (`payment_terms2`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply for the payment terms.
* **Partner Address** (`partner_address`, text, optional): Enter the partner's address.
* **Partner Occupation** (`partner_occupation`, text, optional): Enter the partner's occupation.
* **Partner Status** (`partner_status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available options for the partner's status.
* **Partner Termination** (`partner_termination`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available options for the partner's termination.
* **Agreement Revision** (`agreement_revision`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available options for the agreement revision.
* **Reason** (`agreement_revision_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the agreement revision.
* **Date of Revision** (`agreement_revision_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the agreement revision.
