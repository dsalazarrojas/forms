# Customer Activation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Activation Form is used to collect essential information from customers to activate their accounts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name and last name.
2. Enter a valid email address for account creation.
3. Enter a strong password for account security.
4. Confirm your password to ensure it's correct.
5. If you want to provide your email address for confirmation, enter it and confirm it again.
6. Select yes or no to some options as required.
7. If you provide your phone number, please ensure it's correct.
8. Enter your address if you want to provide it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`first_name`, text, required)
Enter your first name.

### Last Name (`last_name`, text, required)
Enter your last name.

### Email (`email`, email, required)
Enter a valid email address for account creation and confirmation.

### Password (`password`, text, optional)
Enter a strong password for account security.

### Confirm Password (`confirm_password`, text, required)
Confirm your password to ensure it's correct.

### Date of Birth (`date`, date, optional)
Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format. This field is not required.

### Time of Registration (`time`, time, optional)
Enter your time of registration in 24-hour format. This field is not required.

### Phone (`phone`, text, optional)
Enter your phone number. This field is optional.

### Address (`address`, note, optional)
Enter your address. This field is optional.

### Confirm Email (`confirm_email`, email, required)
Confirm your email address.

### Select one of the options (`select_one`, select_one, required)
Select yes or no from the options provided.

### Select multiple options (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional)
Select multiple yes or no options as required.

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled in to proceed with the account activation process. The fields marked as optional can be left blank if not desired.
