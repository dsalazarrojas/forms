## Employee Communication Draft Review Request - Help Guide

### Purpose
The "Employee Communication Draft Review Request" form is used to request a review of an employee's draft communication. This form is typically used by employees to submit their draft for review and feedback from their supervisor or manager. The review will help ensure that the communication is clear, concise, and effective in conveying the intended message.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the employee submitting the draft.
2. Enter your message draft in the "Message Draft" field.
3. Check the "Context Provided" field to indicate whether the context for the communication is clearly understood or not.
4. Enter the deadline for the expected completion of the review.
5. Select the reviewer's name from the list of options.
6. Choose the type of review you are requesting (Review, Approve, or Reject).
7. If you have any comments or feedback, enter them in the "Comments" field.
8. Enter your email address and phone number for follow-up communication.
9. Schedule a meeting time for discussion and review.
10. Click "Submit" to submit the form.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as the employee submitting the draft.
* **Message Draft**: Enter the content of your communication draft.
* **Context Provided**: Check if the context for the communication is clearly understood or not. This helps the reviewer understand the scope and purpose of the communication.
* **Deadline**: Enter the deadline for the expected completion of the review.
* **Reviewer Name**: Select the name of the reviewer assigned to review your draft.
* **Review Type**: Choose the type of review you are requesting (Review, Approve, or Reject). This helps the reviewer know how to approach the review.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you have on the draft.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication with the reviewer.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Meeting**: Enter a meeting time for discussion and review of the draft.
* **Submit**: Click "Submit" to submit the form for review.
