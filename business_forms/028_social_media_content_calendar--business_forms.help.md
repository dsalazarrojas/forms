# Social Media Content Calendar - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Content Calendar is a tool used to plan and schedule social media content. It helps to organize and manage social media content for a specific date, allowing you to create a structured and consistent approach to social media posting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select a Schedule Date for the content you want to post. This date will determine when your content will be posted on social media.
2.  Choose the Social Platform where the content will be posted, such as Facebook, Twitter, or Instagram.
3.  Select the Content Type, which can be text, image, or video.
4.  Enter a brief Content Topic related to the content you are posting.
5.  Provide a detailed Content Description of the content you are posting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Date (1)** (`schedule_date`, date, Optional): Select a date to schedule the social media content. This date will determine when the content will be posted.
* **Social Platform (2)** (`social_platform`, select_one, Optional): Choose a social media platform to post the content, such as Facebook, Twitter, or Instagram.
* **Content Type (3)** (`content_type`, select_one, Optional): Select the type of content you will be posting, which can be text, image, or video.
* **Content Topic (4)** (`content_topic`, text, Optional): Enter a brief topic related to the content you are posting.
* **Content Description (5)** (`content_description`, text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of the content you are posting.
