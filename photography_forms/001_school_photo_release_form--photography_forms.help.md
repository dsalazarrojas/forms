# School Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Photo Release Form is used by school administrators and staff to collect information from parents/guardians for school photoshoots. The form serves as a way to inform parents about the usage of their child's photos and to obtain their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the correct photo type and usage from the available options.
3. Provide a brief description of the photo and any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the parent/guardian who is providing the consent.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the parent's email address (if available).
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the child who will be photographed.
* **Student Id** (`student_id`, Text, Optional): Enter the student ID number (if applicable).
* **Photo Date** (`photo_date`, Date, Optional): Select the date the photo was taken or will be taken.
* **Photo Type** (`photo_type`, Select One, Required): Select whether the photo is for 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Photo Location** (`photo_location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location where the photo was taken or will be taken.
* **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, Text, Required): Briefly describe the photo and include any relevant details.
* **Photo Usage** (`photo_usage`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the purpose of the photo usage (School, Marketing, Online).
* **Consent** (`consent`, Select One, Required): Select whether or not you give consent to the photo usage ('True', 'False').
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the photo.
