# photography_project_leave_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photography project leave form is designed to help project team members request a leave or deactivate a project in a structured and standardized process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Photography Project Title** (Field 1). This is the title of the project you are requesting to leave or deactivate. 
2. Select the **Project Duration** (Field 2). This is the duration of the project you are requesting to leave or deactivate.
3. Enter the **Start Date** (Field 3) when the project started.
4. Enter the **End Date** (Field 4) when the project will end or was ended.
5. Choose the **Project Status** (Field 5). Select the status of the project you are requesting to leave or deactivate from the provided options: Active, Inactive, or On Hold.
6. Provide a **Reason for Leave** (Field 6). This is a text field where you can enter a brief description of the reason for leaving or deactivating the project.
7. Add any additional comments or context in the **Comment** (Field 7) field.
8. Enter your **Contact Email** (Field 8). This is the email address where project team members can contact you for further information or discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Photography Project Title**: Enter the title of the project you are requesting to leave or deactivate.
* **2. Project Duration**: Select the duration of the project using the provided options (e.g., days, weeks, months, etc.).
* **3. Start Date**: Enter the date when the project started.
* **4. End Date**: Enter the date when the project will end or was ended.
* **5. Project Status**: Select the status of the project (Active, Inactive, or On Hold) you are requesting to leave or deactivate.
* **6. Reason for Leave**: Enter a brief description of the reason for leaving or deactivating the project.
* **7. Comment**: Add any additional comments or context about the project.
* **8. Contact Email**: Enter your email address where project team members can contact you for further information or discussion.
