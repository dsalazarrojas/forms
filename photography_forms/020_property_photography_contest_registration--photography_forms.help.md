# Property Photography Contest Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information from participants about their photo entries, real estate team, photographer, organization, agreement status, photo date, photo time, and agreement date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your Participant Details. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select all your photo entries from the list of options.
3. Choose your Real Estate Team from the list of options.
4. Fill in your Photographer name.
5. Fill in your Organization name.
6. Choose your Agreement Status from the list of options.
7. Fill in the Photo Date.
8. Fill in the Photo Time.
9. Fill in the Agreement Date.

	+ Participant Details is a text field where you can provide any relevant information about yourself.

	+ This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about the contest agreements.

	+ Choose your Real Estate Team from the list of options.

	+ Fill in your Photographer name.
