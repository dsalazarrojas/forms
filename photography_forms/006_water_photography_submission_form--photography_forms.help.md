# Water Photography Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Water Photography Submission Form is a platform for photographers to share their work with the world. This form allows you to upload and submit your photographs, share your story, and connect with a community of water enthusiasts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Submission Form**: Provide a brief description of your submission, including the title and any relevant context.
2. **Submission Info**: Enter a concise and accurate account of your submission, including details about your image.
3. **Photographer Info**: Share information about yourself as the photographer, such as your name and contact details.
4. **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
5. **Upload Image**: Select and upload your image to share with the world.
6. **Image Credit**: Specify the credit information for your image, including any copyrights or permissions.
7. **Image Description**: Add a detailed description of your image, including any relevant details or context.
8. **Submission Type**: Select the type of submission you are making (Water Contest, Exhibition, or Gallery).
9. **Submission Date**: Enter the date of your submission.
10. **Submission Time**: Enter the time of your submission.
11. **File Upload**: Upload any relevant files associated with your submission.
12. **Image Size**: Specify the size of your image.
13. **Image Resolution**: Enter the resolution of your image.
14. **Photographer Name**: Enter your name as the photographer.
15. **Photographer Website**: Share your website URL.
16. **Photographer Facebook**: Share your Facebook profile URL.
17. **Photographer Twitter**: Share your Twitter profile URL.
18. **Photographer Email**: Enter your email address.
19. **Photographer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
20. **Submission Status**: Select the status of your submission (In Progress, Published, or Rejected).
21. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your submission.
22. **File Type**: Specify whether you have included any files with your submission (Yes or No).
23. **File Size**: Enter the size of the uploaded file.
24. **File Description**: Add a description of the uploaded file.
25. **Submission Date**: Enter the date of your submission.
