# Photographer Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photographer Onboarding Form is designed to collect information about photographers who want to work with our company. This form helps us to evaluate a photographer's qualifications, experience, and availability, and to determine if they are a good fit for our projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and enter the required information.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk *).
3. Use the options provided for select fields to select the most relevant information.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Studios Name** (`studio_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your photography studio or business.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your studio's address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Bio** (`bio`, text, optional): Write a brief bio about your photography experience and style.
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been working as a photographer.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Enter any relevant education or training you have received.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Select the days and times you are available to work.
* **Photography Equipment** (`equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Select the equipment you have access to and are comfortable using.
* **Services Offered** (`services`, select_multiple, optional): Select the services you offer as a photographer (e.g. portrait, wedding, product).
* **Rates** (`rates`, select_multiple, optional): Select the rate ranges you offer for your services.
* **Social Links** (`social_links`, text, optional): Enter your social media links (e.g. Instagram, Facebook).
* **About** (`about`, text, optional): Write a brief description of your photography style and approach.
* **Contact Person** (`contact`, select_one, optional): Select the contact person for your studio.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, optional): Select the location where you are based.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_multiple, optional): Select the social media platforms you use.
* **Website URL** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your website URL.
* **Profile Picture** (`profile_picture`, text, optional): Upload a profile picture of yourself.
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, text, optional): Upload a cover letter explaining why you are a good fit for our projects.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Upload your resume or CV.
* **References** (`references`, select_multiple, optional): Select the references you would like to use.
* **Availability Dates** (`availability_dates`, date, optional): Enter the dates you are available to start and end work.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to start work.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to end work.
