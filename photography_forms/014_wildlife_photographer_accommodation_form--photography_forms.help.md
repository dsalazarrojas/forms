# wildlife_photographer_accommodation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from wildlife photographers about their accommodation needs while traveling. This information will be used to provide tailored quotes and arrangements for the photographers' accommodations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Please fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Ensure that the dates you enter are correct and in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* Select the correct accommodation type from the provided options.
* If you have any specific accommodation preferences, type them in the "Accommodation Preferences" field.
* You may also list any special requests you have in the "Special Requests" field.
* If you would like to be informed about equipment rental options, please enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Finally, confirm that the information you've entered is accurate by typing "I confirm" in the "Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer Name**: Please enter your name as it should appear on the invoice.
* **Dates Start**: Enter the start date of your stay in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Dates End**: Enter the end date of your stay in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Location**: Enter the specific location where you will be staying (city, country, or region).
* **Accommodation Type**: Select the type of accommodation that suits your needs from the provided options.
* **Accommodation Preferences**: If you have any specific requests or preferences for your accommodation, please type them in this field.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any additional requests or requirements that are not listed above, please type them in this field.
* **Equipment Rental**: If you are interested in renting equipment, please enter your email address so we can get in touch with you with rental options.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about your accommodation arrangements.
* **Confirmation**: Type "I confirm" to verify that the information you've entered is accurate.
