# Second Shooter Assistant Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Second Shooter Assistant Agreement is a form that ensures both the photographer and the second shooter are clear about their roles and responsibilities during a collaborative photography project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Photographer**'s name and contact information. This is the person leading the photography project.
2.  Enter the **Client**'s name and contact information. This is the person or entity commissioning the photography project.
3.  Enter the **Second Shooter**'s name and contact information. This is the assistant helping the photographer during the project.
4.  Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** of the photography project. This ensures both parties are clear on the project timeline.
5.  Enter the **Location** where the photography project will take place.
6.  Provide detailed **Shoot Details** about the project, including any specific requirements or instructions.
7.  Enter the **Assistant Fee** and **Assistant Hours** for the second shooter's compensation.
8.  Enter the **Assistant Rate** per hour for the second shooter.
9.  Both the **Client** and the **Photographer** must sign the form to agree to its terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer**: This is the person leading the photography project. Enter their name and contact information.
*  **Client**: This is the person or entity commissioning the photography project. Enter their name and contact information.
*  **Second Shooter**: This is the assistant helping the photographer during the project. Enter their name and contact information.
*  **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the photography project. This is the date when the project begins.
*  **End Date**: Enter the end date of the photography project. This is the date when the project ends.
*  **Location**: Enter the location where the photography project will take place.
*  **Shoot Details**: Provide detailed information about the project, including any specific requirements or instructions.
*  **Assistant Fee**: Enter the total compensation for the second shooter's services.
*  **Assistant Hours**: Enter the total number of hours the second shooter will work on the project.
*  **Assistant Rate**: Enter the rate per hour for the second shooter's services.
*  **Client Signature**: This is where the client confirms they have read and agree to the terms of the contract.
*  **Photographer Signature**: This is where the photographer confirms they have read and agree to the terms of the contract.
*  **Date Client Signed**: Enter the date the client signed the contract.
*  **Date Photographer Signed**: Enter the date the photographer signed the contract.
