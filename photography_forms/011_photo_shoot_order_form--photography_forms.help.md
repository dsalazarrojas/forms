# Photo Shoot Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Shoot Order Form is designed to gather necessary information for a photo shoot order, which includes shoot details, client information, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the shoot details in the "Enter Shoot Details" field.
2. Select the date for the photo shoot in the "Date" field.
3. Choose the time for the photo shoot in the "Time" field.
4. Choose the type of photo to be taken in the "Photo Type" field.
5. Specify the number of photos to be taken in the "Number of Photos" field.
6. Enter the location where the photo shoot will take place in the "Location" field.
7. Choose the size of the photo in the "Photo Size" field.
8. Specify the orientation of the photo in the "Photo Orientation" field.
9. Select the package selection in the "Package Selection" field.
10. Enter the shoot name in the "Shoot Name" field.
11. Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
12. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
13. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
14. Choose the payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
15. Enter the card number in the "Card Number" field.
16. Enter the expiration date of the payment card in the "Exp Date" field.
17. Choose the expiration month of the payment card in the "Exp Month" field.
18. Choose the expiration year of the payment card in the "Exp Year" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Shoot Details:** Enter a brief description of the photo shoot, such as the theme, concept, or any specific requests.
* **Date:** Choose the date for the photo shoot.
* **Time:** Choose the time for the photo shoot.
* **Photo Type:** Choose the type of photo to be taken, such as portrait, landscape, or event.
* **Number of Photos:** Specify the number of photos to be taken.
* **Location:** Enter the location where the photo shoot will take place.
* **Photo Size:** Choose the size of the photo.
* **Photo Orientation:** Specify the orientation of the photo, such as landscape or portrait.
* **Package Selection:** Choose the package selection for the photo shoot.
* **Shoot Name:** Enter the name of the photo shoot.
* **Your Name:** Enter your name, which will be associated with the photo shoot.
* **Email:** Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
* **Phone:** Enter your phone number, which can be used for communication and updates.
* **Payment Method:** Choose the payment method for the photo shoot.
* **Card Number:** Enter the card number for payment.
* **Exp Date:** Enter the expiration date of the payment card.
* **Exp Month:** Choose the expiration month of the payment card.
* **Exp Year:** Choose the expiration year of the payment card.
* **Tips:** Please make sure to double-check all the fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
