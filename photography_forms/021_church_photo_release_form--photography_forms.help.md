# Church Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Photo Release Form is a crucial document that ensures participants understand and agree to the usage of their photographs taken during events or activities. By completing this form, participants grant permission for the use of their images and provide essential information about themselves and the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name of Participant" field.
2.  Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field.
3.  Enter your **Phone Number** (if applicable) in the "Phone Number" field.
4.  Provide your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field.
5.  Specify the **Event or Activity Name** in the "Event or Activity Name" field.
6.  Enter the **Event Date** in the "Event Date" field.
7.  Mention the **Event Location** (if applicable) in the "Event Location" field.
8.  Select "Yes" or "No" to **Authorize Photography** of your participation in the "I authorize photography of my participation" field.
9.  Describe the **Description of Photographs to Be Taken** in the "Description of Photographs to Be Taken" field.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" to grant **Permission for Use of Photographs** in the "Grant Permission for Use of Photographs" field.
11. Choose **Authorized Uses** for the photographs (if applicable).
12. If you are under 18, specify if you are a **Minor** and provide your **Parent or Guardian Name** and **Email (if under 18)**.
13. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm **Consent of Parent or Guardian** (if under 18).
14. Confirm that the information provided is **Accurate and Complete** in the "I confirm this information is accurate and complete" field.
15. Finally, complete the **Form Completion Date** in the "Form Completion Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name of Participant** (`participant_name`): Enter your full name.
*   **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
*   **Event or Activity Name** (`event_name`): Specify the name of the event or activity.
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event.
*   **Event Location** (`event_location`): Mention the location where the event is being held (if applicable).
*   **I authorize photography of my participation** (`authorize_photography`): Select "Yes" if you agree to photography, "No" otherwise.
*   **Description of Photographs to Be Taken** (`photo_description`): Describe the kind of photographs you will be taking during the event.
*   **Grant Permission for Use of Photographs** (`grant_permission`): Select "Yes" if you grant permission for use of your photographs.
*   **Authorized Uses** (`authorized_uses`): Choose all the authorized uses of your photographs.
*   **Minor Information** (`is_minor`, `parent_guardian_name`, `parent_guardian_email`, `parent_consent`): If you are under 18, enter the relevant information as required.
*   **Parent or Guardian Consent** (`parent_consent`): If under 18, select "I consent" or "I do not consent".
*   **Accurate and Complete** (`agreement_confirmation`): Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
*   **Form Completion Date** (`form_date`): Complete the date when you are submitting this form.
