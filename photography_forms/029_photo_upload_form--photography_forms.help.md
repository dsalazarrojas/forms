# Photo Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Upload Form is designed to collect information about the photo you are uploading. It helps us understand the context and details of the photo, which is necessary for proper upload and processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill in all the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) in the label.
* Select the correct options for each field as instructed.
* For the "What's the purpose of the photo?" field, briefly describe the purpose of the uploaded photo.
* For the "What's the date of the photo?" field, select the date the photo was taken using the date picker.
* For the "Who is the photographer?" field, select the correct option (Self, Friend, or Professional).
* For the "What type of event is the photo related to?" field, select one or more event types that apply to the photo (e.g., Birthday party, Wedding, Graduation).
* For the "What's the location where the photo was taken?" field, enter the location where the photo was taken.
* For the "What's the time of the event?" field, select the time of the event (24-hour format).
* For the "What's your email address?" field, enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* For the "What's your phone number?" field, enter your phone number.
* For the "Upload a photo" field, upload a photo with a maximum size of 5MB.
* For the "Description of the photo" field, enter a brief description of the photo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What's the purpose of the photo?** (`photo_upload_form_page_1`, text, required): Briefly describe the purpose of the uploaded photo. This field helps us understand the context of the photo.
* **What's the date of the photo?** (`photo_upload_form_page_2`, date, required): Select the date the photo was taken using the date picker. This field ensures we have accurate timestamp information for the photo.
* **Who is the photographer?** (`photo_upload_form_page_3`, select_one, required): Select the photographer who took the photo. This field helps us know the origin of the photo.
* **What type of event is the photo related to?** (`photo_upload_form_page_4`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more event types that apply to the photo. This field helps us categorize the photo for easier search and retrieval.
* **What's the location where the photo was taken?** (`photo_upload_form_page_5`, text, required): Enter the location where the photo was taken. This field provides spatial context to the photo.
* **What's the time of the event?** (`photo_upload_form_page_6`, time, required): Select the time of the event (24-hour format). This field helps us understand the timing of the photo.
* **What's your email address?** (`photo_upload_form_page_7`, email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **What's your phone number?** (`photo_upload_form_page_8`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Upload a photo** (`photo_upload_form_page_9`, text, required): Upload a photo with a maximum size of 5MB. Please ensure the uploaded file is in a supported format and within the size limit.
* **Description of the photo** (`photo_upload_form_page_10`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the photo. This field helps us understand the content of the photo.
