# Nonprofit Photography Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Photography Fellowship Application is a form designed to collect information from non-profit photography organizations for a fellowship application. This form is intended to gather details about the applicant, their photography portfolio, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure you are applying for the fellowship with the correct non-profit organization.
3. Attach your photography portfolio, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Applicant Information
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_information`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of yourself and your non-profit organization.

### 2. Portfolio Images
* **Portfolio Images** (`portfolio_images`, text, optional): In this field, please provide a brief description of your photography portfolio, including any relevant images or projects.

### 3. Proposal Details
* **Proposal Details** (`proposal_details`, text, optional): This field is for explaining why you are applying for the fellowship and what you hope to achieve with the award.

### 4. Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): This field is for providing contact information for the applicant or their representative.

### 5. Applicant Info
* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional information about the applicant.

### 6. Applicant Portfolio
* **Applicant Portfolio** (`applicant_portfolio`, text, optional): In this field, please provide a link or description of your online photography portfolio.

### 7. Proposal Proposal
* **Proposal Proposal** (`proposal_proposal`, text, optional): This field is for describing your proposal for the fellowship, including any specific goals or objectives.

### 8. Contact Contact
* **Contact Contact** (`contact_contact`, text, optional): This field is for providing contact information for the applicant or their representative.

### 9. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address.

### 10. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number.

### 11. Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This is the submit button to finish and send the application. 

Note: This help guide is a general explanation and does not cover specific requirements and guidelines of the fellowship application. Please refer to the fellowship website for more detailed information. 

**Tips**

* Make sure to proofread your application before submitting.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Attach any supporting documents or images as necessary. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the fellowship administrators.
