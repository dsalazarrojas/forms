# Prayer Card Design Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for custom prayer card designs. Please provide all required fields to ensure efficient processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review your submission before submitting.
3. Click the submit button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required): Enter a brief title for your prayer card design request.
* **Category** (`form_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of the prayer card design request (e.g., "Photography Forms").
* **Description** (`form_description`, `text`, required): Describe the details of your request.
* **Image** (`form_image`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the images to be used for your prayer card design.
* **Layout** (`form_layout`, `select_one`, required): Select the layout of your prayer card design (e.g., "2x6").
* **Color** (`form_color`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the color scheme for your prayer card design.
* **Text** (`form_text`, `text`, required): Enter the text to be included on your prayer card.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Email** (`form_email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address for easy contact.
* **Date** (`form_date`, `date`, required): Select the desired date for completion.
* **Time** (`form_time`, `time`, required): Select the desired time for completion.
* **Note** (`form_note`, `note`, required): Enter any notes or additional information for the designer.
* **Comment** (`form_comment`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or instructions.
* **Assignee** (`form_assignee`, `text`, required): Enter the person to be assigned the design task.
* **Status** (`form_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of your request.
* **Priority** (`form_priority`, `select_one`, required): Select the priority level of your request.
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional comments for the designer.
* **Tags** (`form_tags`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any relevant tags for the design.
* **Created** (`form_created`, `date`, required): The date the request was created.
* **Updated** (`form_updated`, `date`, required): The date the request was last updated.
* **Created By** (`form_created_by`, `text`, required): The person who created the request.
* **Updated By** (`form_updated_by`, `text`, required): The person who last updated the request.
* **Status 2** (`form_status_2`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of your request (similar to "Status").
* **Comments 2** (`form_comments_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional comments for the designer.
* **Tags 2** (`form_tags_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any relevant tags for the design.
