**photography_use_authorization_form - Help Guide**
=============================================

## Purpose
The "photography_use_authorization_form" is a form used to obtain authorization for usage of photography images, ensuring the rights and terms of usage are understood and agreed upon by the photographer and other relevant parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Photographer Information**
	* Enter the name of the photographer in the "Photographer" field.
2. **Image Usage Details**
	* Indicate whether the images are used for "Yes" or "No" by selecting the corresponding option.
3. **Image Acquisition Date**
	* Enter the date of image acquisition in the "Date of image acquisition" field.
4. **Usage Rights**
	* Select the usage rights for the images by choosing "Yes" or "No".
5. **Usage Terms**
	* Note any additional usage terms or conditions (optional).
6. **Contact Information**
	* Enter the email address of the photographer or relevant party in the "Email minimalist" field.
7. **Additional Contact Information (Optional)**
	* Enter the telephone number of the photographer or relevant party (optional).
8. **Agreement and Review Details (Optional)**
	* Indicate whether you agree to or need to review the agreement terms (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer**: Enter the name of the photographer or relevant party.
* **Images**: Select "Yes" if the images are used for a specific purpose, otherwise select "No".
* **Date of image acquisition**: Enter the date when the images were acquired.
* **Usage rights**: Select "Yes" if the usage rights are agreed upon, otherwise select "No".
* **Usage terms**: Note any additional usage terms or conditions.
* **Email minimalist**: Enter the email address of the photographer or relevant party.
* **Telephone**: Enter the telephone number of the photographer or relevant party (if providing contact information).
* **Comments**: Select "Yes" or "No" for any additional comments or remarks.
* **Agreement terms**: Enter any additional agreement terms or conditions (if applicable).
* **Review terms**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if review of agreement terms is required.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately complete the form with the required information to avoid any potential issues.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness of the information provided.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the relevant party or contact information provided in the form.
