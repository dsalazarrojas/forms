# Holiday Mini Session Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the client and their holiday mini session details for a holiday mini session contract. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information to finalize the contract between the photographer and the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Session Details** page.
2. On this page, you will find fields for **Session Date**, **Session Time**, and **Location**. Please fill out these fields accordingly.
3. Move on to the **Client Information** section.
4. Fill out the **Client Name**, **Contact Email**, and **Contact Phone** fields.
5. In the **Contract** section, choose **Yes** or **No** for **Signature** and **Consent**.
6. If you are accepting the payment terms, choose **Yes** or **No** for **Payment Terms**.
7. Finally, fill out the **Payment Terms Date** and **Payment Terms Signed Date** fields, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Details**: This page provides the necessary information for the holiday mini session, including **Session Date**, **Session Time**, and **Location**.
  - **Session Date**: The date of the holiday mini session.
  - **Session Time**: The time of the holiday mini session.
  - **Location**: The location of the holiday mini session.
- **Client Name**: The name of the client for the holiday mini session.
- **Contact Email**: The email address of the client's contact person.
- **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the client's contact person.
- **Contracted For**: This field is optional and can be filled out if you want to specify what the contract is for (e.g., a specific session or package).
- **Notes**: Any additional notes about the client or the session.
- **Signature**: Choose **Yes** if you have signed the contract, or **No** if you haven't.
- **Consent**: Choose **Yes** if you consent to the terms of the contract, or **No** if you don't.
- **Payment Terms**: This field is optional and can be filled out if you want to specify the payment terms.
- **Payment Terms Signed**: This field should be filled out if you have signed the payment terms.
- **Payment Terms Date**: The date you signed the payment terms.
- **Payment Terms Signed Date**: The date you signed the payment terms.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
