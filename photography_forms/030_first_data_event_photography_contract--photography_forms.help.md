# first-data-event-photography-contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The first-data-event-photography-contract form is used by photographers to document and prepare for events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Event Details** field with a brief description of the event.
2. Enter the **Photographer Info** field with your name and contact information.
3. Fill in the **Date Event** field with the date of the event.
4. Fill in the **Time Event** field with the time of the event.
5. Enter the **Client Info** field with the client's name and contact information.
6. Fill in the **Event Location** field with the location of the event.
7. Enter the **Client Contact** field with the client's contact information.
8. Fill in the **Event Notes** field with any additional notes about the event.
9. Sign with your signature in the **Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (text, required): A brief description of the event. 
* **Photographer Info** (text, optional): Your name and contact information. 
* **Date Event** (date, optional): The date of the event. 
* **Time Event** (time, optional): The time of the event. 
* **Client Info** (text, optional): The client's name and contact information. 
* **Event Location** (text, optional): The location of the event. 
* **Client Contact** (text, optional): The client's contact information. 
* **Event Notes** (note, optional): Any additional notes about the event. 
* **Client Email** (email, optional): The client's email address. 
* **Signature** (note, optional): Your signature. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* This form is for photography event purposes only.
