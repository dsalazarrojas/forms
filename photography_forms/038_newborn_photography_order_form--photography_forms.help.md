# Newborn Photography Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Newborn Photography Order Form is a form designed to gather essential information for booking a newborn photography session. Please fill out this form to provide us with your details and consent for the session.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in your customer details**: Please provide your name and email address in the respective fields. Your email address will be used for communication and to send you the contract and any necessary documents.
2. **Select a session date and time**: Choose the date and time for your newborn photography session. Please make sure to select a date and time that works for you.
3. **Consent to terms and conditions**: Read through and agree to our Terms and Conditions. This is a mandatory step before proceeding.
4. **Choose a payment method**: Select your preferred payment method for the session.
5. **Sign and confirm the contract**: Review and sign the contract that outlines the terms of the session.
6. **Add any additional notes**: If you have any further questions or requests, please include them in the "Notes" field.
