# General Photography Wedding Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Photography Wedding Contract form is designed to collect essential details about the customer and their wedding day to create a customized contract that meets their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the "Customer Information" field.
2. Enter your wedding information, including the date and location of the wedding, in the "Wedding Information" field.
3. Fill in your bride's information in the "Bride Information" field.
4. Enter your groom's information in the "Groom Information" field.
5. Describe the contract terms and conditions, including any specific requirements or expectations, in the "Contract Information" field.
6. Specify the payment terms and conditions in the "Payment Terms" field.
7. Check the services that will be provided (yes/no) in the "Services" field.
8. Provide any additional information that is relevant to the contract in the "Additional Information" field.
9. Add your signature to confirm your agreement with the terms in the "Signature" field.
10. Finalize your agreement with a brief statement in the "Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Customer Information
• **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
• **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
• **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.

### Wedding Information
• **Wedding Date**: Enter the date of your wedding.
• **Wedding Location**: Enter the location of your wedding venue.

### Bride Information
• **Bride Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
• **Bride Contact Info**: Enter your email address and phone number where you can be contacted.

### Groom Information
• **Groom Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
• **Groom Contact Info**: Enter your email address and phone number where you can be contacted.

### Contract Information
• **Contract Terms**: Describe the specific requirements or expectations for the photography services.

### Payment Terms
• **Payment Terms**: Specify the payment terms and conditions, including any late fees or penalties.

### Services
• **Services**: Check the services that will be provided (yes/no) for photo shoots, editing, and other services.

### Additional Information
• **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information that is relevant to the contract.

### Signature
• **Signature**: Add your signature to confirm your agreement with the terms.

### Agreement
• **Agreement**: Finalize your agreement with a brief statement.
