# Mini Photo Shoot Session Contract Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mini Photo Shoot Session Contract Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a photo shoot session between the client and the photographer. This form ensures that both parties are on the same page and understand their responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields. While some fields are not marked as required, it's essential to fill out each field to ensure accurate and complete information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. Photographer Information** (`photographer_info`, text, not required)
This field is for the photographer's contact information. Please enter the photographer's name, phone number, and email address.

### **2. Client Information** (`client_info`, text, not required)
This field is for the client's contact information. Please enter your name, phone number, and email address.

### **3. Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, not required)
This field is for the photographer to specify the payment terms of the photo session, including the price and any payment methods accepted.

### **4. Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, text, not required)
This field is for the photographer to outline their cancellation policy, including any fees or penalties for cancelling the session.

### **5. Photo Session Details** (`photo_session_details`, text, not required)
This field is for the photographer to provide any additional information about the photo session, such as the location and number of photos to be taken.

### **6. Photo Session Date** (`photo_session_date`, date, not required)
This field is for the photographer to specify the date of the photo session.

### **7. Photo Session Time** (`photo_session_time`, time, not required)
This field is for the photographer to specify the time of the photo session.

### **8. Session Location** (`session_location`, text, not required)
This field is for the photographer to specify the location of the photo session.

### **9. Studio Location** (`studio_location`, text, not required)
This field is for the photographer to specify the location of their studio or where the photo session will take place.

### **10. Photo Session Terms** (`photo_session_terms`, text, not required)
This field is for the photographer to outline any additional terms or conditions of the photo session.

### **11. Photo Equipment** (`photo_equipment`, text, not required)
This field is for the photographer to specify any equipment they will be using during the photo session.

### **12. Photo Session Price** (`photo_session_price`, number, not required)
This field is for the photographer to specify the price of the photo session.

### **13. Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, not required)
This field is for the client to sign and date the contract, indicating their agreement to the terms and conditions.

### **14. Photographer Signature** (`photographer_signature`, text, not required)
This field is for the photographer to sign and date the contract, indicating their agreement to the terms and conditions.

### **15. Contract Version** (`contract_version`, text, not required)
This field is for the photographer to specify the version number of the contract.

### **16. Photo Session Terms and Conditions** (`photo_session_terms_and_conditions`, text, not required)
This field is for the photographer to outline the terms and conditions of the photo session.

### **17. Photo Session Terms and Conditions Text** (`photo_session_terms_and_conditions_text`, text, not required)
This field is for the photographer to specify the text of the terms and conditions.

### **18. I Agree to the Photo Session Terms and Conditions** (`photo_session_terms_and_conditions_checkbox`, select_one, not required)
This field is for the client to agree or disagree to the terms and conditions of the photo session. Select "I Agree" to confirm your agreement.

### **19. Client Notes** (`client_notes`, text, not required)
This field is for the client to enter any additional notes or comments.

### **20. Photographer Notes** (`photographer_notes`, text, not required)
This field is for the photographer to enter any additional notes or comments.

### **21. Contract Completion Date** (`contract_completion`, date, not required)
This field is for the photographer to specify the date the contract was completed.

### **22. Photo Session Terms and Conditions**
This field is for the photographer to outline any additional terms or conditions of the photo session.

**Tips**

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is included.
* Ensure both parties sign and date the contract to confirm agreement.
* This contract is a template, and you may need to customize it according to your specific needs.
* Keep a record of the signed contract for your records.
