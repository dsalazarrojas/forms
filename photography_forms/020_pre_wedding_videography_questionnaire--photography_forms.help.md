# Pre Wedding Videography Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from wedding clients about their videography preferences, wedding details, and other essential information to ensure that their wedding video is tailored to their needs and expectations. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary details for your pre-wedding videography services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section of the form, providing the required and optional information as prompted.
2. Review each field for the correct information and make sure to save your progress if you need to exit and come back to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Information** (<name>): A text field to enter the details of your wedding, such as date, location, and time.
* **Videography Preferences** (<type>): A select field with options for the duration of the videography service (8-hour, 12-hour, 16-hour). Choose the option that best suits your needs.
* **Date and Time** (<type>): A date field to enter the date and time of your wedding.
* **Videographer Information** (<type>): A text field to provide information about your videographer, such as name and contact details.
* **Contact Details** (<type>): A text field to enter your contact information, such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Special Requests** (<type>): A select field with options for special requests, such as "None" or "Other" if you have specific needs.
* **Location and Layout** (<type>): A select field to choose the location and layout of your wedding (Outdoor, Indoor, Hybrid).
* **Videographer Notes** (<type>): A note field for your videographer to take notes about your wedding details.
* **Client Comments** (<type>): A text field for any comments or feedback about your videography experience.
* **Review Preferences** (<type>): A select field with options for review preferences, such as "None" or "Other" if you have specific needs.
* **Videographer Availability** (<type>): A select field to indicate if the videographer is available or unavailable for your wedding.
* **Review Preferences** (<type>): A note field for your videographer to record any reviews or feedback about your experience.
* **Special Requests** (<type>): A text field to enter any special requests or instructions for your videographer.
