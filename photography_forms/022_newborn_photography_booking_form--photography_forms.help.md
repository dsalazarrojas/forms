# Newborn Photography Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a newborn photography session with a photographer. It collects essential information about the client, including their personal details and session preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name, Last name, and Email address.
2. Enter your Phone number for communication.
3. Provide the Session date and Session time you are interested in.
4. Enter your Address for contact and session information.
5. Check the box for Consent to the terms and conditions.
6. Choose your preferred Session date and Session time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, e.g., John.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name, e.g., Doe.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, e.g., [johndoe@example.com](mailto:johndoe@example.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication, e.g., 123-456-7890.
* **Session date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date for the photography session.
* **Session time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose the time for the photography session.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for contact and session information.
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, optional): Check this box to confirm you've read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Preferred session date** (`preferred_date`, date, optional): Choose a preferred date for the photography session.
* **Preferred session time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Choose a preferred time for the photography session.

## Tips
* Ensure that you double-check the submitted information before hitting the "Submit" button to avoid any errors.
* If you have any specific requests or requirements for the session, please mention them in the notes section of the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the photographer directly.
