# Photography Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect photography release consent from clients for capturing or publishing their images. It captures the client's name, their agreement to be photographed, the date and time of the photography session, and their signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out this form to give or deny photography release consent.
2. Select your client from the list of options provided in the "Client Information" field.
3. Choose "Grant" or "Deny" in the "Release Consent" field to indicate your consent status.
4. Enter the date and time of the photography session in the "Release Consent Date" and "Release Consent Time" fields.
5. Add any additional notes about the photography session in the "Notes" field (optional).
6. If you wish to include an agreement, you can add it in the "Agreement" field (optional).
7. Sign the form with your name and date in the "Signature" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consent Form**: Please fill out this form to give or deny photography release consent. This field is required.
* **Client Information**: Select your client from the list of options provided. This field is optional.
* **Release Consent**: Choose "Grant" to indicate that you are consenting to be photographed, or "Deny" to indicate that you are not consenting. This field is required.
* **Release Consent Date**: Enter the date of the photography session. This field is required.
* **Release Consent Time**: Enter the time of the photography session. This field is required.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the photography session. This field is optional.
* **Agreement**: If you wish to include an agreement, you can add it in this field. This field is optional.
* **Signature**: Sign the form with your name and date to confirm that you have read and understood the consent terms. This field is optional.
* **Release Consent Date** and **Release Consent Time**: Please enter the date and time of the photography session. The date should be in the format mm/dd/yyyy and the time should be in 12-hour format (e.g., 3:00 PM).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct date and time of the photography session to ensure accurate records.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
