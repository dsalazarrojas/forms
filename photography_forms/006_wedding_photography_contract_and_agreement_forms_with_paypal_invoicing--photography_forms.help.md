# Wedding Photography Contract And Agreement Forms With Paypal Invoicing - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information and details for a wedding photography contract and agreement, including payment terms and client payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field for completeness and clarity before submitting.
3. Make any necessary changes before finalizing your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Photography Contract And Agreement**: A brief overview of the services included in the wedding photography package.
* **Client Details**: A text field for capturing the client's name, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **Billing Information**: A text field for collecting payment details, such as the client's billing name and address.
* **Payment Terms**: A text field for outlining the payment terms and conditions of the contract.
* **Payment Details**: A select one field with options for whether the payment is included in the package or an additional fee.
* **Payment Options**: A select multiple field for selecting payment methods, such as credit card or bank transfer.
* **PayPal Invoicing**: A text field for capturing PayPal payment details, such as the client's PayPal email address.
* **Payment Confirmation**: A time field for scheduling a payment confirmation with the client.
* **Signature**: A text field for capturing the client's digital signature, confirming they reviewed and agreed to the terms of the contract. 

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are complete.
* Ensure accurate and clear communication with the client throughout the submission process.
* Store the completed form for client reference and future communication.
