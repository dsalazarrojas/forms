# photo_restoration_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photo restoration request form is designed to gather information about the photos that require restoration from the team. This form helps the team understand the project requirements and ensures that the photos are restored accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out all the required fields: "Upload Image", "Photo Date", "Photo Upload Status", "Image Resolution", "Photo Type", and "Photo Description", "Photo Location", "Photo Size", "Project Deadline", and "Project Lead".
*   Provide a detailed description of the photo and its condition to help the team understand the restoration requirements.
*   Choose the correct photo upload status to indicate if the image has been uploaded or not.
*   Indicate if the photo resolution is required or not.
*   Check if the photo has a project deadline and provide the date if necessary.
*   If applicable, fill out the project lead's contact information.
*   Choose the correct photo type to indicate if it's a restoration request or not.
*   Provide any additional information about the photo, such as its orientation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Upload Image** (`photo_upload`, `text`, required)
Upload the photo that requires restoration.

### 2. **Photo Date** (`photo_date`, `date`, optional)
Enter the date of the photo.

### 3. **Photo Condition** (`photo_condition`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Choose the condition of the photo from the following options: good, fair, or poor.

### 4. **Restoration Request** (`restoration_request`, `note`, optional)
Enter any additional comments or requests for the restoration process.

### 5. **Photo Upload** (`photo_upload`, `text`, required)
Enter the upload status of the photo.

### 6. **Image Details** (`image_details`, `text`, required)
Enter a detailed description of the photo to help the team understand its condition and requirements.

### 7. **Image Upload Status** (`image_upload_status`, `select_one`, required)
Choose the status of the uploaded image from the following options: uploaded, processing, failed.

### 8. **Image Resolution** (`image_resolution`, `select_multiple`, required)
Choose the resolution of the photo from the following options: 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 9. **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, `date`, optional)
Enter the deadline for the project if it has one.

### 10. **Project Lead Contact** (`project_lead_contact`, `text`, optional)
Enter the project lead's contact information if available.

### 11. **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Choose if the project has a lead from the following options: 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 12. **Photo Type** (`photo_type`, `select_one`, required)
Choose if the photo requires restoration from the following options: 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 13. **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, `text`, optional)
Enter any additional information about the photo.

### 14. **Photo Location** (`photo_location`, `text`, optional)
Enter the location of the photo.

### 15. **Photo Size** (`photo_size`, `text`, optional)
Enter the size of the photo.

### 16. **Photo Orientation** (`photo_orientation`, `select_one`, optional)
Choose the orientation of the photo from the following options: 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 17. **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, `date`, optional)
Enter the deadline for the project if it has one.

### 18. **Project Lead Email** (`project_lead_email`, `email`, optional)
Enter the project lead's email if available.

### 19. **Project Lead Phone** (`project_lead_phone`, `text`, optional)
Enter the project lead's phone number if available.

Note: This form is designed to gather all necessary information about photos to be restored. Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure a successful restoration process.
