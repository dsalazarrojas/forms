# Actor Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from performers for the purpose of granting usage rights for their likenesses in projects or campaigns. It is intended to be used by production teams, agencies, or other parties responsible for handling such permissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full legal name in the **Performer name** field.
2. Provide the title of the project or campaign in the **Project name** field.
3. Describe how your image, audio, or footage will be used in the **Usage description** field.
4. If applicable, specify your compensation details or note if you're granting free usage in the **Compensation details** field.
5. Confirm your agreement to allow the use of your likeness in the **Consent for use** field.
6. Indicate the time period for which you grant usage rights in the **Duration of rights granted** field.
7. Enter your contact information (address, phone number, or email) in the **Contact information** field for any notices or communications.
8. Sign this form by typing your name in the **Name for signature record** field.
9. Record the date on which you sign this agreement in the **Date signed** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performer name**: Your full legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Project name**: The title of the project or campaign for which you grant usage rights.
* **Usage description**: A brief explanation of how your image, audio, or footage will be used.
* **Compensation details**: Any payment terms or conditions you've agreed upon for the usage.
* **Consent for use**: You must agree to allow the use of your likeness for the specified purposes.
* **Duration of rights granted**: Specify the time period for which you grant usage rights.
* **Contact information**: Provide your address, phone number, or email for any communications or notices.
* **Name for signature record**: Type your full legal name as it will be used as a signature record.
* **Date signed**: Record the date you sign this agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure you understand the terms and conditions.
* Keep a copy of this signed agreement for your records.
