# Wedding Flowers Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a wedding event for our flower services. It includes details about the client, the wedding ceremony, the theme and style of the event, and other related information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in your name and email address under "Client Name" and "Client Email" respectively.
- Select the category of the event (Photography, Event, or Wedding).
- Enter the theme/style of the wedding under "Theme/Style".
- Provide the wedding date and time under "Wedding Date" and "Wedding Time".
- Describe the wedding vision and additional information under "Wedding Vision" and "Additional Info" respectively.
- Enter the name of your business and its contact details (phone and email).
- Sign the form electronically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it would appear on the contract.
* **Client Email** (Required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Category** (Required): Choose the type of event you are booking flowers for (Photography, Event, or Wedding).
* **Theme/Style** (Required): Describe the theme or style of the wedding (e.g. traditional, modern, rustic).
* **Wedding Date** (Required): Enter the date of the wedding event.
* **Wedding Time** (Required): Enter the time of the wedding event.
* **Wedding Vision** (Required): Describe your vision for the wedding decorations and flower arrangements.
* **Additional Info** (Optional): Provide any additional information about the event or flowers required.
* **Business Name** (Required): Enter your business name as it would appear on the contract.
* **Venue** (Optional): Enter the venue where the event will take place.
* **Notes** (Optional): Provide any notes about the event or flowers required.
* **Agree to Terms** (Required): Select the option indicating you agree to the terms of service.
* **Signed** (Required): Enter the date and time you signed this form.
* **Form Signature** (Required): Provide your electronic signature.
* **Form Signature 2** (Required): Provide your electronic signature again for confirmation.
* **Business Title** (Required): Enter your business title as it would appear on the contract.
* **Terms of Service** (Required): Enter the terms of service as they would appear on the contract.
* **Signed On** (Required): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Form Signature Time** (Required): Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Business Phone** (Optional): Enter your business phone number for contact.
* **Business Email** (Optional): Enter your business email for contact.
* **Form Signature 3** (Required): This field is not described, please ignore it.
*   **Form Signature 4** (Required): This field is not described, please ignore it.
*   **Form Signature 5** (Required): This field is not described, please ignore it.

Note: Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form. This form requires a digital signature to be completed. If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
