# Wedding Photoshoot Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information and agreements for a wedding photoshoot. It includes basic requests, special add-ons, contact details, and contract signing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you understand all the sections and options.
* Fill out the basic requests section with the necessary information.
* Choose any special add-ons that you require.
* Provide your contact details.
* Select the correct contract signing option.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Requests** (`basic_requests`, select_multiple, required: false): 
This section allows you to list the basic requests you have for the photoshoot. Please select all the requests that apply from the options provided.

* **Special Add-ons** (`special_addons`, select_multiple, required: false): 
This section allows you to choose any special add-ons you require for the photoshoot. Please select all the add-ons that apply from the options provided.

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): 
Please enter your contact details, including your name, address, and any other relevant information.

* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_conditions`, text, required: false): 
This section is for you to provide any additional terms and conditions you have for the photoshoot. Please include any specific requirements or expectations.

* **Photographer Details** (`photographer_details`, select_one, required: false): 
Please select the correct details of the photographer you wish to work with. This will help us ensure that we are on the same page regarding the photoshoot.

* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required: false): 
Please provide your contact information, including your name and email.

* **Contract Signing** (`contract_signing`, select_one, required: false): 
Please select the correct contract signing option to finalize the agreement.

Note: Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is provided. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
