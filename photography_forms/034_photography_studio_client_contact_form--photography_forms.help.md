# Photography Studio Client Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Studio Client Contact Form is designed to gather essential information from clients to ensure a smooth and efficient experience with our photography studio. This form helps us understand your needs, preferences, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Scroll down to the first page, "User Inputs".
2. Fill out the "user_inputs" field with your name or the name of the person you're contacting.
3. Move to the "Contact Info" field and provide your email address or phone number.
4. On the next page, "Session Info", select your preferred date and time for the session.
5. In "Preferences", let us know about any specific requirements or requests you have.
6. In "Upload Files", you can upload any supporting documents or images.
7. In "Message", type any additional comments or instructions.
8. Move to the "Select One" field, "How did you hear about us?", and choose your answer (e.g., "Yes", "No", or "Other").
9. In "Select Multiple", choose the services you'd like to book (e.g., "Yes", "No", or "Other").
10. Fill out any remaining fields on the subsequent pages, "Text input", "Integer input", "Decimal input", "Select One 2", "Select Multiple 2", "Date 2", "Time 2", "Email 2", "Phone 2", "Text input 2", "Integer input 2", "Decimal input 2", and "Note".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Inputs** (`user_inputs`, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the person you're contacting.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your email address or phone number.
- **Session Info** (`session_info`, text, optional): Select your preferred date and time for the session.
- **Preferences** (`preferences`, text, optional): Let us know about any specific requirements or requests you have.
- **Upload Files** (`upload_files`, text, optional): Upload any supporting documents or images.
- **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Type any additional comments or instructions.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for the session.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose a time for the session.
- **How did you hear about us?** (`select_one`, select, required): Choose how you heard about us (e.g., "Yes", "No", or "Other").
- **What services do you want?** (`select_multiple`, select multiple, required): Select the services you'd like to book (e.g., "Yes", "Yes", or "Other").
- **Text input** (`text`, text, optional): Fill out this field for any additional comments.
- **Integer input** (`integer`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value.
- **Decimal input** (`decimal`, number, optional): Enter a decimal value.
- **How many people?** (`select_one_2`, select, required): Choose how many people will be attending (e.g., "Yes", "No", or "Other").
- **Select multiple** (`select_multiple_2`, select multiple, optional): Select multiple options (e.g., "Yes", "Yes", or "Other").
- **Date 2** (`date_2`, date, optional): Select a date.
- **Time 2** (`time_2`, time, optional): Choose a time.
- **Email 2** (`email_2`, email, optional): Provide an email address.
- **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, text, optional): Enter a phone number.
- **Text input 2** (`text_2`, text, optional): Fill out this field for any additional comments.
- **Integer input 2** (`integer_2`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value.
- **Decimal input 2** (`decimal_2`, number, optional): Enter a decimal value.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Type any additional comments.
