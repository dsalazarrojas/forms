# photography_exhibit_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photography exhibit approval form is designed to gather essential information from both the artist and the exhibition team to facilitate the review and approval process of photography exhibits. It is a straightforward and organized guide to ensure a smooth and transparent communication process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name as the photography artist in the "Photography Artist" field.
2. Enter the title of your proposal in the "Proposal Title" field.
3. Describe your proposal in the "Proposal Description" field.
4. If applicable, provide the date of your proposal in the "Proposal Date" field.
5. Similarly, provide the date of the exhibition in the "Exhibition Date" field.
6. Enter your name as the artist in the "Artist Name" field.
7. Provide your contact information in the "Artist Contact" field.
8. Enter the address of your gallery in the "Gallery Address" field.
9. Provide the address of the exhibition location in the "Exhibition Address" field.
10. Enter the contact information of the exhibition in the "Exhibition Contact" field.
11. Upload any relevant files for the proposal in the "Proposal Files" field.
12. Check the "Artist Agreement" field to indicate your understanding of the terms.
13. Check the "Exhibition Agreement" field to indicate the exhibition's understanding of the terms.
14. If necessary, provide any review comments in the "Review Comments" field.
15. Select the "Approval Status" from the dropdown menu to indicate the approval status of your proposal.
16. If the proposal is approved, enter the name of the approver in the "Approved By" field.
17. If necessary, provide the date of approval in the "Approved Date" field.
18. If rejected, provide the reason for rejection in the "Rejected Reason" field.
19. Enter any exhibition terms in the "Exhibition Terms" field.
20. Check the "Artist Acknowledgement" field to indicate your understanding of the terms.
21. Check the "Exhibition Acknowledgement" field to indicate the exhibition's understanding of the terms.
22. Provide your signature in the "Exhibition Signature" field.
23. Provide your signature in the "Artist Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photography Artist (1)**: This is your name as the photographer or artist submitting the proposal.
* **Proposal Title (2)**: Enter a concise title for your proposal, ideally summarizing the main idea or subject of your work.
* **Proposal Description (4)**: Describe your proposal in detail, including the concept, goals, and any relevant information.
* **Proposal Date (5)**: If you are submitting a proposal, provide the date of submission. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Exhibition Date (6)**: If you are submitting a proposal for a specific exhibition, provide the date of the exhibition. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Artist Name (7)**: This is your name as the artist submitting the proposal.
* **Artist Contact (8)**: Provide your contact information, including your email, phone number, or any other relevant details.
* **Gallery Address (9)**: If you have a gallery or studio, enter its address here.
* **Exhibition Address (10)**: If the exhibition is taking place at a specific location, enter its address here.
* **Exhibition Contact (11)**: Provide the contact information of the exhibition team or organizer.
* **Proposal Files (12)**: Upload any relevant files or documents related to your proposal, such as images, videos, or text files.
* **Artist Agreement (13)**: Check this field to indicate your understanding and acceptance of the terms and conditions.
* **Exhibition Agreement (14)**: Check this field to indicate the exhibition's understanding and acceptance of the terms and conditions.
* **Review Comments (15)**: If you have any comments or feedback regarding the review process, provide them here.
* **Approval Status (16)**: Select the status of your proposal from the dropdown menu, indicating whether it is approved, rejected, or pending.
* **Approved By (17)**: If your proposal is approved, enter the name of the person or team that approved it.
* **Approved Date (18)**: If your proposal is approved, provide the date of approval.
* **Rejected Reason (19)**: If your proposal is rejected, provide a brief explanation of why it was not approved.
* **Exhibition Terms (20)**: Enter any terms or conditions specific to the exhibition, such as deadlines or submission guidelines.
* **Artist Acknowledgement (21)**: Check this field to indicate your understanding and acceptance of the exhibition's terms and conditions.
* **Exhibition Acknowledgement (22)**: Check this field to indicate the exhibition's understanding and acceptance of the artist's terms and conditions.
* **Exhibition Signature (23)**: Provide your signature to confirm your involvement in the exhibition.
* **Artist Signature (24)**: Provide your signature to confirm your understanding and acceptance of the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any confusion or miscommunication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the exhibition team.
* This form is designed to be used for both artists and exhibitions, so feel free to modify it as needed to suit your specific needs.
