# Basic Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from potential clients who are interested in hiring a photographer. It helps us understand their needs and preferences for photography services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last names in the "First name" and "Last name" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email address" field. We promise not to share your email address.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
4. Select the type of photography services you are interested in from the "What type of photography are you interested in?" dropdown menu.
5. If you selected "Other," please describe the type of photography service you are interested in.
6. If applicable, provide a desired event date, event location, and a description of your event.
7. Optionally, select a preferred photography style, approximate number of final photos needed, desired editing level, color preferences (if applicable), budget range, and delivery format for the photos.
8. Choose your intended use for the photographs and your preferred time for us to contact you.
9. Provide any additional message or special requests in the "Additional message or special requests" field.
10. Finally, confirm your consent to receive communication about your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last name**: A text field to enter your last name.
* **Email address**: A text field to enter your email address, which we will not share.
* **Phone number**: A text field to enter your phone number.
* **What type of photography are you interested in?**: A dropdown menu with options for different photography services (e.g., Portraits, Weddings, Events, etc.).
* **If other, please describe**: A text field to provide more details if you selected "Other" in the previous question.
* **Desired event date**: A date field to enter the date of your event, if applicable.
* **Event location**: A text field to enter the location of your event, if applicable.
* **Please describe your event**: A text field to enter a description of your event.
* **Preferred photography style**: A dropdown menu to select a preferred photography style.
* **Approximate number of final photos needed**: A dropdown menu to select the number of photos you need.
* **Desired editing level**: A dropdown menu to select the desired level of editing.
* **Color preferences (if applicable)**: A text field to enter any color preferences for your photos.
* **Budget range**: A dropdown menu to select a budget range.
* **Preferred delivery format for photos**: A dropdown menu to select how you prefer to receive your photos.
* **Intended use for photographs**: A dropdown menu to select the use of the photos.
* **Additional message or special requests**: A text field to provide any additional message or special requests.
* **Best time to contact you**: A dropdown menu to select a preferred time to contact you.
* **I consent to receive communication about my inquiry**: A dropdown menu to confirm your consent to receive communication about your inquiry.
