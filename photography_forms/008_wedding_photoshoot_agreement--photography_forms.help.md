# Wedding Photoshoot Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary details for a wedding photoshoot. It includes information about the photoshoot, the couple, and any additional agreements or terms that need to be met. Completing this form will help ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for both parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Photoshoot Details**: Enter the details of the photoshoot, including the date and time.
2.  **Couple Info**: Enter the couple's information, including their names and any other relevant details.
3.  **Photographer Info**: Enter the photographer's information, including their name and contact details.
4.  **Location**: Enter the location of the photoshoot.
5.  **Photographer Notes**: Add any additional notes or instructions for the photographer.
6.  **Agreement Terms**: Review and select the agreement terms that apply to the photoshoot.
7.  **Terms and Conditions**: Review and select the terms and conditions that apply to the photoshoot.
8.  **Contact Details**: Enter the contact information for the couple or their representative.
