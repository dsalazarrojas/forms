# Risk Waiver Form And Publicity Release - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Waiver Form And Publicity Release is a document that must be completed by individuals who have participated in a photo shoot or event. It collects their personal details and their consent for their images to be used for publicity purposes. The form is essential for legal and administrative reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Provide the required information accurately and completely.
2. Check all applicable boxes to agree to the terms of the publicity release and risk waiver.
3. Sign the form with a witness who is present.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text, Required): This field is for the individual's name.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, Select One, Required): This field should be completed with the relationship of the individual with the person or organization responsible for the photo shoot or event. Possible options are: Parent, Child, Friend.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Required): This field is for the individual's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Required): This field is for the individual's email address.
* **Publicity Agreement** (`publicity_agreement`, Select Multiple, Required): Check the boxes if you agree to the terms of the publicity release.
* **Risk Waiver Agreement** (`risk_waiver_agreement`, Select Multiple, Required): Check the boxes if you agree to the terms of the risk waiver.
* **Publicity Release** (`publicity_release`, Select One, Required): Check the box if you agree to the publicity release.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the photo shoot or event.
* **Time** (`time`, Time, Required): Enter the time of the photo shoot or event.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, Note, Required): Sign this field with a witness who is present.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the witness.
* **Witness Phone** (`witness_phone`, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the witness.
* **Witness Email** (`witness_email`, Email, Required): Enter the email address of the witness.
