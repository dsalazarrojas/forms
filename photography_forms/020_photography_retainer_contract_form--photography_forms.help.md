# photography_retainer_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the creation of photography retainer contracts between photographers and their clients. It helps ensure that all necessary details are captured and agreed upon, ensuring a clear understanding of the relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in your personal details, including First Name, Last Name, Email, Address, City, and Zip.
- Choose the Retainer Type: Hourly, Package, or Flat Fee.
- Set the Retainer Start Date, Contract Start Date, and dates for the Photo Shoots.
- Select the Payment Method: Credit/Debit Card, Invoice, or ACH.
- Specify the Number of Photo Shoots, Photo Shoot Duration, and Photo Shoot Frequency (if applicable).
- Choose how you would like to receive your photos: through Photo Delivery.
- Optionally, you can add notes and terms for your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, required): Your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (Text, required): Your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (Email, required): The email address to be used for communication.
* **Address** (Text, required): Your address for service delivery.
* **City** (Text, required): The city where services are to be rendered.
* **Zip** (Text, required): The zip code for delivery and billing purposes.
* **Retainer Type** (Select One, required): Choose between Hourly, Package, or Flat Fee for the type of service you require.
* **Retainer Start Date** (Date, required): The date on which the retainer contract begins.
* **Contract Start Date** (Date, required): The date on which the contract begins.
* **Contract End Date** (Date, required): The date on which the contract ends.
* **Contract Notes** (Text, required): Notes about the contract terms and conditions.
* **Payment Terms** (Text, required): Specify the payment terms for the service.
* **Payment Terms Frequency** (Text, required): Describe the frequency of payments.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, required): Choose how you would like to make payments: Credit/Debit Card, Invoice, or ACH.
* **Payment Terms Frequency Description** (Text, required): Describe the payment terms and conditions.
* **Number of Photo Shoots** (Number, required): Specify the number of photo shoots included in the contract.
* **Photo Shoot Duration** (Text, required): Describe the duration of each photo shoot.
* **Photo Shoot Frequency** (Text, required): Describe how often photo shoots will be conducted.
* **Photo Delivery** (Text, required): Specify how you would like to receive photos.
* **Photo Editing** (Text, optional): Describe any photo editing services required.
* **Photo Storage** (Text, optional): Specify how photos are to be stored.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Text, optional): Include any additional terms and conditions not covered in the other fields.

Note: This is a general guide and may need to be tailored according to specific business requirements and needs. Please consult with a lawyer or business advisor for specific advice on creating a retainer contract.
