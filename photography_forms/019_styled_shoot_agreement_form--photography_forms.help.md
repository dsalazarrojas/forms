# Styled Shoot Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for styled shoots. A styled shoot is a photo shoot where a photographer and a planner collaborate to create a visually appealing and well-planned photo shoot. This form helps to capture the necessary details and permissions required for a successful styled shoot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the necessary information in each field as prompted.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, which are indicated by an asterisk (\*) next to the field label.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the field-by-field explanations below for more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer**: Enter the name of the photographer(s) involved in the shoot. This could be you, if you are the photographer, or someone you are collaborating with.
* **Planner**: Enter the name of the planner(s) involved in the shoot. This could be you, if you are the planner, or someone you are collaborating with.
* **Venue**: Enter the location where the shoot will take place. This could be a specific building, a park, or any other location that is relevant to the shoot.
* **Creative Vendor**: Enter the name of any creative vendors involved in the shoot, such as a hair stylist or makeup artist.
* **Shoot Details**: Enter any additional details about the shoot, such as the number of people involved, the type of equipment needed, or any other relevant information.
* **Image Usage Permissions**: Describe how the images from the shoot will be used. For example, will they be used online, in print, or for social media?
* **Roles**: Select the roles of the people involved in the shoot. This helps to clarify their responsibilities and expectations.
* **Shoot Start Date**: Enter the date and time when the shoot will begin.
* **Shoot End Date**: Enter the date and time when the shoot will end.
* **Shoot Location**: Enter the location of the shoot. This could be the same as or different from the venue.
* **Image Usage**: Select the ways in which the images from the shoot will be used. For example, all, online, social media, print, or web.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the shoot.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will help the planner and photographer to contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This will help the planner and photographer to contact you with any questions or updates.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter any tools or equipment that will be used during the shoot.
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for the form. This can be used to track the shoot and its associated forms.
* **Output File**: Enter the name of the file where the shoot information will be exported to.
* **Category**: Enter the category for the shoot. This can help to filter and organize the shoots.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the shoot. This can help to summarize the shoot and its goals.
* **Title**: Enter a title for the shoot. This can help to identify the shoot and its purpose.
* **Category 2**: Enter another category for the shoot. This can help to filter and organize the shoots.
* **Assigned Tool 2**: Enter another tool or equipment that will be used during the shoot.
* **Description 2**: Enter another brief description of the shoot. This can help to summarize the shoot and its goals.
* **Form Ids 2**: Enter another unique ID for the form. This can be used to track the shoot and its associated forms.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that the shoot is properly documented and that all parties are aware of their responsibilities.
* Double-check the information entered in each field to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the field-by-field explanations above for more information.
