# Daycare Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Photo Release Form is a document that parents or guardians sign to grant permission for the daycare or childcare facility to use and share photos taken of their child. This form helps ensure that parents are aware of how their child's photos will be used and shared, and it's a requirement for the daycare to maintain a good relationship with its clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the photo release form, which should be the same as the name of the form.
2. Enter the date when the photos were taken.
3. Enter the name and any other relevant details of the person or organization that took the photos.
4. Enter the purpose or reason for taking the photos.
5. Enter the location where the photos were taken.
6. Enter your name and contact information as the contact person for the photo release.
7. Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms of the photo release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title** (1): Enter the title of the photo release form, which should be the same as the name of the form.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
- **Date Taken** (2): Enter the date when the photos were taken.
  - Type: Date
  - Required: No
- **Photo Taken By** (3): Enter the name and any other relevant details of the person or organization that took the photos.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
- **Photo Taken For** (4): Enter the purpose or reason for taking the photos.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
- **Release Type** (5): Choose one of the following options:
  - Yes: Grant permission for the daycare to use and share the photos.
  - No: Do not grant permission for the daycare to use and share the photos.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: No
- **Location** (6): Enter the location where the photos were taken.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
- **Contact** (7): Enter your name and contact information as the contact person for the photo release.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
- **Parent Signature** (8): Sign and date this field to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms of the photo release.
  - Type: Note (Signature)
  - Required: No
- **Daycare Signature** (9): Sign and date this field to acknowledge that the daycare has read and understood the terms of the photo release.
  - Type: Note (Signature)
  - Required: No

## Tips
- Please make sure to read and understand the terms of the photo release before signing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the daycare contact person.
- This form is a requirement for the daycare to maintain a good relationship with its clients.
- Please sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms of the photo release.
