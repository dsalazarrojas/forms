# Image Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Image Submission Form is designed for photographers to submit their images for review, exhibition, or other purposes. This form collects information about the photographer and the submitted image, helping us to process and review submissions efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information about yourself and your submission.
3. Review your submission before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Submit Your Image** (`submission_form`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your image.
- **image description** (`image_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your image, including any notable features or context. (Note: This field is optional, but it helps us understand your image better.)
- **photographer name** (`photographer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the photographer of the submitted image.
- **photographer email** (`photographer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
- **photographer phone** (`photographer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication purposes.
- **photographer website** (`photographer_website`, text, optional): Enter your website or portfolio URL, which will help us learn more about you and your work.
- **title** (`image_title`, text, required): Enter a title for your image, which will be used for identification and categorization.
- **url** (`image_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your image on an external platform, if applicable.
- **tags** (`image_tags`, select multiple, optional): Select up to three tags that best describe your image, which will help us categorize and filter submissions.
- **license** (`image_license`, select one, optional): Select whether your image is available for use under a specific license (yes or no), which will help us understand the image's usage rights.

## Tips
- Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information to avoid delays in processing your submission.
- If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact us before submitting your form.
- We appreciate your contributions and thank you for considering submitting your image!
