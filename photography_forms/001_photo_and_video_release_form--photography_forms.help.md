# Photo And Video Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a photo and video release from clients, ensuring they have given their consent for the photographer to use their photo and video content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill out each field with the corresponding information and sign where required. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and accurately. If you need help with a specific field, refer to the explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Details

* **Client Details** (`client_details`, `text`, `required: false`): Fill in your name, and any other contact information you may have.
* **Photo And Video Details** (`photo_and_video_details`, `text`, `required: false`): Describe the photo and video shoot or event you are attending/participating in.
* **Release Type** (`release_type`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Choose whether you give permission for the photographer to use your photos and videos (Yes/No).
* **How To Use** (`how_to_use`, `text`, `required: false`): Briefly explain how you want your photos and videos to be used.
* **Photo Credit** (`photo_credit`, `text`, `required: false`): Provide the name and contact information for the photographer or person responsible for capturing your photo and video content.
* **Photo Usage** (`photo_usage`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Specify how you want your photos to be used (e.g. social media, website, print, etc.).
* **Video Usage** (`video_usage`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Specify how you want your videos to be used (e.g. social media, website, etc.).
* **Other Uses** (`other_uses`, `text`, `required: false`): If there are any other specific uses you would like your photos and videos to be used for, please specify.
* **Other** (`other`, `text`, `required: false`): If there is anything else you would like to mention or request, please include it here.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, `required: false`): Sign the form to confirm your consent.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, `required: false`): Date when you signed the form.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, `required: false`): Time when you signed the form.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `required: false`): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `required: false`): Your phone number.
* **Other Contact Info** (`other_contact`, `text`, `required: false`): If you have any other contact information, please provide it.
* **Other Contact Method** (`other_contact_method`, `text`, `required: false`): If you have any other contact method, please provide it.
* **Other Contact Email** (`other_contact_email`, `text`, `required: false`): If you have any other contact email, please provide it.
* **Other Contact Phone** (`other_contact_phone`, `text`, `required: false`): If you have any other contact phone, please provide it.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, `required: false`): Sign the form to confirm your consent.
* **Client Date** (`client_date`, `date`, `required: false`): Date when you signed the form as a client.
* **Client Time** (`client_time`, `time`, `required: false`): Time when you signed the form as a client.
* **Photographer Signature** (`photographer_signature`, `text`, `required: false`): Sign the form to confirm that the photographer has obtained your consent.
* **Photographer Date** (`photographer_date`, `date`, `required: false`): Date when you, as the photographer, signed the form.
* **Photographer Time** (`photographer_time`, `time`, `required: false`): Time when you, as the photographer, signed the form.
