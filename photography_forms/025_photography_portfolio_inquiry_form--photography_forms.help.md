# photography_portfolio_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photography portfolio inquiry form collects information from users for the organization's marketing or sales team to get started on a new project or client engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and other contact information (Phone, Email, Website).
2. Select the services you are interested in (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
3. Let us know if you are interested in location-specific work (Yes/No).
4. Fill out any additional information you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer** (`photographer`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be represented in our portfolio.
* **Services** (`services`, select_one, optional): Select the services you are interested in, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, optional): Check the locations where you are interested in working, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Contact Info** (`contact`, number, optional): Enter your contact information (phone number) for us to get in touch with you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to get in touch with you.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your website URL for us to visit your portfolio online.
