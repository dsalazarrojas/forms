# Standard Wedding Photography Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a contract for a wedding photography service, used to collect information about the client's wedding and ensure a clear understanding of the photography services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the required fields with your details.
2. Choose your preferred photo package from the dropdown menu.
3. Read through the terms and conditions and acknowledge that you understand and agree to them.
4. Sign the form to confirm your contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`client_info`):
	* This field is used to collect your name and contact information.
* **Ceremony Details** (`ceremony_details`):
	* Please provide details about the ceremony, including the date, time, location, and any specific requirements you have.
* **Reception Details** (`reception_details`):
	* Please provide details about the reception, including the date, time, location, and any specific requirements you have.
* **Photo Package** (`photo_package`):
	* Choose your preferred photo package from the dropdown menu. The options are:
		+ Standard
		+ Deluxe
		+ Premium
	* Please select the package that best suits your photography needs.
* **Wedding Details** (`wedding_details`):
	* Please provide any additional information about your wedding that will help us capture its uniqueness.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`):
	* Please read through this section carefully and acknowledge that you understand and agree to the terms of the photography services.
* **Signature** (`signature`):
	* Please sign the form to confirm your contract.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We recommend reviewing the terms and conditions carefully before signing the contract.
