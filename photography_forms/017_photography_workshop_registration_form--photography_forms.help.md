# Photography Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from participants to manage workshop attendance and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the form accurately with your full name, email, and phone number.
2. Select a payment method (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer) and enter the payment amount.
3. Add any additional workshop information or notes, if necessary.
4. Click the submit button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or government-issued ID.
* **Email** (required): Enter your valid email address that you use for correspondence.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Payment Method** (required): Choose your preferred method of payment from the options provided (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Amount** (required): Enter the exact amount you wish to pay for the workshop. Ensure this is in the correct format to avoid processing issues.
* **Workshop Info** (optional): If you have any additional information or requests regarding the workshop, add it here.
* **Participant Notes** (optional): If you have any personal requests or preferences for the workshop, enter them here.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your registration and complete the process.
