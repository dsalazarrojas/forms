# Jennifer Rood Photography Client Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from clients for their photography sessions with Jennifer Rood Photography.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide basic information about yourself.
2. Specify your session details, including any specific requirements or expectations.
3. Enter your session time if applicable.
4. List the equipment you plan to bring or require.
5. Describe the photo details, such as the type of photos you want taken or any specific locations you'd like to capture.
6. Enter any release information, such as model releases or permissions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Basic Information** (`basic_information`, text, Optional): This field is for entering your basic information.
- **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, Optional): Provide any specific details or requirements you have for your session.
- **Session Time** (`session_details_2`, Time, Optional): If you have a specific time scheduled, enter it here.
- **Equipment** (`equipment`, text, Optional): List any equipment you will need or plan to bring.
- **Photo Details** (`photo_details`, text, Optional): Describe the type of photos you want taken, such as posed or candid shots.
- **Release Information** (`release_info`, text, Optional): Enter any model releases or permissions related to your session.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- Make sure to include all necessary details to ensure a smooth and successful session.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact form support.
- Note that this form does not store sensitive or personal information and is used for internal purposes only.
