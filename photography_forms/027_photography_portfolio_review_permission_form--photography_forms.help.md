# photography_portfolio_review_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photography_portfolio_review_permission_form is used for requesting permission from a photographer to review and access their portfolio. This form helps in ensuring that the review process is properly initiated and acknowledged by both parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required details.
2. Ensure that you have attached all necessary documents such as photos and any other required files.
3. Click on the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer Information**: This field requires the name and other relevant details of the photographer. Please enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the contact details of the photographer, such as their email address and phone number.
* **Photo Portfolio**: Attach relevant photos from the photographer's portfolio.
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about the photographer's work.
* **Permission**: Select one of the options to indicate what permission you are requesting. Please choose one of the options based on your needs.
* **Comments**: This field is optional and can be used to provide any additional comments about the photographer.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is optional and can be left blank. It is used to assign a specific tool to the photographer.
* **Submitted**: This field displays the date when the form was submitted.
