# Photography Tour Client Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Photography Tour Client Survey is a form designed to collect feedback and data from clients who have participated in a photography tour. This information will help us improve our tour offerings and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each question carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Enter your responses in the text fields, select from options for select-one fields, or enter dates and times for date and time fields.
3. If you have any feedback or comments, please use the text field provided.
4. Finally, submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Photography Tour Client Survey** (`form_start`): This is the introduction page of the survey. Please take a moment to read through the instructions.
* **What was the title of the tour you took?** (`tour_title`): Please enter the title of the tour you participated in.
* **Which type of tour was this?** (`tour_type`): Select from the options provided:
	+ Landscape
	+ Wildlife
	+ City
	+ Nature
* **How would you rate the tour?** (`tour_rating`): Enter a numerical value to rate the tour.
* **How satisfied were you with the tour?** (`tour_satisfaction`): Select from the options provided:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Do you have any feedback or comments about the tour?** (`feedback`): Please use this text field to provide any feedback or comments you have about your tour experience.
* **Do you want to hear from us about your feedback?** (`contact_us`): If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your contact information below.
* **What is your email?** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of the tour** (`date`): Enter the date you participated in the tour.
* **Start time of the tour** (`start_time`): Enter the start time of the tour.
* **End time of the tour** (`end_time`): Enter the end time of the tour.
* **Length of the tour in hours** (`tour_length`): Enter the length of the tour in hours.
* **How would you rate the photography guide?** (`guide_rating`): Select from the options provided:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Fair
	+ Very Bad
* **What is the name of the photography guide?** (`guide_name`): Enter the name of the photography guide who led your tour.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve our services.
