# Black Friday Photography Booking Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the necessary information from clients for booking photography sessions during the Black Friday sales period.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Book Your Black Friday Photography Session --**: This is the introductory page of the form, which provides a brief message about limited availability and special Black Friday rates.
2.  **First Name**: Enter your first name as per your identification documents.
3.  **Last Name**: Enter your last name as per your identification documents.
4.  **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for correspondence and updates.
5.  **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you.
6.  **Type of Session**: Select the type of session you wish to book from the provided options.
7.  **Preferred Session Date**: Choose a date that suits you best from the available options or provide an alternate date if not listed.
8.  **Preferred Session Time**: Select the preferred time for your session from the available options.
9.  **Session Duration**: Select the duration of your session from the provided options.
10. **Session Location Type**: Choose the type of location for your session from the given options.
11. **Session Location Details**: Provide a description or address of the location for your session.
12. **Number of People in Session**: Enter the number of people participating in the session.
13. **Special Requests or Notes**: If you have any props, theme ideas, or specific shot preferences, provide them here.
14. **Package Selection**: Choose one of the available Black Friday offers for your session.
15. **Deposit Payment Confirmation**: Confirm if you have already paid 50% of the deposit or if you will pay at the time of booking or require a payment plan.
16. **Preferred Payment Method**: Select the preferred method of payment from the provided options.
17. **How did you hear about us?**: If you've visited our website, social media, or any other means, let us know so we can improve our services.
18.  **Booking Request Date**: Enter the date on which you are requesting to book your session.
