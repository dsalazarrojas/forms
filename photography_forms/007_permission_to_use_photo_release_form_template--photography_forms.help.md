## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the releasor_details field with the details of the person or organization releasing the photo.
2. Fill out the releasee_details field with the details of the person or organization granting the release.
3. Provide photo_details about the photo being released.
4. Enter permission_details about the photo's usage.
5. Fill out the releaser fields with the details of the people responsible for releasing and granting the release.
6. Enter the signature fields with the required number of signatures.
7. Enter the date fields with the corresponding dates.
8. Sign and date the agreement fields.
