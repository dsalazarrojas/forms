# Model Release Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Model Release Agreement is a form used to obtain the necessary releases from models (individuals who are the subjects of a photograph) before their images are used for any commercial purpose. This form is used to ensure that all parties involved have agreed to the terms and conditions of the photo shoot, including the use of their likeness, image, or other identifying characteristics.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **User Name** in the first field. This is the name of the person who is granting the release.
2. Provide your **Date of Birth** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`. This is required if you are under 18 years old.
3. Enter your **Contact Information** in the field below. This can include your address, phone number, and email address.
4. Select your **Relationship** with the model. You can choose from the options **Parent**, **Guardian**, or **Other**.
5. Check the box next to **Release Agreement** to indicate that you have read and understand the model release agreement.
6. If you are a parent or guardian, enter your **Model Name** to confirm the name of the individual who is granting the release.
7. Enter your **Email** address in the format `user@example.com`. This will be used to contact you if needed.
8. Enter your **Phone** number in the format `(123) 456-7890`.
9. Enter your **Address** including your city, state, and zip code.
10. Select your **State** from the options provided. This is the state where you currently reside.
11. Enter your **Country**. Select **USA** or **Canada** if you are in one of these countries.
12. Check the box next to **Agree to Terms** to acknowledge that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the photo shoot.
13. Enter your **Signed By** name to confirm that you have signed the release.
14. Enter the date you signed the release in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
15. Enter the time you signed the release in the format `12:00 PM`.
16. Enter the **Model Release Agreement** in the field provided. This is a confirmation that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the photo shoot.
17. Enter the name of the **Model Name** to confirm the name of the individual who is granting the release.
18. If you are a parent or guardian, enter the name of the **Parent/Guardian Name**.
19. Enter the **Parent/Guardian Address** if you are a parent or guardian.
20. Enter the **Parent/Guardian Phone** number if you are a parent or guardian.
21. Enter the **Parent/Guardian Email** address if you are a parent or guardian.

**Tips**

* Make sure to enter all information accurately and completely.
* Review the terms and conditions of the photo shoot before checking the box next to **Agree to Terms**.
* If you are a parent or guardian, ensure that you are signing on behalf of the model and have their permission to do so.
* Keep a copy of the signed release for your records.
* Contact us if you have any questions or concerns about the form or the photo shoot.
