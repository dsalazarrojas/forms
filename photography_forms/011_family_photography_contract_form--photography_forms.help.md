# Family Photography Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a document for capturing essential details between the photographer and the client before, during, and after a photography session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are filled in.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure that your information is correct.
3. Save and submit the form to complete the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the form, which will serve as the title for the contract.
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name and contact information for communication purposes.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you would like to be contacted by the photographer for any updates or changes regarding your session.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not want to be contacted for updates or changes.
* **Services** (`services`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose the services you would like to be included in your session (e.g., portrait photography, event coverage, etc.).
* **Description of Services** (`services_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the services you've selected.
* **Pricing** (`pricing`, `text`, required: false): Enter the agreed-upon price for your photography session.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you agree to the payment terms and conditions.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not agree to the terms.
* **Description of Payment Terms** (`payment_terms_description`, `text`, required: false): If you select 'No' for payment terms, provide a brief explanation for your selection.
* **Contract Expires** (`contract_expires`, `date`, required: false): Date on which the contract will expire.
* **Contract Signed Date** (`contract_signed`, `date`, required: false): Date when the contract was signed.
* **Contract Signed** (`contract_signed`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select 'Yes' to confirm that you have signed the contract.
	+ Select 'No' if you have not signed the contract.
* **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose the type of contract that applies to your session (e.g., single session, ongoing sessions, etc.).
