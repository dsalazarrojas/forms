# Pet Photography Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Photography Contact Form is designed to gather information from pet owners who are interested in scheduling a pet photography session. This form helps pet photographers and related businesses capture the necessary details to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for both the pet and the photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Describe your pet's details in the "Pet Details" field.
5. Select the date of the photography session in the "Session Date" field.
6. Choose the time of the photography session in the "Session Time" field.
7. Enter the name of the photographer you are working with in the "Photographer" field.
8. Specify the type of pet you are bringing to the session in the "Pet Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Pet Details** (note, required/optional): Describe your pet's breed, age, size, and any unique characteristics.
* **Session Date** (date, required/optional): Choose the date you would like to schedule the photography session.
* **Session Time** (time, required/optional): Select the time you would like to schedule the photography session.
* **Photographer** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the photographer you are working with.
* **Pet Type** (text, required/optional): Specify the type of pet you are bringing to the session (e.g., cat, dog, bird, etc.).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about the date or time, please contact us to discuss options.
* If you are working with a specific photographer, please make sure to provide their name to ensure a smooth experience.
