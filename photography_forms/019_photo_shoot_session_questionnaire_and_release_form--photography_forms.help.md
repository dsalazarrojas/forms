# Photo Shoot Session Questionnaire And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a photo shoot session, including contact information, photo shoot details, and release forms. It helps both the photographer and the client to communicate effectively and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name and email address on the top section of the form.
2. Choose the type of photo shoot you are interested in (e.g., landscape, portrait, still life, fashion, commercial, and event).
3. Provide the location of the shoot, including the date and start and end times.
4. Describe the photo shoot in detail, including any special notes or additional services required.
5. Provide the contact information of the person responsible for the shoot, including their name, phone number, and email.
6. Choose whether you would like to release your photos, and provide any additional notes.
7. Finally, review and submit the form to ensure all information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1): Enter your full name to identify yourself as the client.
* **Email** (2): Enter your email address for future communication and notifications.
* **Phone** (4): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Location** (5): Enter the physical location of the shoot.
* **Date of Shoot** (6): Enter the date of the shoot, in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2023-02-20).
* **Location of Shoot** (7): Enter the exact location of the shoot, including address and GPS coordinates.
* **Type of Photo** (8): Choose the type of photo shoot (e.g., landscape, portrait, still life, fashion, commercial, and event).
* **Release Form** (9): Sign and date the release form to grant permission for the photographer to use your photos.
* **Photo Release** (10): Choose whether you would like to release your photos, and provide any additional notes.
* **Photo Release Note** (11): If you choose to release your photos, provide any additional notes or conditions.
* **Contact Person** (12): Enter the name of the person responsible for the shoot.
* **Contact Phone** (13): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (14): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Photo Shoot Date** (15): Enter the date of the photo shoot, in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2023-02-20).
* **Start Time** (16): Enter the start time of the shoot, in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00).
* **End Time** (17): Enter the end time of the shoot, in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00).
* **Duration** (18): Enter the duration of the shoot, in hours (e.g., 2 hours).
* **Location of Shoot** (19): Enter the exact location of the shoot, including address and GPS coordinates.
* **Description of Shoot** (20): Describe the photo shoot in detail, including any specific requirements or expectations.
* **Weather** (21): Enter the expected weather conditions during the shoot.
* **Special Notes** (22): Enter any additional notes or requirements for the shoot.
* **Additional Photos** (23): Enter any additional photos or requirements for the shoot.
