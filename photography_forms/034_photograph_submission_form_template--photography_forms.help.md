# photograph_submission_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows photographers to submit their photographs for a specific event or event series. It is meant for photographers who have captured images for an event that they would like to submit for publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and title of the photographer.
2. Enter the name of the event or event series you are submitting for.
3. Add a URL to the photograph you are submitting.
4. Enter a title for the photograph.
5. Provide a description of the photograph.
6. Add a keyword or tag for the photograph.
7. Enter the date and time the photograph was taken.
8. Add the time of day the photograph was taken (optional).
9. Enter your email address.
10. Enter your phone number.
11. Add any additional notes about the photograph.
12. Upload or provide a URL to the photograph file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **photographer_name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your name as the photographer.
* **event_name** (Text, Required: False): Enter the name of the event or event series you are submitting for.
* **photo_url** (Text, Required: False): Add a URL to the photograph you are submitting.
* **photo_title** (Text, Required: False): Enter a title for the photograph.
* **photo_description** (Text, Required: False): Provide a description of the photograph.
* **photo_tag** (Text, Required: False): Add a keyword or tag for the photograph.
* **photo_date_time** (Date, Required: False): Enter the date and time the photograph was taken.
* **photo_time** (Time, Required: False): Add the time of day the photograph was taken.
* **photographer_email** (Email, Required: False): Enter your email address.
* **photographer_phone** (Text, Required: False): Enter your phone number.
* **photo_note** (Note, Required: False): Add any additional notes about the photograph.
* **photo_file** (Text, Required: False): Upload or provide a URL to the photograph file.

## Tips
* Please make sure to submit high-quality photographs.
* Please ensure that the photograph is relevant to the event or event series.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
