# Location Scouting Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a quote for location scouting services for your photography project. Please fill out the form with the required information to receive a detailed quote for your project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Select the relevant options for "Production Type", "Location Scouting Services", "Shoot Dates", "Shoot Locations", "Job Status", and "Job Type" as per your project requirements.
3. Enter the estimated budget, budget currency, and budget currency symbol as per your project requirements.
4. Fill in the contact person, contact email, contact phone, and contact name information accurately.
5. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled out accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quote Request** (`1`, `text`, required):
	+ This is the title of your quote request.
* **Contact Person** (`2`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the name of the person who is requesting the quote.
* **Production Team** (`3`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the name of the production team for your project.
* **Production Type** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select the type(s) of production you need location scouting services for (e.g., photo shoot, film shoot, etc.).
* **Location Scouting Services** (`5`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select the location scouting services you need (e.g., location scouting, location management, etc.).
* **Shoot Dates** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select the dates for your shoot (e.g., 2022-01-01, 2022-01-15, 2022-02-01).
* **Shoot Locations** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select the location(s) for your shoot (e.g., location 1, location 2, location 3).
* **Shoot Duration** (`8`, `time`, required):
	+ Enter the duration of your shoot.
* **Shoot Details** (`9`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the details of your shoot.
* **Location Details** (`10`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the location details for your shoot.
* **Client** (`11`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the name of your client.
* **Contact E-mail** (`12`, `email`, required):
	+ Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`13`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Project Budget** (`14`, `number`, required):
	+ Enter the estimated budget for your project.
* **Estimated Budget** (`15`, `number`, required):
	+ Enter the estimated budget for your project.
* **Estimated Budget Currency** (`16`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the currency of the estimated budget (e.g., USD, EUR, etc.).
* **Estimated Budget Currency Symbol** (`17`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the symbol for the estimated budget currency (e.g., $, €, etc.).
* **Project Manager** (`18`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the name of the project manager.
* **Contact Name** (`19`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Title** (`20`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Department** (`21`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the department of the contact person.
* **Job Code** (`22`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the job code for your project.
* **Job Status** (`23`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select the status of your project (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
* **Job Type** (`24`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the type of job for your project (e.g., commercial, editorial, etc.).
* **Notes** (`25`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter any additional notes about your project.
