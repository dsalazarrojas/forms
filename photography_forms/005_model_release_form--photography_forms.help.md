# Model Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Model Release Form is used to gather information from individuals who have been photographed and to obtain a model release, which grants permission for the photographer or production team to use the images taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill in your full name in the **name** field.
* Enter your email address in the **email** field.
* Provide your Date of Birth in the **date_of_birth** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear in any credits or acknowledgments related to the photographs.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where we can contact you for any further questions or requests regarding the photographs.
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1999).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
