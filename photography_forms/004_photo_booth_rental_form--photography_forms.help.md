# Photo Booth Rental Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a photo booth rental request. It helps event organizers and clients provide details about their event, including contact information, event specifics, and photo booth requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information:
	* Enter your first name in the `Contact First Name` field.
	* Enter your last name in the `Contact Last Name` field.
	* Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
2. Provide event details:
	* Enter the date of your event in the `Event Date` field (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
	* Enter the time of your event in the `Event Time` field (in 12-hour format).
	* Enter the location of your event in the `Event Location` field.
	* Enter the duration of your event in the `Event Duration` field (in hours).
3. Describe your event:
	* Enter a brief description of your event in the `Event Description` field.
4. Specify photo booth requirements:
	* Select the type of photo booth you need:
		+ Single
		+ Double
		+ Triple
		+ Quad
	* Indicate if you need a photo booth with equipment:
		+ Yes
		+ No
	* Specify the services you need for your photo booth:
		+ Yes (e.g., printing, editing)
		+ No

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photo Booth Rental Form** (Page 1): A brief description of this page is not provided as it is the form title.
* **Contact First Name** (Page 1): Your first name. 
* **Contact Last Name** (Page 1): Your last name.
* **Email** (Page 1): Your email address. Please use a valid email format (e.g., name@example.com).
* **Phone** (Page 1): Your phone number. This can be a landline or mobile number.
* **Event Date** (Page 1): The date of your event (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Event Time** (Page 1): The time of your event (in 12-hour format).
* **Event Location** (Page 1): The location of your event.
* **Event Duration** (Page 1): The duration of your event (in hours).
* **Event Description** (Page 1): A brief description of your event.
* **Photo Booth Type** (Page 1): Select the type of photo booth you need:
	+ Single: One photo booth.
	+ Double: Two photo booths.
	+ Triple: Three photo booths.
	+ Quad: Four photo booths.
* **Photo Booth Size** (Page 1): Check if you need a photo booth of a specific size:
	+ Yes: A photo booth of the selected size.
	+ No: Any size.
* **Photo Booth Equipment** (Page 2): Check if you need a photo booth with specific equipment:
	+ Yes: A photo booth with the required equipment.
	+ No: No photo booth equipment required.
* **Photo Booth Services** (Page 2): Check the services you need for your photo booth:
	+ Yes: A photo booth with the selected service.
	+ No: No service required.
* **Photo Booth Availability** (Page 2): Check if the photo booth is available:
	+ Yes: The photo booth is available.
	+ No: The photo booth is not available.
* **Photo Booth Availability Details** (Page 2): Enter any additional details about the photo booth availability.
