# photography_lighting_gear_requisition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for photographers or videographers to request specific equipment and services for their shoots.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact information (photographer's name, contact email, and contact phone number).
2. Describe the equipment you need for your shoot.
3. Provide the date and time you need to pick up and return the equipment.
4. Specify the type of shoot you are doing (e.g., photo shoot, video shoot, etc.).
5. Provide any additional notes or details about the shoot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photography Team Member** (`photographer_name`, text, required): Enter your name and role within the photography team.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for any questions or concerns.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any questions or concerns.
* **Equipment Requested** (`equipment_requested`, text, required): Describe the specific equipment you need for your shoot (e.g., camera, lens, light, etc.).
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required): Enter the date you need to pick up the equipment.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required): Enter the date you will return the equipment.
* **Status** (`status`, text, required): Describe the status of your equipment request.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or details about your equipment request.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, text, required): Specify the type of equipment you are requesting (e.g., photo, video, etc.).
* **Production Type** (`production_type`, text, required): Describe the type of production you are doing (e.g., photo shoot, video shoot, etc.).
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, required): Enter the time you need to pick up the equipment.
* **Return Time** (`return_time`, time, required): Enter the time you will return the equipment.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the shoot.
* **Client** (`client`, text, required): Enter the name of the client you are working with.
* **Crew Members** (`crew_members`, text, required): List the members of your crew.
* **Crew Contact Info** (`crew_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the contact information for your crew.
* **Production Date** (`production_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the production.
* **Production Time** (`production_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the production.
* **Shoot Type** (`shoot_type`, text, required): Describe the type of shoot you are doing (e.g., photo shoot, video shoot, etc.).
* **Shoot Location** (`shoot_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the shoot.
* **Shoot Setup** (`shoot_setup`, text, required): Describe the setup of the shoot.
* **Shot List** (`shot_list`, text, required): Provide a list of shots you will be taking.
* **Shoot Notes** (`shoot_notes`, text, required): Provide any additional notes or details about the shoot.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to submit your form.

Note: The required fields are marked as "required" in the form schema, which means they must be filled out for the form to be submitted.
