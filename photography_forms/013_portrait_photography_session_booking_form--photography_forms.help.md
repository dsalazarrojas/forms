# Portrait Photography Session Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers book a portrait photography session with our studio. It collects essential information such as contact details, session preferences, and payment options. By filling out this form, you'll be able to book a session and schedule a time and date that suits you best.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and last name in the "client name" and "last name" fields.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Choose a date for your photography session in the "booking date" field.
5. Select a time for your session in the "booking time" field.
6. Choose a location for your session in the "location" field.
7. Specify the style of photography you prefer in the "photo style" field.
8. Select the type of session you'd like to book (portrait, landscape, wedding, event) in the "session type" field.
9. Choose how you'd like to confirm your booking (email, phone, in-person) in the "confirmation method" field.
10. Select a payment method (cash, card, online) in the "payment method" field.
