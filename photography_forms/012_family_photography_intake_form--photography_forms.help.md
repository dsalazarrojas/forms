# Family Photography Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information for a family photography session. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure that we have all the necessary details to provide an excellent experience for the family.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply work through each page and fill out the required fields. You can skip any fields that are not applicable to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Family Members
* **Family Members** (`family_members`, select_multiple, optional): List all family members that will be photographed. Please include names, ages, and any special requests for each family member.

### Date and Time of Session
* **Date and Time of Session** (`date_and_time`, date, optional): Select the date and start time of the session. Please ensure that the time selected is realistic and allows for enough time to take the necessary photos.

### Photographers Name
* **Photographers Name** (`photographer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the photographer assigned to your session.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, select_one, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted. Please choose one of the options provided.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number if you selected "Phone" as your contact method.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email if you selected "Email" as your contact method.

### Contact Address
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your contact address if you selected "Other" as your contact method.

### Session Date
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the session.

### Session Start Time
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, time, optional): Select the start time of the session.

### Session End Time
* **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, time, optional): Select the end time of the session.

### Session Length
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, select_one, optional): Select the length of the session. Please choose one of the provided options.

### Location
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the session.

### Photography Style
* **Photography Style** (`photography_style`, text, optional): Describe the photography style you prefer for the session.

### Special Requests
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): List any special requests you may have for the session.

### Photo Package
* **Photo Package** (`photo_package`, select_one, optional): Select a photo package that suits your needs.

### Session Notes
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the session.

### Photographer Notes
* **Photographer Notes** (`photographer_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes for the photographer.

### Follow Up Instructions
* **Follow Up Instructions** (`follow_up_instructions`, text, optional): Provide any follow-up instructions for the session.

### Contact Instructions
* **Contact Instructions** (`contact_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any contact instructions for the session.

### Follow Up Contact
* **Follow Up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, text, optional): Enter your follow-up contact information.

### Follow Up Contact Method
* **Follow Up Contact Method** (`follow_up_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method of follow-up contact. Please choose one of the options provided.

### Follow Up Contact Time
* **Follow Up Contact Time** (`follow_up_contact_time`, time, optional): Select the time of follow-up contact.

### Follow Up Contact Note
* **Follow Up Contact Note** (`follow_up_contact_note`, note, optional): Enter any follow-up contact note.
