# Wedding Package With Price Change - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help wedding vendors or planners create and customize their wedding packages with a price change option.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
1. **Client Details (Page 1)**: Enter the client's basic contact and personal information.
	* This includes fields to input their name, address, and other relevant details.
2. **Package Details (Page 2)**: Enter the specifics of the package being offered, including any price changes.
	* This is where you will fill in the details of the package, including any price changes.
3. **Agreement Part (Page 3)**: Add any additional comments or terms and conditions related to the package.
	* This is where you can add any additional information or terms that the client needs to know.
