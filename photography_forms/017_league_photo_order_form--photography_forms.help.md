# League Photo Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a photo session for a League event. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure we can provide the best service for your photography needs.

## How To Complete This Form

- First, fill out the form fields with the required information.
- Make sure to select the correct photography service and photo package options.
- Choose the date and time for your photo session.
- If you have any additional notes or requests, please include them in the "Notes" field.
- Click "Submit" to send the form to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name as you would like to be addressed.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your last name as you would like to be addressed.
- **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address to be used for communication.
- **Phone** (`phone_number`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number to be used for contact.
- **Photography Service** (`photo_service`, Select Multiple, Optional):
  - Choose the photography services you are interested in: "Yes" for the service, "No" for no service.
  - You can select multiple services if applicable.
- **Photo Package** (`photo_package`, Select One, Optional):
  - Choose the photo package that suits your needs: "Yes" for the package, "No" for no package.
- **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, Date, Optional): Choose a date for your photo session.
- **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, Time, Optional): Choose a time for your photo session.
- **Photo Session Type** (`photo_session_type`, Select One, Optional):
  - Choose the type of photo session: "Yes" for the session, "No" for no session.
- **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or requests for your photo session.
