# Top Enroller Photo Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and photo metadata from participants or teams for general photo submission purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, denoted by the required label.
2. Enter the photo metadata (caption, credits, etc.) in the corresponding fields.
3. Confirm your submission by checking the box at the bottom of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Info (1)**: Enter your name, team or participant name (if applicable).
- **Photo (2)**: Upload or enter the photo URL.
- **Details (3)**: Enter a brief description of the photo.
- **Date (4)**: Select the date the photo was taken.
- **Time (5)**: Select the time the photo was taken.
- **Photo Details (6)**: Enter any additional photo metadata or credits.
- **Photo Credit (7)**: Enter any credits or permissions required for the photo.
- **Submission Confirmation (8)**: Check this box to confirm your submission.
- **Participant Photo (9)**: Upload or enter the participant's photo URL.
- **Team Photo (10)**: Upload or enter the team's photo URL.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your submission is complete.
- Double-check your photo and metadata for accuracy.
- If you have any questions, please contact <form@gic.mx>.
- Do not submit duplicate forms for the same photo or participant.
