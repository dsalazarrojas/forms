# Glass Album Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for placing orders for photo albums.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information.
2. Make sure to enter your date of birth in the format specified (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. Enter a valid email address.
4. Double-check your entered information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* Make sure to enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* Enter a valid email address to ensure our team can contact you regarding your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [support email].
