# Pet Portrait Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow pet owners to submit an order for a pet portrait photo session. The form collects information about the pet's details, the desired photography style, and session information, including the location, time, and session length.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information, including pet name, owner name, phone number, email, and pet breed.
2. Choose the desired photography style and location for the session.
3. Select the preferred session length and time slot.
4. Choose a payment method (online, in-studio, or phone).
5. Fill in any additional information or special instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, required): This field is for the name of your pet.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, required): This field is for your name as the pet's owner.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): This field is for your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): This field is for your email address in case we need to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, optional): This field is for your pet's date of birth.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, optional): This field is for the time of the appointment, but this field is not currently being used.
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, optional): This field is to select the breed of your pet from the options provided.
* **Portrait Size** (`portrait_size`, optional): This field is to select the desired size of the portrait from the options provided.
* **Photography Style** (`photography_style`, optional): This field is to select the desired photography style from the options provided.
* **Portrait Location** (`portrait_location`, optional): This field is to select the desired location for the portrait session from the options provided.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, optional): This field is for any additional information or special requests you may have.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, optional): This field is to select the payment method (online, in-studio, or phone).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, optional): This field is to fill in the payment amount (this field is currently not used).
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, optional): This field is to select the type of pet (cat, dog, or other).
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, optional): This field is for the date of the appointment, but this field is not currently being used.
* **Appointment Time Slot** (`appointment_time_slot`, optional): This field is to select the time slot for the appointment (AM or PM).
* **Photography Session Length** (`photography_session_length`, optional): This field is to select the desired length of the photography session from the options provided.
* **Portrait Session Length** (`portrait_session_length`, optional): This field is to select the desired length of the portrait session from the options provided.

Note: Some fields in this form are currently not used (e.g. `payment_amount`, `appointment_date`, `appointment_time_slot`), but are included in the form for potential future use.
