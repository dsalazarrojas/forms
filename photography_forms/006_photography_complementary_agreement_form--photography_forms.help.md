# photography_complementary_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for establishing a collaborative agreement between a photographer and a client, covering essential details for a successful project execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the service description in the **Service Description** field, providing a brief overview of the project.
2. Fill in the **Photographer Information** field with your name, contact details, and any other necessary information.
3. In the **Client Information** field, enter your client's name, contact details, and any relevant information.
4. The **Service Details** field is a note-taking section for any additional comments or notes about the project.
5. In the **Payment Details** field, specify the payment terms, including any relevant payment methods or amounts.
6. The **Terms and Conditions** field is for outlining the terms of service, including cancellation policies, refunds, or any other essential conditions.
7. In the **Photographer** field, confirm your name and contact details.
8. The **Client Kontakt** field is for the client to provide their contact information, which can be left blank if not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Description** (Page 1, field 1): A brief description of the photography service being offered, including the type of service, duration, and any specific requirements.
* **Photographer Information** (Page 1, field 2): Enter your name, contact details, and any other necessary information for the client to contact you or your team.
* **Client Information** (Page 1, field 3): Enter the client's name, contact details, and any relevant information about the project or service.
* **Service Details** (Page 1, field 4): Additional comments or notes about the project, such as specific dates, times, or requirements.
* **Payment Details** (Page 1, field 5): Specify the payment terms, including any relevant payment methods or amounts.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Page 1, field 6): Outline the terms of service, including cancellation policies, refunds, or any other essential conditions.
* **Photographer** (Page 1, field 7): Confirm your name and contact details to ensure the client knows how to contact you or your team.
* **Client Kontakt** (Page 1, field 8): This field is for the client to provide their contact information, which can be left blank if not required.
