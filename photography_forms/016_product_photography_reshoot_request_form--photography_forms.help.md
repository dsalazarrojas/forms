# Product Photography Reshoot Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a photography reshoot request. If you are not a photographer or do not have a specific reason for requesting a reshoot, please do not fill out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as prompted. Please make sure to provide a brief description of why you are requesting a reshoot and include any relevant images or deadlines if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Information** (Form Information, text, optional):
	+ This field is for general information and is not required.
* **Reshoot Reason** (Reshoot Reason, text, optional):
	+ Please provide a brief description of why you are requesting a reshoot.
* **Reference Image 1** (Reference Image 1, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to upload or paste a reference image, if applicable.
* **Reference Image 2** (Reference Image 2, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to upload or paste a second reference image, if applicable.
* **Deadline** (Deadline, date, optional):
	+ Enter a deadline for the reshoot, if applicable. This should be in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Reshoot Deadline** (Reshoot Deadline, date, optional):
	+ This field is not explicitly required but seems to be a redundant field. Please ignore it.
* **Reshoot Deadline Time** (Reshoot Deadline Time, time, optional):
	+ This field is not explicitly required and its purpose is unclear.
* **Reshoot Deadline Date** (Reshoot Deadline Date, date, optional):
	+ This field seems to be a duplicate of the "Deadline" field.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or notes you may have for the reshoot request.

## Tips
Please make sure to provide a clear and concise description of why you are requesting a reshoot, and include any relevant images or deadlines.
