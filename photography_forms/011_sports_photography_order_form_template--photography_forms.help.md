# Sports Photography Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to order sports photography services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number in the relevant fields.
2. Choose your preferred date and time for the photo shoot.
3. Select your photography package from the options provided.
4. Provide any additional details about your package preferences.
5. Choose your payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (`1`: Name)
* Label: Name
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter your name to help us identify you.
* Required: No

### Email (`2`: Email)
* Label: Email
* Type: Email
* Description: Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* Required: No

### Phone (`3`: Phone)
* Label: Phone
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* Required: No

### Date (`4`: Date)
* Label: Date
* Type: Date
* Description: Choose your preferred date for the photo shoot.
* Required: No

### Time (`5`: Time)
* Label: Time
* Type: Time
* Description: Choose your preferred time for the photo shoot.
* Required: No

### Photography Package (`6`: Photography Package)
* Label: Photography Package
* Type: Select Multiple
* Description: Choose one or more of the following packages:
	+ Basic: A basic package with minimal editing and delivery options.
	+ Standard: A standard package with more editing and delivery options.
	+ Premium: A premium package with advanced editing and delivery options.
* Required: Yes

### Package Details (`7`: Package Details)
* Label: Package Details
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter any additional details about your package preferences.
* Required: No

### Payment Method (`8`: Payment Method)
* Label: Payment Method
* Type: Select One
* Description: Choose your preferred payment method:
	+ Credit Card: Pay using a credit card.
	+ Bank Transfer: Pay using a bank transfer.
* Required: Yes

### Message (`9`: Message)
* Label: Message
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* Required: No
