# School Photo Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Photo Order Form is a tool designed to collect student information and order details to help schools efficiently manage photo orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the student's name and other required information in the "Student Information" section.
2. Provide the order details, such as the type of photo and any special requests, in the "Order Details" section.
3. Enter the payment information in the "Payment Information" section.
4. Finally, specify the photo information, including the photo type and any additional details, in the "Photo Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Information** (`student_info`, `text`, not required): Enter the student's name and any other relevant details.
- **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, not required): Provide any specific details about the photo order, such as the type of photo or any special requests.
- **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, not required): Enter the payment method and any additional payment information.
- **Photo Information** (`photo_info`, `text`, not required): Specify the photo type and any other details about the photo order.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the necessary fields for accurate data processing.
- Double-check the entered information before submission.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact form@gic.mx.
