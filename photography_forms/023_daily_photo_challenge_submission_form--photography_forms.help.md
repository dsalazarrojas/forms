# Daily Photo Challenge Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect photos submitted for a daily photo challenge, where users can add tags to describe their photos using multiple-choice options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the `photo_title` field with a brief description of your photo.
2. Enter the date your photo was taken in the `date_taken` field.
3. If you have multiple tags to associate with your photo, select the relevant ones from the 20 `photo_tags` fields. Each field contains multiple-choice options for different themes or categories.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **photo_entry**: Enter a brief description of your photo (optional).
* **date_taken**: Enter the date your photo was taken.
* **photo_title**: Enter a short title for your photo.
* **photographer_name**: Enter your name as the photographer.
* **photo_description**: Enter a longer description of your photo (optional).
* **photo_tags** ( fields 6-25): Select the relevant tags that best describe your photo's theme or category from the provided options.
