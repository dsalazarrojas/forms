# Photo Waiver Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be completed by individuals who are participating in a photography session. It gathers essential information for both the subject and the photographer to ensure a successful and respectful experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question carefully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
- If you are unsure about any question, you may request clarification from the photographer.
- Once completed, please review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`page_1`): A brief explanation of the form's purpose and the photography session. Please read this section carefully to understand what the form is about and what is required of you.
* **Name and Contact Information** (`page_2`): This section requires your name and contact information, which will be used to communicate with you about the photography session and any necessary follow-up actions.
* **Photography Information** (`page_3`): Provide information about the photography session, including dates, times, and locations. Be as detailed as possible to ensure the photographer is well-prepared.
* **Photo Release Information** (`page_4`): This section asks for permission to use your likeness and image in promotional materials. Please carefully consider what you are granting permission for.
* **Waiver Information** (`page_5`): Read and agree to the waiver terms, which outline your responsibilities and rights as a participant. This is a crucial part of the form, so please take your time and review carefully.
* **Signature** (`page_6`): Sign the form to indicate that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms outlined in the previous sections.
* **Review and Confirm** (`page_7`): Carefully review all the information you have provided, ensuring it is accurate and complete. Once satisfied, confirm your submission.

## Tips
- Please note that this form is a mandatory requirement for participating in a photography session.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the photography session, you can contact the photographer at any time.
- Completing this form accurately and thoroughly helps ensure a successful and enjoyable experience for everyone involved.
