# headshot_event_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The headshot_event_registration form is designed to collect necessary information for a photography event registration process. It includes details about the event, attendees, and consent for participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Details** section with information about the event you are registering for.
2. Provide your **Attendee Details** in the second section.
3. Select your choice of consent in the **Consent** section, either "I agree" or "Not now".
4. If you have additional session details, you can provide them in the **Session Details** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Details** (text): Fill in the name or description of the event.
- **Attendee Details** (text): Provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as an attendee.
- **Consent** (select one):
  - "I agree" to participate in the event.
  - "Not now" to decline or indicate that you will not be able to attend at this time.
- **Session Details** (text): Provide any additional information about the sessions you are interested in or have already signed up for, such as dates, times, and topics.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
- Double-check your consent choice before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact event organizers directly.
