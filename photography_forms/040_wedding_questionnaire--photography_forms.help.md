# Wedding Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information from couples about their upcoming wedding. It is intended to help photographers and other wedding vendors understand the couple's needs and preferences. This form will be used to gather essential details about the wedding, including contact information, date and time, venue, number of guests, services required, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Introduction" field, where you can briefly describe your wedding and your expectations.
2. In the "Contact Info" field, select how the couple would like to be contacted (Name, Email, or Phone).
3. Enter the date and time of your wedding in the "Date and Time" field.
4. Enter the venue where your wedding will take place in the "Venue" field.
5. Indicate the number of guests attending your wedding in the "Number of Guests" field.
6. Select the services you would like to book from the "Services Wanted" field (Photography, Videography, or Wedding Party).
7. In the "Additional Services" field, provide any other services you would like to inquire about.
8. Enter any notes or comments you have in the "Notes and Comments" field.
9. If applicable, enter your email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
11. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
12. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" field (Online, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
12. Finally, enter any additional payment details in the "Payment Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: A brief description of your wedding and your expectations.
* **Contact Info**: How you would like to be contacted (Name, Email, or Phone).
* **Date and Time**: The date and time of your wedding.
* **Venue**: The location where your wedding will take place.
* **Number of Guests**: The number of guests attending your wedding.
* **Services Wanted**: Select the services you would like to book (Photography, Videography, or Wedding Party).
* **Additional Services**: Any other services you would like to inquire about.
* **Notes and Comments**: Any additional notes or comments you have.
* **Email**: Your email address, if you would like to be contacted via email.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number, if you would like to be contacted via phone.
* **Address**: Your physical address, if you would like to provide it.
* **Payment Method**: How you would like to make payment (Online, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
* **Payment Details**: Any additional payment details you would like to provide.

This help guide has been written with the intention of assisting you in completing the Wedding Questionnaire form with ease. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
