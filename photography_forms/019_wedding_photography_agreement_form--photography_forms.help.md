# Wedding Photography Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Photography Agreement Form is a document that helps us understand your wedding plans and preferences, ensuring that we can provide you with the best possible experience on your special day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your email address in the "Email" field. This is the primary contact method we will use to communicate with you.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport in the "Name" field.
3. Select the date of your wedding in the "Date" field. This should be the date when you plan to get married.
4. Choose the time of your wedding in the "Wedding Time" field. Please select the time of day when your wedding ceremony will start.
5. Fill in the location of your wedding, including the address and any other relevant details, in the "Location" field.
6. Choose which photography service you require in the "Services" field. This will help us understand your photography needs for the wedding. You can choose between Full Service, Half Service, or Partial Service.
7. In the "Terms and Conditions" field, please describe any specific requirements or requests you have for your photography service.
8. In the "Cancellation Policy" field, outline any conditions under which you would need to cancel or postpone your wedding photography session.
9. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for communication and coordination in the "Contact Person" field.
10. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Contact E-mail" field.
11. Fill in the phone number of the contact person in the "Contact Phone" field.
12. If you wish, you can add your signature in the "Signature" field, indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email**: Enter your primary contact email address that we will use for communication.
* **Name**: Fill in your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Date**: Select the date of your wedding.
* **Wedding Time**: Choose the start time of your wedding ceremony.
* **Location**: Enter the location of your wedding, including the address and any other relevant details.
* **Services**: Choose which photography service you require: Full Service, Half Service, or Partial Service.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Describe any specific requirements or requests you have for your photography service.
* **Cancellation Policy**: Outline any conditions under which you would need to cancel or postpone your wedding photography session.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for communication and coordination.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Fill in the phone number of the contact person.
* **Signature**: If you wish, add your signature indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
