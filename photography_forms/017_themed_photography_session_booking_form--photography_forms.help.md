# Themed Photography Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Themed Photography Session Booking Form is designed to collect information from clients interested in booking a themed photography session. This form will help the photographer to gather details about the client's personal information, session preferences, and any other relevant details to prepare for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide details about the session you are interested in booking, including the date and time you prefer.
3. Choose the type of session you are interested in booking (e.g., Portrait, Family, Wedding, Event, or Other).
4. Specify the number of guests attending the session.
5. Add any additional session notes or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (personal_info)**: This section collects your personal information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Name (name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any session details or changes.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address so we can send you session reminders and updates.
* **Session Details (session_details)**: Provide a brief description of the session you are interested in booking, including any specific themes or ideas you'd like to incorporate.
* **Location (location)**: If applicable, provide the location where you would like to have the session.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date you prefer for the session.
* **Time (time)**: Enter the time you prefer for the session.
* **Session Type (session_type)**: Choose the type of session that best suits your needs (e.g., Portrait, Family, Wedding, Event, or Other).
* **Number of Guests (num_guests)**: Specify the number of people attending the session.
* **Session Notes (notes)**: Add any additional details or instructions about the session, such as specific poses or locations.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and detailed information will help us to better prepare for your session. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
