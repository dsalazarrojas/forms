# photo_waiver_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photo_waiver_release_form is designed to collect information and obtain consent for photoshoots involving people. This form ensures that all parties understand their rights and responsibilities regarding the use of photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions about the photo shoot details in the relevant fields.
2.  Review and confirm your answers to ensure accuracy.
3.  Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **photo_waiver** (1, number, required): Enter the number of photos taken during the shoot.
* **photo_description** (2, text, optional): Briefly describe the photo shoot, including any notable details.
* **date** (3, date, optional): Enter the date the photo shoot took place.
* **time** (4, time, optional): Enter the time the photo shoot started or ended.
* **photo_release** (5, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" to release the photos for use or "No" for non-release.
* **photo_model_release** (6, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" to release the models' rights for use or "No" for non-release.
* **photo_location** (7, text, optional): Describe the location where the photo shoot took place.
* **photo_camera** (8, text, optional): Specify the camera used during the shoot.
* **photo_editing_software** (9, text, optional): Mention any photo editing software used during editing.
* **photo_print_release** (10, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" to release the photos for printing or "No" for non-release.
* **photo_rights_release** (11, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" to release the rights for use or "No" for non-release.
* **photo_rights_model_release** (12, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" to release model's rights or "No" for non-release.
* **photo_rights_model_release2** (13, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" to release model's rights or "No" for non-release.
* **photo_rights_model_release3** (14, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" to release model's rights or "No" for non-release.
* **photo_rights_model_release4** (15, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" to release model's rights or "No" for non-release.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have permission to collect and use the data collected in this form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
* This form should only be used for photoshoots where the subjects are people. If you have any non-human subjects (e.g. landscapes, objects), please use a different form.
