# Photo Print Release Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Print Release Form Template is a document used to obtain permission from a client to print their photos. This form helps ensure that the client understands and consents to the printing of their photos, and it also provides a record of their agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Select the print release status from the "Print Release" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the date of photo capture in the "Capture Date" field.
4. Specify the location where the photo was captured in the "Capture Location" field.
5. Select the release permission for the client to print their photos in the "Release Permission" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the date the release was signed in the "Release Date" field.
7. Sign the "Client Signature" field with your signature.
8. Enter the date of your signature in the "Signature Date" field.
9. Check the "Print Release Agreement" dropdown menu to agree or disagree with the print release agreement.
10. Enter any print restrictions in the "Print Restriction" field.
11. Provide details for any print restrictions in the "Print Restriction Details" field.
12. Enter details for photo capture in the "Photo Capture Details" field.
13. Enter the release agreement terms and conditions in the "Release Agreement Terms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`photo_print_release_form_template`, text, optional): Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Print Release** (`photo_print_release`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the client grants permission to print their photos.
	+ Select "Print" to allow printing of the photos.
	+ Select "Do not print" to restrict printing of the photos.
* **Capture Date** (`photo_capture_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the photo was captured.
* **Capture Location** (`photo_capture_location`, text, optional): Specify the location where the photo was captured.
* **Release Permission** (`photo_release`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the client's release permission.
	+ Select "Print" to allow printing of the photos.
	+ Select "Do not print" to restrict printing of the photos.
	+ Select "Unknown" if you are unsure of the client's release permission.
* **Release Date** (`photo_release_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the release was signed.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, optional): Sign your signature as the client.
* **Signature Date** (`client_signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Print Release Agreement** (`print_release_agreement`, select_one, optional): Check the print release agreement.
	+ Select "Agree" to agree with the print release agreement.
	+ Select "Disagree" to disagree with the print release agreement.
* **Print Restriction** (`photo_print_restriction`, text, optional): Enter any print restrictions.
* **Print Restriction Details** (`photo_print_restriction_details`, text, optional): Provide details for any print restrictions.
* **Photo Capture Details** (`photo_capture_details`, text, optional): Provide details for photo capture.
* **Release Agreement Terms** (`photo_release_agreement_terms`, text, optional): Enter the release agreement terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Make sure to read through the form carefully before completing it.
- If you are unsure of any field, ask your representative for assistance.
- Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
- Review the release agreement terms and conditions carefully before agreeing or disagreeing with them.
