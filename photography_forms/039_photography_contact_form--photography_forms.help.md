# Photography Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect client information for photography sessions. It is essential to provide detailed and accurate information to help us deliver the best possible experience for you and your family.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's name, spouse's name (if applicable), and number of siblings.
2. Provide your phone number and email address so we can get in touch with you.
3. Choose a due date for your photography session.
4. Add any notes or comments, and sign your name in the provided space.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Information** (text): Enter your name and any information you want us to have about you.
- **Client Name** (text): This is your name, as you would like to be referred to during the photography session.
- **Spouse** (text): If you're bringing a spouse or significant other, please enter their name.
- **Due Date** (date): Choose the date you would like to have your photography session.
- **Address** (text): Enter your address so we can get in touch with you or send you reminders.
- **Phone Number** (text): Your phone number will be used for scheduling and contact purposes.
- **Number of Siblings** (number): How many siblings are coming along for the photo shoot?
- **Signature** (note): Please sign your name here to confirm you've reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions.
- **Email** (email): We'll use this email to send you updates, reminders, and any additional information you might need.
