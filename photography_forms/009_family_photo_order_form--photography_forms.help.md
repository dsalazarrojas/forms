# Family Photo Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to order photo packages for multiple families. To complete the form, users need to provide the necessary information for each family member and select the photo type and date and time of the order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Order Photo Packages**: Select the photo package you want to order.
2. Fill in the information for each family member, including their name, email, and phone number. You can add up to 6 family members.
3. **Photo Type**: Choose the type of photo you want to order (landscape, portrait, square, or panorama).
4. Enter the date and time of the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Photo Packages**: This field allows you to select the photo package you want to order.
* **Child 1 Name**: Enter the name of the first child.
* **Parent 1 Email**: Enter the email of the first parent.
* **Parent 1 Phone**: Enter the phone number of the first parent.
* **Child 2 Name**: Enter the name of the second child.
* **Parent 2 Email**: Enter the email of the second parent.
* **Parent 2 Phone**: Enter the phone number of the second parent.
* **Child 3 Name**: Enter the name of the third child.
* **Parent 3 Email**: Enter the email of the third parent.
* **Parent 3 Phone**: Enter the phone number of the third parent.
* **Child 4 Name**: Enter the name of the fourth child.
* **Parent 4 Email**: Enter the email of the fourth parent.
* **Parent 4 Phone**: Enter the phone number of the fourth parent.
* **Child 5 Name**: Enter the name of the fifth child.
* **Parent 5 Email**: Enter the email of the fifth parent.
* **Parent 5 Phone**: Enter the phone number of the fifth parent.
* **Child 6 Name**: Enter the name of the sixth child.
* **Parent 6 Email**: Enter the email of the sixth parent.
* **Parent 6 Phone**: Enter the phone number of the sixth parent.
* **Photo Type**: Select the type of photo you want to order (landscape, portrait, square, or panorama).
* **Date of Order**: Enter the date of the order.
* **Time of Order**: Enter the time of the order.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the order.
* **Total Cost**: This field is populated automatically based on your selections.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the necessary information for each family member.
* Double-check the photo type and date and time of the order to ensure everything is correct.
* If you have any additional notes, use this field to provide any necessary details about the order.
