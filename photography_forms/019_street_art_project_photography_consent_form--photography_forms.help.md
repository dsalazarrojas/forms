# Street Art Project Photography Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather contact information and location details from individuals who are interested in participating in the Street Art Project or providing their consent to be photographed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your contact and location information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: Enter a title for your project or a brief description of your street art piece. (optional)
* **Name**: Your name as it should appear in the project's documentation. (optional)
* **Email**: Your email address where you can be reached for further information or collaboration. (optional)
* **Phone**: Your phone number where you can be reached. (optional)
* **Address**: The physical location where your street art is situated. (optional)
* **City**: The city where your street art is located. (optional)
* **State**: The state where your street art is located. (optional)
* **Zip**: The zip code of the city where your street art is located. (optional)
* **Country**: The country where your street art is located. (optional)

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication and collaboration.
