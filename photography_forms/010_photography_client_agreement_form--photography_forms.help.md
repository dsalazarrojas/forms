# Photography Client Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Client Agreement Form is a document that clients use to agree on the terms of a photography session. It collects information about the client's session, personal details, and their consent to submit photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Photography Session Details** field with information about your photography session, such as date, time, location, and type of photography session (e.g., portrait, wedding, event).
2. Provide your **Client Details** in the following fields:
	* Client Name
	* Contact Information (email, phone number, or address)
3. Read and sign the **Agreement** field, which outlines the terms and conditions of the photography session.
4. If you agree with the terms, sign the **Submission** field by typing your name and date.
5. Finally, sign the **Signature** field by typing your name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photography Session Details** (`photography_session_details`):
	+ A field to provide information about your photography session.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Client Details** (`client_details`):
	+ A field to provide your personal details.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Agreement** (`agreement`):
	+ A field to read and sign the terms of the photography session.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Submission** (`submission`):
	+ A field to agree to submit photos.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Signature** (`signature`):
	+ A field to sign your name and date to confirm agreement.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully, as it is a legally binding document.
* Read and understand the terms of the agreement before signing it.
* Make sure to print and sign the form before the photography session if you plan to submit photos.
