# Photography Client Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Photography Client Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from clients after a photography service has been provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first page with your name, email, phone number, and date and time of service.
2. Choose the category of service you received (e.g. Photography, Wedding, Portrait, etc.).
3. Rate your satisfaction with the service.
4. Add any comments or feedback you would like to share.
5. If applicable, assign the feedback to a specific staff member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (<name>, text, optional)
Your first name should be filled in here.

### Last Name (<name>, text, optional)
Your last name should be filled in here.

### Email (<label>, email, optional)
Enter your email address.

### Phone Number (<label>, text, optional)
Enter your phone number.

### Date of Service (<label>, date, optional)
Enter the date on which you received the photography service.

### Time of Service (<label>, time, optional)
Enter the time of service.

### Feedback (<label>, text, optional)
Share any feedback or comments about the service you received.

### Category (<label>, select_one, optional)
Choose the category of service you received (e.g. Photography, Wedding, Portrait, etc.).

### Rating (<label>, number, optional)
Rate your satisfaction with the service on a scale of 1-5.

### Comments (<label>, text, optional)
Add any additional comments or feedback you would like to share.

### Assigned To (<label>, select_multiple, optional)
Choose the staff member(s) who provided the service.

## Tips
- Please fill out all required fields.
- If you need help with a specific field, please do not hesitate to ask.
- This form is intended for internal use only. Any information collected will be reviewed by organization staff. 
- Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
