# headshot_session_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for potential clients to provide information about their photography session requirements. Please fill it out to help us better understand your needs and provide a more personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as needed. You can skip any field you don't want to provide information about. Once you've filled out all the required and optional fields you'd like to complete, click the submit button to send your inquiry to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Select the date you are interested in scheduling your photography session.
* **Photographer Preferences** (`photographer_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): If you have a preferred photography style, please select from the options (Landscape, Portrait, or Other). This will help us match you with the best photographer for your needs.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): If you have a specific location in mind for your photography session, please enter it here.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, optional): If you have a preferred time of day for your photography session, please select it from the available options.
* **Inquiry Message** (`inquiry_message`, text, optional): If you have any additional information or questions about your photography session, please enter it here.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted to discuss your photography session, please provide your contact number here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted via email to discuss your photography session, please provide your email address here.

Note: The fields are designed to be flexible and optional, so you can provide as much or as little information as you'd like.
