# photography_session_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photography session agreement form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a photography session. It is intended to be filled out by the client before or after the session to ensure that both parties are on the same page and understand their responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. Please ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information to avoid any potential issues or disputes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What will be covered during the session** (`session_coverage`): Please describe the details of what will be covered during the session, including any specific services or products that will be provided.
* **Session location** (`session_location`): Enter the location where the session will take place. This can be a physical address or a specific studio/location name.
* **Session costs** (`session_costs`): Provide an estimate of the total costs for the session, including any applicable taxes or fees.
* **Terms and conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the session, which may include any limitations of liability, cancellation policies, or other important details.
* **Customers' e-signature** (`customers_signature`): Please provide your electronic signature to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Session date** (`session_date`): Enter the date of the photography session. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Session time** (`session_time`): Enter the time of the photography session. Please use the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Session duration** (`session_duration`): Estimate the length of the photography session. This can include any time slots or packages that have been agreed upon.
