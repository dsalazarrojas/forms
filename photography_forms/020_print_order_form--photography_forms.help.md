# Print Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a print order form used to collect information from customers for print orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Email" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
3. Select the "Print Type" of your order from the drop-down menu (e.g., Print Type, Digital, Other).
4. Choose the "Print Size" that suits your needs from the available options (e.g., 8x10, 11x14, 12x16, Other).
5. Select the "Print Paper" type (Luster, Matte, Glossy) for your order.
6. If you have any additional notes or comments, enter them in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter the "Vendor Name" and "Vendor Email" if you are working with a third-party vendor.
8. Enter the "Order Status" and provide any comments in the "Order Status Comments" field.
9. Select the "Payment Method" (Cash, Credit/Debit, Other) and enter the total cost of the order.
10. Select the "Payment Status" (Paid, Unpaid) to reflect the current status of the payment.
11. Enter any "Vendor Notes" or comments from the vendor.
12. Enter the "Order Number" and "Order ID" if available.
13. Enter any "Customer Notes" or comments.
14. If necessary, enter the "Print Date" and "Shipping Date" to track the order's progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name.
* **Customer Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Order Date** (Date, Optional): If you know the date of the order, enter it here.
* **Print Type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of print (Print Type, Digital, Other).
* **Print Size** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the size of the print (8x10, 11x14, 12x16, Other).
* **Print Paper** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of print paper (Luster, Matte, Glossy).
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Vendor Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the vendor.
* **Vendor Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the vendor.
* **Vendor Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the vendor.
* **Order Status** (Select One, Optional): Select the current status of the order (New, In Progress, Complete, Cancelled).
* **Order Status Comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any comments about the order status.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Optional): Select the payment method (Cash, Credit/Debit, Other).
* **Total Cost** (Number, Optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Status** (Select One, Optional): Select the payment status (Paid, Unpaid).
* **Vendor Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any comments from the vendor.
* **Order Number** (Text, Optional): Enter the order number.
* **Order ID** (Text, Optional): Enter the order ID.
* **Customer Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any comments from the customer.
* **Print Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the print is due.
* **Shipping Status** (Select One, Optional): Select the shipping status (Shipping, Not Shipped).
* **Shipping Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the shipping date.
