# Wedding Videography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers who are interested in hiring a videographer for their wedding. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any misunderstandings or miscommunications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name and contact email address.
2. Enter the date of your wedding ceremony.
3. Provide the location of your wedding ceremony.
4. Enter the location of your reception.
5. Select whether you want to purchase a video package or not.
6. If you do purchase a video package, fill in the details of your selected package.
7. Sign the contract, and we will contact you to finalize the details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the person who will be the main contact person for your wedding event.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address where we can contact you for any questions or updates.
* **Wedding Date**: Enter the date of your wedding ceremony. This is an important field, as it will help us schedule our services accurately.
* **Ceremony Location**: Enter the location where your wedding ceremony will take place. This will help us to prepare for the shoot.
* **Reception Location**: Enter the location where your reception will take place. This will help us to prepare for the post-ceremony coverage.
* **Video Package**: If you're interested in purchasing a video package, please select "Yes" from the options. This will allow us to discuss more details about the package with you.
* **Video Package Details**: If you selected "Yes" for the video package, please fill in the details of your selected package. This will help us to provide you with a clear understanding of what you've purchased.
* **Signature**: Please sign this contract to confirm that you've read and understood the terms and conditions of this form.
* **Package Details 1-5**: If you've selected "Yes" for the video package, please fill in the details of your selected package. These fields are specific to each package, so make sure to fill in the correct details according to your selection.
