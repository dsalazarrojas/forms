# Newborn Photography Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and consent from parents for newborn photography services. It helps the photography studio to onboard new clients and ensure that parents understand the terms and conditions of the services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you understand each field's purpose.
2. Enter your photography studio's information (photographer's name, studio address, phone number, and email).
3. Provide your studio's license number.
4. Choose how you would like your images to be used (All, Some, Only with a photographer, Only with a client).
5. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
6. Sign and date the form.
7. Provide your client's information (including name, address, phone number, and email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photography Studio Information** (`photographer_info`, note, required: false): This field is used to provide your photography studio's name, address, and contact information.
* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, note, required: false): This field is used to collect information about the parent, but it is not a required field.
* **Model Release** (`consent`, select_one, required: true): Choose how you would like your images to be used (All, Some, Only with a photographer, Only with a client).
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_conditions`, note, required: false): Read and agree to the studio's terms and conditions.
* **Signature** (`signature`, date, required: false): Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
* **Studio Address** (`studio_address`, text, required: false): Enter your studio's address.
* **Photographer's Name** (`photographer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the photographer.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Studio License** (`studio_license`, text, required: false): Enter your studio's license number.
* **Shoot Date** (`shoot_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the photo shoot.
* **Shoot Time** (`shoot_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the photo shoot.
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, note, required: false): This field is used to collect information about the client, but it is not a required field.
* **Photographer's Information** (`photographer_info`, note, required: false): This field is used to collect information about you as the photographer, but it is not a required field.
* **Contract Version** (`contract_version`, text, required: false): Enter the version of the contract you are using.
