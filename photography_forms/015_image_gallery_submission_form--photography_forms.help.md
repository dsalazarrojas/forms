# Image Gallery Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Image Gallery Submission Form is a simple form designed to collect metadata for a new image submission to the gallery. Fill out this form to add your image to the gallery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Form Title** field with a brief description of your image.
2. In the **Image** field, enter the URL of the image you want to submit.
3. Provide a description of your image in the **Description** field (optional).
4. Select a category for your image by choosing from the options in the **Category** dropdown menu.
5. Add any relevant tags to your image by selecting multiple options in the **Tags** dropdown menu.
6. Enter your name or username as the **Author** of the image.
7. Click the **Submit** button to submit your image to the gallery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`image_gallery_submission_form`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your image.
* **Image** (`image`, text, required): Enter the URL of the image you want to submit.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a description of your image to help other users understand it better.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select a category for your image from the available options.
* **Tags** (`tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple tags for your image to help other users find it easier.
* **Author** (`author`, text, required): Enter your name or username as the author of the image.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your image to the gallery.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, optional): Click this button to cancel your submission and return to the previous page.
* **Back to Form** (`back_to_form`, text, optional): Click this button to return to the form after cancellation.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting your image.
* If you are having trouble selecting a category or tags, you can contact your administrator for assistance.
* If you have already submitted an image with similar information, please do not submit it again to avoid duplication.
