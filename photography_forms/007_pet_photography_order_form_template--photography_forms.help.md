# pet_photography_order_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to be filled out by customers who have booked a pet photography session. It helps guide the photographer in preparing for the session by collecting essential information about the pet and the customer's preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Check that you have indeed booked a pet photography session with the photographer.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required and optional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Pet Type
* **Pet Type** (yes/no, required: false):
	+ If your pet is a yes, indicate that the pet is a yes. This field is optional and does not require a specific type of pet.
2. **Contact Email** (email, required: false):
	+ Enter your contact email address. This will be used to communicate with you about your session and any questions or concerns.
3. **Pet Name** (text, required: false):
	+ Provide your pet's name. This will help the photographer to personalize the photo session and identify your pet in the photos.
4. **Pet Breed** (yes/no, required: false):
	+ Indicate if your pet is a yes or no, or leave blank. This information can help the photographer with posing and capturing the best photos.
5. **Photo Session** (date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of the photo session. This will ensure that both you and the photographer are on the same page for the session.
6. **Photos Taken** (number, required: false):
	+ Indicate how many photos you would like to have taken during the session. This will help the photographer plan the session and ensure enough photos are taken.
7. **Photo Packages** (yes/no, required: false):
	+ Choose the photo package that suits your needs. This will determine the type and number of photos you will receive.
8. **Photo Package Details** (text, required: false):
	+ Provide any special requests or notes about the photo package you have chosen.
9. **Package Price** (number, required: false):
	+ This field is not applicable as it is not a field in the original form.
10. **Total Price** (number, required: false):
	+ This field is not applicable as it is not a field in the original form.
11. **Session Time** (yes/no, required: false):
	+ Indicate if you have a specific time in mind for the session or not.
12. **Session Date** (date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of the photo session if you have already decided on it.
13. **Photo Location** (text, required: false):
	+ Provide the location for the photo session. This could be your home, a park, or any other location.
14. **Special Requests** (text, required: false):
	+ Enter any special requests or notes for the photo session. This could be anything from "no flash" to "no background noise".

Note: Some fields may not be required but are recommended to provide the best possible experience for your pet and the photographer.
