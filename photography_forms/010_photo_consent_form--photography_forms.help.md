# Photo Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from the subject of a photo shoot for the use of their likeness. It helps establish the terms and conditions of the use of their photograph.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the form information (page 1).
2.  Provide a description of the photograph being taken.
3.  Specify where the photo was taken (page 2).
4.  If applicable, indicate if a model release is required (page 3).
5.  Provide information about the model's release (page 4).
6.  Provide information about the photographer (page 5).
7.  Specify the intended photo usage (page 6).
8.  Record the date and time of the photo shoot (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Form Information
*   **Form Information** (`Form Information`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for filling out your name and contact information to ensure that you, the form creator, can contact you with any form-related queries or updates.

### 2. Photo Description
*   **Photo Description** (`Photo Description`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for describing the purpose and context of the photograph being taken. Be sure to be as specific as possible so that the subject can understand the intended use of their likeness.

### 3. Photo Location
*   **Photo Location** (`Photo Location`, `text`, required/optional): Specify where the photograph was taken to ensure the subject's understanding of the context.

### 4. Model Release
*   **Model Release** (`Model Release`, `text`, required/optional): If a model release is required for this photo shoot, please describe the circumstances in which you will seek their release in this field. Otherwise, indicate "Not Applicable."

### 5. Model Information
*   **Model Information** (`Model Information`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for collecting information about the model (or subject) of the photograph. Please be specific about the model's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

### 6. Photographer Information
*   **Photographer Information** (`Photographer Information`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for providing your name and contact information. This will help the subject contact you regarding any photo-related queries or concerns.

### 7. Photo Usage
*   **Photo Usage** (`Photo Usage`, `text`, required/optional): Here, describe the intended use of the photograph. Be as specific as possible so that the subject can understand how their likeness will be used.

### 8. Date
*   **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required/optional): Record the date of the photo shoot.

### 9. Time
*   **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required/optional): Record the time of the photo shoot.
