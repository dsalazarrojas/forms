# Request A Photoshoot Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a photoshoot with the photographer. It will help us understand your needs and provide accurate quotes and confirmations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your name and email address to help us contact you.
2. Choose the type of service you are interested in (Wedding, Portrait, Commercial, or Event).
3. Select a date and time for the photoshoot.
4. Enter the location where the photoshoot will take place.
5. Write a brief message explaining your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**:
	+ Type: Text Field
	+ Description: Please enter your name to help us know who is making the request.
* **Email Address**:
	+ Type: Email Field
	+ Description: Please enter your email address so we can contact you to confirm the photoshoot details.
* **Phone Number**:
	+ Type: Text Field
	+ Description: If you prefer to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number here.
* **Your Website**:
	+ Type: Text Field
	+ Description: If you have a website, feel free to enter it here so we can check out your work.
* **Service Type**:
	+ Type: Select One Field
	+ Description: Choose the type of service you are interested in: Wedding, Portrait, Commercial, or Event.
	+ Options: 
		- Wedding
		- Portrait
		- Commercial
		- Event
* **Date**:
	+ Type: Date Field
	+ Description: Please select the date for the photoshoot.
* **Time**:
	+ Type: Time Field
	+ Description: Choose the time for the photoshoot.
* **Location**:
	+ Type: Text Field
	+ Description: Enter the location where the photoshoot will take place.
* **Message**:
	+ Type: Note Field
	+ Description: Write a brief message explaining your request, including any specific requirements or details you'd like us to know.

## Tips
- Please fill out all required fields (marked with *) accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about anything, feel free to contact us for clarification.
- We will review your form submission and be in touch with you soon after to confirm the details.
