# Sample Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from photographers for a standard photo shoot. It's a template to gather essential details for project management and communication between the photographer and the commissioning organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first name" field (Field 1).
2. Fill in your last name in the "last name" field (Field 2).
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field (Field 3).
4. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field (Field 4).
5. Enter your date of birth in the "date of birth" field (Field 5).
6. Enter your address in the "address" field (Field 6).
7. Specify the number of photos you expect to take in the "photo count" field (Field 7).
8. Choose a date for the photo shoot from the calendar (Field 8).
9. Select the type of photo shoot from the options "Yes" or "No" in the "photo type" field (Field 9).
10. Specify the size of the photo in the "photo size" field (Field 10).
11. Enter the location of the photo shoot in the "photo location" field (Field 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Field 1): Enter your personal first name. This is a general name identifier.
* **Last name** (Field 2): Enter your family name or surname. This is a general name identifier.
* **Email** (Field 3): Enter your email address where the commissioning organization can contact you.
* **Phone** (Field 4): Provide your phone number where the commissioning organization can contact you.
* **Date of birth** (Field 5): Enter the date of your birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (Field 6): Enter your full address for the commissioning organization to contact you.
* **Photo count** (Field 7): Enter the number of photos you plan to take during the shoot.
* **Shoot date** (Field 8): Choose a date from the calendar for the photo shoot.
* **Photo type** (Field 9): Select "Yes" if you are offering portrait photography or "No" if you are not.
* **Photo size** (Field 10): Select multiple options to indicate the size of the photo you plan to take. For example, select "Yes" for large photo, "No" for medium, etc.
* **Photo location** (Field 11): Enter the exact location where you plan to take the photo shoot.
