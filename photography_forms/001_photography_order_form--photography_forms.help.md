# Photography Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information and session details for photography services. It is intended for customers to input their contact details, select their preferred session packages, and schedule a session date and time. The form also allows customers to provide additional notes and email address and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your customer information, including your name and any other relevant details in the "Customer Information" field.
2. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number, in the "Contact Information" field.
3. Select one or more session packages from the list in the "Session Packages" field. You can choose multiple packages.
4. Choose a session date and time in the "Session Date" and "Session Time" fields.
5. Provide any additional notes about your session in the "Notes" field.
6. Ensure you have entered all required information, as indicated by the asterisk (*) next to the field labels.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: Enter your name and any other relevant customer details in this field.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your email address and phone number in this field.
* **Session Packages**: Select one or more session packages that you are interested in. These packages may offer different options and pricing.
* **Session Date**: Choose a date for your photography session.
* **Session Time**: Choose a time slot for your photography session.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments about your session in this field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your session.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you about your session.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have entered all required information, indicated by an asterisk (*) next to the field labels.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Review your selected session packages and dates for accuracy.
- Use the "Notes" field to provide any additional context or information about your session.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
