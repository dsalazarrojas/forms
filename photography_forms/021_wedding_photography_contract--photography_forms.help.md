# Wedding Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients for a wedding photography service. It will help the photographer understand the client's preferences and requirements, ensuring a successful and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Fill out each field with the corresponding information:
	* **Wedding Date**: Enter the date of the wedding.
	* **Contact Person**: Select the person you would like to be the primary contact for the wedding.
	* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
	* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client.
	* **Wedding Time**: Enter the time of the wedding.
	* **Event Type**: Select the type of event (e.g., ceremony, reception, etc.).
	* **Start Address**: Enter the address of the wedding location.
	* **End Address**: Enter the address of the post-wedding location (if different from the start address).
	* **Photographer Credit**: Enter any specific credit or style you would like the photographer to use.
	* **Vendor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the vendor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Date**: A date field where you can enter the date of the wedding.
* **Vendor Name**: A text field to enter the name of the vendor or vendor's company.
* **Contact Person**: A selection field to choose the primary contact person for the wedding.
	+ Option 1: John Doe
	+ Option 2: Jane Doe
	+ Option 3: Both
* **Contact Email**: An email field to enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Client Name**: A text field to enter the name of the client.
* **Wedding Time**: A time field to enter the time of the wedding.
* **Event Type**: A selection field to choose the type of event (e.g., ceremony, reception, etc.).
	+ Option 1: Ceremony
	+ Option 2: Reception
	+ Option 3: Both
	+ Option 4: Other
	+ Option 5: Multiple
* **Start Address**: A text field to enter the address of the wedding location.
* **End Address**: A text field to enter the address of the post-wedding location (if different from the start address).
* **Photographer Credit**: A text field to enter any specific credit or style you would like the photographer to use.
* **Vendor Phone**: A text field to enter the phone number of the vendor.

## Tips

* Double-check the fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure the contact person and email address are correct to facilitate smooth communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the photographer or vendor.
