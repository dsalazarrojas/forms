# Newborn Photography Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from new parents about their preferences and needs regarding our photography services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, contact information, and any other details about yourself.
2. Provide information about your preferred session dates.
3. Review our studio's policies and decide on your agreement.
4. Add any notes or additional preferences you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Details** (`parent_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, contact information, and any other details about yourself.
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, text, optional): Describe your preferred session preferences.
* **Policy** (`policy_consent`, select_one, optional): Select whether you agree or do not agree with our studio's policy.
* **Session Dates** (`session_dates`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred session dates.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or preferences you may have.
* **Parent Preferences** (`parent_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any additional preferences you may have.
* **Studio Policy Consent Date** (`studio_policy_consent_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you agree to our studio policy.
* **Studio Policy Consent Time** (`studio_policy_consent_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you agree to our studio policy.
* **Studio Policy Consent Length** (`studio_policy_consent_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of time you agree to our studio policy.
* **Studio Policy Consent** (`studio_policy_consent`, select_one, optional): Select whether you agree or do not agree with our studio policy.
* **Studio Policy 2** (`studio_policy_consent_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you agree or do not agree with our studio policy 2.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible in your responses.
* Some fields are optional, so feel free to skip them if not applicable.
* If you are unsure about any question, please don't hesitate to contact us.
