# Employee Photo Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Photo Release Form is a form used to obtain consent from employees to use their photograph for marketing, branding, and social media purposes. This form helps to ensure that employees are aware of and agree to how their photos will be used by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Employee Name**, **Date Of Birth**, **Employee Id**, **Email**, and **Phone Number** fields with your relevant information.
2. Select the **Photo Location** where your photo was taken.
3. Enter the **Photo Date** and **Photo Time** when your photo was taken.
4. Provide a brief **Photo Description** of the photo.
5. Choose the intended **Photo Usage** (Marketing and Branding, Social Media, or Other).
6. If you selected "Other" for **Photo Usage**, provide a brief description of how you would like your photo to be used.
7. Check the box next to **Consent** to indicate your agreement to the photo being used.
8. Sign and date your **Signature** to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your birthdate in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1999-02-15).
* **Employee Id**: Enter your employee ID number or badge number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Photo Location**: Describe the location where your photo was taken (e.g., office, park, event).
* **Photo Date**: Enter the date when your photo was taken (e.g., 2022-02-15).
* **Photo Time**: Enter the time when your photo was taken (e.g., 08:00).
* **Photo Description**: Provide a brief description of the photo (e.g., "Taken during a company event").
* **Photo Usage**: Select how you would like your photo to be used (Marketing and Branding, Social Media, or Other).
* **Other**: If you selected "Other" for **Photo Usage**, provide a brief description of how you would like your photo to be used.
* **Consent**: Check the box to indicate your agreement to your photo being used.
* **Signature**: Sign and date your name to confirm your consent.
