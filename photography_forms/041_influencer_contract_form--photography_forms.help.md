# Influencer Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and manage influencer contracts with partners. It captures necessary information for both influencers and advertisers, including contract details, payment terms, and e-signatures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form title.
2. Enter the influencer's name.
3. Enter the advertiser's name.
4. Enter the advertiser's email address.
5. Select the social media accounts to be used for the campaign.
6. Choose the start and end dates of the campaign.
7. Enter the payment fees.
8. Choose the payment method.
9. Select whether you will sign or not sign the electronic signature.
10. Repeat the same steps for the advertiser to sign.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (text): Enter a brief title of the contract.
* **Influencer Name** (text): Enter the name of the influencer who will be working with the advertiser.
* **Advertiser Name** (text): Enter the name of the person or company who will be partnering with the influencer.
* **Advertiser Email** (email): Enter the email address of the advertiser.
* **Social Media Accounts** (select multiple): Choose the social media platforms where the campaign will take place.
* **Campaign Start Date** (date): Enter the start date of the campaign.
* **Campaign End Date** (date): Enter the end date of the campaign.
* **Payment Fees** (number): Enter the payment amount for the campaign.
* **Payment Method** (select one): Choose the payment method between Bank Transfer, Check, and PayPal.
* **E Signature** (select one): Select whether to sign or not sign the electronic signature.
* **Influencer E Signature** (select one): Select whether the influencer will sign or not sign the electronic signature.
* **Advertiser E Signature** (select one): Select whether the advertiser will sign or not sign the electronic signature.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any issues or disputes.
* Use the correct email address for the advertiser.
* Be clear and specific when selecting the payment method and fees.
* Ensure that both the influencer and advertiser sign the electronic signature to confirm the contract terms.
