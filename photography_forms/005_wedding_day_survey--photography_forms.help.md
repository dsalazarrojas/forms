# Wedding Day Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Day Survey form is designed to collect information about a wedding event. It gathers details such as date, time, location, theme, and other aspects of the wedding ceremony and reception.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the wedding date and time in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the wedding theme from the provided options.
3. Choose the wedding venue from the options provided.
4. Determine if guests will be invited and indicate the number of guests.
5. Select the preferred attire for the wedding.
6. Choose the wedding color scheme.
7. Select the wedding floral scheme.
8. Decide on wedding favors.
9. Choose the photo shoot type.
10. Determine the photo coverage and editing preferences.
11. Select the ceremony location and type.
12. Choose the reception location and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the wedding.
* **Wedding Time** (`wedding_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the wedding.
* **Wedding Place** (`wedding_place`, text, required): Enter the location of the wedding.
* **Number of Guests** (`wedding_guests`, number, optional): If you're planning to invite guests, enter the expected number.
* **Wedding Theme** (`wedding_theme`, select_one, required): Choose a theme from Classic, Casual, Beach, Garden, Outdoor, Formal, or Rustic.
* **Wedding Place Name** (`wedding`, text, optional): Enter a descriptive name for the wedding location.
* **Wedding Details** (`wedding_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional details about the wedding.
* **Wedding Venue** (`wedding_venue`, select_one, required): Choose a venue from Home, Restaurant, Hall, Beachside, Hotel, or Outdoor Space.
* **Guest List** (`wedding_guest_list`, select_multiple, optional): Check if guests will be invited and indicate the number.
* **Attire** (`wedding_attire`, select_multiple, required): Choose the preferred attire for the wedding (White Tie, Black Tie, Semi-Formal, Casual, Formal, or Beach).
* **Wedding Color Scheme** (`wedding_color_scheme`, select_one, required): Select a color scheme from Pastel, Neutral, Bold, Monochromatic, or Multicolor.
* **Wedding Floral Scheme** (`wedding_floral_scheme`, select_one, required): Choose a floral scheme from Floral, Floral with Greenery, Green, Dried, or No Flowers.
* **Wedding Favors** (`wedding_favors`, select_multiple, optional): Check if favors will be given and indicate the type.
* **Wedding Favors Message** (`wedding_favors_message`, text, optional): Enter a message for the wedding favors if applicable.
* **Photo Shoot Type** (`photo_shoot_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of photo shoot (Wedding Party, Elopement, Same-Day Party, Family Party, Couple Shoot, or Other).
* **Photo Coverage** (`photo_coverage`, select_multiple, required): Select the level of photo coverage (Full, Partial, Quick, or None).
* **Photo Editing** (`photo_editing`, select_one, optional): Choose the photo editing level (Light, Standard, Advanced, Pro, or None).
* **Ceremony Location** (`ceremony_location`, text, optional): Enter the ceremony location.
* **Ceremony Type** (`ceremony_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of ceremony from Traditional, Civil, Symbolic, Spiritual, or Other.
* **Ceremony Details** (`ceremony_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the ceremony.
* **Reception Location** (`reception_location`, select_one, required): Choose the reception location from Hotel, Restaurant, Private Residence, Home, or Outdoor Space.
* **Reception Time** (`reception_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the reception.
* **Reception Date** (`reception_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the reception.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter contact information for the wedding.
