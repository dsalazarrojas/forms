# Accessibility Project Photography Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent and demographic information from participants in an accessibility project where photography is involved. It captures details such as participant information, project specifics, and consent permissions for the use of photographs.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Start with Participant Information**: Begin by providing your full name and email address. This information is required to identify you as a participant.
2. **Provide Project Details**: Enter the name of the accessibility project you are participating in and the location where the photos will be taken. These details help us understand the context of the photography session.
3. **Consent and Permissions**: Choose the permitted uses of your photographs and select the duration of consent. This is where you grant permission for the use of your photos.
4. **Additional Considerations**: If you are under 18 years old, please enter your parent or guardian's name. Also, describe any special accommodations you may need during the photography session.
5. **Sign and Date**: Finally, date and sign this form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for any questions or concerns.
* **Phone Number**: Optional: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for any additional information.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birth date for age verification purposes.
* **Is the Participant Under 18 Years of Age**: If you are under 18, mark Yes, and a guardian's name will be requested in the next field.
* **Parent or Guardian Name (if applicable)**: If you selected Yes for the previous field, enter your parent or guardian's name.
* **Accessibility Project Name**: Enter the name of the accessibility project you are participating in.
* **Date of Photography Session**: Enter the date when the photography session will take place.
* **Location of Photography**: Enter where the photos will be taken.
* **Consent Granted**: Select Yes, I consent to grant permission for the use of your photographs.
* **Permitted Uses of Photographs**: Select all the permitted uses of your photographs.
* **Duration of Consent**: Choose how long you grant permission for the use of your photos.
* **Special Accommodations**: Describe any accessibility needs or accommodations you may require during the photography session.
* **Additional Conditions or Restrictions**: Note any specific conditions under which your photos may or may not be used.
* **Date of Signature**: Date and sign this form to confirm your consent.
