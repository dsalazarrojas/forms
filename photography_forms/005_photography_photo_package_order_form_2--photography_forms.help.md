# Photography Photo Package Order Form 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a photography event or project, ensuring that all necessary details are captured for efficient and accurate processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event or project (Yes or No)
2. Enter the date of the event
3. Enter the time of the event
4. Provide the location of the event
5. Enter any additional event details
6. Specify the number of guests attending the event
7. Select the photographer involved
8. Choose your photo package options:
	* Photo sessions
	* Photo editing
	* Photo deliveries
	* Photo retouching
	* Print services
9. Select the services you require from the photo package

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event type** (id: 1, type: select_one, required: false): Select the type of event or project you are ordering photos for.
* **Event date** (id: 2, type: date, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event time** (id: 3, type: time, required: false): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event location** (id: 4, type: text, required: false): Provide the location of the event.
* **Event details** (id: 5, type: text, required: false): Enter any additional details about the event.
* **Event guests** (id: 6, type: text, required: false): Specify the number of guests attending the event.
* **Photographer** (id: 7, type: text, required: false): Select the photographer involved.
* **Photo package** (id: 8, type: select_multiple, required: false): Choose your photo package options:
	+ Yes or No for each service
* **Photo sessions** (id: 9, type: select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether to include photo sessions in your package.
* **Photo editing** (id: 10, type: select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether to include photo editing in your package.
* **Photo deliveries** (id: 11, type: select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether to include photo deliveries in your package.
* **Photo retouching** (id: 12, type: select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether to include photo retouching in your package.
* **Print services** (id: 13, type: select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether to include print services in your package.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid any potential issues with the order.
* Review your selection carefully before submitting the form to ensure that your choices are correct.
