# Photography Club Theme Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used by photography club members to cast their votes on various themes. The form collects information on the theme title, description, image, location, time, duration, voting status, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Theme Status: Select the current status of the theme.
	* Voting Open: Choose the time when the voting is open.
2. Fill in any of the optional fields:
	* Photography Group (User Input): Enter the name of the photography group.
	* Event Date: Enter the date of the event.
	* Event Description: Enter a brief description of the event.
	* Theme Name: Enter a name for the theme.
	* Theme Image: Enter a URL or upload an image for the theme.
	* Location: Enter the location of the event.
	* Event Time: Enter the time of the event.
	* Event Duration: Enter the duration of the event.
	* Notes: Add any additional notes about the theme.
	* Contact Phone: Enter your contact phone number.
	* Contact Email: Enter your contact email address.
3. Click the "Submit" button to finalize your input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Theme Status**: Select the current status of the theme. This field is required and should be set to either "Open for Voting", "Closed for Voting", or "Cancelled".
* **Voting Open**: Choose the time when the voting is open. This field is required and has the following options:
	+ 09:00 - 12:00 AM
	+ 12:00 - 03:00 AM
	+ 03:00 - 06:00 AM
	+ 06:00 - 09:00 AM
	+ 09:00 - 12:00 PM
	+ 12:00 - 03:00 PM
	+ 03:00 - 06:00 PM
	+ 06:00 - 09:00 PM
* **Photography Group**: Enter the name of the photography group. This field is optional.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event. This field is optional.
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event. This field is optional.
* **Theme Name**: Enter a name for the theme. This field is optional.
* **Theme Image**: Enter a URL or upload an image for the theme. This field is optional.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the event. This field is optional.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event. This field is optional.
* **Event Duration**: Enter the duration of the event. This field is optional.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the theme. This field is optional.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number. This field is optional.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address. This field is optional.
* **Submit**: Finalize your input.

Note: The fields marked as "required" must be filled in to successfully submit the form.
