# Photography Shoot Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information related to a photography shoot, including client and assistant details, shoot location, equipment, and additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Basic Information
1. Enter the **Form Title** to briefly describe the shoot in a few words.
2. Enter the **Client Name** in full. This is a required field and is used to identify the client for whom the shoot is being done.
3. Choose the **Shoot Date** from the calendar to schedule the shoot.
4. Enter the **Shoot Location** in free-form text. You can include details such as address or any other relevant information.
5. Check if an **Assistant** is present, and if so, enter their name in the provided field.
6. Select **Yes/No** for **Assistant Role** to indicate if the assistant is present or not.
7. Enter the **Assistant Email** to contact them.
8. Enter the **Assistant Phone** number to reach out to them.
9. Enter the **Shoot Type** to describe the type of shoot (e.g. portrait, wedding, etc.).
10. Enter **Shoot Location Details** to provide any additional information about the location.
11. Select **Yes/No** for **Shoot Equipment** to indicate if the shoot requires any special equipment.
12. Enter any **Shoot Notes** to provide additional information about the shoot.
13. Enter the **Client Contact** name in case the client needs to be reached directly.
14. Enter the **Client Email** to contact the client.
15. Enter the **Client Phone** number to reach out to the client.
16. Enter the **Location Address** to provide the location of the shoot.
17. Enter the **Assistant Address** to provide the assistant's address.
18. Enter the **Assistant City** for the assistant's location.
19. Enter the **Assistant State** for the assistant's location.
20. Enter the **Assistant Zip** for the assistant's location.
21. Enter the **Assistant Phone** number for the assistant.
22. Enter **Client Phone 2** in case the client has a different phone number.
23. Enter the **Shoot Time** to schedule the shoot.
24. Enter the **Shoot Duration** to indicate how long the shoot will last.
25. Enter any **Additional Shoot Comments** to provide extra information about the shoot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: Enter a brief description of the shoot in a few words.
* **Client Name**: The full name of the client for whom the shoot is being done.
* **Shoot Date**: Select the date for the shoot from the calendar.
* **Shoot Location**: Enter the location of the shoot, including any details that may be necessary.
* **Assistant**: Enter the name of the assistant if present.
* **Assistant Role**: Select **Yes/No** to indicate if the assistant is present or not.
* **Assistant Email**: Enter the assistant's email to contact them.
* **Assistant Phone**: Enter the assistant's phone number.
* **Shoot Type**: Describe the type of shoot (e.g. portrait, wedding, etc.).
* **Shoot Location Details**: Enter any additional details about the shoot location.
* **Shoot Equipment**: Select **Yes/No** to indicate if the shoot requires any special equipment.
* **Shoot Notes**: Enter any additional information about the shoot.
* **Client Contact**: Enter the client's contact name in case they need to be reached directly.
* **Client Email**: Enter the client's email to contact them.
* **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Location Address**: Enter the location address.
* **Assistant Address**: Enter the assistant's address.
* **Assistant City**: Enter the assistant's city.
* **Assistant State**: Enter the assistant's state.
* **Assistant Zip**: Enter the assistant's zip code.
* **Assistant Phone**: Enter the assistant's phone number.
* **Client Phone 2**: Enter the client's secondary phone number.
* **Shoot Time**: Select the time for the shoot.
* **Shoot Duration**: Enter the duration of the shoot.
* **Additional Shoot Comments**: Enter any extra information about the shoot.
