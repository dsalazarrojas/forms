# lake_monticello_photo_contest - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lake_monticello_photo_contest form is designed to collect user information and photo entries for a photography contest. Please fill out the form to participate in the contest by providing a photo and submitting the required information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select a category for your photo (Nature, City, People, Landmarks).
3. Provide a description for your photo (if desired).
4. Enter your email address and contact phone number (if desired).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required): Select a category for your photo from the options provided (Nature, City, People, Landmarks).
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description for your photo, if desired. This will help us get more context about your photo.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone Ext** (`contact_phone_ext`, text, optional): Enter any phone extension for your contact phone number.
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Please enter some basic information about yourself, including any necessary details to complete the form.
* **Photo** (`upload_image`, text, optional): Upload or provide information about your photo entry.
* **Photo Details** (`photo_details`, text, optional): Provide some context about your photo entry.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the photo was taken, if desired.

## Tips
- Make sure to select a valid category for your photo.
- Enter accurate and up-to-date contact information.
- Provide a brief and clear description of your photo.
- Use the "User Information" field to provide necessary details about yourself.
- Upload or provide information about your photo entry, and provide any necessary details about it.
