# Holiday Mini Session RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Mini Session RSVP Form is designed to collect essential details from clients for their scheduled holiday mini sessions. This form will help us confirm their availability and preferences, ensuring a smooth experience for both the client and our photography team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Guest Email" field.
3. Indicate your RSVP status by selecting "True", "False", or "Unable to attend" in the "RSVP" field.
4. If unable to attend, provide any special requests or notes in the "Notes" field.
5. Confirm your session details by selecting "True", "False", or "Unable to attend" in the corresponding fields (Session Date, Session Time, Session Location, etc.).
6. If you have any special requests, indicate them in the "Special Requests" field.
7. Confirm your availability and preferences by selecting "True", "False", or "Unable to attend" in the "Confirmation" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, `email`, optional): Please provide your email address.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, `select_one`, optional): Select "True" if you will attend, "False" if you won't, or "Unable to attend" if you cannot attend.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): If unable to attend, please provide any special requests or notes.
* **Session Date Confirmation** (`session_date_confirmation`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm your availability for the session by selecting "True", "False", or "Unable to attend".
* **Session Time Confirmation** (`session_time_confirmation`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm your availability for the session time by selecting "True", "False", or "Unable to attend".
* **Session Location Confirmation** (`session_location_confirmation`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm your availability for the session location by selecting "True", "False", or "Unable to attend".
* **Special Requests Confirmation** (`special_requests_confirmation`, `select_one`, optional): If you have any special requests, indicate them by selecting "True" or "Unable to attend".
* **Holiday Mini Session Confirmation** (`holiday_mini_session_confirmation`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm your attendance by selecting "True", "False", or "Unable to attend".
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have any special requests, please indicate them.
* **Photographer Availability Confirmation** (`photographer_availability_confirmation`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm your availability for the photographer by selecting "True", "False", or "Unable to attend".
* **Photographer Notes** (`photographer_notes`, `note`, optional): If unable to attend, please provide any notes or requests for the photographer.
* **Photographer Note** (`photographer_note`, `note`, optional): If unable to attend, please provide any notes or requests for the photographer.
* **Holiday Mini Session Notes** (`holiday_mini_session_notes`, `note`, optional): If unable to attend, please provide any notes or requests for the holiday mini session.

Note: Please fill out the form once and accurately, and ensure that you're providing correct information to avoid confusion and miscommunication.
