> # Patient Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Photo Release Form is used to obtain a patient's consent for using their photograph for medical purposes. This form ensures that the patient's privacy and consent is respected and documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of consent in the "Consent Date" field, if applicable.
2. Provide your name and title in the "Photographer Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
4. Choose whether you have obtained the patient's consent for using their photograph by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Photo Release Consent" field.
5. If you have obtained consent, enter the date and details of the photograph capture in the "Date Of Capture" and "Capture Details" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Date**: Enter the date when the patient gave their consent for using their photograph.
* **Photographer Name**: Enter your name and title, for example, "John Doe, MD".
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical chart.
* **Photo Release Consent**: Choose "Yes" if you have obtained the patient's consent for using their photograph, and "No" if they have not.
* **Date Of Capture**: If the patient gave consent for using their photograph, enter the date when the photograph was taken.
* **Capture Details**: Enter a brief description of when, where, and how the photograph was taken.
