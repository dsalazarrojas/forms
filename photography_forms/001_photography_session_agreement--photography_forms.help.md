# photography_session_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential details from clients before a photography session, ensuring a smooth and efficient experience for both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Review the form**: Take a moment to review the questions and fields on the form.
2. **Read each section carefully**: Complete each section step-by-step, ensuring you understand the requirements and options presented.
3. **Provide accurate information**: Answer questions truthfully and thoroughly, providing all necessary details.
4. **Sign and date the form**: Complete the electronic signature fields with your digital signature, indicating your agreement to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Questions (Page 1)**:
	+ **General Questions** (`general_questions`, text, optional): This section is for capturing any general questions or comments you'd like to share with the photographer.
* **Location (Page 2)**:
	+ **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you grant permission for the photographer to capture images at your preferred location(s).
* **Date/Time (Page 3)**:
	+ **Date/Time** (`date_time`, date, optional): Choose a date and time for the photography session.
* **Session Details (Page 4)**:
	+ **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional session-specific details, such as clothing or props.
* **Coverage (Page 5)**:
	+ **Coverage** (`coverage`, select_one, optional): Select the type of photography coverage you've agreed upon (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
* **Costs (Page 6)**:
	+ **Costs** (`costs`, number, optional): Enter the costs associated with the photography session.
* **Terms and Conditions (Page 7)**:
	+ **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_conditions`, text, optional): Review and agree to the terms and conditions outlined by the photographer.
* **E-Signature (Page 8)**:
	+ **E-Signature** (`e_signature`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" or "No".
	+ **E-Signature Field** (`e_signature_field`, text, optional): Enter your name for electronic signature purposes.
	+ **E-Signature Date** (`e_signature_date`, date, optional): Select the date you electronically sign the form.
	+ **E-Signature Time** (`e_signature_time`, time, optional): Choose the time you electronically sign the form.
	+ **E-Signature IP** (`e_signature_ip`, text, optional): Enter your IP address for electronic signature verification.
	+ **E-Signature Notes** (`e_signature_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes related to the electronic signature.
* **Photography License (Page 9)**:
	+ **Photography License** (`photography_license`, text, optional): Review and acknowledge the photography license terms.
* **Photography Release (Page 10)**:
	+ **Photography Release** (`photography_release`, text, optional): Sign the photography release to grant permission for the images' use.
