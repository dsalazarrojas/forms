# Photography Session Booking Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for booking a photography session with a client. It captures essential information about the client and photographer, and includes a contract that outlines the terms and conditions of the session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information. The form is divided into several sections.
2. Provide your photographer's information, including their name, contact details, and any additional notes.
3. Enter the client's information, including their name, contact details, and any additional notes.
4. Enter the session details, such as the date, time, location, and number of participants.
5. Review and agree to the contract terms, which outline the expectations and responsibilities of both the client and photographer.
6. Sign the contract to confirm your agreement.
7. Sign the form as the client to confirm your acceptance of the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Photographer Information (Text)**: Enter the name and contact details of the photographer handling the session.
  • This field is used to record the photographer's details for contact and communication purposes.
- **Client Information (Text)**: Enter the name and contact details of the person or organization booking the session.
  • This field is used to record the client's details for billing and communication purposes.
- **Session Details (Text)**: Enter the date, time, location, and number of participants for the session.
  • This field is used to record the session specifics to ensure both parties are on the same page.
- **Contract Terms (Text)**: Read and agree to the contract that outlines the expectations and responsibilities of both the client and photographer.
  • This field is an essential part of the booking process, outlining what the client and photographer agree to.
- **Client Agreement (Text)**: Enter your agreement to the contract terms.
  • This field is used to confirm the client's acceptance of the contract terms.
- **Photographer Signature (Text)**: Sign to confirm the photographer's acceptance of the contract terms.
  • This field is used to confirm the photographer's agreement to the contract terms.
- **Client Signature (Text)**: Sign to confirm your acceptance of the contract terms.
  • This field is used to confirm the client's agreement to the contract terms.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
- Review the contract terms carefully before signing.
- Sign both the photographer and client signature fields to complete the form.
- Keep the form as a record of the booking agreement.
