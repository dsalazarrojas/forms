# Photography Payment Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Payment Plan Form is designed to collect information about photography payment plans. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the user's photography payment plan is properly configured and meets their expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate product from the "select_product" field.
2. Enter the price of the photography service you are purchasing.
3. Enter any applicable discounts.
4. Choose a date and time for the photography service.
5. Add any additional notes about the photography service.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Select the category or categories that apply to your photography service.
9. Add any relevant tags to describe your photography service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Photography Payment Plan Form** (`text`, required/optional): This field is not a standard field and is likely used to identify the form's purpose or title.
- **select_product** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select the product you are purchasing, which can be 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **price** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the price of the photography service you are purchasing.
- **discount** (`number`, required/optional): Enter any applicable discounts for the photography service.
- **date** (`date`, required/optional): Choose a date for the photography service.
- **time** (`time`, required/optional): Choose a time for the photography service.
- **note** (`note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the photography service.
- **email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **category** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the categories that apply to your photography service, which can be 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **tags** (`text`, required/optional): Add any relevant tags to describe your photography service.
