# photo_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photo release form is used to obtain a client's agreement to release their photos for use. This form is essential for ensuring that clients understand and agree to the terms of photo usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the client(s) for whom you are obtaining the photo release from. You can choose one or multiple clients.
2.  Enter the date the photo release is being obtained.
3.  Enter the time the photo release is being obtained.
4.  Enter the name of the photographer who took the photos.
5.  Enter a contact method to reach out to the client in case of any issues.
6.  Sign the photo release form to indicate your agreement to the photo usage terms.
7.  Provide a brief description of the photo release.
8.  Choose whether you agree or disagree with the photo usage terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client** (Select Multiple): Select the client(s) for whom you are obtaining the photo release. Multiple clients can be chosen.
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date the photo release is being obtained. (Not required, but recommended for record-keeping.)
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time the photo release is being obtained. (Not required, but recommended for timestamping.)
* **Photographer** (Text): Enter the name of the photographer who took the photos.
* **Contact** (Text): Enter a contact method to reach out to the client in case of any issues.
* **Signature** (Note): Sign the photo release form to indicate your agreement to the photo usage terms.
* **Description** (Text): Provide a brief description of the photo release.
* **Agreement** (Select One): Choose whether you agree or disagree with the photo usage terms. (Required field.)
