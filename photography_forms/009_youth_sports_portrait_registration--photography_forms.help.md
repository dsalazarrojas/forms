# Youth Sports Portrait Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for capturing athlete and parent information for a youth sports event. It collects details about the athlete, parent or guardian contact information, photo package preferences, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Review each field carefully to ensure completeness and correctness.
* Use the provided options for fields with dropdown selections.
* Double-check for errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Details (`athlete_details`, text, optional):** Enter the athlete's name and any other relevant personal details.
* **Parent Contacts (`parent_contacts`, text, optional):** Provide the parent or guardian's name, phone number, and email address.
* **Photo Package (`photo_package`, select_one, optional):** Select the preferred photo package type (Basic, Deluxe, or Premium).
* **Athlete Photo Consent (`athlete_photo_consent`, select_multiple, optional):** Indicate whether the athlete consents to having their photo taken (Yes, No, or Maybe).
* **School Details (`school_details`, text, optional):** Enter the athlete's school name and grade level.
* **League Details (`league_details`, text, optional):** Enter the athlete's league or team name.
* **Parent Consent (`parent_contact_consent`, text, optional):** Confirm that a parent or guardian has reviewed and agreed to the photo consent.
* **Additional Info (`athlete_additional_info`, text, optional):** Provide any extra information or comments about the athlete.

## Tips
* Double-check for spelling errors and completeness before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language when filling in the text fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the provided explanations or contact the form administrator.
