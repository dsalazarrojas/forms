# photography contest - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photography contest form is designed for photographers to submit their work and provide additional details about the photo and themselves. This form is used for collecting information for a contest or competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your photo in the "Title" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your photo in the "Description" field.
3. Enter the URL of your photo in the "Photo URL" field.
4. Enter your name as a photographer in the "Photographer's Name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Select a date for the photo in the "Date" field.
8. Select a time for the photo in the "Time" field.
9. Enter any additional information about the photo in the "Note" field.
10. Select a category for the photo in the "Category" field.
11. Select any relevant tags for the photo in the "Tags" field.
12. Upload multiple images for the photo by entering the URLs in the corresponding "Image 1", "Image 2", "Image 3", "Image 4", and "Image 5" fields.
