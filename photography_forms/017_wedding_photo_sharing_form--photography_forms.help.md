# Wedding Photo Sharing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their wedding or engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields.
2. Select the correct options for each field as required.
3. Ensure that you provide a valid email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form ID**: A unique identifier for the form. Enter your preferred ID.
* **Wedding Photo Sharing Form**: A brief description of the form. This field is required.
* **Are you married or engaged?**: Select 'Yes' if you are married or engaged, 'No' otherwise. This field is required.
* **Date of the wedding**: Enter the date of your wedding or engagement in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Time of the wedding**: Enter the time of your wedding or engagement in the format 'HH:MM'.
* **Shared Photos**: Select the type of photos you want to share: 'Album' or 'Video'. This field is required.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information about your wedding or engagement.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Reunion Photos**: Select the options you would like to share. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Enter valid email and phone numbers.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us for assistance.
