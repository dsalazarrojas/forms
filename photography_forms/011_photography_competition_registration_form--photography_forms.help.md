# photography_competition_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants in a photography competition. It gathers basic contact information, photo submission details, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out all required fields, marked with a *.
* Fill out optional fields only if you have relevant information to provide.
* Ensure that the email address you provide is valid and up-to-date.
* Select only the photo URLs that you own or have permission to use.
* Enter the date of the photo in the correct format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your surname as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional):
	+ Enter a valid and up-to-date email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Photo URL** (`photo_url`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select only the URLs of the photos you own or have permission to use.
* **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, `text`, optional):
	+ Briefly describe your photo, including the title, location, and any notable features.
* **Photo Credit** (`photo_credit`, `text`, optional):
	+ If you are not the copyright holder, enter the name and contact information of the copyright holder.
* **Photo Date** (`photo_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date the photo was taken in the correct format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).

## Tips

* Ensure that all selected photo URLs are valid and up-to-date.
* Be honest about your photo ownership and permissions.
