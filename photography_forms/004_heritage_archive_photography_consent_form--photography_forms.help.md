# Heritage Archive Photography Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and consent from subjects of photographs to be archived, displayed, or used in various ways.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the subject's information in the "Subject Information" field.
2. Provide details about the photograph in the "Photograph Information" field.
3. Specify the intended use of the photograph by selecting the desired option in the "Photograph Use" field.
4. If necessary, provide additional details or conditions for use in the "Permission Details" field.
5. Enter the archive name where the photograph will be stored in the "Archive Name" field.
6. Provide information about the photographer in the "Photographer Information" field.
7. (Optional) Enter a URL to a location where the photograph can be viewed online in the "Image URL" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subject Information** (Text, Optional): Enter the subject's name, contact information, and any relevant details.
* **Photograph Information** (Text, Optional): Describe the photograph, including the date and location it was taken.
* **Permission Details** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional conditions or instructions for the use of the photograph.
* **Photograph Use** (Select One, Required): Choose how the photograph will be used, such as "Archive", "Museum", "Website", "Social Media", "Print", or "Display".
* **Subject Permission** (Note, Optional): Enter a brief note about the subject's permission and any conditions or restrictions.
* **Archive Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the archive where the photograph will be stored.
* **Photographer Information** (Text, Optional): Enter your name, contact information, and any relevant details.
* **Image URL** (Text, Optional): Enter the URL of a location where the photograph can be viewed online, if available.
