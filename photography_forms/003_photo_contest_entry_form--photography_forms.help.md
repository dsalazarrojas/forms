# Photo Contest Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for users to submit their photo entries for a photo contest. It captures basic contact information and allows users to upload and describe their photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter a title for your photo in the "Photo Title" field.
5. Enter a caption for your photo in the "Photo Caption" field.
6. Upload your photo by clicking on the "Upload Photo" field.
7. Select the category that best describes your photo from the "Select Category" dropdown menu.
8. If you have taken your photo at a specific date and time, enter it in the "Date Taken" field.
9. If you wish, select multiple categories that best describe your photo in the "Select Multiple Category" dropdown menu.
10. If you have any further comments or notes, enter them in the "Note" field.
11. If you have additional photos you wish to upload, you can do so in the "Upload Additional Photo" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to contact you for further communication.
* **Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name to be associated with your photo entry.
* **Phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Photo Title** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a title for your photo to give it a brief description.
* **Photo Caption** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a short description of your photo.
* **Upload Photo** (`text`, required/optional): Upload your photo to be displayed in the contest.
* **Select Category** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select the category that best describes your photo from the dropdown menu.
* **Date Taken** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date you took your photo.
* **Select Multiple Category** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select multiple categories that best describe your photo from the dropdown menu.
* **Time Taken** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time you took your photo.
* **Note** (`note`, required/optional): Enter any further comments or notes about your photo.
* **Upload Additional Photo** (`text`, required/optional): Upload additional photos for your entry.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure your photo entry is complete.
* You can upload photos of any format, including JPEG, PNG, and other common image formats.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
* We reserve the right to review and moderate all photo entries to ensure they meet the contest rules and guidelines.
