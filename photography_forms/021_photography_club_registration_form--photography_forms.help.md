# Photography Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help you complete the photography club registration form accurately and efficiently. Please read on to learn about each field and how to fill it out correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first and last name in the respective fields.
2.  Enter your email address for communication with the club.
3.  Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
4.  Select your date of birth.
5.  Choose your level of photography experience (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
6.  Choose your photography style (Portrait, Landscape, or Wildlife).
7.  Select how often you shoot (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
8.  Select your membership status (Active, Pending, or Inactive).
9.  Provide a brief bio about yourself.
10.  If you have a website, provide the URL.
11.  Select your social media platforms (Facebook, Instagram, or Twitter).
12.  Upload your photo to showcase your photography work.
13.  Read and agree to the photo license terms.
14.  Select your photo license level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
15.  Provide your member since date.
16.  Select your last activity frequency (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
17.  Select your last membership renewal date.
18.  Enter your photo license expiration date.
19.  Provide any additional notes about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and updates from the club.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Select your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Photography License**: Choose your level of photography experience (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Photography Equipment**: Select all the types of photography equipment you use (DSLR, Mirrorless, or Point and Shoot).
* **Photography Style**: Choose your photography style (Portrait, Landscape, or Wildlife).
* **How often do you shoot?**: Select how often you shoot photographs (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **What's your experience level?**: Choose your level of photography experience (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **What genre do you shoot?**: Choose the type of photography you do (Portrait, Landscape, or Wildlife).
* **Bio**: Enter a brief description about yourself and your photography background.
* **Member Since**: Enter the date you joined the club.
* **Last Activity Date**: Enter the date of your last activity in the club.
* **Last Activity Frequency**: Select the frequency of your last activity (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Last Membership Renewal**: Enter the date of your last membership renewal.
* **Photo License Expiration**: Enter the date of your photo license expiration.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about yourself or your photography experience.
