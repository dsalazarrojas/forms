# Wedding Shot List Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Shot List Worksheet is a tool for photographers to document the details of a wedding event. It helps to capture essential information such as the wedding date, location, and contact information of the couple.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the details of the wedding event.
2. Enter the photographer's name and any notes or insights you have about the event.
3. Provide the couple's name and contact information.
4. Enter the number of guests attending the wedding.
5. Enter the wedding location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer's Name** (photographer_name, text, optional): Enter your name as the photographer for this wedding event.
* **Date of the Wedding** (date_of_the_wedding, text, optional): Enter the date of the wedding, including the month, day, and year.
* **Location of the Wedding** (location_of_the_wedding, text, optional): Enter the location of the wedding event, including the venue name, address, or city.
* **Number of Guests** (number_of_guests, number, optional): Enter the estimated number of guests attending the wedding.
* **Photographer's Notes** (photographer_notes, text, optional): Enter any notes or insights you have as the photographer about the event, such as special moments or requests from the couple.
* **Couple's Name** (couple_name, text, optional): Enter the names of the couple getting married.
* **Couple's Contact Information** (couple_contact_info, text, optional): Enter the couple's contact information, such as their email address, phone number, or emergency contact details.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy to ensure you have all the necessary information.
* Make sure to save a copy of the completed form for your reference during and after the event.
* You can add any additional comments or notes as you see fit to ensure you're prepared for the wedding event.
