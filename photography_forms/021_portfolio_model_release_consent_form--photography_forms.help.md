# portfolio_model_release_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The portfolio model release consent form is a crucial document that ensures users understand the terms and conditions of sharing their portfolio model with others. It's essential to complete this form carefully to avoid any potential issues or miscommunications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  On the **User Information** page, enter your name and any other relevant contact information as required.
2.  On the **Model Information** page, select the correct response to the question about releasing the model, indicating whether you give your consent to release the model or not.
3.  On the **Release Details** page, enter any additional information about the model's release, such as the reason for the release or any specific conditions.
4.  On the **Date** page, select the date you want to release the model.
5.  Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (User Information, text, required): This section is where you enter your name and any other relevant contact information as required. It's essential to ensure your identity is accurately represented for the model's release process.
* **Model Information** (Model Information, select_multiple, optional): Here, you select whether you give your consent to release the model or not. Choose the correct response based on your understanding of the model's terms and conditions.
* **Release Details** (Release Details, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the model's release, such as the reason for the release or any specific conditions. This section helps clarify the context of the model's release.
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): Select the date you want to release the model, ensuring you're aware of the timeline for the release process.
* **Signature** (Signature, note, optional): Sign and date this form to confirm your consent for releasing the model, which is a critical step in the process. 

## Tips
* Please ensure you understand the terms and conditions before completing this form.
* Review the model's details carefully before releasing it.
* This form is an essential part of the model release process; complete it carefully to avoid any issues.
