# Equine Photography Session Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about your equine photography session preferences and contact details. It will help us understand your requirements and provide you with the best experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, please ensure you have all the necessary information ready, including your preferred dates and times for the session.
2. Select your preferred dates and time slots from the options provided in the **Preferred Dates** field.
3. In the **Preferred Time** field, select your preferred time for the session.
4. In the **Package Preferences** field, choose the package that best suits your needs from the options provided.
5. Finally, enter your **Contact Info** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): This field is a text input where you can enter any additional information about your session preferences or requirements.
* **Preferred Dates** (`preferred_dates`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more dates for your photography session. You can choose from the options provided.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Select a specific time for your photography session. Please note that time slots may vary based on your chosen date.
* **Package Preferences** (`package_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from the options to select the package that best suits your needs and preferences.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information (e.g., name, email, phone number) so we can get in touch with you about your session details.

## Tips
- Be accurate and clear in your responses to ensure we understand your requirements correctly.
- If you have any specific questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
- Please allow sufficient time for us to respond to your inquiry, and we'll be in touch soon.
