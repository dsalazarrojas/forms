# Photography Club Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Club Attendance Form is designed to collect information from members or attendees of a photography club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the meeting date you are attending or want to attend.
2. Choose the meeting time you will be attending or want to attend.
3. Enter your name.
4. Provide your contact information (phone number or email).
5. Write any comments or notes you have about the meeting (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date (meeting_date)**: Enter the date of the meeting you are attending or want to attend.
* **Meeting Time (meeting_time)**: Choose the time of the meeting you are attending or want to attend.
* **Attendees (attendees)**: Enter the names of the members or attendees who will be present.
* **Name (name)**: Enter your name, as you would like to be referred to within the club.
* **Contact Info (contact_info)**: Enter your phone number or any other contact information you would like to share.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Comments (comments)**: Enter any comments, notes, or suggestions you have about the meeting or the club (optional).

## Tips
* Be sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that we can contact you efficiently.
* If you are attending the meeting, please try to be on time.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special needs, please let us know in your comments.
