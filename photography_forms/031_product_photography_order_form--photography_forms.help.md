# Product Photography Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect the necessary information for a product photography order. You will be asked to provide your customer's details, product name, and quantity, as well as the image details for the products you want to photograph.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Provide the name of the product(s) you want to photograph in the "Product Name" field.
3. Enter the quantity of the product(s) you want to photograph in the "Quantity" field.
4. Upload the product image file in the "Product Image" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer who will be receiving the photographed product.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product you want to photograph.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of products you want to photograph.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, optional): Upload the image file of the product you want to photograph.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and legible customer and product details to ensure smooth processing of your order.
* You can upload multiple product image files if needed.
* If you have any specific requirements or requests for the photography order, please provide them in the "Additional Notes" field (not shown in this example, but you can add one if needed).
