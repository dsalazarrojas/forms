# Camera Inventory Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps organizations and businesses manage their camera inventory by collecting crucial information about each camera.

## How To Complete This Form
- To begin, fill out the required fields:
	* Camera Equipment ID: Enter the unique identifier assigned to this camera.
	* Camera Model: Enter the name of the camera's manufacturer and model.
	* Serial Number: Enter the device serial number.
	* Condition: Select the current status of the camera (New, Good, Fair, Needs Repair).
	* Storage Location: Enter where the camera is stored.
- The following fields are optional:
	* Purchase Date: If you have records, enter the date this camera was purchased.
	* Last Service Date: If you have records, enter the date of the last maintenance.
	* Assigned To: If applicable, enter the person currently responsible for the camera.
	* Warranty Expiration Date: If the camera has a warranty, enter its expiration date.
	* Notes: Add any additional comments or remarks about this camera.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Equipment ID** (Equipment ID, text, required): A unique identifier assigned to the camera for tracking purposes.
- **Camera Model** (Camera Model, text, required): Enter the name of the camera's manufacturer and model.
- **Serial Number** (Serial Number, text, required): Enter the device's serial number for reference and tracking.
- **Purchase Date** (Date, date, optional): If you have records, enter the date the camera was purchased.
- **Condition** (select_one, required): Select the current status of the camera (New, Good, Fair, Needs Repair).
- **Storage Location** (Text, required): Enter where the camera is stored.
- **Assigned To** (Text, optional): If applicable, enter the person currently responsible for the camera.
- **Last Service Date** (Date, optional): If you have records, enter the date of the last maintenance.
- **Warranty Expiration Date** (Date, optional): If the camera has a warranty, enter its expiration date.
- **Notes** (Text, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks about this camera.
- **Warranty Expiration Date** (Date, optional): If the camera has a warranty, enter its expiration date.

## Tips
- Be accurate when filling out the camera's serial number and purchase date.
- Make sure to update the form if there are any changes to the camera's condition, storage location, or warranty status.
- If the camera is damaged or lost, report it immediately to prevent misplacing it.
