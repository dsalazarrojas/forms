# Photoshoot Client Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture and document crucial information from clients before a photo shoot, ensuring a smooth and successful collaboration between the photographer and the client.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact information, including name, email address, and phone number.
2. Specify the date and duration of the photography session.
3. Choose a location for the shoot.
4. Provide details about the photography equipment to be used during the session.
5. Read and sign the agreement to acknowledge your understanding of the rights and responsibilities.
6. Sign the agreement to confirm your commitment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your contact address, email address, and phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and payment confirmation.
* **Phone** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication and scheduling purposes.
* **Photographer** (`photographer_name`, text, required): Enter your photographer's name and contact information.
* **Photography Session** (`photography_session`, date, required): Choose the date for the photography session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, time, required): Specify the duration of the photography session.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Choose the location where the photography session will take place.
* **Photo Shoot Details** (`photo_shoot_details`, text, required): Describe the specifics of the photo shoot, such as clothing, props, and other requirements.
* **Photography Equipment** (`photography_equipment`, text, required): Specify the equipment to be used during the photography session.
* **Client's Rights** (`client_rights`, note, required): Please read and understand the photographer's rights and responsibilities before signing the agreement.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, note, required): Read and sign the agreement to acknowledge your understanding of the rights and responsibilities.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): This field is for your signature, indicating your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required): This field is for the client's signature, indicating their agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Photo Shoot Details** (`photo_shoot_details`, text, required): Describe the specifics of the photo shoot, such as the theme, style, and any specific requirements.
* **Photography Session** (`photography_session`, date, required): Choose the date for the photography session.
