# Team Maple States Color Match Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Maple States Color Match Form is a tool to help new business starters create a color match template for their business. It will guide you through a series of questions and provide visual aids to aid in the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through the text on the first page to understand the context and purpose of the form.
2. Select an image from the image library on the second page to aid in the color-matching process.
3. Upload your own image on the third page, if needed.
4. Enter your email and phone number on the respective pages for contact information.
5. Select a date for the event or launch date of your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Maple States Color Match Form** (`welcome`, note, optional): This is the introduction page of the form. Please read through it to understand the purpose and context of the form.
* **color-matching-exercise** (`team_maple_states_color_form_questions`, text, optional): This is a text-based exercise to help you understand the color-matching process. It's a good idea to read through it before proceeding.
* **color-matching-image** (`team_maple_states_color_match_form_image`, select_one, optional): Select an image from the library to aid in the color-matching process. This image will be used as a visual aid.
* **upload-image** (`team_maple_states_color_match_form_image_upload`, text, optional): If you have an image that you'd like to use for the color-matching process, you can upload it here. This field is optional.
* **email** (`team_maple_states_color_match_form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes. This field is for your convenience so that we can get in touch with you.
* **phone** (`team_maple_states_color_match_form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This field is for your convenience so that we can get in touch with you.
* **date** (`team_maple_states_color_match_form_date`, date, optional): Select a date for the event or launch date of your business. This field is optional but recommended for planning purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to read through the text on the first page to fully understand the context and purpose of the form.
* Select an image that is relevant to your business or industry to aid in the color-matching process.
* Use the uploaded image feature if you have an image that's not in our library.
* Don't forget to enter your contact information so that we can get in touch with you for any follow-up questions or concerns.
