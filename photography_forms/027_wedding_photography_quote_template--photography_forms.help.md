# Wedding Photography Quote Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Photography Quote Template is a form used to collect information from clients and photographers to generate a quote for the wedding photography services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details as a photographer, including your name, email, phone number, experience, and education.
2.  Select your photography packages and services offered.
3.  Fill in the client's personal details, including their name, email, phone number, location, and date.
4.  Select the client's preferred photography package and services.
5.  Fill in the quote and any additional notes for the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Wedding Quote (1)**: Enter a brief description of the wedding, including details about the location, date, and other relevant information.
*   **Location (2)**: Enter the location of the wedding venue.
*   **Wedding Date (3)**: Enter the date of the wedding.
*   **Photographer (4)**: Enter your name as the photographer.
*   **Email (5)**: Enter your email address.
*   **Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Standard Package (7)**: Select the standard package you wish to offer the client.
*   **Hours (8)**: Enter the number of hours you will work on the wedding.
*   **Rate (9)**: Enter your rate for the photography services.
*   **Experience (10)**: Briefly describe your experience as a photographer.
*   **Specialties (11)**: List any specialties you offer as a photographer.
*   **Education (12)**: List your educational background.
*   **Resume (13)**: Upload a resume to showcase your experience and qualifications.
*   **Gallery (14)**: Upload images of your previous work.
*   **Links (15)**: Provide any relevant links to your social media or website.
*   **Services (16)**: Select the services you offer as a photographer.
*   **Client (17)**: Enter the client's name.
*   **Client Email (18)**: Enter the client's email address.
*   **Client Phone (19)**: Enter the client's phone number.
*   **Client Location (20)**: Enter the client's location.
*   **Date (21)**: Enter the client's date of availability.
*   **Package Option 2 (22)**: Select the second package option you wish to offer the client.
*   **Quote (23)**: Enter the quote for the client.
*   **Signature (24)**: Sign the quote to confirm.
*   **Notes (25)**: Add any additional notes for the client.
