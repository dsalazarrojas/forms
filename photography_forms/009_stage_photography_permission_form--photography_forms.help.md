# Stage Photography Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission from participants to capture their photograph during an event. The form is used to gather information about the participant, the event, and the photographer, as well as to obtain consent for photography and signatures from both the participant and the photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete the participant information section with your details.
2. Select the options that apply to you for the participant photo consent.
3. Provide any additional information as needed in the additional info section.
4. Fill in the date and time that the photography will be captured.
5. Enter the name and contact information of the photographer.
6. Enter the start and end time of the event.
7. Enter the date of the event.
8. Sign and date the form with your signature.
9. Sign and date the form with the photographer's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: Enter your details such as name, address, and contact number. This information is used to identify you as the participant.
* **Event Info**: Enter the details of the event such as the event description, location, and start and end times.
* **Photographer Information**: Enter the name and contact information of the photographer who will be capturing your photo.
* **Participant Photo Consent**: Select all that apply for the options provided. This will indicate your consent for photography to be captured.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information that you want to provide about yourself or the event.
* **Photo Capture Date and Time**: Enter the date and time that the photography will be captured.
* **Photo Capture Time**: Enter the time of day when the photography will be captured.
* **Photographer Name**: Enter the name of the photographer who will be capturing your photo.
* **Photographer Contact**: Enter the contact information of the photographer.
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign and date your signature to indicate your consent for photography to be captured.
* **Photographer Signature**: Sign and date your signature to indicate your consent for capturing the participant's photo.
* **Event Venue**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm your agreement to the terms of the form.
* **Photographer Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm your agreement to the terms of the form.
