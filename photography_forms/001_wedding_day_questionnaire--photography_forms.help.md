# Wedding Day Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients about their wedding day details, including ceremony types, contact information, sponsors, organizers, special add-ons, and extra information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the basic information about the wedding, including any basic details that you want to share with us.
2. Select the type of ceremony you would like to have (Cermony A, Cermony B, or Cermony C).
3. Provide your contact details so we can get in touch with you.
4. If applicable, choose any sponsors for your wedding day.
5. Choose any special add-ons you would like to have.
6. Provide any extra information you feel is relevant to your wedding day.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Info**: This field is used to capture any basic information about the wedding. Please type in any details you think we should know about.
* **Ceremonies**: This field allows you to select the type of ceremony you would like to have. Please choose one of the following options: Cermony A, Cermony B, or Cermony C.
* **Contact Details**: This field is used to capture your contact information so we can get in touch with you. Please provide your name, email, and phone number.
* **Sponsors**: This field allows you to choose any sponsors for your wedding day. Please choose any of the following options: Sponsor A, Sponsor B, or Sponsor C.
* **Organizers**: This field is used to choose any organizers for your wedding day. Please choose any of the following options: Organizer A, Organizer B, or Organizer C.
* **Requests**: This field is used to capture any extra information about your wedding day. Please type in any details you feel are relevant.
* **Special Add-ons**: This field allows you to choose any special add-ons for your wedding day. Please choose any of the following options: Add-on A, Add-on B, or Add-on C.
* **Extra Info**: This field is used to capture any extra information about your wedding day. Please type in any details you feel are relevant.
