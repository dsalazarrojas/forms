# Portrait Photography Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for clients and customers to provide feedback on their portrait photography session experiences. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form to help us understand your thoughts and satisfaction with our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these simple steps:

* Click on each page to navigate between them.
* Fill out the required fields (session_rating and overall_rating) and provide any additional comments or feedback in the comments field.
* Select one or more images from the favorite_images dropdown that best represent your favorite photos from the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Rating
* **Session Rating** (`session_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".

### Overall Rating
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any additional feedback or comments about your session. This is a great opportunity to provide specific details about what we did well or areas for improvement.

### Favorite Images
* **Favorite Images** (`favorite_images`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more images from your session that you enjoyed the most.
