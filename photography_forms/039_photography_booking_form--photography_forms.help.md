# Photography Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information from customers (or photographers) to book a photography session. It is used to gather details such as the photographer's information, client information, session details, payment, product, contact, services, photoshoot date, photoshoot time, photos taken, price, and photographer's experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety or skip fields that do not apply to your situation.
2. Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Photographer Information** (photographer_information):
  Enter your name and any other relevant information as a photographer.
  (Type: text, Required: false)

- **Client Information** (client_information):
  Enter your name and any other relevant information as a client.
  (Type: text, Required: false)

- **Session Details** (session_details):
  Briefly describe the session you are booking.
  (Type: text, Required: false)

- **Payment** (payment):
  Enter the payment information for the session.
  (Type: text, Required: false)

- **Product** (product):
  If applicable, enter any product information.
  (Type: text, Required: false)

- **Contact** (contact):
  Enter your name and contact information.
  (Type: text, Required: false)

- **Services** (services):
  Select the type of shoot you are looking to book:
  Full-Day Shoot, Half-Day Shoot, Single Shot.
  (Type: select_multiple, Required: false)

- **Photoshoot Date** (photoshoot_date):
  Select the date of the photoshoot.
  (Type: date, Required: false)

- **Photoshoot Time** (photoshoot_time):
  Select the start and end time of the photoshoot.
  (Type: time, Required: false)

- **Photos Taken** (photos_taken):
  Enter the expected number of photos to be taken during the session.
  (Type: number, Required: false)

- **Price** (price):
  Enter the cost of the session.
  (Type: number, Required: false)

- **Photographer Experience** (photographer_experience):
  Enter any relevant experience you have as a photographer.
  (Type: text, Required: false)

## Tips
- Double-check the form for accurate information before submission.
- If you are unsure of any field, consult with the photographer or staff before proceeding.
