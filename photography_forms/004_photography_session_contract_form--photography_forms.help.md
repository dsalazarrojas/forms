# photography_session_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a photography session contract for a client, ensuring both parties have a clear understanding of the session details and terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Session details**:
	* Enter a brief description of the session, including any relevant details about the event or subject.
2. **Photographer details**:
	* Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
3. **Client details**:
	* Enter the client's name and any other relevant contact information.
4. **Session date and time**:
	* Enter the date and time of the session.
5. **Session location**:
	* Enter the location of the session.
6. **Contact information**:
	* Enter the client's email address or other relevant contact information.
7. **Session type**:
	* Choose between 'Outdoor' or 'Indoor' session type.
8. **Session length**:
	* Choose multiple options for session length, e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours.
9. **Session length options**:
	* Enter any additional details about the session length, if necessary.
10. **Session price**:
	* Enter the price of the session.
11. **Payment terms**:
	* Enter any relevant details about payment terms, including due date and method of payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session details**: A brief description of the session, including any relevant details.
	+ Type: text, optional
* **Photographer details**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
	+ Type: text, optional
* **Client details**: Enter the client's name and any other relevant contact information.
	+ Type: text, optional
* **Session date**: The date of the session.
	+ Type: date, optional
* **Session time**: The time of the session.
	+ Type: time, optional
* **Location**: The location of the session.
	+ Type: text, optional
* **Contact**: The client's email address or other relevant contact information.
	+ Type: email, optional
* **Session type**: Choose between 'Outdoor' or 'Indoor' session type.
	+ Type: select_one, required
* **Session length**: Choose multiple options for session length, e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours.
	+ Type: select_multiple, required
* **Session length options**: Enter any additional details about the session length, if necessary.
	+ Type: text, optional
* **Session price**: The price of the session.
	+ Type: number, required
* **Payment terms**: Enter any relevant details about payment terms, including due date and method of payment.
	+ Type: text, optional

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the photographer or the client directly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
