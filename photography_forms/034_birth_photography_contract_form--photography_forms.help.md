# Birth Photography Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a standard contract for birth photography services, outlining the terms and conditions for both the photographer and the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information as the photographer (fields 3-7).
2. Fill in your contact information as the client (fields 9-12).
3. Specify the service details, including the expected birth date, location, and duration (fields 14-16).
4. Choose the deliverables and payment options (fields 20-23 and 25-30).
5. Agree to the terms and conditions, including photo rights, usage, and confidentiality (fields 34-36 and 38-39).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Birth Photography Contract --**:
	+ This is a section header for the introduction to the contract.
* **Please review and complete this contract**:
	+ This is a note section that serves as an introduction to the contract.
* **-- Photographer Information --**:
	+ This section is for the photographer to fill in their contact information.
* **Photographer Full Name** (field 4):
	+ This is the full name of the photographer.
* **Photographer Business Name** (field 5):
	+ This is the name of the photographer's business or studio.
* **Photographer Email** (field 6):
	+ This is the email address of the photographer.
* **Photographer Phone** (field 7):
	+ This is the phone number of the photographer.
* **-- Client Information --**:
	+ This section is for the client to fill in their contact information.
* **Client Full Name** (field 9):
	+ This is the full name of the client (parent or guardian).
* **Client Email** (field 10):
	+ This is the email address of the client.
* **Client Phone** (field 11):
	+ This is the phone number of the client.
* **Client Address** (field 12):
	+ This is the street address of the client.
* **-- Service Details --**:
	+ This section is for the client to specify the service details.
* **Expected Birth/Service Date** (field 14):
	+ This is the estimated due date for the photography service.
* **Birth Location** (field 15):
	+ This is where the birth will take place (hospital, birth center, or home).
* **Photographer Service Duration** (field 16):
	+ This is the time frame for the photographer's service.
* **Included Pre-Birth Sessions** (field 17):
	+ This is the number of prenatal shoots included in the contract.
* **Included Post-Birth Sessions** (field 18):
	+ This is the number of postpartum shoots included in the contract.
* **-- Deliverables --**:
	+ This section is for the client to choose the deliverables.
* **Estimated Number of Final Photos** (field 20):
	+ This is the approximate number of photos to be delivered.
* **Timeline for Edited Photos Delivery** (field 21):
	+ This is when the edited photos will be ready for delivery.
* **Photo Delivery Format** (field 22):
	+ This is how the photos will be delivered (digital files, USB drive, album, etc.).
* **Level of Retouching Included** (field 23):
	+ This is the level of editing included in the contract.
* **-- Fees and Payment --**:
	+ This section is for the client to review and agree to the payment terms.
* **Session Fee** (field 25):
	+ This is the cost for the photography service.
* **Additional Charges** (field 26):
	+ This is for any additional charges (travel, prints, etc.).
* **Total Contract Fee** (field 27):
	+ This is the sum of all fees.
* **Deposit Required** (field 28):
	+ This is the amount due to secure the date.
* **Deposit Due Date** (field 29):
	+ This is when the deposit is due.
* **Balance Due Date** (field 30):
	+ This is when the final payment is due.
* **Accepted Payment Methods** (field 31):
	+ This is how payment can be made (check, credit card, etc.).
* **Cancellation Policy** (field 32):
	+ This is the policy for cancellation of the contract.
* **-- Photo Rights and Usage --**:
	+ This section is for the client to agree to the photo rights and usage.
* **Client Rights to Photos** (field 34):
	+ This is what the client can do with the photos.
* **Photographer Rights to Use Photos** (field 35):
	+ This is what the photographer can do with the photos.
* **Privacy and Confidentiality Agreement** (field 36):
	+ This is the agreement to keep the photos confidential.
* **Backup Photographer Provided** (field 38):
	+ This is whether a backup photographer will be provided in case of emergency.
* **I Agree to the Terms of this Contract** (field 39):
	+ This is the final agreement to the terms of the contract.
* **Contract Date** (field 40):
	+ This is the date the contract is signed.
