# Photography Portfolio Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect comprehensive information from photographers to showcase their work and contact information. It is intended for potential clients to find and contact photographers for projects.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for optional fields to give potential clients a comprehensive view of your work.
3. Double-check your contact information to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer**: Enter your name as it appears on your business cards or website.
* **Project Description**: Provide a short description of the project you're showcasing. Keep it concise and clear.
* **Project Title**: Enter the title of the project you're submitting. This can be a single word or a short phrase.
* **Project Date**: Enter the date(s) you completed the project. This can be a range of dates or a single date.
* **Project Style**: Select the style(s) of project you're submitting (e.g., Landscape, Portrait, Wedding).
* **Portfolio Website**: Enter your portfolio website URL.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your professional email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where potential clients can reach you.
* **Portfolio Experience**: Enter your experience level in photography (e.g., Student, Hobbyist, Professional).
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select how you prefer to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, Social Media).
* **Portfolio Style**: Select the style(s) of photography you specialize in (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Portfolio Location**: Enter the location(s) where you've worked or specialize in (e.g., City, State, Country).
* **Contact Social Media**: Enter your social media handles (e.g., Instagram, Twitter, Facebook).
* **Photographer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business cards or website.
* **Yes/No** fields are for indicating whether or not you specialize in a particular style or have a preferred contact method.

## Tips

* Double-check your contact information for accuracy to avoid missing potential clients.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to give potential clients a comprehensive view of your work.
* Keep your project descriptions concise and clear.
* Make sure to select all applicable styles and locations for your portfolio.
* Ensure your social media handles are up-to-date and professional.
* This form will be used to find and contact you for potential projects.
