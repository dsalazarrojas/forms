# Photoshoot Session Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the booking process for photoshoot sessions. It is intended to be completed by individuals interested in booking a photoshoot session with a professional photographer. The form helps to gather necessary information such as name, email, preferred date and time, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field. This is a required field and will be used to identify you.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used to communicate with you regarding your photoshoot session.
3. Select a date for the photoshoot from the calendar in the "Date" field. This is a required field as it will schedule your photoshoot session.
4. Choose a time for the photoshoot from the options provided in the "Time" field. This will ensure that we can schedule your session accordingly.
5. Enter the location where the photoshoot will take place in the "Location" field (optional). This can be the location of your choice or our studio.
6. Read and agree to the "Model Consent" terms by typing "I Agree" in the text field. This is a required field as it ensures we have your consent to capture images of you.
7. Upload any required documents (such as contracts) in the "Contract" field. This is a required field as it may be necessary for us to have a valid contract on file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your active and valid email address. This will be used for communication purposes only.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Select a date for the photoshoot session from the calendar provided. Please ensure that your selection is a valid date.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Choose a time for the photoshoot session from the available options. Please select a time that works best for you.
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the photoshoot. This can be your home, office, or our studio.
* **Model Consent** (`Model Consent`, `text`, required): Read and agree to our model consent terms by typing "I Agree" in the text field.
* **Contract** (`Contract`, `text`, required): Upload any required contracts or documents for the photoshoot session. This is for your protection and to ensure that we have a valid contract on file.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter accurate information in all fields, especially your email address.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please let us know in advance to ensure a smooth photoshoot experience.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
