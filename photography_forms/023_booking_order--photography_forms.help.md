# Booking Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a photography session for various types of events such as weddings, portraits, and product photography. Please fill out the form with your details to secure your date and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Select the correct options for each field.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_full_name`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for photography details and inquiries.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, required): Enter your best contact number for communication.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, required): Select the type of photography you need (e.g., wedding, portrait, etc.).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, required): Enter the date of the event (e.g., 2024-02-15).
* **Event Start Time** (`event_time`, required): Enter the start time of the event (e.g., 10:00 AM).
* **Photography Duration Needed** (`event_duration`, required): Select the length of the photography session (e.g., 1-2 hours, full day).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, required): Enter the address of the event.
* **Expected Number of People** (`number_of_guests`, required): Enter an approximate number of guests attending the event.
* **Preferred Photography Style** (`photography_style`, required): Select the style of photography you prefer (e.g., traditional, candid, etc.).
* **Package Type** (`package_type`, required): Select the package that suits your needs.
* **Required Deliverables** (`deliverables`, required): Select the items you want to receive from the photography session.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, optional): Enter any specific shots or requirements you have.
* **Photography Budget** (`budget`, optional): Select your budget range for the photography session.
* **Deposit Payment** (`deposit_payment`, required): Confirm if you're ready to secure your date with a deposit payment.
