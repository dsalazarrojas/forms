# Wedding Photography Schedule - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a guide for the photographer to collect essential details from couples before their wedding day. By providing accurate and complete information, couples can help the photographer create a tailored schedule for capturing their special moments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Wedding Details** (`wedding_photography_details`): Enter a brief description of the wedding, including the ceremony and reception locations.
2. **Photographer's Name** (`photographer_name`): Enter your photographer's name or the name of the person handling the photography services.
3. **Couple's Details** (`couple_details`): Enter your names and contact information.
4. **Preferred Packages** (`preferred_packages`): Select the packages you are interested in. Please note that the photographer will guide you on the most suitable packages for your needs.
5. **Special Shot Requests** (`special_shot_requests`): Select any special shots or activities you'd like to include during the photo shoot.
6. **Shot Order** (`shot_order`): Choose the timing and sequence of your photo shoot (Pre-wedding, Wedding Day, Wedding Night, or Post-wedding).
7. **Photo Locations** (`photo_locations`): Select the locations where the photographer will take your pictures.
8. **Photo Locations List** (`photo_locations_list`): Provide a list of specific locations you'd like the photographer to visit.
9. **Photo Styles** (`photo_styles`): List your preferred photo styles for each location.
10. **Travel Details** (`travel_details`): Provide any travel information required for the photo shoot (e.g., transportation, parking, or accommodations).
11. **Additional Travel Info** (`additional_travel_info`): Provide any extra details regarding travel or logistics.
12. **Accommodations** (`accommodations`): If you're staying out of town, please provide accommodation details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Details**: A short description of the wedding, including ceremony and reception locations.
* **Photographer's Name**: The name of the person handling the photography services.
* **Couple's Details**: Your names and contact information for communication.
* **Preferred Packages**: Select the most suitable packages for your needs. Please note that the photographer will provide guidance.
* **Special Shot Requests**: Choose any special shots or activities you'd like to include.
* **Shot Order**: The timing and sequence of your photo shoot (Pre-wedding, Wedding Day, Wedding Night, or Post-wedding).
* **Photo Locations**: Select locations for the photo shoot.
* **Photo Locations List**: A specific list of locations you'd like to visit.
* **Photo Styles**: List your preferred photo styles for each location.
* **Travel Details**: Provide any travel information required.
* **Additional Travel Info**: Extra details about travel or logistics.
* **Accommodations**: If staying out of town, provide accommodation details.
