# Boudoir Photography Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boudoir Photography Application Form is designed to collect information from clients for a personalized boudoir photography session. It helps the photographer understand the client's preferences, requirements, and expectations for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
- Fill out the form in one go, or break it down into sections as per the headings.
- Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`client_email_addr`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where the photographer can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone_no`, text, required): Enter your phone number where the photographer can reach you.
* **Preferred Session Date** (`session_dt`, date, required): Choose a date for your boudoir photography session.
* **What is the occasion for your boudoir session?** (`session_occasion`, text, required): Briefly describe the occasion or reason for the session.
* **How comfortable do you feel in front of the camera?** (`camera_comfort`, select_one, required): Choose how you feel in front of the camera to help the photographer plan the session.
* **What style are you looking for?** (`style_pref`, select_multiple, required): Select the styles that you are interested in, such as Classic/Elegant, Dark/Moody, Bright/Airy, Glamorous, Natural/Minimalist.
* **Would you like to add professional hair and makeup services?** (`add_hmua`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want to include professional hair and makeup services.
* **Do you permit the photographer to use images for social media / portfolio?** (`privacy_choice_select`, select_one, required): Decide if you want to give permission for the photographer to use the images on social media or keep them private.
* **Any questions or specific areas you want to highlight?** (`client_questions`, text, optional): Add any questions or areas you'd like to be sure to capture during the session.
* **I understand a non-refundable deposit is required to book** (`deposit_confirm`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the deposit policy.
