## # Photography Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information for a photography session, including contact details, session specifics, and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the contact information section** (`Contact Information`, `id: 1`): Provide your full name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you for session updates and details.
2. **Select the number of people in your party** (`Number of People`, `id: 2`): Indicate how many individuals will be participating in the photography session.
3. **Choose a session date and time** (`Session Date` and `Session Time`, `id: 3` and `id: 4`): Select a date and time for your photography session from the available options.
4. **Specify the session location** (`Location`, `id: 5`): Choose the location where the photography session will take place.
5. **Indicate if you require a photography session** (`Photography Type`, `id: 6`): Select 'Yes' if you need a photography session, and 'No' otherwise.
6. **Review and sign the contact agreement** (`Contact Agreement`, `id: 7`): This is a standard agreement to protect both you and the photographer. Please take a moment to review and sign it.
7. **Choose a payment option** (`Deposit Payment`, `id: 8` and `Payment`, `id: 9`): Select how you will make your deposit payment (if applicable) and how you will pay for the session (e.g., Bank Transfer, Credit Card, Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (id: 1)**: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
* **Number of People (id: 2)**: Choose the number of individuals participating in the photography session (e.g., 1, 2, 3+).
* **Session Date (id: 3)**: Select a date for your photography session. Ensure the date is a valid date and within the specified session dates.
* **Session Time (id: 4)**: Choose the time of your photography session. Be aware that some time slots may have limited availability.
* **Location (id: 5)**: Indicate the location of your photography session, such as a studio, home, or outdoor setting.
* **Photography Type (id: 6)**: If you require a photography session, select 'Yes'. This will be used to tailor the services to your needs.
* **Contact Agreement (id: 7)**: Please take the time to review and sign this agreement to protect both you and the photographer.
* **Deposit Payment (id: 8)**: If applicable, indicate if you will make a deposit payment for the session.
* **Payment (id: 9)**: Choose how you will make payment for the session (e.g., Bank Transfer, Credit Card, Other).

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out this form accurately and completely.
* Double-check the session date, time, and location for any conflicts or errors.
* Review the contact agreement carefully before signing.
* Make sure to select the correct payment option.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the photographer or event organizer.
