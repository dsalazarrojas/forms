# Trade For Print Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade For Print Agreement form is designed to help photographers and clients collaborate on trade photography agreements. It's a basic form that gathers necessary details for a smooth and professional trade photography experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Photographer Details** section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. In the **Model Details** section, enter your model's name, contact information, and any other relevant information.
3. Review the **Agreement Terms** section to ensure you understand the expectations and responsibilities for both parties.
4. Read over the **Payment Terms** section to understand the payment conditions and any other financial details.
5. Once you've reviewed and filled out all sections, double-check the information for accuracy and completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer Details** (Text, Required/Optional: false):
	+ This field is intended to gather basic contact information and details about the photographer.
	+ Please provide your name, phone number, and any other relevant details that will help the model contact you.
* **Model Details** (Text, Required/Optional: false):
	+ Enter the model's name, phone number, and any other essential contact details.
* **Agreement Terms** (Text, Required/Optional: false):
	+ Review the terms of the trade agreement carefully, including any expectations, responsibilities, and conditions.
	+ Make sure you understand what's expected of both parties before submitting the form.
* **Payment Terms** (Text, Required/Optional: false):
	+ Carefully review the payment terms and conditions, including any financial details and expectations.
	+ Ensure you understand the payment terms before submitting the form.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submission.
- Review the agreement terms carefully to avoid any confusion.
- Keep the payment terms clear and transparent for a smooth trade experience.
