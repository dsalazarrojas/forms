# Photography Booking Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you book a photography session with a professional photographer. It collects necessary information from the client, such as their contact and booking details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Client Information** section with the client's name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide details about the booking, including the date, time, and location of the photo shoot.
3. Enter your contact information so that we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**
	+ `<label>Client Information</label> (required)`
	+ This section is where you provide personal details about the client, such as their name, email address, and phone number.
* **Booking Details**
	+ `<label>Booking Details</label> (required)`
	+ In this section, enter the date and time of the photo shoot, and the location where it will take place.
* **Contact Information**
	+ `<label>Contact Information</label> (required)`
	+ Enter your own contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number, so that we can get in touch with you regarding the booking.
