# Photo Collage Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Collage Upload Form is designed to collect information about a photo collage, including the collage's entry and any files related to it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the photo collage in the "Photo Collage Entry" field.
2. Upload files related to the photo collage from the "Files" field.
3. You can add multiple files by selecting multiple files from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Photo Collage Entry
* **Photo Collage Entry** (`photo_collage_entry`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the photo collage.

### File Input
* **File Input** (`file_input`, `text`, optional): This field is not visible to the user, so you can ignore it.

### Files
* **Files** (`file`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more files related to the photo collage from the available options. You can select up to 25 files.
