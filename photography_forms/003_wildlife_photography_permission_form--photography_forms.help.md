# Wildlife Photography Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request permission from wildlife organizations or landowners for taking photos of their property or the animals that live there. It helps to document the photo shoot, understand what is acceptable and not, and ensure that photographers and their organizations comply with any regulations or rules set forth by the owners.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately to the best of your ability.
- Provide clear and concise responses to each question.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to this guide for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer Information**: Please enter your name, contact information, and any other details requested below.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the contact person's name, email, and phone number for the landowner or organization.
* **Photo Location**: Provide the exact location where the photos were taken.
* **Date**: Enter the date the photo was taken.
* **Time**: Enter the time the photo was taken.
* **Photo Subjects**: Choose all the subjects that were photographed (Wildlife, Landscapes, Flora, Fauna, or Other).
* **Description of Photo Location**: Briefly describe the location where the photos were taken.
* **Brief Description of Photos**: Describe what was photographed (e.g., a bird in flight, a mountain landscape, etc.).
* **Type of Permission**: Select whether you are requesting General or Specific Event permission.
* **Expiration Date and Time**: If applicable, enter the date and time when the permission will expire.
* **Notes**: If you have any additional information or requests, enter them here.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the contact person's name for the landowner or organization.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
