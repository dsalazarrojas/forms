# Photo Release Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Release Permission Form is a document used to obtain consent from subjects to use and share their photos. It is designed for events, photoshoots, or other photography endeavors where subjects are photographed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **user_name** field. This field is where you will enter your name as the subject being photographed.
2. Enter your email address in the **user_email** field. This field is where you will enter your email address where you can be reached.
3. Enter the name of the event or photoshoot in the **event_name** field. This field is where you will enter the name of the event or photoshoot you were a part of.
4. Enter the date of the event or photoshoot in the **event_date** field. This field is where you will enter the date the event or photoshoot took place.
5. Enter the name of the photographer who took your photo in the **photographer_name** field. This field is where you will enter the name of the person who took your photo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_name** (Text): Enter your name as the subject being photographed.
* **user_email** (Email): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **event_name** (Text): Enter the name of the event or photoshoot you were a part of.
* **event_date** (Date): Enter the date the event or photoshoot took place.
* **photographer_name** (Text): Enter the name of the person who took your photo.

## Tips

* Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure that your consent is clear.
* If you are unable to fill out this form, please contact the photographer for further information.
* If you have any questions or concerns about using this form, please contact GIC at form@gic.mx.
