# Wedding DVD Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding DVD Application Form is a tool for couples to submit their wedding DVD application. This form helps us understand the details of their wedding, including the order type, delivery method, and billing address. The form is designed to be completed by couples who wish to order a custom DVD of their special day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Make sure to select the correct order type (wedding, rehearsal, ceremony, reception, or post-wedding).
3. Choose the preferred delivery method (email, online order, or in-person).
4. Enter the billing address, including country, city, state, and street.
5. Enter the shipping address, including country, city, state, and street.
6. Add any additional notes about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Type (order_type)**: Select the type of event you are ordering the DVD for (wedding, rehearsal, ceremony, reception, or post-wedding).
* **Delivery Method (delivery_method)**: Choose how you would like to receive your DVD (email, online order, or in-person).
* **Billing Address (billing_address)**: Enter your billing address.
* **Billing Address Street (billing_address_street)**: Enter the street address associated with your billing address.
* **Billing Address City (billing_address_city)**: Enter the city associated with your billing address.
* **Billing Address State (billing_address_state)**: Enter the state associated with your billing address.
* **Billing Address Country (billing_address_country)**: Select the country associated with your billing address.
* **Billing Address Note (billing_address_note)**: Add any additional notes about your billing address.
* **Shipping Address (shipping_address)**: Enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping Address Street (shipping_address_street)**: Enter the street address associated with your shipping address.
* **Shipping Address City (shipping_address_city)**: Enter the city associated with your shipping address.
* **Shipping Address State (shipping_address_state)**: Enter the state associated with your shipping address.
* **Shipping Address Country (shipping_address_country)**: Select the country associated with your shipping address.
* **Shipping Address Note (shipping_address_note)**: Add any additional notes about your shipping address.
* **Order Notes (order_notes)**: Add any additional notes about your order.
