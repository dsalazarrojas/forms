# engagement_shoot_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The engagement_shoot_reservation_form is a tool used by photographers to gather information from couples about their engagement shoots.

## How To Complete This Form
To reserve a photography engagement shoot using this form, follow these steps:

1. **Couple Names**: Enter the names of the couple who will be taking the photo shoot.
2. **Date of Event**: Enter the date when the event will take place.
3. **Time of Event**: Enter the time when the event will happen.
4. **Location**: Enter the location where the event will be held.
5. **Photographer**: Enter the name of the person taking your photographs.
6. **Email**: Enter your email address so that the photographer can contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Couple Names** (`couple_names`, `text`, not required): Enter the names of the couple participating in the photo shoot.
- **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, `date`, not required): Select a date when the event will take place.
- **Time of Event** (`time_of_event`, `time`, not required): Choose a time for the event if it has a specific time.
- **Location** (`location`, `text`, not required): Enter the venue or place where the event will be held.
- **Photographer** (`photographer`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the person taking the photographs.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address for the photographer to contact you.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your entered information for accuracy.
- If you're unsure about a particular field, contact the photographer or consult the help documentation for guidance.
- Fill in as much information as you can to ensure the photographer has a clear understanding of your event.
