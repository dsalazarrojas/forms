# we_quality_u_can_trust_asking_for_donation_for_mask_food_face_shields_for_our_health_care - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for individuals or organizations seeking to ask for donations for mask, food, or face shields for our health care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your request for donations in the "We @ Quality U Can Trust Asking For Donation For Mask,Food,Face Shields For Our Health Care" field.
2. Select the category of your request from the "CATEGORY" dropdown menu.
3. Provide a brief description of your request in the "DESCRIPTION" field.
4. If you have assigned a tool or resource to your request, enter its ID in the "ASSIGNED TOOL" field.
5. If you would like to specify a file output for your request, enter the file path or name in the "OUTPUT FILE" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **We @ Quality U Can Trust Asking For Donation For Mask,Food,Face Shields For Our Health Care** (`form_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of your request for donations in this field.
* **CATEGORY** (`form_category`, `text`, required/optional): Select the category of your request from this field.
* **DESCRIPTION** (`form_description`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your request.
* **ASSIGNED TOOL** (`form_assigned_tool`, `text`, required/optional): If you have assigned a tool or resource to your request, enter its ID in this field.
* **OUTPUT FILE** (`form_output_file`, `text`, required/optional): If you would like to specify a file output for your request, enter the file path or name in this field.
