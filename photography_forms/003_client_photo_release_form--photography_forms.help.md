# Client Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Photo Release Form is a document that grants permission to the photographer to use the client's photos in various ways. This form is used to ensure that both the photographer and the client are clear about how the photos will be used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Full Name of Client" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the date of the photography session from the calendar in the "Date of Photography Session" field.
5. Choose the location of the session in the "Location of Photography Session" field.
6. Select how you want your photos to be used in the "Scope of Photo Release" field.
7. Choose the duration of usage rights in the "Duration of Usage Rights" field.
8. Decide if you require attribution for the photos in the "Do you require credit/attribution for photos used?" field.
9. Indicate if minors are present in the photos and if parental consent was obtained in the "Are there minors in the photos?" and "If minors are included, is parental consent obtained?" fields.
10. Agree to the terms of the release in the "I agree that the photographer may use these photos as described above" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name of Client** (Text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
- **Email Address** (Email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone Number** (Text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Date of Photography Session** (Date, required): Select the date of the photography session from the calendar.
- **Location of Photography Session** (Text, optional): Enter the location where the photography session took place.
- **Scope of Photo Release** (Select One, required): Choose how you want your photos to be used (Personal use only, Social media and website, Print advertisements, Digital marketing, or All promotional purposes).
- **Duration of Usage Rights** (Select One, required): Choose the duration of usage rights (One year, Two years, Five years, Indefinite, or Specific event only).
- **Do you require credit/attribution for photos used?** (Select One, required): Decide if you want your name credited with each photo use.
- **Are there minors in the photos?** (Select One, required): Indicate if minors are present in the photos.
- **If minors are included, is parental consent obtained?** (Select One, optional): If minors are included, indicate if parental consent was obtained.
- **Do you agree that the photographer may use these photos as described above** (Select One, required): Check the box to agree to the terms of the release.
- **Signature Date** (Date, required): Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
