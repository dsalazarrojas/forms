# Wedding Photography Lease Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to formalize the agreement between a photographer and their client for wedding photography services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client information section, including their name, address, and contact details.
2. Enter the photographer's information, including their name, address, and contact details.
3. Read and agree to the contract terms.
4. Sign and date the agreement.
5. Review and agree to the terms of the agreement.
6. Enter your email and phone number for future contact.
7. Enter your address for future correspondence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, optional): This section is for the client to provide their personal details, including name, address, and contact information.
* **Photographer Information** (`photographer_info`, text, optional): This section is for the photographer to provide their personal details, including name, address, and contact information.
* **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, text, optional): This section outlines the terms of the contract, including the scope of work, payment terms, and cancellation policies.
* **Signature Terms** (`signature_terms`, text, optional): This section explains the signature requirements and any specific conditions for signing the agreement.
* **Signature 1** (`signature_1`, text, optional): This is the first signature block for the client to sign the agreement.
* **Signature 2** (`signature_2`, text, optional): This is the second signature block, usually for the photographer or other authorized person.
* **Agreement Review** (`agreement_review`, text, optional): This section is for the client to review and agree to the terms of the agreement.
* **Agreement Acceptance** (`agreement_acceptance`, text, optional): This section is for the client to indicate their acceptance of the agreement terms.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): This field is for the date the agreement is signed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): This field is for the time the agreement is signed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for the client's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for the client's phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): This field is for the client's address.

## Tips

* Please make sure to read and review the terms of the agreement carefully before signing.
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Save a copy of the signed agreement for future reference.
