# Lookbook Photo Shoot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Lookbook Photo Shoot Application Form" is designed for photographers to apply for a photo shoot opportunity. It helps the photographer to showcase their skills, experience, and services to potential clients. By completing this form, photographers can provide all the necessary information required by the client or agency to assess their suitability for the shoot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your services.
2. Provide a detailed description of your project, including the title, description, and any other relevant details.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly, and make sure to review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Photographer Name** (text)
* This is your full name as a photographer.
* Enter your name in the correct format (e.g., John Doe).

### 2. **Brand Name** (text)
* If you are applying as a freelancer or independent photographer, enter your personal brand name.
* If you are working with a company, enter the company name.

### 3. **Project Title** (text)
* This is the title of the project you are applying for.
* Be concise and descriptive.

### 4. **Project Description** (text)
* Provide a detailed description of the project, including the scope and objectives.
* Make sure to highlight your unique selling points and services offered.

### 5. **Model Age** (number)
* This is the age of the model you are applying for.
* Enter a whole number (e.g., 25).

### 6. **Model Height** (number)
* This is the height of the model you are applying for.
* Enter a whole number (e.g., 175cm).

### 7. **Model Contact Email** (email)
* This is the email address of the model you are applying for.
* Make sure to use a valid email format (e.g., john.doe@example.com).

### 8. **Model Contact Phone** (text)
* This is the phone number of the model you are applying for.
* Enter a valid phone number format (e.g., +123-456-7890).

### 9. **Model Experience** (text)
* This is your level of experience in the photography industry.
* Be honest and provide details about your expertise and qualifications.

### 10. **Portfolio URL** (text)
* This is the URL of your portfolio website or social media profile.
* Make sure to provide a live link.

### 11. **Availability** (text)
* This is your availability for the shoot (e.g., days, dates, time slots).
* Be specific and provide details about your schedule.

### 12. **Shoot Location** (text)
* This is the location where the photo shoot will take place.
* Be specific and provide the exact address or a nearby landmark.

### 13. **Shoot Date** (date)
* This is the date of the photo shoot.
* Enter a valid date format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).

### 14. **Shoot Time** (time)
* This is the time of the photo shoot.
* Enter a valid time format (e.g., HH:MM).

### 15. **Shoot Duration** (text)
* This is the duration of the photo shoot.
* Be specific and provide the exact length of the shoot.

### 16. **Compensation** (text)
* This is the compensation you are expecting for the shoot.
* Be realistic and provide a fair estimate.

### 17. **Model Agreement** (note)
* This is a special note for the model agreement.
* You can provide any additional terms or conditions here.

### 18. **Model Reposession** (text)
* This is a note for the model's repossession of equipment or materials.
* Be specific and provide details about the process.

### 19. **Brand Agreement** (text)
* This is a note for the brand's agreement on the shoot.
* Be specific and provide details about the process.

By following these guidelines, you should be able to complete the "Lookbook Photo Shoot Application Form" accurately and effectively. Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
