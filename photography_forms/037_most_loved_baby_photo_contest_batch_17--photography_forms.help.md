# most_loved_baby_photo_contest_batch_17 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is part of a contest to collect information about baby photo habits and preferences from batch 17. It's designed to gather insights on how participants store, share, and interact with their baby photos.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the basic information about your baby.
2. Share your photo habits, including how you store and share your baby's photos.
3. Express your preferences regarding baby photos, such as your favorite photo.
4. Describe your photo frequency and journaling habits.
5. Plan any upcoming photo shoots for your baby.
6. If you have a favorite photo, share it.
7. Add any additional information about your baby's photos.
8. Finally, provide any tips you'd like to share about baby photos.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Baby Information** (`basic_info`, text, optional): Enter your baby's name and age.
- **Photo Information** (`photo_info`, text, optional): Share a brief description of your photo habits, such as how often you take photos or how you share them with family and friends.
- **Storage and Sharing** (`storage_and_sharing`, text, optional): Explain how you store and share your baby's photos, including any online platforms or physical albums you use.
- **Photo Preferences** (`photo_preferences`, text, optional): Share your favorite baby photo and why it's special to you.
- **Photo Frequency and Journaling** (`photo_frequency_and_journaling`, text, optional): Describe your photo frequency and journaling habits, including how often you take photos and how you keep track of milestones.
- **Photo Shoots** (`photo_shoots`, text, optional): Plan any upcoming photo shoots for your baby, including the dates and locations.
- **Favorite Photo** (`favorite_photo`, text, optional): Share a description of your favorite baby photo.
- **Photo Tips** (`photo_tips`, text, optional): Add any helpful tips for others who are taking photos of their own babies.
- **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your baby's photos, such as favorite photographers or photography locations.
