# Christmas Photography Session Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to provide information and preferences for their Christmas photography session. It gathers details about the client's session preferences, including date, time, session type, location, and styling preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your client's information in the Client Information section, including First Name, Last Name, and Email Address.
2. Provide your preferred session date and time in the Session Details section.
3. Choose the type of session you're interested in (e.g., Family portrait, Individual portrait, etc.).
4. Specify the total number of people to be photographed and the ages of children (if applicable).
5. Choose the session location (e.g., Studio, Outdoor location, Home, etc.).
6. If you're planning an outdoor session, specify an alternative location in case of bad weather.
7. Select your preferred styling options, including outfit theme, color palette, and whether props are provided.
8. Add any special requests or additional notes in the Special Requests section.
9. Finally, confirm the image usage consent by selecting the desired option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required): Enter your preferred session date.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, required): Enter your preferred session time.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose the session duration (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour, etc.).
* **Type of Session** (`session_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of session you're interested in (e.g., Family portrait, Individual portrait, etc.).
* **Total Number of People** (`number_of_people`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of people to be photographed.
* **Ages of Children** (`children_ages`, `text`, optional): If applicable, specify the ages of the children.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, `select_one`, required): Choose the session location (e.g., Studio, Outdoor location, etc.).
* **Location Details** (`location_details`, `text`, optional): If you're choosing an outdoor location, provide additional details about the location.
* **Backup Location** (`backup_location`, `text`, optional): If you're planning an outdoor session, specify an alternative location in case of bad weather.
* **Outfit Theme or Dress Code** (`outfit_theme`, `text`, optional): Specify your preferred outfit theme or dress code.
* **Preferred Color Palette** (`color_palette`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred color palette (e.g., Traditional red and green, Warm tones, etc.).
* **Props or Decorations Provided** (`props_available`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether props or decorations will be provided by the photographer or by you.
* **Specific Shots** (`specific_shots`, `text`, optional): Add any specific shots you'd like to have taken during the session.
* **Additional Notes or Requests** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or requests you have.
* **Image Usage Consent** (`image_usage`, `select_one`, required): Confirm the image usage consent by selecting the desired option.
