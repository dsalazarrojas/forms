# Photography Exhibition Theme Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Exhibition Theme Voting Form is a tool for users to submit their photography concept and idea to be considered for an exhibition theme. This form helps the exhibition organizers to gather relevant and diverse themes from users, which will be used to curate and finalize the exhibition theme.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields with your photography concept title, description, and image URL.
2. Select one or more concepts that you feel best represent your idea.
3. If applicable, provide your email and phone number for future contact.
4. Add any additional information that might help us understand your concept.
5. Choose a date and time for the exhibition (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photography Concept Title** (`concept_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title of your photography concept that captures the main idea.
* **Describe the concept** (`concept_description`, text, required): Provide a short description of your photography concept, explaining what it's about and what it represents.
* **Concept Image URL** (`concept_image_url`, text, required): Upload an image that represents your photography concept. Please paste the URL of your image.
* **Your Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Any other information** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional information that you feel is relevant to your concept.
* **Select a concept** (`selected_concept`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more concepts that best represent your idea from the options provided.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for the exhibition, if you have a specific date in mind.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose a time for the exhibition, if you have a specific time in mind.
* **Theme** (`theme`, text, optional): Enter a theme that best describes your photography concept.
