# Portrait Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details from the client for a portrait photography session. It will ask for the client's contact information, details about the session, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's details in the "Client Details" section, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. In the "Contract Details" section, provide a brief description of the photography session, including the date, time, location, and any specific requirements or requests.
3. In the "Payment Terms" section, specify the payment method and amount due for the photography session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (required):
	+ **Client Name** (text): The client's full name.
	+ **Contact Information** (text): The client's contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Contract Details** (required):
	+ **Session Description** (text): A brief description of the photography session, including date, time, and location.
* **Payment Terms** (required):
	+ **Payment Method** (text): The method of payment for the photography session (e.g., cash, credit card, etc.).
	+ **Amount Due** (text): The total amount due for the photography session.
