# Wedding Photography Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us capture essential information about your wedding photography booking. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure we can provide the best experience for you on your special day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including full name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the date and start time of your event, along with the venue where it will take place.
3. Choose the services you are interested in booking (e.g., Wedding, Engagement, Graduation).
4. Add any special requests or notes you'd like to share with us.
5. Choose the photographer tool assigned to your booking (e.g., chatjimmy).
6. Submit the form to confirm your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (required):** This is the first step in getting in touch with us. Enter your full name, email, and phone number so we can contact you about your booking.
* **Full Name (required):** Please enter your full name as it will be displayed in our records and on your photos.
* **Email (required):** This is where we'll be sending you important updates and information about your booking.
* **Phone Number (required):** Our team will be using this to contact you about your booking and any changes.
* **Date of Event (required):** Select the date of your wedding, engagement, or graduation.
* **Start Time (required):** Choose the start time of your event.
* **Venue (optional):** If applicable, please provide the venue where the event will take place.
* **Photographer Notes (optional):** Share any additional information or requests you have for your photographer.
* **Services (optional):** Select the services you'd like to book (e.g., Wedding, Engagement, Graduation).
* **Special Requests (optional):** Add any special requests or notes you'd like to share with us.
* **Assigned Tool (required):** Choose the photographer tool assigned to your booking (e.g., chatjimmy).
* **Form Submission (optional):** This is an optional field for any additional comments or questions you may have.

Note: The required fields are marked as "required" in the form and should be filled out carefully to ensure we can provide the best experience for you.
