# Pet Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and obtain consent from pet owners to use and release their pet's photos. It's a crucial document that ensures the photographer and the pet owner are on the same page regarding the use of the pet's photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with the required information about your pet and your preferences.
2. Select the desired options for the type of signature (digital, wet ink, or stamp) and photo release (yes or no).
3. Enter the date and time of the photo shoot.
4. Provide your contact information (name, email, phone number) for the photographer and yourself.
5. Add any additional information or comments about the photo shoot.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. pet_photo_release_form (page 1)
* **Pet Photo Release Form** (`pet_photo_release_form`, text, optional): This is the main heading of the form. It's not a required field.
### 2. owner_signature (page 2)
* **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, select_one, optional): Select one of the three options: '1', '2', or '3'. This is where the owner will sign to agree to the terms and conditions.
### 3. signature_type (page 3)
* **Signature Type** (`signature_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you'd like to receive your signature: Digital E-Signature, Wet Ink Signature, or Stamp Signature.
### 4. date_signed (page 4)
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Enter the date when you signed the form.
### 5. time_signed (page 5)
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, optional): Enter the time when you signed the form.
### 6. photo_taken (page 6)
* **Photo Taken** (`photo_taken`, date, optional): Enter the date when the photo was taken.
### 7. photo_uploaded (page 7)
* **Photo Uploaded** (`photo_uploaded`, date, optional): Enter the date when the photo was uploaded.
### 8. photo_released (page 8)
* **Photo Released** (`photo_released`, date, optional): Enter the date when the photo was released.
### 9. form_completed (page 9)
* **Form Completed** (`form_completed`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about completing the form.
### 10. signature_image (page 10)
* **Signature Image Number** (`signature_image`, number, optional): If you have a signature image, enter its number here.
### 11. signature_image_file_name (page 11)
* **Signature Image File Name** (`signature_image_file_name`, text, optional): If you have a signature image, enter its file name here.
### 12. owner_name (page 12)
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
### 13. owner_email (page 13)
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
### 14. owner_phone (page 14)
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
### 15. photographer_name (page 15)
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the photographer.
### 16. photographer_email (page 16)
* **Photographer Email** (`photographer_email`, email, optional): Enter the photographer's email address.
### 17. photographer_phone (page 17)
* **Photographer Phone** (`photographer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the photographer's phone number.
### 18. location (page 18)
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the photo was taken.
### 19. photo_credits (page 19)
* **Photo Credits** (`photo_credits`, text, optional): Enter any additional credits or information about the photo.
### 20. terms_and_conditions (page 20)
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any additional terms or conditions related to the photo release.
### 21. photo_rights (page 21)
* **Photo Rights** (`photo_rights`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about photo rights.
### 22. photo_description (page 22)
* **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the photo.
### 23. photo_license (page 23)
* **Photo License** (`photo_license`, text, optional): Enter any information about the photo license.
### 24. photo_retention (page 24)
* **Photo Retention** (`photo_retention`, text, optional): Enter any information about photo retention.
### 25. notes (page 25)
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the photo shoot.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask the photographer for clarification.
* Ensure that the form is accurately filled out and signed.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
