# Fan Photo Experience Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Fan Photo Experience Booking Form is designed to be completed by event organizers to book a photography session with a designated photographer. This form helps collect essential information to provide a tailored experience for the event's photo needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields to identify yourself as the event organizer.
2. Provide a brief description of the event in the **Event Description** field.
3. Choose the location and start and end times of the event in the respective fields.
4. Select the photographer assigned to your event from the dropdown options.
5. Specify the date and time of the booking in the **Session Date** and **Session Time** fields.
6. Choose the duration of the photo session from the options provided (5 minutes, 10 minutes, 20 minutes, 30 minutes, or 40 minutes).
7. If you have any specific requests or additional photos required, provide them in the **Special Requests** field.
8. Optionally, you can provide a comment about your event or photo session.
9. Finally, confirm the booking status of your event by selecting **Open** if the booking is open for selection, **Closed** if it is not open for new booking, and **Cancelled** if the booking is cancelled.
10. Once you have filled out all the necessary fields, review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer**: Enter your name to identify yourself as the event organizer.
* **Name**: Enter your name to associate it with the event booking.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to contact you about the event.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any potential follow-up communication.
* **Date**: Choose the date of your event.
* **Time**: Choose the start and end times of your event.
* **Start Time**: Select the start time of your event.
* **End Time**: Select the end time of your event.
* **Event Description**: Provide a brief description of your event.
* **Location**: Enter the location of your event.
* **Photographer**: Select the photographer assigned to your event.
* **Photo Session**: Select the duration of the photo session (5 minutes, 10 minutes, 20 minutes, 30 minutes, or 40 minutes).
* **Session Date**: Choose the date of the photo session.
* **Session Time**: Select the time of the photo session.
* **Photo Count**: Enter the expected number of photos required.
* **Additional Photos**: Enter the number of additional photos required.
* **Special Requests**: Provide any specific requests or requirements for the photo session.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or notes about the event or photo session.
* **Assigned Photographer**: Select the photographer assigned to your event.
* **Booking Status**: Select the status of your event booking (Open, Closed, or Cancelled).
* **Booking Date**: Choose the date of the booking.
* **Booking Time**: Select the time of the booking.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name for identification.
* **Submitted Time**: Select the time of submission.
* **Submitted Date**: Choose the date of submission.

Note: This guide is based on the provided YAML structure, but it assumes that most fields are optional. Ensure you provide accurate information as necessary and required.
