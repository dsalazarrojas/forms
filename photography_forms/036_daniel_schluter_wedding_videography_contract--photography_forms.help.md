# daniel_schluter_wedding_videography_contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information for a wedding videography contract between a client and a videographer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Client Info** section with the client's name and other relevant details.
2. Provide contact information, such as address, phone number, and email, in the **Contact Info** section.
3. Enter the **Wedding Info** section with the date and time of the wedding.
4. Select the services to be offered by the videographer in the **Services** section.
5. Provide details about the services offered in the **Service Details** section.
6. Enter the **Location** of the wedding.
7. Fill in the **Address** of the wedding location.
8. Provide the name of the **Contact Person** responsible for the wedding.
9. Enter the **Phone** number and **Email** of the contact person.
10. Add any **Notes** about the wedding in the **Notes** section.
11. Sign and date the **Signature** section for both the client and the vendor.
12. Confirm the **Payment Terms** and **Payment Method**.
13. Update the **Contract Status** to reflect the current status of the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info** (1, text, required): This field is intended for the client's name and other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (2, text, required): This field is for the contact person's information, including address, phone number, and email.
* **Wedding Info** (3, text, required): Enter the date and time of the wedding.
* **Services** (4, select_multiple, required): Select the services to be offered by the videographer.
* **Service Details** (5, select_multiple, required): Provide details about the services offered.
* **Date** (6, date, required): Enter the date of the wedding.
* **Start Time** (7, time, required): Enter the start time of the wedding.
* **End Time** (8, time, required): Enter the end time of the wedding.
* **Location** (9, text, required): Enter the location of the wedding.
* **Address** (10, text, required): Enter the address of the wedding location.
* **Contact Person** (11, text, required): Provide the name of the contact person responsible for the wedding.
* **Phone** (12, text, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Email** (13, email, required): Enter the contact person's email.
* **Notes** (14, note, required): Add any notes about the wedding.
* **Signature** (15, text, required): Sign and date the contract.
* **Terms and Conditions** (16, text, required): Confirm the terms and conditions of the contract.
* **Review** (17, text, required): Review the contract.
* **Payment** (18, number, required): Confirm the payment amount.
* **Payment Terms** (19, text, required): Confirm the payment terms.
* **Payment Method** (20, text, required): Confirm the payment method.
* **Client Signature** (21, text, required): Sign and date the contract for the client.
* **Vendor Signature** (22, text, required): Sign and date the contract for the vendor.
* **Payment Confirmation** (23, text, required): Confirm the payment.
* **Contract Status** (24, select_one, required): Update the contract status.
* **Assigned User** (25, text, required): Assign a user to manage the contract.
