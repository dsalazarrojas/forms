# Portrait Photography Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help photographers and clients agree on the terms and details of a portrait photography session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information in the Photographer Information field.
2. Enter your contact information in the Client Information field.
3. Provide contact information for yourself or your representative in the Contact Information field.
4. Describe the session details, including date, time, location, and other relevant information.
5. Read and agree to the Legal Terms by selecting "Yes" in the I agree to the legal terms field.
6. Sign the contract electronically by providing your E-Signature.
7. Date and time the contract electronically by providing the E-Sign Date and E-Sign Time.
8. If necessary, add any additional session details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer Information (1)** (`Photographer Information`, text, required): Enter your name, contact number, and email to ensure we can reach you with any questions or to confirm session details.
* **Client Information (2)** (`Client Information`, text, required): Please enter your name and contact information so we can keep in touch with you.
* **Contact Information (3)** (`Contact Information`, text, required): Enter your representative's name and contact information if you're not available during the session.
* **Session Details (4)** (`Session Details`, text, required): Describe the photo shoot, including date, location, and any other details we should know.
* **Legal Terms (5)** (`Legal Terms`, note, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the contract.
* **I agree to the legal terms (6)** (`Agreed`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm you've read and agreed to the terms.
* **E-Signature (7)** (`E-Signature`, text, required): Type your name to sign the contract electronically.
* **Date (8)** (`Date`, date, required): Date the contract electronically.
* **Time (9)** (`Time`, time, required): Time the contract electronically.
* **E-Sign Date (10)** (`E-Sign Date`, date, required): Date the contract electronically.
* **E-Sign Time (11)** (`E-Sign Time`, time, required): Time the contract electronically.
* **Session Details 1-10 (12-21)** (`Session Details 1-10`, text, optional): Add any additional session details if needed.
* **Client Agreement (22)** (`Client Agreement`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to confirm you've read and agree to the contract terms.
* **Contract Version (23)** (`Contract Version`, text, optional): Enter the version of the contract if you're making changes or updates.
* **Notes (24)** (`Notes`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes about the session.
* **E-Signature (Agreed) (25)** (`E-Signature (Agreed)`, text, required): Type your name to sign the contract electronically and confirm your agreement.
