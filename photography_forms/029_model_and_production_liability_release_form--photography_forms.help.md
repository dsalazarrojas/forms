# Model And Production Liability Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a release from a model, actor, or talent for the use of their likeness and image in a production. The form ensures that both the production company and the model, actor, or talent understand and agree on the terms of use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Model Name** field.
2. Provide your contact information in the **Contact Information** field.
3. Enter the name of the production company in the **Production Company** field.
4. Describe the project details in the **Project Details** field.
5. Select the project start date in the **Project Start Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Model Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Information** (Text, Optional): Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number, so we can reach you to confirm your release.
* **Production Company** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the production company that will be using your likeness and image.
* **Project Details** (Text, Optional): Describe the project, including the project type, location, and any other relevant information.
* **Project Start Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date when the project is expected to start.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
* Please review the form carefully before signing it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
