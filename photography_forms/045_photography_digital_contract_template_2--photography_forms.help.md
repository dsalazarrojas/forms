# photography_digital_contract_template_2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information and terms for a photography digital contract between the photographer and the client. This template ensures both parties have a clear understanding of their responsibilities and the contract terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start from the top and work your way down, filling in each section as required.
2. Ensure you have a clear understanding of each field before proceeding.
3. Double-check the form before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, optional): This section is for collecting basic information about the client, including their name, contact details, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide the client's contact details, such as address, phone number, and email address.
* **Shoot Date** (`shoot_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the photo shoot, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Equipment Requirements** (`equipment_requirements`, text, optional): Specify the photography equipment the client requires for the shoot, such as cameras, lenses, or lighting.
* **Copyright Details** (`copyright_details`, text, optional): Explain the copyright details, including any specific permissions granted to the client.
* **Usage Restrictions** (`usage_restrictions`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Choose 'Yes' if the client has specific restrictions on how the images can be used.
	+ Choose 'No' if there are no usage restrictions.
* **Contract Term** (`contract_term`, select one, optional):
	+ Choose 'Yes' if the contract term is agreed upon.
	+ Choose 'No' if the contract term is not agreed upon.
* **Photographer Details** (`photographer_details`, text, optional): Provide any necessary details about the photographer's role or involvement in the shoot.
