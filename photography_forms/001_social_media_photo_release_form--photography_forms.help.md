# Social Media Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Photo Release Form is used to gather necessary information for sharing photos on social media platforms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the subject's name in the **Subject Name** field.
2. Enter the subject's email address in the **Subject Email** field.
3. Enter the photographer's name in the **Photographer Name** field.
4. Enter the photographer's email address in the **Photographer Email** field.
5. Select the social media platforms on which you want to share the photos in the **Social Media Platforms** field.
6. Enter the date you plan to release the photo in the **Release Date** field.
7. Enter the number of photos you want to share in the **Number of Photos** field.
8. Enter a brief description of the photo in the **Photo Description** field.
9. Select the type of photo copyright you want to apply in the **Photo Copyright** field.
10. Specify how you want to use the photo in the **Photo Usage** field.
11. Enter the photo's location in the **Photo Location** field.
12. Enter the date and time of the photo in the **Photo Date** and **Photo Time** fields respectively.
13. Enter the duration of the photo shoot in the **Photo Duration** field.
14. Specify the type of photo in the **Photo Type** field.
15. Enter any additional notes in the **Photo Notes** field.
16. Provide a signature in the **Release Signature** field.
17. Enter the date you signed the form in the **Date Signed** field.
18. Provide any additional release notes in the **Release Notes** field.
19. Enter a title for the social media post in the **Social Media Post** field.
20. Enter the text of the post in the **Post Text** field.
21. Add any relevant tags to the post in the **Post Tags** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Social Media Photo Release Form**: This is the title of the form.
*   **Subject Name** (1): Enter the subject's name here.
*   **Subject Email** (2): Enter the subject's email address here.
*   **Photographer Name** (3): Enter the photographer's name here.
*   **Photographer Email** (4): Enter the photographer's email address here.
*   **Social Media Platforms** (6): Select the social media platforms on which you want to share the photos.
    +   Instagram
    +   Facebook
    +   Twitter
    +   TikTok
    +   Pinterest
    +   YouTube
*   **Release Date** (7): Enter the date you plan to release the photo.
*   **Number of Photos** (8): Enter the number of photos you want to share.
*   **Photo Description** (9): Enter a brief description of the photo.
*   **Photo Copyright** (10): Select the type of photo copyright you want to apply.
*   **Photo Usage** (11): Specify how you want to use the photo.
*   **Photo Location** (12): Enter the location where the photo was taken.
*   **Photo Date** (13): Enter the date the photo was taken.
*   **Photo Time** (14): Enter the time the photo was taken.
*   **Photo Duration** (15): Enter the duration of the photo shoot.
*   **Photo Type** (16): Specify the type of photo.
*   **Photo Notes** (17): Enter any additional notes about the photo.
*   **Release Signature** (18): Provide a signature here.
*   **Date Signed** (19): Enter the date you signed the form.
*   **Date Modified** (20): Enter the date the form was modified.
*   **Release Notes** (21): Enter any additional release notes.
*   **Social Media Post** (22): Enter a title for the social media post.
*   **Post Text** (23): Enter the text of the post.
*   **Post Tags** (24): Add any relevant tags to the post.
