# Family Photograph Session Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from families about their photography session preferences and needs. Please answer the questions honestly to help us create a customized experience for your family.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Select the correct options for each field, if applicable.
3. If a field is not applicable to you, please leave it blank or type "N/A" (not applicable).
4. If you have any special requests or concerns, please mention it in the "Special Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): Briefly describe the purpose of your photography session (e.g., birthday, holiday, graduation, etc.).
* **Family Member Names** (`family_member_names`, text, optional): List the names of all family members who will be participating in the session.
* **Family Member Ages** (`family_member_ages`, text, optional): List the ages of all family members who will be participating in the session.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any updates or reminders about your session.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any updates or reminders about your session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your photography session. Please note that we can discuss and adjust this date with you if needed.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Choose a time for your photography session. Please note that we can discuss and adjust this time with you if needed.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, text, optional): Choose the length of your photography session (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours).
* **Number of Family Members** (`number_of_family_members`, number, optional): Enter the number of family members who will be participating in the session.
* **Family Member Gender** (`family_member_gender`, select_one, required): Select the gender of each family member participating in the session.
* **Family Member Relationship** (`family_member_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship between each family member participating in the session.
* **Photography Style** (`photography_style`, select_multiple, required): Select the style of photography you prefer (e.g., Natural, Formal, Outdoor).
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, text, optional): Choose the location for your photography session. Please note that we can discuss and adjust this location with you if needed.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or concerns, please mention them here.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or information about your photography session.
