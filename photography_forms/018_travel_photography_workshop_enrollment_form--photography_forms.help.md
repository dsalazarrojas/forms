# Travel Photography Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for one of our travel photography workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by entering your information in the respective fields.
2. Select your preferred workshop option(s) under "workshop preferences".
3. Enter your email and workshop date and time if required.
4. Add any additional notes or comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant information** (`participant_info`, `text`, optional): A space for you to provide any relevant information about yourself as a participant.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Workshop preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more workshop options that interest you.
* **Workshop dates** (`workshop_dates`, `date`, optional): Select the date for the workshop you are interested in.
* **Workshop time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, optional): Select the time for the workshop you are interested in.
* **Workshop date and time** (`workshop_date_time`, `text`, optional): Enter the date and time of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Participant info 2** (`participant_info2`, `text`, optional): A second space for you to provide any additional information about yourself as a participant.
* **Name** (`participant_info2_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the participant who will be attending.
* **Email** (`participant_info2_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the participant who will be attending.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate information to ensure a smooth experience for both you and the workshop organizers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Note that the form is not a registration confirmation, and we will follow up with you to confirm your participation.
