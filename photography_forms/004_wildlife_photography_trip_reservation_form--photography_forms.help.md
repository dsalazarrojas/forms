# wildlife_photography_trip_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a wildlife photography trip reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill in your first name.
- Fill in your last name.
- Enter your email address.
- Enter your phone number.
- Select the trip date.
- Enter the trip length.
- Choose the trip location.
- Enter the number of participants.
- Select the participant types (Yes/No).
- If required, provide additional participant information.
- Select the payment method (Yes/No).
- Enter the payment date.
- Enter the payment amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (text, required)
*   Your first name is the first name you want to go by.

### Last Name (text, required)
*   Your last name is the last name you want to go by.

### Email (email, required)
*   Enter your email address to enable us to contact you.

### Phone (text, required)
*   Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.

### Trip Date (date, required)
*   Select the date you want to take the trip.

### Trip Length (number, required)
*   Enter the length of your trip in the format of hours.

### Trip Location (text, required)
*   Choose the location of your trip.

### Number Of Participants (number, required)
*   Enter the number of participants on your trip.

### Participant Types (select_multiple, required)
*   Indicate if you are bringing any participant types (Yes/No).

### Additional Participant Information (text, optional)
*   This field is optional but can be used to provide extra participant information.

### Payment Method (select_one, required)
*   Indicate if you will pay for your trip (Yes/No).

### Payment Date (date, required)
*   Enter the date you will be making the payment.

### Payment Amount (number, required)
*   Enter the amount of money you will be paying for your trip.

### Payment Notes (text, optional)
*   If you have any payment notes, use this field to add a comment.
