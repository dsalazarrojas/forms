# Portrait Photo Shoot Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Portrait Photo Shoot Registration form is designed to collect information from clients and photographers for a portrait photo shoot session. This form helps to gather essential details about the client, session date and time, and photographer's information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Client Information**, including your name, email, and any other relevant details.
2. Select a **Session Date** for the photo shoot.
3. Choose a **Session Time** for the photo shoot.
4. Fill in your **Photographer Information**, including any relevant details about the photographer.
5. Enter your **Contact Info**, including a primary contact method (e.g., phone number, email, or address).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (client_info)**:
	+ This field is used to collect your identifying information as a client. Please enter your full name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Session Date (session_date)**:
	+ Select a date for the photo shoot. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Session Time (session_time)**:
	+ Choose a time for the photo shoot. Please select the hour and minute format (e.g., 09:00, 10:30, etc.).
* **Photographer Information (photographer_info)**:
	+ Provide any relevant details about the photographer, such as their name, contact method, or any special instructions.
* **Contact Info (contact_info)**:
	+ Enter your primary contact method, including a phone number, email address, or mailing address. This ensures we can reach you if there are any last-minute changes or cancellations.
