# photo_release_form_for_minors - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photo release form for minors is used to grant permission for minors to be photographed or recorded, ensuring that all parties involved have given their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out your name as the parent or guardian of the minor.
2. Enter the name of the minor child.
3. Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
4. Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
5. Enter the date of birth of the minor child.
6. Enter the date of birth of the adult (parent/guardian) filling out the form.
7. Review and sign the form to acknowledge that you have given your consent for the minor to be photographed or recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Photo Release Form For Minors** (`photo_release_form_for_minors`, text, required): This is the title of the form.
- **minor_child_name** (`minor_child_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the minor child who is being photographed or recorded.
- **parent_or_guardian_name** (`parent_or_guardian_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the minor child.
- **email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
- **phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
- **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter the date of birth of the minor child.
- **date_of_birth_child** (`date_of_birth_child`, date, required): Please enter the date of birth of the adult (parent/guardian) filling out the form.
- **date_of_birth_adult** (`date_of_birth_adult`, date, required): (Note: This field was not necessary, but was included in the original YAML. You can safely ignore this field and simply enter the date of birth of the adult in the field above.)

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have filled out all fields accurately and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask the photographer or event coordinator for guidance.
- This form is required for events where minors are involved and photography is taking place.
