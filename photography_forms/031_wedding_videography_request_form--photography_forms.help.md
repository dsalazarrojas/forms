# Wedding Videography Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential clients or customers about their wedding videography needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select the relevant options for your wedding videography needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Videography Request Form** (1): Enter a brief description of your wedding videography request.
* **package** (2): Select the package that best suits your needs:
	+ Basic Package: Suitable for small, intimate weddings.
	+ Deluxe Package: Offers additional services and features.
	+ Elite Package: Customized and high-end services.
* **Add-ons** (3): Select any additional services you'd like to add to your package:
	+ Cinematographer: Additional camera operator for better coverage.
	+ Wedding Planner: Help with wedding planning and organization.
	+ Wedding Coordinator: Assistance with wedding day logistics.
* **Videographer Details** (4): Enter your preferred videographer's name and contact information.
* **Experience** (5): Describe your preferred videographer's experience and qualifications.
* **Style** (6): Enter your preferred videographer's style or specialty (e.g. documentary, narrative, etc.).
* **Equipment** (7): Describe the equipment you'd like the videographer to use.
* **Availability** (8): Enter the availability of your preferred videographer.
* **Wedding Details** (9): Enter details about your wedding, including date, location, and number of guests.
* **Date** (10): Enter the date of your wedding.
* **Location** (11): Enter the location of your wedding.
* **Number of Guests** (12): Enter the expected number of guests at your wedding.
* **Number of Days** (13): Enter the number of days you'd like the videographer to work.
* **Contact Info** (14): Enter your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Name** (15): Enter your name.
* **Email** (16): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (17): Enter your phone number.
* **Message** (18): Enter any additional message or comments.
* **Form Submission** (19): Enter your submission information, including any notes or comments.
* **Signature** (20): Enter your signature.
* **Date Submitted** (21): Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
* **IP Address** (22): Enter the IP address of the device you used to submit this form.
* **User Agent** (23): Enter the User Agent of the device you used to submit this form.
* **User OS** (24): Enter the Operating System of the device you used to submit this form.
* **User Browser** (25): Enter the Browser of the device you used to submit this form.
