# Youths Photography Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Photography Order Form is designed for customers to place orders for youth photography services. This form allows you to provide your information, select print order options, and enter a payment amount.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details in the **Youth Photography Reservation** field. This is a required field.
2. Select your **Print Order** options from the dropdown menu. You can choose multiple options.
3. Enter your **Payment Amount** in the provided field. This field is optional but recommended.
4. Enter your **Email** address if you want to receive updates or notifications.
5. Add your **Phone** number if you'd like to be contacted for any updates or queries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Youth Photography Reservation** (`Youth Photography Reservation`, text, required): Enter your name or a brief description to identify your order.
* **Print Order** (`Print Order`, select multiple, not required): Select the print options you want for your photos. Please select all the options you're interested in.
* **Payment Amount** (`Payment Amount`, number, not required): Enter the amount you'd like to pay for your order. If you're unsure about the price, you can contact us for guidance.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, not required): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates or notifications about your order.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for any updates or queries.

## Tips
* Please ensure you enter the correct payment amount to avoid any issues with your order.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
