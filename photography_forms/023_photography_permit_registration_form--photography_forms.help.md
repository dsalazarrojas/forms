# Photography Permit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to register your event or photography session with the permit requirements of your jurisdiction. This form will help you gather the necessary information to ensure compliance with local laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Venue Name**: Enter the name of the venue or location where the event or photography session will take place.
2.  **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for the event or photography session.
3.  **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the event or photography session.
4.  **Photographer Name**: Enter your name as the photographer or person responsible for the session.
5.  **Photographer Organization**: Enter the name of your organization or company associated with the photography session.
6.  **Street Address**: Enter the street address where the event or photography session will take place.
7.  **City**: Enter the city where the event or photography session will take place.
8.  **State**: Select the state where the event or photography session will take place.
9.  **Zip**: Enter the zip code where the event or photography session will take place.
10. **Event Start Date**: Enter the date when the event or photography session will begin.
11. **Event End Date**: Enter the date when the event or photography session will end.
12. **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event or photography session in 24-hour format (e.g., 10:00 AM).
13. **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of the event or photography session in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
14. **Event Location**: Enter a detailed description of the location where the event or photography session will take place.
15. **Event Description**: Briefly describe the purpose and nature of the event or photography session.
16. **Photo Permit Type**: Select the type of photography permit you require (e.g., Still Photography, Video, Film, Other).
17.  **Photo Permit Purpose**: Select all applicable purposes for the photography permit (e.g., Wedding, Commercial, Event, Other).
18.  **Photo Permit Duration**: Select the duration of the photography session (e.g., One day, One hour, Half day, Half hour, Two hours, Three hours, Four hours, Five hours, Full day, Multiple days, Unknown).
19.  **Photo Permit Fee**: Enter the permit fee associated with the photography session.
20.  **Photo Permit Expiration**: Enter the expiration date of the permit, if applicable.
21.  **Photo Permit Status**: Select the status of the permit (e.g., Active, Inactive, Unknown).
22.  **Photo Permit Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the permit.
23.  **Assigned To**: Indicate if the permit has been assigned to someone or organization.
24.  **Assigned By**: Indicate if the permit has been assigned by someone or organization.
25.  **Submitted By**: Indicate if the permit has been submitted by someone or organization.
