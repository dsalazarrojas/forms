# Event Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Photography Contract form is used to collect information about an event, including the type, date, location, and client information, as well as the photographer's details and terms of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event details, including the event type, date, location, and time.
2. Enter the photographer's details, including their name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide the client's details, including their name, email, and phone number.
4. Select the services you have agreed to provide (e.g., photography, videography, editing).
5. Choose the payment method (e.g., bank transfer, cash, check).
6. Enter the event photography fees and cancellation fees.
7. Review and sign the agreement section to confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**
	+ **Event Type**: Select the type of event (e.g., wedding, party, graduation).
	+ **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event (mm/dd/yyyy).
	+ **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event (hh:mm AM/PM).
	+ **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event (e.g., venue, address).
* **Event Photography Contract**
	+ **Photographer Details**: Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Client Details**
	+ **Client Name**: Enter the client's name.
	+ **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address.
	+ **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Photography Services**: Select all the photography services you will provide (e.g., photography, videography, editing).
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment (e.g., bank transfer, cash, check).
* **Event Photography Fees**: Enter the fees for the event photography services.
* **Cancellation Fees**: Enter the fees for cancellation.
* **Cancellation Terms**: Enter any cancellation terms and conditions.
* **Event Photography Terms**: Enter any additional terms and conditions for the event photography services.
* **Client Signature**: The client signs to confirm their understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **Photographer Signature**: The photographer signs to confirm their understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **Agreement**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the agreement.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the agreement.
