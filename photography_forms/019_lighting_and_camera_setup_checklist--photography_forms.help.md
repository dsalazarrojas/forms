# Lighting And Camera Setup Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a checklist designed to aid photographers in setting up their equipment and settings for a successful photo shoot. It covers various aspects of lighting and camera setup, ensuring that everything is in place before commencement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the necessary camera equipment from the options provided in the "Camera Setup" page.
2. Choose the type of lighting you will be using for the shoot from the options in the "Lighting Setup" page.
3. Indicate any additional equipment that may be required in the "Other Equipment" page.
4. Fill in the date and time of the shoot in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
5. Select the type of camera and the specific settings you will be using for the shoot.
6. Specify the type of lighting to be used and the location of the shoot.
7. Complete the fields for the photographer and assistant's names if they are present.
8. Enter any additional notes or equipment required for the shoot in the "Notes" field.
9. Finally, fill in your signature and the date you signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camera Setup** (`camera_setup`, Select Multiple): Choose all the necessary camera equipment for the shoot, such as Camera, Lens, and Additional Equipment.
* **Lighting Setup** (`lighting_setup`, Select Multiple): Select the type of lighting to be used, such as Light, Stands, and Diffusers.
* **Other Equipment** (`other_equipment`, Select Multiple): Indicate any additional equipment required for the shoot.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Text): Enter any additional notes or information about the shoot.
* **Date** (`date`, Date): Fill in the date of the shoot.
* **Time** (`time`, Time): Fill in the time of the shoot.
* **Camera Type** (`camera_type`, Select One): Choose the type of camera to be used (DSLR, Mirrorless, or Film).
* **Camera Settings** (`camera_settings`, Text): Enter the specific camera settings to be used.
* **Lighting Type** (`lighting_type`, Select One): Select the type of lighting to be used (Natural or Artificial).
* **Location** (`location`, Select One): Choose the location of the shoot (Studio, Outdoor, or Indoor).
* **Photographer** (`photographer`, Text): Enter the name of the photographer.
* **Assistant** (`assistant`, Text): Enter the name of the assistant.
* **Equipment** (`equipment`, Text): Enter any additional equipment required for the shoot.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, Text): Enter any additional notes about the shoot.
* **Review** (`review`, Text): Enter a review or feedback about the shoot.
* **Signature** (`signature`, Text): Sign the form to confirm completion.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, Date): Fill in the date you signed the form.

Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise specified as "required" in the original form schema.
