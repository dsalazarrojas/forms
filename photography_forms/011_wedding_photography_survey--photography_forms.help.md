# Wedding Photography Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Photography Survey is a form designed to collect information from clients about their wedding photography needs. This information helps photographers to better understand the client's requirements, preferences, and expectations, ensuring a more personalized and enjoyable experience for both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your name, phone number, and email address in the Client Information section (page 1).
2. Fill in your contact information, including address and any relevant contact person in the Contact Information section (page 2).
3. Describe the wedding event, date, location, and any special details in the Wedding Details section (page 3).
4. In the Photographer Information section (page 4), input any questions or concerns you may have for the assigned photographer or photographer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (Client Information, Text, Optional):
	+ Please input your full name, phone number, and email address to enable easy contact and communication with the photographer.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, Text, Optional):
	+ Provide your home or business address, or any alternative contact person's address, to facilitate smooth communication and logistics.
* **Wedding Details** (Wedding Details, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the date, location, and any other relevant details about the wedding event.
* **Photographer Information** (Photographer Information, Text, Optional):
	+ Share any thoughts, questions, or preferences you have about the assigned photographer or the photography services provided.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and specific as possible when providing your contact and wedding details.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please do not hesitate to share them with the assigned photographer.
