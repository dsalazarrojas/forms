# photography_mini_session_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photography_mini_session_questionnaire_form is designed to collect information from photography clients, including contact details, session preferences, and other relevant information. This form is intended to be used for sessions with photographers, but the information could be adapted or extended to suit other contexts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your client's information in the "Client Information" section.
2. Choose the contact method that best suits your needs in the "Contact Method" section.
3. Schedule the session date and time in the "Date" section.
4. Specify the session location in the "Location" section.
5. Indicate the type of session in the "Session Type" section.
6. Enter any client preferences in the "Client Preferences" section.
7. Enter any photographer preferences in the "Photographer Preferences" section.
8. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" section.
9. Enter your email and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" sections.
10. Enter your mailing address in the "Mail" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (text, required/optional): Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Method** (select_one, required/optional): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Mail).
* **Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date of the photography session.
* **Time** (time, required/optional): Specify the time of the photography session.
* **Location** (text, required/optional): Enter the location where the photography session will take place.
* **Session Type** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of session (Portrait, Event, or Other).
* **Client Preferences** (text, required/optional): Enter any specific preferences or requirements you have for the session.
* **Photographer Preferences** (text, required/optional): Enter any specific preferences or requirements you have for the photographer.
* **Notes** (note, required/optional): Add any additional information or comments about the session.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Mail** (text, required/optional): Enter your mailing address.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Be as specific as possible when entering session details to ensure a smooth experience.
* Use the "Notes" field to include any additional comments or instructions that may be useful for the photographer.
