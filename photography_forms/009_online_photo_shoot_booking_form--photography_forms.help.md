# Online Photo Shoot Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers to help you as a photographer prepare for their photoshoot.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your customer details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. Choose the type of session you are booking (e.g. Session, E-commerce Photoshoot, Editorial Photoshoot, Social Media Photoshoot).
3. Provide a brief description of your photoshoot requirements.
4. (Optional) If you have any further questions or requests, please let us know in the text box.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer Details**
* **Name**: Your name (required)
* **Email**: Your email address (required)
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number (required)

### 2. **Session Type**
* **Session Type**: Select the type of session you are booking:
	+ Session: A general photoshoot.
	+ E-commerce Photoshoot: A photoshoot for an online store or business.
	+ Editorial Photoshoot: A photoshoot for a publication or editorial purpose.
	+ Social Media Photoshoot: A photoshoot for social media.

### 3. **Description**
* **Description**: Briefly describe what you are looking for in your photoshoot. This could include what you want to achieve, what kind of images you want, and any specific requirements you may have.

### 4. **Purpose**
* **Purpose**: If you have any further questions or requests, please let us know in this text box.

### 5. **Further Questions**
* **Further Questions**: If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them here.

# Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing your description and further questions.
* Make sure to proofread your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
Contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
