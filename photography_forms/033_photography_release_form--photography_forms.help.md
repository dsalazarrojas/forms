# Photography Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information and permissions related to a photography shoot. It is used to document the details of the capture, the photographer's and client's details, and the usage rights of the images. This form is used by photographers to ensure they have the necessary permissions and information to use their photos for commercial purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Title** of the photography shoot. This is a brief description of the photography project.
2. Select the **Category** of the shoot. This indicates the type of photography project (e.g., commercial, personal, etc.).
3. Provide a **Description** of the shoot. This is a brief description of the event or subject being photographed.
4. Enter the **Photographer's Details**. This includes the name and contact information of the photographer.
5. Enter the **Client's Details**. This includes the name and contact information of the client.
6. Enter the **Location** where the photography shoot took place.
7. Enter the **Date Of Capture** when the photography shoot took place.
8. Enter the **Time Of Capture** when the photography shoot took place.
9. Enter the **Camera Details**. This includes the camera model and other relevant details.
10. Enter the **Camera Settings** used during the shoot.
11. Indicate the **Usage Rights** of the photos. Select all that apply.
12. Provide any other **Usage Rights** that are not listed.
13. Sign and date the form with the **Signed By** section.
14. Date and sign the form with the **Signed On** date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photography Release Form**: Enter the title of the photography shoot.
* **Category**: Select the type of photography project (e.g., commercial, personal, etc.).
	+ If unsure, select "Other" and provide a brief description.
* **Description**: Briefly describe the event or subject being photographed.
* **Photographer Details**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Client Details**: Enter your client's name and contact information.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the photography shoot took place.
* **Date Of Capture**: Enter the date when the photography shoot took place.
* **Time Of Capture**: Enter the time when the photography shoot took place.
* **Camera Details**: Enter the camera model and other relevant details.
* **Camera Settings**: Enter the camera settings used during the shoot (e.g., ISO, aperture, shutter speed).
* **Usage Rights**: Select all that apply regarding the usage of the photos.
	+ "Yes" indicates the usage rights are granted for commercial purposes.
	+ "No" indicates the usage rights are not granted for commercial purposes.
* **Usage Rights Other**: If there are other usage rights not listed, provide a brief description.
* **Signed By**: Sign and date the form with your name.
* **Signed On**: Date and sign the form with the date.
