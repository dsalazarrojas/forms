# Photography And Videography Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect event and contact details for photography and videography requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as prompted.
2. Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photography And Videography Request Form** (1, text, required/optional: false): This is the title of the form. You can enter any text that you want to describe this event. 
* **Event Details** (2, text, required/optional: false): Here, provide a brief description of the event, including any relevant details about what will be captured. 
* **Event Date** (3, date, required/optional: false): Enter the date of the event. This will help ensure the photos and videos are captured at the correct time.
* **Location** (4, text, required/optional: false): Provide the location of the event. This could be a physical address or a description of where the event is located.
* **Event Time** (5, time, required/optional: false): Enter the time of the event. This will help ensure the photos and videos are captured at the correct time.
* **Contact Details** (6, text, required/optional: false): Here, enter any details about who to contact for the event or other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Email** (7, email, required/optional: false): Enter the contact email for the event. This will help us get in touch with you if we need more information.
* **Contact Phone** (8, text, required/optional: false): Enter the contact phone number for the event. This will help us get in touch with you if we need more information.

## Tips
- Please double-check all fields before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
