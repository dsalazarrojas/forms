# Photography Accident Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Accident Waiver form is a document that waives any potential liability for injuries or damages suffered by you or your heirs during a photography session. It serves as a release of liability for the photographer and their representatives.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please fill in all the required information, including your name, the date of the incident, the location of the incident, and a description of what happened.
*   Select the type of incident that occurred.
*   Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding of the terms.
*   Read the waiver carefully to understand your rights and responsibilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Photography Accident Waiver** (`photography_accident_waiver`, text, required):
    This field is not a field to fill. It's the title of the form and is automatically generated.
*   **client_name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Your name as the subject of the photography session. If you are representing someone else, please enter their name instead.
*   **date_of_incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, optional): The date of the incident that occurred during the photography session. If there was no incident, you can leave this field blank.
*   **location_of_incident** (`location_of_incident`, text, optional): The location where the incident occurred. If there was no incident, you can leave this field blank.
*   **type_of_incident** (`type_of_incident`, select_one, optional):
    *   Select the type of incident that occurred. Please choose from the following options:
        - **Accident**: If you experienced an accident or injury during the shoot.
        - **Injury**: If you experienced an injury during the shoot.
        - **Illness**: If you fell ill during the shoot.
        - **Damage**: If you experienced damage to your property or equipment.
*   **description_of_incident** (`description_of_incident`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of what happened during the shoot, including any relevant details about the incident.
*   **medical_certification** (`medical_certification`, note, optional): A field for medical certification or documentation. This field is not for filling out; it's for attaching a relevant medical certification or documentation.
*   **release_of_liability** (`release_of_liability`, select_multiple, optional):
    *   I, the undersigned, hereby release and hold harmless the photographer and their representatives from any and all liability for any injuries or damages suffered by me or my heirs during the photoshoot.
    *   I, the undersigned, hereby grant permission to the photographer to use my likeness in their promotional and marketing materials.
    *   I, the undersigned, hereby grant to the photographer, and their assignees and successors, a non-exclusive license to use the photographs taken during the photoshoot for their own personal and commercial use.

## Tips

*   Please read the waiver carefully and understand its terms before signing.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the photographer or their representative.
*   This form is not a substitute for medical attention or treatment. If you experience any injuries or illnesses during the shoot, please seek medical attention immediately.
