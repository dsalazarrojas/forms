# Photography Session Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to book a photography session with a professional photography business or studio. Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure a successful and enjoyable experience for both you and the photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your contact information, including your name and email address or phone number.
2. Specify the number of people attending the session.
3. Choose a date for the session.
4. Select a time for the session.
5. Choose a location for the session.
6. Select the type of session you want (indoor or outdoor).
7. Agree or decline to be contacted after the session.
8. Enter the deposit amount for the session.
9. Agree or decline to the copyright agreement.
10. Select a payment method (bank transfer, credit card, or Payoneer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (contact_info):
  • Label: Contact Information
  • Type: text
  • Required: Yes
  • Description: This is where you'll provide your name and contact details (email or phone number). Please ensure they are accurate and up to date.

* **Number of People** (number_of_people):
  • Label: Number of People
  • Type: number
  • Required: Yes
  • Description: This is where you specify the number of people attending the session, including yourself.

* **Session Date** (session_date):
  • Label: Session Date
  • Type: date
  • Required: Yes
  • Description: Choose a date for your session, ideally one that works for both you and the photographer.

* **Session Time** (session_time):
  • Label: Session Time
  • Type: time
  • Required: Yes
  • Description: Select a time slot that suits your schedule.

* **Location** (location):
  • Label: Location
  • Type: text
  • Required: Yes
  • Description: Indicate a location for your session, either at home, a studio, or outdoors.

* **Type of Session** (type_of_session):
  • Label: Type of Session
  • Type: select_multiple
  • Required: Yes
  • Description: Please select whether your session is indoor or outdoor.

* **Contact Agreement** (contact_agreement):
  • Label: Contact Agreement
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: Yes
  • Description: You may want to be contacted by the photographer after the session for follow-up or other purposes. 

* **Deposit Amount** (deposit_amount):
  • Label: Deposit Amount
  • Type: number
  • Required: Yes
  • Description: Enter the deposit amount you are willing to pay for the session.

* **Copyright Agreement** (copyright_agreement):
  • Label: Copyright Agreement
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: Yes
  • Description: You need to agree to the copyright terms and conditions set by the photographer. Please read and understand before proceeding.

* **Payment** (payment):
  • Label: Payment
  • Type: select_multiple
  • Required: Yes
  • Description: Select your preferred payment method (bank transfer, credit card, or Payoneer).
