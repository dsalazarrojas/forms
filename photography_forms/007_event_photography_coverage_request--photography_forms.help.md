# Event Photography Coverage Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential details for event photography coverage requests. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that your event is accurately captured.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of event you are requesting coverage for (Event, Wedding, or Birthday).
2. Enter the scheduled start time for the event.
3. Enter the scheduled end time for the event.
4. Provide the event location.
5. Enter the date of the event.
6. Specify the client budget for the event.
7. Share a link to the photographer's portfolio.
8. Choose the number of photographers you require for the event.
9. Specify the equipment required for the event.
10. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the event.
11. Enter the email address of the contact person.
12. Enter the phone number of the contact person.
13. Write any additional message or information about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Type**: Select the type of event (Event, Wedding, or Birthday) for which you are requesting photography coverage. This will help us understand the scope and style of your event.
* **Schedule Start Time**: Enter the exact start time for your event. This is necessary to ensure our photographers arrive on time and capture your event's beginning.
* **Schedule End Time**: Enter the expected end time for your event. This allows us to plan the coverage duration accurately.
* **Location**: Provide the exact location of the event, including any specific details that might be relevant for photography purposes (e.g., stage, podium, etc.).
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of your event. This is crucial to ensure that our photographers are aware of the correct dates and can plan accordingly.
* **Client Budget**: Specify the budget allocated for photography services. This helps us understand your expectations and allocate resources accordingly.
* **Photographer Details**: Please provide a link to the photographer's portfolio, so we can review their work and ensure it aligns with your style and expectations.
* **Number Of Photographers**: Choose the number of photographers you require for your event. This helps us plan the coverage and ensure sufficient personnel are present.
* **Equipment**: Specify the equipment required for your event (e.g., lights, lenses, etc.). This allows us to bring the necessary gear and ensure a smooth shoot.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the event. This helps us communicate effectively and clarify any last-minute requests.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person. This is the primary contact point for us to send updates and confirmations.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person. This is for urgent or last-minute communications.
* **Message**: Write any additional information or specific requests you might have for your event photography coverage. This is the perfect place to mention anything that might be crucial to capturing your event's essence.
