# Free Local Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Free Local Assistance Request Form is used to collect information from users who are requesting free local assistance. It helps the organization understand the user's needs and provide the necessary support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select the type of assistance you are requesting (Assistance with Food or Emergency Assistance).
2. Choose your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or In Person).
3. Provide details about your request in the space provided.
4. If applicable, select your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit, or Other).
5. Enter the payment amount (if applicable).
6. Select the current status of your request (Open or Closed).
7. Fill out the form accurately to ensure that the assistance is provided efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`User Input`, `text`, optional): This field is intended for you to enter any additional information about your request. It is not required, so you can leave it blank if you do not have any extra information to provide.
* **Request Type** (`Request Type`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the type of assistance you are requesting (Assistance with Food or Emergency Assistance). This will help us understand the nature of your request.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or In Person) so that we can reach out to you efficiently.
* **Request Details** (`Request Details`, `text`, optional): Provide as much detail as possible about your request in this space. This will help us understand your needs and provide the necessary support.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, please select your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit, or Other).
* **Payment Amount** (`Payment Amount`, `number`, optional): If you selected a payment method, enter the payment amount in this field.
* **Request Status** (`Request Status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current status of your request (Open or Closed). This will help us track the progress of your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you complete the form accurately to ensure that the assistance is provided efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* If you would like to update your request, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
