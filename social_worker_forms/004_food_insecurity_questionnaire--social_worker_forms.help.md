# Food Insecurity Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a household's food security status, helping social workers assess and identify areas of need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions on each page to the best of your ability.
2.  Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
3.  If you are unsure about any question, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Household Members
* **Household Members** (Number): Enter the total number of members living in your household.

### Household Type
* **Household Type** (Select Multiple): Choose the type of household you live in:
	+ Household
	+ Institution

### How Many Members Live in the Household?
* **How many members live in the household?** (Number): Enter the total number of members living in your household.

### Age of Head of Household
* **Age of Head of Household** (Number): Enter the age of the head of household.

### Household Income Range
* **Household Income Range** (Select One): Choose the income range of your household:
	+ Less than $25
	+ $0
	+ $25,000 - $49,000
	+ $50,000 - 000 or more

### Household Food Security Level
* **Household Food Security Level** (Select One): Choose the level of food security in your household:
	+ Very Low
	+ Low
	+ Moderate
	+ High

### If Other, Describe
* **If other, describe** (Text): If your household's food security level is not listed above, please describe it.

### Reason for Food Insecurity Level
* **Reason for Food Insecurity Level** (Select Multiple): If your household experiences food insecurity, choose the reasons below:
	+ Not enough money
	+ No food access
	+ Food prices too high
	+ Not enough food options
	+ Other

### Other Reason for Food Insecurity Level
* **Other Reason for Food Insecurity Level** (Text): If there is another reason for food insecurity not listed above, please describe it.

### Household Food Security Programs Served
* **Household Food Security Programs Served** (Select Multiple): If your household has received any of the following programs or services, select them:
	+ SNAP
	+ WIC
	+ Other

### Other Programs Served
* **Other Programs Served** (Text): If there are other programs or services your household has received, please describe them.

### Other 2
* **Other 2** (Text): If you need to add another reason for food insecurity or other program received, please describe it.
