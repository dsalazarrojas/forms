# Medical Employment Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Employment Information Form is designed to collect essential details about an employee's employment status, including their contact information, emergency contact, medical insurance, and emergency contact address. This form helps ensure that your team has accurate and up-to-date information in case of any medical emergencies or important employment-related matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

- **Start by reviewing each page carefully**. This form consists of multiple pages, each with its own set of questions.
- **Answer every question carefully**. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
- **Save your progress frequently**. In case you need to stop and come back later, your progress will be saved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Employee Contact Information
• **Employee Contact Information** (Select Multiple, Optional): This field is used to select whether the employee has any contact information that the employer should be aware of. Select 'Yes' if the employee has shared their contact information with the employer, or 'No' if they have not.

### 2. Emergency Contact
• **Emergency Contact** (Text, Optional): In this field, please enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency. This person will be notified if the employee is involved in a medical emergency or other employment-related matter.

### 3. Medical Insurance
• **Medical Insurance** (Text, Optional): This field is for you to enter the details of the employee's medical insurance information. Please provide the name of the insurance provider, policy number, and any other relevant details.

### 4. Emergency Contact Address
• **Emergency Contact Address** (Text, Optional): Please enter the address of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency. This will help the employer send important documents or communicate with the emergency contact efficiently.

## Tips
- **Double-check your answers** before submitting the form. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- **Ask for help if you're unsure**. If you're unsure about any field or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your HR department or a colleague for assistance.
