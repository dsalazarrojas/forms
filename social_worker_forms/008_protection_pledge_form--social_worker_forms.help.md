# Protection Pledge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Protection Pledge Form is used to collect information from employees or students to confirm their willingness to protect sensitive information and follow confidentiality guidelines. It is a crucial tool for ensuring the security and trustworthiness of confidential data.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please select all the options that apply to you from the first field, "protection_pledge_form".
2. In the second field, "employee_or_student", select either "Employee" or "Student" as appropriate.
3. Enter your name in the "name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. For the "date_of_birth" field, enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **protection_pledge_form**: This field is a select multiple field where you can choose all the options that apply to you. The options are "Yes" and "No". Please select all the options that you agree with.
* **employee_or_student**: This field is a select one field where you can choose either "Employee" or "Student". Please select the option that best describes your status.
* **name**: This field is a text field where you can enter your name.
* **email**: This field is an email field where you can enter your email address.
* **phone**: This field is a text field where you can enter your phone number.
* **date_of_birth**: This field is a date field where you can enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
