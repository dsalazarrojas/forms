# Racism Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Racism Survey is designed to gather information on individuals' experiences with racism and discrimination. This form is intended to help us better understand the prevalence and impact of racism and discrimination in our community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with *.
2. Provide honest and accurate information about your experiences with racism and discrimination.
3. Select the options that best describe your experiences from the provided lists.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes that you feel are relevant to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Racism Survey** (Survey Title): This is the title of the survey. Please fill in a brief description of the purpose of the form.
* **Demographic Information** (Demographic Info): Provide a brief description of your demographic information, such as your race, ethnicity, gender, and age.
* **Race** (Race): Select one of the options that best describes your racial identity. This will help us understand your experiences with racism in the context of your racial identity.
* **Ethnicity** (Ethnicity): Select all the options that apply to your ethnic identity. This will help us understand your experiences with racism in the context of your ethnic identity.
* **Gender** (Gender): Select one of the options that best describes your gender.
* **Age** (Age): Enter your age in years.
* **Education Level** (Education Level): Select one of the options that best describes your education level.
* **Education Field** (Education Field): Select one of the options that best describes your field of education.
* **Experience With Discrimination** (Experience With Discrimination): Select one of the options that best describes your experience with discrimination. Please answer honestly, even if it's difficult.
* **Type of Discrimination** (Type of Discrimination): Select one of the options that best describes the type of discrimination you've experienced.
* **Level of Discrimination** (Level of Discrimination): Select one of the options that best describes the level of discrimination you've experienced.
* **Frequency of Discrimination** (Frequency of Discrimination): Select one of the options that best describes how often you've experienced discrimination.
* **Location** (Location): Provide a brief description of where you've experienced discrimination.
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date when you experienced discrimination.
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time of day when you experienced discrimination.
* **Note** (Note): Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience with discrimination.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address, if you have one. (Please note that your email address will not be published in the results of the survey.)
* **Phone** (Phone): Enter your phone number, if you have one. (Please note that your phone number will not be published in the results of the survey.)

Note: Please keep in mind that some fields are not required to be filled in, and you can skip them if you don't feel comfortable providing that information.
