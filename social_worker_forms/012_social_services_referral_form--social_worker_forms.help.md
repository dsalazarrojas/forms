# Social Services Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Social Services Referral Form is a tool used by social workers to refer clients to various services that can provide them with assistance and support. This form helps you to identify the client's needs and match them with the most suitable service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information on the **Client Info** page.
2. Select the type of service you are referring the client to from the **Service Referral** dropdown menu on the second page.
3. If you have specific service details, such as the number of sessions or visits required, enter them on the **Service Details** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Info
* **Client Info** (Text, Optional): Enter your name and contact information so we can reach out to you with any questions or follow-up information.

### Service Referral
* **Service Referral** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of service you are referring the client to. The options are:
	+ Case Management: Helps clients with daily living skills and provides support for basic needs.
	+ Community Support: Provides social support and connection to community resources.
	+ Home Visits: Offers in-home visits for clients who are unable to leave their homes.
	+ Information and Referral: Connects clients with other social services and resources.
	+ Intake Coordination: Helps clients navigate the intake process for other services.

### Service Details
* **Service Details** (Number, Optional): If you have specific details about the service, such as the number of sessions or visits required, enter them here.

### Tips
* Please ensure that you have obtained the client's consent before submitting the form.
* Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure the client receives the most suitable service.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
