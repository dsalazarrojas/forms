# Shared Housing Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shared Housing Verification Form is designed to collect information about a tenant or renter's living situation, including their relationship with their landlord or property manager, the terms of their lease or rent agreement, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide accurate information about yourself as the tenant or renter.
2. Enter the start and end dates of your lease or rent agreement.
3. Enter the details of your relationship with your landlord or property manager.
4. Enter the frequency and amount of rent you pay.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments about your living situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Information** (`tenant_info`, text, required): Enter your name and any other identifying information as the tenant or renter.
* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start`, date, required): Enter the date your lease or rent agreement began.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end`, date, required): Enter the date your lease or rent agreement will end.
* **Landlord Information** (`landlord_info`, text, required): Enter the name and any other identifying information about your landlord or property manager.
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of rent you pay, if applicable.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you pay rent (e.g., Every month, Every 2 weeks, etc.).
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, required): Enter the date you moved into your shared housing unit.
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, date, required): Enter the date you moved out of your shared housing unit.
* **Unit Number** (`unit_number`, number, required): Enter the number of your shared housing unit.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address of your shared housing unit.
* **Renter Name** (`renter_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the renter or tenant.
* **Renter Phone** (`renter_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the renter or tenant.
* **Renter Email** (`renter_email`, text, optional): Enter your email address as the renter or tenant, if applicable.
* **Relationship** (`renter_relationship`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you are related to your landlord or property manager (e.g., Spouse, Partner, etc.).
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your landlord or property manager.
* **Landlord Phone** (`landlord_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your landlord or property manager.
* **Landlord Email** (`landlord_email`, text, optional): Enter the email address of your landlord or property manager, if applicable.
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose how often you pay rent (e.g., Weekly, Bi-Monthly, Monthly).
* **Rent Frequency Other** (`rent_frequency_other`, text, optional): Enter any other rent frequency not listed (e.g., Quarterly).
* **Rent Amount Other** (`rent_amount_other`, text, optional): Enter any other rent amount not listed.
* **Unit Type** (`unit_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of unit you occupy (e.g., Single, Shared, Multi-Family).
* **Unit Bedrooms** (`unit_bedrooms`, select_multiple, required): Choose the number of bedrooms in your unit (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **Unit Bathrooms** (`unit_bathrooms`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the number of bathrooms in your unit (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **Unit Square Feet** (`unit_square_feet`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the square footage of your unit (e.g., 1-100, 100-200, 200-500).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your living situation, if applicable.
