# Digital Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Emergency Contact Form is designed for social workers to quickly gather contact information and relationship details about emergency contacts for clients, patients, or other individuals they interact with.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Select your relationship to the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
6. Enter the name of the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
7. Enter the email address of the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Email" field.
8. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
9. Enter the address of the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Address" field.
10. Select the relationship of the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
11. If necessary, enter additional information about the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Other" field.

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization or affiliation.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select your relationship to the emergency contact (e.g. Parent, Spouse, Colleague).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact.
