# Protest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Protest Survey form is used to collect data on protests and demonstrations.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select Protest Participation

*   **Protest Participation** (`Yes`, `No`, required: false): This field asks whether you participated in a protest or demonstration. Please select "Yes" if you participated and "No" if you did not.
