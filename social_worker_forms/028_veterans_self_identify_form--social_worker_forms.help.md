# Veterans Self Identify Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterans Self Identify Form is used to collect essential information for the administration of veterans' benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with your personal details.
2. Answer the relevant questions about your military service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Service Connected** (`service_connected`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are connected to the military service.
* **Veteran ID Document** (`veteran_id_document`, select_multiple, required): Check if you have a veteran ID document.
* **Veteran ID Number** (`veteran_id_number`, number, required): Enter your veteran ID number if applicable.
* **Veteran ID Type** (`veteran_id_type`, select_one, required): Indicate the type of veteran ID you have.
* **Veteran ID Location** (`veteran_id_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you obtained your veteran ID.
* **Veteran ID Expiration** (`veteran_id_expiration`, date, optional): Enter the expiration date of your veteran ID.
* **Veteran ID Issuer** (`veteran_id_issuer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the entity that issued your veteran ID.
* **Veteran ID Issuer By** (`veteran_id_issued_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the entity that issued your veteran ID.
* **Veteran ID Replaces** (`veteran_id_replaces`, text, optional): Enter the reason why you replaced your veteran ID.
* **How Connected to Service** (`service_connected_how`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the ways you are connected to the military service.
* **Veteran ID Replaces With** (`veteran_id_replaces_with`, text, optional): Enter the reason why you replaced your veteran ID.
* **Veteran ID Replaces Date** (`veteran_id_replaces_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your veteran ID was replaced.
* **Veteran ID Replaces Reason** (`veteran_id_replaces_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason why you replaced your veteran ID.
* **Veteran ID Active** (`veteran_id_is_active`, select_one, optional): Indicate if your veteran ID is active.
* **Service Connected Date** (`service_connected_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you connected to the military service.
* **Service Connected Years** (`service_connected_years`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you were connected to the military service.
* **Service Connected Months** (`service_connected_months`, number, optional): Enter the number of months you were connected to the military service.
* **Last Four Digits** (`veteran_id_last_four`, text, optional): Enter the last four digits of your veteran ID.
* **Contact Language** (`contact_language`, select_one, optional): Indicate your preferred contact language.
* **Other Service Connected** (`service_connected_other`, text, optional): Enter any other information about your connection to the military service.
