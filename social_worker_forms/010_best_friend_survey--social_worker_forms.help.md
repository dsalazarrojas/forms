# Thinking
This form is designed to help you assess and reflect on your friendships. It will ask you questions about your best friend, covering aspects such as how you met, qualities of your friend, and the impact of your friendship on your life. It will help you think about what makes your friendship special and valuable.
The form has 17 questions, and most of them are required fields. However, fields 2 and 12 are optional, allowing you to choose whether or not to provide an email address or discuss the challenges you've faced in your friendship.
# Best Friend Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help you assess and reflect on your friendships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions about your best friend, such as how you met, their qualities, and the impact of your friendship on your life.
2.  Be honest and sincere in your responses, as this will help you get a better understanding of your friendship.
3.  Most questions are required, but you can choose not to answer field 2 (Email Address) or field 12 (Friendship Challenges).
4.  Once you have answered all the questions, click the submit button to finish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (1, text, required): This is the name to identify you, the respondent.
* **Email Address** (2, email, optional): This is your email address, if you choose to provide it. We may use it to send you the results.
* **Your Best Friend Name** (3, text, required): This is the name of your friend you are surveying.
* **How Long Have You Been Friends** (4, select_one, required): Choose the duration of your friendship from the given options.
* **How Did You Meet** (5, select_one, required): Choose how you met your friend from the given options.
* **Best Qualities of Your Friend** (6, select_multiple, required): Select all the qualities that you admire and appreciate about your friend.
* **Favorite Activity Together** (7, select_one, required): Choose your favorite activity you enjoy doing with your friend.
* **How Often Do You Communicate** (8, select_one, required): Choose your typical communication frequency with your friend.
* **Trust Level** (9, select_one, required): Rate your level of trust towards your friend.
* **Supportive** (10, select_one, required): Rate how supportive your friend is to you.
* **Most Memorable Moment Together** (11, text, required): Share a special moment you've had with your friend.
* **Have You Faced Challenges** (12, text, optional): Describe any difficulties you've faced in your friendship, if any.
* **What Do You Admire Most About Your Friend** (13, text, required): Write what makes your friend special to you.
* **How Has This Friendship Impacted Your Life** (14, text, required): Describe the positive influence of your friendship on your life.
* **What Do You Look Forward To With Your Friend** (15, text, optional): Write about future activities or goals you plan with your friend.
* **Message To Your Friend** (16, text, optional): Write a message for your friend, if you wish.
* **Friendship Strength** (17, select_one, required): Rate the overall strength of your friendship from the given options.
