# Veterans Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from veterans for the purpose of registration and record-keeping. It collects personal details, military affiliation, and contact information to ensure accurate and up-to-date records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your First Name (Field 1).
* Enter your Middle Initial, if applicable (Field 2).
* Provide your Last Name (Field 3).
* Enter your Date of Birth (Field 4).
* Select your Branch of Service (Field 5).
* Enter your Social Security Number (Field 6).
* Enter your Date of Discharge, if applicable (Field 7).
* Provide your Email (Field 8).
* Enter your Phone Number (Field 9).
* Enter your Address (Field 10).
* Enter your VA ID Number, if applicable (Field 11).
* Enter your Contact Phone Number (Field 12).
* Select your Veteran Status (Field 13).
* Select your Service Connected Status (Field 14).
* Provide any additional notes regarding your Veteran Status (Field 15).
* Enter any additional notes regarding your Service Connected Status (Field 16).
* Enter the name of your Emergency Contact (Field 17).
* Enter the phone number of your Emergency Contact (Field 18).
* Specify your relationship with your Emergency Contact (Field 19).
* Enter the address of your Emergency Contact (Field 20).
* Enter the email of your Emergency Contact (Field 21).
* Enter any updates regarding your Veteran Status (Field 22).
* Enter any updates regarding your Service Connected Status (Field 23).
* Finally, please sign and date this form (Field 24).
* Add any additional notes (Field 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Field 1)** (`text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Middle Initial (Field 2)** (`number`, optional): Enter your middle initial, if applicable.
* **Last Name (Field 3)** (`text`, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth (Field 4)** (`date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Branch of Service (Field 5)** (`select_one`, optional): Select your branch of service from the options provided.
* **Social Security Number (Field 6)** (`text`, optional): Enter your social security number.
* **Date of Discharge (Field 7)** (`date`, optional): Enter your date of discharge, if applicable.
* **Email (Field 8)** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (Field 9)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address (Field 10)** (`text`, optional): Enter your address.
* **VA ID Number (Field 11)** (`text`, optional): Enter your VA ID number, if applicable.
* **Contact Phone (Field 12)** (`text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Veteran Status (Field 13)** (`select_one`, required): Select your veteran status from the options provided.
* **Service Connected Status (Field 14)** (`select_one`, optional): Select your service connected status from the options provided.
* **Veteran Status Note (Field 15)** (`note`, optional): Provide any additional notes regarding your veteran status.
* **Service Connected Status Note (Field 16)** (`note`, optional): Provide any additional notes regarding your service connected status.
* **Emergency Contact Name (Field 17)** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Field 18)** (`text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (Field 19)** (`text`, optional): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address (Field 20)** (`text`, optional): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email (Field 21)** (`email`, optional): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Veteran Status Update (Field 22)** (`text`, optional): Enter any updates regarding your veteran status.
* **Service Connected Status Update (Field 23)** (`text`, optional): Enter any updates regarding your service connected status.
* **Signature (Field 24)** (`date`, optional): Please sign and date this form.
* **Notes (Field 25)** (`note`, optional): Provide any additional notes.
