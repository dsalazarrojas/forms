# Personal And Family Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and family information from users in a social setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Click on the "Person Information" page to provide personal details.
- Click on the "Family Information" page to provide family details.
- Click on the "Contact Information" page to provide contact details.
- Click on the "Estate Information", "Assets", "Legal Documents", "Account Information", and "Tax Information" pages to provide other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Person Information (page 1)
* **Person Information** (`person_info`, `text`, required: false): This is where you will enter the person's basic information.
 
* **Family Information** (`family_info`, `text`, required: false): This is where you will enter information about your family members.
 
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): This is where you will enter the person's contact details.
 
### Family Information
* **Family Information** (`family_info`, `text`, required: false): This is where you will enter information about your family members.
 
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): This is where you will enter the person's contact details.
 
### Estate Information
* **Estate Information** (`estate_info`, `text`, required: false): This is where you will enter information about the person's estate.
 
### Assets
* **Assets** (`assets`, `text`, required: false): This is where you will enter information about your assets.
 
### Legal Documents
* **Legal Documents** (`legal_documents`, `text`, required: false): This is where you will enter information about legal documents.
 
### Account Information
* **Account Information** (`account_info`, `text`, required: false): This is where you will enter information about your account.
 
### Tax Information
* **Tax Information** (`tax_info`, `text`, required: false): This is where you will enter information about tax details.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to ask for help.
