# Stay At Home Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stay At Home Survey is a questionnaire designed to gather information about the experiences and challenges faced by stay-at-home workers, including stay-at-home mothers and fathers. Its purpose is to help businesses understand their needs and improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
2.  Choose all relevant options for the question "stay_at_home_survey_questions".
3.  Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "stay_at_home_survey_comments" field.
4.  Select the category or industry that best describes your work from the "category" field.
5.  Enter a brief description of your work in the "description" field.
6.  Optionally, select the tool assigned to you from the "assigned_tool" field.
7.  Finally, provide your preferred file format for the output in the "output_file" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stay At Home Survey** (`stay_at_home_survey`, text, optional): A field where you can enter your answer to the survey question.
* **Stay At Home Survey Questions** (`stay_at_home_survey_questions`, select multiple, optional): Choose all that apply from the list of options.
	+ Stay at home survey is a questionnaire used by businesses to find out how stay-at-home mothers and fathers are enjoying their lifestyles.
	+ No coding features!
* **Stay At Home Survey Comments** (`stay_at_home_survey_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the survey.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Select the category or industry that best describes your work.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your work.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select one, optional): Select the tool assigned to you from the list of options.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Choose your preferred file format for the output.
