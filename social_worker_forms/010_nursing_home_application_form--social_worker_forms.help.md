# Nursing Home Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nursing Home Application Form is a tool used to collect personal and medical information from individuals applying for admission to a nursing home. It is crucial to fill it out accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **User Info (Page 1)**: Fill in your personal details to help our team get to know you better.
* <strong>**Name:**</strong> Please provide your full name as per your government documents.
* <strong>**Date of Birth:**</strong> Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (dd/mm/yy).
2. **Contact Details (Page 2)**: Share your contact information so we can reach out to you.
* <strong>**Address:**</strong> Enter your home or mailing address.
* <strong>**Phone Number:**</strong> Provide your contact phone number.
3. **Medical Details (Page 3)**: If you have any medical conditions, please disclose them accurately.
* <strong>**Do you hold a medical certificate?**</strong> Select <strong>Yes</strong> or <strong>No</strong>.
* <strong>**Type of medical certificate**</strong> (if you answered <strong>Yes</strong> above): Please select all that apply.
* <strong>**Medical Certificate Issue Date:**</strong> Enter the date when the medical certificate was issued (if applicable).
* <strong>**Medical Certificate Issuer:**</strong> Enter the name of the medical certificate issuer (if applicable).
4. **Nursing Home Details (Page 4)**: Share any information about your preferred nursing home.
* <strong>**Nursing Home Details:**</strong> Describe your desired level of care or any specific needs you might have.
* <strong>**Nursing Home Preferences:**</strong> Share any other preferences or requirements you may have for your stay in the nursing home.
5. **Family Info (Page 5)**: If applicable, provide information about your family members or emergency contacts.
* <strong>**Family Info:**</strong> Describe your family situation, if any.
6. <strong>Submit the Form</strong>: Once you have completed all pages, submit the form for further processing.
