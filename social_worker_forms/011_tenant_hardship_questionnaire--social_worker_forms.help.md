# Tenant Hardship Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Hardship Questionnaire is a form designed to gather information from tenants who are experiencing financial or other types of hardships. This form will help us understand the nature of the hardship and provide targeted support and resources to those in need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide detailed answers to the questions.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. If you are unsure about any question or section, please ask a representative for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Loss** (`Job Loss`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about the reason for job loss. Please select the correct option from the list provided.
* **Financial Status** (`Financial Status`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about your current financial status. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Income Loss** (`Income Loss`, `number`, optional): If you have experienced income loss, please enter the total amount.
* **Business Hardship** (`Business Hardship`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about any business hardship you are experiencing. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Financial Support** (`Financial Support`, `text`, optional): If you are seeking financial support, please describe what kind of support you need.
* **Business Financial Support** (`Business Financial Support`, `text`, optional): This question is similar to the previous one, but focuses on business financial support.
* **Business Financial Support Details** (`Business Financial Support Details`, `text`, optional): Please specify what kind of business financial support you are seeking.
* **Business Financial Support Amount** (`Business Financial Support Amount`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the total amount of business financial support you are seeking.
* **Tenant Financial Support** (`Tenant Financial Support`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about the type of financial support you are seeking as a tenant. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Tenant Financial Support Details** (`Tenant Financial Support Details`, `text`, optional): Please describe what kind of financial support you are seeking as a tenant.
* **Tenant Financial Support Amount** (`Tenant Financial Support Amount`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the total amount of financial support you are seeking as a tenant.
* **Have you filed taxes?** (`Have you filed taxes?`, `select_one`, required): Please answer this question truthfully to indicate whether you have filed taxes or not.
* **Financial Assistance** (`Financial Assistance`, `text`, optional): If you are seeking financial assistance, please describe your situation.
* **Financial Assistance Status** (`Financial Assistance Status`, `select_one`, required): Please indicate the status of your financial assistance application.
* **Financial Assistance Details** (`Financial Assistance Details`, `text`, optional): Please describe the status of your financial assistance application.
* **Other Financial Assistance Details** (`Other Financial Assistance Details`, `text`, optional): This question is similar to the previous one, but focuses on other financial assistance.
* **Other Financial Assistance Status** (`Other Financial Assistance Status`, `select_one`, optional): This question is similar to the previous one, but asks about the status of other financial assistance.
* **Other Hardship Details** (`Other Hardship Details`, `text`, optional): Please describe any other hardships you are experiencing.
* **Other Financial Support Details** (`Other Financial Support Details`, `text`, optional): Please specify what kind of financial support you are seeking as an alternative.
* **Other Financial Support Amount** (`Other Financial Support Amount`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the total amount of financial support you are seeking as an alternative.
* **Tenant Hardship Details** (`Tenant Hardship Details`, `text`, optional): Please provide a detailed description of the hardship you are experiencing as a tenant.
* **Tenant Hardship Solutions** (`Tenant Hardship Solutions`, `text`, optional): Please specify the steps you are taking to address the hardship as a tenant.
* **Tenant Hardship Solutions Details** (`Tenant Hardship Solutions Details`, `text`, optional): This question is similar to the previous one, but asks for more details about the solutions.

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any question or section, please ask a representative for assistance.
* This form is for internal use only and will not be shared with anyone without your consent.
