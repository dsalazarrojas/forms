# Social Worker Home Visit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Worker Home Visit Checklist is a tool used to gather information about a client's home environment, risk level, and potential areas of concern during a social worker's visit. This form helps social workers to identify potential risks and provide recommendations for support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the introduction page, which provides a brief overview of the form's purpose and instructions.
2. On each page, read the label next to each field to understand what information is being requested.
3. Complete each field with the relevant information about the client.
4. Ensure all required fields are completed before moving on to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page provides a brief overview of the form's purpose and instructions. Read this page carefully before starting the form.
* **Risk Assessment**: This field asks you to assess the risk level of the client. Select 'Yes' if you identify any risks, 'No' if you do not.
* **Client Information**: This field is for recording any additional information about the client.
* **Home Environment**: This field is for describing the client's home environment. Please provide details about the client's living situation and any concerns you have.
* **Client Status**: This field is for recording any updates on the client's status, such as their current condition or recent events.
* **Safety Risks**: This field is for identifying any safety risks you've observed during the visit.
* **Risk Factors**: This field is for recording any risk factors you've identified during the visit.
* **Recommendations**: This field is for providing any recommendations you'd like to make for the client's support.

## Tips
* Be sure to complete all required fields, marked as 'Required' in the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or colleagues for guidance.
* This form is for internal use only, and any information shared will be kept confidential.
