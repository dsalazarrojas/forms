# Case Closure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Closure Form is used to document the closure of a case, ensuring that all necessary information is recorded and that the case is properly closed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the case details, including case number, case name or title, and date opened.
2. Select the correct reason for closure from the options provided.
3. Enter the assigned worker's name, ID number, and department.
4. Choose the overall case outcome from the options provided.
5. Select all services provided from the options.
6. Rate client satisfaction with services provided.
7. If applicable, indicate follow-up care is needed.
8. List any referrals made upon closure.
9. Enter final case notes.
10. Enter the date of case closure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Case Number (1)**: Unique identification number for the case.
* **Case Name or Title (2)**: Brief description of the case.
* **Date Case Was Opened (3)**: Date the case was opened.
* **Date Case is Being Closed (4)**: Date the case is being closed.
* **Case Duration (5)**: The total duration of the case, calculated from open to close date.
* **Assigned Social Worker or Case Manager Name (6)**: Name of the assigned social worker or case manager.
* **Worker ID Number (7)**: Identification number of the assigned social worker or case manager.
* **Department (8)**: Department the assigned social worker or case manager works for.
* **Supervising Manager Name (9)**: Name of the supervising manager.
* **Primary Reason for Case Closure (10)**: Select a reason for case closure from the options provided.
* **Detailed Explanation of Closure (11)**: Provide a detailed explanation for the closure reason selected.
* **Overall Case Outcome (12)**: Choose the overall outcome of the case from the options provided.
* **Goals Achieved (13)**: Brief description of what was accomplished.
* **Services Provided (14)**: Select all services provided from the options provided.
* **Client Satisfaction (15)**: Rate client satisfaction with services provided.
* **Is Follow-up Care Recommended? (16)**: Determine if follow-up care is needed.
* **Agencies or Services Referred To (17)**: List any agencies or services referred to upon closure.
* **Final Case Notes (18)**: Enter important information for the file.
* **Date of Case Closure (19)**: Date the case is being closed.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submission.
* Ensure all required fields are completed.
* If unsure, contact your supervisor for guidance.
