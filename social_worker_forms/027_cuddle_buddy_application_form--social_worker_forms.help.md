# Cuddle Buddy Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cuddle Buddy Application Form is designed to help social workers find and connect with potential Cuddle Buddies. The form collects necessary information about the applicant, their cuddle request, contact information, and review form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Information page with your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
2. On the Cuddle Request page, describe the specific support you are looking for from a Cuddle Buddy. Be as detailed as possible.
3. If you have any contact information (e.g., phone number, social media handles), you can fill it out on the Contact Information page. However, this is optional.
4. On the Cuddle Buddy Info page, provide information about what you are looking for in a Cuddle Buddy, such as specific skills or interests.
5. Finally, fill out the Review Form page with any additional comments or feedback about your experience with Cuddle Buddies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **User Name** (text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be known by the Cuddle Buddy system.
* **User Email** (text, required): Enter your email address for communication and verification purposes.
### Cuddle Request
* **Cuddle Request** (text, required): Describe the specific support you are looking for from a Cuddle Buddy. Be as detailed as possible.
### Contact Information
* **Contact Info** (text, optional): If you have a phone number, social media handle, or other contact information, enter it here.
### Cuddle Buddy Info
* **Cuddle Buddy Info** (text, required): Specify what you are looking for in a Cuddle Buddy, such as specific skills or interests.
### Review Form
* **Review Form** (text, optional): Write any comments or feedback about your experience with Cuddle Buddies.

## Tips
* Be honest and clear in your cuddle request and cuddle buddy info.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to ask your social worker for guidance.
* Once you submit the form, a social worker will review it and connect you with a Cuddle Buddy.
