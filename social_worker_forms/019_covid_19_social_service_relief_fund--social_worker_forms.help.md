# COVID 19 Social Service Relief Fund - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Social Service Relief Fund is designed to provide financial assistance to individuals or families who have been impacted by the COVID-19 pandemic. This form helps social workers assess your situation and allocate resources to support your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what is required.
2. Fill out the form in a quiet and private area, free from distractions.
3. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
4. If you need help or clarification on any field, ask your social worker for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date**: `application_date` (Date of application, required): The date you are submitting this application. Enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **First Name**: `applicant_first_name` (Text, required): Your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: `applicant_last_name` (Text, required): Your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth**: `date_of_birth` (Date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number**: `phone_number` (Text, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Email Address**: `email_address` (Email, optional): Your contact email. If you have an email address, please enter it here.
* **Home Address**: `home_address` (Text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City**: `city` (Text, required): Your city of residence.
* **State**: `state` (Text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP Code**: `zip_code` (Text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Total Household Size**: `household_size` (Number, required): The number of people in your household.
* **Number of Children Under 18**: `children_count` (Number, required): The number of children under 18 living in your household.
* **Total Monthly Household Income**: `household_income` (Number, required): Your household income before taxes.
* **Primary Source of Income**: `income_source` (Select one, required): Select your primary source of income.
* **How has COVID-19 impacted your finances?**: `covid_financial_impact` (Select multiple, required): Select all that apply to describe the impact of COVID-19 on your finances.
* **What type of assistance do you need?**: `assistance_type_needed` (Select multiple, required): Select all that apply to describe the type of assistance you require.
* **Amount of Assistance Requested**: `assistance_amount_requested` (Number, required): The amount of financial assistance you are requesting. Enter it in USD.
* **Current Housing Status**: `housing_status` (Select one, required): Describe your current housing situation.
* **Monthly Rent or Mortgage Payment**: `rent_mortgage_amount` (Number, optional): If you rent or own a home, please enter your monthly rent or mortgage payment.
* **Are you behind on rent or mortgage payments?**: `housing_arrears` (Select one, required): Describe your current rent or mortgage payment status.
* **Are you behind on utility payments?**: `utility_arrears` (Select one, required): Describe your current utility payment status.
* **Food Security**: `food_security` (Select one, required): Describe your food security status in the past month.
* **Do you currently receive any public assistance?**: `public_assistance` (Select multiple, required): Check all public assistance programs you receive.
* **Have you applied for other relief assistance?**: `other_assistance_applied` (Select one, required): Describe your application status for other relief assistance.
* **Personal Statement**: `personal_statement` (Text, required): Provide a detailed explanation of your situation and why you need assistance.
* **How did you hear about this relief fund?**: `referral_source` (Select one, required): Describe how you learned about this relief fund.
* **Case Worker or Social Worker Name**: `case_worker_name` (Text, optional): If you have a case worker or social worker, enter their name here.
* **Case Worker Contact Information**: `case_worker_contact` (Text, optional): Enter your case worker's contact information here.
* **Applicant Signature Date**: `applicant_signature_date` (Date, required): Date you signed this form.
* **I declare that the information provided is true and accurate**: `truthfulness_statement` (Select one, required): Verify the truthfulness of the information you have provided.

## Tips
- Answer each question carefully and honestly.
- If you need help or clarification, don't hesitate to ask your social worker.
- Ensure you have completed the form accurately to avoid delays in receiving assistance.

This guide is designed to help you complete the "COVID-19 Social Service Relief Fund" form accurately and effectively. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your social worker for assistance.
