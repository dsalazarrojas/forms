# ndia_information_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NDIA Information Consent Form is designed to gather essential information from clients, ensuring that they understand and agree to the terms of their engagement with NDIA.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and the information required.
2.  Select the relevant options for each field as required.
3.  Enter any relevant details in the text fields as needed.
4.  Sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the relevant client details that apply to you.
	+ Select the client's name, date of birth, address, email, and phone number if applicable.
* **Contact Details** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the contact details that apply to you.
	+ Select home address, mobile number, email address, or phone number as needed.
* **Emergency Contact Details** (Select Multiple, Required): Provide the contact details of your emergency contact.
	+ Select the name, relationship, phone number, or address of your emergency contact.
* **Purpose of Consultation** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the purpose of your consultation.
	+ Choose between General Assessment, Review of NDIS plan, or Development of NDIS plan as required.
* **NDIA Services Provided** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the services provided by NDIA.
	+ Choose between NDIS plan management, Plan review and updates, or Funding management as required.
* **Communication Preferences** (Select Multiple, Required): Select your preferred communication method.
	+ Choose between Email, Phone, SMS, or In-person as needed.
* **Consent** (Date, Required): Date and sign to confirm your consent.
* **Signature** (Time, Required): Sign to confirm your signature.
* **Signature Note** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes as needed.

## Tips
* Please ensure you understand the purpose and the information required for each field before completing the form.
* Review the form carefully and select the relevant options for each field.
* Enter any relevant details in the text fields as needed.
* Sign the form to confirm your consent and confirm your signature.
