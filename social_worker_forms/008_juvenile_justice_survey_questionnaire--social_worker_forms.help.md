# juvenile_justice_survey_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The juvenile justice survey questionnaire is a tool to collect information from social workers and professionals in the juvenile justice system. It helps us understand their experiences, opinions, and perceptions about juvenile justice.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete each page of the form carefully and honestly.
- For questions that require an answer, enter your response in the text field.
- For selection questions, choose one or more options that best represent your answer.
- For date and time questions, use the standard date and time formats (MM/DD/YYYY and HH:MM AM/PM).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief note for context and information about the form.
- **What Is Juvenile Justice** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of what you understand by "juvenile justice."
- **Who Is Involved** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the individuals or groups that are involved in juvenile justice in your area.
  - Option 1: Juvenile courts
  - Option 2: Social workers
  - Option 3: Law enforcement
  - Option 4: Other (please specify)
- **What Does Juvenile Justice Do** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following answers:
  - Option 1: Provide support and services for juvenile offenders
  - Option 2: Monitor and track juvenile offenders
- **Additional Comments** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any additional comments or information you think might be helpful to us.
- **Date** (`6`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you are completing this form (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Time** (`7`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you are completing this form (HH:MM AM/PM).
- **Name** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
- **Email** (`9`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thoughtful when answering the questions.
- If you have any doubts or concerns about the form, please let us know.
- We value your input and appreciate your participation in this survey.
