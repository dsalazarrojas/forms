# Community Services Intake Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about you and your household in order to provide you with the best possible support and services. Please fill out this form as accurately and completely as possible to help our staff understand your needs and provide the assistance you require.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section of the form as thoroughly as possible.
2. Make sure to answer all required questions (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. If you need help or clarification on any question, please don't hesitate to reach out to our staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, `note`, required: false): This is a note field to provide any additional information about your personal details.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your last name.
* **Middle Name** (`middle_name`, `text`, required: false): This is an optional field for your middle name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: true): Please enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select your gender.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required: true): Enter your best contact number.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required: true): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your state.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required: true): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Last 4 Digits of SSN** (`ssn`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, please enter the last 4 digits of your SSN.
* **Household Information** (`household_info`, `note`, required: false): This is a note field to provide any additional information about your household.
* **Total Household Members** (`household_size`, `number`, required: true): Please enter the number of people living in your household.
* **Number of Dependents** (`dependents_count`, `number`, required: false): If you have dependents, please enter their number.
* **Names and Ages of Household Members** (`household_members`, `text`, required: false): List the names and ages of all household members.
* **Housing Information** (`housing_info`, `note`, required: false): This is a note field to provide any additional information about your living situation.
* **Current Housing Status** (`housing_status`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select your current housing status.
* **Monthly Rent or Mortgage** (`monthly_rent`, `number`, required: false): If applicable, please enter your monthly rent or mortgage.
* **Risk of Eviction** (`eviction_risk`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select if you are at risk of eviction.
* **Income Information** (`income_info`, `note`, required: false): This is a note field to provide any additional information about your household income.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select your employment status.
* **Total Monthly Household Income** (`monthly_income`, `number`, required: true): Please enter your total household income.
* **Income Sources** (`income_sources`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Please select all applicable income sources.
* **Monthly Expenses** (`expenses`, `note`, required: false): This is a note field to provide any financial obligations.
* **Monthly Utility Costs** (`monthly_utilities`, `number`, required: false): If applicable, please enter your monthly utility costs.
* **Monthly Food Costs** (`monthly_food`, `number`, required: false): If applicable, please enter your monthly food costs.
* **Monthly Medical Costs** (`monthly_medical`, `number`, required: false): If applicable, please enter your monthly medical costs.
* **Monthly Debt Payments** (`monthly_debt`, `number`, required: false): If applicable, please enter your monthly debt payments.
* **Services Needed** (`services_needed`, `note`, required: false): This is a note field to provide any additional information about the services you need.
* **Types of Assistance Needed** (`service_types`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Please select all the services you need.
* **Describe Your Needs** (`service_description`, `text`, required: true): Please describe your specific needs.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select the urgency level of your needs.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select how you heard about us.
* **Have You Received Services from Us Before** (`previous_services`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select if you have received services from us before.
* **Consent and Authorization** (`consent`, `note`, required: false): This is a note field to provide any important agreements.
* **Consent to Receive Services** (`consent_services`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select if you consent to receive services.
* **Consent to Verification of Information** (`consent_verification`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select if you consent to verification of your information.
* **Certify This Information Is True** (`accuracy_statement`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select if this information is true.
* **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, `text`, required: true): Please type your full name.
* **Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required: true): Please enter the date.

## Tips

* Please be as thorough and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you need help or clarification on any question, please don't hesitate to reach out to our staff.
* We value your privacy and confidentiality. Please only enter information that you are comfortable sharing with us.
* This form is intended to be completed by the applicant. If you are assisting someone, please do not fill out this form on their behalf.
