# Petition To Support Seniors Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from individuals who wish to support seniors in their community, helping to identify potential supporters and gauge their level of interest in assisting seniors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing the requested information.
2. For fields with options, select the relevant answer from the provided choices.
3. Enter your contact information to allow potential supporters to reach out to you.
4. Provide a message if you have any additional information to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Petition To Support Seniors** (`petition_to_support_seniors_form`, select_one, required: false): This field asks if you would like to support seniors. Please select 'Yes' if you are interested in supporting seniors.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks about your contact information. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Please enter your address so that potential supporters can reach out to you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so that potential supporters can contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that potential supporters can reach out to you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Time Available** (`time_available`, time, required: false): Enter a time slot when you are available to support seniors.
* **Message** (`message`, note, required: false): This field is for any additional information you would like to share.
* **Support Seniors** (`support_seniors`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are willing to support seniors.
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the ways you are willing to collaborate with others.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* Make sure to enter correct information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Your responses will be used to identify potential supporters and gauge their interest in assisting seniors.
* Completing this form does not commit you to any specific action, it only expresses your willingness to support seniors.
