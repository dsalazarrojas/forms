## Hospice Notice Of Election Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospice Notice of Election Form is used to document the election of a physician or other caregiver to provide hospice care to a patient. This form should only be completed for patients who are electing to receive hospice care.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully to understand the requirements and purpose of each field.
2.  Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
3.  Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.
4.  Enter the patient's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5.  Enter the patient's contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
6.  Enter the physician's name in the "Physician Name" field.
7.  Enter the physician's address in the "Physician Address" field.
8.  Document the patient's care wishes in the "Care Wishes" field.
9.  Enter the hospice ID in the "Hospice ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name who is electing to receive hospice care.
*   **Patient Address** (`Patient Address`, text, required): Enter the patient's address who is electing to receive hospice care.
*   **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required): Enter the patient's contact number who is electing to receive hospice care.
*   **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, required): Enter the patient's contact email who is electing to receive hospice care.
*   **Physician Name** (`Physician Name`, text, required): Enter the physician's name who will be providing care to the patient.
*   **Physician Address** (`Physician Address`, text, required): Enter the physician's address who will be providing care to the patient.
*   **Care Wishes** (`Care Wishes`, note, optional): Document any specific care wishes or preferences of the patient.
*   **Hospice ID** (`Hospice ID`, text, optional): Enter the hospice ID number for the patient.

## Tips
*   Make sure to review the form carefully to understand the requirements and purpose of each field.
*   Ensure that the patient has been properly informed and understands the election process before completing this form.
*   Double-check for accuracy and completeness in each field, especially for contact information and physician details.
*   Keep the "Care Wishes" field concise and focused on the patient's specific preferences.
*   Enter the "Hospice ID" correctly for accurate tracking and record-keeping.
*   Use a clear and legible handwriting for all fields, as this form will be used as a reference document.
*   Complete the form as soon as possible after the patient has elected to receive hospice care, as delay may cause confusion or miscommunication.
