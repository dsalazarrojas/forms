# Gender Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's gender identity, expression, and role. This information will help us better understand the diversity of our community and ensure that our services are inclusive and supportive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and each question.
2. Answer each question truthfully, as all answers are optional.
3. Make sure to select all applicable options for each question.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select gender identity** (`select_gender_identity`, Optional):
	+ This question asks you to select your gender identity. Please choose one or more of the following options:
		- Yes: You identify as male.
		- No: You do not identify as male.
* **Select gender expression** (`select_gender_expression`, Optional):
	+ This question asks you to select whether your gender expression aligns with your gender identity. Please choose one or more of the following options:
		- Yes: Your gender expression aligns with your gender identity.
		- No: Your gender expression does not align with your gender identity.
* **Select gender role** (`select_gender_role`, Optional):
	+ This question asks you to select whether you identify with specific gender roles or stereotypes. Please choose one or more of the following options:
		- Yes: You identify with one or more specific gender roles or stereotypes.
		- No: You do not identify with specific gender roles or stereotypes.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and truthfully, as all answers are optional.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please ask your supervisor or the help desk for assistance.
* This form is designed to be used in a non-judgmental and supportive environment, and all information collected will be treated with respect and care.
