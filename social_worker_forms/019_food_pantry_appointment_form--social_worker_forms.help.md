# Food Pantry Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Pantry Appointment Form is designed for scheduling appointments with clients for the Food Pantry program. This form captures essential details for smooth communication and appointment management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the client's name, contact information, and appointment specifics.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Name** (`name`, text, optional)
Enter the client's name as you would like it to appear on the appointment schedule.
### 2. **Date** (`date`, date, optional)
Select the date of the desired appointment from the calendar.
### 3. **Time** (`time`, time, optional)
Select the time of the desired appointment.
### 4. **Location** (`location`, text, optional)
Choose the location for the appointment, such as the Food Pantry's main office or a specific community location.
### 5. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
Enter the client's phone number for communication purposes.
### 6. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
Enter the client's email address for communication and appointment reminders.
### 7. **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional)
Enter the client's name for the appointment record.
### 8. **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional)
Enter the client's phone number for appointment purposes.
### 9. **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional)
Enter the client's email address for appointment reminders and communication.
