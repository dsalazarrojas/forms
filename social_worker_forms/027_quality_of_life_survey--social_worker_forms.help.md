# Quality Of Life Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Of Life Survey is designed to collect information about your general well-being, health, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
- You can complete the form on any device with a web browser, including desktop computers, smartphones, or tablets.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields, marked as "required".
- You can submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`General Info`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect any additional information you'd like to share about your situation.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your current address to help us get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for us to contact you in case of any urgent matters.
* **Email** (`Email`, `text`, required: false): Provide your email address to receive any updates or information from us.
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name to help us know you better.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name to help us know you better.
* **Health Info** (`Health Info`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your current health situation in brief.
* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, `text`, required: false): If you have any medical conditions, please provide brief details about them.
* **Health Insurance** (`Health Insurance`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, mention any health insurance you have.
* **Meds** (`Meds`, `text`, required: false): List any ongoing medications or treatments if relevant to your health situation.
