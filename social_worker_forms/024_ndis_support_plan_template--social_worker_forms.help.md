# NDIS Support Plan Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NDIS Support Plan Template is a document used to collect and document important information about an individual's support needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by providing your personal details, including your name, date of birth, and other relevant information.
- Next, provide contact details, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
- Select all goals that apply to you, as they relate to your support needs.
- Indicate any supports you currently need, such as assistance with daily living, transportation, or personal care.
- Provide notes on your progress, including any successes, challenges, and changes.
- Outline next steps you will take to achieve your goals.
- Finally, provide any comments or feedback for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Details
- **Title:** (`personal_details`, `text`, **required:** false): This field is used to enter your personal details.
### Contact Details
- **Contact Details:** (`contact_details`, `text`, **required:** false): This field is used to enter your contact information.
### Goals
- **Goals:** (`goals`, `select_one`, **required:** false): This field is used to select all goals that apply to you.
  - Select all goals that apply to you, such as 'Independence', 'Community Participation', 'Employment' or others.
### Supports Needed
- **Supports Needed:** (`supports_needed`, `select_multiple`, **required:** false): This field is used to select any supports you currently need.
  - Select all supports you currently need, such as 'Daily Living' 'Transportation', 'Personal Care' or others.
### Progress
- **Progress:** (`progress`, `note`, **required:** false): This field is used to provide notes on your progress.
### Next Steps
- **Next Steps:** (`next_steps`, `text`, **required:** false): This field is used to outline next steps you will take to achieve your goals.
### Review
- **Review:** (`review`, `text`, **required:** false): This field is used to provide any comments or feedback.
### Review Comments
- **Review Comments:** (`review_comments`, `text`, **required:** false): This field is used to enter any comments or feedback.
