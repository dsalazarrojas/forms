# rescue_home_visit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "rescue_home_visit_form" is designed to provide a standardized format for documenting rescue missions where services were called in response to a person in need. It is used by social workers to report on the status of rescue services and gather information for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Person in need location" field to input the location where the person in need was found or reported.
2. In the "Rescue services responding" field, select "Yes" if rescue services were sent to the location, and "No" if they were not.
3. If rescue services were sent, select "Yes" in the "Rescue services arrived" field, and "No" otherwise.
4. The "Notes" field is an open-ended text area where you can provide any additional comments or information related to the situation.
5. In the "Contact person in need" field, you can input any relevant contact information for the person in need, such as their name or phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Person in need location**: Enter the physical address or location where the person in need was found or reported. This helps us track the location of the incident and allocate resources efficiently.
* **Rescue services responding**: Select this field to indicate if rescue services were sent to the location. This is a key field in determining the response to the incident.
* **Rescue services arrived**: Select this field to indicate if rescue services actually arrived at the location. This helps us determine if the services were effective in responding to the situation.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information related to the situation, such as the condition of the person in need or any specific challenges encountered.
* **Contact person in need**: Enter any contact information for the person in need, such as their name or phone number. This helps us keep records of communication and follow-up actions.
* **Contact person in need**: Note that this field is optional, but it's a good idea to include it to ensure we can reach out to the person in need if needed.
 
## Tips
* Always ensure the location of the person in need is accurate to allocate resources effectively.
* If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask for clarification or guidance.
* This form is meant to be a quick reference guide, so fill it out as accurately and efficiently as possible.
