# NDIS Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NDIS Access Request Form is used by social workers to request access to NDIS services on behalf of their clients. It is designed to be a simple and straightforward way to collect necessary information about the client and their service needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user details, including your name, email address, and any other relevant information.
2. Enter your contact details, such as your phone number and address.
3. Describe the service you are seeking access to and any relevant details about the service.
4. Provide information about the service provider and delivery details.
5. If you have any additional comments or notes, please include them in the "Client Comments" field.
6. Finally, provide details about the social worker who will be delivering the service, including their name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (Text, Optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Details** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number and address for contact purposes.
* **Service Details** (Text, Optional): Describe the service you are seeking access to and any relevant details about the service.
* **Service Delivery** (Text, Optional): Provide information about how the service will be delivered, including any relevant dates or times.
* **Service Provider** (Text, Optional): Enter the name and contact information of the service provider.
* **Client Comments** (Note, Optional): If you have any additional comments or notes, please include them here.
* **Social Worker Details** (Text, Optional): Enter the name and contact information of the social worker who will be delivering the service.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authority for clarification.
* This form is intended for social workers, please do not attempt to use it for any other purpose.
