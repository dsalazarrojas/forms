# Social Worker Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Worker Intake Form is a tool used to gather essential information about a new client's background, medical history, and support systems. This form helps social workers to provide personalized support services, identify potential areas of concern, and develop a comprehensive understanding of the client's needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Please ensure that you have all necessary information before starting the form.
3. Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your social worker for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Client** (1)
	+ A brief description of the client's reason for seeking services.
* **New Client Info** (2)
	+ A brief description of the client's background information.
* **Contact Info** (3)
	+ The client's contact information, including their name, phone number, and email.
* **Address** (4)
	+ The client's current address.
* **Medical History** (5)
	+ A brief description of the client's medical history, including any ongoing medical conditions or health concerns.
* **Medications** (6)
	+ A list of any medications the client is currently taking.
* **Allergies** (7)
	+ A list of any allergies or sensitivities the client has.
* **Emergency Contact** (8)
	+ The name and contact information of the client's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (9)
	+ Additional information about the client's emergency contact.
* **Family Info** (10)
	+ A brief description of the client's family dynamics and relationships.
* **Social Support** (11)
	+ A description of the client's social support system, including friends and family.
* **Insurance Info** (12)
	+ Information about the client's insurance coverage.
* **Insurance Type** (13)
	+ The type of insurance the client has (e.g., medical, non-medical).
* **Insurance Number** (14)
	+ The client's insurance number.
* **Medical Provider** (15)
	+ The name and contact information of the client's medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Relationship** (16)
	+ The nature of the client's relationship with their medical provider.
* **Medical Notes** (17)
	+ Any additional medical notes or comments.
* **Mental Health History** (18)
	+ A brief description of the client's mental health history.
* **Mental Health History Info** (19)
	+ Additional information about the client's mental health history.
* **Substance Abuse History** (20)
	+ A brief description of the client's substance abuse history.
* **Substance Abuse History Info** (21)
	+ Additional information about the client's substance abuse history.
* **Legal History** (22)
	+ A brief description of the client's legal history.
* **Legal History Info** (23)
	+ Additional information about the client's legal history.
* **Employment Info** (24)
	+ Information about the client's employment status.
* **Employment Notes** (25)
	+ Any additional employment notes or comments.

## Tips
* Please keep the form concise and focused on essential information.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your social worker for clarification.
* The information collected on this form will be used to inform the development of a comprehensive support plan.
* Please ensure that the information provided is up-to-date and accurate.
