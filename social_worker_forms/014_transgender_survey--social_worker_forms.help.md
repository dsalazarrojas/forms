# Transgender Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transgender Survey is a form used to collect demographic and medical information from transgender individuals. This form is designed to ensure that healthcare providers have the necessary information to provide adequate care and support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **first_name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **last_name** field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the **date_of_birth** field.
4. Select your gender identity from the **gender_id** field.
5. Provide the date of your transgender history in the **date_of_trans_history** field.
6. Select your current gender identity from the **gender_identity** field.
7. If applicable, provide any relevant medical conditions in the **medical_condition** field.
8. Describe any relevant medical history in the **medical_history** field.
9. If applicable, select your mental health status from the **mental_health** field.
10. Provide the name of your medical provider in the **medical_provider** field.
11. Provide the name of your medical insurance provider in the **medical_insurance_provider** field.
12. Select your medical insurance type from the **medical_insurance** field.
13. Select your medical insurance status from the **medical_insurance_status** field.
14. If applicable, provide your medical insurance group number in the **medical_insurance_group** field.
15. If applicable, provide your medical insurance group ID in the **medical_insurance_group_id** field.
16. Provide the name of the emergency contact person in the **emergency_contact_name** field.
17. Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person in the **emergency_contact_relationship** field.
18. Provide the phone number of the emergency contact person in the **emergency_contact_phone** field.
19. Provide the email address of the emergency contact person in the **emergency_contact_email** field.
20. Provide the address of the emergency contact person in the **emergency_contact_address** field.
21. If applicable, provide a signature of the patient in the **patient_signature** field.
22. If applicable, provide a photo of the patient's signature in the **patient_signature_image** field.
23. If applicable, provide any additional medical notes in the **medical_note** field.
24. If applicable, provide any additional medical notes in the **medical_note_2** field.
25. If applicable, provide any additional medical notes in the **medical_note_3** field.
