# family_declaration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Family Declaration Form is used to declare family members and their relationships to the social worker. The form helps the social worker to gather necessary information about each family member, including their name and birthdate. The form also asks about the relationship between the social worker and each family member, which is a critical aspect of building a trusting and supportive relationship with the family.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Family Declaration Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of each family member in the "Family Name" field.
2. Enter the name and birthdate of each family member in the following fields.
3. For each family member, select "Yes" or "No" in the "family_member_**_relationship" field to indicate whether you are related to them.
4. If you are not related to a family member, enter a brief explanation in the corresponding "family_member_**_relationship_other" field.
