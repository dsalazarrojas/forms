# Homeless Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homeless Verification Form is used by social workers to gather essential information about individuals who claim to be homeless or in need of assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately with your personal details.
2. Ensure that you have provided all the required information.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name**: This field is used to capture your full name. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address**: This field is used to record your current address. If you do not have a fixed address, please describe your location or circumstances.
* **Date Of Birth**: This field is used to determine your age and ensure that you are eligible for services. Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Email**: This field is optional and used for communication purposes only. Please enter a valid email address if you wish to receive updates and notifications about your case.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your social worker for guidance.
* Remember that this form is used to assess your eligibility for services, so please provide truthful and accurate information.
