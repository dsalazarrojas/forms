# NDIS Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate referrals for the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) for patients who need support services. This form is used to gather information from healthcare providers to help streamline the referral process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's ID in the "Patient ID" field. This is a unique identifier for the patient.
2. In the "Referral Reason" field, provide a clear description of the referral reason for the patient.
3. In the "Support Requirements" field, select all the support options that the patient requires.
4. In the "Requested Supports" field, select all the support services that the patient is requesting.
5. In the "Contact Name", "Contact Phone", and "Contact Email" fields, provide the contact information for the healthcare provider submitting the referral.
6. In the "Submitted By" field, enter the ID of the healthcare provider submitting the referral.
7. In the "Submitted Date" field, select the date the referral was submitted.
8. In the "Assessor Name", "Assessor Phone", and "Assessor Email" fields, provide the contact information for the assessor responsible for assessing the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, number, required): Enter the patient's unique identifier.
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, required): Provide a clear description of the referral reason for the patient.
* **Support Requirements** (`support_requirements`, select_multiple, required): Select all the support options that the patient requires.
* **Requested Supports** (`requested_supports`, select_multiple, required): Select all the support services that the patient is requesting.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider submitting the referral.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider submitting the referral.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the healthcare provider submitting the referral.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, number, required): Enter the ID of the healthcare provider submitting the referral.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Select the date the referral was submitted.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the assessor responsible for assessing the patient.
* **Assessor Phone** (`assessor_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the assessor responsible for assessing the patient.
* **Assessor Email** (`assessor_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the assessor responsible for assessing the patient.
