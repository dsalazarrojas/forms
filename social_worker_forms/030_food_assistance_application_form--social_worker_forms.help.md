# Food Assistance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking food assistance to determine their eligibility for food programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the sections below with your information as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure you have provided all necessary information.
3. Once you have completed the form, submit it for review and further assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your personal details, including your name, address, and contact information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your primary contact information, including phone number and email address, so we can get in touch with you easily.
* **Food Needs** (`food_needs`, `text`, required: false): Describe your food needs and requirements, such as dietary restrictions or food allergies.
* **Household Info** (`household_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about your household members, including number of dependents and their ages.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of a person we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Income Info** (`income_info`, `text`, required: false): Report your income level and any other financial information that may be relevant.
* **Income Frequency** (`income_frequency`, `number`, required: false): Enter the frequency of your income, for example, weekly, bi-weekly, etc.
* **Food Assistance Type** (`food_assistance_type`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the type of food assistance you are eligible for, such as Food Stamps or WIC (Women, Infants, and Children).
* **Food Bank** (`food_bank`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Check the food banks that you are interested in receiving assistance from, such as True or False.

Please note that some fields are required to be filled out to submit the form. Ensure you provide accurate and complete information to ensure efficient processing of your application.
