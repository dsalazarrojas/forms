# Household Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a household's characteristics, demographics, and economic status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by answering each question to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that you answer all required questions (if any).
3. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
4. Review your answers for accuracy and completeness.
