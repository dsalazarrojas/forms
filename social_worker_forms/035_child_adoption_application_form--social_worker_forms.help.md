### Child Adoption Application Form - Help Guide

#### Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from applicants interested in becoming foster or adoptive parents. The information collected will be used to assess the applicant's suitability to provide a stable and loving home for a child. Please take the time to answer each question accurately and thoroughly.

#### How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you answer all required fields.
- If a field is marked as optional, feel free to fill it out if you have the information available.
- Be honest and accurate in your answers.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`applicant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (`applicant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Marital status** (`marital_status`, select one, required): Choose your current marital status from the options provided.
* **Spouse or partner name (if applicable)** (`spouse_name`, text, optional): If you are married or in a domestic partnership, enter your spouse or partner's name.
* **Number of children currently in household** (`children_count`, number, required): Enter the total number of children you have in your household.
* **Ages of current children** (`children_ages`, text, optional): Enter the ages of your current children.
* **Preferred age of child to adopt** (`preferred_age`, select one, required): Select the preferred age of child you are interested in adopting.
* **Gender preference** (`gender_preference`, select one, required): Choose whether you have a gender preference for the child you wish to adopt.
* **Openness to children with special needs** (`special_needs_openness`, select one, required): Indicate your level of openness to adopting a child with special needs.
* **Willing to take sibling groups** (`sibling_placement`, select one, required): Select whether you are willing to take sibling groups.
* **Home ownership status** (`home_ownership`, select one, required): Choose your current home ownership status.
* **Number of bedrooms for child** (`home_bedrooms`, number, required): Enter the number of bedrooms available for the child in your home.
* **Employment status** (`employment_status`, select one, required): Choose your current employment status.
* **Approximate household income** (`household_income`, select one, optional): Select your approximate household income range.
* **Financial stability assessment** (`financial_stability`, select one, required): Indicate your level of financial stability.
* **Personal and professional references** (`references_provided`, text, required): Provide the names of two professional references who can vouch for your suitability as a parent.
* **Motivation for adoption** (`motivation`, text, required): Explain why you are interested in adopting a child.
* **Previous parenting or caregiving experience** (`parenting_experience`, text, required): Describe any previous parenting or caregiving experience you have.
* **How you will handle challenges and support the child** (`challenges_commitment`, text, required): Outline how you plan to handle challenges and provide support to the child.
* **Consent to background check and home study** (`background_check_consent`, select one, required): Confirm that you consent to a background check and home study.
* **Declaration of truthfulness** (`truth_declaration`, select one, required): Confirm that all information provided is truthful and accurate.

Please fill out the form with care and attention to detail. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
