# Case Management Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Management Statement Form is designed to collect information about an individual's case management. This form provides a framework for caseworkers to report on the status of their cases, including client interactions, progress updates, and any challenges or issues encountered.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out this form for each case you are managing.** This ensures that all relevant information is captured consistently and accurately.
2. **Provide detailed answers to each question.** Be as specific as possible, especially when explaining client interactions, progress, and challenges.
3. **Use the correct format for dates.** Use the MM/DD/YYYY format for all date fields.
4. **Select the correct options for each question.** Ensure that you choose the best option that accurately reflects the client's situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Case Number (id: 3)**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the case.
* **Client Full Name (id: 5)**: Record the client's full name, as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (id: 7)**: Enter the client's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Case Open Date (id: 9)**: Record the date the case was opened, in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Current Case Status (id: 10)**: Select the status of the case from the provided options:
	+ Open - active
	+ Open - pending action
	+ On hold
	+ Closing - in progress
	+ Closed
	+ Transferred
* **Service Period Covered (id: 11)**: Describe the reporting timeframe, e.g., "Last week," "This month," etc.
* **Period Start Date (id: 12)**: Enter the start date of the reporting period in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Period End Date (id: 13)**: Enter the end date of the reporting period in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Services Provided During Period (id: 14)**: Select all the services provided during the reporting period from the options:
	+ Case assessment
	+ Service planning
	+ Resource linkage
	+ Advocacy
	+ Crisis intervention
	+ Care coordination
	+ Transportation assistance
	+ Housing assistance
	+ Employment support
	+ Benefits enrollment
	+ Counseling
	+ Life skills training
	+ Other
* **Frequency of Contact (id: 15)**: Select how often the client has been seen during the reporting period:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ As needed
* **Date of Last Contact (id: 16)**: Enter the date of the last contact with the client in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Type of Last Contact (id: 17)**: Select the type of last contact from the options:
	+ In-person office visit
	+ In-person home visit
	+ Phone call
	+ Video conference
	+ Email
	+ Text message
	+ Third party contact
* **Progress Assessment (id: 18)**: Provide a detailed account of the client's progress.
* **Progress Toward Goals (id: 19)**: Select the status of progress toward the client's goals:
	+ Significant progress
	+ Moderate progress
	+ Minimal progress
	+ No progress
	+ Regression
* **Goals Achieved This Period (id: 20)**: Record any goals achieved during the reporting period.
* **Barriers Encountered (id: 21)**: Describe any challenges or barriers encountered during the reporting period.
* **Level of Client Engagement (id: 22)**: Select the client's level of engagement:
	+ Very engaged - proactive
	+ Engaged - cooperative
	+ Somewhat engaged - inconsistent
	+ Minimally engaged - resistant
	+ Not engaged - non-compliant
* **Client Compliance with Plan (id: 23)**: Select the client's level of compliance with the service plan:
	+ Fully compliant
	+ Mostly compliant
	+ Partially compliant
	+ Non-compliant
	+ Unable to assess
* **Current Client Needs (id: 24)**: Select all the current needs of the client from the options:
	+ Housing assistance
	+ Financial assistance
	+ Employment services
	+ Healthcare access
	+ Mental health services
	+ Substance abuse treatment
	+ Childcare
	+ Transportation
	+ Legal services
	+ Educational services
	+ Food assistance
	+ Social support
	+ Other
* **Unmet Needs (id: 25)**: Describe any unmet needs of the client.
* **Current Risk Status (id: 26)**: Select the current risk status:
	+ Low risk - stable
	+ Moderate risk - some concerns
	+ High risk - significant concerns
	+ Critical risk - immediate danger
* **Safety Concerns (id: 27)**: Describe any safety concerns or risk factors.
* **Collateral Contacts This Period (id: 28)**: Record any collateral contacts made during the reporting period.
* **Referrals Made (id: 29)**: Record any referrals made during the reporting period.
* **Pending Actions (id: 30)**: Describe any pending actions or issues awaiting completion.
* **Planned Next Steps (id: 31)**: Record any planned next steps or interventions.
* **Next Scheduled Contact (id: 32)**: Enter the date of the next scheduled contact in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Case Recommendations (id: 33)**: Provide any professional recommendations for the case.
* **Additional Comments (id: 34)**: Record any additional comments or information.
* **Case Manager Signature Date (id: 35)**: Enter the date and time the form was signed in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Supervisor Review Required (id: 36)**: Select if a supervisor review is required:
	+ Yes - pending review
	+ Yes - completed
	+ No - not required
