# Adoption Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adoption Certificate Form is designed to provide a standardized and efficient way to document adoption proceedings. This form is intended to be used by social workers, adoption agencies, and other professionals involved in the adoption process. It collects essential information about the adoptive parents, the adoptee, and the adoption details, as well as the agency's verification and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the certificate identification section, which includes a unique serial number for the certificate.
2. Enter the date the certificate was issued.
3. Move on to the party information section, where you'll provide the full legal name of the adoptive parent(s) and the adoptee's legal name.
4. In the adoption details section, select the type of adoption (e.g., child, pet, or adult) and enter the place of finalization (city and state).
5. If applicable, provide the court order date and the name of the presiding judge.
6. The agency verification section is optional and allows you to add the name of the person signing on behalf of the agency, their full name, and contact information.
7. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Serial Number**: A unique identifier for the certificate.
* **Date Issued**: The date the certificate was issued.
* **Adoptive Parent 1**: The full legal name of the primary adoptive parent.
* **Adoptive Parent 2**: The full legal name of the secondary adoptive parent (if applicable).
* **Adoptee Legal Name**: The legal name of the child or pet being adopted.
* **Type of Adoption**: Select the type of adoption (e.g., child, pet, or adult).
* **Place of Finalization**: Enter the city and state where the adoption took place.
* **Court Order Date**: The date of the court order (if applicable).
* **Presiding Judge**: The name of the judge who signed the decree (if applicable).
* **Authorizing Official Name**: The name of the person signing on behalf of the agency.
* **Agency Full Name**: The full name of the agency.
* **Contact Information**: The agency's contact information (phone or email).
