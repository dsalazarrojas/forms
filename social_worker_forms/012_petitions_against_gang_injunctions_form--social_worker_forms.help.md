# Petitions Against Gang Injunctions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from community members about their experiences and opinions on gang injunctions, which are legal tools used to prevent and investigate gang-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have a genuine understanding of the topics and questions presented.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Select all the relevant fields that apply to your experiences or opinions.
4. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **root causes of gang activity** (`root_causes_gang_activity`, text, optional): Please describe the underlying reasons that contribute to gang activity in your community. This could include economic, social, or cultural factors.
* **policies and practices that uphold civil liberties** (`policies_and_practices`, text, required): Describe the policies and practices that you believe are effective in upholding civil liberties and preventing gang-related activities. Be specific and provide examples.
* **unjust punishment** (`unjust_punishment`, text, optional): If you have experienced or witnessed unjust punishment related to gang injunctions, please describe it in detail.
* **community awareness about the impacts of gang injunctions** (`community_awareness`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on how to educate the community about the impacts of gang injunctions, such as the effects on public safety, youth involvement, or community relations.
* **increase accountability** (`accountability`, text, optional): Suggest ways to increase accountability among law enforcement, policymakers, or community leaders regarding gang injunctions.
* **empower communities** (`community_empowerment`, text, optional): Describe initiatives or strategies that can empower communities to take ownership and address gang-related issues.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, select_multiple, required): Please indicate whether you are submitting this form to express your opinion or to report a personal experience related to gang injunctions. Select all that apply.
	+ Yes: I am expressing my opinion or reporting a personal experience.
	+ No: I am submitting this form for other reasons.
* **email notification** (`email_notification`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to receive a confirmation of your form submission or updates on this issue.
* **phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to receive a call or update from us regarding your form submission.

Note: The required fields are marked as "required", and the optional fields are not marked as required. Please ensure that you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
