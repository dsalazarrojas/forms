# Social Workers Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Workers Membership Form is a registration form for social workers to join and submit their details for membership in the organization. This form collects essential information about the social worker, including their name, contact information, organization, role, and membership type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your organization or affiliation in the "Organization" field.
6. Select your role within the organization from the "Role" dropdown menu.
7. Choose your membership type (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually) in the "Membership Type" dropdown menu.
8. (Optional) Add any additional notes about your membership in the "Notes" field.
9. Review and submit your completed form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Required): Enter a valid email address that you can use for communication from the organization.
* **Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Organization** (Required): Enter your organization or affiliation where you work.
* **Role** (Required): Select your role within the organization from the dropdown menu (Administrator, Member, or Guest).
* **Membership Type** (Required): Choose how often you would like to be invoiced for your membership (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Notes** (Optional): Add any additional information about your membership that you'd like to be included in your record.
* **Submit** (Required): Review and submit your completed form to complete your membership registration.
