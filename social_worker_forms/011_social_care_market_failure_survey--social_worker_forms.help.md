# Social Care Market Failure Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on their experience with the social care market failure, including their access to services, perceived effectiveness of policies, and overall satisfaction with the survey process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all the mandatory questions (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Select the social care services you have access to.
3. Report how often you access these services.
4. Indicate if you experience gaps in any of the services.
5. Describe the gaps you experience if you answered "yes" in the previous question.
6. Evaluate the effectiveness of current social care policies.
7. Suggest improvements to the policies.
8. Answer questions about your experience in completing this form, including how easy or hard it was, how long it took, and what device and location you used.
9. Provide contact information if you want to receive a confirmation email after submitting your feedback.
10. Finally, select your preferred submission confirmation method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, `optional`): This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **What are the social care services you have access to?** (`2`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the social care services you have access to, such as Childcare Services, Housing Services, Financial Assistance, etc.
* **How often do you access these services?** (`3`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Indicate how often you access each selected social care service.
* **Are there service gaps in any of the social care services you need?** (`4`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Yes/No, if yes, select the services with gaps.
* **If yes, which services have gaps?** (`5`, `text`, `required`): Describe the gaps you experience in each selected service.
* **How has the gap impacted you?** (`6`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Rate the impact of the gaps on your experience.
* **How effective do you find the current social care policies to be?** (`7`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Evaluate the effectiveness of the current policies.
* **What would you suggest to improve the policies?** (`8`, `text`, `required`): Provide suggestions to improve the policies.
* **Name** (`16`, `text`, `optional`): Provide your name.
* **Email** (`13`, `email`, `optional`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`14`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`15`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your address.
* **How did you find this survey?** (`17`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Rate your experience in completing this survey, including how easy or hard it was, how long it took, and what device and location you used.
* **How satisfied are you with this survey?** (`18`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the survey.
* **Comments** (`10`, `text`, `optional`): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Submission Comments** (`11`, `text`, `optional`): Add any comments about the submission process.
* **Contact Details** (`12`, `text`, `optional`): Provide your contact details for future reference.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`24`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select if you want to receive a confirmation email after submitting your feedback.
* **Other submission confirmation preferences** (`25`, `text`, `optional`): Provide any other preferences for submission confirmation.

# Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Try to complete the form in one sitting, but it's okay to save and resume later.
* The form should take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete.
* If you have any technical issues while completing the form, please let us know. 
* If you have any other suggestions or feedback on the form, please include them in the "policy suggestions" field. 

Note: The form's layout and content may be adjusted based on user feedback. 

This help guide is a guide to assist users in completing the form and is subject to change based on feedback and updates.
