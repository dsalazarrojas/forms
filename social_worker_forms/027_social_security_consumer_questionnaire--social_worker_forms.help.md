# Social Security Consumer Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from social services clients about their consumer experience and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Complete each section of the form according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This section explains the purpose of the form and its importance in providing better services.

### Demographics
* **Demographics** (`demographics`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of yourself, including your age, occupation, and any other relevant information.

### Awareness
* **Awareness** (`awareness`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all that apply regarding your awareness of our services:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

### Usage
* **Usage** (`usage`, `text`, required: false): Describe how you have used our social services, including any specific services you have used.

### Benefits
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all that apply regarding the benefits you have received from our services:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

### Concerns
* **Concerns** (`concerns`, `text`, required: false): Describe any concerns you have about our services.

### Satisfaction
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): How satisfied are you with our services?
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied

### Contact Information
* **Contact information** (`contact_info`, `email`, required: false): Please provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible for each section.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

Note: The fields marked as `required: false` are optional, but providing information in these sections will help us better understand your needs and improve our services.
