# Moving Forward Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moving Forward Survey is designed to help social workers reflect on their experiences, goals, and challenges. It collects information on their accomplishments, lessons learned, and areas for improvement, aiming to assist them in identifying next steps and making positive changes in their profession.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Form Title**: Enter a brief description of the survey, which will help in identifying its context and purpose.
2. **Introduction**: This field is optional, but it provides space for any preliminary comments or context that may be helpful for the surveyor.
3. **Reflection for the past year**: Share your reflections on the past year, focusing on your experiences and any notable events.
4. **Goals and Objectives**: List your goals and objectives for the current period.
5. **Challenges**: Describe the key challenges you faced in the past year.
6. **Accomplishments**: Share your accomplishments, highlighting the ones you are most proud of.
7. **Lessons Learned**: Reflect on the lessons you learned during the past year.
8. **Areas for Improvement**: Choose one or more areas where you could improve, such as communication, time management, or team management.
9. **Next Steps**: Outline your next steps, considering the lessons learned and areas for improvement.
10. **Comments**: Add any additional comments or insights that are not covered in the other fields.
11. **Submission Date**: This field will automatically be populated once you submit the form, displaying the date and time of submission.
