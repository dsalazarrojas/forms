# Ethnographic Research Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your behaviors, activities, devices, demographics, relationships, and other relevant aspects for ethnographic research. This will help researchers gather a comprehensive understanding of the research topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and provide honest and accurate responses.
2. Complete each section according to the instructions provided.
3. Make sure to answer all the required fields (marked as 'required' in the form).
4. You can skip fields that are not applicable to your situation.
5. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your researcher or contact the research team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_behaviors** (page 1): Please describe your daily behaviors and routines.
	+ This field is intended to capture your regular activities and habits.
* **participant_activities** (page 2): What activities do you engage in on a daily basis?
	+ Please select all the activities that apply to you.
* **participant_acts** (page 3): How often do you perform certain actions?
	+ Please select 'Yes' or 'No' for each option.
* **participant_comments** (page 4): Do you have any comments about the participant's behavior or activities?
	+ Please select 'Yes' or 'No' for your response.
* **survey_accessibility** (page 5): How accessible do you think this survey is for you?
	+ Please provide any comments or feedback about the survey's accessibility.
* **participant_devices** (page 6): What devices do you use to access the survey?
	+ Please select one option that applies to you.
* **participant_demographics** (page 7): What demographic information would you like to share about yourself?
	+ Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **participant_relationships** (page 8): Do you have any relationships with other participants or researchers?
	+ Please select 'Yes' or 'No' for your response.
* **participant_notes** (page 9): Do you have any notes or comments about the participant's relationships?
	+ Please select 'Yes' or 'No' for your response.
* **participant_other** (page 10): Is there any other information you would like to share about yourself?
	+ Please provide any relevant information.
* **participant_other_comments** (page 11): Do you have any other comments about this survey or your experience?
	+ Please provide any comments or feedback.
* **participant_other_notes** (page 12): Do you have any other notes or comments about this survey?
	+ Please select 'Yes' or 'No' for your response.
