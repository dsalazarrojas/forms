# Social Support Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Support Assistance Request Form is used to gather information from individuals or organizations requesting social support assistance within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the requested personal and contact information of the individual or organization submitting the request.
2. Select the mental health condition and/or crisis or issue being addressed.
3. Provide a description of the crisis or issue.
4. Fill in the submitter's department and name.
5. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): This is the name of the individual or organization submitting the request.

### 2. **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): This is the email address of the individual or organization submitting the request.

### 3. **Phone** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): This is the phone number of the individual or organization submitting the request.

### 4. **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): This is the name of the contact person responsible for the request.

### 5. **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): This is the address of the individual or organization submitting the request.

### 6. **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_number`, `text`, required/optional): This is the emergency contact number of the individual or organization submitting the request.

### 7. **Mental Health Condition** (`mental_health_condition`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the mental health condition being addressed, such as Depression, Anxiety, or Substance abuse.

### 8. **Description** (`mental_health_crisis_description`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a description of the crisis or issue being addressed.

### 9. **Name 2** (`name_2`, `text`, required/optional): This is the name of the individual or organization submitting the request (if different from name field 1).

### 10. **Email 2** (`email_2`, `email`, required/optional): This is the email address of the individual or organization submitting the request (if different from email field 1).

### 11. **Phone Number 2** (`phone_number_2`, `text`, required/optional): This is the phone number of the individual or organization submitting the request (if different from phone number field 1).

### 12. **Department** (`department`, `text`, required/optional): Select the department within the organization responsible for the request.

### 13. **Submit Request** (`submit_request`, `date`, required/true): Select the date the request was submitted.

### 14. **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional information about the request.

### 15. **Jotform** (`jotform`, `text`, required/true): This field is intended for internal use and does not apply to external users.

### 16. **Reported By** (`reported_by`, `text`, required/true): This is the name of the individual or organization submitting the request.

### 17. **Report Date** (`report_date`, `date`, required/true): Select the date the request was submitted.

### 18. **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, `text`, required/true): This is the name of the reviewer responsible for the review.

### 19. **Review Status** (`review_status`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the review status of the request (New Submission, Submission in Progress, Submission Approved, Submission Declined).

### 20. **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any comments or feedback from the reviewer.

### 21. **Follow-up** (`followup`, `text`, required/optional): This is a field for the reviewer to provide follow-up information.

### 22. **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select the department within the organization submitting the request.

### 23. **Department 2** (`department_2`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select the department within the organization submitting the request (if different from submitter_department field).

### 24. **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, required/true): This is the name of the individual or organization submitting the request.

### 25. **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required/true): This is the email address of the individual or organization submitting the request.

### 26. **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required/true): This is the phone number of the individual or organization submitting the request.

### 27. **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required/true): This is the name of the individual or organization submitting the request.

### 28. **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, `date`, required/true): Select the date the request was submitted.

### 29. **Review Status2** (`review_status2`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the review status of the request (Awaiting Review, Approved, Declined).

### 30. **Submitter Department2** (`submitter_department2`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select the department within the organization submitting the request.

### 31. **Submitter Name2** (`submitter_name2`, `text`, required/true): This is the name of the individual or organization submitting the request.

### 32. **Submitter Email2** (`submitter_email2`, `email`, required/true): This is the email address of the individual or organization submitting the request.

### 33. **Submit Date2** (`submit_date2`, `date`, required/true): Select the date the request was submitted.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals to submit on behalf of the individual or organization.
* Review and understand the form's purpose and scope before submitting.
* Complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
* Follow-up with the individual or organization submitting the request as needed.
* Keep in mind that this form is for internal use within the organization.
* If you are an external user, do not fill out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the designated department or contact person for clarification.
