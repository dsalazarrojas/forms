# Senior Citizen Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Citizen Support Request Form is a tool used to collect information from senior citizens who require support. It is designed to gather essential details to provide the best possible support.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your information, including your first name, last name, date of birth, and phone number (if applicable).
2.  Select the service you require from the list provided.
3.  Enter any additional comments or requests you may have.
4.  Ensure your email address is accurate and active.
5.  Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Page**: This field is a placeholder page and should be left blank.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you have one. This will help us contact you for any further assistance.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your active and accurate email address. This will help us communicate with you efficiently.
*   **Service Request**: Select the service you require from the provided options. Please choose one of the following:
    *   Home Help: General assistance with daily tasks.
    *   Transportation: Help with transportation or mobility.
    *   Health Services: Medical assistance or health-related support.
    *   Social Services: Emotional or social support.
    *   Other: Please specify your own service request.
*   **Service Type**: Select the service type related to your request. Please choose one of the options above, or select "Other" if you need something different.
*   **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or requests you may have. This will help us understand your needs better.
*   **First Name**: Enter your first name as you would like it to be recorded.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name as you would like it to be recorded.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Gender**: Select your gender from the options provided.

Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
