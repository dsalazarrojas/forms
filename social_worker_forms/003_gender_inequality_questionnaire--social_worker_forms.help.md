# Gender Inequality Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about personal experiences and perceptions of gender inequality in local communities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as you can.
2. Select the options that best match your personal experiences and perceptions.
3. Complete all fields as required or optional, depending on the field's designation.
