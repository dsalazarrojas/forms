# Animal Abuse Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand experiences, perceptions, and challenges related to animal abuse among professionals and individuals who have encountered or witnessed animal abuse in their work or personal capacity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select your role or profession from the list provided.
2. Choose your years of experience in the field.
3. Indicate if you have personally encountered suspected animal abuse.
4. Select all types of abuse or neglect you have witnessed.
5. Specify how often you report suspected animal abuse.
6. Choose the barriers that prevent you from reporting.
7. Assess your knowledge of animal protection laws in your area.
8. Indicate if you have received professional training on identifying animal abuse.
9. Select all training topics that would be most helpful for you.
10. Rate the effectiveness of authority responses when abuse is reported.
11. Indicate if you are aware of the link between animal abuse and domestic violence.
12. Confirm if your agency collaborates with animal welfare organizations.
13. Evaluate how dealing with animal abuse cases affects you emotionally.
14. Select all support options that would help you address animal abuse more effectively.
15. Provide any additional comments or observations.
