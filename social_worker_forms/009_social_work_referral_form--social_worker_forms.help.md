# Social Work Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Work Referral Form is designed to facilitate the referral process for students who require social services. It gathers essential information from teachers and other relevant parties to ensure that the student receives the necessary support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "student_name" field.
2. Select the student's grade level from the "student_grade" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the recommended services for the student from the "recommended_services" field.
4. Select the assigned tool from the "assigned_tool" field.
5. Provide any additional contact information in the "contact_info" field.
6. Enter any relevant notes in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name** (Student Name, text, required): Enter the student's name in this field.
* **teacher** (Teacher, text, not required): Enter the teacher's name in this field. This field is for the teacher's reference, and while not required, it can provide additional context.
* **referral_date** (Referral Date, date, not required): Enter the date when the referral was made. This field is not required but can help with tracking and record-keeping.
* **student_grade** (Student Grade, select_one, required): Select the student's grade level from the dropdown menu.
* **behavior_description** (Behavior Description, text, not required): Enter any relevant details about the student's behavior in this field.
* **recommended_services** (Recommended Services, select_multiple, required): Choose the services that the student requires, such as counseling, tutoring, or mentorship.
* **contact_info** (Contact Info, text, not required): Provide any additional contact information about the student or teacher.
* **notes** (Notes, note, not required): Enter any relevant notes about the student or referral process.
* **student_id** (Student ID, number, not required): This field is currently not used and can be ignored.
* **teacher_id** (Teacher ID, number, not required): This field is currently not used and can be ignored.
* **assigned_tool** (Assigned Tool, select_one, required): Select the tool assigned to the student for support.
* **submitted_date** (Submitted Date, date, not required): Enter the date when the form was submitted, which is automatically recorded.
