# disaster_relief_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and other necessary information from individuals affected by a disaster. The purpose of this form is to gather data for aid distribution, resource allocation, and response planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information for the individual affected by the disaster.
2. Provide details about the victim, including any relevant contact details.
3. Enter contact information for emergency contacts.
4. If applicable, provide insurance information to aid in aid distribution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information**:
	+ <label>Contact Information</label> (contact_information, text, required: false)
		- This field is for the individual affected by the disaster to provide their contact information.
* **Victim Information**:
	+ <label Victim Information</label> (victim_info, text, required: false)
		- This field is for providing details about the victim, such as their condition and any relevant context.
* **Contact Details**:
	+ <label>Contact Details</label> (contact_details, text, required: false)
		- This field is for providing additional contact details, such as phone numbers or email addresses.
* **Emergency Contact**:
	+ <label>Emergency Contact</label> (emergency_contact, text, required: false)
		- This field is for providing contact information for emergency contacts, such as next of kin or legal guardians.
* **Insurance Information**:
	+ <label>Insurance Information</label> (insurance_info, text, required: false)
		- If applicable, this field is for providing insurance information to aid in aid distribution and resource allocation.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or assistance.
- In the event of a duplicate form submission, please refrain from submitting again unless instructed to do so.
