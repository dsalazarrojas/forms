# Public Housing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations to apply for public housing. It is used to understand your needs and allocate resources effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by *) and any applicable optional fields.
2. Enter your User Name, Address, and Date Of Birth in the respective fields.
3. Select your Income Level, Employment Status, and Housing Status from the provided options.
4. Enter your Phone Number, Email, and Organization information (if applicable).
5. Provide a Note if you need to add any additional information.
6. Sign and date the form with your Submitter Signature, Name, and Date Submitted.
7. Enter the Submitter Relationship and Organization information (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your physical address where you currently reside.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Income Level** (`income_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select your current income level, if applicable. Options: High, Low.
* **Household Size** (`household_size`, number, optional): Enter the number of people living in your household, if applicable.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number, if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address, if applicable.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current employment status. Options: Unemployed, Employed, Retired.
* **Housing Status** (`housing_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current housing status. Options: Renting, Owning, Public Housing.
* **Income Source** (`income_source`, text, optional): Enter your income source, if applicable.
* **Income Frequency** (`income_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how frequently you receive income. Options: Weekly, Monthly, Yearly.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional information you think is necessary to support your application.
* **Submitter Signature** (`submitter_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form with your name and date submitted.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required): Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Relationship** (`submitter_relationship`, text, optional): Enter your relationship to the submitter, if applicable.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization's name, if applicable.
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, text, optional): Enter your organization's address, if applicable.
* **Organization Phone** (`organization_phone`, text, optional): Enter your organization's phone number, if applicable.
* **Organization Email** (`organization_email`, email, optional): Enter your organization's email address, if applicable.
