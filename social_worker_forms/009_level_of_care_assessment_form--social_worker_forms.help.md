# level of care assessment form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the level of care required for a patient based on their medical condition, medical history, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's basic information on page 1, including their name, date of admission, and medical diagnosis.
2. On page 2, select the appropriate nursing level and choose one or more rehabilitation programs that the patient may require.
3. On page 3, list all the patient's current medications.
4. On page 4, include a detailed medical history, including any relevant medical information that may impact the patient's care.
5. On pages 5-8, fill out the facility information, including their name, phone number, fax number, email address, address, city, state, zip code, and country.
6. On page 9, provide any additional notes about the patient.
7. On page 10, select the assessment date.
8. On pages 11-12, provide the assessor's contact information, including their name, title, and contact information.
9. Once you have completed all the pages, review the form for completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient Date of Admission** (`patient_date_of_admission`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date the patient was admitted to the facility.
* **Medical Diagnosis** (`medical_diagnosis`, `text`, `required`): Enter the patient's current medical diagnosis.
* **Nursing Level** (`nursing_level`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the appropriate nursing level for the patient.
* **Rehabilitation Program** (`rehabilitation_program`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select one or more rehabilitation programs that the patient may require.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, `required`): List all the patient's current medications.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, `required`): Include a detailed medical history, including any relevant medical information that may impact the patient's care.
* **Medical Status** (`medical_status`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the patient's current medical status (e.g. stable, critical, etc.).
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, `text`, `optional`): Add any additional notes about the patient.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date the assessment was completed.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the assessor.
* **Assessor Title** (`assessor_title`, `text`, `required`): Enter the title of the assessor.
* **Assessor Contact** (`assessor_contact`, `text`, `required`): Enter the contact information of the assessor.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the hospital.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the facility.
* **Facility Phone** (`facility_phone`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the phone number of the facility.
* **Facility Fax** (`facility_fax`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the fax number of the facility.
* **Facility Email** (`facility_email`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the email address of the facility.
* **Facility Address 1** (`facility_address_1`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the first line of the facility's address.
* **Facility Address 2** (`facility_address_2`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the second line of the facility's address.
* **Facility City** (`facility_city`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the city of the facility.
* **Facility State** (`facility_state`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the state of the facility.
* **Facility Zip** (`facility_zip`, `text`, `required`): Enter the zip code of the facility.
* **Facility Country** (`facility_country`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the country of the facility.
* **Facility Region** (`facility_region`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the region of the facility.

Note: This help guide is a general guide and may not cover every possible scenario. If you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out to the facility administration.
