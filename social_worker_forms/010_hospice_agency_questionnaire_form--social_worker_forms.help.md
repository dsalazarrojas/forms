# Hospice Agency Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from hospice agencies, which provides end-of-life care to individuals who are terminally ill.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agency Information** (`agency_information`): This field is for the agency's basic information, such as their name, address, and contact details.
* **Questionnaire Form** (`agency_questionnaire_form`): This field is for the agency's questionnaire form, which is used to collect more detailed information about the agency's services and certifications.
* **Required Standards** (`required_standards`): This field is for the agency's required standards and regulations.
* **Certification** (`certification`): This field is for the agency's certification status and details.
* **Additional Questions** (`additional_questions`): This field is for any additional questions that the agency would like to answer.
* **Certifying Agency** (`certifying_agency`): This field is for the agency's certifying agency information.
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`): This field is for the agency's certification date.
* **Certifying Agency Name** (`certifying_agency_name`): This field is for the agency's certifying agency name.
* **Certifying Agency Address** (`certifying_agency_address`): This field is for the agency's certifying agency address.
* **Certifying Agency Phone** (`certifying_agency_phone`): This field is for the agency's certifying agency phone number.
* **Certifying Agency Email** (`certifying_agency_email`): This field is for the agency's certifying agency email address.
* **Certifying Agency Website** (`certifying_agency_website`): This field is for the agency's certifying agency website.
* **Agency Director** (`agency_director`): This field is for the agency's director's contact information.
* **Agency Executive** (`agency_executive`): This field is for the agency's executive's contact information.
* **Agency Staff** (`agency_staff`): This field is for the agency's staff's contact information.
* **Agency Hours** (`agency_hours`): This field is for the agency's hours of operation.
* **Agency Services** (`agency_services`): This field is for the agency's services offered.
* **Agency Funding Source** (`agency_funding_source`): This field is for the agency's funding source.
* **Agency Services Areas** (`agency_services_areas`): This field is for the agency's services areas.
* **Agency Certification Date** (`agency_certification_date`): This field is for the agency's certification date.
* **Agency License Number** (`agency_license_number`): This field is for the agency's license number.
* **Agency License Expiration** (`agency_license_expiration`): This field is for the agency's license expiration date.
* **Agency Certifying Agency** (`agency_certifying_agency`): This field is for the agency's certifying agency information.
* **Agency Additional Info** (`agency_additional_info`): This field is for any additional information about the agency.

Please note that this form is designed to be completed by the agency itself, not by patients or caregivers. If you are a patient or caregiver, please contact the agency directly for more information.
