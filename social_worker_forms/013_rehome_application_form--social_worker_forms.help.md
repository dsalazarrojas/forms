# Rehome Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from you and your pet to help us find a suitable home for your furry friend.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer the questions in this form as accurately and thoroughly as possible. Each section is designed to help us understand your pet's needs and your living situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Info
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required): Please provide your name and contact information to help us get in touch with you.
### 2. Pet Info
* **Pet Info** (`pet_info`, text, required): Tell us about your pet, including their species, age, size, and any special needs or health issues they may have.
### 3. Application
* **Application** (`application`, text, required): Briefly describe why you are interested in rehoming your pet and what kind of home they might be suited for.
### 4. Adoption Status
* **Adoption Status** (`adoption_status`, text, required): Indicate whether you are currently working with another agency or organization to rehome your pet.
### 5. Home Situation
* **Home Situation** (`home_situation`, text, required): Describe your current living situation, including your address, availability, and any relevant details about your home.
### 6. Pet Suitable
* **Pet Suitable** (`pet_suitable`, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you are sure your pet is suitable for rehoming, "False" if you are not, or "unsure" if you are still unsure.
### 7. Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
### 8. Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person who can be reached in case of an emergency.

Note: This form is for non-human users (pets) and is designed to be filled out by the pet owner.
