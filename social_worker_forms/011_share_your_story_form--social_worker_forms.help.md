# Share Your Story Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Share Your Story Form is a platform for customers to share their experiences, testimonials, and media (photos or videos) with us. We value your feedback and would love to hear about your interactions with our service.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Share Your Story Form" from the available forms.
2. Review each page carefully and fill in the relevant fields according to your experience.
3. You can choose which fields to fill in or leave blank, depending on your preference.
4. Once you've completed all the pages, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Experience
• **Customer Experience** (`customer_experience`, text, optional): Share your experience with our service. This is a text field where you can type in your thoughts on how our service has helped you.

### Testimonial
• **Testimonial** (`testimonial`, text, optional): Share a brief testimonial about our service. This is a text field where you can type in your thoughts on how our service has benefited you.

### Photo
• **Photo** (`photo`, number, optional): Share a photo related to your experience with our service. You can either upload a photo or provide a reference to a photo URL.

### Video
• **Video** (`video`, select_one, optional): Have you shared a video related to our service? Select "Yes" if you have, or "No" if you haven't.
• Note: The "Video" field is optional, but please be aware that sharing a video can greatly enhance your story.
• Tip: If you have shared a video, please provide the link or a description of where it can be found.
