# hospice_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from patients and their families to improve the quality of hospice care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the patient who received hospice care.
2.  Provide your feedback about your experience with our hospice care team.
3.  Rate your satisfaction with the care you received.
4.  Enter the name of the social worker who provided care to you or your loved one.
5.  Enter the date of the feedback.
6.  Enter the name of the hospice where you received care.
7.  Enter your contact phone number (optional).
8.  Enter your email address (optional).
9.  Provide additional comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient who received hospice care.
* **Feedback**: Provide your feedback about your experience with our hospice care team.
* **Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the care you received. Please rate on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Social Worker Name**: Enter the name of the social worker who provided care to you or your loved one.
* **Feedback Date**: Enter the date you are providing this feedback.
* **Hospice Name**: Enter the name of the hospice where you received care.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Rating Score**: Enter the rating score you gave above.
* **Comments**: Provide additional comments about your experience.

Note: All fields are optional except for the Patient Name field, which is required.
