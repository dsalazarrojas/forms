# Imprest Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Imprest Management Form is used to update and manage imprest accounts for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the **Employee Name** field.
2. Enter the employee's ID number in the **Employee Id** field.
3. Enter the account number in the **Account Number** field.
4. Enter the amount of the imprest account in the **Amount** field.
5. Select the date of the imprest account in the **Date** field.
6. Select the imprest account type (Cash, Accrual, or Expense) from the **Imprest Account Type** dropdown menu.
7. Select the account status (Active, Inactive, or Overdue) from the **Account Status** dropdown menu.
8. Select whether the account is a (Yes or No) from the **Account Type** dropdown menu.
9. Enter the account balance, if applicable, in the **Account Balance** field.
10. Enter the account balance date, if applicable, in the **Account Balance Date** field.
11. Enter any additional notes about the account in the **Account Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Imprest Form**: This is the main field for entering imprest account information.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee associated with this imprest account.
* **Employee Id**: Enter the ID number of the employee associated with this imprest account.
* **Account Number**: Enter the account number associated with this imprest account.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount associated with this imprest account.
* **Date**: Select the date associated with this imprest account.
* **Imprest Account Type**: Select the type of imprest account (Cash, Accrual, or Expense).
* **Account Status**: Select the status of the account (Active, Inactive, or Overdue).
* **Account Type**: Select whether the account is a (Yes or No).
* **Account Balance**: Enter the balance associated with this imprest account, if applicable.
* **Account Balance Date**: Enter the date associated with this imprest account balance, if applicable.
* **Account Notes**: Enter any additional notes about this imprest account, if applicable.
* **Account Type**: Select the type of account (not to be confused with the **Imprest Account Type** field).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field's label or hint for more information.
* Double-check your imprest account information for accuracy before submitting the form.
