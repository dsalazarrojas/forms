# Lease Assistance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your housing situation, allowing us to provide you with the necessary assistance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. Please make sure to answer all required fields, as indicated by the "required" label.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your lease.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your lease.
* **Reason For Assistance** (`reason_for_assistance`, text, required): Select the reason you are seeking assistance with your housing.
* **Assistance Requested** (`assistance_requested`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be assisted. Options: Assistance With Finding a Home, Assistance With Finding a Apartment, Other.
* **Other Assistance** (`assistance_other`, text, optional): If "Other" is selected, please describe the assistance you are seeking.
* **Assistance Notes** (`assistance_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your assistance request.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your landlord.
* **Landlord Phone** (`landlord_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your landlord.
* **Landlord Email** (`landlord_email`, email, required): Enter the email of your landlord.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property you are having issues with.
* **Rent Start Date** (`rent_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your rent.
* **Rent End Date** (`rent_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your rent.
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of rent you pay.
* **Lease Duration** (`lease_duration`, text, required): Enter the duration of your lease.
* **Rent Payment Method** (`rent_payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you pay your rent. Options: Cash, Check, Online Banking.
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you pay your rent. Options: Monthly, Weekly, Bi-Weekly.
* **Other Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency_other`, text, optional): If "Other" is selected, please describe the frequency of your rent payments.
* **Rent Notes** (`rent_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your rent payments. 

Please note that the "Other" options in fields like "Reason For Assistance" and "Rent Frequency" allow you to describe your unique situation, which will help us better understand your needs. If unsure, contact your local social worker for guidance. 

You can ask your landlord to fill out the last 4 fields (Landlord Name, Landlord Phone, Landlord Email, and Property Address) as this information is usually available to them.
