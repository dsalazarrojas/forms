# respite_case_notes_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information about respite care cases. It provides a structured way to document the care provided, goals, and status of respite care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Respite Case field with a brief description of the case.
2. Enter the name of the respite client in the Respite Client field.
3. Record the Date of Contact and Time of Contact for the respite care provided.
4. Enter the number of contacts made and the Next Contact Date and Next Contact Time.
5. Select the Respite Care Provided frequency and duration from the dropdown menus.
6. Select the Respite Care Support options that were provided.
7. Select the Respite Care Status and Next Contact Status.
8. Enter any Respite Care Notes and Respite Care Goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respite Case**: A brief description of the case, e.g. "John's mother has dementia and needs help with bathing."
* **Respite Client**: The name of the person receiving respite care, e.g. "John Doe".
* **Date of Contact**: The date of the respite care contact.
* **Time of Contact**: The time of the respite care contact.
* **Number of Contacts**: The number of respite care contacts made.
* **Next Contact Date**: The date of the next scheduled respite care contact.
* **Next Contact Time**: The time of the next scheduled respite care contact.
* **Respite Care Provided**: The frequency of respite care provided, e.g. "Weekly".
* **Respite Care Duration**: The duration of the respite care provided, e.g. "30 minutes".
* **Respite Care Support**: The support provided during the respite care, e.g. "Meal preparation" and "Light housekeeping".
* **Respite Care Status**: The status of the respite care case, e.g. "Active" or "Suspended".
* **Next Contact Status**: The status of the next scheduled respite care contact, e.g. "Scheduled" or "Completed".
* **Respite Care Notes**: Any additional notes about the respite care contact.
* **Respite Care Goals**: The goals for the respite care case, e.g. "Improve mobility" or "Increase independence".
