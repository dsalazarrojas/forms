# Workers' Rights Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workers' Rights Petition Form is designed to collect data from employees regarding their workplace experiences, which can aid in identifying areas of improvement and provide evidence for potential future actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name, workplace address, employer's name, and other relevant details about your work.
2. Choose your type of work (e.g., hourly, salaried, contract, or freelance).
3. Indicate your union affiliation and benefits received.
4. Provide information about the safety measures in place at your workplace.
5. Write a justification for why you are filling out this form.
6. If applicable, provide your employer's resolution to your concerns.
7. Add any recommendations you have for your workplace.
8. Sign the form with your employee signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Worker Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Workplace Address (2)**: Provide the address of your workplace.
- **Employer Name (3)**: Enter the name of your employer or organization.
- **Workplace Phone (4)**: Enter the phone number of your workplace.
- **Workplace Email (5)**: Enter your workplace email address.
- **Date of Last Pay (6)**: Enter the date of your last payment.
- **Time of Last Pay (7)**: Enter the time of your last payment.
- **Last Pay Wage (8)**: Enter your last wage or salary amount.
- **Last Pay Period (9)**: Enter the period of your last pay (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly).
- **Type of Work (10)**: Choose the type of work you do (e.g., hourly, salaried, contract, freelance).
- **Union Affiliation (11)**: Check if you are affiliated with a union.
- **Benefits Received (12)**: Check the benefits you receive from your workplace.
- **Workplace Safety Measures (13)**: Check the safety measures in place at your workplace.
- **Justification (14)**: Write a clear and concise reason for why you are filling out this form.
- **Resolution (15)**: If applicable, provide your employer's resolution or response to your concerns.
- **Recommendations (16)**: Provide any suggestions or recommendations for improvement.
- **Employee Signature (17)**: Sign the form with your employee signature.
- **Employer Agreement (18)**: If applicable, have your employer sign and date the form.

**Tips**
- Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
- Ensure that you have permission from your employer before sharing this form with them.
- If you are having trouble understanding a field, ask a supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
- Keep a copy of this form for your records.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the relevant authorities.
