# Case Management Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Management Referral Form is used by social workers to collect information about an individual who needs case management services. The form helps social workers assess the individual's needs and create a plan for their care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Referral Date**: Enter the date the referral was made.
2. **Referrer Name**: Enter the full name of the referrer.
3. **Referrer Title**: Enter the job title of the referrer.
4. **Referrer Organization**: Enter the name of the organization or agency making the referral.
5. **Referrer Email**: Enter the contact email of the referrer.
6. **Referrer Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the referrer.
7. **Type of Referral**: Select the type of referral (Self-referral, Professional referral, etc.).
8. **Urgency Level**: Select the urgency level of the referral (Routine, Priority, etc.).
9. **Client Information**: Enter any relevant information about the person being referred.
10. **Client Name**: Enter the full name of the individual being referred.
11. **Client Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the individual.
12. **Client Gender**: Select the client's gender (Male, Female, Non-binary, etc.).
13. **Client Address**: Enter the current address of the individual.
14. **Client City**: Enter the city of the individual's current address.
15. **Client State**: Enter the state or province of the individual's current address.
16. **Client ZIP Code**: Enter the postal code of the individual's current address.
17. **Client Phone**: Enter the individual's best contact phone number.
18. **Client Email**: Enter the individual's email address (if available).
19. **Preferred Language**: Select the preferred language for communication.
20. **Interpreter Needed**: Indicate if an interpreter is needed for language assistance.
21. **Reason for Referral**: Enter the reason for the referral.
22. **Presenting Issues**: Select any current concerns or issues the individual is facing.
23. **Current Services**: Enter any active services the individual is currently receiving.
24. **Relevant Service History**: Enter any relevant service history.
25. **Relevant Medical Conditions**: Enter any relevant medical conditions.
26. **Mental Health Diagnoses**: Enter any mental health diagnoses.
27. **Current Medications**: Enter any current medications.
28. **Risk Factors**: Select any known risk factors.
29. **Protective Factors**: Enter any known protective factors.
30. **Support System**: Select the level of support the individual has (Strong, Moderate, etc.).
31. **Housing Status**: Select the individual's current housing status.
32. **Income Status**: Select the individual's income status.
33. **Health Insurance Status**: Select the individual's health insurance status.
34. **Active Legal Issues**: Select any active legal issues.
35. **Requested Services**: Select any specific services the individual is requesting.
36. **Client Goals**: Enter any desired outcomes.
37. **Consent Status**: Select the consent status.
38. **Documents Attached**: Select any documents attached to the referral.
39. **Additional Information**: Enter any additional relevant information.
40. **Referrer Signature Date**: Enter the date and signature of the referrer.

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If an interpreter is needed, indicate this in the "Interpreter Needed" field.
* If the individual has a preferred language, select this in the "Preferred Language" field.
* If there are any relevant medical conditions or mental health diagnoses, enter them in the corresponding fields.
* If the individual is currently receiving any services, list them in the "Current Services" field.
