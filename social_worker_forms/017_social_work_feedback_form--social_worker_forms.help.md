# Social Work Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from social workers, providing an opportunity for clients, colleagues, and managers to offer their opinions and insights. The feedback is meant to be used for improvement and development within the organization. This form is intended to be a comprehensive and standardized way to collect feedback, allowing the organization to better understand the needs and challenges faced by social workers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select "Client" as the feedback type if the feedback is related to a specific client.
* Select "Colleague" as the feedback type if the feedback is related to a colleague or co-worker.
* Select "Manager" as the feedback type if the feedback is related to a manager or supervisor.
* Enter a brief description of the feedback in the description field.
* Enter the date you completed the feedback in the Date Completed field.
* Select the time you completed the feedback in the Time Completed field.
* Select the service worker who completed the feedback in the Completed By field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Rating** (1): A score out of 5 that indicates the satisfaction level with the client interaction.
	+ Enter a number between 1 and 5 to rate your satisfaction with the client interaction.
* **Rating Scale** (2): The level of satisfaction, ranging from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
	+ Select one of the options: 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.
* **Feedback Type** (3): The type of feedback you are providing.
	+ Select "Client", "Colleague", or "Manager".
* **Description** (4): A brief explanation of the feedback.
	+ Enter a text description of the feedback.
* **Date Completed** (5): The date you completed the feedback.
	+ Enter the date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Time Completed** (6): The time you completed the feedback.
	+ Enter the time in the format "HH:MM".
* **Completed By** (7): The service worker who completed the feedback.
	+ Select "Service Worker 1", "Service Worker 2", or "Service Worker 3".
