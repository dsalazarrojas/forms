# Rental Assistance Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Assistance Program Application Form is a tool for applicants to apply for rental assistance, providing social workers with necessary information to assess and process these applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
3. Provide your City and State in the corresponding fields.
4. Enter your Zip Code in the "Zip Code" field.
5. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
8. Enter your Social Security Number in the "Social Security Number" field.
9. Select your rental agency from the list of available options.
10. Enter your household income in the "Household Income" field.
11. Enter the size of your household in the "Household Size" field.
12. Enter the rent amount you pay in the "Rent Amount" field.
13. Enter the number of months you have been in the rental program in the "Months in Rental Program" field.
14. Enter the total dollar benefit you have received in the "Total Dollar Benefit" field.
15. Select your benefit status as "Active" or "Inactive".
16. Enter the amount of benefit you have received in the "Benefit Amount" field.
17. Enter the number of months you have benefited from the program in the "Months Benefited" field.
18. Enter the date you received the last benefit in the "Last Benefit Received" field.
19. Enter the date your next benefit is scheduled in the "Next Benefit Scheduled" field.
20. Enter the total benefit you have received in the "Total Benefit Received" field.
21. Enter the monthly benefit you receive in the "Monthly Benefit" field.
22. Select whether you receive cash, voucher, both, or other type of benefit.
23. Enter any additional information about cash in the "Cash" field.
24. Enter any additional information about voucher in the "Voucher" field.
25. Enter any additional information about other in the "Other" field.
26. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Address**: Enter your current address where you live.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter your social security number for identification purposes.
* **Rental Agency**: Select your rental agency from the list of available options.
* **Household Income**: Enter your current household income.
* **Household Size**: Enter the number of people living in your household.
* **Rent Amount**: Enter the amount of rent you pay.
* **Months in Rental Program**: Enter the number of months you have been in the rental program.
* **Total Dollar Benefit**: Enter the total dollar amount you have received from the program.
* **Benefit Status**: Select whether you are currently active or inactive in the program.
* **Benefit Amount**: Enter the amount of benefit you have received.
* **Months Benefited**: Enter the number of months you have benefited from the program.
* **Last Benefit Received**: Enter the date you received the last benefit.
* **Next Benefit Scheduled**: Enter the date your next benefit is scheduled.
* **Total Benefit Received**: Enter the total benefit amount you have received.
* **Monthly Benefit**: Enter the amount of monthly benefit you receive.
* **Benefit Type**: Select whether you receive cash, voucher, both, or other type of benefit.
* **Cash**: Enter any additional information about cash benefits you receive.
* **Voucher**: Enter any information about voucher benefits you receive.
* **Other**: Enter any other type of benefit you receive.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
