# Citizen Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Citizen Profile Form is used to collect demographic information from citizens for social services purposes. This form helps to gather essential details about a citizen's background, contact information, and social services needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each select-one field (e.g., gender, employment status, education level, marital status).
3. If you have special needs or accommodations required, please describe them in the "Special Needs or Accommodations Required" field.
4. Ensure that you have the correct contact information for emergency contact persons.
5. Select all social services that you are interested in or require (Select all that apply).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (`First Name`, `text`, **required**): This field is for your given name.
* **Last Name (3)** (`Last Name`, `text`, **required**): This is the last name of the citizen filling out this form.
* **Middle Name (4)** (`Middle Name`, `text`, **optional**): This is the middle name of the citizen filling out this form. If you do not have a middle name, you may leave this field blank.
* **Date of Birth (5)** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, **required**): This is the date of birth of the citizen filling out this form. Enter the date in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Gender (6)** (`Gender`, `select_one`, **required**): Please select your gender identity from the provided options.
* **Citizen ID Number (7)** (`Citizen ID Number`, `text`, **required**): This is the ID number of the citizen filling out this form.
* **Email Address (9)** (`Email Address`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Primary Phone Number (10)** (`Primary Phone Number`, `text`, **required**): Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes.
* **Alternate Phone Number (11)** (`Alternate Phone Number`, `text`, **optional**): This is the alternate phone number of the citizen filling out this form.
* **Street Address (13)** (`Street Address`, `text`, **required**): Enter the street address of the citizen filling out this form.
* **City (14)** (`City`, `text`, **required**): Enter the city name of the citizen filling out this form.
* **State or Province (15)** (`State or Province`, `text`, **required**): Enter the state or province name of the citizen filling out this form.
* **ZIP or Postal Code (16)** (`ZIP or Postal Code`, `text`, **required**): Enter the ZIP or postal code of the citizen filling out this form.
* **Country (17)** (`Country`, `text`, **required**): Enter the country name of the citizen filling out this form.
* **Employment Status (19)** (`Employment Status`, `select_one`, **required**): Please select your employment status from the provided options.
* **Occupation or Job Title (20)** (`Occupation or Job Title`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your occupation or job title.
* **Employer Name (21)** (`Employer Name`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your employer name.
* **Highest Level of Education (23)** (`Highest Level of Education`, `select_one`, **required**): Please select your highest level of education from the provided options.
* **Marital Status (25)** (`Marital Status`, `select_one`, **required**): Please select your marital status from the provided options.
* **Number of Dependents (26)** (`Number of Dependents`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the number of dependents you have.
* **Emergency Contact Name (27)** (`Emergency Contact Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (28)** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Types of Services Needed (30)** (`Types of Services Needed`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select all services you need or are interested in (Select all that apply).
* **Special Needs or Accommodations Required (31)** (`Special Needs or Accommodations Required`, `text`, **optional**): Describe any special needs or accommodations you require.

Note: Fields marked as **required** are mandatory and must be filled out for the form to be considered complete.
