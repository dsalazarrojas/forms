# Gender Based Violence Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reporting incidents of gender-based violence. Its purpose is to collect information about reported incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "Reported Incident Details" page.
2. Fill in all required fields, marked with ***, and any additional information as needed.
3. Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: This is the title of the form, which is automatically populated and cannot be changed.
* **Reported Incident Details**: Please provide a brief description of the incident, including date, time, and any other relevant details.
* **Survivor Involvement**: Indicate if the survivor was present during the incident (Survivor Present), not present (Survivor Absent), or deceased (Survivor Deceased).
* **Perpetrator Involvement**: Indicate if the perpetrator was present during the incident (Perpetrator Present), not present (Perpetrator Absent), or deceased (Perpetrator Deceased).
* **Incident Characteristics**: Please provide any additional information about the incident, such as any witnesses or injuries.
* **Location Details**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Time of Day**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Contact Details**: Enter any contact information for the survivor or other relevant parties.
* **Consent**: Indicate if the survivor gave consent to report this incident.
* **Follow Up**: Indicate if you plan to follow up with the survivor or perpetrator.
* **Next Steps**: Select all relevant next steps for your response, such as following up with the survivor or perpetrator, contacting police or social services.
* **Case Manager**: Enter the email address of the case manager who will be working on this case.
* **Agency**: Enter the name and address of the agency responsible for this case.
* **Agency Name**: Enter the name of the agency responsible for this case.
* **Agency Address**: Enter the address of the agency responsible for this case.
* **Agency Phone**: Enter the phone number of the agency responsible for this case.
* **Agency Email**: Enter the email address of the agency responsible for this case.
* **Survivor Name**: Enter the name of the survivor.
* **Survivor Age**: Enter the age of the survivor.
* **Survivor Gender**: Select the gender of the survivor, if known.
* **Perpetrator Name**: Enter the name of the perpetrator.
* **Perpetrator Gender**: Select the gender of the perpetrator, if known.
* **Perpetrator Age**: Enter the age of the perpetrator.
* **Perpetrator Relationship**: Select all relevant relationships between the perpetrator and the survivor.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name and contact information if submitting on behalf of the survivor.
