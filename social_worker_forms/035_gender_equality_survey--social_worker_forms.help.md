# Gender Equality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on perceptions of gender equality in the workplace. It will help organizations identify areas where they may be contributing to gender inequality and make informed decisions to create a more inclusive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the introduction page to understand the purpose of the survey.
2. Answer the questions on gender treatment, discrimination, support system, and gender balance.
3. Rate your work-life balance on a scale of 1-5.
4. Submit the form.

**Tips**

* Please be honest in your answers to provide accurate information.
* If you experience any form of discrimination or discomfort in the workplace, report it immediately to your HR department or supervisor.
* This form is designed to be completed in under 10 minutes. If you have any questions or need clarification, contact your HR department or a designated person for assistance.
