# Rescue Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rescue Application Form is a tool used by animal rescue organizations and shelters to collect information from adopters and store vital data about adopted pets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the requested information.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Adopters Name
• **Adopters Name (1)**: This field is used to collect the adopter's name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.

### Pet Name
• **Pet Name (2)**: Enter the name of the pet you are adopting.

### Pet Type
• **Pet Type (3)**: Choose the type of pet you are adopting, such as Cat, Dog, or Other.

### Adoption Date
• **Adoption Date (4)**: Enter the date you adopted the pet.

### Adoption Time
• **Adoption Time (5)**: Enter the time you adopted the pet.

### Rescue Organization
• **Rescue Organization (6)**: Enter the name of the rescue organization from which you adopted the pet.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* Double-check the information you provide for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the rescue organization directly.
