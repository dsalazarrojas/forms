# Temporary Custody Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Temporary Custody Form is a tool used to document temporary living arrangements between parents and their children, ensuring everyone involved is aware of their responsibilities and rights in such situations. This form is used to provide a clear understanding of the temporary custody arrangement, including the parents' and child's residences, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Temporary Custody Form" page or link to access the form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information:
   - `parent_1_name`: The name of the primary parent or adult responsible for the child's care.
   - `parent_2_name`: The name of the secondary parent or adult responsible for the child's care (if applicable).
   - `parent_1_residence`: The address where the primary parent or adult responsible for the child's care resides.
   - `parent_2_residence`: The address where the secondary parent or adult responsible for the child's care resides (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Temporary Custody Form**: This is the main form title and description.
* **Parent 1 Name**: The name of the primary parent or adult responsible for the child's care. Please provide the full name of the parent, including their title (e.g., Mr., Ms., Mrs., etc.).
* **Parent 1 Residence**: The address where the primary parent or adult responsible for the child's care resides. This includes the complete address, including the street number and city.
* **Parent 2 Name**: The name of the secondary parent or adult responsible for the child's care (if applicable). Please provide the full name of the parent, including their title (e.g., Mr., Ms., Mrs., etc.).
* **Parent 2 Residence**: The address where the secondary parent or adult responsible for the child's care resides (if applicable). This includes the complete address, including the street number and city.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly, as it will be used to document temporary living arrangements.
* Review and proofread your input before submission to ensure all fields are correctly filled.
* If you are unsure about any field or need help, contact the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a professional.
* This form can be used as a starting point for further discussion or agreement on custody arrangements between parents or adults.
* Store this form securely and confidentially, and only share it with authorized parties.
* This form is not a substitute for legal advice or a court order. Consult a professional if you need formal or binding agreements.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions, please contact `form@gic.mx`.
