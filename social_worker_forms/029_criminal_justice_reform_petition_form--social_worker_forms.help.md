# Criminal Justice Reform Petition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect input from individuals on the topic of criminal justice reform. We value your feedback and suggestions and would like to hear your thoughts on how to improve the criminal justice system.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit your feedback, simply fill out this form by providing the required information. If you need help with any of the fields, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (contact-name, text, required): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Contact Email** (contact-email, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and responses to your feedback.
* **Contact Telephone** (contact-telephone, text, required): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates and responses to your feedback.
* **Contact Address** (contact-address, text, required): Enter your full address so we can respond to your feedback.
* **Contact City** (contact-city, text, required): Enter your city of residence.
* **Contact State** (contact-state, text, required): Enter your state of residence.
* **Contact Zip** (contact-zip, number, required): Enter your zip code to help us respond to your feedback.
* **Petition Message** (petition-message, note, required): Please provide a clear and concise message on why you support or oppose criminal justice reform.
* **Criminal Justice Issue** (criminal-justice-issue, select_one, required): Select the issue you are passionate about:
	+ Racial Profiling
	+ Mandatory Minimum Sentences
	+ Police Misconduct
	+ False Imprisonment
	+ Overly Broad Sentencing
* **Additional Information** (additional-information, note, required): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields to avoid your submission being rejected.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact us at [insert contact information].
* We value your feedback and look forward to hearing your thoughts on criminal justice reform.
