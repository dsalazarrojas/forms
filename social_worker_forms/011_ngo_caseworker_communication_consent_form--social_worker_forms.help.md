# NGO Caseworker Communication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The NGO Caseworker Communication Consent Form is designed to be completed by caseworkers who interact with individuals who are or may be in contact with children or vulnerable adults, such as foster parents or caseworkers who visit homes or conduct home visits. This form is a critical tool for maintaining confidentiality, ensuring informed consent and transparency in communication with the individuals they serve. Completing this form ensures that you, as a caseworker, have obtained the necessary consent from individuals about how you will communicate with them, which helps prevent misunderstandings, miscommunication, and potential legal issues.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**: Complete all the fields with the required information.
2. **Review the form carefully**: Double-check all the fields to ensure that you have filled out the correct information.
3. **Save and submit the form**: Once you have filled out the form, click "Submit" to save and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to enter your contact information, such as your name, title, and organization.
* **Consent to Communication** (`consent_to_communication`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more methods by which you are willing to communicate with the individual. Options include Phone, Email, SMS, and Mail.
* **Consent to Email** (`consent_to_email`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more methods by which you are willing to communicate with the individual via email. Options include Email and Mail.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Mail** (`mail`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more methods by which you are willing to communicate with the individual via mail. Options include Mail and Other.
* **Consent to SMS** (`consent_to_sms`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more methods by which you are willing to communicate with the individual via SMS. Options include SMS and Other.
* **Mail Frequency** (`mail_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select how frequently you are willing to communicate with the individual via mail. Options include Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Email Frequency** (`email_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select how frequently you are willing to communicate with the individual via email. Options include Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Mail Address** (`mail_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the mail address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Mail** (`emergency_contact_mail`, `text`, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Other Contacts** (`other_contacts`, `text`, optional): Enter any other contacts who should be notified in case of an emergency.
* **Other Contacts Relationship** (`other_contacts_relationship`, `text`, optional): Describe the relationship with the other contacts.
* **Other Contacts Phone** (`other_contacts_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of other contacts.
* **Other Contacts Mail** (`other_contacts_mail`, `text`, optional): Enter the email address of other contacts.
* **Other Contacts Address** (`other_contacts_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the mail address of other contacts.
* **Emergency Contacts List** (`emergency_contacts_list`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select if you want to create a list of emergency contacts. Options include True or False.
* **Consent to Share** (`consent_to_share`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select if you are willing to share information with others. Options include True or False.
* **Consent to Share Details** (`consent_to_share_details`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select if you are willing to share details with others. Options include True or False.
* **Consent to Share with** (`consent_to_share_with`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select with whom you are willing to share information. Options include Self or Others.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any other comments about your communication preferences.

Note: The optional fields can be left blank if you do not want to provide that information.
