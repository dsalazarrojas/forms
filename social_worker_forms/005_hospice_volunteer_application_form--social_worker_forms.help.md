# Hospice Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals who wish to volunteer their time to support hospice care. It will help us understand their availability, role, and frequency of volunteering, allowing us to match them with suitable volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
- Select "Other" as an option for "Volunteer Role" if your role does not match any of the provided options.
- Choose one or more days you are available to volunteer from the list of days of the week.
- Select how often you would like to volunteer from the options: "Once", "Ongoing", or "As needed".
- If you have any relevant comments or information, please share them in the "Comments" field.
- Please sign the "Signature" field to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
  - Required: No, but please enter your name for identification purposes.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
  - Required: No, but please enter your last name for identification purposes.
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
  - Required: No, but please enter your email address so we can contact you about volunteer opportunities.
- **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
  - Required: No, but please enter your phone number so we can contact you about volunteer opportunities.
- **Volunteer Role**: Select the role you are applying for:
  - Required: Yes, please choose one of the following options: Volunteer, Caregiver, Support, or Other (specify).
- **Availability**: Choose one or more days you are available to volunteer:
  - Required: Yes, please choose at least one day.
- **Volunteer Hours**: Enter the number of hours you can volunteer per session.
  - Required: No, but please be honest about your availability.
- **Volunteer Start Date**: Enter the date you are able to start volunteering.
  - Required: No, but please enter a start date so we can plan volunteer opportunities.
- **Volunteer End Date**: Enter the date you plan to stop volunteering.
  - Required: No, but please enter an end date so we can plan volunteer opportunities.
- **Volunteer Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to volunteer:
  - Required: Yes, please choose one of the following options: Once, Ongoing, or As needed.
- **Volunteer Duties**: If you have any additional information about your volunteer duties, please share.
  - Required: No, but please use this space to provide any additional context.
- **Comments**: Share any additional information you feel is relevant to your application.
  - Required: No, but please use this space to provide any additional context.
- **Signature**: Sign your application to confirm your agreement with the terms.
  - Required: Yes, please sign to confirm your application.
- **Confirm Signature**: Confirm your signature by re-entering your name.
  - Required: Yes, please re-enter your name to confirm your signature.
