# Home Care Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Care Inquiry Form is a tool used to collect information about individuals in need of home care services. This form helps us understand their personal and care requirements, enabling us to provide the necessary support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide your Address, City, and State to help us understand your location.
4. Enter your Zip code for further location identification.
5. Specify the Services you need, describing them briefly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This field is required to capture your given name at birth or the name you prefer to be known as.
* **Last Name**: This field is necessary to record your surname as per your identification documents.
* **Email**: Please enter a valid Email address for contact and communication purposes. This could be either your personal or work email address.
* **Phone**: This is your contact phone number for further communication and assistance.
* **Address**: Provide your physical address or the address where you would like to receive care services.
* **City**: Enter the city where you live or the city where you require care services.
* **State**: Select your state of residence or the state where you require care services.
* **Zip**: Enter your postal zip code for further location identification.
* **Services needed**: Describe the specific services you require for home care in this field. Be as detailed as possible to enable us to provide you with the right support.
