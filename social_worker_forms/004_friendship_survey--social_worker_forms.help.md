# Friendship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Friendship Survey form is designed to gather information about relationships among students, teachers, and peers. It helps identify areas of support and improvement opportunities in friendships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a title for the survey (if required).
2. Select your responses to the questions about your friendships and relationships.
3. Rate the quality of your friendships.
4. Provide any additional feedback for survey improvement.
5. Submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Friendship Survey** (`survey_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a title for the survey. This title will be used to identify your survey.
* **How do you feel about your friends?** (`friend_survey_questions`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **What support do you need from friends?** (`friend_support_needs`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **What support do you provide to friends?** (`friend_support_provided`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Relationship with friends** (`student_relationships`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **What support do you need from teachers?** (`student_support_needs`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **What support do you provide to students?** (`teacher_support_provided`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Quality of friendship** (`friend_quality`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one:
	+ Good
	+ Poor
* **How can you improve the survey?** (`survey_instructions`, text, required/optional): Enter any suggestions for improving the survey.
* **Form Submissions** (`form_submissions`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips
Remember, the more honest and accurate your responses, the better we can understand your friendships and relationships. If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please use the "How can you improve the survey?" field.
