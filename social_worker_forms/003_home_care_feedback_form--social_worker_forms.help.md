# Home Care Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Care Feedback Form is a tool for patients to provide feedback on their experience with our care services. Your input will help us improve our services and better serve our patients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as you need to answer the question.
3. If a field is not required, you can skip it if you are unable to provide an answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Feedback (1)** (`patient_feedback`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any comments or concerns you have about your experience with our care services. Please be as honest and detailed as possible.
* **Care Services (2)** (`care_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the care services you received during your visit. For example, if you received counseling, case management, or medication support, select them from the list.
* **Rating (3)** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with our care services on a scale of 1-5.
* **Date of Visit (4)** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): If you can remember, please provide the date you visited our office.
* **Time of Visit (5)** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): If you can remember, please provide the time you visited our office.
* **Social Worker (6)** (`social_worker`, select_one, optional): Please select the social worker who provided your care services. This will help us to track any issues or concerns they may have raised during your visit.
* **Comments (7)** (`comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them in this field.
* **Submit (8)** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Click the submit button to send your feedback to us.

## Tips
* Please be honest and detailed in your feedback.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask us for clarification.
* You can skip any fields you are unable to answer, but please note that we may not be able to follow up on your feedback without the necessary information.
