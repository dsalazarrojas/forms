# Domestic Violence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about students who have experienced domestic violence. The data collected will help the school administration track and monitor domestic violence incidents and provide support to students in need.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For select multiple options, choose all that apply.
3. For select one options, choose only one option.
4. Enter any additional information you have about the student's situation in the text field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Surveyed (1)**: Please indicate how many students have reported domestic violence incidents.
* **Victimized Family (2)**: Check all that apply to indicate if the victim's family is involved in the domestic violence incident. 
  - **Yes**
  - **No**
* **Perpetrator (3)**: Choose the person responsible for the domestic violence incident. 
  - **Victim's father**
  - **Victim's mother**
  - **Other**
* **Perpetrator Location (5)**: Check all that apply to indicate the location of the perpetrator of the domestic violence incident. 
  - **Home**
  - **Other**
* **School Location (6)**: Check all that apply to indicate the location of the incident at school. 
  - **School**
  - **Home**
  - **Other**
* **Other Info (7)**: Enter any additional information about the student's situation that may be relevant to the school administration.
* **Perpetrator Location 2 (8)**: Please enter the location of the perpetrator of the domestic violence incident.
* **School Location 2 (Not present in the schema)**: Not applicable, please ignore.
* **Religion (4)**: Not applicable, please ignore.
* **Other Info 2 (Not present in the schema)**: Not applicable, please ignore. 

Note: This form is for tracking purpose only. Any information entered will not be shared with anyone and is only for internal use.
