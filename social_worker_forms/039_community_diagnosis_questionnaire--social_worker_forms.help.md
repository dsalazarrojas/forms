# Community Diagnosis Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this questionnaire is to collect information about the community you work with, including your role within the community, major challenges faced by the community, vulnerable groups that require support, service gaps that exist, existing strengths within the community, and priority actions recommended to address these challenges. This information will help in understanding the needs and potential areas of improvement in the community.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your role within the community from the options provided on Page 1.
2. On Page 1, list the major challenges you have observed or are aware of within the community.
3. Identify vulnerable groups that require support on Page 1.
4. If you have observed any service gaps, provide a list on Page 1.
5. List any existing strengths or assets within the community that can be built upon on Page 2.
6. On Page 2, provide a short list of priority actions that you recommend to address the challenges faced by the community.
7. If you wish to be contacted for further questions or additional support, provide your email address on Page 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Respondent role in community** (`respondent_role`, `text`, required): This field requires you to select your role within the community, e.g., resident service provider.
- **Major community challenges** (`major_challenges`, `text`, required): Here, provide a list of the top challenges faced by the community in your view.
- **Vulnerable groups in community** (`vulnerable_groups`, `text`, optional): Identify any groups within the community that are in need of support.
- **Service gaps observed** (`service_gaps`, `text`, optional): List any services that are currently lacking within the community.
- **Existing community strengths** (`existing_strengths`, `text`, optional): Mention any existing assets or resources within the community that can be built upon.
- **Priority actions recommended** (`priority_actions`, `text`, required): Provide a short list of actions you recommend to address the community's challenges.
- **Contact for follow up** (`contact_for_follow_up`, `email`, optional): If you wish to be contacted for further questions or support, provide your email address here.

## Tips

* Please answer these questions based on your experience and knowledge of the community.
* Be as specific as possible when describing the challenges, vulnerable groups, service gaps, existing strengths, and priority actions.
* Use bullet points or short sentences to make it easier to read and understand.
* If you have any additional information or clarification that you would like to add, please feel free to do so in the email you provide in the "Contact for follow up" field.
