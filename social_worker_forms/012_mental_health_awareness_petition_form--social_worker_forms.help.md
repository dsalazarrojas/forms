# Mental Health Awareness Petition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect mental health awareness and support requests from individuals and organizations. The purpose of this form is to gather information about your mental health concerns, your organization's or company's name, and your contact details. This information will help us create targeted awareness campaigns and provide tailored support to promote mental health well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank if you do not have the information or prefer not to provide it.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Please enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Organization (3)**: If you are representing an organization, enter its name here. If you are an individual, you can leave this field blank.
* **Email (4)**: Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you with any updates or follow-ups.
* **Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number. This will be used to contact you with any urgent or time-sensitive matters.
* **Comments (6)**: Use this field to share your mental health awareness and support concerns, as well as any relevant context or information.
* **Signature Date (7)**: Enter the date you are signing this petition. This will help us track when you signed up.

## Tips

* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Ensure your email address and phone number are accurate to ensure you can be reached.
* If you are representing an organization, please ensure you have permission to provide this information on behalf of the organization.
