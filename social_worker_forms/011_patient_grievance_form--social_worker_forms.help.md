# Patient Grievance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Grievance Form is used to document and report concerns or complaints made by patients. This form helps us understand and address your concerns, ensuring that we can provide better care and service to you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Provide your name and date of birth.
2. Enter your contact information (phone number and email).
3. Describe your concern or complaint in detail.
4. Submit the form to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your address for contact or follow-up purposes, please enter it here. If not, you can leave it blank.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted via email, please enter your email address. If not, you can leave it blank.
* **Description of Complaint** (`complaint_description`, text, required): Explain your concern or complaint in as much detail as possible, so we can better understand and address your issue.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your concern or complaint.
* If you have any supporting documents or evidence, please attach them with your form.
* We'll review and respond to your form as soon as possible. If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us.
