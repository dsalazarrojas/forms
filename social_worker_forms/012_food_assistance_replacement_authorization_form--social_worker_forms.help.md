# Food Assistance Replacement Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a Social Worker's authorization for a food assistance program.

## How To Complete This Form

### Page 1: Applicant Information

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_information`, text, required): Enter the name and any other relevant details about the applicant.
* **Household Members** (`household_members`, text, required): List the members of the applicant's household.
* **Case Manager Information** (`case_manager_information`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the case manager.

### Page 2: Request Information

* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, required): Enter a brief description of why the applicant is requesting a food assistance replacement card.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date the applicant made the request.
* **Benefit Card Status** (`benefit_card_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the benefit card, which can be active, inactive, lost, or stolen.
* **Reason for Replacement** (`replacement_reason`, text, required): Enter the reason for requesting a replacement card.
* **Replacement Card Request** (`replacement_card_request`, select_multiple, required): Select whether the applicant is requesting a new card, a replacement card, or both.
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form is being certified.
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or authorization notes.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the applicant.

### Page 3: Certification Information

* **Certifying Officer Name** (`certifying_officer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the certifying officer.
* **Certifying Officer Title** (`certifying_officer_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the certifying officer.
* **Certifying Officer Date** (`certifying_officer_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form is certified.
* **Certifying Officer Signature** (`certifying_officer_signature`, text, required): Enter the signature of the certifying officer.
* **Certifying Officer Title Abbreviation** (`certifying_officer_title_abbreviation`, text, required): Enter the abbreviation of the certifying officer's title.
* **Certifying Officer Supervisor Signature** (`certifying_officer_supervisor_signature`, text, required): Enter the signature of the certifying officer's supervisor.
* **Certifying Officer Supervisor Title** (`certifying_officer_supervisor_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the certifying officer's supervisor.
