# Temporary Guardianship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for providing information about temporary guardianship. It's likely used for situations where a person is unable to manage their own affairs and needs temporary assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *) with the correct information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you've provided accurate and complete information.
3. If you're unsure about any field, ask for help or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`temporary_guardianship_form_1_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the individual who requires temporary guardianship.
* **Email** (`temporary_guardianship_form_1_email`, email, required: false): Enter the individual's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`temporary_guardianship_form_1_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the individual's phone number (if applicable).
* **Date of Birth** (`temporary_guardianship_form_1_date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the individual's date of birth.
* **Guardianship Type** (`temporary_guardianship_form_1_guardianship_type`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose one or more of the following options:
		- Adult
		- Emergency
		- Limited
		- Standby
* **Reason for Guardianship** (`temporary_guardianship_form_1_guardianship_reason`, text, required: false): Enter the reason why temporary guardianship is required.
* **Start Date** (`temporary_guardianship_form_1_guardianship_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the temporary guardianship.
* **End Date** (`temporary_guardianship_form_1_guardianship_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the temporary guardianship.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date for the individual.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask for help or clarification.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required information is provided.
