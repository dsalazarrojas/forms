# Social Worker Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Worker Application Form is designed to collect information from social workers applying to work with vulnerable populations. The form helps the evaluation committee assess the applicant's suitability and qualifications for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field completely and accurately, taking into account the hints and explanations provided for each field.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields, as indicated by the label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Applicant Information** (text, required)
* A brief description of yourself, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

### 2. **Work Experience** (select multiple, required)
* Please select all relevant work experiences, including previous positions, roles, or relevant volunteer work.

### 3. **Education Background** (text, required)
* Describe your educational background, including degrees, diplomas, or certifications related to social work.

### 4. **Skills** (select one, required)
* Indicate your level of proficiency in various social work-related skills, such as communication, empathy, or problem-solving.

### 5. **Certifications** (text, required)
* List any relevant social work certifications or licenses you hold.

### 6. **Personal Info** (number, required)
* Enter your age, as this field will be used to verify your identity.

### 7. **Contact Info** (email, required)
* Provide your email address for contact and follow-up purposes.

### 8. **Submit** (text, required)
* This field is the final step. Make sure to review and submit your application carefully.

### 9. **Date of Birth** (date, required)
* Enter your date of birth to verify your age.

### 10. **Time of Birth** (time, required)
* Enter your time of birth to verify your time of day.

### 11. **Notes** (note, optional)
* This field is optional, but can be used to provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your application.

### 11. **Submit** (text, required)
* This is the final submit button. Make sure to review all information before submitting.
