# responsive_share_your_story_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to share their personal stories and experiences with our services. Your feedback and insights will help us improve and provide better services for you and others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form completely, but you do not have to answer all questions if you do not have information to provide.
* For any field that is optional, you can skip it if you do not have the information requested.
* Please be as detailed as possible in your responses, but do not worry about grammar or spelling mistakes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can briefly introduce yourself and provide context for your story.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `number`, required: false): This field is asking how long you have been working with our services.
* **Your Story** (`your_story`, `text`, required: false): This is the main section where you can share your story and experiences with our services. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): If you have any feedback or comments about our services, you can provide them here.
* **Services** (`services`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the services you have used from our options:
  * Service 1
  * Service 2
  * Service 3
* **Date of Experience** (`date_of_experience`, `date`, required: false): This field is for you to provide the date when you experienced the service.
* **Time of Experience** (`time_of_experience`, `time`, required: false): This field is for you to provide the time of experience (optional).
* **Any Other Info** (`any_other_info`, `note`, required: false): If you have any other information you want to share, you can do so here.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your responses.
* Please respect the time and effort of our staff who review your submissions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your organization's contact information.
