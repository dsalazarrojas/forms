# Young Women's Program Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Young Women's Program Referral Form is used to refer young women to the program for support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *)
2. Enter accurate and up-to-date information
3. Review and verify the information before submitting the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the school name?** (`form_title`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the school that is referring the student to the program.
* **contact_name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the contact person who is referring the student to the program.
* **Program Officer** (`program_officer_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the program officer who is overseeing the program.
* **Date of Referral** (`date_of_referral`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date when the referral was made.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the source of the referral.
* **Source of Referral** (`referral_source_2`, text, optional): This is a duplicate field of Referral Source, used for consistency and redundancy.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source_3`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the source of the referral again.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the student being referred to the program.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): This field is used to enter the email of the contact person who is referring the student to the program.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the phone number of the contact person who is referring the student to the program.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the program.
* **Program Officer Email** (`program_officer_email`, email, optional): This field is used to enter the email of the program officer who is overseeing the program.
* **Program Officer Phone** (`program_officer_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the phone number of the program officer who is overseeing the program.
* **What type of Referral?** (`referral_type`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the type of referral.
* **Other Referral Type** (`other_referral_type`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the type of referral if it's not one of the listed options.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the grade level of the student.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the start date of the program.
* **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, optional): This field is used to enter the date when the referral was made.
* **Time of Referral** (`time_of_referral`, time, optional): This field is used to enter the time of the referral.
* **Program Status** (`program_status`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the status of the program.
* **Other Program Status** (`program_status_2`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select multiple status of the program.
* **Student Gender** (`student_gender`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the gender of the student.
* **Contact Name 2** (`contact_name_2`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the contact person who is referring the student to the program.
* **Program Notes** (`program_notes`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any additional notes about the program.
* **Referral Source 4** (`referral_source_4`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the source of the referral again.

## Tips
Please ensure that you are a valid referrer and that the referral is not already in our database to avoid duplication.
