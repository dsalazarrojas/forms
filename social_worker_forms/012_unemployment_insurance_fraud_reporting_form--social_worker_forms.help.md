# Unemployment Insurance Fraud Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report potential unemployment insurance fraud cases. It collects information about a suspect employee, their employment history, and the reporter's details.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the suspect employee and the fraud case.
2.  Provide a clear and concise explanation of the reason for reporting.
3.  Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (`employee_name`):** Enter the suspect employee's name as it appears on their records.
* **Employee ID (`employee_id`):** Enter the suspect employee's identification number or ID.
* **Reason for Report (`reason_for_report`):** Provide a clear and concise explanation of why you're reporting this case.
* **Suspect Employee Name (`suspect_employee_name`):** Enter the name of the suspect employee.
* **Suspect Employee ID (`suspect_employee_id`):** Enter the ID of the suspect employee.
* **Suspect Reason (`suspect_reason`):** Explain why you suspect this employee of committing fraud.
* **Suspect Last Date (`suspect_last_date`):** Enter the last date of the suspect's employment.
* **Suspect First Date (`suspect_first_date`):** Enter the first date of the suspect's employment.
* **Suspect Job Title (`suspect_job_title`):** (Optional) Enter the suspect's job title.
* **Suspect Department (`suspect_department`):** (Optional) Enter the suspect's department.
* **Suspect Work Location (`suspect_work_location`):** (Optional) Enter the suspect's work location.
* **Suspect Supervisor (`suspect_supervisor`):** (Optional) Enter the suspect's supervisor's name.
* **Suspect Manager (`suspect_manager`):** (Optional) Enter the suspect's manager's name.
* **Suspect Reported By (`suspect_reported_by`):** (Optional) Enter the person who reported this case.
* **Suspect Other (`suspect_other`):** (Optional) Enter any additional details about the suspect.
* **Employer Name (`employer_name`):** Enter the name of the employer involved.
* **Employer ID (`employer_id`):** Enter the ID of the employer involved.
* **Employer Address (`employer_address`):** Enter the employer's address.
* **Employer City (`employer_city`):** Enter the city of the employer's address.
* **Employer State (`employer_state`):** Enter the state of the employer's address.
* **Employer Zip (`employer_zip`):** Enter the zip code of the employer's address.
* **Report Date (`report_date`):** (Optional) Enter the date of submission.
* **Reported By (`reported_by`):** Enter your name and contact information.
* **Comments (`comments`):** Provide any additional comments or information about the case.

## Tips

*   Be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out this form.
*   If you're unsure about any information, contact our department for guidance.
*   Do not submit duplicate reports for the same case.
*   If you've already reported a case involving the same suspect employee, contact our department directly for updates.
