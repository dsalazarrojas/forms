# Whanau Ora Funding Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Whanau Ora Funding Application Form is designed to collect information for social workers applying for funding. It is a single application form that should not be completed multiple times for the same funding purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all fields where applicable. If a field is not applicable, simply leave it blank.
2. Ensure the "Organization Name" field is filled in with your organization's name.
3. Enter your organization's address in the "Address" field.
4. In the "Contact Person" field, enter your name.
5. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. In the "Purpose of Funding" field, briefly explain the reason for your funding application.
8. In the "Required Documents" field, list any supporting documents you are providing with your application.
9. Enter the total amount of funding required in the "Total Funding Required" field.
10. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have selected one of the options in the "Select One Option" field.
11. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have selected multiple of the options in the "Select Multiple" field.
12. Enter a valid date in the "Date" field.
13. Enter a valid time in the "Time" field.
14. In the "Note" field, add any additional comments about your application.
15. Enter your email address in the "Email Option" field.
16. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Option" field.
17. Add any additional comments in the "Note Option" field.
18. Enter a valid date in the "Date Option" field.
19. Enter a valid time in the "Time Option" field.
20. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have selected multiple of the options in the "Select Multiple Option" field.
21. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have selected one of the options in the "Select One Option 2" field.
22. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have selected multiple of the options in the "Select Multiple Option 2" field.
23. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have selected multiple of the options in the "Select Multiple Option 3" field.
24. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have selected one of the options in the "Select One Option 3" field.
25. Enter a valid date in the "Date Option 2" field.
