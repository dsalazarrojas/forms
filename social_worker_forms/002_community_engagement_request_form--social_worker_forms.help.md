## Community Engagement Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Community Engagement Request Form is used to collect information about community engagement requests from organizations or individuals. The form helps the community engagement team to better understand the type of engagement, expected attendance, and resources required.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Organization or individual name** field with the name of the requesting entity (organization or person).
2. Enter the **Contact person full name** of the primary contact for this request.
3. Provide the **Contact email** for correspondence and follow-up.
4. Optionally, fill in the **Contact phone** number if applicable.
5. Select the **Type of engagement requested** from the available options (Workshop, Presentation, Community meeting, Volunteer support, or Other).
6. Enter the **Proposed date** for the engagement (optional).
7. Estimate the **Number of participants expected** for the engagement.
8. List the **Resources required** for the engagement (e.g., AV equipment, space, materials, volunteers).
9. Briefly describe the **Purpose and goals** of the engagement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization or individual name**: Enter the name of the requesting group or person.
* **Contact person full name**: Enter the full name of the primary contact for this request.
* **Contact email**: Enter the email address best suited for correspondence and follow-up.
* **Contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the primary contact (optional).
* **Type of engagement requested**: Select the type of engagement you would like to request. Options include:
	+ Workshop: A structured presentation and discussion on a specific topic.
	+ Presentation: A shorter presentation or talk on a specific topic.
	+ Community meeting: A meeting for a group or community discussion.
	+ Volunteer support: Requesting volunteers for a specific project or event.
	+ Other: Specify a different type of engagement in the purpose and goals field.
* **Proposed date**: Choose a date for the engagement. This helps the engagement team plan and schedule accordingly.
* **Number of participants expected**: Estimate the number of people expected to attend the engagement.
* **Resources required**: List the resources needed for the engagement, such as:
	+ AV equipment
	+ Space
	+ Materials
	+ Volunteers
* **Purpose and goals**: Briefly describe the objectives and goals of the engagement.

## Tips
Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible to ensure the engagement team can efficiently process and respond to your request. If you have any additional information or details that may be relevant, please include them in the purpose and goals field.

This help guide is here to support you in completing the Community Engagement Request Form. If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to contact the engagement team for assistance.
