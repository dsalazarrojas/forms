# family_management_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about family dynamics, relationships, and management styles within a family unit. It's designed to be completed by family members or the family unit as a whole.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read each question and select the most accurate response for your family.
2. Answer all questions honestly, as your input will help social workers or counselors better understand your family's dynamics.
3. If a question doesn't apply to your family, select "Other" or "Not applicable" when prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **family_dynamics** (`family_dynamics`, note, required: false): Briefly describe the current dynamics within your family.
* **family_members** (`family_members`, select_multiple, required: false): List all members of your family.
* **family_relationships** (`family_relationships`, select_one, required: false): Choose one relationship that best describes your family relationships.
* **family_goals** (`family_goals`, text, required: false): Describe your family's short-term and long-term goals.
* **family_practices** (`family_practices`, text, required: false): Describe any regular practices or routines within your family.
* **management_styles** (`management_styles`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all management styles currently used within your family.
* **family_dynamics_2** (`family_dynamics_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer "Yes" if any of the listed dynamics apply to your family.
* **family_relationships_2** (`family_relationships_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer "Yes" if any of the listed relationships apply to your family.
* **management_styles_2** (`management_styles_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer "Yes" if any of the listed management styles apply to your family.
* **family_dynamics_3** (`family_dynamics_3`, note, required: false): Briefly describe any additional family dynamics that don't fit in the other fields.
* **family_stresses** (`family_stresses`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all stressors currently affecting your family.
* **support_systems** (`support_systems`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether or not your family has a support system.
* **family_counseling** (`family_counseling`, date, required: false): Provide the date of your last family counseling session (if applicable).
* **family_counseling_frequency** (`family_counseling_frequency`, time, required: false): Report the frequency of your family counseling sessions (if applicable).
* **family_counseling_duration** (`family_counseling_duration`, text, required: false): Describe the duration of your family counseling sessions (if applicable).
* **family_dynamics_2** (`family_dynamics_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer "Yes" if any of the listed dynamics apply to your family.
* **family_relationships_2** (`family_relationships_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer "Yes" if any of the listed relationships apply to your family.
* **management_styles_2** (`management_styles_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer "Yes" if any of the listed management styles apply to your family.

## Tips

- Answer honestly and thoroughly, as your input will help social workers or counselors better understand your family's situation.
- If a question doesn't apply to your family, select "Other" or "Not applicable" to avoid confusion.
- Review each question carefully and take your time to provide accurate information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us for assistance.
