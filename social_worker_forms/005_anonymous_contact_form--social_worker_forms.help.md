# Anonymous Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anonymous Contact Form is a tool for collecting anonymous feedback, inquiries, or comments from users. It is designed to be a safe and secure way for users to reach out without revealing their identity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Select the subject of your message.
3. Enter a brief description of your inquiry or feedback.
4. Choose your preferred contact method (optional).
5. If you selected a contact method, select the best time to contact you.
6. (Optional) Choose the department or service of interest to help direct your request.
7. (Optional) Select the urgency level of your request.
8. Provide any additional notes or information to help respond to your query.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subject** (`Subject`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your message.
* **Message or inquiry** (`Message or inquiry`, `text`, required): Provide details of your inquiry or feedback.
* **Preferred contact method** (`Preferred contact method`, `select_one`, optional): Leave blank to remain anonymous, or select one of the following options:
	+ No contact please
	+ Email follow up
	+ Phone follow up
* **Best time to contact** (`Best time to contact`, `time`, optional): If you selected a contact method, choose the best time to contact you. Please format the time using 24-hour clock (e.g., 09:00).
* **Department or service of interest** (`Department or service of interest`, `text`, optional): This will help direct your request to the relevant department or service.
* **Urgency level** (`Urgency level`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options to help prioritize your response:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
* **Additional notes** (`Additional notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any further information to help respond to your query.

## Tips
* Please be respectful and considerate of the person responding to your query.
* Use a clear and concise language to ensure your message is easy to understand.
* Do not use this form for commercial purposes or to spam our team.
* All responses will be reviewed and prioritized based on their urgency level and department or service selected.
* Your identity will remain anonymous unless you choose to be contacted.
