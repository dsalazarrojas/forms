# Townhouse Development Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for residents to provide feedback on their experience with the townhouse development process. Your feedback will help us understand what works well and what areas need improvement, enabling us to create a better living environment for all.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand each question and field.
2. Answer all fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. For select multiple and select one fields, select all that apply.
4. For the email field, enter your email address.
5. For the note fields, provide a detailed description of your thoughts and concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Resident Information**: This field is for your name and contact information. Enter your name and email address to help us contact you if needed.
- **Questions**: Indicate whether you have any questions or concerns regarding the development. Select all that apply.
- **Satisfaction Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the development on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
- **Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the development.
- **Contact Information**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
- **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or concerns you have about the development.
- **Questions 2**: Indicate whether you have any additional questions or concerns regarding the development.
- **Contact Information 2**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
- **Comments 2**: Provide any additional comments or concerns you have about the development.
- **Suggestions 2**: Provide any additional suggestions or ideas you have for improving the development.
- **Satisfaction Rating 2**: Rate your satisfaction with the development on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
- **Questions 3**: Indicate whether you have any additional questions or concerns regarding the development.

## Tips
- Please provide honest and detailed feedback to help us improve the development.
- If you have any additional concerns or suggestions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We appreciate your feedback and thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
