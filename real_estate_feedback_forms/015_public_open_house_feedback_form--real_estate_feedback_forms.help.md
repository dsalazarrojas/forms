# Public Open House Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from visitors who have attended an open house event. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the attendee's experience, provide a rating and comments about the event, and collect contact information for follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Open House Details field with any relevant information about the event you attended.
2. Enter your Contact Info (email address) in the Contact Info field.
3. If you have any questions or comments, type them in the Question field.
4. Provide your feedback about the event in the Feedback field.
5. Rate the Open House Rating field to indicate your satisfaction with the event.
6. Enter a score in the Rating Score field to further elaborate on your rating.
7. Type any additional comments or suggestions in the Open House Comments field.
8. Enter your contact number in the Contact Number field.
9. Enter your contact email address in the Contact Email field.
10. Finally, enter the location of the Open House in the Open House Location field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Open House Details**: Enter any relevant information about the open house event you attended, such as the date and time it took place.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you.
* **Question**: If you have any questions or concerns about the event, type them here.
* **Feedback**: Provide your thoughts and opinions about the open house event.
* **Open House Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-10.
* **Rating Score**: Enter a score to further elaborate on your rating, if needed.
* **Open House Comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about the event.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number so that we can follow up with you.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you.
* **Open House Location**: Enter the location of the open house event, if different from the one listed on the form.
