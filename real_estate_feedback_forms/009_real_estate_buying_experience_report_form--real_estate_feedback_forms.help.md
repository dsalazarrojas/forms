# Real Estate Buying Experience Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their real estate buying experience. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and share your thoughts on your recent purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your home location, where you purchased the property. This will help us understand the local market and services we offer.
2. Select the type of home you purchased (Condo, Detached, Townhouse, or Apartment).
3. Enter the purchase price of the property (if applicable).
4. Provide the date you completed the purchase.
5. If you worked with a realtor, please provide their name and rate their performance (1-5).
6. Evaluate the communication you received from us (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
7. Rate the quality of the home inspection (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
8. Rate the closing process (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
9. Once you have completed these fields, click submit and we will receive your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Location**: Enter the city, state, or province where you purchased your property. If you're unsure, please check the address label you received with your property documents.
* **Home Type**: Select the type of property you purchased, such as Condo, Detached, Townhouse, or Apartment.
* **Purchase Price**: Enter the price you paid for your property, if applicable.
* **Purchase Date**: Enter the date you completed the purchase of your property. 
* **Realtor Name**: If you had a realtor assist you during the purchasing process, please enter their name. This will help us understand who provided excellent service.
* **Realtor Rating**: Rate your realtor's performance on a scale of 1-5. This will help us evaluate their effectiveness.
* **Communication**: Evaluate the communication you received from us (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Home Inspection**: Rate the quality of the home inspection (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor). A good inspection can help avoid costly repairs down the line.
* **Closing Process**: Rate the closing process (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor). This will help us identify any areas for improvement.
* **Tips**: Take the time to provide detailed feedback, and don't hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
