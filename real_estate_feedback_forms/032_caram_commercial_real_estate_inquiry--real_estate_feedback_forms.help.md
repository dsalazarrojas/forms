# CaraM Commercial Real Estate Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients in need of commercial real estate solutions. It helps us understand their specific requirements and preferences to provide tailored recommendations and support in finding the right commercial space.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your business information, such as the name of your company and type of business or organization.
3. Specify the type of property you are looking for, including the desired property type, location, and square footage requirements.
4. Share your financial information, including your budget range and preferred financing method.
5. Indicate your timeline for occupying the space and preferred lease term.
6. If applicable, provide details about your growth plans and expansion timeline.
7. Finally, add any additional information or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your name**: Your name, please.
* **Email address**: Your email address for communication.
* **Phone number**: Your phone number for follow-up calls or discussions.
* **Company name**: If applicable, provide your company's name.
* **Type of business or organization**: Select the type of business or organization you represent (e.g., Retail, Office, Industrial, etc.).
* **Desired property type**: Choose the type of property you are looking for (e.g., Office Space, Retail Space, Industrial Building, etc.).
* **Preferred location or area**: Specify the location or area you prefer.
* **Required square footage**: If you have a specific square footage requirement, please provide it.
* **Minimum square footage**: If you have a minimum square footage requirement, please provide it.
* **Maximum square footage**: If you have a maximum square footage requirement, please provide it.
* **Special requirements or features needed**: If you have any special requirements or features, such as parking or accessibility, please describe them.
* **Budget range for monthly rent or purchase**: Select your budget range for the space (e.g., Under 2000, 2000-5000, etc.).
* **Financing method preferred**: Choose your preferred financing method (e.g., Cash Purchase, Mortgage Financing, Lease, etc.).
* **Down payment available**: If you have a down payment available, please specify it.
* **Approximate credit score range**: If you are comfortable sharing your credit score range, please select from the options provided.
* **When do you need to occupy the space?**: Choose your preferred timeline for occupying the space (e.g., Immediately, Within 1 month, etc.).
* **Preferred lease term**: Select your preferred lease term (e.g., 1 Year, 2 Years, etc.).
* **Do you anticipate needing more space in the future?**: If you anticipate needing more space in the future, please select True.
* **Expansion timeline**: If you plan to expand, please describe your expansion timeline.
* **Brief description of your business**: Please provide a brief description of your business.
* **Any additional information or questions?**: If you have any additional information or questions, please share them.
