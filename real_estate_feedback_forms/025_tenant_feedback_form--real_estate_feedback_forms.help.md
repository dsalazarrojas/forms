# Tenant Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tenant Feedback Form is designed to collect information from tenants about their experience with a rental property or landlord. This form helps us understand their satisfaction with the property's condition, amenities, and any issues they encountered during their tenancy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Be as detailed as possible when providing feedback.
3. Ensure you are providing feedback for a property that you have actually rented or visited.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant info (Text)**: Enter a brief description of yourself, such as your name and contact information.
* **Feedback (Text)**: Describe your experience with the property, including any positive and negative feedback.
* **Date (Date)**: Enter the date you started or ended your tenancy with the property.

Note: If you have already provided feedback for the same property, you will not be able to submit a new form.
