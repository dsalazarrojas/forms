# Seller Disclosure Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Seller Disclosure Form is a document used to report any potential hazards and disclose relevant property information to potential buyers. This form is intended to ensure transparency and provide buyers with a clear understanding of any issues that may affect the property.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Check the "Hazards" field for any potential issues that may affect the property.
3. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Comments" field.
4. Review and agree to the terms in the "Agree" field.
5. Sign the form to acknowledge that you have completed it accurately.
6. Date the form to ensure that it is up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seller Disclosure Form Property** (`seller_disclosure_form_property`): Briefly describe the property in question.
* **Seller Disclosure Form Address** (`seller_disclosure_form_address`): Enter the address of the property in question.
* **Seller Disclosure Form Hazards** (`seller_disclosure_form_hazards`): Indicate if there are any hazards or potential issues affecting the property. Choose from the options 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Seller Disclosure Form Comments** (`seller_disclosure_form_comments`): Provide any additional comments or information about the property.
* **Seller Disclosure Form Additional Comments** (`seller_disclosure_form_additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or information not mentioned earlier.
* **Agree** (`seller_disclosure_form_agree`): Check the box to acknowledge that you have completed the form accurately and to the best of your knowledge.
* **Signature** (`seller_disclosure_form_signature`): Sign the form to confirm that you have completed it accurately and thoroughly.
* **Date** (`seller_disclosure_form_date`): Enter the date you completed the form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please seek assistance from the relevant authorities.
* Review the form before signing to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
