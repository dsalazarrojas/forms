# House Appraisal Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The House Appraisal Checklist Form is a comprehensive tool used to assess the condition and value of a property. It helps appraisers and reviewers gather accurate and consistent information about a property's condition, size, and location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information about the property.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible.
3. Use the options provided in the "Roof Condition" and "Foundation" fields to select the condition of the property.
4. If applicable, use the "Neighborhood" field to describe the surrounding area.
5. Use the "Photo URL" field to upload a photo of the property or provide a link to an online photo.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Appraiser** (`1`): Enter the name of the appraiser performing the assessment.
* **Address** (`2`): Enter the address of the property, including street number and street name.
* **Sq Ft** (`3`): Enter the approximate square footage of the property.
* **Lot Size** (`4`): Enter the size of the property's lot or land size in square feet.
* **Roof Condition** (`5`): Select the condition of the roof from the options: "Poor", "Fair", "Good", or "Excellent".
* **Foundation** (`6`): Select all applicable conditions for the foundation from the options: "Cracked" or "Level".
* **Neighborhood** (`7`): Describe the surrounding neighborhood, including any nearby amenities or features.
* **Neighbourhood** (`8`): Select the type of neighborhood from the options: "Urban", "Suburban", or "Rural".
* **Comments** (`9`): Add any additional comments or notes about the property.
* **Additional Comments** (`10`): Select all applicable additional comments from the options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Photo URL** (`11`): Upload or link to a photo of the property for reference.
* **Appraiser Signature** (`12`): Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed accurately and thoroughly.
* **Reviewer Signature** (`13`): Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been reviewed and approved.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and consistent information throughout the form.
* Use the options provided in the "Roof Condition" and "Foundation" fields to ensure accurate and consistent assessments.
* Use the "Photo URL" field to upload photos that are clear and well-lit.
* Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed and reviewed accurately and thoroughly.
