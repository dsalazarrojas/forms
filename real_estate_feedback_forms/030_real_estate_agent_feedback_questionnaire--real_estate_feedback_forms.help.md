# real_estate_agent_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The real_estate_agent_feedback_questionnaire form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experience with real estate agents. This form is used to help identify areas of improvement and ensure that the agent's performance meets the highest standards.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Client Name** field with the client's name as it appears on their contact information.
2. Enter the **Contact Number** field with the client's valid phone number.
3. Enter the **Email** field with the client's valid email address.
4. If applicable, select a **Service Rating** from the dropdown menu to indicate the client's satisfaction level with the service provided.
5. If applicable, select an **Agent Rating** from the dropdown menu to rate the agent's performance.
6. In the **Feedback** field, provide any comments or concerns you may have about your experience with the agent.
7. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the **Suggestions** field.
8. Provide any additional **Comments** about your experience with the agent.
9. Leave the **Signature** field blank for now, as it is not required.
10. Enter the **Date** field with the date of the feedback, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
11. Enter the **Time** field with the time of the feedback, in the format hh:mm AM/PM.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name as it appears on their contact information.
* **Contact Number**: Enter a valid phone number for the client.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for the client.
* **Service Rating**: Select a rating that indicates your satisfaction level with the service provided. The options are:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Agent Rating**: Select a rating that indicates the agent's performance. The options are:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Feedback**: Provide any comments or concerns you may have about your experience with the agent.
* **Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them here.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your experience with the agent.
* **Signature**: This field is not required, but leave it blank for now.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the feedback, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the feedback, in the format hh:mm AM/PM.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information in each field.
* If you have any concerns or issues, provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have suggestions for improvement, be specific and clear.
* This form is designed to help us improve our services, so please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
