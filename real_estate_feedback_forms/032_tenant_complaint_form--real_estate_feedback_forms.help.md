# Tenant Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Complaint Form is used by tenants to report any issues or complaints they have regarding their rental property. This form collects information about the complaint, including the date it occurred, contact details, and a description of the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a clear and concise description of the issue in the "Complaint Details" field.
2. Enter the date on which the complaint occurred in the "Date" field, in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
3. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field, using the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" (e.g., 123-456-7890).
4. Enter a detailed description of the issue in the "Description" field.
5. If you have an email address, enter it in the "Email Address" field.
6. Finally, provide your signature in the "Tenant Signature" field, indicating that you are the tenant reporting this complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Complaint** (Required): Enter a clear and concise description of the issue or complaint.
* **Date** (Optional): Enter the date on which the complaint occurred, in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Contact Number** (Required): Enter your contact number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Description** (Required): Enter a detailed description of the issue or complaint.
* **Email Address** (Optional): If you have an email address, enter it in this field.
* **Tenant Signature** (Required): Provide your signature, indicating that you are the tenant reporting this complaint.
