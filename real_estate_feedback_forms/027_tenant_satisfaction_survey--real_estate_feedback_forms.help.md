# Tenant Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Satisfaction Survey is a collection of questions designed to gather feedback from tenants about their experiences with the rental property. The survey is intended to help landlords and property managers understand tenant satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your honest responses. Please provide detailed answers where possible. The form is divided into pages, each with a unique set of questions. Make sure to answer all required fields before moving on to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your property name** (Property Name, text, required): Please provide the name of the property where you reside.
* **Where do you live** (Location, text, required): Please enter the location where you currently reside.
* **How long have you lived there** (Lease Length, number, required): Please enter the number of months or years you have lived in the property.
* **What is your level of satisfaction** (Satisfaction Score, number, required): Please rate your level of satisfaction with the property on a scale of 1-5.
* **Are you likely to renew your lease** (Reinstate Lease, select one, required): Yes or no answer to indicate if you plan to renew your lease.
* **Do you have any comments** (Comments, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or comments, please enter them here.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Please enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date First Moved In** (Date First Moved In, date, optional): Please enter the date you first moved into the property.
* **Estimated Move Out Date** (Estimated Move Out Date, date, optional): Please enter the estimated date you plan to move out of the property.
* **Date Survey Taken** (Date Survey Taken, date, optional): Please enter the date this survey was completed.
* **How would you rate your building** (Building Rating, number, required): Please rate the overall building on a scale of 1-5.
* **Which of the following amenities would you like** (Amenities, select multiple, optional): Choose the amenities you would like to see added to the property.
* **How would you rate the management** (Management Rating, number, optional): Please rate the management team on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the communication** (Communication Rating, number, optional): Please rate the communication with the management team on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the maintenance** (Maintenance Rating, number, optional): Please rate the maintenance team on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the cleanliness** (Cleanliness Rating, number, optional): Please rate the cleanliness of the property on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the noise** (Noise Rating, number, optional): Please rate the level of noise in the property on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the security** (Security Rating, number, optional): Please rate the overall security of the property on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the safety** (Safety Rating, number, optional): Please rate the overall safety of the property on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the maintenance response time** (Maintenance Response Time, number, optional): Please rate the promptness of the maintenance team's response on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the maintenance response** (Maintenance Response, number, optional): Please rate the effectiveness of the maintenance team's response on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the maintenance quality** (Maintenance Quality, number, optional): Please rate the quality of the maintenance work on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the emergency services** (Emergency Services, number, optional): Please rate the effectiveness of the emergency services on a scale of 1-5.

Tips: Please take your time to answer each question as honestly and accurately as possible. Your feedback is invaluable to us and helps us improve the property and services we provide.
