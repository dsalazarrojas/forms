# Real Estate Data Privacy Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a user-facing survey that gathers opinions on the privacy of real estate data. It is not a required form, and users can complete it on a voluntary basis. The information collected will help us understand users' perspectives on real estate data privacy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your opinion on the Real Estate Data Privacy Opinion Survey by choosing one of the four options: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
2. Indicate your consent to participate in the study by selecting either Agree or Decline.
3. If you choose to rate the risk involved in real estate data privacy, select a number between 0 and 100.
4. Provide a brief description of the risks you are aware of in the Risk Description field.
5. Indicate your level of awareness about the risks involved in real estate data privacy by selecting one of the three options: Highly Aware, Somewhat Aware, or Not Aware.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Real Estate Data Privacy Opinion Survey** (`real_estate_data_privacy_opinion_survey`, select multiple, **required: false**): This field asks for your opinion on the privacy of real estate data. Select one of the four options: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select one, **required: false**): This field asks if you agree or decline to participate in the study. Select either Agree or Decline.
* **Risk Rating** (`risk_rating`, number, **required: false**): This field asks you to rate the risk involved in real estate data privacy on a scale of 0 to 100.
* **Risk Description** (`risk_description`, text, **required: false**): This field is for you to provide a brief description of the risks you are aware of in real estate data privacy.
* **Risk Awareness** (`risk_awareness`, select multiple, **required: false**): This field asks for your level of awareness about the risks involved in real estate data privacy. Select one of the three options: Highly Aware, Somewhat Aware, or Not Aware.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions based on your genuine opinion and awareness.
* Make sure to select only one option for each field, unless specified otherwise.
* If you are unsure or have any questions, feel free to contact us.
