# real_estate_showing_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from real estate agents or brokers about a showing of a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields with the required information:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Real Estate Showing** (`1`, `Real Estate Showing`, `optional`): This field is where you can describe the property showing event. It's a text field where you can provide a brief summary of what happened during the showing.
- **Date** (`2`, `Date`, `optional`): This field is where you provide the date of the property showing. Select the date from the calendar or enter it manually in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Time** (`3`, `Time`, `optional`): This field is where you provide the time of the property showing. Select the time from the options provided or enter it manually in the correct format (HH:MM).
- **Property Type** (`4`, `Property Type`, `optional`): This field is where you select the type of property being shown. Options are condo, house, lot, townhouse, or land.
- **Agent Name** (`5`, `Agent Name`, `optional`): This field is where you enter the name of the real estate agent or broker who attended the showing.
- **Agent Company** (`6`, `Agent Company`, `optional`): This field is where you enter the name of the real estate agent's company.
- **Showing Agent** (`7`, `Showing Agent`, `optional`): This field is where you enter the name of the agent or broker who conducted the showing.
- **Property Address** (`8`, `Property Address`, `optional`): This field is where you enter the address of the property.
- **Property City** (`9`, `Property City`, `optional`): This field is where you enter the city of the property's location.
- **Property State** (`10`, `Property State`, `optional`): This field is where you enter the state of the property's location.
- **Property Postal Code** (`11`, `Property Postal Code`, `optional`): This field is where you enter the postal code of the property's location.
- **Contact Email** (`12`, `Contact Email`, `optional`): This field is where you enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Contact Phone** (`13`, `Contact Phone`, `optional`): This field is where you enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields (none in this case).
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can always contact your administrator for assistance.
- Save a copy of the form for your records and reference it when needed.
