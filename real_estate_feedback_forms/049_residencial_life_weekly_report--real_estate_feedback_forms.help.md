# Residential Life Weekly Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from residents on their experience and satisfaction with residential life, including their living area and meal options. The feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance the resident's life.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, one page at a time.
2. For questions with dropdown menus, select one or more options that best reflect your experience and satisfaction.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Residential Life Experience** (`residential_life_experience`, `text`, required: false): Describe your experience living in the residential life in a few sentences. This will help us understand your overall impression.
* **Satisfaction With Living Area** (`satisfaction_with_living_area`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option that best describes your satisfaction with your living area. Options are:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Satisfaction With Meal Options** (`satisfaction_with_meal_options`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that best describe your satisfaction with the meal options. Options are:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Suggestions For Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, required: false): Provide any suggestions you have for improving residential life. This can include any issues you have encountered and how we can address them.
* **Comments On Residential Life** (`comments_on_residential_life`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your residential life. This can include your experiences, concerns, or ideas for improvement.
