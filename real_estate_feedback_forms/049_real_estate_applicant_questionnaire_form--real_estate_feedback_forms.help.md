# real_estate_applicant_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from home buyers who are applying for a real estate loan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your contact information, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Indicate if you are a home buyer.
3. Select your financial status.
4. Provide your income, employment status, and employment years of experience.
5. Choose the type of property you are looking to purchase.
6. Select your desired location.
7. Enter your budget for the loan.
8. Choose the type of loan you are applying for (variable or fixed).
9. Enter the loan amount.
10. Provide your employment income details.
11. Indicate if you are self-employed.
12. Select your employment status and years of experience.
13. Enter any notes or additional comments you would like to provide.
14. (Optional) Provide your phone number, email address, date of birth, and time.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Home buyer** (`home_buyer`, `select_one`, required): Select "True" if you are a home buyer.
* **Financial status** (`financial_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the financial status that best describes your current situation (stable, financially stable).
* **Income** (`income`, `number`, required): Enter your income.
* **Employment status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your employment status (employed, self-employed, unemployed).
* **Employment years** (`employment_years`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have been employed.
* **Property type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of property you are looking to purchase (house, apartment, townhouse, condo).
* **Desired location** (`desired_location`, `select_one`, required): Select the desired location (city, suburb, town, regional).
* **Budget** (`budget`, `number`, required): Enter the budget for the loan.
* **Loan type** (`loan_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the type of loan you are applying for (variable, fixed).
* **Loan amount** (`loan_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the loan amount.
* **Employment income** (`employment_income`, `number`, required): Enter your employment income.
* **Self-employed** (`self_employed`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate if you are self-employed.
* **Employment status 2** (`employment_status_2`, `select_one`, required): Select your employment status (employed, self-employed, unemployed).
* **Employment years 2** (`employment_years_2`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have been employed.
* **Employment status 3** (`employment_status_3`, `select_one`, required): Select your employment status (employed, self-employed, unemployed).
* **Employment years 3** (`employment_years_3`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have been employed.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you would like to provide.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time.
* **Time 2** (`time_2`, `time`, optional): Enter the time.

Note: Please fill in the form accurately and completely to avoid any errors. If you have any issues or concerns, contact our support team.
