# residential_move_in_experience_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to capture your experience with your residential unit, including details about your move-in date, living conditions, and lease information. Your feedback will help us better understand your experience and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer each field truthfully to ensure your feedback is accurate.
2. Enter your move-in date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Select the correct answer for each of the multiple-choice questions.
4. Be as descriptive as possible in the text fields, such as when providing comments or describing your move-in issues.
5. Make sure to fill out all required fields as they are marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. move_in_date (Required)
\* Enter the date you moved into your unit in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.

### 2. moved_in (Required)
\* Select "Yes" if you have moved into your unit, and "No" if you have not.

### 3. move_in_condition (Required)
\* Select "Yes" if your unit was in good condition when you moved in, and "No" if there were any issues.

### 4. move_in_experience (Optional)
\* Provide any comments or additional details about your experience when moving into your unit.

### 5. property_manager (Required)
\* Enter the name of your property manager or contact person.

### 6. move_in_issues (Optional)
\* Describe any issues you encountered when moving into your unit.

### 7. comments (Optional)
\* Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

### 8. move_in_experience_rating (Required)
\* Rate your experience moving into your unit on a scale of 1-10.

### 9. property_name (Required)
\* Enter the name of your property.

### 10. unit_number (Required)
\* Enter the unit number of your unit.

### 11. building (Required)
\* Enter the name of the building where your unit is located.

### 12. floor (Required)
\* Enter the floor number of your unit.

### 13. unit_size (Required)
\* Enter the size of your unit (e.g., studio, one-bedroom, etc.).

### 14. parking (Required)
\* Select "Yes" if you have access to parking, and "No" if you do not.

### 15. elevator (Required)
\* Select "Yes" if your building has an elevator, and "No" if it does not.

### 16. pets (Required)
\* Select "Yes" if you have pets, and "No" if you do not.

### 17. utilities (Required)
\* Select the utilities you have access to in your unit (e.g., electricity, water, etc.).

### 18. appliances (Required)
\* Select the appliances you have access to in your unit (e.g., stove, refrigerator, etc.).

### 19. amenities (Required)
\* Select the amenities you have access to in your unit (e.g., gym, pool, etc.).

### 20. lease_length (Required)
\* Enter the length of your lease in months or years.

### 21. lease_start_date (Required)
\* Enter the start date of your lease in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.

### 22. lease_end_date (Required)
\* Enter the end date of your lease in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.

### 23. lease_type (Required)
\* Select the type of lease you have (e.g., month-to-month, year-to-year, etc.).

### 24. move_out_date (Optional)
\* Enter the date you moved out of your unit in the format of MM/DD/YYYY (if applicable).
