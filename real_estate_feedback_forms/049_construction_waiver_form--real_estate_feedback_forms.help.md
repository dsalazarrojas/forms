# Construction Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Construction Waiver Form is a legal document used to waive lien rights on a construction project. This form is typically used by a claimant (general contractor, subcontractor, material supplier, or laborer) to release their lien rights on a property after completing work.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Construction Waiver Form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your claimant name and role (general contractor, subcontractor, material supplier, or laborer) in the **Claimant Name** and **Claimant Role** fields.
2. Select the type of waiver you are providing (Conditional Waiver and Release on Progress Payment, Unconditional Waiver and Release on Progress Payment, Conditional Waiver and Release on Final Payment, or Unconditional Waiver and Release on Final Payment) in the **Waiver Type** field.
3. Enter the project name and address in the **Project Name** and **Project Address** fields, respectively.
4. Enter the payment amount being provided in the **Payment Amount** field.
5. Select the date of the payment in the **Payment Through Date** field.
6. Enter the property's legal description from the deed or title in the **Property Legal Description** field.
7. Enter a brief description of the work performed in the **Description of Work** field.
8. If any exceptions or disputes exist, enter them in the **Exceptions** field.
9. Enter the description of the lien rights being waived in the **Rights Being Waived** field.
10. Verify that all the information entered is true and correct by selecting one of the two options in the **Verification Statement** field.
11. Sign the **Claimant Signature** field with your full name as the claimant.
12. Enter your title or position in the **Claimant Title** field.
13. Enter the date of signing in the **Signature Date** field.
14. If a notary is required or not, select **True** or **False** in the **Notary Required** field.
15. If notarization is required, enter the notary's name in the **Notary Name** field.
16. Enter the notary's commission number in the **Notary Commission Number** field.
17. Enter the notary's commission expiration date in the **Notary Commission Expiration** field.
18. Sign the **Notary Signature** field with the notary's full name.
19. Enter the date of notarization in the **Notary Seal Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Waiver Date**: Date of this waiver
* **Project Name**: Name of construction project
* **Project Address**: Property location
* **Property Owner Name**: Legal owner name
* **Contractor Name**: Company or individual
* **Contractor Address**: Business address
* **Contractor Phone**: Contact number
* **Contractor Email**: Email address
* **Waiver Type**: Type of lien waiver
* **Claimant Name**: Party waiving rights
* **Claimant Role**: Position on project
* **Payment Amount**: Amount being paid
* **Payment Through Date**: Work covered by this payment
* **Check Number**: Payment reference (optional)
* **Property Legal Description**: From deed or title
* **Description of Work**: What work was performed
* **Exceptions**: Any retainage or disputes
* **Rights Being Waived**: Description of lien rights
* **Verification Statement**: Statement of truth
* **Claimant Signature**: Type full name as signature
* **Claimant Title**: Position or role
* **Signature Date**: Date signed
* **Witness Name**: Witness to signature (optional)
* **Witness Signature**: Type full name as signature (optional)
* **Notary Required**: Is notarization needed
* **Notary Name**: Name of notary (optional)
* **Notary Commission Number**: Commission ID (optional)
* **Notary Commission Expiration**: When does commission expire (optional)
* **Notary Signature**: Type full name as signature (optional)
* **Notary Seal Date**: Date of notarization (optional)

Please ensure that all information is accurate and complete before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
