# Real Estate Offer Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Offer Questionnaire Form is used to gather information about real estate offers, including address details and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete the form with the necessary details for the real estate offer you are submitting.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address**: Enter the address details for the real estate offer.
* **Contact Name (contact_name)**: Enter the name of the contact person associated with the offer.
* **Contact Number (contact_number)**: Enter the contact number for the contact person.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the email address of the contact person (if applicable).
* **Offer Price (offer_price)**: Enter the price of the real estate offer.
* **Offer Terms (offer_terms)**: Enter any additional terms or conditions related to the offer.
* **Offer Details (offer_details)**: Enter any other details about the offer.
* **Contact Date (contact_date)**: Enter the date and time you would like to contact the contact person.
* **Contact Time (contact_time)**: Enter the time you would like to contact the contact person.
* **Contact Method (contact_method)**: Select the method by which you would like to contact the contact person (Contact, Email, or Phone).
* **Offer Status (offer_status)**: Select the status of the offer (Pending, Confirmed, or Declined).
* **Contact Status (contact_status)**: Select the status of the contact (Pending, Confirmed, or Declined).
* **Address 2 (address_2)**: Enter any additional address details (if applicable).
* **Contact 2 (contact_2)**: Enter the name of the contact person for the second address (if applicable).
* **Contact Number 2 (contact_number_2)**: Enter the contact number for the second contact person (if applicable).
