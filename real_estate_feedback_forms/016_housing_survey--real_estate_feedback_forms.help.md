# Housing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from renters about their experience with a rental property. It aims to collect their opinions and comments about their overall experience, including the property type, conditions, and services provided.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please select the correct property type from the provided options.
*   Answer each question based on your real experience with the property.
*   Please answer all questions truthfully, and do not leave any section blank unless you are unsure or have no opinion.
*   Take your time to read each question carefully before answering.
*   Once you have completed the form, please submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **property_type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of property that you rented or are currently renting. This could be residential or commercial property.
*   **property_name** (`property_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the property that you rented or are currently renting.
*   **tenant_name** (`tenant_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name.
*   **rent_date** (`rent_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you started or stopped renting the property.
*   **rent_amount** (`rent_amount`, number, optional): Please enter the amount you paid in rent.
*   **satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Please select how satisfied you are with your overall experience with the property.
*   **comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with the property.
*   **email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address.
*   **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number.
*   **property_condition** (`property_condition`, select_one, optional): Please select the condition of the property that you rented or are currently renting.
*   **rent_frequency** (`rent_frequency`, select_one, optional): Please select how often you have to pay rent.
*   **communication** (`communication`, select_one, optional): Please select how often you receive communication from the property management or staff.
*   **repair_service** (`repair_service`, select_one, optional): Please select how often you have access to repair services for the property.
*   **cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, select_one, optional): Please select the cleanliness of the property that you rented or are currently renting.
*   **amenities** (`amenities`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the amenities that are provided in the property.
*   **safety** (`safety`, select_one, optional): Please select the safety of the property that you rented or are currently renting.
*   **neighborliness** (`neighborliness`, select_one, optional): Please select the neighborliness of the property that you rented or are currently renting.
*   **maintenance** (`maintenance`, select_one, optional): Please select how often you have access to maintenance services for the property.
*   **management** (`management`, select_one, optional): Please select how helpful the property management or staff is.
*   **overall_experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, optional): Please select your overall experience with the property.
*   **suggestions** (`suggestions`, note, optional): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the property.
*   **tenant_contact** (`tenant_contact`, select_one, optional): Please select whether you have contacted the tenant or property management directly.
*   **landlord_contact** (`landlord_contact`, select_one, optional): Please select whether you have contacted the landlord or property staff directly.
*   **emergency_contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, optional): Please select whether you have an emergency contact number or email provided by the property management or staff.
*   **other_comments** (`other_comments`, note, optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the property.

## Tips

*   Please answer all questions truthfully and based on your real experience with the property.
*   Take your time to read each question carefully before answering.
*   Do not leave any section blank unless you are unsure or have no opinion.
*   Complete the form in one sitting to ensure accuracy and consistency in your answers.
