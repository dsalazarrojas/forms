# Laboratory Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laboratory Access Request Form is a tool used to request access to a laboratory facility for various reasons such as lab work or other purposes. This form is meant to be completed by individuals who need to access the laboratory, and it will be reviewed by the assigned approvers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number (if applicable).
4. Specify the name and location of the laboratory facility you are requesting access to.
5. Select a start and end date for your visit.
6. Choose the purpose of your visit (e.g., lab work, other).
7. Select all categories that apply to your visit (e.g., Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
8. If necessary, provide additional comments about your visit.
9. Identify the person assigned to handle your request.
10. Provide the name of the person submitting the request (i.e., you).
11. Specify who will be approving your access request (i.e., approver).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number (if applicable).
* **Laboratory Facility Name** (`laboratory_facility_name`, text, optional): Specify the name of the laboratory facility you are requesting access to.
* **Laboratory Facility Location** (`laboratory_facility_location`, text, optional): Specify the location of the laboratory facility you are requesting access to.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Select the start date of your visit.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Select the end date of your visit.
* **Purpose of Visit** (`purpose_of_visit`, select_one, required): Choose the reason for your visit (e.g., lab work, other).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your visit.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): Select all categories that apply to your visit (e.g., Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Identify the person assigned to handle your request.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional): Specify the name of the person submitting the request (i.e., you).
* **Approver** (`approver`, text, optional): Specify who will be approving your access request.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have permission to access the laboratory facility before submitting this form.
* If you are unsure about the purpose of your visit, contact the laboratory facility directly.
* Ensure you have selected all relevant categories for your visit.
* Be accurate when entering your contact information.
