# ivd_machine_calibration_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on the machine's calibration status. It helps track the machine's condition, maintenance, and compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Survey Information**: This is the introductory page of the form. It provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
2. Fill out the required fields: The fields are grouped into sections. Fill out each section completely, starting from **Machine Information** to **Submission Information**.
3. Review and submit: Once you have filled out all the fields, review the form for accuracy and completeness. Make sure to include any additional notes if necessary. Then, submit the form.
