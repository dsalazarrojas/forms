# Laboratory Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laboratory Safety Checklist is a critical form used to evaluate the safety measures in place in a laboratory. This form assesses the adequacy of electrical equipment, fire safety, chemical storage, personal protective equipment, emergency procedures, and the review and corrective action process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form by selecting the correct answers for each field. The answers are usually "Yes" or "No", except for "Chemical Storage" and "Emergency Procedures" which are text fields.
- Make sure to answer all fields, as indicated by the "*" required symbol next to the field label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Electrical Equipment**: Check if you have a reliable electrical equipment, such as working switches, sockets, and circuit breakers.
- **Fire Safety**: Confirm that you have a working fire alarm, fire extinguishers, and an escape plan in place.
- **Chemical Storage**: Describe the storage area for chemicals, including the location, accessibility, and labeling of containers.
- **Personal Protective Equipment**: Check if you have and use personal protective equipment, such as lab coats, gloves, and safety glasses.
- **Emergency Procedures**: Describe your emergency procedures, including evacuation routes, emergency contact numbers, and the location of the fire extinguisher.
- **Review and Corrective Action**: Confirm that you regularly review and take action on safety-related incidents.
- **Signature**: Include your name and date to confirm that you have completed this form and acknowledge its contents.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer truthfully and accurately.
- If unsure about any field, consult your supervisor or a safety expert.
- Complete this form periodically to ensure the laboratory safety measures are up-to-date and effective.
