# Laboratory Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Laboratory Leave Of Absence Request" form is used by laboratory staff to request leave or absences from their work schedule. This form helps the laboratory manager to approve or deny leave requests, ensuring that the necessary work is covered and that the laboratory's operations are not disrupted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Name (Field 1) to identify yourself as the laboratory staff member requesting the leave.
2. Enter your Employee ID (Field 2) if you have it. This is not required but can help with tracking your leave.
3. Choose a Start Date (Field 3) for your leave, making sure it's accurate and within the approved dates.
4. Select an End Date (Field 4) for your leave, ensuring it's within the approved dates.
5. Provide a Reason For Absence (Field 5) to justify your leave request. Be as specific as possible.
6. Choose whether the supervisor has approved (Field 6) your leave request.
7. Add any Comments (Field 7) if you want to provide additional information or clarification.
8. Optionally, select a Submit Time (Field 8) for when you submitted the form.
9. Review the Form Submitted (Field 9) field for reference, but it's not required to be filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (1)** (text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your laboratory ID badge.
* **Employee ID (2)** (number, optional): If you have an employee ID, enter it here to help track your leave request.
* **Start Date (3)** (date, optional): Choose the start date of your leave, making sure it's accurate and within the approved dates.
* **End Date (4)** (date, optional): Select the end date of your leave, ensuring it's within the approved dates.
* **Reason For Absence (5)** (text, optional): Provide a clear explanation for your leave request.
* **Supervisor Approval (6)** (select_one, optional): Select 'approve' if your supervisor has approved your leave request.
* **Comments (7)** (note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your leave request.
* **Submit Time (8)** (time, optional): If required, select the time you submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted (9)** (text, optional): This field is not editable; you can view the date and time of your submission.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your dates for accuracy and consistency with your supervisor.
* Be specific with your Reason For Absence to help facilitate approval.
* If you have any additional comments, please include them in the Comments field.
* If you encounter any issues while filling out the form, contact the laboratory manager for assistance.
