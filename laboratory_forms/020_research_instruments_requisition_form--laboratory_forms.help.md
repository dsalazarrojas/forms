# Research Instruments Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to request research instruments, specifying the instruments requested, their category, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps listed above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Instruments Requested** (`instrument_requested`, text, required): Enter the name or description of the research instrument you are requesting.
* **Research Instruments Category** (`research_instrument_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the research instrument (Instrumentation Services, Equipment, or Supplies).
* **Instrument Model** (`research_instrument_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the research instrument, if applicable.
* **Research Instruments Description** (`research_instrument_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the research instrument, including its purpose and usage.
* **Research Instruments Quantity** (`research_instrument_quantity`, number, required): Specify the quantity of the research instrument required.
* **Purchase Approved By** (`purchase_approved_by`, select_multiple, optional): Select who approved the purchase of the research instrument (Director, Manager, or Researcher).
* **Department Requested** (`department_requested`, select_one, optional): Choose the department that requested the research instrument (Lab, R&D, or Admin).
* **Research Instruments Price** (`research_instrument_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the research instrument.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, optional): Enter the date the research instrument was received.
* **Date Required** (`date_required`, date, optional): Enter the date the research instrument is required.
* **Time Required** (`time_required`, time, optional): Specify the time the research instrument is required.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the research instrument.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person requesting the research instrument.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person requesting the research instrument.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Finalize the submission of the form. You can choose to "Submit", "Save for Later" for further editing, or "Cancel" to discard the form.
