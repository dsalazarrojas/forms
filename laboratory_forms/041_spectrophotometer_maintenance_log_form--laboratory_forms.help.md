Spectrophotometer Maintenance Log Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the maintenance activities performed on a spectrophotometer, including maintenance status, description, date, and category. It helps in keeping a record of the equipment's maintenance history and ensures that all necessary information is captured for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the maintenance activity in the "Maintenance Description" field.
2. Select the current status of the maintenance from the "Maintenance Status" dropdown menu. This will indicate if the equipment is Functional, Non-Functional, or Under Maintenance.
3. Choose the maintenance category from the "Maintenance Category" dropdown menu. This will help in categorizing the maintenance activity, such as Calibration, Repair, or Other.
4. Enter the date of the maintenance activity in the "Maintenance Date" field.
5. If necessary, provide additional remarks about the maintenance activity in the "Remarks" field.
6. If applicable, select the technician's ID from the "Technician ID" field.
7. Click "Submit" to save the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Log** (Text Field): This field is used to enter a brief description of the maintenance activity.
* **Equipment Id** (Number Field): This field is used to enter the unique ID of the spectrophotometer equipment.
* **Maintenance Description** (Text Field): This field is used to enter a detailed description of the maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance Date** (Date Field): This field is used to enter the date of the maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance Status** (Select Field): This field is used to select the current status of the maintenance from the dropdown menu. Options include Functional, Non-Functional, and Under Maintenance.
* **Maintenance Category** (Select Field): This field is used to select the category of the maintenance activity from the dropdown menu. Options include Calibration, Repair, and Other.
* **Remarks** (Text Field): This field is used to enter additional comments or remarks about the maintenance activity.
* **Technician Id** (Number Field): This field is used to enter the ID of the technician who performed the maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance Status** (Select Field): This field is used to select the current status of the maintenance from the dropdown menu. Options include Functional, Non-Functional, and Under Maintenance.
* **Submit** (Button Field): This button is used to save the completed form.
