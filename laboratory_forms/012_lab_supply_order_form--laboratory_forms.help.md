# Lab Supply Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lab Supply Order Form is designed to collect information for ordering laboratory supplies. This form is used to gather user details, payment method, and order specifics to efficiently process and deliver the requested supplies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **User Input** field with your name, email, and any other relevant information.
2. Select the **Payment Method** from the dropdown list. You can choose between **Online Payment** or **Offline Payment** options.
3. In the **Items Ordered** field, provide a list or description of the supplies you need to order.
4. Enter the **Total Items** number to specify the quantity of each item you're ordering.
5. Add any additional **Order Notes** that the supplier or lab management should be aware of.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (text): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information for your order.
* **Payment Method** (select_one): Select between **Online Payment** or **Offline Payment** options to specify how you'll complete your order.
* **Items Ordered** (text): Describe the supplies you need to order. Be as detailed as possible so that the supplier can accurately process your request.
* **Total Items** (number): Enter the total quantity of each item you're ordering.
* **Order Notes** (note): Add any additional comments or instructions for the supplier to take note of.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields to ensure that your order is processed efficiently.
* Double-check your payment method and total items to avoid errors.
* Use the **Order Notes** field to provide any specific instructions or special requests.
