# Science Lab Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from participants in a laboratory setting, ensuring they understand and agree to the safety protocols and procedures of the lab.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Participant Information**: Provide general information about yourself, such as your name, age, and contact details.
2.  **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it should be recorded in the lab's records.
3.  **Parent Guardian Information**: If you are under 18, provide information about your parent or guardian's contact details.
4.  **Parent Name**: Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
5.  **Contact Info**: Provide any additional contact information that may be useful for lab staff to reach you in case of an emergency.
6.  **Emergency Contact Info**: List any emergency contact details, including the name and contact information of a person to be notified in case of an incident.
7.  **Participant Signature**: Sign this form to indicate that you have read and understood the liability waiver, safety acknowledgment, and other terms and conditions.
8.  **Parent Signature**: If you are under 18, sign this form to confirm that your parent or guardian has also reviewed and agreed to the terms.
9.  **Liability Waiver**: This section outlines the risks and responsibilities associated with participating in the lab activities. Please read and acknowledge that you have understood these risks.
10. **Safety Acknowledgment**: Verify that you are aware of and agree to follow the lab's safety protocols and procedures.
11.  **Participant Information 2**: This section may be used to gather additional information about you, such as your address or any medical conditions.
12.  **Participant Name 2**: Enter your name again, which may be required for record-keeping purposes.
