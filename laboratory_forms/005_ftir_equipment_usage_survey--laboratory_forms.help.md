# FTIR Equipment Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report the usage and satisfaction of scientific instruments in the laboratory, helping to gather data for improving instrument efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select the instrument you have used from the options provided.
* Rate your satisfaction level with the instrument.
* Indicate your training needs for the instrument.
* Answer whether you used the instrument recently or not.
* Provide any feedback or comments about your experience with the instrument.
* Indicate your submission status.
* If applicable, select additional instruments used.
* Provide the date and time of submission.
* Select the form status.
* Answer whether you used the instrument recently or not (again, for confirmation).
* Provide any additional comments or information.
* Select whether the form was submitted successfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instrument Used** (`Instrument Used`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the scientific instrument you used from the options provided (FT-IR, Raman, NMR, or Other).
* **Satisfaction Level** (`Satisfaction Level`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction level with the instrument from 1-10.
* **Training Needs** (`Training Needs`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your training needs for the instrument (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, or None).
* **Instrument Usage Frequency** (`Instrument Usage Frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer whether you used the instrument recently or not (Yes, No, or Other).
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any feedback or comments about your experience with the instrument.
* **Submission Status** (`Submission Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your submission status (Not Started, In Progress, Completed).
* **Additional Instruments** (`Additional Instruments`, `select_one`, required: false): Select any additional instruments used.
* **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, `date`, required: false): Provide the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`Submission Time`, `time`, required: false): Provide the time of submission.
* **Form Status** (`Form Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the form status (Not Started, In Progress, Completed).
* **Instrument Usage Frequency 2** (`Instrument Usage Frequency 2`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer whether you used the instrument recently or not (Yes, No, or Other).
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or information.
* **Submission User** (`Submission User`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name or identity.
* **Form Submit** (`Form Submit`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether the form was submitted successfully (True, False, or Unknown).
* **Submission Time 2** (`Submission Time 2`, `time`, required: false): Provide the time of submission (for confirmation).
* **Training Needs 2** (`Training Needs 2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your training needs for the instrument (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, or None).
* **Instrument Used 2** (`Instrument Used 2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the instrument used.
* **Submission Status 2** (`Submission Status 2`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your submission status (Not Started, In Progress, Completed).
* **Instrument Usage Frequency 3** (`Instrument Usage Frequency 3`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer whether you used the instrument recently or not (Yes, No, or Other).
* **Submission User 2** (`Submission User 2`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name or identity (for confirmation).
