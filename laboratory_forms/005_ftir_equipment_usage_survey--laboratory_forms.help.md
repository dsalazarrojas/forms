# FTIR Equipment Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report the usage and satisfaction of scientific instruments in the laboratory, helping to gather data for improving instrument efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select the instrument you have used from the options provided.
* Rate your satisfaction level with the instrument.
* Indicate your training needs for the instrument.
* Answer whether you used the instrument recently or not.
* Provide any feedback or comments about your experience with the instrument.
* Indicate your submission status.
* If applicable, select additional instruments used.
* Provide the date and time of submission.
* Select the form status.
* Answer whether you used the instrument recently or not (again, for confirmation).
* Provide any additional comments or information.
* Select whether the form was submitted successfully.
