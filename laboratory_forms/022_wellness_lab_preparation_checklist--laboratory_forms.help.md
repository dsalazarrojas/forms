# Wellness Lab Preparation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Lab Preparation Checklist is a form designed to collect essential information from patients before their lab test or procedure. This form ensures patients are prepared and provide necessary documentation to laboratory staff.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each field description and complete each section thoroughly.
2. Answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. *Appointment Details* (`appointment_details`, text, required)
Provide your appointment date, time, and any relevant details about your visit.

*   This field is required for the laboratory staff to schedule your appointment correctly.
*   Please ensure you provide the correct date and time for your appointment.

### 2. *Pre-lab Steps* (`pre_lab_steps`, text, required)
Describe any pre-lab preparations or instructions given to you by the healthcare provider.

*   This field is required to inform laboratory staff of any necessary preparations for your test or procedure.
*   Please provide any relevant instructions or guidelines given to you by your healthcare provider.

### 3. *Medical History* (`medical_history`, text, optional)
Share any relevant medical history or conditions that may affect your lab test or procedure.

*   This field is optional but can provide valuable context to laboratory staff.
*   If you have any relevant medical history, please describe it briefly.

### 4. *Medication List* (`medication_list`, text, optional)
List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.

*   This field is optional but can help laboratory staff understand your current medication regimen.
*   Please list any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.

### 5. *Contact Info* (`contact_info`, text, required)
Provide your contact information for laboratory staff to reach out to you with results or questions.

*   This field is required for laboratory staff to contact you with results or ask questions.
*   Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date contact information.

### 6. *Emergency Contact* (`emergency_contact`, text, required)
Name and contact information for an emergency contact person.

*   This field is required in case laboratory staff need to contact someone in case of an emergency.
*   Please provide the name and contact information for an emergency contact.

### 7. *Medical History 2* (`medical_history_2`, text, optional)
Additional medical history or conditions that may affect your lab test or procedure.

*   This field is optional but can provide more context to laboratory staff.
*   If you have any additional medical history or conditions, describe them briefly.

### 8. *Lab Info* (`lab_info`, text, required)
Provide any specific lab information or instructions given to you by your healthcare provider.

*   This field is required to inform laboratory staff of any necessary lab-specific information.
*   Please provide any relevant lab information given to you by your healthcare provider.

### 9. *Lab Equipment* (`lab_equipment`, text, optional)
List any specific lab equipment or materials you will need for your test or procedure.

*   This field is optional but can help laboratory staff prepare for your appointment.
*   Please list any necessary lab equipment or materials.

### 10. *Lab Procedure* (`lab_procedure`, text, optional)
Describe the lab procedure or test you will undergo.

*   This field is optional but can provide context for laboratory staff.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about the procedure, please ask your healthcare provider.

### 11. *Lab Supervisor* (`lab_supervisor`, text, optional)
Name and contact information for the laboratory supervisor.

*   This field is optional but can provide additional contact information for laboratory staff.
*   Please provide the name and contact information for the laboratory supervisor.

### 12. *Test Results* (`test_results`, text, optional)
Any specific test results or expectations you have for your lab test or procedure.

*   This field is optional but can provide additional context for laboratory staff.
*   If you have any specific test results or expectations, describe them briefly.

### 13. *Lab Report* (`lab_report`, text, optional)
Additional lab reports or results you would like to receive.

*   This field is optional but can provide additional information to laboratory staff.
*   If you have any specific lab report requirements, describe them briefly.

### 14-25. *Lab Report 2-13* (`lab_report_2`, `lab_report_3`, etc., text, optional)
Additional lab reports or results you would like to receive.

*   These fields are optional but can provide additional context to laboratory staff.
*   If you have any specific lab report requirements, describe them briefly.

## Tips
*   Please answer all required fields thoroughly and accurately.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, ask your healthcare provider or laboratory staff for clarification.
*   Ensure you provide accurate contact information for laboratory staff to reach out to you with results or questions.
