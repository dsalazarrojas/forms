# university_lab_safety_gear_order_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit requests for safety gear to be ordered for the department's use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your department from the options provided.
2. Choose the type of equipment you need.
3. Specify the quantity required.
4. Choose a date for the requested items.
5. Enter the contact person in charge.
6. Enter the contact person's phone number.
7. Enter the contact person's email address.
8. Provide any additional comments about the request in the department comments field.
9. Provide any additional comments about the order in the order comments field.
10. Choose who the request will be assigned to.
11. Select the status of the request (Active or Inactive).
12. Enter a form ID for easy tracking.
13. Choose a date for submission.
14. Choose a date for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Department** (`user_department`, select_multiple, optional): Select your department from the options provided.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of equipment you need (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Specify the quantity required for the equipment.
* **Date Requested** (`date_requested`, date, optional): Choose a date for the requested items.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_multiple, optional): Enter the contact person in charge.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Department Comments** (`department_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments about the request in the department comments field.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments about the order in the order comments field.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Choose who the request will be assigned to (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Status** (`status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the request (Active or Inactive).
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter a form ID for easy tracking.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for submission.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for review.
