# Laboratory Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laboratory Reservation Form is used to request a laboratory reservation for a specific time slot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the department requesting the lab reservation.
2. Enter the name of the laboratory you are requesting.
3. Specify the equipment you plan to use in the lab.
4. Choose a location for the lab reservation.
5. Select the time slot for the reservation.
6. Enter the start and end times of the reservation.
7. Specify the intended use of the laboratory.
8. Enter the date of the reservation.
9. Provide any additional comments.
10. Enter your phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Requesting** (1, text, optional): Enter the name of the person requesting the lab reservation.
* **Department Requesting** (2, select_one, optional): Select the department requesting the lab reservation.
	+ Options: Department of Biology, Department of Chemistry, Department of Physics, Department of Mathematics, Other
* **Lab Name** (3, text, optional): Enter the name of the laboratory you are requesting.
* **Lab Equipment** (4, text, optional): Specify the equipment you plan to use in the lab.
* **Lab Location** (5, text, optional): Choose a location for the lab reservation.
* **Time Slot** (6, date, optional): Select the time slot for the reservation.
* **Start Time** (7, time, optional): Enter the start time of the reservation.
* **End Time** (8, time, optional): Enter the end time of the reservation.
* **Intended Use** (9, text, optional): Specify the intended use of the laboratory.
* **Reservation Date** (10, date, optional): Enter the date of the reservation.
* **Comments** (11, note, optional): Provide any additional comments.
* **Phone Number** (12, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (13, email, optional): Enter your email address.
