**University Laboratory Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is designed to help inspectors and lab technicians record the condition and maintenance status of equipment in a university laboratory setting. It's used to track equipment inspections, maintenance schedules, and other relevant information to ensure that laboratory equipment is in good working condition and meets safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the user's name in the "user name" field. This field is optional but helps to identify the person who conducted the inspection.
2. Enter the equipment's ID number in the "equipment id" field. This field is also optional but helps to identify the specific equipment being inspected.
3. Choose the equipment's condition from the "equipment condition" dropdown menu. This field helps to classify the equipment as Good, Fair, or Poor.
4. Select the maintenance status from the "maintenance status" dropdown menu. This field helps to identify whether the equipment is due for scheduled or unscheduled maintenance.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments about the inspection in the "notes" field.
6. Choose the date and time of the inspection using the "inspection date" and "inspection time" fields.
7. Enter the last maintenance date and the last maintenance status using the "last maintenance date" and "last maintenance status" fields.
8. Enter the date for the next maintenance using the "next maintenance date" field.
9. Select the next maintenance status from the "next maintenance status" dropdown menu.
10. Enter the equipment model, make, serial number, and description in their respective fields.
11. Enter the name of the person responsible for the inspection and maintenance in the "inspection responsible person" and "maintenance responsible person" fields.
12. Enter the location where the equipment is used in the "location" field.
13. Select the equipment type and category from their respective dropdown menus.
14. Enter the name of the person who last used the equipment in the "last user" field.
15. Enter the date when the last update was made in the "last updated" field.
16. Select the equipment status from the "equipment status" dropdown menu.
17. Select the maintenance frequency from the "maintenance frequency" dropdown menu.
18. Enter the name of the person assigned to the equipment in the "assigned to" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name (1, user_name, optional)**: Enter your name so we can identify who conducted the inspection.
* **Equipment ID (2, equipment_id, optional)**: Enter the equipment's ID number to identify the specific equipment being inspected.
* **Equipment Condition (3, equipment_condition, optional)**: Classify the equipment as Good, Fair, or Poor based on its current condition.
* **Maintenance Status (4, maintenance_status, optional)**: Choose whether the equipment is due for scheduled or unscheduled maintenance.
* **Notes (5, notes, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Inspection Date (6, inspection_date, optional)**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Inspection Time (7, inspection_time, optional)**: Enter the time of the inspection in the format HH:MM.
* **Last Maintenance Date (8, last_maintenance_date, optional)**: Enter the date of the last maintenance performed on the equipment.
* **Last Maintenance Status (9, last_maintenance_status, optional)**: Classify the last maintenance status as Scheduled, Unscheduled, or None.
* **Next Maintenance Date (10, next_maintenance_date, optional)**: Enter the date for the next maintenance.
* **Next Maintenance Status (11, next_maintenance_status, optional)**: Classify the next maintenance status as Scheduled, Unscheduled, or None.
* **Equipment Model (12, equipment_model, optional)**: Enter the equipment model number.
* **Equipment Make (13, equipment_make, optional)**: Enter the equipment's make.
* **Equipment Serial Number (14, equipment_serial_number, optional)**: Enter the equipment's serial number.
* **Equipment Description (15, equipment_description, optional)**: Enter a brief description of the equipment.
* **Inspection Responsible Person (16, inspection_responsible_person, optional)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the inspection.
* **Maintenance Responsible Person (17, maintenance_responsible_person, optional)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the maintenance.
* **Location (18, location, optional)**: Enter the location where the equipment is used.
* **Equipment Type (19, equipment_type, optional)**: Select the type of equipment (General Laboratory Equipment, Specialized Equipment, or Other).
* **Equipment Category (20, equipment_category, optional)**: Select the category of equipment (General Equipment, Specialized Equipment, or Other).
* **Last User (21, last_user, optional)**: Enter the name of the person who last used the equipment.
* **Last Updated (22, last_updated, optional)**: Enter the date when the last update was made.
* **Equipment Status (23, equipment_status, optional)**: Classify the equipment status as In Good Condition, In Fair Condition, In Poor Condition, Maintenance Required, Maintenance Needed Soon, or Maintenance Scheduled.
* **Maintenance Frequency (24, maintenance_frequency, optional)**: Select the frequency of maintenance (Every 1 day, Every 1 week, Every 1 month, Every 6 months, or Every 1 year).
* **Assigned To (25, assigned_to, optional)**: Enter the name of the person assigned to the equipment.
 
## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Use the correct format for dates and times (MM/DD/YYYY for dates and HH:MM for times).
* If you're unsure about a field, refer to your supervisor or maintenance staff for guidance.
* Update the form regularly to reflect any changes in equipment condition or maintenance status.
