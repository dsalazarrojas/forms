# research_lab_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and document research laboratory incidents, providing a structured format for collecting information about specific events that occurred in the research lab. The form's primary purpose is to assist investigators in gathering accurate and comprehensive data about the incident.
## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by filling in the required fields, such as **Incident Summary**, **Date of Incident**, **Time of Incident**, **Location of Incident**, **Incident Nature**, **Description of Incident**, **Reported By**, **Investigator Name**, and **Incident ID**.
- Select the relevant **Incident Nature** from the provided options.
- Enter the **Incident ID**, which is a unique identifier for the incident.
- Enter the **Research Institution ID** and **Research Lab ID**, which identify the institution and lab where the incident occurred.
- If applicable, fill in the remaining fields such as **Investigation Status**, **Root Cause**, **Actions Taken**, **Follow-up**, **Notes**, and **Follow-up Actions Taken**.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Incident Summary**: A brief description of the incident.
  * (**Incident Summary**, text, required)
- **Date of Incident**: The date when the incident occurred.
  * (**Date of Incident**, date, required)
- **Time of Incident**: The time when the incident occurred.
  * (**Time of Incident**, time, required)
- **Location of Incident**: The location where the incident occurred.
  * (**Location of Incident**, text, required)
- **Incident Nature**: The type of incident that occurred (e.g., **Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4**).
  * (**Incident Nature**, select one, required)
- **Description of Incident**: A detailed description of what happened.
  * (**Description of Incident**, text, required)
- **Investigation Status**: The current status of the investigation.
  * (**Investigation Status**, select one, optional)
- **Root Cause**: The underlying reason for the incident (if known).
  * (**Root Cause**, text, optional)
- **Actions Taken**: Any actions taken in response to the incident.
  * (**Actions Taken**, text, optional)
- **Follow-up**: Details about any follow-up activities related to the incident.
  * (**Follow-up**, text, optional)
- **Notes**: Additional comments or notes about the incident.
  * (**Notes**, text, optional)
- **Reported By**: The person who reported the incident.
  * (**Reported By**, text, required)
- **Investigator Name**: The person who is investigating the incident.
  * (**Investigator Name**, text, required)
- **Investigator Contact**: The contact information for the investigator.
  * (**Investigator Contact**, text, optional)
- **Incident Reported Date**: The date when the incident was reported.
  * (**Incident Reported Date**, date, optional)
- **Incident Closed Date**: The date when the incident was closed.
  * (**Incident Closed Date**, date, optional)
- **Follow-up Completed Date**: The date when follow-up activities were completed.
  * (**Follow-up Completed Date**, date, optional)
- **Follow-up Status**: The status of the follow-up activities.
  * (**Follow-up Status**, select one, optional)
- **Follow-up Actions Taken**: Any actions taken during follow-up activities.
  * (**Follow-up Actions Taken**, text, optional)
- **Follow-up Notes**: Additional comments about the follow-up activities.
  * (**Follow-up Notes**, text, optional)
- **Incident ID**: A unique identifier for the incident.
  * (**Incident ID**, number, required)
- **Research Institution ID**: The ID of the institution where the incident occurred.
  * (**Research Institution ID**, number, required)
- **Research Lab ID**: The ID of the research lab where the incident occurred.
  * (**Research Lab ID**, number, required)
- **Incident Number**: A unique number for the incident.
  * (**Incident Number**, number, optional)
- **Investigator**: The person conducting the investigation.
  * (**Investigator**, text, optional)
## Tips
- Be concise when filling out the form, providing only the most relevant information.
- Use clear and descriptive language to explain what happened.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
- Review the form carefully for accuracy before submitting it.
