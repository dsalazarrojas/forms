# University Laboratory Session Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Laboratory Session Evaluation Form is used by laboratory staff to collect feedback and ratings from students about their recent laboratory sessions. This form helps to identify areas of improvement and to provide students with a platform to express their thoughts and suggestions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Select the relevant session details from the dropdown menu.
3. Provide your name and contact information if you want to be contacted for further feedback.
4. Rate the session and provide any comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a brief section where you can write your thoughts and feelings about the session. There is no specific requirement for this field, so you can write as much or as little as you like.
* **Session Details**: Select the session that this form is for from the dropdown menu. This will help the laboratory staff to identify which session you are referring to in your feedback.
* **Instructor**: Please provide the name of the instructor who led the session. This will help the instructor to receive feedback specifically about their teaching style and methods.
* **Materials**: Select the materials used during the session that you found to be most helpful or not helpful. You can choose from the options provided.
* **Rating**: Rate the session on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest. This will give an indication of how you felt about the session.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional thoughts or suggestions, please provide them here. This will help the laboratory staff to understand your perspective and to make improvements.
* **Suggestions**: Similar to comments, this is a field where you can provide any additional suggestions you may have for improvement.
* **Email**: If you would like to be contacted by the laboratory staff for further feedback or clarification, please provide your email address.
* **Phone**: If you would like to be contacted by phone for further feedback or clarification, please provide your phone number.
* **Phone**: Note: This is not necessary but it's there just in case.

Please take the time to fill out this form thoroughly, as it will help us improve our services and ensure that future laboratory sessions are enjoyable and beneficial for everyone involved.
