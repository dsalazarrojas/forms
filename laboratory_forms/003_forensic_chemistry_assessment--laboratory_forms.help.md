# Forensic Chemistry Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Forensic Chemistry Assessment form is used to collect and document the forensic chemistry assessment results for a given sample or case.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, select the "Forensic Chemistry Assessment" form from the relevant menu or dashboard.
2.  Review the form fields carefully and ensure that you are familiar with each field's purpose and requirements.
3.  Complete each field according to the instructions provided in the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Knowledge Check Questions**: This field is used to capture answers to knowledge check questions related to the assessment. Enter your responses in the text area provided.
* **Technique Ratings**: This field is used to rate the technique employed during the assessment. Enter a numerical value between 1 and 10, where 1 represents the lowest rating and 10 represents the highest rating.
* **Integrity Confirmed**: Select "Correct" if the assessment results are confirmed to be true and accurate. Select "Incorrect" if the assessment results are not confirmed to be true and accurate. Select "No Answer" if no answer is provided.
* **Technique Confidence**: This field is used to capture the confidence level of the technique employed during the assessment. Enter a numerical value between 1 and 10, where 1 represents the lowest confidence level and 10 represents the highest confidence level.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is used to capture any additional comments or notes related to the assessment. Enter your comments in the text area provided.
* **Submission Type**: Select the submission type from the options provided, such as "Lab Exercise", "Technique Check", or "Other (please specify)".
* **Other Submission Type**: If "Other (please specify)" is selected, enter the type of submission in this field.
* **Other Submission Details**: If "Other (please specify)" is selected, enter more details about the submission in this field.
* **Submitted By**: This field is used to capture information about the person who submitted the form. Enter your name in the text area provided.
* **Submitted At**: This field is used to capture the date and time when the form was submitted. This field is automatically populated based on the system's timestamp.
* **Submitted To**: This field is used to capture information about the person or department that the form was submitted to. Enter their name in the text area provided.
* **Review Status**: Select the review status of the form from the options provided, such as "Approved", "Rejected", or "Review Required".
* **Submitted By Email**: This field is used to capture the email address of the person who submitted the form. Enter their email address in the format "example@example.com".
* **Submitted By Phone**: This field is used to capture the phone number of the person who submitted the form. Enter the phone number in the format "123-456-7890".
* **Submitted By Phone Type**: Select the type of phone from the options provided, such as "Work", "Home", or "Mobile".
* **Extension**: This field is used to capture the extension number of the person who submitted the form. Enter the extension number in the format "123".
* **Phone Number**: This field is used to capture the phone number of the person who submitted the form. Enter the phone number in the format "123-456-7890".
* **Area Code**: This field is used to capture the area code of the person who submitted the form. Enter the area code in the format "123".
* **Carrier**: This field is used to capture the carrier information of the person who submitted the form. Enter the carrier information in the format "Example Carrier".
* **Comments**: This field is used to capture any comments or notes related to the submission. Enter your comments in the text area provided.
* **Submitted By Name**: This field is used to capture the name of the person who submitted the form. Enter your name in the text area provided.
* **Submitted By Title**: This field is used to capture the title of the person who submitted the form. Enter their title in the text area provided.
* **Submitted By Department**: This field is used to capture the department of the person who submitted the form. Enter their department in the text area provided.
* **Submitted By Lab**: This field is used to capture the lab information of the person who submitted the form. Enter their lab information in the text area provided.
* **Submitted By Location**: This field is used to capture the location of the person who submitted the form. Enter their location in the text area provided.
