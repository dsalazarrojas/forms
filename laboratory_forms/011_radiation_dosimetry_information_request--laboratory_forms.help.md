**Radiation Dosimetry Information Request - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to radiation exposure incidents for accurate reporting and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is your radiation exposure?** (`radiation_exposure`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of your radiation exposure incident.
*   **Dose Amount (mSv)** (`dose_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of radiation you were exposed to, in milliseverts (mSv).
*   **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Provide the location where the exposure occurred.
*   **Date of Exposure** (`date_of_exposure`, date, required): Enter the date of the radiation exposure incident.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
*   **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): List any medical conditions you may have.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Contact EMail** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the hospital or facility where the exposure occurred (if applicable).
*   **Hospital Address** (`hospital_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the hospital or facility where the exposure occurred (if applicable).
*   **Radiation Type** (`radiation_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of radiation involved in the exposure.
*   **Contact Department** (`contact_department`, text, optional): Enter the name of the department responsible for the contact (if applicable).
*   **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, required): Enter the date you received the exposure information.
*   **Date of Notification** (`date_of_notification`, date, required): Enter the date you were notified of the exposure.

**Tips**

*   Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department for guidance.
*   Please provide as much detail as possible for accurate and complete information. 

Please refer to the form's "Required" status in the field-by-field explanation to determine the fields that must be filled in for accurate reporting. 

Please note that some fields may not be applicable, but it is still recommended to fill them in for completeness sake.
