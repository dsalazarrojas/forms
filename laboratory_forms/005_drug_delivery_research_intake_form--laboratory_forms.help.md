# Drug Delivery Research Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for researchers to submit information about their research inquiries, such as areas of interest, contact details, and follow-up requirements. It is essential to provide accurate and detailed information to ensure efficient handling of your research inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant area(s) of interest for your research from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide your name and contact email address for follow-up communication.
3. Enter your location (e.g., department or institution).
4. If your area of interest is not listed, provide a brief description in the "Other" field.
5. Choose the frequency of follow-up reminders you would like to receive (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
6. Select the team member you would like to be assigned to for follow-up actions.
7. Enter the follow-up date and time (if applicable).
8. Provide any additional notes or next steps for the follow-up action.
9. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Inquiries** (2, **research_inquiries**, Select One, required: false): Select one or more of the areas of interest that apply to your research inquiry.
	+ This field is not required but is essential to categorize and prioritize your research inquiry.
* **Name** (3, **name**, Text, required: false): Enter your name to be associated with the research inquiry.
* **Description** (4, **description**, Text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your research inquiry if it does not fit into any of the listed areas of interest.
* **Contact Email** (5, **contact_email**, Email, required: false): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Phone** (6, **contact_phone**, Text, required: false): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Location** (7, **location**, Text, required: false): Enter your location (e.g., department or institution).
* **Research Area of Interest** (8, **research_area_of_interest, Select Multiple, required: true): Select all that apply from the list of research areas of interest for your research inquiry.
* **Research Area of Interest Other** (9, **research_area_of_interest_other**, Text, required: false): Enter the area of interest not listed in the previous field.
* **Follow-up Request** (10, **follow_up_request**, Date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to be followed up on.
* **Follow-up Time** (11, **follow_up_time**, Time, required: false): Enter the time you would like to be followed up on.
* **Follow-up Date** (12, **follow_up_date**, Date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to be followed up on.
* **Next Steps** (13, **next_steps**, Text, required: false): Provide any additional notes or next steps for the follow-up action.
* **Notes** (14, **notes**, Note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments for the follow-up action.
* **Assigned To** (15, **assigned_to**, Select One, required: false): Select the team member you would like to be assigned to for follow-up actions.
* **Follow-up Reminders** (16, **follow_up_reminders, Select Multiple, required: false): Choose the frequency of follow-up reminders you would like to receive.
* **Reminders Sent** (17, **reminders_sent, Number, required: false): Enter the number of reminders already sent.
* **Next Follow-up** (18, **next_follow_up, Date, required: false): Enter the date and time of the next follow-up.
* **Follow-up Time** (19, **next_follow_up_time, Time, required: false): Enter the time of the next follow-up.
* **Follow-up By** (20, **follow_up_by, Text, required: false): Enter the person responsible for follow-up actions.
* **Follow-up Notes** (21, **follow_up_notes, Note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments for the follow-up action.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (22, **follow_up_frequency, Select One, required: false): Choose the frequency of follow-up actions.
* **Follow-up Last Sent** (23, **follow_up_last_sent, Select One, required: false): Select the most recent follow-up action sent.
* **Follow-up Sent To** (24, **follow_up_sent_to, Select Multiple, required: false): Select the person(s) to whom the follow-up action was sent.
* **Follow-up Last Sent To** (25, **follow_up_last_sent_to, Select Multiple, required: false): Select the person(s) to whom the last follow-up action was sent.
