# University Lab Safety Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Lab Safety Equipment Requisition Form is used to collect information on laboratory safety equipment needs. This form is essential for ensuring that all lab personnel are aware of the safety protocols and guidelines in place to prevent accidents and ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the department name where the equipment is needed.
2. Provide the correct lab name where the equipment will be used.
3. Describe the safety equipment you are requesting in detail.
4. Specify the correct location within the lab where the equipment will be stored or used.
5. Enter the quantity of the required equipment.
6. Indicate whether the approval from relevant personnel has been received.
7. If approved, enter the date of approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department** (1): This field is used to enter the name of the department where the equipment is required (e.g. Chemistry, Biology, Physics).
* **Lab** (2): This field is used to enter the name of the lab where the equipment will be used (e.g. Laboratory 1, Laboratory 2, etc.).
* **Equipment Type** (3): This field is used to describe the type of safety equipment you are requesting (e.g. fire extinguishers, first aid kits, etc.).
* **Description** (4): This field is used to provide a detailed description of the equipment you are requesting.
* **Location** (5): This field is used to specify the correct location within the lab where the equipment will be stored or used.
* **Quantity** (6): This field is used to enter the number of equipment units you are requesting.
* **Approved by** (7): This field is used to indicate whether the approval from relevant personnel has been received.
* **Approved date** (8): This field is used to enter the date of approval if it has been received.
