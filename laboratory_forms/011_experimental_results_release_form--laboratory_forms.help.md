# experimental_results_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The experimental results release form is a tool for laboratory personnel to share experimental results with designated recipients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Form Recipient Name field with the name of the person or team you want to share the results with.
2. Enter the recipient's email address in the Recipient Email field.
3. Select the Form Purpose from the options provided (Test, Other, or Training).
4. Enter the Recipient Phone number for the person or team (optional).
5. Provide the Date of Birth (if applicable) of the person or team receiving the results.
6. Sign the Form Signature field to confirm that the results are being shared with the intended recipient.
7. Enter the time when the form was signed at (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Recipient Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the person or team receiving the results.
* **Recipient Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the person or team receiving the results.
* **Form Purpose** (select one, optional): Select the reason for sharing the experimental results (Test, Other, or Training).
* **Recipient Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or team receiving the results.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Provide the Date of Birth of the person or team receiving the results, if applicable.
* **Form Signature** (note, optional): Sign this field to confirm that the results are being shared with the intended recipient.
* **Form Signed At** (time, optional): Enter the time when the form was signed. 

## Tips
* Double-check the recipient's details for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you've selected the correct Form Purpose for your specific needs.
* If you're unsure about any field or the form's purpose, contact your supervisor or lab manager for clarification.
