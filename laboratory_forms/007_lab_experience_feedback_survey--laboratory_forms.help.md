# Lab Experience Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect feedback from users about their experience with the laboratory facilities, equipment, and instructions. Your input will help us understand your experiences and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the different pages and fill in the required information. If a field is marked as required, please make sure to fill it in. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text fields provided at the end of each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facilities** (Facilities): A text area to provide any notes or comments about the facilities. (Note)
* **Laboratory Instructions** (Lab Instructions): A text area to provide any additional comments or suggestions about the laboratory instructions. (Note)
* **Equipment Quality** (Equipment Quality): Rate the quality of the equipment used in the lab on a scale of 1-5. 
* **Instruction Quality** (Instruction Quality): Rate the quality of the instructions provided by the lab staff on a scale of 1-5.
* **Facilities Condition** (Facilities Condition): A text area to provide any comments or suggestions about the facilities condition. (Text)
* **Equipment Quality** (Equipment Quality): A text area to provide any comments or suggestions about the equipment quality. (Text)
* **Overall Experience** (Overall Experience): A text area to provide an overall summary of your experience. (Text)
* **Comments** (Comments): A text area to provide any additional comments or suggestions. (Note)
* **Facilities Suggestions** (Facilities Suggestions): A text area to provide any suggestions about the facilities. (Text)
* **Lab Instructions Improvement** (Lab Instructions Improvement): A text area to provide any suggestions about the laboratory instructions. (Text)
* **Equipment Suggestions** (Equipment Suggestions): A text area to provide any suggestions about the equipment. (Text)
* **Instruction Suggestions** (Instruction Suggestions): A text area to provide any suggestions about the instructions. (Text)
* **Facilities Rating** (Facilities Rating): Rate the facilities on a scale of 1-5.
* **Equipment Rating** (Equipment Rating): Rate the equipment on a scale of 1-5.
* **Instruction Rating** (Instruction Rating): Rate the instructions on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Rating** (Overall Rating): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Will Recommend** (Will Recommend): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you will recommend the lab.
* **Will Recommend To** (Will Recommend To): Select "Yes" or "No" for the people you will recommend the lab to.
* **Comments on Services** (Comments on Services): A text area to provide any comments or suggestions about the services provided by the lab.

Please note that this survey is completely optional, and all responses will be treated with confidentiality and respect. Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey and provide your feedback!
