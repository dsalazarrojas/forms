# Plant Cell Experiment Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plant Cell Experiment Feedback form is designed to collect feedback from students or researchers on their experiences with plant cell exercises, lab activities, or experiments. The form is intended to help instructors evaluate the effectiveness of the lab material, provide guidance on areas for improvement, and inform future developments of the course content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to fill out all mandatory fields.
3. If you have any comments or concerns, use the note fields.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name** (Student name, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): This field is optional and will be used for communication purposes. If you do not have an email address, do not worry about it.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date you completed the lab activity or exercise.
* **Time** (Time, time, required): Select the time you spent on the lab activity or exercise.
* **Select One** (Select One, select_one, required): Choose one of the options provided that best describes your experience.
* **Select Multiple** (Select Multiple, select_multiple, optional): Select as many options as apply from the list provided that best describe your experience.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): If you have any comments or concerns, please use this field to add them.
* **Select One Date** (Select One Date, select_one, optional): Choose one date from the list provided that best describes a relevant date for your experience.
* **Select Multiple Date** (Select Multiple Date, select_multiple, optional): Select as many dates as apply from the list provided that best describe your experience.
* **Date Range** (Date Range, date, optional): Enter a date range that best describes your experience.
* **Time Range** (Time Range, time, optional): Select a time range that best describes the time spent on the lab activity or exercise.
* **Lab Instructor Name** (Lab Instructor Name, text, optional): Please enter the name of your lab instructor.
* **Lab Instructor Comments** (Lab Instructor Comments, text, optional): If you have any comments or concerns for the instructor, please use this field.
* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): This field is optional and will not be used for communication purposes. If you do not have a phone number, do not worry about it.
* **Lab Exercise** (Lab Exercise, text, optional): Please describe the lab exercise you completed.
* **What did you learn** (What did you learn, text, required): Please describe what you learned from the lab exercise.
* **What did you find difficult** (What did you find difficult, text, optional): If you found any part of the lab exercise difficult, please describe it.
* **What did you find interesting** (What did you find interesting, text, optional): If you found any part of the lab exercise interesting, please describe it.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when answering each question.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your instructor for guidance.
* Make sure to submit only once.
* Please do not submit false or misleading information.
