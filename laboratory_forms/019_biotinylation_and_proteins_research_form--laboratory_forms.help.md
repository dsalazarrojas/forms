# Biotinylation And Proteins Research Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from researchers working with biotinylation, a technique used to modify and study proteins. By completing this form, you will be providing detailed information about your background, experience, and research goals related to biotinylation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your researcher information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide details about your institution, including the name and department where you work.
3. Select the types of proteins you have experience with and are currently studying.
4. Describe your research objectives and goals related to biotinylation.
5. Choose the biotinylation methods you use and are skilled with.
6. Select the detection methods you employ.
7. List the sample preparation techniques you use.
8. Identify the applications and outcomes of your research.
9. Select the areas where your research is applied.
10. Describe your key findings or results.
11. Provide information on your publications related to biotinylation.
12. List 2-3 recent publications.
13. Share any additional comments or information about your research.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`researcher_first_name`, `text`, `required`): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`researcher_last_name`, `text`, `required`): Your last name.
* **Email Address** (`researcher_email`, `email`, `required`): Your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (`researcher_phone`, `text`, `required`): Your contact phone number.
* **Professional Title** (`researcher_title`, `text`, `required`): Your position or role.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, `text`, `required`): The name of the university or research organization you work for.
* **Department or Lab** (`department`, `text`, `required`): Your department or lab.
* **Institution Type** (`institution_type`, `select_one`, `required`): The type of organization where you work, such as University, Research Institute, etc.
* **Experience with Biotinylation** (`biotinylation_experience`, `select_one`, `required`): Select how many years you have worked with biotinylation.
* **Types of Proteins You Study** (`protein_types_studied`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the types of proteins you have experience with.
* **Current Research Objectives** (`research_objectives`, `text`, `required`): Describe your research goals related to biotinylation.
* **Biotinylation Methods** (`biotinylation_methods`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the biotinylation methods you use.
* **Detection Methods** (`detection_methods`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select how you detect biotinylated proteins.
* **Sample Preparation Techniques** (`sample_preparation`, `text`, `required`): List the methods you use to prepare samples.
* **Application Areas** (`application_areas`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select where your research is applied, such as Diagnostic Development, Therapeutic Development, etc.
* **Key Findings or Results** (`key_findings`, `text`, `required`): Describe your key findings or results.

* **Citation Impact or H-Index** (`citation_impact`, `text`, `required`): If available, provide your citation impact or H-Index.
