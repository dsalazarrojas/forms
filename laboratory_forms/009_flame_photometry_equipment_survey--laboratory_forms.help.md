# Flame Photometry Equipment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users about their flame photometry equipment for maintenance or troubleshooting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the equipment name in the "Equipment Name" field.
2. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
3. Select a rating for the equipment from the "Rating" field.
4. If you have any comments, enter them in the "Comments" field.
5. Enter the date you used the equipment in the "Date" field.
6. Enter the time you used the equipment in the "Time" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. List any issues you experienced with the equipment in the "List any issues" field, and describe each issue in more detail in the "Issue description" field.
10. Indicate the priority of improvement for each issue in the "Priority of Improvement" field.
11. Enter any suggestions you have for improving the equipment in the "Suggestion" field.
12. Finally, select how you want to submit the form: "Submit", "Save and continue editing", or "Cancel".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Name**: Enter the name of the flame photometry equipment you are surveying.
* **User Name**: Enter your name, which will be associated with this survey.
* **Rating**: Select one of the following ratings:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the equipment.
* **Date**: Enter the date you used the equipment.
* **Time**: Enter the time you used the equipment.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **List any issues**: Select any issues you experienced with the equipment:
	+ Equipment damage
	+ Calibration issue
	+ Instruction manual not clear
	+ Other (please describe)
	+ None
* **Issue description**: Describe each issue you selected in the previous field.
* **Priority of Improvement**: Indicate the priority of improvement for each issue.
* **Suggestion**: Enter any suggestions you have for improving the equipment.
* **Submit**: Select how you want to submit the form:
	+ Submit
	+ Save and continue editing
	+ Cancel

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all relevant fields to provide detailed and accurate information.
* If you have any issues, describe each one in detail in the "Issue description" field.
* If you have any suggestions, enter them in the "Suggestion" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your administrator.
