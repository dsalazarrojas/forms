# Botanical Specimen Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for botanical specimen requisition requests from researchers, institutions, and individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that you provide a valid email address and phone number for contact.
3. Clearly state the research project title and description.
4. Select the correct specimen type and taxonomic groups of interest.
5. Specify the approximate quantity needed and loan duration.
6. Choose the preferred shipping method.
7. If necessary, provide special handling requirements or funding source information.
8. Sign and date the form as electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name (1)**: Provide your full name as the person requesting the specimens.
* **Requester Title (2)**: Enter your professional title or position.
* **Institution Name (3)**: Enter the name of your affiliated institution or university.
* **Institution Address (4)**: Enter the complete mailing address of your institution.
* **Requester Email (5)**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Requester Phone (6)**: Provide a contact phone number.
* **Request Date (7)**: Enter the date of this requisition request.
* **Research Project Title (8)**: Enter a title for your research project or study.
* **Research Description (9)**: Describe the objectives and goals of your research project.
* **Specimen Type Requested (10)**: Select all necessary specimen types needed for your research (e.g. Herbarium sheets, Seed samples, etc).
* **Taxonomic Groups of Interest (11)**: Choose relevant plant families or genera related to your research project.
* **Geographic Region of Interest (12)**: Select the regions relevant to your research project (if applicable).
* **Approximate Quantity Needed (13)**: Choose the estimated number of specimens required.
* **Requested Loan Duration (14)**: Select the loan duration you need for the specimens.
* **Destructive Sampling Required (15)**: Specify if your research requires destructive analysis of specimens.
* **Preferred Shipping Method (16)**: Choose the desired shipping method for specimens.
* **Special Handling Requirements (17)**: If necessary, describe any special storage or handling requirements for the specimens.
* **Funding Source (18)**: If applicable, provide information about the grant or funding agency supporting your research.
* **Previous Loans from This Institution (19)**: Check if you have borrowed specimens from this institution before.
* **Return Assurance (20)**: Agree to return specimens in good condition by the agreed date.
* **Requester Signature (21)**: Sign your name as electronic signature.
* **Supervisor or Department Head Name (22)**: Enter the name of your supervising professor or department head (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments (23)**: Add any other relevant information for the curator.
Note: All fields marked as "required" are mandatory to submit the form.
