# Weekly Experiment Findings Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report the findings and progress of weekly experiments. It should be filled out by the person responsible for submitting the report at the end of each week.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the start date of the experiment in the "Week Start Date" field.
2. Enter the end date of the experiment in the "Week End Date" field.
3. Enter the name of the project in the "Project Name" field.
4. Enter the name of the experiment in the "Experiment Name" field.
5. Describe the outcome of the experiment in the "Experiment Outcome" field.
6. Optional: Enter any analysis or results from the experiment in the "Analysis" field.
7. Optional: Enter your plan for the next week in the "Next Week Plan" field.
8. Select the type of experiment (Experiment, Test, or Research) in the "Experiment Type" field.
9. Select the status of the experiment (Active, Completed, In Progress, or Canceled) in the "Experiment Status" field.
10. Enter the number of weeks of progress made on the experiment in the "Weekly Progress" field.
11. List any additional notes about the experiment in the "Experiment Notes" field.
12. Select the lab members involved in the experiment in the "Lab Members" field.
13. Select the team members involved in the experiment in the "Team Members" field.
14. Select the collaborators involved in the experiment in the "Collaborators" field.
15. Select the experiment manager responsible for the experiment in the "Experiment Manager" field.
16. Select the reviewer(s) assigned to the experiment in the "Reviewer 1" through "Reviewer 4" fields.
17. Select the person who submitted the report in the "Submitted By" field.
18. Select the person who created the report in the "Created By" field.
19. Select the person who last updated the report in the "Updated By" field.
20. Enter the date and time when the report was last updated in the "Updated At" field.
21. Enter the date and time when the report was originally submitted in the "Reported At" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Week Start Date** (`week_start_date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the start date of the experiment.
* **Week End Date** (`week_end_date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the end date of the experiment.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of the project.
* **Experiment Name** (`experiment_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of the experiment.
* **Experiment Outcome** (`experiment_outcome`, `text`, **Required**): Describe the outcome of the experiment.
* **Analysis** (`analysis`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter any analysis or results from the experiment.
* **Next Week Plan** (`next_week_plan`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your plan for the next week.
* **Experiment Type** (`experiment_type`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the type of experiment (Experiment, Test, or Research).
* **Experiment Status** (`experiment_status`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the status of the experiment (Active, Completed, In Progress, or Canceled).
* **Weekly Progress** (`weekly_progress`, `number`, **Required**): Enter the number of weeks of progress made on the experiment.
* **Experiment Notes** (`experiment_notes`, `text`, **Optional**): List any additional notes about the experiment.
* **Lab Members** (`lab_members`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the lab members involved in the experiment.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the team members involved in the experiment.
* **Collaborators** (`collaborators`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the collaborators involved in the experiment.
* **Experiment Manager** (`experiment_manager`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the experiment manager responsible for the experiment.
* **Reviewer 1** (`reviewer1`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the reviewer(s) assigned to the experiment.
* **Reviewer 2** (`reviewer2`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the reviewer(s) assigned to the experiment.
* **Reviewer 3** (`reviewer3`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the reviewer(s) assigned to the experiment.
* **Reviewer 4** (`reviewer4`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the reviewer(s) assigned to the experiment.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the person who submitted the report.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the person who created the report.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the person who last updated the report.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date and time when the report was last updated.
* **Reported At** (`reported_at`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date and time when the report was originally submitted.
