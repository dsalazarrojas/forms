# Genetic Feedback Loop Research Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect data for genetic feedback loop research, which involves studying the effects of genetic variations on specific traits or behaviors. The data collected will help researchers understand and analyze the genetic feedback loop phenomenon.

## How To Complete This Form

This form has several pages with different fields to be filled out. Please follow the instructions on each page to complete the form correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Details** (`study_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief summary of your study, including the purpose, goals, and any relevant background information.
* **Subject Info** (`subject_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter information about the subjects participating in the study, including their age, gender, and any other relevant details.
* **Feedback Loop Type** (`feedback_loop_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of feedback loop you are researching, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Study Duration** (`study_duration`, `text`, required: false): Enter the duration of the study, including the start and end dates.
* **Researcher Name** (`researcher_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and title/position as a researcher.
* **Researcher Email** (`researcher_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Study Notes** (`study_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the study.
* **Subject ID** (`subject_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter the ID number or code for the subject participating in the study.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any applicable options for the consent form, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Study Date** (`study_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the study.
* **Study Time** (`study_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the study.
* **Study Location** (`study_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the study.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If a field is not applicable, try to leave it blank rather than providing incorrect information.
* Use the correct format for date and time fields.
* Select the correct options for the consent form field.
* If providing an email address, make sure it is valid and properly formatted.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
