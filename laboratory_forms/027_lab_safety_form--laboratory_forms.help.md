# Lab Safety Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and report on lab safety procedures and risks. It is essential to complete this form to ensure that laboratory workers and supervisors are aware of potential hazards and are taking adequate measures to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all applicable questions truthfully and accurately.
2. Select only the options that apply to your laboratory's safety procedures and risks.
3. Provide a clear and detailed description for each field as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Lab Safety Form**
*   **Lab Safety Form** (`lab_safety_form`, text, optional):
    * This is the title of the form and is not a field to fill.
*   **Lab Safety Risks**
    * This field is used to list any potential risks or hazards present in the laboratory. For example, if you are working with chemicals, you should include chemical spills, explosions, or other potential risks associated with those chemicals.
*   **Lab Safety Measures**
    * In this field, describe the measures you will take to mitigate the risks identified in the lab safety risks field. This could include wearing protective equipment, following safety protocols, or taking other steps to minimize risk.
*   **Lab Safety Training**
    * Select the option that best describes your current level of lab safety training. This will help track and record your training status.
*   **Lab Safety Equipment**
    * Describe the safety equipment available in the laboratory, such as fire extinguishers, first aid kits, or other emergency equipment.
*   **Lab Safety Emergency Procedures**
    * Describe the emergency procedures in place in case of an accident or spill. This could include what steps to take, who to contact, or where to report the incident.
*   **Lab Safety Supervision**
    * Describe the supervision levels in place, such as who is responsible for ensuring safety or who is in charge during lab work.
*   **Lab Safety Training Certification**
    * Select the option that best describes your lab safety training certification, such as OSHA 30 or OSHA 100.

## Tips
*   This form is meant to be a helpful tool to document and record your lab safety procedures and risks.
*   It is not a substitute for actual safety procedures and protocols already in place.
*   Ensure that you complete the form truthfully and accurately.
