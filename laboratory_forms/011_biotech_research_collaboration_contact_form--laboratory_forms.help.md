# Biotech Research Collaboration Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Biotech Research Collaboration Contact Form is a tool used to connect researchers and organizations seeking collaboration opportunities in the biotech field. This form helps you provide essential information about your organization, research focus, and project details to facilitate a potential partnership.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Section 1: Organization Information**
	* Fill in your organization's name in the **Organization Name** field.
	* Select the type of your organization from the **Organization Type** dropdown menu.
	* Provide the website of your organization in the **Organization Website** field.
2. **Section 2: Contact Person**
	* Enter the contact person's first name in the **First Name** field.
	* Enter the contact person's last name in the **Last Name** field.
	* Provide the contact person's job title in the **Job Title** field.
	* Enter the contact person's department or research area in the **Department or Lab** field.
	* Enter the contact person's primary email address in the **Email Address** field.
	* Enter the contact person's phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
3. **Section 3: Collaboration Interest**
	* Check all the collaboration types that interest you from the **Type of Collaboration Being Sought** dropdown menu.
	* Describe your research focus in the **Research Focus Areas** field.
4. **Section 4: Project Details**
	* Enter a working title for your proposed project in the **Proposed Project Title** field.
	* Provide a brief description of your collaboration idea in the **Project Description** field.
	* Enter the expected start date and duration of the project in the **Proposed Project Timeline** field.
	* Select the funding status of your project from the **Funding Status** dropdown menu.
5. **Section 5: Background and Experience**
	* Enter your years of experience in the biotech field in the **Years of Experience in Biotech** field.
	* Describe your previous collaborations in the **Previous Collaborations** field.
6. **Section 6: Logistics**
	* Select the preferred location for collaboration from the **Preferred Collaboration Location** dropdown menu.
	* Check if you're willing to sign a confidentiality agreement in the **Are You Willing to Sign a Confidentiality Agreement** field.
	* Select how you prefer to handle intellectual property in the **Intellectual Property Preference** dropdown menu.
7. **Section 7: Additional Information**
	* Enter any additional notes or comments about your collaboration interest in the **Additional Notes or Comments** field.
	* Select your preferred method of contact in the **Preferred Contact Method** dropdown menu.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling out this form to ensure accurate submission.
* Double-check that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, you can contact us for assistance.
