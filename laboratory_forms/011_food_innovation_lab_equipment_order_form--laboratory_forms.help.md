# Food Innovation Lab Equipment Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit requests for equipment purchases in the Food Innovation Lab. It is intended for lab personnel to provide information about the equipment they need, including the equipment type, quantity, priority, and status. This form is reviewed and approved by lab managers before being sent to suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the **Form Header** section.
2. Select the department and building where you will be working with the equipment.
3. Enter the equipment's name, quantity, type, description, priority, and status.
4. Provide any additional notes about the equipment.
5. Select the reviewer's name and provide any review comments.
6. Confirm whether the equipment was requested by the lab or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID badge.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Department** (6, select_one, optional): Select the department you will be working with the equipment (Lab Supplies, Lab Equipment, or Other).
* **Building** (7, text, required): Enter the building where you will be working with the equipment (e.g., Main Building, North Building).
* **Floor** (8, number, required): Enter the floor number where you will be working with the equipment.
* **Room** (9, text, required): Enter the room number where you will be working with the equipment.
* **Lab** (10, text, required): Enter the lab number where you will be working with the equipment.
* **Notes** (11, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the equipment.
* **Equipment** (12, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you want to purchase or not purchase the equipment.
* **Quantity** (13, number, required): Enter the quantity of equipment needed.
* **Type** (14, select_one, optional): Select the type of equipment (Yes or No).
* **Description** (15, text, required): Enter a brief description of the equipment.
* **Priority** (16, select_one, optional): Select the priority level of the equipment (Low, Medium, High).
* **Status** (17, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the equipment (In Progress, Ordered, Shipped, In Stock).
* **Requester** (18, text, required): Enter the name of the person requesting the equipment.
* **Date Requested** (19, date, required): Enter the date the equipment was requested.
* **Ship Date** (20, date, required): Enter the ship date of the equipment.
* **Ship Status** (21, select_one, optional): Select the current ship status of the equipment (In Progress, In Transit, Shipped, In Stock).
* **Ship Comments** (22, text, required): Enter any comments about the equipment's shipment.
* **Reviewer** (23, text, required): Enter the name of the reviewer who approved the equipment request.
* **Review Comments** (24, text, optional): Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Requested By** (24, select_one, optional): Select whether the equipment was requested by the lab or not (Yes or No).

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that the equipment is ordered correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the lab manager or technical support for assistance.
* This form is only for equipment requests, do not use it for other purposes.
