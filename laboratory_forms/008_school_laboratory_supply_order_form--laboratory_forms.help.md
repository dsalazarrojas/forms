# School Laboratory Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help school personnel or users order laboratory supplies. It provides a structured way to gather information about the required laboratory supplies, making it easier for the supplier to process the order and deliver the requested items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of equipment or supplies needed from the list provided in the "Equipment Category" field.
2. Enter the total quantity of each item ordered in the "Total Quantity" field.
3. List the specific supplies ordered in the "Lab Supplies Ordered" field.
4. Choose the frequency of the order (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly) in the "Order Frequency" field.
5. Enter the date and time the order is needed in the "Date Ordered" and "Time Ordered" fields, respectively.
6. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Comments" field.
7. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
8. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Equipment Category**: Select the type of laboratory equipment or supplies needed (e.g., Microscopes, Pipettes, Test Tubes, Lab Coats).
- **Total Quantity**: Enter the total number of each item ordered (e.g., 5 microscopes).
- **Lab Supplies Ordered**: List the specific laboratory supplies ordered (e.g., pipettes, test tubes, lab coats).
- **Order Frequency**: Choose how often the order will be needed (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
- **Date Ordered**: Enter the date when the order is needed.
- **Time Ordered**: Enter the time when the order is needed.
- **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about the order.
- **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address.
- **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
