# Pathology Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a template for creating a pathology report. The report is a document that summarizes the results of a patient's laboratory tests and procedures. The form is used by laboratory professionals to ensure that all necessary information is collected and presented in a clear and concise manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **patient_name** field.
2. Enter the date of collection in the **date_of_collection** field.
3. Enter the date the report was received in the **date_received** field.
4. Choose the specimen type from the list of options in the **specimen_type** field.
5. Enter the pathology test code in the **pathology_test_code** field.
6. Enter the test results in the **results** field.
7. Enter the final diagnosis in the **final_diagnosis** field.
8. Choose the clinical comments option that best describes the case in the **clinical_comments** field.
9. Select the uploaded files (if any) in the **uploaded_files** field.
10. Choose who submitted the report from the list of options in the **submitted_by** field.
11. Enter the date the report was submitted in the **date_submitted** field.
12. Choose when the report was submitted in the **time_submitted** field.
13. Choose the option for the submitted to field.
14. Choose how the report was submitted.
15. Choose the clinical service option that best describes the case.
16. Choose the pathology test type option that best describes the case.
17. Choose the department option that best describes the case.
18. Enter the patient ID number.
19. Choose who uploaded the report.
20. Choose the report status option.
21. Enter the date the report was received (if not already filled).
22. Choose the clinical status option.
23. Choose the pathology service option.
24. Choose the specimen ID number.
25. Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **patient_name** (patient_name, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears in the medical record.
- **date_of_collection** (date, required): Enter the date of the laboratory tests or procedures.
- **date_received** (date, required): Enter the date the report was received in the laboratory.
- **specimen_type** (text, required): Choose the type of specimen collected (e.g. blood, urine, tissue).
- **pathology_test_code** (text, required): Enter the pathology test code as per the laboratory's internal guidelines.
- **results** (text, required): Enter the results of the laboratory tests or procedures.
- **final_diagnosis** (text, required): Enter the final diagnosis or conclusion of the case.
- **clinical_comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional clinical comments about the case.
- **uploaded_files** (select_multiple, optional): Select any files that were uploaded with the report (e.g. images, lab results).
- **submitted_by** (select_one, required): Choose who submitted the report from the list of options.
- **date_submitted** (date, required): Enter the date the report was submitted.
- **time_submitted** (time, optional): Choose when the report was submitted.
- **submitted_to** (select_one, optional): Choose the option for the submitted to field.
- **submitted_via** (select_one, optional): Choose how the report was submitted.
- **clinical_service** (select_one, optional): Choose the clinical service option that best describes the case.
- **department** (select_one, required): Choose the department that best describes the case.
- **patient_id** (number, required): Enter the patient ID number.
- **uploaded_by** (select_one, required): Choose who uploaded the report from the list of options.
- **report_status** (select_one, required): Choose the report status option.
- **date_received** (date, required): Enter the date the report was received.
- **clinical_status** (select_one, required): Choose the clinical status option.
- **pathology_service** (select_one, optional): Choose the pathology service option.
- **pathology_test_type** (select_one, required): Choose the pathology test type option.
- **specimen_id** (number, optional): Enter the specimen ID number.
- **submitted_to_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person or department submitted to.
- **clinical_service** (select_one, optional): Choose the clinical service option.
- **pathology_test_type** (select_one, required): Choose the pathology test type option.
- **specimen_id** (number, optional): Enter the specimen ID number.

**Tips**
- Make sure to fill all required fields.
- Choose the correct options for each field based on the case.
- Use the uploaded files if necessary.
- Review the report carefully before submitting.
