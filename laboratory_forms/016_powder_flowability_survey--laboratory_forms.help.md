# Powder Flowability Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Powder Flowability Survey is designed to assess the flowability of powders, which is a crucial parameter in various industrial and scientific applications. This form helps in evaluating the flowability of powders used in different processes, including pharmaceutical, food, and chemical industries. The survey collects information on the handling, storage, and flowability of powders, providing valuable insights for powder manufacturers, researchers, and quality control teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction on the first page, which provides context and background information on the powder flowability survey.
2. Answer the questions in the general handling and flowability sections, providing information on the powder's general handling characteristics and flowability.
3. Rate the flowability of the powder on the scale of 0-5, indicating its flowability from poor to excellent.
4. Provide comments on the flowability rating, if necessary.
5. Answer the questions in the handling, storage, and transportation sections, providing information on how the powder is typically handled, stored, and transported.
6. Complete the flowability rating, handling rating, and average flowability rating sections.
7. Enter information on the type of powder being evaluated.
8. Enter any comments or additional information in the comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1): This is the starting page that provides background information on the powder flowability survey. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose and guidelines of the survey.
* **General Handling** (`powder_handling`, Page 2): This field asks for a brief description of the general handling characteristics of the powder, such as its behavior when poured or poured into a container.
* **Powder Flowability** (`powder_flowability`, Page 3): This field asks for a brief description of the powder's flowability, or how easily it flows or falls from a container.
* **Flowability Rating** (`flowability_rating`, Page 4): Rate the powder's flowability on a scale of 0 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Flowability Rating Comment** (`flowability_rating_comment`, Page 5): Provide any comments or explanations for the flowability rating.
* **Flowability Scale** (`flowability_scale`, Page 6): This field asks for a more detailed rating of the powder's flowability, on a scale of 0-5.
* **Flowability Scale Comment** (`flowability_scale_comment`, Page 7): Provide any comments or explanations for the flowability scale rating.
* **Handling** (`handling`, Page 8): This field asks for information on how the powder is typically handled, such as its behavior when poured or poured into a container.
* **Handling Comment** (`handling_comment`, Page 9): Provide any comments or explanations for the handling characteristics.
* **Storage** (`storage`, Page 10): This field asks for information on how the powder is typically stored, such as in a container or bag.
* **Storage Comment** (`storage_comment`, Page 11): Provide any comments or explanations for the storage conditions.
* **Packaging** (`packaging`, Page 12): This field asks for information on how the powder is typically packaged, such as in a bag or container.
* **Packaging Comment** (`packaging_comment`, Page 13): Provide any comments or explanations for the packaging conditions.
* **Transportation** (`transportation`, Page 14): This field asks for information on how the powder is typically transported, such as by truck or plane.
* **Transportation Comment** (`transportation_comment`, Page 15): Provide any comments or explanations for the transportation conditions.
* **Handling Rating** (`handling_rating`, Page 16): Rate the powder's handling characteristics on a scale of 0-5.
* **Handling Rating Comment** (`handling_rating_comment`, Page 17): Provide any comments or explanations for the handling rating.
* **Average Flowability Rating** (`flowability_rating_averaged`, Page 18): This field displays the average flowability rating of the powder.
* **Average Flowability Rating Comment** (`flowability_rating_averaged_comment`, Page 19): Provide any comments or explanations for the average flowability rating.
* **Powder Type** (`powder_type`, Page 20): This field asks for the type of powder being evaluated.
* **Powder Type Comment** (`powder_type_comment`, Page 21): Provide any comments or explanations for the powder type.
* **Flowability Rating Range** (`flowability_rating_range`, Page 22): This field asks for a range of flowability ratings for the powder.
* **Flowability Rating Range Comment** (`flowability_rating_range_comment`, Page 23): Provide any comments or explanations for the flowability rating range.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Page 24): This is the final comments section, where you can enter any additional information or comments.

Please note that some fields may have additional hints or explanations provided by the survey administrator.
