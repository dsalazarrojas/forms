# Medical Laboratory Technician Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants who are interested in becoming a medical laboratory technician. It is used to gather personal and professional details that will be used to evaluate your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the first section.
2. Provide your medical license number (if applicable).
3. Enter any other required information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (Text, Required: False)
• This field is used to collect your first name.

### 2. **Last Name** (Text, Required: False)
• This field is used to collect your last name.

### 3. **Date Of Birth** (Date, Required: False)
• Please enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.

### 4. **Contact Email** (Email, Required: False)
• Enter your valid contact email address.

### 5. **Work Phone** (Text, Required: False)
• Enter your work phone number.

### 6. **Address** (Text, Required: False)
• Enter your mailing address.

### 7. **Medical License Number** (Text, Required: False)
• If you hold a medical license, enter the number.

## Tips
- Ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- Review the form carefully before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
