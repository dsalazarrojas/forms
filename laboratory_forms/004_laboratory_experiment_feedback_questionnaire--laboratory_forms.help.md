This help guide is intended to assist end-users in completing the "Laboratory Experiment Feedback Questionnaire" form. The form is used to gather feedback from participants in laboratory experiments, and its purpose is to understand their experiences and perceptions of the experiment. This form is designed to collect demographic information, feedback about the experiment, and any additional comments or suggestions participants may have.

To ensure that the form is completed accurately and effectively, please review this guide carefully before starting.

## Purpose
The "Laboratory Experiment Feedback Questionnaire" is used to gather feedback from participants in laboratory experiments. The form collects demographic information, feedback about the experiment, and additional comments or suggestions from participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required demographic fields, including Laboratory Experiment ID, Lab Experiment Name, Participant Name, Participant ID, and Phone.
2. Provide feedback about the experiment by answering the open-ended questions.
3. Indicate your likelihood of participating in another similar experiment.
4. Select your level of enjoyment during the lab.
5. Add any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Laboratory Experiment ID** (required): Enter the unique identifier of the laboratory experiment you participated in.
* **Lab Experiment Name** (required): Enter the name of the laboratory experiment you participated in.
* **Participant Name** (required): Enter your name as the participant in the laboratory experiment.
* **Participant ID** (required): Enter your participant ID as assigned by the lab.
* **How did you feel about the experiment?** (optional): Share your thoughts about the experiment. This is an open-ended question to gather your feelings and impressions.
* **What did you learn from the experiment?** (optional): Share what you learned or gained from participating in the laboratory experiment.
* **How likely are you to participate in another similar experiment?** (required): Indicate your likelihood of participating in another similar experiment. Choose one of the options: "Very Likely", "A Bit", or "Not Very Likely".
* **Did you enjoy the lab?** (optional): Indicate your level of enjoyment during the laboratory experiment. Choose one of the options: "Very Much", "A Bit", or "Not Much".
* **Additional Comments** (optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you have about the laboratory experiment.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Lab Experiment Date** (required): Enter the date of the laboratory experiment you participated in.
* **Lab Experiment Time** (required): Enter the time of the laboratory experiment you participated in.
* **Minimalist** (optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions about the laboratory experiment or any other topic.
