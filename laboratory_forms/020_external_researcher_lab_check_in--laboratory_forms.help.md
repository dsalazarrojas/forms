# External Researcher Lab Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The External Researcher Lab Check In form is designed for external researchers to check in when visiting the lab, providing necessary information about their visit.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your **Form Title** in the text field at the top of the page. This field is optional.
2. Provide your **researcher_name** in the text field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your **contact_email** in the email field. This is a required field.
4. If you have a **phone**, enter it in the text field. This field is optional.
5. Enter the name of the **lab_name** you are visiting. This is a required field.
6. Select the **check_in_time** when you plan to check in. This is a required field in the format of HH:MM.
7. Enter the duration of your visit in **visit_duration** field. This is the number of hours you plan to visit.
8. Select the **visit_purpose** of your visit from the dropdown options (Other, Meeting, Training, Tour).
9. Choose which lab **lab_equipment** you plan to use during your visit. You can select multiple options.
10. If you have any comments or information to share with the lab staff, enter them in the **researcher_comments** field. This field is optional.
11. If you have any comments or information to share with the lab staff, enter them in the **lab_comments** field. This field is optional.
12. Finally, select **Submit** to complete and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: Enter a brief title for your visit, e.g., "External Researcher Visit for Meeting".
* **researcher_name**: Enter your name as you want it to be displayed in the lab's records.
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached during or after your visit.
* **phone**: If you have a phone number, enter it so lab staff can contact you if needed.
* **lab_name**: Enter the name of the lab you are visiting.
* **check_in_time**: Select when you plan to check in to the lab, e.g., "08:30".
* **visit_duration**: Enter the number of hours you plan to spend in the lab.
* **visit_purpose**: Select the reason for your visit (Other, Meeting, Training, Tour).
* **lab_equipment**: Choose which lab equipment you plan to use, e.g., "1", "2", and "3".
* **researcher_comments**: Enter any comments or information to share with the lab staff.
* **lab_comments**: Enter any comments or information to share with the lab staff.
* **submit**: Select this option to complete and submit your form.
