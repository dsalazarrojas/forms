# Lab Equipment Inventory Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lab Equipment Inventory Survey is a form used to collect information about the laboratory equipment in the lab. It helps to keep track of the status of the equipment, including its condition, maintenance status, and who is responsible for its maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Title**: Enter the title of the equipment, if any.
2. **Equipment Name**: Enter the name of the equipment you are reporting on.
3. **Equipment Model**: Enter the model number or manufacturer's name of the equipment.
4. **Equipment Type**: Choose the type of equipment from the options provided.
5. **Location**: Enter the location where the equipment is kept or used.
6. **Condition**: Describe the condition of the equipment. Is it in good working order or does it need maintenance?
7. **Maintenance Status**: Select all the options that apply to the maintenance status of the equipment.
8. **Responsible ST**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the maintenance and upkeep of the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Name**: The name of the equipment, such as a microscope or a centrifuge.
* **Equipment Model**: The model number or manufacturer's name of the equipment, if available.
* **Equipment Type**: The type of equipment, such as a PCR machine or a chromatography system.
* **Location**: The room number, floor, or section where the equipment is located.
* **Condition**: A brief description of the condition of the equipment, e.g., "In good working order" or "Needs maintenance".
* **Maintenance Status**: The maintenance status of the equipment, which can be a combination of the following:
	+ Option 1: "Needs maintenance"
	+ Option 2: "Under maintenance"
	+ Option 3: "Requires replacement"
* **Responsible ST**: The name of the person or team responsible for the maintenance and upkeep of the equipment.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct equipment information to avoid duplicate entries.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure that the inventory is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the laboratory manager or the maintenance team for guidance.
