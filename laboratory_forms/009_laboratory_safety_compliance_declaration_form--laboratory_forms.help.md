# Laboratory Safety Compliance Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laboratory Safety Compliance Declaration Form is used to collect information about the laboratory's safety compliance, including declaration of safety rules, emergency contact, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your declaration of safety compliance in the "laboratory-safety-compliance-declaration-form-label" field.
2. Provide your contact email in the "contact-person-label" field.
3. Enter your emergency contact information in the "emergency-contact-label" field.
4. Select your declaration from the options provided in the "laboratory-safety-compliance-declaration-form-label" field.
5. Choose relevant options from the "laboratory-safety-compliance-declaration-form-2-label" field.
6. Enter any additional comments or information in the "laboratory-safety-compliance-declaration-form-6-label" field.
7. Enter the number of laboratory safety compliance declaration forms you have completed in the "laboratory-safety-compliance-declaration-form-4-label" field.
8. Enter the time you spent on laboratory safety compliance declaration in the "laboratory-safety-compliance-declaration-form-6-label" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **lab: Laboratory Safety Compliance Declaration Form (1)**:
  - A date input field where you need to declare the date of the last safety compliance declaration.
* **Contact Person (2)**:
  - An email input field where you can enter your contact email.
* **Emergency Contact (3)**:
  - A text field where you can enter your emergency contact information.
* **Lab: Laboratory Safety Compliance Declaration Form (4)**:
  - A select one field where you need to choose your declaration from the provided options.
* **Lab: Laboratory Safety Compliance Declaration Form 2 (5)**:
  - A required number input field where you need to enter the count of completed laboratory safety compliance declaration forms.
* **Lab: Laboratory Safety Compliance Declaration Form 3 (6)**:
  - A text field where you can enter any additional comments or information.
* **Lab: Laboratory Safety Compliance Declaration Form 4 (7)**:
  - A number input field where you can enter your completion time.
* **Lab: Laboratory Safety Compliance Declaration Form 5 (8)**:
  - A number input field where you can enter your completion number.
* **Lab: Laboratory Safety Compliance Declaration Form 6 (9)**:
  - A time input field where you can enter the time spent on laboratory safety compliance declaration.
