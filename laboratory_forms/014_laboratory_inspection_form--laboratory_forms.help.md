# Laboratory Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laboratory Inspection Form is a tool for facility managers and inspectors to collect and document information about laboratory facilities, equipment, and safety procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability and provide as much detail as possible.
2. Complete the form in a sequential manner, starting from the "Facility Information" page and working through each page until you have finished.
3. Be specific and clear in your responses, and avoid leaving blank fields unless explicitly allowed by the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Information (Page 1)** (`facility_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the laboratory facility, including its name, location, and a short summary of its purpose.
* **Inspection Date (Page 2)** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection, including the month, day, and year.
* **Inspector Information (Page 3)** (`inspector_info`, text, optional): Provide information about the inspector's name, title, and contact details.
* **Inspection Time (Page 4)** (`inspection_time`, time, optional): Record the start and end time of the inspection.
* **Facility Status (Page 5)** (`facility_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the facility from the options: Open, Closed, or Pending.
* **Facility Address (Page 6)** (`facility_address`, text, optional): Enter the physical address of the laboratory facility.
* **Facility Manager (Page 7)** (`facility_manager`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of the facility manager.
* **Facility Notes (Page 8)** (`facility_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the facility.
* **Inspection Notes (Page 9)** (`inspection_notes`, text, optional): Record any notes or observations made during the inspection.
* **Facility Risk (Page 10)** (`facility_risk`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the level of risk associated with the facility from the options: Low, Moderate, or High.
* **Facility Equipment (Page 11)** (`facility_equip`, text, optional): Describe the type and quantity of equipment used in the facility.
* **Facility Certifications (Page 12)** (`facility_certifications`, text, optional): List any relevant certifications or licenses held by the facility.
* **Facility Safety Standards (Page 13)** (`facility_safety_standards`, text, optional): Describe the safety standards followed by the facility.
* **Facility Emergency Plan (Page 14)** (`facility_emergency_plan`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact information and procedures for the facility.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" should be completed only if required by the facility or inspector.
