# NIPT Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NIPT Referral Form is designed to collect information from patients or caregivers about their medical history, contact details, and relevant test results from previous laboratory tests. This form helps healthcare professionals gather necessary data for further diagnosis and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "Patient Information" page to provide basic information about the patient, such as demographics and medical history.
2. Move on to the "Contact Information" page to enter the patient's contact details, including phone numbers and addresses.
3. On the "Patient Tests" page, choose relevant options from the dropdown menu to select previously conducted tests for the patient.
4. In the "Laboratory Details" section, select the type of laboratory test required for further diagnosis.
5. Finally, enter any additional test details in the "Test Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional): Enter the patient's basic information, including name, age, and medical history.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Provide the patient's contact details, such as phone number, email, or address.
* **Patient Tests** (`patient_tests`, select_one, optional): Select from the dropdown menu the relevant tests that have been conducted on the patient. This will help healthcare professionals understand the patient's medical background.
* **Laboratory Details** (`laboratory_details`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of laboratory test required for further diagnosis. This will help healthcare professionals understand the necessary test to be conducted.
* **Test Details** (`test_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the test to be conducted, such as the test date or results.
