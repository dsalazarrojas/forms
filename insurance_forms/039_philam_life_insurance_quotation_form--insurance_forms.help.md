# philam-life-insurance-quotation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The philam-life-insurance-quotation-form is designed to gather information for life insurance quotations. This form will collect personal details and insurance-related information from you to provide you with accurate insurance quotes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form correctly, follow these steps:
- Please provide your **name** in the field marked 'name'.
- Enter your **address** in the field marked 'address'.
- Provide your **phone number** in the field marked 'phone'.
- Enter your **email address** in the field marked 'email'.
- Enter your **date of birth** in the format 'mm/dd/yyyy' in the field marked 'date-of-birth'.
- Select the insurance product that suits your needs from the options in the field marked 'insurance-product'.
- Choose the coverage type that best suits your requirements from the options in the field marked 'coverage-type'.
- Enter the coverage amount you wish to have in the field marked 'coverage-amount'.
- Select the start date of your insurance coverage in the format 'mm/dd/yyyy' in the field marked 'start-date'.
- If applicable, choose the end date of your insurance coverage in the format 'mm/dd/yyyy' in the field marked 'end-date'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
  - **Address** (text): Enter your address as it appears on your identification. 
  - **Phone** (text, not required): Please enter your phone number for further verification purposes.
  - **Email** (text, not required): Enter your email address for further communication.
  - **Date of Birth** (date, not required): Enter your date of birth in the format 'mm/dd/yyyy'.
  - **Insurance Product** (select_one, not required): Select the insurance product that you are interested in.
  - **Coverage Type** (select_multiple, not required): Select the coverage type that best suits your needs.
  - **Coverage Amount** (number, not required): Enter the coverage amount you wish to have.
  - **Start Date** (date, not required): Select the start date of your insurance coverage in the format 'mm/dd/yyyy'.
  - **End Date** (date, not required): Select the end date of your insurance coverage in the format 'mm/dd/yyyy'.

## Tips
- Ensure that you enter accurate and correct information as it will be used to provide you with a suitable insurance quote.
- Please ensure that you have all required documents and information before filling out this form.
- This form is for informational purposes only and is not a binding contract.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for further assistance.
