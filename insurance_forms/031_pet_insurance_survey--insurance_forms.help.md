# Pet Insurance Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form, Pet Insurance Survey, is designed to collect information from pet owners about their pets and their insurance needs. By filling out this form, you will be providing valuable insights to help us better understand your pet-related requirements and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each field with the requested information about your pet.
2. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or contact us directly.
3. Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form, especially with regards to your pet's age and policy status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Pet Insurance Survey**:
    This is the title of the form, it's just a label.
- **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of pet you own, such as cat, dog, bird, etc.
- **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required: false): Enter your pet's name.
- **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of insurance you are interested in, such as health, liability, life, etc.
- **Age** (`age`, number, required: false): Enter your pet's age.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Policy Length** (`policy_length`, number, required: false): Enter the length of the policy you're interested in.
- **Coverage** (`coverage`, text, required: false): Describe the type of coverage you're looking for.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the payment method you prefer, such as credit card, bank transfer, etc.
- **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select how often you want to make payments, such as monthly, annually, etc.
- **Policy Cost** (`policy_cost`, number, required: false): Enter the policy cost you're expecting.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them here.
- **Policy Provider** (`policy_provider`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your current policy provider.
- **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your current policy, such as active, inactive, cancelled, etc.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by pet owners, not policyholders. If you're not sure which field is applicable to you, please contact us directly for assistance.
