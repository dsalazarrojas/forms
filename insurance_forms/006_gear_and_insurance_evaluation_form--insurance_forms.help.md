# Gear And Insurance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for evaluating or managing insurance policies related to equipment or gear.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting your user name or identity from the "User Name" field.
2. Choose the type of equipment from the "Equipment Type" field.
3. Indicate the condition of the equipment you are evaluating by selecting one or more options from the "Equipment Condition" field.
4. If applicable, provide the name of the insurance provider in the "Insurance Provider" field.
5. If relevant, enter the coverage start date in the "Coverage Start Date" field, and the coverage end date in the "Coverage End Date" field.
6. If you are providing policy information, enter the premium amount in the "Premium" field, and the policy number in the "Policy Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Name
• **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your user name or identity for reference and tracking purposes.

### Equipment Type
• **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the type of equipment you are evaluating (e.g., machine, vehicle, etc.).

### Equipment Condition
• **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Choose one or more conditions that apply to the equipment (e.g., new, used, damaged, etc.).

### Insurance Provider
• **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, required: false): If you are providing insurance-related information, enter the name of the insurance provider (e.g., company name).

### Coverage Start Date
• **Coverage Start Date** (`coverage_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the coverage period if applicable.

### Coverage End Date
• **Coverage End Date** (`coverage_end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the coverage period if applicable.

### Premium
• **Premium** (`premium`, `number`, required: false): Enter the premium amount if you are providing policy information.

### Policy Number
• **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the policy number if you are providing policy information.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have selected all relevant options for "Equipment Condition" to accurately represent the equipment's condition.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant personnel or refer to the relevant policy documentation.
* Please double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
