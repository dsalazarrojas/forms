# Insurance Verification Staffing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request insurance verification, which is an essential step in verifying the identity and eligibility of an individual or group for insurance coverage.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have permission to access the form and that no similar request has been made recently or is currently pending.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Insurance Verification" field, depending on whether you need insurance verification.
3. If you answered "Yes", fill in the "Insurance Verification Provider" field with the name and contact information of the insurance provider.
4. Fill out the "Date of Birth" field with the relevant date.
5. Optionally, provide your contact phone number and email address for further communication.
6. If necessary, enter the fax number and address for additional information or contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Request** (`staff_request`, text, required): Fill in your official name and title or department.
* **Insurance Verification** (`insurance_verification`, select multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you require insurance verification, or "No" if not necessary.
* **Insurance Verification Needed** (`insurance_verification_needed`, date, optional): Enter the date by which the insurance verification is required.
* **Insurance Verification Provider** (`insurance_verification_provider`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the insurance provider.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Fill in the relevant date of birth.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number for further communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address for further communication.
* **Fax Number** (`fax_number`, text, optional): Enter the fax number for additional information or contact.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address for further information or contact.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and complete information to support the insurance verification process.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling the form, contact your manager or the insurance department for assistance.
* Ensure that you have necessary permissions and approvals before completing the form.
* Please check that no similar request has been made recently or is currently pending before submitting the form.
