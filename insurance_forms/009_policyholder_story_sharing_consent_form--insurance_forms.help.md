# Policyholder Story Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Policyholder Story Sharing Consent Form is a tool used by insurance companies to collect information and consent from policyholders regarding the sharing of their stories. This form will help you understand their preferences and boundaries, ensuring you can maintain a strong relationship with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the relevant options for each question, considering your preferences and boundaries.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policyholder Story Sharing Consent Form**: This is the title of the form, which is here to inform you what the form is about.
* **Collect policyholder testimonials**: This question asks if you are willing to share your story with the insurance company. Select 'Yes' if you are comfortable sharing your story.
* **Follow-up**: This question inquires about your preference for follow-up communication. If you'd like to be contacted, select 'Yes'.
* **Approved story sharing**: This question confirms if you have given consent to share your story. Select 'Yes' to confirm.
* **Manage data collection**: This question allows you to specify if and how you want your contact information to be managed. You can type in your preferred method of contact management.
* **Follow-up frequency**: This is a numeric field where you can specify how often you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Follow-up method**: This question asks how you'd like to be contacted for follow-up. Select your preferred method (e.g., phone, email, mail).
* **Policyholder contact information**: This question is asking for your contact information so the insurance company can follow up with you.
* **Follow-up schedule**: This is a date and time field where you can specify when you'd like to be contacted.
* **Approved story sharing**: This question reconfirms your consent to share your story. Select 'Yes' to confirm.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the insurance company.
* Remember to review and save your completed form for your records.
