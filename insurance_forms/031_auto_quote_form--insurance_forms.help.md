# Auto Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Quote Form is designed to collect information from primary applicants for auto insurance quotes. This form is used to determine eligibility and provide accurate quotes based on the provided information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the primary applicant section, which includes your full name, date of birth, occupation, and zip code. 
2. Next, fill out the vehicle information section, which includes the vehicle year, make, model, and VIN (if available). 
3. Select your desired coverage level and deductible amount for your insurance quote. 
4. Indicate whether you are currently insured, and provide any additional comments or special requirements. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (full_name): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth): Provide your date of birth for eligibility and age-based rating.
* **Occupation** (occupation): State your current profession.
* **Zip Code** (zip_code): Enter your zip code to determine regional insurance rates.
* **Vehicle Year** (year): Enter the year of manufacture for your vehicle.
* **Make** (make): Enter the make of your vehicle, e.g. Honda.
* **Model** (model): Enter the model of your vehicle, e.g. CR-V.
* **VIN** (vin): If available, provide the 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number.
* **Desired Coverage Level** (desired_coverage): Select the amount of protection you need:
	+ **Basic Liability**: Select for limited coverage.
	+ **Mid-level Coverage**: Select for moderate coverage.
	+ **Full Protection**: Select for comprehensive protection.
* **Deductible Amount** (deductible_amount): Choose your out-of-pocket cost per claim:
	+ **250**: Select for lowest possible cost.
	+ **500**: Select for moderate cost.
	+ **1000**: Select for highest possible cost.
* **Are you currently insured?** (currently_insured): Select yes if you have an active policy.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Provide any special requirements or history.
