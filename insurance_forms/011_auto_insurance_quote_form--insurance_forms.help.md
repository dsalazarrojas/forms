# Auto Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Insurance Quote Form is designed to collect information necessary to provide personalized insurance quotes. This form is divided into sections that cover personal information, vehicle details, and insurance preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form structure and identify the required fields.
2. Fill out each section with accurate and complete information.
3. Select the correct options for each field according to your profile and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Get Your Auto Insurance Quote
* **Introduction (Optional)**: This section serves as an introduction and guide to the form.
### Personal Information
* **First Name (Required)**: Your legal first name.
* **Last Name (Required)**: Your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth (Required)**: Your date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
* **Gender (Required)**: Select your gender.
* **Email Address (Required)**: The email address where you'd like to receive your quote.
* **Phone Number (Required)**: Your best contact number.
### Vehicle Information
* **Vehicle Make (Required)**: The manufacturer of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model (Required)**: The model name of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year (Required)**: The year of manufacture of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model (Required)**: (same as above)
* **Mileage (Required)**: The odometer reading of your vehicle.
* **Primary Vehicle Use (Required)**: How you use your vehicle.
* **Estimated Annual Mileage (Required)**: How many miles you drive per year.
* **Driving History (Optional)**: A note section for you to provide any relevant driving history.
### Accidents and Violations
* **Accidents in Past 3 Years (Required)**: The number of at-fault accidents in the past 3 years.
* **Violations in Past 3 Years (Required)**: The number of traffic tickets in the past 3 years.
* **DUI Conviction History (Required)**: Whether you have any DUI convictions in the past 5 years.
### Insurance Preferences
* **Coverage Preferences (Required)**: The level of coverage you desire.
* **Current Coverage Limits (Optional)**: Your current insurance coverage limits if known.
* **Preferred Deductible (Required)**: The amount of deductible you prefer.
* **Additional Drivers (Required)**: Whether you have other drivers who will drive your vehicle.
* **Number of Vehicles (Required)**: The number of vehicles you'd like to insure.
* **Current Insurance Company (Optional)**: The company insuring you currently.
* **Policy Expiry (Optional)**: The expiration date of your current policy.
* **Discounts You May Qualify For (Optional)**: Select all discounts you may be eligible for.
* **How Soon Do You Need a Quote (Required)**: When you'd like to receive your quote.
