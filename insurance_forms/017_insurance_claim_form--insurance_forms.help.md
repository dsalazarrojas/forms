# insurance_claim_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "insurance_claim_form" is a form used to document and submit insurance claims. This form is designed to gather relevant information from the policy holder to process and evaluate their claim. Completing this form accurately and fully will help expedite the claim process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to understand each section.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Ensure you provide all required information, marked as "Required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Page (Page 1)
- **First Page**: This is the starting page of the form. No specific information is required here.

### Policy Holder's Name (Page 2)
- **Policy Holder's Name**: Please enter the name of the policy holder as it appears on their insurance policy.
  * Required: No, but recommended for proper claim processing

### Policy Holder's Phone Number (Page 2)
- **Policy Holder's Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the policy holder. This will help us contact them if needed during the claim process.
  * Required: No, but recommended for efficient communication

## Tips
- Make sure to complete the form carefully and accurately to avoid delays in processing your claim.
- If you are unsure about any information, contact our support team for assistance.
- Attach any supporting documentation or proof as necessary to substantiate your claim.
