# international_medical_expense_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for international medical expense claims. It gathers essential details for processing and resolving such claims.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and fill out all required fields.
2. Ensure all information is accurate and complete.
3. Attach supporting documents when prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your claim.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, optional): Provide medical details related to your claim.
* **Medical Expenses** (`medical_expenses`, text, optional): List your medical expenses, including costs and descriptions.
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, text, optional): Describe your claim in detail.
* **Claimant Information** (`claimant_info`, text, optional): Enter your personal information, including name, address, and contact details.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, text, optional): Provide your insurance information, including policy numbers and provider details.
* **Billing Information** (`billing_info`, text, optional): Enter billing details, including invoices and payment methods.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Describe how you plan to pay for your expenses.
* **Claimant Contact** (`claimant_contact`, text, optional): Provide contact information for the claimant.
* **Medical Institution Information** (`medical_institution_info`, text, optional): Enter details about the medical institution that provided treatment.
* **Claim Status History** (`claim_status_history`, text, optional): Describe any previous claim status updates.
* **Medical Certificate Information** (`medical_certificate_info`, text, optional): Attach or describe medical certificates or certificates related to your claim.
* **Claim Review** (`claim_review`, text, optional): Describe any relevant medical reviews or assessments.
* **Claim Rejection Reasons** (`claim_rejection_reasons`, text, optional): Provide reasons for claim rejections or denials.
* **Claim Closure** (`claim_closure`, text, optional): Describe the resolution or outcome of your claim.
* **User Agreement** (`user_agreement`, text, optional): Acknowledge and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign this document to confirm your identity and agreement.
* **Claimant Signature** (`claimant_signature`, text, optional): Sign this document to confirm your claimant identity and agreement.
* **Medical Certificate File** (`medical_certificate_file`, text, optional): Attach medical certificates or related documents.
* **Claimant Certificate File** (`claimant_certificate_file`, text, optional): Attach claimant certificates or related documents.
* **Claimant Proof of Residence** (`claimant_proof_of_residence`, text, optional): Provide proof of your residence.
* **Claimant ID** (`claimant_id`, text, optional): Enter your claimant ID number.

## Tips
