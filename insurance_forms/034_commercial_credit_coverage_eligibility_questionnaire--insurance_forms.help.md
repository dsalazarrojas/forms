# Commercial Credit Coverage Eligibility Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commercial Credit Coverage Eligibility Questionnaire is a form designed to help businesses assess their creditworthiness and coverage needs. It is intended for business owners or operators who wish to determine their eligibility for credit coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Business Details section with your company's information.
2. Answer the Credit History questions about your business's credit score and past experiences with credit coverage.
3. Determine your Coverage Needs, including the desired coverage amount and purpose.
4. Assess your Financial Stability, including your business's profitability, cash flow, and debt-to-income ratio.
5. Identify any Risk Factors that may affect your business's creditworthiness.
6. Review the Eligibility Assessment section to determine your business's eligibility for credit coverage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (1): Enter your company's legal name.
* **Contact Name** (2): Enter the name of your primary contact person.
* **Contact Title** (3): Enter your contact person's title in the company.
* **Email Address** (4): Enter your company's email address.
* **Phone Number** (5): Enter your company's contact phone number.
* **Business Type** (7): Select your company's industry or sector.
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Retail
	+ Wholesale
	+ Services
	+ Construction
	+ Technology
	+ Healthcare
	+ Finance
	+ Other
* **Years in Operation** (8): Select how long your business has operated.
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 3-5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ 10+ years
* **Number of Employees** (9): Enter the total number of employees.
* **Annual Revenue** (10): Enter your company's gross annual revenue.
* **Business Credit Score Range** (12): Select your business's estimated credit score.
	+ Excellent (750+)
	+ Good (700-749)
	+ Fair (650-699)
	+ Poor (600-649)
	+ Very Poor (below 600)
	+ Unknown
* **Previous Credit Coverage** (13): Select whether you currently have credit coverage or have had it in the past.
	+ Yes Currently Have
	+ Yes Had Previously
	+ No Never Had
* **Outstanding Debts** (14): Enter your company's total outstanding business debts.
* **Bankruptcy History** (15): Select whether your business has filed for bankruptcy.
	+ None
	+ Chapter 7
	+ Chapter 11
	+ Chapter 13
	+ Other
* **Coverage Amount Needed** (17): Enter the desired coverage amount.
* **Coverage Purpose** (18): Select the reason for needing coverage.
	+ Accounts Receivable Protection
	+ Trade Credit Insurance
	+ Export Credit Insurance
	+ Loan Protection
	+ Contract Security
	+ Other
* **Primary Customer Type** (19): Select the type of customers you primarily serve.
	+ Consumers (B2C)
	+ Businesses (B2B)
	+ Government
	+ International
	+ Mixed
* **Accounts Receivable Percentage** (20): Select the percentage of accounts receivable as a percentage of assets.
	+ 0-10%
	+ 11-25%
	+ 26-50%
	+ 51-75%
	+ Over 75%
* **Profitability Status** (22): Select your business's profitability status.
	+ Profitable
	+ Break Even
	+ Operating at Loss
	+ Newly Established
* **Cash Flow Status** (23): Select your company's current cash flow situation.
	+ Strong Positive
	+ Moderate Positive
	+ Neutral
	+ Negative
	+ Variable
* **Debt to Income Ratio** (24): Select your business's debt-to-income ratio.
	+ Less than 20%
	+ 20-40%
	+ 40-60%
	+ 60-80%
	+ Over 80%
* **Industry Risk Level** (26): Select the risk level of your industry.
	+ Low Risk
	+ Moderate Risk
	+ Average Risk
	+ Elevated Risk
	+ High Risk
* **Customer Concentration** (27): Select the percentage of revenue from your top customer.
	+ Less than 10%
	+ 10-25%
	+ 26-50%
	+ 51-75%
	+ Over 75%
* **International Sales** (28): Select the percentage of international revenue.
	+ None
	+ 1-10%
	+ 11-25%
	+ 26-50%
	+ Over 50%
* **Existing Insurance** (30): Select whether you have current credit coverage.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Claims History** (31): Select the number of past credit claims filed.
	+ None
	+ 1-2 Claims
	+ 3-5 Claims
	+ More than 5
* **Additional Comments** (32): Enter any other information that may be relevant to your application.
