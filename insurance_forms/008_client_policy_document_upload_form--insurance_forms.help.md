# Client Policy Document Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to upload and process client policy documents for various insurance purposes. It ensures that your documents are correctly uploaded and reviewed for accurate and efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Email Address, Phone Number, and Mailing Address.
2. Provide the Policy Number, which can be found on your policy declaration page.
3. Choose the Policy Type (e.g., Auto Insurance, Homeowners Insurance, etc.).
4. Select the Insurance Company Name from the list provided.
5. Upload the Policy Declaration Page and Full Policy Document.
6. If you have any active claims, provide the Claim Details if necessary.
7. Choose your preferred contact method and best time to contact you.
8. Specify the purpose of uploading the documents (e.g., New Policy Application, Policy Renewal, etc.).
9. If you have an Insurance Agent, provide their name and contact information.
10. Confirm that you consent to processing of your documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Insurance Policy Document Upload** (`form_header`): This is the main header for the form, where you can upload your insurance policy documents.
  * Upload your policy declaration page and full policy document.
- **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address that you want to be contacted on.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Mailing Address** (`address`): Enter your current mailing address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
- **Policy Number** (`policy_number`): Enter the policy number found on your policy declaration page.
- **Policy Type** (`policy_type`): Choose the type of insurance policy you have (e.g., Auto Insurance, Homeowners Insurance, etc.).
- **Policy Effective Date** (`policy_effective_date`): Enter the date when your coverage starts. Leave this field blank if it's not applicable.
- **Policy Expiration Date** (`policy_expiration_date`): Enter the date when your coverage ends. Leave this field blank if it's not applicable.
- **Coverage Amount** (`coverage_amount`): Choose the coverage amount of your policy (e.g., Under $50,000, $50,000-$100,000, etc.).
- **Upload Policy Declaration Page** (`upload_policy_declaration`): Upload the main page showing coverage details.
- **Upload Full Policy Document** (`upload_policy_full_document`): Upload the complete policy terms and conditions.
- **Upload Government-Issued ID** (`upload_id_document`): Upload your driver's license, passport, or state ID.
- **Upload Proof of Address** (`upload_proof_of_address`): Upload a utility bill, bank statement, or lease agreement to prove your address.
- **Upload Additional Documents** (`additional_documents`): Upload any additional documents relevant to your policy.
- **Purpose of Document Upload** (`purpose_of_upload`): Specify why you are submitting these documents (e.g., New Policy Application, Policy Renewal, etc.).
- **Insurance Agent Name** (`agent_name`): If you have an insurance agent, enter their name here.
- **Insurance Agent Contact** (`agent_contact`): If you have an insurance agent, provide their contact information (email or phone).
- **Do You Have Any Active Claims** (`current_claim_status`): If you have active claims, select 'True', and provide claim details if necessary.
- **Claim Details** (`claim_details`): If you have active claims, provide brief details about the claim.
- **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`): Choose how you want to be contacted (email, phone call, text message, etc.).
- **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`): Choose the best time to contact you (morning, afternoon, evening, etc.).
- **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`): Let us know how you heard about us (referral, online search, social media, etc.).
- **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`): If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them here.
- **I Consent to Processing of My Documents** (`consent_to_process`): Confirm that you consent to the processing of your documents by selecting 'Yes I consent'.
