# Directors And Officers Insurance Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store information about Directors and Officers insurance policies, including general information, director and officer details, policy coverage, payment terms, and policy status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select each page from the form to start entering information.
   - Page 1: General Information
   - Page 2: Directors Information
   - Page 3: Officers Information
   - Page 4: Directors Experience
   - Page 5: Officers Experience
   - Page 6: Directors Education
   - Page 7: Officers Education
   - Page 8: Coverage Details
   - Page 9: Coverage Options
   - Page 10: Coverage Start Date
   - Page 11: Coverage End Date
   - Page 12: Policy Limit
   - Page 13: Deductible
   - Page 14: Renewal Date
   - Page 15: Premium
   - Page 16: Payment Terms
   - Page 17: Payment Schedule
   - Page 18: Coverage Address
   - Page 19: Coverage Location
   - Page 20: Policy Status
   - Page 21: Policy Status Note
   - Page 22: Coverage Notes
   - Page 23: Coverage Address Note
   - Page 24: Policy Notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter any additional general information about the policy.
- **Directors Information** (`2`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter information about the directors of the company, including names, positions, and dates of birth.
- **Officers Information** (`3`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter information about the officers of the company, including names, positions, and dates of birth.
- **Directors Experience** (`4`, `text`, required: false):
  Describe the experience of the directors, including their work history and relevant experience in the field.
- **Officers Experience** (`5`, `text`, required: false):
  Describe the experience of the officers, including their work history and relevant experience in the field.
- **Directors Education** (`6`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter the education details of the directors, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
- **Officers Education** (`7`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter the education details of the officers, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
- **Coverage Details** (`8`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter the details about the coverage, including policy number and policy type.
- **Coverage Options** (`9`, `select_one`, required: false):
  Select the type of coverage, such as Directors and Officers Liability Insurance or Management Liability Insurance.
- **Coverage Start Date** (`10`, `date`, required: false):
  Enter the start date of the coverage.
- **Coverage End Date** (`11`, `date`, required: false):
  Enter the end date of the coverage.
- **Policy Limit** (`12`, `number`, required: false):
  Enter the policy limit.
- **Deductible** (`13`, `number`, required: false):
  Enter the deductible amount.
- **Renewal Date** (`14`, `date`, required: false):
  Enter the renewal date of the policy.
- **Premium** (`15`, `number`, required: false):
  Enter the premium amount.
- **Payment Terms** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
  Select the payment terms, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Payment Schedule** (`17`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
  Select the payment schedule, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Coverage Address** (`18`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter the address where the coverage is effective.
- **Coverage Location** (`19`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter the location of the coverage.
- **Policy Status** (`20`, `select_one`, required: false):
  Select the policy status, such as Active, Inactive, or Canceled.
- **Policy Status Note** (`21`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter a note about the policy status.
- **Coverage Notes** (`22`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter any additional notes about the coverage.
- **Coverage Address Note** (`23`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter any additional address notes.
- **Policy Notes** (`24`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter any additional policy notes.
