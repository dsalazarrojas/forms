# Home Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a home or residential property, including its owner's details, insurance coverage, and any related assets or policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and contact details in the "Home Owner Information" field.
* Provide your home's serial number and any other relevant details in the "Home Owner Serial Number" field.
* Select the insurance agency that covers your home in the "Insurance Coverage" field (if applicable).
* Choose the warranty status of your home's assets in the "Warranty Status" field (if applicable).
* Enter the unit price of the asset in the "Unit Price" field (if applicable).
* Specify the room location of the asset in the "Room Location" field (if applicable).
* Provide a description of the asset in the "Item Description" field (if applicable).
* Enter the purchase date of the asset in the "Purchase Date" field (if applicable).
* Enter the item serial number of the asset in the "Item Serial Number" field (if applicable).
* Specify the insurance deductible amount in the "Insurance Deductible" field (if applicable).
* Enter the owner's email and phone number in the "Home Owner Email" and "Home Owner Phone" fields (if applicable).
* Provide the insurance provider's name in the "Insurance Provider" field (if applicable).
* Enter the policy number in the "Policy Number" field (if applicable).
* Enter the policy start and end dates in the "Policy Start Date" and "Policy End Date" fields (if applicable).
* Specify the policy status in the "Policy Status" field (if applicable).
* Enter the policy renewal date in the "Policy Renewal Date" field (if applicable).
* Specify the policy renewal status in the "Policy Renewal Status" field (if applicable).
* Enter the policy coverage in the "Policy Coverage" field (if applicable).
* Enter the policy limit in the "Policy Limit" field (if applicable).
* Enter the policy claim amount in the "Policy Claim Amount" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Owner Information** (home_owner_information, text, required): Enter your name and contact details in this field.
* **Home Owner Serial Number** (home_owner_serial_num, text, required): Enter the serial number of your home or property.
* **Insurance Coverage** (insurance_coverage, select_multiple, required): Select the insurance agency that covers your home or property (if applicable).
* **Warranty Date** (warranty_date, date, required): Enter the warranty date of your home's assets (if applicable).
* **Warranty Status** (warranty_status, select_one, required): Choose the warranty status of your home's assets (if applicable).
* **Unit Price** (unit_price, number, required): Enter the unit price of the asset (if applicable).
* **Room Location** (room_location, text, required): Specify the room location of the asset (if applicable).
* **Item Type** (item_type, text, required): Provide a description of the asset (if applicable).
* **Item Description** (item_description, text, required): Enter a description of the asset (if applicable).
* **Purchase Date** (purchase_date, date, required): Enter the purchase date of the asset (if applicable).
* **Item Serial Number** (item_serial_num, text, required): Enter the item serial number of the asset (if applicable).
* **Insurance Deductible** (insurance_deductible, text, required): Enter the insurance deductible amount (if applicable).
* **Home Owner Email** (home_owner_email, email, required): Enter the owner's email address (if applicable).
* **Home Owner Phone** (home_owner_phone, text, required): Enter the owner's phone number (if applicable).
* **Insurance Provider** (insurance_provider, text, required): Enter the insurance provider's name (if applicable).
* **Policy Number** (policy_number, text, required): Enter the policy number (if applicable).
* **Policy Start Date** (policy_start_date, date, required): Enter the policy start date (if applicable).
* **Policy End Date** (policy_end_date, date, required): Enter the policy end date (if applicable).
* **Policy Status** (policy_status, select_one, required): Choose the policy status (if applicable).
* **Policy Renewal Date** (policy_renewal_date, date, required): Enter the policy renewal date (if applicable).
* **Policy Renewal Status** (policy_renewal_status, select_one, required): Choose the policy renewal status (if applicable).
* **Policy Coverage** (policy_coverage, text, required): Enter the policy coverage (if applicable).
* **Policy Limit** (policy_limit, number, required): Enter the policy limit (if applicable).
* **Policy Claim Amount** (policy_claim_amount, number, required): Enter the policy claim amount (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please consult the relevant documentation or contact the responsible party for clarification.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid any errors or discrepancies.
