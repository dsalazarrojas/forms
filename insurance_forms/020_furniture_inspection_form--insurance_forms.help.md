# Furniture Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about a piece of furniture during an inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter the condition of the furniture in the "condition" field.
3. Describe any damage or issues with the furniture in the "damage" field.
4. Add any comments or notes about the furniture in the "comments" field.
5. Select the status of the furniture from the options provided in the "status" field.
6. Enter the contact details of the person who inspected the furniture in the "contact details" field.
7. If the furniture was assigned to someone, select "Yes" in the "assigned" field and enter the assigned tool or person in the "assigned tool" field.
8. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **furniture**: A unique identifier for the piece of furniture being inspected.
	+ Type: Number
	+ Required: No
* **condition**: A description of the current condition of the furniture.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **damage**: A description of any damage or issues with the furniture.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **comments**: Additional notes or comments about the furniture.
	+ Type: Note
	+ Required: No
* **date inspected**: The date of the inspection.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: Yes
* **inspected by**: A confirmation that the inspection was conducted.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
* **status**: A status update on the furniture.
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Required: No
* **status label**: A description of the status of the furniture.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **contact details**: Contact information for the person who inspected the furniture.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **contact name**: The name of the person who inspected the furniture.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **contact email**: The email address of the person who inspected the furniture.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: No
* **assigned**: Confirmation of assignment to someone or something.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: No
* **assigned tool**: The tool or person assigned to the furniture.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **created by**: The person who created the form.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **updated by**: The person who last updated the form.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **created at**: The date and time the form was created.
	+ Type: Time
	+ Required: No
* **updated at**: The date and time the form was last updated.
	+ Type: Time
