# Insurance Card Image Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Card Image Upload Form is used to upload images of insurance cards for categorization and further processing. This form is a crucial tool for insurance company representatives, agents, or customers to provide accurate and efficient service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the insurance card from the "Category" field.
2. Upload the image of the insurance card using the "Upload Insurance Card Image" field.
3. Upload any other relevant documents using the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (Insurance Company/Health Insurance/Auto Insurance)**: Select the type of insurance card you are uploading. This will help us categorize the image and process it accordingly.
* **Upload Insurance Card Image**: Upload a clear and high-quality image of your insurance card. Make sure the image is well-lit, in focus, and easily readable.
* **Upload Other Documents (ID Card, Other Documents, License, Medical Card, Vehicle Registration, Proof Of Insurance)**: Upload any additional documents related to the insurance card, such as ID cards, licenses, medical cards, vehicle registrations, or proof of insurance.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the uploaded images and documents are clear and legible.
* Avoid uploading low-quality images or documents that are difficult to read.
* If you need help or have any questions, feel free to contact our support team.
