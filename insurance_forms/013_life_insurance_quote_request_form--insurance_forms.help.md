# life_insurance_quote_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The life insurance quote request form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking life insurance coverage. This form helps our team understand the customer's needs and provides a quote for the requested coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Age.
2. Select the type of insurance product you are interested in (e.g., life or health insurance).
3. Enter your contact number and email address.
4. Provide your address and zip code.
5. Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., phone or email).
6. If you are working with an agent, enter their name and number.
7. Fill in your Date of Birth, Time of Birth, and any other required fields.
8. Add a note if you have any specific request or question.
9. Confirm if you would like to request a quote.
10. Enter the date and time for the quote request.
11. Finally, enter the start and end dates for the coverage and policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, optional): This is your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, optional): This is your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Age** (3, number, optional): This is your age. Please enter your current age.
* **Insurance Product** (4, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of insurance product you are interested in. Select 'Yes' for life insurance or 'No' for health insurance.
* **Coverage Type** (5, select_one, optional): Choose the type of coverage you need. Select 'Life' or 'Health' depending on your needs.
* **Contact Number** (6, text, optional): This is your contact number. Please enter a valid phone number or extension.
* **Email** (7, email, optional): This is your email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Address** (8, text, optional): This is your mailing address. Please enter a valid address.
* **Zip Code** (9, text, optional): This is your zip code. Please enter a valid zip code.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (10, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted. Select 'Yes' for phone or 'No' for email.
* **Agent Name** (11, text, optional): If you are working with an agent, enter their name.
* **Agent Number** (12, text, optional): If you are working with an agent, enter their contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (13, date, optional): This is your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (14, time, optional): This is your time of birth.
* **Request Note** (15, note, optional): Add any additional information or questions you have about your quote request.
* **Quote Request** (16, select_one, optional): Confirm if you would like to request a quote.
* **Quote Request Date** (17, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to request the quote.
* **Quote Request Time** (18, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to request the quote.
* **Coverage Start Date** (19, date, optional): Enter the start date for the coverage.
* **Coverage End Date** (20, date, optional): Enter the end date for the coverage.
* **Coverage Start Time** (21, time, optional): Enter the start time for the coverage.
* **Coverage End Time** (22, time, optional): Enter the end time for the coverage.
* **Policy Start Date** (23, date, optional): Enter the start date for the policy.
* **Policy End Date** (24, date, optional): Enter the end date for the policy.
* **Policy Start Time** (25, time, optional): Enter the start time for the policy.
