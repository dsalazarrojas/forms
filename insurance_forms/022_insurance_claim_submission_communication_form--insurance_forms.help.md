# Insurance Claim Submission Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit insurance claims and communicate with policyholders regarding their claims. It captures essential information about the claim, including policyholder details, claim type, claim date, and submission method, among other relevant fields.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Policyholder Details**: Enter the policyholder's name, address, and any other relevant contact information in this field.
2. **Claim Type**: Select the type of claim that the policyholder is submitting (e.g., Auto, Property, Home, Business).
3. **Claim Date**: Enter the date the claim was submitted.
4. **Claim Time**: Enter the time the claim was submitted.
5. **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of the insurance provider.
6. **Policy Number**: Enter the policy number associated with the claim.
7. **Claim Amount**: Enter the total amount of the claim.
8. **Submission Method**: Select how the claim was submitted (e.g., Mail, Phone, Email, In Person).
9. **Contact Details**: Enter any additional contact information for the policyholder.
10. **Address**: Enter the policyholder's address.
11. **Email**: Enter the policyholder's email address.
12. **Phone**: Enter the policyholder's phone number.
13. **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the claim.
14. **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to review or manage the claim.
15. **Submit Claim**: Select the status of the claim (Submit, Decline, Pending).
16. **Review Claim**: Select the status of the claim (Approve, Reject, Hold).
17. **Submit Review**: Select the status of the review (Approve, Reject, Hold).
18. **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the claim.
19. **Submitted On**: Enter the date the claim was submitted.
20. **Updated On**: Enter the date the claim was last updated.
21. **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the claim.
22. **Updated By**: Enter the name of the person who last updated the claim.
23. **Submitted At**: Enter the time the claim was submitted.
24. **Updated At**: Enter the time the claim was last updated.
25. **Name**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policyholder Details** (`policyholder_details`, text, optional): Enter the policyholder's name, address, and any other relevant contact information in this field.
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of claim that the policyholder is submitting (e.g., Auto, Property, Home, Business).
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the claim was submitted.
* **Claim Time** (`claim_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the claim was submitted.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the insurance provider.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, number, optional): Enter the policy number associated with the claim.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of the claim.
* **Submission Method** (`submission_method`, select_one, optional): Select how the claim was submitted (e.g., Mail, Phone, Email, In Person).
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the policyholder.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the policyholder's address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the policyholder's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the policyholder's phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the claim.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Select the tool assigned to review or manage the claim.
* **Submit Claim** (`submit_claim`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the claim (Submit, Decline, Pending).
* **Review Claim** (`review_claim`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the claim (Approve, Reject, Hold).
* **Submit Review** (`submit_review`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the review (Approve, Reject, Hold).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the claim.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): Enter the date the claim was submitted.
* **Updated On** (`updated_on`, date, optional): Enter the date the claim was last updated.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the claim.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who last updated the claim.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, time, optional): Enter the time the claim was submitted.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, time, optional): Enter the time the claim was last updated.
* **Name** (`submitted_by_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the claim.

Note: The fields with a type of "date", "time", and "select_one" are not required but can be filled if necessary.
