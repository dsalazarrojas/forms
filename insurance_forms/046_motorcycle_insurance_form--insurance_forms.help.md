# motorcycle_insurance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information about motorcycles for insurance purposes. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure efficient processing of your insurance claims or requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Year of Manufacture field with the year the motorcycle was produced.
2. Enter the Make of the motorcycle in the Make field.
3. Specify the Model of the motorcycle in the Model field.
4. Identify the Owner(s) of the motorcycle in the Owner field.
5. Enter the License number of the motorcycle in the License number field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Year of Manufacture**: A field to enter the year the motorcycle was produced.
* **Make**: A field to enter the manufacturer's name of the motorcycle (e.g. Yamaha, Harley-Davidson, etc.).
* **Model**: A field to enter the specific model of the motorcycle (e.g. Yamaha FZ-1, Harley-Davidson Fat Boy, etc.).
* **Owner**: A field to identify the owner(s) of the motorcycle (e.g. your name, company name, etc.).
* **License Number**: A field to enter the license plate number of the motorcycle.
