# Internal Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internal Investigation Form is used to collect information for internal investigations within the organization. This form is intended for employees who will be conducting and submitting investigations, as well as their supervisors or reviewers who will review and approve the results.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Enter your information as the submitter:
   - Investigator's name: `investigator_name`
   - Email: `investigator_email`
   - Start date of the investigation: `start_date`
   - End date of the investigation: `end_date`
   - Job level of the submitter (Director, Manager, Supervisor, or Other): `submitter_job_level`
3. Select the status of the case (Open, In Progress, or Closed): `case_status`
4. Record the date the case was closed: `case_status_date`
5. Enter your comments and agree/disagree with the case status: `submitter_comments`, `submitter_agree`, `submitter_disagree`
6. Review and approve the form, providing any necessary comments: `reviewer_comments`

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Investigation Details**: A note section for capturing the details of the investigation.
* **Investigator Name**: The name of the employee conducting the investigation, as well as their role in the organization.
* **Investigator Email**: The email address of the employee conducting the investigation.
* **Start Date**: The date the investigation began.
* **End Date**: The date the investigation ended.
* **Case Type**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if this case is related to a particular type.
* **Case Type List**: A text field for entering a list of case types.
* **Case Status**: Select the status of the case (Open, In Progress, or Closed).
* **Case Status Date**: The date the case was closed or the status change happened.
* **Case Status Comments**: Additional comments regarding the case status.
* **Submitter Information**: Information about the person submitting the form.
*   - **Name**: The name of the employee submitting the form.
*   - **Email**: The email address of the employee submitting the form.
*   - **Phone**: The phone number of the employee submitting the form.
*   - **Department**: The department of the employee submitting the form.
*   - **Job Title**: The job title of the employee submitting the form.
*   - **Job Level**: The job level of the employee submitting the form (Director, Manager, Supervisor, or Other).
*   - **Location**: The location of the employee submitting the form.
*   - **Agree**: Agree or disagree with the case status.
*   - **Disagree**: If disagreeing with the case status, provide comments here.
*   - **Undecided**: If undecided about the case status, provide comments here.
*   - **Submitter Comments**: Additional comments from the submitter.
*   - **Reviewer Comments**: Comments from the reviewer after reviewing the form.
