# Renewal Questionnaire Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Renewal Questionnaire Form is designed to collect business renewal information from customers or users. This form is intended to gather essential data for renewal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the `Renewal Questionnaire Form` section, which is the first part of the form.
2. Next, provide business details in the `Business Details` section.
3. Finally, submit the `Contact Information` section to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Renewal Questionnaire Form** (`renewal_questionnaire_form`, text, optional):
    This section is the starting point for the form. It is the first part of the form that users will see.
* **Business Details** (`business_details`, text, optional):
    In this section, users will provide information about their business, such as its nature or type.
    This information is essential for the renewal process.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional):
    Here, users will enter their contact details, including any necessary contact methods or addresses.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all sections completely and accurately to avoid any delays or issues with the renewal process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or support team.
