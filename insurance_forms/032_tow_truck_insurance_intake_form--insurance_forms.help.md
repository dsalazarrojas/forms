# Tow Truck Insurance Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tow Truck Insurance Intake Form is a crucial document that helps to collect information from tow truck owners to facilitate the insurance process. It ensures that the insurance provider receives accurate information about any incidents that may affect their coverage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Fill in your name and contact information in the "Owner Name", "Owner Address", "Owner Phone", and "Owner Email" fields. 
3. Provide details about your vehicle, such as "Vehicle Year", "Vehicle Make", "Vehicle Model", and "Vehicle Color" in the respective fields. 
4. Enter the year you purchased the vehicle in the "Vehicle Year Purchased" field.
5. Enter your vehicle's license plate number in the "Vehicle License" field.
6. Select the towing date in the "Towing Date" field.
7. Enter the location where the incident occurred in the "Towing Location" field.
8. Enter the time of the incident in the "Towing Time" field.
9. Provide a brief description of the damage in the "Damage Description" field.
10. If a witness was present, provide their statement in the "Witness Statement" field.
11. If a police report was filed, provide it in the "Police Report" field.
12. Enter the towing service fee in the "Towing Service Fee" field.
13. Enter the total claim amount in the "Total Claim Amount" field.
14. Select the payment method used for the claim in the "Payment Method" field.
15. Enter the insurance provider's contact information in the "Insurance Provider Contact Info", "Insurance Provider Phone", and "Insurance Provider Email" fields.
16. Provide any additional notes in the "Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Name (1)**: This is the field where you enter your name as the owner of the towed vehicle.
* **Insurance Provider (2)**: This is the field where you enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Vehicle Year (3)**: This is the field where you enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make (4)**: This is the field where you enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model (5)**: This is the field where you enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color (6)**: This is the field where you enter the color of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year Purchased (7)**: This is the field where you enter the year you purchased the vehicle.
* **Vehicle License (8)**: This is the field where you enter your vehicle's license plate number.
* **Owner Address (9)**: This is the field where you enter your address.
* **Owner Phone (10)**: This is the field where you enter your phone number.
* **Owner Email (11)**: This is the field where you enter your email address.
* **Towing Date (12)**: This is the field where you select the date the incident occurred.
* **Towing Location (13)**: This is the field where you enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Towing Time (14)**: This is the field where you select the time of the incident.
* **Damage Description (15)**: This is the field where you provide a description of the damage.
* **Witness Statement (16)**: This is the field where you provide a statement from any witness present during the incident.
* **Police Report (17)**: This is the field where you provide a copy of the police report filed.
* **Towing Service Fee (18)**: This is the field where you enter the fee charged by the towing service.
* **Total Claim Amount (19)**: This is the field where you enter the total amount claimed.
* **Payment Method (20)**: This is the field where you select the method of payment used.
* **Insurance Provider Contact Info (21)**: This is the field where you enter the contact information for your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Provider Phone (22)**: This is the field where you enter the phone number of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Provider Email (23)**: This is the field where you enter the email of your insurance provider.
* **Notes (24)**: This is the field where you provide any additional information or notes.
