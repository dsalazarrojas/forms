# Auto Insurance Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients to provide personalized auto insurance quotes and coverage recommendations. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide a complete and accurate picture of your insurance needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your client's information, including their full name, contact email, and phone number.
2. Move on to provide details about the vehicle, such as make and model, year, and Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
3. Specify the desired coverages and deductibles you want for your vehicle.
4. If applicable, indicate if you are an existing policyholder and provide the name of your previous carrier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: This field is required to ensure that we have accurate contact information for you.
* **Email**: Please enter your best contact email address so we can keep you informed about your insurance options and coverage.
* **Phone**: Enter your best contact number so we can reach you with any questions or concerns.
* **Address**: Enter your address in the format of Street, City, State, Zip.
* **Make and Model**: Enter the make and model of your vehicle, e.g. Subaru Outback.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of your vehicle, e.g. 2019.
* **VIN**: If you have a 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN), please enter it here.
* **Garage Location**: Please select where your vehicle is parked overnight, as this affects your insurance rates and coverage options.
	+ **Attached Garage**: Garage is attached to your home.
	+ **Detached Garage**: Garage is not attached to your home.
	+ **Carport**: A separate garage for your vehicle.
	+ **Street**: The vehicle is parked on the street.
	+ **Driveway**: The vehicle is parked in a driveway.
* **Desired Coverages**: Select the types of protection you want for your vehicle, such as Liability, Collision, Comprehensive, Uninsured Motorist, and Medical Payments.
* **Preferred Deductible**: Choose your preferred deductible amount for each coverage type.
* **Are you an existing policyholder?**: Indicate if you already have coverage with another carrier.
* **Previous Carrier Name**: If you have coverage elsewhere, please enter the name of your previous carrier.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to ensure we can provide you with the best possible insurance options.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
* This form is designed to guide you through the process, but if you need assistance or clarification, don't hesitate to reach out.
