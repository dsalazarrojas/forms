# Insurance Opt Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Opt Out Form is used to document a student's or patient's decision to opt out of their insurance coverage. This form is typically used when a student or patient wants to decline or cancel their insurance coverage for any reason. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the relevant parties are informed of the decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. For fields that are not required, you can leave them blank if they do not apply to your situation.
3. Make sure to sign the form to confirm your decision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student or patient who is opting out of their insurance coverage.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter the student ID number for the student or patient who is opting out.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, select_one, required): Select the name of the insurance provider the student or patient is opting out of.
* **Reason for Opt-out** (`reason_for_opt_out`, text, optional): Explain the reason for opting out of insurance coverage.
* **Date of Opt out** (`date_of_opt_out`, date, optional): Enter the date when the student or patient plans to opt out of their insurance coverage.
* **Time of Opt out** (`time_of_opt_out`, time, optional): Enter the time when the student or patient plans to opt out of their insurance coverage.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign the form to confirm your decision.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee who is completing the form.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter the employee ID number for the employee who is completing the form.
* **Insurance Provider 2** (`insurance_provider_2`, select_one, optional): Select the name of any other insurance provider the student or patient may have.
* **Reason for Opt-out 2** (`reason_for_opt_out_2`, text, optional): Explain the reason for opting out of another insurance provider.
* **Date of Opt out 2** (`date_of_opt_out_2`, date, optional): Enter the date when the student or patient plans to opt out of another insurance provider.
* **Time of Opt out 2** (`time_of_opt_out_2`, time, optional): Enter the time when the student or patient plans to opt out of another insurance provider.
* **Signature 2** (`signature_2`, note, optional): Sign the form to confirm your decision.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient who is opting out.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, optional): Enter the patient ID number for the patient who is opting out.
* **Insurance Provider 3** (`insurance_provider_3`, select_one, optional): Select the name of any other insurance provider the patient may have.
* **Reason for Opt-out 3** (`reason_for_opt_out_3`, text, optional): Explain the reason for opting out of another insurance provider.
* **Date of Opt out 3** (`date_of_opt_out_3`, date, optional): Enter the date when the patient plans to opt out of another insurance provider.
* **Time of Opt out 3** (`time_of_opt_out_3`, time, optional): Enter the time when the patient plans to opt out of another insurance provider.
* **Signature 3** (`signature_3`, note, optional): Sign the form to confirm your decision.
* **Other Insurance Provider** (`other_insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of any other insurance provider the student or patient may have.
* **Insurance ID** (`insurance_id`, text, optional): Enter the insurance ID number for the student or patient's insurance coverage.
* **Signature 4** (`signature_4`, note, optional): Sign the form to confirm your decision.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): Sign the form with your employee name and ID to confirm that you are completing the form on behalf of the student or patient.
