# sdc_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **sdc_application_form** is designed to collect information about applicant data. It is intended for use in various contexts where applicant details are required. This form aims to capture the essentials of an applicant's profile, including personal and optional information. Before proceeding, verify that this form is relevant to your specific use case and not a duplicate of another form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **applicant_name**, which is a text field for the applicant's name.
2. Enter your **email**, ensuring it is in the correct format for email validation.
3. Select the **application_date**, which is the date you are submitting your application for.
4. Choose the **application_type** from the dropdown menu, which represents the type of application.
5. Enter your **phone** number for contact purposes, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **applicant_name** (`applicant_name`, text, required: false): The field to input the applicant's name.
- **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address you want to be contacted on.
- **application_date** (`application_date`, date, required: false): Select the date you are submitting your application.
- **application_type** (`application_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of application you are submitting.
- **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide a phone number for contact purposes, if needed.
