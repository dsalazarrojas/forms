# public_adjuster_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and manage contracts between customers and adjusters. It gathers essential information for the insurance industry, including customer details, contract terms, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide the adjuster's name and phone number in the relevant fields.
3. Select the type of contract you wish to create (Adjuster Contract, Public Adjuster Contract, or Other).
4. If applicable, choose the contract duration (30 Days, 60 Days, or No Limit).
5. Enter the contract price (if applicable).
6. Confirm whether the contract will have a sign-off by selecting 'True' or 'False' (if not applicable, select 'Undecided').
7. Add any notes or comments in the notes field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Adjuster Name** (`adjuster_name`, text, optional): Enter the adjuster's name.
* **Adjuster License Number** (`adjuster_license_number`, text, optional): Enter the adjuster's license number (if applicable).
* **Adjuster Phone** (`adjuster_phone`, text, optional): Enter the adjuster's phone number.
* **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of contract you wish to create (Adjuster Contract, Public Adjuster Contract, or Other).
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, date, optional): Enter the contract date (if applicable).
* **Contract Duration** (`contract_duration`, select_multiple, optional): Select the contract duration (30 Days, 60 Days, or No Limit).
* **Contract Price** (`contract_price`, number, optional): Enter the contract price (if applicable).
* **Contract Sign-off** (`contract_signoff`, select_one, required): Confirm whether the contract will have a sign-off by selecting 'True' or 'False' (if not applicable, select 'Undecided').
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any notes or comments about the contract.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your selections and entries for correctness and consistency.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form's purpose and use your best judgment.
* This form is a crucial document in the insurance industry, and its accuracy is essential for the contracting process.
