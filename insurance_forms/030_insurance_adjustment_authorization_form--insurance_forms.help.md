# Insurance Adjustment Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Insurance Adjustment Authorization Form" is designed to collect information from users to process adjustments related to insurance claims or payments. This form ensures accuracy and completeness in the adjustment process, and its contents are reviewed and validated by a representative of the insurance company.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user input in the first field, which is a text box for you to describe your needs or requests.
2. Move on to the second field, "Insurance Policy", and enter the relevant policy details as prompted.
3. In the "Authorization Details" field, provide clear details about the adjustment you are authorizing, such as the type of change and date of adjustment.
4. Enter the adjustment amount in the "Adjustment Amount" field. Please use a numerical format, as it is a required field.
5. Finally, provide any additional "Payment Terms" in the last field, which is also a text box for you to describe your payment arrangement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your description of the claim or payment adjustment you wish to make.
* **Insurance Policy** (`insurance_policy`, text, optional): Enter details about the insurance policy related to the adjustment, such as policy number or coverage type.
* **Authorization Details** (`authorization_details`, text, optional): Enter clear information about the adjustment, including the type of change and date of adjustment.
* **Adjustment Amount** (`adjustment_amount`, number, required): Enter the specific amount of the adjustment, using numerical format.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Enter any additional payment arrangements or terms you wish to specify.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately to avoid delays or complications in the adjustment process.
* Double-check the information entered for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your insurance representative for clarification or guidance.
