# cyber_insurance_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an organization's cybersecurity insurance coverage to provide a comprehensive picture of their risk management and insurance needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. Fill out the form in a step-by-step manner, one page at a time.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Insurance Company**
* Insurance Company (*text*, required/optional): Enter the name of the insurance company providing coverage.
*   Insurance Company (*text*, optional) Enter a brief description of the insurance company, if desired.

### **Risk Level**
* Risk Level (*select_one*, required): Select the level of risk for the organization: Low, Medium, or High.

### **Annual Revenue**
* Annual Revenue (*number*, optional): Enter the annual revenue of the organization.

### **Employees**
* Employees (*text*, optional): Enter the number of employees in the organization, if desired.

### **Assets**
* Assets (*number*, optional): Enter the value of the organization's assets.

### **Insurance Policy**
* Insurance Policy (*select_multiple*, required): Select the type(s) of insurance policy: Liability, Property, or Business.

### **Business Hours**
* Business Hours (*time*, optional): Enter the business hours of operation, if desired.

### **Security Controls**
* Security Controls (*select_multiple*, required): Select the level of security controls implemented: Implemented, Partial, or Not Implemented.

### **Cyber Insurance Coverage**
* Cyber Insurance Coverage (*select_one*, required): Select the level of coverage: Comprehensive, Limited, or Basic.

### **Coverage Type**
* Coverage Type (*select_multiple*, required): Select the type(s) of coverage: Cyber Liability, Data and System, or Media Liability.

### **Coverage Amount**
* Coverage Amount (*number*, optional): Enter the coverage amount, if desired.

### **Premium**
* Premium (*number*, optional): Enter the premium amount, if desired.

### **Payment Frequency**
* Payment Frequency (*select_one*, optional): Select the payment frequency: Annual, Semi-Annual, or Quarterly.

### **Payment Method**
* Payment Method (*select_multiple*, optional): Select the payment method: Online, Offline, or Bank Transfer.

### **Payment Date**
* Payment Date (*date*, optional): Enter the payment date.

### **Payment Time**
* Payment Time (*time*, optional): Enter the payment time, if desired.

### **Payment Amount**
* Payment Amount (*number*, optional): Enter the payment amount, if desired.

### **Payment Status**
* Payment Status (*select_one*, optional): Select the payment status: Paid, Pending, or Unpaid.

### **Policy Holder**
* Policy Holder (*select_multiple*, optional): Select the policy holder type: Individual or Company.

### **Policy Type**
* Policy Type (*select_one*, optional): Select the policy type: Comprehensive, Limited, or Basic.

### **Policy Start Date**
* Policy Start Date (*date*, optional): Enter the policy start date, if desired.

### **Policy End Date**
* Policy End Date (*date*, optional): Enter the policy end date, if desired.

### **Policy Status**
* Policy Status (*select_one*, optional): Select the policy status: Active, Inactive, or Canceled.

### **Policy Description**
* Policy Description (*note*, optional): Enter a brief description of the policy, if desired.
