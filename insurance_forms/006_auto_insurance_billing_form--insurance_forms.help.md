# Auto Insurance Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide billing and payment information for your auto insurance policy. It's designed to gather essential details for efficient and accurate billing processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting.
3. Review and verify your details before final submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Policyholder Information**: Please provide your policy details as required.
  - **First Name**: Enter your legal first name.
  - **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name.
  - **Policy Number**: Enter your insurance policy number.
  - **Email Address**: Enter your primary email for billing communications.
  - **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number.
  - **Billing Address**: Enter your street address for billing.
  - **City**: Enter your city of residence.
  - **State**: Choose your state of residence.
  - **ZIP Code**: Enter your 5-digit ZIP code.
- **Billing Information**: This field is for details about your billing.
  - (No further explanation provided, as it's a note field)
- **Billing Period**: Choose the billing period for this payment.
  - **Current Month**: For payments due in the current month.
  - **Previous Month**: For payments due in the previous month.
  - **Quarterly**: For payments due quarterly.
  - **Semi-Annual**: For payments due semi-annually.
  - **Annual**: For payments due annually.
  - **Past Due**: For payments already past due.
- **Amount Due**: Enter the total amount due.
- **Invoice Number**: Enter the reference number from the bill.
  - (Note: This field is optional.)
- **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method.
  - **Credit Card**: For credit card payments.
  - **Debit Card**: For debit card payments.
  - **Bank Transfer**: For bank transfers.
  - **Check**: For check payments.
  - **Money Order**: For money order payments.
  - **Online Payment**: For online payments.
- **Card Type**: If paying by credit or debit card, choose the type.
  - **Visa**: For Visa card payments.
  - **MasterCard**: For MasterCard payments.
  - **American Express**: For American Express card payments.
  - **Discover**: For Discover card payments.
  - **Not Applicable**: If not paying by card.
- **Card Number**: Enter the credit or debit card number (XXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX).
  - (This field is required only when paying by card.)
- **Expiration Date**: If paying by card, enter the MM-YYYY.
  - (This field is required only when paying by card.)
- **CVV**: Enter the 3-digit security code.
  - (This field is required only when paying by card.)
- **Preferred Billing Cycle**: Choose your preferred billing cycle.
  - **Monthly**: For monthly billing.
  - **Quarterly**: For quarterly billing.
  - **Semi-Annual**: For semi-annual billing.
  - **Annual**: For annual billing.
- **Enroll in AutoPay**: Choose to enroll in automatic payments.
  - **Yes enroll me**: To enroll in auto-pay.
  - **No one-time payment**: To make a one-time payment.
  - **Already enrolled**: If already enrolled.
- **Preferred Payment Date**: Choose your preferred payment date.
  - **1st of month**: For payment on the first day of the month.
  - **15th of month**: For payment on the 15th day of the month.
  - **Last day of month**: For payment on the last day of the month.
  - **Same as policy date**: For payment on the same date as your policy.
  - **Other**: For any other preferred date.
- **Additional Notes**: Enter any billing questions or comments.
  - (This field is optional.)
- **Payment Authorization**: Confirm your payment authorization.
  - **I Agree**: To authorize this payment.
  - **I Do Not Agree**: To decline payment authorization.
