# Insurance Referral Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Referral Verification Form is used to verify the details of a patient's insurance referral. This form is meant for active referrals only.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Date of Birth.
4. Enter your Social Security Number.
5. Enter your Address.
6. Enter your Phone number.
7. Enter your Email.
8. Select whether the insurance provider is Yes or No.
9. Enter the Insurance Referral Date.
10. Select the Insurance Referral Status: Active or Inactive.
11. Enter any comments regarding the referral.
12. Enter the Insurance Referral ID.
13. Enter the person Assigned To.
14. Enter the Insurance Company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First Name, Text, Optional):** Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name, Text, Optional):** Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth, Date, Optional):** Enter your date of birth in the required format.
* **Social Security Number (Social Security Number, Text, Optional):** Enter your social security number.
* **Address (Address, Text, Optional):** Enter your address.
* **Phone (Phone, Text, Optional):** Enter your phone number.
* **Email (Email, Email, Optional):** Enter your email address.
* **Insurance Provider (Insurance Provider, Select One, Optional):** Select Yes if the insurance provider is active, No otherwise.
* **Insurance Referral Date (Insurance Referral Date, Date, Optional):** Enter the date of the insurance referral.
* **Insurance Referral Status (Insurance Referral Status, Select One, Optional):** Select Active if the referral is active, Inactive if it is not.
* **Comments (Comments, Text, Optional):** Enter any comments regarding the referral.
* **Insurance Referral ID (Insurance Referral ID, Text, Optional):** Enter the insurance referral ID.
* **Assigned To (Assigned To, Text, Optional):** Enter the person assigned to the referral.
* **Insurance Company (Insurance Company, Text, Optional):** Enter the insurance company name.
