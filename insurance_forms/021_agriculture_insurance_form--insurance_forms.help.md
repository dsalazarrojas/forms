# Agriculture Insurance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from farmers, ranchers, or business owners who need agricultural insurance. It will be used by insurance professionals to determine the best coverage options for you based on your operations and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following sections:
1.  Provide your full legal name or business name in the "Applicant Name" field.
2.  Enter your farm's physical address in the "Farm Address" field.
3.  Enter your phone number and email address in the "Phone Number" and "Email Address" fields, respectively.
4.  Select the type(s) of coverage you need from the options provided in the "Policy Type Requested" field.
5.  Enter the total value you want to insure in the "Desired Coverage Amount" field.
6.  Choose the deductible amount you are comfortable with in the "Deductible Preference" field.
7.  Indicate whether you have filed a claim in the past 5 years in the "Past Claims History" field.
8.  Describe your farming activities in the "Description of Operations" field.
9.  Enter the total land area you own or lease in the "Total Acreage" field. Leave this field blank if not applicable.
10.  If applicable, enter the total value of your livestock in the "Total Livestock Value" field.
11.  If applicable, enter the total value of your equipment in the "Total Equipment Value" field.
12.  Describe any risk mitigation measures you take in the "Risk Mitigation Measures" field. This is optional.
13.  Finally, select the desired start date for your coverage in the "Desired Effective Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (text, required): Enter your full legal name or business name.
* **Farm Address** (text, required): Enter the physical location of your insured property.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Policy Type Requested** (select_multiple, required): Select the type(s) of coverage you need.
* **Desired Coverage Amount** (number, required): Enter the total value you want to insure.
* **Deductible Preference** (select_one, required): Choose the deductible amount you are comfortable with.
* **Past Claims History** (select_one, required): Indicate whether you have filed a claim in the past 5 years.
* **Description of Operations** (text, required): Brieify describe your farming activities.
* **Total Acreage** (number, required): Enter the total land area you own or lease. Leave this field blank if not applicable.
* **Total Livestock Value** (number, required): If applicable, enter the total value of your livestock. Leave this field blank if not applicable.
* **Total Equipment Value** (number, required): If applicable, enter the total value of your equipment. Leave this field blank if not applicable.
* **Risk Mitigation Measures** (text, optional): Describe any risk mitigation measures you take, such as fencing, irrigation, etc.
* **Desired Effective Date** (date, required): Select the desired start date for your coverage.

Note: Please make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and thoroughly. If you are unsure about any of the information, please ask for help.
