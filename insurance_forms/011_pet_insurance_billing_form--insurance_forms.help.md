# Pet Insurance Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment information for pet insurance plans. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure timely processing of your payment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, select the payment method for your pet insurance plan.
2.  Enter the payment amount due.
3.  Choose a due date for the payment.
4.  Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
5.  Enter the pet's name, breed, and insurance provider name.
6.  Select the payment status and enter any additional comments or details.
7.  Enter the billing address, city, state, and zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **payment_method** (Select One): Choose how you want to pay for your pet insurance plan. Options are Pet Insurance Payment Method and Other.
    *   Select the payment method that applies to your situation.
*   **payment_amount** (Number): Enter the amount due for your pet insurance plan.
    *   Enter the exact amount due, including any applicable discounts or adjustments.
*   **due_date** (Date): Select the due date for your payment.
    *   Choose the date when the payment is expected to be made.
*   **payment_method_details** (Text): Enter any additional details about the payment method selected.
    *   Provide any extra information about your payment method, if applicable.
*   **pet_owner_name** (Text): Enter your name.
    *   Enter your full name.
*   **owner_contact_info** (Email): Enter your email address.
    *   Enter your valid email address for communication.
*   **pet_insurance_plan_id** (Number): Enter your pet insurance plan ID.
    *   Enter the ID associated with your pet insurance plan.
*   **payment_status** (Select Multiple): Select the payment status for your pet insurance plan.
    *   Choose between Paid, Unpaid, and Pending.
*   **comments** (Text): Enter any additional comments.
    *   Provide any comments or notes about your payment.
*   **billing_address** (Text): Enter your billing address.
    *   Enter your address where you want to receive billing information.
*   **billing_city** (Text): Enter your city.
    *   Enter your city for the billing address.
*   **billing_state** (Text): Enter your state.
    *   Enter your state for the billing address.
*   **billing_zip** (Number): Enter your zip code.
    *   Enter your zip code for the billing address.
*   **owner_name** (Text): Enter your name.
    *   Enter your full name.
*   **owner_email** (Email): Enter your email address.
    *   Enter your valid email address for communication.
*   **owner_phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
    *   Enter your contact phone number.
*   **pet_name** (Text): Enter your pet's name.
    *   Enter your pet's name.
*   **pet_breed** (Text): Enter your pet's breed.
    *   Enter your pet's breed.
*   **vet_name** (Text): Enter your vet's name.
    *   Enter your vet's name.
*   **insurance_provider_name** (Text): Enter the insurance provider's name.
    *   Enter the insurance provider's name.
*   **payment_terms** (Text): Enter payment terms.
    *   Enter any special payment terms or conditions.
*   **payment_method_other** (Text): If you choose "Other" as your payment method, enter an explanation.
    *   Provide an explanation for your chosen payment method.
*   **payment_amount_other** (Text): If you enter an "Other" payment method, enter the amount due.
    *   Enter the amount due for your chosen payment method.
*   **payment_terms_other** (Text): If you enter an "Other" payment method, enter the payment terms.
    *   Enter any special payment terms or conditions for your chosen payment method.
*   **payment_status_other** (Text): If you enter an "Other" payment method, choose a payment status.
    *   Choose a payment status between Paid, Unpaid, and Pending for your chosen payment method.
