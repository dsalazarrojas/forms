# Insurance Agency Resignation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by employees of an Insurance Agency to notify their agency of their intention to leave. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the form fields with accurate and complete information about your resignation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, Optional): Your Employee ID if known.
* **Reason for Resignation** (`reason_for_resignation`, text, Optional): Please specify a brief reason for your resignation.
* **Date of Resignation** (`date_of_resignation`, date, Required): Enter the date you expect your last day of work to be.
* **Last Day of Work** (`last_day_of_work`, date, Required): Enter the date you expect your last day of work to be.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, Optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, Optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, note, Optional): Add any additional notes for your manager.
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of your agency.
* **Agency Address** (`agency_address`, text, Optional): Enter the address of your agency.
* **Agency Phone** (`agency_phone`, text, Optional): Enter the phone number of your agency.
* **Agency Email** (`agency_email`, email, Optional): Enter the email address of your agency.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, Required): Sign the form with your signature.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, Required): Sign the form with your manager's signature.
* **Agency Signature** (`agency_signature`, text, Required): Sign the form with your agency's signature.
* **Date of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, date, Optional): Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Employee Note** (`employee_note`, note, Optional): Add any additional notes for the employee.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, note, Optional): Add any additional notes for the manager.
* **Agency Note** (`agency_note`, note, Optional): Add any additional notes for the agency.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, Optional): Select the current status of the form submission, either Active or Inactive.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, note, Optional): Enter any additional submission notes.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, text, Optional): Enter the name of who submitted the form.
* **Submitted on** (`submitted_on`, date, Optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.

Note: This is a template guide, actual fields, labels and rules may change based on your agency needs and policies. Always review and verify the form accuracy with your HR and agency management team.
