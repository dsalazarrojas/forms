# sports_release_of_liability_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information and obtain consent from individuals participating in sports or activities to ensure their safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Ensure that you have read and understood the form before signing it.
3. Sign the form with your signature.
4. Have a doctor sign the form after reviewing your medical history and conditions.
