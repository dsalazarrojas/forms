# Renewal And Check Up Worksheet  - Business Coverage - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for business coverage renewal and check-up. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information about your business to ensure seamless insurance coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your business's first name, last name, and date of birth.
2. Enter your business's email address and phone number.
3. Provide your business's name, address, and type (e.g., Retail, Manufacturing, etc.).
4. Enter your business's annual revenue and select the correct business type for each additional location or entity.
5. Enter your business's current insurance coverage and coverage limit.
6. Provide the renewal date for your business's insurance coverage.
7. Enter any additional contact information for your business, including email, phone, company name, address, business type, annual revenue, and coverage limit.
8. Add any additional notes or comments as needed.
