# Insurance Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Complaint Form is a tool for policyholders to report issues related to their insurance policies. Fill out this form to inform your insurance provider about the nature of your complaint and receive assistance.

## How To Complete This Form

- To start, click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
- Fill in the relevant "Policyholder Information" options that apply to you.
- Select the "Complaint Type" that best describes your issue.
- Enter the "Complaint Date" if the issue occurred on a specific date.
- Provide a detailed description of your complaint in the "Complaint Description" field.
- Enter your "Policy Number" if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Details (Complaint Date)**: This field is where you'll enter the date on which the issue occurred. This is optional, but providing the date can help us better understand the timeline of your complaint.
* **Policyholder Information**: Select the correct policyholder options that apply to you. This helps us identify your relationship with the policy.
* **Complaint Type**: Choose the type of issue you're experiencing - Coverage Issue, Claim Issue, or Service Issue - to help us understand the nature of your complaint.
* **Policy Number**: If you have a policy number, please enter it. This helps us verify your policy and address your issue more efficiently.
* **Complaint Description**: Be as detailed as possible about the issue you're experiencing. This description will help us assess and resolve your complaint effectively.
