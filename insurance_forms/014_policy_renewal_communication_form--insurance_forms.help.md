# policy_renewal_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The policy renewal communication form is a tool used to collect policyholder information and update policy status and details. It is designed for efficient communication and record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the policyholder's information in the **Policyholder Info** field.
2. Provide the policyholder's contact details in the **Contact Details** field.
3. Add any additional policy details if necessary in the **Policy Details** field.
4. Select the policyholder's preferred communication preferences in the **Communication Preferences** field.
5. Enter the policyholder's contact information in the **Contact Info** field.
6. Note any relevant policyholder comments or notes in the **Policyholder Notes** field.
7. Select the current policy status in the **Policy Status** field.
8. Choose the type of policy (Term Life or Whole Life) in the **Policy Type** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policyholder Info**: Enter the policyholder's name or account number (if available).
* **Contact Details**: Provide the policyholder's email, phone number, or any other contact method.
* **Policy Details**: Add any relevant policy specifics, such as policy number or policy type.
* **Communication Preferences**: Choose how you want to receive communication from us, e.g., email, phone call, or mail.
* **Contact Info**: Enter the policyholder's contact information, such as email or phone number.
* **Policyholder Notes**: Note any relevant policyholder comments or notes.
* **Policy Status**: Select the current status of the policy (e.g., active, inactive, cancelled).
* **Policy Type**: Choose between Term Life or Whole Life policy types.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure efficient communication.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer back to your policy documents or contact your insurance provider.
* This form is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for official policy documents or communication.
