# Health Insurance Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Declaration Form is a crucial document that provides details about an individual's health insurance coverage. It's essential to fill this form accurately to avoid any discrepancies or errors in your insurance status.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your patient information on page 1.
2. Select your insurance provider from the options on page 2.
3. Enter your effective and expiration dates, as well as coverage start and end dates on page 3.
4. Provide your insurance coverage details on page 4.
5. Enter your employer and policy number information on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Please enter your full name, date of birth, and any other relevant information about yourself. (Page 1)
* **Insurance Provider**: Select the insurance provider that covers you. (Page 2)
* **Effective Date**: Enter the date your insurance coverage became effective. (Page 3)
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the date your insurance coverage will expire. (Page 3)
* **Coverage Start Date**: Enter the date your insurance coverage started. (Page 4)
* **Coverage End Date**: Enter the date your insurance coverage will end. (Page 4)
* **Insurance Coverage**: Provide a brief description of your insurance coverage. (Page 4)
* **Group Number**: Enter the group number assigned to you by your insurance provider. (Page 5)
* **Coverage Start Date**: Enter the date your insurance coverage started. (Page 5)
* **Employer**: Enter your employer's name and contact information. (Page 5)
* **Policy Number**: Enter the policy number assigned to you by your insurance provider. (Page 5)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information for any errors or discrepancies.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your insurance provider for clarification.
* This form is not intended for use by anyone other than the individual themselves. Only authorized personnel with the right access and permissions should use this form.
