# Personal Injury Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to personal injury claims from clients. It is intended to gather details about the client's contact information, insurance coverage, the circumstances of the injury, and any relevant medical or legal details.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill in your client's name in the field "Client Name".
2.  Enter your client's contact number in the field "Contact Number" (optional).
3.  Provide your client's email address in the field "Email Address" (optional).
4.  Select the type of insurance your client has in the field "Insurance Type" (Automobile, Workers Compensation, or Workers Comp).
5.  Enter the date of the injury in the field "Injury Date" (YYYY-MM-DD format).
6.  Select the time of the injury in the field "Injury Time" (HH:MM format).
7.  Provide the location where the injury occurred in the field "Location of Injury".
8.  Enter a brief statement about what happened in the field "Witness Statement".
9.  Write your personal statement about the incident in the field "Client Statement".
10. If you have any additional information, please enter it in the field "Additional Info" (optional).
11.  You can upload documents related to the claim in the field "Upload Documents".
12. You can upload pictures related to the claim in the field "Upload Pictures".
13. You can upload videos related to the claim in the field "Upload Videos".
14. Provide your medical information in the field "Medical Info".
15. List the name of any medical experts involved in the field "Medical Experts".
16. Enter the name of your law firm in the field "Law Firm".
17. Enter the name of your attorney in the field "Law Firm Attorney" (optional).
18. Enter the name of the attorney handling the case in the field "Attorney".
19. Enter the case number in the field "Case Number".
20. Enter the current status of the case in the field "Case Status".
21. Enter any notes about the case in the field "Case Notes".
22. Enter the name of your client's insurance company in the field "Insurance Company".
23. Enter any details about the insurance claim in the field "Insurance Claim".
24. Please ensure that all the required fields (marked with a *) are completed to ensure that the form is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name* (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your client's name to begin the intake process.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, optional): Enter your client's contact number for communication.
* **Email Address** (email_address, email, optional): Enter your client's email address for communication.
* **Insurance Type* (`insurance_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of insurance your client has (Automobile, Workers Compensation, or Workers Comp).
* **Injury Date* (`injury_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the injury (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Injury Time* (`injury_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the injury (HH:MM format).
* **Location of Injury* (`location_of_injury`, text, required): Provide the location where the injury occurred.
* **Witness Statement* (`witness_statement`, note, required): Enter a brief statement about what happened.
* **Client Statement* (`client_statement`, note, required): Write your personal statement about the incident.
* **Additional Info** (additional_info, text, optional): If you have any additional information, please enter it here.
* **Upload Documents* (`upload_documents`, note, required): You can upload documents related to the claim.
* **Insurance Documents** (insurance_documents, note, required): You can upload documents related to the insurance claim.
* **Upload Pictures* (`upload_pictures`, note, required): You can upload pictures related to the claim.
* **Upload Videos* (`upload_videos`, note, required): You can upload videos related to the claim.
* **Medical Info* (`medical_info`, text, required): Provide your medical information.
* **Medical Experts* (`medical_experts`, text, required): List the name of any medical experts involved.
* **Law Firm* (`law_firm`, text, required): Enter the name of your law firm.
* **Law Firm Attorney** (law_firm_attorney, text, optional): Enter the name of your attorney.
* **Attorney** (attorney, text, optional): Enter the name of the attorney handling the case.
* **Case Number* (`case_number`, number, required): Enter the case number.
* **Case Status* (`case_status`, text, required): Enter the current status of the case.
* **Case Notes* (`case_notes`, text, required): Enter any notes about the case.
* **Insurance Company* (`insurance_company`, text, required): Enter the name of your client's insurance company.
* **Insurance Claim* (`insurance_claim`, text, required): Enter any details about the insurance claim.
* **Attorney Name* (`attorney_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the attorney handling the case.
* **Upload Attachments* (`upload_attachments`, note, required): Please do not complete this field as it has no corresponding field in the form.
