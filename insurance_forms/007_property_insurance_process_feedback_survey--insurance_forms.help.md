# Property Insurance Process Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the property insurance process from users. It's a subjective evaluation tool to help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions carefully and ensure you're providing feedback on a single process.
2. Rate each stage of the property insurance process based on your experience.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience with the property insurance process, using a scale of 1-5.
* **Stage 1** (`stage_1`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you felt about the length of Stage 1:
	+ Too long
	+ Too short
	+ Just right
* **Stage 2** (`stage_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the stages of the process that you felt were too long or too short:

	+ Too short

	+ Too long

	+ Just right
* **Communication** (`communication`, `text`, required: false): Provide any comments or suggestions about how we can improve communication during the process.
* **Stage 4** (`stage_4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the stages of the process that you felt were too long or too short:
	+ Too long

	+ Too long

	+ Just right
* **Timeliness** (`timeliness`, `number`, required: false): Rate how timely you felt the process was, using a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Service** (`overall_service`, `number`, required: false): Rate the overall service you received during the process, using a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the process.
