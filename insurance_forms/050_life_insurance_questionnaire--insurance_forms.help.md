# Life Insurance Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create or update a life insurance policy by collecting necessary information for policy issuance or policy renewal. Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure efficient processing of your policy application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the **Personal Details** section.
2. Enter your contact information in the **Contact Info** section.
3. Select your preferred **Coverage Options** from the list.
4. Enter your **Premium Details**, including the premium amount and policy term.
5. Provide **Beneficiary Information** for your policy.
6. If applicable, include any **Insurance History** for reference.
7. Select your preferred **Policy Details**.
8. Choose your preferred **Payment Plan**.
9. Review and acknowledge the **Review and Acknowledge** section.
10. Sign the **Signature** section to confirm your information.
11. If applicable, upload **Policy Documents**.
12. Select your preferred **Coverage Types** for your policy.
13. Confirm your **Premium Amount**, **Policy Term**, and **Premium Frequency**.
14. Set your **Policy Start Date** and **Policy Start Time**.
15. Enter your **Death Benefit**, **Rider Benefits**, and **Premium Payment Method**.
16. Select your preferred **Payment Frequency** and **Payment Start Date**.
17. Finally, confirm your **Payment Terms** and **Payment Start Time**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coverage Options (Select one or more)**: This section allows you to choose from a list of coverage types, including Whole Life, Term Life, Level Term, Universal Life, Index Universal Life, and Variable Universal Life.
* **Premium Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to pay as premium for your policy.
* **Policy Term**: Enter the term of your policy, in years.
* **Premium Frequency**: Choose how often you want to pay your premium, from Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, or Annually.
* **Policy Start Date**: Enter the date you want your policy to start.
* **Policy Start Time**: Enter the start time of your policy, in 24-hour format.
* **Death Benefit**: Enter the death benefit amount for your policy.
* **Rider Benefits**: Select any additional benefits you want to include with your policy, such as Accidental Death Benefit, Waiver of Premium Benefit, Disability Benefit, Long-Term Care Rider, or Short-Term Care Rider.
* **Premium Payment Method**: Choose how you want to pay your premium, from Cash, Automatic Bank Draft, or Other.
* **Payment Terms**: Enter the payment terms, in years.
* **Payment Frequency**: Choose how often you want to pay your premium, from Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, or Annually.
* **Payment Start Date**: Enter the date you want to start paying your premium.
* **Payment Start Time**: Enter the start time of your premium payment, in 24-hour format.
* **Policy Status**: This section is for your reference, showing the current status of your policy.
* **Policy Documents**: If required, upload any policy documents, such as policy certificates or riders.
* **Coverage Types**: Select all applicable coverage types that you want to include with your policy.
* **Signature**: Sign this section to confirm that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of your policy.
* **Review and Acknowledge**: Review all the information you have entered and acknowledge that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of your policy.
