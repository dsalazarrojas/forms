# Out Of Network Benefits Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Out-of-Netwrok Benefits Inquiry form is designed to gather information about a patient's out-of-network benefits, including their out-of-pocket costs and coverage details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Select the date of service for the patient's out-of-network benefit from the **Date of Service** field.
3. Identify the healthcare provider who is treating the patient in the **Provider Name** field.
4. Indicate if the patient has out-of-network benefits by selecting either **Yes** or **No** in the **Out-of-Network Benefit** field.
5. If the patient has out-of-network benefits, describe the benefit in the **Out-of-Network Benefit Description** field.
6. Enter the contact number and email for follow-up in the **Contact Number** and **Contact Email** fields, respectively.
7. Indicate if a follow-up is required by selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **Follow-up Required** field.
8. Select the follow-up method for the patient's out-of-network benefit in the **Follow-up Method** field.
9. Enter the follow-up date and time in the **Follow-up Date** and **Follow-up Time** fields, respectively.
10. Enter the location for the follow-up in the **Follow-up Location** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their insurance card or identification.
* **Date of Service**: Select the date when the patient received services outside of their network.
* **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who treated the patient.
* **Out-of-Network Benefit**: Select **Yes** if the patient has out-of-network benefits; otherwise, select **No**.
* **Out-of-Network Benefit Description**: Describe the patient's out-of-network benefit, including any out-of-pocket costs.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number for the patient or their representative.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address for the patient or their representative.
* **Follow-up Required**: Select **Yes** if a follow-up is necessary for the patient's out-of-network benefit; otherwise, select **No**.
* **Follow-up Method**: Select the method of follow-up communication (e.g., **Yes**, **No**).
* **Follow-up Date**: Enter the date for the follow-up communication.
* **Follow-up Time**: Enter the time for the follow-up communication.
* **Follow-up Location**: Enter the location for the follow-up communication.
