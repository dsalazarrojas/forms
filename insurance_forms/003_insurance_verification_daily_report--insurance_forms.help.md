# Insurance Verification Daily Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Verification Daily Report form is designed to collect and verify information for insurance claims on a daily basis. It serves as a tool to track claim statuses, gather necessary details from claimants and submitters, and facilitate note-taking and communication among authorized personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Company Name of the insurance provider.
2. Select the Claim Date of the claim.
3. Enter the Claim Number of the claim.
4. Enter the name of the Insurance Provider.
5. Enter the name of the Claimant.
6. Enter the phone number of the Claimant.
7. Enter the email address of the Claimant.
8. Select the Insurance Claim Status from the options (Open, Closed, or Pending).
9. Enter any additional information or notes about the claim in the Claim Note section.
10. Enter your name as the Submitter.
11. Enter your phone number as the Submitter.
12. Enter your email address as the Submitter.
13. Select the date you are submitting the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the insurance provider. This is usually the name of the company that issued the policy.
* **Claim Date**: Select the date the claim was made. This is usually the date the claimant submitted the claim.
* **Claim Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the claim. This is usually provided by the insurance provider.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of the company that issued the policy.
* **Claimant Name**: Enter the name of the person making the claim.
* **Claimant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the claimant. This can be used for contact purposes.
* **Claimant Email**: Enter the email address of the claimant. This can be used for communication.
* **Insurance Claim Status**: Select the status of the claim from the options (Open, Closed, or Pending).
* **Claim Note**: Enter any additional information or notes about the claim. This can be used for future reference.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the report.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number as the person submitting the report.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address as the person submitting the report.
* **Submitter Date**: Select the date you are submitting the report.
