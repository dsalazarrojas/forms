# insurance_coverage_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about insurance coverage inquires from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the field provided.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field provided.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the format of `username@example.com`.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX-XXX-XXXXX` (e.g., 555-123-4567).
5. Enter your **Birth Date** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
6. Select the type of **Insurance Type** you are interested in:
	* **Life**
	* **Health**
	* **Home**
7. Select the type of **Coverage** you need:
	* **Auto**
	* **Bike**
	* **House**
8. Enter the start date of your **Policy Start Date** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
9. Enter the end date of your **Policy End Date** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
10. Enter the name of the **Agent's Name** responsible for your policy (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name here. This is a required field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here. This is a required field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address here, in the format `username@example.com`. This is a required field.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX-XXX-XXXXX` (e.g., 555-123-4567). This is a required field.
* **Birth Date**: Enter your birth date in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`. This is a required field.
* **Insurance Type**: Select the type of insurance you are interested in:
	+ **Life**: For life insurance.
	+ **Health**: For health insurance.
	+ **Home**: For home insurance.
* **Coverage**: Select the type of coverage you need:
	+ **Auto**: For car insurance.
	+ **Bike**: For bike insurance.
	+ **House**: For home insurance.
* **Policy Start Date**: Enter the start date of your policy in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`. This is a required field.
* **Policy End Date**: Enter the end date of your policy in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`. This is a required field.
* **Agent's Name**: Enter the name of the agent responsible for your policy (optional).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check that your email address and phone number are entered correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the support team for assistance.
