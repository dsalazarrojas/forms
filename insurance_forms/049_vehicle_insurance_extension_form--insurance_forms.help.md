# vehicle-insurance-extension-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to extend or modify an existing vehicle insurance policy. It is designed to capture the necessary information to update or add to the current coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of insurance coverage you need to extend or modify.
2.  Enter the make of your vehicle.
3.  Enter the model of your vehicle.
4.  Enter the year of your vehicle.
5.  Enter the date your vehicle's insurance coverage is set to start.
6.  Enter the date your vehicle's insurance coverage is set to end.
7.  Enter your name as the driver or policyholder.
8.  Enter your phone number as the driver or policyholder.
9.  Enter your email as the driver or policyholder.
10.  Enter the license plate number of your vehicle.
11.  Enter the state of your vehicle's license plate.
12.  Enter the country of your vehicle's license plate.
13.  Enter the expiration date of your vehicle's license plate.
14.  Enter the name of your insurance agent.
15.  Enter your insurance agent's phone number.
16.  Enter your insurance agent's email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Insurance Type** (insurance_type): Select the type of insurance coverage you need to extend or modify.
* **Vehicle Make** (vehicle-make): Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (vehicle-model): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (vehicle-year): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Insurance Start Date** (insurance-start-date): Enter the date your vehicle's insurance coverage is set to start.
* **Insurance End Date** (insurance-end-date): Enter the date your vehicle's insurance coverage is set to end.
* **Driver Name** (driver-name): Enter your name as the driver or policyholder.
* **Driver Phone** (driver-phone): Enter your phone number as the driver or policyholder.
* **Driver Email** (driver-email): Enter your email as the driver or policyholder.
* **Vehicle Plate** (vehicle-plate): Enter the license plate number of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Plate State** (vehicle-plate-state): Enter the state of your vehicle's license plate.
* **Vehicle Plate Country** (vehicle-plate-country): Enter the country of your vehicle's license plate.
* **Vehicle Plate Expiration Date** (vehicle-plate-expiration-date): Enter the expiration date of your vehicle's license plate.
* **Insurance Agent Name** (insurance-agent-name): Enter the name of your insurance agent.
* **Insurance Agent Phone** (insurance-agent-phone): Enter your insurance agent's phone number.
* **Insurance Agent Email** (insurance-agent-email): Enter your insurance agent's email.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the information you enter to ensure it matches your current vehicle insurance policy.
* Review and correct any entered information before submission.
* If you need help, contact your insurance agent or the support team.
