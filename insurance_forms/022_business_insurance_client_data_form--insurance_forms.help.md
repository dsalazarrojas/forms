# Business Insurance Client Data Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Insurance Client Data Form is a crucial tool for capturing and processing essential information from business clients to provide tailored insurance solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2. Enter the business name and contact name in the required fields.
3. Fill in the contact email and business address in the required fields.
4. Provide the contact phone number and annual revenue if applicable.
5. Select the main industry sector of the business.
6. Specify the approximate number of employees and current insurance policies (if any).
7. Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business name**: Enter the legal or trading name of your business.
* **Contact name**: Enter the name of your primary contact for insurance matters.
* **Contact email**: Enter your primary contact email for policy communications.
* **Contact phone**: Enter your business phone number for easy communication (optional).
* **Business address**: Enter the physical address of your business operations.
* **Industry**: Select the main industry sector of your business (choose from a list or type if not provided).
* **Annual revenue**: Enter your approximate yearly revenue.
* **Number of employees**: Enter the total number of staff including part-time employees.
* **Current policies**: List existing insurance policies with the policy number or description.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling out the form to ensure we provide the best possible insurance solutions for your business.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
