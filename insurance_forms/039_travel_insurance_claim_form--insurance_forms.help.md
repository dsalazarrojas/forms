# travel_insurance_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for travelers to submit claims related to their travel insurance policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the reason for your claim.
2.  Enter the date you departed for your trip.
3.  Enter the date you returned from your trip.
4.  Select the type of trip.
5.  Enter your policy number.
6.  Enter the name of the travel company.
7.  Enter your name.
8.  Enter your email address.
9.  Enter your phone number.
10. Enter the name of the policy holder.
11. Enter the email address of the policy holder.
12. Enter the phone number of the policy holder.
13. Enter any additional information about your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **reason_for_claim** (Select One): Select the reason for your claim. Options include:
    *   Trip was cancelled
    *   Trip was postponed
    *   Other
*   **start_date**: Enter the date you departed for your trip.
*   **end_date**: Enter the date you returned from your trip.
*   **claim_submission_date**: Enter the date you submitted your claim. (Renamed from "claim_date")
*   **trip_type**: Select the type of trip. Options include:
    *   Business
    *   Leisure
*   **policy_num**: Enter your policy number.
*   **travel_company**: Enter the name of the travel company.
*   **agent_name**: Enter your name.
*   **email**: Enter your email address.
*   **phone**: Enter your phone number.
*   **policy_holder**: Enter the name of the policy holder.
*   **policy_holder_email**: Enter the email address of the policy holder.
*   **policy_holder_phone**: Enter the phone number of the policy holder.
*   **claim_details**: Enter any additional information about your claim. (Renamed from "claim_details")

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have all necessary information to fill out the form accurately.
*   If you need help or have questions, please contact us.
*   Ensure that you submit your claim within the required timeframe to avoid delays.
