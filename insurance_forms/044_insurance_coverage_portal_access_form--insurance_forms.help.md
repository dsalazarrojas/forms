# insurance_coverage_portal_access_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The insurance coverage portal access form is a tool used to collect and manage client information for insurance purposes. It is designed for agents or representatives to gather essential details about clients for insurance coverage needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Client Details**: Start by entering the client's name and email address. Make sure the email address is correct as it will be used to communicate with the client.
2. **Coverage Information**: Select the relevant coverage type and level from the provided options. This will help ensure that you have the correct information for the client's coverage needs.
3. **Additional Details**: Enter the client's phone number, date of birth, and other relevant details as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address for communication purposes.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number for contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_prophets`, date, required): Enter the client's date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Coverage Type** (`coverage_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of coverage the client requires.
* **Coverage Level** (`coverage_level`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable coverage levels for the client.
* **Date Provided** (`date_provided`, date, required): Enter the date when the coverage was provided to the client.
* **Time Provided** (`time_provided`, time, required): Enter the time when the coverage was provided to the client.
* **Insurance Number** (`insurance_number`, text, required): Enter the client's insurance number.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required): Enter the client's insurance provider.
* **Client Note** (`client_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the client.
* **Insurance Note** (`insurance_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the insurance.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are completed to avoid form submission errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to the form's purpose and instructions for clarification.
