# LIFE Needs Analysis - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LIFE Needs Analysis form is designed to collect information on an individual's life needs and circumstances to identify areas where additional support or coverage may be necessary.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that applies to you.
3. For text fields, provide a brief description of your response.
4. If a field is optional, you can skip it if you don't have information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Needs Analysis** (`needs_analysis`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your current life needs and circumstances.
* **Life Needs** (`life_needs`, select_one, required/optional): Please select 'Yes' if you have any life needs that require attention, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Family Info** (`family_info`, text, required/optional): If applicable, provide a brief description of your family situation, such as number of dependents or family members.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): If applicable, provide a brief description of your medical history, including any conditions that may impact your life needs.
* **Life Needs Determination** (`life_needs_determination`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of your life needs and determination.
* **Insurance Status** (`insurance_status`, select_one, required/optional): Please select 'Active' if you have current insurance coverage, and 'Inactive' otherwise.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all the insurance providers that apply to you.
* **Coverage Details** (`coverage_details`, text, required/optional): If applicable, provide a brief description of your current coverage details, such as policy numbers or provider names.

## Tips

* Please answer all fields that apply to your situation.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can ask for clarification.
* This form is designed to help identify areas where additional support or coverage may be necessary, so please answer truthfully and thoroughly.
