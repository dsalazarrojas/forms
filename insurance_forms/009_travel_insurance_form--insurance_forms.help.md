# Travel Insurance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Insurance Form is designed to collect information about your travel plans, such as your destination, trip duration, and travel insurance preferences. This form helps us better understand your travel requirements and provide personalized insurance options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Determine if you want to purchase travel insurance. Choose between 'True' and 'False' options.
2.  If you answered 'True', provide a brief description of your trip in the 'Description' field.
3.  In the 'Purpose of the Travel' field, explain why you are traveling.
4.  Enter your destination in the 'Destination' field.
5.  Finally, specify the duration of your trip in the 'Duration' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Would you like travel insurance?** (travel_insurance, select_one, optional):
    This field asks if you would like to purchase travel insurance. Choose 'True' if you wish to purchase travel insurance, and 'False' otherwise.

*   **Description** (travel_insurance_description, note, optional):
    Provide a brief description of your trip to help us understand your requirements.

*   **Purpose of the travel** (travel_insurance_purpose, text, optional):
    In this field, explain why you are traveling, including the nature of your trip, e.g., leisure, business, etc.

*   **Destination** (travel_insurance_destination, text, optional):
    Enter your destination in this field. This will help us provide you with relevant travel insurance options.

*   **Duration** (travel_insurance_duration, number, optional):
    Specify the duration of your trip. This field is used to calculate insurance premiums and provide you with accurate quotes.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all questions truthfully to get accurate travel insurance quotes.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for assistance.
- This form is for travel insurance purposes only; we may use the information provided to contact you with personalized insurance quotes or recommendations.
