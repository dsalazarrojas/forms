# Loss Control Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help organizations assess and improve their loss control measures. It guides users through a series of questions to gather information about potential risks, their locations, and the control measures in place to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please complete each section according to your organization's loss control processes and procedures.
* Answer each question truthfully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or risk manager for guidance.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Loss Control Survey

* **loss_control_survey**: This is the name of the form, which will be saved as is in the database.
* **risk_assessment**: A brief assessment of potential risks to your organization.
* **hazard_location**: The location where the risk is present.
* **risk_factors**: A list of factors that contribute to the risk.
* **risk_factors_description**: A description of each risk factor.
* **risk_factors_control**: A list of control measures in place to mitigate the risk.
* **loss_control_plan**: A plan to control or mitigate the risk.
* **loss_control_measures**: A list of measures taken to control or mitigate the risk.
* **loss_control_measures_description**: A description of each control measure.
* **loss_control_measures_control**: The effectiveness of each control measure.
* **loss_control_measures_sustainability**: How sustainable is each control measure.
* **loss_control_measures_cost**: The cost of each control measure.
* **loss_control_measures_frequency**: How often each control measure is reviewed or updated.
* **loss_control_measures_status**: The status of each control measure.
* **loss_control_measures_status_description**: A description of each measure's status.
* **loss_control_survey_date**: The date of the loss control survey.
* **loss_control_survey_time**: The time of the loss control survey.
* **loss_control_survey_notes**: Any additional notes or comments about the survey.

### 2. Risk Control Survey Date

* **risk_control_survey_date**: The date of the risk control survey.

### 3. Notes

* **loss_control_survey_notes**: Any additional notes or comments about the survey.

### Tips

* Please ensure you have permission from your supervisor or risk manager before sharing any information.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not share it with external parties.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or risk manager for guidance.
