# Animal Care Insurance Claim Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Animal Care Insurance Claim Request" form is a critical tool for policyholders to seek reimbursement for veterinary care, illnesses, or other issues related to their insured pets. This form collects essential information about the policyholder, pet, and treatment costs to process claims efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your information: 
   - **Policyholder Full Name**: Enter the name of the insured pet owner.
   - **Email Address**: Provide your contact email for communication.
   - **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
   - **Address**: Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.

2.  Complete pet information: 
   - **Pet's Name**: Enter the name of the pet.
   - **Species**: Select the type of animal (e.g., Dog, Cat, Bird, Exotic, or Other).
   - **Breed**: Enter the pet's breed or mix.
   - **Pet Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth or approximate age of the pet.

3.  Describe the incident or illness: 
   - **Incident Details**: Provide a short description of the incident or illness (optional).
   - **Date of Incident or Illness Onset**: Enter the date when the incident or illness occurred.
   - **Type of Claim**: Choose the type of claim category (e.g., Accident or injury, Illness or disease, Routine wellness, Emergency care, Surgery, Medication, Diagnostic testing, or Other).
   - **Describe What Happened**: Provide a detailed description of the incident or symptoms.

4.  Treatment and costs: 
   - **Veterinary Clinic Name**: Enter the name of the clinic where treatment was received.
   - **Clinic Address**: Enter the clinic's street address, city, state, and zip code.
   - **Veterinarian Name**: Enter the name of the treating veterinarian.
   - **Veterinarian Phone**: Enter the clinic's phone number.
   - **Diagnosis**: Enter the veterinary diagnosis.
   - **Treatments Provided**: Describe the treatments received.
   - **Examination Fee**: Enter the examination fee in dollars.
   - **Treatment Cost**: Enter the total treatment cost in dollars.
   - **Medication Cost**: Enter the medication cost in dollars.
   - **Diagnostic Testing Cost**: Enter the diagnostic testing cost in dollars.
   - **Other Costs**: Enter any additional expenses.

5.  Supporting documents: 
   - **Attach Supporting Documents**: Upload supporting documents such as veterinary invoices, medical records, prescriptions, lab results, imaging reports, or referral letters.
   - **Select Documents**: Choose the type of documents you are attaching (e.g., Veterinary invoice, Medical records, Prescription receipts, Lab results, Imaging reports, Referral letter, or Other).

6.  Claim details: 
   - **Total Claim Amount**: Enter the sum of all costs.
   - **Have You Filed Previous Claims for This Pet**: Choose whether you've filed claims for this pet before.
   - **Is This Related to a Pre-existing Condition**: Indicate whether the claim is related to a pre-existing condition.
   - **Pre-Existing Condition Status**: If unsure, choose 'Unsure'.

7.  Finalize your claim: 
   - **Payment Preference**: Choose how you'd like to receive reimbursement (e.g., Direct deposit, Check by mail, or Same as previous claims).
   - **I Declare that the Information Provided is True and Accurate**: Confirm that the information is accurate.
   - **Policyholder Signature**: Sign with your full name.
   - **Date**: Enter today's date.

## Tips

- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Attach supporting documents as needed to process the claim efficiently.
- Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
- Double-check for any errors or inaccuracies.
- If you're unsure about any information, contact your insurance provider for assistance.
