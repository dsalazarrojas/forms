# healthcare_insurance_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare insurance subscription form is a form used to collect information from clients who wish to subscribe to a healthcare insurance plan. This form is used to gather necessary details such as contact information, insurance provider, policy numbers, and dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "client_name" field.
2. Provide the client's contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
3. Enter the client's email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter the client's address in the "address" field.
5. Indicate if the client has an insurance provider in the "insurance_provider" field.
6. Enter the client's policy number in the "policy_number" field.
7. Enter the client's policy effective date and time in the "policy_effective_date" and "policy_effective_time" fields.
8. Enter the client's policy expiration date and time in the "policy_expiration_date" and "policy_expiration_date" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`): This field is a placeholder for the form header.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter the client's name in this field.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the client's contact phone number in this field.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the client's email address in this field.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter the client's address in this field.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`): Indicate if the client has an insurance provider.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`): Enter the client's policy number.
* **Policy Effective Date** (`policy_effective_date`): Enter the client's policy effective date.
* **Policy Expiration Date** (`policy_expiration_date`): Enter the client's policy expiration date.
* **Policy Effective Time** (`policy_effective_time`): Enter the client's policy effective time.

Note: This form does not have any required fields, so you can complete it as needed.
