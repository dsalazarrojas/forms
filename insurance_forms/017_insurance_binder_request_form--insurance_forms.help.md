# Insurance Binder Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for an insurance binder request. A binder is a legal document that confirms a customer's insurance coverage.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in all required fields (marked as required) and provide relevant information.
*   For non-required fields, only fill in the information if it is relevant and necessary.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Insurance Binder Request Form (`insurance_binder_request_form`)**: This is the title of the form.
*   **Policy Number (`policy_number`)**: Enter the policy number associated with the policyholder's account.
*   **Policy Effective Date (`policy_effective_date`)**: Enter the effective date of the policy, if applicable.
*   **Policy End Date (`policy_end_date`)**: Enter the end date of the policy, if applicable.
*   **Policy Holder (`policy_holder`)**: Enter the name of the policyholder.
*   **Insurance Company (`insurance_company`)**: Select whether the policyholder is affiliated with the insurance company. You can select multiple options if applicable.
*   **Agent (`agent`)**: Enter the name of the policyholder's insurance agent, as this is a required field.
*   **Binder Status (`binder_status`)**: Select the status of the binder. Options include Pending, Active, Inactive, and Cancelled.
*   **Comments (`comments`)**: Enter any additional comments or details about the policyholder's insurance coverage.
