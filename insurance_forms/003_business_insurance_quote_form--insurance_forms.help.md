# Business Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Insurance Quote Form is designed to gather information about your business to provide an accurate and tailored quote for your insurance needs. Please complete this form accurately to ensure we understand your business requirements and circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each field carefully, as it will help us better understand your business needs.
2. If unsure, please ask your contact person for clarification.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name (1)**: Enter the legal name of your business.
* **Business Established Date (2)**: Provide the date your business was founded.
* **Business Address (3)**: Enter the physical location of your business operations.
* **Contact Person Name (4)**: Enter the name of your primary contact person for the policy.
* **Email Address (5)**: Enter the email address for policy communications.
* **Phone Number (6)**: Enter the contact phone number for policy communications.
* **Type of Business (7)**: Select the primary type of business you operate.
* **Number of Employees (8)**: Enter the total number of employees, including the owner(s).
* **Annual Revenue (9)**: Select the approximate yearly revenue of your business.
* **Coverage Types Needed (10)**: Select all applicable coverage types required for your business.
* **Desired General Liability Limit (11)**: Select the desired coverage amount for general liability.
* **Have you had Prior Claims (12)**: Answer 'True' or 'False' if your business has had prior insurance claims in the last 5 years.
* **If Yes, Please Describe (13)**: If you answered 'True', please provide details about the previous claims.
* **Do you have Safety Programs (14)**: Answer 'True', 'False', or 'In Development' if your business has safety protocols or training programs.
* **Preferred Deductible Amount (15)**: Select the preferred deductible amount for your policy.
* **When do you need Coverage to Start (16)**: Enter the desired effective date for your coverage.
* **Additional Information (17)**: Provide any other relevant details about your business or policy needs.

## Tips
- Please review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate information.
- If unsure, please ask your contact person for clarification.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
