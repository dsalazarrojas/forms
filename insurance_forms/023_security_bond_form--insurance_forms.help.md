# Security Bond Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect employee bond details from employees, ensuring they are committed to their employment contract.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee ID number.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
3. Provide your business name (if applicable).
4. Enter the amount of the bond.
5. Choose the payment term (First Month, Second Month, Third Month).
6. Select whether the payment is (Paid or Unpaid).
7. Enter the payment date.
8. Add any notes related to the bond.
9. Enter your employee address.
10. Enter your business address (if applicable).
11. Enter your email address.
12. Enter your phone number.
13. Select who submitted the form (chatjimmy or johndoe).
14. Finally, select the date you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee ID**: A unique number assigned to you as an employee. Please fill in the correct number.
  * Type: number
  * Required: false
- **Employee Name**: Your name as it appears on your ID. Please enter your full name.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Business Name**: The name of your business if you have one. If not applicable, leave blank.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Bond Amount**: The amount of the bond you are committing to.
  * Type: number
  * Required: false
- **Bond Date**: The date you are committing to the bond.
  * Type: date
  * Required: false
- **Payment Terms**: Select the term for the payment (First Month, Second Month, Third Month).
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: false
- **Payment Status**: Choose whether the payment is (Paid or Unpaid).
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: false
- **Payment Date**: The date the payment was made.
  * Type: date
  * Required: false
- **Notes**: Any additional comments related to the bond.
  * Type: note
  * Required: false
- **Employee Address**: Enter your address as an employee.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Business Address**: The address of your business if you have one. If not applicable, leave blank.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Email**: Your email address.
  * Type: email
  * Required: false
- **Phone**: Your phone number.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the form (chatjimmy or johndoe).
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: false
- **Submitted On**: The date you submitted the form.
  * Type: date
  * Required: false
