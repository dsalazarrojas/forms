# Moving Company Damage Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moving Company Damage Claim Form is used to report damages that occurred during a move. It helps our team to gather essential information to assess and process your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information, such as your name and contact details.
2. Provide a detailed description of the damaged item.
3. Indicate the condition of the damaged item using the provided options: Excellent, Fair, or Poor.
4. Enter the value of the damaged item.
5. Specify the amount you are claiming for the damage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **customer_email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **customer_phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **damaged_item_description**: Describe the damaged item in detail, including its type and any other relevant features.
* **item_location**: Indicate the location where the item was damaged.
* **item_condition**: Choose the condition of the damaged item using the options: Excellent, Fair, or Poor.
* **item_value**: Enter the monetary value of the damaged item.
* **claim_amount**: Enter the amount you are claiming for the damage.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and detailed descriptions to help us process your claim efficiently.
- Use the correct email address and phone number where you can be reached.
- If you have any supporting documentation or photos of the damage, please attach them to the form or submit them separately.
