# Insurance Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Insurance Contact Form is used to collect information from applicants about their insurance claims or policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and complete answers to each question.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details** (applicant_details, text, Required): This field is where you will enter your personal details, such as your name, address, and contact information.
* **Emergency Details** (emergency_details, text, Optional): If you have any emergency contact information or special circumstances that the insurance company should be aware of, please enter them here.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, Optional): Enter your contact details, such as your phone number and email address.
* **Policy Details** (policy_details, text, Optional): Provide any relevant information about your policy, such as policy number, policy type, or policy expiration date.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have accurate and complete information before submitting the form.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors or omissions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the insurance company's customer support for assistance.
