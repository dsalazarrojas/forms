# Insurance Claim Distance Declaration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare distances traveled during insurance-related claims.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Claim Distance** field with a description of the distance traveled.
2.  Enter the **From Location** where your journey started.
3.  Enter the **To Location** where your journey ended.
4.  Select the **Date Of Travel** when your journey took place.
5.  Choose the **Mode Of Transport** used for the journey (e.g., automobile, bicycle, train, bus, or other).
6.  Enter the **Travel Start Time** and **Travel End Time** when your journey began and ended.
7.  Enter the **Travel Distance** covered during your journey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Claim Distance**: Describe the distance traveled during your journey.
*   **From Location**: Enter the starting point of your journey.
*   **To Location**: Enter the ending point of your journey.
*   **Date Of Travel**: Select the date when your journey took place.
*   **Travel Start Time**: Enter the time when your journey began.
*   **Travel End Time**: Enter the time when your journey ended.
*   **Mode Of Transport**: Choose the mode of transport used for your journey (e.g., automobile, bicycle, train, bus, or other).
*   **Travel Distance**: Enter the total distance covered during your journey.
*   **Travel Details**: Enter any additional details about your journey that may be relevant to your claim.
