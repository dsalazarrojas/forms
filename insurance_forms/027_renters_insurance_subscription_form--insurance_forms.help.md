# Renters Insurance Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information to create a renters insurance policy for your home or rental property. Please fill out the form accurately to provide the required details for your policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and fill out the required information. You can navigate between pages using the "Next" and "Back" buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Tenant Information (Page 1)
* **Tenant Information**: Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself as the renter. This information will help us understand your identity and needs better. (<em>This field is optional.</em>)

### Policy Details (Page 2)
* **Policy Details**: Enter a brief description of the rental property and your role as the renter. This helps us assess the risk and provide an accurate quote for your insurance. (<em>This field is optional.</em>)

### Type of Insurance (Page 3)
* **Type of Insurance**: Choose the type of insurance that best suits your needs. You can select either "renters" for personal insurance, "landlord" for insurance for the property owner, or "property" for insurance for the property itself. (<em>This field is required.</em>)

### Coverage (Page 3)
* **Coverage**: Select the types of coverage you would like to include in your policy. You can choose "renters", "personal", or both for comprehensive coverage. (<em>This field is required.</em>)

### Premium (Page 4)
* **Premium**: Enter your desired or calculated premium amount for your insurance policy. This value will be used to finalize your policy details. (<em>This field is optional, but required for accurate policy creation.</em>)

### Effective Date (Page 5)
* **Effective Date**: Enter the date you would like your policy to start. This is the date your policy will begin protecting you. (<em>This field is optional.</em>)

### Expiration Date (Page 5)
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the date you would like your policy to expire. This is the date your policy will end and need to be renewed or updated. (<em>This field is optional.</em>)

### Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
