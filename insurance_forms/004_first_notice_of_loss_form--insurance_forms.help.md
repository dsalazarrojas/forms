# First Notice Of Loss Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The First Notice Of Loss Form is used to report an incident or loss that may be covered under an insurance policy. It is an essential document to provide details about the event and support the claims process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the insured's name and address in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose the relevant loss type(s) from the list provided.
3. Provide information about the location where the loss occurred.
4. Enter the date and time of the loss.
5. Report the extent of the damage or loss.
6. If available, provide contact information for any witnesses.
7. If a police report was filed, please enter the police report number and location.
8. If an adjuster is assigned to your case, please provide their name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Insured Name** (`insured_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or entity insured under the policy.
* **Insured Address** (`insured_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the insured person or entity.
* **Loss Type** (`loss_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type(s) of loss that occurred (e.g., theft, vandalism, natural disaster).
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Describe the location where the loss occurred.
* **Loss Date** (`loss_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the loss.
* **Loss Time** (`loss_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the loss.
* **Damage Amount** (`damage_amount`, number, required): Report the estimated amount of damage or loss.
* **Damage Description** (`damage_description`, note, required): Describe the extent of the damage or loss.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): If available, enter the name of a witness or person who witnessed the loss.
* **Witness Address** (`witness_address`, text, optional): If available, enter the address of the witness.
* **Witness Phone** (`witness_phone`, text, optional): If available, enter the phone number of the witness.
* **Police Report Number** (`police_report_number`, text, optional): If a police report was filed, enter the police report number.
* **Police Report Location** (`police_report_location`, text, optional): If a police report was filed, enter the location where the report was filed.
* **Adjuster Name** (`adjuster_name`, text, optional): If an adjuster is assigned to your case, enter their name.
* **Adjuster Phone** (`adjuster_phone`, text, optional): If an adjuster is assigned to your case, enter their phone number.

**Tips**

* Please provide as much detail as possible to help us understand the extent of the loss.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask for help or guidance from your insurance representative.
* This form is an essential document to support your claim, so please fill it out accurately and completely.
