# Acord Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Acord Cancellation Form is used to initiate the cancellation of an insurance policy.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the form carefully and fill it out completely.
2. Ensure that you are submitting only one form for each policy being cancelled.
3. Provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth cancellation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy holder name** (`policy_holder_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the insured individual or organization.
* **Policy number** (`policy_number`, `text`, required): Enter the insurance policy identifier.
* **Insurer name** (`insurer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the insurance company.
* **Cancellation effective date** (`cancellation_effective_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the policy cancellation should take effect.
* **Reason for cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, `text`, required): Enter a brief reason for cancelling the policy.
* **Returned premiums** (`returned_premiums`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if any premium refund is expected.
* **Broker or agent** (`broker_or_agent`, `text`, optional): Enter the broker handling the cancellation if applicable.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter a phone number for confirmation.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `text`, optional): Enter an email for cancellation notice.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Enter an authorized signature or typed name.
* **Date signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date of signature.
* **Additional instructions** (`additional_instructions`, `text`, optional): Enter any special processing instructions.
