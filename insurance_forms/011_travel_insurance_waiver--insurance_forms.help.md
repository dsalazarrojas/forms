# travel Insurance Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about travel insurance waivers. It is used to verify that employees have the necessary insurance coverage for travel.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the Traveler's Name.
2. Enter the Contact Email for the person who is responsible for the travel insurance waiver.
3. Fill in the Policy Number (if available).
4. Enter the Effective Date of the policy.
5. Enter the Expiration Date of the policy.
6. Select the type of policy you hold (Travel Insurance or Other).
7. Specify the area that is covered by your insurance policy.
8. Enter the name of your insurance provider.
9. Enter the policy status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Traveler's Name** (Traveler's Name, text, **required**): This is the name of the person who will be traveling.
  - **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, **required**): This is the email address of the person responsible for the travel insurance waiver.
  - **Policy Number** (Policy Number, text, **optional**): This is the number assigned to your policy by your insurance provider.
  - **Effective Date** (Effective Date, date, **required**): This is the date when your policy started.
  - **Expiration Date** (Expiration Date, date, **required**): This is the date when your policy ends.
  - **Policy Type** (Policy Type, select_one, **optional**): Please select the type of policy you hold (Travel Insurance or Other).
  - **Coverage Area** (Coverage Area, text, **optional**): Describe the area that is covered by your insurance policy.
  - **Insurance Provider** (Insurance Provider, text, **optional**): This is the name of your insurance provider.
  - **Policy Status** (Policy Status, text, **optional**): Please describe the status of your policy (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
