# travel_insurance_claim_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "travel_insurance_claim_records_release_form" is used to release or manage travel insurance claim records. This form is intended for insurance companies or claims administrators to update and release claims information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the form title, which should describe the purpose of the form and the type of claim being made.
2. Select the category for the claim, such as "Insurance Forms" or "Other".
3. Provide a description of the claim, including any relevant details or circumstances.
4. Repeat steps 1-3 for each additional form section, as the form has multiple sections with similar fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can enter a brief description of the form and the type of claim being made.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category for the claim, such as "Insurance Forms" or "Other".
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the claim, including any relevant details or circumstances.
* **Form Title2** (`form_title2`, `text`, required: false): This field is similar to the "Form Title" field and is likely a duplicate or an optional field for further description.
* **Category2** (`category2`, `select_one`, required: false): Another category selection field, similar to the "Category" field.
* **Description2** (`description2`, `text`, required: false): Another description field, similar to the "Description" field.
* ... (similar fields continue)

## Tips
* Be clear and concise in your descriptions to ensure accurate and efficient claim processing.
* If you're unsure about a field or its purpose, please contact your claims administrator or refer to the travel insurance policy document for clarification.
