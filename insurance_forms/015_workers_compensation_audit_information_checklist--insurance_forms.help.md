# Workers' Compensation Audit Information Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workers' Compensation Audit Information Checklist is a form used to collect information for an audit of workers' compensation policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the policy details and ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
2. Enter the policy audit start and end dates in the respective fields.
3. Enter the employee docket number.
4. Enter the insurance policy number, which is a required field.
5. Select the policy audit frequency and status from the provided options.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the policy.
7. Attach any supporting documentation or files related to the policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Worker Compensation Audit Details** (`worker_compensation_audit_details`): A brief description of the policy audit details.
* **Policy Audit Start Date** (`policy_audit_start_date`): Enter the start date of the policy audit.
* **Policy Audit End Date** (`policy_audit_end_date`): Enter the end date of the policy audit.
* **Employee Docket** (`employee_docket`): Enter the employee docket number for the policy.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (`insurance_policy_number`): A required field that contains the insurance policy number for the policy being audited.
* **Policy Audit Frequency** (`policy_audit_frequency`): Select whether the policy is audited annually or not.
* **Policy Audit Status** (`policy_audit_status`): Select the current status of the policy audit, whether it is active, inactive, or pending.
* **Policy Audit Comments** (`policy_audit_comments`): Add any comments or notes about the policy.
* **Policy Audit Attachments** (`policy_audit_attachment`): Attach any supporting documentation or files related to the policy. This field can be used to attach multiple files.

Note: The field labels are based on the provided YAML fields and may not be as clear as desired. If you are unsure about a particular field, please refer to the field's label and the provided instructions.
