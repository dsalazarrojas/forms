# Vehicle And Home Insurance Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information necessary for a vehicle and home insurance quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your vehicle information in the "Vehicle Information" section.
2. Enter your home insurance information in the "Home Insurance Information" section.
3. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
4. Specify your vehicle type and usage in the "Vehicle Type and Usage" section.
5. Select your home coverage type and quote type in the "Home Coverage Type and Quote Type" section.
6. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_information`, `text`, `Vehicle Information`):
	+ This section is where you will enter information about your vehicle.
* **Home Insurance Information** (`home_insurance_information`, `text`, `Home Insurance Information`):
	+ This section is where you will enter information about your home insurance needs.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, `Contact Details`):
	+ This section is where you will enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, `select_one`, `Vehicle Type`):
	+ Please select the type of vehicle you are insuring (car, home, other).
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, `Vehicle Year`):
	+ Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, `Vehicle Make`):
	+ Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, `text`, `Home Address`):
	+ Enter the address of your home.
* **Home State** (`home_state`, `select_one`, `Home State`):
	+ Please select the state in which your home is located.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `Email`):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `Phone`):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Vehicle Usage** (`vehicle_use`, `select_multiple`, `Vehicle Usage`):
	+ Please select how you use your vehicle (daily, weekly, monthly, other).
* **Home Coverage Type** (`home_coverage_type`, `select_multiple`, `Home Coverage Type`):
	+ Please select the type of home coverage you need (basic, extended, umbrella).
* **Quote Type** (`quote_type`, `select_multiple`, `Quote Type`):
	+ Please select the type of quote you are interested in (monthly, quarterly, six monthly, yearly).

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
