# Fire Damage Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is the Fire Damage Claim Form, designed to collect relevant information from individuals who have experienced fire damage. Please use this form to provide accurate and complete data to support your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your contact information (Claimant Information) to identify you as the person making the claim.
2. Describe the fire accident that caused the damage, including date, time, and location (Fire Accident).
3. Provide details about the damage caused by the fire, such as extent of the damage (Damage Details).
4. Enter your insurance information, including policy number and provider (Insurance Information).
5. Indicate the current status of your claim (Claim Status).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Claimant Information
*   **Claimant Information (Claimant Information)** (`claimant_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you provide your contact details, such as your name, address, and phone number. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.

### Fire Accident
*   **Fire Accident (accident_info, text, required: false): Describe the fire accident that caused the damage. This includes the date, time, and location of the incident. Be as specific as possible to help with the claim process.

### Damage Details
*   **Damage Details (damage_details, text, required: false): Here, you will describe the extent of the damage caused by the fire. This could include details about the severity of the damage, the affected areas, and any other relevant information. 

### Insurance Information
*   **Insurance Information (insurance_info, text, required: false): Enter your insurance policy number and provider's name. This information is essential to process your claim.

### Claim Status
*   **Claim Status (claim_status, select_one, required: true): Choose one of the following options:
    *   **Open**: The claim has been reported but not yet processed.
    *   **Submitted**: The claim has been submitted to the insurance company.
    *   **Approved**: The claim has been approved by the insurance company.
    *   **Denied**: The claim has been denied by the insurance company.
