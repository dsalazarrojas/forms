# insurance_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your insurance needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the form header section, including "What is your insurance goal" and "Form Header Question 2".
2. Select the type of insurance that best suits your needs from the "Policy Benefits" and "Insurance Type" options.
3. Specify your insurance goals and objectives from the "Insurance Goal" options.
4. Enter your age and contact details (phone and email) in the respective fields.
