# Truck Quote Form Long - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from truck drivers or owners to provide a quote for truck insurance. Please fill out the form with your personal details to receive an estimate of your insurance costs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" fields.
3. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter the city where your address is located.
5. Select your state of residence from the drop-down list in the "State" field.
6. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your given name (e.g., John).
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your surname (e.g., Doe).
* **Address** (Text, Required): Enter your street address, including the number and apartment number (if applicable).
* **City** (Text, Required): Enter the city where your address is located.
* **State** (Select One, Required): Select the state where you reside from the drop-down list.
* **Zip** (Number, Required): Enter your five-digit zip code.
