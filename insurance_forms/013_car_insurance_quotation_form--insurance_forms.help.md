# Car Insurance Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive car insurance quotation form designed to gather essential details from applicants to generate a quotation for car insurance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling in your personal details on Page 1.
2. On Page 2, provide information about the vehicle you wish to insure, including year, make, model, VIN, and mileage.
3. If you have additional vehicles, you can fill in details about them on the same page.
4. On Page 3, provide information about all drivers who will be covered, including their name, relationship to you, date of birth, and other relevant details.
5. On Page 4, choose from various coverages such as additional coverages, deductibles, and quote delivery preferences.
6. On Page 5, indicate your coverage preferences and quote request details.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1 - Applicant Information

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Required): Your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Required): Your date of birth.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Required): The email address you'd like your quote sent to.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, Required): Your contact number.
* **Address** (`address`, Required): Your residential address.
* **City** (`city`, Required): The city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, Required): The state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, Required): Your ZIP code.
* **Driver's License Number** (`drivers_license`, Required): Your driver's license number.
* **License State** (`license_state`, Required): The state where your license was issued.
* **Gender** (`gender`, Required): Your gender.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, Required): Your marital status.

### Page 2 - Vehicle Information

* **Vehicle 1 Year** (`vehicle_1_year`, Required): The year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle 1 Make** (`vehicle_1_make`, Required): The make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle 1 Model** (`vehicle_1_model`, Required): The model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle 1 VIN** (`vehicle_1_vin`, Optional): The Vehicle Identification Number of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle 1 Annual Mileage** (`vehicle_1_mileage`, Required): The estimated annual mileage of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle 1 Primary Use** (`vehicle_1_use`, Required): How you use the vehicle.

### Page 3 - Driver Information

* **Number of Drivers** (`number_of_drivers`, Required): The number of drivers to be covered.
* **Driver 1 Name** (`driver_2_name`, Optional): The name of each driver (if not the applicant).
* **Driver 1 Relationship** (`driver_2_relationship`, Optional): The relationship of each driver (if not the applicant).
* **Driver 1 Date of Birth** (`driver_2_dob`, Optional): The date of birth of each driver (if not the applicant).
* **Driving History** (`driving_history`, Optional): Your driving record.

### Page 4 - Additional Coverages

* **Additional Coverages** (`additional_coverages`, Optional): Choose any additional coverages you'd like to include.
* **Deductible Preference** (`deductible_preference`, Required): Choose the deductible amount for your coverage.
* **Quote Delivery Preference** (`quote_delivery`, Required): How you'd like your quote delivered.

### Page 5 - Quote Information

* **Current Insurance** (`current_insurance`, Optional): The current insurance provider.
* **Currently Insured** (`currently_insured`, Required): Whether you currently have insurance.
* **Coverage Lapse** (`coverage_lapse`, Required): Whether you've had any gaps in coverage.
* **Quote Request Date** (`quote_date`, Required): The date of your quote request.

## Tips

* Please fill the form accurately to ensure the best possible quote.
* If you have any further questions, please contact us.
* We will be in touch with you soon with your quote.
* Please note that this form is for car insurance quotation only, if you have any other insurance needs, please contact us separately.
