# Life Insurance Underwriting Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Insurance Underwriting Questionnaire is a form used to collect information for insurance policy underwriting purposes. This information is essential for the underwriting process, which involves assessing and evaluating the risks associated with insuring a policyholder.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policyholder Info** (`policyholder_info`): This is the first section of the form where you will enter your name and other identifying information.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your address where you would like to receive communication about your policy.
* **Policy Amount** (`policy_amount`): Enter the amount of the policy you are applying for.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`): Select how often you would like to make payments for your policy (One Time, Recurring, Quarterly, or Monthly).
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`): Select the type of policy you are applying for (Term, Whole, or Variable).
* **Policy Status** (`policy_status`): Select the status of your policy (Active, Pending, or Denied).
* **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`): Enter the start date of your policy.
* **Policy End Date** (`policy_end_date`): Enter the end date of your policy.
* **Payment Frequency 2** (`payment_frequency_2`): Select how often you would like to make payments for your policy (One Time, Recurring, Quarterly, or Monthly).
* **Payment Term** (`payment_term`): Enter the term of your payment schedule.
* **Premium Payment Type** (`premium_payment_type`): Select how you would like to make premium payments (One Time or Recurring).
* **Payment Frequency 3** (`payment_frequency_3`): Select how often you would like to make payments for your premium (One Time, Recurring, Quarterly, or Monthly).
* **Payment Term 2** (`payment_term_2`): Enter the term of your payment schedule for premium payments.
* **Premium Payment Term** (`premium_payment_term`): Enter the payment term for your premium payments.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select how you would like to make premium payments (Bank Transfer, Cash, or Credit Card).
* **Payee** (`payee`): Enter the name and contact information of the payee.
* **Premium Date** (`premium_date`): Enter the date of your premium payment.
* **Premium Amount** (`premium_amount`): Enter the amount of your premium payment.
* **Premium Frequency** (`premium_frequency`): Select how often you would like to make premium payments (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Premium Term** (`premium_term`): Enter the term of your premium payments.
* **Payee Address** (`payee_address`): Enter the address of the payee.
* **Payee Phone** (`payee_phone`): Enter the phone number of the payee.
* **Payee Email** (`payee_email`): Enter the email address of the payee.
* **Premium Status** (`premium_status`): Select the status of your premium payment (Paid, Pending, or Unpaid).
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your policy.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your insurance representative for assistance.
