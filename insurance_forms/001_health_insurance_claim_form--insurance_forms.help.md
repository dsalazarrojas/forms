# Health Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit claims for medical or non-medical services. Please select the correct category and provider to ensure accurate processing of your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your claim is processed correctly.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your insurance provider or claims administrator for guidance.
- Make sure to attach any required supporting documents or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the claim.
* **Claimant Address** (`claimant_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the person submitting the claim.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the claim.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address of the person submitting the claim.
* **Claimant Date of Birth** (`claimant_dob`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the person submitting the claim.
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`, select_one, optional): Select 'medical' for medical services or 'non-medical' for non-medical services.
* **Claimant Insurance Provider** (`claimant_insurance_provider`, select_one, optional): Select the insurance provider you are currently enrolled with.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the claim was submitted.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the claim (if known).
* **Claim Total Claim Cost** (`claim_total_claim_cost`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of the claim.
* **Claim Payment Method** (`claim_payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method used to pay for the claim (if applicable).

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields are complete before submitting the form.
- Make sure to keep your contact information up-to-date in case additional information is needed.
- If you are unsure or have questions, contact your insurance provider or claims administrator for assistance.
