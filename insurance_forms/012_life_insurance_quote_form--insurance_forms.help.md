# Life Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This life insurance quote form is designed to help you gather quotes for life insurance policies that suit your needs. By filling out this form, you'll be able to get a quick and accurate quote that takes into account your personal details, insurance preferences, and policy requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form and understand each field's purpose.
2.  Fill out the form carefully, ensuring you have all the necessary information and documents.
3.  If you're unsure about any field, it's best to seek clarification before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Information** (`form_info`, `text`, required: false): This is the heading that appears at the top of the form.
*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like it to appear on your policy documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`dob`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your mailing address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
*   **Premium Amount** (`premium_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount you would like to pay for your policy.
*   **Policy Term** (`policy_term`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the term of your policy. Options:
    *   10 years
    *   20 years
    *   30 years
*   **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of policy you need.
    *   Whole Life
    *   Term Life
    *   Universal Life
*   **Smoker** (`smoker`, `select_one`, required: false): Are you a smoker?
    *   True
    *   False
*   **Beneficiary** (`beneficiary`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose who you would like to be your beneficiary.
    *   Father
    *   Mother
    *   Spouse
    *   Friend
    *   Family Member
*   **Coverage** (`coverage`, `number`, required: false): Enter the coverage amount you would like to have.
*   **Rider** (`rider`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose which riders you would like to include.
    *   Rider 1
    *   Rider 2
    *   Rider 3
    *   Rider 4
    *   Rider 5
*   **Policy Cost** (`policy_cost`, `number`, required: false): Enter the policy cost you would like to pay.
*   **Premium Frequency** (`premium_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how often you would like to pay your premiums.
    *   Monthly
    *   Quarterly
    *   Annually
*   **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the effective date of your policy.
*   **Payment Term** (`payment_term`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the payment term for your policy.
    *   5 years
    *   10 years
    *   15 years
    *   20 years
*   **Payment Plan** (`payment_plan`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you would like to pay your premiums.
    *   Single Payment
    *   Monthly Payment Plan
    *   Quarterly Payment Plan
*   **Tax ID** (`tax_id`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose which type of tax ID you have.
    *   SSN
    *   EIN
    *   ITIN
    *   Passport
*   **Driver License** (`driver_license`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose which type of driver license you have.
    *   Driver License
    *   Non-Resident Alien
    *   Not a Citizen
*   **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the status of your policy.
    *   Active
    *   Inactive
    *   Lapsed
*   **Policy Notes** (`policy_notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional policy notes.
*   **Additional Policy Notes** (`policy_notes_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional policy notes.
*   **Policy Notes 3** (`policy_notes_3`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional policy notes.
*   **Policy Notes 4** (`policy_notes_4`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional policy notes.

## Tips

*   Be sure to double-check each field for accuracy before submitting.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
*   This form is not intended to be a comprehensive guide to life insurance, but rather a tool to get you a basic quote. For a more detailed explanation of life insurance, please refer to a professional.
*   Please be prepared for a quote that may not meet your exact requirements. This form is for basic quotes only.
