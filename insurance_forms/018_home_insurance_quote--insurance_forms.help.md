# Home Insurance Quote - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information required for a home insurance quote. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to provide the best possible quote for your home insurance needs.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting this form, please make sure you have all necessary documents and information ready. This form will guide you through the required fields to gather your home insurance information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Insurance Quote** (`1`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the type of insurance quote you are looking for.
* **Policy Type** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional: false): Choose the type of policy you are interested in:
	+ Homeowners
	+ Renters
	+ Landlords
* **Address** (`3`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your current address.
* **City** (`4`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the city where your address is located.
* **State** (`5`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the state where your address is located.
* **Zip Code** (`6`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the zip code of your address.
* **Policy Deductible** (`7`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the policy deductible amount you are willing to pay.
* **Coverage Amount** (`8`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the coverage amount you need for your home.
* **Contact Info** (`9`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your contact information.
* **Policy Start Date** (`10`, `date`, required/optional: false): Enter the start date of your policy.
* **Policy End Date** (`11`, `date`, required/optional: false): Enter the end date of your policy.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our insurance representative.
* This form is for informational purposes only, and any submitted data will not be stored or used for any other purpose than for insurance quote purposes.
