# Medical Premium Calculation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Premium Calculation Form is designed to collect information from applicants for medical premium calculations. This form helps to gather necessary details to estimate and calculate premiums accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form sections sequentially**: Complete each section one by one, starting from "Applicant Details" to "Additional Info".
2. **Provide accurate information**: Enter your actual details and facts for each field.
3. **Review and correct**: Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Applicant Details**
• **Applicant Details** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant details as prompted. This section is used to identify the applicant.

### **Dependents**
• **Dependents** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): List any dependents you have, including their names, ages, and relationships (e.g., spouse, children, parents). This helps us understand your family situation.

### **Coverage Preferences**
• **Coverage Preferences** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Indicate your preferred coverage options, such as coverage type (e.g., individual, family), coverage range (e.g., single, multi-year), or any other relevant details. This section helps us estimate your premium accurately.

### **Premium Estimation**
• **Premium Estimation** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Leave this field blank as it is not meant to be filled out. This field is not meant for input.

### **Additional Info**
• **Additional Info** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any relevant information not covered in the previous sections. This is an open-ended field for you to share any additional details that might help with the premium calculation.
