# Life Insurance Policy Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from policyholders on their experience with their life insurance policy. It aims to collect information about their policy details, satisfaction, and ratings for various aspects of their policy and the issuer. The data collected will help us improve our services and policies to better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your policy number
2. Provide information about your policy provider
3. Select the type of policy you have
4. Enter your policy purchase date and confirm it
5. Choose the type of policy benefits
6. Select the policy's deductible
7. Enter the policy premium
8. Rate your policy experience
9. Provide any additional comments or comments about the issuer
10. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Policy Number**: Enter the number of your policy, which can be found on your policy documents.
* **Policy Provider**: Provide the name of the company that issued your policy.
* **Policy Type**: Select the type of policy you have, which can be Whole life, Term life, Universal life, or Variable life.
* **Policy Status**: Enter the current status of your policy, if it's not applicable, simply enter "N/A".
* **When did you purchase the policy?**: Enter the date you purchased the policy.
* **When was the policy purchased?** (Select one of the options): Confirm if you purchased the policy or not.
* **Policy Purchase Date Range**: Enter the range of dates when you purchased the policy, if not applicable, simply leave blank.
* **Who issued the policy?**: Enter the name of the company that issued your policy.
* **Policy Issuer 2**: Select if you had multiple issuers for your policy.
* **What is the policy's benefits?**: Enter the benefits of your policy.
* **What are the policy's benefits?** (Select multiple options): Confirm the benefits of your policy.
* **What is the policy's deductible?**: Enter the deductible of your policy.
* **What is the deductible?** (Select one of the options): Confirm the deductible of your policy.
* **How much is the policy's premium?**: Enter the premium of your policy.
* **Policy Premium 2**: Enter any additional comments about the premium.
* **How would you rate the policy?**: Rate your satisfaction with the policy on a scale of 1-5.
* **Policy Purchaser Rating 2**: Rate your satisfaction with the policy on a scale of Very satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
* **Why did you choose this policy?**: Enter any additional comments about why you chose this policy.
* **Policy Purchaser Comment 2**: Enter any additional comments about your policy purchasing experience.
* **How would you rate the issuer?**: Rate the issuer's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Policy Issuer Rating 2**: Rate the issuer's performance as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Policy Issuer Comment**: Enter any comments about your experience with the issuer.
* **Policy Issuer Comment 2**: Enter any additional comments about your experience with the issuer.
* **How satisfied are you with the policy?**: Enter any comments about your satisfaction with the policy.
* **Policy Purchaser Satisfaction 2**: Enter any comments about your satisfaction with the policy.

Note: All required fields are marked as "required: true" in the form schema. Please ensure you complete all required fields before submitting the form.
