# Auto Insurance Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This auto insurance declaration form is used to declare and confirm coverage limits for an individual's auto insurance policy. It gathers information about the policy owner, their vehicles, and any additional coverage options they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each page carefully and thoroughly. Ensure that you have access to your policy information before starting.
2. Enter your primary insured name and policy number in the respective fields.
3. Select the effective and expiration dates for your policy.
4. Enter your coverage limits for bodily injury, property damage, comprehensive, and collision claims.
5. Provide details on your vehicles, including year, make, model, and VIN.
6. Indicate whether roadside assistance and rental reimbursement are included in your policy.
7. List any additional drivers on your policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Primary Insured Name** (Primary Insured Name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your policy.
* **Policy Number** (Policy Number, text, required): Enter your active insurance policy ID.
* **Policy Effective Date** (Policy Effective Date, date, required): Enter the date when your coverage began.
* **Policy Expiration Date** (Policy Expiration Date, date, required): Enter the date when your current term ends.
* **Bodily Injury Limit per Person** (Bodily Injury Limit per Person, number, required): Maximum amount per injured person.
* **Bodily Injury Limit per Accident** (Bodily Injury Limit per Accident, number, required): Maximum amount for all injuries in one incident.
* **Property Damage Limit** (Property Damage Limit, number, required): Maximum coverage for damage to others property.
* **Comprehensive Deductible** (Comprehensive Deductible, number, required): Your out-of-pocket for non-collision claims.
* **Collision Deductible** (Collision Deductible, number, required): Your out-of-pocket for collision claims.
* **Vehicle 1 Details** (Vehicle 1 Details, text, required): Year, Make, Model, and VIN of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle 2 Details** (Vehicle 2 Details, text, optional): Leave blank if not applicable; otherwise, enter year, make, model, and VIN of your second vehicle.
* **Roadside Assistance Included** (Roadside Assistance Included, select_one, required): Check 'True' if roadside assistance is covered; 'False' otherwise.
* **Rental Reimbursement Included** (Rental Reimbursement Included, select_one, required): Check 'True' if a rental car is covered during repairs; 'False' otherwise.
* **Additional Drivers on Policy** (Additional Drivers on Policy, text, optional): List any other authorized drivers on your policy.
