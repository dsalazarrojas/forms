# Commercial Building Insurance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your commercial building to provide you with an accurate insurance quote. Please fill out this form carefully, providing as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all fields are filled in as required.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting to ensure you have provided all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant Name** (1): Enter the full legal name or business name of the applicant or policy holder.
- **Applicant Type** (2): Select the type of applicant, e.g., Individual, Corporation, LLC, Partnership, or Non-Profit.
- **Email Address** (3): Enter the primary contact email of the applicant or policy holder.
- **Phone Number** (4): Enter the contact number of the applicant or policy holder.
- **Property Information** (5): This section serves as a header for the next set of fields related to property details.
- **Property Address** (6): Enter the complete address of the commercial building being insured.
- **Property Type** (7): Select the type of commercial building, e.g., Office Building, Retail Store, Warehouse, or Hotel.
- **Year Built** (8): Enter the year the building was constructed.
- **Total Square Footage** (9): Enter the total square footage of the building.
- **Number of Floors** (10): Enter the number of floors in the building.
- **Construction Type** (11): Select the building's construction type, e.g., Wood Frame, Steel Frame, Concrete, Masonry, or Mixed.
- **Coverage Details** (12): This section serves as a header for the next set of fields related to coverage requirements.
- **Building Value** (13): Enter the estimated replacement value of the building.
- **Contents Value** (14): Enter the value of the building's contents.
- **Desired Coverage Amount** (15): Enter the total coverage amount requested.
- **Coverage Type** (16): Select the type of coverage needed, e.g., Basic, Broad, Special, or All Risk.
- **Additional Coverages** (17): Choose any additional coverages you would like to include, e.g., Business Interruption, Equipment Breakdown, Flood, or Cyber Liability.
- **Claims History** (18): This section serves as a header for the next set of fields related to claims history.
- **Previous Claims** (19): Indicate if you have had previous insurance claims.
- **Claims Details** (20): If you have had previous claims, describe them in this field.
- **Safety Features** (21): This section serves as a header for the next set of fields related to safety features.
- **Fire Protection** (22): Select any fire protection systems installed, e.g., Smoke Detectors, Fire Alarms, or Sprinkler System.
- **Security System** (23): Choose any security features installed, e.g., Alarm System, Security Cameras, or Security Guards.
- **Business Information** (24): This section serves as a header for the next set of fields related to business details.
- **Business Type** (25): Enter the type of business conducted, e.g., Retail, Service-based, or Manufacturing.
- **Years in Business** (26): Select how long the business has been in operation.
- **Number of Employees** (27): Enter the total number of employees at the location.
- **Payment Information** (28): This section serves as a header for the next set of fields related to payment details.
- **Payment Frequency** (29): Select the preferred payment schedule, e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual, or Annually.
- **Payment Method** (30): Choose the method of payment, e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check.
- **Additional Comments** (31): Enter any additional information or notes about your application.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
