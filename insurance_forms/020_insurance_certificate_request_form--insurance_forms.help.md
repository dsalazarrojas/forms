# Insurance Certificate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Certificate Request Form is designed to collect information from insurance companies to process and verify the insurance certificates of their clients. This form is intended for insurance company representatives to fill out the form with their client's policy details and request a certificate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the Policy Number of your client's insurance policy.
2. Enter the full name of your insurance company or agency.
3. Select the type of insurance certificate you are requesting (Full or Partial).
4. Fill in the start date of the insurance policy.
5. Fill in the end date of the insurance policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, required: false): Enter the Policy Number of your client's insurance policy.
* **Name Of Insurer** (`name_of_insurer`, text, required: false): Enter the full name of your insurance company or agency.
* **Certificate Type** (`certificate_type`, select_one, options: Full, Partial): Select the type of insurance certificate you are requesting (Full or Partial).
* **Date Of Insurance Start** (`date_of_insurance_start`, date, required: false): Fill in the start date of the insurance policy.
* **Date Of Insurance End** (`date_of_insurance_end`, date, required: false): Fill in the end date of the insurance policy.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure efficient processing of your insurance certificate request.
