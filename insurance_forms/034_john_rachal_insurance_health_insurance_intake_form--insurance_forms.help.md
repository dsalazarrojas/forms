# john_rachal_insurance_health_insurance_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The John Rachal Insurance Health Insurance Intake Form is a document used to collect information about a person's health insurance coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Review the form carefully and answer all questions accurately.
* Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
* Enter your birth date in the "birth date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* Enter your address in the "address" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "phone number" field.
* Enter your email address in the "email" field.
* Indicate whether or not you have health insurance in the "insurance type" field.
* If applicable, enter your policy number in the "policy number" field.
* Enter the effective date of your policy in the "effective date" field, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* Enter the expiration date of your policy in the "expiration date" field, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`first name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
- **Last name** (`last name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
- **Birth date** (`birth date`, `date`, required: false): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your physical address.
- **Phone number** (`phone number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Insurance type** (`insurance type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" if you have health insurance, "No" otherwise.
- **Policy number** (`policy number`, `text`, required: false): If you have health insurance, enter your policy number.
- **Effective date** (`effective date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the effective date of your policy in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Expiration date** (`expiration date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the expiration date of your policy in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
