# Insurance Benefits Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information about an insurance policy, including patient details and policy-related information. This form is used to ensure that patient information is accurate and up-to-date, and that payment and administrative details are correctly recorded.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2.  Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
3.  Enter the patient's date of birth in the "patient_dob" field (in the format mm/dd/yyyy).
4.  Select the insurance provider from the dropdown menu in the "insurance_provider" field.
5.  Enter the policy number in the "policy_number" field.
6.  Enter the policy's effective date in the "policy_effective_date" field.
7.  Enter the policy's expiration date in the "policy_expiration_date" field.
8.  Select the policy's status (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled) in the "policy_status" field.
9.  Enter the policy cancellation date in the "policy_cancellation_date" field, if applicable.
10. Enter your signature in the "patient_signature" field.
11. Enter the insurance provider's signature in the "insurance_provider_signature" field.
12. Enter the insurance administration fee in the "insurance_administration_fee" field.
13. Enter the policy administration fee in the "policy_administration_fee" field.
14. Select the payment method (Cash, Credit Card, or Check) in the "payment_method" field.
15. Enter the payment date in the "payment_date" field.
16. Select the payment status (Paid, Unpaid, or Partial) in the "payment_status" field.
17. Enter any additional policy notes in the "policy_notes" field.
18. Enter the patient's address in the "patient_address" field.
19. Enter the policy address in the "policy_address" field.
20. Enter the policy contact number in the "policy_contact_number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **insurance_benefits_consent_form_label**: This field is the title of the form.
* **patient_name**: Enter the patient's name, as recorded on their identification or medical records.
* **patient_dob**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **insurance_provider**: Select the insurance provider from the provided dropdown menu.
* **policy_number**: Enter the policy number assigned to the patient's insurance policy.
* **policy_effective_date**: Enter the date the policy became effective.
* **policy_expiration_date**: Enter the date the policy expires.
* **policy_cancellation_date**: Enter the date the policy was cancelled, if applicable.
* **policy_status**: Select the policy status (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled).
* **patient_signature**: Enter your signature as the patient or authorized representative.
* **insurance_provider_signature**: Enter the signature of the insurance provider or authorized representative.
* **insurance_administration_fee**: Enter the fee charged by the insurance provider for administration services.
* **policy_administration_fee**: Enter the fee charged by the policyholder for administration services.
* **payment_method**: Select the method of payment (Cash, Credit Card, or Check).
* **payment_date**: Enter the date the payment was made.
* **payment_status**: Select the payment status (Paid, Unpaid, or Partial).
* **policy_notes**: Enter any additional notes about the policy.
* **patient_address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **policy_address**: Enter the policyholder's address.
* **policy_contact_number**: Enter the policyholder's contact number.

Note: Some fields may have specific formatting or validation rules. Please ensure that you enter information accurately and according to the field's requirements. If you are unsure, please ask for clarification or guidance.

Tips:

*   Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
*   Double-check for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please reach out to your supervisor or the form's creator for assistance.
