# Life Insurance Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Insurance Lead Generation Form is a tool used to collect information from potential customers who are interested in purchasing life insurance policies. The form helps insurance agents to understand the customer's needs and provide quotes and follow up on the leads.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For fields with a dropdown menu, select the option that best describes your information.
3. For fields with a date picker, select the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. For fields that require a signature, please print or write your name clearly.
5. Please provide your email address and phone number for the insurance agent to contact you.
6. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_first_name`, `text`, required): This field is for the customer's first name.
* **Customer Last Name** (`customer_last_name`, `text`, required): This field is for the customer's last name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): This field is for the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): This field is for the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Street** (`customer_street`, `text`, required): This field is for the customer's street address.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, `text`, required): This field is for the customer's city.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, `text`, required): This field is for the customer's state.
* **Customer Zip** (`customer_zip`, `text`, required): This field is for the customer's zip code.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, `note`, optional): This field is for any additional notes about the customer.
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, `select_one`, required): This field is to select the type of policy you are interested in (e.g. Term Life Insurance, Whole Life Insurance, etc.).
* **Coverage Amount** (`coverage_amount`, `number`, required): This field is to enter the coverage amount you are interested in.
* **Policy Term** (`policy_term`, `number`, optional): This field is to enter the policy term in years.
* **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, `date`, required): This field is to select the date you would like to start the policy.
* **Policy End Date** (`policy_end_date`, `date`, required): This field is to select the date you would like to end the policy.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required): This field is to select how you would like to be contacted (e.g. Email, Phone, etc.).
* **Customer Agreement** (`customer_agreement`, `select_one`, required): This field is to agree or disagree with the terms and conditions of the policy.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, `text`, required): This field is for the customer's signature.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, `note`, optional): This field is for the insurance agent to note when the form was submitted.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, `text`, optional): This field is for the insurance agent to note who submitted the form.
* **Insurance Agency** (`insurance_agency`, `text`, optional): This field is for the insurance agent's name.
* **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, `date`, optional): This field is not actually required, but it is used by the insurance agent to note the start date of the policy.
* **Policy End Date** (`policy_end_date`, `date`, optional): This field is not actually required, but it is used by the insurance agent to note the end date of the policy.
