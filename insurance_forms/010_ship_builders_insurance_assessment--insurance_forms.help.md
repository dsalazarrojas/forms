# Ship Builders Insurance Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from ship builders for insurance assessment purposes. It helps insurers assess the risks associated with ship building and determine the appropriate coverage options for the shipyard.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the requested information in each field. Most fields are optional, but some may be required depending on the specific circumstances. Please ensure that you provide accurate and clear information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: A field for entering any relevant information about the user or shipyard.
* **Shipyard Details**: A field for entering details about the shipyard, such as its location or size.
* **Contract Details**: A field for entering any relevant contract information, such as contract dates or amounts.
* **Operational Risks**: A field for listing any operational risks associated with the shipyard, such as accidents or equipment malfunctions.
* **Risk Management**: A field for describing how operational risks are managed within the shipyard.
* **Claims History**: A field for listing any past claims made against the shipyard.
* **Coverage Options**: A field for selecting the desired coverage options for the shipyard.
* **Coverage Details**: A field for entering any relevant details about the selected coverage options.
* **Notes**: A field for adding any additional notes or comments about the shipyard.
* **Submit**: A button to submit the completed form for assessment.
