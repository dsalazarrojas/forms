# Contact Lens Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help patients and claimants submit claims for contact lens coverage through their insurance provider. It collects necessary information to verify the claim, including patient details, insurance policy information, and prescription details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient's first name, last name, and ID number.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth.
3. Provide the insurance policy number and group number (if applicable).
4. Enter the insurance provider's name and the prescribing doctor's name.
5. Enter the National Provider Identifier (NPI) number of the prescribing doctor.
6. Enter the name of the clinic or practice where the lenses were prescribed.
7. Enter the date the lenses were prescribed.
8. Enter the primary diagnosis code and description.
9. Select the type of contact lenses used (e.g., Soft Spherical, Soft Toric, etc.).
10. Select the medical necessity for the lenses (e.g., Keratoconus, Post-Surgical, etc.).
11. Enter the lens prescription details (e.g., power, base, etc.).
12. Enter the number of lenses used.
13. Enter the price per lens.
14. Calculate the total claim amount.
15. If applicable, confirm if you have previously claimed contact lenses in the past 12 months.
16. Confirm if all supporting documents (e.g., receipts, prescriptions) are attached.
17. Enter your name as the person submitting the claim.
18. Select your relationship to the patient (e.g., self, parent, etc.).
19. Sign and date the form digitally.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient First Name**: Enter your first name as recorded in your membership records.
* **Patient Last Name**: Enter your last name as recorded in your membership records.
* **Patient ID Number**: Enter your member ID as recorded in your membership records.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter your insurance policy number as recorded in your membership records.
* **Group Number**: Enter your group number (if you're covered under a group policy).
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Prescribing Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the eye care provider who prescribed the lenses.
* **Provider NPI Number**: Enter the National Provider Identifier (NPI) number of the prescribing doctor.
* **Clinic or Practice Name**: Enter the name of the clinic or practice where the lenses were prescribed.
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date the lenses were prescribed.
* **Diagnosis Code - ICD-10**: Enter the primary diagnosis code as recorded in your medical records.
* **Diagnosis Description**: Enter a brief description of the condition being treated.
* **Type of Contact Lenses**: Select the type of contact lenses used (e.g., Soft Spherical, Soft Toric, etc.).
* **Medical Necessity**: Select the reason why the contact lenses are medically necessary (e.g., Keratoconus, Post-Surgical, etc.).
* **Lens Parameters**: Enter the prescription details for the lenses (e.g., power, base, etc.).
* **Quantity of Lenses**: Enter the number of lenses used.
* **Price Per Lens**: Enter the cost per lens.
* **Total Claim Amount**: Calculate the total claim amount based on the quantity of lenses and price per lens.
* **Have You Previously Claimed Contact Lenses**: Confirm if you have previously claimed contact lenses in the past 12 months.
* **Supporting Documents Attached**: Confirm if all supporting documents are attached (e.g., receipts, prescriptions).
* **Claimant Name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the claim.
* **Relationship to Patient**: Select your relationship to the patient (e.g., self, parent, etc.).
* **Signature**: Sign the form digitally to confirm submission.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date the form was signed.
