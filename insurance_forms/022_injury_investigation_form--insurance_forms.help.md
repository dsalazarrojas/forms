# Injury Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Injury Investigation Form is a crucial document used to investigate and record incidents that occurred within an organization. It helps to gather all necessary information to provide a clear and accurate account of the incident.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Injury Investigation Form, follow these steps:

1. **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company involved in the incident.
2. **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who was involved in the incident.
3. (Optional) **Date of Incident**: Enter the date of the incident, if known. This is a required field if you're investigating a historical incident.
4. (Optional) **Time of Incident**: Enter the time of the incident, if known. This is a required field if you're investigating a historical incident.
5. **Description of Incident**: Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any witness statements or observations.
6. **Employee ID**: Enter the employee ID number of the employee involved in the incident.
7. **Investigation Status**: Select the current status of the investigation from the options provided.
8. (Optional) **Investigation Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments related to the investigation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Employer Name, text, required):
	+ The name of the company where the incident occurred.
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required):
	+ The name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Date of Incident** (Date of Incident, date, optional):
	+ The date the incident occurred. If known, this field is required for historical incidents.
* **Time of Incident** (Time of Incident, time, optional):
	+ The time the incident occurred. If known, this field is required for historical incidents.
* **Location of Incident** (Location of Incident, text, optional):
	+ The location where the incident occurred.
* **Description of Incident** (Description of Incident, text, required):
	+ A detailed description of the incident, including any witness statements or observations.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, number, required):
	+ The employee ID number of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Witness Statement** (Witness Statement, text, optional):
	+ Enter any witness statements or observations related to the incident.
* **Investigation Status** (Investigation Status, select_one, required):
	+ Select the current status of the investigation from the options provided.
* **Investigation Notes** (Investigation Notes, note, optional):
	+ Provide any additional notes or comments related to the investigation.
