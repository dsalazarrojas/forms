# Specialty Products Insurance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Specialty Products Insurance Form is designed to collect information about specialty products, including their type, value, coverage details, and relevant policy holder information. This form helps users provide accurate and complete data for insurance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant product type from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the product value, if applicable.
3. Provide the coverage start date, if applicable.
4. Enter the coverage end date or text description, if applicable.
5. Specify the coverage term, if applicable.
6. Enter the coverage start time, if applicable.
7. Add a brief description of the product.
8. Choose the coverage status (Active or Inactive).
9. Enter the policy holder's name.
10. Enter the policy holder's email address.
11. Enter the policy holder's phone number.
12. Enter the policy holder's address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Type** (`product_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of specialty product you wish to insure. This field is a dropdown menu where you can choose from the available options.
* **Product Value** (`product_value`, `number`, required: false): If you wish to specify the value of the product, please enter it here. Note that this field is not required.
* **Coverage Start Date** (`coverage_start_date`, `date`, required: false): If applicable, enter the start date of the coverage. If not, please leave this field blank.
* **Coverage End Date** (`coverage_end_date`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, enter the end date of the coverage. If not, please leave this field blank.
* **Coverage Term** (`coverage_term`, `number`, required: false): If applicable, enter the term of the coverage. If not, please leave this field blank.
* **Coverage Start Time** (`coverage_start_time`, `time`, required: false): If applicable, enter the start time of the coverage. If not, please leave this field blank.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the product.
* **Coverage Status** (`coverage_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of the coverage (Active or Inactive).
* **Policy Holder Name** (`policy_holder_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Email** (`policy_holder_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Phone** (`policy_holder_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Address** (`policy_holder_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the policy holder.
