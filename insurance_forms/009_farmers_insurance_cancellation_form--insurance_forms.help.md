# Farmers Insurance Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from policyholders for policy cancellation.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the policyholder's name.
2.  Enter the policyholder's email address.
3.  Select the reason for cancellation.
4.  Provide the policy number.
5.  Specify the cancellation date.
6.  Enter any additional reason for cancellation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cancellation Reason**: This field allows you to specify the reason for cancelling the policy. Choose from the provided options or type in a custom reason.
* **Policyholder Name**: Enter the full name of the policyholder, as it appears on the policy.
* **Policyholder Email**: Enter the email address associated with the policyholder's account.
* **Agent Name**: Enter the name of the agent or representative assisting with the cancellation.
* **Reason Code**: Choose the reason code for the policy cancellation. This may include options like CANCELLATION, EXPIRED, INACTIVE, or OTHER.
* **Policy Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the policy being cancelled.
* **Cancellation Date**: Specify the date on which the policy will be cancelled.
* **Reason for Cancellation**: Provide a detailed explanation for the cancellation, if necessary.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in for the form to be processed correctly.
* If you are unable to cancel the policy online, please contact your agent or Farmers Insurance representative for assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
