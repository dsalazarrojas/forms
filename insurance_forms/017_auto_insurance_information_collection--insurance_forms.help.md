# Auto Insurance Information Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your auto insurance policy. Please complete it accurately to ensure smooth processing and avoid any potential issues.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the "Primary Driver Name," "Date of Birth," and "Driver's License Number" sections with your relevant information.
2.  Enter your vehicle's "Vehicle Year," "Vehicle Make," "Vehicle Model," and "VIN Number."
3.  Estimate the "Annual Mileage" for your vehicle.
4.  Choose the "Primary Vehicle Use" from the provided options.
5.  If applicable, provide your current insurance details, such as "Current Insurance Carrier" and "Current Policy Expiration."
6.  Select any safety features that apply for potential discounts.
7.  Finally, report any "Number of Accidents" or "Number of Tickets" you've had in the last 3 years.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Primary Driver Name** (Required)
The full name of the primary driver of the vehicle.

### **Date of Birth** (Required)
The date of birth of the primary driver in the MM-DD-YYYY format.

### **Driver's License Number** (Required)
The number on your driver's license as it appears.

### **Vehicle Year** (Required)
The year the vehicle was manufactured.

### **Vehicle Make** (Required)
The make of your vehicle, e.g., Honda.

### **Vehicle Model** (Required)
The model of your vehicle, e.g., Civic.

### **VIN Number** (Required)
A unique 17-digit identification number for your vehicle.

### **Annual Mileage Estimate** (Required)
An estimate of how many miles you drive per year.

### **Primary Vehicle Use** (Required)
Select the main purpose of your vehicle from the provided options.

### **Current Insurance Carrier** (Optional)
If you have current insurance, enter the name of your existing company.

### **Current Policy Expiration** (Optional)
Enter the date your current policy expires.

### **Safety Features** (Optional)
Select all safety features that apply for potential discounts.

### **Number of Accidents** (Last 3 Years) (Required)
Report any total accidents you've had in the last 3 years.

### **Number of Tickets** (Last 3 Years) (Required)
Report any moving traffic violations you've received in the last 3 years.

## Tips
- Double-check all required fields for accuracy and completeness.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant authority or seek clarification.
- Ensure you have all necessary information before completing this form.
