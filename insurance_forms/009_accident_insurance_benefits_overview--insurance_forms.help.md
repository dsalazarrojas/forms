# Accident Insurance Benefits Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect employee information and benefits details for the accident insurance benefits process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Employee Full Name**: Type your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Enter your Employee ID**: Provide your employee ID number as assigned by the organization.
3. **Enter your Email Address**: Type your official email address used for internal communication.
4. **Select your Date of Birth**: Format the date as MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year).
5. **Select your Department**: Choose the department you belong to.
6. **Select your Employment Status**: Choose your employment status from the options provided.
7. **Choose your Current Accident Insurance Plan**: Select the plan you currently hold or choose "Not currently enrolled."
8. **Choose your Coverage Level**: Select the level of coverage you have or want to have.
9. **Enter the Date of Enrollment**: Format the date as MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year), if applicable.
10. **Acknowledge your benefits review**: Select the option that best describes your understanding of the benefits.
11. **Indicate if you have reviewed the benefits summary**: Select the option to confirm your understanding.
12. **List any Questions or Concerns**: Type any specific questions or concerns you have about your benefits.
13. **Choose to make changes to your coverage**: Select the options to upgrade, downgrade, or maintain your coverage.
14. **Enter the name of your beneficiary**: Provide the name of the person you have designated as beneficiary.
15. **Select the beneficiary relationship**: Choose the relationship with your beneficiary.
16. **Enter the Date of Acknowledgement**: Format the date as MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee ID**: Your employee ID number as assigned by the organization.
* **Email Address**: Your official email address used for internal communication.
* **Date of Birth**: Format the date as MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year).
* **Department**: The department you belong to.
* **Employment Status**: Your current employment status.
* **Current Accident Insurance Plan**: The plan you currently hold or are interested in.
* **Coverage Level**: The level of coverage you have or want to have.
* **Date of Enrollment**: Format the date as MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year), if applicable.
* **Benefits Reviewed**: Confirm your understanding of the benefits summary.
* **Benefits Understood**: Indicate your level of understanding of the benefits, coverage, and claim process.
* **Questions or Concerns**: List any specific questions or concerns you have about your benefits.
* **Coverage Changes**: Indicate if you want to make changes to your coverage.
* **Primary Beneficiary Name**: Provide the name of the person you have designated as beneficiary.
* **Beneficiary Relationship**: Choose the relationship with your beneficiary.
* **Date of Acknowledgement**: Format the date as MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year).

Note: Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately to avoid any errors or delays in the benefits process.
