# Claim Adjuster Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Claim Adjuster Check In Form is designed to capture essential information from claim adjusters during site visits. This form helps document the adjuster's findings, ensures data accuracy, and aids in communication with clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Adjuster name** field with your full name.
2. Enter the **Claim number** field with the internal claim identifier provided by your organization.
3. Select the **Visit date** field by clicking on the calendar icon or typing in the date of the site visit.
4. Type in the **Client name** field with the policyholder's name.
5. Choose the **Purpose of visit** field by selecting an option that best describes the reason for the visit.
6. Provide a **Brief summary** of your observations and findings in the **Observations summary** field.
7. If applicable, upload **Photo attachment instructions** for the site visit.
8. Describe the **Recommended next steps** to be taken in the case.
9. If desired, type in the **Client acknowledgement signature** with your typed name.
10. Ensure to update the **Submission time** with the current date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Adjuster name** (Full name, Required): Enter your full name as the claim adjuster.
* **Claim number** (Internal claim identifier, Required): Enter the claim number provided by your organization.
* **Visit date** (Date of site visit, Required): Select the date of the site visit using the calendar icon or type in the date manually.
* **Client name** (Policyholder name, Required): Type in the policyholder's name for accurate identification.
* **Purpose of visit** (Assessment, interview, etc., Required): Select an option that describes the reason for the visit from the provided options.
* **Observations summary** (Brief findings and conditions, Optional): Briefly describe your observations and findings from the site visit.
* **Photo attachment instructions** (Upload photos through portal, Optional): Follow the upload instructions for photo attachment.
* **Recommended next steps** (Further inspections, estimates, approvals, Optional): Describe the next steps to be taken in the case.
* **Client acknowledgement signature** (Typed name accepted, Optional): If desired, type in your name for client acknowledgement.
* **Submission time** (Use current date and time, Required): Ensure to update the submission time with the current date and time.
