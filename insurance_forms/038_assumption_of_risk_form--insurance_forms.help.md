# Assumption Of Risk Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an individual's health and liability status before participating in activities such as sports, adventure activities, travel, events, recreation, and training programs. It is essential to complete this form to confirm that you understand the risks involved and to ensure that you have the necessary medical clearance and insurance coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details such as first name, last name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Select the type of activity you are participating in.
3. Provide a detailed description of the activity and the date it will take place.
4. If applicable, disclose any known health conditions, medications, or allergies.
5. Confirm your physical capabilities and any limitations.
6. Confirm that you have discussed this activity with your physician.
7. Acknowledge the risks involved in the activity.
8. Waive liability claims against the activity organizers.
9. Confirm your insurance coverage.
10. Provide emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Fill in your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Fill in your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (3, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Are You 18 Years or Older** (4, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you are 18 years or older, 'No' otherwise.
* **Email Address** (5, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (6, text, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Type of Activity** (7, select_one, required): Select the type of activity you are participating in, such as Sports, Adventure Activity, or Travel.
* **Specific Activity Description** (8, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the activity, including any necessary details.
* **Activity Date** (9, date, required): Enter the date of the activity.
* **Activity Location** (10, text, required): Enter the location where the activity will take place.
* **Organizer or Facility Name** (11, text, required): Enter the name of the activity organizer or facility.
* **Known Health Conditions or Injuries** (12, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, enter them here. This information will help us tailor the activity to suit your needs.
* **Current Medications** (13, text, optional): If you are taking any medications, list them here.
* **Allergies** (14, text, optional): If you have any allergies, list them here.
* **Are You Pregnant** (15, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are pregnant, 'No' otherwise.
* **Do You Have Physical Limitations** (16, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you have any physical limitations, 'No' if you do not.
* **Limitation Details** (17, text, optional): If you have any physical limitations, describe them here.
* **Have You Discussed This Activity with Your Physician** (18, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you have discussed this activity with your physician, 'No' otherwise.
* **I Acknowledge the Risks Involved in this Activity** (19, select_one, required): Select 'I Acknowledge' to confirm that you understand the risks involved.
* **I Waive Liability Claims against Organizers** (20, select_one, required): Select 'I Agree to Waive' to confirm that you release liability claims against the activity organizers.
* **Do You Have Personal Liability or Health Insurance** (21, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you have personal liability or health insurance, 'No' otherwise.
* **Insurance Provider and Policy Number** (22, text, optional): If you have insurance, enter your insurance provider and policy number here.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (23, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (24, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Form Completion Date** (25, date, required): Enter the date this form was completed.
