# Last Will And Testament Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information necessary for creating a Last Will and Testament. Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the personal details on page 1.
2. On page 2, indicate if you have beneficiaries, and if so, list them.
3. On page 3-6, fill in information about your estate, assets, liabilities, debts, and insurance policies as required.
4. Review and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Details (Page 1)
* **Personal details (personal_details)** (text, required/optional): This is your name, address, and contact information.

### Beneficiaries (Page 2)
* **Beneficiaries (beneficiaries)** (select_multiple, required): If you have beneficiaries, select "Yes" and list their names and relationships.

### Executor (Page 3)
* **Executor (executor)** (text, required/optional): This is the person or organization you appoint to manage your estate after your death.

### Estate (Page 4)
* **Estate (estate)** (text, required/optional): Describe your estate, including property, assets, and other relevant information.

### Property (Page 5)
* **Property (property)** (text, required/optional): List your real property, such as land and buildings.

### Financial (Page 6)
* **Financial (financial)** (text, required/optional): Provide information about your financial situation, including income, expenses, and investments.

### Assets (Page 7)
* **Assets (assets)** (text, required/optional): List all your assets, including cash, investments, and personal property.

### Liabilities (Page 8)
* **Liabilities (liabilities)** (text, required/optional): List all your outstanding liabilities, including debts and other financial obligations.

### Debts (Page 9)
* **Debts (debts)** (text, required/optional): List any outstanding debts, including personal debts and debts in dispute.

### Insurance (Page 10)
* **Insurance (insurance)** (text, required/optional): List any insurance policies relevant to your estate.

### Other Expenses (Page 11)
* **Other expenses (other_expenses)** (text, required/optional): List any other expenses related to your estate.

### Funeral Expenses (Page 12)
* **Funeral expenses (funeral_expenses)** (text, required/optional): Estimate any funeral expenses that will be covered by your estate.

### Charitable Donations (Page 13)
* **Charitable donations (charitable_donations)** (text, required/optional): List any charitable donations you wish to make from your estate.

### Other Donations (Page 14)
* **Other donations (other_donations)** (text, required/optional): List any other donations you wish to make from your estate.

### Assets and Investments (Page 15)
* **Assets and investments (assets_investments)** (text, required/optional): List any significant assets and investments that make up your estate.

### Other Assets (Page 16)
* **Other assets (other_assets)** (text, required/optional): List any other assets not mentioned above.

### Assets in Use (Page 17)
* **Assets in use (assets_in_use)** (text, required/optional): List any assets that are currently being used for business or other purposes.

### Liabilities in Use (Page 18)
* **Liabilities in use (liabilities_in_use)** (text, required/optional): List any liabilities that are currently being used for business or other purposes.

### Debts in Use (Page 19)
* **Debts in use (debts_in_use)** (text, required/optional): List any debts that are currently being used for business or other purposes.

### Debts in Arrears (Page 20)
* **Debts in arrears (debts_in_arrears)** (text, required/optional): List any debts that are past due or in arrears.

### Debts in Dispute (Page 21)
* **Debts in dispute (debts_in_dispute)** (text, required/optional): List any debts that are disputed or in question.

### Other Debts (Page 22)
* **Other debts (other_debts)** (text, required/optional): List any other debts not mentioned above.

### Insurance Policies (Page 23)
* **Insurance policies (insurance_policies)** (text, required/optional): List any insurance policies relevant to your estate.

### Other Policies (Page 24)
* **Other policies (other_policies)** (text, required/optional): List any other policies not mentioned above.

### Notes (Page 25)
* **Notes (notes)** (text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information about your estate.
