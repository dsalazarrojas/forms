# Medical Insurance Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a patient's insurance information and contact details for medical facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your name, date of birth, and insurance provider** (patient_info):
	* Please enter your full name as it appears on your insurance card.
	* Provide your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
	* Enter the name of your insurance provider.
2. **Enter your insurance information** (insurance_info):
	* Provide your insurance card number or ID number.
	* Enter your group/plan number (if applicable).
3. **Enter your contact details** (contact_details):
	* Provide your address (street address, city, state, and zip code).
	* Enter your contact phone number and/or email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (patient_info)**:
	+ This field is where you should enter your name as it appears on your insurance card.
	+ Your date of birth should be entered in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Insurance Information (insurance_info)**:
	+ Enter your insurance card number or ID number.
	+ If you have a group or plan number, please enter it here.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**:
	+ Enter your address to receive mail or messages.
	+ If you have a contact phone number, please enter it.
	+ If you have a contact email address, please enter it.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth insurance coverage and communication.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask your healthcare provider for assistance.
* This form is designed to be used in conjunction with other medical documentation and records.
* Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
