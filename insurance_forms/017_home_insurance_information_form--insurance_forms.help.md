# home_insurance_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who are applying for home insurance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your address and zip code to help the insurance company verify your location.
2. Provide your phone number and email address so that the insurance company can contact you.
3. Select the type of insurance you need (e.g., Homeowners, Renters, etc.).
4. Enter the square footage of your home.
5. Enter the value of your home.
6. If you have a mortgage on your property, select 'True' or 'False' in the Mortgage Info field.
7. Choose your preferred insurance provider (e.g., State Farm, Allstate, etc.).
8. Select who the policy is for (e.g., Self, Spouse, etc.).
9. Specify the relationship between the policy holder and you (e.g., Parent, Child, etc.).
10. Finally, provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your insurance application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address**: Enter the address where you live.
*   **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code to verify your location.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number so that the insurance company can contact you.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address so that the insurance company can contact you.
*   **Insurance Type**: Select the type of insurance you need (e.g., Homeowners, Renters, etc.).
*   **Square Footage**: Enter the square footage of your home.
*   **Home Value**: Enter the value of your home.
*   **Mortgage Info**: Select 'True' or 'False' if you have a mortgage on your property.
*   **Insurance Provider**: Choose your preferred insurance provider (e.g., State Farm, Allstate, etc.).
*   **Policy Holder**: Select who the policy is for (e.g., Self, Spouse, etc.).
*   **Policy Holder Relationship**: Specify the relationship between the policy holder and you (e.g., Parent, Child, etc.).
*   **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your insurance application.
