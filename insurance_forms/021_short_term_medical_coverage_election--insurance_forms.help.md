# Short Term Medical Coverage Election - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for short term medical coverage for employees who are eligible. It helps the HR department process employee enrollments and manage coverage information efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose your medical coverage option from the drop down menu. Select "Group Plan", "Individual Plan", or "Cobra".
2. Enter your coverage start and end dates. These dates should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Choose your coverage type. Select "Full-Time", "Part-Time", or "Student".
4. Enter your group name and ID. This information will be used to identify your coverage.
5. Select your department from the list of available options.
6. Enter your job title.
7. Choose whether you have a manager. If you do not have a manager, select "No".
8. Select your team from the list of available options.
9. Enter your location.
10. Enter any additional details about your coverage type, start date, and end date.
11. Select your enrollment status. Choose "Active", "Inactive", or "Pending".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Coverage Election**: Choose your medical coverage option. Select "Group Plan", "Individual Plan", or "Cobra".
* **Coverage Start Date**: Enter the date you would like your coverage to start. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Coverage End Date**: Enter the date you would like your coverage to end. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Coverage Type**: Choose your coverage type. Select "Full-Time", "Part-Time", or "Student".
* **Group Name**: Enter your group name.
* **Group ID**: Enter your group ID.
* **Enrollment Date**: Enter the date you enrolled in the coverage.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID.
* **Department**: Select your department from the list of available options.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Manager**: Choose whether you have a manager. If you do not have a manager, select "No".
* **Team**: Select your team from the list of available options.
* **Location**: Enter your location.
* **Coverage Type Description**: Enter any additional details about your coverage type.
* **Coverage Start Date Description**: Enter any additional details about your coverage start date.
* **Coverage End Date Description**: Enter any additional details about your coverage end date.
* **Enrollment Status**: Select your enrollment status. Choose "Active", "Inactive", or "Pending".
