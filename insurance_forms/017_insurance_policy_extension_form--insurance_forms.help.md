# Insurance Policy Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Policy Extension Form is used to collect information from customers who want to extend their current insurance policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and determine if you are eligible to extend your policy. This form is for customers who are looking to continue their insurance coverage.
2.  Select "Yes" if you would like to extend your policy, and "No" otherwise.
3.  Choose the type of policy you have (e.g. Health, Auto, Home, Life, or Other).
4.  If you select "Other", provide a brief explanation of the policy type in the field below.
5.  Enter the reason you want to extend your policy.
6.  Enter the policy ID number (if you have it).
7.  Enter the date you would like to start the extension from.
8.  Enter the date your policy would normally expire.
9.  Enter the amount you would like to extend your policy for (in months).
10.  Enter the policy holder's name.
11.  Enter the policy holder's email address.
12.  Enter the policy holder's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policy Request Form** (`policy_request_form`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if you would like to extend your policy, and "No" otherwise.
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of policy you have (e.g. Health, Auto, Home, Life, or Other).
* **Reason for Extension** (`reason_for_extension`, text, required: false): Enter a brief explanation of why you want to extend your policy.
* **Policy ID** (`policy_id`, number, required: false): Enter the policy ID number, if you have it.
* **Extension Date** (`extension_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to start the extension from.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date your policy would normally expire.
* **Extension Amount** (`extension_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount you would like to extend your policy for (in months).
* **Policy Holder's Name** (`policy_holder_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder's Email** (`policy_holder_email`, email, required: false): Enter the policy holder's email address.
* **Policy Holder's Contact** (`policy_holder_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the policy holder's phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double check your information for errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
