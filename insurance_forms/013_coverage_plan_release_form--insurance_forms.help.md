# Coverage Plan Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coverage Plan Release Form is used to collect information about an employee's coverage plan, which includes details about their health, dental, and life insurance. This form helps the employee and the HR department review and approve the coverage plan before it goes into effect.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the coverage plan details in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide the reason for releasing the coverage plan.
3. Enter the effective and termination dates of the coverage plan.
4. Enter the employee's ID and manager's name and title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Coverage
* **Coverage** (`coverage_plan_release_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the coverage plan.
### Insurance
* **Insurance** (`insurance_company`, text, optional): Enter the insurance company providing the coverage plan.
### Reason
* **Reason** (`reason_for_release`, text, optional): Enter the reason for releasing the coverage plan.
### Effective
* **Effective** (`effective_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the coverage plan will start.
### Termination
* **Termination** (`termination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the coverage plan will end.
### Employee
* **Employee** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter the employee's ID number.
### Reason
* **Reason** (`reason_for_termination`, text, optional): Enter the reason for terminating the coverage plan.
### Manager
* **Manager** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the manager's name.
### Title
* **Title** (`manager_title`, text, optional): Enter the manager's title.
