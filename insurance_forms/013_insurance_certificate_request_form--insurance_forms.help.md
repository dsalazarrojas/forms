# Insurance Certificate Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an insurance certificate from a vendor. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to facilitate the processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the vendor information section with the correct details about the vendor you are requesting the certificate for.
2. Select the type of certificate you are requesting.
3. If you are requesting an "Other" certificate type, provide additional information in the corresponding fields.
4. Upload any required documents.
5. Provide contact information for the vendor.
6. Enter the certificate number, date, and expiration date as applicable.
7. Add any additional information or requests in the "Additional Info" field.
8. Provide your signature and the date of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Info (Vendor Info)** (`vendor_info`, text, required=false): Enter the name and contact information of the vendor you are requesting the certificate for.
* **Certificate Type (Certificate Type)** (`certificate_type`, select_one, required=false): Select the type of certificate you are requesting. If not listed, choose "Other" and provide details in the "Other Certificate Type" field.
* **Upload Documents (Upload Documents)** (`upload_documents`, text, required=false): Upload any required documents for the certificate.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info)** (`contact_info`, text, required=false): Enter the contact information for the vendor.
* **Business Name (Business Name)** (`business_name`, text, required=false): Enter the name of the business you are representing.
* **Address (Address)** (`address`, text, required=false): Enter the address of the business.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone`, text, required=false): Enter the phone number of the business.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, required=false): Enter the email address of the business.
* **Certificate Number (Certificate Number)** (`certificate_number`, number, required=false): Enter the certificate number as required.
* **Certificate Date (Certificate Date)** (`certificate_date`, date, required=false): Enter the date of the certificate.
* **Certificate Expiration (Certificate Expiration)** (`certificate_expiration`, date, required=false): Enter the expiration date of the certificate.
