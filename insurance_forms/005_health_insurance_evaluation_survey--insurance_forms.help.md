# Health Insurance Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings on health insurance plans and providers. It will help us understand your experience with health insurance plans, including their quality, coverage, and overall satisfaction. Your input will be used to improve our understanding of health insurance services and provide better support to our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your personal experience with the health insurance plan and provider.
2. Fill in the required fields with as much detail as possible.
3. Select the best answer for the "Rating" and "Experience Rating" questions.
4. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the "Recommend Provider" question.
5. Use the "Additional Comments" field to provide any additional information that might be helpful for us to know.
6. Finally, provide your contact details if you have any further questions or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Health Insurance Plan** (`health_insurance_plan`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of the health insurance plan you are providing feedback on.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the plan on a scale of 1-5.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the insurance provider you are evaluating.
* **Policy Holder** (`policy_holder`, text, optional): Enter the policy holder's name.
* **Coverage Area** (`coverage_area`, text, optional): Enter the geographic area covered by the plan.
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, text, optional): Describe the services offered by the plan.
* **Experience Rating** (`experience_rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience with the plan on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the plan on a scale of 1-5.
* **Recommend Provider** (`recommend_provider`, select_one, optional): Do you recommend this provider? (Yes/No)
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information you think might be helpful.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your name and contact information if you have any follow-up questions or comments.
