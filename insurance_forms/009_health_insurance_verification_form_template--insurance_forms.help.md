# health_insurance_verification_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify health insurance information of a patient. It is essential for ensuring that the patient's insurance details are accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's birthdate in the "Birthdate" field. This should be in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. (Optional) If applicable, enter the name of the person associated with the insurance policy in the "Policyholder Name" field.
4. Enter the relationship of the policyholder to the patient in the "Policyholder Relationship" field.
5. Select the insurance provider from the list in the "Provider" field. This field is required to ensure accuracy.
6. Enter the policy number in the "Policy Number" field.
7. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Enter the patient's email address in the "Email" field.
9. (Optional) If you have any additional notes or comments, enter them in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Name
* **Patient Name** (text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their insurance card or identification.
### Birthdate
* **Birthdate** (date, required): Enter the patient's birthdate in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
### Policyholder Name
* **Policyholder Name** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the person associated with the insurance policy.
### Policyholder Relationship
* **Policyholder Relationship** (text, optional): Enter the relationship of the policyholder to the patient (e.g., parent, spouse, child).
### Provider
* **Provider** (select_one, required): Select the insurance provider from the list.
### Policy Number
* **Policy Number** (text, required): Enter the policy number as it appears on the insurance card or identification.
### Phone
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
### Email
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
### Note
* **Note** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the patient's insurance information.
