# Health Insurance Exclusions Review Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to review and document exclusions from health insurance coverage. Exclusions are specific conditions or circumstances that are not covered by the insurance policy. This form helps insurance professionals to accurately identify and document these exclusions, ensuring that policyholders are aware of what is and isn't covered under their policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **User Input** field on page 1, providing any relevant details about the policyholder or policy.
2. Select the applicable **Exclusion Type** from the drop-down menu, specifying which type of exclusion applies (e.g., Medical, Dental, Vision, etc.).
3. Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** (if applicable) for the exclusion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the policyholder or policy. This field is for additional information that might be helpful for insurance professionals reviewing the exclusion.
* **Exclusion Type** (`exclusion_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of exclusion that applies. This field has the following options:
	+ Medical Exclusion
	+ Dental Exclusion
	+ Vision Exclusion
	+ Hearing Exclusion
	+ Mental Health Exclusion
	+ Mental Health and Substance Abuse Exclusion
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the exclusion. If the exclusion is continuous, you can leave this field blank.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the exclusion. If the exclusion is continuous, you can leave this field blank.

## Tips
When completing this form, make sure to select the correct **Exclusion Type** based on the applicable condition or circumstance. If an exclusion is continuous, you can leave the **Start Date** and **End Date** fields blank.
