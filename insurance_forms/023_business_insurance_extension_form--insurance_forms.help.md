# Business Insurance Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Insurance Extension Form is a necessary step for business owners and policy holders to request an extension of their policy term. This form helps gather all the required information to facilitate the extension process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting it.
2. Fill in your details accurately and truthfully.
3. If you have supporting documents, indicate that you have provided them.
4. Enter the contact email address where you would like to be reached for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy holder name (Policy holder name)** (`policy_holder_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the policy holder as shown on the policy document.
* **Policy number (Policy number)** (`policy_number`, text, required): Provide the existing policy number or identifier.
* **Extension reason (Extension reason)** (`extension_reason`, text, required): Explain the reason for requesting the extension of the policy term.
* **Requested extension period (Requested extension period)** (`requested_extension_period`, number, required): Enter the number of months needed for the policy extension.
* **Additional premium expected (Additional premium expected)** (`additional_premium_expected`, number, optional): If known, estimate the additional premium cost associated with the extension.
* **Supporting documents (Supporting documents)** (`supporting_documents`, select_one, optional): Indicate if supporting documents are provided (True/False).
* **Contact email (Contact email)** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Notes (Notes)** (`notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional information for the underwriters to review.
