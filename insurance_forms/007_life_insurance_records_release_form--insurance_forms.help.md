# Life Insurance Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Life Insurance Records Release Form is designed to collect information about a policyholder's life insurance policy, including their name, address, policy details, and relationships. This form is essential for policyholders and authorized parties to provide accurate details to release life insurance records.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you have all the necessary information and documentation required to complete the form accurately.
2. Start by filling in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
3. Enter the policyholder's Policy Number and Effective Date.
4. If applicable, select the Policy Type from the provided options (Life, Death, or Disability).
5. Enter the Policy Holder's name and select their relationship to you (Spouse, Child, Parent, etc.) from the options.
6. Enter the name of the Authorized Party and select their relationship to you (Self, Spouse, Parent, etc.) from the options.
7. If desired, enter the Contact Phone number for the Authorized Party.
8. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter the first name of the policyholder.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter the last name of the policyholder.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the policyholder's address, if applicable.
* **Policy Number** (Number, Required): Enter the life insurance policy number.
* **Effective Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the policy became effective.
* **Expiration Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the policy expires.
* **Policy Type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of policy (Life, Death, Disability).
* **Policy Holder** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the policyholder.
* **Relationship to Policy Holder** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the relationship to the policyholder (Spouse, Child, Parent).
* **Authorized Party** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the authorized party.
* **Relationship to Authorized Party** (Select One, Required): Select the relationship to the authorized party (Self, Spouse, Parent).
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact phone number of the authorized party.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and correctly.
* Double-check the entries before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field or information, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
