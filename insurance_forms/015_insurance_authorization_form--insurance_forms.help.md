# Insurance Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain insurance authorization and disclosure of a patient's medical information. It's essential for ensuring that a patient's medical records are accurately documented and that their healthcare providers are aware of any insurance coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your patient information in the **Patient Information** section.
2. Provide your insurance details in the **Insurance Information** section.
3. Indicate the **Goals of Care** you want to achieve.
4. If applicable, provide any relevant **Medical History** information.
5. Authorize **Disclosure of Medical Information** to your healthcare provider.
6. Sign and date the form, including your **Signature** and the **Date Signed**.
7. If necessary, provide your **Provider Name** and **Provider Title**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Please enter your name, address, and contact information.
* **Insurance Information**: Enter your insurance provider name, policy number, and any other relevant details.
* **Goals of Care**: Select one or more of the options to indicate the purpose of your care:
	+ **Medical Treatment**: For medical treatment purposes only.
	+ **Non-Medical**: For non-medical purposes only.
	+ **Both**: For both medical and non-medical purposes.
* **Medical History**: If you have a medical condition or history that may impact your care, please provide any relevant details.
* **Authorization to Disclose**: Please indicate whether you authorize your healthcare provider to disclose your medical information.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms of this form.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Provider Name** and **Provider Title**: If you are a healthcare provider, enter your name and title.
* **Time Signed**: Enter the time you signed this form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form should only be filled out once for each patient encounter.
