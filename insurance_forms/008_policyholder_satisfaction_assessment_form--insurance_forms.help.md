# Policyholder Satisfaction Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Policyholder Satisfaction Assessment Form is a tool used to collect feedback from policyholders on their experience with insurance services. It is designed to help us understand what we are doing well and what we need to improve to better serve our policyholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your policyholder name in the "Policyholder Name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field (this field is not required, but it will help us get in touch with you with any follow-up questions).
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (this field is not required, but it will help us get in touch with you with any follow-up questions).
4.  Rate the "Service Quality" by selecting one of the options provided.
5.  Rate your "Satisfaction Level" by selecting one of the options provided.
6.  Enter any comments you may have in the "Comments" field.
7.  If applicable, enter your "Policyholder ID" and "Provider Name".
8.  Track your "Policyholder Satisfaction" by selecting one or more of the provided options.
9.  Track your "Provider Satisfaction" by selecting one or more of the provided options.
10.  Track your "Service Experience" by selecting one or more of the provided options.
11.  Enter any "Additional Comments" you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Policyholder Name** (`policyholder_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your policy document.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you with any follow-up questions.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you with any follow-up questions.
*   **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_one, required): Rate your experience with the service quality provided. Select one of the options: Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
*   **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction level with the service. Select one of the options: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments you may have about your experience.
*   **Policyholder ID** (`policyholder_id`, text, optional): Enter your policyholder ID if you have one.
*   **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your provider.
*   **Policyholder Satisfaction** (`policyholder_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Track your policyholder satisfaction by selecting one or more of the provided options: Very High, High, Medium, Low, Very Low.
*   **Provider Satisfaction** (`provider_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Track your provider satisfaction by selecting one or more of the provided options: Very High, High, Medium, Low, Very Low.
*   **Service Experience** (`service_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Track your service experience by selecting one or more of the provided options: Very Positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, Very Negative.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments you may have about your experience.

This guide will help you complete the form and provide us with valuable feedback on your experience with our insurance services. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
