# Auto Insurance Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess potential risks associated with auto insurance policies. We'll use the information you provide to understand your risk profile and determine the best insurance terms for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question accurately and thoroughly. If you're unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for clarification. The form is divided into two sections: Personal Risk Factors and Vehicle Risk Factors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification or driver's license.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age as a number.
* **Primary Occupation** (`primary_occupation`, text, required): This is your primary occupation or industry. Some professions have lower risk profiles, so try to be as specific as possible (e.g., "Software Engineer" rather than just "Computer Scientist").
* **Accidents in Last 3 Years** (`accidents_count`, number, required): Include both at-fault and not-at-fault accidents.
* **Speeding Tickets in Last 3 Years** (`tickets_count`, number, required): Enter the total number of moving violations (speeding tickets).
* **DUI History** (`dui_history`, select_one, required): Select the most accurate answer from the options provided.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, text, required): Enter the year, make, model, and trim of your vehicle.
* **Estimated Annual Mileage** (`annual_mileage`, number, required): Enter an estimate of how many miles you'll drive per year.
* **Night Driving Frequency** (`night_driving_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you drive after dark.
* **Vehicle Storage Security** (`vehicle_storage_security`, select_one, required): Indicate where your vehicle is kept overnight.
* **Installed Safety Technology** (`installed_safety_tech`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable safety features installed in your vehicle (e.g., forward collision warning, lane departure warning).
* **Risk Underwriter Comments** (`underwriter_comments`, text, optional): This field is for internal use only, so you can skip it if you're unsure.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough when answering each question.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for clarification.
- If you have questions or concerns, reach out to us for assistance.
