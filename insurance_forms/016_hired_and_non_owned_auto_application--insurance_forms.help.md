# Hired And Non Owned Auto Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a vehicle used for business purposes or not, and to gather necessary details for determining the appropriate insurance coverage for the vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct business use of the vehicle.
2. Enter the driver's license ID number.
3. Choose the type of vehicle from the provided options.
4. Enter the vehicle's make.
5. Enter the vehicle's year.
6. Enter the vehicle's model.
7. Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
8. Select the appropriate vehicle use from the provided options.
9. Enter the year the vehicle was purchased.
10. Enter the year of the last inspection.
11. Enter the year of the last registration.
12. Provide information about the prior insurance coverage.
13. Enter the name of the prior insurance company.
14. Enter the effective date of prior insurance coverage.
15. Enter the expiry date of prior insurance coverage.
16. Provide the name of the prior insurer.
17. Provide the contact information for the prior insurer.
18. Enter the phone number of the prior insurer.
19. Enter the email of the prior insurer.
20. Enter the address of the prior insured.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Use** (`business_use`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to determine if the vehicle is used for business or non-business purposes. Select the correct option from the provided list.
* **Driver's License ID** (`driver_license_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter your driver's license ID number.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the type of vehicle from the provided options.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required: false): Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, required: false): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required: false): Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Identification Number** (`vehicle_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
* **VIN** (`vin`, `text`, required: false): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
* **VIN2** (`vin2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the second Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
* **VIN3** (`vin3`, `text`, required: false): Enter the third Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Use** (`vehicle_use`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the appropriate vehicle use from the provided options.
* **Year of Purchase** (`vehicle_year_of_purchase`, `number`, required: false): Enter the year the vehicle was purchased.
* **Year of Last Inspection** (`vehicle_year_of_last_inspection`, `number`, required: false): Enter the year of the last inspection.
* **Year of Last Registration** (`vehicle_year_of_last_registration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the year of the last registration.
* **Prior Insurance Coverage** (`prior_insurance_coverage`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about the prior insurance coverage.
* **Prior Insurance Company** (`prior_insurance_company`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the prior insurance company.
* **Prior Insurance Effective Date** (`prior_insurance_effective_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the effective date of prior insurance coverage.
* **Prior Insurance Expiry Date** (`prior_insurance_expire_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the expiry date of prior insurance coverage.
* **Prior Insurer** (`prior_insurer`, `text`, required: false): Provide the name of the prior insurer.
* **Prior Insurer Contact** (`prior_insurer_contact`, `text`, required: false): Provide the contact information for the prior insurer.
* **Prior Insurer Phone** (`prior_insurer_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the prior insurer.
* **Prior Insurer Email** (`prior_insurer_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter the email of the prior insurer.
* **Prior Insured Address** (`prior_insured_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the prior insured.
* **Policyholder Information** (`policyholder_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information about the policyholder.
