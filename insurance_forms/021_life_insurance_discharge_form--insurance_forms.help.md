# Life Insurance Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide information required to complete a life insurance discharge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the beneficiary information in the "Beneficiary Info" field.
2.  Provide details about the policy information in the "Policy Info" field.
3.  Enter the claimant's information in the "Claimant Info" field.
4.  Enter the policy holder's information in the "Policy Holder Info" field.
5.  Specify the discharge details in the "Discharge Details" field.
6.  Confirm the discharge in the "Discharge Confirmation" field.
7.  Sign and confirm the discharge by selecting "True" in the "Claimant Signature" field.
8.  Specify who is signing the discharge (Beneficiary, Claimant, Policy Holder) in the "Policy Holder Signature" field.
9.  Enter the policy holder's title in the "Policy Holder Title" field.
10. Enter the policy holder's name in the "Policy Holder Name" field.
11. Enter the policy holder's phone number in the "Policy Holder Phone" field.
12. Enter the policy holder's address in the "Policy Holder Address" field.
13. Enter the claimant's phone number in the "Claimant Phone" field.
14. Enter the claimant's email address in the "Claimant Email" field.
15. Enter the claimant's date of birth in the "Claimant Date of Birth" field.
16. Enter the claimant's name in the "Claimant Name" field.
17. Specify the discharge date in the "Discharge Date" field.
18. Add any additional notes from the claimant in the "Claimant Note" field.
19. Add any additional notes from the policy holder in the "Policy Holder Note" field.
20. Enter the policy holder's phone number 2 in the "Policy Holder Phone 2" field.
21. Enter the policy holder's address 2 in the "Policy Holder Address 2" field.
22. Enter the policy holder's title 2 in the "Policy Holder Title 2" field.
23. Enter the policy holder's date of birth in the "Policy Holder Date of Birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Beneficiary Info** (text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the beneficiary who will receive the life insurance payout.
- **Policy Info** (text, required): Enter details about the life insurance policy, including policy number, effective date, and policy type.
- **Claimant Info** (text, required): Enter the claimant's information, including their name, address, and contact number.
- **Policy Holder Info** (text, required): Enter the policy holder's information, including their name, address, and contact number.
- **Discharge Details** (text, required): Provide details about the reasons for the policy discharge.
- **Discharge Confirmation** (text, required): Confirm that the policy discharge is accurate and complete.
- **Claimant Signature** (select_one, required): Sign the discharge document by selecting "True" to confirm.
- **Policy Holder Signature** (select_multiple, required): Select who is signing the discharge document (Beneficiary, Claimant, Policy Holder).
- **Policy Holder Title** (text, optional): Enter the policy holder's title (e.g., Mr., Mrs., or Dr.).
- **Policy Holder Name** (text, optional): Enter the policy holder's full name.
- **Policy Holder Phone** (text, optional): Enter the policy holder's phone number.
- **Policy Holder Address** (text, optional): Enter the policy holder's address.
- **Claimant Phone** (text, optional): Enter the claimant's phone number.
- **Claimant Email** (email, optional): Enter the claimant's email address.
- **Claimant Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter the claimant's date of birth.
- **Claimant Name** (text, optional): Enter the claimant's full name.
- **Discharge Date** (date, optional): Enter the date the policy discharge is effective.
- **Claimant Note** (note, optional): Add any additional notes from the claimant.
- **Policy Holder Note** (note, optional): Add any additional notes from the policy holder.
- **Policy Holder Phone 2** (text, optional): Enter the policy holder's phone number 2.
- **Policy Holder Address 2** (text, optional): Enter the policy holder's address 2.
- **Policy Holder Title 2** (text, optional): Enter the policy holder's title 2.
- **Policy Holder Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter the policy holder's date of birth.
