# quick_quote_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The quick_quote_form is designed to gather customer information and payment details for a quote or order. It is intended for sales or customer service use cases where a quote or proposal is needed for a customer's order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information in the "Customer Information" section.
2. Enter the order details, including any relevant information, in the "Order Details" section.
3. Enter the payment information for the order in the "Payment Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (Customer Information, text, required: no)**: Enter your full name and contact information so that we can follow up with you regarding your quote or order.
* **Order Details (Order Details, text, required: no)**: Describe the order you would like us to quote, including any specific details or requirements.
* **Payment Information (Payment Information, text, required: no)**: Enter the payment method and any relevant payment details.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that we can generate an accurate quote or proposal for your order.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
- This form is for sales or customer service use cases where a quote or proposal is needed for a customer's order. If you are not sure if this form is the correct tool for your needs, please contact us for further guidance.
