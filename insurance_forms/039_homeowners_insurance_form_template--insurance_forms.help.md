# Homeowners Insurance Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from homeowners regarding their insurance needs.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is divided into different sections. To complete it, follow these steps:

1. Provide your address.
2. Enter the type of home you own.
3. Select the coverage level and type of insurance you require.
4. Enter the coverage limit you desire.
5. Select the deductible for your insurance.
6. Enter the premium amount you are willing to pay.
7. Add any additional information about yourself and your home.
8. Enter the signature below to confirm your details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address** (`1`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This section is where you enter your address where you reside.
* **Street** (`2`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter the street address where you reside.
* **City** (`3`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select your state from the drop-down list.
* **Postal Code** (`5`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter the postal code where you reside.
* **Date Of Birth** (`6`, `date`, required: false)
	+ Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YY.
* **Social Security Number** (`7`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter your social security number. This will be kept confidential.
* **Email** (`8`, `email`, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`9`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Type Of Home** (`10`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the type of home you own from the drop-down list.
* **Square Footage** (`11`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Enter the square footage of your home.
* **Years Lived** (`12`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Enter the years you have lived in your home.
* **Type of Insurance** (`13`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the type of insurance you require.
* **Coverage Type** (`14`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the type of coverage you desire.
* **Coverage Level** (`15`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the coverage level you require.
* **Coverage Deductible** (`16`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the deductible amount you are willing to pay.
* **Coverage Limit** (`17`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the coverage limit you require.
* **Payment Type** (`18`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the payment frequency you prefer.
* **Premium Amount** (`19`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Enter the premium amount you are willing to pay.
* **Notes** (`20`, `note`, required: false)
	+ Enter any additional information you want to share with us.
* **Additional Info** (`21`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select any additional information you would like to provide.
* **Home Value** (`22`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Enter the value of your home.
* **Mortgage Balance** (`23`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Enter the mortgage balance on your home.
* **Year Built** (`24`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the year your home was built from the drop-down list.
* **Signature** (`25`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Sign below to confirm your details.
