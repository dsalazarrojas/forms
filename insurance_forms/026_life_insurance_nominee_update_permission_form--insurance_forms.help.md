# Life Insurance Nominee Update Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to update the details of a nominee associated with a life insurance policy. Authorized individuals can use this form to make changes to the policyholder's name, email, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are authorized to use this form for the purpose of updating a life insurance policy nominee.
2. Fill in the following fields:
	* **Policyholder Name:** Enter the name of the policyholder.
	* **Policyholder Email:** Enter the email address associated with the policyholder.
	* **Nominee Name:** Enter the name of the nominee.
	* **Nominee Birthdate:** Enter the birthdate of the nominee.
	* **Nominee Address:** Enter the address of the nominee.
	* **Nominee Relationship:** Enter the relationship between the policyholder and the nominee.
	* **Policy Number:** Enter the policy number associated with the nominee.
	* **Policy Status:** Select the status of the policy (Active, Inactive, Pending).
	* **Update Reason:** Enter the reason for updating the nominee information.
	* **Updated By:** Enter the name of the person updating the information.
	* **Updated Date:** Enter the date of the update.
	* **Confirmation:** Add any additional comments or notes about the update.
	* **Nominee DOB (Date of Birth):** Enter the date of birth of the nominee.
	* **Nominee Occupation:** Enter the occupation of the nominee.
	* **Nominee Income:** Enter the income of the nominee.
	* **Nominee Marital Status:** Select the marital status of the nominee (Married, Single, Divorced).
	* **Nominee Relationship Status:** Select the relationship status of the nominee (Active, Inactive, Pending).
	* **Policy Status Reason:** Enter any reason for the policy status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policyholder Name** (`policyholder_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the policyholder.
* **Policyholder Email** (`policyholder_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address associated with the policyholder.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the nominee.
* **Nominee Birthdate** (`nominee_birthdate`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the birthdate of the nominee.
* **Nominee Address** (`nominee_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the nominee.
* **Nominee Relationship** (`nominee_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the relationship between the policyholder and the nominee.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the policy number associated with the nominee.
* **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the status of the policy (Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Update Reason** (`update_reason`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the reason for updating the nominee information.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person updating the information.
* **Updated Date** (`updated_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the update.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the update.
* **Nominee DOB (Date of Birth)** (`nominee_dob`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of birth of the nominee.
* **Nominee Occupation** (`nominee_occupation`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the occupation of the nominee.
* **Nominee Income** (`nominee_income`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the income of the nominee.
* **Nominee Marital Status** (`nominee_marital_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the marital status of the nominee (Married, Single, Divorced).
* **Nominee Relationship Status** (`nominee_relationship_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the relationship status of the nominee (Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Policy Status Reason** (`policy_status_reason`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any reason for the policy status.
