# Referral - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact and policy information for referral purposes. It is used to gather information about the policy holder, insurance company, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are filling out the correct information.
2. Fill in the required fields, starting with the contact information section.
3. Enter the policy holder's contact information in the corresponding fields.
4. Select the insurance company and policy details as per the options provided.
5. Enter the policy holder's address and contact details.
6. Choose the referral source and insurance type.
7. Fill in any additional details as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (1)** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): This field is for entering the contact person's information such as name and phone number.
* **Phone (2)** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Email (3)** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Insurance Company (4)** (`insurance_company`, text, required: false): Select the insurance company from the provided options.
* **Date of Birth (5)** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the policy holder's date of birth.
* **Agent Signature (6)** (`agent_signature`, text, required: false): This field is for the agent's signature.
* **Policy Number (7)** (`policy_number`, text, required: false): Enter the policy number.
* **Policy Holder (8)** (`policy_holder`, text, required: false): Enter the policy holder's name.
* **Agent Name (9)** (`agent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the agent's name.
* **Date of Insured (10)** (`date_of_insured`, date, required: false): Enter the date the policy was insured.
* **Referral Source (11)** (`referral_source`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the referral source from the options provided.
* **Insurance Type (12)** (`insurance_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of insurance from the provided options.
* **Insurance Coverage (13)** (`insurance_coverage`, number, required: false): Enter the insurance coverage amount.
* **Policy Status (14)** (`policy_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the policy status from the options provided.
* **Policy Holder Address (15)** (`policy_holder_address`, text, required: false): Enter the policy holder's address.
* **Policy Holder City (16)** (`policy_holder_city`, text, required: false): Enter the policy holder's city.
* **Policy Holder State (17)** (`policy_holder_state`, text, required: false): Enter the policy holder's state.
* **Policy Holder Zip (18)** (`policy_holder_zip`, text, required: false): Enter the policy holder's zip code.
* **Policy Holder Country (19)** (`policy_holder_country`, text, required: false): Enter the policy holder's country.
* **Policy Holder Phone (20)** (`policy_holder_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the policy holder's phone number.
* **Referral Date (21)** (`referral_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of referral.
