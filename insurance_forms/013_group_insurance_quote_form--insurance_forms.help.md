# Group Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from a group or representative of a group for an insurance quote. Please fill out the form accurately to provide the necessary details for a quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your group's information in the "Group info" field.
2. Specify the type of quote you are requesting in the "Quote request" field.
3. Select the terms of the quote in the "Quote terms" field.
4. Choose the insurance products you are interested in from the "Insurance products" field.
5. Select the frequency of the quote in the "Quote frequency" field.
6. Enter the quote amount in the "Quote amount" field.
7. Provide any additional information about the group's insurance needs in the "Additional info" field.
8. Enter the phone number of the group's representative in the "Phone number" field.
9. Enter the email address of the group's representative in the "Email" field.
10. Choose the insurer from the "Insurer" field.
11. Add any additional comments or notes about the quote in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Group info** (text, required: false): Enter your group's name, contact person's name, and any other relevant details.
* **Quote request** (text, required: false): Specify the type of quote you are requesting (e.g., annual, monthly, etc.).
* **Quote terms** (text, required: false): Select the terms of the quote (e.g., term, premium, etc.).
* **Insurance products** (select multiple, required: false): Choose the insurance products you are interested in (e.g., life, health, etc.).
* **Quote frequency** (select one, required: false): Select the frequency of the quote (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **Quote amount** (number, required: false): Enter the quote amount.
* **Insurance coverage** (note, required: false): Provide any relevant information about the group's insurance coverage needs.
* **Additional info** (text, required: false): Add any additional information about the group's insurance needs.
* **Phone number** (text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the group's representative.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter the email address of the group's representative.
* **Insurer** (select one, required: false): Choose the insurer for the quote.
* **Note** (note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the quote.
