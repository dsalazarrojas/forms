# Responsive Life Insurance Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you get a life insurance quote. Please fill out the form with your personal details to get an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with your personal information.
2. Review your answers before submitting the form.
3. Submit the form to receive your life insurance quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age as a number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Coverage Start Date** (`coverage_start_date`, date, required): Select the start date of your desired coverage period.
* **Coverage End Date** (`coverage_end_date`, date, required): Select the end date of your desired coverage period.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Life Insurance Company** (`life_insurance`, select multiple, optional): Select the life insurance company you are interested in.
* **Coverage Type** (`coverage_type`, select one, optional): Select the type of coverage you are interested in (Whole Life, Term Life, Universal Life).
* **Policy Holder** (`policy_holder`, text, optional): Enter the name of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Relationship** (`policy_holder_relationship`, select multiple, optional): Select the relationship of the policy holder (Parent, Child, Spouse).
* **Policy Holder Age** (`policy_holder_age`, number, optional): Enter the age of the policy holder.
* **Premium** (`premium`, number, optional): Enter the premium you are willing to pay.
* **Quote** (`quote`, number, optional): Enter the quote amount you are expecting.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled out to submit the form. Optional fields can be left blank if you do not have the information or do not want to provide it.
