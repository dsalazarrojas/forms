# Property Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects property information for financial calculations and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Property Quote Form** is a data collection tool. Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Read each section carefully and make sure to provide correct and up-to-date information.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Street address** (`property_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the property.
* **Type of property** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of property (e.g., condo, townhouse, house).
* **Estimated value of property** (`estimated_value`, `number`, required): Enter the estimated value of the property.
* **Number of units** (`num_units`, `text`, required): Enter the number of units in the property (if applicable).
* **Annual property tax** (`annual_property_tax`, `number`, required): Enter the annual property tax amount.
* **Annual homeowners insurance premium** (`annual_homeowners_insurance_premium`, `number`, required): Enter the annual homeowners insurance premium amount.
* **Annual rental income** (`annual_rental_income`, `number`, required): Enter the annual rental income (if applicable).
* **Annual property taxes** (`annual_property_taxes`, `number`, required): Enter the annual property taxes amount.
* **Annual property insurance premium** (`annual_property_insurance_premium`, `number`, required): Enter the annual property insurance premium amount.
* **Annual property maintenance cost** (`annual_property_maintenance_cost`, `number`, required): Enter the annual property maintenance cost amount.
* **Annual property management fee** (`annual_property_management_fee`, `number`, required): Enter the annual property management fee amount.
* **Other expenses** (`other_expenses`, `text`, required): Describe any other expenses related to the property.
* **Other expenses value** (`other_expenses_value`, `number`, required): Enter the value of other expenses.
* **Annual property tax rate** (`annual_property_tax_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the annual property tax rate percentage.
* **Annual property insurance premium rate** (`annual_property_insurance_premium_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the annual property insurance premium rate percentage.
* **Annual property maintenance cost rate** (`annual_property_maintenance_cost_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the annual property maintenance cost rate percentage.
* **Annual property management fee rate** (`annual_property_management_fee_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the annual property management fee rate percentage.
* **Other expenses rate** (`other_expenses_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the rate of other expenses percentage.

## Tips

* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your financial advisor or property management company.
