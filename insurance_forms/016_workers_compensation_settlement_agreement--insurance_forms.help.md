# Workers' Compensation Settlement Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of a workers' compensation settlement between an employer and an employee. It captures information about the parties involved, claim number, agreement date, agreement details, and signatures of all parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Complete all fields that are marked as "required" to ensure that the form is complete.
*   Enter the employer's name and contact information in the "Employer's Name" field.
*   Enter the insurer's name and contact information in the "Insurer's Name" field.
*   Enter the claim number associated with the case in the "Claim Number" field.
*   Enter the date of the agreement in the "Agreement Date" field.
*   Provide a brief description of the agreement in the "Agreement Details" field.
*   Sign the form with the employee's signature in the "Employee's Signature" field.
*   Sign the form with the employer's signature in the "Employer's Signature" field.
*   Sign the form with the insurer's signature in the "Insurer's Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employer's Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name and contact information of the employer involved in the settlement.
*   **Insurer's Name** (`insurer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name and contact information of the insurer involved in the settlement.
*   **Claim Number** (`claim_number`, `number`, required): Enter the claim number associated with the case.
*   **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the agreement.
*   **Agreement Details** (`agreement_details`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the agreement.
*   **Employee's Signature** (`employee_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form with the employee's signature.
*   **Employer's Signature** (`employer_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form with the employer's signature.
*   **Insurer's Signature** (`insurer_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form with the insurer's signature.
