# Insurance Claim Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process insurance claims after treatment or settlement has been finalized. It is required for all claimants to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth claimant process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your name.
2.  Enter your date of birth.
3.  Enter your social security number.
4.  Enter your phone number (optional).
5.  Enter your email address.
6.  Describe the insurance claim you are making.
7.  List any claimant documents that will be used in the claim.
8.  Choose if you are insured.
9.  Describe your claimant injuries.
10.  Describe your treatment or medical condition.
11.  Choose your claimant's insurer (e.g., auto, home, business, etc.).
12.  Choose the type of policy you have (e.g., active, inactive, canceled).
13.  Enter the deductible amount for your policy.
14.  (Optional) Enter the deductible amount for your policy.
15.  Choose your claimant's provider (e.g., doctor, hospital, clinic, etc.).
16.  Choose your claimant's counselor's gender (e.g., male, female, other).
17.  Enter your claimant counselor's age.
18.  (Optional) Enter the date of your claimant's counselor's appointment.
19.  (Optional) Enter your claimant's counselor's address.
20.  Enter your claimant's provider's address.
21.  (Optional) Enter your claimant's provider's address.
22.  (Optional) List any claimant provider's that will be used in the claim.
23.  (Optional) List any claimant provider's that will be used in the claim.
24.  (Optional) List any claimant provider's that will be used in the claim.
25.  (Optional) Enter your claimant's provider's address.

*   **prophets** (Text, Required): Enter your name.
*   **date_of_birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **social_security_number** (Text, Required): Enter your social security number.
*   **phone_number** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
*   **prophets_description** (Text, Required): Describe the insurance claim you are making.
*   **Claimant Documents** (Multiple Select, Required): List any supporting documents that will be used in the claim (e.g., ID card, claimant statement, police report, witness statement).
*   **Claimant Insured** (Select One, Required): Choose if you are insured.
*   **Claimant Injuries** (Text, Required): Describe your claimant injuries.
*   **Claimant Treatment** (Text, Required): Describe your treatment or medical condition.
*   **Claimant Medical** (Text, Optional): (Optional) Describe any additional medical information.
*   **Claimant Insurer** (Select One, Required): Choose your claimant's insurer (e.g., auto, home, business, etc.).
*   **Claimant Policy** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of policy you have (e.g., active, inactive, canceled).
