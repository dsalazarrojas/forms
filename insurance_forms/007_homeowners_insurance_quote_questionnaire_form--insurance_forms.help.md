# Homeowners Insurance Quote Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the purpose of creating a homeowner's insurance quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your address in the "Home Address" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the type of insurance you are interested in from the "Type of Insurance" field.
5. Choose the type of coverage you need for your insurance from the "Insurance Coverage Type" field.
6. Enter the date you want your coverage to start in the "Coverage Start Date" field.
7. Enter the date your coverage will end in the "Coverage End Date" field.
8. Select the location where your coverage will be effective from the "Coverage Location" field.
9. Enter any additional details about your coverage location in the "Coverage Location Note" field.
10. Specify the type of home you own from the "Type of Home" field.
11. Enter the year your home was built in the "Year Home Built" field.
12. Enter the age of your home in the "Home Age" field.
13. Enter the size of your home in square feet in the "Home Size SQFT" field.
14. Enter the value of your home in the "Home Value" field.
15. Enter the annual premium for your policy in the "Policy Annual Premium" field.
16. Specify the currency of your policy annual premium in the "Policy Annual Premium Currency" field.
17. Enter your agent's name in the "Agent Name" field.
18. Enter your agent's phone number in the "Agent Phone" field.
19. Select the status of your policy from the "Policy Status" field.
20. Enter any additional details about your policy status in the "Policy Status Note" field.
21. Enter any notes about your policy effective date in the "Policy Effective Date Note" field.
22. Enter any notes about your policy issuance date in the "Policy Issuance Date Note" field.
23. Enter any notes about your policy in the "Policy Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Address (id: 1)**: Fill in your home's address.
* **Email (id: 2)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (id: 3)**: Provide your phone number.
* **Type of Insurance (id: 4)**: Select the type of insurance you are interested in.
	+ Options: Homeowners, Renters, Condo Co-op.
* **Insurance Coverage Type (id: 5)**: Choose the type of coverage you need for your insurance.
	+ Options: Liability, Property, Umbrella.
* **Coverage Start Date (id: 6)**: Enter the date you want your coverage to start.
* **Coverage End Date (id: 7)**: Enter the date your coverage will end.
* **Coverage Location (id: 10)**: Select where your coverage will be effective.
	+ Options: Primary Residence, Secondary Residence, Other.
* **Coverage Location Note (id: 11)**: Enter any additional details about your coverage location.
* **Type of Home (id: 12)**: Specify the type of home you own.
	+ Options: Single Family Residence, Duplex, Triplex, 4 Family Residence, Other.
* **Year Home Built (id: 13)**: Enter the year your home was built.
* **Home Age (id: 14)**: Enter the age of your home.
* **Home Size SQFT (id: 15)**: Enter the size of your home in square feet.
* **Home Value (id: 16)**: Enter the value of your home.
* **Policy Annual Premium (id: 17)**: Enter the annual premium for your policy.
* **Policy Annual Premium Currency (id: 18)**: Specify the currency of your policy annual premium.
	+ Options: USD, CAD, EUR.
* **Agent Name (id: 19)**: Enter your agent's name.
* **Agent Phone (id: 20)**: Enter your agent's phone number.
* **Policy Status (id: 21)**: Select the status of your policy.
	+ Options: Active, Inactive, Pending.
* **Policy Status Note (id: 22)**: Enter any additional details about your policy status.
* **Policy Effective Date Note (id: 23)**: Enter any notes about your policy effective date.
* **Policy Issuance Date Note (id: 24)**: Enter any notes about your policy issuance date.
* **Policy Note (id: 25)**: Enter any notes about your policy.
