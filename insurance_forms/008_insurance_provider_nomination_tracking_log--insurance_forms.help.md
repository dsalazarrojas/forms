# Insurance Provider Nomination Tracking Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track and manage the nomination process of insurance providers. It helps users to store and organize information about nominated providers in an organized and structured way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the provider details, including their name.
2. Enter the nomination date.
3. Select the nomination status from the provided options (Pending Review, Approved, or Rejected).
4. List the benefits team members involved in the nomination process.
5. Enter the agency name.
6. Enter the name of the person who reviewed the nomination.
7. Add any comments about the nomination.
8. Enter the vendor information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Provider Details
• **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the insurance provider being nominated.
### Nomination Date
• **Nomination Date**: Enter the date the nomination was made.
### Nomination Status
• **Nomination Status**: Select the current status of the nomination from the provided options:
  • **Pending Review**: The nomination is still under review.
  • **Approved**: The nomination has been approved.
  • **Rejected**: The nomination has been rejected.
### Benefits Team Member
• **Benefits Team Member**: List the benefits team members involved in the nomination process.
### Agency Name
• **Agency Name**: Enter the name of the agency associated with the nomination.
### Reviewer
• **Reviewer**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the nomination.
### Comments
• **Comments**: Add any comments or feedback about the nomination.
• **Notes**: A dedicated field for additional comments or feedback.
### Vendor
• **Vendor**: Enter the vendor information related to the nomination.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use the correct nomination status to track the progress of the nomination.
* List all benefits team members involved in the nomination process.
* Enter accurate agency and reviewer information.
* Use the comments field to add any additional feedback or comments about the nomination.
