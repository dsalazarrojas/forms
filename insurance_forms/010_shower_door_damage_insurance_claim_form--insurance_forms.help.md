# Shower Door Damage Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for an insurance claim related to damage caused by a shower door. Fill out the form to provide details about the claimant, the type of damage, and other relevant information to process the claim.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "User Input" field with any additional information about the claim.
2. Enter the claimant's details in the "Claimant Information" field.
3. Select the type of damage from "Claim Type" dropdown list.
4. Provide a description of the damage in the "Claim Details" field.
5. Enter the claimant's property address in the "Property Address" field.
6. Select the date of the claim from the "Claim Date" field.
7. Enter the claimant's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
8. If applicable, enter the claimant's policy number in the "Policy Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: A text field for general information about the claim.
* **Claimant Information**: A text field for the claimant's details.
* **Claim Type**: Select the type of damage that occurred (Water damage, Glass breakage, or Other).
* **Claim Details**: A field for providing a description of the damage.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property where the damage occurred.
* **Claim Date**: Select the date the damage occurred.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the claimant's contact number.
* **Policy Number**: If the claimant has a policy, enter the policy number.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the claim can be processed correctly.
- If unsure about any field, please contact the insurance company for assistance.
- Double-check the information provided before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
