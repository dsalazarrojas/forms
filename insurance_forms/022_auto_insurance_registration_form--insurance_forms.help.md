# Auto Insurance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from policyholders and their vehicle owners to process their insurance policy. It is typically completed by policyholders who wish to enroll in or change their insurance coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your policyholder information, including your full legal name, date of birth, driver's license ID, email address, phone number, and any other required fields.
2. Verify the vehicle registration information, including VIN, make, model, and year.
3. Select your desired effective date for coverage to begin.
4. Choose your preferred payment frequency and communication method for policy documents.
5. Agree to the policy terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policyholder Name** (`Policyholder Name`, text, required): Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Driver's License ID** (`Driver's License ID`, text, required): Enter your state driver's license number.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number for contact.
* **VIN** (`VIN`, text, required): Enter the 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number for your vehicle.
* **Make** (`Make`, text, required): Enter the make of your vehicle, e.g., Ford.
* **Model** (`Model`, text, required): Enter the model of your vehicle, e.g., Explorer.
* **Year** (`Year`, number, required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **License Plate Number** (`License Plate Number`, text, optional): If your vehicle has a license plate number, enter it here.
* **Desired Effective Date** (`Desired Effective Date`, date, required): Select the date you would like your coverage to begin.
* **Payment Frequency** (`Payment Frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to make payments.
* **Communication Preference** (`Communication Preference`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to receive policy documents.
* **Agree to Policy Terms** (`Agree to Policy Terms`, select_one, required): Confirm that all provided information is accurate and you agree to the policy terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before submission to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
- Ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
- Review and agree to the policy terms and conditions.
