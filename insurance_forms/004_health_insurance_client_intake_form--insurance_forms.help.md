# Health Insurance Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from clients seeking health insurance. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your insurance needs are assessed and managed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal details (First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Email Address, Phone Number, Client Type, and Address) accurately and truthfully.
2. Provide the necessary insurance information (Insurance Provider, Policy Details, Policy Number, Policy Start Date, and Policy End Date).
3. Fill out the payment details (Payment Method, Payment Date, Payment Status, and Payment Amount) if applicable.
4. Add any additional client notes or policy notes if required.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your identity and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Client Type** (`client_type`, text, required/optional): Select the type of client (e.g., individual, family, business).
- **Policy Details** (`policy_details`, text, required/optional): Describe the policy you are seeking or have.
- **Address Line 1** (`address_line1`, text, required/optional): Enter your address line 1 as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Address Line 2** (`address_line2`, text, required/optional): Enter your address line 2 as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required/optional): Enter your postal code as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your insurance provider.
- **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, text, required/optional): Enter the type of insurance you seek or have.
- **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your policy number as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your policy in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Policy End Date** (`policy_end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your policy in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, text, required/optional): Describe the status of your policy (e.g., active, inactive, pending).
- **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your client.
- **Broker Name** (`broker_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your broker or insurance broker.
- **Broker License Number** (`broker_license_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the broker's license number as it appears on their identification documents.
- **Policy Notes** (`policy_notes`, text, required/optional): Describe any additional details or notes about your policy.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text, required/optional): Select the payment method used (e.g., cash, credit card).
- **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of payment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, text, required/optional): Describe the status of your payment (e.g., paid, pending, outstanding).
- **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, text, required/optional): Enter the amount paid for your policy.
- **Client Signature** (`signature`, time, required/optional): Sign and date this form to confirm your identity and agreement.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to this help guide for clarification.
* If you need assistance or have questions, contact your insurance provider or broker for guidance.
