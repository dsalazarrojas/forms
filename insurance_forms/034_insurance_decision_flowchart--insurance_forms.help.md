# Insurance Decision Flowchart - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Decision Flowchart is a tool used to determine the type of insurance coverage that best suits an individual's needs based on their risk factors and preferences. This form helps users assess their risk factors, determine their risk tolerance, and choose the most suitable type of insurance coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For questions with multiple options, select the answer that best applies to you.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details_first_name`, `contact_details_last_name`, `contact_details_email`, `contact_details_phone`):
	+ Enter your first name.
	+ Enter your last name.
	+ Enter your email address.
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors_0`, `risk_factors_1`, `risk_factors_2`, `risk_factors_3`, `risk_factors_4`, `risk_factors_5`, `risk_factors_6`):
	+ For each question, select 'Yes' or 'No' based on your medical conditions, smoking habits, and other risk factors.
* **Risk Preferences** (`risk_preferences_0`, `risk_preferences_1`, `risk_preferences_2`, `risk_preferences_3`, `risk_preferences_4`, `risk_preferences_5`):
	+ Select your risk tolerance for each question: Low, Medium, or High.
* **Coverage Preferences** (`coverage_preferences_0`, `coverage_preferences_1`, `coverage_preferences_2`):
	+ Select the type of insurance coverage you prefer: Life, Health, Auto, Home, Auto and Home, or Other (please specify).
* **Confirm and Sign** (`confirm_and_sign`):
	+ Review your answers and confirm that they are accurate.
	+ Sign your name to complete the form.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by one person at a time. If you need to complete the form for multiple individuals, please submit the form separately for each person.
