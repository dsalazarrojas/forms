# Car Insurance Accident Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about a car accident involving your vehicle. It will help your insurance provider understand the circumstances of the accident and process your claim efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Provide accurate and detailed information about your policy and vehicle.
3. Be as thorough as possible when describing the accident, including any supporting evidence you may have.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policy Number**: Enter your insurance policy number as it appears on your policy documents.
* **Policyholder Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your policy.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for claim communications.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for claim communications.
* **Accident Date**: Enter the date the accident occurred.
* **Time of Accident**: Enter the time the accident occurred.
* **Location of Accident**: Enter the street address or intersection where the accident happened.
* **City**: Enter the city where the accident occurred.
* **State**: Enter the state where the accident occurred.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the ZIP code of the accident location.
* **Weather Conditions**: Select the weather conditions on the day of the accident.
* **Road Conditions**: Select the road conditions at the time of the accident.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the manufacturer of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model name of your vehicle.
* **VIN**: Enter your vehicle's identification number.
* **License Plate Number**: Enter your vehicle's license plate number.
* **Vehicle Damage Description**: Describe the damage to your vehicle.
* **Other Party Information**: If there were other parties involved, provide details about them.
* **Other Driver Name**: Enter the full name of the other driver.
* **Other Driver Phone**: Enter the contact number of the other driver.
* **Other Driver Insurance**: Enter the name of the other driver's insurance company.
* **Other Driver Policy Number**: Enter the other driver's policy number.
* **Other Vehicle Information**: Enter the year, make, model, and license plate number of the other vehicle.
* **Witness Information**: If there were witnesses, select "True" and enter their names and contact information.
* **Witness Details**: If there were witnesses, enter any relevant details about them.
* **Police Report**: If a police report was filed, select "True" and enter the report number.
* **Police Department**: Enter the police department that responded.
* **Injuries**: Select whether there were any injuries.
* **Injury Description**: If there were injuries, describe them.
* **Accident Description**: Describe how the accident occurred.
* **Photos Available**: Select whether you have photos of the accident.
* **Form Submission Date**: This field will automatically be filled out with the current date.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have all necessary supporting evidence, such as photos or police reports.
* If you're unsure about any field or require clarification, contact your insurance provider for assistance.
