# FHD Agency Overview Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The FHD Agency Overview Assessment form is designed to gather information about an agency, providing a comprehensive overview of its details. This form is intended to be completed by someone with authority level responsibilities within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the agency name and other relevant details in the provided text fields.
2. If applicable, enter the due date and time for the assessment.
3. Select the relevant options for the agency's contact information, such as title and position.
4. Provide any additional information about the agency in the text fields for agency description and agency services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agency Name**: Enter the name of the agency being assessed.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the agency.
* **Agency Address**: Enter the physical address of the agency.
* **Agency Phone**: Enter the phone number of the agency.
* **Agency Email**: Enter the email address of the agency.
* **Assessment Due Date**: Enter the date by which the assessment is due.
* **Assessment Due Time**: Enter the time by which the assessment is due.
* **Assessment Completed Date**: Enter the date on which the assessment was completed.
* **Assessment Completed Time**: Enter the time on which the assessment was completed.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the assessment.
* **Agency Type**: Enter the type of agency (e.g., government, non-profit, private).
* **Agency Size**: Enter the size of the agency (e.g., small, medium, large).
* **Agency Experience**: Enter any relevant experience of the agency.
* **Agency Industry**: Enter the industry in which the agency operates.
* **Agency Services**: Enter any services offered by the agency.
* **Agency Description**: Enter a brief description of the agency.
* **Agency License**: Enter the license type or number of the agency.
* **Agency Status**: Enter the current status of the agency.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact E-Mail**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Title**: Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Contact Role**: Enter the role of the contact person.
* **Contact Position**: Enter the position of the contact person.
* **Contact Address**: Enter the address of the contact person.
* **Agency License Expiration**: Enter the expiration date of the agency license.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about the agency.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary fields have been completed.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant personnel for clarification.
