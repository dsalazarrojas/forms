# Health Insurance Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect health insurance information from customers or clients. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your insurance needs are properly assessed.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out each page of the form sequentially.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out. Required fields are marked as such in the field descriptions.
- Use the correct format for date fields (DD/MM/YYYY).
- Use a valid email address for the email field.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask the insurance representative for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address if you have one to share.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple prophets from the list (though this field is optional).
* **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of insurance you are interested in.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, optional): Enter your policy number if you have one.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your insurance needs.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Select the category for your insurance inquiry.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you have a specific person assigned to handle your case, enter their name.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): You will receive an output file with your insurance information after submission.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for errors before submission.
- Ensure all fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- If you have any further questions, please contact the insurance representative.
