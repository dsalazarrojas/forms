# insurance_policy_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users about their insurance policies. It is intended to be used for verifying existing policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user input in the "User Input" field.
2. Provide information about your policy in the "Policy Details" field.
3. Select the status of your policy in the "Policy Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`text`, optional): This field is for you to input any relevant information about yourself or your policy. You can add any details that you think are important for the insurance company to verify your policy.
* **Policy Details** (`text`, optional): Please provide any relevant details about your policy, such as the policy number, policyholder's name, or other relevant information.
* **Policy Status** (`text`, optional): Select the status of your policy from the following options: active, inactive, pending, or cancelled. Please select the correct status of your policy according to your understanding of the policy's current status.
