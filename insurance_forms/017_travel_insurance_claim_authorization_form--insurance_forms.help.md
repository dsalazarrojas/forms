# travel_insurance_claim_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a travel insurance claim. Please complete it carefully to ensure that your claim is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the form with your personal details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Describe the incident that resulted in your claim, including the date, location, and any relevant details.
3. Specify the claimant's travel details, including the type of travel and any relevant documentation.
4. Select the relevant policy provider and policy number.
5. Complete any additional fields as required by your claimant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Describe the incident that resulted in your claim in detail.
* **Claimant Details** (`claimant_details`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about the claimant, such as their address or contact information.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): Specify the location of the incident.
* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the claimant.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Claimant Contact Info** (`claimant_contact_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional contact information for the claimant.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, required): Enter the policy number for the insurance policy related to this claim.
* **Policy Provider** (`policy_provider`, text, required): Specify the provider of the insurance policy.
* **Claim Description** (`claim_description`, text, required): Describe the claim you are submitting.
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you are claiming.
* **Claim Submitted By** (`claim_submitted_by`, text, required): Enter your name as the submitter of the claim.
* **Claim Submitted On** (`claim_submitted_on`, date, required): Enter the date on which the claim was submitted.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of your claim from the options provided.
* **Claim Comments** (`claim_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the claim.
* **Insurance Type** (`insurance_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of insurance policy that applies to this claim.
* **Policy Coverage** (`policy_coverage`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of coverage that applies to this claim.
* **Claimant Traveler Details** (`claimant_traveler_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the claimant's travel.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of incident that resulted in your claim.
* **Claimant Flight Details** (`claimant_flight_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional flight details related to the claim.
* **Claimant Travel Document** (`claimant_travel_document`, text, optional): Provide any additional travel documents related to the claim.
* **Claimant Travel Agent** (`claimant_travel_agent`, text, optional): Provide any additional travel agent information related to the claim.
* **Claimant Travel Agency** (`claimant_travel_agency`, text, optional): Provide any additional travel agency information related to the claim.
* **Claimant Travel Agreement** (`claimant_travel_agreement`, text, optional): Provide any additional travel agreement information related to the claim.
