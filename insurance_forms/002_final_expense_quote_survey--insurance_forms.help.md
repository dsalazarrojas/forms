# Final Expense Quote Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Final Expense Quote Survey is a form used to gather information from clients to provide a quote for their final expenses. This form is used by our insurance agency to understand the client's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have been directed by an internal team member or have a genuine use case for the final expense quote survey.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
3. Complete each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (optional): This is a note field where you can enter any additional information relevant to the client's quote.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name as they would like it to appear on their quote.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address. This will help us contact them with any updates or additional information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number. This will help us contact them with any updates or additional information.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the client's date of birth. This will help us understand their age for the quote purposes.

Note: The fields marked as optional can be left blank if not applicable or relevant to the client's quote.
