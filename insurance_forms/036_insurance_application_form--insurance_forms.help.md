# insurance_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for insurance applications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your User Information.
2. Provide your Contact Information.
3. Select the type of insurance you are applying for.
4. Enter the coverage dates (start and end).
5. Provide the Policy Holder's Information.
6. Specify the policy holder's contact details.
7. Check the policy status and term.
8. Select the coverage type.
9. Confirm the policy holder's ID.
10. Optionally, select the premium frequency and provide any additional coverage notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (Required)
	+ A field for you to enter your name.
* **Contact Information** (Required)
	+ A field for you to enter your contact method (phone, email, or mail).
* **Select Insurance Type** (Required)
	+ A dropdown menu to select the type of insurance you are applying for (Life, Health, Auto, Home, or Other).
* **Coverage Date** (Required)
	+ A field for you to enter the coverage start and end dates.
* **Contact Method** (Optional)
	+ A dropdown menu to select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or mail).
* **Contact Person** (Optional)
	+ A field for you to enter the contact person's name.
* **Policy Holder Information** (Required)
	+ A field for you to enter the policy holder's details.
* **Coverage Start** (Required)
	+ A field for you to enter the coverage start date.
* **Coverage End** (Required)
	+ A field for you to enter the coverage end date.
* **Policy Holder Contact Information** (Required)
	+ A field for you to enter the policy holder's contact details.
* **Policy Holder Contact** (Optional)
	+ A field for you to enter any additional contact details.
* **Policy Holder Email** (Optional)
	+ A field for you to enter the policy holder's email address.
* **Policy Holder Address** (Optional)
	+ A field for you to enter the policy holder's address.
* **Premium Frequency** (Optional)
	+ A dropdown menu to select the frequency of premium payments (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Policy Status** (Required)
	+ A dropdown menu to select the policy status (Active or Inactive).
* **Policy Term** (Required)
	+ A dropdown menu to select the policy term (Month, Month-Year, or Year).
* **Policy Holder ID** (Required)
	+ A field for you to enter the policy holder's ID.
* **Coverage Type** (Required)
	+ A dropdown menu to select whether coverage is yes or no.
* **Coverage Term** (Required)
	+ A field for you to enter the coverage term.
* **Policy Start Date** (Required)
	+ A field for you to enter the policy start date.
* **Policy End Date** (Required)
	+ A field for you to enter the policy end date.
* **Premium Frequency** (Optional)
	+ A dropdown menu to select the frequency of premium payments (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Coverage Start Date** (Optional)
	+ A field for you to enter the coverage start date.
* **Coverage End Date** (Optional)
	+ A field for you to enter the coverage end date.
* **Policy Holder Contact** (Optional)
	+ A field for you to enter any additional contact details.
* **Policy Holder Email** (Optional)
	+ A field for you to enter the policy holder's email address.
* **Policy Holder Address** (Optional)
	+ A field for you to enter the policy holder's address.

Note: Fields with a "Required" label in bold are required to be filled out.
