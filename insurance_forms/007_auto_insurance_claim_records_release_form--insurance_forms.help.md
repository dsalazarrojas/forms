# Auto Insurance Claim Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to authorize the release of your insurance claim records. It captures essential information about you and the records being released, ensuring that your identity is verified and the records are released to the correct parties.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all the required fields carefully.
2. Review each field's description and hint for more information.
3. If you're unsure about any field, contact your insurance provider for clarification.
4. Make sure to check the "Authorization Agreement" field to confirm your understanding of the form's purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your legal first name. For example, John.
* **Middle Name**: Optional middle name. Leave blank if you don't have a middle name.
* **Last Name**: Your legal last name. For example, Smith.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format. For example, 02-15-1990.
* **Social Security Number**: Your 9-digit Social Security Number in XXX-XX-XXXX format. For example, 123-45-6789.
* **Email Address**: Your primary email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your best contact number.
* **Street Address**: Your current address. Include your street number and name.
* **City**: Your city of residence.
* **State**: The state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code**: Your 5-digit ZIP code.
* **Insurance Company Name**: The name of your insurance provider.
* **Policy Number**: Your policy number with the insurance company.
* **Claim Number**: The claim number associated with your policy.
* **Date of Accident**: The date of the incident that led to the claim.
* **Records to be Released**: Select which records to release, such as the complete claim file, police report, medical records, repair estimates, payment records, correspondence, photos and evidence, or witness statements.
* **Release Records To**: Who should receive the records. You can choose yourself, your attorney, medical provider, repair shop, another insurance company, or government agency.
* **Recipient Name**: The name of the person or organization to receive the records.
* **Recipient Address**: The address where to send the records.
* **Purpose of Release**: The reason you're requesting the records. For personal records, legal proceedings, medical treatment, insurance claim, or employment.
* **Time Period for Records**: The time period you want the records to cover. For example, last 6 months, last 1 year, last 3 years, last 5 years, or complete history.
* **Authorization Expiration**: When the authorization should expire. Choose 30 days from signing, 60 days from signing, 90 days from signing, 6 months from signing, 1 year from signing, or no expiration.
* **Additional Authorizations**: Any special instructions or clarifications.
* **Date of Signature**: The date you sign this form.
* **Witness Name**: Optional witness name.
* **Authorization Agreement**: Confirm that you understand the form's purpose and agree to the release of your records. Choose "I Agree and Authorize" to confirm.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill out all required fields correctly.
* Review each field's description and hint for more information.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your insurance provider for clarification.
* Keep a record of who received this form and who authorized its completion.
* This form is not intended for multiple uses. It's a one-time authorization for records release. 

Please review the instructions carefully before filling out the form to ensure you complete it correctly. 

Please note: The information provided here is intended for guidance purposes only and is specific to this form. If you need further clarification, please contact your insurance provider. 

Please sign this form carefully, as it is your official authorization for records release.
