# Property Damage Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report any damage that occurs to company property. It helps to document and track such incidents, facilitating communication and resolution processes with relevant stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Provide a detailed description of the damage in the "Damage Description" field.
3. Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Damage Date" and "Damage Time" fields, respectively.
4. Report the value of the damaged property in the "Property Value" field.
5. Indicate the type of damage in the "Damage Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information for further communication and follow-up.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact details for the relevant stakeholders to get in touch with you.
* **Damage Description** (`damage_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the damage, including any relevant details that might help in resolving the incident.
* **Damage Date** (`damage_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the damage occurred.
* **Damage Time** (`damage_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the damage incident. If not known, leave blank.
* **Property Value** (`property_value`, number, optional): Report the monetary value of the damaged property.
* **Damage Type** (`damage_type`, text, optional): Indicate the type of damage (e.g., physical, electrical, etc.). If not known, leave blank.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If unsure about any field, ask a supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* Attach any supporting documentation or photos to the form to help with the resolution process.
