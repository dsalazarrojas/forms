# Financial Insurance Application Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Insurance Application Request Form is a template designed to collect necessary information for a financial insurance application. This form helps to gather personal and financial details that will be reviewed by the finance department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your user information (page 1) with your personal details.
2. Provide loan details (page 2) if applicable.
3. Enter your credit score (page 3).
4. Select your financial institution (page 4) from the available options.
5. Enter your address (page 5) if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (Page 1, User Information): This section is where you can enter your name, phone number, and email address. Please fill out this information accurately.
* **Loan Details** (Page 2, Loan Details): If you're applying for a loan, fill out this section with the loan amount, interest rate, and loan duration. If you're not applying for a loan, you can leave this section blank.
* **Credit Score** (Page 3, Credit Score): Enter your current credit score (e.g., 700-850) to help us assess your creditworthiness.
* **Financial Institution** (Page 4, Financial Institution): Choose one of the available financial institutions (e.g., Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Chase) that you're associated with.
* **Address** (Page 5, Address): Enter your current address if applicable. This will help us verify your identity.
