# Event Insurance Partner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Insurance Partner Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from insurance partners to ensure they are properly registered for events. This includes providing contact details, event insurance information, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Insurance Partner Information page with your contact details.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date event insurance information on the Insurance Partner Details page.
3. Review and submit the form to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Insurance Partner Information (Page 1)** (`name`, `type: text`, required: false): Please fill out your name and contact information, including your email and phone number. This will help us get in touch with you for any queries or updates.
* **Insurance Partner Details (Page 2)** (`name`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide accurate information about your insurance company, including your insurance type or policy details. This will help us verify your insurance coverage and ensure a smooth event experience.
* **Submit (Page 3)** (`name`, `type: text`, required: false): Review and submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out this form only for events that have been explicitly agreed upon with the event organizers.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact <form@gic.mx>
