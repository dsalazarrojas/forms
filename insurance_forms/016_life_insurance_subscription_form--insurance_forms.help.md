# life_insurance_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information and insurance details for life insurance subscriptions.

## How To Complete This Form
This form has several sections that you need to fill in order to complete it. Please start by selecting the contact information section and filling in your contact details. Then, move to the insurance details section and select the type of life insurance you are interested in (Individual or Group). After that, select the policy type and policy duration. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Fill in your contact details, such as your name and address.
* **Insurance Details** (`insurance_details`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of life insurance you are interested in (Individual or Group).
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of policy you want to subscribe to (Term or Whole).
* **Policy Duration** (`policy_duration`, `date`, optional): Enter the duration of the policy.
* **Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of the policy.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `select_one`, optional): Select the contact person responsible for the policy.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Fill in your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Fill in your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Fill in your address.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the policy.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (none required by default, but please ensure all fields are completed as per your needs).
* Use a valid email address for the Email field.
* The Policy Duration field should be in the correct date format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* The Start Date field should be in the correct date format (yyyy-mm-dd).
