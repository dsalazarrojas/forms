Insurance Email Signature Form - Help Guide
=====================================

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employee information for insurance purposes. It is intended for use by employees and their managers to provide accurate and up-to-date information for insurance-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Name, which is a required field.
2. Enter your Employee Email, which is also a required field.
3. Select your Job Title from the provided options.
4. Enter your Manager Name, which is a required field.
5. Enter your Manager Email, which is also a required field.
6. Select your Department from the provided options. Note that this is not a required field.
7. Select your Insurance Carrier from the provided options. This is a required field.
8. Enter your Onboarding Date, which is a required field. Please format this date as MM/DD/YYYY.
9. You may optionally select your Rebrand Date. If you select this, enter the date as MM/DD/YYYY.
10. Choose how you would like to contact us for updates from the provided options. This is a required field.
11. Finally, provide any additional information in the Note field, which is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (text, required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee Email (email, required)**: Enter your email address that we will use to contact you.
* **Job Title (text, required)**: Select your job title from the provided options.
* **Manager Name (text, required)**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email (email, required)**: Enter your manager's email address that we will use to contact them.
* **Department (select multiple, optional)**: Select your department from the provided options. This helps us understand your role within the company.
* **Insurance Carrier (select one, required)**: Select your insurance carrier from the provided options.
* **Onboarding Date (date, required)**: Enter the date you were on boarded. Format this date as MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Rebrand Date (date, optional)**: Enter the date you were rebranded. If you select this, format this date as MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Info Update (select one, required)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted for updates.
* **Note (note, optional)**: Provide any additional information you would like to include with your submission.
