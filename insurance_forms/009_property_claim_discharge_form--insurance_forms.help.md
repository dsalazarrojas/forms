# Property Claim Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information related to a property claim discharge, which is a process where an insurance company formally closes a claim, releasing the policyholder from further liability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the policyholder's name in the `insured-name` field.
2. Enter the claim number in the `claim-number` field.
3. Select the current claim status from the `claim-status` dropdown menu.
4. Enter the date of the claim in the `claim-date` field.
5. Select the insurance company from the `insurance-company` dropdown menu.
6. Select the reason for discharge from the `reason-for-discharge` dropdown menu.
7. Check the box to confirm that the agreement was signed by the policyholder.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Claim Discharge Form**: This is the form title, which is automatically populated based on the form's metadata.
* **insured-name**: Enter the policyholder's full name as it appears on the policy contract or insurance card.
* **claim-number**: Enter the unique number assigned to the claim by the insurance company.
* **claim-status**: Select the current status of the claim from the dropdown menu.
	+ Claim Open: The claim is still open and active.
	+ Claim Closed: The claim has been formally closed.
	+ Claim Discharged: The claim has been discharged, and the policyholder is released from liability.
	+ Claim Denied: The claim has been denied, and the policyholder is not liable.
* **claim-date**: Enter the date when the claim was initially filed or when it was formally closed.
* **insurance-company**: Select the insurance company from the dropdown menu.
* **reason-for-discharge**: Select the reason for discharge from the dropdown menu.
	+ Reason 1
	+ Reason 2
	+ Reason 3
* **agreement-signed**: Check the box if the policyholder has signed the agreement to formally close the claim.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to the insurance company or agent.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of this form, please consult with a legal expert or the insurance company's regulatory department.
