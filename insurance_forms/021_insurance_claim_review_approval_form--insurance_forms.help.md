# Insurance Claim Review Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information necessary for the review and approval of an insurance claim.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the policyholder's information, including their name and contact number.
2.  Provide details about the claim, including the policy number, insurance provider, and incident date and time.
3.  Indicate whether attachments are required or not.
4.  Complete the form with accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth review and approval process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policyholder Information** (`policyholder_information`, `text`, required): Enter the policyholder's full name and contact number.
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the claim, including any relevant supporting documentation.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether any attachments, such as receipts or photos, are required for the review process.
* **Insurance Claim Reviewer** (`insurance_claim_reviewer`, `text`, required): Enter the name and contact information of the insurance claim reviewer.
* **Date Of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Time Of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, required): Enter the name and contact information of the insurance provider.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, `text`, required): Enter the policy number for the claim.
* **Policyholder Name** (`policyholder_name`, `text`, required): Enter the policyholder's name.
* **Policyholder Contact Number** (`policyholder_contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the policyholder's contact number.
* **Insurance Claim Status** (`insurance_claim_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the claim (Active or Inactive).
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date for the review (if different from the incident date).
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any comments or additional information about the review process.
