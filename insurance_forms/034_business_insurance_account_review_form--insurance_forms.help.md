# Business Insurance Account Review Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Insurance Account Review Form is designed to gather information about a 
business's insurance account, including their current coverage types, risk profile, 
and satisfaction with their current premiums. This information will help identify 
potential areas for improvement or adjustments to their policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please ensure that you have the following information 
available:
* The business's name
* The primary policy number (e.g. GL-123456789)
* A list of their current coverage types (e.g. General Liability, Professional 
  Liability (E&O), etc.)
* A description of any recent business changes (e.g. new location, staff hiring, 
  etc.)
* A brief description of any claims filed in the last 12 months
* Your satisfaction with your current premiums (rate from 1 (Very Dissatisfied) 
  to 5 (Very Satisfied))
* Your interest in additional coverage types (e.g. expanding or changing coverage)
* The status of your recent risk assessment (e.g. completed within last 6 months, 
  etc.)
* Any recommended policy adjustments (e.g. limits, deductibles, etc.)
* The target date for your next comprehensive review

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (Client Legal Name)**
  + Label: Client Name
  + Type: Text
  + Required: Yes
  + Explanation: Enter the name of the insured business.

* **Primary Policy Number**
  + Label: Primary Policy Number
  + Type: Text
  + Required: Yes
  + Explanation: Enter the primary policy number (e.g. GL-123456789).

* **Current Coverage Types**
  + Label: Current Coverage Types
  + Type: Select Multiple
  + Required: Yes
  + Explanation: Select all policies currently held with us (e.g. General Liability, 
    Professional Liability (E&O), etc.).

* **Recent Business Changes**
  + Label: Recent Business Changes (Last 12 Months)
  + Type: Select Multiple
  + Required: Yes
  + Explanation: Select any factors affecting your risk profile (e.g. new location, 
    significant increase in revenue, major staff hiring, etc.).

* **Claim History 12m Summary**
  + Label: Claim History in Last 12 Months
  + Type: Text
  + Required: Yes
  + Explanation: Briefly describe any claims filed in the last 12 months.

* **Satisfaction with Current Premiums**
  + Label: Satisfaction with Current Premiums
  + Type: Number
  + Required: Yes
  + Explanation: Rate from 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied) your 
    satisfaction with current premiums.

* **Interest in Additional Coverage**
  + Label: Interest in Additional Coverage
  + Type: Select One
  + Required: Yes
  + Explanation: Indicate if you feel underinsured in any areas (Yes, Maybe, 
    No).

* **Risk Assessment Update**
  + Label: Risk Assessment Update
  + Type: Select One
  + Required: Yes
  + Explanation: Has a formal risk audit been completed recently (Completed within last 
    6 months, completed 6-12 months ago, etc.)?

* **Recommended Policy Adjustments**
  + Label: Recommended Policy Adjustments
  + Type: Text
  + Required: Yes
  + Explanation: Note any specific changes to limits or deductibles.

* **Next Scheduled Review Date**
  + Label: Next Scheduled Review Date
  + Type: Date
  + Required: Yes
  + Explanation: Enter the target date for your next comprehensive review.

* **Reviewing Agent Signature**
  + Label: Reviewing Agent Signature
  + Type: Text
  + Required: Yes
  + Explanation: Type your full name to certify the account review.

* **Review Completion Date**
  + Label: Review Completion Date
  + Type: Date
  + Required: Yes
  + Explanation: Enter the date this review was completed.
