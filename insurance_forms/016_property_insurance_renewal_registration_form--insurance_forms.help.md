# Property Insurance Renewal Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is meant for users to provide information about their property insurance renewal registrations. It is not for creating new policies but rather for updating or renewing existing ones.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the necessary details about your property, policy, and policy holder.
2. Review and verify the information you provide to ensure it is accurate.
3. If necessary, use the alternative fields for any additional or specific information not captured by the primary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Details (property_insurance_renewal_registration_form_page_1)**
	+ This is the first page of the form where you will input information about your property.
* **Policy Start Date (policy_start_date)**
	+ This is the date when your policy started.
* **Policy End Date (policy_end_date)**
	+ This is the date when your policy ended or will end.
* **Policy Status (policy_status)**
	+ This is the status of your policy, which can be Active, Inactive, or Cancelled.
* **Policy Holder Name (policy_holder_name)**
	+ This is the name of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Email (policy_holder_email)**
	+ This is the email address of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Phone (policy_holder_phone)**
	+ This is the phone number of the policy holder.
* **Policy Coverage Type (policy_coverage_type)**
	+ This is the type of coverage your policy provides, which can be Home, Auto, or Business.
* **Policy Deductible (policy_deductible)**
	+ This is the amount you must pay out of your own pocket before the policy kicks in.
* **Policy Limit (policy_limit)**
	+ This is the maximum amount your policy will cover.
* **Policy Start Date Alt (policy_start_date_alt)**
	+ This is an alternative date for when your policy started.
* **Policy End Date Alt (policy_end_date_alt)**
	+ This is an alternative date for when your policy ended or will end.
* **Policy Status Alt (policy_status_alt)**
	+ This is an alternative status for your policy, which can be Active, Inactive, or Cancelled.
* **Policy Holder Name Alt (policy_holder_name_alt)**
	+ This is an alternative name for the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Email Alt (policy_holder_email_alt)**
	+ This is an alternative email address for the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Phone Alt (policy_holder_phone_alt)**
	+ This is an alternative phone number for the policy holder.
* **Policy Coverage Type Alt (policy_coverage_type_alt)**
	+ This is an alternative type of coverage your policy provides.
* **Policy Deductible Alt (policy_deductible_alt)**
	+ This is an alternative amount you must pay out of your own pocket before the policy kicks in.
* **Policy Limit Alt (policy_limit_alt)**
	+ This is an alternative maximum amount your policy will cover.
* **Policy Notes (policy_notes)**
	+ This is a note about your policy.
* **Property Insurance Policy Notes (property_insurance_policy_notes)**
	+ This is a note about your property insurance policy.
* **Property Address (property_address)**
	+ This is the address of the property covered by your policy.
* **Property Address Alt (property_address_alt)**
	+ This is an alternative address for the property covered by your policy.
* **Policy Notes Alt (policy_notes_alt)**
	+ This is an alternative note about your policy.
* **Property Insurance Notes Alt (property_insurance_notes_alt)**
	+ This is an alternative note about your property insurance policy.
