# Travel Insurance Decline Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Travel Insurance Decline Form is designed to collect information from users who are declining travel insurance. This form helps to understand the reasons behind the decline, ensuring that the decision is well-justified and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill out the "Reason for Declining Travel Insurance?" field with a brief explanation of why you are declining travel insurance.
3. If necessary, provide more details about your reason in the "Can you provide more details?" field.
4. Select the main reason for declining travel insurance in the "Main Reason for Decline" field.
5. If applicable, provide additional information about your reason in the "Additional Info" field.
6. Confirm that the information provided is correct by checking the "Is this correct?" checkbox.
7. Finally, provide a final reason for declining travel insurance in the "Final Reason for Decline" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reason for Declining Travel Insurance?** (`travel_insurance_decline_form_page_1`, text, required): This field is the main reason for declining travel insurance. Please provide a brief explanation of why you are declining travel insurance.
* **Can you provide more details?** (`decline_details`, text, optional): If the reason for declining travel insurance is not clear, provide more details about your reason in this field.
* **Main Reason for Decline** (`main_reason_decline`, text, required): Select the main reason for declining travel insurance from the options provided. This will help us understand the underlying reason for the decline.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info_decline`, text, optional): If you have additional information about your reason for declining travel insurance, please provide it in this field.
* **Confirm Decline Reason** (`confirm_decline`, checkbox, required): Confirm that the information provided is correct by checking this box.
* **Final Reason for Decline** (`final_decline_reason`, text, required): This field is the final reason for declining travel insurance. Please provide a clear and concise explanation.
* **Last Reason for Decline** (`last_reason_decline`, text, required): This field is the last reason for declining travel insurance. Please provide a clear and concise explanation.
* **Last Info about Decline** (`final_info_decline`, text, optional): If you have additional information about your reason for declining travel insurance, please provide it in this field.
