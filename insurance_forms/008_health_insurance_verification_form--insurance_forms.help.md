# Health Insurance Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Insurance Verification Form is designed to collect information from users about their health insurance coverage. This form helps us understand your insurance details and ensure that you have the correct coverage in place.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and fill in the required fields to ensure accurate information.
2. Enter your correct and up-to-date information to avoid any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (1)**: This section collects your basic contact information.
* **Insurance Type (2)**: Select your type of insurance from the dropdown options, such as Medicare, Medicaid, Private Insurance, or Self-Pay.
* **Insurance Number (3)**: Enter your unique insurance identification number.
* **Effective Date (4)**: Enter the start date of your insurance coverage.
* **Expiration Date (5)**: Enter the end date of your insurance coverage.
* **Coverage Start (6)**: Enter the start date of your insurance coverage period.
* **Coverage End (7)**: Enter the end date of your insurance coverage period.
* **Medical Record (8)**: This is a text field for you to enter any additional medical information related to your coverage.
* **User Relationship (9)**: Select how you are related to the secondary user or policy holder.
* **Phone (10)**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **Email (11)**: Enter your primary email address.
* **Address (12)**: Enter your primary address.
* **Policy Number (13)**: Enter your policy number.
* **Group ID (14)**: Enter your group ID number.
* **Group Name (15)**: Enter your group name.
* **Billing Name (16)**: Enter the name of the billing person or organization.
* **Billing Address (17)**: Enter the address of the billing person or organization.
* **Billing Phone (18)**: Enter the phone number of the billing person or organization.
* **Billing Email (19)**: Enter the email address of the billing person or organization.
* **Secondary User Name (20)**: Enter the name of the secondary user.
* **Secondary Phone (21)**: Enter the phone number of the secondary user.
* **Secondary Email (22)**: Enter the email address of the secondary user.
* **Policy Holder (23)**: Select whether you hold the policy or not.
* **Policy Holding Date (24)**: Enter the date you hold the policy.
* **Policy Holding Reason (25)**: Enter the reason why you hold the policy.
