# Insurance Plan Participant Information - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information about participants in an insurance plan, including demographics, contact details, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the demographic fields (First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, etc.) with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Enter your contact information (Email, Phone, Address, etc.).
3. Provide emergency contact details (Name, Relationship, Phone, etc.).
4. Select the relevant insurance plan(s) you are enrolled in.
5. Enter any additional insurance-related information (Policy Number, Enrollment Date, etc.).
6. Add any necessary notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Address Line 1** (Text): Enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (Text): Enter your address line 2.
* **Zip Code** (Number): Enter your postal code.
* **City** (Text): Enter your city or town.
* **State** (Select One): Select your state from the provided options.
* **Insurance Plan** (Select Multiple): Select the insurance plans you are enrolled in.
* **Enrollment Date** (Date): Enter your enrollment date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Text): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Email): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (Text): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Insurance Card ID** (Number): Enter your insurance card ID number.
* **Participant Signature** (Time): Date and time of participant's signature.
* **Insurance Signature** (Time): Date and time of insurance company's signature.
* **Signature Date** (Date): Date of the participant's signature.
* **Insurance Effective Date** (Date): Date the insurance plan becomes effective.
* **Insurance Expiration Date** (Date): Date the insurance plan expires.
* **Policy Number** (Text): Enter your policy number.
* **Notes** (Note): Add any additional comments or notes about your insurance information.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by participants, so please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your insurance representative or administrator.
