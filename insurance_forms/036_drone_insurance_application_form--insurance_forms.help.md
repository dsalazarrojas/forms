# Drone Insurance Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information necessary to determine your eligibility for drone insurance coverage. Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful application.

## How To Complete This Form

* Ensure you have all required information about your drone, such as its specifications, serial number, and any existing damage or issues.
* Provide clear and detailed information about your payment preferences, including your preferred method of payment and any relevant financial information.
* If you have any existing insurance coverage or policies, provide details about them in the relevant fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, required: false):
	+ This section is where you will provide your personal information, including your name, address, and contact details.
* **Drone Properties** (`drone_properties`, text, required: false):
	+ In this field, please describe your drone's specifications, including its make, model, serial number, and any unique features or modifications.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_information`, text, required: false):
	+ If you have existing insurance coverage or policies that may affect your eligibility for this application, provide details about them here.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_information`, text, required: false):
	+ This section is where you will provide your preferred method of payment and any relevant financial information.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all necessary documentation and information about your drone before submitting this form.
