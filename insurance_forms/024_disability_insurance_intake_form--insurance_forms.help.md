# Disability Insurance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended to collect necessary information from clients who are interested in or currently applying for disability insurance. The form is designed to gather relevant details about the client's medical status, insurance preferences, and contact information, which will facilitate efficient processing and communication.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and clear information.
2. For fields with no clear instructions, provide the best-possible information to the field's label.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Information (Page 1)

* **Client Information (Client Information)** (`text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description or details about the client.

### Medical Information (Page 1)

* **Medical Information (Medical Information)** (`text`, required: true): Please provide detailed and accurate medical information, such as any existing medical conditions, treatments, or relevant medical records.

### Insurance Information (Page 1)

* **Insurance Information (Insurance Information)** (`text`, required: false): Please provide any relevant information about the client's current or previous insurance coverage.

### Contact Information (Page 1)

* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`text`, required: false): Please provide the best contact method and details for communication with the client.
