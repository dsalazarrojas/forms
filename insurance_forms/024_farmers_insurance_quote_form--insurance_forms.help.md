# farmers_insurance_quote_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from farmers to provide them with an accurate insurance quote for their farm operations, property, or livestock.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field below.
2. Enter your Last Name in the next field.
3. Provide your Email address in the following field. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
4. Enter your Phone number in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false):
	+ A field for entering your first name.
	+ This field is not required, but providing a first name can help us tailor the quote to your personal preferences.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false):
	+ A field for entering your last name.
	+ This field is not required, but providing a last name can help us get a more accurate quote.
### Email
* **Email** (`<email>`, `email`, required: false):
	+ A field for entering your email address.
	+ Please use a valid email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
	+ This field is not required, but providing an email can help us get in touch with you with any quote details or updates.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`<phone_number>`, `text`, required: false):
	+ A field for entering your phone number.
	+ This field is not required, but providing a phone number can help us reach out to you with any further questions or updates.
## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us directly.
* We will get back to you with a quote as soon as possible.
