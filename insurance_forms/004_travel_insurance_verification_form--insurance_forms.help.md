# Travel Insurance Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and verify travel insurance policy information from customers. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the data is up-to-date and reliable.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have the necessary permissions to access and use this form.
2. Select the "Travel Insurance Info" page and fill out the policy details.
3. Move on to the "Traveler Info" page and provide the traveler's details.
4. Fill out the "Policy Terms" page, if applicable.
5. Enter the "Policy Number" page and provide the policy number, if available.
6. Finally, provide travel details on the "Travel Details" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Insurance Info (text, not required)**: This field is used to capture general details about the travel insurance policy.
* **Traveler Info (text, not required)**: Enter the traveler's name, contact number, and email address, if available.
* **Policy Terms (text, not required)**: If the policy has specific conditions or restrictions, please list them here.
* **Policy Number (text, not required)**: Enter the policy number, if provided by the insurance company.
* **Travel Details (text, not required)**: Describe the traveler's trip, including destination, dates, and any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy to ensure that the data is correct and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the insurance company for assistance.
* Please note that this form is for insurance company use only, and not for public submission or sharing.
