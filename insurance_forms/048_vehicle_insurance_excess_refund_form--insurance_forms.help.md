# Vehicle Insurance Excess Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to claim a vehicle insurance excess refund. It's essential to provide accurate information to help with the processing of your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Ensure all required fields are filled in with accurate information.
2. Review each field carefully and provide additional information if necessary.
3. Submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Vehicle Make** (optional): Select the make of your vehicle from the list. If your vehicle is not listed, please enter the make in the text field below.
* **Vehicle Year** (optional): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Insurance Provider** (optional): Select the insurance provider you have with from the list. If your insurance provider is not listed, please contact your provider for further assistance.
* **Excess Amount** (optional): Enter the excess amount you are claiming for.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (required): Enter your vehicle registration number as it appears on your vehicle's registration.
* **Date of Incident** (required): Enter the date of the incident, in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Reason for Claim** (required): Please enter a brief description of the reason for your claim.
* **Insurance Claim** (required): Please provide a detailed description of your insurance claim.
* **Vehicle Damage** (optional): Select the level of damage to your vehicle from the list. If your vehicle has suffered minor, moderate, or severe damage, select the relevant option.
* **Witness Details** (required): If there were witnesses to the incident, please provide their details.
* **Claimant Details** (optional): If you are claiming on behalf of someone else, please select the relevant option.
* **Supporting Documents** (required): Please attach any supporting documents relevant to your claim.
* **Vehicle Insurance Excess** (required): Enter the amount you are claiming for your vehicle insurance excess.
* **Vehicle Insurance Excess Reason** (required): Please enter a brief description of why you are claiming your vehicle insurance excess.

Please note that all fields are required unless otherwise indicated as optional.
