# personal_accident_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from claimants about an accident they have experienced.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name and phone number.
2. Enter the date of the accident, using the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Describe the location where the accident occurred.
4. Provide the name and contact information of the person who can be contacted about the accident.
5. Describe the injury you sustained during the accident.
6. Provide a detailed description of the accident, including any relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Information** (`claimant_information`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name and contact information.
* **Accident Date** (`accident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the accident, using the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Describe the location where the accident occurred.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Provide the name and contact information of the person who can be contacted about the accident.
* **Injury Description** (`injury_description`, text, required/optional): Describe the injury you sustained during the accident.
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the accident, including any relevant details.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any supporting documentation, such as photos or medical records, be sure to attach them to the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the person responsible for handling claims.
