# life_insurance_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The life_insurance_application_form is used to collect demographic and contact information for life insurance applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with the requested information. You can start by filling out the first page, which includes the following fields:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is listed on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is listed on your identification.
* **Birthday** (`birthday`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yy.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as it is listed on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as it is listed on your identification.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your home or work address as it is listed on your identification.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional): Enter your occupation or job title as it is listed on your identification.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and completely, as this information will be used to determine insurance eligibility and coverage.
