# Health Insurance Claim Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Claim Monitoring Form is used to track and monitor the status of health insurance claims. This form helps users to submit information about their claims, which is useful for insurance companies to keep track of claims and provide timely resolutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the claim details in the **Claim Details** field (Page 1).
2. Provide information about the **Claimant Info** (Page 2).
3. Specify the **Healthcare Provider** involved (Page 3).
4. Choose the **Claim Status** from the options provided in the **Claim Status** field (Page 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your claim, including any relevant dates, amounts, or other details.
* **Claimant Info** (`claimant_info`, `text`, optional): This field is meant to capture information about the individual who is submitting the claim. Please enter your name and contact information so that we can get in touch with you about your claim.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, `text`, optional): If your healthcare provider is involved in the treatment, please enter their name and contact information so we can follow up with them if needed.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following claim status options:
	+ **Claim Open**: This means the claim is currently being reviewed by the insurance company.
	+ **In Progress**: The claim is being processed by the insurance company.
	+ **Closed**: The claim has been resolved and you should receive a resolution letter soon.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields for accurate claim processing.
* If you have any supporting documents or evidence for your claim, please attach them separately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
