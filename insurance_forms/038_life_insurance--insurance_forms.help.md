# Life Insurance - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Insurance form is used to collect information for the application of a life insurance policy. This form is designed to gather essential details about the policy holder, including contact information and policy specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you are filling out this form for the purpose of applying for a life insurance policy.
2. Enter your policy number in the "Policy Number" field (if applicable).
3. Provide your name in the "Policy Holder" field.
4. Enter your birth date in the "Birth Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field in the format [email@example.com](mailto:email@example.com).
8. Enter your age in the "Policy Holder Age" field.
9. Enter the date the policy is to start in the "Policy Start Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your policy number if you have one. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Policy Holder** (`policy_holder`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name as it appears on your insurance card or identification.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your current address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address in the format [email@example.com](mailto:email@example.com).
* **Policy Holder Age** (`policy_holder_age`, `number`, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the policy is to start in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
