# BHAVESHDAS VAISHNAV Curriculum Vitae - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive profile creation tool for BHAVESHDAS VAISHNAV, designed to capture essential information about a candidate's professional background, skills, and preferences. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to help you complete it accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To get the most out of this form, please follow these general steps:

1. Fill out the **Full Name** field with your name as it appears on official documents.
2. Enter your valid **Email Address** that is associated with your personal or professional profile.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
4. Enter your current **Location** (city and country).
5. Write a brief **Professional Summary** that showcases your career experience and relevant skills.
6. Enter your **Highest Qualification**, including the degree and field of study.
7. Specify your **Total Years of Professional Experience**.
8. Optionally, list your **Current or Most Recent Job Title** and **Employment History** for context.
9. List your **Key Skills**, **Technical Skills**, and any **Languages Spoken** you're proficient in.
10. If applicable, include any **Professional Certifications or Licenses**.
11. Provide the URL of your **LinkedIn Profile** (if applicable).
12. Link to your **Portfolio or Website** (if applicable).
13. Optionally, specify your **Desired Position or Role**.
14. Mention your **Salary Expectations** (if applicable).
15. Choose your **Availability to Start**, using the date format (e.g., "January 1, 2024").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents (e.g., passport, ID card).
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address associated with your personal or professional profile. This should be the one used by your current or former employer, or the one you use for professional communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes, including potential employers or colleagues.
* **Current Location**: Enter your current city and country.
* **Professional Summary**: A brief overview of your career experience and relevant skills (max 200-250 words).
* **Highest Qualification**: Enter your degree and field of study.
* **Total Years of Professional Experience**: Enter the number of years of experience.
* **Current or Most Recent Job Title**: Optional. If you're currently employed, enter your current job title. If you've recently changed or left a job, enter your most recent job title.
* **Employment History**: Optional. List previous positions and companies for context.
* **Key Skills**: List your main professional skills.
* **Technical Skills**: List software, tools, or programming languages you're proficient in.
* **Languages Spoken**: List languages and proficiency levels.
* **Professional Certifications or Licenses**: Optional. If applicable, list any certifications or licenses you hold.
* **LinkedIn Profile URL**: Optional. If you have a LinkedIn profile, provide the URL.
* **Portfolio or Website**: Optional. Link to your portfolio or website for showcase.
* **Desired Position or Role**: Optional. Specify the type of role or position you're looking for.
* **Salary Expectations**: Optional. Enter your expected compensation range.
* **Availability to Start**: Choose your availability to start, using the date format (e.g., "January 1, 2024").
