# Provider Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Provider Survey form is a tool used to collect information from health care providers about their services, contact details, and other relevant data. This form is essential for maintaining up-to-date records and facilitating communication between our organization and the providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Provider Survey form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the provider before starting the form.
2. Enter the provider's name in the **Provider Name** field.
3. Select the provider type from the dropdown menu in the **Provider Type** field.
4. Enter the contact number in the **Contact Number** field.
5. Enter the provider's email address in the **Email** field.
6. Enter the provider's address in the **Address 1** and **Address 2** fields.
7. Enter the provider's zip code in the **Zip** field.
8. Enter the provider's fax number in the **Fax** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Provider Type** (`provider_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of healthcare provider from the dropdown menu:
	+ Healthcare
	+ Service
	+ Other
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the contact number of the provider.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the provider.
* **Address 1** (`address_1`, text, required: false): Enter the street address of the provider.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, required: false): Enter the suite or floor number of the provider's address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, required: false): Enter the zip code of the provider's address.
* **Fax** (`fax`, text, required: false): Enter the fax number of the provider.
