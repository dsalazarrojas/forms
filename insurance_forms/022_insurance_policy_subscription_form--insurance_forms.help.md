# insurance_policy_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The insurance_policy_subscription_form is a template for collecting information related to an insurance policy subscription. This form is meant to be used by insurance companies, agents, or brokers to gather essential details from clients or policyholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information as requested.
2. Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to ensure all necessary fields are completed accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Client Information (`client_info`)
* **Client Information**: This field is where you can input any relevant details about the client or policyholder. You can include their name, contact information, or any other relevant information.

### 2. Insurance Information (`insurance_info`)
* **Insurance Information**: In this field, you can provide details about the insurance policy being subscribed to. This could include policy numbers, policy types, or any other relevant information related to the policy.

### 3. Agent Information (`agent_info`)
* **Agent Information**: This field is meant for the insurance agent or broker to input their information, including their name, contact details, or any other relevant information.

### 4. Payment Terms (`payment_terms`)
* **Payment Terms**: Here, you can specify the payment terms and conditions related to the insurance policy subscription. This could include payment methods, schedules, or any other payment-related information.

### 5. Submission (`submission`)
* **Submission**: This field is for the final submission of the form, where you can review and confirm the information entered before proceeding with the insurance policy subscription.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any errors or discrepancies.
* Double-check the form for accuracy before final submission.
* If you need further assistance or have questions, contact form@gic.mx.
