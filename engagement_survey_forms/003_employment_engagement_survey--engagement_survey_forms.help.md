# employment_engagement_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employment_engagement_survey form is designed to measure employee engagement and satisfaction in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and read each question carefully.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
4. If you are unsure about any question, ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
