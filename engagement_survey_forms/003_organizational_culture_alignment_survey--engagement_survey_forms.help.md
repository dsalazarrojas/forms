# Organizational Culture Alignment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Culture Alignment Survey is designed to gather feedback on the alignment between the organization's values and practices and the employee's expectations and experiences. It aims to identify areas of strength and weakness in the organizational culture and inform data-driven decisions to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and as specifically as possible.
2. Select the appropriate answer for each question based on your experience and understanding of the organizational culture.
3. You can skip questions that you are not familiar with or that do not apply to your role or position.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
