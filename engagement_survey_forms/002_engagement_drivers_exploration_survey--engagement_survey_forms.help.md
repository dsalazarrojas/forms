# Engagement Drivers Exploration Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on engagement drivers for employees. It is meant to be a one-time submission, and responses will be treated as confidential.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details: Employee Name, Job Title, Department, and Employee Email.
2. Select the preferred communication method(s) for your manager.
3. Choose the preferred frequency of communication with your manager.
4. Confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Employee Name
 * **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it is officially recorded in the company's HR system.

### 2. Job Title
 * **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title as it is officially recorded in the company's HR system.

### 3. Department
 * **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter the department where you work.

### 4. Location
 * **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Enter your work location.

### 5. Manager Name
 * **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's name as it is officially recorded in the company's HR system.

### 6. Manager Job Title
 * **Manager Job Title** (`manager_job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's job title as it is officially recorded in the company's HR system.

### 7. Employee Email
 * **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, required): Enter your official company email address.

### 8. Manager Email
 * **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your manager's official company email address, if available.

### 9. Manager Phone
 * **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your manager's contact number.

### 10. Manager Phone Type
 * **Manager Phone Type** (`manager_phone_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you prefer to be contacted by your manager: Home, Mobile, or Work.

### 11. Manager Contact Preference
 * **Manager Contact Preference** (`manager_contact_preference`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how you prefer to be contacted by your manager. Select all that apply: Email, Phone, In-person.

### 12. Manager Contact Method
 * **Manager Contact Method** (`manager_contact_method`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the methods you prefer for communication with your manager. Select all that apply: Email, Phone, Text.

### 13. Manager Communication Frequency
 * **Manager Communication Frequency** (`manager_communication_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how often you prefer to communicate with your manager: Daily, Weekly, Monthly.

### 14. Manager Communication Channel
 * **Manager Communication Channel** (`manager_communication_channel`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the channels you prefer for communication with your manager. Select all that apply: Phone, Email, In-person.

### 15. Manager Communication Method
 * **Manager Communication Method** (`manager_communication_method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the method for communication with your manager: Phone call, Email, In-person meeting.

### 16. Manager Communication Frequency 2
 * **Manager Communication Frequency 2** (`manager_communication_frequency2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how often you prefer to communicate with your manager: Daily, Weekly, Monthly.

### 17. Manager Communication Channel 2

### 18. Manager Communication Method 2

### 19. Manager Communication Frequency 3
 * **Manager Communication Frequency 3** (`manager_communication_frequency3`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how often you prefer to communicate with your manager: Daily, Weekly, Monthly.

### 20. Manager Communication Channel 3
 * **Manager Communication Channel 3** (`manager_communication_channel3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the channels you prefer for communication with your manager. Select all that apply: Phone, Email, In-person.

### 21. Manager Communication Method 3
 * **Manager Communication Method 3** (`manager_communication_method3`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the method for communication with your manager: Phone call, Email, In-person meeting.
