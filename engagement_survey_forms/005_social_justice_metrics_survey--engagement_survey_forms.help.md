# Social Justice Metrics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Justice Metrics Survey is designed to measure employee engagement with social justice metrics, gauging awareness, inclusion, and sense of belonging within the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. For text fields, provide a brief explanation in the provided space.
4. If a question is not relevant to you, please indicate this by selecting "not at all".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **social_justice_survey (Page 1)**: Briefly describe your overall experience with social justice in the workplace. 
* **experience_of_awareness (Page 2)**: Select how aware you are of social justice issues in your workplace. This will help us understand how much you are aware of the social justice initiatives and policies we have in place.
	+ very high: You have a strong understanding of social justice initiatives and policies.
	+ fairly high: You have some knowledge of social justice initiatives and policies.
	+ somewhat high: You have a general idea of social justice initiatives and policies.
	+ not at all: You do not have much knowledge of social justice initiatives and policies.
* **experience_of_inclusion (Page 3)**: Select how inclusive you feel your workplace is in promoting social justice. This will help us understand how you perceive our efforts to foster an inclusive environment.
	+ very inclusive: Our workplace feels highly inclusive and welcoming.
	+ fairly inclusive: Our workplace feels somewhat inclusive, but there is room for improvement.
	+ somewhat inclusive: Our workplace has some inclusive elements, but it is not a priority.
	+ very exclusive: Our workplace feels largely exclusive and unwelcoming.
* **sense_of_belonging (Page 4)**: Select all that apply to describe your level of belonging in the workplace. This will help us understand how connected you feel to our workplace community.
	+ very strongly belong: I feel a strong sense of belonging to our workplace community.
	+ somewhat belong: I feel somewhat connected to our workplace community.
	+ not at all: I do not feel much connection to our workplace community.
* **perceptions_of_equality (Page 5)**: Provide a brief explanation of your perceptions of equality in the workplace. This will help us understand your views on equality initiatives and policies we have in place.
* **perceptions_of_inclusion (Page 6)**: Provide a brief explanation of your perceptions of inclusion in the workplace. This will help us understand your views on inclusion initiatives and policies we have in place.
* **social_justice_actions (Page 7)**: Select all that apply to describe actions you have taken or would take to promote social justice. This will help us understand your level of engagement with social justice initiatives.
	+ advocacy: I have promoted or would promote social justice through speaking out against injustice.
	+ education: I have educated or would educate others about social justice.
	+ ally: I have stood in solidarity with others on social justice issues or would do so.
	+ support: I have supported or would support social justice initiatives.
* **feedback_on_inclusion_initiative (Page 8)**: Provide a brief explanation of your feedback on the current inclusion initiative. This will help us understand your views on our efforts to foster an inclusive environment.
