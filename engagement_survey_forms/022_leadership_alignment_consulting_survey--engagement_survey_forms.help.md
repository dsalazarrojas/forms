# leadership_alignment_consulting_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leadership alignment and consulting survey is a tool designed to help leaders assess their alignment with the organization's vision, mission, and values. It's a self-assessment instrument meant to help leaders identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please navigate through the 5 pages of the form, and select the answer that best matches your response to each question.
2. Try to answer each question as concisely as possible, while ensuring your answers are accurate and reflective of your current situation.
3. For each question, select only one response option that best represents your answer.
4. If a question seems unclear or you're unsure of an answer, please ask for clarification or contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **Page 1**: This is the introduction page, which serves as a warm-up to get you ready to start answering the questions.
* **Page 2**: This page asks you about your understanding of the organization's vision, mission, and values.
* **Page 3**: This page inquires about your current leadership practices and behaviors that support or hinder your alignment with the organization.
* **Page 4**: This page explores your relationships with your team members and other stakeholders.
* **Page 5**: This page is dedicated to your feedback and suggestions on how to improve the current state of leadership alignment.

### Page 3
* **Page 3** (`page_3`, `text`, required `false`): This page asks about your current leadership practices and behaviors that support or hinder your alignment with the organization. Please provide a brief description of your current actions that promote or impede your leadership alignment.

### Page 4
* **Page 4** (`page_4`, `text`, required `false`): This page explores your relationships with your team members and other stakeholders. Describe how you interact with your team and other stakeholders to promote or hinder your leadership alignment.

### Page 5
* **Page 5** (`page_5`, `text`, required `false`): This page is dedicated to your feedback and suggestions on how to improve the current state of leadership alignment. Provide any ideas or insights you have on areas for improvement and how to bridge the gaps.
