# Patient Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Engagement Survey is a tool used to collect data from patients about their engagement and satisfaction with healthcare services provided. This form is intended to help healthcare providers understand patient preferences, needs, and experiences, which can inform improvements to care quality and patient outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and accurately to provide useful feedback.
2. Select the options that best apply to your experience and demographics.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions** (`1`, `note`, optional): This section is not applicable for end users, as it is a note type field. The survey questions are designed to be answered by patients.
* **Patient Engagement** (`2`, `text`, optional): Describe your overall engagement with our healthcare services.
* **Satisfaction** (`3`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the care you received on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Experience** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options to describe your experience: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Healthcare Services** (`5`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the healthcare services that apply to your visit, such as Primary Care, Specialist Care, Lab Tests, Surgery, Other.
* **Comments** (`6`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email** (`7`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address, if you wish to be contacted for follow-up or additional information.
* **Phone** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted by phone.
* **Patient Identifier** (`9`, `date`, optional): Not applicable for end users.
* **Date Of Birth** (`10`, `date`, optional): Not applicable for end users.
* **Time** (`11`, `time`, optional): Not applicable for end users.
* **Patient Id** (`12`, `select_multiple`, optional): Not applicable for end users.
* **Patient Name** (`13`, `text`, optional): Not applicable for end users.
* **Patient Relationship** (`14`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options to describe your relationship with the patient: Family Member, Caregiver, Friend, Other.
* **Provider Name** (`15`, `text`, optional): Not applicable for end users.
* **Department** (`16`, `text`, optional): Not applicable for end users.
* **Shift** (`17`, `time`, optional): Not applicable for end users.
* **Doctor** (`18`, `text`, optional): Not applicable for end users.
* **Gender** (`19`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the gender options that apply to you, such as Male, Female, Other.
* **Ethnicity** (`20`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options to describe your ethnicity: Asian, Black, Hispanic, White, Other.
* **Language** (`21`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options to describe your language: English, Spanish, French, Other.
* **Age** (`22`, `number`, optional): Not applicable for end users.
* **Date** (`23`, `date`, optional): Not applicable for end users.
* **Time** (`24`, `time`, optional): Not applicable for end users.
* **Comments** (`25`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience, if you wish.

Note: Some fields are not applicable for end users and can be removed from the guide.
