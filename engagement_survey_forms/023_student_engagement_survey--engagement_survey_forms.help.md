# student_engagement_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The student engagement survey is a tool used to gather feedback from students about their experience with a course or class. This feedback is essential for instructors to understand their teaching effectiveness and make necessary adjustments to improve the learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully and respond to the best of your ability.
2. Select the most appropriate answer for each question based on your experience with the course or class.
3. Be honest and provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your responses.
4. Do not repeat answers if you've already submitted feedback for this course or class.
