## Teamwork Contribution Recognition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teamwork Contribution Recognition Form is used to collect information about a team member's contributions to a project or team effort. This form is intended to be used by team members to recognize and acknowledge the efforts of their colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing as much detail as possible about the team member's contributions.
2. Ensure that you are not duplicating a nomination that has already been submitted.
3. If you are unsure, please check with your team lead or manager to confirm whether a nomination for the same team member has already been submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Details** (`1`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the team member's contributions.
* **Teamwork Impact** (`2`, `text`, required): Describe the positive impact the team member's contributions had on the project or team.
* **Supporting Evidence** (`3`, `text`, optional): If you have any supporting evidence (e.g. screenshots, documents, etc.), you can upload it here.
* **Nomination Status** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the nomination (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* **Upload Supporting Evidence** (`5`, `text`, optional): If you selected "Approved" as the nomination status, you can upload supporting evidence here.
* **Label** (`6`, `text`, optional): Add a label to the uploaded evidence.
* **Description** (`7`, `text`, optional): Add a description to the uploaded evidence.
* **Submit** (`8`, `text`, optional): Click this button to submit the form.

## Tips
* Be as specific and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your team lead or manager.
* Please note that the submission of this form is not a guarantee of approval or recognition. The nomination will be reviewed and evaluated by a team lead or manager before being finalized.
