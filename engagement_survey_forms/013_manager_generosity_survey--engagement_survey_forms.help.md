# Manager Generosity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to assess the level of support, recognition, and opportunities provided by a manager to their team members. It consists of 25 questions, divided into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of a manager's behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly, considering your experiences with your current or previous manager.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best reflects your frequency of experience (Frequently, Sometimes, Rarely).
3. For open-ended questions (feedback comments), provide specific examples or descriptions.
4. Make sure to answer all required questions, marked as (required: true).
5. For optional questions, feel free to provide any relevant comments or feedback.
