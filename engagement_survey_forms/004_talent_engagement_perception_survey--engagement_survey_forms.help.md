# Talent Engagement Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Engagement Perception Survey is a tool to measure employee engagement, gather feedback, and identify areas of improvement within teams and departments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your honest feedback.
2. Answer each question thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (`first_name`, text, optional)
Your first name is a required field for identification purposes. Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Job Title (`job_title`, text, optional)
Enter your official job title as per your position within the organization. This will help us understand your role and responsibilities in relation to the team.

### Department (`department`, text, optional)
Please enter the department you belong to within the organization. This will help us understand your team dynamics and relationships.

### Team (`team`, text, optional)
Enter the name of your team or unit within the department. This will help us understand your team's composition and interactions.

### Manager (`manager`, text, optional)
Enter the name of your direct manager or supervisor. This will help us understand your reporting lines and chain of command.

### Email (`email`, email, optional)
Enter your official email address associated with your work. This will help us reach out to you for further questions or clarification.

### Phone (`phone`, text, optional)
Enter your official phone number associated with your work. This will help us contact you in case of any further questions or clarification.

### Questions (`questions`, note, optional)
Please provide any questions, concerns, or comments you have about the team, department, or organization. This is a free-form text area where you can write your thoughts and opinions.

### Feedback (`feedback`, text, optional)
Enter your general feedback and suggestions on how to improve the team and organization. This will help us identify areas of improvement and make necessary changes.
