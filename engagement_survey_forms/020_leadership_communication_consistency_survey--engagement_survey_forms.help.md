# leadership-communication-consistency-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leadership-communication-consistency-survey form aims to assess an individual's perception of leadership communication within their team. It covers the clarity, frequency, and alignment of leadership messages to gauge the team's overall understanding of leadership communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and select the most accurate response from the provided options.
2. Answer all questions honestly, as your input contributes to a broader team evaluation.
3. Use the provided hints for each question to clarify any doubts.
4. Submit your completed form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leadership Communication Consistency (1)**: Select whether you consider the team leader's communication to be consistent or inconsistent.
    • Yes: You believe the team leader's communication is consistent.
    • No: You do not believe the team leader's communication is consistent.
* **Team Leadership Clarity (2)**: Indicate the level of clarity you perceive from the team leader regarding team goals and objectives.
    • Enter a value between 0 and 10, where 10 represents maximum clarity.
* **Team Leadership Frequency (3)**: Rate the frequency of communication from the team leader.
    • Select the frequency at which you perceive the team leader to be communicating.
    • Yes: Communication is frequent and regular.
    • No: Communication is infrequent or irregular.
* **Team Leadership Alignment (4)**: Choose how well the team leader's messages align with team goals and objectives.
    • Select all that apply:
        + Yes: Messages are aligned with team goals and objectives.
        + No: Messages are not aligned with team goals and objectives.
* **Leadership Message Clarity (5)**: Rate the overall clarity of leadership messages.
    • Enter a value between 0 and 10, where 10 represents maximum clarity.
* **Team Leadership Message Frequency (6)**: Rate the frequency of leadership messages.
    • Select the frequency at which you perceive the team leader to be communicating.
    • Yes: Communication is frequent and regular.
    • No: Communication is infrequent or irregular.
* **Team Leadership Message Alignment (7)**: Choose how well leadership messages align with team goals and objectives.
    • Select all that apply:
        + Yes: Messages are aligned with team goals and objectives.
        + No: Messages are not aligned with team goals and objectives.
* **Leadership Message Clarity (8)**: Rate the overall clarity of leadership messages.
    • Select all that apply:
        + Yes: Messages are clear.
        + No: Messages are not clear.
* **Leadership Communication Questions (9)**: Select whether you feel the team leader asks questions to understand team concerns.
    • Yes: The team leader asks questions to understand team concerns.
    • No: The team leader does not ask questions to understand team concerns.
* **Additional Leadership Message (10)**: If you have any additional comments or feedback about leadership communication, enter it here.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your responses, as they contribute to team improvement.
* If you're unsure of a question or need clarification, ask your team lead or admin for help.
* Your input is valuable to the team, so please take the time to complete the form thoroughly.
