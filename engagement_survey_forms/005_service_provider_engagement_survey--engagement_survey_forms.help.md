# Service Provider Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to measure the engagement and performance of service providers. It helps to identify areas of improvement and ensure that service providers are aligned with the organization's goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
3. If you have any feedback or suggestions, please provide them in the "internal feedback" or "external feedback" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Provider Engagement Survey Form Template (Required)**: This field is the main section of the form where you can provide your feedback on your engagement with the organization.
* **Performance (Required)**: This field asks you to rate your performance as a service provider, with a scale of 1-10. Please rate your performance honestly, taking into account your achievements and challenges.
* **Satisfaction (Optional)**: This field gives you the opportunity to express your satisfaction with your job as a service provider. Please choose one of the options: Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
* **Recommendations (Optional)**: This field is for you to provide any recommendations or suggestions for improvement. Please be specific and provide concrete ideas.
* **Email (Required)**: Please enter your email address to ensure that you receive any follow-up communication or feedback from the organization.
* **Service Provider (Required)**: This field asks you to confirm whether you are a service provider or not. Please choose "Yes" if you are a service provider, and "No" otherwise.
* **Internal Feedback (Optional)**: This field is for you to provide any internal feedback or suggestions for improvement. Please be specific and provide concrete ideas.
* **External Feedback (Optional)**: This field is for you to provide any external feedback or suggestions for improvement. Please be specific and provide concrete ideas.

Note: The fields marked as "Optional" can be skipped if you are not comfortable providing feedback in that area. However, please keep in mind that providing feedback is essential for the organization to understand your needs and concerns.
