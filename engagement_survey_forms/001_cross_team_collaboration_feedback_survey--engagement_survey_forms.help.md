# Cross Team Collaboration Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather feedback from team members on their collaboration experiences with Team A and Team B. Your honest answers will help identify areas of improvement and facilitate open discussions within the teams.

Please answer the following questions by selecting the best options for each field. You can skip fields that are not relevant to your experiences.
