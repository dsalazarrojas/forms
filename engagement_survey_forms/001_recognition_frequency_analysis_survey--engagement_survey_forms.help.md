# Recognition Frequency Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recognition Frequency Analysis Survey is designed to gather information about how often employees receive recognition and the challenges that come with it.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this survey, please follow these steps:

1.  Select all questions that apply to you.
2.  Choose your answers based on your experience and perception.
3.  Fill in any required fields with as much detail as possible.
4.  Leave blank fields that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Giving Recognition** (`employee_giving_recognition`, select_one, required): Select how often you give recognition to your employees.
	+ Choose one of the following options:
		- Very Often
		- Often
		- Rarely
* **Employee Received Recognition** (`employee_received_recognition`, select_multiple, required): Check how often you received recognition from your supervisor/manager/team leader.
	+ Select all that apply:
		- Very Often
		- Often
		- Rarely
* **Supervisor Giving Recognition** (`supervisor_giving_recognition`, select_one, not required): This question is not required, but it will help us understand if your supervisor gives recognition to you or other employees.
	+ Choose one of the following options:
		- Very Often
		- Often
		- Rarely
* **Barriers to Giving Recognition** (`barriers_to_giving_recognition`, text, required): Describe any barriers you face when giving recognition to employees.
* **Ideas to Improve Recognition** (`ideas_to_improve_recognition`, note, required): Share your ideas on how to improve the recognition process.
* **Frequency of Recognizing Employees** (`frequency_of_recognizing`, date, not required): This question is not required, but it will help us understand when you recognize employees. Please use the format: `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Frequency of Recognizing Employees** (`frequency_of_recognizing_employees`, time, not required): This question is not required, but it will help us understand how often you recognize employees. Please use the format: `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Manager's Perception of Recognition** (`manager_perception_of_recognition`, select_multiple, required): Share your manager's perception of recognition in the team.
	+ Select all that apply:
		- Very Important
		- Somewhat Important
		- Not Important
* **Department Recognition** (`department_recognition`, select_one, required): Check if recognition is given in your department.
	+ Choose one of the following options:
		- True
		- False
* **Frequency of Recognition by Department** (`frequency_of_recognition_by_department`, number, not required): This question is not required, but it will help us understand when recognition is given in your department. Please use the format: `###`.
* **Employee Input** (`employee_input`, text, required): Share any additional information about recognition that you think is important.
* **Employee Input Notes** (`employee_input_notes`, note, required): Add any additional comments about recognition that you'd like to share.
* **Manager Input** (`manager_input`, text, not required): Share any thoughts or comments from your manager about recognition.
* **Manager Input Notes** (`manager_input_notes`, note, not required): Add any additional comments from your manager about recognition.
* **Team Leaders Input** (`team_leaders_input`, text, not required): Share any thoughts or comments from team leaders about recognition.
* **Team Leaders Input Notes** (`team_leaders_input_notes`, note, not required): Add any additional comments from team leaders about recognition.
* **Other** (`other`, text, not required): Share any other information about recognition that you'd like to share.
* **Other Notes** (`other_notes`, note, not required): Add any additional comments about recognition that you'd like to share.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Share your email address for any follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number_input`, text, not required): Share your phone number for any follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Comments** (`comments`, select_multiple, not required): Check if you have any comments about the recognition process.
	+ Select all that apply:
		- True
		- False
		- Unsure
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Share any additional comments about recognition.
* **Frequency of Recognition by Manager** (`frequency_of_recognition_by_manager`, time, not required): This question is not required, but it will help us understand when recognition is given by your manager. Please use the format: `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Frequency of Recognition by Team** (`frequency_of_recognition_by_team`, time, not required): This question is not required, but it will help us understand how often recognition is given by your team. Please use the format: `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Frequency of Recognition by Leaders** (`frequency_of_recognition_by_leaders`, time, not required): This question is not required, but it will help us understand when recognition is given by team leaders. Please use the format: `HH:MM:SS`.

Please note that all questions marked as "required" are mandatory and must be answered for the survey to be complete.
