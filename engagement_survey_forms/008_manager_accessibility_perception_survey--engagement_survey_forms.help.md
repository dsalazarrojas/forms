# Manager Accessibility Perception Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Manager Accessibility Perception Survey is designed to gauge the manager's perspective on their own approachability and communication style in the workplace. This survey aims to help managers become more aware of their behavior and attitudes towards employees, ultimately fostering a more inclusive and supportive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. For questions 1-3, select a number from 0 to 10 to indicate how you rate your approachability and responsiveness in the corresponding area.
3. For question 4, provide any additional comments or feedback about your approachability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manager Approachability** (`manager_approachability`, number, Optional): This question assesses your approachability in general management. Think of how open and accessible you are when interacting with your team members. Rate your approachability on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Responsiveness** (`responsiveness`, number, Optional): This question evaluates how responsive you are to your team's needs and concerns. Consider how quickly and effectively you respond to their queries and requests. Rate your responsiveness on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Communication** (`communication`, number, Optional): This question measures the quality of your communication with your team members. Think of how clearly and effectively you convey ideas, information, and expectations. Rate your communication on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Approachability Comments** (`approachability_comments`, note, Optional): This question provides an opportunity to add any additional feedback or insights about your approachability. Please use this space to share any thoughts or comments that didn't fit into the previous questions.
