# Communication Timeliness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Communication Timeliness Survey aims to understand the experience of employees with communication response times, satisfaction, and perceived impact of delays on work and projects within the organization. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and best practices to increase communication timeliness.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly, and to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with multiple options, select the answer that best represents your experience or feelings.
3. If you have any suggestions or comments, please provide them in the "Improvement Suggestions" field.
4. Finally, provide your name and email address if you wish to be contacted for further discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`, Text, Optional): Enter your full name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Participant Email** (`Participant Email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Department** (`Department`, Select One, Required): Select the department you work in.
* **Role Level** (`Role Level`, Select One, Required): Choose your level in the organization.
* **Tenure** (`Tenure`, Select One, Required): Indicate how long you've been with the company.
* **Response Time Satisfaction** (`Response Time Satisfaction`, Note, Optional): Share your thoughts about response times.
* **Email Response Time** (`Email Response Time`, Select One, Required): How satisfied are you with email response times?
* **Meeting Response Time** (`Meeting Response Time`, Select One, Required): How satisfied are you with meeting invitation response times?
* **Decision Communication Time** (`Decision Communication Time`, Select One, Required): How satisfied are you with how quickly decisions are communicated?
* **Feedback Response Time** (`Feedback Response Time`, Select One, Required): How satisfied are you with feedback response times?
* **Information Delivery Time** (`Information Delivery Time`, Select One, Required): How satisfied are you with how quickly information is shared?
* **Urgency Handling** (`Urgency Handling`, Select One, Required): How well are urgent communications handled?
* **Typical Response Time** (`Typical Response Time`, Select One, Required): What is the typical response time you experience?
* **Expected Response Time** (`Expected Response Time`, Select One, Required): What do you think the expected response time should be?
* **Communication Delays** (`Communication Delays`, Select One, Required): How often do you experience communication delays?
* **Delay Causes** (`Delay Causes`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the reasons for most communication delays.
* **Impact Assessment** (`Impact Assessment`, Note, Optional): Share your thoughts on the impact of delays on your work.
* **Work Impact** (`Work Impact`, Select One, Required): How much impact do communication delays have on your work?
* **Project Impact** (`Project Impact`, Select One, Required): Have communication delays led to project delays?
* **Stress Level** (`Stress Level`, Select One, Required): How much stress do communication delays cause you?
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`Improvement Suggestions`, Text, Required): Share your suggestions on how to improve communication timeliness.
* **Best Practices** (`Best Practices`, Text, Optional): Share any communication practices you think work well.
* **Willing to Improve** (`Willing to Improve`, Select One, Optional): Are you willing to participate in improvement initiatives?
* **Survey Date** (`Survey Date`, Date, Required): Please provide the date you completed this survey.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, use the "Improvement Suggestions" field.
* Keep your answers concise and clear.
* If you need help or clarification on any question, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
