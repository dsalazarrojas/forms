# Recognition Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Recognition Survey is designed to gather feedback and nominations for employee recognition programs. It helps identify employees who have made a positive impact and allows the nominator to provide details about the nominee's actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the **Recognition Nominations** page.
2. Enter the **Nomination Type** of the employee being recognized (e.g., "Team Member", "Leader", "Colleague").
3. Provide a brief **Impact Story** about the employee's actions and how they have positively affected the team or organization.
4. Select the **Recognition Program Experience** that best describes the nominee's experience with recognition programs (e.g., "New to the program", "Regular participant", "Leader/Champion").
5. Enter a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate the level of recognition the nominee deserves (e.g., 1 = minimal, 5 = outstanding).
6. Provide any additional **Comments** about the nominee's achievements.
7. Enter the **Email Address** of the person submitting the nomination.
8. Enter the **Phone Number** of the person submitting the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recognition Nominations** (`nomination_form`): Enter a brief title or description of the nomination.
* **Nomination Type** (`nomination_type`): Select the type of recognition being awarded (e.g., "Team Member", "Leader", "Colleague").
* **Impact Story** (`impact_story`): Provide a brief story about the nominee's actions and how they have positively affected the team or organization.
* **Recognition Program Experience** (`recognition_program_experience`): Select the nominee's experience with recognition programs (e.g., "New to the program", "Regular participant", "Leader/Champion").
* **Rating** (`rating`): Enter a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate the level of recognition the nominee deserves.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional information about the nominee's achievements.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter the email address of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the nomination.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing the **Impact Story** and **Comments**.
* Ensure that the **Nomination Type** and **Recognition Program Experience** are accurate and relevant to the nominee's actions.
* Enter a rating that reflects the actual level of recognition the nominee deserves.
