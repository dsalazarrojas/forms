# Teamwork Project Advancement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Teamwork Project Advancement Survey" form is designed to gather feedback from team members about their experiences and perspectives on a specific project. This form aims to collect opinions on aspects such as collaboration, milestones, challenges, improvement ideas, project management, and overall project rating. The survey results will help project managers and the team identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the project's progress and overall success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the questions carefully to understand what is being asked.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields (e.g., "collaboration" and "milestones"), select one or multiple options that best match your experience or opinion.
3. For text fields (e.g., "challenges", "improvement ideas", and "project manager"), type your responses in the provided text boxes.
4. For "project manager rating", enter a numerical rating (1-5) that reflects your assessment of the project manager's performance.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): Briefly introduce yourself or provide any necessary context for your responses.
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, select_one, optional):
	+ "Good": This option is selected if you experienced positive collaboration with other team members.
	+ "Neutral": This option is selected if you experienced average collaboration with other team members.
	+ "Bad": This option is selected if you experienced poor collaboration with other team members.
* **Milestones** (`milestones`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ 'True': Select this option if you feel that the project's milestones were well-planned and achieved.
	+ 'False': Select this option if you feel that the project's milestones were not well-planned or were not met.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, optional): Describe any challenges or obstacles you faced during the project.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, optional): Share ideas on how the project could be improved in the future.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the project manager in the provided text box.
* **Project Manager Rating** (`project_manager_rating`, number, optional): Rate the project manager's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
