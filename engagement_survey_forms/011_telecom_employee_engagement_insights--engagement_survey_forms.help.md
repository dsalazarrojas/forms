# telecom_employee_engagement_insights - Help Guide
## Purpose
The telecom_employee_engagement_insights form is designed to collect feedback and insights from telecom employees about their engagement with the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select all the relevant options for the following fields:
	* Select the employees you are taking the survey about
	* Choose whether or not they have answered feedback questions
	* Enter any comments you have about their experience with the telecom team
	* Select whether or not their manager has been available
	* Choose which telecom department they are part of
	* Enter their job title
	* Enter their work hours
	* Enter their start date
	* Enter their end date
	* Select their work location
2. Make sure to fill out all the fields correctly and accurately, as they are all required unless noted otherwise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **telecom_employees** (`telecom_employees`, Select Multiple, required): Select all the telecom employees you are taking the survey about.
- **feedback_questions** (`feedback_questions`, Select One, required): Choose whether or not the survey respondents have answered feedback questions.
- **comments** (`comments`, Note, required): Enter any comments you have about their experience with the telecom team.
- **manager_name** (`manager_name`, Select One, required): Select whether or not their manager has been available.
- **telecom_department** (`telecom_department`, Select One, required): Choose which telecom department they are part of.
- **job_title** (`job_title`, Text, required): Enter their job title.
- **work_hours** (`work_hours`, Number, required): Enter their work hours.
- **start_date** (`start_date`, Date, required): Enter their start date.
- **end_date** (`end_date`, Date, required): Enter their end date.
- **work_location** (`work_location`, Select One, required): Select their work location.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields to complete the form.
- Double-check the accuracy of your responses before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please feel free to ask for clarification.
- This form will help us gather valuable insights and feedback about your engagement with the telecom team.
