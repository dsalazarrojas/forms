# Operator Orientation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from operators on their onboarding experience. It aims to gather information on the quality of training provided, challenging parts of the onboarding process, and what aspects of the process were helpful or least helpful.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer each question in the fields provided.
2.  For multiple-choice questions, select all applicable options.
3.  Enter your responses in the required fields and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current job title/position?** (`current_job_title`, text, required)
    This field asks for your current job title or position. Please enter it exactly as it is written in your job description or as you identify yourself in your organization.
* **How would you rate the quality of the training provided?** (`rate_training_quality`, number, required)
    Rate the quality of the training provided on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 is very poor and 10 is excellent.
* **What was the most challenging part of the onboarding process?** (`challenging_part`, text, required)
    Describe the most difficult part of your onboarding experience.
* **How effective was the onboarding process in preparing you?** (`effectiveness_of_onboarding`, number, required)
    Rate the effectiveness of the onboarding process on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 is not effective at all and 10 is very effective.
* **What did you find most helpful during the onboarding process?** (`helpful_during_onboarding`, select_multiple, required)
    Select all the options that best describe what was most helpful during your onboarding experience:
    *   Manual
    *   Online Training
    *   Mentorship
    *   Other (please specify)
* **What did you find least helpful or confusing during the onboarding process?** (`least_helpful_during_onboarding`, text, required)
    Describe the least helpful or confusing aspect of your onboarding experience.
* **How would you rate the supportiveness of your colleagues during the onboarding process?** (`supportiveness_of_colleagues`, number, required)
    Rate the supportiveness of your colleagues on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 is not supportive at all and 10 is very supportive.
* **Did you feel adequately prepared for your new role at the end of the onboarding process?** (`feel_prepared_for_new_role`, checkbox, required)
    Answer whether you felt adequately prepared for your new role.
*   True
    *   False
* **How confident are you in your ability to perform your new job responsibilities?** (`confidence_in_job_responsibilities`, number, required)
    Rate your confidence in performing your new job responsibilities on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 is not confident at all and 10 is very confident.
* **What suggestions do you have for improving the onboarding process?** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, required)
    Provide suggestions on how the onboarding process can be improved.
* **Any additional comments or suggestions you'd like to share about your onboarding experience?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional)
    Share any additional thoughts or suggestions about your onboarding experience.
* **How likely are you to recommend this onboarding process to a friend or colleague?** (`would_recommend_onboarding`, number, required)
    Rate your likelihood of recommending the onboarding process on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 is not likely at all and 10 is very likely.
