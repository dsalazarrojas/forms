# Leadership Skill Development Needs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the leadership team understand the current leadership skills and development needs of its members. It will guide the team in identifying areas where members need improvement and provide guidance on the next steps for development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly, and take about 5-7 minutes to complete the survey.
2. Review each page carefully to ensure you're answering all the questions.
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification or assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Current Leadership Skills
**Current Leadership Skills** (`current_leadership_skills`, text, optional)
* This is a free text field where you can describe your current leadership skills and experiences.
* Please provide a brief summary of your leadership skills and any relevant experiences you've had in the past.

### Development Priorities
**Development Priorities** (`development_priorities`, select one, optional)
* Choose one of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* This question helps us understand which areas you'd like to focus on for your development.

### Training Preferences
**Training Preferences** (`training_preferences`, text, optional)
* This is a free text field where you can describe your preferred training methods or formats.
* Please provide a brief description of how you like to learn and what type of training you'd find most helpful.

### Skills Needed
**Skills Needed** (`skills_needed`, select multiple, required)
* Choose all the skills you believe you need to improve or develop.
* This question helps us identify areas where the team needs support and guidance.

### Next Steps
**Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional)
* This is a free text field where you can describe your next steps or actions you're planning to take.
* Please provide a brief summary of your plans for development and growth.

### Coaching Preferences
**Coaching Preferences** (`coaching_preferences`, select one, optional)
* Choose one of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* This question helps us understand how you'd like to receive coaching or guidance.

### Training Goals
**Training Goals** (`training_goals`, text, optional)
* This is a free text field where you can describe your training goals or objectives.
* Please provide a brief description of what you hope to achieve through training and development.

### Evaluation
**Evaluation** (`evaluation`, select one, optional)
* Choose one of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* This question helps us evaluate the effectiveness of our development programs and services.
