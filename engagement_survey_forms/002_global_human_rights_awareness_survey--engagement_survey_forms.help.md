# Global Human Rights Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the level of awareness and understanding of human rights among respondents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction, which provides background information on human rights.
2. Answer the background knowledge question with a brief text response.
3. Select your answer to "Understanding Human Rights" by choosing one of the options provided.
4. For "Attitude Toward Human Rights", select all the options that apply to your attitude.
5. For "Practice Human Rights", select all the options that apply to your current practices.
6. For "Human Rights Violations", select all the options that apply to human rights violations you are aware of.
7. For "Human Rights Issues", select all the options that apply to human rights issues you are aware of.
8. For "Human Rights Advocacy", select all the options that apply to your human rights advocacy efforts.
9. Write a brief text response for "Human Rights Education".
10. Read and fill out the conclusion section, which is a final note to sum up your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional): This is a brief background information about human rights.
- **Background Knowledge** (`background_knowledge`, `text`, optional): Respond to this question with a brief text response.
- **Understanding Human Rights** (`understanding_human_rights`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about your level of understanding on human rights. Choose 'Yes' if you understand human rights well, 'No' otherwise.
- **Attitude Toward Human Rights** (`attitude_toward_human_rights`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that apply to your attitude toward human rights.
  • **Yes**: You have a positive attitude toward human rights.
  • **No**: You do not have a positive attitude toward human rights.
- **Practice Human Rights** (`practice_human_rights`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that apply to your current human rights practices.
  • **Yes**: You currently practice human rights in your daily life.
  • **No**: You do not currently practice human rights in your daily life.
- **Human Rights Violations** (`human_rights_violations`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that apply to human rights violations you are aware of.
  • **Yes**: You are aware of human rights violations.
  • **No**: You are not aware of human rights violations.
- **Human Rights Issues** (`human_rights_issues`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that apply to human rights issues you are aware of.
  • **Yes**: You are aware of human rights issues.
  • **No**: You are not aware of human rights issues.
- **Human Rights Advocacy** (`human_rights_advocacy`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that apply to your human rights advocacy efforts.
  • **Yes**: You engage in human rights advocacy.
  • **No**: You do not engage in human rights advocacy.
- **Human Rights Education** (`human_rights_education`, `text`, optional): Respond to this question with a brief text response.
- **Conclusion** (`conclusion`, `note`, optional): Sum up your responses in a final note.
