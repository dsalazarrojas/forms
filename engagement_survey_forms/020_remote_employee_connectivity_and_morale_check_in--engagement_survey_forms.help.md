# remote_employee_connectivity_and_morale_check_in - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help assess remote employees' feelings of connection and provide insights on improving remote work arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question to the best of your abilities.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience.
3. Be honest and provide detailed descriptions where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Connection** (`remote_employee_connection`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your experience with your team members while working remotely. How do you feel about your connections with them?
* **Morale** (`morale_check_in`, `text`, required: false): Describe how your work environment and remote work arrangement affect your morale. Do you feel engaged, motivated, or demotivated?
* **Feedback on remote work** (`feedback_remote_work`, `text`, required: false): Share any positive or negative feedback on remote work arrangements. What has worked well, and what hasn't?
* **Communication Issues** (`communication_issues`, `text`, required: false): If you have encountered any communication issues while working remotely, describe them in detail.
* **Communication Requests** (`communication_requests`, `text`, required: false): If there are any specific communication requests you'd like to share with your team or manager, write them down here.
* **Remote Work Schedule** (`remote_work_schedule`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your experience with your remote work schedule. Do you feel it's effective, or are there any adjustments needed?
* **How often do you like to be communicated with?** (`communication_frequency`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose how often you'd like to be communicated with while working remotely.
	+ **Every day**
	+ **Every other day**
	+ **Several times a week**
	+ **Once a week**
	+ **Only if necessary**
* **Are you currently working remotely?** (`remote_working_status`, `select_one`, required: true)
	+ **True**
	+ **False**

## Tips

- Be honest and accurate when answering questions to provide valuable insights.
- Use the space provided to elaborate on your responses.
- If you have any suggestions or ideas on how to improve remote work, share them with your team or manager.
