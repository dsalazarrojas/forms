# Early Childhood Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Early Childhood Engagement Survey is a tool designed to gather feedback and engagement information from teachers regarding early childhood education and development. This survey will help the institution understand teacher engagement and identify areas for improvement in supporting teachers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your teacher name in the "Teacher Name" field.
2. Answer "How old are you" by entering a number indicating your age.
3. Choose your level of engagement by selecting one of the rating options (Low, Medium, High).
4. If you have years of experience as a teacher, enter that number.
5. Select all that apply from the list of items you would like to see added or improved in early childhood education.
6. If you have a suggestion that is not on the list, please type it in the "Please specify other" field.
7. Write any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. If you have a specific date in mind for when you would like to see changes take place, enter that date in the "When would you like to see these changes take place" field.
11. If you have a specific time of day in mind for when you would like to see changes take place, enter that time in the "What time of day would you like to see these changes take place" field.
12. Choose how often you would like to see changes take place (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
13. If you have a specific schedule in mind for when you would like to see changes take place, type it in the "Please specify other" field.
14. Write any additional comments or suggestions in the "Do you have any comments" field.
15. Finally, indicate who assigned this form to you in the "Who assigned this form" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name (Teacher Name, Text, Optional)**: Please enter your name as a teacher.
* **How old are you (How old are you, Number, Optional)**: Enter your age as a number.
* **How would you rate your engagement (How would you rate your engagement, Select One, Optional)**: Choose how engaged you feel in your early childhood education role (Low, Medium, High).
* **How many years of experience do you have as a teacher (How many years of experience do you have as a teacher, Number, Optional)**: Enter the number of years you have been a teacher.
* **Which of the following items would you like to see added or improved (Which of the following items would you like to see added or improved, Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select all that apply from the list of items you would like to see improved or added.
* **Please specify other (Please specify other, Text, Optional)**: If there is an item on the list that you would like to see improved or added, but it is not listed, type it in this field.
* **Comments (Do you have any comments, Note, Optional)**: Write any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Email (Email, Email, Optional)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Phone, Text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number.
* **When would you like to see these changes take place (When would you like to see these changes take place, Date, Optional)**: Enter the date you would like to see changes take place.
* **What time of day would you like to see these changes take place (What time of day would you like to see these changes take place, Time, Optional)**: Enter the time you would like to see changes take place.
* **How often would you like to see these changes take place (How often would you like to see these changes take place, Select One, Optional)**: Choose how often you would like to see changes take place (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Please specify other (Please specify other, Text, Optional)**: If you have a specific schedule in mind for when you would like to see changes take place, type it in this field.
* **Comments (Do you have any comments, Note, Optional)**: Write any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Who assigned this form (Who assigned this form, Text, Optional)**: Indicate who assigned this form to you.
