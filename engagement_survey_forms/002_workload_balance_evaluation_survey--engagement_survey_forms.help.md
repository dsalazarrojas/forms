# Workload Balance Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workload Balance Evaluation Survey is a tool to help teams assess their workload distribution and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as honestly and accurately as possible. You will be asked to select or enter numbers for various aspects of your team's workload. Please answer all questions that apply to your team, and provide as much detail as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Team Size (`team_size`, `number`, required: false)
This question asks about the number of team members you have in your team. Please enter a whole number, e.g., 5 for a team of 5 members.

### 2. Team Balance (`team_balance`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
This question asks you to select whether your team experiences "Yes" or "No" balance. "Yes" means that each team member has roughly the same amount of work, while "No" means that some team members have significantly more work than others.

### 3. Workload Fairness (`workload_fairness`, `number`, required: false)
This question asks you to estimate the fairness of your team's workload distribution on a scale of 1-10. A score of 1 means that some team members have extremely unfair workloads, while a score of 10 means that all team members have a perfectly fair workload.

### 4. Stress Levels (`stress_levels`, `number`, required: false)
This question asks you to rate how stressed you feel about your workload on a scale of 1-10. A score of 1 means you are not stressed at all, while a score of 10 means you are extremely stressed.

### 5. Support Levels (`support_levels`, `number`, required: false)
This question asks you to rate how much support you receive from your team and management on a scale of 1-10. A score of 1 means you receive very little support, while a score of 10 means you receive complete support.

### 6. Workload Capacity (`workload_capacity`, `number`, required: false)
This question asks you to estimate the maximum capacity of your team's workload. Please enter a whole number, e.g., 50 for a team that can handle a maximum of 50 tasks.

### 7. Team Capacity (`team_capacity`, `number`, required: false)
This question asks you to estimate the actual workload that your team can handle at any given time. Please enter a whole number, e.g., 20 for a team that can handle an actual workload of 20 tasks.

### 8. Team Capacity Proportion (`team_capacity_proportion`, `number`, required: false)
This question asks you to calculate the proportion of tasks your team can handle compared to its capacity. Please enter a decimal number between 0 and 1, e.g., 0.8 for a team that can handle 80% of its capacity.

### 9. Workload Balance (`workload_balance`, `number`, required: false)
This question asks you to estimate the balance of your team's workload. Please enter a whole number, e.g., 8 for a team that has a perfectly balanced workload.

### 10. Stress Proportion (`stress_proportion`, `number`, required: false)
This question asks you to calculate the proportion of tasks that cause stress. Please enter a decimal number between 0 and 1, e.g., 0.5 for 50% of tasks that cause stress.

### 11. Support Proportion (`support_proportion`, `number`, required: false)
This question asks you to calculate the proportion of tasks that have support. Please enter a decimal number between 0 and 1, e.g., 0.8 for 80% of tasks that have support.

### 12. Overall Satisfaction (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false)
This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with your team's workload balance on a scale of 1-10. A score of 1 means you are very dissatisfied, while a score of 10 means you are very satisfied.

## Tips
- Please answer questions as honestly and accurately as possible.
- If a question does not apply to your team, please enter a value of 0.
- If you are unsure about a question, please consult with your team or manager before submitting the form.
- This survey is designed to help your team identify areas for improvement. Please take a few minutes to complete it thoroughly.
