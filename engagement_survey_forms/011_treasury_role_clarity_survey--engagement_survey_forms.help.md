# Treasury Role Clarity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Role Clarity Survey is designed to gather information about the roles and responsibilities within a specific team or department.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Read and understand each page carefully before proceeding.
2.  Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3.  Use the provided text fields to provide clear and concise feedback.
4.  Select all that apply from the multiple-choice questions.
5.  Ensure to fill out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro**: This page serves as an introduction to the survey. It provides a brief explanation of the survey's purpose and significance.
* **Role Responsibilities**: On this page, select all the role responsibilities that apply to you. The options provided are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Feedback**: This page is where you can provide feedback about your role and responsibilities. Type your comments in the provided text field.
* **Comments**: On this page, you can provide additional comments about your role and responsibilities. Type your comments in the provided text field.
* **Submit**: On this page, you can submit your survey. Select 'Submit' to complete the survey and 'Cancel' to exit the survey without saving any changes. 

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Provide clear and concise feedback and comments.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
