# Startup Workplace Culture Employment Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from employees on the startup workplace culture, job satisfaction, and team collaboration. The feedback will help the management team understand the strengths and weaknesses of the company culture and make informed decisions to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field, marked as **required**.
2. For fields that are not marked as **required**, you can provide any additional information you feel is relevant.
3. Make sure to answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Company Name
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.

### 2. Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as you are known in the company.

### 3. Department
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department you belong to.

### 4. Feedback Question 1
* **Feedback Question 1** (`feedback_question_1`, text, required): Answer this question honestly, describing what you think is the biggest strength of our company culture.

### 5. Feedback Question 2
* **Feedback Question 2** (`feedback_question_2`, text, required): Answer this question honestly, describing what you think is the biggest area for improvement in our company culture.

### 6. Feedback Question 3
* **Feedback Question 3** (`feedback_question_3`, text, required): Answer this question honestly, describing any other concerns or suggestions you have about our company culture.

### 7. Feedback Question 4
* **Feedback Question 4** (`feedback_question_4`, text, required): Answer this question honestly, describing any positive aspects of our company culture that you think are unique and worth noting.

### 8. Feedback Question 5
* **Feedback Question 5** (`feedback_question_5`, text, required): Answer this question honestly, describing any areas of concern or room for improvement in our company culture.

### 9. Company Size
* **What type of company size?** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select the size of your company from the options provided.

### 10. Job Satisfaction
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your level of job satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.

### 11. Team Collaboration
* **Team Collaboration** (`team_collaboration`, number, required): Rate your level of team collaboration on a scale of 1-5.

### 12. Company Culture
* **Company Culture** (`company_culture`, number, required): Rate your level of satisfaction with the company culture on a scale of 1-5.

### 13. How Important is Job Satisfaction?
* **How important is job satisfaction?** (`job_satisfaction_important`, number, required): Rate the importance of job satisfaction for you on a scale of 1-5.

### 14. How Important is Team Collaboration?
* **How important is team collaboration?** (`team_collaboration_important`, number, required): Rate the importance of team collaboration for you on a scale of 1-5.

### 15. How Important is Company Culture?
* **How important is company culture?** (`company_culture_important`, number, required): Rate the importance of company culture for you on a scale of 1-5.

### 16. Job Satisfaction Scale
* **Job Satisfaction Scale** (`job_satisfaction_scale`, number, required): Rate your overall job satisfaction on a scale of 1-10.

### 17. Team Collaboration Scale
* **Team Collaboration Scale** (`team_collaboration_scale`, number, required): Rate your level of team collaboration on a scale of 1-10.

### 18. Company Culture Scale
* **Company Culture Scale** (`company_culture_scale`, number, required): Rate your level of satisfaction with the company culture on a scale of 1-10.

### 19. Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the current date.

### 20. Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the current time.

### 21. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.

### 22. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

### 23. Note
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments.

### 24. Email Phone Note
* **Email Phone Note** (`email_phone_note`, note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments related to your email and phone number.

### 25. Tips
* Please make sure to answer all questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please provide them in the "Note" or "Email Phone Note" fields.
* The data collected will be used to improve the company culture and make it more enjoyable for all employees.
