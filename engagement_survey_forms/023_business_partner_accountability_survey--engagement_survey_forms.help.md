Business Partner Accountability Survey - Help Guide
=====================================

## Purpose
The Business Partner Accountability Survey is a tool used to evaluate the performance and partnership with your business partners. This survey helps to assess the quality of their work, communication, reliability, and overall performance. The results of this survey will be used to identify areas of improvement and strengthen the partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the evaluation date of this review.
2. Enter your name and the company you represent.
3. Enter the name and company of the partner you are evaluating.
4. Rate the partner's performance in each category using the provided scales (1-5 or 1-10).
5. Provide any specific concerns or areas for improvement.
6. Decide if you would continue working with the partner in the future.
7. Optional: provide specific suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Date** (evaluation_date, date, required): The date this review is being conducted.
* **Evaluator Name** (evaluator_name, text, required): Your name and the company you represent.
* **Evaluator Company** (evaluator_company, text, required): The company you represent.
* **Partner Company Name** (partner_company_name, text, required): The name of the partner company being evaluated.
* **Partner Primary Contact** (partner_contact_name, text, required): The main contact person at the partner company.
* **Duration of Partnership** (relationship_duration, select_one, required): How long have you partnered with them? Choose from:
	+ Less than 3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 3+ years
* **On-Time Delivery/Performance** (delivery_timeliness, number, required): Rate their timeliness (1-5).
* **Quality of Work/Products** (quality_of_work, number, required): Rate the quality of their work (1-5).
* **Communication Responsiveness** (communication_responsiveness, number, required): Rate how quickly they respond (1-5).
* **Professional Conduct** (professionalism, number, required): Rate their professionalism (1-5).
* **Problem Resolution Ability** (problem_resolution, number, required): Rate their ability to resolve issues (1-5).
* **Overall Reliability** (reliability, number, required): Rate how reliable they are (1-5).
* **Cost Value Assessment** (cost_value, number, required): Is the price fair for the quality (1-5).
* **Innovation and Ideas** (innovation_contribution, number, optional): Rate their innovation and ideas (1-5).
* **Relationship Strength** (relationship_strength, number, required): Rate the relationship (1-5).
* **Partner Strengths** (strengths, text, required): What are their strengths?
* **Areas for Improvement** (areas_for_improvement, text, required): What could they improve?
* **Specific Concerns or Issues** (specific_concerns, text, optional): Any specific problems?
* **Future Partnership** (future_partnership, select_one, required): Would you continue working with them? Choose from:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Maybe
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
* **Recommendations for Improvement** (recommendations, text, optional): Specific suggestions for improvement.
* **Overall Rating** (overall_rating, number, required): Overall score (1-10).
