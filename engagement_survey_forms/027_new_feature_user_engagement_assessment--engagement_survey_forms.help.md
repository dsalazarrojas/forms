# New Feature User Engagement Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user feedback and assess their engagement with a new feature. It is intended to be filled out by users after experiencing the feature, and is used to inform product development and improvement decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions regarding the new feature's launch status and your overall experience with it.
2. Select how satisfied you are with the feature from the available options.
3. Indicate how likely you are to adopt and use the feature.
4. Provide any additional comments you may have about the feature.
5. Rate the roadmap for future development of the feature.
6. If you have any further comments or concerns, please include them in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Has feature launched**: This question asks whether the new feature has been launched. Please answer 'True' if the feature is available to you, 'False' otherwise.
* **How would you rate your overall experience**: This question asks you to rate your overall experience with the new feature, on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **How satisfied are you with the feature**: This question asks you to select how satisfied you are with the feature from the available options (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
* **How likely are you to adopt and use the feature**: This question asks you to indicate how likely you are to adopt and use the feature.
* **What would you like to see improved**: This question is for any additional comments or suggestions you have about the feature.
* **How would you like to contact us**: This question is for any feedback or contact information you would like to share with our team.
* **How would you rate the roadmap for future development**: This question asks you to rate the roadmap for future development of the feature from the available options (On track, Off track, Not planned).
* **Additional comments**: This field is for any additional comments or concerns you may have about the feature.
