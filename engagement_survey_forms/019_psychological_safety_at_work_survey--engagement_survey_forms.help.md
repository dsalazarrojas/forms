# Psychological Safety At Work Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychological Safety At Work Survey is designed to measure psychological safety, trust, and open communication among team members. It helps the organization understand the team's psychological safety climate, which is crucial for creating a healthy work environment. By providing accurate and honest responses, you contribute to the development of strategies and policies that enhance team safety and overall well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read each question carefully and consider your honest response.
2.  For questions that ask you to select all that apply (team_safety and manager_safety), choose all options that best describe your experience.
3.  For questions with numerical scores (team_safety_score, team_member_safety_score, trust_score, comments_score), answer with a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
4.  Provide detailed comments where necessary (comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Introduction** (1): This is an optional text field for any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with psychological safety at work.
*   **Team Safety** (2): Choose all that apply if you feel that your team has an environment where:
    *   You can share ideas and opinions without fear of negative consequences.
    *   Your team members are comfortable sharing their feelings and emotions.
    *   You feel secure in your role and can express yourself without fear of judgment.
*   **Trust** (3): Choose the option that best describes your level of trust in your team:
    *   Yes, I trust my team completely.
    *   Somewhat, but there are some issues.
    *   No, I do not trust my team at all.
*   **Openness** (4): This field is not applicable in this version of the form.
*   **Comments** (5): Share your honest thoughts and opinions about psychological safety in your team in the text field. This is an essential part of helping us understand your perspective.
*   **Manager Safety** (6): Choose all that apply if you feel that your manager has a safe and supportive work environment:
    *   You can share ideas and opinions without fear of negative consequences.
    *   Your manager is approachable and supportive.
    *   You feel secure in your role and can express yourself without fear of judgment.
*   **Team Safety Score** (7): Answer with a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest, to indicate your perception of your team's overall safety.
*   **Team Member Safety Score** (8): Answer with a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest, to indicate your perception of your team member's overall safety.
*   **Trust Score** (9): Answer with a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest, to indicate your level of trust in your team.
*   **Comments Score** (10): This field is not applicable in this version of the form.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate in your responses, as they will help us create a safe and supportive work environment.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative or the form administrator.
