# Economic Block Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form collects information from users about their economic block participation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form instructions and ensure you have read and understood them.
2. Provide your contact details, including your role and contact preferences.
3. Indicate your motivation and expectation levels for participating in the economic block.
4. Specify your contact preferences, including phone and email.
5. If necessary, provide any additional contact details not covered by the previous fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Role**: Select the role you hold within the economic block (Economic Block Administrator, Economic Block Manager, or Other).
* **Roles**: Select all roles you hold within the economic block (e.g., Economic Block Administrator, Economic Block Manager, Other).
* **Motivations**: Enter your motivations for participating in the economic block (e.g., "to contribute to the community," "for personal gain," etc.).
* **Expectation**: Select your expectation level for the economic block (High, Medium, Low).
* **Expectation Frequency**: Indicate how often you expect the economic block to be implemented (Often, Occasionally, Rarely).
* **Contact Preferences**: Select your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, Other).
* **Contact Preferences Other**: Enter any additional contact preferences not covered by the previous field.
* **Role Other**: Enter any additional roles you hold within the economic block.
* **Roles Other**: Enter any additional roles not covered by the previous field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Details Other**: Enter any additional contact details not covered by the previous fields.
