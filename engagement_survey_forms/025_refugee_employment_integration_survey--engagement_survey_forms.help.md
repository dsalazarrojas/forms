# Refugee Employment Integration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refugee Employment Integration Survey is a tool used to gather information on the employment needs and experiences of refugees, aiming to support their integration into the local workforce.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please select the options that best match your current employment status.
- Choose all language skills that you possess.
- Describe any support needs you have that are not listed in the provided options.
- Provide a brief description of your current employment status.
- If you are looking for employment, please specify what type of job you are seeking.
- Describe the type of job you are looking for, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Job Status** (`Job Status`, `select_one`, `false`): Select one of the options that best describes your current employment status:
  - Employed
  - Unemployed
  - Looking for a job
- **Language Skills** (`Language Skills`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Choose all the language skills you possess:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Support Needs** (`Support Needs`, `text`, `false`): If there are any support needs you require that are not listed, please describe them here.
- **Employment Status** (`Employment Status`, `text`, `false`): Please provide a brief description of your current employment status.
- **Looking for employment** (`Looking for employment`, `note`, `false`): If you are looking for employment, please specify what type of job you are seeking.
- **Job Sought** (`Job Sought`, `text`, `false`): Describe the type of job you are looking for, if applicable.

## Tips
- Please respond honestly and accurately to ensure the information collected is helpful in creating targeted support services.
- If you have completed this form previously, please do not submit this form again.
