# Risk Perception Benchmark Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Risk Perception Benchmark Survey is designed to help assess your risk perception and identify areas where you may need to improve your risk management practices. This survey will help you understand your risk-related attitudes, behaviors, and concerns. Please answer the questions honestly, and your responses will be kept confidential.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as they appear, and make sure to select one or more options for each question.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields marked with a star (*) symbol.
3. You can review and revise your answers before submitting the form.
