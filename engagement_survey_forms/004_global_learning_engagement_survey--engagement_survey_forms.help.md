# Global Learning Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Learning Engagement Survey is a tool to help us understand your experience with our program. Your feedback is valuable in helping us identify areas of improvement and enhance our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Enter your actual name, as this is a required field.
3. Select your level of engagement and experience with the program.
4. Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10.
5. Provide any additional suggestions for improvement.
6. Enter a valid email address for us to contact you.
7. If you have any comments, please share them with us.
8. Finally, select one of the options for submission to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (Text)**: A brief introduction to this form where you can type a few words about yourself and your experience with our program.
* **What is your name?** (`user_name`, Text, Required): This field is to collect your actual name. Please enter it as it appears on your identification documents.
* **How would you describe your level of engagement with our program?** (`engagement_level`, Text, Required): Describe your level of engagement with our program. This could be high, medium, or low.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how would you rate your experience with our program?** (`experience_rating`, Number, Required): Rate your experience with our program on a scale of 1-10. 1 means you had a very poor experience, while 10 means you had an excellent experience.
* **What do you wish to improve?** (`suggestions`, Text, Required): Share your thoughts on what we could do better.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Provide a valid email address for us to contact you about your feedback.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, Required): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them with us.
* **How would you rate your experience with our program?** (`program_experience`, Select One, Required): Select one of the provided options that best describes your experience with our program.
* **Program Experience Multiple** (`program_experience_multiple`, Select Multiple, Required): Select one or more options that best describe your experience with our program.
* **Submit** (`submission`, Select One, Required): Choose one of the options to complete and submit your form.
