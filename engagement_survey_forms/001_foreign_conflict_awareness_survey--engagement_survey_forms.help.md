# Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey, a tool designed to gather your opinions and experiences on foreign conflicts.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Start by reading the guide and understanding what each field means.
2. Read each question carefully and answer according to your personal experience and opinion.
3. Select the correct options for each field as instructed.
4. Make sure to answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey
 * **Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey** (text, text, required): This field allows you to enter your title or a brief statement about the conflict you are aware of.

### 2. Public Awareness
 * **Public Awareness** (select_one, somewhat, not very much, not at all, optional): How much do you think the public is aware of foreign conflicts?
   - Select one of the options: Somewhat, Not very much, or Not at all.

### 3. Information Sources
 * **Information Sources** (select_multiple, yes, no, optional): What sources of information do you use to stay informed about foreign conflicts?
   - Select all that apply: Yes, No.

### 4. News Sources
 * **News Sources** (text, text, optional): What news sources do you use to stay informed about foreign conflicts (e.g. TV, social media, news websites)?

### 5. Social Media
 * **Social Media** (select_multiple, yes, no, optional): How do you consume news on social media?
   - Select all that apply: Yes, No.

### 6. Government
 * **Government** (select_multiple, yes, no, optional): What sources from the government do you consult for information about foreign conflicts?
   - Select all that apply: Yes, No.

### 7. Media Sources
 * **Media Sources** (text, text, optional): What media sources do you use to stay informed about foreign conflicts (e.g. books, documentaries, newsletters))?

### 8. Social Media Sources
 * **Social Media Sources** (select_multiple, yes, no, optional): How do you consume media from social media?
   - Select all that apply: Yes, No.

### 9. Perceptions
 * **Perceptions** (select_one, very positive, somewhat positive, negative, optional): How do you perceive foreign conflicts?
   - Select one of the options: Very positive, Somewhat positive, or Negative.

### 10. Perception Sources
 * **Perception Sources** (select_multiple, yes, no, optional): What sources do you rely on for your perceptions about foreign conflicts?
   - Select all that apply: Yes, No.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* For select fields, select all that apply.
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
