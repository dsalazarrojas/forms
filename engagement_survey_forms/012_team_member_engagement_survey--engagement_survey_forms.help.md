# Team Member Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Member Engagement Survey is designed to gather information about your feelings and perceptions regarding your work experience. The survey consists of multiple questions that cover various aspects of your role and the company.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, fill in all the required fields.
- For questions that have a dropdown menu, select the relevant option from the list.
- For open-ended questions, provide a brief but clear answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **employee_name** (Required)
* This is a text field where you enter your name for reference and identification purposes.

### 2. **job_title** (Optional)
* This field is used to describe your job title. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.

### 3. **job_satisfaction** (Required)
* This question asks how satisfied you are with your job on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".

### 4. **communication_with_management** (Optional)
* This question inquires about your satisfaction with communication from management. Feel free to provide any feedback or thoughts you have on this matter.

### 5. **communication_with_colleagues** (Optional)
* Similar to the previous question, this field is about your satisfaction with communication from colleagues. Again, provide any feedback or thoughts you may have.

### 6. **work_life_balance** (Optional)
* This question asks about how you feel about your work-life balance, again on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very poorly" and 5 is "Very well".

### 7. **team_satisfaction** (Required)
* This question is similar to the previous one, asking about your satisfaction with your team on a scale of 1-5.

### 8. **company_satisfaction** (Optional)
* This question inquires about your overall satisfaction with the company. Feel free to provide any feedback or thoughts you have.

### 9. **overall_satisfaction** (Required)
* Similar to previous questions, this is on a scale of 1-5, asking about your overall satisfaction with the company on a scale of 1-5.

### 10. **feedback** (Optional)
* Check all that apply if you have any feedback for the company or team regarding your work experience.

### 11. **action_plan** (Optional)
* Check all that apply if you have an action plan to improve your work experience or the team's performance.

### 12. **next_steps** (Optional)
* This field is for any further comments or thoughts you may have about your experience or the team.
