# Faculty Engagement Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Engagement Feedback Form is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions on your level of engagement with the university or institution. The information collected will help us understand your experiences, suggestions, and outcomes, ultimately informing future engagement initiatives. Please take a few minutes to read through each section carefully and answer accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully.
2. Answer the questions honestly and accurately based on your experiences with the university or institution.
3. Provide detailed descriptions or explanations where applicable.
4. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Institution** (1)
* **Institution**: The name of your university or institution.

### **Department** (2)
* **Department**: The department where you are employed or affiliated with. This is where you work or study.

### **Faculty Member** (3)
* **Faculty Member**: A unique identifier for your faculty member profile.

### **Feedback** (4)
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts on your level of engagement with the university or institution. This could include any positive or negative experiences, suggestions, or concerns.

### **Suggestions** (5)
* **Suggestions**: Indicate if you have any suggestions for improving university engagement. You can choose one or multiple options.

### **Expected Change** (6)
* **Expected Change**: Indicate when you expected the engagement initiatives to have a positive impact on your work or studies. Please select one of the provided time frames.

### **Actual Change** (7)
* **Actual Change**: Describe, if any, the actual changes you experienced as a result of the engagement initiatives. Provide a detailed explanation if applicable.

### **Expected Outcome** (8)
* **Expected Outcome**: Indicate if you had expected a positive outcome from the engagement initiatives. You can choose one or multiple options.

### **Actual Outcome** (9)
* **Actual Outcome**: Describe, if any, the actual outcomes you experienced as a result of the engagement initiatives. Provide a detailed explanation if applicable.

Please ensure that you have completed all fields before submitting the form. If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the relevant support staff.
