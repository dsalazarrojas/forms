# onboarding_process_improvement_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The onboarding process is a crucial step in ensuring new hires feel welcome and prepared to perform their job duties. This form aims to collect feedback on the onboarding process from managers or HR personnel, focusing on areas that require improvement and how to make the process more effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field to ensure we can contact you for further clarification or follow-up.
3. Fill in the "phone" field with your phone number.
4. Confirm that your email address is correct by selecting the "True" option in the "email_confirmed" field.
5. Confirm that your phone number is correct by selecting the "True" option in the "phone_confirmed" field.
6. Select the correct option for the "new hire" field, depending on when you started your new hire.
7. Provide any comments or feedback in the "comments" field if you have any suggestions or observations.
8. Select the correct option for "feedback" to indicate when the new hire received feedback from their manager.
9. Select the correct option for "feedback_to_manager" to indicate when you provided feedback to your manager.
10. Enter the date and time you submitted this form using the "submission_date" and "submission_time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **onboarding_process_improvement_survey** (text, text, not required): Please enter your name or title.
* **new_hire** (text, text, required): This field is used to identify when you started your new hire. Please select "new hire first week" if you started within the first week of your employment, "new hire after two weeks" if you started after two weeks, or "new hire after three weeks" if you started after three weeks.
* **feedback** (select_one, select_one, required): This field is used to indicate when the new hire received feedback from their manager. Please select "new hire first week" if you received feedback within the first week of your employment, "new hire after two weeks" if you received feedback after two weeks, or "new hire after three weeks" if you received feedback after three weeks.
* **comments** (text, text, not required): If you have any suggestions or observations on the onboarding process, please enter them in this field.
* **manager** (text, text, not required): This field is not currently used in this form.
* **feedback_to_manager** (select_multiple, select_multiple, not required): This field is used to indicate when you provided feedback to your manager. Please select all the options that apply.
* **submission_date** (date, date, not required): This field is used to track the submission date and time of this form.
* **submission_time** (time, time, not required): This field is used to track the submission time of this form.
* **email** (email, email, required): Please enter your email address to ensure we can contact you for further clarification or follow-up.
* **phone** (text, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **email_confirmed** (select_one, select_one, required): Confirm that your email address is correct by selecting "True".
* **phone_confirmed** (select_one, select_one, required): Confirm that your phone number is correct by selecting "True".
* **submission_date** (date, date, required): Please enter the date you submitted this form.
* **submission_time** (time, time, required): Please enter the time you submitted this form.
* **phone** (text, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **email_confirmed** (select_one, select_one, required): Confirm that your email address is correct by selecting "True".
