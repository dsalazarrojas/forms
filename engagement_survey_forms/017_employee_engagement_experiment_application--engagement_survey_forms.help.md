# Employee Engagement Experiment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees about their engagement with their workplace. It's likely a tool for HR departments or management teams to gather feedback on various aspects of employee experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with a brief description of the form's purpose.
2. Choose a category from the **Form Category** dropdown list that best describes the form's topic (e.g., "Engagement Survey Forms").
3. Enter a brief description of the form in the **Form Description** field.
4. Enter your **User ID** if you have one.
5. Enter the **Form Type** (e.g., "Survey").
6. Enter the **Submission URL** if you have one.
7. Select the **Assigned Tool** (e.g., "Google Forms").
8. Fill in the **Category 1** through **Category 15** fields with brief descriptions of the categories you're surveying.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: Enter a brief description of the form's purpose.
* **Form Category**: Choose a category that best describes the form's topic. This will help sort and filter forms for future reference.
* **Form Description**: Enter a brief description of the form's purpose and content.
* **User ID**: Enter your unique identifier if you have one. This helps track responses and ensure anonymity.
* **Form Type**: Select the type of form (e.g., "Survey").
* **Submission URL**: Enter the URL where the form was submitted from.
* **Created At**: This field is automatically populated and indicates when the form was created.
* **Updated At**: This field is automatically populated and indicates when the form was last updated.
* **Form ID**: This field is automatically populated and uniquely identifies the form.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool used to create and manage the form (e.g., "Google Forms").
* **Category 1** through **Category 15**: Enter brief descriptions of the categories you're surveying. These fields are designed to help you categorize and filter responses.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the form administrator at form@gic.mx.
* Remember, this form is designed to help improve employee engagement, so your input is valuable!
