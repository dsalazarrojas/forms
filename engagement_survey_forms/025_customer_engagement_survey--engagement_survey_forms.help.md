# Customer Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Engagement Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the company's customer service team. This form is meant to help us understand how we can improve our support processes and ensure that our customers have a positive experience.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the page number to begin answering the questions for that page.
2. For each question, choose the relevant answer from the options provided.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Customer Service Experience (Page 1)
- **Customer Service Experience** (1): Answer to this question will help us understand your overall satisfaction with our customer service experience.

### 2. Satisfaction (Page 1)
- **Satisfaction** (2): This question helps us understand how satisfied you are with your experience.

### 3. Communication Effectiveness (Page 1)
- **Communication Effectiveness** (3, text): Please provide a short comment on how our communication was effective or ineffective.

### 4. Respond to Inquiries (Page 1)
- **Respond to Inquiries** (4, select_one): Rate how quickly our representative responded to your inquiry.

### 5. Resolution of Concerns (Page 1)
- **Resolution of Concerns** (5, select_multiple): Choose all the ways in which your concerns were resolved.

### 6. Follow-up (Page 1)
- **Follow-up** (6, select_one): Rate how often our representative follows up with you after an interaction.

### 7. Employee Attitude (Page 1)
- **Employee Attitude** (7, select_multiple): Rate the attitude of our customer service representatives.

### 8. Knowledge Base (Page 1)
- **Knowledge Base** (8, select_one): Rate how well our representatives have a thorough knowledge of our products and services.

### 9. Customer Service Representatives (Page 1)
- **Customer Service Representatives** (9, select_one): Rate the friendliness of our customer service representatives.

### 10. Resolution Method (Page 1)
- **Resolution Method** (10, select_one): Choose the method used to resolve your issue.

### 11. Time of Resolution (Page 1)
- **Time of Resolution** (11, select_one): Rate how long it took to resolve your issue.

### 12. Resolution Method Contact (Page 1)
- **Resolution Method Contact** (12, select_one): Choose the contact method used to resolve your issue.

### 13. Follow-up Time (Page 1)
- **Follow-up Time** (13, select_one): Rate how often you receive follow-ups after an interaction.

Note: Please answer each question as honestly and accurately as possible. Your feedback is invaluable to us and helps us improve our customer service experience.
