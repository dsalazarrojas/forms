# Identity Change Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Identity Change Survey form is used to collect information from employees who are changing their identity, including name, email, phone number, and date of birth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "First Name", "Last Name", "Email", "Phone Number", and "Date of Birth" fields with your current information.
2. If you are changing your identity, fill in the corresponding "New" fields (e.g., "New First Name", "New Email", etc.).
3. Provide a reason for the change in the "Reason for Change" field.
4. Make sure to complete all required fields accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your current first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your current last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your current email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your current phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your current date of birth.
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`, text, required): Explain the reason for your identity change.
* **New First Name** (`new_first_name`, text, required): Enter your new first name (if changing).
* **New Last Name** (`new_last_name`, text, required): Enter your new last name (if changing).
* **New Email** (`new_email`, email, required): Enter your new email address (if changing).
* **New Phone Number** (`new_phone_number`, text, required): Enter your new phone number (if changing).
* **New Date of Birth** (`new_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your new date of birth (if changing).
* **New Reason for Change** (`new_reason_for_change`, text, required): Explain the reason for your new reason for change (if changing).

Note: This form is designed to be completed accurately and carefully. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
