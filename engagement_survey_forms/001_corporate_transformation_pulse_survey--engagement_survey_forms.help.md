# Corporate Transformation Pulse Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on their sentiment and confidence towards corporate transformation initiatives. The data gathered will help inform and improve the effectiveness of these initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Select one of the available options for "Overall Employee Sentiment" and "Confidence in Change". These fields are required.
2. For "Communication Effectiveness", enter a numerical value (0-10) to indicate how effectively information is being communicated to you about the transformation initiatives.
3. If you choose to answer "Confidence in Change Rating", you will have an optional text field to provide more details on your confidence in change.
4. If you have any additional comments or thoughts on the confidence in change, you can express them in the free-text field "Confidence in Change".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Overall Employee Sentiment
* **Overall Employee Sentiment** (`select_one`, required): This field allows you to express your overall sentiment towards the corporate transformation initiatives. Please select one of the available options: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.

### Confidence in Change
* **Confidence in Change** (`select_one`, required): This field measures your confidence in the effectiveness of the transformation initiatives. Please select one of the available options: High, Medium, or Low.

### Confidence in Change Rating
* **Confidence in Change Rating** (`note`, optional): If you have a specific rating for your confidence in the effectiveness of the transformation initiatives, you can provide a numerical value (0-10) in this text field.

### Communication Effectiveness
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`number`, required): This numerical field asks you to rate how effectively information is being communicated to you about the transformation initiatives. Please enter a value between 0 and 10.

### Confidence in Change
* **Confidence in Change** (`text`, optional): This free-text field is for any additional comments or thoughts you have on your confidence in change. If you feel the need to express more than the previous fields, you can use this field to elaborate.
