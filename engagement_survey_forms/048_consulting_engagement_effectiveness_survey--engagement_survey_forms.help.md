# Consulting Engagement Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather information about the consulting engagement that was conducted with the client. The feedback collected through this survey will help assess the effectiveness of the engagement and provide valuable insights that can be used to improve future engagements and enhance the client relationship.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by reviewing the form**: Before starting to complete the form, take a moment to read through it and understand what is being asked.
2. **Fill out the form**: Answer each question in the space provided, being as specific as possible.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send it to the intended recipient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name**: This field is optional and allows you to provide the name of the client that was engaged with.
* **Project scope**: Briefly describe the project scope or the objectives of the engagement.
* **Achieved objectives**: Select one of the options to indicate to what extent the objectives of the engagement were met:
	+ All objectives met
	+ Most objectives met
	+ Some objectives met
	+ Objectives not met
* **Stakeholder satisfaction**: Select one of the options to indicate the overall satisfaction from stakeholders:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Measurable outcomes**: Provide specific key metrics that improved as a result of the engagement (optional).
* **Lessons learned**: Provide any important lessons or observations that were noted during the engagement (optional).

## Tips

* Be honest and objective when answering the questions.
* Provide specific examples or instances that illustrate your answers.
* Take a moment to review the form before submitting it to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, include them in the "Lessons learned" field.
