# Manager Feedback Responsiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manager Feedback Responsiveness Survey is designed to collect feedback from managers on their response times to team members' requests. This form helps gauge how quickly managers are responding, which is crucial for effective communication and collaboration within the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email.
4. Select your Job Title.
5. Enter your Department.
6. Select how quickly you typically respond to requests from team members (0-15 minutes, 15-30 minutes, 30-45 minutes, or 45-60 minutes).
7. Select a date for when you last responded to a team member's request.
8. Enter any notes about your interactions with team members.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required: false): Please enter your first name as it is written on your business card or ID badge.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required: false): Please enter your last name as it is written on your business card or ID badge.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your company email address.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required: false): Enter your job title as it appears on your company's organizational chart.
* **Department** (Department, text, required: false): Enter the name of the department you belong to.
* **Manager Response Time** (Manager Response Time, select_one, required: false): Select how quickly you typically respond to team members' requests. Please choose from the options:
	+ 0-15 minutes
	+ 15-30 minutes
	+ 30-45 minutes
	+ 45-60 minutes
* **Response Date** (Response Date, date, required: false): Select a date for when you last responded to a team member's request.
* **Manager Note** (Manager Note, note, required: false): Enter any notes about your interactions with team members. This can include information about specific requests, successes, or challenges.
