# Diversity And Equity In Research Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about diversity and equity in research environments. The purpose of this survey is to gather data on individual experiences, perceptions, and opinions on diversity and inclusion in research settings. Your input will help us better understand the current state of diversity and equity in research environments and inform future initiatives to create a more inclusive and diverse research community.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the order they appear.
2. Select the options that best fit your answers.
3. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes you have.
