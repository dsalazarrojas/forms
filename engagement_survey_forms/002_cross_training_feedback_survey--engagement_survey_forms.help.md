# cross_training_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cross-training feedback survey is designed to gather information from participants who have undergone cross-training programs. This feedback will help us improve our training programs to better meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by reading the introduction page.
2. Enter your participant name (if you want to provide feedback, even if not required).
3. Select the name of the training program you participated in.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the program (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
5. Enter any comments or feedback you have about the program.
6. Rate the facilitators' performance (excellent, good, average, bad, terrible).
7. Rate the quality of the materials used during the program (excellent, good, average, bad, terrible).
8. Rate the facilities or environment of the program (excellent, good, average, bad, terrible).
9. Rate the time management of the program (excellent, good, average, bad, terrible).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to the cross training feedback survey**: This page is an introduction to the form and is required to be filled.
* **participant_name**: Enter the name of the participant who is providing feedback. (Optional)
* **what is the training program name**: Enter the name of the training program you participated in.
* **rating over all satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the program, using the following options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **Enter your feedback comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you have about the program. (Required)
* **rating facilitators**: Rate the performance of the facilitators, using the following options: excellent, good, average, bad, terrible.
* **rating materials**: Rate the quality of the materials used during the program, using the following options: excellent, good, average, bad, terrible.
* **rating facilities**: Rate the facilities or environment of the program, using the following options: excellent, good, average, bad, terrible.
* **rating time management**: Rate the time management of the program, using the following options: excellent, good, average, bad, terrible.
