# Teacher Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this Teacher Engagement Survey is to collect feedback and engagement metrics from teachers in the education sector. This information will help us understand how our tools and services are being used and how we can improve them to better support teachers like you.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Make sure to answer all the required fields, as indicated by the "required" tag next to each field.
- If a field is optional, feel free to provide as much information as you like, but do not be shy to submit the form without filling it out if you don't have an answer or prefer not to share it.
- If you are unsure about any field, try your best to provide the most accurate information you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **First Name** (text, optional)
This field is for your first name. Please enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed.

### 2. **Last Name** (text, optional)
This field is for your last name. Please enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed.

### 3. **Email** (email, optional)
This field is for your contact email. Please enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about your feedback.

### 4. **Phone Number** (text, optional)
This field is for your contact phone number. If you would like us to get in touch with you by phone, please enter your phone number here.

### 5. **Teacher ID** (number, optional)
This field is for your teacher ID. Please enter your teacher ID if you have one.

### 6. **Department** (text, optional)
This field is for your department. Please enter the name of your department at your school.

### 7. **School Administrators** (select multiple, optional)
Select all the school administrators you have interacted with or would like to interact with. Please choose from the following options: Principal, Director.

### 8. **Teacher Survey** (select one, optional)
This field is to gauge your level of engagement with our tools and services. Please select one of the following options: Strongly agree, Neither, Strongly Disagree.

### 9. **Engagement** (number, optional)
This field is to provide an indication of your level of engagement with our tools and services. Please enter a number between 1 and 10, where 1 means low engagement and 10 means high engagement.

### 10. **Date of Birth** (date, optional)
This field is for your date of birth. Please enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### 11. **Time of Birth** (time, optional)
This field is for your time of birth. Please enter your time of birth in the format hh:mm AM/PM.

### 12. **Notes** (note, optional)
This field is for any additional comments or notes you might have about your experience with our tools and services.

### 13. **Assigned Tools** (select multiple, optional)
Select all the tools that have been assigned to you. Please choose from the following options: chatjimmy, Other.

Note: This is a sample form and actual fields and options may vary depending on the specific form version.
