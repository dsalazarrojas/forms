# Employee Motivation Reward Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register employees for the motivation and reward program. The program aims to boost employee morale and motivation by recognizing and rewarding their achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Make sure you are eligible for the program. Check with your manager and HR department to confirm your eligibility.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, using the following fields:
3. Click submit to send the form to your manager for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name as it appears on their official identification documents.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID number as assigned by the company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department or team name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title as assigned by the company.
* **Preferred Reward** (`preferred_reward`, select one, optional): Choose your preferred reward type from the options provided. You can select only one reward option.
* **Reward Frequency** (`reward_frequency`, select multiple, optional): Choose the frequencies at which you would like to receive your rewards from the options provided. You can select multiple frequencies.
* **Reward Amount** (`reward_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you would like to receive as a reward. Please note that this is optional and only applies to reward frequencies that have an associated reward amount.
* **Reward Type** (`reward_type`, select one, optional): Choose the type of reward you would prefer from the options provided. You can select only one reward type.
* **Employee Consent** (`employee_consent`, select multiple, optional): Choose whether you consent to the terms and conditions of the reward program. Please select at least one option to consent.
* **Manager Consent** (`manager_consent`, select multiple, optional): Choose whether your manager consents to the terms and conditions of the reward program. Please select at least one option to consent.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that you have filled out all required information accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your manager or HR department for clarification.
- Once you have submitted the form, it will be reviewed and approved by your manager.
