# lgbtq_allyship_in_nonprofits_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help organizations assess their level of LGBTQ+ allyship and identify areas for improvement. It's a survey that collects information on the organization's current state of LGBTQ+ allyship, including training and awareness, and provides insights into areas where they can improve.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. For select multiple-choice questions, choose all that apply.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **lgbtq_inclusion** (`lgbtq_inclusion`, text, optional): This question asks about your organization's current level of LGBTQ+ inclusion. Please provide a brief description of your organization's policies and practices regarding LGBTQ+ inclusion.
* **allyship_training** (`allyship_training`, select_one, optional): This question inquires about the level of allyship training provided to staff. Choose 'True' if your organization offers regular allyship training, 'False' if not, or 'Prefer not to say' if you're unsure.
* **progress_tracking** (`progress_tracking`, date, optional): This question is about tracking progress in LGBTQ+ allyship. If your organization has a system to track progress, provide the date of the most recent update.
* **barriers** (`barriers`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the barriers that your organization faces in promoting LGBTQ+ allyship. Examples include 'Communication barriers', 'Lack of training', and 'Fear of saying the wrong thing'.
* **training_necessary** (`training_necessary`, select_multiple, optional): Identify which areas of training you believe are necessary to improve LGBTQ+ allyship. Choose from 'Communication skills', 'Understanding the LGBTQ+ community', 'Leadership and management', or 'Other'.
* **lgbtq_awareness** (`lgbtq_awareness`, time, optional): This question is about your organization's awareness of LGBTQ+ issues. If you have a specific date or time frame for when awareness began, please provide it.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): This is an open-ended field for any additional feedback or comments you'd like to share about your organization's LGBTQ+ allyship.
* **email_address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter a contact email address for your organization's LGBTQ+ allyship lead or liaison.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter a contact phone number for your organization's LGBTQ+ allyship lead or liaison.
