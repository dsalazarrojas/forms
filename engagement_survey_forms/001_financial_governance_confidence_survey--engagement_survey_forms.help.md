# Financial Governance Confidence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Financial Governance Confidence Survey is a tool used to gauge the confidence level of employees in the financial governance of your company. This survey aims to understand your opinions and experiences regarding the company's financial performance, strategy, and overall governance practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly, considering your current experience and observations within the company.
2. Select the options that best reflect your thoughts on each question.
3. If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Governance** (`Financial Governance`, `note`, optional): This section is a brief introduction to the survey and explains the purpose of the Financial Governance Confidence Survey.
* **Who is the CEO of your company** (`leadership`, `select_one`, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you are aware of the current CEO, 'No' otherwise.
* **Financial Performance** (`financial_performance`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the ways you perceive the financial performance of your company, from 'Yes' (you are satisfied) to 'No' (you are not satisfied).
* **How do you feel about the company's overall strategy?** (`strategy`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Poor' to indicate your level of satisfaction with the company's overall strategy.
* **How do you feel about communication between the CEO and team members?** (`communication`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'Yes', 'No', or both to indicate the level of communication you experience between the CEO and team members.
* **How do you feel about decision-making within the company?** (`decision_making`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose 'Yes', 'No', or both to indicate your confidence in decision-making processes within the company.
* **Risk Management** (`risk_management`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'Yes', 'No', or both to indicate your level of confidence in the company's risk management practices.
* **What do you think is the biggest compliance issue in your company?** (`compliance`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose from 'Yes', 'No', or both to indicate your perception of compliance issues in your company.
* **How would you rate the quality of our accounting and financial management?** (`finance`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'Good', 'Poor', or both to rate the quality of accounting and financial management.
* **How do you perceive your level of involvement in the decision-making process of your company?** (`governance`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose 'High', 'Low', or both to indicate your level of involvement in decision-making processes.
* **What do you think about the level of management accountability?** (`management`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'High', 'Low', or both to indicate your perception of management accountability.
* **How do you perceive the level of regular review and feedback from your manager or leader?** (`review`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose 'High', 'Low', or both to indicate your perception of regular review and feedback.
