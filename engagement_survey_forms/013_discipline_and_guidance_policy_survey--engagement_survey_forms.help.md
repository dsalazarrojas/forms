# discipline_and_guidance_policy_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help the organization understand the current state of policy awareness and engagement among its employees, and to collect feedback and suggestions for policy improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Be specific in your responses to provide accurate and helpful feedback.
3. If you have any concerns or suggestions, feel free to express them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a short text field where you can provide a brief introduction or context about your answers.
* **What is the current level of policy awareness among employees in your team/department?** (`policy_awareness`): This question is used to gauge the current level of policy awareness among employees. You can choose from the following options:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **How effective do you think the policy is in its current form?** (`policy_effectiveness`): This question is used to understand how you think the policy is effective. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Not Very Effective
* **Do you have any improvement ideas for the policy?** (`improvement_ideas`): This is a text field where you can provide any improvement ideas you may have for the policy.
* **Do you have an email address that we can contact you at?** (`email`): This is the email address where you can be contacted.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`): This is your phone number.
* **What are your comments about this survey?** (`comments_1`): This is a text field where you can provide any comments or feedback about the survey.
* **Do you have a preferred way of communication (e.g., email, phone, or in-person)?** (`communication_pref`): This question is used to understand your preferred way of communication.
* **What is your level of engagement with the policy?** (`engagement_level`): This question is used to gauge your level of engagement with the policy. You can choose from the following options:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **What are your concerns or suggestions for the policy?** (`comments_2`): This is a text field where you can provide any concerns or suggestions about the policy.
* **How effective do you think the policy is overall?** (`policy_effectiveness_level`): This question is used to understand how you think the policy is effective overall. You can choose from the following options:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Not Very Effective
* **Do you have any comments or feedback about the policy?** (`comments_3`): This is a text field where you can provide any comments or feedback about the policy.
* **What is your email address?** (`comments_4`): This is your email address.
* **Comments 6** (`comments_6`): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (`policy_improvement`): This is a text field where you can provide any suggestions for policy improvement.
* **Any final comments?** (`comments_8`): This is a text field where you can provide any final comments.
