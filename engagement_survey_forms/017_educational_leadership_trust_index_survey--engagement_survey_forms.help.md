# Educational Leadership Trust Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Leadership Trust Index Survey is designed to measure the level of trust a school leader has towards their team, stakeholders, or peers. This information is crucial in identifying areas of improvement and developing effective leadership strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, select the school from the dropdown menu in the `school_name` field.
2. Choose the size of the school from the dropdown menu in the `school_size` field. You can select one or multiple sizes that apply to your school.
3. Select the leadership style that best describes your approach to leadership in the `leadership_style` field.
4. Indicate the level of trust you have towards your team, stakeholders, or peers in the `trust_level` field. You can select one or multiple levels that apply to your situation.
5. In the `communication` field, provide a brief description of your communication style and how you engage with your team, stakeholders, or peers.
6. In the `leadership_team` field, describe your leadership team's composition and dynamics.
7. If you have any feedback or suggestions for improvement, please provide it in the `feedback` field.
8. In the `open_ended_question` field, you can provide any additional information or insights that you think are relevant to the survey.
9. Finally, in the `follow_up_question` field, you can ask us a question about the survey or provide any further details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school_name** (`school_id`, select_one, required): Select the name of the school you are evaluating.
* **school_size** (`school_size`, select_multiple, required): Choose the size of the school that applies to your situation. You can select one or multiple sizes.
* **goals** (`goals`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your school's goals and objectives. 
* **leadership_style** (`leadership_style`, select_one, required): Select the leadership style that best describes your approach to leadership.
* **trust_level** (`trust_level`, select_multiple, required): Indicate the level of trust you have towards your team, stakeholders, or peers. You can select one or multiple levels.
* **communication** (`communication`, text, optional): Describe your communication style and how you engage with your team, stakeholders, or peers.
* **leadership_team** (`leadership_team`, text, optional): Describe your leadership team's composition and dynamics.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or suggestions for improvement.
* **open_ended_question** (`open_ended_question`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or insights that you think are relevant to the survey.
* **follow_up_question** (`follow_up_question`, text, optional): Ask us a question about the survey or provide any further details.
* **follow_up_answer** (`follow_up_answer`, text, optional): This is a field for follow-up answers.
