# Stakeholder Engagement Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Stakeholder Engagement Survey Form is designed to collect information from stakeholders about their experiences, opinions, and concerns regarding a project. This form will help project managers and team members understand the stakeholder's level of engagement and provide a better understanding of the project's overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each stakeholder you are surveying.
2. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in each field.
3. Ensure that you are not duplicating any previous entries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Outcome** (`project_outcome_label`, text, optional): Briefly describe the project's outcome or result.
* **Stakeholder Name** (`stakeholder_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the stakeholder being surveyed.
* **Role In Project** (`role_in_project_label`, select_one, optional): Select the role of the stakeholder in the project:
	+ Stakeholder
	+ Project Team Member
	+ Project Team Lead
	+ Project Manager
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter the stakeholder's email address (if applicable).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the stakeholder's contact number (if applicable).
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the stakeholder's department (if applicable).
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the stakeholder's location (if applicable).
* **Stakeholder Survey Comments** (`stakeholder_survey_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the stakeholder's engagement.
* **Engagement Score** (`engagement_score`, number, optional): Enter a score from 0 to 10 to indicate the stakeholder's engagement level with the project.
* **Date Of Survey** (`date_of_survey`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the survey was conducted.
* **Time Of Survey** (`time_of_survey`, time, optional): Enter the time at which the survey was conducted.

Note: All fields are optional, but please ensure that you fill out as much information as possible to provide a comprehensive understanding of the stakeholder's engagement.
