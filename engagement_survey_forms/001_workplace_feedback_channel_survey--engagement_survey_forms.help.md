# Workplace Feedback Channel Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey aimed at gathering data about the effectiveness of workplace communication channels. The goal is to help teams understand their strengths and weaknesses and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the channel you use most frequently for reporting or communication. You can choose from the options provided in Field 1.
2. If you have used other channels not listed, please specify them in Field 2.
3. Indicate how often you respond to messages in Field 3.
4. Choose the time taken to respond to messages in Field 4. This field provides a range of options for response time.
5. Rate the level of satisfaction with response time in Field 5.
6. Enter the average time taken to respond to messages in Field 6 and Field 7.
7. Calculate the difference between the average response time (earlier) and (later) in Field 9.
8. Rate the level of satisfaction with response time taken by managers to respond to messages in Field 10.
9. Enter the average time taken by managers to respond to messages in Field 11, Field 12, and Field 13.
10. Calculate the difference between the average response time by managers (earlier) and (later) in Field 14.
11. In fields 15 to 23, please enter any additional comments or feedback about the communication channels.
