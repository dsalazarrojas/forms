# Collaboration Workflow Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from team members about their experience with the collaboration workflow, including their satisfaction with the current workflow, biggest challenges they face, and their preferred communication channel.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and provide honest feedback.
- Answer all questions that apply to you or your role in the team.
- If you have additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Respondent name**: Enter your name, if you'd like to be identified as the respondent.
  (Text, Optional)
- **Team or department**: Select the team or department you belong to.
  (Text, Optional)
- **Satisfaction with current workflow**: Rate your satisfaction with the current workflow on a scale from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
  (Select one of 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5)
- **Biggest challenge in collaboration**: Describe the biggest challenge you face in collaboration.
  (Short text)
- **Tools used for collaboration**: Select all tools you use for collaboration.
  (Select multiple options: Email, Chat, Project management, Shared documents)
- **Suggestions for improvement**: Provide practical ideas for improving the collaboration workflow.
  (Short text)
- **Preferred communication channel**: Select your preferred communication channel for collaboration.
  (Select one of Email, Chat, Video calls, In person)
- **Is training required?**: Indicate if you feel additional training is needed for collaboration.
  (Select True or False)
- **Additional comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the collaboration workflow.
  (Free text)
