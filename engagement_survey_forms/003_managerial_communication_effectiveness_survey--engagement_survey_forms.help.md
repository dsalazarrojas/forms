# Managerial Communication Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the effectiveness of managerial communication within an organization. It assesses the frequency of feedback, approachability, and communication skills used by managers to ensure clear and effective communication. This survey aims to identify areas of improvement in managerial communication.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please read the introduction on the first page.
2. Answer each question on the subsequent pages according to your experiences or observations.
3. Choose the most suitable options for each question.
4. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, note, required: false): This is a brief overview of the survey and its purpose. Please read this section to understand the context and objectives of the form.
* **Managerial Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, text, required: false): Describe your observations or experiences about the communication skills of managers, such as their ability to clearly articulate their thoughts, actively listen to others, and provide constructive feedback.
* **Approachability** (`approachability`, select_one, required: false): Choose how approachable you find the manager. Options include:
	+ Option 1: Very approachable
	+ Option 2: Somewhat approachable
	+ Option 3: Not very approachable
	+ Option 4: Not at all approachable
	+ Other: Please describe your answer
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the channels used by the manager for communication, such as email, phone, meetings, or text messages. You can also choose "Other" if the channel is not listed.
* **Listening Skills** (`listening_skills`, text, required: false): Describe the manager's listening skills, such as their ability to actively listen to others, ask clarifying questions, and provide feedback.
* **Frequency of Feedback** (`feedback_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Choose how often you receive feedback from the manager:
	+ Option 1: Very often
	+ Option 2: Sometimes
	+ Option 3: Rarely
	+ Option 4: Never
	+ Other: Please describe your answer
* **Development Efforts** (`development_efforts`, text, required: false): Describe any additional efforts or initiatives the manager has taken to support the development and growth of team members.
