# Team Superlative Nominations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather nominations for team members to receive awards or recognition within their organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team member's name in the "Nominee Name" field.
2. Describe why the nominee deserves the award or recognition in the "Reason for Nomination" field.
3. Select the category that the nominee fits into (Team Player, Other).
4. Select the reason for the nomination (Team Player, Other).
5. Choose the nomination date for the award.
6. Select the team leadership level of the nominee (Team Lead, Other).
7. Select the nomination frequency (Less than 1 in 2 months, 1 in 2-3 months, etc.).
8. Select the recognition frequency (Less than 1 in 2 months, 1 in 2-3 months, etc.).
9. Select the department(s) of the team member (Sales, Operations, etc.).
10. Enter up to 3 team member nominations, separated by commas.
11. Enter up to 3 team lead nominations, separated by commas.
12. Select the award frequency (1-2 times, 3-5 times, etc.).
13. Enter any additional comments about the team member or award.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Member** (`team_nomination`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the team member being nominated.
* **Nominee Name** (`name_of_nominee`, `text`, optional): Enter the full name of the team member being nominated.
* **Reason for Nomination** (`reason_for_nomination`, `text`, optional): Describe in 1-2 sentences why the team member deserves the award.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, optional): Select the category that the team member fits into (Team Player, Other).
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the reason for the nomination (Team Player, Other).
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the nomination.
* **Team Leadership** (`team_leadership`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the team leadership level of the team member (Team Lead, Other).
* **Communication** (`communication`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the level of communication skills of the team member (Communication, Other).
* **Adaptability** (`adaptability`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the level of adaptability skills of the team member (Adaptability, Other).
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the level of collaboration skills of the team member (Collaboration, Other).
* **Initiative** (`initiative`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the level of initiative skills of the team member (Initiative, Other).
* **Results Driven** (`results_driven`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the level of results driven skills of the team member (Results Driven, Other).
* **Recognition Worth** (`recognition_worth`, `select_one`, optional): Select the level of recognition worthiness of the team member (Highly Recognizable, Somewhat Recognizable, Not Recognizable).
* **Award Worth** (`award_worth`, `select_one`, optional): Select the level of award worthiness of the team member (Highly Worth, Somewhat Worth, Not Worth).
* **Award Type** (`award_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of award or recognition (Cash, Gift, Other).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments about the team member or award.
* **Team Leadership Level** (`team_leadership_level`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the team leadership level of the team member (Team Lead, Other).
* **Nomination Frequency** (`nomination_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the nomination frequency (Less than 1 in 2 months, 1 in 2-3 months, etc.).
* **Recognition Frequency** (`recognition_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the recognition frequency (Less than 1 in 2 months, 1 in 2-3 months, etc.).
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the team lead level of the team member (Team Lead, Other).
* **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the department of the team member (Sales, Operations, etc.).
* **Team Member Nominations** (`team_member_nomination`, `text`, optional): Enter up to 3 team member nominations, separated by commas.
* **Team Lead Nominations** (`team_lead_nomination`, `text`, optional): Enter up to 3 team lead nominations, separated by commas.
* **Award Frequency** (`award_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the award frequency (1-2 times, 3-5 times, etc.).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about this form.
