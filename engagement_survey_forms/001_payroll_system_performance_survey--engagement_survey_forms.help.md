# payroll_system_performance_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The payroll system performance survey is designed to collect feedback and performance data from employees about their current payroll system. This data will help our team evaluate and improve the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply click on the "Submit" button at the end of each page to move on to the next page. Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with a red asterisk (*). If you encounter any issues, please contact your HR representative for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Payroll System Performance Survey** (Page 1)
* **Payroll System Performance Survey** (payroll_system_performance_survey_page_1): This is the title of the form, it's just a text field for you to fill out and describe your thoughts about the current payroll system.

### 2. **Employee Name** (employee_name)
* **Employee Name** (employee_name): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or personnel card.

### 3. **Payroll System Rating** (payroll_system_rating)
* **Payroll System Rating** (payroll_system_rating): Rate your current payroll system from 1-5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is excellent.

### 4. **Employee ID** (employee_id)
* **Employee ID** (employee_id): Please enter your employee ID number as it appears on your ID badge or personnel card.

### 5. **Department** (department)
* **Department** (department): Select the department where you currently work: 
  - Finance
  - HR
  - IT
  - Marketing
  - Operations

### 6. **Payroll Frequency** (payroll_frequency)
* **Payroll Frequency** (payroll_frequency): Select how frequently you currently receive your pay: 
  - Monthly
  - Bimonthly
  - Quartanly (Note: this option might not be available in your area)
  - Bi-Quarterly
  - Yearly

### 7. **Payroll Method** (payroll_method)
* **Payroll Method** (payroll_method): Choose how you currently process your pay: 
  - In-house
  - Third Party

### 8. **Payroll Frequency Method** (payroll_frequency_method)
* **Payroll Frequency Method** (payroll_frequency_method): Select how frequently your pay is processed: 
  - Automated
  - Manual

### 9. **Feedback** (feedback)
* **Feedback** (feedback): Provide any additional feedback about your payroll system experience. This is an open text field for you to share your thoughts.

### 10. **Comments** (comments)
* **Comments** (comments): Leave any additional comments about your payroll system experience.

### 11. **Email** (email)
* **Email** (email): Please enter your work email.

### 12. **Phone** (phone)
* **Phone** (phone): Please enter your work phone number.

### 13. **Date** (date)
* **Date** (date): Select the date you are completing this form.

### 14. **Time** (time)
* **Time** (time): Select the time you are completing this form.

### 15. **Payroll Survey Rating** (payroll_survey_rating)
* **Payroll Survey Rating** (payroll_survey_rating): Rate your overall experience with the payroll system from 1-5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is excellent.

### 16. **Payroll Survey Rating 2** (payroll_survey_rating_2)
* **Payroll Survey Rating 2** (payroll_survey_rating_2): Rate your overall experience with the payroll system from 1-5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is excellent.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields marked with a red asterisk (*).
* If you encounter any issues, please contact your HR representative for assistance.
* Please note that the "Quartanly" option is not available in some regions.
* This form is for your feedback and performance data only. Do not share your answers with anyone without explicit permission from the HR department.
