# Compensation Fairness Perception Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Compensation Fairness Perception Survey is designed to gather anonymous insights into employees' perceptions of fairness in compensation within the organization. Your participation in this survey will help us understand how employees view pay fairness and identify areas for improvement. Your responses are completely anonymous and will not influence any individual pay decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. You can complete the form in any order, but start with Page 1: -- Survey Introduction --.
3. For numeric questions, select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "very unfair" and 5 is "very fair."
4. For select_multiple questions, choose up to three factors that should influence pay decisions.
5. For select_one questions, select only one option that best represents your answer.
6. For text fields, provide brief and actionable suggestions for improving fairness in compensation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Survey Introduction --**: This page serves as an introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully to understand the survey's purpose and guidelines.
* **tenure with organization in years**: Estimate the approximate number of years you've worked with our organization.
* **how fair is pay in your view**: Rate how fair you think pay is in your view, from 1 (very unfair) to 5 (very fair).
* **clarity of pay policy**: Select how clear you think pay decisions are communicated within the organization. Options are:
	+ Very clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ Not clear
* **perceived bias in pay decisions**: Select the best option that represents your perception of bias in pay decisions. Options are:
	+ No bias
	+ Some bias
	+ Significant bias
* **factors that should influence pay**: Select up to three factors that you think should influence pay decisions. Options are:
	+ Performance
	+ Tenure
	+ Market rates
	+ Skills
	+ Diversity goals
* **suggestions for improving fairness**: Provide brief and actionable suggestions for improving fairness in compensation. Please be specific and concrete.
* **willing to participate in feedback discussion**: Select whether you are willing to discuss your feedback further with HR. Options are:
	+ True
	+ False
* **submission date**: This field is automatically populated with the date and time of your submission. Please do not fill in this field.

Note: Please complete the form at your own pace, and do not worry about running out of time. Take breaks if needed. If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact the HR department for assistance.
