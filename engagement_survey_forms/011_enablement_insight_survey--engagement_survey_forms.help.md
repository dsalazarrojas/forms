# Enablement Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Enablement Insight Survey is a tool used to gather information about enablement programs. The data collected through this form will help us better understand the progress and effectiveness of our enablement initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the requested information about the enablement program you are providing feedback on.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback on Programs** (id: 1, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the enablement program being reviewed.
* **Program Name** (id: 2, text, required: false): Enter the name of the enablement program.
* **Program Type** (id: 3, select_multiple, required: false): Select all types that apply to the program.
* **Rating** (id: 4, select_one, required: false): Choose a rating for the program based on its overall performance.
* **Comments** (id: 5, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the program.
* **Date** (id: 6, date, required: false): Enter the date you are reviewing the program.
* **Time** (id: 7, time, required: false): Enter the time you are reviewing the program.
* **Email** (id: 8, email, required: false): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone** (id: 9, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Assigned To** (id: 10, select_one, required: false): Select who the program is assigned to.
* **Category** (id: 11, select_one, required: false): Select the category of the program.
* **Subcategory** (id: 12, select_one, required: false): Select the subcategory of the program.
* **Form ID** (id: 13, text, required: false): Enter the form ID (optional).
* **Created By** (id: 14, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who created the form (optional).
* **Submitted By** (id: 15, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form (optional).
* **Submitted At** (id: 16, date, required: false): Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted To** (id: 17, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person the form was submitted to (optional).
* **Enablement Program** (id: 18, text, required: false): Enter the name of the enablement program (optional).
* **Program Type** (id: 19, select_one, required: false): Select the type of program.
* **Program Description** (id: 20, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the program.
* **Program Start Date** (id: 21, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date** (id: 22, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the program.
* **Enablement Type** (id: 23, select_one, required: false): Select the type of enablement.
* **Enablement Status** (id: 24, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the enablement.
