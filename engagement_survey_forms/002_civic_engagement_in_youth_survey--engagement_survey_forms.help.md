# Civic Engagement In Youth Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information on the civic engagement habits and attitudes of young people.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the correct answer for each question.
2. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Take as much time as you need, and ask for help if you need it.
