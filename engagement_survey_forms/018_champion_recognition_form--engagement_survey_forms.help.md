# Champion Recognition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for recognizing and showcasing the achievements of individuals within an organization or community. It helps to identify and celebrate outstanding contributions, leadership, and exceptional qualities of nominees. The form will be used to collect feedback and support from colleagues, supervisors, or other members of the organization or community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your name and email address to be associated with the nomination.
2.  Enter the nominee's name, title, organization, and email address.
3.  Describe how you know the nominee (colleague, manager, team member, etc.).
4.  Select the type of recognition or award the nominee is being nominated for.
5.  Provide a brief explanation for the nomination, including specific achievements and impact.
6.  If you have supporting documents (e.g., photos, certificates), confirm that they are attached and describe what they are.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Nomination Date (1) (Date, required)
*   A date field where you can enter the date of the nomination.

### Nominator's Name (2) (Text, required)
*   Enter your full name to associate with the nomination.

### Nominator's Email (3) (Email, required)
*   Enter your email address to enable communication and feedback.

### Your Organization or Department (4) (Text, optional)
*   If you work or volunteer for an organization, enter its name here.

### Nominee's Full Name (5) (Text, required)
*   Enter the name of the individual being recognized.

### Nominee's Title or Position (6) (Text, optional)
*   Enter the job title or position of the nominee.

### Nominee's Organization or Department (7) (Text, required)
*   Enter the name of the organization where the nominee works.

### Nominee's Email (8) (Email, optional)
*   Enter the email address of the nominee.

### Your Relationship to Nominee (9) (Select One, required)
*   Choose how you know the nominee (colleague, manager, team member, etc.).

### Recognition Category (10) (Select One, required)
*   Select the type of recognition or award the nominee is being nominated for.

### Why You Nominate This Person (11) (Text, required)
*   Explain why you are nominating this person and their specific achievements.

### Specific Achievements or Accomplishments (12) (Text, required)
*   Provide a detailed description of the nominee's accomplishments and how they made an impact.

### Impact on Organization or Community (13) (Text, required)
*   Describe the impact of the nominee on the organization or community.

### Outstanding Qualities (14) (Text, optional)
*   What makes this person exceptional and worthy of recognition.

### Measurable Results or Metrics (15) (Text, optional)
*   If possible, provide numbers or data showing the impact of the nominee's work.

### Support From Other Colleagues (16) (Select One, optional)
*   Confirm if other colleagues have supported this nomination.

### Is Nominee Aware of This Nomination (17) (Select One, optional)
*   Confirm if the nominee is aware of this nomination.

### Supporting Documents (18) (Select One, optional)
*   Confirm if you have attached supporting documents (photos, certificates, etc.).

### Description of Supporting Documents (19) (Text, optional)
*   Describe the supporting documents attached.

### Permission for Public Recognition (20) (Select One, optional)
*   Confirm if the nominee is willing to be publicly recognized.

Note: The form fields are labeled according to their purpose and are designed to guide you through the process of completing the Champion Recognition Form. The explanations provided are intended to be helpful and not exhaustive, and you can always reach out to [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for any further assistance or clarification.
