# Team Cohesion Improvement Initiative Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Cohesion Improvement Initiative Application Form is used to gather information about a team's goals, challenges, and proposed solutions to improve team dynamics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (team_name, text, required): Enter the name of your team.
* **Project Lead Name** (project_lead_name, text, required): Enter the name of the project lead.
* **Team Size** (team_size, number, optional): Enter the size of your team (number of members).
* **Department** (department, text, optional): Enter the department your team belongs to.
* **Team Cohesion Improvement Initiative** (team_cohesion_improvement_initiative, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the team cohesion improvement initiative you are proposing.
* **Team Cohesion Improvement Initiative Description** (team_cohesion_improvement_initiative_description, text, optional): Describe in detail why you need this team cohesion improvement initiative.
* **Team Objectives** (team_objectives, text, optional): Describe the objectives you aim to achieve.
* **Team Challenges** (team_challenges, text, optional): List the challenges your team is facing.
* **Team Success Factors** (team_success_factors, text, optional): Describe the factors that contribute to your team's success.
* **Team Cohesion Improvement Initiative Outcome** (team_cohesion_improvement_initiative_outcome, text, optional): Suggest the expected outcome of this initiative.
* **Team Cohesion Improvement Initiative Suggestions** (team_cohesion_improvement_initiative_suggestions, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or ideas you have for improving team cohesion.
* **Team Cohesion Improvement Initiative Implementation Plan** (team_cohesion_improvement_initiative_implementation_plan, text, optional): Describe the steps to be taken to implement the proposed initiative.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Make sure to follow the steps outlined above.
* If you have any questions, contact your project lead or team administrator.
