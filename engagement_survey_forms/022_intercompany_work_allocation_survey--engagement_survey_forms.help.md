# Intercompany Work Allocation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your role and tasks within the intercompany work allocation process. Your responses will help us identify areas for improvement and suggest potential changes to make the process more efficient and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each field to provide your input. You can enter text, select from the provided options, or provide a numerical value.
2. If you have any suggestions for improving the process, please provide a brief note explaining your idea.
3. Once you've completed all the fields, click the submit button to send your input to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the intercompany work allocation process?** (`intercompany_work_allocation_survey`, text, optional): Please describe your specific role or responsibilities within the intercompany work allocation process.
* **What types of intercompany tasks do you currently perform?** (`intercompany_tasks`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Intercompany tasks that involve communication with other departments
	+ Intercompany tasks that involve collaboration with other teams
	+ Intercompany tasks that require data sharing
* **How much time do you spend on intercompany tasks?** (`intercompany_time_allocation`, number, optional): Please enter a numerical value to estimate the amount of time you dedicate to intercompany tasks.
* **What are some challenges you face when performing intercompany tasks?** (`challenges`, note, optional): Describe any difficulties or obstacles you experience when performing intercompany tasks.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improving the intercompany work allocation process?** (`suggestions`, select_one, optional):
	+ Improve communication between departments
	+ Increase task allocation efficiency
	+ Enhance data sharing practices

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing your role and tasks.
* Select all applicable options for intercompany tasks.
* If you have suggestions, explain why you think they would be beneficial.
* Your input will help us make the intercompany work allocation process more efficient and effective.
