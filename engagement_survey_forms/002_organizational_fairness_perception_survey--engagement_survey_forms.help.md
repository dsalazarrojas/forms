# Organizational Fairness Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the perception of employees on various aspects of fairness within the organization. It aims to gather feedback on whether employees feel treated fairly in terms of workload, pay, respect, leadership, equity, and other aspects. The survey helps identify areas where the organization can improve to create a more inclusive and equitable work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill in your Employee Name in the designated field.
2. Then, select your department from the list (if not required, you can leave this field blank).
3. After that, select the job title that best describes your position.
4. For the remaining fields, select one or more options that reflect your perception of fairness in each aspect. For example, if you think you are treated fairly in terms of Fair Treatment, select 'Yes'. If you think you are not treated fairly, select 'No'. If your situation is different, select 'Other' and describe it in the 'Fair Treatment' field.
5. Fill out the rest of the form similarly, choosing one or more options for each field that reflect your perception of fairness.
6. Don't forget to fill out all required fields marked as 'required'. If you cannot fill out a field, please provide a comment explaining the reason.
