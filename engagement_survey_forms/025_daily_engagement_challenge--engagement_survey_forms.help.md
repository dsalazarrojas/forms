# daily_engagement_challenge - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information about your daily engagement challenge, including your name, email, phone number, and challenge start and finish dates. This form is intended to help us understand your progress and performance on the challenge. Please answer the following questions to provide us with accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Provide your Challenge Start Date and Challenge Finish Date in the date fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**: no): Your first name is a required field and should be filled out accurately to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required**: no): Your last name is a required field and should be filled out accurately to identify you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**: no): Enter your valid email address to enable us to contact you about your challenge progress.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **required**: no): Your phone number will help us to contact you about your challenge progress.
* **Challenge Start Date** (`challenge_start_date`, date, **required**: no): Enter the date when you started the challenge. This will help us to track your progress accurately.
* **Challenge Finish Date** (`challenge_finish_date`, date, **required**: no): Enter the date when you finished the challenge. This will help us to evaluate your performance and provide feedback.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any issues or need help with the form, please contact us.
* You can fill out this form at any time, but please note that the information provided will only be used to track your progress on the challenge.
