# Team Communication Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Team Communication Effectiveness Survey is a tool used to assess individual team members' perceptions of their own communication with others on their team. The survey collects data on communication frequency, channels, conflict resolution, and overall satisfaction to improve team dynamics and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer the questions as honestly and accurately as possible.
2.  Select the options that best describe your experiences and perceptions.
3.  Ensure you've filled out all required fields before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Team Communication Scale** (2): Rate how often you communicate with team members.
    \* Rarely: Communicate very infrequently (e.g., when necessary).
    \* Occasionally: Communicate occasionally (e.g., when necessary, but not frequently).
    \* Frequently: Communicate regularly (e.g., daily or weekly).
    \* Almost Always: Communicate very frequently (e.g., constantly).
*   **How do you resolve conflicts** (7): Choose all methods you use to resolve conflicts.
    \* Avoid: Try to avoid conflicts altogether.
    \* Address: Address conflicts directly with team members.
    \* Delegate: Delegate conflict resolution to a supervisor or other team member.
    \* Refer to a third party: Involve others, such as HR or a mediator.
*   **What channels do you use for collaboration** (5): Choose all methods you use for collaboration.
    \* In-person: Face-to-face meetings or discussions.
    \* Video call: Virtual meetings or video conferencing.
    \* Shared doc: Collaborative documents or file sharing.
    \* Task list: To-do lists or task management tools.
*   **Can team members provide feedback?** (6): Answer if team members provide feedback.
    \* True: Team members often provide feedback.
    \* False: Team members rarely or never provide feedback.
*   **Submission Location** (20): Where did you submit this survey?
    \* Online: Through a digital platform or website.
    \* In person: In a physical location, such as an office or meeting room.
*   **Submission Device** (21): What device did you use to submit this survey?
    \* Computer: A desktop or laptop computer.
    \* Phone: A mobile phone or smartphone.
    \* Tablet: A tablet computer or device.
*   **Submission Language** (22): In what language did you submit this survey?
    \* English: The survey was submitted in English.
    \* French: The survey was submitted in French.
    \* Spanish: The survey was submitted in Spanish.
*   **Submission Confirmation** (24): Do you confirm you've submitted this survey?
    \* Confirm: Yes, I confirm I've submitted this survey.
    \* No confirm: No, I haven't submitted this survey.
*   **Submission Agreement** (25): Do you agree with the submission of this survey?
    \* Agree: Yes, I agree with the submission.
    \* Disagree: No, I disagree with the submission.

Note: The submission fields (Submission, Submission Device, Submission Language, Submission Location, Submission Confirmation, and Submission Agreement) are intended to capture survey submission data and metadata, such as the device used, location, and language. Please ensure to fill out these fields accurately to help improve the survey experience.
