# Peer To Peer Relationship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help you reflect on your relationship with someone else and gather information about your connection with them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully and answer based on your actual relationship with the person.
2. For each question, select the option that best represents your experience or feelings about this person.
3. Please answer all questions, as they are all required fields.
