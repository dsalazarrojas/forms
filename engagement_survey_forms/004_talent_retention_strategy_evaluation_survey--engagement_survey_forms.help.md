# Talent Retention Strategy Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of our talent retention strategy. It will ask you questions about your level of satisfaction with your role, the effectiveness of our retention initiatives, and other relevant topics. Your feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance our talent management practices.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all required questions marked with a `**`.
2. Provide accurate and honest responses to all questions.
3. Fill out the form on a computer or mobile device with a stable internet connection.
