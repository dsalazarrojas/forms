# Team Motivation Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the motivation and engagement level of a team, assessing various aspects such as morale, communication, and workload impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Introduction: Read the brief introduction on the first page.
2. Answer each question carefully, selecting the most accurate response for each field.
3. Select the correct option for each multiple-choice question.
4. Submit your responses when you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Morale** (`team_morale`, number, required): Rate the overall morale of your team, with higher numbers indicating higher morale.
* **Team Recognition** (`team_recognition`, select_one, required): Choose whether team members feel recognized for their work.
* **Team Communication** (`team_communication`, select_multiple, required): Select all the ways in which team communication is effective or ineffective.
* **Team Workload** (`team_workload`, date, optional): Enter the current workload level of your team, if applicable.
* **Manager Support** (`manager_support`, number, optional): Rate the level of support provided by managers.
* **Team Lead Support** (`team_lead_support`, select_one, optional): Choose who provides the most support to the team.
* **Employee Engagement** (`employee_engagement`, select_multiple, required): Select all the levels of employee engagement (e.g., Highly Engaged, Not Engaged).
* **Work-Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, number, optional): Rate the team's work-life balance.
* **Recognition Importance** (`recognition_important`, number, optional): Rate the importance of recognition for the team's motivation.
* **Team Morale Level** (`team_morale_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current morale level of the team.
* **Team Recognition Level** (`team_recognition_level`, select_one, optional): Choose the current recognition level of the team.
* **Team Communication Level** (`team_communication_level`, select_one, required): Select the current level of team communication.
* **Team Support Level** (`team_support_level`, select_one, optional): Choose the current level of team support.
* **Manager Support Level** (`manager_support_level`, select_one, optional): Choose the current level of manager support.
* **Team Lead Support Level** (`team_lead_support_level`, select_one, optional): Choose the current level of team lead support.
* **Workload Level** (`workload_level`, select_one, required): Select the current workload level of the team.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate employee satisfaction.
* **Team Satisfaction** (`team_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Select all the levels of team satisfaction (e.g., High, Low).
* **Recognition Level** (`recognition_level`, select_one, required): Choose the current recognition level of the team.
* **Manager Satisfaction** (`manager_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate manager satisfaction.
* **Team Lead Satisfaction** (`team_lead_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate team lead satisfaction.
* **Workload Frequency** (`workload_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of high workload.
* **Workload Impact** (`workload_impact`, select_multiple, required): Select all the levels of workload impact (e.g., High, Low).

## Tips

* Answer honestly and accurately to provide a thorough assessment of the team's motivation and engagement.
* Consider sharing your responses with your team members or manager to facilitate open discussion and improvement.
* Complete this form regularly to monitor changes in the team's sentiment over time.
