# Healthcare Team Collaboration Dynamics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences and challenges with collaboration within your healthcare team. Your feedback will help us better understand the dynamics of your team's collaboration and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and answer the questions truthfully. You can choose from the options provided or type in a brief description if you have an alternative answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Dynamics**: This page is meant to be a brief introduction to the survey and provide some context. Please write your thoughts about your team's overall dynamics. (<name> `team_dynamics`, <type> `note`)
* **Collaboration Challenges**: Check all the ways in which you experience challenges in collaborating with your team. (<name> `collaboration_challenges`, <type> `select_multiple`)
* **Teamwork Challenges**: Check the specific challenges you face in working with your team. (<name> `teamwork_challenges`, <type> `select_one`)
* **Improvement Ideas**: If you have any ideas or suggestions for improving collaboration within your team, please share them with us. (<name> `improvement_ideas`, <type> `text`)
* **Communication Challenges**: Check all the ways in which you experience challenges in communicating with your team. (<name> `communication_challenges`, <type> `select_multiple`)
* **Team Leadership**: Check your experience with team leadership. (<name> `team_leadership`, <type> `select_one`)
* **Collaboration Tools**: Check all the tools you use for collaboration. (<name> `collaboration_tools`, <type> `select_multiple`)
* **Communication Channels**: Check all the channels you use for communication. (<name> `communication_channels`, <type> `select_multiple`)
* **Collaboration Platforms**: Check all the platforms you use for collaboration. (<name> `collaboration_platforms`, <type> `select_multiple`)
* **Team Meetings**: Check all the types of team meetings you attend. (<name> `team_meetings`, <type> `select_multiple`)
* **Collaboration Process**: Check the collaboration process you follow. (<name> `collaboration_process`, <type> `select_one`)
* **Team Dos**: This field is meant to be a list of things your team does well. (<name> `team_dos`, <type> `select_multiple`)
* **Team Doos**: This field is meant to be a list of things your team does poorly. (<name> `team_doos`, <type> `select_multiple`)
* **Communication Do**: This field is meant to be a list of things your team does poorly in terms of communication. (<name> `communication_do`, <type> `select_multiple`)
* **Leadership Do**: This field is meant to be a list of things your team's leadership does poorly. (<name> `leadership_do`, <type> `select_one`)
* **Collaboration Do**: This field is meant to be a list of things your team does poorly in terms of collaboration. (<name> `collaboration_do`, <type> `select_multiple`)
* **Communication Doos**: This field is meant to be a list of things your team does poorly in terms of communication. (<name> `communication_doos`, <type> `select_multiple`)
* **Platforms Do**: This field is meant to be a list of things your team does poorly in terms of platforms. (<name> `platforms_do`, <type> `select_multiple`)
* **Team Meets**: This field is meant to be a list of things your team does poorly in terms of team meetings. (<name> `team_meets`, <type> `select_multiple`)
* **Process Do**: This field is meant to be a list of things your team does poorly in terms of process. (<name> `process_do`, <type> `select_one`)

## Tips
Please answer honestly and thoroughly, and take your time to provide as much detail as possible. Your feedback is valuable to us and will help us improve collaboration within your healthcare team.
