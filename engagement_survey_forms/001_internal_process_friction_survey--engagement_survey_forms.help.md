# Internal Process Friction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help identify and document process bottlenecks within the organization, allowing teams and individuals to gather information about the areas that are causing difficulties or hindrances in their processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, and provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the primary function of this form?** (`introduction`, `text`, optional): This field is meant for providing a brief introduction or description of the form's purpose. Please keep it concise and clear.
* **Where is the primary process bottleneck?** (`process_bottleneck`, `select_one`, optional): This field helps identify where the biggest problem in the current process lies. Choose one of the provided options or describe it in your own words.
* **Describe the pain point in your process** (`pain_point_description`, `text`, optional): In this field, thoroughly describe the difficulty or hindrance you're experiencing in your process. Be as detailed as possible.
* **What impact does this process bottleneck have on your work?** (`impact_on_work`, `text`, optional): Here, describe how this process bottleneck affects your productivity or morale.
* **What suggestions do you have for improving this process?** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, optional): In this field, think about what would make the process easier and more efficient, and provide your ideas.
* **What is the current status of this issue?** (`current_status`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to track the current status of the issue. Choose from the provided options or describe it in your own words.
* **What are your next steps on this issue?** (`next_steps`, `text`, optional): Here, describe your next actions on addressing this issue.
* **What is your estimate of the priority for this process bottleneck?** (`process_bottleneck_priority`, `number`, optional): This field is meant for providing a numerical estimate of the priority of the process bottleneck. Please use a number to indicate its level of importance.
* **What is your estimate of the impact of this process bottleneck?** (`process_bottleneck_impact`, `number`, optional): In this field, provide a numerical estimate of the impact of this process bottleneck. This will help in understanding its severity.
* **How often do you encounter this process bottleneck?** (`process_bottleneck_frequency`, `number`, optional): This field helps in tracking the frequency of the process bottleneck. Provide a numerical value to indicate how often you experience it.
* **Frequency of process bottleneck 2** (`process_bottleneck_frequency_2`, `number`, optional): This field is similar to the previous one, but for another process bottleneck. If you have more than one, please provide a separate value.
* **Frequency of process bottleneck 3** (`process_bottleneck_frequency_3`, `number`, optional): Another field for tracking frequency of another process bottleneck.
* **Frequency of process bottleneck 4** (`process_bottleneck_frequency_4`, `number`, optional): Yet another field for tracking frequency of another process bottleneck.
* **Frequency of process bottleneck 5** (`process_bottleneck_frequency_5`, `number`, optional): Additional field for tracking frequency of another process bottleneck.
* **Frequency of process bottleneck 6** (`process_bottleneck_frequency_6`, `number`, optional): Another field for tracking frequency of another process bottleneck.
* **Frequency of process bottleneck 7** (`process_bottleneck_frequency_7`, `number`, optional): Another field for tracking frequency of another process bottleneck.
* **Frequency of process bottleneck 8** (`process_bottleneck_frequency_8`, `number`, optional): Another field for tracking frequency of another process bottleneck.
* **Frequency of process bottleneck 9** (`process_bottleneck_frequency_9`, `number`, optional): Another field for tracking frequency of another process bottleneck.
* **Frequency of process bottleneck 10** (`process_bottleneck_frequency_10`, `number`, optional): Another field for tracking frequency of another process bottleneck.
* **How often do process bottlenecks occur in your team?** (`frequency_of_process_bottlenecks`, `number`, optional): This field helps in tracking the frequency of process bottlenecks across your team.
* **What is the average time spent on this process bottleneck?** (`average_time`, `number`, optional): In this field, provide the average time spent on this process bottleneck.
* **What is the severity of this process bottleneck?** (`process_bottleneck_severity`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to describe the severity of the process bottleneck. Choose from the provided options or describe it in your own words.
* **Severity of process bottleneck 2** (`process_bottleneck_severity_2`, `select_one`, optional): Another field for describing the severity of another process bottleneck.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
* If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to reach out to your team or management for guidance.
* Keep in mind that the more information you provide, the better the analysis and potential solutions will be.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
