# Board Engagement And Availability Survey - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your full name as a board member.
2. Select the date you are completing the survey.
3. Choose your board position (Chair, Vice Chair, Secretary, Treasurer, Board Member, Committee Chair, etc.).
4. Indicate how long you have been on the board (less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 3-5 years, etc.).
5. Choose your approximate meeting attendance percentage (100%, 90-99%, 75-89%, etc.).
6. Select your available weekdays (multiple options).
7. Select your available evenings (multiple options).
8. If you are available on weekends, choose your preferred availability (Saturday Morning, Saturday Afternoon, etc.).
9. Choose how often you prefer meetings to occur (weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
10. Select your preferred meeting duration (1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).
11. Choose how you prefer to meet (in-person, virtually, etc.).
12. Select committees you are interested in or none at all.
13. Indicate how engaged you feel (very engaged, engaged, etc.).
14. Choose your preferred communication method (email, phone call, etc.).
15. Select any challenges you face that affect your engagement (time constraints, scheduling conflicts, etc.).
16. Provide any additional comments or feedback about your availability.
17. Finally, indicate your willingness to take on more responsibilities.
18. Enter any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Board Member Name**: Enter your full name as a board member.
* **Survey Date**: Select the date you are completing the survey.
* **Your Board Position**: Choose your current role on the board (Chair, Vice Chair, Secretary, etc.).
* **Tenure on Board**: Indicate how long you have been on the board (less than 1 year, 1-2 years, etc.).
* **Meeting Attendance Rate**: Choose your approximate attendance percentage at meetings (100%, 90-99%, 75-89%, etc.).
* **Availability Weekdays**: Select the days of the week you are available to meet (Monday Morning, Monday Afternoon, etc.).
* **Availability Evenings**: Select the evenings you are available to meet (Monday Evening, Tuesday Evening, etc.).
* **Availability Weekends**: If you are available on weekends, choose your preferred availability (Saturday Morning, Saturday Afternoon, etc.).
* **Preferred Meeting Frequency**: Choose how often you prefer meetings to occur (weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Preferred Meeting Duration**: Select your preferred meeting duration (1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).
* **Preferred Meeting Format**: Choose how you prefer to meet (in-person, virtually, etc.).
* **Committee Interest**: Select committees you are interested in or none at all.
* **Current Engagement Level**: Indicate how engaged you feel (very engaged, engaged, etc.).
* **Preferred Communication Method**: Choose your preferred communication method (email, phone call, etc.).
* **Barriers to Engagement**: Select any challenges you face that affect your engagement (time constraints, scheduling conflicts, etc.).
* **Additional Support Needed**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your availability.
* **Willingness to Increase Involvement**: Indicate your willingness to take on more responsibilities (yes, definitely, possibly, etc.).
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your availability.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Select the options that best represent your current situation.
* If you are unsure about any option, choose the one that best fits your circumstances.
* This form will help the organization better understand your needs and preferences, which will inform future meetings and decision-making processes.
