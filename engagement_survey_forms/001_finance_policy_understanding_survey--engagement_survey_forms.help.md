# Finance Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us better understand your thoughts and insights on our finance policy. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to strengthen our financial management practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for questions that ask for a simple affirmation or negation.
3. For questions with multiple options, choose all that apply.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Note" field to provide more information.
5. Finally, review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **policy_awareness**: This question is designed to gauge your level of understanding of our current finance policy. Please answer "Yes" if you have a good grasp of the policy, "No" if you need further clarification.
* **finance_policy_clarity**: We want to know if our finance policy is clear and easy to follow. Answer "Yes" if you find the policy straightforward, "No" if it's unclear or confusing.
* **feedback**: Share any suggestions or comments you have regarding our finance policy. This will help us identify areas for improvement.
* **finance_policy_improvements**: If you have specific suggestions or ideas for improving our finance policy, list them here.
* **internal_finance_guidelines**: We want to know if our internal guidelines are aligned with our finance policy. Answer "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
* **hr_finance_compliance_teams**: Are there any teams within HR that need to be aware of or compliant with our finance policy? If so, list them here.
* **date**: This is an optional field for you to provide a date relevant to the finance policy, such as a specific meeting or review date.
* **time**: Similar to the "date" field, this is an optional field for you to provide a time relevant to the finance policy.
* **contact_email**: If you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback or suggestions, please provide your email address.
* **contact_phone**: If you'd rather be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.
* **other_info**: If you have any additional information regarding the finance policy, please share it here.
* **assigned_tool**: If you have suggestions for tools or resources that could aid in implementing our finance policy, list them here.
* **form_id**: This field is for internal use only and is used to track form submissions.
* **category**: This field is also for internal use only and is used to categorize form submissions.
* **description**: A field for you to provide a brief description of your submission, for internal reference.
* **output_file**: If you'd like to provide an attachment or file related to your submission, upload it here.
* **form_ids**: If you have any additional form IDs related to your submission, list them here.
* **form_title**: This is the title of the form you're submitting, which will be used for internal reference.
* **form_category**: This field is for internal use only and is used to categorize form submissions.
* **form_description**: A brief description of your submission, for internal reference.
* **form_awareness**: If you'd like to gauge our team's awareness of the finance policy, answer accordingly.
* **form_improvements**: If you have suggestions for improving our finance policy, list them here.
* **form_improvement**: This field is a duplicate of "form_improvements".
* **form_guidelines**: We want to know if our form guidelines are aligned with our finance policy. Answer "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
* **form_teams**: If there are any teams within finance that need to be aware of or compliant with our finance policy, list them here.

Note: Please keep your answers concise and clear, and feel free to ask if you have any questions or concerns.
