# Employment Advocacy Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Advocacy Engagement Survey is designed to measure employee engagement with the company's advocacy program. It aims to collect data on employee awareness, participation, communication, and follow-up methods to help the company evaluate the program's effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page and ensure you understand the questions and options provided.
2. Answer each question truthfully, using the options provided.
3. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number, so the company can follow up with you.
5. Choose the follow-up method you prefer for the company to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction**: This page welcomes you to the survey and provides an explanation of its purpose and importance.

### Awareness
* **Awareness**: This question asks if you are aware of the company's advocacy program. Please select "Yes" if you are aware of the program, or "No" if you are not.
### Communication
* **Communication**: This question asks if you feel well-informed about the program through regular communication from the company. Please select "Yes" if you feel well-informed, or "No" if you do not.
### Participation
* **Participation**: This question asks if you actively participate in the company's advocacy efforts. Please select "Yes" if you do, or "No" if you do not.
### Follow-up
* **Follow-up**: This question asks if you would like the company to follow up with you on the advocacy program progress. Please select "Yes" if you would like follow-up, or "No" if you do not.
### Comments
* **Comments**: This field is for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions about the advocacy program.
### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments**: This field is for you to provide any additional information that may be helpful for the company to know.
### Contact Information
* **Contact Info**: Please provide your contact information so the company can follow up with you on the survey results.
### Contact Email
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so the company can follow up with you on the survey results.
### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so the company can follow up with you on the survey results.
### Follow-up Method
* **Follow-up Method**: This question asks how you would like the company to follow up with you (e.g., email, phone, or in-person). Please select "Yes" for each method you prefer.
### Follow-up Email
* **Follow-up Email**: Enter your email address so the company can follow up with you using email.
### Follow-up Phone
* **Follow-up Phone**: Enter your phone number so the company can follow up with you using phone.
### Follow-up Mail
* **Follow-up Mail**: Enter your mail address so the company can follow up with you using mail.
### Follow-up In-Person
* **Follow-up In-Person**: This field is for you to enter the address where you can be reached in-person for follow-up.
### Follow-up Notes
* **Follow-up Notes**: This field is for you to provide any additional details on follow-up method preferences.
