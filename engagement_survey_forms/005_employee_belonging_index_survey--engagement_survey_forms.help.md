# Employee Belonging Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Belonging Index Survey is designed to measure employee perceptions of their sense of belonging in the workplace. It's a tool for the organization to better understand employee feelings and make informed decisions to improve employee well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly, and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the most accurate response to each question, taking into account your personal experiences in the workplace.
3. You can complete this form in multiple pages; each page is clearly labeled.
4. When prompted, you may select multiple options for some questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the survey** (<name>: `page_1`, <type>: `note`): This page is an introduction to the survey, and you are not required to answer any questions on this page.
* **How often do you feel valued and appreciated** (<name>: `page_2`, <type>: `select_one`, <label>): This question asks about the frequency with which you feel valued and appreciated in your role. Please select "Yes" if you usually feel this way, and "No" if you do not.
* **How likely are you to recommend this organization** (<name>: `page_3`, <type>: `select_multiple`, <label>): This question asks about your willingness to recommend the organization to others. Select all options that apply, such as "Yes" if you would recommend the organization, or "No" if you would not.
* **How often do you feel included** (<name>: `page_4`, <type>: `select_one`, <label>): This question asks about the frequency with which you feel included in the workplace. Please select "Yes" if you usually feel this way, and "No" if you do not.
* **How likely are you to report** (<name>: `page_5`, <type>: `time`, <label>): This question asks about the time it would take for you to report any issues or concerns you might have. Please answer in the format "X hours" or "X days", e.g., "1 hour" or "3 days".
Note: This field is optional, and if you do not wish to answer this question, you can skip it.
