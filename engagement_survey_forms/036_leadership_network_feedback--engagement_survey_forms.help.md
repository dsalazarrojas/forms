# Leadership Network Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from team members, managers, and other stakeholders on the effectiveness and engagement of the leadership network. The goal is to gather subjective opinions on various aspects of leadership programs, experiences, and communication to make data-driven decisions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your honest opinion.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the option that best represents your response.
3. For note fields, provide a brief but detailed comment.
4. Ensure you answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Leadership programs** (`1`, note, required: false): Please provide any feedback you have on the leadership programs, such as what works well, what doesn't, and suggestions for improvement.
* **Experiences** (`2`, select_one, required: false): Choose how frequently you participate in leadership experiences (Strongly agree, Moderately agree, Neutral, Weakly agree, Strongly disagree).
* **Communication** (`3`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the ways you typically receive leadership communication (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Events** (`4`, select_one, required: false): Choose how frequently you attend leadership events (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, Never).
* **Communication channels** (`5`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the channels you use for leadership communication (Email, Phone, In-person, Online).
* **Leadership development** (`6`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the leadership development opportunities you've had (Strongly agree, Moderately agree, Neutral, Weakly agree, Strongly disagree).
* **Engagement opportunities** (`7`, select_one, required: false): Choose how frequently you engage in leadership opportunities (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, Never).
* **Overall satisfaction** (`8`, select_one, required: false): Choose how satisfied you are with the leadership network (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
* **Open-ended comments** (`9`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the leadership network.
* **E-mail** (`10`, email, required: false): Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted for further discussions).
* **Phone** (`11`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted for further discussions).

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Provide specific examples to support your answers.
* If you have suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them in the open-ended comments field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [contact email or phone number].
