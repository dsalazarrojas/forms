# parent_engagement_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parent_engagement_survey form is designed to gather information from parents about their engagement with the school, including communication habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form completely, providing accurate and clear information for each field.
2.  Be sure to answer all required questions, indicated by a red asterisk (\*) in the field label.
3.  For fields with options, select the response that best fits your situation.
4.  Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your child's school.
* **Parent Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your name as a parent.
* **How often do you visit your child's classroom?** (3, select_one, required): Select how often you visit your child's classroom, such as "School events" or "Monthly".
* **What activities do you participate in at school?** (4, select_multiple, required): Select all activities you participate in at school, such as "PTA" or "Parent-teacher conferences".
* **School hours** (5, number, required/optional): Enter the number of hours you spend at school per week.
* **How many hours per week do you spend with your child's teacher?** (6, number, required/optional): Enter the number of hours you spend with your child's teacher per week.
* **How many times have you attended school events this year?** (7, number, required/optional): Enter the number of times you've attended school events this year.
* **What is your preferred method of communication?** (8, select_one, required): Select your preferred method of communication, such as "Email" or "Phone".
* **What is your preferred method of contact?** (9, select_one, required): Select how you prefer to be contacted, such as "Email" or "Phone".
* **How often do you check your email for updates from the teacher?** (10, select_one, required/optional): Select how often you check your email for updates from the teacher.
* **Email address** (12, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **How often do you check your email for updates from the teacher?** (13, select_one, required): Select how often you check your email for updates from the teacher.
* **Phone number** (14, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred time to call or meet** (15, time, required/optional): Select the preferred time to call or meet.
* **What time of day do you prefer to be called or met?** (16, time, required/optional): Select the time of day you prefer to be called or met.
* **What is your role at the school?** (17, select_one, required): Select your role at the school, such as "Parent" or "Teacher".
* **What do you do?** (18, select_one, required): Select what you do at the school, such as "PTA member" or "Parent volunteer".
* **How can we contact you?** (19, select_one, required): Select how you can be contacted, such as "Phone" or "Email".
* **How do you prefer to be reached?** (20, select_one, required): Select how you prefer to be reached, such as "Phone" or "Email".
* **How often do you meet with teachers or school staff?** (21, select_one, required/optional): Select how often you meet with teachers or school staff.
* **How often do you receive emails from teachers or staff?** (22, select_one, required/optional): Select how often you receive emails from teachers or staff.
* **What time of day is best to meet?** (23, time, required/optional): Select the best time to meet.
* **What time of day is best to call?** (24, time, required/optional): Select the best time to call.
* **What is the best time to meet or call?** (25, time, required/optional): Select the best time to meet or call.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the school administrator or teacher.
- This form is intended to gather information to improve school communication and engagement. Your input is invaluable.
