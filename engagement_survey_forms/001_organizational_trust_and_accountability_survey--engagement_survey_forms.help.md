# Organizational Trust And Accountability Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Organizational Trust And Accountability Survey is designed to assess the level of trust and accountability within the organization. It consists of five form pages, each focusing on specific aspects of the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each form page carefully to understand the questions and their relevance.
2.  For each page, select the appropriate option for each question based on your genuine opinion or experience within the organization.
3.  Ensure you complete all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Leadership Team** (`page_1_1`, select_multiple, required): This section asks about your perception of the leadership team's effectiveness. Choose one or more of the options that best describe your feelings:
    *   Strongly Agree: You completely agree with the leadership team's decisions and actions.
    *   Somewhat Agree: You generally agree, but with some reservations.
    *   Neutral: You neither agree nor disagree.
    *   Somewhat Disagree: You mostly disagree.
    *   Strongly Disagree: You strongly disagree with the leadership team's decisions and actions.
*   **Accountability** (`page_1_2`, text, required): Describe the level of accountability you experience in your work or interactions with the leadership team. This could include feelings of transparency, clear expectations, or open communication.
*   **Team** (`page_2_1`, select_one, required): Choose the team you work with or are most familiar with:
    *   Team A
    *   Team B
*   **Team** (`page_2_2`, select_one, required): Select another team from the options provided:
    *   Team A
    *   Team B
*   **Transparency** (`page_3_1`, select_multiple, required): Choose your level of agreement regarding the transparency of information within the organization:
    *   Strongly Agree
    *   Somewhat Agree
    *   Neutral
    *   Somewhat Disagree
    *   Strongly Disagree
*   **Accountability** (`page_4_1`, select_multiple, required): Select your level of agreement with the accountability within your team:
    *   Strongly Agree
    *   Somewhat Agree
    *   Neutral
    *   Somewhat Disagree
    *   Strongly Disagree

## Tips
*   Answer honestly and authentically to provide valuable feedback.
*   If unsure, consider your experiences and perceptions within the organization.
*   For each question, select the option that best fits your opinion or situation.
