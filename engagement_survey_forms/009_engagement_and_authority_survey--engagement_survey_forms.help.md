# Engagement And Authority Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about users' engagement and authority levels with your company. It will help the company understand how users perceive and interact with the company, which can inform marketing strategies and customer satisfaction initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly, as they are designed to help the company understand your thoughts and feelings.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience with the company.
3. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Introduction" sections to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This is a space for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the company.
* **How do you feel about the company**: This question asks about your emotional response to the company, with options ranging from "Strongly disagree" to "Strongly agree".
* **How do you know us**: This question asks about the channels through which you have interacted with the company, with options including "Social Media", "Email", "Phone Number", "In Person", and "Other".
* **Trust Level**: This question asks about your level of trust in the company, with a numeric scale.
* **Authority Level**: This question asks about your level of confidence or authority in the company, with a numeric scale.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions based on your honest experience with the company.
* Do not worry if you're not sure or have no answer for a particular question; you can skip it if you're not sure.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out to the company.
