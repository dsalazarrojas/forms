# Emotional Intelligence In Leadership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emotional Intelligence In Leadership Survey is a self-assessment tool designed to help leaders reflect on their emotional intelligence and leadership abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and authentically.
2. For the select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the most accurate option that best describes your thoughts or behaviors.
3. For the text fields, provide as much detail as possible to help you reflect on your abilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **intro**: This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully and understand the context of the assessment.
* **leadership-behavior**: This question asks about your ability to demonstrate leadership behaviors that support emotional intelligence. Please select all the options that apply to you.
	+ 'Yes' - You frequently demonstrate these behaviors.
	+ 'No' - You rarely or never demonstrate these behaviors.
* **leadership-style**: This question asks about your preferred leadership style. Please select the option that best describes your approach to leadership.
	+ Option 1: You tend to be more task-focused and results-driven.
	+ Option 2: You balance task-focused and people-focused approaches.
	+ Option 3: You tend to be more people-focused and relationship-driven.
* **communication**: This text field is for you to reflect on your communication skills, specifically how you communicate with your team members.
* **emotional-awareness**: This question asks about your ability to recognize and manage your own emotions. Please select all the options that apply to you.
	+ 'Yes' - You frequently recognize and manage your emotions.
	+ 'No' - You rarely or never recognize or manage your emotions.
* **conflict-resolution**: This question asks about your ability to resolve conflicts with your team members. Please select the option that best describes your approach.
	+ Option 1: You tend to address conflicts directly and assertively.
	+ Option 2: You balance directness with empathy and understanding.
	+ Option 3: You tend to avoid conflicts or delegate resolution to others.
* **results**: This text field is for you to reflect on your results-driven approach and how you balance it with emotional intelligence.
* **summary**: This question asks you to reflect on your self-awareness and ability to recognize your strengths and areas of improvement. Please select all the options that apply to you.
	+ 'Yes' - You frequently reflect on your self-awareness and abilities.
	+ 'No' - You rarely or never reflect on your self-awareness or abilities.
* **feedback**: This text field is for you to provide feedback on your self-assessment and any areas you'd like to improve on.
* **comments**: This question asks you to provide any additional comments or insights on your emotional intelligence and leadership abilities. Please select all the options that apply to you.
	+ 'Yes' - You frequently provide comments and insights.
	+ 'No' - You rarely or never provide comments or insights.
