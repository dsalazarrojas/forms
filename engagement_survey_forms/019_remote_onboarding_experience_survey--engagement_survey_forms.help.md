# Remote Onboarding Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from new hires about their remote onboarding experience. It will ask questions about their communication with managers and colleagues, overall experience, and other relevant aspects of their onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete this form carefully, as it will help us understand your remote onboarding experience.
- Answer all questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible.
- If you have any trouble or concerns while filling out this form, please contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire?** (`new_hire`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all categories that apply to you. You are currently or previously employed with the company.
  + Select one or more options that match your current status with the company.
* **Onboarding Process** (`onboarding_process`, `text`, required): Please describe the onboarding process you went through.
  + This field is for free-text input. Provide a detailed description of your onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Duration in Hours** (`onboarding_duration`, `number`, required): Please enter the total hours you spent on onboarding.
  + This field is for numerical input. Enter the total hours you spent on onboarding.
* **Communication with Manager** (`communication_with_manager`, `select_one`, required): How would you rate your communication with your manager?
  + Select one option that best describes your experience.
* **Communication with Colleagues** (`communication_with_colleagues`, `select_multiple`, required): How would you rate your communication with colleagues?
  + Select all categories that apply to your experience.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, required): How would you rate your overall experience with your onboarding process?
  + Select one option that best describes your experience.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, required): Do you have any feedback or suggestions for improving the onboarding process?
  + This field is for free-text input. Provide any thoughts or suggestions you have.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address.
  + Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number.
  + Enter a valid phone number.
* **Onboarding Support** (`onboarding_support`, `select_multiple`, required): How would you rate the support you received during onboarding?
  + Select all categories that apply to your experience.
