# Team Productivity Barriers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your team's productivity barriers and potential solutions to overcome them. Your input will help us improve team performance and productivity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Select the options that best describe your team's situation.
3. If you need more space to provide your answers, use the note fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Performance** (`team_performance`, select_one, optional): This question helps us understand how your team is performing currently. Select the option that best describes your team's performance.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the size of your team. This will help us understand the dynamics of your team.
* **Collaboration Level** (`collaboration_level`, select_one, optional): This question measures the level of collaboration within your team. Select the option that best describes your team's collaboration level.
* **Communication Level** (`communication_level`, select_one, optional): This question assesses the quality of communication within your team. Select the option that best describes your communication level.
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, text, optional): Please describe your work environment, including any physical or digital spaces where you work.
* **Work Environment Description** (`work_environment_description`, note, optional): If you need more space to describe your work environment, use this field.
* **Work Schedules** (`work_schedules`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the type of schedules you work with. This will help us understand your team's flexibility.
* **Conflicts** (`team_conflict`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of conflicts within your team.
* **Manager Support** (`manager_support`, select_one, optional): This question assesses the level of support you receive from your manager. Select the option that best describes your manager's support level.
* **Resources Availability** (`resources_availability`, select_one, optional): This question measures the availability of resources within your team. Select the option that best describes your team's resource availability.
* **Feedback Mechanism** (`feedback_mechanism`, select_one, optional): This question helps us understand how you receive feedback within your team. Select the option that best describes your team's feedback mechanism.
* **Productivity Barriers** (`productivity_barriers`, note, optional): List some of the key barriers you're facing within your team.
* **Manager Satisfaction** (`manager_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): This question assesses the level of satisfaction your manager has with your team's performance. Select the option that best describes your manager's satisfaction level.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): This question measures the level of satisfaction within your team. Select the option that best describes your team's satisfaction level.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, note, optional): Briefly describe the action items you plan to implement based on your observations.
* **Action Items Implementation** (`action_items_implementation`, select_one, optional): This question helps us understand how confident you are in implementing your action items. Select the option that best describes your confidence level.
* **Implementation Plan** (`implementation_plan`, select_one, optional): This question measures the level of planning for implementing solutions. Select the option that best describes your implementation plan.
* **Progress Monitors** (`progress_monitors`, select_one, optional): This question assesses the frequency of progress monitoring within your team. Select the option that best describes your progress monitoring frequency.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, select_one, optional): This question helps us understand how frequently you evaluate your team's performance. Select the option that best describes your evaluation frequency.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, select_one, optional): This question measures the frequency of evaluations within your team. Select the option that best describes your evaluation frequency.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the communication channels you used to gather information.
* **Communication Frequency** (`communication_frequency`, select_one, optional): This question assesses the frequency of communication within your team. Select the option that best describes your communication frequency.
* **Collaboration Tools** (`collaboration_tools`, select_multiple, optional): List the collaboration tools you used.
* **Other Collaboration Tools** (`other_collaboration_tools`, select_multiple, optional): If you used other collaboration tools not listed, select this option.

# Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when providing answers.
* If you need more space to provide your answers, use the note fields provided.
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
