# Exam Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exam Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from students for their exam registrations. It helps us understand their exam schedules, preferences, and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student information, including any additional comments.
2. Select the type of exam you are taking.
3. Choose the date and start time of your exam.
4. Select the location where you will be taking the exam.
5. Specify the duration of your exam.
6. If necessary, add any additional notes about your exam.
7. Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing your name and any other relevant student information.
* **Exam Type** (`exam_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of exam you are taking from the provided options.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your exam in the correct format (e.g., 2024-03-16).
* **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of your exam in the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Exam Location** (`exam_location`, select_one, required/optional): Select the location where you will be taking the exam from the provided options.
* **Exam Duration** (`exam_duration`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the duration of your exam from the provided options (e.g., Short, Long).
* **Exam Notes** (`exam_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your exam if necessary.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Provide your contact information, which can be your address, phone number, or any other relevant details.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.

## Tips
* Be accurate and precise when filling out the form to avoid errors.
* Ensure you have the necessary information ready before completing the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field label or consult with your teacher or instructor for clarification.
