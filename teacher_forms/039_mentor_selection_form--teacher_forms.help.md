# Mentor Selection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mentor Selection Form is used to determine whether a teacher would like to be mentored by someone in their subject area. This form is typically used by teachers who require guidance and support in their professional development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Select Mentor" dropdown menu and choose the option that best suits your needs.
2. If applicable, enter your name and email address for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Mentor**: This field allows you to choose whether you would like to be mentored by someone in your subject area. Please select "Yes" if you are interested in being mentored, and "No" otherwise.
* **Name**: If you choose to be mentored, please enter your name in this field so that the system can better assist in matching you with a suitable mentor.
* **Email**: If you provide your email address, the system will be able to contact you directly for further information or to facilitate the mentorship matching process.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have carefully selected the "Select Mentor" option before proceeding, as this will affect the mentor selection process. If you have any questions or concerns about the mentor selection form, please do not hesitate to contact us.
