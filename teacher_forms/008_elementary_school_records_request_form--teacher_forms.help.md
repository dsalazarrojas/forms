# Elementary School Records Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request records from the elementary school, which can be student-related documents, academic records, or other pertinent information. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing the student's full name, which should include their surname and initial name (if applicable).
2. If available, enter the student's ID number for easier identification.
3. Fill in the contact email address of the person or organization requesting the record.
4. For the Date of Birth field, ensure to use the YYYY-MM-DD format (year-month-day).
5. Select the grade level of the student from the options provided.
6. Explain the reason for requesting the record in the Reason for Request field.
7. Provide any comments or feedback from the teacher or other relevant parties in the Teacher Comments field.
8. Choose the school year from the dropdown options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): This field is used to provide the student's full name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the student's ID number, if available.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the parent's full name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email address of the person or organization requesting the record.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Provide the student's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, number, optional): Select the grade level of the student from the provided options.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Explain the reason for requesting the record.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments from the teacher or other relevant parties.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, select_one, optional): Select the school year from the provided options.
