# Secret Santa Form For Students - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Santa Form For Students is used to collect information from students to facilitate a Secret Santa gift exchange. This form helps us gather essential details such as name, email, phone number, and other preferences to ensure a smooth and enjoyable gift-giving experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name
2. Enter your last name
3. Enter your school email address
4. Optionally, enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted)
5. Select your gift preference
6. Indicate your gift budget
7. (Optional) Enter your student ID
8. (Optional) Enter your teacher's name
9. (Optional) Enter the name of your class
10. (Optional) Enter the name of your school
11. Leave a message for your teacher
12. Leave a message for your fellow students
13. Leave a message for your parents
14. (Optional) Leave a message for the teacher
15. Leave a message for your fellow students

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official student records.
* **Last name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official student records.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your school email address that your teacher uses to contact you.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): If you want to be contacted, enter your phone number. This is not required but can be helpful for the gift-giver.
* **Gift preference** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of gift you would like to receive or would like to give. Please select one of the options provided.
* **Gift budget** (Number, Required): Indicate your budget for the gift, and we'll try to keep it within that range.
* **Student ID** (Number, Optional): If you want to share your student ID with your teacher, enter it here. This is not required.
* **Teacher name** (Text, Optional): If you want to share a personal message with your teacher, enter their name. This is not required.
* **Class name** (Text, Optional): If you want to share a message with your class, enter the name of your class. This is not required.
* **School name** (Text, Optional): If you want to share a message with your school, enter the name of your school. This is not required.
* **Message to teacher** (Text, Optional): If you have a personal message for your teacher, enter it here.
* **Message to students** (Text, Optional): If you have a message for your fellow students, enter it here.
* **Message to parents** (Text, Optional): If you have a message for your parents, enter it here.
* **Teacher message** (Text, Optional): If you have a message for your teacher, enter it here.
* **Students message** (Text, Optional): If you have a message for your fellow students, enter it here.
