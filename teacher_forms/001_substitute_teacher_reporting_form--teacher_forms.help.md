# Substitute Teacher Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Substitute Teacher Reporting Form is a tool used to gather information from substitute teachers about their experiences and observations during a lesson or period. This form aims to capture key details that can inform the regular teacher and school administration in making decisions about student support and instructional adjustments.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter your name as the substitute teacher in the "Substitute Teacher Name" field. This is a required field, and your name will help us identify you as the substitute teacher.
*   Fill in the "Class Name" field with the name of the class you taught. This is also a required field, as it helps us track the class you were responsible for.
*   Provide any notes about the lesson or period in the "Lesson Notes" field. This is an optional field, but it's a great way to offer additional context or insights about your experience.
*   Indicate the student behaviors you observed, if any, in the "Student Behaviors" field.
*   Report whether or not the students were present during the lesson by selecting one of the options in the "Student Attendance" field.
*   Attach a lesson plan for the lesson or period, if applicable, in the "Lesson Plan" field.
*   If applicable, report any issues or incidents that occurred during the lesson or period in the "Lesson Plan" field.
*   Specify the date and time of the lesson or period in the "Date" and "Time" fields. These fields are required for record-keeping purposes.
*   Enter your email address in the "Substitute Email" field, which is optional but recommended for future communication.
*   You can also choose to report "Yes" or "No" in the "Reported By" field, which is not applicable to this form.
*   Finally, if you have any further comments or observations, feel free to include them in the "School Year" field, which is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Substitute Teacher Name** (`substitute_teacher_name`, text, Required): Enter your name as the substitute teacher.
*   **Class Name** (`class_name`, text, Required): Fill in the name of the class you taught.
*   **Lesson Notes** (`substitute_lesson_notes`, text, Optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the lesson.
*   **Student Behaviors** (`student_behaviors`, text, Optional): Record any notable student behaviors you observed during the lesson.
*   **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, select_multiple, Required): Select whether students were present, absent, or late during the lesson.
*   **Lesson Plan** (`lesson_plan`, note, Required): Attach a lesson plan for the lesson or period.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, Required): Fill in the date of the lesson or period.
*   **Time** (`time`, time, Required): Specify the time of the lesson or period.
*   **School Year** (`school_year`, text, Optional): Record any further comments or observations.
*   **Substitute Email** (`substitute_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address for future communication.
*   **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, Required): Enter the name of the regular teacher.

Note: The "Reported By" field is a placeholder and does not serve any purpose in this form. You can ignore it.
