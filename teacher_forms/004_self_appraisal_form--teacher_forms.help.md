# Self Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Appraisal Form is a tool for teachers to evaluate and reflect on their own performance and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro Page**: The intro page serves as an introduction to the form.
* **Student Information**: Select one or more of the following options for student information: Student Name, Student ID, or Student Email.
* **Performance Rating**: Rate your performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Areas for Growth**: Choose one or more of the following areas for growth: Communication, Leadership, Problem Solving, Teamwork, or Adaptability.
* **Recommendations**: Add any recommendations for the student.
* **Evaluation Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the evaluation.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or feedback for the student.
* **Signed By**: Select who signed the evaluation (Teacher or HR).
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the form (Teacher or HR).
* **Submitted Date and Time**: Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Form Version**: Enter the version of the form.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or feedback.
* **Evaluating Frequency**: Choose how often the self-appraisal form is evaluated (Annually, Bi-Annually, or Semi-Annually).
* **Evaluations Status**: Select the status of the evaluation (Active, Inactive, or Completed).
* **Review Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the review.
* **Self Evaluation Rating**: Rate your own performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Self Improvement Plan**: Add any plans for improvement.
* **Follow Up Date and Time**: Enter the date and time for follow-up.
* **Signed By Signature**: Add your signature.
* **Submitted By Signature**: Add the signature of the person who submitted the form.
* **Review Notes**: Add any review notes.
