# Students Cumulative Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect student data and track their academic progress over several years. It's used by educators and administrators to monitor student performance and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and detailed information about the student.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Use the provided hints and labels as a guide for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter the student's identification number, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the student's address where they can be contacted.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address where they can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number where they can be contacted.
* **Grade 1** (`grade1`, number, optional): Enter the student's grade 1 score, if applicable.
* **Grade 2** (`grade2`, number, optional): Enter the student's grade 2 score, if applicable.
* **Grade 3** (`grade3`, number, optional): Enter the student's grade 3 score, if applicable.
* **Grade 4** (`grade4`, number, optional): Enter the student's grade 4 score, if applicable.
* **Grade 5** (`grade5`, number, optional): Enter the student's grade 5 score, if applicable.
* **Grade 6** (`grade6`, number, optional): Enter the student's grade 6 score, if applicable.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the student.

## Tips
* Double-check the student's information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If the student's grade scores are missing, please provide any available information.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer to your department head or administrator for guidance.
