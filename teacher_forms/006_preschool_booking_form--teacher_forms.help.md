# Preschool Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians for their child's preschool booking. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the parent or guardian's information, including their name, contact details, and any additional information they wish to provide.
2. Enter the child's information, including their date of birth.
3. Select the preferred start and end dates for the booking.
4. Choose the preferred days of the week for the booking.
5. Select a time for the booking.
6. Review and verify all the entered information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Information** (`preschool_booking_form_parent_info`, Text, Required): Please enter your name and contact information.
* **Child Information** (`preschool_booking_form_child_info`, Text, Required): Enter your child's name and other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`preschool_booking_form_contact_info`, Text, Required): Enter your contact information so we can reach you.
* **Booking Details** (`preschool_booking_form_booking_details`, Text, Required): Describe the nature of your booking (e.g., regular attendance, one-time visit).
* **Additional Information** (`preschool_booking_form_additional_info`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional context or details that might help us better understand your booking request.
* **Parent Signature** (`preschool_booking_form_parent_signature`, Text, Optional): If applicable, please sign and date this form to confirm your consent.
* **Child DOB** (`preschool_booking_form_child_dob`, Text, Required): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Start Date** (`preschool_booking_form_start_date`, Date, Optional): Choose the start date for the booking.
* **End Date** (`preschool_booking_form_end_date`, Date, Optional): Choose the end date for the booking.
* **Days of Week** (`preschool_booking_form_days_of_week`, Select One, Optional): Select the preferred days of the week for the booking.
* **Time** (`preschool_booking_form_time`, Time, Optional): Select the preferred time for the booking.
* **Additional Info** (`preschool_booking_form_additional_info`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your booking.

## Tips
- Double-check all entered information before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions or authorizations to book on behalf of someone else.
- Please contact us if you have any questions or concerns about the form or the booking process.
