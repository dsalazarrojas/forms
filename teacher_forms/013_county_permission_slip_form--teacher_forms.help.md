# county_permission_slip_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The county_permission_slip_form is a document used to grant permission for students to participate in school activities, such as field trips or outings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and student's name.
2. Enter the student's date of birth.
3. Provide your contact information (address, city, state, and zip code).
4. Enter your contact phone number.
5. Provide emergency contact information (name and phone number).
6. Enter the student's school name and the number of students participating.
7. Enter the bus driver's name and phone number (if applicable).
8. Enter the trip date and return date.
9. Enter the start and end time of the activity.
10. Enter the duration of the activity.
11. Enter your school email and teacher email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the student's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for emergency contact information.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the guardian (if applicable).
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the guardian (if applicable).
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the teacher (if applicable).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school.
* **Student Count** (`student_count`, text, required): Enter the number of students participating in the activity.
* **Bus Number** (`bus_number`, text, optional): Enter the bus number (if applicable).
* **Bus Driver Name** (`bus_driver_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the bus driver (if applicable).
* **Bus Driver Phone** (`bus_driver_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the bus driver (if applicable).
* **Trip Date** (`trip_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the activity.
* **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the return from the activity.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the activity.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the activity.
* **Duration** (`duration`, text, required): Enter the duration of the activity.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required): Enter your school email.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, required): Enter your email.
