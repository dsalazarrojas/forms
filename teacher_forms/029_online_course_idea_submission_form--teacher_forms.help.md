# Online Course Idea Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Online Course Idea Submission Form" is designed to collect and document ideas from teachers for online courses. This form will help the course development team understand the course concept, identify potential areas of interest, and evaluate the feasibility of the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a title for your course idea.
2. Provide a brief description of your course idea.
3. Select the target audience for your course.
4. Choose the format of your course (Online, Offline, or Workshop).
5. Choose who submitted the idea (Teacher, Administrator, or Student).
6. Enter your contact information and phone number (optional).
7. Select the course length in terms of hours.
8. Attach any supporting materials or files for your course idea (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **course_title** (Text, required): Enter the title of your course idea. This should be a concise and descriptive name that reflects the course's content and purpose.
* **idea_description** (Text, optional): Briefly describe your course idea in a few paragraphs. This is your chance to share your vision and creativity with the course development team.
* **target_audience** (Select One, optional): Select the target audience for your course. This will help the development team understand the intended students and tailor the course content accordingly.
* **submission_date** (Date, required): Enter the date you are submitting your course idea. This helps the team track the submission timeline and evaluate the idea's age.
* **course_format** (Select Multiple, required): Select the format of your course. This can be Online, Offline, or a combination of both.
* **submission_by** (Select One, required): Choose who submitted the idea. This will help the team understand the submitter's role and expertise.
* **contact_information** (Email, optional): Enter your email address so the development team can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **contact_phone** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number for added contact information.
* **course_length** (Number, optional): Estimate the length of your course in hours.
* **submission_file** (Note, optional): Attach any supporting materials or files for your course idea. This can include images, videos, or other relevant documents.
