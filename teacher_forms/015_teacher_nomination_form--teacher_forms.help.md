# Teacher Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a teacher nomination form that allows you to recognize and reward teachers for their outstanding work and contributions. It's used to collect information about the teacher you'd like to nominate, the reason for the nomination, and your own contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the nomination category (yes/no) for the teacher you'd like to nominate.
2. Enter the teacher's name.
3. Enter the school's name.
4. Provide a brief nomination reason for the teacher.
5. Enter the nominee's name.
6. Enter the nominee's department.
7. Enter the nominee's job title.
8. Enter the nominee's years of service.
9. Enter the nominee's email address.
10. Enter the nominee's phone number.
11. Enter your name as the submitter.
12. Enter your title.
13. Enter your department.
14. Enter your email address.
15. Enter your phone number.
16. Provide any additional submitter comments.
17. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.
18. Select "yes" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Category** (<em>Nomination Category</em>, <em>Select Multiple</em>, not required): Select "yes" or "no" to indicate whether you are nominating this teacher.
* **Teacher Name** (<em>Teacher Name</em>, <em>Text</em>, required): Enter the name of the teacher you'd like to nominate.
* **School Name** (<em>School Name</em>, <em>Text</em>, not required): Enter the name of the school where the teacher works.
* **Nomination Reason** (<em>Nomination Reason</em>, <em>Note</em>, not required): Provide a brief reason for your nomination.
* **Nominee Name** (<em>Nominee Name</em>, <em>Text</em>, not required): Enter the name of the nominee.
* **Nominee Department** (<em>Nominee Department</em>, <em>Text</em>, not required): Enter the department of the nominee.
* **Nominee Job Title** (<em>Nominee Job Title</em>, <em>Text</em>, not required): Enter the job title of the nominee.
* **Nominee Years of Service** (<em>Nominee Years of Service</em>, <em>Number</em>, not required): Enter the number of years the nominee has served.
* **Nominee Email** (<em>Nominee Email</em>, <em>Email</em>, not required): Enter the email address of the nominee.
* **Nominee Phone** (<em>Nominee Phone</em>, <em>Text</em>, not required): Enter the phone number of the nominee.
* **Submitter Name** (<em>Submitter Name</em>, <em>Text</em>, not required): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Title** (<em>Submitter Title</em>, <em>Text</em>, not required): Enter your title.
* **Submitter Department** (<em>Submitter Department</em>, <em>Text</em>, not required): Enter your department.
* **Submitter Email** (<em>Submitter Email</em>, <em>Email</em>, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (<em>Submitter Phone</em>, <em>Text</em>, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Comments** (<em>Submitter Comments</em>, <em>Note</em>, not required): Provide any additional submitter comments.
* **Submitter Agrees** (<em>Submitter Agrees</em>, <em>Text</em>, not required): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Submitter Submit** (<em>Submitter Submit</em>, <em>Select One</em>, not required): Select "yes" to submit the form.
* **Submitter Signature** (<em>Submitter Signature</em>, <em>Note</em>, not required): Sign your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Note** (<em>Submitter Note</em>, <em>Note</em>, not required): Provide any additional submitter notes.
* **Submitter Additional Notes** (<em>Submitter Additional Notes</em>, <em>Note</em>, not required): Provide any additional submitter notes.
* **Submitter Acknowledge** (<em>Submitter Acknowledge</em>, <em>Text</em>, not required): Confirm that you acknowledge the submission.
* **Submitter Agrees to Terms** (<em>Submitter Agrees to Terms</em>, <em>Text</em>, not required): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permission to nominate someone before filling out this form.
* Check that a nomination for the same teacher has not been submitted already.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing your nomination reason and submitter comments. This will help the nomination process and the submitter's reputation.
