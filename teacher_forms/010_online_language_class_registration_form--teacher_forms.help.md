# Online Language Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for online language class registration. It is intended to capture essential details from students and teachers for the successful management of online classes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with accurate information.
2. Select the correct level of language proficiency (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced) that matches your skills.
3. Choose the start and end dates for your online class session.
4. Provide your teacher's rating (Excellence, Satisfactory, or Needs Improvement).
5. Optional fields are not mandatory and can be filled in as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Language Class ID** (`language_class_id`, number, required): This field is used to uniquely identify the online language class. Enter the class ID provided by the language class management system.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): This field is meant for the teacher's name who will be leading your online language class.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Please fill this with your name as the student registered for the language class.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to help the teacher contact you for class updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for any communication with the teacher.
* **Language Class Level** (`language_class_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of language proficiency you are registering for: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you want the online class to start on. Please use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you want the online class to end on. Please use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Class Time** (`class_time`, time, optional): Select the time slot for your online class session.
* **Teacher Rating** (`teacher_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your satisfaction level with your teacher. Choose from: Excellence, Satisfactory, or Needs Improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): If you have any comments or feedback to share with the teacher, please type them in here.
