# School Club Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Club Application Form is designed for students to apply for school clubs. It collects information from students about their interests, club type, school year, student name, member status, student email, and student phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of club you are interested in.
2. Choose the school year you are in.
3. Enter your name and email.
4. Select your member status.
5. Provide your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Club Name** (`school_club_name`, `text`, Required: No): Enter the name of the school club you are interested in.
* **Club Type** (`club_type`, `select_one`, Required: No): Choose the type of club you want to join.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, `select_multiple`, Required: No): Select the school year(s) you are in.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, Required: No): Enter your name for the application.
* **Member Status** (`member_status`, `select_one`, Required: No): Select your member status in the club.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, Required: No): Enter your student email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, Required: No): Enter your student phone number.
