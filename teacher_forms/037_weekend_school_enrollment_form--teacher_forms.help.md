# weekend_school_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The weekend_school_enrollment_form is designed to gather information from parents to enroll their children in weekend school programs. This form is used to determine the student's availability and schedule their sessions in the selected class.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Complete all required fields**: Please make sure to fill in all the required fields marked with a red asterisk (\*). These fields are necessary for the enrollment process.
2. **Choose one or multiple classes**: Select one or more classes from the list of options provided in the Class Choice field.
3. **Enter student and contact information**: Provide accurate and up-to-date information about the student and the contact person.
4. **Schedule start and end dates**: Enter the start and end dates for the student's sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Class Choice**: \* (Select one or multiple classes from the options provided)
	+ Select one or multiple classes from the list of options to determine the student's schedule.
* **Student Name**: (Text field) - Enter the student's name.
	+ Enter the name of the student to be enrolled in the weekend school program.
* **Student Date of Birth (DOB)**: (Date field) - Enter the student's date of birth.
	+ Enter the student's date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Parent Name**: (Text field) - Enter the parent's name.
	+ Enter the name of the contact person providing the information.
* **Student Email**: (Email field) - Enter the student's email address.
	+ Enter the student's email address for communication purposes.
* **Student Phone**: (Text field) - Enter the student's phone number.
	+ Enter the student's phone number for easy contact.
* **Start Date**: (Date field) - Enter the start date for the student's sessions.
	+ Enter the date when the student's sessions are scheduled to start.
* **End Date**: (Date field) - Enter the end date for the student's sessions.
	+ Enter the date when the student's sessions are scheduled to end.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to avoid any issues during the enrollment process.
* Please note that the form may not be complete without submitting all the required fields.
* If you need help or clarification about any field, please contact the educational institution for assistance.
