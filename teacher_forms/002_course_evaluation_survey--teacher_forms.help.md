# Course Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from students about their experience in a course.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your experience and opinion about the course.
2. Select one or multiple answers for each question, depending on the field type.
3. Make sure to answer all fields that are marked as "required".
4. Fill in any text fields (e.g., course hours).
5. Once you have finished answering, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to the course administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Content** (`course_content`, text, required: false): Describe the course content, including the quality and relevance of the materials, lectures, and other course materials.
* **Teaching Quality -** (`teaching_quality`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate the teaching quality of the course on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Learning Experience -** (`learning_experience`, select_one, required: false): Rate your learning experience in the course on a scale from Very Good to Poor.
* **Teaching Methods** (`teaching_methods`, text, required: false): Describe the teaching methods used in the course, including any notable strengths or weaknesses.
* **Course Outcome** (`course_outcome`, text, required: false): Describe the course outcome, including any notable achievements or areas for improvement.
* **Instructor Support** (`instructor_support`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate the instructor support on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Student Resources** (`student_resources`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate the student resources available to you on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Class Size** (`class_size`, select_one, required: false): Rate the class size as Small (20-49), Medium (50-99), Large (100-200), or Very Large (200+).
* **Course Hours** (`course_hours`, text, required: false): Fill in the number of hours you spent on the course.
* **Course Format** (`course_format`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate the course format on a scale from Live to Online.
