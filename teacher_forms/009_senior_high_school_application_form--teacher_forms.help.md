# senior_high_school_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The senior high school application form is a mandatory part of the application process for senior high school. It requires students to provide personal and academic information, extracurricular activities, and parent contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and parent name.
2. Enter your graduation date and GPA.
3. Select your extracurricular activities.
4. Choose the school you wish to attend from the provided options.
5. Write a personal statement.
6. Enter your parent's contact information, including their name, email, and phone number.
7. Upload any additional files that support your application.
8. Review and submit the form once all fields are filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (id: 1)
* **First Name**: This field is required and is where you should enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.

### Last Name (id: 2)
* **Last Name**: This field is also required and is where you should enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.

### School Name (id: 3)
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your current high school.

### Graduation Date (id: 4)
* **Graduation Date**: Enter the date you expect to graduate from high school.

### GPA (id: 5)
* **GPA**: Enter your current GPA.

### Extracurricular (id: 6)
* **Extracurricular**: Select one or more extracurricular activities you have been involved in at your current high school. Examples include sports teams, school clubs, community service, and leadership positions within the student government or student newspaper.

### School Choice (id: 7)
* **School Choice**: Choose the school you wish to attend from the provided options.

### Personal Statements (id: 8)
* **Personal Statements**: Write a brief statement explaining why you are a good fit for the school you have chosen and what qualities you possess that make you a strong candidate. This is an opportunity to express your passions and interests.

### Parent Name (id: 9)
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of your parent or guardian.

### Parent Email (id: 10)
* **Parent Email**: Enter the email address of your parent or guardian.

### Parent Phone (id: 11)
* **Parent Phone**: Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.

### Additional Info (id: 12)
* **Additional Info**: This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information that you feel is relevant to your application.

### Uploaded Files (id: 13)
* **Uploaded Files**: Select any files that support your application, such as letters of recommendation or awards certificates.
