# Teacher Intent To Return Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a teacher's intent to return to their job or position. It helps in organizing and processing the return process efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have a legitimate reason to return.
2. Fill out the required fields accurately and completely.
3. Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your return.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`): Enter your unique teacher ID number.
* **School Year** (`school_year`): Enter the current school year you are returning to.
* **Department** (`department`): Check the box if you are returning to your regular department.
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`): Check the box if you are returning to your regular subject area.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`): Enter the name of the course you are returning to teach.
* **Return Reason** (`return_reason`): Provide a brief explanation for your return.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter the tool assigned to you during your absence.
* **Category** (`category`): Enter any relevant category (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a brief description of your return.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`): Enter any related form IDs if applicable.
* **Category** (`category`): Enter any relevant category (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No') (same as above, but with different ID due to duplicate field name).
* **Output File** (`output_file`): Enter the path where you want to save the output file.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter the tool assigned to you during your absence (same as above, but with a different field name).

Note: Some fields may appear to be duplicates, but they are intended to allow for different contexts or scenarios. Ensure you fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
