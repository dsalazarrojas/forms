# Cub Scout Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's registration details for Cub Scouts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your child's first name, last name, and email address.
2. Provide your child's date of birth.
3. Select their scout level (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
4. Enter your emergency contact information, including their first name, last name, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your child's last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your child's email address.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Scout Group** (text): Enter your child's scout group (optional).
* **Address** (text): Enter your child's address (optional).
* **Phone** (text): Enter your child's phone number (optional).
* **Scout Level** (select_one): Select your child's scout level (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (text): Enter the first name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (text): Enter the last name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (email): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If any of the fields are not relevant to your child, it's okay to leave them blank.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us at [contact information].
