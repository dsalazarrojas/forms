# Secondary School Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secondary School Report Form is designed to collect information from teachers about a student's performance, progress, and other relevant details in a secondary school setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as needed, ensuring you provide accurate and relevant information about the student.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name and title as a teacher.
* **Subject** (`subject`, select_one, required/optional): Select the subject you are reporting on (e.g., mathematics, English, etc.).
* **Grade** (`grade`, number, required/optional): Enter the student's current grade level (e.g., 9, 10, 11, etc.).
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's full name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required/optional): If you have the student's email address, please enter it here. This will help the school administration team contact the student directly.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required/optional): If you have the student's phone number, please enter it here. This will help the school administration team contact the student directly.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the report (e.g., month/day/year).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any relevant comments or observations about the student's performance or progress.
* **Teacher Form Comments** (`teacher_form_comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about the student's performance or progress.
* **Student Progress** (`student_progress`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the relevant progress areas where the student has made improvement (e.g., 'Yes' for areas where the student has achieved progress).
