# Learning Modality Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about how you, as a teacher, present and communicate with your students. The goal is to help us better understand your teaching modality and how you engage with your students.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, navigate through the pages of questions, one by one, and answer each question to the best of your ability.
*   For select fields (e.g., "How do you usually present information to your students?" and "What types of technology do you use to support your teaching?"), choose one or multiple options that best describe your current practice.
*   For numeric fields (e.g., "What is the typical length of your lessons?"), enter a number that corresponds to the length of your lessons in minutes.
*   Make sure to complete all pages of the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (page 1) (`page_one`, `text`, required: `false`): This page provides a brief description of the form and its purpose.
*   **How do you usually present information to your students?** (page 2) (`page_two`, `select_one`, required: `false`): This question asks about your preferred method of presenting information to your students, such as Verbal, Visual, Auditory, or Kinesthetic.
*   **What is the typical length of your lessons?** (page 3) (`page_three`, `number`, required: `false`): Please enter the average length of your lessons in minutes.
*   **What type(s) of materials do you usually use to support your lessons?** (page 4) (`page_four`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose one or more of the following options: Handouts, Chalkboard, Whiteboard, Digital display.
*   **Do you use any other methods or tools?** (page 5) (`page_five`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select any additional methods or tools you use, such as Video, Audio recording, Podcast, Online lectures.
*   **What types of technology do you use to support your teaching?** (page 6) (`page_six`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose one or more of the following options: Computer, Tablet, Smartphone, Laptop.
*   **What type(s) of feedback do you usually receive from students?** (page 7) (`page_seven`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose one or more of the following options: Verbal, Written, Electronic.
*   **How often do you usually receive feedback from students?** (page 8) (`page_eight`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Please select one of the following options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Never.
*   **What types of feedback do you usually give students?** (page 9) (`page_nine`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select one or more of the following options: On progress, At the end, On completion, At evaluation.
*   **How many students do you usually give feedback to?** (page 10) (`page_ten`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the number of students you typically give feedback to.
*   **How do you usually communicate with your students?** (page 11) (`page_eleven`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose one or more of the following options: One-on-one, Group discussion, Email, Phone call.
