# Request For Student And School Resources Funding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3. Double-check that all required fields are filled out.
4. Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Enrollment** (`id: 1`, `name: school_enrollment`, `type: number`): Enter the number of students enrolled in your school.
* **Financial Situation** (`id: 2`, `name: financial_situation`, `type: text`): Describe your school's current financial situation, including any challenges or difficulties you are facing.
* **Student Resources** (`id: 3`, `name: student_resources`, `type: select_one`): Indicate whether your school has resources available for students, with the options being "Yes" or "No".
* **Teacher Resources** (`id: 4`, `name: teacher_resources`, `type: select_multiple`): Indicate which resources are available for teachers, with the options being "Yes" or "No" for each resource type.
* **Enrollment Funding** (`id: 5`, `name: enrollment_funding`, `type: number`): Enter the amount of funding available for enrollment-related expenses.
* **Financial Funding** (`id: 6`, `name: financial_funding`, `type: select_one`): Indicate whether your school has access to financial funding, with the options being "Yes" or "No".
* **Other Funding Sources** (`id: 7`, `name: other_funding_sources`, `type: text`): List any other funding sources not included in the previous fields.
* **School Resources** (`id: 8`, `name: school_resources`, `type: select_multiple`): Indicate which school resources are available, with the options being "Yes" or "No" for each resource type.
* **Total Resources Needed** (`id: 9`, `name: total_resources_needed`, `type: number`): Enter the total amount of resources needed to support your school.
* **School Budget** (`id: 10`, `name: school_budget`, `type: date`): Enter your school's current budget date.
* **School Funding** (`id: 11`, `name: school_funding`, `type: time`): Estimate the time spent on school funding activities.
* **Teacher Funding** (`id: 12`, `name: teacher_funding`, `type: select_one`): Indicate whether teachers have access to funding, with the options being "Yes" or "No".
* **Student Funding** (`id: 13`, `name: student_funding`, `type: select_multiple`): Indicate which funding sources are available to students, with the options being "Yes" or "No" for each source type.
* **School Grants** (`id: 14`, `name: school_grants`, `type: select_one`): Indicate whether your school has access to school grants, with the options being "Yes" or "No".
* **School Loans** (`id: 15`, `name: school_loans`, `type: select_multiple`): Indicate which types of school loans are available, with the options being "Yes" or "No" for each type.
* **School Donations** (`id: 16`, `name: school_donations`, `type: select_one`): Indicate whether your school receives school donations, with the options being "Yes" or "No".
* **School Investments** (`id: 17`, `name: school_investments`, `type: select_multiple`): Indicate which types of school investments are available, with the options being "Yes" or "No" for each type.
* **Total Funding Sources** (`id: 18`, `name: total_funding_sources`, `type: select_multiple`): Indicate all the funding sources available to your school, with the options being "Yes" or "No" for each source type.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you need help with any field, contact your school representative.
* This form is confidential, but your school's resource needs will be used to help support your school.
