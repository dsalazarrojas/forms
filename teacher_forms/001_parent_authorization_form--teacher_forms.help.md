# Parent Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from parents or legal guardians to authorize a child's participation in an activity or program.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please provide the required and optional fields with accurate and truthful information.
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out with the correct type of information (e.g., email for the Email field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name
* First name: This is the child's first name.
### Last name
* Last name: This is the child's last name.
### Relationship
* Relationship: This is your relationship to the child (e.g., mother, father, guardian).
### Date of birth
* Date of birth: The child's date of birth.
### Parent's address
* Parent's address: Your home or current address for contact and communication purposes.
### Parent's phone number
* Parent's phone number: Your contact phone number.
### Email
* Email: Your email address for efficient communication.
