# Migrant Student Academic And Career Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a migrant student's academic and career aspirations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page carefully, and make sure to answer all required fields.
2. Review each page for accuracy and completeness before proceeding to the next page.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please seek guidance from your teacher or staff member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Migrant Student Information** (`migrant_student_academic_and_career_plan_form_1`, text, Optional): This field is for general information about the migrant student. It is not a required field, but it helps us understand their background.
* **Student Name** (`migrant_student_academic_and_career_plan_form_2`, text, Optional): This field is for the student's name. Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Student ID** (`migrant_student_academic_and_career_plan_form_3`, text, Optional): This field is for your student ID number or passport number. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Migrant Education Background** (`migrant_student_academic_and_career_plan_form_4`, text, Optional): This field is for describing your migrant education background. Please provide any relevant information about your education in your home country or previous educational institutions.
* **Language Spoken** (`migrant_student_academic_and_career_plan_form_5`, select_multiple, Optional): This field is for selecting the languages you are proficient in. Please choose all languages you have a good understanding of.
* **Date of Birth** (`migrant_student_academic_and_career_plan_form_6`, date, Optional): This field is for your date of birth. Please enter it in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Grade Level** (`migrant_student_academic_and_career_plan_form_7`, select_one, Optional): This field is for indicating your current grade level. Please select the corresponding option that best describes your level of education.
* **Grade Level Explanation** (`migrant_student_academic_and_career_plan_form_8`, text, Optional): This field is for providing any further explanation about your grade level. Please use it to clarify any discrepancies or provide additional information.
* **Migrant Student Career Goals** (`migrant_student_academic_and_career_plan_form_9`, select_multiple, Optional): This field is for selecting your career goals. Please choose all options that apply.
* **Career Goal Description** (`migrant_student_academic_and_career_plan_form_10`, text, Optional): This field is for describing your career goals in more detail. Please be as specific as possible.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields to ensure the form is complete and submitted correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please seek guidance from your teacher or staff member.
* Please review each page carefully before proceeding to the next page.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your teacher or staff member for assistance.
