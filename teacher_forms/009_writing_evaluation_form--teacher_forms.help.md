# Writing Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate a student's writing quality, clarity, organization, grammar, and creativity. It is filled out by an instructor or educator to provide feedback on a student's writing assignment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the student's name in the **Student Name** field.
2. Enter the title of the writing assignment in the **Title** field.
3. Evaluate the writing quality by selecting one of the following options:
	* **Excellent**: The writing is of high quality, meets the requirements and expectations.
	* **Needs Improvement**: The writing requires some improvement, but has some positive qualities.
	* **Unsatisfactory**: The writing does not meet the requirements and expectations.
4. Evaluate the **Clarity** by selecting all that apply from the following options:
	* **Clear**: The writing is easy to understand.
	* **Somewhat Clear**: The writing is somewhat easy to understand, but has some areas that need clarification.
	* **Unclear**: The writing is difficult to understand.
5. Evaluate the **Organization** by selecting one of the following options:
	* **Good**: The writing is well-organized and easy to follow.
	* **Fair**: The writing has some organizational issues, but is still somewhat coherent.
	* **Poor**: The writing is disorganized and difficult to follow.
6. Evaluate the **Grammar** by selecting one of the following options:
	* **Good**: The writing has proper grammar and punctuation.
	* **Fair**: The writing has some grammatical errors, but is still mostly correct.
	* **Poor**: The writing has many grammatical errors.
7. Enter your constructive feedback in the **Constructive Feedback** field.
8. (Optional) Enter any additional comments or suggestions in the **Constructive Feedback** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: The student's name, as it appears on their work.
* **Title**: The title of the writing assignment, as it appears on the work.
* **Writing Quality**: Evaluate the overall quality of the writing.
* **Clarity**: Evaluate how easy or difficult it is to understand the writing.
* **Organization**: Evaluate the organization and structure of the writing.
* **Grammar**: Evaluate the grammar and punctuation in the writing.
* **Creativity**: Evaluate the student's creativity and originality in the writing.
* **Constructive Feedback**: Enter any specific, actionable feedback or suggestions for the student.
* **Additional Comments**: (Optional) Enter any additional comments or suggestions for the student.
