# Parental Leave Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request parental leave for an employee. It is essential for employees to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that their leave request is processed correctly and promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information and attachments ready before starting.
2. Enter your Employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Select the start date of your leave in the "start_date" field.
4. Select the end date of your leave in the "end_date" field.
5. Provide a clear reason for your leave in the "reason" field.
6. Confirm that your manager has approved or denied your request in the "Manager Confirmation" field.
7. (Optional) You may attach a file to support your request in the "Attachment URL" field.
8. (Optional) If you have a message for your manager, enter it in the "Employee Message" field.
9. Finally, enter a message for your manager to respond in the "Manager Message" field, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, required): Enter your employee ID number, as assigned by the HR department.
* **start_date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you plan to start your leave from.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you plan to end your leave to.
* **reason** (`reason`, `text`, required): Provide a clear and concise reason for your leave request.
* **Manager Confirmation** (`Manager Confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Choose "Approved" if your manager has approved your request, "Denied" if they have denied it, or "Pending" if they are still considering it.
* **Employee Message** (`Employee Message`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or messages for your manager, enter them here.
* **Attachment URL** (`Attachment URL`, `text`, optional): Upload any relevant attachment supporting your leave request, such as a doctor's note or a supporting document.
* **Manager Message** (`Manager Message`, `text`, optional): If your manager has a message to share with you, enter it here.
