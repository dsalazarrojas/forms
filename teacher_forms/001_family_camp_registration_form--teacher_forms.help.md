# Family Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Camp Registration Form is designed to collect information about your family members who will be attending the camp program. This form helps us to understand your family's needs and ensure that we can provide a safe and enjoyable experience for all campers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your family's information in the "Family Information" section.
2. Fill out the "Camper Information" section with details about each camper who will be attending.
3. Answer the "Medical History" questions to help us understand any health concerns we should be aware of.
4. If necessary, provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact Information" section.
5. If you have any previous camp experience, please share it in the "Previous Camp Activity" section.
6. Add any additional comments or information you think we should know in the "Additional Comments" section.
7. Finally, provide information on your camper's health status and any medical conditions in the "Camper Health Status" and "Medical Conditions" sections.
8. If your camper has any allergies, please select all applicable options in the "Allergies" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Information (text)** (`family_info`, required: false): Provide your family's full name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Camper Information (text)** (`camper_info`, required: false): List each camper's name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical History (text)** (`medical_history`, required: false): Share any medical conditions or concerns we should be aware of.
* **Emergency Contact Information (text)** (`emergency_contact_info`, required: false): Provide the name, phone number, and relationship of the emergency contact person.
* **Previous Camp Activity (text)** (`previous_camp_activity`, required: false): If you have any previous camp experience, please share it with us.
* **Additional Comments (text)** (`additional_comments`, required: false): Add any other information you think we should know about your family.
* **Camper Health Status (select_one)** (`camper_health_status`, required: false): Select "Good" if your camper is in good health, "Fair" if they have some health concerns, or "Poor" if they have significant health concerns.
* **Medical Conditions (select_multiple)** (`medical_conditions`, required: false): Select all applicable medical conditions from the list.
* **Allergies (select_multiple)** (`allergies`, required: false): Select all applicable allergies from the list.

## Tips
* Please be as thorough as possible when providing information about your family and campers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will review and verify the information provided before allowing your camper to participate in the camp program.
