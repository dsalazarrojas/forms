# nursery_school_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us gather essential information about your child in order to provide the best possible care and education.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with accurate and up-to-date information about your child.
2. Make sure to check the "required" fields, as these are essential for us to process your child's registration.
3. Double-check your contact information to ensure we can reach you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name**: This is your child's name.
	+ **Type**: Text field for you to enter your child's name.
	+ **Required**: Not required, but please ensure you enter accurate information.
* **parent_name**: This is your name and contact information as the child's parent or guardian.
	+ **Type**: Text field for you to enter your name.
	+ **Required**: Not required, but please ensure you enter accurate information.
* **student_age**: This is your child's age.
	+ **Type**: Number field for you to enter your child's age.
	+ **Required**: Not required, but please ensure you enter accurate information.
* **date_of_birth**: This is your child's date of birth.
	+ **Type**: Date field for you to enter your child's date of birth.
	+ **Required**: Not required, but please ensure you enter accurate information.
* **phone_number**: This is your contact phone number.
	+ **Type**: Text field for you to enter your contact phone number.
	+ **Required**: Not required, but please ensure you enter accurate information.
* **email_address**: This is your contact email address.
	+ **Type**: Email field for you to enter your contact email address.
	+ **Required**: Not required, but please ensure you enter accurate information.
* **address**: This is your contact address.
	+ **Type**: Text field for you to enter your contact address.
	+ **Required**: Not required, but please ensure you enter accurate information.

## Tips
- Please ensure you complete the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our nursery school administration.
