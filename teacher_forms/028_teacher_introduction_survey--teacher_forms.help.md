# teacher_introduction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help teachers introduce themselves by providing essential information about their background, experience, and areas of expertise. It will be used to populate a database or system for teacher pairing or resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by providing the required information in each field.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure effective matching.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`teacher_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official records or ID cards.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official records or ID cards.
* **Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter your official email address for communication purposes.
* **Job Title** (`teacher_job_title`, select_one, required): Select your job title from the options provided (Teacher, Counselor, Admin).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your current or primary school.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been teaching or working in your field.
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, text, required): Enter the subject area you specialize in or teach.
* **Notes** (`teacher_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your experience or expertise.
* **Selected Files** (`selected_files`, select_multiple, optional): Select Yes if you have selected files or resources for use, No otherwise.
* **Selected Tools** (`selected_tools`, select_multiple, optional): Select Yes if you have selected tools or equipment for use, No otherwise.
* **School Name 2**, **School Name 3**, **School Name 4** (`school_name_2`, `school_name_3`, `school_name_4`, text, optional): Enter the names of additional schools you are affiliated with.
* **Years of Experience 2**, **Years of Experience 3**, **Years of Experience 4** (`years_of_experience_2`, `years_of_experience_3`, `years_of_experience_4`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been teaching or working at each additional school.
* **Subject Area 2**, **Subject Area 3**, **Subject Area 4** (`subject_area_2`, `subject_area_3`, `subject_area_4`, text, optional): Enter the subject areas you specialize in or teach at each additional school.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure effective matching.
* Review and submit the form once you have completed all the fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the administrator or support team for assistance.
