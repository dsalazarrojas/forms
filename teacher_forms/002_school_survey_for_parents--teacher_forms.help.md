# School Survey For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about the parent-teacher relationship and teaching methods in a school setting. It will help us understand the level of parent involvement, communication, and perception of teaching effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select one or more options for each field, depending on your response.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **In Person Teaching (1)** (`Yes`, `No`, `Partial`): 
  This question asks if you prefer in-person teaching methods, such as classroom instruction and direct interaction with teachers. Choose one or more options that apply to you.

* **Parent Involvement (2)** (`Very Involved`, `Slightly Involved`, `Not Involved`): 
  This question assesses your level of involvement in your child's education. Choose the option that best describes your level of participation.

* **Online Teaching (3)** (`Yes`, `No`): 
  This question determines your preference for online teaching methods, such as webinars and digital resources. Choose the option that applies to you.

* **Teaching Hours (4)** (number): 
  This question asks you to provide the number of hours you spend with teachers discussing your child's education. Please enter a number that best represents your actual time spent.

* **Parent Involvement Frequency (5)** (`Daily`, `Weekly`, `Monthly`, `Yearly`): 
  This question asks about how often you are involved in your child's education. Choose the option that best describes your frequency of involvement.

* **Number of Teaching Days (6)** (number): 
  This question asks you to provide the number of days you have interacted with teachers discussing your child's education. Please enter a number that best represents your actual interactions.

* **Does Parent Talk with Teacher (7)** (`Yes`, `No`, `Rarely`): 
  This question determines if you often or rarely have conversations with teachers about your child's education. Choose the option that best describes your interaction frequency.

* **Parent Participation (8)** (`Yes`, `No`): 
  This question assesses your willingness to participate in your child's education. Choose the option that applies to you.

* **Teacher Parent Relationship (9)** (`Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): 
  This question asks you to rate the quality of your relationship with teachers. Choose the option that best describes your relationship.

* **Student Feedback (10)** (`Positive`, `Neutral`, `Negative`): 
  This question asks for your opinion on the effectiveness of teaching methods and materials. Choose the option that best describes your perception.

* **Parent Feedback (11)** (`Positive`, `Neutral`, `Negative`): 
  This question asks for your feedback on the school's teaching methods and materials. Choose the option that best describes your opinion.

* **Parent Awareness (12)** (`Yes`, `No`, `Partial`): 
  This question asks about your understanding of your child's education. Choose the option that best describes your level of awareness.

* **Teaching Tools (13)** (`Yes`, `No`): 
  This question assesses the use of teaching tools and resources in the school. Choose the option that applies to you.

* **Teaching Materials (14)** (`Standard`, `Supplemental`): 
  This question asks about the types of teaching materials used in the school. Choose the option that best describes the materials used.

* **Communication Channels (15)** (`Email`, `Phone`, `Text`, `In Person`, `Other`): 
  This question asks about the channels used for communication between parents and teachers. Choose one or more options that apply to you.

* **Teacher Feedback (16)** (`Positive`, `Neutral`, `Negative`): 
  This question asks for your opinion on the effectiveness of teacher feedback. Choose the option that best describes your perception.

Please take your time to answer each question carefully and honestly. Your input will help us improve the parent-teacher relationship and teaching methods in the school. Thank you for participating in this survey!
