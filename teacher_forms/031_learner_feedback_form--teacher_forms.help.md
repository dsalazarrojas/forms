# learner_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The learner_feedback_form is a tool used to gather feedback from learners about their experiences with a course. It helps instructors and course creators understand the course's strengths and weaknesses, allowing them to make improvements and provide better learning experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the link to access the form.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed.
3. Choose your answer to the select_one field (Overall Feeling).
4. Submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Course Info** (`course_info`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your experience with the course. This can include any comments or observations you have about the course content, structure, or delivery.
- **Student Progress** (`student_progress`, text, required: false): Describe your progress in the course. This can include information about your strengths, weaknesses, or areas where you're struggling.
- **Overall Feeling** (`overall_feeling`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you generally feel about the course. Please select one of the following options: Very Positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, or Very Negative.

## Tips
- Be honest and provide constructive feedback.
- If you're taking the course, fill in the form after completing it or at the end of the course.
- If you're not taking the course, you can still fill in the form with general feedback.
