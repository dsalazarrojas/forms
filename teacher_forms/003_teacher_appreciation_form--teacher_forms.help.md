# teacher_appreciation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The teacher_appreciation_form is designed to collect feedback and appreciation from students and colleagues of a teacher, which will help in understanding what works well and what can be improved in the teacher's practice.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Introduction
Before filling out the form, take a moment to reflect on your relationship with the teacher. Think about the qualities or actions that you appreciate most about the teacher's practice.

### Step 2: Provide Feedback
Complete the form by selecting the appreciation_type, specifying the appreciation_amount, and providing a message or appreciation_name, all of which will help in understanding your perspective on the teacher's performance.

### Step 3: Specify Appreciation Details
Select the appreciation_date and appreciation_time, which will help in understanding the context of your appreciation.

### Step 4: Specify Appreciation Targets
Select who you would like to appreciate, which could be students, colleagues, or both.

### Step 5: Review and Submit
Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Appreciation Form Fields** (<type>: text): This is the main section of the form where you can provide your thoughts, suggestions, and feedback about the teacher's practice.
* **Appreciation Name** (<type>: text): Provide a specific name or title that describes what you appreciate about the teacher's practice.
* **Message** (<type>: text): Write a brief message or description of your appreciation for the teacher's practice.
* **Appreciation Date** (<type>: date): Select the date you would like to appreciate the teacher's practice on.
* **Appreciation Time** (<type>: time): Select the time you would like to appreciate the teacher's practice on.
* **Appreciation Type** (<type>: select_multiple): Select one or more of the following options: Token, Verbal.
* **Appreciation Amount** (<type>: number): Provide a specific amount or quantity of appreciation you would like to show.
* **Appreciation Who** (<type>: select_multiple): Select one or more of the following options: Student, Colleagues, Both.

## Tips

* Be sincere and specific when providing feedback and appreciation.
* Focus on the positive qualities or actions of the teacher's practice.
* Avoid criticizing or complaining about the teacher's practice.
* Use clear and concise language in your message and appreciation name.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
