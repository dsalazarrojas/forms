# Teacher Metacognition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Metacognition Survey is designed to help teachers reflect on their current metacognitive practices, identify areas for improvement, and understand how often they engage in metacognitive practices that impact student learning outcomes. This survey aims to aid in developing targeted professional development programs and improving teaching quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and think about your current metacognitive practices as a teacher.
2. Select the options that best describe your current practices for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your metacognitive practices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Metacognitive Practices** (`metacognitive_practices`, select_multiple): This question asks you to select all the metacognitive practices you currently engage in, such as Planning and Preparation, Classroom Questioning, Feedback, and Self-Reflection. Choose all the options that apply to your teaching practices.
* **Metacognitive Practices Frequency** (`metacognitive_practices_frequency`, select_multiple): This question asks you to select how often you engage in metacognitive practices like Planning and Preparation, Classroom Questioning, Feedback, and Self-Reflection. Choose all the options that apply to your teaching practices.
* **Reflection** (`reflection`, select_multiple): This question asks you to select the types of reflections you make, such as Pre-class reflections, Post-class reflections, and Self-Reflections. Choose all the options that apply to your teaching practices.
* **Metacognition Frequency** (`metacognition_frequency`, select_one): This question asks you to select how often you engage in metacognitive practices. Choose the option that best describes your frequency.
* **Impact on Student** (`impact_on_student`, select_one): This question asks you to select the impact of your metacognitive practices on your students. Choose the option that best describes your perception.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, text): This question asks you to describe any challenges you face in implementing metacognitive practices. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text): This question asks you to suggest any areas for improving metacognitive practices. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Teacher Involvement** (`teacher_involvement`, select_one): This question asks you to select the level of your involvement with metacognitive practices. Choose the option that best describes your involvement.
* **Metacognitive Practices Influence** (`metacognitive_practices_influence`, select_one): This question asks you to select the influence of metacognitive practices on your teaching. Choose the option that best describes your perception.

Remember, the more honest and detailed your responses, the more valuable the survey results will be for developing targeted professional development programs.
