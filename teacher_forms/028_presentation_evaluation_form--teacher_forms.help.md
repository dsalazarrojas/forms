# Presentation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Presentation Evaluation Form is designed to help you provide constructive feedback on presentations given by others. This form guides you through the key aspects of a presentation, allowing you to evaluate its content, organization, and overall effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the entire form carefully before providing feedback.
2. Answer each question based on your observations and experiences.
3. Be honest and constructive in your feedback, providing specific examples whenever possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Here's what each field is about and how to answer it:

* **presentation evaluation form**:
	+ **Label:** presentation_evaluation_form
	+ **Type:** Note (this is a free-text field)
	+ **Purpose:** This field is for writing a brief summary or overall impression of the presentation. You can share your thoughts on its content, organization, and delivery.
* **participant_insights**:
	+ **Label:** participant_insights
	+ **Type:** Text (open-ended question)
	+ **Purpose:** Provide insights into what the presenter did well or struggled with during the presentation. This could be about their verbal or non-verbal communication, engagement, or overall performance.
* **comments**:
	+ **Label:** comments
	+ **Type:** Text (open-ended question)
	+ **Purpose:** Share any observations, suggestions, or areas for improvement you noticed during the presentation. This field is for providing specific, actionable feedback.

## Tips
* Be specific with your feedback, providing concrete examples whenever possible.
* Focus on the presentation itself, rather than the presenter's personality or character.
* Proofread your answers to ensure they are clear and concise.
* If you're unsure about something, don't hesitate to ask for clarification or provide a neutral comment.
* Remember, this form is a tool for growth and improvement, so aim for constructive feedback that helps the presenter become better.
