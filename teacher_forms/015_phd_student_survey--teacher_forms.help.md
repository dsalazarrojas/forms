# phd-student-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The phd-student-survey form is designed to collect information from PhD students about their background, research interests, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your background information on the first page.
2. Select your research interests from the available options on the second page.
3. Choose your education level on the third page.
4. Enter your contact information on the fourth page.
5. Enter your date of birth on the fifth page.
6. Select your preferred time of day on the sixth page.
7. Add any additional notes or comments on the seventh page.
8. Enter your email address on the eighth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **background-info** (`text`, optional): This field is used to enter your background information, such as your work experience, research experience, or any other relevant details about yourself.
* **research-interests** (`select_one`, required): Select one or more research interests that align with your work or goals. This will help us understand your areas of research focus.
* **education** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose your education level, such as Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD.
* **contact-info** (`text`, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, or any other relevant details.
* **date-of-birth** (`date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **time-of-day** (`time`, optional): Select your preferred time of day in 24-hour format, such as 10:00 or 14:30.
* **note** (`note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments you have.
* **email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
