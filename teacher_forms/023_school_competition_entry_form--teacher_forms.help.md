# School Competition Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Competition Entry Form is used to create or update a competition entry in the school competition management system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the appropriate options for the competition type, status, and other relevant fields.
3. Enter the competition schedule and duration details.
4. Provide the teacher's and school's contact information.
5. Save the form to create or update the competition entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Competition Entry Form** (`competition_entry_form`): This is the main field to describe the competition entry.
* **Schedule Competition** (`schedule_competition`): Enter the schedule details of the competition.
* **Register Competition** (`register_competition`): Enter the registration details of the competition.
* **School Details** (`school_details`): Enter the school's details, including name, location, and building number.
* **Competition Type** (`competition_type`): Select the type of competition (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Competition Date** (`competition_date`): Enter the date of the competition.
* **Competition Start Time** (`competition_start_time`): Enter the start time of the competition.
* **Competition End Time** (`competition_end_time`): Enter the end time of the competition.
* **Competition Duration** (`competition_duration`): Enter the duration of the competition.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`): Enter the teacher's name associated with the competition.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`): Enter the teacher's email.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the school's name.
* **School Location** (`school_location`): Enter the school's location.
* **School Building** (`school_building`): Enter the school's building number.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`): Enter the room number associated with the competition.
* **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`): Enter the teacher's ID.
* **Competition ID** (`competition_id`): Enter the competition ID.
* **Entry ID** (`entry_id`): Enter the entry ID.
* **Competition Status** (`competition_status`): Select the competition status (e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive').
* **Competition Status Date** (`competition_status_date`): Enter the date of the competition status change.
* **Competition Status Note** (`competition_status_note`): Enter a note about the competition status change.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter the tool assigned to the competition.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`): Enter the form ID.
* **Category** (`category`): Enter the category of the competition.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
* Double-check the entered information for accuracy.
* Use the correct format for dates and times.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your school's competition management system documentation or consult with a colleague.
