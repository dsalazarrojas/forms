# Online Parent Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Online Parent Information Form" is a crucial tool for teachers and school administrators to collect vital information from parents about their children. This form aims to establish a connection between parents, teachers, and school administrators, ensuring that everyone involved is informed and supportive of the child's educational journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Student First Name
	* Student Last Name
	* Student Grade
	* School
	* Teacher
2. Optional fields:
	* Parent Organization
	* PTA Group
	* Parent Advocate
	* Parent Coordinator
	* School Administrator

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your name as it is written on your identification documents. This field is required.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it is written on your identification documents. This field is required.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication. This field is required.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached. This field is required.
* **Student First Name**: Enter your child's first name. This field is required.
* **Student Last Name**: Enter your child's last name. This field is required.
* **Student Grade**: Enter your child's grade level. This field is required.
* **School**: Enter the name of your child's school. This field is required.
* **Teacher**: Enter the name of your child's teacher. This field is required.
* **Parent Organization**: If you are part of a parent organization, enter its name here. This field is optional.
* **PTA Group**: If you are part of a PTA group, enter its name here. This field is optional.
* **Parent Advocate**: If you are a parent advocate, enter your role here. This field is optional.
* **Parent Coordinator**: If you are a parent coordinator, enter your role here. This field is optional.
* **School Administrator**: If you are a school administrator, enter your role here. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for any errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your child's teacher or school administrator for clarification.
* Remember to save your work regularly to avoid losing any progress.
