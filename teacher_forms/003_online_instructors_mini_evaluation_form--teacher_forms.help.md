# Online Instructors Mini Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help instructors improve their teaching skills by providing them with feedback on their communication style, organization, and course content. The feedback will help them to better understand what works and what needs improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review the sections below and answer the questions in each section based on your experience with the instructor.
2. For each question, choose the best answer that reflects your thoughts and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you think of the instructor's communication style?** (Section 1, text, required: false)
	+ This question is asking about the instructor's ability to clearly and effectively communicate with the class.
	+ Please describe your thoughts on how the instructor communicated with you, including their tone, pace, and clarity of explanation.
* **What was your experience with the instructor's organization?** (Section 1, text, required: false)
	+ This question is asking about the instructor's ability to structure and organize the lesson material.
	+ Please think about how well the instructor managed the class time, presented the material, and made sure you had a clear understanding of what was expected.
* **How clear were the course objectives?** (Section 2, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question is asking about the clarity and specificity of the course objectives.
	+ Rate the clarity of the course objectives, choosing from the options: Clear, Somewhat clear, Somewhat unclear, Very unclear.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of the course content?** (Section 3, number, required: false)
	+ This question is asking about your overall impression of the course material.
	+ Rate the quality of the course content on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Was the course relevant to your needs?** (Section 4, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This question is asking about whether the course met your needs and interests.
	+ Choose from the options: Yes, No, Somewhat, Not relevant.
* **Would you recommend this course to a colleague?** (Section 5, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question is asking about your confidence in recommending the course to others.
	+ Choose from the options: Yes, No.
* **Would you recommend this course to a friend or peer?** (Section 5, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question is asking about your confidence in recommending the course to others outside of your professional network.
	+ Choose from the options: Yes, No.

## Tips
* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional comments" section.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback to the instructor!
