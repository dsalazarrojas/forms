# Cyberbullying And Online Aggression Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on teachers' and students' experiences with cyberbullying and online aggression, aiming to identify areas for improvement and provide insights on how to address these issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as honestly and accurately as possible. You may select one or multiple options for each field, depending on your experiences and circumstances.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Info** (`survey_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional information about your experience with cyberbullying and online aggression.
* **Have you had any experience as a teacher?** (`teacher_experience`, select_one, optional): Answer "Yes" if you have had experience as a teacher, and "No" otherwise.
* **Type of Institution** (`institution_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of institution where you work, such as School, University, or College.
* **How old are the students you interact with?** (`student_age`, select_multiple, optional): Select the age range of the students you interact with, such as 13-15, 16-18, or 19-21.
* **Age of Teacher** (`teacher_age`, number, optional): Enter your age.
* **How many years have you taught?** (`teacher_experience`, text, optional): Describe how many years you have been teaching.
* **How often do you see students displaying aggressive behavior?** (`student_behavior`, select_multiple, optional): Select how often you see students displaying aggressive behavior, such as Never, Rarely, Sometimes, or Often.
* **How often do you feel stressed when dealing with students displaying aggressive behavior?** (`teacher_feeling`, select_multiple, optional): Select how often you feel stressed when dealing with students displaying aggressive behavior, such as Never, Rarely, Sometimes, or Often.
* **How often do students feel stressed when interacting with you?** (`student_feeling`, select_multiple, optional): Select how often students feel stressed when interacting with you, such as Never, Rarely, Sometimes, or Often.
* **What kind of help do you receive?** (`teacher_help`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of help you receive, such as Colleague, School Administrator, or Mental Health Professional.
* **What kind of support do the students receive?** (`student_support`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of support the students receive, such as Colleague, School Administrator, or Mental Health Professional.
* **How satisfied are you with your current job?** (`teacher_satisfaction`, number, optional): Enter a number indicating how satisfied you are with your current job.
* **How satisfied are the students with their current job?** (`student_satisfaction`, number, optional): Enter a number indicating how satisfied the students are with their current job.
* **What kind of support system do you have in place?** (`teacher_support_system`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of support systems you have in place, such as Colleague, School Administrator, or Mental Health Professional.
* **What kind of support system do the students have in place?** (`student_support_system`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of support systems the students have in place, such as Colleague, School Administrator, or Mental Health Professional.
* **How often do you give help to students displaying aggressive behavior?** (`teacher_help_given`, select_multiple, optional): Select how often you give help to students displaying aggressive behavior, such as Never, Rarely, Sometimes, or Often.
* **How often do students seek help for displaying aggressive behavior?** (`student_help_given`, select_multiple, optional): Select how often students seek help for displaying aggressive behavior, such as Never, Rarely, Sometimes, or Often.
* **What is the reason you give help to students displaying aggressive behavior?** (`teacher_help_given_reason`, text, optional): Describe the reason you give help to students displaying aggressive behavior.
* **What is the reason students seek help for displaying aggressive behavior?** (`student_help_given_reason`, text, optional): Describe the reason students seek help for displaying aggressive behavior.
* **Do you believe that this form helped you identify areas for improvement?** (`teacher_conclusion`, select_one, optional): Answer "Yes" if you believe this form helped you identify areas for improvement, and "No" otherwise.

## Tips
- Answer honestly and accurately to provide valuable insights.
- You may select one or multiple options for each field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
- This form will help identify areas for improvement and provide insights on how to address these issues.
