# Preschool Teacher Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Teacher Application Form is used to gather basic information from applicants who are interested in working as a preschool teacher at our institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and last name in the "first_name" and "last_name" fields.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email_address" field.
5. List your highest qualification or degree earned in the "highest_qualification" field.
6. Enter the name of the preschool you are interested in applying for in the "preschool_name" field.
7. Select the position you are applying for from the list in the "position_applied_for" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name, such as John.
* **Last Name** (`teacher_lastname`, text, required): Enter your last name, such as Doe.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY, such as 01/01/1990.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number, such as 123-456-7890.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address, such as johndoe@example.com.
* **Highest Qualification** (`highest_qualification`, text, required): Enter your highest degree or qualification earned, such as Bachelor of Education.
* **Preschool Name** (`preschool_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the preschool you are applying for, such as "ABC Preschool".
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied_for`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more of the available positions from the list, such as "Preschool teacher" and "Intern". 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
* Be as specific as possible when listing your highest qualification and preschool name.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
