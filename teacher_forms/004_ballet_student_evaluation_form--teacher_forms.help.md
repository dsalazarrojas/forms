# Ballet Student Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ballet Student Evaluation Form is a comprehensive assessment tool used to evaluate the technical and artistic progress of ballet students. It provides instructors with an objective and standardized way to measure student development and provide actionable feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's full name as it appears on their record.
2. Enter the student's age in years.
3. Enter the student's date of birth.
4. Enter the name of the parent or guardian contact person.
5. Enter the primary phone number of the parent or guardian.
6. Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
7. Select the current class level of the student.
8. Enter the number of years the student has been training in ballet.
9. Rate the student's technique, posture and alignment, flexibility, musicality, artistry, and turnout.
10. Rate the student's jumps and allegro, pirouettes, and turns.
11. Rate the student's attendance rate in class.
12. Rate the student's attitude and effort in class.
13. Enter any specific strengths the student exhibits in ballet.
14. Enter any areas where the student needs improvement in ballet.
15. Enter the instructor's recommendations for the student.
16. Indicate if the student is ready for level advancement.
17. Indicate if the student is ready for recital or competition.
18. Select the current evaluation period (term or semester).
19. Enter the instructor's name.
20. Enter the date of the evaluation.
21. Enter any additional comments or observations about the student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_full_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their record.
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, number, required): Enter the student's age in years.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for the student.
* **Parent Contact Phone** (`parent_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the primary phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email Address** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian for progress reports and communications.
* **Current Class Level** (`class_level`, select_one, required): Select the current class level of the student from the provided options.
* **Years of Training** (`years_of_training`, number, required): Enter the number of years the student has been training in ballet.
* **Technique Rating** (`technique_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the student's overall technical proficiency from the provided options.
* **Posture and Alignment** (`posture_alignment`, select_one, required): Rate the student's body positioning and form from the provided options.
* **Flexibility** (`flexibility`, select_one, required): Rate the student's range of motion and stretching ability from the provided options.
* **Musicality** (`musicality`, select_one, required): Rate the student's rhythm and timing with music from the provided options.
* **Artistry and Expression** (`artistry_expression`, select_one, required): Rate the student's emotional connection and performance quality from the provided options.
* **Turnout** (`turnout`, select_one, required): Rate the student's external rotation of legs from hips from the provided options.
* **Jumps and Allegro** (`jumps_allegro`, select_one, required): Rate the student's jumping technique and elevation from the provided options.
* **Pirouettes and Turns** (`pirouettes_turns`, select_one, required): Rate the student's turning technique and balance from the provided options.
* **Attendance Rate** (`attendance_rate`, select_one, required): Rate the student's class participation consistency from the provided options.
* **Attitude and Effort** (`attitude_effort`, select_one, required): Rate the student's work ethic and classroom behavior from the provided options.
* **Student Strengths** (`strengths`, text, required): Enter specific areas where the student excels in ballet.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, required): Enter specific skills that the student needs to develop in ballet.
* **Teacher Recommendations** (`teacher_recommendations`, text, required): Enter suggestions for continued growth and improvement from the instructor.
* **Ready for Level Advancement** (`ready_for_advancement`, select_one, required): Indicate if the student is ready for level advancement from the provided options.
* **Performance Readiness** (`performance_readiness`, select_one, required): Indicate if the student is ready for recital or competition from the provided options.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, select_one, required): Select the current evaluation period (term or semester) from the provided options.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the instructor's name.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the student.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Be honest and objective in your evaluations to provide actionable feedback for the student.
* Use the provided options for ratings and selections whenever possible.
* Enter specific strengths and areas for improvement to support the student's growth and development.
* Proofread your responses carefully before submitting the form.
