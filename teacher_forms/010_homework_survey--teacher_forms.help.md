# Homework Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from teachers on their homework experiences, perceptions, and opinions. It's crucial to provide accurate and useful data for the development and improvement of educational materials and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your honest opinion and experience.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your responses.
3. Do not repeat or copy responses from other teachers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Homework Survey Form** (`homework_survey_form`, text, required/optional): This field is the title of the form. Please do not fill in anything here as this is a placeholder.
* **Questions** (`questions`, text, required/optional): Please provide any relevant information or comments about your experiences or questions related to homework.
* **Question 1: Please describe a challenging experience you had with homework recently?** (`question_1`, text, required/optional): Describe a specific experience you had with homework that was difficult or frustrating.
* **1. Answer to Question 1** (`question_1_answer`, text, required/optional): Respond to the challenging experience you described in Question 1.
* **Question 1 Response** (`question_1_response`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional information or context related to the challenging experience.
* **Question 2: What do you think is the most common reason students struggle with homework?** (`question_2`, number, required/optional): Choose one number from the options provided.
* **2. Response to Question 2** (`question_2_response`, text, required/optional): Explain your answer to Question 2.
* **Question 3: Have you ever used any homework help resources (e.g., online tutorials, study groups)?** (`question_3`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose all that apply from the options provided.
* **3. Response to Question 3** (`question_3_response`, text, required/optional): Describe any resources you have used for homework help.
* **Question 4: Which of the following best describes your attitude towards homework?** (`question_4`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one option from the list.
* **4. Response to Question 4** (`question_4_response`, text, required/optional): Explain your attitude towards homework.
* **Question 5: Do you usually start homework at home or school?** (`question_5`, date, required/optional): If date is selected, provide the date in standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD). Otherwise, enter a time.
* **5. Response to Question 5** (`question_5_response`, text, required/optional): Explain why you usually start homework at home or school.
* **Question 6: How many minutes do you usually spend on homework per day?** (`question_6`, time, required/optional): Enter the time in 24-hour format, e.g., 08:00 for 8:00 am, 14:00 for 2:00 pm.
* **6. Response to Question 6** (`question_6_response`, text, required/optional): Explain why you usually spend that amount of time on homework.
* **Question 7: Which of the following is a barrier to completing homework on time?** (`question_7`, number, required/optional): Choose one or more options from the list.
* **7. Response to Question 7** (`question_7_response`, text, required/optional): Describe any barriers you have to completing homework on time.
* **Question 8: Do you use an email address for homework submission?** (`question_8`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address or a 'No' if you do not.
* **8. Response to Question 8** (`question_8_response`, text, required/optional): Explain why you do or do not use an email address for homework submission.
* **Question 9: Do you have any suggestions for improving homework experiences?** (`question_9`, text, required/optional): Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving homework experiences.
* **10. Response to Question 9** (`question_9_response`, text, required/optional): Explain your suggestions or ideas.
* **Question 10: Do you have any additional comments about homework?** (`question_10`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or information about homework.
* **10. Response to Question 10** (`question_10_response`, text, required/optional): Explain any additional comments or information.
* **Question 11: Have you ever been part of a study group?** (`question_11`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose all that apply from the options provided.
* **11. Response to Question 11** (`question_11_response`, text, required/optional): Describe any study groups you've been part of.
* **Question 12** (`question_12`, not applicable): This field is not applicable, please skip it.
* **No further questions** (`question_13`, not applicable): This field is not applicable, please skip it.

## Tips
* Please be as honest and detailed as possible in your responses.
* Do not repeat or copy responses from other teachers.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them in the space provided at the end of the form.
