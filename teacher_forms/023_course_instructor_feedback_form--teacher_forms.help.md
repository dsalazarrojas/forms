# Course Instructor Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from instructors about their courses and interactions with students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Log in as an instructor with access to the relevant course and student data.
2. Select the instructor ID, student ID(s), and course ID for the feedback you want to provide.
3. Enter the feedback title and provide a detailed feedback description in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instructor ID** (`instructor_id`, select_one, optional): Select your instructor ID to associate your feedback with your account.
* **Student ID(s)** (`student_id`, select_multiple, optional): Select the student ID(s) whose feedback you are providing.
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, select_one, optional): Select the course ID for the feedback you are providing.
* **Feedback Title** (`feedback_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your feedback.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your feedback.

## Tips
* Please provide specific examples or anecdotes to make your feedback more constructive and helpful.
* Keep your feedback concise and focused on one issue per feedback entry.
* If you need to provide feedback on multiple students or courses, select all relevant IDs and provide separate feedback for each.
* Remember to submit your feedback after filling out all required fields, or use the "Cancel" option to discard your changes.
