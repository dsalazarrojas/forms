# Preschool Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from parents and guardians to enroll their child in our preschool program. This form is essential to ensure that we have accurate and up-to-date information about each child and their parents. Please complete this form carefully and thoroughly to help us provide the best possible experience for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out each field with the required information.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Preschool Enrollment Form** (`preschool_enrollment_form`, text, required): This field is the main section of the form where you can enter your child's enrollment information. Please provide a brief description of your child's enrollment details, such as name, date of birth, and any special needs information.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Parent Contact Number** (`parent_contact_number`, text, required): Please provide your contact number so we can reach out to you with important updates and information about your child's preschool experience.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will review your submission and follow up with you to confirm your child's enrollment.
