# Teacher Weekly Plan Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Teacher Weekly Plan Form Template" is designed to help teachers create and document their weekly plans, including planning for teaching, lessons, and class details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Week Planning" field and provide a brief overview of your weekly teaching goals and objectives.
2. In the "Teaching Planning" field, describe your teaching strategies and activities for the week.
3. In the "Lesson Planning" field, detail your lesson plans for each class, including topics and materials.
4. The "Class Planning" field is where you should describe your plans for managing and engaging your students.
5. Finally, provide any relevant class details in the "Class Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Week Planning** (`Week Planning`, text, required/optional): 
    This is the first field in the form where you need to provide a brief overview of your weekly teaching goals and objectives.
* **Teaching Planning** (`Teaching Planning`, text, required/optional): 
    In this field, describe your teaching strategies and activities for the week, such as lesson plans, classroom management, and student engagement.
* **Lesson Planning** (`Lesson Planning`, text, required/optional): 
    Detail your lesson plans for each class, including topics, materials, and any specific activities or assignments.
* **Class Planning** (`Class Planning`, text, required/optional): 
    Describe your plans for managing and engaging your students, including classroom environment, student behavior, and assessment.
* **Class Details** (`Class Details`, text, required/optional): 
    Provide any additional details about your classes, such as class size, student demographics, or special needs students.
