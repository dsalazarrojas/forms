# Pronouns Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pronouns Quiz form is designed to collect your pronouns preferences and other related details. This information will be used for analysis and research purposes to better understand your preferences and help improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answer for each question by clicking on the radio buttons or selecting from the provided options.
2. Enter your email address and phone number correctly.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments if you wish to share more information.
4. Agree to receive updates about the project by selecting the correct option.
5. Finally, submit your response by selecting the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you prefer to be referred to?** (`first_page`, `select_one`, required): This field asks you about your preferred pronouns. Please select the correct option that best represents your preference. This will help us understand how you would like to be addressed.
* **What pronouns do you use to refer to yourself?** (`second_page`, `select_multiple`, required): Here, you are asked to choose the pronouns you use to refer to yourself. Select all the pronouns that apply to you.
* **What time do you usually wake up?** (`time_of_day`, `time`, optional): This field asks about your wake-up time. Please enter your wake-up time in 24-hour format, e.g., 06:00 or 12:00.
* **Do you prefer to be referred to by your name or a neutral pronoun?** (`preferred_pronoun`, `select_one`, required): This question is about your preference for how you are addressed. Please select 'Yes' if you prefer a neutral pronoun or 'No' if you prefer your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address correctly. This will be used to contact you about the project.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter your phone number correctly.
* **Notes** (`note`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes you'd like to share, please enter them here.
* **Do you agree to receive updates about the project?** (`agree_to_updates`, `select_one`, required): Please select 'Yes' if you agree to receive updates or 'No' if you don't.
* **What time do you usually go to bed?** (`bedtime`, `time`, optional): This field asks about your bedtime. Please enter your bedtime in 24-hour format, e.g., 22:00 or 00:00.
* **Do you prefer to use the restroom with which pronoun?** (`restroom_pronoun`, `select_one`, required): This question is about your preference for the restroom pronoun. Please select the correct option.
* **Other notes** (`other_notes`, `note`, optional): If you have any other comments or notes, please enter them here.
* **Do you agree to receive emails?** (`agree_to_emails`, `select_one`, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you agree to receive emails or 'No' if you don't.
