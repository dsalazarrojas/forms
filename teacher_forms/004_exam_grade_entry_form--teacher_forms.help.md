# Exam Grade Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exam Grade Entry Form is a tool for teachers to input student grades and performance data in a structured way. This form is used to track student progress and achievement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Student Name field with the student's name.
2. Enter the Grade field with the student's achieved grade for the exam.
3. Select the Date field to choose the date of the exam.
4. Add any relevant Teacher Comments about the student's performance.
5. Enter the Exam Name field to identify the exam.
6. Select the Subject field to indicate the subject of the exam.
7. Enter the Class field to indicate the class the student belongs to.
8. Enter the Student Identifier field to provide a unique identifier for the student.
9. Optionally, enter the Submitted By field to record who submitted the grade.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter the student's full name.
* **Grade (Number, Optional)**: Enter the grade achieved by the student (e.g., 80, 90, etc.).
* **Date (Date, Optional)**: Select the date of the exam.
* **Teacher Comments (Text, Optional)**: Add any comments about the student's performance.
* **Exam Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the exam.
* **Subject (Text, Optional)**: Select the subject of the exam.
* **Class (Text, Optional)**: Enter the class the student belongs to.
* **Student Identifier (Text, Optional)**: Enter a unique identifier for the student.
* **Submitted By (Text, Optional)**: Enter who submitted the grade.
