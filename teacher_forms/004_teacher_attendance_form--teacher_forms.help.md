# Teacher Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Teacher Attendance Form is designed to monitor and record teachers' attendance during various events like workshops, seminars, and conferences. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field (field 11).
2. Select your attendance status in the "Attendance Status" field (field 9).
3. Indicate your sync app usage in the "Sync Apps" field (field 8).
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (field 10).
5. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field (field 11).
6. Enter any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field (field 12).
7. Leave any notes in the "Notes" field (field 13).
8. If applicable, select the type of workshop or seminar you attended in the "Workshop Type" field (field 14).
9. Enter the duration of the event in the "Duration" field (field 15).
10. Enter the start and end times of the event in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields (fields 16 and 17, respectively).
11. Select your timezone in the "Timezone" field (field 18).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Participation** (`workshop_participation`, text, optional): Describe the teacher's participation in the workshop.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): The date of the event you attended.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the teacher who attended the event.
* **Attendance** (`workshop_attendance`, text, optional): Record the teacher's attendance status, if any specific details need to be mentioned.
* **Seminar Participation** (`seminar_participation`, text, optional): Describe the teacher's participation in the seminar.
* **Conference Participation** (`conference_participation`, text, optional): Describe the teacher's participation in the conference.
* **Other** (`other_participation`, text, optional): Record any other relevant event or activity the teacher participated in.
* **Sync Apps** (`sync_apps`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you use sync apps during the event.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate the teacher's attendance status.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide the teacher's phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments about the event or activity.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Leave any notes or observations about the event or activity.
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of workshop the teacher attended.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the event in hours.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, select_one, optional): Select the teacher's timezone.
