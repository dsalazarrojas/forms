# Late Retake Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Late Retake Exam Form is used to request a late retake for an exam. This form helps you to provide the necessary information for your request, ensuring a smooth and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your request in the "Request Late Retake" field.
2. Provide the reason for your request in the "Reason" field.
3. Indicate if you are taking classes that require a late retake.
4. Choose whether you want to submit your request.
5. Click "Submit Request" to finalize your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Request Late Retake
* **Request Late Retake (text)**: Enter your request for a late retake in this field.

### Reason
* **Reason (text)**: Provide a brief explanation of why you are requesting a late retake.

### Classes
* **Classes (select_multiple)**: If you are taking classes that require a late retake, select "Yes" for all applicable classes.

### Submit Request
* **Submit Request (select_one)**: Choose "Yes" to submit your request for a late retake or "No" to indicate you do not want to submit it.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise reason for your request.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always contact your teacher or academic advisor for guidance.
* This form can be submitted multiple times, but each submission will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.
