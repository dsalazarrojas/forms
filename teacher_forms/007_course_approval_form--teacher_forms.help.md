# course_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The course approval form is used to submit courses for approval by the course administration team. It ensures that courses are properly vetted and validated before being made available to students. Fill out this form to submit your course for review, and a member of the team will review and act on it accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Course Name** field with the name of the course you are submitting.
2. Select the relevant **Category** from the dropdown list.
3. Provide a brief **Description** of the course, if necessary.
4. Choose the **Status** of the course from the dropdown list (Pending Review, In Progress, or Approved).
5. If applicable, provide a specific **Date** and **Time** for the course.
6. Add any additional **Notes** about the course.
7. Enter the **Contact Name** and **Email** of the person submitting the course, if not already provided.
8. Include a phone number for the contact person in the **Phone** field.
9. Attach a **Course File** if required or necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the course you are submitting for approval.
* **Category** (select_one, required): Choose a category for your course from the dropdown list.
* **Description** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the course, if necessary.
* **Status** (select_multiple, required): Select the status of the course (Pending Review, In Progress, or Approved).
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter a specific date for the course, if applicable.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter a specific time for the course, if applicable.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about the course.
* **Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person submitting the course.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter the email of the contact person submitting the course.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person submitting the course.
* **Course File** (text, optional): Attach a file related to the course, if necessary.
