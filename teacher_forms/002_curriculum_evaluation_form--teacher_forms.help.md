# Curriculum Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Evaluation Form is designed to gather essential information from teachers about their students' performance in a course. This form helps teachers assess the progress of their students and provides valuable insights to make informed decisions about course development and student support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the corresponding page for each field based on the relevant section in the form.
2. Enter your answers to each question or section in the designated field.
3. Ensure that you fill out all required fields marked as "required".
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### <page 1: Course Progress>
- **Course Progress** (`1`, text, required: false): Use this field to evaluate the progress of your students in the course, focusing on the content covered, discussions, and activities conducted during the course duration.

### <page 2: Student Engagement>
- **Student Engagement** (`2`, text, required: false): Describe the level of engagement of your students during the course, including their participation in discussions, activities, and other classroom interactions.

### <page 3: Student Motivation>
- **Student Motivation** (`3`, text, required: false): Assess the motivation of your students towards learning, including their interest in course topics and willingness to learn.

### <page 4: Teacher Support>
- **Teacher Support** (`4`, text, required: false): Evaluate the level of support provided by the teacher, including guidance, feedback, and encouragement.

### <page 5: Course Materials>
- **Course Materials** (`5`, text, required: false): Describe the quality and adequacy of course materials, including textbooks, handouts, and online resources used in the course.

### <page 6: Student Strengths>
- **Student Strengths** (`6`, text, required: false): Identify the strengths and skills demonstrated by your students during the course, including problem-solving abilities, creativity, and critical thinking.

### <page 7: Student Weaknesses>
- **Student Weaknesses** (`7`, text, required: false): Identify areas where students need improvement, including weaknesses in understanding concepts, completing tasks, and applying skills.

### <page 8: Course Progress Overall>
- **Course Progress Overall** (`8`, number, required: false): Provide an overall evaluation of student progress in the course, on a scale from 1 to 10.

### <page 9: Course Recommendations>
- **Course Recommendations** (`9`, text, required: false): Make recommendations for improvements or changes to the course, including suggestions for new topics, activities, or resources.

### <page 10: Teacher Comments>
- **Teacher Comments** (`10`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the student's performance, including strengths and weaknesses observed.

## Tips
- Be objective and honest when evaluating student progress.
- Ensure that you are specific and detailed in your comments.
- Review and revise your answers carefully before submission.
- Use this form to support student development and course improvement.
