# School Of Nursing Grading Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Of Nursing Grading Report Form is designed to help teachers evaluate and grade nursing students' academic progress. This form provides a structured framework for teachers to provide feedback on individual assignments and overall performance, helping to identify areas of strength and areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Describe the student's academic progress in the "Student Academic Progress" field.
3. Select the relevant assignments that the student has completed in the "Assignments" field.
4. Choose the grading scale for each assignment in the "Grading Scale" field.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Grading Comments" field.
6. Enter a brief description of the student's overall performance in the "Overall Performance" field.
7. Enter the date the assignment was given to the student in the "Date Assigned" field.
8. Enter the date the assignment was due in the "Date Due" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student being evaluated.
* **Student Academic Progress**: Describe the student's progress in a brief paragraph, focusing on their strengths and areas for improvement.
* **Assignments**: Select all the assignments the student has completed to date. This will help you keep track of their work and provide specific feedback.
* **Grading Scale**: Choose the relevant grading scale for each assignment from the provided options. This scale will help you evaluate the student's work based on their performance.
* **Grading Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback on the student's work. This is an opportunity to offer specific suggestions or encouragement.
* **Overall Performance**: Summarize the student's overall performance, highlighting their strengths and areas for growth.
* **Date Assigned**: Enter the date the assignment was given to the student, helping you keep track of the timeline.
* **Date Due**: Enter the date the assignment was due, ensuring you have a clear understanding of the deadline.
