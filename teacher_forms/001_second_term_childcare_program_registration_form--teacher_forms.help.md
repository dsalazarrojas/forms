# Second Term Childcare Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Second Term Childcare Program Registration Form is used to register a child with the Childcare Program. This form is used to provide additional information about the child and their current teacher, which will be used by program administrators to determine the best course of action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) below.
2. Enter the child's ID number.
3. Enter the teacher's ID number.
4. Enter the child's date of birth.
5. Enter the teacher's name.
6. Enter the contact number for the teacher or caregiver.
7. Enter the child's email address.
8. Enter the child's address.
9. Enter the school name.
10. Enter the child's grade level.
11. Select the teacher's education level.
12. Enter the program name.
13. Enter the program duration.
14. Select the program frequency.
15. Enter the start date of the program.
16. Enter the end date of the program.
17. Select the status of the child's registration.
18. Enter any additional notes about the child.
19. Enter the assigned tool for the child.
20. Enter the person who created the registration.
21. Enter the person who last updated the registration.
22. Enter the date the registration was last updated.
23. (Optional) Enter the person who deleted the registration.
24. (Optional) Enter the date the registration was deleted.
25. (Optional) Enter the reason for deleting the registration.
26. (Optional) Enter any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child ID** (`child_id`, number, required: false): Enter the child's ID number.
* **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`, number, required: false): Enter the teacher's ID number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the teacher's or caregiver's contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the child's email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the child's address.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the child's school name.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, number, required: false): Enter the child's grade level.
* **Teacher Education** (`teacher_education`, select_one, required: false): Select the teacher's education level.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required: false): Enter the program name.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the program duration.
* **Program Frequency** (`program_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the program frequency.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the program start date.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the program end date.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required: false): Select the child's registration status.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the child.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Enter the assigned tool for the child.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, required: false): Enter the person who created the registration.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, required: false): Enter the person who last updated the registration.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, required: false): Enter the date the registration was last updated.
* **Deleted By** (`deleted_by`, text, required: false): (Optional) Enter the person who deleted the registration.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, date, required: false): (Optional) Enter the date the registration was deleted.
* **Deleted Reason** (`deleted_reason`, text, required: false): (Optional) Enter the reason for deleting the registration.
* **Notes 1** (`notes_1`, note, required: false): (Optional) Enter any additional notes.

Note: This form has 25 fields, most of which are optional. Please fill in only the required fields and any additional information as necessary.
