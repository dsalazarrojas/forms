# ielts_essay_writing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help teachers assess and provide feedback on students' IELTS essay writing submissions. The form allows teachers to evaluate the essay title, content, conclusion, and overall score, as well as provide comments and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill in each field according to your evaluation and assessment of the student's essay.
* For each field, provide a clear and concise response to the corresponding question or prompt.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, which are marked as "required".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Essay Title** (`essay_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the student's essay.
* **Write an essay** (`essay_content`, `text`, required): Enter the student's essay content.
* **Essay Conclusion** (`conclusion`, `text`, required): Enter the conclusion of the student's essay.
* **Score** (`essay_score`, `number`, required): Enter a score for the student's essay out of 10.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Provide feedback and suggestions for improvement.
* **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, `number`, required): Enter an overall score for the student's essay out of 10.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required): Enter any suggestions for improvement.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any comments from the teacher.
* **Rubric** (`rubric`, `text`, required): Enter the rubric used to evaluate the essay.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments.
* **Essay Writing Form Completion Status** (`essay_writing_form_completion_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the form completion.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submission Date Time** (`submission_date_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Score Band** (`score_band`, `select_one`, optional): Select the score band for the essay.
* **Score Band Description** (`score_band_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the score band.
* **Overall Score Band** (`overall_score_band`, `select_one`, optional): Select the overall score band.
* **Overall Score Band Description** (`overall_score_band_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the overall score band.
* **Comments from Students** (`comments_from_students`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments from students.
* **Submission Method** (`submission_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select the submission method.
* **Submission Method Description** (`submission_method_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the submission method.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the submission status.
* **Submission Status Description** (`submission_status_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the submission status.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any submission comments.

This form is designed to be completed by teachers to provide feedback and assessment on students' IELTS essay submissions. The form includes fields for entering scores, comments, and suggestions, as well as fields for describing the submission method and status.
