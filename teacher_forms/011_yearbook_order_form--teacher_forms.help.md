# Yearbook Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Yearbook Order Form is designed to collect information from students and teachers to process yearbook orders. This form is intended to gather essential details such as student information, order specifics, payment details, and teacher information, to ensure accurate and efficient yearbook production.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in all the required fields**: While most fields are not marked as "required," the form will not save until all fields are filled. Make sure to complete all fields as you proceed through the form.
2. **Provide accurate information**: Double-check the accuracy of your submissions for student and teacher names, as these will be used for yearbook personalization and contact purposes.
3. **Use clear and concise text for payment details**: Clearly indicate the payment method and amount for accurate processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Student Info**
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, optional): This is the section for students to input their name and other relevant details. Please ensure that the student's name is spelled correctly to avoid any confusion.

### **Order Details**
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): In this section, students can provide any additional information they want to include in the yearbook, such as their favorite quote, hobbies, or interests.

### **Payments**
* **Payments** (`payments`, text, optional): Here, students can specify how they intend to pay for the yearbook (e.g., cash, check, or online transfer).

### **Teacher Info**
* **Teacher Info** (`teacher_info`, text, optional): This section is for teachers to provide their name and contact information for yearbook distribution and any other necessary communications.

## Tips
* Be mindful of the accuracy of your submissions to avoid any confusion or errors in yearbook production.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form's support team (form@gic.mx) for assistance.
* The form is designed to be completed by students and teachers.
