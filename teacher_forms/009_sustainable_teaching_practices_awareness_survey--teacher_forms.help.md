# Sustainable Teaching Practices Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your awareness and implementation of sustainable teaching practices in your classroom or school. The purpose of this form is to:

* Raise awareness of the importance of sustainable teaching practices
* Identify areas where you currently implement sustainable teaching practices
* Identify barriers to adopting sustainable teaching practices
* Gather feedback and suggestions for future adoption of sustainable teaching practices

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand each question and option.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
4. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you can.
5. Make sure to submit the form when you are finished.
