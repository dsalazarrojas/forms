# Field Trip Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Trip Waiver Form is used to capture and store information for field trips, ensuring that all necessary details are recorded and reported accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Trip Details" section with the relevant information for the field trip, including the trip's date and time.
2. Provide the participant's name, age, and gender (if applicable) in the "Participant Information" section.
3. Enter the guardian's or emergency contact's name and phone number (if applicable) in the "Contact Information" section.
4. If necessary, list any medical conditions, allergies, or medications that the participant may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Field Trip Details**: Enter a brief description of the field trip, including the date and time of the trip.
* **Participants Name**: Enter the name of the participant for whom you're filling out this form.
* **Participant Age**: Enter the age of the participant. This field is optional, but it's recommended for accurate reporting.
* **Participant Gender**: Select the participant's gender from the options provided (Male, Female, Other).
* **Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the participant's guardian or emergency contact.
* **Guardian Contact**: Enter the phone number of the participant's guardian or emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the participant, if necessary.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions the participant has.
* **Allergy Medication**: List any allergies or medications the participant takes.
* **First Participant Name**: Enter the name of the first participant (if applicable).
* **Second Participant Name**: Enter the name of the second participant (if applicable).
* **Third Participant Name**: Enter the name of the third participant (if applicable).
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter any assigned tool or equipment used for the field trip (if applicable).
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for the form, if required.
* **Created At**: Display the date and time the form was created.
* **Updated At**: Display the date and time the form was last updated.
* **Created By**: Display the name of the user who created the form.
* **Updated By**: Display the name of the user who last updated the form.
* **Created By Time**: Display the time of day the form was created.
* **Updated By Time**: Display the time of day the form was last updated.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the field trip.

## Tips
- Ensure you're using the correct form ID and filling out the form for the intended purpose.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy, especially for participant information and medical conditions.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to your internal procedures or consult with your supervisor.
