# School Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their overall experience at their school. Your input will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each page carefully to understand the questions and options available.
2.  Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
3.  If you're unsure about any question, ask your teacher or school administrator for guidance.
4.  Submit the completed form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Overall Quality** (Overall Quality, Select multiple, Optional)
  Overall quality of the school in general.
  Please select the options that best fit your experience.

- **Teachers Support** (teacher_support, Text, Optional)
  How do you think the teachers support your learning process?
  Provide a brief written explanation of your thoughts.

- **Facilities And Equipment** (facilities_and_equipment, Number, Optional)
  How would you rate the school's facilities and equipment?
  Use a number (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, 5) to express your opinion.

- **Communication** (communication, Select one, Optional)
  How often do you think the school communicates with you?
  Please select the option that best represents your experience.

- **Feedback** (feedback, Note, Optional)
  Is there anything you'd like to tell us about your experience at school?
  Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.

- **Class Size** (class_size, Number, Optional)
  How would you rate the class size in your school?
  Use a number (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, 5) to express your opinion.

- **Teacher Quality** (teacher_quality, Text, Optional)
  What do you think about the quality of teaching at your school?
  Please provide a brief written explanation of your thoughts.

- **School Safety** (school_safety, Text, Optional)
  How do you rate the school's safety and security measures?
  Provide a brief written explanation of your thoughts.

## Tips
- Please be honest and sincere with your feedback.
- If you have any concerns or issues that you'd like to discuss, feel free to reach out to your teacher or school administrator.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this form, as your input will help us make your school experience even better!
