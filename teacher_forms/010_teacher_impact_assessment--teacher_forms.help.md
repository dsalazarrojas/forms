# Teacher Impact Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Impact Assessment is a form used to evaluate a teacher's performance, identify areas for improvement, and inform decisions related to personnel actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to complete it.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing specific examples and details to support your responses.
3. Ensure you are familiar with the context and goals of the assessment before completing it.
4. Save your answers before submitting the form to avoid losing your work.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **teacher_impact_assessment_1** (text, optional): A brief description of the teacher's area of impact, such as subject area or grade level.
* **teacher_impact_assessment_2** (text, optional): A detailed explanation of the teacher's impact on students, colleagues, or the school.
* **teacher_impact_assessment_3** (number, optional): A numerical assessment of the teacher's impact, e.g., number of students impacted or percentage of increase in student performance.
* **teacher_impact_assessment_4** (number, optional): A numerical assessment of the teacher's effectiveness, e.g., number of successful projects completed or percentage of students meeting standards.

* Be specific and detailed in your answers to provide a thorough assessment.
