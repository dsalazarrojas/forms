# School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Enrollment Form is used to collect information from parents and schools for student enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
2. Enter your parent's email address in the "Parent Email" field.
3. Enter your child's name in the "Child Name" field.
4. Select the child's grade level in the "Grade Level" field.
5. If necessary, select the program's name from the "Program Name" field.
6. Enter the date of enrollment in the "Date" field.
7. Enter the time of enrollment in the "Time" field.
8. Add any comments about the child's enrollment in the "Comments" field.
9. Select whether the student has submitted the form themselves or if someone else submitted it in the "Submitted By" field.
10. Enter the date the form was submitted in the "Submitted On" field.
11. Select whether the child is assigned to a teacher in the "Assigned To" field.
12. Select the status of the student's enrollment in the "Status" field.
13. Select the type of program the student is enrolled in from the "Program Type" field.
14. Select the location of the enrollment in the "Location" field.
15. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
16. Enter the school's name in the "School Name" field.
17. Enter the teacher's email address in the "Teacher Email" field.
18. Enter the teacher's phone number in the "Teacher Phone" field.
19. Enter the teacher's address in the "Teacher Address" field.
20. Enter the school's email address in the "School Email" field.
21. Enter the school's phone number in the "School Phone" field.
22. Enter the school's address in the "School Address" field.
23. Enter a description of the program in the "Program Description" field.
24. Enter the school's website in the "School Website" field.
25. Enter the email address of the school administrator in the "School Admin Email" field.
