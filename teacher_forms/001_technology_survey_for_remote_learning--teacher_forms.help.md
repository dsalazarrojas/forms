# Technology Survey For Remote Learning - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on their experience with remote learning and different technologies used for remote learning.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your abilities, and feel free to skip any questions that do not apply to you.
2. If you have any issues or concerns with the technology, please describe them in the "Can you describe any issues you experienced with the technology?" field.
3. If you have any suggestions or improvements for us, please share them in the "Can you suggest any changes or improvements?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How's your remote learning experience?** (`remote_learning_experience`, `text`, optional): This question is designed to gather your overall experience with remote learning. Please provide a brief description of your experience, including any positive or negative aspects.
* **What technology would you like to see more of?** (`technology_survey`, `select_one`, optional): This question is designed to help us understand what technologies you are interested in seeing more of. Select one of the options provided, or type in your own answer if it's not listed.
* **How do you rate your instructor?** (`survey_questions`, `note`, optional): This question is designed to understand your experience with your instructor. Please provide a brief description of your experience, including any positive or negative aspects.
* **Was remote learning easy to use?** (`survey_questions_2`, `select_one`, optional): This question is designed to understand your experience with the ease of use of the technology. Select 'True' if you found it easy to use, and 'False' otherwise.
* **How likely are you to recommend this technology?** (`survey_questions_3`, `number`, optional): This question is designed to understand your likelihood of recommending the technology to others. Select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 means you are unlikely to recommend, and 10 means you are very likely to recommend.
* **What did you like most about remote learning?** (`survey_questions_4`, `text`, optional): This question is designed to understand what aspects of remote learning you enjoyed the most. Please provide a brief description of your favorite experience.
* **What did you like least about remote learning?** (`survey_questions_5`, `text`, optional): This question is designed to understand what aspects of remote learning you found most challenging or frustrating. Please provide a brief description of your least favorite experience.
* **Would you use this technology again?** (`survey_questions_6`, `select_one`, optional): This question is designed to understand your intention to use the technology again in the future. Select 'True' if you intend to use it again, and 'False' otherwise.
* **How many devices did you use for remote learning?** (`survey_questions_7`, `number`, optional): This question is designed to understand the number of devices you used for remote learning. Please select the number of devices you used.
* **How would you rate the quality of the course content?** (`survey_questions_8`, `number`, optional): This question is designed to understand your experience with the course content. Select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 means poor quality, and 10 means excellent quality.
* **Did you receive technical support when needed?** (`survey_questions_9`, `select_one`, optional): This question is designed to understand whether you received the support you needed during remote learning. Select 'True' if you received the support, and 'False' otherwise.
* **What's the average amount of time you spent on remote learning per day?** (`survey_questions_10`, `time`, optional): This question is designed to understand your daily time commitment to remote learning. Please enter the average time you spent on remote learning per day.
* **How likely are you to recommend this technology to other students?** (`survey_questions_11`, `select_one`, optional): This question is designed to understand your likelihood of recommending the technology to others. Select 'True' if you are likely to recommend, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Have you experienced any issues with the technology?** (`survey_questions_12`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question is designed to understand any technical issues you experienced. Select all the issues that apply to you.
* **Can you describe any issues you experienced with the technology?** (`survey_questions_13`, `text`, optional): This question is designed to provide a detailed description of any technical issues you experienced. Please provide a brief description of the issue, and any steps you took to resolve it.
* **Did you use any other technology for remote learning?** (`survey_questions_14`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question is designed to understand what other technologies you used for remote learning. Select all the technologies you used.
* **Which technology did you use for remote learning?** (`survey_questions_15`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question is designed to understand which technology you used for remote learning. Select all the technologies you used.
* **What other technologies do you think should be used for remote learning?** (`survey_questions_16`, `text`, optional): This question is designed to understand your suggestions for new technologies that could be used for remote learning. Please provide a brief description of your suggestions.
* **Would you like to be contacted by our team?** (`survey_questions_17`, `select_one`, optional): This question is designed to understand whether you would like to be contacted by our team. Select 'True' if you would like to be contacted, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback?** (`survey_questions_18`, `text`, optional): This question is designed to understand any other comments or feedback you have. Please provide any additional thoughts or suggestions you might have.
* **How many students did you teach or learn with?** (`survey_questions_19`, `number`, optional): This question is designed to understand the number of students you taught or learned with. Please select the number of students.
* **Have you ever used this technology for remote learning before?** (`survey_questions_20`, `select_one`, optional): This question is designed to understand whether you have used the technology before. Select 'True' if you have used it before, and 'False' otherwise.
* **How many remote learning opportunities have you participated in?** (`survey_questions_21`, `number`, optional): This question is designed to understand the number of remote learning opportunities you participated in. Please select the number of opportunities.
* **What do you think about using this technology for remote learning?** (`survey_questions_22`, `text`, optional): This question is designed to understand your overall impression of the technology. Please provide a brief description of your thoughts.
* **Can you suggest any changes or improvements?** (`survey_questions_23`, `text`, optional): This question is designed to understand any changes or improvements you think would be beneficial. Please provide a brief description of your suggestions.

Note: Some fields may not be applicable to your experience, and it's okay to leave them blank or select 'N/A' if necessary.
