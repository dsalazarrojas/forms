# Student Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Grant Application Form is designed to collect information from students to support their grant applications. This form aims to gather relevant details about the students, their goals, and their financial requirements to ensure that the grant committee has an accurate and comprehensive understanding of each applicant's needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date details for each field.
3. Make sure to fill in all fields, as marked as required.
4. Review your application before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Grant Aim** (`grant_aim`, text, Optional): Describe the purpose and goals of the grant you are applying for. This is an opportunity to explain why you need the grant and how it will benefit you.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, Optional): Enter your current address where you can be contacted or send mail.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Grant Amount** (`grant_amount`, number, Optional): Enter the total amount of grant you are applying for.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* Use a clear and concise format for your grant aim to ensure it is well understood.
* If you are unable to complete the form, contact us for assistance.
* We will review your application once it is submitted.
