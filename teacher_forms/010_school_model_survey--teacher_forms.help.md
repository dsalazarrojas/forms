# School Model Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Model Survey is designed to collect information from teachers about their current school model. This information will be used to identify areas of strength and weakness and help improve the school model.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select All That Apply** (school_model_survey, select_multiple, required: false): This section allows you to choose the options that best describe your school model. Please select all the options that apply to your school.
2. **Parent Preferences** (school_model_survey_2, text, required: false): In this section, please provide a short description of your parent preferences. This will help us understand your thoughts and opinions about the school model.
3. **Parent Preferences 2** (school_model_survey_3, select_one, required: false): Please select 'Yes' if you support the current school model.
4. **Parent Preferences 3** (school_model_survey_4, number, required: false): This field is not clearly labeled, so we will assume it's a numerical value representing your school model score. Please enter a number between 1 and 10 to represent your school model score.
5. **Parent Preferences 4** (school_model_survey_5, date, required: false): This field is likely for a specific date or deadline. Please enter a date in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
6. **Parent Preferences 5** (school_model_survey_6, time, required: false): This field is likely for a specific time of day. Please enter a time in the 12-hour format (hh:mm AM/PM).
7. **Parent Preferences 6** (school_model_survey_7, email, required: false): This field is likely for an email address. Please enter a valid email address.
8. **Parent Preferences 7** (school_model_survey_8, select_one, required: false): Please select 'Yes' if you support the current school model.
9. **Parent Preferences 8** (school_model_survey_9, text, required: false): In this section, please provide a short description of your additional preferences.
10. **Parent Preferences 9** (school_model_survey_10, text, required: false): In this section, please provide a short description of your other preferences.
