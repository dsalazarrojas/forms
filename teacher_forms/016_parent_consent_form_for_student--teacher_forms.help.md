# Parent consent form for student - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for student activities, programs, or events. It ensures parental involvement and approval for the student to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the student's name in the `Student Name` field.
- Enter your name in the `Parent Name` field.
- Select the event or activity category in the `Category` field. You can choose 'Yes' or 'No' based on your approval.
- Enter the date and time of the event in the `Date` and `Time` fields respectively.
- Enter any notes or comments related to the event in the `Note` field.
- Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
- Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
- Select your consent in the `Parent Consent` field. You can choose 'Yes' or 'No' based on your approval.
- Fill in the form ID number in the `Form ID` field.
- Enter the name of the tool assigned to the student in the `Assigned Tool` field.
- Enter the category ID for the form in the `Category ID` field.
- Enter the name of the output file related to the form in the `Output File` field.
- Enter the version of the form in the `Version` field.
- Enter the name of the person who created the form in the `Created By` field.
- Enter the date and time the form was created in the `Created At` and `Updated At` fields respectively.
- Enter the name of the person who last updated the form in the `Updated By` field.
- Enter the category of the form in the `Form Category` field. You can choose 'Yes' or 'No' based on your approval.
- Enter the name of the tool assigned to the student in the `Assigned Tool 2` field.
- Enter the category ID for the form in the `Category Id 2` field.

* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Form ID** (`Form ID`, `number`, required): Enter the form ID number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the tool assigned to the student.
* **Category ID** (`Category ID`, `number`, required): Enter the category ID for the form.
* **Output File** (`Output File`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the output file related to the form.
* **Version** (`Version`, `text`, required): Enter the version of the form.
* **Created By** (`Created By`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Updated At** (`Updated At`, `date`, required): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Updated By** (`Updated By`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who last updated the form.
* **Form Category** (`Form Category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category for the form. You can choose 'Yes' or 'No' based on your approval.
