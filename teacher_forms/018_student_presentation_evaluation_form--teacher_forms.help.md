# Student Presentation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide instructors with a structured way to evaluate a student's presentation. It assesses the student's performance in several areas, including content, delivery, and overall impression.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the presentation and fill out this form.
2. For each field, provide your assessment based on the presentation you reviewed.
3. Be specific in your feedback, providing examples or anecdotes that demonstrate your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Slide Number**: Enter the slide number of the presentation.
* **Title**: Enter a brief title of the presentation.
* **Speaker**: Enter the name of the person who presented.
* **Body**: Summarize the content of the presentation in a few sentences.
* **Overall Score**: Evaluate the presentation as a whole, using a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback on the presentation.
* **Presentation Skills**: Select all the skills that the speaker demonstrated:
	+ Effective
	+ Well-organized
	+ Clear
	+ Good
	+ Not good
* **Communication Skills**: Select all the skills that the speaker demonstrated:
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
	+ Fair
	+ Needs improvement
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions for the speaker.
* **Presentation Materials**: Select all the materials that were used effectively:
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
	+ Fair
	+ Not good
* **Overall Impression**: Select all the impressions you have of the presentation:
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
	+ Fair
	+ Not good
* **Area for Improvement**: Provide any suggestions or areas for improvement for the speaker.
* **Comments for Improvement**: Provide any additional comments or feedback on the areas for improvement.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Provide any suggestions or advice for the speaker to improve their presentation skills.
