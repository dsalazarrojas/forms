# Student Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Enrollment Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect information from students enrolling in a course or program. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page carefully, and make sure to enter accurate information.
2. Please enter your first name in the "First Name" field (page 1).
3. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field (page 2).
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field (page 3).
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (page 4).
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field (page 5).
7. If applicable, enter the fee you are paying in the "Fee" field (page 6).
8. Select your preferred payment method in the "Payment Gateway" field (page 7).
9. If you have any additional fees, enter them in the "Additional Fees" field (page 8).
10. Enter the course name, if applicable, in the "Course Name" field (page 9).
11. Enter the start and end dates of the course in the "Course Start Date" and "Course End Date" fields (pages 10-11).
12. Enter the name of your teacher, if applicable, in the "Teacher Name" field (page 12).
13. Enter your student ID in the "Student ID" field (page 13).
14. Upload your student image (optional) in the "Student Image" field (page 14).
15. Enter the date of your enrollment in the "Student Enrollment Date" field (page 15).
16. Select your payment status in the "Payment Status" field (page 16).
17. Select your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" field (page 17).
18. Enter any additional notes, if applicable, in the "Notes" field (page 18).
19. Select your enrollment status in the "Enrollment Status" field (page 19).
20. Select your gender in the "Student Gender" field (page 20).
21. Enter your age in the "Age" field (page 21).
22. Enter your grade level in the "Grade" field (page 22).
23. Enter the course ID, if applicable, in the "Course ID" field (page 23).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (page 1): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (page 2): Enter your valid email address.
* **Contact Number** (page 3): Enter your contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (page 4): Enter your date of birth.
* **Address** (page 5): Enter your address.
* **Fee** (page 6): Enter the fee you are paying.
* **Payment Gateway** (page 7): Select your preferred payment method (PayPal, Stripe, or Bank Transfer).
* **Additional Fees** (page 8): If applicable, enter any additional fees.
* **Additional Fees 2** (page 9): If applicable, enter any additional fees.
* **Course Name** (page 10): Enter the name of the course you are enrolling in.
* **Course Start Date** (page 11): Enter the start date of the course.
* **Course End Date** (page 12): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Teacher Name** (page 13): Enter the name of your teacher.
* **Student ID** (page 14): Enter your student ID.
* **Student Image** (page 15): Upload your student image (optional).
* **Student Enrollment Date** (page 16): Enter the date of your enrollment.
* **Payment Status** (page 17): Select your payment status (Paid, Pending, or Unpaid).
* **Payment Method** (page 18): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
* **Notes** (page 19): Enter any additional notes.
* **Notes 2** (page 20): Enter any additional notes.
* **Enrollment Status** (page 21): Select your enrollment status (Enrolled, Not Enrolled, or Pending).
* **Student Gender** (page 22): Select your gender (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Age** (page 23): Enter your age.
* **Grade** (page 24): Enter your grade level.
* **Course ID** (page 25): Enter the course ID (optional).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out each page accurately and completely.
* If you have any doubts or issues while filling out the form, contact your school administrator or support team.
