# Parent Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from parents, allowing schools to communicate with them effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
2. Enter your parent's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Add your parent's phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Provide your parent's address in the "Address" field (optional).
5. Share any additional comments or notes about your parent's contact information in the "Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Fill in your parent's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your parent's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Add your parent's phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide your parent's home or work address.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or notes about your parent's contact information.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure efficient communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact the school administration.
* You can leave the "Address" field blank if not applicable or if you're unsure about what to enter.
