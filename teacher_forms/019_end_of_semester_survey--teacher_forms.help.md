# end_of_semester_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The end_of_semester_survey form is designed to gather feedback from teachers on the quality and effectiveness of the course content, instructor performance, and overall student experience at the end of each semester. This data helps the institution to identify areas of improvement and provide better services to teachers and students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the survey.
2. Answer each question in the provided fields, making sure to fill out all required fields.
3. Choose the correct institution from the dropdown list.
4. Provide your email address and phone number if you wish to receive a copy of your survey results or have other inquiries.
5. Submit the form to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which semester are you referring to** (`semester`, `text`, required): This field asks you to specify the semester you are providing feedback on.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the course you are teaching.
* **Course Description** (`course_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the course, if desired.
* **Student feedback on course content** (`student_feedback`, `text`, required): Share your insights on how well the course content met the needs of students.
* **Overall experience in the course** (`overall_experience`, `text`, required): Evaluate your overall experience in the course, including any areas of improvement you think are necessary.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you wish to receive a copy of your survey results or have other inquiries, enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you prefer to be contacted over the phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Selected institution** (`selected_institution`, `select_one`, required): Choose the institution you are affiliated with.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you have for improvement.
* **Comments on institution** (`comments_institution`, `text`, optional): Provide feedback on your institution experience.
