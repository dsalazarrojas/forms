# science_simulation_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The science simulation feedback form is designed to collect feedback from students and teachers on science simulations or experiments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Info section with your contact information.
2. Provide information about the student who completed the simulation, including their name, class, and grade level.
3. Enter your teacher name and any necessary comments.
4. Rate the simulation and provide additional comments.
5. Set the submission status and provide any necessary notes.
6. Identify who submitted the form and who it was submitted to.
7. Date and time of submission.
8. If applicable, select the submission status and provide notes on the reason.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's name, class, and grade level.
* **Teacher Info** (`teacher_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and any necessary comments.
* **Science Simulation Feedback** (`science_simulation_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Rate the simulation and provide additional comments.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments.
* **Experiment Name** (`experiment_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the science simulation or experiment.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your teacher name.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's name.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `number`, required: false): Enter the student's grade level.
* **Student Class** (`student_class`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's class.
* **Student Group** (`student_group`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student group.
* **Teacher Rating** (`teacher_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your performance as a teacher.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments from the teacher's perspective.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate the simulation or experiment.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Set the submission status to Approved, Not Approved, etc.
* **Submission Status Note** (`submission_status_note`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Provide notes on the reason for the submission status.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required: false): Identify who submitted the form (Student, Teacher, Other).
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, `select_one`, required: false): Identify who the form was submitted to (Class Teacher, Department Head, School Admin).
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitted On Time** (`submitted_on_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of submission.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and honest feedback to help improve the science simulations or experiments.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can contact the form administrator for clarification.
* You can submit the form as many times as necessary, but please avoid duplication.
* The form is designed to collect feedback for educational purposes only.
