# Open Science Educator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for Open Science Educators to apply for a program or opportunity. It collects contact information and academic qualifications for internal review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Email, and Phone (if applicable) on the first page.
2. Select your University from the list on the second page.
3. Enter your current Position in the department or institution.
4. Provide a brief description of your Qualifications.
5. Select your highest Education level (B.S., B.A., Ph.D., M.S.).
6. Choose your level of Experience (Less than 1 year, 1-5 years, 5-10 years, More than 10 years).
7. Select your Department (Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics, Physics).
8. Choose your Role (Teacher, Researcher, Professor, Lecturer).
9. Select your Institution (University of California, Berkeley, University of Oxford, MIT, Harvard University).
10. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **University** (`university`, select_multiple, optional): Select your university from the list.
* **Position** (`position`, text, optional): Enter your current position in the department or institution.
* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your qualifications.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Leave this field blank, as it's intended for internal review.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click on this button to submit your application.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Leave this field blank, it's not intended for end users.
* **Education** (`education`, select_multiple, optional): Select your highest education level (B.S., B.A., Ph.D., M.S.).
* **Experience** (`experience`, select_one, optional): Choose your level of experience (Less than 1 year, 1-5 years, 5-10 years, More than 10 years).
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select your department from the list.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your role (Teacher, Researcher, Professor, Lecturer).
* **Institution** (`institution`, select_multiple, optional): Select your institution from the list.

Note: This form is for internal use only, so please be concise and honest in your answers. Your application will be reviewed internally, and we will get back to you with the results.
