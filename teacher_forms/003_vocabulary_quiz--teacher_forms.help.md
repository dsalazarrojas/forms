# Vocabulary Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vocabulary Quiz form is a collection of fields designed to test students' vocabulary knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Vocabulary Quiz form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your student name in the **Student Name** field.
2. Optionally, provide your email address and phone number in their respective fields.
3. Select your quiz results from the available options in the **Quiz Results** field.
4. For the **Select One Answer** field, choose either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your answer.
5. For the **Select Multiple Answers** field, select all the answers that apply from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Quiz Questions**: This is not a field for user input, but rather a label indicating that this form section is about quiz questions.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, which will likely be used to identify you in the quiz results.
- **Score** (`score`, number, optional): If you have a score, you can enter it here. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.
- **Quiz Results** (`quiz_results`, note, optional): Describe your quiz results, which will help instructors understand your performance.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address to allow instructors to contact you about your quiz results.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which will be used to contact you about your quiz results.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time when you took the quiz.
- **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth, which will help instructors understand your demographic characteristics.
- **Select One Answer** (`select_one_answer`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to answer the question.
- **Select Multiple Answers** (`select_multiple_answers`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the answers that apply from the available options.
  - Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each question.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure accurate quiz results.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
