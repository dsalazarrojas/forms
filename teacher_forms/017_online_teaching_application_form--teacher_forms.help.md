# Online Teaching Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from teachers who wish to apply for a teaching position. It will help us understand their qualifications, experience, and background.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose your preferred course from the options provided in the "Course Choice" field.
2. If you have any relevant certifications, please enter them in the "Certifications" field. This could include any teaching-related certifications, such as TESOL or TEFL.
3. In the "CV" field, please upload your Curriculum Vitae or Resume, detailing your educational background, work experience, and any other relevant information.
4. You may upload any additional documents that support your application, such as letters of recommendation or references, in the "Upload Additional Document" field.
5. Please enter your name and contact information in the "Name", "Email", and "Phone" fields.
6. Finally, provide a brief description of the course(s) you are interested in teaching and your qualifications for teaching in the "Course Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Course Choice (Select One)
* **Course Choice**: Select the course(s) you are interested in teaching.

    - This field allows you to choose which course(s) you would like to teach.
    - Please select only one option from the provided list.

### Certifications (Text)
* **Certifications**: Enter any relevant teaching certifications you hold.

    - This field is for entering any relevant teaching certifications you may have.
    - Examples include TESOL or TEFL certifications.

### CV (Text)
* **CV**: Upload your Curriculum Vitae or Resume.

    - This field is for uploading your CV or Resume, which should include your educational background, work experience, and any other relevant information.
    - Please make sure to upload a clear and readable PDF or Word document.

### Upload Additional Document (Text)
* **Upload Additional Document**: Upload any additional documents that support your application.

    - This field is for uploading any additional documents that support your application, such as letters of recommendation or references.
    - Please make sure to upload a clear and readable PDF or Word document.

### Name (Text)
* **Name**: Enter your full name.

    - This field is for entering your full name.
    - Please make sure to enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email (Email)
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address.

    - This field is for entering your professional email address.
    - Please make sure to enter a valid email address that you check regularly.

### Phone (Text)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.

    - This field is for entering your phone number.
    - Please make sure to enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.

### Course Details (Text)
* **Course Details**: Provide a brief description of the course(s) you are interested in teaching and your qualifications for teaching.

    - This field is for providing a brief description of the course(s) you are interested in teaching and why you are qualified to teach it.
    - Please make sure to be clear and concise in your description.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your application and look forward to reviewing it.
