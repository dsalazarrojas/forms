# Special Needs Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's special needs, particularly in a medical setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the medical professional who will be handling the individual's case (Medical Professional: Select One).
2. Identify the individual's patient status (Patient Status: Select One).
3. If applicable, specify the individual's disability (Patient Disability: Select Multiple).
4. Describe the recovery process for the individual (Recovery Process: Text).
5. List any special needs the individual may have (Special Needs: Select Multiple).
6. Provide any additional needs the individual may have (Additional Needs: Text).
7. Specify any accommodations that need to be made for the individual (Accommodations: Date).
8. Provide the individual's medical history (Medical History: Text).
9. Specify the treatment plan for the individual (Treatment Plan: Select Multiple).
10. Note the duration of any medical procedures (Medical Procedure: Time).
11. Note the date planned for discharge (Discharge Planned: Date).
12. Note the date for follow-up appointments (Follow Up Date: Date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Professional**: Select the medical professional who will be handling the individual's case. Choose from Medical Doctor, Nurse, or Other.
* **Patient Disability**: Identify the individual's disability, if any. Select all that apply from Physical Disability, Mental Disability, or Other Disability.
* **Recovery Process**: Describe the recovery process for the individual, e.g., "Physical therapy, Occupational therapy, etc."
* **Special Needs**: Specify any special needs the individual may have, e.g., Wheelchair Access, Hearing Assistance, Vision Assistance, Communication Assistance.
* **Additional Needs**: Provide any additional needs the individual may have.
* **Accommodations**: Note the date when accommodations need to be made for the individual.
* **Medical History**: Provide the individual's medical history.
* **Patient Status**: Identify the individual's patient status, e.g., Stable, Unstable, Critical.
* **Treatment Plan**: Specify the treatment plan for the individual, e.g., Physical Therapy, Occupational Therapy, Speech Therapy, Other Therapy.
* **Medical Procedure**: Note the duration of any medical procedures.
* **Discharge Planned**: Note the date planned for discharge.
* **Follow Up Date**: Note the date for follow-up appointments.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If unsure about any field, please consult the medical professional handling the individual's case.
* This form is designed to collect data for medical purposes only.
