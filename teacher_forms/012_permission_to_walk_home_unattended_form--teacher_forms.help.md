# Permission To Walk Home Unattended Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to grant permission to students to walk home unattended from school. It is intended for use by school administrators, teachers, or other authorized personnel to document the permission given to students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of school event or activity that the student is participating in.
2. Enter the student's name as it appears on their school records.
3. Enter the parent's name as it appears on their school records.
4. Select the student's grade level.
5. Obtain the parent's signature as confirmation of their agreement.
6. Enter the date and time when the student is authorized to leave school unattended.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Walk Home from School Event** (`school_event`, `select_one`, Required):
	+ Select the type of school event or activity that the student is participating in. This could be a school event, after-school club, field trip, school play, or school dance.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, Required):
	+ Enter the student's name as it appears on their school records.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, Required):
	+ Enter the parent's name as it appears on their school records.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `select_one`, Required):
	+ Select the student's grade level.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, `text`, Required):
	+ Obtain the parent's signature as confirmation of their agreement.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, Required):
	+ Enter the date when the student is authorized to leave school unattended.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, Required):
	+ Enter the time when the student is authorized to leave school unattended.

## Tips
* Make sure to obtain a valid parent signature before granting permission for a student to walk home unattended.
* Double-check the student's grade level and parent's name to ensure accuracy.
* Keep a record of completed forms for future reference.
* Review and update this form as needed to reflect changing school policies or events.
