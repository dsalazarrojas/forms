# School Audition Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from teachers to apply for a school audition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the fields "First Name", "Last Name", "Email", and "Contact Number".
2. Enter your school's details in the "School Name" field.
3. Indicate your relationship with the school in the "Teacher Name" field.
4. Select the type of audition you are applying for in the "Audition Type" field.
5. If applicable, provide the date and time of the audition in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
6. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field.
7. Confirm that you have submitted the form by selecting "True" in the "Submitted" field.
8. Finally, enter any additional information or references in the "School Audition ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (email): Enter your professional email address.
* **Contact Number** (text): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **School Name** (text): Enter the name of the school you are affiliated with.
* **Teacher Name** (text): Enter your official name as it appears on your identification.
* **School Year** (number): Enter the current school year you are working on (e.g., 2023-2024).
* **Audition Type** (select_one): Select the type of audition you are applying for (Singing, Dancing, Acting).
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of the audition (if known).
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of the audition (if known).
* **Note** (note): Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Submitted** (select_one): Confirm that you have submitted the form by selecting "True".
* **School Audition ID** (text): Enter any additional information or references about the audition.
* **Uploaded By** (text): Enter the name of the person uploading the form (e.g., "John Doe, Teacher").
