# teacher_extra_resource_organization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "teacher_extra_resource_organization_form" is a tool for teachers to collect and organize extra resources related to their teaching activities. It helps standardize and streamline the process of creating, managing, and storing resources, making it easier for teachers to access and utilize them effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the available options for each field carefully.
2. Fill out the form with the relevant information for your extra resource.
3. Ensure that you complete all required fields (Classroom Resources and Resource Type).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Extra Resource**: This is the title of the extra resource you are adding. Enter a brief and descriptive name for your resource.
* **Classroom Resources**: Indicate whether the extra resource is related to classroom activities or not.
* **Resource Name**: Enter the name of the extra resource (e.g., a book, video, or activity).
* **Resource URL**: Enter the URL or location where the resource can be found (e.g., website, DOI, or location).
* **Resource Description**: Describe the extra resource in a few sentences, including its relevance and purpose.
* **Resource Date**: Enter the date when the extra resource was created or updated.
* **Resource Time**: Enter the time spent on the extra resource.
* **Resource Type**: Select the type of extra resource (e.g., text, image, video, etc.).
* **Resource Grade Level**: Select the grade level of the extra resource (e.g., High, Low, etc.).
* **Resource Subjects**: Select the subjects related to the extra resource (e.g., Science, Math, etc.).
