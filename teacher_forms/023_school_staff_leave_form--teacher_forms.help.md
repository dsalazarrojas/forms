# School Staff Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Staff Leave Form is designed to help staff members report their leave details, including leave dates, reasons, and frequency. This form will help the management or HR team to track and process leave requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose whether you are a staff member or not.
2. Select the start and end dates of your leave period.
3. Enter the start and end times of your leave period.
4. Select the type of leave you are taking (yes/no).
5. Enter the reason for your leave start and end (if applicable).
6. Specify the duration of your leave.
7. Select whether you take leave frequently or not.
8. Enter your name.
9. Enter your email address and phone number.
10. Add any additional comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Member** (`staff_member`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether you are a staff member or not.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your leave period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your leave period.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of your leave period.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of your leave period.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of leave you are taking (yes/no).
* **Start Reason** (`start_reason`, text, required): Enter the reason for your leave start.
* **End Reason** (`end_reason`, text, required): Enter the reason for your leave end.
* **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`, number, required): Specify the duration of your leave.
* **Leave Frequency** (`leave_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you take leave frequently or not.
* **Name** (`staff_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Add any additional comments about your leave.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the assigned tool (likely a note or note-taking tool).
