# Class Placement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a student's academic and personal details for the purpose of making informed class placement decisions. The data collected will help teachers and administrators determine the best class placement for the student to ensure they receive the right level of academic challenge and support.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form in the following sections, starting from student information to class placement recommendation.
*   Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
*   Provide as much detail as possible in each field to support the class placement decision.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all sections are complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, **required**): Enter the student's name as it appears on their official documents.
*   **Student ID Number** (`Student ID Number`, text, **required**): Enter the student's ID number as per school records.
*   **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, **required**): Enter the student's date of birth.
*   **Current Grade Level** (`Current Grade Level`, text, **required**): Describe the student's current grade level in the curriculum.
*   **Student Email** (`Student Email`, email, **optional**): Enter the student's email address if available.
*   **Student Phone** (`Student Phone`, text, **optional**): Enter the student's phone number if available.
*   **Parent/Guardian Name** (`Parent/Guardian Name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
*   **Parent Contact Number** (`Parent Contact Number`, text, **required**): Enter the parent or guardian's contact number.
*   **Reading Level Assessment** (`Reading Level Assessment`, select_one, **required**): Choose the reading level assessment from the options below grade level, at grade level, above grade level, advanced, or needs assessment.
*   **Math Level Assessment** (`Math Level Assessment`, select_one, **required**): Choose the math level assessment from the options below grade level, at grade level, above grade level, advanced, or needs assessment.
*   **Recent Standardized Test Scores** (`Recent Standardized Test Scores`, text, **optional**): Enter any recent standardized test scores if available.
*   **Current GPA/Grade Average** (`Current GPA/Grade Average`, number, **optional**): Enter the student's current GPA or grade average if available.
*   **Academic Strengths** (`Academic Strengths`, text, **required**): Describe the student's academic strengths.
*   **Academic Challenges or Areas for Improvement** (`Academic Challenges or Areas for Improvement`, text, **required**): Describe any academic challenges or areas for improvement for the student.
*   **Learning Style Preference** (`Learning Style Preference`, text, **optional**): Describe the student's learning style preference (e.g., visual, auditory, kinesthetic).
*   **Special Education** (`Special Education`, select_one, **required**): Choose if the student is currently in a special education program true, false, or under evaluation.
*   **Has IEP (Individualized Education Plan)** (`Has IEP (Individualized Education Plan)`, select_one, **optional**): Choose if the student has an IEP true, false, or in development.
*   **Required Accommodations or Modifications** (`Required Accommodations or Modifications`, text, **optional**): Describe any required accommodations or modifications for the student.
*   **Recommended Class Placement** (`Recommended Class Placement`, select_one, **required**): Choose the recommended class placement from the options accelerated or advanced class, regular curriculum, below grade level support, special education class, combination approach, under review.
*   **Justification for Placement Recommendation** (`Justification for Placement Recommendation`, text, **required**): Describe the reason for the recommended class placement.
*   **Additional Services or Support Needed** (`Additional Services or Support Needed`, text, **optional**): Describe any additional services or support needed for the student.
*   **Teacher Notes and Observations** (`Teacher Notes and Observations`, text, **optional**): Enter any notes or observations from the teacher.

Note: The fields with **required** labels are mandatory and must be filled out for the form to be considered complete.
