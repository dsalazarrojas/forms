# Teacher Of The Year Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Teacher Of The Year" nomination form is designed to collect student nominations for a teacher who has made a positive impact on their educational experience. This form is intended to be filled out by students who wish to acknowledge the dedication and excellence of their teachers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Nomination Form** section with your personal message or reason for nominating this teacher.
2.  Enter the **Teacher's Name**, **School Name**, and **Teacher ID** in the corresponding fields.
3.  Describe in detail why you are nominating this teacher for the award in the **Reason Nominating Teacher** field.
4.  Enter your **Student Name** and **Student Signature** to authenticate the nomination.
5.  Enter the **Teacher's Contact Information** so that we can reach them with the news of their nomination.
6.  Record the **Nomination Date** when you are nominating this teacher.
7.  Finally, have your teacher sign the **Teacher Signature** to confirm their agreement with the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Nomination Form**: A text field where you can type in your reason for nominating the teacher.
*   **Nomination Form - Teacher**: Another text field where you can provide more details on why this teacher is worthy of the award.
*   **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher you are nominating.
*   **School Name**: Enter the name of the school where this teacher works.
*   **Student Name**: Enter your own name.
*   **Reason Nominating Teacher**: Provide more details on why you're nominating this teacher.
*   **Teacher ID**: Enter the teacher's ID number.
*   **Teacher Contact**: Enter the teacher's contact information.
*   **Nomination Date**: Enter the date of the nomination.
*   **Student Signature**: Sign your name to authenticate the nomination.
*   **Teacher Signature**: Have the teacher sign their name to confirm their agreement with the nomination.
*   **School Admin Signature**: Have the school administrator sign their name to verify the nomination.
*   **School Admin Name**: Enter the name of the school administrator.
