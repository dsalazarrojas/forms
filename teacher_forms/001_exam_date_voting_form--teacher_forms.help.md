# Exam Date Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exam Date Voting Form is a tool for students to vote on exam dates, helping instructors to plan and schedule exams that accommodate the majority of students' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the page for "Exam Date Voting Form" and choose the answer "Yes" or "No" depending on your availability.
2. On the next page, select the date of the next exam if you will be available.
3. On the last page, you can enter any additional comments or feedback related to your availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exam Date Voting Form** (`exam_date_voting_form_page`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" to indicate you are available for the exam on the given date or "No" if you are not. This helps the instructor plan accordingly.
* **Date of Next Exam** (`exam_date_voting_form_page_2`, date, required): Select the date you are available for the exam. This field is only required if you selected "Yes" in the previous field.
* **Student Response** (`exam_date_voting_form_page_3`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your availability or any special considerations for the exam schedule.
