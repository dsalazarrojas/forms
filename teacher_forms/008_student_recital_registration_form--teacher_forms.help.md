# student_recital_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow students to register for and provide details about their recital performances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are submitting as a student.
2. Fill out all required fields with accurate information.
3. Provide all necessary event details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents (e.g., ID, passport, etc.). This will be used to identify you in the registration process.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents (e.g., ID, passport, etc.). This will be used to identify you in the registration process.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for communication purposes and to send you notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number. This will be used for communication purposes and to schedule callbacks.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your event (e.g., recital, performance, etc.).
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your event (e.g., recital, performance, etc.).
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your event.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your teacher's name.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as a student performing in the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required/optional): Enter a name for your event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe your event (e.g., recital, performance, etc.).

## Tips

* Double-check all entered information for accuracy.
* Ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your event organizers or teachers.
