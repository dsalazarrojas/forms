# Marching Band Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marching Band Registration Form is used to collect information from students and staff to register students for the marching band program. This form is designed to be completed by teachers and staff members to provide accurate and efficient student registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's First Name.
2. Fill in the student's Last Name.
3. Enter the student's Email address.
4. Enter the student's Phone number.
5. Fill in the Parent Name (if applicable).
6. Select the student's Instrument.
7. Fill in the Grade level.
8. Select the student's Section.
9. Fill in any additional details about the Section (optional).
10. Fill in any additional details about the student's Section (optional).
11. Enter the Emergency Contact's Name (if applicable).
12. Enter the Emergency Contact's Phone number (if applicable).
13. Specify the relationship of the Emergency Contact (if applicable).
14. Fill in the Student ID number.
15. Fill in the Student Grade level.
16. Select the Student Section status (yes or no).
17. Fill in the Student Grade Level (if not automatically populated).
18. Enter the Student's Instrument (if not automatically populated).
19. Fill in the Student's Parent Name (if not automatically populated).
20. Fill in the Teacher's Name.
21. Enter the Teacher's Email address.
22. Enter the Teacher's Phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter the student's last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Parent Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the parent's name if applicable.
* **Instrument** (Text, Optional): Enter the student's instrument.
* **Grade** (Number, Required): Enter the student's grade level.
* **Section** (Select One, Required): Select the student's section.
* **Section Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional details about the section.
* **Section Details 2** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional details about the student's section.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text, Optional): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Relationship** (Text, Optional): Specify the relationship of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Student ID** (Text, Required): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Student Grade** (Text, Required): Enter the student's grade level.
* **Student Section** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the student's section status.
* **Student Grade Level** (Text, Optional): Enter the student's grade level (if not automatically populated).
* **Student Instrument** (Text, Optional): Enter the student's instrument.
* **Student Parent** (Text, Optional): Enter the student's parent's name.
* **Student Parent Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the parent's name.
* **Teacher** (Text, Required): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Email** (Email, Required): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Submit** (Text, Not Required): Click to submit the form.
