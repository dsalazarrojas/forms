# excused_absence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an excused absence for a student, providing details about the student and reason for the absence.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about the student and reason for absence.
2.  Make sure to include the student's email address in the Teacher Email field, as it's required for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Student Name (Full student name)**:
    *   Enter the student's full name, as it appears on their school records.
*   **Date**:
    *   Enter the date of the absence, using the format: `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Reason**:
    *   Enter a concise description of the reason for the absence, without including any confidential or sensitive information.
*   **Parent**:
    *   Enter the parent's name, as it appears on their contact information.
*   **Student Email**:
    *   Enter the student's email address, used for communication purposes.
*   **Teacher Email**:
    *   Enter your email address, as it's required for communication with parents and staff.
*   **School**:
    *   Enter the name of the school where the student is enrolled.
*   **Start Time**:
    *   Enter the start time of the absence, using the 24-hour format (e.g., `10:00`).
*   **End Time**:
    *   Enter the end time of the absence, using the 24-hour format (e.g., `12:00`).
*   **Duration**:
    *   Enter the duration of the absence, in hours and minutes (e.g., `2 hours 30 minutes`).
*   **Comments**:
    *   Enter any additional comments or notes related to the absence.
*   **Submission**:
    *   Select the status of the absence: Immediate, Pending, or Approved.

## Tips
*   Make sure to review and update this form regularly to ensure it remains accurate and up-to-date.
*   Use this form for all excused absences, and keep a record of submitted forms for tracking and reporting purposes.
