# Teacher's Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher's Registration Form is used to collect necessary information from teachers for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. If you wish, you can enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (optional).
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field (optional).
7. Enter your zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
8. Select your role from the "Role" dropdown menu (e.g., Teacher, Trainer, Instructor, or Lecturer).
9. Enter your course details in the "Course" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address here.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number here.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): If you want to provide your date of birth for any reason, enter it here.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address here (if you wish to provide it).
* **Zip Code** (Text, Required): Enter your zip code here.
* **Role** (Select One, Required): Select your role as a teacher or instructor from the options provided (Teacher, Trainer, Instructor, or Lecturer).
* **Course** (Text, Required): Enter your course details here.

Tips:
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Enter your details accurately and truthfully to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to this help guide for clarification.
