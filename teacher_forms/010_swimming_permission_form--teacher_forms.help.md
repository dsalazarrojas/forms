# Swimming Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Swimming Permission Form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians of a swimmer to provide necessary permissions and details about the swimmer's health, skill level, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the location where the swimmer will be swimming from the options provided. You can choose one or multiple locations.
2.  Provide additional information about where the swimmer will be swimming if necessary.
3.  Enter the swimmer's name, email, and phone number.
4.  Enter the parent/guardian's name, email, and phone number.
5.  Provide an emergency contact's name and contact information.
6.  Enter the swimmer's date of birth.
7.  Enter the swimmer's height.
8.  Enter the swimmer's weight.
9.  Select a unit of measure for the swimmer's height.
10.  Enter the swimmer's height measurement.
11.  Rate the swimmer's skill level.
12.  Provide information about the swimmer's diet.
13.  Provide information about the swimmer's medication.
14.  Enter the dosage and frequency of medication.
15.  Enter any notes about the swimmer's medication.
16.  Enter any special needs the swimmer may have.
17.  Enter the swimmer's prescription number.
18.  Enter any allergies the swimmer has.
19.  Enter any reactions to medication.
20.  Enter any instructions for medication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Swimming Location** (swimming_permission_form, Select Multiple): Select the location where the swimmer will be swimming from the options provided. You can choose one or multiple locations.
*   **Additional Information** (swimming_permission_details, Text): If necessary, provide more details about where the swimmer will be swimming.
*   **Swimmer's Name** (swimmer_name, Text): Enter the swimmer's name.
*   **Swimmer's Email** (swimmer_email, Email): Enter the swimmer's email address.
*   **Swimmer's Phone** (swimmer_phone, Text): Enter the swimmer's phone number.
*   **Parent/Guardian's Name** (parent_name, Text): Enter the parent/guardian's name.
*   **Parent/Guardian's Email** (parent_email, Email): Enter the parent/guardian's email address.
*   **Emergency Contact** (emergency_contact, Text): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Phone** (emergency_phone, Text): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
*   **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, Date): Enter the swimmer's date of birth.
*   **Swimmer's Height** (swimmer_height, Text): Enter the swimmer's height.
*   **Swimmer's Weight** (swimmer_weight, Text): Enter the swimmer's weight.
*   **Swimmer's Height Unit** (swimmer_height_unit, Select One): Select a unit of measure for the swimmer's height (ft, in, cm, m).
*   **Swimmer's Height Measurement** (swimmer_height_measurement, Number): Enter the swimmer's height measurement.
*   **Swimmer's Skill Level** (swimmer_skill_level, Select Multiple): Rate the swimmer's skill level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
*   **Swimmer's Diet** (swimmer_diet, Text): Provide information about the swimmer's diet.
*   **Swimmer's Medication** (swimmer_medication, Text): Provide information about the swimmer's medication.
*   **Dosage of Medication** (swimmer_medication_dosage, Text): Enter the dosage of medication.
*   **Frequency of Medication** (swimmer_medication_frequency, Text): Enter the frequency of medication.
*   **Notes about Medication** (swimmer_medication_notes, Text): Enter any notes about the swimmer's medication.
*   **Swimmer's Special Needs** (swimmer_special_needs, Text): Enter any special needs the swimmer may have.
*   **Prescription Number** (swimmer_medication_prescription, Text): Enter the swimmer's prescription number.
*   **Swimmer's Allergy** (swimmer_medication_allergy, Text): Enter any allergies the swimmer has.
*   **Medication Reaction** (swimmer_medication_reaction, Text): Enter any reactions to medication.
*   **Instructions for Medication** (swimmer_medication_instructions, Text): Enter any instructions for medication.

## Tips
Please fill the form accurately and completely to ensure the swimmer's safety during swimming lessons or programs.
