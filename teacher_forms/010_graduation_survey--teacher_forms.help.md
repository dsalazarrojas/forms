# Graduation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Survey is designed to gather feedback from graduates about their experience with our institution. This information will help us improve our programs and services to better support future graduates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. If a field is not applicable to you, please mark it as "Not Applicable" or provide a brief explanation.
3. If you have any feedback or comments, please use the "Feedback Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (graduate_name)**: Please enter your first name.
* **Major (graduate_major)**: Please enter your major or field of study.
* **Graduation Date (graduation_date)**: Please enter the date you graduated or expect to graduate.
* **Post Graduation Plans (postgrad_plan)**: Briefly describe your post-graduation plans or goals.
* **Overall Satisfaction (overall_satisfaction)**: Select how satisfied you are with your experience at our institution. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Feedback Comments (feedback_comments)**: Please provide any comments or suggestions you have about your experience.
* **Email (email)**: Please enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone (phone)**: Please enter your phone number (optional).

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your responses.
- Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your feedback.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
