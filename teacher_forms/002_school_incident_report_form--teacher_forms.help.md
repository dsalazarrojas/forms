# School Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Incident Report Form is a tool to collect and document incidents that occur on school premises. It is essential to provide accurate and detailed information to facilitate effective investigation and resolution of incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Provide all required information, including student name, staff name, date, time, location, description of the incident, and the responder's contact information.
3. Select the correct incident type and student status from the provided options.
4. If applicable, provide any additional notes related to the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student involved in the incident.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the staff member involved in the incident or who reported the incident.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred, including the school building or area.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of what happened, including any relevant details.
* **Responder Name** (`responder_name`, text, required): Enter your name and title (if applicable).
* **Responder Title** (`responder_title`, text, optional): Enter your title or position.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of incident that occurred from the provided options.
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select any applicable student status related to the incident from the provided options.
* **School Location** (`school_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the school or building where the incident occurred.
* **Responder Email** (`responder_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Responder Phone** (`responder_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the incident.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your school administration.
* If you have already submitted an incident report, do not submit another one unless the incident is a recurrence or a new development.
