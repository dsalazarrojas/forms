# Online Learning Parental Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain parental consent for online learning activities, ensuring a safe and effective online learning experience for students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent" field.
2. Provide the parent's email address in the "Parent Email" field.
3. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
4. Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
5. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher" field.
6. Enter the name of the school the student attends in the "School Name" field.
7. Enter the school's email address in the "School Email" field.
8. Enter the date of the form submission in the "Date" field.
9. Enter the time of the form submission in the "Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent** (`parent_name`, Optional): Enter the parent's name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, Optional): Enter the parent's email address.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, Optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, Optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Teacher** (`teacher_name`, Optional): Enter the teacher's name.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, Optional): Enter the name of the school the student attends.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, Optional): Enter the school's email address.
* **Date** (`date`, Optional): Enter the date of the form submission.
* **Time** (`time`, Optional): Enter the time of the form submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to ensure that the form submission is valid.
* Double-check the information provided before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the form's maintainer at form@gic.mx.
