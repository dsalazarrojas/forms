# Return To In Person School Information And Commitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Return To In Person School Information And Commitment Form" is a tool used to gather essential details about students returning to a school setting after an absence. This form ensures that the school has up-to-date information about returning students and helps maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the school information, including any relevant details such as the school name and location.
2.  Provide the student's name accurately.
3.  Enter the parent's email address for contact purposes.
4.  Enter the parent's phone number for easy communication.
5.  Indicate whether the parent has given permission for the student to return to school.
6.  Specify whether the student has given permission to return to school.
7.  Enter the date of when the student can attend school again.
8.  Enter the date when the student is expected to return to school.
9.  Add any additional comments or notes relevant to the student's return.
10. Finally, indicate any next steps or action items for the school staff to follow up on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Information** (`school_information`, text, required/false): Enter the school name and location where the student is returning to. This could include the school name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Student's Name** (`students_name`, text, required/false): Enter the student's full name accurately.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required/false): Enter the parent's email address for easy contact purposes.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required/false): Enter the parent's phone number for easy communication.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, select_one, required/false): Indicate whether the parent has given permission for the student to return to school. Options are 'True', 'False', or Not Sure.
* **Student Permission** (`student_permission`, select_multiple, required/false): Specify whether the student has given permission to return to school. Options are 'True', 'False', or multiple selections can be made.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, date, required/false): Enter the date when the student can attend school again.
* **Teacher Permission** (`teacher_permission`, select_one, required/false): Indicate whether the teacher has given permission for the student to return to school. Options are 'True', 'False'.
* **School Start Date** (`school_start_date`, date, required/false): Enter the date when the student is expected to return to school.
* **School End Date** (`school_end_date`, date, required/false): Enter the date when the student is expected to leave school again.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/false): Add any additional comments or notes relevant to the student's return.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/false): Add any additional comments or notes relevant to the student's return.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, note, required/false): Add any next steps or action items for the school staff to follow up on.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required/false): Add any additional comments or notes relevant to the student's return.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, select_multiple, required/false): Specify any next steps or action items for the school staff to follow up on. Options are multiple selections can be made.

Please note: Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately, and the form is submitted correctly to avoid any confusion or delays in processing the student's return.
