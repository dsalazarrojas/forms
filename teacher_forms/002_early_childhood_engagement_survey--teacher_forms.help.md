# Early Childhood Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from teachers in early childhood education to evaluate the effectiveness of our program and gather insights from their experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page according to your experiences and preferences.
2. Select the options that best describe your feelings about each question.
3. Provide any additional feedback in the "Feedback" field, if you have something specific to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Preschool Teachers** (`preschool_teacher`, Select Multiple, Required): Please select "Yes" if you are a preschool teacher and have experience with our program, or "No" if you are not a preschool teacher or do not have experience with our program.
* **Kindergarten Teachers** (`kindergarten_teacher`, Select Multiple, Required): Please select "Yes" if you are a kindergarten teacher and have experience with our program, or "No" if you are not a kindergarten teacher or do not have experience with our program.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, Number, Required): Rate your overall experience with our preschool/kindergarten program on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is "Poor" and 10 is "Excellent".
* **Communication** (`communication`, Select One, Required): How often do you feel you are able to effectively communicate with parents/guardians? Please select one of the options:
	+ Option 1: Rarely
	+ Option 2: Sometimes
	+ Option 3: Often
* **Support From Colleagues** (`support_from_colleagues`, Select One, Required): How would you rate the support from your colleagues? Please select one of the options:
	+ Option 1: Poor
	+ Option 2: Neutral
	+ Option 3: Good
	+ Option 4: Very Good
	+ Option 5: Excellent
* **Support From Director** (`support_from_director`, Select One, Required): How would you rate the support from your director? Please select one of the options:
	+ Option 1: Poor
	+ Option 2: Neutral
	+ Option 3: Good
	+ Option 4: Very Good
	+ Option 5: Excellent
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, Select One, Required): How would you rate the time management within the program? Please select one of the options:
	+ Option 1: Poor
	+ Option 2: Neutral
	+ Option 3: Good
	+ Option 4: Very Good
	+ Option 5: Excellent
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Note, Optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Please provide your email address if you would like to be contacted for further feedback or updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Please provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further feedback or updates.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, Date, Optional): This field is automatically populated by the system and should not be edited.

Note: All fields marked as "Required" must be completed before submission.
