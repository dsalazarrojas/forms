# Beginning Of The Year Student Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a student at the beginning of the school year to help teachers create a personalized learning plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please review each field carefully and provide accurate information. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask your child or the teacher for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (Student Name)** (`student_name`, text, **Required**): Fill in your child's full name.
* **Grade Level (Grade Level)** (`grade_level`, select_one, **Required**): Choose your child's grade level from the provided options.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth)** (`date_of_birth`, date, **Required**): Enter your child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Parent/Guardian Name (Parent/Guardian Name)** (`parent_name`, text, **Required**): Fill in your child's parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent Email (Parent Email)** (`email_parent`, email, **Required**): Enter your child's parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone (Parent Phone)** (`phone_parent`, text, **Required**): Fill in your child's parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Student Favorite Subject (Student Favorite Subject)** (`favorite_subject`, select_one, **Required**): Choose your child's favorite subject from the provided options.
* **How Does Your Child Learn Best (How Does Your Child Learn Best)** (`learning_style`, select_one, **Required**): Choose how your child learns best from the provided options.
* **Extracurricular Interests (Extracurricular Interests)** (`extracurricular_interests`, text, **Optional**): If your child is interested in any extracurricular activities, clubs, or sports, please list them here.
* **Student Strengths (Student Strengths)** (`strengths`, text, **Required**): Fill in your child's areas of excellence, such as reading, writing, or math.
* **Areas for Improvement (Areas for Improvement)** (`challenges`, text, **Optional**): If there are any areas where your child needs improvement, please list them here.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations (Special Needs or Accommodations)** (`special_needs`, text, **Optional**): If your child has any special needs or learning disabilities, please provide that information.
* **Allergies or Medical Conditions (Allergies or Medical Conditions)** (`allergies`, text, **Optional**): If your child has any allergies or medical conditions, please list them here.
* **How Does Your Child Interact with Peers (How Does Your Child Interact with Peers)** (`social_interaction`, select_one, **Optional**): Choose how your child interacts with their peers from the provided options.
* **Does Your Child Read at Home (Does Your Child Read at Home)** (`home_reading`, select_one, **Optional**): Choose how often your child reads at home from the provided options.
* **Average Screen Time Daily (Average Screen Time Daily)** (`screen_time`, select_one, **Optional**): Choose your child's average screen time from the provided options.
* **Any Behavioral Concerns (Any Behavioral Concerns)** (`behavioral_concerns`, text, **Optional**): If you have any concerns about your child's behavior, please list them here.
* **What Are Your Expectations This Year (What Are Your Expectations This Year)** (`classroom_expectations`, text, **Optional**): If you have any expectations for your child's academic performance this year, please list them here.
* **Ways Parent Can Support Learning (Ways Parent Can Support Learning)** (`parent_support`, text, **Optional**): If you have any ideas on how you can support your child's learning at home, please list them here.
