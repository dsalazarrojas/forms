# Community Education Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from community education participants on their experience with a specific program or course. The feedback will help us understand their overall satisfaction, the quality of the course content, and the effectiveness of the instructors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name (if you wish to share it).
2. Enter your email address for follow-up (optional).
3. Select the program or course you attended.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the program.
5. Rate the course content quality.
6. Rate the instructor's effectiveness.
7. Rate the quality of materials provided.
8. Rate the course pace.
9. Rate the difficulty level of the course.
10. Answer if the course met your expectations.
11. Specify what skills or knowledge you gained.
12. Choose how you will apply what you learned.
13. Rate how likely you are to recommend this program to others.
14. Choose if you are interested in taking future courses.
15. Provide suggestions for future course topics.
16. Suggest improvements for the program.
17. Add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (Optional)**: This is where you can enter your name if you wish to share it with us.
* **Email Address**: This is an optional field for follow-up purposes. If you enter your email address, we may contact you with further questions or to discuss your feedback.
* **Program or Course Name**: Select the program or course you attended to help us understand which one you are providing feedback on.
* **Instructor Name**: If you wish to mention the instructor, you can do so here.
* **Completion Date**: Enter the date you completed the program.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **Content Quality**: Rate the quality of the course content on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Instructor Effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of the instructor on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Quality of Materials**: Rate the quality of materials provided on a scale from Excellent to Not Applicable.
* **Course Pace**: Rate the pace of the course on a scale from Too Fast to Too Slow.
* **Difficulty Level**: Rate the difficulty level of the course on a scale from Too Easy to Too Hard.
* **Did Course Meet Expectations**: Choose if the course met your expectations or not.
* **Skills or Knowledge Gained**: Describe what skills or knowledge you gained from the program.
* **Will You Apply What You Learned**: Choose how likely you are to apply what you learned from the program.
* **Would You Recommend This Program**: Rate how likely you are to recommend this program to others.
* **Interested in Future Courses**: Choose if you are interested in taking future courses.
* **Topics You Would Like to See**: Suggest topics you would like to see in future courses.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Provide any suggestions on how we can improve the program.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any other comments or feedback you have.
