# Presentation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about a presentation, providing an opportunity for speakers to improve their skills and for presenters to understand how their presentation was received.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Rate the presentation and answer the related questions.
2. Comment on the presentation content.
3. Rate the speaker's presentation skills.
4. Comment on the speaker's presentation materials and body language.
5. Rate the speaker's confidence.
6. Comment on the room climate and sound quality.
7. Rate your overall satisfaction.
8. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
9. Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up discussion).
10. Enter your phone number (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up discussion).
11. Enter any additional feedback.
12. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Presentation Feedback**: This field is for rating the presentation and providing a brief comment on your experience.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Rate Presentation**: Rate the presentation as Good or Fair.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: No
* **Feedback Rating Answer**: This field is for answering the rate of the presentation.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Presentation Content**: Comment on the presentation content.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Speaker's Presentation Skills**: Rate the speaker's presentation skills as Yes or No.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: No
* **Speaker Presentation Skills Answer**: This field is for answering the rating of the speaker's presentation skills.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Speaker's Presentation Materials**: Comment on the speaker's presentation materials.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Speaker's Body Language**: Comment on the speaker's body language.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Speaker's Confidence**: Rate the speaker's confidence as Yes or No.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: No
* **Speaker Confidence Answer**: This field is for answering the rating of the speaker's confidence.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Room Climate**: Rate the room climate as Yes or No, and comment on it.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: No
* **Room Climate Answer**: This field is for answering the rating of the room climate.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Sound Quality**: Rate the sound quality as Good or Poor.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: No
* **Sound Quality Answer**: This field is for answering the rating of the sound quality.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction as Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: No
* **Overall Satisfaction Answer**: This field is for answering the rating of your overall satisfaction.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions for improvement.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up discussion).
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: No
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up discussion).
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Additional Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Submit**: Click "Submit" to complete the form.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Back**: Click "Back" to go back to previous page.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Email (optional)**: Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up discussion).
	+ Type: email
