# Parent Teacher Student Meeting Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help parents and teachers schedule meetings for students. It allows you to enter the meeting details, such as date, time, and participant names. This will help ensure that both parties are on the same page and can coordinate meeting arrangements conveniently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on "New Meeting" to begin filling out the form.
2.  Enter the desired meeting date using the date picker.
3.  Enter the meeting time in the provided input field.
4.  Fill in the student's name in the text input field.
5.  Enter the parent's name in the text input field.
6.  Enter the teacher's name in the text input field.
7.  Enter the parent's email address in the format "example@example.com".
8.  Enter the parent's phone number in the text input field.
9.  Click on "Submit" to save your meeting details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Parent-Teacher-Student Meeting Sign Up Form Title**: This is the title of the meeting that you're signing up for. Enter a brief and descriptive title to help identify the meeting purpose. (Text input, required)
*   **Parent-Teacher-Student Meeting Sign Up Form Date**: This is the date of the meeting. Use the date picker to select a date that works for you. (Date picker, required)
*   **Parent-Teacher-Student Meeting Sign Up Form Time**: This is the time of the meeting. Enter the time in the provided input field. (Time input, required)
*   **Parent-Teacher-Student Meeting Sign Up Form Student Name**: This is the name of the student attending the meeting. Enter the student's full name accurately. (Text input, required)
*   **Parent-Teacher-Student Meeting Sign Up Form Parent Name**: This is the name of the parent attending the meeting. Enter the parent's full name accurately. (Text input, required)
*   **Parent-Teacher-Student Meeting Sign Up Form Teacher Name**: This is the name of the teacher attending the meeting. Enter the teacher's full name accurately. (Text input, required)
*   **Parent-Teacher-Student Meeting Sign Up Form Parent Email**: This is the email address of the parent who signed up for the meeting. Enter a valid email address in the format "example@example.com". (Email input, required)
*   **Parent-Teacher-Student Meeting Sign Up Form Parent Phone**: This is the phone number of the parent who signed up for the meeting. Enter the parent's valid phone number. (Text input, required)

## Tips

*   Ensure that you've entered accurate and correct information for each field.
*   Double-check the meeting details before submitting the form to avoid errors.
*   Use the date picker for a precise date selection.
