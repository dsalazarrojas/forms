# preschool summer camp enrollment form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for enrolling your child in the preschool summer camp. Please fill out this form to provide us with essential information about your child and yourself.

## How To Complete This Form
- To begin, fill out each section completely.
- Please make sure to double-check your entries before submitting the form.
- If you need help with any question, please do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Parent Name
* **Parent Name**: Enter your name, the legal name of the parent or guardian of the child.
### 2. Contact Number
* **Contact Number**: Enter your primary contact number, where we can reach you for any questions or updates.
### 3. Birthdate
* **Birthdate**: Enter your child's birthdate in MM/DD/YYYY format.
### 4. Start Date
* **Start Date**: Select the date you want your child to start the summer camp program.
### 5. End Date
* **End Date**: Select the date you want your child to end the summer camp program.
### 6. Child Name
* **Child Name**: Enter your child's name, as it appears on their official documents.
### 7. Parent Email
* **Parent Email**: Enter your valid email address where we can contact you about your child's summer camp experience.
### 8. Payment Method
* **Payment Method**: Select the way you want to pay for the summer camp program. This can be credit card, cash, or check.
### 9. Emergency Contact Name
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name and relationship of the person we should contact in case of an emergency.
### 10. Emergency Contact Number
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the person we should contact in case of an emergency.
### 11. Medical Notes
* **Medical Notes**: If your child has any medical conditions or allergies, please provide us with notes so we can take necessary precautions.
### 12. Additional Info
* **Additional Info**: Select any additional information about your child's needs or requirements. This can include allergies, medical conditions, or special needs.
### 13. Medical Release
* **Medical Release**: Indicate if the child has any medical conditions that require special handling during the summer camp program.
### 14. Emergency Contact Relationship
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship between the child and the emergency contact person.
### 15. Emergency Contact Permission
* **Emergency Contact Permission**: Indicate if you grant permission to contact the emergency contact person in case of an emergency.
### 16. Emergency Contact Permission
* **Additional Info**: Enter any further comments or notes you wish to provide regarding your child's needs or requirements.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form completely and submit it once you have finished.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need help, please contact us at [Your Email/Contact Info].
