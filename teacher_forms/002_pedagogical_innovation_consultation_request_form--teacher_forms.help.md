# Pedagogical Innovation Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
=============================================

## Purpose
The Pedagogical Innovation Consultation Request Form is designed to collect information from educators and administrators to support pedagogical innovation in education. The form helps identify areas of innovation and potential solutions to enhance teaching and learning practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your options for "Select One Option", "Select One Date", and "Select One Decimal" questions based on your preferences.
3. Choose multiple options for "Select Multiple Options", "Select Multiple Dates", and "Select Multiple Decimals" questions as needed.
4. Enter any relevant phone numbers and dates in the corresponding fields.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
6. Review your form submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Name** (`name`, `text`, required)
 Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.

### 2. **Email** (`email`, `email`, required)
 Enter your valid email address to contact you for further discussions.

### 3. **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional)
 Enter any phone number you'd like to be reached at for further discussions.

### 4. **Select One Option** (`select_one`, `Yes/No`, required)
 Choose "Yes" if you support the innovation, "No" if you don't.

### 5. **Select One Date** (`select_one`, `Yes/No`, optional)
 Choose "Yes" if you support the date, "No" if you don't.

### 6. **Select One Decimal** (`select_one`, `Yes/No`, optional)
 Choose "Yes" if you support the decimal, "No" if you don't.

### 7. **Select Multiple Options** (`select_multiple`, `Yes/No`, optional)
 Choose multiple options that apply to you for further discussions.

### 8. **Select Multiple Dates** (`select_multiple`, `Yes/No`, optional)
 Choose multiple dates that apply to you for further discussions.

### 9. **Select Multiple Decimals** (`select_multiple`, `Yes/No`, optional)
 Choose multiple decimals that apply to you for further discussions.

### 10. **Select One Decimal** (`select_one`, `Yes/No`, optional)
 Choose "Yes" if you support the decimal, "No" if you don't.

### 11. **Select One Integer** (`select_one`, `Yes/No`, optional)
 Choose "Yes" if you support the integer, "No" if you don't.

### 12. **Select Multiple Integers** (`select_multiple`, `Yes/No`, optional)
 Choose multiple integers that apply to you for further discussions.

### 13. **Email List** (`text`, `email list`, optional)
 Enter a list of email addresses separated by commas if you'd like to be reached at these email addresses.

### 14. **Email List 2** (`text`, `email list 2`, optional)
 Enter a list of email addresses separated by commas if you'd like to be reached at these email addresses.

### 15. **Note** (`note`, `Note`, optional)
 Add any additional notes or comments that might be helpful for further discussions.

### 16. **Time List** (`time`, `time list`, optional)
 Enter a list of time slots you are available for meetings or discussions.

### 17. **Phone List** (`text`, `phone list`, optional)
 Enter a list of phone numbers you'd like to be reached at.

### 18. **Phone List 2** (`text`, `phone list 2`, optional)
 Enter a list of phone numbers you'd like to be reached at.

Remember to review your form submission before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
