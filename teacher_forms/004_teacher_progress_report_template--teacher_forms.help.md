# Teacher Progress Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from teachers on the progress of their students. It is used to document student progress, provide feedback, and communicate with parents or administrators.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in the student's name and ID (if applicable).
- Select the teacher's name and the student's grade level.
- Choose the subject area and grade level for the report.
- Enter the date and time of the report.
- Write any comments or feedback you have for the student.
- Select the student's progress level (e.g., Unsatisfactory, Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, or Excellent).
- Finally, sign and save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the student.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's ID, if applicable.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the teacher.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the student's grade level (e.g., Level 1, Level 2, Level 3).
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, text, required/optional): Enter the subject area or grade level for the report.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the grade level for the report (e.g., Pre-Kindergarten, Kindergarten, etc.).
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have for the student.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the report.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the report.
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments from the student.
* **Student Progress** (`student_progress`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the student's progress level (e.g., Unsatisfactory, Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, or Excellent).
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign the report.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check the student's information for errors.
- Use the form for its intended purpose only.
- Save the form securely and share it with relevant parties as needed.
