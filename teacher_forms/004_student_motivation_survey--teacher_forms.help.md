# Student Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Motivation Survey is designed to gather information about students' motivations, strengths, weaknesses, needs, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading through the form carefully to understand what each question is asking.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask your teacher or class advisor for guidance.
