The evaluation teacher data form is used to collect data from teachers on their performance and evaluations. This form is intended for internal use and is used by administrators to assess teacher performance, identify areas for improvement, and inform professional development. It is expected that the form will be filled out by teachers on a regular basis, such as at the end of each semester or school year.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Teacher Name**: Fill out the teacher's name in this field. This will help us identify the teacher being evaluated.
2. **Select Department**: Choose the department the teacher is affiliated with. This will help us understand the teacher's area of specialization.
3. **Enter School Year**: Input the current school year the teacher is working in. This will help us track progress and trends over time.
4. **Select School Grade**: Choose the grade level the teacher is working with. This will help us understand the teacher's level of responsibility.
5. **Rate Teacher Performance**: Select the teacher's performance rating from the options provided. This will help us gauge their effectiveness in the classroom.
6. **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date the evaluation was completed. This will help us track when the evaluation was done.
7. **Evaluation Time**: Enter the time of day the evaluation was completed. This will help us understand when the evaluation took place.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name (text, required)**: Enter the teacher's name in this field. This is a required field to help us identify the teacher being evaluated.
* **Department (text, optional)**: Choose the department the teacher is affiliated with. This will help us understand the teacher's area of specialization.
* **School Year (number, optional)**: Input the current school year the teacher is working in. This will help us track progress and trends over time.
* **School Grade (number, optional)**: Choose the grade level the teacher is working with. This will help us understand the teacher's level of responsibility.
* **Teacher Rating (select one, optional)**: Select the teacher's performance rating from the options provided. This will help us gauge their effectiveness in the classroom.
* **Evaluation Date (date, optional)**: Enter the date the evaluation was completed. This will help us track when the evaluation was done.
* **Evaluation Time (time, optional)**: Enter the time of day the evaluation was completed. This will help us understand when the evaluation took place.

### Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure accurate data collection.
* If a field is optional, make sure to fill it out if it applies to the teacher being evaluated.
* If you need help with this form, contact your supervisor or administrator for assistance.
