# Student Weekly Music Practice Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Weekly Music Practice Record Form is used to record a student's music practice record, including details on practice sessions, practice time, practice frequency, and teacher and student ratings. This form helps students and teachers to track progress and facilitate communication between them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the music sessions the student had during the week by choosing all the days they practiced.
2. Enter the time spent on practice.
3. Choose the practice frequency from the options provided.
4. Enter the practice goals.
5. Fill out the teacher comments.
6. Fill out the student comments.
7. Fill out additional comments from the student.
8. Choose the practice frequency.
9. Enter the practice duration.
10. Select the student's level of proficiency.
11. Enter the teacher's rating.
12. Enter the teacher's comments.
13. Enter the student's rating.
14. Enter the submitted date.
15. Enter the teacher's name.
16. Enter the form ID.
17. Enter the tool assigned to the student.
18. Enter the student's name.
19. Enter the student's grade.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all the relevant music sessions the student had during the week.
* Enter the practice time accurately.
* Enter the student's level of proficiency accurately.
* Enter the teacher's and student's ratings accurately.
* Enter the submitted date accurately.
* Enter the student's name and grade accurately.
* Enter the teacher's name and comments accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
