# Field Trip Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Field Trip Permission Form is a document that must be completed by parents or guardians to grant permission for their child to participate in a field trip. The form helps to ensure that students are properly supervised and authorized to participate in off-site activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the appropriate options for Parent or Guardian Permission.
3. Sign the form to confirm your permission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, required): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, required): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Parent or Guardian Permission** (`student_permission`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the options provided to grant permission for the field trip.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the school name.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as the teacher.
* **Field Trip Date** (`field_trip_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the field trip.
* **Field Trip Time** (`field_trip_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the field trip.
* **Bus Number** (`bus_number`, text, required): Enter the bus number.
* **Bus Driver** (`bus_driver`, text, required): Enter the bus driver's name.
* **Bus Phone** (`bus_phone`, text, required): Enter the bus driver's phone number.
* **Teacher License** (`teacher_license`, text, required): Enter the teacher's license number.
* **Teacher ID Number** (`teacher_id_number`, text, required): Enter the teacher's ID number.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, text, required): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required): Enter the student's home address.
* **Student City** (`student_city`, text, required): Enter the student's city.
* **Student State** (`student_state`, text, required): Enter the student's state.
* **Student Zip** (`student_zip`, text, required): Enter the student's zip code.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Chaperone Name** (`chaperone`, text, required): Enter the chaperone's name.
* **Chaperone Email** (`chaperone_email`, email, required): Enter the chaperone's email address.
* **Chaperone Phone** (`chaperone_phone`, text, required): Enter the chaperone's phone number.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the teacher or school administration.
