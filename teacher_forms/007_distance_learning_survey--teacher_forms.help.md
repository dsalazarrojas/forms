## Distance Learning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and survey data from teachers on their experiences with distance learning tools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by filling in the **Title** field with the title of the distance learning course or program you are taking or working with.
*   Select the **Category** that best describes the distance learning course or program you are taking or working with from the drop-down menu.
*   In the **Description** field, provide a brief description of your experience with the distance learning course or program.
*   If applicable, specify the **Assigned Tool** you used for the distance learning course or program.
*   In the **Feedback** field, share any feedback or comments you have about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Title (form_title)**: The title of the distance learning course or program you are taking or working with.
*   **Category (form_category)**: Select the category that best describes the distance learning course or program you are taking or working with.
*   **Description (form_description)**: A brief description of your experience with the distance learning course or program.
*   **Assigned Tool (form_assigned_tool)**: If applicable, specify the tool used for the distance learning course or program.
*   **Feedback (form_feedback)**: Share any feedback or comments you have about your experience.

## Tips
*   Be sure to answer all required fields, marked with an \*.
*   You can always ask if you have any questions or need assistance with filling out the form.
*   The feedback field is an optional field but is highly encouraged for improving the distance learning courses or programs.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields, try to fill it in as best as you can and don't hesitate to ask for help.
*   Remember that the feedback field is where you can provide suggestions or comments about the form itself.
*   If you have any issues with the form, please contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
