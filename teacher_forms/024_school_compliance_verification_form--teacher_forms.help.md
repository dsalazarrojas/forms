# School Compliance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "School Compliance Verification Form" is used to collect and verify information about a student's compliance status within a school setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly.
2. Review each field for its relevance and importance.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submission.

* **student-name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their official records.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter the student's ID number as issued by the school.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Last Date Attended** (`date_last_attended`, date, required): Enter the date the student last attended the school.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the student's contact number where they can be reached.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address (not required but recommended).
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, select_one, required): Select the student's status (active, inactive, or pending).
* **Staff Member** (`staff_member`, select_multiple, optional): Select the staff members involved with the student (active, inactive, or pending).
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the parent's name (not required but recommended).
* **Parent Relationship** (`parent_relationship`, text, optional): Describe the parent's relationship with the student (not required but recommended).
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign the form to acknowledge completion.
