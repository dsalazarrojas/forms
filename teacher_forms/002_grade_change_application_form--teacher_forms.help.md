# grade_change_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The grade change application form is a tool for instructors to request grade changes for a student. This form is to be used for legitimate reasons, such as calculation errors or other administrative errors, and should not be used to inflate a student's grade.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. Each field is required, as indicated by the required label next to each field. Please make sure to include all necessary information to facilitate a thorough review.
2. **Enter the student's name** in the "Student Name" field. This is the name of the student whose grade will be changed.
3. **Enter the grade to be changed** in the "Grade to Change" field. This is the new grade that the instructor wishes to assign to the student.
4. **Explain the reason for the grade change** in the "Reason for Change" field. Please be as detailed as possible, including any supporting evidence or documentation.
5. **Enter your name and title as the instructor** in the "Instructor Name" field.
6. **Enter the date the grade change became effective** in the "Date of Change Became Effective" field. This is the date when the grade change should be applied.
7. **Enter the date the grade change was requested** in the "Date of Change Request Made" field. This is the date when the instructor first submitted the request.
8. **Add a signature** in the "Student Signature" field, if applicable. This is to confirm the student's agreement or disagreement with the grade change.
9. **Add your signature** in the "Instructor Signature" field. This confirms that the instructor has reviewed and agreed to the grade change.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (text)**: Enter the name of the student whose grade is being changed.
* **Grade to Change (number)**: Enter the new grade that the instructor wishes to assign to the student.
* **Reason for Change (text)**: Explain the reason for the grade change, including any supporting evidence or documentation.
* **Instructor Name (text)**: Enter your name and title as the instructor requesting the grade change.
* **Date of Change Became Effective (date)**: Enter the date when the grade change should be applied.
* **Date of Change Request Made (date)**: Enter the date when the instructor first submitted the request.
* **Student Signature (note)**: If applicable, add a signature to confirm the student's agreement or disagreement with the grade change.
* **Instructor Signature (note)**: Add your signature to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the grade change.
