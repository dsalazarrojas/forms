# Course Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Selection Form is designed to help teachers make informed decisions about course selection for their students. It guides them through the process of choosing multiple courses and providing options for side projects, while also allowing for schedule management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reading the form instructions carefully.
2. Select the courses you wish to consider for your students using the "select_courses" dropdown menu.
3. Decide if you wish to offer side projects for your students by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "side_project" dropdown menu.
4. Enter any additional schedule management information in the "schedule_management" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **select_courses** (select multiple, required: false): This dropdown menu allows you to choose the courses you wish to consider for your students. You can select one or multiple options.
* **side_project** (select one, required: false): This dropdown menu asks if you wish to offer side projects for your students. You can select "Yes" if you wish to provide extra projects for them or "No" if you do not.
* **schedule_management** (text, required: false): This text field is available for you to enter any additional schedule management information for your students. You can enter as much or as little information as you need.
