# Preschool Parent Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Preschool Parent Survey Form is a tool used by parents to provide feedback on their experiences and satisfaction with our preschool program. The form aims to collect valuable insights from parents to help us improve our services and better serve our students and families. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to help us gather your thoughts and opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you are answering all the questions that apply to you.
2. Enter your feedback in the designated fields, using your own words for the text fields (parent_comments and parent_name) or selecting from the options for the parent_satisfaction field.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Parent Comments
* **Parent Comments (parent_comments)**: This is a text field where you can provide any comments or feedback you have about our preschool program. Please share your thoughts and suggestions to help us improve our services.

### 2. Parent Satisfaction
* **Parent Satisfaction (parent_satisfaction)**: This field asks about your level of satisfaction with our preschool program. Please select one of the options below to indicate your level of satisfaction:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied

### 3. Email
* **Email (email)**: This field is optional, but if you would like to be kept informed about upcoming events, updates, and announcements from our preschool, please enter your email address here.

### 4. Parent Name
* **Parent Name (parent_name)**: Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed for our records. This will help us personalize our communication with you and your family.
