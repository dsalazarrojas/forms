# Virtual Summer School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Summer School Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from students and teachers for the summer school program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to complete the form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Student Information

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter the parent's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, `date`, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the student's current grade level from the options provided.
* **Address** (`student_address`, `text`, required): Enter the student's address as it appears on official documents.
* **Enrollment Status** (`student_enrollment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the student's enrollment status from the options provided.

### Teacher Information

* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required): Enter the teacher's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, `email`, required): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Notes** (`teacher_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the teacher's involvement.
* **Subject Area** (`teacher_subject_area`, `text`, optional): Enter the teacher's subject area of expertise.
* **Teacher Enrollment Status** (`teacher_enrollment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the teacher's enrollment status from the options provided.
* **Start Date** (`teacher_starting_date`, `date`, required): Enter the teacher's start date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2020).
* **End Date** (`teacher_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the teacher's end date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/31/2020).
* **Hours** (`teacher_hours`, `text`, required): Enter the teacher's total hours worked for the summer school program.
* **Comments** (`teacher_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes from the teacher.

### Form Information

* **Student Parent Permission** (`student_parent_permission`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether the student's parent has given permission for their child to participate.
* **Teacher Permission** (`teacher_permission`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select whether the teacher has given permission to participate.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the school.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, required): Enter a unique ID for the form submission.
* **Date of Enrollment** (`student_enrollment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of enrollment in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Teacher School Name** (`teacher_school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the teacher's school.
* **Teacher Enrollment Date** (`teacher_enrollment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the teacher's enrollment date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2020).
