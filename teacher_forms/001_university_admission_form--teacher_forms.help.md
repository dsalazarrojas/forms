# University Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from students for the university admission process. It will help the admissions team to get in touch with you and guide you through the next steps of your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid email account, as it will be used to communicate with you about your application.
2. Fill in your student name, making sure it accurately represents your identification and name as it appears on any government-issued ID.
3. Provide your student email, which will be used as a primary contact method.
4. Enter your student phone number, including the country code and any leading zero if necessary.
5. Fill in your date of birth, following the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on any government-issued ID.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your student email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_number`, number, required/optional): Enter your student phone number, including the country code and any leading zero if necessary.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in mm/dd/yyyy format.

## Tips
* Please fill in all fields accurately and completely.
* Ensure you enter your email address correctly, as it will be used to communicate with you.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the university admissions team.
* Review your form before submitting it to ensure all fields are filled correctly.
