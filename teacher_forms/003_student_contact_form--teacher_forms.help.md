# Student Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect student information and contact details for teachers, parents, and other school officials to communicate effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the student's name and student email address (if applicable) in the first two fields.
2. Enter your name and email address (if applicable) in the parent fields.
3. Select the student's grade level, subjects they're taking, and school name from the respective dropdown menus.
4. Enter any additional school address information (if necessary).
5. Specify the reason for contact.
6. Enter your school ID.
7. Fill in the teacher's name and teacher email address (if applicable) in the third set of fields.
8. Select the teacher's subjects they're teaching, school name, and school address (if necessary).
9. Sign the student and teacher signature fields with your name.
10. Date and time of submission.
11. Fill in your name and position/title.
12. Optionally, upload a school logo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (1): Enter the student's name as it appears on their official records.
* **Student Email** (2): Enter the student's email address, if applicable. This is not required.
* **Parent Name** (3): Enter your name, as the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Parent Email** (4): Enter your email address, if you have one. This is not required.
* **Parent Phone** (5): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Student Grade** (6): Select the student's grade level from the dropdown menu.
* **Student Subjects** (7): Select the student's subjects from the dropdown menu.
* **School Name** (8): Enter the school name where the student is enrolled.
* **School Address** (9): Enter the school address, if you'd like to provide it.
* **Student DOB** (10): Enter the student's date of birth. This is not required.
* **Contact Reason** (11): Specify the reason for contact.
* **School ID** (12): Enter your school ID number.
* **Teacher Name** (13): Enter the teacher's name who will be in contact with the student.
* **Teacher Email** (14): Enter the teacher's email address, if applicable. This is not required.
* **Teacher Phone** (15): Enter the teacher's phone number, if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Teacher Subjects** (16): Select the teacher's subjects from the dropdown menu.
* **Teacher School Name** (17): Enter the teacher's school name.
* **Teacher Address** (18): Enter the teacher's address, if you'd like to provide it.
* **Student Signature** (19): Sign with your name as the student.
* **Parent Signature** (20): Sign with your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Teacher Signature** (21): Sign with your name as the teacher.
* **Date Submitter** (22): Date and time of submission.
* **Submitter Name** (23): Enter your name, as the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Position** (24): Enter your position or title.
* **School Logo** (25): Optionally, upload a school logo image file.
