# Student Daily Work Hours Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students record their work hours and progress during regular school hours. It helps teachers and school administrators monitor student productivity and stay updated on student work status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Student ID.
2. Enter your teacher's name.
3. Select the date of the day you completed work hours.
4. Enter the start time of your work hours.
5. Enter the end time of your work hours.
6. (Optional) Enter the subject or task you worked on.
7. (Optional) Enter the assignment ID or task reference number.
8. (Optional) Add any comments about your work hours.
9. Enter the total number of work hours you completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter your student ID number as provided by the school administration.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your teacher who assigned or supervised your work hours.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of the day you completed work hours.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the time you started working on your assignment or project.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the time you ended your work hours for the day.
* **Subject** (`subject`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the subject or subject area you worked on.
* **Assignment ID** (`assignment_id`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the assignment or task reference number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or notes about your work hours.
* **Student Work Hours** (`student_work_hours`, number, required): Enter the total number of work hours you completed during the day.
