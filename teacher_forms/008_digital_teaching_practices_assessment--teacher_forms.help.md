# Digital Teaching Practices Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from teachers on their digital teaching practices. Please take a few minutes to complete the form accurately, and don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions.

## How To Complete This Form
-   To complete this form, simply fill in the fields according to the guidelines provided below.
-   Please enter your information in the corresponding fields, making sure to select the correct options where applicable.
-   Make sure to submit the form once you have filled all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Name** (Text, Optional): This field is used to capture the name of your school. If you are not sure, please leave it blank.
- **Teacher Name** (Text, Optional): This field is for your name as a teacher. This is a required field, please provide your name accurately.
- **Grade Level** (Select One, Optional): Select the grade level you are currently teaching, which can be PK-8, 9-12, or Post-Secondary.
- **Subject Area** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the subject area(s) you teach, such as Math, Language Arts, Science, or Other (please specify).
- **EdTech Used** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the educational technology tools you use, including Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Classroom, or Other (please specify).
- **Challenges** (Text, Optional): If you faced any challenges in your teaching practices, please describe them in this field.
- **Teacher Notes** (Note, Optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or notes about your teaching experiences.
- **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address. This will help us contact you about your progress and support.
- **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number. This will be useful for us to contact you if needed.
- **Date** (Date, Optional): This field is for the date you completed this form.
- **Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time you completed this form.
- **Submit** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description to indicate that you have completed this form accurately.
