# Student Technology Device Use Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect student agreement for technology device use in school settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the correct device type you are using for school purposes.
2. Choose how you plan to use the device (Personal, School, or Work).
3. Provide a clear understanding of the usage policy and sign below if you agree to it.
4. Provide your school email and student name for verification purposes.
5. Sign your name as a signature of agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Technology Device Use** (`technology_device_use`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of your plan for using the technology device in school.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, text, required/optional): Please read and agree to the terms and conditions of technology device use in school.
* **Device Type** (`device_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of device you are using (Laptop, Tablet, Smartphone, Desktop, Mobile Phone).
* **Usage Policy** (`usage_policy`, text, required/optional): Please read and agree to the usage policy for technology devices in school.
* **Device Use** (`device_use`, select_multiple, required): Please select how you plan to use the device (Personal, School, Work).
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required/optional): Please provide your school email address.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Please provide your student name.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required/optional): Please provide your teacher's name for verification purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Please provide the date you are signing this agreement.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Please provide the time you are signing this agreement.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required/optional): Please sign your name below as a signature of agreement.

Note: The optional fields are not required for the form to be considered complete, but they are included for additional information that can be useful to the school or teacher. The fields marked as `required` are mandatory and must be completed for the form to be considered valid.
