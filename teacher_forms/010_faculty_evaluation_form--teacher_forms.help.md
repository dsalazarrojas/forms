# Faculty Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students evaluate their faculty members, providing a structured means to assess the quality of instruction, department support, and overall performance of faculty members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and fields that are being asked.
2. Select the rating for each question, using the number scale provided.
3. Provide feedback and comments as needed, using the text fields.
4. Make sure to complete all required fields (instructor_rating, comments, overall_rating) before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Faculty Rating** (`faculty_rating`, number, required: false): This field allows you to rate the faculty member's overall teaching effectiveness. Rate from 1 to 5, with 5 being the highest rating.
* **Instructor Rating** (`instructor_rating`, number, required: true): This field is required and asks you to rate the faculty member's instructional skills. Rate from 1 to 5, with 5 being the highest rating.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Use this field to provide additional comments or feedback about your experience with the faculty member.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: true): This field is required and provides space to express any thoughts, suggestions, or criticisms about your experience with the faculty member.
* **Department Rating** (`department_rating`, number, required: false): This field allows you to rate the support provided by the department. Rate from 1 to 5, with 5 being the highest rating.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required: false): This field asks you to rate your overall experience with the faculty member. Rate from 1 to 5, with 5 being the highest rating.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your evaluations.
* Keep your feedback and comments constructive and respectful.
* Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all required fields.
