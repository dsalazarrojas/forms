# Academic Performance Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about an instructor's performance in a course. It helps to assess the instructor's strengths and areas for improvement and provides valuable insights for the instructor to enhance their teaching methods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your full name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the course taught by the instructor in the "Course Name" field.
3. Enter the full name of the instructor being evaluated in the "Instructor Name" field.
4. For each of the subsequent fields:
   * Rate your overall satisfaction with the instructor.
   * Rate the instructor's teaching clarity.
   * Rate the relevance of the assignments.
   * Rate the instructor's encouragement of class participation.
   * Rate the quality of the feedback.
   * Rate the quality of course materials.
5. If you would like to suggest an area of improvement for the instructor, you can type in the "Suggestions for Improvement" field.
6. If you would like to comment on the instructor's strengths, you can do so in the "Instructor Strengths" field.
7. You can also add any additional comments about the instructor or course in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, **Text**, Required): Enter your full name to identify yourself.
- **Course Name** (`course_name`, **Text**, Required): Enter the name of the course taught by the instructor.
- **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, **Text**, Required): Enter the full name of the instructor being evaluated.
- **Overall Satisfaction with Instructor** (`overall_satisfaction`, **Select One**, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the instructor on a scale from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
- **Teaching Clarity** (`teaching_clarity`, **Select One**, Required): Rate the instructor's teaching clarity on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
- **Assignment Relevance** (`assignment_relevance`, **Select One**, Required): Rate the relevance of the assignments to course objectives on a scale from Highly Relevant to Not Relevant.
- **Class Participation Encouragement** (`class_participation_encouragement`, **Select One**, Required): Rate the instructor's encouragement of class participation on a scale from Always to Never.
- **Quality of Feedback** (`feedback_quality`, **Select One**, Required): Rate the instructor's feedback quality on a scale from Very Helpful to Not Helpful.
- **Quality of Course Materials** (`course_materials_quality`, **Select One**, Required): Rate the quality of course materials on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
- **Accessibility Outside Class** (`accessibility_outside_class`, **Select One**, Required): Rate the instructor's accessibility outside of class on a scale from Always Available to Not Available.
- **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, **Text**, Optional): Provide any area of improvement you would like to suggest for the instructor.
- **Instructor Strengths** (`instructor_strengths`, **Text**, Optional): Mention any strengths you see in the instructor.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, **Text**, Optional): Add any other comments about the instructor or course.
