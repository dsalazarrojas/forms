# Preschool Student Skills Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Student Skills Assessment Form is a tool used to evaluate the skills and abilities of preschool students. It helps educators and caregivers understand each student's strengths and weaknesses in various areas, making it easier to develop effective educational plans and strategies for supporting their growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the student's profile and familiarize yourself with their abilities and characteristics.
2. Select the corresponding answer based on your observations of the student's skills and abilities.
3. Enter your response in the designated space provided for each question.
4. Repeat the process for each section of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pre-academic Skills** (`pre_academic_skills`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to assess a student's pre-academic skills, such as alphabet knowledge, number recognition, and basic math concepts.
	+ Please answer based on your observations of the student's ability to recognize and identify letters and numbers, and perform basic math operations.
* **Social Skills** (`social_skills`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to assess a student's social skills, such as cooperation, empathy, and self-control.
	+ Please answer based on your observations of the student's ability to interact with peers and adults, and exhibit positive social behaviors.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to assess a student's communication skills, such as speaking, listening, and writing.
	+ Please answer based on your observations of the student's ability to express themselves through verbal and non-verbal communication.
* **Problem Solving Skills** (`problem_solving_skills`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to assess a student's problem-solving skills, such as critical thinking, reasoning, and creativity.
	+ Please answer based on your observations of the student's ability to identify and solve problems.
* **Language Skills** (`language_skills`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to assess a student's language skills, such as reading, writing, and vocabulary.
	+ Please answer based on your observations of the student's ability to understand and use language effectively.
* **Art Aptitude** (`art_aptitude`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to assess a student's art aptitude, such as creativity, imagination, and fine motor skills.
	+ Please answer based on your observations of the student's ability to create and express themselves through art.
* **Fine Motor Skills** (`fine_motor_skills`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to assess a student's fine motor skills, such as hand-eye coordination and dexterity.
	+ Please answer based on your observations of the student's ability to perform fine motor tasks.
* **Gross Motor Skills** (`gross_motor_skills`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to assess a student's gross motor skills, such as running, jumping, and throwing.
	+ Please answer based on your observations of the student's ability to perform gross motor activities.
* **Math Concepts** (`math_concept`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to assess a student's math concepts, such as understanding of numbers and mathematical operations.
	+ Please answer based on your observations of the student's ability to understand math concepts.
* **Math Facts Family** (`math_fact_family`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to assess a student's math facts, such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division facts.
	+ Please answer based on your observations of the student's ability to recall math facts.
* **Social Interaction** (`social_interaction`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to assess a student's social interaction, such as cooperation, sharing, and empathy.
	+ Please answer based on your observations of the student's ability to interact with peers and adults.
* **Emotional Intelligence** (`emotional_intelligence`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to assess a student's emotional intelligence, such as self-awareness, self-regulation, and social awareness.
	+ Please answer based on your observations of the student's ability to understand and manage their emotions.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when answering each question.
* Use the provided space to explain your observations and reasoning.
* Please ensure that your answers are based on your direct observations of the student, rather than assumptions or second-hand information.
* This form is intended to be completed for each student, and the results can be used to track progress over time.
