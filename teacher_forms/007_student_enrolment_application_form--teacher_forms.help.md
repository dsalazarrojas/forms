# Student Enrolment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Enrolment Application Form is a document used to collect information from students applying for enrollment. It is used to verify the student's details and assess their eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read the form and its instructions before starting.
- Ensure you have all necessary documents and information readily available.
- Complete each field according to the instructions and explanations provided.
- Review your form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Information** (`student_information`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the student's personal details. Please provide the student's name, date of birth, and any other relevant information. 
- **Enrolment Information** (`student_enrolment`, text, optional): Enter the student's enrolment preferences, including the grade level and program of interest. 
- **Parent Details** (`parent_details`, text, optional): If the student is under 18, please enter the parent's or guardian's name and contact information. 
- **Guardian Details** (`guardian_details`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the guardian's name and contact information. 
- **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter the student's home address.
- **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
- **Tips**: Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date. It's also recommended to attach any supporting documents, if necessary, to ensure a smooth application process.
