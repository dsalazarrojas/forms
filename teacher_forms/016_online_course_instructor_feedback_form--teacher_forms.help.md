# online-course-instructor-feedback-form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from instructors about their online courses. This form helps us understand their experiences and opinions, which we use to improve our courses and better serve students.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "Course Name" field and enter the name of the course you are providing feedback on.
2. Enter your "Instructor Name" in the provided field.
3. In the "Comment" field, share your thoughts and observations about the course.
4. If you'd like to rate your experience, use the "Rating" field. This is optional but helps us understand your level of satisfaction.
5. In the "Course Feedback" field, provide any specific feedback you may have about the course.
6. Enter any suggestions you may have for improving the course in the "Suggestion" field. This is optional.
7. Enter your "Email" address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback.
8. Enter your "Phone" number in case we need to discuss your feedback further.
9. Select how you submitted this feedback from the "Submitted By" field. This could be "Student", "Parent", "Teacher", "Admin", or "Other".
10. Enter the date and time you submitted this feedback in the "Submitted On" and "Submitted At" fields.
11. Finally, enter your email address again in the "Submitted By Email" field to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name** (Required): Enter the name of the course you are providing feedback on.
* **Instructor Name** (Required): Enter your name as the instructor providing feedback.
* **Comment** (Required): Share your thoughts and observations about the course.
* **Rating** (Optional): Rate your experience from 1-5, where 1 is lowest and 5 is highest.
* **Course Feedback** (Required): Provide specific feedback about the course.
* **Suggestion** (Optional): Enter any suggestions you may have for improving the course.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number in case we need to discuss your feedback further.
* **Submitted By** (Required): Select how you submitted this feedback from the options provided.
* **Submitted On** (Required): Enter the date you submitted this feedback.
* **Submitted At** (Required): Enter the time you submitted this feedback.
* **Submitted By Email** (Required): Enter your email address again to confirm your submission.
