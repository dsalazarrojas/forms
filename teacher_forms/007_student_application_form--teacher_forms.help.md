# Student Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students applying to a program or institution. It's meant to be filled out once per student to ensure efficient processing of their applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your student's information in the relevant fields, including their name, email, parent's name, class name (if applicable), program name (if applicable), and application date.
2.  Ensure that you fill in the required fields correctly, indicated by the "(Required)" label in the field explanations below.
3.  Fill in your student's contact information, including their phone number and email (if applicable).
4.  Finally, fill in your own contact information as the teacher or representative submitting the application on behalf of the student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, Required): Enter the full name of the student applying.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, Optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, Optional): Enter the parent's or guardian's name.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the student's class (if applicable).
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the program the student is applying to.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date of the application submission.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, Optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name as the teacher or representative submitting the application.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, Optional): Enter the student's address.
* **Student City** (`student_city`, text, Optional): Enter the city where the student lives.
* **Student State** (`student_state`, text, Optional): Enter the state where the student lives.
* **Student Postal Code** (`student_postal_code`, text, Optional): Enter the student's postal code or zip code.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, Optional): Enter the parent's or guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, Optional): Enter the parent's or guardian's phone number.
