# Parent Permission Form For Field Trip - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request permission from parents for their child to participate in a field trip. It is typically filled out by the teacher leading the trip and submitted to the parents for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the field trip details in the "Field Trip Details" field.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
4. Enter the student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
5. Enter the parent's phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
6. Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
7. Enter the parent's email address in the "Parent Email" field.
8. Enter the school name in the "School Name" field.
9. Select the trip dates in the "Trip Dates" field.
10. Select the trip time in the "Trip Time" field.
11. Check the "Permission Given" box if permission has been granted.
12. If permission has been denied, enter the reason for denial in the "Reason for Denied Permission" field.
13. Sign and date the form with your teacher signature in the "Teacher Signature" field.
14. Sign and date the form with the parent signature in the "Parent Signature" field.
15. Sign and date the form with the school signature in the "School Signature" field.
16. Enter your name as the teacher in the "Teacher Name" field.
17. Date the form in the "Date" field.
18. Enter the trip location in the "Trip Location" field.
19. Enter the school address in the "School Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Permission Form** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the field trip.
* **Field Trip Details** (text, required): Enter the details of the field trip, including the destination and activities.
* **Student Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the student participating in the field trip.
* **Parent Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Student Phone** (text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Parent Phone** (text, optional): Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Student Email** (text, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Parent Email** (text, optional): Enter the parent's email address.
* **School Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the school.
* **Trip Dates** (date, optional): Select the dates of the field trip.
* **Trip Time** (time, optional): Select the time of the field trip.
* **Permission Given** (select_one, optional): Check the box if permission has been granted.
* **Reason for Denied Permission** (text, optional): Enter the reason for denied permission.
* **Teacher Signature** (text, optional): Sign and date the form with your teacher signature.
* **Parent Signature** (text, optional): Sign and date the form with the parent signature.
* **School Signature** (text, optional): Sign and date the form with the school signature.
* **Teacher Name** (text, optional): Enter your name as the teacher.
* **Date** (date, optional): Date the form.
* **Trip Location** (text, optional): Enter the location of the field trip.
* **School Address** (text, optional): Enter the address of the school.
