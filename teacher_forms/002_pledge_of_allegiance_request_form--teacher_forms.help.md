# Pledge Of Allegiance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about Pledge Of Allegiance requests from teachers in a structured and organized manner. It helps to improve communication and streamline the process of tracking and managing pledge requests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have a legitimate reason for submitting a Pledge Of Allegiance request.
2. If you're unsure about any of the fields, it's best to consult with the relevant department or supervisor.
3. Fill out the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School ID** (`school_id`, number, required: false): Enter the school ID number for the school related to your pledge request.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as a teacher.
* **Date Of Pledge** (`date_of_pledge`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the pledge in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time Of Pledge** (`time_of_pledge`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the pledge in the format hh:mm AM/PM.
* **Location Of Pledge** (`location_of_pledge`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the pledge was made, such as a specific room or building.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your pledge request.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Pledge Request Status** (`pledge_request_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your pledge request from the options: Active, Inactive, Pending.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or details about your pledge request.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Select the tool assigned to your pledge request from the options: chatjimmy, chatbot, other.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required: false): Enter the file name or link of the output file related to your pledge request.

Please note that some fields may not be required, but providing accurate and relevant information will help ensure that your request is processed correctly. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the relevant department or supervisor.
