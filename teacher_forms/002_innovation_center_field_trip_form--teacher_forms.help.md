# innovation_center_field_trip_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students, likely to sign up or register for an activity, event, or program related to an innovation center. The form gathers general information about the student, such as name, contact details, and grade level.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name fields. These are your basic name details.
2. Select your grade level from the dropdown list. This will help us know which group you belong to.
3. (Optional) Enter your Email address for further communication. 
4. (Optional) Enter your Phone number for emergency contact.
5. (Optional) Enter your School Code, this is your school's unique code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here. This will be displayed in our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here. This will be displayed in our records.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Choose your current grade level from the dropdown list. This will help us know which group you belong to.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for any updates or information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want us to have it on record for emergency contact.
* **School Code** (`school_code`, text, optional): Enter your school's code, usually provided by your school administration.
