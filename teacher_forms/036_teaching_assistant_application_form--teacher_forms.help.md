# Teaching Assistant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals interested in becoming teaching assistants. This form helps to gather relevant data from applicants, including their background, skills, and availability, to facilitate the evaluation and consideration of their applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information as requested on the **Applicant Information** page.
2. Enter your contact details on the **Contact Info** page.
3. Share your work experience and relevant details on the **Work History** page.
4. Outline your educational background and qualifications on the **Education Background** page.
5. List your professional references, if applicable.
6. Describe your skills and abilities on the **Skills** page.
7. Mention your availability to start working as a teaching assistant on the **Availability** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicants_info`, text, **optional**): Please provide your name and any other details you feel are relevant to your application.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, **optional**): Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Work History** (`work_history`, text, **optional**): Describe your previous work experience, including job titles, duration of employment, and relevant responsibilities.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, **optional**): Outline your educational background, including institutions attended, degrees earned, and relevant coursework.
* **References** (`references`, text, **optional**): If applicable, list your professional references and their contact information.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, **optional**): List your skills and abilities that align with the teaching assistant role.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, **optional**): Mention your availability to start working as a teaching assistant, including specific dates or timescales.
