# Teacher Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this Teacher Recommendation Form is to enable teachers to submit a recommendation for a student, providing valuable insights about their abilities and character.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the student's name in the "student_name" field.
* Enter the student's email address in the "student_email" field.
* Enter the student's school name in the "school_name" field.
* Select the student's grade level in the "student_grade" field.
* Sign the form with your name in the "teacher_signature" field.
* Select your title as a teacher in the "teacher_title" field.
* Indicate your years of service in the "teacher_years_of_service" field.
* Select your department in the "teacher_department" field.
* Provide a comment about the student in the "teacher_comment" field.
* Select the student's attendance status in the "student_status" field.
* Select the student's status of the recommendation as "Complete" or "Incomplete" in the "teacher_recommendation_status" field.
* Select the student's gender in the "student_gender" field.
* Enter the student's address in the "student_address" field.
* Enter the student's phone number in the "student_phone" field.
* Select the student's school in "student_previous_school" and "student_current_school".
* Enter the student's birth date in the "student_birth_date" field.
* Select the date for "teacher_signature_date" and "teacher_recommendation_date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name** (text, optional): Enter the student's name.
* **student_email** (email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **teacher_name** (text, optional): Enter your name as a teacher.
* **school_name** (text, optional): Enter the student's school name.
* **student_grade** (number, optional): Select the student's grade level.
* **teacher_signature** (text, optional): Sign the form with your name.
* **teacher_title** (select_one, optional): Select your title as a teacher.
* **teacher_years_of_service** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate your years of service.
* **teacher_department** (select_one, required): Select your department.
* **teacher_comment** (text, optional): Provide a comment about the student.
* **student_status** (select_one, required): Select the student's status.
* **student_previous_school** (text, optional): Enter the student's previous school.
* **student_current_school** (text, optional): Enter the student's current school.
* **student_gender** (select_one, required): Select the student's gender.
* **student_address** (text, optional): Enter the student's address.
* **student_phone** (text, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **student_birth_date** (date, optional): Enter the student's birth date.
* **student_attendance** (date, optional): Select the date for student attendance.
* **student_attendance** (time, optional): Select the time for student attendance.
* **teacher_recommendation_status** (select_one, required): Select the status of the recommendation as "Complete" or "Incomplete".
* **teacher_recommendation_date** (date, optional): Select the date for the teacher's recommendation.
* **teacher_recommendation** (text, optional): Enter the teacher's comment.
* **teacher_signature_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the teacher's signature.
* **student_attendance** (date, optional): Select the date for the student's attendance.
* **student_previous_school** (text, optional): Enter the student's previous school.
* **student_current_school** (text, optional): Enter the student's current school.

Note: Please ensure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
