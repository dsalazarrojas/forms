# Application Form For University - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Application Form For University is used to collect personal and educational information from applicants to our university. This information is used to assess the applicant's eligibility and qualifications for admission to our university.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Gather Required Documents
Please ensure that you have all the necessary documents and information ready before starting to fill out the form. This includes your legal name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and mailing address.

### Step 2: Fill Out Each Section
The form is divided into sections, each of which corresponds to a different area of information. Please read the explanations for each section carefully and fill out the form accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Name** (Field 2): Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Date of Birth** (Field 3): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (Field 4): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (Field 5): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Mailing Address** (Field 6): Enter your permanent address where you can receive mail and packages.
* **Secondary School Name** (Field 8): Enter the name of the secondary school you attended.
* **Year of Completion** (Field 9): Enter the year you completed your secondary education.
* **Grade Point Average** (Field 10): Enter your GPA from your secondary school.
* **Standardized Test Results** (Field 11): Enter the results of any standardized tests you have taken, such as SAT or ACT scores.
* **Degree Level Sought** (Field 13): Select the degree level you are seeking, such as Bachelor of Science or Graduate Degree.
* **Field of Study** (Field 14): Enter the field of study you are interested in.
* **Why do you want to study at our university?** (Field 17): Explain why you are interested in studying at our university.
* **What are your career goals?** (Field 18): Describe your future career goals.
* **Highlight any special achievements or awards** (Field 19): Describe any special achievements or awards you have received.
* **Extracurricular involvement and leadership** (Field 20): Describe any extracurricular activities or leadership roles you have held.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact our university's admissions office for clarification.
- Double-check your work before submitting the form to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.
