# Teacher Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Engagement Survey is a tool used to gather information about how engaged teachers are with their school, colleagues, and the overall school environment. This form is likely used by school administrators or HR departments to assess the level of engagement among teachers and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the **What is your name** field.
2. Enter your school's location in the **school location** field.
3. Provide the address of your school in the **school address** field.
4. Enter your school's phone number in the **school phone** field.
5. Provide your school's email address in the **school email** field.
6. Select your level of engagement with the school on a scale from 1-10 in the **teacher engagement level** field.
7. Indicate the level of support you receive from your colleagues on a scale from 1-10 in the **level of support** field.
8. Choose the communication level you prefer to receive from school administrators on a scale from High, Medium, or Low in the **communication level** field.
9. Finally, type any additional feedback you have in the **feedback message** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (`teacher_engagement_survey`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your school ID card or personnel profile.
* **school location** (`school_location`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your school, e.g., "John F. Kennedy High School".
* **school address** (`school_location_address`, text, required: false): Enter the physical address of your school, including street number and postal code.
* **school phone** (`school_location_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your school's phone number.
* **school email** (`school_location_email`, text, required: false): Enter your school's email address.
* **teacher engagement level** (`teacher_engagement`, number, required: false): On a scale from 1-10, how engaged do you feel with your school and your role?
* **level of support** (`teacher_support`, number, required: false): On a scale from 1-10, how much support do you receive from your colleagues and school administrators?
* **communication level** (`communication_level`, select_multiple, required: false): How do you prefer to receive communication from school administrators? (High, Medium, or Low)
* **feedback message** (`feedback_message`, text, required: false): Any additional feedback or suggestions you have about the school or your role?
