# E Learning Student Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Learning Student Performance Evaluation Form is used to collect data on student progress, submission dates and times, and other relevant information that helps to assess a student's performance and identify areas where improvement is needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form after each assignment submission.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter the date of the last submission in the "Date of last submission" field.
4. Enter the time spent on the task in the "Time spent on task" field.
5. Enter the score earned in the "Score earned" field.
6. Provide submission comments in the "Submission comments" field.
7. Indicate student progress in the "Student progress" field.
8. Select the submission status in the "Submission status" field.
9. Select the submission date and time in the "Submission date" and "Submission time" fields.
10. Enter submission comments in the "Submission Comments 2" field.
11. Indicate student performance in the "Student performance" field.
12. Enter teacher comments in the "Teacher comments" field.
13. Enter the student's ID in the "Student ID" field.
14. Enter the assignment ID in the "Assignment ID" field.
15. Enter the course ID in the "Course ID" field.
16. Select the submission status 2 in the "Submission Status 2" field.
17. Enter the time spent on task in the "Time on task" field.
18. Select the time on task status in the "Time on task status" field.
19. Select the task status in the "Task status" field.
20. Select the submission time 2 in the "Submission Time 2" field.
21. Enter the submission datetime in the "Submission datetime" field.
22. Select the student status in the "Student status" field.
23. Enter the teacher comments 2 in the "Teacher Comments 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Date of last submission** (`date_of_last_submission`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the last submission.
* **Time spent on task** (`time_spent_on_task`, `number`, required): Enter the time spent on the task.
* **Score earned** (`score_earned`, `number`, required): Enter the score earned by the student.
* **Submission comments** (`submission_comments`, `text`, required): Provide comments about the submission.
* **Student progress** (`student_progress`, `select_one`, required): Indicate student progress (Yes, No, Other).
* **Submission status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the submission status (Yes, No, Other).
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the submission date.
* **Submission time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required): Enter the submission time.
* **Submission status 2** (`submission_status_2`, `select_one`, required): Select the submission status 2 (Yes, No, Other).
* **Submission Time 2** (`submission_time_2`, `time`, required): Enter the submission time 2.
* **Submission datetime** (`submission_datetime`, `date`, required): Enter the submission datetime.
* **Student status** (`student_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the student status (Yes, No, Other).
* **Teacher comments** (`teacher_comments`, `text`, required): Provide comments from the teacher.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, required): Enter the student ID.
* **Assignment ID** (`assignment_id`, `text`, required): Enter the assignment ID.
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, `text`, required): Enter the course ID.
* **Time on task** (`time_on_task`, `time`, required): Enter the time spent on the task.
* **Time on task status** (`time_on_task_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the time on task status (Yes, No, Other).
* **Task status** (`task_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the task status (Yes, No, Other).
* **Student performance** (`student_performance`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate student performance (Yes, No, Other).
* **Teacher Comments 2** (`teacher_comments_2`, `text`, required): Provide comments from the teacher.
