# School Laboratory Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture order details and other essential information from users for laboratory supplies. It has four pages: "Order Details", "Equipment", "Delivery Information", and "Confirmation". The form is intended for teachers and staff to record their laboratory supply orders in a centralized platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Order Details" page and fill in the required fields, including the "School Laboratory Supply Order Form" title and any additional information you need to provide.
2. On the "Equipment" page, select "Yes" or "No" for each equipment type to indicate if you need it or not.
3. On the "Delivery Information" page, enter any additional information about your delivery preferences.
4. On the "Confirmation" page, confirm your order by selecting "Yes" or "No".
5. Finally, add any additional information you have on the "Additional Information" page, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Laboratory Supply Order Form** (`title`, required): This is the title of your order, which should be a brief description of the items you are ordering.
* **Order Details** (`school_laboratory_supply_order_form_page_1`, text, required): Enter any additional details about your order here.
* **Equipment** (`school_laboratory_supply_order_form_page_2`, select_multiple, required): Indicate which equipment you need by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option.
* **Delivery Information** (`school_laboratory_supply_order_form_page_3`, text, required): Provide any additional information about your delivery preferences here.
* **Confirmation** (`school_laboratory_supply_order_form_page_4`, select_one, required): Confirm your order by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Additional Information** (`school_laboratory_supply_order_form_page_5`, note, optional): Enter any other comments or information you want to provide about your order.
