# Virtual Field Trip Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Field Trip Registration Form is used to collect information from participants for a virtual field trip. This form aims to streamline the registration process and gather all necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address (if required).
2. Select one option from the "Select One" field.
3. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field (if required).
4. If applicable, choose a Date and Time for the field trip.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
6. Enter your Phone Number (if required).
7. Finally, click the submit button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (required/optional): Enter your first name. This field is used to identify you by your given name.
* **Last Name** (required/optional): Enter your last name. This field is used to identify you by your surname.
* **Email** (required/optional): Enter your valid email address. This field is used to contact you regarding the field trip.
* **Phone Number** (required/optional): Enter your phone number. This field is used in case we need to contact you directly.
* **Select One** (required/optional): Choose one option from the provided list. This field is used to gather specific information about your preferences.
* **Select Multiple** (required/optional): Choose one or more options from the provided list. This field is used to gather specific information about your preferences.
* **Date** (required/optional): Choose a date for the field trip. This field is used to schedule the trip.
* **Time** (required/optional): Choose a time for the field trip. This field is used to schedule the trip in conjunction with the date.
* **Note** (required/optional): Add any additional comments or details about your participation. This field is used to capture any extra information that may be relevant to your registration.
* **Phone Number** (required/optional): Enter your phone number. This field is used to contact you directly.
