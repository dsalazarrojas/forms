# Who Am I Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Who Am I Quiz form is a simple tool designed to collect information about a person. It appears to be a straightforward survey asking about a person's age, email, phone number, teacher's information, and other characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the quiz, simply follow these steps:

1. Follow the instructions provided on each page to complete each section.
2. For the first page, select a response to the "Who am I" question.
3. Enter your answer to the "What is your answer?" question.
4. Provide your age.
5. Optional sections are for your teacher's name and gender.
6. Review your input for accuracy before proceeding.

* **Who am I** (`who_am_i`, Select One, required: false): This field is asking you to select whether you are a teacher or not.
* **What is your answer?** (`answer`, text, required: false): Enter a brief answer to the question about who you are.
* **How old are you** (`age`, number, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Age hint** (`age_hint`, note, required: false): This field is not used in the current implementation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **What is your teacher's name?** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): If you are a teacher, enter your name.
* **How old is your teacher?** (`teacher_age`, number, required: false): Enter your teacher's age, if applicable.
* **What is your gender?** (`gender`, select multiple, required: false): Select all applicable answers (e.g., Male, Female, Other, etc.).
