# Student Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Course Evaluation Form is a tool used to collect feedback from students about their experiences in a course. It helps instructors and course administrators understand student perceptions and opinions about the course's quality, materials, and overall experience. By filling out this form, you will be providing valuable insights that can inform future course improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `teacher` field with the name of your instructor.
2. Select your overall satisfaction with the course materials using the `Evaluation Scale` field.
3. Provide a brief comment about your overall experience in the `overall_experience` field.
4. If you have any specific feedback about the course materials, you can enter it in the `course_materials` field.
5. Finally, you can add any additional comments or thoughts about the course in the `student_course_evaluation_form` field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your instructor who taught the course.
* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, select_one, optional): Select a number from 1 to 5 to indicate your overall satisfaction with the course materials.
* **Course** (`course_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the course you took.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about your overall experience in the course.
* **Course Materials** (`course_materials`, text, optional): Share any feedback about the course materials you found useful or not.
* **Student Course Evaluation** (`student_course_evaluation_form`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about the course.

## Tips
- Be honest and provide specific feedback about your experiences.
- Keep your comments brief and concise.
- If you have any suggestions for improving the course, feel free to mention them in the `overall_experience` field.
