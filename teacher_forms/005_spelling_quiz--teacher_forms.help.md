# Spelling Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spelling Quiz form is designed to assess student's spelling skills, providing a structured format for teachers to collect and analyze data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Spelling Quiz form, follow these steps:

1. **Student Information**: Enter the student's name and email address on pages 1 and 6.
2. **Quiz Information**: On page 2, provide a list of words for the student to spell. On page 12, enter the correct answers to each word.
3. **Grading and Feedback**: On pages 3 and 4, select the correct and incorrect answers, and specify the grading criteria on page 16. You can also provide feedback and grading scale on page 18.
4. **Teacher Information**: On pages 6 and 7, enter your name and email address.
5. **Additional Details**: On pages 8, 9, and 10, enter the class name, subject, and grade level.
6. **Submit**: Once all fields are filled in, submit the form to review and analyze the data collected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name.
* **2. List of words to spell** (`spelling_list`, note, not required): Provide a list of words for the student to spell.
* **3. Answer Key** (`answer_key`, note, required): Enter the correct answers to each word.
* **4. Grading Options** (`grading`, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the student's answers are correct, partially correct, or incorrect.
* **5. Student Email** (`student_email`, email, not required): Enter the student's email address.
* **6. Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **7. Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **8. School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the school name.
* **9. Class Name** (`class_name`, text, required): Enter the class name.
* **10. Subject** (`subject`, text, required): Enter the subject.
* **11. Grade Level** (`grade`, select_one, not required): Select the grade level of the class.
* **12. Word List** (`word_list`, note, required): Enter the correct spelling of each word.
* **13. Student Answer** (`student_answer`, select_multiple, not required): Select whether the student's answers are correct or incorrect.
* **14. Correct Answers** (`correct_answers`, select_multiple, not required): Select the correct answers.
* **15. Incorrect Answers** (`incorrect_answers`, select_multiple, not required): Select the incorrect answers.
* **16. Grading Criteria** (`grading_criteria`, select_multiple, not required): Select the grading criteria.
* **17. Grading Scale** (`grading_scale`, select_multiple, not required): Select the grading scale.
* **18. Feedback** (`feedback`, select_multiple, not required): Select whether feedback is required or not.

Note: The "Select Multiple" type fields allow you to select more than one option at a time.
