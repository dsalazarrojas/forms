# Elementary School T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elementary School T Shirt Order Form is designed to collect information from teachers and students at elementary schools to process T-shirt orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the teacher you would like to order a T-shirt for.
2. Select the student(s) you would like to order a T-shirt for.
3. Choose the size of the T-shirt.
4. Choose the color of the T-shirt.
5. Enter the quantity of T-shirts to order.
6. Select the payment method (Card, PayPal, or Cash).
7. Enter the card number, expiration date, and expiration year for credit card payments.
8. Enter the CVV (Card Verification Value) for credit card payments.
9. Enter the ZIP (Zoning Improvement Plan) code for credit card payments.
10. Enter the student's name.
11. Enter the student's age.
12. Enter the teacher's name.
13. Enter the teacher's email address.
14. Enter the student's email address.
15. Enter the student's phone number.
16. Enter the teacher's phone number.
17. Select the teacher's school.
18. Select the school location.
19. Enter the order date.
20. Enter the teacher's salary (for internal use only).
21. Enter the total cost of the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher** (Teacher 1, Teacher 2, Teacher 3): 
  Select the teacher you would like to order a T-shirt for.
* **Student** (Student 1, Student 2, Student 3): 
  Select the student(s) you would like to order a T-shirt for.
* **T-shirt Size** (Small, Medium, Large): 
  Choose the size of the T-shirt.
* **T-shirt Color** (Red, Blue, Green): 
  Choose the color of the T-shirt.
* **Quantity**: 
  Enter the number of T-shirts to order.
* **Payment Method** (Card, PayPal, Cash): 
  Select the payment method for the order.
* **Card Number** ('1234', '5678', '9012): 
  Enter the credit card number for card payments.
* **Expiration Date**: 
  Enter the expiration date of the credit card.
* **Expiration Year** ('2024', '2025', '2026): 
  Enter the expiration year of the credit card.
* **CVV** ('1', '2', '3): 
  Enter the Card Verification Value for credit card payments.
* **ZIP**: 
  Enter the Zoning Improvement Plan code for credit card payments.
* **Student Name**: 
  Enter the student's name.
* **Student Age**: 
  Enter the student's age.
* **Teacher Name**: 
  Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Email**: 
  Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Student Email**: 
  Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone**: 
  Enter the student's phone number.
* **Teacher Phone**: 
  Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher School**: 
  Select the teacher's school.
* **School Location**: 
  Select the school location.
* **Order Date**: 
  Enter the order date.
* **Teacher Salary**: 
  Enter the teacher's salary (for internal use only).
