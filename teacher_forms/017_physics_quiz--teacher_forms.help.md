# Physics Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Physics Quiz form is designed to collect information from teachers about their physics-related activities, lessons, or projects. The form aims to gather data on the effectiveness and interest in the topics being taught, as well as the instructors' opinions on the subject matter.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter a brief title** for your physics quiz or project (field **physics_quiz_form**).
2. **Enter questions** related to the physics topic you are teaching or researching (fields **question_1** to **question_5**).
3. **Enter corresponding answers** for each question (fields **answer_1** to **answer_5**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **physics_quiz_form**: 
  **Title** (`text`, `physics_quiz_form`, optional): Enter a brief title for your physics quiz or project.
* **question_1**: 
  **Question 1** (`text`, `question_1`, optional): Enter the first question related to the physics topic you are teaching or researching.
* **answer_1**: 
  **Answer 1** (`text`, `answer_1`, optional): Enter the answer or response to question 1.
* **question_2**: 
  **Question 2** (`text`, `question_2`, optional): Enter the second question related to the physics topic you are teaching or researching.
* **answer_2**: 
  **Answer 2** (`text`, `answer_2`, optional): Enter the answer or response to question 2.
* **question_3**: 
  **Question 3** (`text`, `question_3`, optional): Enter the third question related to the physics topic you are teaching or researching.
* **answer_3**: 
  **Answer 3** (`text`, `answer_3`, optional): Enter the answer or response to question 3.
* **question_4**: 
  **Question 4** (`text`, `question_4`, optional): Enter the fourth question related to the physics topic you are teaching or researching.
* **answer_4**: 
  **Answer 4** (`text`, `answer_4`, optional): Enter the answer or response to question 4.
* **question_5**: 
  **Question 5** (`text`, `question_5`, optional): Enter the fifth question related to the physics topic you are teaching or researching.
* **answer_5**: 
  **Answer 5** (`text`, `answer_5`, optional): Enter the answer or response to question 5.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide clear and concise questions and answers to get the most accurate feedback.
* If you need more space to elaborate, you can use the text fields to add any additional information.
* If you need help or have questions about the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
