# Distance Learning Feedback Form For Students - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their experience with the distance learning class. Your input will help the instructor to make the class better for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by the red asterisk *).
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. Choose your answers carefully from the options for select fields.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Distance Learning Class ID**: Enter the class ID number provided by the instructor.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name. 
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. 
* **Email**: Enter your email address. 
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number. 
* **Comments**: Share any thoughts or comments about the class.
* **Suggestions**: Suggest improvements or changes to the class.
* **Feedback**: Share any feedback about the class, such as its quality or the instructor's performance.
* **Class Rating**: Rate the class on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Would you take the class again?**: Choose 'Yes', 'No', or 'Maybe' based on your experience.
* **Would you recommend the class to a friend?**: Choose 'Yes', 'No', or 'Unsure' based on your experience.
* **Date**: Select the date you attended the class (optional).
* **Time**: Select the time you attended the class (optional).
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the class you attended.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the instructor who led the class.
* **Class Name**: Enter the name of the class you attended.
* **Comments 2**: Share any additional thoughts or comments about the class.
* **Suggestions 2**: Suggest improvements or changes to the class.
* **Feedback 2**: Share any additional feedback about the class.
* **Class Rating 2**: Rate the class on a scale from 1 to 5 (optional).
* **Would you take the class again? 2**: Choose 'Yes', 'No', or 'Maybe' based on your experience.
* **Would you recommend the class to a friend? 2**: Choose 'Yes', 'No', or 'Unsure' based on your experience.
