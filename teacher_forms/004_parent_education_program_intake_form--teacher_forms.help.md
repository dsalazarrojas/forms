# Parent Education Program Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Education Program Intake Form is a crucial document that helps us understand your child's needs and goals for the program. It is essential to provide accurate and personalized support for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined below:

1. Enter your first name in the **first name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **last name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.
5. Choose your preferred contact type from the **select type** field.
6. Enter the date you are available for the program in the **date** field.
7. Select your preferred program options from the **select multiple** field.
8. Provide any additional comments or notes about your child's needs and goals in the **note** field.
9. Enter your availability for the program in the **time** field.
10. Enter your email address again in the **email 2** field (this is a duplicate field for verification purposes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name to identify yourself as a parent or caregiver.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name to identify yourself as a parent or caregiver.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your child's program.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your child's program.
* **Select type** (`select_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, or in-person).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you are available for the program.
* **Select multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Choose which program options you are interested in.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Provide any additional comments or notes about your child's needs and goals.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter your availability for the program.
* **Email 2** (`email 2`, email, required): Enter your email address again for verification purposes.
