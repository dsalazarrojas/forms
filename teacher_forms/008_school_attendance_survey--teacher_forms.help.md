# School Attendance Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Attendance Survey is a form designed to collect information from students and teachers about a student's attendance in school. This form helps administrators and teachers track attendance and identify patterns or issues.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the student's name in the **Student Name** field.
2. Select the school where the student attends.
3. Enter the date of the attendance period or event (if applicable).
4. Fill in the teacher's name and email address (if applicable).
5. Check the **Student Was Absent** field to indicate if the student was absent on the specified date.
6. For students who were absent, select the reason for absence from the **Reason Absent** field.
7. Check the **Student Attendance** field to indicate the student's overall attendance status (e.g., yes, no, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their records.
* **School**: Select the school where the student is currently enrolled.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the attendance period or event (if applicable).
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the teacher's name who is responsible for the student's attendance (if applicable).
* **Teacher Email**: Enter the teacher's email address (if applicable).
* **Teacher Phone**: Enter the teacher's phone number (if applicable).
* **Student Attendance**: Check 'Yes' if the student attended, 'No' if they did not, or select another option if necessary.
* **Student Was Absent**: Check 'Yes' if the student was absent on the specified date, and select other options if necessary.
* **Reason Absent**: Select the reason why the student was absent (if applicable).
