# Digital Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Digital Awareness Survey" is designed to gather information from teachers on their awareness and perception of digital tools and technologies. This survey aims to collect data on their digital skills, attitude towards digital tools, and concerns or challenges they face while using digital tools.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "Survey Title" field with a brief description of the survey. This will help us understand the purpose and context of the survey.
2. Read the "Intro Message" field carefully, which provides background information about the survey.
3. For the "Awareness Level" field, select one of the two options - "High" or "Low", depending on your level of awareness about digital tools and technologies.
4. If required, provide your "Technology Familiarity" score by entering a numeric value.
5. If required, provide your "Behavior Tendency" score by entering a numeric value.
6. In the "Attitude Towards Digital" field, provide your honest opinion about your attitude towards digital tools and technologies.
7. For the "Digital Skills" field, select all the skills you possess, such as "Yes" for skills you are familiar with, and "No" for skills you are not familiar with.
8. If required, provide your "Digital Skills Level" score by entering a numeric value.
9. For the "Technology Adoption" field, select one of the two options - "Yes" or "No", depending on whether you have adopted digital tools in your teaching.
10. If required, select all the technologies you use by checking the boxes for "Yes".
11. In the "Concerns and Challenges" field, write down any concerns or challenges you face while using digital tools and technologies.
12. In the "Suggestions and Comments" field, provide any suggestions or comments you have about digital tools and technologies.
13. If required, provide your email address and phone number for follow-up purposes.
14. In the "Follow-up Message" field, type any further comments or messages you want to share.
15. If required, provide a date for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): A brief description of the survey.
* **Intro Message** (`intro_message`, text, optional): Background information about the survey.
* **Awareness Level** (`awareness_level`, select_one, optional): Select one of the two options - "High" or "Low", depending on your level of awareness about digital tools and technologies.
* **Technology Familiarity** (`technology_familiarity`, number, optional): A numeric score indicating your level of familiarity with digital tools and technologies.
* **Behavior Tendency** (`behavior_tendency`, number, optional): A numeric score indicating your tendency to use digital tools and technologies.
* **Attitude Towards Digital** (`attitude_towards_digital`, text, optional): Your honest opinion about your attitude towards digital tools and technologies.
* **Digital Skills** (`digital_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the skills you possess, such as "Yes" for skills you are familiar with, and "No" for skills you are not familiar with.
* **Digital Skills Level** (`digital_skills_level`, number, optional): A numeric score indicating your level of digital skills.
* **Technology Adoption** (`technology_adoption`, select_one, optional): Select one of the two options - "Yes" or "No", depending on whether you have adopted digital tools in your teaching.
* **Technology Usage** (`technology_usage`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the technologies you use.
* **Concerns and Challenges** (`concerns_and_challenges`, text, optional): Write down any concerns or challenges you face while using digital tools and technologies.
* **Suggestions and Comments** (`suggestions_and_comments`, note, optional): Provide any suggestions or comments you have about digital tools and technologies.
* **Contact and Follow-up** (`contact_and_follow_up`, email, optional): Provide your email address and phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Follow-up Message** (`follow_up_message`, note, optional): Type any further comments or messages you want to share.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Provide a date for follow-up purposes.
