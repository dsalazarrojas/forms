# trivia_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The trivia quiz form is designed to collect information about your preferences and interests. It's a fun and engaging way to learn more about yourself and others.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "what_is_your_name" field with your name.
2. Answer the three questions: "what_is_your_favorite_book", "what_is_your_favorite_color", and "what_is_your_favorite_food".
3. Make sure to fill out all the questions, even if they are not required, to get a more comprehensive understanding of yourself.
4. Once you've filled out all the questions, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what_is_your_name** (text, optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **what_is_your_favorite_book** (text, optional): Type the title of your favorite book, even if you've read it recently or not.
* **what_is_your_favorite_color** (text, optional): Choose your favorite color from your favorite color palette.
* **what_is_your_favorite_food** (text, optional): Select your favorite food item, whether it's a sweet or savory option.
