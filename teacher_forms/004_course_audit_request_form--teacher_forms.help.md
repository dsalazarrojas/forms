# Course Audit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Audit Request Form is used by students to request an audit for a course they have completed or are currently taking. This form helps instructors and administrators evaluate a student's progress and performance in the course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select "Course ID" to choose the course you want to request an audit for.
3. Enter your "Student Name" and "Student Email" (if provided).
4. Enter the "Reason for Audit" to explain why you are requesting an audit.
5. Choose the "Faculty Approvals" and "Student Approval" options as instructed by your instructor.
6. Select "Educational Institution Approval" as required by your institution.
7. Enter the "Date Requested" and "Date Completed" for the audit.
8. If applicable, provide "Faculty Comments" and "Student Comments" for additional information.
9. Set the "Status" of the audit to "Approved", "Denied", or "In Progress".
10. Enter the "Form Submission ID" for tracking purposes.
11. Select the "Assigned To" option to choose the person responsible for reviewing the audit.
12. If desired, enter "Assigned User", "Comments", "Date Reviewed", and "Reviewd By" for tracking and follow-up purposes.
13. If required, enter "Faculty Signature", "Student Signature", "Educational Institution Signature", and "Form Signature" to confirm completion of the audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course ID**: Enter the ID number of the course you are requesting an audit for.
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as a student.
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course you are requesting an audit for.
* **Reason for Audit**: Explain why you are requesting an audit for the course. This will help the instructor understand your request.
* **Faculty Approvals**: Select the approval option as instructed by your instructor.
* **Student Approval**: Select the approval option as instructed by your instructor.
* **Educational Institution Approval**: Select the approval option as required by your institution.
* **Date Requested**: Enter the date you are requesting the audit for.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date the audit was completed.
* **Faculty Comments**: If applicable, enter any comments from the instructor about your performance.
* **Student Comments**: If applicable, enter any comments from you about the audit process.
* **Status**: Set the status of the audit to "Approved", "Denied", or "In Progress".
* **Form Submission ID**: Enter the ID number of the form submission for tracking purposes.
* **Assigned To**: Select the person responsible for reviewing the audit.
* **Assigned User**: Enter the name of the person assigned to review the audit.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the audit process.
* **Date Reviewed**: Enter the date the audit was reviewed.
* **Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the audit.
* **Faculty Signature**: If required, enter the instructor's signature to confirm completion of the audit.
* **Student Signature**: If required, enter your signature to confirm completion of the audit.
* **Educational Institution Signature**: If required, enter your institution's signature to confirm completion of the audit.
* **Form Signature**: If required, enter a signature to confirm completion of the audit.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time**: Enter the time the form was submitted.

Note: Some fields may not be required depending on the specific use case or institution's requirements. It's essential to review the form carefully and follow any additional guidelines provided by your instructor or institution.
