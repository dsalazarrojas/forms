# teacher_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The teacher satisfaction survey is designed to collect feedback from teachers on their experience and satisfaction with certain aspects of their work environment at the school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page and field carefully to ensure you have provided accurate information.
2. Answer each question truthfully and concisely, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, denoted by asterisk (*).
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School environment
* School environment (1): Describe the overall environment at the school, including facilities, equipment, and resources available to you.

### Communication with admin
* Communication with admin (2): How would you rate your experience with communication from school administrators? (e.g. email, phone, meetings)

### Resources provided
* Resources provided (3): What resources (e.g. technology, training, materials) have you found to be helpful or lacking in your work at the school?

### Feedback
* Feedback (4): Do you have any suggestions or comments about your experience at the school?

### Support from colleagues
* Support from colleagues (5): How would you rate the level of support you receive from your colleagues?

### Support from admin
* Support from admin (6): How would you rate the level of support you receive from school administrators?

### Time management
* Time management (7): How would you rate the level of time management support and structure at the school?

### Teacher role
* Teacher role (8): How would you rate the clarity of expectations and responsibilities of your teacher role?

### Overall satisfaction
* Overall satisfaction (9): On a scale of 0-10, rate your overall satisfaction with your role and work environment at the school.

### Communication with students
* Communication with students (10): How would you rate the level of communication and engagement with students in your classes?

Note: The numbers in parentheses represent the ID of the field on the form.
