# Lecture Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The **Lecture Evaluation Form** is a tool used by instructors and staff to evaluate lectures. This form provides a structured format for giving feedback on a lecture, allowing evaluators to provide specific and detailed information about their assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form on all specified pages (in this case, page 1, which is the only page).
2.  Read each section carefully and answer the questions as completely as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Introduction
* **Introduction** (`lecture_evaluation_form_1`, text, optional): Briefly introduce your comments and thoughts about the lecture. This is the starting point for your assessment.

### Page 2: Content
* **Content** (`lecture_evaluation_form_2`, select multiple, optional): Indicate whether the content of the lecture was clear and coherent. Choose all the options that apply:
	+ **Yes**: The content was clear and easy to follow.
	+ **No**: The content was unclear or difficult to understand.

### Page 3: Speaker
* **Speaker** (`lecture_evaluation_form_3`, select one, optional): Evaluate the speaker's presentation skills. Choose one of the following options:
	+ **Yes**: The speaker was engaging and presented the material effectively.
	+ **No**: The speaker did not present the material effectively.

### Tips
* Please be specific with your feedback, focusing on the strengths and weaknesses of the lecture.
* If you have suggestions for improvement, include them in the **Introduction** field.
* Remember to save your evaluation before submitting, so you can review your answers later.
* If a section is not applicable, you don't need to provide an answer for that section.
