# Academy Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Academy Application Form" is a document that helps us understand a student's needs and gather essential information for processing their application to our academy.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Please fill out each field carefully and accurately, and make sure to provide all required information.
2. If a field is marked as "Required," you must fill it out.
3. If a field is marked as "Optional," you can fill it out if you feel it's relevant and helpful for your application.
4. Please make sure to fill out each field according to the hints provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`Student Full Name`, `text`, Required): Please enter your legal name as it appears on your birth certificate.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, Required): Please enter your date of birth to verify your age.
* **Grade Level Applying For** (`Grade Level Applying For`, `select_one`, Required): Please select the grade level you are applying for (e.g., Preschool, Kindergarten, Grade 1-5, etc.).
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`Parent or Guardian Full Name`, `text`, Required): Please enter the full name of your parent or guardian who will be the primary contact person.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`Parent or Guardian Email`, `email`, Required): Please enter the email address of your parent or guardian for admissions correspondence.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`Parent or Guardian Phone`, `text`, Required): Please enter the daytime contact number of your parent or guardian.
* **Residential Address** (`Residential Address`, `text`, Required): Please enter your full street address, city, and state.
* **Name of Previous School Attended** (`Name of Previous School Attended`, `text`, Optional): Please enter the name of the school you previously attended if applicable.
* **Primary Reason for Leaving Previous School** (`Primary Reason for Leaving Previous School`, `select_one`, Required): Please select the primary reason for leaving your previous school (e.g., Relocation, Seeking Better Programs, Graduation, or Other).
* **List any Academic Awards or Honors** (`List any academic awards or honors`, `text`, Optional): Please list any academic awards or honors you have received.
* **Interests outside of Academics** (`Interests outside of academics`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Please select all interests outside of academics that apply to you (e.g., Music, Sports, Arts, Coding, Debate, Drama).
* **List any Known Allergies or Medical Conditions** (`List any known allergies or medical conditions`, `text`, Optional): Please list any known allergies or medical conditions that are important for student safety.
* **Does the student require special education services?** (`Does the student require special education services?`, `select_one`, Required): Please select 'True' if the student requires special education services (e.g., IEP or 504 plan).
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`Parent or Guardian Signature`, `text`, Required): Please type your full legal name as the parent or guardian.
* **Application Date** (`Application Date`, `date`, Required): Please select the current date.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and carefully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your parent or guardian for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our academy's admissions team.
