# Teacher Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Observation Form is used by administrators and observers to collect data on teacher performance and practice in the classroom.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form for each teacher you observe, ensuring that the information is accurate and specific to the teacher and the lesson or class period observed.
2. Enter the teacher's name and subject in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the date and time of the observation.
4. Describe the practice areas observed, such as instructional strategies, classroom management, and overall teaching quality.
5. Choose a rating for each of the three practice areas (Rating 1, Rating 2, and Rating 3).
6. Provide additional comments and action plans for the teacher.
7. Select the rating for the teacher's overall performance.
8. Enter any additional comments or follow-up actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher** (`teacher_observation_form`): Enter the name of the teacher being observed.
* **Subject** (`subject`): Select the subject(s) taught by the teacher.
* **Observation Date** (`observation_date`): Enter the date of the observation.
* **Practice Area Observed** (`practice_area_observed`): Describe the specific areas of practice observed during the lesson or class period.
* **Observation Time** (`observation_time`): Enter the time of the observation.
* **Rating 1** (`rating_1`): Choose a rating for the first practice area observed (Excellent, Good, or Needs Improvement).
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`): Choose a rating for the second practice area observed (Excellent, Good, or Needs Improvement).
* **Rating 3** (`rating_3`): Choose a rating for the third practice area observed (Excellent, Good, or Needs Improvement).
* **Observation Notes** (`observation_notes`): Provide any additional notes or comments about the observation.
* **Teacher Rating** (`teacher_rating`): Choose a rating for the teacher's overall performance (Excellent, Good, or Needs Improvement).
* **Action Plan** (`action_plan`): Provide any specific actions or steps the teacher can take to improve practice areas.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`): Enter any follow-up actions or steps.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`): Enter the teacher's ID number.
* **School Year** (`school_year`): Enter the school year of the observation.
* **Semester** (`semester`): Select the semester of the observation (Fall, Spring, or Summer).
* **Teacher Rating Average** (`teacher_rating_average`): Choose a rating for the teacher's average performance (3-3.5, 3.6-4, or Less than 3).
* **Teacher Rating Count** (`teacher_rating_count`): Enter the count of ratings given.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Last Updated By** (`last_updated_by`): Enter the name of the person who last updated the form.
* **Last Updated By ID** (`last_updated_by_id`): Enter the ID number of the person who last updated the form.
* **Last Updated By User** (`last_updated_by_user`): Enter the name of the person who last updated the form.
