# Presentation Peer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Presentation Peer Feedback Form is designed to collect constructive feedback from peers on presentations made during meetings, workshops, or other group discussions. This form helps presenters identify areas of improvement and make their presentations more effective.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, select the Presentation Peer Feedback Form from the available options.
- Enter your rating by selecting one of the provided options: Excellent, Satisfactory, or Needs improvement.
- Write any additional feedback or comments in the text area.
- Enter the presenter's name as it appears on their presentation materials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Presentation Peer Feedback Form** (`presentation_peer_feedback_form`, Text, Optional): This is the main form section where you can enter your feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, Number, Optional): Select the overall rating of the presentation, using the options: Excellent, Satisfactory, or Needs improvement.
* **Scale** (`rating_scale`, Select One, Optional): This is a more detailed scale for evaluating the presentation's quality. Choose from: Excellent, Satisfactory, or Needs improvement.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions for the presenter.
* **Presenter Name** (`presenter_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the presenter's name as it appears on their presentation materials.

## Tips
- Be honest and constructive with your feedback.
- Keep your comments concise and relevant.
- Use the scale and rating system to provide a clear and consistent evaluation.
- If the presenter has multiple presentations, provide feedback for each presentation separately.
