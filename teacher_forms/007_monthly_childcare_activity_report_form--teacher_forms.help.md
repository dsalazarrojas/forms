# Monthly Childcare Activity Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help childcare providers report and record their daily activities during a month. It gathers information on individual child names, email addresses, time spent with children, and other relevant details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First name and Last name fields with your actual name.
2. Provide your Email address for easy contact and record-keeping purposes.
3. Select the Date of the reported month (if applicable).
4. Enter the Start Time and End Time of your daily activities with the children.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text, optional): Enter your actual first name.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your actual last name.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for contact and record-keeping purposes.
* **Date** (date, optional): Select the date of the month you are reporting for (if you are reporting for a specific month).
* **Start Time** (time, optional): Enter the start time of your daily activities with the children.
* **End Time** (time, optional): Enter the end time of your daily activities with the children.
