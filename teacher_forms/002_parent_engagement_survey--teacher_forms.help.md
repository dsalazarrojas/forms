# parent_engagement_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parent_engagement_survey form is designed to collect information from parents about their involvement and communication preferences with a school or educational institution. This form is meant to gather feedback and understand parent engagement, helping schools and institutions tailor their approach to better serve parents and students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each field thoroughly and accurately to ensure the collected data is reliable and useful.
2. You may skip fields that do not apply to you.
3. Ensure you are submitting the form for its intended purpose and not for other reasons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_is_your_role** (Text): 
	+ Please enter your role as a parent or guardian (e.g., mother, father, caregiver).
* **what_is_your_name** (Text): 
	+ Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **what_is_your_contact_info** (Text): 
	+ Provide your contact information (e.g., email, phone number).
* **how_do_you_wish_to_receive_news** (Select Multiple):
	+ Choose how you would like to receive news and updates (Email, Text, Phone, None).
* **what_is_your_best_method_of_communication** (Select Multiple):
	+ Select your preferred method of communication (Email, Text, Phone, In-person).
* **parent_involvement** (Select One):
	+ Indicate your level of involvement in your child's education (Very involved, Somewhat involved, Not involved).
* **frequency_of_communication** (Select One):
	+ Choose how often you would like to be communicated with (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).

## Tips
- Ensure you have filled out the form accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the school or institution's administration.
- Your feedback and input are invaluable to helping us better understand parent engagement and improve our services.
