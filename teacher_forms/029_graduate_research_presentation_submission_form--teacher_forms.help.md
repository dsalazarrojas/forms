# Graduate Research Presentation Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for graduate research presentation submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Ensure that you, as the submitter, have acknowledged your involvement in the presentation.
3. Ensure that you, as the researcher, have acknowledged your involvement in the presentation.
4. If required, select the correct institutional review status and provide any necessary comments or review dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Research Presentation (1)
- **Research Presentation** (`research_presentation`, `text`, required): This is the title of your research presentation. Provide a clear and concise title that accurately describes your research.

### Research Topic (2)
- **Research Topic** (`research_topic`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether your research topic is yes or no. If yes, ensure it is an original and not previously presented or published.

### Date (3)
- **Date** (`research_date`, `date`, optional): This field is for the date of your research presentation, if applicable. If not applicable, leave this field blank.

### Institution (4)
- **Institution** (`research_institution`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable institutions related to your research.

### University Email (5)
- **University Email** (`university_email`, `email`, optional): This is your university email address. This field is optional, but recommended for contact purposes.

### Contact Phone (6)
- **Contact Phone** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): This is your contact phone number. Provide a valid phone number where you can be reached.

### Research File (7)
- **Research File** (`research_file`, `text`, optional): This is the file name and version of your research presentation.

### Researcher Name (8)
- **Researcher Name** (`researcher_name`, `text`, required): This is your full name as the researcher.

### Researcher Affiliation (9)
- **Researcher Affiliation** (`researcher_affiliation`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable affiliations related to your research.

### Submitter Name (10)
- **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the person submitting the research presentation.

### Submitter Email (11)
- **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required): This is your email address for contact purposes.

### Submitter Phone (12)
- **Submitter Contact Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required): This is your valid contact phone number.

### Institutional Review (13)
- **Institutional Review** (`research_institutional_review`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, select whether your research has undergone an institutional review.

### Institutional Review Date (14)
- **Institutional Review Date** (`research_institutional_review_date`, `date`, optional): If you selected yes for institutional review, provide the date of the review.

### Institutional Review Notes (15)
- **Institutional Review Notes** (`research_institutional_review_notes`, `text`, optional): If you selected yes for institutional review, provide any necessary notes.

### Institutional Review Status (16)
- **Institutional Review Status** (`research_institutional_review_status`, `select_one`, optional): If you selected yes for institutional review, select the status.

### Institutional Review Comments (17)
- **Institutional Review Comments** (`institutional_review_comments`, `text`, optional): If you selected yes for institutional review, provide any comments.

### Submitter Signature (18)
- **Submitter Signature** (`submitter_signature`, `select_one`, optional): If required, select yes if you have signed the submission.

### Submitter Declaration (19)
- **Submitter Declaration** (`submitter_declaration`, `text`, optional): Provide your declaration as the submitter.

### Submitter Acknowledgement (20)
- **Submitter Acknowledgement** (`submitter_acknowledgement`, `text`, optional): Acknowledge your involvement in the presentation.

### Submitter Date (21)
- **Submitter Date** (`submitter_date`, `date`, optional): This is the date of submission.

### Submitter Time (22)
- **Submitter Time** (`submitter_time`, `time`, optional): This is the time of submission.

### Researcher Acknowledgement (23)
- **Researcher Acknowledgement** (`researcher_acknowledgement`, `text`, optional): Acknowledge your involvement in the presentation.

### Researcher Declaration (24)
- **Researcher Declaration** (`researcher_declaration`, `text`, optional): Provide your declaration as the researcher.

### Researcher Signature (25)
- **Researcher Signature** (`researcher_signature`, `select_one`, optional): If required, select yes if you have signed the submission.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled.
- Verify the accuracy of the institutional review status and date.
- Ensure that you have acknowledged and signed the submission.
- Double-check for any typos or errors before submitting.
