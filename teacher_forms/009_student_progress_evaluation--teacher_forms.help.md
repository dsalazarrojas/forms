# Student Progress Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Student Progress Evaluation form is used to track and evaluate student progress. It collects information on first name, grade, and parent name, and allows teachers to record and analyze student performance over time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To get started, open the Student Progress Evaluation form.
2. Enter the student's **First Name** in the "First name" field.
3. Enter the student's **current grade** in the "Grade" field.
4. Enter the parent's **name** in the "Parent name" field.
5. Once you have filled in all the required and optional fields, click "Submit" to save your progress evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's first name, such as John, Jane, etc.
* **Grade** (`grade`, number, optional): Enter the student's current grade level, such as 2nd, 3rd, 4th, etc.
* **Parent name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the parent's name, such as John Smith, Jane Doe, etc.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure accurate progress tracking and evaluation.
* If the student has a nickname or preferred name, you can enter that instead of their full name.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or IT department for assistance.

Note: This guide is meant to be user-friendly, so I have assumed the form is intended to collect basic student information for progress tracking. If any field has a more complex purpose or requirements, please let me know and I will update this guide accordingly.
