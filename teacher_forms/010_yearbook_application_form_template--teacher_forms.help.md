# Yearbook Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yearbook application form template is designed to collect information about students from teachers or instructors. It's likely used for student evaluations or assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter relevant information for each field, making sure to answer all questions truthfully.
2. Fill out the form fields in the provided order, starting from "student_details" and ending with "student_info_6".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_details**: Enter the student's basic information, such as name, grade level, or other relevant details.
* **student_info**: Enter any additional student information not included in "student_details".
* **teacher_info**: Enter the teacher's or instructor's name and other relevant details.
* **role_assignment**: Describe the student's role in the yearbook (e.g., editor, illustrator, etc.).
* **yearbook_process**: Explain the process used to complete the yearbook.
* **student_evaluations**: Enter teacher comments, grades, or feedback about the student's performance.
* **student_info_2**, **student_info_3**, **student_info_4**, **student_info_5**, **student_info_6**: Enter further details about the student, such as strengths, areas for improvement, or specific skills.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out each field truthfully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your teacher or instructor for clarification.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
