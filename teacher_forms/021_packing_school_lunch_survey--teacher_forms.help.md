# Packing School Lunch Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your opinions and preferences about the school lunch program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as instructed. You will be asked about your feelings and satisfaction with the lunch options available, as well as questions about specific types of food. You can choose one of the options for each question, or leave a comment if you prefer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Lunch Feelings** (Yes/No, id: 2):
  Select whether you feel positive or negative about the school lunch program.
  * Yes: You feel positive about the lunch program.
  * No: You do not feel positive about the lunch program.

* **School Lunch Picky** (Number, id: 3):
  Please enter a number between 1 and 10 to indicate how picky you are about food.
  * 1: Very picky
  * 2: Somewhat picky
  * 3: Not very picky
  * 4: Not at all picky

* **School Lunch Satisfaction** (Number, id: 4):
  Please enter a number between 1 and 10 to indicate your satisfaction with the lunch options.
  * 1: Very dissatisfied
  * 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
  * 3: Neutral
  * 4: Somewhat satisfied
  * 5: Very satisfied

* **Lunch Type - Turkey** (Yes/No, id: 5):
  Select whether you prefer or do not prefer turkey as a lunch option.
  * Yes: You prefer turkey as a lunch option.
  * No: You do not prefer turkey as a lunch option.

* **Lunch Type - Tofu** (Yes/No, id: 6):
  Select whether you prefer or do not prefer tofu as a lunch option.
  * Yes: You prefer tofu as a lunch option.
  * No: You do not prefer tofu as a lunch option.

* **Lunch Type - Peanut Butter** (Yes/No, id: 7):
  Select whether you prefer or do not prefer peanut butter as a lunch option.
  * Yes: You prefer peanut butter as a lunch option.
  * No: You do not prefer peanut butter as a lunch option.

* **Lunch Type - Egg** (Yes/No, id: 8):
  Select whether you prefer or do not prefer egg as a lunch option.
  * Yes: You prefer egg as a lunch option.
  * No: You do not prefer egg as a lunch option.

* **School Lunch Survey Questions** (Text, id: 9):
  This is a text area for any additional comments or questions you may have.

* **School Lunch Survey Comments** (Text, id: 10):
  This is a text area for any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the text areas.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask a colleague or contact the school administration.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
