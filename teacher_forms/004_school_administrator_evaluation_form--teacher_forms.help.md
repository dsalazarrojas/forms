# School Administrator Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Administrator Evaluation Form is used to provide constructive feedback to school administrators on their performance, communication, leadership, and overall impact on the school community. This form should be completed honestly and objectively, focusing on specific examples or incidents that illustrate the administrator's strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure only one evaluation per school administrator is submitted.
- Review the form and answer questions honestly and objectively.
- Provide specific examples or incidents that support your evaluation.
- Ensure you have permission from the school administrator before submitting the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (Note): This section provides an opportunity to introduce yourself and your role in the school. Please include your name, position, and the date of the evaluation period.
  * This section is not required.
* **School Administrator Performance** (Select One):
  * Choose one of the following options:
    + Excellent: The school administrator consistently demonstrates exceptional performance and meets all expectations.
    + Very Good: The school administrator generally performs well, but there may be some minor areas for improvement.
    + Good: The school administrator meets most expectations, with some room for improvement.
    + Fair: The school administrator struggles to meet expectations, with notable areas for improvement.
    + Poor: The school administrator consistently fails to meet expectations.
  * Please provide specific examples to support your choice.
* **Communication Skills** (Select Multiple):
  * Choose one or more of the following options:
    + Excellent: The school administrator communicates effectively and clearly with staff, students, and parents.
    + Very Good: The school administrator generally communicates well, but may have some areas for improvement.
    + Good: The school administrator meets most communication expectations, with some room for improvement.
    + Fair: The school administrator struggles with communication, with notable areas for improvement.
    + Poor: The school administrator consistently fails to communicate effectively.
* **Leadership Style** (Number):
  * Rate the school administrator's leadership style on a scale of 1-5, where:
    + 1: Very Poor
    + 2: Poor
    + 3: Fair
    + 4: Good
    + 5: Excellent
  * Please provide specific examples to support your rating.
* **Feedback** (Text):
  * Provide detailed, constructive feedback about the school administrator's performance.
  * Focus on specific examples or incidents that illustrate their strengths and weaknesses.
* **School Administrator** (Select One):
  * Choose one of the following options:
    + Mr. Smith
    + Mrs. Doe
* **Communication 2** (Select Multiple):
  * Choose one or more of the following options:
    + Excellent
    + Very Good
    + Good
    + Fair
    + Poor
* **Leadership 2** (Number):
  * Rate the school administrator's leadership style on a scale of 1-5.
* **Evaluation Summary** (Text):
  * Summarize your overall evaluation of the school administrator's performance.
  * Highlight specific strengths and weaknesses.

## Tips
- Be honest and objective in your evaluation.
- Provide specific examples to support your feedback.
- Focus on constructive suggestions for improvement.
- Ensure you have permission from the school administrator before submitting the evaluation.
