# Faculty Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather feedback from faculty members about their teaching experience in a class. This feedback helps the institution to understand the strengths and weaknesses of the class and make informed decisions to improve the teaching quality and overall class experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question truthfully based on your experience with the class.
2.  Select the most suitable option for each question that best reflects your feelings or experiences.
3.  If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please use the "Do you have any feedback or suggestions?" field.
4.  If you would like to be contacted for further discussions or feedback, please provide your email address in the "How would you like to be contacted?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **How interesting is your class?** (`class_interesting`, number, required: false): This question asks you to rate how interesting you find the class on a scale from 1 to 10.
*   **What is the difficulty level of this class?** (`class_difficulty`, select_one, required: true): Please select the option that best describes the difficulty level of the class. This will help us understand how challenging or easy the class is for you.
*   **How satisfied do you feel about the class?** (`class_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all the options that best describe your level of satisfaction with the class.
*   **What is the teaching style of your instructor?** (`teaching_style`, text, required: false): Please describe the teaching style of your instructor, including any strengths or weaknesses you've observed.
*   **Have you used the class materials?** (`course_materials`, select_one, required: false): Let us know if you've used the class materials and if they were helpful or not.
*   **How well does your instructor communicate with you?** (`communication`, select_one, required: true): Select the option that best describes how well your instructor communicates with you.
*   **How interesting is the course content?** (`course_content`, number, required: false): Rate the interest level of the course content on a scale from 1 to 10.
*   **Do you have any feedback or suggestions?** (`feedback`, note, required: false): This is your chance to provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have about the class.
*   **How would you like to be contacted?** (`email`, email, required: false): If you would like to be contacted for further discussions or feedback, please provide your email address.
*   **How would you like to be contacted?** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you would like to be contacted for further discussions or feedback, please provide your phone number.
