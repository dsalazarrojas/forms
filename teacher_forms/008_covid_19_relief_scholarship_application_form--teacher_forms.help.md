# COVID 19 Relief Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from students to determine eligibility for the COVID-19 relief scholarship. Please answer the questions carefully to provide a complete application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Make sure to check the "Required" checkbox next to each field.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is complete and accurate.
4. Sign and date the form with your signature and the signature of a parent or guardian if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`1`, `application_date`, `required`): Date of application.
* **Student First Name** (`2`, `student_first_name`, `required`): Legal first name of the student.
* **Student Last Name** (`3`, `student_last_name`, `required`): Legal last name of the student.
* **Date of Birth** (`4`, `date_of_birth`, `required`): Date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Student Email** (`5`, `student_email`, `required`): Email address of the student.
* **Student Phone** (`6`, `student_phone`, `required`): Contact number of the student.
* **Home Address** (`7`, `home_address`, `required`): Street address of the student.
* **City** (`8`, `city`, `required`): City where the student resides.
* **State** (`9`, `state`, `required`): State where the student resides.
* **ZIP Code** (`10`, `zip_code`, `required`): Postal code of the student's address.
* **School Name** (`11`, `school_name`, `required`): Current school attended by the student.
* **Current Grade Level** (`12`, `grade_level`, `required`): Current grade level of the student.
* **Current GPA** (`13`, `gpa`, `required`): Grade point average of the student.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`14`, `parent_guardian_name`, `required`): Full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`15`, `parent_guardian_phone`, `required`): Contact number of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`16`, `parent_guardian_email`, `required`): Email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Total Household Size** (`17`, `household_size`, `required`): Number of people in the household.
* **Annual Household Income** (`18`, `household_income`, `required`): Annual income of the household before taxes.
* **How has COVID-19 impacted your family financially** (`19`, `covid_financial_impact`, `required`): Select all that apply.
* **Please describe how COVID-19 has impacted your family** (`20`, `financial_impact_description`, `required`): Detailed explanation of the financial impact.
* **Scholarship Amount Requested** (`21`, `scholarship_amount_requested`, `required`): Amount of scholarship money requested.
* **How will you use the scholarship funds** (`22`, `intended_use_of_funds`, `required`): Select all that apply.
* **List your academic achievements and honors** (`23`, `academic_achievements`, `optional`): Awards and recognition received by the student.
* **List your extracurricular activities and community service** (`24`, `extracurricular_activities`, `optional`): Clubs, sports, and volunteering activities.
* **Personal Statement** (`25`, `personal_statement`, `required`): Why the student deserves this scholarship.
* **Teacher Reference Name** (`26`, `teacher_reference_name`, `optional`): Name of the teacher who can vouch for the student.
* **Teacher Reference Email** (`27`, `teacher_reference_email`, `optional`): Email address of the teacher.
* **Additional Comments** (`28`, `additional_comments`, `optional`): Anything else to share.
* **Applicant Signature Date** (`29`, `applicant_signature_date`, `required`): Date signed.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature Date** (`30`, `parent_guardian_signature_date`, `required`): Date signed.
