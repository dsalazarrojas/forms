# Remote Learning Survey For Kindergarten Kids - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your thoughts and feelings about remote learning. It's a short and fun way to help us understand how you enjoy learning from home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Read through each question carefully.
2. Choose your answers for each question.
3. Hit submit to send your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction**: This is the start of our form. Please take a minute to read this, and then you can move on.
    * Type your thoughts, feelings, and experiences about remote learning.
- **Effectiveness**: How do you think remote learning is for you?
    * Do you think it's very helpful, not helpful, or somewhere in between?
- **Challenges**: What's hard or fun about remote learning for you?
    * Select all the things that are a bit tricky or fun for you.
    * Examples: Communication (e.g., talking to teachers and friends), Engagement (e.g., staying focused), Organization (e.g., keeping track of time), Resources (e.g., having enough materials).
- **Date**: What date did you start remote learning?
    * Type the date you started learning from home, even if it's not today.
    * If you're not sure, that's okay! Just type the best you can.

## Tips
* Remember, this form is just for you, and we want to hear your honest thoughts!
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask your teacher or parent.
* You can ask a grown-up to help you if you need it!
