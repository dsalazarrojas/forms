# Student Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Release Form is designed to collect necessary information from students, allowing teachers to understand their needs and provide an efficient and effective education.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Student Details** section with your student information.
2. Enter the **Teacher Name** of the teacher you are working with.
3. Sign with your **Parent Signature** in the designated area.
4. Provide some information to the **School Admin**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Details**
	+ A text field for the student to provide their details, such as name, student ID, or any other relevant information.
* **Teacher Name**
	+ The name of the teacher who is working with you.
* **Parent Signature**
	+ A place for you or your parent/guardian to sign and provide your consent.
* **School Admin**
	+ Some text field for the school admin to use for administrative purposes.
