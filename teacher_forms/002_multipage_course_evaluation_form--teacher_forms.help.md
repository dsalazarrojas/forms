# MultiPage Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect feedback from users about courses they have taken. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the course's effectiveness, teaching methods, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the course information section, which includes the title and description of the course.
2. Next, rate the course's subject matter using the provided options.
3. Then, describe the course materials used in the evaluation.
4. Evaluate the instructing techniques used in the course.
5. For each section, select one option that best describes your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, optional): Please provide a brief title of the course you are evaluating.
* **Course Description** (`course_description`, text, optional): Describe the course in a few sentences, including its main topics and objectives.
* **Subject Matter** (`subject_matter`, text, optional): Rate the course's subject matter, such as its relevance, depth, and accuracy.
* **Course Materials** (`course_materials`, text, optional): Describe the course materials used, including textbooks, resources, and any other materials used.
* **Instructing Techniques** (`instructing_techniques`, text, optional): Evaluate the teaching methods used in the course, including presentation style, engagement, and overall effectiveness.
* **Section** (`section`, select_one, optional): Select one option that best describes your experience with this section.
	+ Option 1: The section was well-structured and easy to follow.
	+ Option 2: The section was somewhat challenging, but still understandable.
	+ Option 3: The section was confusing and difficult to follow.
	+ Option 4: I didn't complete this section.
* **Section** (`section_2`, select_one, optional): Select one option that best describes your experience with this section.
	+ Option 1: The section was well-structured and easy to follow.
	+ Option 2: The section was somewhat challenging, but still understandable.
	+ Option 3: The section was confusing and difficult to follow.
	+ Option 4: I didn't complete this section.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): If you would like to provide feedback about the instructor, please enter their name.

## Tips
Please fill out each field completely and accurately to provide valuable feedback. You can also add any additional comments or suggestions you have about the course.
