# Sunday School Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on Sunday school program performance. It gathers information from students, teachers, parents, and other staff members to help improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field according to your role and the specific information being requested. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Feedback**: This field is for students to provide feedback about their experience in Sunday school. Please enter your thoughts and comments about your time in the program.
* **Subject Name**: This field is for teachers or staff to provide the name of the subject they teach or are involved with.
* **Teacher Name**: This field is for teachers or staff to provide their name for identification purposes.
* **Parent Feedback**: This field is for parents to provide feedback about their child's experience in Sunday school. Please enter your thoughts and comments about your child's time in the program.
* **Parent Name**: This field is for parents to enter their name for identification purposes.
* **Staff Feedback**: This field is for staff members to provide feedback about their experience in Sunday school. Please enter your thoughts and comments about your role in the program.
* **Other Staff Member**: This field is for other staff members to provide feedback about their experience with another staff member.
* **Overall Rating**: This field is for staff members to rate their overall experience with the program. Please select a rating from 1 to 5, with 5 being the highest.
* **Date**: This field is for staff members to enter the date they completed the form. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time**: This field is for staff members to enter the time they completed the form. Please use the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Which of the following?**: This field is for staff members to select one of the options provided. Please choose the answer that best describes your experience.
* **Select Multiple**: This field is for staff members to select multiple options that best describe their experience. Please select all that apply.
