# Registration Form English Learning Programs - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from teachers about their English learning programs. The form helps us to understand teacher details, program specifics, and availability for the language programs they offer. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness of your program information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each field to start filling out the form. Each section is labeled and explained below.
2. Enter the required fields (Program ID, Education, Language, and Availability) to submit a valid form.
3. Fill out the remaining fields to provide additional information about your program.
4. Review and edit your form before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program ID** (`english_learning_programs_program_id`): Enter a unique identifier for your English learning program. This helps us track your program's progress and status.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`): Enter your name as the teacher leading the English learning program. This will be used to communicate with you about your program.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address to contact you about your program or any questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for further communication about your program.
* **Teacher Profile Picture** (`teacher_profile_picture`): Upload your profile picture to be displayed on our website and marketing materials.
* **Start Date** (`program_start_date`): Enter the start date of your English learning program.
* **End Date** (`program_end_date`): Enter the end date of your English learning program.
* **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the name of the school associated with your English learning program.
* **Education** (`teacher_education`): Choose the highest level of education you have completed (Bachelor's, Master's, or Doctoral).
* **Experience** (`teacher_experience`): Enter the number of years of teaching experience you have.
* **Language** (`teacher_language`): Select all languages you are proficient in (English, Spanish, French).
* **Availability** (`teacher_availability`): Enter your teaching availability in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Description** (`teacher_description`): Enter a brief description of your English learning program.
* **Additional Information** (`teacher_additional_info`): Enter any additional information about your English learning program.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`): Click to submit your completed form to us.

Note: This guide assumes that you have already uploaded your teacher profile picture. If you haven't, please do so before filling out this form.
