# Self Assessment For Teaching Programs - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your teaching experience and background. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to help us assess your fit for our teaching programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and contact email address.
2. Enter your contact phone number and any other relevant contact information.
3. Provide your date of birth and start date of teaching experience.
4. Select your years of teaching experience.
5. Choose your teaching fields, subjects, and levels.
6. Specify any other teaching languages or levels you have experience with.
7. Enter your highest degree earned and the institution you received it from.
8. If your highest degree is not listed, please specify it in the "Highest Degree Other" field.
9. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Email**: Your contact email address. This will be used to contact you regarding your application.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Your contact phone number. Please enter your work or personal phone number.
* **Contact Phone**: Your contact phone number. (Duplicate of "Contact Phone Number")
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth. Please enter the date you were born.
* **Start Date**: The date you started teaching. Please enter the date you began your teaching career.
* **End Date**: The date you ended teaching. (Optional)
* **Years of Experience**: Your years of teaching experience. Please enter the number of years you have been teaching.
* **Teaching Field**: The subject area you teach. Choose from Math, Science, English, or Language Arts.
* **Teaching Subject**: The specific subject you teach. Choose from Math, Science, English, or Language Arts.
* **Teaching Level**: The level of students you teach. Choose from Elementary, Middle School, High School, or College.
* **Teaching Language**: The language you teach. Choose from English, Spanish, French, Chinese, or Other.
* **Teaching Language Other**: Any other language you teach. Please enter the language.
* **Teaching Language Level**: The level of language proficiency. Choose from Elementary, Intermediate, Advanced, or None.
* **Teaching Language Level Other**: Any other language level you have experience with. Please enter the level.
* **Highest Degree**: Your highest degree earned. Choose from Bachelor, Master, or PhD.
* **Highest Institution**: The institution where you earned your highest degree. Please enter the name of the institution.
* **Highest Degree Other**: If your highest degree is not listed, please specify it here.
* **Highest Degree Other Text**: Please enter a description of your highest degree.
* **Highest Degree Institution**: The institution where you earned your highest degree. Please enter the name of the institution.
* **Highest Degree Other**: If your highest degree institution is not listed, please specify it here.
* **Highest Degree Other Text**: Please enter a description of your highest degree institution.

Note: The fields marked as "Other" allow you to provide additional information that does not fit into the provided categories. Please use them to provide any additional details that are relevant to your teaching experience.
