# Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their experience with a particular course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Course" field with the name of the course you are evaluating.
2. Rate the overall quality of the course using the "Overall" field.
3. Evaluate the instructor by selecting one of the options in the "Rate this instructor" field.
4. Describe what you learned from the course in the "What did you learn from this course?" field.
5. Indicate whether you would take this course again by selecting one of the options in the "Would you take this course again?" field.
6. Add any additional comments or feedback you have about the course in the "Comments" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field if you want to be contacted with further questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course** (Text): Enter the name of the course you are evaluating.
* **Overall** (Number): Rate the overall quality of the course, with higher numbers indicating better quality.
* **Rate this course** (Select One): Select one of the options to rate the course.
* **Rate this instructor** (Select One): Select one of the options to evaluate the instructor's performance.
* **What did you learn from this course?** (Text): Describe what you learned from the course, what skills or knowledge you gained, etc.
* **Would you take this course again?** (Select One): Indicate whether you would take the course again, with one of the options indicating a positive response.
* **Comments** (Text): Add any additional comments or feedback you have about the course.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted with further questions or concerns.

# Tips
* Be honest and objective when completing the form.
* Take your time to provide thoughtful responses to each field.
* If you have any issues or concerns about the course, include them in the "Comments" field.
