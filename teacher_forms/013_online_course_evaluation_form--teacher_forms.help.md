# Online Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form was designed to gather feedback on the quality and effectiveness of our online courses. The responses will help us understand our students' opinions and identify areas for improvement, enabling us to provide better learning experiences for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step-by-Step Instructions

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Evaluate each section based on your experience with the course.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or more options that best fit your opinion.
3. For text fields, write your thoughts and comments clearly and concisely.
4. For optional fields, feel free to provide feedback if you have thoughts on those topics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with the overall quality of the course?** (`overall_quality`, `select_one`, **Required**)
	+ This field helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the course's quality.
	+ Choose one of the three options: Satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
* **Do you feel that the course content is relevant to your goals and interests?** (`course_relevance`, `select_multiple`, **Required**)
	+ This field helps us know if the course content aligns with your objectives and interests.
	+ Choose one or more of the options: True or False.
* **What are your thoughts on the course organization and structure?** (`course_organization`, `text`, **Optional**)
	+ This field allows you to provide feedback on the course's layout and organization.
	+ Write your thoughts clearly, and we will use it to improve the course.
* **How do you rate the instructor's teaching style?** (`instructor_teaching_style`, `number`, **Optional**)
	+ This field is used to rate the instructor's teaching approach and engagement.
	+ Enter a number from 1 (least effective) to 5 (most effective).
* **Do you have any suggestions for improving the course content?** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, **Optional**)
	+ This field is for you to provide specific ideas on how to make the course better.
	+ Write your suggestions, and we will consider them for future course updates.
* **Would you recommend this course to a friend or colleague?** (`recommendation`, `select_one`, **Required**)
	+ This field is used to gauge the course's value and recommendation potential.
	+ Choose one of the two options: True or False.
* **How likely are you to take this course in the future?** (`future_likelyhood`, `number`, **Optional**)
	+ This field is used to measure your interest in taking the course again or recommending it to others.
	+ Enter a number from 1 (least likely) to 5 (most likely).
* **What time of day do you usually take the course?** (`course_time_of_day`, `time`, **Optional**)
	+ This field helps us understand your preferred schedule for taking the course.
	+ Choose a time of day that works best for you.
* **Do you have any other comments or suggestions?** (`other_comments`, `note`, **Optional**)
	+ This field is for any additional thoughts or comments on the course.
	+ Write as much as you need to provide a clear and detailed feedback.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `email`, **Required**)
	+ This is a required field for us to get in touch with you for any further questions or discussions.
	+ Enter your email address, and we will use it to contact you regarding your feedback and suggestions.
