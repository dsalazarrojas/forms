# Summer School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who are enrolling in the summer school program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Student Information**:
	* Please fill in your name, email, and phone number.
2. **Select a Course**:
	* Choose the course you are interested in enrolling in.
3. **Student Contact Information**:
	* Enter your student email, address, city, state, and postal code.
4. **Teacher Information**:
	* Enter your teacher's name, email, and phone number.
	* Enter your teacher's address, city, state, and postal code.
5. **Submission Type**:
	* Select the type of submission you are making (Application, Enrollment, or Registration).
6. **Program Information**:
	* Enter the program name and enrollment type.
	* Select the program start and end dates.
7. **Additional Comments**:
	* If you have any additional comments or information, please enter them here.
8. **Confirmation**:
	* Confirm your course selection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your name in the format "Last, First".
* **Student Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Student Address**: Enter your address.
* **Student City**: Enter your city.
* **Student State**: Enter your state or province.
* **Student Postal Code**: Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **Student Country**: Enter your country of residence.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter your teacher's name.
* **Teacher Email**: Enter your teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Phone**: Enter your teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher Address**: Enter your teacher's address.
* **Teacher City**: Enter your teacher's city.
* **Teacher State**: Enter your teacher's state or province.
* **Teacher Postal Code**: Enter your teacher's postal code or zip code.
* **Teacher Country**: Enter your teacher's country of residence.
* **Submission Type**: Select the type of submission you are making (Application, Enrollment, or Registration).
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program you are enrolling in.
* **Enrollment Type**: Enter the type of enrollment you are making (e.g., full-time, part-time).
* **Program Start Date**: Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date**: Enter the end date of the program.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for help or clarification.
* This form is for general purposes only, and you should only use it for enrolling in the summer school program.
* If you need to make any changes, please fill out this form again with the corrected information.
