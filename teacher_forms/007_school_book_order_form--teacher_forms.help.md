# School Book Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Book Order Form is used by teachers or staff to record and process orders for school books or materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **School Book Order** field with the details of the order.
2. Enter the date of the order in the **Order Date** field.
3. Enter the due date for the order in the **Due Date** field.
4. Enter the total cost of the order in the **Order Total** field.
5. Select the payment method and payment gateway for the order.
6. Enter the **School Name** and **School Address** for delivery purposes.
7. Enter the **Teacher Name**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Book Order**: Enter the details of the order, such as the title, subject, or book description.
* **Order Details**: Enter any additional information related to the order.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date the order was placed.
* **Due Date**: Enter the due date for the order, typically the date by which the order should be fulfilled or delivered.
* **Order Total**: Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment, such as Square or PayPal.
* **Payment Gateway**: Select the payment gateway to use for the order.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of the school.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the teacher placing the order.
