# Secondary School Teaching Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secondary School Teaching Job Application Form is a document used to collect information from potential candidates for teaching positions at secondary schools. This form will be used to assess the qualifications and background of applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Applicant Information" page.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" page.
3. Describe your educational background in the "Education Background" page.
4. List your previous work experience, if any, in the "Employment History" page.
5. Provide information about your references, if applicable, in the "References" page.
6. Outline your teaching qualifications and qualifications in the "Teaching Qualifications" page.
7. List any relevant experience you have in the "Experience" page.
8. Specify your job-specific skills in the "Job-Specific Skills" page.
9. If you have any teaching certifications, list them in the "Teaching Certifications" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Applicant Information
* **Applicant Information** (`Applicant Information`, text, required): This page collects the general information about the applicant. Please fill in the required fields, such as your name and email address.

### 2. Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required): This page collects your contact details, including phone number and address. Ensure your email address is correct to ensure timely communication with you.

### 3. Education Background
* **Education Background** (`Education Background`, text, required): Describe your academic background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.

### 4. Employment History
* **Employment History** (`Employment History`, text, required): List your previous work experience, if any, including job titles and duration of employment.

### 5. References
* **References** (`References`, text, required): Provide information about your professional references, including their names and contact details.

### 6. Teaching Qualifications
* **Teaching Qualifications** (`Teaching Qualifications`, text, required): Describe your teaching qualifications, including any relevant diplomas or certificates.

### 7. Experience
* **Experience** (`Experience`, text, required): List any relevant work experience you have, including job titles and duration of employment.

### 8. Job-Specific Skills
* **Job-Specific Skills** (`Job-Specific Skills`, text, required): Specify your job-specific skills, including any relevant software or equipment proficiency.

### 9. Teaching Certifications
* **Teaching Certifications** (`Teaching Certifications`, text, required): List any teaching certifications you hold, including dates and institutions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in thoroughly and accurately.
* If you have any doubts about a particular field, please don't hesitate to contact the school's HR department.
* This form is for internal use only, and your information will be kept confidential.
