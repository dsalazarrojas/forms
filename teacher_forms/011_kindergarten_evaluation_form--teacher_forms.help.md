# kindergarten_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The kindergarten evaluation form is a tool used by teachers to document and assess student progress in a kindergarten setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the **Student Name** field.
2. Fill in the student's **Birth Date** in the **Birth Date** field.
3. Enter the teacher's name in the **Teacher** field.
4. Provide the name of the student's class in the **Classroom** field.
5. Describe the student's progress in the **Student Progress** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Text, required): Enter the student's name as it is written on their official records.
* **Birth Date** (Date, required): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Teacher** (Text, optional): Enter your name as the teacher responsible for evaluating the student.
* **Classroom** (Text, optional): Enter the name of the student's class, if applicable.
* **Student Progress** (Text, required): Describe the student's overall progress, strengths, and areas for improvement in the kindergarten setting.

## Tips
- Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure accurate evaluation.
- If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or school administration for guidance.
- This form is for internal use only and should not be shared with anyone outside the educational institution.
