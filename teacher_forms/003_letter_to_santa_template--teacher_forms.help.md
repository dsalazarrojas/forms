# Letter To Santa Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for writing a letter to Santa Claus, allowing you to express your wishes and thoughts about the holiday season. It's designed to be a fun and creative way to share your feelings with Santa, and is intended for both children and adults to use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information on each page, and don't worry if you need help along the way.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (`letter_to_santa_template`, text, required/optional): This is the first page of the form, where you can introduce yourself to Santa. Please fill in your name as you'd like Santa to address you.
* **What is your favorite holiday tradition?** (`letter_to_santa_template_question`, select_multiple, required/optional): On this page, you can choose whether you have a favorite holiday tradition, such as decorating the tree or making cookies. Select all that apply from the list.
* **What is the reason you love the holiday season?** (`letter_to_santa_template_answer`, text, required/optional): This is a space for you to share why the holiday season is special to you. Be as creative as you'd like, and don't worry about grammar or spelling.
* **What is your wish to Santa?** (`letter_to_santa_template_wishes`, note, required/optional): This is a special note-taking field where you can write your wishes directly to Santa. Think of this as a personal letter from you to Santa, and be as sincere and heartfelt as you can.
* **What else would you like to share with Santa?** (`letter_to_santa_template_additional_wishes`, text, required/optional): This is another text field where you can share any additional thoughts or wishes you have with Santa. Think of this as a bonus note to Santa, and don't worry about length or format.
