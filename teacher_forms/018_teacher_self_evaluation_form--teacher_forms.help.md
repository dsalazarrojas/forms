# Teacher Self Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Self Evaluation Form is a tool for teachers to assess their own performance and identify areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer each question in as much detail as possible.
2. For overall rating, select a number between 1 and 10 to indicate your self-assessment of your teaching performance.
3. In the text fields, provide a brief description or example of your teaching practices or experiences.
4. Be honest and reflective in your answers, as this form is meant to be a personal assessment of your performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Rating (1)**: This field is used to rate your overall performance as a teacher. Select a number between 1 and 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Teaching Effectiveness (Teaching Effectiveness)**: Describe your teaching practices that promote student learning and achievement.
* **Lesson Planning (Lesson Planning)**: Share an example of a well-planned lesson that led to positive student outcomes.
* **Student Engagement (Student Engagement)**: Describe how you encourage and support student participation and engagement in the classroom.
* **Student Progress (Student Progress)**: Share an example of a student's progress or achievement that you are proud of.
* **Teacher Availability (Teacher Availability)**: Describe your availability to support students, parents, and colleagues.
* **Teacher Support (Teacher Support)**: Describe how you support your students' needs and well-being.
* **Communication (Communication)**: Describe your communication style with students, parents, and colleagues.
* **Feedback (Feedback)**: Share any feedback you have received from students, parents, or colleagues that you found helpful or not.
* **Feedback**: This field is optional and meant to be used for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
## Tips

* Be honest and reflective in your answers, as this form is meant to be a personal assessment of your performance.
* Take your time to review each question carefully and provide detailed answers.
* Use examples or specific anecdotes to illustrate your teaching practices.
* This form is designed to be a self-assessment tool, so be kind to yourself and focus on areas for improvement rather than self-criticism.
