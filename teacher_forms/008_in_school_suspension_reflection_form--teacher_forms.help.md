# In School Suspension Reflection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and reflect on incidents involving student suspension. It gathers information on the student's name, incident details, and teacher's comments, as well as the duration and date of the suspension. This form helps teachers and administrators to better understand the incidents and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select the student's grade level from the "Student Grade Level" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the incident date in the "Incident Date" field.
4. Provide detailed information about the incident in the "Incident Details" field.
5. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
6. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
7. If applicable, enter the duration of the suspension in the "Suspension Duration" field.
8. Select the date of the suspension in the "Suspension Date" field.
9. If desired, enter the teacher's comments in the "Teacher Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name to associate their identification with the incident.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number, used for identification and tracking purposes.
* **Student Grade Level**: Select the student's grade level from the dropdown menu to categorize the incident.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Incident Details**: Provide a detailed account of what happened during the incident.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter your name as the teacher involved in the incident.
* **Suspension Duration**: If a suspension was given, enter the duration of the suspension in hours or days.
* **Suspension Date**: Select the date of the suspension in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Teacher Comment**: If you'd like to add any additional comments about the incident or student's behavior.
