# Parent Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from parents about their experience with the educational program, allowing administrators to gauge satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions according to your honest opinion of the program.
2. Fill out the required fields, such as the email address.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Program Rating** (`parent_program_rating`, number, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What do you think of the educational program?** (`question_1`, text, not required): Provide a brief answer to this open-ended question about your experience with the program.
* **Parent Satisfaction** (`parent_satisfaction`, select_one, not required): Select how satisfied you are with the program from the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Bad.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, not required): If you are a parent participating in the program with a specific teacher, provide their name.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, not required): If you have any additional feedback or comments about the program, provide them here.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, not required): If you are familiar with the name of the program, provide it here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time you completed the form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Goals** (`goals`, select_multiple, not required): Check the boxes next to the goals that you feel have been met by the program.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, number, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale, but this field seems to be identical to `parent_program_rating`, so it is likely redundant.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide accurate feedback to help us improve the program.
* Fill out all required fields and provide as much detail as possible for your answers.
* If you have any further questions, contact us at the email address you provide. 

Note: The fields "Satisfaction Level" seems to be identical in purpose to "Parent Program Rating", it's suggested to remove it from the form to avoid redundancy.
