# Natural Resources Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Natural Resources Quiz form is designed to collect information from users about natural resources, their types, and conservation practices. This form is intended to be a simple and user-friendly tool for collecting data on natural resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Natural Resources Quiz form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page according to your knowledge of natural resources.
2. Make sure to select multiple options when prompted for types of natural resources.
3. Complete the text fields to provide detailed information about natural resources and conservation practices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. natural_resources_important**: This field is optional and allows you to provide a brief description or name of a natural resource.
* **2. natural_resources_sustainability**: This field is also optional and provides space for you to write about the sustainable management of natural resources.
* **3. natural_resources_types**: This field is a multiple-choice question where you can select one or more of the following options:
	+ Water
	+ Land
	+ Minerals
	+ Air
* **4. conservation_practices**: This field is optional and allows you to provide detailed information about conservation practices related to natural resources.
