# Online Learning Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your thoughts, feedback, and information about online learning experiences. Your responses will help us better understand your experiences and improve our online learning services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please use the format "example@example.com".
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
5. Select your learning experience in the "learning_experience" field by choosing one or more of the options provided.
6. Provide any additional feedback you have about your online learning experience in the "feedback" field.
7. Enter the name of the course you have taken in the "course_name" field.
8. Enter the length of the course in the "course_length" field.
9. Select the difficulty level of the course in the "course_difficulty" field by choosing one of the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (first_name)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (last_name)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter your last name.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, `required: false`): Enter your email address in the format "example@example.com".
* **Phone Number (phone_number)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter your phone number.
* **Learning Experience (learning_experience)** (`select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select one or more of the options provided to describe your learning experience. Options:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
* **Feedback (feedback)** (`text`, `required: false`): Provide any additional feedback about your online learning experience.
* **Course Name (course_name)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the course you have taken.
* **Course Length (course_length)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter the length of the course in hours or other unit of measurement.
* **Course Difficulty (course_difficulty)** (`select_one`, `required: false`): Select the difficulty level of the course. Options:
	+ Easy
	+ Medium
	+ Difficult
