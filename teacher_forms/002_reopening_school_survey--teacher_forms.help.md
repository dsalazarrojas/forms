# Reopening School Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reopening School Survey is a form designed to gather information from parents about their children's school experiences and preferences for reopening.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select all the options that apply to your situation and follow the steps outlined in the Field-by-Field Explanation section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   *   **Send Children To School** (`send_children_to_school`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose which of the following options best describes your child(rens) school experience:
        *   'Yes' - Your child will attend school.
        *   'No' - Your child will not attend school.
*   *   **School Type** (`school_type`, `text`, optional): Describe your child's school experience.
*   *   **Parent Contact Info** (`parent_contact_info`, `select_one`, required): Select the response that best describes your contact information:
        *   'Yes' - You are willing to provide your contact information.
        *   'No' - You are not willing to provide your contact information.
*   *   **Reopen School** (`reopen_school`, `select_one`, required): Select the response that best describes your preference for school reopening:
        *   'Yes' - You are in favor of reopening the school.
        *   'No' - You are not in favor of reopening the school.
*   *   **Send Info To School** (`send_info_to_school`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose which of the following options best describes how you would like to receive information:
        *   'Yes' - Send information to school.
        *   'No' - Do not send information to school.
*   *   **Send Info To School Admins** (`send_info_to_school_admins`, `select_one`, optional): Select the response that best describes how you would like to receive administrative information:
        *   'Yes' - You would like to send information to school admins.
        *   'No' - You would not like to send information to school admins.
*   *   **Send Info To Parents** (`send_info_to_parents`, `select_one`, optional): Select the response that best describes how you would like to receive information from:
        *   'Yes' - You would like to receive information.
        *   'No' - You would not like to receive information.
*   *   **Send Info To Parents Admins** (`send_info_to_parents_admins`, `select_one`, optional): Select the response that best describes how you would like to receive information from admins:
        *   'Yes' - You would like to receive information from admins.
        *   'No' - You would not like to receive information from admins.
*   *   **Send Info To Both** (`send_info_to_both`, `select_one`, optional): Select the response that best describes how you would like to receive information from both school and parents:
        *   'Yes' - You would like to receive information from both.
        *   'No' - You would not like to receive information from both.
*   *   **School Survey Response** (`school_survey_response`, `select_one`, optional): Select the response that best describes your child's school experience:
        *   'Yes' - Your child has a positive experience.
        *   'No' - Your child has a negative experience.
*   *   **Parent Comments** (`parent_comments`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please type them here.
