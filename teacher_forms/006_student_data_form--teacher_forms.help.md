# Student Data Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Data Form is designed to collect essential information about students, including their name, parents' details, contact information, and address.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the mother's name in the "Mother's Name" field.
3. Enter the father's name in the "Father's Name" field.
4. If applicable, enter the home phone number in the "Home Phone" field.
5. If applicable, enter the cell phone number in the "Cell Phone" field.
6. If applicable, enter the work phone number in the "Work Phone" field.
7. Enter the student's address in the "Address" field.
8. Enter the student's email address in the "Email Address" field (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: <i>Enter the student's full name.</i>
* **Mother's Name**: <i>Enter the mother's full name.</i>
* **Father's Name**: <i>Enter the father's full name.</i>
* **Home Phone**: <i>If applicable, enter the home phone number of the student.</i> (<small>Required: No</small>)
* **Cell Phone**: <i>If applicable, enter the cell phone number of the student.</i> (<small>Required: No</small>)
* **Work Phone**: <i>If applicable, enter the work phone number of the student.</i> (<small>Required: No</small>)
* **Address**: <i>Enter the student's address, including street, city, state/province, and postal code.</i> (<small>Required: Yes</small>)
* **Email Address**: <i>Enter the student's email address.</i> (<small>Required: No</small>)
