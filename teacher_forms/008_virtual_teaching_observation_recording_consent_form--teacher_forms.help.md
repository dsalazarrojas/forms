# virtual_teaching_observation_recording_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The virtual_teaching_observation_recording_consent_form is a consent form for recording virtual teaching observations. It ensures that participants are aware of and agree to the recording of their virtual teaching sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and complete the form accordingly.
2. Answer each question based on your understanding and intentions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Virtual Observation Consent**: This is the main section of the form where you will provide consent to being recorded. Please select "I agree" if you give consent, or "I do not agree" otherwise.
* **Participant**: Please select your role as a participant in this virtual teaching observation. Choose between "teacher" or "participant" accordingly.
* **Recording of Observation**: Select the type of recording you are comfortable with, either "video" or "audio". Note that this field is optional and only required if you are providing a specific recording preference.
* **Purpose of Observation**: If you have any specific purpose or goal for this observation, please provide it in this text field.
* **Additional Notes**: Any further comments or details regarding this observation are welcome in this text area.
* **Email**: Please provide your email address, which will be used for communication related to this observation.
* **Phone**: If you have a preferred phone number, you can provide it, which will be used for scheduling or other communication purposes.
* **Date and Time**: Please select a date and time for the observation, if applicable.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you have a specific tool or platform assigned for the observation, please select it from the options provided, which are "chatjimmy" or "another tool". This field is optional and only required if you have a specific tool in mind.
