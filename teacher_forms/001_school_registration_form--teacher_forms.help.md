# School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who are registering for a school program or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields for the student's basic information, such as **Student Info**, **Date Of Birth**, and **Phone**.
2. Choose the student's classes by selecting the relevant options in the **Classes** field.
3. Enter the student's contact information, including their **Contact Info**, **Emergency Contact**, and **Emergency Contact Info**.
4. Provide medical information, if applicable, in the **Medical Info** field.
5. Fill out the **Guardian Info** and **Guardian Relationship** fields to provide information about the student's guardian.
6. Optionally, you can include the student's **Student Notes** for the school's reference.
7. Ensure that the student's **Student Email** and **Parent Contact** fields are completed accurately.
8. Finally, verify your selections in the **Student Interests** field and select the relevant options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's name and any other relevant information.
* **Classes** (`class_selection`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the classes that the student is currently enrolled in or has interest in.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's contact information, such as their address and any other relevant details.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the student's interests and hobbies.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's home address.
* **Guardian Info** (`guardian_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's guardian's name and contact information.
* **Parent Contact** (`parent_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the parent's contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional emergency contact information.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any medical information that is relevant to the student's health.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`student_phone`, number, required/optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Guardian Relationship** (`guardian_relationship`, select_one, required/optional): Select the relationship between the student and their guardian.
* **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the student.
* **Parent Contact** (`parent_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the parent's contact information.
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the student's interests and hobbies.
* **Guardian Info** (`guardian_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's guardian's name and contact information.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the school administration.
* Take your time to fill out the form carefully, as it will be used to process the student's registration.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification or guidance from the school staff.
