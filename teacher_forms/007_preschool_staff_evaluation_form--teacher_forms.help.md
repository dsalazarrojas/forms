# Preschool Staff Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Staff Evaluation Form is designed to evaluate the performance and effectiveness of preschool staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, select the staff member you wish to evaluate and provide feedback on their performance. You may select one or more staff members from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Member**: (Staff Member 1, Select One, Required): Select the staff member you wish to evaluate.
* **Teacher Form**: (Teacher Form, Select Multiple, Not Required): Choose the staff member's teaching methods or qualities you wish to praise or address, such as Staff Member 1 or Staff Member 2.
* **Evaluation Form**: (Evaluation Form, Text, Not Required): Provide any specific feedback or comments about the staff member's performance.
* **Comments**: (Comments, Note, Not Required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the staff member's performance.

## Tips
- Be specific and sincere when providing feedback.
- Focus on the staff member's performance rather than personal opinions.
- Keep the evaluation form simple and concise, one staff member at a time.
