# Student Interaction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Interaction Form is a tool for students to provide feedback and interact with teachers, instructors, or other educational staff. This form is used to collect detailed information about student interactions, likely for assessing student progress, evaluating teaching methods, or other pedagogical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the relevant options for the "Student ID" field to identify the student interacting with the teacher/instructor.
2. Enter your name in the "Teacher Name" field, if applicable.
3. Provide detailed feedback or comments in the text fields ("Student Interaction 1-16").
4. Fill out the date and time of the interaction in the "Interaction Date" and "Interaction Time" fields.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (`student_id`, select_one, required): Select the student's ID number from the dropdown menu to identify the student interacting with the teacher.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the teacher or instructor interacting with the student.
* **Student Interaction 1-16** (`student_interaction_1`, `student_interaction_2`, ..., `student_interaction_16`, text, optional): Provide detailed feedback, comments, or other information about the interaction with each student.
* **Interaction Date** (`interaction_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the interaction.
* **Interaction Time** (`interaction_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the interaction.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the interaction.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and specific as possible when providing feedback or comments.
* Ensure you fill out all relevant fields to provide a comprehensive record of the interaction.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your instructor or educational staff for clarification.
