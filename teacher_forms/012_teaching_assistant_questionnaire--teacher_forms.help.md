# Teaching Assistant Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from teachers about their interactions and experiences with the teaching assistant service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please feel free to include them in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address, including the domain name (e.g., example@domain.com).
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter the department or team you are part of, if applicable.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required: false): Enter your role or position within your department, if applicable.
* **Frequency of Interaction** (`frequency_of_interaction`, select_one, required: false): Choose how often you interact with the teaching assistant service:
	+ Rarely
	+ Occasionally
	+ Frequently
	+ Always
* **Mode of Interaction** (`mode_of_interaction`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the modes of interaction you typically use with the teaching assistant service:
	+ Phone call
	+ Email
	+ In-person
	+ Other
* **Mode of Interaction Other** (`mode_of_interaction_other`, text, required: false): If you chose "Other", describe the mode of interaction you use.
* **Tool Used** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Choose the tool you typically use with the teaching assistant service:
	+ chatjimmy
	+ Other
* **Other Tool** (`other_tool`, text, required: false): If you chose "Other", describe the tool you use.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or suggestions about the service.
* **How Satisfied with Service** (`how_satisfied_with_service`, select_one, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the teaching assistant service:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
