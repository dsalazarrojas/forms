# Student Performance Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a simple tool for teachers to provide a brief overview of a student's performance. Use it to note down your observations, thoughts, and feedback on a student's capabilities and progress.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Start Form" button to begin.
2. On the first page, type a brief introduction to explain the context of the evaluation.
3. On the second page, describe the student's performance, focusing on their strengths and areas that need improvement.
4. On the third page, discuss the student's academic progress, highlighting their accomplishments and any challenges they face.
5. Click "Submit" to save your input and complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Performance Evaluation Form (Page 1: Introduction)**
	+ **Student Performance Evaluation Form (text, optional):** A brief description of the form's purpose and the context of the evaluation. This is a free-form text field where you can introduce the purpose and scope of the evaluation.
* **Student Performance (Page 2)**
	+ **Student Performance (text, optional):** A description of the student's performance, highlighting their strengths and weaknesses. Try to be as detailed and specific as possible, providing examples or anecdotes if needed.
* **Academic Progress (Page 3)**
	+ **Academic Progress (text, optional):** A review of the student's academic progress, including their achievements and any difficulties they've faced. This is a good place to note down any notable events or milestones in their academic journey.

## Tips

* Be objective and fair in your assessment, focusing on facts and observations rather than personal opinions.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your comments, wherever possible.
* Keep your entries concise and focused on the main points, avoiding unnecessary details.
* Remember that this form is a tool for record-keeping and feedback. Treat it as a professional and respectful document.
