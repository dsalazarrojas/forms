# Teacher Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Information Form is a crucial document for teachers to provide their essential details to our system. This form will help us understand who you are, what you do, and your relevant experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **first_name** field. This is the first name of your full name.
2. Enter your last name in the **last_name** field. This is the last name of your full name.
3. Enter your **email_address**. This is your work email address used for communication.
4. Enter your **phone_number**. This is your work phone number for easy contact.
5. Enter your **teacher_number**. This is a unique identifier for you as a teacher.
6. Select your **subject_area** from the list. This represents the subject area you are most specialized in.
7. Select your **school** from the list. This is the school you are affiliated with.
8. Enter your **department**. This is your department or team in the school.
9. Enter your **position**. This is your role or title in the school.
10. Enter your **date_of_birth**. This is your date of birth.
11. Enter your **experience**. This is a description of your professional experience.
12. Enter your **profile_picture**. This is a photo of yourself to make our system more friendly.
13. (Optional) Upload your **cv** and **resume** to provide further documentation of your credentials.
14. Enter your **teacher_profile** and **teacher_profile2**. These are additional descriptions of your personality and teaching approach.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name here.
* **last_name** (text, required: true): Enter your last name here.
* **email_address** (email, required: true): Enter your work email address.
* **phone_number** (text, required: false): Enter your work phone number.
* **teacher_number** (number, required: true): Enter your unique identifier.
* **subject_area** (select_one, required: true): Select the subject area you are most specialized in.
* **school** (select_one, required: true): Select the school you are affiliated with.
* **department** (text, required: true): Enter your department or team in the school.
* **position** (text, required: true): Enter your role or title in the school.
* **date_of_birth** (date, required: true): Enter your date of birth.
* **experience** (text, required: true): Enter a description of your professional experience.
* **profile_picture** (text, required: false): Upload a photo of yourself to make our system more friendly.
* **cv** (text, required: false): Upload your resume or CV for further verification.
* **resume** (text, required: true): Upload your resume for further verification.
* **teacher_profile** (text, required: true): Enter a description of your personality and teaching approach.
* **teacher_profile2** (text, required: true): Enter another description of your personality and teaching approach.
