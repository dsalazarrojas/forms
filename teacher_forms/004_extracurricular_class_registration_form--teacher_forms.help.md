# extracurricular_class_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The extracurricular class registration form is designed to help teachers register their extracurricular activities with essential details to facilitate processing by the administration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details:
	* Student Name: Enter your name as it appears on your student ID.
	* Parent Name: Enter your parent's name as they appear on your student ID or the school's system.
2. Confirm your participation:
	* Select "Yes" if you are participating in the activity, "No" if not.
3. Provide your availability:
	* Date: Enter the date you are available to participate.
	* Time: Enter the time you are available to participate.
4. Enter contact information:
	* Email: Enter your email for communication with the administration.
	* Phone: Enter your phone number for contact.
5. Optional (if applicable):
	* Note: Provide any additional comments or notes that the administration might need.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Parent Name** (text, required): Enter your parent's name as they appear on your student ID or the school's system.
* **activity** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you are participating, "No" if not.
* **date** (date, required): Enter the date you are available to participate.
* **time** (time, required): Enter the time you are available to participate.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email for communication with the administration.
* **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **note** (note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes that the administration might need.
* **activity_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the activity you wish to register for.
