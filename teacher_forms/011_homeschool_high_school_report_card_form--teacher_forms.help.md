# Homeschool High School Report Card Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homeschool High School Report Card Form is a document used to track the progress of homeschool high school students. It is completed by both the student and their teacher, and provides a way to evaluate student learning and achievement throughout the year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Student Information**: This page is the first page of the form and is where you will enter your student's basic information. Please fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. **Subject 1-3**: These three pages are for entering grades or assessments for each subject. Please enter the appropriate information for each subject, taking care to follow any specific formatting or instructions provided.
3. **Comments**: This page is for providing any additional comments or feedback about the student's progress.
4. **Teacher Signature**: This page is where the teacher reviews and signs the form, indicating that it has been completed and reviewed.
5. **Student Signature**: This page is where the student reviews and signs the form, indicating that they have reviewed and agreed upon the information provided.
6. **Date Submitted and Date Completed**: These pages are for indicating the date the form was submitted and the date it was reviewed and completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information**: This page is used to enter basic student information such as name, grade level, or other relevant details.
	+ *Student's name*: Please enter the student's name as it is spelled and capitalized correctly.
	+ *Grade level*: Enter the student's grade level, which indicates the student's level of study in the homeschooling program.
* **Subject 1**: This page is for entering grades or assessments for the first subject.
	+ *Subject*: Enter the subject name and grade or assessment information, following any specific instructions provided.
* **Subject 2**: This page is for entering grades or assessments for the second subject.
	+ *Subject*: Enter the subject name and grade or assessment information, following any specific instructions provided.
* **Subject 3**: This page is for entering grades or assessments for the third subject.
	+ *Subject*: Enter the subject name and grade or assessment information, following any specific instructions provided.
* **Comments**: This page is for providing any additional comments or feedback about the student's progress.
	+ *Comments*: Please provide any relevant and accurate comments about the student's progress and learning.
* **Teacher Signature**: This page is where the teacher reviews and signs the form, indicating that it has been completed and reviewed.
* **Student Signature**: This page is where the student reviews and signs the form, indicating that they have reviewed and agreed upon the information provided.
* **Date Submitted**: This page is for indicating the date the form was submitted.
