# Personnel Emergency Response Group Training Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track training records for personnel within emergency response groups.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form in a step-by-step manner, following the order of the fields.
- Answer each question honestly and accurately.
- Ensure that you are not duplicating any existing records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User** (`User`, text, optional): Enter the name of the personnel member receiving the training.
* **Emergency Responder Group** (`Emergency Responder Group`, select multiple, optional): Check all applicable emergency responder groups that this training applies to.
	+ This field allows you to select multiple groups to which the training applies.
* **Training Date** (`Training Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the training took place.
* **Training Location** (`Training Location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the training took place.
* **Training Instructor** (`Training Instructor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the training instructor.
* **Training Time** (`Training Time`, time, optional): Enter the time the training started.
* **Training Duration** (`Training Duration`, number, optional): Enter the total duration of the training event (in minutes).
* **Training Status** (`Training Status`, select one, optional): Select the status of the training event from the options below.
	+ Completed: The training was completed successfully.
	+ In Progress: The training is currently ongoing.
	+ Cancelled: The training was cancelled or postponed.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or team lead for clarification.
- Ensure that you save your progress before leaving the form.
- Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
