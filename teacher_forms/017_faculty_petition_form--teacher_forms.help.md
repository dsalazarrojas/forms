# Faculty Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Petition Form is a tool for faculty members to communicate their needs, concerns, or ideas to the university administration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Choose the department and subject area that best describes the issue or concern you are petitioning about.
2. Enter the faculty member's name and contact email and phone number, so that you can be reached for further clarification.
3. Briefly describe the issue or concern in the message field.
4. Optionally, provide a title and a specific date and time for your petition.
5. Finally, choose who submitted the petition (e.g., department head, faculty member, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Faculty Petition Form** (`faculty_petition_form_page_1`, text, optional)
    - This is the title of the form. Enter your brief description of the issue or concern.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional)
    - Choose the department that best describes the issue or concern you are petitioning about. You can select multiple departments if applicable.
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, select_one, optional)
    - Choose the subject area that best describes the issue or concern you are petitioning about.
* **Faculty Member's Name** (`faculty_member_name`, text, optional)
    - Enter your name, so that you can be reached for further clarification.
* **Title** (`title`, text, optional)
    - Enter a concise title of your petition, if you wish to.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional)
    - Briefly describe the issue or concern you are petitioning about.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional)
    - Enter your email address, so that you can be reached for further clarification.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional)
    - Enter your phone number, so that you can be reached for further clarification.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional)
    - Choose who submitted the petition (e.g., department head, faculty member, etc.).
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional)
    - Enter the date on which you submitted the petition.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, optional)
    - Enter the time at which you submitted the petition.
