# Elementary School Message Recap - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Elementary School Message Recap form is designed for teachers to send updates to parents and staff. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your name as a teacher
* Choose the school name from the dropdown list
* Write your message to the parents (required field)
* Write your message to the staff (optional field)
* Choose a date and time for the message (optional)
* Select the subject of your message, e.g., "Upcoming Event," "Report Card," or "Progress Update"
* Select both the parents and staff as recipients of your message
* Enter the class name or section of the students you are reporting on
* Enter any additional notes about the event or message

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Teacher Name (teacher_name)**: Enter your name as a teacher in the school.
* **Select School Name (school_name)**: Choose the name of your school from the dropdown list.
* **Message to Parents (message_to_parents)**: Write a message for parents regarding your child's activities, progress, or other school events. This is a required field.
* **Message to Staff (message_to_staff)**: If you have any important announcements or updates, write your message here. This field is optional.
* **Date (date)**: If you want to schedule a future message, select the date. This field is not required.
* **Time (time)**: Choose the time for the message. This is a required field.
* **Subject (subject)**: Enter a brief description of your message, e.g., "Upcoming Event," "Report Card," or "Progress Update." This is a required field.
* **Recipient (recipient)**: Select both parents and staff as recipients of your message. This is a required field.
* **Select School (school_id)**: Choose the name of your school from the dropdown list. This is a required field.
* **Class (class)**: Enter the class or section of the students you are reporting on. This is a required field.
* **Notes (notes)**: Enter any additional notes about the event or message, if needed. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Make sure to keep your messages concise and clear.
* Check the school's communication policy before sending messages.
* Use this form for updates and announcements that are not already being tracked through other channels.
