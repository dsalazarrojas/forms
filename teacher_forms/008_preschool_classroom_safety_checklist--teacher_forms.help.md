# Preschool Classroom Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Classroom Safety Checklist is designed to ensure the safety and well-being of students and staff in preschool settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options for each section, considering the safety features and equipment available in the classroom.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed, as indicated by the "required" tag.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Aid Kits** (First Aid Kit 1, First Aid Kit 2, First Aid Kit 3):
	+ Select the first aid kit(s) available in the classroom.
	+ This field is required as it is essential for treating minor injuries and accidents.
* **Emergency Exit** (Door 1, Door 2, Door 3):
	+ Choose the primary exit route for the classroom.
	+ This field is required as it ensures that students and staff know the safe exit route in case of an emergency.
* **Fire Prevention** (Fire Extinguisher, Fire Alarm, Fire Exit Sign):
	+ Select all fire prevention measures available in the classroom.
	+ This field is required as it helps prevent fires and ensures everyone's safety.
* **Medical Kits** (Medical Kit 1, Medical Kit 2, Medical Kit 3):
	+ Choose the medical kit(s) available in the classroom.
	+ This field is required as it provides medical assistance in case of an accident or injury.
* **Fire Suppression** (Fire Suppression 1, Fire Suppression 2, Fire Suppression 3):
	+ Select all fire suppression systems or measures available in the classroom.
	+ This field is required as it helps prevent fires and ensures everyone's safety.
* **Electrical Outlets** (Electrical Outlet 1, Electrical Outlet 2, Electrical Outlet 3):
	+ Choose the primary electrical outlet(s) used in the classroom.
	+ This field is required as it ensures safe and proper use of electricity in the classroom.
* **Safety Signs** (Safety Sign 1, Safety Sign 2, Safety Sign 3):
	+ Select all safety signs posted in the classroom.
	+ This field is required as it informs students and staff of safety rules and procedures.
* **Emergency Procedures** (Emergency Procedure 1, Emergency Procedure 2, Emergency Procedure 3):
	+ Choose the emergency procedures documented and followed in the classroom.
	+ This field is required as it provides a clear plan in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
* If unsure about any field, consult with a supervisor or colleague for clarification.
* This form is an essential tool for maintaining a safe and healthy environment for preschool students and staff.
