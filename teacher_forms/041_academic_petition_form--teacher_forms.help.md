# Academic Petition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Academic Petition Form" is designed for students or staff to submit formal petitions for academic reasons. This form helps in gathering all necessary details to process and address the petition efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

### Complete each field as instructed:

*   **-- Academic Petition -- (Page 1)**
    Please acknowledge that you are submitting a formal academic petition.
*   **Petitioner name (Page 2)**
    Enter your full name as a student or staff member submitting the petition.
*   **Contact email (Page 2)**
    Provide your email address for confirmation and follow-up purposes.
*   **Petition subject (Page 3)**
    Give a brief title summarizing the content of your petition.
*   **Detailed petition description (Page 3)**
    Provide a detailed description of your petition, including reasons and supporting facts.
*   **Requested outcome (Page 4)**
    Specify the resolution or outcome you are seeking.
*   **Supporting documents (Page 4)**
    If you have any attachments, list or describe them for reference.
*   **Department contact (Page 4)**
    If applicable, provide the name of the department contact person.
*   **Signature confirmation (Page 5)**
    Type your full name to confirm the submission of your petition.
*   **Date submitted (Page 5)**
    Date and timestamp of when the petition was filed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Petitioner name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the individual submitting the petition.
*   **Contact email** (`email`, `email`, required): Your email address for confirmation and any follow-up communication.
*   **Petition subject** (`text`, `text`, required): A brief title summarizing the content of your petition.
*   **Petition details** (`text`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of your petition, including any supporting reasons and facts.
*   **Requested outcome** (`text`, `text`, required): Specify the specific resolution or outcome you are seeking from the petition.
*   **Supporting documents** (`text`, `text`, optional): List or describe any attachments or evidence that support your petition.
*   **Department contact** (`text`, `text`, optional): If known, enter the name of the department contact person related to the petition.
*   **Signature confirmation** (`text`, `text`, required): Type your full name to confirm the submission of your petition.
*   **Date submitted** (`date`, `date`, required): Date and timestamp when the petition was filed.

## Tips

*   Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   Attach any supporting evidence or documents if relevant to your petition.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant department or use the contact email provided.
*   This form is for academic purposes only, ensuring all submissions align with academic guidelines and codes of conduct.
