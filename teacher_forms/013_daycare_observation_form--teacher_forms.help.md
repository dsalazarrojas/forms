# Daycare Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Observation Form is designed to gather observations and feedback from teachers on the behavior and interaction of children in their care. This information will help inform the development of care plans and improve the overall care and well-being of the children.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer the questions on each page, selecting all applicable options for each field.
2. Enter your observations and feedback in the text fields provided.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Child Interaction** (Interaction, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the days you observed the child interacting with their parent or carer.
	+ Yes: Observed interaction (e.g. cuddling, holding, talking).
	+ No: No interaction (e.g. child was busy playing, sleeping, or eating).
* **Parent Involvement** (Parent Involvement, select_one, required: false): Indicate the level of parent involvement in the child's daily activities.
	+ Yes: Parents actively involved (e.g. attending to the child's needs, helping with activities).
	+ No: No parent involvement (e.g. parents not available, child self-sufficient).
* **Child Behavior** (Child Behavior, select_multiple, required: false): Describe the child's behavior during the observation period.
	+ Yes: Observed positive behaviors (e.g. sharing, empathy, cooperation).
	+ No: Observed challenging behaviors (e.g. tantrums, aggression, refusal to follow instructions).
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required: false): Enter any additional observations or comments you have about the child's behavior and interaction.
* **Teacher Comments** (Teacher Comments, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the child's behavior and interaction.
* **Teacher Signature** (Teacher Signature, text, required: false): Sign and date this form to confirm that it has been completed.
* **Parent Signature** (Parent Signature, text, required: false): If applicable, sign and date this form to confirm that it has been reviewed and agreed upon by the parent.
* **Date** (Date, date, required: false): Enter the date on which the observation took place.
* **Time** (Time, time, required: false): Enter the time on which the observation took place.
