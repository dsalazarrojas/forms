# Student Textbook Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Textbook Request Form is used to request textbooks for students in the classroom. This form is typically filled out by teachers to communicate with the school's textbook management team regarding student requests for textbooks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the student's current textbook status to ensure they do not already have the requested book.
2.  Select the desired answer to "Request A Textbook" based on whether the student requires a new textbook.
3.  Enter the student's name in the "Students Name" field.
4.  Enter the ISBN (International Standard Book Number) of the textbook in the "Textbook ISMN" field.
5.  Enter the title of the textbook in the "Textbook Title" field.
6.  Choose the grade level of the student from the "Grade Level" drop-down menu.
7.  Enter your name as the teacher who is requesting the textbook in the "Teacher Name" field.
8.  Briefly explain why the student is requesting the textbook in the "Student Requesting" field.
9.  Enter the ISBN of the second textbook in the "Textbook Isbn2" field (only if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request A Textbook**: Check yes if the student needs a new textbook, and no if they do not need one. This option will help the textbook management team prioritize your request.
* **Students Name**: Enter the full name of the student who needs the textbook.
* **Textbook ISMN**: Enter the 10-digit or 13-digit ISBN of the requested textbook.
* **Textbook Title**: Enter the title of the textbook that the student needs.
* **Grade Level**: Choose the grade level of the student from High or Low.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter your name as the teacher who is making this request.
* **Student Requesting**: Briefly explain why the student is requesting this textbook. For example, the textbook is required for the current lesson or unit.
* **Textbook Isbn2**: If the student needs more than one textbook, enter the ISBN of the second textbook in this field.
