# School Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students seeking admission to a school.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the following fields:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email (make sure it is a valid email address)
	* Contact Number (mobile or landline number of the student or parents/guardians)
	* Date of Birth
	* School Name (name of the school the student is applying to)
	* Address (current address of the student)
	* Gender (select one of the options provided)
	* School Type (select one of the options provided)
	* Level of Study (select one of the options provided)
	* Medium of Study (select one of the options provided)
	* Parents Name (name of the parents/guardians)
	* Parent Contact Number (mobile or landline number of the parents/guardians)
	* Emergency Contact Number (mobile or landline number of the parents/guardians)
	* Guardian Name (name of the guardian)
	* Guardian Contact Number (mobile or landline number of the guardian)
	* Student ID (student ID number or any unique identifier)
	* Student Photo (student photo preferably with a clear face)
	* Student Signature (student signature)
2. Upload the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the student's last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the student's or parents/guardians contact number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (e.g., 12/05/1999).
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school the student is applying to.
* **Address**: Enter the current address of the student.
* **Gender**: Select the student's gender from the provided options.
* **School Type**: Select the type of school the student is applying to.
* **Level of Study**: Select the level of study the student is applying for.
* **Medium of Study**: Select the medium of study the student is applying for.
* **Parents Name**: Enter the name of the parents/guardians.
* **Parent Contact Number**: Enter the mobile or landline number of the parents/guardians.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the mobile or landline number of the parents/guardians.
* **Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the guardian.
* **Guardian Contact Number**: Enter the mobile or landline number of the guardian.
* **Student ID**: Enter a unique student ID number.
* **Student Photo**: Upload a clear photo of the student with a visible face.
* **Student Signature**: Get the student's signature on the form.
* **Upload Date**: This field is automatically filled out when the form is uploaded.
* **Uploaded By**: Select who uploaded the form.
* **Uploaded At**: This field is automatically filled out when the form is uploaded.
* **Uploaded By Title**: Enter the title of the person who uploaded the form.
* **Uploaded By Name**: Enter the name of the person who uploaded the form.
